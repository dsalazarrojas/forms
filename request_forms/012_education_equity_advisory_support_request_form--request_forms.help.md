# Education Equity Advisory Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request support from the Education Equity Advisory team for educational resources and services. Please fill out this form to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the School or District Name where you are requesting support.
2.  Enter the name of the Contact Person responsible for the request.
3.  Enter the Email address of the Contact Person.
4.  Enter the Phone number of the Contact Person.
5.  Specify the Best Time to Contact the Contact Person.
6.  Describe the Question or Issue you are experiencing.
7.  Select the type of Support you are requesting (Guidance, Coaching, or Program Support).
8.  Enter a unique School or District ID.
9.  If applicable, select the specific tool or resource you have been assigned to use.
10.  Provide any additional Notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School or District Name (required)
*   School or District Name: A text field to enter the name of the school or district requesting support.
### Contact Person (required)
*   Contact Person: A text field to enter the name of the person requesting support.
### Email (required)
*   Email: An email field to enter the contact person's email address.
### Phone (required)
*   Phone: A text field to enter the contact person's phone number.
### Best Time to Contact (required)
*   Best Time to Contact: A time field to specify the best time to contact the contact person.
### Question or Issue (required)
*   Question or Issue: A text field to describe the specific question or issue you are experiencing.
### Requested Support (required)
*   Requested Support: A select multiple field to choose the type of support you are requesting (Guidance, Coaching, or Program Support).
### School or District ID (required)
*   School or District ID: A number field to enter a unique School or District ID.
### Assigned Tool (required)
*   Assigned Tool: A select one field to choose the specific tool or resource you have been assigned to use (chatjimmy or Other).
### Notes (optional)
*   Notes: A note field to provide additional information about your request.
