# digitalAgentCommissionRequestForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for digital agents to request commissions for their work. It's intended for external use, ensuring secure submission of commission requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer**: Enter the customer's number or identifier for your digital agent account. If you're unsure which customer number to use, please contact your account manager for clarification.
* **Commission**: Enter the amount of the commission you are requesting.
* **Commission Date**: Enter the date you completed the work for which you are requesting the commission.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately enter your customer number to ensure timely and correct payment processing.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your account manager for assistance.
- Only submit one form per commission request; duplicates will not be processed.
