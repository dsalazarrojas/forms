# Creator Platform Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creator Platform Support Request Form is a tool for creators to submit their requests and issues to the platform support team. This form helps the support team to understand the creator's concerns and provide timely and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Request Form` field with a clear and concise description of your request or issue.
2. In the `Details` field, provide more information about your request or issue, including any relevant context or details.
3. If your request has a specific priority level (e.g., urgent, regular), choose the corresponding option in the `Priority` field.
4. If you have any attachments or supporting files related to your request, select them in the `Attachments` field.
5. Finally, provide a detailed description of your request or issue in the `Description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`creator_platform_support_request_form`, text, required: false)
  - This field is a short description of your request or issue.
  - Please keep it concise and to the point.
* **Details** (`creator_support_request_details`, text, required: false)
  - This field is where you can provide more context or information about your request or issue.
  - If you have any supporting documents or resources, you can upload them here.
* **Priority** (`creator_platform_support_request_form_priority`, select_one, required: false)
  - This field helps the support team to prioritize your request.
  - Please select the level of priority that best reflects your request's urgency.
  - Options: High, Medium, Low
* **Attachments** (`creator_support_request_attachments`, select_multiple, required: false)
  - This field allows you to attach any files or documents that support your request.
  - You can select up to three attachments.
* **Description** (`creator_support_request_description`, text, required: false)
  - This field requires you to provide a detailed description of your request or issue.
  - Please be as comprehensive as possible to help the support team understand your concern.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a clear reason for submitting a request and that it is not a repeat of a previous issue.
- If you are unsure about the priority level of your request, consult the platform's priority guidelines for reference.
- Attach only files that are relevant to your request or issue to minimize clutter and ensure efficient processing.
