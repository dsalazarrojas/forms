# Flight Rescheduling Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flight Rescheduling Request Form is used to request changes to a flight schedule. This form is used to provide information about the flight, the traveler, and the reason for the reschedule request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Review the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person traveling.
* **Flight Number** (text, required): Enter the number of the flight being rescheduled.
* **Departure Date** (date, required): Enter the original departure date of the flight.
* **Travel Agent** (text, required): Enter the name of the travel agent handling the flight.
* **Reason for Request** (text, required): Describe the reason for the reschedule request.
* **Reschedule Date** (date, required): Enter the new departure date of the flight.
* **Reschedule Time** (time, required): Enter the new departure time of the flight.
* **Travel Agent Contact** (text, required): Enter the contact information for the travel agent handling the flight.
* **Reschedule Type** (select_one, required): Choose between "Rescheduling" or "Cancellation".
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the flight.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address associated with the traveler.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the traveler.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the flight.
* **Travel Company** (text, required): Enter the name of the travel company.
* **Origin** (select_one, required): Choose the city of origin for the flight.
* **Destination** (select_one, required): Choose the destination city for the flight.
* **Travel Type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the type of travel (Business or Leisure).
* **Travel Agency** (select_one, required): Choose the travel agency handling the flight.
* **Travel Class** (select_multiple, required): Choose the class of service for the flight.
* **Passenger Count** (number, required): Enter the number of passengers on the flight.
* **Travel Date** (date, required): Enter the original travel date of the flight.
* **Travel Time** (time, required): Enter the original travel time of the flight.
* **Flight Status** (select_one, required): Choose the status of the flight (Departed, Arrived, Cancelled, Delayed).

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
