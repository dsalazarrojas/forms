# Voice Channel Activation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Voice Channel Activation Form is designed to collect information about the user's needs and preferences for voice channel activation. It helps us understand how you'd like to interact with our company through voice assistants like Alexa or Google Assistant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as indicated in the form.
2. Select the options that best match your needs and preferences.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voice Channel Activation** (`voice_channel_activation_form`, text, required): This is the main field where you enter your voice channel activation request.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required): Describe your purpose for using the voice channel, e.g., for customer support, product information, or entertainment.
* **Access Needs** (`access_needs`, select multiple, optional): If you have specific access needs for your voice channel, please select them from the list. You can also choose "Other" if your needs are not listed.
* **Activation Timing** (`activation_timing`, time, optional): If you have a preferred timing for your voice channel activation, please enter it in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 12:00).
* **Voice Channel Name** (`voice_channel_name`, text, optional): If you have a specific name for your voice channel, please enter it. This will help us to identify your channel more easily.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any additional information about your voice channel, please let us know in the "Purpose" field.
- If you select "Other" in the "Access Needs" field, please provide more details in the "Purpose" field.
