# Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sales Consultant Estimate Form is a tool used by sales consultants to estimate services and provide a professional quote to clients. It ensures accurate and reliable estimates, building trust with clients and aiding in closing deals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Information** field with the client's details, which is a required field.
2. Select the **Services Needed** options that apply to the client's project, which is also a required field.
3. For each selected service, provide the estimated hours and price in the corresponding fields.
4. Select the **Terms** field to confirm that you have accurately estimated the services and are ready to proceed.
5. Finally, select the **Submit** button to complete the form and provide the estimate to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Client Information
* **Client Information** (text, required): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any relevant details.

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Services Needed
* **Services Needed** (select multiple, required): Select all the services that apply to the client's project.
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Service 1
* **Service 1** (select one, required): Select the service that applies to the client's project.
  - Options: 'Yes', 'No'

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Service 1 Hours
* **Service 1 Hours** (number, required): Enter the estimated hours for the selected service.
  - Example: 5

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Service 1 Price

### Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Service 1 Currency
