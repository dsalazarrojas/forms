# Mining Safety Gear Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request mining safety gear for use in a mining environment. Please submit this form if you need any of the following items: safety helmets, safety glasses, respirators, gloves, or any other safety gear that is not already stocked in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user information section with your name and phone number.
2. Indicate whether you are requesting equipment (yes or no).
3. Provide a description of the equipment you are requesting (if applicable).
4. Specify if you want the supervisor to review your request before it is submitted (yes or no).
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
6. Choose a date for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required): Enter your name and phone number to identify yourself as the requester.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records or ID.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your work phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your work email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Equipment Requested** (Equipment Requested, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting equipment, 'No' otherwise.
* **Equipment Description** (Equipment Description, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting, including any specifications or quantities needed.
* **Supervisor Review** (Supervisor Review, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you want the supervisor to review your request before it is submitted, 'No' otherwise.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Choose a date for the submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed descriptions of the equipment you are requesting.
* If the supervisor has reviewed and approved your request, ensure all submitted comments are accurate.
* Please be patient with our processing times for your request, as we need to review each submission carefully.
