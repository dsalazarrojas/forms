# Church Budget Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Budget Request Form is used to collect financial request information from church departments and committees. It ensures that all budget requests are submitted with accurate and complete information, aiding in the review and decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your name, title or position, email address, phone number, and department or ministry information in the "Requestor Information" section.
2. In the "Budget Details" section, select the requested budget period (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually) and enter the corresponding dates for the budget period.
3. Choose the category for your request (e.g., personnel, supplies, equipment, programs, etc.) and provide a description of your request.
4. Explain how your request will benefit the church and its members, and be prepared to provide a contingency plan if funding is reduced.
5. Confirm that your request has been approved by the department or committee authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Requestor**: Enter your name as it appears on your official church documents.
* **Title or Position**: Enter your title or position within the church (e.g., pastor, ministry leader, committee chair, etc.).
* **Email Address**: Enter your official church email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your church phone number for quick contact.
* **Department or Ministry**: Enter the department or ministry you are representing (e.g., Sunday school, music ministry, youth group, etc.).
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the budget for.
* **Budget Period**: Select the budget period for which you are requesting funds (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually).
* **Budget Period Dates**: Enter the specific dates for the budget period (e.g., January to June or July to December).
* **Request Category**: Choose the category for your request (e.g., personnel, supplies, equipment, programs, etc.).
* **Description of Request**: Provide a detailed description of what the funds will be used for and why.
* **Justification and Expected Outcomes**: Explain how your request will benefit the church and its members, and be prepared to provide a contingency plan if funding is reduced.
* **Total Amount Requested**: Enter the total amount of money requested.
* **Budget Breakdown**: List and itemize the specific costs and expenses for your request.
* **Funds Currently Available in Department**: Enter any funds that are currently available within your department.
* **Previous Budget Requests This Year**: Provide any previous budget requests made this year and their status.
* **Expected Impact on Members**: Describe the expected impact on the church members and how they will benefit from your request.
* **Timeline for Spending**: Provide a detailed timeline for spending the requested funds.
* **Contingency Plan**: Be prepared to adjust your request if funding is partial or reduced.
* **Approved by Department or Committee Authority**: Confirm that your request has been approved by the department or committee authority.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the approver or department/committee leader.
