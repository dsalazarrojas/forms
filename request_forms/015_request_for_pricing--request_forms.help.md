# Request For Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your pricing request from our team. Please fill it out completely and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible response to your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a descriptive title for your pricing request in the "Request For Pricing" field.
2. In the "Price Inquiry" field, briefly describe the product or service you are interested in and the price range you are looking for.
3. In the "Price Range" field, provide the specific price range you are looking for.
4. Select "Buyer" or "Seller" from the dropdown menu in the "Buyer/Seller" field to indicate your role in the transaction.
5. Choose from the list of options in the "Goods/Services" field to indicate what you are requesting a price for.
6. In the "Price Inquiry Type" field, select "Ask for Range" if you are looking for a price range, or "Ask for Specific" if you are looking for a specific price.
7. If you are looking for a minimum price, enter it in the "Min Price" field.
8. If you are looking for a maximum price, enter it in the "Max Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request For Pricing** (`request_for_pricing_form`, text, optional): A brief title for your pricing request. This will help our team quickly understand the context of your inquiry.
- **Price Inquiry** (`price_inquiry`, text, optional): A descriptive field where you can explain what product or service you are interested in and the price range you are looking for.
- **Price Range** (`price_range`, text, optional): If you are looking for a specific price range, enter it here.
- **Buyer/Seller** (`buyer_seller`, select_one, optional): Select "Buyer" if you are the buyer, "Seller" if you are the seller.
- **Goods/Services** (`goods_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the list of options what you are requesting a price for.
- **Established Price** (`established_price`, number, optional): If you know the established price of the product or service, you can enter it here.
- **Price Inquiry Type** (`price_inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Ask for Range" if you are looking for a price range, "Ask for Specific" if you are looking for a specific price.
- **Min Price** (`min_price`, number, optional): If you are looking for a minimum price, enter it here.
- **Max Price** (`max_price`, number, optional): If you are looking for a maximum price, enter it here.
- **Price Range** (`price_range`, text, optional): This field is for entering a specific price range if "Price Inquiry Type" is set to "Ask for Range".
