# Construction Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Construction Estimate Form is a tool used to gather essential information from customers about a construction project. It helps in preparing and providing a detailed estimate for a construction project, including the total cost, labor hours, equipment costs, and other relevant expenses. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately to provide a comprehensive and reliable estimate to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the "Estimate Date" which is the date when the estimate is prepared.
2.  Enter the "Estimate Number" which is a unique reference number for the estimate.
3.  Enter the "Customer Name" which is the name of the client or property owner.
4.  Enter the "Customer Email" and "Customer Phone" which are the email and phone number of the client.
5.  Enter the "Project Address" which is the location where the work will be performed.
6.  Select the "Project Type" from the given options such as New Construction, Addition, Renovation, etc.
7.  Enter the "Project Description" which is a detailed scope of work for the project.
8.  Enter the "Materials Cost", "Labor Cost", "Equipment Cost", "Subcontractor Cost", "Permit and Inspection Fees", "Disposal and Dump Fees", "Overhead Cost" which are all monetary values.
9.  Select the "Contingency Percentage" or enter the "Contingency Amount" depending on your preference.
10.  Enter the "Estimated Labor Hours" which is the total hours expected for the project.
11.  Enter the "Total Estimate Amount" which is the sum of all costs.
12.  Enter the "Estimate Valid Until" which is the expiration date for this estimate.
13.  Enter the "Payment Terms" which is the proposed payment schedule.
14.  Enter the "Estimated Timeline" which is the expected project duration.
15.  Enter the "Exclusions" which is a list of what is NOT included in this estimate.
16.  Enter the "Additional Notes" which is any other relevant information.
17.  Finally, enter the "Prepared By" which is the name of the estimator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estimate Date**: The date when this estimate is prepared.
* **Estimate Number**: A unique reference number for the estimate.
* **Customer Name**: The name of the client or property owner.
* **Customer Email**: The email address for the estimate delivery.
* **Customer Phone**: The contact phone number of the client.
* **Project Address**: The location where the work will be performed.
* **Project Type**: The category of construction work, e.g., New Construction, Addition, etc.
* **Project Description**: A detailed scope of work for the project.
* **Materials Cost**: The total cost of materials.
* **Materials Details**: A list of major materials included in the project.
* **Labor Cost**: The total labor expense.
* **Estimated Labor Hours**: The total hours expected for the project.
* **Equipment Cost**: The equipment rental or usage fees.
* **Subcontractor Cost**: The costs for subcontracted work.
* **Permit and Inspection Fees**: The costs for permits and inspections.
* **Disposal and Dump Fees**: Debris removal costs.
* **Overhead Cost**: The administrative and overhead expenses.
* **Contingency Percentage**: The percentage of contingency reserve. Enter 0 if no percentage is desired.
*   **Contingency Amount**: A dollar amount for contingency. Enter 0 if no amount is desired.
*   **Profit Margin**: The profit amount or percentage.
*   **Total Estimate Amount**: The sum of all costs.
*   **Estimate Valid Until**: The expiration date for this estimate.
*   **Payment Terms**: The proposed payment schedule.
*   **Estimated Timeline**: The expected project duration.
*   **Exclusions**: A list of what is NOT included in this estimate.
*   **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information.
*   **Prepared By**: The name of the estimator.
