# Request For Salary Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Salary Change Form is a tool used by employees to submit requests for changes to their salary. This form is used to gather necessary information from the employee and their manager for the purpose of salary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and manager's contact information, including their email address and phone number.
3. Enter your manager's department and location.
4. Select "Submit" to send the request to your manager for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and email address to identify yourself.
### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
### Salary
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter your current salary.
### Reason
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your salary change request.
### Manager Comments
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions from your manager.
### User Comments
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions from you.
### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select "Submit" to send the request to your manager for review and approval, or "Save and Continue" to save the request but not submit it.
### Manager Approval
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select "Approve" to approve the request, or "Deny" to deny it.
### User Approval
* **User Approval** (`user_approval`, select_one, optional): Select "Approve" to approve the request, or "Deny" to deny it.
### Form ID
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This is an internal field that tracks the form's ID.
### Timestamp
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, time, optional): This is a timestamp field that records when the form was submitted.
### Timestamp 2
* **Timestamp 2** (`timestamp2`, time, optional): This is another timestamp field that records when the form was submitted.
### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
### Manager Phone
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
### Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
### Manager Address
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, text, optional): Enter your manager's work address.
### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
### Manager Department
* **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, text, optional): Enter your manager's department.
### User Department
* **User Department** (`user_department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
### Manager Location
* **Manager Location** (`manager_location`, text, optional): Enter your manager's location.
### User Location
* **User Location** (`user_location`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
* Please note that any submitted salary change requests will be reviewed and approved according to company policies and procedures.
