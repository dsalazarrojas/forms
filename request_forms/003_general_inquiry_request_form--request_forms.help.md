# General Inquiry Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit general inquiries and requests to the administrator. It is meant for users who need assistance or have questions that cannot be answered through other channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `name` field with your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your valid `email` address to ensure prompt response.
3. If applicable, provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Write a brief description of your request in the `request` field.
5. Optionally, provide additional details about your request in the `request_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Required): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Your valid email address to ensure prompt response.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Your phone number, if applicable.
* **Request** (`request`, Text, Required): A brief description of your request.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, Text, Optional): Additional details about your request.
