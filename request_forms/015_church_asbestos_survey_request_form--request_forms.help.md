# Church Asbestos Survey Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a church asbestos survey, which is crucial for ensuring the safety of individuals and the church property. The information collected will help the surveyor assess the presence, location, and condition of asbestos-containing materials within the building.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
- Enter the name and contact information of the person requesting the survey.
- Provide the address of the church property to be surveyed.
- Select the correct reason for the survey request.
- Indicate if there are any areas with limited access.
- If applicable, provide preferred dates for the site visit.
- Check if there are any previous asbestos survey reports available.
- Add any additional property notes or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Church**: A text field to enter the name of the church or organization.
* **Denomination**: A text field to enter the denomination of the church.
* **Property Address**: A text field to enter the full address of the building to be surveyed.
* **Approximate Age of Building**: A number field to enter the approximate age of the building.
* **Requester Name**: A text field to enter the name of the person requesting the survey.
* **Position or Role**: A text field to enter the position or role of the person requesting the survey.
* **Contact Phone Number**: A text field to enter the contact phone number of the person requesting the survey.
* **Contact Email Address**: An email field to enter the contact email address of the person requesting the survey.
* **Reason for Survey Request**: A select field with options for choosing the reason for the survey request. Choose one of the following:
	+ Routine Maintenance
	+ Planned Renovation
	+ Demolition
	+ Real Estate Transaction
	+ Suspected Damage
* **Are there areas with limited access?**: A text field to describe any areas within the building with limited access.
* **Preferred Dates for Site Visit**: A text field to enter the preferred dates for the site visit.
* **Are there any previous asbestos survey reports available?**: A select field with options for yes, no, or unknown.
* **Additional Property Notes or Concerns**: A text field to enter any additional notes or concerns about the property.
