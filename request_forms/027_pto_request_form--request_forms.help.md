# PTO Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTO Request Form is a crucial tool for employees to submit their paid time off requests to the management team. This form helps to streamline the process of requesting time off, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed by the manager before approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Employee Name**. This is a required field, so please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
2. Next, select a **Start Date** and **End Date** for your time off. These dates should reflect the actual days you plan to be away from work.
3. Provide a brief **Reason** for your time off. This will help the manager understand your request and make an informed decision.
4. Select your current **Employee Status** (Active or Inactive).
5. If necessary, enter your **Manager Name**, **Manager Email**, and **Manager Phone**. This information will be used to contact your manager for further discussion or approval.
6. Include any **Manager Comment** you'd like to share with the manager.
7. Finally, select a **Request Date**, which is the date you'd like your request to be reviewed and approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This is the field where you should enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Start Date**: Choose the date you plan to start your time off from.
* **End Date**: Select the date you plan to end your time off on.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for your time off request.
* **Employee Status**: Choose your current status (Active or Inactive).
* **Manager Name**: If you have a manager, enter their name here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Manager Email**: If you have a manager's email address, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Manager Phone**: If you have a manager's phone number, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Manager Comment**: This is a text field where you can include any additional comments or notes for your manager.
* **Request Date**: Choose the date by which you'd like your request to be reviewed and approved.
