# Community Engagement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Engagement Request Form is a tool for requesting engagement from the community, such as events, partnerships, or resources. This form ensures that all requests are documented and reviewed by the community team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Full Name of Requestor (Page 1, Field 1)
	* Contact Email Address (Page 1, Field 3)
	* Contact Phone Number (Page 1, Field 4)
	* Type of Engagement Requested (Page 1, Field 5)
	* Primary Goal of Engagement (Page 1, Field 6)
	* Target Audience (Page 1, Field 7)
	* Proposed Date (Page 1, Field 8)
	* Proposed Location (Page 1, Field 9)
	* I agree to follow community guidelines (Page 1, Field 13)
2. If applicable, provide additional information:
	+ Organization or Group Name (Page 1, Field 2)
	+ Estimated Number of Participants (Page 1, Field 10)
	+ Resources Requested from Community (Page 1, Field 11)
	+ Additional Information or Project Description (Page 1, Field 12)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name of Requestor (Page 1, Field 1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Organization or Group Name (Page 1, Field 2)**: If applicable, provide the name of your organization or group.
- **Contact Email Address (Page 1, Field 3)**: Enter your contact email address for communication and follow-up.
- **Contact Phone Number (Page 1, Field 4)**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
- **Type of Engagement Requested (Page 1, Field 5)**: Choose the type of engagement requested (e.g., Educational Workshop, Social Event).
- **Primary Goal of Engagement (Page 1, Field 6)**: Describe the primary goal or objective of the engagement.
- **Target Audience (Page 1, Field 7)**: Indicate the intended audience for the engagement.
- **Proposed Date (Page 1, Field 8)**: Select a date for the engagement.
- **Proposed Location (Page 1, Field 9)**: Enter the proposed location for the engagement.
- **Estimated Number of Participants (Page 1, Field 10)**: If requested, estimate the number of participants for the engagement.
- **Resources Requested from Community (Page 1, Field 11)**: Check any resources you need from the community (e.g., meeting space, audio-visual equipment).
- **Additional Information or Project Description (Page 1, Field 12)**: Provide any additional details or context about the engagement.
- **I agree to follow community guidelines (Page 1, Field 13)**: Confirm that you will adhere to community guidelines and rules.
