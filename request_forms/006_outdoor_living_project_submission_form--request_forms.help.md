# Outdoor Living Project Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect project information from customers or clients about their outdoor living projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your project details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Enter your customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Describe the project requirements in the "Project Requirements" field.
4. Outline the project scope in the "Project Scope" field.
5. Provide customer contact information in the "Customer Contact" field.
6. Specify the project location in the "Project Location" field.
7. Enter the project budget in the "Project Budget" field.
8. Set the project deadline in the "Project Deadline" field.
9. Upload any relevant project files in the "Project Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`): This field is for entering your project's name, description, or any other relevant details.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant customer information.
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements`): Describe the project's requirements and specifications in this field.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`): Outline the project's goals, objectives, and scope of work.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Enter the project's location or site where the work will be done.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter the project's estimated budget or cost.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`): Set a realistic deadline for the project's completion.
* **Project Files** (`project_files`): Upload any relevant files, documents, or images related to your project.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when entering your project details and customer information.
* Attach any relevant files that will help us understand your project better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your submission and look forward to reviewing your project.
