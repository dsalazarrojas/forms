# Voice Commerce Integration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses integrate their e-commerce platforms with voice commerce features, enabling customers to use voice assistants to interact with their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of voice commerce feature you want to integrate (e.g., Voice Assistant, Voice Assistant with Display, etc.).
2. Choose the voice channels you want to integrate with (e.g., Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, etc.).
3. Select the e-commerce platforms and store fronts you want to integrate with (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.).
4. Provide additional information about your integration requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Integration Request
* **Integration Request** (`voice_commerce_integration_form`, text, not required): This is the main question to be answered, but it seems it was left out of the form. Please, add it here.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required): In this field, provide a brief description of your project, including the type of products or services you want to integrate with voice commerce features. 

### Voice Features
* **Voice Features** (`voice_commerce_features`, select_one, required): Select the type of voice features you want to integrate with your e-commerce platform. This could include Voice Assistant, Voice Assistant with Display, Voice Assistant with Display and Text-to-Speech, or Text-to-Speech Only.

### Voice Channels
* **Voice Channels** (`voice_commerce_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose the voice channels you want to integrate with, such as Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Other.

### Ecom Platforms
* **Ecom Platforms** (`ecom_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the e-commerce platforms you want to integrate with, such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce.

### Ecom Storefronts
* **Ecom Storefronts** (`ecom_storefronts`, text, not required): If you have multiple store fronts, provide the URLs or names of each one.

### Ecom Storefront IDs
* **Ecom Storefront IDs** (`ecom_storefront_ids`, text, not required): If you have multiple store fronts, provide the IDs or names of each one.

### Payment Gateways
* **Payment Gateways** (`payment_gateways`, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment gateways you want to integrate with, such as PayPal, Stripe, or Authorize.net.

### Voice Commerce Regions
* **Voice Commerce Regions** (`voice_commerce_regions`, text, not required): If you have multiple regions, provide the names or URLs of each one.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, not required): Use this field to provide any additional information about your integration requirements or concerns.

## Tips
* Please be concise and clear in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, don't hesitate to ask for help.
* We'll review your form submission and get back to you with further guidance.
