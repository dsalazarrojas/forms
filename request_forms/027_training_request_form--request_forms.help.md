# Training Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit and manage training requests. It helps gather information about the employee who needs training, the training type, location, date, time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the employee's ID number.
2.  Enter the company name.
3.  Specify the training request details in the "Training Request" field.
4.  Choose the type of training required in the "Training Type" field.
5.  Select the training location in the "Training Location" field.
6.  Enter the training date using the date format.
7.  Enter the training time in the 24-hour format.
8.  Specify the training duration in hours.
9.  Select the training status: Active, Completed, or Pending.
10.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
11.  Enter the email address of the employee or person requesting the training.
12.  Enter the phone number of the employee or person requesting the training.
13.  Select whether the training is assigned to someone or not.
14.  Add any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
15.  Select whether the request was submitted by the employee or someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): This is the ID number of the employee who needs training.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company the employee works for.
*   **Training Request** (`training_request`, text, not required): Describe the specific training or topic the employee needs.
*   **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of training required (e.g., classroom, online, on-the-job).
*   **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_multiple, not required): Select the location(s) where the training will take place.
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the training using the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the training in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, not required): Specify the duration of the training in hours.
*   **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the status of the training (Active, Completed, or Pending).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the training request.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the employee or person requesting the training.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the employee or person requesting the training.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, not required): Select whether the training is assigned to someone or not (Yes, No).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or information about the training request.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, not required): Select whether the request was submitted by the employee or someone else (Yes, No).
