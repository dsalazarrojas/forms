# Supply Forecast Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for forecasting purposes, allowing them to provide a contact form to get in touch with the supply team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" field.
2. Select your location from the "Location" dropdown list.
3. Choose the type of forecast you are requesting (Supply or Demand).
4. Choose the type of forecast you are requesting (Supply or Demand).
5. Select your contact method (1 or 2).
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Provide any additional information about your request in the "Additional Information" field.
9. Select the date and time you would like to submit your forecast request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact (form_1)**: Enter your name and/or title to contact you.
* **Location (form_2)**: Select your location from the options provided. This information will help the supply team to better understand your needs.
* **Request Type (form_3)**: Select the type of forecast you are requesting. You can choose between 'Supply' and 'Demand'.
* **Supply/Demand (form_4)**: Confirm your request type selection.
* **Contact Information (form_5)**: Select your preferred contact method. Please note that option 2 is not further explained, so assume it's an alternate contact method.
* **Phone Number (form_6)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email (form_7)**: Enter your email address so we can respond to you.
* **Additional Information (form_8)**: Provide any additional information about your request that would be helpful for the supply team to understand your needs.
* **Submission Date (form_9)**: Select the date when you would like to submit your forecast request.
* **Submission Time (form_10)**: Select the time when you would like to submit your forecast request.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your forecast request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact our support team.
* You can always reach out to us if you need further assistance.
