# Name Change Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Name Change Request Form is designed to collect information for a name change request. Please fill out this form with your correct and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your new name as you would like it to appear on your records.
2.  Enter your current address.
3.  Enter your city of current residence.
4.  Enter your state of current residence (US, CA, or NY).
5.  Enter your country of current residence.
6.  Enter your date of birth.
7.  Select your state of current residence (US, CA, or NY).
8.  Select your new state of residence (US, CA, or NY).
9.  Enter your city of current residence.
10. Enter your city of new residence.
11. Enter your reason for requesting a name change.
12. Sign the form to confirm your identity.
13. Provide your name and contact information if you are requesting on behalf of another person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name Change Request** (`name_change_request`): Enter the reason why you are requesting a name change. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your records.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your current address where you can be reached.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city of current residence.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state of current residence (US, CA, or NY).
* **Country** (`country`): Enter your country of current residence.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Current Resident State** (`current_resident_state`): Select your state of current residence (US, CA, or NY).
* **New Resident State** (`new_resident_state`): Select your new state of residence (US, CA, or NY).
* **Current Resident City** (`current_resident_city`): Enter your city of current residence.
* **New Resident City** (`new_resident_city`): Enter your city of new residence.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting a name change.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date this form to confirm your identity.
* **Affiant** (`affiant`): If you are requesting on behalf of another person, please enter their name.
* **Affiant Phone** (`affiant_phone`): If you are requesting on behalf of another person, please enter their phone number.
* **Affiant Email** (`affiant_email`): If you are requesting on behalf of another person, please enter their email address.
