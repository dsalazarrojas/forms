# Pet Cremation Service Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Cremation Service Request form is designed to collect information for the pet cremation service request. This form helps pet owners or their representatives provide necessary details about the deceased pet and the requested service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your pet's name and type in the corresponding fields.
3. Check or select the correct options for cremation date and location.
4. Enter your contact information to facilitate communication.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Cremation Request** (`cremation_request`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the pet cremation service request.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the pet that requires cremation services.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are the pet owner.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the weight of the pet (not applicable if 'No' is selected for 'Pet Type').
* **Owner Contact Info** (`owner_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the pet owner, such as phone number or email.
* **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the pet's veterinarian (if applicable).
* **Cremation Date** (`cremation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a specific date for the cremation service.
* **Cremation Location** (`cremation_location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose a location for the cremation service (e.g., 'Yes' for a specific location, 'No' for no preferred location).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the cremation service.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the cremation service.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the cremation service.

## Tips
* Double-check the cremation date to ensure it aligns with the available schedule.
* Be thorough when providing contact information for efficient communication.
* Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.
