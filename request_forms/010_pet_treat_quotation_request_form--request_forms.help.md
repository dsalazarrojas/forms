# Pet Treat Quotation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners who want to request a quote for pet treat services. By filling out this form, pet owners can provide us with the necessary details to provide an accurate quote for their pet's needs. Please note that this form is for internal use only and not intended for external users. If you are not a pet owner, please do not fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, or Other).
2. Choose the quote frequency you require (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
3. Indicate if you need a one-off or ongoing quote.
4. Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your pet's needs.
5. Fill in your contact information (name, phone number, and email).
6. Choose whether you require a signature on the quote or not.
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Treat Request**: This is where you can select the type of pet you have. Please choose one of the options provided (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Information**: Please provide detailed information about your pet, including breed, color, weight, age, and size.
* **Contact Name**: This is where you can enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Message**: Use this field to provide any additional information about your pet's needs or preferences.
* **Quote Request**: Select the type of quote you require (Quote 1, Quote 2, or Quote 3).
* **Date Request**: Indicate the date you require the quote to be ready by.
* **Time Request**: Indicate the time you require the quote to be ready by.
* **Follow-up**: If you have any further information or instructions for us, please include it in this field.
* **Pet Type**: Repeat the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Breed**: Provide a detailed description of your pet's breed.
* **Pet Color**: Enter your pet's color.
* **Pet Weight**: Enter your pet's weight (in kg or lbs).
* **Pet Age**: Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Size**: Enter your pet's size (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Pet Health**: Provide any health information about your pet.
* **Quote Frequency**: Select the frequency you require the quote (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Quote Type**: Indicate if you require a one-off or ongoing quote.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about your pet's needs or preferences.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Submitter Signature**: Indicate if you require a signature on the quote or not.

Please note that this form is for internal use only, and all fields are optional, except for fields that are marked as required.
