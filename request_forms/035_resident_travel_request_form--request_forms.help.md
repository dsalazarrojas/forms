# Resident Travel Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for residents to request travel information for official purposes, including hotel preferences, flight preferences, travel dates, and travel status. The form will help our travel agents to process and arrange travel arrangements efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select relevant options for hotel, flight, travel dates, and travel status.
3. Ensure that you have attached all required documents and supporting materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_request** (text)
	+ A brief description of your travel request.
* **hotel_preferences** (select multiple)
	+ Select the preferred hotel options for your travel. Please choose from the available options.
* **flight_preferences** (select multiple)
	+ Select the preferred flight options for your travel. Please choose from the available options.
* **travel_dates** (date)
	+ Enter the travel dates, including start and end dates.
* **arrival_airport** (select one)
	+ Select the arrival airport for your travel.
* **arrival_time** (time)
	+ Enter the estimated arrival time.
* **departure_airport** (select one)
	+ Select the departure airport for your travel.
* **departure_time** (time)
	+ Enter the estimated departure time.
* **travel_agent** (note)
	+ If applicable, enter any relevant travel agent information.
* **comments** (note)
	+ Enter any additional comments or remarks about your travel request.
* **travel_purpose** (select one)
	+ Select the purpose of your travel.
* **travel_budget** (number)
	+ Enter your estimated travel budget.
* **travel_duration** (number)
	+ Enter the duration of your travel.
* **travel_frequency** (select multiple)
	+ Select the travel frequency options for your trip.
* **travel_group** (select one)
	+ Select the travel group options for your trip.
* **travel_type** (select multiple)
	+ Select the travel type options for your trip.
* **travel_location** (note)
	+ If applicable, enter the travel location information.
* **travel_notes** (note)
	+ Enter any additional travel notes or remarks.
* **travel_documents** (select multiple)
	+ Select any travel documents required.
* **travel_status** (select one)
	+ Select the travel status.
* **travel_date** (date)
	+ Enter the travel date.
* **travel_start_date** (date)
	+ Enter the start date of your travel.
* **travel_end_date** (date)
	+ Enter the end date of your travel.
* **travel_duration_days** (number)
	+ Enter the duration of your travel in days.
* **travel_frequency_days** (select multiple)
	+ Select the travel frequency options in days.
* **travel_group** (select one)
	+ Select the travel group options for your trip.
* **travel_type** (select multiple)
	+ Select the travel type options for your trip.

Note that all fields are optional and can be filled out based on your specific travel needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our travel agents for assistance.
