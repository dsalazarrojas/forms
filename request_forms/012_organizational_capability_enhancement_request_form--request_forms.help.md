# Organizational Capability Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect requests for process improvements, tools, or other capabilities from employees. This form will help to evaluate and prioritize these requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, fill out the form with the necessary information. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to your request. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
* **Form Header** (`Form Header`, note, optional): This is the header section of the form, which is informational only and not required.

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.

### Employee Email
* **Employee Email** (`Employee Email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.

### Employee Phone
* **Employee Phone** (`Employee Phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.

### Process Improvement
* **Process Improvement** (`Process Improvement`, text, optional): Describe the specific process or improvement you are requesting.

### Tool Request
* **Tool Request** (`Tool Request`, text, optional): If you are requesting a new tool, describe the tool and its proposed use.

### Capability Request
* **Capability Request** (`Capability Request`, text, optional): Describe the capability you are requesting, such as a new software or hardware.

### Priority Level
* **Priority Level** (`Priority Level`, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of your request, which is High, Medium, or Low.

### Description
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Describe the improvement request in detail, including any additional context or information.

### Reviewers
* **Reviewers** (`Reviewers`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reviewers you would like to be involved in the evaluation of your request.

### Reviewers Additional Info
* **Reviewers Additional Info** (`Reviewers Additional Info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for the reviewers, such as their role in the evaluation process.

### Date Requested
* **Date Requested** (`Date Requested`, date, optional): If you are requesting a specific time frame for the completion of your request, enter the date.

### Time Requested
* **Time Requested** (`Time Requested`, time, optional): If you are requesting a specific time frame for the completion of your request, enter the time.

### Status
* **Status** (`Status`, select_one, optional): The status of your request can be In Progress, Approved, or Denied.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or context to your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and clear.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the IT department for clarification.
* Review and proofread your request before submitting it to ensure it is complete and correct.
