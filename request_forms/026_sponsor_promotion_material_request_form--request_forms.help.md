# Sponsor Promotion Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Promotion Material Request Form is a form used to request promotion materials from sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. First, select the type of material you are requesting (Sponsor Material, Promotion, or Other).
2. Provide more details about your request in the Material Type 1, 2, and 3 fields.
3. If you are requesting materials, specify their quantity in the Material Quantity 1, 2, and 3 fields.
4. Choose a color for your material in the Material Color 1 field.
5. Finally, select the relevant reports for your request in the Report 1, 2, and 3 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Material 1** (Select Multiple, required): Select all applicable sponsor materials you are requesting.
* **Material Type 1** (Text, not required): Describe the type of material you are requesting.
* **Material Type 2** (Text, not required): Describe the type of material you are requesting (if different from Material Type 1).
* **Material Type 3** (Text, not required): Describe the type of material you are requesting (if different from Material Type 1 and 2).
* **Material Quantity 1** (Number, not required): Specify the quantity of the material you are requesting.
* **Material Quantity 2** (Number, not required): Specify the quantity of the material you are requesting (if different from Material Quantity 1).
* **Material Quantity 3** (Number, not required): Specify the quantity of the material you are requesting (if different from Material Quantity 1 and 2).
* **Material Color 1** (Select One, not required): Choose a color for your material.
* **Report 1** (Date, not required): Select the relevant report for your request.
* **Report 2** (Date, not required): Select the relevant report for your request (if different from Report 1).
* **Report 3** (Date, not required): Select the relevant report for your request (if different from Report 1 and 2).

## Tips
- Make sure to select all applicable sponsor materials for accurate categorization.
- Be specific when describing the material type and quantity.
- Choose a color for your material to ensure proper identification.
- Select the relevant reports to ensure accurate reporting.
