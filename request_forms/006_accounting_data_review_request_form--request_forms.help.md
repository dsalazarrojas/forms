# Accounting Data Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for accounting data reviews in a clear and concise manner. It helps the review team to understand the scope and urgency of the review, ensuring that all requests are thoroughly understood and managed efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

* Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Fill out the form completely, as incomplete requests may delay the review process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all details are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Please enter your name and title, e.g., John Doe, Finance Manager.
* **Requester email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication about the review.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department involved in the request, e.g., Accounting, Finance, or Human Resources.
* **Review type** (`review_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of review required, e.g., Transaction audit, Reconciliation, Compliance review, or Financial statement review.
* **Accounting period for review** (`accounting_period`, text, required): Enter the accounting period to be reviewed, e.g., Q1 2026 or Jan 2026 to Mar 2026.
* **Data sources to be reviewed** (`data_sources`, text, required): List the systems or file names containing the data to be reviewed.
* **Priority level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency of the request, e.g., Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Expected completion date** (`expected_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the target date for completing the review, if applicable.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or notes to support the request.
* **Confidentiality requirements** (`confidentiality_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any special handling instructions for confidential data.
* **Approval required from** (`approval_required_from`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the approver or manager.
* **Additional instructions** (`additional_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for the review team to know.

By carefully completing the form, you will ensure that your accounting data review request is understood and managed effectively, allowing the review team to provide accurate and efficient service.
