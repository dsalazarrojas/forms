# Business Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Expense Reimbursement Form is used by employees to submit a request for reimbursement for business-related expenses incurred during a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out your employee information, including your name and department.
2.  Enter the date the form is being submitted and the start and end dates of the expense period.
3.  Specify the total amount being requested for reimbursement.
4.  Provide a detailed breakdown of expenses, including travel, lodging, meal, entertainment, office supplies, and other expenses.
5.  Indicate the status of receipts, and if necessary, provide a reason for missing receipts.
6.  Explain the business purpose and benefit of the expenses.
7.  Certify that all expenses are accurate and business-related.
8.  Choose your preferred method of reimbursement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID.
*   **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, optional): Enter your employee ID if you want to reference it for reimbursement processing.
*   **Department** (Department, text, required): Indicate the department you belong to for accurate expense classification and tracking.
*   **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor for approval and verification.
*   **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Enter the date the form is being submitted for processing.
*   **Expense Period Start** (Expense Period Start, date, required): Specify the start date of the expense period for accurate tracking and approval.
*   **Expense Period End** (Expense Period End, date, required): Enter the end date of the expense period for accurate expense tracking and reporting.
*   **Total Reimbursement Amount** (Total Reimbursement Amount, number, required): Specify the total amount being requested for reimbursement.
*   **Travel Expenses** (Travel Expenses, number, optional): Break down travel-related expenses, such as flights and transportation.
*   **Lodging Expenses** (Lodging Expenses, number, optional): Enter lodging and accommodation-related expenses.
*   **Meal Expenses** (Meal Expenses, number, optional): Specify meal and beverage expenses.
*   **Entertainment Expenses** (Entertainment Expenses, number, optional): Enter expenses related to client entertainment and events.
*   **Office Expenses** (Office Expenses, number, optional): Break down office supplies and equipment expenses.
*   **Other Expenses** (Other Expenses, number, optional): Include any other business-related expenses not specified above.
*   **Receipt Status** (Receipt Status, select_one, required): Indicate the status of receipts, including whether you have all, most, some, or no receipts.
*   **Reason for Missing Receipts** (Reason for Missing Receipts, text, optional): Provide a reason for missing receipts if you selected "No receipts" or "Some receipts missing".
*   **Business Purpose** (Business Purpose, text, required): Explain the business purpose of the expenses, including why they were incurred.
*   **Business Benefit** (Business Benefit, text, optional): Explain how the expenses benefited the company.
*   **Certification** (Certification, select_one, required): Certify that all expenses are accurate and business-related.
*   **Reimbursement Method** (Reimbursement Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of reimbursement, including direct deposit, check, or corporate card credit.
