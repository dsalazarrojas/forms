# Residential Tenancy Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Tenancy Reference Request Form is a document used to request and collect information from current or past tenants about their rental history and past experiences with the property management company.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form completely and accurately. The form is organized into separate sections for each piece of information that we need from you. Here is a step-by-step guide to completing this form:

1. **Provide Your Basic Information**: Fill out the fields for Tenant Name, Address, and Move In/Out Dates.
2. **Choose Your Type of Dwelling**: Select the type of dwelling you occupied during your tenancy.
3. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide the name, phone number, and email of the person who can vouch for you as a referee.
4. **Select Past Violations (If Applicable)**: If you have had any past issues with your rental, such as non-payment of rent or damage to property, please select all that apply.
5. **Add Additional Details (If Applicable)**: If you selected "Other" under Past Violations, please provide a brief description of the incident.
6. **Reason for Move Out**: If you are no longer a tenant, please provide a brief explanation for your departure from the rental property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information**:
	+ Enter your name as the tenant who occupied the rental property during the specified dates.
* **Address**:
	+ Select the type of dwelling you occupied during your tenancy. Options are Apartment, House, Condo, and Townhouse.
* **Tenant Referee Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the person who can vouch for your tenancy.
* **Tenant Referee Phone**:
	+ Enter the contact phone number of your referee.
* **Tenant Referee Email**:
	+ Enter the email address of your referee.
* **Past Violations**:
	+ Select all past issues that you had with your rental, such as non-payment of rent or damage to property. If you have had "Other" issues, please select that option and provide additional details in the next field.
* **Other Past Violations Details**:
	+ If you selected "Other" under Past Violations, provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Move In Date**:
	+ Enter the date you moved into the rental property.
* **Move Out Date**:
	+ Enter the date you moved out of the rental property.
* **Reason for Move Out**:
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your departure from the rental property.

## Tips
* Please have the tenant's consent and permission to access their past rental history before submitting this form.
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the property management staff for assistance.
