# Quick Drive Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Drive Setup Form is a form used to collect essential information from the drive requester. This form is necessary for us to process and execute the drive request effectively. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth drive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Drive Details section:
	* Provide the drive details, including the type of drive, duration, and date.
2. Choose the Scheduling Details and Drive Type options:
	* Select the scheduling details and drive type that suits your needs.
3. Enter the Pickup and Drop Off details:
	* Provide the pickup location, drop off location, and any relevant notes.
4. Enter the Drive Requester information:
	* Fill out the drive requester's contact information, including phone number, email, name, and organization.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drive Details (1)** (`drive_details`, required, text): Enter the details of the drive, including the type, duration, and date.
* **Scheduling Details (2)** (`scheduling_details`, required, select_one): Select the scheduling details for the drive.
* **Pickup Details (3)** (`pickup_details`, required, text): Enter the pickup details, including any relevant notes.
* **Drop Off Details (4)** (`drop_off_details`, required, text): Enter the drop off details, including any relevant notes.
* **Pickup Location (5)** (`pickup_location`, required, text): Enter the location where the drive will start.
* **Drop Off Location (6)** (`drop_off_location`, required, text): Enter the location where the drive will end.
* **Drive Duration (7)** (`drive_duration`, required, text): Enter the duration of the drive.
* **Drive Date (8)** (`drive_date`, required, date): Enter the date of the drive.
* **Drive Time (9)** (`drive_time`, required, time): Enter the time of the drive.
* **Drive Type (10)** (`drive_type`, required, text): Enter the type of drive.
* **Notes (11)** (`notes`, optional, note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Phone Number (12)** (`phone_number`, optional, text): Enter the phone number of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester (13)** (`drive_requester`, required, email): Enter the email of the drive requester.
* **Drive Type Option 1 (14)** (`drive_type_option_1`, required, select_one): Select the drive type option 1.
* **Drive Type Option 2 (15)** (`drive_type_option_2`, required, select_multiple): Select the drive type option 2.
* **Drive Type Option 3 (16)** (`drive_type_option_3`, required, select_one): Select the drive type option 3.
* **Drive Requester Phone (17)** (`drive_requester_phone`, optional, text): Enter the phone number of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester Email (18)** (`drive_requester_email`, required, email): Enter the email of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester Name (19)** (`drive_requester_name`, optional, text): Enter the name of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester Organization (20)** (`drive_requester_organization`, optional, text): Enter the organization of the drive requester.
* **Scheduling Details Option 1 (21)** (`scheduling_details_option_1`, required, select_one): Select the scheduling details option 1.
* **Scheduling Details Option 2 (22)** (`scheduling_details_option_2`, required, select_one): Select the scheduling details option 2.
* **Drive Requester Address (23)** (`drive_requester_address`, optional, text): Enter the address of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester City (24)** (`drive_requester_city`, optional, text): Enter the city of the drive requester.
* **Drive Requester State (25)** (`drive_requester_state`, optional, text): Enter the state of the drive requester.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with anyone outside of our organization.
