# lease_data_transfer_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of transferring lease data. It gathers information about the lessee, contact methods, transfer details, and status updates to track and record lease transfers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the `User Info` field.
2. Select at least one contact method from the `Contact Methods` field.
3. Choose the transfer details from the `Lease Details` field.
4. Provide the date of transfer in the `Date of Transfer` field.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the transfer period in the `Start Date` and `End Date` fields, respectively.
6. Select the transfer type from the `Transfer Type` field.
7. Select the contact method and transfer method from their respective fields.
8. Enter the transfer reason and status updates as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, Required/Optional): Enter your user information.
* **Contact Methods** (`contact_methods`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select at least one contact method to reach you for further information.
* **Lease Details** (`lease_details`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Choose the lease details.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date of transfer.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the start date of the transfer period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, Required/Optional): Enter the end date of the transfer period.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the transfer type.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Select the contact method.
* **Transfer Method** (`transfer_method`, `select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the transfer method.
* **Transfer Reason** (`transfer_reason`, `text`, Required): Enter the transfer reason.
* **Transfer Note** (`transfer_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional transfer note.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any contact note.
* **Lease Note** (`lease_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any lease note.
