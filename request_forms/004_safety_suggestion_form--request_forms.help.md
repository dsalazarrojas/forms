# Safety Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Safety Suggestion Form is designed to collect suggestions and ideas from employees to improve workplace safety. This form is meant to be a tool for employees to provide feedback and recommendations on how to prevent accidents, injuries, and illnesses in the workplace. By using this form, employees can submit their ideas and concerns, and management can review and act upon them to create a safer work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
2.  In the `prophets_suggestion` field, provide a brief description of your safety suggestion or concern.
3.  If you have more details or information about your suggestion, you can add them in the `more_details` field.
4.  If you have a proposed solution for the issue, you can enter it in the `proposed_solution` field.
5.  Optionally, you can add any additional comments you'd like to share in the `employee_comments` field.
6.  Your manager or supervisor can review and add their comments in the `manager_comments` field.
7.  If necessary, the safety officer can add any further comments or feedback in the `safety_officer_comments` field.
8.  Finally, the `status` field is used to track the progress of your safety suggestion, where you can choose from "Approved", "Rejected", or "In Progress".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **prophets_suggestion** (text, required): Provide a brief description of your safety suggestion or concern.
* **more_details** (text, optional): If you have more details or information about your suggestion, add them here.
* **proposed_solution** (text, optional): If you have a proposed solution for the issue, enter it here.
* **employee_comments** (text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to express.
* **manager_comments** (text, optional): Your manager or supervisor can add comments or feedback here.
* **safety_officer_comments** (text, optional): If necessary, the safety officer can add comments or feedback here.
* **status** (select one, optional): Choose from "Approved", "Rejected", or "In Progress" to track the progress of your safety suggestion.
* **date_received** (date, optional): This field is automatically populated with the date your suggestion was submitted.
