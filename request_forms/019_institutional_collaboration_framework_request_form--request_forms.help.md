# Institutional Collaboration Framework Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Institutional Collaboration Framework Request Form is used to capture information for institutional collaboration requests involving external partners. This form helps facilitate the collaboration process by providing a structured way to gather essential details about the collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields to fill out. Please complete each section thoroughly and accurately to ensure a successful submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institutional Collaboration Framework Request Form** (`form_title`, text, false): This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief and descriptive title for your collaboration request.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, false): Describe the main objective of your collaboration request. This should be a concise summary of the purpose and goals of the collaboration.
* **Scope** (`scope`, text, false): Outline the scope of the collaboration, including any specific areas of focus or responsibilities.
* **Timelines** (`timelines`, date, false): Provide a start and end date for the collaboration, if applicable.
* **Collaborators** (`collaborators`, select_multiple, false): Select any collaborators who will be involved in the project, such as Partner 1, Partner 2, or Partner 3.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the partner with whom you are collaborating.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, false): Enter the email address of the partner with whom you are collaborating.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of the partner with whom you are collaborating.
* **Partner Institution** (`partner_institution`, text, false): Enter the name of the partner institution with whom you are collaborating.
* **Partner Department** (`partner_department`, text, false): Enter the name of the partner department with whom you are collaborating.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field to ensure a successful submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant team lead.
- Submitting this form does not guarantee approval, but it is a necessary step in the collaboration process.
