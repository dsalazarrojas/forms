# Animal Medical Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide medical support for animals that require immediate attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the facility or organization where the animal is currently being kept.
2. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the animal's care.
3. Choose the urgency level of the medical issue:
  	* Critical - Life Threatening: The animal is in a life-threatening situation.
	* Urgent - Needs immediate attention: The animal needs immediate attention, but it is not life-threatening.
	* Non-Urgent - Regular support: The animal's medical issue is not urgent.
	* Scheduled Maintenance: The animal is scheduled for maintenance.
4. Enter a brief description of the animal's species and breed.
5. Summarize the current medical condition of the animal.
6. Select all the specific medical needs the animal requires:
	* Emergency Surgery
	* Routine Surgery
	* Advanced Diagnostics - MRI/CT
	* Long-term Medication
	* Wound Care
	* Physical Therapy or Rehab
7. Enter the estimated total cost of care for the animal.
8. Enter the funding amount requested.
9. Check if any supporting medical records have been attached or sent via separate email.
10. Enter your phone number for immediate follow-up if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility or Organization Name**: Enter the name of the facility or organization where the animal is currently being kept.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the animal's care.
* **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level of the medical issue.
* **Animal Species and Breed**: Enter a brief description of the animal's species and breed.
* **Current Condition Summary**: Summarize the current medical condition of the animal.
* **Specific Medical Needs**: Select all the specific medical needs the animal requires.
* **Estimated Total Cost of Care**: Enter the estimated total cost of care for the animal in USD.
* **Funding Amount Requested**: Enter the funding amount requested.
* **Have Supporting Medical Records Been Provided?**: Check if any supporting medical records have been attached or sent via separate email.
* **Requester Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for immediate follow-up if needed. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information is accurate and complete to ensure the best possible care for the animal.
* If any supporting medical records are attached, ensure they are up-to-date and relevant to the animal's condition.
* If the animal's condition changes, update the form accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team for assistance.
