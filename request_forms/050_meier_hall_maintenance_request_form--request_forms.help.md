# Meier Hall Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by Meier Hall staff to submit maintenance requests for various types of work, such as repairs, replacements, and upgrades, to be reviewed and addressed by the Facilities Management team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the room number where the maintenance work is needed.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue or problem that needs to be addressed.
3. Select the type of maintenance work required (Repair, Replace, or Upgrade).
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so that the Facilities Management team can reach out to you for further discussion.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments or notes about the issue.
6. Choose who from the list to assign to handle the maintenance request (Chatjimmy or Other).
7. Optionally, select a date and time for the work to be done.
8. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Room Number**: This is the number of the room where the maintenance work is needed.
* **Description**: This is where you can provide a brief description of the issue or problem that needs to be addressed.
* **Maintenance Type**: Select one or more of the following options: Repair, Replace, or Upgrade.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email and phone number so that the Facilities Management team can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the issue, if necessary.
* **Assign to**: Choose who from the list to handle the maintenance request (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Date**: Enter the date when the work needs to be done, if you are scheduling it in advance.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the work needs to be done, if you are scheduling it.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the Facilities Management team can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the Facilities Management team can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: This field is currently labeled as "Phone", but please note that it is actually meant to be a field to enter your phone number.
