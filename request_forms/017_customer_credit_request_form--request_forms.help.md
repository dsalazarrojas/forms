# Customer Credit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Credit Request Form is a tool designed to collect information from customers who are requesting credit from your company. This form helps to gather essential details that will aid in processing their credit request, ensuring that the necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2.  Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3.  If you need to attach any supporting documents or additional information, you can do so by contacting the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Credit Request Form (Note)**: This field is a brief summary of the purpose of the credit request.
* **Customer Name (Text)**: This field is the customer's full name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Customer Email (Email)**: Enter the customer's valid email address where they can be reached for further communication.
* **Customer Phone (Text)**: Provide the customer's contact phone number for any follow-up calls or verification.
* **Customer Company (Text)**: This field is for the customer's company name (if applicable).
* **Customer Address (Text)**: Enter the customer's official address where they can be reached.
* **Customer City (Text)**: This field is for the customer's city or town name.
* **Customer State (Text)**: Enter the state or province where the customer resides.
* **Customer Country (Text)**: Provide the country where the customer is from.
* **Customer Credit Request (Text)**: This field is a detailed description of the credit request, including the reason for the request.
* **Customer Reference (Text)**: This field is for any additional information or reference that the customer wishes to provide.
* **Customer Note (Note)**: This field is an optional area for the customer to include any further notes or comments about their credit request.

## Tips
*   Always fill in the form accurately and completely.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, please ask for clarification.
*   Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it.
