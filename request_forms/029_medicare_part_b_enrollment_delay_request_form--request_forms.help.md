# Medicare Part B Enrollment Delay Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a delay in Medicare Part B enrollment. If you have already applied for Medicare Part B and are seeking a delay in your enrollment start date, please proceed to fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Ensure that you complete the required fields, which include "Preferred Contact Method" (Phone, Email, or Mail). This will help us to contact you effectively and promptly process your request.
2. **Be honest and concise in your response**: Provide accurate and clear information to facilitate the processing of your request.
3. **Submit your form**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to our team for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Individual Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Reason for Delay**: Provide a clear explanation for why you are requesting a delay in your Medicare Part B enrollment start date.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail). This will ensure that we can reach you effectively.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your request.

## Tips

* Please note that this form is for individual requests only. If you are representing someone else, please ensure that you are authorized to submit on their behalf.
* Be aware that our team will review and process your request promptly, and we will contact you via your preferred contact method to discuss further if necessary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
