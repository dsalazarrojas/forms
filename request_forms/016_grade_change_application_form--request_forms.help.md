# grade-change-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a grade change for a student. A grade change application is a formal request for a modification of a student's grade in the grading system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name as it appears in the grading system.
2. Enter the course code as it appears in the grading system.
3. Enter the grade to be applied (e.g., A, B, C, etc.).
4. Enter a brief reason for the grade change.
5. Enter the date of the assignment or activity that the grade change is for.
6. Enter the original due date of the assignment or activity.
7. Enter the grade that was originally given to the student.
8. Enter the new grade that you wish to apply.
9. Enter a justification for the grade change.
10. Add your instructor signature and signature date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears in the grading system.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required): Enter the course code as it appears in the grading system.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the instructor of record for the course.
* **Grade to Give** (`grade_to_give`, number, optional): Enter the grade you wish to apply (e.g., A, B, C, etc.).
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the grade change.
* **Date of Assignment** (`date_of_assignment`, date, required): Enter the date of the assignment or activity that the grade change is for.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the original due date of the assignment or activity.
* **Grade Given** (`grade_given`, number, optional): Enter the original grade that was given to the student.
* **Grade to Change** (`grade_to_change`, number, optional): Enter the new grade you wish to apply.
* **Justification for Change** (`justification_for_change`, text, optional): Enter a detailed explanation for the grade change.
* **Signature** (`signature`, time, optional): Add your instructor signature and signature date.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form is submitted.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Use clear and concise language when providing justification for the grade change.
* Keep a record of your grade change applications for future reference.
