# Pharmaceutical Packaging Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pharmaceutical products and their packaging requirements from production or logistics teams. This data will help in the packaging design, material sourcing, and other related processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the product for which you are requesting packaging information.
2. Enter any relevant details about the product, such as its name, type, or batch number.
3. Specify the packaging requirements, including any specific packaging materials or dimensions needed.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the request.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form Information (1)** (`form_info` , text, optional)
This field is for any brief description of the product you are requesting packaging for.
### **Product Information (2)** (`product_info` , text, optional)
This field is for entering any relevant details about the product, such as its name, type, or batch number.
### **Packaging Information (3)** (`packaging_info` , text, optional)
This field is for specifying any specific packaging requirements or dimensions needed for the product.
### **Additional Notes (4)** (`notes` , text, optional)
This field is for any additional comments or information you want to provide about your packaging request.
### **Submit Information (5)** (`submit_info` , text, optional)
This field is for any final comments or information you want to provide before submitting your request.

## Tips
- Be concise in your descriptions to ensure accurate packaging information is gathered.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant production or logistics team.
- You can submit this form multiple times if you need to request packaging information for multiple products.
