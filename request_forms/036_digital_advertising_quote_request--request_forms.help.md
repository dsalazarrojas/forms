# Digital Advertising Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Advertising Quote Request form is a tool to gather information about your digital advertising campaign and provide a quote for the services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with a red asterisk (\*) to ensure accurate quote generation.
2. Be as detailed and comprehensive as possible when filling out the form.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (User Name, text, *): Your full name for contact purposes.
* **Company** (Company, text, *): Your company's name where applicable.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, *): Your contact email for future communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, *): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Budget** (Budget, number, ): Optional field for campaign budget, not required if not applicable.
* **Campaign Start Date** (Campaign Start Date, date, *): The date when your campaign is scheduled to start.
* **Campaign End Date** (Campaign End Date, date, *): The date when your campaign is scheduled to end.
* **Ad Budget Allocation** (Ad Budget Allocation, select_one, *): Choose how you want to allocate your ad budget.
* **Target Audience** (Target Audience, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting your target audience.
* **Social Media Channels** (Social Media Channels, select_one, ): Optional field for selecting social media channels for your campaign.
* **Ad Placement** (Ad Placement, select_one, ): Optional field for selecting ad placement.
* **Ad Type** (Ad Type, select_one, *): Choose the type of ad you want to run.
* **Ad Media Types** (Ad Media Types, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting ad media types.
* **Ad Frequency** (Ad Frequency, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting ad frequency.
* **Ad Budget** (Ad Budget, number, *): Your total ad budget for the campaign.
* **Ad Start Date** (Ad Start Date, date, ): Optional field for selecting ad start date.
* **Ad End Date** (Ad End Date, date, ): Optional field for selecting ad end date.
* **Total Budget** (Total Budget, number, *): Your total campaign budget.
* **Target Location** (Target Location, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting target location.
* **Target Demographics** (Target Demographics, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting target demographics.
* **Marketing Channels** (Marketing Channels, select_multiple, ): Optional field for selecting marketing channels.
* **Ad CPM** (Ad CPM, number, ): Your ad cost per thousand impressions.
* **Ad RPM** (Ad RPM, number, *): Your ad revenue per thousand impressions.
* **Ad CPC** (Ad CPC, number, *): Your ad cost per click.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, *): Any additional information about your campaign.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers to facilitate the quote generation process.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for assistance.
