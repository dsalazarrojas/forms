# Audio Engineer Assistance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Audio Engineer Assistance Request Form is a tool for users to communicate their audio-related needs to the audio team. The form is designed to gather essential details about the services required, dates and times, and equipment involved. This information helps us provide efficient and effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and the name of your department or team.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
3. Select the date and start time of the service you need.
4. Choose the location of the service (venue or room).
5. Enter the name of the event or project.
6. Select the type of assistance required (e.g., recording session, live sound mixing, etc.).
7. Specify any equipment that will be used.
8. Provide a detailed description of your requirements.
9. Indicate the priority level of your request (routine, upcoming event, critical, or emergency).
10. Upload any relevant files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name for our records.
* **Department or Team**: Enter the name of your department or team to help us identify the context of your request.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we'll use to contact you about your request.
* **Date of Service Needed**: Select the date when you need the service.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the service.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the service (optional).
* **Venue or Room Location**: Enter the location where the service will take place.
* **Event or Project Name**: Enter the name of the event or project related to your request.
* **Type of Assistance Required**: Choose the primary task you need assistance with (e.g., recording session, live sound mixing, etc.).
* **Specific Equipment to be Used**: Describe any equipment that will be used for the service.
* **Detailed Description of Request**: Please provide as much context as possible to help us understand your needs.
* **Priority Level**: Indicate the level of priority for your request (routine, upcoming event, critical, or emergency).
* **Please ensure all relevant files are sent to audio-team@company.com**: This is not a field to fill but a note to ensure that you upload any relevant files for our review.
