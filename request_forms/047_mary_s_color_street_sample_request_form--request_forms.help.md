# Mary's Color Street Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a sample from Mary's Color Street. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your sample request is fulfilled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your customer name in the `Customer name` field.
3. Enter your customer email address in the `Customer email` field.
4. Enter your customer phone number in the `Customer phone` field.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about your sample request in the `Customer note` field.
6. Choose the tool you would like to use for your sample (if applicable) from the `Assigned Tool` select box.
7. Enter the date and time you would like to receive your sample in the `Date` and `Time` fields.
8. Select the employee who will be submitting your sample request from the `Submitted by` field.
9. Enter your customer's address in the `Customer Address` field.
10. Finally, enter a unique form ID in the `Form ID` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sample request form for Marys Color Street** (`form_details`, text, not required): This field is for entering a brief description of your sample request.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Customer email** (`customer_email`, text, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Customer phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Customer note** (`customer_note`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your sample request.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter a unique ID for your sample request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to receive your sample.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to receive your sample.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the employee submitting your sample request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, not required): Choose the tool you would like to use for your sample.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter your customer's address for shipping or other relevant purposes.
