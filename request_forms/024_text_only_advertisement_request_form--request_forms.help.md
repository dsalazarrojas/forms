# Text Only Advertisement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request creation or alteration of a text-only advertisement. It collects necessary details to ensure that the advertisement accurately represents the desired message, product, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information in the corresponding fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information the user would like to provide about their request, such as specific design preferences or requirements not captured elsewhere on the form.
* **Advertisement Details** (`advertisement_details`, text, optional): Here, you can enter detailed information about the advertisement, including the product or service it's promoting, the target audience, and any specific requirements or goals it needs to achieve.
* **Advertisement Image** (`advertisement_image`, text, optional): Although the form is for text-only ads, if you have a specific image in mind or need help with image-related aspects of your ad, you can mention it here.

## Tips
Please ensure you provide all the requested information for a thorough understanding of your requirements. This will help in creating a text-only advertisement that accurately represents your desired message or product.
