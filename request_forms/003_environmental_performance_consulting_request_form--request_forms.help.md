# Environmental Performance Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users who would like to request consulting services in order to ensure that the request is properly handled and no duplication of requests occurs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked as `required`.
2. Enter your contact number and email address to ensure that we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide a clear and concise message about the services you need.
4. If relevant, add your company, department, and employee id for identification.
5. If you are requesting consulting services from a specific consultant, please fill out the consultant name and role fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Please fill out this field with your name and other necessary identifying information. This is a required field and needs to be filled out correctly.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so that we can reach you to discuss your request. This field is not required but it's highly recommended for us to contact you back.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so that we can send you updates on your request. This field is not required but it's highly recommended for us to be able to keep in touch with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so that we can reach you for further questions or updates. This field is not required but it's highly recommended for us to contact you back.
* **Message**: Clearly and concisely write down the services you need and any other information you think is relevant. This field is not required but it's recommended for us to fully understand your request.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, please fill out this field with the correct name of the company. This field is not required but it will help us to better understand your request.
* **Department**: If you are representing a company, please fill out this field with the name of the department you are from. This field is not required but it will help us to better understand your request.
* **Employee ID**: If you are representing a company, please fill out this field with your employee ID. This field is not required but it will help us to better understand your request.
* **Consultant Name**: If you are requesting services from a specific consultant, please fill out this field with their name. This field is not required but it will help us to better assign the request to the correct consultant.
* **Consultant Role**: If you are requesting services from a specific consultant, please fill out this field with their role. This field is not required but it will help us to better understand the type of services you need.
