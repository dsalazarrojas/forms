# Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your commercial space and your organization's needs, challenges, and goals for optimizing your space. The information gathered will help us better understand your space's unique requirements and provide more accurate and effective solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant sections based on your organization's type and industry.
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each section, taking care to follow the instructions and guidelines provided for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely, as these are essential for us to provide the best possible solutions.
4. For multiple-choice fields, select the options that best describe your situation.
5. Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 2, text, required): The full legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 3, select_one, required): The type of your organization (Corporation, Small Business, Non-Profit, Government Agency, Educational Institution, Healthcare Facility, Retail Business).
* **Industry** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 4, select_one, required): The primary industry sector of your organization (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Hospitality, Education, Government, Other).
* **Employee Count** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 5, select_one, required): The total number of employees in your organization (1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1000+).
* **Contact Name** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 6, text, required): The full name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 7, text, required): The job title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 8, email, required): The email address for communications.
* **Contact Phone** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 9, text, required): The direct phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Space Details** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 10, note, required): Information about your commercial space, including its purpose and any notable features.
* **Facility Type** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 11, select_one, required): The type of your commercial facility (Office Building, Warehouse/Distribution, Retail Store, Manufacturing Plant, Mixed-Use, Medical Facility, Restaurant).
* **Total Square Footage** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 12, number, required): The total area of your commercial space.
* **Number of Floors** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 13, number, required): The number of floors in your commercial space.
* **Current Occupancy** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 14, number, required): The current occupancy rate percentage of your commercial space.
* **Optimization Challenges** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 15, select_multiple, required): Select the challenges you are facing with your commercial space (Inefficient Layout, Storage Issues, Poor Traffic Flow, Underutilized Space, Accessibility Concerns, Safety Compliance, Energy Efficiency, Aesthetic Updates).
* **Primary Goals** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 16, select_multiple, required): Select the primary goals for optimizing your commercial space (Increase Capacity, Improve Workflow, Reduce Costs, Enhance Safety, Modernize Design, Improve Accessibility, Sustainability Goals).
* **Budget Range** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 17, select_one, required): The estimated budget for optimization (Under $10,000, $10,000 - $25,000, $25,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, Over $250,000).
* **Timeline Preference** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 18, select_one, required): The preferred timeline for optimization (Immediately, Within 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, Flexible).
* **Additional Requirements** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 19, text, optional): Any special requirements or considerations for optimization.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form, 20, date, optional): The preferred date for the initial consultation.

Note: Please provide as much detail as possible in each field to enable us to offer the best solutions for your commercial space needs. If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
