# RIA Registration Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RIA Registration Support Request Form is used to collect information for RIA registration support requests. This form will help us to assist you with your registration and answer any questions or concerns you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the firm name in the **Firm Name** field.
2. Enter the firm's URL in the **Firm URL** field.
3. Provide the firm's email address in the **Firm Email** field.
4. Select the status of your firm in the **Status** field.
5. Answer the question about jurisdiction in the **Jurisdiction** field.
6. Provide the type of jurisdiction in the **Jurisdiction Type** field.
7. If necessary, select a date for your registration in the **Registration Date** field.
8. Describe the support issue you are experiencing in the **Support Issue** field.
9. Enter any additional information or message in the **Request Message** field.
10. If applicable, attach any relevant files or documents to the **Request Attachment** field.
11. Provide contact information for support in the **Support Contact** field.
12. Enter the support email address in the **Support Email** field.
13. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions in the **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** field.
14. Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy statement in the **I have read and agree to the privacy statement** field.
15. Select "Yes" to confirm that you are ready to submit the form in the **Submit Form** field.
16. Enter a thank you message to the user in the **Thank you message** field.
17. Describe the next steps for the user in the **Next Steps** field.
18. Enter any firm data in the **Firm Data** field.
19. Enter any jurisdiction data in the **Jurisdiction Data** field.
20. Select a date for the registration in the **Registration Date Data** field.
21. Enter any support data in the **Support Data** field.
22. Enter the firm URL in the **Firm Url Data** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Firm Name** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of your firm.
* **Firm URL** (2, text, optional): Enter the URL of your firm.
* **Firm Email** (3, email, optional): Enter the email address of your firm.
* **Status** (5, select_one, optional): Select the status of your firm.
* **Jurisdiction** (6, select_one, optional): Answer the question about jurisdiction.
* **Jurisdiction Type** (7, text, optional): Enter the type of jurisdiction.
* **Registration Date** (8, date, optional): If necessary, select a date for your registration.
* **Registration Date** (9, date, optional): Another field for registration date, likely for confirmation.
* **Support Issue** (10, text, optional): Describe the support issue you are experiencing.
* **Request Message** (11, text, optional): Enter any additional information or message.
* **Request Attachment** (12, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Support Contact** (13, text, optional): Provide contact information for support.
* **Support Email** (14, email, optional): Enter the support email address.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (15, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy statement** (16, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy statement.
* **Submit Form** (17, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are ready to submit the form.
* **Thank you message** (18, text, optional): Enter a thank you message to the user.
* **Next Steps** (19, text, optional): Describe the next steps for the user.
* **Firm Data** (20, text, optional): Enter any firm data.
* **Jurisdiction Data** (21, text, optional): Enter any jurisdiction data.
