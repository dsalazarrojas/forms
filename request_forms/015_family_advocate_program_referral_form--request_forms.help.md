# Family Advocate Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Advocate Program Referral Form is designed to provide support to families in need by gathering essential information from the referrer and the family being referred.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following the steps above, ensuring you have all the necessary information about the individual and their family.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter the individual's first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter the individual's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the individual's email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the individual's phone number.
* **Date of birth** (5, date, optional): Enter the individual's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (6, text, optional): Enter the individual's address.
* **Family members** (7, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable family members from the options provided.
* **Advocate program** (8, select_one, required): Select the appropriate advocate program for the family from the options provided.
* **Notes** (9, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the family's situation, if necessary.
