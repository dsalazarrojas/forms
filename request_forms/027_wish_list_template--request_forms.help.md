# Wish List Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wish List Template is a form used to collect user wishes and preferences for future reference. It is meant to be a comprehensive and structured way to express your desires, needs, or goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your wish in the "Wish List Template" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your wish in the "Wish Description" field. This is an important field as it will help us understand your wish better.
3. Choose a category for your wish from the available options in the "Wish Category" field. This will help us group similar wishes together.
4. Select all relevant tags for your wish from the available options in the "Wish Tags" field. Tags help us quickly identify the context of your wish.
5. If applicable, choose the date and time for your wish in the "Wish Date" and "Wish Time" fields.
6. If your wish is location-specific, enter the location in the "Wish Location" field.
7. Choose the status of your wish from the available options in the "Wish Status" field. This will help us understand the current state of your wish.
8. Assign a priority level to your wish from 1-5 in the "Wish Priority" field.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about your wish in the "Wish Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`Wish List Template`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your wish.
* **Wish Description** (`wish_description`, text, required): Describe your wish in detail.
* **Wish Category** (`wish_category`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your wish from the available options.
* **Wish Tags** (`wish_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant tags for your wish.
* **Wish Date** (`wish_date`, date, required): Enter the date for your wish.
* **Wish Time** (`wish_time`, time, optional): If applicable, choose the time for your wish.
* **Wish Location** (`wish_location`, text, optional): Enter the location for your wish.
* **Wish Status** (`wish_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your wish from the available options.
* **Wish Priority** (`wish_priority`, number, required): Assign a priority level to your wish from 1-5.
* **Wish Notes** (`wish_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your wish.
