# Digital Wallet Access Recovery Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for users to request recovery of their digital wallet access. It is a self-service option for customers who need to regain access to their digital wallet account due to a forgotten password or other account-related issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are submitting a valid request.
2. Fill in your user details, including your name and any other relevant information.
3. Enter your email address to verify your identity and allow us to contact you regarding your request.
4. Provide your phone number to help us contact you for further assistance.
5. Upload any supporting files or documentation required for the recovery process.
6. Submit the form to initiate the recovery process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (user_details)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us identify you.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address associated with your digital wallet account. This will help us verify your identity and contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for further assistance with your account recovery.
* **File Upload (file)**: Upload any supporting files or documentation required for the recovery process, such as a photo ID or other relevant documentation.
