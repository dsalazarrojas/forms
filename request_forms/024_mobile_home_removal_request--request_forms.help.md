# Mobile Home Removal Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who would like to request the removal of their mobile home from the system. Please note that all requests are internally reviewed before any action is taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, please ensure that you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Fill in the Site Details field with your mobile home's address or other relevant details.
3.  Provide your contact information in the Contact Information field.
4.  Specify the reasons for the removal in the Reasons for Removal field.
5.  Upload any relevant documents or signatures that support your request.
6.  Provide the contact information of the person handling the request, including their name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details** (text, required/optional): Please enter the address or any other relevant details about your mobile home.
* **Contact Information** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information so our team can get in touch with you.
* **Reasons for Removal** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the reasons that apply for the removal of your mobile home.
* **Upload Documents** (text, required/optional): Upload any supporting documents or signatures that back up your request.
* **Contact Person** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person handling the request.
* **Contact E-mail** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person handling the request.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person handling the request.
* **Upload Signature** (text, required/optional): Upload any supporting signatures.
* **Site Status** (select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of your mobile home.
* **Prophets Removal** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional details that may be relevant.

## Tips

* Please fill out all fields that apply to your request.
* Use clear and concise language when providing your contact information.
* Attach all relevant documents and signatures to support your request.
* Ensure that the person handling the request has all the necessary information.
