# Nonprofit Organization Speaker Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a speaker for a nonprofit organization's event. Please fill out the form carefully to provide the necessary information for our team to consider your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the category of the event (Nonprofit Organization Speaker Request Form, Request Forms, or Other).
2. Provide a description of the event.
3. Choose the type of event (Public, Private, or Both).
4. Enter the start and end dates of the event.
5. Enter your name as the speaker (this could be yourself, or the name of your representative).
6. Add your phone number.
7. Add your email address.
8. Provide the location of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your event. This will help us quickly understand the type of request you're making.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the event and its purpose. This will help us understand the context and relevance of your request.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Public, Private, or Both) to help us determine the scope of the request.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Speaker Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person representing you or your organization.
* **Speaker Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can easily get in touch with you.
* **Speaker Email**: Enter your email address or that of your representative.
* **Event Location**: Provide the location of the event, including the city, state, or region.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure that we have all the necessary information to consider your request.
* If you have any additional information or attachments that support your request, please attach them to this form.
* We'll respond to your request as soon as possible after receiving it.
