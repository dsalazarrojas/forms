# Twitch Unban Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who have been banned from Twitch and want to request to be unblocked. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for the moderators to review and potentially lift the ban.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by filling in the **User Information** page.
- Fill out the **Twitch Unban Request** field with a clear and concise explanation of why you want to be unblocked.
- In the **Reason** field, provide a detailed explanation of the reason for your unban request.
- If you have a Twitch Channel, you can provide it in the **Twitch Channel** field.
- If you have a Discord username, you can provide it in the **Discord Username** field.
- If you have a specific time and date for your unban request, you can provide it in the **Unban Request Date** and **Unban Request Time** fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): This page is where you can provide a brief description of yourself, including your username and a short message explaining why you are requesting to be unblocked.
* **Twitch Unban Request** (`unban_request`, `text`, required: false): This field should be used to explain why you want to be unblocked. Please provide a clear and concise explanation that includes all the relevant details.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide a detailed explanation of the reason for your unban request.
* **Twitch Channel** (`twitch_channel`, `text`, required: false): If you have a Twitch channel, you can provide it in this field.
* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, `text`, required: false): If you have a Discord username, you can provide it in this field.
* **Unban Request Date** (`unban_request_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for selecting a specific date for your unban request.
* **Unban Request Time** (`unban_request_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for selecting a specific time for your unban request.
## Tips

* Please be clear and concise in your answers.
* Make sure to provide all the relevant information.
* Be honest and respectful in your unban request.
* You can use this form multiple times, but be aware that the moderators may review all your submissions before making a decision.
* If you have any additional information or context that you feel is important to include, please provide it in the **Twitch Unban Request** field.
