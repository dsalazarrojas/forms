# Common Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for requesting assistance with a variety of topics, such as IT issues, maintenance, HR concerns, and general inquiries. It is designed to provide a structured way to capture relevant information from the requester, allowing us to better understand and address their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and contact phone number in the "Requester Information" section.
3. Select the relevant category for your request from the "Category of Request" dropdown menu.
4. Provide a brief subject or title for your request in the "Brief Subject or Title of Request" field.
5. Write a detailed description of your request in the "Detailed Description of the Request" field.
6. Choose the urgency level of your request from the "Urgency Level" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, select a deadline for your request in the "Requested Completion Date" field.
8. List any supporting documents or references in the "Description of Any Supporting Documents" field.
9. Indicate your preferred method of contact in the "Preferred Method of Contact" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the date you submitted the request in the "Date Submitted" field.
11. Add any additional comments for the reviewer in the "Any Additional Comments for the Reviewer" field.
12. Finally, confirm that you understand that your request will be reviewed and you will be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up and status updates.
* **Contact Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Your Department/Organization (Optional)**: If you are submitting on behalf of an organization, enter its name.
* **Category of Request (Required)**: Select the most relevant category for your request.
* **Brief Subject or Title of Request (Required)**: Enter a brief subject or title for your request.
* **Detailed Description of the Request (Required)**: Describe what you need help with, being as detailed as possible.
* **Urgency Level (Required)**: Select the urgency level of your request.
* **Preferred Method of Contact (Required)**: Select your preferred method of contact (e.g., phone call, email, text message, in-person visit).
* **Date Submitted (Required)**: Enter the date you submitted this request.
* **Any Additional Comments for the Reviewer (Optional)**: Add any additional comments or context that may help the reviewer understand your request.
* **I understand that my request will be reviewed and I will be contacted (Required)**: Confirm that you understand the process and that you will be contacted once your request is reviewed.

This form is designed to help us understand your needs efficiently and effectively. Please take a few minutes to fill it out carefully, and we will do our best to assist you.
