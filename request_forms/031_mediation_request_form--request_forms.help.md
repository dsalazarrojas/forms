# mediation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for mediation requests. It helps the relevant department to understand the details of your request, allowing them to process it efficiently and ensure a proper outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all the required information, including your request details.
- Select the appropriate options for mediation type, participants, and other relevant fields.
- Review and verify your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your mediation request.
* **Date Of Mediation** (`date_of_mediation`, `date`, optional): Provide the date of the mediation meeting or event.
* **Time Of Mediation** (`time_of_mediation`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the mediation meeting or event.
* **Mediation Location** (`mediation_location`, `text`, optional): Specify the location where the mediation took place or is scheduled to take place.
* **Mediation Type** (`mediation_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of mediation you are requesting, such as "Conflict Resolution" or "Facilitation".
* **Mediation Participants** (`mediation_participants`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all relevant participants involved in the mediation, such as parties or individuals.
* **Assigned Counselor** (`assigned_counselor`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the assigned counselor or mediator.
* **Requestor Details** (`requestor_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your mediation request.

## Tips
- Ensure that your request is not a duplicate.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department.
