# e_commerce_app_integration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "e_commerce_app_integration_request_form" is used to gather information from users who want to integrate their application with our system. This form helps us understand their needs and requirements to provide a smooth integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **App Details** in the "App Details" field.
2. Enter your **App ID** in the "App ID" field.
3. Provide your **Name** in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your **Domain** in the "Domain" field.
5. Describe your **Use cases** in the "Use cases" field.
6. Attach any relevant **Documentation** for your application.
7. Choose the type of **Integration type** you need (SaaS, Partner, or Custom).
8. Select the **Team** members involved in the integration (SaaS or Partner).
9. Add any additional **Comments** about your integration request.
10. Enter your **Email** for contact.
11. Enter your **Phone** number for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **App Details** (App Details, text, required: false): This field is used to describe your application's details.
* **App ID** (app_id, number, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for your application.
* **Name** (name, text, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Domain** (domain, text, required: false): Enter your application's domain name.
* **Use cases** (use_cases, text, required: false): Describe how you plan to use our system.
* **Documentation** (documentation, text, required: false): Attach any relevant documentation for your application.
* **Integration type** (integration_type, select_one, required: false): Choose between SaaS, Partner, or Custom integration types.
* **Team** (team, select_multiple, required: false): Select the team members involved in the integration (SaaS or Partner).
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about your integration request.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up.

Note: Required fields are not actually required, as per the input schema. The form is designed to be filled out once per app integration request, so it's assumed that users will fill it out correctly.
