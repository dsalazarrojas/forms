# H4h Meetings And Room Response Draft - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about an upcoming meeting, including the room and space information. It will help you gather necessary data for meeting planning and space allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the meeting in the "H4h Meetings And Room Response Draft" field.
2. Choose the meeting room from the list of options.
3. Select a meeting date using the date picker.
4. Enter the meeting time using the time picker.
5. Provide a brief response to the meeting purpose.
6. Choose the type of meeting space required (Meeting Room, Conference Room, or Breakout Room).
7. Choose the type of meeting (In Person, Virtual, or Both).
8. Finally, select the meeting purpose from the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **H4h Meetings And Room Response Draft (form_title)**: Enter a brief title for the meeting.
* **Meeting Room (form_room)**: Choose the room where the meeting will be held.
* **Meeting Date (form_meeting_date)**: Select the date of the meeting using the date picker.
* **Meeting Time (form_meeting_time)**: Enter the time of the meeting using the time picker.
* **Response (form_response)**: Provide a brief response or comment about the meeting.
* **Meeting Space (form_meeting_space)**: Enter the meeting space required (e.g., Meeting Room, Conference Room, Breakout Room).
* **Room Type (form_room_type)**: Choose the type of room required for the meeting (e.g., Meeting Room, Conference Room, Breakout Room).
* **Meeting Type (form_meeting_type)**: Choose the type of meeting (e.g., In Person, Virtual, Both).
* **Meeting Purpose (form_meeting_purpose)**: Enter a brief description of the meeting's purpose.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct meeting date and time to avoid conflicts.
* Select the correct meeting room and type to ensure availability and accessibility.
* Be brief but clear in your response to the meeting purpose field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your meeting coordinator for clarification.
