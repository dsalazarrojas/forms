# Browser Tab Renaming Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their needs and requirements for a custom browser tab renaming extension.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
- Select the appropriate option for the "Type of Request" field to ensure that your request is categorized correctly.
- For the "Feature or Enhancement Description" field, provide a detailed explanation of what you need.
- If you have any reference links or screenshots, please provide them in the "Reference Links or Screenshots" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Company or Organization**: If you are representing a company or organization, enter its name.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Type of Request**: Select one of the following options:
  - Feature Request: You are requesting a new feature for the browser tab renaming extension.
  - Bug Report: You have found a bug or issue with the current extension.
  - Enhancement: You are suggesting an improvement or optimization for the extension.
  - Integration Request: You want to integrate the extension with another product or service.
  - Other: Describe your request in the "Feature or Enhancement Description" field.
- **Feature or Enhancement Description**: Provide a detailed explanation of the feature or enhancement you are requesting.
- **Use Case or Business Need**: Explain why you need this feature or enhancement.
- **Number of Users Affected**: If your request affects multiple users, estimate the number.
- **Primary Browser**: Select the browser you are using for this extension.
- **Operating System**: Select the operating system you are using.
- **Priority Level**: Select the urgency level of your request:
  - Critical: Your request is urgent and requires immediate attention.
  - High: Your request is important and should be addressed soon.
  - Medium: Your request is somewhat important but can be addressed later.
  - Low: Your request is not urgent and can be addressed at your convenience.
- **Requested Timeline**: Select when you need this feature or enhancement:
  - Immediately: You need this feature or enhancement as soon as possible.
  - Within 1 week: You need this feature or enhancement within a week.
  - Within 1 month: You need this feature or enhancement within a month.
  - Flexible: You are flexible with the timeline.
- **Competitor Features to Match**: If you know of any competitor features that match your request, list them.
- **Reference Links or Screenshots**: Provide any reference links or screenshots that support your request.
- **Willing to Beta Test**: Select whether you are willing to beta test the new feature or enhancement:
  - True: You are willing to beta test.
  - False: You are not willing to beta test.
  - Maybe: You are undecided.
