# Return Material Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the return of materials purchased from us. Please complete this form to request return or exchange of material.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your details in the fields provided. Please fill out all relevant fields as accurately as possible.
2.  If you are not sure about any field, please leave it blank or contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Return Material Authorization Form** (`return_material_authorization_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form and is not editable.
*   **Customer** (`customer`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or name of the person requesting the return.
*   **Material** (`material`, text, required/optional): Enter the name or description of the material you are returning.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of material you are returning.
*   **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price per unit of the material.
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount you are claiming as the cost of the material. 
*   **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the cost of shipping the material back.
*   **Return Reason** (`return_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for return, e.g., "defective", "not wanted", "not as described".

## Tips
*   Please ensure the material is still under warranty or within the return period.
*   Check that this form is not already submitted for the same material to avoid duplicates.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
