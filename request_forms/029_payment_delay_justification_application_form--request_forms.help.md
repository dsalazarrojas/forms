# Payment Delay Justification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Delay Justification Application Form is a tool used to document the reason for payment delays. It helps us to identify and address the underlying causes of payment delays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Not received payment" or "Not received payment confirmation" if the delay in payment was due to not receiving the payment or its confirmation.
2. Choose the correct supporting documents relevant to your case.
3. Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Delay Justification**: Explain the reason for the payment delay in this field.
* **New Date**: Enter the date on which the payment was due or the date on which the delay occurred.
* **Reason**: Choose one of the options provided or enter a custom reason for the payment delay.
* **Supporting Documents**: Select the documents that support your application.
	+ **Supporting Document 1** to **Supporting Document 33**: These fields are for uploading additional documents that support your case. You can upload up to 33 documents.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your completed application.
