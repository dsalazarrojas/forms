# Equipment Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Repair Request Form is a form used to report issues with your equipment to the support team. This form helps us quickly gather the necessary information to diagnose and resolve your issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the Customer Name, Customer Email, and Customer Phone fields.
2. Enter the model of your equipment in the Equipment Model field.
3. Enter the serial number of your equipment in the Equipment Serial Number field.
4. Describe the problem you are experiencing with your equipment in the Equipment Problem Description field.
5. If you would like to schedule a request, select a date and time in the Request Date and Request Time fields. This is optional.
6. Finally, add any additional information you would like to provide to the support team in the Request Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (text, required)**: Please enter your name and contact information in this field.
* **Customer Email (email, required)**: Please enter your email address where the support team can contact you.
* **Customer Phone (text, optional)**: Please enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further details.
* **Equipment Model (text, required)**: Please enter the model of your equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number (text, required)**: Please enter the serial number of your equipment.
* **Equipment Problem Description (text, required)**: Please describe the problem you are experiencing with your equipment.
* **Request Date (date, optional)**: If you would like to schedule a request, select a date for the repair.
* **Request Time (time, optional)**: If you would like to schedule a request, select a time for the repair.
* **Request Message (text, optional)**: Please enter any additional information or comments you would like to include with your request.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the problem you are experiencing with your equipment.
* If you have any photos or videos that can help us diagnose the issue, please attach them to your request.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact the support team for clarification.
