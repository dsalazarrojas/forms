# Theme Park Disability Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Disability Access Request Form is designed to collect information from visitors with disabilities who require special assistance at the theme park. This form helps us to provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience for all our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below. Please note that all fields are optional, but providing more information will help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theme Park Disability Access Request Form**: This is the main title of the form. Please enter a brief description of your disability or special assistance request.
* **Theme Park Visitor Info**: Please enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you about your visit.
* **Theme Park Disability Type** (select one):
	+ Visually Impaired: If you are blind or have difficulty seeing.
	+ Physical Impaired: If you have a physical disability that affects your mobility or dexterity.
	+ Hearing Impaired: If you are deaf or have difficulty hearing.
	+ Cognitive Impaired: If you have a cognitive disability that affects your processing of information.
	+ Other: If you have a different type of disability not listed above.
* **Theme Park Disability Type Other**: If you selected "Other" above, please enter a brief description of your disability.
* **Theme Park Contact Person**: If you have a personal assistant or caregiver accompanying you, please enter their name and contact information.
* **Theme Park Date Time**: Please enter the date and time of your planned visit.
* **Theme Park Phone**: If you have a phone number where we can reach you during your visit, please enter it.
* **Theme Park Email**: If you have a preferred email address where we can contact you during your visit, please enter it.
* **Theme Park Notes**: If you have any additional information or requests about your visit, please enter it here.
* **Theme Park Comments**: If you have any feedback or suggestions about our theme park, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much information as possible about your disability and special assistance needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our Guest Services team in advance.
* We will respond to your request within 3-5 business days.
* Thank you for helping us provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all our guests!
