# Wildlife Veterinary Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a wildlife veterinary permit, which is typically required for handling, possessing, or transporting wildlife. The purpose of this form is to gather information from the permit applicant to assess their request and ensure compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the fields below to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth processing of your request.
3. Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Species**: Choose the type of species you are requesting a permit for. Please select the correct species from the options provided.
* **Species ID**: Enter the Species ID number if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Owner Name**: If you are the owner of the species, enter your name. If you are not the owner, enter the name of the owner or contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further clarification or verification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Treatment Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the treatment or procedure being requested.
* **Dosage**: If applicable, enter the dosage or amount of the treatment.
* **Treatment Details**: Provide a brief description of the treatment or procedure.
* **Submit Agreement**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms and conditions of the permit and 'No' if you do not agree.
* **Submit Name**: If you are not the owner, enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submit Email**: Enter a valid email address of the person submitting the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your local wildlife authority for clarification.
* This form is for official use only, and the information provided will be used to assess your request according to applicable regulations.
