# Courier Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement for expenses incurred during a delivery. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide delivery details on the first page, including any relevant information about the delivery.
2. Enter the time spent on the delivery, including hours and minutes.
3. If applicable, enter the gas costs incurred during the delivery.
4. Select any additional purchases made during the delivery from the list of options.
5. Enter the time card details, if applicable.
6. Add any comments about the delivery.
7. Select the user responsible for submitting the form.
8. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (delivery_details): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including the location and any other relevant details.
* **Time Spent** (time_spent): Enter the total time spent on the delivery in hours and minutes.
* **Gas Costs** (gas_costs): Enter the total gas costs incurred during the delivery.
* **Additional Purchases** (additional_purchases): Select any additional purchases made during the delivery from the list of options.
* **Time Card** (time_card): Enter the time card details, if applicable.
* **Comments** (comments): Add any comments about the delivery.
* **Assigned User** (assigned_user): Select the user responsible for submitting the form.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time): Enter the time of submission.
