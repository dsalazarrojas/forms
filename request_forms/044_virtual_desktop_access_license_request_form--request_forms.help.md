# Virtual Desktop Access License Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the virtual desktop, a virtualized environment for users to work on specific projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **user_name**, which is your name that will be displayed on the virtual desktop.
2. Enter your **job_title**, which is your job title in the company.
3. If you are requesting access for a department, please enter the name of the department you are a part of.
4. If you are requesting access for a team, please enter the name of the team you are a part of.
5. Provide a clear and concise **reason_for_request**, explaining why you need access to the virtual desktop.
6. Enter your **manager_name**, who will be responsible for reviewing and approving your request.
7. Enter your **manager_email**, which is the email address of your manager.
8. Enter your **manager_phone**, which is the phone number of your manager.
9. If you have a **supporting_document**, please attach it as evidence for your request.
10. Select the approval status from the options provided in the **approval** field.
11. Enter any additional **comments** regarding your request.
12. Select the **date** you are requesting access to the virtual desktop.
13. If needed, select the **time** you are requesting access to the virtual desktop.
14. Select the status of your request from the options provided in the **status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_name**: Please enter your name that will be displayed on the virtual desktop. This field is required.
* **job_title**: Please enter your job title in the company. This field is required.
* **department**: If you are requesting access for a department, please enter the name of the department you are a part of. This field is optional.
* **team**: If you are requesting access for a team, please enter the name of the team you are a part of. This field is optional.
* **reason_for_request**: Please provide a clear and concise explanation of why you need access to the virtual desktop. This field is required.
* **manager_name**: Please enter your manager's name who will be responsible for reviewing and approving your request. This field is optional.
* **manager_email**: Please enter your manager's email address. This field is required.
* **manager_phone**: Please enter your manager's phone number. This field is optional.
* **supporting_document**: If you have a supporting document, please attach it as evidence for your request. This field is optional.
* **approval**: Select the approval status of your request from the options provided. This field is required.
* **comments**: Please enter any additional comments regarding your request. This field is optional.
* **date**: Please select the date you are requesting access to the virtual desktop. This field is required.
* **time**: If needed, select the time you are requesting access to the virtual desktop. This field is optional.
* **manager_approval_date**: Please select the date of your manager's approval. This field is optional.
* **manager_approval_time**: If needed, select the time of your manager's approval. This field is optional.
* **manager_comments**: Please enter any comments from your manager. This field is optional.
* **user_comments**: Please enter any comments from you. This field is optional.
* **status**: Select the status of your request from the options provided. This field is required.

Note: The fields that are marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable. The fields that are marked as "required" must be filled in order to submit the form.
