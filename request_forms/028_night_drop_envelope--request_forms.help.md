# night_drop_envelope - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "night_drop_envelope" form is a tool for users to submit requests for assistance or support. It is used to collect contact information and a brief description of the issue or inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "night_drop_envelope" form link to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
3. Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive responses or updates.
* **Request** (Request, text, optional): Describe your request or issue in a few sentences.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, optional): If you have a specific tool you'd like to be associated with your request, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any attachments or additional information, you may want to attach them separately or include them in your "Request" field.
* If you are experiencing issues with the form, contact the support team for assistance.
