# SEO Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The SEO Client Questionnaire is designed to gather information from SEO clients to better understand their needs, project requirements, and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form and understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out the form completely, leaving no fields blank.
3.  Be accurate and clear in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: This field is optional, but if filled out, it provides context and helps our team understand your organization.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Email**: This field is optional, but having your email address helps us contact you efficiently.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but having your phone number helps us contact you quickly.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Website**: This field helps our team get a clear picture of your online presence.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **What are your website design needs?**: This field is where you can describe your specific design requirements, such as logo creation, content creation, or other services.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Budget**: This field is where you can indicate your budget for the project.
  * Type: number
  * Required: no
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: Choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list to indicate how you would like to be contacted.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: no
* **Contact Method**: Choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list to indicate your preferred contact method.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: no
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any other details you would like to share with our team.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that best fits your project from the available options.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: no
* **Submitted By**: This field is for recording internal purposes and does not affect your project.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Submitted Date**: This field is automatically populated and records the date the form was submitted.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no
