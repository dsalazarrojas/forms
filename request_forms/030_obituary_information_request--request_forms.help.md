# Obituary Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's obituary. Obituaries are formal announcements or public notices, typically published in newspapers, about the passing of someone, including details about their life, funeral, or death. This form requests essential information about the individual who has passed away to create an online obituary for public viewing and sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Select all family members affected by the passing.
2.  Enter the name of the deceased.
3.  Provide the date of death.
4.  Specify the place where the deceased passed away.
5.  Enter the name and contact information of the funeral home involved.
6.  Write a brief text about the deceased.
7.  Choose whether to share the obituary publicly or privately.
8.  If choosing to share publicly, enter a link to the obituary.
9.  Enter your name and email address as the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Members**: Indicate whether the family members of the deceased are affected by the passing. This will help us know how to contact the correct family members with information about the online obituary.
* **Name Of Deceased**: Enter the name of the individual who has passed away. This is a required field for creating the obituary.
* **Date Of Death**: Enter the date of death, including the day, month, and year. This will help us create an accurate obituary.
* **Place Of Death**: Provide the location where the individual passed away. This is a required field for creating the obituary.
* **Funeral Home**: Enter the name and contact information of the funeral home involved in the arrangements.
* **Obituary Text**: Write a brief, 150-200 word text about the deceased. This text will be used to create the content of the obituary. Please keep the tone respectful and informative.
* **Share Obituary**: Decide whether you want to share the obituary publicly or keep it private. If public, the link will be provided to everyone. If private, only those you specify will have access to it.
* **Share Obituary Link**: If you selected to share publicly, enter a link to the obituary on a third-party website or social media platform.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter your name, as the person submitting the form.
* **Form Submitter Email**: Enter your email address, as the contact for the submitter.
* **Submit**: Once all the required fields are filled, select "Submit" to finalize the request. If you select "Cancel", your information will be discarded, and you can start again.

Tips:
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to create a complete and correct obituary.
* If you're experiencing technical difficulties or issues, please contact our support team for assistance.
