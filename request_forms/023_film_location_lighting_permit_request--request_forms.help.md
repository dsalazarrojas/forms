# Film Location Lighting Permit Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for filmmakers, production companies, or other entities to request a lighting permit for an external location shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide an overview of your project, including the title, genre, and a brief summary.
2. Enter the details of the location(s) where the shoot will take place.
3. List the key crew members, including names, positions, and contact information.
4. Enter the contact details for the person responsible for the shoot.
5. Provide any additional permit details or specific requirements for the shoot.
6. Attach any required documents or supporting information.
7. Declare that the above information is true and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief overview of your project, including the title, genre, and a summary of the shoot.
* **Location Details** (`location_details`, text, optional): Enter the address and any other relevant details about the location(s) where the shoot will take place.
* **Crew Details** (`crew_details`, text, optional): List the key crew members, including names, positions, and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the contact details for the person responsible for the shoot.
* **Permit Details** (`permit_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional permit details or specific requirements for the shoot.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any required documents or supporting information.
* **I declare that the above information is true and correct** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign this declaration to ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form. Please wait for 5 seconds before closing this window.
* **Please wait for 5 seconds before closing this window** (`confirm`, text, optional): This message is displayed after submitting the form.
