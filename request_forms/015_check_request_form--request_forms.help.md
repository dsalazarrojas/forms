# Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Check Request Form is used for internal employees or departments to request checks for payments to various entities, such as vendors, employees, or donations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure the form is completed accurately and according to company policies.
2. Fill in the "Requesting Department Name" with the department initiating the request.
3. Enter the "Date of Request" for tracking and approval purposes.
4. For "Payee Full Name or Organization", enter the name of the recipient or vendor.
5. If applicable, provide the "Payee Tax ID (SSN/EIN)" for first-time payments to vendors.
6. Enter the "Complete Mailing Address" for payment purposes.
7. Provide the "Total Amount to be Paid" in the specified format ($).
8. Select the "Reason for Payment" from the dropdown list, indicating the payment purpose.
9. Select the "Payment Priority" from the dropdown options.
10. Confirm that funds are available in the "Funds available in budget" field.
11. Indicate if a supporting invoice is attached with "Supporting Invoice Attached".
12. Confirm that approval has been obtained with "Funds available in budget".
13. Enter the "Authorized Approver Name" and "Approver Title" for verification and approval purposes.
14. Enter the "Internal Reference Number" for internal tracking purposes (optional).
15. Add any additional notes for the Finance Department in the "Notes for Finance Department" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requesting Department Name** (Required): Enter the name of the department submitting the check request.
* **Date of Request** (Required): Enter the date the request was made for tracking and approval purposes.
* **Payee Full Name or Organization** (Required): Enter the full name or organization of the recipient or vendor.
* **Payee Tax ID (SSN/EIN)** (Optional): If the payment is for a first-time vendor, enter their tax ID for tax compliance.
* **Complete Mailing Address** (Required): Enter the complete mailing address of the recipient.
* **Total Amount to be Paid** (Required): Enter the total amount to be paid in the specified format ($).
* **Reason for Payment** (Required): Select the reason for the payment from the dropdown list.
* **Payment Priority** (Required): Select the payment priority from the dropdown options.
* **Funds available in budget** (Required): Confirm that the required funds are available in the budget.
* **Supporting Invoice Attached** (Required): Indicate if a supporting invoice is attached for verification purposes.
* **Authorized Approver Name** (Required): Enter the name of the authorized approver for the payment.
* **Approver Title** (Required): Enter the title of the authorized approver for verification purposes.
* **Internal Reference Number** (Optional): Enter an internal reference number for tracking and reference purposes.
* **Notes for Finance Department** (Optional): Enter any additional notes for the Finance Department.

Please fill out this form accurately and in accordance with company policies for a smooth and efficient check request process. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the Finance Department for clarification.
