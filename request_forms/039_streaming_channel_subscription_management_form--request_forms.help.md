# streaming_channel_subscription_management_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage subscription preferences and changes for streaming channels. It helps us understand your preferences and tailor the streaming experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields to provide your subscription preferences and changes.
2. Review and update your information as needed.
3. Submit your completed form to ensure we accurately manage your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Preferences** (`subscription_preferences`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field allows you to specify your subscription preferences for the streaming channel.
* **Plan Changes** (`plan_changes`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ Please describe any changes you would like to make to your subscription plan.
* **Notification Choices** (`notification_choices`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ Select or describe how you would like to receive notifications from our platform.
* **Subscription Changes** (`subscription_changes`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ Describe any changes you would like to make to your subscription status.

## Tips

* Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting your form.
* Use plain language to describe your preferences and changes.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask our support team for assistance.
