# Social Media Post Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Post Request Form is designed to collect information for social media posts from coworkers and partners. This form helps streamline the process of creating content for social media channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Coworker Email field with the email address of the person who requested the social media post.
2. Enter the Partner Name of the person or organization requesting the post.
3. Select the Social Media Channels where you'd like to post the content.
4. Write the Post Text that you'd like to post on the chosen channels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coworker Email** (`coworker_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who requested the social media post. This helps us keep track of who requested the post.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the post. This helps us know who the post is for.
* **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the list of available social media channels where you'd like to post the content.
	+ **Instagram**: For posts that are meant for the Instagram platform.
	+ **Facebook**: For posts that are meant for the Facebook platform.
	+ **Twitter**: For posts that are meant for the Twitter platform.
	+ **Email**: For posts that are meant for email marketing or newsletters.
	+ **Other**: For posts that are meant for other platforms not listed here.
* **Post Text** (`post_text`, text, optional): Enter the text that you'd like to post on the chosen channels.
