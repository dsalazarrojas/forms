# Vendor Product Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from vendors for product verification purposes. It collects details about the product, supplier, and other relevant information to facilitate the verification process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the product, including any relevant details or comments.
2. Enter specific details about the product, such as its name, description, and any other relevant information.
3. Fill out the supplier information, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
5. Provide any additional request information, such as any specific requirements or deadlines.
6. Enter any review information, including any feedback or comments.
7. If necessary, upload any relevant documentation or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the product. Please enter any relevant details or comments about the product.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter specific details about the product, such as its name, description, or any other relevant information.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the supplier's information, including their name, contact details, or any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your contact information, including your name, email, or any other relevant details.
* **Request Information** (`request_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional request information, such as any specific requirements or deadlines.
* **Review Information** (`review_info`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any review information, including feedback or comments.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_doc`, text, optional): This field is used to upload any relevant documentation or attachments related to the product.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is used to submit the form and complete the verification process.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your supplier or contact us for assistance.
* Make sure to attach any relevant documentation or attachments to support your response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for help.
