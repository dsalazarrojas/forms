# request_for_proposal_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to send a request for proposal (RFP) to the system. Fill out this form to submit your proposal to our team for review and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your request, such as "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement with certain terms.
2. Enter your contact information, including company name, contact person, and email address.
3. Provide details about the project, including name, description, deadline, and budget.
4. Choose your preferred payment terms and project location.
5. Upload any supporting files or documents.
6. Read and confirm the terms and conditions of the proposal.
7. Finally, submit your form to send it to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. contact_info (Select Multiple)**: Check yes or no to indicate your contact information's availability
* **2. company_name (Required)**: Enter your company name as you would like it to be displayed in the proposal
* **3. contact_person (Required)**: Enter your name as the contact person for this proposal
* **4. project_name (Required)**: Enter the name of the project you are submitting for evaluation
* **5. project_description (Required)**: Enter a brief description of the project, including any relevant details or context
* **6. project_deadline (Required)**: Enter the deadline for the project, using the date format (yyyy-mm-dd)
* **7. project_budget (Optional)**: Enter your proposed budget for the project, if applicable
* **8. payment_terms (Optional)**: Check yes or no to indicate your preferred payment terms, such as payment schedules or milestones
* **9. contact_email (Required)**: Enter your email address to be used for communication about this proposal
* **10. contact_phone (Required)**: Enter your phone number to be used for communication about this proposal
* **11. company_address (Optional)**: Enter your company address, if applicable
* **12. project_location (Optional)**: Check yes or no to indicate the location of the project, if applicable
* **13. file_upload (Optional)**: Upload any supporting files or documents, such as resumes or proposals
* **14. terms_and_conditions (Required)**: Read and confirm the terms and conditions of the proposal
* **15. submit (Required)**: Select "Submit" to send your proposal to our team for review and evaluation
* **16. back (Optional)**: Go back to the previous page for corrections or changes
* **17. cancel (Optional)**: Cancel your proposal submission
* **18. save (Optional)**: Save your form for later, but it will not be submitted
* **19. reset (Optional)**: Reset all fields to their default values
* **20. delete (Optional)**: Delete your proposal submission
* **21. confirm (Optional)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the terms and conditions
* **22. submit_form (Required)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the proposal terms
* **23. back_to_form (Optional)**: Go back to the previous page for corrections or changes
* **24. save_changes (Optional)**: Save your changes to the form
* **25. cancel_submission (Optional)**: Cancel your proposal submission
* **26. upload_file (Optional)**: Upload any supporting files or documents
* **27. save_uploaded_file (Optional)**: Save your uploaded file
* **28. delete_uploaded_file (Optional)**: Delete your uploaded file

## Tips
- Make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed correctly.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
