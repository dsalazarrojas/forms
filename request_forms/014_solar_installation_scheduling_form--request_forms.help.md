# Solar Installation Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for solar installation scheduling purposes. It helps solar energy companies to efficiently manage customer requests and installation processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure that all fields have accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Installation Details (installation_details)**: Provide information about the solar installation you are requesting, such as system size and type, location, and any specific requirements.
* **Schedule Details (schedule_details)**: Specify your preferred installation schedule, including date and time slots.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your email address and any other contact information.
* **Notes (notes)**: Leave any additional comments or requests you have for the installation process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your entries carefully before submission to avoid errors or misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the solar energy company for assistance.
