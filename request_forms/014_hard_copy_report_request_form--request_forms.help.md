# Hard Copy Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hard Copy Report Request Form is used to request a report to be sent to you in various formats. You can choose the delivery date, time, and method you prefer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose your preferred delivery date.
3. Select your preferred delivery time.
4. Choose how you would like to receive your report: by email, print, or digital copy.
5. If you have any comments or additional information, you can add it in the "Comments" field.
6. Click "Submit" to send in your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, required: no): This is a placeholder field to add a note or instructions if needed. It is not required but can be useful for adding context or reminders.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: no): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be addressed.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your email address where you would like to receive the report.
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: no): Choose the date you would like to receive the report.
* **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: no): Select the time you would like to receive the report.
* **delivery_preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_one, required: no): Choose how you would like to receive the report: by email, print, or digital copy.
* **confirm_delivery** (`confirm_delivery`, select_multiple, required: no): Confirm your chosen delivery preference(s) in this field, though you can't actually select multiple options.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required: no): If you have any additional comments or information, please add it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address for report delivery.
- Be as specific as possible when choosing your delivery date and time.
- Select the delivery method that best suits your needs.
- If you have any further requests or comments, use the "Comments" field.
