# Proposal Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for proposals, which are used to present and discuss potential ideas, projects, or plans for future initiatives or projects. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way to gather essential details about the proposal, including its title, category, summary, start and end dates, contact information, and attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the **Proposal Title**, a brief title that summarizes the proposal.
2. Choose a **Category** from the list of available options. This helps to categorize and filter proposals for easier reference.
3. Write a short **Summary** of the proposal, highlighting its key points and benefits.
4. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the proposal, if applicable.
5. Provide your **Contact Name**, which is the person to contact for further information or follow-ups.
6. Enter your **Contact Email** for communication purposes.
7. Optionally, provide a **Contact Phone** number for added contact options.
8. You can upload **Attachments 1-4** to support your proposal with additional documents or files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, text, required: false): A brief title that summarizes the proposal.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Choose a category from the list to help categorize and filter proposals.
* **Summary** (`summary`, text, required: false): Write a short summary of the proposal, highlighting its key points and benefits.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the proposal, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the proposal, if applicable.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): The name of the person to contact for further information or follow-ups.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Optionally, provide your contact phone number for added contact options.
* **Attachment 1** (`attachment_1`, text, required: false): You can upload attachment 1 to support your proposal with additional documents or files.
* **Attachment 2** (`attachment_2`, text, required: false): You can upload attachment 2 to support your proposal with additional documents or files.
* **Attachment 3** (`attachment_3`, text, required: false): You can upload attachment 3 to support your proposal with additional documents or files.
* **Attachment 4** (`attachment_4`, text, required: false): You can upload attachment 4 to support your proposal with additional documents or files.
