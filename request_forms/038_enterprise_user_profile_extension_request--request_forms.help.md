# Enterprise User Profile Extension Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an extension to an Enterprise User Profile, which may include changes to user permissions, roles, or other profile attributes. This form should only be submitted after the manager has approved the changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the approval of your manager before submitting this form.
* Check your current Enterprise User Profile to ensure you are not submitting duplicate requests.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Approval** (`Manager Approval`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the manager's approval status from the dropdown menu.
  * Choose the correct approval status from the options:
    + 1. Approved
    + 2. Rejected
    + 3. Under Review
* **User Id** (`User Id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your user ID.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address. Please ensure the email address is valid and up-to-date.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required: true): Enter your job title. This should match your current job title.
* **Manager Id** (`Manager Id`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Manager Approval Reason** (`Manager Approval Reason`, `text`, required: true): Enter a brief reason for your manager's approval.
* **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the approval status of your request:
  * Approved
  * Rejected
  * Under Review
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you are requesting the profile extension.

## Tips
* Be sure to submit this form only after receiving approval from your manager.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your administrator for assistance.
