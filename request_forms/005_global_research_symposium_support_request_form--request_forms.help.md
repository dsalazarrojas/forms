# Global Research Symposium Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Research Symposium Support Request Form is a tool used to collect and manage support requests from users for the Global Research Symposium. It helps the support team to identify and prioritize support needs, ensuring that users receive timely and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide your phone number if you have one.
3. Specify the name of the event you are requesting support for.
4. Choose the category related to your support request.
5. Write a brief description of your support need in the "Support Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, required): Choose the category related to your support request.
* **Name** (`Name`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, optional): Specify the name of the event you are requesting support for.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, optional): Choose the date of the event you are requesting support for. Note: This field is a date picker and requires a valid date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Support Request** (`Support Request`, optional): Write a brief description of your support need.
* **Description** (`Description`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your support request.
* **Required Field** (`Required Field`, optional): This field is currently not intended to be used and should be ignored.

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
