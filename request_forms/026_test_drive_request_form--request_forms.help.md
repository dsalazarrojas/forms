# Test Drive Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Test Drive Request Form is used to request a test drive on a vehicle. It is a critical step in our business process to understand potential customers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select "Yes" if you want to request a test drive or "No" if you do not.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the make of the vehicle you are interested in (Chevrolet, Ford, or Toyota).
6. Select the color of the vehicle (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Purple, Pink, Brown, Gray, Black, White, or Silver).
7. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for the year of the vehicle (i.e., whether it's a recent model).
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the mileage of the vehicle (i.e., whether it's low mileage).
10. Select where you would like to take the test drive (Dealership or Airport).
11. Choose a test date and time.
12. Add any comments or notes about your test drive experience.
13. Select the person assigned to handle your test drive request (chatjimmy, chatjimmy1, or chatjimmy2).
14. Select the date and time you completed the form.
15. Select the person who completed the form (John, Jane, or Bob).
16. Select the date and time you submitted the form.
17. Select the person who submitted the form (Bob or John).
18. Select the email of the person who submitted the form.
19. Select the status of your test drive request (In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled).
20. Add any additional comments or notes about your test drive experience.
21. Enter a unique ID for your test drive request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Drive Request** (`test_drive_request_form`, select_multiple, Required): This is the main question of the form, where you select "Yes" or "No" to request a test drive.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Optional): Enter your name for our reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you about your test drive request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Make** (`make`, select_one, Optional): Select the make of the vehicle you are interested in (Chevrolet, Ford, or Toyota).
* **Model** (`model`, select_multiple, Optional): Select whether you are interested in a vehicle with the model "Yes" or "No".
* **Color** (`color`, select_one, Optional): Select the color of the vehicle you are interested in (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Purple, Pink, Brown, Gray, Black, White, or Silver).
* **VIN** (`vin`, text, Optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number for the vehicle you are interested in.
* **Year** (`year`, select_multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are interested in a recent model.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, select_multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are interested in a vehicle with low mileage.
* **Test Location** (`test_location`, select_one, Optional): Select where you would like to take the test drive (Dealership or Airport).
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, Optional): Choose a test date for your test drive.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, Optional): Choose a test time for your test drive.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, Optional): Add any comments or notes about your test drive experience.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, Optional): Select the person assigned to handle your test drive request (chatjimmy, chatjimmy1, or chatjimmy2).
* **Form Completed On** (`form_completed_on`, date, Optional): The date and time you completed the form.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, select_one, Optional): Select the person who completed the form (John, Jane, or Bob).
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, Optional): The date and time you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, Optional): Select the person who submitted the form (Bob or John).
* **Form Submitted By Email** (`form_submitted_by_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the form.
* **Test Drive Status** (`test_drive_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the status of your test drive request (In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled).
* **Test Drive Comment** (`test_drive_comment`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your test drive experience.
* **Test Drive Notes** (`test_drive_notes`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your test drive experience.
* **Test Drive Request ID** (`test_drive_request_id`, text, Optional): Enter a unique ID for your test drive request. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when adding comments or notes.
* Our team will review your form and get back to you soon.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
