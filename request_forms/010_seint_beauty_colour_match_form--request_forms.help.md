# seint_beauty_colour_match_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "seint_beauty_colour_match_form" is a form designed to gather information from customers interested in finding a color match for an item. This form helps the sales team or customer service rep to understand the customer's preferences and needs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Form Number** if you have one.
2. Provide your **Customer Name**.
3. Enter your **Customer Email**.
4. Enter your **Customer Phone** number.
5. Enter your **Customer Address 1**, which is the street address.
6. Enter your **Customer Address 2**, which is the apartment or suite number.
7. Enter your **Customer City**.
8. Choose whether the **Customer State** is required for the color match (yes or no).
9. Choose whether the **Customer Postal Code** is required for the color match (yes or no).
10. Choose whether the **Customer Country** is required for the color match (yes or no).
11. Add any additional **Customer Notes** regarding your color match requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Number** (`form_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your unique form number if you have one. This is not required, but it will help us keep track of your submission.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Address 1** (`customer_address_1`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **Customer Address 2** (`customer_address_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your apartment or suite number, if applicable.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city you live in.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the customer state is required for the color match (yes or no).
* **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the customer postal code is required for the color match (yes or no).
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the customer country is required for the color match (yes or no).
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your color match requirements.
