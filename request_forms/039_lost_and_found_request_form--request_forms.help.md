# Lost and Found Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about lost and found items on a location. You can use this form to report a lost item or an item you found on a location. The collected information will help the location's management team or authorities to identify and locate the item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a detailed description of the lost item in the **Form Data** field.
2. Provide the name of the lost item in the **Item Name** field.
3. Specify the type of the lost item in the **Item Type** field.
4. Enter the location where the item was last seen or found in the **Location** field.
5. If you are reporting a found item, specify the tool or device used to find it in the **Assigned Tool** field.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about the lost item in the **Details** field.
7. Enter your name in the **Submitted By** field.
8. Enter your location in the **Location** field.
9. If available, enter the latitude and longitude of your location in the **Location Lat** and **Location Lon** fields.
10. Enter the date and time when you submitted this form in the **Submitted At** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the lost item in this field.
* **Lost Items** (`lost_items`, `text`, required: false): This field is likely redundant, and you can ignore it.
* **Details** (`details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information about the lost item.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed name for the lost item.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, `text`, required: false): Specify the type of the lost item.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the item was last seen or found.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If you used a tool or device to find the item, specify it here.
* **Status** (`status`, `text`, required: false): This field is likely not relevant, and you can ignore it.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the lost item.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to report the lost item.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `text`, required: false): This field is not relevant.
* **Location Lat** (`location_lat`, `text`, required: false): Enter the latitude of your location.
* **Location Lon** (`location_lon`, `text`, required: false): Enter the longitude of your location.
* **Submitted By Lat** (`submitted_by_lat`, `text`, required: false): Enter the latitude of your location when reporting the lost item.
* **Submitted By Lon** (`submitted_by_lon`, `text`, required: false): Enter the longitude of your location when reporting the lost item.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `text`, required: false): This field is not relevant.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `text`, required: false): This field is not relevant.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `text`, required: false): This field is not relevant.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date and time when you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information about the lost item.
* Use the **Form Data** field to provide a detailed description of the lost item.
* If you are reporting a found item, please mention the tool or device used to find it.
