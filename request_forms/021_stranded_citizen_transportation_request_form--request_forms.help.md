# stranded_citizen_transportation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stranded Citizen Transportation Request Form is designed to gather information from stranded citizens who require assistance. This form helps us understand the citizen's situation, provide timely and relevant aid, and track the status of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name as the citizen seeking help.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person we should contact for further assistance.
3. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
4. Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
5. Describe your location to help us pinpoint your exact location.
6. Choose the type of vehicle you are stranded with (e.g., Car, Truck, Boat).
7. Enter the number of people stranded with you.
8. Report how long you have been stranded.
9. Specify the type of help you need (e.g., Medical, Food, Water).
10. Provide any additional information that will help us better understand your situation.
11. Briefly describe the situation to help us provide the right assistance.
12. Choose the transportation method you would like to use (e.g., Airplane, Helicopter, Boat, Car, Train).
13. Enter the estimated transportation time.
14. If applicable, specify the tools or equipment we should bring along.
15. Select the status of your request (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Citizen Name** (`citizen_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the citizen seeking help.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person we should contact for further assistance.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Describe your location to help us pinpoint your exact location.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of vehicle you are stranded with (e.g., Car, Truck, Boat).
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people stranded with you.
* **Stranded Since** (`stranded_since`, `date`, optional): Report how long you have been stranded.
* **Help Type** (`help_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify the type of help you need (e.g., Medical, Food, Water).
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information that will help us better understand your situation.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about your situation.
* **Transportation Request** (`transportation_request`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the situation to help us provide the right assistance.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the transportation method you would like to use (e.g., Airplane, Helicopter, Boat, Car, Train).
* **Transportation Time** (`transportation_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the estimated transportation time.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the tools or equipment we should bring along.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your request (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Tips
* Please be as descriptive as possible when providing your information.
* If you are already receiving assistance from another agency, please do not submit this form multiple times.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the assigned personnel for clarification.
* This form is designed to gather information for the purpose of providing timely and relevant aid. Please be patient and understanding as we process your request.
