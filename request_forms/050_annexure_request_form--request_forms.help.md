# Annexure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for an annexure, which is a document that provides additional information or clarification to a main document.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of annexure you are requesting and provide additional details about its purpose.
3. Specify the request date and urgency level for your annexure.
4. Choose your preferred format and delivery method for the annexure.
5. Indicate your access level and confidentiality requirements.
6. Confirm whether your request requires special authorization.
7. If applicable, provide the name and signature of the authorizing person.
8. List any additional documents needed.
9. Specify any special instructions or requirements.
10. Choose how you would like to be notified when the annexure is ready.
11. Finally, review and agree to use the annexure according to policy and type your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, `text`, required): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Employee or Member ID** (2, `text`, optional): If you are an employee or member, please enter your ID number.
* **Email Address** (3, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (5, `text`, required): Enter the name of your department or division.
* **Position Title** (6, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Request Date** (7, `date`, required): Enter the date you are requesting the annexure.
* **Type of Annexure Requested** (8, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of annexure you are requesting from the provided options.
* **Annexure Number or Reference** (9, `text`, optional): If you know the annexure number or reference, please enter it.
* **Related Main Document** (10, `text`, required): Describe the main document this annexure relates to.
* **Document Reference Number** (11, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the document reference number.
* **Purpose of Request** (12, `select_one`, required): Choose the purpose of your request from the provided options.
* **Please Describe Purpose** (13, `text`, required): Provide additional details about the purpose of your request.
* **Urgency Level** (14, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency level for your request from the provided options.
* **Required By Date** (15, `date`, required): Enter the date by which you need the annexure.
* **Preferred Format** (16, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred format for the annexure from the provided options.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (17, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred delivery method for the annexure from the provided options.
* **Access Level Required** (18, `select_one`, required): Choose the access level required for the annexure from the provided options.
* **Confidentiality Requirements** (19, `select_one`, required): Choose the confidentiality requirements for the annexure from the provided options.
* **Does This Require Special Authorization** (20, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether your request requires special authorization.
* **Authorizing Person Name** (21, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the authorizing person.
* **Authorizing Signature** (22, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the authorizing signature.
* **Cost Center or Budget Code** (23, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the cost center or budget code.
* **Have You Requested This Annexure Before** (24, `select_one`, required): Check whether you have requested this annexure before.
* **Previous Request Date** (25, `date`, optional): If yes, enter the date of your previous request.
* **Are Additional Documents Needed** (26, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether additional documents are needed.
* **List Additional Documents** (27, `text`, optional): If yes, specify the additional documents needed.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (28, `text`, optional): Provide any special instructions or requirements.
* **How Should You Be Notified** (29, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be notified when the annexure is ready.
* **Additional Comments** (30, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **I Agree to Use This Annexure According to Policy** (31, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to use the annexure according to policy.
* **Electronic Signature** (32, `text`, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Submission Date** (33, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.

# Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
