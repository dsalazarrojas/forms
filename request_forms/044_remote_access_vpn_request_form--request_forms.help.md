# remote_access_vpn_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request remote access to the company's virtual private network (VPN). Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your information accurately.
2. Choose the correct "Vpn Type" that applies to your request.
3. Provide a clear reason for your request.
4. Add any additional comments that may be necessary for processing.
5. Choose your approver, which should ideally be your manager.
6. Make sure to include your request date.
7. You may leave the approved date and status fields empty for the moment, as these will be filled in by our IT department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User**: <text> - Enter your full name.
* **Vpn Type**: <select_one> 
  - Choose from the options: VPN, Remote, Local.
* **Reason**: <text> - Please provide a clear reason for requesting remote access.
* **Location**: <text> - Enter the location you will be accessing the VPN from.
* **Duration**: <number> - Enter the duration of your request in number of days.
* **Start Date**: <date> - Enter the start date of your request.
* **End Date**: <date> - Enter the end date of your request.
* **Comments**: <text> - Add any additional comments that may be necessary.
* **Approver**: <select_one> 
  - Choose from the options: Manager, Team Lead, IT Department.
* **Request Date**: <date> - The date your request was submitted.
* **Approved Date**: <date> - Will be filled in by our IT department when your request is approved.
* **Status**: <select_one> 
  - Choose from the options: Pending, Approved, Denied.
* **Notes**: <text> - Add any additional information you would like to include.
* **User ID**: <text> - Enter your employee ID number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting remote access.
* You will be contacted via email with instructions on how to access the VPN once your request is approved.
* If your request is denied, you will be informed via the email you provided.
