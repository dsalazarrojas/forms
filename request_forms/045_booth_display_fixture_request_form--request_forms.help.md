# Booth Display Fixture Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Booth Display Fixture Request Form is designed to be completed by exhibitors to request display fixtures for their booths at an event. This form helps the event staff to determine the type and quantity of display fixtures needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the exhibitor information section with your company name and contact details.
2. Enter the event details, including the name of the event and the date it will take place.
3. Specify the booth number and the size of your booth.
4. Choose the type of display fixtures you need, including the quantity.
5. Indicate the type of content you will display, such as products or brochures.
6. Provide information about the weight capacity of the items you will display.
7. Select the setup date for your display fixtures.
8. If necessary, specify any special requirements for your display fixtures.
9. Finally, confirm your acceptance of the rental fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Name** (`exhibitor_name`, text, required): Enter your company name or the name of the person or organization that will be exhibiting at the event.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person who will be handling contact for your booth.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person who will be handling contact for your booth.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling contact for your booth.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event where you will be displaying.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your assigned booth.
* **Booth Size** (`booth_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your booth from the available options: 10x10, 10x20, 20x20, 20x30, or Custom size.
* **Fixture Type Needed** (`fixture_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of display fixtures you need, such as Display tables, Shelving units, Hanging systems, Wall mounted displays, Product stands, Signage holders, Lighting fixtures, or Specialty fixtures.
* **Quantity Needed** (`fixture_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of display fixtures you require.
* **Type of Display Content** (`display_content`, select_one, required): Choose what type of content you will display, such as Products, Brochures, Posters, Digital displays, or Mixed media.
* **Weight Capacity Needed** (`weight_capacity`, select_one, required): Select the approximate weight capacity of the items you will display, such as Light (under 10 lbs), Medium (10-50 lbs), Heavy (50-100 lbs), or Very heavy (100+ lbs).
* **Setup Date** (`setup_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you will set up your display fixtures.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements you have for your display fixtures.
* **Accept Fixture Rental Fees** (`rental_fee_acceptance`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept the rental fees or need more pricing information.

Please review this guide to ensure you have filled out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event staff.
