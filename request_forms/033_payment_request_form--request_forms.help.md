# Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Request Form is used to submit payment requests for various purposes. This form captures relevant information about the payment amount, frequency, terms, method, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Payment Request** option from the available options.
2. Enter the **Payment Amount** if required or applicable.
3. Choose the **Payment Frequency** (one-time or recurring) if required or applicable.
4. Select the **Payment Terms** that apply to your request (payment term 1 or payment term 2).
5. Specify the **Payment Date** if required or applicable.
6. Choose the **Payment Method** (method 1 or method 2) if required or applicable.
7. Enter any additional **Payment Notes** if necessary.
8. Select the **Payment Status** (status 1, status 2, or status 3) if required or applicable.
9. Enter the **Payment Amount Due** if required or applicable.
10. Enter the **Payment Total** if required or applicable.
11. Specify the **Payment Date Due** if required or applicable.
12. Enter any additional information in the **Payment Method Other** field if required or applicable.
13. Enter any additional information in the **Payment Terms Other** field if required or applicable.
14. Enter any additional information in the **Payment Status Other** field if required or applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment request. This field is required if you have chosen the "recurring" payment frequency.
* **Payment Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the payment request (one-time or recurring).
* **Payment Terms**: Select the terms that apply to your payment request (payment term 1 or payment term 2).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date on which the payment is due.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method of payment (method 1 or method 2). This field is required if you have chosen the "one-time" payment frequency.
* **Payment Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the payment request.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment request (status 1, status 2, or status 3).
* **Payment Amount Due**: Enter the amount of the payment that is due.
* **Payment Total**: Enter the total amount of the payment request.
* **Payment Date Due**: Enter the date on which the payment is due.
* **Payment Method Other**: Enter any additional information about the payment method if required or applicable.
* **Payment Terms Other**: Enter any additional information about the payment terms if required or applicable.
* **Payment Status Other**: Enter any additional information about the payment status if required or applicable.
* **Payment Request**: Select the reason for the payment request (request 1 or request 2).
