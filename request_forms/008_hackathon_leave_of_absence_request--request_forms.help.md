# <thinking>

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate leave of absence requests for hackathons. It gathers information from team members who wish to take a leave of absence for hackathons, allowing team leads and administrators to manage and track these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to input accurate and relevant information in each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hackathon Leave Form** (`hackathon_leave_form`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Team Members** (`team`, `text`, required): List the team members participating in the hackathon.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to start your leave from.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to end your leave by.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the team lead in charge of the hackathon.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the team lead.
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Team** (`team`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which team you are a part of.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to you for the hackathon.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required): Upload any relevant files or output for your leave request.
