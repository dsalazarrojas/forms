# Spa Service Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spa Service Quote Request form is used to collect information for creating a quote for a spa service. It is divided into several sections to gather relevant details about your visit and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the services that interest you from the **Service Interests** field.
2. Choose your desired **Visit Details** (Yes/No).
3. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** in the respective text fields.
4. Choose a specific **Visit Date** and **Visit Time** from the date and time picker.
5. Select the type of **Service Type** you are interested in from the dropdown list.
6. Optionally, describe any additional **Services** you require in the text field.
7. If you plan to visit the spa, indicate **Spa Location** (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Interests** (Select Multiple):
    This field allows you to select all the services you are interested in. Choose all that apply to create a comprehensive quote.
* **Visit Details** (Select Multiple):
    Indicate if you have any special visit details (e.g., early arrival, specific equipment needs).
* **Name** (Text):
    Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the quote.
* **Email** (Email):
    Enter your email address where you would like to receive the quote.
* **Phone** (Text):
    Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further questions or to confirm the quote.
* **Visit Date** (Date):
    Choose a specific date for your visit.
* **Visit Time** (Time):
    Choose a specific time for your visit.
* **Service Type** (Select One):
    Select the type of service you are interested in (Facial, Massage, Other).
* **Services** (Text):
    If you have any additional services you require, describe them in this field.
* **Spa Location** (Select Multiple):
    Indicate if you plan to visit our spa location.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are filling this form for your own service.
* Provide accurate information to receive a correct quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for further assistance.
