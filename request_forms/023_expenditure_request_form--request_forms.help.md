# Expenditure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expenditure Request Form is a standardized form used by employees to request expenses that require approval from their manager. This form is intended to be used for legitimate business purposes and should not be used for personal expenses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Expenditure Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the expenditure type, description, and amount.
2. Select the correct expenditure type from the options provided.
3. Enter your employee's abbreviation and ID.
4. Enter your manager's abbreviation and ID.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes.
6. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expenditure Type**: Select the category of the expenditure you are requesting, such as "Office supplies" or "Travel".
* **Expenditure Description**: A brief description of the expense you are requesting.
* **Expenditure Amount**: The total amount of the expense.
* **Employee Abbreviation**: A short identifier for your employee, usually an initial followed by your last name (e.g., "JSmith").
* **Employee ID**: Your unique employee ID number.
* **Manager Abbreviation**: A short identifier for your manager, usually an initial followed by their last name (e.g., "BJohnson").
* **Manager ID**: Your manager's unique ID number.
* **Email**: Your work email address.
* **Phone**: Your work phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information you want to provide about your request.
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to send your request to your manager for approval.
* **Manager Comments**: Any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Request Form**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 18**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 20**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 21**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 22**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 23**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 24**: This field is not required and should be left blank.
* **Request Form 25**: This field is not required and should be left blank.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide all required information to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Double-check your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for clarification.
