# Video Metadata Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to update video metadata, ensuring that video content is up-to-date and easily searchable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Video Id" field and enter the ID number of the video you want to update.
2.  In the "Video Title" field, enter a brief and accurate title for the video.
3.  Choose the "Update Type" by selecting one of the available options (Update, Delete, Merge, or Other).
4.  If applicable, enter a specific date for the "Update Date".
5.  Enter a description of the update in the "Update Description" field.
6.  Choose the "Update Status" by selecting one of the available options (Draft, Published, Archived, or Unpublished).
7.  Enter the name of the person who made the update in the "Update By" field.
8.  If desired, enter the name of the person who submitted the video in the "Submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Video Metadata Update Form** (`video_metadata_update_form`, text, optional): This form serves as the main title of the form.
*   **Video Id** (`video_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the video you want to update.
*   **Video Title** (`video_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and accurate title for the video.
*   **Update Type** (`update_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to specify the type of update (Update, Delete, Merge, or Other).
*   **Update Date** (`update_date`, date, optional): Enter a specific date for the update, if applicable.
*   **Update Description** (`update_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the update.
*   **Update Status** (`update_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options to specify the status of the update (Draft, Published, Archived, or Unpublished).
*   **Update By** (`update_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who made the update.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the video.

**Tips**

*   Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate metadata updates.
*   Double-check the video ID to ensure you're updating the correct video.
*   Use specific dates for updates to maintain a clear and organized record.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or your organization's guidelines.

Please review your entries carefully before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes or duplication of updates. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out for assistance.
