# Peer To Peer Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to request payment from one another. It is a tool to facilitate internal transactions between colleagues, ensuring that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "email" field. This is a required field and will be used to verify your identity and contact information.
4. If you have a **Phone Number**, you can enter it in the "phone" field. This is not a required field.
5. Enter a **Message** to provide context for the payment request in the "message" field. This is not a required field.
6. Enter the **Amount** you are requesting to be paid in the "amount" field. This is a required field.
7. Choose the **Payment Method** you prefer by selecting from the available options: **PayPal**, **Stripe**, or **Bank Transfer**. This is a required field.
8. If you need to provide any additional **Payment Details**, you can type them in the "payment_details" field. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself to the person you are requesting payment from.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to further identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to facilitate communication and verification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to provide an additional contact option.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Enter a message to provide context for the payment request.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required): Enter the exact amount you are requesting to be paid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive the payment from the available options.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `note`, optional): If needed, enter any additional payment details or instructions. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Be respectful of your colleagues and ensure that your requests are legitimate and meet the organization's internal policies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or the relevant department for assistance.
