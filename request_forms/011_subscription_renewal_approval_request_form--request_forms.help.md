# Subscription Renewal Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect subscription renewal approval requests from users. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information for approval decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your subscription details.
2. Provide the subscription name.
3. Choose the renewal period (one-time or recurring).
4. Enter the start date of the subscription renewal.
5. Enter the end date of the subscription renewal.
6. Select the subscription type (monthly or yearly).
7. Provide a justification for the subscription renewal.
8. Add any additional notes about the subscription.
9. Choose whether the manager has approved or rejected the subscription renewal.
10. Enter any comments from the manager.
11. Choose whether the team has approved or rejected the subscription renewal.
12. Enter any comments from the team.
13. Enter the status of the subscription renewal (pending, approved, or rejected).
14. Enter the date the form was updated.
15. Enter the date the form was created.
16. Enter your name, so we know who submitted the request.
17. Select the prophet who will be responsible for the subscription renewal (Bobby, Jimi, or Jim).
18. Enter the amount of prophets associated with the subscription renewal.
19. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription details**: Enter a brief description of the subscription being renewed.
* **Subscription Name**: Enter the name of the subscription being renewed.
* **Renewal Period**: Choose whether the subscription renewal is one-time or recurring.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the subscription renewal.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the subscription renewal.
* **Subscription Type**: Select the type of subscription (monthly or yearly).
* **Justification**: Provide a brief explanation for the subscription renewal.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information about the subscription renewal.
* **Manager Approval**: Choose whether the manager has approved or rejected the subscription renewal.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Team Approvals**: Choose whether the team has approved or rejected the subscription renewal.
* **Team Comments**: Enter any comments from the team.
* **Status**: Select the status of the subscription renewal (pending, approved, or rejected).
* **Updated At**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Created At**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name, so we know who submitted the request.
* **Prophet**: Select the prophet who will be responsible for the subscription renewal.
* **Prophets Ammount**: Enter the amount of prophets associated with the subscription renewal.

This form is intended to be completed by users who want to submit a subscription renewal approval request. Please fill in all relevant fields and submit the form for review and approval.
