# School Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Material Request Form is a tool for students, parents, or school staff to request materials and resources needed for academic or administrative purposes. This form helps the school administration track and manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief title for your request in the `School Material Request Form` field.
2. Select the category for your request from the dropdown menu under `Category`.
3. Enter a detailed description of the material or resource you are requesting in the `Description` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `Email` field to facilitate communication about your request.
5. Provide your phone number in the `Phone` field for further communication.
6. Enter the date you would like to make the request in the `Request Date` field.
7. Enter the time you would like to make the request in the `Request Time` field.
8. Enter the location where you would like to pick up the material in the `Request Location` field.
9. List the specific items you are requesting in the `Requested Items` field, being as detailed as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Material Request Form**: Provide a brief title for your request.
* **Category**: Select the type of request you are making (Request Forms, Other).
* **Description**: Enter a detailed description of the material or resource you are requesting.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to make the request.
* **Request Time**: Enter the time you would like to make the request.
* **Request Location**: Enter the location where you would like to pick up the material.
* **Requested Items**: List the specific items you are requesting, being as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not submitted a similar request in the past to avoid duplication.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the school administration for assistance.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your request, you can email them to the school administration along with your form submission.
