# Retrofit Planning Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retrofit Planning Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals involved in a project. This form helps project managers and HR teams track and manage project details, including personal information, project status, start and end dates, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. (Optional) Enter your date of birth if you have one.
4. Enter the date of your retrofit, which is the date you started your work on the project.
5. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
6. Provide your project name and description.
7. Select the current status of your project (active or inactive).
8. Enter your project manager's name (if applicable).
9. Enter the start and end dates of your project.
10. Enter the location of your project.
11. Add any additional notes about your project.
12. Enter your contact person's name (if you have a contact person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Date of Retrofit** (`date_of_retrofit`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started your work on the project.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your project (active or inactive).
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your project.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have a project manager, enter their name to ensure they receive updates.
* Use the YYYY-MM-DD format for dates.
* If you have any questions, contact your project manager or HR representative.
