# Ahm Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an appointment with our team to discuss potential services or projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred date and time for the appointment.
3. Indicate your service interest(s) and describe your project.
4. If applicable, select a budget range and how you heard about us.
5. Confirm if this is an urgent request.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a date that works best for you to have the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time of day that suits you best.
* **Service Interest** (`service_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more services you are interested in.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Describe your project in a few sentences or bullet points.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a specific budget in mind, select it from the provided options.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you discovered our services.
* **Is this urgent?** (`urgent_request`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you need a rush turnaround or not.

Note: The form will not allow you to select multiple dates or times. If you need to book multiple appointments, please submit separate requests.
