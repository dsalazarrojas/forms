# Course Cleanup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Cleanup Request Form is a tool used to request maintenance and updates for courses. This form helps users submit their requests efficiently, ensuring that courses are properly maintained and updated in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the **User Email** field. This is the address where you will receive updates and notifications about your request.
2. Provide your **User Name** in the field below. This will help us identify you when discussing your request.
3. If you're requesting maintenance or updates for a specific course, provide the **Course Title** in the field provided.
4. Enter the **Department** where the course is offered, or where you are requesting the maintenance.
5. If you're requesting a schedule update, enter the new **Location** where the course will take place.
6. Select the type of request you are submitting, which can be **Request**, **Update**, or **Maintenance** from the **Issue Type** dropdown menu.
7. Provide a detailed description of your request in the **Request Details** field.
8. Optionally, you can also specify the time of submission in the **Submission Time** field.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes in the **Form Submission** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you will receive updates and notifications about your request.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Provide your name so we can identify you when discussing your request.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): If you're requesting maintenance or updates for a specific course, enter its title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department where the course is offered, or where you are requesting the maintenance.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you're requesting a schedule update, enter the new location where the course will take place.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): Select **Request**, **Update**, or **Maintenance** based on the type of request you are submitting.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Optionally, specify the time of submission.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
