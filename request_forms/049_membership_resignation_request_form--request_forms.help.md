# Membership Resignation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by members to notify the organization of their intention to disaffiliate. It provides a formal way to submit resignation request and is used by the organization to process such requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before You Start
Before filling out this form, please ensure you are a current member of the organization. If you are unsure of your membership status, you can contact the membership department to verify.

### To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Attach a clear and concise reason for your resignation.
3. Ensure your email and phone number are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Member Name
* **Member Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your membership records.

### 2. Resignation Date
* **Resignation Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to leave the organization.

### 3. Reason
* **Reason** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation for your resignation. Please be respectful in your explanation.

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your membership.

### 5. Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number associated with your membership.

### 6. Signature
* **Signature** (`note`, required/optional): Sign this form to confirm that you are the member requesting resignation. Your signature is a formal acknowledgment of this request.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact the membership department.
