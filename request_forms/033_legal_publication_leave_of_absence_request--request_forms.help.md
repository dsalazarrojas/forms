# Legal Publication Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a legal publication leave of absence for employees in the publishing industry. It is intended to be a simple and efficient way to track and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Publishing, Editing, Vacation, or Sick).
2. Enter your name and job position.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
5. Provide a brief description of the leave reason.
6. Select the manager who will approve your leave request.
7. List the managers and team leads who will be involved in your leave approval process.
8. Select the current status of your leave request (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Publication Leave Request Form** (`publication_leave_request_form`): This is the title of the form, which should be filled out to indicate that this is a leave request form.
* **Name** (`name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`): Enter your current job position.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title as it appears on your official records.
* **Leave Date Start** (`leave_date_start`): Enter the start date of your leave in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Leave Date End** (`leave_date_end`): Enter the end date of your leave in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Publishing, Editing, Vacation, or Sick).
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`): Provide a brief description of the reasons for your leave request.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Select the manager who will approve your leave request.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`): List the managers who will be involved in your leave approval process.
* **Team Lead Approval** (`team_lead_approval`): Select the team lead who will approve your leave request.
* **Team Lead Approvals** (`team_lead_approvals`): List the team leads who will be involved in your leave approval process.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`): Select the current status of your leave request (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Created By** (`created_by`): This field is not used in the leave request process and can be ignored.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`): This field is not used in the leave request process and can be ignored.

## Tips
* Be sure to discuss your leave request with your manager and team lead before filling out this form.
* Make sure to select the correct leave type and reason for your request.
* Double-check the managers and team leads involved in your leave approval process.
* Keep an eye on the status of your leave request and follow up with the relevant parties if necessary.
