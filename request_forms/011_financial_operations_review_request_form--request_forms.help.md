The Financial Operations Review Request Form is used by finance department staff to request reviews of financial transactions and operations. This form helps to identify and document requests for review, ensuring that all relevant information is captured and that requests are properly assigned to the correct team. Please note that this form should only be used for requesting reviews, not for submitting actual reviews. If you are an operations team member, you should not submit this form. Instead, you should submit your reviews directly through the designated review platform.

# Financial Operations Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Operations Review Request Form is a tool to capture and track requests for review of financial transactions and operations within the finance department. It helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured and that requests are properly assigned to the correct team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options for your request:
	* If you are requesting a review, select "Yes" for request_form_submission.
	* If you are not requesting a review, select "No".
2. Provide a brief description of your request in the request_description field.
3. Add any relevant context or additional information in the context field.
4. List any supporting documents or relevant details in the documents field.
5. Specify the priority level of your request in the priority field:
	* Financial Review
	* High
	* Low
6. Choose the team that should be assigned to review your request:
	* Financial Team
	* Accounting Team
	* Operations Team
7. Select a date to indicate when you submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_form_submission (Yes/No)**: This field indicates whether you are requesting a review or not. Please select "Yes" if you need someone to review your request.
* **request_description**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your request. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **context**: This field is for providing any additional context or information that may be relevant to your request.
* **documents**: List any supporting documents or relevant details related to your request.
* **review_request**: Please note that this field is not used in the review process. It's just a placeholder field.
* **priority (Financial Review/High/Low)**: This field helps us understand the urgency and importance of your request. Please select the priority level that best fits your situation.
* **assigned_team (Financial Team/Accounting Team/Operations Team)**: Choose the team that should be assigned to review your request.
* **submitted_on**: Select a date to indicate when you submitted the request. Please make sure to select a date in the correct format.
