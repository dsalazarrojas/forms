# Breaking Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breaking Up Form is designed to help individuals break up with someone in a clear, compassionate, and respectful manner. It guides you through a series of questions to help you express your feelings and intentions to your partner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and take some time to reflect on your feelings and intentions.
2. Answer all the required fields (marked as "true") to provide a complete and clear message.
3. Choose your answers wisely, as they will be used to shape the tone and content of your breakup message.
4. Review and edit your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`sender_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed in the message.
* **Your Email** (`sender_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as it will be used to send the message.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person you are breaking up with.
* **Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you were in the relationship.
* **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of relationship you were in.
* **Main Reason for Breakup** (`breakup_reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the main reason for the breakup.
* **Explain Your Reason** (`breakup_reason_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason you selected.
* **Personal Feelings Statement** (`feelings_statement`, `text`, required): Share how you feel about the breakup.
* **Positive Memories** (`positive_memories`, `text`, optional): Mention any positive memories or experiences you had in the relationship.
* **Solutions You Tried** (`attempted_solutions`, `text`, optional): Describe any solutions or efforts you made to fix the relationship.
* **Future Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to handle future contact.
* **Return of Belongings** (`belongings_return`, `select_one`, required): Select how you want to handle the return of belongings.
* **Social Media Preference** (`social_media_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to handle social media contact.
* **Mutual Friends** (`mutual_friends`, `text`, optional): Suggest how you want to handle mutual friends.
* **Message Tone** (`message_tone`, `select_one`, required): Select the tone you want to convey in the message.
* **Final Message** (`final_message`, `text`, required): Provide a final message to share with your partner.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to deliver the message.
* **Do You Have Support** (`support_system`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have support system in place.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you are certain about the breakup.

Please use this guide to help you complete the form and craft a breakup message that is clear, compassionate, and respectful. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
