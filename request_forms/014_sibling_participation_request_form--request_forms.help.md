# Sibling Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sibling Participation Request Form is designed to collect information from siblings to participate in events or programs. This form will help us understand their interests, preferences, and other essential details to create a wonderful experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your request information, such as your name and contact details, in the `Request Info` section.
2. In the `Parent Info` section, provide your parent's or guardian's contact information, if applicable.
3. In the `Sibling Info` section, enter your sibling's name and other relevant details.
4. Select the program(s) and event(s) you are interested in participating in the `Program` and `Events` sections.
5. Confirm your participation in the `Confirm Program & Event` sections.
6. Review and confirm your submission in the `Review Program & Event Info` section.
7. Click the `Submit` button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, email address, and other contact details.
* **Parent Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your parent's or guardian's name, email address, and phone number, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Sibling Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your sibling's name.
* **Program** (Select One, Required): Select one program you are interested in participating in.
* **Events** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all events you are interested in participating in.
* **Confirm Program & Event** (Text, Optional): Confirm your participation in the selected program and event.
* **Confirm Program** (Text, Optional): Confirm your participation in the selected program.
* **Confirm Event** (Text, Optional): Confirm your participation in the selected event.
* **Confirm Program & Event Info** (Text, Optional): Confirm the program and event information you provided.
* **Review Program & Event Info** (Text, Optional): Review the program and event details you've selected.
* **Submit** (Text, Not Required): Click to submit your request.
* **Review Program** (Text, Optional): Review your participation in the program.
* **Review Event** (Text, Optional): Review your participation in the event.
* **Review Program & Event Info** (Text, Optional): Review the program and event details you've selected.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are interested in participating.
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you are interested in participating.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Program & Events** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all programs and events you are interested in participating in.
* **Confirm Review** (Text, Optional): Confirm your review of the program and event details.

Please take your time to review the form carefully and ensure accurate completion. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for clarification.
