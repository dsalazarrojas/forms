# Risk Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Consultant Quote Form is designed to collect vital information from clients to create quotes for risk consulting services. This form helps us understand the client's risk profile, requirements, and preferences, enabling us to provide accurate and tailored quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Client Name**: Provide the name of the client for whom you're creating the quote.
2. **Enter Risk Consultant**: Enter the name of the risk consultant assigned to the client.
3. **Quote Date**: Select the date for which you're creating the quote.
4. **Risk Description**: Describe the risk associated with the client's project or operation.
5. **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for contact purposes.
6. **Quote Price**: Enter the price for the risk consulting services.
7. **Quote Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the quote.
8. **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the client.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client or the quote.
10. **Quote Date Range**: Select the date range for which you're creating the quote.
11. **Quote Price Range**: Enter the price range for the risk consulting services.
12. **Quote Terms Range**: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the quote range.
13. **Assigned To Range**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the client range.
14. **Notes Range**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client or the quote range.
15. **Risk Level**: Select the risk level associated with the client's project or operation (High or Low).
16. **Risk Type**: Select whether the risk is present ('Yes' or 'No') in the client's project or operation.
17. **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address for contact purposes.
18. **Risk Category**: Select the risk category associated with the client's project or operation (Yes or No).
19. **Quote Type**: Select the quote type associated with the client's project or operation (Yes or No).
20. **Notes 2**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client or the quote.
21. **Risk Description 2**: Describe the risk associated with the client's project or operation 2.
22. **Risk Level 2**: Select the risk level associated with the client's project or operation 2 (High or Low).
23. **Risk Type 2**: Select whether the risk is present 2 ('Yes' or 'No') in the client's project or operation 2.
24. **Notes 3**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client or the quote.
25. **Quote Terms 3**: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the quote 3.
