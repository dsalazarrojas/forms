# Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a religious exemption for the Covid vaccine. It collects information from employees who have a legitimate religious reason for not getting vaccinated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing your personal information, including your employee name, employee ID, and contact details.
2. Provide a valid reason for your request for a religious exemption. Be as detailed as possible to help HR and management review your request.
3. If you have any supporting documents, upload them to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID or employee record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number or employee record ID.
* **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a clear and detailed explanation for your request for a religious exemption. Be specific about your religious beliefs or practices that prevent you from getting vaccinated.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documents, such as a letter from your religious leader or a document explaining your specific religious practice or belief, upload them here.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Religious Dedication** (`religious_dedication`, text, optional): Provide a brief statement about your religious dedication or practice that prevents you from getting vaccinated.
* **Exemption Request Date** (`exemption_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you are requesting the exemption.
