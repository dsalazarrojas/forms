The purpose of this form is to collect information from artists, labels, and publishing companies for music royalty adjustments. This form is used to submit requests for adjustments to music royalties, which are payments made to creators and rights holders for the use of their work in various contexts, such as radio airplay, TV broadcasts, or digital streaming. The form ensures that all relevant details are gathered and submitted in a structured way to facilitate accurate and efficient processing of royalty adjustment requests.

# music_royalty_adjustment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music royalty adjustment request form is designed to collect submission information, artist details, label and publishing information, document uploads, adjustment requests, and review details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information in each field.
3. Upload any supporting documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submission Information
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, `text`, not required): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your submission and any relevant context.
* **Artist Submission** (`artist_submission`, `text`, not required): Enter the name or identifier of the artist related to this submission.
* **Label Submission** (`label_submission`, `text`, not required): Enter the name or identifier of the label related to this submission.
* **Publishing Submission** (`publishing_submission`, `text`, not required): Enter the name or identifier of the publishing company related to this submission.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, `text`, not required): Upload any supporting documents relevant to your submission, such as contracts or proof of payment.
* **Adjustment Request** (`adjustment_request`, `text`, not required): Describe the specific royalty adjustment you are requesting.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or context for the reviewer to consider during the review process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and documents before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
