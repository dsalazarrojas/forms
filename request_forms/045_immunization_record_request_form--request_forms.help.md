# Immunization Record Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to immunization records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as follows:
- Type in your name.
- Enter your date of birth.
- Enter your address.
- Enter your email.
- Enter your phone number.
- Explain the reason for your request.
- Add your signature.
- Enter a Form ID (if available).

*   *Type: Text*
