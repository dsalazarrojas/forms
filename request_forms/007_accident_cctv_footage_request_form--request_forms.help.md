# Accident CCTV Footage Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request CCTV footage related to accidents that occurred within a building or on its premises. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the incident and the requested footage. The form is typically used to support insurance claims, internal investigations, and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the requestor's information: Enter your full name, role, email address, phone number, and organization or company name.
2. Provide incident details: Enter the date and start and end times of the incident, the location of the incident, and a brief description of what happened.
3. Specify the footage request: Choose the type of incident, select the purpose of the request, and specify the preferred delivery format.
4. Additional information: If applicable, provide any additional notes or special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name (<name>, <type> text, required): Your full name as the person making the request.
* **Role or Position (<name>, <type> select_one, required): Your position or role related to the incident, such as "Security Officer" or "Property Manager".
* **Email Address (<name>, <type> email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (<name>, <type> text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization or Company Name (<name>, <type> text, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Date of Incident (<name>, <type> date, required): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Approximate Start Time of Incident (<name>, <type> time, required): Enter the approximate start time of the incident.
* **Approximate End Time of Incident (<name>, <type> time, optional): Enter the approximate end time of the incident, if known.
* **Location of Incident (<name>, <type> text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, such as a building name or a specific address.
* **Type of Incident (<name>, <type> select_one, required): Choose the type of incident that occurred, such as "Vehicle Accident" or "Slip, Trip, or Fall".
* **Description of the Incident (<name>, <type> text, required): Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
* **Specific Camera Locations Requested (<name>, <type> text, optional): If known, enter the camera IDs or area names that you are requesting footage from.
* **Purpose of Footage Request (<name>, <type> select_one, required): Choose the purpose of your request, such as "Insurance Claim" or "Internal Investigation".
* **Police Report Number (if applicable) (<name>, <type> text, optional): If applicable, enter the police report number related to the incident.
* **Urgency of Request (<name>, <type> select_one, required): Choose the urgency of your request, such as "Standard" or "Urgent".
* **Preferred Delivery Format (<name>, <type> select_one, required): Choose your preferred delivery format, such as "Digital Download Link" or "USB Drive".
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions (<name>, <type> text, optional): Provide any additional information or instructions related to your request.
