# patient_record_lookup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_record_lookup_form is designed to retrieve patient records in a fast and efficient manner. This form will help users to quickly access patient information, including demographics, medical providers, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the `Patient ID` of the patient you want to look up in the corresponding field. This is a required field, so please ensure it is filled out accurately.
2. If you know the `Date of Birth` of the patient, you can optionally select it from the calendar. If you do not know the date of birth, it is not necessary to fill out this field.
3. You can optionally provide the patient's `Name` if you know it. If you do not know the name, it is not necessary to fill out this field.
4. You can optionally select the `Contact Number` if you have it. If you do not have it, it is not necessary to fill out this field.
5. Select the `Medical Group` from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's medical group.
6. Optionally, provide any known `Medical Condition` information.
7. Select the `Medical Provider` from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's medical provider.
8. Select the `Medical Specialty` from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's medical specialty.
9. Select the `Patient Category` from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's category.
10. Fill out the `Date of Submission` field accurately.
11. Enter the `Submitter ID` of the person submitting the request.
12. Enter the `Submitter Name` of the person submitting the request.
13. Optionally, provide any additional details about the submitter in the `Submitter Title`, `Submitter Phone`, `Submitter Email`, `Submitter Department`, `Submitter Job Title`, and `Submitter Organization` fields, but these are not required.
14. Finally, fill out the `Date of Death` and `Date of Death Certified` fields if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient ID**: A required field to look up a patient's record. Enter the unique ID of the patient you want to look up.
* **Date of Birth**: Optional field to filter by date of birth. If you know the date of birth, select it from the calendar to narrow down the search results.
* **Patient Name**: Optional field to filter by patient name. If you know the name, fill it out to find the corresponding patient record.
* **Contact Number**: Optional field to filter by contact number. If you have the contact number, fill it out to find the corresponding patient record.
* **Medical Group**: A required field to select the medical group of the patient. Choose the group from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's medical group.
* **Medical Condition**: Optional field to provide any known medical condition information.
* **Medical Provider**: A required field to select the medical provider of the patient. Choose the provider from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's medical provider.
* **Medical Specialty**: A dropdown menu to select the medical specialty of the patient.
* **Patient Category**: A required field to select the patient category. Choose the category from the dropdown menu that matches the patient's category.
* **Date of Submission**: A required field to record the date of submission.
* **Submitter ID**: A required field to record the submitter's ID.
* **Submitter Name**: A required field to record the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Title**, **Submitter Phone**, **Submitter Email**, **Submitter Department**, **Submitter Job Title**, **Submitter Organization**: Optional fields to provide additional details about the submitter.
* **Date of Death**: Optional field to record the date of death.
* **Date of Death Certified**: A required field to record the date of death certification.
