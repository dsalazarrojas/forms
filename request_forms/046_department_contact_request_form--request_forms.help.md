# Department Contact Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to allow end-users to submit contact requests to departments within the organization. It is designed to gather information necessary for departments to respond to and address the submitted requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the department you wish to contact from the list of available options.
3. Select the type(s) of inquiry you wish to make (e.g., meeting, question, issue).
4. Provide any additional comments or context about your inquiry.
5. Enter your email address (if desired) so the department can contact you directly.
6. Enter your phone number (if desired) so the department can contact you directly.
7. Select the date you wish the department to follow up with you about your inquiry (if desired).
8. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, **optional**): Enter any relevant details about yourself for the department to address your inquiry properly.
* **Select Department** (`department`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the department you wish to contact from the list of available options.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional contact information, such as your name or departmental affiliation.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the type(s) of inquiry you wish to make (e.g., meeting, question, issue).
* **Additional Comment** (`comment`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional context or comments about your inquiry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address so the department can contact you directly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number so the department can contact you directly.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Select the date you wish the department to follow up with you about your inquiry (if desired).

## Tips
* Be sure to submit the form completely and accurately to ensure the department can address your inquiry properly.
* Use the "Inquiry Type" field to indicate the purpose of your contact request.
* Use the "Additional Comment" field to provide any additional context or comments about your inquiry.
