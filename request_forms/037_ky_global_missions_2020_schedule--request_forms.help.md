# ky_global_missions_2020_schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ky_global_missions_2020_schedule form is designed to collect information about global and district missions. It is intended for mission coordinators to plan and manage their missions effectively. The form is used to schedule and assign missions to teams or individuals, with various options for mission types, start and end dates, locations, and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the schedule date for your mission.
2.  Choose the type of mission you want to schedule (Global or District).
3.  Enter the start and end dates for your mission.
4.  Provide the location for your mission.
5.  Add any additional details about your mission.
6.  Select the type of mission you are assigned to (Global or District).
7.  Enter the start and end dates for your assigned mission.
8.  Enter the location for your assigned mission.
9.  Add any additional details about your assigned mission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required: false): This field is where you enter the date for your mission. You can select a date from the calendar or enter it manually in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Mission Type** (`mission_type`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to choose the type of mission you want to schedule. Select either Global or District to indicate the scope of your mission.
* **Mission Start Date** (`mission_start_date`, date, required: false): This field is for entering the start date of your mission.
* **Mission End Date** (`mission_end_date`, date, required: false): This field is for entering the end date of your mission.
* **Mission Location** (`mission_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location for your mission.
* **Mission Details** (`mission_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your mission.
* **Assigned Mission Type** (`assigned_mission_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of mission you have been assigned to. Choose between Global or District.
* **Assigned Mission Start Date** (`assigned_mission_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your assigned mission.
* **Assigned Mission End Date** (`assigned_mission_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your assigned mission.
* **Assigned Mission Location** (`assigned_mission_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your assigned mission.
* **Assigned Mission Details** (`assigned_mission_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about your assigned mission.
