# Outreach Brochure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request outreach brochures from our organization. Fill out the form below to provide us with your information, and we will review your request carefully to ensure that we can provide you with the necessary brochure.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank, but it is recommended to fill it out if possible.
- For the "Delivery Method" field, select all applicable options to ensure that we can meet your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information you would like to share with us, such as special requests or comments.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Fill in your name as you would like it to be printed on the brochure.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted to confirm the delivery details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates about the brochure delivery.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): If you would like to request more than one brochure, specify the quantity here.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where you would like to receive the brochure.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable methods for us to deliver the brochure to you.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about your delivery preferences.
* **Date of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for delivery, enter it here.
* **Time of Delivery** (`time_of_delivery`, time, optional): If you have a specific time for delivery, enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or requests you have.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant school information that may be helpful for our team to know.
* **School District** (`school_district`, text, optional): If applicable, select the school district you are affiliated with.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your school or institution.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your school or institution.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your school or institution.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your school or institution.
* **School Country** (`school_country`, text, optional): If you are outside the United States, select your country of origin.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your school or institution.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of your school or institution.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your school or institution.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of your school or institution.
* **Other School Info** (`school_other_info`, text, optional): Provide any other information about your school or institution that may be relevant.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Provide any other information or comments you would like to share.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Share any other comments or requests you have.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us. We will review your form submission carefully and respond with any further instructions or updates.
