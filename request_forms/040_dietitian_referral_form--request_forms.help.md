# Dietitian Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide essential information to a dietitian for nutrition services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the referral type and any other required fields.
2. Enter the client's name and contact information.
3. Provide the referral date and time.
4. Enter the reason for referral.
5. Add any additional referral notes.
6. Select the medical and dietetic specialties involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the type of referral you are making.
  - Referral: This indicates a referral to a dietitian.
  - Non-Referred: This is for general progress monitoring or follow-up appointments.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): 
  Enter the client's name.
* **Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): 
  Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): 
  Enter the client's email address.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): 
  Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, optional): 
  Enter the time of the referral.
* **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, optional): 
  Describe why you are making the referral.
* **Referring Dietitian Name** (`referring_dietitian_name`, text, optional): 
  Enter the name of the dietitian referring the client.
* **Client Condition** (`client_condition`, text, optional): 
  Describe the client's condition that requires referral to a dietitian.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): 
  Enter any relevant medical history for the client.
* **Referral Notes** (`referral_notes`, text, optional): 
  Add any additional notes for the referral.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select all relevant medical specialties involved (e.g. Cardiologist, Endocrinologist).
* **Dietetic Specialty** (`dietetic_specialty`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select all relevant dietetic specialties involved (e.g. Diabetes, Gastroenterology).
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, optional): 
  Enter the client's medical record number.
* **Referral Form** (`referral_form`, text, optional): 
  Fill in any relevant information about the client's referral form.
