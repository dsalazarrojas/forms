# Skilled Trade Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their skilled trade service requests. It helps us gather the necessary details to efficiently manage and fulfill these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Service Details**: Enter a brief description of the required service (e.g., "Fix a leaky faucet").
2. **Trade Type**: Select one of the available trade types (e.g., Electrician, Plumber, HVAC Technician).
3. **Address**: Enter the address where the service is required (e.g., "123 Main St").
4. **Service Description**: Provide a detailed description of the required service (e.g., "Repair a leaky faucet in the kitchen").
5. **Schedule Date**: Choose a date and time for the service to be completed (e.g., "Next Wednesday at 2 PM").
6. **Schedule Time**: Select a time of day for the service to be completed (e.g., "2:00 PM").
7. **Contact Name**: Enter your name and contact information (e.g., "John Doe, 555-555-5555, johndoe@email.com").
8. **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (e.g., "555-555-5555").
9. **Contact E-mail**: Enter your contact email address (e.g., "johndoe@email.com").
10. **Message**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the service (e.g., "Please bring a wrench for the repair").
11. **Upload Documents**: (Optional) Attach any relevant documents or images related to the service request (e.g., a picture of the broken faucet).

* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required=false)
	+ Enter a brief description of the required service.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any additional information or questions, use the "Message" field.
* If you need to provide documents or images, upload them using the "Upload Documents" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact our support team.
