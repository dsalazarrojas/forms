# Elections Volunteer Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elections Volunteer Request Form is a tool to collect information from individuals who are interested in volunteering for election-related activities. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable information to our team to help us organize volunteer opportunities effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button below to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your Name in the "Name" field.
3. Add your Contact Information, including any relevant details about yourself.
4. Provide your Contact Number for us to reach out to you.
5. Indicate your availability for volunteering by selecting your "Best Volunteer Days" from the options provided.
6. Let us know if you are available to volunteer ("Yes" or "No") and specify your preferred "Volunteer Type" if applicable.
7. Enter your volunteer-related skills and expertise in the "Skills" field.
8. If you are willing to volunteer, specify the number of hours you can commit to volunteering in the "Volunteer Hours" field.
9. Choose your preferred "Work Location" where you would like to volunteer.
10. Enter your Email address so we can contact you.
11. Confirm your Email address by entering it again in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Volunteer Request
This is the main request for volunteering. Please type your request and we will review it.

*   **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
*   **Contact Information**: Provide any relevant information about yourself, such as your address or any relevant medical conditions.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Best Volunteer Days**: Select the days you are available to volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Hours**: Specify the number of hours you can volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Type**: If you have a specific skill set or type of volunteering you are interested in, select it here.
*   **Skills**: List your relevant skills or expertise.
*   **Volunteer Hours**: Specify the number of hours you can volunteer per session.
*   **Work Location**: Choose where you would like to volunteer.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
*   **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure we have it correctly.

## Tips
*   Please ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date to ensure we can contact you.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
