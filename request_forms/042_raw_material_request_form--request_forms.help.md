# Raw Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about raw materials requested for procurement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1. Enter the **Request Date** of the material needed.
2. Provide a detailed **Material Description** for the supplier to understand what material is being requested.
3. Specify the **Material Type** and **Material ID** for easier reference and tracking.
4. Enter the **Material Quantity** required from the supplier.
5. Enter the **Supplier Name**, **Supplier Email**, and **Supplier Phone** for contact information.
6. Enter the **Order Due Date** for the supplier to know when the material is needed.
7. Select the current **Order Status** from the provided options.
8. Enter the **Supplier Location** if relevant.
9. Add any **Notes** for additional information.
10. Enter your **User Name** and **User Email** for internal tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: Enter the date when you are requesting the material.
* **Material Description**: Provide a detailed description of the material needed.
* **Material Type**: Enter the type of material being requested.
* **Material ID**: Enter the unique ID for the material.
* **Material Quantity**: Enter the quantity of material needed.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier providing the material.
* **Supplier Email**: Enter the email address of the supplier for contact.
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Order Due Date**: Enter the date when the material is due.
* **Order Status**: Select the current status of the order from the options.
* **Supplier Location**: Enter the location of the supplier if relevant.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the material request.
* **User Name**: Enter your name for tracking purposes.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address for tracking purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* Use the form for legitimate material requests and not for duplicate requests.
