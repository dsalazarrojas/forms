# public_venue_lighting_scheme_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a public venue lighting scheme application. This information will be used to assess and implement the requested lighting scheme for the venue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the "Project Details" field with a brief description of your project.
* Provide the relevant "Dates" for your project in the respective field.
* Specify the "Lighting Requirements" needed for your project.
* Enter the "Venue Details" where the lighting scheme will be applied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (project_details, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for a brief description of your project. Please provide a clear and concise overview of the project you are applying for.
* **Dates** (dates, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the relevant dates related to your project. This could be the start and end dates of your project, or specific dates for events and deadlines.
* **Lighting Requirements** (lighting_requirements, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for specifying the lighting requirements needed for your project. Be as detailed as possible about the type and amount of lighting required.
* **Venue Details** (venue_details, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing the details of the venue where the lighting scheme will be applied. This could be an address, location, or any other relevant information about the venue.
