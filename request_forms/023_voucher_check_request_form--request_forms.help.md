# Voucher Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Voucher Check Request Form is designed to facilitate the submission of voucher requests. This form is used to capture the necessary information to process and review the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure that you have all the required information to proceed.
2. On the "Submit Request" page, select "Yes" if you want to submit the request or "No" if you don't.
3. If submitting the request, move to the next page and fill out the required fields with the requested information.
4. Select the voucher type from the list.
5. Enter the voucher number.
6. Provide the business name and contact details.
7. Specify the services and amounts requested.
8. Add any additional request notes if necessary.
9. Review the form and ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request**: Select "Yes" if you want to submit the request or "No" if you don't.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business making the request.
* **Services Provided**: Describe the services provided by the business.
* **Amount Requested**: Enter the amount requested for the voucher.
* **Reason For Request**: Enter the reason for the request.
* **Date Of Service**: Enter the date of service.
* **Time Of Service**: Enter the time of service.
* **Voucher Type**: Select the type of voucher being requested.
* **Voucher Number**: Enter the voucher number.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Business Email**: Enter the business email.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the business phone number.
* **Request Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the request.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title as the submitter.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments as the submitter.
* **Review Status**: Select the review status of the request (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments about the review.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for guidance.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact your administrator or support team.
