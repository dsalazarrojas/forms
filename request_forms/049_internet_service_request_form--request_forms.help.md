# Internet Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from users regarding their internet service requests. This includes contact information, preferred contact method, and details about the service requested.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, making sure to answer all required fields.
* Click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide additional contact information if needed.
* **Contact Information** (2) (Select multiple, Optional):
	+ Choose all that apply.
	+ Yes: You have provided your contact information and are willing to be contacted.
	+ No: You have not provided your contact information.
* **Service Requests** (3) (Number, Optional):
	+ Enter the number of services you need assistance with.
* **Service Requests Description** (4) (Text, Optional):
	+ Briefly describe the type of service you need.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (5) (Select one, Optional):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted.
	+ Email: We will contact you via email.
	+ Phone: We will contact you via phone.
	+ SMS: We will contact you via SMS.
	+ In-person: We will meet with you in person.
* **Email** (6) (Email, Optional):
	+ Enter your email address if you have not provided it in the contact information field.
* **Phone** (7) (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your phone number if you have not provided it in the contact information field.
* **Requested Time** (8) (Time, Optional):
	+ Enter the time you are available to be contacted.
* **Requested Date** (9) (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date you are available to be contacted.
* **Notes** (10) (Note, Optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (11) (Select one, Optional):
	+ Choose a tool you would like to be assigned.
	+ chatjimmy: You will be assigned chatjimmy.
	+ other: Specify the tool in the next field.
* **Other Assigned Tool** (12) (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of the tool you would like to be assigned if you did not choose "chatjimmy".
* **Submit** (13) (Text, Optional):
	+ Click on this button to send your request.
* **Cancel** (14) (Text, Optional):
	+ Click on this button if you need to cancel your request.

## Tips

* Please be aware that if you have previously submitted a request, it will be marked as duplicated and not processed.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
