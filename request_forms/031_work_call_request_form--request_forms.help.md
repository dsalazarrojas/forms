# Work Call Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Call Request Form is used for requesting a work call with a manager. It's a simple form that helps you schedule a meeting to discuss work-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required and optional fields as needed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the meeting you are requesting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `number`, optional): Enter the time of the meeting you are requesting.
* **Will you be meeting?** (`meeting_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are certain that you will meet, and "No" if you are not sure or unable to meet.
* **Meeting attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select who will be attending the meeting (e.g., colleagues, stakeholders, or team members).
* **Meeting notes** (`meeting_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Work request** (`work_request`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific work requests or topics you would like to discuss during the meeting.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific tools or resources you may need for the meeting.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `submit`, required): Click on this button to submit your form and request a work call.

## Tips
* Be clear and specific when filling out the form to ensure the manager has a clear understanding of your needs.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any mistakes or errors.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, please reach out to your team lead for assistance.
