# Marketing Request Form 2015 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Request Form 2015 is a tool designed to collect marketing requests from users. It is intended to gather information about the requestor, their marketing activities, and communication methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you have any additional comments or information, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requestor Information
* **Requestor Information (Required)** (`requestor_info`, `text`, Required): This field is where you provide your name and contact information.
### Activity Information
* **Activity Information (Required)** (`activity_info`, `text`, Required): This field is where you describe the marketing activity you are interested in.
### Communication Information
* **Communication Information (Required)** (`communication_info`, `text`, Required): This field is where you specify how you would like to be contacted for further information about the activity.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments (Optional)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your marketing request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is submitted correctly.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
