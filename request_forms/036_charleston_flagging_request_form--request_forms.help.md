# Charleston Flagging Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Charleston Flagging Request Form is used to gather information needed to provide flagging services for construction projects, events, or other projects that require flaggers. The form helps us understand project details and ensure we have the necessary resources to deliver quality flagging services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
2. Be as accurate as possible when providing project and contact information.
3. Select the correct options for project type and flagging type.
4. If you need to provide equipment, choose the relevant option.
5. Submit the form after completing all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Request Information
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, Required): The date when you are requesting flagging services.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, Required): Your name.
  - Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, `text`, Required): Your contact number.
  - Enter your direct contact number.
- **Email Address** (`requestor_email`, `email`, Required): Your email address.
  - Enter your direct email address.
- **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, `text`, Optional): Your organization or company name.
  - If you represent a company or organization, enter it here.
- **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, Required): The address or area where flaggers are needed.
  - Enter the address or location where flaggers will be working.

### Project Details
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, Required): Name of the construction or traffic project.
  - Enter the name of the project.
- **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, Required): Type of project you are requesting.
  - Choose the project type from the options: Construction, Roadwork, Traffic Control, Event Setup, Utility Work, or Other.
- **Start Date for Flagging Services** (`start_date`, `date`, Required): When you need flaggers to start their services.
  - Enter the start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **End Date for Flagging Services** (`end_date`, `date`, Required): When flaggers' services are expected to end.
  - Enter the end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Daily Hours Needed** (`hours_needed`, `text`, Required): Number of hours flaggers are needed per day.
  - Enter the number of hours in numbers.
- **Number of Flaggers Needed** (`flaggers_count`, `number`, Required): Number of flaggers required.
  - Enter the number of flaggers you need.
- **Type of Flagging Needed** (`flagging_type`, `select_one`, Required): Type of flagging service you need.
  - Choose the flagging type from the options: Traffic Control, Parking Management, Crowd Control, Directional, or Mixed Services.
- **Will You Provide Equipment** (`equipment_provided`, `select_one`, Required): Whether you will provide equipment or not.
  - Choose whether you will provide equipment or if not provided.
- **On-Site Project Supervisor Name** (`onsite_supervisor`, `text`, Required): Person directing the project.
  - Enter the on-site project supervisor's name.
- **Supervisor Phone Number** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, Required): Direct contact at site.
  - Enter the on-site supervisor's contact number.
- **Any Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, Optional): Specific training, certifications, or languages needed.
  - If you have any special requirements, please enter them here.
