# Call Or Conference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Call Or Conference Request Form is used to request a call or conference with a representative from our organization. To ensure that your request is processed efficiently, please fill out this form with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Requester Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Requester Email Address" field to ensure that you receive confirmations and scheduling information.
3. If applicable, enter the name of your organization in the "Organization or Company" field.
4. Choose a preferred date and time in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields. Please note that local time and time zone are required for time slots.
5. Select the duration of the call in minutes in the "Requested Duration in Minutes" field. Typical durations are 30 or 60 minutes.
6. Choose the meeting format from the options provided in the "Call Type" field.
7. If you have attendees joining the call, list their names and roles separated by commas in the "Expected Attendees" field (optional).
8. Briefly describe the agenda or topics to be discussed in the "Agenda Summary" field.
9. If you have any special requirements or needs, such as accessibility requests, please enter them in the "Special Requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Requester Email Address**: Enter your email address used for scheduling and confirmations.
* **Organization or Company**: If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a date for the call or conference.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time for the call or conference, including local time and time zone.
* **Requested Duration in Minutes**: Enter the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Call Type**: Choose the meeting format (e.g., Phone Call, Video Conference, In Person, Hybrid).
* **Expected Attendees**: List the names and roles of attendees separated by commas (optional).
* **Agenda Summary**: Briefly describe the agenda or topics to be discussed.
* **Special Requirements**: List any special requirements or needs (e.g., accessibility, technical setup).
