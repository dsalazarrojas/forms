# Parental App Blocking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request blocking of a specific app on a child's device. Please fill out this form to report any app that you feel is inappropriate or problematic for your child to use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's device ID in the "Child Device" field. This is the unique identifier assigned to the child's device.
2. In the "Reason for Block" field, briefly explain why you want the app blocked.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Blocked App" field, indicating if the app is blocked or not.
4. Enter the app version if it's blocked.
5. Enter the date and time the app was blocked.
6. In the "Blocked User" field, enter the user who owns the child device.
7. Enter the reason for blocking the app in the "Blocked Reason" field.
8. Add any additional notes for the approver in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the name and email of the approver in the "Approver" and "Approver Email" fields.
10. Enter the approver's phone number in the "Approver Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Device (child_device)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the child's device.
* **Reason for Block (reason_for_block)**: Briefly explain why you want the app blocked.
* **Blocked App (blocked_app)**: Select "Yes" to block the app or "No" if it's not blocked.
* **Blocked App Version (blocked_app_version)**: Enter the version of the blocked app.
* **Date Blocked (date_blocked)**: Enter the date the app was blocked.
* **Blocked Time (time_blocked)**: Enter the time the app was blocked.
* **Blocked User (blocked_user)**: Enter the user who owns the child device.
* **Blocked Reason (blocked_reason)**: Enter the reason for blocking the app.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes for the approver.
* **Approver (approver)**: Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Email (approver_email)**: Enter the email of the approver.
* **Approver Phone (approver_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the approver.
