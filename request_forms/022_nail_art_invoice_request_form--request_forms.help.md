# Nail Art Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request form for nail art services, allowing customers to provide information about their desired nail art design style, contact details, and other relevant information. It ensures that the service provider receives the necessary details to deliver a personalized and high-quality nail art experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select all relevant options for the desired design style from the "Design" section.
* Choose your preferred style from the "Style" section.
* Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Design** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select one or more options that describe the desired nail art design style (e.g., American, French, Modern).
* **Style** (Select One):
	+ Choose a specific style for your nail art design, such as French Tip, Square Nail, Sticker, or Glitter.
* **Contact Details** (Text):
	+ Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure that the service provider can contact you with any questions or updates about your requested nail art design.
