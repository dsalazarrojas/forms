# Free Local Assistance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Local Assistance Request Form is used to request assistance with a specific local issue or need. This form helps gather information to provide you with the right support and resources to address your concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully** to ensure you are requesting the correct type of assistance.
2. Make sure you are filling out the form only once, as duplicate requests may be denied.
3. Provide clear and concise information in the "Request Form Data" and "User Data" fields, as they are used to understand your request.
4. If you have any additional comments or details not included in the "Request Form Data" and "User Data" fields, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" field (not shown in this help guide, but included in the actual form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form Data (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your request, including any relevant details or context. This field is optional but will help us better understand your needs.
* **User Data (Text)**: Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Form Submission ID (Number)**: This is automatically generated and not required to be filled in. If you filled it in, please ignore it.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your request for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- If you are unsure about what to write in a field, just leave it blank.
 
Please submit your request and we'll do our best to assist you.
