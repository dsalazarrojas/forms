# Landscaping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Request Form is used to request various landscaping services for a specific area or property. This form helps us understand your needs and schedule the necessary services efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Landscaping Request**:
	* Enter a brief description of the landscaping work you need, including any specific details or requirements.
2. **Service Type**:
	* Select the type of landscaping service you need (Landscaping, Lawn Care, Tree Trimming, or Other).
3. **Contact Number**:
	* Enter your phone number, so we can contact you to discuss the details of your request.
4. **Contact Email**:
	* Enter your email address, so we can send you updates and confirmations.
5. **Service Requests**:
	* Describe any additional landscaping services or requests you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landscaping Request**: A brief description of the landscaping work you need.
* **Service Type**: The type of landscaping service you're requesting (e.g., Landscaping, Lawn Care, Tree Trimming, or Other).
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number for us to call you back.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address for us to send updates and confirmations.
* **Service Requests**: Any additional landscaping services or requests you have.
