# Restricted Antibiotic Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a restricted antibiotic for a patient. It is intended for healthcare providers to request antibiotics that are not commonly available or require special authorization. Please ensure that you are completing this form for a valid medical reason and not simply to request a medication that is readily available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and address.
2. Enter the healthcare provider's information, including their name.
3. Select the antibiotic that is being requested from the list of options.
4. Provide the dosage and frequency of the requested antibiotic.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes from the healthcare provider.
6. Choose the approver's status for the request (Approved, Denied, Pending, or Rejected).
7. Select the approver's name and department from the dropdown options.
8. Enter the approver's reason for the status chosen.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: This field is used to collect the patient's name, date of birth, and address.
  * **Patient Name**: The patient's name, including first name and last name.
  * **Date of Birth (D.O.B.)**: The patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  * **Patient Address**: The patient's address.
* **Antibiotic Requested**: This field is used to select the antibiotic that is being requested.
  * Please select the antibiotic from the list of options, which includes commonly restricted antibiotics.
* **Dosage**: This field is used to enter the dosage of the requested antibiotic.
  * Please enter the dosage in a clear and concise format.
* **Frequency**: This field is used to enter the frequency of the requested antibiotic.
  * Please enter the frequency in a clear and concise format.
* **Route**: This field is used to enter the route of administration for the requested antibiotic.
  * Please enter the route in a clear and concise format.
* **Provider Note**: This field is used to enter any comments or notes from the healthcare provider.
  * Please enter any additional information that may be helpful for the approver.
* **Pharmacist Note**: This field is used to enter any additional comments or notes from the pharmacist.
  * Please enter any additional information that may be helpful for the approver.
* **Approver Name**: This field is used to select the approver's name.
  * Please select the approver's name from the dropdown options.
* **Approver Department**: This field is used to enter the approver's department.
  * Please enter the approver's department.
* **Approver Date**: This field is used to enter the date of the approver's decision.
  * Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Approver Time**: This field is used to enter the time of the approver's decision.
  * Please enter the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Approver Reason**: This field is used to enter the approver's reason for the decision.
  * Please enter a clear and concise reason for the decision.
* **Approver Status**: This field is used to select the approver's status (Approved, Denied, Pending, or Rejected).
  * Please select the approver's status from the dropdown options.
* **Additional Info**: This field is used to enter any additional information that may be helpful for the approver.
  * Please enter any additional information that may be helpful for the approver.
