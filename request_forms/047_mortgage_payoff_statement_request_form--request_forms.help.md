# Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form is a form used to request a mortgage payoff statement from our team. This form is designed to collect necessary information to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your loan account.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter your mortgage account number to help us locate your loan.
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select, optional): Select the type of loan you are referring to (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount you would like to pay off.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, optional): Enter the term of your loan (in years, months, or weeks).
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to request the payoff statement by.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person requesting the payoff statement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to include with your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address to help us get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to prevent delays in processing your request.
* Only submit one request per loan account to prevent duplication.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your request, contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Phone" and "Contact Email" fields.
