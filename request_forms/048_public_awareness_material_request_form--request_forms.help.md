# public_awareness_material_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting public awareness materials in a specific format and quantity. Please use this form to submit your request for materials that will be used for public awareness purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "General Information" page with your name and any relevant details about your request.
2. On the "Contact Information" page, provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
3. On the "Request Details" page, describe the material you are requesting and specify the format and quantity needed.
4. If you have any special instructions for the delivery, such as a preferred delivery date or time, please include them on the "Special Instructions" page.
5. Finally, upload any relevant files or documents for our review and processing.

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required): This page provides a space for you to enter your name and any other relevant information about your request.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your name so we can contact you about your request.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.

* **Preferred Format** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the format you prefer your material to be in (e.g., PDF, Word doc, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`6`, `text`, required): Specify the quantity of materials you need.
* **Delivery Location** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific location where you would like to receive your materials, please enter it here.
* **Special Instructions** (`8`, `text`, optional): If you have any special instructions for the delivery, such as a preferred delivery date or time, please enter them here.
