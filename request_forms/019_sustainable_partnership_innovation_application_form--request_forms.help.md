# Sustainable Partnership Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sustainable Partnership Innovation Application Form" is designed for individuals or organizations to share their project details and partnership interests with our company. It aims to facilitate an efficient and effective review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the appropriate "Applicant Type" from the available options (Innovator, Partner, or Grantee).
2. Enter your "Project Name" in the provided field.
3. Provide a detailed description of your "Project Description".
4. Select the "Project Status" from the available options (In Progress, Pending, Approved, or Disapproved).
5. Input your "Contact Email" address.
6. Fill in your "Contact Phone" number.
7. Enter your "Applicant Name" and the "Partner Name" (if applicable).
8. For "Project Start Date" and "Project End Date", select the corresponding dates from the calendar (note: these fields are optional).
9. Enter the "Assigned Tool" (if assigned).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name (1)**: Enter the name of your project or partnership proposal.
* **Applicant Type (2)**: Select one of the three options: Innovator, Partner, or Grantee. This indicates your role in the partnership.
* **Project Description (3)**: Describe your project or proposal in detail.
* **Project Status (4)**: Choose from the available options: In Progress, Pending, Approved, or Disapproved. This indicates the current stage of your project.
* **Contact Email (5)**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Input your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Applicant Name (7)**: Fill in your name as the applicant.
* **Partner Name (8)**: Enter the name of your partner (if applicable).
* **Project Start Date (9)**: Select the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date (10)**: Select the end date of your project (if known).
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: Enter the tool assigned to your project (if any).
