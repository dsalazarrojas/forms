# ICE Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICE Submission Form is a tool used to collect information about incidents or near-miss incidents that occurred within the organization. It is intended to be completed by individuals who have witnessed or are aware of an incident, with the goal of providing a clear and concise account of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure that you have all the necessary information about the incident before starting to fill out the form.
* Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to select the correct status (Open, In Progress, or Resolved) to indicate the current state of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident** (`incident_form`): Describe the incident or near-miss incident you are reporting.
* **Concerns** (`concern_form`): If relevant, please list any concerns or issues related to the incident.
* **Events** (`event_form`): Describe any events or circumstances leading up to and surrounding the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Reporter** (`reporter`): Enter your name or the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the incident report.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the current status of the incident (Open, In Progress, or Resolved).
