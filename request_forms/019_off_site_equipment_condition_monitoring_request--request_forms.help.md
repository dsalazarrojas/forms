# Off Site Equipment Condition Monitoring Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request condition monitoring of equipment located off-site. When submitting this form, you are requesting that a technician or assessor visits the site to inspect and monitor the condition of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the relevant details of your request in the text fields.
2. Select the desired monitoring frequency from the dropdown options.
3. Enter the date of the requested inspection, if known.
4. Enter your phone number and any additional notes for the assessor, if applicable.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scheduling Details**: Enter any relevant scheduling details, such as any specific dates or time frames that need to be avoided.
* **Equipment Details**: Enter information about the equipment to be monitored, including make, model, and any relevant notes.
* **Site Access Notes**: Enter any notes specific to site access, such as security or parking restrictions.
* **Service Team Name**: Enter the name of the service team or group responsible for this equipment.
* **Assessor Name**: Enter the name of the assessor assigned to this task, or who you would like to have assigned.
* **Assessor Email**: Enter the email address for the assessor, for contact or follow-up information.
* **Assessor Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the assessor, for contact or follow-up information.
* **Assessor Notes**: Enter any specific notes or instructions for the assessor, such as special requirements or preferences.
* **Monitoring Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you would like the equipment condition to be monitored (Weekly, Fortnightly, or Monthly).
* **Monitoring Date**: Enter the date of the scheduled monitoring, or when you would like it to take place.
* **Monitoring Time**: Enter the specific time of day for the monitoring, if known.
* **Assessor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the assessor, for follow-up or scheduling purposes. (Note: This field is optional, but can be useful for scheduling and coordination.)
