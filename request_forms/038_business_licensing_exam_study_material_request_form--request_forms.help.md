# Business Licensing Exam Study Material Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request study materials related to a business licensing exam. It is designed to gather essential information from the requester to ensure that they receive the relevant materials in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Requester name" field.
2. Provide your valid contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
3. Specify which licensing exam you are preparing for in the "License exam type" field.
4. Choose your preferred method of receiving the study materials in the "Delivery method" field.
5. If you have selected "Postal mail" as your preferred delivery method, fill in your postal address in the "Postal address" field.
6. If you have selected "Number of copies" as an option that applies to you, fill in the number of printed materials you need.
7. Add any additional comments or requests in the "Additional notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (required): This field is used to capture your full name for the sake of identification and contact purposes.
* **Contact email** (required): This field is necessary to send the study materials to your email address. Please ensure that it's a valid contact email.
* **License exam type** (required): Specify which licensing exam you are preparing for so that we can provide you with the relevant study materials.
* **Delivery method** (required): Choose your preferred method for receiving the study materials: Email, Postal mail, or Download link.
* **Postal address** (optional): If you have selected "Postal mail" as your preferred delivery method, provide your complete postal address for us to send the materials.
* **Number of copies** (optional): If you have selected "Postal mail" as your preferred delivery method and need multiple copies of the materials, specify the number of copies you require.
* **Additional notes** (optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any further instructions or requests related to your study materials.
