# Refund Status Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to initiate a conversation with the support team about the status of your refund. Please provide detailed information to help us resolve your issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate status for your refund request from the "Request Refund Status" field.
2. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your order number in the "Order ID" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with your refund in the "Description of Issue" field.
6. Select the current status of your refund from the "Current Refund Status" field.
7. If you have any additional information to share, enter it in the "Additional Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Refund Status** (select multiple): 
    This field allows you to select the status of your refund request. Select either "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the current state of your request.
* **Contact Name** (text): 
    This is your name, used to identify you in our records.
* **Contact Email** (email): 
    This is your email address, used for us to contact you about your issue.
* **Order ID** (number): 
    This is your order number, used to track your order.
* **Description of Issue** (text): 
    Describe the issue you are experiencing with your refund in this field.
* **Current Refund Status** (select one): 
    Select the current status of your refund from the options provided.
* **Additional Message** (text): 
    This is an optional field where you can provide any further information you think would be helpful to resolve your issue.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide detailed information in the "Description of Issue" field to help us understand your issue.
- If you have any supporting documentation or attachments, please include them with your message.
- Our support team will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
