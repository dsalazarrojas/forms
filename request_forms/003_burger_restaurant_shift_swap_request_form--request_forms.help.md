# Burger Restaurant Shift Swap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a shift swap, allowing them to switch their shift with another colleague. It is a required field for employees to provide their information and to ensure that the shift swap is properly coordinated and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the **Employee name** field.
2. Enter your internal staff identifier if you have one in the **Employee id** field.
3. Select the date of your current shift in the **Current shift date** field.
4. Enter the start and end time of your current shift in the **Current shift time** field.
5. Enter the name of the employee you propose to swap with in the **Requested swap with** field.
6. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the swap in the **Reason for swap** field.
7. Select whether the swap requires manager approval or not in the **Manager approval needed** field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact phone** field.
9. Add any additional notes for scheduling team in the **Additional notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name**: Enter your full name as it appears in your employee profile.
* **Employee id**: Enter your internal staff identifier if you have one, this helps the scheduling team to easily identify you in their system.
* **Current shift date**: Enter the date of your current shift, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current shift time**: Enter the start and end time of your current shift, in the format hh:mm AM/PM, e.g. 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM.
* **Requested swap with**: Enter the name of the employee you propose to swap with, this should be the name of the employee you are trying to switch shifts with.
* **Reason for swap**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the swap, this helps the scheduling team to understand your request.
* **Manager approval needed**: Select 'True' if this swap requires manager approval, otherwise select 'False'.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number, this is used for quick coordination and communication.
* **Additional notes**: Add any additional information that can help the scheduling team to process your request efficiently.

## Tips
* Make sure you are not duplicating an existing request.
* Ensure that you are not submitting the form for a shift that is already filled or scheduled.
* Double-check that you are providing accurate information to avoid any misunderstandings.
