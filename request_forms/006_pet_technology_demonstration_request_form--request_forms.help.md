# Pet Technology Demonstration Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for pet technology demonstrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct category that best describes the demonstration you are interested in.
3. Provide your contact email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Description" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Technology Demonstration Request Form** (`pet_technology_demo_request_form`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to describe the type of pet technology demonstration you are interested in. Please make sure to enter a clear and concise description of the demonstration.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required)
	+ This field is used to select the category that best describes the demonstration you are interested in. Please choose from the list of available categories.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
	+ This field is used to enter your contact email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to enter your contact phone number. Please make sure to enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required)
	+ This field is used to enter your company name. Please enter your company name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter your location. Please enter the city and state where you are located.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter any additional comments or questions you may have. Please make sure to keep your description clear and concise.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your request and get in touch with you as soon as possible to schedule the demonstration.
