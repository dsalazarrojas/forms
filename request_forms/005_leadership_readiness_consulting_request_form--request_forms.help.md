# Leadership Readiness Consulting Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request leadership consulting services to address specific leadership development, organizational strategy, change management, operational excellence, talent management, or digital transformation needs. By completing this form, you will be able to provide us with the necessary information to assess your needs and provide tailored consulting services to support your leadership readiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the leadership consulting needs that you require in the "Leadership Consulting Needs" field.
5. Choose your preferred method of contact in the "Preferred Method of Contact" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your name to help us identify you and provide personalized support.
* **Email (text, required: false)**: Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding your consulting request.
* **Phone Number (number, required: false)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you via phone.
* **Leadership Consulting Needs (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select one or more of the following options to identify your leadership consulting needs:
	+ Leadership Development
	+ Organizational Strategy
	+ Change Management
	+ Operational Excellence
	+ Talent Management
	+ Digital Transformation
	+ Business Process Improvement
* **Preferred Method of Contact (select_one, required: false)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Chat
	+ Meeting
* **Comments (note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your consulting request.
* **Submit (text, required: false)**: Click this button to submit your consulting request.
