# simulation_log_copy_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a copy of a simulation log. It will collect information about your team members, request details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form and fill in all the required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
3. Fill in any additional information in the text fields.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the form and click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Simulation Log**: A text field to enter a brief description of the simulation log you are requesting.
* **Team Members**: A selection of team members with whom you work, if any. You can select multiple team members.
* **Request Details**: A text field to enter a brief summary of what you are requesting.
* **Requested By**: A selection of whether you are requesting for yourself or someone else.
* **Review Type**: A selection of whether you would like to review the simulation log in real-time or not.
* **Review Date**: A date field to enter the date you would like to review the simulation log.
* **Review Time**: A time field to enter the time you would like to review the simulation log.
* **Review Duration**: A number field to enter the duration of the review.
* **Reviewers**: A selection of who will review the simulation log.
* **Notes**: A text field to enter any additional information.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter your date of birth.
* **Data Collection**: A selection of whether data collection is required.
* **Data Collection Date**: A date field to enter the date of data collection.
* **Data Collection Time**: A time field to enter the time of data collection.
* **Data Collection Duration**: A number field to enter the duration of data collection.
* **Data Collection Frequency**: A selection of how often data collection will occur.
* **Data Collection Frequency Type**: A selection of the type of data collection.
* **Comments**: A text field to enter any comments.
* **Submit**: A button to submit your request.
* **Confirm**: A text field to confirm your request.
* **Cancel**: A button to cancel your request.
* **Back**: A button to go back.
* **Next**: A button to go to the next page.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to understand. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our support team.
