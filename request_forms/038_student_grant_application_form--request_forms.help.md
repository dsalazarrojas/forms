# student_grant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to apply for grants. It collects necessary information to assess their eligibility and review their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details on page 1 of the form.
2. Provide your contact information and education history on page 1.
3. Share your school information and financial details on page 1.
4. List any relevant education certifications you've earned on page 1.
5. Provide information about your financial aid on page 1.
6. Finally, submit your grant application form on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you'll enter your basic personal information, such as your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): In this field, provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so we can reach you for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Education History** (`Education History`, `text`, required: false): Describe your educational background, including any relevant courses, degrees, or diplomas you've earned.
* **School Info** (`School Info`, `text`, required: false): Share information about the schools you've attended, including the name, location, and dates of attendance.
* **Financial Info** (`Financial Info`, `text`, required: false): Here, you'll provide information about your income, expenses, and financial situation that may affect your grant eligibility.
* **Education Certifications** (`Education Certifications`, `text`, required: false): If you have any relevant education certifications, such as language proficiency or specialized skills, list them here.
* **Financial Aid Info** (`Financial Aid Info`, `text`, required: false): Describe any financial aid you've received or are currently receiving, including scholarships, grants, or loans.
* **Student Grant Info** (`Student Grant Info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to specify the grant you're applying for and any relevant supporting documents you're attaching.
* **Submission Form** (`Submission Form`, `text`, required: false): You'll submit your grant application form here, attaching any required documents or supporting materials.

## Tips
Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
