# legal_document_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "legal_document_access_request_form" is a form for requesting access to legal documents. It is intended to be used by authorized personnel within an organization to ensure that the necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently. This form serves as a centralized platform for submitting requests, reducing errors and ensuring that all required details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of legal document you are requesting access to from the "legal_document_type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the document ID, if applicable.
3. Provide your name as it should be displayed on the document.
4. Enter the name of the organization you are requesting access to.
5. Enter the address of the organization you are requesting access to.
6. Enter the date you are requesting access to, if applicable.
7. Specify the purpose of your request.
8. Enter your contact email address.
9. Enter your contact phone number.
10. Choose the tool assigned to process your request from the "assigned_tool" dropdown menu.
11. If additional information is required to process your request, provide it in the "additional_information" field.
