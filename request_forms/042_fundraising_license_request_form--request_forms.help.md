# Fundraising License Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Fundraising License Request Form is a tool used to request a license for fundraising purposes. This form is intended for individuals or organizations seeking to raise funds for a specific cause or project. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary information required to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field as instructed below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Once you have filled out the form, submit it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, Required: False):
  A text field to input your name.
* **email** (Email, Required: True):
  A field to input your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address to facilitate communication with us.
* **organization** (Select One, Required: True):
  Select from the provided options or type "Other" to describe your organization.
* **description** (Text, Required: False):
  A text field to add any additional information about your request.
* **amount** (Number, Required: True):
  The amount you are requesting to be licensed for fundraising purposes.
* **contact_number** (Text, Required: True):
  Your contact number for follow-up communication.
* **contact_email** (Email, Required: False):
  A field to input your email address to contact you for follow-up communication.
* **extra_info** (Text, Required: False):
  Additional information you would like to provide about your request.
