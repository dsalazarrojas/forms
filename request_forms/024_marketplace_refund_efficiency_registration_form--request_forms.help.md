# Marketplace Refund Efficiency Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their refund requests, allowing for efficient and accurate refund processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure your refund request is processed efficiently.
2. Provide valid reasons for requesting a refund, such as issues with your order or problems with a product.
3. Be prepared to provide additional information or documentation as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (`1`, number, required: false): Enter the number of your order that you would like to request a refund for. This helps our team quickly identify your order and process your refund.
* **Customer Name** (`2`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your order. This is used to verify your identity and ensure we are processing the correct refund.
* **Customer Email** (`3`, email, required: false): Enter the email address associated with your order. This helps us contact you with updates on your refund status.
* **Refund Reason** (`4`, text, required: false): Describe the reason you are requesting a refund, such as a product defect or shipping issue.
* **Product Condition** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the condition of the product you are requesting a refund for, such as "like new", "gently used", or "used - major scratches".
* **Photo 1** (`6`, text, required: false): Upload a photo of the product that supports your refund reason, if applicable.
* **Photo 2** (`7`, text, required: false): Upload another photo of the product that supports your refund reason, if applicable.
* **Photo 3** (`8`, text, required: false): Upload additional photos of the product that support your refund reason, if applicable.
* **Photo 4** (`9`, text, required: false): Upload more photos of the product that support your refund reason, if applicable.
* **Photo 5** (`10`, text, required: false): Upload any final photos of the product that support your refund reason, if applicable.
* **Customer Phone** (`11`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Customer Message** (`12`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about your refund request.
* **Customer Address** (`13`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery of any replaced or exchanged items.
* **Order Status** (`14`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of your order, such as "new", "used", "refunded", or "cancelled".
* **Order ID** (`15`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of your order, if known.
* **Refund Reason Description** (`16`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the reason for your refund request, if not already described above.
