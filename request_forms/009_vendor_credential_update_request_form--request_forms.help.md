# Vendor Credential Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Credential Update Request Form is designed to allow vendors to request updates to their credentials. This form is used to report lost or stolen certificates, expired certificates, or to correct duplicate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Select the type of request**: Choose from the drop-down menu whether you are submitting an Initial Request or an Update Request.
2.  Select the reason for your request: You can choose from the options "Lost or Stolen Certificate", "Expired Certificate", "Other", or "Duplicate or Incorrect Information".
3.  Attach any supporting documentation: If necessary, you can upload relevant documents to support your request.
4.  Provide vendor information: Enter your vendor name, contact information, email, and address.
5.  Enter any additional comments or information: This field can be used to provide any additional comments or information to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Request Type** (Initial Request, Update Request): This field allows you to select the type of request you are submitting. Initial Requests are for new requests, while Update Requests are for changes to existing requests.
*   **Reason For Request** (Lost or Stolen Certificate, Expired Certificate, Other, Duplicate or Incorrect Information): This field allows you to specify the reason for your request. Choose the option that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
*   **Supporting Documentation**: This field is used to attach any relevant documents that support your request. Please ensure that you attach all necessary documents to ensure a smooth processing experience.
*   **Vendor Name**: This field is used to enter your company's name.
*   **Vendor Contact Info**: This field is used to enter your company's contact information.
*   **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: This field is used to enter your company's phone number.
*   **Address**: This field is used to enter your company's address.
*   **Additional Comments**: This field can be used to provide any additional comments or information to support your request.
