# Vastu Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a consultation or meeting with a representative from our organization. We use this information to schedule a suitable appointment time and ensure that you receive the best possible support for your property needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the property details page.
2. Select the type of property you are interested in.
3. Choose your preferred appointment time.
4. Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or SMS).
5. Enter your contact number and email (if applicable).
6. Click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the property you are interested in.
* **Property Age** (`property_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Residential
	+ Commercial
	+ Industrial
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_appointment_time`, date, optional): Select a date for your preferred appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Day** (`preferred_appointment_day`, date, optional): Select a date for your preferred appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_appointment_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your preferred appointment.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ SMS
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that we can process your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us directly.
