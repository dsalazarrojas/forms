# Indoor Air Purifier Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the installation of an indoor air purifier for a specific unit in the building.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide all necessary information.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unit Address** (`unit_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the unit where the air purifier is to be installed.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information. This will help building management contact you to discuss the installation.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for building management to contact you regarding the installation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for building management to contact you regarding the installation.
* **Unit Size** (`unit_size`, text, required/optional): Enter the size of your unit (e.g. studio, 1 bedroom, 2 bedrooms, etc.).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time when you would like the installation to take place.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a specific time slot you are available for the installation.

# Tips
* If you are not sure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the building management for clarification.
* If you are filling out the form for multiple units, please submit one form per unit.
* Please provide as much information as possible to make it easier for us to process your request.
