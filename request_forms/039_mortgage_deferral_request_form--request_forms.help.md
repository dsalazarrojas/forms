# Mortgage Deferral Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who have been approved for a mortgage and are requesting to postpone their payments due to a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Type in the reason for your deferral request in the "Reason for Deferral" field.
5. If you are currently in a mortgage, select the correct option in the "Current Mortgage" field.
6. Enter the date you would like to defer your mortgage payments in the "Deferral Date" field.
7. Enter the amount you would like to defer in the "Amount Deferrred" field.
8. Enter the total value of your mortgage in the "Total Mortgage" field.
9. Select the date when you would like to end the deferral in the "End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Enter your full name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address that we have on file.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number that we have on file.
* **Reason for Deferral (Reason for Deferral, text, required)**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your deferral request.
* **Current Mortgage (Current Mortgage, select_multiple, optional)**: If you are currently in a mortgage, select "Yes" to indicate that you are currently paying your mortgage.
* **Deferral Date (Deferral Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you would like to start your deferral.
* **Amount Deferrred (Amount Deferrred, number, required)**: Enter the amount you would like to defer.
* **Total Mortgage (Total Mortgage, number, required)**: Enter the total value of your mortgage.
* **End Date (End Date, date, required)**: Select the date when you would like to end the deferral.
