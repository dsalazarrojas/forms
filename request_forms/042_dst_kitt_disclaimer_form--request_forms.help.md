# DST KITT Disclaimer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DST KITT Disclaimer Form is used to gather information for a specific purpose. To ensure that your submission is relevant and not a duplicate, please review the form purpose and only submit if you are eligible to do so. The form purpose should be clearly understood before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form purpose and ensure it is relevant to your situation.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you are entering the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form 1** (`request_form_1`, number, optional): This field is used to gather numerical information.
* **Select One** (`request_form_2`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`request_form_3`, select_multiple, optional): Please select multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (`request_form_4`, date, optional): Enter a date in the correct format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time** (`time_form`, time, optional): Enter a time in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Note** (`note_form`, note, optional): Enter a brief note or comment about your submission.
* **Email** (`email_form`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_form`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Another Select One** (`request_form_9`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`request_form_10`, select_multiple, optional): Please select multiple options from the list.
* **Another Date** (`request_form_11`, date, optional): Enter another date in the correct format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Another Time** (`another_time_form`, time, optional): Enter another time in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Another Note** (`another_note_form`, note, optional): Enter another brief note or comment about your submission.
* **Another Email** (`another_email_form`, email, optional): Enter another email address.
* **Another Phone** (`another_phone_form`, text, optional): Enter another phone number.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
