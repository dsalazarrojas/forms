# Equipment Return Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equipment Return Form is used to submit requests for equipment return. Please ensure that you have read and understood the guidelines before proceeding. This form is intended for [staff/personnel] and [students] to report equipment that is broken, lost, or needs to be returned to the [department] or [location]. If you have already submitted a similar request, please do not resubmit. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the guidelines and ensure you are submitting a new request.
* Select "Yes" for the request form question.
* Provide accurate information in the text fields (equipment_details, teacher_details, request_details, and return_details).
* Choose the correct school department(s) and staff member(s) involved.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for the staff_details question.
* Select "Yes" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form (1)**: 
    This field is used to indicate whether you are requesting to return equipment or not.
* **Equipment Details (2)**: 
    Enter a brief description of the equipment being returned, including its location and the reason for return.
* **Teacher Details (3)**:
    Please provide the name and department of the teacher responsible for the equipment.
* **School Department (4)**: 
    Select the school department that the equipment belongs to or is being returned to.
* **Request Details (5)**: 
    Describe the specific reasons for returning the equipment or any supporting documentation.
* **Return Details (6)**: 
    Provide any additional details or comments about the return process.
* **Staff Details (7)**: 
    Select the staff member(s) responsible for the equipment or return process.
* **Submit (8)**: 
    Review and confirm that the form is complete and accurate before submitting it.

Note: This form is only for reporting equipment that is broken, lost, or needs to be returned to the department or location.
