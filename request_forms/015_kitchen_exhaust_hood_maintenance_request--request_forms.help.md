# Kitchen Exhaust Hood Maintenance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a kitchen exhaust hood maintenance request. It is used by facilities management teams to gather details about the maintenance needs of kitchen exhaust hoods in various locations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of the maintenance need in the **Maintenance Request** field.
2.  Specify the location of the hood in the **Location** field.
3.  Indicate if the maintenance is for a new installation or a repair in the **Type** field.
4.  Provide a more detailed description of the maintenance need in the **Description** field.
5.  Enter the name of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment** field.
6.  Enter the model of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Model** field.
7.  Enter the serial number of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Serial Number** field.
8.  Enter a brief description of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Description** field.
9.  Enter the location of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Location** field.
10. Enter the size of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Size** field.
11. Enter the condition of the equipment or hood in the **Equipment Condition** field.
12. Specify the priority of the maintenance request in the **Priority** field.
13. Enter the date the maintenance is needed by in the **Date** field.
14. Enter the time of the maintenance in the **Time** field.
15. Enter the name of the person making the request in the **Requestor** field.
16. Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
17. Enter the email address of the person making the request in the **Requested By** field.
18. Enter the contact number of the person making the request in the **Contact Number** field.
19. Enter the name of the person making the request in the **Contact Name** field.
20. Enter the name of the facility where the hood is located in the **Facility Name** field.
21. Enter the location of the facility in the **Facility Location** field.
22. Enter a unique identifier for the location in the **Location ID** field.
23. Enter the type of service or maintenance required in the **Service Type** field.
24. Enter the type of equipment or hood in the **Equipment Type** field.
