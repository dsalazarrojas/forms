# Vehicle Transport Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a vehicle transport quote request. It is designed to be filled out by customers or users who need to transport vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of vehicle being transported from the "Vehicle Type" field.
2. Enter the pickup location of the vehicle in the "Origin Location" field.
3. Enter the drop off location of the vehicle in the "Destination Location" field.
4. Select the mode of transportation from the "Type of Transport" field.
5. Select the size of the vehicle from the "Vehicle Size" field.
6. Enter the estimated weight of the vehicle in the "Estimated Weight" field.
7. Enter the length of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Length" field.
8. Enter the width of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Width" field.
9. Enter the height of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Height" field.
10. Enter the weight of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Weight" field.
11. Enter any additional cargo information in the "Additional Cargo Information" field.
12. Enter the estimated cost of the transport in the "Estimated Cost" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type (Auto, Truck, Motorcycle, etc.)**: Select the type of vehicle being transported.
* **Origin Location**: Enter the pickup location of the vehicle.
* **Destination Location**: Enter the drop off location of the vehicle.
* **Type of Transport (Air, Ground)**: Select the mode of transportation.
* **Vehicle Size (Small, Medium, Large)**: Select the size of the vehicle.
* **Estimated Weight**: Enter the estimated weight of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length**: Enter the length of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Width**: Enter the width of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Height**: Enter the height of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Weight**: Enter the weight of the vehicle.
* **Additional Cargo Information**: Enter any additional cargo information.
* **Estimated Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of the transport.
