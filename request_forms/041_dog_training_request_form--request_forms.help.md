# Dog Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Request Form is a tool used to request dog training sessions. It allows you to provide information about your dog and its needs to help us better serve you and your furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your full name, dog breed, and any additional comments you may have.
2. Select the date and time for your desired training session.
3. Choose the clinic and veterinarian you would like to work with.
4. Select the duration of your appointment.
5. Identify the size of your dog.
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): Enter the breed of your dog.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to schedule your dog's training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to schedule your dog's training session.
* **Choose Clinic** (`clinic_choice`, select_one, required): Choose one of our clinics:
	+ Clinic 1
	+ Clinic 2
	+ Clinic 3
* **Choose Veterinarian** (`veterinarian_choice`, select_one, required): Choose one of our veterinarians:
	+ Veterinarian 1
	+ Veterinarian 2
	+ Veterinarian 3
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, select_multiple, required): Select the duration of your appointment:
	+ Half an hour
	+ One hour
	+ Two hours
* **Dog Size** (`dog_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your dog:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your dog's training needs or concerns.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure you select both a clinic and veterinarian for your appointment.
* Make sure to provide accurate information about your dog's breed, size, and any health concerns.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please include them in the additional comments section.
