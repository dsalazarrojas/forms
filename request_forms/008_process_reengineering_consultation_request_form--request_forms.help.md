# Process Reengineering Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from you about your process and provide a structured way to express your needs and expectations for a process reengineering consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Enter your contact details to ensure we can reach out to you for further discussion.
3. Select all objectives you are interested in achieving through the process reengineering consultation.
4. Describe your current challenges and areas of concern in the "Challenges" field.
5. Choose your preferred meeting time and date, taking into account your schedule and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false):
	* This is the contact information we will use to reach out to you for further discussion and to provide feedback on your consultation.
- **Objectives** (`objectives`, select_multiple, required: false):
	* Select all the objectives you are interested in achieving through the process reengineering consultation. This may include:
		+ Financial Savings
		+ Operational Efficiency
		+ Cost Reduction
- **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false):
	* Describe your current challenges and areas of concern in a few sentences. This will help us better understand the context and provide tailored solutions.
- **Preferred Meeting Time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, time, required: false):
	* Choose a time that works best for you. Please note that meeting times are subject to change based on the availability of our experts.
- **Preferred Meeting Date** (`preferred_meeting_date`, date, required: false):
	* Select a date that works best for you. Please note that meeting dates are subject to change based on the availability of our experts.
- **Preferred Meeting Time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, time, required: false):
	* Choose a time that works best for you. Please note that meeting times are subject to change based on the availability of our experts.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that we can better understand your needs.
- If you have any additional information or resources that you would like to attach, please do so in the "Additional Information" field (not included in this form).
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
