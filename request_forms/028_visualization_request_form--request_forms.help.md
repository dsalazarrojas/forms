# visualization_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your visualization request, ensuring that the design team has all necessary details to create an effective and efficient design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields:
    *   Category: Select the category that best describes your request (Visualization, Design, or Development).
    *   Service Type: Choose the type of service you require (Visualization, Design, or Development).
    *   Priority: Choose the priority level of your request (High, Medium, or Low).
2.  Fill in the optional fields:
    *   Business: A brief description of the project's business context.
    *   Name: Your name or the name of the person representing the business.
    *   Email: Your email address or the contact email of the person representing the business.
    *   Phone Number: The phone number of the person representing the business.
    *   Phone Number: The phone number of the person representing the business (This field is identical to id 4 and might be redundant).
    *   Description: A detailed description of the project and its requirements.
    *   Designer Requirements: Any specific requirements for the design team to keep in mind.
    *   Business Description: A more detailed description of the project and its business context.
    *   Project Details: Additional information about the project that might be helpful for the design team.
    *   Expected Delivery Date: The date by which you expect to receive the design.
    *   Expected Delivery Time: The time by which you expect to receive the design.
    *   Budget: The budget allocated for the design project.
    *   Deadline: The deadline for the design project.
    *   Comments: Any additional comments or notes for the design team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes your request (Visualization, Design, or Development).
*   **Service Type** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of service you require (Visualization, Design, or Development).
*   **Business** (`1`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the project's business context.
*   **Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Your name or the name of the person representing the business.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, optional): Your email address or the contact email of the person representing the business.
*   **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the person representing the business.
*   **Designer Requirements** (`8`, `note`, optional): Any specific requirements for the design team to keep in mind.
*   **Business Description** (`9`, `text`, optional): A more detailed description of the project and its business context.
*   **Project Details** (`10`, `text`, optional): Additional information about the project that might be helpful for the design team.
*   **Expected Delivery Date** (`11`, `date`, optional): The date by which you expect to receive the design.
*   **Expected Delivery Time** (`12`, `time`, optional): The time by which you expect to receive the design.
*   **Budget** (`13`, `number`, optional): The budget allocated for the design project.
*   **Deadline** (`14`, `date`, optional): The deadline for the design project.
*   **Priority** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level of your request (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Status** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
*   **Comments** (`17`, `note`, optional): Any additional comments or notes for the design team.
