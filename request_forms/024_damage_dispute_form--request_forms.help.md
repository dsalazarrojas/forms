# damage_dispute_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and report property damage disputes, allowing the company to efficiently collect and review relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of the property details, including any relevant information about the location and condition of the property.
2. Describe the damage in detail, including any circumstances surrounding the event or action that caused the damage.
3. Enter the date the damage occurred.
4. Enter the customer's contact details, including their name, email address, and phone number.
5. Upload any supporting photos of the damage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Describe the property where the damage occurred, including its address and condition.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage, including any circumstances surrounding the event or action that caused the damage.
* **Date of Damage** (`damage_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the damage occurred.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the customer, such as their contact method or other relevant contact details.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the damage.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who reported the damage.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the damage.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): Upload any supporting photos of the damage that can help to document the issue.
* **Photo 1** (`photo_one`, text, optional): Upload the first photo of the damage as supporting evidence.
* **Photo 2-** (`photo`, text, optional): Upload additional photos of the damage as supporting evidence.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to provide a detailed description of the damage to help resolve the issue efficiently.
- Upload clear and legible photos of the damage to support your claim.
- Ensure all fields are filled out as required, but do not worry if not all fields are relevant for your particular scenario.
