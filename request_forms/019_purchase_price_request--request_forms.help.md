# Purchase Price Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for customers to inquire about the price of a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information in a clear and concise manner.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields (if any).
3. If you have any additional information that you would like to share, you can use the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This is the field where you can enter your name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Items** (`items_wanted`, text, optional): Describe the product or service you are interested in purchasing.
* **Description** (`purchase_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what you are looking for.
* **Additional Info** (`contact_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information that you would like to share with us, please enter it here.
* **Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your request.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will respond to your request as soon as possible.
