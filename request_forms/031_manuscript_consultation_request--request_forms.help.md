# Manuscript Consultation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request consultation services for your manuscript. A manuscript consultation is a process where our team of experts review and provide feedback on your work to help you refine and improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Manuscript Consultation Request** (`manuscript_consultation_request`): This is the title of the request. Please enter a brief description of your manuscript consultation request.
2. **Project Details** (`project_details`): Please provide details about the project you are working on, including the genre, target audience, and any other relevant information.
3. **Manuscript Details** (`manuscript_details`): Describe the focus and content of your manuscript.
4. **Files** (`files`): Please attach relevant files related to your manuscript, such as drafts or previous versions.
5. **Session** (`session`): Describe the session format and style you prefer (e.g., in-person, phone, video, or online).
6. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you about your consultation.
7. **Writers Details** (`writers_details`): This field is optional, but if applicable, please provide details about the writers involved in your project.
8. **Editors Details** (`editors_details`): This field is also optional, but if applicable, please provide details about the editors involved in your project.
9. **Writing Coaches Details** (`writing_coaches_details`): This field is optional, but if applicable, please provide details about the writing coaches involved in your project.
10. **Session Time** (`session_time`): If you have a preferred time for the session, please let us know.
11. **Session Date** (`session_date`): If you have a preferred date for the session, please let us know.
12. **Manuscript ID** (`manuscript_id`): If you have a manuscript ID, please provide it.
