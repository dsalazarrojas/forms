# Request For Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Leave Of Absence Form is used to notify the organization about an employee's intention to take a leave of absence. This form ensures that the employee and the management are aware of the planned absence, facilitating a smooth work process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure that you have all necessary details about your leave such as start and end dates, reason for absence, and expected return date.
2.  Fill in your employee details such as name, email, and employee ID.
3.  Provide your manager's name and email for them to approve or decline your leave request.
4.  If you want to, you can provide additional comments or details.
5.  Finally, submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (`employee_name`):** Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or in company records.
* **Employee Email (`employee_email`):** Enter your official email address as per company records.
* **Start Date (`start_date`):** Choose the date when your leave starts.
* **End Date (`end_date`):** Choose the date when your leave ends.
* **Reason for Absence (`reason`):** Briefly state the reason for your absence.
* **Return Date (`return_date`):** Choose the date when you expect to return to work.
* **Expected Return Date (`expected_return_date`):** This field might already be filled in with the value of the previous "Return Date" field. You can edit this field if you have different expectations. 
* **Leave Length (`leave_length`):** This field calculates the number of work days in your leave. It is not editable.
* **Employee ID (`employee_id`):** Enter your employee ID as per company records.
* **Company ID (`company_id`):** This is usually your company's ID and is not required from the employee's side.
* **Manager Name (`manager_name`):** Enter your manager's name as per the company records.
* **Manager Email (`manager_email`):** Enter your manager's email address as per company records.
* **Department (`department`):** Enter your department in which you work (optional).
* **Position (`position`):** Enter your job position (optional).
* **Location (`location`):** Enter your location (optional).
* **Reason for Absence (`reason_for_absence`):** This field is optional for employee's to provide additional reasons.
* **Manager Comments (`manager_comments`):** Your manager might provide comments or feedback on your leave request.
* **Company Name (`company_name`):** Enter your company's name (optional).
* **Company Address (`company_address`):** Enter your company's address (optional).
* **Company Phone (`company_phone`):** Enter your company's phone number (optional).
* **Company Website (`company_website`):** Enter your company's website (optional).
* **Employee Comments (`employee_comments`):** You can provide any additional comments you may have.
* **Employee Signature (`employee_signature`):** This is a mandatory field where you sign to confirm the details you have provided.
* **Manager Signature (`manager_signature`):** This is a mandatory field where the manager signs to confirm the leave request.

Please ensure that you fill out this form with care, as it is a formal document. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your HR or concerned authorities.
