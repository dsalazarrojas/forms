# Airline Staff Safety Gear Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Airline Staff Safety Gear Requisition Form is designed to collect information from employees for the purpose of issuing safety gear to support their work activities. This form helps ensure that employees receive the necessary safety equipment for their specific needs, reducing potential safety risks and ensuring compliance with airline regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully and ensuring all required fields are complete before submitting the form.
2. Enter the required employee information, including:
	* Full Name
	* Employee ID Number
	* Department (select one from the options: Flight Crew, Ground Handling, Maintenance/Engineering, In-flight Services, Cargo Operations, or Security)
	* Job Title
3. Select the type(s) of safety gear that you need from the list provided. If multiple items are required, you can select more than one option.
4. If applicable, enter the item size for each selected gear type.
5. Provide a reason for the requisition, which could be for a new issue (initial supply), replacement (worn out), replacement (damaged), lost item, or size exchange.
6. If requesting a replacement or a damaged item, describe the damage in detail.
7. Specify the quantity required for each item.
8. Choose the urgency of the request, which could be urgent (operational safety risk), standard, or planned replacement.
9. Provide the name of your approving supervisor.
10. Confirm that the gear will be used for official duties only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### <i class="icon"></i> Employee Information <i class="icon"></i>

* **<i class="icon"></i> Full Name **: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Employee ID Number **: Provide your assigned employee identification number.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Department **: Choose your department from the select menu to help us better understand the required gear.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Job Title **: Enter your official job title to ensure we issue the correct gear for your responsibilities.

### <i class="icon"></i> Safety Gear Requisition <i class="icon"></i>

* **<i class="icon"></i> Type of Gear Requested **: Select the type(s) of safety gear you need from the list to help us prepare the necessary equipment.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Item Sizes (if applicable) **: If applicable, enter the size for each gear type selected.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Reason for Requisition **: Choose why you are requesting the safety gear, which could be for new issue (initial supply), replacement (worn out), replacement (damaged), lost item, or size exchange.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Description of Damage (if replacement requested) **: If you selected a reason for replacement, describe the damage in detail to help us understand the issue.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Quantity for each Item **: Specify the quantity required for each gear type selected.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Request Urgency **: Choose the urgency of the request, which could be urgent (operational safety risk), standard, or planned replacement.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Name of Approving Supervisor **: Provide the name of your approving supervisor.
* **<i class="icon"></i> Confirm that the gear will be used for official duties only **: Confirm that the safety gear will only be used for official purposes.

## Tips

* If you have any questions or need assistance with the requisition process, please contact your supervisor or safety officer.
* Ensure all fields are complete and accurate to avoid delays in processing your request.
