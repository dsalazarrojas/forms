**Request for Additional Information Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect additional information from clients or stakeholders about their requests. It is used to gather specific details that are not included in the standard request form. The form is essential for ensuring that all necessary information is gathered to process the request correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Select the relevant Client, Stakeholder, or Request Type from the dropdown menus.
3.  Enter the required information in the text fields (e.g., service name, service type, date, and time).
4.  Provide any additional comments or attachments as necessary.
5.  Verify that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Request Form**: Select the client who requested the form from the list.
*   **Stakeholder Request Form**: Select the stakeholders who are involved in the request from the list.
*   **Additional Form Information**: Enter any additional details about the request.
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the request.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the service or request.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the service or request.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the client or stakeholder.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client or stakeholder.
*   **Request Form Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the request.
*   **Attachment**: Attach any supporting documents or files as needed.
*   **Additional Details 3**: Enter any additional details about the request.
*   **Request Type**: Select the type of request (e.g., Option A, Option B, or Option C).
*   **Service Name**: Enter the name of the service or request.
*   **Service Type**: Enter the type of service or request.
*   **Service Date**: Enter the date of the service or request.
*   **Service Time**: Enter the time of the service or request.
*   **Request Form Service Name 2**: Enter the name of the service or request (if necessary).
*   **Request Form Service Type 2**: Enter the type of service or request (if necessary).
*   **Request Form Service Date 2**: Enter the date of the service or request (if necessary).
*   **Request Form Service Time 2**: Enter the time of the service or request (if necessary).
*   **Request Form Attachment 2**: Attach any supporting documents or files as needed.
*   **Request Form Comments 3**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the request.
*   **Request Form Email 2**: Enter the email address of the client or stakeholder (if necessary).

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
