# Backdate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backdate Request Form is used to request a change in the date of a record or transaction. This ensures that records are accurate and consistent.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the fields as follows:
- Enter your name and department.
- Provide the Reference ID for the record or transaction.
- Enter the current date on the record or transaction.
- Enter the requested retrospective date.
- Select the primary reason for the backdate request.
- Provide a detailed justification for the backdate request, explaining why this change is necessary and accurate.
- Describe any supporting documentation or files that justify this request.
- Indicate the potential impact of this change on other records.
- Enter the name of the approving manager.
- Certify that this request is made to ensure record accuracy and agree to the terms of misrepresentation.
- Date and time the request was submitted.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Internal Transaction/Record Backdate Request --**: This is a heading, not a form field. Please ignore this.
* **Name of Requester**: Enter the name of the person requesting the backdate.
* **Department**: Enter the department of the person requesting the backdate.
* **Record or Transaction Reference ID**: Enter the ID number of the record or transaction to be backdated.
* **Current Date on Record**: Enter the current date on the record or transaction.
* **Requested New (Retrospective) Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the backdate to be changed to.
* **Primary Reason for Request**: Select one of the following reasons:
	+ Clerical/Entry Error
	+ Delayed System Availability
	+ Document Received After Posting
	+ Correction of Previous Entry
	+ Other (Explain below)
* **Detailed Justification for Backdate**: Explain in as much detail as possible why this change is necessary and accurate.
* **Supporting Documentation Description**: Describe any files or emails that justify this request. Although this field is optional, it's recommended you attach relevant supporting documentation.
* **Impact of Change**: Indicate how this change will affect other records or financial reports.
* **Name of Approving Manager**: Enter the name of the manager who needs to approve this request.
* **I certify and agree**: Select 'I certify and agree' if you agree that this request is made to ensure record accuracy.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date and time of when you submitted this request. 

## Tips
- Make sure to explain thoroughly why the backdate is necessary and accurate.
- Provide supporting documentation where possible.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals in place before submitting this form.
- Keep a record of your submitted requests.
- If the request is for someone else, make sure the manager's name is accurate.
