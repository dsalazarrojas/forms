# Property Boundary Survey Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Boundary Survey Request form is used to submit a request for a property boundary survey. This form gathers information about the surveyor's details, site visit details, and any other relevant information needed for the survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the surveyor's information, including their name, phone number, and email.
2. Enter the property address and ID.
3. Select the request date and time.
4. Choose whether or not to conduct a site visit.
5. If conducting a site visit, enter the site visit date, time, and location.
6. Enter any follow-up details, including whether or not to follow up and the follow-up date, time, and location.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
8. Enter the email and phone number of the person submitting the request.
9. Select the submitted by status.
10. Enter the submitted by name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Request** (`Survey Request`, text, optional): A brief description of the survey request.
* **Surveyor Info** (`Surveyor Info`, number, optional): The surveyor's information, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): The address of the property being surveyed.
* **Property ID** (`Property ID`, number, optional): The ID of the property being surveyed.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, optional): The date the survey request was made.
* **Request Time** (`Request Time`, time, optional): The time the survey request was made.
* **Site Visit** (`Site Visit`, select_one, optional): Choose whether or not to conduct a site visit.
* **Site Visit Date** (`Site Visit Date`, date, optional): The date of the site visit.
* **Site Visit Time** (`Site Visit Time`, time, optional): The time of the site visit.
* **Site Visit Location** (`Site Visit Location`, text, optional): The location of the site visit.
* **Site Visit Description** (`Site Visit Description`, text, optional): A brief description of the site visit.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether or not to follow up and enter the follow-up details.
* **Follow Up Date** (`Follow Up Date`, date, optional): The date of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow Up Time** (`Follow Up Time`, time, optional): The time of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow Up Location** (`Follow Up Location`, text, optional): The location of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow Up Description** (`Follow Up Description`, text, optional): A brief description of the follow-up visit.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): The email of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Surveyor Name** (`Surveyor Name`, text, optional): The name of the surveyor.
* **Surveyor Phone** (`Surveyor Phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the surveyor.
* **Site Visit Status** (`Site Visit Status`, select_one, optional): The status of the site visit (Active or Inactive).
* **Surveyor Email** (`Surveyor Email`, email, optional): The email of the surveyor.
* **Additional Surveyor Info** (`Additional Surveyor Info`, text, optional): Any additional information about the surveyor.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one, optional): Choose whether or not the request was submitted by someone else.
* **Submitted By Name** (`Submitted By Name`, text, optional): The name of the person submitting the request.
