# Password Reset Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a password reset when you have forgotten your password. Fill out this form to initiate the password reset process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" to confirm that you are requesting a password reset.
2. Enter your email address that is associated with your account.
3. Enter your username.
4. Enter your desired new password.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit** (`form_control`, yes/no, required: false): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are requesting a password reset. This will submit your request and trigger the password reset process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address that is associated with your account. This will help the system identify you and send a password reset link to this email address.
* **Username** (`username`, text, required: false): Enter your username to help the system identify you and associate the password reset request with your account.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required: false): Enter your desired new password. This will replace your current password after the reset process is complete.
