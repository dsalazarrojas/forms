# Co Working Station Reassignment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Co Working Station Reassignment Request Form is a tool for employees to request a change in their coworking station. This form helps gather information about the reason for the request, the desired station, and any additional details that the facilities team may need to know.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid reason for your request, as manager approval is required.
2. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or personnel file.
3. Enter the number or location of your current station.
4. Specify the desired station or location for your reassignment.
5. Provide a brief explanation for your request, including any necessary details for the facilities team.
6. If applicable, specify a date when you would like to start working at the new station.
7. Select the status of your manager's approval from the options provided.
8. If necessary, provide contact information for the facilities team to reach you.
9. If applicable, mention any special equipment or accessibility needs you may require.
10. Add any additional notes or information that the facilities team should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your ID or personnel file.
* **Current desk or station**: Enter the number or location of your current station, e.g., "Desk 123" or "Room 456".
* **Requested desk or station**: Specify the desired station or location for your reassignment, e.g., "Desk 789" or "Room 123".
* **Reason for request**: Provide a brief explanation for your request, including any necessary details for the facilities team. Please be clear and concise about your reasons.
* **Date reassignment needed**: If you have a specific date for your reassignment, please enter it in the format YYYY-MM-DD. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Manager approval**: Select the status of your manager's approval from the options provided (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Contact phone**: If you need to be contacted for further information, please provide your best contact phone number.
* **Accommodation needs**: If you require any special equipment or have accessibility needs, please mention them here.
* **Additional notes**: Add any additional information that the facilities team should be aware of, such as equipment you may need to bring to the new station.
