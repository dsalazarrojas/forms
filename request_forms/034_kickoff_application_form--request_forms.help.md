# Kickoff Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kickoff Application Form is a crucial part of the project management process. It enables us to gather and process the necessary information about new projects before they start. This form is a single-page form designed to collect project details from team members and project managers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Project Info" section, where you can provide a brief description of the project. This field is optional.
2. In the "Request Info" section, you can give additional details about the project's requirements.
3. Select one of the options in the "Team Info" dropdown menu to indicate whether you are the Team Lead or a Team Member.
4. In the "Project Description" field, describe the project in as much detail as possible.
5. Choose one or multiple options in the "Project Status" dropdown menu to indicate the project's current status.
6. Enter the names of all team members participating in the project in the "Team Members" field. This field is optional.
7. Enter the project's request deadline in the "Request Deadline" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
8. Select a meeting date from your calendar in the "Meeting Date" field.
9. Select a meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field.
10. If the project has a budget, enter it in the "Project Budget" field.
11. Choose one or multiple options in the "Project Status2" dropdown menu to indicate the project's status again.
12. Finally, select your role in the "Team Lead" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Info (Project Info)**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Request Info (Request Info)**: Provide any additional details about the project's requirements.
* **Team Info (Team Info)**: Indicate whether you are the Team Lead or a Team Member.
* **Project Description (Project Description)**: Describe the project in as much detail as possible.
* **Project Status (Project Status)**: Choose the current status of the project (Active, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Team Members (Team Members)**: Enter the names of all team members participating in the project.
* **Request Deadline (Request Deadline)**: Enter the project's request deadline in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Meeting Date (Meeting Date)**: Select a meeting date from your calendar.
* **Meeting Time (Meeting Time)**: Select a meeting time.
* **Project Budget (Project Budget)**: Enter the project's budget if it applies.
* **Project Status2 (Project Status2)**: Choose the current status of the project again (Active, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Team Lead (Team Lead)**: Select whether you are the Team Lead or a Team Member.
