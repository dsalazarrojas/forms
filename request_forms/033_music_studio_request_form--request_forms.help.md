# music_studio_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_studio_request_form is used to request a music-related service or product from the music studio. It's a simple form with several fields that help the music studio team understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Category of your request from the dropdown list.
2. Provide a brief Description of your request.
3. Enter the Client's name (if applicable).
4. Enter the Musician's name (if applicable).
5. Select the Date and Time for your request.
6. Specify the Duration of your request.
7. If your request requires an Output File, provide the file details.
8. Choose the Assigned Tool for your request (if applicable).
9. Add any Notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`music_studio_request_form_category`, select_one): Select the type of music-related service or product you're requesting.
* **Description** (`music_studio_request_form_description`, text): Briefly describe the music-related service or product you're looking for.
* **Client** (`music_studio_request_form_client`, text): Enter the client's name (if applicable).
* **Musician** (`music_studio_request_form_musician`, text): Enter the musician's name (if applicable).
* **Date** (`music_studio_request_form_date`, date): Select the date for your request.
* **Time** (`music_studio_request_form_time`, time): Select the time for your request.
* **Duration** (`music_studio_request_form_duration`, number): Specify the duration of your request in minutes.
* **Output File** (`music_studio_request_form_output_file`, text): If required, provide the output file details.
* **Assigned Tool** (`music_studio_request_form_assigned_tool`, select_one): Choose the tool assigned to your request (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`music_studio_request_form_notes`, text): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've filled out all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the music studio team for guidance.
* This form is for music-related services only; if you need assistance with other requests, please use the relevant form.
