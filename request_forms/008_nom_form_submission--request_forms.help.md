# NOM Form Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to aid in requesting a service or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and any supporting materials as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your request.
* **Nom Request Date** (`nom_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are requesting the service or support for.
* **Name** (`nom_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Phone Number** (`nom_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`nom_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Description** (`nom_description`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Upload File** (`nom_upload_file`, text, optional): If you have any supporting materials or documents to attach, please upload them here.
* **Organization** (`nom_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Department** (`nom_department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you are affiliated with.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for your contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting materials or documents, please attach them to the upload field.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, please contact the support team.
