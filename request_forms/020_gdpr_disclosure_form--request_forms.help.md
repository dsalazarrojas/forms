# GDPR Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Disclosure Form is a tool designed to inform users about the collection, storage, and usage of their personal data, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your data management practices.
2. Fill in the form completely and truthfully, as the information collected will be used to ensure compliance with GDPR guidelines.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your organization's data protection officer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Information Collection** (`information_collection`, text, optional): This section is used to describe the types of information that will be collected from users. Please explain what types of data will be collected, including what categories of personal data (e.g., contact information, browsing history, etc.).
* **What Information Will Be Used** (`purpose`, text, optional): In this section, explain what purposes the collected information will be used for, such as marketing, customer service, or product development.
* **Data Storage** (`data_storage`, text, optional): Describe where and how user data will be stored, such as on servers, in databases, or in the cloud.
* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, text, optional): Explain the measures in place to protect user data, including encryption, access controls, and data backup procedures.
* **Data Retention** (`data_retention`, text, optional): Explain how long user data will be kept and when it will be deleted or disposed of.
* **Data Sharing** (`data_sharing`, text, optional): Describe with whom and what data will be shared, such as with third-party service providers or partners.
* **Data Security** (`data_security`, text, optional): Explain the measures in place to secure user data, including firewalls, antivirus software, and data encryption.
* **Data Deletion** (`data_deletion`, text, optional): Describe the procedures for deleting user data, including when and how it will be done.
* **Data Amendment** (`data_amend`, text, optional): Explain how users can request changes or updates to their data, including the process and timeframe for such requests.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling in this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your organization's data protection officer for clarification.
- This form is an essential part of GDPR compliance, and your honesty is crucial for maintaining a secure and trustworthy relationship with our users.
