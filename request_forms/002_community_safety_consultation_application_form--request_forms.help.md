# Community Safety Consultation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals or organizations that are requesting a safety consultation. This form will be used to schedule and follow up on the consultation, and to understand the type of consultation requested and the specific location of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
2. Specify the type of consultation you are requesting, choosing from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief summary of your concerns or incidents.
4. Select your preferred consultation dates.
5. Specify the location of concern (e.g., address or nearest intersection).
6. Attach any supporting documents or evidence.
7. Select the urgency level for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name**: This is the name of the individual or organization requesting the consultation. Please provide your full name, including any titles or prefixes.
* **Contact email**: This is the email address that we will use to schedule and follow up on the consultation. Please ensure that this email is accurate and active.
* **Contact phone number**: This is your preferred phone number for urgent contact. If you don't have a phone number, please leave this field blank.
* **Organization name**: If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization. This is optional but helps us to understand the context of your request.
* **Type of consultation requested**: Select the primary focus area of your consultation from the provided options. This will help us to understand the type of consultation you need.
* **Summary of concerns or incidents**: Please provide a brief description of the safety issue you are concerned about. This will help us to understand the nature of your request.
* **Preferred consultation dates**: Provide 2 to 3 date options for your consultation. This will help us to schedule the consultation at a time convenient for you.
* **Location of concern**: Specify the address or nearest intersection that is related to your safety concern. This will help us to understand the specific location that needs to be addressed.
* **Supporting documents or evidence**: If you have any documents or evidence that can support your request, please describe them here.
* **Urgency level**: Select the urgency level for your request, from Low to Immediate. This will help us to prioritize your consultation accordingly.
