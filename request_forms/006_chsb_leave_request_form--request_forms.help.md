# CHSB Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a request for leave of absence from work, which must be submitted to your supervisor for review and approval. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your leave request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details:
   - **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your record.
   - **Employee ID**: Enter your staff identification number.
   - **Department**: Enter your current department.
   - **Position Title**: Enter your job title.
2. Select your **Leave Type**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Annual Leave, Sick Leave, etc.).
3. Provide leave dates:
   - **Leave Start Date**: Enter the first day of your leave.
   - **Leave End Date**: Enter the last day of your leave.
   - **Expected Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
   - **Total Days Requested**: Enter the total number of working days you are taking.
4. Provide additional information:
   - **Reason for Leave**: Enter a brief explanation for your leave request.
   - **Pending Projects or Tasks**: If you have any outstanding work, list it here.
   - **Coverage Arrangements**: If someone will be covering your duties, enter their name.
   - **Emergency Contact During Leave**: If you are reachable during your leave, enter your phone number.
   - **Will You Check Email**: Choose how often you will check your email during your leave.
   - **Any Previously Scheduled Leave**: Check if this leave request conflicts with previously scheduled leave.
5. Submit your request:
   - **Supervisor Approval**: Your supervisor will review and approve your request.
   - **Employee Signature**: Sign your name as confirmation of your request.
