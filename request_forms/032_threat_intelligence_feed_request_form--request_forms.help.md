# Threat Intelligence Feed Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Threat Intelligence Feed Request Form is a tool used to gather and organize threat intelligence. This form helps you request and provide details about threat intelligence feeds, which can be used to enhance your organization's security posture and stay up-to-date on potential threats.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Requestor Information section.
2. Move on to the Security Team Information section, where you can provide details about your security team.
3. In the Feed Details section, you can provide specific information about the feed you're requesting.
4. The Feed Options section allows you to specify any additional options for your request.
5. Once you've completed all the fields, click 'Submit' to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Information**
	+ **Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as the person making this request.
	+ **Email** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Security Team Information**
	+ **Team Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your security team or department.
	+ **Team Contact** (`title`, `text`, required): Provide the email address or phone number of the team contact person.
* **Feed Details**
	+ **Feed Type** (`title`, `text`, required): Specify the type of threat intelligence feed you're requesting (e.g., threat feed, vulnerability feed).
	+ **Feed Frequency** (`title`, `text`, required): Select the frequency of the feed (e.g., real-time, daily, weekly).
* **Feed Options**
	+ **Language** (`title`, `text`, required): Select the language you'd like the feed to be in.
	+ **Format** (`title`, `text`, required): Choose the format of the feed (e.g., JSON, CSV).
* **Submit** (no label, `text`, required): Click 'Submit' to send your request.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to form@gic.mx.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to submit this request.
