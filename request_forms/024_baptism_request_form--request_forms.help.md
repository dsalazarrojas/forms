# Baptism Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baptism Request Form is designed to collect information for a baptism service, whether for the individual requesting the baptism or for someone else. This form helps us understand your relationship to the candidate, the type of baptism requested, and preferred ceremony details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Date of this request
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Your full name
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Your email contact
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Your contact phone
* **Relationship to Candidate** (`request_relationship`): Choose how you are related to the candidate:
	+ Myself
	+ Child
	+ Spouse
	+ Parent
	+ Sibling
	+ Other Family Member
	+ Friend
* **Baptism Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`): Full name of person to be baptized
* **Candidate Date of Birth** (`candidate_dob`): Birth date of candidate
* **Current Age** (`candidate_age`): Age of candidate
* **Type of Baptism Requested** (`baptism_type_requested`): Choose the type of baptism ceremony:
	+ Infant Baptism
	+ Child Baptism
	+ Adult Baptism
	+ Emergency/ Private Baptism
	+ Conditional Baptism
	+ Reaffirmation of Baptism
* **Reason for Baptism Request** (`reason_for_request`): Briefly explain why you are requesting the baptism
* **Faith Commitment Statement** (`faith_commitment`): Brief statement of commitment to faith (optional)
* **Preferred Baptism Date** (`preferred_date`): Preferred date for the ceremony
* **Is This Date Flexible?** (`date_flexibility`): Is the preferred date flexible? (optional)
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Preferred time for the ceremony:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Flexible
	+ Weekend Service
* **Preferred Service Location** (`service_location`): Preferred location for the ceremony:
	+ Church Sanctuary
	+ Chapel
	+ Outdoor Location
	+ River or Lake
	+ Home
	+ No Preference
* **Church Affiliation** (`church_affiliation`): Your parish or church
* **Do You Have a Godparent/Sponsor?** (`has_sponsor`): Yes, No, or Need Help Finding One
* **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_information`): Name and contact of sponsor (optional)
* **Prior Religious Training** (`prior_religious_training`): Education about baptism completed?:
	+ Yes, Completed
	+ In Progress
	+ Not Yet Started
	+ Not Required
* **Needs Pre-Baptism Counseling?** (`needs_counseling`): Would you like pre-baptism counseling? (optional)
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`): Anything else we should know? (optional)
