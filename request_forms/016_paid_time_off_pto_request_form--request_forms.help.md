# Paid Time Off Pto Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting paid time off requests. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it is displayed on your ID badge or employee card.
2. **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your paid time off.
3. **End Date**: Enter the date you would like your paid time off to end.
4. **Reason**: Briefly explain the reason for your paid time off request.
5. **Days Off**: Specify the number of days you need for paid time off.
6. **Pay Status**: Select the current pay status of your time off request. This will determine how the time off will be paid.
7. **Approval Status**: Select the status of the request as sent to the approver.
8. **Comments**: Provide any comments or explanations regarding your paid time off request.
9. **Approve/Reject**: Select whether the approver has approved or rejected your paid time off request.
10. **Approval Date**: The date the approver has approved or rejected your paid time off request.
11. **Manager Comments**: The comments provided by the approver.
12. **Time Off Request Form**: Select whether this is a time off request form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time Off Request**: This is the main section of the form. Enter your time off request details here.
* **Employee Name**: This is your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or card.
*   **Start Date**: The date you would like to start your paid time off.
*   **End Date**: The date you would like your paid time off to end.
*   **Reason**: The reason for your paid time off request.
*   **Days Off**: The number of days you need for paid time off.
*   **Pay Status**: The current status of your pay.
*   **Approval Status**: The status of your request as sent to the approver.
*   **Comments**: Any comments or explanations regarding your paid time off request.
*   **Approve/Reject**: Whether the approver has approved or rejected your paid time off request.
*   **Approval Date**: The date the approver has approved or rejected your request.
*   **Manager Comments**: The comments provided by the approver.
*   **Time Off Request Form**: This option determines whether this is a time off request form.

# Tips
* Double-check that your details are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct pay status and approval status.
* If you have any additional comments or explanations, provide them in the comments section.
