# Theater Property Inventory Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Property Inventory Request Form is used to collect information about properties and set pieces from the theater. This form is essential for maintaining accurate records of the collected items and ensuring that the correct information is conveyed to the right people.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the property or set piece from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of the property or set piece in the description field.
3. Enter the date requested for collection.
4. Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email).
5. Enter the address of the property or location where the item will be collected from.
6. Enter the city and zip code of the property or location.
7. Add any additional notes about the collection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theater Property Inventory Request Form**: The title of the form is not a required field, but it is recommended to enter a relevant title to help identify the purpose of the form.
* **Category**: Select the category of the property or set piece from the dropdown menu. This helps the collection team understand the type of item being collected.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the property or set piece, including its condition and any notable features. This helps the collection team understand what they are collecting.
* **Date Requested**: Enter the date when the collection is scheduled or requested. This helps the collection team plan and schedule the collection process.
* **Requested By**: Enter your name and title/position to help the collection team identify who is requesting the collection.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case the collection team needs to contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in case the collection team needs to contact you electronically.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the property or location where the item will be collected from. This helps the collection team find the location.
* **City**: Enter the city of the property or location. This helps the collection team understand the location.
* **State**: Enter the state of the property or location. This is not required but can help with regional coordination.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the property or location. This helps the collection team understand the location.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the collection process. This helps the collection team prepare for the collection.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the collection process, contact the collection team directly.
* This form is for requesting collection of properties and set pieces from the theater. Do not use this form for other purposes.
