## Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide

### Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to gather information from users who require a specific service. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own specific fields.

### First Name
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### Last Name
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (text, required): Enter a valid email address to receive further communication.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.

### Service Details
* **Service type** (select one, optional): Select the type of service you require (e.g., maintenance, repair, etc.).
* **Service location** (select multiple, optional): Select the location where the service is required (e.g., home, office, etc.).
* **Service date** (date, optional): Select a date for your service request.
* **Service time** (time, optional): Select a time for your service request.
* **Service time** (number, optional): Enter a specific time for your service request.
* **Service frequency** (select one, optional): Select the frequency of your service request (e.g., one time, regular, etc.).

### Additional Information
* **Enquiry details** (text, optional): Provide any additional details or questions about your service request.
* **Assigned tool** (text, optional): If you have a specific tool assigned for your service request, enter it here.
* **Report** (note, optional): This field is for any further comments or observations about your service request.

This form is designed to be completed by users who require a specific service, and it is intended to provide the necessary information to the service provider to fulfill the user's request. By following the steps and explanations above, users will be able to complete the form accurately and effectively.
