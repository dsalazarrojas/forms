# Zoological Conservation Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoological Conservation Leave Of Absence Form is designed for employees to request a leave of absence for conservation-related activities. This form allows employees to provide necessary details for their leave, such as start and end dates, job title, department, location, reason for leave, and return date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name as the requester**: This field should be filled with your name as the employee requesting the leave.
2. **Select a start date**: Choose the date you plan to start your leave by selecting it from the calendar.
3. **Choose an end date**: Enter the date you plan to return to work after your leave.
4. **Enter your job title**: Provide your current job title.
5. **Select your department**: Choose the department you belong to.
6. **Enter your location**: Provide the location where you will be during your leave.
7. **Enter reason for leave**: Briefly explain why you are taking the leave for conservation activities.
8. **Select a return date**: Choose the date you plan to return to work after your leave.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester**: This field is where you enter your name as the employee requesting the leave.
* **Leave Start Date**: Select a date from the calendar to indicate when you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date**: Enter a date to indicate when you plan to return to work after your leave.
* **Job Title**: Provide your current job title to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* **Department**: Choose your department to help us understand which team you belong to.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you will be during your leave for our records.
* **Reason For Leave**: This field should be filled with a brief explanation of why you are taking this leave for conservation activities.
* **Return Date**: Select a date to confirm when you plan to return to work after your leave.
 

## Tips

* Make sure to enter correct dates and information for accurate processing.
* Ensure the reason for leave is directly related to conservation efforts.
* If you are unsure about any part of this process, contact your HR representative for assistance.
