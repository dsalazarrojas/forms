# vacation_time_off_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for vacation or other reasons. Please use this form to submit your request for time off, and make sure to fill out all the required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose the reason for your time off from the "reason" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the number of hours you would like to take off.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your time off.
5. Enter the start and end times of your time off (if applicable).
6. If necessary, provide any additional comments about your request.
7. Click the "submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **vacation_time_off_request_form** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the form. It is not editable.
- **reason** (`reason`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to choose the reason for your time off. Please select "vacation" for a vacation, "sick" for a sick day, or "other" for any other reason not listed.
- **hours_requested** (`hours_requested`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the number of hours you would like to take off. Please enter a whole number, as partial hours are not allowed.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the start date of your time off.
- **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the end date of your time off.
- **start_time** (`start_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the start time of your time off.
- **end_time** (`end_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the end time of your time off.
- **comment** (`comment`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments about your request.
- **submit_button** (`submit_button`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to submit your request. Clicking this button will complete your submission.
