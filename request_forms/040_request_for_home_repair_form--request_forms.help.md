# Request For Home Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow homeowners or authorized representatives to request home repairs, maintenance, or services from the property management team. By filling out this form, you can report issues and schedule maintenance tasks in a convenient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Form Title** field with a brief description of your repair request, ideally including your name or a unique identifier for the request.
2. Enter your **Customer Name**, which should be the name of the homeowner or the authorized representative submitting the request.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** details so that the property management team can easily reach out to you for further information or to schedule follow-up actions.
4. Enter your **Address** to help the property management team locate the issue.
5. Select whether you are requesting a **Service Required** (Yes or No).
6. If necessary, provide **Additional Details** about the issue you are experiencing.
7. Finally, enter the **Submitted By** date and time to track the status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text, not required): A brief description of your repair request, ideally including your name or a unique identifier for the request.
* **Customer Name** (text, not required): The name of the homeowner or authorized representative submitting the request.
* **Contact Email** (email, not required): Your contact email address for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (text, not required): Your contact phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Address** (text, not required): The address of the property where the issue is located.
* **Service Required** (select_one, not required): Select Yes to indicate a service is needed or No to indicate no service is required.
* **Additional Details** (note, not required): A text field for you to provide any additional information about the issue you are experiencing.
* **Submitted By** (date, not required): Enter the date and time you submitted this request to track the status of your issue.
* **Submitted Time** (time, not required): Enter the time you submitted this request to track the status of your issue.

Note: The fields marked as "not required" are not mandatory, but it is highly recommended to fill them out for accurate tracking and communication.
