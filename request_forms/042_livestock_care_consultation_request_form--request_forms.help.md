# Livestock Care Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a consultation for livestock care. It collects information about the farm, location, animal type, and other relevant details. The form is designed to help our team better understand your needs and provide personalized advice and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Farm Name** field, where you can enter the name of your farm.
2. Next, enter the **location** of your farm.
3. Select the **animal type** that you need consultation for (Cat, Horse, Cow, Pig, Sheep, or Goat).
4. Enter the **consultation date** you would like to schedule.
5. If you have a **phone number**, please enter it so we can contact you.
6. If you have an **email address**, please enter it so we can contact you.
7. Provide a brief **description** of the consultation you are requesting.
8. If you have any **priority level** for your consultation (High, Medium, or Low), please select it.
9. Check the **schedule follow-up** box if you would like to schedule a follow-up consultation.
10. Finally, enter any **additional comments** you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of your farm or the name of your farm's location.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your farm, including the street address, city, and state.
* **animal_type**: Select the type of animal you need consultation for.
* **consultation_date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the consultation you are requesting.
* **priority**: Select the priority level for your consultation (High, Medium, or Low).
* **schedule_follow_up**: Check this box if you would like to schedule a follow-up consultation.
* **additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments you have about your consultation request.
