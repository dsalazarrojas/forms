# Emergency Evacuation Assistance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about emergency evacuations that require assistance. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us respond to your situation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide accurate details about the emergency.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the necessary information.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your name. It will be used to identify you in our records.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is where you enter your contact email address. It will be used to contact you for more information or to provide updates.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your phone number. It will be used to contact you for more information or to provide updates.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your address. It will be used to locate you in case of an emergency.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter the contact person's name. It will be used to reach out to them if necessary.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): This is where you select how you are related to the emergency contact. Please choose one of the provided options.
*   **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This is where you select your relationship status (Single, Married, Divorced, Separated, or Widowed).
*   **Reason for Emergency Evacuation** (`emergency_evacuation_reason`, `text`, required: false): This is where you explain the reason for the emergency.
*   **Date of Emergency** (`emergency_evacuation_date`, `date`, required: false): This is where you enter the date of the emergency.
*   **Time of Emergency** (`emergency_evacuation_time`, `time`, required: false): This is where you enter the time of the emergency.
*   **Location of Emergency** (`emergency_evacuation_location`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter the location of the emergency.
*   **Description of Emergency** (`emergency_evacuation_description`, `text`, required: false): This is where you provide a detailed description of the emergency.
*   **Support Needed** (`emergency_evacuation_support_needed`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This is where you select the types of support you require during the emergency (Medical, Transportation, Food and Water, Accommodation, Other).
*   **Additional Notes** (`emergency_evacuation_notes`, `note`, required: false): This is where you enter any additional information or comments about the emergency.
*   **Signature** (`emergency_evacuation_signature`, `text`, required: false): This is where you sign the form, indicating you understand and agree to the terms of the emergency evacuation assistance.
*   **Agreement to Evacuate** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement`, `text`, required: false): This is where you acknowledge your agreement to the terms of the emergency evacuation assistance.
*   **Emergency Evacuation Agreement Terms** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_terms`, `text`, required: false): This is where you review the terms of the emergency evacuation assistance.
*   **Signer Name** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_signer_name`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your name as the signer of the agreement.
*   **Date** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_date`, `date`, required: false): This is where you enter the date of the agreement.
*   **Time** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_time`, `time`, required: false): This is where you enter the time of the agreement.
*   **Location** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_location`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter the location of the agreement.
*   **Agreement Notes** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_notes`, `note`, required: false): This is where you enter any additional comments or notes about the agreement.
*   **Signature Location** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_signature_location`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter the location where you sign the agreement.
*   **Signature Name** (`emergency_evacuation_agreement_signature_name`, `text`, required: false): This is where you enter your name as the signature of the agreement.
