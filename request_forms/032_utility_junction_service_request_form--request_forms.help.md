# Utility Junction Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Junction Service Request Form is a tool used by customers to submit service requests to Utility Junction. The form is designed to gather information about the customer's service request, including the type of service needed, the service area, and the date and time of the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you are requesting (e.g., electricity, water, or gas).
2. Choose the service area where you need the service.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address (if applicable).
4. Provide your address.
5. Enter the date and time of your service request.
6. Add any additional comments or information about your request.
7. Select the tool that you would like to be assigned to assist with your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, required): This field is for you to enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, required): This field is for you to select the type of service you need (e.g., electricity, water, or gas).
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, required): This field is for you to select the area where you need the service.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): This field is for you to enter your name as the contact person for this service request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, optional): This field is for you to enter your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding this service request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding this service request.
* **Address** (`address`, required): This field is for you to enter your address where the service will be provided.
* **Description** (`description`, optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or information about your service request.
* **Utility Junction Service Request** (`utility_junction_service_request`, required): This field is for you to enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or information about your service request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, optional): This field is for you to select the tool that you would like to be assigned to assist with your service request.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, required): This field is for you to enter the date of your service request.
* **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, optional): This field is for you to enter the time of your service request.
* **Service Request Notes** (`service_request_notes`, optional): This field is for you to add any additional information about your service request.
