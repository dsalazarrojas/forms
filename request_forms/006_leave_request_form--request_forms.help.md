# Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leave Request Form is a tool used to submit leave requests to the HR department. It is used to collect essential information for processing leave requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the start and end dates of your requested leave period.
* Specify if this is a leave request (`Yes` or `No`).
* Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
* Provide a brief reason for your leave request.
* Enter your name as the employee requesting the leave.
* Enter your manager's name.
* Enter your department.
* Provide your contact email address.
* Provide your contact phone number.
* Enter your manager's email address.
* Enter your manager's phone number.
* Click the "Submit" button to submit your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Enter the start date of your leave period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Enter the end date of your leave period.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one): Choose `Yes` if you are requesting leave, `No` otherwise.
* **Leave Days** (`leave_days`, number): Enter the number of leave days you are requesting.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text): Provide a brief reason for your leave request.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text): Enter your name as the employee requesting the leave.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter your manager's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Enter your department.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text): Click to submit your leave request form.
