# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is used to report an accident or injury that may be covered by your insurance provider. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Email address and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Describe the Injury Details, including a brief description of what happened.
4. Enter the date and time of the injury.
5. Select the type of claim you are making (e.g., Car Accident, Injury, Other, Property Damage).
6. If your claim type is "Other", provide a brief description in the "Claim Type Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`contact_details_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`, note, required): Describe the injury or accident in as much detail as possible.
* **Injury Date** (`injury_details_2`, date, required): Enter the date of the injury in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Injury Time** (`injury_details_3`, time, required): Enter the time of the injury in the format 12:00 PM/12:00 PM.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of claim you are making (e.g., Car Accident, Injury, Other, Property Damage).
* **Claim Type Other** (`claim_type_other`, text, required (if Claim Type is "Other")): If your claim type is "Other", enter a brief description of the claim in this field.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Injury Details" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Please fill out the form as accurately as possible to ensure your claim is processed efficiently.
