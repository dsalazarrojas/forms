# Distribution Cost Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to distribution costs. It is intended for employees to provide detailed information about their distribution costs and justify their requests for adjustments. The form should only be filled out once per distribution cost adjustment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Distribution Cost**: Enter the distribution cost that you would like to adjust.
	* **Justification**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the adjustment request.
	* **Request Details**: Add any additional information that supports your request.
	* **Form Submitted On**: Date and time the form was submitted.
	* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name, title, and department.
	* **Submitted To**: Enter the name, title, and department of the person or team responsible for processing your request.
	* **Status**: Select the status of your request (e.g., "Pending Review", "Approved", "Denied").
2. Optionally, fill in any additional fields:
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department.
	* **Submitter Position**: Enter your position.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID**: Unique identifier for this form submission.
* **Distribution Cost**: The specific distribution cost you are requesting to adjust.
* **Justification**: A clear and concise explanation for why you are requesting this adjustment.
* **Request Details**: Additional information to support your request.
* **Form Submitted On**: Date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By**: Information about the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted To**: Information about the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitted To Title**: Title of the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitted To Email**: Email address of the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitted To Phone**: Phone number of the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitted To Department**: Department of the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitted To Position**: Position of the person or team processing the request.
* **Submitter Name**: Name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Position**: Position of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter ID**: Unique identifier for the person submitting the form.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or information about the request.
