# Solar Power System Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a service request for your solar power system. Please provide your details to help us better understand and assist with your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and service requirements.
2. Select the service type(s) that you need help with (Installation, Maintenance, or General Services).
3. Choose the system type for your solar power system (Grid-tie, Off-grid, or Hybrid).
4. Specify the size of your system.
5. Provide your address so we can better understand your location.
6. Finally, leave a message with any additional information you have about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry`, text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_email`, email, Required): Your email address will be used to communicate with you regarding your service request.
* **Phone** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Service Type** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_service_type`, select_multiple, Required): Select the type of service you need:
	+ **Installation**: Installation of a new solar power system.
	+ **Maintenance**: Scheduled maintenance for your existing system.
	+ **General Services**: General inquiries or other services not related to installation or maintenance.
* **System Type** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_system_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of your solar power system:
	+ **Grid-tie**: Grid-connected system.
	+ **Off-grid**: Off-grid or off-grid hybrid system.
	+ **Hybrid**: Hybrid system combining different types of energy sources.
* **System Size** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_size`, number, Required): Enter the total size of your solar power system in kilowatt (kW) or megawatt (MW).
* **Address** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_address`, text, Required): Provide your address so we can better understand your location.
* **Message** (`solar_power_system_service_enquiry_message`, text, Required): Add any additional information you have about your service request.
