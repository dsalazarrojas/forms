# copier_service_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The copier service appointment request form is designed to gather information from customers to schedule a copier service appointment. This form will help the service team to prepare and provide the best possible experience for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the customer information page with your personal and contact details.
- Move on to the appointment information page to schedule your desired appointment date and time.
- In the service information page, provide a brief description of the nature of your service visit, such as a routine maintenance or repair.
- Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all information is accurate before sending it to the service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`name`, text, required): Enter your active phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Email Address** (`name`, text, required): Enter your personal email address for communication and updates.
* **Address** (`name`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide an address for our record-keeping, please enter it here.

### Appointment Information
* **Appointment Date** (`name`, text, required): Select the date you'd like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`name`, text, required): Choose the desired time for the appointment from the available options.
* **Duration** (`name`, text, optional): If you have a preferred length for the appointment, please let us know (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).

### Service Information
* **Service Type** (`name`, text, required): Select the type of service you need (e.g., routine maintenance, repair, or installation).
* **Service Description** (`name`, text, required): Briefly describe the nature of your service visit.

### Submit Information
* **Message** (`name`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or questions you have for the service team.
* **Upload Files** (`name`, optional): If you have supporting documents or files, you can upload them here.
* **Submit** (`name`, button, required): Review and submit your form to schedule your appointment.
