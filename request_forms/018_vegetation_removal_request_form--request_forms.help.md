# Vegetation Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request the removal of vegetation from properties, allowing property owners or their representatives to communicate their needs and provide necessary information for efficient vegetation removal services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the request form to ensure you have the necessary information and attachments ready.
2. Fill in the "Property Details" field with the property's address or a brief description to help us locate the site.
3. In the "Tree Removal Request" field, describe the specific areas where vegetation is requested to be removed.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. If required, upload a document or additional information in the "Upload Document" and "Upload Additional Info" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details**: Enter the property address or a brief description to help us locate the site where vegetation removal is needed.
* **Tree Removal Request**: Describe the specific areas where vegetation is requested to be removed. You can also mention any specific trees, branches, or obstacles that need attention.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you for further information or clarification.
* **Upload Document**: Attach any relevant documents, such as photos, videos, or reports, to support your request.
* **Upload Additional Info**: If you have any additional information about the request, such as concerns or special requests, upload it here.
* **Confirm Removal**: Confirm that you are requesting the removal of vegetation from the specified areas.
