# Surgical Recovery Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Recovery Leave Of Absence Request form is a form used by employees to request a leave of absence for a surgical procedure. This form helps ensure that your request is processed correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  If you have already submitted a request for the same surgery date, do not resubmit another request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (User Information, text, not required): Please fill in your name and other personal details as required.
*   **reason for surgery** (reason for surgery, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the reason for your surgery.
*   **dates of surgery** (dates of surgery, select multiple, not required): Select the dates of your surgery.
*   **Doctor/Surgeon Name** (Doctor/Surgeon Name, text, not required): Enter the name of your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Doctor/Surgeon Title** (Doctor/Surgeon Title, text, not required): Enter your doctor or surgeon's title.
*   **Return to work plan** (Return to work plan, text, not required): Describe your return-to-work plan, including any specific dates you may need to return to work by.
*   **Doctor/Surgery Communication Plan** (Doctor/Surgery Communication Plan, text, not required): Explain the communication plan with your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Manager Approval** (Manager Approval, select one, not required): Indicate whether your manager has approved your request.
*   **Approval Date** (Approval Date, date, not required): Enter the date of approval.
*   **Return to work date** (Return to work date, date, not required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
*   **Medical Certification 1** (Medical Certification 1, text, not required): Provide any additional medical certification as required by your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Medical Certification 2** (Medical Certification 2, text, not required): Provide any additional medical certification as required by your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Doctor Contact Info 1** (Doctor Contact Info 1, text, not required): Enter your doctor or surgeon's contact information.
*   **Doctor Contact Info 2** (Doctor Contact Info 2, text, not required): Enter any additional contact information of your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Manager Signature** (Manager Signature, text, not required): Obtain your manager's signature and approval.
*   **Return to work note** (Return to work note, text, not required): Add any relevant notes about your return to work plan.
*   **Doctor/Surgery Notes** (Doctor/Surgery Notes, text, not required): Add any notes from your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, not required): Sign this form to confirm your submission.
*   **Manager Signature 2** (Manager Signature 2, text, not required): Sign your manager's signature for approval.
*   **Manager Comment** (Manager Comment, text, not required): Add any comments or approval notes from your manager.
*   **Employee Comment** (Employee Comment, text, not required): Add any comments or approval notes from you.
*   **Medical Certification 3** (Medical Certification 3, text, not required): Provide any additional medical certification as required by your doctor or surgeon.
*   **Manager Comment 2** (Manager Comment 2, text, not required): Add any additional comments or approval notes from your manager.
*   **Employee Comment 2** (Employee Comment 2, text, not required): Add any additional comments or approval notes from you.
*   **Submit** (Submit, text, not required): Once you have filled out the form completely, click the submit button to send the form.
