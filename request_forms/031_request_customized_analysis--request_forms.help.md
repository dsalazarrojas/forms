# Request Customized Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Customized Analysis form is designed to gather information for a customized analysis request. This form allows you to provide detailed information about your project, which will be used to tailor the analysis to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form fields to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
- Fill out each field according to your project's specific details.
- Double-check your responses for completeness and accuracy.
- Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (Project Details)** (`project_details`, `note`, not required): This field is a note field where you can provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Manager (Project Manager)** (`project_manager`, `text`, not required): This field is for the project manager's name or contact information.
* **Team Members (Team Members)** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate whether or not your team members will be involved in the analysis. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Start Date (Start Date)** (`start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date (End Date)** (`end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Estimated Hours (Estimated Hours)** (`estimated_hours`, `number`, not required): Enter the estimated hours required for the analysis.
* **Actual Hours (Actual Hours)** (`actual_hours`, `number`, not required): Enter the actual hours spent on your project.
* **Project Description (Project Description)** (`project_description`, `text`, not required): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Request Details (Request Details)** (`request_details`, `text`, not required): Add any additional details or comments about your request.
* **Submitter (Submitter)** (`submitter`, `email`, not required): Enter your work email address.
* **Submitter Email (Submitter Email)** (`submitter_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your work email address for follow-up communications.
* **Submitter Phone (Submitter Phone)** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your work phone number or contact information.
* **Submitter Address (Submitter Address)** (`submitter_address`, `text`, not required): Enter your work address or location.
* **Submitter Department (Submitter Department)** (`submitter_department`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate whether or not you are submitting on behalf of your department. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submitter Job Title (Submitter Job Title)** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Submitter Company (Submitter Company)** (`submitter_company`, `text`, not required): Enter your work company or organization.
* **Submitter Notes (Submitter Notes)** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes from the submitter.
* **Submitter Message (Submitter Message)** (`submitter_message`, `note`, not required): This is a note field for any additional comments.
* **Submitter Message to Manager (Submitter Message to Manager)** (`submitter_message_to_manager`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate whether or not you want to send a message to the manager. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Message (Submitter Message to Manager Message)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_message`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or messages to the manager.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Email (Submitter Message to Manager Email)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the manager's email address for follow-up communications.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Phone (Submitter Message to Manager Phone)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the manager's phone number or contact information.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Message (Submitter Message to Manager Message)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_message`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or messages to the manager.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Notes (Submitter Message to Manager Notes)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_notes`, `text`, not required): Add any additional notes for the manager.
* **Submitter Message to Manager Department (Submitter Message to Manager Department)** (`submitter_message_to_manager_department`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate whether or not you want to send a message to the manager's department. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
