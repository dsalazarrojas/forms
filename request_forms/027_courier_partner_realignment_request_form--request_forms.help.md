# Courier Partner Realignment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a change in the status of a partner in the courier services system. Use this form to submit a realignment request when the current status does not accurately reflect the partner's current situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the partner's name in the "Partner Name" field.
2. Select the realignment date and time in the "Realignment Date" and "Realignment Time" fields.
3. Provide a reason for the realignment in the "Reason for Realignment" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number and email address in the "Contact Phone" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
5. Select the new status of the partner from the "Realignment Status" field.
6. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
7. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of the partner you are requesting to realign.
* **Realignment Date**: Select the date on which the realignment should occur.
* **Realignment Time**: Select the time on which the realignment should occur.
* **Reason for Realignment**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the realignment.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person making the realignment request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the realignment request.
* **Realignment Status**: Select the new status of the partner.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about the realignment request.
* **Form Submission ID**: This field is automatically populated and not required to be filled.
* **Created At**: This field displays the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Updated At**: This field displays the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Created By**: Displays the person who created the form submission.
* **Updated By**: Displays the person who last updated the form.
* **Deleted At**: This field displays the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Deleted By**: Displays the person who deleted the form.
