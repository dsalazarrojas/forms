# Regional Product Listing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users interested in listing their products regionally. It helps our team understand their needs and preferences for a smoother product listing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about yourself and your product.
2. Upload the necessary images to support your product listing request.
3. Review and confirm your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Request Form** (<type>text, <name>product_request_form): 
  Enter the description and details of your product, including its name, category, and a brief overview of its features and benefits.

- **Contact Information** (<type>text, <name>contact_form):
  Provide your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you regarding your product listing request.

- **Shipping Information** (<type>text, <name>shipping_form):
  Enter the shipping address and any additional details about shipping requirements or constraints.

- **Payment Information** (<type>text, <name>payment_form):
  If applicable, provide payment information to facilitate the listing process, though no payment is currently required to submit the form.

- **Upload Image** (<type>text, <name>upload_image_form):
  Attach the images of your product to support your listing request. These images can be in various formats and will help our team understand your product better.

- **Confirm** (<type>text, <name>confirm_form):
  Verify that you have accurately completed all fields and agree to the terms of the regional product listing process.

- **Additional Information** (<type>text, <name>additional_information_form):
  Share any additional details about your product or listing request that might be relevant for our team to consider.

- **Confirm Payment** (<type>text, <name>confirm_payment):
  Confirm that all payment information is accurate and correct, though no payment is currently required to submit the form.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
