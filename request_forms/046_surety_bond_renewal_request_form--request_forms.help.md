# surety-bond-renewal-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request the renewal of a surety bond. It is designed to collect necessary information from customers for the purpose of renewing their bond.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "customer-name" field.
2. Enter the surety bond number in the "surety-bond-number" field.
3. Enter the bond expiration date in the "bond-expiration-date" field.
4. Enter the date the request is received in the "request-date" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "submit-request" field to confirm whether you want to submit the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): This is the header of the form that explains the purpose of the form.

### customer-name
* **customer-name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name.

### surety-bond-number
* **surety-bond-number** (`surety_bond_number`, text, required): Enter the surety bond number of the bond to be renewed.

### bond-expiration-date
* **bond-expiration-date** (`bond_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the bond expiration date.

### request-date
* **request-date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date the request is received.

### customer-email
* **customer-email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted.

### customer-phone
* **customer-phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.

### notes
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the request.

### submit-request
* **submit-request** (`submit_request`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit the request for bond renewal, or "No" if you have changed your mind.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
