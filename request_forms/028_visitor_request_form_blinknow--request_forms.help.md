# visitor_request_form_blinknow - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting contact information from visitors, allowing us to get in touch with them for any further assistance or follow-up. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure we can respond to your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to proceed.
2. Fill out the form fields as required and accurate to your information.
3. If you need help with filling a specific field, read the explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address that you check regularly. We'll use this to get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further assistance.

## Tips
- Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
- If you have any doubts about which information to provide, you can contact BlinkNow's customer service team for assistance.
- If you're unable to complete the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
