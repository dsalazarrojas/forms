# Availability Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Availability Inquiry Form is designed to collect essential information from pet owners to schedule services for their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Owner Full Name**, which is the pet owner's legal name.
2. Provide the pet owner's **Owner Email**, which will be used for confirmations.
3. Enter the pet owner's **Owner Phone**, which is the best contact number for the pet owner.
4. Enter the **Pet Name**, which is the name of the pet.
5. Select the **Pet Type**, which is the species or breed of the pet.
6. Select the **Preferred Service**, which is the service the pet owner is requesting for their pet.
7. Choose the **Available Time Slots**, which are the times when the pet is available for service.
8. If desired, select a **Preferred Start Date** for the service to begin.
9. Enter any **Special Requests or Notes**, which are any health concerns or instructions for the pet's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Full Name**: Enter the pet owner's legal name.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the pet owner's preferred email for confirmations.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the pet owner's best contact number.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Type**: Select the species or breed of the pet (e.g., Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Preferred Service**: Select the service needed for the pet (e.g., Grooming, Boarding, Veterinary visit, Daycare).
* **Available Time Slots**: Choose all the time slots when the pet is available for service (e.g., Weekday mornings, Weekday afternoons, Weekend mornings, Weekend afternoons).
* **Preferred Start Date**: Select the date service should begin (optional).
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any health concerns or instructions for the pet's care.
