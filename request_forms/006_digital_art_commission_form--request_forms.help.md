# Digital Art Commission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Art Commission Form is used to gather information about commissioning a digital art piece. This includes details about the artist, client, artwork, commission terms, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form for a valid commission request.
* Ensure that you are either an artist or a client to fill out the form accurately.
* If you are an artist, select "Yes" for "Artist Information" to indicate that you are the artist.
* If you are a client, select "No" for "Artist Information" to indicate that the artist is someone else.
* Select the relevant option for "Artwork Type" to indicate the type of artwork being commissioned.
* Select the relevant option for "Commission Details" to indicate the nature of the commission.
* Enter the price range of the commission in the "Price Range" field.
* Enter the price you are willing to pay in the "Artist Price" field.
* Enter the budget you are willing to spend in the "Client Budget" field.
* Enter any payment terms or conditions in the "Payment Terms" field.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for "Commission Agreement" to indicate whether you agree to the commission terms.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for "Contract Length" to indicate whether you want a contract for the commission.
* Enter the commission percentage in the "Commission Percentage" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Digital Art Commission Form** (`digital_art_commission_form`): This is the main title of the form.
* **Artist Information** (`artist_info`): Select "Yes" if you are the artist commissioning the piece. Select "No" if you are a client looking for an artist.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`): Select "Yes" if you are the client commissioning the piece. Select "No" if you are an artist looking for a client.
* **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`): Enter a brief description of the artwork you are commissioning.
* **Artwork Type** (`artwork_type`): Select the type of artwork you are commissioning (e.g., drawing, painting, sculpture).
* **Commission Details** (`commission_details`): Select the nature of the commission (e.g., one-time, ongoing, series).
* **Price Range** (`price_range`): Enter the range of price you are willing to pay for the commission.
* **Artist Price** (`artist_price`): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the commission.
* **Client Budget** (`client_budget`): Enter the budget you are willing to spend on the commission.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any payment terms or conditions you would like to include in the commission agreement.
* **Commission Agreement** (`commission_agreement`): Select "Yes" if you agree to the commission terms. Select "No" if you do not.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`): Select "Yes" if you want a contract for the commission. Select "No" if you do not.
* **Commission Percentage** (`commission_percentage`): Enter the commission percentage you are willing to pay.
