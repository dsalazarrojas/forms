# Invoice Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Adjustment Request Form is used to request adjustments to invoices that have been issued to customers. This form allows users to specify the type of adjustment, amount, and description of the change needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of adjustment you need to make. Choose from Overcharge, Undercharge, or Billing Error.
2. Enter the Invoice Number of the invoice you are adjusting.
3. Provide a description of the adjustment.
4. If necessary, enter the Date of the overcharge or undercharge.
5. Enter the Amount of the overcharge or undercharge.
6. Enter any additional notes for the overcharge or undercharge.
7. Enter the customer's name.
8. Enter the customer's phone number (if available).
9. Enter the customer's email address (if available).
10. Enter the customer's address, city, state, and country.
11. Enter the customer's zip code.
12. Provide any additional notes for the customer.
13. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Adjustment Request
* **Adjustment Request**: Select the type of adjustment you need to make: Overcharge, Undercharge, or Billing Error. (Required)
### 2. Adjustment Amount
* **Adjustment Amount**: Enter the amount of the adjustment. (Optional)
### 3. Invoice Number
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the number of the invoice you are adjusting. (Optional)
### 4. Description
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the adjustment. (Optional)
### 5. Overcharge Date
* **Overcharge Date**: Enter the date of the overcharge. (Optional)
### 6. Overcharge Amount
* **Overcharge Amount**: Enter the amount of the overcharge. (Optional)
### 7. Overcharge Note
* **Overcharge Note**: Enter any additional notes for the overcharge. (Optional)
### 8. Undercharge Date
* **Undercharge Date**: Enter the date of the undercharge. (Optional)
### 9. Undercharge Amount
* **Undercharge Amount**: Enter the amount of the undercharge. (Optional)
### 10. Undercharge Note
* **Undercharge Note**: Enter any additional notes for the undercharge. (Optional)
### 11. Billing Error Date
* **Billing Error Date**: Enter the date of the billing error. (Optional)
### 12. Billing Error Note
* **Billing Error Note**: Enter any additional notes for the billing error. (Optional)
### 13. Customer Information
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer. (Optional)
### 14. Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. (Optional)
### 15. Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address. (Optional)
### 16. Customer Address
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address. (Optional)
### 17. Customer City
* **Customer City**: Enter the customer's city. (Optional)
### 18. Customer State
* **Customer State**: Enter the customer's state. (Optional)
### 19. Customer Country
* **Customer Country**: Enter the customer's country. (Optional)
### 20. Customer Zip
* **Customer Zip**: Enter the customer's zip code. (Optional)
### 21. Customer Note
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes for the customer. (Optional)
### 22. Submit
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct invoice number to ensure the adjustment is applied to the correct invoice.
* Enter as much information as possible to provide a clear and concise adjustment request.
* Use the "Description" field to provide additional context for the adjustment.
* If the customer contact information is available, enter it to help with communication.
