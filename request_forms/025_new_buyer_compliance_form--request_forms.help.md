# New Buyer Compliance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Buyer Compliance Form is designed to collect information from new buyers to ensure compliance with our business guidelines and regulations. This form helps to verify the buyer's details and preferences before proceeding with the purchase process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure you provide all necessary information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you have not missed any required fields.
3. If you need help with any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Buyer Compliance Form** (`new_buyer_compliance_form`, text, required: false): This is the main form section where you can provide a brief description of your compliance preferences. Please be concise and clear in your response.
* **New Buyer Compliance Section** (`new_buyer_compliance_section`, number, required: false): In this field, please enter the relevant section number from our compliance documentation that applies to your purchase.
* **New Buyer Compliance Date** (`new_buyer_compliance_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your compliance, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **New Buyer Compliance Time** (`new_buyer_compliance_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your compliance, in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **New Buyer Compliance File** (`new_buyer_compliance_file`, text, required: false): Please attach any relevant files or documents that support your compliance preferences, such as receipts or certification.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand our compliance guidelines before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is a mandatory part of our buying process, so please complete it accurately and thoroughly.
