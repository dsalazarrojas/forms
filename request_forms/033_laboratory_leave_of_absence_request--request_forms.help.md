# Laboratory Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Leave Of Absence Request form is used to submit a formal request for leave from the laboratory, including the request date, end date, reason for leave, and other relevant details such as email and phone number. The form ensures that all required information is collected and reviewed by the relevant parties for approval or denial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information, including your request date, end date, reason for leave, and assigned tool.
2. Select the correct assigned tool from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional comments or details in the comments field.
4. Verify your email and phone number are accurate.
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Information**: This field is used to describe your leave request. Enter a brief description of your absence, including the start and end dates.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your leave from, following the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Date**: Enter the date you expect to return to work, following the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Reason For Leave**: Provide a valid reason for your leave, this may include personal, medical, or other approved leave categories.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Check with your supervisor to obtain approval for your leave. Select 'Yes' to indicate approval or 'No' to indicate denial.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or details you wish to share with the lab management.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool your lab management has assigned to you for your leave period.
* **Output File**: This field is for any additional output files or attachments you may have related to your leave request.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have not submitted a similar request in the past 30 days.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Be concise and clear in your request information and comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or lab management for guidance.
