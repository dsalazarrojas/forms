# University Library Resource Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Library Resource Requisition Form is used to request access to library resources by users. This form allows users to specify the resource they need, the quantity required, their department, and their priority/urgency level, ensuring that their requests are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the User Info section with your name and email address.
2. Select the type of resource you are requesting (Book, Journal, or Database).
3. Enter the quantity of the resource you need (e.g., 3).
4. Choose your department (Arts, Science, or Engineering).
5. Select your priority/urgency level (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Enter the name of the resource you are requesting.
7. Choose the status of your request (Awaiting Review, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Click submit to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
• **User Info** (`text`, `required`: false): Provide information about yourself, including your name and email address.

### Resource Type
• **Resource Type** (`select_one`, `required`: false): Choose the type of resource you are requesting (Book, Journal, or Database).

### Resource Quantity
• **Resource Quantity** (`number`, `required`: false): Enter the quantity of the resource you need.

### User Email
• **User Email** (`email`, `required`: false): Enter your email address associated with your university account.

### Priority/Urgency
• **Priority/Urgency** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Select your priority/urgency level (Low, Medium, or High).

### User Department
• **User Department** (`select_one`, `required`: false): Choose your department (Arts, Science, or Engineering).

### Resource Name
• **Resource Name** (`text`, `required`: false): Enter the name of the resource you are requesting.

### Review Status
• **Review Status** (`select_multiple`, `required`: false): Select the status of your request (Awaiting Review, Approved, or Rejected).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the library staff for assistance.
