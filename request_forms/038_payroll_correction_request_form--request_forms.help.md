# Payroll Correction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request corrections to an employee's payroll records. Please use this form to report any errors or discrepancies in your pay information, such as incorrect salary, benefits, or other compensation-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** in the field below.
2. Choose the **Correction Date** when the error or discrepancy occurred.
3. Provide a detailed description of the **Discrepancy Description**.
4. Specify the **Request Reason** for the correction.
5. Enter the **Correction Amount**, if applicable.
6. Select who **Corrected By** to ensure proper attribution of the correction.
7. Add any additional **Comments** related to the correction.
8. Enter your **Email** address so that we can contact you for further clarification if needed.
9. Enter your **Phone Number** for easy contact.
10. If necessary, attach any relevant **Attachments** to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or pay stub.
* **Correction Date**: The date when the error or discrepancy was first noticed.
* **Discrepancy Description**: A detailed description of the error or discrepancy.
* **Request Reason**: The reason why you are requesting the correction.
* **Correction Amount**: If the correction involves a financial adjustment, enter the amount of the adjustment.
* **Corrected By**: Select the person who made the original correction or reported the error.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments related to the correction.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Attachment 1**: Attach any relevant documents or evidence supporting your correction request.
* **Corrected By**: Select who corrected the error or discrepancy. 

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required, but please provide them if you have the necessary information.
