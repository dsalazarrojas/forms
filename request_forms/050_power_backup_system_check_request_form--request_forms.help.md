## power_backup_system_check_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to check the power backup system of a facility. It's essential to fill out this form for facilities that require power backup systems to be checked. Please ensure that the system is properly checked before submitting the form to avoid delays in the check process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Generator ID** (`generator_id`, number, optional): Provide the ID of the generator, if applicable.
* **Facility Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the facility where the power backup system is located.
* **Schedule Request** (`schedule_request`, date, optional): Specify the schedule for checking the power backup system.
* **Start Date** (`schedule_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the scheduled check.
* **End Date** (`schedule_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the scheduled check.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the power backup system.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents that may be required for the check.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If the form is incomplete or inaccurate, it may lead to delays in the check process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or our support team.
