# Documentary Project Archive Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request archival services for documentary projects. It is designed to capture relevant information about the project, including its title, category, description, contact details, and any additional notes or files that may be required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the required fields:
	* Category (e.g., "Request Forms")
	* Title and description of the project
	* Your email and phone number for contact
2. Click "Next Page" to proceed to the next set of fields:
	* Enter any additional notes (optional)
	* Attach relevant documents or media files (optional)
	* Specify the request type (e.g., "Request Form", "Donation Form", "Request Form for Collection")
3. Review your information and make any necessary corrections or updates before clicking "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one): Choose the category for your project from the provided options.
* **Title** (`form_title`, text): Enter the title of your project.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name** (`form_name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Note** (`form_note`, note): Add any additional notes about your project (optional).
* **Files** (`form_files`, text): Attach relevant documents or media files (optional).
* **Date** (`form_date`, date): Enter the date of your request.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time): Enter the time of your request.
* **Request Type** (`form_request_type`, select_one): Choose the type of request you are making (e.g., "Request Form", "Donation Form", "Request Form for Collection").
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text): Click to submit your request.
* **Cancel** (`form_cancel`, text): Click to cancel your request.
* **Next Page** (`form_next_page`, text): Click to proceed to the next page.
* **Previous Page** (`form_previous_page`, text): Click to return to the previous page.
* **Error Message** (`form_error_message`, text): Review any error messages that may appear.
* **Page 1** (`form_page_1`, text), **Page 2** (`form_page_2`, text), **Page 3** (`form_page_3`, text), **Page 4** (`form_page_4`, text), **Page 5** (`form_page_5`, text), **Page 6** (`form_page_6`, text), **Page 7** (`form_page_7`, text), **Page 8** (`form_page_8`, text), **Page 9** (`form_page_9`, text): These fields are used to navigate between pages.

Note: The "Page X" fields are not actually fields to be filled out, but rather buttons to navigate between pages.
