# winery_donation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winery Donations Request Form is used by wineries to submit requests for donations to support their events. This form is an optional tool for wineries to provide detailed information about their events and requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct event date and time.
2. Provide a detailed description of your event and its purpose.
3. List any supporting documents that are relevant to your event.
4. Choose the representative from your winery who will be in charge of your event.
5. Select the event manager who will be handling your event.
6. Fill in any other required fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Winery Information**: This field is required to collect basic information about your winery. Enter the name and description of your winery.
* **Event Details**: This field is used to describe the details of your event, including its purpose and description.
* **Supporting Documents**: This field is used to upload any supporting documents that are necessary for your event.
* **Donation Inquiry**: This field is an optional inquiry for additional support or resources.
* **Follow-up**: This field is a note for any additional comments or requests.
* **Winery Representative**: This field is used to select the authorized representative from your winery who will be handling your event.
* **Event Manager**: This field is used to select the event manager who will be handling your event.
* **Event Date**: This field is used to select the date of your event.
* **Event Time**: This field is used to select the time of your event.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling this form with accurate and complete information.
* Ensure that you are not making duplicate requests for the same event.
* If you are not an authorized representative of the winery, please do not fill this form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
