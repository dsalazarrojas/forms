# Imaging Prior Authorization Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request imaging services on behalf of a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide the patient's demographic information, including their name, date of birth, address, and contact phone number.
2.  Enter the patient's medical record number.
3.  Specify the insurance provider and plan for the patient.
4.  Enter the reason for the imaging request.
5.  Specify the clinical services provided.
6.  Enter the name and contact information of the requestor.
7.  Enter the name and address of the physician who ordered the imaging request.
8.  Select the imaging date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth** (2): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (3): Enter the patient's address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (4): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (5): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Insurance Provider** (6): Select 'Yes' if the patient has an insurance provider, 'No' otherwise.
* **Insurance Plan** (7): Select the patient's insurance plan.
* **Reason for Request** (8): Enter the reason for the imaging request.
* **Clinical Services Provided** (9): Enter the clinical services provided.
* **Requestor Name** (10): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Requestor Organization** (11): Enter the organization the requestor is from.
* **Requestor Phone Number** (12): Enter the requestor's phone number.
* **Requestor Email** (13): Enter the requestor's email address.
* **Physician Name** (14): Enter the name of the physician who ordered the imaging request.
* **Physician Address** (15): Enter the address of the physician who ordered the imaging request.
* **Medical Record** (16): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Imaging Request** (17): Enter the imaging request details.
* **Imaging Date** (18): Select the date on which the imaging was performed or is scheduled to be performed.
* **Imaging Time** (19): Select the time on which the imaging was performed or is scheduled to be performed.

Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely to avoid any errors or delays in the processing and approval process.
