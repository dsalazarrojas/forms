# Experience Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees requesting an experience certificate for external purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee details, such as your name and ID number.
2. Enter your dates of experience, including the start and end dates of your employment.
3. Provide any additional employment details you would like to have included on the certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Please enter your employee details, including your name and ID number. This information will be used to identify you as the employee who is requesting the experience certificate.
* **Dates of Experience** (`experience_dates`, text, optional): Enter the start and end dates of your employment with the company. This will help verify your employment dates and ensure the certificate is accurate.
* **Employment Details** (`employment_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to have included on the certificate, such as job title, job function, or any notable achievements during your employment.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your dates of employment to ensure they are accurate.
* If you are unsure about any information required on the form, contact your HR department for clarification.
* If you need to request an experience certificate for an event or period that has not yet occurred or ended, please do not submit this form until that event or period has occurred or ended.
