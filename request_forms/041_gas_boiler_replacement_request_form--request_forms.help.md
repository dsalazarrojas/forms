# Gas Boiler Replacement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gas Boiler Replacement Request Form is designed to gather information about a gas boiler that requires replacement or repair. This form will help maintenance teams to efficiently process and prioritize requests for gas boiler replacement or repair.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate boiler type from the options provided. Choose "Boiler Replacement" if the boiler is damaged beyond repair, or "Repair" if it can be fixed.
2. Enter the date the boiler was installed, if known.
3. Provide a text description of where the boiler is located.
4. Add any notes from the engineer that may be relevant to the request.
5. Describe the issue with the boiler in detail.
6. If applicable, enter the estimated cost to replace the boiler and the cost of any labor involved.
7. Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
8. Choose the status of the job from the options provided (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
9. Provide a description of the job.
10. Enter the date the job was completed, if applicable.
11. Identify the person assigned to work on the job.
12. Choose the priority level of the job from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
13. Enter the estimated time it will take to complete the job.
14. Enter the actual time it took to complete the job, if applicable.
15. Enter any updates on the job status.
16. Enter the customer's email address.
17. Enter the customer's phone number.
18. Enter the customer's address.
19. Enter any other job ID related information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Boiler Type** (select_one): Choose the type of boiler replacement or repair request you are making. This will help the maintenance team to prioritize and schedule jobs accordingly.
* **Installation Date** (date): Enter the date the boiler was installed, if known. This information can be useful for maintenance teams to track boiler usage and maintenance history.
* **Installation Location** (text): Provide a description of where the boiler is located, including the room and building number. This information will help the maintenance team to locate the boiler for replacement or repair.
* **Engineer Notes** (text): Add any notes from the engineer that may be relevant to the request. This can include information about the boiler's condition, maintenance history, or other relevant details.
* **Customer Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or information from the customer about the issue they are experiencing with the boiler.
* **Customer Signature** (text): Enter the customer's signature, confirming they have reviewed and agree to the request.
* **Engineer Signature** (text): Enter the engineer's signature, confirming they have reviewed and will process the request.
* **Gas Boiler Model** (text): Enter the model number of the gas boiler in question.
* **Gas Boiler Serial Number** (text): Enter the serial number of the gas boiler.
* **Boiler Condition** (select_multiple): Choose the current condition of the boiler, Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Issue Description** (text): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the boiler in detail.
* **Gas Boiler Replacement Cost** (number): Enter the estimated cost to replace the gas boiler, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter the name of the customer as it appears on their account.
* **Job Status** (select_one): Choose the status of the job from the options provided (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Job Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Completion Date** (date): Enter the date the job was completed, if applicable.
* **Assigned To** (text): Identify the person assigned to work on the job.
* **Priority** (select_one): Choose the priority level of the job from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
* **Estimated Completion Time** (time): Enter the estimated time it will take to complete the job.
* **Actual Completion Time** (time): Enter the actual time it took to complete the job, if applicable.
* **Job Status Update** (text): Enter any updates on the job status.
* **Customer Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (text): Enter the customer's address.
* **Job ID** (text): Enter any other job ID related information.
