# Student Government Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Government Purchase Request Form is a document used to manage and track purchases made by the student government for the benefit of their operations and activities.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and accurately
* Attach any supporting documentation or receipts as necessary
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Request** (1): A brief description of the purchase being requested.
* **Department** (2): Select all departments that will be impacted by the purchase.
* **Budgeted Amount** (3): The total amount budgeted for the purchase.
* **Justification** (4): Provide a clear and concise explanation of why the purchase is necessary for the student government operations.
* **Purchase Type** (5): Indicate whether the purchase is for a new or used item.
* **Purchase Description** (6): A detailed description of the purchase being requested.
* **Purchase Date** (7): The date the purchase is expected to be made.
* **Total Cost** (8): The total cost of the purchase.
* **Approved By** (9): Indicate if the purchase has been approved by the relevant authorities.
* **Purchase Request Comments** (10): Additional comments or notes about the purchase.
* **Submitted By** (11): Indicate if the person submitting the form is a student or staff member.
* **Submitted Date** (12): The date the form was submitted.
* **Review Comments** (13): Additional comments or notes from reviewers.
* **Reviewed By** (14): Indicate if the purchase has been reviewed by the relevant authorities.
* **Reviewed Date** (15): The date the purchase was reviewed.
* **Status** (16): The current status of the purchase (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitted By** (17): The person who submitted the form, not to be confused with **Submitted By** (11).
