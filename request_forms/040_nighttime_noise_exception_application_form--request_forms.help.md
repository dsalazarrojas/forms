# Nighttime Noise Exception Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nighttime Noise Exception Application Form is used to request exceptions to regular nighttime noise policies. This form is intended for residents or guests to provide information about their noise-related exceptions and exceptions to their lease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your lease agreement.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your lease agreement.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your lease agreement.
	* **Address**: Enter your address as it appears on your lease agreement.
2. Provide details about your exception:
	* **Noise Duration**: Enter the length of time you will be making noise.
	* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
	* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your event.
	* **Reason**: Enter a brief reason for your exception request.
3. Add supporting documentation:
	* **Supporting Documents**: You may upload any supporting documents related to your exception request.
4. Choose your exception request type:
	* **Submit Request**: Choose "Exception" to indicate that you are requesting an exception to the regular nighttime noise policy.
	* **Submit Request Reason**: Enter a brief reason for choosing "Exception".
	* **Submit Request Date**: Enter the date of your request.
5. Select your submitter type:
	* **Submitter**: Choose "Staff" or "Resident" or "Guest" depending on your affiliation with the building.
	* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the request.
	* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
	* **Submitter Address**: Enter the address of the person submitting the request.
