# URL Indexing Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request URL indexing for a given website or page. It helps us verify the correctness of website URLs and their associated metadata.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: **Email**, **Phone**, **URL**, **Type**, and **Priority**.
2. Optionally, provide your **Name** and **Comments**.
3. Select the correct **Type** from the list (e.g., Homepage, Blog, E-commerce).
4. Choose the **Priority** level of the request (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
5. Click "Submit" to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: A text field to input your name. This is optional, but helps us associate your request with your identity.
* **Email**: A required email field to input your contact email address. This is used to notify you about the status of your request.
* **Phone**: A required text field to input your phone number. This is used for further communication.
* **URL**: A required text field to input the URL of the page you want to request indexing for.
* **Type**: A select menu to choose the type of your website (e.g., Homepage, Blog, E-commerce). This helps us understand the purpose of the URL.
* **Priority**: A select menu to choose the priority level of your request (e.g., Low, Medium, High). This helps us schedule the request accordingly.
* **Comments**: An optional text area to provide any additional comments or information about your request.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your convenience.
