# Engineering Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Engineering Request Form is a standardized document for requesting resources from internal engineering teams. Fill out this form accurately to ensure that your requests are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Form Data** section with relevant details about your request.
2. Provide your company's information in the **company** field.
3. Enter your name in the **name** field.
4. Enter your job title in the **job title** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
7. If applicable, select the correct date in the **date** field.
8. Select a specific time slot in the **time** field.
9. For **select one**, choose the relevant option from the provided list.
10. If necessary, choose multiple options for **select multiple**.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments in the **note** field.
12. Assign the request to a specific user in the **assigned user** field.
13. Select the status of your request in the **status** field.
14. If relevant, assign a tool to your request in the **assigned tool** field.
15. Finally, select whether a specific tool will be assigned to your request with the **assigned tool** field.
