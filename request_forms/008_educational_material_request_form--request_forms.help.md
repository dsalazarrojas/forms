# Educational Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Material Request Form is designed to help users request access to educational materials that are not readily available through existing resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of request you need**: Choose from Book Request or Resource Request.
2. Fill out the form with the required information: title, book request, resource request, title of the material, author, year of publication, and description.
3. Click submit to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (1): Select the type of request you need. This will help us understand the nature of your request.
* **Book Request** (2): Choose the type of book you are looking for. Please select from the available options.
* **Resource Request** (3): Select multiple resources you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Title** (4): Enter the title of the material you are requesting.
* **Author** (5): Enter the author of the material you are requesting.
* **Year of Publication** (6): Enter the year the material was published.
* **Subject** (7): Enter a brief description of the subject matter of the material.
* **Description** (8): Enter a detailed description of the material you are requesting, including any additional context or background information.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you are requesting multiple materials, please fill out the form multiple times, one for each material.
* Please ensure that your request is not a duplicate by reviewing our existing catalog.
