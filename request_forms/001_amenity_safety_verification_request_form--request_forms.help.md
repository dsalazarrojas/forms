# Amenity Safety Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate the safety verification process for an amenity within an organization or facility. It collects information from the requestor, including their contact details and the amenity specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Provide Your Details
- Please enter your full name as the requestor.
- Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
- Enter your contact phone number for easy reach-out.

### 2. Select Amenity Details
- Choose the type of amenity you are requesting verification for.
- Enter the specific location or address of the amenity.

### 3. Select Verification Type
- Choose the type of verification needed for the amenity.

### 4. Provide Additional Information
- If applicable, mention any known safety concerns or issues with the amenity.
- Choose the priority level of the verification request.
- Add any additional comments or information about the request.

### 5. Schedule Verification
- Choose a preferred date for the verification, if applicable.

### 6. Finalize Your Request
- Please review and ensure all details are accurate before submitting.
- Click on "Submit Request" to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`): Enter your full name as the individual requesting the amenity safety verification.
	+ Required Field
* **Requestor Email Address** (`requestor_email`): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
	+ Required Field
* **Requestor Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
	+ Required Field
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of your organization or facility (if applicable).
	+ Optional Field
* **Type of Amenity** (`amenity_type`): Choose the type of amenity you are requesting verification for.
	+ Required Field
* **Amenity Location** (`amenity_location`): Enter the specific location or address of the amenity.
	+ Required Field
* **Type of Verification Needed** (`verification_type`): Choose the type of verification needed for the amenity.
	+ Required Field
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the last safety inspection performed.
	+ Optional Field
* **Known Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`): Describe any known issues or concerns with the amenity.
	+ Optional Field
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`): Choose the priority level of the verification request.
	+ Required Field
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any other relevant information about the request.
	+ Optional Field
* **Preferred Verification Date** (`preferred_verification_date`): Choose a preferred date for the verification, if applicable.
	+ Optional Field
* **Site Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`): Enter any special instructions for accessing the amenity.
	+ Optional Field
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`): Confirm that the information is accurate and submit the request.
	+ Optional Field

## Tips

- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in the verification process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- We will review and follow-up on your request as per the selected priority level and preferred verification date.
