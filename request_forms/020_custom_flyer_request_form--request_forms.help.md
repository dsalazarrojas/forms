# Custom Flyer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a custom flyer design from a design team. It is meant to gather necessary information from the submitter to guide the designer in creating a high-quality design that meets the client's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out the form, please make sure you are authorized to submit a design request on behalf of your company or organization. This will help us to ensure that we are designing a flyer that meets your company's brand guidelines.

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field to help the designer create a design that meets your needs.
3. Review your submission for errors and completeness before submitting it to the design team.
