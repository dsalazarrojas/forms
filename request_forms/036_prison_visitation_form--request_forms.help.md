# Prison Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals visiting a correctional facility to provide necessary information for visitation purposes. It ensures that visitors and prison officials have the required information in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Contact Email, Contact Address, and Date of Visit.
3. Specify the purpose of your visit and type of visitors accompanying you.
4. If applicable, indicate whether an email has been sent to the prisoner, attorney, or BCC.
5. Optionally, provide a purpose of visit and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (Required): Your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Email** (Required): The email address to which the visitation information will be sent.
* **Contact Address** (Required): Your contact address for any follow-up communication.
* **Date of Visit** (Required): The date of your visit to the correctional facility.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (Required): The time you plan to arrive at the facility.
* **Number of Visitors** (Optional): Specify the number of people visiting with you.
* **Visitor Type** (Optional): Select the type of visitor you are (Guest, Staff, Family Member).
* **Purpose of Visit** (Optional): Specify the reason for your visit.
* **Note** (Optional): Any additional information or notes about your visit.
* **Email Sent to Prison** (Optional): Indicate whether an email was sent to the prisoner.
* **Email Sent Date** (Optional): The date an email was sent to the prisoner.
* **Email Sent Time** (Optional): The time an email was sent to the prisoner.
* **Email Sent to Prisoner** (Optional): Indicate whether an email was sent to the prisoner.
* **Email Sent to Attorney** (Optional): Indicate whether an email was sent to the attorney.
* **BCC** (Optional): Indicate whether BCC was used when sending the email.
* **Email Subject** (Optional): The subject of the email sent.
* **Email Body** (Optional): The body of the email sent.
* **Send Reminders** (Optional): Indicate whether reminders were sent to the prisoner.
* **Reminders Sent** (Optional): The number of reminders sent.
* **Next Reminders** (Optional): The time for the next reminders.
* **Last Reminders** (Optional): The time of the last reminders sent.
* **Email Reminders Sent** (Optional): The number of reminders sent via email.
