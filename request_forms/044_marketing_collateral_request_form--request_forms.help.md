# Marketing Collateral Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request marketing collateral, such as brochures, sell sheets, and catalogues, from the marketing team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with your request information.
2. Choose the marketing collateral type that you are requesting.
3. Fill in the description field with a detailed description of your request.
4. Click submit to send your request to the marketing team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Marketing Collateral Request** (marketing_collateral_request_form, text, required): Enter a brief title for your marketing collateral request.
- **Business Owner** (business_owner, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the business owner submitting the request.
- **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required): Enter your contact number where the marketing team can reach you.
- **Email** (email_address, email, required): Enter your email address where the marketing team can contact you.
- **Request Date** (request_date, date, required): Choose the date you need the marketing collateral by.
- **Description** (description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the marketing collateral you are requesting.
- **Marketing Collateral Type** (marketing_collateral_type, select_one, required): Select the type of marketing collateral you are requesting, such as Marketing Brochure, Brochure, Catalog, Sell Sheet, etc.
- **Marketing Collateral Quantity** (marketing_collateral_quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of marketing collateral you are requesting.
