# Building Admission Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help visitors request access to a building or facility. Completing this form will help us process your request quickly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Your Personal Information
* **Visitor Full Name** (`visitor_name`, **text**, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, **text**, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`, **text**, required): Enter your contact number.

### Step 2: Schedule Your Visit
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, **date**, required): Select the date of your visit.
* **Requested Visit Date** (`requested_visit_date`, **date**, required): Select when you would like to visit.
* **Requested Visit Time** (`requested_visit_time`, **time**, required): Select your preferred visit time.
* **Estimated Duration** (`estimated_duration`, **select_one**, required): Select how long you expect to stay.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the building you are visiting.
* **Building Address** (`building_address`, **text**, required): Enter the full address of the building.

### Step 3: Provide Visit Details
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the person you are visiting.
* **Host Department** (`host_department`, **text**, optional): Enter the department of the person you are visiting.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, **email**, optional): Enter the email of the person you are visiting.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`, **select_one**, required): Select the reason for your visit.
* **Visit Description** (`visit_description`, **text**, required): Describe the purpose of your visit.

### Step 4: Confirm Your Visit Details
* **Previous Visits** (`previous_visits`, **select_one**, required): Confirm if you have visited before.
* **Identification Type** (`identification_type`, **select_one**, required): Select the type of ID you will present.
* **Background Check Required** (`background_check_required`, **select_one**, optional): Confirm if a background check is required.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, **select_one**, required): Confirm you agree to the building rules.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, **text**, optional): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
