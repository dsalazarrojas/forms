# Nirsevimab Eligibility And Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for Nirsevimab, a medication that requires careful evaluation before administration. Patients can fill out this form to provide their information and submit a request for approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your patient information in the **patient_information** field.
2.  Provide your contact details in the **patient_contact_details** field.
3.  Describe your medical history in the **medical_history** field.
4.  Specify the details of your eligibility for Nirsevimab in the **eligibility_details** field.
5.  Describe the approval process you have undergone in the **approval_process** field.
6.  Enter any review comments in the **review_comments** field.
7.  Sign and date the form with **signature_1** and **submission_date** fields.
8.  Specify the submission details in the **submission_device**, **submission_browser**, **submission_os**, **submission_ip_address**, **submission_url** fields.
9.  Optionally, enter additional submission information such as **submission_user**, **submission_language**, **submission_timezone**, **submission_timezone_offset**, **submission_user_country**, **submission_user_city** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_information** (1): Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant patient information.
* **patient_contact_details** (2): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **medical_history** (3): Describe your medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **eligibility_details** (4): Specify the details of your eligibility for Nirsevimab, including any relevant medical documentation or test results.
* **approval_process** (5): Describe the approval process you have undergone, including any relevant medical procedures or consultations.
* **review_comments** (6): Enter any comments or feedback from the review process.
* **signature_1** (7): Sign and date the form with your signature.
* **submission_date** (8): Enter the date of submission.
* **submission_time** (9): Enter the time of submission.
* **submission_user** (10): Enter your username or ID.
* **submission_location** (11): Enter the location of submission.
* **submission_device** (12): Enter the device you used to submit the form.
* **submission_ip** (13): Enter your IP address.
* **submission_browser** (14): Enter the browser you used to submit the form.
* **submission_os** (15): Enter the operating system of your device.
* **submission_referrer** (16): Enter the referrer or source of your submission.
* **submission_url** (17): Enter the URL of the page where you submitted the form.
* **submission_ip_address** (18): Enter your IP address.
* **submission_user_agent** (19): Enter your user agent.
* **submission_user_language** (20): Enter your language preference.
* **submission_user_timezone** (21): Enter your time zone.
* **submission_user_timezone_offset** (22): Enter your time zone offset.
* **submission_user_country** (23): Enter the country where you are located.
* **submission_user_city** (24): Enter the city where you are located.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our medical staff for assistance.
