# Request For Cleaning Services Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Request for Cleaning Services Form is designed to provide the cleaning services team with the necessary information to deliver high-quality cleaning services to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer name** in the **Customer name** field.
2. Select the cleaning services you require (Cleaning, Laundry, or Ironing) in the **Services** field.
3. Enter the **Date of service** when you would like the cleaning services to take place.
4. Choose the **Time of service** when you would like the cleaning services to take place.
5. Add any additional **Notes** about your cleaning service request.
6. Enter your **Contact number** so we can reach you back.
7. If you have a **Email** address, please enter it so we can contact you.
8. Finally, select **Submit** to send in your form, or **Cancel** if you do not want to proceed with your cleaning service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request for cleaning services**: This is a brief description of the service you are requesting. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Customer name**: This is your name, which will help us identify who is making the request.
* **Services**: Select one or multiple services you would like to have (Cleaning, Laundry, or Ironing).
* **Date of service**: This is when you would like the cleaning service to take place.
* **Time of service**: This is when you would like the cleaning service to take place.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for any additional information you would like to provide about your request.
* **Contact number**: This is your contact number, so we can reach out to you about your request.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Submit**: Select this option to send in your form, or **Cancel** if you do not want to proceed with your cleaning service request.
