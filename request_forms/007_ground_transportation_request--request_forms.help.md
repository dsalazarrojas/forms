# Ground Transportation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request ground transportation services, such as taxi or shuttle services, for users who need assistance with travel.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" field.
2. Select a date for your request in the "Request Date" field.
3. Enter the location where you would like to be picked up in the "Pick Up Location" field.
4. Enter the location where you would like to be dropped off in the "Drop Off Location" field.
5. Choose your preferred mode of transportation from the "Type of Transportation" field.
6. Enter the number of passengers in the "Number of Passengers" field.
7. Select a time for your travel in the "Time of Travel" field.
8. If you have any additional information or requests, enter it in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Request Date**: Select the date for which you are requesting ground transportation services.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Pick Up Location**: Enter the location where you would like to be picked up.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Drop Off Location**: Enter the location where you would like to be dropped off.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Type of Transportation**: Choose your preferred mode of transportation (e.g., Car, Bus, Train).
	+ **Required**: No
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of people you will be traveling with.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Time of Travel**: Select the time for which you would like to be picked up.
	+ **Required**: No
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or requests you may have.
	+ **Required**: No

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure that your ground transportation request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your administrator for assistance.
* Make sure to review your request carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate.
