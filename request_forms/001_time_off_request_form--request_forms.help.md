# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is a tool used to request time off from work. It helps you to specify your request details, including start and end dates, duration, and reasons for leave. This form is meant for requesting time off for various reasons, such as vacation, sick leave, or personal appointments. Please note that this form is not meant for duplicate submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Time Off Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including first name, last name, email, and work phone.
2. Select the correct time zone for your time off request.
3. Choose whether you have a time interval (yes, no, or other).
4. Fill in the start and end dates of your time off.
5. Specify the duration of your time off.
6. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (yes, no, or other).
7. Fill in the start and end times of your time off.
8. Add any comments or additional information for your time off request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your first and last name, email, and work phone number to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the start date of your time off.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the end date of your time off.
* **Time Interval** (`leave_interval`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you have a time interval (yes, no, or other) for your time off.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the time zone where you will be taking your time off.
* **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`, text, required/optional): Specify the reason for your time off request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or additional information for your time off request.
* **Time Off Request** (`time_off_request`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you are making a time off request (yes, no, or other).
* **Start Time** (`time_off_start_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the start time of your time off.
* **End Time** (`time_off_end_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the end time of your time off.
* **Work Phone** (`contact_work_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Work Email** (`contact_work_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address to help us contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Duration** (`time_off_duration`, text, required/optional): Specify the duration of your time off.
* **Time Off Days** (`time_off_days`, text, required/optional): Specify the number of days for your time off.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (yes, no, or other).
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, text, required/optional): Specify the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, text, required/optional): Specify the end time of your leave.
* **Start Date** (`leave_start_date_2`, text, required/optional): Choose the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`leave_end_date_2`, text, required/optional): Choose the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (`leave_reason_2`, text, required/optional): Specify the reason for your leave.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or additional information for your leave request.
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, text, required/optional): Specify the start time of your workday.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, text, required/optional): Specify the end time of your workday.
