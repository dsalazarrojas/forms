# Financial Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers or clients for the purpose of conducting a financial consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the financial institution you are affiliated with from the drop-down list.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter the name and contact information of your financial advisor.
4. Select the topic of the consultation (you can select multiple topics).
5. Add any additional notes or details about the consultation.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Institution** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the financial institution you are affiliated with (e.g. Bank, Insurance Company, Wealth Management Firm, etc.).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Financial Advisor** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of your financial advisor.
* **Consultation Topic** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more topics for the consultation (e.g. General Consultation, Investment Advice, Insurance Planning, etc.).
* **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or details about the consultation.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (none in this case).
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
