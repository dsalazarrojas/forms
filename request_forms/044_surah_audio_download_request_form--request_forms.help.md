# Surah Audio Download Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surah Audio Download Request Form is designed to collect information for fulfilling user requests for specific Surah audio downloads.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start the process, click on the "Surah Audio Download Request Form" link.
2.  Read each field explanation below to understand what information is needed and why.
3.  Fill in the fields accurately and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Request Details (Field 1)
*   **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your request. This is where you can describe the specific Surah audio you need.

### 2. Name (Field 2)
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Provide your name as you want it to be recorded in our records.

### 3. Email (Field 3)
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further information about your request.

### 4. Contact Number (Field 4)
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you have a contact number, please enter it so we can reach out to you about your request.

### 5. Mosque (Field 5)
*   **Mosque** (`mosque`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available mosques. This helps us identify your location and provide relevant information.

### 6. Audio Type (Field 6)
*   **Audio Type** (`audio_type`, select one, optional): Choose the specific type of audio you are requesting (Quran, Naat, Wazifa, or Other).

### 7. Reason for Request (Field 7)
*   **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for why you need this particular Surah audio.

### 8. Follow-up (Field 8)
*   **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select multiple, optional): Let us know if you'd like to be contacted after fulfilling your request.

### 9. Additional Notes (Field 9)
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): If you have any further information or comments about your request, please add them here.
