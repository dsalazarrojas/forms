# Student Advise Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Advise Request Form is a tool for students to request guidance or advice from academic advisors. It helps to ensure that advisors are aware of the student's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the department or topic you are seeking advice on.
3. Choose your preferred advisor (if applicable).
4. Indicate your areas of interest and programs related to your request.
5. Confirm whether you have submitted a request in the past.
6. Accept the terms and conditions of the form.
7. Confirm that you agree to the privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Select the department or topic you are seeking advice on.
* **Topic** (`topic`, text, not required): Enter the specific topic you are seeking guidance on.
* **Advisor** (`advisor`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred advisor (if applicable).
* **Mentorship Type** (`mentorship_type`, select_multiple, not required): Select the type of mentorship you are seeking.
* **Programs** (`programs`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate whether you are affiliated with any programs related to your request.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, text, not required): Enter any topics that interest you and relate to your request.
* **Related Programs** (`related_programs`, text, not required): Enter any related programs you are affiliated with.
* **Request For** (`request_for`, select_one, not required): Confirm that this is a new request.
* **Related Topics** (`related_topics`, text, not required): Enter any related topics that may be relevant to your request.
* **Related Programs Other** (`related_programs_other`, text, not required): Enter any other related programs not listed above.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or information you believe are relevant to your request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, not required): Confirm that you have submitted a request in the past.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, select_one, not required): Accept the terms and conditions of the form.
* **Agree to Privacy** (`agree_to_privacy`, select_one, not required): Confirm that you agree to the privacy policy.

Note: This is a general guide, you should always refer to your institution's specific policies and procedures for the most accurate and up-to-date information.
