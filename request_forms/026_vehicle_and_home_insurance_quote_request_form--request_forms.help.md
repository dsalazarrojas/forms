# Vehicle And Home Insurance Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a vehicle and home insurance quote. It is intended to gather information about your vehicle and home details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select or enter the requested information in the corresponding fields. If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can consult the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vehicle you own or would like to insure from the dropdown menu.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one, optional): Select the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your home.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address.
* **Home Year Built** (`home_year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year your home was built.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your driver license number.
* **Driver License State** (`driver_license_state`, select_one, optional): Select your driver license state.
* **Driver License Expiration** (`driver_license_expiration`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date of your driver license.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle license number.
* **Vehicle License State** (`vehicle_license_state`, select_one, optional): Select your vehicle license state.
* **Vehicle Vehicle ID Number** (`vehicle_vehicle_id_number`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle vehicle ID number.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vehicle_vin`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle VIN number.
* **Vehicle Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_vehicle_make`, select_one, optional): Select the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Vehicle Year of Manufacture** (`vehicle_vehicle_year_of_manufacture`, number, optional): Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Home Home Type** (`home_home_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your home.
* **Home Home Address** (`home_home_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address.
* **Home Home Year Built** (`home_home_year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year your home was built.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Vehicle ID Number** (`vehicle_vehicle_id_number`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle vehicle ID number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the fields that apply to you.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
