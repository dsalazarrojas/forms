# Autopay Change Or Cancellation Request - Help Guide

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Type** (Select the type of change you need): Select the type of change you need to make in the "Autopay Change Or Cancellation Request" form. The options are:
	+ Change payment method
	+ Update billing amount
	+ Change payment date
	+ Cancel autopay entirely
	+ Temporarily suspend autopay
* **Account Holder Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name as it appears on the account.
* **Account Number** (Text, Required): Enter the account or customer number associated with the account.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive confirmation of the change.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **Current Payment Method** (Select, Required): Select the current payment method used for autopay. The options are:
	+ Credit Card (Visa/Mastercard/Amex)
	+ Debit Card
	+ Bank Account (ACH)
	+ PayPal
	+ Other digital wallet
* **New Payment Method Type** (Select, Optional): If changing the payment method, select the new method to use. The options are:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Bank Account (ACH)
	+ PayPal
	+ Same as current
	+ Not applicable - cancelling
* **New Card Last Four Digits** (Text, Optional): If changing the payment method to a card, enter the last 4 digits of the new card number.
* **Billing Amount** (Number, Optional): If changing the billing amount, enter the new recurring amount.
* **Payment Frequency** (Select, Optional): Select how often payments should occur. The options are:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Preferred Payment Date** (Select, Optional): If changing the payment date, select the preferred payment date. The options are:
	+ 1st of month
	+ 15th of month
	+ Last day of month
	+ Every Monday
	+ Every Friday
	+ On invoice date
* **Effective Date** (Date, Required): Select the date when the change should take effect.
* **Reason for Change or Cancellation** (Text, Optional): If you wish to provide additional information, enter it here.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): If you have any other comments, enter them here.
* **I authorize this change to my autopay settings** (Select, Required): Check to confirm you authorize the change.
* **Date of Authorization** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are authorizing the change.

Please fill out the form carefully, as incorrect information may delay or affect the processing of your change request.
Make sure to double-check your email address, account number, and phone number for accuracy.
Enter the new card last 4 digits only if changing the payment method to a card.
Enter the new recurring amount only if changing the billing amount.
