# Credit Note Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Note Request Form is used to generate a credit note for customers who wish to offset a debt against their existing credit balance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a valid credit balance.
* Enter the date of the credit request.
* Enter the amount of the credit note.
* Enter your name as the credit requestor.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **credit_request_date** (credit_request_date, date, true): Enter the date of the credit request in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **credit_note_amount** (credit_note_amount, number, true): Enter the amount of the credit note to be generated.
* **credit_requestor_name** (credit_requestor_name, text, true): Enter your name as the credit requestor.
* **credit_requestor_email** (email, email, true): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your credit request.
* **credit_requestor_phone** (credit_requestor_phone, text, false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure the credit request date matches the date of your original invoice or payment.
* Only submit one request per date to avoid duplication and ensure efficient processing of your credit request.
