# Material Specification Approval Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for new or revised material specifications to be added to the inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the material specification in the "Material Specification" field, describing the new or revised material to be approved.
2. Provide a detailed description of the request in the "Request Form" field, including any relevant information or justification for the request.
3. Enter the requestor's details in the "Requestor Details" fields:
	* "Requestor Title": Enter your job title or position.
	* "Requestor Name": Enter your name.
	* "Requestor Phone": Enter your phone number.
	* "Requestor Email": Enter your email address.
4. Select the "Prophets Form" field to choose the correct material specification option from the provided list.
5. Enter the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields if applicable, or leave them blank if not required.
6. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Material Specification** (`material_specification_approval_request`): Enter a detailed description of the new or revised material specification.
* **Request Form** (`request_form`): Provide a detailed description of the request, including any relevant information or justification.
* **Requestor Details** (`requestor_details`): Enter your job title, name, phone number, and email address.
* **Requestor Title** (`requestor_title`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`): Enter your name.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of the request, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`): Enter the time of the request, in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Prophets Form** (`prophets_form`): Choose the correct material specification option from the provided list.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about the request.
