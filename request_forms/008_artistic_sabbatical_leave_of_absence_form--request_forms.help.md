# Artistic Sabbatical Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a sabbatical leave of absence for artistic purposes, allowing you to focus on artistic projects, research, or professional development. Please complete this form thoroughly to ensure your absence is approved and your needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using your real name as it appears on your official records.
2. Enter your department or faculty name in the "Department or Faculty" field.
3. Select the start and end dates of your leave in the "Leave Start Date" and "Leave End Date" fields, respectively.
4. In the "Primary Purpose of Sabbatical" field, select one of the provided options that best describes your artistic project or goal.
5. Provide a detailed description of your project proposal in the "Detailed Project Proposal" field.
6. If applicable, specify the funding or grant source for your project in the "Funding or Grant Source" field.
7. Indicate whether you agree to return to your position for at least one year after your sabbatical in the "I agree to return to my position for at least one year" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number while on leave in the "Contact Phone Number during Absence" field, if desired.
9. Finally, select the correct option to acknowledge the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist/Employee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Department or Faculty**: Enter your department or faculty name where you are affiliated.
* **Leave Start Date**: Select the date you wish to begin your sabbatical leave.
* **Leave End Date**: Select the date you wish to end your sabbatical leave.
* **Primary Purpose of Sabbatical**: Choose one of the provided options that best describes your artistic project or goal:
	+ **Creative Production**: For projects that focus on creating new artistic works.
	+ **Scholarly Research**: For research projects that aim to contribute to the field of art or art-related fields.
	+ **Professional Development**: For projects that enhance your artistic skills or expertise.
	+ **International Exchange**: For projects that involve international collaboration or research.
	+ **Other**: For any other valid artistic purpose not listed above.
* **Detailed Project Proposal**: Describe your artistic project proposal in detail, including what you will accomplish and why it is important.
* **Anticipated Benefit to the Organization**: Explain how your sabbatical will improve your future work and contributions to the organization.
* **Funding or Grant Source**: If applicable, specify the funding or grant source for your project.
* **I Agree to return to my position for at least one year**: Check "I Agree" to indicate your commitment to returning to your position after your sabbatical.
* **Contact Phone Number during Absence**: Enter your contact phone number during your absence, if desired.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date and time you are submitting your application.
* **Supervisor Approval**: This section is for administrative use only and does not require input from the applicant.
* **Return Agreement**: Acknowledge your commitment to return to your position for at least one year after your sabbatical.
