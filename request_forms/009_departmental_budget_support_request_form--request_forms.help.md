# Departmental Budget Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Departmental Budget Support Request Form is a tool designed to collect information from users who require budget support from their department. Completing this form ensures that your request is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form title, a short description of the request.
2. Provide the context of your request, a brief explanation of why you need the budget support.
3. Attach any relevant documents that support your request.
4. Date and time of your budget request.
5. Give a detailed description of the budget you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: (`departmental_budget_support_request_form`, Text, Optional) A short description of your request, ideally no longer than 60 characters.
* **Context**: (`context`, Text, Optional) A brief explanation of why you need the budget support, this will help us understand the purpose of your request.
* **Documents**: (`documents`, Note, Optional) Any supporting documents that will help us process your request.
* **Budget Request Date**: (`budget_request_date`, Date, Optional) The date when you are requesting the budget support.
* **Budget Request Time**: (`budget_request_time`, Time, Optional) The time when you are requesting the budget support.
* **Budget Description**: (`budget_description`, Text, Optional) A detailed description of the budget you are requesting, explain how much and what you need it for. 

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
- Attach any relevant documents that support your request.
- Be as detailed as possible in your budget description.
- Check that your form title is no longer than 60 characters.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
