# Withdrawal Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Withdrawal Authorization Form is a form that must be completed by a student's parent or guardian to authorize the student's withdrawal from a program or activity. This form serves as a formal document that indicates the student's permission to withdraw from the program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name as it appears on their student ID.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Fill in the parent's name.
4. Enter the parent's phone number.
5. Provide a reason for the student's withdrawal.
6. Sign the student's signature line below "Reason for Absence".
7. Sign the parent's signature line below "Reason for Absence".
8. (Optional) If the student is returning, fill in the date they are returning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Page 1): Enter the student's name as it appears on their student ID.
* **Student ID** (Page 1): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Parent Name** (Page 1): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (Page 1): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Reason for Absence** (Page 1): Provide a brief explanation for the student's withdrawal.
* **Student Signature** (Page 1): The student must sign below "Reason for Absence" to indicate their agreement.
* **Parent Signature** (Page 1): The parent or guardian must sign below "Reason for Absence" to indicate their agreement.
* **Date Submitted** (Page 2): The date and time the form was submitted. *Optional*
* **Date Returned** (Page 2): The date and time the student is returning. *Optional*
* **Teacher Name** (Page 2): Enter the name of the teacher authorizing the student's withdrawal.
* **Teacher Phone** (Page 2): Enter the phone number of the teacher authorizing the student's withdrawal.
* **Comments** (Page 2): Enter any additional comments or explanations about the student's withdrawal.

## Tips
* Make sure to have the student and parent sign the form in the presence of the teacher to ensure authenticity.
* Ensure that the student and parent understand the implications of the withdrawal and the date of return.
* This form is required for every withdrawal, even if the student is returning or not.
