# Expense Form Closing Event (7/12 14) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for expense reports to determine if an expense event has been closed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the submitter.
2. Answer whether the expense event has been closed.
3. Enter the expense date.
4. Provide a brief description of the goals of the expense event.
5. Enter the amount of the expense.
6. Select whether invoices have been uploaded.
7. Select whether receipts have been uploaded.
8. Provide any additional supporting information.
9. Enter your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter's Information** (`page_1`, text, required/optional): This field is for the submitter to enter their name and any other relevant information.
* **Submitter's Information** (`submitter_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for the submitter to enter any additional information they deem necessary.
* **Expense Details** (`Expense Details`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the expense event has been closed.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the expense event.
* **Goals** (`Goals`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the goals of the expense event.
* **Amount** (`page_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Amount (USD)** (`page_3_1`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the expense in USD.
* **Upload Invoices** (`page_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether invoices have been uploaded.
* **Upload Receipts** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether receipts have been uploaded.
* **Supporting Information** (`page_6`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional supporting information.
* **Comments** (`page_7`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments about the expense event.
* **Submitter's Contact** (`page_8`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Phone** (`page_8_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`page_8_2`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter's Info** (`page_9`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
