# immigration_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The immigration_enquiry_form is designed for individuals to submit an immigration enquiry, providing essential information about their personal details and the reason for their enquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional). Please enter your full first name as it appears on your passport or ID.
2.  Enter your **Surname** (`surname`, `text`, optional). Please enter your full surname as it appears on your passport or ID.
3.  Specify your **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, `text`, optional). Please enter the country where you were born or hold citizenship.
4.  Explain the **Reason for Enquiry** (`reason_for_enquiry`, `text`, optional). Please provide a brief description of your immigration-related question or concern.
5.  Select your **Enquiry Type** (`enquiry_type`, `select_one`, required). Please choose one of the following options: `General Enquiry`, `Document Verification`, or `Process Information`.
6.  Enter your **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional). Please format your contact number as `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `+X(XXX) XXX-XXXX` to ensure it's correctly understood.
7.  Enter your **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional). Please provide a valid email address for correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full first name as it appears on your passport or ID.
*   **Surname** (`surname`, `text`, optional): Enter your full surname as it appears on your passport or ID.

## Tips
