# Performance Venue Layout Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Venue Layout Change Request Form is used to collect and process requests for changes to the performance venue's layout. This form helps to ensure that all layout change requests are properly documented and reviewed by the relevant stakeholders before approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the reason for the proposed layout change in the "Reason for Layout Change" field.
2. Specify the proposed layout change details in the "Proposed Layout Change" field.
3. Indicate the proposed time of the change in the "Time of Proposed Change" field.
4. Choose the correct date format for the proposed change in the "Date Of Proposed Change Date" field.
5. Review and confirm the approval status of the proposed change.
6. Review and confirm the layout change approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Layout Change Request**: Enter a brief description of the proposed layout change.
* **Reason for Layout Change**: Enter a clear and concise reason for the proposed layout change.
* **Proposed Layout Change**: Enter the proposed layout change details.
* **Proposed Layout Change Details**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you want to propose the change.
* **Approval Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the current status of the proposed change.
* **Layout Change Approval**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the change is approved.
* **Date of Proposed Change**: Choose the date in one of the following formats: 'YYYY-MM-DD', 'MM/dd', 'yy/yy', 'd-M-D-YYYY', 'yy-M-D-YYYY', or 'yy/yy/MD'.
* **Time of Proposed Change**: Choose the time in one of the following formats: 'HH:MM', 'HH:MM:SS', 'HH', or 'MM'.
* **Date Of Proposed Change Date**: Enter the proposed date in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Time Of Proposed Change**: Enter the proposed time in 'HH:MM' format.
* **Date_of_proposed_change_date_mm**: Enter the proposed date in 'MM/dd' format.
* **Date_of_proposed_change_date_yy**: Enter the proposed date in 'yy' format.
* **Date_of_proposed_change_date_yy_yy**: Enter the proposed date in 'yy/yy' format.
* **Date Of Proposed Change Date Ymd**: Enter the proposed date in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Date of Proposed Change (D-M-D-YYYY)**: Enter the proposed date in 'D-M-D-YYYY' format.
* **Date of Proposed Change (MM/dd)**: Enter the proposed date in 'MM/dd' format.
* **Date of Proposed Change (yy-M-D-YYYY)**: Enter the proposed date in 'yy-M-D-YYYY' format.
* **Date of Proposed Change (yy/yy/MM/dd)**: Enter the proposed date in 'yy/yy/MM/dd' format.
* **Date of Proposed Change (D-M)**: Enter the proposed date in 'D-M' format.
* **Date Of Proposed Change Time**: Enter the proposed time in 'HH:MM' format.
* **Time of Proposed Change (MM)**: Enter the proposed time in 'MM' format.
* **Time of Proposed Change (MMM)**: Enter the proposed time in 'MMM' format.
* **Time of Proposed Change (HH)**: Enter the proposed time in 'HH' format.
