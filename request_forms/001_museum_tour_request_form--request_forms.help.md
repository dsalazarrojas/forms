# Museum Tour Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Tour Request Form is a tool for schools and educational institutions to request a tour at our museum. This form helps us understand the visitor's details and ensure that we can provide an enjoyable and educational experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information accurately and completely. This includes your First name, Last name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Choose a date and time for your visit. Please note that these fields are not required.
3. Select the number of visitors attending the tour.
4. Enter the name of your school.
5. If you are bringing a teacher or group, enter their name(s).
6. Choose a tour duration that suits your needs: 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
7. Select the type of school group (Kindergarten, Elementary, High School, College, or University).
8. If you have any special requests, please let us know in the box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your visit. Note that this field is not required, but it helps us plan for your tour.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a start time for your visit. Similar to the Date of Visit field, this is not required.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Choose an end time for your visit. Like Start Time, this field is not required.
* **Number of Visitors** (`number_of_visitors`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of visitors attending the tour.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your teacher or group leader.
* **Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, select one, required/optional): Choose a tour duration from 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
* **School Group** (`school_group`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the type of school group you are bringing (Kindergarten, Elementary, High School, College, or University).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
