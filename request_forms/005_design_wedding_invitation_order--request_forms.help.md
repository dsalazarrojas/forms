# Design Wedding Invitation Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Design Wedding Invitation Order" form is a tool used to request design services for wedding invitations. It gathers essential details to create a comprehensive wedding invitation order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
* Fill in the required fields with the necessary details for your wedding invitation order.
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Invitation Order** (1. Wedding Invitation Order, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the details of the wedding invitation order you would like to design.
* **Wedding Details** (2. Wedding Details, text, required: false):
	+ Provide wedding date, location, and any other relevant details to ensure we have a clear understanding of your vision.
* **Guest Information** (3. Guest Information, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name, phone number, and email of your guests to help us tailor the design to their preferences.
* **Quantities** (4. Quantities, text, required: false):
	+ Specify the quantity of invitations you need for your wedding.
* **Working Hours** (5. Working Hours, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any specific working hours you have for your design project.
* **Projected Ship Date** (6. Projected Ship Date, date, required: false):
	+ Select the date by which you would like to receive your designed wedding invitations.
* **Contact Info** (7. Contact Info, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional contact information you have for yourself or your partner.
* **Payment Terms** (8. Payment Terms, text, required: false):
	+ Specify any specific payment terms or arrangements you have for your design project.
* **Notes** (9. Notes, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or requests you have for your wedding invitation design.
* **Email** (10. Email, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (11. Phone, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Address** (12. Address, text, required: false):
	+ Provide your shipping address for us to send your designed wedding invitations.
* **City** (13. City, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your city for us to ensure correct shipping.
* **State** (14. State, select one, required: false):
	+ Select the state where you will be receiving your designed wedding invitations.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (15. Zip/Postal Code, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your zip code for us to ensure correct shipping.
* **Contract** (16. Contract, select multiple, required: false):
	+ Confirm if you have a contract with us or another design service.
* **Contract 1** (17. Contract 1, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any details regarding your contract with us or another design service.
* **Contract 2** (18. Contract 2, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional details regarding your contract with us or another design service.
* **Contract 3** (19. Contract 3, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any further details regarding your contract with us or another design service.
* **Contract 4** (20. Contract 4, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any final details regarding your contract with us or another design service.

By following these steps and filling out the form, you will be able to provide us with all the necessary information to create a beautiful and personalized wedding invitation design for you.
