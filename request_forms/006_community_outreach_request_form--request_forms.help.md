# Community Outreach Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from community members who are seeking assistance, resources, or services from the organization. Please use this form to submit your request and provide detailed information about your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Provide your contact information**: Fill out your first and last name, and a valid email address.
2. **Choose your role**: Describe your position or affiliation with the organization or group you are representing.
3. **Describe your request**: Choose the type of request you are making and explain the details of your request thoroughly.
4. **Provide additional information**: Specify if you have a budget available for paid services and if you have any other requirements or needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Information** (Page 1):
	+ **First Name**: Provide your first name as per your identity.
	+ **Last Name**: Provide your last name as per your identity.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Your Role** (Page 1):
	+ This field is to describe your position or affiliation with the organization or group you are representing.
* **Request Details** (Page 1):
	+ This is the main description of your request, where you can specify what assistance or services you are seeking.
* **Type of Request** (Page 2):
	+ Choose the type of request you are making: Volunteer Support, Event Assistance, Resource Request, Speaking Engagement, Partnership Opportunity, Information Request, or Other.
* **Detailed Description** (Page 2):
	+ This field is for further elaboration of your request and any specific requirements.
* **Event Date** (Page 3):
	+ If applicable, provide the date of the event or activity you are seeking assistance for.
* **Event Time** (Page 3):
	+ If applicable, provide the start time of the event or activity you are seeking assistance for.
* **Location** (Page 3):
	+ Specify the location where the event or activity will take place.
* **Number of Volunteers Needed** (Page 4):
	+ If applicable, indicate the number of volunteers you are seeking.
* **Skills or Expertise Required** (Page 4):
	+ Select all the skills or expertise you require for the event or activity.
* **Resources or Materials Needed** (Page 4):
	+ Describe any resources or materials you need for the event or activity.
* **Urgency Level** (Page 5):
	+ Choose how soon you need the assistance or services: Urgent (Within 1 Week), Soon (1-2 Weeks), Moderate (2-4 Weeks), Flexible (1-2 Months), or Planning Ahead (2+ Months).
* **Is Budget Available** (Page 5):
	+ Specify if you have a budget available for paid services: True, No - Volunteer Based, or Partial Funding.
* **Have You Worked With Us Before** (Page 5):
	+ If applicable, mark 'True' if you have previously worked with the organization.
* **Additional Information** (Page 6):
	+ Provide any additional information that would be helpful for us to know about your request.
* **Submit** (Page 6):
	+ Once you have filled out all the required information, click submit to send your request to the organization.
