# AI Tools Subscription Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Tools Subscription Request form is used to request access to specific AI tools or services. This form helps administrators determine the appropriate level of access and duration of use for each requested tool.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and department/team requesting access.
2. Enter the exact name of the AI tool or service you are requesting access to.
3. Describe how you plan to use the tool or service (intended use case).
4. Select the required access level (Read only, Contributor, or Admin).
5. Choose the duration of access needed (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or Indefinite).
6. If applicable, specify the name of the manager or approver who needs to approve your request.
7. Provide any additional comments or justification for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Enter your first and last name.
	+ This field is required to identify you as the person requesting access.
* **Department/Team**: Enter the department or team requesting access to the AI tool or service.
	+ This field is required to help administrators understand who is requesting access.
* **Requester email**: Enter your email address for notifications and approvals.
	+ This field is required for internal communication and approval tracking.
* **Tool or service name**: Enter the exact name of the AI tool or service you are requesting access to.
	+ This field is required to ensure the correct tool or service is accessed.
* **Intended use case**: Describe how you plan to use the AI tool or service.
	+ This field is required to understand how you plan to utilize the tool.
* **Access level required**: Select the level of access needed for the AI tool or service.
	+ Options:
		- Read only: Access for viewing and basic functionality.
		- Contributor: Access for creating and editing basic content.
		- Admin: Full access to manage and configure the tool.
* **Duration of access needed**: Select the duration of access required for the AI tool or service.
	+ Options:
		- 1 month: Access for a limited time period.
		- 3 months: Access for a moderate time period.
		- 6 months: Access for a long-term period.
		- Indefinite: Access with no time limit.
* **Approval required from**: If applicable, specify the name of the manager or approver who needs to approve your request.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps administrators track approval chains.
* **Additional comments or justification**: Provide any additional comments or justification for your request.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps administrators understand your request better.

## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your responses.
* Ensure you have filled out a similar form in the past or have the necessary information to complete this form.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, contact the form administrators for clarification.
