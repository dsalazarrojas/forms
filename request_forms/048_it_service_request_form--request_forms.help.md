# it_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_service_request_form is designed to collect information from end-users about their IT service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing in the **request_details** field.
2. Choose the category that best describes the type of request you are making (e.g., **high**, **low**, or **medium**).
3. Select the status of the request (e.g., **open** or **closed**).
4. Indicate whether the issue should be assigned to someone (**Yes** or **No**).
5. Enter the due date for the request (if applicable).
6. Choose the start and end times for the request (if applicable).
7. Provide your email address so that the IT team can contact you about your request.
8. Optionally, provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **request_details** (text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **description** (text, optional): Provide additional details about the issue (if needed).
* **category** (select, optional): Choose the category that best describes the type of request you are making (e.g., **high**, **low**, or **medium**).
* **status** (select, optional): Select the status of the request (e.g., **open** or **closed**).
* **assigned_to** (select, optional): Indicate whether the issue should be assigned to someone (**Yes** or **No**).
* **due_date** (date, optional): Enter the due date for the request.
* **start_time** (time, optional): Choose the start time for the request.
* **end_time** (time, optional): Choose the end time for the request.
* **submitter** (email, optional): Provide your email address so that the IT team can contact you about your request.
* **phone** (text, optional): Optionally, provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
