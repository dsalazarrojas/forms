# Building Entry Pass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Entry Pass Request Form is designed to collect information from visitors who need to access a building or campus. This form will help to streamline the process of gaining access, ensuring that visitors have the necessary clearance and access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and company/organization.
2. Enter your job title or position.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Specify the purpose of your visit, including the date and start and end times.
6. Choose the type of visit (e.g. Business meeting, delivery, etc.).
7. Enter a detailed description of the purpose of your visit.
8. Enter the name of the host or contact person.
9. Enter the host's department or location.
10. Enter the host's email address.
11. If required, specify the building location, floor/room number, and parking needs.
12. If required, specify any special access areas needed.
13. Choose your visitor category (e.g. internal employee, business partner, etc.).
14. Specify the frequency of your visits.
15. If applicable, specify the number of visitors and names of additional visitors.
16. Review and agree to the building rules and security procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Company or organization**: Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job title or position**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Email address**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Visit details**: Enter a brief description of your visit purpose.
* **Requested visit date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Start time**: Enter the start time of your visit.
* **End time**: Enter the end time of your visit.
* **Purpose of visit**: Choose the type of visit (e.g. Business meeting, delivery, etc.).
* **Detailed description**: Enter a detailed description of the purpose of your visit.
* **Host or contact person name**: Enter the name of the host or contact person.
* **Host department or location**: Enter the host's department or location.
* **Host email address**: Enter the host's email address.
* **Building location**: Enter the building or campus location.
* **Floor/room number**: Enter the specific floor or room number.
* **Parking needed**: Specify if you need parking.
* **Special access areas needed**: Specify any special access areas needed.
* **Visitor category**: Choose your visitor category (e.g. internal employee, business partner, etc.).
* **Frequency of visits**: Specify the frequency of your visits.
* **Number of visitors**: If applicable, enter the number of visitors.
* **Names of additional visitors**: If applicable, enter the names of additional visitors.
* **Background check status**: Specify your background check status.
* **Badge preference**: Choose your badge preference.
* **Acknowledgment**: Review and agree to the building rules and security procedures.

Note: Some fields are not always required, but they are included in the form to ensure that visitors provide the necessary information for access management.
