# Faculty Engagement Data Access Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to data about faculty members for a specified purpose. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to help us understand your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the following information:
	* A brief description of the purpose of the request.
	* The name of the faculty member you are requesting data about.
	* The start and end dates of the period for which you are requesting data.
	* The duration of the request.
	* The faculty department of the faculty member.
	* Your acknowledgment of having reviewed or not reviewed the policy related to data access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Request** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): A short description of the purpose of the request.
* **Purpose** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the reason for the request.
* **Faculty Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the faculty member you are requesting data about.
* **Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): The start date of the period for which you are requesting data.
* **End Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): The end date of the period for which you are requesting data.
* **Duration** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): The length of time you are requesting access to data.
* **Faculty Department** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): The department of the faculty member you are requesting data about.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your understanding of the policy related to data access by selecting one of the options.
	+ You can select 'I have reviewed and understand the policy.', 'I have not reviewed the policy.', or 'I am unsure.'
