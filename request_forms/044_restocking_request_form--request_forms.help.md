# Restocking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restocking request form is used to request additional supplies of products on campus for business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the product that you need to restock from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter the quantity you would like to restock.
3.  Enter the contact person's name and their business email and phone number.
4.  Specify the location on campus where you need the product restocked.
5.  Click submit to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What product do you need to restock** (product_id, select_one, required): Select the product that you need to restock from the dropdown menu. This field will help the campus team know what product to restock.
* **How much would you like to restock** (quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity you would like to restock. Please be as accurate as possible to help the campus team plan and order the correct amount of supplies.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person who will be responsible for receiving the restocked product.
* **Business Email** (email, email, required): Enter the business email address of the contact person.
* **Business Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter the business phone number of the contact person.
* **Location on Campus** (restock_location, text, required): Specify the location on campus where you need the product restocked. This will help the campus team know where to deliver the product.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to help the campus team process the request efficiently.
* Please do not duplicate requests for the same product, check with the campus team if you are unsure.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the campus team.
