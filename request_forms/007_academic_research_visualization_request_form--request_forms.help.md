# Academic Research Visualization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by researchers to request visualizations for their academic projects. It helps visualization experts understand the project's requirements and deliver the most suitable visualizations for the researcher's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Project Overview** section with a brief description of your research project.
2. Provide the **Title of the Research Project**, including the full name of the study.
3. Enter the **Name of Principal Investigator**, who is the lead researcher for the project.
4. Enter the **PI Contact Email**, which should be an official institutional email address.
5. In the **Visualization Requirements** section, select the type of visualization you need, such as a static chart or a 3D model.
6. Describe the **Dataset to be Visualized** in brief, including the number of variables and observations.
7. Choose the **Current Format of Your Data**, such as CSV or JSON.
8. Select the **Intended Audience** for the visualization, e.g., peer researchers, general public, or students.
9. In the **Project Logistics** section, select the **Desired Completion Date** for the visualization.
10. Indicate the **Urgency of the Request**, such as Low, Medium, or High.
11. If needed, specify if the visualization needs to be **Publication-Ready**.
12. If required, specify any **Specific Color or Branding Requirements**.
13. If applicable, provide a **Link to the Data Repository**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Overview**: A brief description of your research project.
* **Title of the Research Project**: The full name of the study.
* **Name of Principal Investigator**: The lead researcher for the project.
* **PI Contact Email**: Official institutional email address.
* **Visualization Type**: Choose the most appropriate format for your needs.
* **Dataset to be Visualized**: Briefly describe the dataset, including variables and observations.
* **Current Format of Your Data**: Select how your data will be provided (CSV, JSON, etc.).
* **Intended Audience**: Select all who the visualization is intended for (peer researchers, general public, etc.).
* **Desired Completion Date**: Requested turnaround time for the visualization.
* **Urgency of the Request**: Indicate the importance of the timeline.
* **Publication-Ready**: If the visualization needs to be publication-ready.
* **Specific Color or Branding Requirements**: If you have specific color or branding requirements.
* **Link to Data Repository**: If you have a link to where the data can be accessed.
