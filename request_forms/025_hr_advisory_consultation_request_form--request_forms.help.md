# HR Advisory Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Advisory Consultation Request Form is designed to allow employees to request HR consultations at their preferred time. This form helps the HR department schedule meetings efficiently and ensure that employees can receive guidance and advice in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a preferred appointment time that works best for you.
2. Provide a brief description of the reason for your consultation in the "details" field.
3. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (request_form): This field is a text box where you can enter your request for HR consultation.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (preferred_appointment_time): Choose a time that works best for your consultation. You can use the time picker to select a specific time.
* **Details** (details): Provide a brief explanation of why you need HR consultation. Be as detailed as needed, but concise enough to understand your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose a time that is realistic and works for everyone involved.
* Be as specific as possible when requesting consultation time to ensure the HR department can schedule accordingly.
* If you need to make a follow-up request, please submit a new form to avoid confusion.
