# Cloth Design Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloth Design Form is used to collect information for custom cloth design orders. Fill out this form with your details to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as "Required" in bold) to ensure we can process your order correctly.
2. Choose your preferred fabric type, category, and size from the provided options.
3. Select your color preference from the available options.
4. If you have any custom measurements or instructions, provide them in the designated field.
5. Indicate your desired completion date for your order.
6. Choose your estimated budget range.
7. Select how you heard about us.
8. Finally, confirm that you have read and accepted our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the order.
* **Email Address** (customer_email, email, required): We will send order confirmations and updates to this email address.
* **Phone Number** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for order updates and questions.
* **Fabric Type** (fabric_type, select_one, required): Select your preferred fabric material from the options provided (Cotton, Silk, Polyester, Linen, Wool, Rayon, Denim).
* **Cloth Category** (cloth_category, select_one, required): Select the type of garment you need (Shirt, Dress, Pants, Skirt, Jacket, Suit, Other).
* **Size** (size_selection, select_one, required): Choose your size from the available options (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, Custom).
* **Color Preference** (color_preference, select_one, required): Select your primary color preference from the options provided (Black, White, Navy, Gray, Red, Blue, Green, Custom).
* **Design Style** (design_style, select_one, required): Select your preferred style aesthetic (Casual, Formal, Business, Sporty, Bohemian, Classic, Trendy).
* **Custom Measurements** (custom_measurements, text, optional): Provide any specific measurements if needed.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, optional): Add any additional details or requirements.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required): Enter the number of items to order.
* **Desired Completion Date** (deadline_date, date, required): Choose when you need your order ready.
* **Budget Range** (budget_range, select_one, optional): Select your estimated budget from the options provided (Under $50, $50 - $100, $101 - $200, $201 - $500, Over $500).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (referral_source, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about us (Social Media, Friend or Family, Online Search, Advertisement, Previous Customer, Other).
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (terms_accepted, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and accepted our policies.

Please review and complete the form carefully to ensure that we can create your custom cloth design as per your preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
