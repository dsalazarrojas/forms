# Power Line Clearance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Power Line Clearance Application Form is a tool for users who require access to power lines for work or other activities. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide all the required information for the clearance process.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Provide detailed information about your work, including job details and the specific tasks you will perform on the power lines.
3. Enter your work location and contact number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
4. Select the relevant power line clearance type and equipment needed for your work.
5. Enter the date and start and end times for your work on the power lines.
6. Provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**:
	+ **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your name and other contact details, which will be used for communication and reference.
* **Work Details**:
	+ **Work Details** (`work_details`, text, required: false): Please provide detailed information about your work, including job type, equipment, and any other relevant details.
* **Job Details**:
	+ **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required: false): Provide specific job details, such as task type and location.
* **Location**:
	+ **Location** (`location`, text, required: true): Enter the exact location where you will be working on the power lines.
* **Contact Number**:
	+ **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: true): Enter your contact number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Power Line Clearance**:
	+ **Power Line Clearance** (`power_line_clearance`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of power line clearance you require.
* **Work Date**:
	+ **Work Date** (`work_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date when you will be working on the power lines.
* **Start Time**:
	+ **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: true): Enter the time when you will start working on the power lines.
* **End Time**:
	+ **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: true): Enter the time when you will end working on the power lines.
* **Equipment**:
	+ **Equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the equipment you will be using for your work.
* **Power Line Clearance Notes**:
	+ **Power Line Clearance Notes** (`power_line_clearance_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information about your power line clearance needs.
* **Contact Notes**:
	+ **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes for communication and reference.
