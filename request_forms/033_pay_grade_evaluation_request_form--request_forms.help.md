# Pay Grade Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay Grade Evaluation Request Form is used by employees to request changes to their current pay grade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please make sure you have a valid reason for requesting a pay grade change.
* Fill in your Employee ID, Employee Name, and Request Date.
* Choose the new Pay Grade you are requesting.
* Add any supporting documents that are relevant to your request.
* Have your manager sign and date the form.
* Have your manager add their signature, title, phone number, and email.
* Enter a valid reason for the manager's approval.
* Sign the form as the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (Number, Required): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Employee Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Request Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your request.
* **Pay Grade** (Select One, Required): Choose the new pay grade you are requesting.
* **Manager Signature** (Text, Required): Have your manager sign and date this field.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Required): Enter your manager's full name as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Reason for Change** (Text, Required): Enter a valid reason for your pay grade change request.
* **Supporting Documents** (Text, Optional): Add any supporting documents that are relevant to your request.
* **Manager Title** (Text, Required): Enter your manager's title as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager ID** (Number, Required): Enter your manager's ID number.
* **Pay Band** (Select One, Required): Choose the new pay band you are requesting.
* **Pay Band Grade** (Text, Required): Enter the grade for the new pay band you are requesting.
* **Employee Signature** (Text, Required): Sign and date this field as the employee.
* **Employee Title** (Text, Required): Enter your title as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Manager Approval** (Text, Optional): Have your manager approve or decline your request in this field.
* **Manager Approval Reason** (Text, Optional): Enter a reason for your manager's approval or decline.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have valid reasons for requesting a pay grade change before submitting this form.
* Ensure all fields marked 'Required' are completed.
* Ensure your manager completes their signature, title, phone number, and email before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have supporting documents that are relevant to your request.
* Submitting this form multiple times within a 3-month period for the same pay grade will not be accepted.
* Please allow 2-3 business days for your manager to review and process your request.
