# Out Of Network Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an out-of-network service request from a patient.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields. If a field is optional, fill it out if you have relevant information to provide.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field, as it will be used to facilitate communication between you and your medical provider or insurance company.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or consult with your medical provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **out_of_network_request** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to describe their out-of-network service request.
* **provider_details** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to provide information about their medical provider.
* **medical_record** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to provide a copy of their medical record or a description of their medical history.
* **request_details** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to provide more details about their request.
* **supporting_documentation** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to upload any supporting documentation related to their request.
* **patient_details** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to provide their contact information and any other relevant details.
* **insurance_details** (Text, Optional): This field is for the patient to provide information about their insurance coverage.
* The following fields (out_of_network_request_2 to out_of_network_request_19) are additional text fields for the patient to provide more information about their request. These fields are not explained in detail as they are likely duplicates of the above fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your medical provider or insurance company.
* This form is for information purposes only and should not be used for billing or payment purposes.
