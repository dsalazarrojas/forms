# Canopy Quotation Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your canopy quotation request needs, ensuring that we can provide a tailored solution for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each field, as described below.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask in the "Questions or Concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 4`, `First Name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`id: 5`, `Last Name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`id: 7`, `Email Address`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 8`, `Phone Number`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`id: 9`, `Street Address`, required): Enter the address of your project location.
* **City** (`id: 10`, `City`, required): Enter the city where your project is located.
* **State/Province** (`id: 11`, `State/Province`, required): Select the province or state where your project is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`id: 12`, `ZIP or Postal Code`, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your project location.
* **Type of Canopy** (`id: 14`, `Type of Canopy You Need`, required): Select the type of canopy you require, such as Fixed Roof Canopy, Retractable Canopy, etc.
* **Installation Location Type** (`id: 15`, `Installation Location Type`, required): Select the type of location where the canopy will be installed, such as Residential Backyard, Residential Deck, etc.
* **Estimated Coverage Area** (`id: 16`, `Estimated Coverage Area`, required): Approximate the size of the area to be covered by the canopy in square feet.
* **Location Dimensions** (`id: 17`, `Location Dimensions`, optional): If known, provide the length and width of the project location.
* **When do you need this installed?** (`id: 19`, `When do you need this installed?`, required): Select when you need the installation, such as Immediately, Within 1 month, etc.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`id: 20`, `Preferred Installation Date`, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for installation.
* **Special Features or Requirements** (`id: 22`, `Special Features or Requirements`, optional): Select any special features or requirements you have for the canopy, such as Motorized/Automated, Water Resistant, etc.
* **Climate Considerations** (`id: 24`, `Climate Considerations`, optional): Select the climate considerations for your project, such as Heavy rain area, High wind area, etc.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`id: 26`, `Estimated Budget Range`, optional): Select an approximate budget range for your project.
* **Do you need additional services?** (`id: 28`, `Do you need additional services?`, optional): Select any additional services you require, such as Installation, Maintenance plan, etc.
* **Project Description** (`id: 30`, `Project Description`, optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Any Questions or Concerns?** (`id: 31`, `Any Questions or Concerns?`, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please provide them here.
* **How would you prefer we contact you?** (`id: 32`, `How would you prefer we contact you?`, required): Select your preferred method of contact, such as Email, Phone, etc.

Note: The required fields are marked with `required: true`. It is essential to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure we can provide a suitable quotation.
