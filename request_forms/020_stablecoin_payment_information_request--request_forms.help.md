# Stablecoin Payment Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a payment made or to be made by a Stablecoin user.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your phone number and address if required.
3. Fill in the payment details, date of payment, and amount paid.
4. Select the payment method used.
5. Upload any supporting documents as required.
6. Add any comments or remarks if necessary.
7. Provide your signature and date/time of signing.
8. Finally, review your information carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stablecoin Payment Information Request**: This is the title of the form.
* **Name**: This field is where you should enter your full name. Please enter the name that you wish to be used in the payment records.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address here. This will be used as a point of contact.
* **Phone Number**: If required, enter your phone number here.
* **Address**: If required, provide your address here.
* **Payment details**: Provide a brief description of the payment, including the reason for the payment.
* **Date of Payment**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount of money paid.
* **Purpose of Payment**: Briefly describe the purpose of the payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method used for the payment from the available options.
* **Stablecoin Address**: Enter the Stablecoin address where the payment was made or is to be made to.
* **Signature**: Please sign this form to acknowledge that you have filled out the form accurately and to the best of your abilities.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or remarks related to the payment.
* **Uploaded Files**: Upload any supporting documents as required by the form.
* **Form Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you have reviewed and verified the information provided.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date on which you signed this form.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time at which you signed this form.
* **Amount Received**: If required, enter the amount received.
* **Purpose Signed By**: If required, enter the purpose of signing.
* **Signed By**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
