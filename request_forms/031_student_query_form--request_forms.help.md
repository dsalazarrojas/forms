# Student Query Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect queries from students about various aspects of their studies, such as requests for information, clarification on policies, or concerns about their program. Please use this form to submit a query and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name** in the "Student Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Choose the **Type of Query** by selecting one of the options provided in the "Type of Query" dropdown. If unsure, you can select "No" for queries that do not fit into the given options.
3. Provide a brief **Description of Query** in the "Description of Query" field, detailing your concern or question.
4. Enter your **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the "Contact Phone" field. This is a required field.
6. If you have a specific **Department of Contact**, please select it from the dropdown. If not, you can select "Yes" and we will get back to you with the relevant department's information.
7. Fill in the name of the **Department** you are inquiring about in the "Department" field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or official documents.
* **Type of Query** (Type of Query, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are looking for information on a specific topic or policy, "No" if you are unsure or need general guidance, or use the dropdown options as a starting point.
* **Description of Query** (Description of Query, text, optional): Write a brief description of your question or concern.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your official university email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **Department of Contact** (Department of Contact, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to be directed to the relevant department or select from the options provided.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter the name of the department you are inquiring about.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): This button will submit your query to us.
