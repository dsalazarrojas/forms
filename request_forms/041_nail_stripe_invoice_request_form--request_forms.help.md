# nail_stripe_invoice_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nail Stripe Invoice Request Form" is used to collect information for generating an invoice related to nail stripe services. This form helps to record details such as stripe color, width, length, style, material, and other relevant information required for the invoice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields such as stripe width, stripe length, stripe style, and stripe material.
3. Enter the price and quantity of the stripe in the respective fields.
4. If applicable, fill in the stripe color, stripe total price, stripe tax, and stripe note fields.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the requester in the respective fields.
6. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **stripe_width** (2, required): Enter the width of the stripe in inches or millimeters.
* **stripe_length** (text, optional): Enter the length of the stripe, e.g., 1 foot, 2 feet, etc.
* **stripe_style** (select_one, optional): Choose the style of the stripe from the dropdown options.
* **stripe_material** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of material used for the stripe from the dropdown options.
* **stripe_price** (2, optional): Enter the price of the stripe.
* **stripe_quantity** (2, optional): Enter the quantity of the stripe.
* **stripe_color_price** (2, optional): Enter the price per unit of the stripe color.
* **stripe_quantity_price** (2, optional): Enter the total price for the specified quantity of the stripe color.
* **stripe_total_price** (2, optional): Enter the total price of all stripes.
* **stripe_tax** (2, optional): Enter the tax rate for the stripe.
* **stripe_note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the stripe.
* **requesters_email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the person requesting the stripe.
* **requester_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the stripe.

Note: Please be careful with formatting when entering the phone number, ensure to include the area code (e.g., 123-456-7890).
