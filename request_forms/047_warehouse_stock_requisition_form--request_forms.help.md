# Warehouse Stock Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Stock Requisition Form is used to request stock from the warehouse department for a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Requisition Details section with a brief description of why you need the stock.
2. Enter the Stock Code of the item you are requesting.
3. Specify the quantity of stock you need.
4. Select the Unit of Measurement (UoM) for the item.
5. Provide a valid reason for your request in the Reason field.
6. Add any additional comments in the Comments field (optional).
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requisition Details**: A brief description of why you are requesting the stock.
* **Stock Code**: The stock code of the item you are requesting. If you're unsure, please contact the warehouse department for assistance.
* **Quantity**: The number of units you need. Please ensure this is accurate to avoid overship.
* **Unit of Measurement (UoM)**: The unit of measurement for the item (e.g., piece, kg, etc.). Choose the appropriate UoM from the dropdown list.
* **Reason**: A valid reason for requesting the stock. Please be specific and provide enough detail for the warehouse department to process your request.
* **Comments** (optional): Any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Date**: The date you are expecting to receive the stock. Please format the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Time**: The expected time for delivery (optional). Please format the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Email**: Your contact email address. Please ensure this is valid and up-to-date.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number. Please include the country code if necessary.
