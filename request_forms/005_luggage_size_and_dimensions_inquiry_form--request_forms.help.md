# Luggage Size And Dimensions Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a customer's luggage in order to facilitate a proper inquiry about its size and dimensions. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below.

* Click on the "Submit" button at the bottom of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Luggage Id** (1, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier of your luggage if you have one.
* **Luggage Type** (2, select_one, optional): Select the type of luggage you are inquiring about. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Hand Luggage
	+ Check-in Luggage
	+ Special Luggage
* **Length** (3, number, optional): Enter the length of your luggage in centimeters.
* **Width** (4, number, optional): Enter the width of your luggage in centimeters.
* **Height** (5, number, optional): Enter the height of your luggage in centimeters.
* **Weight** (6, number, optional): Enter the weight of your luggage in kilograms.
* **Volume** (7, number, optional): Enter the volume of your luggage in liters.
* **Comments** (8, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information about your luggage, please enter it here.
* **Email** (9, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (10, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Shipping Address** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select the shipping address from the provided options.
* **Shipping City** (12, select_one, optional): Select the city where the shipping address is located.
* **Shipping State** (13, select_one, optional): Select the state where the shipping address is located.
* **Shipping Zip** (14, select_one, optional): Select the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Submit** (15, text, optional): Please type a brief description of the inquiry in the space provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at the email address provided.
* Please note that the more accurate information you provide, the better we can serve you.
