# seo_content_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Content Request Form is a tool for marketing and content teams to collect information about the type of content they need to create. This form helps to streamline the content creation process by gathering essential details about the content request, including the type of content, target audience, and desired publication date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review our existing content library to ensure you're not duplicating a request.
2.  Choose the type of content you need (e.g., blog post, brand article).
3.  Select the target audience for your content (e.g., new customers, existing customers).
4.  Choose the posting platforms where you'd like your content published (e.g., website, social media, blog).
5.  Enter your desired publish date for the content.
6.  Select the length of the content.
7.  Choose the type of submission you're making (e.g., brand story, product info, blog post).
8.  Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, company name, and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Fields**: (Form Fields, text, optional) This field is where you'll describe the specific content you're requesting.
* **Request For**: (Request For, text, optional) Briefly describe the purpose or objective of the content you're requesting.
* **Type Of Content**: (Type Of Content, select multiple, optional) Choose the type of content you need, such as blog posts, brand articles, or product information.
* **Topic**: (Topic, text, optional) Enter a brief description of the topic or subject of the content.
* **Target Audience**: (Target Audience, select multiple, optional) Select the target audience for your content, such as new customers or existing customers.
* **Posting Platforms**: (Posting Platforms, select multiple, optional) Choose the platforms where you'd like your content published, such as website, social media, or blog.
* **Desired Publish Date**: (Desired Publish Date, date, optional) Enter the desired date for the content to be published.
* **Content Length**: (Content Length, number, optional) Enter the expected length of the content.
* **Submission Type**: (Submission Type, select one, optional) Choose the type of submission you're making, such as brand story, product info, or blog post.
* **Submitter**: (Submitter, email, optional) Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Submitter Phone**: (Submitter Phone, text, optional) Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Marketing Agency**: (Marketing Agency, select one, optional) Choose the marketing agency handling your content request.
* **Submitter Company**: (Submitter Company, text, optional) Enter your company name.
* **Submitter Notes**: (Submitter Notes, text, optional) Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your request.
* **Submitter Title**: (Submitter Title, text, optional) Enter your title or position within your company.
* **Submitter Department**: (Submitter Department, text, optional) Enter the department within your company you're representing.
* **Submitter Job Title**: (Submitter Job Title, text, optional) Enter your job title within your company.
* **Submitter Phone**: (Submitter Phone, text, optional) Enter your company phone number.
* **Submitter Address**: (Submitter Address, text, optional) Enter your company's address.
* **Submitter City**: (Submitter City, text, optional) Enter your company's city.
* **Submitter State**: (Submitter State, text, optional) Enter your company's state or province.
* **Submitter Postal**: (Submitter Postal, text, optional) Enter your company's postal code.
* **Submitter Country**: (Submitter Country, text, optional) Enter your company's country.
* **Submitter Notes**: (Submitter Notes, text, optional) Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your request.
