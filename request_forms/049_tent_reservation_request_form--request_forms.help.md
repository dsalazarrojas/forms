# Tent Reservation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users requesting a tent reservation. The form helps us to understand your requirements and process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Requestor's Name**.
2. Provide the **Request Date** when you would like your reservation to start.
3. Specify the **Reservation Date** when you would like your reservation to end.
4. Enter the **Number of Guests** you will be bringing with you.
5. Enter the **Camp Name** you would like to reserve a tent at.
6. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** so we can reach out to you with updates.
7. If applicable, add any additional **Message** about your reservation.
8. Agree to the **Accept Terms** and **Accept Privacy** policies to continue with your request.
9. Choose the **Payment Method** you would like to use to pay for your reservation (Online or Offline).
10. Select the **Payment Status** of your reservation (Pending, Paid, or Canceled).
11. Choose the **Request Status** of your reservation (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
12. Finally, fill in the **Payment Date** if you have made a payment for your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor's Name**: A field for you to enter your name.
* **Request Date**: A field for you to specify when you would like your reservation to start.
* **Request Time**: A field for you to specify what time you would like your reservation to start (please format as 24-hour clock, e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Number of Guests**: A field for you to enter the number of people you will be bringing with you.
* **Reservation Date**: A field for you to specify when you would like your reservation to end.
* **Reservation Time**: A field for you to specify what time you would like your reservation to end (please format as 24-hour clock, e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Camp Name**: A field for you to select the camp you would like to reserve a tent at.
* **Contact Email**: A field for you to enter your contact email so we can reach out to you with updates.
* **Contact Phone**: A field for you to enter your contact phone number.
* **Message**: A field for you to add any additional comments about your reservation.
* **Accept Terms**: A field for you to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Accept Privacy**: A field for you to agree to our privacy policy.
* **Payment Method**: A field for you to select how you will be paying for your reservation (Online or Offline).
* **Payment Status**: A field for you to specify the status of your payment (Pending, Paid, or Canceled).
* **Request Status**: A field for you to specify the status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Payment Date**: A field for you to enter the date of your payment if you have already made a payment for your reservation.
