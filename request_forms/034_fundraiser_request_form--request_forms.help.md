# Fundraiser Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or organizations seeking to make a donation to our organization. The form collects information necessary for us to process and acknowledge the donation. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth donation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field on the form to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to include all required information for us to process your request.
3. Double-check your answers before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, not required): A brief description of the purpose of your request.
* **Donation Info** (`donation_info`, text, not required): Describe the type of donation you are requesting (e.g., one-time, recurring, or a specific project).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and address for our records.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Your phone number for follow-up or additional information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, not required): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Organization Description** (`organization_description`, text, not required): Briefly describe the organization and its purpose.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, not required): Specify the amount of the donation you are requesting (if not required, please leave blank).
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, text, not required): If applicable, specify the donation frequency (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are authorized to submit a request on behalf of your organization.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us via email or phone.
* We appreciate your interest in supporting our cause and look forward to reviewing your request!
