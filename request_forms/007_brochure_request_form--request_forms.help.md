# Brochure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for creating a brochure tailored to your specific needs. It will guide you through a series of questions to ensure that your brochure requirements are met accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately
- Provide all required information to ensure your brochure is created correctly
- Review and submit the form once completed

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Requester Information --**: This section gathers your contact information to facilitate communication and brochure delivery.
  * **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
  * **Organization/Company**: If applicable, enter your organization's name.
- **Brochure Subject**: Select the topic or product your brochure is about (e.g., Product Information, Service Overview).
- **Purpose of Brochure**: Choose how the brochure will be used (e.g., Marketing Campaign, Trade Show, Educational).
- **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of brochures you require.
- **Target Audience**: Select who the brochure is intended for (e.g., Consumers, Businesses, Healthcare Professionals).
  * This field is optional; you can select all that apply.
- **-- Design Preferences --**: This section allows you to choose your brochure design preferences.
  * **Preferred Style**: Select the style of the brochure (e.g., Professional, Creative, Modern).
  * **Color Scheme Preference**: Choose your preferred color scheme for the brochure.
  * **Content Requirements**: Select what content to include in the brochure (e.g., Product Features, Pricing Information).
  * **Content Requirements (continued)**: Select what content to include in the brochure (e.g., Product Features, Pricing Information).
  * **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requirements or instructions for the brochure.
- **-- Delivery Information --**: This section deals with brochure delivery preferences.
  * **Preferred Delivery Method**: Select how you want to receive the brochure (e.g., Digital PDF, Printed Copies, Both).
  * **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for printed brochure delivery.
  * **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date you need the brochure by.
  * **Budget Range**: Enter your budget for the brochure, if applicable.
  * **Additional Requests**: Enter any other requests or information you have.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled out to ensure accurate brochure creation.
