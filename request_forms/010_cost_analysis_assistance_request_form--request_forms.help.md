# Cost Analysis Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for cost analysis and decision-making purposes. It captures details about a project, including expected and actual completion dates, contact person, and cost-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details in the "Project Details" field.
2. If applicable, upload any relevant project files in the "Project Files" field.
3. Provide a description of your question or concern in the "What is your question for this project?" field.
4. Enter the expected and actual completion dates of the project.
5. Select the contact person responsible for the project from the "Contact Person" field.
6. Provide your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
7. If relevant, provide a description of your question in the "Description" field.
8. If necessary, attach a file to your question or concern in the "Question Attachment" field.
9. Indicate your answer to the question of whether the actual cost of the project is higher or lower than budgeted in the "Actual Cost" field.
10. Indicate whether the cost variance is positive or negative.
11. Answer the question of whether the actual cost of the project is higher or lower than expected in the "Actual Cost Answer" field.
12. Select an option from the "Contact Person Answer" field indicating who is responsible for the project.
13. If necessary, provide an email address or phone number for further communication in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
14. Indicate your answer to the question of whether the budgeted cost of the project is higher or lower than expected in the "Budgeted Cost Answer" field.
15. Indicate the cost variance in the "Cost Variance Answer" field.
16. If necessary, attach a file to your question or concern in the "Question Attachment Answer" field.
17. Select an option from the "Question Answer" field indicating your response to the question.
18. Finally, select an option from the "Contact Person Answer" field indicating the contact person's response to the question.
19. Select an option from the "Budgeted Cost Answer" field indicating your response to the question.
20. Indicate the cost variance answer in the "Cost Variance Answer" field.
21. Select an option from the "Actual Cost Answer" field indicating your response to the question.
