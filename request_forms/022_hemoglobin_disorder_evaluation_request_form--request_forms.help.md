# Hemoglobin Disorder Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hemoglobin Disorder Evaluation Request Form is used to gather information from patients or their representatives to request an evaluation of a hemoglobin disorder. This form helps the medical team to understand the nature of the disorder and make informed decisions about further action. Please note that this form is not for patients with a confirmed hemoglobin disorder diagnosis.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Provide a brief description of your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3. Specify the type of hemoglobin disorder you are referring to in the "Hemoglobin Disorder" section.
4. Select the type of evaluation you are requesting (Lab, Imaging, or Other) in the "Evaluation Request" section.
5. Add any additional information that may be relevant to your condition in the "Additional Information" section.
6. If you have lab test results, please enter them in the "Lab Test Results" section.
7. Indicate whether the patient's hemoglobin disorder has been evaluated by the medical team (Yes/No) in the "Medical Team" section.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (1): Enter your name, contact number, and address to help us reach you regarding your request.
* **Medical History** (2): Provide a concise summary of your medical history, including any previous conditions, treatments, or surgeries related to hemoglobin disorders.
* **Hemoglobin Disorder** (3): Describe the hemoglobin disorder you are experiencing or have been diagnosed with.
* **Evaluation Request** (4): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Lab: Blood test to diagnose or monitor the disorder
	+ Imaging: Radiological tests like X-rays, MRI, or CT scans to visualize the disorder
	+ Other: Specify the type of evaluation you are requesting (e.g., consultation with specialist)
* **Additional Information** (5): Provide any relevant context or additional details that may aid in the evaluation process.
* **Lab Test Results** (6): If you have received lab test results, please enter them here.
* **Hemoglobin Disorder Evaluations** (7): Indicate whether the disorder has been evaluated by the medical team (Yes/No) or if it has not been evaluated (Not Evaluated).
* **Medical Team** (8): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes: The medical team has evaluated or is evaluating the patient’s hemoglobin disorder.
	+ No: The medical team has not evaluated or is not evaluating the patient’s hemoglobin disorder.
* **Submit** (9): Click this button to send the form and submit your request for evaluation.
