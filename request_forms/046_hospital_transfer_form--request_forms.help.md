# Hospital Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Transfer Form is a document used to collect information necessary for the transfer of a patient between facilities or healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Ensure that the form is properly signed by the physician or authorized healthcare provider.
3. Attach any additional information or supporting documents as needed, following the guidelines above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hospital_transfer_form_page**: Enter a brief description of the patient's transfer purpose.
* **patient_details**: Provide the patient's identifying information, including their name and date of birth.
* **patient_condition**: Document the patient's medical condition or reason for transfer.
* **transfer_reason**: Briefly explain the reason for the patient's transfer.
* **transfer_date**: Enter the date of the patient's transfer.
* **hospital_transfer_form_note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions related to the patient's transfer (optional).
* **assigned_tool**: If applicable, specify the medical device or equipment assigned to the patient.
* **transfer_facility**: Indicate whether the patient will be transferred to a 'Yes' or 'No' facility.
* **physician_signature**: Sign and date the form, indicating that it is complete and accurate.
* **physician_name**: Enter your name as the responsible physician.
* **physician_title**: Enter your title or professional designation (optional).
* **date**: Enter the date of the form completion (optional).
* **time**: Enter the time of the form completion (optional).
* **email**: Enter your work email address (optional).
* **phone**: Enter your work phone number (optional).
* **address**: Enter your work address (optional).
* **patient_signature**: If the patient can sign, include their signature (optional).
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name, if different from the one above (optional).
* **patient_relationship**: Specify the patient's relationship to the person signing (optional).
* **facility_transfer_reason**: Enter the reason for the transfer, if different from the one above (optional).
* **facility_transfer_type**: Enter the type of facility or department the patient will be transferred to (optional).
* **facility_transfer_facility**: Enter the name of the facility or department the patient will be transferred to (optional).
* **facility_transfer_date**: Enter the date of the transfer to the new facility or department (optional).
* **facility_transfer_time**: Enter the time of the transfer to the new facility or department (optional).
* **facility_transfer_note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions related to the transfer (optional).

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and consistently, and that additional information is attached or noted as needed.
