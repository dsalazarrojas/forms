# Low Occupancy Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide employees through the process of requesting a leave of absence from work. This form will be used by employees to submit their leave request, and the relevant managers and team leads will be notified for approval and review.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each with a specific field to fill out. Please complete each field carefully and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official company ID or employee record.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Select the date when you will be taking your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Select the date when you will be returning to work.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your leave.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select the department(s) involved in your leave.
* **Team Lead Approval** (`team_lead_approval`, select_one, required): Select the status of team lead approval.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select the status of manager approval.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If the manager has any comments, enter them here.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the manager approved your request.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your leave in days.
* **Leave Frequency** (`leave_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your leave.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, date, optional): If required, sign and date the form with your signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, date, optional): If required, sign and date the form with your manager's signature.
