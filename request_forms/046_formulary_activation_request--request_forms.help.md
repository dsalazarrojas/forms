Formulary Activation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request activation of a formulary. Please fill out the form completely, providing all necessary information, to ensure a smooth review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all necessary information.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form's purpose and process.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Formulary Activation Request Form** (Formulary Activation Request Form, Text, Optional): This is the main form where you can provide any additional information relevant to your request.
* **Form Name** (Form Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the form you are requesting to be activated.
* **Form Version** (Form Version, Text, Optional): Enter the version of the form you are requesting to be activated.
* **Form Type** (Form Type, Select One, Optional):
	+ Open: Select this option if the form is currently open and ready for review.
	+ Proposed: Select this option if the form is a proposed form that needs to be reviewed and approved.
	+ Closed: Select this option if the form is currently closed and not active.
* **Category** (Category, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more categories that apply to your request.
* **Status** (Status, Select One, Optional):
	+ Active: Select this option if the form is currently active and needs to be updated.
	+ Inactive: Select this option if the form is currently inactive and needs to be activated.
* **Review By** (Review By, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or team that will review your request.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date when you expect the review to take place.
* **Submitter** (Submitter, Text, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submit Date** (Submit Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Approver** (Approver, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person approving the request.
* **Approve Date** (Approve Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the request was approved.
* **Reject Reason** (Reject Reason, Text, Optional): Enter the reason why the request was rejected.
* **Notes** (Notes, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your request.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Fax** (Fax, Text, Optional): Enter your fax number or the fax number of the person submitting the request.
* **Form Owner** (Form Owner, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the form's owner or the person responsible for the form.
* **Form Owner Email** (Form Owner Email, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the form's owner or the person responsible for the form.
* **Form Owner Phone** (Form Owner Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the form's owner or the person responsible for the form.
* **Form Owner Fax** (Form Owner Fax, Text, Optional): Enter the fax number of the form's owner or the person responsible for the form.
* **Reviewer** (Reviewer, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the reviewer or the person responsible for reviewing the request.
* **Reviewer Email** (Reviewer Email, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer or the person responsible for reviewing the request.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Reviewer Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer or the person responsible for reviewing the request.
* **Reviewer Fax** (Reviewer Fax, Text, Optional): Enter the fax number of the reviewer or the person responsible for reviewing the request.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid delays in the review and approval process.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the form's owner or the person responsible for the form.
- Please submit the form only once and do not repeat your request.
