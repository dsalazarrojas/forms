# Electric Power Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Electric Power Transfer Request Form is used to collect information for electric power transfer requests. This form is essential for our team to process your transfer request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2.  Make sure to select the correct service type and service provider for your transfer request.
3.  Enter your transfer date and transfer time (if applicable).
4.  Provide a brief transfer reason to help our team understand your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electric Service Transfer Request**: Enter a brief description of your electric service transfer request.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number for our system to verify your identity.
* **Service Type**:
	+ Select either Residential or Commercial to specify the type of service you have.
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date you would like to transfer your service.
* **Transfer Time**: If applicable, enter the time you would like to transfer your service.
* **Notes**: This field is optional, but if you have any additional information or requests, feel free to include them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Service Provider**: Select the service provider that you are transferring to or from (e.g. ABC Electric, DEF Electric).
* **Service Address**: Enter the address of the service you are transferring.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address for our records.
* **Transfer Amount**: Enter the amount of energy being transferred.
* **Transfer Reason**: Enter a brief reason for your transfer request.
* **Customer Approval**: Select Yes or No to confirm that you have approved the transfer request.
