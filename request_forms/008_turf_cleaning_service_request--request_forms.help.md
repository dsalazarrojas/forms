# Turf Cleaning Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request turf cleaning services. You can use this form to submit a request for lawn mowing, trimming, edging, or yard cleanup services for your front, back, or other areas of your property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, contact phone number, and preferred date and time for the service.
2. Select the service type you need (e.g., lawn mowing, trimming, edging, or yard cleanup).
3. Choose the areas of your property that need service (e.g., front yard, back yard, or other).
4. Enter your customer details, including name, email, and phone number.
5. Submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): This field is not currently used, but you can enter any additional information you think is relevant to your request.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you to confirm your request.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the service when it is most convenient for you. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 05/22/2023).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the service when it is most convenient for you. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, optional): Select the service type you need from the following options:
	+ Lawn Mowing
	+ Lawn Trimming
	+ Lawn Edging
	+ Yard Cleanup
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, select multiple, optional): Select the areas of your property that need service:
	+ Front Yard
	+ Back Yard
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your request.
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the request.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with confirmation of your request.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you to confirm your request.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address so we can accurately map and visit your property.
