# Additional Score Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request additional score reports for a student's standardized exam scores. The form gathers information from the student, including their contact details, exam information, and the reason for requesting the report. The form also includes fields for the recipient's details and the preferred delivery method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Student Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identity documents.
	* **Student ID / Candidate Number**: Enter your student ID or candidate number as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for updates.
	* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates.
2. Select your exam information:
	* **Type of Standardized Exam**: Choose the type of exam you took (e.g. SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, etc.).
	* **Date of Exam**: Enter the date you took the exam.
	* **Scores to be Reported**: Specify which scores you want to be reported (e.g. overall score, section scores, etc.).
3. Choose your recipient information:
	* **Institution / Organization Name**: Enter the name of the institution or organization to receive the score report.
	* **Recipient Mailing Address / Email**: Enter the recipient's mailing address or email address where the score report should be sent.
4. Select your delivery method:
	* **Delivery Method**: Choose the method for delivering the score report (e.g. electronic delivery, physical mail, etc.).
5. Acknowledge and agree to the fee:
	* **I acknowledge the fee for additional score reports**: Confirm that you understand and agree to pay the fee for the additional score report.
6. Specify your reason for the request:
	* **Reason for Additional Report**: Select the reason for requesting the report (e.g. college application, employment verification, etc.).
7. Authorize the release of scores:
	* **I authorize the release of my test scores to the recipient above**: Confirm that you authorize the release of your scores to the chosen recipient.
8. Submit your request:
	* Enter the date you are submitting this request.
