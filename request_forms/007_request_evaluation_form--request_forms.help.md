# Request Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Evaluation Form is used to collect and track requests from clients, vendors, or internal stakeholders. It helps us to evaluate their needs and take appropriate actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
2. If you are a client, please provide your company name, location, and contact information, including phone number and email.
3. If you are a vendor, please describe your request and your contact method (e.g. email or phone).
4. Be as specific as possible when describing your requirements and any deadlines or time-sensitive requests.
5. Use the date and time fields to indicate when you would like the request to be processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Fill in your company name, location, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Requirements Info** (`requirements_info`, text, optional): Describe the services or products you are requesting.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you would like to be contacted (e.g. email or phone).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Requirements** (`requirements`, text, optional): Describe in detail what you are requesting.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Indicate the date you would like the request to be processed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Indicate the time you would like the request to be processed.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your company.
* **Contact Method Type** (`contact_method_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate the type of contact method (e.g. email or phone).
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Indicate the date the request was made.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in as much information as possible to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
