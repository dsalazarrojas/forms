# Requisition Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Requisition Form Template is a generic form designed to create and manage requests for various items or services. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to submit official business requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the item or service requested.
2. If applicable, provide the cost of the item or service.
3. Select the services or options requested.
4. Enter the date the item or service is required.
5. Enter the contact person's name.
6. Enter the contact person's email address.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number.
8. Enter the name of the person submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requisition Form Template** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief title for your request.
- **Item Description** (`item_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the item or service being requested.
- **Item Cost** (`item_cost`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the cost of the item or service.
- **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services or options requested. Options include "Option 1" and "Other".
- **Requisition Date** (`requisition_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the item or service is required.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Requisitioner Name** (`requisitioner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.

## Tips
- Ensure that each field is complete and accurate before submitting.
- Only submit a single requisition form per item or service request.
- If you need to submit multiple requests, complete each form individually and submit separately.
