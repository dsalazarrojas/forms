# Data Integration Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from team members who want to access technical environments for data integration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to provide accurate information.
2. Select the required fields (if any) and fill out the optional fields with relevant details.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName**: Your first name. Please enter your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **LastName**: Your last name. Please enter your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email**: Your company email address. This is the email address associated with your work account.
* **Address**: Your address or office location.
* **City**: The name of the city where your office is located.
* **Country**: The name of your country of residence.
* **Technical Environments**: Select the technical environments you need access to for data integration purposes. Please choose one or more options as needed.
* **Pilot Goals**: Briefly describe the objectives you aim to achieve through data integration. This is a text area for you to provide details about your goals.
* **Technical Skills**: If you have any technical skills that are relevant to data integration, list them here.
* **Contact Person**: The person who will be your contact for any questions or concerns related to data integration.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Start Date**: The date you would like to start the data integration process.
* **End Date**: The date you would like the data integration process to end.
* **Duration**: The length of time you expect the data integration process to take.
* **Frequency**: The frequency of data integration sessions. Choose one of the options provided.
* **Name**: Your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Position**: Your job title or position.
* **Department**: The department you work in.
* **Organization**: The name of your company or organization.
* **Email**: Your company email address. Please note that this is different from the "Contact Email" field.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the submitter.
* **Role**: Your role in the organization.
* **Contact**: Briefly describe who will be your contact for data integration.
* **Notes**: Any additional information you would like to provide about your data integration needs.
* **Submitter**: Your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Organization**: The name of the company or organization submitting the form.
* **Submitter Role**: The role of the person submitting the form.
