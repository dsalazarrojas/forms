# Publication Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publication Request Form is designed to collect information about publications and authors interested in publishing their work. This form is used to submit requests for publications in various types of publications, including books, journals, magazines, and newspapers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **E-mail**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
2. Confirm your collaboration preferences:
	* **Are you interested in working together?**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to collaborate with the publisher on your publication.
3. Choose your publication type:
	* **Type of publication**: Select the type of publication you are interested in (Book, Journal, Magazine, Newspaper, or Other).
4. Provide publication details:
	* **Publication details**: Enter a brief description of your publication.
5. Enter author information:
	* **Author name**: Enter your name as it will appear on the publication.
	* **Author Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted as an author.
	* **Institution**: Enter the institution you are associated with.
6. Enter submission details:
	* **Submitter name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the publication.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
	* **Submitter Institution**: Enter the institution you are associated with (if applicable).
	* **Work title**: Enter the title of your publication.
	* **Submission date**: Enter the date you would like to submit your publication.
	* **Year**: Enter the year of your publication.
	* **Number of Pages**: Enter the number of pages in your publication.
7. Add any additional notes:
	* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your publication.
