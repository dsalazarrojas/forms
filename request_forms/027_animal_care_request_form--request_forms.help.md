# Animal Care Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Care Request Form is used by pet owners to request animal care services such as appointments, examinations, and vaccinations for their pets. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the necessary information is collected for the pet's care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information:
	* **Pet Owner Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment confirmation.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* **Home Address**: Enter your home address for contact purposes.
2. Provide information about your pet:
	* **Pet's Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
	* **Species**: Select the type of animal you have (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
	* **Breed**: Enter your pet's breed or mix.
	* **Age**: Enter your pet's age in years.
	* **Weight**: Enter your pet's weight in pounds.
	* **Sex**: Select your pet's sex (male or female).
3. Select the type of service you need:
	* **Type of Service Requested**: Choose the type of service you need (e.g., wellness exam, sick visit, etc.).
4. Schedule an appointment:
	* **Request Date**: Enter the date you prefer to schedule the appointment.
	* **Preferred Time**: Select the time of day you prefer (morning, afternoon, or evening).
	* **Alternative Dates**: If you have other available dates, enter them here.
5. Provide additional information:
	* **Reason for Visit**: Describe the reason for your pet's visit.
	* **Current Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms your pet is experiencing.
	* **Duration of Symptoms**: Select how long your pet has been experiencing symptoms.
	* **Medications**: If your pet is currently taking medications, list them here.
	* **Allergies**: If your pet has any known allergies, list them here.
	* **Previous Visits**: If your pet has been to the clinic before, select yes.
6. Confirm:
	* **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm that you consent to any necessary treatment for your pet.
	* **Owner Signature**: Sign your full name as the pet owner.
	* **Date**: Enter the current date.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible care for your pet.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the clinic staff.
