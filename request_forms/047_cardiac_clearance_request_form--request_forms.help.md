# Cardiac Clearance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiac Clearance Request Form is designed to collect information about a patient's cardiac history and medical conditions to determine whether they are cleared for cardiac clearance. This form helps to ensure that patients are thoroughly evaluated before undergoing medical procedures or activities that may be risky for their heart health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. For "Reason for cardiac clearance," select the reason that best describes your situation from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the planned procedure or activity date in the "Planned procedure or activity date" field.
7. For "Previous cardiac events or conditions," select all relevant medical conditions you have experienced.
8. In the "Current cardiac medications" field, list any medications you are currently taking.
9. If you have a family history of cardiac disease, please select the correct option.
10. In the "Chest pain or discomfort?" field, select your response.
11. In the "Shortness of breath?" field, select your response.
12. In the "Heart palpitations?" field, select your response.
13. In the "Dizziness or fainting spells?" field, select your response (if applicable).
14. In the "Swelling in legs or ankles?" field, select your response (if applicable).
15. In the "Smoking status" field, select your response.
16. For "Current exercise frequency," select the frequency with which you exercise.
17. In the "Other medical conditions" field, list any other relevant medical conditions you have.
18. In the "Authorization and signature" field, select "Yes - I Authorize" to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name (patient_full_name)**: Enter your full name.
* **Date of birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Email address (patient_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number (patient_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Reason for cardiac clearance (reason_for_clearance)**: Select the reason for your cardiac clearance request from the dropdown menu.
* **Planned procedure or activity date (procedure_date)**: Enter the date of the planned procedure or activity.
* **Previous cardiac events or conditions (previous_cardiac_events)**: Select all relevant medical conditions you have experienced.
* **Current cardiac medications (current_medications)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Family history of cardiac disease (family_cardiac_history)**: Select whether you have a family history of cardiac disease.
* **Chest pain or discomfort (chest_pain)**: Select your response regarding chest pain or discomfort.
* **Shortness of breath (shortness_of_breath)**: Select your response regarding shortness of breath.
* **Heart palpitations (heart_palpitations)**: Select your response regarding heart palpitations.
* **Dizziness or fainting spells (dizziness_syncope)**: Select your response regarding dizziness or fainting spells (if applicable).
* **Swelling in legs or ankles (edema)**: Select your response regarding swelling in legs or ankles (if applicable).
* **Smoking status (smoking_status)**: Select your smoking status.
* **Current exercise frequency (exercise_frequency)**: Select the frequency with which you exercise.
* **Other medical conditions (additional_medical_conditions)**: List any other relevant medical conditions you have.
* **Authorization and signature (authorization_and_signature)**: Select "Yes - I Authorize" to confirm your request.
