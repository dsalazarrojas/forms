# Confidential Employee Reference Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect confidential feedback from a colleague or manager who has worked with an employee to help evaluate their performance and behavior. The feedback collected will help in making informed decisions about future opportunities for the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that the candidate is aware that you are providing a confidential reference.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing specific examples or incidents that demonstrate the candidate's skills, reliability, and communication abilities.
3. Select the correct options for each rating scale to reflect your honest assessment of the candidate's performance.
4. Fill out any additional comments or reasons for leaving, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_name`, required): Enter the full name of the candidate being referred.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, required): Enter the position the candidate is being referred for.
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, required): Enter the name of the person providing the reference (e.g., supervisor, colleague, or manager).
* **Company/Organization** (`reference_company`, required): Enter the name of the company or organization the reference is from.
* **Relationship to Candidate** (`relationship_to_candidate`, required): Select the relationship you have with the candidate (e.g., Immediate Supervisor, Department Manager, Colleague/Peer, Client/Vendor, or HR Representative).
* **Technical Proficiency and Skills** (`technical_skills_rating`, required): Rate the candidate's technical skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Reliability and Dependability** (`reliability_rating`, required): Rate the candidate's reliability and dependability from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills** (`communication_skills`, required): Rate the candidate's communication and interpersonal skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Would You Rehire This Individual?** (`rehire_eligibility`, required): Select whether you would rehire the candidate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or reasons for leaving, if applicable.
* **I Certify This Reference Is Accurate and Confidential** (`signature_confirmation`, required): Select to confirm that the reference is accurate and confidential.

Note: The form is divided into sections for easy navigation and reference. Please read each section carefully and fill out the form accurately and honestly.
