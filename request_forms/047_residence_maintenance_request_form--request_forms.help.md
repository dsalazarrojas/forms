# Residence Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residence Maintenance Request Form is used to document and track maintenance activities, such as repairs, replacements, and other upkeep tasks, in a residence setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the type of maintenance required (e.g., "Fix a leaky faucet" or "Replace a broken light bulb").
2. Select the priority level of the request (e.g., High, Medium, or Low).
3. Enter the date and time of the occurrence (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief description of the issue or problem.
5. Select the type of maintenance requested (e.g., "Maintenance Request" or "Other").
6. Enter the contact information of the requester (if applicable).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
8. Select whether attachments are required or not.
9. Submit the form to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **maintenance_request** (Text, required): Enter the type of maintenance required (e.g., "Fix a leaky faucet" or "Replace a broken light bulb").
* **date_of_occurrence** (Date, required): Enter the date and time of the occurrence (if applicable).
* **description_of_issue** (Text, required): Provide a brief description of the issue or problem.
* **maintenance_type** (Select One, required): Select the type of maintenance requested (e.g., "Maintenance Request" or "Other").
* **priority** (Select One, required): Select the priority level of the request (e.g., High, Medium, or Low).
* **assigned_tool** (Text, not required): Enter the contact information of the person assigned to the request (if applicable).
* **maintenance_status** (Select One, required): Select the status of the request (e.g., "In Progress," "Completed," or "Pending").
* **maintenance_requestor** (Text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person or department making the request (if applicable).
* **department** (Text, not required): Enter the department of the person or organization making the request (if applicable).
* **contact_number** (Text, not required): Enter the contact phone number of the person or organization making the request (if applicable).
* **email** (Email, not required): Enter the email address of the person or organization making the request (if applicable).
* **notes** (Text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **additional_information** (Text, not required): Provide any additional information relevant to the request.
* **requestor** (Text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person or organization making the request (if applicable).
* **location** (Text, not required): Enter the location where the maintenance request is to be performed (if applicable).
* **maintenance_requester** (Text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person or organization making the request (if applicable).
* **date_requested** (Date, not required): Enter the date the request was made (if applicable).
* **status** (Select One, not required): Select the status of the request (e.g., "Open," "In Progress," or "Completed").
* **assigned_to** (Text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person assigned to the request (if applicable).
* **due_date** (Date, not required): Enter the due date for the request (if applicable).
* **completed_date** (Date, not required): Enter the completion date of the request (if applicable).
* **last_updated** (Date, not required): Enter the last updated date and time of the request (if applicable).
* **comments** (Text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **attachments** (Select Multiple, not required): Select whether attachments are required or not.
* **custom_fields_1** (Text, not required): This field is not used in this form, but is left for custom use.

Note: The fields that are marked as "not required" can be filled in or left blank, depending on the user's preference.
