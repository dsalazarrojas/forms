# Disaster Resource Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Resource Access Request Form is used to collect information from users who need access to disaster resources. This form helps us establish a point of contact in case of a disaster and ensures that we can quickly and efficiently provide the necessary resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Emergency Contact Information**:
    *   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the individual or group requesting resources.
    *   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or group requesting resources.
    *   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or group requesting resources.
2.  Select your **Relationship**:
    *   If applicable, choose the relationship between you and the individual or group requesting resources.
3.  Enter the **Location**:
    *   Provide the location where the resources are needed.
4.  Describe the **Resource Requested**:
    *   Provide a brief description of the resources needed.
5.  Specify the **Quantity**:
    *   Enter the quantity of resources needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Emergency Contact Information (1)**
    *   **Emergency Contact Name (contact_name)**: This field is for entering the name of the individual or group requesting resources.
    *   **Emergency Contact Email (contact_email)**: This field is for entering the email address of the individual or group requesting resources.
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: This field is for entering the phone number of the individual or group requesting resources.
*   **Relationship (5)**: This field is for selecting the relationship between you and the individual or group requesting resources. If not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Location (6)**: This field is for providing the location where the resources are needed.
*   **Resource Requested (7)**: This field is for describing the resources needed.
*   **Quantity (8)**: This field is for entering the quantity of resources needed.
