# HOA Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Disclosure Form is a document that allows Homeowners Association (HOA) representatives to provide accurate and necessary information about their association. This form is essential for HOAs to keep track of and communicate with their members, ensuring that users can access relevant details such as contact information, fees, services, and rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your HOA's name in the "HOA Name" field.
2. Enter your HOA's address in the "HOA Address" field.
3. Provide the city where your HOA is located in the "City" field.
4. Enter your HOA's state in the "State" field.
5. Fill in your HOA's zip code in the "Zip" field.
6. Enter your HOA's phone number in the "HOA Phone" field.
7. Provide your HOA's email address in the "HOA Email" field.
8. Enter your HOA's fees in the "HOA Fees" field.
9. Describe the services your HOA offers in the "HOA Services" field.
10. Mention any maintenance responsibilities in the "HOA Maintenance" field.
11. List any rules that apply to your HOA in the "HOA Rules" field.
12. Upload or link to any relevant documents related to your HOA in the "HOA Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **HOA Name (Text, Required)**: This is the name of your Homeowners Association. Please fill in your HOA's name as it appears on any official documents or communication.
- **HOA Address (Text, Required)**: Enter the physical address where your HOA office is located. This can include a street address, suite number, or P.O. box.
- **City (Text, Required)**: Enter the city where your HOA is located.
- **State (Text, Required)**: Enter the state where your HOA is located.
- **Zip (Text, Required)**: Enter your HOA's zip code.
- **HOA Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your HOA's phone number where you can be reached.
- **HOA Email (Email, Required)**: Provide your HOA's official email address where you can be reached.
- **HOA Fees (Text, Required)**: Describe the fees associated with your HOA, including any monthly or annual fees.
- **HOA Services (Text, Required)**: Describe the services your HOA offers, such as maintenance, landscaping, or security.
- **HOA Maintenance (Text, Required)**: Mention any maintenance responsibilities that fall under your HOA, such as trash collection or snow removal.
- **HOA Rules (Text, Required)**: List any rules that apply to your HOA, including any restrictions or requirements for homeowners.
- **HOA Documents (Text, Required)**: Upload or link to any relevant documents related to your HOA, such as bylaws, meeting minutes, or financial reports.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Double-check that you have filled in all required fields.
- If you have any attachments or links to documents, ensure they are easily accessible and up-to-date.
