# Home Theater Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in requesting a quote for a home theater system installation. The form will help you gather all the necessary details about your project, including contact information and quote requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): This is the main section where you can provide details about your home theater project. Please describe your project in a concise and clear manner, including any specific requirements or expectations you have.
2. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This section is for you to provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your quote.
3. **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide any additional information about what you are looking for in your quote. Be as specific as possible about your requirements and preferences.
4. **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, required/optional): If you would like to submit any supporting documents or files along with your quote request, please upload them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: A description of your home theater project, including any specific requirements or expectations you have.
* **Contact Info**: Your name, email address, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Quote Details**: Additional information about what you are looking for in your quote.
* **Submission Information**: Any supporting documents or files you would like to submit along with your quote request.
