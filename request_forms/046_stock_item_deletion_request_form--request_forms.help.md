# Stock Item Deletion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Item Deletion Request Form is used to request the deletion of a stock item from the inventory. This process is used to remove outdated or unnecessary items to keep the inventory organized and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your main information in the **Main Information** page. This page is for general information about the stock item you wish to delete.
2. Provide your email address and any other relevant contact information on the **Email and Contact Info** page.
3. Select the stock item you wish to delete on the **Select** page.
4. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it on the **Other Information** page.
5. If you have any further information, enter it on the **Other** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the stock item's name and any other relevant information.
* **Email and Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your email address and any other contact information.
* **Select** (`3`, `text`, required): Select the stock item you wish to delete from the list.
* **Other Information** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about the stock item you wish to delete.
* **Other** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter any further information you have about the stock item you wish to delete.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your correct email address to ensure that we can contact you for more information.
- If you're requesting to delete multiple stock items, fill out this form multiple times.
- Please be specific when entering information, especially when selecting the stock item to delete.
