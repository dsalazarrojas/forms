# Content Monitoring Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Monitoring Request Form is used to request ongoing content monitoring for specific web pages or social media handles. This form helps us track and respond to critical content issues in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department (section "Requestor Details") to identify yourself and your team.
2. Provide the URL or social media handle of the content that requires ongoing monitoring (section "Content for Monitoring").
3. Choose the desired frequency of monitoring (section "Desired Monitoring Frequency") to suit your needs.
4. Select the reason for monitoring (section "Reason for Monitoring") to help us prioritize tasks.
5. Indicate the urgency level of monitoring (section "Urgency Level") to ensure we respond promptly.
6. Choose your preferred notification method (section "Preferred Notification Method") for reports.
7. Optionally, specify any additional instructions or keywords to watch for (section "Special Instructions").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Requestor**: Enter your name to help us contact you for updates and notifications.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list to help us route your request to the right team.
* **Content URL or Social Media Handle**: Enter the exact location of the content to be monitored, including the URL or social media handle.
* **Desired Monitoring Frequency**: Choose how often we should check this content, such as Real-time Alerts, Daily Summary, Weekly Audit, or Monthly Review.
* **Reason for Monitoring**: Select why you want to monitor this content, such as Brand Sentiment Tracking, Competitor Activity, or Customer Feedback and Reviews.
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate the level of urgency for monitoring, from Low to High, to help us prioritize tasks.
* **Preferred Notification Method**: Choose how you want to receive reports, such as Email Digest, Slack Channel Notification, or Dashboard Update.
* **Duration of Monitoring (Days)**: Optionally, specify the number of days you want this request to remain active.
* **Special Instructions**: Provide any specific keywords or phrases to watch for during monitoring.
