## Promotion Material Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request promotional materials for events or activities. Please ensure that you only fill out this form for events or activities that are not already approved or on-going. To prevent duplication, please check the [Internal Event Calendar](link-to-calendar) before submitting your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
2. Select the event date using the "Event Date" field.
3. Choose the material(s) you are requesting with the "Material Requested" field.
4. Enter the purpose of the event in the "Purpose of Event" field.
5. Add any relevant example designs for your event in the "Example Designs" field, if applicable.
6. Provide any personal contact information in the "Personal Informations" field, if applicable.
7. Optionally, provide your address in the "Address" field, if required for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Promotion Material Request** (1, promotion_material_request): Enter a brief description of the promotional material you are requesting, e.g., "brochures, posters, or flyers".
* **Event Date** (2, event_date): Enter the date of the event, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Material Requested** (3, material_requested): Select the material(s) you are requesting from the list, e.g., "Material A", "Material B", or "Material C".
* **Purpose of Event** (4, purpose_event): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your event, e.g., "Marketing campaign for new product launch".
* **Example Designs** (5, example_designs): If required, attach any example designs or visuals that you would like us to consider for your promotional material.
* **Email** (7, email): Enter your official email address to ensure we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (8, phone): Enter your official phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (9, address): If required for delivery, enter your physical address to ensure prompt delivery of your promotional material.
