# Painting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painting Request Form is a tool for clients to submit their painting requests to the art studio. It collects the client's contact information and the details of the requested painting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter a brief description of your painting request in the "Message" field.
6. Specify the type of art you are interested in in the "Art Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required/optional: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required/optional: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required/optional: false, type: email): Enter a valid email address to help us contact you back.
* **Phone** (required/optional: false, type: text): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Message** (required/optional: false): Provide a brief description of your painting request.
* **Art Type** (required/optional: false, type: text): Specify the type of art you are interested in (e.g., oil painting, landscape, etc.).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for a smooth processing of your request.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, consider uploading them with your form submission.
- If you need to make changes to your request, contact us at the email provided in the form.
