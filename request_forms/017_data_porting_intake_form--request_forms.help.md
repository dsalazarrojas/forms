# Data Porting Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information from the requestor, including their contact details and transfer details, to facilitate the data porting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and detailed information. You can navigate through the pages using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This section is used to gather basic information about you, the requestor. Please fill out this section with your general information.

* **Transfer Details (Page 2)**: This section is used to collect more specific information about the data transfer. Please provide as much detail as possible.

* **Contact Information (Page 3)**: This section is used to collect your contact information, including phone and email. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

* **Notes (Page 4)**: This section is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your request. Please keep your notes concise and clear.

* **Requestor Phone (Page 5)**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the required format.

* **Data Transfer Method (Page 6)**: This field is used to select the method of data transfer. Please choose the method that best suits your needs.

* **Requestor Email (Page 7)**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your email address in the required format.

* **Request Type (Page 8)**: This field is used to select the type of request you are making. Please choose the type that best describes your request.

* **Transfer Type (Page 9)**: This field is used to select the type of transfer you are requesting. Please choose the type that best describes your transfer.

* **Transfer Date (Page 10)**: This field is used to specify the date of the transfer. Please enter the date in the required format.

* **Transfer Time (Page 11)**: This field is used to specify the time of the transfer. Please enter the time in the required format.

* **Notes 2 (Page 12)**: This section is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your request. Please keep your notes concise and clear.

* **Support Info (Page 13)**: This field is used to select whether you need support or not. Please choose the option that best describes your needs.

* **Requestor Phone Extension (Page 14)**: This field is used to collect your phone extension. Please enter your extension number in the required format.

* **Requestor Work Phone (Page 15)**: This field is used to collect your work phone number. Please enter your work phone number in the required format.

* **Requestor Cell Phone (Page 16)**: This field is used to collect your cell phone number. Please enter your cell phone number in the required format.

* **Requestor Email 2 (Page 17)**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your email address in the required format.

* **Transfer Date (2) (Page 18)**: This field is used to specify the date of the transfer. Please enter the date in the required format.

* **Transfer Time 2 (Page 19)**: This field is used to specify the time of the transfer. Please enter the time in the required format.

* **Notes 2 2 (Page 20)**: This section is used to provide any additional comments or notes about your request. Please keep your notes concise and clear.

* **Requestor Name (Page 21)**: This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your name in the required format.

* **Transfer Method (Page 22)**: This field is used to specify the method of transfer. Please enter the method in the required format.

* **Requestor Department (Page 23)**: This field is used to collect your department. Please enter your department in the required format.

* **Requestor Manager (Page 24)**: This field is used to collect the name of your manager. Please enter the name of your manager in the required format.

* **Requestor Work Phone 2 (Page 25)**: This field is used to collect your work phone number. Please enter your work phone number in the required format.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any errors or delays in processing your request.
* Please do not resubmit your request if you have already submitted one previously.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us for support.
