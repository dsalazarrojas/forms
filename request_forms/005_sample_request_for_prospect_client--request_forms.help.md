# sample_request_for_prospect_client - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from prospects and clients about their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your request in the `request_details` field.
2. Enter the prospect's name in the `client_name` field.
3. Enter the prospect's email address in the `prospect_client_email` field.
4. Enter the prospect's phone number in the `prospect_client_phone` field.
5. Enter the prospect's company name in the `prospect_client_company` field.
6. Select your preferred contact method in the `preferred_contact_method` field.
7. Add any additional comments in the `additional_comments` field.
8. Click the "submit" button to save your request.
