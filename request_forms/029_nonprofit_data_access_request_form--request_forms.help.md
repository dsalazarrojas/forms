# Nonprofit Data Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for non-employees of the organization to request access to data for their own use. It helps to ensure that all requests are properly documented, communicated, and approved internally.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Requester Name** in the "Requester Name" field.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the "Requester Email" field.
3.  Provide your **Organization** name or affiliation.
4.  Briefly describe the **Purpose** of your data access request.
5.  Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of your requested access.
6.  Indicate the **Frequency** of access you need: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or specify other if needed.
7.  Define the **Access Duration** in terms of months.
8.  If applicable, describe the **End Reason** of your access request.
9.  Provide a brief description of the **Data Requester**.
10.  Specify what **Data Requested**.
11.  Provide a point of contact, including your **Contact Email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name (id: 2)**: The name of the person or entity requesting access.
* **Requester Email (id: 3)**: The email address of the person or entity requesting access.
* **Organization (id: 4)**: The name of the organization or affiliation of the requester.
* **Purpose (id: 6)**: A brief description of the reason for requesting access to data.
* **Start Date (id: 7)**: The date when the access is requested to start.
* **End Date (id: 8)**: The date when the access is requested to end.
* **Frequency (id: 9)**: The frequency of access to data: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or other if specified.
* **Access Duration (id: 10)**: The duration of access in months.
* **End Reason (id: 11)**: A brief description of the reason why access will end.
* **Data Requester (id: 12)**: A brief description of the person or entity that will be requesting the data.
* **Data Requested (id: 13)**: A description of the specific data requested.
* **Contact (id: 14)**: The email address to contact for further information or questions.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out this form, so that your request is processed efficiently.
*   Ensure you have a valid email address for communication and follow-up.
*   Review and update your request as needed, if circumstances change. 

This guide will help non-employees of the organization complete the form properly and increase the chances of a swift and successful request.
