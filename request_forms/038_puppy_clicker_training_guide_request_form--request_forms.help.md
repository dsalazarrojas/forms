# Puppy Clicker Training Guide Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your pet and your needs, so that we can provide the best possible guidance on using clicker training with your pet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully. The more information you provide, the better we can assist you with your pet's training needs.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required). These fields are essential for us to understand your needs and provide accurate guidance.
3. If you have any additional information or context that you'd like to share, use the "Additional Notes" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): If you're a business or organization seeking guidance, enter the name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Please describe your role or relationship to the pet (e.g., owner, trainer, rescuer).
* **Rescue or Business** (`rescue_or_business`, select_one, required): Please select whether this is for a pet rescue or a business.
* **Species** (`species`, text, required): Please enter the species of your pet.
* **Breed** (`breed`, text, required): Please enter the breed of your pet.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Please enter your pet's age in years.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Please provide any additional information or context about your pet's training needs.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required): If you have any further information or questions, please enter them here.
* **JotForm ID** (`jotform_id`, text, not required): This field is for internal use, please ignore.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date you're seeking guidance on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please enter the time you're seeking guidance on.
 
**Tips**

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with a specific issue, don't hesitate to ask.
* We'll do our best to provide you with personalized guidance on using clicker training with your pet.
