# Hair Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to schedule a hair consultation with our team. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that our team can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred hair cutting style from the "Preferred Haircutting Style" field.
5. Choose the hair treatment system that best suits your hair in the "Hair Treatment System" field.
6. Choose a time for your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.
7. Select your preferred appointment day from the "Preferred Appointment Day" field.
8. Select a date for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This is the field where you can enter your customer name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: This is the field where you can enter your email address. Our team will use this to contact you about your appointment.
* **Phone**: This is the field where you can enter your phone number. Our team may contact you via phone to confirm your appointment or provide updates.
* **Preferred Haircutting Style**: This is a selection field where you can choose your preferred haircutting style. Please select "Bobbed" if you prefer a short style with no layers, or choose "Other" if you have a different preference.
* **Hair Treatment System**: This is a selection field where you can choose the hair treatment system that suits your hair best. Please select "Hair Treatment System 1", "Hair Treatment System 2", or "Hair Treatment System 3" based on your hair needs.
* **Appointment Time**: This is a field where you can select a time for your appointment. Please choose a time that works best for you.
* **Preferred Appointment Day**: This is a selection field where you can choose a day for your appointment. Please select "Yes" if you are available on the selected day, or "No" if you are not.
* **Appointment Date**: This is a field where you can select a date for your appointment. Please choose a date that is at least 7 days in the future.

### Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let our team know in the space provided.
* Our team will contact you to confirm your appointment and provide further instructions.
* Please allow at least 7 days for us to schedule and confirm your appointment.
