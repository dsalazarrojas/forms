# Yacht Delivery Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request quotes for yacht deliveries. It gathers essential information about the yacht, contact person, and delivery details to provide accurate quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **yacht_type**: Select the type of yacht being transported (Yacht, Sailboat, or Boat).
* **yacht_size**: Choose the size of the yacht (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **contact_first_name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **contact_last_name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the contact person.
* **quote_request**: Enter any specific requests or notes from the contact person.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **additional_info**: Enter any additional information about the delivery request.
* **yacht_value**: Enter the value of the yacht being delivered.
* **quote_value**: Enter the quote value (required field).
* **yacht_delivery_quote**: Enter the date of the yacht delivery quote.
* **contact_address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **quote_status**: Select the status of the quote (Active or Inactive).
* **contact_id**: Enter the ID of the contact person (not clearly labeled, but may be necessary for internal use).
* **quote_id**: Enter the ID of the quote (not clearly labeled, but may be necessary for internal use).
* **delivery_status**: Select the status of the delivery (Active or Inactive).
* **note**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **created_at**: This field is not user-fillable and shows the date the quote was created.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the quote request.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* The form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you have any difficulties, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
