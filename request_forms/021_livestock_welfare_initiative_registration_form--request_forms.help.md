# Livestock Welfare Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Welfare Initiative Registration Form is used to gather information from participants about their participation in the Livestock Welfare Initiative. This includes their interest in participating, type of farm, and specific concerns related to animal welfare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your participant information in the "Participant Information" field.
2. If applicable, provide details about yourself as a participant in the "Participant Details" field.
3. In the "Contact" field, provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
4. Select your "Participant Type" from the options provided.
5. If you participate in any programs, select them in the "Participant Programs" field.
6. Provide any relevant information about the animal(s) in the "Animal Information" field.
7. Share any specific animal welfare concerns you may have in the "Welfare Concerns" field.
8. Finally, provide any farm-related information in the "Farm Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information): Provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a participant in the Livestock Welfare Initiative.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details): If you wish to share more details about yourself, please use this field for any additional information.
* **Contact** (Contact): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Participant Type** (Participant Type): Select one of the options that best describes your role in the Livestock Welfare Initiative. 
* **Participant Programs** (Participant Programs): If you participate in any programs related to animal welfare, select them from the options provided. 
* **Animal Information** (Animal Information): Provide any relevant information about the animal(s) you are involved with, such as breed, size, and location.
* **Animal Details** (Animal Details): Share any additional details about the animal(s), such as health conditions or behavioral issues.
* **Welfare Concerns** (Welfare Concerns): If you have any specific concerns about animal welfare, please share them in this field.
* **Farm Information** (Farm Information): Provide any farm-related information that you feel is relevant, such as farm size or farm location.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (Phone): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields with a "required" designation are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
* If you have already registered with us, you may not need to fill out this form again. Check with us if you are unsure.
