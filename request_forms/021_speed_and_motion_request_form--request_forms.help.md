# Speed And Motion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed And Motion Request Form is used to gather information about experiments that require speed and motion analysis. This form helps the user submit a request for an experiment, providing details about the experiment's start and end times, speed, motion, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **experiment_start_time** and **experiment_end_time**.
2. Enter the **speed** of the experiment as a numerical value.
3. Provide a brief description of the **experiment_details**.
4. Choose the **experiment_type** from the list of options.
5. Select the person or people to be assigned to the experiment from the list of options in **assigned_to**.
6. Select the type of request from the list of options in **request_type**.
7. Fill in the **submitted_by** field with your email address.
8. Optionally, enter any additional information in the **notes** field.
9. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **experiment_start_time**: Enter the start time of the experiment.
* **experiment_end_time**: Enter the end time of the experiment.
* **speed**: Enter the speed of the experiment as a numerical value.
* **experiment_details**: Provide a brief description of the experiment.
* **experiment_type**: Choose the type of experiment from the list of options.
* **assigned_to**: Select the person or people assigned to the experiment.
* **request_type**: Select the type of request from the list of options.
* **date**: Enter the date of submission (this field is automatically populated).
* **submitted_by**: Enter your email address.
* **submitted_by_phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **submitted_at**: Enter the date and time of submission (this field is automatically populated).
* **updated_at**: Enter the date and time of last update (this field is automatically populated).
