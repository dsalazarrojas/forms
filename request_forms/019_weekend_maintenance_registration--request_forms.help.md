# weekend-maintenance-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance activities that take place on weekends. It allows users to submit a request for maintenance, including the type of maintenance, locations involved, and details about the work to be performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of maintenance required from the list of options.
2. Indicate whether the maintenance locations are on campus or not.
3. Enter the start and end dates for the weekend maintenance.
4. Enter the start and end times for the maintenance, if applicable.
5. Provide the name and contact information of the person or department requesting the maintenance.
6. Enter any relevant safety remarks or comments for the maintenance crew.
7. Enter a brief request for the weekend maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Request Type** (`maintenance_request_type`, select_one): Choose the type of maintenance required. This will help the maintenance team prioritize and schedule the work.
* **Locations** (`maintenance_locations`, select_multiple): Check all the locations where the maintenance will take place. This will help the maintenance team understand the scope of the work.
* **Weekend Start Date** (`weekend_start_date`, date): Enter the date when the weekend maintenance is scheduled to start. This will help the maintenance team plan and prepare for the work.
* **Weekend End Date** (`weekend_end_date`, date): Enter the date when the weekend maintenance is scheduled to end. This will help the maintenance team plan and prepare for the work.
* **Maintenance Start Time** (`maintenance_start_time`, time): Enter the time when the maintenance is scheduled to start, if applicable. This will help the maintenance team plan and prepare for the work.
* **Maintenance End Time** (`maintenance_end_time`, time): Enter the time when the maintenance is scheduled to end, if applicable. This will help the maintenance team plan and prepare for the work.
* **Requester Name** (`maintenance_requester_name`, text): Enter the name and title of the person or department requesting the maintenance.
* **Requester Phone** (`maintenance_requester_phone`, text): Enter the contact information of the person or department requesting the maintenance.
* **Maintenance Location** (`maintenance_location`, text): Enter the address or location where the maintenance will take place.
* **Safety Remarks** (`safety_remarks`, text): Enter any safety remarks or comments that the maintenance team needs to be aware of during the maintenance.
* **Weekend Maintenance Request** (`weekend_maintenance_request`, text): Enter a brief summary of the maintenance request.

This form is designed to make it easy for users to request weekend maintenance activities and provide all the necessary information for the maintenance team to complete the work.
