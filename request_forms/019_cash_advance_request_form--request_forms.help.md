# Cash Advance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Advance Request Form is used to request a cash advance from the company for various reasons such as emergency, hardship, or medical expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Cash Advance Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Full Name in the Employee Full Name field.
2. Enter your Employee ID Number in the Employee ID Number field.
3. Select your Department in the Department field.
4. Select your Job Position in the Job Position field.
5. Enter the Date of Hire in the Date of Hire field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
6. Enter the Request Date in the Request Date field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. Specify the Amount Requested in the Amount Requested field.
8. Provide a Reason for Advance Request in the Reason for Advance Request field.
9. Select the Category of Request in the Category of Request field from the provided options.
10. Specify the Date Amount is Needed By in the Date Amount is Needed By field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
11. Choose a Proposed Repayment Method in the Proposed Repayment Method field from the provided options.
12. Specify the Repayment Period in months in the Repayment Period (months) field.
13. Enter your Current Annual Salary in the Current Annual Salary field.
14. Select your Payroll Frequency in the Payroll Frequency field from the provided options.
15. Check if you have previously received advances in the Have you received previous advances field.
15. If yes, provide Details of Previous Advances in the Details of Previous Advances field.
16. If any previous advances are outstanding, enter the Current Outstanding Balance on Previous Advances field.
17. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate and true by selecting Yes, I confirm in the Employee Authorization field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification card or other official documents.
* **Employee ID Number** (Text, Required): Enter your employee ID number, which is typically a unique identifier assigned to you by the company.
* **Department** (Text, Required): Select the department you belong to from the provided options.
* **Job Position** (Text, Required): Select your job position or role within the company from the provided options.
* **Date of Hire** (Date, Required): Enter the date you were hired by the company in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Request Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are requesting the advance in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Amount Requested** (Number, Required): Specify the amount you are requesting as a cash advance.
* **Reason for Advance Request** (Text, Required): Explain why you are requesting a cash advance. Be clear and concise in your explanation.
* **Category of Request** (Select One, Required): Select the category of request that best fits your reason for requesting the advance from the provided options.
* **Date Amount is Needed By** (Date, Required): Enter the date you need the amount by in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Proposed Repayment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose how you propose to repay the advance from the provided options.
* **Repayment Period (months)** (Number, Required): Specify the number of months it will take you to repay the advance.
* **Current Annual Salary** (Number, Required): Enter your current annual salary.
* **Payroll Frequency** (Select One, Required): Select how frequently you are paid from the provided options.
* **Have you received previous advances** (Select One, Required): Check if you have received previous advances.
* **Details of Previous Advances** (Text, Optional): If you have received previous advances, provide details about them.
* **Current Outstanding Balance on Previous Advances** (Number, Optional): If any previous advances are outstanding, enter the current balance.
* **Employee Authorization** (Select One, Required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and true by selecting Yes, I confirm.

Note: This help guide is for reference only and should be reviewed and updated according to the company's policies and procedures.
