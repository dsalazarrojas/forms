# Internal Medicine Clinical Rotation Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help medical staff request an internal medicine clinical rotation. It allows users to provide details about the rotation, including the start and end dates, type, location, and any relevant notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
2. Enter your clinical rotation details in the "Clinical Rotation Details" field.
3. Select the start date of your rotation from the calendar picker.
4. Select the end date of your rotation from the calendar picker.
5. Choose the type of rotation you are requesting (e.g., Internal Medicine, General Medicine, Palliative Care).
6. Select the locations where you will be rotating (e.g., Hospital A, Hospital B, Hospital C).
7. If applicable, enter the name of your preceptor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinical Rotation Details** (`clinical_rotation_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your clinical rotation, including any relevant details about your goals and objectives.
* **Rotation Start Date** (`rotation_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your rotation. This can be any future date.
* **Rotation End Date** (`rotation_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your rotation. This can be any future date.
* **Rotation Type** (`rotation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of rotation you are requesting, such as Internal Medicine, General Medicine, or Palliative Care.
* **Rotation Location** (`rotation_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the locations where you will be rotating, such as Hospital A, Hospital B, or Hospital C.
* **Rotation Preceptor** (`rotation_preceptor`, text, optional): Enter the name of your preceptor, if applicable.
* **Rotation Notes** (`rotation_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your rotation.

## Tips

* Make sure to clearly and concisely describe your clinical rotation details to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the rotation coordinator for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and any information provided will be kept confidential.
