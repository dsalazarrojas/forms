# Equipment Request And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equipment Request And Release Form is used to request or release equipment within the organization. This form helps track the status of equipment requests and releases, ensuring that the necessary procedures are followed for proper approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to the form and select "Request Page" or "Release Page" depending on your intention.
2.  Fill in the necessary fields as prompted, including the date of request, date of return, device description, and other relevant details.
3.  If you are requesting equipment, select the type of equipment and confirm the condition of the device.
4.  If you are releasing equipment, provide the necessary details about the equipment being released.
5.  Add any additional remarks or comments as necessary.
6.  Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Request Page** 
(Request Page)

*   This is the starting point for submitting a new equipment request. Select "Request Page" to begin the process.

### **2. Release Page** 
(Release Page)

*   This is the starting point for releasing equipment back into the inventory. Select "Release Page" to begin the process.

### **3. Date of Request** 
(date_of_request)

*   Enter the date on which the equipment was requested.

### **4. Date of Return** 
(date_of_return)

*   Enter the date on which the equipment is due to be returned.

### **5. Device Description** 
(device_description)

*   Provide a brief description of the equipment being requested or released.

### **6. Requested By** 
(requested_by)

*   Enter the name of the person or team requesting or releasing the equipment.

### **7. Device Condition** 
(device_condition)

*   Indicate the condition of the equipment being requested or released.

### **8. Equipment Type** 
(equipment_type)

*   Select the type of equipment being requested or released.

### **9. Equipment Location** 
(equipment_location)

*   Enter the location where the equipment is currently located or will be located.

### **10. Remarks** 
(remarks)

*   Add any additional comments or remarks about the equipment request or release.

### **11. Equipment Name** 
(equipment_name)

*   Enter the name of the equipment being requested or released.

### **12. Organization** 
(organization)

*   Enter the name of the organization requesting or releasing the equipment.
