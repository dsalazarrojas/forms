# Clubhouse Room Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clubhouse Room Request Form is a tool used to collect information about meeting room requests. It helps us understand what type of room is needed, when it's needed, and what resources are required to make the meeting a success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Select the type of room you need.
3. Choose a date and time for your meeting.
4. Specify the expected number of participants.
5. Describe the purpose of your meeting.
6. If this is a recurring meeting, select the frequency and duration.
7. Check off any special equipment or services you need.
8. Add any additional setup or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: This is the name of the person requesting the room. Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or email address.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you to confirm your request.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please enter it. This is not required, but it can help us reach out to you if needed.
* **Request Date**: This is the date you need the room for. Please select a specific date for your meeting.
* **Organization or Department Name**: If you're representing a company or department, please enter their name.
* **Room Type Needed**: Choose the type of room you need:
	+ Meeting Room: A general-purpose room for meetings.
	+ Conference Room: A room with a projector and whiteboard for presentations.
	+ Classroom: A room with seating and tables for classes or workshops.
	+ Private Room: A room for private meetings or interviews.
	+ Multi-Purpose Room: A room that can be used for various purposes.
	+ Virtual Room: A virtual meeting room for online meetings.
* **Desired Date**: This is the date you would like to have the room for. Please select a specific date for your meeting.
* **Start Time**: This is the start time for your meeting.
* **End Time**: This is the end time for your meeting.
* **Expected Number of Participants**: Please estimate the number of people attending your meeting.
* **Purpose of Meeting**: Describe the purpose of your meeting in a few words.
* **Is This a Recurring Meeting?**: If this is a recurring meeting, select the frequency:
	+ No, One-Time: A one-time meeting.
	+ Weekly: A meeting that repeats every week.
	+ Bi-Weekly: A meeting that repeats every two weeks.
	+ Monthly: A meeting that repeats every month.
	+ Other Schedule: Select this option if your meeting schedule is different.
* **Recurring Duration**: If this is a recurring meeting, describe how long the meetings will last.
* **Equipment Needed**: Check off any equipment you need:
	+ Projector and Screen: A projector and screen for presentations.
	+ Whiteboard: A whiteboard for note-taking.
	+ Conference Phone: A conference phone for phone calls.
	+ Video Conference Setup: A setup for video conferencing.
	+ WiFi Access: Access to the WiFi network.
	+ Catering Service: A service to provide food and drinks.
	+ Recording Equipment: Equipment for recording meetings.
* **Special Setup or Requirements**: Add any special requests or requirements.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to help us assess your room request.
