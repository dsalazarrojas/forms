# plastic_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to plastic materials or equipment from a facility or lab.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your information as the requestor.
* Select the facility where you would like to access the materials or equipment.
* Choose which labs you would like to have access to.
* Provide the date you need to start using the materials or equipment.
* If necessary, select a reason for your request.
* Enter your email and phone number.
* Leave any additional comments.
* Select the person who will approve or decline your request.
* Select the status of your request (Pending, Approved, Declined).
* Select the person who submitted your request.
* Select the status of your request regarding facilities (Manager 1, Manager 2, Manager 3).
* Select the status of your request regarding labs (Manager 1, Manager 2, Manager 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor** (`Requestor`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Facility** (`Facility`, select_one, optional): Select the facility where you would like to access the materials or equipment.
* **Lab** (`Lab`, select_multiple, optional): Select which labs you would like to have access to.
* **Date Needed** (`Date Needed`, date, optional): Provide the date you need to start using the materials or equipment in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time Needed** (`Time Needed`, time, optional): Provide the time you need to start using the materials or equipment.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, note, optional): Provide a reason for your request, if necessary.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments about your request.
* **Approved By** (`Approved By`, select_one, optional): Select the person who will approve or decline your request.
* **Approved Date** (`Approved Date`, date, optional): The date your request was approved.
* **Declined By** (`Declined By`, select_one, optional): Select the person who declined your request.
* **Declined Date** (`Declined Date`, date, optional): The date your request was declined.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one, optional): Select the person who submitted your request.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, date, optional): The date your request was submitted.
* **Facility Approved** (`Facility Approved`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding facilities.
* **Facility Declined** (`Facility Declined`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding facilities.
* **Lab Approved** (`Lab Approved`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding labs.
* **Lab Declined** (`Lab Declined`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding labs.
* **Submitted** (`Submitted`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request (Pending, Approved, Declined).
* **Facility Submitted** (`Facility Submitted`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding facilities.
* **Lab Submitted** (`Lab Submitted`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request regarding labs.
