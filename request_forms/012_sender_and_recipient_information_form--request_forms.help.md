# Sender And Recipient Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about the sender and recipient of a package or item, including contact details, delivery preferences, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Sender Information" section with your own details.
2. Enter the recipient's information in the "Recipient" section.
3. Select the delivery method and status from the respective dropdown menus.
4. If required, provide a delivery date and time for when the item is expected to be delivered.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Select the sender and recipient options from the respective dropdown menus.
7. Finally, review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient** (`recipient_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the recipient's name and any other relevant details.

* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the item is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the item is expected to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the item is expected to be delivered.
