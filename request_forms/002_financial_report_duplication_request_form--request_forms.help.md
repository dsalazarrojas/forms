# financial_report_duplication_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The financial report duplication request form is designed to collect information from employees to request a financial report duplication from their supervisor or team lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information and details before submitting.
2. Fill out the form completely and thoroughly, using the available fields to provide accurate and concise information.
3. Submit the form to your supervisor or team lead for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe your request for a financial report duplication. Please be as detailed and clear as possible, including any relevant context or circumstances that may affect your request.
- **Report Preferences** (`report_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can specify any specific preferences or requirements for the duplicated report, such as format, date range, or other specific details.
- **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information related to your request, such as the reason for the duplication or any relevant deadlines.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and details before submitting the form.
- If you're requesting a financial report duplication for a period already covered by an existing report, it might be more efficient to review the original report instead.
- You can always contact your supervisor or team lead if you have any questions or need further assistance.
