# Animal Adoption Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Adoption Application Form is a crucial document that helps the shelter assess an individual's or household's suitability for adopting an animal. It's essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure the animal's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

*   Read each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
*   Ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
*   Do not duplicate information that you know the shelter already has about you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. -- Animal Adoption Application --
This is the initial page with a brief introduction to the adoption process. It's not a field that requires filling out.

### 2. Applicant Full Name
*   **Applicant Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email Address
*   **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your current email address. This will help the shelter staff contact you regarding your adoption application.

### 4. Phone Number
*   **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number. This will help the shelter staff contact you regarding your adoption application.

### 5. -- Adopter Background --
This is an informational section about your background as an adopter. It's not a field that requires filling out.

### 6. Street Address
*   **Street Address** (`text`, required): Enter your current street address where you currently live.

### 7. City, State, and Zip Code
*   **City, State, and Zip Code** (`text`, required): Enter your city, state, and zip code where you currently live.

### 8. Type of Animal Interested In
*   **Type of Animal Interested In** (`select_one`, required): Choose the type of animal you're interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, or Small Animal).

### 9. Specific Animal Name (If Known)
*   **Specific Animal Name (If Known)** (`text`, optional): If you know the name of a specific animal, enter it here.

### 10. -- Care and Environment --
This is an informational section about providing care and environment for the animal. It's not a field that requires filling out.

### 11. Where will the animal spend most of its time?
*   **Where will the animal spend most of its time?** (`select_one`, required): Choose where the animal will spend most of its time (Indoors, Outdoors, Both).

### 12. Number of hours the animal will be alone per day
*   **Number of hours the animal will be alone per day** (`number`, required): Enter the number of hours the animal will be left alone per day.

### 13. Primary plan for providing animal exercise
*   **Primary plan for providing animal exercise** (`select_one`, required): Choose how you plan to exercise the animal (Daily Walks, Fenced Yard, Professional Walker, Other).

### 14. -- References --
This is an informational section about providing references. It's not a field that requires filling out.

### 15. Current or Previous Veterinarian Name and Phone
*   **Current or Previous Veterinarian Name and Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your current or previous veterinarian's name and phone number.

### 16. Personal Reference Name and Phone Number
*   **Personal Reference Name and Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter a non-family member's name and phone number who can vouch for you as an adopter.

### 17. Do you consent to a scheduled home visit?
*   **Do you consent to a scheduled home visit?** (`select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to a scheduled home visit by shelter staff (`True` or `False`).
