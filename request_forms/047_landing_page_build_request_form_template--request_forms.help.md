# Landing Page Build Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a landing page build for your project. We need details about your project to create an effective page that represents your business or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your project title and details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Select the status of your project (Active or Inactive) in the "Status" field.
3. Enter your project's deadlines in the "Project Deadlines" section, including start and end dates and duration.
4. Describe your project's objectives in the "Project Objectives" field.
5. Enter your project's description in the "Project Description" field.
6. Choose whether you have a project manager and enter their email and phone number (if applicable).
7. Provide your project's budget information (if any).
8. If your project has any specific requirements, enter them in the "Project Requirements" field.
9. Select whether your project requires approvals and enter any other approvals information (if needed).
10. Add any additional comments or notes for the project deadlines section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of your project and any other relevant details that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Team Members**: Select whether your project has team members.
* **Project Deadlines**: Enter your project's start and end dates, as well as its duration.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your project.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your project.
* **Project Objectives**: Describe the main objectives of your project.
* **Project Description**: Enter a description of your project.
* **Status**: Select whether your project is currently Active or Inactive.
* **Project Manager**: Enter your project manager's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your project manager's email.
* **Phone**: Enter your project manager's phone number.
* **Project Requirements**: Enter any specific requirements for your project.
* **Requirements**: Provide a description of your project's requirements.
* **Approvals**: Select whether your project requires approvals and enter any other approvals information (if needed).
* **Project Approvals Other**: Enter any other project approval information.
* **Project Approvals Other 2**: Enter any additional project approval information.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your project deadlines.
* **Project Deadlines Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your project's deadlines.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your project's deadlines.
* **Project Deadlines Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments about your project's deadlines.
* **Project Deadelines Comments 3**: Add any additional comments or notes about your project's deadlines.
