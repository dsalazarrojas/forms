# Add On Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Add On Permission Request Form is designed to gather information for approval or implementation purposes. It helps ensure that all necessary details are captured and that requests are properly documented and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Requester name field with your name.
2. Enter the Department name where the request is being made from.
3. Describe the Add On you are requesting in the Add on description field.
4. Provide a Business justification for why you need the Add On.
5. (Optional) If the add-on is denied, describe the Impact of denial.
6. (Optional) If there's a deadline for approval or implementation, enter the Required by date.
7. Suggest an Approver's name who can review and approve your request.
8. (Optional) If you have any Attachments or related documents, enter a description.
9. (Optional) Provide Any Additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name**: Enter your name.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department requesting the add-on.
* **Add on description**: Describe the add-on you are requesting.
* **Business justification**: Explain why you need the add-on.
* **Impact of denial**: Describe the consequences if the add-on is not approved.
* **Required by date**: Enter the deadline for approval or implementation.
* **Approver name**: Suggest a name of the approver or manager who can review and approve your request.
* **Attachments description**: Describe any related attachments or documents.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional information or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a unique add-on description to avoid duplication of requests.
* If you need help or clarification, contact form@gic.mx.
