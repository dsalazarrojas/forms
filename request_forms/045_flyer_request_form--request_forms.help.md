# Flyer_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a flyer for a specific date and location. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information to facilitate the processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all the required fields.
2.  Make sure to enter your correct full name and contact number.
3.  Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you back.
4.  Select a specific date for the requested flyer.
5.  Choose the time slot you are requesting for the flyer.
6.  Provide the exact location where the flyer is needed.
7.  Enter a brief description of your request.
8.  Once you are satisfied that all fields are correct, submit the form to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Flyer Details**: A brief description of the flyer you are requesting. This field is not required.
*   **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on any official documents.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you back about your request status.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number where we can reach you if we need more information.
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event for which you are requesting a flyer. Please select a date that is in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Choose the specific time slot you are requesting for the flyer.
*   **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the exact location where the flyer is needed.
*   **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your request so we can understand the purpose of the flyer.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are accurate and clear to facilitate the processing of your request.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the description field.
*   We will contact you back once we receive your request to confirm the details.
*   Please allow a reasonable time frame for us to process your request.
