# Employee Permanent Residency Assistance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Permanent Residency Assistance Request Form is used to record and verify employee permanent residency information. This form is required for employees who wish to update or verify their permanent residency status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee's details, including name, employer, and address.
2. Provide the reason for your permanent residency request.
3. Select the current permanent residency status.
4. Enter the date of your permanent residency application and expiration date (if applicable).
5. Enter your address and postal code, as well as your employer's address and postal code.
6. Add any notes or remarks about your permanent residency application.
7. Sign the form, both as the employee and employer, to confirm that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee's name as it appears on their permanent residency document.
* **Employer** (`employer`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employer's name as it appears on their business registration document.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for your permanent residency request.
* **Permanent Residency Status** (`permanent_residency_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current permanent residency status from the provided options.
* **Employee Permanent Residency Date** (`employee_permanent_residency_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your permanent residency application.
* **Permanent Residency Application Number** (`permanent_residency_application_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the number of your permanent residency application.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee's address.
* **Employee Postal Code** (`employee_postal_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee's postal code.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer Postal Code** (`employer_postal_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employer's postal code.
* **Permanent Residency Application Date** (`permanent_residency_application_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your permanent residency application.
* **Permanent Residency Expiration Date** (`permanent_residency_expiration_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your permanent residency expiration (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, required/optional): Add any notes or remarks about your permanent residency application.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Signature Date** (`employee_signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Employer Signature Date** (`employer_signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your employer's signature.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign the form as the employee, confirming that the information is accurate.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign the form as the employer, confirming that the information is accurate.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you understand the form and its purpose.
* **Employer Acknowledgement** (`employer_acknowledgement`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you understand the form and its purpose.
* **Permanent Residency Status Notes** (`permanent_residency_status_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your permanent residency status.
* **Permanent Residency Application Notes** (`permanent_residency_application_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your permanent residency application.
* **Permanent Residency Rejection Reason** (`permanent_residency_rejection_reason`, `text`, required/optional): If your application was rejected, provide a reason for the rejection.
