# Housing Rehabilitation Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Rehabilitation Program Application Form is designed to collect information from property owners or managers that meet the program's eligibility criteria. This form is used to gather data on properties and households that are eligible for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information about your property and household before starting the application process.
* Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
* Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Property Info** (text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the property you are applying for, including its location, size, and any relevant details.
* **2. Owner Info** (text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **3. Household Info** (text, optional): Please describe the household that will be occupying the property, including the number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
* **4. Household Occupants** (text, optional): List the names and contact information of all household members, including children and pets.
* **5. Household Income** (number, optional): Please provide the monthly household income.
* **6. Property Value** (number, optional): Please enter the approximate value of the property.
