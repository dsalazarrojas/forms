# equipment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_request_form is a simple form designed to gather information from equipment owners and potential clients about their equipment-related needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine who will be filling out the form. If you're an equipment owner, please answer the form questions truthfully. If you're a client, please have the equipment owner fill out the form.
2. Answer the questions truthfully about your studio and equipment situation.
3. Select the correct answer for who will be filling out the form.
4. If you're filling out the form on behalf of the equipment owner, please sign the acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have a studio** (`have_studio`, `text`, optional): This field is used to check if you have a studio of your own.
* **Have equipment** (`have_equipment`, `text`, optional): This field is used to confirm if you have equipment.
* **Want to make profit** (`wanted_profit`, `text`, optional): This field is used to know if you're looking to make a profit from your equipment.
* **Can fill out this form** (`can_fill_out`, `select_one`, required): Select who will be filling out this form: "Equipment owner will fill out this form" or "The clients will fill out this form".
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, `note`, optional): This is a field for acknowledgement from the equipment owner, to be filled out by the equipment owner if they're filling out the form themselves.
