# Home Sale Pre Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request pre-approval for a home sale. It helps us gather necessary information to facilitate the sale process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and contact information.
2. Select the type of property being sold (e.g. Condo, Townhouse, Single Family).
3. Choose the status of the property (e.g. Active, Sold, Pending).
4. Provide any additional property notes if necessary.
5. Enter the pre-approval request number.
6. If applicable, enter the sale price, down payment, and sale terms.
7. Enter the date of the sale.
8. If applicable, enter the agent's name, phone number, and email.
9. Enter any reason for pre-approval, if required.
10. Confirm the sale status and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address** (text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Property Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of property being sold (e.g. Condo, Townhouse, Single Family).
* **Property Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the property (e.g. Active, Sold, Pending).
* **Property Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional property notes.
* **Pre-Approval Request** (number, required): Enter the pre-approval request number.
* **Sale Price** (number, optional): Enter the sale price of the property.
* **Down Payment** (number, optional): Enter the down payment amount.
* **Sale Terms** (text, optional): Enter any sale terms.
* **Sale Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the sale.
* **Agent Name** (text, optional): Enter the agent's name.
* **Agent Phone** (text, optional): Enter the agent's phone number.
* **Agent Email** (email, optional): Enter the agent's email address.
* **Pre-Approve Reason** (text, optional): Enter any reason for pre-approval.
* **Sale Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the sale status (e.g. Active, Sold, Pending).
* **Sale Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional sale notes.
* **Property Type 2** (select_one, optional): Select a second property type (e.g. Condo, Townhouse, Single Family).
* **Property Status 2** (select_multiple, optional): Select a second property status (e.g. Active, Sold, Pending).
* **Sale Price 2** (number, optional): Enter a second sale price.
* **Down Payment 2** (number, optional): Enter a second down payment amount.
* **Sale Terms 2** (text, optional): Enter any additional sale terms.
* **Sale Date 2** (date, optional): Enter a second sale date.
