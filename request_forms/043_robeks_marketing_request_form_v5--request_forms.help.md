# Robeks Marketing Request Form V5 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users interested in requesting marketing services or collaboration from Robeks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: "User Name" and "Job Request".
2. Provide your "Email" and "Company" if you wish to share more context about your request.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`User Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **email** (`Email`, email, optional): Provide your business or professional email address, if applicable.
* **Job Request** (`Job Request`, text, required): Describe the specific job request or marketing service you are interested in.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more context about your company, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Job Request" field to ensure that Robeks can effectively understand your needs.
* If you're representing a company, please ensure that you have permission to share your company's information.
* For any technical issues or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
