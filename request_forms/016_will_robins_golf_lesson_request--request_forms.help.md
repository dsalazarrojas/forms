# Will Robins Golf Lesson Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate users in requesting golf lessons from the instructor. Please ensure you only submit a single request per person, as this will help the instructor better manage their schedule and provide more personalized services. If you've already submitted a request, you can still edit and submit the existing request if you'd like to change any information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the following form with your details and preferences.
2. Submit the form and await further instructions from the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input**: A field for entering your personal details. Please enter your name, and any other information relevant to your request.
* **lesson_type**: Choose whether you want a regular golf lesson or not. This will help the instructor tailor their approach to your needs.
* **lesson_date**: Select the date for your golf lesson. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy to ensure accurate scheduling.
* **lesson_time**: Choose the time slot for your golf lesson. Please note that availability might be limited depending on instructor's schedule.
* **location**: Indicate where you want to have your golf lesson, or leave blank for instructor's discretion.
* **instructor**: Indicate whether you want the lesson to be conducted by a particular instructor or not.
* **lesson_topics**: Select the topics you want to focus on during your lesson, or leave blank to leave it up to the instructor.
* **special_request**: If you have any special requests or needs, please describe them here.
* **email**: Enter your email address for the instructor to contact you for further instructions.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for the instructor to contact you in case of any urgent matters.

## Tips

* Be specific with your preferences to help the instructor provide the best experience.
* Please ensure your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* The instructor will respond with further instructions once they receive your form submission.
* Please wait patiently for the instructor to contact you for scheduling and details.
