# Meeting Attendance Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request removal from a meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the meeting details page and ensure that the meeting you are requesting to be removed from is correctly identified.
2. On the reason for removal page, provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting to be removed.
3. Check the box next to "Requestor: Yes" if you are requesting to be removed, and check the box next to "Meeting attendees: No" if you are not attending the meeting.
4. Fill in your contact information in the email and phone number fields, so that the meeting organizer can respond to your request.
5. Provide any additional context or details in the comments field, if needed.
6. Submit the form to request to be removed from the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting details**: This page asks for the meeting title, date, and time. Make sure to select the correct meeting from the list to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* **Reason for removal**: Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting to be removed from the meeting.
* **Requestor**: Check the box next to "Yes" to confirm that you are requesting to be removed from the meeting.
* **Meeting attendees**: Check the box next to "No" to confirm that you are not attending the meeting.
* **Meeting attendees**: This field is a checkbox with multiple options, asking if you are requesting to be removed from attending or have already attended the meeting.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional context or details about your request, if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the meeting organizer can respond to your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the meeting organizer can contact you, if needed.
* **Additional notes**: Provide any additional information or context that you think is relevant to your request.
