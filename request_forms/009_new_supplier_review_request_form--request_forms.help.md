# New Supplier Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect supplier information, including their contact details, offered services or products, and type of review requested. The purpose of this form is to streamline the review process and ensure that all relevant information is gathered in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your supplier's contact details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of service or product your supplier offers (e.g., Invoice, Quote, Certificate).
3. Choose the type of review you are requesting (e.g., Procurement, Operations).
4. Optionally, submit additional comments or requests related to your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): Enter your supplier's name, email, and phone number to ensure we can reach out to them for further information.
* **Offerings** (select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options that best describe your supplier's offerings:
	+ Invoice
	+ Quote
	+ Certificate
* **Documents** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following documents that your supplier may need to provide:
	+ Invoice
	+ Quote
	+ Certificate
* **Review Request** (select one, optional): Choose the type of review you are requesting:
	+ Procurement
	+ Operations

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information to help us review your supplier efficiently.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, use the "Submit Request" field to provide further context.
* Only submit one form per supplier to avoid duplicate reviews.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
