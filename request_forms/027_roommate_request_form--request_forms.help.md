# Roommate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find a suitable roommate for your living space. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about your preferences and needs. This will help us match you with potential roommates who share similar interests and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the first page of the form, which asks if you want roommates. Select "Yes" if you are interested in sharing a living space with others, or "No" if you are not.
2. On the second page, enter your desired move-in date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
3. On the third page, enter your expected move-out date, if applicable.
4. On the following pages, enter your personal details:
	* Name
	* Phone number
	* Email address
	* Preferred rooms or specific roommates you have mentioned
	* Description of yourself and what you are looking for in a roommate
	* Any house rules or specific requirements you have
	* If you have any other specific requirements for potential roommates, please specify them here

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Roommates Wanted** (`roommates_wanted`): This question asks if you are interested in finding a roommate or not. Select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`): This field is used to specify your desired move-in date. Enter the date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy". 
* **Expected Move Out Date** (`expected_move_out_date`): This field is only required if you have a specific date in mind for when you plan to move out. Enter the date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Name** (`name`): This is your full name.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so that potential roommates can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so that potential roommates can contact you.
* **Preferred Rooms** (`preferred_rooms`): If you have a specific room or type of room you prefer, enter it here.
* **Description** (`description`): Please tell us a bit about yourself and what you're looking for in a roommate. This will help us understand your preferences and match you with compatible individuals.
* **House Rules** (`house_rules`): If you have any specific rules or requirements for living in the space, such as quiet hours or cleanliness standards, enter them here.
* **Other Roommates Wanted** (`other_roommates_wanted`): If you have specific requirements or preferences for other roommates, such as age, interests, or background, enter them here.
* **Chat ID** (`chat_id`), **Jid** (`jid`), **Server** (`server`), **Room** (`room`), **Channel** (`channel`): These fields are for communication purposes and should be filled out if you use any chat or messaging platforms for communication.

**Tips**

* Be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers, as this will help us find the most suitable match for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Remember to proofread your form before submitting to ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
