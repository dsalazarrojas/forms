# Waste Recycling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Waste Recycling Form" is designed to help our organization gather essential information about waste recycling practices among its community. This form aims to collect detailed insights on the types, quantities, and descriptions of waste generated, which will assist in developing more effective recycling programs and reducing waste effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To effectively fill out the "Waste Recycling Form," follow these steps:
1.  Ensure you're submitting original data and not duplicating a previous submission.
2.  Review the form carefully and enter accurate information in each field.
3.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable insights.
4.  If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the field-by-field explanations for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Waste Recycling Form** (waste_recycling_form, text, required): Enter your brief description of the form's purpose. This field is mandatory and should be filled out accurately.
*   **Waste Type** (waste_type, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you practice waste recycling, 'No' if you do not. This field helps our organization understand the prevalence of recycling practices in your community.
*   **Quantity** (quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of waste you generate for the selected waste type. Ensure to answer accurately for precise analysis.
*   **Description** (description, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of the waste generation process or any other relevant information you'd like to share. Although this field is optional, your input will contribute significantly to our understanding of waste management.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your submissions for the best possible outcomes.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations for clarification.
- If you have suggestions or ideas for improving waste recycling practices in our community, consider sharing them in the "Description" field.
