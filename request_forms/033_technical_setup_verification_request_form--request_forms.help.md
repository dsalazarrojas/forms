# technical_setup_verification_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for technical setup verification. It is not intended for other uses, such as general feedback or support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. You can access the form through the [URL] provided by your administrator or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### contact_info
• **contact_info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for users to provide their contact information, such as their name, phone number, and/or email address. Please provide a brief description of your setup and any relevant details.

### technical_verification_info
• **technical_verification_info** (`technical_verification_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is meant for users to provide any technical information about their setup, such as software versions, hardware models, or other relevant details. Please provide as much detail as possible so the support team can assist you accurately.

### upload_docs
• **upload_docs** (`upload_docs`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for users to upload any relevant documents or images to support their request. Please ensure that the uploaded files are in a format that can be easily viewed and processed by the support team.

### scheduling
• **scheduling** (`scheduling`, `date`, required/optional): This field is meant for users to select a date and time for the support team to contact them back. Please enter the date and time that works best for you, and the support team will do their best to accommodate it.

### contact_person
• **contact_person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for users to provide the name and title of the person they are contacting, as well as their role or department. Please be as specific as possible so the support team knows who to contact.

### contact_email
• **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is meant for users to provide an email address where the support team can contact them. Please ensure that the email address you provide is active and checked regularly.

## Tips
- Please fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
- Ensure that any uploaded documents are in a format that can be easily viewed and processed by the support team.
- Respond to any follow-up questions or requests from the support team in a timely manner.
- If you have any issues with the form or its purpose, please contact your administrator or support team for assistance.
