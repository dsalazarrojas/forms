# Marketing Material Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Material Printing Request Form is used to submit requests for printing marketing materials, such as brochures, flyers, and posters, to the marketing team. Please use this form to specify the details of the materials you need printed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes the marketing material you are requesting to be printed.
2. Enter the title of the material in the "Marketing Material Printing Request Form" field.
3. Choose the quantity you need printed.
4. Select the deadline for completion.
5. Upload any relevant artwork for the material.
6. Add any additional notes or comments you have about the request.
7. Select the phone number and email of the person submitting the request.
8. Select the agency or team that will handle the printing process.
9. Choose the files you would like to upload.
10. Add any additional notes or comments for the agency.
11. Select the agreement type.
12. Choose the print format, size, color, finish, paper, orientation, bleed, and any other relevant details.
13. Enter the total pages and sheets of the material.
14. Upload any additional files or images.
15. Select any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Material Printing Request Form** (form_title): Enter the title of the material you are requesting to be printed. This should be a clear and concise title that accurately describes the material.
* **Category** (form_category): Select the category that best describes the marketing material you are requesting to be printed, such as brochure, flyer, or poster.
* **Quantity** (form_quantity): Enter the quantity of the material you need printed.
* **Deadline** (form_deadline): Enter the date by which you need the material printed and delivered.
* **Artwork** (form_artwork): Upload any relevant artwork or designs for the material.
* **Notes** (form_notes): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Phone** (form_phone): Enter your phone number for the agency to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Email** (form_email): Enter your email address for the agency to contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Agency** (form_agency): Select the agency or team that will handle the printing process.
* **Upload** (form_upload): Select the files you would like to upload for the agency to use.
* **Notes 2** (form_notes_2): Enter any additional comments or notes for the agency.
* **Agreed** (form_agreed): Select the agreement type that you have with the agency.
* **Agreement Date** (form_agreement_date): Enter the date of the agreement with the agency.
* **Comments** (form_comments): Enter any additional comments or notes about the printing process.
* **Comments 2** (form_comments_2): Enter any additional comments or notes for the agency.
* **Comments 3** (form_comments_3): Enter any additional comments or notes for the agency.
* **Agreement Type** (form_agreement_type): Select the type of agreement you have with the agency.
* **Print Format** (form_print_format): Choose the format of the printed material, such as A4 or A5.
* **Print Size** (form_print_size): Choose the size of the printed material, such as A4 or A3.
* **Print Color** (form_print_color): Choose the color of the printed material, such as CMYK or Pantone.
* **Print Finish** (form_print_finish): Choose the finish of the printed material, such as matte or glossy.
* **Print Paper** (form_print_paper): Choose the type of paper to print on, such as cotton or recycled.
* **Print Orientation** (form_print_orientation): Choose the orientation of the printed material, such as portrait or landscape.
* **Print Bleed** (form_print_bleed): Choose the bleed area for the printed material, such as 0 or 3mm.
* **Printing Notes** (form_printing_notes): Enter any additional comments or notes about the printing process.
* **Total Pages** (form_total_pages): Enter the total number of pages of the material.
* **Total Sheets** (form_total_sheets): Enter the total number of sheets of the material.
* **File 1** (form_file_upload_1): Upload the first file for the agency to use.
* **File 2** (form_file_upload_2): Upload the second file for the agency to use.
* **File 3** (form_file_upload_3): Upload the third file for the agency to use.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct category and agreement type to ensure the agency has the necessary information to process your request.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
