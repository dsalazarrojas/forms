# cultural_performance_travel_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural performance travel support request form is designed to gather information from performers, event organizers, and other stakeholders involved in cultural events and performances that require travel support. This form helps to streamline the process of requesting travel support and ensures that all relevant information is captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Performer Information section with your name, title, and contact details.
2. Provide details about the tour or event you are participating in, including the name, dates, and locations.
3. Specify the dates you would like to travel for the tour or event.
4. Mention the locations you will be visiting during the tour or event.
5. Describe your travel requirements and support needs in the Travel Details section.
6. Indicate if you need any travel support in the Travel Support section.
7. Enter your contact information in the Contact section.
8. If applicable, identify the funder or organization providing support.
9. Finally, submit your request by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Information** (`Performer Information`, text, optional):
	+ This is the section for you to input your name and title as a performer.
* **Tour/Event Information** (`Tour/Event Information`, text, optional):
	+ Provide the name, dates, and locations of the tour or event you are participating in.
* **Dates** (Dates, text, optional):
	+ Specify the dates you would like to travel for the tour or event.
* **Locations** (Locations, text, optional):
	+ Mention the locations you will be visiting during the tour or event.
* **Travel Details** (Travel Details, text, optional):
	+ Describe your travel requirements and support needs.
* **Travel Support** (Travel Support, text, optional):
	+ Indicate if you need any travel support.
* **Contact** (Contact, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including name, title, and email.
* **Funder** (Funder, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, identify the funder or organization providing support.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional):
	+ Click the "Submit" button to submit your support request.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information for correctness.
- If you need to request travel support, describe your needs clearly and specifically in the Travel Details and Travel Support sections.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification or assistance.
