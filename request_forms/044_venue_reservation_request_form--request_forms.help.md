# Venue Reservation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting venue reservations for events or meetings. Please provide accurate information to help us process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure you've filled in all relevant fields to provide a complete description of your event.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting, as it will be used to process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, Text): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Venue Location** (`venue_location`, Text): Enter the location of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text): Enter a phone number that can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email): Enter a valid email address that can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Select One): Choose the type of event you are requesting a reservation for.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, Text): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, Text): Enter any additional details about the event that might be helpful for us to know.
  (Note: This field is optional, but providing additional information can help us process your request more efficiently.)

## Tips
- Be sure to enter a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you with any updates or questions.
- If you're requesting a reservation for an event that has already been requested, please do not resubmit the form. Instead, contact the contact person or email address provided in your previous request.
