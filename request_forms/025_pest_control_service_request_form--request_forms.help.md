# Pest Control Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request pest control services for your property. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that our team can provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of service you are requesting (Interior or Exterior).
2. Choose a date for the treatment from the calendar.
3. Select the locations where you would like the treatment to take place.
4. Pick the type of pest you are dealing with.
5. Provide a brief description of the pest problem you are experiencing.
6. Add any relevant contact information.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request a service** (`customer_service_request`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of service you are requesting from our pest control team. Please choose either "Interior" or "Exterior" based on your needs.
* **Treatment Date** (`treatment_request_date`, date, required): This field is used to select a date for the treatment. Please choose a date and time that is convenient for you.
* **Location** (`treatment_location`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the locations where you would like the treatment to take place. Choose as many locations as needed, such as "Living Room", "Kitchen", or "Bedroom".
* **Type of pest** (`pest_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of pest you are dealing with. Please choose from the available options such as "Cockroach", "Rat", or "Mouse".
* **Describe the pest** (`pest_description`, text, required): This field is used to provide a brief description of the pest problem you are experiencing. Please be as detailed as possible to help our team understand the issue.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, required): This field is used to add any relevant contact information that our team can use to reach out to you. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This field is used to enter your phone number. Please make sure to enter it accurately, as our team will use it to contact you for further clarification or updates.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address. Our team will use it to send you updates and reminders about your scheduled treatment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that our team can provide the best possible service.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to contact our team for assistance.
* We will review your request and contact you within 24-48 hours to confirm the details.
* If you have any further assistance or follow-up questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
