# Business Idea Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Business Idea Consultation Request form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your personal profile, including your full name and contact email.
2. Provide information about your previous startup experience and the current stage of development of your business idea.
3. Describe your business idea, including its name, model, and target market and audience.
4. Specify the specific areas you need help with and your availability for meeting.
5. Confirm your understanding of the consultation terms and confidentiality policy.
6. Finally, submit the form to initiate the consultation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`entrepreneur_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Email** (`entrepreneur_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Previous Startup Experience** (`previous_startup_experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of your previous startup experience.
* **Idea Name or Working Title** (`business_idea_working_title`, `text`, required): Enter the name or working title of your business idea.
* **Business Model Overview** (`business_idea_concept_summary`, `text`, required): Describe how your idea will make money in a few words.
* **Target Market and Audience** (`target_market_audience_desc`, `text`, required): Identify your primary customers for this business idea.
* **Current Stage of Development** (`current_development_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current stage of development of your business idea.
* **Specific Questions for the Consultant** (`specific_questions_for_consultant`, `text`, required): Specify what areas you would like help with during the consultation.
* **Meeting Availability** (`consultation_meeting_availability`, `text`, required): Provide your general availability for meeting with the consultant.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source_consultation`, `text`, optional): If you have any referral information, please provide it.
* **Consultation Term Agreement** (`confidentiality_terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the consultation terms and confidentiality policy.
* **Date of Request** (`request_submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.

Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, and our team will review your request to determine the next steps for your business idea consultation.
