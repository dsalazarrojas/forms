The purpose of this form is to allow users to request a meeting space for a specific day of the week and time. This form is designed to be completed by users who need to schedule a meeting, and the information collected will be used to determine the best possible meeting time and space. Please note that this form should not be completed if you are requesting a meeting that has already been scheduled or planned.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the day of the week you wish to schedule the meeting in the "Meeting Space" field.
2. Enter the start and end times of the meeting in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
3. Specify the duration of the meeting in the "Meeting Duration" field.
4. Choose the preferred meeting location from the available options in the "Meeting Location" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the meeting in the "Meeting Date" and "Meeting Time" fields, respectively.
6. If desired, add any additional meeting notes in the "Meeting Notes" field.

# Meeting Space Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Space Request Form is a tool used to schedule meetings with the available meeting spaces in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Space** (`meeting_space_request`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the day of the week you wish to schedule the meeting.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the meeting in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the meeting in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more meeting spaces from the available options.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format "MM/dd/yyyy".
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `date`, required): Enter the time of the meeting in the format "MM/dd/yyyy".
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the meeting if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct day of the week for the meeting.
* Be specific with the meeting start and end times.
* Choose a location that fits the meeting size and type.
* If you need to schedule multiple meetings in the same date and time, please contact the meeting space administrator for further assistance.
