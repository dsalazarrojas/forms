# Virtual Peer Mentorship Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request virtual or in-person peer mentorship support from students at the same school or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
3. If you have any questions or need help, contact the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form_1`): This is the main section of the form where you can provide any additional information or context that will help us match you with the best possible mentor.
* **Email** (`email`): \*Please enter your email address that you check regularly. This will be used to communicate with you about your mentorship match.
* **Phone** (`phone`): \*This is your phone number that you would like to be reached at if we need to contact you urgently.
* **School Program Name** (`school_program_name`): \*Enter the name of your current or recently completed program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`): \*Enter the duration of your program in months.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`): \*Enter the date you expected to start your program. This will help us match you with mentors who have similar experience.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`): \*If you need support in any area, describe it here. If not, just leave this field blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`): \*If you have any additional comments or information that will help us understand your needs, provide them here.
* **Mentorship Type** (`mentorship_type`): \*Choose between "Virtual" and "In-person" to determine the type of mentorship support you prefer.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): \*Enter the name of your school or university.
