# School Zone Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Zone Support Request Form is designed to help school administrators and support staff gather information about potential issues or requests related to school zones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are requesting support for a school zone.
2. Enter the name of the school zone you are requesting support for.
3. Provide a brief description of the issue or request.
4. Choose the type of issue or request (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Describe the issue or request in detail.
6. Specify any assistance or support you require.
7. Enter the date and time you would like to receive support.
8. Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the school zone.
9. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the school zone.
10. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the school zone.
11. Add any additional comments or notes about the issue or request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Zone Support Request** (`school_zone_support_request_form`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are requesting support for a school zone.
* **School Zone** (`school_zone_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school zone you are requesting support for.
* **School Zone Location** (`school_zone_location`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the location of the school zone.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of issue or request you are experiencing (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **School Zone Issue Description** (`school_zone_issue_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the issue or request in detail.
* **School Zone Assistance Request** (`school_zone_assistance_request`, text, required/optional): Specify any assistance or support you require.
* **School Zone Support Request Date** (`school_zone_support_request_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time you would like to receive support for the school zone.
* **School Zone Support Request Time** (`school_zone_support_request_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to receive support for the school zone.
* **School Zone Contact Person** (`school_zone_contact_person`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the school zone.
* **School Zone Email** (`school_zone_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the school zone.
* **School Zone Phone** (`school_zone_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the school zone.
* **School Zone Comments** (`school_zone_comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue or request.
