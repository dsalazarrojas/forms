# Smart Queue Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Queue Management Application Form is designed to collect information from visitors to the application, enabling efficient queue management and timely responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "Instructions" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify you as a visitor.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about your visit.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your visit.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are requesting a service or not.
* **Queue Length** (`queue_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the queue you are currently waiting in.
* **Queue Status** (`queue_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of your queue (Active or Inactive).
* **Queue Time** (`queue_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you expect to wait in the queue.
* **Visitor Note** (`visitor_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your visit.
* **Visitor Details** (`visitor_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about your visit.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will respond to your request as soon as possible.
