# Front Seat Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Front Seat Access Request Form is a form used to request access to the front seat for a school or tour group. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you are requesting access for a school or tour group
2. Fill out the form with the requested information (group name, date, location, and contact number)
3. Confirm your request with the relevant tour leader or school representative
4. Submit the form to request access to the front seat

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School or Tour Group** (`school_or_tour_group`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school or tour group.
* **Front-Seat Access Request** (`front_seat_access_request_form_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the front-seat access request in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Tour Leader** (`tour_leader_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tour leader responsible for your group.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for the tour leader or school representative.
* **School Event Date** (`school_event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the school event.
* **School Event Location** (`school_event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the school event.
* **Tour Leader Confirmation** (`tour_leader_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the tour leader has approved the front seat access request. Options: Yes, No.
* **School Confirmation** (`school_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the school representative has approved the front seat access request. Options: Yes, No.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you've filled out the form accurately and truthfully. Options: Yes, No.

## Tips
Please ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the tour leader or school representative for clarification.
