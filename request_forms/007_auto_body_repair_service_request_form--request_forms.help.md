# Auto Body Repair Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their vehicle's repair needs, ensuring that we can provide efficient and effective service to fix the issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details: Provide your full name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
2. Enter your vehicle's details: Enter the make, model, and year of your car to help us identify the issue.
3. Describe the service required: Select all applicable services needed for your vehicle.
4. Add any additional details about the damage: Briefly describe what happened or what needs fixing.
5. Indicate if this repair is an insurance claim: If your repair is covered by insurance, select "Yes."
6. Choose a date for your vehicle's appointment: Pick a date that works best for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Provide your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Provide your contact phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Car Make**: Enter your car's make (e.g., Chevrolet).
* **Car Model**: Enter your car's model (e.g., Silverado).
* **Car Year**: Enter your car's year (e.g., 2022).
* **License Plate**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number for identification.
* **Service Required**: Select all applicable services needed for your vehicle (e.g., Dent Repair, Scratch Repair, Paint Job).
* **Damage Description**: Briefly describe what happened or what needs fixing.
* **Is this an insurance claim?**: Select "Yes" if your repair is covered by insurance; otherwise, select "No".
* **Insurance Company Name**: If your repair is an insurance claim, enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Choose a date for your vehicle's appointment.
* **Is the vehicle driveable?**: Select "Yes" if your vehicle is driveable; otherwise, select "No".

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for help.
- We'll get in touch with you once we receive your form submission to discuss your vehicle's repair further.
