# Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a supply chain disruption forecast for a specific product, segment, region, or date range. This helps our team to identify potential disruptions and take necessary actions to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product(s) affected by the disruption.
2. Select the segment(s) impacted by the disruption.
3. Choose the region(s) where the disruption is expected to occur.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the disruption.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request** (`form_1`): This is the main title of the form, it's a required field to help us identify the purpose of the request.
* **Product 1** (`product_1`): Select the product(s) affected by the disruption. This helps us to narrow down the potential causes and impact of the disruption.
* **Product 2** (`product_2`): Select the second product affected by the disruption. This is optional and can be left blank if only one product is affected.
* **Segment 1** (`segment_1`): Select the segment(s) impacted by the disruption. This can be a specific department, team, or group within the company.
* **Segment 2** (`segment_2`): Select the second segment impacted by the disruption. This is optional and can be left blank if only one segment is impacted.
* **Region 1** (`region_1`): Choose the region(s) where the disruption is expected to occur. This is essential for us to identify potential regional causes and effects.
* **Region 2** (`region_2`): Select the second region where the disruption is expected to occur. This is optional and can be left blank if only one region is affected.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the disruption. This is a required field to provide a clear timeline for our analysis.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the disruption. This is also a required field to understand the duration of the disruption.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the disruption. This is optional but helps us to have a more accurate timeline.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the disruption. This is optional but assists us in scheduling our analysis.
* **Note 1** (`note_1`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the disruption. This is an optional field but helps us to better understand the context.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`): Enter any further notes or comments. This is also an optional field.
* **Email 1** (`email_1`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone 1** (`phone_1`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This is optional but recommended for urgent matters.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`): Enter your email address for contact purposes. This is optional.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This is optional.
* **Form 2** (`form_2`), **Form 3** (`form_3`), **Form 4** (`form_4`), **Form 5** (`form_5`), **Form 6** (`form_6`), **Form 7** (`form_7`), **Form 8** (`form_8`): These fields are currently not used and should be left blank.

## Tips
- Please select all applicable products, segments, and regions.
- Use specific dates and times for the disruption's start and end dates.
- If you have additional notes or comments, provide them in the **Note 1** and **Note 2** fields.
- Review your submission carefully before sending it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
