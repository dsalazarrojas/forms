# Music DVDs Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request music DVDs from our collection. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and we will do our best to accommodate your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields with a red asterisk (\*) as these are required fields.
2. Fill out the Music Store field with your preferred music store or source.
3. Choose the music genre that suits your taste.
4. Enter the artist or band you are interested in.
5. Select the music DVDs you would like to request.
6. Provide your date and time for when you would like to collect your requested music DVDs.
7. Enter your email address for us to contact you with updates.
8. If you would like to provide an alternate contact method, please enter your phone number.
9. If you have any additional comments or requests, please use the message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Music Store** (form_1): Enter the name of your preferred music store or source for your music requests.
    *   Required field
*   **Music Genre** (form_2): Choose your preferred music genre.
    *   Select one of the available options: 1, 2, or 3.
    *   Required field
*   **Music Artist** (form_3): Enter the name of the artist or band you are interested in.
    *   Optional field
*   **Music DVDs** (form_4): Select the music DVDs you would like to request from our collection.
    *   Select multiple DVDs.
    *   Required field
*   **Date** (form_5): Provide the date you would like to collect your requested music DVDs.
    *   Required field
*   **Time** (form_6): Provide the time you would like to collect your requested music DVDs.
    *   Optional field
*   **Email** (form_7): Enter your email address for us to contact you with updates.
    *   Required field
*   **Phone** (form_8): Enter your phone number as an alternate contact method.
    *   Optional field
*   **Message** (form_9): Use this field to provide any additional comments or requests.
    *   Optional field
*   **Additional Comments** (form_10): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
    *   Optional field

Please note that some of these fields might be redundant or have similar functions. However, filling out this form will help us better understand your music preferences and requests.
