# marketing_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marketing_request form is a tool for collecting contact information and details about marketing requests. It is intended to be used by customers, partners, or prospects to submit their marketing-related inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure that you submit the form only once for each marketing request.
- If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to enable us to respond to your request.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like us to consider for your request.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time of day for your request.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
- **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Describe your marketing request in detail.

## Tips
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to ensure accurate processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- We will respond to your request as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
