# l_c_expeditions_travel_consulting_request_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting travel and consulting services. It collects information about your trip or vacation preferences and provides the necessary details for the consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of trip or vacation you are interested in (e.g. Beach, City, Mountain).
2. Choose the destination for your trip.
3. Enter the departure and arrival dates for your trip.
4. Select the departure and arrival times for your trip.
5. Provide a note with any additional information about your trip.
6. Specify the number of people traveling.
7. Choose the services you require (e.g. Hotel, Flight, Car).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your trip/vacation type** (required): This field is used to select the type of trip or vacation you are interested in (e.g. Beach, City, Mountain).
* **Destination** (required): Choose the location for your trip.
* **Departure Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to leave for your trip.
* **Departure Time** (required): Enter the time you plan to leave for your trip.
* **Arrival Date** (required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at your destination for your trip.
* **Arrival Time** (required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at your destination for your trip.
* **Note** (required): Provide any additional information about your trip.
* **Number of people** (required): Specify the number of people traveling with you.
* **Services** (required): Choose the services you require for your trip (e.g. Hotel, Flight, Car).
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
