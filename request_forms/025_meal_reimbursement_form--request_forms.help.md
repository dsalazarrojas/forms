# Meal Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for meal reimbursement for business-related expenses. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your request in the "Date of Request" field.
2. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the appropriate expense category from the options provided in the "Expense Category" field.
4. Enter the amount you are claiming for reimbursement in the "Amount Claimed" field.
5. Optionally, enter any additional remarks or comments in the "Remarks" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Request** (`dateofrequest`, date, true): Enter the date on which you are submitting your request for meal reimbursement.
* **Employee ID** (`employeepassword`, number, true): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Expense Category** (`expense_category`, select_one, true): Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of meal expense you are claiming (e.g., Lunch, Dinner, etc.).
* **Amount Claimed** (`amountclaimed`, number, true): Enter the total amount you are claiming for reimbursement.
* **Date Approved** (`dateapproved`, date, false): This field will be populated by the system when your request is approved. Do not fill this out.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, false): Enter any additional comments or explanations for your request, if needed.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, false): Enter your name or a brief description of your request for submission purposes only.
