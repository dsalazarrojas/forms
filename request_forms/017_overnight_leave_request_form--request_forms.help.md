# Overnight Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Overnight Leave Request Form is used to submit a request for overnight leave from work. This form is intended for employees to submit their leave requests for approved overnight absences, ensuring they are accounted for in the company's scheduling and attendance system.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your name, contact method (phone or email), and contact person (if applicable).
2. **Specify travel dates**: Enter your start and end dates for the requested leave period.
3. **State your reason for leave**: Briefly explain why you need to take overnight leave.
4. **Choose your leave type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (Personal, Business, or Vacation).
5. **Indicate the duration**: Enter the length of your requested leave period.
6. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any further details or comments, use the "note" field to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as an email or phone number.
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, text, required: false): Enter your start and end dates for the requested leave period. Ensure that these dates match the dates you need to be off from work.
* **Reason for Leave** (Reason for Leave, text, required: false): Briefly explain why you need to take overnight leave. Be as concise as possible.
* **Contact Method** (contact method, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted in case the HR or management team needs to reach you regarding your leave request. Options include: Phone, Email, or Message.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your leave period.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your leave period.
* **Contact Person** (contact person, text, required: false): If you are requesting leave for a specific individual or group, enter their name here.
* **Contact Person Email** (contact person email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone** (contact phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Leave Type** (Leave Type, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of leave you are requesting (Personal, Business, or Vacation).
* **Duration** (duration, number, required: false): Enter the length of your requested leave period.
* **Note** (note, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or details regarding your leave request.
