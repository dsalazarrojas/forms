# Motorsport Track Booking Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to request booking of a motorsport track for an event. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the date of your event from the calendar.
2. Enter the name of your team or organization.
3. Provide your contact email address so we can reach out to you.
4. Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up questions or confirmations.
5. Finally, enter the name of the facility you're interested in booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Request** (`track_booking_request`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the nature of your booking request (e.g., race event, training session, etc.).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date you're interested in booking the track for.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team or organization associated with this booking request.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with further questions or confirmations.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up questions or confirmations.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility (motorsport track) you're interested in booking.

## Tips
* Please ensure the date you select is available and not already booked.
* We'll check for duplicate requests on the same date, so make sure to check with us before submitting a new form if you've already sent a request for the same date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your booking request, don't hesitate to contact us.
