# Bank Fee Clarification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Bank Fee Clarification Request Form" is used to request clarification on bank fees that are unclear or disputed. If you are experiencing issues with a bank fee, this form is designed to help you provide the necessary information to investigate and potentially resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your account and the fee in question.
2. Select the correct reason for your inquiry and provide a detailed explanation of the issue.
3. Choose your preferred method of communication for the bank's response.
4. Review and acknowledge that an inquiry does not guarantee a refund or reversal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name
Full Name: This is the name on your account, as it appears on your bank statement.

*   **Full Name (Required)**: Provide your full name as it appears on your account.

### 2. Account Number
Account Number: This is the account number associated with the fee in question.

*   **Account Number (Required)**: Enter the account number where the fee was charged.

### 3. Primary Phone Number
Primary Phone Number: This is the phone number where we can contact you with updates on your inquiry.

*   **Primary Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your primary phone number.

### 4. Email Address
Email Address: This is the email address where we can contact you with updates on your inquiry.

*   **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address.

### 5. Fee Details Header
Fee Details Header: Please provide details about the fee in question.

*   This is a note section for you to provide additional details about the fee you are disputing.

### 6. Date of Fee Charge
Date of Fee Charge: Enter the date the fee was charged to your account.

*   **Date of Fee Charge (Required)**: Enter the date the fee was charged.

### 7. Exact Fee Amount
Exact Fee Amount: Enter the exact amount of the fee in question.

*   **Exact Fee Amount (Required)**: Enter the exact fee amount.

### 8. Fee Description on Statement
Fee Description on Statement: Enter the description of the fee as it appears on your statement.

*   **Fee Description on Statement (Required)**: Enter the fee description from your statement.

### 9. Inquiry Reason Header
Inquiry Reason Header: This is the reason for your inquiry.

*   This is a note section for you to read and understand the available options for your inquiry reason.

### 10. Why Are You Requesting Clarification?
Why Are You Requesting Clarification?: Select the reason why you are disputing the fee.

*   **Why Are You Requesting Clarification? (Required)**: Select the reason for your inquiry from the available options.

### 11. Detailed Explanation of Inquiry
Detailed Explanation of Inquiry: Provide additional context to help us investigate the issue.

*   **Detailed Explanation of Inquiry (Required)**: Provide any additional context that will help us understand your issue.

### 12. Requested Resolution
Requested Resolution: Select the outcome you are looking for with regards to the fee in question.

*   **Requested Resolution (Required)**: Select the outcome you are looking for from the available options.

### 13. Follow-up Preference
Follow-up Preference: How do you want us to follow up with you on your inquiry?

*   This is a note section for you to choose your preferred method of communication.

### 14. How Should We Contact You with the Result?
How Should We Contact You with the Result?: Choose your preferred method of communication for our response.

*   **How Should We Contact You with the Result? (Required)**: Choose your preferred method of communication.

### 15. Inquiry Submission Acknowledgement
Inquiry Submission Acknowledgement: Acknowledge that an inquiry does not guarantee a refund or reversal.

*   **Inquiry Submission Acknowledgement (Required)**: Acknowledge that an inquiry does not guarantee a refund or reversal.

### 15. Request Submission Date
Request Submission Date: The date and time the form was submitted.

*   **Request Submission Date (Required)**: This date and time will be automatically generated upon form submission.
