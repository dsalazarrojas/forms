# Hospital Patient Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients being discharged from a hospital. It ensures that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with the requested information. Make sure to enter your name and any other required fields (indicated by a *) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. patient_name (text):** Enter the name of the patient being released from the hospital.
* **2. date_of_birth (date):** Provide the patient's date of birth.
* **3. address (text):** Enter the patient's address.
* **4. hospital_name (text):** Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was being treated.
* **5. patient_contact_info (text):** Enter any relevant contact information for the patient.
* **6. medical_facility (text):** Enter the medical facility where the patient was being treated.
* **7. medical_staff (text):** Enter the name of the medical staff member in charge of the patient's care.
* **8. medical_conditions (text):** Describe the medical conditions or treatments the patient received while in the hospital.
* **9. patient_consent (text):** Check a box to indicate whether the patient has given consent for their release.
* **10. release_date (date):** Enter the date the patient is being released from the hospital.
* **11. medical_facts (note):** Provide any additional medical facts or notes about the patient's condition.
* **12. patient_signature (text):** Sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate and that you have been informed about your release.
* **13. medical_provider (text):** Enter the name of the medical provider responsible for the patient's care.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- If any field is marked as required, it must be filled out before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with hospital staff or seek additional guidance.
