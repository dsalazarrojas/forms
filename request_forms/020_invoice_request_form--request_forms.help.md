# Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Request Form is designed to collect essential information from clients for their invoice requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the **Client Contact Information** page.
2. Provide details about the order in the **Order Details** page.
3. Fill in the **Invoice Details** page with any relevant information.
4. Enter the **Delivery Address** of the client.
5. Choose a **Payment Method** for the invoice.
6. List the **Order Items** for the invoice.
7. Enter the **Total** amount for the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Contact Information** (`title`: Contact Information, `type`: text, optional): Enter the name, email, phone number, or any other relevant contact information of the person or company requesting the invoice.
* **Order Details** (`title`: Order Details, `type`: text, optional): Describe the order or service that the invoice is related to. This could be a project, product, or service.
* **Invoice Details** (`title`: Invoice Details, `type`: text, optional): Specify any specific details about the invoice, such as invoice date, invoice number, or any special requirements.
* **Delivery Address** (`title`: Delivery Address, `type`: text, optional): Enter the delivery address of the item or service requested.
* **Payment Method** (`title`: Payment Method, `type`: text, optional): Choose the payment method for the invoice (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Order Items** (`title`: Order Items, `type`: text, optional): List the items or services included in the order.
* **Total** (`title`: Total, `type`: text, optional): Enter the total amount for the invoice.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure that the invoice request is processed correctly.
* Use this form only for valid and relevant requests.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the support team for assistance.
