# Campus Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request event or meeting spaces on campus. It's essential to fill it out accurately and truthfully to ensure your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with your information and the event details.
*   Make sure to include all required fields and accurate information to help the event coordinator process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester Name** (`Requester Name`, `text`, required): Your full name as it appears on your official institutional ID.
*   **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Your institutional email address, used for communication and approval tracking.
*   **Department or Organization** (`department_or_org`, `text`, required): The department or organization hosting the event.
*   **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, required): A brief descriptive title of the event.
*   **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required): A brief overview of the event's purpose and activities.
*   **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): The date you would like to have the event or meeting take place.
*   **Alternative Date** (`alternative_date`, `date`, optional): An alternative date if the primary date is not available.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): The start time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 pm).
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): The end time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00 for 3:00 pm).
*   **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`, `number`, optional): Estimated number of attendees, if known.
*   **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, `text`, required): The building and room or outdoor location you've chosen for the event.
*   **Equipment Needs** (`equipment_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any equipment needed, such as microphones, projectors, or internet access.
*   **Food or Beverage Service** (`food_or_beverage`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you're planning food or beverages for the event.
*   **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Describe any accessibility, security, or other requirements for the event.
*   **Approver Name** (`approval_signature`, `text`, optional): The name of the department approver or admin signing off on this request.

## Tips

*   Please ensure the form is filled out correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you need assistance with filling out this form, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
