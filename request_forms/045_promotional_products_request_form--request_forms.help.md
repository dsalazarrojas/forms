# Promotional Products Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Promotional Products Request Form is designed to collect information from customers or companies requesting promotional products. This form will help the organization process these requests efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Enter the number of items being requested in the "Requested Quantity" field.
3. Select the desired color from the "Requested Color" dropdown list.
4. Select the desired size from the "Requested Size" dropdown list.
5. Select the desired style from the "Requested Style" dropdown list.
6. Select the desired finish from the "Requested Finish" dropdown list.
7. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
8. Enter the contact person's name in the "Company Contact Name" field.
9. Enter the company email address in the "Company Email" field.
10. Enter the company phone number in the "Company Phone" field.

12. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
