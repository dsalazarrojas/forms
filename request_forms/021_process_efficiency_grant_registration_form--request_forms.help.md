# Process Efficiency Grant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Efficiency Grant Registration Form is a crucial tool for grant applicants to register their grant applications with relevant details. This form helps streamline the grant registration process and ensures that all necessary information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Grant Applicant**: Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the grant application.
2.  **Grant Title**: Enter a brief title for the grant you are registering.
3.  **Grant Description**: Provide a detailed description of the grant you are applying for.
4.  **Grant Amount**: Enter the amount of the grant you are requesting.
5.  **Grant Start Date**: Enter the start date of the grant.
6.  **Grant End Date**: Enter the end date of the grant.
7.  **Grant Status**: Select the current status of the grant (Active or Inactive).
8.  **Grant Category**: Select the category of the grant you are registering (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
9.  **Grant Notes**: If you have any additional information or comments about the grant, enter it here.
10. **Email**: Enter your email address.
11. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
12.  **Address**: Enter your address.
13.  **City**: Enter the city where you are located.
14.  **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
15.  **Submitter**: Enter your name.
