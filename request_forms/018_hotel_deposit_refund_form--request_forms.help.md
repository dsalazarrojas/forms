# Hotel Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a guest's deposit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields and submit the form:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Deposit Refund** (`hotel_deposit_refund_form`):
	+ This field is required and should be filled out with the reason the guest's deposit is being refunded.
* **Name** (`name`):
	+ This is the name of the guest whose deposit is being refunded.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ Enter the email address of the guest whose deposit is being refunded.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ Enter the phone number of the guest whose deposit is being refunded.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason`):
	+ Enter the reason why the guest's deposit is being refunded.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`):
	+ Enter the amount of the deposit that is being refunded. If you are unsure, you can check the original deposit receipt.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`):
	+ Enter the total amount of the refund, including any interest or fees.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about the refund.
