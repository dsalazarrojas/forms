# Visual Effects Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to visual effects in a project. It captures essential details of the request, including the studio, project, reason for request, expected resolution date and time, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant studio from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the project(s) affected by the adjustment request.
3. Provide a brief reason for the request.
4. Specify the expected resolution date and time.
5. Enter any additional details regarding the adjustment request.
6. Choose the status of the request (New, In-Progress, or Done).
7. Optionally, add any comments or remarks related to the request.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up communication.
9. Specify who the request has been assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Studio**: Select the studio related to the adjustment request.
* **Project**: Choose the project(s) that will be affected by the adjustment request.
* **Reason For Request**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the adjustment request.
* **Expected Resolution Date**: Specify the expected resolution date for the request.
* **Expected Resolution Time**: Specify the expected resolution time for the request.
* **Adjustment Request Details**: Enter any additional details regarding the adjustment request.
* **Submit Request**: Choose the status of the request (Approve, Reject, or In-Progress).
* **Comment**: Enter any comments or remarks related to the request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Assigned To**: Specify who the request has been assigned to.
* **Status**: Specify the status of the request (New, In-Progress, or Done).
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the request.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the request was last updated.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the request.
* **Created At**: Enter the date and time the request was created.
