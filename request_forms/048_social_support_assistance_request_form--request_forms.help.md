# social_support_assistance_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social support assistance request form is designed to collect user information and mental health concerns to provide targeted support and resources.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your user details, including your name, and any other relevant information.
2.  If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, describe the circumstances of the crisis.
3.  Enter your contact number to allow us to reach out and provide support.
4.  Enter your email address to allow us to provide support and resources.
5.  Select the mental health issue you are experiencing from the options provided.
6.  Choose the services you are requesting for support.
7.  Provide any additional comments or information that may help us better understand your needs.
8.  Finally, confirm your agreement to the form collecting your data for support purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Please provide your full name and any other relevant user information.
*  **Mental Health Crisis**: If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, describe the circumstances of the crisis in this field.
*  **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number to allow us to reach out and provide support.
*  **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to provide support and resources.
*  **Mental Health Issue**: Select the mental health issue you are experiencing from the options provided.
*  **Requested Service**: Choose the services you are requesting for support.
*  **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that may help us better understand your needs.
*  **User Agreement**: Please confirm that you agree to this form collecting your data for support purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information to allow us to provide the best possible support. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
