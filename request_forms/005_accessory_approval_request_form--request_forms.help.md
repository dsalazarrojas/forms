To avoid duplication, please ensure that you only submit requests for items that are not already available in the office or that you have not already requested.

# Accessory Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accessory Approval Request Form is used to submit requests for accessories needed for your work. This form ensures that all requests are reviewed and approved by supervisors, guaranteeing that only necessary and justified requests are fulfilled.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, employee ID, and department.
2. Enter your email address and phone number (if available).
3. Select the accessory category and provide a brief description or name of the accessory.
4. Enter the quantity and estimated unit cost of the accessory.
5. Provide a brief explanation of why the accessory is needed for your work.
6. Select the urgency level of your request.
7. If applicable, provide the name of your preferred vendor or supplier.
8. Finally, provide any additional notes or information relevant to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester Information
* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official company records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department you belong to (e.g., Administration, Engineering, etc.). Please choose from the provided options (Administration, Engineering, Finance, Human Resources, IT, Marketing, Operations, Sales, Other).
### Accessory Information
* **Accessory Name or Description** (`accessory_name`): Provide a brief description or name of the accessory you are requesting, including the model if applicable.
* **Accessory Category** (`accessory_category`): Select the category of the accessory you are requesting (e.g., Computer peripherals, Office furniture, Safety equipment, etc.). Please choose from the provided options (Computer peripherals, Office furniture, Safety equipment, Uniforms and apparel, Tools and hardware, Other).
* **Quantity Requested** (`quantity`): Enter the number of units of the accessory you are requesting.
* **Estimated Unit Cost** (`estimated_unit_cost`): Enter the approximate cost per unit of the accessory in dollars.
* **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`): This field will be automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit cost you entered.
* **Business Justification** (`justification`): Provide a brief explanation of why this accessory is necessary for your work.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`): Select the urgency level of your request (Low - Within 30 days, Medium - Within 2 weeks, High - Within 1 week, Urgent - Immediate need).
* **Preferred Vendor or Supplier (if any)** (`preferred_vendor`): If you have a preferred vendor or supplier, enter their name here.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Provide any other information relevant to your request.
### Approval Information
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of the supervisor who will be approving your request.
* **Date of Request** (`request_date`): This field will be automatically populated with the current date.
