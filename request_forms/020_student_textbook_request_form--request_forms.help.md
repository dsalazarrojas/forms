# Student Textbook Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Textbook Request Form is a tool for teachers to request textbooks on behalf of their students. This form helps the library and other teams process textbook requests efficiently and ensure that students have the necessary materials for their studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the option that best represents who submitted the form (Form Submitted By).
2.  In the book request details field, provide a brief description of the requested textbook, including the title, author, and any other relevant information.
3.  If the textbook is requested, select "Yes" from the dropdown list.
4.  Choose the option that best represents who requested the textbook (Requested By).
5.  Select the option that best represents for whom the textbook is requested (Requested For).
6.  Enter the title of the textbook.
7.  Enter the author of the textbook.
8.  Enter the textbook's ISBN number.
9.  Enter the price of the textbook.
10. Enter the quantity of textbooks requested.
11. Enter the date the request was made.
12. Enter the date when the textbook is needed.
13. Enter any status updates about the request.
14. Enter your name as the requesting teacher.
15. Enter your email address.
16. Enter your phone number.
17. Enter the course name or number.
18. Enter the course name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submitted By (select_one)**: Please select the option that best represents who submitted the form (e.g., Teacher, Student, etc.).
* **book_request_details (text)**: Enter a brief description of the requested textbook.
* **textbook_requested (select_multiple)**: Indicate if the textbook is requested (yes/no).
* **Requested By (select_one)**: Choose the option that best represents who requested the textbook (e.g., Teacher, Student, etc.).
* **Requested For (select_one)**: Choose the option that best represents for whom the textbook is requested (e.g., Student, Class, etc.).
* **Textbook Title (text)**: Enter the title of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Author (text)**: Enter the author of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook ISBN (text)**: Enter the ISBN number of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Price (number)**: Enter the price of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Quantity (text)**: Enter the quantity of textbooks requested.
* **Date Of Request (date)**: Enter the date the request was made.
* **Requested Date (date)**: Enter the date when the textbook is needed.
* **Status (text)**: Enter any status updates about the request.
* **Teacher Name (text)**: Enter your name as the requesting teacher.
* **Teacher Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Teacher Course (text)**: Enter the course name or number.
* **Course (text)**: Enter the course name.

Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to facilitate smooth processing of your request.
