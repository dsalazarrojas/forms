# Telemedicine E Visit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine E-Visit Request Form is used to collect information from patients prior to a telemedicine visit. This form helps the healthcare provider prepare for the visit and ensures the patient receives the proper care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   First, fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
*   Enter your first name and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
*   Enter your email address in the Email field.
*   Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
*   Select the type of appointment you are requesting in the Appointment Type dropdown menu.
*   Choose a date and time for the appointment in the Date and Time fields.
*   Enter the reason for your visit in the Reason for Visit field.
*   If you have any relevant medical history, please enter it in the Medical History field.
*   Finally, select your insurance provider in the Insurance Provider field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (<font size="4">text</font>): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (<font size="4">text</font>): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (<font size="4">email</font>): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (<font size="4">text</font>): Enter your phone number.
*   **Appointment Type** (<font size="4">select_one</font>): Select the type of visit you are requesting:
    *   New Patient: This is your first visit with our healthcare provider.
    *   Follow-up Visit: You have seen this healthcare provider before and need to follow up on a previous visit.
    *   Check-in: This is an appointment to check in with your healthcare provider.
*   **Date** (<font size="4">date</font>): Enter the date of your appointment.
*   **Time** (<font size="4">time</font>): Enter the time of your appointment.
*   **Reason for Visit** (<font size="4">text</font>): Enter a brief description of why you are visiting.
*   **Medical History** (<font size="4">note</font>): Enter any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your visit.
*   **Insurance Provider** (<font size="4">text</font>): Enter your insurance provider's name and ID number (if applicable).
