# Company Credit Card Issuance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees requesting a company credit card for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Employee full name as it should appear on the card.
2. Enter your Internal Employee ID (if available or leave blank if not required).
3. Select the department that is requesting the card.
4. Choose the type of card you need:
	* Corporate card: for general business use
	* Procurement card: for procurement purposes only
	* Travel card: for travel and expense purposes
5. Suggest a proposed spending limit for the card.
6. Provide a brief explanation for the card issuance (why the card is needed).
7. Enter your Manager's name for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee full name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on the card.
* **Internal Employee ID**: Enter your internal employee ID (if available). If not required, leave this blank.
* **Department**: Select the department requesting the card.
* **Card type requested**: Choose the type of card that best fits your business needs (Corporate, Procurement, or Travel).
* **Requested monthly limit**: Suggest a proposed spending limit for the card.
* **Justification for card issuance**: Provide a brief explanation for the card issuance (why the card is needed).
* **Manager name for approval**: Enter the name of your manager who will approve the card.

## Tips

* Only submit one request per form.
* Ensure that your manager's name is accurate and up-to-date.
* Provide clear and concise justification for card issuance to ensure prompt approval.
