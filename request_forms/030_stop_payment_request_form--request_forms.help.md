# Stop Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stop Payment Request Form is designed to be filled out by account holders to request the cancellation or reversal of a payment that has been already processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill out the required fields, marked with **<mark>**. The form has the following fields that are marked as required:
	+ **Request Status**: Select the current status of your request.
* Fill out any other fields that you feel necessary to explain your request.
* Review your completed form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check Number** (`check_number`, text, <optional>): This field is intended to capture a unique identifier for the check being requested to be cancelled.
* **Account Holder Name** (`account_holder_name`, text, <optional>): This field is for your account holder name.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, <optional>): This field is for your account number.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, <optional>): This field is for the reason why you are requesting the stop payment.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, <optional>): This field is for the name of your bank.
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, <optional>): This field is for the date when you are requesting the stop payment.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, <required>): Select the current status of your request (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, <optional>): This field is for any additional comments you may want to add to your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, text, <optional>): This field is not currently in use. Please do not fill it out.
