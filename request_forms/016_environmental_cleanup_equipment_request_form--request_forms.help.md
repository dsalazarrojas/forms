# environmental_cleanup_equipment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request environmental cleanup equipment for various types of equipment, including water pumps, filtration systems, and purification systems. Fill out the form to provide necessary information for your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your form ID (if you have one) in the `Form ID` field. This is an optional field.
2. Select the type of equipment you are requesting from the `Equipment Type` dropdown list.
3. Enter the delivery date you would like the equipment to be delivered by.
4. Provide your contact details in the `Contact Details` field, including any relevant information about your organization, name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Optionally, provide the name and details of the volunteer group or organization you are working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID**: This is an optional field to help us track your request. If you have a specific form ID, please enter it here.
* **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment you are requesting from the dropdown list. This will help us determine the correct equipment and prioritize your request.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like the equipment to be delivered by. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Details**: Provide your organization's details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for this request.
* **Contact Organization**: Enter your organization's name as the contact organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **Volunteer Group**: Optionally, provide the name and details of the volunteer group or organization you are working with.
* **Volunteer Organization**: Optionally, provide the name of the organization the volunteer group is affiliated with.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally, provide the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Form Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
