# Designer Handbag Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or donors who wish to request a designer handbag donation. We kindly request that you fill out the form accurately and honestly, as your details will be reviewed internally for eligibility and authenticity. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understood the form's purpose and guidelines.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Make sure you have attached all required documents or uploaded relevant photos and files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (<contact_info>): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Donor Details** (<donor_details>): Provide a brief description of why you are requesting a designer handbag donation.
* **Request Info** (<request_info>): Describe the specific handbag style or model you are interested in.
* **Photo Upload** (<photo_upload>): If applicable, upload a photo of the handbag you are requesting.
* **Upload Document** (<upload_document>): Upload any supporting documents required for your request.
* **Submit** (<submit_button>): Review and submit your request.
* **Contact Phone** (<contact_phone>): Enter your primary phone number for communication.
* **Contact Email** (<contact_email>): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Alternative Phone** (<contact_phone_alt>): Enter any alternative phone number for communication.
* **Upload Image** (<upload_image>): Upload any relevant images to support your request.
* **Upload Document** (<upload_document_alt>): Upload any alternative supporting documents.
* **Request Date** (<request_date>): Select the date you are requesting the handbag.
* **Request Time** (<request_time>): Select the time you are requesting the handbag.
* **Select One** (<select_one>): Choose one option from the select options.
* **Select Multiple** (<select_multiple>): Choose multiple options from the select options.
* **Designer Select** (<designer_select>): Choose one designer option from the provided list.
* **Designer Select Multiple** (<designer_select_multiple>): Choose multiple designer options.
* **Designer Date Select** (<designer_date_select>): Choose one designer date option.
* **Designer Date Select Multiple** (<designer_date_select_multiple>): Choose multiple designer date options.
* **Designer Time Select** (<designer_time_select>): Choose one designer time option.
* **Designer Time Select Multiple** (<designer_time_select_multiple>): Choose multiple designer time options.
* **Designer Date and Time Select** (<designer_date_and_time_select>): Choose one designer date and time option.
* **Designer Date and Time Select Multiple** (<designer_date_and_time_select_multiple>): Choose multiple designer date and time options.
* **Designer Integer Select** (<designer_integer_select>): Choose one designer integer option.
* **Designer Integer Select Multiple** (<designer_integer_select_multiple>): Choose multiple designer integer options.
* **Designer Integer Select 3** (<designer_integer_select_3>): Choose one designer integer option 3.
* **Designer Integer Select Multiple 3** (<designer_integer_select_3_multiple>): Choose multiple designer integer options 3.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional" but it's best to fill out as much information as possible to ensure a smooth review and approval process. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for clarification. 

Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly, and that you have attached all required documents or uploaded relevant photos and files. This will greatly assist our review and approval process. Thank you!
