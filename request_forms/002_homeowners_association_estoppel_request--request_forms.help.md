# Homeowners Association Estoppel Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for an estoppel certificate for your property. The purpose of this form is to collect information needed to verify your identity and property details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Home Owner Details** section.
2. Select the estoppel certificate type you are requesting in the **Estoppel Certificate Request** section.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method in the **Delivery Preferences** section.
4. Select the authorities who have signed the authorization in the **Signed Authorization** section.
5. Enter your property details in the **Property Details** section.
6. Enter your delivery address in the **Delivery Address** section.
7. Provide the property manager's details in the **Property Manager Details** section.
8. Enter the buyer's details in the **Buyer Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Owner Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as the home owner.
* **Estoppel Certificate Request** (`text`, required: false): Select the type of estoppel certificate you are requesting (e.g., 'Estoppel Certificate 1', 'Estoppel Certificate 2', etc.).
* **Delivery Preferences** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to receive your estoppel certificate (e.g., 'Mail', 'Email', etc.).
* **Signed Authorization** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the authorities who have signed the authorization (e.g., 'Authorization 1', 'Authorization 2', etc.).
* **Property Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your property.
* **Delivery Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to receive your estoppel certificate.
* **Property Manager Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter the property manager's name and contact information.
* **Buyer Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter the buyer's name and contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
- If you are requesting an estoppel certificate for a property that is already in the process of obtaining one, your request will be rejected.
- You can modify the form as needed, but please ensure that the changes do not affect its functionality or compatibility.
