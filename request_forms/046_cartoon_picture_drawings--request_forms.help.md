# Cartoon Picture Drawings - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to create a custom cartoon picture drawing. Customers will be asked to provide their contact details, preferences for the drawing style and background, number of subjects, and delivery options, including payment method and currency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the artist can create a high-quality drawing.
2. Make sure to select the correct drawing style, background, and number of subjects to match your request.
3. Upload reference photos as required to help the artist get the likeness correct.
4. Choose your preferred delivery method and currency to complete the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where your digital proofs will be sent.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address if you are ordering a physical print.
* **Preferred Cartoon Style** (`drawing_style`, `select_one`, required): Choose the style of the cartoon from the options provided.
	+ Classic Disney
	+ Superhero or Comic
	+ Caricature
	+ Anime or Manga
	+ Minimalist or Flat
	+ Hand-Drawn Sketch
* **Number of People or Pets in Drawing** (`number_of_people`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of subjects to be illustrated.
* **Background Preference** (`background_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose the background preference from the options provided.
	+ Solid Color
	+ Transparent
	+ Scenic or Themed
	+ From Photo
* **Any Special Elements or Props to Include?** (`special_elements`, `text`, optional): Enter any special elements or props you would like to include in the drawing.
* **How Many Reference Photos Will You Provide?** (`photo_upload_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of reference photos you will provide.
* **Description of the Primary Photo** (`main_reference_desc`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the primary photo to help the artist get the likeness correct.
* **Specific Notes on Facial Features or Expressions** (`facial_features_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific notes on facial features or expressions.
* **Final Product Format** (`delivery_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the final product format from the options provided.
	+ Digital Only (High Res)
	+ Digital and Physical Print
	+ Canvas Wrap
	+ T-Shirt
	+ Coffee Mug
* **Physical Print Size (if Applicable)** (`print_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the physical print size from the options provided.
	+ 5x7
	+ 8x10
	+ 11x14
	+ 16x20
	+ N/A
* **Delivery Urgency** (`delivery_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the delivery urgency from the options provided.
	+ Standard (7 to 10 days)
	+ Rush (2 to 3 days)
	+ Next Day (Express Fee)
* **Estimated Total** (`estimated_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated total cost for your reference.
* **Currency** (`currency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the currency for the transaction from the options provided.
	+ USD
	+ EUR
	+ GBP
	+ AUD
	+ CAD
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred payment method from the options provided.
	+ PayPal
	+ Credit Card
	+ Venmo
	+ Apple Pay
* **I Understand this is for Personal Use Only** (`usage_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the usage terms for the drawing.
* **Digital Signature for Order Confirmation** (`digital_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a digital signature for order confirmation.

Note: The form is designed to collect information for a custom cartoon picture drawing service, and the fields are designed to ensure that the artist can create an accurate and satisfactory drawing.
