# Deal Memo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deal Memo Submission form is used to collect and record the essential details of a business deal or agreement. The form ensures that the submission is accurate, complete, and represents a true and accurate representation of the deal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Carefully review each field to ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
- Fill in the deal details accurately and completely.
- Ensure that all information submitted is true and accurate.
- Confirm that you have not altered the deal details in any way.
- Select the correct acknowledgement options to indicate your agreement that the deal details are accurate.
- Enter the correct submission date and your employee ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Deal Details** (`deal_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the essential details of the deal or agreement. Please fill this in accurately and completely.
- **Deal Terms** (`deal_terms`, select multiple, required): Select all the terms that apply to the deal.
- **Deal Acknowledgments** (`deal_acknowledgments`, select one, required): Choose one of the options to confirm that you have not altered the deal details in any way and that all information is accurate.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are submitting the deal memo.
- **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number to identify yourself as the person submitting the deal memo.

## Tips
- Be precise and accurate when filling in the deal details.
- Double-check the deal terms and acknowledge your understanding of them.
- Ensure that all information submitted is true and accurate.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the relevant department for guidance.
