# Car Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Car Request Form is designed to gather information for vehicle requests, including fleet assignments, rentals, and test drives. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure prompt processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Information** (`id: 1`)
	+ This field is a note section for you to provide your contact details.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`)
	+ A required field for your first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`)
	+ A required field for your last name.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`)
	+ A required field for your email address, used for request communications.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`)
	+ A required field for your phone number.
* **Department** (`id: 6`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your department name.
* **Employee ID** (`id: 7`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your employee ID.
* **Company Name** (`id: 8`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your company name.
* **Request Details** (`id: 9`)
	+ A note section for you to provide vehicle request information.
* **Request Type** (`id: 10`)
	+ A required field to select the type of vehicle request (e.g., fleet assignment, rental reservation, test drive).
* **Request Date** (`id: 11`)
	+ A required field for the current date.
* **Needed By Date** (`id: 12`)
	+ A required field for the date you need the vehicle.
* **Return Date** (`id: 13`)
	+ If applicable, please provide the return date of the vehicle.
* **Duration** (`id: 14`)
	+ A required field to select the duration of the vehicle request.
* **Vehicle Preferences** (`id: 15`)
	+ A note section for you to provide vehicle preferences.
* **Vehicle Category** (`id: 16`)
	+ A required field to select the type of vehicle category (e.g., economy, compact, mid-size).
* **Specific Vehicle** (`id: 17`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your specific vehicle preference.
* **Transmission Type** (`id: 18`)
	+ A required field to select the transmission type (e.g., automatic, manual).
* **Fuel Type** (`id: 19`)
	+ A required field to select the fuel type (e.g., gasoline, diesel, hybrid).
* **Passenger Capacity** (`id: 20`)
	+ A required field to select the number of passengers.
* **Luggage Capacity** (`id: 21`)
	+ A required field to select the luggage capacity.
* **Required Features** (`id: 22`)
	+ A select multiple field for must-have vehicle features (e.g., GPS navigation, Bluetooth).
* **Additional Services** (`id: 23`)
	+ A select multiple field for any additional services required (e.g., GPS navigation rental, child safety seat).
* **Purpose of Request** (`id: 24`)
	+ A required field to select the purpose of the vehicle request (e.g., business travel, client meetings).
* **Purpose Details** (`id: 25`)
	+ A note section for you to elaborate on the purpose of the request.
* **Travel Destinations** (`id: 26`)
	+ A note section for you to provide travel destinations.
* **Estimated Mileage** (`id: 27`)
	+ A select field for the estimated mileage of the vehicle.
* **Driver Information** (`id: 28`)
	+ A note section for you to provide driver information.
* **Driver's License Number** (`id: 29`)
	+ A required field for your driver's license number.
* **License State** (`id: 30`)
	+ A required field for the state that issued your license.
* **License Expiration** (`id: 31`)
	+ A required field for the expiration date of your license.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 32`)
	+ A required field for your date of birth.
* **Driving Record** (`id: 33`)
	+ A required field to select your driving record (e.g., clean, minor violations, major violations).
* **Additional Drivers** (`id: 34`)
	+ A select field for whether there will be other drivers.
* **Additional Driver Names** (`id: 35`)
	+ A note section for you to list additional driver names.
* **Pickup Preferences** (`id: 36`)
	+ A note section for you to provide pickup information.
* **Pickup Location** (`id: 37`)
	+ A required field to select the pickup location (e.g., main office, airport location).
* **Pickup Address** (`id: 38`)
	+ A note section for you to provide the specific address for pickup.
* **Pickup Time Preference** (`id: 39`)
	+ A note section for you to specify your preferred pickup time.
* **Payment Information** (`id: 40`)
	+ A note section for you to provide payment details.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 41`)
	+ A required field to select the payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card).
* **Billing Department** (`id: 42`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your billing department name.
* **Cost Center** (`id: 43`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your cost center.
* **Authorization** (`id: 44`)
	+ A note section for you to provide approval information.
* **Manager Approval** (`id: 45`)
	+ A required field to select whether manager approval is obtained (e.g., yes, pending).
* **Manager Name** (`id: 46`)
	+ If applicable, please provide your manager name.
* **Special Requests** (`id: 47`)
	+ A note section for you to provide any special requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 48`)
	+ A note section for you to provide any other information.
* **Terms Agreement** (`id: 49`)
	+ A required field to select whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submission Date** (`id: 50`)
	+ A required field for the current date.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
* Ensure you provide all required fields and information for a prompt processing of your vehicle request.
