# Digital Experience Enhancement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who want to request enhancements to their digital experience on a specific platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the digital experience you are referring to (Field 1: Description).
2. Specify your application's name (Field 2: Application Name).
3. Choose the priority level for your request (Field 3: Priority).
	* If you have an urgent need, select High.
	* If it's a moderate priority request, select Medium.
	* If it's less urgent, select Low.
4. Enter the date by which you would like your request to be fulfilled (Field 4: Deadline).
5. If applicable, provide the expected completion date (Field 5: Expected Completion Date).
6. Select the team member who will be handling your request (Field 6: Team Member).
7. Add any additional comments or information about your request (Field 7: Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Name**: This is where you enter the name of the digital experience you are referring to (e.g., 'New Feature X').
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the digital experience you are referring to (e.g., 'Enhance user interface').
* **Priority**: Choose the priority level for your request:
	+ High: Very urgent or time-sensitive.
	+ Medium: Moderately urgent or time-sensitive.
	+ Low: Less urgent or not time-sensitive.
* **Deadline**: Enter the date by which you would like your request to be fulfilled.
* **Expected Completion Date**: If applicable, provide the expected completion date for the request.
* **Team Member**: Select the team member who will be handling your request.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the description field to ensure accurate handling of your request.
* If you have a specific team member in mind, select them from the options provided in the Team Member field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the responsible team member or manager for guidance.
* Please note that this form is used for digital experience enhancements, not for general feedback or support requests.
