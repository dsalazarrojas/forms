# FOIA Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FOIA Request Form Template is used to collect information from individuals or organizations making a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request. This form is essential for our organization to respond to your request accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information.
2. Provide a clear and concise explanation of the purpose of your request.
3. If applicable, specify the date of your request and the specific information you are requesting.
4. Indicate whether any files are attached to your request.
5. Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., letter, email, phone, in-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter its name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birthday`, date, optional): If you would like to provide your date of birth, please do so.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address to help us contact you efficiently.
* **Purpose of request** (`purpose_of_request`, text, required): Clearly explain the purpose of your request.
* **Date of request** (`date_of_request`, date, optional): If you know the date you made your request, please enter it.
* **Specific information requested** (`specific_info_requested`, text, optional): If you have specific information you would like to request, please describe it.
* **Files attached** (`files_attached`, note, optional): If you have attached any files to your request, please describe them briefly.
* **Files requested** (`files_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate if any files are requested.
* **Contact method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (letter, email, phone, in-person).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your request and respond according to our policies and procedures.
