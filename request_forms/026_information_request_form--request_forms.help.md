# information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from users. It is intended to be used for requests, inquiries, or other general purposes where minimal details are required. Please note that this form is not designed for complex or sensitive topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form purpose and ensure you have a good understanding of what information you need to provide.
2. Fill in the **Basic Information** section with your personal details. This section is not required, so feel free to leave it blank if not necessary.
3. Enter your **email** (if you have one) in the corresponding field.
4. Provide your **Contact Name** in the designated field.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Information**: This section allows you to provide your basic contact details. It's not required, but it helps us understand who you are better.
* **email**: If you have an email address, please enter it here. This will help us contact you, if needed.
* **Contact Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please enter it here. This will allow us to contact you more easily.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is entered correctly to facilitate communication.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
