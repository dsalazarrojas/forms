# CRM Setup Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users in setting up their Customer Relationship Management (CRM) environment correctly. It helps us understand your CRM requirements and ensure that we configure your setup effectively. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, making sure to provide accurate information about your CRM setup.
2. Please answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Use the provided hints to help you complete each field correctly.
4. Review your completed form to ensure that it accurately reflects your CRM setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Project Stakeholder** (`id: 2`): Enter the name and contact information of the primary person responsible for this CRM project.
* **Business Domain** (`id: 3`): Specify the business domain or department that this CRM is for (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Support).
* **Fiscal Year Start Month** (`id: 5`): Select the month when your fiscal year starts (used for sales forecasting).
* **Primary Business Currency** (`id: 6`): Choose the currency used in your business (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP).
* **Core Lead Sources** (`id: 7`): List common ways you acquire leads (e.g., trade shows, referrals, online advertising).
* **Sales Pipeline Stages** (`id: 8`): Define the stages of your sales pipeline (e.g., Qualification, Discovery, Proposal, Negotiation).
* **Enable Automated Lead Notifications** (`id: 10`): Choose whether to enable automated lead notifications.
* **Applicable Data Regulations** (`id: 12`): Select the data regulations that apply to your business (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA).
* **Data Retention Period** (`id: 13`): Specify the data retention period in years (if not required, this field is optional).
* **Targeted Discovery Completion Date** (`id: 15`): Enter the date when you plan to complete the discovery phase.
* **Expected Go-Live Date** (`id: 16`): Enter the date when you expect to go live with your CRM.
* **Additional Setup Instructions** (`id: 17`): Add any additional setup instructions or requirements not covered in this form.
