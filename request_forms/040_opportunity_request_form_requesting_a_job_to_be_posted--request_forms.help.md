# opportunity_request_form_requesting_a_job_to_be_posted - Help Guide
## Purpose
The opportunity request form is a crucial tool for requesting a job to be posted on the internal job board. This form helps to centralize job postings, making it easier for employees and managers to find and apply to job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job description, which should give a brief overview of the job's responsibilities and tasks.
2. Provide the job requirements, including skills, qualifications, and experience needed for the position.
3. Enter the salary range for the job, including the minimum and maximum salary.
4. Select the employment type, which can be full-time, part-time, or contract.
5. Enter the work hours, which should be accurate and up-to-date.
6. Choose the benefits offered by the company, such as medical, dental, and vision insurance.
7. Select a job posting date for the position.
8. Set the job status as "open" to indicate that the job is available for application.
9. Enter the contact name, email, and phone number of the person responsible for the job posting.
10. Enter the number of job openings available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Description** (`Job Description`): A brief summary of the job's responsibilities and tasks. This field is required.
* **Job Requirements** (`Job Requirements`): A detailed list of the skills, qualifications, and experience needed for the position. This field is required.
* **Salary Range** (`Salary Range`): The minimum and maximum salary for the job. This field is required.
* **Employment Type** (`Employment Type`): The type of employment, which can be full-time, part-time, or contract. This field is required.
* **Work Hours** (`Work Hours`): The number of hours the employee is expected to work. This field is required.
* **Benefits** (`Benefits`): A list of benefits offered by the company, such as medical, dental, and vision insurance. This field is required.
* **Job Posting** (`Job Posting`): The date when the job is posted. This field is required.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`): The status of the job, which can be "open" or "close". This field is required.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`): The name of the person responsible for the job posting. This field is required.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`): The email of the person responsible for the job posting. This field is required.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`): The phone number of the person responsible for the job posting. This field is required.
* **Job Openings** (`Job Openings`): The number of job openings available. This field is required.
* **Job Openings Per Year** (`Job Openings Per Year`): An optional field to provide more information about job openings.
* **Job Opening** (`Job Opening`): An optional field to provide more details about the job opening.
* **Notes** (`Notes`): An optional field for any additional comments or information.
