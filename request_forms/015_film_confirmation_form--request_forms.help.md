# film_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a film submission. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your film is properly documented and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the next field.
3. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for contact.
5. Type in the **Film Title** of the submission.
6. If applicable, enter the **Festival Name** where the film was submitted.
7. Choose the **Genre** of the film (if not specified, you can leave this field blank).
8. Enter the **Submission Date** of the film (YYYY-MM-DD format).
9. Enter the **Submission Time** of the film (HH:MM format).
10. Provide a brief **Synopsis** of the film (optional).
11. Enter your **Contact** information for any follow-up.
12. If you're submitting a film with a specified **Film Length**, please enter the duration in minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Film Title**: Type in the title of the film.
* **Festival Name**: Enter the name of the festival where the film was submitted (if applicable).
* **Genre**: Choose the genre of the film (if specified).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the film submission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of the film submission in HH:MM format.
* **Synopsis**: Provide a brief summary of the film (optional).
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information for any follow-up.
* **Film Length**: If applicable, enter the length of the film in minutes.
