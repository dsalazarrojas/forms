# ประก FM Transmitter License Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "ประก FM Transmitter License Inquiry Form" is used to collect information from customers who are inquiring about an FM transmitter license. This form is designed to gather essential details about the customer, including their name, contact information, and license-related data.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field. Please use a valid email format.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Enter your address in the `address` field.
6. Enter the license number related to your inquiry in the `license_number` field.
7. Describe the supporting documents you would like to submit, if any, in the `supporting_documents` field.
8. Provide any additional technical details relevant to your inquiry in the `technical_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to which you would like to receive communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address, which may be used for correspondence or verification purposes.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, `text`, required): Enter the license number associated with your inquiry.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required): Describe the supporting documents you would like to submit, if any.
* **Technical Details** (`technical_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional technical details relevant to your inquiry.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are submitting supporting documents, please make sure they are clear and relevant to your inquiry.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for further assistance.
