# Piercing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Request Form is used to gather information about a client's piercing request, including their contact information, piercing preferences, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the **Client Details** field.
2. Select the specific piercings you are interested in getting from the **Selected Piercings** dropdown list.
3. Choose the body part where the piercing will be done from the **Body Part** dropdown list.
4. Indicate your pain level, from 1-5 or 6-10, in the **Pain Level** field.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field (optional).
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
8. Select a submission date for your piercing request from the calendar.
9. Indicate when you plan to complete the form in the **Form Completion** field.
10. Lastly, provide your name in the **Submitted By** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Text): Enter your name and contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you about your piercing request.
* **Selected Piercings** (Select Multiple): Choose the specific piercings you are interested in getting, selecting all that apply from the options:
	+ Eyelid
	+ Navel
	+ Other
* **Body Part** (Select One): Choose the body part where the piercing will be done from the options:
	+ Navel
	+ Back
	+ Other
* **Pain Level** (Select One): Indicate your pain level, from 1-5 or 6-10.
* **Note** (Note): Provide any additional comments or notes about your piercing request.
* **Email** (Text): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your piercing request.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Submission Date** (Date): Select a submission date for your piercing request.
* **Form Completion** (Time): Indicate when you plan to complete the form.
* **Submitted By** (Text): Provide your name, or optionally, the name of the person submitting the form on your behalf.
