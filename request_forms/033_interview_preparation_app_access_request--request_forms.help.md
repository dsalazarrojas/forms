# Interview Preparation App Access Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for access to the Interview Preparation App. This includes information about the user's name, title, and reason for request, as well as interview details and contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user name in the "User Name" field.
2. Select your user title from the "User Title" dropdown menu.
3. Select the type of request you are submitting (e.g. "Interview Prep App Access").
4. Enter the date of the interview you are requesting access for.
5. Enter the time of the interview you are requesting access for.
6. Select the location of the interview you are requesting access for.
7. Select the reason for your request from the "Reason for Request" dropdown menu.
8. Select the contact method you prefer (e.g. "Phone" or "Email").
9. Enter any additional details about your request in the "Additional Details" field.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" from the "User Agreement" dropdown menu.
11. If desired, select the support team(s) you would like to be contacted for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your user name.
* **User Title** (`user_title`, `select_one`, optional): Select your user title from the dropdown menu.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of request you are submitting.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the interview you are requesting access for.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the interview you are requesting access for.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the interview you are requesting access for.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the interview you are requesting access for.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reason for your request from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the contact method you prefer (e.g. "Phone" or "Email").
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about your request.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions".
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the support team(s) you would like to be contacted for review.
* **Other Support Team** (`other_support_team`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the other support team you would like to be contacted.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter comments for review.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, optional): The status of your request will be displayed here.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): The support team(s) that reviewed your request will be displayed here.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): The date and time your request was reviewed will be displayed here.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, optional): The time your request was reviewed will be displayed here.
* **Reviewed By Title** (`reviewed_by_title`, `select_one`, optional): The title of the support team that reviewed your request will be displayed here.

Tips:

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting your request.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
