# Manufacturing Line Shutdown Notice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Line Shutdown Notice Request Form is designed to notify stakeholders of production line shutdowns, whether planned or unplanned. This form is intended for production planners, production line managers, and facility managers to report manufacturing line shutdowns, ensuring that all necessary parties are informed and involved in the shutdown process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the production line from the options provided.
2. Enter the date and time when the production line will start and end being shut down.
3. Choose whether the shutdown is planned or unplanned.
4. Enter your name, title, and contact information as the representative submitting the request.
5. Enter the production planner's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
6. Enter the department and facility information associated with the shutdown.
7. Review and submit the form to notify stakeholders of the production line shutdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **production_line** (text): Enter the production line being shut down.
* **start_date** (date): Enter the date and time when the production line will start being shut down.
* **end_date** (date): Enter the date and time when the production line will end being shut down.
* **notice_type** (select_one): Select "planned" if the shutdown is previously scheduled and "unplanned" if it's an unexpected stop.
* **production_line_manager** (text): Enter the production line manager's name.
* **reason** (text): Enter the reason for the shutdown.
* **manager_signature** (select_one): Select the manager's signature for the shutdown notice.
* **manager_name** (text): Enter the manager's name.
* **manager_phone** (text): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **manager_email** (email): Enter the manager's email address.
* **production_planner** (text): Enter the production planner's name.
* **production_planner_name** (text): Enter the production planner's name.
* **production_planner_phone** (text): Enter the production planner's phone number.
* **production_planner_email** (email): Enter the production planner's email address.
* **department** (text): Enter the department associated with the shutdown.
* **department_name** (text): Enter the department's name.
* **department_headquarters** (text): Enter the department's headquarters address.
* **facility** (text): Enter the facility associated with the shutdown.
* **facility_name** (text): Enter the facility's name.
* **facility_shift** (text): Enter the facility's shift information.
* **facility_manager** (text): Enter the facility manager's name.
* **facility_manager_name** (text): Enter the facility manager's name.
* **facility_manager_phone** (text): Enter the facility manager's phone number.
* **facility_manager_email** (email): Enter the facility manager's email address.
