# Contact Tag Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Tag Management Form is used to request changes to contact tags or segmentation within the system. This process is essential for maintaining data accuracy and facilitating targeted marketing or sales efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields to ensure we can contact you regarding your request.
2. Select the system or tool affected by the tag change.
3. Provide the contact identifier (e.g., email or ID).
4. List the tags you want to apply to the contact.
5. If applicable, list the tags you want to remove.
6. Briefly explain the reason for the change.
7. Specify the effective date when you want the tag change to take effect.
8. Check if managerial approval is required for the changes.
9. Provide any additional instructions that might be helpful for implementing the change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name** (Requester name): Enter your name to associate this request with you.
* **Requester Email** (Requester email): Enter your email address for follow-up and confirmation purposes.
* **System or Tool** (System or tool): Choose the system or tool where the contact is located (e.g., CRM or marketing platform).
* **Contact Identifier** (Contact identifier): Enter the email or ID of the contact to be tagged or segmented.
* **Tags to Apply** (Tags to apply): List the tags you want to assign to the contact.
* **Tags to Remove** (Tags to remove): If applicable, list the tags you want to remove from the contact.
* **Reason for Change** (Reason for change): Provide a brief explanation of why the tag change is necessary.
* **Effective Date** (Effective date for change): Choose the date when you want the tag change to take effect.
* **Approval Needed from Manager** (Approval needed from manager): If required, check if managerial approval is needed for this change.
* **Additional Instructions** (Additional instructions): Provide any extra details that might help implement the change successfully.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the system or tool documentation or contact your supervisor for guidance.
- If managerial approval is required, ensure to submit your request well in advance of the desired effective date. 
- Double-check the accuracy of your request before submission, as changes may be irreversible.
