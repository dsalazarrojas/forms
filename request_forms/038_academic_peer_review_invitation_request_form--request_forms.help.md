# Academic Peer Review Invitation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Review Invitation Request Form is used to request peer review invitations for a manuscript submission. This form helps gather necessary information from the requester to facilitate the peer review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in your name and email in the corresponding fields to ensure that you can be contacted for follow-up discussions.
2.  Provide the working title of the manuscript in the "Manuscript title" field.
3.  Enter a brief abstract or summary of the manuscript in the "Manuscript abstract" field.
4.  Indicate the key topics or methods required for the reviewer's expertise in the "Reviewer expertise required" field.
5.  Select the preferred timeline for the review process in the "Preferred review timeline" field.
6.  Disclose any conflicts of interest or affiliations in the "Conflicts of interest" field.
7.  Consent to being contacted directly by the reviewers in the "Consent to contact suggested reviewers" field.
8.  Enter any additional relevant information in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the editor or author requesting review.
*   **Requester email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Provide your contact email for follow-up discussions.
*   **Manuscript title** (`manuscript_title`, text, required): Input the working title of the manuscript submission.
*   **Manuscript abstract** (`manuscript_abstract`, text, optional): Enter a brief abstract or summary of the manuscript, up to 300 words.
*   **Reviewer expertise required** (`suggested_reviewer_expertise`, select multiple, required): Indicate which topics or methods are required for the reviewer's expertise (e.g., Quantitative methods, Qualitative methods, Theoretical analysis, Pedagogy, Policy analysis).
*   **Preferred review timeline** (`preferred_timeline`, select one, required): Select the typical turnaround time for the review process in weeks (e.g., 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 6 weeks).
*   **Conflicts of interest** (`conflicts_of_interest`, text, optional): List any affiliations or relationships that may be relevant to the review process.
*   **Consent to contact suggested reviewers** (`consent_for_contact`, select one, required): Choose whether or not to consent to being contacted directly by the suggested reviewers.
*   **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the manuscript or the review process.
