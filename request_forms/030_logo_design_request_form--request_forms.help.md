# Logo Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for logo design services for your business or individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of requestor (business or individual)
2. Select the type of logo you are looking for (2D, 3D, Vector, or Monogram)
3. Provide your company's budget for the logo design
4. Enter your contact information (email and date)
5. Optionally, provide additional information about your business or project

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, optional): Please enter your name to be associated with the logo design request.
* **business type** (select_one, required): Choose between Individual or Business to determine the type of requestor.
* **industry** (select_multiple, optional): Select the industry related to your business (e.g., E-commerce, Finance, Health, Marketing, Non-profit, Technology).
* **budget** (number, optional): Enter your budget for the logo design project.
* **logo type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of logo you are looking for (2D, 3D, Vector, or Monogram).
* **description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your logo design needs.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further discussion.
* **phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **upload logo file** (text, optional): Upload any existing logo file(s) you have for reference.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date you need the logo design project completed by.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time you would like the project completed by.
* **note** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or instructions for the designer.
* **additional info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your business or project.
* **business info** (text, optional): Enter any relevant business information (e.g., company description, mission statement).
* **location** (text, optional): Enter your business location.
* **website** (text, optional): Enter your business website.
