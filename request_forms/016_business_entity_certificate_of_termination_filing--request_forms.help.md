# Business Entity Certificate Of Termination Filing - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to file a Certificate of Termination for a business entity. It is a document that notifies the state or government of the termination or dissolution of a business entity. This form is used to ensure that all legal obligations have been met before the business ceases operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the entity information section with the business entity's registered name, DBA name (if applicable), and entity type.
2. Enter the date of incorporation/formation and registration number for the business entity.
3. Enter the principal address of the business entity.
4. In the termination details section, enter the effective termination date and choose the reason for termination.
5. Enter any additional termination details if necessary.
6. In the authorization section, enter the name, title, and contact information of the person filing the termination.
7. Confirm that all legal obligations have been met.
8. Date and sign the form to complete the filing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Entity Name** (`entity_legal_name`): Enter the registered name of the business entity.
* **DBA Name (if applicable)** (`dba_name`): Enter the operating name of the business entity if it is different from the registered name.
* **Entity Type** (`entity_type`): Select the type of business entity (e.g. Corporation, LLC, Partnership, etc.).
* **Date of Incorporation/Formation** (`incorporation_date`): Enter the date the business entity was created.
* **Registration or License Number** (`registration_number`): Enter the state or federal ID number for the business entity.
* **Principal Address** (`principal_address`): Enter the last known business address of the entity.
* **Effective Termination Date** (`termination_date`): Enter the date the business entity is ceasing operations.
* **Reason for Termination** (`termination_reason`): Choose the reason for termination (e.g. Voluntary Dissolution, Merger, Bankruptcy, etc.).
* **Additional Termination Details** (`termination_details`): Enter any additional context for termination if necessary.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (`authorized_person_name`): Enter the name of the person filing the termination.
* **Title** (`authorized_person_title`): Enter the position or role of the person filing the termination.
* **Representative Address** (`authorized_person_address`): Enter the contact address of the person filing the termination.
* **Phone Number** (`authorized_person_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the person filing the termination.
* **Email Address** (`authorized_person_email`): Enter the contact email of the person filing the termination.
* **Confirm Compliance** (`compliance_confirmation`): Confirm that all legal obligations have been met.
* **Filing Date** (`signature_date`): Date and sign the form to complete the filing.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for future reference.
