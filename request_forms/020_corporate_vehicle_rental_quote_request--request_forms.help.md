# Corporate Vehicle Rental Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a corporate vehicle rental quote for a specific period of time. It is intended for employees or authorized users within the organization to provide information about the rental details required to get an accurate quote. Please note that duplicate or unnecessary submissions may be deleted to ensure the integrity of the quote request process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Details** (Quote Details, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant details about the quote you are requesting.
* **Rent Start Date** (Rental Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start your rental. Please use the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Rent Time** (Rent Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to start your rental.
* **Rent Location** (Rent Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you plan to rent the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (Vehicle Type, select one, required/optional): Select the type of vehicle you require, such as Sedan, SUV, Van, or Truck.
* **Rental Days** (Rental Days, number, required/optional): Enter the number of days you need the vehicle for rent.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to start your rental.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to end your rental.
* **Rental Provider** (Rental Provider, select one, required/optional): Select whether you have a preferred rental provider or if you would like to be assigned one.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your rental request.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication about the quote or rental details.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for this rental request.
* **Company** (Company, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Title** (Contact Title, text, required/optional): Enter your contact title or position.
* **Department** (Department, text, required/optional): Enter your department name.
* **Location** (Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your company or department.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them in for a more accurate quote. Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
