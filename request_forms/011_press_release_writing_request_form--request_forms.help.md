# Press Release Writing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information for the creation of press releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information for the press release you want to create.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the company or organization associated with the press release.
* **Company Logo** (`company_logo`, text, required: false): Enter the URL or location of the company logo to be used with the press release.
* **Press Release Date** (`press_release_date`, date, required: false): Specify the date the press release will be released.
* **Press Release Time** (`press_release_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the press release will be released.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title that summarizes the purpose of the press release.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event or news being announced in the press release.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event or occurrence being announced in the press release.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event or occurrence.
* **Press Release Text** (`press_release_text`, text, required: false): Enter the text of the press release.
* **Press Release Image** (`press_release_image`, text, required: false): Enter the URL or location of the image to be used with the press release.
* **Press Release Video** (`press_release_video`, text, required: false): Enter the URL or location of the video to be used with the press release.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name and title of the contact person associated with the press release.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required: false): Specify the deadline for the press release.
* **Priority** (`priority`, text, required: false): Select the priority level of the press release.
* **Press Release Status** (`press_release_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the press release.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure the press release is completed correctly.
* Review the entire form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors or duplicate submissions.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
