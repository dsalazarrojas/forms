# Content Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Idea Submission Form is used to submit new content ideas for review and implementation. This form helps ensure that all relevant information is captured to make informed decisions about content creation and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name and email address to identify yourself as the submitter.
2.  Select your department or team to help us understand your role in the organization.
3.  Enter the date of your idea submission.
4.  Provide a brief title for your idea.
5.  Choose the category of content you are proposing (e.g., blog post, video, social media campaign).
6.  Describe your content idea in detail.
7.  Identify the target audience for your content.
8.  Specify the objective of your content (e.g., educate, generate leads, build brand awareness).
9.  List the key topics to be covered in your content.
10. Suggest a title or headline for your content.
11. Choose the format of your content (e.g., how-to guide, list article, interview).
12. Estimate the word count for your content.
13. Suggest keywords for search engine optimization (SEO).
14. If applicable, provide competitor references or internal subject matter experts to consult.
15. Determine the urgency level of your content (e.g., low, medium, high, urgent).
16. Propose a timeline for publication.
17. Indicate whether this content is part of a larger campaign.
18. Identify the distribution channels for your content.
19. Specify the visual assets required (e.g., featured image, infographic, screenshots).
20. Define how success will be measured.
21. Add any additional notes or relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (1): Your full name to identify yourself as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (2): Your email address to contact you for further questions or discussion.
* **Submitter Department** (3): Your department or team to help us understand your role.
* **Submission Date** (4): Date of idea submission to track progress.
* **Idea Title** (5): Brief title for your content idea.
* **Idea Category** (6): What type of content are you proposing (e.g., blog post, video, social media campaign).
* **Idea Description** (7): Describe your content idea in detail.
* **Target Audience** (8): Who is your content for?
* **Content Objective** (9): What goal will your content achieve (e.g., educate, generate leads, build brand awareness).
* **Key Topics** (10): What topics will be covered in your content.
* **Proposed Headline** (11): Suggested title or headline for your content.
* **Content Format** (12): What format should your content take (e.g., how-to guide, list article, interview).
* **Estimated Word Count** (13): Approximate length of your content.
* **Suggested Keywords** (14): Search engine optimization (SEO) keywords for your content.
* **Competitor References** (15): Similar content from competitors.
* **Internal Subject Matter Experts** (16): Internal experts to consult or interview.
* **Urgency Level** (17): How urgent is your content (e.g., low, medium, high, urgent).
* **Proposed Timeline** (18): When should your content be published.
* **Related Campaigns** (19): Is this part of a larger campaign.
* **Distribution Channels** (20): Where should your content be published (e.g., company blog, website, Facebook, Instagram).
* **Visual Assets Needed** (21): What visuals are required (e.g., featured image, infographic, screenshots).
* **Success Metrics** (22): How success will be measured.
* **Additional Notes** (23): Any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Be as specific and detailed as possible when describing your content idea.
* Make sure to include all relevant information to help us make informed decisions.
* Check your answers for completeness and accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
