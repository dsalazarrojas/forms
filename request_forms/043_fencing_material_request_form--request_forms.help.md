# Fencing Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request fencing material for a specific project or purpose. Please provide accurate and complete information to facilitate efficient processing and delivery of the materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. On the "Request Information" page, enter a brief description of the fencing material you need (e.g., for a new fence, repair, or replacement).
3. On the "Material Type" page, select the type of fencing material you need (Vinyl, Aluminum, or Wood).
4. On the "Material Quantity" page, enter the amount of material you require.
5. On the "Material Width" page, enter the width of the material you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Information (fencing_material_request_form_1)**: Enter a brief description of the fencing material you need, such as "New fence for back yard" or "Repairing a section of existing fence".
* **What type of fencing material do you need (material_type)**: Select the type of material you require from the options:
	+ Vinyl
	+ Aluminum
	+ Wood
* **How many materials do you need (material_quantity)**: Enter the amount of material you require. Please ensure this is a realistic quantity and not too large or too small.
* **Material width (material_width)**: Enter the width of the material you need. This will help ensure the material is cut to the correct size for your project.
