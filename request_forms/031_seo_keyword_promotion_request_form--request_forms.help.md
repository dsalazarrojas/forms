# SEO Keyword Promotion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about SEO keyword promotion requests from users. It is intended for SEO professionals or those familiar with SEO terminology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the keyword(s) you want to promote from the "Keyword Selection" field.
2. Choose the purpose, target audience, frequency, relevance, compromise, impact, tracking, status, priority, and any other relevant details about your keyword promotion.
3. Enter any additional information in the "User Input" field.
4. Enter your promotion request in the "Promotion Request" field.
5. Add any further details in the "Promotion Details" field.
6. Select the budget for your keyword promotion in the "Keyword Budget" field.
