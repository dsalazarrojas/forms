# credit_report_hard_inquiry_dispute_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a credit report hard inquiry dispute request. Please fill it out if you have a dispute regarding your credit report.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to describe the purpose of your request.
2. Select the category of your request from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of your request.
4. If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them here.
5. Select the authorizer who will be handling your request.
6. Choose the support team that will be handling your request.
7. Select the routing choices for your request.
8. If you have any additional files, you can upload them here.
9. Enter the date and time of the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your request.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of your request from the options provided:
	+ Request Forms
	+ Credit Report Forms
	+ Other
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Description** ( `description`, text, optional): This field should not be confused with the above "Description" field, as it serves the same purpose but without additional context.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, text, optional): If you have supporting documents, you can upload them here.
* **Authorizer** (`authorizer`, select_one, optional): Select the authorizer who will be handling your request:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Other
* **Support Team** (`support_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the support team that will be handling your request:
	+ Team A
	+ Team B
	+ Team C
* **Date** (`document_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the document.
* **Time** (`document_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the document.
* **Routing Choice** (`routing_choice`, select_multiple, optional): Select the routing choices for your request:
	+ Routing to C1
	+ Routing to C2
	+ Routing to C3
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the form.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): If you have additional files, you can upload them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for your request.
- Ensure that you have selected the correct category and authorizer for your request.
- If you have supporting documents, make sure to upload them correctly.
- Choose the correct support team and routing choices for your request.
- Enter the correct date and time of the document.
- If you have additional files, make sure to upload them correctly.
