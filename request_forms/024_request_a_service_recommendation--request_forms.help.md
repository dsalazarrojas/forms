# Request A Service Recommendation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request a service recommendation from our team. To ensure that your request is processed efficiently, please provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your input in the "User Input" field.
2.  Select the type of service you are requesting from the options provided in the "Service Type" field.
3.  Enter the name of the service you are requesting in the "Service Name" field.
4.  If necessary, provide more details about the service in the "Service Details" field.
5.  Select the date you would like to request the service in the "Request Date" field.
6.  Choose the priority level of your service request from "Low Priority", "Medium Priority", or "High Priority" in the "Service Priority" field.
7.  Enter the location where you would like to receive the service in the "Service Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`text`, required: false): This field is a text field where you can enter any additional information or comments about your request.
- **Service Type** (`select_one`, required: true): Select one of the service types from the options provided to identify the type of service you are requesting.
- **Service Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the service you are requesting. 
- **Service Details** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional details about the service you are requesting, if necessary.
- **Request Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to request the service.
- **Service Priority** (`select_one`, required: true): Select the priority level of your service request from "Low Priority", "Medium Priority", or "High Priority".
- **Service Location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location where you would like to receive the service.
