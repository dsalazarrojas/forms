# Environmental Education Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate collaborations between institutions for environmental education purposes. If you are looking to collaborate on a project or share resources, please fill out the form below.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of your collaboration request in the **Form Submission Details** field.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Specify the details of your request in the **Request Details** field.
4. Choose the partner institutions you would like to collaborate with from the list of options in the **Partner Institutions** field. This list includes:
	* University of California
	* University of Michigan
	* University of Texas
5. Enter your school's name in the **School Name** field, if applicable.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, if applicable.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Contact Phone** field, if applicable.
8. Enter your school's website in the **School Website** field, if applicable.
9. Enter your school's address in the **School Address** field, if applicable.
10. Enter your zip code in the **Zip Code** field, if applicable.
11. Enter the name of the contact person in the **Contact Person** field, if applicable.
12. Enter the name of the department in the **Department** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission Details**: A brief description of your collaboration request.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Request Details**: Provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Partner Institutions**: Select one or more partner institutions from the list to collaborate with.
* **School Name**: Enter your school's name, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **School Website**: Enter your school's website, if applicable.
* **School Address**: Enter your school's address, if applicable.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code, if applicable.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review and process your form submission within a reasonable timeframe.
