# Job Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Work Order Form is used to collect information for job orders, which are vital for project management and follow-ups. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field. This is your personal name.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the Last Name field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your **Email** in the Email field. This is your contact email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the Phone Number field. This is your contact phone number.
5. Specify the **Payment Amount** in the Payment Amount field. This is the amount you're willing to pay for the job order.
6. (Optional) Upload a file using the **File Upload** field. This is for attaching supporting documents or images.
7. Enter a brief description of the job in the **Order Description** field. This is an optional field for additional context.
8. (Optional) Upload another file using the **File Upload 2** field. This is for attaching more supporting documents or images.
9. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu. This will be used for processing payments.
10. Select the current **Payment Status** from the dropdown menu. This indicates the current state of your payment.
11. Enter the **Due Date** for the job order. This is the date by which the job must be completed.
12. Select the **Job Type** options that apply to your job order. This helps categorize and prioritize tasks.
13. Choose the current **Job Status** from the dropdown menu. This indicates the progress of your job order.
14. (Optional) Enter the **Work Order 2** date. This is an optional field for tracking additional dates.
15. (Optional) Enter the **Work Order 3** date. This is another optional field for tracking additional dates.
16. Enter the **Due Date** for payments. This is the date by which payments must be received.
17. Choose the **Payment Term** from the dropdown menu. This determines the payment schedule.
18. Enter the **Customer Name**. This is the name of the person or company ordering the job.
19. (Optional) Choose your preferred **Payment Method 2** from the dropdown menu. This will be used for processing payments.
20. Select the current **Payment Status 2** from the dropdown menu. This indicates the current state of your payment.
21. (Optional) Enter the **Work Order 4** date. This is an optional field for tracking additional dates.
22. Enter the **Customer Email**. This is the contact email address of the customer.
23. Enter the **Customer Phone**. This is the contact phone number of the customer.
24. (Optional) Upload another file using the **File Upload 3** field. This is for attaching more supporting documents or images.

- **Work Order 3** (`date`, optional): Enter another optional date for tracking additional dates.
