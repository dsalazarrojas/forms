# it_request_ticket - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_request_ticket" form is used to submit requests for assistance on internal tools and services. When you submit a ticket, you will be able to track and follow up on the status of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Category**: Choose the category that best describes your request from the dropdown menu. This helps the support team understand the nature of your request.
2. **Provide a Description**: Briefly describe your request in the text field. This is a required field and should include all relevant details about your request.
3. **Choose an Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that you are using for this request from the dropdown menu. This helps the support team understand the context of your request.
4. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`it_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your request from the dropdown menu. For example, if you are requesting help with a specific tool, choose "Request Forms".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your request in this text field. Please include all relevant details about your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool that you are using for this request from the dropdown menu.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click the "Submit" button to submit your request. If you are unsure about the submission process, you can also use the "Note" field for additional comments.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or information about your request. This field is not required but can be helpful for the support team to understand your needs better.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): If you know the date and time of when you submitted your request, you can enter it in this field. This can help the support team track the timeline of your request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, optional): If you know the time of day when you submitted your request, you can enter it in this field. This can help the support team track the timeline of your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* Use the "Note" field to add any additional comments or information about your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help from the support team.
