# Mass Intention Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mass Intention Request Form is designed to gather information for the Mass intentions to be held at our church. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way of capturing the details of the Mass intentions, including the date and time of the event, the name of the person the Mass is being requested for, and any additional comments or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Date of the Event (page 1) to schedule the Mass for the desired date.
2. Select the Time of the Mass (page 1) to specify the exact time of the Mass.
3. Choose how you are requesting the Mass (page 3) to indicate if it is for yourself or for someone else.
4. Select the Intention of the Mass (page 3) to choose the type of Mass you are requesting.
5. If the Mass is not for yourself, specify the name of the person the Mass is for (page 4).
6. Add any Additional Comments (page 5) to provide further context or requests for the Mass.
7. If necessary, provide the Location of the Mass (page 5) to specify where the Mass will be held.
8. Choose the Saint (page 5) and provide their name (page 6) if desired.
9. If the Mass is not for yourself, specify who the Mass is for (page 6).
10. If desired, make any Liturgical Requests (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of the Event (Mass Intention Date)** (`mass_intention_date`): Enter the date of the Mass you are requesting.
* **Time of the Mass (Mass Intention Time)** (`mass_intention_time`): Enter the exact time of the Mass you are requesting.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`): Choose if the Mass is being requested for yourself or for someone else.
	+ I am requesting the Mass for myself
	+ I am requesting the Mass for someone else
* **Intention of the Mass** (`mass_intention_type`): Choose the type of Mass you are requesting.
	+ Requiem
	+ Rosary
	+ Mass of the Sick
	+ Mass of the Dead
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional Comments** (`mass_intention_additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or requests for the Mass.
* **Location of the Mass** (`mass_location`): Provide the location where the Mass will be held, if applicable.
* **Saint** (`mass_saint`): Choose if you would like to dedicate the Mass to a Saint.
	+ Saint
	+ Other
* **Saint's Name** (`mass_saint_name`): If you chose the option above, provide the name of the Saint.
* **Mass is for** (`mass_for_who`): Choose if the Mass is for yourself or for someone else.
	+ Me
	+ Someone Else (specify below)
* **Mass is for (if not for the requestor)** (`mass_for_who_else`): If the Mass is not for yourself, provide the name of the person the Mass is for.
* **Liturgical Requests** (`mass_liturgy_requests`): Provide any additional requests for the Mass, if desired.
