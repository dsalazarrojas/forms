# Hardware Evaluation Kit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a hardware evaluation kit. Please fill out the form below with your request information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company and contact information in the "Company" and "Contact Number" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Specify the department or team you are part of in the "Department" field.
4. Enter any additional request details in the "Request Details" field.
5. Select the type of evaluation kit you are requesting in the "Evaluation Kit Type" field.
6. Choose whether you would like to be evaluated in the "Evaluation Kit Request" field.
7. Decide if you would like a follow-up after submitting the form in the "Follow Up" field and select the preferred method of communication in the "Follow Up Method" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (Company): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (Department): Enter the department or team you are part of.
* **Request Details** (Request Details): Enter any additional details or context related to your request.
* **Evaluation Kit Type** (Evaluation Kit Type): Select the type of evaluation kit you are requesting (Hardware, Software, or Other).
* **Evaluation Kit Request** (Evaluation Kit Request): Choose whether you would like to be evaluated (Yes/No).
* **Follow Up** (Follow Up): Decide if you would like a follow-up (Yes/No).
* **Follow Up Method** (Follow Up Method): Select the method you prefer for follow up (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Notes** (Notes): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, but you can leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrator.
