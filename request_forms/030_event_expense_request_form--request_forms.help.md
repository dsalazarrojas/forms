# Event Expense Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Expense Request Form is a tool for employees to submit information about their event expenses. This form is used to gather necessary details to process the request for reimbursement or approval of the expense.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Info page (page 1) with your personal details. 
2. Provide information about the expense on the Expense Details page (page 2). 
3. Submit the form to proceed with your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, required): Please fill in your name and any other relevant employee information such as your department or job title.

- **Expense Details** (`expense_details`, text, required): Describe the expense you are claiming. Please include the date of the event, event location, description of the expense, and any relevant receipts or supporting documents.

- **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Please click on this field to submit your request for expense reimbursement or approval.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed promptly.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any supporting documents or receipts, attach them to the expense details field for further review.
