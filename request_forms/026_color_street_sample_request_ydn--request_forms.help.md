# Color Street Sample Request - YDN - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a free sample from Your Dream Nails (YDN). Fill out the form to get your free sample.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Start" to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Provide your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
5. Choose your preferred nail style from the "Preferred Style" dropdown menu.
6. Indicate your color preferences in the "What colors do you love?" field.
7. Decide if you'd like to join the YDN mailing list by selecting "Would you like to join my mailing list for sales?".
8. Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Free Sets, Extra Income, or Not at this time) in the "Interested in earning free sets or extra income?" field.
9. Optionally, provide any extra information or messages in the "Message for me" field.
10. Finally, select how you heard about YDN (e.g., Facebook, friend, ad, etc.).
11. If you have a Facebook name, enter it to be tagged in the YDN VIP group.
12. Click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Request Your Free Sample - Your Dream Nails --**: This is just an introductory note explaining the purpose of the form. 
* **Full Name** (`ydn_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`ydn_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Mailing Address** (`ydn_address`, `text`, required): Provide your mailing address to receive your free sample.
* **-- Nail Style Preferences --**: This is just an explanatory note for this section.
* **What colors do you love?** (`ydn_color_choice`, `text`, required): Enter your preferred colors for the free sample.
* **Preferred Style** (`ydn_style_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Solids, Glitters, or Designs for your free sample.
* **-- Stay Informed --**: This is just an explanatory note for this section.
* **Would you like to join my mailing list for sales?** (`ydn_newsletter`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like to be on the YDN mailing list.
* **How did you find YDN?** (`ydn_heard_about`, `text`, optional): Select how you heard about YDN (e.g., Facebook, friend, ad, etc.).
* **Your Facebook Name** (`ydn_facebook_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your Facebook name to be tagged in the YDN VIP group.
* **Interested in earning free sets or extra income?** (`ydn_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose if you're interested in free sets, extra income, or not at this time.
* **Message for me** (`ydn_extra_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any extra information or messages you'd like to convey.
