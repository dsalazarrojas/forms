# Forestry Survey Tools Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request tools for forestry survey operations. Please use this form for specific tool requests related to forestry surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form fields as prompted, making sure to select the correct tool type and assigned tool.
* Ensure that the tool type and assigned tool match your survey needs.
* Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your request.
* Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **base_info**: Enter a brief description of your survey area or location.
* **assigned_tool**: Select the tool assigned to your team. Please note that this field is a duplicate of **assigned_tool** on Page 1, and only select one option.
* **assigned_tool_2**: Select the tool assigned to your team. (This field is a duplicate of **assigned_tool** on Page 1. Please ensure only one tool is selected.)
* **tool_type**: Select the type of tool required for your survey, such as surveyor or GPS.
* **base_info_2**: Enter any additional information related to your survey area or location.
* **date**: Enter the date of your planned survey operation.
* **base_info_3** and **base_info_4**: Enter any additional information related to your survey operation, such as notes or comments.

## Tips
* Ensure that your submission is relevant and not a duplicate request.
* Fill out the form carefully, as your request will be processed accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the survey operations team.
