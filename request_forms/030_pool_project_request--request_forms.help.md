# Pool Project Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a pool project. Please fill out the form with your project details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project title and description in the "Pool Project Request" field.
2. Select the type of pool you are interested in (rectangular, freeform, or kidney) in the "Pool Type" field.
3. Enter the length of your pool in the "Length" field.
4. Enter the width of your pool in the "Width" field.
5. Enter the depth of your pool in the "Depth" field.
6. Choose the shape of your pool (round, kidney, or irregular) in the "Pool Shape" field. You can also provide a brief description of your desired shape in the "Shape Description" field.
7. Check any additional features you would like to include in your pool (heated, saltwater, or saltwater with solar) in the "Pool Feature" field.
8. If you selected any additional features, describe them in the "Pool Feature Description" field.
9. Select the location of your pool (backyard, rooftop, or other) in the "Pool Location" field. You can also provide a brief description of your desired location in the "Location Description" field.
10. Enter your name and phone number in the "Name" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
11. Enter your email address and a message with your request in the "Email" and "Message" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Project Request** (1): A brief description of your project.
* **Pool Type** (2): The type of pool you are interested in (rectangular, freeform, or kidney).
	+ Required: Yes
* **Length** (3): The length of your pool.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Width** (4): The width of your pool.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Depth** (5): The depth of your pool.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Pool Shape** (6): The shape of your pool (round, kidney, or irregular).
	+ Required: No
* **Shape Description** (7): A brief description of your desired shape.
	+ Required: No
* **Pool Feature** (8): Additional features you would like to include in your pool (heated, saltwater, or saltwater with solar).
	+ Type: Select Multiple
* **Pool Feature Description** (9): A description of your selected features.
	+ Required: No
* **Pool Location** (10): The location of your pool (backyard, rooftop, or other).
	+ Type: Select One
* **Location Description** (11): A brief description of your desired location.
	+ Required: No
* **Name** (12): Your name.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone** (13): Your phone number.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email** (14): Your email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Message** (15): A message with your request.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Pool Design** (17): The design style of your pool (custom, semi-custom, or semi-custom with fiberglass).
* **Design Description** (18): A description of your desired design style.
* **Design Materials** (19): The materials you would like to use for your pool design.
* **Pool Feature Description** (not explicitly defined in original YAML): This field is not explicitly defined in the original YAML. I assume it was an error or a missing field, so I've removed it from the guide.

This guide should be followed carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information for your pool project request.
