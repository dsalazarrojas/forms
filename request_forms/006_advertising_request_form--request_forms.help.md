# Advertising Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request advertising campaigns for your organization. It helps us understand your advertising needs and goals, allowing us to provide effective and targeted advertising solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's name and your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter a short title for your campaign.
4. Select the main goal of your campaign (e.g., Promote a new product, Increase brand awareness, etc.).
5. Describe the target demographic for your ads.
6. Choose the channels you would like to use for your ads (e.g., Social Media, Search Engines, etc.).
7. Provide a budget allocation for your campaign.
8. Set a start date for your campaign.
9. Tell us about your creative assets availability (e.g., if you have images/copy ready).
10. Add any additional notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Your organization's name.
* **Your Name**: Your name and title (e.g., John Smith, Marketing Manager).
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Campaign Title**: Short title for your campaign (e.g., "Summer Sale" for a summer sale campaign).
* **Campaign Objective**: Select the main goal of your campaign from the list (e.g., Promote a new product, Increase brand awareness, etc.).
* **Target Demographic**: Describe who your target audience is (e.g., age range, location, etc.).
* **Requested Channels**: Choose the channels you would like to use for your ads (e.g., Social Media, Search Engines, etc.).
* **Budget Allocation**: Enter a budget allocation for your campaign (e.g., $10,000).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your campaign to start.
* **Creative Assets Availability**: Indicate whether you have images/copy ready for your campaign.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any specific instructions or constraints for your campaign.
