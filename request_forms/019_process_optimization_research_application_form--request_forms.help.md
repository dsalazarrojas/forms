# Process Optimization Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a proposal for a process optimization research project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project title and description.
2. Clearly articulate the research question or area you want to focus on.
3. Specify the process area you want to improve.
4. Define the objective of your project.
5. Outline the expected outcome of your project.
6. Describe any process improvements you envision.
7. List any necessary research approvals or authorizations.
8. Identify the team members involved in the project.
9. Provide your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
10. Choose a date and time for the project start-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your project that summarizes the main focus of your research.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, required): Clearly describe the problem or question you want to answer through your research.
* **Process Area** (`process_area`, text, required): Identify the specific area of the process you want to improve.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text): Provide a detailed description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, required): Specify the field of research relevant to your project.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, required): Define the main objective of your project.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, text, required): Outline the desired outcome of your project.
* **Process Improvement** (`process_improvement`, text): Describe any process improvements you envision.
* **Research Approvals** (`research_approvals`, text): List any necessary research approvals or authorizations.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text): Identify the team members involved in the project.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the contact person for the project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Choose a date for the project start-up.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Choose a time for the project start-up.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.

## Tips

* Be as clear and concise as possible in your answers.
* Ensure your project proposal is well-structured and easy to understand.
* Proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
