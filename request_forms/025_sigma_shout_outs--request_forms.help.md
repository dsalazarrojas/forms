# Sigma Shout Outs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sigma Shout Outs form is used to collect information for recognizing and rewarding team members for their outstanding work and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* First Name: Enter the first name of the team member you wish to recognize.
	* Last Name: Enter the last name of the team member you wish to recognize.
	* Email: Enter the team member's email address.
	* Phone: Enter the team member's phone number (optional).
	* Date: Select the date of the achievement or contribution.
	* Time: Select the time of the achievement or contribution.
	* Message: Enter a message to acknowledge and explain the team member's achievement or contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the first name of the team member you wish to recognize.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the last name of the team member you wish to recognize.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **not required**): Enter the team member's email address. Please ensure to format it as a standard email address (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **not required**): Select the date of the achievement or contribution.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **not required**): Select the time of the achievement or contribution.
* **Message** (`message`, note, **not required**): Enter a message to acknowledge and explain the team member's achievement or contribution.
