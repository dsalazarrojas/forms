# New Initiative Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new initiatives or proposals from users. It's a general-purpose tool for submitting ideas or improvements within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in this text field.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this text field.
3. **Description**: Describe the initiative or proposal you are submitting in a few sentences.
4. **Benefits**: Outline the advantages or benefits of your proposal.
5. **Impact**: Describe the potential impact or effect of your initiative.
6. **Responsible Party**: Identify the individual or team responsible for this party.
7. **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, specify the tool(s) assigned to this initiative.
8. **Category**: Select the category that best describes your initiative (e.g., Request Forms, Report Forms, or Other).
9. **Tags**: Choose one or more relevant tags to help categorize your initiative (e.g., tag 1, tag 2, tag 3, or tag 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	+ This field is used to identify you as the submitter of the initiative.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	+ This field is used to identify you as the submitter of the initiative.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the initiative or proposal you are submitting.
	+ This field should be concise and to the point, providing a clear understanding of your idea.
* **Benefits**: Outline the advantages or benefits of your proposal.
	+ This field should provide a clear understanding of what makes your proposal valuable.
* **Impact**: Describe the potential impact or effect of your initiative.
	+ This field should highlight the potential results or consequences of your proposal.
* **Responsible Party**: Identify the individual or team responsible for this party.
	+ This field should clearly state who is accountable for this initiative.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, specify the tool(s) assigned to this initiative.
	+ This field should include any relevant tools or resources required for the initiative.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your initiative.
	+ This field should help categorize your proposal for easier tracking and reference.
* **Tags**: Choose one or more relevant tags to help categorize your initiative.
	+ This field should include any relevant keywords or labels that describe your proposal.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help ensure that your proposal is considered thoroughly and efficiently.
