# Flyers Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for printing flyers. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete details to ensure that your printing request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Company Name" field with the correct name of the company or organization making the request.
2. Enter the contact person's details in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Provide the company's address in the "Company Address" field.
5. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. If applicable, enter the job title of the contact person in the "Job Title" field.
7. Indicate the number of pages required for printing in the "Number of Pages" field.
8. Select the paper size for the printing from the options listed in the "Paper Size" field.
9. Choose the paper orientation from the options listed in the "Paper Orientation" field.
10. Select whether color printing is required or not in the "Color Printing" field.
11. Choose the color type from the options listed in the "Color Type" field.
12. Select the finish for the printing from the options listed in the "Finish" field.
13. Enter the quantity of flyers required in the "Quantity" field.
14. Enter the unit price for the printing in the "Unit Price" field.
15. Calculate the subtotal by multiplying the quantity by the unit price in the "Subtotal" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (1)** (`text`, `Company Name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or organization making the request.
* **Contact Person (2)** (`text`, `Contact Person`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (3)** (`email`, `Contact Email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Company Address (4)** (`text`, `Company Address`, required/optional): Enter the company's address.
* **Contact Phone (5)** (`text`, `Contact Phone`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Job Title (6)** (`text`, `Job Title`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Number of Pages (7)** (`number`, `Number of Pages`, required/optional): Indicate the number of pages required for printing.
* **Paper Size (8)** (`select_one`, `Paper Size`, required/optional): Select the paper size for the printing from the options listed.
* **Paper Orientation (9)** (`select_multiple`, `Paper Orientation`, required/optional): Choose the paper orientation from the options listed.
* **Color Printing (10)** (`select_one`, `Color Printing`, required/optional): Select whether color printing is required or not.
* **Color Type (11)** (`select_multiple`, `Color Type`, required/optional): Choose the color type from the options listed.
* **Finish (12)** (`select_one`, `Finish`, required/optional): Select the finish for the printing from the options listed.
* **Quantity (13)** (`number`, `Quantity`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of flyers required.
* **Unit Price (14)** (`number`, `Unit Price`, required/optional): Enter the unit price for the printing.
* **Subtotal (15)** (`number`, `Subtotal`, required/optional): Calculate the subtotal by multiplying the quantity by the unit price.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid errors in processing your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or need further assistance, please contact the printing department.
* This form is intended for internal use only. If you are an external partner or customer, please refer to your contract or agreement for further instructions.
