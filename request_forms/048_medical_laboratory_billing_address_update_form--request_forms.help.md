# Medical Laboratory Billing Address Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Laboratory Billing Address Update Form is used to update the billing address associated with a medical laboratory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Laboratory ID number in the "Laboratory ID" field. This is a unique identifier for the laboratory and is required to be filled in.
2. Enter the new billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
3. Enter the street number and name in the "Street" field.
4. Enter the city where the laboratory is located in the "City" field.
5. Enter the zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
6. Enter the start date of the new billing period in the "Start Date" field.
7. Enter the end date of the new billing period in the "End Date" field.
8. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
9. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
10. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a short title for the updated billing address. This field can be left blank if desired.
* **Laboratory ID** (`laboratory_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the laboratory.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the new billing address for the laboratory.
* **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter the street number and name of the laboratory.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the laboratory is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter the postal code for the laboratory's location.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the new billing period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the new billing period.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the laboratory.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to make changes to the laboratory's billing address. 
Also, please enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with the laboratory's billing.
