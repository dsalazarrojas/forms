# Omnichannel Support Integration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Omnichannel Support Integration Application Form is a tool for customers to provide support and integration requests to your organization. This form helps to streamline the process of gathering necessary information from customers, reducing response times, and improving overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the **Support Channels** you would like to integrate with.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
3. Describe your **Integration Goals** and **Submission Type**.
4. Select the type of **Integration Type** you desire.
5. Enter any additional **Notes** if necessary.
6. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Contact Info** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your company's contact information.
* **Support Channels** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the support channels you want to integrate with. (e.g., email, phone, chat)
* **Integration Goals** (4, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your integration request.
* **Submission Type** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of submission (e.g., urgent, standard, delayed).
* **Email** (6, email, required/optional): Enter your company email.
* **Phone** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Date** (8, date, required/optional): Enter the date you need support for.
* **Time** (9, time, required/optional): Enter the time you need support for.
* **Note** (10, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Integration Type** (11, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of integration (e.g., API, SDK, data import).
* **Company Address** (12, text, required/optional): Enter your company address.
* **Integration Goals 2** (13, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your second integration request.
* **Support Email** (14, email, required/optional): Enter the support email.
* **Support Phone** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the support phone number.
* **Support Date** (16, date, required/optional): Enter the date you need support for.
* **Integration Goals 3** (17, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your third integration request.
* **Submission Type 2** (18, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of submission (e.g., urgent, standard, delayed).
* **Support Hours** (19, time, required/optional): Enter the support hours.
* **Support Channels 2** (20, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the support channels you want to use.
* **Support Hours 2** (21, time, required/optional): Enter the support hours for your second integration request.
* **Integration Goals 4** (22, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your fourth integration request.
* **Submission Type 3** (23, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of submission (e.g., urgent, standard, delayed).
* **Integration Goals 5** (24, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your fifth integration request.
* **Integration Goals 6** (25, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of your sixth integration request.
