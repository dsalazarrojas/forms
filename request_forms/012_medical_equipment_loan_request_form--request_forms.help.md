# Medical Equipment Loan Requestæk - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Equipment Loan Requestæk form is designed to provide a structured way for users to request loaned medical equipment. This form captures essential information about the equipment, loan details, and user preferences to ensure efficient loan management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the equipment being loaned (e.g., Hospitals, Clinics, Universities, Labs).
2. Choose the equipment type (e.g., General Medical Equipment, Surgical Equipment, Diagnostic Equipment).
3. Enter a description of the equipment being loaned.
4. Select the loan start and end dates.
5. Enter a reason for the loan.
6. Provide borrower details (e.g., name, contact information).
7. Select the loan status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_id**: (Number): Enter a unique identifier for the loan request. This is not required, but it helps with tracking and organization.
* **Category** (select_one): Select the category the equipment belongs to, such as Hospitals, Clinics, Universities, Labs.
* **Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the equipment being loaned.
* **Equipment Type** (select_one): Choose the type of equipment, such as General Medical Equipment, Surgical Equipment, Diagnostic Equipment, or Other.
* **Loan Date** (Date): Enter the date the equipment will be loaned.
* **Loan Start** (Date): Enter the start date of the loan.
* **Loan End** (Date): Enter the end date of the loan.
* **Reason** (Text): Enter a reason for the loan.
* **Borrower Details** (Text): Enter the name and contact information of the borrower.
* **Loan Status** (select_one): Select the status of the loan, such as Active, Inactive, Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate smooth loan management.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact your medical staff or equipment administrator.
