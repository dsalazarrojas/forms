# Request For Tags And Controls - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage requests for tags and controls across various categories. It ensures that all relevant information is collected and tracked for efficient processing and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate category for your request from the list of available options.
2. Enter the name and abbreviation of the tag you are requesting.
3. Provide your name and email address so that we can contact you about the status of your request.
4. Enter a brief description of the request.
5. Identify the team members who should review your request.
6. Specify the priority level of your request (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
7. Select the current status of your request (In Review, Rejected, Approved).
8. Optionally, enter the date you submitted the request and the date you expect it to be reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Select One): Choose the category for your request from the options provided. This helps us route your request to the right team for review and management.
* **Tag Name** (Text): Enter the name of the tag you are requesting. Try to be as specific as possible to ensure we understand your request accurately.
* **Tag Abbreviation** (Text): Enter a brief abbreviation for the tag name to help with searches and documentation.
* **Submitter Name** (Text): Please provide your name so that we can contact you about the status of your request.
* **Submitter Email** (Email): Enter your email address so that we can get back to you about your request.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text): If you would like to be contacted over the phone, provide your phone number.
* **Request Description** (Text): Give a brief description of the request. This helps the reviewers understand the context and requirements of your request.
* **Reviewers** (Select Multiple): Choose the team members who should review your request. This ensures the right people are involved in the review process.
* **Priority** (Select Multiple): Set the priority level of your request (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Review Status** (Select One): Select the current status of your request (In Review, Rejected, Approved).
* **Request Date** (Date): Enter the date you submitted the request. This helps us track the request timeline.
* **Review Date** (Date): If known, enter the expected review date or the date of the last review.
* **Assigned To** (Select One): Identify the person assigned to review or manage your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the request database before submitting a new request to avoid duplicates.
* Provide clear and concise descriptions to ensure the reviewers understand your request accurately.
* Ensure you select the correct category and reviewers for your request to get it to the right people.
* Update the review status and priority as your request moves through the process.
