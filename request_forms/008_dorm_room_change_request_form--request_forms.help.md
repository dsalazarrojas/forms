# Dorm Room Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dorm Room Change Request Form is used to submit requests to change a student's dorm room configuration. This includes changing the room type (e.g., single, double, triple), dorm number, or both.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before submitting the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Submit the form through the designated channel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter your student ID number as required for identity verification.
* **Current Room Number** (`current_room_number`): Provide your current room number to help us locate your room.
* **New Room Number** (`new_room_number`): Enter the desired room number for your new configuration.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`): Briefly explain the reason for your room change request.
* **New Room Type** (`new_room_type`): Select your desired room type from the options (Single, Double, or Triple).
* **Current Dorm Number** (`current_dorm_number`): Enter your current dorm building number.
* **New Dorm Number** (`new_dorm_number`): Enter your desired dorm building number.
* **Date of Change Made** (`date_change_made`): Record the date you made the change.
* **Time of Change Made** (`time_change_made`): Record the time you made the change.
* **Reason for Change Made** (`reason_for_change_made`): Briefly explain the reason for making the change.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your current and desired room configurations.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to speed up the review process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your residence life support team.
