## Stop Payment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is designed for customers to request a stop payment on an existing account. A stop payment is a request to temporarily or permanently prevent payments from being drawn from a customer's account. This form is intended to gather necessary information to process the stop payment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
2. Provide a brief reason for stopping payments on the account.
3. Enter the name of the company associated with the account.
4. Enter the account number associated with the account.
5. Select a payment date to stop payments from being drawn from the account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
* **Reason for Stopping Payments** (`reason_for_stopping_payments`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for why payments need to be stopped.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the account.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, number, optional): Enter the account number to be associated with the stop payment request.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Select the date when payments should be stopped from the account.

## Tips

* Ensure that the customer's name, company name, and account number are accurate to prevent delays in processing the stop payment request.
* If the customer has multiple accounts to be stopped, please fill out a separate form for each account.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
