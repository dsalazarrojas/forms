# Network Coverage Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Coverage Inquiry Form is designed to collect details about network-related issues or requests from users. This form aims to gather essential information to facilitate timely and effective assistance from network administrators or support teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have not previously submitted a similar request. 
* Fill out the form with accurate and clear details about your network coverage inquiry.
* Enter your desired network service type (e.g., Ethernet, Wi-Fi, 4G).
* Select your desired coverage type (e.g., Full, Partial, None).
* Provide any additional follow-up details or notes if needed.
* Review your submission to ensure it is complete and accurate before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_details**: Enter a brief description of your network-related issue or request.
* **network_status**: Describe the status of your network connection (e.g., connected, disconnected, slow).
* **network_type**: Select the type of network you are experiencing issues with (e.g., Ethernet, Wi-Fi, 4G).
* **coverage_type**: Choose the type of coverage you are experiencing issues with (e.g., Full, Partial, None).
* **follow_up**: Enter any additional details about your issue or request.
* **notes**: Provide any further comments or context about your issue or request.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate assistance.
* If you have previously submitted a similar request, please refer to the relevant support ticket for further assistance.
