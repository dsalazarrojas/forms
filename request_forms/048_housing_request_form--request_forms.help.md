# Housing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a housing request, allowing users to express their needs or preferences regarding housing in a specific area or type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether or not you are requesting housing (yes/no).
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Choose a specific date for your visit (if applicable).
7. Choose a specific time for your visit (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Housing Request** (`housing_request`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting housing, 'No' otherwise.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like to be housed or currently reside.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Choose a specific date for your visit (if applicable).
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Choose a specific time for your visit (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure you have a clear and concise request.
* Double-check the information provided for accuracy.
* If you have already submitted a similar request, please do not resubmit, as this may lead to confusion or duplication of effort.
