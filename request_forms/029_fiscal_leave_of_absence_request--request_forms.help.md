# Fiscal Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence from work due to various reasons such as personal or family emergencies, illnesses, or other approved events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title as they appear on your official personnel file.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave.
3. Choose the reason for your leave from the provided options.
4. Select your expected return date.
5. Choose the approval status of your leave.
6. Choose your manager who will be responsible for approving your leave.
7. Select the type of leave you are requesting.
8. Choose the start and end times of your leave.
9. Enter any comments from your manager.
10. Choose the duration of your leave.
11. Enter your employee ID.
12. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
13. Enter your manager's ID.
14. Choose your employee status.
15. Choose the status of your leave.
16. Enter the time and date of form submission.
17. Choose the status of form submission.
18. Enter your department.
19. Enter any approval reason from your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official personnel file.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your official personnel file.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Choose the reason for your leave from the provided options.
* **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Choose the approval status of your leave from the options provided.
* **Approval Manager** (`approval_manager`, select_one, required): Choose your manager who will be responsible for approving your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting.
* **Leave Start Time** (`leave_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your leave.
* **Leave End Time** (`leave_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your leave.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments from your manager.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your leave from the options provided.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID as it appears on your official personnel file.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, number, required): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required): Choose your employee status from the options provided.
* **Leave Status** (`leave_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your leave from the options provided.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your form submission from the options provided.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, required): Enter your department as it appears on your official personnel file.
* **Approval Reason** (`approval_reason`, text, optional): Enter any approval reason from your manager.
