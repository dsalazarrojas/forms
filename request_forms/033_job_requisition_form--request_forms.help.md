# Job Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Requisition Form is a tool designed to gather information that can be used to create original forms tailored to specific industries and categories, leveraging public survey topics as inspiration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing the form's structure**, understanding that it consists of four pages, each of which is an optional section for free-text responses or descriptions.
2. **Choose which pages are relevant to your needs**, as indicated by the labels on each page.
3. **Fill out the selected pages** with your relevant information.
