# red_saree_styling_advice_request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients about their preferences and event details for red Saree styling services. The form will help our team understand your needs and style your event with precision.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate information.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required") with your preferences and event details.
3. For fields marked as "Optional", you can provide additional information if you wish, but it's not required.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter any specific preferences you have for your event, such as color scheme, theme, or special requests.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required):
	+ Describe your event, including date, time, location, and any other important details that our team needs to know.
* **Stylist Preferences** (`stylist_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any favorite stylists or specific preferences for the stylist who will be working with you, please list them here.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing event details to ensure our team can create a perfect style plan for you.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your input and will do our best to accommodate your preferences.
