# Animal Surrender/Intake Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an animal being surrendered or intaken by an animal shelter or rescue organization. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure the best possible outcome for the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please review the information below carefully. If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the shelter staff.

1.  Start by gathering the owner information, including the owner's name, phone number, and email address.
2.  Provide details about the animal, such as its name, type, breed, age, weight, color, and health status.
3.  Answer questions about the animal's behavior, such as its interaction with other animals, children, and any behavioral issues.
4.  Provide information about the animal's medical history, vaccinations, and any allergies or sensitivities.
5.  Specify the reason for surrendering or intaking the animal.
6.  Share any additional notes or instructions for the animal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Owner Full Name* (owner_name): Your complete name, as you would like to be addressed.
    *Hint: Your name as you would like to be known.* 
*   *Phone Number* (owner_phone): Best contact number.
    *Hint: Ensure you can be reached in case of emergencies.* 
*   *Email Address* (owner_email): Contact email address.
    *Hint: Ensure the shelter can reach you via email.* 
*   *Street Address* (owner_address): Current residential address.
    *Hint: This will be used to contact you in case of an emergency.* 
*   *City* (owner_city): City of residence.
    *Hint: This will help the shelter contact you.* 
*   *State* (owner_state): State or province.
    *Hint: This will help the shelter contact you.* 
*   *Zip Code* (owner_zip): Postal code.
    *Hint: This will help the shelter contact you.* 

*   *Animal Name* (animal_name): Pet's name.
    *Hint: This is the name the animal is known by.* 
*   *Type of Animal* (animal_type): Species.
    *Hint: The type of animal you are surrendering or intaking.* 

*   *Breed* (animal_breed): Breed or mixed breed.
    *Hint: If known, please specify the animal's breed.* 
*   *Age* (animal_age): Age in years or months.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's care needs.* 
*   *Weight* (animal_weight): Weight in pounds.
    *Hint: The animal's weight will help determine its care needs.* 
*   *Color or Markings* (animal_color): Physical description.
    *Hint: Describe the animal's coat color or any notable markings.* 
*   *Gender* (animal_gender): Male or female.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's behavior and care needs.* 

*   *Behavior Around Other Dogs* (good_with_dogs): How the animal behaves around other dogs.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's compatibility with other animals.* 
*   *Behavior Around Cats* (good_with_cats): How the animal behaves around cats.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's compatibility with other animals.* 
*   *Behavior Around Children* (good_with_children): How the animal behaves around kids.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's compatibility with children.* 

*   *Reason for Surrender* (reason_for_surrender): Main reason.
    *Hint: This will help determine the reason for the animal's surrender.* 
*   *Reason for Surrender Details* (reason_details): Explanation of the reason.
    *Hint: This will help the shelter understand the situation better.* 
*   *Length of Ownership* (length_of_ownership): How long you have owned the animal.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's care history.* 
*   *Previous Owner Count* (previous_owners): Number of owners before you.
    *Hint: This will help determine the animal's ownership history.* 
*   *Preferred Outcome* (requested_outcome): Preferred outcome.
    *Hint: This will help determine what you hope for the animal.* 

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   Provide as much detail as possible to ensure the shelter can provide the best care for the animal.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, please do not hesitate to contact the shelter for assistance.
*   Ensure that you provide the required information to avoid any delays in the surrender or intake process.
