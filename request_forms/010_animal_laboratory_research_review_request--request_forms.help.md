# Animal Laboratory Research Review Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Animal Laboratory Research Review Request form is designed for researchers to obtain approval for using animals in their studies. This form requires detailed information about the study, including the principal investigator's name, project title, funding agency, proposed start and end dates, number of animals requested, species, justification for animal use, description of procedures, and method of euthanasia. The form is essential for ensuring that all animal research projects are thoroughly reviewed and approved before commencement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your research project.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review and understand the implications of the research project on the animals involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Investigator Name**: Enter your name as the principal investigator or lead researcher of the project.
* **Project Title**: Provide the full title of your research study, including any necessary details or descriptions.
* **Funding Agency**: Indicate the source of your research funds, such as a government agency or private organization.
* **Proposed Start Date**: Enter the expected start date of your research project.
* **Proposed End Date**: Enter the expected end date of your research project.
* **Number of Animals Requested**: Enter the total number of animals required for the duration of your project.
* **Species to be Studied**: Select the species you plan to study, such as mice, rats, or monkeys.
* **Justification for Animal Use**: Explain why animals are necessary for your research project and how they will be used.
* **Description of Procedures**: Summarize the major experimental steps and procedures to be performed on the animals.
* **Pain or Distress Category**: Select the category that best describes the level of pain or distress involved in your research, using the USDA classification.
* **Method of Euthanasia**: Describe the method of euthanasia you plan to use, following AVMA guidelines.
* **Internal Ethical Review ID (Optional)**: Enter any internal ethical review ID number, if applicable.
