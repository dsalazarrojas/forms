# Discount Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request discounts or special promotions from our team. It is intended for customers who have questions or need clarification on their existing orders or invoices. Please use it only once per issue or question, and do not submit duplicate requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, simply fill out the form below. You can navigate between pages using the arrows or by clicking on the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Get in touch
* **Get in touch** (`text`, optional): Enter your name or company name to get in touch with our team. This will help us identify you and ensure we respond to your request.

### Page 2: Details
* **Details** (`select_one`, required): Select the reason for your request from the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
Please choose the option that best describes your reason for requesting a discount.

### Page 3: Submit
This page is not required for form submission. It is a note to our team.

### Page 4: Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`number`, optional): Enter your phone number to enable our team to contact you directly.

### Page 5: Email
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address to enable our team to contact you directly.

### Page 6: Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number to enable our team to contact you directly.

### Page 7: Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments you have about your request. This will help our team better understand your needs.
