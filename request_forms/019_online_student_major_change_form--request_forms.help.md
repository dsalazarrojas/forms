# Online Student Major Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Student Major Change Form is used by students to request a change of major in their academic program. This form helps the student affairs or academic advising office track and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Major Request field with your current major.
2. Select the Previous Major you were previously in from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide a Reason for Change in the text field if you wish to explain the reason for your major change request.
4. Select the New Major you wish to enroll in from the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional Comments if you have any further explanations or information to provide.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Major Request**: Enter your current major.
* **Previous Major**: Select the major you were previously in.
* **Reason for Change**: Provide a brief explanation for requesting a major change (optional).
* **New Major**: Select the new major you wish to enroll in.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or explanations.
* **Signature**: Confirm that you have submitted this request.
* **Date of Submission**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Time of Submission**: The time the form was submitted (not visible to users).
* **Contact Number**: Your contact information in case the office needs to reach out to you.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Contact Person**: Your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Student").
* **Submitted By**: The person who submitted the form (typically the student).
* **Submitted On**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Updated By**: The person who last updated the form.
* **Updated On**: The date the form was last updated.
* **Status**: The current status of the request (Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Notes**: Any additional notes or comments from the office.
* **Submitted to**: The person or office the form was submitted to.

Note: Fields marked as required are necessary for the form to be processed.
