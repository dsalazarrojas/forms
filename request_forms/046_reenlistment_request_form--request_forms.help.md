# Reenlistment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reenlistment Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to reenlist or re-enter the military or defense service. This form is used to gather essential details that will be reviewed and evaluated for reenlistment eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select your military service history from the available options.
4. Provide a brief reason for your reenlistment request.
5. Choose a date for your intended reenlistment.
6. Summarize your request in the review field (this is a free-text field for additional comments).
7. If you're eligible, confirm your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address associated with your military or defense service.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Military Service** (`Military Service`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the military services you have served in or are interested in reenlisting into. Typically, this would include the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, or Coast Guard.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your reenlistment request. This is your opportunity to outline your motivations, goals, and any relevant experience.
* **Reenlistment Date** (`Reenlistment Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to reenlist or re-enter the military or defense service.
* **Review** (`Review`, `note`, required): Summarize your reenlistment request in this field. If you have additional comments or questions, this is the place to provide them.
* **Sign** (`Sign`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your reenlistment request by selecting 'True' from the options. This ensures that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of reenlistment.
