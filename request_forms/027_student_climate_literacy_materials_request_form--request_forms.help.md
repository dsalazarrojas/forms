# Student Climate Literacy Materials Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Climate Literacy Materials Request Form is a tool for students to share their interest in climate change topics and request educational resources. This form helps students who are looking for educational materials, such as textbooks, articles, or multimedia content, related to climate change.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for follow-up communication.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you for further questions or clarification.
4.  Choose your department from the list in the "Department" field.
5.  Select whether you are interested in "Yes" or "No" for climate change resources in the "Resource Request" field.
6.  If you have any comments or questions, type them in the "Comments" field.
7.  If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request Form (Request Form, text, required):** This field is where you can briefly explain why you are interested in climate change resources.
*   **Name (Name, text, required):** This is your name.
*   **Email (Email, email, required):** This is your email address used for follow-up communication.
*   **Phone Number (Phone Number, number, required):** This is your phone number used for follow-up contact.
*   **Department (Department, text, required):** Choose your department from the provided list.
*   **Resource Request (Resource Request, select multiple, required):** Choose whether you are interested in "Yes" or "No" for climate change resources.
*   **Comments (Comments, text, optional):** If you have any comments or questions, type them here.
*   **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional):** If you have any additional comments or requests, type them here.
