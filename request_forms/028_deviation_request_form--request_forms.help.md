# Deviation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request deviations or changes to a business's service. It collects information about the business, the reason for the request, and the impact on the service, contract, and customers. It also tracks the status of the deviation request, who it was submitted by, and who it was assigned to.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure that the request is not a duplicate of an existing deviation request.
* Enter your business's name as it appears on the contract.
* Provide a clear and concise reason for the request.
* Describe the impact of the deviation on the service and contract.
* Provide the date and time the deviation is expected to occur.
* Specify the status of the request (approved, denied, or pending).
* Enter your contact information (email, phone number, and business address).
* If applicable, attach any supporting documentation or evidence.

## Tips
* Ensure that the request is complete and accurate before submitting it.
* Be clear and concise in your reason for the request.
* Attach any supporting documentation to demonstrate the impact on the service and contract.
* Communicate with the assigned person to track the progress of the request.
* Follow up with the person handling the request to ensure the deviation is addressed.
