# Medical Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Request Form is a template used to request medical records from one healthcare provider or facility to another. This form is designed to be completed by a patient or their authorized representative to facilitate the transfer of medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form, which is the name of the medical record request.
2. Select the category of information you are requesting (e.g., "Request Forms").
3. Provide a brief description of the reason you are requesting the medical records.
4. Enter your name and date of birth.
5. Enter the name of the healthcare facility or provider you are requesting the records from.
6. Enter the address and zip code of the healthcare facility or provider.
7. Select the state where the facility is located.
8. Enter the name and signature of the patient (or their representative).
9. Enter the name and signature of the healthcare provider or facility.
10. Select the request type (e.g., "Release" or "Copy").
11. Enter the date you are requesting the records be released or copied.
12. Enter the date the records were received.
13. Enter the date the records were verified.
14. Provide a reason for requesting the medical records.
