# Mechanical Pencil Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Pencil Request Form is a means for employees to submit requests for mechanical pencils. This form will be reviewed and processed by the management team to ensure that the requests are legitimate and meet the company's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Select your Department.
3. Enter the desired Quantity of pencils (if applicable).
4. Choose your preferred Pencil Color (if applicable).
5. Enter your Request Reason (if applicable).
6. Enter your Email and Phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Quantity** (`pencil_quantity`, `number`, optional): If you would like to request a specific quantity of pencils, please enter the number here.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, optional): If you would like to specify a particular date for your request, please enter it here.
* **Pencil Color** (`pencil_color`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred pencil color from the available options.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, optional): If you would like to provide a reason for your request, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your work phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your request, please contact the management team.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
