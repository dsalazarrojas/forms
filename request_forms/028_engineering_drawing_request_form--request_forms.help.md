# Engineering Drawing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Drawing Request Form is a tool for customers to request engineering drawings from the engineering team. This form helps to gather necessary information about the customer's request, ensuring that their needs are met in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "customer_name" and "customer_email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
3. Describe your project in the "project_description" field.
4. Select the type of drawing you need using the "drawing_type" dropdown menu.
5. Check the relevant documents required for your request in the "required_documents" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date and time you would like to submit the drawing in the "submission_date" and "submission_time" fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional notes or information about your request in the "notes" field.
8. Enter the tool or equipment you need to complete the drawing in the "assigned_tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your request.
  - This will help us to easily identify and reference your request.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
  - This is used to address your request and contact you for follow-up.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  - This is used to contact you about the status of your request.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This is used for additional contact or follow-up.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Describe your project in detail.
  - Provide as much information as possible to ensure we understand your needs.
- **Drawing Type** (`drawing_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of drawing you need.
  - 2D Drawing: A 2D representation of your project.
  - 3D Modeling: A 3D model of your project.
  - Other: If none of the above options apply, choose "Other" and provide a brief description.
- **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, select_multiple, optional): Check the documents you need.
  - Project Proposal: A proposal outlining your project scope and objectives.
  - Specifications: Detailed specifications for your project.
  - Other: If none of the above apply, choose "Other" and provide a brief description.
  - Attachments: Any additional files or information that will help us complete the drawing.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to submit the drawing.
  - This will help us schedule your drawing according to your needs.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to submit the drawing.
  - This will help us schedule your drawing according to your needs.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your request.
  - This is an opportunity to add any context or details that may be relevant to your request.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or equipment you need to complete the drawing.
  - This will help us ensure that we have the necessary resources to complete your request.
