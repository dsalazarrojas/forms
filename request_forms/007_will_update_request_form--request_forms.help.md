# Will Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Will Update Request Form is designed to collect information for will update requests, which typically involve updating existing records or data. This form ensures that all relevant information is accurately updated and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details in the text fields as required. For the `name` fields, please ensure that you provide your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field, following the standard email format (username @ domain).
3. If applicable, enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Select the relevant options for the `signed_authorization` field, confirming that you have signed the required agreements or confirm the relevant points.
5. Enter the date of the event or update in the `date` field, following the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
6. Click submit to send your request for updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **will_update_request_form**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of the update request.
- **name**: A text field where you can enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **name_2**: Another text field for additional name information.
- **email**: A field for your email address, following the standard email format (username @ domain).
- **phone**: A text field for your phone number.
- **date**: A date field where you can enter the date of the event or update, following the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
- **signed_authorization**: A multiple-selection field where you can confirm that you have signed the required agreements or confirm the relevant points, with options being 'Yes' and 'No'.
