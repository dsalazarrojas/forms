# realtor_review_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for realtors to collect feedback and reviews from clients on their performance. It helps realtors to evaluate and improve their communication style, response time, and overall service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the rector's name in the 'realtor name' field.
2. Choose the type of review (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) in the 'review type' dropdown.
3. Enter the rector's contact information in the 'realtor contact' field.
4. Enter the rector's email in the 'realtor email' field.
5. Enter the purchase price in the 'purchase price' field.
6. Enter feedback in the 'feedback' field.
7. Write a review in the 'review' field.
8. Enter the agent's name in the 'agent name' field.
9. Enter the agent's phone number in the 'agent phone' field.
10. Enter the agent's email in the 'agent email' field.
11. Enter the agent's agency in the 'agent agency' field.
12. Enter the purchased property in the 'purchased property' field.
13. Enter the purchase date in the 'purchase date' field.
14. Select whether the review was agreed upon by the rector in the 'review agent' field.
15. Select whether the review was agreed upon by the agent in the 'review agreed' field.
16. Select whether the review was reviewed in the 'reviewed' field.
17. Enter the rector's license in the 'realtor license' field.
18. Enter the date the review was agreed upon in the 'review agreed date' field.
19. Enter the date the review was reviewed in the 'reviewed date' field.
20. Enter the property that was reviewed in the 'reviewed property' field.
21. Enter the agent's name in the 'realtor agent' field.
22. Enter the date the review was reviewed by the agent in the 'review agent date' field.
23. Select the rector's name from the dropdown list in the 'realtor name' field.
