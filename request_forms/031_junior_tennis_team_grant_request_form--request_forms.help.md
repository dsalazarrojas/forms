# Junior Tennis Team Grant Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Tennis Team Grant Request Form is used by junior tennis teams to request funding for their activities, events, and initiatives. The form is reviewed and approved by the organization to ensure that the request meets the necessary criteria. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the team details section, which includes the team name, team leader's name and contact information, and team address. 
2. Enter the grant amount you are requesting and specify the purpose of the grant, such as team uniforms, tournament fees, coaching and training, or miscellaneous expenses. 
3. Select the type of grant you are requesting, which can be for equipment and supplies, team travel, team training, or miscellaneous expenses. 
4. Provide your bank account information, including the bank account name and account number. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your junior tennis team.
* **Team Lead**: Enter the name and contact information for the team leader.
* **Team Address**: Enter the team's address.
* **Grant Amount Request**: Enter the amount of money you are requesting as a grant.
* **Grant Use**: Briefly describe the purpose of the grant, such as team uniforms, tournament fees, coaching and training, or miscellaneous expenses.
* **Grant Start Date**: Enter the start date of the grant period.
* **Grant End Date**: Enter the end date of the grant period.
* **Bank Account Name**: Enter the name of your bank account.
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter your bank account number.
* **Grant Type**: Select the type of grant you are requesting, which can be for equipment and supplies, team travel, team training, or miscellaneous expenses.
* **Grant Purpose**: Select the specific reason for your grant request, which can be team uniforms, tournament fees, coaching and training, or miscellaneous expenses.
* **Grant Type Multiple**: Select any additional types of grants you are requesting, which can be equipment and supplies, team travel, team training, or miscellaneous expenses. 

## Tips
* Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Make sure to attach any necessary supporting documents or receipts for the grant request.
