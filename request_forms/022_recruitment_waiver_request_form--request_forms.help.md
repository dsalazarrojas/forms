# Recruitment Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Waiver Request Form is used to request a waiver for an application or a position. This form is completed by the applicant to provide supporting details and waiver requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the `applicant_details` field with your name, job title, and contact information.
2.  Select the relevant `waiver_requests` option from the list to specify the type of waiver you are requesting.
3.  Provide any supporting details in the `supporting_details` field.
4.  Choose the status of your waiver request in the `waiver_decision` field.
5.  If necessary, select a date and time in the `date_received` and `time_received` fields.
6.  Finally, leave a note for the recruiter in the `recruiter_note` field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, not required): Enter your request for a waiver, including the specific position or application you are inquiring about.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): Enter your name, job title, and contact information (email, phone number, etc.) to help us contact you.
* **Waiver Requests** (`waiver_requests`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to specify the type of waiver you are requesting (e.g. "Choice 1", "Choice 2", or "Choice 3").
* **Supporting Details** (`supporting_details`, text, not required): Provide any additional information that supports your request, such as previous work experience or references.
* **Waiver Decision** (`waiver_decision`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your waiver request (e.g. "Approve", "Reject", or "Review Later").
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, not required): Enter the date you submitted this request, if needed.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, time, not required): Enter the time you submitted this request, if needed.
* **Recruiter Note** (`recruiter_note`, note, not required): Leave a note for the recruiter, if needed, to explain your request or provide additional context.
