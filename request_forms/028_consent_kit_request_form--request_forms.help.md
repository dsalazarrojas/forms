# Consent Kit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consent Kit Request Form is designed to gather essential information from you to process and deliver the required consent kits accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, phone number, and organization information (if applicable) accurately and completely.
2. Select the type of consent kit you need from the available options.
3. Specify the quantity of kits required.
4. Clearly state the intended use and target population for the consent kits.
5. Choose your preferred delivery method and specify the shipping address, city, state/province, postal code, and country for delivery.
6. Indicate the urgency level of your request.
7. If customization is required, provide the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date when you are requesting the consent kit.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`): Select the type of organization (e.g., Healthcare Facility, Educational Institution, etc.).
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department or division if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your position or role if applicable.
* **Type of Consent Kit Requested** (`kit_type`): Select the type of consent kit you need (e.g., General Consent Kit, Medical Consent Kit, etc.).
* **Preferred Language** (`kit_language`): Select the language for the kit materials.
* **Other Language** (`other_language`): If you selected an other language, specify it here.
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`): Enter the number of kits you need.
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`): Clearly state how you plan to use the consent kits.
* **Target Population** (`target_population`): Specify the target population for the consent kits.
* **When Do You Need the Kit** (`timeline`): Enter the required delivery date.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`): Select the urgency level of your request (e.g., Routine, Standard, Urgent, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select your preferred delivery method (e.g., Physical Mail, Express Courier, etc.).
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter the shipping address for delivery.
* **City** (`shipping_city`): Enter the city for delivery.
* **State or Province** (`shipping_state`): Enter the state or province for delivery.
* **Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`): Enter the ZIP or postal code for delivery.
* **Country** (`shipping_country`): Enter the country for delivery.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`special_instructions`): If any special delivery instructions are required, enter them here.
* **Is Customization Needed** (`customization_needed`): Select if customization is required.
* **Customization Details** (`customization_details`): If customization is required, provide the necessary details.
* **Have You Requested Before** (`previous_requester`): Select if you have requested a kit previously.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`): Select the source of your information about this service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any other information that may be helpful.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`): Select the current status of your request (e.g., Submitted, Processing, Shipped, etc.).
