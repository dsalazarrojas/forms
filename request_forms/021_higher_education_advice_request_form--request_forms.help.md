# higher_education_advice_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students who are
seeking advice on their higher education path. It is designed to help
experts provide guidance on college and university selection,
admission, and financial aid processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your student information in the "Student Info" field.
2.  Share your academic achievements in the "Academic Achievements" field.
3.  Describe any special circumstances that may impact your education
    path in the "Special Circumstances" field.
4.  If applicable, provide details on any scholarships or financial aid
    you are seeking in the "Scholarship Info" field.
5.  Share information about your college application experience in the
    "College Application" field.
6.  Describe your education history in the "Education History" field.
7.  Share information about your family background in the "Family
    Background" field.
8.  Explain any personal circumstances that may impact your education
    path in the "Personal Circumstances" field.
9.  Finally, provide any information relevant to your college search
    experience in the "College Search" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your name, age, and contact information in this field.
* **Academic Achievements** (`academic_achievements`, text, required: false): Share your academic achievements, such as grades, awards, or notable projects.
* **College Info** (`college_info`, text, required: false): Describe the colleges or universities you are interested in or have applied to.
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, text, required: false): Explain any challenges or obstacles you are facing that may impact your education path.
* **Scholarship Info** (`scholarship_info`, text, required: false): If you are seeking financial aid, provide details on the scholarships or grants you are applying for.
* **College Application** (`college_application`, text, required: false): Share your experiences and challenges with the college application process.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, required: false): Describe your educational background, including any prior diplomas or certifications.
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, text, required: false): Share information about your family, such as parents' education level or occupation.
* **Personal Circumstances** (`personal_circumstances`, text, required: false): Explain any personal challenges or circumstances that may impact your education path.
* **College Search** (`college_search`, text, required: false): Provide any information relevant to your college search experience, including any colleges or universities you have visited or researched.
