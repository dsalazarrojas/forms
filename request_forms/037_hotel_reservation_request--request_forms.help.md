# Hotel Reservation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Request form is designed for guests to book a room at our hotel. To ensure that your request is processed correctly, please make sure you have provided all the required information.

### How To Complete This Form

To book a room at our hotel, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Select your arrival and departure dates.
3. Choose the number of guests and provide their names.
4. Specify any additional notes or requests you may have.
5. Select any extra services you require (e.g. room service, extra towels).
6. Confirm your selection of extra services.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Guests Name** (2): If booking for a group, please list all names separated by commas.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Arrival Date** (5): Select the date you will arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date** (6): Select the date you will depart from the hotel.
* **Room** (7): Please specify the type of room you would like to book (e.g. single, double, suite).
* **Additional Notes** (8): If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them here.
* **Name of Guests** (9): If booking for a group, please list all names separated by commas.
* **Number of Guests** (10): Please select the number of guests in your group.
* **Arrival Time** (11): If you plan to arrive at the hotel at a specific time, please select it here. This is optional.
* **Departure Time** (12): If you plan to depart from the hotel at a specific time, please select it here. This is optional.
* **Extra Services** (13): Please choose whether you require any extra services (e.g. room service, extra towels).
* **Extra Services Options** (14): Please select any additional extra services you require.
* **Field 15** (15): This field is not used and can be ignored.

### Tips

* Please make sure to provide all required information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the Additional Notes field.
* Please note that extra services are subject to availability and additional fees may apply.
