# Water Meter Calibration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request calibration of a water meter. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for the maintenance team to perform the calibration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide the water meter details in the Water Meter Details section.
3. Select the location where the water meter is installed.
4. Enter the date of the last calibration.
5. Enter the current meter reading.
6. Provide the serial number of the meter.
7. Specify the type of meter.
8. (Optional) Enter the meter model.
9. Provide your contact number.
10. Select the locations of the meter.
11. Enter any additional comments about the meter or calibration.
12. Enter your email address.
13. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This is the section where you will enter your details.
	+ **Name, Email, Contact Number**: Enter your name, email address, and contact number.
* **Water Meter Details**: This is the section where you will enter details about the water meter.
	+ **Serial Number, Meter Type, Meter Model**: Enter the serial number, type, and model of the meter.
* **Location**: Select the location where the meter is installed from the options provided.
* **Date of Calibration**: Enter the date of the last calibration performed on the meter.
* **Meter Reading**: Enter the current reading of the meter.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the meter.
* **Meter Type**: Specify the type of meter.
* **Meter Location**: Select the locations of the meter.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number or the contact number of the owner.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the meter or calibration.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to submit the form.
