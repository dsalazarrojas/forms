# gdlr_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gdlr_request_form is used to collect information from users who are requesting data for research purposes. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure that we can fulfill their requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
* Ensure that you are providing accurate information.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to sign the form with your signature, date, and company name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_details** (`request_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of the data you are requesting.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your name or the name of your company.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your contact information (phone number, email, etc.).
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ This field is for your work email address.
* **data_types** (`data_types`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the type(s) of data you are requesting (e.g., yes or no).
* **data_location** (`data_location`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for the location where you plan to use the data.
* **purpose_for_erasure** (`purpose_for_erasure`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for the purpose of your data request.
* **request_date** (`request_date`, date, required: false):
	+ This field is for the date you expect to receive the data.
* **signature** (`signature`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose whether you confirm that the information you provide is accurate.
* **signature_date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false):
	+ This field is for the date you sign the form.
* **signature_name** (`signature_name`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your name, to be signed on the form.
* **signature_title** (`signature_title`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your title or position.
* **signature_company** (`signature_company`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your company name.
* **signature_position** (`signature_position`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for your position or department.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to the best of your ability.
