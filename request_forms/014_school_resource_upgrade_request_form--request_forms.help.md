# School Resource Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Resource Upgrade Request Form is used to request upgrades to school resources, such as equipment or facilities. This form helps the school administration to track and prioritize resource upgrade requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. If you have any questions or need help, contact your school's resource upgrade team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill out your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill out your last name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Select the name of your school.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of resource you are requesting to upgrade (e.g., equipment, facilities).
* **Upgrade Description** (`upgrade_description`, text, required): Describe the specific resource you are requesting to upgrade.
* **Budget Estimate** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional): Provide an estimate of the budget required for the upgrade (if known).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, High).
* **School Contact** (`school_contact`, select_multiple, required): Choose the school contact person responsible for your request (e.g., Head of School, Department Chair, Teacher).
* **Facility Upgrade** (`facility_upgrade`, select_one, required): Select whether a facility upgrade is part of your request (Yes, No).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the resource you are requesting to upgrade.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your school's resource upgrade team for assistance.
