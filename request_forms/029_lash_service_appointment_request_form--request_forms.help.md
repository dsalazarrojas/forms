# Lash Service Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a lash service appointment for a client. It captures essential information required for the appointment to be booked and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Form Data" section with any relevant information about the appointment.
2. Enter the client's "Client Info" in the second section.
3. Select the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" for the appointment.
4. Choose the "Lash Technician" and "Service Type" for the appointment.
5. Enter the "Number of Appointments" and "Number of Sessions" required for the service.
6. Finally, select the "Length of Appointment" in minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to the appointment.
* **Client Info**: Enter the client's name, phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date for which the appointment is desired.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time slot for which the appointment is desired.
* **Lash Technician**: Select all technicians involved in the appointment, multiple selections are possible.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of lash service desired.
* **Number of Appointments**: Enter the number of appointments required.
* **Number of Sessions**: Enter the number of sessions.
* **Length of Appointment**: Enter the duration of the appointment in minutes.
