# Invoice Payment Acceleration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Payment Acceleration Request Form is used to request expedited payment to a vendor for an invoice that is due soon. This form helps to gather necessary information to facilitate the acceleration of payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Enter the vendor's information, including their name, email address, and any necessary documents.
* Select the payment method and term, as well as the submission status.
* Enter the date and time of submission.
* Review and confirm your entry before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information (vendor_info)**: Enter the vendor's name and any relevant details.
* **Invoice Information (invoice_info)**: Provide the invoice number, date, and due date to justify the payment acceleration request.
* **Payment Information (payment_info)**: Specify the amount to be paid and any relevant notes or comments.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Select the payment term that applies (e.g., net 30).
* **Submission Workflow (submission_workflow)**: Choose the submission workflow status (e.g., Draft, Published, Submitted).
* **Vendor Documents (vendor_documents)**: Attach any necessary documents from the vendor.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method (e.g., bank transfer, credit card).
* **Payment Term (payment_term)**: Select the payment term that applies (e.g., net 15, net 30).
* **Submission Workflow Status (submission_workflow_status)**: Choose the submission status that applies.
* **Vendor Email (vendor_email)**: Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)**: Enter the time of submission.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing the payment acceleration request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
