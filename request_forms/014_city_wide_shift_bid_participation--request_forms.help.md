# City Wide Shift Bid Participation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Wide Shift Bid Participation form is used to collect information about employees' shift preferences for the purpose of shift assignment and scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose your preferred shifts for each choice field (First, Second, and Third Choice Shift).
4. Select your preferred weekend rotation (if applicable).
5. Provide a reason for your bid request (if applicable).
6. Confirm your willingness to work overtime.
7. Confirm that you understand how bids are awarded.
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, Text, Required): Enter your employee ID number as assigned by HR.
* **Current Department** (`department_name`, Select One, Required): Select your current department from the provided options.
* **Current Assigned Shift** (`current_shift_title`, Text, Required): Enter your current assigned shift title.
* **Seniority Date** (`seniority_date`, Date, Required): Enter your seniority date, used for bid ranking.
* **First Choice Shift** (`first_choice_shift`, Select One, Required): Select your first preferred shift from the provided options.
* **Second Choice Shift** (`second_choice_shift`, Select One, Required): Select your second preferred shift from the provided options.
* **Third Choice Shift** (`third_choice_shift`, Select One, Required): Select your third preferred shift from the provided options.
* **Preferred Weekend Rotation** (`weekend_preference`, Select One, Optional): Select your preferred weekend rotation (if applicable).
* **Reason for Bid Request** (`shift_bid_reason`, Text, Optional): Provide a reason for your bid request (if applicable).
* **Willingness to Work Overtime** (`overtime_willingness`, Select One, Required): Indicate your willingness to work overtime if needed.
* **Understanding of Bidding Process** (`bidding_agreement`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand how bids are awarded based on seniority and operational needs.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_timestamp`, Date, Required): The date and time the form is submitted.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select only one option for each choice field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, consult your supervisor or HR representative.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
