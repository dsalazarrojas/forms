# Marketing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit marketing requests to our team. Please use this form for all marketing-related inquiries, such as requesting marketing activities, product promotions, or event planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the date for your request from the calendar in the "Request Date" field.
6. Select the time for your request from the time picker in the "Request Time" field.
7. Choose a specific type of marketing request from the options provided in the "Request Type" field.
8. Provide a brief message describing your marketing request in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to help our team address you personally and efficiently process your request.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to help our team tailor their response to your specific needs.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address to enable our team to contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to allow our team to contact you directly about your request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Select the date on which you'd like to fulfill your marketing request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, not required): Select the time on which you'd like to fulfill your marketing request. If not selected, it will be fulfilled at a time convenient to our team.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Marketing Activities
	+ Product Promotion
	+ Event Planning
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Provide a brief and clear message describing your marketing request. This will help our team understand your needs and fulfill your request efficiently.
