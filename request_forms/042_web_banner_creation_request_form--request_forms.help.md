> # Web Banner Creation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams create web banners for various purposes. It captures essential information required for web banner creation, including image, title, description, and other relevant details. Fill out this form to submit your request and our team will create an engaging and effective web banner for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the required information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, including the image, title, description, and target URL.
3. Make sure to provide accurate information, especially the image file name and URL.
4. Review and adjust your answers as needed before submitting the form.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at: form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: Provide a brief description of the web banner you are requesting. This will help our team understand the context and requirements of your request.
* **Banner Image**: Upload or provide the image file name and URL that you would like to use for the web banner.
* **Banner Title**: Enter a short and catchy title for your web banner. This will be displayed on the banner and help grab the attention of your audience.
* **Banner Description**: Write a short and descriptive text that explains what your web banner is about.
* **Target URL**: Enter the URL where your web banner will be displayed. This is where our team will find the relevant information for the banner's content and context.
* **Banner Image Alt Text**: Provide a descriptive text for the banner image, this will be used for accessibility purposes.
* **Requestor Name**: Please enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Requestor Email**: Enter your email address so our team can contact you for further information or clarification.
* **Team Review Comments**: If you have any additional comments or instructions for our team, you can provide them here.
* **Assigned Designer**: Choose a designer that you would like to work on your project from the options listed. Our team will assign them to work on your web banner creation.
