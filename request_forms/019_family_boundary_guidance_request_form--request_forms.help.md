# Family Boundary Guidance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Boundary Guidance Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking guidance on family boundaries. This form will help us understand your concerns, challenges, or needs, allowing us to provide the most appropriate support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Date of Birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
4. Add a valid Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your first name, which will help us understand your identity. Enter your first name as it is commonly known to you or your family.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your last name, which will help us confirm your identity. Enter your last name as it is commonly known to you or your family.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 02/12/1990). This will help us understand your age and potentially relevant circumstances.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your request. This will ensure we can send you the necessary support and guidance.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is for your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed. Enter a number that we can use to reach you efficiently.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter your information accurately and thoroughly, as this will help us provide the best possible support.
- If you have any attachments or additional documentation that may help us better understand your request, please include them with your form submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our support team.
