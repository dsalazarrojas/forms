# Roadside Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roadside Assistance Request Form is a tool used to gather information from users who need roadside assistance services. This form helps our team to understand the user's requirements and provide the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of assistance you need from the options provided in the "Request Type" field. Please choose only one option.
2. Provide your vehicle's registration number in the "Vehicle Registration" field.
3. Describe the issue with your vehicle in the "Description" field.
4. Enter your address, phone number, and contact person's name in their respective fields.
5. Choose the location where the service is required ("On-Site", "Remote", or "Both").
6. Select the service type ("Roadside Service", "Towing Service", or "Roadside and Towing Service").
7. Choose whether the service is required locally or across a longer distance ("Local", "Long Distance", or "Both").
8. Provide a message for our team to communicate with you effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Registration** (`Vehicle Registration`, `text`, required): Please enter your vehicle's registration number for our records.
* **Request Type** (`Request Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of assistance you need. Please select only one option.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Describe the issue with your vehicle in detail.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your address for our team to locate you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.
* **Location** (`Location`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose where the service is required ("On-Site", "Remote", or "Both").
* **Estimated Time** (`Estimated Time`, `time`, optional): If you know the estimated time required for the service, please provide it.
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of service you need ("Roadside Service", "Towing Service", or "Roadside and Towing Service").
* **Service Area** (`Service Area`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether the service is required locally or across a longer distance ("Local", "Long Distance", or "Both").
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person our team should contact.
* **Service Time** (`Service Time`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred time for the service, please provide it.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Service Address** (`Service Address`, `text`, optional): If your service area is a specific location, please enter it.
* **Service Phone** (`Service Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the service provider's phone number.
* **Service Email** (`Service Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the service provider's email address.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, optional): Provide a message for our team to communicate with you effectively.

Note: The form's fields and options are self-explanatory. If you have any confusion or need further assistance, please contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
