# LLC Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LLC Extension Request Form is a form designed to collect information from users for the purpose of requesting an LLC (Limited Liability Company) extension. This form is used by the authorities to process and review the request, ensuring that the necessary information is collected and verified before making a decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2.  Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3.  Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Enter your license number in the "License Number" field.
7.  If you have any supporting documents, upload them in the "Supporting Documents" field. This is optional.
8.  Enter your authorization in the "Authorization" field.
9.  If you are requesting a deadline, select the date for the "Deadline" field.
10.  If you need to follow up, select the time for the "Follow-up" field.
11.  Select the type of your company from the options provided in the "Company Type" field.
12.  Enter the reason for your LLC extension request in the "Reason" field.
13.  Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field. This is optional.
14.  Enter the form ID, which will be provided to you.
15.  Enter the name of the tool assigned to you for this task, if applicable.
16.  Enter the date and time when you submitted the form in the "Submitted At" and "Submitted Time" fields. These fields will be automatically populated.
17.  Enter your username in the "Submitted By" field.
18.  If the form has been approved, enter the date and time when it was approved in the "Approved At" and "Approved Time" fields.
19.  If the form has been approved by someone, enter their name in the "Approved By" field.
20.  If you have any comments, enter them in the "Comments" field. This is optional.
21.  Select the status of the form from the options provided in the "Status" field.
22.  Enter the name of the person or department this form was submitted to in the "Submitted To" field.
23.  Enter the date and time when you submitted the form in the "Submitted Date" and "Submitted Time" fields. These fields will be automatically populated.
24.  Enter your username in the "Submitted User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **License Number** (text, required): Enter your license number.
* **Supporting Documents** (text, optional): If you have any supporting documents, upload them here.
* **Authorization** (text, required): Enter your authorization.
* **Deadline** (date, optional): If you need a specific deadline, select the date.
* **Follow-up** (time, optional): If you need to follow up, select the time.
* **Company Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of your company (Public, Private, or Both).
* **Reason** (text, required): Enter the reason for your LLC extension request.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Form ID** (text, required): Enter the provided form ID.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): If assigned, enter the tool.
* **Submitted At** (date, optional): This field will be automatically populated.
* **Submitted By** (text, optional): This field will be automatically populated.
* **Approved At** (date, optional): If the form has been approved, enter the date and time.
* **Approved By** (text, optional): If the form has been approved by someone, enter their name.
* **Comments** (text, optional): If you have any comments, enter them here.
* **Status** (select_one, required): Select the status of the form (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitted To** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person or department this form was submitted to.
* **Submitted Date** (date, optional): This field will be automatically populated.
* **Submitted Time** (time, optional): This field will be automatically populated.
* **Submitted User** (text, optional): Enter your username.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out to submit the form. The fields that are optional can be left blank, but filling out any information in these fields can provide more context to your request.
