# Workstation Privacy Shield Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workstation Privacy Shield Enrollment Form is used to collect information about workstations and their associated privacy shield enrollments. This form helps ensure that workstations are properly configured with the necessary privacy settings, which is crucial for maintaining employee data protection and compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Workstation location
	* Workstation ownership
	* Employee ID
	* Workstation ID
	* Department
	* Building location
	* Room number
	* Employee name
	* Reason for request
2. Select the relevant options for:
	* Workstation location
	* Workstation ownership
	* Approval required
	* Workstation privacy shield enrolled
	* Workstation privacy shield activated
	* Workstation privacy shield expires at
	* Workstation privacy shield renewal status
3. Enter any additional information in the notes fields:
	* Notes
	* Workstation privacy shield renewal notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workstation location**: Select the location where the workstation is situated.
* **Workstation ownership**: Choose the department or entity that owns the workstation.
* **Workstation ownership**: Select the correct ownership type (e.g., Employee prophets company, IT Department, Facility).
* **Request date**: Enter the date when the workstation privacy shield was requested.
* **Request time**: Enter the time when the workstation privacy shield was requested.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee ID associated with the workstation.
* **Employee name**: Enter the name of the employee associated with the workstation.
* **Workstation ID**: Enter the ID of the workstation.
* **Department**: Enter the department associated with the workstation.
* **Building location**: Enter the location of the building where the workstation is situated.
* **Room number**: Enter the room number where the workstation is located.
* **Reason for request**: Enter a brief explanation for the reason the workstation privacy shield was requested.
* **Approval required**: Choose whether approval is required for the workstation privacy shield enrollment.
* **Workstation privacy shield enrolled**: Select whether the workstation has a privacy shield enrollment.
* **Workstation privacy shield activated**: Select whether the workstation's privacy shield is activated.
* **Workstation privacy shield expires at**: Select when the workstation's privacy shield will expire.
* **Workstation privacy shield renewal date**: Enter the date when the workstation's privacy shield needs to be renewed.
* **Workstation privacy shield renewal time**: Enter the time when the workstation's privacy shield needs to be renewed.
* **Workstation privacy shield renewal status**: Select the status of the workstation's privacy shield renewal.
* **Workstation privacy shield renewal notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the workstation's privacy shield renewal.
* **Workstation privacy shield replacement status**: Select whether the workstation's privacy shield replacement status is active.
