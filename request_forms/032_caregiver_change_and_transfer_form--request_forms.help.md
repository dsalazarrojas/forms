# Caregiver Change and Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caregiver Change and Transfer Form is used to request a change in the care plan for a client, typically when there is a need to replace or modify the current caregiver.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. If you are submitting on behalf of the client, please ensure that the client's name and ID number are included.
3. Select the correct options for "Requesting Party" and "Client Preference Regarding Change" to indicate who is making the request and the client's wishes.
4. Choose the correct "Type of Change Requested" option that best describes the reason for the change.
5. Ensure that the "Current Caregiver" and "New Caregiver" fields are filled out accurately, if applicable.
6. In the "Reason for Change Request" field, provide a brief explanation of why the change is being requested.
7. Choose the correct method of notification for the client and the agency or care provider.
8. Select the correct location where care documentation is kept.
9. Review the form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client or Patient Name (1)**: The name of the person receiving care.
* **Client ID Number (2)**: The patient or account number associated with the client.
* **Date of Request (3)**: The date on which the change request is being submitted.
* **Effective Date of Change (4)**: The date on which the change should take effect.
* **Requesting Party (5)**: The party making the request, which can be a client or family member, healthcare provider, care coordinator, or agency staff.
* **Name of Person Making Request (6)**: The name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Phone Number (7)**: The contact phone number of the person making the request.
* **Type of Change Requested (8)**: The reason for the change request, such as substituting a caregiver temporarily or permanently.
* **Current Caregiver Name (9)**: The name of the caregiver currently providing care.
* **Current Caregiver ID (10)**: The employee or staff number of the current caregiver.
* **New Caregiver Name (11)**: The name of the proposed new caregiver.
* **New Caregiver ID (12)**: The employee or staff number of the proposed new caregiver.
* **Reason for Change Request (13)**: A brief explanation of why the change is being requested.
* **Client Preference Regarding Change (14)**: The client's wishes regarding the change, which can be client requested, approved, neutral, or opposed.
* **Has Family Been Notified? (15)**: Whether the family has been notified of the change request.
* **Agency or Provider Approval Status (16)**: The current status of the change request in the agency or care provider's system.
* **If Temporary - Substitute Caregiver Details (17)**: If the change is temporary, provide the name and availability of the substitute caregiver.
* **Transition Plan (18)**: A description of how the change will be implemented and transitioned.
* **Overlap Period or Training Needed (19)**: Whether the caregivers will work together during the transition period.
* **Overlap Duration (20)**: The duration of the transition period.
* **Services to Continue Uninterrupted (21)**: The services that must not be disrupted during the transition.
* **Special Instructions for New Caregiver (22)**: Important information for the incoming caregiver.
* **How Will Client Be Notified? (23)**: How the client will be informed of the change.
* **Location of Client Care Documentation (24)**: The location where care documentation is kept.
* **I Authorize This Change Request (25)**: A confirmation that the requestor authorizes the change.
* **Additional Notes or Special Considerations (26)**: Any other important information or special considerations.
