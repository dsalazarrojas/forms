# writ_of_garnishment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The writ of garnishment request form is used to request a writ of garnishment for a specific purpose, such as to satisfy a debt or other financial obligation. This form is typically used by a financial institution or a court to gather information about the person requesting the writ and the details of the garnishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the request form with your details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the correct garnishment type (e.g., Garnishment, Other).
3. Choose the reason for the garnishment (e.g., Court ordered, Bankruptcy, Other).
4. Enter the amount to be garnished.
5. If applicable, provide the bankruptcy code and court filing date.
6. Upload any supporting documents, such as the garnishment document and address.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments.
