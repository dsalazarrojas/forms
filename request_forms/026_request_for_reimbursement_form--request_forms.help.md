# Request For Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement for company-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the member requesting reimbursement.
2. Enter the name of the item or service you are requesting reimbursement for.
3. Enter the date you made the purchase or incurred the expense.
4. Enter the amount you spent on the item or service.
5. Provide a brief reason for the purchase or expense.
6. Upload a copy of your receipt for the purchase or expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the member requesting reimbursement.
- **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the item or service you are requesting reimbursement for.
- **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, required): Enter the date you made the purchase or incurred the expense.
- **Amount Spent** (`amount_spent`, number, required): Enter the exact amount you spent on the item or service.
- **Reason for Purchase** (`reason_for_purchase`, text, required): Provide a brief reason for the purchase or expense.
- **Receipt Upload** (`receipt_upload`, note, required): Upload a copy of your receipt for the purchase or expense. Please ensure that the receipt is clear and legible.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Once you have completed all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your request for reimbursement.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have not already submitted a request for the same expense.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing the reason for the purchase or expense.
- Upload a clear and legible copy of your receipt to help with the reimbursement process.
