# payment_exception_escalation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit payment exceptions for escalation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant payment details from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the payment date and time if applicable.
3. Input the amount related to the payment exception.
4. Provide a detailed description of the payment exception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Exception Details** (`payment_exception_details`, text, **optional**): This field allows you to describe the payment exception in as much detail as possible.
  - Enter a brief summary or description of the payment exception.
- **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, **optional**): Select the type of payment that is related to the payment exception.
  - Choose the correct payment type from the drop-down menu.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the payment that is related to the payment exception.
  - Use the date format to enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of the payment that is related to the payment exception.
  - Use the time format to enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, **optional**): Enter the amount related to the payment exception.
  - Enter the amount in the currency of the payment.
- **Description** (`description`, text, **optional**): This field is for you to provide any additional information about the payment exception.
  - Enter as much detail as possible to help in resolving the issue.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure efficient resolution of the payment exception.
- If unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
- This form is a one-time submission, and once submitted, the payment exception will be escalated to the relevant team for further action.
