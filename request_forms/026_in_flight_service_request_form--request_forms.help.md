# in_flight_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The in-flight service request form is designed to collect information from passengers about services required during a flight.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Make sure to provide all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the introduction page of the form. It is not a required field.

### Passenger Details
* **Passenger Details** (`passenger_details`, text, optional): Provide your details, including your name and any other relevant information.

### Service Request
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, select one, optional): Select the type of service you require:
	+ Urgent
	+ Non-Urgent

### Service Requested
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, text, optional): Describe the service you need.

### Flight Number
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter your flight number.

### Flight Date
* **Flight Date** (`flight_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your flight.

### Flight Time
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your flight.

### Seat Number
* **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, number, optional): Enter your seat number.

### Passenger Count
* **Passenger Count** (`passenger_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of passengers traveling with you.

### Flight Status
* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`, select one, optional): Select the current status of your flight:
	+ Departing
	+ Delayed
	+ Arrived

### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of service you require:
	+ Catering
	+ Cabin Crew
	+ Other

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your service request.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Submit your form after filling out all the required fields.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible to ensure that the airline can provide the necessary services.
