# corporate_transformation_plan_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate transformation plan request form is used to collect information about corporate transformation projects within the company. This form helps ensure that all the necessary details are captured and aligned with the company's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To fill out this form, please make sure you have reviewed the information carefully and are aware that it will be used for the planning and tracking of your transformation project.
2. Start by filling out the "Transformation Objectives" field with a brief description of your project's goals and objectives.
3. In the "Focus Areas" field, select the areas of the company that this transformation project will focus on.
4. If applicable, select the "Transformation Type" and "Stakeholders" fields to identify the type of transformation and the team members involved.
5. Enter the "Timeline Start Date" and "Timeline End Date" fields to establish the duration of your project.
6. If applicable, enter the "Budget" field to provide an estimate of the project's budget.
7. Finally, review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transformation Objectives (Text)** (`Transformation Objectives`, `required`, `true`): A brief description of your project's goals and objectives.
* **Focus Areas (Select Multiple)** (`Focus Areas`, `optional`, `false`): Select the areas of the company that this transformation project will focus on.
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
* **Timeline (Date)** (`Timeline`, `optional`, `false`): Enter the start and end dates of your project.
* **Budget (Number)** (`Budget`, `optional`, `false`): Enter an estimate of your project's budget.
* **Transformation Type (Select One)** (`Transformation Type`, `optional`, `false`): Select the type of transformation your project represents (Internal or External).
  - Internal
  - External
* **Stakeholders (Select Multiple)** (`Stakeholders`, `optional`, `false`): Select the team members involved in your project.
  - PMO
  - Leadership
  - Team
* **Start Date (Date)** (`Start Date`, `optional`, `false`): Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date (Date)** (`End Date`, `optional`, `false`): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Budget Value (Number)** (`Budget Value`, `optional`, `false`): Enter the value of your project's budget.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure all the necessary information is captured.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your project manager or the corporate transformation team.
* This form is only intended for corporate transformation projects and not for other types of projects.
