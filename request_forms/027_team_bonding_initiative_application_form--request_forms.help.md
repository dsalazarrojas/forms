# Team Bonding Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Bonding Initiative Application Form is a tool for team members or managers to propose team bonding activities that promote collaboration, productivity, and team building within their team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the activity name and date in the respective fields.
2. Select the activity type from the provided options.
3. Enter the number of participants and budget for the activity.
4. Add any additional comments or information about the activity.
5. Confirm your email address and phone number (for communication with HR).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Bonding Initiative Application (Page 1, team_bonding_initiative_application_form_page_1)**: Enter a brief description of the proposed team bonding activity.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Activity Name (team_bonding_initiative_activity_name)**: Enter the name of the activity to be held.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Activity Date (team_bonding_initiative_activity_date)**: Enter the date the activity will take place.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Location (team_bonding_initiative_location)**: Select the location of the activity from the provided options.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Number of Participants (team_bonding_initiative_number_of_participants)**: Enter the number of team members participating in the activity.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Budget (team_bonding_initiative_budget)**: Enter the estimated budget for the activity.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Comments (team_bonding_initiative_comments)**: Add any additional comments or information about the activity.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Manager Comments (team_bonding_initiative_manager_comments)**: Add any comments or information from the team manager.
* **Team Bonding Initiative HR Approvals (team_bonding_initiative_hr_approvals)**: Select the status of your HR approval.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Email (team_bonding_initiative_email)**: Enter your email address for HR to contact.
* **Team Bonding Initiative Phone (team_bonding_initiative_phone)**: Enter your phone number for HR to contact.

## Tips
* Ensure that the activity aligns with the organization's goals and objectives.
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
