# Service Downgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests to downgrades a service. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page labeled "Downgrade Details" and enter the necessary information about the service you want to downgrades.
2. On the second page, select the reason for your service downgrade request.
3. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or information you'd like to include with your request.
4. On the next page, select the approval for your comments.
5. Finally, click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Downgrade Details** (`downgrade_details`, text, optional): This is the main section for providing details about the service you want to downgrades. Please enter the necessary information here.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Select the reason for your service downgrade request from the available options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to include with your request.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_one, required): Select the approval for your comments.
* **Comments Approval** (`comments_approval`, select_one, required): Select the approval for your comments.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your service downgrade request.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us process your request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* We will review and process your request as soon as possible.
