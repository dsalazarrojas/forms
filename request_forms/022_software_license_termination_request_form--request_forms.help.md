# Software License Termination Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the termination of a software license for an employee's account. It helps to gather necessary information for a smooth termination process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
3. Enter your employee title in the `employee_title` field.
4. Describe the reason for termination in the `reason_for_termination` field.
5. Select the department associated with your termination in the `department` field.
6. Provide any comments about the termination in the `comments` field (optional).
7. Verify that you have received confirmation of termination by selecting one or more options from the `termination_confirmation` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **software_license_termination_request_form** (`software_license_termination_request_form`, `text`, required: false): This is the form title.
* **request_reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for requesting license termination. This field should describe why you are requesting termination and any relevant details that support your request.
* **license_details** (`license_details`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the license you are terminating.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your employee name as registered on your profile.
* **employee_title** (`employee_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your employee title as registered on your profile.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's name who has been assigned to you.
* **manager_title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's title who has been assigned to you.
* **termination_date** (`termination_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of termination, if known.
* **reason_for_termination** (`reason_for_termination`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for termination. You can refer to the provided options for guidance.
* **termination_confirmation** (`termination_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options to confirm the termination status:
	+ Employee has cancelled request
	+ IT has confirmed termination
	+ Other - please provide reason
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the termination.
* **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department associated with the termination.

## Tips

* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check the provided options for `termination_confirmation` before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or questions, refer to your supervisor or the IT department.
* Complete the form before proceeding with the termination.
