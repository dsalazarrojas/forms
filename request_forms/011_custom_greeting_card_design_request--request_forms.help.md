# custom-greeting-card-design-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to request a custom-designed greeting card for special occasions or events. Please fill out the form to specify your preferences and ensure that your card is designed according to your wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select your preferred style options:
	* Style Preferences: Choose the style of your custom greeting card from the options provided.
2. Choose your card type:
	* Card Type: Select the type of card you want (e.g., yes or no).
3. Specify the card size:
	* Card Size: Choose the size of your card (e.g., yes or no).
4. Select your message type:
	* Message Type: Choose the type of message you want to include in your card.
5. Select your font style:
	* Font Style: Choose the font style of your card.
6. Enter a personal message:
	* Card Message: Type in the message you want to include in your card.
7. Provide delivery details (optional):
	* Delivery Details: If you want to include any special delivery instructions, type them in this field.
8. Set a delivery date (optional):
	* Delivery Date: If you want to schedule the card to be delivered on a specific date, select it from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Style Preferences** (select_multiple): Choose the style of your custom greeting card from the options provided.
* **Card Type** (select_one): Select the type of card you want (e.g., yes or no).
* **Card Size** (select_one): Choose the size of your card (e.g., yes or no).
* **Message Type** (select_one): Choose the type of message you want to include in your card.
* **Font Style** (select_one): Choose the font style of your card.
* **Card Message** (text): Type in the message you want to include in your card.
* **Delivery Details** (note): If you want to include any special delivery instructions, type them in this field.
* **Delivery Date** (date): If you want to schedule the card to be delivered on a specific date, select it from the calendar.
