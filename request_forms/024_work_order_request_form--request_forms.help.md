# Work Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Request Form is a simple form designed to gather essential information about customer requests.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields respectively.
2. Select the type of service required from the "Service Type" field.
3. Provide the location where the service is needed in the "Location" field.
4. Give a brief description of the service required in the "Description" field.
5. If applicable, specify the date and time for the service in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
6. Add any additional contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
7. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
This is the field for your name.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
This is the field for your email address.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, text, required: false): 
Select the type of service you are requesting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): 
This is the field to enter the location where the service is needed.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): 
Give a brief description of the service required.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): 
Enter the date you would like the service to be done on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): 
Enter the time you would like the service to be done at.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): 
Add any additional contact information that may be helpful for us to know about.
 
## Tips
* Double-check your email for correct formatting before submitting.
* Make sure to include all relevant information for prompt service.
* Please be as specific as possible with your service type, location, and schedule.
* If you have any questions or need help with your form submission, feel free to contact us.
