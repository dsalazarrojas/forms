# Business Communication Phrase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Communication Phrase Request Form is designed to help create tailored communication phrases for various contexts and target audiences. This form will guide the user through a series of questions to gather essential information about the requester, the communication context, and the desired outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Requester Details section with your name and email address.
2. Select the communication context where the phrase will be used (e.g., Email, Marketing Materials, etc.).
3. Choose the desired tone and audience for the phrase.
4. Enter the phrase subject or topic.
5. Describe the desired outcome of the phrase (e.g., Convince, Inform, etc.).
6. Provide the most important message to convey.
7. Select the languages needed for the phrase.
8. Check if cultural sensitivity is required.
9. Choose the preferred phrase length.
10. Add any additional context or requirements.
11. Select the desired timeline for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Communication Context** (`communication_context`, `select_one`, required): Choose where the phrase will be used (e.g., Email, Marketing Materials, etc.).
* **Desired Tone** (`tone_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the tone you want to convey (e.g., Professional and Formal, Friendly and Approachable, etc.).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, required): Choose who will see the phrase (e.g., Customers, Potential Clients, etc.).
* **Phrase Subject or Topic** (`phrase_subject`, `text`, required): Describe the main topic or subject of the phrase.
* **Current Phrase or Statement** (`current_phrase`, `text`, optional): If you currently use a phrase or statement, enter it here for reference.
* **Desired Outcome** (`desired_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the desired outcome of the phrase (e.g., Convince, Inform, etc.).
* **Key Message to Convey** (`key_message`, `text`, required): Enter the most important message to convey.
* **Languages Needed** (`languages_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the languages required for the phrase.
* **Cultural Sensitivity Required** (`cultural_sensitivity`, `text`, optional): Check if cultural sensitivity is necessary.
* **Phrase Length Preference** (`length_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred phrase length (e.g., One Liner, Medium Phrase, etc.).
* **Additional Context or Requirements** (`additional_context`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional context or requirements.
* **Timeline for Delivery** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired timeline for delivery (e.g., Urgent, This Week, etc.).
