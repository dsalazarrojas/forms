# disneyland_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your trip to Disneyland. Please fill out the form with your details so we can assist you with planning and booking your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select your destination (Disneyland) from the drop down menu.
2. Enter your travel dates (from and to dates in YYYY-MM-DD format).
3. Enter the number of guests in your party.
4. Fill in your first name.
5. Fill in your last name.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Choose a travel agent from the dropdown menu:
	* Jimmy
	* Jane
	* John
9. If you have any additional information to provide, please enter it in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Destination**: Enter the name of the destination, which is Disneyland.
- **Travel Dates**: Enter your travel dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people in your party.
- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Travel Agent**: Select a travel agent to assist you with your trip:
	+ Jimmy
	+ Jane
	+ John
- **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your trip.
