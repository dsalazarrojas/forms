# Concierge Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concierge Request Form is designed to help guests communicate their personal requests and preferences for the concierge service. This form will guide you through the process of submitting your request, and each section of the form will provide further information on what information is required and why it's necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Select the category of service you need.
2. Enter the date you need the service.
3. Choose the time you prefer.
4. Indicate the urgency level of your request.
5. Provide your guest information (name, contact details, and room number if applicable).
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Specify any additional services you may require.
8. Confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Type
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, required): This is the category of service you need. Please select one of the options provided, such as Restaurant Reservations, Transportation Services, Event Tickets, Tour Booking, Shopping Assistance, Personal Services, Business Services, or Other.
### Request Date
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, required): This is the date you need the service. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY, with the first three digits being the month, day of the month, and year, respectively.
### Request Time
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, optional): This is your preferred time for the service. If you have a specific time in mind, you can enter it in 24-hour format.
### Urgency Level
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, required): This is how urgent you need the service. Please select one of the options provided: Low No Rush, Medium Within 24 Hours, High Same Day, or Urgent Immediate.
### Guest Information
* **Guest Information** (`guest_information`, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional details about yourself and your preferences.
### Guest Name
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, required): This is your full name, which will be used to address you by the concierge team.
### Room Number
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, optional): If you are staying in a room, please enter the room number to help the concierge team locate you more efficiently.
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, required): This is your contact email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, required): This is your contact phone number, which will be used for communication and confirmation.
### Stay Dates
* **Stay Dates** (`stay_dates`, optional): If you are staying for an extended period, please provide the dates you will be staying.
### Service Details
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, optional): If you have any specific requirements or requests for the service, please provide them here.
### Number of Guests
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, optional): If you are booking for a group, please indicate the number of guests.
### Preferred Vendor
* **Preferred Vendor** (`preferred_vendor`, optional): If you have a preferred vendor or service provider, you can specify it here.
### Budget Range
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, optional): Please select one of the budget options provided to indicate your estimated budget for the service.
### Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them here.
### Dietary Restrictions
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions for the service, please indicate them here.
### Accessibility Needs
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): If you have any mobility or accessibility requirements, please specify them here.
### Additional Services
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, optional): If you require any additional services, please select them from the options provided.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, optional): Please select one of the payment options provided to indicate how you will pay for the service.
### Confirmation Preference
* **Confirmation Preference** (`confirmation_preference`, required): Please select one of the options provided to indicate how you would like to receive confirmation of your request.
### Previous Similar Requests
* **Previous Similar Requests** (`previous_requests`, optional): If you have used this service before, please indicate if it was the same or a different service.
### Referral Source
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, optional): If you were referred to this service by someone, please indicate the source.
### Feedback Consent
* **Feedback Consent** (`feedback_consent`, optional): If you are willing to provide feedback, please indicate your preference here.
### Terms Agreement
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, required): Please accept the concierge terms and conditions by selecting the option provided.
