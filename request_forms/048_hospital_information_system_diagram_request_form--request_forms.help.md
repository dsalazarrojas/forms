# Hospital Information System Diagram Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps hospital staff and management assess and improve the accuracy, completeness, and up-to-dateness of the hospital's information system diagram.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the status of your hospital's information system diagram:
	* Select "Yes" if the diagram is up-to-date, accurate, and complete.
	* Select "No" if the diagram is not up-to-date, accurate, or complete.
2. Enter your name in the text field.
3. Enter the name of your department in the text field.
4. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the information system diagram in the text field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the text field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Information System Diagram Request** (`id: 1`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field asks whether your hospital's information system diagram is up-to-date, accurate, and complete.
	+ Select "Yes" for "yes" and "No" for "no".
* **Name** (`id: 2`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name for contact and reference purposes.
* **Department** (`id: 3`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of your department or unit.
* **Contact Person** (`id: 4`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the person responsible for the information system diagram.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`id: 6`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact email address.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to help improve the hospital's information system diagram.
- If you select "No" for "Is the hospital's information system diagram up-to-date, accurate, and complete?", please provide additional comments or explanations for further clarification.
