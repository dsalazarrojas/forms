# Audio Visual Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Visual Setup Request Form is used to request audio-visual equipment and technical services for events. This form collects information from the requestor and event organizers to ensure that the AV team can provide the right equipment and support for each event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address in the **Full Name**, **Department or Team**, and **Email Address** fields.
2. Enter the venue or room number where the event will take place in the **Venue or Room Number** field.
3. Choose the primary type of setup required for your event from the **Primary Type of Setup** dropdown menu (e.g., Standard Presentation, Panel Discussion, Hybrid Meeting, etc.).
4. Select any additional specialized technical needs from the **Specialized Technical Needs** dropdown menu (e.g., Additional Power Strips, Hardwired Ethernet, etc.).
5. Indicate if an on-site technician is needed during the event in the **Is an on-site technician needed during the event?** dropdown menu.
6. Provide any specific layout instructions or additional furniture needs in the **Specific Layout Instructions** and **Additional Furniture Needs** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the requestor.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Venue or Room Number** (`venue_room_number`, text, required): Enter the venue or room number where the event will take place.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Setup Completion Deadline** (`setup_deadline`, time, required): Enter the date and time when the setup should be completed.
* **Primary Type of Setup** (`setup_type`, select_one, required): Choose the primary type of setup required for your event.
* **Specialized Technical Needs** (`specialized_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional specialized technical needs for your event.
* **Is an on-site technician needed during the event?** (`on_site_tech_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if an on-site technician is needed during the event.
* **Specific Layout Instructions** (`layout_instructions`, text, required): Provide any specific layout instructions for the event.
* **Additional Furniture Needs** (`furniture_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional furniture needs for your event.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of a contact person for emergency purposes.
