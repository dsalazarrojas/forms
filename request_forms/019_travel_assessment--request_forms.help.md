# Travel Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Assessment form is designed to collect information from customers about their travel plans, including their customer information, travel dates, travel locations, and other relevant details. This form helps us better understand their travel preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Provide the travel dates in the "Travel Dates" field.
5. Enter the travel time in the "Travel Time" field.
6. Specify the travel location in the "Travel Location" field.
7. Enter any additional customer comments in the "Customer Comments" field.
8. Select the type of travel (Domestic or International) in the "Travelling Type" field.
9. Select the travel mode (Car, Train, Plane, or Bus) in the "Travel Mode" field.
10. Add any customer notes in the "Customer Note" field.
11. Select the assigned agent from the list of available options in the "Assigned Agent" field.
12. Set the assigned date in the "Assigned Date" field.

- **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, required: false)
  This field is not required, but it is recommended to fill it out for reference purposes.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false)
  Please enter the customer's name here.

- **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, required: false)

- **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required: false)
  Set the assigned date.

Tips:
