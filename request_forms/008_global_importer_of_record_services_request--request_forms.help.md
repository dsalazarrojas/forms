# Global Importer Of Record Services Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from importers or exporters who need to request services from a global network of importers of record. The form will be used to gather necessary details for approval or access to this network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company details.
2. Select your shipment routes.
3. Describe your products.
4. Choose your terms of agreement.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details (Company Details)**: This is a text field where you can enter your company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Shipment Routes (Shipment Routes)**: This is a select multiple field where you can choose one or more of the available shipment routes. Please select the routes that apply to your request.
* **Product Descriptions (Product Descriptions)**: This is a note field where you can provide a detailed description of the products you are requesting services for.
* **Terms Agreement (Terms Agreement)**: This is a select one field where you can choose the terms of agreement that apply to your request. Please select the option that best matches your needs.
* **Shipment Route 1 (Shipment Route 1)**: This is a text field where you can enter the details of your first shipment route.
* **Shipment Route 2 (Shipment Route 2)**: This is a text field where you can enter the details of your second shipment route.
* **Shipment Route 3 (Shipment Route 3)**: This is a text field where you can enter the details of your third shipment route.
* **Email (Email)**: This is an email field where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: This is a text field where you can enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields to ensure a smooth request process.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
