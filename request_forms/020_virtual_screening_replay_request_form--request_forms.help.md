# Virtual Screening Replay Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Screening Replay Request Form is designed to allow users to request a replay of a virtual screening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Info** with your name and any other personal details.
2. Enter the **Screening Info**, including the details of the screening you are requesting a replay of.
3. Enter a **Message** if you have any additional information you would like to share with the team.
4. Select the **Request Type** as either "Request" or "Other" to specify the type of request you are making.
5. If applicable, select the **Screening Date** and **Screening Time** of the screening you are requesting a replay of.
6. Enter the **Screening ID** if you have it.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** if you want to be contacted for further information.
8. Optionally, enter any additional information in the **Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Info**: This field is for entering personal details, such as your name.
- **Screening Info**: Enter details about the screening you are requesting a replay of.
- **Message**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with the team.
- **Request Type**: Choose the type of request you are making (Request or Other).
- **Screening Date**: Select the date of the screening you are requesting a replay of.
- **Screening Time**: Select the time of the screening you are requesting a replay of.
- **Screening ID**: Enter the ID of the screening you are requesting a replay of.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Message**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with the team.

Please fill in the fields according to your needs, and the team will follow up with you soon.
