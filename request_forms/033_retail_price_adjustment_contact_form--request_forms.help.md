# retail_price_adjustment_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail price adjustment contact form is a tool for retailers to contact the platform for price adjustments or inquiries. It allows retailers to provide necessary information for processing their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an *asterisk*.
2. Provide as much relevant information as possible in the fields.
3. Ensure that the submitted information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retailer Information
* **Retailer Info** (`retailer_info`, `text`, *required*): Enter your company name or name as it appears on your profile.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, *required*): Enter the product name or ID related to the price adjustment.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, `text`, *required*): Provide the reason for the price adjustment or inquiry.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, *required*): Attach any relevant supporting documents for your price adjustment.
