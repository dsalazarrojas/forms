# Parking Pass Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a parking pass for a vehicle to park on the premises. Please fill out this form with the required information to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (1): Enter the reason for your parking pass request in this text field. This is a required field.
* **Vehicle Details** (2): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant details in this text field. This is a required field.
* **Driver Details** (3): Enter your name, email, and phone number in this text field. This is a required field.
* **Parking Pass Details** (4): Enter the parking pass details, including the start and end dates and times in this text field. This is a required field.
* **Submit Button** (5): Click this button to submit your request after filling out all the required fields.
* **Parking Location** (6): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting parking in a specific location, or 'No' if you are not sure or do not need to specify a location. 
* **Parking Dates** (7): Enter the date(s) you will be parked on the premises in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parking Time** (8): Enter the time(s) you will be parked on the premises in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Parking Duration** (9): Enter the number of hours you will be parked on the premises. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the parking authority for clarification.
* Please note that the parking pass will be issued based on the information provided in this form.
