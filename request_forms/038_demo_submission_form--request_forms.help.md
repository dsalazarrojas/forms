# demo_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The demo_submission_form is a tool used to collect information from customers interested in a demo session. This form helps us understand their contact details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Select your preferred time for the demo.
3. Choose a preferred date for the demo.
4. Indicate your interest in the demo by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the 'What interests you about this service?' field.
5. Provide your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter your company name (if applicable).
8. Enter your customer name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`text`, required: false): Please fill in any additional information you'd like to share about your interests or expectations from the demo.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer submitting this form.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Company Name** (`text`, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter the company name.
* **What interests you about this service?** (`select_one`, required: false): Indicate your level of interest in the service by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Preferred time for the demo** (`time`, required: false): Choose a time that suits you best for the demo.
* **Preferred date for the demo** (`date`, required: false): Select a date you'd like the demo to be held on.
 
## Tips
- Be honest when answering the "What interests you about this service?" field, as this helps us tailor the demo to your needs.
- Please ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
