# Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and request expense reimbursements for business-related expenses. It captures relevant details such as date, type, and amount of the expense, as well as other supporting information like description, status, and category. This form will be reviewed and processed internally to ensure accurate and prompt reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title**: Enter a short and descriptive title for the form, which will serve as a reference for the expense request.
2. **Expense Date**: Select the date of the expense was incurred. Please note that the date should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Expense Type**: Choose the type of expense that was incurred, such as "Meeting", "Travel", or "Entertainment".
4. **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
5. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the expense, including any relevant details.
6. **Expense Status**: Indicate the status of the expense, such as "Approved", "Rejected", or "Pending".
7. **Expense Category**: Choose the category of the expense, such as "Travel", "Food and Beverages", or "Accommodation".
8. **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for correspondence related to the expense reimbursement.
9. **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
10. **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the expense.
11. **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: (`form_title`, `text`, optional) Enter a short title of the expense request.
* **Expense Date**: (`expense_date`, `date`, optional) Select the date of the expense.
* **Expense Type**: (`expense_type`, `select_one`, optional) Choose the type of expense that was incurred.
*   - Meeting
*   - Travel
*   - Entertainment
* **Amount**: (`amount`, `number`, optional) Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Description**: (`description`, `text`, optional) Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Status**: (`expense_status`, `select_multiple`, optional) Indicate the status of the expense.
*   - Approved
*   - Rejected
*   - Pending
* **Expense Category**: (`expense_category`, `select_one`, optional) Choose the category of the expense.
*   - Travel
*   - Food and Beverages
*   - Accommodation
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, optional) Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, optional) Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Note**: (`note`, `text`, optional) Add any additional notes or comments about the expense.
* **Submit**: (`submit`, `text`, optional) Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
- Double-check the date, amount, and type of expense to ensure accuracy.
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the expense.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the assigned user.

Note: The fields with "required: false" are not mandatory and can be left blank if not applicable.
