# Local Partnership Proposal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Partnership Proposal Request Form is a form designed to help local businesses and organizations propose partnership opportunities with us. This form should be completed thoroughly and accurately to help us understand your proposal and potential partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your business name and contact information, including contact name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe the type of partnership you are seeking to establish with us.
3. Explain the specific details of your proposal, highlighting how your business aligns with our goals and objectives.
4. Clearly specify the type of business you represent (e.g. retail, service, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your business name.
* **Contact Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your contact name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Address** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Partnership Type** (6, text, required/optional): Describe the type of partnership you are seeking to establish with us.
* **Partnership Description** (7, text, required/optional): Elaborate on the specific details of your proposal, highlighting how your business aligns with our goals and objectives.
* **Business Type** (8, text, required/optional): Clearly specify the type of business you represent (e.g. retail, service, etc.).
* **Proposal Details** (9, text, required/optional): Summarize the specific details of your proposal, including any relevant attachments or supporting materials.
