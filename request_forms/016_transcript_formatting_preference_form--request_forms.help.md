# Transcript Formatting Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transcript Formatting Preference Form is used to collect user preferences for transcript formatting. This form is typically filled out before receiving a transcript, and the preferences selected will dictate how the transcript is formatted.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Request Form" section with your name and any relevant details about your request.
2. In the "Delivery Preferences" section, select your preferred delivery method for the transcript.
3. In the "Transcript Type" section, choose the type of transcript you would like to receive:
	* Audio Only: You will receive a transcript of the audio recording.
	* Video with Audio: You will receive a transcript and the accompanying video recording.
	* Text Only: You will receive a transcript of the text recording.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form (Transcript Formatting Preference Form Request Form)**
	+ This field is used to capture any additional details about your request that may be relevant to the transcript formatting.
* **Delivery Preferences (Transcript Formatting Preference Form Delivery Preferences)**
	+ This field is used to select how you would like to receive the transcript. Please note that this selection will influence the formatting of the transcript.
* **Transcript Type (Transcript Formatting Preference Form Transcript Type)**
	+ This field is used to select the type of transcript you would like to receive. Please select one of the following options:
		- Audio Only: You will receive a transcript of the audio recording.
		- Video with Audio: You will receive a transcript and the accompanying video recording.
		- Text Only: You will receive a transcript of the text recording.
