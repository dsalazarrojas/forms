# Water Use Optimization Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect registration information for water use optimization programs, enabling us to better understand and support these efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct water source for your program from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide your contact name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Enter the program start and end dates to indicate the timeframe of your program.
4. Enter the total amount of water used during the program period.
5. Choose the current water quality level of your program.
6. In the 'Program Goal' field, describe the primary objective of your water use optimization program.
7. Enter your contact address and zip code to help us get in touch with you.
8. If available, select the assigned tool from the dropdown menu to associate the tool with your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of your water use optimization program.
* **Contact Name**: Type your name as the person in charge of the program.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to call or discuss any details further.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the starting date of your program.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the ending date of your program.
* **Total Water Used**: Provide the total amount of water used during the program period.
* **Water Source**: Select the source of the water used from the dropdown menu:
	+ Aquifer
	+ Well
	+ Reservoir
	+ River
	+ Lake
* **Water Quality**: Select the current water quality level:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Program Goal**: Describe the primary goal of your water use optimization program.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address for further communication.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your program (optional).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the assigned tool from the dropdown menu (optional):
	+ Jimmy
	+ Bob
	+ Joe
