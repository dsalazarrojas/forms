# Home Curfew Monitoring Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Curfew Monitoring Request Form is a form used to request monitoring of a individual's home curfew. This form helps to collect information necessary for monitoring and tracking of the individual's monitoring status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter the date you are requesting to start monitoring, in the `request_date` field.
3. Enter the scheduled time for the monitoring, in the `scheduled_time` field.
4. Enter the duration of the monitoring, in the `monitoring_duration` field.
5. Enter the frequency of the monitoring, in the `monitoring_frequency` field.
6. Provide contact details in the `contact_details` field.
7. Enter the name of the coordinator, in the `coordinator_name` field.
8. Enter the agency ID, in the `agency_id` field.
9. Enter the start date of the monitoring, in the `monitoring_start_date` field.
10. Enter the end date of the monitoring, in the `monitoring_end_date` field.
11. Select the current status of the monitoring from the options provided in the `monitoring_status` field.
12. Optionally, add any additional comments in the `additional_comments` field.
13. Add any notes in the `notes` field.
14. Select the communication channel to be used for updates, in the `communication_channel` field.
