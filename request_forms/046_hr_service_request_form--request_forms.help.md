# Hr Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hr Service Request Form is a tool for employees to submit requests for HR services. This form is used to gather necessary information for HR to efficiently process and handle service requests. Please make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately, and avoid duplicating requests as it may cause unnecessary delays in processing.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the "Request Service" question. If you need to request a service, please select "Yes" and proceed to the next step.
2. Enter the location of the service request in the "Location" field.
3. Enter the name of the company or organization requesting the service in the "Company Name" field.
4. Choose the type of service requested from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date when the service is needed in the "Service Date" field.
6. Provide information about the person requesting the service in the "Requestor Info" field.
7. Enter the contact information of the person requesting the service, including their name, email address, and phone number.
8. Choose the type of request being made from the "Request Type" dropdown menu.
9. Provide any additional comments or information about the request in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter a brief description of the service requested in the "Service Requested" field.
11. Add any additional comments or information about the request in the "Additional Comments" field.
12. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.
