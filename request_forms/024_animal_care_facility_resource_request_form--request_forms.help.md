# Animal Care Facility Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help animal care facilities request resources and supplies from a central organization. It allows facilities to provide detailed information about their needs, enabling the central organization to allocate resources efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out your personal details:
    *   **Requestor Full Name**: Provide your full name as the contact person for the facility.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up.
2.  Enter facility details:
    *   **Facility Name**: Provide the name of your animal care facility.
    *   **Facility Address**: Enter the street, city, state, and zip code of your facility.
3.  Select facility type and resource category:
    *   **Type of Facility**: Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., Animal shelter, veterinary clinic, etc.).
    *   **Resource Category**: Select the type of resources you need (e.g., medical supplies, food and nutrition, etc.).
4.  Specify the resources and quantities needed:
    *   **Medical Supplies Needed**: Choose all medical supplies required.
    *   **Food and Nutrition Supplies**: Choose all food and nutrition supplies needed.
    *   **Cleaning Supplies Needed**: Choose all cleaning supplies required.
    *   **Equipment Requests**: Choose all equipment required.
    *   **Facility Supplies**: Choose all facility supplies needed.
5.  Provide detailed information about the resources needed:
    *   **Itemized List of Requested Items**: Enter a list of specific items and quantities required.
    *   **Estimated Quantities**: Enter the estimated quantity of each item needed.
6.  Set delivery expectations:
    *   **Urgency Level**: Indicate how soon the resources are needed (critical, within 1 week, within 2-4 weeks, or when available).
    *   **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you need the resources.
7.  Confirm budget and other details:
    *   **Is There a Budget Available for This Request?**: Indicate if you have a budget for the resources.
    *   **Available Budget Amount**: If applicable, enter the budget amount available.
8.  Confirm previous requests and submit:
    *   **Have You Submitted Resource Requests Before?**: Indicate if this is a new or renewed request.
    *   **Justification for Request**: Explain why these resources are needed.
    *   **How Will These Resources Impact Animal Care?**: Explain the expected outcomes of receiving these resources.
    *   **Have You Sought Resources from Other Sources?**: Indicate if you've explored other options.
    *   **Preferred Contact Method for Follow-up**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
    *   **Additional Comments**: Add any other information relevant to your request.
    *   **Requestor Signature**: Sign with your full name.
    *   **Date**: Enter the current date.

This form is designed to guide you through the process of requesting resources and supplies for your animal care facility. Please ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information to help the central organization allocate resources efficiently.
