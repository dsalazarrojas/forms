# Planning Permission Search Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for search of planning permissions. The goal is to gather information from the user to facilitate the search process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your search query:
	* Provide a valid address on the first page.
	2. Select the type of search you need:
		+ Landmark
		+ Property
		+ Address
3. Enter your contact information:
	* Enter your email address.
	* Enter your phone number.
4. Provide date of birth (optional, but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Search Address**: Enter the address you want to search for planning permissions. This will help us narrow down our search. (Required: False)
* **Search Type**: Select the type of search you need:
	+ **Landmark**: Search for planning permissions related to a specific landmark or monument.
	+ **Property**: Search for planning permissions related to a property or real estate.
	+ **Address**: Search for planning permissions related to a specific address.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your search results. (Required: False)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your search results. (Required: False)
* **Date of Birth**: If you are a property owner, you might be required to provide your date of birth. This field is optional but recommended for verification purposes. (Required: False)

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your search address to avoid mistakes.
* If you need help with your search, don't hesitate to contact us.
