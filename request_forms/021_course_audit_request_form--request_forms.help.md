# Course Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Audit Request Form is used to collect information about course audit requests from students. This form helps us to efficiently gather necessary details about the student's course audit, making it easier to process and address their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Name** of the course you are requesting an audit for.
2. Enter your **Student ID** number.
3. Provide your **Student Email** address.
4. Enter your **Student Phone** number.
5. If necessary, include any additional **Student Note** information.
6. Enter the **Course Number** of the course you are requesting an audit for.
7. Specify the **Audit Term** you are requesting the audit for.
8. Include any additional **Course Note** information, if necessary.
9. Provide a brief explanation for the **Reason for Request**.
10. Choose the **Request Date** for the audit.
11. Optionally, include any additional **Approval Note** information.
12. Select the **Approval Status**, which can be either **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Pending**.
13. Enter the **Approver Name**.
14. Optionally, include any additional **Approval Note** information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): The name of the course you are requesting an audit for.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Your student ID number. 
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Your student phone number.
* **Student Note** (`student_note`, note, optional): Any additional information about the student.
* **Course Number** (`course_number`, text, optional): The course number you are requesting an audit for.
* **Audit Term** (`audit_term`, text, optional): The term you are requesting the audit for.
* **Course Note** (`course_note`, note, optional): Any additional information about the course.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): A brief explanation for the request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): The date you are requesting the audit.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, note, optional): Any additional approval information.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the approval, which can be **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Pending**.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): The name of the person who approved the request.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, note, optional): Any additional information from the approver.
