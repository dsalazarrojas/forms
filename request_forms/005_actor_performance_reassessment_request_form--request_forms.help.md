# Actor Performance Reassessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Performance Reassessment Request Form is used to request a review of an actor's performance, providing a formal process for reassessing and improving their skillset. This form is typically used when there has been a change in circumstances, new information has become available, or when a review is required for contractual purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a legitimate reason for requesting a reassessment.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Actor Full Name**: Enter the name of the actor whose performance is to be reassessed.
	* **Production Title**: Enter the title of the production the actor was involved in.
	* **Role/Character**: Enter the role or character the actor played.
	* **Direct Supervisor/Department Head**: Enter the name of the direct supervisor or department head who will be performing the reassessment.
	* **Date of Original Assessment**: Enter the date the original assessment was made.
	* **Reason for Reassessment Request**: Select a valid reason for the reassessment request.
	* **Detailed Justification**: Provide a clear explanation of why the reassessment is needed.
	* **Specific Areas for Review**: Select all relevant areas that need to be reviewed.
	* **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level for the reassessment.
	* **Acknowledgment**: Confirm that the reassessment is subject to reviewer availability.
3. Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Actor Full Name** (`actor_full_name`, `text`, **required**): The name of the actor whose performance is being reassessed.
* **Production Title** (`production_name`, `text`, **required**): The title of the production the actor was involved in.
* **Role/Character** (`character_role`, `text`, **required**): The role or character the actor played.
* **Direct Supervisor/Department Head** (`department_head`, `text`, **required**): The name of the direct supervisor or department head who will be performing the reassessment.
* **Date of Original Assessment** (`original_assessment_date`, `date`, **required**): The date the original assessment was made.
* **Reason for Reassessment Request** (`reason_for_request`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Select one of the following reasons:
		- Improvement in specific skills
		- Discrepancy in original feedback
		- New performance data available
		- Change in role requirements
		- Other
* **Detailed Justification** (`detailed_justification`, `text`, **required**): A clear explanation of why the reassessment is needed.
* **Specific Areas for Review** (`performance_areas`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ Select all relevant areas that need to be reviewed:
		- Character Interpretation
		- Physical Performance
		- Vocal Work
		- Emotional Range
		- Professionalism and Reliability
		- Collaboration with Cast/Crew
* **Supporting Evidence Link** (`evidence_submission`, `text`, **optional**): A link to supporting evidence, such as new footage or performance logs.
* **Preferred Reviewer** (`requested_reviewer`, `text`, **optional**): The name of a preferred reviewer (if applicable).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Choose one of the following urgency levels:
		- Low - Regular Review Cycle
		- Medium - Next Production Milestone
		- High - Immediate Contractual Review Required
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Confirm that the reassessment is subject to reviewer availability.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide clear and concise reasoning for the reassessment request.
* Only select areas that genuinely require review.
* Be prepared to provide supporting evidence for your claim.
* Understand that the reviewer's availability may impact the timeline for the reassessment.
