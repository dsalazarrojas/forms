# Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Support Request Form is designed to gather information about support requests from customers or end-users. This form helps support team members to quickly and effectively address the issue at hand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Problem Description field with a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing.
2. Enter the contact name and contact phone number (if available) in their respective fields.
3. If applicable, provide the contact's email address.
4. In the Support Request field, provide a detailed description of the issue or your request.
5. If necessary, attach relevant files or documents to the Support Request field using the Attachments field.
6. Once you have completed all the required information, review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Problem Description** (Required): A clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Contact Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the contact person associated with the issue.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the contact's phone number (if available).
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the contact's email address (if available).
* **Support Request** (Required): A detailed description of the issue or your request.
* **Attachments** (Optional): Attach relevant files or documents to support your request.
