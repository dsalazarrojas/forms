# Envelope Efficiency Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Envelope Efficiency Registration Form is designed to gather information from users to improve the efficiency of envelope-related processes. This form is typically used in conjunction with other forms to facilitate data collection and processing. Before filling out this form, please check that you are not duplicating an existing registration and that your submission aligns with the intended use case. If you're unsure, please contact your local contact for guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
3. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
4. **Address**: Enter your mailing address.
5. **City**: Enter your city or town.
6. **Province/State**: Select your province or state of residence (if required).
7. **Zip**: Enter your postal zip code.
8. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your submission (optional).
9. **Date**: Enter the date of your visit or event (if required).
10. **Time**: Enter the time of your visit or event (if required).
11. **Submit**: Choose the action you'd like to take:
	* Submit: Complete and submit your form.
	* Save for Later: Save your form for later completion.
	* Cancel: Discard your form and start over.
12. **First Name 2**: Enter your first name (if not already provided).
13. **Last Name**: Enter your last name (if not already provided).
14. **Province**: Select your province or state of residence (if not already provided).
15. **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number (if not already provided).
16. **Address 2**: Enter your mailing address (if not already provided).
17. **City 2**: Enter your city or town (if not already provided).
18. **Postal**: Enter your postal code (if not already provided).
19. **Country**: Enter your country of residence (if not already provided).
