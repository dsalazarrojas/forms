# CRM Support Ticket Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CRM Support Ticket Form is used by employees to submit support issues to the CRM system for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit a support ticket, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your information, ensuring all required fields are completed before submission.
2. Select the department that best aligns with your issue.
3. Choose the category that best describes your issue.
4. Indicate the priority level of your issue.
5. Provide a brief summary of your issue in the subject field.
6. Describe your issue in detail in the description field.
7. If applicable, provide the number of users affected and any relevant steps to reproduce the issue.
8. If applicable, describe the expected behavior and the browser or device you are using when the issue occurs.
9. Attach any relevant screenshots or files (optional).
10. Specify your preferred resolution time.
11. Provide any additional comments or information to help the support team assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Your Name (`submitter_name`)
* A field to enter your full name for contact purposes.
### Email Address (`submitter_email`)
* A field to enter your email address where updates will be sent.
### Phone Number (`submitter_phone`)
* An optional field to enter your contact phone number.
### Department (`department`)
* Select the department that best aligns with your issue.
### Ticket Category (`ticket_category`)
* Select the category that best describes your issue.
### Priority Level (`priority_level`)
* Indicate the urgency of your issue (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
### Subject (`subject`)
* Enter a brief summary of your issue.
### Detailed Description (`description`)
* Describe your issue in detail.
### Number of Users Affected (`affected_users`)
* If applicable, indicate how many people are impacted by the issue.
### Steps to Reproduce (`steps_to_reproduce`)
* If applicable, list the steps that cause the issue.
### Expected Behavior (`expected_behavior`)
* If applicable, describe the expected behavior instead.
### Browser or Device (`browser_or_device`)
* If applicable, describe the browser or device you are using when the issue occurs.
### Attachments (`attachments`)
* You can attach relevant screenshots or files for further reference.
### Preferred Resolution Time (`preferred_resolution_time`)
* Indicate when you would like the issue resolved.
### Additional Comments (`additional_comments`)
* Provide any additional information to help the support team assist you.
