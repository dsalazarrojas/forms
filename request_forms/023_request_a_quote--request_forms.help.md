# Request A Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request A Quote" form is used to gather information from customers to create a quote for a particular service or product. This form helps the customer's sales team generate an accurate quote based on the provided details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Requested By" option that best describes your request.
2. Choose the delivery options for your quote.
3. Select the type of quote you are requesting.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
5. Add any additional comments or attachments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requested By
• **Requested By** (`requested_by`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select how your request is made. Please choose the option that best describes how your request is made.
### Quote Delivery
• **Quote Delivery** (`quote_delivery`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select how you would like to receive your quote. Please choose the options that best fit your needs.
### Quote Type
• **Quote Type** (`quote_type`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the type of quote you are requesting. Please choose the option that best describes your request.
### Contact Name
• **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): This field is a text input where you can enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): This field is an email input where you can enter your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): This field is a text input where you can enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number as it appears on your official documentation.
### Quote Message
• **Quote Message** (`quote_message`): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or messages about your request. Please be as detailed as possible.
### Quote Status
• **Quote Status** (`quote_status`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the status of your quote. Please choose the option that best describes your quote status.
### Quote Priority
• **Quote Priority** (`quote_priority`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the priority of your quote. Please choose the option that best describes your needs.
### Quote Notes
• **Quote Notes** (`quote_notes`): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional notes or comments about your request. Please be as detailed as possible.
### Quote Tags
• **Quote Tags** (`quote_tags`): This field is a dropdown menu where you can select any tags that best describe your request. Please choose the options that best fit your needs.
### Quote File
• **Quote File** (`quote_file`): This field is a text input where you can upload any relevant files related to your request. Please only upload relevant files.
### Quote Attachment
• **Quote Attachment** (`quote_attachment`): This field is a text input where you can upload any additional attachments related to your request. Please only upload relevant attachments.
### Quote Additional Attachment
• **Quote Additional Attachment** (`quote_additional_attachment`): This field is a text input where you can upload any additional attachments related to your request. Please only upload relevant attachments.
### Quote Comments
• **Quote Comments** (`quote_comments`): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments about your request. Please be as detailed as possible.
### Quote Additional Comments
• **Quote Additional Comments** (`quote_additional_comments`): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments about your request. Please be as detailed as possible.
### Quote Total
• **Quote Total** (`quote_total`): This field is a number input where you can enter the total amount of your quote. Please enter a valid number.
### Quote Discount
• **Quote Discount** (`quote_discount`): This field is a number input where you can enter the discount amount of your quote. Please enter a valid number.
### Quote Tax
• **Quote Tax** (`quote_tax`): This field is a number input where you can enter the tax amount of your quote. Please enter a valid number.
### Quote Grand Total
• **Quote Grand Total** (`quote_grand_total`): This field is a number input where you can enter the grand total amount of your quote. Please enter a valid number.
