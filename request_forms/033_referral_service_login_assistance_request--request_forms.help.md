# Referral Service Login Assistance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers who are having trouble logging into their Referral Service account. The form is for internal use only and is intended to help our support team provide assistance and resolve login issues. Please note that we may not be able to verify the information provided without further confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to initiate the assistance request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Referral Service Login Assistance Request** (`login_help`, Text, Optional)
This field is for you to describe the issue you are experiencing with logging into your Referral Service account. Be as detailed as possible to help our support team assist you.

### 2. **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Optional)
If you know your username, please enter it here to help our support team locate your account.

### 3. **Referral Link** (`referral_link`, Text, Optional)
If you know the login link you were trying to access, please enter it here.

### 4. **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
If you know your email address associated with your account, please enter it here. This will help us verify your account.

### 5. **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional)
If you have any contact information for yourself, please enter it here in case we need to reach out to you for further assistance.

### 6. **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, Note, Optional)
If you have any additional comments or details about your login issue, please provide them here. This will help our support team better understand your situation.

### 7. **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional)
Click this button to send your assistance request to our support team.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue to help our support team assist you efficiently.
* If you have any further questions or concerns after receiving assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us again.
