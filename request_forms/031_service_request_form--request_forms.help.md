# Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Request Form is a tool designed for customers or internal stakeholders to submit service requests to us. This form allows you to provide detailed information about your request, ensuring that we understand your needs accurately and can efficiently process your service request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what we need from you.
2. Provide a brief description of your service request in the first text field.
3. Enter your name in the second field.
4. Enter your email address in the third field, ensuring it's a valid email format (e.g., [user@example.com](mailto:user@example.com)).
5. Enter your phone number in the fifth field. Please format it according to your local dialing conventions (e.g., +1-123-456-7890 or 01234-567890).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request Form 1** (Text Field)
	+ This is the main description field where you provide a detailed account of your service request. Be concise and clear about what you need help with.

* **Name** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification or records.

* **Email** (Email Field)
	+ Enter a valid email address associated with your identity or the one you wish to be contacted at.

* **Phone Number** (Text Field)
	+ Enter your contact phone number according to your local dialing conventions (e.g., +1-123-456-7890 or 01234-567890).

* **Service Request Form 5**, **Service Request Form 6**, **Service Request Form 7**, and **Service Request Form 8** (Text Fields)
	+ These fields are reserved for additional information or details about your service request. If you have specific requirements or notes, please use these fields to provide them.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your service request in the first field.
- Ensure your email and phone number are accurate and formatted correctly.
- Use the extra fields to provide any additional information that might aid in processing your service request.
