# song_request_form--request_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a song request, including song details and requester details. It is intended for optional use and is designed to gather relevant information to facilitate the processing and fulfillment of song requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to the form and click on the "Song Request Form" page.
2.  Enter the required and optional information in the form fields as prompted.
3.  Once you have filled out all the necessary information, click "Submit" to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Song Request Form Requester Details** (`<text>`): Please enter your name and details for your song request. This field is required.
	+ **Requester Name** (`<text>`, Required): Enter your name as the person or entity requesting the song.
	+ **Requester Email** (`<email>`, Optional): Enter the email address associated with your request.
	+ **Requester Phone** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable) associated with your request.
	+ **Requester Comments** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
	+ **Requester Message** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter any additional message or information about your request.
* **Song Details** (`<text>`): Please enter the song details.
	+ **Song Title** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter the title of the song you are requesting.
	+ **Song Artist** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter the artist or performer of the song.
	+ **Song Album** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter the album associated with the song.
	+ **Song Year** (`<number>`, Optional): Enter the year the song was released.
	+ **Song Genre** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter the genre of the song.
* **Is Event** (`<select_one>`, Optional): Check if this request is related to an event.
* **Is Automated** (`<select_one>`, Optional): Check if this request is automated.
* **Is Promotion** (`<select_one>`, Optional): Check if this request is for promotion.
* **Is Automated** (`<select_one>`, Optional): Check if this request is for automated purposes.
* **Requester is Event** (`<select_one>`, Optional): Check if this requester is related to an event.

Note: The select_one fields are for yes/no options.
