# Account Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals requesting access to an account for a specific system or application. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your manager.
3. Choose the correct access type and level requested.
4. Specify the system or application name for which access is required.
5. Enter any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the request process.
6. Sign the form electronically to confirm the accuracy of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Requester Employee ID** (`requester_employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number as issued by your organization.
* **Requester Department** (`requester_department`, text, required): Enter your department name as it appears in the company directory.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address as it appears in the company directory.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name as it appears on their ID or other official documents.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your direct manager's email address as it appears in the company directory.
* **Access Type Requested** (`access_type_requested`, select_one, required): Choose the type of access you are requesting (e.g., New Account, Access Modification, Access Restoration, Privilege Escalation, Temporary Access, or Emergency Access).
* **System/Application Name** (`system_application_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the system or application for which access is required.
* **Access Level Requested** (`access_level_requested`, select_one, required): Choose the level of access you need (e.g., Read Only, Standard User, Power User, Administrator, Super User, or Custom).
* **Specific Permissions Needed** (`specific_permissions_needed`, text, required): Enter any specific permissions or modules you need access to.
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, text, required): Enter the reason why you need this access.
* **Duration of Access** (`duration_of_access`, select_one, required): Choose how long you need this access (e.g., Permanent, Temporary - Less than 1 week, Temporary - 1-4 weeks, etc.).
* **Requested Start Date** (`requested_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you need the access to begin.
* **Requested End Date** (`requested_end_date`, date, not required): If your request is temporary, enter the date when you need the access to end.
* **Security Training Completed** (`security_training_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed the required security training.
* **Compliance Acknowledgment** (`compliance_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you acknowledge and agree to comply with all security policies.
* **Approval Required** (`approval_required`, select_one, required): If this request requires approval beyond your manager, choose the type of approval needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your request that might be helpful for the request process.
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`, text, required): Sign the form electronically to confirm the accuracy of your request.
