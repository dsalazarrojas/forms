# free-client-consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for scheduling a free consultation. It collects data on the client's preferences for the meeting, including their name, schedule preferences, and language requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Choose a meeting date and time in the "Schedule a Meeting" field.
5. Select your preferred meeting time from the options provided in the "Preferred Meeting Time" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
8. Select the consultant you would like to be assigned to you from the "Assigned Consultant" field.
9. Choose your preferred language from the options provided in the "Preferred Language" field.
10. Select the type of meeting you would like to schedule (In Person, Phone Call, Video Call, or Email) in the "Schedule Type" field.
11. Indicate your availability for the meeting in the "Availability" field.
12. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text) in the "Preferred Communication" field.
13. Confirm that you are available to meet by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Meeting" field.
14. Enter the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field.
15. Choose the meeting duration (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour) in the "Meeting Duration" field.
16. Select the type of meeting (Personal, Business, or Social) in the "Meeting Type" field.
17. Indicate the priority of the meeting (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Meeting Priority" field.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.

* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred language for the meeting.
* **Schedule Type** (`schedule_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of meeting (In Person, Phone Call, Video Call, or Email).
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate your availability for the meeting.

* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the meeting location here.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the meeting duration (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour).
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of meeting (Personal, Business, or Social).
