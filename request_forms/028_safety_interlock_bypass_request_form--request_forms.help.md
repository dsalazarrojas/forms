# Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a safety interlock bypass, which allows access to areas or routes that would otherwise be restricted for safety reasons. A bypass is granted only for legitimate reasons and after careful review by the safety team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your answer to "safety_interlock_bypass_request_form" (Yes or No) to indicate if you are requesting a bypass. If you answer "Yes", you will be prompted to provide more information.
2. If you answered "Yes", enter the name of the maintenance team responsible for the bypass in the "maintenance_team" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the route or area you are requesting access to in the "route_description" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of the scheduled activity in the "start_time" and "end_time" fields, respectively.
5. If applicable, enter any risk controls that will be taken to mitigate hazards in the "risk_controls" field.
6. Enter the date of the last safety review, which should be within the last 3 months, in the "safety_review_date" field.
7. Select whether the bypass has been approved by the safety team ("Yes" or "No") in the "approver" field.
8. Enter any additional comments regarding your request in the "comments" field.
9. Select the current status of your request ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "approved_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form** (<safety_interlock_bypass_request_form>, select_one, required): This field asks if you are requesting a safety interlock bypass. Answer "Yes" to indicate that you are requesting a bypass.
  * If you answer "Yes", you will be prompted to provide more information.
- **Maintenance Team** (<maintenance_team>, text, optional): This field should be filled out with the name of the maintenance team responsible for the bypass.
  * If a maintenance team is not specified, your request may be denied.
- **Route Description** (<route_description>, text, optional): This field should be filled out with a brief description of the route or area you are requesting access to.
  * This will help the safety team understand your request better.
- **Start Time** (<start_time>, time, optional): This field should be filled out with the start time of the scheduled activity.
  * If you don't know the start time, you can leave this field blank.
- **End Time** (<end_time>, time, optional): This field should be filled out with the end time of the scheduled activity.
  * If you don't know the end time, you can leave this field blank.
- **Risk Controls** (<risk_controls>, text, optional): This field should be filled out with any risk controls that will be taken to mitigate hazards.
  * If there are no risk controls, you can leave this field blank.
- **Safety Review Date** (<safety_review_date>, date, optional): This field should be filled out with the date of the last safety review.
  * The date should be within the last 3 months.
- **Approver** (<approver>, select_multiple, optional): This field should be filled out with "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the bypass has been approved by the safety team.
  * If the bypass has not been approved, answer "No".
- **Comments** (<comments>, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments regarding your request.
  * Keep your comments concise and relevant.
- **Approved Status** (<approved_status>, select_one, optional): This field should be filled out with the current status of your request ("Active" or "Inactive").
  * If your request is inactive, you can select "Inactive".
