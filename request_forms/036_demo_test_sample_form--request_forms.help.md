# demo_test_sample_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting a demonstration or product information. It is meant to gather basic customer information and specific product details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "REQUEST FORM" field with a brief description of your demonstration or product request.
2. Select a "demo type" from the options provided (e.g., "demo", "product", or "other").
3. Enter your customer details, including your phone number and email address.
4. Fill out the "customer name" field with your full name.
5. Enter the price of the product you are interested in (if applicable).
6. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "other notes" field.
7. Fill out the "form id" field with a unique number.
8. Select the "category" of your request (e.g., "request forms" or "other").
9. Enter any additional description of your product or demonstration request.
10. Enter any customer note or demonstration notes.
11. Finally, upload your "output file" if applicable.
