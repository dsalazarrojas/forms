# Student Email Account Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect relevant information from students for the purpose of creating or updating their email account. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing and avoid potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Student Email Account Request Form" link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   * Student Information
   * Email Address
   * Password
   * Student ID
   * Student Email
3. For optional fields:
   * Department
   * Faculty
   * Student Phone
   * Student Cell
   * Student Cell Extension
   * Student Work Phone
   * Student Work Cell
   * Student Address
   * Departmental Email
   * Faculty Email
   * Other Department Email

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (1)**: Enter your student name, ID number, or other relevant details.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your university-recognized student email address (e.g., @university.edu).
* **Password (3)**: Enter a password of your choice (at least 8 characters).
* **Verify Password (4)**: Confirm your password to ensure accuracy.
* **Student ID (5)**: Enter your student ID number if available.
* **Department (6)**: Check 'Yes' if you belong to a specific department.
* **Confirm (7)**: Reconfirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Student Email (9)**: Enter your student email.
* **Email Domain (10)**: Check the domain of your email address.
* **Student Email Address (11)**: Check 'Yes' if your student email is correct.
* **Departmental (12)**: Check 'Yes' if your departmental email is correct.
* **Faculty (13)**: Check 'Yes' if your faculty email is correct.
* **Student Phone (14)**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Student Phone Extension (15)**: Enter your student phone extension.
* **Student Cell (16)**: Enter your student cell number.
* **Student Cell Extension (17)**: Enter your student cell extension.
* **Student Work Phone (18)**: Enter your student work phone number.
* **Student Work Phone Extension (19)**: Enter your student work phone extension.
* **Student Work Cell (20)**: Enter your student work cell number.
* **Student Work Cell Extension (21)**: Enter your student work cell extension.
* **Student Address (22)**: Enter your student address.
* **Departmental Email (23)**: Enter your departmental email.
* **Faculty Email (24)**: Enter your faculty email.
* **Other Department Email (25)**: Enter other department email.
* **Submit (7)**: Click to submit your request.
* **Confirm (8)**: Reconfirm your request to ensure accuracy.
* **Student Phone (14)**: Enter your student phone number.
* **Student Cell (16)**: Enter your student cell number.
* **Student Cell Extension (17)**: Enter your student cell extension.

* **Student Work Cell (20)**: Enter your student work cell number.
