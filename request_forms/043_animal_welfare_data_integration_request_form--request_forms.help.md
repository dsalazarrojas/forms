# Animal Welfare Data Integration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Welfare Data Integration Request Form is designed to help you request data integration for animal welfare data. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide us with essential information about your organization and the data integration needs you have. Please fill out the form carefully, and we will review your request and follow up with you to discuss further details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Fill out the Requester Information section**: This section will ask for your name, job title, email, and phone number. Make sure to use your full name and title accurately.
2. **Provide Organization Information**: This section will ask for your organization's name, type, address, and website. Make sure to provide accurate contact information.
3. **Specify Integration Needs**: This section will ask for the purpose and scope of the integration, the specific data you need, and the desired start date and implementation timeline.
4. **Describe Current Systems and Technical Capabilities**: This will help us understand your current systems and technical capabilities.
5. **Indicate Partnerships and Security Requirements**: This section will ask about your existing partnerships, data security requirements, and privacy compliance needs.
6. **Specify Support and Maintenance Needs**: This will help us understand the level of support and maintenance you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your official job title as it appears in the organization's directory or business card.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your work phone number where we can reach you.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your official email address where we can communicate with you.
* **Organization Name**: Please enter the name of your organization as it appears on your letterhead or website.
* **Organization Type**: Please select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Animal Shelter, Animal Rescue, Nonprofit, etc.).
* **Organization Address**: Please enter your organization's street address.
* **Organization Website**: Please enter the URL of your organization's website.
* **Purpose of Integration**: Please select the reason for the data integration (e.g., Rescue Coordination, Data Analytics, Research, etc.).
* **Primary Goals**: Please describe the primary goals you want to accomplish through data integration.
* **Data Types Needed**: Please select the specific data you need for integration (e.g., Animal Records, Medical History, Adoption Data, etc.).
* **Data Sources**: Please describe the data sources you want to integrate with.
* **Data Integration Frequency**: Please select how often you want data to sync (e.g., Real-time, Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Current Software/Platform**: Please enter the name of your current system or platform.
* **Current Database Type**: Please select the type of database you are using (e.g., SQL, NoSQL, Spreadsheets, etc.).
* **Technical Capabilities**: Please describe your organization's technical capabilities.
* **Partner Organizations**: Please list the partner organizations you want to connect with.
* **Established Partnerships**: Please indicate if you have existing data-sharing agreements.
* **Partnership Details**: Please describe your current partnerships.
* **Desired Start Date**: Please enter the date you want to begin integration.
* **Implementation Timeline**: Please describe the expected implementation timeline.
* **Budget Available**: Please enter the budget available for integration.
* **Technical Resources**: Please describe the technical resources available for the project.
* **Data Security Requirements**: Please describe your data security requirements (e.g., encryption, access controls, etc.).
* **Privacy Compliance**: Please select the privacy compliance requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, etc.).
* **Data Ownership and Control**: Please describe who owns and controls the data.
* **Access Controls Needed**: Please describe the access controls needed.
* **Technical Support Needed**: Please select the level of technical support required (e.g., 24/7, Business Hours, etc.).
* **Staff Training Needed**: Please select if you require full, basic, or minimal training for your staff.
* **Maintenance and Update Expectations**: Please describe the expectations for data maintenance and updates.
* **Additional Information or Requirements**: Please provide any additional information or requirements we should know about.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and we will review your request and follow up with you to discuss further details.
