# Vendor Payment Hold Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a payment hold from the finance team for a specific vendor. A payment hold is used to prevent a vendor from receiving payment for an invoice.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to save your changes.
2. Select "Vendor Payment Hold Request Form" as the form type.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
4. Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`vendor_payment_hold_request_form`, text, required): This is the name of the form which helps identify the purpose of the form.
* **Invoice Details** (`invoice_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the invoice for which you are requesting a payment hold.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_id`, number, optional): Enter the invoice number for which you are requesting a payment hold.
* **Vendor** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor for which you are requesting a payment hold.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you expect payment.
* **Reason for Hold** (`hold_reason`, select_one, optional): Select a reason for the payment hold from the options Late payment, Incorrect payment method, or Missing payment document.
* **Amount of Payment** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of payment for which you are requesting a hold.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_one, optional): Select the team that will review your request: Finance Team, Procurement Team, or Accounts Team.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all relevant fields for the reviewer to make an informed decision.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all details are accurate.
- Submit only one request per form, if multiple requests are needed, use separate form submissions. 

Note: The reviewer selected will review and decide on the payment hold request.
