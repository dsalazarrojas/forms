# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form is used to collect information about pets and their owners for scheduling meet and greet sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the pet type from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the owner's first name.
3. Enter the owner's last name.
4. Enter the pet owner's email address.
5. Enter the pet owner's phone number.
6. (Optional) Select the pet breed.
7. (Optional) Enter the pet size.
8. (Optional) Enter the pet's age.
9. (Optional) Enter the pet's weight.
10. (Optional) Enter the pet's diet.
11. Select a date and time for the meet and greet session.
12. Select a time for the meet and greet session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type (Select One)**: This field allows you to choose the type of pet you own (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).
* **First Name**: Enter the owner's first name as it appears on their identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter the owner's last name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the pet owner.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for the pet owner.
* **Pet Breed (Optional)**: If you have a purebred pet, select its breed from the dropdown menu.
* **Pet Size (Optional)**: Enter the size of your pet (small, medium, large, etc.).
* **Pet Age (Optional)**: Enter the age of your pet.
* **Pet Weight (Optional)**: Enter the weight of your pet.
* **Pet Diet (Optional)**: Enter any specific diet requirements your pet may have (e.g. grain-free, vegan, etc.).
* **Pet Age (Optional)**: Enter the age of your pet.
* **Meet Date**: Select a date for the meet and greet session.
* **Meet Time**: Select a time for the meet and greet session.
