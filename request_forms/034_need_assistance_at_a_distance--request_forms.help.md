# Need Assistance At A Distance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Need Assistance At A Distance" form is designed to gather information from users who need support or help from a remote location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Describe your medical problem in the "Medical Problem" field.
7. Select the doctors you would like to contact from the list in the "Best Doctors" field.
8. Use the "Contact Form" field to provide any additional comments or information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is known to others.
- **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is known to others.
- **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Contact Number** (text, required: false): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
- **Date Of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Medical Problem** (text, required: true): Describe the issue or concern you are experiencing.
- **Best Doctors** (select multiple, required: true): Select the doctors you would like to contact from the list below.
  - Dr. Smith
  - Dr. Johnson
  - Dr. Williams
  - Dr. Davis
- **Contact Form** (note, required: true): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about your request.
