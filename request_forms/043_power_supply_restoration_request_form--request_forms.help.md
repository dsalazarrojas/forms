# Power Supply Restoration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Supply Restoration Request Form is used to collect information about power supply restoration requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Facility Manager ID in the "Facility Manager ID" field. This is a unique identifier for the facility manager who is submitting the request.
2. Enter the name of the facility affected by the power outage in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the power outage in the "Outage Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the power outage in the "Outage Time" field.
5. Enter the duration of the power outage in the "Duration" field.
6. Enter the number of affected customers in the "Affected Customers" field.
7. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional information about the power outage.
8. Select all the power supply systems affected by the power outage from the "Power Supply Systems Affected" dropdown menu. (e.g., Power Lines, Transformer, Distribution, Transmission)
9. Select the current restoration status from the "Restoration Status" dropdown menu. (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Cancelled)
10. Finally, enter your name or ID in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Manager ID** (1): Enter your Facility Manager ID, which is a unique identifier for the facility manager who is submitting the request.
* **Facility Name** (2): Enter the name of the facility affected by the power outage.
* **Outage Date** (3): Enter the date of the power outage in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Outage Time** (4): Enter the time of the power outage in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Duration** (5): Enter the duration of the power outage in hours.
* **Affected Customers** (6): Enter the number of customers affected by the power outage.
* **Notes** (7): Provide any additional information about the power outage.
* **Power Supply Systems Affected** (8): Select all the power supply systems affected by the power outage. (e.g., Power Lines, Transformer, Distribution, Transmission)
* **Restoration Status** (9): Select the current restoration status from the dropdown menu. (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Cancelled)
* **Submitted By** (10): Enter your name or ID.
