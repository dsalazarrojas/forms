# Credit Memo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credit Memo Request Form is a document used to provide a detailed description of credits and debits related to a transaction or series of transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the necessary fields with the required information. Each field has an optional or required status, which is denoted in parentheses. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request details
* **Request details** (`request_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the request. Please include any relevant information that will help us process your credit memo request.

### Amount Requested
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, number, optional): This field is for specifying the amount of the credit memo requested.

### Reason for Refund
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, optional): This field is for providing a detailed explanation of the reason for the credit memo request.

### Account Number
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): This field is for providing your account number, which is required for our accounting records.

### Billing address
* **Billing address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): This field is for providing your billing address, which is helpful for our accounting records.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): This field is for providing the name of the contact person associated with the credit memo request.

### Contact E-mail
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is for providing the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for providing the phone number of the contact person.

### Credit Memo Details
* **Credit Memo Details** (`credit_memo_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional details about the credit memo request.

### Request Date
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): This field is for specifying the date the credit memo request was made.

### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): This field is for specifying any payment terms associated with the credit memo request.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This field is for adding any additional information that may be helpful for our accounting team to process your credit memo request.

## Tips
* Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your credit memo request is processed efficiently.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact your accounting department.
