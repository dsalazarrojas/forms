# Maintenance Request Contact Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with the required information:

1. **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your user account or organization.
3. **Phone**: Provide a valid phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
4. **Message**: Type a clear and concise description of the maintenance request or concern.
5. **Reason**: Select the reason for the maintenance request. Please choose 'Yes' for issues that are currently affecting the facility or 'No' for general inquiries.
6. **Description**: Provide more details about the maintenance request, including any relevant information or context.
7. **Priority**: Indicate the urgency level of the maintenance request. Choose 'Low', 'Medium', or 'High' based on the criticality and severity of the issue.
8. **Expected Resolution Time**: Estimate the time frame for the resolution of the request, if known.
9. **Assigned To**: Select the person or team member you'd like to be assigned to handle your maintenance request.
10. **Confirmation**: Confirm that you'd like to receive a confirmation email after your request has been submitted. (Please note that this field is required.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Your official email address associated with your user account or organization.
* **Phone**: A valid phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Message**: A clear and concise description of the maintenance request or concern.
* **Reason**: Select the reason for the maintenance request, indicating whether it's affecting the facility or a general inquiry.
* **Description**: Provide more details about the maintenance request, including any relevant information or context.
* **Priority**: Choose the urgency level of the maintenance request, with 'Low' for non-urgent issues and 'High' for critical issues.
* **Expected Resolution Time**: Estimate the time frame for the resolution of the request, if known.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person or team member you'd like to be assigned to handle your maintenance request.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you'd like to receive a confirmation email after your request has been submitted.

## Tips

* Be as clear and concise as possible when describing the maintenance request or concern.
* Attach any relevant supporting documents or images to provide more context.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the assigned person or team.
* Please note that this form is for maintenance requests only. If you have a general inquiry or question, please use the designated inquiry form.
