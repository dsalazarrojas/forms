# Literature Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literature Change Request Form is used to request changes to literature materials, such as corrections, updates, or additions. This form ensures that your changes are accurately documented and reviewed by the relevant team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct **Category** for your request (e.g., "Request Forms") to ensure your form is properly processed.
2. Provide a clear **description** of the change you're requesting, including the specific reasons and details for the change.
3. Specify the **Approved By** person or team who will review and approve the change.
4. Select the person or team who will **submit the request** (e.g., "chatjimmy").
5. Enter the **Date** the request is submitted.
6. Provide any additional **Comments** regarding the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (1)
	+ This is the main section where you'll provide your request.
* **Category** (2)
	+ Select the category for your request. This helps the team process your form correctly.
* **Description** (3)
	+ Describe the change you're requesting, including reasons and details.
* **Approved By** (4)
	+ Specify the person or team responsible for reviewing and approving your request.
* **Submitted by** (5)
	+ Choose the person submitting the request.
* **Date** (6)
	+ Enter the date the request is submitted.
* **Time** (7)
	+ Enter the time the request is submitted.
* **Comments** (8)
	+ Provide any additional comments or context for your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* Double-check that you've selected the correct category and approved person/team.
* If you're unsure about any field or need assistance, reach out to the relevant team for guidance.
