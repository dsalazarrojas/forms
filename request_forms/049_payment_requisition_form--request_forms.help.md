# Payment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Requisition Form is used to request payment for work or services provided. It captures information about the employee, payment details, and user involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee" field.
2. Select the employee's department in the "Department" field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Choose the type of payment being requested ("Yes" or "No") in the "Payment Type" field.
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
6. Enter the date of the payment in the "Payment Date" field.
7. Choose whether the payment is assigned to a user ("Yes" or "No") in the "Assigned User" field.
8. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the "Phone" field.
10. Select the company name in the "Company" field (optional).
11. Select the department of the user in the "Department 2" field (optional).
12. Enter the goals or purpose of the payment in the "Goals" field (optional).
13. Enter any comments about the payment in the "Comments" field (optional).
14. Select the status of the payment ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Status" field.
15. Choose the payment method ("Yes" or "No") in the "Payment Method" field.
16. Select whether the form was submitted by the employee ("Yes" or "No") in the "Submitted by" field.
17. Select whether the form was created by the employee ("Yes" or "No") in the "Created by" field.
18. Select whether the form was updated by someone else ("Yes" or "No") in the "Updated by" field.
19. Select whether the form was approved by someone else ("Yes" or "No") in the "Approved by" field.
20. Select whether the form was declined by someone else ("Yes" or "No") in the "Declined by" field.
21. Enter any comments about the payment in the "Comments 1" field (optional).
22. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted" field.
23. Enter the date the form was updated in the "Updated" field.
24. Select whether the form has been deleted ("Yes" or "No") in the "Deleted" field.

* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the payment.
* **Department**: Select the department of the employee.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Payment Type**: Choose the type of payment being requested.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of the payment.
* **Assigned User**: Select whether the payment is assigned to a user.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company (optional).
* **Department 2**: Select the department of the user (optional).
* **Goals**: Enter the goals or purpose of the payment (optional).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about the payment (optional).
* **Status**: Select the status of the payment.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method.
* **Submitted by**: Select whether the form was submitted by the employee.
* **Created by**: Select whether the form was created by the employee.
* **Updated by**: Select whether the form was updated by someone else.
* **Approved by**: Select whether the form was approved by someone else.
* **Declined by**: Select whether the form was declined by someone else.
* **Comments 1**: Enter any comments about the payment (optional).
* **Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Updated**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Deleted**: Select whether the form has been deleted.

* Double-check the accuracy of the payment details.
