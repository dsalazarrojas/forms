# Subscription Pause Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers or their representatives to request to pause or stop a subscription. It is intended to be filled out by customers who wish to temporarily or permanently pause their subscription due to various reasons. This form is reviewed and approved by our team before any action is taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply go through the following steps:

1. Select either 'pause' or 'stop' from the first option to indicate your desired action for your subscription.
2. Provide a brief reason for pausing the subscription in the 'reason for pause' field (only required if you selected 'pause').
3. Choose a date when you expect to resume your subscription (only required if you selected 'pause').
4. Enter any additional contact information you would like to share with us in the 'contact info' field.
5. Enter your name in the 'name' field.
6. Enter your email address in the 'email address' field.
7. Enter your phone number in the 'phone number' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pause or stop subscription** (`subscription_pause_request_form_1`): Select the desired action for your subscription, which can be either 'pause' or 'stop'.
* **reason for pause** (`reason_for_pause`): Please provide a brief reason for pausing your subscription.
* **expected date of resume** (`expected_date_of_resume`): Only fill out this field if you selected 'pause' as your desired action. This date is when you expect to resume your subscription.
* **contact info** (`contact_info`): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any additional contact information you would like to share with us.
* **name** (`name`): Enter your full name here.
* **email address** (`email`): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone number** (`phone_number`): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct action ('pause' or 'stop') for your subscription.
* Be as specific as possible when providing the reason for pause and expected date of resume.
* If you would like to be contacted by us, please provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
