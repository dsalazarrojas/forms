# Heavy Equipment Access Mat Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to heavy equipment or materials on a site. It ensures that the site manager and job manager are aware of and approve each request for equipment access.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out this form to make a new request for heavy equipment or material access.
* Select the options for each field as requested.
* Ensure all fields are filled out completely before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: This is the main section of the form where you provide the details of your request.
    * **Request Form** (`request_form`, `text`, optional): This is the main field for entering your request details. Please provide as much information as possible about the equipment or material you need access to.
* **Site Manager**: This field indicates whether the site manager has approved your request.
    * **Site Manager** (`site_manager`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the site manager has approved your request, 'No' otherwise.
* **Mat Number**: This is the number of the material or equipment being requested.
    * **Mat Number** (`mat_number`, `text`, required): Please enter the mat number of the equipment or material you are requesting access to.
* **Mat Type**: This field indicates whether the equipment or material is active or inactive.
    * **Mat Type** (`mat_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the equipment or material is active, 'No' if it's inactive.
* **Request Date**: This is the date you are requesting access to the equipment or material.
    * **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you are requesting access to the equipment or material.
* **Request Time**: This is the time you are requesting access to the equipment or material.
    * **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, required): Please enter the time you are requesting access to the equipment or material.
* **Site Location**: This is the location on the site where the equipment or material is needed.
    * **Site Location** (`site_location`, `text`, required): Please enter the location on the site where you need access to the equipment or material.
* **Job Number**: This is the job number associated with the request.
    * **Job Number** (`job_number`, `text`, required): Please enter the job number associated with your request.
* **Contractor**: This field indicates whether a contractor is involved in the request.
    * **Contractor** (`contractor`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if a contractor is involved in your request, 'No' otherwise.
* **Rental Provider**: This field indicates whether a rental provider is involved in the request.
    * **Rental Provider** (`rental_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if a rental provider is involved in your request, 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Manager**: This field indicates whether the job manager is aware of the request.
    * **Job Manager** (`job_manager`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if the job manager is aware of your request, 'No' otherwise.
* **Mat Comment**: This is a field for site manager comments.
    * **Mat Comment** (`mat_comment`, `note`, required): This field is for site manager comments on your request.
* **Email**: This is your contact email address.
    * **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: This is your contact phone number.
    * **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Request Status**: This field indicates the status of your request.
    * **Request Status** (`request_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your request, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Site Manager Comment**: This field is for site manager comments.
    * **Site Manager Comment** (`site_manager_comment`, `note`, optional): This field is for site manager comments on your request.
* **Job Manager Comment**: This field is for job manager comments.
    * **Job Manager Comment** (`job_manager_comment`, `note`, optional): This field is for job manager comments on your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check that you have filled out all fields correctly before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your site manager or job manager for assistance.
