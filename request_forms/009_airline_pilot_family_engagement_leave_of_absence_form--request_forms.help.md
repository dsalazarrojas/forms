# Airline Pilot Family Engagement Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for airline pilots to request a leave of absence for family engagement purposes. Please use this form to submit your request in accordance with the company pilot agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your Pilot Full Name, Employee ID Number, and Current Rank.
2.  Select the correct Fleet Type and Leave Category from the provided options.
3.  Enter the total number of days you are requesting for leave.
4.  Briefly describe the reason for your leave.
5.  If applicable, provide information on scheduled flights affected during the leave period and indicate if you are scheduled for standby.
6.  Confirm that you are requesting this leave in accordance with the company pilot agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pilot Full Name** (`pilot_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your pilot ID.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your Employee ID Number as issued by the airline.
* **Current Rank** (`pilot_rank`, select_one, required): Select your current rank from the provided options (Captain, First Officer, Second Officer, or Training Captain).
* **Fleet Type** (`fleet_type`, text, required): Enter the type of aircraft fleet you are currently flying on (e.g. A320, B787).
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to begin your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Leave Category** (`leave_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of leave you are requesting (Parental Leave, Family Care Leave, Marriage Leave, Bereavement Leave, or Family Engagement Event).
* **Total Number of Days Requested** (`total_days_requested`, number, required): Enter the total number of days you are requesting for leave.
* **Description of Leave Purpose** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for your leave in 1-2 sentences.
* **Scheduled Flights Affected** (`scheduled_flights_affected`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the flight numbers affected during the leave period.
* **Are you scheduled for standby during this period?** (`standby_obligations`, select_one, required): Select if you are scheduled for standby during the leave period.
* **Has a potential swap or backup been identified?** (`backup_pilot_available`, text, optional): If applicable, describe if a potential swap or backup has been identified.
* **I confirm that I am requesting this leave in accordance with the company pilot agreement** (`pilot_signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are requesting this leave in accordance with the company pilot agreement.
