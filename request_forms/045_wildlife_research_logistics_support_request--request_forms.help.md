# Wildlife Research Logistics Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to aid research teams in requesting support and resources for their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your project's title and a brief description of your research objectives.
2. Specify the project location, duration, and start/end dates.
3. Choose the type of support you need, such as logistics, research, or field support.
4. Select the project's type (e.g., conservation research, wildlife monitoring, research development).
5. Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
6. Describe your research objectives, area, and any specific equipment or resources you need.
7. If applicable, provide the project's budget, research budget, and any other relevant financial information.
8. Specify any additional personnel or resources you need for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Briefly describe your project's title.
- **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of your project.
- **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, number, optional): Enter the project's duration in days or weeks.
- **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the project's start date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the project's end date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Research Team** (`research_team`, text, optional): Mention any research teams involved in the project.
- **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, optional): Specify the research institution or organization.
- **Field Support** (`field_support`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of support you need (Wildlife Support Team, Logistics Team, or Research Team).
- **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): Select the project's type (Conservation Research, Wildlife Monitoring, or Research Development).
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, text, optional): Describe your research objectives and goals.
- **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, optional): Specify the research area or location.
- **Research Description** (`research_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your research.
- **Project Funding** (`project_funding`, text, optional): Specify the project's funding source or budget.
- **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, text, optional): Describe the funding source.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the project's status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your research.
- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter the project's budget.
- **Research Budget** (`research_budget`, text, optional): Describe your research budget.
- **Personnel Needed** (`personnel_needed`, text, optional): Specify any additional personnel needed for the project.
- **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, text, optional): Describe any equipment needed for the project.
- **Vehicle Needed** (`vehicle_needed`, text, optional): Specify any vehicles needed for the project.
- **Field Location** (`field_location`, text, optional): Enter the field location where the research will take place.
