# Water Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Service Request Form is a tool for requesting water services such as Water Service, Sewer Service, and Meter Service. This form is intended to gather information from the service requester and facilitate the processing of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Water Service Request Form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name as the service requester in the **Service Requester Name** field.
* Enter your email address in the **Service Requester Email** field.
* Provide your address in the **Service Address** field.
* Specify the city in your address in the **Service Address City** field.
* Select the type of service you require from the options provided in the **Service Type** field (e.g., Water Service, Sewer Service, Meter Service).
* Choose the type of problem you are experiencing from the options provided in the **Service Problem Type** field (e.g., Leaking Faucets, Low Water Pressure, Discolored Water, Other).
* Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing in the **Problem Description** field.
* Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
* Select the date of the issue you are experiencing in the **Request Date** field.
* Optionally, provide any additional comments or details about your request in the **Service Requester Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Requester Name**: Enter your name as the person requesting the service.
* **Service Requester Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Service Address**: Enter your address where the service is required.
* **Service Address City**: Enter the city in your address.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require (e.g., Water Service, Sewer Service, Meter Service).
* **Service Problem Type**: Select the type of problem you are experiencing (e.g., Leaking Faucets, Low Water Pressure, Discolored Water, Other).
* **Problem Description**: Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Request Date**: Select the date of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Service Requester Comments**: Optionally, provide any additional comments or details about your request.
