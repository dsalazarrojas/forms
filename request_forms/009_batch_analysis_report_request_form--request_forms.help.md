# Batch Analysis Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Batch Analysis Report Request Form is used to request a batch analysis report from a laboratory or testing facility. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the report is generated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your work email address that is used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your work phone number that is used for communication.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the department that you belong to, such as Quality Assurance, Research and Development, Production, Manufacturing, Laboratory, or Other.
* **Batch ID Number** (`batch_id`, text, **Required**): Enter the unique identifier for the batch.
* **Batch Name** (`batch_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the product or material name associated with the batch.
* **Batch Production Date** (`batch_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date when the batch was produced.
* **Type of Analysis Required** (`analysis_type`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select all the types of analysis that apply, such as Chemical Composition, Physical Properties, Microbiological Testing, Purity Analysis, Contaminant Screening, Stability Testing, or Quality Verification.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the priority level of the analysis, such as Standard, Priority, Rush, or Emergency.
* **Sample Quantity** (`sample_quantity`, text, **Required**): Enter the amount of sample provided.
* **Sample Storage Requirements** (`sample_storage`, text, **Optional**): Specify any special storage needs for the sample.
* **Testing Standards to Follow** (`testing_standards`, select_multiple, **Optional**): If required, select the testing standards to follow, such as ISO 9001, ISO 17025, ASTM, USP, EP, Internal SOP, or Other.
* **Turnaround Date** (`turnaround_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date when you need the results.
* **Preferred Report Format** (`report_format`, select_one, **Required**): Select the preferred report format, such as PDF Document, Excel Spreadsheet, Both PDF and Excel, or Digital Dashboard.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **Optional**): Enter any other relevant information that you would like to share with the laboratory or testing facility.
