# Process Optimization Roadmap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Process Optimization Roadmap Request Form is designed to collect and document requests for process improvement from end-users. This form is used to identify areas where processes can be optimized or improved, enabling the business to streamline and enhance performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate request for process optimization. Verify with your supervisor or process owner that your request is not a duplicate or already in the queue.
2.  Click on the "Process Optimization Request" page to start filling out the form.
3.  Enter a brief description of your goal in the "Goal" field. This is a required field, so be sure to include all relevant details about what you aim to achieve with this process improvement.
4.  Select a specific timeline for completion in the "Timeline" field. Be realistic with your expected completion date, taking into account any constraints or dependencies.
5.  Choose the relevant consultant from the list of options in the "Consultant" field. If you're not sure which consultant is best suited, you can discuss with your supervisor or process owner.
6.  Select any applicable operations teams from the list in the "Operations Team" field. Be sure to choose all teams relevant to your process.
7.  In the "Additional Info" field, provide any further details or context that will help the consultant and operations teams understand your request.
8.  If you need to prioritize this request, enter a number in the "Priority" field. This will help the consultant and operations teams allocate resources accordingly.
9.  Enter the deadline for completion in the "Deadline" field. Ensure this date is realistic and takes into account any constraints or dependencies.
10.  If the consultant or operations teams need further clarification or details, select the review status and provide comments in the "Review Status" and "Review Comments" fields. These are optional but can be useful for the consultant or operations teams to address any issues or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Process Optimization Request**: 
  * This field is a note-taking area to briefly describe your process optimization request. Use this to summarize your idea and provide a quick reference for future review.
* **Goal**: 
  * Enter a clear and concise description of your goal for process optimization. This could be an area for improvement in the business process, a problem to be solved, or a new idea for enhancement.
* **Timeline**: 
  * Select a specific date for completion, taking into account any dependencies, resources, and potential constraints.
* **Consultant**: 
  * Choose the relevant consultant from the provided options. This will ensure that the right specialist is assigned to handle your process optimization request.
* **Operations Team**: 
  * Select all relevant operations teams required to execute your process optimization request. This will help ensure that the right teams are engaged in the project.
* **Additional Info**: 
  * Provide any additional details or context that will support your process optimization request.
* **Priority**: 
  * If necessary, assign a priority level for your request, helping the consultant and operations teams to allocate resources effectively.
* **Deadline**: 
  * Enter the expected completion date for your process optimization request. Be realistic with your deadline and take into account any dependencies or constraints.
* **Review Status**: 
  * If the consultant or operations teams need further clarification or details, select the review status to indicate their progress.
* **Review Comments**: 
  * Provide comments or feedback for the consultant or operations teams to address any issues or concerns.
