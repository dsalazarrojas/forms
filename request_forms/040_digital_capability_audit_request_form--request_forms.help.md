# Digital Capability Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Capability Audit Request Form is a tool used to gather information about digital capabilities you are requesting access to. This form will help our team evaluate and fulfill your requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, review the fields below:

*   Each field is labeled with a brief description to help you provide accurate information.
*   If a field is not relevant to your request, leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Name** (`user_input_1`, Text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears in our records.
*   **Requesting Team** (`user_input_2`, Select One, Optional): Choose the team that is requesting access to the digital capability.
*   **Request Type** (`user_input_3`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate which type of access you are requesting.
*   **Date Requested** (`user_input_4`, Date, Optional): Enter the date on which you are requesting access.
*   **Time Requested** (`user_input_5`, Time, Optional): Enter the time on which you are requesting access.
*   **Location** (`user_input_6`, Text, Optional): Specify where you will be accessing the digital capability (e.g., on campus or remotely).
*   **Contact** (`user_input_7`, Email, Optional): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (`user_input_8`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Description** (`user_input_9`, Note, Optional): Provide a brief description of the requested digital capability.
*   **Reason** (`user_input_10`, Text, Optional): Explain why you are requesting access to the digital capability.
*   **Notes** (`user_input_11`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or requests.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`user_input_12`, Select One, Optional): Choose the tool assigned to fulfill your request.
*   **Form Owner** (`user_input_13`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for fulfilling your request.
*   **Form Version** (`user_input_14`, Text, Optional): Enter the version of the form you are using.
*   **Last Updated** (`user_input_15`, Date, Optional): Enter the last date this form was updated.
*   **Last Updated Time** (`user_input_16`, Time, Optional): Enter the last time this form was updated.
*   **Last Updated User** (`user_input_17`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the last user who updated this form.
*   **Last Updated By** (`user_input_18`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the last user who updated this form.
*   **Last Updated Date** (`user_input_19`, Date, Optional): Enter the date this form was last updated.
*   **Last Updated Time** (`user_input_20`, Time, Optional): Enter the time this form was last updated.
*   **Last Updated By User** (`user_input_21`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the last user who updated this form.
*   **Submission Date** (`user_input_22`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
*   **Submission Time** (`user_input_23`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
*   **Submission User** (`user_input_24`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears in our records.
*   **Submission By** (`user_input_25`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the user who submitted this form.

## Tips
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your team lead or IT representative.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any delays in processing your request.
