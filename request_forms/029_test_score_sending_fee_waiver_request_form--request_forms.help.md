# Test Score Sending Fee Waiver Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your reason for requesting a fee waiver for sending your test scores to educational institutions. Please answer each question carefully and provide as much detail as possible to support your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current status in our program.
2. Enter your name as the student applicant.
3. Enter your guardian/parent's name.
4. Enter your student email address.
5. Enter your student phone number.
6. Enter the name of your school or institution.
7. Select the year you attended.
8. Enter the start and end dates of your attendance.
9. Select the type of score sending fee waiver requests you are making (e.g., test scores, score reports, etc.).
10. Briefly describe the documentation supporting your request for a score sending fee waiver.
11. Provide an electronic signature of your guardian or parent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Status in our Program** (Select One): This field asks for your current status in our program (e.g., pending, active, etc.). Please select the option that best describes your situation.
* **Student Name** (Text): Enter your name as the student applicant.
* **Guardian/Parent Name** (Text): Enter the name of your guardian or parent who is signing this form.
* **Student Email** (Email): Enter your student email address. This is a required field.
* **Student Phone** (Text): Enter your student phone number.
* **School/Institution Name** (Text): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Year of Attendance** (Number): Select the year you attended the institution.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of your attendance.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of your attendance.
* **Type of Score Sending Fee Waiver Requests** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply for the type of score sending fee waiver requests you are making (e.g., test scores, score reports, etc.).
* **Documentation of Score Sending Fee Waiver Requests** (Text): Briefly describe the documentation supporting your request for a score sending fee waiver.
* **eSignature of Guardian/Parent** (Text): Provide an electronic signature of your guardian or parent.

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and provide as much detail as possible to support your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your request and respond as soon as possible.
