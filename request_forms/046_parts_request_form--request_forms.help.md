# Parts Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parts Request Form is a tool used by the support team to collect information from customers about their parts requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the customer making the request.
2. Enter your email address (optional).
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Enter the name of the product you are requesting.
5. Enter the quantity of the product you need.
6. If you have any additional notes about your order, please add them in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Required): Please enter your name as the customer making the request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, Optional): This field is for entering your email address. If you have one, please provide it to help us get in touch with you faster.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it here. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, Required): Please enter the name of the product you are requesting.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Required): Enter the quantity of the product you need. This will help us ensure we get you the correct amount.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, Optional): If you have any additional notes or instructions about your order, please add them here. This can include any special requests or concerns you may have.

## Tips
Please ensure you enter accurate and complete information in each field to help us process your request efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
