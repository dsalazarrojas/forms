# internal_communication_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal communication requests between employees. You can use this form to send a message to a specific employee about a general inquiry, issue, or request for IT, Finance, HR, or Facilities assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee ID to help us verify your identity.
2. Provide your name so we can address you personally in our response.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used as a point of contact for our response.
4. Type the content of your message and describe the issue or inquiry.
5. Select the category of issue or request that best describes your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (1, Number, Required): Enter your employee ID, which is a unique identifier for you in our system.
* **Name** (2, Text, Required): Type your name, which will help us address you personally in our response.
* **Email** (3, Email, Required): Enter your email address, which will be used for our response.
* **Message** (4, Text, Required): Describe the issue or inquiry you have, and provide any relevant details.
* **Issue Type** (5, Select One, Required): Choose the category that best describes your message (General, IT, Finance, HR, or Facilities).
* **Attachment** (6, Note, Optional): If you have any supporting files or documents, you can attach them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Employee ID, Name, Email, Message, and Issue Type).
* If you are unsure about any of the categories, please choose the most general category (General).
