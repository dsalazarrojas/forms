# Tutorial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users requesting a tutorial or educational resource. The data collected will be used to tailor the tutorial to the user's needs and ensure it is relevant and helpful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it is a valid address.
3. If applicable, enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select the grade level you are interested in the "Grade Level" field.
5. Enter the subject you are interested in learning more about in the "Subject" field.
6. Enter the name of your teacher or instructor in the "Teacher Name" field.
7. Briefly describe the specific question or topic you are interested in learning about in the "Question" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field. This is optional.
9. Select the tool assigned to you by your teacher or instructor in the "Assigned Tool" field. If you don't have an assigned tool, select "None".
10. Finally, enter the date you are submitting this request in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation (ID, passport, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your email account.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): If you are a student or employee of a school, enter the name of your school. If you are not affiliated with a school, leave this field blank.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the grade level you are currently at or are interested in (1, 2, 3, or 4).
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Enter the subject you are interested in learning more about (e.g., Math, Science, English, etc.).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher or instructor.
* **Question** (`question`, text, required): Briefly describe the specific question or topic you are interested in learning about.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here. This is optional.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you by your teacher or instructor. If you don't have an assigned tool, select "None".
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
