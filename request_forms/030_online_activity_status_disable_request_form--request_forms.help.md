# online_activity_status_disable_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request disabling of online activities. A user can fill out this form when they have a valid reason for requesting the disabling of an online activity and want to provide additional information or context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including providing your email and phone number if applicable.
2. Select the correct team from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose whether the online activity is active or inactive.
4. Select your user preferences for the online activity.
5. Enter a brief reason for requesting the disabling of the online activity.
6. Enter any additional comments regarding the request.
7. Choose who the request should be assigned to.
8. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User info**: This field is required and should contain the user's name and contact information.
  - It will help us to contact you regarding your request.
- **Reason**: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide a valid reason for requesting the disabling of the online activity.
  - It will help us to understand the context of your request.
- **Date request**: This field is optional and should be the date when you would like the online activity to be disabled.
  - It will help us to schedule the disabling of the online activity.
- **User preferences**: This field allows you to select your preferences for the online activity.
  - It will help us to ensure that the online activity is disabled according to your preferences.
- **Team**: This field is optional and should be the team you are a part of.
  - It will help us to determine which team you are requesting the disabling for.
- **Online activity**: This field is optional and should be whether the online activity is active or inactive.
  - It will help us to understand the current status of the online activity.
- **Submit**: This field is required and should be used to submit your complete form.
  - It will help us to process your request.
- **Email**: This field is optional and should be your email address.
  - It will help us to contact you regarding your request.
- **Phone**: This field is optional and should be your phone number.
  - It will help us to contact you regarding your request.
- **Status**: This field is optional and should be the status of the online activity.
  - It will help us to understand the current status of the online activity.
- **Comments**: This field is required and should contain any additional comments or information regarding your request.
  - It will help us to understand your request better.
- **Assigned to**: This field is optional and should be the person you would like the request to be assigned to.
  - It will help us to determine who will process your request.
