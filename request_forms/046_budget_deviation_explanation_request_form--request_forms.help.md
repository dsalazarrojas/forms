# Budget Deviation Explanation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Budget Deviation Explanation Request Form is a tool used to report and track financial discrepancies, ensuring transparency and accurate management of departmental budget deviations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Ensure that you are reporting a legitimate deviation that has not been previously approved or denied.
*   Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all necessary information.
*   Review the form before submitting it to ensure that you have included all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request date** (`request_date`, required): The date on which the deviation was discovered or requested.
    Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Requester name** (`requester_name`, required): The person requesting the deviation.
    Enter the name of the person requesting the deviation.
*   **Department** (`department`, required): The department requesting the deviation.
    Enter the name of the department requesting the deviation.
*   **Amount deviation** (`amount_deviation`, required): The amount over or under budget.
    Enter the amount in currency (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.). 
*   **Reason for deviation** (`reason_for_deviation`, required): Explain why the budget deviated.
    Clearly and concisely explain the reason for the deviation.
*   **Proposed action** (`proposed_action`, optional): Suggested corrective or approval action.
    Enter a suggested action to address the deviation (optional).
*   **Approval status** (`approval_status`, required): Finance approval status.
    Select from the options: Approved, Pending, Denied.
*   **Approver comments** (`approver_comments`, optional): Finance notes on the decision.
    Enter notes from the finance department regarding the approval status (optional).

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check the form before submitting it to avoid any errors or miscommunication.
*   Be clear and concise in your explanations to avoid any confusion.
