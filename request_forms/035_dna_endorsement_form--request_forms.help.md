# dna_endorsement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about DNA samples for endorsement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) on each page.
2. For the "Reason For Request" field, explain the reason why you are requesting a DNA sample.
3. Provide details about the DNA sample in the "DNA Sample" field.
4. Enter the collection date and time for the DNA sample.
5. If applicable, specify the collection location where the DNA sample was collected.
6. Enter the submitter's information, including their name and agency affiliation.
7. Choose the method used to collect the DNA sample.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Individual/Agency** (`text`, optional): This field allows you to select whether this is an individual or agency request.
* **Reason For Request** (`text`, required): This field is for providing a brief explanation of why the DNA sample is requested.
* **DNA Sample** (`text`, required): This field is for providing details about the DNA sample, such as its type or origin.
* **Collection Date** (`date`, required): This field is for entering the date when the DNA sample was collected.
* **Collection Time** (`time`, optional): This field is for entering the time when the DNA sample was collected.
* **Collection Location** (`text`, required): This field is for specifying the location where the DNA sample was collected.
* **Submitter** (`text`, required): This field is for entering the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Agency** (`text`, required): This field is for specifying the submitter's agency affiliation.
* **Collection Method** (`text`, required): This field is for choosing the method used to collect the DNA sample.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes about the DNA sample.
