# Membership Freeze Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by a user if they wish to request a membership freeze. This feature is typically used when a user is concerned about their account's impact on the community or when they need to pause their account temporarily due to personal reasons.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Membership Freeze Request" link/button to access the form.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in the required fields:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
   - Reason for Request
3. If applicable, provide an additional reason for your request in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
4. Enter a detailed explanation for your request in the "Please enter the reason for your request" field.
5. Enter your password to confirm your identity.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the request to the platform administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your account.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and verification purposes.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Enter the reason why you're requesting a membership freeze. Please be specific and honest in your explanation.
* **Other (If applicable)** (`reason_for_request_other`, text, optional): If your reason for requesting a membership freeze is not listed, enter a brief description of your reason here.
* **Please enter the reason for your request** (`other_reason`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation for your request, including any supporting evidence or context that may help the administrators understand your request.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Enter your account password to confirm your identity and authenticity of the request.
