# Community Radio Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Radio Access Request Form is used to request access to the community radio studio and airtime. This form is for requesting studio access, airtime, or equipment rental for live radio shows, studio recording, interview, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Requesting Individual or Group** (`radio_requestor_name`): Enter the name of the individual or group requesting access.
* **Current Member Status** (`member_status_selection`): Select the member status that best fits your group or individual status:
	+ Active Station Member: A member who has already participated in a live broadcast at the station.
	+ Non-Member Resident: A non-member individual or group that is not yet a member of the station.
	+ Local Student: A student from the local area who may be eligible for special privileges.
	+ Organization Partner: A partner organization with a formal agreement with the station.
* **Contact Email Address** (`radio_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the individual or group requesting access.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`radio_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the individual or group requesting access.
* **Request Type** (`radio_request_type`): Select the primary reason for your request:
	+ Airtime - Live Radio Show: For live broadcasts.
	+ Studio Recording Time: For recording purposes.
	+ Studio Interview: For interviews.
	+ Equipment Rental: For equipment rental.
	+ Volunteer Training: For training purposes.
	+ Other: For other purposes, specify below.
* **If Other - Please Specify** (`request_type_other_specify`): If you selected "Other" above, enter a brief description of your request.
* **Proposed Show or Project Title** (`proposed_radio_title`): Enter a brief title of your proposed show or project.
* **Brief Summary of Your Show or Project** (`proposed_radio_summary`): Describe your proposed show or project in a brief summary.
* **Frequency of Access Request** (`radio_request_frequency`): Select the frequency of access you require:
	+ One-time Special Event: For one-time events.
	+ Weekly Recurring: Regular weekly broadcasts.
	+ Monthly Recurring: Regular monthly broadcasts.
* **Requested Start Date** (`radio_start_date_requested`): Select the date when you would like to start accessing our studio and equipment.
* **Preferred Time Slot - Start** (`preferred_radio_time_start`): Select your preferred start time for your broadcast or recording.
* **Preferred Time Slot - End** (`preferred_radio_time_end`): Select your preferred end time for your broadcast or recording.
* **Days of the Week Requested** (`radio_days_of_week_requested`): Select the days of the week you would like to have access to the studio and equipment.
* **Technical Support Needed?** (`radio_technical_support_needs`): Check if you need technical support or training for your access.
* **Have You Completed Our Radio Studio Training Course?** (`completed_radio_training_check`): Confirm if you have completed the required training course.
* **Radio Access Terms and Conditions** (`radio_terms_conditions_note`): Read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* **Today's Date** (`radio_request_submission_date`): Select the current date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the station administration.
