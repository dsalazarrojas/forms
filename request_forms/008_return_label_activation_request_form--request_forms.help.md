## Return Label Activation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return Label Activation Request Form is used to activate a return label for a customer. This form requires information about the order details, return reasons, and customer contact information. The form helps the customer service team to process the return request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for the return. Choose one or multiple options from the list provided:
	* Return for a refund
	* Return for an exchange
	* Return for store credit
2. Enter your order details, which will help the customer service team to locate your order and process the return efficiently.
3. Provide your email address so that the customer service team can contact you for further information or updates.
4. Enter your phone number, if you have one, so that the customer service team can contact you for further information or updates.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your return request.
6. Enter your name and address so that the customer service team can contact you and update you on the status of your return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter the order number or a brief description of your order, which will help the customer service team to locate your order quickly.
* **Return Reasons** (`return_reasons`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple reasons for the return. This will help the customer service team to process your return request efficiently.
* **Customer Consent** (`customer_consent`, number, optional): If you have any specific instructions or conditions for the return, enter them here. Note that this field is not required.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that the customer service team can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here so that the customer service team can contact you for further information or updates.
* **Notes** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your return request.
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name, which will help the customer service team to address you correctly.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your address, which will help the customer service team to contact you and update you on the status of your return.

This help guide is designed to make it easy for customers to fill out the form correctly and efficiently. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact our customer service team.
