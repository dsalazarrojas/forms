# Repair Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Quote Form is used to collect information from customers about their repair needs and preferences. This information is used to provide an accurate quote for the requested service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Title" field with a brief description of the repair service.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "customer name" field.
3. Provide the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the customer's email address in the "email address" field.
5. Describe the service that needs to be repaired in the "service description" field.
6. Select the type of service needed from the "service type" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the estimated cost of the service in the "Estimated Cost" field.
8. Provide a detailed description of the repair in the "Repair Description" field.
9. Specify who authorized the repair in the "Authorized By" field.
10. Enter the date of authorization in the "Authorization Date" field.
11. Check the "Repair Agreement" field to confirm the customer's agreement to the repair.
12. Select the satisfaction guarantee from the "Satisfaction Guarantee" dropdown menu.
13. Select the services that have been agreed upon in the "Service Agreed" field.
14. Enter the service address in the "service address" field.
15. Enter the postal code of the service location in the "Postal Code" field.
16. Enter the service city in the "service city" field.
17. Enter the service state in the "service state" field.
18. Enter the service country in the "service country" field.
19. Enter any additional comments or notes about the service in the "service note" field.
20. Specify the status of the service in the "Service Status" dropdown menu.
21. (Optional) Enter any service comments or notes in the "Service Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (`title`, text, optional): A brief description of the repair service.
- **customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): The name of the customer.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): The customer's phone number.
- **email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): The customer's email address.
- **service description** (`service_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the service that needs to be repaired.
- **service type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): The type of service needed.
- **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, required): The estimated cost of the service.
- **Repair Description** (`repair_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the repair.
- **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, text, required): The person who authorized the repair.
- **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, optional): The date of authorization.
- **Repair Agreement** (`repair_agreement`, note, required): A confirmation of the customer's agreement to the repair.
- **Satisfaction Guarantee** (`satisfaction_guarantee`, select_one, required): The satisfaction guarantee for the service.
- **Service Agreed** (`service_agreed`, select_multiple, required): The services that have been agreed upon.
- **service address** (`service_address`, text, required): The service address.
- **Postal Code** (`service_postal_code`, number, required): The postal code of the service location.
- **service city** (`service_city`, text, required): The service city.
- **service state** (`service_state`, text, required): The service state.
- **service country** (`service_country`, text, required): The service country.
- **service note** (`service_note`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the service.
- **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the service.
- **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, required): The status of the service.
- **Service Priority** (`service_priority`, select_multiple, optional): The priority of the service.
- **Service Type Details** (`service_type_details`, text, optional): Any additional details about the service type.
- **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the service.
