# Workplace Violence Restraining Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is to be completed in case of a workplace incident involving violence. It is a crucial document that helps identify potential threats and take necessary measures to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose the correct options from the dropdown menus when applicable.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location of the incident.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Witness Statement**: Enter your witness statement.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Employer Information**: Enter your employer's information.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Incident Reporting Officer**: Enter the name of the officer who reported the incident.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Witness Signature**: Enter your signature.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Witness Date**: Enter the date of your witness statement.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Witness Time**: Enter the time of your witness statement.
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Reporting Officer Signature**: Enter the signature of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer Date**: Enter the date of the incident reporting officer's statement.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer Time**: Enter the time of the incident reporting officer's statement.
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer Name**: Enter the name of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer Job Title**: Enter the job title of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Reporting Officer E-mail**: Enter the email of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Required: `true`
	+ Options: `Label 1`, `Label 2`, `Label 3`
* **Incident Category**: Choose the category of the incident.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Required: `true`
	+ Options: `Label 1`, `Label 2`, `Label 3`
* **Protective Action Request**: Choose the protective action you would like to take.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Required: `true`
	+ Options: `Label 1`, `Label 2`, `Label 3`
* **Protective Measures**: Enter the measures you would like to take.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `true`
* **Perpetrator Details**: Enter the perpetrator's details.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Witness Signature**: Enter your signature.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Reporting Officer E-mail**: Enter the email of the incident reporting officer.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: `true`
