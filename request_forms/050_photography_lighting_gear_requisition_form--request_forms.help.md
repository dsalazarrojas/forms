# photography_lighting_gear_requisition_form_request_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for requisitioning photography lighting gear for an event or shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Form Details section with as much information as possible about the event or shoot.
2. Select the camera model and lens model that you plan to use.
3. Provide the location where the photography lighting gear will be needed.
4. Specify the equipment you are requesting.
5. Identify the contact person responsible for the requisitioning process.
6. Enter the contact's email address.
7. Enter the contact's phone number.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (Form Details, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the event or shoot, including any relevant details such as date, time, and venue.
* **Camera Model** (camera_model, text, required/optional): Select the model of camera you plan to use for the event or shoot.
* **Lens Model** (lens_model, text, required/optional): Select the model of lens you plan to use for the event or shoot.
* **Location** (location, text, required/optional): Enter the exact location where the photography lighting gear will be needed, including any relevant details such as building or room names.
* **Equipment Requested** (equipment_requested, text, required/optional): Specify the lighting equipment you are requesting, including any specific lights, power sources, or other gear.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, required/optional): Identify the person responsible for requisitioning and managing the photography lighting gear.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the requisitioning process or equipment requirements.
