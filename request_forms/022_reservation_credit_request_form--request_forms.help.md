The purpose of the Reservation Credit Request Form is to collect information from customers who wish to request a reservation credit. This form is used to capture details about the customer's reservation request, including their contact information, phone number, address, and credit card details. 

When submitting a reservation credit request, please ensure that you are not duplicating a previous request for the same purpose. If you have already submitted a credit request for a similar reservation, please do not submit another form.

### Reservation Credit Request Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary to process a reservation credit request.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Reservation Credit Request field with a brief description of your reservation request.
* Provide your contact information in the Contact Information field.
* Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field, using the XXX-XXXX format.
* Enter your address in the Address field.
* If you are submitting a credit card payment, enter your credit card number, card holder's name, and card type in the respective fields.
* If required, select your credit card type (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex) from the Card Type field.
* Finally, enter the expiration date of your credit card in the Expiration Date field.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Credit Request (1)**: A brief description of your reservation request.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Your phone number, in the XXX-XXXX format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Address (4)**: Your physical address.
* **Credit Card Number (5)**: The number of your credit card.
* **Card Holder Name (6)**: The name of the card holder.
* **Card Type (7)**: Select the type of credit card you are using (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
* **Expiration Date (8)**: The expiration date of your credit card in MM/YY format (e.g., 06/20).
