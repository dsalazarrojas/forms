# web_browsing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit web browsing requests, allowing your organization to track and manage these tasks efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your request in the **Web Browsing Request Title** field.
2. Provide your name as the **Requestor**.
3. Select the **Request Priority** as High, Medium, or Low, depending on the urgency of your request.
4. Fill in the **Date Submitted** and **Time Submitted** if the request is time-sensitive.
5. Provide a detailed **Description** of the task you want someone to perform.
6. Choose the **Status** of your request as Pending, In Progress, or Done.
7. If assigned, fill in the **Assigned To** field with the name of the person responsible for the task.
8. Optionally, select a **Due Date** for the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Web Browsing Request Title**: A brief title for your request.
* **Requestor**: The name of the person submitting the request.
* **Request Priority**: High, Medium, or Low, depending on the request's urgency.
* **Date Submitted**: The date the request was submitted.
* **Time Submitted**: The time the request was submitted.
* **Description**: A detailed description of the task you want someone to perform.
* **Status**: The current status of your request (Pending, In Progress, or Done).
* **Assigned To**: The name of the person responsible for completing the task.
* **Due Date**: The deadline for completing the task.

## Tips
