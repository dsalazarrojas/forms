# Printing Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers requesting a printing quotation. The form will be used to gather essential details such as contact information, printing requirements, and additional notes to provide an accurate quotation. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your quotation request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information to ensure that your quotation request is processed correctly.
2. Enter your name and email to allow us to contact you regarding your quotation.
3. Provide your phone number to enable us to reach out to you with any further questions or concerns.
4. Specify the quantity of printed materials needed.
5. Choose the paper size and orientation that suits your printing needs.
6. Select the color preference for your printed materials (Color, Black and White, or Gray).
7. Provide any additional notes or comments regarding your printing requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`printing_quotation_request_form_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our reference.
* **Email** (`printing_quotation_request_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your quotation.
* **Phone** (`printing_quotation_request_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
* **Quantity** (`printing_quotation_request_form_quantity`, number, optional): Specify the number of printed materials needed.
* **Paper Size** (`printing_quotation_request_form_paper_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the paper size that suits your printing needs (Large, Medium, Small).
* **Paper Orientation** (`printing_quotation_request_form_paper_orientation`, select_one, optional): Select the paper orientation that best suits your printing needs (Portrait, Landscape).
* **Color** (`printing_quotation_request_form_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color preference for your printed materials (Color, Black and White, Gray).
* **Additional Notes** (`printing_quotation_request_form_additional_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding your printing requirements.
* **Additional Notes 2** (`printing_quotation_request_form_additional_notes_2`, note, optional): Enter any further comments or notes regarding your printing requirements.
* **Requestor Name** (`printing_quotation_request_form_requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who is making the request.
