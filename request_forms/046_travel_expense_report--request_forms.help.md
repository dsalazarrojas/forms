# Travel Expense Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Expense Report form is for submitting expenses incurred while traveling for work-related activities. Please complete this form to request reimbursement for your travel expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the form's required fields:
    * Date of travel (Page 1, travel_date field): Enter the date of your trip.
    * Destination (Page 1, destination field): Enter the city or location you traveled to.
    * Travel Mode (Page 1, travel_mode field): Select whether you traveled by plane, train, car, etc.
    * Travel Cost (Page 1, travel_cost field): Enter the total cost of your trip.
- If necessary, fill out the non-required fields:
    * Purpose (Page 1, purpose field): Enter the business purpose of your trip.
    * Notes (Page 1, notes field): Enter any additional information or comments about your trip.
    * Email (Page 1, email field): Enter your email address.
    * Phone (Page 1, phone field): Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date (Page 1)**: Enter the date of your trip in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Destination (Page 1)**: Enter the city or location you traveled to.
* **Purpose (Page 1)**: Enter the business purpose of your trip, if applicable.
* **Travel Mode (Page 1)**: Select whether you traveled by plane, train, car, etc.
* **Travel Cost (Page 1)**: Enter the total cost of your trip.
* **Notes (Page 1)**: Enter any additional information or comments about your trip.
* **Email (Page 1)**: Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted about your report.
* **Phone (Page 1)**: Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted about your report.

## Tips
- Make sure to attach all necessary receipts and supporting documents to your report.
- Ensure that you have not already submitted a travel expense report for this trip.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or department manager for guidance.
