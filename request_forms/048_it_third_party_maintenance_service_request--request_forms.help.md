# IT Third Party Maintenance Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Third Party Maintenance Service Request form is designed to collect information from customers or users regarding a third-party maintenance service request related to IT. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from the user, ensuring that the maintenance service request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully and understand what information is required for each section.
* Fill in the Request Information section with details about the maintenance service request.
* In the Vendors Information section, provide details about the third-party vendor(s) involved in the maintenance service request.
* In the Schedule Information section, specify the desired schedule for the maintenance service request.
* If there is any additional information that is not covered in the previous sections, provide it in the Additional Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Information**: (`request_info`, text, required: false)
This section is designed to gather general information about the maintenance service request. Please provide a brief description of the service request, including any relevant dates and times.
- **Vendors Information**: (`vendor_info`, text, required: false)
This section is intended to gather details about the third-party vendor(s) involved in the maintenance service request. Provide the name and contact information of the vendor(s).
- **Schedule Information**: (`schedule_info`, text, required: false)
This section is used to specify the desired schedule for the maintenance service request. Please provide the dates and times you would like to have the service performed.
- **Additional Information**: (`additional_info`, text, required: false)
This section is for any additional information that may be relevant to the maintenance service request. Please provide any details that are not covered in the previous sections.
