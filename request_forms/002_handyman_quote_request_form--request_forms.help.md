# Handyman Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handyman Quote Request Form is a tool for users to submit a quote request for various services such as repairs, maintenance, and other services. It helps our team understand your requirements and provide you with a detailed quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the type of service you require (e.g., General Maintenance, Emergency Repair, or Other).
3. Specify the services you need (e.g., Repair, Maintenance, or Other).
4. Estimate the time required for the service.
5. Provide the date and time you would like the service to be performed.
6. Enter your location.
7. Add any additional information that may help us provide a more accurate quote.
8. Enter your name, phone number, and email address as contact information.
9. Write a message describing your request in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Please enter your name, phone number, and email address to help us contact you.
* **Request Type**: Choose the service type that best describes your request (e.g., General Maintenance, Emergency Repair, or Other).
* **Services Needed**: Enter the specific services you need, such as Repair, Maintenance, or Other).
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you may require (e.g., Repair, Maintenance, or Other).
* **Estimated Time**: Enter the estimated time required for the service.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like the service to be performed.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like the service to be performed.
* **Location**: Enter your location for our team to visit.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address as contact information.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Message**: Write a detailed message describing your request.
