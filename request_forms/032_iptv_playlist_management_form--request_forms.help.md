## Purpose
The IPTV Playlist Management Form is used to manage and update IPTV playlists for TV systems. It gathers information about the user requesting the update, the request details, TV system information, and permission settings for maintenance and support.

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email, and any other identifying information.
2. In the Request Info section, provide a detailed description of the request.
3. In the TV Info section, enter details about the TV system, such as its name, model, and location.
4. In the Permission Info section, select the relevant team(s) that should have access to the playlist update.
5. Provide ongoing maintenance details in the Ongoing Maintenance section.
6. List the Support Team Members who will be responsible for the update in the Support Team section.
7. In the Ongoing Maintenance Tasks section, describe the specific tasks to be performed.
