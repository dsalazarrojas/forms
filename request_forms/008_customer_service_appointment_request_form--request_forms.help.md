# customer_service_appointment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer service appointment request form is a tool for customers to submit their requests for assistance or support from the customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `customer_email` field. This will help the customer service team contact you back.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the `customer_phone` field.
4. In the `service_offered` field, select one or more of the options to indicate the type of service you are requesting.
5. Choose a date for the requested service in the `requested_date` field.
6. Select a time for the requested service in the `requested_time` field.
7. If you have any additional comments or information to provide, enter them in the `additional_comments` field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Request Form** (`customer_service_request_form`): This is the title of the form, which should not be filled out.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name so we can address your request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you via phone.
* **Service Offered** (`service_offered`): Select one or more of the options to indicate the type of service you are requesting.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are requesting assistance with a specific issue.
	+ Select "No" if you are not requesting any services.
* **Requested Date** (`requested_date`): Choose a date for the requested service.
* **Requested Time** (`requested_time`): Select a time for the requested service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
* **Submit** (`customer_service_request_form_submit`): Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when submitting your request.
* If you are requesting a follow-up appointment, please ensure you have selected the correct date and time.
* The customer service team will review and respond to your request as soon as possible.
