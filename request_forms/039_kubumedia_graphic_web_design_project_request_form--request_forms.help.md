# kubumedia_graphic_web_design_project_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather project requirements for a graphic web design project. Users are requested to fill in the form with their project details to enable the development team to initiate the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your project in the "Project Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Select the type of project you are requesting in the "Project Type" field.
7. Enter the deadline for the project in the "Project Deadline" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
8. Enter the estimated budget for the project in the "Project Budget" field.
9. Select the current status of your project in the "Project Status" field.
10. Add any additional notes about your project in the "Project Notes" field.
11. Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter a brief title of your project that summarizes your request.
* **Project Description** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Provide a short description of your project to help the development team understand your requirements.
* **Contact Name** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your name to enable the development team to contact you directly.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required: False):
	+ Enter your email address so the development team can contact you for further details.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Project Type** (Select One, Required: False):
	+ Choose the type of project you are requesting from the options provided.
* **Project Deadline** (Date, Required: False):
	+ Enter the deadline for the project in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Project Budget** (Number, Required: False):
	+ Enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Project Status** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select the current status of your project from the options provided.
* **Project Notes** (Note, Required: False):
	+ Add any additional notes or information about your project.
* **Form Submit** (Select One, Required: False):
	+ Click "Send" to submit the form to the development team.
* **Form Reset** (Select One, Required: False):
	+ Click "Clear" to reset the form and start fresh.
