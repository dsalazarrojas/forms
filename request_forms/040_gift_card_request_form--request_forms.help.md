Gift Card Request Form - Help Guide
================================

## Purpose
The Gift Card Request Form is designed to collect information from business users to request gift cards. This form is meant to be filled out by businesses that want to purchase gift cards for their employees or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are a valid business with a registered account.
2.  Fill in your business information, such as name and address, if you want to be contacted by us.
3.  Enter a valid email address for contact.
4.  Specify the value of the gift card you are requesting.
5.  Choose the business type of your company (Small, Medium, or Large).
6.  Add any message or comment you would like to include with your gift card request.
7.  Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User
*   **User** (`<name>: User`, `<type>: email`, `required: true`):
    *   Enter your company's email address that will be the recipient of the gift card.
### Gift Card Value
*   **Gift Card Value** (`<name>: gift_card_value`, `<type>: number`, `required: true`):
    *   Enter the value of the gift card you are requesting, which will be used for purchasing purposes.
### Business Name
*   **Business Name** (`<name>: business_name`, `<type>: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Optional: Enter your company's name if you want us to contact you.
### Business Address
*   **Business Address** (`<name>: business_address`, `<type>: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Optional: Enter your business address if you want us to contact you.
### Contact Person
*   **Contact Person** (`<name>: contact_person`, `<type>: text`, `required: true`):
    *   Enter a person to contact in case we need more information or clarification about your request.
### Email
*   **Email** (`<name>: email`, `<type>: email`, `required: false`):
    *   Optional: Enter an email address for us to contact you.
### Phone
*   **Phone** (`<name>: phone`, `<type>: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Optional: Enter your business phone number in case we need to contact you.
### Business Type
*   **Business Type** (`<name>: business_type`, `<type>: select_one`, `required: true`):
    *   Choose your business type from the provided options: Small Business, Medium Business, or Large Business.
### Submit
*   **Submit** (`<name>: submit`, `<type>: text`, `required: true`):
    *   This is the button to click to send your gift card request.
### Gift Card Message
*   **Gift Card Message** (`<name>: gift_card_message`, `<type>: text`, `required: false`):
    *   Enter a message or comment to include with your gift card request.
 
Tips
*   Please ensure the gift card value is accurate and corresponds to the actual value you are requesting.
*   Make sure to enter a valid email address for contact.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at your provided email address.
