# Wallpaper Request For Browser Extension - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users requesting a wallpaper for a browser extension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes why you are requesting a wallpaper.
2. Choose the type of product that the wallpaper will be used for (e.g. browser extension, mobile app, web app).
3. Provide any additional design requests or notes about the wallpaper.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for further communication.
5. Select the tool that you would like to work with (e.g. chatjimmy, other).
6. Choose the status of your submission (e.g. pending, in progress, done).
7. Add any additional notes about your submission.
8. Fill in the date and time you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **WallPaper Request** (`request_form`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter your wallpaper request.
* **Select Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes why you are requesting a wallpaper. This will help us understand your wallpaper preferences.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of product that the wallpaper will be used for (e.g. browser extension, mobile app, web app).
* **Design Request** (`design_request`, `note`, required: false): This field is where you can enter any additional design requests or notes about the wallpaper.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the tool that you would like to work with (e.g. chatjimmy, other).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of your submission (e.g. pending, in progress, done).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Fill in the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Fill in the time you submitted the form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, required: false): This field will be automatically populated with the time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, required: false): This field will be automatically populated with the time the form was updated.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Choose the correct category and product type to ensure that your wallpaper request is properly documented and assigned to the right development team member.
* Be specific with your design requests and notes to help the development team understand your preferences.
