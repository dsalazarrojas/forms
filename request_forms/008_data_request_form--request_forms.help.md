# data_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "data_request_form" is used to collect information from clients and partners for data requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to fill out the form.
2. Review the form sections and fields to understand what information is required.
3. Fill out each field accurately and consistently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Partner Name (2)**: Enter the name of the partner.
* **Contractor Name (3)**: Enter the name of the contractor.
* **Updated Information (4)**: Enter any updated information relevant to the request.
* **Request Date (5)**: Enter the date of the request in the requested format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Request Time (6)**: Enter the time of the request in the requested format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Client Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the client.
* **Client Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Partner Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the partner.
* **Partner Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the partner.
* **Contractor Email (11)**: Enter the email address of the contractor.
* **Contractor Phone (12)**: Enter the phone number of the contractor.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields or requirements, please consult with the relevant authorities or stakeholders.
* Fill out the form in a clear and legible format to ensure easy reading and processing.
