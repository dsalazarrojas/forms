# Equipment Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a piece of equipment that is in your possession. Please complete this form to report your equipment and provide details about its condition, purchase price, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Owner Information" page to provide details about the equipment's owner, such as their name and contact information.
2. On the "Equipment Description" page, enter a brief description of the equipment.
3. On the "Equipment Condition" page, select the condition of the equipment using the provided options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. On the "Equipment Serial Number" page, enter the serial number of the equipment.
5. On the "User Information" page, enter your own information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
6. On the "User Email" page, enter your email address.
7. On the "User Phone" page, enter your phone number.
8. On the "Date Purchased" page, select the date the equipment was purchased.
9. On the "Purchase Price" page, enter the amount paid for the equipment.
10. On the "Vendor Information" page, enter any additional information about the vendor.
11. Finally, on the "Remarks" page, enter any additional comments or remarks about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information (Owner Name, Email, Phone, Address)**: Enter the owner's name, email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Equipment Description (Description)**: Enter a brief description of the equipment, including its type, model, and any distinguishing features.
* **Equipment Condition (Condition)**: Select the condition of the equipment using one of the provided options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Equipment Serial Number (Serial Number)**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **User Information (Name, Email, Phone)**: Enter your own information, including your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **User Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **User Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date Purchased (Date)**: Select the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Purchase Price (Amount)**: Enter the amount paid for the equipment.
* **Vendor Information (Vendor Information)**: Enter any additional information about the vendor.
* **Remarks (Additional Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the equipment.
