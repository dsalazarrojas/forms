# Pool Management Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pool Management Request Form is designed to collect information for pool management purposes. To confirm that you are submitting a valid request, please review the form and ensure that you are requesting the correct type of service and providing accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Request" field, indicating if you are making a service request.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Pool Type" field, indicating the type of pool you are requesting management for.
3. Enter the "Name of Pool" field with the name of the pool you are requesting management for.
4. Enter the "Location" field with the location of the pool you are requesting management for.
5. In the "Service Requested" field, enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
6. Enter the "Service Date" field with the date of the service request.
7. Enter the "Time" field with the time of the service request.
8. In the "Service" field, enter the type of service you are requesting.
9. In the "Service Details" field, enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
10. Enter the "Pool Manager" field with the name of the person managing the pool.
11. Enter the "Phone" field with the contact phone number of the pool manager.
12. Enter the "Email" field with the email address of the pool manager.
13. Enter the "Pool Size" field with the size of the pool.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Pool Type" field, indicating the type of pool you are requesting management for.
15. In the "Pool Size Range" field, enter the range of pool sizes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" if you are making a service request, "No" if you are not making a service request.
* **Pool Type (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" for a specific type of pool, "No" otherwise.
* **Name of Pool**: Enter the name of the pool you are requesting management for.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the pool you are requesting management for.
* **Service Requested**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date of the service request.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the service request.
* **Service**: Enter the type of service you are requesting.
* **Service Details**: Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Pool Manager**: Enter the name of the person managing the pool.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the pool manager.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the pool manager.
* **Pool Size**: Enter the size of the pool.
* **Pool Type (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" for a specific type of pool, "No" otherwise.
* **Pool Size Range**: Enter the range of pool sizes.

Note: This form is for general pool management purposes and is not for emergency or urgent situations. If you have an emergency or urgent situation, please contact your pool manager directly.
