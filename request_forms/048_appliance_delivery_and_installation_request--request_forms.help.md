# Appliance Delivery And Installation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Appliance Delivery And Installation Request form is designed to collect information for the delivery and installation of appliances. It helps us understand your needs and preferences for a smooth and successful delivery and installation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your unique **Request ID** to identify your request.
2. Provide your **Request Date** for the delivery and installation.
3. Enter your **Customer Full Name**, including your first and last name.
4. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you for any updates or changes.
5. Specify your **Service Type**, which includes options like Delivery only, Installation only, Delivery and installation, Haul away old appliance, or Other.
6. Select the **Appliance Type** of the appliance you need delivery and installation for (e.g., Refrigerator, Range or oven, Dishwasher, etc.).
7. Enter the **Appliance Details**, including the brand and model of the appliance.
8. Indicate where the **Appliance was Purchased** (Our store, Another retailer, Online, or Other).
9. Provide your **Order Number** if it's applicable.
10. Enter your **Street Address**, **City**, and **State or Province** for delivery purposes.
11. Specify the **Property Type** of your residence (House, Apartment, Condominium, Townhouse, Mobile home, or Other).
12. Select the **Floor Level** where the appliance will be installed (e.g., Ground floor, 2nd floor, 3rd floor, etc.).
13. Indicate if an **Elevator is Available** for your building (Yes, No, Not Applicable).
14. If there is no elevator, specify the **Number of Flights of Stairs**.
15. Describe any **Access Challenges** you may have for the delivery and installation team (e.g., narrow doorways, tight corners, etc.).
16. Specify the **Preferred Delivery Date** and **Alternate Delivery Date** for your convenience.
17. Choose your **Preferred Time Window** for delivery (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
18. Specify any **Installation Requirements**, such as water or gas connections.
19. Indicate if you need the **Haul Away Service** for an old appliance.
20. Enter the **Old Appliance Type** and its condition (Working or Not working).
21. Provide any **Special Instructions** for the delivery and installation team.
22. Enter your **Gate or Entry Code** if applicable.
23. Specify **Parking Instructions** for the delivery team.
24. Indicate if **Someone will be Home** during the delivery and installation.
25. Choose your **Contact Preference** for any updates or changes.
26. Finally, provide your **Digital Signature** and the **Signature Date** to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request ID**: Unique request number
* **Request Date**: Date of request
* **Customer Full Name**: First and last name
* **Email Address**: Contact email
* **Phone Number**: Contact number
* **Service Type**: What service do you need (Delivery only, Installation only, Delivery and installation, Haul away old appliance, or Other)
* **Appliance Type**: What appliance you need delivery and installation for (e.g., Refrigerator, Range or oven, Dishwasher, etc.)
* **Appliance Details**: Brand and model of the appliance
* **Where was the Appliance Purchased**: Store or online retailer where the appliance was purchased
* **Order Number**: If applicable
* **Street Address**: Full street address for delivery purposes
* **City**: City or town
* **State or Province**: State or province
* **Property Type**: Type of residence (House, Apartment, Condominium, Townhouse, Mobile home, or Other)
* **Floor Level**: Which floor the appliance will be installed on
* **Elevator Available**: If there is an elevator in your building (Yes, No, Not Applicable)
* **Number of Flights of Stairs**: If there is no elevator
* **Access Challenges**: Any challenges for the delivery and installation team (e.g., narrow doorways, tight corners, etc.)
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: First choice date for delivery
* **Alternate Delivery Date**: Second choice date for delivery
* **Preferred Time Window**: When works best for delivery (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime)
* **Installation Requirements**: Any special requirements for installation (e.g., water or gas connections)
* **Haul Away Service**: If you need old appliance removal
* **Old Appliance Type**: Type of old appliance that needs removal
* **Old Appliance Condition**: Condition of old appliance (Working or Not working)
* **Special Instructions**: Any specific requirements or instructions for delivery and installation
* **Gate or Entry Code**: If applicable
* **Parking Instructions**: Where to park the delivery vehicle
* **Someone will be Home**: If someone will be present during delivery and installation
* **Contact Preference**: How you would like to be contacted for updates or changes
* **Digital Signature**: Your digital signature to confirm your request
* **Signature Date**: Date signed

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed to guarantee a successful delivery and installation.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your appliance and delivery preferences.
* If you have any special requirements or instructions, please specify them clearly.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns during the delivery and installation process.
