# inventory_import_issue_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "inventory_import_issue_contact_form" form is intended to collect contact information and details related to import-related issues.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact form with your name or the name of the contact person.
2. Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, or any other relevant details.
3. Add any additional information related to the issue you are reporting or inquiring about.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form (Required)**: A field to enter your name or the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Info (Required)**: Enter your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Additional Info (Optional)**: Provide any extra information that might be helpful in addressing the issue or providing context for the import-related problem.
## Tips
* Please ensure the contact information field is filled out for a timely response.
* Be as detailed as possible with the additional information field to help us better understand and assist with the import-related issue.
