# Academic Health Related Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Health Related Leave of Absence Request form is designed to support students who need to take a temporary leave from academic activities due to health reasons. This form is used to request a leave from the university, which can be in the form of a medical leave or other types of health-related leaves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Student Name
2.  Enter your Student ID Number
3.  Enter your Student Email
4.  Enter your primary contact phone number
5.  Select your Program/Major
6.  Enter the Academic Year and Term you are currently in
7.  Choose the Type of Health-Related Leave you are requesting
8.  Enter the Proposed Start and End Dates of your leave
9.  Specify the total number of days you will be absent
10. Indicate if you will be providing medical documentation
11.  Specify how this leave will impact your academic progress
12.  List any courses that will be affected by this leave
13.  If applicable, mention any academic support you will need upon return
14.  Provide the name and relationship of an emergency contact person

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your student records.
*   **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your official student identification number.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your official institutional email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your primary contact phone number.
*   **Program/Major** (`program_major`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your current academic program or major.
*   **Academic Year** (`academic_year`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the current academic year and term.
*   **Type of Health-Related Leave** (`leave_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose from:
    +   Physical illness or injury
    +   Mental health condition
    +   Chronic health condition
    +   Medical treatment
    +   Pregnancy-related
    +   Other
*   **Proposed Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date you would like the leave to begin.
*   **Proposed Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the expected date when you plan to return.
*   **Duration of Leave (Days)** (`duration_days`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the total number of days you will be absent.
*   **Medical Documentation** (`medical_documentation`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose from:
    +   Yes, I will provide documentation
    +   In process of obtaining
    +   Unable to obtain at this time
    +   Documentation attached
*   **Impact on Academic Progress** (`impact_on_academics`, `text`, **Required**): Describe how this leave will affect your academic standing.
*   **Courses Affected** (`courses_affected`, `text`, **Optional**): List the courses that will be affected by this leave.
*   **Academic Support Needed** (`academic_support_needed`, `text`, **Optional**): Specify the academic support you will need upon return.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, **Required**): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
