# Governor Contact Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Governor Contact Request Form is used to facilitate communication between constituents and the governor's office. Fill out this form to submit a contact request, choose a contact method, select follow-up preferences, and track the status of your request.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, start by selecting "Constituent Input" to describe your contact request.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted (email or phone) by selecting "Contact Type".
3. Briefly describe your request in the "Contact Request" field.
4. Select whether you would like to be followed up after your request is processed ("Follow Up").
5. Decide whether the governor's office should engage in constituent outreach ("Constituent Outreach").
6. Select the desired response time for the governor ("Governor Response").
7. Choose how the contact should be routed within the office ("Route Contact").
8. Select the current status of your request ("Request Status").
9. Optionally, add any notes about your request in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Constituent Input** (`Constituent Input`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the contact request.
* **Contact Type** (`Contact Type`, select_one, required): Choose how the governor's office should contact you (Email, Phone, In-Person, Other).
* **Contact Request** (`Contact Request`, text, required): Briefly describe the request.
* **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, text, required): Select whether you would like to be followed up after your request is processed.
* **Constituent Outreach** (`Constituent Outreach`, select_multiple, required): Decide whether the governor's office should engage in constituent outreach.
* **Governor Response** (`Governor Response`, time, required): Select the desired response time for the governor.
* **Route Contact** (`Route Contact`, select_multiple, required): Choose how the contact should be routed within the office.
* **Request Status** (`Request Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your request (New, Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
