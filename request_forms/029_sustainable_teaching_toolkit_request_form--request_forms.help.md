# Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form is a simple form that helps us understand your needs for educational resources. Please fill out this form to request access to the toolkit, and our team will get back to you with the necessary resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the "Form Data" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Teacher Name" field to indicate your affiliation with a school.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "School Department" field to select whether you are a teacher or not.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Choose the date and time of your visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Request Resources" field to indicate whether you would like to request resources.
9. Choose the type of resource you need from the "Resource Types" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Language" field to choose the language of the resource.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Format" field to choose the format of the resource.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "File Type" field to choose the type of file you need.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Country" field to choose the country of the resource.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "State/Province" field to choose the state or province of the resource.
15. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "City" field to choose the city of the resource.
16. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Region" field to choose the region of the resource.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Zip/Postal Code" field to choose the zip or postal code of the resource.
