# Special Event Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Event Request Form is designed to collect information about events that require special arrangements or services. It helps organizers and event coordinators gather essential details about an event, such as its category, description, date, location, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Select the Category**: Choose the correct category for your event from the drop-down menu. If your event doesn't fit into any of the provided categories, select "Other".
2.  **Provide a Description**: Enter a brief description of your event.
3.  **Enter Event Details**: Provide as much information as possible about your event, including the event type, duration, start and end dates, location, and venue (if applicable).
4.  **Specify the Fee**: Check if a fee is required for your event and select the correct fee type.
5.  **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your contact phone number, email, and address to help us get in touch with you.
6.  **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send your event request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (form_category)**: The category of your event, such as film set, location scout, or other.
* **Description (form_description)**: A brief description of your event.
* **Event Details (form_event_duration)**: The duration of your event.
* **Event Type (form_event_type)**: The type of event (e.g., film set, location scout, or other).
* **Start Date (form_event_start)**: The start date of your event.
* **End Date (form_event_end)**: The end date of your event.
* **Location (form_event_location)**: The location of your event.
* **Venue (form_event_venue)**: The venue where your event will take place (if applicable).
* **Space (form_event_space)**: The space required for your event (if applicable).
* **Fee (form_event_fee)**: Check if a fee is required for your event.
* **Fee Amount (form_event_fee_amount)**: If a fee is required, enter the amount.
* **Fee Type (form_event_fee_type)**: The type of fee (Rental or Hosting).
* **Note (form_event_note)**: Any additional notes about your event.
* **Phone (form_event_phone)**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (form_event_email)**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address (form_event_address)**: Your address for contact purposes.
* **City (form_event_city)**: The city of your event location.
* **State (form_event_state)**: The state of your event location.
* **Zip (form_event_zip)**: The zip code of your event location.
* **Host (form_event_host)**: The person hosting or coordinating your event.
* **Notes (form_event_notes)**: Additional notes about your event (e.g., specific equipment or personnel required).
* **Submit (form_event_submit)**: Click to send your event request.
