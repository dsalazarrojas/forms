# Public Service Request Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users requesting services from our public services. It is intended to help us understand their needs and provide the best possible service. Please note that this form should only be used for new service requests. If you have already submitted a service request, please do not resubmit your form. Duplicate service requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, but please be patient as we aim to process each request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (address, email, and phone number).
2. Choose the type of service you are requesting.
3. Write a detailed message describing your service request.
4. If you have any additional information or comments, please include them in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your name to help us address you personally.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of service you are requesting (Service 1, Service 2, or Service 3). This will help us direct your request to the correct department.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address to help us locate you and provide the best possible service.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you quickly and efficiently.
* **Service Request Message** (`service_request_message`, `text`, optional): Describe your service request in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand your needs and provide the best possible service.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here. This is a space for any extra details that might be relevant to your service request.
