# Karaoke Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Karaoke Request Form is designed to help users submit requests for karaoke songs and events. Fill out this form to inform the karaoke team about the songs and events you would like to be part of.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with the necessary information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the karaoke team directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophets Song Selection**: A text field to enter the name of the song you would like to request.
* **Song Request**: A text field to enter any additional information about the song you would like to request.
* **Singer Requesting**: A text field to enter your name or the name of the person requesting the song.
* **Song Duration**: A number field to enter the estimated duration of the song.
* **Date**: A date field to enter the date you would like to request the song.
* **Time**: A time field to enter the time you would like to request the song.
* **Event Name**: A text field to enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: A date field to enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: A time field to enter the time of the event.
* **Event Duration**: A text field to enter the estimated duration of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to make multiple requests, please fill out this form multiple times, as each form submission will be processed as a separate request.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the karaoke team directly.
