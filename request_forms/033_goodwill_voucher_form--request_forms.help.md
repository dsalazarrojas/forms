# Goodwill Voucher Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Goodwill Voucher Form is designed to collect essential information about voucher requests, ensuring that each submission is valid and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information, including your name and any other relevant details in the "basic_info" field.
2. Provide detailed information about the voucher you're requesting in the "voucher_info" field.
3. If you have any contact information you'd like to share, you can do so in the "contact_info" field (optional).
4. Select the necessary voucher details (item1 - item8) that apply to your request in the "voucher_details" field.
5. Confirm your submission by selecting the correct option in the "submit_confirmation" field.
6. Provide your signature in the "signature" field (optional).
7. Enter the date and time of submission in the "date" and "time" fields, respectively.
8. If you'd like to track when you submitted, you can enter that date and time in the "submit_date" and "submit_time" fields (optional).

* **voucher_info** (Text Field, Required): Provide a detailed description of the voucher you're requesting.
* **contact_info** (Text Field, Optional): Share your contact information to enable follow-up communication.
* **voucher_details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the voucher details that apply to your request (item1 - item8).
* **submit_confirmation** (Select One, Required): Confirm your submission by selecting the correct option.
* **signature** (Note Field, Optional): Provide your signature, if required.
* **date** (Date Field, Required): Enter the date of submission.
* **time** (Time Field, Required): Enter the time of submission.
* **submit_date** (Date Field, Optional): If you'd like to track when you submitted, enter the date.
* **submit_time** (Time Field, Optional): If you'd like to track when you submitted, enter the time.
