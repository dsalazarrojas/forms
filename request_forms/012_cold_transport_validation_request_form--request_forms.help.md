# Cold Transport Validation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the validation of a cold transport shipment to ensure that all necessary safety and regulatory requirements are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name and email address as the requester.
2. Provide your department and organization name.
3. Choose the type of shipment, such as pharmaceuticals, vaccines, or blood products.
4. Describe the shipment in detail, including the contents and packaging type.
5. Specify if the shipment contains hazardous materials and if temperature requirements are needed.
6. Choose the temperature range and tolerance for the shipment.
7. Enter the shipment weight and dimensions.
8. Select the packaging type, such as insulated shipper or refrigerated container.
9. Enter the pickup and delivery addresses, including contact names and phone numbers.
10. Choose the pickup and delivery times, including dates and time windows.
11. Select the maximum acceptable transit time for the shipment.
12. Choose the temperature monitoring method during transit.
13. Select the required documentation for the shipment.
14. Enter any special handling instructions.
15. Enter the declared value for insurance purposes.
16. Choose if a signature is required upon delivery.
17. Indicate if return shipment is needed.
18. Enter the budget or cost center code for billing purposes.
19. Add any additional comments or information about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name**: Enter your full name as the person requesting the validation.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Department**: Enter your department name as it is known within your organization.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization name as it is known within your industry.
* **Type of Shipment**: Choose the type of shipment you are requesting, such as pharmaceuticals, vaccines, or blood products.
* **Shipment Description**: Describe the shipment in detail, including the contents and packaging type.
* **Contains Hazardous Materials**: Indicate if the shipment contains hazardous materials.
* **Shipment Temperature Requirement**: Choose the temperature range and tolerance for the shipment.
* **Shipment Weight**: Enter the shipment weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Shipment Dimensions**: Enter the shipment dimensions.
* **Packaging Type**: Select the packaging type, such as insulated shipper or refrigerated container.
* **Pickup Address**: Enter the address where the shipment will be picked up.
* **Pickup Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person at the pickup location.
* **Pickup Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person at the pickup location.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the shipment will be delivered.
* **Delivery Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person at the delivery location.
* **Delivery Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person at the delivery location.
* **Requested Pickup Date**: Enter the date when the shipment should be collected.
* **Requested Pickup Time**: Enter the preferred time window for pickup.
* **Required Delivery Date**: Enter the date by which the shipment must be delivered.
* **Required Delivery Time**: Enter the time window for delivery.
* **Maximum Acceptable Transit Time**: Choose the longest acceptable duration for transit.
* **Temperature Monitoring Required**: Choose the method of temperature monitoring during transit.
* **Documentation Required**: Select all required documents for the shipment.
* **Special Handling Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for handling the shipment.
* **Shipment Insurance Value**: Enter the declared value for insurance purposes.
* **Signature Required on Delivery**: Choose if a signature is required upon delivery.
* **Return Shipment Needed**: Indicate if the shipment needs to be returned.
* **Budget or Cost Center Code**: Enter the budget or cost center code for billing purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the shipment.
