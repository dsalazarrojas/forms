# shipping_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about shipments, including shipping details, contact information, and delivery dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the shipping details section with relevant information about the shipment.
2. Provide the contact information section with any relevant details about the person or organization responsible for the shipment.
3. Enter the delivery date and time for the shipment.
4. Select the shipping method (e.g. Ground, Air, Sea).
5. Select the shipping status (e.g. Pending, Shipped, Delivered, Canceled).
6. Add any additional shipping comments as necessary.
7. Enter the contact information for the person or organization responsible for the shipment.
8. Add any additional contact notes as necessary.
9. If applicable, select the shipping method 2 and shipping status 2 for the second shipment.
10. Add any additional shipping comments 2 as necessary.
11. Enter the contact information for the second shipment.
12. Add any additional contact notes 2 as necessary.
13. Enter the shipping details 2 for the second shipment.
