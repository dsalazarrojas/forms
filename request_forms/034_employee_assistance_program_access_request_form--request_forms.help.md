# Employee Assistance Program Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), a confidential service designed to provide support for employees with personal or work-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing the reason for your request.
3. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID
**Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional)
Enter your employee ID, which is your unique identifier in the organization.

### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)
Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your work email address where you can be reached for follow-up communications.

### Job Title
**Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
Enter your current job title in the organization.

### Department
**Department** (`department`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your department where you are currently working.

### Location
**Location** (`location`, text, optional)
Enter the location where you are currently working.

### Reason for Request
**Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional)
Provide a detailed description of the reason you are requesting EAP access. This will help the HR department to process your request efficiently.
