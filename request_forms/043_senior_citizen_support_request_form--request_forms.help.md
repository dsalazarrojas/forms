# Senior Citizen Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from senior citizens seeking support. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details to assess and address the support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your basic information, including your name, email, phone number, address, city, and state.
2. Specify the type of support you need and the services you are interested in.
3. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your request.
4. Upload any supporting documents or files that will help us assess your request.
5. If you have a caregiver, please provide their name, relationship, phone number, email, and address.
6. Specify the emergency contact person's name, relationship, phone number, email, and address.
7. Provide your medical history and any supporting documents or information that will help us understand your needs.
8. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, click "Submit" to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info**: This section collects your basic information, including name, email, phone number, address, city, and state. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Name**: Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication about your support request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication about your support request.
* **Address**: Enter your full address, including street address and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Select your state of residence from the provided options.
* **Support Type**: Specify the type of support you need, such as "Yes" for in-home support or "No" for other types of support.
* **Services Needed**: Please describe the specific services you are interested in, such as meal preparation or housekeeping.
* **Additional Info**: This is an open field for you to provide any additional information that will help us understand your support request.
* **Upload Files**: Upload any supporting documents or files that will help us assess your request.
* **Caregiver Name**: If you have a caregiver, please provide their name.
* **Relationship To Caregiver**: Describe your relationship to the caregiver (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).
* **Caregiver Phone**: Enter the caregiver's phone number.
* **Caregiver Email**: Enter the caregiver's email address.
* **Caregiver Address**: Enter the caregiver's address.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the emergency contact person's address.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history or information that will help us understand your needs.
* **Supporting Docs**: Upload any supporting documents or information that will help us assess your request.
