# Planning Condition Change Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a planning condition change on a project. Before submitting this form, please ensure that you have not already submitted a duplicate request for the same condition change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Project name**: Enter the name of the project that needs to be changed.
	* **Request date**: Enter the date when the request was submitted.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
2. Optionally, add additional information:
	* **Request type**: Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether this is a request for a condition change or not.
	* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the condition change you are requesting.
	* **Project manager**: Select the project manager who will be responsible for the condition change.
	* **Team lead**: Select the team lead who will be impacted by the condition change.
	* **Team members**: Select any team members who will be impacted by the condition change.
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
	* **Status**: Choose the status of the condition change (Active, Inactive).
	* **Priority**: Choose whether the condition change is high or low priority.
	* **Urgency**: Choose whether the condition change has urgency or not.
	* **Category**: Choose the category of the condition change.
	* **Location**: Choose the location of the condition change.
	* **Submitter**: Choose whether you are the person submitting the request.
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations.
	* **Project status**: Choose the status of the project.
	* **Follow-up date**: Enter the date for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up time**: Enter the time for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up user**: Choose the person who will follow-up on the condition change.
	* **Follow-up status**: Choose the status of the follow-up.
	* **Follow-up comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting.
* Be as detailed and specific as possible in the description and comments fields.
* If you need to make changes to the condition change, please update the relevant fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us for assistance.
* We will review and respond to your request promptly.
* Please note that all fields are optional, but providing more information will help us better understand and process your request.
