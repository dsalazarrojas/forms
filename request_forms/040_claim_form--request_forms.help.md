# Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Claim Form is used to collect information about a claim, which is a formal request for compensation or resolution of a specific issue or incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full legal name in the "Claimant name" field.
2. Select the type of claim you are submitting (e.g., Property, Health, Travel, or Other).
3. Enter the date when the incident or issue occurred.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue.
5. If known, enter the amount claimed.
6. Note any supporting documents or evidence you are uploading via the "Supporting documents note" field.
7. Provide your contact email address for correspondence related to your claim.
8. If you have a phone number, you can optionally enter it.
9. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided by signing the "Declaration" field.
10. Finally, use today's date in the "Submission date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (Full legal name, text): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Claim Type** (Select one: Property, Health, Travel, Other, select_one): Choose the category that best describes your claim.
* **Incident Date** (Date, date): Enter the date when the incident or issue occurred.
* **Incident Description** (Description, text): Provide a detailed description of the incident or issue.
* **Amount Claimed** (Amount, number): If known, enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Supporting Documents Note** (Note, note): Upload or note any supporting documents or evidence you are providing, such as receipts, reports, or photos.
* **Contact Email** (Email, email): Enter your email address for correspondence related to your claim.
* **Contact Phone** (Phone, text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Declaration** (Declaration, note): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Submission Date** (Submission, date): Enter today's date.

**Tips**:
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the incident or issue.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, upload them via the "Supporting documents note" field.
