# Chapel Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by individuals seeking support or counseling services from a chaplain or counselor. It captures demographic information, support request details, and contact information to ensure that the chaplain or counselor can provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "Request Date" field to input the date of your request for support.
2. Fill in your "Requester Name" in the "Requester Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your affiliation with the institution (e.g., Faculty, Staff, Student, Alumni, Community Member, or Other) in the "Your Affiliation" field.
6. Choose the type of support you are seeking (e.g., Spiritual Counseling, Faith Community, Prayer Request, Grief Support, Life Coaching, Conflict Resolution, Sacraments, or Other) in the "Type of Support Requested" field.
7. Select the urgency level of your request (e.g., Immediate - Crisis, Urgent, Non-urgent).
8. Briefly describe the situation or concern that requires support in the "Situation or Concern Description" field.
9. If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have received support before.
10. If applicable, select the chaplain or counselor you would prefer to meet with (if you have a preference).
11. If applicable, select your preferred meeting format (e.g., In-Person, Phone, Video Call, Email).
12. If applicable, enter the preferred meeting time.
13. If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any confidentiality concerns.
14. If applicable, enter any confidentiality details.
15. If applicable, enter the support or counseling goals you hope to achieve.
16. If applicable, enter the source of your referral.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you consent to be contacted by the chaplain or counselor.
18. If applicable, enter the name of the emergency contact person.
19. If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
20. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your request for support.
* **Requester Name (Full name, Required)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address (Contact email, Required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (Contact phone, Required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Your Affiliation (Affiliation, Optional)**: Select your affiliation with the institution (e.g., Faculty, Staff, Student, Alumni, Community Member, or Other).
* **Type of Support Requested (Support type, Required)**: Choose the type of support you are seeking (e.g., Spiritual Counseling, Faith Community, Prayer Request, Grief Support, Life Coaching, Conflict Resolution, Sacraments, or Other).
* **Urgency Level (Urgency level, Required)**: Select the urgency level of your request (e.g., Immediate - Crisis, Urgent, Non-urgent).
* **Situation or Concern Description (Text, Required)**: Briefly describe the situation or concern that requires support.
* **Previous Support (Yes/No, Optional)**: If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have received support before.
* **Preferred Chaplain or Counselor (Preferred chaplain, Optional)**: If applicable, select the chaplain or counselor you would prefer to meet with (if you have a preference).
* **Preferred Meeting Format (Meeting format, Optional)**: If applicable, select your preferred meeting format (e.g., In-Person, Phone, Video Call, Email).
* **Preferred Meeting Time (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, enter the preferred meeting time.
* **Confidentiality Concerns (Yes/No, Optional)**: If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any confidentiality concerns.
* **Confidentiality Details (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, enter any confidentiality details.
* **Support or Counseling Goals (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, enter the support or counseling goals you hope to achieve.
* **How Did You Hear About This Service (Text, Optional)**: If applicable, enter the source of your referral.
* **Consent to Contact (Yes/No, Required)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you consent to be contacted by the chaplain or counselor.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Name, Optional)**: If applicable, enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Phone number, Optional)**: If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.

Note: Fields marked as "Required" must be completed before submitting the form.
