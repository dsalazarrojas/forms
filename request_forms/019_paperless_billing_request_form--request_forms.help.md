# Paperless Billing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the transition from paper bills to electronic statements. By filling out this form, you will be opting out of paper statements and switching to electronic statements. This process is usually completed to reduce paper waste and make billing more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information on each page. You can submit the form after reviewing and filling out each section. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your representative or our billing department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your account.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive electronic statements.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to contact you about any billing-related matters.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address we have on file for you, or your billing address if you are a business.
* **Switch Request Date** (`switch_request_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you would like to switch to electronic statements.
* **Reason for Switching** (`reason_for_switching`, text, required/optional): Please explain why you are opting out of paper statements.
* **Preferred Statement Frequency** (`preferred_statement_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you would like to receive electronic statements. Options include:
	+ Electronic Statements
	+ Paper Statements
	+ Both
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or notes for our billing team, please include them here.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): If you are representing a company, please enter their name.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required/optional): If you are representing a company, please enter their phone number.

Note: Some fields may not be required if you are not a business or do not have the required information. Please follow the same format for your specific situation.
