# Personal Shopping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal shopping information from customers for the sales team to process their requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, which is your full name for identification purposes.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, a valid email that the sales team can contact you with.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which will allow the sales team to contact you directly.
4. Select the **Request Date**, the date you would like your shopping to take place.
5. Choose the items you would like to purchase from the drop-down list.
6. If you have any specific comments, add them in the **Additional Comments** field. This will help the sales team understand your shopping requirements.
7. Choose the **Sales Person** who will be handling your shopping request from the drop-down list.
8. If available, choose the **Assigned Tool** for your shopping experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Fill in your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter a valid email address that the sales team can contact you with.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for direct contact.
* **Request Date** (Request Date, date, required): Select the date you would like your shopping to take place.
* **Shopping List** (Shopping List, select_multiple, required): Choose the items you would like to purchase from the drop-down list.
* **Item 1** (Item 1, text, optional): Add specific details for the first item you've chosen, if required.
* **Item 2** (Item 2, text, optional): Add specific details for the second item you've chosen, if required.
* **Item 3** (Item 3, text, optional): Add specific details for the third item you've chosen, if required.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, required): Add any relevant comments about your shopping request.
* **Sales Person** (Sales Person, select_one, required): Choose the sales person who will be handling your shopping request.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Choose the tool that will be used to process your request, if required.
