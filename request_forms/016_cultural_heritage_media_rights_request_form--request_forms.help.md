# cultural_heritage_media_rights_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural heritage media rights request form is a tool for requestors to submit requests for cultural heritage media rights. This form helps to ensure that the necessary information is collected to process the request correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name and contact information in the requestor fields.
* Enter the name and address of the institution or organization you are requesting on behalf of in the institution fields.
* Select the purpose of your request from the available options (Educational, Commercial, Other, Personal).
* Specify the type and format of the media item you are requesting rights for.
* Fill out any additional comments you have about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **request_form** (request_form, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of your request.

### 2. **request_date** (request_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date you are submitting your request.

### 3. **requestor** (requestor, text, required: false)
Enter your name as the person submitting the request.

### 4. **requestor_contact** (requestor_contact, text, required: false)
Enter your contact information.

### 5. **institution** (institution, text, required: true)
Enter the name of the institution or organization you are requesting on behalf of.

### 6. **contact_person** (contact_person, text, required: true)
Enter the name and title of the contact person.

### 7. **contact_email** (contact_email, email, required: false)
Enter the email of the contact person.

### 8. **requestor_address** (requestor_address, text, required: false)
Enter your address.

### 9. **requestor_address_2** (requestor_address_2, text, required: false)
Enter your secondary address.

### 10. **institution_name** (institution_name, text, required: true)
Enter the name of the institution or organization.

### 11. **institution_type** (institution_type, text, required: false)
Enter the type of institution or organization.

### 12. **institution_address** (institution_address, text, required: false)
Enter the address of the institution or organization.

### 13. **institution_city** (institution_city, text, required: false)
Enter the city where the institution is located.

### 14. **institution_state** (institution_state, text, required: false)
Enter the state where the institution is located.

### 15. **institution_zip** (institution_zip, text, required: false)
Enter the postal code where the institution is located.

### 16. **institution_country** (institution_country, select_one, required: true)
Select the country where the institution is located.

### 17. **purpose** (purpose, select_one, required: true)
Select the purpose of your request (Educational, Commercial, Other, Personal).

### 18. **media_type** (media_type, text, required: false)
Enter the type of media you are requesting.

### 19. **media_format** (media_format, text, required: false)
Enter the format of the media you are requesting.

### 20. **media_year** (media_year, number, required: false)
Enter the year the media was created.

### 21. **media_id** (media_id, text, required: false)
Enter the ID of the media.

### 22. **media_title** (media_title, text, required: false)
Enter the title of the media.

### 23. **media_location** (media_location, text, required: false)
Enter the location where the media is located.

### 24. **rights_holder** (rights_holder, text, required: false)
Enter the rights holder.

### 25. **additional_comments** (additional_comments, note, required: false)
Enter any additional comments about your request.
