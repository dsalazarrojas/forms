# photo_restoration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "photo_restoration_request_form" is a form used to request photo restoration services. This form helps users provide necessary information about the image they want to restore and the type of service required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Form Submission** field with a brief description of your photo restoration request, including the type of restoration needed and any specific areas of concern.
2. If applicable, upload the image you want to restore in the **Image Upload** field.
3. Select the required **Restoration Services** from the list.
4. Add any additional information about the image in the **Image Details** field.
5. Enter any special requests or comments in the **Client Request** field.
6. If necessary, select a **Project Deadline** for completion of the restoration services.
7. Choose a **Priority** level (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate how urgent your request is.
8. Add any additional comments in the **Notes** field.
9. Enter your **Email** address so that the service provider can contact you.
10. Enter your **Phone** number if you would like to be contacted by phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`): A brief description of your photo restoration request.
* **Image Upload** (`image_upload`): Upload the image you want to restore or provide a link to it.
* **Restoration Services** (`restoration_services`): Select the type of restoration service you require (e.g., Restorative Retouching, Color Correction).
* **Image Details** (`image_details`): Additional information about the image, such as its size or format.
* **Client Request** (`client_request`): Special requests or comments about your photo restoration request.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`): Select a date for completion of the restoration services if necessary.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Choose a priority level (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate how urgent your request is.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so that the service provider can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Upload Image** (`upload_image`): Upload the actual image file if necessary.
