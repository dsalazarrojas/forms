# Artist Offer Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Offer Sheet is a form designed to collect information from artists or performers about their offer to participate in an event or project. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth communication process between the event/project requester and the artist/performer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form in sections**: The form is divided into several sections. Fill out each section step by step, from Requester Information to Terms of Offer.
2. **Read each field carefully**: Each field has a label and a hint to help you understand what information is required.
3. **Provide accurate information**: Make sure to fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter the name of the person making the offer (requester).
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person making the offer (requester).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person making the offer (requester).
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter the business name of the person making the offer (requester).
* **Artist or Performer Name**: Enter the name of the artist or performer.
* **Artist Email**: Enter the email address of the artist or performer.
* **Artist Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the artist or performer.
* **Event or Project Name**: Enter the name of the event or project.
* **Type of Event or Project**: Select the type of event or project (e.g., Concert, Corporate Event, Gig, etc.).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event or project.
* **Event Location or Venue**: Enter the location of the event or project.
* **Description of Work Required**: Describe what the artist or performer will do during the event or project.
* **Duration of Performance or Work**: Select the duration of the performance or work (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, etc.).
* **Offered Compensation Amount (GBP)**: Enter the amount of compensation offered to the artist or performer.
* **Type of Compensation**: Select the type of compensation (e.g., Full payment upfront, Half payment upfront, etc.).
* **Artist Agrees to These Offer Terms**: Confirm acceptance of the offer by the artist or performer.
* **Date of This Offer**: Enter the date when the offer is being made.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all the information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the artist/performer or requester for clarification.
* This form is a communication document, and accurate information is essential to avoid misunderstandings.
