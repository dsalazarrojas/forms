# Factory Energy Source Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a review of your factory's energy source, enabling the energy team to assess and provide feedback on the current energy use and planning. This information will help in identifying areas for improvement and opportunities for sustainability and production planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the necessary information, making sure to provide accurate data.
2. If you're unsure about any field, you can refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more information.
3. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Info** (`factory_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of your factory, including its name and location.
* **Current Energy Use** (`energy_use`, number, optional): Please enter your current energy use in units of megawatt-hours (MWh) or megawatt (MW).
* **Planned Energy Changes** (`planned_energy_changes`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have planned any energy changes, such as changes in energy sources or efficiency improvements. Possible answers are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (`sustainability_initiatives`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the applicable sustainability initiatives your factory has implemented, such as 'Yes' for each option.
* **Production Planning** (`production_planning`, number, optional): Enter your current production planning data, including production volume and production planning cycles.
* **Energy Assessments** (`energy_assessments`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last energy assessment or audit.
* **Energy Teams** (`energy_teams`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your energy teams, including team members and their roles.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your energy source and planning.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the tool or system used for energy management and planning.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter your email address so the review request can be directed to the correct person.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure a thorough review.
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the energy team.
* Once the form is submitted, you will receive feedback and guidance from the energy team.
