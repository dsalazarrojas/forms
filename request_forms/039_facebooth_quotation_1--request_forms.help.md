# Facebooth Quotation 1 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to provide details about an event or party, including its name, date, location, type, and other relevant information. This information is likely used to generate quotes or proposals for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `event_name` field with the name of the event or party.
2. Enter the date of the event or party in the `event_date` field.
3. Enter the time of the event or party in the `time` field.
4. Fill in the `location` field with the venue or location of the event or party.
5. Select the type of event or party from the `event_type` field.
6. Enter the name of the event organizer or coordinator in the `event_organizer` field.
7. Enter the budget for the quote in the `quote_budget` field.
8. Enter any additional message or notes in the `quote_message` field.
9. If applicable, upload a quote image in the `quote_image` field.
10. Select the status of the quote from the `quote_status` field.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `quote_note` field.
12. Enter the email address of the person who will be handling the quote in the `quote_email` field.
13. Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling the quote in the `quote_phone` field.
14. Select the person assigned to handle the quote from the `quote_assigned` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event or party.
* **event_date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event or party.
* **time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event or party.
* **location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the venue or location of the event or party.
* **event_type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of event or party (Event, Party, Conference).
* **event_organizer** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event organizer or coordinator.
* **quote_budget** (`number`, required: false): Enter the budget for the quote.
* **quote_message** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional message or notes.
* **quote_image** (`select_one`, required: false): Select if a quote image is required (True, False, Unknown).
* **quote_status** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the quote (Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **quote_note** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **quote_email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person who will be handling the quote.
* **quote_phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling the quote.
* **quote_assigned** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the person assigned to handle the quote (chatjimmy, John Doe, Jane Doe).
