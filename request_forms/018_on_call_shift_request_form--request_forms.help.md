# On Call Shift Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request to be on-call for a specific shift. It ensures that your team's on-call schedule is up-to-date and helps in allocating resources efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Employee Name to associate your request with your profile.
2.  Enter the Start Date for the shift you are requesting.
3.  Enter the End Date for the shift you are requesting.
4.  Select your Department to ensure your request is routed to the correct team.
5.  Choose if you are a Team Lead or not.
6.  Choose the type of shift you are requesting.
7.  Select the duration of the shift in hours.
8.  Choose the frequency of shifts you are available to work.
9.  Enter the Start Time for the shift you are requesting.
10.  Enter the End Time for the shift you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your profile.
*   **Start Date** (`shift_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to start the shift.
*   **End Date** (`shift_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the shift will end.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Select the department you belong to, to ensure the request is routed to the correct team.
*   **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are a Team Lead or not.
*   **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of shift you are requesting (e.g., emergency, routine, etc.).
*   **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the shift in hours.
*   **Shift Frequency** (`shift_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the frequencies you are available to work (e.g., weekdays, weekends, etc.).
*   **Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to start the shift.
*   **End Time** (`shift_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the shift will end.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are only submitting one request for each shift you need.
- Double-check your request for accuracy before submission.
- You can submit multiple requests for different shifts if needed.
