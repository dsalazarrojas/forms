# Cultural Program Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a cultural program for your event, allowing our team to review and schedule it in our calendar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.
2. Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely, as this will help us process your request efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for a brief description of your event.
* **Event Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the name of your event, which will help us identify it in our records.
* **Event Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the date you are requesting for your event.
* **Event Time** (`4`, `time`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the time you are requesting for your event.
* **Event Location** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the location where your event will take place.
* **Event Description** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for a more in-depth description of your event, helping us understand your request better.
* **Event Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the type of cultural program you are requesting (e.g., Dance Performance, Theater Performance, Workshop, Class).
* **Contact Name** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the name of the person or team contact for your request.
* **Contact Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the email address of the person or team contact for your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for the phone number of the person or team contact for your request.
* **Submit** (`11`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This button submits your request once you have filled out all the required fields.
* **Review** (`12`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Request Status** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ This field shows the status of your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Assigned To** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field shows who the request is assigned to (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).

## Tips
* Make sure to check your email for any updates on your request status.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your contact person.
