# Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enhancement Request Form is used to submit and track enhancement requests to the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Enhancement Request Form** section with your enhancement request's description.
2. Select the **Enhancement Type** that best categorizes your request.
3. Choose the **Status** of your request (Active or Inactive).
4. If applicable, provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
5. Attach any relevant **File Uploads**.
6. Enter your **Reviewer Comments** in the **Reviewer Comments** field.
7. Fill in the **Request Date** of your request.
8. If necessary, provide **Additional Comments** about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enhancement Requester Name**: Your name will be displayed to the reviewer for easy identification.
* **Description**: A clear description of the enhancement you are requesting.
* **Enhancement Type**: Select the type of enhancement you are requesting (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Select Options**: Choose any applicable options for your request (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Date**: The date your request was submitted.
* **Time**: The time your request was submitted.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for easy contact.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for easy contact.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information about your request.
* **File Uploads**: Attach any relevant files to support your request.
* **Reviewer Comments**: The reviewer's comments about your request.
* **Approval Status**: The status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Status**: The status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Submit**: Submit your enhancement request.
* **Cancel**: Cancel your enhancement request.
* **Reviewer**: The name of the reviewer who is assigned to your request.
* **Enhancement Number**: A unique number assigned to your request.
* **Request Date**: The date your request was submitted.
