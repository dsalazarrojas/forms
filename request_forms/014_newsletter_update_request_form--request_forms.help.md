# Newsletter Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update a user's preferences for receiving newsletters and promotions. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you need to provide the following information:
  - Your name and contact details.
  - Your preference for receiving newsletters (Newsletters, Promotions, News & Updates, or No Newsletters or Promotions).
  - Your time zone.
  - The date of your request.
  - The format you prefer to see dates in (Month, Day of Month, Month Day, or Year).
  - (Optional) Your phone number and any date abbreviations you prefer.
  - The time of your request.
  - Your preferred time zone abbreviation.
  - Confirm your request by typing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for newsletters and promotions.
* **Newsletter Preference** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you want to receive Newsletters, Promotions, News & Updates, or No Newsletters or Promotions.
* **Time Zone** (Select One, Required): Select your local time zone from the options provided.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your request using the format of the month, day and year.
* **Date Format** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to see dates displayed (Month, Day of Month, Month Day, or Year).
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Date Abbreviations** (Text, Optional): Enter any date abbreviations you prefer.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your request in 24-hour format.
* **Time Zone Abbreviation** (Text, Optional): Enter your local time zone abbreviation.
* **Confirm** (Text, Required): Type your name to confirm your request.

Please fill out the form carefully, as any incorrect information may lead to delays or errors with your newsletter updates.
