# Database Audit Extension Documentation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request access to sensitive data for their work. It is intended to streamline the process and ensure that the requests are properly documented and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2.  Enter your Job Title and Department (if applicable).
3.  Provide a clear Justification for why you need access to the data.
4.  Attach any Supporting Materials that support your request.
5.  Choose the correct Route to (IT Department or Security Department).
6.  Enter the Approval Date and Approval Time.
7.  Select the Approval Status (Approved or Rejected).
8.  Enter the Assigned Tool.
9.  Enter any Comments (if necessary).
10.  Enter the Assigned To.
11.  Enter the Approval User ID.
12.  Enter the Route to ID (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your first name as per your official documentation.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your last name as per your official documentation.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Please enter your official email ID.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your official job title.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter your department name.
*   **Justification** (`justification`, `text`, `required`): Please provide a clear and concise reason for requesting access to sensitive data.
*   **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, `text`, `required`): Attach any relevant documents or files that support your request.
*   **Route to** (`route_to`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the correct department to route your request to (IT Department or Security Department).
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, `required`): Please enter the date you are approving the request.
*   **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, `time`, `required`): Please enter the time you are approving the request.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select whether the request is approved or rejected.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the tool assigned to you for this task.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`): If necessary, please add any comments for the reviewer.
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the name of the person assigned to this task.
*   **Approval User ID** (`approval_user_id`, `text`, `required`): Please enter the ID of the user who approved this request.
*   **Route to ID** (`route_to_id`, `text`, `optional`): If necessary, please enter the ID of the department that this request is routed to.

Note: The fields with asterisk (\*) next to them are mandatory and should be filled out by all users. The fields without any asterisk are optional and can be filled out by users if required.
