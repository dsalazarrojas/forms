# Change Of Circumstances Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Change Of Circumstances Form" is used to report changes in your circumstances, such as address, employment, or marital status. This information helps us update your records and ensure that our policies and procedures are applied accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you submitted this form.
2. Provide your full name as it should appear on your records.
3. Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
4. If you have an identification number (e.g., government ID or SSN), enter it here.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Select all changes that apply to your circumstances.
8. Enter the effective date of the changes.
9. If your address is changing, enter the new address.
10. If you're changing your name, enter the new name details.
11. If you're changing your employment, enter the new job title, employer, etc.
12. If you're changing your marital status, select the new status and enter the marriage date (if applicable) and spouse's name (if applicable).
13. If you have any family status changes, enter the new details.
14. If your income is changing, enter the new income details.
15. If you're providing supporting documentation, select 'Yes' and enter the type of documentation attached.
16. Enter the reason for submitting this form.
17. Provide any additional information that's relevant to your changes.
18. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission Date (1)**: Enter the date you're submitting this form.
* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your records.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Identification Number (4)**: If you have an identification number (e.g., government ID or SSN), enter it here.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Changes in Circumstances (7)**: Select all changes that apply to your circumstances.
* **Effective Date of Changes (8)**: Enter the date when the changes take effect.
* **New Address (if applicable) (9)**: If your address is changing, enter the new address.
* **Name Change Details (10)**: If you're changing your name, enter the new name details.
* **Employment Change Details (11)**: If you're changing your employment, enter the new job title, employer, etc.
* **Marital Status (12)**: Select the new marital status and enter the marriage date (if applicable) and spouse's name (if applicable).
* **Family Status Changes (13)**: If you have any family status changes, enter the new details.
* **Income Change Details (14)**: If your income is changing, enter the new income details.
* **Supporting Documentation Provided (15)**: If you're providing supporting documentation, select 'Yes' and enter the type of documentation attached.
* **Type of Documentation Included (16)**: If you're attaching documentation, enter the type of documentation.
* **Reason for Submitting This Form (17)**: Enter the reason for submitting this form.
* **Additional Information (18)**: Provide any other relevant details.
* **I Certify the Information is Accurate (19)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

Tips:

* Ensure you're completing the form for a valid reason and not duplicating a previous submission.
* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy before submitting.
* Attach relevant documentation to support your changes.
* If you're unsure about any field or process, ask for assistance.
