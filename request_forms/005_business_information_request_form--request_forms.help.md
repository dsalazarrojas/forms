# Business Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business information requests in a structured and secure manner. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your request is properly processed and that the required information is delivered to you in a timely and secure fashion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:
1.  Fill in your name and organization if you are requesting information on behalf of a company or group.
2.  Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3.  Specify your phone number for contact.
4.  Enter the date of your request.
5.  Provide the target business name.
6.  Enter the known address of the target business.
7.  Choose your relationship to the target business.
8.  Select all types of information you need.
9.  Specify the reason you need the information.
10.  Provide a detailed explanation of why you need the information.
11.  Describe how you will use the obtained information.
12.  Confirm your commitment to maintain confidentiality of the received information.
13.  Choose how urgently you need the information.
14.  Select how you would like to receive the information.
15.  Confirm if you have written authorization from the target business.
16.  Provide any additional requirements or special considerations.
17.  Provide professional references who can verify your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requesting Party Name** (`requesting_party_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person or organization requesting information.
* **Requesting Organization** (`requesting_organization`, text, required): If you are requesting information on behalf of a company or group, enter the name of the organization.
* **Requestor Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Requestor Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request.
* **Target Business Name** (`target_business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the target business.
* **Target Business Address** (`target_business_address`, text, optional): If known, enter the address of the target business.
* **Relationship to Target Business** (`relationship_to_target`, select_one, required): Choose how you are related to or connected with this business.
* **Type of Information Requested** (`information_type_requested`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of information you need.
* **Purpose of Information Request** (`purpose_of_request`, select_one, required): Choose the reason you need the information.
* **Purpose Description** (`purpose_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of why you need the information.
* **How Information Will Be Used** (`information_usage`, text, required): Describe how you will use the obtained information.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to maintain confidentiality of the received information.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose how urgently you need the information.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`preferred_delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive the information.
* **Authorization Documentation** (`authorization_documentation`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have written authorization from the target business.
* **Legal Basis for Request** (`legal_basis_for_request`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the legal basis that allows you to request this information.
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Provide any special requirements or considerations for this request.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide names of professional references who can verify your request.

Note: Ensure all required fields are accurately filled in to avoid delay or rejection of your request.
