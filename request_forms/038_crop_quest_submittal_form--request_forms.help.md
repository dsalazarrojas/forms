# Crop Quest Submittal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new customers seeking crop consultation services from the consultant. It is used to collect contact and consultation details to facilitate communication and service provision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired "Form Title" in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide your name and contact information, including your "customer name", "phone number", and "email address".
3. Optionally, enter any additional notes about your customer in the "Customer Notes" field.
4. Specify your budget for the consultation service.
5. Enter any specific "Goals" you have for the consultation.
6. Enter the name of the consultant you would like to work with, and their contact information including "phone number" and "email address".
7. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the consultant in the "Consultant Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your submittal request. This field is optional.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Your name.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number.
* **Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Your address.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required: false): Any additional notes about you.
* **Budget** (`customer_budget`, number, required: false): Your budget for the consultation service.
* **Goals** (`customer_goals`, text, required: false): Any specific goals you have for the consultation.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required: false): The name of the consultant you would like to work with.
* **Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, required: false): The consultant's phone number.
* **Email** (`consultant_email`, email, required: false): The consultant's email address.
* **Notes** (`consultant_notes`, note, required: false): Any additional notes about the consultant.

## Tips
* Ensure that the "Form Title" is concise and relevant to the consultation request.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information for yourself and the consultant.
* Be specific about your goals and expectations for the consultation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
