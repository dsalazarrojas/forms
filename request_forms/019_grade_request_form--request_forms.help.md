# Grade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grade Request Form is used to make a grade request to a teacher, allowing students to ask for an update to their grade in a particular subject or course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your grade request in the `grade_request_form_1` field.
2. Enter your grade ID, which is usually your student ID number.
3. Enter the new grade you are requesting.
4. Provide a reason for the grade change in the `reason_for_change` field.
5. Attach your signature in the `student_signature` field.
6. Attach your teacher's signature and comments in the `teacher_signature` and `teacher_comments` fields respectively.
7. Enter the date and time you are requesting the grade change in the `date_requested` and `time_requested` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **grade_request_form_1**: Enter a clear and concise description of your grade request.
* **grade_id**: Enter your student ID number, usually assigned by the school or instructor.
* **new_grade**: Enter the new grade you are requesting, following the format of the original grade.
* **reason_for_change**: Explain why you are requesting the grade change, providing a clear and concise description.
* **student_signature**: Attach your signature, usually by printing or writing your name.
* **teacher_signature**: Attach your teacher's signature, usually by printing or writing their name, after they have reviewed and accepted your grade request.
* **teacher_comments**: Allow your teacher to add any comments or notes regarding your grade request.
* **date_requested**: Enter the date you are requesting the grade change, usually in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time_requested**: Enter the time you are requesting the grade change, usually in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the course syllabus and discuss with your teacher before submitting this form.
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach a clear and legible signature in the `student_signature` and `teacher_signature` fields.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records, including the teacher's comments and your signature.
