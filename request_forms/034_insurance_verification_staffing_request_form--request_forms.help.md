# Insurance Verification Staffing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request insurance verification staffing for patients in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options from dropdown fields.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Verification Staffing Request** (`insurance_verification_staffing_request_form`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter the reason for the insurance verification request.
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant facility information that may be helpful for the request.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant patient information that may be helpful for the request.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's insurance information, including policy number, coverage details, and any other relevant information.
* **Staffing Request** (`staffing_request`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific staffing requests or requirements for the patient's treatment or procedure.
* **Verification Request** (`verification_request`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional verification requests or requirements for the patient's insurance information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature to confirm the request.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the staff member who submitted the request.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, optional): Select the staff member or department that the request was submitted to.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the staff member who submitted the request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the staff member who submitted the request.
* **Fax** (`fax`, `text`, optional): Enter the fax number of the staff member who submitted the request.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of insurance the patient has.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the insurance provider for the patient.
* **Facility** (`facility`, `select_one`, optional): Select the facility where the patient will receive treatment or procedure.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the request.
* **Submitted By Full Name** (`submitted_by_full_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the staff member who submitted the request.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the request.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person requesting the verification.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the facility where the patient will receive treatment or procedure.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the request.
