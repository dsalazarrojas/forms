# Feature Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions and feedback from customers on features they would like to see added or improved in our product. This will help us better understand your needs and priorities for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter any additional comments or information in the "form_data" field.
3. Provide your name in the "customer_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field.
5. In the "feature_request" field, describe the feature you would like to see added or improved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_data**: A text field where you can enter any additional comments or information about your feature request.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions about your feature request.
* **feature_request**: A text field where you describe the specific feature you would like to see added or improved. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your needs.

## Tips

* Please be specific and concrete when describing your feature request.
* If you have any images or screenshots to support your request, you can attach them to the "form_data" field.
* We appreciate your feedback and will review it carefully to prioritize our development roadmap.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
