# Staff Development Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture staff development requests from employees, which are then reviewed and approved by managers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee ID (ID not visible in this documentation).
2. Select the department you belong to (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
3. Enter your job title and job title description.
4. Select the job title level (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
5. Enter the start and end dates of the requested development.
6. Select the status of your request (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
7. Select the submitted by and approved by fields (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
8. Add any additional notes if necessary.
9. Enter your email and phone number.
10. Provide your address.
11. Enter the submitted date and approved date (system-generated fields).
12. Add any additional form notes if necessary.
13. Enter your comments as an employee.
14. Enter your manager's comments.
15. Add any status notes if necessary.
16. Enter the reason for approval or denial.
17. Finally, review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_form (text)**: A text field for entering a brief description of your request.
* **employee_info (text)**: A text field for entering additional information about yourself (optional).
* **job_title (text)**: Enter your current job title.
* **department (select_one)**: Select the department you belong to (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **job_title_id (number)**: Enter your job title ID.
* **job_title_description (text)**: Enter a brief description of your job title.
* **job_title_level (select_multiple)**: Select the level of your job title (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **start_date (date)**: Enter the start date of your requested development.
* **end_date (date)**: Enter the end date of your requested development.
* **status (select_one)**: Select the status of your request (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **submitted_by (select_multiple)**: Select the person submitting your request.
* **approved_by (select_multiple)**: Select the person approving your request.
* **notes (note)**: Enter any additional notes about your request.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **address (text)**: Enter your address.
* **submitted_date (date)**: System-generated field, not editable.
* **approved_date (date)**: System-generated field, not editable.
* **form_notes (note)**: Enter any additional notes about your request.
* **employee_comments (note)**: Enter your comments as an employee.
* **manager_comments (note)**: Enter your manager's comments.
* **status_notes (note)**: Enter any status notes about your request.
* **approval_reason (text)**: Enter the reason for approval.
* **denial_reason (text)**: Enter the reason for denial.
* **submission_date (date)**: System-generated field, not editable.

Please note that the fields with system-generated labels are not editable. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your HR department.
