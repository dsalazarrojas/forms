# Embroidery Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in requesting an embroidery quote from a business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
* For each field, make sure to provide the most up-to-date and complete information.
* Review and submit the form to send it to the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, not required): 
This field is for you to enter your name and title. Please type in your name as it appears on official documents, and your job title if applicable.
 
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): 
Enter the contact information of the person who will receive the quote, or the person handling the inquiry.
 
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, not required): 
Enter information about your company such as name, address, and any other relevant details.
 
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, not required): 
Describe the specific item or service you are interested in getting a quote for, including any specific design requirements, material requests, and deadlines.
 
## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the business can better understand your quote request.
* If you have any specific requirements or questions, feel free to include them in the "Quote Request" field.
* Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully before submitting it to the business.
