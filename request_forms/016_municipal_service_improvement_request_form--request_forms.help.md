# Municipal Service Improvement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from citizens about improvements or concerns they have regarding municipal services, such as road maintenance, parks and recreation, waste management, and other services.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Select the Relevant Service Type
- On page 2, please select the category that best describes the service related to your request. Choose from the options:
  - Road Maintenance
  - Parks and Recreation
  - Waste Management
  - Other

### Step 2: Provide a Clear Description of the Issue
- On page 3, write a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing. This will help us better understand the problem and provide a more accurate response.

### Step 3: Specify the Location
- On page 4, enter the specific location where the issue is occurring. This could be a street address, neighborhood, or landmark.

### Step 4: Prioritize Your Request
- On page 5, select the priority level for your request. This will help us schedule and allocate resources accordingly:
  - Low
  - Medium
  - High

### Step 5: Choose Your Preferred Follow-up Method
- On page 6, select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up:
  - Phone
  - Email
  - In-Person

### Step 6: Provide Additional Information
- On page 7, enter any additional context or information about your request.

### Step 7: Submit Your Request
- Once you've completed all the fields, submit your request. Please review your entry carefully before submitting to ensure that all required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_2`)**: Select the category that best describes the service related to your request.
* **Issue Description (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_3`)**: Enter a clear and detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Location (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_4`)**: Enter the specific location where the issue is occurring.
* **Priority (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_5`)**: Select the priority level for your request (Low, Medium, High).
* **Follow-up (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_6`)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Phone, Email, In-Person).
* **Follow-up Details (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_7`)**: Enter any additional context or information about your request.
* **Additional Information (`municipal_service_improvement_request_form_page_8`)**: Enter any additional details that are relevant to your request.

## Tips
- Be as clear and specific as possible when describing the issue you are experiencing.
- If you have any additional supporting documents or images, consider attaching them to your request.
- If you are unsure or have questions, feel free to contact us at: form@gic.mx.
