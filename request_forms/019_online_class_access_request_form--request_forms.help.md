# online_class_access_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request access to an online class. It collects information from learners to process their requests and ensure smooth communication with the approvers for further action.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as required).
2. Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields before clicking the "Submit" button.
3. Double-check that the course selection, start and end dates, and other relevant information is accurate.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Comments" field to include them.
5. Click "Submit" to send your request to the approvers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details (1)** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of the course you are requesting access to.
* **Learner Name (2)** (`text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Learner Email (3)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Learner Phone (4)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Course Selection (5)** (`select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" if you have selected the course, and "No" if you have not.
* **Start Date (6)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you would like to start the course on (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **End Date (7)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you would like to end the course on (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Instructor (8)** (`note`, required): This field is currently not being used, but it's a placeholder for potential future use. Please disregard.
* **Approver (9)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose all approvers who should be notified of your request (currently only "Yes" or "No" options).
* **Comments (10)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Submit (11)** (`text`, optional): Click this button to send your request to the approvers.
* **Cancel (12)** (`text`, optional): Click this button to cancel your request and start over.
