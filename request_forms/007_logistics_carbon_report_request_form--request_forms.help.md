# Logistics Carbon Report Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a logistics carbon report. The report helps track and analyze the carbon emissions related to logistics operations. The information collected here will be used to provide a detailed report that will aid in reducing carbon emissions and improving logistics efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct options for Contact Information.
2. Enter your Email Address.
3. Provide your Carbon Impact (optional).
4. Add any Comments (optional).
5. Select the Date Of Submission and Time Of Submission (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Contact Information</span> (`contactinformationpage`, select_multiple, **required**)
Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3 to provide your contact information.

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Email Address</span> (`emailaddresspage`, email, **optional**)
Enter your valid email address to contact you for any further information or clarification.

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Carbon Impact</span> (`carbonimpactpage`, number, **optional**)
Provide an estimate of the carbon impact of your logistics operations (in tons of CO2).

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Comments</span> (`commentssection`, note, **optional**)
Add any additional comments or information related to your logistics operations.

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Date Of Submission</span> (`dateofsubmissionpage`, date, **optional**)
Select the date of submission of your logistics carbon report.

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Time Of Submission</span> (`timeofsubmissionpage`, time, **optional**)
Select the time of submission of your logistics carbon report.

### <span style="font-weight: bold;">Contact Person</span> (`contactpersonpage`, text, **optional**)
Enter the name of the contact person associated with your logistics operations.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or email the email address provided in the form.
