> **Counseling Referral Form - Help Guide**


## Purpose
The Counseling Referral Form is used to collect information from students who are referred to counseling services. This form helps us understand the reasons for referral and gather essential details from the student, such as their contact information and counseling status. 

Before starting, please ensure you are filling out this form for a valid reason, and not for testing or other non-relevant purposes. If you are unsure, please contact us to clarify. Also, please avoid filling out this form multiple times for the same individual. Duplicate submissions will not be accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
* Complete each section with relevant information.
* Use the available options to provide multiple selections when required.
* Fill out any additional comments or notes section if needed.
* Finally, submit the form.


## Field-by-Field Explanation


* **Counselor Information** (`id 1`): Provide a brief description of the reason for referral.
* **User Information** (`id 2`): Enter the student's name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Illegal Substance** (`id 3`): Choose 'Yes' if the student is using illegal substances, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Referral Reason** (`id 4`): Select all reasons why the student was referred for counseling.
* **Counseling Program** (`id 5`): Choose if the student is in a counseling program.
* **School Counselor** (`id 6`): Select if the student has a school counselor.
* **Private Physician** (`id 7`): Choose if the student has a private physician.
* **Institution** (`id 8`): Select the institution the student is attending.
* **Additional Comments** (`id 9`): Provide any additional information that may be useful.
* **Date Completed** (`id 10`): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Date Started** (`id 11`): Enter the date the counseling began.
* **Date of Referral** (`id 12`): Enter the date of referral.
* **User Referral** (`id 13`): Choose if the student was referred by the user.
* **School Referral** (`id 14`): Choose if the student was referred by the school.
* **Phone Number** (`id 15`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **E-mail Address** (`id 16`): Enter the student's email address.
* **User E-mail** (`id 17`): Enter the user's email address.
* **User Name** (`id 18`): Enter the user's name.
* **User Phone** (`id 19`): Enter the user's phone number.
* **Private Physician Info** (`id 20`): Enter the private physician's information.
* **Counselor Info** (`id 21`): Enter the counselor's information.
* **User Agreed** (`id 22`): Choose if the user agreed.
* **Counselor Agreed** (`id 23`): Choose if the counselor agreed.
* **User Referral Agreed** (`id 24`): Choose if the user agreed to the referral.
* **Counselor Referral Agreed** (`id 25`): Choose if the counselor agreed to the referral.
