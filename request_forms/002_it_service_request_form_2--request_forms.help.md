# it_service_request_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_service_request_form_2 is a form designed to collect information about IT service requests. It helps customers or employees report issues related to IT services in a structured and efficient way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your email address in the E-mail field, which will be used to contact you about your issue.
3. Select the relevant Category for your issue from the dropdown menu.
4. Describe your problem in the Problem Description field.
5. Add any additional comments, if needed.
6. If you are reporting an issue with a device, select the Repair Category from the dropdown menu.
7. Finally, select the assigned tool from the dropdown menu to help us route the issue to the correct team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to contact you about your issue.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your issue (Hardware, Software, or Network).
* **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, text, optional): Describe your problem in detail.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or context about your issue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Repair Category** (`repair_category`, select_one, optional): If reporting an issue with a device, select the correct Repair Category (Desktop, Laptop, or Server).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the assigned tool to help us route the issue to the correct team (chatjimmy or chat jimmy). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible to help us provide a more efficient and effective service.
* If you are unsure about the category or repair category, please contact your IT support team for guidance.
* We will respond to your issue as soon as possible, and we may contact you via phone or email for further information.
