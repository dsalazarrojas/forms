# Packaging Lifecycle Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Packaging Lifecycle Certification Registration Form is a tool used to register an organization's packaging lifecycle certification. This form provides a structured way to gather and record essential information about an organization's packaging processes and sustainability practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the Organization Details section with your organization's name, address, and contact information.
3. Select the correct Lifecycle Stage of your product from the options provided.
4. List the Certification Bodies your organization has partnered with.
5. Mention any Sustainability Programs your organization has implemented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Details
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, `text`, required): Please enter your organization's name, address, and contact information to allow us to accurately track and contact them.
### Lifecycle Stage
* **Lifecycle Stage** (`lifecycle_stage`, `text`, required): Select the current stage of your product's lifecycle from the options provided. This is an important step in ensuring that you are accurately reporting your product's lifecycle status.
### Certification Bodies
* **Certification Bodies** (`certification_bodies`, `text`, required): List any third-party certification bodies your organization has partnered with to demonstrate its commitment to sustainability and transparency.
### Sustainability Programs
* **Sustainability Programs** (`sustainability_programs`, `text`, required): Describe any sustainability programs your organization has implemented to reduce its environmental impact.
### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Review all information before submitting. This is the final step in the registration process.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Please note that this form is used for internal tracking purposes only.
