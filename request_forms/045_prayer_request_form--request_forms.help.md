# Prayer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prayer Request Form is a tool used to collect and process prayer requests from users. It is designed to help us understand your needs and provide support in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe your prayer request in the "Prayer Request" field, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the type of prayer request you are submitting (e.g., Healing, Miraculous Intervention).
4. If desired, specify a date and time for the prayer request.
5. If needed, specify the duration of the prayer request.
6. Optionally, select a tool or department to be assigned to process your request.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Your name, which will be used to address your request.
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address, used to contact you about your request.
* **Prayer Request** (text, required): A detailed description of your prayer request, including as much information as possible.
* **Prayer Request Type** (select multiple, optional): The type of prayer request you are submitting (e.g., Healing, Miraculous Intervention).
	+ Select one or more of the options provided to categorize your request.
* **Prayer Request Date** (date, optional): The date you would like to submit your prayer request for.
* **Prayer Request Time** (time, optional): The time you would like to submit your prayer request.
* **Prayer Request Duration** (number, optional): The length of time for which you would like to submit your prayer request for.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): A tool or department you would like to be assigned to process your request.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your prayer request.
* Ensure that your request is not already duplicated and will be processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
