# Medicare Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Refund Form is a standardized form used to gather information from patients to process refunds for their medical claims.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medicare Refund Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in to identify the form purpose.
* **Claim ID** (`claim_id`, number, not required): This field is used to track the patient's medical claim. If you don't have a claim ID, you can skip this field.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Fill in the name of the patient for whom you're requesting a refund.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the original claim.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason`, text, not required): Describe the reason for the refund.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, not required): Fill in the amount of the refund.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who provided the service.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your contact email address so that we can get in touch with you regarding the refund.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): This field is typically used by authorized personnel to acknowledge receipt of the form.
* **Attachments** (`uploaded_file`, note, not required): You can attach any supporting documents or evidence to support your claim.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* You can skip optional fields if you don't have the necessary information.
