# Notary_appointment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request an appointment with a notary for various purposes, such as notarization of documents or other services. The form gathers information to confirm the details of the request, including the date, time, and the notary to be assigned.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your request.
2. Enter a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
3. Choose a date and time for the appointment.
4. Select a notary for your appointment.
5. Confirm the notary's availability for the selected date and time.
6. Confirm the date and time of the appointment.
7. Confirm the notary assigned to your appointment.
8. Confirm the notary's availability for the selected date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Notary_appointment_request_form (1)**: Enter a title for your request. This is optional.
* **Category (2)**: Select a category for your request, such as "Request Forms" or "Other Forms".
* **Description (3)**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
* **Date (4)**: Select a date for the appointment. Use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time (5)**: Select a time for the appointment. Use the 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
* **Customer Name (6)**: Enter the name of the customer or individual requesting the notary service.
* **Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the customer or individual requesting the notary service.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the customer or individual requesting the notary service.
* **Notary (9)**: Select a notary for your appointment. The notaries available are "Notary 1", "Notary 2", and "Notary 3".
* **Notary Availability (10)**: Confirm the notary's availability for the selected date and time. Select all the times that the notary is available.
* **Confirm Date (11)**: Confirm the selected date for the appointment.
* **Confirm Time (12)**: Confirm the selected time for the appointment.
* **Confirm Notary (13)**: Confirm the notary assigned to your appointment.
* **Confirm Notary Availability (14)**: Confirm the notary's availability for the selected date and time.
* **Confirm Notary 2 (15)**: Select another notary for your appointment. This field is not required.
* **Confirm Time 2 (16)**: Confirm the time for the appointment. This field is not required.
* **Confirm Date 2 (17)**: Confirm the date for the appointment. This field is not required.
* **Confirm Form (18)**: This is a confirmation field. Please fill in your form carefully before submitting.
