# Global Education Partnership Endorsement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a global education partnership endorsement. Fill in the details of your organisation and proposal to help us understand your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Organisation, Contact Person's Name, Email Address, and all "Yes/No" fields. 
   These fields are marked as required and cannot be skipped.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Global Education Partnership Information**: This is a free text field for any additional information about your global education partnership request.
  * **Name of Organisation**: Please fill in the name of your organisation.
  * **Contact Person's Name**: Please fill in the name of the contact person for your organisation.
  * **Organisation**: Please select 'Yes' if your organisation is currently a partner with us, otherwise select 'No'.
  * **Email Address**: Please fill in the email address of the contact person for your organisation.
  * **Proposal**: Please describe your proposal or proposal details.
  * **Review**: If your proposal needs review, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
  * **Submit**: If your request needs submission, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
  * **Confirm**: If your request needs confirmation, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
