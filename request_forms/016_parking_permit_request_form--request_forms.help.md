# Parking Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parking permit request form is used to request parking permits on campus for personal vehicles or company vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

*   Fill in all the necessary information accurately and thoroughly.
*   Be as specific as possible when selecting the department and the spaces requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears in our system.
*   **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, not required): This is a unique identifier for you as an employee.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, not required): Select the department you belong to.
    +   *Sales*: Sales department
    +   *Marketing*: Marketing department
    +   *IT*: IT department
*   **Space Requested** (`space_requested`, select_multiple, not required): Select the parking spaces you are requesting.
    +   *Parking Space 1*: Parking Space 1
    +   *Parking Space 2*: Parking Space 2
    +   *Parking Space 3*: Parking Space 3
*   **Parking Spaces** (`parking_spaces`, date, not required): Enter the date you need the parking permit for.
*   **Shift** (`shift`, time, not required): Enter the time you need the parking permit for.
*   **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, not required): Enter any relevant information about your vehicle.
*   **Parking Permit Request** (`parking_permit_request`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your parking needs.

## Tips
*   Please submit one request per vehicle per calendar day.
*   If you have multiple requests, please submit them separately.
*   If you are unsure about the form or need clarification, please reach out to your supervisor or the parking department.
