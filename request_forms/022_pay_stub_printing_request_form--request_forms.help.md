# Pay Stub Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay Stub Printing Request Form is designed to collect information for the printing of pay stubs for employees who have requested this service. This form helps in efficiently processing the request and ensures that the pay stub is delivered to the correct person at the correct location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your request. If you're requesting a pay stub, choose "Request Forms."
2. Provide a brief description of the reason for your request.
3. Enter your employee name and ID (if applicable).
4. Select the delivery purpose of your pay stub (e.g., Payroll, HR, or Other).
5. Choose where you'd like your pay stub to be delivered (e.g., Office, Home, or Mail).
6. Specify the desired delivery frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
7. Provide a specific date for delivery (if required).
8. Select a time for delivery (if required).
9. Add any notes or comments about your request (optional).
10. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
11. Assign the request to a specific person (if applicable).
12. Enter the person submitting the form (if different from above).
13. Add any additional comments or notes for the form submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pay Stub Printing Request Form** (Required): This is the title of the form. You can leave it as is, as it's a required field.
* **Category** (Required): Select the category for your request. If you're requesting a pay stub, choose "Request Forms."
* **Description** (Optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for your request.
* **Employee Name** (Optional): Enter your employee name.
* **Employee ID** (Optional): Enter your employee ID (if you have one).
* **Delivery Purpose** (Optional): Choose the delivery purpose of your pay stub (e.g., Payroll, HR, or Other).
* **Delivery Location** (Optional): Choose where you'd like your pay stub to be delivered (e.g., Office, Home, or Mail).
* **Delivery Frequency** (Optional): Specify the desired delivery frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Delivery Date** (Optional): Provide a specific date for delivery (if required).
* **Delivery Time** (Optional): Select a time for delivery (if required).
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (Optional): Assign the request to a specific person.
* **Submitter** (Optional): Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Form Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter Notes** (Optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the form submitter.
* **Submitted By** (Optional): Enter the person who submitted the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as "Required" in the form schema. Please fill in all required fields for accurate processing.
