# Operations Planning Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operations Planning Assistance Request Form is used to gather information from end users for operations planning assistance requests. This form helps us to categorize and prioritize requests efficiently, ensuring that the necessary resources are allocated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the available categories and select the one that best fits your request.
2. Fill in the requested information, ensuring that all required fields are completed correctly.
3. Provide a brief description of your request, including any necessary details.
4. Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you regarding your request.
5. Choose a support request type that accurately describes your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_category`, select_one): Select the category that best describes your request.
  * Category 1
  * Category 2
  * Category 3
* **Description** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_description`, text): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Email** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_email`, email): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_phone`, text): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Request Date** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_request_date`, date): Enter the date when your request is needed.
* **Request Time** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_request_time`, time): Enter the time when your request is needed.
* **Request Notes** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_request_notes`, text): Provide any additional notes regarding your request.
* **Support Request Type** (`operations_planning_assistance_request_form_support_request_type`, select_one): Choose the support request type that best describes your needs.
  * Type 1
  * Type 2
  * Type 3
