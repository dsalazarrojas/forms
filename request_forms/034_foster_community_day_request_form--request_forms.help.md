# Foster Community Day Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to provide a structured and organized way to collect requests from community members for a community day event. It is not intended to be a generic request form, but rather a specific tool for organizing and facilitating a community day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the form title**: Enter a brief description of the event or activity you are requesting to be included in the community day.
2. **List Prophets**: If you are a community leader or have a specific role in the event, list the "Prophets" (presumably a placeholder for key team members or organizers).
3. **Select Date and Time**: Choose the date and time for the community day event.
4. **Specify Location**: Enter the location where the community day event will take place.
5. **Provide Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form (text)**: A brief description of the event or activity you are requesting to be included in the community day. This should be a clear and concise summary of your proposal.
* **Prophets (text)**: If you are a community leader or have a specific role in the event, list the key team members or organizers. This is not a required field, but it can be useful for event organizers to get in touch with you.
* **Date (date)**: Choose a specific date for the community day event. This field is required, so make sure to pick a date that works for everyone involved.
* **Time (time)**: Select a specific time for the community day event. This field is not required, so feel free to leave it blank or specify a time that fits your schedule.
* **Location (text)**: Enter the location where the community day event will take place. This field is not required, but it's essential to have a specific location for the event.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request. This field is not required, but it can help event organizers understand your proposal.
* **Chatjimmy (email)**: This field seems to be a placeholder for a specific tool or platform used for communication or organization. Since it's required, please enter a valid email address or other relevant contact information.
