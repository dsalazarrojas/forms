# Digital Payment Integration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Payment Integration Request Form is used to request digital payment integration services. This form is designed to help customers integrate digital payment methods such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal into their businesses or personal accounts. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information (indicated by an asterisk *) for each field. This will help us understand your request accurately and efficiently.
2. Select the integration type that best suits your needs from the "Integration Type" field.
3. Choose the payment methods you're interested in integrating (multiple selections are allowed for the "Payment Method" field).
4. Enter the amount of money you'd like to transfer or use for the integration (if applicable).
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your request, if needed.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, Optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (text, Optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Integration Type** (select_one, Optional): Select the type of digital payment integration you need:
	+ Apple Pay
	+ Google Pay
	+ PayPal
* **Account Info** (text, Optional): Provide any additional account information relevant to your integration request.
* **Date** (date, Optional): Enter the date of the payment or integration request.
* **Time** (time, Optional): Enter the time of the payment or integration request.
* **Note** (note, Optional): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more payment methods you'd like to integrate:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Wire Transfer
	+ Check
* **Amount** (number, Optional): Enter the amount of money you'd like to transfer or use for the integration (if applicable).
* **Submit** (text, Optional): Click this button to send your request.
