# Staff Coverage Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Staff Coverage Request Form is designed to capture the necessary information required for staff coverage, including request dates, employee details, shift timing, and manager contact information. This form ensures that all relevant details are accurately recorded, making it easier to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form accurately and completely**, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. **Check our records** to avoid duplicate submissions.
3. **Enter your own contact details** for your manager's attention.
4. **Provide clear notes** if needed to support your request.
5. **Submit the form**, and we will process it as soon as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`): <i> Enter the date you're requesting staff coverage for. Ensure it's in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd). If you're unsure, use the calendar icon to help you select the date. </i>
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): <i> Enter your name, as this is the staff member making the request. Use the format as it appears on your personnel card or your email address. </i>
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`): <i> Enter the start time of your shift. This is the time you want the staff coverage to begin. Format as HH:MM. </i>
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`): <i> Enter the end time of your shift. This is the time you want the staff coverage to end. Format as HH:MM. </i>
* **Request Hours** (`request_hours`): <i> Enter the number of hours you're requesting staff coverage for. This is the duration of your shift, not the total coverage hours. </i>
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): <i> Enter your manager's name as it appears on their personnel card or email address. </i>
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): <i> Enter your manager's phone number. This will help them quickly contact you if needed. </i>
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): <i> Enter your manager's email address. This is where they can be reached for more information. </i>
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`): <i> If needed, enter a brief note about your request. This can help explain your absence or provide more context. </i>
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): <i> This field will be filled out by the system after your request has been approved. </i>

**Tips**

* Make sure to submit your request on time to allow for proper scheduling.
* If you need to change or cancel your request, contact your manager directly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team.
