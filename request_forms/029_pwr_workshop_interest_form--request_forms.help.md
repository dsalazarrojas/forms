# pwr_workshop_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your interest in attending a workshop on [workshop topic] in [location]. We will use this information to better understand your preferences and tailor the workshop to suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your location where you would like to attend the workshop.
2. Indicate your interest in the workshop.
3. Select your preferred time for the workshop (if you have one).
4. Choose a date for the workshop (if you have a preference).
5. Once you have filled in all the necessary information, click the submit button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter the location where you would like to attend the workshop. This can be a city, state, or region.

### Workshop Interest
* **Workshop Interest** (`workshop_interest`, text, optional): Indicate your interest in the workshop by providing a brief description of what you are interested in. This can be a specific topic or a general area of interest.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for the workshop, select it from the available options. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): If you have a preferred date for the workshop, select it from the available options. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when indicating your location and interest in the workshop.
* If you have a preferred time or date, select one that works best for you.
* You can always let us know if your preferences change.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them in the form.

Note: This help guide is based on the assumption that the workshop topic and location are not specified. If you are filling out this form for a specific workshop, please replace [workshop topic] and [location] with the actual topic and location of the workshop.
