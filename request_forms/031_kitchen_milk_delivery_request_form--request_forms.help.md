# Kitchen Milk Delivery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Milk Delivery Request Form is used to request milk delivery for your household. Please ensure you have a legitimate milk delivery request before submitting this form. If you have already submitted a similar request, your previous request will be cancelled and a new one will be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone Number.
4. Choose the type(s) of milk you would like to be delivered.
5. Select a date for delivery.
6. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates about your milk delivery.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number to receive calls or messages about your milk delivery.
* **Milk Type** (`Milk Type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type(s) of milk you would like to be delivered. You can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Milk 1
	+ Milk 2
	+ Milk 3
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, required): Choose a date for your milk delivery. Please note that delivery dates can be selected as per your preference, but milk will be delivered only when it's available.
