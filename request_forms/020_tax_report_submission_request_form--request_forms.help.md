# Tax Report Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Report Submission Request Form is used to collect information for tax report submissions. This form helps our team gather necessary details to review and process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the required documents (if any) ready for upload before starting the submission process.
2. Fill out this form with accurate and complete information.
3. If you need help with a specific field, please refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tax_report_submission_request_form (Text)**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information regarding your tax report submission.
* **Business Name (Text)**: Enter your business name, as it appears on your tax reports.
* **Tax Report Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of tax report you are submitting. Options include W2, 1095, and Other.
* **Tax Report Due Date (Date)**: Enter the due date of your tax report, following the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Supporting Documents (Text)**: Provide a list of supporting documents you are uploading with your submission.
* **Uploaded Documents (Select Multiple)**: Select the types of documents you are uploading. Options include Tax Return and Financial Statement.
* **Tax Report Submission Status (Select One)**: Choose the status of your submission. Options include Submitted, Pending, and Cancelled.
* **Tax Report Submission Comment (Text)**: Enter any additional comments regarding your tax report submission.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Tax Report Submission Requester (Text)**: Enter your name and title, or the name and title of the person requesting the tax report submission.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required. If you have any issues or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
