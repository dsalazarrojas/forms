# Urgent Healing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urgent Healing Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking urgent healing services. This form is intended for those who need immediate attention and care. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the type of healing you are seeking (**Healing Type**).
6. If applicable, select the spiritual guides you would like to work with (**Spiritual Guides**).
7. Choose the level of urgency for your request (**Services Urgency**).
8. Select the healing services you would like to receive (**Healing Services**).
9. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share (**Additional Comments**).
10. Indicate how you would prefer to be followed up on (**Follow Up**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
  - This is a required field for us to contact you.
  - Please provide your first name, as this will help us to identify you and prioritize your request.
- **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name (if known).
  - This is an optional field, but it's helpful for our records and for you to be able to provide a full name.
  - If you don't provide your last name, we will still handle your request.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
  - This is an optional field, but it's helpful for us to contact you.
  - If you don't provide your email address, we will still handle your request.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This is an optional field, but it's helpful for us to contact you directly.
  - If you don't provide your phone number, we will still handle your request.
- **Healing Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of healing you are seeking.
  - This will help us to determine the best course of action for your request.
  - Options are Spiritual Healing, Holistic Healing, and Energy Healing.
- **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Provide your date of birth.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to understand your background and provide more personalized services.
  - Enter the date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy."
- **Time of Birth** (Time, Optional): Provide your time of birth.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to understand your background and provide more personalized services.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Spiritual Guides** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the spiritual guides you would like to work with.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to tailor our services to your needs.
  - Options are Shaman, Healer, and Energy Worker.
- **Services Urgency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the level of urgency for your request.
  - This will help us to prioritize your request.
  - Options are Low, Medium, and High.
- **Healing Services** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the healing services you would like to receive.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to provide the best services for your needs.
  - Options are Spiritual Guidance, Energy Work, and Healing Sessions.
- **Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to better understand your needs.
  - Please be as detailed as possible.
- **Healer Response** (Select One, Optional): Indicate how you would like to be followed up on by the healer.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to communicate with you efficiently.
  - Options are Yes and No.
- **Follow Up** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you would like to be followed up on.
  - This is an optional field, but it helps us to communicate with you efficiently.
  - Options are Phone, Email, and In Person.

Note: Please complete this form only if it's truly urgent, and make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure we handle your request efficiently.
