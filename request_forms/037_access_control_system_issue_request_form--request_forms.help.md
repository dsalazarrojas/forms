# Access Control System Issue Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to report issues with the access control system, allowing our team to identify and resolve problems efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal information:
    *   Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
    *   Enter your work email address in the "Work Email" field, which will be used for status updates.
2.  Identify the issue:
    *   Select the category of the issue from the dropdown list under "Category of Issue".
    *   Describe the location of the issue in the "Specific Door or Access Point Location" field.
    *   If applicable, provide your badge number for reference.
3.  Provide additional details:
    *   Explain the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
    *   If you've observed any error messages or light colors, describe them in the "Describe any error messages or light colors observed" field.
4.  Specify the resolution:
    *   Choose the priority level of the issue from the dropdown list under "Priority Level".
    *   If you've experienced this issue before, select "Yes" in the "Have you experienced this issue before?" field.
    *   Let us know if you'd like to provide a photo of the fault in the "Will you provide a photo of the fault?" field.
5.  Schedule the resolution:
    *   Suggest a time for a technician to visit to resolve the issue in the "When is the best time for a technician to visit?" field.
6.  Request updates:
    *   Check "Yes" in the "Notify me when this is resolved?" field to receive an update when the issue is resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as the person reporting the issue.
* **Work Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address for status updates and further communication.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Identify your primary work group or department.
* **Category of Issue** (`issue_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary category of the issue from the provided dropdown list.
*   *   **Badge or Card Not Working**
*   *   **Door Not Opening**
*   *   **Door Not Locking**
*   *   **System Software Error**
*   *   **Intercom Fault**
* **Specific Door or Access Point Location** (`location_of_issue`, `text`, required): Describe the exact location of the issue.
* **Badge Number** (`badge_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a badge number, enter it for reference.
* **Describe any error messages or light colors observed** (`error_message`, `text`, optional): Provide any error messages or light colors you've observed related to the issue.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency of the resolution from the dropdown list:
    *   *   **Low - Minor Inconvenience**
    *   *   **Medium - Impacting Operations**
    *   *   **High - Security Risk**
* **Have you experienced this issue before?** (`recurring_issue`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you've encountered this issue before.
* **Will you provide a photo of the fault?** (`photo_provided`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes - Emailing separately" if you're willing to provide a photo of the issue.
* **When is the best time for a technician to visit?** (`preferred_resolution_time`, `text`, optional): Suggest a time for a technician to visit and resolve the issue.
* **Notify me when this is resolved?** (`notification_request`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" to receive an update when the issue is resolved.
