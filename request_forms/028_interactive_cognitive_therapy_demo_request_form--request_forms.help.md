# interactive_cognitive_therapy_demo_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a demo for interactive cognitive therapy. It is intended for gathering information about the user for the demo and ensures that all relevant details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone number
5. Select a date for the demo
6. Select a time for the demo
7. Choose the services you are interested in
8. Add any additional information you feel is relevant

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (date, required)**: Select a date for the demo. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time (time, required)**: Select a time for the demo. Please use the 24-hour clock format.
* **Interested In (select_one, required)**: Choose the services you are interested in. Selecting "Other" allows you to provide more information in the Additional Information field.
* **Additional Information (note, optional)**: Provide any additional details that you think would be helpful to know.
* **Confirm Phone (text, required)**: Re-enter your phone number to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
