# HR Policy Digitization Request Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees to request HR policy digitization. The purpose of the form is to gather necessary details to initiate the process of converting printed or outdated HR policies into digital formats for easy access and updating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Choose the correct "Policy Type" to describe the type of policy you are requesting to be digitized.
3. Select the "Policy Status" to indicate the current status of the policy.
4. Provide the "Request Date" in the desired date format.
5. Optionally, fill in "Policy Details" with additional context or explanations.
6. If necessary, select "Priority" to indicate the level of urgency for the digitization request.
7. Finally, click submit to send the form for HR review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form (1)**: The text field where you can describe the policy you would like to digitize in a brief summary.
* **Policy Details (2)**: Enter any additional details or context about the policy, if necessary.
* **Policy Type (3)**: Choose the type of policy you are requesting to be digitized. Options include:
	+ Policy Description
	+ Procedure
	+ Policy Manual
	+ Policy Handbook
	+ Employee Handbook
	+ Other
* **Policy Status (4)**: Select the status of the policy. Options include:
	+ In Progress
	+ Draft
	+ Completed
	+ On Hold
* **Request Date (5)**: Enter the date when you requested the policy digitization.
* **Requested By (6)**: Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or email.
* **Requested By Department (7)**: Enter the department you are associated with.
* **Requested By Title (8)**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Priority (9)**: Choose the level of priority for your request, with options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Request Details (10)**: Enter any additional context, explanations, or comments about your request.
