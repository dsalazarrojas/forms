# Laser Cutting Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to request services from a laser cutting service, allowing you to provide detailed information about your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully to ensure you've filled out all the required information.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Design Upload (`design_upload`)

*   **Design Upload**: Upload your design file to enable our team to process your request. Please ensure that your design file is in a compatible format.

### 2. Laser Cutting Service Request Form

*   **Laser Cutting Service Request Form**: Enter a brief description of your request. This field is not required but can help our team better understand your needs.

### 3. Delivery Preferences (`delivery_preferences`)

*   **Delivery Preferences**: Select your preferred delivery option from the list:
    *   Fast Delivery: Receive your order as soon as possible.
    *   Standard Delivery: Receive your order within the normal production timeframe.
    *   Priority Delivery: Receive your order with priority processing.

### 4. Design Details (`design_details`)

*   **Design Details**: Provide a detailed description of your design, if needed, for our team to better understand your request.

### 5. Cut Size (`cut_size`)

*   **Cut Size**: Enter the size you need the laser cutting service to perform. This value will be used to process your request.

### 6. Material (`material`)

*   **Material**: Select the material you need the laser cutting service to work with:
    *   Acrylic
    *   ABS
    *   Polycarbonate
    *   Wood

### 7. Production Timeframe (`production_timeframe`)

*   **Production Timeframe**: Enter the timeframe in which you need your order produced. This will be used to schedule and plan your production.

### 8. Production Date (`production_date`)

*   **Production Date**: Enter the specific date you need your order to be produced. This will be used to schedule production.

### 9. Fabrication Location (`fabrication_location`)

*   **Fabrication Location**: Enter the location where you need your order fabricated. This will be used to plan and coordinate production.

### 10. Fabrication Notes (`fabrication_notes`)

*   **Fabrication Notes**: Provide any additional notes or instructions for our team to follow during fabrication.

### 11. Contact Details (`contact_details`)

*   **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information so our team can reach out to you with updates or questions.

Please note that some fields are optional, but it's essential to fill out all required fields accurately for a smooth and efficient service. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
