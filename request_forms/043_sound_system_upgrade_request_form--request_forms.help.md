# Sound System Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound System Upgrade Request Form is designed to collect information and requests for sound system upgrades from users. This form is essential for system administrators to track and manage all the upgrade requests, ensuring timely and efficient resolution of these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your Organization Name.
* Select the type of upgrade request (Audio Equipment Upgrade, Audio Equipment Purchase, or Other).
* Specify whether you want to upgrade equipment (Yes/No).
* Provide a description of the current equipment.
* Describe the desired upgrade equipment or specifications.
* Explain the reason for your request.
* Enter your contact information (Email, Phone Number).
* Optionally, add any comments or notes about your request.
* Select a Status (New, Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
* Choose a Category (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter your organization's name.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`): Select the type of upgrade request (Audio Equipment Upgrade, Audio Equipment Purchase, or Other).
* **Upgrade Equipment** (`upgrade_equipment`): Specify whether you want to upgrade equipment (Yes/No).
* **Current Equipment Description** (`current_equipment_description`): Describe the current equipment.
* **Desired Equipment Description** (`desired_equipment_description`): Describe the desired upgrade equipment or specifications.
* **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`): Briefly explain why you are requesting the upgrade.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your contact information (Email, Phone Number).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Optionally, add any comments or notes about your request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): This field is auto-generated and displays the user who is assigned to handle your request.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`): This field is auto-generated and displays when your request was assigned to a user.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): This field is auto-generated and displays the user who last updated your request.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): This field is auto-generated and displays when your request was last updated.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the current status of your request (New, Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Status Description** (`status_description`): Briefly describe the status of your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Optionally, add any additional comments about your request.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): This field is auto-generated and displays the user who created your request.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): This field is auto-generated and displays when your request was created.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): This field is auto-generated and displays the unique ID of your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field is auto-generated and displays the tool assigned to handle your request.
* **Category** (`category`): This field is optional and should be filled out if necessary.
