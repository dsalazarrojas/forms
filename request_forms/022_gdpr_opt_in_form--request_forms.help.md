# GDPR Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to receive updates and marketing offers from the organization. It is not a required form, and you can choose not to provide any information if you don't want to receive updates or marketing offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference for receiving updates and marketing offers.
2. If you choose to receive updates and marketing offers, enter your email address in the required format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
3. If you provide your email address, you can optionally enter your phone number and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Optin
* **Optin** (`Optin`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate your preference for receiving updates and marketing offers. Select "Yes" to receive updates and marketing offers, or "No" if you don't want to receive any.
### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address if you choose to receive updates and marketing offers. Enter your email address in the required format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number if you provide your email address. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
### Name
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name if you provide your email address and/or phone number. Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.

## Tips
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, you can always choose not to provide any information if you don't want to receive updates or marketing offers.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number in the required format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com` and `123-456-7890`).
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the organization's support team.
