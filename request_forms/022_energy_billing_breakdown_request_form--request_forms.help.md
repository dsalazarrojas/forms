# Energy Billing Breakdown Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an energy billing breakdown, which will provide you with a detailed analysis of your energy consumption and charges. This analysis will help you understand your energy consumption patterns and identify potential areas for cost savings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "User Details" section.
2. Enter your account number in the "Account Number" field.
3. Select the billing cycle for which you would like to receive the analysis (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual).
4. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please provide them in the "Question" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so we can contact you with the analysis.
6. If you have any phone number where we can contact you, please enter it in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: This is where you will provide your personal details to help us process your request.
* **Account Number**: Please enter your account number to associate your request with your energy account.
* **Billing Cycle**: Select the billing cycle for which you would like to receive the analysis. This could be Monthly, Quarterly, or Bi-Annual.
* **Question**: If you have any specific questions or concerns, please provide them here.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with the analysis.
* **Phone**: If you have a preferred phone number where we can contact you, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your account number for accuracy.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a request for a billing breakdown in the past 6 months.
* Please allow 2-3 business days for processing your request.
