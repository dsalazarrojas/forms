# Court Document Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Court Document Access Form is designed to collect information about requested documents and facilitate access to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Form" page with general information about the document you are requesting.
2. Select the type of document you are requesting on the "Document Type" page.
3. Enter the date of the document on the "Document Date" page.
4. Choose the status of the document on the "Document Status" page.
5. Enter your contact information on the "Requestor Contact" page.
6. State your reason for requesting the document on the "Reason for Request" page.
7. Choose your preferred contact method on the "Preferred Contact" page.
8. Provide any additional comments on the "Comments" page.
9. Select the user who should be assigned the document on the "Assigned User" page.
10. Confirm the submitted date and time on the "Request Date" page.
11. Confirm the form submission by selecting "Submit".
