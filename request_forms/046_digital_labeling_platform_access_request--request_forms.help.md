# digital_labeling_platform_access_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request access to the digital labeling platform. It gathers information about your project, email address, phone number, and your understanding of the platform's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Project Name** field with a brief description of your project.
2. Provide a detailed **Project Description** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the format `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional).
5. Select the type of **Project Type** (Yes/No).
6. Confirm that you **Agree to Terms** (Yes/No).
7. Select that you **Understand and Accept** the terms (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter a brief name for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`requester_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of project (Yes/No).
* **I agree to Terms** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **I understand and Accept** (`terms_consent`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you understand the terms and conditions and accept them.

## Tips

* Please provide a clear and concise description of your project.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Read and understand the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* Duplicate requests will not be processed.
