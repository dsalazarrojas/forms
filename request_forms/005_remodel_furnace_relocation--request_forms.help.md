# Remodel Furnace Relocation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for relocating and remodeling a furnace in a building. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approval before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `furnace_area` field to specify the current location of the furnace.
2. If necessary, attach documents or supporting files to the `file_upload` field.
3. Select a date and time for the `schedule_relocation` field to schedule the relocation of the furnace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **furnace_area** (furnace_area, text, required): Enter the current location of the furnace, including the room or area where it is currently installed.
* **file_upload** (file_upload, text, optional): If you need to attach any documents or supporting files, such as plans or specifications, for the relocation and remodeling process, upload them here.
* **schedule_relocation** (schedule_relocation, date, optional): Choose a date and time to schedule the relocation of the furnace. Be sure to consider the impact on the surrounding area and ensure that the schedule does not conflict with other planned tasks or events.
