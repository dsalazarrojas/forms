# Organizational Chart Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Chart Update Request Form is used to update the organizational chart of an employee, ensuring that changes are accurately reflected and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
* Enter your current Job Title.
* Select your Department (if applicable).
* Indicate whether your Manager's Title is accurate.
* If necessary, select the dates you would like to report on (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title and title change request, if any.
* **Department**: Select 'Yes' to indicate a department change or 'No' if there is no change.
* **Manager Title**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that your manager's title is accurate or 'No' if it needs to be updated.
* **Report Date**: Enter the dates you would like to report on, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This field is optional and only necessary if you need to report on specific dates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Check your answers for correctness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your manager or HR department for assistance.
