# Institutional Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Institutional Data Access Request Form is a form used to collect information about the requester's details, the purpose of their request, and the data they are interested in accessing. It is used to help the institutional data access team review and process requests in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields with your details and information about your request.
2. Make sure to provide your correct contact information, including your phone number and email address.
3. Clearly state the purpose of your request and provide any additional details that may be relevant.
4. Check the box to indicate your consent to proceed with the request.
5. Enter the duration for which you would like to access the data.
6. Enter the name of the institution you are representing.
7. Enter the data you are interested in accessing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Purpose** (Text, Optional): Explain the reason for your request and what you plan to use the data for.
- **Duration** (Date, Optional): Indicate how long you would like to access the data for.
- **Consent** (Select One, Optional): Check this box to indicate your consent to proceed with the request.
- **Requester Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
- **Requester Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
- **Purpose Details** (Text, Optional): Provide additional information about the purpose of your request.
- **Institution Details** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the institution you are requesting data from.
- **Data Requested** (Text, Optional): Be specific about the data you are requesting access to. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any supporting documents or files to attach, please attach them along with your form submission.
* If you are representing an institution, please make sure to enter the correct institution name.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us directly.
