# Symposium Leave Of Absance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Symposium Leave Of Absence Request form is used to request a Leave of Absance for a symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User information in the User field.
2. Select the Symposium you are requesting a Leave of Absance for from the Symposium field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your Leave of Absance in the Start Date and End Date fields.
4. Provide a Reason for your request in the Reason field.
5. Select your Manager(s) for approval in the Manager field.
6. Add any additional comments in the Comment field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User (User)** (`text`, `required`): Enter your name and any other relevant user information.
* **Symposium (Symposium)** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose the symposium you are requesting a Leave of Absance for. Options are: Symposium 1 and Symposium 2.
* **Start Date (Start Date)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date you would like your Leave of Absance to start.
* **End Date (End Date)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date you would like your Leave of Absance to end.
* **Reason (Reason)** (`text`, `required`): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a Leave of Absance.
* **Manager (Manager)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Choose your manager(s) who will approve your request.
* **Comment (Comment)** (`text`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
