# Vacant Property CCTV Monitoring Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacant Property CCTV Monitoring Request form is designed to collect information from property owners or managers to request monitoring services for their vacant properties. This form helps the property owner or manager to provide details about the property, the monitoring services needed, and any additional information relevant to the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information page with any relevant details about the property.
2. On the Monitoring Details page, select the specific monitoring details that apply to your request.
3. On the Authorization page, provide any necessary authorization details.
4. On the Submission page, provide any additional submission details.
5. On the Additional page, add any other relevant information about the property or request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Please provide general information about the property, such as its address, location, or any other relevant details.
* **Monitoring Details**: Select the monitoring services you need for your property, such as camera locations or monitoring frequencies.
* **Authorization**: Provide any necessary authorization details, such as passwords or access codes.
* **Submission**: Add any submission details, such as how you plan to submit the request or any other relevant information.
* **Additional**: Add any other relevant information about the property or request that is not covered in the previous fields.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure prompt processing of your request.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or support team for assistance.
- Double-check your submission details to avoid errors or misunderstandings.
