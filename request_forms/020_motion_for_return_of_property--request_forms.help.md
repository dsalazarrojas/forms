# Motion For Return Of Property - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the return of property that has been taken or seized by the authorities. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering your request for return of property in the "request_form" field. This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of the property you are requesting be returned.
2. Next, enter the "caseid" number, which is the unique identifier assigned to your case. This helps the authorities track your request.
3. Select the date you are requesting the return of property in the "motiondate" field. This date should be in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
4. Choose the type of property you are requesting be returned from the "propertytype" dropdown menu. You can select from "property", "land", or "vehicle".
5. If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, indicate "Yes" or "No" in the "supportingdocs" field.
6. Enter the case number assigned to your case in the "casenumber" field. This helps the authorities track your request.
7. Provide a clear and concise reason for requesting the return of property in the "returnreason" field.
8. Select the type of return you are requesting from the "returnrequesttype" dropdown menu. You can choose from "return", "abandon", or "lost".
9. Enter the date you are requesting the return of property in the "returnrequestdate" field. This date should be in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
10. If you have a witness or witnesses who can support your request, enter their names in the "supportingwitnesses" field.
11. Finally, enter your signature in the "signature" field to confirm your request.
12. If you have any additional witness statements, enter them in the "witnessstatement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_form** (text, required): This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of the property you are requesting be returned.
* **caseid** (text, required): Enter the unique case number assigned to your case.
* **motiondate** (date, required): Select the date you are requesting the return of property.
* **propertytype** (select_one, required): Choose the type of property you are requesting be returned (property, land, or vehicle).
* **property_description** (note, optional): Provide any additional information about the property you are requesting be returned.
* **supportingdocs** (select_multiple, optional): If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, indicate "Yes" or "No".
* **casenumber** (number, required): Enter the case number assigned to your case.
* **return_reason** (text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for requesting the return of property.
* **return_request_type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of return you are requesting (return, abandon, or lost).
* **return_request_date** (date, required): Select the date you are requesting the return of property.
* **return_reason_description** (note, optional): Provide additional information about the reason for requesting the return of property.
* **signature** (note, required): Enter your signature to confirm your request.
* **witness_statement** (text, optional): If you have any additional witness statements, enter them here.
* **supporting_witnesses** (select_multiple, optional): If you have any witness or witnesses who can support your request, enter their names.
* **motion_status** (select_one, required): Select the status of your motion (active or inactive).
