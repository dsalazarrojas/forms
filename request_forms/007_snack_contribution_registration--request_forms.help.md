# Snack Contribution Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a contribution for a snack, meal, or other consumable item to be served at an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name to identify yourself as the contributor.
2. Select the date of the event you are contributing to.
3. Enter the name of the event.
4. Specify the number of servings of your contribution.
5. Choose the type of event (e.g. Snack, Meal, Potluck).
6. If you have any dietary restrictions for your contribution, select them from the list.
7. Enter the location of the event.
8. Enter the start and end times of the event.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about your contribution.
10. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: (`user_name`, text, **required**) - Fill in your full name as you would like to be referred to on the event sign-in.
* **Event Date**: (`event_date`, date, **required**) - Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Name**: (`event_name`, text, **required**) - Enter the name of the event.
* **Servings**: (`servings`, number, **required**) - Indicate how many servings of your contribution you are providing.
* **Event Type**: (`event_type`, select_one, **required**) - Choose the type of event (e.g. Snack, Meal, Potluck).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, **optional**) - Select any dietary restrictions for your contribution.
* **Location**: (`location`, text, **optional**) - Enter the location of the event.
* **Start Time**: (`start_time`, time, **required**) - Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: (`end_time`, time, **required**) - Enter the end time of the event.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, text, **optional**) - Add any additional notes about your contribution.

## Tips
* Make sure to only submit one form per contribution to avoid duplication.
* Double-check the accuracy of your submission before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer.
