# Youth Services Resource Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Services Resource Directory Submission form is designed to collect information from users about their services, resources, and needs. The form helps create a comprehensive directory that caters to the diverse needs of youth in the community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name, and any other relevant details.
2. Share information about your organization or group, including their contact details.
3. Indicate whether your services offer yes or no to the following options:
   * Services
   * Service type
   * Service subtype
   * Service frequency
   * Language
   * Gender
   * Age range
   * Target population
4. Fill out the contact information, including the contact name, email, phone number, and notes.
5. Add any supporting files or descriptions to provide more context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`id: 1`): This field is for you to enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Organization Information** (`id: 2`): This field is for you to enter information about your organization or group, including their contact details.
* **Services** (`id: 3`): Indicate whether your services offer yes or no to the following:
	+ Yes: Yes, we offer this service.
	+ No: We do not offer this service.
* **Submit** (`id: 4`): Select yes if you are sure about the submission, no otherwise.
* **E-mail** (`id: 5`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 6`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`id: 7`): Enter the date of your service or resource.
* **Time** (`id: 8`): Enter the time of your service or resource.
* **Description** (`id: 9`): Provide a brief description of your service or resource.
* **Location** (`id: 10`): Enter the location of your service or resource.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 11`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`id: 12`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 13`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Service List** (`id: 14`): This field is for you to list other services offered by your organization or group.
* **Notes** (`id: 15`): Add any additional information or notes about your service or resource.
* **File 1** (`id: 16`), **File 2** (`id: 17`), **File 3** (`id: 18`): You can attach supporting files to provide more context to your submission.
* **Service Type** (`id: 19`), **Service Subtype** (`id: 20`), **Service Frequency** (`id: 21`), **Language** (`id: 22`), **Gender** (`id: 23`), **Age Range** (`id: 24`), **Target Population** (`id: 25`): Indicate whether your services offer yes or no to the following options.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and comprehensive information.
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* The submission process may take a few seconds to process, please be patient.
