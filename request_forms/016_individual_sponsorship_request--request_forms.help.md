# Individual Sponsorship Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to request sponsorship for a specific project or event. It helps establish a relationship between the individual and the potential sponsor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your sponsor request information in the first section.
2. Enter the details of your potential sponsor in the second section.
3. Share your project or event information in the third section.
4. Explain your relationship with the potential sponsor in the fourth section.
5. Add any additional contact information in the fifth section.
6. Upload any relevant files or documents in the sixth section (if required).
7. Provide a brief summary of your project or event in the seventh section.
8. Finally, click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Request Info (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the sponsor request, including the project or event name, purpose, and any relevant dates or deadlines.
* **Sponsor Details (Text)**: Enter the details of your potential sponsor, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **Request Info (Text)**: Share as much information as possible about your project or event, such as the budget, timeline, and expected outcomes.
* **Sponsor Relationship (Text)**: Explain how you know the potential sponsor, and any prior interactions or collaborations you've had with them.
* **Contact Info (Text)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Sponsor Requirements (Text)**: If required, list any specific requirements or expectations you have for the sponsor, such as budget constraints or specific skills needed.
* **Request Submission (Text)**: Enter a brief summary of your project or event.
* **Upload Files (Text)**: Upload any relevant files or documents that support your request, such as project plans, timelines, or marketing materials.
