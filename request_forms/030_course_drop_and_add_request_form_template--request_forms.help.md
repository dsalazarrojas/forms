# Course Drop And Add Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to a student's course schedule. It is typically used by students to add or drop courses, and it ensures that the student's advisor has been consulted and agrees with the request before it's submitted.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including your Student ID and Student Name.
2. Select your Academic Program from the list provided.
3. Enter a brief Reason for your request.
4. If necessary, enter your Advisor's name and email address. If you do not know your advisor's email address, you can leave this field blank.
5. Enter your Reg Office contact information, including their name and phone number.
6. If you are requesting a course drop, enter your Student Signature below.
7. If you are requesting a course add, enter your Advisor Signature below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`user_input`): This section is for you to enter your personal details, including your student ID and name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter your student ID number here.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`): Select the academic program that you are enrolled in from the list provided.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Enter a brief reason for your request to add or drop a course.
* **Advisor Approval** (`advisor_approval`): If you are requesting to add or drop a course, enter the date when you received approval from your advisor.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`): If you are requesting to drop a course, enter your signature here.
* **Registrar Note** (`registrar_note`): Leave this field blank, as it is only for the registrar's office to use.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`): Leave this field blank, as it will be automatically populated when the form is submitted.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`): Enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`): Enter your advisor's name, if you know it.
* **Advisor Email** (`advisor_email`): Enter your advisor's email address, if you know it.
* **Reg Office** (`reg_office`): Enter the name and contact information of the person in the reg office who is handling your request.
* **Reg Office Phone** (`reg_office_phone`): Enter the phone number of the reg office contact.
* **Department** (`department`): This field is not currently being used in the form, so you can leave it blank.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have received approval from your advisor before submitting this form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the reg office for assistance.
