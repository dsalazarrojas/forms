# Cards Design Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit their card design requests, providing essential details such as contact information, card specifications, design requirements, and deadlines. This information helps our design team create custom cards that meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your full name, company/practice name (if applicable), and contact email address.
2. Select the type of card you need (e.g., Business Card, Greeting Card, etc.).
3. Choose the size and orientation of the card (e.g., Standard, Square, Horizontal, or Vertical).
4. Select the material and finish of the card (e.g., Premium Matte, High-Gloss, etc.).
5. Enter the quantity of cards you need.
6. Upload or link to any design assets (e.g., logos, images) for the card.
7. Enter the exact text to be printed on the card.
8. Enter your brand's colors in hex code (if applicable).
9. Set the deadline for delivery.
10. Provide any additional design requests or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_full_name`): Enter your full name as the customer requesting the card design.
* **Company/Practice Name** (`customer_company_practice_name`): If you're representing a company or practice, enter their name. This field is optional for personal use.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email_address`): Enter the email address where you'd like to receive the design proofs.
* **Card Type** (`card_type_selected`): Select the purpose of the card (e.g., Business Card, Greeting Card, etc.).
* **Card Size** (`card_size_dimensions`): Choose the preferred size of the card (e.g., Standard, Square, A7, etc.).
* **Orientation** (`orientation_layout_selected`): Select the orientation of the card (e.g., Horizontal, Vertical).
* **Material & Finish** (`material_finish_selection`): Choose the type of paper and coating for the card (e.g., Premium Matte, High-Gloss, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity_ordered_total`): Enter the number of cards you need.
* **Design & Text Details** (`design_elements_header`): This is a header for the next section, providing space for additional design requests or comments.
* **Design/Logo Upload or Link** (`design_upload_link_reference`): Upload or link to any design assets (e.g., logos, images) for the card.
* **Card Text and Messaging** (`card_text_messaging_content`): Enter the exact text to be printed on the card.
* **Brand Colors** (`brand_colors_hex_codes`): Enter your brand's colors in hex code (if applicable).
* **Requested Deadline Date** (`order_deadline_date`): Set the deadline for delivery.
* **Additional Design Request Details** (`additional_comments_request`): Enter any specific font or layout requests.
