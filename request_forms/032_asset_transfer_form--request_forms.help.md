# Asset Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Transfer Form is designed to document and facilitate the transfer of assets between employees, departments, or locations within the organization. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is gathered and communicated to the relevant parties involved in the transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department as the requester.
2. Provide the asset's asset ID and name.
3. Select the asset category (e.g., Computer Equipment, Office Furniture, etc.).
4. Enter the current location of the asset.
5. Identify the current custodian (who has the asset).
6. Specify the new location of the transfer.
7. Indicate the new custodian's department.
8. Choose the reason for the transfer.
9. Select the transfer type (permanent or temporary).
10. If temporary, specify the transfer duration.
11. If temporary, enter the expected return date.
12. Describe the asset's condition.
13. List any accessories included with the asset.
14. Choose the transport method (e.g., hand-carry, company vehicle, etc.).
15. If applicable, specify the transport cost.
16. Confirm if insurance is required during transport.
17. Confirm if IT approval is required for electronic assets.
18. Confirm the supervisor's approval.
19. Confirm the asset manager's approval.
20. Sign the form with your name to confirm your request.
21. The new custodian will sign the form to acknowledge receipt of the asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date (1)**: The date you are requesting the transfer.
* **Requester Name (2)**: Your name as the person requesting the transfer.
* **Requester ID (3)**: Your employee or account number.
* **Department (4)**: Your department that is requesting the transfer.
* **Email Address (5)**: Your email for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Your contact number.
* **Asset ID (7)**: The asset tag number.
* **Asset Name (8)**: The description of the asset.
* **Asset Category (9)**: The type of asset (e.g., Computer Equipment, Office Furniture, etc.).
* **Current Location (10)**: Where the asset is currently located.
* **Current Custodian (11)**: Who currently has the asset.
* **New Location (12)**: Where the asset will be transferred to.
* **New Custodian (13)**: The department of the new custodian.
* **New Custodian Department (14)**: The department of the new custodian.
* **Reason for Transfer (15)**: Why the transfer is happening.
* **Transfer Type (16)**: Whether the transfer is permanent or temporary.
* **Transfer Duration (17)**: If temporary, specify the duration of the transfer.
* **Expected Return Date (18)**: If temporary, specify the expected return date.
* **Current Asset Condition (19)**: The state of the asset.
* **Accessories Included (20)**: Any accessories that come with the asset.
* **Transport Method (21)**: How the asset will be transported (e.g., hand-carry, company vehicle, etc.).
* **Transport Cost (22)**: If applicable, specify the transport cost.
* **Is Insurance Required (23)**: Confirm if insurance is required during transport.
* **Is IT Approval Required (24)**: Confirm if IT approval is required for electronic assets.
* **Supervisor Approval (25)**: Confirm the supervisor's approval.
* **Asset Manager Approval (26)**: Confirm the asset manager's approval.
* **Transfer Completed Date (27)**: The date when the transfer was completed.
* **Requester Signature (28)**: Sign with your name to confirm your request.
* **New Custodian Signature (29)**: The new custodian signs to acknowledge receipt of the asset.
