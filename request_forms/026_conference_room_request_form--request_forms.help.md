# Conference Room Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a conference room for meetings, appointments, or events. Please note that conference rooms are subject to availability and may not be available for every request. This form helps us determine the best time slot for the meeting, allowing us to ensure that the room is available and the right equipment is in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in the required information, including:

1. Your full name and contact information (fields 1-3)
2. Meeting details (fields 4-13)
	* Select the preferred room for your meeting (field 6)
	* Choose a secondary room preference in case the first choice is not available (field 7)
	* Specify the date and start and end times for your meeting (fields 8-10)
	* Indicate if this is a recurring meeting (field 11) and specify the recurrence pattern if applicable (field 12)
3. Meeting title and description (fields 13-14)
4. Number of attendees (field 15)
5. Types of attendees (field 16)
6. Room setup and equipment requests (fields 17-18)
7. Catering and special requests (fields 19-20)
8. Additional comments or special requirements (field 21)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (Field 1, Text, Required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address** (Field 2, Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on your request.
* **Phone Number** (Field 3, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you via phone.
* **Department** (Field 4, Text, Required): Enter your department to help us better understand your request.
* **Room Preference** (Field 6, Select One, Required): Choose your first preference for the room type. If the first choice is not available, we will contact you to confirm an alternate room.
* **Alternate Room Choice** (Field 7, Select One, Optional): Choose your second preference for the room type in case the first choice is not available.
* **Requested Date** (Field 8, Date, Required): Select the date of your meeting.
* **Start Time** (Field 9, Time, Required): Select the start time of your meeting.
* **End Time** (Field 10, Time, Required): Select the end time of your meeting.
* **Is This a Recurring Meeting** (Field 11, Select One, Required): Indicate if this is a one-time meeting or a recurring meeting.
* **Recurrence Pattern** (Field 12, Text, Optional): If this is a recurring meeting, specify the pattern (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Meeting Title** (Field 13, Text, Required): Enter a clear and concise title of your meeting.
* **Meeting Description** (Field 14, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your meeting, including purpose and agenda.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (Field 15, Number, Required): Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Attendee Types** (Field 16, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the types of attendees (e.g., internal staff, external clients).
* **Room Setup Preference** (Field 17, Select One, Required): Choose the room setup preference (e.g., boardroom, theater).
* **Equipment Requests** (Field 18, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the equipment you require for your meeting (e.g., projector, screen).
* **Catering Request** (Field 19, Select One, Required): Indicate if you need catering and choose the level of catering required.
* **Catering Details** (Field 20, Text, Optional): If you requested catering, provide more details on your catering requirements.
* **Special Requirements** (Field 21, Text, Optional): Enter any special requirements or accessibility needs.
* **Parking Required** (Field 22, Number, Optional): Enter the number of parking spaces required.
* **Visitor Badges Required** (Field 23, Number, Optional): Enter the number of visitor badges required.
* **Priority Level** (Field 24, Select One, Required): Choose the importance level of your meeting.
* **Additional Comments** (Field 25, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your meeting.
* **I Confirm This Request** (Field 26, Select One, Required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
