# Travel Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for requesting travel reimbursement for business trips. Fill out the form with your details and travel information, and submit it to be reviewed and approved by your approver.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your travel request information, including the trip start and end dates, travel destination, and travel reason.
2. Specify the travel amount and currency used.
3. Attach any necessary travel documents.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, department, job title, and location.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments about your trip.
6. Select the status of your travel request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
7. Enter the date your travel request was approved or submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Request**: Enter a brief description of your travel purpose and destination. 
* **Trip Start Date**: Enter the start date of your trip. Format should be mm/dd/yyyy. 
* **Trip End Date**: Enter the end date of your trip. Format should be mm/dd/yyyy. 
* **Travel Destination**: Enter the city or location you are visiting. 
* **Travel Reason**: Enter a brief description of why you are traveling for business. 
* **Travel Amount**: Enter the total cost of your trip. 
* **Travel Currency**: Select the currency used for the trip. Options are USD, EUR, GBP, JPY. 
* **Other (please specify)**: If you are using a different currency, specify it here. 
* **Travel Documents**: Attach any necessary documents related to your travel, such as receipts or boarding passes. 
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address. 
* **Your Phone**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification. 
* **Your Department**: Enter your department name. 
* **Your Job Title**: Enter your job title. 
* **Your Location**: Enter your physical location. 
* **Your Organization**: Enter the name of your organization. 
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about your trip. 
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person or department that will review and approve your travel request. 
* **Approver Department**: Enter the department of the person or department that will review and approve your travel request. 
* **Approver Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person or department that will review and approve your travel request. 
* **Approver Location**: Enter the location of the person or department that will review and approve your travel request. 
* **Approver Comments**: Add any comments from the approver. 
* **Status**: Select the status of your travel request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending). 
* **Date Approved**: Enter the date your travel request was approved. 
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date your travel request was submitted. 

Note: Fields marked as "required: false" can be filled out if you have the information available, but are not required for submission. 

Tips:

* Make sure to attach all necessary travel documents to support your request.
* Ensure that your approver has all necessary information to review your request.
* Follow up with your approver for a status update on your request. 

Please submit the form after filling out all necessary fields and attaching required documents.
