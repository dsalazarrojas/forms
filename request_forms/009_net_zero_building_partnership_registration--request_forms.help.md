# Net Zero Building Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form allows you to register your organization as a partner or collaborator with the Net Zero Buildings project. The goal is to connect your organization with potential partners and collaborators and to establish a partnership that benefits both parties. Please make sure you are filling out this form if you are a potential partner, collaborator, or affiliate that wants to work together with the Net Zero Buildings project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your organization's information and select your role.
2. Provide details about your organization and its interest in the Net Zero Buildings project.
3. Fill out any other required fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: The name of your organization.
* **Street Address**: The address of your organization's main office or location.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of your organization's main office or location.
* **Email**: The email address of your organization.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number of your organization.
* **Contact First Name**: The first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name**: The last name of the contact person.
* **Role**: Select the role of your organization, such as Partner, Collaborator, or Affiliate.
* **Expertise & Interests**: Briefly describe your organization's expertise and interests related to the Net Zero Buildings project.
* **Partnership Type**: Select the type of partnership, such as General Partnership, Joint Partnership, or Collaborative Partnership.
* **Partnership Status**: Select the current status of your partnership, such as In Development, In Progress, or Active.
* **Onboarding Date**: The date your organization plans to start working with the Net Zero Buildings project.
* **Onboarding Time**: The time of day your organization plans to start working with the Net Zero Buildings project.
* **Partner Details**: Add any additional information about your organization and its collaboration with the Net Zero Buildings project. 

Note: This form is intended to gather general information about your organization and its interest in the Net Zero Buildings project. Please be as concise as possible and avoid providing sensitive or confidential information.
