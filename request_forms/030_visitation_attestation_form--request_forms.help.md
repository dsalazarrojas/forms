# Visitation Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from visitors about their visits, including their identity, contact details, and the circumstances of their visit. It generates an official attestations document that can be used to verify the identity of visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select the Date of your visit from the calendar.
6. Select the Time of your visit from the drop-down menu.
7. Enter the location you visited.
8. Briefly describe the Purpose of your visit.
9. Enter a Witness Statement, describing any notable events or circumstances you witnessed.
10. Select an Attestor Statement, confirming that you were present at the location and have nothing to hide.
11. Select your Signature, indicating your acceptance of the attestations document.
12. Finally, confirm your attestations by selecting the appropriate options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your official identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_visit`, date, required): Select the date of your visit from the calendar.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_visit`, time, required): Select the time of your visit from the drop-down menu.
* **Location Visited** (`location_visited`, text, required): Enter the exact location you visited.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_attestation`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required): Describe any notable events or circumstances you witnessed.
* **Attestor Statement** (`attestor_statement`, text, required): Confirm that you were present at the location and have nothing to hide.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your acceptance of the attestations document.
* **Confirm Attestation** (`confirm_attestation`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to confirm your attestations.
