# Email Migration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request the migration of your email account from one service provider to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with your information and details.
* Make sure to select the correct "current provider" and "new provider" from the provided options.
* If you are requesting the email migration for a specific reason, provide a brief description of the reason.
* Review your information carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Migration Request**: This is the main description of why you want to migrate your email account.
* **Current Provider**: Select the service provider that you are currently using.
* **New Provider**: Select the service provider you would like to move your email account to.
* **Migration Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start the email migration.
* **New Email**: This will be the email address of the new email account that you will be using after migration.
* **Reason for Migration**: Briefly describe the reason for the email migration request.
* **Current Email**: This will be the email address you are currently using that you would like to migrate.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information regarding the email migration request, you may enter them here.

**Tips**
* Make sure to select the correct options for "current provider" and "new provider" to ensure that your email account is migrated to the correct service provider.
* If you are unsure of the email migration process or have any questions, do not hesitate to contact your internal support team for assistance.
