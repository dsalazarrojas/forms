# Vehicle Interior Cleaning Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or users about their vehicle interior cleaning requests. It is used to gather details about the vehicle, appointment scheduling, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Vehicle Details**: Enter a brief description of your vehicle, including its make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
2. **Appointment Details**: Provide your desired appointment date and time for the vehicle interior cleaning service.
3. **Contact Details**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
4. **Service Preferences**: If you have any specific preferences for the cleaning service, such as a preferred cleaning method or any additional services you'd like to include, please let us know in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Details** (vehicle_details): A text field where you can enter a description of your vehicle, including its make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
* **Appointment Details** (appointment_details): A text field for scheduling your appointment for the vehicle interior cleaning service. Please enter your desired date and time for the service.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details): A text field where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Service Preferences** (preferences): A text field where you can specify any special requests or preferences for the cleaning service, such as a preferred cleaning method or additional services you'd like to include.
