# Artwork Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients requesting custom artwork. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the artwork meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) next to the label.
2. For fields that have options, select the one that best fits your needs.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing information, especially for the project description, artwork type, and dimensions or format required.
4. If you are unsure about a field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Client Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you represent a company or organization, enter the name here.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the project you are requesting artwork for.
* **Detailed Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Describe the artwork you need, including the purpose, size, and any specific requirements.
* **Purpose of Artwork** (`project_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of the artwork (e.g., Marketing Material, Website, Publication, etc.).
* **Type of Artwork Needed** (`artwork_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of artwork you need (e.g., Illustration, Logo, Banner, etc.).
* **Preferred Art Style or Aesthetic** (`style_preference`, `text`, optional): Describe the style or aesthetic you prefer for the artwork.
* **Color Palette or Preferences** (`color_palette`, `text`, optional): Provide any color preferences or palettes you have.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Describe who the artwork will be seen by.
* **Dimensions or Format Required** (`dimensions_or_format`, `text`, required): Provide the specifications for the artwork.
* **Reference Materials or Inspiration** (`reference_materials`, `text`, optional): Provide any reference materials or inspiration for the artwork.
* **Copyright Ownership** (`copyright_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Select the copyright ownership you prefer (e.g., Client Owns All Rights, Artist Retains Some Rights, etc.).
* **Number of Revision Rounds Included** (`revision_rounds`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of revisions included in the budget.
* **Estimated Budget** (`estimated_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Project Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required): Enter the project deadline.
* **Final File Format Needed** (`file_format_needed`, `text`, optional): Specify the final file format required.
* **Additional Notes or Requirements** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or requirements for the project.
* **Authority to Approve Final Artwork** (`approval_authority`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and title of the person authorized to approve the final artwork.
