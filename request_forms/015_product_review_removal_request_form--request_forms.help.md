# Product Review Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Review Removal Request Form is designed to facilitate the removal of a product review that a customer feels is incorrect, outdated, or in violation of community guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1.  In the "Product URL" field, enter the URL of the product review you would like to have removed.
2.  In the "Reason for Request" field, select the reason for your request. This could be "Removed Product" or "Other". If you choose "Other", please provide a description in the text box.
3.  In the "Evidence Upload" field, you can upload supporting evidence that helps to justify your request. This can be a screenshot, document, or any other relevant attachment.
4.  In the "Comments" field, add any additional comments or information that will help support your request.
5.  In the "Form Data" field, please enter any additional relevant information that is not covered in other fields.
6.  In the "Email" field, enter your email address to contact you for further discussion.
7.  In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number for additional verification.
8.  In the "Product Name" field, enter the product name relevant to the review you are requesting to be removed. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Review Removal Form** (`Review Removal Form`,  text,  optional): This is the form where you can enter your request for product review removal. Please provide a brief description of your request.
*  **Product URL** (`Product URL`,  text,  optional): Enter the URL of the product review you would like to remove.
*  **Reason for Request** (`Reason for Request`,  select_one,  required): Select the reason for your request from "Removed Product" or "Other".
*  **Evidence Upload** (`Evidence Upload`,  text,  optional): Upload supporting evidence that justifies your request. 
*  **Comments** (`Comments`,  text,  optional): Add any additional comments or information to support your request.
*  **Form Data** (`Form Data`,  text,  optional): Enter any additional relevant information that is not covered in other fields.
*  **Email** (`Email`,  email,  optional): Enter your email address to contact you for further discussion.
*  **Phone** (`Phone`,  text,  optional): Enter your phone number for additional verification.
*  **Product Name** (`Product Name`,  text,  optional): Enter the product name relevant to the review you are requesting to be removed.
