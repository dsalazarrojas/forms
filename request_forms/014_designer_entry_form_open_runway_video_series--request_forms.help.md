# Designer Entry Form OPEN Runway Video Series - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer Entry Form is a tool for designers to submit their work for the OPEN Runway Video Series. This form helps curate and categorize entries based on the provided information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in the required fields (marked with **Required**):
    *   **Designer Entry Form OPEN Runway Video Series** (`form_open_runway_video_series`): Enter the title of your work.
    *   **Description** (`form_open_runway_video_series_description`): Enter a brief description of your work.
2.  Fill in the optional fields (marked as not required):
    *   **Category** (`form_open_runway_video_series_category`): Select the category of your work from the provided options.
    *   **Niche** (`form_open_runway_video_series_niche`): Enter a niche or area of focus for your work.
    *   **Episode Structure** (`form_open_runway_video_series_episode_structure`): Enter the structure or format of your episode.
    *   **Collaborations** (`form_open_runway_video_series_collaborations`): Enter any relevant collaborations or partnerships.
    *   **Niche Audiences** (`form_open_runway_video_series_niche_audiences`): Enter the type of audiences targeted by your work. You can select multiple options.
    *   **Younger Audiences** (`form_open_runway_video_series_niche_audiences_1`): Enter details about the younger audiences.
    *   **Lay Audiences** (`form_open_runway_video_series_niche_audiences_2`): Enter details about the lay audiences.
    *   **Managerial Audiences** (`form_open_runway_video_series_niche_audiences_3`): Enter details about the managerial audiences.
    *   **Participate** (`form_open_runway_video_series_participate`): Enter any information about your participation in the project.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show`): Enter details about the mini fashion show.
    *   **Fashion Show Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_fashion_show_wears`): Enter any fashion show wears.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_1`): Enter details about the mini fashion show.
    *   **Casual Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_2`): Enter details about the casual wears.
    *   **Formal Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_3`): Enter details about the formal wears.
    *   **Semi-Formal Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_4`): Enter details about the semi-formal wears.
    *   **Party Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_5`): Enter details about the party wears.
    *   **Occasional Wears** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_6`): Enter details about the occasional wears.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show 1** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_7`): Enter details about the mini fashion show 1.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show 2** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_8`): Enter details about the mini fashion show 2.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show 3** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_9`): Enter details about the mini fashion show 3.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show 4** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_10`): Enter details about the mini fashion show 4.
    *   **Mini Fashion Show 5** (`form_open_runway_video_series_mini_fashion_show_11`): Enter details about the mini fashion show 5.
