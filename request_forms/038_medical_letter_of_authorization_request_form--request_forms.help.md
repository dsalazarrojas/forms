# Medical Letter Of Authorization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients regarding their medical history, treatment plan, and authorization type for medical procedures. This information is necessary for our medical team to prepare medical letters of authorization for external use. Please ensure you only submit this form if you are a patient or a representative authorized to act on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections in order.
2. Enter your patient's name and relevant medical information in the "Patient Information" section.
3. Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" section.
4. Outline the treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" section.
5. Select the type of authorization needed in the "Authorization Type" section.
6. Enter the expiration date for the authorization in the "Expiration Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`medical_letter_of_authorization_request_form_patient_information`, text, required): This field is used to enter the patient's name and other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_letter_of_authorization_request_form_medical_condition`, text, optional): Here, you can describe the patient's current medical condition or condition being treated.
* **Treatment Plan** (`medical_letter_of_authorization_request_form_treatment_plan`, text, optional): In this field, outline the steps or procedures that need to be followed for the patient's treatment.
* **Authorization Type** (`medical_letter_of_authorization_request_form_authorization_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of authorization required (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Expiration Date** (`medical_letter_of_authorization_request_form_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date by which the authorization will expire.

## Tips
- Please make sure to include all necessary and relevant information to ensure accurate preparation of the medical letter of authorization.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our medical team for assistance.
- This form must be completed accurately and truthfully, as the information collected will be used for external purposes.
