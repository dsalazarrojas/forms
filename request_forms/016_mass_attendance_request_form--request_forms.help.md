# Mass Attendance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request attendance at a mass or religious service. Please fill out the form with your information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:
1. Provide your individual name in the "Individual Name" field.
2. If applicable, list any family members who will be attending with you in the "Family Members" field.
3. In the "Reason for Attendance" field, please enter a brief explanation for your request.
4. In the "Contact Details" field, enter any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
5. Choose a date and time for the mass in the "Date of Mass" and "Time of Mass" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, optional): Briefly describe your request.
* **Individual Name** (`individual_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, optional): List any family members who will be attending (e.g. Spouse, Children, Parents).
* **Reason for Attendance** (`reason`, text, optional): Explain why you would like to attend the mass (e.g. Baptism, Wedding, Funeral).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Mass** (`date_of_mass`, date, optional): Choose a date for the mass.
* **Time of Mass** (`time_of_mass`, time, optional): Choose a time for the mass.
