# Customer Loyalty Merge Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Merge Request Form is designed to collect relevant information about the customer to facilitate their loyalty merging process. This form is used by staff members to gather the necessary details for merging multiple customer records into one unified record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the form fields with the customer's information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Loyalty Merge Request Form 1**: A brief description of the customer loyalty merging request.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City**: Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State**: Enter the customer's state or province.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter the customer's zip or postal code.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.

## Tips
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department.
* Please fill out this form only if you are an authorized staff member.
