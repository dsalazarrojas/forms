# UCC Filing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about UCC filings. It is used for new filings, not for filing corrections or updates. If you're seeking to file a UCC-1 or UCC-3 form, you should use a different form. If you're seeking to file a UCC-5 form, this form should be used instead.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Filer Name" field with the name of the person or business filing the UCC.
2. In the "Ucc Filing Type" field, select the type of UCC form you're filing (e.g., UCC1B, UCC3, UCC5).
3. Enter the "Ucc Filing Number" if you have one.
4. Enter the "Filing Date" and "Filing Time" of the UCC filing.
5. Fill out the "Filer Address", "Authorized Person Name", "Authorized Person Title", and "Authorized Person Relationship" fields with the relevant information.
6. Enter the "Authorized Person Phone" and "Authorized Person Email" fields with the contact information for the authorized person.
7. Enter the "Authorized Person Address" field with the address of the authorized person.
8. In the "Ucc Filing Status" field, select the status of the filing (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
9. In the "Review Status" field, select the status of the review (e.g., Review, Pending Review, Approved, Rejected).
10. Fill out the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Email", "Submitter Phone", "Submitter Address", "Submitter Relationship", and "Submitter Title" fields with the relevant information.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
12. Finally, click "Submit" to finish filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Filer Name**: Enter the name of the person or business filing the UCC.
* **Ucc Filing Type**: Select the type of UCC form you're filing (e.g., UCC1B, UCC3, UCC5).
* **Ucc Filing Number**: Enter the UCC filing number if you have one.
* **Filing Date**: Enter the date of the UCC filing.
* **Filing Time**: Enter the time of the UCC filing.
* **Filer Address**: Enter the address of the person or business filing the UCC.
* **Authorized Person Name**: Enter the name of the authorized person for the UCC filing.
* **Authorized Person Title**: Enter the title of the authorized person for the UCC filing.
* **Authorized Person Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the authorized person to the filer.
* **Authorized Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the authorized person.
* **Authorized Person Email**: Enter the email of the authorized person.
* **Authorized Person Address**: Enter the address of the authorized person.
* **Ucc Filing Status**: Select the status of the UCC filing (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Review Status**: Select the status of the review (e.g., Review, Pending Review, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter the address of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter the title of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Company**: Enter the company name of the person or company submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the submitter.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
