# Road Sign Placement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Sign Placement Application Form is used to submit requests for the placement of road signs. This form helps us to gather the necessary information to determine the feasibility and priority of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Location Name" and "Location Details" sections with the specific details about the location where you want the sign to be placed.
2. Choose the type of road sign required from the dropdown menu in the "Road Type" field.
3. Enter the street directions and landmark or reference point where you want the sign to be placed in the "Street Directions" and "Landmark or Reference Point" fields, respectively.
4. Upload a map to help us better understand the location (optional).
5. Fill in the "Requestor Information" and "Submitter Information" sections with your and submitter's details.
6. Add any comments if you want to provide additional context.
7. Finally, sign the form with your submitter's signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details**: Briefly describe the purpose of the request.
* **Road Type**: Select the type of road sign required. Please choose from the following options: Bollard, Guidepost, Traffic Island, or Other.
* **Location Name**: Enter the name of the location where you want the sign to be placed.
* **Location Details**: Enter any additional details about the location, such as the specific spot where you want the sign to be placed.
* **Street Directions**: Enter the street directions to help us better understand the location.
* **Landmark or Reference Point**: Enter a landmark or reference point to help us pinpoint the exact location.
* **Location Map**: Upload a map to help us better understand the location (optional).
* **Requestor Information**: Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or context about your request.
* **Submitter's Signature**: Date and sign the form with your submitter's signature.
* **Submitter's Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter's Name**: Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter's Contact**: Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter's Title**: Enter the submitter's title.
* **Submitter's Email**: Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter's Address**: Enter the submitter's address.
* **Submitter's Organization**: Enter the submitter's organization or company.
* **Submitter's Message**: Provide any additional comments or context about your request.
* **Submitter's Signature**: Date and sign the form with the submitter's signature.
