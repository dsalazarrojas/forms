# fraudulent_transaction_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a review of a potentially fraudulent transaction. It is used to gather information from the review requester about the suspicious activity and the details of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Enter the transaction ID to identify the transaction.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Enter the transaction date.
5. Enter the amount of the transaction.
6. Describe the suspicious activity.
7. Choose the review requester from the dropdown list.
8. Add any supporting documents (if applicable).
9. Enter the submission date and time.
10. (Optional) Enter any additional review notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transaction ID**: Enter the ID of the transaction to be reviewed.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer involved in the transaction.
* **Transaction Date**: Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Suspicious Activity**: Describe the suspicious activity that led to the review request.
* **Supporting Document**: Attach any supporting documentation related to the transaction (e.g. receipts, emails, etc.).
* **Review Requester**: Select the individual or team member who is requesting the review.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the review.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time the review request was submitted.
* **Reviewer Comment**: Enter any additional comments from the reviewer.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach any relevant supporting documents to help the reviewer understand the transaction.
* Be specific and clear in your description of the suspicious activity.
* Choose the correct review requester from the dropdown list to ensure proper escalation.
