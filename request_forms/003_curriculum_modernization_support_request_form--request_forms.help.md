# Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form is used to request support for modernizing a curriculum. This form is intended for users to provide information about their curriculum modernization needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide information about the curriculum you would like to modernize by filling out the `curriculum_info` field.
2. Select the type of support you need by choosing one or more options for the `request_type` field.
3. Fill out your contact information, including your email address in the `email` field, which is a required field.
4. Set the date and time of submission using the `submission_date` and `submission_time` fields.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes in the `submission_comments` field.
6. Finally, select the current status of your submission using the `submission_status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Curriculum Information (curriculum_info)** (`text`, required): Please provide a brief description of the curriculum you would like to modernize.
* **Submission Type (request_type)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Curriculum Review
	+ Curriculum Update
	+ Curriculum Revision
* **Submission Date (submission_date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date on which you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Note (submission_note)** (`note`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so that the support team can contact you.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Submission Status (submission_status)** (`select_one`, required): Select the current status of your submission:
	+ New
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Reopened
* **Submission Comments (submission_comments)** (`text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submission Attachment (submission_attachment)** (`text`, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents to support your submission.
* **Submitter's Name (submitters_name)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter's Department (submitters_department)** (`text`, optional): Enter your department.
