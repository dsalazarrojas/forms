# Door Collection Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Door Collection Request Form is used to request access and management of your door collections.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Input** - This is the first page of the form. Enter any relevant information you have regarding your request.
2. **Form Input** - In this page, provide any additional context or data that is important for your request.
3. **Collection Input** - Enter any details related to the collection you are requesting access to.
4. **Confirm** - Confirm your request, this is your last step, ensure that you have filled out the form accurately.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, optional): Enter your details to get started with the process.
* **Form Input** (`Form Input`, `text`, optional): Provide any extra data that helps your request.
* **Collection Input** (`Collection Input`, `text`, optional): Add more details regarding your door collection.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `text`, optional): Make sure your information is correct before you submit your request.
