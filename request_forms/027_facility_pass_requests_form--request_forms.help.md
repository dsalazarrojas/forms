# Facility Pass Requests Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facility Pass Requests Form is a tool for employees to request access to a facility or restricted area. This form allows you to provide necessary information to support your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Facility Pass Requests" tab to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the "Contact Information" field from the dropdown menu, choosing the relevant contact for your request.
3. Enter your desired "Request Date" in the date field.
4. Choose the start time for your request in the "Start Time" field.
5. Select the employees you would like to grant access to, from the "Employees" field.
6. Provide a brief reason for your request in the "Reason" field.
7. Enter your name and any additional details in the "Requester" field.
8. Specify the location of the requested access in the "Location" field.
9. Add any additional comments or description of your request in the "Description" field.
10. If needed, select "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate your request status.
11. Add any additional comments if your request has been approved or denied.
12. Finally, select the status of your request from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Pass Requests** (`1`): This field is the main heading for the form, indicating that you are making a request for facility pass.
* **Contact Information** (`2`): Select the contact information of the person requesting the facility pass. This is likely the person submitting the form.
* **Request Date** (`3`): Enter the date you would like to request access to the facility.
* **Start Time** (`4`): Choose the start time of your requested facility access.
* **Employees** (`5`): Select the employees you would like to grant access to.
* **Reason** (`6`): Provide a brief explanation of why you need to access the facility.
* **Requester** (`7`): Enter your name and any additional details about your request.
* **Location** (`8`): Specify the location of the facility you are requesting access to.
* **Description** (`9`): Add any additional comments or description of your request.
* **Approval** (`10`): Select "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate your approval status of the request.
* **Reject** (`11`): If your request has been denied, select "Reject" to confirm denial. Otherwise, this field is not necessary.
* **Comments** (`12`): Add any additional comments if your request has been approved or denied.
* **Status** (`13`): Select the status of your request from the dropdown menu.
* **Facility Pass Comments 2** (`14`): Add any additional comments related to your facility pass request.
* **Facility Pass Requester 2** (`15`): Enter your name and any additional details about your request.
* **Requesting** (`16`): Enter your name and any additional details about your request.
* **Facility Pass Location 2** (`17`): Specify the location of the facility you are requesting access to.
* **Facility Pass Status 2** (`18`): Select the status of your request from the dropdown menu.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Review and double-check your information before submitting your request.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your facility manager or the person who submitted this form.
