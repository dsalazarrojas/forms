# ร ก กาย ท ร รง Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ร ก กาย ท ร รง Request Form" is a form designed to collect information from patients and healthcare professionals for requesting medical services. This form is intended to streamline the process of requesting and managing medical services, ensuring accurate and efficient communication between patients, healthcare providers, and administrative staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Name** in the first field. This is a required field, please ensure you enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Select a **Request Date** from the calendar dropdown. This field allows you to choose the date when you are requesting medical services.
3. Choose your **Patient Gender** from the dropdown menu. This will help us identify your demographic information.
4. Select the **Request Type** that best describes your medical service needs. You can choose from "Gait Trainer," "Balance Trainer," or "Other" depending on your specific requirements.
5. If you have any medical **Medical Specialty**, please select from our options. This will help us understand your specific needs.
6. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes. This is an optional field, but we recommend entering your email for efficient communication.
7. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes. This is an optional field, but we recommend entering your phone number for urgent communication.
8. Enter your **Address** where you can be contacted or visited. This is an optional field, but we recommend entering your address for efficient communication.
9. Enter the **Contact Person** who will be responsible for coordinating your appointment. This is an optional field.
10. Enter the **Contact Number** of the contact person. This is an optional field.
11. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date field.
12. Enter your **Nationality** information, if applicable.
13. Select your **Blood Group** from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your medical requirements.
14. Enter your **Religion** information, if applicable.
15. Enter your **Occupation**.
16. Enter your **Emergency Contact** information. This is an optional field.
17. Enter the **Emergency Contact Number** of the emergency contact person. This is an optional field.
18. Enter the **Emergency Contact Address** of the emergency contact person. This is an optional field.
19. Enter your **National ID** number, if applicable.
20. Enter the name of the **Doctor** who will be attending to your medical needs.
21. Select the **Doctor Specialty** of the attending doctor. This will help us understand their expertise.
22. Enter the **Doctor Contact Number** of the doctor. This is an optional field.
23. Enter the **Doctor Address** of the doctor. This is an optional field.
24. Enter the name of the **Hospital** where you will be visiting.
25. Enter the **Hospital Address** where you will be visiting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Request Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date when you are requesting medical services.
* **Patient Gender** (Select One, Optional): Choose your gender to help us understand your demographic information.
* **Request Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select from "Gait Trainer," "Balance Trainer," or "Other" depending on your specific medical requirements.
* **Medical Specialty** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select from our options to help us understand your specific medical needs.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for urgent communication.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address for efficient communication.
* **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's name who will be responsible for coordinating your appointment.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Nationality** (Text, Optional): Enter your nationality information.
* **Blood Group** (Select One, Optional): Select your blood group from the dropdown menu.
* **Religion** (Text, Optional): Enter your religion information.
* **Occupation** (Text, Optional): Enter your occupation.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact person's address.
* **National ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your national ID number.
* **Doctor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the doctor's name who will be attending to your medical needs.
* **Doctor Specialty** (Select One, Optional): Select the doctor's specialty from our options.
* **Doctor Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the doctor's address.
* **Hospital Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the hospital name where you will be visiting.
* **Hospital Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the hospital address where you will be visiting.
