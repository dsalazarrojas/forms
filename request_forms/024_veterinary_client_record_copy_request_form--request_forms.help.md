# Veterinary Client Record Copy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Client Record Copy Request Form is used to request a copy of a client's medical record from a veterinary clinic or hospital. This form is used to collect the necessary information from the client or their representative to facilitate the sharing of the medical record.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client whose medical record you are requesting.
2. **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number, if available.
3. **Email**: Enter the client's email address, if available.
4. **Medical Record Number**: Enter the medical record number of the client (if known).
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth.
6. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who is requesting the record copy.
7. **Request Type**: Select the type of request being made (Pet, Medical Record, Other).
8. **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting the record copy.
9. **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information you would like to provide in your request.
10. **Request Type**: Select all types of requests you are making, using the dropdown options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: The name of the client whose medical record is being requested.
* **Phone**: The client's phone number, if available.
* **Email**: The client's email address, if available.
* **Medical Record Number**: The medical record number of the client (if known).
* **Date of Birth**: The client's date of birth.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person who is requesting the record copy.
* **Request Type**: Select all relevant types of requests you are making, such as Pet or Medical Record.
* **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting the record copy.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information you would like to provide in your request.
* **Reason for Request**: The reason for requesting the record copy.
 
## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much information as possible to facilitate the request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the client or their representative for clarification.
* The client must have given their consent for the record copy to be shared before submitting this form.
