# Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reimbursement Request Form is designed to gather information about work-related expenses that need to be reimbursed. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit a clear and concise request for reimbursement, reducing the chance of errors and speeding up the processing time.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the Transaction Form
* **Transaction**: This field is used to describe the reason for your reimbursement request. Provide a brief description of the transaction, including the date, location, and any relevant details.

### Step 2: Upload Receipts
* **Receipts**: Attach copies of your receipts for the transactions listed above. This will help the reimbursement team quickly verify your request. 

### Step 3: Review and Submit
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out the form and attached all necessary receipts, review your information carefully before submitting. Double-check that all required fields are filled out and that your receipts are attached before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transaction**: This field is used to describe the reason for your reimbursement request. This could be a business lunch, travel expense, or any other work-related expense that requires reimbursement. Please provide a brief description of the transaction, including the date, location, and any relevant details.
* **Receipts**: Attach copies of your receipts for the transactions listed above. This will help the reimbursement team quickly verify your request.
* **Submit**: This button will submit your reimbursement request for processing. Please review your information carefully before clicking this button.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach all necessary receipts to support your reimbursement request.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact your supervisor for clarification.
* If you have already submitted a reimbursement request for the same expense, do not submit another form. Contact your supervisor instead.
