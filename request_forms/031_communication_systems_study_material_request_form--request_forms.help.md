# Communication Systems Study Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to request study materials for their Communication Systems course. The form collects information about the student's request, including the type and quantity of materials needed, and any additional comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Make sure to provide the required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
3. Choose the correct options for the select fields, such as the year level and urgency level.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Information** (`id: 1`): A note field where you can provide any additional information about your request.
* **Request Date** (`id: 2`): A date field where you should enter the date when this request is submitted.
* **Request ID** (`id: 3`): A unique ID assigned to this request, used for tracking purposes.
* **Student Information** (`id: 4`): A note field where you can provide additional personal details.
* **Student Name** (`id: 5`): Enter your full name.
* **Student ID** (`id: 6`): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Email** (`id: 7`): Enter your email address.
* **Program Course** (`id: 8`): Enter the name of your program or course of study.
* **Year Level** (`id: 9`): Choose your current year of study from the available options.
* **Course Information** (`id: 10`): A note field where you can provide additional details about the course.
* **Course Code** (`id: 11`): Enter the course code or number.
* **Course Name** (`id: 12`): Enter the full course name.
* **Instructor Name** (`id: 13`): Enter the name of your course instructor.
* **Semester Term** (`id: 14`): Choose the current semester or term from the available options.
* **Material Request** (`id: 15`): A note field where you can provide details about the materials you need.
* **Material Type** (`id: 16`): Choose the type of materials you need from the available options.
* **Specific Materials** (`id: 17`): List the specific materials you need.
* **Request Reason** (`id: 18`): Choose the reason why you need these materials from the available options.
* **Urgency Level** (`id: 19`): Choose the urgency level of your request from the available options.
* **Format Preference** (`id: 20`): Choose your preferred format for the materials.
* **Previous Access** (`id: 21`): Choose whether you have accessed these materials before.
* **Access Barriers** (`id: 22`): If you have accessed the materials before, provide any barriers that prevented you from accessing them.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 23`): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have about your request.
* **Delivery Method** (`id: 24`): Choose how you would like to receive the materials.
* **Acknowledgment** (`id: 25`): Confirm that you understand that your request will be processed within 5 business days.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and make sure to submit it completely before sending it in.
