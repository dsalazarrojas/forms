# Mocion Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about meeting submissions, including meeting dates, times, locations, and details about the proposed motion. The form is intended to help proposers submit their ideas and proposals for review and discussion within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
2. For the "Proposers" field, select all relevant individuals who are proposing or supporting the motion.
3. For the "Proposed By" field, indicate whether the motion is proposed by the submitter or not.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Submitter Info", "Submitter Name", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" fields.
5. Provide a detailed description of the motion in the "Motion Description" field.
6. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (1)**: The date of the meeting where the motion will be discussed.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us know when the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Time (2)**: The time of the meeting where the motion will be discussed.
	+ Like the meeting date, this field is optional but helps us know when the meeting starts and ends.
* **Meeting Location (3)**: The location of the meeting where the motion will be discussed.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us know where the meeting will take place.
* **Proposers (4)**: The individuals proposing or supporting the motion.
	+ This field is a list of options, choose all relevant individuals.
* **Proposers Name (5)**: The name of the individual proposing or supporting the motion.
	+ This field is optional and is intended to provide more details about the proposers.
* **Proposers Email (6)**: The email address of the individual proposing or supporting the motion.
	+ This field is optional and is intended to provide more details about the proposers.
* **Motion Number (7)**: A unique number assigned to the motion.
	+ This field is optional and is used for internal tracking purposes.
* **Motion Subject (8)**: A brief summary of the motion.
	+ This field is optional and provides a brief overview of the motion.
* **Motion Description (9)**: A detailed description of the motion.
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand the proposal in more detail.
* **Proposed By (10)**: Indicate whether the motion is proposed by the submitter or not.
	+ Choose "Yes" if the motion is proposed by the submitter, otherwise choose "No".
* **Submitter Info (11)**: Additional information about the submitter.
	+ This field is optional and provides more context about the individual submitting the motion.
* **Submitter Name (12)**: The name of the individual submitting the motion.
	+ This field is optional and provides more details about the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (13)**: The email address of the individual submitting the motion.
	+ This field is optional and is intended to provide more details about the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (14)**: The phone number of the individual submitting the motion.
	+ This field is optional and provides more details about the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments (15)**: Additional comments or notes from the submitter.
	+ This field is optional and provides more details about the submitter's thoughts on the motion.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general overview of the form and its fields. Please refer to your organization's policies and procedures for specific guidelines and requirements.
