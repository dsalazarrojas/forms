# data_compliance_certification_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting requests for compliance certifications. Compliance officers or personnel use it to request reviews from the compliance team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the submitter on page 1.
2. Select or provide the relevant supporting documents on page 2.
3. Grant consent to review on page 3.
4. Choose the IT support team that will assist with the certification on page 4.
5. Provide the desired review completion date and time on page 5.
6. Select the review completion time frame (e.g., within 1 day, 1 week, 2 weeks) on page 6.
7. Choose the review completion time (morning, afternoon, evening) on page 7.
8. Enter the tool assigned to the reviewer on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submitter (submitter)** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select or upload supporting documents relevant to your certification request. These documents may include agreements, policies, or relevant records.
- **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): Grant your consent for the review process and certification.
- **Compliance Team** (`compliance_team`, `text`, optional): You can select or enter the compliance team that will handle your request.
- **IT Support** (`it_support`, `select_one`, required): Choose the IT support team (Compliance and Risk, Data Protection and Security, or IT and Network) that will assist with the certification.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your certification request.
- **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your certification request.
- **Review Completion Date** (`review_completion_date`, `select_one`, required): Select the time frame within which the review should be completed (e.g., within 1 day, 1 week, 2 weeks).
- **Review Completion Time** (`review_completion_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the review completion time (morning, afternoon, evening).
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to the reviewer for the certification review.

## Tips
- Be thorough when providing supporting documents to ensure a smooth review process.
- Clearly grant your consent to initiate the review process.
- Select the most suitable IT support team for the certification review.
- Ensure the assigned tool is suitable for the certification review.
- Coordinate with the assigned tool and compliance team to set and meet the review completion time frame.
