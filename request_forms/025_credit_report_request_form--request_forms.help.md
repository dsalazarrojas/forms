# credit_report_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a credit report from a user, allowing them to select the type and frequency of the report they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information (Page 1).
2. Select the type of credit report you require from the "Report Type" dropdown menu (Page 2).
3. Choose the frequency of the report you require from the "Report Frequency" dropdown menu (Page 3).
4. Enter any additional information about your request in the "Request Details" field.
5. Enter the date you wish to request the credit report from the "Request Date" field.
6. Select the person who will be requesting the credit report from the "Requested By" dropdown menu.
7. Optionally, enter your credit score if applicable.
8. Enter any credit history information you wish to include.
9. Enter any contact information if you wish to be contacted about the report.
10. Enter a contact name, phone number, and email address if you wish to be contacted by that person.
11. Enter a reason for requesting the credit report if applicable.
12. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Page 1): Enter your personal information, including any relevant details about yourself.
* **Credit Report** (Page 2): Select the type of credit report you require, such as Credit Report, Credit Inquiry Report, or Credit Verification Report.
* **Request Details** (Page 3): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Request Date** (Page 4): Enter the date you wish to request the credit report.
* **Requested By** (Page 5): Select the person who will be requesting the credit report.
* **Credit Score** (Page 6): Enter your credit score, if applicable.
* **Credit History** (Page 7): Enter any credit history information you wish to include.
* **Report Type** (Page 8): Select the type of report you require.
* **Report Frequency** (Page 9): Select the frequency of the report you require.
* **Contact Information** (Page 10): Enter any contact information if you wish to be contacted about the report.
* **Contact Name** (Page 11): Enter the name of the person who will be contacting you.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 12): Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacting you.
* **Contact Email** (Page 13): Enter the email address of the person who will be contacting you.
* **Request Reason** (Page 14): Enter a reason for requesting the credit report, if applicable.
* **Notes** (Page 15): Enter any additional notes about your request.
* **Submit** (Page 16): Review and submit your completed form.
* **Cancel** (Page 17): Cancel your request and start over.
* **Save and Preview** (Page 18): Save your progress and preview your completed form.
* **Delete** (Page 19): Delete your saved form.
* **Saved** (Page 20): View your saved form.
* **Updated** (Page 21): View the date your form was last updated.
* **Created** (Page 22): View the date your form was created.
* **Deleted** (Page 23): View the date your form was deleted.
* **User ID** (Page 24): View the ID of the user who created your form.
