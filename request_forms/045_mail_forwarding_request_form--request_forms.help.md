# Mail Forwarding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a mail forwarding request for various reasons such as forwarding mail to a different address, another office, or an email account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your sender's address in the "Sender Address" field.
2. Next, enter the postal address of the sender in the "Sender Postal Address" field.
3. Enter the recipient's address in the "Recipient Address" field.
4. Provide the postal address of the recipient in the "Recipient Postal Address" field.
5. Select the type of forwarding you require from the "Forwarding Type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your name in the "Requestor Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Requestor Email" field.
8. Provide the reason for your request in the "Reason for Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mail Forwarding Request** (`request_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your mail forwarding request, including the reason for the request.
* **Sender Address** (`sender_address`, text, optional): Enter the sender's physical address (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **Sender Postal Address** (`sender_postal_address`, text, optional): Enter the sender's postal address (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
* **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you would like the mail to be forwarded (e.g., 456 Elm St, Anytown, USA).
* **Recipient Postal Address** (`recipient_postal_address`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's postal address (e.g., 456 Elm St, Anytown, USA 12345).
* **Forwarding Type** (`forwarding_request_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from the following options:
	+ Mail to a different address
	+ Forward to another office
	+ Mail to an email account
	+ Other (please describe)
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name associated with the request.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your mail forwarding request.
