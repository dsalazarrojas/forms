# Gym Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Maintenance Request Form is used to gather information for maintenance requests at the gym. This helps the maintenance team to prioritize and address the issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Describe the issue you are experiencing in the "Maintenance Request" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue in the "Description" field.
3. Select the type of issue you are experiencing in the "Issue Type" field. Options include "Equipment", "Facility", "Other", and "Services".
4. Choose the priority of the issue in the "Priority" field. Options include "High", "Low", and "Medium".
5. Assign the issue to a staff member in the "Assigned To" field. You can choose "chatjimmy" or "other" if the issue is not assigned to a specific staff member.
6. Update the status of the issue in the "Status" field. Options include "Open", "In Progress", and "Closed".
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the issue in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter the name of the facility in the "Facility Name" field.
9. Enter the location of the facility in the "Facility Location" field.
10. Enter the type of facility in the "Facility Type" field.
11. Enter the person who submitted the request in the "Submitted By" field.
12. Enter the date the request was submitted in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Request** (`maintenance_request`, text, required): This is the text field where you can describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the issue.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the type of issue, such as "Equipment", "Facility", "Other", and "Services".
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Choose the priority of the issue, such as "High", "Low", and "Medium".
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the staff member to whom the issue is assigned, such as "chatjimmy" or "other".
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Update the status of the issue, such as "Open", "In Progress", and "Closed".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, text, required): Enter the type of facility.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the request was submitted.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to help the maintenance team to resolve the issue promptly.
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully, as the information will be used to track and manage maintenance requests.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please ask someone from the maintenance team for assistance.
