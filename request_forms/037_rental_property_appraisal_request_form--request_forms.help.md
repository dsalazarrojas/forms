# rental_property_appraisal_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental property appraisal request form is a tool used by appraisers to gather information from requestors about a rental property. This form serves as a standard template for requesting appraisals, ensuring that all relevant details are captured consistently across different appraisals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information:
   - Submitter: Enter your name.
   - Contact Name: Enter your name.
   - Contact Phone: Enter your phone number.
   - Contact Email: Enter your email address.
2. Enter the property information:
   - Property Address: Enter the address of the rental property.
   - Property Type: Enter the type of property (e.g., single-family home, duplex, apartment complex, etc.).
   - Property Use: Enter the intended use of the property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
   - Property Status: Enter the current status of the property (e.g., vacant, occupied, etc.).
   - Property Location: Enter the location of the property.
   - Property Size: Enter the size of the property in square feet or acres.
   - Property Year Built: Enter the year the property was built.
   - Property Condition: Enter the current condition of the property.
3. Enter the appraiser's information:
   - Appraiser Name: Enter the name of the appraiser.
   - Appraiser Phone: Enter the appraiser's phone number.
   - Appraiser Firm: Enter the name of the appraiser firm.
   - Appraiser License: Enter the appraiser's license number.
   - Appraiser License State: Enter the state where the appraiser is licensed.
4. Provide the request details:
   - Request Date: Enter the date you would like to request the appraisal.
   - Request Time: Enter the time you would like to request the appraisal.
   - Request Reason: Enter the reason for the appraisal.
   - Requestor Comments: Enter any additional comments or requests.
   - Requestor Signature: Sign and date the form to confirm your request.
   - Appraiser Signature: Enter the appraiser's signature and date.
