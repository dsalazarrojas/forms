# Urban Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Urban Leave Request Form is used to submit a leave request to your manager and team leader for approval. It captures details about the leave, including start and end dates, reason for leave, and submitter's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields respectively.
2. Provide a brief reason for your leave in the **Reason** field.
3. Enter your **Employee ID**, **Manager ID**, and **Team Leader ID** if applicable.
4. Select whether you are a team member in the **Team Members** field.
5. Enter your **Submitter's Email** and **Submitter's Phone** if applicable.
6. Add any additional notes in the **Submitter's Note** and **Approver's Note** fields if required.
7. Select the **Approval Status** as 'Approved' or 'Rejected'.
8. Choose the **Approved By** field as 'Manager', 'Team Leader', or 'HR'.
9. Select the leave to be submitted to as 'HR Manager', 'Team Leader', or 'Manager'.
10. Select the submitter as 'John', 'Alice', or 'Bob'.
11. Enter the **Manager Name**, **Team Name**, and **Team Location**.
12. Enter your **Submitter Name**, **Submitter Job Title**, and **Manager Email**, **Team Leaders Email**, and **Manager Job Title** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (Required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (Required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (Required): Provide a brief reason for your leave.
* **Employee ID** (Required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Manager ID** (Required): Enter your manager's ID.
* **Team Leader ID** (Optional): Enter your team leader's ID if applicable.
* **Team Members** (Optional): Select whether you are a team member.
* **Submitter's Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter's Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter's Note** (Optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Approver's Note** (Optional): Add any additional notes for the approver.
* **Approval Status** (Required): Select whether the leave is approved or rejected.
* **Approved By** (Optional): Select the person who approves the leave.
* **Submitted To** (Optional): Select the leave to be submitted to.
* **Submitter** (Required): Select the person submitting the leave.
* **Manager Name** (Required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Team Name** (Required): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Location** (Required): Enter your team's location.
* **Submitter Name** (Required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Job Title** (Required): Enter your job title.
* **Manager Email** (Required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Team Leaders Email** (Required): Enter your team leader's email address.
* **Manager Job Title** (Optional): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Team Leaders Job Title** (Optional): Enter your team leader's job title.
