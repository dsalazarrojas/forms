# Hairdressers Appointment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hairdressers Appointment Request Form is used to request a hairdresser's appointment. This form helps us understand your service requirements, contact details, and appointment preferences so we can tailor our service to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please indicate whether or not you require a service by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Select Service" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the services you require from the list in the "Services" field.
6. Choose whether you want to book an appointment with a specific stylist by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Stylists" field.
7. Select the appointment date from the calendar in the "Appointment Date" field.
8. Choose the time of your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select Service**: This field is used to indicate whether or not you require a service. Select "Yes" if you need a service, or "No" if you don't.
- **Name**: This field is where you can enter your name.
- **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
- **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address. Please ensure you enter a valid email address.
- **Services**: This field is used to select the services you require. Choose as many services as you need from the list.
- **Stylists**: This field is used to choose whether or not you want to book an appointment with a specific stylist. Select "Yes" if you want to book with a specific stylist, or "No" if you don't mind who you're booked with.
- **Appointment Date**: This field is used to select the date of your appointment. Choose a date from the calendar provided.
- **Appointment Time**: This field is used to choose the time of your appointment. Select a time that suits you best.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide your correct contact information so we can get in touch with you.
- Select the services you truly require to ensure we provide the best service for you.
- Choose a date and time that suits you best, taking into account our available slots.
