## Agent Feature Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from agents regarding their feature requests and applications. The form is used to centralize the intake of product innovation and feature requests, allowing the product team to effectively track and prioritize these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the applicant.
2. Specify the team or department associated with your feature request.
3. Enter a valid email address for status updates.
4. Provide a brief title for your proposed feature.
5. Select the impact level of your feature (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
6. Describe the problem your feature solves.
7. Detail the functionality of your proposed feature.
8. Specify the platforms affected by your feature (e.g., Web, Mobile, API, Desktop).
9. Estimate the number of users who will benefit from your feature.
10. Choose the development priority for your feature (Top Priority, High, Medium, or Low).
11. Provide links to supporting research or examples (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the applicant.
* **Team or Department** (`team_name`, text, required): Specify the team or department associated with your feature request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for status updates.
* **Proposed Feature Name** (`feature_name`, text, required): Provide a brief title for your proposed feature.
* **Impact Level** (`impact_level`, select_one, required): Choose the impact level of your feature (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
* **Problem this feature solves** (`problem_statement`, text, required): Describe the problem your feature addresses.
* **Detailed description of feature functionality** (`feature_description`, text, required): Describe how your feature should work.
* **Platforms affected** (`affected_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Specify the platforms affected by your feature (e.g., Web, Mobile, API, Desktop).
* **Estimated number of users benefited** (`user_benefit_count`, number, optional): Estimate the number of users who will benefit from your feature.
* **Priority for development** (`development_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the development priority for your feature (Top Priority, High, Medium, or Low).
* **Links to supporting research or examples** (`supporting_research`, text, optional): Provide links to supporting research or examples (e.g., URLs to documentation or competitor examples).

## Tips

* Please keep your responses clear and concise.
* Use proper format and grammar.
* Attach relevant attachments or links as supporting evidence.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.
