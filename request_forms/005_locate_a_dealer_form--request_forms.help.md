# Locate A Dealer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about locating a dealer, either for establishing contact or for other business-related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select whether you are trying to **Locate A Dealer** or not by choosing an option from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the **Location** of the dealer in the text field.
3. (Optional) Select which **Dealers** you are interested in contacting (if any).
4. (Optional) Enter the **Phone** number of the dealer.
5. (Optional) Enter the **E-mail** of the dealer.
6. (Optional) Add any additional **Notes** about the dealer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Locate A Dealer** (`locate_a_dealer_form`, select_one, required: false): This field is a dropdown menu that asks whether you are trying to locate a dealer or not. You can choose either "Yes" or "No".
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: true): This field is a text field where you need to enter the location of the dealer.
* **Dealer** (`dealer_id`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or more dealers that you are interested in contacting.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is a text field for entering the phone number of the dealer.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is an email field for entering the email of the dealer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is a free-text field for adding any additional comments or information about the dealer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the required field "Location" to ensure that the form is complete.
* Use the "Locate A Dealer" field to filter dealers if you are looking for a specific one.
* The "Dealer" field is optional, but it can help you narrow down your search results.
* The "Phone" and "E-mail" fields are not required, but they can provide additional information for the dealer.
