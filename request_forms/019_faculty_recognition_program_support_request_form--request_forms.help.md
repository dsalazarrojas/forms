## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in your faculty department
2. Enter your faculty name
3. Provide your email address
4. Enter your phone number
5. Specify your faculty position
6. Provide the reason for your request
7. Give details about your request
8. Enter your name as the submitter
9. Provide your email address as the submitter
10. Enter your phone number as the submitter
11. Confirm if you have the department head's contact details
12. Select your department chair
13. Select your college
14. Confirm your faculty's college affiliation
15. Select whether your department has multiple faculty members
16. List the faculty members in your department (if applicable)
17. Enter your department's name as the submitter
18. Enter your department's email address
19. Enter your department's phone number
20. Enter your department's mailing address
21. Enter your department's fax number
22. Enter your department's website URL

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Faculty Department** (`faculty_department`, text, required): Enter your faculty department (e.g., Mathematics, Computer Science, etc.)
* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, text, required): Enter your faculty name as it should be displayed in the Faculty Recognition Program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Faculty Position** (`faculty_position`, text, required): Specify your position in your department (e.g., Professor, Chair, etc.).
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your support request.
* **Support Details** (`support_details`, text, required): Provide additional details about your request.
* **Submitter's Name** (`submitters_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitters_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitters_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Department Head Contact Information** (`department_head`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have the department head's contact details.
* **Department Chair** (`chair`, select_one, required): Select your department chair.
* **College** (`college`, select_one, required): Select your college.
* **Faculty's College Affiliation** (`faculty_college`, select_one, required): Confirm your faculty's college affiliation.
* **Multiple Faculty Members** (`faculty_members`, text, required): If you have multiple faculty members, list them here.
* **Submitter's Department** (`submitters_department`, text, required): Enter your department's name as the submitter.
* **Department Email** (`department_email`, email, required): Enter your department's email address.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, text, required): Enter your department's phone number.
* **Department Address** (`department_address`, text, required): Enter your department's mailing address.
* **Department Fax** (`department_fax`, text, required): Enter your department's fax number.
* **Department Website** (`department_website`, text, required): Enter your department's website URL.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, email, required): Enter your department head's email address.
* **Department Head Phone** (`department_head_phone`, text, required): Enter your department head's phone number.
