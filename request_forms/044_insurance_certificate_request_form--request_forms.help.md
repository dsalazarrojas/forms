# Insurance Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users for insurance certificate requests. Please use this form to request insurance certificates for life, health, or property insurance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information as prompted.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the insurance company that issued the policy. Please enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Certificate Type** (`certificate_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the type of insurance certificate being requested. Please choose from the options: Life Insurance, Health Insurance, or Property Insurance.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the policy number of the insurance policy.
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Date of Issue** (`date_of_issue`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when the insurance policy was issued.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when the insurance certificate was issued.
* **Certificate Status** (`certificate_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the current status of the insurance certificate. Please choose from the options: Approved, Pending, or Denied.
* **Insurance Certificate ID** (`insurance_certificate_id`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the unique identifier of the insurance certificate.
* **Insurance Certificate Date** (`insurance_certificate_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when the insurance certificate was issued.
