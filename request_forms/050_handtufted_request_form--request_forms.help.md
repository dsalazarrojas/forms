# Handtufted Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handtufted Request Form is used to collect information from customers who require a custom hand-tufted item. It is designed to ensure that customers provide all necessary details for the requested hand-tufted item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* `email`: Enter your email address.
	* `phone`: Enter your phone number.
	* `flatweave`: Select whether you want your hand-tufted item flatweaved or not.
2. You may also add additional notes in the `notes` field if you want to provide more information.
3. Once you have filled out all required fields, click submit to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **email (Email, required)**: This is your email address. Please enter the email address you used to register on our website or the email address your company uses for official communications.
* **phone (Phone, required)**: This is your phone number. Please enter your phone number so we can reach you to discuss your request.
* **flatweave (Flatweave, required)**: Select whether you want your hand-tufted item flatweaved or not. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **notes (Notes, optional)**: This is an optional field where you can provide additional information about your request.
  If you have any specific requirements, preferences, or concerns, you can mention them here.
  Please keep your notes brief and concise for easy review.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your hand-tufted item request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible to discuss your hand-tufted item request.
