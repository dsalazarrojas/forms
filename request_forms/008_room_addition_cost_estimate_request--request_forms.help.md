# Room Addition Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you provide essential details about your room addition project. Please fill out the form accurately to help us understand your project's specifics and provide you with a comprehensive cost estimate.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting "Project Type" to specify if your project is a "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand if your project requires our expertise.
2.  Describe your project in the "Project Details" field, providing any relevant information about your project.
3.  Select the "Project Size" options that best fit your project's size and scope.
4.  Choose the "Room Location" where your project will take place.
5.  Select the "Room Size" options that suit your project's needs.
6.  Enter your project's budget and estimated cost, if known.
7.  Provide your project's status: "Active" or "Inactive".
8.  Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, so we can get in touch with you.
9.  Set your project's deadline and start date.
10. Enter your project's address.
11. Select the "Project Manager" and "Project Contractor" options that apply to your project.
12. Add any additional information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Type** (Select One): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your project requires our expertise.
*   **Project Details** (Text): Enter a brief description of your project.
*   **Project Size** (Select Multiple): Select the size options that fit your project, such as "Small," "Medium," or "Large."
*   **Room Location** (Select One): Choose where your project will take place, such as "Yes" or "No."
*   **Room Size** (Select Multiple): Select the size options for your room, like "Yes" or "No."
*   **Project Budget** (Number): Enter your project's budget.
*   **Estimated Cost** (Number): Provide your estimated project cost.
*   **Project Status** (Select One): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your project's status.
*   **Contact Info** (Note): Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
*   **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
*   **Project Deadline** (Date): Set your project's deadline.
*   **Project Start Date** (Date): Enter your project's start date.
*   **Project End Date** (Date): Enter your project's end date.
*   **Project Address** (Text): Enter your project's address.
*   **Project Manager** (Select One): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a project manager.
*   **Project Contractor** (Select One): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a project contractor.
*   **Additional Info** (Text): Enter any additional information about your project.
