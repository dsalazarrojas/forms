# Material Recovery Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Recovery Fund Application Form is designed to collect information for material recovery fund applications. This form is meant to be completed by individuals or representatives of organizations seeking funding for specific projects or initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and email address in the first name field and email field.
2. Enter your phone number and address in the phone number field and address field.
3. Specify the amount of funding you are requesting in the Requested Amount field.
4. Provide a brief description of the project or initiative you are seeking funding for in the Description field.
5. Explain the reason you are requesting funding in the Reason for Request field.
6. If necessary, indicate the source of your funding request in the Funding Source field.
7. Identify the person or entity you would like to be the contact person in case we need to reach out to you in the Contact Person field.
8. Enter the name of the organization you are representing in the Organization field, if applicable.
9. Select the funding agency you would like to receive funding from in the Funding Agency field.
10. List the invoices you would like to be covered by the funding in the Invoices field, if applicable.
11. Choose the source of your funding in the Funding Source field.
12. Add any additional information or notes in the Note field.
13. Click the Submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Required: False): Enter your address.
* **Requested Amount** (Number, Required: False): Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Description** (Text, Required: False): Provide a brief description of the project or initiative you are seeking funding for.
* **Reason for Request** (Text, Required: False): Explain the reason you are requesting funding.
* **Funding Source** (Text, Required: False): If necessary, indicate the source of your funding request.
* **Contact Person** (Text, Required: False): Identify the person or entity you would like to be the contact person in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Organization** (Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the organization you are representing, if applicable.
* **Date Submitted** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Time Submitted** (Time, Required: False): Enter the time you submitted this application.
* **Funding Agency** (Select One, Required: False): Select the funding agency you would like to receive funding from.
* **Invoices** (Select Multiple, Required: False): List the invoices you would like to be covered by the funding, if applicable.
* **Funding Source** (Select One, Required: False): Choose the source of your funding.
* **Note** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional information or notes.
* **Submit** (Text, Required: False): Click the Submit button to submit your application.

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly, and that you have a valid reason for requesting funding.
