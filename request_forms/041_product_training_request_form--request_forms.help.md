# product_training_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **product training request form** is a tool used to gather information from users who require training on a specific product. This form is designed to collect relevant details about the user, including their name, company name, training date, training time, and duration of the training. The collected information will be used to provide relevant training materials and support to the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Company Name** in the second field. This is an optional field.
3. Select a **Training Date** for the requested training. This field accepts date values in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
4. Choose a **Training Time** for the requested training. This field accepts time values in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
5. Enter the **Duration** of the training in numeric values.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last names in this field.
### Company Name (`Company Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
### Training Information
* **Training Date** (`Training Date`, `date`, required): Select the date on which you would like to receive the training.
* **Training Time** (`Training Time`, `time`, optional): Choose the time slot you would prefer for the training.
* **Duration** (`Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the number of minutes you expect to dedicate to the training.

## Tips
- Double-check that your information is accurate before submitting the form.
- Make sure to select the correct date and time for your training session.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to include them in the form's additional remarks (not provided in this help guide).
