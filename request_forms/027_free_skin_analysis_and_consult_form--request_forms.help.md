# Free Skin Analysis And Consult Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users who would like to request a free skin analysis and consult.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the User Input field with your information.
* Select your skin type from the Skin Type dropdown menu.
* Check any skin problems you are experiencing.
* If you have a proposed solution, enter it in the Solution field.
* Enter your email address in the Email Input field.
* Enter your phone number in the Phone Input field.
* Choose a date for the analysis or consult in the Date Input field.
* Choose a time for the analysis or consult in the Time Input field.
* Enter any additional notes about your analysis or consult in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, Number, Optional): Please enter your name and any relevant information about yourself.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose your skin type from the dropdown menu.
* **Skin Problems** (`skin_problems`, Select Multiple, Optional): Check any skin problems you are experiencing.
* **Solution** (`solution`, Text, Optional): If you have a proposed solution, enter it here.
* **Email Input** (`email_input`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Input** (`phone_input`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Input** (`date_input`, Date, Optional): Choose a date for the analysis or consult.
* **Time Input** (`time_input`, Time, Optional): Choose a time for the analysis or consult.
* **Note** (`note_input`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your analysis or consult.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Required): Press to submit your request for a free skin analysis and consult.
