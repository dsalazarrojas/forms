# Procurement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for procurement requests, allowing users to submit their needs and preferences for purchasing goods or services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself or your organization.
2. Describe the procurement details in as much detail as possible.
3. Select the type of procurement request you are submitting (if applicable).
4. Choose the relevant contact information (if applicable).
