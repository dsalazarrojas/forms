# Family Member Proxy Transfer Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a family member proxy transfer. A proxy transfer is when a user wants to transfer the ownership of a family member to another person in their care or authority. This form is used to gather necessary information for the proxy transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* User Name: Enter the name of the user requesting the proxy transfer.
	* First Name: Enter the first name of the family member to be transferred.
	* Last Name: Enter the last name of the family member to be transferred.
	* Email: Enter the email address of the family member to be transferred.
	* Transfer Date: Enter the date when the transfer is scheduled to take place.
	* Transfer Time: Enter the time when the transfer is scheduled to take place.
	* Authorization Letter: Enter a brief explanation of the reasons for the transfer.
	* Supporting Documents: Attach any relevant documents that support the transfer request.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as *required*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter the name of the user requesting the proxy transfer.
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the family member to be transferred.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the family member to be transferred.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the family member to be transferred. (Note: The email will be used to contact the family member if needed.)
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date when the transfer is scheduled to take place.
* **Transfer Time**: Enter the time when the transfer is scheduled to take place.
* **Authorization Letter**: Enter a brief explanation of the reasons for the transfer.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents that support the transfer request. (Note: This can include letters of authorization from the family member, medical documents, or other supporting evidence.)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about the transfer request. (This field is optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
* Attach any supporting documents that can help facilitate the transfer process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
