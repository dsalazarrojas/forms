# Hybrid Battery Maintenance Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Battery Maintenance Cost Estimate Request form is used to collect information about a hybrid vehicle's battery maintenance needs for internal use only. It is designed to be completed by authorized personnel to estimate the cost of battery maintenance and repair for a hybrid vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information about the vehicle, including its VIN, make, model, year, and mileage.
2. Select the correct type and battery type of the vehicle.
3. Indicate if the service requested is needed.
4. Provide a cost estimate and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submitted By**: This field is not required but it will help us to keep track of who submitted this form.
* **Name**: Please enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **VIN**: Please enter the Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you are submitting this form for (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Battery Type**: Select the type of battery used in the vehicle (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Make**: Please enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Model**: Please enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Year**: Please enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Trim Level**: Please enter the trim level of the vehicle.
* **Mileage**: Please enter the mileage of the vehicle.
* **Service Requested**: Select the service requested (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Cost Estimate**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to estimate the cost of the service.
* **Notes**: Please provide any additional notes about the service.
* **Service Center**: Please enter the service center where the service will be performed.
* **Submitter**: Select if you are the submitter (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Created By**: This field is not required but it will help us to keep track of who created this form.
* **Submitted By**: This field is not required but it will help us to keep track of who submitted this form.
* **Created**: This field is not required and will automatically populate the date and time this form was created.
* **Submitted**: This field is not required and will automatically populate the date this form was submitted.
* **Updated**: This field is not required and will automatically populate the date this form was last updated.
* **Updated By**: This field is not required but it will help us to keep track of who last updated this form.
* **Submitted At**: This field is not required and will automatically populate the date this form was submitted.
* **Updated At**: This field is not required and will automatically populate the date this form was last updated.
