# Dossier Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dossier Request Form is a document used to submit a formal request for a dossier. This form gathers information from the submitter and allows them to request a dossier for specific purposes such as new hire, transfer, or separation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Dossier Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Request Type.
2. Provide your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the Request Type from the options provided (New Hire, Transfer, or Separation).
4. Enter your Date of Request and Reason for Request.
5. Add any additional Request Details if necessary.
6. Fill out the Submitter information: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Relationship, and Signature.
7. Finally, add any additional Notes or Comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (First Page): This is the first page of the form where you can add any additional notes or comments.
* **First Name** (First Name, Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Email): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text): Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Death** (Date of Death, Date): Enter the date of death (if applicable).
* **File** (File, Text): Enter any additional file information.
* **Note** (Note, Note): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Request Type** (Request Type, Select One): Select the reason for the request (New Hire, Transfer, or Separation).
* **Submitter** (Submitter, Text): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Department** (Department, Text): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, Text): Enter your job title.
* **Request Date** (Request Date, Date): Enter the date of the request.
* **Reason for Request** (Reason for Request, Text): Enter the reason for the request.
* **Request Details** (Request Details, Text): Enter any additional details about the request.
* **Submitter Email** (Submitter Email, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Submitter Phone, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Relationship** (Submitter Relationship, Text): Enter your relationship to the person requesting the dossier (e.g., Manager, HR Representative).
* **Submitter Message** (Submitter Message, Text): Enter a message or explanation for the request (optional).
* **Submitter Notes** (Submitter Notes, Text): Enter any additional notes about the request (optional).
* **Submitter Comments** (Submitter Comments, Text): Enter any comments or feedback about the request (optional).
* **Submitter's Signature** (Submitter's Signature, Text): Sign your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter's Name** (Submitter's Name, Text): Enter your name.
* **Submitter's Company** (Submitter's Company, Text): Enter your company name.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
