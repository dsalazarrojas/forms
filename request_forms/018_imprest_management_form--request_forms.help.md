# Imprest Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Imprest Management Form is used to manage and track Imprest accounts. This form is a tool for accounting and financial management purposes, and is used to record and update information about Imprest accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Imprest Account number in the **Imprest Account** field.
2. Select the **Account Manager** responsible for the account.
3. Choose the **Account Type** from the drop-down menu (Cash or Non-Cash).
4. Enter the current **Account Balance**.
5. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** for the account period.
6. Enter a brief **Imprest Description** of the account.
7. Choose the **Account Status** (Active or Inactive).
8. Enter the **Accounting Code** and **Account Code** for the account.
9. Select the **Financial Department** responsible for the account (Cash Management, Accounts Payable, or Accounting).
10. Enter the **Department**, **Branch**, and **Contact Person** details.
11. Enter the **Phone** and **Email** contact information.
12. Enter the **Location** and **Address** details.
13. Add any **Notes** about the account.
