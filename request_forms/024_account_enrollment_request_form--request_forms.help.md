# Account Enrollment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information to enroll you or your organization in our account system. It helps us determine your account type, service plan, and preferred start date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Choose your preferred account type and service plan from the available options.
3. Select your preferred start date for the account.
4. Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
5. Optionally, provide any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name is required to be filled in the format "Last Name, First Name".
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" or "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Provide your full street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Account Type Requested** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from one of the following options:
	+ Individual
	+ Business
	+ Joint
	+ Student
	+ Non-profit
* **Service Plan or Tier** (`service_plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose from one of the following options:
	+ Basic
	+ Standard
	+ Premium
	+ Enterprise
* **Company or Organization Name (if applicable)** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a business or organization, enter the name here.
* **Tax ID or EIN (for business accounts)** (`tax_id`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a business, enter your tax ID or EIN here.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from one of the following options:
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Referral from a friend
	+ Advertisement
	+ Trade show or event
	+ Other
* **Preferred Account Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter your preferred start date for the account.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes, I agree".
* **I Acknowledge the Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes, I acknowledge".
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or special requests you may have.
