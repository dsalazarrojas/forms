# product_demo_and_freebie_sample_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request form for product demos and freebies. You can use it to request a demo of a product and potentially receive a freebie.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your request details in the "Request Details" field.
2. Select the product you are interested in from the "Product Name" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type of product you are interested in from the "Product Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the contact person's name and information in the "Contact Person" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Choose a demo date from the "Demo Date" field.
8. Choose a demo time from the "Demo Time" field.
9. Select any additional sample requests you have from the "Sample Requests" dropdown menu.
10. If applicable, enter any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
11. Choose how you'd like to receive your demo request confirmation from the "Request Confirmation" dropdown menu.
12. Finally, enter any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
13. Click on the "Form Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your product demo request.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Select the name of the product you are interested in.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of product you are interested in.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's name and information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Demo Date** (`demo_date`, date, required: false): Choose a demo date.
* **Demo Time** (`demo_time`, time, required: false): Choose a demo time.
* **Sample Requests** (`sample_requests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional sample requests you have.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required: false): If applicable, enter any additional information.
* **Request Confirmation** (`request_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to receive your demo request confirmation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required: false): Click on this button to submit your form.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can submit the form without filling out any of the fields. However, please ensure that you have filled out the required fields accurately to increase your chances of receiving a demo and a freebie. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to contact us.
