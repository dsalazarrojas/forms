# CNA Shift Restoration Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CNA Shift Restoration Request form is designed to help you, as a Certified Nursing Assistant, request restoration of your shifts due to various reasons such as scheduling errors, system errors, or emergency coverages. Please ensure that you are completing this form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help us understand your request better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, employee ID, email address, phone number, and workplace.
2. Provide your supervisor's name and the original shift date and time.
3. Select the type and reason for your restoration request.
4. Describe what happened and provide any supporting documentation if necessary.
5. Identify who can verify your request and if you've had any previous communication about this issue.
6. Specify the urgency level and number of hours to be restored.
7. Confirm if there's a pay implication and specify your preferred resolution.
8. Add any additional comments, if necessary, and indicate the date of your request.
9. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **CNA Name** (1): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Employee ID** (2): Enter your staff ID number as assigned to you by your workplace.
* **Email Address** (3): Provide your work or personal email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your best contact number where you can be reached.
* **Facility Name** (5): Enter the name of your workplace.
* **Unit or Department** (6): Enter the unit or department you're assigned to.
* **Supervisor Name** (7): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Original Shift Date** (8): Enter the date of the original shift in question.
* **Original Shift Time** (9): Enter the scheduled shift hours.
* **Type of Restoration Request** (10):
	+ **Shift Reinstatement**: Request to reinstate a previously cancelled shift.
	+ **Hours Restoration**: Request to restore hours that were lost due to various reasons.
	+ **Pay Correction**: Request to correct any pay-related issues.
	+ **Schedule Correction**: Request to correct any scheduling issues.
	+ **Other**: Specify any other reason not listed above.
* **Reason for Request** (11):
	+ **Scheduling Error**: Request due to scheduling errors.
	+ **System Error**: Request due to system errors.
	+ **Emergency Coverage**: Request due to emergency coverage.
	+ **Approved Leave Reversal**: Request to reverse approved leave.
	+ **Administrative Error**: Request due to administrative errors.
	+ **Other**: Specify any other reason not listed above.
* **Detailed Explanation** (12): Describe what happened, including any supporting documentation.
* **Supporting Documentation** (13): List any attachments that can support your request.
* **Witness or Verifier** (14): Identify who can verify your request.
* **Previous Communication** (15):
	+ **Yes with Supervisor**: Yes, you've discussed this with your supervisor.
	+ **Yes with HR**: Yes, you've discussed this with HR.
	+ **Yes with Scheduling**: Yes, you've discussed this with the scheduling team.
	+ **No this is first contact**: You haven't discussed this with anyone yet.
* **Urgency Level** (16):
	+ **Critical - Payroll Affected**: Urgent, as it affects your pay.
	+ **High - Schedule Impact**: Urgent, as it affects your schedule.
	+ **Medium - Planning Purpose**: Medium urgency, as it affects planning.
	+ **Low - For Records**: Low urgency, for record-keeping purposes.
* **Number of Hours** (17): Enter the number of hours to be restored.
* **Pay Impact** (18):
	+ **Yes underpaid**: You've been underpaid for the shift.
	+ **Yes overpaid**: You've been overpaid for the shift.
	+ **No pay impact**: No pay implication.
	+ **Unsure**: You're unsure about the pay implication.
* **Preferred Resolution** (19): Specify the outcome you're looking for.
* **Deadline for Resolution** (20): Enter the date by which you'd like the issue resolved.
* **Additional Comments** (21): Add any other relevant information.
* **Request Date** (22): Date and time of your request.
* **Employee Signature** (23): Sign your name as a confirmation of your request.

Please ensure that you've filled out all required fields accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your request better. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative.
