# Script Adaptation Permission Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Script Adaptation Permission Request form is used to request permission to adapt a script into another medium, such as a stage production or a screen adaptation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the title of the script and the name of the producer.
2. Choose the type of adaptation you are requesting, such as "adaptation for screen" or "adaptation for stage".
3. Select the dates for the request and review of the adaptation.
4. Choose the status of the script, such as "draft" or "registered".
5. Enter the email and phone number of the producer.
6. Choose the status of the adaptation, such as "approved", "pending", or "denied".
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about the adaptation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Script Adaptation Permission Request** (`script_adaptation_permission_request`): Enter a brief description of the adaptation request.
* **Title** (`title`): Enter the title of the script you are adapting.
* **Producer Name** (`producer_name`): Enter the name of the producer of the script.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`): Enter the name of the production company behind the script.
* **Script** (`script`): Enter a brief description of the script you are adapting.
* **Script ID** (`script_id`): Enter the ID of the script you are adapting. This should be a unique number assigned to the script.
* **Adaptation Type** (`adaptation_type`): Choose the type of adaptation you are requesting, such as "adaptation for screen" or "adaptation for stage".
* **Adaptation For** (`adaptation_for`): Choose multiple options for the type of adaptation you are requesting, such as "adaptation for screen" and "adaptation for stage".
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are requesting permission for the adaptation.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date you expect the review of the adaptation to be completed.
* **Script Status** (`script_status`): Choose the status of the script, such as "draft" or "registered".
* **Producer Email** (`producer_email`): Enter the email of the producer of the script.
* **Producer Phone** (`producer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the producer of the script.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter the name of the production company behind the script.
* **Adaptation Status** (`adaptation_status`): Choose the status of the adaptation, such as "approved", "pending", or "denied".
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the review of the adaptation.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the adaptation.
* **Adaptation Notes** (`adaptation_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the adaptation.
* **Adaptation Terms** (`adaptation_terms`): Enter any additional terms about the adaptation.
* **Adaptation Rights** (`adaptation_rights`): Enter any additional rights about the adaptation.
* **Script Adaptations** (`script_adaptations`): Enter any additional adaptations of the script.
* **Production Status** (`production_status`): Enter the current status of the production.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the production.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`): Choose whether an agreement has been signed with the producer.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (`agreement_signed_date`): Enter the date the agreement was signed.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Make sure to choose the correct adaptation type and status for your script.
* If you have any additional questions, please contact the production team.
