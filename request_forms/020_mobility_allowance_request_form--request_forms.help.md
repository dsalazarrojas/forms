Mobility Allowance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mobility Allowance Request Form is used to request assistance with mobility-related needs, such as wheelchair or scooter rentals, for employees who require support with mobility. This form helps ensure that requests are properly documented and addressed by the relevant department.

Before submitting this form, please note that:

*   You should only submit this form if you have a legitimate mobility-related need. 
*   If you've already submitted a form for the same purpose, please do not submit another.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3.  Enter your work email in the `Email` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5.  Select the type of mobility assistance you need in the `Mobility Type` field.
6.  Specify the frequency of your need in the `Frequency` field.
7.  Describe your reason for requesting mobility assistance in the `Reason for Request` field.
8.  Add any additional comments or information in the `Additional Comments` field.
9.  Enter the date of your request in the `Date Requested` field.
10. Enter the time of your request in the `Time Requested` field.
11. Specify the location of your need in the `Location` field.
12. If an assigned tool is required, enter the name of the tool in the `Assigned Tool` field.
13. Finally, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your work phone number for communication and coordination.
*   **Mobility Type** (Select one, Optional): Choose the type of mobility assistance you require, such as a wheelchair or a scooter.
*   **Frequency** (Select multiple, Optional): Specify how often you need mobility assistance, such as daily or weekly.
*   **Reason for Request** (Text, Optional): Explain your need for mobility assistance, including any medical conditions or limitations.
*   **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your request.
*   **Date Requested** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your request for mobility assistance.
*   **Time Requested** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your request for mobility assistance.
*   **Location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where you need mobility assistance.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): If an assigned tool is required, enter its name.
*   **Submitted On** (Date, Optional): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

Please review your form submission for accuracy and completeness before clicking the submit button.
