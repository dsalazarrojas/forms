# Food Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request food or non-food donations from individuals or organizations to be delivered to one of the following locations:
* Food Bank
* Shelter
* Pantry

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of donation you would like to make (Food or Non-Food).
2. Enter your name and contact number to enable the organization to contact you for further details.
3. Specify the location where you would like the donation to be delivered.
4. Enter the quantity of items you would like to donate.
5. Choose the type of donation you are offering (Canned Goods, Fresh Fruits and Vegetables, or Baked Goods).
6. Provide a date and time when the donation will be available for collection.
7. Briefly describe the donation to help us understand what you are offering.
8. If applicable, provide an email address to facilitate further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food or Non-Food**: Select the type of donation you would like to make (Food or Non-Food).
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with two options: Food or Non-Food.
* **Name**: Enter your name to allow the organization to contact you with further details.
	+ This field is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for the organization to get in touch with you.
	+ This field is a text field for your contact number.
* **Location**: Specify where you would like the donation to be delivered.
	+ Select one or multiple locations from the dropdown menu (Food Bank, Shelter, or Pantry).
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of items you would like to donate.
	+ This field is a number field where you can enter the quantity.
* **Type of Donation**: Choose the type of donation you are offering.
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with three options (Canned Goods, Fresh Fruits and Vegetables, or Baked Goods).
* **Available Date**: Enter the date when the donation will be available for collection.
	+ This field is a date field where you can select a date.
* **Available Time**: Enter the time when the donation will be available for collection.
	+ This field is a time field where you can select a time.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the donation to help us understand what you are offering.
	+ This is a text field where you can write a short description.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, enter it to facilitate further communication.
	+ This field is a text field for your email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your request.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the donation to help us understand what you are offering.
