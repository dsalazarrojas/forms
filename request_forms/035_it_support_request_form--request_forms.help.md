# IT Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their IT support requests so that the IT department can assist with troubleshooting and resolving technical issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, and enter any additional details as required.
2. Answer all required fields to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internet Connection** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Yes, No. This field helps the IT department understand whether you are experiencing internet connectivity issues.
* **Operating System** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Good, Fair. This field helps the IT department understand the performance of your operating system.
* **Problem Details** (Text Field)
	+ A text field where you can enter a detailed description of your issue.
* **Internet Speed** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Yes, No. This field helps the IT department understand whether you are experiencing slow internet speeds.
* **Contact Info** (Email Field)
	+ An email field where you can enter your contact information so that the IT department can reach out to you for further assistance.
* **Phone Number** (Text Field)
	+ A text field where you can enter your phone number in case the IT department needs to contact you directly.
* **Problem Type** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Yes, No. This field helps the IT department understand the type of issue you are experiencing.
* **Problem Priority** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Yes, No. This field helps the IT department understand the urgency of your issue.
* **Assigned To** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Yes, No. This field helps the IT department understand who is responsible for resolving your issue.
* **Problem Description** (Text Field)
	+ A text field where you can enter a detailed description of your issue.
* **Problem Resolution** (Text Field)
	+ A text field where you can enter any additional information about how your issue was resolved.
* **Time Spent** (Number Field)
	+ A numerical field where you can enter the time spent on resolving your issue.
* **Comments** (Text Field)
	+ A text field where you can add any additional comments about your issue.
* **Problem Status** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown menu with two options: Active, Inactive. This field helps the IT department understand the current status of your issue.
* **Form ID** (Number Field)
	+ A numerical field that is automatically generated and not relevant for users to fill in.
