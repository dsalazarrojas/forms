# Funeral Home Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Home Access Request Form is designed to request access to a funeral home for a specific date and time. This form is used to collect information from users who need to visit or attend a service at the funeral home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your information in the required fields.
2. Fill in your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide the date and time you need access to the funeral home.
4. Submit the form to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Funeral Home Access Request** (`funeral_home_access_request_form`): A brief description of the purpose of your visit.
	+ Please provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting access to the funeral home.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): 
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): 
	+ Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`): 
	+ Select the date you need access to the funeral home.
* **Time** (`time`): 
	+ Select the time you need access to the funeral home.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is for requesting access to the funeral home, not for booking or reserving a service.
* If you need to book or reserve a service, please contact the funeral home directly.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact the funeral home staff.
* If you have already submitted a request, please do not resubmit.
