# Visa Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a visa quotation from our international travel agency. Please provide necessary travel and customer information to help us process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your travel details.
2. Select your travel agency from the options provided.
3. Enter your departure and arrival dates.
4. Provide your contact information for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to describe your travel plans, including the destination, duration, and any other relevant details. Be as detailed as possible to help us provide an accurate visa quotation.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all applicable travel agencies that are relevant to your travel plans.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to depart from your country of origin.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to arrive at your destination country.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required: false): If you are working with a travel agent, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for visa quotation updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for further information.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please include them in the `Travel Details` field.
* If you are not sure what information is required for your visa quotation, please contact our travel agency for assistance.
