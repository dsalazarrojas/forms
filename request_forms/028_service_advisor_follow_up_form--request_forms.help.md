# Service Advisor Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Advisor Follow Up Form is used to gather feedback and insights from customers after a service visit or interaction. This form helps the service advisor to understand the customer's satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in a clear and concise manner.
2. Choose the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or insights you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ServiceAdvisorFollowUpForm** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form`, text, not required): This field is for any general comments you may have about your experience.
* **How did it go?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, select_one, not required): This question asks how satisfied you were with the service you received. Please select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
* **How did it go?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments you may have about your experience.
* **Will you use us again?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, select_multiple, not required): This question asks if you would use our services again. Please select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What could we have done better?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, text, not required): This field is for any suggestions you may have on how we could improve our service.
* **Comments** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, text, not required): This field is for any additional comments or insights you may have.
* **Contact Number** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, text, not required): This field is for your contact number, which will help us to follow up with you.
* **Email** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_question`, email, not required): This field is for your email address, which will help us to follow up with you.

## Tips
- Please be honest and clear in your feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know so we can address them.
- If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please share them with us.
