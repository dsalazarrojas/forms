# Government Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Records Request Form is designed to collect information for government record requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Upload any necessary supporting documents (if applicable).
3. Review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Government Agency** (2): Select the relevant government agency related to your request.
* **Request Date** (3): Enter the date of your request.
* **Description** (4): Provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Contact Email** (5): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (6): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **File Upload** (7): Upload any supporting documents related to your request.
* **Submit Request** (8): Confirm that you have submitted your request.
* **Confirm Submission** (9): Confirm your submission status.
* **Confirm Submitted By** (10): Confirm who submitted the request.
* **Confirm Submitted At** (11): Confirm the date and time of submission.
* **Confirm Submission Date** (12): Confirm the submission date.
* **Confirm Submission Time** (13): Confirm the submission time.
* **Confirm Submitted By Name** (14): Confirm the name of the submitter.
* **Confirm Submitted At Time** (15): Confirm the time of submission.
* **Submitter** (16): Select the submitter from the available options.
* **Confirm Submission Date Field** (17): Confirm the date of submission (text field).
* **Confirm Submission Time Field** (18): Confirm the time of submission (time field).
* **Confirm Submitted By Name Field** (19): Confirm the name of the submitter (text field).
* **Confirm Submitted At Field** (20): Confirm the time of submission (select multiple options).
* **Confirm Submitted At Time Field** (21): Confirm the time of submission (select multiple options).
* **Submitted By** (22): Select the person who submitted the request.
* **Confirm Submission Status** (23): Confirm the status of the submission.
* **Confirm Submission Notes** (24): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
