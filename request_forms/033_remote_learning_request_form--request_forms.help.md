# Remote Learning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request remote learning arrangements for students. Fill out the form if you need to access course materials, assignments, or exams remotely due to personal circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details in the fields below. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide a valid reason for your remote learning request.
3. Specify your preferred communication method for the remote learning arrangement.
4. Enter your contact information and your teacher's contact information if necessary.
5. Add any additional comments or questions you have about your remote learning request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you about your remote learning request.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID if you are a student.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
- **School ID** (`school_id`, text, optional): Enter your school ID if you are a student.
- **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Explain why you are requesting remote learning arrangements. Be as specific as possible.
- **Learning Plan** (`learning_plan`, text, optional): Provide details about the course(s) or activity you would like to access remotely.
- **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you prefer to communicate about your remote learning arrangement (Online, Phone, Email, In Person, or Other).
- **Parent E-mail** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your parent's email address if you are not a student (e.g., for parental consent).
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name if you are a student.
- **Teacher E-mail** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your teacher's email address.
- **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's phone number.
- **School E-mail** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
- **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your remote learning request.
- **Remote Learning Request Form Comments** (`remote_learning_request_form_comments`, note, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional comments or information about your remote learning request.
