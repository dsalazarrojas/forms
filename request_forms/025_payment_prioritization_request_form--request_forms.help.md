# Payment Prioritization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for requesting payment prioritization for various payments. Fill out this form to submit your payment requests for review and potential prioritization by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. **Payment Purpose**: Please select a reason for your payment request. If your payment is for a specific department, select their name.
2. **Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount of the payment.
3. **Payment Due Date**: Enter the date when the payment is due.
4. **Vendor Name**: If you are making a payment to a vendor, enter their name.
5. **Department**: Select the department that is responsible for the payment. This will help us understand where the payment is intended for. You can select multiple departments if the payment is related to multiple departments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Purpose** (payment_purpose, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the payment request.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, optional): Enter the exact amount of the payment.
* **Payment Due Date** (payment_due_date, date, optional): Enter the date by which the payment is due.
* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name, text, optional): If this payment is to a vendor, enter their name.
* **Department** (department, select_multiple, optional): Select the department(s) responsible for the payment. This can be multiple departments if the payment is related to multiple departments.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that your form submission is accurate and complete to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any additional information or supporting documentation, please attach it to this form or provide it separately to help us better understand your request.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or our team for guidance.
