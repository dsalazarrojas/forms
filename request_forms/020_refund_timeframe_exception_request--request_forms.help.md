# Refund Timeframe Exception Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a purchase that falls outside of the standard refund timeframe. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your purchase and the reason for requesting an exception to the refund policy.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the guidelines and instructions before filling out the form.
- Gather all required information before starting the form.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by \*\*).
- Use clear and concise language to describe your situation.
- If a field is optional, only provide information if it is applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase details** (`purchase_details`, text, \*): Provide a clear description of the purchase you are requesting a refund for, including the type of product or service, the date of purchase, and any other relevant details.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, \*): Explain the reason why you are requesting an exception to the refund policy. Be as detailed and specific as possible to help the reviewer understand your situation.
* **Refund date** (`refund_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you are requesting the refund for.
* **Purchase date** (`purchase_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you made the purchase.
* **Supporting document** (`supporting_document`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documentation, such as receipts or emails, attach them here.
* **Exception details** (`exception_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the reviewer to understand your situation.
* **Customer info** (`customer_info`, note, optional): If you would like to provide additional information about the customer, such as their contact information or account number, you can include it here.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): If you would like to provide your contact information to be reached out, you can include it here.
