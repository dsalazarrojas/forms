# Vision And Hearing Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about students' vision and hearing screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Please ensure that you are the appropriate person completing this form.
* Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If the student has any medical conditions or concerns, please note it in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name
**Student Name** (1): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
### Teacher Name
**Teacher Name** (2): Enter your name as the teacher responsible for the student.
### Grade
**Grade** (3): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, etc.).
### Comments
**Comments** (4): If there are any additional comments or concerns, please include them here.
### Date of Vision Test
**Date of Vision Test** (5): Enter the date of the vision test.
### Results of Vision Test
**Results of Vision Test** (6): Check "Yes" if the vision test results were positive; check "No" otherwise.
### Date of Hearing Test
**Date of Hearing Test** (7): Enter the date of the hearing test.
### Results of Hearing Test
**Results of Hearing Test** (8): Check "Yes" if the hearing test results were positive; check "No" otherwise.
### Comments 2
**Comments** (9): If there are any additional comments or concerns related to the hearing test, please include them here.
### Nurse's Signature
**Nurse's Signature** (10): Please sign this field with your nurse's signature.
