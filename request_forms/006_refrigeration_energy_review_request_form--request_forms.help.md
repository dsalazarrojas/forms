# Refrigeration Energy Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refrigeration Energy Review Request Form is used to gather information about your refrigeration system, enabling energy consultants to assess its energy efficiency and identify opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information about your refrigeration system.
2. Fill out all the fields with accurate and clear information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your energy consultant for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Refrigeration Energy Review Request** (`refrigeration_energy_review_request`, text, required: false)
This field is the main title of the form, which you should fill out with a brief description of your refrigeration system and the reason for your energy review request.

### 2. **System Information** (`system_information`, text, required: false)
Please provide a detailed description of your refrigeration system, including its configuration, operation, and any recent changes or upgrades.

### 3. **System Records** (`system_records`, text, required: false)
Enter any relevant records or documentation about your refrigeration system, such as maintenance history, energy usage data, or other related information.

### 4. **Assessments** (`assessments`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all assessments that apply to your refrigeration system. Please choose from:
	+ Yes: The system meets the expected standards for energy efficiency.
	+ No: The system does not meet the expected standards for energy efficiency.

### 5. **Assigned Energy Consultant** (`assigned_energy_consultant`, select_one, required: false)
Select the energy consultant assigned to review your refrigeration system. Please choose from:
	+ Yes: You have been assigned an energy consultant.
	+ No: You have not been assigned an energy consultant.

### 6. **Review Requests** (`review_requests`, number, required: false)
Enter the number of review requests you have made for this system in the past year.

### 7. **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date on which you submitted this form.
