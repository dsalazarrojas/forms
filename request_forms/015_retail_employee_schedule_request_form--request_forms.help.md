# Retail Employee Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Employee Schedule Request Form is used to submit a new schedule request or adjust an existing one for retail employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant shifts and stores from the available options.
3. If you need to request a shift adjustment, select "Unavailability" and provide a reason.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes for your manager's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store ID\* (Number)**: Enter the ID of the store where you want to schedule your shift.
* **Employee ID\* (Number)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Shift ID\* (Number)**: Enter the ID of the shift you want to schedule or adjust.
* **Start Date\* (Date)**: Enter the date you want to start your shift.
* **End Date\* (Date)**: Enter the date you want to end your shift.
* **Schedule Request (Text)**: Briefly describe your schedule request, including any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Available Date (Date)**: Enter a date when you are available to work.
* **Shift Status\* (Select One)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Available: You are available to work on this shift.
	+ Unavailable: You are not available to work on this shift. Please provide a reason in the "Reason" field.
	+ Holiday: You want to schedule this shift as a holiday.
* **Reason (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more reasons for your unavailability (if applicable). This will help your manager understand your situation.
* **Shift Status Comments (Text)**: Enter any additional comments about your shift status, if needed.
* **Department\* (Select Multiple)**: Select the department(s) you are working with on this shift.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Notes (Text)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your shift request.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to avoid any confusion or delays in processing your request.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* Double-check your shift status and availability before submitting the form.
