# Battery Donation Drop Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals or groups to schedule a battery drop off at our facility. It helps us to plan and prepare for the collection, ensuring safe and efficient processing. Please complete this form to let us know when and what you plan to bring.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your information, including your name, contact email and phone number.
2. Choose the date and time you plan to drop off your batteries.
3. Select the types of batteries you are donating.
4. Provide an approximate number of batteries and their estimated weight.
5. Describe the condition of your batteries.
6. Let us know how you heard about us and if you need a receipt for your donation.
7. If you're interested, indicate your interest in volunteering with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name, as we'll use it to contact you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Provide your contact email so we can reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Optional): If you're representing a company or group, please enter their name.
* **Drop Off Date** (`drop_off_date`, Date, Required): Choose a date for the drop off. We recommend checking our calendar for availability.
* **Drop Off Time** (`drop_off_time`, Time, Required): Select a time that suits you best.
* **Types of Batteries** (`battery_types`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all types of batteries you're donating (e.g., AA Alkaline, 9V Batteries).
* **Estimated Quantity** (`estimated_quantity`, Number, Required): Approximate number of batteries you'll bring.
* **Estimated Weight** (`estimated_weight`, Number, Optional): If you know the weight, please enter it in pounds.
* **Battery Condition** (`battery_condition`, Select One, Required): Describe the state of your batteries (e.g., New Unused, Used - Good Condition).
* **How Are Batteries Packaged** (`packaging_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the method your batteries are packaged in (e.g., Original Packaging, Plastic Bags).
* **Is This Your First Donation?** (`first_time_donor`, Select One, Required): Answer if this is your first time donating batteries.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, Select One, Optional): Tell us how you found out about us (e.g., Website, Social Media).
* **Do You Need a Receipt?** (`receipt_needed`, Select One, Required): If you want a receipt for tax purposes, select 'True'.
* **Interested in Volunteering?** (`volunteer_interest`, Select One, Optional): If you're interested in helping us, select 'True'.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share with us.
