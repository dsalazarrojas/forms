# Bus Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Request Form is used to collect information from students and parents to determine the need for bus transportation. This form is designed to ensure that all students' transportation requirements are met while maintaining the efficiency and accuracy of the transportation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the correct options for the student's grade level, request type, and transportation schedule.
3. Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful in understanding the student's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`Student Full Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the student's legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, `text`, **required**): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter the student's birth date.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the student's current grade level.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`Parent or Guardian Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`Parent Phone Number`, `text`, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Home Address** (`Home Address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the student's full street address.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city or town where the student lives.
* **Zip Code** (`ZIP Code`, `text`, **required**): Enter the student's zip code.
* **Transportation Need** (`Transportation Need`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any reason for the transportation request.
* **Request Type** (`Request Type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of transportation request.
* **Eligibility Reason** (`Eligibility Reason`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the reason why the student is eligible for transportation.
* **Distance from School** (`Distance from School`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the approximate distance from the student's home to the school.
* **Preferred Pickup Location** (`Preferred Pickup Location`, `text`, **required**): Enter the preferred pickup location for the student.
* **Preferred Drop-off Location** (`Preferred Drop-off Location`, `text`, **required**): Enter the preferred drop-off location for the student.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`Special Needs or Accommodations`, `select_one`, **required**): Select any special needs or accommodations the student requires.
* **Special Needs Description** (`Special Needs Description`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any special needs or accommodations descriptions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any medical conditions the student may have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Transportation Schedule** (`Transportation Schedule`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the transportation schedule.
* **Sibling Information** (`Sibling Information`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any sibling information.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
