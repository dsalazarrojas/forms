# Cosmetics Product Development Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cosmetics Product Development Request Form is designed to collect information from customers or internal teams for the development of new cosmetic products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your product (e.g., skin care, hair care, makeup, etc.).
2. Provide a brief description of your product.
3. Upload or enter the product image (optional).
4. If applicable, provide the alternative text for the product image.
5. Specify the product price (optional).
6. Choose the product color(s) (if applicable).
7. Select the product size (if applicable).
8. Specify the product weight (if applicable).
9. Choose the product packaging type (e.g., tube, bottle, jar, etc.).
10. Provide the launch date for the product (if known).
11. Enter the contact person's name (if applicable).
12. Enter the company name.
13. Enter the company address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your product (e.g., skin care, hair care, makeup, etc.).
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload or enter the product image.
* **Product Image Alt** (`product_image_alt`, text, optional): Provide alternative text for the product image.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Specify the price of your product.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the color(s) of your product.
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your product.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, optional): Specify the weight of your product.
* **Product Packaging** (`product_packaging`, select_one, required): Choose the type of packaging for your product (e.g., tube, bottle, jar, etc.).
* **Product Launch Date** (`product_launch_date`, date, optional): Provide the launch date for your product.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your company.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are not submitting duplicate requests.
* If you have already submitted a request for a similar product, please check your previous submissions for any updates or changes.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure that your product development request is processed accurately.
