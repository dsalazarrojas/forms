# credit_reference_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking to provide a credit reference. The purpose of this form is to gather details from the contact person, including their name and email address, as well as the address of the organization or entity they represent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use the correct format for an email address (e.g., [username]@[domain].com).
3. Type in the address of the organization or entity you represent in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name in this field. This is the name that will be associated with your credit reference.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this format: [username]@[domain].com. This will help us verify your identity and ensure that you are who you claim to be.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Type in the address of the organization or entity you represent. This will help us understand your affiliation with the entity.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and clearance to provide the requested information.
* If a similar form has already been submitted by someone with the same name and email address or organization address, please do not fill out this form again.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
