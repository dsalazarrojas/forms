# stage_mic_replacement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stage_mic_replacement_request_form" is a request form used to submit requests for stage microphone replacement. Fill out the form to initiate the replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Stage Microphone Request" field with a brief description of the issue or reason for replacement.
2. Select the "Urgency Level" that best describes the priority of your request from the dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, attach any relevant photos or images to the "Photo Attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage Microphone Request** (`stage_mic_request`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe the reason for microphone replacement, such as "Stage 3A microphone is not functioning" or "New microphone required for upcoming event".
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, optional): Categorize the priority of your request as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Photo Attachment** (`photo_attachment`, `text`, optional): Attach photos or images that support your request, if necessary, such as a picture of the broken microphone or the event setup.

## Tips
* Ensure that the description in the "Stage Microphone Request" field is clear and concise.
* Select a suitable "Urgency Level" that reflects the actual priority of your request.
* Only attach photos or images when necessary to support your request.
