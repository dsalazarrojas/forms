# Rescheduled Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rescheduled Training Request Form is used to request a rescheduled training event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the employee's information, including their name and contact information.
3. Provide the new training date and time.
4. Enter the manager's name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the reason for rescheduling the training event.
6. Select the preferred method of notification.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes.
8. Read and agree to the submitter's agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Please enter the name of the employee requesting the training change.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date and time of the original training event that needs to be rescheduled.
* **Old Training Date**: Enter the old date and time of the original training event.
* **New Training Date**: Enter the new date and time of the rescheduled training event.
* **Training Time**: Enter the start and end time of the rescheduled training event.
* **Training Duration**: Enter the duration of the rescheduled training event.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location of the rescheduled training event.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who is responsible for the original training event.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager who is responsible for the original training event.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager who is responsible for the original training event.
* **Reason for Change**: Explain the reason for the rescheduling request.
* **Notification Method**: Select the preferred method of notification (Automated, Phone Call, Email, or In App).
* **Notification Method Message**: Enter a message to be included with the notification (optional).
* **Stakeholder Email**: Enter the email address of the stakeholder who should be notified.
* **Stakeholder Phone**: Enter the phone number of the stakeholder who should be notified.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Submitter Agree**: Select "Agree" to confirm that you have filled out the form accurately.
* **Submitter Disagree Comment**: Enter any comments or reasons for not agreeing with the submitted changes.
* **Submitter Disagree Reason**: Enter a reason for not agreeing with the submitted changes (optional).
