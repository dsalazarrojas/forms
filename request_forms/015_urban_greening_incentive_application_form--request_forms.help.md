# Urban Greening Incentive Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Greening Incentive Application Form is designed to collect relevant data from applicants for urban greening projects. This data will be used to review and process the applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide details about the project you are applying for, including the project name, description, budget, and start and end dates.
3. Select the type of funding you are seeking (Grant, Loan, Donation).
4. Indicate the current status of your project (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
6. Enter your applicant information, including name, address, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (1): Enter your contact information to be able to receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Site Address** (2): This is the address of the location where the project is situated.
* **Contact Phone** (3): This is your phone number, where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (4): This is your email address, where you will receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Contact Name** (5): Enter your name for reference and contact purposes.
* **Project Name** (6): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (7): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (8): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Project Budget Currency** (9): Select the currency of your project budget.
* **Project Start Date** (10): Enter the date when your project started.
* **Project End Date** (11): Enter the date when your project is expected to end.
* **Funding Type** (12): Select the type of funding you are seeking (Grant, Loan, Donation).
* **Funding Amount** (13): Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **Project Status** (14): Indicate the current status of your project (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Project Notes** (15): Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Applicant Name** (16): Enter your name.
* **Applicant Address** (17): Enter your address.
* **Applicant Phone** (18): Enter your phone number.
* **Applicant Email** (19): Enter your email address.
* **Application Form Attachment** (20): If you have any supporting documents, attach them here.
* **Supporting Documents** (21): Enter any additional supporting documents you would like to include with your application.
* **Applicant Notes** (22): Add any additional notes or comments about yourself or your application.
* **Reviewer Comments** (23): If you have any comments or questions for the reviewers, enter them here.
* **Review Status** (24): Indicate the status of your application (Approved, Rejected, Not Yet Reviewed).
* **Reviewer Name** (25): Enter the name of the reviewer who will be handling your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We will review your application carefully and contact you if we need more information.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding during the review process.
