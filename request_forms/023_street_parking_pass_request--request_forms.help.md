# Street Parking Pass Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Street Parking Pass Request" form is used for individuals or companies to request permission for parking passes, typically for events, deliveries, or other special occasions where parking spaces are limited. This form collects essential details about the vehicle and its owner for efficient and secure parking management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out each page completely, ensuring you provide all required and optional details.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes or omissions.
4. If you have any issues or concerns, refer to the "Tips" section for additional guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`): This field is used to provide a brief description of the vehicle you are requesting parking for.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): This field is used to provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): This field allows you to select the make of your vehicle from the provided options (Ford, Chevrolet, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Other).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): This field is used to select the model of your vehicle from the provided options (Camaro, Mustang, Prius, Civic, Accord, Other).
* **Plate Number** (`plate_number`): Please enter your vehicle's license plate number in this field.
* **Vehicle Color** (`color`): Enter the color of your vehicle in this field.
* **Parking Location** (`parking_location`): This field is used to provide the exact parking location where you will be parking your vehicle.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Please enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): If you have any additional information about your parking request, please provide it in this field.
* **Parking Date** (`parking_date`): Enter the date you plan to park your vehicle.
* **Parking Time** (`parking_time`): Enter the time you plan to park your vehicle.
* **Street** (`street`): Enter the name of the street where you plan to park your vehicle.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the name of the city where you plan to park your vehicle.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state where you plan to park your vehicle.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of the parking location.
* **Address 1** (`address1`): Enter the address of your parking location (if different from the street address).
* **Address 2** (`address2`): Enter the additional address information (if any).
* **Note** (`note`): This field is used for any additional comments or notes about your parking request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field allows you to select the assigned tool or equipment for your parking request from the provided options (chatjimmy, Other).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): This field is used to store the unique ID of the form submission.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of your parking request.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your parking request.
* **Title** (`title`): Enter a title for your parking request.

## Tips
If you're unsure about any field or have questions about the form, please refer to the "Contact Us" section for support.
