# Book Estimate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your book project requirements and provide you with an accurate estimate for our book-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select the type of service you need for your book project.
3.  Provide details about your book, such as title, author name, and approximate word count.
4.  Let us know about your manuscript status, target audience, and book genre.
5.  Describe your project and any specific requirements you have.
6.  Indicate how flexible you are with the completion deadline and your preferred contact method.
7.  Finally, provide us with your estimated budget range and any additional comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter the email address we will use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for follow-up questions.
* **Company Name** (4): If you're representing a company or organization, enter it here (optional).
* **Type of Service** (5): Select the type of service you require for your book project (e.g., book editing, cover design, etc.).
* **Book Title** (6): Enter the title of your book.
* **Book Author** (7): Enter the author name of your book.
* **Manuscript Status** (8): Indicate the status of your manuscript (not started, first draft, second draft, etc.).
* **Approximate Word Count** (9): Enter the total number of words in your manuscript (if known).
* **Approximate Page Count** (10): If you know, enter the total number of pages in your manuscript.
* **Book Genre** (11): Select the genre of your book (fiction, non-fiction, memoir, etc.).
* **Target Audience** (12): Describe the intended reader of your book.
* **Project Description** (13): Describe your project and goals in detail.
* **Specific Requirements** (14): If you have any specific requirements or preferences, enter them here.
* **Desired Completion Date** (15): Enter the date by which you'd like to see the project completed (if known).
* **Estimated Budget Range** (16): Select the budget range you're expecting for the project.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (17): Indicate how flexible you are with the completion deadline.
* **Have You Published Before** (18): Check if you've published a book before.
* **Manuscript Sample** (19): Indicate if you have a manuscript sample available for us to review.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (20): Describe how you found out about our services.
* **Additional Comments** (21): Share any other information that might help us better understand your project.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (22): Select how you prefer to be contacted by us (email, phone, text, etc.).
* **Request Date** (23): Enter the date on which you're requesting our services.
