# Cleaning Service Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your cleaning service request. It will help us understand your needs and preferences for cleaning services such as residential, deep, move-in/move-out, window, carpet, and office cleaning. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us tailor our service to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure we can provide the best service for your needs.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the necessary information.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`<id: 2>`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`<id: 3>`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`<id: 4>`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where we can contact you for communication about your request.
* **Phone Number** (`<id: 5>`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Type of Cleaning Service Requested** (`<id: 7>`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of cleaning service you need, such as residential, deep, move-in/move-out, window, carpet, or office cleaning.
* **Property Type** (`<id: 8>`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of property you need cleaned, such as single family home, apartment, condo, office space, or commercial building.
* **Property Address** (`<id: 9>`, `text`, required): Enter the full address of the property needing cleaning.
* **Property Size** (`<id: 10>`, `text`, optional): Enter the approximate square footage of the property.
* **Preferred Cleaning Date** (`<id: 12>`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`<id: 13>`, `select_one`, required): Choose a time that suits you best for the cleaning service.
* **Service Frequency** (`<id: 14>`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency of the cleaning service you need, such as one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or as needed.
* **Recurring Start Date** (`<id: 15>`, `date`, optional): If this is a recurring service, enter the start date for the service.
* **Areas to be Cleaned** (`<id: 17>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas that need cleaning, such as kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living/dining areas, hallways, floors, windows, carpets, or all areas.
* **Additional Services Requested** (`<id: 19>`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional cleaning services needed, such as special cleaning tasks, enter them here.
* **Special Instructions or Preferences** (`<id: 20>`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions or preferences for the cleaning service.
* **Allergies or Product Sensitivities** (`<id: 21>`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities to cleaning products, enter them here.
* **How Will the Cleaning Crew Access the Property?** (`<id: 23>`, `select_one`, required): Choose how the cleaning crew will access the property, such as I will be home, hidden key location, keypad/door code, garage door opener, or other arrangement.
* **Detailed Access Instructions** (`<id: 24>`, `text`, optional): If needed for key location or code, enter detailed access instructions here.
* **Pets in the Property** (`<id: 25>`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if there are pets in the property, such as no pets, dogs, cats, or other.
* **Pet Details** (`<id: 26>`, `text`, optional): If you have pets, enter details such as breed, temperament, or special care needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`<id: 28>`, `select_one`, required): Choose how we should contact you, such as phone, email, text message, or either.
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`<id: 29>`, `text`, optional): Enter your preferred time to contact you, such as morning, afternoon, or any time.
* **How Urgent Is Your Request?** (`<id: 31>`, `select_one`, required): Choose how urgent your request is, such as not urgent, somewhat urgent, urgent, or very urgent.
* **Budget Range** (`<id: 32>`, `select_one`, optional): Enter your approximate budget for the cleaning service.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`<id: 33>`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete in your responses to ensure we can provide the best service for your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us.
* Please allow at least 24 hours for us to review your request and provide a response.
