# Fingerprinting Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fingerprinting Quote Request Form is used to collect information for a fingerprinting quote request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your quote request in the "Quote Request" field.
2. Select the schedule preferences that apply to your request (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Provide details about the service you require (e.g., equipment type, technician name).
4. Enter the schedule date and start time for your request.
5. Select the fingerprinting method and location for your service.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone, company, department, and title).
8. Provide any additional comments or additional service details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, `text`, required): A brief description of your quote request.
* **Schedule Preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the schedule preferences that apply to your request (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `note`, optional): Provide details about the service you require (e.g., equipment type).
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the schedule date for your request.
* **Schedule Start Time** (`schedule_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the schedule start time for your request.
* **Schedule End Time** (`schedule_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the schedule end time for your request.
* **Fingerprinting Method** (`fingerprinting_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the fingerprinting method for your service.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location for your service.
* **Technician** (`service_details_1`, `select_one`, optional): Select the technician for your service.
* **Equipment Type** (`service_details_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the equipment type for your service.
* **Additional Equipment** (`service_details_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional equipment for your service.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your title.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Schedule Recurring** (`schedule_recurring`, `select_one`, optional): Select if this is a recurring schedule.
* **Recurring Frequency** (`schedule_recurring_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the recurring frequency.
* **Recurring Start Date** (`recurring_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date for your recurring schedule.
* **Recurring End Date** (`recurring_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date for your recurring schedule.
* **Recurring Time** (`recurring_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the recurring start time for your schedule.
* **Recurring Comment** (`recurring_comment`, `note`, optional): Enter any recurring comments.
* **Location 1** (`location_1`, `text`, optional): Enter location 1 details.
* **Location 2** (`location_2`, `text`, optional): Enter location 2 details.
* **Location 3** (`location_3`, `text`, optional): Enter location 3 details.
* **Service Details 4** (`service_details_4`, `text`, optional): Enter service details 4.
* **Service Details 5** (`service_details_5`, `text`, optional): Enter service details 5.
* **Service Details 6** (`service_details_6`, `text`, optional): Enter service details 6.
* **Service Details 7** (`service_details_7`, `text`, optional): Enter service details 7.
* **Service Details 8** (`service_details_8`, `text`, optional): Enter service details 8.
* **Additional Service Details** (`additional_service_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional service details.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or service details, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" or "Service Details" fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
