# Sustaining Capex Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather project ideas for sustaining capex projects that support the ongoing operation of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project idea in the "Project Ideas" text field on page 1.
2. If you have specific budget numbers in mind, enter them in the "Budget Ideas" number field on page 1.
3. In the "Justification" text field on page 2, explain why your project idea is valuable and worthy of consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Ideas (Project Ideas)** (`project_ideas`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project idea.
* **Budget Ideas (Budget Ideas)** (`budget_ideas`, number, optional): Enter any specific budget numbers you have in mind for your project.
* **Justification (Justification)** (`justification`, text, optional): Explain why your project idea is valuable and worthy of consideration.
