# Faculty Development Session Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Development Session Material Request Form is designed to help faculty members request materials for their development sessions. Please fill out this form to request materials for your professional growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your department from the list of available options in the "Faculty Department" field.
4. Enter the date you are requesting materials for in the "Request Date" field.
5. Describe the material you are requesting in the "Material Description" field.
6. Specify the amount of material you are requesting in the "Material Amount" field.
7. If applicable, enter the cost of the material in the "Material Cost" field.
8. Explain the reason for your request in the "Reason for Request" field.
9. Specify who you would like the materials to be assigned to in the "Assigned to" field.
10. Finally, enter the date you would like to submit the request in the "Submit Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your university account.
* **Faculty Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department from the list of available options.
* **Request Date** (`material_request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting materials for.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, required): Describe the material you are requesting.
* **Material Amount** (`material_amount`, number, required): Specify the amount of material you are requesting.
* **Material Cost** (`material_cost`, number, required): If applicable, enter the cost of the material.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Explain why you are requesting the material.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Specify who you would like the materials to be assigned to.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to submit the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed the form's purpose and are eligible to request materials before filling out the form.
* Duplicate requests will not be accepted.
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the material you are requesting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for clarification.
* Once you have filled out the form, please review it carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
