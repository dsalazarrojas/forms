# Office Visit Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scheduling office visits. It is used to book appointments for visitors to attend meetings or appointments with office staff. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary details are collected efficiently and accurately, making it easier for both the visitor and the office staff to coordinate the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
*   Select the date of your visit from the calendar picker.
*   Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
*   Provide a brief reason for visiting in the "Reason for Visit" field.
*   Enter your contact number and the contact person (if applicable).
*   Estimate the duration of your visit.
*   Indicate the frequency of your visits.
*   If you have a visitor ID, enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your visit from the calendar picker. This will be used to schedule the visit in our calendar.
*   **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
*   **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of the purpose of your visit.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number so we can reach you to confirm the visit.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): If you are visiting with someone, enter their name or title.
*   **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, text, required: false): Estimate the duration of your visit.
*   **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, text, required: false): Indicate the frequency of your visits (e.g., one-time, regular, etc.).
*   **Visitor ID** (`visitor_id`, text, required: false): If you have a visitor ID, enter it to help us identify you more efficiently.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to select a date and time that works best for you and our office staff.
*   Try to provide as much detail as possible in the "Reason for Visit" field to help us prepare for your visit.
*   If you have any specific requests or requirements, let us know in the "Reason for Visit" field as well.
*   If you have a visitor ID, please enter it to help us track your visits more efficiently.
