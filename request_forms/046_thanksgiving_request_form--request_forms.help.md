# thanksgiving_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "thanksgiving_request_form" is a form designed to collect information for food requests or other holiday-related matters. Please use it to submit your needs and requirements for the holiday period.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the "thanksgiving_request_form", follow these steps:

1. Select a type of request (Food Request, Other Request, etc.) from the options provided.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Choose a date for the event.
5. Set a time for the event.
6. Add a message describing your needs and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_type**: Choose the type of request you have (e.g., Food Request, Other Request).
* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or contact information.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **date**: Choose a specific date for the requested event or service.
* **time**: Select a specific time for the requested event or service.
* **message**: Briefly describe your needs and requirements for the holiday season.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- Make sure to submit your request before the holiday season ends.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact your supervisor or HR department.
- This form is intended for internal use, so ensure your request is legitimate and not a prank or joke.
- Duplicate requests may be removed and won't be processed.
