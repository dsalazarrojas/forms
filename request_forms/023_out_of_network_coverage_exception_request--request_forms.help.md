# out_of_network_coverage_exception_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect necessary information from claimants regarding their request for out-of-network coverage exceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Request Details**: In the "Request Details" section, provide a brief description of your request, if necessary.
2. **Requester Information**: Enter the details of the person making the request, which includes any additional relevant information.
3. **Claimant Information**: Enter the details of the claimant, which includes their address, phone, email, date of birth, gender, and relationship status.
4. **Provider Information**: If applicable, enter the details of the healthcare provider related to your request.
5. Submit your form once you have filled out all necessary sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details**: A brief description of your request for out-of-network coverage.
* **Requester Information 1**: Details of the person making the request.
* **Requester Information 2**: Additional details of the person making the request.
* **Claimant Information 1**: The address of the claimant.
* **Claimant Information 2**: The claimant's address.
* **Claimant Information 3**: Another claimant's address.
* **Claimant Information 4**: More details about the claimant's address.
* **Claimant Address**: The physical address of the claimant.
* **Claimant Address 2**: Another address of the claimant.
* **Claimant Address 3**: A third address of the claimant.
* **Claimant Address 4**: More details about the claimant's address.
* **Claimant Phone**: The claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant Phone 2**: The claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant Email**: The email of the claimant.
* **Claimant Email 2**: More details about the claimant's email.
* **Claimant Date of Birth**: The claimant's date of birth.
* **Claimant Date of Birth 2**: The claimant's date of birth.
* **Claimant Gender**: The claimant's gender.
* **Claimant Gender 2**: The claimant's gender.
* **Claimant Relationship**: The relationship of the claimant to the person making the request.
* **Provider Information**: Details of the healthcare provider related to your request.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may cause delays or rejections.
* Proofread your form before submission to catch any errors.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your team or supervisor for assistance.
