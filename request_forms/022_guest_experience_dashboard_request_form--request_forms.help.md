# Guest Experience Dashboard Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from guests about their experience on our platform. By filling out this form, you'll be able to provide us with valuable feedback and preferences that will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your relevant information.
2. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank.
3. Review your input carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. General Information
* **General Information**: A field to provide any additional context or information about your experience. 
### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address to receive updates about your dashboard preferences and any other communications from us.
### 3. Date and Time
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of your visit to help us understand your experience more accurately.
### 4. Email
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your dashboard preferences and any other communications.
### 5. Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your dashboard preferences and any other communications.
### 6. Dashboard Preferences
* **Dashboard Preferences**: Choose your preferred dashboard view from the available options (Default, Custom 1, Custom 2) to help us tailor your dashboard to your needs.

### Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to provide us with the most accurate information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your input and will review it carefully to improve our service.
