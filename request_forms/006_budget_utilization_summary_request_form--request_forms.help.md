# Budget Utilization Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Utilization Summary Request Form is a tool used to request a budget utilization summary from our team. This form helps us understand your specific needs and requirements for the summary, allowing us to provide you with the most accurate and useful information possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and department to let us know who is requesting the summary and what area it concerns.
2. Enter your contact email and phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or for delivery.
3. Select the period for which you are requesting the summary.
	* Choose "Current Month" for the current fiscal month.
	* Choose "Previous Month" for the previous fiscal month.
	* Choose "Current Quarter" for the current fiscal quarter.
	* Choose "Previous Quarter" for the previous fiscal quarter.
	* Choose "Year to Date" for the current fiscal year to date.
	* Choose "Full Fiscal Year" for the entire current fiscal year.
	* Choose "Custom Period" to select a custom start and end dates.
4. If you chose "Custom Period", enter the start and end dates for the period you are requesting.
5. Select the budget category you are interested in (e.g., "Personnel", "Operations", etc.).
6. Choose how you would like to receive the summary (e.g., "PDF Report", "Excel Spreadsheet", etc.).
7. Select the detail level of the summary you need (e.g., "Executive Summary Only", "Summary with Key Metrics", etc.).
8. Choose what comparisons to include in the summary (e.g., "Budget vs Actual", "Month over Month", etc.).
9. Select how you would like to receive the summary (e.g., "Email Attachment", "Shared Drive Link", etc.).
10. Choose the urgency of your request (e.g., "Routine - Within 5 Business Days", etc.).
11. Enter a brief description of why you need the summary.
12. Finally, enter any additional requirements or instructions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as the person requesting the summary.
* **Department** (2, text, required): Enter your department or division name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your contact email address for delivery and correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Request Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting the summary.
* **Budget Period** (6, select_one, required): Choose the period for which you are requesting the summary.
* **Custom Start Date** (7, date, required if "Custom Period" is selected): If you chose "Custom Period", enter the start date of the period you are requesting.
* **Custom End Date** (8, date, required if "Custom Period" is selected): If you chose "Custom Period", enter the end date of the period you are requesting.
* **Project Code** (9, text, optional): Enter the project or cost center code relevant to your request.
* **Budget Category** (10, select_one, required): Choose the budget category you are interested in.
* **Preferred Format** (11, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive the summary.
* **Detail Level** (12, select_one, required): Choose the level of detail you need in the summary.
* **Include Comparisons** (13, select_multiple, required): Choose what comparisons you would like to include in the summary.
* **Delivery Method** (14, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive the summary.
* **Urgency** (15, select_one, required): Choose the urgency of your request.
* **Purpose of Request** (16, text, required): Enter a brief description of why you need the summary.
* **Additional Requirements** (17, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or notes you may have.
