# Landscaping Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your landscaping service request from us. Please fill out the form below to help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Request" page to start filling out the form.
*   Enter your name in the "Name" field.
*   Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
*   Select the service you are interested in from the "Services" field.
*   Choose a date for the service in the "Schedule" field.
*   Choose a time for the service in the "Time" field.
*   If you have any additional information or comments, use the "Additional Information" field.
*   Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request** (landingservice\_request): A brief description of your service request.
*   **Name** (Name): Enter your real name.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (Phone): Enter your real phone number.
*   **Services** (Services): Select one or more services you are interested in.
	+ Lawn Care
	+ Yard Work
	+ Garden Design
*   **Schedule** (Schedule): Enter the date for the service.
*   **Time** (Time): Enter the time for the service.
*   **Additional Information** (Additional Information): If you have any additional comments or details, enter them here.
	+ Yes
	+ No
*   **Submit** (Submit): Click to send your service request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide your real name and a valid email address.
*   Select only one or more services you are interested in.
*   Use the "Additional Information" field to provide any extra details or comments about the service request.
*   Check your email address and phone number carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
