# Creative Services Job Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Creative Services Job Form" is designed to gather information about job requests, allowing our team to efficiently manage and prioritize tasks. This form is used to capture essential details about each job, including job type, paper size, envelope size, ink color, paper weight, job title, and a description of the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant job type from the options provided.
2.  Choose the correct paper size, envelope size, and ink color required for the job.
3.  Answer the yes/no questions about paper weight and if the job needs to be printed on both sides.
4.  Enter the job title and provide a detailed description of the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of job you are requesting. This will help us understand the job's requirements and categorize it accordingly.
- **Paper Size** (`paper_size`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the size of paper needed for the job. This will help us determine the correct paper size for the printing process.
- **Envelope Size** (`envelope_size`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the envelope required for the job. This will ensure that the envelope is printed correctly and efficiently.
- **Ink Color** (`ink_color`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the color of ink you need for the job. This will help us understand the color scheme and ensure that the job is printed correctly.
- **Paper Weight** (`paper_weight`, `select_one`, required/optional): Answer yes/no questions about the paper weight required for the job. This will help us determine the correct paper weight for the printing process.
- **Job Title** (`describe_job`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the job, a short and descriptive name that will help us identify the job's purpose.
- **Describe Job** (`describe_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the job, including any relevant details or instructions. This will help us understand the job's requirements and ensure that it is completed accurately and efficiently.
- **Job Form Description** (`job_form_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the job form, a brief summary of the form's purpose and requirements. This will help us understand the job's context and ensure that it is completed accurately and consistently.
