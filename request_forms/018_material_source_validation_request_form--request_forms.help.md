# Material Source Validation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about material sources for validation purposes. Fill out the form with accurate details to help us verify the authenticity and legitimacy of the material sources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the supplier from the list provided in the `Supplier Info` field.
2. Choose the applicable certifications from the options in the `Material Certifications` field.
3. Fill out the rest of the fields with relevant information, such as numerical data for `Material Information 1` and `Material Information 2`, if applicable.
4. Provide any additional details in the `Note` fields.
5. Ensure you complete all mandatory fields (`Supplier Info` and `Material Certifications`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, select_one, required): Choose one supplier from the provided list.
* **Material Certifications** (`material_certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable certifications for the material.
* **Material Information 1** (`material_info_1`, number, optional): Enter any relevant numerical information about the material (e.g., quantity, weight).
* **Material Information 2** (`material_info_2`, number, optional): Enter any further numerical information about the material (e.g., measurement, dimension).
* **Date of Delivery** (`date_of_delivery`, date, optional): Specify the date of delivery for the material.
* **Time of Delivery** (`time_of_delivery`, time, optional): Specify the time of delivery for the material.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the material source.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's website URL.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's country of origin.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, optional): Provide any further comments or notes about the supplier.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, text, optional): Enter the time zone of the supplier.
