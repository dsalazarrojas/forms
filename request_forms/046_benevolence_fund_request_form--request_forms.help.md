# Benevolence Fund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Benevolence Fund Request Form is a form designed to collect information from individuals or families requesting assistance through the Benevolence Fund. It is essential to ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to aid in evaluating the need and urgency of the assistance required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the requirements.
2.  Fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3.  Be specific when providing your reason for requesting assistance.
4.  Ensure you are eligible for the assistance you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your circumstances.
*  **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): This is your email address that we may contact you with for further information or updates.
*  **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*  **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address where you currently reside.
*  **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city you currently reside in.
*  **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state of residence.
*  **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code to aid in location verification.
*  **Date of Request** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are requesting assistance.
*  **Type of Assistance Needed** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of assistance you are requesting (Medical Expenses, Housing Assistance, Food Assistance, Utilities, Education, Disaster Relief, Emergency Assistance, Other).
*  **Amount of Funds Requested** (`amount_requested`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of funds you are requesting.
*  **Justification for Request** (`justification`, `text`, required): Explain the reason why you need this assistance in a brief and clear description.
*  **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level of your request (Immediate, Urgent, Soon, Not Urgent).
*  **Current Financial Situation** (`current_situation`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your current financial situation.
*  **Other Resources or Assistance** (`other_resources`, `text`, optional): If you have sought help from other sources, please mention them here.
*  **Family Status** (`family_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your family status (Single, Married, Single Parent, Other).
*  **Number of Dependents** (`number_dependents`, `number`, optional): If applicable, please enter the number of dependents you have.
*  **I Certify All Information Is True** (`truthfulness`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I Agree' if you certify that all information is true.
*  **I Consent to Information Being Used for Evaluation** (`privacy_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I Agree' to give permission to use your information for evaluation purposes.
*  **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submit this form.

Please note that all fields are required, and you must provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful evaluation of your request. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
