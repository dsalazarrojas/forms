# Equipment Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Replacement Request Form is used to request replacement of equipment from the organization's assets. This form gathers essential information to facilitate a smooth and efficient replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the equipment ID to help us locate the equipment being replaced.
2. Enter the equipment name accurately to ensure we can identify the correct item.
3. Provide a clear description of the reason for replacement.
4. Enter the part number for the replacement equipment.
5. Select a date for your request, ensuring it is in a format that can be easily understood (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Choose a priority level for your request (Normal, High, or Low).
7. Select the equipment status (Available or Not Available).
8. Enter the requestor's name and contact information (email and phone number).
9. Identify the department the equipment belongs to.
10. Enter the asset number and serial number of the equipment.
11. Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated.
12. Select the category of the replacement equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the equipment being replaced.
* **Equipment Name**: Provide the name of the equipment being replaced.
* **Reason for Replacement**: Describe the reason why the equipment needs to be replaced.
* **Part Number**: Enter the part number for the replacement equipment.
* **Request Date**: Choose the date you want the equipment to be replaced (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Request Time**: If desired, select the time for the replacement request (optional).
* **Requestor Name**: Enter your name for the request.
* **Requestor E-mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Requestor Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Department**: Identify the department the equipment belongs to.
* **Asset Number**: Enter the asset number of the equipment being replaced.
* **Asset Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment being replaced.
* **Asset Location**: Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated.
* **Equipment Status**: Select the status of the equipment (Available or Not Available).
* **Request Status**: Select the status of the request (New, Used, or Repair).
* **Assigned To**: Select the person or team responsible for handling the request (optional).
* **Priority**: Choose the priority level for the request (Normal, High, or Low).
* **Category**: Enter the category of the replacement equipment.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the request (optional).
* **Submitter**: Enter your name for the submitter.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department for the submitter.
* **Submitter E-mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to facilitate a smooth and efficient replacement process. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department.
