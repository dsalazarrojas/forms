# vehicle-maintenance-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle-maintenance-request form is a tool for vehicle owners to report any issues or defects they have identified with their vehicle. This form helps to gather detailed information about the vehicle's condition, allowing the maintenance team to provide a more accurate diagnosis and repair estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the make of your vehicle from the list of options.
2. Select the model of your vehicle from the list of options.
3. Choose the year of your vehicle from the list of options.
4. Describe the issue or defect you have identified with your vehicle in the description field.
5. Select all the issues that apply to your vehicle from the list of options.
6. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address (if available).
7. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle-make** (`vehicle-make`, select_one, required): Choose the make of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **vehicle-model** (`vehicle-model`, select_one, required): Choose the model of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **vehicle-year** (`vehicle-year`, select_one, required): Choose the year of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the issue or defect you have identified with your vehicle in as much detail as possible.
* **issue-identified** (`issue-identified`, select_multiple, required): Select all the issues that apply to your vehicle from the list of options.
* **contact-info** (`contact-info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **phone-number** (`phone-number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **email-address** (`email-address`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).

## Tips

* Ensure that you accurately describe the issue or defect you have identified with your vehicle.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the issues that apply to your vehicle.
* Enter your contact information accurately and completely.
