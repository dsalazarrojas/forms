# Employee Reasonable Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to request a reasonable accommodation to help them perform their job duties or participate in work-related activities. A reasonable accommodation is a change to the work environment or workplace that would help to eliminate a barrier to performing the job duties or workplace activities. This form ensures that employees can request necessary accommodations without duplicating effort or confusion, and ensures that all necessary information is collected to process their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the `employee_id` field.
3. Select your department from the options in the `department` field.
4. Enter your job title in the `job_title` field.
5. Describe the accommodation you are requesting in the `requested_accommodation` field.
6. Provide more details about the accommodation you are requesting in the `accommodation_description` field.
7. Indicate whether the accommodation will have any impact on your ability to perform your job duties or participate in work-related activities. If it will, select `Yes` in the `impact` field.
8. Indicate whether the accommodation has been provided by the organization in the `accommodation_provided` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **employee_id** (Number, Required): Enter your employee ID as it appears on your personnel record.
* **department** (Text, Optional): Select your department from the provided options.
* **job_title** (Text, Optional): Enter your current job title.
* **requested_accommodation** (Text, Optional): Describe the accommodation you are requesting.
* **accommodation_description** (Text, Optional): Provide more details about the accommodation you are requesting.
* **impact** (Select, Required): Indicate if the accommodation will have any impact on your ability to perform your job duties or participate in work-related activities.
* **accommodation_provided** (Select, Required): Indicate if the accommodation has been provided by the organization.
 
## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
