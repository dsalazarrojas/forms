## HOA Records Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HOA Records Request Form is designed to collect and manage requests for various types of records. This form helps to ensure that all requests are properly documented, assigned, and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Request Type**: Choose the type of request you are submitting (e.g., HOA Records, Maintenance Requests, or Other).
2. Provide **Contact Method**: Specify how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
3. Enter your **Phone Number** or **Email Address**: Fill in your phone number or email address so that we can contact you regarding your request.
4. Choose the **Requested Date**: Select the date when you expect the records to be processed.
5. Provide any additional **Notes**: Include any relevant details about your request.
6. Choose the **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that you would like to be assigned for the task.
7. Select the **Status**: Choose the status of your request (Pending, In Progress, Complete, or Reopened).
8. Add **Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about your request.
9. Select the **Form ID**: Choose the ID for the form (optional).
10. Select the **Category**: Choose the category for your request (Request Forms, Response Forms, or Other).
11. Specify whether the request was **Submitted By** and **Submitted To**: Indicate whether you or someone else submitted the request.
12. Select the **Submitted At**: Choose the date when the request was submitted.
13. Select the **Updated At**: Choose the date when the request was last updated.
14. Select the **Created At**: Choose the date when the request was created.
15. Select the **Deleted At**: Choose the date when the request was deleted (if applicable).
16. Specify the **Created By**: Choose who created the request (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Information**: This section is for identifying the homeowner or administrator who is submitting the request.
* **Request Type**: Choose the type of request you are submitting (e.g., HOA Records, Maintenance Requests, or Other).
* **Contact Method**: Specify how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Request Date**: Select the date when you expect the records to be processed.
* **Request Time**: Enter the time when you expect the records to be processed (optional).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that you would like to be assigned for the task (e.g., chatjimmy or other).
* **Status**: Select the current status of your request (Pending, In Progress, Complete, or Reopened).
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Form ID**: Choose the ID for the form (optional).
* **Category**: Choose the category for your request (Request Forms, Response Forms, or Other).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether you or someone else submitted the request.
* **Submitted To**: Indicate whether someone else submitted the request.
* **Submitted At**: Choose the date when the request was submitted.
* **Updated At**: Choose the date when the request was last updated.
