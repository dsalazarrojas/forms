# Driving Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for driving records. Users must fill out all the required fields for a complete request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with a asterisk (*). For each field, enter your information as prompted. If a field is optional, you may skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, *): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, *): Enter your phone number.
* **License Number** (`user_license_number`, text, *): Enter your driver license number.
* **Date of Birth** (`user_date_of_birth`, date, *): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Issued By** (`request_issued_by`, text, *): Enter who issued your license or permit.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, *): Enter the date on which you are making the request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, *): Enter the time on which you are making the request.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text): Optional - enter the reason for your request.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, text): Optional - enter the type of request you are making.
* **Driver License State** (`driver_license_state`, select_one, *): Select the state where your license or permit was issued (CA, CO, FL, IL, IN, KS, KY, ME, MD, MI, MO, MS, MT, NE, OH, OK, OR, PA, SC, TN, TX, UT, VA, WA, WI, WV, WY).
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, *): Enter your driver license number.
* **Driver License Type** (`driver_license_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of your driver license (Class A, Class B, Class C, Class D, Class E, Class F, Class G, Class H, Class I, Class R, Class R-II, Class R-III, Class R-IV, Class R-ND, Commercial, Motorcycle, Private, School Bus, Taxicab, Tractor-Plate).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text): Optional - enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, text): Optional - enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text): Optional - enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, *): Enter your vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_vehicle_type`, text): Optional - enter the type of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_vehicle_color`, text): Optional - enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_vehicle_year`, text): Optional - enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_vehicle_make`, text): Optional - enter the make of your vehicle.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, text): Optional - enter your signature.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text): Optional - enter your address.
* **User City** (`user_city`, text): Optional - enter your city.
