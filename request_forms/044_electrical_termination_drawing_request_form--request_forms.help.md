# Electrical Termination Drawing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an electrical termination drawing for your project. Please ensure you have the required information and authorization to submit this form. To avoid duplication, please check if you have already submitted a request for the same project before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
4. Enter the drawing number related to your project.
5. Specify the date of the electrical termination you are requesting.
6. Enter the time of the electrical termination you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **Drawing Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter the drawing number related to your project.
* **Termination Date (Date, Required)**: Specify the date of the electrical termination you are requesting.
* **Termination Time (Time, Required)**: Enter the time of the electrical termination you are requesting.
