# Quality Improvement Plan Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Quality Improvement Plan Request Form is used to collect information and feedback from teams to identify areas of improvement in a project, allowing for the creation and implementation of a quality improvement plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the project.
2. Enter the project title.
3. Specify the department involved in the project.
4. Identify the team lead and members responsible for the project.
5. Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
6. Describe the proposed quality improvement.
7. List any action items related to the improvement proposal.
8. Update the status of the action items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the project.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): A short title of the project.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department involved in the project.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): The name of the team lead responsible for the project.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): A list of team members who are involved in the project.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time you are submitting this form.
* **Improvement Proposal** (`improvement_proposal`, note, optional): A detailed description of the proposed quality improvement.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional): A list of actions or items related to the improvement proposal.
* **Action Items 2** (`action_items_2`, text, optional): Additional actions or items related to the improvement proposal.
* **Action Item Status** (`action_item_status`, select_one, optional): Update the status of the action items, choosing from "In Progress" or "Done".
