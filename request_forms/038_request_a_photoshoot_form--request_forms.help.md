## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3.  Use the provided options for select fields or select all relevant services you offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Do you have a website?**: If you have a website, share your website URL to showcase your work and services. This helps the photographer learn more about your style and services.
*   **What services do you offer?**: Choose all the services you offer, such as Wedding, Event, Newborn, Family, or Other. This helps the photographer understand your specialties and tailor their services accordingly.

*   **Name of photographer**: Share your name as the photographer or contact person for this photoshoot.
*   **Email of photographer**: Enter your email address so the photographer can contact you about the photoshoot details.
*   **Phone number of photographer**: Provide your phone number for easy communication and coordination.
*   **Date of photoshoot**: Enter the date you're interested in having the photoshoot.
*   **Time of photoshoot**: Choose the time slot for the photoshoot.
