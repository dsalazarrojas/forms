# Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form is a tool for employees to request access to the Marketing Keyword Research Tool. This form helps to track and manage access requests, ensuring that only authorized personnel are granted access to the tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address. These are required fields.
2. Enter your department, if applicable.
3. Provide a valid reason for your access request.
4. Select the correct access option from the options provided.
5. You do not need to provide a Tool Version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email**: Your work email address. This will be used for communication related to your access request.
* **Department**: Enter your department if you are requesting access on behalf of your team.
* **Reason**: A valid reason for requesting access to the Marketing Keyword Research Tool. Be clear and concise.
* **Access Requested**: Select the option that best describes your need for access:
	+ Option 1: View-Only Access
	+ Option 2: Edit Access
	+ Option 3: Admin Access
* **Tool Version**: Not required, but you can enter a version number if you want.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved your access request.
* **Date Approved**: The date your access request was approved.
* **Time Approved**: The time your access request was approved.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in your information accurately and completely.
* Provide a clear and valid reason for your access request.
* Be patient and wait for the approver's confirmation before accessing the tool.
* If you encounter any issues with your access request, contact your team lead or IT department.
