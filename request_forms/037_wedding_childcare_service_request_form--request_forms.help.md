# wedding_childcare_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to request wedding child care services. The form will be used to gather contact details, event information, and child care requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your main contact information.
2. Enter the contact details of the family member or friend of the couple.
3. Share the event details (e.g., wedding date, location, and time).
4. Describe the child care requirements (e.g., number of children, ages, and any special needs).
5. Specify the desired schedule for child care services.
6. Include any additional information you would like to share.
7. Confirm your contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`main_info`, text, required/optional): This is the initial section to provide your contact information and introduce yourself to the couple.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of the family member or friend of the couple who submitted this form.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Share the event details such as the wedding date, location, and time.
* **Child Care Details** (`childcare_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the child care requirements such as the number of children, ages, and any special needs.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, text, required/optional): Specify the desired schedule for child care services (e.g., full-time, part-time, flexible).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required/optional): Include any additional information you would like to share with the couple.
* **Confirm Contact** (`confirm`, text, required/optional): Re-enter your contact information to confirm your details.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details or comments you would like to share.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number) so the couple can get back to you.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required/optional): Re-check your contact information to ensure accuracy.
