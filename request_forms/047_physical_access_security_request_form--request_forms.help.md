# Physical Access Security Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to request physical access to secure areas within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Type** (employees, contractors, visitors, others): Select your user type from the options provided.
  - This field is used to determine your clearance level for accessing secure areas.
- **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your badge or ID card.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  - This field is used to contact you in case of any issues with your access request.
- **Department** (Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Operations, Others): Choose your department from the options provided.
  - This field is used to determine your departmental clearance level.
- **Location**: Enter the specific location you plan to access.
  - Be as specific as possible so that your request can be accurately processed.
- **Reason**: Specify the reason for your access request.
  - Be clear and concise about your purpose.
- **Date**: Select the date you plan to access the secure area.
  - Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time**: Select the time you plan to access the secure area.
  - Use 24-hour time format.
- **Shift**: Enter your shift number (if applicable).
  - This field is used for scheduling purposes.
- **Approval Details**: Enter any additional approval details.
  - This field is for any relevant information about your approval.
- **Submission Details**: Enter any additional submission details.
  - This field is for any information you want to add to your submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit your access request at least 24 hours before your planned access date.
- Ensure that you have the necessary clearance level and approval for accessing the secure area.
- Be respectful of others' privacy and security protocols while in the secure area.
