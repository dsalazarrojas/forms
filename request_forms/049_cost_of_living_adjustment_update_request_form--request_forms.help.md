# Cost Of Living Adjustment Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cost Of Living Adjustment Update Request Form" is designed to help employees request adjustments to their compensation to account for the high cost of living in their location. This form is used by employees to provide information about their request and for managers to approve or deny the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee" field.
2. Enter your current location in the "Location" field.
3. Select the start date of your request in the "Start Date" field.
4. Select the end date of your request in the "End Date" field.
5. Briefly explain the reason for your request in the "Reason" field.
6. Fill out the "Manager Name", "Manager Email", and sign with your "Employee Signature" and your manager's "Manager Signature" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Location** (text, required): Enter your current location.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Select the date when you first noticed the cost of living adjustment need.
* **End Date** (date, required): Select the date when you expect the adjustment to end.
* **Reason** (text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your request.
* **Cost Of Living Adjustment** (number, optional): Enter the adjustment amount, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (email, required): Enter your manager's email.
* **Employee Signature** (text, required): Sign the form to confirm your request.
* **Manager Signature** (text, required): Sign the form to approve or deny the request.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Form Submitted At** (date, optional): This field will be filled out automatically.
* **Status** (select one, required): Select "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending".
* **Submitted By** (select one, required): Select your name from the dropdown list.
* **Submitted To** (select one, required): Select your manager's name from the dropdown list.
* **Created By** (select one, required): Select the person who created the form from the dropdown list.
* **Assigned To** (select one, required): Select the person who assigned the form from the dropdown list.
* **Updated By** (select one, required): Select the person who last updated the form from the dropdown list.
* **Updated At** (date, optional): This field will be filled out automatically.
* **Deleted At** (date, optional): This field will be filled out automatically.
* **Deleted By** (select one, optional): Select the person who deleted the form from the dropdown list.
* **Deleted By Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the person who deleted the form.
* **Created At** (date, optional): This field will be filled out automatically.
* **Updated By Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the person who last updated the form.
* **Form ID** (number, required): This field will be filled out automatically.

**Tips:**

* Be sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
