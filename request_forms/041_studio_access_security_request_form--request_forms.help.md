# Studio Access Security Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Studio Access Security Request Form is used to request access to a secure area of the studio. This form is a necessary step for all users who need to enter this secure area, whether it's for work or personal purposes. Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting access and have filled out all required fields accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting access to the secure area.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as such in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes. Please use the format `username@company.com`.
* **Department** (department, select_one, required): Select your department from the list to ensure that your request is routed to the correct team.
* **Date of Visit** (date, date, optional): If you're requesting access for a specific date, enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Visit** (time, time, optional): If you're requesting access for a specific time, enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Reason for Visit** (reason_for_visit, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
* **Studio Details** (studio_details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the studio you'll be visiting.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Requested By** (requested_by, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team requesting access on your behalf, if applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department for guidance.
* Be specific and detailed in your description of the reason for your visit and the studio details.
* Attach any supporting documents or materials that may be necessary for your request.
