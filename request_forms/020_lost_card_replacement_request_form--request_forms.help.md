# Lost Card Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a lost card replacement in the case of an incident involving a card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident.
3. Submit the form to request a lost card replacement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_data** (text, required): Enter any additional comments or details about the incident.
* **incident_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **incident_location** (text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **card_details** (text, required): Enter any specific details about the lost card, such as card number or type.
* **incident_type** (select multiple, optional): Select all that apply for types of incidents (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **reportee** (text, required): Enter the name of the person who experienced the incident.
* **contact_info** (text, required): Enter the contact information of the person who experienced the incident.
* **employer_school** (select one, optional): Select the type of school or employer associated with the incident.
* **lost_card_details** (text, required): Enter any additional details about the lost card.
* **incident_description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **incident_id** (text, required): Enter the ID number of the incident.
* **incident_reported** (date, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **incident_reporter** (text, required): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **incident_image** (text, optional): Attach an image related to the incident.
* **incident_video** (text, optional): Attach a video related to the incident.
* **incident_audio** (text, optional): Attach an audio recording related to the incident.
* **incident_document** (text, optional): Attach a document related to the incident.
* **incident_image2** (text, optional): Attach an additional image related to the incident.
* **incident_video2** (text, optional): Attach an additional video related to the incident.
* **incident_audio2** (text, optional): Attach an additional audio recording related to the incident.
* **incident_document2** (text, optional): Attach an additional document related to the incident.
* **incident_image3** (text, optional): Attach a third image related to the incident.
* **incident_video3** (text, optional): Attach a third video related to the incident.
* **incident_audio3** (text, optional): Attach a third audio recording related to the incident.
* **incident_document3** (text, optional): Attach a third document related to the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Attach any relevant images, videos, or audio recordings to support your incident report.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
