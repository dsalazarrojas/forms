# Holiday Party Song Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your holiday party song requests. Please answer these questions to ensure your favorite songs are included in our party playlist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read all instructions and guidelines before filling out the form.
2. Select your desired **Party Theme** to help us tailor the playlist.
3. Choose the types of **Singing**, **Dancing**, or **Playing** activities you wish to do for our holiday party.
4. Specify your preferred **Music Genre** (e.g., Rock, Pop, Classical, or Jazz).
5. Fill out your **Song Preferences** with the genres, themes, and activities that interest you.
6. Provide any **Special Requests** or messages you have for the party.
7. Answer any additional questions regarding the **Song Details** and **Holiday Songs**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Theme** (2): Specify the theme you would like our party to have.
  * Example: Christmas, Halloween, New Year's Eve
* **Song Preferences** (3): Select the genres and types of activities you would like to do for our holiday party.
  * Example: Rock, Pop, Dancing
* **Special Instruments** (4): If you would like to use any special instruments, choose them here.
  * Example: Saxophone, Drums, Keyboard
* **Song Request** (5): Enter your desired song title or performer.
* **Singer Duet** (6): Enter a date for when you would like to have a singer duet.
* **Song Type** (7): Choose whether you want to sing an original or cover song.
* **Holiday Songs** (8): Select the types of holiday songs you would like to have at the party.
  * Example: Singing, Dancing, Playing
* **Music Genre** (9): Select the music genre you prefer.
  * Example: Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz
* **Special Requests** (10): Enter any special requests or messages you have for the party.
* **Song Request 2** (15): Enter your second desired song title or performer.
* **Song Request Message 2** (16): Enter a message regarding your second song request.
* **Holiday Songs Message** (17): Enter a message regarding holiday songs at the party.
* **Holiday Songs Message 2** (18): Enter a message regarding holiday songs at the party.
* **Special Message** (19): Enter any special message you have for the party.
* **Song Type Message** (20): Enter a message regarding the type of song you would like.
* **Holiday Songs Type Message** (21): Enter a message regarding the type of holiday song you would like.
* **Singer Duet Message** (22): Enter a message regarding singer duet at the party.
* **Singer Duet Message 2** (23): Enter a message regarding singer duet at the party.
