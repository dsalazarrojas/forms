# Personal Shopper Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Shopper Request Form is designed to collect information from personal shoppers or customers who are requesting specific products or services. This form helps us understand their preferences and needs, allowing us to provide better service and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the date of your session in the "Session Date" field.
5. Enter the time of your session in the "Session Time" field.
6. Select the products you are interested in from the list provided in the "Requested Products" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
7. Indicate your shopper preference (Male, Female, or Other) in the "Shopper Preference" field.
8. Add any additional information or notes about your session in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your session.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any further information.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date, required): Enter the date of your session.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, time, required): Enter the time of your session.
* **Requested Products** (`Requested Products`, select_one, required): Select one or multiple products you are interested in:
	+ Shoes
	+ Clothing
	+ Accessories
	+ Other
* **Shopper Preference** (`Shopper Preference`, select_multiple, required): Select your shopper preference:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
