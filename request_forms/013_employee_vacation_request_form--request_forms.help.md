# Employee Vacations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to submit a vacation request to their employer. It helps the management team to plan and prepare for staff coverage during your absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form details (page 1) with your employee ID, vacation dates, and any other relevant information.
2. Select one option from the "Select One" field (page 2) to indicate your vacation type or preference.
3. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field (page 3) if applicable.
4. Enter your preferred start and end dates for your vacation in the "Date" field (page 4).
5. Select your preferred start and end times for your vacation in the "Time" field (page 5).
6. Enter your email address for us to contact you with any updates or questions (page 6).
7. Provide a brief description of your request in the "Note" field (page 7).
8. Enter the number of days you plan to take off (page 8).
9. Confirm the number of people that will be affected by your absence (page 9).
10. Finally, select an option from the "Select One" field (page 10) to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (page 1): Please fill in your employee ID and vacation dates to help us process your request.
* **Select One** (page 2): Choose one option to indicate your vacation type or preference.
* **Select Multiple** (page 3): If applicable, choose multiple options for any additional information or preferences.
* **Date** (page 4): Enter your preferred start and end dates for your vacation.
* **Time** (page 5): Select your preferred start and end times for your vacation.
* **Email** (page 6): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Note** (page 7): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Number of Days** (page 8): Enter the number of days you plan to take off.
* **Number of People Affected** (page 9): Confirm the number of people that will be affected by your absence.
* **Confirmation** (page 10): Select an option to confirm your request.
