# Club Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting funding from the club to support your club's activities and expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To request funding, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your request.
2. Provide your club's official name.
3. Enter the name of the club's president or lead.
4. Enter the email address of the club's president or lead.
5. Enter the phone number of the club's president or lead.
6. Enter the number of active members in your club.
7. Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
8. Choose the purpose of your funding request from the available options.
9. Explain why your club needs the funding and how it will be spent.
10. Describe the expected impact of the funding on your club.
11. Enter the date you plan to use the funds.
12. Provide a detailed breakdown of how you plan to spend the funds.
13. If applicable, list any other sources of funding your club currently has.
14. If applicable, describe any fundraising efforts your club has already attempted.
15. Check whether you have the support of your faculty advisor for this funding request.
15. If applicable, describe how your club plans to be sustainable in the future after receiving funding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Date of Request**
 * Date of Request (submission_date): The date when you are requesting funding for your club.
 
### **2. Club Name**
 * Club Name (club_name): Enter the official name of your club.
 
### **3. Club President or Lead**
 * Club President or Lead (club_president): Enter the name of the club's president or leader.
 
### **4. Club President Email**
 * Club President Email (president_email): Enter the email address of the club's president or lead.
 
### **5. Club President Phone**
 * Club President Phone (president_phone): Enter the phone number of the club's president or lead.
 
### **6. Current Club Membership**
 * Current Club Membership (club_membership): Enter the number of active members in your club.
 
### **7. Amount Requested**
 * Amount Requested (funding_amount): Enter the total funding amount you are requesting.
 
### **8. Purpose of Funding Request**
 * Purpose of Funding Request (funding_purpose): Choose the reason for your funding request from the options provided.
 	+ Equipment Purchase
 	+ Event or Competition
 	+ Travel
 	+ Training or Education
 	+ Facility Rental
 	+ Scholarship or Awards
 	+ Operating Costs
 	+ Other
 
### **9. Detailed Justification**
 * Detailed Justification (detailed_justification): Explain why your club needs the funding and how you plan to spend it.
 
### **10. Expected Impact**
 * Expected Impact (expected_impact): Describe how the funding will benefit your club.
 
### **11. Timeline for Use**
 * Timeline for Use (timeline): Enter the date when you plan to use the funds.
 
### **12. Budget Breakdown**
 * Budget Breakdown (budget_breakdown): Detail how you plan to spend the funds.
 
### **13. Current Funding Sources**
 * Current Funding Sources (current_funding): If applicable, list any other sources of funding your club currently has.
 
### **14. Fundraising Efforts**
 * Fundraising Efforts (fundraising_attempts): If applicable, describe any fundraising efforts your club has already attempted.
 
### **15. Requires Faculty Advisor Approval**
 * Requires Faculty Advisor Approval (approval_authority): Check if you have your faculty advisor's support for this funding request.
 
### **16. Plan for Future Sustainability**
 * Plan for Future Sustainability (sustainability_plan): If applicable, describe how your club plans to be sustainable in the future after receiving funding.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled in order to submit the form. Fields marked as "optional" (not required) can be filled or left empty at your discretion.
