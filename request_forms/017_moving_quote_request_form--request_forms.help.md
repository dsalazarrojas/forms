# Moving Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are requesting moving services. It is intended to help us provide a quote for the moving services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information (Form Submitter and Email).
2. Provide details about the moving (Moving Details).
3. Upload any relevant files related to the moving (File Uploader).
4. Choose which tool will be assigned to assist you with your moving needs (Assigned Tool).
5. If you have any additional information or messages, please include them in the Message field.
6. Finally, review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, `text`, required: false): Your name and contact information. Please enter your name and any relevant contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your moving quote.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your moving quote.
* **Moving Details** (`moving_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about the moving, such as the location, size of the move, and any other relevant information.
* **Moving Date** (`moving_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the moving.
* **Moving Time** (`moving_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the moving.
* **File Uploader** (`file_uploader`, `text`, required: false): Upload any files related to the moving, such as pictures or documents.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information or agreements related to the moving.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose which tool will be assigned to assist you with your moving needs. This will ensure that you are matched with the right assistant.
* **Message** (`submitter_message`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information or messages related to the moving.
