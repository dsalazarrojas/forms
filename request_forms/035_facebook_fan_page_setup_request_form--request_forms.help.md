# facebook_fan_page_setup_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a lead to set up 
a Facebook fan page. It is an important part of the sales process as it 
enables the company to connect with potential customers and showcase 
their brand on the social media platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the required 
information about the lead. You can find a list of fields below with 
their corresponding explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Details (lead_details)**: Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Lead Name (lead_name)**: Enter the name of the lead.
* **Lead Email (lead_email)**: Enter the email address of the lead.
* **Lead Phone (lead_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the lead.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, feel free 
to contact our support team.
* The form is not mandatory, but it is essential for the successful 
setup of the Facebook fan page.
