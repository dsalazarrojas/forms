# Trip Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Request Form is designed to collect essential details about trips, including business or personal nature, travel dates, purpose, and associated travel agency information. This form helps in efficient trip planning, coordination, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trip Request Form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if the trip is for business or personal purposes.
2.  Enter the departure date of the trip.
3.  Enter the return date of the trip.
4.  Describe the purpose of the trip.
5.  Provide your email address.
6.  Enter your phone number.
7.  Indicate if the travel agency is associated with the trip.
8.  If the agency is associated, provide the agency address, contact person, phone number, and email address.
9.  Optionally, add any additional notes about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Is this a business or personal trip? (<name>is_business_trip</name>, <type>select_one</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    This field helps identify if the trip is for business or personal purposes. Please select 'Yes' for business trips and 'No' for personal trips.
*   **When is the departure date? (<name>travel_departure_date</name>, <type>date</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Enter the date when you plan to leave for the trip.
*   **When is the return date? (<name>travel_return_date</name>, <type>date</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Enter the date when you plan to return from the trip.
*   **What is the purpose of the trip? (<name>trip_purpose</name>, <type>text</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Describe the reason for your trip.
*   **Email address (<name>email</name>, <type>email</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Enter your email address so we can contact you about your trip.
*   **Phone number (<name>phone</name>, <type>text</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your trip.
*   **Is this travel agency associated with the trip? (<name>is_travel_agency_associated</name>, <type>select_one</type>, <required>true</required>):** 
    Indicate if the travel agency is associated with your trip. If yes, please provide additional agency details.
*   **Travel agency address (<name>travel_agency_address</name>, <type>text</type>, <required>false</required>):** 
    Enter the address of the travel agency associated with your trip.
*   **Travel agency contact person (<name>travel_agency_contact</name>, <type>text</type>, <required>false</required>):** 
    Enter the name of the person at the associated travel agency you'll be contacting.
*   **Travel agency phone number (<name>travel_agency_phone</name>, <type>text</type>, <required>false</required>):** 
    Enter the phone number of the travel agency associated with your trip.
*   **Travel agency email (<name>travel_agency_email</name>, <type>email</type>, <required>false</required>):** 
    Enter the email address of the travel agency associated with your trip.
*   **Notes (<name>notes</name>, <type>note</type>, <required>false</required>):** 
    Provide any additional information or comments about your trip. 

**Tips:**
*   Please make sure to double-check your entered dates for accuracy.
*   If you're unsure about any field, you can contact our support team for assistance.
*   This form is for your convenience, and accuracy is key for smooth trip planning and coordination. 
*   If you need to update your trip information, please contact our team to do so.
