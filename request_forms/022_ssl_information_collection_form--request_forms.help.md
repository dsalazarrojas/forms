# ssl_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ssl_information_collection_form is a request form used to collect information about a website's SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) certificate. This form is used to gather contact details, CSR (Certificate Signing Request) data, domains, SSL type, email address, phone number, domain type, and expiration date for SSL renewal purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your CSR data, which is used for SSL certificate generation.
3. Select multiple domains if applicable.
4. Choose the type of SSL certificate you are requesting.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Provide your phone number.
7. Select the type of domain you are requesting.
8. Enter the domain name.
9. Enter the expiration date of your current SSL certificate.
10. Choose whether to renew your SSL certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_details):
	+ This is the section for you to enter your contact details.
* **CSR Data** (csr_data):
	+ Enter your CSR data here.
* **Domains** (domains):
	+ Select multiple domains if you have more than one.
* **SSL Type** (ssl_type):
	+ Choose the type of SSL certificate you are requesting.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Domain Type** (domain_type):
	+ Select the type of domain you are requesting.
* **Domain Name** (domain_name):
	+ Enter your domain name.
* **Expiration Date** (expiration_date):
	+ Enter the date of your current SSL certificate expiration.
* **Renew** (renew):
	+ Choose whether to renew your SSL certificate.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your CSR data for accuracy.
* Select the correct domain type and expiration date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
