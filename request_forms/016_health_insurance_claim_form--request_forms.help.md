# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to a patient's health insurance claim. It is used to record and process claims in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
- Select the correct claim type, claim status, payment status, and insurance plan from the dropdown menus.
- Ensure that you fill in the correct zip codes for the patient and provider addresses.
- Fill in any additional comments or notes if necessary.
- Double-check all the information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, Required): Your first name, which should be the first name you were given at birth or by which you are commonly known.
* **Last Name** (2, text, Required): Your last name, which is the name your parents or the law gave you at birth.
* **Email** (3, email, Optional): A valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, Optional): A number for you to be reached.
* **Date of Service** (5, date, Optional): The date of the service you are claiming for.
* **Provider Name** (6, text, Optional): The name of your healthcare provider.
* **Claim Type** (7, select_one, Required): Select the type of claim you are submitting (Claim, Pre-authorization, Denial).
* **Claim Amount** (8, number, Optional): The amount you are claiming.
* **Claim Status** (9, select_one, Required): Select the status of your claim (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Patient ID** (10, number, Optional): A unique identifier for the patient.
* **Payment Status** (11, select_one, Required): Select the payment status of your claim (Pending, Received, Refunded).
* **Insurance Info** (12, text, Optional): Additional information about your insurance.
* **Claim Number** (13, number, Optional): A claim number if available.
* **Patient Address** (14, text, Optional): A physical address for the patient.
* **Patient City** (15, text, Optional): The city of the patient's residence.
* **Patient State** (16, select_one, Optional): Select the state where the patient resides (California, New York, Florida).
* **Provider Address** (17, text, Optional): A physical address for the provider.
* **Claim Note** (18, note, Optional): Any additional notes about the claim.
* **Insurance Plan** (19, select_one, Optional): Select the type of insurance plan (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
* **Patient Zip** (20, text, Optional): A zip code for the patient's address.
* **Provider Zip** (21, text, Optional): A zip code for the provider's address.
* **Patient Country** (22, text, Optional): The country of the patient's residence.
* **Provider Country** (23, text, Optional): The country of the provider's residence.
* **Payment Method** (24, select_one, Optional): Select the payment method (Check, Credit card, Bank transfer).
* **Submitted By** (25, select_one, Optional): Select who submitted the claim (User, AI, Human).
