# Software Product Demo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for potential customers to request a demo of one of our software products. It will help us understand your needs and preferences to provide a tailored demo experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the software product you are interested in (e.g., "Product 1", "Product 2", or "Product 3") in the "Software Product" field.
2.  Enter your company information, including name, address, website, and size (small, medium, large).
3.  Provide your contact information, such as email address and phone number.
4.  Choose a date and time for the demo.
5.  Specify the type of demo you are interested in (online, in-person, or phone).
6.  Optionally, you can provide more details about your demo preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information** (`company_info`): Enter your company name, address, and website.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter your name and email address.
* **Software Product** (`software_product`): Select the software product you are interested in.
* **Demo Date** (`demo_date`): Enter the date you prefer for the demo.
* **Demo Time** (`demo_time`): Enter the time you prefer for the demo.
* **Demo Details** (`demo_details`): Optionally, enter any additional details about your demo preferences.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`): Enter your company website.
* **Software Version** (`software_version`): Enter the software version you are using.
* **Demo Requester** (`demo_requester`): Enter your name as the person requesting the demo.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): Select your company size (small, medium, large).
* **Demo Location** (`demo_location`): Enter the location for the demo.
* **Demo Duration** (`demo_duration`): Enter the duration of the demo.
* **Demo Type** (`demo_type`): Select the type of demo you prefer (online, in-person, or phone).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Enter the name of the person assigned to you.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter your company address.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`): Enter your company industry.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Demo Status** (`demo_status`): Enter the current status of your demo request.
* **Demo Note** (`demo_note`): Optionally, enter any additional notes about your demo request.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Optionally, enter any follow-up information.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Optionally, enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Optionally, enter the follow-up time.

Please submit this form once you have filled out all the necessary fields. If you have any further questions, feel free to contact us.
