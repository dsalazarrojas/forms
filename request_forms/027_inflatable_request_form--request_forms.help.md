# Inflatable Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inflatable Request Form is designed to collect essential details about an inflatable party event. Please use this form to provide information about your party to help us better understand and plan for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide party details such as party name, date, and location.
2. Choose the inflatable type and size you desire.
3. Enter contact information for communication and follow-up.
4. Add any additional information or special requests you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Party Details
* **Party Name** (`party_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your party, e.g., "John's Birthday Party".
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your party in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Party Location** (`party_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your party.

### Inflatable Information
* **Inflatable Type** (`inflatable_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of inflatable you desire, e.g., "Balloon" or "Castle".
* **Inflatable Size** (`inflatable_size`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific size requirement, enter it here.
* **Inflatable Color** (`inflatable_color`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred color, enter it here.
* **Inflatable Color2** (`inflatable_color2`, `text`, optional): If you have a second preferred color, enter it here.

### Guest and Contact Information
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, required): Enter the expected number of guests.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you prefer contact through email.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the party.
* **Contact Number2** (`contact_number2`, `text`, optional): If you have a secondary contact number, enter it here.
* **Email2** (`email2`, `email`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted through a secondary email, enter it here.

### Additional Information
* **Party Theme** (`party_theme`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific party theme, enter it here.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information, enter it here.
* **Inflatable Preferences** (`inflatable_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred inflatable type, e.g., "Balloon" or "Castle".
* **Inflatable Type2** (`inflatable_type2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the second inflatable type you desire.
* **Party Location2** (`party_location2`, `text`, optional): If you have a location for a second inflatable, enter it here.
* **Party Date2** (`party_date2`, `date`, optional): If you have a date for a second inflatable, enter it here.
* **Party Name2** (`party_name2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the second inflatable party.
* **Party Theme2** (`party_theme2`, `text`, optional): If you have a theme for the second inflatable, enter it here.

### Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing information about your party to help us better plan and prepare for your event.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
