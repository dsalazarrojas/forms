# portal_access_pre_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The portal_access_pre_survey form is a pre-requisite for requesting access to a secure portal or system. It ensures that users understand and agree to the terms and conditions before accessing the portal. The form gathers information about the user's reason for access and requires acknowledgement of policies related to access control and security.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select a reason for your access request from the list of options provided.
* Read and acknowledge the policies related to access control and security.
* Provide any additional comments or notes to support your access request in the text fields at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Portal access request**: Enter a brief description of your access request.
* **Intended use**: Describe how you plan to use the portal or system.
* **Policy acknowledgement**: Select one of the options to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the policies related to access control and security.
* **Reason for access**: Select one or more reasons for your access request from the list of options.
* **Security clearinghouse**: Enter any additional information related to security clearances or clearances required for access.
* **Policy acknowledgement date**: Enter the date of your policy acknowledgement (optional).
* **Policy acknowledgement time**: Enter the time of your policy acknowledgement (optional).
* **Policy acknowledgement 10-25**: Enter any additional comments or notes to support your access request in the corresponding text fields.
