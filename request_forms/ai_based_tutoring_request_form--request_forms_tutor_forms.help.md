# AI Based Tutoring Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Based Tutoring Request Form is designed to collect information from students to provide effective tutoring assistance. By submitting this form, you will help us understand your learning needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our support services to meet your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including full name, email address, and phone number (optional).
2. Select your grade level and school or institution name (if applicable).
3. Choose the primary subject area you need help with.
4. Specify your preferred learning style.
5. Select the days and time slots you are available for tutoring.
6. Indicate your average study hours per week (optional).
7. Mention any learning challenges you face (optional).
8. Share your previous tutoring experience (optional).
9. State your goals for tutoring (optional).
10. Add any additional comments or notes about your needs (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`): Please enter your first and last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email_address`): We will use this to contact you about your tutoring session.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): Optional, but if you provide it, we can use it for urgent communication.
* **Grade/Year level** (`grade_level`): Select your current academic level (e.g., 9th grade, college freshman).
* **School or institution name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of your current educational institution, if applicable.
* **Subject area** (`subject_area`): Choose the primary subject for which you need help.
* **Specific topic or area needing help** (`specific_topic`): Specify the area where you need extra support (e.g., algebra, organic chemistry, essay writing).
* **Current grade in this subject (%)** (`current_grade`): Enter your approximate current grade percentage.
* **Preferred learning style** (`learning_style`): Select the method you learn best (e.g., visual, auditory, reading/writing).
* **Preferred session length** (`preferred_session_length`): Indicate how long you would like each tutoring session to be.
* **Days available for tutoring** (`available_days`): Select all the days you are available for tutoring (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Preferred time of day** (`preferred_time`): Choose when you would prefer to have tutoring sessions (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Average study hours per week** (`hours_studied`): Optional, but enter the number of hours you typically study per week.
* **Main challenges faced** (`challenges`): Select all the challenges you face in learning (e.g., time management, difficulty with material).
* **Previous tutoring experience** (`previous_tutoring`): Mention if you have had tutoring before (e.g., online, in-person, both).
* **Goals for tutoring** (`goals`): State your objectives for seeking tutoring (e.g., improve grades, enhance confidence).
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`): Add any other information about your needs or concerns.
* **Consent to be contacted** (`consent_to_contact`): Please select "True" to consent to being contacted about your request, and "False" otherwise.
