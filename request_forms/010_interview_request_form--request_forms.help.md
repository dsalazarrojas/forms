# Interview Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Request Form is used by internal teams to request interviews with candidates who have shown interest in a job opening. This form helps you to gather necessary information about the candidate and their background, making it easier to evaluate them for the job.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit an interview request.
2. Enter the candidate's email address in the "Candidate Email" field to ensure we can contact them directly.
3. Enter the candidate's phone number in the "Candidate Phone" field for easy contact.
4. Enter the title of the job the candidate is applying for in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter the name of the candidate in the "Candidate Name" field.
6. Enter the company's location in the "Company Location" field to ensure we can contact them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Email**: Enter the candidate's email address to ensure we can contact them directly.
* **Candidate Phone**: Enter the candidate's phone number for easy contact.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job the candidate is applying for.
* **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Company Location**: Enter the location of the company that the candidate works for or has worked for in the past.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate processing.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can reach out to your team for clarification.
* Please ensure that the candidate's contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
