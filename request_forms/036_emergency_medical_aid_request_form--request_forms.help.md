# Emergency Medical Aid Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations requesting emergency medical aid in a specific location. It is used by aid providers to assess the aid request and provide timely and efficient assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
2. Enter the name of the contact person and their phone number and email address.
3. Specify the location where the aid is needed, including latitude, longitude, and elevation (if applicable).
4. Describe the medical need or condition that requires aid.
5. Select the aid providers that will be able to assist with the aid request.
6. Enter the date and time of the aid request.
7. Provide the name and contact information of the person requesting aid.
8. Add any additional notes about the aid request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Medical Aid**: Enter a brief description of the medical need or condition.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person making the aid request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person making the aid request.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Location**: Enter the location where the aid is needed.
* **Aid Location Latitude**: Enter the latitude of the aid location.
* **Aid Location Longitude**: Enter the longitude of the aid location.
* **Aid Location Elevation**: Enter the elevation of the aid location (if applicable).
* **Medical Need Description**: Enter a more detailed description of the medical need or condition.
* **Aid Providers**: Select the aid providers that can assist with the aid request.
* **Aid Request Date**: Enter the date of the aid request.
* **Aid Request Time**: Enter the time of the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Organization**: Enter the name of the organization making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Contact Note**: Add any additional notes about the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Role**: Enter the role of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Address**: Enter the address of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester City**: Enter the city of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester State**: Enter the state of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Country**: Enter the country of the person making the aid request.
* **Aid Requester Notes**: Add any additional notes about the aid request.
