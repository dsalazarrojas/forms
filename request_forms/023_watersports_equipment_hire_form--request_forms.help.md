# watersports_equipment_hire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book or reserve watersports equipment for a specific date range. Please provide the required information to help us fulfill your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of watersport you are interested in (e.g., skim, kayak, jet ski, etc.).
2. Choose the equipment type(s) you want to hire (e.g., kayak, jet ski, paddleboard, etc.).
3. Enter the number of people participating in the watersport activity.
4. Select the start and end dates for your watersport activity.
5. Choose the start and end times for your watersport activity.
6. Add any extra notes or comments about your request (optional).
7. Provide your contact email and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose Watersport** (`choose_watersports`, select_one): Select the type of watersport you are interested in (e.g., skim, kayak, jet ski, etc.).
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple): Choose the equipment type(s) you want to hire (e.g., kayak, jet ski, paddleboard, etc.).
* **Number of People** (`number_people`, number): Enter the number of people participating in the watersport activity.
* **Start Date** (`date_start`, date): Select the start date for your watersport activity. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD (year-month-day).
* **End Date** (`date_end`, date): Select the end date for your watersport activity. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD (year-month-day).
* **Start Time** (`time_start`, time): Choose the start time for your watersport activity in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **End Time** (`time_end`, time): Choose the end time for your watersport activity in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).
* **Extra Note** (`extra_note`, note): Add any extra comments or notes about your request (optional).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email (optional).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
