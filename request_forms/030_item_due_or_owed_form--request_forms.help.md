# Item Due Or Owed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Item Due Or Owed Form" is used to report items that are due or owed, such as rent, bills, or other types of payments. This form helps the user to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the item's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Item Due" field with the exact date when the item is due.
2. Provide contact details about the item in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Add any relevant status updates in the "Status Updates" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Due** (1, number, required): Enter the exact date when the item is due. This field is required to ensure accurate reporting.
* **Contact Details** (3, text, optional): Provide any relevant contact information, such as phone number, email, or address, about the item.
* **Status Updates** (4, text, optional): Add any relevant status updates about the item, such as progress or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the internal database to verify the item's status before filling out this form.
* Only report items that are already created or have a confirmed status.
