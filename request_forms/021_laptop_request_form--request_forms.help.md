# Laptop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laptop Request Form is a tool used to collect information from users to process their laptop requests. Users must complete the form with their personal and laptop request information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by entering your personal details, including your name and contact information.
- Then, provide your school information, including the school's address and email.
- Finally, specify the laptop(s) you would like to request.
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid submission errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify who is making the request.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for more details.
* **Laptops Requested** (`laptop_request`, text, optional): Describe the laptop(s) you would like to request, including the type and quantity needed.
* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter your school's information to help us understand the context of your request.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to reach out to you for more information.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school's address to help us deliver the laptops to you.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school's email address for communication purposes.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school's phone number to contact them for more information.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- You can request multiple laptops, but each request should be specified clearly in the "Laptops Requested" field.
- If you have any specific requirements or attachments, you can upload them as additional information, though this is not required.
