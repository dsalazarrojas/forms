# Internal Miscellaneous Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internal Miscellaneous Information Form is designed to collect internal information from users for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
3. Select your department from the "department" field.
4. Enter a description of the miscellaneous information in the "description" field (optional).
5. Select any relevant options from the "submit" field (optional).
6. Enter the date you would like to schedule a follow-up (if necessary) in the "follow_up" field.
7. Enter the time you would like to schedule a follow-up (if necessary) in the "follow_up_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for internal communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for internal contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department affiliation from the list.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the miscellaneous information (optional).
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant options from the list.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule a follow-up (if necessary).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to schedule a follow-up (if necessary).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out this form correctly and accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact the administrative team.
* This form is intended for internal purposes only, and the collected data will be used for administrative purposes.
