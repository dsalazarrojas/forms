# PPP Loan Forgiveness Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPP (Paycheck Protection Program) Loan Forgiveness Application Form is a document used to report and apply for loan forgiveness under the PPP program for businesses affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field carefully, as they are all required fields for the PPP loan forgiveness process.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Info**: This field is where you should enter your company's name and contact information. Please enter your business name, address, and contact person's name and phone number.
- **Loan Details**: Here, you should provide information about the original PPP loan received by your company, including the loan amount and date of disbursement.
- **Employment Details**: This section is for providing information about your company's employment status, including the number of employees and their job titles.
- **Income Proof**: Attach proof of income, such as tax returns or bank statements, to demonstrate your company's financial situation.
- **Employment Verification**: Please provide verification of your company's employment status, such as a letter from your bank or a letter from the government.
- **Loan Terms**: Enter the terms of your loan, including the interest rate and repayment term.
- **Loan Amount**: Enter the total amount of the loan you are applying for forgiveness.
- **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the loan in months or years.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the entire form carefully before submitting it.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your loan officer or contact us.

Note: This form is a general guideline, and specific instructions may vary depending on your organization's requirements and procedures. Please refer to your loan officer or contact us for further guidance.
