# Laboratory Space Lease Letter Of Intent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Space Lease Letter Of Intent is a document that outlines the terms and conditions for the use of a laboratory space. This form helps to ensure that the space is being used in accordance with established guidelines and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid Email address to ensure we can contact you for any further information.
3. Provide a Phone number where you can be reached.
4. Select a Start Date for the lease term.
5. Select an End Date for the lease term.
6. Choose the Lease Type from the options provided (Fixed-term, Flexible-term, or Month-to-Month).
7. Select the Start Time for the lease term.
8. Select the End Time for the lease term.
9. Choose the Days of the Week for the lease term.
10. Select the Hours of the Day for the lease term.
11. Provide the Address of the laboratory space.
12. Select the City of the laboratory space.
13. Select the State of the laboratory space.
14. Enter the Zip code of the laboratory space.
15. Select the Country of the laboratory space.
16. Enter the name of the Lab Manager's First Name.
17. Enter the name of the Lab Manager's Last Name.
18. Enter a valid Email address for the Lab Manager.
19. Enter the Phone number of the Lab Manager.
20. Enter the Organization of the Lab Manager.
21. Enter the Address of the Lab Manager.
22. Enter the Title of the Lab Manager.
23. Click submit to send the form to us.
</how_to_complete>

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid Email address that we can use to contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (`user_phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select a date when the lease term is to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select a date when the lease term is to end.
* **Lease Type** (`lease_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Fixed-term, Flexible-term, or Month-to-Month lease types.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select a time of day when the lease term is to start.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select a time of day when the lease term is to end.
* **Days of the Week** (`days_of_week`, select_multiple, required): Choose the days of the week when the laboratory space is to be used.
* **Hours of the Day** (`hours_of_day`, select_multiple, required): Choose the hours of the day when the laboratory space is to be used.
* **Address** (`user_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the laboratory space.
* **City** (`user_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the laboratory space.
* **State** (`user_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the laboratory space.
* **Zip** (`user_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the laboratory space.
* **Country** (`user_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the laboratory space.
* **Lab Manager First Name** (`lab_manager_first_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the Lab Manager's first name.
* **Lab Manager Last Name** (`lab_manager_last_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the Lab Manager's last name.
* **Lab Manager Email** (`lab_manager_email`, email, required): Enter a valid Email address for the Lab Manager.
* **Lab Manager Phone** (`lab_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for the Lab Manager.
* **Lab Manager Organization** (`lab_manager_organization`, text, required): Enter the organization of the Lab Manager.
* **Lab Manager Address** (`lab_manager_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the Lab Manager.
* **Lab Manager Title** (`lab_manager_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the Lab Manager.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Double-check the selected dates and times to ensure they align with your schedule.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact us for clarification.
- This form is a letter of intent and is not a binding contract.
- This form will be reviewed and confirmed by the relevant authorities before it is finalized.
