# Bathtub Safety Equipment Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the installation of safety equipment in a bathtub for residents with mobility issues.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form to request assistance with bathtub safety equipment installation.
- Provide accurate information about the resident, including their name and contact phone number.
- Select the safety equipment needed, considering the resident's mobility level and wall surface type.
- Specify how the installation will be paid for.
- Ensure the resident authorizes drilling into walls for safety equipment mounting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name** (`resident_full_name`, required): Enter the name of the person who will use the safety equipment.
* **Requestor Name (if different)** (`requestor_name`, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their name here.
* **Installation Address** (`installation_address`, required): Enter the street address where the equipment will be installed.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter a contact phone number in case we need to reach you for further information.
* **Safety Equipment Requested** (`equipment_needs_header`, note): This is a header for selecting safety equipment. Please choose all that apply from the options below.
* **Items for Installation** (`safety_items_selection`, required): Select from the following options:
	+ Safety Grab Bar (Straight)
	+ Angled Grab Bar
	+ Wall-mounted Shower Seat
	+ Non-slip Adhesive Flooring
	+ Hand-held Shower Head
	+ Bathtub Transfer Bench
	+ Walk-in Tub Conversion
* **Wall Surface Type** (`wall_surface_type`, required): Select the type of wall surface material where the bars will be mounted (e.g., Ceramic Tile, Fiberglass Insert).
* **Current Mobility Level of the User** (`mobility_level`, required): Select the resident's current mobility level to help us suggest optimal placement.
	+ Independent (needs minor support)
	+ Uses Walker or Cane
	+ Uses Wheelchair
	+ Requires Full Assistance
* **Urgency of Installation** (`urgency_installation`, required): Specify the urgency of the installation:
	+ High (Imminent safety risk)
	+ Medium (Within 2 weeks)
	+ Routine (Within 1 month)
* **How will the installation be paid for?** (`payment_source`, required): Select the payment method (e.g., Private Pay, Medicare/Medicaid Grant).
* **Insurance Policy/ID Number** (`insurance_id_number`, optional): If applicable, enter the insurance policy or ID number.
* **Authorize Drilling into Walls** (`permission_to_drill`, required): Confirm authorization for drilling into walls for safety equipment mounting.
* **Date of Request** (`request_date`, required): Enter the date of the request.

**Tips**: 
- Please ensure that the resident is aware that the form has been submitted, and they understand the requested equipment and installation process.
- If the resident has any concerns or questions, please contact the installation team for assistance.
- The installation team will contact you to schedule the installation once the form is reviewed and approved.
