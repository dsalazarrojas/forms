# Opposition To Venue Changes Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opposition to Venue Changes Submission Form is a document used to express opposition to venue changes. This form is intended for users who wish to voice their disagreement with proposed changes to a venue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Submission Form section with a brief statement explaining your opposition.
2. Enter your name and email address in the Submitter Name and Submitter Email fields, respectively.
3. Describe the venue change you oppose in the Venue Name and Venue Change Description fields.
4. Provide evidence or supporting information for your objection in the Opposition Evidence field.
5. Select a date for submission in the Submission Date field.
6. Optionally, you may provide additional comments about your submission in the Submission Form Comments field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Submitter Phone field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Form**: A brief statement explaining your opposition to the venue change.
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the opposition.
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the opposition.
* **Objection Description**: A more detailed explanation of the venue change you are opposing.
* **Venue Name**: The name of the venue that is undergoing a change.
* **Venue Change Description**: A description of the change being proposed.
* **Opposition Evidence**: Any supporting information or evidence you have for your objection.
* **Submission Date**: The date you are submitting your opposition.
* **Submission Form Comments**: Optional comments about your submission.
* **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the opposition.

Note: This form is not intended for users who wish to propose changes to a venue. If you are looking to suggest a new venue, please use the [Venue Change Proposal Form].
