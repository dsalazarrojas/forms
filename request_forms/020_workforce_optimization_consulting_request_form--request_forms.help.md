# Workforce Optimization Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about your project and needs, so we can tailor our workforce optimization consulting services to meet your business objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely, including all required and optional fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your project and challenges.
3. Choose relevant options for the consultation request field to ensure we understand the scope of work needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Inquiry** (Text, Optional): Describe your project in a few sentences, including its purpose, goals, and any relevant background information.
* **Challenges** (Text, Optional): List the specific challenges you are facing with your project, including any obstacles or limitations.
* **Project Details** (Text, Optional): Provide more detailed information about your project, including timelines, resources, and any specific requirements.
* **Consultation Request** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more options that best describe the type of consultation you need:
	+ Option 1: Review and improve existing processes
	+ Option 2: Identify and implement new technologies or tools
	+ Option 3: Develop a comprehensive project plan
* **Schedule Consultation** (Time, Optional): Select a time slot for the consultation, taking into account your availability and our consulting schedule.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when describing your project and challenges to ensure we can provide the best consulting services.
* If you have any additional information or context that may be helpful for the consultation, please attach it as an extra document or mention it in the comments field.
