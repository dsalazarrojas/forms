# Generic Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to request a transcript from an institution. It is intended for students, administrators, or other individuals who need to obtain a transcript for academic or verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **email address** to help the institution contact you with the status of your request.
2. Enter the **Student Number** assigned to you by the institution.
3. Provide your **Student Name** to associate with your request.
4. Choose the type of **Transcript Type** you require (e.g., Official or Academic).
5. Enter the **Date Needed** in the format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure timely production.
6. Add any **Comments** regarding your request, if necessary.
7. Choose whether the **Transcript** has been **Submitted** or not.
8. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user Email**: Enter the email address you wish to use as contact information. This will help the institution reach out to you about the status of your request.
* **Student Number**: Enter the student number assigned to you by the institution. This is a unique identifier associated with your student record.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student record. This will help the institution verify your identity.
* **Transcript Type**: Choose the type of transcript you require (e.g., Official or Academic). This will help the institution produce the correct type of transcript.
* **Date Needed**: Enter the date you need the transcript by in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help the institution schedule the transcript production accordingly.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding your request. This will help the institution understand your needs better.
* **Submitted**: Indicate whether the transcript has been submitted or not. If you select "Not Submitted," please provide additional information in the **Comments** field.
* **Comments** (under **Submitted**): If you select "Not Submitted," enter any additional details that would help the institution understand the status of your transcript.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to avoid delays in the transcript production process.
* If you are requesting multiple transcripts, please submit each request separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the institution's administration for assistance.
