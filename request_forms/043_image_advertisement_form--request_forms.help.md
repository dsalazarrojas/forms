# Image Advertisement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Image Advertisement Form is a tool for marketing teams to create and manage image advertisements. This form helps in capturing vital information about the advertisement, including its type, budget, duration, and dates. The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to meet the diverse needs of different marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed in the next section.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### form_for_image_advertisement (id: 1)
* **Form for Image Advertisement** (Label): This field is the main title of the form.
* **No hint provided**: This field is not required and can be left blank.

### image_advertisement (id: 2)
* **Image Advertisement** (Label): This field is used to describe the advertisement in brief.
* **No hint provided**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
