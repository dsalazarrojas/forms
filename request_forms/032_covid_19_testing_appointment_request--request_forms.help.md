# COVID 19 Testing Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a COVID-19 testing appointment for individuals who wish to get tested for the virus. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from you to schedule an appointment at your preferred location and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your demographic information (page 1: Request Date, First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Phone Number, Email Address, Home Address, City, State, ZIP Code).
2. Select your insurance status and provide insurance information if applicable (page 1).
3. Provide your medical history (page 1: Insurance Status, Reason for Testing, Current Symptoms, Symptom List, Exposure History).
4. Choose your appointment preferences (page 1: Preferred Appointment Date, Preferred Appointment Time, Alternative Appointment Date, Appointment Type Preference, Testing Location Preference).
5. Specify your transportation and language needs (page 1: Transportation Needs, Interpreter Needed, Preferred Language).
6. Add any additional comments or questions you may have (page 1: Additional Comments or Questions).
7. Confirm your contact method for appointment confirmation (page 2: Contact Preference).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: The date you are requesting the appointment for.
* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other).
* **Phone Number**: A contact number we can reach you at.
* **Email Address**: A contact email we can reach you at.
* **Home Address**: Your home address where you can be reached at.
* **City**: The city where you live in.
* **State**: The state where you live in.
* **ZIP Code**: The postal code of your address.
* **Do you have health insurance**: Select true if you have health insurance, false otherwise.
* **Insurance Provider**: If applicable, enter your insurance provider name.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If applicable, enter your insurance policy number.
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: If you have a primary care physician, enter their name.
* **Physician Phone**: If you have a primary care physician, enter their office number.
* **Why do you want to get tested**: Select the primary reason for your testing (Experiencing symptoms, Close contact with positive case, Work requirement, School requirement, Travel requirement, Medical procedure requirement, Event requirement, Personal peace of mind, Healthcare provider recommendation).
* **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms**: Select true if you are currently experiencing symptoms, false otherwise.
* **Which symptoms**: Select all symptoms that you are currently experiencing (Fever or chills, Cough, Shortness of breath, Fatigue, Muscle aches, Headache, Loss of taste or smell, Sore throat, Congestion, Nausea or vomiting, Diarrhea).
* **When did symptoms start**: If you are experiencing symptoms, enter the date they started. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19**: Select true if you have been exposed, false, or not sure.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Enter your preferred appointment date.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: If you have a preferred time, select it from the provided options or leave blank.
* **Alternative Appointment Date**: If you have an alternate date preference, enter it.
* **Appointment Type Preference**: Select the type of testing you prefer (Drive-through testing, Walk-in testing, Scheduled appointment, Home collection).
* **Testing Location Preference**: Select the location you prefer for testing (Main testing center, Satellite location, Mobile testing unit, Any available location).
* **Transportation Needs**: Select yes if you need transportation assistance, no otherwise.
* **Interpreter Needed**: Select yes if you need interpreter services, false otherwise.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Other).
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Do you need any special accommodations**: Enter any special accommodations you may need.
* **Signature Date**: Date of signature.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, select "Not sure" or leave it blank and provide further information later.
* If you require transportation assistance, we will make arrangements to provide it.
* If you need interpreter services, we will arrange for language assistance.
* We will review and respond to your submission as soon as possible.
