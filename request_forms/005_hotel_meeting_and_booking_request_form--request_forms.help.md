# Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form is designed for customers to request a meeting or event at our hotel. This form helps our staff understand the customer's needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of meeting you are requesting: Meeting, Conference, or Event.
2. Select the desired date and time for your meeting.
3. Enter the hotel name where you would like to hold your meeting.
4. Specify the number of guests attending.
5. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person responsible for your meeting.
6. Ensure you accurately fill out all required fields to ensure efficient processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Type (1)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select whether you are requesting a Meeting, Conference, or Event.
* **Meeting Type (2)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of meeting you are interested in, such as Meeting, Conference, or Event.
* **Hotel Name (3)** (`text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of the hotel where you would like to hold your meeting. This will help our team find the best location for your event.
* **Date (4)** (`date`, required/optional): Select the date you would like to hold your meeting. Please ensure the date is in the correct format.
* **Start Time (5)** (`time`, required/optional): Choose the start time for your meeting. This will help our team prepare for the event.
* **End Time (6)** (`time`, required/optional): Select the end time for your meeting.
* **Guests (7)** (`number`, required/optional): Specify the number of guests attending your meeting. This will help our team with seating and arrangements.
* **Contact Person (8)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your meeting. This will ensure we have the right person to contact you with any questions or concerns.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
* Our team will review your request and contact you with further instructions or confirmation.
