# Emacs Holiday Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emacs Holiday Request form is a tool for employees to request holidays for themselves or their team members. This guide will walk you through the steps to fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct team lead for your request.
3. Review and submit the form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (1, Date, Required): Enter the start date of your holiday request in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date** (2, Date, Required): Enter the end date of your holiday request in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Employee Name** (3, Text, Required): Enter the name of the employee requesting the holiday.
* **Team Name** (4, Text, Optional): If you are requesting the holiday for a team member, enter their team name. This field is not required.
* **Manager Name** (5, Text, Required): Enter the name of the manager for the team or employee requesting the holiday.
* **Reason for Leave** (6, Text, Required): Enter a brief explanation for the holiday request.
* **Start Time** (7, Time, Required): Enter the start time of your holiday in the format `HH:MM`.
* **End Time** (8, Time, Required): Enter the end time of your holiday in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Start Date 2** (9, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the holiday in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date 2** (10, Date, Required): Enter the end date of the holiday in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Employee Email** (11, Email, Required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Team Lead** (12, Select One, Required): Select the name of the team lead who will be approving your holiday request.
* **Employee Phone** (13, Text, Required): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Team Lead 2** (14, Select One, Optional): Select the name of the team lead who will be approving your holiday request. This field is optional.
* **Team Lead 3** (15, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the names of the team leads who will be approving your holiday request. This field is optional.

Remember to review and submit the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team lead or manager.
