# Stay On Campus Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Stay On Campus Request Form" is a form designed to request permission to stay on campus beyond the typical check-out date. It's used to provide essential information about the student's stay, including their reason for staying, planned departure time, and contact details for their resident advisor and submitter. This form helps ensure that students can provide accurate and up-to-date information about their stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Id, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the university.
2. Provide a clear reason for requesting to stay on campus, including specific details about your circumstances.
3. Select a time for your planned departure from campus.
4. (Optional) Enter your name and resident advisor's name and phone number, if applicable.
5. (Required) Sign the form digitally with your name and date.
6. (Required) Sign the form digitally with your name and date as the submitter.
7. Enter the submitter's email and select the department they belong to (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id (1)**: Enter your unique university-issued Student Id number.
* **Reason for Staying (2)**: Describe, in detail, the reasons for requesting to stay on campus beyond the typical check-out date.
* **Planned Departure Time (3)**: Select the time you expect to leave campus.
* **Your Name (4)**: Enter your name, if you are not the submitter or resident advisor.
* **Signature (5)**: Sign the form digitally with your name.
* **Resident Advisor (6)**: If you are staying on campus and have a resident advisor, enter their name.
* **Resident Advisor Phone (7)**: If you are staying on campus and have a resident advisor, enter their phone number.
* **Resident Advisor Note (8)**: If you are staying on campus and have a resident advisor, leave a note or any additional information for them.
* **Submitter (9)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form on your behalf.
* **Signature (10)**: Sign the form digitally with your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (11)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Note (12)**: Leave a note or any additional information for the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (13)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Department (14)**: Select the department the submitter belongs to, if applicable.
* **Date (15)**: Enter the date you are signing the form as the submitter.
* **Department (16)**: Select the department you belong to, if you are submitting the form on behalf of another student.
