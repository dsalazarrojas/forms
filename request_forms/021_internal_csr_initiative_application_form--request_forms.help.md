# internal_csr_initiative_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees submit internal projects or initiatives for corporate support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide company information to help the corporate team understand the context of the project.
2. Describe the project goals and objectives.
3. Explain the project's expected impact.
4. List the necessary resources required for the project.
5. Identify the project leads and their roles.
6. Select the project status (active or inactive).
7. Set a realistic project deadline.
8. Provide a budget for the project.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
10. Enter your name and job title for project coordination purposes.
11. Select whether the company will be involved (yes or no).
12. Select the department(s) involved (yes or no).
13. Select the location(s) involved (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information (1)**: Enter your company's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Project Goals (2)**: Describe the purpose and objectives of your project or initiative.
* **Project Impact (3)**: Explain how your project will affect the company or its employees.
* **Resource Needs (4)**: List the resources required for the project, such as equipment, personnel, or budget.
* **Project Leads (5)**: Identify the employee(s) responsible for leading the project.
* **Project Status (6)**: Select whether the project is active or inactive.
* **Project Deadline (7)**: Enter a realistic date for project completion.
* **Budget (8)**: Enter a budget for the project expenses.
* **Email Address (9)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Company Phone (10)**: Enter your company phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employee Name (11)**: Enter your name for project coordination.
* **Employee Job Title (12)**: Enter your job title for project coordination.
* **Company Involvement (13)**: Select whether the company will be involved in the project (yes or no).
* **Department(s) Involved (14)**: Select the department(s) involved in the project (yes or no).
* **Location(s) Involved (15)**: Select the location(s) involved in the project (yes or no).
