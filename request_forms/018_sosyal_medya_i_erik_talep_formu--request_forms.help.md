# Sosyal Medya İçerik Talep Formu - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting social media content, likely for posting or publication purposes. Fill out the form with your details to help our team create and manage social media content effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief note about your request, if needed (Field 1).
2. Enter your name for identification purposes (Field 2: Name).
3. Provide your email address for communication (Field 3: Email).
4. Share your phone number for contact (Field 4: Phone).
5. Schedule a specific date and time for your social media content (Field 5 and 6: Date and Time).
6. Select one of the provided options for a specific reason or context (Field 7: Select One).
7. Choose multiple options as needed for your request (Field 8: Select Multiple).
8. Provide additional text for your social media content or any other information (Field 9: Text).
9. If necessary, specify an integer value for numerical data (Field 10: Integer).
10. If needed, enter a decimal value for precision (Field 11: Decimal).
11. Select a specific date for your content publication (Field 12: Date).
