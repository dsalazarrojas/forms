# Bathroom Store Returns Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bathroom Store Returns Request Form is a simple, step-by-step guide to help you initiate a return or exchange for a product purchased from our store. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Full Name** as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the **Order Number** found on your receipt or confirmation email.
3. Select the **Date of Purchase** when you purchased the item.
4. Enter your **Email Address** for status updates on your return.
5. Select the **Item(s) to be Returned** by entering the item name and SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) as it appears on your receipt.
6. Choose the **Reason for Return** from the provided options.
7. Select the **Current Condition of the Item** from the provided options.
8. Select whether the original **Package is available?**
9. Choose the **Desired Resolution** from the provided options.
10. Select how you would like to **Return the Item** from the provided options.
11. Confirm that you understand and agree to the **Returns Policy**.
12. Finally, select the **Date of Request** when you are submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name**: This field is required to verify your identity. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number found on your receipt or confirmation email to help us locate your order.
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the item to help us track the purchase date.
* **Email Address**: This field is required for us to send status updates on your return.
* **Item(s) to be Returned**: Enter the item name and SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) as it appears on your receipt to help us locate the item.
* **Reason for Return**: Select one of the provided options to explain why you are requesting a return or exchange.
* **Current Condition of the Item**: Select the current condition of the item to help us process your return.
* **Is original packaging available?**: Select whether the original packaging is available for return.
* **Desired Resolution**: Choose the resolution you would like to have for your return (Full Refund, Store Credit, Exchange for Same Item, or Exchange for Different Item).
* **How will you return the item?**: Select how you would like to return the item (Drop off at Store, Prepaid Shipping Label Request, or Schedule a Pickup).
* **Returns Policy Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the returns policy.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are submitting the form to help us track the request.

Note: All fields marked as required are compulsory to complete the form. Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your return request is processed efficiently.
