# nail_it_with_maggie_color_street_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nail_it_with_maggie_color_street_order_form is designed to collect information from customers who want to place an order for color products or services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and click "Submit" when you are finished.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Details** (order_details, text, optional): Describe the details of your order.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, optional): Enter the date you want to place the order for.
* **Order Time** (order_time, time, optional): Enter the time you want to place the order for.
* **Customer Message** (customer_message, text, optional): Enter any additional message or comments you have for the order.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any trouble with the form, feel free to contact us.
* We will respond to your order as soon as possible after receiving it.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and will not be shared with any third parties.
