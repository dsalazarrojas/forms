# Certification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Certification Request Form is used to request verification of enrollment, degree, or other educational information from your medical school. This form helps the registrar's office process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you are requesting the correct type of certification needed.
3. Provide accurate information for the recipient and delivery method.
4. Review and confirm your consent to release information to the specified recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name (1)** (`student_full_name`, required/optional): Enter your full legal name as recorded in medical school.
* **Student ID Number (2)** (`student_id_number`, required/optional): Your medical school identification number.
* **Current Email Address (3)** (`current_email_address`, required/optional): Enter your current email address for updates and confirmation.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number_contact`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Name of Medical School (5)** (`name_of_medical_school`, required/optional): Enter the full name of your medical school or institution.
* **Year of Graduation (6)** (`year_of_graduation`, required/optional): Enter the year you graduated or expect to graduate.
* **Degree Type (7)** (`medical_degree_type`, required/optional): Select the degree you are pursuing or have earned (e.g., MD, DO, MBBS, etc.).
* **Current Enrollment Status (8)** (`enrollment_status_current`, required/optional): Select your current status at the medical school (e.g., enrolled, on leave, graduated, etc.).
* **Type of Certification Needed (9)** (`type_of_certification_needed`, required/optional): Select what type of certification is needed (e.g., verification of enrollment, verification of degree, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request (10)** (`purpose_of_request`, required/optional): Select the purpose of the certification request (e.g., residency application, state licensing board, etc.).
* **Receiving Organization Name (11)** (`organization_recipient_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization that will receive the certification.
* **Recipient Mailing Address (12)** (`recipient_mailing_address`, required/optional): Enter the full address where the document should be sent.
* **Recipient Email for Digital Delivery (13)** (`recipient_email_digital_delivery`, required/optional): Enter the email address for electronic delivery if accepted by the organization.
* **Delivery Method Preference (14)** (`delivery_method_preference`, required/optional): Select how you want the document delivered (e.g., electronic secure upload, regular mail, etc.).
* **Consent to Release Information (15)** (`student_consent_authorization`, required/optional): Select whether you consent to release your information to the specified recipient.
* **Request Submission Date (16)** (`date_of_request_submission`, required/optional): Enter today's date for request submission.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions (17)** (`additional_comments_request`, required/optional): Enter any special details for the registrar's office.
