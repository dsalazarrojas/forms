# script_pitch_meeting_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a meeting for a script pitch, providing an opportunity to showcase and discuss a project with the relevant team or department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide clear and concise information about your script or project in the "Script Details" field.
2. Write a brief summary of your project in the "Logline" field.
3. Choose a suitable "Preferred Meeting Time" that works for you and the review team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Script Details** (`script_details`, Text, Optional): This field allows you to provide a brief description or synopsis of your script or project.
* **Logline** (`logline`, Text, Optional): A logline is a one-line summary of your project, providing a quick and engaging overview.
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, Time, Optional): Select a time slot that you are available to meet with the review team, taking into account their schedules and availability.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep your script details and logline concise and engaging.
* Choose a meeting time that allows sufficient time for discussion and review.
* Double-check your availability and the review team's schedule to ensure a smooth meeting process.
