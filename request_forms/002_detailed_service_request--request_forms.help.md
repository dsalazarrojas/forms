# Detailed Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about service requests from customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the correct service type from the options provided.
4. Enter the appointment date and time in the respective fields.
5. Provide a brief description of the service required in the "Service Details" field.
6. Enter the contact phone number, email, and any additional notes.
7. Enter the contact's name, phone number, email, and any additional notes.
8. Select the service status and provide any additional notes.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the service required.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of service required from the options provided.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the appointment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the appointment in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the service required.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact email.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the service location.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the contact.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the service from the options provided.
* **Service Status Notes** (`service_status_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the service status.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the service request in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the service request in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Service Request Details** (`service_request_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the service request.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the service location.
* **Service Status Updated** (`service_status_updated`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the service update from the options provided.
* **Service Status Updated Notes** (`service_status_updated_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the service status update.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer email.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language in the "Service Details" and "Service Request Details" fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the service provider for assistance.
