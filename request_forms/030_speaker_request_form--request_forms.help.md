# Speaker Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Speaker Request Form is used to gather essential information about a speaker for various events. This form is essential for organizing and communicating with speakers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the speaker's name, company name, email, phone number, and URL.
2. Choose whether or not the speaker is available for topics 1 and 2.
3. Provide a brief speaker bio and upload any relevant images, videos, or audio files.
4. Select the correct event time and date for the speaker.
5. Enter the location and any additional information about the speaker.
6. Ensure that the assigned tool and form category are correct.
7. Provide a description of the form and any additional notes.
8. Upload any additional files related to the form.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter the speaker's name as they would like to be referred to during the event.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the speaker's company or organization name.
- **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, required): Enter the speaker's email address for contact purposes.
- **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, required): Enter the speaker's phone number.
- **Speaker URL** (`speaker_url`, text, required): Enter the speaker's personal or professional URL.
- **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, note, required): Enter a brief biography or description of the speaker (less than 1000 characters).
- **Speaker Topics** (`speaker_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select the topics the speaker is available for (Yes or No).
- **Speaker Topics 2** (`speaker_topics_2`, select_one, required): Select the specific topic 2 the speaker is available for (Yes or No).
- **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, text, required): Upload an image of the speaker.
- **Speaker Video** (`speaker_video`, text, required): Upload a video of the speaker.
- **Speaker Audio** (`speaker_audio`, text, required): Upload audio files of the speaker.
- **Speaker Date** (`speaker_date`, date, required): Select the date for the speaker's engagement.
- **Speaker Time** (`speaker_time`, time, required): Select the time for the speaker's engagement.
- **Speaker Location** (`speaker_location`, text, required): Enter the location for the speaker's engagement.
- **Speaker Additional Info** (`speaker_additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the speaker.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Select the tool assigned to the speaker.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
- **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required): Select the category for the form.
- **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the form.
- **Output File** (`form_output_file`, text, required): Upload any additional files related to the form.
- **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the form.
- **Form Created At** (`form_created_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated and not required.
- **Form Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated and not required.
