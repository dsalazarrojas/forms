# CPD Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The CPD Request Form is used to submit requests for Continuing Professional Development (CPD) training or professional growth opportunities. It is a necessary step in the process for requesting funding or approval for training events that align with your role and responsibilities in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the CPD Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*). Required fields are marked with "required: true" in the form schema.
2. Enter your personal details, including your full name and employee ID.
3. Select your current department from the dropdown options.
4. Provide detailed information about the training request, including:
	* The title of the CPD course or training event
	* The name of the organization or provider
	* The proposed start and end dates
	* The total estimated cost, including any associated travel or materials
	* A professional development rationale that explains how this training will benefit your role and the organization
5. If applicable, mention which specific performance goal this training event aligns with
6. Finally, enter your line manager's name for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it is recorded in our HR system. This is a required field to ensure proper identification and record-keeping.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID assigned to you by the HR department. This is also a required field for tracking purposes.
* **Department**: Select your current department from the options provided. This helps us to identify which department you are representing when requesting CPD training.
* **CPD Course Title**: Provide the full name of the course or training event you are requesting. This gives us a clear understanding of what you are seeking and helps us to evaluate its relevance to your role and responsibilities.
* **Training Provider**: Enter the name of the organization or provider offering the training. This is a required field as it helps us to understand the credibility and quality of the training event.
* **Proposed Start Date** and **Proposed End Date**: Provide the start and end dates for the training event. This is required to plan for resource allocation and scheduling.
* **Total Estimated Cost**: Include all associated costs such as travel, materials, or any other expenses related to the training event. This helps us to evaluate the budget implications.
* **Professional Development Rationale**: Write a clear explanation of how this training will benefit your role and the organization. This is a required field to demonstrate the value and relevance of the CPD request.
* **Alignment with Annual Goals**: If applicable, specify which specific performance goal this training event aligns with. This helps us to evaluate the connection between your request and our organizational goals.
* **Line Manager Name**: Enter your line manager's name for approval and validation of your CPD request. This is a required field as it ensures that your line manager is aware of your request and can provide their endorsement.

**Tips**:

* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with approval.
* If you are requesting a large or complex training event, please provide supporting documentation or references where possible.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form or the CPD request process, contact your HR representative or supervisor for assistance.
