# Painting Commission Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to submit their work for potential commission by art collectors, galleries, or other interested parties. It collects information about the artwork, artist, and commission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Artist Name** and **Artist Email** in the respective fields. These will help us contact you about your commission request.
2. Provide your **Artist Phone** number so we can reach out to you for further discussion if needed.
3. Select the type of commission you are offering (e.g., **Original**, **Print**, or **Digital**).
4. Enter a brief description of the artwork and any relevant image details.
5. Enter the commission cost associated with the artwork.
6. You can **Upload Image** and **Upload Art** files to showcase your artwork, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on any future correspondence about your commission.
* **Artist Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication about your commission.
* **Artist Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you for further discussion if needed.
* **Commission Type**: Select the type of commission you are offering:
	+ **Original**: A one-of-a-kind artwork created specifically for this commission.
	+ **Print**: A reproduction of the artwork, such as a print or poster.
	+ **Digital**: A digital version of the artwork, such as a JPEG or PNG file.
* **Image Details**: Enter a brief description of the artwork, including any relevant details about the medium, size, and style.
* **Art Description**: Enter a more detailed description of the artwork, including any inspiration or story behind it.
* **Commission Cost**: Enter the cost associated with the commission, including any relevant fees or taxes.
* **Upload Image**: You can **Upload Image** files to showcase your artwork, but this is optional.
* **Upload Art**: You can **Upload Art** files to showcase your artwork, but this is optional.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have a clear and concise description of your artwork.
- Proofread your form submission carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
