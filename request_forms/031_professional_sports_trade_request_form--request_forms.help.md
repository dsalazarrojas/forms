# professional_sports_trade_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The professional sports trade request form is designed to collect information for trade proposals between teams in the sports industry. This form will guide you through the process of submitting a trade request, including details about the trade proposal, dates, values, and acceptance status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the team requesting the trade.
2. Enter the trade proposal details.
3. Specify the trade date.
4. Enter the trade value.
5. Describe the trade terms.
6. Add any additional notes for the trade requester.
7. Set the trade acceptance status (Accept, Reject, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team** (Team): Enter the name of the team requesting the trade.
* **Trade Proposal** (Trade Proposal): Describe the trade proposal, including the specifics of what you are offering or requesting.
* **Trade Details** (Trade Details): Provide additional information about the trade, such as any conditions or terms.
* **Trade Date** (Trade Date): Select the date of the trade.
* **Trade Value** (Trade Value): Enter the value associated with the trade.
* **Trade Terms** (Trade Terms): Outline the terms of the trade.
* **Trade Requester Notes** (Trade Requester Notes): Add any additional comments or notes for the trade requester.
* **Trade Acceptance Status** (Trade Acceptance Status): Select the acceptance status of the trade (Accept, Reject, or Pending).

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary authorization and clearance to submit a trade request.
* Verify that the trade proposal is clear and concise to avoid any confusion.
* Be aware of the trade value and terms to ensure a fair and reasonable trade.
* Respond to any additional notes or comments from the team offering the trade.
* Update the trade acceptance status as soon as possible after receiving a decision.
