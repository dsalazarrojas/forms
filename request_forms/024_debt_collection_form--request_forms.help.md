# Debt Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debt Collection Form is used to gather essential details from debtors to facilitate effective debt collection. Please ensure you are filling out this form for the correct purposes and do not create duplicate entries for the same debt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as required (e.g., Contact Method).
2. Enter your details accurately, as the form will be used to communicate with the debtor.
3. Be as thorough as possible in providing the debt description and other relevant details.
4. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to collect debt information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Method** (id: 8, required): This field is used to select the most suitable method to contact the debtor (e.g., Phone, Email, or other). Choose one of the available options or select "Other (please specify)" to enter your preferred contact method.
* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter the debtor's first name.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Enter the debtor's last name.
* **Email Address (id: 3)**: Enter the debtor's email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter the debtor's phone number.
* **Debt Description (id: 5)**: Provide a detailed description of the debt, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Total Amount (id: 6)**: Enter the total amount of the debt.
* **Payment Terms (id: 7)**: Specify the payment terms, such as due date or payment method.
* **Other (please specify) (id: 9)**: If the debtor has a preferred contact method not listed, enter it here.
* **Total Amount** (id: 6): Enter the total amount of the debt.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate in filling out the form to ensure effective communication with the debtor.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, consult with your colleagues or superiors before proceeding.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorities to collect debt information before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
