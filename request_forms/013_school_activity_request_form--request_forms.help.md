# School Activity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Activity Request Form is used to submit requests for school activities, events, and other related matters. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting.
3. Ensure that you have not submitted a similar request previously.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (`main`, text, **optional**): This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of your request.
* **General info** (`general_info`, text, **optional**): In this field, please provide any additional information that is not covered in the main section.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, **optional**): Specify the name of the event or activity you are requesting.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date when the event or activity is scheduled to start.
* **End date** (`end_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date when the event or activity is scheduled to end.
* **Number of participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, **optional**): If applicable, specify the number of people participating in the event or activity.
* **Location** (`location`, text, **optional**): Provide the venue or location where the event or activity will take place.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the person handling the event or activity.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address of the person handling the event or activity.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the person handling the event or activity.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form to avoid delays in processing your request.
- Ensure that you have all necessary contact information to facilitate communication and follow-up.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
