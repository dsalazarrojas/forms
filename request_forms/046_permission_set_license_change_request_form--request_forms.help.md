# Permission Set License Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permission Set License Change Request Form is used to submit requests for changes to your permission sets. This form collects necessary information to understand your reason for the change, who should be involved in the decision-making process, and what date the request was submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name in the "Full name" field.
2.  Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your job title in the "Job title" field.
4.  Choose the type of request you are submitting (IT or Manager).
5.  Enter the date you are requesting the change.
6.  Provide the reason for your request in the "Reason for request" field.
7.  Select the names of the approvers who should review your request.
8.  Enter the email address of the approver(s).
9.  (Optional) If you have any additional comments for the approver, you can enter them in the "Approver Comments" field.
10. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
12. (Optional) If you are requesting the change for your company, please enter its name in the "Company" field.
13. (Optional) You can also select the department you are requesting the change for from the "Department (2)" dropdown menu.
14. If you have an alternative job title, you can enter it in the "Other job title" field.
15. You can also enter your job title in the "Job title (3)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This is required.
- **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the list. This is required.
- **Job title**: Enter your job title as it appears in your official documents. This is required.
- **Request type**: Choose whether your request is IT-related or manager-related. This is required.
- **Request date**: Enter the date you are submitting this request. This is required.
- **Reason for request**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for your request. This is required.
- **Approver Name**: Select the name(s) of the person who should review your request. This is required.
- **Approver Email**: Enter the email address of the person who should review your request. This is required.
- **Submission Date**: (Optional) Enter the date this request was submitted.
- **Approver Comments**: (Optional) If you have any additional comments for the approver, you can enter them here.
- **Submitter Name**: Enter your name. This is required.
- **Email**: Enter your email address. This is required.
- **Company**: (Optional) If you are requesting the change for your company, enter its name.
- **Department (2)**: (Optional) Select the department you are requesting the change for from the dropdown menu.
- **Jobtitle**: (Optional) Enter your job title.
- **Other job title**: (Optional) If you have an alternative job title, enter it here.
- **Job title (3)**: (Optional) You can enter your job title again if needed.
- **Approver**: (Optional) This field is currently not used.
- **Submitter**: (Optional) This field is currently not used.

Tips:
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any unnecessary delays.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
