# Financial Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals or entities seeking financial assistance. It will help our team assess their eligibility and provide support accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your income and employment status.
2. Provide details about your employment status, including your employment type, start date, and end date (if applicable).
3. Select your current financial status and any financial institutions you are involved with.
4. Enter your contact number and email address (if applicable).
5. If applicable, provide details about your employment type and status for up to three instances.
