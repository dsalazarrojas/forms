# jennas_color_street_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to report their observations of the street's color palette.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your name for reference purposes.
2. Enter the quantity of the color(s) observed.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the color is present on the street.
4. Add any additional notes about the color.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
7. Choose the date and time of your observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name** (text, required): This is your name for reference purposes.
* **quantity** (number, optional): Enter the number of times the color appears on the street.
* **color** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate the color is present on the street, 'No' otherwise.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the color.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your observation.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your observation.
* **select_one** (select_one, optional): This field is currently not used, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **select_multiple** (select_multiple, optional): This field is currently not used, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
