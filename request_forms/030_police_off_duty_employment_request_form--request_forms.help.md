# Police Off Duty Employment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for requesting time off for employment-related duties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form title and officer information, including your name and department. Ensure you're accurate with your information to avoid mistakes.
2. Indicate whether you are in the police department or not. This is crucial for the department to process your request correctly.
3. Provide your requested start and end dates for your time off.
4. Specify the length of your shift, if applicable.
5. Give details about your shift, if applicable.
6. Explain your reason for requesting time off.
7. Add any additional contact information, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **officer** (text, required): Enter your officer number or ID.
* **officer_name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your police department ID.
* **department** (select multiple, required): Check if you are in the police department. This will help the department process your request correctly.
* **request_date** (date, required): Enter the date you're requesting time off.
* **return_date** (date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **shift_length** (text, required): Enter the length of your requested shift, if applicable.
* **shift_details** (text, required): Provide details about your shift, if applicable.
* **request_reason** (text, required): Explain why you're requesting time off.
* **contact_info** (note, required): Add any additional contact information, if necessary.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
- Provide enough details to facilitate processing your request efficiently.
