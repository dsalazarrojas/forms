# Fire Rated Attic Access Hatch Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to fire-rated hatches for the attic space. Please use this form to provide the necessary information for the installation and delivery of the hatches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the location of the hatch in the `hatch_location` field.
* Provide the width and height of the hatch in the `hatch_width` and `hatch_height` fields, respectively.
* Select the material of the hatch from the `material_id` dropdown menu.
* Enter the thickness of the hatch in the `thickness` field.
* Provide the fire resistance rating of the hatch in the `fire_resistance_rating` field.
* Describe any special installation requirements in the `installation_requirements` field.
* Enter the delivery date required for the installation in the `delivery_date` field.
* Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the installation in the `name`, `email` and `phone` fields.
* Describe any specific delivery instructions in the `delivery_instructions` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hatch_location**: Enter the location where the hatch will be installed, such as "attic space, 3rd floor".
* **hatch_width**: Enter the width of the hatch in feet.
* **hatch_height**: Enter the height of the hatch in feet.
* **material_id**: Select the type of material for the hatch from the dropdown menu.
* **thickness**: Enter the thickness of the hatch in inches.
* **fire_resistance_rating**: Enter the fire resistance rating of the hatch, such as "1 hour" or "2 hour".
* **installation_requirements**: Describe any special requirements for the installation, such as electrical or plumbing connections.
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date when the hatch is required for delivery.
* **installers**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the installation.
* **delivery_instructions**: Describe any specific instructions for the delivery process.
* **name**: Enter the name of the person requesting the hatch.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the hatch.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting the hatch.
