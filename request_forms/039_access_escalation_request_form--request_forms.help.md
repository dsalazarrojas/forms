# Access Escalation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Access Escalation Request Form is used to request elevated access to specific areas or data systems within the organization. Fill out the form below to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "Requestor Details" section.
2. Describe your current access permissions in the "Current Access" section.
3. Specify the new access level you are requesting in the "Escalation Request" section.
4. Choose which areas or systems you need access to in the "Specific Areas or Data Systems" section.
5. Select the duration of the elevated access in the "Duration" section.
6. If the access is temporary, choose an expiry date in the "Expiry Date" section.
7. Provide a detailed business justification for the escalation in the "Business Justification" section.
8. Indicate if the request is time-critical in the "Is this request time-critical?" section.
9. Choose your manager's email address where the request will be sent for approval.
10. Acknowledge that you have read the Elevated Access Policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as per official records.
- **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as per your role within the organization.
- **Employee or Staff ID** (`staff_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee or staff ID.
- **Describe your current access permissions** (`existing_access_levels`, text, required): Briefly describe what access you currently have.
- **What elevated permissions are you requesting?** (`requested_access_level`, text, required): Specify the new access level you are requesting.
- **Specific areas or data systems** (`areas_requiring_access`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas or systems you need access to (e.g. Financial Systems, HR PII, Research Data, etc.).
- **Duration** (`duration_of_escalation`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the elevated access (Temporary, Permanent).
- **If temporary, when should access expire?** (`expiry_date`, date, optional): Choose an expiry date for project-based access.
- **Provide a detailed business justification for this escalation** (`justification`, text, required): Give a clear reason for the escalation.
- **Is this request time-critical?** (`urgent_request`, select_one, required): Indicate if the request has a time constraint.
- **Supervisor or Manager Email for Approval** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address where the request will be sent for approval.
- **I acknowledge that I have read the Elevated Access Policy** (`policy_compliance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the policy.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise justification for your escalation request.
- Ensure that you have the necessary approval from your manager before submitting this form.
- Review the fields carefully to avoid missing any required information.
