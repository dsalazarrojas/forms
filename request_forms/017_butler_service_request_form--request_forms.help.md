# Butler Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Butler Service Request Form is designed to capture detailed information from guests regarding their service requests. This form is used to ensure that the butler staff can provide personalized services to meet the guests' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the Guest Name field with the full name of the guest.
2.  Enter the Room Number where the guest is staying.
3.  Provide the Contact Phone Number for the guest.
4.  Enter the Contact Email address for the guest.
5.  Select the Type of Service Requested from the provided options (Room Service, Housekeeping, Laundry, Concierge, Dining Arrangement, Transportation, Personal Shopping, or Other).
6.  Specify the Requested Service Date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7.  Select the Requested Service Time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
8.  Choose the Time Flexibility for the service, with options being Exact Time Required, Within 30 Minutes, Within 1 Hour, or Flexible.
9.  Enter a brief description of the Detailed Service Request, specifying exactly what the guest needs.
10.  If applicable, describe any Special Requirements or Preferences.
11.  If food service is involved, specify any Dietary Restrictions.
12.  If the guest has a specific Budget Consideration, enter it here.
13.  Select the Urgency Level of the request, with options being Immediate, High Priority, Standard, or Low Priority.
14.  If the guest has any Previous Arrangements related to this request, enter them here.
15.  If the guest has a preference for a specific Butler or Staff Member, enter their name here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the guest.
* **Room Number** (Optional): Enter the room number where the guest is staying.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Required): Enter the phone number where the guest can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the email address for the guest.
* **Type of Service Requested** (Required): Select the primary service needed from the provided options.
* **Requested Service Date** (Required): Enter the date when the service is needed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Requested Service Time** (Required): Select the preferred time for the service in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Time Flexibility** (Required): Choose how flexible the guest is with their requested time.
* **Detailed Service Request** (Required): Describe exactly what the guest needs.
* **Special Requirements or Preferences** (Optional): Describe any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): If food service is involved, specify any dietary restrictions.
* **Budget Consideration** (Optional): If the guest has a specific budget consideration, enter it here.
* **Urgency Level** (Required): Select the urgency level of the request from the provided options.
* **Previous Arrangements** (Optional): If the guest has any previous arrangements related to this request, enter them here.
* **Preferred Butler or Staff Member** (Optional): If the guest has a preference for a specific butler or staff member, enter their name here.
