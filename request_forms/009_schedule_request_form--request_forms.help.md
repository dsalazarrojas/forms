# Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Request Form is designed to collect schedule requests from users, which are then used to track and manage schedule changes. This form helps to ensure that schedule requests are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields, which are marked as required.
* Enter your start and end dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 12/01/2022).
* Select the schedule type that best suits your request (Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
* Enter your employee name and any other relevant information.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Request**: Enter a brief description of your schedule request.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your schedule request (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your schedule request (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Schedule Type**: Select the type of schedule you are requesting (Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
* **Employee**: Enter your employee name or ID to associate your request with your account.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure efficient processing of your schedule request.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact our support team.
* You can save your progress at any time and continue later if needed.
