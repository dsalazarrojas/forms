# store_opening_permit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The store opening permit request form is used by store owners to submit their application for a store opening permit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the store name, address, and other required fields accurately and completely.
2. Provide your store owner's details, including name, title, and contact information.
3. Enter the store's launch date, closure date, and other relevant dates.
4. Specify the store's operational hours, capacity, and equipment needs.
5. Confirm the store's launch status and provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your store.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, required): Provide the store's address.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's email address.
* **Store Owner Name** (`store_owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the store owner.
* **Store Owner Title** (`store_owner_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the store owner.
* **Store Launch Date** (`store_launch_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the store is launching.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `text`, optional): Specify the store's operational hours.
* **Store Capacity** (`store_capacity`, `number`, required): Enter the store's capacity.
* **Store Floor Plan** (`store_floor_plan`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of the store's floor plan.
* **Store Equipment List** (`store_equipment_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the store needs any specific equipment.
* **Store Permit Issued Date** (`store_permit_issued_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the store permit was issued.
* **Store Opening Date** (`store_opening_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the store is opening.
* **Store Closure Date** (`store_closure_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the store will close.
* **Store Owners** (`store_owners`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify if there are any other store owners.
* **Store License Number** (`store_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the store's license number.
* **Store License Type** (`store_license_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of store license.
* **Store License Issuer** (`store_license_issuer`, `text`, optional): Enter the issuer of the store license.
* **Store Launch Cost** (`store_launch_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the cost of launching the store.
* **Store Launch Fund** (`store_launch_fund`, `text`, optional): Enter the launch fund.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required): Enter the store's location.
* **Store Description** (`store_description`, `text`, required): Provide a description of the store.
* **Store Launch Status** (`store_launch_status`, `text`, optional): Enter the launch status of the store.
* **Store Launch Note** (`store_launch_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Store Launch Image** (`store_launch_image`, `text`, optional): Upload a launch image or provide a link to it.
