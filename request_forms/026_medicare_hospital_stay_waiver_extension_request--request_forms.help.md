# Medicare Hospital Stay Waiver Extension Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients seeking to extend their Medicare hospital stay waiver.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Patient Information
- **Patient Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Contact Number** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's contact number for follow-up purposes.

### Step 2: Enter Hospital Information
- **Hospital Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is currently staying.
- **Address** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the hospital's address for easy contact or reference.
- **Contact Number** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the hospital's contact number for follow-up or inquiries.

### Step 3: Enter Doctor Information
- **Doctor/Healthcare Provider Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the doctor or healthcare provider in charge of the patient's care.
- **Title** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor or healthcare provider's title (e.g., MD, Dr., RN).
- **Contact Number** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter the doctor or healthcare provider's contact number for follow-up or inquiries.

### Step 4: Describe Request Details
- **Request Details** (`title`, `text`, required): Explain the reason for the waiver extension request.

### Step 5: Attach Supporting Documentation
- **Supporting Documentation** (`title`, `text`, required): Attach any relevant medical records or evidence to support the request.

### Step 6: Sign and Date
- **Signature** (`title`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm its accuracy and authenticity.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Attach clear and legible supporting documentation where necessary.
- Proofread the form before submitting to prevent errors or inaccuracies.
