# table_extension_slide_repair_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to request repairs for tables that require extension slides. The form is used to gather details about the customer, table, and repair requirements, as well as appointment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter customer information**: Fill in the customer name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. **Provide table details**: Describe the table you would like to repair, including its location, size, and any specific features.
3. **Provide repair information**: Specify the type of repair required (e.g. replacement, re-glue, re-attach) and any relevant notes.
4. **Schedule an appointment**: Provide your preferred dates and times for the repair appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Name, Contact Number, Email)**: This section is for entering the customer's name, phone number, and email address. Please ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date so we can contact you to schedule a repair.
* **Table Details**: Describe the table that requires repair, including its size, material, and any specific features that may be relevant to the repair.
* **Repair Information**: Specify the type of repair required (e.g. replacement, re-glue, re-attach) and any additional notes or instructions.
* **Appointment Information**: Choose a preferred date and time for the repair appointment. Our team will contact you to confirm the schedule.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Our team will review your request and be in touch to schedule a repair at your preferred appointment time.
