# Resident Complaint Summary Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Residency Complaint Summary Request Form gathers information about a resident's complaint to facilitate efficient processing and response from the management team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you've filled out all required fields.
2. Answer questions according to your experience or information about the incident.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Complaint Summary Request Form** (`resident_complaint_summary_request_form`, Select Multiple): 
   Select one or more reasons why the resident is filing this complaint. This could be "Yes" for one or more issues.
* **Resident Complaint Type** (`resident_complaint_type`, Select One): 
   Choose the type of complaint that best describes the issue. Options include Maintenance Issue, Noise Complaint, or Other.
* **Resident Complaint Description** (`resident_complaint_description`, Text): 
   Provide a detailed description of the resident's complaint, including the incident's circumstances.
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, Text): 
   Enter the name of the resident filing the complaint.
* **Resident Contact Method** (`resident_contact_method`, Select One): 
   Choose how the resident would like to be contacted, such as by phone or email.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, Text): 
   Enter the name of the staff member involved in the incident or responsible for addressing it.
* **Staff Contact Method** (`staff_contact_method`, Select One): 
   Choose how the staff member would like to be contacted, such as by phone or email.
* **Staff Department** (`staff_department`, Text): 
   Enter the department of the staff member involved in the incident or responsible for addressing it.
* **Resident Address** (`resident_address`, Text): 
   Enter the resident's address, if necessary.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Date): 
   Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, Time): 
   Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, Text): 
   Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, Text): 
   Enter any relevant witness statements or additional comments about the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, Select Multiple): 
   Check any actions taken to address the resident's complaint.
