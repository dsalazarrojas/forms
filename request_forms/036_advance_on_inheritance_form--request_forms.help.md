# Advance On Inheritance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advance On Inheritance Form is used to request an advance on an inheritance, which is a payment made to the beneficiary before the final distribution of the estate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your beneficiary's full name in the "Beneficiary Full Name" field.
2. Describe your relationship to the deceased in the "Relationship to Deceased" field.
3. If applicable, provide the contact information for your legal counsel in the "Legal Counsel Contact Info" field.
4. Enter the name or case number of the estate in the "Estate Name or Case Number" field.
5. Enter the total estimated value of the inheritance in the "Total Estimated Inheritance Value" field.
6. Specify the amount you are requesting as an advance in the "Requested Advance Amount" field.
7. Provide a brief explanation for the advance request in the "Primary Reason for Advance Request" field.
8. Confirm that you understand how an advance on the inheritance will affect your final distribution in the "Understanding of Reduction" field.
9. Confirm that you accept the repayment terms if the estate is insufficient to cover the advance in the "Repayment Terms Accepted" field.
10. Sign the form with your full name in the "Beneficiary Signature" field.
11. Enter the date of your request in the "Date of Request" field.
12. If applicable, provide verification from the executor or notary in the "Executor and Notary Verification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Beneficiary Full Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the person or entity inheriting the estate.
* **Relationship to Deceased** (Required): Describe your connection to the deceased, e.g., spouse, child, or heir.
* **Legal Counsel Contact Info** (Optional): If you have legal representation, provide their contact details for communication purposes.
* **Estate Name or Case Number** (Required): Enter the name or case number of the estate for reference.
* **Total Estimated Inheritance Value** (Required): Enter the approximate value of the inheritance.
* **Requested Advance Amount** (Required): Specify the amount you are requesting as an advance.
* **Primary Reason for Advance Request** (Required): Briefly explain the need for an early distribution.
* **Understanding of Reduction** (Required): Confirm that you comprehend how an advance will affect your final distribution.
* **Repayment Terms Accepted** (Required): Agree to the repayment terms if the estate is insufficient to cover the advance.
* **Beneficiary Signature** (Required): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date of Request** (Required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Executor and Notary Verification** (Optional): Provide verification from the executor or notary for office use.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are not duplicating a previous request or that the request is not already being processed.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, consult with the relevant authority for guidance.
