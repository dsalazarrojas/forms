# Mileage Credit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Credit Request Form is used to request mileage credit for business trips or other work-related travel. To request mileage credit, you must be an employee who has actually taken a business trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Employee Name, Travel Dates, Travel Location, Trip Purpose, and Mileage Amount.
2. Provide any supporting documentation for the trip (in the Documentation field).
3. Select the approval status for your request.
4. Specify if prophets were involved in the trip.
5. Choose whether mileage records are accurate.
6. Add any comments or explanations for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: This is the name of the employee requesting mileage credit. Please enter your own name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Employee ID**: This is the employee ID number associated with the employee name. If you don't know your employee ID number, please check with HR or your manager.
* **Travel Dates**: This is the date range of your business trip. Please enter the start and end dates of your trip in MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/2024 to 01/03/2024).
* **Travel Location**: This is the location of your business trip. Please enter the city or city-state pair where you traveled (e.g., New York, NY).
* **Trip Purpose**: This is a brief description of the reason for your business trip (e.g., conference, meeting, client visit).
* **Documentation**: Please upload any supporting documents for your trip, such as receipts, itineraries, or emails confirming your travel.
* **Mileage Amount**: This is the total mileage you are requesting credit for. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 100).
* **Approval**: Choose whether your manager has approved your request. If you're unsure, check with your manager.
* **Prophets 1**: If prophets were involved in your trip, please specify the name or role of the prophet(s).
* **Prophets 2**: This is another field for specifying any additional prophets involved in your trip.
* **Prophets 3**: Another field for specifying any additional prophets involved in your trip.
* **Mileage Records**: If mileage records are accurate, please select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No" if they are not.
* **Comments**: This is an optional field for adding any additional comments or explanations for your request.
