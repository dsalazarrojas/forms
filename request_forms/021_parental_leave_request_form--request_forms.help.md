# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parental Leave Request Form is used to request time off for parental leave. This form is required for all employees who need to take time off for family-related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** (1) in the first field. This is your full name as it appears on your employee badge.
2. Enter your **Job Title** (2) in the second field. This is the position you currently hold within the company.
3. If you have a specific **Date of Leaving** (3), please enter it in the third field. If you do not have a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
4. If you know the **Returning Date** (4), please enter it in the fourth field. If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.
5. In the fifth field, please provide a brief **Reason for Leaving** (5). This should include information about your family situation or any other relevant details.
6. Enter your department in the **Department** (6) field. This will help us to process your request accurately.
7. Select **Manager Approval** (7) as 'Yes' or 'No' in the last field. This indicates whether your manager has approved your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1): Your full name as it appears on your employee badge.
* **Job Title** (2): The position you currently hold within the company.
* **Date of Leaving** (3): If you have a specific date for your leave, please enter it here. If you do not have a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Returning Date** (4): If you know when you will return to work, please enter the date here. If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Reason for Leaving** (5): A brief description of your family situation or any other relevant details.
* **Department** (6): Enter your department to help us process your request accurately.
* **Manager Approval** (7): Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your request, or 'No' if they have not.
* **Comments** (8): Any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your request.
