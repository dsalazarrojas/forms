# Calibration Certificate Retrieval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the retrieval of calibration certificates for equipment that requires calibration. It allows users to request certificates for various types of requests, such as original, duplicate, digital, historical records, or compliance documentation. The form requires the user to provide information about themselves, the equipment, and the nature of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your full name and email address.
2. Provide your organization's name and department (if applicable).
3. Enter the Equipment ID or serial number of the equipment for which you are requesting the certificate.
4. Describe the equipment and its type.
5. Specify the calibration dates for the equipment.
6. Select the type of request you are making (e.g., original certificate, duplicate copy, etc.).
7. Choose the urgency level of your request (e.g., routine, urgent, critical).
8. Select your preferred delivery method (e.g., email, mail, digital download).
9. If applicable, provide the delivery address.
10. Specify the purpose of your request (e.g., audit preparation, compliance verification, legal requirement, etc.).
11. Add any additional notes or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the department you are from.
* **Equipment ID or Serial Number** (`equipment_id`, text, required): Enter the equipment's ID or serial number.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required): Describe the equipment being calibrated.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of equipment from the options provided.
* **Calibration Date From** (`calibration_date_from`, date, required): Enter the start date of the calibration period.
* **Calibration Date To** (`calibration_date_to`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the calibration period (if applicable).
* **Known Certificate Number (optional)** (`certificate_number`, text, optional): If you have a known certificate number, enter it here.
* **Type of Request** (`request_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of request you are making from the options provided.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of your request from the options provided.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred delivery method from the options provided.
* **Delivery Address (if mail)** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): If you selected mail as your preferred delivery method, enter the delivery address here.
* **Purpose for Requesting Certificate** (`purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of your request from the options provided.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your request.
