# Logo Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request custom logo designs for your business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure you have uploaded a valid logo file.
3. Select the correct options for the design type, color scheme, font size and type, logo position, order type, and order frequency.
4. Enter the required contact information.
5. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logo Description** (`logo_description`): A brief description of the logo you would like to have designed. 
* **Logo Type** (`logo_type`): Select the type of logo you would like to have designed. Options are "Logo with Icon", "Logo without Icon", and "Custom Icon".
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`): Select one or more color schemes for the logo. Options are "Monochromatic", "Duotone", "Tricolor", and "Polychrome".
* **Font Size** (`font_size`): Enter the font size for the logo. This field is optional.
* **Font Type** (`font_type`): Select the font type for the logo. Options are "Sans-Serif", "Serif", and "Script".
* **Background Color** (`background_color`): Enter the background color of the logo.
* **Text Color** (`text_color`): Enter the text color of the logo.
* **Logo Position** (`logo_position`): Select the position of the logo on your design. Options are "Left", "Right", "Center", and "None".
* **Logo File** (`logo_file`): Upload a valid logo file.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your design needs or preferences.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of the company or organization.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the company or organization.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of the company or organization.
* **State** (`state`): Select the state of the company or organization.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of the company or organization.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`): Select the type of order you would like to place. Options are "One time order" and "Ongoing order".
* **Order Frequency** (`order_frequency`): Select the frequency of the order. Options are "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually", and "One-time".
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the order.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): Upload any additional files for your design.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`): Confirm your submission to complete the form.
