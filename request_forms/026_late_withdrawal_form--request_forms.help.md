# Late Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a late withdrawal from an institution or organization. The form is typically used when a student, employee, or member is requesting to withdraw from an academic or professional program late in the semester or year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you are requesting late withdrawal or not. Select "Yes" if you are requesting late withdrawal and "No" if you are not.
2. Provide a brief description of your reason for requesting late withdrawal in the "Reason for Late Withdrawal" field.
3. If required, select the "Institutional Agreement" field to indicate if you have an agreement with the institution for late withdrawal.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any supporting documents for your request.
5. Enter your email address in the "Your E-mail" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Your Phone" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field, if necessary.
8. Finally, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Late Withdrawal** (`late_withdrawal_form`): Choose whether you are requesting late withdrawal or not.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`): A brief description of your reason for requesting late withdrawal.
* **Reason for Late Withdrawal** (`reason_for_late_withdrawal`): A detailed explanation of why you are requesting late withdrawal.
* **Institutional Agreement** (`institutional_agreement`): Select "Yes" if you have an agreement with the institution for late withdrawal.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): Select "Yes" if you have any supporting documents for your request.
* **Your E-mail** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Your Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any additional information or comments you would like to provide.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your reason for requesting late withdrawal is valid and meets the institution's criteria.
* Make sure to attach any required supporting documents.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or institution's administration.
* The form should be filled out in its entirety before submitting it.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
