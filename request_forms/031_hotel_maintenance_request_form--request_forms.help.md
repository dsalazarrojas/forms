# Hotel Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for hotel guests and employees to submit maintenance requests for various issues within the hotel, such as plumbing, electrical, or general maintenance. You can use this form to report a one-time issue or an ongoing problem that requires attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of maintenance request you need help with.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue.
3. Enter your contact details, including phone number and email.
4. Choose when you would like the maintenance to be performed (if applicable).
5. Submit your request or save it for later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Type** (`maintenance_request_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of maintenance request you need help with, such as Plumbing, Electrical, or General Maintenance.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing in the hotel. Be as detailed as possible to help our maintenance team understand the problem.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as your room number or a specific location within the hotel.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like the maintenance to be performed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like the maintenance to be performed.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, required): Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your request. Alternatively, you can choose "Save for Later" to store your request and come back to it later. Note that "Cancel" will discard your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have permission to make maintenance requests if you are not a hotel guest.
* If you are a guest, please note that our maintenance team will do their best to address the issue as soon as possible.
* If you need immediate attention, please contact the front desk or management for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [insert email or phone number].
