# Generic Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information and details from users for a generic submission. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each page carefully to understand what information is required.
* Fill in the necessary fields with accurate and complete information.
* Review your submission before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, Optional): This field is used to capture any additional general information about your request.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, Optional): Enter your contact details, including your name and other relevant information.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, Optional): If you have any supporting documents related to your request, upload them here.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, Optional): Provide any additional information that you believe is relevant to your request.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of request you are submitting (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate if you are submitting on behalf of a team (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you are submitting as an individual or a team (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`submitter_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the support team for assistance.
* Review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button to avoid errors.
