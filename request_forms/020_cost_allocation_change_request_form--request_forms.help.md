# Cost Allocation Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Allocation Change Request Form is used to request changes to cost allocations. Users can fill out this form to submit a request, providing relevant details about the category, type, reason, and other information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your request.
2. Choose the type of change you are requesting.
3. Provide a brief description of the change.
4. Select the cost center involved in the change.
5. Specify the type of change you are requesting.
6. Enter any comments or reasons for the change.
7. Provide your name, email, phone number, and department (if applicable).
8. Select the approver's name, email, and department (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (Select One): Select the category related to your request.
* **request_type** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of change you are requesting.
* **description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the change.
* **cost_center** (Select One): Select the cost center involved in the change.
* **change_type** (Select One): Specify the type of change you are requesting.
* **reason** (Text): Enter any comments or reasons for the change.
* **comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date of the change.
* **time** (Time): Enter the time of the change.
* **submitter** (Text): Enter your name.
* **submitter_email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **submitter_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_designation** (Text): Enter your designation or position.
* **submitter_department** (Text): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **submitter_designation_other** (Text): If your designation is not listed, enter it here.
* **submitter_date** (Date): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **submitter_time** (Time): Enter the time you submitted the request.
* **submitter_cost_center** (Select One): Select the cost center you are submitting the request from.
* **submitter_reason** (Text): Enter any reason for submitting the request.
* **manager_approvement_status** (Select One): Select the approver's approval status.
* **manager_comments** (Text): Enter any comments from the approver.
* **approver** (Text): Enter the approver's name.
* **approver_phone** (Text): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **approver_email** (Email): Enter the approver's email address.

Note: Some fields might be optional, please only fill them out if they are relevant to your request.
