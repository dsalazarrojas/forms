# real_estate_brochure_design_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate agents or brokers to request brochure design services. It's meant to collect essential information to create a custom brochure for their clients or properties.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the required information. 
  - If a section is marked as optional (not required), it's up to you to determine what information to provide.
  - Make sure to complete all the required sections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide any additional information about the brochure design request. Please describe the purpose of the brochure, its intended use, and any specific requirements you have.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): In this section, provide your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Brochure Preferences** (`brochure_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your brochure preferences, such as color scheme, font style, and any other design elements you'd like to see in the brochure.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Clicking this button will submit the form and send your request to our team. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required sections before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, please ask us for clarification.
- Our team will review your submission and contact you with any questions or to confirm your brochure design request.
