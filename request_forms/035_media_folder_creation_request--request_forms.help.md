# Media Folder Creation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a media folder creation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Details" field with a brief description of your request.
2. Choose whether the media type is "Yes", "No", or other.
3. Enter the folder name and folder description in their respective fields.
4. Select the submitter's email address.
5. Choose whether you are a department or not.
6. Select whether you are a team member or not.
7. Enter the submit date (optional).
8. Add any additional notes.
9. Choose the request status (optional).
10. Fill in the form submitter's name and email (optional).
11. Enter the form version (optional).
12. Fill in the form submitter's name and email (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details**: A brief description of your request.
* **Media Type**: Choose whether the media type is "Yes", "No", or other.
* **Folder Name**: Enter the name of the folder you are requesting.
* **Folder Description**: Enter a brief description of the folder.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date when you are requesting the folder (optional).
* **Folder Path**: Enter the path for the folder.
* **Submitter**: Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Department**: Choose whether you are a department or not.
* **Team Members**: Choose whether you are a team member or not.
* **Team Name**: Enter the team name (optional).
* **Team Lead**: Enter the team lead's name (optional).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes.
* **Request Status**: Choose the request status (optional).
* **Form Submitter**: Fill in the form submitter's name (optional).
* **Request Form Version**: Enter the form version (optional).
* **Form Submitter Email**: Enter the form submitter's email (optional).
* **Form Submitter 2**: Fill in the form submitter's name (optional).
* **Submitter 2**: Enter the submitter's email (optional).
* **Team**: Choose whether you are a team or not.
* **Team Members 2**: Choose whether you are a team member or not.
* **Form Submitter 2**: Fill in the form submitter's name (optional).
