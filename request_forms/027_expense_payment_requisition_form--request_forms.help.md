# Expense Payment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Payment Requisition Form is used to request payment for expenses incurred by employees or departments within an organization. This form helps to track, record, and manage financial transactions related to these expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and which fields are optional.
2. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields to help with processing your request.
4. Make sure to proofread your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_1**: This field is for entering the description of the expense.
- **form_2**: This field is for entering the amount of the expense.
- **form_3**: This field is for selecting the category of the expense (e.g., travel, office supplies, etc.).
- **form_4**: This field is for entering the date of the expense.
- **form_5**: This field is for selecting the department or team responsible for the expense.
- **form_6**: This field is for entering the project code or task ID related to the expense.
- **form_7**: This field is for entering a brief justification or explanation of the expense.
- **form_8**: This field is for entering any supporting documents or receipts related to the expense.
- **form_9**: This field is for selecting the method of payment for the expense.
- **form_10**: This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about the expense.
- **form_11**: This field is for selecting the status of the expense request (e.g., approved, pending, etc.).
- **form_12**: This field is for entering the name and contact information of the person who submitted the request.
- **form_13**: This field is for selecting the approver or responsible person for the expense.
- **form_14**: This field is for entering the date the request was submitted.
- **form_15**: This field is for entering the date the request was approved or rejected.
- **form_16**: This field is for entering any notes or comments from the approver.
- **form_17**: This field is for entering the budget code or account used for the expense.
- **form_18**: This field is for selecting the cost type (e.g., reimbursable, non-reimbursable, etc.).
- **form_19**: This field is for selecting the payment frequency (e.g., one-time, recurring, etc.).
- **form_20**: This field is for entering any other relevant information about the expense.
- **form_21**: This field is for selecting the expense type (e.g., travel, meals, etc.).
- **form_22**: This field is for entering the currency of the expense (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
- **form_23**: This field is for entering any supporting information about the expense.
- **form_24**: This field is for entering any other relevant information about the expense.
- **form_25**: This field is for entering any final comments or notes about the expense.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please consult with the relevant authorities within your organization.
- This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be used for financial management and reporting within the organization.
