# New Client Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Request Form is used to capture information about new clients and their requests for services. This form helps our team to understand the client's needs and to assign the correct tools and resources to fulfill their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the services you are requesting from the "Service Requested" field. You can choose one or multiple services.
6. Choose the tool you would like to be assigned for your request from the "Assigned Tool" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your request in the "Notes" field.
8. If you are representing a business or organization, select your client type from the "Client Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email (Email, Required: False)**: Enter a valid email address to enable us to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Service Requested (Select Multiple, Required: True)**: Select the services you are requesting. You can choose one or multiple services.
* **Date of Request (Date, Required: False)**: If relevant, enter the date of your request. This is optional.
* **Assigned Tool (Select One, Required: True)**: Choose the tool you would like to be assigned for your request.
* **Notes (Note, Required: False)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Client Type (Select One, Required: False)**: If you are representing a business or organization, select your client type. This is optional.
* **Company (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your company name, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your request.
* If you need help with filling out this form, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you are unsure about any of the services or tools, you can ask us for clarification.
