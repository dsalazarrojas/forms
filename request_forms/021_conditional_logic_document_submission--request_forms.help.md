# Conditional Logic Document Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conditional Logic Document Submission form is a comprehensive form designed to collect documents and information for various types of submissions. It ensures accurate and secure submission of required documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of document you are submitting (e.g., Employment Documents, Academic Records, etc.).
2. Provide your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
3. If applicable, select your employment status and provide your years of experience.
4. If uploading documents, choose the main document and any additional documents you wish to include.
5. Briefly describe the documents you are submitting.
6. Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the accuracy of the submitted documents.
7. Indicate your consent to verify the submitted documents.
8. Acknowledge that you understand the organization's privacy policy.
9. Finally, confirm that you have reviewed and agree to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Submission Type (id: 2)
Submission Type: Select the type of document you are submitting (e.g., Employment Documents, Academic Records, etc.). This will help determine the conditional questions that will be displayed.

### Submission Date (id: 3)
Submission Date: Enter the date of submission. This date should match the date of document creation.

### Full Name (id: 5)
Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email Address (id: 6)
Email Address: Enter your contact email address.

### Phone Number (id: 7)
Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.

### Date of Birth (id: 8)
Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.

### Applicant Type (id: 9)
Applicant Type: Select your relationship to the organization (e.g., Employee, Student, Client, etc.).

### Reference Number (id: 10)
Reference Number: Enter the case or application ID number (if applicable).

### Document Upload Section (id: 11)
Document Upload Section: This is where you can upload your files.

### Primary Document (id: 12)
Primary Document: Select the main document you are submitting.

### Secondary Document (id: 13)
Secondary Document: Select any additional documents you wish to include.

### Supporting Documents (id: 14)
Supporting Documents: Enter any extra documents.

### Document Description (id: 15)
Document Description: Briefly describe the documents you are submitting.

### Additional Questions (id: 16)
Additional Questions: These questions will appear based on your submission type selections and will require your answers to continue with the submission process.

### Current Employment Status (id: 17)
Current Employment Status: If submitting employment documents, select your employment status.

### Years of Experience (id: 18)
Years of Experience: If uploading employment documents, provide your years of experience.

### Education Level (id: 19)
Education Level: Select your highest level of education completed.

### Professional Certifications (id: 20)
Professional Certifications: List any professional certifications you hold.

### Declaration (id: 21)
Declaration: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the accuracy of the submitted documents.

### Accuracy Statement (id: 22)
Accuracy Statement: Confirm that the documents are accurate.

### Consent to Verify (id: 23)
Consent to Verify: Indicate your consent to allow verification of documents.

### Privacy Acknowledgment (id: 24)
Privacy Acknowledgment: Understand and acknowledge the organization's privacy policy.

### Submit Confirmation (id: 25)
Submit Confirmation: Finally, confirm that you have reviewed and agree to submit the form.

Note: The conditional questions will only appear based on your submission type selections, and the required fields are marked as such in the form.
