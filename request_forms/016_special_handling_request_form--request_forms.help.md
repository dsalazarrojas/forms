# Special Handling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit special handling requests for bespoke services such as special delivery options, equipment handling, or other tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with the required information to submit a valid request.
- Ensure that you select all relevant options that apply to your specific request.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting to avoid duplicates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pickup Request** (1, number, required): Enter a unique number to identify the pickup request.
- **Request Type** (2, select_multiple, not required): Select 'Yes' if you need special handling, e.g., for equipment or delivery; 'No' if no special handling is required.
- **Handling Facility** (3, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' if your request requires specific facilities or equipment; 'No' if no specialized facility is needed.
- **Request Date** (4, date, not required): Enter the date when the special handling is required; if no date is specified, the request will be considered as general.
- **Pickup Details** (5, select_multiple, not required): Select 'Yes' if you need specific pickup details or services; 'No' if no special pickup details are required.
- **Delivery Details** (6, select_multiple, not required): Select 'Yes' if you need special delivery services; 'No' if no delivery services are required.
- **Contact Email** (7, email, not required): Enter your contact email to receive updates or notifications about your request.
- **Contact Phone** (8, text, not required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication about your request.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information in the form to avoid misunderstandings or delays.
- If you need further assistance or clarification, please contact the relevant department or support team.
