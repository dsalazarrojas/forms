# Veterinary Media Appearance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us consider your request for media appearances related to veterinary media, such as TV shows, documentaries, or interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your veterinary media appearance request details.
2. On the second page, provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
3. On the third page, provide information about the pet related to your media appearance request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <u>veterinary_media_appearance_request</u> (First Page)
* **Veterinary Media Appearance Request** (`veterinary_media_appearance_request`, text, required): This field is where you can briefly describe your media appearance request, including the type of media (e.g., TV show, documentary, interview), the purpose of your appearance, and any specific details that might be relevant to your request.

### 2. <u>contact_info</u> (Second Page)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This field is where you can provide your contact information, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant details that might help us get in touch with you.

### 3. <u>pet_info</u> (Third Page)
* **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, text, optional): If your media appearance request is related to a specific pet, you can provide information about the pet here, including its name, species, and breed (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please fill out each field as completely and accurately as possible to help us process your request efficiently.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals from your organization or relevant parties before submitting this form.
* We'll review each submission to prevent duplication and ensure it aligns with our media appearance guidelines.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
