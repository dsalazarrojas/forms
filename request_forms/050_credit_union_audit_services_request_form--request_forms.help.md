# Credit Union Audit Services Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for credit union staff or members to submit a request for audit services. It helps us track and manage requests for audits, ensuring timely and accurate service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name to identify yourself.
2. Select the type of audit services you require (Audit Services, Credit Union Services, Other, or Other 2).
3. Enter the name of the credit union you are affiliated with.
4. If you are not the credit union contact, enter your name and email address for contact information.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it for further communication.
7. Choose a date to request the audit services.
8. Select the start and end dates for the audit.
9. Provide your contact address (optional).
10. Optionally, provide additional information for audit scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your audit request.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of audit services you require. Options are:
	+ Audit Services
	+ Credit Union Services
	+ Other
	+ Other 2
* **Credit Union** (`credit_union`, text, required): Enter the name of the credit union you are affiliated with.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you are not the credit union contact, enter your name here.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for further communication.
* **Audit Request Date** (`audit_request_date`, date, required): Choose the date to request the audit services.
* **Audit Start Date** (`audit_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the audit.
* **Audit End Date** (`audit_end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the audit.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Provide your contact address (optional).
* **Audit Scheduling** (`audit_scheduling`, text, optional): Optionally, provide additional information for audit scheduling.

## Tips

* Please ensure your contact email address is correct to facilitate communication.
* If you have any additional requirements or requests, you can provide them in the "Audit Scheduling" field.
* If you are not the credit union contact, make sure to enter your name and contact information correctly.
* Double-check your dates for accuracy.
