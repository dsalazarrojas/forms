# Cash Value Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Value Information Request Form is designed to collect essential details about the item or property that needs to be valued. This form will help us understand your requirements and provide an accurate valuation report.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the **Request Information** section:
    * Provide a brief description of the item or property you need valued.
    * Enter the **Request Date** and **Request Number**.
2.  Enter the **Requestor Information** section:
    * Fill in your name and title/position.
    * Enter your organization or department name.
    * Enter your phone number and email address.
3.  Enter the **Item or Property Information** section:
    * Provide a detailed description of the item or property you want valued.
    * Enter the **Quantity** of items or properties.
    * Choose the **Item Condition** from the options provided.
4.  Enter the **Valuation Request Details** section:
    * Select the **Purpose of Valuation** from the options provided.
    * Enter the **Date Valuation Needed By** (if applicable).
5.  Enter the **Market Information** section:
    * If applicable, provide information about similar items recently sold.
    * Enter the original purchase price (if known).
    * Provide information about current market listing prices.
6.  Enter the **Appraisal Request** section:
    * Check if a professional appraisal is required.
7.  Enter the **Supporting Documentation** section:
    * Select all relevant documents you have available.
8.  Enter the **Additional Notes or Comments**:
    * Add any other relevant information about the item or property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date (MM/DD/YYYY)**: This is the date the request was made, which will help us track and follow up on your request.
* **Request Number**: This is a unique identifier for your request, which will be used to reference your valuation report.
* **Requestor Name**: This is the name of the person making the request, who should be authorized to represent the requestor or have explicit permission to submit on their behalf.
* **Job Title or Position**: This is the title or position of the person making the request.
* **Organization or Department**: This is the organization or department the requestor belongs to.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address of the person making the request.
* **Item or Property Description**: This is a detailed description of the item or property you want valued.
* **Quantity**: This is the number of items or properties you want valued.
* **Item Condition**: This is the condition of the item or property, which will be used to assess its value.
* **Date of Acquisition (MM/DD/YYYY)**: If known, enter the date of acquisition of the item or property.
* **Valuation Purpose**: This is the main reason for the valuation request.
* **Date Valuation Needed By (MM/DD/YYYY)**: If applicable, enter the date by which the valuation needs to be completed.
* **Comparable Items Recently Sold**: If applicable, provide information about similar items recently sold.
* **Original Purchase Price (if known)**: If known, enter the original purchase price of the item or property.
* **Current Market Listing Prices**: If known, provide information about the current market listing prices for similar items.
* **Is a Professional Appraisal Needed?**: Check if a professional appraisal is required.
* **Supporting Documentation Available**: Select all relevant documents you have available.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Any other relevant information about the item or property can be added here.
