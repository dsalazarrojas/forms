# Work From Home Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work From Home Request Form is designed to help you request to work from home when you need to do so. It collects information about your employee status and the dates you are requesting to work from home. This form helps to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Provide your Employee Info**: Please enter your full name and employee ID.
2. **Specify your Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start working from home.
3. **Specify your End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end working from home.
4. **Describe your Reason**: Please provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting to work from home.
5. **E-Signature**: You can add a digital signature to confirm that you have filled out this form accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (Employee Info, Text, Optional): This field is intended to collect your employee name and ID for identification purposes. Please provide your full name and employee ID if you have it.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to start working from home.
* **End Date** (End Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to end working from home.
* **Reason** (Reason, Text, Optional): This field is intended to provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting to work from home. Be as specific as possible.
* **E-Signature** (E-Signature, Text, Optional): You can add a digital signature to confirm that you have filled out this form accurately and truthfully. Please use your actual signature or a digital equivalent. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for your request before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your reason for requesting to work from home.
* Make sure to enter accurate dates to avoid any discrepancies.
