# Tattoo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tattoo Submission Form is a tool for artists and clients to submit information about a tattoo, including client details, submission details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Info**: Enter the client's name, email, and phone number to identify the individual who submitted the tattoo.
2. **Submission Type**: Select whether the submission is from a client or artist.
3. **Submission Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the submission.
4. **Submission Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
5. **Artist Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the artist.
6. **Client Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments from the client.
7. **Submission Files**: Attach any relevant files or documents to the submission.
8. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
9. **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
10. **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
11. **Artist Name**: Enter the artist's name.
12. **Artist Email**: Enter the artist's email address.
13. **Artist Phone**: Enter the artist's phone number.
14. **Studio Info**: Add any additional comments or notes about the submission.
15. **Tattoo Info**: Enter any information about the tattoo, such as design or location.
16. **Artwork Info**: Enter any additional information about the artwork.
17. **Submission Status**: Select the current status of the submission.
18. **Artist Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments from the artist.
19. **Submission Date 2**: Enter the date of a second submission.
20. **Submission Time 2**: Enter the time of a second submission.
21. **Submission Notes 2**: Add any additional comments or notes about the second submission.
