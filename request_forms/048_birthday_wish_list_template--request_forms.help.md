# Birthday Wish List Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This template is designed to help you create a list of gift ideas for your special day. It will help you specify your preferences, including budget, gift style, and interests, and provide a list of specific items you'd like to receive as gifts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name, birthday date, age, email address, and phone number in the Birthday Person Information section.
2. Choose your budget range, gift style, and interests in the Gift Preferences section.
3. Add up to 5 items to your wish list, including descriptions, price ranges, and links to purchase.
4. Specify up to 3 experiences you'd like to have.
5. Add any special requests, such as charitable donation preferences or allergies/restrictions.
6. Choose who to share your wish list with and add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Birthday Date**: Enter the date of your birthday in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age Turning**: Enter your current age.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Budget Range**: Choose a budget range that works for you.
* **Gift Style Preferences**: Choose the types of gifts you prefer.
* **Interests and Hobbies**: Specify your interests and hobbies.
* **Wish Item 1**: Add the first item you'd like to receive as a gift.
* **Wish Item 1 Description**: Describe the item in detail.
* **Wish Item 1 Price Range**: Enter the approximate price of the item.
* **Wish Item 1 Link**: Enter a link to purchase the item (optional).
* **Wish Item 2**: Add the second item you'd like to receive as a gift.
* **...**: Repeat the process for up to 5 wish items.
* **Experience Wish 1**: Specify an experience you'd like to have.
* **Experience Wish 2**: Specify another experience you'd like to have.
* **Experience Wish 3**: Specify a third experience you'd like to have.
* **Charitable Donation Wish**: Specify a charitable cause you're interested in supporting.
* **Gift Recipient Details**: Add any special instructions for gifts.
* **Allergies or Restrictions**: Specify any allergies or restrictions you have.
* **Share This Wish List**: Choose who to share your wish list with.
* **Notes for Sharing**: Add any additional notes when sharing your wish list.

## Tips

* Be specific when adding items to your wish list to help gift-givers understand what you'd like.
* Consider adding links to websites or online marketplaces where the items can be purchased.
* Share your wish list with your loved ones to make gift-giving more enjoyable and meaningful.
* If you have any allergies or restrictions, be sure to specify them in the Allergies or Restrictions field.
* If you'd like to share your wish list with specific people, enter their names or email addresses in the Share This Wish List field.
