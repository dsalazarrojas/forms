# Over The Air Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Over The Air Update Request Form" is used to request an Over The Air (OTA) update for a device. This form is intended for users to submit information about their device, including its ID, name, firmware version, and update preferences. The form helps our team to gather necessary details to process the update request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your device's ID in the "Device Id" field.
2.  Provide the name of your device in the "Device Name" field.
3.  Enter the current firmware version of your device in the "Firmware Version" field.
4.  Select the date for the update in the "Update Date" field (if available).
5.  Choose the time of the update from the "Update Time" field (if available).
6.  If you have a preferred schedule for the update, select it in the "Schedule Update" field.
7.  Add any additional details in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Device Id (1)
**Device Id** (number): This is the unique identifier for your device. Enter the correct number to help us process your update request efficiently.

### Device Name (2)
**Device Name** (text): Enter the name of your device to ensure we have accurate information about the device you're updating.

### Firmware Version (3)
**Firmware Version** (text): Provide the current firmware version of your device. This helps us to determine the necessary updates and ensure compatibility.

### Update Date (4)
**Update Date** (date): If you have a preferred date for the update, select it in this field. This is not required, but helps us to plan the update process.

### Update Time (5)
**Update Time** (time): If you have a preferred time for the update, select it in this field. Like the update date, this is not required but helps us to coordinate the update.

### Schedule Update (6)
**Schedule Update** (date): If you have a specific schedule for the update, select it in this field. This ensures we deliver the update according to your needs.

### Description (7)
**Description** (text): Add any additional details or context about your update request in this field. This is optional but helps us to understand your needs better.
