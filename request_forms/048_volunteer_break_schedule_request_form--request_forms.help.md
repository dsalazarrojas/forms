# Volunteer Break Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by volunteers to request breaks or time off from their work schedule. Volunteers can use this form to submit their requests to their managers or administrators for approval.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as the volunteer making the request.
2. Specify the date you are requesting a break or time off for.
3. Choose the duration of your requested break.
4. Select the type of break you are requesting (Morning, Lunch, or Afternoon).
5. If you need to contact your manager, provide their name, email address, and phone number.
6. Specify how your manager would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text).
7. If necessary, include any additional notes for your manager's reference.
8. Indicate whether your manager has approved or denied your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Break Schedule Request**: This is where you can enter your request for a break or time off.
* **Volunteer Break Schedule Date**: Enter the date you are requesting a break or time off for.
* **Volunteer Break Duration**: Enter the number of hours you need for your break.
* **Volunteer Break Start Time**: Enter the time you would like your break to start.
* **Volunteer Break End Time**: Enter the time your break will end.
* **Volunteer Break Type**: Choose the type of break you are requesting (Morning, Lunch, or Afternoon).
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as the volunteer making this request.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Contact Method**: Choose how your manager would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text).
* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes for your manager's reference.
* **Manager Approvals**: Indicate whether your manager has approved or denied your request.
