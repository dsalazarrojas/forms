# public_record_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a public record.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Provide your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization name" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your city in the "City" field.
6. Enter your state in the "State" field.
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter your fax number in the "Fax" field.
11. Specify the purpose of your request in the "Purpose of request" field.
12. Select the type of record you are requesting from the "Type of record" field.
13. If necessary, enter the date of the record in the "Date of record" field.
14. If necessary, enter the record number in the "Record number" field.
15. Sign the form electronically in the "Signature" field.
16. Date the form in the "Date signed" field.
17. If the form has been modified, enter the date and time it was modified in the "Date modified" and "Time modified" fields.
18. If the form has been deleted, enter the date and user who deleted it in the "Deleted at" and "Deleted by" fields.
19. Enter any additional notes about your request in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Organization name** (Text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Address** (Text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (Text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (Text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (Text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (Text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Purpose of request** (Text, optional): Specify the purpose of your request.
* **Type of record** (Select One, optional): Select the type of record you are requesting.
* **Date of record** (Date, optional): If necessary, enter the date of the record.
* **Record number** (Text, optional): If necessary, enter the record number.
* **Signature** (Text, optional): Sign the form electronically.
* **Date signed** (Date, optional): Date the form.
* **Date modified** (Date, optional): If the form has been modified, enter the date it was modified.
* **Time modified** (Time, optional): If the form has been modified, enter the time it was modified.
* **Modified by** (Text, optional): If the form has been modified, enter the user who modified it.
* **Created by** (Text, optional): Enter the user who created the form.
* **Created at** (Date, optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated at** (Date, optional): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Deleted at** (Date, optional): If the form has been deleted, enter the date it was deleted.
* **Deleted by** (Text, optional): If the form has been deleted, enter the user who deleted it.
* **Notes** (Text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your request.
