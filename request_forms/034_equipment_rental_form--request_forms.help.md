# Equipment Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Rental Form is used to collect information from customers about their equipment rental requests. This information is used to process and fulfill their requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a piece of equipment, 'No' if you are not.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting this piece of equipment, 'No' if not.
* **Requested Start Date** (`requested_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start renting the equipment.
* **Requested End Date** (`requested_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to stop renting the equipment.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the equipment rental team for assistance.
- Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are requesting multiple pieces of equipment, please make sure to select 'Yes' for each of them.
