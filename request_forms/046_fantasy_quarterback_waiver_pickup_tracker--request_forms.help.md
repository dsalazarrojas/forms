# fantasy_quarterback_waiver_pickup_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the waiver status of fantasy sports players, specifically their priority level for waiver pickup. It helps users efficiently manage and communicate the status of waivers in fantasy sports leagues or communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Information** (page 1) to identify yourself and your league or community affiliation.
2. Select the **Team Name** (page 1) of the player you are submitting or picking up.
3. Enter the **Player Name** (page 1) of the player in question.
4. Choose the **Waiver Status** (page 2) of the player from the available options (Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled, Unknown).
5. Select the **Priority Level** (page 2) of the player from the available options (Low, Medium, High), as this is a required field.
6. Optionally, provide any additional **Notes** (page 2) regarding the player's status or situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name and any relevant contact information to help identify you and your league or community affiliation.
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the team for which the player is being submitted or picked up.
* **Player Name** (Player Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the fantasy sports player in question.
* **Waiver Status** (Waiver Status, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the current waiver status of the player from the available options (Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled, Unknown).
* **Priority Level** (Priority Level, select_one, required):
	+ Select the priority level of the player for waiver pickup, with options Low, Medium, or High.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the player's waiver status or situation.
