# Reimbursement Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement for expenses incurred by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Request Form** section (page 1) with a clear and concise description of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
2. Enter the date you incurred the expense.
3. Enter the time you incurred the expense.
4. Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
5. Enter the amount of the expense, including currency and decimal points (e.g., $10.99).
6. You do not need to enter any information for the **Submit** field.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the expense in the **note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, optional): A clear and concise description of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date you incurred the expense.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time you incurred the expense.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): The amount of the expense, including currency and decimal points (e.g., $10.99).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Additional notes or comments about the expense.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
- This form is not intended for urgent or emergency reimbursements. If you need reimbursement for such situations, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department for help.
