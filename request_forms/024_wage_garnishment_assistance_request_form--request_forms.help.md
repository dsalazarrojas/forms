# Wage Garnishment Assistance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wage Garnishment Assistance Request Form is used to request help with wage garnishment, a legal process used to collect debts or court-ordered payments from an employee's paycheck. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose one or more of the legal aid clinics or HR teams that can assist with wage garnishment.
2. Select the type of financial counselor you would like to speak with, if applicable.
3. Enter the date of the last wage garnishment, if applicable.
4. Enter the amount you owe, if applicable.
5. Choose the type of wage garnishment (Bank, Court Ordered, or Other).
6. Enter the name of your employer.
7. Enter your employee ID number.
8. Enter your address.
9. Enter your city.
10. Enter your state.
10. Enter your zip code.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Aid Clinic/HR Team**: Select one or more legal aid clinics or HR teams that can assist with wage garnishment. This will help the selected team determine the best course of action for your situation.
* **Financial Counselor**: Select a financial counselor to discuss your situation and potential options. This field is optional, but can provide additional assistance with understanding your financial obligations.
* **Date of Last Garnishment**: If you have had a wage garnishment in the past, enter the date it occurred.
* **Amount Owe**: If you are requesting help with wage garnishment due to an outstanding debt, enter the amount you owe.
* **Wage Garnishment Type**: Choose the type of wage garnishment you are experiencing (Bank, Court Ordered, or Other).
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Employee Address**: Enter your home address, if you have not moved recently.
* **Employee City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Employee State**: Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **Employee Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address, if you have one.

Note: All fields are optional, unless marked as required. If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the selected legal aid clinic or HR team for guidance.
