# Proxy Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used to request proxy authorization for a specific reason. Ensure that you are using this form for the correct purpose and not for other reasons. This form is not meant to be used for other requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your Email in the "Email" field (in the format of `username@domain.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your Scope from the dropdown list in the "Scope" field. This field has three options, please select the correct one for your purpose.
6. Enter the start date of your request in the "Valid From" field (in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`).
7. Enter the end date of your request in the "Valid To" field (in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`).
8. Enter your Organization in the "Organization" field.
9. Fill in any additional Proxy Contact Details in the "Proxy Contact Details" field.
10. Enter the proxy contact's Email in the "Proxy Email" field.
11. Enter the proxy contact's Phone number in the "Proxy Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Scope**: Select your scope for the request.
	+ **Scope-option-1**: This option is for [insert description here, best-effort].
	+ **Scope-option-2**: This option is for [insert description here, best-effort].
	+ **Scope-option-3**: This option is for [insert description here, best-effort].
* **Valid From**: Enter the start date of your request in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Valid To**: Enter the end date of your request in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization.
* **Proxy Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details for your proxy.
* **Proxy Email**: Enter the contact's email address.
* **Proxy Phone**: Enter the contact's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check the Scope field for correct selection.
* Enter dates in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
