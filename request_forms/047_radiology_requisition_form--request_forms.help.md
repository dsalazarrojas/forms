# Radiology Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiology Requisition Form is a comprehensive form designed to facilitate the process of requesting radiology services such as X-ray, MRI, CT Scan, Ultrasound, and Mammogram. This form collects essential information from the patient, healthcare provider, and facility staff to ensure accurate and efficient radiology services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill in the patient's details, including first name, last name, date of birth, and contact number.
3. Select the type of radiology order from the available options (X-ray, MRI, CT Scan, Ultrasound, or Mammogram).
4. Enter the patient's ID number and facility name.
5. Provide a brief order description for the radiology service.
6. Add any additional facility and patient notes if necessary.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the radiology order.
8. Enter any additional facility notes if required.
9. Finally, add any patient notes if necessary.
10. If applicable, enter the name and signature of the ordering physician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the facility name where the radiology service will be performed.
* **Radiology Order**: Select the type of radiology service required (X-ray, MRI, CT Scan, Ultrasound, or Mammogram).
* **Patient ID Number**: Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Order Description**: Provide a brief description of the radiology service required.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the facility address where the radiology service will be performed (optional).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the radiology order.
* **Facility Notes**: Add any additional facility-related notes if necessary.
* **Patient Notes**: Add any additional patient-related notes if necessary.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the ordering physician (if applicable).
* **Physician Signature**: Enter the signature of the ordering physician (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the selected radiology order to ensure it matches the required service.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the radiology department for assistance.
