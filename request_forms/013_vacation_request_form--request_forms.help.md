# Vacation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Request Form is a formal request for time off, allowing employees to plan and coordinate their leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary fields with your details. You can access the form from the designated link or by searching for it within the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID number for verification purposes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to start your vacation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to end your vacation.
* **Vacation Type** (`vacation_type`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable vacation types that apply to your request (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days you plan to be off.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your vacation.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your vacation.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have reviewed and understood the company's vacation policy before submitting a request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for clarification.
- Duplicate submissions will be considered invalid; only submit one request per instance.
- Be as specific as possible when providing your start and end dates.
- Ensure your request aligns with your department's scheduling needs.
