# retail_space_leasing_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request retail space leasing for a business or individual. It gathers essential information about the property and the client's needs. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your landlord details in the "landlord_details" field. 
2. Provide the tenant's information in the "tenant_details" field. 
3. If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can upload them in the "form_attachments" field. (Optional)
4. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_name_2" field.
5. Enter the contact person's email in the "contact_email_2" field.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone_2" field.
7. Enter the budget for the retail space in the "budget_2" field.
8. Specify the space requirements in the "space_requirements_2" field.
9. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_name_3" field.
10. Enter the contact person's email in the "contact_email_3" field.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone_3" field.
12. Specify the space requirements in the "space_requirements_3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **landlord_details (text, required)**: Please enter your name and any other relevant details about your property.
* **tenant_details (text, required)**: Please enter the name and contact information for the tenant who is requesting the retail space.
* **form_attachments (text, optional)**: If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you can upload them here. 
* **submit (text, required)**: Please enter a brief message with your request.
* **contact_name_2 (text, required)**: Please enter the name of the contact person for this request.
* **contact_email_2 (email, required)**: Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone_2 (text, required)**: Please enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **budget_2 (number, required)**: Please enter the budget for the retail space.
* **space_requirements_2 (text, required)**: Please specify the space requirements for the retail space.
* **contact_name_3 (text, required)**: Please enter the name of the contact person for this request.
* **contact_email_3 (email, required)**: Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone_3 (text, required)**: Please enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **space_requirements_3 (text, required)**: Please specify the space requirements for the retail space.
