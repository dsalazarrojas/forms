# Writing Residency Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Writing Residency Leave Of Absence Form is used to request a residency leave of absence for a specific period of time. This form helps the requesting individual provide relevant information about their leave request, including the reason for the leave, the start and end dates of the residency program, and other supporting details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are eligible to submit this form. Ensure that your request is valid and not a duplicate.
2. Answer the following questions:
	* **Residency Leave Request** (select one): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a leave of absence.
	* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are currently in or wish to attend.
	* **Program Start Date**: Enter the date you started the program.
	* **Program End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end the program.
	* **Reason for Leave**: Briefly explain the reason for your leave.
	* **Leave Duration**: Specify the duration of your leave.
	* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
	* **Leave End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
	* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return from your leave.
	* **Reason for Leave** (select one): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a leave for a specific reason.
	* **Contact Person**: Select "Yes" if you have a contact person for your leave.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Department**: Select the department that will be handling your leave request.
	* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status of your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residency Leave Request**: 
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that you are requesting a residency leave of absence.

* **Program Start Date** (date): Enter the date you started the program.
* **Program End Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to end the program.
* **Reason for Leave** (text): Briefly explain the reason for your leave.
* **Leave Duration** (number): Specify the duration of your leave in days, weeks, or months.
* **Leave Start Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Return Date** (date): Enter the date you plan to return from your leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (select one): 
	+ Select "Yes" if you are requesting a leave for a specific reason.
* **Contact Person** (select one): 
	+ Select "Yes" if you have a contact person for your leave.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (select multiple): Select the department that will be handling your leave request.
* **Approval Status** (select multiple): Select the approval status of your leave request.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your department or manager for clarification.
