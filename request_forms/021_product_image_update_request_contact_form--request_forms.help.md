# Product Image Update Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Image Update Request Contact Form is designed to collect information about product image updates from customers or stakeholders. It helps internal teams to gather necessary information for updating product images in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the update request in the "Request Form" field.
2. Provide the URL of the product image that needs an update.
3. Explain the reason for the update in the "update_reason" field.
4. Specify the date and time when the update should take place in the "update_date" and "update_time" fields.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
7. Optionally, add any additional information in the "update_request" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`1`, text, optional): A brief description of the update request.
* **Product Image URL** (`2`, text, optional): The URL of the product image that needs an update.
* **Update Reason** (`3`, text, optional): Explain the reason for the update.
* **Update Request** (`4`, text, optional): Optionally add any additional information for the update.
* **Contact Email** (`5`, email, optional): The email address of the person to contact for updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`6`, text, optional): The phone number of the person to contact for updates.
* **Product Image URL** (`7`, text, optional): The URL of the product image that needs an update.
* **Update Date** (`8`, date, optional): The date when the update should take place.
* **Update Time** (`9`, time, optional): The time when the update should take place.
* **Submit** (`10`, text, optional): Click to send the request.
