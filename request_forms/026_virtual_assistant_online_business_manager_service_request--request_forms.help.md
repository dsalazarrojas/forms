# Virtual Assistant / Online Business Manager Service Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant / Online Business Manager Service Request form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in requesting virtual assistant or online business management services. This form will help us understand their needs and provide them with the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your service request: In the "Service Request" field, describe the type of virtual assistant or online business management service you are interested in.
2. Add service details: In the "Service Details" field, provide a brief description of the service you need.
3. Provide business contact information: Enter your business name, contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
4. Choose follow-up method and frequency: Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-ups and how often you would like them to be sent.
5. Agree to our terms: Select "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.
6. Submit your request: Once you have completed all the above steps, click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required): A brief description of the virtual assistant or online business management service you are interested in.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): A detailed description of the service you need.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Your business name or the name of the business you represent.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): The name of the person we should contact for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number (if you provide this, we will use it to contact you for follow-ups).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address (this is how we will contact you for follow-ups).
* **Service Needed** (`service_needed`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you are interested in (Virtual Assistant, Online Business Manager, or Both).
* **Other Service** (`service_needed_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, describe the service you need here.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-ups (Phone, Email, In-app).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-ups (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Text** (`agreement_text`, text, required): A text field for you to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Your digital signature (not required but recommended).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Your name or the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Company** (`submitter_company`, text, required): The name of your company or the company you represent.
* **Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, required): Your job title or position.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Your email address or the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Your phone number or the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Agreement Text** (`submitter_agreement_text`, text, required): A text field for the submitter to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note, optional): Any additional notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Submit** (`submitter_submit`, text, required): A text field to submit the request.
