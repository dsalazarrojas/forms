# Academic Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request academic records, such as transcripts, enrollment verification, or degree verification, from the academic institution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill out the requester's information**:
    *   Enter your full name as it appears on your academic records.
    *   If you've used a different name in the past, enter that name here.
    *   Enter your date of birth.
    *   Provide your student ID number.
    *   Enter your email address.
    *   Enter your phone number.
2.  **Specify your attendance dates**:
    *   Enter the dates you attended the institution.
3.  **Degree earned (if applicable)**:
    *   If you've earned a degree, select it from the list provided.
4.  **Choose the type of record requested**:
    *   Select the type of record you need, such as official transcript, unofficial transcript, or enrollment verification.
5.  **Specify the number of copies needed**:
    *   Enter the number of copies of the record you require.
6.  **Select the delivery method**:
    *   Choose how you'd like to receive the record (electronic, mail, or pick up in person).
7.  **Recipient's information (if applicable)**:
    *   If the record is for someone else, enter their name and address.
8.  **State the purpose of the request**:
    *   Select the reason for your request (employment, graduate school admission, transfer, or personal records).
9.  **Specify the urgency of the request**:
    *   Choose how soon you need the record (standard processing time, rush processing, or same-day processing).
10. **Agree to the consent statement**:
    *   Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authorize the release of the records.
11. **Specify the date of request**:
    *   Enter the date you're submitting this form.
