# monthly_business_expense_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track business expenses for the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month and expense type for the expense you are requesting.
2. Enter the amount of the expense.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments about the expense.
4. Fill out the employee and manager information.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, optional): This section provides a brief description of what this form is for and what it is used to track.
* **month** (`month`, `select_one`, optional): Select the month for which the expense occurred.
* **expense_type** (`expense_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of expense being requested (e.g., travel, meals, supplies).
* **expense_amount** (`expense_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or notes about the expense.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the employee making the request.
* **department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the employee making the request.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the manager of the employee making the request.
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the manager of the employee making the request.
* **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the manager of the employee making the request.
* **employee_email** (`employee_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the employee making the request.
* **employee_phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee making the request.
* **manager_title** (`manager_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the manager.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the expense occurred.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the expense occurred.
* **business_expense_type** (`business_expense_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of business expense (e.g., Business Expenses, Other Expenses, Other).
* **expense_category** (`expense_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category of the expense.
* **expense_subcategory** (`expense_subcategory`, `select_one`, optional): Select the subcategory of the expense.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the expense request.
* **submitted_to** (`submitted_to`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to whom the expense request is being submitted.
* **submitted_at** (`submitted_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time the expense was submitted.
* **submitted_on** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required): Enter the date the expense was submitted.
* **submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the expense request.
