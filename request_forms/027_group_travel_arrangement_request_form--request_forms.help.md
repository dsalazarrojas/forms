# Group Travel Arrangement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Travel Arrangement Request Form is used to gather information for group travel arrangements. This form helps us understand the details of your group travel, including category, travel date, travel time, travel notes, email, phone number, description, assigned tool, and output file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the category of your group travel arrangement.
*   Enter the travel date for your group.
*   Provide the travel time for your group.
*   Add any additional travel notes.
*   Enter the email address of your group's leader or representative.
*   Enter the phone number of your group's leader or representative.
*   Describe your group's travel purpose and details.
*   If applicable, assign a specific tool to your group.
*   Upload any output file relevant to your group travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category**: Choose the category of your group travel arrangement. This will help us understand the type of travel your group is conducting.
*   **Travel Date**: Enter the date of your group travel.
*   **Travel Time**: Provide the time of your group travel.
*   **Travel Note**: Add any additional notes related to your group travel.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of your group's leader or representative. This will help us ensure that you receive important notifications and updates.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your group's leader or representative.
*   **Description**: Describe your group's travel purpose and details.
*   **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, assign a specific tool to your group. This will help us understand which tool your group will be using.
*   **Output File**: Upload any output file relevant to your group travel. This could be a report, document, or any other file that may be useful for your group.
