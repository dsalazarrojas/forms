# Warehouse Slot Allocation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Slot Allocation Request Form is used to collect information from warehouse team members about their slot allocation requests. This form helps the warehouse management team to efficiently manage and allocate slots for different items, ensuring that everyone's needs are met and resources are utilized effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Request Form 1" field with your request details.
2. Provide specific information about the item in the "Item Details" field.
3. If applicable, indicate whether the item can be allocated ("Yes" or "No") in the "Item Allocation" field.
4. Enter the timing details for the request in the "Timing Details" field.
5. If any special requirements or conditions apply, provide details in the "Special Requirements" field.
6. In "Request Form 2", you can add any additional information or notes about your request.
7. Update the item status in the "Item Status" field if necessary.
8. Enter the warehouse details (e.g. rack number, location) in the "Warehouse Details" field.
9. Finally, enter your allocation request in the "Allocation Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form 1** (`request_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Item Details** (`item_details`, text, optional): Provide specific information about the item you are requesting (e.g., product name, quantity, etc.).
* **Item Allocation** (`item_allocation`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you would like the item to be allocated ("Yes" or "No").
* **Timing Details** (`timing_details`, text, optional): Enter the timing details for your request (e.g., specific date or time period).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If there are any special requirements or conditions that apply to your request, provide details here.
* **Request Form 2** (`request_form_2`, text, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your request.
* **Item Status** (`item_status`, text, optional): Update the item status if it's necessary (e.g., changed from one status to another).
* **Warehouse Details** (`warehouse_details`, text, optional): Enter the warehouse details (e.g., rack number, location).
* **Allocation Request** (`allocation_request`, text, optional): Enter your allocation request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the warehouse management team for clarification.
