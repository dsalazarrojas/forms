# Policy Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Cancellation Form is designed to help users request the cancellation of their policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of policy you wish to cancel from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your reason for cancelling the policy in the text field provided.
3. Enter the date you wish to cancel the policy from the date picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **policy_type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of policy you wish to cancel from the dropdown menu. This will help us process your request efficiently.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise reason for cancelling your policy. This will help us understand your request.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to cancel your policy. This will help us schedule the cancellation process.
