# real_estate_offer_request_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit real estate offers on properties for which you are a registered real estate agent or broker. It helps to provide all necessary information for other stakeholders to review and respond to your offer. By filling out this form, you will be able to keep track of your submitted offers and communicate with clients and other real estate professionals about the status of each offer.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields to ensure your offer is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Real Estate Agency** (`real_estate_agency`, text, required: false): Enter your real estate agency name.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter your client's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the property's address for which you are making an offer.
* **Offer Price** (`offer_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of your offer.
* **Offer Date** (`offer_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your offer. Please use the standard date format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a real estate agent, 'No' otherwise.
* **Email** (`real_estate_agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you are a real estate agent.
* **Phone** (`real_estate_agent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you are a real estate agent.
* **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to your client about the offer status.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you need to make changes or updates to your offer, please contact the real estate platform support team.
- You can use this form multiple times to submit different offers on the same property.
