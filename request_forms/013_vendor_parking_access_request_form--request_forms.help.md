# Vendor Parking Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for requesting access to a vendor parking area. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the individual requesting access, including their name, company name, email, phone number, and preferred parking location. By filling out this form, you will be requesting permission to park on site. Please ensure that this form is submitted only once for each individual requesting access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `UserName` field.
2. Enter your company name in the `companyName` field.
3. Enter your work email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Enter the specific parking location you are requesting access to in the `Parking Location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **UserName**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **companyName**: Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your company or work account.
* **Phone**: Enter your office or work phone number.
* **Parking Location**: Be as specific as possible about the parking location you need access to, including the building or department name and floor number if applicable.
