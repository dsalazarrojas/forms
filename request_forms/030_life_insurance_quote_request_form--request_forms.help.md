# life_insurance_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you get a life insurance quote by providing us with necessary personal and policy details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, age, height, and weight.
2. Provide your medical details if applicable.
3. Choose your preferred contact method and quote frequency.
4. Select the type of policy you are interested in, such as life, accident, disability, or critical illness.
5. Choose the coverage type, policy type, and policy status.
6. Select the agent you would like to work with.
7. Provide the date and time you would like to receive your quote.
8. Enter the age of the policy holder.
9. Enter your height and weight in the corresponding units.
10. Indicate your smoker status.
11. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`name`, `text`, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your ID or insurance card.
* **Medical Details** (`text`, required): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them briefly.
* **Contact Details** (`text`, required): Provide your preferred contact method for receiving the quote (phone, email, or text).
* **Quote Preferences** (`text`, optional): Describe your preferred quote frequency (e.g. weekly, monthly, quarterly).
* **Policy Coverage** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Life
	+ Accident
	+ Disability
	+ Critical Illness
	+ Long Term Care
	+ Umbrella
	+ Specialty
	+ Travel
	+ Other
* **Coverage Type** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Whole
	+ Term
	+ Universal
	+ Annual Renewable
	+ Single
	+ Group
* **Policy Type** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Individual
	+ Joint
	+ Group
* **Quote Frequency** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-Annually
	+ Annually
* **Quote Type** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Term
	+ Whole
	+ Variable
* **Policy Status** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Agent Details** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred agent:
	+ Agent 1
	+ Agent 2
	+ Agent 3
* **Policy Holder** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Self
	+ Spouse
	+ Parent
	+ Child
* **Quote Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date you would like to receive your quote.
* **Quote Time** (`time`, optional): Select the time you would like to receive your quote.
* **Life Insured** (`text`, optional): If applicable, provide your life insured's name and contact information.
* **Policy Holder Age** (`number`, required): Enter the age of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Height** (`number`, required): Enter the height of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Weight** (`number`, required): Enter the weight of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Height Unit** (`text`, required): Select the unit of measurement for the policy holder's height (inches, cm, etc.).
* **Policy Holder Weight Unit** (`text`, required): Select the unit of measurement for the policy holder's weight (lbs, kg, etc.).
* **Smoker Status** (`select_one`, required): Select your current smoker status:
	+ Non-Smoker
	+ Smoker
