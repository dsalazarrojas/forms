# Workplace Culture Suggestion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions and ideas from employees regarding workplace culture improvements. It is meant for employees to submit their thoughts and feedback on how to create a better work environment. Please ensure that your suggestions are constructive and respectful, and that you have a clear understanding of what you are suggesting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Be concise and clear in your suggestions.
3. Provide your name and email address to help the team lead or manager contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Ideas** (`employee_ideas`, text, required): This is where you should type in your ideas and suggestions for improving workplace culture. Try to be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts and opinions on what's working or not working in the current culture.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Add any additional suggestions or ideas you have. This is an opportunity to think outside the box!
* **Improvement Priority** (`improvement_priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of urgency for your suggestion. Options are Low, Medium, and High.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can keep you informed about the status of your suggestion.
* **Employee E-mail** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your suggestion.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact number in case we need to discuss your suggestion further.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Identify the team you are suggesting improvements for.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): If your team lead is a specific manager or director, please choose their name. 
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you want to be contacted for follow-up on your suggestion (True, False, or Not Specified).
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): If you want to be contacted, specify a time for follow-up.
