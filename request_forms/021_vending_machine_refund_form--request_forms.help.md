# Vending Machine Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vending Machine Refund Form is used to request a refund for a transaction made on a vending machine. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information to process your refund.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Submit the form after you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (customer_details, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information to help us identify you as the customer who made the purchase.
* **Order ID** (order_id, number, optional): If you have an Order ID for the transaction, please provide it to help us locate the corresponding record in our system.
* **Transaction Date** (transaction_date, date, optional): Please specify the date of the transaction for which you are requesting a refund.
* **Reason for Refund** (reason_for_refund, text, optional): Describe the reason for the refund, such as a machine error or a product not meeting your expectations.
* **Refund Amount** (refund_amount, number, optional): Please specify the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Product Name** (product_name, select_one, optional): Select the product that you are requesting a refund for from the available options.
* **Customer Signature** (customer_signature, select_multiple, optional): If you agree to the terms and conditions of the refund process, please select "Yes" to continue. If you disagree, please select "No" and provide a reason for your disagreement.
* **Machine Serial Number** (machine_serial_number, text, optional): If you have the machine serial number, please provide it to help us locate the machine.
* **Refund Status** (refund_status, select_one, optional): Please select the status of your refund request from the available options.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, optional): If you have any additional information about the tool used to process your refund, please provide it.
* **Category** (category, text, optional): Any additional category or classification related to your refund request.
* **Description** (description, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or information about your refund request.
