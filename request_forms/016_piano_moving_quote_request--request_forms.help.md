# Piano Moving Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a piano moving quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to allow us to communicate with you regarding your quote.
3. Provide your Phone number for us to contact you if needed.
4. Select your Address type (Street address, PO Box, or Rural delivery).
5. Specify the type of piano you need to move.
6. Choose a Date of move and Time of move for your piano.
7. If you have any Comments or special instructions, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required/optional): This is your first name. It is not required, but please fill it out if possible.
* **Last name** (text, required/optional): This is your last name. Like first name, it's not required but it helps to personalize our communication.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): This is the email address we will use to contact you regarding your quote.
* **Phone number** (text, required/optional): This is your phone number. We may need to contact you to confirm details.
* **Address** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like us to address your piano (Street address, PO Box, or Rural delivery).
* **Piano type** (text, required/optional): Specify the type of piano you need to move (e.g., upright, baby grand, etc.).
* **Date of move** (date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to have your piano moved.
* **Time of move** (time, required/optional): Select the time you would like your piano moved.
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): If you have any special instructions or comments, please enter them here.
