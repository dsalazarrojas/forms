# Marketing Collaboration Brief Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request marketing collaboration briefs from internal teams. It is intended for project leads and team members to gather essential information about a marketing project, ensuring that all necessary details are captured before the project commences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. Review the optional fields for any additional details that may be relevant to your project.
3. Submit the completed form to the designated project lead or team member for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Details** (`brand_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief overview of the brand, including its name, industry, or any other relevant details.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required/true): Enter the primary objective of the marketing project. This will help the project lead and team members understand the project's purpose and scope.
* **Timelines** (`timelines`, text, required/optional): Outline the project timelines, including the start and end dates, or any significant milestones.
* **Budgets** (`budgets`, text, required/optional): Provide an estimated budget for the project, which will help in planning and resource allocation.
* **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the relevant supporting materials that will aid in the project's success, such as images, videos, or documents.
* **Brand Assets** (`brand_assets`, note, required/optional): Use this field for any additional brand-specific information or resources that are relevant to the project.
* **Agency Info** (`agency_info`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the external agency or team that will be working on the project.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the project lead or team member responsible for the project.
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements`, text, required/true): This is a critical field that outlines the project's requirements, such as the target audience, channels, or any specific deliverables.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any further details or concerns that you would like to share with the project lead or team member, please use this field.
* **Agency Preferences** (`agency_preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the preferred agency or team to work on the project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one, required/true): Select the project lead or team member who will be responsible for overseeing the project.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, number, required/optional): If required, specify the number of team members needed for the project.
