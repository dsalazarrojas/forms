# Veterinary Staff Training Session Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Staff Training Session Request Form is used to collect information about veterinary staff training needs and preferences. This form helps administrators and training coordinators plan and schedule training sessions for veterinary staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select whether or not you need a training session.
2. Provide the name of the clinic.
3. Select the staff member requesting the training.
4. Choose the type of training session you need.
5. Specify the topic of the training session.
6. Select a date and time for the training session.
7. Enter the duration of the training session.
8. Provide the number of participants for the session.
9. Specify the location for the training session.
10. Choose the status of the training session.
11. Enter any additional comments or requests you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Training Session** (`<type: select_multiple>`, required/optional): Check this box to indicate that you need a training session.
* **Clinics Name** (`<name: text>`, required/optional): Enter the name of the clinic where you work.
* **Staff Name** (`<name: select_one>`, required/optional): Select the name of the staff member requesting the training.
* **Training Session Type** (`<name: select_one>`, required/optional): Choose the type of training session you need (e.g., "Training Session" or "Other").
* **Training Topic** (`<name: text>`, required/optional): Enter the topic of the training session.
* **Training Date and Time** (`<name: date>`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you need for the training session.
* **Training Duration** (`<name: number>`, required/optional): Enter the length of the training session.
* **Number of Participants** (`<name: number>`, required/optional): Enter the number of participants for the training session.
* **Session Location** (`<name: text>`, required/optional): Enter the location for the training session.
* **Session Status** (`<name: select_one>`, required/optional): Choose the status of the training session (e.g., "Active", "Pending", or "Completed").
* **Requester Comments** (`<name: text>`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you have.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please ensure you provide all required information for your request to be processed correctly.
