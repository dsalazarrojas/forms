# Archived Website Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Archived Website Request" form is used to capture information from users when they are unable to access a specific webpage or page topic on the website. This form helps the relevant team to identify and resolve the issue promptly, ensuring that users can find the information they need on the website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is the best way to contact you, but you can also choose to be contacted by phone if you prefer.
3.  Select the type of content you are looking for from the "Type of Content Requested" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the URL of the original page you were trying to access, if you know it.
5.  Describe the page you were trying to access in the "Describe What You Were Looking For" field.
6.  If you used specific search terms to find the page, enter them in the "Search Terms Used" field.
7.  Select the general topic area of the page you were looking for from the "General Topic Area" dropdown menu.
8.  Indicate the level of urgency for your request from the "How Urgent is Your Request" dropdown menu.
9.  Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
10. Finally, add any additional context or comments that might help the team understand your request in the "Additional Context or Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name (visitor_name)** (`visitor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself. This will help the team address your request efficiently.
*   **Email Address (visitor_email)** (`visitor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly with updates on your request.
*   **Phone Number (visitor_phone)** (`visitor_phone`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
*   **Type of Content Requested (request_type)** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of content you are requesting from the dropdown menu, such as a product page, service information, or support documentation.
*   **Original Page URL - if known (original_page_url)** (`original_page_url`, `text`, optional): If you know the URL of the page you were trying to access, enter it here.
*   **Describe What You Were Looking For (page_description)** (`page_description`, `text`, required): Describe the page you were trying to find in this field.
*   **Search Terms Used (search_terms)** (`search_terms`, `text`, optional): If you used specific search terms to find the page, enter them here.
*   **General Topic Area (page_topic)** (`page_topic`, `select_one`, optional): Select the general topic area of the page you were looking for from the dropdown menu.
*   **How Urgent is Your Request (urgency_level)** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the level of urgency for your request from the dropdown menu.
*   **Preferred Contact Method (contact_preference)** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted, either by email or phone.
*   **Additional Context or Comments (additional_context)** (`additional_context`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional context or comments that might help the team find the information you need.
