# retail_voice_assistant_integration_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Voice Assistant Integration Application form is designed to collect information from retail businesses that want to integrate their store with the Retail Voice Assistant platform. This form is used to document business owner's requests for support or integration with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the store ID.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose the type of request you have (integration or support).
6. Enter a brief description of your request (optional).
7. (Optional) Choose a location for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store ID** (`store_id`, number, required): Enter the store ID for the retail business.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as the representative of the retail business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "integration" and "support" to indicate the type of request you have.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your request (optional).
* **Location** (`location`, time, optional): Enter the location where the request will be handled (optional).
