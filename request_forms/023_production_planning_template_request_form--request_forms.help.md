# Production Planning Template Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Planning Template Request Form is a tool for requesting a production planning template. This template is used for organizing and coordinating production activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of template you need by choosing one or more options from the "Production Planning Template" dropdown menu.
2. Fill in the required fields, including "Industry", "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", "Contact Email", "Company", "Location", and "Comments".
3. Provide any additional details or comments about your request in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Planning Template** (`Production Planning Template`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more template types that best match your needs. This will help us to better understand your requirements and provide the most suitable template for you.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, text, optional): If applicable, please specify the industry related to your production planning template request.
* **Volume** (`Volume`, number, optional): If you have a specific volume in mind for your production, please enter it here. This will help us to provide a more tailored template.
* **Features** (`Features`, select_one, optional): Choose one or more features that you need for your production planning template.
* **Delivery Timeframe** (`Delivery Timeframe`, time, optional): If you have a specific delivery timeframe in mind, please enter it here.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, optional): If you have a specific delivery date in mind, please enter it here.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the contact person responsible for this request.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Please provide the contact phone number of the contact person responsible for this request.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Please provide the contact email address of the contact person responsible for this request.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the company associated with this request.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Please provide the location associated with this request.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your request here.

## Tips
* Make sure to clearly and concisely describe your template requirements to help us provide the most suitable template for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll do our best to process your request promptly and provide you with the requested template.
