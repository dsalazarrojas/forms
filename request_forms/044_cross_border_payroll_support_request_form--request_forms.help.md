# cross_boder_payroll_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect support requests from employees for payroll-related issues. It's meant to be used by employees who need assistance with their payroll, and it's essential to ensure that each submission is unique and not a duplicate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your request details.
2. Upload a scanned copy of your passport.
3. Enter your ID number.
4. Upload a scan of your visa.
5. Provide a copy of your residence certificate.
6. Upload a scan of your birth certificate.
7. Choose the priority level of your request.
8. Add any comments you'd like to include.
9. Identify yourself as the person submitting the request.
10. Select the date you submitted the request.
11. Select the current status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required/optional): This is the main section where you can describe your payroll support request in detail.
* **Passport Scanned Copy** (`passport_scanned_copy`, text, required): Please upload a scanned copy of your passport.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, text, required): Enter your ID number.
* **Visa Scan** (`visa_scan`, text, required): Please upload a scan of your visa.
* **Residence Certificate** (`residence_certificate`, text, required): Provide a copy of your residence certificate.
* **Birth Certificate** (`birth_certificate`, text, required): Upload a scan of your birth certificate.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Choose the priority level of your request. Options are High, Medium, and Low.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Identify yourself as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Select the date you submitted the request.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of your request. Options are New, In Progress, and Resolved.
