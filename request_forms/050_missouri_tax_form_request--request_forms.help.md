# Missouri Tax Form Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request tax-related documents or forms from the Missouri tax authority. Fill out this form with your details to submit a request and get the necessary documents or forms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the general information fields: *name*, *phone*, *email*, and *address*.
2. Select the *document type* you are requesting.
3. Enter your *tax ID number*.
4. Provide your *date of birth*.
5. Enter your *zip*.
6. Select your *city*.
7. Select your *state* (Missouri).
8. Select your *country*.
9. Enter any *documents* you are attaching to your request.
10. Select the *request date* you would like to submit the request.
11. Select the *status* of your request (Active or Inactive).
12. Add any *comments* regarding your request.
13. Select the *department* responsible for your request (Yes or No).
14. Enter the *contact name* for the person submitting the request.
15. Enter the *contact email* for the person submitting the request.
16. Enter the *contact phone* number for the person submitting the request.
17. Select the *department 2* responsible for your request (Yes or No).
18. Select the *request type* (Yes or No).
19. Select the *request status* (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter your basic contact information, including your name, phone number, email address, and address.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as it should appear on the document or form.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for contact.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for correspondence.
* **Document Type (5)**: Select the type of document or form you are requesting.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **Tax ID Number (7)**: Enter your tax identification number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth (8)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Zip (9)**: Enter your zip code.
* **City (10)**: Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State (11)**: Select Missouri as your state of residence or work.
* **Country (12)**: Enter your country of residence or work.
* **Documents (13)**: Attach any relevant documents to your request.
* **Request Date (14)**: Select the date you would like to submit the request.
* **Status (15)**: Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments (16)**: Add any comments or notes regarding your request.
* **Department (17)**: Select Yes if the department is responsible for your request, or No if not.
* **Contact Name (18)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Contact Email (19)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the request.
* **Contact Phone (20)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Department 2 (21)**: Select Yes if the second department is responsible for your request, or No if not.
* **Request Type (22)**: Select Yes if you are requesting a specific type of document or form, or No if not.
* **Request Status (23)**: Select Active or Inactive for the status of your request.
