# Vendor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Request Form is a tool used to collect information from vendors about their events, parties, or other activities. This form will be reviewed and processed internally to ensure that we are providing accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information about the event or activity. Make sure to be as detailed as possible, and double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Request** (`vendor_request_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the event or activity.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the vendor and their relation to the event or activity.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event or activity (Event, Party, or Other).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event or activity.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Describe the location of the event or activity.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Please describe the event or activity in detail.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Vendor Request Message** (`vendor_request_message`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or requests from the vendor.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific tools or resources allocated to the vendor.
