# Towing Service Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information for towing services, including vehicle details and authorization. It helps the towing service provider to quickly and accurately collect necessary information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information for the towing request details.
2. Select the correct authorization status.
3. Provide details about the vehicle being towed, including make, model, and year.
4. Enter the location where the vehicle was towed from and the location where it will be dropped off.
5. Confirm the towing service was provided.
6. Add any additional comments about the tow.
7. Enter the name of the towing service company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Towing Request Details**: This field is for entering the details of the towing request, such as a brief description of the incident.
* **Authorization**: This field is for confirming whether the towing service provider has been authorized to tow the vehicle. Select 'Towing authorized' if the service provider has been authorized, otherwise select 'Not authorized'.
* **Towing Vehicle Details**: This field is for providing details about the vehicle being towed, including its make, model, and year. This information will help the service provider to better serve the customer.
* **Towed By**: This field is for entering the name of the person or company that was towing the vehicle.
* **Towed At**: This field is for entering the date and time when the vehicle was towed.
* **Towing Operator**: This field is for entering the name of the towing operator or service provider.
* **Pickup Location**: This field is for entering the location where the vehicle was picked up from.
* **Dropoff Location**: This field is for entering the location where the vehicle will be dropped off.
* **Towing Service Confirmation**: This field is for confirming whether the towing service was provided. Select 'True' if the service was provided, otherwise select 'False'.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for entering any additional comments or details about the tow.
* **Towing Service Name**: This field is for entering the name of the towing service company.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that the towing service provider has all the necessary information to serve you better.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the towing service provider directly for assistance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill in, so please take your time and fill it out carefully.
