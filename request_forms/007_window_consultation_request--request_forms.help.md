# Window Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Consultation Request form is designed to collect information from users for a window consultation. This form helps internal staff to schedule an appointment with the user and understand their preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Company Name** (if applicable) in the "Company Name" field.
4. Input your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Choose your preferred **Time** for the consultation in the "Preferred Time" field.
6. Specify your **Service Needs** by selecting one or more options (e.g., Window Installation, Replacement Windows, or Other (please specify)).
7. Select your desired **Appointment Date** in the "Appointment Date" field.
8. Choose your preferred **Appointment Date** if different from the standard appointment date in the "Preferred Date" field.
9. Select your preferred time slot for the consultation in the "Preferred Time Slot" field (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
10. Choose whether you'd like a follow-up from the "Follow Up" field (True or False).
11. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A field to enter your first name for the consultation.
* **Last Name**: A field to enter your last name for the consultation.
* **Company Name**: An optional field to input your company name (if applicable).
* **Contact Number**: A field to input your contact number for communication.
* **Preferred Time**: A field to select your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Service Needs**: A field to select one or more services you require (e.g., Window Installation, Replacement Windows, or Other (please specify)).
* **Appointment Date**: A field to select the date for the consultation.
* **Preferred Date**: An optional field to select a different appointment date if desired.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: A field to select your preferred time slot for the consultation (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Follow Up**: A field to choose whether you'd like a follow-up (True or False).
* **Email**: A field to input your email address for communication.
