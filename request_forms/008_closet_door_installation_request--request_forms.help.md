# Closet Door Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture detailed information from customers about their closet door installation request. It includes details about the customer, property information, closet specifications, installation requirements, and budget preferences. Please fill out this form to ensure we have the necessary information to provide a tailored solution for your door installation needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your full name and contact details to help us reach out to you with any necessary questions or clarifications.
2. Specify the location of your property and the type of property you own to help us determine the best installation solution.
3. Provide details about the closet location within the property to ensure our team can access the area for installation.
4. Choose the type of door you desire to be installed, considering factors like style, material, and finish.
5. Select your preferred installation date and time flexibility to help us schedule the work accordingly.
6. Indicate your availability for installer access to complete the work at your preferred time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your name, as it will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your primary contact email address to ensure we can reach out to you with updates.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Provide your preferred contact number to be reached at for any questions or clarifications.
* **Property Information** (Note, Optional): Describe your property location and any specific details that might impact our work.
* **Property Address** (Text, Required): Enter the complete street address of your property to help us locate the site.
* **City, State, Zip** (Text, Required): Enter your city, state, and zip to help us understand your location.
* **Property Type** (Select One, Required): Choose between options like residential, commercial, or other to guide our installation approach.
* **Location of Closet** (Select One, Required): Select the area within the property where the closet is located, such as a master bedroom or utility room.
* **Current Closet Type** (Select One, Required): Select the current state of the closet, like open shelving or a curtain rod.
* **Closet Opening Width** (Text, Required): Enter the width of the closet opening in inches or feet for accurate installation.
* **Closet Opening Height** (Text, Required): Enter the height of the closet opening in inches or feet for accurate installation.
* **Door Type and Style** (Note, Optional): Describe your preferred door style and any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Desired Door Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of door you desire, considering factors like bifold, sliding, or swinging doors.
* **Preferred Material** (Select One, Required): Select the material for your door, such as wood, composite, or metal.
* **Preferred Finish or Color** (Text, Optional): Describe the finish or color you prefer for your door.
* **Mirrored Surface** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if you want a mirrored surface on your door.
* **Installation Timeline** (Note, Optional): Describe your desired installation timeline to help us schedule the work accordingly.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (Date, Required): Choose a date for installation, considering your availability and schedule.
* **Flexibility** (Select One, Required): Indicate your flexibility in case of delays or schedule adjustments.
* **Installer Access** (Select One, Required): Choose a time slot for installer access to complete the work.
* **Approximate Budget Range** (Select One, Optional): Select an approximate budget range to guide our pricing and services.
* **Additional Work or Modifcations** (Text, Optional): Describe any additional work or modifications needed for the installation.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you might have about the installation.

Note: Some fields have optional or flexible requirements, allowing you to adjust according to your specific needs and preferences.
