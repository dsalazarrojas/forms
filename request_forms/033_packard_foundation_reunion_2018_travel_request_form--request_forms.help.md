# Packard Foundation Reunion 2018 Travel Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information from attendees who are traveling for the 2018 Packard Foundation Reunion event. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure we can plan accordingly for your travel arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully, as your responses will help us better understand your travel plans.
2. Select the correct options for each field to ensure accurate information is collected.
3. Fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Are you traveling for this event?** (Traveling, Select Multiple, *): This question helps us determine if you will be traveling for the 2018 Packard Foundation Reunion event. Choose either "Yes" or "No" depending on your plans.
- **Are you attending this event?** (Events Attending, Select Multiple, *): This question ensures we know if you will be participating in the event. Select "Yes" or "No".
- **Have you submitted a request?** (Submitting Request, Select Multiple, *): If you've already submitted a request, please select "Yes" so we can avoid duplicating efforts. Otherwise, choose "No".
- **Travel purpose** (Travel Purpose, Select One, *): Here, you can specify the reason for your travel, whether it's Personal or Business.
- **How are you planning to travel?** (Travel Arrangement, Select One, *): Choose how you plan to arrive at the event, whether by plane, car, or other means.
- **Travel information** (Travel Information, Text, *): Please provide any additional travel information or comments you'd like to share with us.
- **Form submission** (Form Submission, Select One, *): This confirms whether you've submitted this form. Choose "Submit" to complete your response or "Not Submit" if you've not completed it.
- **Contact information** (Contact Information, Select Multiple, *): If you have contact information that's up to date, please select "Yes". We will use this to get in touch with you in case of any last-minute updates.
- **Additional travel details** (Travel Details, Text, *): Here, you can provide any further information that will help us better understand your travel needs or plans.
- **Form submission confirmation** (Form Submission, Select One, *): This final question confirms that you've completed the form accurately. Choose "Submit" to confirm your information.
