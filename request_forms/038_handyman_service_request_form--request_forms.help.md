# HandymanserviceRequestForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information, service requests, and additional details from customers. It's meant to be completed by customers who require a handymans service, whether it's for a one-time task or an ongoing service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section (page 1) with your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
2. In the service request section (page 1), select the service that you require from the provided options (Service 1, Service 2, or Service 3).
3. In the service address section (page 1), enter the address where you need the service to be performed.
4. In the service message section (page 1), provide a brief description of the service you require.
5. If you have any additional information or attachments, you can upload them in the upload attachment section (page 2) or provide them in the additional information section (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contactinfo`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number in this section to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Service Request** (`servicerequest`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you require from the options provided.
* **Service Address** (`serviceaddress`, text, required): Enter the address where you need the service to be performed.
* **Service Message** (`servicemessage`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the service you require.

### Page 2: Service Request
* **Upload Attachment** (`uploadattachment`, text, optional): If you have any relevant attachments or files, you can upload them here. This field is optional.
* **Additional Information** (`additionalinfo`, text, required): Provide any additional information or details that may help us understand your service request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure we can process your service request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
