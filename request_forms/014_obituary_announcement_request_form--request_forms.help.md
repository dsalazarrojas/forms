# Obituary Announcement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an obituary announcement for a deceased individual. It includes personal details such as first name, last name, date and time of death, burial date and time, location, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as you will be responsible for the information's correctness and validity.
2. Ensure that you have the required information and permissions to complete the form.
3. Please be aware that this form will be publicly accessible.
4. If you need to make any corrections after submission, please contact the relevant authorities or the administrators of the obituary announcement system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the deceased.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the deceased.
* **Date of death** (`date_of_death`, date, required): Enter the date of death (DD/MM/YYYY format).
* **Time of death** (`time_of_death`, time, optional): Enter the time of death (HH:MM format).
* **Death location** (`death_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the individual passed away.
* **Funeral home address** (`funeral_home_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the funeral home where the service will be held.
* **Obituary text** (`obituary_text`, text, required): Enter a brief biographical description of the deceased.
* **Burial site** (`burial_site`, text, optional): Enter the location of the burial site.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact for further information.
* **Funeral home phone** (`funeral_home_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the funeral home where the deceased was held.
* **Burial site phone** (`burial_site_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the burial site.
* **Death certificate number** (`death_certificate_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the death certificate.
* **Priest contact information** (`priest_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the priest or the person to contact for religious rites.
* **Burial details** (`burial_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the burial service.
* **Burial date** (`burial_date`, date, required): Enter the burial date (DD/MM/YYYY format).
* **Burial time** (`burial_time`, time, required): Enter the burial time (HH:MM format).
* **Burial place** (`burial_place`, text, optional): Enter the location of the burial site.
* **Funeral home** (`funeral_home`, text, optional): Enter the name of the funeral home.
* **Priest name** (`priest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the priest or the person to contact for religious rites.
* **Church name** (`church_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the church.
* **Obituary date** (`obituary_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the obituary announcement (DD/MM/YYYY format).
* **Burial service** (`burial_service`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the burial service.
* **Viewing** (`viewing`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the viewing service.
* **Funeral services** (`funeral_services`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the funeral services.
* **Obituary text 2** (`obituary_text_2`, text, required): Enter any additional biographical information about the deceased.
