# Funeral Place Guest Book Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for the guest book at a funeral place, where guests can express their condolences, share their memories, and sign their name and message. It helps the funeral service staff to manage and respond to these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your first and last name, which will help us to recognize you when we reply to your request.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your request and provide any updates.
3. Write a message to express your condolences and share your memories of the deceased.
4. If you have any phone number, please provide it so we can contact you for any further information.
5. Please select the funeral home where you plan to visit for the funeral service.
6. Choose a service date for the funeral from the provided options.
7. Select the time for the funeral service from the time options provided.
8. Leave a message for the funeral service staff (optional, but appreciated).
9. For those who wish to participate in other funeral services or activities, you may select multiple options.
10. Please provide any notes or information you want to share with us.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name so we can address you properly.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name so we can address you properly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Express your condolences and share your memories of the deceased.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Funeral Home** (`funeral_home`, select_one, optional): Select the funeral home where you plan to visit for the funeral service.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): Choose a service date for the funeral from the provided options.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time for the funeral service from the time options provided.
* **Message 2** (`message_2`, text, optional): Leave another message for the funeral service staff (optional, but appreciated).
* **Funeral Service** (`funeral_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple services or activities you wish to participate in.
* **Other** (`funeral_service_1`, select_one, optional): If the above options do not fit your needs, select "Other" and provide a specific service or activity you would like to participate in.
* **Other 1** (`funeral_service_2`, select_one, optional): If you select "Other" above, please select "Other 1" and provide a specific service or activity you would like to participate in.
* **Other 2** (`funeral_service_3`, select_one, optional): If you select "Other" above, please select "Other 2" and provide a specific service or activity you would like to participate in.
* **Other 3** (`funeral_service_4`, select_one, optional): If you select "Other" above, please select "Other 3" and provide a specific service or activity you would like to participate in.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or notes you would like to share with us.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your request.
