# Environmental Impact Review Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Review Registration form is designed to gather information about a project's environmental impact. This form is intended for projects that may have potential environmental implications and requires review to ensure compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
2. Enter the location of your project in the "Project Location" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Project Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your project.
* **Project Location**: This field is optional but is required for projects that have specific location-based requirements or regulations. Enter the exact location where your project will take place.
* **Project Description**: This field is optional but is recommended to provide a brief summary of your project. Please provide a clear and concise description of your project's purpose, scope, and any relevant details.
* **Project Contact**: This field is optional but is required for any project that requires communication with the project team or stakeholders. Enter your name, email, and phone number to facilitate communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth review process.
- If you have any supporting documents or additional information, please attach them to the form or provide a link to download them.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us through the project contact field or by reaching out to us directly.
