# Truck Air Intake Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers or clients to request upgrades to their truck air intakes. It is an internal tool used by service teams to gather information for upgrading services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Service Type**: Select the type of service you need from the available options (Regular, Express, Emergency, Overnight, Weekend).
2. **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to have your truck air intake upgraded.
3. **Service Team**: Enter the name of the team you would like to work with for this upgrade (if applicable).
4. **Vehicle Number**: Enter your vehicle's plate number or VIN.
5. **Shop Name**: If you are working with a specific shop or mechanic, enter their name here.
6. **Notes**: Add any additional information or notes about your upgrade request.
7. **File Upload**: If you have any supporting files or documentation, upload them here.
8. **Service Requester**: Enter your name or company name as the person or company requesting the upgrade.
9. **Upload File**: Note that the label for this field is incorrect as there is no corresponding `file_upload` field with a `label` field in the YAML. It should be corrected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the available options to indicate the urgency or type of service you need.
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like to have your truck air intake upgraded.
* **Service Team** (`Service Team`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the team you would like to work with for this upgrade.
* **Vehicle Number** (`Vehicle Number`, text, required: false): Enter your vehicle's plate number or VIN for reference.
* **Shop Name** (`Shop Name`, text, required: false): If you are working with a specific shop or mechanic, enter their name here.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional information or notes about your upgrade request.
* **File Upload** (`File Upload`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting files or documentation related to your upgrade request.
* **Service Requester** (`Service Requester`, text, required: false): Enter your name or company name as the person or company requesting the upgrade.
