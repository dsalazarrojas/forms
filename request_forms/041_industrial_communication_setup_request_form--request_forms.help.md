# Industrial Communication Setup Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request setup of industrial communication systems. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for setup and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
2. Provide the **Date** and **Time** for your desired communication setup.
3. Specify the **Request Type**, which can be one of the following:
	- Industrial
	- Network
	- Connectivity
	- If your request type is not listed, please specify it in the **Request Type Other** field.
4. For **Industrial Network** and **Connectivity** fields, please provide the necessary details.
5. Choose the staff member you would like to **Assign** to your request. This will ensure that they can follow up with you.
6. In **Submitter** fields, please provide your personal information, including **Email**, **Phone Number**, **Comments**, **Company**, **Position**, **Submitter Date**, and **Submitter Time**. These details will be used for follow-up purposes.
7. In **Submitter Notes**, please add any additional comments or details that you feel are necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name for internal communication and follow-up purposes.
  * **Last Name**: Your last name for internal communication and follow-up purposes.
  * **Date**: The desired communication setup date.
  * **Time**: The desired communication setup time.
  * **Email**: Your email address for internal communication and follow-up purposes.
  * **Phone Number**: Your phone number for internal follow-up and communication.
  * **Request Type**: The type of communication setup you are requesting (Industrial, Network, or Connectivity).
  * **Request Type Other**: If your request type is not listed, specify it here.
  * **Industrial Network**: The industrial network you are requesting setup for.
  * **Connectivity**: The connectivity you are requesting setup for.
  * **Schedule**: The schedule for the communication setup (not a priority for this form but can be useful for follow-up).
  * **Notes**: Additional comments or details for internal follow-up purposes.
  * **Assigned To**: The staff member you would like to be assigned to your request.
  * **Submitter Email**: Your email address for internal follow-up and communication purposes.
  * **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number for internal follow-up and communication purposes.
  * **Submitter Comments**: Any additional comments or details you would like to share.
  * **Submitter Company**: Your company for internal follow-up and communication purposes.
  * **Submitter Position**: Your position for internal follow-up and communication purposes.
  * **Submitter Date**: The date you submit this form.
  * **Submitter Time**: The time you submit this form.
  * **Submitter Notes**: Any additional comments or details for internal follow-up purposes.
