**Public Health Summit Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to request a leave of absence from the Public Health Summit. It helps to gather necessary information for approval and processing of the request.

When filling out this form, please make sure you haven't already submitted a request for the same event. If you're unsure, you can check with your supervisor or HR representative to confirm.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the event ID of the summit you wish to take a leave of absence for.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose your start and end dates of absence.
6. Specify the reason for your leave.
7. Select the organization you are associated with.
8. Optionally, add any additional comments if needed.
9. Choose the person submitting the form (submitter).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID (1)**: Select the ID of the summit event you wish to take a leave of absence for.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your valid email address for official communication.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Start Date (5)**: Choose the date you plan to start your leave of absence from.
* **End Date (6)**: Choose the date you plan to end your leave of absence until.
* **Reason (7)**: Clearly state the reason for your leave of absence.
* **Organization (8)**: Select the organization you are associated with.
* **Comment (9)**: Optionally add any additional comments related to your request.
* **Submitter (10)**: Choose the person submitting the form on your behalf.
