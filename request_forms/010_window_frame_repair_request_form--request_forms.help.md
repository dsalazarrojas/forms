# Window Frame Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from homeowners or property owners to request repairs related to the window frames of their properties. It helps the maintenance team or repair service provider to understand the nature of the problem and provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the issue with your window frame in the "Request Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Details" field, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. If applicable, attach photos of the issue in the "Photos" field to help the repair team better understand the problem.
4. Specify your preferred appointment time in the "Preferred Appointment Time" field.
5. Enter any additional property details that might be relevant to the repair in the "Property Details" field.
6. Once you have filled out all the required information, click on the "Submit Form" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details**: A brief description of the issue with your window frame.
* **Contact Details**: Your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Photos**: Attach photos of the issue with your window frame to help us better understand the problem.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Specify a preferred time for the repair appointment.
* **Property Details**: Any additional details about your property that might be relevant to the repair.
* **Submit Form**: Click this button to send your request to our team.

## Tips

* Make sure to include clear descriptions and photos to help us understand the issue with your window frame.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements for the repair, such as a preferred schedule, please let us know in the "Property Details" field.
* Our team will review your request and contact you to schedule a repair appointment based on your preferred time and other factors.
