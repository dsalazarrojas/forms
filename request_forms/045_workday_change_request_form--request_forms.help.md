# Workday Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to an employee's workday, which may include changes to their schedule, shift start and end times, or other workday-related requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and the reason for your request in the "Employee Requesting Workday Change" and "Change Reason" fields.
2. Select the day of the week that the change applies to from the dropdown menu.
3. If necessary, enter the Shift Start and End Times and your Employee Workday Preference.
4. If your request is approved, select "Approved" from the "Approved Status" dropdown menu. If it's declined, select "Declined". If it's pending review, select "Pending".
5. Finally, select the status of your request from the "Request Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Workday Change** (`request_workday_change_1`, text, required): Briefly describe the change you are requesting, such as a shift change or a day off.
- **Employee Requesting Workday Change** (`employee_requesting_workday_change_1`, text, optional): Enter your name as the employee making the request.
- **Change Reason** (`change_reason_1`, text, optional): Explain why you are requesting the change.
- **Date of Change** (`date_of_change_1`, date, optional): Enter the date of the change, if applicable.
- **Workday Requesting Workday Change** (`workday_requesting_workday_change_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the day of the week that the change applies to.
- **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time_1`, time, optional): Enter your desired Shift Start Time, if applicable.
- **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time_1`, time, optional): Enter your desired Shift End Time, if applicable.
- **Employee Workday Preference** (`employee_workday_preference_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred workday schedule options, such as "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Late Evening".
- **Manager Review** (`manager_review_1`, date, optional): Enter the date for the manager's review, if applicable.
- **Approved Status** (`approved_status_1`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request, such as "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
- **Request Status** (`request_status_1`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request, such as "Open", "Closed", or "Archived".
- **Reason for Decline** (`reason_for_decline_1`, text, optional): Enter the reason for decline, if the request is declined.
- **Comments** (`comments_1`, text, optional): Add any comments or additional information about your request.
