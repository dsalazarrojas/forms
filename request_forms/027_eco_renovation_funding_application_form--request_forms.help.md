# Eco Renovation Funding Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eco Renovation Funding Application Form is designed to gather necessary information for individuals or entities seeking funding for eco-friendly projects or initiatives. This form helps us understand project details, funding options, and submission requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your project's details in the `Project Details` field.
2. Select the specific funding option you are interested in by answering the questions in the `Select One Funding` and `Select Multiple Funding` fields.
3. Attach any relevant supporting documents to the `Supporting Documents` field.
4. Provide your submission details, including an email address and phone number, in the `Submission Email` and `Submission Phone` fields.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the `Submission Comments` field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: Enter your project's details, including project name, description, and any relevant information.
* **Project Funding**: Enter the amount of funding you are seeking for your project.
* **Project Documents**: Attach any documents related to your project, such as project plans, proposals, or supporting materials.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any documents that support your project, such as receipts, invoices, or certificates.
* **Submission Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you for further information.
* **Submission Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Select One Funding**: Select one of the funding options you are interested in.
* **Select Multiple Funding**: Select multiple funding options you are interested in.
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.
* **Submit Title**: Enter a title for your application.
* **Submit Description**: Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Submit Email**: Re-enter your email address where we can contact you for further information.
* **Submit Phone**: Re-enter your phone number where we can contact you for further information.
