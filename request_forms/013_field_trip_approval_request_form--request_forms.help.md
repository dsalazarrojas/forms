# Field Trip Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to request approval to attend an educational field trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student Name** in the `student_name` field.
2. Enter the **Student Group** you are part of in the `student_group` field.
3. Enter your school's **Name** in the `school_name` field.
4. Select a **Trip Date** for the field trip in the `trip_date` field.
5. Enter the **Trip Time** for the field trip in the `trip_time` field.
6. Enter the **Trip Location** where the field trip will take place in the `trip_location` field.
7. Describe the **Trip Description** and activities you plan to do in the `trip_description` field.
8. Enter the **Sponsor Name** and their contact information (if applicable) in the `sponsor_name` and `sponsor_email` fields.
9. Ensure all required fields (`form_title`, `student_name`, `student_group`, and `school_name`) are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for the field trip request.
* **student_name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the student requesting the field trip.
* **student_group** (`student_group`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student group or class you are part of.
* **school_name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your school's name.
* **trip_date** (`trip_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the field trip.
* **trip_time** (`trip_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the field trip.
* **trip_location** (`trip_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the field trip.
* **trip_description** (`trip_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the activities and events planned for the field trip.
* **sponsor_name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the sponsor or organization providing support for the field trip.
* **sponsor_email** (`sponsor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the sponsor or organization providing support for the field trip.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any potential issues or miscommunications.
* Attach any additional relevant documents or information as required by your school or sponsor.
* Submit the form to your school administrator or sponsor for approval.
