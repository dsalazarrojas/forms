# House Cleaning Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an estimate for house cleaning services. Please fill it out to provide necessary information for the cleaning team to prepare for your job.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
2. Choose the date and time for your requested cleaning service.
3. Specify whether you would like any additional services.
4. Enter the estimated cost of the job.
5. Add any additional notes about your job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Cleaning Estimate**: Enter a brief description of the cleaning services you require (e.g., one-time clean, regular cleaning service, etc.). 
* **Contact Details**: Fill out your name, phone number, and email. Please enter a valid email address as this will help us contact you with the estimated cost and schedule details.
* **Address**: Enter the address where the cleaning service will be performed.
* **Phone**: Select your preferred phone option (Option 1 or Option 2) to allow us to contact you easily.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive the estimated cost and schedule details.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to have the cleaning service performed.
* **Time**: Enter the time on which you would like the cleaning service to start.
* **Services**: Select all the services you require (e.g., Yes for each service you need).
* **Estimated Cost**: Enter the estimated cost for the cleaning service (if known).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your job (e.g., specific areas of focus, allergies, etc.).
