# Emoticon Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their emoticon update requests. Users can submit suggestions or feedback on existing emoticons, and the form helps the team to understand their requests accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Community suggestions` field with any new emoticon suggestions you have. This field is optional.
2. Provide any `Design changes` you think are necessary. This field is optional.
3. Upload any `Supporting files` that can help us understand your request.
4. Add any `Comments` related to your request.
5. If you have some `Feedback` to provide, please enter it.
6. Enter your `Contact Information`.
7. Provide the `Email` address you would like us to contact you with.
8. Enter the `Date` when you would like your request to be processed.
9. Provide a detailed `Request Details` about your request.
10. Select one option from the `Select One` field.
11. Select multiple options from the `Select Multiple` field.
12. Choose one or multiple options from the `Checkboxes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community suggestions** (`community_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any new emoticon suggestions you have.
* **Design changes** (`design_changes`, text, optional): Provide any design changes you think are necessary.
* **Supporting files** (`supporting_files`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files that can help us understand your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments related to your request.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have feedback, please enter it.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address you would like us to contact you with.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like your request to be processed.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your request.
* **Select One** (`select_1`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Checkboxes** (`checkboxes`, checkbox, optional): Choose one or multiple options from the list.
