# Return Goods Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the process of returning goods due to defects or issues. Fill out this form to request authorization to return goods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
2. Type your order ID or reference number.
3. Specify the name of the product you are returning.
4. Describe the reason for your return in the box provided.
5. Select a date for the original order and date when you expect to return the product.
6. Decide whether the return has been authorized and select the correct option.
7. Enter any additional information you have about your return in the note box.
8. Provide your contact email address for communication purposes.
9. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
10. Specify any additional comments or instructions you have for the return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: <customer_name> (<type:text>): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documentation. This will help us identify you and process your request efficiently.
* **Order ID**: <order_id> (<type:number>): Type your order ID or reference number, which can be found on your receipt or order confirmation.
* **Product Name**: <product_name> (<type:text>): Specify the name of the product you are returning.
* **Reason for Returning**: <reason_for_returning> (<type:text>): Describe the reason for your return, e.g., "Defective" or "Damaged".
* **Order Date**: <order_date> (<type:date>): Select the date when you originally ordered the product.
* **Return Date**: <return_date> (<type:date>): Enter a date when you expect to return the product.
* **Authorized**: <authorized> (<type:select_one>): Decide whether the return has been authorized and select "True" or "False".
* **Notes**: <notes> (<type:note>): Enter any additional information you have about your return.
* **Email**: <email> (<type:email>): Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: <phone> (<type:text>): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Comments**: <comments> (<type:select_multiple>): Specify any additional comments or instructions you have for the return.
