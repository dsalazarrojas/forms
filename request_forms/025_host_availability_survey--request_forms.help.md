# Host Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a host's availability for events or meetings. It helps hosts to provide detailed information about their schedule and preferences, making it easier for clients or attendees to plan and coordinate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name as the host.
2. Select the start and end dates for your availability.
3. Choose your start and end times for each day of availability.
4. Specify the duration of your availability.
5. Enter the maximum capacity and maximum number of participants for your event or meeting.
6. Select your preferred days of the week when you are available.
7. Choose your preferred time slots for each day of availability.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your availability.
9. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
10. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Host Name** (`host_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the host, so clients or attendees can easily identify you.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of your availability. This should be the date when you become available to host the event or meeting.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of your availability. This should be the date when you are no longer available to host the event or meeting.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Choose your start time for each day of availability. This will help clients or attendees plan the event or meeting accordingly.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Select your end time for each day of availability.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of your availability in hours or minutes.
* **Maximum Capacity** (`max_capacity`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum number of participants you can accommodate.
* **Maximum Participants** (`max_participants`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum number of participants you can accommodate.
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days of the week when you are available to host events or meetings.
* **Preferred Time Slots** (`preferred_times`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the time slots for each day of availability that you prefer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so clients or attendees can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for clients or attendees to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or in-person).

Note: This form is designed to be completed by hosts to provide detailed information about their availability. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that clients or attendees can plan events or meetings efficiently.
