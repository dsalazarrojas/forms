# Pest Control Cleanup Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pest Control Cleanup Referral Form is used to collect information from customers to request pest control services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pest control service you need from the options provided.
2. Choose all the services you require from the list of options.
3. Enter the address where the service is needed.
4. Choose a date and time for the service.
5. Indicate whether the request is urgent or not.
6. Enter your contact phone number and email.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the service.
8. (Optional) Select the pest control team you would like to be assigned to the job.
9. Enter any further information about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of pest control service do you need?** (`pest_control_service_type`): Select the type of service you require, such as Extermination, Prevention, or Inspection.
* **Which of the following pest control services do you need?** (`pest_control_services`): Choose all the services you require from the list of options, such as Rodent control or Insect control.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`): Enter the address where the service is needed.
* **Date** (`pest_control_cleanup_date`): Enter the date of the desired pest control service.
* **Time** (`pest_control_cleanup_time`): Enter the time of the desired pest control service.
* **Is this an urgent request?** (`is_urgent`): Indicate whether the request is urgent or not.
* **Service Contact Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact number.
* **Service Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email.
* **Additional notes** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the service.
* **Pest Control Services** (`pest_control_service_team`): (Optional) Select the service team you would like to be assigned to the job.
* **Pest Control Referral** (`pest_control_cleanup_referral`): Enter any further information about the referral.
