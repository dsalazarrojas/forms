# Employee Benefits Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees for benefits requests, such as time off, salary changes, or other perks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, job title, and contact information in the relevant fields.
2. Check the box next to "benefit_type" to indicate what type of benefit you are requesting.
3. Provide a brief description of the benefit you are requesting in the "benefit_description" field.
4. Explain the reason for your request in the "reason_for_requesting_benefits" field.
5. If applicable, provide the date and location of the benefit request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### employee_name
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your official documents.

### employee_job_title
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title as it appears on your official documents.

### employee_id
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.

### employee_email
* **Employee Email**: Enter your official company email address.

### employee_phone
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your official company phone number.

### benefit_type
* **Benefit Type**: Check the box next to "Yes" or "No" to indicate what type of benefit you are requesting. Please note that this field is optional, but recommended for easier processing.

### benefit_description
* **Benefit Description**: Provide a brief description of the benefit you are requesting.

### reason_for_requesting_benefits
* **Reason for Requesting Benefits**: Explain the reason for your request in this field. This will help management understand your needs better.

### date_of_request
* **Date of Request**: If applicable, enter the date of the benefit request.

### location_of_request
* **Location of Request**: If applicable, enter the location where you will be requesting the benefit.

### additional_details
* **Additional Details**: If there's anything else you'd like to add or clarify about your request, please enter it here.
