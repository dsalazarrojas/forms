# Election Advertising Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Election Advertising Request Form is a tool for submitting requests for election advertising services. This form helps authorized personnel gather necessary information to process and fulfill your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of election for which you are requesting advertising services (Federal, Statewide, County Municipal, or School Union).
2. Provide a brief description of your campaign materials.
3. Enter the budget for your campaign (if applicable).
4. Choose the advertising platforms you would like to use for your campaign (e.g., Google, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram).
5. Select the target audience for your campaign (Federal, Statewide, County Municipal, or School Union).
6. Enter the date of the election (if known).
7. Provide the contact information for the person responsible for your campaign (name is not required, but email and phone number are).
8. If applicable, enter the duration of your campaign and the start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Election Advertising Request**: This is the main field for your request. Enter a clear and concise description of the services you are requesting.
* **Federal Election Request**: Select the type of election you are requesting services for (Federal, Statewide, County Municipal, or School Union).
* **Campaign Materials**: This is a text field for you to describe your campaign materials (e.g., flyers, brochures, posters).
* **Budget**: Enter the budget for your campaign if you have one.
* **Advertising Platform**: Choose the advertising platforms you would like to use for your campaign (e.g., Google, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram).
* **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for your campaign (Federal, Statewide, County Municipal, or School Union).
* **Election Date**: Enter the date of the election (if known).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact information for the person responsible for your campaign (name, email, phone number).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number for the person responsible for your campaign.
* **Campaign Duration**: Enter the duration of your campaign if applicable.
* **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the start date of your campaign if applicable.
* **Campaign End Date**: Enter the end date of your campaign if applicable.
* **Campaign Materials**: This is a text field for you to describe your campaign materials (e.g., flyers, brochures, posters).
