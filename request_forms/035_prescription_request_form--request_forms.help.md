# Prescription Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from patients or their representatives for prescription requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation before starting.
2.  Review each field carefully and provide accurate and complete information.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please do not leave it blank without clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information (1)**: This section collects basic information about the patient, including their name, address, and contact details.
    *   **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
    *   **Address** (text): Enter the patient's home or work address.
    *   **Phone** (text): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
    *   **Email** (email): Enter the patient's email address for communication.
*   **Medication Needed (2)**: Indicate whether medication is needed, and provide details as required.
    *   **Medication Needed** (select_multiple): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if medication is needed.
*   **Doctor Name (3)**: Enter the name of the doctor who prescribed the medication.
    *   **Doctor Name** (text): Enter the doctor's name as it appears on their identification.
*   **Medicine Name (4)**: Enter the name of the medication required.
    *   **Medicine Name** (text): Enter the exact name of the medicine as prescribed.
*   **Medicine ID (5)**: Enter the ID number of the medication for identification purposes.
    *   **Medicine ID** (number): Enter the numeric ID of the medicine as prescribed.
*   **Dosage (6)**: Enter the dosage instructions for the medication.
    *   **Dosage** (number): Enter the amount of medication to be taken.
*   **Instructions (7)**: Enter any specific instructions for the medication use.
    *   **Instructions** (text): Enter any additional instructions provided by the doctor.
*   **Start Date (8)**: Enter the date the medication should start.
    *   **Start Date** (date): Enter the date the medication should be taken from.
*   **Start Time (9)**: Enter the time the medication should start.
    *   **Start Time** (time): Enter the time of day the medication should be taken.
*   **End Date (10)**: Enter the date the medication should end.
    *   **End Date** (date): Enter the date the medication should be taken until.
*   **End Time (11)**: Enter the time the medication should end.
    *   **End Time** (time): Enter the time of day the medication should be stopped.
*   **Frequency (12)**: Enter how often the medication should be taken.
    *   **Frequency** (text): Enter the frequency of medication use, e.g., daily, weekly.
*   **Note (13)**: Enter any additional notes about the medication.
    *   **Note** (note): Enter any important information about the medication or patient.
*   **Contact Person (16)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the prescription.
    *   **Contact Person** (text): Enter the name of the person responsible for contact.
*   **Contact Phone (17)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
    *   **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email (18)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
    *   **Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Pharmacy (21)**: Enter the name of the pharmacy where the medication will be collected.
    *   **Pharmacy** (text): Enter the name of the pharmacy.
*   **Signature (25)**: Enter your signature to confirm the prescription request.
    *   **Signature** (text): Enter your name as a signature to confirm the request.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and complete when filling out this form to avoid delays or errors.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please do not leave it blank.
