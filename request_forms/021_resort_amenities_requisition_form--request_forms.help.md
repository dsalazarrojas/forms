# Resort Amenities Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Amenities Requisition Form is a form used to request amenities from the resort. This form allows guests to provide their preferences and requirements for their stay, such as special requests, dietary needs, and any other amenities they may need during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the information needed to complete it accurately.
2. Fill in the Guest Request field with any special requests or requirements you have for your stay.
3. Provide details about the amenity you are requesting in the Amenity Details field.
4. Decide whether the resort's routing team should be involved in coordinating your request and select the corresponding option.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Request** (text, optional): This field is where you can provide any special requests or requirements you have for your stay, such as dietary needs or specific room preferences.
* **Amenity Details** (text, optional): In this field, provide as much detail as possible about the amenity you are requesting, including the type of amenity and any specific requirements or conditions.
* **Routing Team** (select one, required): This field helps the resort know whether the routing team should be involved in coordinating your request. Select "Yes" if you would like the resort to contact you about your request, or "No" if you prefer to handle it internally.
* **Comments** (text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about your request, type them in this field. This will help the resort understand your needs better.
