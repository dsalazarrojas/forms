# Trade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request trade-related information for internal record-keeping and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
3. Review and submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business involved in the trade.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the business.
* **Trade Date**: Enter the date of the trade (if applicable).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person involved in the trade.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the business.
* **Message**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the trade.
* **Select Business Partners**: Choose all business partners involved in the trade.
* **Select Trade Type**: Choose the type of trade (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Select Trade Currency**: Choose the currency used for the trade (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Select Trade Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the trade (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Select Payment Method**: Choose the method of payment (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Select Trade Term**: Choose the term of the trade (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Date of Trade**: Enter the date of the trade (if applicable).
* **Time of Trade**: Enter the time of the trade (if applicable).
* **Select Trade Days**: Choose the days of the week for the trade (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
* **Select Trade Time Period**: Choose the time period for the trade (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Select Trade Location**: Choose the location(s) where the trade took place.
* **Select Business Types**: Choose the type(s) of business involved in the trade.
* **Select Trade Currencies**: Choose the currency(ies) used for the trade.
* **Select Trade Frequencies**: Choose the frequency(ies) of the trade.
* **Select Trade Terms**: Choose the term(s) of the trade.
* **Select Payment Methods**: Choose the payment method(s) used for the trade.
* **Select Business Partner**: Choose the business partner involved in the trade.
* **Select Trade**: Choose the trade (e.g., option 1, 2, or 3).

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field's label for guidance.
