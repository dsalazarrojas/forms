# Release Of Medical Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Release Of Medical Information Form" is used to collect information from patients regarding the release of their medical records. It is a crucial step in ensuring that patients are aware of and agree to the disclosure of their medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Indicate whether or not you have medical records that need to be released.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for ownership of the medical record.
5. If applicable, provide additional comments about your medical information.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Provide your phone number.
8. Sign the form, indicating that you have read and understood the information being requested.
9. Date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`release_of_medical_information_form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`release_of_medical_information_form_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Date Of Birth** (`release_of_medical_information_form_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Record Release** (`release_of_medical_information_form_medical_record_release`, select_one, required): Indicate whether or not you have medical records that need to be released.
* **Ownership** (`release_of_medical_information_form_ownership`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you own the medical records, "No" otherwise.
* **Additional Comments** (`release_of_medical_information_form_additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, enter them here.
* **Email** (`release_of_medical_information_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`release_of_medical_information_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Signed** (`release_of_medical_information_form_signed`, select_one, required): Sign the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information being requested.
* **Signature** (`release_of_medical_information_form_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature here.
* **Date** (`release_of_medical_information_form_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (`release_of_medical_information_form_time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Authorized Signature** (`release_of_medical_information_form_authorized_signature`, text, required): Enter the authorized signature of the person submitting the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`release_of_medical_information_form_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, enter them here.
