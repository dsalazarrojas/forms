# Social Media Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Verification Request Form is designed to help you request a review of your social media account to confirm your identity. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have already created a social media account and are attempting to verify its accuracy.
2. Fill in the required information in the form below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Info**: Enter a brief description of the account you are trying to verify (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Username**: Enter your social media account username.
* **Social Media Links**: Enter the links to your social media profiles (e.g. profile url for Facebook).
* **Link 1**, **Link 2**: Enter the links to other social media profiles you'd like us to verify (e.g. profile url for other social media platforms).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Upload Proof of ID**: Upload a photo or scan of your ID to prove your identity.
* **Upload Proof of Address**: Upload a photo or scan of a document with your address to prove your address.
* **Upload Proof of Social**: Upload a photo or scan of your social media profile to prove your social media presence.
* **Upload Proof of Identity**: Upload a photo or scan to prove your identity.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Upload clear and legible photos or scans of required documents.
* Do not resubmit this form if you have already submitted it before. 

Note: This form is only a guide and should not be used for actual verification. For actual verification, please contact a representative of our organization.
