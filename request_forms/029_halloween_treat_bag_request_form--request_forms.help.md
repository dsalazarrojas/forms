# Halloween Treat Bag Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Treat Bag Request Form is a form used to collect information from users who are interested in participating in the Halloween Treat Bag program. The form is used to gather details about the users, their preferences, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the assigned user and contact relationship.
4. If you have any special requests or additional comments, use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
* **User Info** (`text`, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, not required): Enter your birthdate or the date you would like to receive a treat bag.
### Address
* **Address** (`text`, not required): Enter your address to receive the treat bag.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Trick or Treat Time
* **Trick or Treat Time** (`time`, not required): Enter the time you would like to receive a treat bag.
### Number of Treats
* **Number of Treats** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of treats you would like to receive.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or preferences.
### Assigned User
* **Assigned User** (`select_one`, required): Select the user who will be responsible for delivering the treat bag.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select any additional comments or preferences.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, not required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
### Age
* **Age** (`number`, not required): Enter your age for age verification.
### Street
* **Street** (`text`, not required): Enter the street where you would like to receive the treat bag.
### City
* **City** (`text`, not required): Enter the city where you would like to receive the treat bag.
### State
* **State** (`text`, not required): Enter the state where you would like to receive the treat bag.
### Zip Code
* **Zip Code** (`text`, not required): Enter the zip code where you would like to receive the treat bag.
### Number of Family Members
* **Number of Family Members** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of family members participating.
### Number of Children
* **Number of Children** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of children participating.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or preferences.
### Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`text`, not required): Enter any special requests.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`text`, not required): Enter an emergency contact person's name and phone number.
### Contact Relationship
* **Contact Relationship** (`select_one`, required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact.
### Number of Adults
* **Number of Adults** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of adults participating.
### Number of Children
* **Number of Children** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of children participating.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or preferences.
