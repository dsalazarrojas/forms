# Good Faith Estimate Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Good Faith Estimate Request form is used to provide a written statement outlining the cost and scope of work for a specific service. This form will be used to gather information from the user, including their contact details and the details of the service they are requesting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is required to ensure we can contact you about your request.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This will help us identify you and your service request.
3. Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field. This is the date you are requesting the service to be performed.
4. Provide a brief description of the service in the "Description of Work" field. This will help us understand your service request.
5. Enter your estimate for the total cost of the service in the "Total Estimate" field.
6. Choose the payment terms from the dropdown menu in the "Payment Terms" field. This will determine when you are willing to accept payment.
7. Choose how you would like to receive the quote from the dropdown menu in the "Send Quote" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.
9. Enter your details in the "Submitter" field, including your name, email address, and phone number.
10. Enter your address in the "Submitter Address" field.
11. Enter your city in the "Submitter City" field.
12. Choose your state from the dropdown menu in the "Submitter State" field.
13. Choose your country from the dropdown menu in the "Submitter Country" field.
14. Enter your zip code in the "Submitter Zip" field.
15. Enter any additional comments in the "Submitter Comments" field.
16. Agree to the accuracy of the submitted information by checking the "I Agree" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (user_email, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your request.
* **Name** (user_name, text, required): Enter your name to help us identify you and your service request.
* **Date of Service** (service_date, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting the service to be performed.
* **Description of Work** (service_description, note, required): Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Total Estimate** (total_estimate, number, required): Enter your estimate for the total cost of the service.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms, select_one, optional): Choose when you are willing to accept payment. Options are 30, 60, or 90 days.
* **Send Quote** (send_quote, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive the quote. Options are 1, 2, or 3.
* **Submit** (submit_form, text, required): Click to submit your form and request the good faith estimate.
* **Service Terms** (service_terms, note, required): These are the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Submitter** (submitter, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (submitter_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (submitter_address, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Submitter City** (submitter_city, text, required): Enter your city.
* **Submitter State** (submitter_state, select_one, optional): Choose your state from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitter Country** (submitter_country, select_one, optional): Choose your country from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitter Zip** (submitter_zip, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Submitter Comments** (submitter_comments, text, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **I Agree** (submitter_agree, text, required): Check to agree to the accuracy of the submitted information.
* **I Agree Label** (submitter_agree_label, text, required): This is the label for the "I Agree" field.
* **You agree that the submitted information is accurate and true** (submitter_agree_hint, text, required): This is the hint for the "I Agree" field.
* **Submitter** (submitter_agree, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Agreed Label** (submitter_agree, text, required): This is the label for the "Submitter Agreed" field.
* **Submitter Agreed Hint** (submitter_agree, text, required): This is the hint for the "Submitter Agreed" field.
