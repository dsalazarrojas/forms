# Nonprofit Policy Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Policy Review Request Form is used to request a review of a nonprofit organization's policies. This form helps ensure that the organization's policies are up-to-date and effective in meeting the needs of its constituents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the specific policy or area you want to be reviewed.
2. Select the appropriate `Urgency Level` (low, medium, or high) to indicate the importance of the review.
3. Choose the person who should be responsible for reviewing the policy (board or staff).
4. Check if the policy needs review for the current `Deadline` or schedule a specific date for the review.
5. Specify the time frame for the review.
6. Optionally, provide comments about the specific policy or review.
7. Confirm that you've carefully reviewed the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Policy Documents**: Enter the policy document(s) that you want to be reviewed. You can provide as many or as few policies as necessary.
  - This field is not required, but it will help the review process by specifying which policies need review.
- **Review Types**: Select one or multiple review types that apply to the policy. 
  - Options: `Yes`
- **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level of the review. This will help the reviewer prioritize the request.
  - Options: `low`, `medium`, `high`
  - Required
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the policy or review.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the reviewer understand the context.
- **Review Requested By**: Choose who should be responsible for reviewing the policy.
  - Options: `board`, `staff`, `other`
  - Required
- **Review Requested For**: Check if the policy needs review. 
  - Options: `Yes`, `No`
  - Optional
- **Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the review or schedule a specific date.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the reviewer prioritize the request.
  - Format: `mm/dd/yyyy`
- **Time**: Specify the time frame for the review.
  - This field is optional, but it will help the reviewer understand the scope of the review.
  - Format: `HH:MM`
- **Submit**: Confirm that you've carefully reviewed the form before submitting it.
  - This field is not required, but it will help ensure that the reviewer carefully reviews the request.

## Tips
- Please make sure to carefully review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact [contact information].
- The organization's staff and leadership will review the submitted forms to prioritize and schedule the reviews accordingly.
