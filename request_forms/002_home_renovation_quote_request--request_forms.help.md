# Home Renovation Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for homeowners or property owners who are interested in getting a quote for a home renovation project. Fill out this form to submit your details and project requirements to receive a quote from us.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Email**. These fields are required to contact you and provide you with a quote.
2. Select the **Type of Property** you are renovating, such as Single Family Home, Townhouse, Apartment, etc.
3. Provide a brief description of your **Project Details** in the text field below.
4. Enter your estimated **Budget** for the project.
5. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** (Phone, Email, or Text Message) to receive the quote. You can select multiple options if needed.
6. Choose the **Project Location** where the renovation is taking place (Home, Office, or Other).
7. Select the **Project Status** (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
8. Enter the **Project Start Date** and **Project End Date** for the renovation project.
9. Choose the **Project Completion Date** and **Project Description** to provide more information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can send you the quote.
* **Type of Property** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of property you are renovating.
*   *Single Family Home*: A single-family home with one or more stories.
*   *Townhouse*: A multi-unit dwelling with multiple levels.
*   *Apartment*: A multi-unit dwelling in a building.
*   *Condominium*: A type of ownership where you own the unit but not the land.
*   *Loft*: A renovated apartment or room in a building.
*   *Duplex*: A multi-unit dwelling with two levels.
* **Project Details** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Estimated Budget** (Number, Required): Enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you would like to receive the quote.
*   *Phone*: Receive the quote by phone call.
*   *Email*: Receive the quote by email.
*   *Text Message*: Receive the quote by text message.
* **Project Location** (Select One, Required): Choose the location of the project.
*   *Home*: The renovation is taking place at your home.
*   *Office*: The renovation is taking place at an office or commercial property.
*   *Other*: The renovation is taking place at a different location.
* **Project Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the status of the project.
*   *Not Started*: The project has not started yet.
*   *In Progress*: The project is currently in progress.
*   *Completed*: The project is already completed.
* **Project Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Completion Date** (Time, Optional): Enter the completion date of the project.
* **Project Description** (Text, Required): Provide a more detailed description of your project.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can provide you with an accurate quote.
* If you need to update your quote, you can contact us and we will be happy to help.
* We will review and process your form submission within 24-48 hours.
* We will send you a quote via the contact method you selected.
