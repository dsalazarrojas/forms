# Find Dentist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Find Dentist" form is a simple, one-time appointment request form for dental clinics. It gathers information from users to schedule a convenient time and date for their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This will be the name you want to be referred to by the dentist.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This will be your last name.
3. Enter a valid **Email Address** in the "Email" field. This is how we'll contact you.
4. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This is your contact information.
5. Select a **Date** for the appointment from the calendar. Choose a date and time that works for you.
6. Choose a **Time** for the appointment. Select from the available time slots.
7. If necessary, add a **Note** about your appointment. This can be any important information you want to share with the dentist.
8. Select the **Dental Service** you need (e.g., Check up, Cleaning, Surgery, Implant).
9. Choose the **Dental Specialty** you need (e.g., General, Pedodontics, Orthodontics, Oral Surgery).
10. Select how you'd like to **Schedule the Service** (e.g., In Office, In Home, Over the Phone).
11. Confirm your **Email** address.
12. Confirm your **Phone Number**.
13. Confirm the **Date** of your visit.
14. Confirm the **Time** of your visit.
15. Confirm the **Location** of your visit.
16. Finally, select your **Preferred Payment Method** (e.g., Credit Card, Cash, Insurance, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address here. This will be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any important notes about your appointment.
* **Which Dental Service do you need?** (`Which Dental Service do you need?`, select_one, required): Select one of the available dental services (e.g., Check up, Cleaning, Surgery, Implant).
* **Which dental specialty do you need?** (`Which dental specialty do you need?`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the available dental specialties (e.g., General, Pedodontics, Orthodontics, Oral Surgery).
* **How would you like to schedule the service?** (`How would you like to schedule the service?`, select_one, required): Select how you'd like to schedule the service (e.g., In Office, In Home, Over the Phone).
