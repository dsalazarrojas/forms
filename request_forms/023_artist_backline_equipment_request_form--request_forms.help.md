# Artist Backline Equipment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your performance to provide you with the right equipment and support for a successful show.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send your request.
2. Fill out the form fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`): Enter the name of your artist or band.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`primary_contact`): Enter the name of the person responsible for communication.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter a valid email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`): Enter the date of the scheduled performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`): Enter the time of the performance.
* **Venue or Event Name** (`venue_name`): Enter the name of the venue or event.
* **Type of Performance** (`performance_type`): Select the type of performance (e.g. Rock Band, Hip-Hop/Rap, Country, etc.).
* **Number of Band Members** (`band_size`): Enter the number of band members.
* **Band Member Roles** (`band_members`): Enter the names and instruments of each band member.
* **Which Instruments Will You Provide?** (`instruments_provided`): Enter the instruments you will be bringing (e.g. keyboard, drums, etc.).
* **Drum Kit Needed?** (`backline_drums`): Select whether you need a drum kit provided.
* **Drum Kit Specifications** (`drums_specifications`): Enter specifications for the drum kit if needed.
* **Bass Amplifier Needed?** (`bass_amplifier`): Select whether you need a bass amplifier.
* **Do You Require a Sound Engineer?** (`sound_engineer`): Select whether you need technical support.
* **Additional Equipment Requests** (`additional_equipment`): Enter any other equipment or technical needs.
* **Power Requirements** (`power_requirements`): Enter any special electrical needs.
* **Technical Rider Attached or Provided?** (`technical_rider`): Select whether you have attached the technical rider or will provide it separately.
* **Any Other Requests or Special Notes** (`additional_requests`): Enter any final comments or requirements.
