# Find A Legal Service Provider - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in finding a legal service provider for specific needs. It collects information about the user's legal issue category, urgency level, and other relevant details to help identify potential providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and preferences, and click "Submit" to receive a list of relevant legal service providers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_input_name`, text, required): Enter your name for your legal issue.
* **Email** (`user_input_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`user_input_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Notes** (`user_input_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your legal issue.
* **Legal Issue Category** (`user_input_legal_issue_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of your legal issue (e.g., civil, criminal, etc.).
* **Provided By** (`user_input_provided_by`, text, optional): Specify who provided your legal issue (e.g., lawyer, court, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (`user_input_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the urgency level of your legal issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **Provider** (`user_input_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes next to the legal service providers you are interested in (e.g., lawyer, court, etc.).
* **Is Urgent** (`user_input_urgent`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if your legal issue is urgent.
* **Date** (`user_input_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your legal issue.
* **Time** (`user_input_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your legal issue.
* **Comments** (`user_input_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your legal issue.
* **Follow-Up** (`user_input_follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the date for follow-up contact.
* **Status** (`user_input_status`, text, optional): Specify the status of your legal issue.
* **External ID** (`user_input_external_id`, text, optional): Enter any external ID or reference number for your legal issue.
* **Created Date** (`user_input_create_date`, date, optional): Display the date this form was created.
* **Created By** (`user_input_created_by`, text, optional): Display the name of the person who created this form.
* **Updated Date** (`user_input_updated_date`, date, optional): Display the date this form was last updated.
* **Updated By** (`user_input_updated_by`, text, optional): Display the name of the person who last updated this form.
* **Last Updated By** (`user_input_last_updated_by`, text, optional): Display the name of the person who last updated this form.
* **Version** (`user_input_version`, text, optional): Display the version of this form.
* **Deleted At** (`user_input_deleted_at`, text, optional): Display the date this form was deleted.
* **Comments** (`user_input_comments`, note, optional): Display any comments or feedback about this form.
