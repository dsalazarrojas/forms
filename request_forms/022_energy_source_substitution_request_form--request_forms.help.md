# Energy Source Substitution Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Source Substitution Request Form is designed to collect information about energy source substitution requests. This form allows users to submit requests for alternative energy sources, enabling the organization to manage and track these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Energy Source ID**: Enter the current energy source ID.
	* **Requestor Email**, **Requestor Phone**, **Requestor Name**, **Requestor Company**, and **Manager Name**: Provide your contact information and the manager's name associated with the request.
2. Choose an optional field to fill in:
	* **Request Date**: Select the date of the request.
	* **Request Time**: Select the time of the request.
3. Fill in the **New Energy Source ID**: Enter the ID of the new energy source being requested.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: The date when the request was made. This field is not required, but including it helps to track the timeline of requests.
* **Request Time**: The time when the request was made. This field is also not required, but it can help in scheduling and prioritizing requests.
* **Energy Source ID**: The ID of the current energy source being used. This field is required to identify the energy source being substituted.
* **New Energy Source ID**: The ID of the new energy source requested. This field is required to track the requested replacement.
* **Requestor Email**: The email address of the person submitting the request. This field is not required but recommended for internal communication and tracking purposes.
* **Requestor Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the request. This field is not required but recommended for internal communication and tracking purposes.
* **Requestor Name**: The name of the person submitting the request. This field is required for accountability and tracking purposes.
* **Requestor Company**: The company associated with the person submitting the request. This field is not required but recommended for context and reference.
* **Manager Name**: The name of the manager associated with the request. This field is required to ensure that the manager is aware of and approves the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your organization's energy source substitution guidelines or policies.
* If you need assistance with energy source substitutions, contact your organization's energy management team.
* The submitted form will be reviewed and processed according to the organization's energy source substitution policies and procedures.
