# Facilities Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request facilities for events, meetings, or other purposes. Please fill out this form to provide necessary contact information, event details, and facilities requirements for our team to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
2. Provide the **Name Of Event** and **Event Description** for the event that requires facilities.
3. Enter the **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Expected Guest Count** for the event.
4. Specify the facilities required for the event in the **Facilities Requested** section.
5. Optionally, provide additional information and links as needed in the **Event Description**, **Tech Request Link**, and **Facilities Request Link** fields.
6. Enter your contact phone number and email address in the **Contact Phone** and **Contact Email** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, company, or department name to help us quickly identify who to contact regarding your facilities request.
* **Name Of Event** (`name_of_event`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event or meeting that requires facilities.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Expected Guests** (`expected_guests`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Expected Guest Count** (`expected_guests_count`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event (if different from expected_guests).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you about your facilities request.
* **Tech Request Link** (`tech_request_link`, `text`, required: false): Optionally, provide a link to technical documentation or instructions related to your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event that requires facilities.
* **Facilities Requested** (`facilities_requested`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the facilities required for your event (e.g., "Facilities 1", "Facilities 2", "Facilities 3", etc.).
* **Facilities Count** (`facilities_count`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated number of facilities required for your event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for our team to contact you about your facilities request.
* **Facilities Request Link** (`facilities_request_link`, `text`, required: false): Optionally, provide a link to additional information or documentation related to your facilities request.
* **Facilities Description** (`facilities_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the facilities you are requesting.
