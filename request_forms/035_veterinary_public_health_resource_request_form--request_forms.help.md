# Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Public Health Resource Request Form is designed to gather information from veterinary professionals who are seeking public health resources. This form helps the public health team better understand the resource request and act accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Fill in your Address 1.
4. Fill in your Address 2.
5. Fill in your Email.
6. Choose whether you are requesting resources for public health.
7. Choose the resource types you are requesting.
8. Choose the date and time of your resource request.
9. Fill in any resource notes if necessary.
10. Fill in the email and phone number of the resource contact person.
11. Choose whether your request has been submitted.
12. Fill in the date and time of submission.
13. Fill in the submitted note if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first address line to locate you.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your second address line to locate you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid phone number to contact you.
* **Resource Request** (`resource_request`, `select_one`, `required`): Check yes if you are requesting resources for public health.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose the types of resources you are requesting (e.g. equipment, expertise, personnel, etc.).
* **Resource Date** (`resource_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your resource request.
* **Resource Time** (`resource_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of your resource request.
* **Resource Email** (`resource_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email of the resource contact person.
* **Resource Phone** (`resource_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid phone number of the resource contact person.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of your request.
* **Request Note** (`request_note`, `note`, `required`): Fill in any notes for your request if necessary.
* **Request Email** (`request_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email of the request contact person.
* **Request Phone** (`request_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid phone number of the request contact person.
* **Request Location** (`request_location`, `text`, `required`): Enter the location of your request.

* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted Note** (`submitted_note`, `note`, `required`): Fill in any notes for submission if necessary.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and comprehensive as possible when filling out this form.
* Do not fill out this form for any reason other than public health resource requests.
* Double-check your details before submission.
* If you have any questions, contact us for assistance.
