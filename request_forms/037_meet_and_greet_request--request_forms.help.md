# Meet And Greet Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a meet and greet session with your pet at our clinic. It will help us to understand your pet's needs and preferences, so we can provide the best possible experience for both you and your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the requested information, and we will do our best to accommodate your meet and greet request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the pet you would like to meet and greet with. This will help us identify your pet and ensure we are prepared for their visit.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, Optional): Please enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you about your meet and greet request.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of pet you have (Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, Optional): If your pet has a specific breed, please let us know so we can take that into account when preparing for their visit.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, Optional): Enter your pet's age so we can plan for any special needs or accommodations they may have.
* **Pet Diet** (`pet_diet`, select_multiple, Optional): If your pet has any dietary restrictions, please select all that apply (Grain free, Limited, Normal).
* **Pet Notes** (`pet_notes`, note, Optional): If your pet has any medical conditions, behavioral issues, or special requests, please provide any additional information here.
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, note, Optional): If you have any additional requests or concerns, please let us know here.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form only if you have been seen at our clinic before.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* We will do our best to accommodate your meet and greet request, but if we need to make any changes, we will reach out to you directly.
