# Wedding Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Quote Request Form is designed to help you request quotes from various vendors for your wedding event. This form helps you provide essential information to vendors, allowing them to give you accurate quotes tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the vendors you are interested in getting quotes from by choosing from the list of available vendors.
2. Enter the date of your event. This is a required field as the vendor needs to know when to provide their services.
3. If you have a budget in mind, select it from the options provided.
4. Enter the number of guests you expect to attend your wedding.
5. Enter the person your vendor should contact you through.
6. Provide your email address so we can contact you for any updates or follow-ups.
7. Enter your phone number for easy communication.
8. Enter your address for delivery purposes.
9. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the 'Message' field.
10. Finally, enter any message you want to send to the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Vendors**: 
  * Select all the vendors you are interested in getting quotes from.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event. This is a required field.
* **Your Budget**: 
  * Select your budget from the options provided.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you expect to attend your wedding.
* **Contact Person**: 
  * Enter the person your vendor should contact you through.
* **Email**: 
  * Enter your email address for us to contact you for any updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone**: 
  * Enter your phone number for easy communication with the vendor.
* **Address**: 
  * Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Message**: 
  * Enter any additional information you want to provide to the vendor.
* **Vendor Message**: 
  * Enter any message you want to send to the vendor.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Be specific with your budget to get accurate quotes.
* Be clear with your event date and details for the vendor to understand your needs.
* Make sure to enter the correct contact person and contact information to ensure timely responses.
