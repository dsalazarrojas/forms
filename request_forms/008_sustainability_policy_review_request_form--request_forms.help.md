# Sustainability Policy Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a review of the organization's sustainability policy. The review process ensures that the policy is up-to-date and aligns with current environmental and social standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required information.
3. Review the form before submission to catch any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional)
	+ This field is a free-text field where you can provide additional context and details about your request.
* **Required Documents** (`documents`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select all documents that are relevant to your sustainability policy review request. Available options are:
	+ Policy Document
	+ Supporting Document
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Request Priorities** (`priorities`, number, optional)
	+ This field is currently not used. You can skip this field.
* **Contact Person** (`contact`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the email address of the person to contact regarding your request.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the review.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the frequency at which you would like the sustainability policy to be reviewed:
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Bi-Annual
