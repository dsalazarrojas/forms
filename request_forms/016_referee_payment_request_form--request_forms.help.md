# Referee Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Payment Request Form is used to request payment for a referee's services during a tournament or event. This form is typically filled out by the referee or event organizer to submit payment requests for the referee's fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Game ID of the event for which you are requesting payment.
2. Provide your name as the Referee.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the Tournament ID of the event.
5. Enter the name of the event.
6. Select the date of the payment.
7. Enter the amount of payment you are requesting.
8. Indicate whether the payment has been approved or declined.
9. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the payment.
10. Enter any remarks about the event or payment process.
11. Select the name of the person who submitted the form.
12. Select the date the form was submitted.
13. Choose the tool or equipment assigned to you for the event.
14. Enter the internal ID of the form.
15. Note: All dates are in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game ID (1)**: Enter the ID of the game for which you are requesting payment.
* **Referee Name (2)**: Enter your name as the referee.
* **Referee Email (3)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Referee Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Tournament ID (5)**: Enter the ID of the tournament for which you are requesting payment.
* **Event Name (6)**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Date of Payment (7)**: Enter the date of the payment in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Referee Payment (8)**: Enter the amount of payment you are requesting.
* **Payment Approved (9)**: Select whether the payment has been approved or declined.
* **Payment Remaks (10)**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the payment.
* **Remarks (11)**: Enter any remarks about the event or payment process.
* **Submitted By (12)**: Select the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date (13)**: Enter the date the form was submitted in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Assigned Tool (14)**: Choose the tool or equipment assigned to you for the event.
* **Form ID (15)**: Enter the internal ID of the form.
* **Created At (16)**: Note: This field is automatically generated and not editable.
