# Home Renovation Design Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Renovation Design Upload Form is used to collect information from users who want to submit their home renovation designs for evaluation. This form helps in ensuring that the submitted designs meet the necessary standards and requirements for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are:
	* User Name
	* Address
	* Email
	* Phone

Note that the phone number is not necessarily for public contact, but rather as a way for us to get in touch with you in case we need more information.
2. Fill out the optional field:
	* User Name
3. Once you have completed all the required fields, please attach your home renovation design document or file as specified in the instructions provided with this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or a nickname you are commonly known by.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address where the renovation will take place.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your design.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as a means of contact in case we need more information.
* **Home Renovation Design** (``, file, required, not specified in the provided YAML): Attach a PDF or other supported file format of your home renovation design document. Please refer to the instructions provided with this form for more information.
