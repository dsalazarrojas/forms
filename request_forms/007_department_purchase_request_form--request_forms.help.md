# Department Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Purchase Request Form is used by department administrators to request purchases for their department. The form gathers information about the requested purchase, including the department making the request, the reason for the request, and the details of the purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
* Ensure that the form is not a duplicate of a previously submitted request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): This field is for the department making the request. Enter the name of the department that is making the request.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): This field is for the reason for the request. Describe the purpose of the purchase and the expected outcome.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required): Enter the total budget for the requested purchase.
* **Store** (`store`, text, required): This field is for the store or vendor where the purchase will be made.
* **Item** (`item`, text, required): This field is for the specific item being requested.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): This field is for a brief description of the item being requested.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the purchase is expected to be made.
* **Cost** (`cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the purchase.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): This field is for the category or type of item being requested.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information about the purchase.
