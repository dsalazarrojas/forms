# Utility Bill Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Bill Clarification Request Form is used to request help and clarification from the utility company about a specific bill or account issue. This form gathers essential information from the customer, including their name, contact information, and a brief description of the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Select the type of utility service that you have an issue with (e.g., Electricity, Gas, or Water).
3. Enter your address in the Address field.
4. Provide your phone number and email address in the Phone Number and Email fields, respectively.
5. Describe the issue you're experiencing with your bill in the Issue Description field.
6. If applicable, upload any relevant attachments or documents to support your request.
7. Select the type of issue you're experiencing (e.g., Overbilling, Delayed Service, or Other).
8. Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the employee who will be handling your request.
9. Enter the name, phone number, and email address of the manager who will be handling your request.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the Employee Notes and Manager Notes fields.
11. Confirm your request by clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Utility Service** (`utility_service`, select_one, required): Select the type of utility service you have an issue with (e.g., Electricity, Gas, or Water).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue you're experiencing with your bill.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of issue you're experiencing (e.g., Overbilling, Delayed Service, or Other).
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, note, optional): Upload any relevant attachments or documents to support your request.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Upload another file if necessary.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the issue.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the issue.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who will be handling your request.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee who will be handling your request.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee who will be handling your request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager who will be handling your request.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager who will be handling your request.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the manager who will be handling your request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Upload Attachment** (`upload_attachment`, text, optional): Upload another attachment if necessary.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the employee.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the manager.
* **Upload Another Attachment** (`attachment_2`, text, optional): Upload another attachment if necessary.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required): Confirm your request by typing "yes" in this field.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
