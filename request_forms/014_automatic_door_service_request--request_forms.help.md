# Automatic Door Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to request service for automatic doors. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the door, its location, and the type of service needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of service needed from the "Type of Service Needed" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the contact information fields (Contact Person Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Company or Building Name).
3. Provide the location information (Service Address, City, State, and Zip Code).
4. Describe the problem with the door or sensor in the "Describe the Problem" field.
5. Choose the correct type of automatic door from the "Type of Automatic Door" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the year of installation, if known.
7. Select the urgency level of the service needed.
8. Choose a preferred time window for the service.
9. Provide any additional notes or special instructions in the "Additional Notes or Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person Name** (Text, Required): This is the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): This is the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): This is the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Company or Building Name** (Text, Required): This is the name of the company or building where the automatic door is located.
* **Service Address** (Text, Required): This is the location where the automatic door is situated.
* **City** (Text, Required): This is the city where the automatic door is located.
* **State** (Text, Required): This is the state where the automatic door is located.
* **Zip Code** (Text, Required): This is the zip code where the automatic door is located.
* **Door Location Description** (Text, Optional): A description of where the automatic door is located within the building or facility.
* **Type of Automatic Door** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of automatic door (Sliding, Swing, Revolving, Folding, Overhead Rolling, or Other).
* **Door Model or Manufacturer** (Text, Optional): This is the model or manufacturer of the automatic door.
* **Year of Installation** (Number, Optional): Enter the year the automatic door was installed.
* **Type of Service Needed** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of service needed (Repair, Maintenance, Inspection, Installation, Replacement, Emergency Service).
* **Describe the Problem** (Text, Required): Describe the issue or problem with the automatic door.
* **Door Operation Status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the door's operation (Not Opening, Not Closing, Opening Slowly, Closing Slowly, Making Noise, Not Responding to Sensor, Other).
* **Urgency Level** (Select One, Required): Choose the urgency level of the service needed (Critical - Door Out of Service, High - Major Issues, Medium - Some Functionality Loss, Low - Minor Issues).
* **Preferred Service Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the preferred date for the service.
* **Preferred Time Window** (Select One, Required): Choose the preferred time window for the service (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Flexible).
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or special instructions.

Note: It's essential to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the service team can assist you efficiently. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact the service team.
