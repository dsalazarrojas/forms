# Purchase Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document details of a purchase requisition, providing a structured way to gather information from the requester and facilitating the internal purchasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate submission and processing of the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your details as the requester.
2. Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
3. Describe the item you wish to purchase, including its name and a brief description.
4. Enter any additional comments or details about the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Information** (`purchase_requisition_form_1`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and any title or position within the organization, as you would like to be addressed in communications related to the purchase.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Number, Optional): Enter your direct contact number for any communication regarding the purchase.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the exact name of the item you wish to purchase.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the item you are requesting, including any specific features or requirements.
* **Requester Information** (`purchase_requisition_form_2`, Text, Optional): This field is for you to enter any additional information about yourself, such as your department or team affiliation.
* **Requester Information** (`purchase_requisition_form_3`, Text, Optional): Similar to the previous field, use this for any further details regarding your affiliation with the purchase.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to facilitate efficient processing.
* If you have any specific requirements or specifications for the item, include them in the Item Description field.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the relevant team or contact number provided.
