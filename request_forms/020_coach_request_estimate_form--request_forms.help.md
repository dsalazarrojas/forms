# Coach Request Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an estimate for coaching services. Please fill out the form to provide information about your coaching needs so we can provide an estimate of the costs and duration of the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the most suitable options for your coaching needs.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the text fields to help us understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Company/Organization**: Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date when you are requesting this estimate.
* **Type of Coach Needed**: Choose the type of coaching you are seeking (e.g., Life Coach, Executive Coach, etc.).
* **Number of Coaches Needed**: Choose the number of coaches you are looking for (e.g., 1, 2-3, etc.).
* **Expected Duration of Services**: Choose the expected duration of the coaching services.
* **Preferred Session Frequency**: Choose how often sessions should occur (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Preferred Session Format**: Choose how sessions should be delivered (e.g., in person, video conference, etc.).
* **Expected Group Size**: Choose the expected group size for coaching.
* **Primary Coaching Goals**: Describe the primary goals you want to achieve through coaching.
* **Target Audience Description**: Describe the people who will receive coaching.
* **Current Challenges**: Describe the current challenges you are facing.
* **Preferred Start Date**: Choose the date when you would like to begin coaching.
* **Location Preference**: Choose where coaching should take place (if applicable).
* **Is Travel Required**: Indicate if coaches need to travel (true or false).
* **Travel Locations**: Describe any travel locations required.
* **Budget Range**: Choose your estimated budget for coaching services.
* **Have You Used Coaching Before**: Indicate if you have used coaching services before.
* **Previous Experience**: Describe your past experience with coaching.
* **Special Requirements**: Describe any specific needs or accommodations.
* **Decision Timeline**: Choose when you need to make a decision about coaching services.
* **Are You Considering Other Providers**: Indicate if you are getting estimates from other providers.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Describe how you found our services.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are necessary for a complete estimate. Please fill out all required fields for us to provide an accurate estimate.
