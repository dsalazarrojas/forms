# Child Relocation (Move Away) Request Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for requesting relocation due to a move away from the current residence. It gathers information necessary for processing the request, including details about the child's identity and the proposed new residence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the request date of the relocation request submission.
2. Provide the legal first and last name of the parent requesting the relocation.
3. Enter the primary contact email address for the requesting parent.
4. Fill in the primary phone number for the requesting parent.
5. Enter the current full street address of the requesting parent.
6. Enter the legal first and last name of the non-requesting parent or guardian.
7. Enter the phone or email contact information for the non-requesting parent.
8. Enter the child's legal first name.
9. Enter the child's legal last name.
10. Enter the child's date of birth.
11. Enter the child's current age in years.
12. Describe the current custody or visitation arrangement.
13. Enter the city of the proposed new residence.
14. Enter the state or province of the proposed new residence.
15. Enter the approximate distance from the current residence in miles.
16. Enter the planned move date.
17. Choose the primary reason for the proposed move.
18. Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for relocation.
19. Describe how visitation with the other parent will be maintained.
20. Describe the plan for transportation to and from visitation.
21. If applicable, indicate if the other parent has been notified and their response.
22. Choose how the other parent has responded (notified and agrees, objects, etc.).
23. If applicable, enter the existing court case number.
24. If applicable, enter the name of representing attorney.
25. If applicable, enter the attorney's contact information.
26. If applicable, indicate if other children are affected by the relocation.
27. Enter any additional comments or information relevant to this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (1): The date you submitted this relocation request.
* **Requesting Parent First Name** (2): Legal first name of the parent submitting this request.
* **Requesting Parent Last Name** (3): Legal last name of the parent submitting this request.
* **Requesting Parent Email** (4): Primary email address for the requesting parent.
* **Requesting Parent Phone** (5): Primary phone number for the requesting parent.
* **Requesting Parent Address** (6): Current full street address of the requesting parent.
* **Non-Requesting Parent First Name** (7): Legal first name of the other parent or guardian.
* **Non-Requesting Parent Last Name** (8): Legal last name of the other parent or guardian.
* **Non-Requesting Parent Contact** (9): Phone or email contact information for the other parent or guardian.
* **Child First Name** (10): Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (11): Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (12): The child's date of birth.
* **Child Age** (13): The child's current age in years.
* **Current Custody Arrangement** (14): Describe the current custody or visitation arrangement.
* **Proposed Relocation City** (15): The city of the proposed new residence.
* **Proposed Relocation State** (16): The state or province of the proposed new residence.
* **Proposed Relocation Distance** (17): Approximate distance from the current residence in miles.
* **Proposed Move Date** (18): The planned move date.
* **Reason for Relocation** (19): Choose the primary reason for the proposed move.
* **Relocation Details** (20): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for relocation.
* **Proposed Visitation Plan** (21): Describe how visitation with the other parent will be maintained.
* **Transportation Plan** (22): Describe the plan for transportation to and from visitation.
* **Other Parent Response** (23): If applicable, indicate if the other parent has been notified and their response.
* **Court Case Number** (24): If applicable, enter the existing court case number.
* **Attorney Name** (25): If applicable, enter the name of the representing attorney.
* **Attorney Contact** (26): If applicable, enter the attorney's contact information.
* **Additional Children Affected** (27): If applicable, indicate if other children are affected by this relocation.
* **Additional Comments** (28): Any additional information relevant to this request.
