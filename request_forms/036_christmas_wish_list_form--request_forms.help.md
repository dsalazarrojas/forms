# Christmas Wish List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Wish List Form is designed to gather essential information about a child's preferences, needs, and interests to provide them with suitable gifts during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the child's full name in the "Child Full Name" field.
2.  Enter the child's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Select the child's gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the parent or guardian's full name in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
5.  Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for updates and confirmation.
6.  Enter a valid phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used for delivery coordination.
7.  Enter the child's street address in the "Street Address" field.
8.  Enter the city and state or province in the "City" and "State or Province" fields, respectively.
9.  Enter the postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
10. Select the child's clothing size from the "Clothing Size" dropdown menu.
11. Select the child's favorite colors from the "Favorite Colors" dropdown menu.
12. Select the types of toys the child is interested in from the "Toy Preferences" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the child's first wish item in the "First Wish Item" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the child's second and third wish items in the respective fields.
15. If the child requires any accommodations, enter the necessary information in the "Special Needs" field.
16. Select the source of how you heard about the program from the "How Did You Hear About Program" dropdown menu.
17. Finally, confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the program terms by selecting "Yes I agree" in the "Agree to Program Terms" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter first and last name
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Child's date of birth
* **Gender** (3, select_one, required): Select gender (Boy, Girl, or Prefer not to say)
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (4, text, required): Full name of parent or guardian
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): For confirmation and updates
* **Phone Number** (6, text, required): For delivery coordination
* **Street Address** (8, text, required): Full address for delivery
* **City** (9, text, required): Enter the city
* **State or Province** (10, text, required): Enter the state or province
* **Postal Code** (11, text, required): Enter the postal code
* **Clothing Size** (13, select_one, required): Select clothing size (2T-3T, 4T-5T, 6-7 years, 8-10 years, 12-14 years, 16-18 years, Not sure)
* **Favorite Colors** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select favorite colors (Red, Blue, Green, Pink, Purple, Orange, Yellow, Black, White, No preference)
* **Toy Preferences** (16, select_multiple, required): Select types of toys (Action Figures, Dolls, Building Blocks, Stuffed Animals, Arts and Crafts, Board Games, Video Games, Outdoor Toys, Educational Toys, Books, Music, Sports Equipment)
* **First Wish Item** (17, text, required): What they want most
* **Second Wish Item** (18, text, optional): Another item they would like
* **Third Wish Item** (19, text, optional): Another option
* **Shoe Size** (21, text, optional): If shoes are needed
* **Hobbies and Interests** (22, text, optional): What they enjoy doing
* **Favorite Characters** (23, text, optional): Movie, TV, or book characters
* **Number of Siblings** (25, select_one, optional): Select the number of siblings (0 only child, 1 sibling, 2 siblings, 3 siblings, 4 or more siblings)
* **Dietary Restrictions** (26, select_multiple, optional): If receiving food gifts
* **Special Needs** (27, text, optional): Any accommodations to consider
* **How Did You Hear About Program** (29, select_one, required): Select source of how you heard about the program (School, Community Center, Church, Social Services, Friend or Family, Social Media, Other)
* **Agree to Program Terms** (30, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the program terms (Yes I agree, No I do not agree)
