# Deceased Parent Medical Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a deceased parent's medical record. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the accuracy of the medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Indicate your relationship to the parent in the "Relationship to Parent" field.
3. Provide the date of death, hospitalization, and other relevant medical history in the "Date of Death", "Place of Death", "Cause of Death", "Date of Hospitalization", "Place of Hospitalization", and "Doctor's Name" fields.
4. Enter the contact information of the person requesting the medical record in the "Contact Person", "Contact Email", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Address" fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Medical Record Request Notes" field.
6. Sign and date the form by entering your signature and the date in the "Signature Date" and "Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deceased Parent Medical Record Request** (`deceased_parent_medical_record_request_form`, text, required): This is the main heading of the form.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the deceased parent.
* **Relationship to Parent** (`relationship_to_parent`, select multiple, required): Indicate your relationship to the parent (e.g., child, spouse, sibling, etc.).
* **Medical Record Request** (`medical_record_request`, text, required): Describe the purpose of requesting the medical record.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the medical record.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person requesting the medical record.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the medical record.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person requesting the medical record.
* **Medical Record Request Notes** (`medical_record_request_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the medical record request.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, required): Enter the date of the parent's death.
* **Place of Death** (`place_of_death`, text, required): Enter the location where the parent died.
* **Cause of Death** (`cause_of_death`, text, required): Describe the cause of the parent's death.
* **Date of Hospitalization** (`date_of_death_hospitalization`, date, required): Enter the date of the parent's hospitalization.
* **Place of Hospitalization** (`place_of_death_hospitalization`, text, required): Enter the location where the parent was hospitalized.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor who attended to the parent during their hospitalization.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign the form by entering your signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Date and sign the form by entering your name.
