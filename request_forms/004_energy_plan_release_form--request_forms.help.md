# Energy Plan Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Energy Plan Release Form is a critical document that captures essential information from requestors about their energy plans. This form is a standard template that helps ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the mandatory fields (marked as required) with accurate information.
2. Double-check the information you provide to ensure it's correct.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Please enter your company name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your address.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have authority to request energy plans.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please select the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please select the time.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): You may add any additional comments.
* **Energy Plan** (`energy_plan`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you have an energy plan.
* **Utility** (`utility`, select_one, required): Select the type of utility (Electric, Gas, Water).
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Please enter your account number.
* **Street Number** (`street_number`, text, required): Please enter your street number.
* **Street** (`street`, text, required): Please enter your street name.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the submitter.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Please select 'Submit' to complete the form.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to fill out all mandatory fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your energy plan representative.
