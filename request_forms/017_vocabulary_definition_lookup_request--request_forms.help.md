# Vocabulary Definition Lookup Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit vocabulary definition lookup requests to the team. Please fill out the form below to provide necessary information for our team to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are necessary to process your request. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. **User Request**: Enter a clear and concise description of the word or phrase you are seeking clarification on.
2. **Context Type**: Select the context in which you are using the word or phrase (e.g., academic, professional, everyday conversation).
3. **Vocabulary Name**: Enter the exact word or phrase you are seeking clarification on.
4. **Preferred Language**: Indicate if you prefer your response to be in a specific language (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
5. **Learning Level**: Choose the level of learning you are at (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
6. **Subject**: Choose the subject related to the word or phrase you are seeking clarification on.
7. **Resource Type**: Select the type of resource you are seeking (e.g., article, video, podcast).
8. **User Email**: Enter your email address so that we can respond to your request.
9. **User Language**: Select the language you are most comfortable communicating in (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
10. **User Notes**: Provide any additional notes or context about your request.
