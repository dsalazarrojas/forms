# safety_stock_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safety stock request form is used to request additional stock for items that are running low or have reached their reorder point. This form helps ensure that stock levels are maintained to meet business needs and prevent stockouts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the items for which you are requesting safety stock.
2. Choose the type of item you are requesting safety stock for.
3. Enter the quantity of safety stock requested.
4. Enter the reason for the request.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
6. Enter the date on which you need the stock to be received.
7. Enter your contact information so that the request can be responded to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Items** (`item_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select the items for which you are requesting safety stock.
* **Safety Stock Type** (`item_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of item you are requesting safety stock for.
* **Safety Stock Quantity** (`item_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of safety stock requested.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the safety stock request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you need the stock to be received.
* **Requestor Info** (`requestor_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about yourself as the requestor.
* **Name** (`requestor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`requestor_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Contact** (`requestor_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`requestor_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Supervisor Info** (`supervisor_info`, text, optional): Enter information about your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (`supervisor_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's job title.
* **Supervisor Contact** (`supervisor_contact`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's contact information.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Enter the person or department to whom the request was submitted.
