# Team Workspace Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Workspace Setup Form is used to create a team workspace, a centralized location where team members can collaborate and share resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, select the "Pages" tab and navigate through each page, filling in the fields as required.
2.  For each page, review the fields and instructions provided to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Team Leads**: Select "Yes" if you want to grant access to team leads, and "No" otherwise.
    *   This field determines whether team leads will have access to the workspace.
*   **Team Members**: Select "Yes" if you want to grant access to team members, and "No" otherwise.
    *   This field determines whether team members will have access to the workspace.
*   **Workspace Name**: Enter a name for your workspace.
    *   This is the title of your workspace, which will be displayed to team members.
*   **Workspace Description**: Enter a brief description of your workspace.
    *   This field provides a brief overview of your workspace, helping team members understand its purpose.
*   **Collaboration Needs**: Enter any specific collaboration needs or requirements for your workspace.
    *   This field allows you to specify any unique needs or requirements for your workspace, such as permissions or access levels.
*   **Access Level**: Choose the access level for your workspace:
    *   **Admin**: Grants administrative access to the workspace.
    *   **Member**: Grants access to workspace members.
    *   **Observer**: Grants observer status, allowing them to view the workspace but not participate in discussions or decisions.
*   **Workspace Type**: Choose the type of workspace:
    *   **Team Workspace**: For team-based workspaces.
    *   **Personal Workspace**: For personal workspaces.
    *   **Project Workspace**: For project-based workspaces.
*   **Setup Date**: Enter the date you set up the workspace.
    *   This field is automatically filled in by the system and should not be modified.

## Tips
*   Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator for guidance.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to create a team workspace before starting this form.
