# Keyboard Troubleshooting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues with keyboards. By filling out this form, you will help the support team to better understand and troubleshoot the problem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your issue description in the "Describe Your Issue" field.
* If applicable, select the type of problem you are experiencing (Hardware, Software, or Connectivity) in the "Problem Type" field.
* If you have any images or videos related to your issue, you can upload them in the "Problem Image" and "Problem Video" fields.
* If you have any attachments related to your issue, you can upload them in the "Problem Attachment" field.
* Select the priority of your issue (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Issue Priority" field.
* Fill in your submitter information, including your name, email, phone number, location, and comments in the "Submitter Information", "Email", "Phone Number", "Location", and "Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`): Enter your name or any other identifier to associate the request with your account.
* **Describe Your Issue** (`issue_description`): Please describe the issue you are experiencing with your keyboard, including the steps you took when it occurred.
* **Keyboard Model** (`keyboard_model`): If you know the model of your keyboard, please enter it here.
* **Problem Type** (`problem_type`): Select the type of problem you are experiencing (Hardware, Software, or Connectivity).
* **Problem Description** (`problem_description`): If you have any additional information about the problem, describe it here.
* **Problem Image** (`problem_image`): If you have an image related to your issue, upload it here.
* **Problem Video** (`problem_video`): If you have a video related to your issue, upload it here.
* **Problem Attachment** (`problem_attachment`): If you have any attachments related to your issue, upload them here.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`): Select the priority of your issue (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`): Please include your name and contact information here.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`submitter_location`): Enter your location.
* **Comments** (`submitter_comments`): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Device** (`submitter_device`): If you are using a device, enter its type here.
* **Operating System** (`submitter_os`): If you are using a specific operating system, enter it here.
* **Browser** (`submitter_browser`): If you are using a specific browser, enter it here.
