# Smart Access Card Replacement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a replacement for a lost, stolen, or damaged smart access card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the contact email of the person making the request in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Provide a reason for the replacement in the "Reason for Replacement" field (optional).
4. Confirm if the card replacement has been confirmed or not in the "Has the Card Replacement Been Confirmed?" field.
5. Select the category of the smart access card in the "Category" field.
6. Select the department that the employee belongs to in the "Department" field.
7. Select the location where the employee will use the smart access card in the "Location" field.
8. If the card replacement has been confirmed, provide the confirmation details in the "Confirmed By" field.
9. If applicable, provide the title of the person confirming the replacement in the "Confirmed By Title" field.
10. If possible, provide the phone number of the person confirming the replacement in the "Confirmed By Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (text)**: Enter the name of the employee who is requesting the replacement card.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the person making this request.
* **Reason for Replacement (text)**: Provide a brief explanation for why the card replacement is needed (optional).
* **Has the Card Replacement Been Confirmed? (select_one)**: Select "True" if the replacement has been confirmed, "False" otherwise.
* **Category (select_multiple)**: Select one or more categories that the smart access card belongs to (Security, Access, Other).
* **Department (select_one)**: Select the department that the employee belongs to (Finance, HR, IT).
* **Location (select_one)**: Select the location where the employee will use the smart access card (Office, Remote).
* **Confirmed By (text)**: If the replacement has been confirmed, provide the details of the confirmation (optional).
* **Confirmed By Title (text)**: If the replacement has been confirmed, provide the title of the person confirming the replacement (optional).
* **Confirmed By Phone (text)**: If possible, provide the phone number of the person confirming the replacement (optional).
*   Note: Please ensure that the phone numbers and titles are provided if the replacement has been confirmed. 
*   If you are unsure about any field or option, please contact your HR representative or the security team for assistance.
