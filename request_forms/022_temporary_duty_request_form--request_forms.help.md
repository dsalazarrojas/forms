# Temporary Duty Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Duty Request Form is used to submit a request for a temporary change of duty location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Request Date**, the date you wish to be on temporary duty location.
2. In the **Duty Location**, enter the location where you will be temporarily working.
3. In the **Request Reason**, provide a brief explanation for your request.
4. If you are requesting a change in duty location, select **Duty Location Change** as **True**.
5. If you are requesting a change in duty location, provide a reason in the **Duty Location Change Reason** field.
6. Choose the person you wish to be **Assigned To**, selecting one of the available options from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose the person **Assigned By**, selecting one of the available options from the dropdown menu.
8. Optionally, add any additional comments about your request.
9. Enter your **Signature**, which will serve as acknowledgement of the submitted request.
10. Enter your email address as the **Submitted By**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: Enter the date you wish to be on temporary duty location. This will help the reviewer understand when your request is valid for.
* **Duty Location**: Enter the location where you will be temporarily working. This will help the reviewer understand where you will be working during this time.
* **Request Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for your request. This will help the reviewer understand why you are requesting a temporary duty location change.
* **Duty Location Change**: This field allows you to indicate if you are requesting a change in duty location. Select **True** if you are requesting a change, **False** otherwise.
* **Duty Location Change Reason**: If you are requesting a change in duty location, provide a reason for this change. This will help the reviewer understand why you are requesting the change.
* **Assigned To**: Choose the person you wish to be assigned to during your temporary duty location.
* **Assigned By**: Choose the person who will be responsible for assigning you to the temporary duty location.
* **Comments**: Optionally, add any additional comments about your request.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature as acknowledgement of the submitted request.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your email address as the contact information for the person submitting the request.
