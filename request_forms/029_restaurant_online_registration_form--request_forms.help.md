# Restaurant Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for restaurants to provide their information or register for a specific event or promotion. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your restaurant's name in the "restaurant_name" field.
6. Select the type of food your restaurant serves from the "type_of_food" dropdown menu.
7. Choose one or more cuisines your restaurant specializes in from the "cuisine" multiple-choice dropdown menu.
8. If applicable, enter the date you would like to visit or participate in the "date" field.
9. If applicable, select the time you would like to visit or participate in the "time" field.
10. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address. Please note that your email will be used as a contact point for any further communication.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Restaurant Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your restaurant's name.
* **Type of Food** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of food your restaurant serves.
* **Cuisine** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more cuisines your restaurant specializes in.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date you would like to visit or participate.
* **Time** (Time, Optional): If applicable, select the time you would like to visit or participate.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please use this field.
