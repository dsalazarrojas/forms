# Relief Agreement For Community Support - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Relief Agreement For Community Support" form is used to collect contact and agreement details from individuals or groups seeking support from a community. This form ensures that individuals understand and agree to certain guidelines or rules, and collects any additional information that may be relevant for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Read and agree to the guidelines by selecting "I have read and understand the guidelines" in the "consent_to_guidelines" field.
3. Choose your role in the "role" field (Volunteer, Staff, Both, or Other).
4. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the "follow_up_info" field.
5. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, not required): This is the introduction page.
* **name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **consent_to_guidelines** (`consent_to_guidelines`, select_multiple, not required): Read and agree to the guidelines by selecting "I have read and understand the guidelines".
* **agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, not required): Confirm that you have read and understood the guidelines.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, time, not required): Enter the time for follow-up actions.
* **organization** (`organization`, text, not required): Enter the name of the organization you are representing.
* **role_in_organization** (`role_in_organization`, text, not required): Enter your role in the organization.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional contact information.
* **role** (`role`, select_multiple, not required): Choose your role (Volunteer, Staff, Both, or Other).
* **other_role** (`other_role`, text, not required): Enter any additional role information if not listed in the previous field.
* **other_info** (`other_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information.
* **follow_up_info** (`follow_up_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information for follow-up actions.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Enter your signature.
* **date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date.
* **time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time.
* **timezone** (`timezone`, text, not required): Enter your timezone.
* **email_confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, not required): Enter your email address for confirmation.
* **phone_confirmation** (`phone_confirmation`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for confirmation.
* **address_confirmation** (`address_confirmation`, text, not required): Enter your address for confirmation.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Submit the form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to review the guidelines and agree to them before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
