# Petition For Study Resource - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect petitions for study resources from supporters, enabling researchers and educators to gather information about the resources needed for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief title for the resource you are requesting in the "Resource" field.
2. If applicable, provide your contact information in the "Supporter Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resource** (`Resource`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the resource you are requesting.
* **Supporter Details** (`Supporter Details`, text, optional): This section is optional, but if you are providing contact information, please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
	+ **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the individual or entity making the request.
	+ **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the individual or entity making the request.
	+ **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the individual or entity making the request.
	+ **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for the individual or entity making the request.
* **Supporter Details** (`Supporter Details`, text, optional): You may also provide any additional information about your request in this field.
