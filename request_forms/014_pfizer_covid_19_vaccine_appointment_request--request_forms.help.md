## Purpose
This form is for patients to request an appointment for the Pfizer COVID-19 vaccine. It collects your personal details, preferred appointment time and date, and other relevant information to confirm your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred date in the "Date" field.
6. Select your preferred time in the "Time" field.
7. Choose your Time Zone in the "Time Zone" field.
8. Select your preferred Vaccination Site in the "Vaccination Site" field.
9. Confirm your appointment details by selecting "Confirm" in the "Confirm Time", "Confirm Date", and "Confirm Vaccination Site" fields.
