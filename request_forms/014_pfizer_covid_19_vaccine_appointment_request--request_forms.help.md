# Pfizer COVID 19 Vaccine Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to request an appointment for the Pfizer COVID-19 vaccine. It collects your personal details, preferred appointment time and date, and other relevant information to confirm your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred date in the "Date" field.
6. Select your preferred time in the "Time" field.
7. Choose your Time Zone in the "Time Zone" field.
8. Select your preferred Vaccination Site in the "Vaccination Site" field.
9. Confirm your appointment details by selecting "Confirm" in the "Confirm Time", "Confirm Date", and "Confirm Vaccination Site" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates or questions.
* **Date** (date, required): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (time, required): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Time Zone** (select_one, required): Select your time zone to ensure correct scheduling.
* **Vaccination Site** (select_one, required): Select the location where you would like to receive your vaccine.
* **Confirm Time** (select_one, required): Confirm the time for your appointment.
* **Confirm Date** (select_one, required): Confirm the date for your appointment.
* **Confirm Vaccination Site** (select_one, required): Confirm the location for your appointment.
* **Doctor** (text, optional): Enter any message or comments for the doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the doctor.
* **Doctor Time** (time, optional): Enter the doctor's available time.
* **Doctor Time Zone** (select_one, optional): Select the doctor's time zone.
* **Doctor Site** (select_one, optional): Select the doctor's location.
* **Doctor Message** (text, optional): Enter any message for the doctor.
* **Patient Message** (text, optional): Enter any message or comments for the patient.
* **Confirm Doctor** (text, optional): Enter the doctor's name or ID.
* **Confirm Doctor Phone** (text, optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Confirm Doctor Time** (time, optional): Enter the doctor's available time.
* **Confirm Doctor Time Zone** (select_one, optional): Select the doctor's time zone.
* **Confirm Doctor Site** (select_one, optional): Select the doctor's location.
* **Confirm Vaccination Site Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vaccination site.
* **Confirm Time** (select_one, optional): Confirm the time for your appointment.
* **Confirm Time Zone** (select_one, optional): Confirm the time zone for your appointment.
* **Confirm Vaccination Site** (select_one, optional): Confirm the location for your appointment.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions.
