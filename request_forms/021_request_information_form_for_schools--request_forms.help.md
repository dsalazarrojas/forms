# Request Information Form (For Schools) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Information Form for Schools is a platform for school representatives to request information about a specific topic or subject they are interested in learning more about. It is designed to facilitate knowledge sharing and collaboration between our organization and schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Information** (`request_information_form_for_schools`, note, false): Select this field to begin the request process.
- **Your Name** (`contact_name`, text, false): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or contact information.
- **Your Email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Your Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Interested In** (`interested_subject`, text, false): Briefly describe what you would like to learn more about.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have registered with our organization and have access to our platform before submitting this form.
- Check that you are not duplicating a request we may have already addressed to avoid unnecessary communication.
