# Registration Desk Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Desk Material Request Form is designed to collect information from users who need to request materials for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the event details you want to request materials for.
* Choose the date you need the materials to be delivered.
* Select the materials you need (up to 5 options).
* Specify the details of the materials you need (e.g. color, size, etc.).
* Select the delivery location where the materials will be delivered.
* Provide any additional notes or comments about your request.
* Choose whether to submit or cancel your request.
* Fill out your contact information (if you want to be contacted about your request).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter the details of the event you want to request materials for (e.g. name, date, time, etc.). 
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date you need the materials to be delivered.
* **Material Requests** (`material_requests`): Select up to 5 materials you need from the options provided.
* **Material Details** (`material_details`): Specify the details of the materials you need (e.g. color, size, etc.).
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Select the date you need the materials to be delivered.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`): Enter the delivery location where the materials will be delivered.

* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`): Choose whether to submit or cancel your request.
