# Advance Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Advance Requisition Form is designed to collect detailed information from departments requesting items or services for internal use. This form serves as a standardized template for submitting requests, ensuring that all necessary details are provided to facilitate efficient processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into multiple pages, each containing a specific section of the request information.
2.  Please select the correct page corresponding to the section you wish to complete.
3.  Fill out the form according to the instructions on each page.
4.  Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and according to the instructions.
5.  Review your completed form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (2 - `requester_name`): This field is for the name of the person submitting the request. Please enter your department and contact information.
* **Department** (3 - `department`): This field is for identifying the department or team submitting the request.
* **Item or service description** (4 - `item_description`): Provide a detailed description and specifications of the item or service being requested.
* **Quantity needed** (5 - `quantity_needed`): Enter the exact quantity required for the item or service.
* **Justification for requisition** (6 - `justification`): Explain the business reason for the request, providing context for the item or service required.
* **Required by date** (7 - `required_by_date`): If applicable, indicate the specific date by which the item or service is required.
* **Budget code or account** (8 - `budget_code`): If applicable, enter the budget code or account responsible for charging the request.
* **Approval status** (9 - `approval_status`): Select the status of your request, either "Pending", "Approved", or "Denied" for internal use.
* **Submission date** (10 - `submission_date`): Enter the date and time you submitted the request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and according to the instructions.
* Use the "Justification for requisition" field to explain the reason for the request, as this helps in understanding the necessity of the item or service.
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors and rejections.
* If unsure about any field, please consult the relevant departmental contact or seek assistance from the form administrator.
