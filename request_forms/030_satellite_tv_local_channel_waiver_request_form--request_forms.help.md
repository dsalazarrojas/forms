# Satellite TV Local Channel Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to request a waiver for a local channel on a satellite TV system. The form will collect information from customers and employees, including their names, addresses, and the details of their request. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all requests are properly documented and approved before being processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. In the "Channel Waiver Request" section, describe the reason for your waiver request.
3. Provide the date and time of your request in the "Date of Request" and "Time of Request" sections.
4. Finally, sign and date your request with your customer signature and employee signature in the "Customer Signature" and "Employee Signature" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Name, Text, Not Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address, Text, Not Required)**: Enter your residential or business address.
* **Channel Waiver Request (Channel Waiver Request, Text, Not Required)**: Describe the reason for your waiver request.
* **Date of Request (Date of Request, Date, Not Required)**: Enter the date you made the request.
* **Time of Request (Time of Request, Time, Not Required)**: Enter the time you made the request.
* **Customer Signature (Customer Signature, Note, Required)**: Sign and date your request with your signature.
* **Employee Signature (Employee Signature, Note, Required)**: Sign and date your request with the employee's signature.
* **Note**: This field is reserved for additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is properly processed.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, you can include them in the "Customer Signature" or "Employee Signature" fields.
