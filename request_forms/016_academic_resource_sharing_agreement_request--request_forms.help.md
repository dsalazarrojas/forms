# Academic Resource Sharing Agreement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access or share academic resources between institutions or departments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Requester's Information section, which includes:
	* Your full name
	* Your email address
	* Your phone number (optional)
2. Next, specify your institutional and departmental affiliation.
3. Provide a detailed description of the resource being requested.
4. Indicate the purpose of accessing this resource.
5. Select the start and end dates of the requested access.
6. Identify the institution or department providing the resource.
7. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the contact person at the providing institution.
8. Confirm your agreement to handle shared resources in accordance with applicable confidentiality and data protection policies.
9. Add any additional notes or special conditions related to the resource sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester full name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number** (`requester_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it to facilitate communication.
* **Your institution or organization** (`requester_institution`, text, required): Specify your institutional affiliation.
* **Department or unit** (`requester_department`, text, required): Identify your department or unit within your institution.
* **Your role or title** (`requester_role`, text, required): Describe your role or title within your institution.
* **Type of resource being requested** (`resource_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of resource being requested from the list.
	+ Research data or datasets
	+ Laboratory equipment
	+ Library materials
	+ Software or digital tools
	+ Curriculum or course materials
	+ Specialized facilities
	+ Other
* **Detailed description of the resource** (`resource_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the resource being requested.
* **Purpose of accessing this resource** (`purpose_of_access`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of accessing this resource from the list.
	+ Research project
	+ Teaching or curriculum development
	+ Student training
	+ Joint publication
	+ Grant application
	+ Other
* **Requested access start date** (`access_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you wish to start accessing the resource.
* **Requested access end date** (`access_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you wish to end accessing the resource.
* **Name of institution or department providing the resource** (`sharing_institution`, text, required): Specify the name of the institution or department providing the resource.
* **Contact person at the providing institution** (`contact_at_sharing_institution`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the contact person at the providing institution.
* **Agree to handle confidentiality** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to handle shared resources in accordance with applicable confidentiality and data protection policies.
	+ Yes - I agree
	+ No - I need to discuss terms
* **Additional notes or special conditions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or special conditions related to the resource sharing.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or the resource provider.
- This form is designed to facilitate resource sharing between institutions or departments; please fill it out carefully to avoid duplication of effort.
