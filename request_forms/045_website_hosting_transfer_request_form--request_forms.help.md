# Website Hosting Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the transfer of a website's hosting to our service. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps: 

1. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number. 
2. Provide website information, such as the website name and URL. 
3. Specify your hosting preferences, including the hosting provider and plan you are interested in. 
4. Enter your desired transfer date and time. 
5. Choose a transfer type and reason, if applicable. 
6. Confirm your transfer request and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name and title.
  - This field is required for us to contact you and discuss your transfer request.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact email address.
  - This field is required for us to contact you and discuss your transfer request.

- **Website Name** (`website_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the website you are requesting to transfer.
  - This field is required for us to identify the website in question.
- **Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the website you are requesting to transfer.
  - This field is required for us to access and evaluate the website's current state.
- **Hosting Provider** (`hosting_provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current hosting provider of the website.
  - This field is required for us to understand the website's current hosting setup.
- **Hosting Plan** (`hosting_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current hosting plan of the website.
  - This field is required for us to understand the website's current hosting plan.
- **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to transfer the website hosting.
  - This field is required for us to schedule the transfer.

  - This field is required for us to schedule the transfer.
