# Textbook Adoption Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for textbook adoption requests. To be eligible, you must be a student, faculty member, or staff member at the institution. You will be asked to provide basic contact information and details about the textbook you would like to adopt. Please ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and clearly, as this information will be used for processing your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information. If a field is marked as "required", please make sure to fill it in accurately.
2. Select the relevant department from the dropdown menu if applicable.
3. Enter the book title, author, and ISBN numbers in the respective fields.
4. Enter the number of copies you would like to adopt.
5. Choose a date and time for when you would like to adopt the book.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
7. Review and submit the form when you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): 
This field is not applicable in this form. It is likely a placeholder for future development and can be ignored.
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your institutional email address.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are requesting the textbook for.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor for the course.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department that the course belongs to. Options are:
	+ English
	+ Math
	+ History
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the book you would like to adopt.
* **Book ISBN** (`book_isbn`, text, optional): Enter the ISBN number of the book you would like to adopt.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, text, optional): Enter the author of the book you would like to adopt.
* **Request Quantity** (`request_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of copies you would like to adopt.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to adopt the book.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to adopt the book.
* **Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): This field is not applicable and will be filled out internally.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, optional): This field is not applicable and will be filled out internally.
* **Form Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, optional): This field is not applicable and will be filled out internally.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and clearly.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact the relevant department or support staff.
