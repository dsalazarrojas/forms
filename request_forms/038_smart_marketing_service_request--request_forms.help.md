# smart_marketing_service_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The smart_marketing_service_request form is a tool for users to submit requests for marketing services, enabling teams to gather relevant information about their projects and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including any relevant contact details.
2. Describe your project details, such as its scope and objectives.
3. Select all the goals you would like to achieve with the marketing service.
4. Add any additional information that may be helpful in understanding your request.
5. Review your submission to ensure it accurately reflects your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): Provide your name, email address, and any other contact details that you'd like to share with our team.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe your project's scope, objectives, and any relevant context.
* **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all the goals you would like to achieve with the marketing service. This could include increasing brand awareness, generating leads, or improving customer engagement.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to mention about your project or request, please add it here.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Double-check your submission to ensure it accurately represents your needs and expectations.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to help our team understand your request effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to assist you.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you with any further questions or clarifications.
