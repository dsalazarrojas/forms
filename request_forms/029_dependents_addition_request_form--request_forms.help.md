# Dependents Addition Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about dependents that an employee or their family member is adding to their benefit enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the new dependent, including their name, relationship to you (e.g., spouse, child), date of birth, age, and contact details.
2. Select the relationship of the new dependent from the provided options (e.g., Mother, Other).
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, including the dependent's name, relationship, date of birth, age, and contact number.
4. If you are the primary account holder, select the correct answer for "Dependents Adding Dependents" to indicate whether you are adding dependents to your benefits or if someone else is adding dependents to your benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dependent Name** (`dependent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the new dependent.
* **Dependent Relationship** (`dependent_relationship`, `select_one`, true): Select the relationship of the new dependent from the options provided.
* **Dependent Date of Birth** (`dependent_dob`, `date`, true): Enter the date of birth of the new dependent in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Dependent Age** (`dependent_age`, `number`, true): Enter the age of the new dependent.
* **Dependent Gender** (`dependent_gender`, `select_multiple`, true): Select the gender of the new dependent from the options provided.
* **Dependent Contact Number** (`dependent_contact_number`, `text`, true): Enter the contact number of the new dependent.
* **Dependent Email** (`dependent_email`, `email`, true): Enter the email address of the new dependent.
* **Dependent Address** (`dependent_address`, `text`, false): This field is optional. If you want to provide the new dependent's address, you can do so here.
* **Dependents Adding Dependents** (`dependents_adding_dependents`, `select_multiple`, true): Select the correct answer indicating whether you are adding dependents to your benefits or if someone else is adding dependents to your benefits.
