# Declination Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect declination responses, including reasons, dates, and confirmations, from customers or users who wish to decline or reject a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are completing it for a genuine reason.
2. Fill out the "reason for declination" field with the relevant details about your decision to decline the product or service.
3. If necessary, select an additional reason for declination from the available options or provide another reason in the "reason for declination other" field.
4. Confirm the declination method and date in the corresponding fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "declination response form note" field for further clarification.
6. Finally, confirm your declination response in the "declination response form confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declination Response Form** (`declination_response_form`, text, not required): This field is a placeholder for the form name and is not meant to be filled out.
* **Reason for Declination** (`reason_for_declination`, text, not required): Enter the main reason why you are declining or rejecting the product or service.
* **Confirm Declination** (`confirm_declination`, text, not required): Confirm that you are declining or rejecting the product or service.
* **Declination Date** (`decline_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the declination response.
* **Declination Response Form Note** (`declination_response_form_note`, note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about your declination response.
* **Confirm Declination Method** (`confirm_declination_method`, text, not required): Specify the method of declination response.
* **Confirm Declination Date** (`confirm_declination_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the confirm declination response.
* **Reason for Declination Other** (`reason_for_declination_other`, text, not required): Enter any additional reason for declination not listed in the options.
* **Declination Response Form Confirmation** (`declination_response_form_confirmation`, text, not required): Confirm your declination response.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and honestly, as it's intended to provide clear and transparent information about your decision.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is subject to change, and we may add or remove fields as needed.
