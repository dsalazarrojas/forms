# Call Forwarding Deactivation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Call Forwarding Deactivation Request Form is used to request the deactivation of call forwarding services on your account.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the required fields with the correct information for your account.
2. Review and complete the optional fields if applicable.
3. Verify that your electronic signature is accurate and valid.
4. Submit the form to initiate the request for call forwarding deactivation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the account holder.
- **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of your service account.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a number where we can reach you for follow-up.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for confirmation of deactivation.
- **Phone Number for Deactivation** (`phone_line_to_deactivate`, text, required): Enter the specific phone line to deactivate call forwarding on.
- **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, required): Select your current telecommunications provider.
- **Requested Deactivation Date** (`deactivation_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the call forwarding should stop.
- **Requested Deactivation Time** (`deactivation_time`, time, optional): Enter the preferred time for the change to take effect.
- **Reason for Deactivation** (`reason_for_deactivation`, select_one, optional): Enter the reason for deactivating the service (optional).
- **Additional Comments or Instructions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any specific details our team should know.
- **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Please type your full name to authorize this request.
## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your electronic signature before submitting the form.
- If you experience any issues or have questions, contact us at the phone number provided in the Contact Phone Number field.
