# Free Shipping Eligibility Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps you request free shipping on your order. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about your order, and our team will review your request to determine if you are eligible for free shipping.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shipping_method** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the shipping method you used for your order (Ground, Air, or Other). This field helps us understand how you shipped your order, which may affect your eligibility for free shipping.
* **shipping_address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address. This field is used to verify your address, and it's essential to ensure accuracy.
* **customer_note** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your order or request. This field is optional but can help us understand your situation better.
* **contact_method** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Both). This field ensures we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **order_date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you placed your order. This field helps us verify the order date, which may affect your eligibility for free shipping.
* **order_time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you placed your order. This field is optional but can help us narrow down the order details.
* **ship_by** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to ship your order by. This field helps us understand your shipping expectations.
* **shipping_cost** (`text`, required: false): If you have any relevant shipping cost information, enter it here. This field is optional but can help us assess your request.
* **store_location** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select where you shopped (Online, Local, or Other). This field helps us understand where you made your purchase.
* **customer_note_2** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your order or request. This field is optional but can help us understand your situation better.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
* We'll review your request and contact you according to your preferred contact method.
