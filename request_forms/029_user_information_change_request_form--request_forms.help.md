# user_information_change_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request changes to a user's internal information, including but not limited to, profile updates, department affiliation changes, or other internal data updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Enter your user ID
* Provide your first name
* Provide your last name
* Enter your email address
* Enter your phone number
* Select your user type (User, Customer, Staff, or Admin)
* Select your department (Sales, Marketing, Support, or Finance)
* Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID (user_id)**: A unique numeric ID assigned to each user.
* **First Name (first_name)**: Your first name as it appears in your profile.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Your last name as it appears in your profile.
* **Email (email)**: The email address associated with your user account.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Your phone number as it appears in your profile.
* **User Type (user_type)**: Select the category that best describes your role within the company (User, Customer, Staff, or Admin).
* **Department (department)**: Choose the department that best represents your current role or affiliation.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your request for information change.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to reset the form and start over.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you are submitting for only one user's information.
* If you are unsure about any of the form fields, contact your administrator for clarification.
