# Demon Summoning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting a summoning or meeting with a practitioner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demon Summoning Request** (`demon_summoning_request_form`): This is the main title of the form. Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`): Select one of the provided scheduling options to indicate your preferred scheduling time.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`): Provide more details about your request, such as the nature of the summoning or meeting you are requesting.
* **Requestor Information** (`requestor_info`): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate how you would like to be contacted.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`): Enter a brief description of the event or meeting you are requesting.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Scheduling Preferences 2** (`scheduling_preferences_2`): Select one of the provided scheduling options to indicate your preferred scheduling time.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of your request.
* **Practitioner Information** (`practitioner_info`): This is a note field for the practitioner's information. You may use this field to add any relevant notes or comments.
* **Practitioner Availability** (`practitioner_availability`): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the practitioner's availability.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of event or meeting you are requesting.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Enter a brief description of the event or meeting you are requesting.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or meeting you are requesting.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or meeting you are requesting.
* **Notes** (`notes`): This is a note field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This field is automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): This field is automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`): Enter the name of the practitioner you are requesting.
* **Practitioner Title** (`practitioner_title`): Enter the title of the practitioner you are requesting.
* **Practitioner Email** (`practitioner_email`): Enter the email address of the practitioner you are requesting.
* **Practitioner Phone** (`practitioner_phone`): Enter the phone number of the practitioner you are requesting.
* **Practitioner Availability 2** (`practitioner_availability_2`): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the practitioner's availability.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): This field is automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`): This is a note field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`): This is a note field for any additional comments or notes.
