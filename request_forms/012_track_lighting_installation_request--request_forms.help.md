# Track Lighting Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers requesting a lighting installation. It helps electricians or electrical contractors gather necessary details to complete the installation efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the type of installation the customer requires.
4. Provide the number of track lights, track light fixtures, and circuit breakers needed for the installation.
5. Enter the date and time of the installation.
6. Enter any follow-up contact information for the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant customer details, such as their account number or project number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter the customer's full name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for the installation.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Installation Type** (`installation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of installation the customer requires.
* **Number of Track Lights** (`number_of_track_lights`, number, optional): Enter the number of track lights required.
* **Number of Track Light Fixtures** (`number_of_track_light_fixtures`, number, optional): Enter the number of track light fixtures required.
* **Number of Circuit Breakers** (`number_of_circuit_breakers`, number, optional): Enter the number of circuit breakers required.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the installation.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the installation.
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up contact information for the customer.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's instructions or ask your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, so do not share it with customers directly.
