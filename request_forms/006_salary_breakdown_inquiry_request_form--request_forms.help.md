# salary_breakdown_inquiry_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "salary_breakdown_inquiry_request_form" is used to request salary breakdown information for an employee. This form helps us gather essential details about the employee's salary, ensuring we have accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled out.
2. Enter the Employee ID and Employee Name of the relevant employee.
3. Provide the Reason for Inquiry to help us understand your request.
4. Optionally, enter the Salary Range, Start Date, and End Date if applicable.
5. If necessary, attach any Supporting Documents for further reference.
6. Fill out the Submitted By, Reviewed By, and Review Status fields according to your role and the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (2)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee.
* **Employee Name (3)**: Type the name of the employee.
* **Salary Range (4)**: Enter the range of salary for the employee if you have access to this information.
* **Start Date (5)**: If applicable, provide the start date of the salary period.
* **End Date (6)**: If applicable, provide the end date of the salary period.
* **Reason for Inquiry (7)**: Briefly explain why you're requesting the salary breakdown information.
* **Detailed Salary Explanation (8)**: Add any additional details or context about the salary, if needed.
* **Supporting Documents (9)**: Attach any documents that support your request for salary breakdown information.
* **Submission Date (13)**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By (12)**: Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Reviewed By (14)**: Select the people who have reviewed the form.
* **Review Date (15)**: Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **Review Status (17)**: Select the status of the review, such as Approved, Rejected, or In Progress.
* **Status (19)**: Select the overall status of the form, such as New, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Submission Status (22)**: Select the status of the submission, such as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Created At (23)**: Displays the date when the form was created.
* **Updated At (24)**: Displays the date when the form was last updated.
* **Deleted At (25)**: Displays the date when the form was deleted (if applicable). 

## Tips
* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy and completeness before submission.
* Attach supporting documents to provide context and evidence for your request.
* If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the relevant team or department for assistance.
* Be mindful of the submission status and review process to ensure timely and accurate processing of your request.
