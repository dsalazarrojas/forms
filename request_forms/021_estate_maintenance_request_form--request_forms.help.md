# Estate Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Maintenance Request Form is a tool for users to submit requests for maintenance and upkeep of their estate properties. This form collects information about the property and the work required, which helps us prioritize and schedule the work efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Property Request field with a brief description of the maintenance request.
2. Enter the address of the property where the maintenance is needed.
3. Provide a detailed description of the maintenance work required.
4. Choose the priority level for the work (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select a date and time for the maintenance schedule.
6. Enter your contact name and email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Add any additional notes about the maintenance request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Request** (`Property Request`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the maintenance request.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property where the maintenance is needed.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the maintenance work required.
* **Priority** (`Priority`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a priority level for the work (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Schedule Date** (`Schedule Date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the maintenance schedule.
* **Schedule Time** (`Schedule Time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the maintenance schedule.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the maintenance request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about the status of a previous request, please contact our team.
* If you need to make any changes to a submitted request, please reach out to our team.
