# Smart Lock Connectivity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lock Connectivity Request Form is designed to help our team quickly identify and resolve connectivity issues related to your smart lock. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and we will get in touch with you shortly to discuss further steps.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your personal details, including your First Name, Email, and Phone number.
- Enter your Smart Lock's name as it is displayed on your device or in your account.
- Specify your smart lock's location, such as its current address or installation site.
- Choose the model of your smart lock from the provided options.
- Enter the current Firmware Version of your smart lock.
- Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with your smart lock.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled in your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you with updates and information about your issue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Smart Lock Name** (`smart_lock_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your smart lock as it is displayed on your device or in your account.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where your smart lock is currently installed, or the site where you are experiencing the issue.
* **Smart Lock Model** (`smart_lock_model`, select_one, required): Choose the model of your smart lock from the provided options to help us identify the correct firmware version.
* **Firmware Version** (`firmware_version`, text, required): Enter the current firmware version of your smart lock as shown on the device or in your account.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the issues you are experiencing with your smart lock, including any error messages or symptoms you have encountered.
