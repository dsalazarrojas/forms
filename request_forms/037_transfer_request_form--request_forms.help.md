# Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transfer Request Form is a tool used to collect information for internal transfer requests between different departments or teams. This form helps to streamline the process of requesting a transfer and provides a clear and organized way to gather necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page number and proceed to that page.
2. Fill in the required information on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Request Form Page 1**: This is the first page of the form, which may contain introductory information or a brief explanation of the transfer request process. Please fill in any necessary information on this page, such as any additional comments or details.
* **Request Form Information**: This page provides a space to enter any additional information about the transfer request. Please provide any relevant details that will help us understand your transfer needs.
* **Transfer Details**: This page allows you to enter any necessary details about the transfer, such as dates, times, or specific requirements.
* **School Transfer**: On this page, select one of the options from the drop-down menu to indicate the type of school transfer you are requesting. Please choose the option that best describes your transfer needs.
* **Transfer Request Form Page 5**: This page may contain any additional questions or requirements specific to the transfer process. Please fill in any necessary information on this page, such as dates, times, or specific requirements.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required information on each page to ensure that your transfer request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to the relevant department or team for assistance.
