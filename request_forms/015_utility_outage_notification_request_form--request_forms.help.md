# Utility Outage Notification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report utility outages, such as power or water disruptions, to the relevant service provider. Please fill in your details to ensure a swift response and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and contact information in the fields marked as required (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number).
2. Provide a clear description of the situation, including any relevant details (Description field).
3. Enter the address affected by the outage (Address field).
4. Specify the date and duration of the outage (Date of Outage and Duration fields).
5. Select the appropriate response from the options provided (Request field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the outage situation, including any relevant details.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address affected by the outage.
* **Date of Outage** (`date_of_outage`, date, required): Enter the date of the planned or unplanned outage.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, required): Specify the duration of the outage.
* **Request** (`request`, select_one, required): Select the response you would like from the service provider:
	+ `repair`: Repair the utility service as soon as possible.
	+ `repair and bill`: Repair the service and bill the customer for the outage duration.
	+ `bill only`: Only bill the customer for the outage duration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields to submit the form correctly.
- If you have already submitted a report for this event, do not submit another. Instead, contact the utility company to update the existing report.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the utility company directly.
