# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Form is used to submit claims for medical expenses to be reimbursed by insurance providers. This form requires you to provide your personal and medical information, as well as details about your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal and medical details.
2. Enter your name and address in the "patient_name" and "patient_address" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the date of the medical expense in the "claim_date" field.
4. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated you.
5. Specify the amount of the claim in the "claim_amount" field.
6. Enter your healthcare provider's license number.
7. Select the relationship you have with the healthcare provider (e.g., "Option 1" or "Option 2").
8. Enter your insurance ID number.
9. Click the "submit" button to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification. (Required)
  - Enter your name as it is spelled on your ID card or driver's license.
  
- **patient_address**: Your home address. (Optional)
  - Enter your street address, including apartment number (if applicable).
  
- **claim_date**: Date of medical expense. (Optional)
  - Enter the date of the medical expense, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  
- **provider_name**: Name of healthcare provider. (Optional)
  - Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated you.
  
- **claim_amount**: Amount of claim. (Optional)
  - Enter the total amount of the claim, without any dollar signs or commas.
  
- **provider_license_number**: Healthcare provider's license number. (Optional)
  - Enter the healthcare provider's license number as it appears on their ID card or certification.
  
- **patient_relationship**: Your relationship with the healthcare provider. (Optional)
  - Select one of the provided options (e.g., "Option 1" or "Option 2") to describe your relationship with the healthcare provider.
  
- **insurance_id**: Insurance ID number. (Optional)
  - Enter your insurance ID number, as it appears on your ID card or policy document.
  
- **submit**: Submit your claim. (Required)
  - Click the "submit" button to submit your claim for processing.
