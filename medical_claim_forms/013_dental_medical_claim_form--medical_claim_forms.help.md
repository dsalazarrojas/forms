# Dental Medical Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for medical claims related to dental care. It is designed to collect all necessary details for processing claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Info** page with the patient's details.
2. Provide information about the **Medical History** of the patient.
3. Enter details about the **Dental Clinic Info**.
4. Enter the **Treatment Info** for the claim.
5. Provide **Billing Info** for the claim.
6. Enter **Insurance Info** relevant to the claim.
7. Complete the **Doctor Info** section with the necessary details.
8. Sign the **Signature** section with your digital signature.
9. Sign the **Patient Signature** section with the patient's signature.
10. Sign the **Doctor Signature** section with the doctor's signature.
11. Enter the **Date Signed** for the doctor and patient signatures.
12. Enter the **Date Claim Made** for the claim.
13. Enter the **Claim Form Number** if applicable.
14. Enter the **Patient Name** for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (Page 1, Text):
	+ This page collects the patient's basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (Page 2, Text):
	+ This section is for the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **Dental Clinic Info** (Page 3, Text):
	+ This page is for the dental clinic's information, including the clinic's name and address.
* **Treatment Info** (Page 4, Text):
	+ This section is for detailing the treatment provided to the patient, including the type of treatment and cost.
* **Billing Info** (Page 5, Text):
	+ This page is for billing information, including the amount due and payment method.
* **Insurance Info** (Page 6, Text):
	+ This section is for insurance information, including the insurance company and policy number.
* **Doctor Info** (Page 7, Text):
	+ This page is for the doctor's information, including name and qualifications.
* **Signature** (Page 8, Text):
	+ This is the digital signature section for the doctor's signature.
* **Patient Signature** (Page 9, Text):
	+ This is the digital signature section for the patient's signature.
* **Doctor Signature** (Page 10, Text):
	+ This is the digital signature section for the doctor's signature.
* **Date Signed** (Page 11, Date):
	+ This is the date the doctor and patient signatures were made.
* **Date Claim Made** (Page 12, Date):
	+ This is the date the claim was made.
* **Claim Form Number** (Page 13, Text):
	+ This is a unique number assigned to the claim.
* **Patient Name** (Page 14, Text):
	+ This is the patient's name for verification purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check the information for any errors before submission.
- Use the **Date Signed** and **Date Claim Made** fields for timestamping the signatures and claim.
- Use the **Claim Form Number** for tracking and reference purposes.
- Keep the patient's **Patient Name** private and secure.
