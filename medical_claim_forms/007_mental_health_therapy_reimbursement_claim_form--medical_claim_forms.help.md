# Mental Health Therapy Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Therapy Reimbursement Claim Form is a document used to submit claims for reimbursement of mental health therapy sessions. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your claim is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Enter the therapist's name in the `therapist_name` field.
3. Select the date of the treatment in the `treatment_date` field using the calendar picker or by entering the date manually.
4. Enter the session length in the `session_length` field. Please use the format specified in the field (e.g., "1 hour", "2 hours", etc.).
5. Enter the number of sessions attended by the patient in the `number_of_sessions` field.
6. Enter the total cost of the session(s) in the `session_cost` field.
7. Enter the total claim amount in the `claim_total` field.
8. Select the date of the claim submission in the `claim_date` field using the calendar picker or by entering the date manually.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
- **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the therapist who provided the treatment.
- **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the treatment using the calendar picker or enter the date manually in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Session Length** (`session_length`, text, required: false): Enter the length of the session (e.g., "1 hour", "2 hours", etc.).
- **Number of Sessions** (`number_of_sessions`, text, required: false): Enter the number of sessions attended by the patient.
- **Session Cost** (`session_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the session(s).
- **Claim Total** (`claim_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount to be claimed for reimbursement.
- **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the claim submission using the calendar picker or enter the date manually in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your entries before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have all necessary supporting documentation and records before submitting the claim.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or authorized personnel for guidance.
- This form is intended for authorized personnel only, so please confirm that you are allowed to access and complete it.
