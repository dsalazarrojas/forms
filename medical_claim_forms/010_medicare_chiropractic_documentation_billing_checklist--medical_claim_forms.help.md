# Medicare Chiropractic Documentation and Billing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and bill for chiropractic services received by a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Chiropractic Visit Details**: Enter a detailed description of the patient's visit.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
3. **Date of Service**: Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter manually. This is the date when the chiropractic service was provided.
4. **Chiropractor Name**: Enter your name and license number, as required by Medicare regulations.
5. **Patient Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
6. **Billing Info**: Enter the billing information, including the billing address and any other required information.
7. **Diagnosis**: Select from the list of provided options or choose "Other (Please specify)" to enter a custom diagnosis. This field is crucial for accurate billing and insurance processing.
8. **Treatment Plan**: Describe the treatment plan provided for the patient, including any specific treatments, exercises, or recommendations. This field helps ensure that the patient receives the necessary care and that billing is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Chiropractic Visit Details**: A brief description of the patient's visit, including the reason for the visit and any notable events or observations.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Service**: The date when the chiropractic service was provided.
* **Chiropractor Name**: Your name and license number, as required for billing and insurance purposes.
* **Patient Contact Info**: The patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Billing Info**: The billing information, including the billing address and any other required details.
* **Diagnosis**: A list of provided options or a custom diagnosis entered by the user.
* **Treatment Plan**: A description of the treatment plan provided for the patient.
