# Critical Illness Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for claimants (individuals or their representatives) to provide critical illness claim information.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing a title for your claim.
2. Enter your name as the claimant.
3. Enter your address.
4. Enter the date of your admission to the healthcare system.
5. Enter the date of your discharge.
6. Describe your diagnosis.
7. Provide detailed information about your critical illness.
8. Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
9. If relevant, select their license type (e.g., MD, DO).
10. If desired, provide your date of birth.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter the name of the policy holder (typically your insurer).
14. Enter your policy number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Claim Form Title** (`critical_illness_claim_form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title describing your claim.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your residential address.
* **Date of Admission** (`date_of_admission`, date, required): Enter the date you were admitted to the healthcare system.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Enter the date you were discharged from the healthcare system.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, required): Describe your diagnosis in detail.
* **Critical Illness Details** (`critical_illness_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your critical illness.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider License** (`healthcare_provider_license`, select_one, required): Select the type of license your healthcare provider holds (e.g., MD, DO).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): If desired, enter your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Policy Holder Name** (`policy_holder_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your policy holder (typically your insurer).
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter your policy number.
