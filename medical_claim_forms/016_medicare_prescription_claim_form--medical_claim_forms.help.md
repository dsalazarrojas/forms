# Medicare Prescription Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Prescription Claim Form is used to submit a claim for reimbursement for a medical service provided to a claimant. This form is used to report services to Medicare and obtain payment for these services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name** (claimant_name, text, required): Enter the name of the claimant who received the medical service.
* **Claimant Address** (claimant_address, text, optional): Enter the address of the claimant (if different from their name).
* **Date of Service** (date_of_service, date, optional): Enter the date the medical service was provided.
* **Service Providers Name** (service_providers_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who provided the service.
* **Claimant Date of Birth** (claimant_date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the claimant.
* **Medicare ID Number** (medicare_id_number, text, optional): Enter the claimant's Medicare ID number.
* **Prescription Number** (prescription_number, text, optional): Enter the claimant's prescription number.
* **Prescriber Name** (prescriber_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who prescribed the medication.
* **Prescriber Title** (prescriber_title, text, optional): Enter the title of the prescriber (e.g., MD, DO, NP, etc.).
* **Prescriber NPI Number** (prescriber_npi_number, text, optional): Enter the prescriber's NPI (National Provider Identifier) number.
* **Claim Date** (claim_date, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Amount** (claim_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Claim Type** (claim_type, text, required): Select the type of claim (e.g., Part A, Part B, etc.).
* **Claim Status** (claim_status, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the claim (e.g., Open, Closed, Denied, In Progress).
* **Submitter ID** (submitter_id, text, required): Enter the ID of the submitter.
* **Claimant Phone** (claimant_phone, text, optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Prescriber Phone** (prescriber_phone, text, optional): Enter the prescriber's phone number.
* **Claimant Email** (claimant_email, email, optional): Enter the claimant's email address.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant department or healthcare provider.
