# Hospitalization Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and process hospitalization claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the claimant.
2. Enter the address of the claimant.
3. Record the date of hospitalization and the date of discharge.
4. Enter the name and specialty of the admitting doctor.
5. Select the reason for hospitalization from the provided options.
6. Provide the name of the hospital where the claimant was treated.
7. Enter the name of the medical bill provider.
8. Enter the claim number and policy number (if applicable).
9. Record the claim date.
10. Enter the claim amount.
11. Select the claim status.
12. Provide any relevant remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Hospitalization Claim Form (Page 1)

* **Name Of Claimant** (`name_of_claimant`, text, required): Enter the name of the person making the claim.
* **Address Of Claimant** (`address_of_claimant`, text, required): Enter the address of the person making the claim.
* **Date Of Hospitalization** (`date_of_hospitalization`, date, required): Record the date of the hospitalization event (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Date Of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Record the date of discharge from the hospital (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Hospital Details (Page 2)

* **Admitting Doctor Name** (`admitting_doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor who admitted the claimant to the hospital.
* **Admitting Doctor Specialty** (`admitting_doctor_specialty`, text, required): Enter the specialty of the doctor who admitted the claimant.
* **Reason For Hospitalization** (`reason_for_hospitalization`, select_multiple, required): Select all the reasons that apply for the hospitalization event from the provided options.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the claimant was treated.

### Claim Information (Page 3)

* **Medical Bill Provider** (`medical_bill_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical bill provider.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, text, required): Enter the claim number.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter the policy number.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required): Record the date of the claim (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required): Enter the claim amount.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the claim from the provided options.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required): Enter any relevant remarks about the claim.

Note: Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your claim is processed correctly.
