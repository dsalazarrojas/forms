# skincare_facial_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for skincare services, including client details, upcoming visit information, and service expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for the select_one fields (e.g., "Which service did you come from?" and "What time of day is your visit scheduled for?").
3. Enter the correct information for the text fields (e.g., "Client Information", "Upcoming Visit", and "Additional Notes").
4. Enter the correct date and time for the date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself that you'd like to share with the esthetician.
* **Upcoming Visit** (Upcoming Visit, text, required: false): Enter the details of your upcoming visit, including the date and time of your appointment.
* **Service Expectations** (Service Expectations, text, required: false): Enter any specific expectations you have for the services you'll be receiving, such as concerns or areas of focus.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your visit, such as your skin concerns or allergies.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Which service did you come from?** (Microderm, Chemical Peel, Derma-Peel, LED Therapy, select_one, required: true): Select the service you're currently undergoing or have completed.
* **Which of the following services will you be receiving today?** (Microderm, Chemical Peel, Derma-Peel, LED Therapy, select_multiple, required: true): Select the services you'll be receiving during your visit.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your upcoming visit.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false): Enter the time of your upcoming visit.
* **What time of day is your visit scheduled for?** (9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM, select_one, required: true): Select the time of day your visit is scheduled for.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your visit.
