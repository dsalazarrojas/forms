# Salon Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Salon Appointment Form is designed to help clients schedule appointments for various salon services, allowing them to select the desired date and time for their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for your scheduled visit by choosing one of the available options under "Choose Date".
2. Choose the services you wish to have performed during your visit by selecting one or more options under "Select services".
3. Choose the time slots for your visit by selecting one or more options under "Choose Time".
4. Enter your name and email address to be contacted about your appointment.
5. Provide any additional comments or instructions about your visit in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule a visit** (`schedule_visit`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your visit. This will be the date when you would like to come to the salon.
* **Select services** (`select_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more services from the list:
	+ Bridal hair and makeup
	+ Makeup
	+ Haircut
	+ Hair color
	+ Color correction
	+ Bridal party services
* **Choose Date** (`choose_date`, select_one, required/optional): Select a date for your visit from the options provided.
* **Choose Time** (`choose_time`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more time slots for your visit:
	+ 10:00-10:30
	+ 11:00-11:30
	+ 12:00-12:30
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to associate with your appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be contacted about your appointment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted about your appointment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or instructions about your visit.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information for each field to ensure that your appointment is scheduled correctly.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* Please be aware that some services may have limited availability or require additional booking fees.
