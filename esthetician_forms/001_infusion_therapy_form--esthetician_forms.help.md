# Infusion Therapy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infusion Therapy Form is used to document patient information and treatment details for infusion therapy treatments. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that patient records are up-to-date and treatment is properly administered.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully** to ensure you understand the fields and their purpose.
2. **Fill out the form** according to your practice's or organization's guidelines and policies.
3. **Enter the patient's information** in the `patient_information` field, including their name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
4. **Document the infusion treatment** in the `infusion_treatment` field, including the type of treatment, dosage, and any relevant notes.
5. **Provide appointment details** in the `appointment_details` field, such as the date and time of the appointment.
6. **Enter contact information** for the patient and their emergency contact in the `contact_information` and `emergency_contact` fields, respectively.
7. **Document the patient's medical history** in the `medical_history` field, including any relevant allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
8. **Specify the infusion therapist** who will be administering the treatment in the `infusion_therapist` field.
9. **Choose an available time** for the appointment using the `available_time` field. This field is a dropdown menu with options to choose from.
10. **Schedule an appointment date** for the treatment in the `appointment_date` field.
11. **Set the appointment time** for the treatment using the `appointment_time` field.
12. **Specify the infusion location** using the `infusion_location` field, which is a dropdown menu with options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (patient_information)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
* **Infusion Treatment (infusion_treatment)**: Document the type of infusion therapy, dosage, and any notes relevant to the treatment.
* **Appointment Details (appointment_details)**: Enter the date and time of the appointment.
* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)**: Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact, including their name and relationship to the patient.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Document the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Medical History Details (medical_history_details)**: Enter any additional details about the patient's medical history.
* **Infusion Therapist (infusion_therapist)**: Specify the name of the healthcare professional who will be administering the treatment.
* **Available Time (available_time)**: Choose a available time for the appointment from the dropdown menu.
* **Appointment Date (appointment_date)**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time (appointment_time)**: Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Infusion Location (infusion_location)**: Choose the location where the infusion therapy will take place from the dropdown menu.
