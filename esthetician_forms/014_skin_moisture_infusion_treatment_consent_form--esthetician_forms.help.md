# skin_moisture_infusion_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The skin moisture infusion treatment consent form is a form designed to collect client information and obtain consent for a treatment plan. This form is used to gather essential details about the client and their health status, as well as to obtain a signed consent for the treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill in the client's details in the `ClientDetails` field.
* Provide any relevant health information in the `HealthInformation` field.
* Select either 'True' or 'False' to indicate that you have signed and understood the treatment information in the `SignedConsent` field.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the `AdditionalComments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ClientDetails** (ClientDetails, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the client's details. Please fill in the client's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **HealthInformation** (HealthInformation, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any relevant health information about the client. Please include any medical conditions, allergies, or other factors that may affect the treatment.
* **SignedConsent** (SignedConsent, select_one, required: false): This field is used to obtain a signed consent from the client. Please select 'True' to indicate that you have read and understood the treatment information, and 'False' otherwise.
* **TreatmentPlan** (TreatmentPlan, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the treatment plan.
* **AdditionalComments** (AdditionalComments, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the treatment plan or the client.
