# Digital Skin Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Skin Analysis Survey is designed to collect information about a client's skin type, skin tone, and other relevant details. This data will be used to provide personalized skincare advice and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title and description fields with your name and a brief description of why you're taking the survey.
2. Answer the following questions:
	* Select your skin type (Dry, Oily, Sensitive, Normal, Combination, or Other).
	* Choose your skin tone (Fair, Medium, Dark, or Other).
	* Select your preferred skincare product (Product 1, Product 2, or Product 3).
3. Provide a brief description of your skin condition (optional).
4. Enter your birthdate to ensure we can provide personalized advice.
5. Choose your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
6. Select a follow-up date for further analysis.
7. Enter your phone number (optional, for future follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): A brief title to identify the survey.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): A short description of why you're taking the survey.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, so we can address you personally.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, required/optional): Your birthdate for age-based analysis.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required/optional): Select your gender for proper product advice.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_multiple, required/true): Choose your skin type from the options provided.
* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, select_one, required/optional): Select your skin tone from the options provided.
* **Preferred Skincare Product** (`preferred_skincare_product`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred product for recommendations.
* **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe your skin concerns or conditions.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up`, date, required/optional): Choose a follow-up date for further analysis.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for future follow-up calls.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when answering the questions to provide accurate results.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your esthetician for guidance.
- This form is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
