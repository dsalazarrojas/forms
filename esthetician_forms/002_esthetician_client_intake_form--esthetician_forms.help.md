# Esthetician Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Esthetician Client Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from clients before providing esthetic treatments. This form ensures that the esthetician can provide safe and effective care by understanding the client's medical history, allergies, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read through each section of the form to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, using the options provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Medical History**: This section is used to gather information about your past medical experiences and conditions. Please provide a detailed account of any medical conditions or surgeries you have had.
* **Allergies**: Please select all the allergies you are currently experiencing or have had in the past. This will help us ensure your safety during treatment.
* **Skincare Concerns**: If you have any skin concerns, such as acne, aging, or hyperpigmentation, please describe them in this section.
* **Medical History**: This section is used to gather more in-depth information about your medical history, including any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please select any medical conditions you currently experience or have had in the past.
* **Medication**: If you are currently taking any medication, please list them in this section.
* **Medical Procedure**: If you have had any medical procedures, such as laser treatments or chemical peels, please select them from the options provided.
* **Skincare Products**: If you use any skincare products, please list them in this section, including their ingredients and frequency of use.
* **Medical Specialty**: If you have a specific medical specialty, such as diabetes or high blood pressure, please select them from the options provided.
* **Notes**: Any additional information you feel is relevant to your treatment can be noted here.
