# lash_extension_client_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Extension Client Liability Waiver form is used to gather essential information from clients about their medical conditions, emergency contacts, and allergies before undergoing lash extension services. This form helps ensure that all clients provide accurate and reliable information, reducing the risk of potential complications during and after the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your information accurately, including your:
	*   Client Name
	*   Email
	*   Phone number
	*   Address
	*   Date of Birth
2.  If applicable, provide emergency contact information
	*   Emergency Contact Name
	*   Emergency Contact Phone number
3.  Describe your:
	*   Medical Procedure Danger Signs
	*   Medical Procedure Description
	*   Medical Procedure Dosage
4.  If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" or "Allergies" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Medical Procedure Danger Signs** (`medical_procedure_danger_signs`, text, required): Describe any medical conditions that may be relevant to your lash extension procedure.
* **Medical Procedure Description** (`medical_procedure_description`, text, required): Describe the medical procedure you are seeking.
* **Medical Procedure Dosage** (`medical_procedure_dosage`, text, required): Specify the dosage or frequency of the medical procedure you are seeking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you may have.
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or cancellations in your procedure.
