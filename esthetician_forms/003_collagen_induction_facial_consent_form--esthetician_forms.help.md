# Collagen Induction Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collagen Induction Facial Consent Form is a document that clients must sign before receiving a collagen induction facial treatment. This form is used to gather essential information about the client's medical history, skin type, and treatment expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's full name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your skin type.
6. Select any current skin conditions.
7. If applicable, list any allergies or medications.
8. If applicable, describe any recent procedures or treatments.
9. Indicate your recent sun exposure.
10. Confirm your pregnancy status.
11. Choose your treatment goals.
12. Rate your pain tolerance.
13. Accept the potential downtime and recovery risks.
14. Acknowledge the potential risks and side effects.
15. Commit to following aftercare instructions.
16. Declare your commitment to using sunscreen for 2 weeks post-treatment.
17. Sign to consent to the collagen induction facial treatment.
18. Enter any special observations or considerations for your provider.
19. Sign on the consent date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for scheduling or questions.
* **Skin Type** (Required): Select one of the following skin types:
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
	+ Normal
	+ Sensitive
* **Current Skin Conditions** (Required): Select all applicable conditions:
	+ Acne
	+ Rosacea
	+ Eczema
	+ Psoriasis
	+ None
* **Allergies** (Optional): If you have any skin allergies or sensitivities, list them here.
* **Current Medications** (Optional): List any topical or oral medications you are currently taking.
* **Recent Procedures** (Optional): Describe any recent facial procedures or treatments you have undergone.
* **Recent Sun Exposure** (Required): Select one of the following sun exposure levels:
	+ Minimal
	+ Moderate
	+ Significant
	+ Severe Sunburn
* **Pregnancy Status** (Required): Confirm your current pregnancy status:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Prefer not to answer
* **Treatment Goals** (Required): Select all applicable treatment goals:
	+ Reduce Fine Lines
	+ Improve Texture
	+ Reduce Scars
	+ Even Skin Tone
	+ General Rejuvenation
* **Pain Tolerance** (Required): Rate your pain tolerance:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Downtime Acceptance** (Required): Accept the potential downtime and recovery risks:
	+ Yes
* **Risks Understood** (Required): Acknowledge the potential risks and side effects:
	+ Yes
* **Aftercare Agreement** (Required): Commit to following aftercare instructions:
	+ Yes
* **Sunscreen Commitment** (Required): Commit to using sunscreen for 2 weeks post-treatment:
	+ Yes
* **Consent Signature** (Required): Sign to consent to the treatment:
	+ Yes
* **Provider Notes** (Optional): Enter any special considerations or observations for your provider.
* **Consent Date** (Required): Enter the date you sign this consent form.

Please note that some fields are marked as required, indicating that they must be completed for the form to be considered valid.
