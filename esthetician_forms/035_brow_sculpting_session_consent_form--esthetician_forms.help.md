# Brow Sculpting Session Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Brow Sculpting Session Consent Form is a crucial document that you, as the client, will need to complete before your session. This form helps us ensure that we have your consent, medical clearance, and emergency contact information to provide the best possible experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review each section, ensuring you answer all questions truthfully.
2. Fill in your full client name on the first page.
3. Provide the date of your appointment on the second page.
4. List any health conditions that may affect the procedure on the third page.
5. Confirm your consent for the brow sculpting session on the fourth page.
6. Enter any relevant allergy information on the fifth page.
7. Share your emergency contact information on the sixth page.
8. Decide on your photo release preference on the seventh page.
9. Add any additional notes for the technician on the eighth page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Procedure date** (`procedure_date`, date, required): Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Health conditions** (`health_conditions`, text, optional): Mention any medical conditions that might impact the procedure, such as allergies, skin irritations, or previous surgeries.
* **Consent confirmation** (`consent_confirmation`, select_one, required): Choose "I consent" to confirm your agreement for the session.
* **Allergy information** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): Provide details on any relevant allergies or sensitivities you'd like us to know about.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Share your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Photo release** (`photo_release`, select_one, optional): Decide if you permit us to use photos before and after the session.
* **Technician notes** (`technician_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes for the technician to take note of during the session.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any section, don't hesitate to ask our team for guidance.
* The form should take only a few minutes to complete. If you have any concerns or questions, contact us directly.
