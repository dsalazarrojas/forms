# Brow Lamination and Tint Information and Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brow Lamination and Tint Information and Waiver Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before performing brow lamination and tint services. This includes personal details, medical history, service preferences, and informed consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, making sure to answer all questions.
2. Enter your name, date of birth, email address, and phone number in the designated fields.
3. Select your appointment date and service type from the provided options.
4. List any medical conditions you may have and select any allergies or sensitivities you may experience.
5. If you have had previous brow treatments, select the correct option and enter the date of the last treatment if applicable.
6. Indicate your skin sensitivity level and whether you have any known allergies or sensitivities.
7. Confirm your understanding of the procedure and any potential risks involved.
8. Acknowledge that you have received aftercare instructions, if any.
9. Provide informed consent to proceed with the treatment.
10. Select whether you consent to having before and after photos taken for portfolio and social media purposes.
11. If applicable, enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
12. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree with the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address for appointment confirmations and any other relevant communication.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your best contact number for scheduling and emergency purposes.
* **Appointment Date** (5, date, required): Select the date you have scheduled for your brow lamination and tint service.
* **Service Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the service you are receiving, including options for brow lamination only, tint only, lamination and tint, lamination with wax, or a full brow package.
* **Medical Conditions** (7, select_multiple, required): Select any medical conditions that may apply to you, such as pregnancy, eye infections, skin conditions, allergies, recent eye surgery, or chemotherapy treatment.
* **Known Allergies** (8, text, required): List any allergies you may have, especially to cosmetics or dyes.
* **Current Medications** (9, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking that may affect skin sensitivity.
* **Previous Brow Treatments** (10, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have had previous brow treatments, with options for yes, once, or multiple times.
* **Last Brow Treatment Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date of your last brow treatment if you have had previous treatments.
* **Skin Sensitivity** (12, select_one, required): Indicate your skin sensitivity level, with options for very sensitive, somewhat sensitive, normal, or not sensitive.
* **Procedure Understanding** (13, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the brow lamination and tint procedure.
* **Risks Acknowledged** (14, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand potential risks, including irritation or allergic reaction.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (15, select_one, required): Confirm that you have received aftercare instructions, if applicable.
* **Consent to Proceed** (16, select_one, required): Provide informed consent to proceed with the treatment.
* **Photo Consent** (17, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to having before and after photos taken for portfolio and social media purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (18, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (19, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Client Acknowledgment** (20, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree with the information provided, selecting "Yes - I Acknowledge and Agree" to continue with the treatment.

By following this guide, clients will be able to complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring a smooth and safe experience for both the client and the esthetician.
