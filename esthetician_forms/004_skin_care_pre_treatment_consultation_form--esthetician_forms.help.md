# skin_care_pre_treatment_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin care pre-treatment consultation form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their skin health and care needs prior to any skin treatment or consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2.  Answer all required fields.
3.  If a field is optional, provide information if it is relevant to your skin care needs.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, ask your esthetician or dermatologist for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your personal and contact information.
    *   Please enter your full name and any other relevant contact details.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information to facilitate communication about your skin care needs.
    *   Enter your phone number and/or email address so that we can contact you with any further questions or to schedule follow-up appointments.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, including allergies, medical conditions, or previous skin treatments.
    *   List any medical conditions that may affect your skin or skin treatments.
*   **Skin Care Goals** (`skin_care_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe your skin care goals and objectives.
    *   Tell us about your expectations for your skin care needs and any specific goals or concerns.
*   **Skin Care Products** (`skin_care_products`, `text`, required: false): List any skin care products you are currently using.
    *   Share any skin care products you use regularly, including their brand and type.
*   **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your skin type from the following options:
    *   Normal
    *   Oily
    *   Dry
    *   Combination
    *   Sensitive
    *   Rosacea
    *   Acne
    *   Other
    *   Please select the option that best describes your skin type.
*   **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any of the following skin conditions that apply to you:
    *   Acne
    *   Eczema
    *   Psoriasis
    *   Rosacea
    *   Hyper-pigmentation
    *   Other
    *   Check all that apply.
*   **Skin Care Products 2** (`skin_care_products_2`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information or notes about skin care products you are using or interested in.
    *   You can add any comments or notes here.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any allergies or sensitivities you may have:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   If you have any known allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medical conditions you may have:
    *   Yes
    *   No

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
*   This form will help your esthetician or dermatologist to create a tailored treatment plan for your skin care needs.
