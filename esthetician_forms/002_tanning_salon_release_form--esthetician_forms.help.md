# Tanning Salon Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients to confirm their tanning sessions, including their personal information, tanning details, and payment information. It helps the salon to keep track of client interactions and ensures that clients are aware of their commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and phone number.
2. Enter the date and time of your tanning session.
3. Indicate whether you received a tanning service and if you're using a tanning solution (if applicable).
4. Select your preferred tanning fee.
5. Choose your preferred payment method.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you for any further information.
* **Tanning Date**: Enter the date of your scheduled tanning session.
* **Tanning Time**: Enter the time of your scheduled tanning session.
* **Tanning Service**: Check 'Yes' if you're using a tanning service. 
* **Tanning Solution**: Check 'Yes' if you're using a tanning solution, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Tanning Solution 2**: Check 'Yes' if you're using a second tanning solution, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Tanning Fee**: Enter the total fee for your tanning session.
* **Payment Method**: Check 'Yes' if you're paying with a specific method, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment Method 2**: Check 'Yes' if you're paying with a second method, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Signed Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm your commitment.
* **Signed By**: Sign your name to confirm your commitment.
* **Salon Name**: Enter your salon name (if you have one).
* **Salon Address**: Enter your salon address (if you have one).
