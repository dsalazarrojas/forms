# Microdermabrasion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients prior to a microdermabrasion treatment procedure. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the patient's safety and well-being during the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them in the section provided.
3. If you are currently taking any medications, list them in the section provided.
4. Describe the medical procedure you are about to undergo.
5. If there are any risk factors related to your medical history or condition, list them in the section provided.
6. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.
7. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Microdermabrasion Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the form and its purpose.
* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments you have had.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies you have, including medications or substances you are allergic to.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any current medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, required): Describe the specific microdermabrasion treatment you are about to undergo.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `text`, required): List any risk factors related to your medical history or condition that may affect your treatment.
* **Signed Date** (`signed_date`, `date`, required): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `text`, required): Sign your full name to indicate your consent.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or explanations. Please sign and date the form after providing your consent.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Provide up-to-date information about your medical history, allergies, and medications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* Keep a copy of the signed consent form for your records.
