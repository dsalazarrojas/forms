# Dermal Filler Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients before undergoing dermal filler treatments. It aims to ensure that clients are aware of the treatment procedure, potential risks and benefits, and provide their informed consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and completely.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your medical provider for clarification.
3. Sign and date the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History**: Briefly describe any medical conditions, allergies, or surgeries you have had in the past.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies you have, including Sulfonamide, Polysulfate, Latex, Penicillin, or Other (please specify).
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Informed Consent**: Please read and sign this section to indicate that you have understood the treatment procedure, potential risks and benefits, and provide your consent for treatment.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name here to confirm that you have read and understood the above information.
* **Medical Provider**: Please select the name of your medical provider.
* **Procedure**: Select the type of dermal filler treatment you are seeking.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign your name here to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
* **Client Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed this form.

## Tips

* Please review this form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any field.
* If you need to make any changes, please ask your medical provider for approval.
* This form is a reference point for future treatments. Please keep it with your medical provider for future visits.
