# Hair Toner Mix Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their hair toner mix application experience. It is intended to be completed by salon staff to collect details about the client's hair and skin characteristics prior to treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your salon details in the **Salon Details** field.
2. Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether you have used a toner mix in the past.
3. Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether you are scheduled for an appointment for a hair toner mix application.
4. Provide your written consent in the **Client Consent** field.
5. Enter any relevant details about your stylist or technician who will be performing the treatment in the **Stylist Details** field.
6. Enter any relevant details about your pre-service experience or conditions in the **Pre-Service Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Details**: 
  * **Salon Details (Page 1 of 6)**
  * Enter your name and contact information to identify your salon.
  * This field is optional, but it helps us to contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Toner Details**: 
  * **Toner Details (Page 2 of 6)**
  * Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether you have used a toner mix in the past.
  * This field is used to determine whether you have a prior experience with hair toner mixes.
* **Appointment Details**: 
  * **Appointment Details (Page 3 of 6)**
  * Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether you are scheduled for an appointment for a hair toner mix application.
  * This field is used to determine your schedule and availability.
* **Client Consent**: 
  * **Client Consent (Page 4 of 6)**
  * This is a text field where you can provide your written consent for the treatment.
  * Please type your consent here.
* **Stylist Details**: 
  * **Stylist Details (Page 5 of 6)**
  * Enter the name and contact information of your stylist or technician who will be performing the treatment.
  * This field helps us to identify who is providing the service.
* **Pre-Service Details**: 
  * **Pre-Service Details (Page 6 of 6)**
  * Enter any relevant details about your pre-service experience or conditions.
  * This field is used to gather information about your skin and hair condition prior to treatment.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your stylist or technician for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to ask.
