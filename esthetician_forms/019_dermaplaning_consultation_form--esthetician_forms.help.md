# Dermaplaning Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dermaplaning Consultation Form is designed to collect information about patients for dermaplaning consultations. This form will help gather all relevant details about the patient to provide the best service possible.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Personal Details
- **First Name** (Text): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Text): Please enter your last name.
### Step 2: Contact Information
- **Email** (Email): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Step 3: Medical Details
- **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Medical Practitioner** (Text): Please enter the name of your medical practitioner.
### Step 4: Skin Condition
- **Skin Condition** (Select): Select your skin condition from the given options.
### Step 5: Hair Type
- **Hair Type** (Select Multiple): Select all hair types that apply to you.
### Step 6: Treatment Plan
- **Treatment Plan** (Text): Please enter your treatment plan. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 7: Medical History
- **Medical History** (Text): Please enter your medical history. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 8: Allergies
- **Allergies** (Text): Please list any allergies you may have. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 9: Medical Notes
- **Medical Notes** (Text): Please enter any medical notes. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 10: Medical Procedure
- **Medical Procedure** (Text): Please enter the medical procedure. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 11: Expected Outcome
- **Expected Outcome** (Text): Please enter your expected outcome. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 12: Follow Up
- **Follow Up** (Select): If you want to schedule a follow-up, select 'Yes'. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 13: Follow Up Notes
- **Follow Up Notes** (Text): Please enter your follow-up notes. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 14: Follow Up Date
- **Follow Up Date** (Date): Enter the follow-up date in the format YYYY-MM-DD. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 15: Follow Up Time
- **Follow Up Time** (Time): Enter the follow-up time in the format HH:MM. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 16: Follow Up Location
- **Follow Up Location** (Text): Please enter the follow-up location. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 17: Follow Up Contact
- **Follow Up Contact** (Text): Please enter your contact number for follow-up. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 18: Follow Up Notes 2
- **Follow Up Notes 2** (Text): Please enter your follow-up notes 2. (Note: This field is optional.)
### Step 19: Additional Notes
- **Additional Notes** (Text): Please enter any additional notes. (Note: This field is optional.)

Note: The fields marked as 'Note' are text fields with optional status but may have different lengths. The select_one and select_multiple fields are for selecting a condition or multiple conditions from the given options.
