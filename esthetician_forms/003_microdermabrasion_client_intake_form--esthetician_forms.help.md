# Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form is a document used by medical professionals to collect vital information about clients prior to a microdermabrasion treatment. This form aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the client's medical history, skin status, and any relevant medical clearances or contraindications.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, start by providing your client's information in the "Client Information" section.
2.  Please provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3.  Describe your current skin status in the "Skin Status" section.
4.  If you have any relevant information about the microdermabrasion treatment, provide it in the "Microdermabrasion Information" section.
5.  If you have medical clearances or contraindications that may impact the treatment, list them in the "Medical Clearance" section.
6.  Review and sign the "Client Agreement" section.
7.  Provide any emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
8.  Add any additional medical history information in the "Medical History Additional Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This section requires your name, contact information, and any other personal details relevant to the treatment.
* **Medical History**: Please provide a detailed account of any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may impact the microdermabrasion procedure.
* **Skin Status**: Describe your current skin condition, including any areas of concern or specific issues you're experiencing.
* **Microdermabrasion Information**: If you have any questions or concerns about the microdermabrasion procedure, please provide them here.
* **Medical Clearance**: If you have any medical clearances or contraindications that may impact the treatment, list them here.
* **Client Agreement**: Review and sign this section to confirm you understand the terms and conditions of the treatment.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Medical History Additional Info**: Add any additional information about your medical history that may be relevant to the treatment.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough when completing this form to ensure the best possible results from the microdermabrasion treatment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your medical professional for clarification.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for the microdermabrasion treatment, so please complete it carefully and thoroughly.
