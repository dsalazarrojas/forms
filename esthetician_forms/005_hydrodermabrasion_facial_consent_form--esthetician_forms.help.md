## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the client's skin and medical history, treatment acknowledgment, and other relevant details for a hydrodermabrasion facial treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter a brief description of your skin and medical history in the "Client History" page.
2. Acknowledge the treatment by selecting one of the options in the "Treatment Acknowledgment" page.
3. Please sign off on the consent form in the "Signature" page.
4. If necessary, provide additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" page.
5. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" page.
6. Enter your name in the "Client Name" page.
7. Enter your name in the "Esthetician Name" page.
8. Select the date and time of the treatment in the "Date" and "Time" pages.
9. Enter the location of the treatment in the "Location" page.
