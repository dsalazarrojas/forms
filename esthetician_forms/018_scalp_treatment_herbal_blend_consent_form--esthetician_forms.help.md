# Scalp Treatment Herbal Blend Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scalp Treatment Herbal Blend Consent Form is used to gather information about a client's history, allergies, and medical conditions, as well as obtain their consent to perform a scalp treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to the instructions provided.
2. Fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client History (1)**: Please provide a brief description of your past scalp conditions or medical conditions that may affect your treatment. This will help the esthetician administering the treatment understand your needs and provide the best service.
* **Allergies (2)**: Check the box if you have any allergies or sensitivities that may affect your treatment. This includes any allergies or sensitivities to plants, chemicals, or other substances.
* **Signature (3)**: This field is for your digital signature, confirming that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Please provide your date of birth for identification and medical purposes.
* **Contact Info (5)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number, so that we can reach you if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Info (6)**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number.
* **Consent (7)**: Please choose whether you give your consent for the scalp treatment. If you choose not to consent, we will not perform the treatment.
* **Consent Date (8)**: This field is for recording the date of your consent, in case we need to verify your agreement at a later time.
* **Client ID (9)**: This field is for your client ID number, which is used for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Be truthful and accurate when answering the questions on this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, ask your esthetician or spa professional for clarification.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, leave them blank and ask your esthetician or spa professional for guidance.

Note: This guide is not intended to be an exhaustive explanation of the form, but rather a summary of the most important information. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your esthetician or spa professional.
