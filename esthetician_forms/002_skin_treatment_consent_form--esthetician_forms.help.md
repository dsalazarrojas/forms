# Skin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients before administering skin treatments. It is essential to complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that clients understand their rights and responsibilities and that you, as the provider, are aware of any allergies, medical conditions, or other relevant information that may impact treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the **Provider Name**, **Client Address**, **Client Phone**, and **Client Email** fields.
3. Select the date of the service from the calendar in the **Date of Service** field.
4. Check the box next to **Signed Consent** to indicate that the client has read and understood the form and given their consent for treatment.
5. Sign your name in the **Client Signature** field to indicate your acceptance of the form.
6. Date and sign the form in the **Date Signed** and **Provider Signature** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Your full name as the client.
* **Provider Name**: Your name as the service provider.
* **Date of Service**: The date on which the service was provided or is scheduled to be provided.
* **Signed Consent**: Check this box if you, the client, have read and understood this form and agree to the services outlined. 
* **Client Signature**: Sign this field to indicate your acceptance of the form.
* **Date Signed**: The date and time on which you signed this form.
* **Provider Signature**: The date and time on which this form was accepted by the service provider.
* **Client Address**: Your address, which may be required for communication and follow-up care.
* **Provider Address**: The address of the service provider.
* **Client Phone**: Your phone number, which may be required for communication and follow-up care.
* **Client Email**: Your email address, which may be required for communication and follow-up care.
* **Agreed To Terms**: Check this box to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms of service.

Note: The fields marked as required are essential to complete the form accurately. If a field is not required, it is optional and may be omitted if not applicable.
