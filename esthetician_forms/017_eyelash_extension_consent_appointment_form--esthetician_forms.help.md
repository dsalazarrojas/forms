# eyelash_extension_consent_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for a lash extension appointment. The form is designed to gather essential information from users, including their name, email, appointment date and time, and a statement of consent. It also allows users to provide additional notes and a signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the date of your desired appointment in the "Date" field.
4. Select the time of your desired appointment in the "Time" field.
5. Write a statement of consent in the "Consent" field.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can write them in the "Note" field (optional).
7. Sign the form by writing your signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for appointment reminders and updates.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Select the time of your desired appointment in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Consent**: Write a statement of consent for your lash extension appointment, indicating your understanding of the services and any necessary information.
* **Note**: If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them here (optional).
* **Signature**: Sign the form by writing your signature in the provided space. This is a required field, but it will not be checked by a human.
