# Skincare Product Layering Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team collect information from customers about their skincare layering preferences. The goal is to understand how our customers typically layer different products to achieve their desired skincare results. This will help us to develop more effective product recommendations and improve customer experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

- Start by filling out the form on each page. Each page has one or more questions that correspond to different aspects of skincare layering.
- Select your preferred options for each question from the provided list. Some questions may allow you to select multiple options.
- Enter your responses in the designated text fields for questions that require it.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that you have provided accurate information.
- Submit the form once you have completed all questions.
