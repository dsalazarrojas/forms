# Esthetics Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esthetics Client Consultation Form is a document designed to collect information from clients about their skincare needs and medical history. This form helps the esthetician understand the client's specific requirements and provide personalized consultation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure the esthetician has a comprehensive understanding of your needs.
3. Review the form for errors before submitting it to the esthetician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`main_information`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction or context about your visit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and phone number so we can contact you for follow-up appointments or to provide further guidance.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your skin or skincare treatments.
* **Skincare Needs** (`skincare_needs`, text, optional): Describe your current skin concerns or needs, such as acne, aging, or sensitivity.
* **Skincare Preferences** (`skincare_preferences`, text, optional): Share any specific skincare preferences or dislikes you may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have, particularly those related to skincare products or treatments.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): If you take any medications, please list them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your skin or skincare treatments, please list them here.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, text, optional): Describe your skin type (e.g., oily, dry, combination, sensitive).
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate to help us tailor your treatment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up appointments or guidance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your treatment.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address so we can mail you any necessary items or follow-up instructions.
* **Medical History Note** (`medical_history_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your medical history.

Note: The fields marked as `optional` are not required but are recommended for providing the esthetician with a complete understanding of your needs and history.
