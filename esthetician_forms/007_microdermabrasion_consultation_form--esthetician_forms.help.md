# Microdermabrasion Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Consultation Form is a tool for gathering information from potential clients or patients before conducting a microdermabrasion treatment. This form helps the practitioner assess the client's medical history, skin type, and other relevant details to decide whether the treatment is suitable for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name and contact information (email and phone number) in the top section.
2.  Answer the questions and provide information as prompted, making sure to fill out all required fields.
3.  Check the checkboxes and select the options as required for each field.
4.  If you have any medical history or procedures, please list them in the "Medical History" field.
5.  If you have any allergies or medications, please list them in the "Allergies" and "Medications" fields respectively.
6.  Please provide a list of any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
7.  Indicate your microdermabrasion treatment area and frequency in the respective fields.
8.  Select your skin type and indicate whether you have any certifications for microdermabrasion practice or esthetician certification.
9.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
10.  Fill in your emergency contact information (relation, phone, and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical History** (Text): If you have any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies, please provide a brief description in this field.
* **Medical Conditions** (Text): List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Allergies** (Text): List any allergies you have, such as latex or penicillin.
* **Medications** (Text): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medications List** (Text): This field is for listing any other medications you have taken in the past.
* **Microdermabrasion Treatment Area** (Text): Indicate the area(s) of your skin that require microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Microdermabrasion Treatment Frequency** (Select One):
	+ Daily
	+ Every Other Day
	+ Every Two Weeks
	+ Every Three Weeks
	+ Every Four Weeks
* **Skin Type** (Text): Describe your skin type (e.g., dry, oily, combination, sensitive).
* **Microdermabrasion Practice Certification** (Select One):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Eesthetician Certification** (Text): If you have any esthetician certification, please provide details.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Medical Insurance** (Text): If you have medical insurance, please provide the name of your provider.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Text): Describe your relation to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History Notes** (Note): Provide any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Medical History List** (Select Multiple):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Microdermabrasion Practice Consent** (Select One):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Microdermabrasion Practice Consent Date** (Date): If you select "True" for microdermabrasion practice consent, enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Follow-up Visit** (Text): Enter any follow-up visit information.
