# Waxing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Waiver Form is a client-facing document used to obtain consent and signature for waxing services. It is essential for both legal and administrative purposes, ensuring that both the client and our team are aware of the terms and conditions of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand what is required.
2. Select the correct option for "Client Signature" and "Signature Request" fields according to your needs.
3. Fill out your name and phone number in the "Client Name" and "Phone Number" fields.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Consent**: This is a note field for clients to provide their consent for waxing services.
	+ Please type your consent in this field.
* **Signature Request**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how you would like to provide your signature:
	+ Client Signature: Provide your signature on this form.
	+ Waiver: Sign a waiver, which releases our company from liability.
	+ Both: Sign both this form and a waiver.
* **Client Name**: This is a text field for your name to be recorded in our system.
	+ Please type your name as it appears on your government ID or driver's license.
* **Phone Number**: This is a text field for your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: This is an email field for your contact information.
	+ Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
