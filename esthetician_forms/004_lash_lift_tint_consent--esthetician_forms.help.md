# Lash Lift & Tint Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Lift & Tint Consent form is a simple consent form that clients are required to fill out before receiving Lash Lift and Tint services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Full Name** in the field labeled "Full Name". Please make sure to enter your real name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This will be used for communication purposes only and will not be shared with any other entities.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** in the field labeled "Contact Number". This is the number we can use to contact you for any questions or scheduling issues.
4. Select your **Date of Birth** from the date picker.
5. Choose your **Lash Technician** from the dropdown menu. Please select the technician who will be performing your service.
6. Select the **Service Type** you would like to receive, from the dropdown menu. You can choose from Lash Lift, Tint, or Both.
7. Select the **Service Location** from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your real name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes only and will not be shared with any other entities.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number. This is the number we can use to contact you for any questions or scheduling issues.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth from the date picker.
* **Lash Technician**: Choose the technician who will be performing your service.
* **Service Type**: Select the service you would like to receive, from Lash Lift, Tint, or Both.
* **Service Location**: Select the location where you would like to receive your service.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask the staff for help.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your salon/esthetician contact information].
