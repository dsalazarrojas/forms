# Cosmetology Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from cosmetology clients to facilitate a consultation session. It helps the cosmetologist understand the client's hair type, personal details, and service preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by a (*) symbol.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your hair type? (Page 1)
* **What is your hair type?** (`<text>`, required/optional): This field is used to determine your hair type, which will help the cosmetologist understand your hair's texture, porosity, and other characteristics. Please answer this question based on your natural hair type.

### Client Information (Page 2)
* **Client Information** (`<text>`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and any relevant medical conditions. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth consultation process.

### Services (Page 3)
* **Services** (`<text>`, required/optional): This field is used to determine which services you are interested in or require for your consultation. Please select all services that apply to your needs.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform the cosmetologist before the consultation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the cosmetologist during the consultation.
