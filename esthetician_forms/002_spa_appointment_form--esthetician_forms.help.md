# Spa Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Appointment Form is designed to collect information from clients to book appointments at a spa. This form gathers details such as name, contact information, available dates, and services requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name**.
2. Enter your **Last Name**.
3. Provide your **Email** (if applicable).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable).
5. Select one or more **Available Dates** that work best for you.
6. Choose a **Time** that suits you best.
7. Select the **Services** you would like to book.
8. Provide any additional **Notes** about your appointment.
9. Choose how you would like to submit the form, either **Self** or **Agent**.
10. Enter the **Spa Name** (if applicable).
11. Select the **Appointment Type**, either **Booking** or **Cancellation**.
12. Choose the status of your appointment, either **New Booking**, **Confirmed**, or **Pending**.
13. Enter your **Appointment ID** and **Client ID** (if applicable).
14. Enter your **Employee ID** and **Employee Name** (if applicable).
15. Enter your **Appointment Time** and **Appointment Date** (if applicable).
16. Enter your **Appointment Notes** (if applicable).
17. Enter your **Client Email** and **Client Phone** (if applicable).
18. Enter your **Employee Salary** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Available Dates**: Select one or more dates that you are available for the appointment.
* **Available Time**: Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Services**: Select the services you would like to book.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your appointment.
* **Submitted By**: Choose how you would like to submit the form, either **Self** or **Agent**.
* **Spa Name**: Enter the name of the spa if you have one.
* **Form Submitted**: This field is automatically filled in upon form submission.
* **Confirm Appointment**: Confirm if you have an existing appointment.
* **Appointment Type**: Choose the type of appointment, either **Booking** or **Cancellation**.
* **Appointment Status**: Choose the status of your appointment, either **New Booking**, **Confirmed**, or **Pending**.
* **Appointment ID**: Enter your appointment ID (if applicable).
* **Client ID**: Enter your client ID (if applicable).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name (if applicable).
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Choose the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Notes**: Provide any notes about your appointment.
* **Client Email**: Enter your client email (if applicable).
* **Client Phone**: Enter your client phone (if applicable).
* **Employee Salary**: Enter your employee salary (if applicable).

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and easily accessible. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and follow any relevant company policies before proceeding.
