# covid_19_waxing_boutique_waiver_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended to gather information from a medical facility for COVID-19 waxing services. The form is required for all medical facilities to provide information about their medical conditions, allergies, and facility details in order to process a waxing service request at the boutique.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. For each page, select the appropriate option or enter the required information.
3. Once you have completed all the pages, review the form for accuracy and completeness.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Conditions** (`High Blood Pressure`, `Diabetes`, `Allergic Reaction`, `None`): Select the medical condition(s) that apply to you or your staff. This field is required.
* **Allergies** (text): Enter any allergies that you or your staff have. This field is required.
* **Doctor's Name** (text): Enter the name of the doctor or medical professional that will be performing the waxing service. This field is required.
* **Medical Facility Address** (text): Enter the physical address of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter the phone number of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **License** (text): Enter the license number of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **State License** (text): Enter the state license number of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **City** (text): Enter the city of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Zip Code** (text): Enter the zip code of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Country** (text): Enter the country of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **State** (text): Enter the state of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Federal License** (text): Enter the federal license number of the medical facility. This field is required.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This field is optional, please use if necessary. Date and time of signature can be any valid timestamp.
* **Website** (text): Enter the website of the medical facility. This field is required.

Note: The medical facility must be a licensed and accredited facility to be eligible for COVID-19 waxing services. Please ensure that you have all the necessary documents and certifications to support your information.
