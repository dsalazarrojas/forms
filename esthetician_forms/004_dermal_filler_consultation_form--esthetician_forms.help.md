# Dermal Filler Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from patients about their medical history, skin conditions, and other relevant health factors to ensure that the consultant has all necessary information to provide informed and personalized advice during the dermal filler consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Please fill out each field carefully and accurately.**
- **Answer all questions truthfully, and do not leave any fields blank unless it's specifically indicated that it's not required.**
- **Make sure to ask a doctor or healthcare professional for guidance if you're unsure about any part of the form.**
