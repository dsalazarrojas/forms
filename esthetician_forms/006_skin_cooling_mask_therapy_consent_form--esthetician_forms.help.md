# skin_cooling_mask_therapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin cooling mask therapy consent form is designed to collect informed consent from clients who are undergoing skin cooling mask therapy. This form ensures that clients understand the risks and benefits of the treatment and provide consent prior to receiving the therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the questions and instructions.
2. Fill in your health details, including any medical conditions you may have.
3. List any allergies you may have.
4. Indicate your consent to receive skin cooling mask therapy.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
6. Date and time the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header** (1, text, not required): This is the header for the form.
- **Client Health Details** (2, text, not required): Please fill in any health details you may have, such as medical conditions or allergies.
- **Client Medical Conditions** (3, text, not required): List any medical conditions you have that may affect your eligibility for skin cooling mask therapy.
- **Allergies** (4, text, not required): List any allergies you may have that may affect your ability to undergo skin cooling mask therapy.
- **Consent To Treatment** (5, select_multiple, not required): Indicate your consent to receive skin cooling mask therapy. Select 'Yes' if you are willing to receive the treatment.
- **Signature** (6, text, not required): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
- **Signature Date** (7, date, not required): Date the signature to confirm the consent date.
- **Signature Time** (8, time, not required): Time the signature to confirm the consent time.
- **Client Signature Image** (9, text, not required): You may be required to upload a signature image by your healthcare provider. Please follow their instructions for the image requirements.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any question or instruction, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Ensure that you have read and understood the form before signing it.
