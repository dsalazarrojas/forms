# Hyaluron Pen Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from clients regarding their consent to receive hyaluronic pen treatment. It is essential to have this information on file to ensure informed decision-making and medical safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
5. If you have any medical history or conditions, enter it in the "Medical History" field.
6. Select your choice of consent for hyaluronic pen treatment in the "Treatment Consent" field.
7. If you have any known allergic reactions or medical conditions, list them in the "Allergic Reactions" field.
8. If you are currently taking any medications, list them in the "Medication Use" field.
9. Provide your consent for the medical procedure in the "Medical Procedure Consent" field.
10. Select how you made your decision regarding treatment in the "Informed Decision" field.
11. Sign and date the form using the "Client Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Medical History** (optional): List any medical conditions or surgeries you have had.
* **Treatment Consent** (required): Select whether you consent to receive hyaluronic pen treatment.
* **Allergic Reactions** (optional): List any known allergic reactions or medical conditions.
* **Medication Use** (optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedure Consent** (required): Provide your consent for the medical procedure.
* **Informed Decision** (required): Select how you made your decision regarding treatment.
* **Client Signature** (required): Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields before proceeding with the form.
* Be honest and thorough in answering your medical history and medication use.
* Double-check the "Treatment Consent" and "Informed Decision" fields to ensure you understand the implications of treatment.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
