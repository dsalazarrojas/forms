# Eyelash Extension Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyelash Extension Consent Form is designed to inform clients about the eyelash extension procedure, its potential risks, and the equipment used. This form is intended to ensure that clients understand the procedure and give their consent before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields: **Eyelash Extension Consent Form**, **The eyelash extension procedure involves**, **What equipment will be used during the procedure?**, **Potential Risks**, and **The benefits of eyelash extensions include**. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Please sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Eyelash Extension Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which should be left unchanged.
* **The eyelash extension procedure involves**: Describe the steps and details of the eyelash extension procedure. This field should be filled in concisely but accurately.
* **What equipment will be used during the procedure?**: List the equipment that will be used during the eyelash extension procedure. This includes any necessary tools or machinery.
* **Potential Risks**: Describe the potential risks associated with the eyelash extension procedure. Be honest and detailed in your answer.
* **The benefits of eyelash extensions include**: Describe the advantages of the eyelash extension procedure. Explain what clients can expect to gain or achieve from this treatment.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your consent for the procedure. This is a required field and must be completed. 

## Tips
- Please fill in the form carefully and truthfully.
- Make sure to read and understand all the information provided before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your technician/esthetician for clarification.
- This form is for your protection and safety, and filling it out carefully will ensure a smooth and safe procedure.
