# dermal_filler_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for dermal filler treatments. It is used by healthcare professionals to understand the client's needs and medical history before proceeding with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Select the dermal filler procedure and treatment areas.
3. Provide any relevant medical history, current medications, and allergies.
4. Indicate the type of anesthesia and facial treatment required.
5. Confirm your doctor and nurse information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and appointment reminders.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Select the time of your appointment.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information relevant to your treatment.
* **Dermal Filler Procedure**: Choose the type of dermal filler treatment you require, such as DermaFirma, Restylu, or Xeomin.
* **Treatment Areas**: Select the areas of your body where the treatment is required, such as Face, Neck, Chin, etc.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history that may impact your treatment.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, please specify.
* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone Number**: Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Dermal Filler Doctor**: Enter the name of your dermal filler doctor.
* **Dermal Filler Nurse**: Enter the name of your dermal filler nurse.
* **Dermal Filler Technician**: Enter the name of your dermal filler technician.
* **Anesthesia**: Choose the type of anesthesia required, such as None, Lidocaine, or Xylocaine.
* **Facial Treatment**: Select the type of facial treatment required, such as None, Xylocaine, or Loxilene.
* **Botox Treatment**: Select the type of Botox treatment required, such as None, Xylocaine, or Loxilene.
* **Chemical Peel**: Select the type of chemical peel required, such as None, Xylocaine, or Loxilene.
* **Hydroliplic Treatment**: Select the type of hydroliplic treatment required, such as None, Xylocaine, or Loxilene.
* **Radio Frequency Treatment**: Select the type of radio frequency treatment required, such as None, Xylocaine, or Loxilene.
* **Other**: Enter any other relevant information about your treatment.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling in your medical history and medications.
- Ensure you enter accurate contact information for your doctor and nurse.
- Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
