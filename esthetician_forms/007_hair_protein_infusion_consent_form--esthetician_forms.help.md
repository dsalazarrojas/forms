# Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and their consent for hair protein infusion procedures. It's crucial to fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure your treatment plan is tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your client details, including any health information you feel comfortable sharing.
* Select the type of client you are (New, Returning, or Referring).
* Choose the type of treatment you'd like to receive (Hair, Scalp, or both).
* Select the type of protein you'd like to use for your treatment (Plant-based, Animal-based, or Vegan).
* Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with your stylist.
* Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant details.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Health Information**: Share any health-related information that might affect your treatment.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Signed Consent**: Sign this form to confirm you've read and understood the treatment plan.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Select Client Type**: Choose how you know our salon (New, Returning, or Referring).
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Select Treatment**: Select the type of treatment you'd like to receive (Hair, Scalp, or both).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: no
* **Select Protein Type**: Choose the type of protein you'd like to use (Plant-based, Animal-based, or Vegan).
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: no
* **Note**: Share any additional comments or questions you have about your treatment.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: no
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
