# Brow Styling Gel Trial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from participants in a Brow Styling Gel trial, ensuring they are aware of the potential risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's full name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address for trial communication in the "Contact email" field.
3. If the participant has any known skin product allergies, list them in the "Known allergies" field.
4. Confirm willingness to participate in the trial by selecting "I consent" in the "Trial consent" field.
5. If applicable, note any potential reactions or side effects of the Brow Styling Gel in the "Expected side effects" field.
6. Provide availability for post-trial follow up assessments in the "Post trial follow up" field.
7. Confirm permission for the trial to take photographs by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Photo permission" field.
8. Enter the email of the clinician performing the trial in the "Clinician signature email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address for trial communication.
* **Known allergies** (`known_allergies`, text, optional): List any skin product allergies the participant has.
* **Trial consent** (`trial_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm willingness to participate in the trial by selecting "I consent".
* **Expected side effects** (`expected_side_effects`, note, optional): If applicable, note any potential reactions or side effects of the Brow Styling Gel.
* **Post trial follow up** (`post_trial_follow_up`, text, optional): Provide availability for post-trial follow up assessments.
* **Photo permission** (`photo_permission`, select_one, optional): Confirm permission for the trial to take photographs by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Clinician signature email** (`clinician_signature_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the clinician performing the trial.
