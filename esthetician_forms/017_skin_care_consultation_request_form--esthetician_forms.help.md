# Skin Care Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skin Care Consultation Request Form is a form designed to gather essential information from clients about their skin care needs. This information will be used by estheticians to create a personalized consultation plan for each client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field. Make sure to enter it correctly to ensure that you receive important information and updates from us.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field, which will be used to contact you for further communication.
5. Select your birthdate in the Birthdate field.
6. Select any applicable Medical Conditions that affect your skin in the Medical Conditions field. You can choose multiple conditions if necessary.
7. Choose your Skin Type from the options provided in the Skin Type field. This will help us understand your skin better.
8. If you prefer to have a Virtual Consultation, In-Office Visit, or Phone Call, select your Preferred Method in the Preferred Method field.
9. If you have any additional notes about your skin care needs, enter them in the Note field. This is an optional field.
10. If you have medical insurance, select the Medical Insurance field to indicate your status.
11. If you'd like to schedule an appointment, enter your desired Appointment Date and Appointment Time in the respective fields. These are optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name for your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name for your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you for further communication.
* **Birthdate** (`Birthdate`, Date, Required): Select your birthdate to help us understand your skin type and needs better.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, Select One, Required): Choose any medical conditions that affect your skin from the options provided. If you select "Other", please describe the condition in the Note field.
* **Skin Type** (`Skin Type`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your skin type from the options provided to help us understand your skin better.
* **Preferred Method** (`Preferred Method`, Select One, Optional): Select how you would like to have your consultation, such as Virtual Consultation, In-Office Visit, or Phone Call.
* **Note** (`Note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your skin care needs.
* **Medical Insurance** (`Medical Insurance`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your medical insurance status.
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, Date, Optional): Enter your desired appointment date if you have chosen to schedule an appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, Time, Optional): Enter your desired appointment time if you have chosen to schedule an appointment.
