# Microdermabrasion Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the details of microdermabrasion treatments provided to clients. It helps in documenting the treatment process and tracking client information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Microdermabrasion Treatment Page section, which will guide you through the treatment details.
2. Proceed to the Client Info Page section to enter client contact information.
3. You may add any additional notes on the Notes Page section if required.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Microdermabrasion Treatment Page
* **Microdermabrasion Page** (`microdermabrasion_page`, text, required: false): This page is dedicated to recording the microdermabrasion treatment details. Enter your notes and observations in this section.

### 2. Client Info Page
* **Client Info Page** (`client_info_page`, text, required: false): Enter client contact information, such as their name, contact number, or email address, if available.

### 3. Treatment Page
* **Treatment Page** (`treatment_page`, text, required: false): This page is for recording any other treatment details not covered in the Microdermabrasion Page section. Enter any relevant notes or observations.

### 4. Notes Page
* **Notes Page** (`notes_page`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the treatment or client, if required.
