# Skincare Business Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential business collaborators for our skincare business. We use this information to evaluate potential collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name 
2. Enter your Last Name 
3. Enter your Business Name 
4. Enter your Business Website 
5. Choose your Social Media handles (you can choose one or more)
6. Enter the services your business offers 
7. Enter the number of years your business has been in operation 
8. Enter your Annual Revenue 
9. Provide a brief description of your business 
10. Choose why you want to collaborate with us (you can choose one reason) 
11. Enter your Email 
12. Enter your Phone Number 
13. Enter your Business Address 
14. Enter your Zip Code 
15. Enter your State 
16. Enter your City 
17. Enter your Country 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here. This will be used to identify you. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here. This will be used to identify you. 
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your business here. This will help us understand your business identity. 
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of your business website here. This will help us understand your online presence. 
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the following social media handles: Twitter, Instagram, TikTok. This will help us understand how you promote your business. 
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required: false): Enter the services you offer here. This will help us understand what your business is about. 
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation here. This will help us understand your business experience. 
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required: false): Enter your business's annual revenue here. This will help us understand your business size. 
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your business here. This will help us understand what your business is about. 
* **Why Collaborate with us** (`why_collaborate_with_us`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following reasons why you want to collaborate with us: We are passionate about skincare, We are experts in skincare, We are looking for new opportunities. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your business email here. This will be used to contact you. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your business phone number here. This will be used to contact you. 
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address here. This will help us understand where your business is located. 
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required: false): Enter your business zip code here. This will help us understand where your business is located. 
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter your state here. This will help us understand where your business is located. 
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your city here. This will help us understand where your business is located. 
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter your country here. This will help us understand where your business is located. 
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, required: false): Enter your time zone here. This will help us understand your business time zone. 
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following languages: English, Spanish, Chinese. 
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, time, required: false): Enter your timezone offset here. This will help us understand your time zone. 
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, time, required: false): Enter your time zone here. This will help us understand your business time zone. 
* **Timezone DST** (`timezone_dst`, time, required: false): Enter your timezone DST here. This will help us understand your time zone. 
* **Timezone Tz Offset** (`timezone_tz_offset`, number, required: false): Enter your timezone Tz offset here. This will help us understand your time zone. 
* **Timezone Tz** (`timezone_tz`, number, required: false): Enter your timezone Tz here. This will help us understand your time zone.
