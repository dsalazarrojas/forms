# Brow Lamination Course Registration - Help Guide
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (Student name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Provide your email address for course communications.
* **Certification needed** (Certification needed, select_one, required): Indicate if you require certification upon completion of the course. Choose 'True' if you need certification, 'False' if you do not.
* **Course date** (Course date, date, required): Choose a date for the course that works for you.
* **Prior experience** (Prior experience, number, optional): If you have any prior experience in brow services, enter the number of years you have been working in the field.
* **Payment method** (Payment method, select_one, required): Select how you plan to pay for the course: credit card, bank transfer, or invoice.
* **Dietary requirements** (Dietary requirements, text, optional): If you have any dietary requirements for in-person training, enter your requirements in the space provided.
* **Emergency contact** (Emergency contact, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your correct email address for course communications.
* Ensure that your course date is realistic and feasible.
* If you require certification, be prepared to demonstrate your experience and qualifications.
* If you have any dietary requirements, please let us know so we can accommodate your needs.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date emergency contact information.
