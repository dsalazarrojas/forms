Ultrasound Cavitation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ultrasound Cavitation Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing an ultrasound cavitation treatment. It is used to ensure that the patient is aware of the treatment procedure, risks, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options from the multiple-choice fields where applicable.
2. Enter any additional text information in the text fields as required.
3. Sign the form, which is a required step to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`ultrasound_cavitation_consent_form`, Select multiple, required/optional): This field is used to confirm your agreement to undergo the ultrasound cavitation treatment. Select 'Yes' if you agree to the treatment, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Ultrasound Device Information** (`ultrasound_device_information`, Note, required/optional): This field is a note for any additional information about the ultrasound device used in the treatment.
* **Treatment Description** (`treatment_description`, Text, required/optional): This field should contain a brief description of the treatment you are giving consent for.
* **Treatment Procedure** (`treatment_procedure`, Text, required/optional): This field should describe the step-by-step process of the treatment you are giving consent for.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Select one, required/optional): This field is used to specify the specific treatment you are giving consent for (Ultrasound Cavitation or Other treatment).
* **Signature** (`signature`, Date, required/optional): This field is for your signature, which is a required step to confirm your consent.
* **Signature 2** (`signature2`, Time, required/optional): This field is for the date and time you signed the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, required/optional): This field should contain your date of birth, which is not relevant for this form as it does not affect the consent process.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, Select multiple, required/optional): This field is used to provide your contact information (Email, Phone, Address) for communication purposes.
