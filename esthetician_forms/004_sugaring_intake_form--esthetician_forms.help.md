# Sugaring Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before undergoing sugaring treatments. This form helps identify potential medical concerns and ensures that clients are suitable candidates for the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you answer all questions, including optional ones.
3. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please describe them in the 'Medical Conditions' field.
4. Select the correct options for 'Allergies' and 'Medical Conditions' to indicate any relevant issues.
5. For 'Hair Removal Frequency' and 'Hair Removal Area', select the options that best describe your experience.
6. Choose the 'Desired Outcome' that best aligns with your goals for the treatment.
7. Indicate whether you have any 'Medical Concerns' that may impact the treatment.
8. Select the 'Pain Level' you expect to experience during the treatment.
9. Choose the number of 'Sessions' you think you may need.
10. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to 'Notes' based on whether you have any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number**: A valid phone number where we can contact you for follow-up or to schedule appointments.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can send you appointment reminders and updates.
* **Medical History**: Please describe any relevant medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Allergies**: Select 'Yes' if you have any allergies or sensitivities that may impact the treatment. If you do not have any allergies, select 'No'.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions that may impact the treatment. If you do not have any medical conditions, select 'No'.
* **Hair Removal Frequency**: Select the frequency of your current hair removal practices:
	+ **Never**: I never remove my body hair.
	+ **Rarely**: I remove my body hair occasionally.
	+ **Occasionally**: I remove my body hair from time to time.
	+ **Almost Every Day**: I remove my body hair frequently.
* **Hair Removal Area**: Select the areas of your body where you remove hair:
	+ **Full Body**: I remove hair from my entire body.
	+ **Face**: I remove hair from my face.
	+ **Back/Neckline**: I remove hair from my back and neckline.
	+ **Eyebrow**: I remove hair from my eyebrows.
	+ **Bikini Line**: I remove hair from my bikini line.
	+ **Lip/Chin**: I remove hair from my lip and chin.
	+ **Eyelashes**: I remove hair from my eyelashes.
	+ **Full Body/Upper Lip**: I remove hair from my full body and upper lip.
* **Technician**: This is the name and contact information of the technician assigned to your treatment.
* **Appointment Date**: The date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Time**: The time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Area of Body Treated**: Select the areas of your body that will be treated.
* **Medical Concerns**: Select 'Yes' if you have any medical concerns that may impact the treatment. If you do not have any medical concerns, select 'No'.
* **Pain Level**: Select the level of pain you expect to experience during the treatment:
	+ **No Pain**: I do not expect any pain.
	+ **Little Pain**: I expect a little pain.
	+ **Some Pain**: I expect some pain.
	+ **A Lot of Pain**: I expect a lot of pain.
* **Number of Sessions**: Select the number of sessions you think you may need.
* **Desired Outcome**: Select the outcome you hope to achieve with the treatment:
	+ **Smooth and Silky Skin**: I want smooth and silky skin.
	+ **Reduced Hair Growth**: I want reduced hair growth.
	+ **Reduced Ingrown Hairs**: I want reduced ingrown hairs.
* **Notes**: Answer 'Yes' if you have any additional comments or questions. If you do not have any additional comments or questions, select 'No'.
* **Medical Certificate**: Provide any relevant medical certification or document.
* **Consent**: Select 'Yes' if you consent to the treatment. If you do not consent, select 'No'.
* **Consent Date**: The date of your consent.
* **Consent Time**: The time of your consent.
* **Consent By**: The name of the person who obtained your consent.
* **Technician Signature**: The signature of the technician assigned to your treatment.
* **Technician Name**: The name of the technician assigned to your treatment.

## Tips
* Please take your time and answer all questions accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the technician or manager.
* This form is a crucial part of your treatment process, and your answers will help us tailor the services to your needs.
