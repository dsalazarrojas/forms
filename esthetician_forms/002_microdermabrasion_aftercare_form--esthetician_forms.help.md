# Microdermabrasion Aftercare Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Aftercare Form is a tool used to collect information from customers about their microdermabrasion aftercare. This form will help us to provide you with the best possible aftercare advice based on your skin type, microdermabrasion products used, frequency, and any adverse effects experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your skin type by choosing one of the options for "What is your skin type?".
2. Select all the conditions that apply to you from the "skin_condition" dropdown list.
3. Choose the microdermabrasion products you used during the treatment.
4. Choose your treatment frequency from the "Frequency" dropdown list.
5. Select any adverse effects you experienced after the microdermabrasion treatment from the "Adverse effects" dropdown list.
6. If you experienced any other adverse effects not listed, please provide more information in the "other_adverse_effects" text field.
7. If you have any advice or feedback for us, please provide it in the "Microdermabrasion advice" text field.
8. Select any aftercare products you used from the "Aftercare products" dropdown list.
9. If you used any other aftercare products not listed, please provide more information in the "other_aftercare_products" text field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about your experience in the "Notes" text field.
11. Finally, provide your contact information (email and phone number) so we can get back to you with any further questions or advice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your skin type?** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_form`, text, required): This is the first step in providing us with information about your skin type. Please choose one of the options that best describes your skin type.
* **Skin Condition** (`microdermabrasion_skin_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select all the conditions that apply to you, such as normal, sensitive, acne-prone, or oily skin.
* **Products used** (`microdermabrasion_products_used`, select_one, required): Choose the microdermabrasion products you used during the treatment from the dropdown list.
* **Products used (if other)** (`microdermabrasion_products_used_other`, text, optional): If you used any other products not listed, please provide more information here.
* **Frequency** (`microdermabrasion_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose your treatment frequency from the dropdown list (daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **Adverse effects** (`microdermabrasion_adverse_effects`, select_multiple, required): Select any adverse effects you experienced after the microdermabrasion treatment from the dropdown list.
* **Adverse effects (if other)** (`microdermabrasion_adverse_effects_other`, text, optional): If you experienced any other adverse effects not listed, please provide more information here.
* **Microdermabrasion advice** (`microdermabrasion_advice`, text, optional): If you have any advice or feedback for us, please provide it here.
* **Aftercare products** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_products`, select_multiple, optional): Select any aftercare products you used from the dropdown list.
* **Aftercare products (if other)** (`microdermabrasion_aftercare_products_other`, text, optional): If you used any other aftercare products not listed, please provide more information here.
* **Notes** (`microdermabrasion_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`microdermabrasion_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can get back to you with any further questions or advice.
* **Phone** (`microdermabrasion_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can get back to you with any further questions or advice.
* **Date** (`microdermabrasion_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of your microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Time** (`microdermabrasion_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of your microdermabrasion treatment.
