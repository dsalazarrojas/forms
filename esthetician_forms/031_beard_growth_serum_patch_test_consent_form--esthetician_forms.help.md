# Beard Growth Serum Patch Test Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Beard Growth Serum Patch Test Consent Form" is a document used to obtain a client's consent for a beard growth serum patch test, which is a safety measure to assess their skin sensitivity before administering the test.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, as they are used to ensure your safety and a smooth testing experience.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form, indicating that you have read and understood the questions and risks associated with the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your first name as per your identification.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your last name as per your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This field is where you will enter your date of birth to help the esthetician understand your medical history.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This field is where you will enter your contact email to ensure that the esthetician can get in touch with you after the test.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your contact phone number to ensure that the esthetician can reach you after the test.
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): This field is where you will enter the date of the consultation where you were recommended for the patch test.
* **Esthetician Name** (`esthetician_name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter the name of the esthetician who recommended the patch test.
* **Serum Brand** (`serum_brand`, text, required): This field is where you will enter the brand of the beard growth serum being tested.
* **Skin Sensitivity Level** (`skin_sensitivity`, select_one, required): This field is where you will choose how sensitive your skin is, with options: Not Sensitive, Mildly Sensitive, Moderately Sensitive, Very Sensitive.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, text, required): This field is where you will list any known allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): This field is where you will list any current medications you are taking, especially topical ones.
* **Previous Reactions to Products** (`previous_reactions`, text, required): This field is where you will list any previous reactions you have had to products similar to the beard growth serum.
* **Patch Test Location** (`patch_test_location`, select_one, required): This field is where you will choose where the patch test will be performed, with options: Behind Ear, Inner Arm, Neck, Other.
* **Test Procedure Explained** (`test_procedure_explained`, select_one, required): This field is where you will confirm that you understand the test procedure.
* **Possible Reactions Explained** (`possible_reactions_explained`, select_one, required): This field is where you will confirm that you understand the possible reactions associated with the test.
* **Waiting Period Understood** (`waiting_period_understood`, select_one, required): This field is where you will confirm that you understand the 24-48 hour waiting period before using the product.
* **Consent to Patch Test** (`consent_to_patch_test`, select_one, required): This field is where you will indicate your consent to undergo the patch test.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): This field is where you will sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): This field is where you will date and sign the form to confirm that you have understood the form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all questions truthfully, as this will help the esthetician understand your medical history and provide a safe testing experience.
* Make sure to sign and date the form, as this is an indication that you have read and understood the questions and risks associated with the test.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the esthetician before signing the form.
