# Facial Treatment Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vital information from clients before a facial treatment consultation. The goal is to understand their medical history, skin concerns, and preferences to provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the **Client Information** section.
2. Describe your medical history, including any conditions or treatments you have undergone, in the **Medical History** section.
3. Select the skin concerns you would like to address during the treatment in the **Treatment Preferences** section.
4. Specify your treatment goals in the **Treatment Goals** section.
5. Choose your skin type from the **Skin Type** section.
6. List any allergies or sensitivities you have in the **Allergies** section.
7. Mention any medications you are currently taking in the **Medications** section.
8. If a medical procedure is necessary, type it in the **Medical Procedure** section.
9. Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the consultation outcome.
10. Leave any additional notes or comments for the esthetician in the **Medical Notes** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions, procedures, or treatments you have undergone. This information helps the esthetician understand your overall health and any potential risks or complications.
* **Treatment Preferences**: Choose the skin concerns you would like to address during the treatment. Select all that apply.
	+ Acne: Skin issues such as acne, blackheads, or whiteheads.
	+ Aging: Concerns related to premature aging, wrinkles, or skin elasticity.
	+ Hyper-pigmentation: Uneven skin tone or discoloration.
	+ Rosacea: A chronic skin condition causing redness, inflammation, or acne-like symptoms.
	+ Skin Cancer: Suspected or diagnosed skin cancer concerns.
	+ Skin Lesions: Abnormal growths, scars, or marks on the skin.
	+ Sun Damage: Skin damage due to excessive sun exposure.
	+ Vitiligo: A condition causing white or pale patches on the skin.
* **Treatment Goals**: Describe what you hope to achieve from the treatment, such as reducing aging signs or improving skin texture.
* **Skin Type**: Select the type of skin you have:
	+ Normal: Balanced skin type with no major concerns.
	+ Dry: Skin that lacks moisture, causing irritation or flakiness.
	+ Oily: Skin that produces excessive oil, leading to clogged pores or acne.
	+ Combination: Skin with both dry and oily areas.
	+ Sensitive: Skin that reacts easily to products or environmental changes.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have, such as fragrance, dyes, or latex.
* **Medications**: Mention any medications you are currently taking, including supplements or topical creams.
* **Medical Procedure**: If a medical procedure is necessary, type it in this section. For example, "Botox injections" or "chemical peel."
* **Client Signoff**: Sign and date this section to confirm your understanding and agreement with the consultation outcome.
* **Medical Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes for the esthetician to consider during the treatment.
