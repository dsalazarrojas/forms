# Brow Threading Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent for a brow threading service. It captures essential information, such as the client's name, appointment date, and any skin sensitivities or reactions. Additionally, it includes a consent confirmation, photo release, and aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth and safe experience for both the client and the technician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the client's name and appointment date as scheduled.
* If the client has any skin sensitivities or reactions, provide a brief note in the "Skin sensitivities" field.
* Select "I consent" to confirm that the client is aware of and agrees to the service terms.
* If you wish to use a photo of the client for promotional purposes, select "True" in the "Photo release" field.
* In the "Aftercare notes" field, read and acknowledge the aftercare instructions provided.
* Sign your initials in the "Technician initials" field for service record keeping.
* If you have any additional comments or notes regarding the service, you can add them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name (Client name, text, required)**: Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Appointment date (Appointment date, date, required)**: The date of the scheduled appointment for the brow threading service.
* **Skin sensitivities (Skin sensitivities, text, optional)**: A note about any skin sensitivities or reactions the client may have. This helps the technician provide a safe and comfortable experience.
* **Consent confirmation (Select one, required)**: Confirm that the client is aware of and agrees to the service terms.
* **Aftercare notes (Note, optional)**: Read and acknowledge the aftercare instructions provided.
* **Photo release (Select one, optional)**: Select "True" if you wish to use a photo of the client for promotional purposes.
* **Technician initials (Technician initials, text, required)**: Sign your initials for service record keeping.
* **Additional comments (Text, optional)**: Any other notes or comments about the service you'd like to add.
