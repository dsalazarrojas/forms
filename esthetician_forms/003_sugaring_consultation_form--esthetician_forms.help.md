# Sugaring Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients about their preferences for sugaring-related services. It will help aesthetic professionals understand the client's needs and schedule appointments accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the client's information, including their name, contact details, and signature.
2. Choose the desired services and frequencies for each service.
3. Select the preferred and available times for the consultation.
4. Provide notes and comments for the aesthetician and client.
5. Review and submit the form to schedule the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (required)**: The client's full name, for identification purposes.
* **Contact Info (required)**: The client's contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Desired Service**: Choose one or more services you would like to have during the consultation. Options include:
	+ Yes (for the selected service)
	+ No (for no service)
* **Sugaring**: Select yes or no for sugaring services.
* **Sugar Peel**: Select yes or no for sugar peel services.
* **Sugar Mask**: Select yes or no for sugar mask services.
* **Desired Duration (required)**: The length of time the client would like to schedule for the consultation.
* **Expected Frequency**: Choose how often the client would like to have the consultation. Options include:
	+ Yes (for the selected frequency)
	+ No (for no frequency)
* **One session**: Select yes or no for a one-time session.
* **Two sessions**: Select yes or no for two sessions.
* **Multiple sessions**: Select yes or no for multiple sessions.
* **Ongoing**: Select yes or no for ongoing sessions.
* **Preferred Time**: The client's preferred time for the consultation.
* **Available Date**: The date the client is available for the consultation.
* **Available Time**: The time the client is available for the consultation.
* **Client Notes**: Any additional notes or comments the client would like to share with the aesthetician.
* **Aesthetician Notes**: Any notes or comments the aesthetician would like to share with the client.
* **Aesthetician Comments**: Comments for the aesthetician to add.
* **Aesthetician Signature**: Select yes or no to indicate that the appointment is confirmed.
* **Aesthetician Name (required)**: The name of the aesthetician responsible for the consultation.
* **Client Signature (required)**: The client's signature to confirm the appointment.
* **Email (required)**: The client's email address.
* **Phone (required)**: The client's phone number.
* **Address (required)**: The client's address.
* **Notes (required)**: Any additional notes or comments the client would like to add.
