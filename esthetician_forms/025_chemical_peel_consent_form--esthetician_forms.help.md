# Chemical Peel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive consent form for chemical peel procedures, outlining the potential risks, benefits, and aftercare instructions. It ensures that patients are fully informed and understand the treatment they are about to undergo.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, answering all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its significance.
3. Sign the form voluntarily, acknowledging that you have read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, date, required): Enter the scheduled date of the chemical peel procedure.
* **Type of Chemical Peel** (`peel_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of chemical peel you are undergoing (Superficial peel (alpha hydroxy acid), Medium peel (trichloroacetic acid), Deep peel (phenol), Combination peel, Custom formulation).
* **Treatment Area** (`treatment_area`, select_one, required): Indicate the area to be treated (Full face, Forehead only, Cheeks only, Around mouth, Neck, Chest, Hands, Other).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any known medical conditions you have.
* **Skin Conditions** (`skin_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have any skin conditions (Eczema, Psoriasis, Rosacea, Acne, Cold sores, None).
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Current Skincare Products** (`skincare_products`, text, optional): List any skincare products you are using on your skin.
* **Previous Chemical Peels** (`previous_peels`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had chemical peels before.
* **Previous Peel Details** (`previous_peel_details`, text, optional): Describe your previous chemical peel experiences.
* **Recent Sun Exposure** (`sun_exposure`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had significant sun exposure recently.
* **Pregnancy or Nursing Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your pregnancy or nursing status (Yes, pregnant, Yes, nursing, False, Not applicable).
* **Procedure Understanding** (`procedure_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand what the chemical peel procedure involves.
* **Risks Understood** (`risks_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the potential risks and complications of the procedure.
* **Risks Acknowledged** (`risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the procedure.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (`aftercare_instructions`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the aftercare instructions provided.
* **Sun Protection Agreement** (`sun_protection`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to avoid sun exposure and use sunscreen as instructed.
* **No Guarantees Understood** (`no_guarantees`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand that results cannot be guaranteed.
* **Alternative Treatments Discussed** (`alternative_treatments`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have discussed alternative treatment options.
* **Questions Answered** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Confirm that all your questions have been answered.
* **Voluntary Consent** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are signing this form voluntarily.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required): Sign this form to confirm that you consent to the chemical peel procedure.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the treating provider.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provider obtained your consent.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Review each field before signing the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask your provider for clarification.
* Sign the form only after you have thoroughly reviewed and understood the information provided.
* Ensure that you understand the risks and benefits associated with the chemical peel procedure.
