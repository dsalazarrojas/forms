# Skincare Ritual Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your skincare habits, concerns, and preferences to help us better understand your needs and provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your current skincare routine. 
2. Choose the products you currently use, including cleanser, toner, essence, serum, moisturizer, sunscreen, exfoliant, or other.
3. Identify your primary skincare concerns, such as acne, aging, dryness, hyper-pigmentation, redness, or sensitivity.
4. Provide the frequency of your current skincare routine, including morning, evening, both, or rarely.
5. Select the duration of your current skincare routine in minutes.
6. Specify how many days a week you perform your skincare routine.
7. If you have concerns not listed, please specify in the text box.
8. If you have a product recommendation, please specify it in the text box.
9. If you use any other products, please describe them in the text box.
10. If you have any other comments or notes, please provide them in the text box.
11. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
12. Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
13. Provide your name for identification purposes.
14. Enter the date you submitted this form.
15. Select how often you perform your skincare routine, including morning, evening, both, or rarely.
16. Choose the type of your current skincare routine, including daily, occasional, or rarely.
17. Enter the duration of your current skincare routine in minutes.
18. Enter the number of days you perform your skincare routine.
19. If you have any other concerns, please specify in the text box.
20. If you have any other product recommendations, please provide them in the text box.
21. If you use any other products, please describe them in the text box.
22. Please enter any other information about the products you use in the text box.
23. Check the box to indicate you agree to the terms and conditions.

* **Skincare Routine Fields**: A text field to describe your skincare routine.

* **Skincare Duration**: Enter the duration of your skincare routine in minutes.
* **Skincare Days**: Enter the number of days you perform your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Concerns**: Select multiple from a list of common skincare concerns to report your concerns.
* **Skincare Frequency of Use**: Select a date to report the frequency of your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Time of Day**: Select a time to report the time of your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Notes**: A text field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Skincare Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Skincare Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Skincare Submitter's Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Skincare Form Submitted On**: Select a date to report the date you submitted this form.
* **Skincare Routine Frequency**: Select from "Morning", "Evening", "Both", or "Rarely" to report how often you perform your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Type**: Select from "Daily", "Occasional", or "Rarely" to report the type of your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Duration**: Enter the duration of your skincare routine in minutes.
* **Skincare Routine Days**: Enter the number of days you perform your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Concerns Other**: If you have concerns not listed, enter them in the text box.
* **Skincare Product Recommendation**: If you have a product recommendation, enter it in the text box.
* **Skincare Product Relevant**: If you use any other products, describe them in the text box.
