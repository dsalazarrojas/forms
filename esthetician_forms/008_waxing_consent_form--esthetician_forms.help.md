# Waxing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waxing Consent Form is a document that ensures the client understands and agrees to the procedures that will be performed during the waxing process. It includes information about the client's details, consent details, risks, benefits, procedure description, and agreements from both the client and the esthetician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Waxing Consent Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details (page 1).
2. Provide a detailed description of the procedure and any risks associated with it.
3. Answer questions regarding the benefits of the procedure and any medical conditions, allergies, or medications that may affect the procedure.
4. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the consent details.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Please enter your name, address, and contact information. This information will help us better understand your needs and communicate with you effectively.
* **Consent Details**: Provide a detailed description of the consent you are giving, including any conditions or restrictions you have. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Risks**: List any risks associated with the waxing procedure, such as skin irritation or allergic reactions.
* **Benefits**: Describe the benefits of the waxing procedure, including any expected outcomes or results.
* **Procedure Description**: Provide a detailed description of the waxing procedure, including any specific steps that will be taken.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understand the procedures.
* **Client Agreement**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand the consent details.
* **Aesthetician Agreement**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understand your role as the esthetician performing the procedure.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understand the procedures.
* **Aesthetician Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have performed the procedures as described.
* **Date**: The date you signed the form.
* **Time**: The time you signed the form.
* **Duration**: The estimated duration of the procedure.
* **Frequency**: The frequency of the procedure (e.g., how often you plan to have it done).
* **Notes**: Any additional notes or comments regarding the procedure.
* **Emergency Contact**: If there is an emergency contact person, please provide their name and phone number.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect the procedure.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have that may affect the procedure.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking that may affect the procedure.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Client Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments from the client.
* **Emergency Notes**: Provide any emergency notes or comments.
* **Consent Details**: Provide a detailed description of the consent you are giving.
* **Signature Date**: The date you signed the form.
* **Frequency**: The frequency of the procedure (e.g., how often you plan to have it done).

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand all the information provided before signing the form.
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your esthetician for clarification.
- Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
