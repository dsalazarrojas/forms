# Cryotherapy Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Cryotherapy Facial Consent Form to ensure a safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Enter your name and contact information as it appears on your identification card. Please make sure to include your contact number and email address.
* **Medical History**: List any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns that may impact your treatment. Be as detailed as possible, so our team can provide the best care.
* **Treatment Consent**: Confirm that you have read and understood the risks and benefits of the treatment. Please check "yes" if you consent to proceed with the treatment.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure your safety and well-being.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask our team for clarification.
