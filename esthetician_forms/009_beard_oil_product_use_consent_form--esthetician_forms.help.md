# Beard Oil Product Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients to ensure their safety and consent to use a beard oil product. This form helps us understand your health history, product preferences, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Client Information

* **Full Name** (Field 2): Please enter your full name to identify you as our client.
* **Email Address** (Field 3): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (Field 4): Provide your phone number for us to reach out to you if there are any questions or concerns.

### 2. Health and Allergy History

* **Known Allergies** (Field 7): List any allergies you have, such as food allergies, skin allergies, or other allergies that may affect your skin.
* **Current Skin Conditions** (Field 9): Inform us about any skin conditions you have, like dermatitis, psoriasis, or eczema.

### 3. Product Information

* **Beard Oil Product Name** (Field 14): Identify the specific beard oil product you'll be using.
* **I have reviewed the product ingredients** (Field 15): Confirm that you've reviewed the product ingredients.
* **I understand the benefits and uses** (Field 16): Acknowledge that you're aware of the product's benefits and intended use.

### 4. Consent and Acknowledgment

* **I consent to the use of this beard oil product** (Field 18): Confirm that you give your consent to use the product.
* **I acknowledge potential side effects** (Field 19): Understand and acknowledge the potential risks, such as irritation, allergic reactions, or discomfort.
* **Patch test was performed before application** (Field 20): Confirm that a patch test was performed before using the product.
* **I agree to follow care and usage instructions** (Field 21): Agree to follow the instructions provided for using the product.
* **Signature and Date** (Field 22): Sign and date this form to provide consent.
* **Date of Consent** (Field 23): Enter the date when you sign this form.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the form accurately and confidently, ensuring your safety and understanding of the product's use.
