# Beard Cleanse Mask Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide their consent and information for a professional deep cleansing mask treatment. It will help the esthetician understand the client's medical history, allergies, and potential risks associated with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information in the "Client Information" section.
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
	* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
2. In the "Medical History" section, provide any relevant medical information.
	* Select your skin type from the options provided.
	* List any current skin conditions you may have, such as acne or eczema.
	* List any allergies you may have.
3. In the "Treatment Consent" section, confirm your understanding and consent for the treatment.
	* Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the procedure.
	* Select "Yes" to consent to the treatment.
	* Acknowledge potential risks and side effects.
	* Confirm you have been informed about the product ingredients.
	* Confirm you will follow aftercare instructions.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth here, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type from the options:
	+ Sensitive
	+ Normal
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Combination
* **Current Skin Conditions**: List any current skin conditions you may have, such as acne, eczema, or psoriasis.
* **Known Allergies**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Sensitive to Fragrances**: Select "Yes" if you are sensitive to fragrances, "No" otherwise.
* **Recent Facial Treatments or Surgery**: If you have had any recent facial treatments or surgery, list them here.
* **Current Medications Affecting Skin**: If you are taking any medications that affect your skin, list them here.
* **Treatment Description**: This is a description of the treatment you are consenting to. Read it carefully before proceeding.
* **I understand the Treatment Procedure**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand the treatment procedure.
* **I Consent to this Treatment**: Select "Yes" to consent to the treatment.
* **I Acknowledge Potential Risks and Side Effects**: Select "Yes" to confirm you understand potential risks and side effects.
* **I have been Informed about Product Ingredients**: Select "Yes" to confirm you have been informed about the product ingredients.
* **I will Follow Aftercare Instructions**: Select "Yes" to confirm you will follow aftercare instructions.
* **Client Signature Consent**: Sign your name here to confirm your consent.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of consent in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
