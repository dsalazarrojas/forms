# Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help medical professionals and estheticians provide personalized skin care advice and treatment recommendations by gathering information about your skin care concerns and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field as instructed. For select fields, choose all that apply. For text fields, enter your answer in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skin Care Questionnaire**: Please select all the skin concerns you are currently experiencing, such as Acne, Blemishes, Fine lines, etc.
* **Age**: Please enter your age.
* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type from the list of options (Dry, Oily, Combination, Normal, Sensitive, or Rosacea-prone).
* **Skincare Products**: List the skincare products you currently use.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history you may have.
* **Medical Conditions**: Check all medical conditions you have been diagnosed with or have a history of (Acne, Allergies, etc.).
* **Medical Treatments**: Select all medical treatments you are currently undergoing (Antihistamines, Biologics, etc.).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments you would like to share.
* **Skin Condition**: Check all skin conditions you are currently experiencing (Acne, Blemishes, etc.).
* **Medical Professional**: Select the medical professional you are currently consulting (Dermatologist, Esthetician, etc.).
* **Treatment Plan**: Select all treatment plans you would like to pursue (Chemical peeling, Facial, etc.).
