# Eyebrow Lamination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyebrow Lamination Consent Form is a document that clients must complete before their appointment for eyebrow lamination treatment. It is used to gather essential information and obtain consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If applicable, provide your address in the "address" field.
6. Choose the date of your visit from the calendar provided.
7. Select the time of your visit from the provided options.
8. Choose the type of consultation (New client, Follow-up appointment, or Pre-booked appointment).
9. Select the areas to focus on during the consultation (Brows, Haircut, Coloring, Makeup, or any other option not listed).
10. Provide consent to treatment by typing in the "consent_to_treatment" field.
11. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the "additional_info" field.
11. Sign your name in the "signature" field.
12. Decide if you would like to share your data with the business (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, required): Please fill in your first name as it should appear on the form.
* **last_name** (Text, required): Fill in your last name as it should appear on the form.
* **Email** (Email, required): Provide your email address so we can reach you.
* **phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **address** (Text, optional): If applicable, provide your current address.
* **Date** (Date, required): Choose the date of your visit from the provided calendar.
* **Time** (Time, required): Select the time of your visit from the provided options.
* **type_of_consultation** (Select One, required): Choose the type of consultation (New client, Follow-up appointment, or Pre-booked appointment).
* **areas_to_focus** (Select Multiple, required): Select the areas to focus on during the consultation (Brows, Haircut, Coloring, Makeup, or other options not listed).
* **consent_to_treatment** (Note, required): Provide a brief explanation of your consent to the treatment.
* **additional_info** (Text, optional): If you have any additional information, please provide it here.
* **signature** (Text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **share** (Select One, required): Decide if you would like to share your data with the business (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all the required fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask your consultant.
* This form should be completed at least 24 hours before your appointment time.
* Failure to complete this form may result in cancellation of your appointment.
