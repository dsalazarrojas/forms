**Aesthetics Supplementary Assessment - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your background, medical history, and preferences to help the practitioner provide the best possible aesthetic treatment plan for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Ensure that you have not already provided similar information to the practitioner during a previous visit or consultation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
