# Microchanneling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather informed consent from clients prior to undergoing a microchanneling procedure. Microchanneling is a non-invasive treatment aimed at skin rejuvenation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each question and understand the implications before answering.
* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, paying attention to the required and optional fields.
* Select the correct options from the list provided for each question.
* Sign the form by typing your name in the "Sign" field.
* Date and time of the procedure will be filled in automatically, but you can fill it in manually if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Microchanneling Information
* **Microchanneling Information** (`Microchanneling Information`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to provide any additional information about their microchanneling experience or expectations.

### Risks
* **Risks** (`Risks`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to understand and acknowledge the potential risks associated with microchanneling. Please note that these risks may include but are not limited to:

    *   Discomfort during treatment
    *   Redness and swelling post-procedure
    *   Bruising post-procedure
    *   Sensitivity post-procedure

### Benefits
* **Benefits** (`Benefits`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to understand and acknowledge the potential benefits of microchanneling. Please note that these benefits may include but are not limited to:

    *   Improved skin appearance
    *   Reduced fine lines and wrinkles
    *   Enhanced skin texture

### Aftercare
* **Aftercare** (`Aftercare`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to provide any additional information or instructions regarding aftercare after the procedure.

### Informed Consent
* **Informed Consent** (`Informed Consent`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to provide a written statement of their informed consent, indicating that they have understood the potential risks and benefits of the procedure and are willing to undergo it.

### Sign
* **Sign** (`Sign`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to sign their name, indicating that they have read and understood the form and are willing to undergo the procedure.

### Date
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically filled in upon signing, but you can fill it in manually if necessary. Please note that this field is required.

### Time
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): This field is automatically filled in upon signing, but you can fill it in manually if necessary. Please note that this field is required.

### Procedure
* **Procedure** (`Procedure`, `select one`, optional): This field is for the client to select the type of procedure they are seeking to undergo. Please choose one from the list provided:

    *   Microchanneling
    *   Chemical Peel
    *   Derma Fx
    *   Other

### Risks Acceptance
* **Risks Acceptance** (`Risks Acceptance`, `select multiple`, optional): This field is for the client to indicate their acceptance of the potential risks associated with the procedure. Please select all that apply from the list provided:

    *   I understand that the microchanneling procedure may cause some discomfort during treatment.
    *   I understand that the microchanneling procedure may cause some redness and swelling post-procedure.
    *   I understand that the microchanneling procedure may cause some bruising post-procedure.
    *   I understand that the microchanneling procedure may cause some sensitivity post-procedure.
    *   Other (please describe)

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `select multiple`, optional): This field is for the client to provide information about their medical history. Please select all that apply from the list provided:

    *   Are you pregnant or breastfeeding?
    *   Have you ever had cancer?
    *   Have you ever had any previous treatments for skin issues?
    *   Have you taken any medication recently?
    *   Have you had a history of allergic reactions?
    *   Do you take any blood thinners?
    *   Other (please describe)

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `select multiple`, optional): This field is for the client to provide information about their medical condition. Please select all that apply from the list provided:

    *   'True'
    *   'False'
    *   Prefer not to say

### Supervise
* **Supervise** (`Supervise`, `select multiple`, optional): This field is for the client to indicate whether they will be supervised during the procedure. Please select all that apply from the list provided:

    *   'True'
    *   'False'
    *   Prefer not to say

### Contraindicated
* **Contraindicated** (`Contraindicated`, `select multiple`, optional): This field is for the client to indicate whether they have any contraindications for the procedure. Please select all that apply from the list provided:

    *   'True'
    *   'False'
    *   Prefer not to say

### Medical Condition Note
* **Medical Condition Note** (`Medical Condition Note`, `text`, optional): This field is for the client to provide additional information about their medical condition.
