# Aesthetics Customer Satisfaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aesthetics Customer Satisfaction Form is designed to collect information about your experience with our aesthetic services. Your feedback will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Provide as much detail as you can in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (\*)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (\*)**: Your email address will be used for follow-up communication (optional).
* **Date of Visit (\*)**: The date you visited our establishment.
* **Type of Service Received (\*)**: Select the type of aesthetic service you received (e.g., Facial Treatment, Chemical Peel, etc.).
* **Service Provider Name (\*)**: The name of the esthetician who provided the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction (\*)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service received (1-5).
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating (\*)**: Rate the overall satisfaction with the service received (1-5).
* **Service Quality Rating (\*)**: Rate the quality of the service received (1-5).
* **Cleanliness and Hygiene Rating (\*)**: Rate the cleanliness and hygiene of the facility (1-5).
* **Staff Professionalism Rating (\*)**: Rate the professionalism of the staff (1-5).
* **Wait Time Satisfaction (\*)**: Rate your satisfaction with the wait time for your appointment (1-5).
* **Skin Improvement Observed (\*)**: Select whether you observed a significant improvement, some improvement, no change, or a worse condition after the treatment.
* **Comfort Level During Service (\*)**: Rate your comfort level during the service (1-5).
* **Pain or Discomfort Level (\*)**: Rate the pain or discomfort you experienced during the service (1-5).
* **Consultation Quality (\*)**: Rate the quality of the consultation before the service (1-5).
* **Product Quality Rating (\*)**: Rate the quality of the products used during the service (1-5).
* **Likelihood to Return (\*)**: Rate how likely you are to return for future services (1-5).
* **Likelihood to Recommend (\*)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend our services to friends or family (1-5).
* **Interest in Future Appointments**: Select whether you are interested in scheduling future appointments (True, False, or Maybe).
* **Preferred Services for Future Visits**: Select the services you would be interested in receiving in the future (e.g., Facial Treatments, Anti-aging Procedures, etc.).
* **Feedback and Suggestions**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions about your experience.
* **Positive Aspects**: Describe what you liked most about your experience.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe what aspects of our service could be improved.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments about your experience.

Note: The \* indicates a required field. Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
