# spa_service_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "spa_service_survey_form" is used to collect feedback and ratings from spa customers to help management understand their services and staff performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field.
3. Choose a rating for the **Service Rating** by selecting one of the options provided. This field is optional.
4. Choose a rating for the **Staff Rating** by selecting one of the options provided. This field is optional.
5. Choose a rating for the **Overall Spa Rating** by selecting one of the options provided. This field is optional.
6. Enter any **Comments** you may have in the "Comments" field. This is an optional field.
7. Select the **Service** you visited by choosing one or more options from the list. This field is optional.
8. Enter the date of your **Spa Visit** in the "Spa Visit" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Service Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate the service you received from our spa staff. Select one of the options provided.
* **Staff Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate the staff member who served you.
* **Overall Spa Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate your overall experience at the spa.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Service** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the service you visited (e.g., Facials, Massages, Haircuts).
* **Spa Visit** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit to the spa.
