# spa_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa_consent_form is a template designed to collect essential information from clients about their upcoming spa treatments. This form helps facilitate clear communication and ensures clients provide necessary details for their appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the spa_consent_form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Provide a **procedure description**, a brief explanation of the treatment or service you've booked.
3. Enter your **client name**, your name as the client.
4. Select a **date** for your appointment, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Choose a **time** for your appointment, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **procedure description** (`procedure description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the treatment or service you've booked.
  - This field helps the spa staff understand what you've booked and prepares them for your visit.

- **client name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the client.
  - This field is required to identify you as the client and ensure accurate record-keeping.

- **date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your appointment, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - Select a date for your scheduled appointment.

- **time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for your appointment, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.
  - Pick a time slot for your booked appointment.
