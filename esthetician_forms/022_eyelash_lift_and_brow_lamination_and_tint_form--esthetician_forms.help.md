# Eyelash Lift And Brow Lamination And Tint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before providing eyelash lift, lamination, and tint services. It helps the esthetician to identify potential risks, medical conditions, and allergies, ensuring a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Provide your contact information (phone and email).
4. Answer the questions regarding medical conditions, allergies, and medical history.
5. Confirm your consent for the treatment.
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for your visit.
* **Prophets** (`prophet`, select_one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you have a medical condition or allergy that may affect the treatment.
* **Risks** (`risk`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any risks associated with the treatment that may affect you.
* **Procedures** (`procedure`, text, required: false): Describe the procedures you are interested in receiving.
* **Consent To Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm your understanding of the treatment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm your information.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, text, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergy`, text, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): List any medication you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition_2`, text, required: false): Describe any other medical conditions you have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you have a medical history that may affect the treatment.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any risks associated with your medical history.
* **Procedure 2** (`procedure_2`, text, required: false): Describe any other procedures you are interested in receiving.
* **Consent To Treatment 2** (`consent_to_treatment_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm your understanding of the treatment.
* **Signature 2** (`signature_2`, date, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm your information.
