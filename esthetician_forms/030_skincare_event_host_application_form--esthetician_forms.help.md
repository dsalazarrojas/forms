# Skincare Event Host Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Event Host Application Form is used to collect information from individuals or companies seeking to host their events in a skincare-related context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below. Please fill in each field accurately and make sure to review the form thoroughly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is used to describe the event you are applying to host. Please provide a brief description of your event, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.

* **Host Details** (`host_details`, text, required): Here, you should provide your personal details as the host, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.

* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): Enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant company details.

* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the services you will be providing at your event, such as skincare consultations, product demonstrations, or workshops.

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is similar to the first "Event Details" field but seems to be a redundant field. Please disregard this field and focus on providing a detailed description of your event in the first "Event Details" field.

* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, required): In this field, you should provide any additional information or attachments that support your application, such as event schedules, promotional materials, or supporting documents.

* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Here, you should outline your payment terms, including any fees, payment methods, and deadlines for payment.

## Tips
Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form support team for assistance.
