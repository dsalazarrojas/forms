# Skincare Device Demonstration Appointment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a demonstration of a skincare device at a spa. Please fill out the form to provide details of the demonstration and your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact details section with your name, phone number, and email.
2. Select the preferred time for the demonstration from the options provided.
3. Choose the skincare device to be demonstrated from the list.
4. Enter the appointment date and time.
5. Provide the spa address where the demonstration will take place.
6. Add any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the demonstration.
7. Indicate if you would like a follow-up report or not.
8. Confirm if a report was created or not after the demonstration.
9. Set the appointment status as active or inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact**: Your name, phone number, and email address will be used to contact you regarding the appointment.
* **Phone**: Your phone number will be used to contact you about the appointment.
* **Email**: Your email address will be used to contact you about the appointment.
* **Skincare Device**: Select the device you would like to be demonstrated from the list.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time that suits you best for the demonstration.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date for the demonstration.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time for the demonstration.
* **Spa Address**: Enter the address of the spa where the demonstration will take place.
* **Skincare Device Demoed**: Indicate if the demonstration was completed with a report or not.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information related to the demonstration.
* **Follow-up**: Choose whether you would like a follow-up report or not after the demonstration.
* **Skincare Device Demonstration Report**: Confirm if a report was created or not after the demonstration.
* **Appointment Status**: Set the status of the appointment as active or inactive.
* **ID**: Your unique identifier for the appointment.
* **Skincare Device Demonstration**: A dropdown menu for the device to be demonstrated.
* **Other Skincare Device**: A dropdown menu for other devices that are not listed.
* **None**: An option for no demonstration or other reason.
* **Active/Inactive**: A dropdown menu for the appointment status.
* **Report/No Report**: A dropdown menu for follow-up status.
* **Yes/No**: A dropdown menu for demonstration report status.
