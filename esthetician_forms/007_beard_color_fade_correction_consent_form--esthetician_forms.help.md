# Beard Color Fade Correction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and consent for beard color fade correction services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll down to the relevant sections.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Please fill in your full name for contact and reference purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for easy contact and communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Provide your email address for communication and contact purposes.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, **required**): Specify the date when you plan to receive the service.
* **-- Allergy Information --** (`allergy_header`, `note`, **not required**): Please review the previous section carefully for any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Known Allergies to Hair Products** (`known_allergies`, `text`, **not required**): If you have any known allergies or sensitivities, list them in this field.
* **Scalp Sensitivity** (`scalp_sensitivity`, `select_one`, **required**): Specify your scalp sensitivity by selecting one of the options provided.
* **-- Medical Conditions --** (`medical_header`, `note`, **not required**): Please review the previous section carefully for any medical conditions.
* **Scalp Conditions (dandruff, psoriasis, etc.)** (`scalp_conditions`, `text`, **not required**): If you have any scalp conditions, list them in this field.
* **Recent Scalp Injuries or Irritations** (`skin_irritants`, `select_one`, **required**): Specify if you have any recent scalp injuries or irritations by selecting one of the options provided.
* **-- Current Beard Color --** (`beard_color_header`, `note`, **not required**): Please review the current section carefully for color information.
* **Current Beard Color** (`current_beard_color`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current beard color from the provided options.
* **Areas with Fading** (`faded_areas`, `text`, **required**): Specify areas on your face where you are concerned about color fading.
* **Desired Color After Correction** (`desired_color`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your desired color after correction from the provided options.
* **-- Treatment Consent --** (`consent_header`, `note`, **not required**): Please review the previous section carefully for consent information.
* **I understand the fade correction procedure** (`understand_procedure`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that you understand the procedure.
* **I consent to beard color fade correction** (`consent_to_treatment`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that you consent to the service.
* **I acknowledge potential side effects** (`acknowledge_side_effects`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that you acknowledge potential side effects.
* **Patch test performed and approved** (`patch_test_consent`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that a patch test was performed and approved.
* **Client Signature** (`signature_consent`, `text`, **required**): Type in your signature to confirm that you consent to the treatment, and understand the information provided.

**Tips**: Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

Please submit the form when all fields are accurate and complete.
