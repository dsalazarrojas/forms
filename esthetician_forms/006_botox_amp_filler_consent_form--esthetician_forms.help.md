# Botox and Filler Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients before Botox and filler treatments. It is used to get patient consent, understand their medical history, and ensure they are aware of the potential risks and side effects associated with these procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please make sure you have all the required information and documentation ready. This form is typically filled out by patients before their scheduled treatments. Please take your time to fill it out carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required)
* This is the patient's full name as it appears on their ID.
* Please enter your legal name as it is written on your ID.

### 2. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
* This is the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Please enter the date of birth using the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).

### 3. **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required)
* This is the patient's primary contact phone number.
* Please enter your primary phone number.

### 4. **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required)
* This is the patient's email address for appointment confirmations and follow-up communications.
* Please enter your email address.

### 5. **Address** (`patient_address`, text, required)
* This is the patient's complete home address.
* Please enter your complete home address.

### 6. **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required)
* This is the full name of the emergency contact.
* Please enter the full name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### 7. **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required)
* This is the phone number of the emergency contact.
* Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### 8. **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, date, required)
* This is the scheduled date for the procedure.
* Please enter the treatment date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### 9. **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_multiple, required)
* Select all procedures you are consenting to (e.g., Botox injections, Dermal fillers, Combination treatment).
* Please choose the procedures you are consenting to.

### 10. **Treatment Areas** (`treatment_areas`, select_multiple, required)
* Select areas to be treated (e.g., Forehead lines, Crow's feet, Frown lines between eyebrows).
* Please choose the areas to be treated.

### 11. **Previous Treatments** (`previous_treatments`, select_one, required)
* Have you had these treatments before (e.g., Yes, multiple times, Yes, once, No, this is my first time).
* Please choose your response.

### 12. **Date of Last Treatment** (`last_treatment_date`, date, not required)
* If applicable, enter the date of your last treatment.
* This field is optional but please fill it out if you have a previous treatment.

### 13. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required)
* Select any medical conditions that apply (e.g., Pregnancy, Breastfeeding, Neuromuscular disorders, Bleeding disorders).
* Please choose the conditions that apply to you.

### 14. **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required)
* List all medications and supplements you are taking.
* Please list all your current medications and supplements.

### 15. **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required)
* List any allergies, especially to medications or cosmetics.
* Please list any known allergies.

### 16. **Risks Acknowledged** (`risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required)
* I understand the potential risks and side effects.
* Please choose "Yes, I understand".

### 17. **Aftercare Instructions** (`aftercare_instructions`, select_one, required)
* I agree to follow all aftercare instructions.
* Please choose "Yes, I agree".

### 18. **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required)
* I consent to before and after photos for medical records.
* Please choose "Yes, for medical records only" or "Yes, for medical records and marketing".

### 19. **Results Understanding** (`satisfaction_understanding`, select_one, required)
* I understand that results vary and are not guaranteed.
* Please choose "Yes, I understand".

### 20. **Questions or Concerns** (`patient_questions`, text, not required)
* List any questions or concerns you have before proceeding.
* Please enter any questions or concerns you have.

### 21. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required)
* Type your full legal name as signature.
* Please sign your name as it appears on your ID.

### 22. **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required)
* Date of signing.
* Please enter the date of signing.

### 23. **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required)
* Provider to sign.
* Please enter the provider's name.

### 24. **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, not required)
* Witness to sign.
* This field is optional, but please enter the witness's name if present.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
- Please make sure to sign the form with your full legal name and date.
- The form is a requirement for your scheduled treatment, so please take the time to complete it carefully.
