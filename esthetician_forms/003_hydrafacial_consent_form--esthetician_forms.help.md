# Hydrafacial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrafacial Consent Form is used to collect necessary information from clients prior to receiving a hydrafacial treatment. This form aims to gather medical history, contact information, and other relevant details to ensure the client's understanding of the treatment and potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out each field with the required information.
- Make sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask a staff member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Practice** (`1`, text, required): This field is for your medical practice or clinic name. Please enter the name of the organization where you will be receiving your hydrafacial treatment.
* **Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`3`, text, required): Provide your complete address where treatment can be directed or contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted in case of any emergency.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History** (`6`, text, required): Share any past medical conditions, surgeries, or health issues that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (`7`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions not previously disclosed, please list them here.
* **Allergies** (`8`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Medications** (`9`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`10`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of people you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Signature** (`11`, text, required): Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Consent** (`12`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ I have read and understood the above information
	+ I have not read and do not understand the above information
	+ I am unsure about the above information

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask a staff member for assistance.
- Please review and sign this form carefully before submitting it.
