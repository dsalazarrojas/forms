# Lash Length Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lash Length Expectation Survey" is a collection tool used to gather information about users' expectations concerning their lash length and services offered. It's not a mandatory form, meaning you can fill it in as you see fit. This guide will walk you through each field, so you can provide your insights without any worries!

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. You can select all applicable options for each field.
3. If there's no clear category for your answer, use the "Other Lash Service" field for your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lash Length (`Lash Length Expectation Survey`):**
   Select all lash length options that you expect from services. This might include "Yes" for expecting natural-looking lashes, "Yes" for wanting to make them appear longer, or "Yes" for requiring lash lifts.

- **Lash Style (`Lash Style Expectation Survey`):**
   Choose "Yes" for any lash style you're looking for, such as natural, bold, or dramatic.

- **Lash Service (`Lash Service Expectation Survey`):**
   Select "Yes" for any service you expect from your lash technician, such as lash applications, lifts, or removals. If you have a special service in mind, type it in the "Other Lash Service" field.

- **Other Lash Service (`Other Lash Service`):**
   If there's no specific service listed, feel free to type it in here. This is for any custom or unique service you're looking for from your lash technician.

- **Lash Artist (`Lash Artist Expectation Survey`):**
   Choose "Yes" for any specific lash artist you're looking for or would prefer to work with.

- **Lash Length Expectation Survey Comments:**
   Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about your expectations for lash length. This is your chance to give more details.

- **Lash Style Expectation Survey Comments:**
   Share any comments or thoughts you have about your expectations for lash style.

- **Lash Service Expectation Survey Comments:**
   Share your thoughts or comments on your expectations for lash services.

Note: Remember that all fields are optional, so you can choose to fill in as many or as few as you'd like!
