# dermal_filler_treatment_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the details of a dermal filler treatment session, including client information, treatment details, outcome, follow-up schedule, and other relevant notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details (Page 1)
	* Enter the client's name and other relevant information in the "client_details" field.
2. Provide Treatment Details (Page 2)
	* Enter a description of the treatment session, including any notable points or concerns, in the "treatment_details" field.
3. Report Treatment Outcome (Page 3)
	* Select the outcome of the treatment from the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Not Satisfied At All.
4. Schedule Follow-up (Page 4)
	* Indicate if the next follow-up session is scheduled or not.
5. Add Notes and Attach Files (Optional)
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about the treatment in the "notes" field.
	* Attach any relevant files, such as before-and-after photos or consent forms, in the "output_file" field.
6. Confirm Form Category and ID (Optional)
	* Enter the category and ID of the form for internal reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_details** (Page 1)
	* This field is for entering the client's name and any other relevant information about their dermal filler treatment session.
* **treatment_details** (Page 2)
	* Provide a detailed description of the treatment session, including any notable points or concerns.
* **treatment_outcome** (Page 3)
	* Select the outcome of the treatment from the provided options to indicate how well the client responded to the treatment.
* **follow_up_scheduled** (Page 4)
	* Indicate if the next follow-up session is scheduled or not.
* **notes** (Page 5)
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about the treatment session.
* **assigned_tool** (Page 5)
	* Enter the name of any tool or equipment used during the treatment session (not provided as an option, but this field is likely to be used for internal reference).
* **output_file** (Page 5)
	* Attach any relevant files, such as before-and-after photos or consent forms, for future reference.
* **form_id** (Page 6)
	* Enter the unique ID of the form for internal reference.
* **form_category** (Page 6)
	* Enter the category of the form, likely to be used for internal sorting and searching.

**Tips**

* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the provided options for "treatment_outcome" and "follow_up_scheduled" to select the correct responses.
* Keep the "notes" field concise and focused on essential information.
* Attach relevant files to the "output_file" field to support the client's records.
