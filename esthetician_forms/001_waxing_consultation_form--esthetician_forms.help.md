# Waxing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for waxing services, including their name, contact information, wax type, areas to be waxed, and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field (optional).
4. Select the type of waxing service you are interested in from the "Wax Type" dropdown list.
5. Choose the areas to be waxed from the "Areas to Wax" dropdown list.
6. If you have any medical contraindications or allergies, please enter them in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. Enter your desired appointment date and time in the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
*   **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach you.
*   **Wax Type** (select one, required): Choose the type of waxing service you are interested in:
    + Brazilian
    + Bikini
    + Eyebrow
    + Lip
    + Eyelashes
*   **Areas to Wax** (select multiple, required): Choose the areas you would like to wax:
    + Face
    + Bikini
    + Underarms
    + Eyebrows
    + Lips
    + Feet
    + Toes
*   **Wax Contraindications** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns related to waxing, please describe them here.
*   **Allergies** (text, optional): If you have any allergies, please describe them here.
*   **Medical Condition** (select one, optional): Are you currently experiencing any medical conditions that may impact your waxing experience?
    + False
    + True
*   **Appointment Date** (date, required): Choose a date for your appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (date, required): Choose a time for your appointment.
*   **Notes** (text, required): Please enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth appointment process.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   A completed form should take no more than 10-15 minutes to fill out.
*   Please review this guide and contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
