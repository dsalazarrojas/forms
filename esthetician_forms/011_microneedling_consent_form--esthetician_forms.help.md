# Microneedling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients undergoing microneedling treatments. Microneedling is a non-surgical skin treatment that uses a specialized roller device to stimulate collagen production, improve skin texture, and reduce fine lines and wrinkles. The form is used to gather information about the patient's medical history, contact details, and consent to the treatment. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe and successful treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name** and **Procedure Date** in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief **Medical History**, including any previous treatments or health conditions that may be relevant to the microneedling treatment.
3. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your phone number and email address.
4. Select your choice for **Signature** (Yes or No).
5. Fill in your **Patient Statement**, a brief statement confirming your understanding and consent to the treatment.
6. Select your **Physician Signature** (Yes or No).
7. Enter your **Date** and **Time** of the treatment.
8. List the **Assigned Doctor** (Yes or No).
9. Fill in your **Patient Signature** (Yes or No).
10. Enter the **Physician Name**.
11. Fill in the **Medical Group** where you are a member.
12. Enter your **Address** and **Phone Number**.
13. Provide your **Physician's Contact Information**, including their email and address.
14. Enter your **Medical Board** and **License Number**.
15. Fill in your **Physician's License State**.
16. Enter the **Assigned Doctor** (Yes or No).
17. Fill in your **Patient Signature** (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government identification.
* **Procedure Date**: Enter the date you will be undergoing the microneedling treatment.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief statement of your medical history, including any previous treatments or health conditions.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Signature**: Select "Yes" to confirm your understanding and consent to the treatment.
* **Patient Statement**: Enter a brief statement confirming your understanding and consent to the treatment.
* **Physician Signature**: Select "Yes" to confirm your physician's understanding and consent to the treatment.
* **Date**: Enter the date and time of the treatment.
* **Assigned Doctor**: Select "Yes" if your treatment is assigned to a specific doctor, otherwise "No".
* **Patient Signature**: Select "Yes" to confirm your understanding and consent to the treatment.
* **Physician Name**: Enter your physician's name.
* **Medical Group**: Enter the medical group where you are a member.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Physician's Contact Information**: Enter your physician's contact information, including their email and address.
* **Medical Board**: Enter your medical board.
* **License Number**: Enter your license number.
* **Physician's License State**: Enter your physician's license state.
* **Assigned Doctor**: Select "Yes" if your treatment is assigned to a specific doctor, otherwise "No".
* **Patient Signature**: Select "Yes" to confirm your understanding and consent to the treatment.

Note: Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues with your treatment.
