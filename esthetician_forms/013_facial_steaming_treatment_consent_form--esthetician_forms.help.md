# Facial Steaming Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Steaming Treatment Consent Form is used to obtain consent from clients for a facial steaming treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This section is where you will provide your name and contact information.
    * Type your name and contact information in the blank space.
* **Medical History**: This section is where you will list any relevant medical history that the esthetician should be aware of.
    * List any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies in this space.
* **Allergies**: This section is where you will list any allergies that you may have.
    * List any allergies, such as latex or fragrances.
* **Medical Conditions**: This section is where you will list any medical conditions that you have.
    * List any conditions that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Consent**: This section is where you will indicate your consent to receive the facial steaming treatment.
    * Select "Yes" to indicate your consent.
* **Client Signature**: This section is where you will sign your name to indicate your agreement to the treatment.
    * Sign your name and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Esthetician Information**: This section is where you will provide information about the esthetician performing the treatment.
    * Type the name and contact information of the esthetician in this space.
* **Form Completion**: This section is where you will indicate if you have completed the form accurately and honestly.
    * Select "Yes" to indicate that you have completed the form accurately and honestly.
* **Medical Notes**: This section is where you will provide any additional medical notes that the esthetician should be aware of.
    * Type any additional medical notes that are relevant to the treatment.
* **Client Signature 1**: This section is where you will sign your name to confirm your agreement to the treatment.
    * Sign your name and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Review each section carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask the esthetician performing the treatment.
