# Grooming Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their grooming appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select a date for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" field.
4. Choose a time for your appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.
5. Select the type of service you require from the list in the "Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (Text)
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (Email)
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates about your appointment.

### Appointment Date (Date)
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_datetime`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your appointment.

### Appointment Time (Time)
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for your appointment.

### Service (Select One)
* **Service** (`service_required`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the list:
	+ Hair Cut
	+ Nail Service
	+ Massage
	+ Facial

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" field (not shown here, but available in the full form).
