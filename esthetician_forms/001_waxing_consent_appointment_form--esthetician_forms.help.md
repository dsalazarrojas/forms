# Waxing Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and consent for waxing services. It ensures that clients provide their name, phone number, and service selection, as well as their consent to the service. The form also allows clients to select a date and time for their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Select the type of waxing service you would like to receive.
3. Enter your name and phone number in the provided fields.
4. Choose the date and time for your appointment.
5. Select the areas of your body where you would like to receive the service.
6. Confirm your consent to the service.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.
8. Finally, sign the form to confirm your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Date** (`form_2`, date, required): Choose the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Select Time** (`form_3`, time, required): Choose the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Select Service** (`form_4`, select_one, required): Choose the type of waxing service you would like to receive (Waxing Hair Removal, Shaving Hair Removal, or Other).
* **Client Name** (`form_5`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Phone** (`form_6`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Service Area** (`form_7`, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas of your body where you would like to receive the service (Face, Back, Legs, Underarms).
* **Consent** (`form_8`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you consent to the service by selecting the corresponding option.
* **Notes** (`form_9`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Sign** (`form_10`, text, required): Sign the form to confirm your appointment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate information and select the correct services to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us before or after completing the form.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to serving you!
