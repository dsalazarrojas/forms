# Police Jobs Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for police officers or individuals who wish to apply for a job within the police department. It captures information about the applicant, including personal details, background check status, and relevant experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your background check status: "Cleared," "Pending," or "Failed" in the "Background Check Status" field.
5. Enter your police certificate number in the "Police Certificate Number" field.
6. Enter the years of service for the job title you're applying for in the "Years of Service" field.
7. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
8. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
9. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YY.
10. Enter your social security number in the "Social Security Number" field.
11. If you're a current or former police officer, enter your police ID number in the "Police ID Number" field.
12. Select your job title from the options provided in the "Job Title" field.
13. Select the department you're applying for from the options provided in the "Department" field.
14. Select the location where you'd like to work from the options provided in the "Location" field.
15. Enter the reason for your application in the "Reason for Applying" field.
16. Upload any supporting documents in the "Upload Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, required/optional): Enter your home or work address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check_status`, required/optional): Select whether you've cleared, pending, or failed a background check.
* **Police Certificate Number** (`police_certificate_number`, required/optional): Enter your police certificate number if applicable.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, required/optional): Enter the years of service for the job title you're applying for.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, required/optional): Enter your social security number.
* **Police ID Number** (`police_id_number`, required/optional): Enter your police ID number if you are a current or former police officer.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required/optional): Select the job title you're applying for from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`, required/optional): Select the department you're applying for from the options provided.
* **Location** (`location`, required/optional): Select the location where you'd like to work from the options provided.
* **Reason for Applying** (`reason_for_applying`, required/optional): Enter the reason for your application.
* **Upload Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, required/optional): Upload any supporting documents for your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required/optional): Click the submit button to complete your application.
