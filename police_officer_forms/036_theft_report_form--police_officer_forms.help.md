# Theft Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a template for reporting a theft. Its purpose is to collect information about the theft, including details about what happened, where it happened, and who was involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input Form** section with a brief description of what was stolen and what happened.
2. Provide a detailed description of the **Location** where the theft occurred.
3. Enter the **Date Of Loss** and **Time Of Loss** when the theft happened.
4. In the **Witness Details** section, provide any information about any witnesses, including their names, contact information, and a brief description of what they saw.
5. Select the **Police Officer** who will be handling the case from the list of options.
6. You can add any additional **Other Details** about the incident in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input Form** (required): Describe what was stolen and what happened.
* **Description** (required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Location** (required): Describe the location where the theft occurred.
* **Date Of Loss** (required): Enter the date when the theft happened.
* **Time Of Loss** (required): Enter the time when the theft happened.
* **Witness Details** (required): Provide any information about any witnesses, including their names, contact information, and a brief description of what they saw.
* **Police Officer** (required): Select the police officer who will be handling the case from the list of options.
* **Other Details** (optional): Add any additional information about the incident that you think is relevant.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling in the form.
* If you have any supporting evidence or documentation, such as photos or videos, attach them to the form.
* If you are reporting a theft that occurred in a public place, make sure to include the exact location, such as the name of the street or store.
* If you are reporting a theft that occurred at a private residence, make sure to include the address of the location.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, ask for help or clarification from the police department.
