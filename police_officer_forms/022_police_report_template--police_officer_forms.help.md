# police_report_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The police report template is used to document and report incidents to the relevant authorities. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the incident is well-documented and that the relevant information is collected.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Incident Description field with a detailed description of what happened.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the Incident Date field.
3. Select the Suspect's Name and Suspect's Age from the respective dropdown menus.
4. If available, fill in the Witness Name field with the name of any witnesses.
5. Enter the Witness Address field if the witness is known.
6. Sign and date the form with your signature and the current date in the Officer Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Description (1)**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Date (2)**: The date of the incident.
* **Suspect Name (3)**: The name of the person involved in the incident.
* **Suspect Age (4)**: The age of the person involved in the incident. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Witness Name (5)**: The name of any witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Address (6)**: The address of any witnesses to the incident.
* **Officer Signature (7)**: Your signature, which is required to confirm that you have filled out this form accurately.
