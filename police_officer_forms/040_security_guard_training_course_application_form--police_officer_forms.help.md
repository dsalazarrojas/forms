# Security Guard Training Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by security guards to apply for a training course. It is designed to collect necessary information for the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your personal details in the form.
2. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your date of birth to verify your identity.
4. Fill in your security guard license number, if applicable.
5. Specify the type of training course you are interested in.
6. Enter the details of your emergency contact, including their name and phone number.
7. Select the relationship with your emergency contact.
8. Enter the emergency contact's email address.
9. Select the start and end dates of the training course.
10. Enter the address of the training facility.
11. Enter the cost of the training course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to verify your identity.
* **Security Guard License Number** (`security_guard_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your security guard license number, if applicable.
* **Training Course Type** (`training_course_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of training course you are interested in (Basic, Advanced, or Refresher).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Training Course Start Date** (`training_course_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training course.
* **Training Course End Date** (`training_course_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training course.
* **Training Facility Address** (`training_facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the training facility.
* **Training Course Cost** (`training_course_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the training course.
