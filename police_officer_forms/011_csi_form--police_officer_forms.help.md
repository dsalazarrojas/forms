# CSI Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSI Form is a document used by police officers to report details about an incident, including their involvement and any evidence collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the CSI Form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information about the incident and your involvement.
* Fill in the required fields (marked as required) with accurate and detailed information.
* For fields with options, select the correct option that best reflects your response.
* Leave optional fields blank if you don't have any additional information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a police officer, as it appears on your badge or personnel records.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required): Enter the department- assigned case ID or case number for the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location of Scene** (`location_of_scene`, text, required): Enter the address or GPS reference of the incident location.
* **Evidence Collected** (`evidence_collected`, text, required): Briefly describe any evidence collected from the scene, such as items taken or seized.
* **Chain of Custody Confirmed** (`chain_of_custody`, select_one, required): Confirm if procedures were followed for the chain of custody of evidence.
* **Photos Taken at Scene** (`photos_taken`, number, optional): If photos were taken at the scene, enter the number of photos taken.
* **Additional Investigative Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional observations or witness details that may be relevant to the case.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure a clear and complete record of the incident.
* Review and revise your responses before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or information, consult with your supervisor or colleagues for guidance.
