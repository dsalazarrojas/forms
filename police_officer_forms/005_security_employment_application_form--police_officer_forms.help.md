# Security Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Employment Application Form is used to collect information from external candidates applying for security employment with the police department. The form helps the hiring team to verify the candidate's identity, employment history, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6. Select your **Job Title** from the available options in the "Job Title" field.
7. Choose your **Employment Status** by selecting one or more of the available options (Police Officer, Security Guard, Security Officer) in the "Employment Status" field.
8. Enter the number of **Job Experience in Years** you have in the "Job Experience in Years" field.
9. Select the **Date Hired** for your current or previous employment in the "Date Hired" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your current address or the address where you can be contacted.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title you are applying for (e.g., Security Officer, Police Officer).
* **Employment Status**: Select one or more of the available options that describe your current or previous employment status (e.g., Police Officer, Security Guard, Security Officer).
* **Job Experience in Years**: Enter the total number of years you have worked in the selected job title.
* **Date Hired**: Select the date you were hired for your current or previous employment.
