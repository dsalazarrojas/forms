# Offense Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions provided for each field.
2.  Ensure that you provide all required information.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Report Form (1)**: Enter a brief description of the incident. This field is used to capture a summary of the incident.
*   **Offense Date (2)**: Enter the date of the incident. Please use the format: MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of Incident (3)**: Enter the time of the incident. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Location (4)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Suspect Name (5)**: Enter the name of the suspect involved in the incident.
*   **Suspect ID (6)**: Enter the suspect's ID number.
*   **Officer Name (7)**: Enter your name.
*   **Officer ID (8)**: Enter your ID number.
*   **Incident Details (9)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
*   If you need help or have questions, refer to your supervisor or the relevant departmental resources.
*   Review your work carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
