## Sheriff Candidate Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sheriff Candidate Petition form is used to collect information from individuals who support or endorse a sheriff candidate. This form helps the sheriff department to gather data on supporters, their contact information, and reasons for supporting the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred **Party** from the available options in the "Party" field.
6. Choose the **Candidate(s)** you support from the available options in the "Candidate" field.
7. Enter the **Position** you are supporting the candidate for in the "Position" field.
8. List the members of your **Party** in the "Party Members" field.
9. Enter a brief **Reason** for why you support the candidate in the "Reason" field.
10. Select the **Support** options that apply to you from the available options in the "Support" field.
11. Choose your **Sign** option from the available options in the "Sign" field.
12. Enter the date and time you signed this form in the "Date SIGNED" and "Time SIGNED" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Party** (Select One, Optional): Select the party you are affiliated with or support.
* **Candidate** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the candidate(s) you support for the position.
* **Position** (Text, Optional): Enter the position you are supporting the candidate for.
* **Party Members** (Text, Optional): List the members of your party, including their first and last names.
* **Reason** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief reason for why you support the candidate.
* **Support** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the types of support you can provide, such as volunteer or donation.
* **Sign** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you want to sign this petition, such as digitally or in person.
* **Date SIGNED** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time SIGNED** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you signed this form.

## Tips
* Ensure you are not duplicating a previously submitted entry.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use the "Select Multiple" and "Select One" fields as instructed.
