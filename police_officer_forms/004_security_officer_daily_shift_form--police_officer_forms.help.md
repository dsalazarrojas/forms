# Security Officer Daily Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Officer Daily Shift Form is used to record and document the daily activities and incidents of security officers. This form helps to ensure that security officers are accurately reporting their daily shift, including any incidents or issues encountered during their shift. The form is designed to be a quick and efficient way to document and report daily activities, reducing the need for additional paperwork or lengthy reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your officer information in the first page of the form.
2. Select your officer rank and unit from the corresponding dropdown menus.
3. Enter the date and time you started and ended your shift.
4. Record any notes or incidents encountered during your shift in the shift notes section.
5. Report any incidents that occurred during your shift, including their description and any relevant details.
6. Select your current status (active or inactive) and add any comments if necessary.
7. Add any comments or feedback from your supervisor.
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Record any security incidents that occurred during your shift, including their description and any relevant details.
