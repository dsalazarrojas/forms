# Police Officer Interview Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from police officers about their interview experience. It includes questions about the interviewer's performance, the candidate's feedback, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required fields. Make sure to provide feedback about your experience, rating, and comments about the form and interview process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the interviewer.
* **Rating** (`interview_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback about your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments about your interview experience.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you are working in.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the agency you are working for.
* **Interviewer Performance** (`interviewer_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's performance, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Courtesy** (`interviewer_courtesy`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's courtesy, from Excellent to Very Poor.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the interviewer's communication skills, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Knowledge Skills** (`knowledge_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the interviewer's knowledge skills, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Preparation** (`preparation`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's preparation, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's time management, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's professionalism, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's punctuality, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Attitude** (`attitude`, select_one, optional): Rate the interviewer's attitude, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Feedback to Candidate** (`feedback_to_candidate`, select_multiple, optional): Rate how well you were given feedback about your application, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Post-Interview Process** (`post_interview_process`, select_one, optional): Rate the post-interview process, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Comments about Form** (`comments_about_form`, text, optional): Share any comments about the form itself.
* **Comments about Process** (`comments_about_process`, text, optional): Share any comments about the interview process.

Note: All fields are optional and can be filled in at your discretion.
