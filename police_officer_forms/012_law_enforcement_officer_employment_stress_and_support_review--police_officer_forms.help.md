# Law enforcement officer employment stress and support review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from law enforcement officers about their stress and support experiences in their workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and select the options that apply to your situation.
2. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
3. Make sure to save your progress and come back to the form if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stressors** (Select multiple): 
  * This question asks about the types of stressors you experience in your work.
  * Examples include: 
    + High workload
    + Unclear expectations
    + Lack of support
    + Physical or mental health issues
  * Choose all that apply.
* **Support services** (Select one): 
  * This question asks about the types of support services you currently use or would like to use.
  * Options include: 
    + Counseling
    + Mental health resources
    + Physical fitness programs
    + Team-building activities
    + Mental health services
    + Wellness programs
* **Support resources** (Select one):

* **Retention efforts** (Select one):

* **Stress and support** (Note):
