# Church Security Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the security features and procedures of a church's facilities. It will help identify potential threats, gaps, and vulnerabilities, and provide recommendations for improvements to increase the church's overall security posture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the facility information, including the church's name and address.
2.  Assess the security features of the church, including main entrances, emergency exits, exterior lighting, surveillance cameras, and access control systems.
3.  Evaluate the church's emergency response plan, staff security procedures, and emergency communication system.
4.  Identify any known threat concerns and provide a description.
5.  Provide recommended security improvements and indicate their priority level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Church Name** (ID: 2): Enter the full name of the church.
* **Church Address** (ID: 3): Enter the full address of the church, including the street address and any additional information.
* **Assessment Date** (ID: 4): Enter the date the assessment was conducted.
* **Assessor Name** (ID: 5): Enter your name and title.
* **Assessor Title** (ID: 6): Enter your title or position.
* **Main Entrances Have Controlled Access** (ID: 8): Answer whether the main entrances of the church have controlled access. Choose 'Yes' if they do, 'No' if they don't, or 'Partial' if they have some level of control.
* **Emergency Exits Are Properly Marked and Accessible** (ID: 9): Answer whether the emergency exits of the church are properly marked and accessible. Choose 'Yes' if they are, 'No' if they aren't, or 'Partial' if they have some level of accessibility.
* **Adequate Exterior Lighting in Parking Areas** (ID: 10): Answer whether the parking areas of the church have adequate exterior lighting. Choose 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Adequate', 'Poor', or 'Very Poor'.
* **Surveillance Cameras Installed** (ID: 11): Answer whether surveillance cameras are installed in the church. Choose 'Yes' if they are, 'No' if they aren't, or 'Partial' if they have some level of coverage.
* **Areas with Camera Coverage** (ID: 12): Describe the areas covered by the surveillance cameras.
* **Building Access Controlled and Monitored** (ID: 14): Answer whether the church's building is accessed and monitored. Choose 'Yes' if it is, 'No' if it isn't, or 'Partial' if it has some level of control.
* **Key Card or Electronic Lock System** (ID: 15): Answer whether a key card or electronic lock system is used for access control. Choose 'Yes' if it is, 'No' if it isn't.
* **Key Management and Control Procedures in Place** (ID: 16): Answer whether key management and control procedures are in place. Choose 'Yes' if they are, 'No' if they aren't, or 'Needs Improvement'.
* **Security Staff or Trained Greeters Present During Events** (ID: 18): Answer whether security staff or trained greeters are present during events. Choose 'Always', 'Usually', 'Sometimes', 'Rarely', or 'Never'.
* **Background Checks for All Staff and Volunteers** (ID: 19): Answer whether background checks are performed on all staff and volunteers. Choose 'Yes' if they are, 'No' if they aren't, or 'Partial'.
* **Written Emergency Response Plan** (ID: 21): Answer whether a written emergency response plan is in place. Choose 'Yes' if it is, 'No' if it isn't, or 'In Development'.
* **Regular Emergency Drills Conducted** (ID: 22): Answer whether regular emergency drills are conducted. Choose 'Monthly', 'Quarterly', 'Annually', or 'Never'.
* **Emergency Communication System** (ID: 23): Answer whether an emergency communication system is in place. Choose 'Yes' if it is, 'No' if it isn't, or 'Partial'.
* **Known Threat Concerns** (ID: 25): Answer whether there are any known threat concerns. Choose 'Yes' if there are, 'No' if there aren't, or 'Uncertain'.
* **Description of Threat Concerns** (ID: 26): Describe any known threat concerns.
* **Identified Security Gaps or Vulnerabilities** (ID: 28): Describe any identified security gaps or vulnerabilities.
* **Recommended Security Improvements** (ID: 29): Suggest any recommended security improvements.
* **Priority Level for Improvements** (ID: 30): Choose the priority level for any recommended improvements.
