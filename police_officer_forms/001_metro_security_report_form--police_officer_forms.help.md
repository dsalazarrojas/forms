# Metro Security Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metro Security Report Form is designed to collect information about security incidents or crimes that have occurred in the Metro area. By filling out this form, you can provide crucial details that help law enforcement agencies respond and investigate the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above, including entering the suspect's information, crime details, and witness information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): Enter a brief header for the report.
* **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`, text, optional): Enter the suspect's name.
* **Suspect Address** (`suspect_address`, text, optional): Enter the suspect's address.
* **Crime Date** (`crime_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the crime occurred.
* **Crime Time** (`crime_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the crime occurred.
* **Crime Location** (`crime_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the crime took place.
* **Crime Description** (`crime_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the crime.
* **Suspect Demeanor** (`suspect_demeanor`, select_one, optional): Specify the suspect's demeanor during the crime (e.g., Aggressive, Belligerent, Combative, Hostile, Passive, or Sedate).
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, text, optional): Enter the witness's address.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, optional): Enter the witness's phone number.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Title** (`reporting_officer_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as the reporting officer.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the report was submitted.
