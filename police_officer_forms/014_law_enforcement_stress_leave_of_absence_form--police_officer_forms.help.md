# Law Enforcement Stress Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Law Enforcement Stress Leave Of Absance Form is used to record and report the need for an officer to take a leave of absence due to stress. This form is essential for law enforcement agencies to track and manage officer's absences related to stress, ensuring that the officer's well-being and work performance are not compromised.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill in the required information in the corresponding fields.
*   Start by filling out the form in order, from the first page to the last.
*   Double-check that all the required fields are completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the officer taking the leave of absence.
*   **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, select_one, optional): Choose the officer who is reporting the need for the leave of absence. If no one is available, leave this field blank.
*   **Date Start** (`date_start`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the leave of absence. If the exact date is not known, enter the earliest possible date.
*   **Date End** (`date_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the leave of absence. If the exact date is not known, enter the latest possible date.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for the officer's stress-related leave of absence.
*   **Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be useful for the reviewing officer.
*   **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_one, optional): Choose the officer who will review the form.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the officer submitting the form.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department of the officer taking the leave of absence.
*   **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor who will be informed of the officer's absence.

## Tips

*   Before filling out the form, ensure that you have discussed the officer's stress-related absence with them and have their consent.
*   If the officer's stress-related absence is due to a work-related incident, be sure to include details of the incident in the "Details" field.
*   The form should be kept confidential and should only be accessed by authorized personnel.
