# Consent To Search Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from a person for a police officer to conduct a search on their person, belongings, or property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to sign the form in the presence of the person giving consent.
3. Ensure that the person understands their rights and has given their consent freely and voluntarily.
4. If an interpreter is used, record their name and language used.
5. If a witness is present, record their name and badge number.
6. If the person has any limitations on their consent, document them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`consent_date`): The date on which the person gave their consent.
* **Time** (`consent_time`): The time on which the person gave their consent.
* **Location** (`location`): The location where the person gave their consent.
* **Person Giving Consent** (`person_name`): The full name of the person giving consent.
* **Date of Birth** (`person_dob`): The date of birth of the person giving consent.
* **Address** (`person_address`): The current address of the person giving consent.
* **Phone Number** (`person_phone`): The contact phone number of the person giving consent (optional).
* **Identification Provided** (`person_id_description`): A description of the type of identification provided.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`): The number on the person's identification.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`): The name of the police officer conducting the search.
* **Officer Badge Number** (`officer_badge_number`): The badge number of the police officer conducting the search.
* **Police Department** (`officer_department`): The police department or agency conducting the search.
* **Case or Incident Number** (`case_number`): The related case or incident number (optional).
* **Reason for Requested Search** (`reason_for_search`): The purpose of the search.
* **Scope of Search** (`scope_of_search`): The areas or items that may be searched (choose all that apply).
* **Scope Details** (`scope_details`): A description of specific areas or items that may be searched.
* **Were Rights Advised** (`rights_advised`): Confirmation that the person was informed of their rights.
* **Rights Advisement Details** (`rights_details`): A description of what rights were explained.
* **Is Consent Given Voluntarily** (`voluntary_consent`): Confirmation that the person's consent is voluntary.
* **Was Any Coercion Used** (`coercion_present`): Confirmation of whether coercion was used.
* **Were Any Threats Made** (`threats_made`): Confirmation of whether threats were made.
* **Were Any Promises Made** (`promises_made`): Confirmation of whether improper promises were made.
* **Does Person Understand** (`person_understanding`): Confirmation that the person understands their rights.
* **Was Interpreter Used** (`interpreter_used`): Confirmation that a translator was used.
* **Interpreter Name** (`interpreter_name`): The name of the interpreter (optional).
* **Language** (`language`): The language used for communication (optional).
* **Was Witness Present** (`witness_present`): Confirmation that a witness was present.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): The name of the witness (optional).
* **Witness Badge Number** (`witness_badge`): The badge number of the witness (optional).
* **Consent Decision** (`consent_decision`): The decision to consent or not.
* **Any Limitations on Consent** (`limitations`): Any restrictions or limitations on the consent.
* **Person Signature** (`person_signature`): The signature of the person giving consent.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`): The signature of the police officer conducting the search.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): The signature of the witness (optional).

Note:
This form is designed to ensure that the person giving consent understands their rights and has given their consent freely and voluntarily. The officer and witness (if present) are also required to sign the form as confirmation of the consent process.
