# Jail Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jail Release Form is used to document the release of individuals from jail or custody.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
* Fill in the release date and time, specifying the exact date and time the individual will be released.
* Provide the release location, where the individual will be released.
* Describe the reason for the release, including any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date): Enter the date the individual will be released from jail or custody.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time): Specify the exact time the individual will be released.
* **Release Location** (`release_location`, text): Enter the location where the individual will be released.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text): Describe the reason for the release, including any relevant details.

## Tips
* Double-check the release date and time to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for the release.
* If the release location is not fixed, consider leaving it blank or providing an alternative location.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant authorities before completing the form.
