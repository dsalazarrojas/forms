# police_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The police complaint form is a general-purpose document used to report crimes, incidents, or other matters related to law enforcement. It is designed to help the authorities gather relevant information to investigate and address the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the space provided.
3. Fill in the name of the person involved (victim, suspect, or witness).
4. If you are a witness, provide your contact information so that the authorities can reach out to you for further questions.
5. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
6. Provide the date and time of the incident.
7. If you are a witness, provide a statement about what you saw or experienced.
8. Enter any additional contact information for the witness, if applicable.
9. Fill in the name and badge number of the responding police officer (if applicable).
10. Write a brief report of the incident in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu. Choose one of the following options: Police, Theft, Assault, Suspicious, or Other.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details such as names, dates, times, and locations.
* **Victim Name** (`victim_name`): If you are reporting on behalf of the victim, please fill in their name.
* **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`): If you know the name of the suspect, please fill it in. If not, leave it blank.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): If you are a witness, fill in your name so that the authorities can contact you.
* **Witness Contact** (`witness_contact`): If you are a witness, provide your contact information (email, phone number, etc.) so that the authorities can reach out to you for further questions.
* **Crime Location** (`crime_location`): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Crime Date** (`crime_date`): Provide the date of the incident.
* **Crime Time** (`crime_time`): Provide the time of the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`): If you are a witness, provide a statement about what you saw or experienced.
* **Witness Contact Info** (`witness_contact_info`): If you are a witness, provide any additional contact information for the authorities to reach out to you.
* **Police Officer Name** (`police_officer_name`): Fill in the name of the responding police officer (if applicable).
* **Police Officer Badge Number** (`police_officer_badge_number`): Fill in the badge number of the responding police officer (if applicable).
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`): Write a brief report of the incident, summarizing the essential details.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information.
- Ensure that the fields are accurate and truthful.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the relevant authorities directly for clarification.
- This form is for general purposes and may not be specific to your local police department's procedures or requirements.
- If you have already submitted a similar form for the same incident, please do not repeat the effort, as it may cause confusion and redundancy in the investigation process.
