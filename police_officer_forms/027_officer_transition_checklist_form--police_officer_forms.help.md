# Officer Transition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Officer Transition Checklist Form is used to document the details of an officer's transition within the police department. It helps ensure a smooth transfer of responsibilities and duties between officers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the date of the transition from the calendar input field.
2. Enter the name of the current officer, as well as the name of the officer taking over.
3. Provide a brief description of the transition details in the "Transition Details" field.
4. Enter the phone numbers of both the current and next officers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transition Date** (`transition_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transition.
* **Current Officer Name** (`current_officer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the officer currently handling the duties.
* **Next Officer Name** (`next_officer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the officer taking over the duties.
* **Transition Details** (`transition_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the key points of the transition, including any specific tasks or responsibilities that need to be addressed.
* **Current Officer Phone** (`current_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the current officer.
* **Next Officer Phone** (`next_officer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the officer taking over.
