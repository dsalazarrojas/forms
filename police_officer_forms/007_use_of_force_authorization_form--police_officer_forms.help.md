# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive report on incidents and suspects involved in the incident. This form is designed to collect information from police officers in the field, providing a clear and concise way to document the details of each incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the incident in the "Form Title" field.
2. Enter the form ID number in the "Form ID" field.
3. Enter the name of the officer completing the form in the "Officer Name" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Time of Day" fields.
5. Enter the name of the suspect(s) involved in the incident in the "Suspect Name" field or select from the "Suspect Other Names" field.
6. Select the force level of the incident from the "Force Level" field.
7. Enter any details about the incident in the "Description" field.
8. List any witnesses who witnessed the incident in the "Witnesses" field or select from the "Witness Other" field.
9. If a vehicle was involved in the incident, select the type of vehicle from the "Vehicle Involved" field.
10. Select the type of vehicle used by the suspect from the "Suspect Vehicle" field.
11. Identify the officer(s) involved in the incident in the "Officer Involved" field or select from the "Officer Other" field.
12. Select the type of weapons used in the incident from the "Weapons Used" field.
13. Select any injuries sustained by the suspect in the "Suspect Injuries" field.
14. Select any injuries sustained by the officer in the incident in the "Officer Injuries" field.
15. Identify any other factors involved in the incident in the "Other Involves" field.
16. Select any resistances or difficulties encountered by the suspect during the incident in the "Suspect Resistances" field.
17. Select any resistances or difficulties encountered by the officer during the incident in the "Officer Resistances" field.
18. Identify any other resistances or difficulties encountered by the suspect during the incident in the "Suspect Other Resistances" field.
19. Identify any other resistances or difficulties encountered by the officer during the incident in the "Officer Other Resistances" field.
20. Select any other involves or factors involved in the incident in the "Suspect Involves" field.
21. Select any other involves or factors involved in the incident in the "Suspect Other Involves" field.
