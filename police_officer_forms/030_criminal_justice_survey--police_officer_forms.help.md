# Criminal Justice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Criminal Justice Survey is a form designed to collect information about criminal justice incidents. This form aims to gather data on crime types, police responses, and other relevant details to help law enforcement agencies understand crime patterns and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
* Select the crime type that best describes the incident (Crime Type field).
* Provide a brief description of the crime scene, including any relevant details (Crime Details field).
* If applicable, describe the police response to the incident (Police Response field).
* Describe the impact of the crime on the victims or community (Impact field).
* Provide details about the victim(s) involved, including their description and any other relevant information (Victim Details field).
* Describe the suspect(s), including their physical appearance and any other relevant information (Suspect Description field).
* Specify the location where the incident occurred (Location field).
* Describe any witness(es) who saw or experienced the incident (Witness Description field).
* Provide a detailed statement from the witness(es) regarding the incident (Witness Statement field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crime Type** (Select One: Murder, Assault, Theft): Select the type of crime that best describes the incident.
* **Crime Details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the crime scene, including any relevant details.
* **Police Response** (Text): If applicable, describe the police response to the incident.
* **Impact** (Text): Describe the impact of the crime on the victims or community.
* **Victim Details** (Text): Provide details about the victim(s), including their description and any other relevant information.
* **Suspect Description** (Text): Describe the suspect(s), including their physical appearance and any other relevant information.
* **Location** (Text): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness Description** (Text): Describe any witness(es) who saw or experienced the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (Text): Provide a detailed statement from the witness(es) regarding the incident.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and concise as possible when providing information.
* Ensure all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* This form is intended for collecting data for research and analysis purposes only.
