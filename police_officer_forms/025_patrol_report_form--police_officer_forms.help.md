# Patrol Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patrol Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur during police patrol activities. This form helps police officers document details about the incident, their unit, and any suspects involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all required fields**. Each field with a red asterisk (\*) is mandatory.
2. **Select relevant options** for each field where applicable.
3. **Provide clear and concise answers** to the questions.

## Tips
* Always provide accurate and clear information.
* If unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or a colleague.
* Review and verify the form before submission.
* Use the form to document all incidents, including those that may not be reported to the central authorities.
