# illegal_fireworks_reporting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for reporting incidents involving fireworks-related activities in a police jurisdiction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Report Details section with the date and time the incident occurred.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
3. If applicable, provide information about the suspect(s) involved, including their distinguishing features.
4. If applicable, provide information about the witness(es) involved, including their age, gender, eye color, and any other relevant details.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details**: 
	+ (1) **Report Details** (`report_details`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date**:
	+ (2) **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional): The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**:
	+ (3) **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional): The time the incident occurred.
* **Location**:
	+ (4) **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Description**:
	+ (5) **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): A detailed description of what happened.
* **Suspect Information**:
	+ (6) **Suspect Information** (`suspect_info`, `text`, optional): Any information about the suspect(s) involved.
* **Suspect Vehicle**:
	+ (7) **Suspect Vehicle** (`suspect_vehicle`, `text`, optional): Any information about the suspect vehicle.
* **Witness Information**:
	+ (8) **Witness Information** (`witness_info`, `text`, optional): Any information about the witness(es) involved.
* **Witness Vehicle**:
	+ (9) **Witness Vehicle** (`witness_vehicle`, `text`, optional): Any information about the witness vehicle.
* **Witness Statement**:
	+ (10) **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `text`, optional): A statement from the witness.
* **Evidence Collected**:
	+ (11) **Evidence Collected** (`evidence_collected`, `text`, optional): Any evidence collected related to the incident.
* **Suspect Distinguishing Feature**:
	+ (12) **Suspect Distinguishing Feature** (`suspect_distinguishing_feature`, `text`, optional): Any distinguishing features of the suspect.
* **Other Suspect Distinguishing Feature**:
	+ (13) **Other Suspect Distinguishing Feature** (`suspect_distinguishing_feature_other`, `text`, optional): Any other distinguishing features of the suspect.
* **Suspect Age**:
	+ (14) **Suspect Age** (`suspect_age`, `number`, optional): The age of the suspect.
* **Suspect Gender**:
	+ (15) **Suspect Gender** (`suspect_gender`, `select_one`, required): The gender of the suspect.
* **Suspect Eye Color**:
	+ (16) **Suspect Eye Color** (`suspect_eye_color`, `select_multiple`, optional): The eye color of the suspect.
* **Suspect Hat Worn**:
	+ (17) **Suspect Hat Worn** (`suspect_hat_worn`, `select_multiple`, optional): Whether the suspect was wearing a hat.
* **Suspect Gloves Worn**:
	+ (18) **Suspect Gloves Worn** (`suspect_gloves_worn`, `select_multiple`, optional): Whether the suspect was wearing gloves.
* **Suspect Scars**:
	+ (19) **Suspect Scars** (`suspect_scars`, `select_multiple`, optional): Whether the suspect had any scars.
* **Witness Age**:
	+ (20) **Witness Age** (`witness_age`, `number`, optional): The age of the witness.
* **Witness Gender**:
	+ (21) **Witness Gender** (`witness_gender`, `select_one`, optional): The gender of the witness.
* **Witness Eye Color**:
	+ (22) **Witness Eye Color** (`witness_eye_color`, `select_multiple`, optional): The eye color of the witness.
* **Witness Hat Worn**:
	+ (23) **Witness Hat Worn** (`witness_hat_worn`, `select_multiple`, optional): Whether the witness was wearing a hat.
* **Witness Gloves Worn**:
	+ (24) **Witness Gloves Worn** (`witness_gloves_worn`, `select_multiple`, optional): Whether the witness was wearing gloves.
* **Officer Signature**:
	+ (25) **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, `text`, optional): The signature of the officer completing the form.

## Tips

* This form is intended for reporting incidents involving fireworks-related activities.
* Be as thorough as possible when completing the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact your supervisor or a colleague for guidance.
