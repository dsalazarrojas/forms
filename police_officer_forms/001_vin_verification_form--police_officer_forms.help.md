# vin_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by police officers to verify the details of a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the vehicle information section, including the VIN, make, year, color, model, and license plate.
2. Indicate the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive) and whether it is a vehicle you are currently using (Yes or No).
3. Check the vehicle's status (Active or Inactive).
4. Choose whether the vehicle has an owner (Yes or No).
5. Choose whether the vehicle has a driver (Yes or No).
6. Choose whether the vehicle has a passenger (Yes or No).
7. Choose whether the vehicle has been manufactured in the current year (Yes or No).
8. Add any additional notes about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_info**: Enter the vehicle's basic information (VIN, make, year, color, model, and license plate).
* **vin**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g. Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **year**: Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **year_manufactured**: Enter whether the vehicle was manufactured in the current year (Yes or No).
* **vin_check**: Check the VIN to verify its accuracy.
* **check_vin_result**: The result of the VIN check (if available).
* **officer_name**: Enter your name and title (e.g. Officer John Doe, Badge Number 123).
* **vehicle_status**: Indicate the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).
* **vehicle_type**: Indicate whether the vehicle is currently being used (Yes or No).
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g. Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **license_plate**: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **vin_status**: Indicate the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).
* **vehicle_owner**: Indicate whether the vehicle has an owner (Yes or No).
* **vehicle_driver**: Indicate whether the vehicle has a driver (Yes or No).
* **vehicle_passenger**: Indicate whether the vehicle has a passenger (Yes or No).
* **vehicle_passenger**: Indicate whether the vehicle has a passenger (Yes or No).
* **notes**: Add any additional notes about the vehicle.
* **contact_email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_notes**: Add any additional contact information or notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the VIN to ensure it is accurate.
* If the vehicle has a complex or unusual make, be sure to research it to ensure the information is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the vehicle's details, do not hesitate to add additional notes to explain your concerns.
* If you need to contact the owner or driver of the vehicle, be sure to use the contact information they provided.
