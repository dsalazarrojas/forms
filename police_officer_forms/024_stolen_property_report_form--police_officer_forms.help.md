# stolen_property_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stolen Property Report Form is used to report a stolen item to the police. This form gathers essential information about the missing item, including its description, location, and any suspect or witness information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible about the missing item.
2. Enter the date and time the property was taken.
3. Specify the location where the property was taken.
4. Provide a description of the property and any relevant details about its value.
5. If applicable, provide information about any suspects or witnesses.
6. Enter your contact information and the police department you are reporting to.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
8. Finally, sign the form with your name and title to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Missing Item** (`missing_item`, text, optional): A brief description of the item that was stolen.
* **Description Of Property** (`description_of_property`, text, optional): A detailed description of the property, including any distinguishing features or marks.
* **Date Taken** (`date_taken`, date, optional): The date the property was taken.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, time, optional): The time the property was taken.
* **Location Taken** (`location_taken`, text, optional): The location where the property was taken.
* **Description Of Loss** (`description_of_loss`, text, optional): A detailed description of the circumstances surrounding the loss or theft.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): The serial number of the property (if applicable).
* **Value Of Property** (`value_property`, number, optional): The estimated value of the property.
* **Description Of Property Details** (`description_of_property_details`, text, optional): Additional details about the property, such as its color, size, or material.
* **Suspect Info** (`suspect_info`, text, optional): Information about the suspect, including their name, description, or last seen location.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, optional): Information from any witness, including their name, description, or account.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, text, optional): The name of the officer filling out the form.
* **Badge Number** (`badge_number`, text, optional): The badge number of the officer filling out the form.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, date, optional): The date the form was submitted.
* **Time Reported** (`time_reported`, time, optional): The time the form was submitted.
* **Location Reported** (`location_reported`, text, optional): The location where the form was submitted.
* **Police Department** (`police_department`, text, optional): The police department receiving the report.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Contact information for the submitter.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, text, optional): The signature of the officer filling out the form.
* **Police Department Logo** (`police_department_logo`, text, optional): The logo of the police department.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The current date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The current time.
* **Officer Notes** (`officer_notes`, text, optional): Notes from the officer filling out the form.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Please confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
