# Firearms Training Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Firearms Training Release Form is a document used by police departments or other organizations to collect demographic information and obtain consent from individuals participating in firearms training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the form, which is also the title of the form.
2. Select a category for the form, choose one of the two options: "Police Officer Forms" or "Other Forms".
3. Enter a brief description of the form, which will help others understand the purpose of the form.
4. Read and agree to the participant agreement section.
5. Provide your participant name and address.
6. Enter the date of the participant.
7. Select a start date for the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firearms Training Release Form** (Page 1, Title):
	+ This is the title of the form, and it's also the title of the form.
* **Category** (Page 1, Category):
	+ Select the category for this form, choose between "Police Officer Forms" or "Other Forms".
* **Description** (Page 1, Description):
	+ Enter a brief description of the form, which will help others understand the purpose of the form.
* **Participant Agreement** (Page 1, Participant Agreement):
	+ This is an agreement that outlines the terms and conditions of the participant's participation.
	+ Please read and agree to this section.
* **Participant Name** (Page 2, Participant Name):
	+ Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Address** (Page 2, Participant Address):
	+ Enter your address as the participant.
* **Date of Participant** (Page 2, Date of Participant):
	+ Enter the date of the participant.
* **Training Start Date** (Page 3, Training Start Date):
	+ Select a start date for the training.

## Tips

- Ensure you complete all required fields to avoid any issues with submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the description or ask the form administrator for assistance.
- Make sure to review the participant agreement carefully before agreeing to it.
- If you need any help or have questions, contact the form administrator: form@gic.mx.
