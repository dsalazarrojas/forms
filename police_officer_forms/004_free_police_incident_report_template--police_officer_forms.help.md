# Free Police Incident Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a basic template for reporting incidents that have occurred during a police operation or in a police precinct. It's intended to gather necessary information about the incident, including the report date, report time, location, and a detailed description of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report date and time of the incident in the "Report Date" and "Report Time" fields.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident location in the "Incident Location" field.
3. Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any relevant facts, circumstances, and observations, in the "Incident Description" field.
4. If there's any additional information about the incident that you'd like to include, such as witness statements or other relevant details, enter it in the "Incident Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the incident, such as witness statements or other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the incident, consult with your superiors or other relevant authorities before submitting the form.
* This form is a basic template, and you may need to add or remove fields based on the specific requirements of your department or operation.
