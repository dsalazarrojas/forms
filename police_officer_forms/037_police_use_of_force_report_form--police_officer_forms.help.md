The Police Use Of Force Report Form is used to document and report incidents of police use of force. It gathers information about the incident, the officers involved, and the reporting officer.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  The form is divided into several sections. Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2.  Complete all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Enter your information in the designated fields. If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field explanation below.
