# Motor Vehicle Accident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motor Vehicle Accident Report Form is used to document and report motor vehicle accidents, providing essential information about the incident, involved vehicles, and witnesses. This form will help gather details about the accident, including the type of vehicle involved, the date and time of the accident, the location of the accident, and the witnesses involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle involved in the accident.
2. Enter the date of the accident.
3. Enter the time of the accident.
4. Enter the vehicle's make and year.
5. Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
6. Enter the driver's license number (if applicable).
7. Enter the driver's name (if applicable).
8. Select the type of collision (e.g., rear-end, front-end, side collision, etc.).
9. Enter the location of the accident.
10. Enter the police officer's name and ID (if applicable).
11. Enter the report date and time (if applicable).
12. Enter the report number (if applicable).
13. Enter the case number (if applicable).
14. Enter the incident date and time (if applicable).
15. Enter the witness names (if applicable).
16. Enter the witness contact information (if applicable).
17. Select the witness relation (e.g., witness of the driver, another vehicle, pedestrian, etc.).
18. Enter the witness description (if applicable).
19. Enter the witness signature (if applicable).
20. Enter the officer's signature (required).
21. Enter any additional comments from the officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one): Select the type of vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Accident Date** (`accident_date`, date): Enter the date of the accident (MM/DD/YY).
* **Accident Time** (`accident_time`, time): Enter the time of the accident (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text): Enter the make of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number): Enter the year of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`, text): Enter the driver's license number (if applicable).
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text): Enter the driver's name (if applicable).
* **Type of Collision** (`vehicle_type_of_collision`, select_one): Select the type of collision (e.g., rear-end, front-end, side collision, etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter the location of the accident.
* **Police Officer Name** (`police_officer_name`, text): Enter the police officer's name (if applicable).
* **Police Officer ID** (`police_officer_id`, text): Enter the police officer's ID (if applicable).
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date): Enter the date of the report (MM/DD/YY).
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time): Enter the time of the report (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Report Number** (`report_number`, text): Enter the report number (if applicable).
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text): Enter the case number (if applicable).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Enter the date of the incident (MM/DD/YY).
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time): Enter the time of the incident (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, text): Enter the names of the witnesses (if applicable).
* **Witness Contact Info** (`witness_contact_info`, text): Enter the contact information of the witnesses (if applicable).
* **Witness Relation** (`witness_relation`, select_multiple): Select the relation of the witnesses (e.g., witness of the driver, another vehicle, pedestrian, etc.).
* **Witness Description** (`witness_description`, text): Enter a description of the witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text): Enter the witness's signature (if applicable).
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, text): Enter the officer's signature (required).
* **Officer Comments** (`officer_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments from the officer.
