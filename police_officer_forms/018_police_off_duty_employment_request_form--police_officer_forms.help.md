# police_off_duty_employment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps police officers understand how to complete the Police Officer Off-Duty Employment Request Form to request permission for off-duty employment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review your department's policies on off-duty employment to ensure you understand the guidelines and any restrictions that apply.
2.  Fill out your officer details:
    *   **officer_details** (`officer_details`, text, optional): Enter your full name and badge number or ID number, as per your department's requirement.
3.  Provide job details:
    *   **job_details** (`job_details`, text, optional): Describe the job you are applying for, including the company name, job title, and any other relevant information.
4.  Enter employment date:
    *   **employment_date** (`employment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you intend to start working the off-duty job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **officer_details**: Enter your name as it appears on your official police department ID. This is typically required by the department for record-keeping purposes.
*   **job_details**: Provide as much detail as possible about the off-duty job you are applying for. This can include the company name, job title, and any other relevant information that will help the department assess the request.
*   **employment_date**: If your off-duty employment is for a specific period, enter the start date. If it's an ongoing or indefinite arrangement, you can enter "ongoing" or leave it blank.
