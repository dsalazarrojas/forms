# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to send non-urgent messages, requests, and information to the company or team.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is where your message will be sent.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field if you wish to be contacted by phone.
5. Select the **Subject** of your message from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Meeting Request", "Support Request", or "Other".
6. Type your **Message** in the "Message" field. Be as detailed as you need to convey your information.
7. If you wish to include a personalized **Greeting** for the recipient, type it in the "Greeting" field.
8. If you're representing a company, enter the **Company Name** in the "Company" field.
9. If you're contacting us in a professional capacity, specify your **Position** in the "Position" field.
10. Once you've filled out all the fields, click the submit button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name for us to address you.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name for us to address you.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for us to reply to your message.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Subject** (select_one): Choose the subject of your message from the dropdown menu.
* **Message** (note): Type your message in this field, including any details you think are relevant.
* **Greeting** (text): Type a personalized greeting for the recipient, if desired.
* **Company** (text): Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
* **Position** (text): Specify your position within the company, if applicable.
* **Meeting Request**: A subject for messages related to meeting scheduling.
* **Support Request**: A subject for general support inquiries.
* **Other**: A subject for all other types of messages.
* **Other (no other field explanation)**: A field for any other type of message not covered by the other subjects.
