# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Information Collection Form is designed to collect contact details and preferences for various purposes, such as newsletter subscriptions, event invitations, or other contact-related activities. This form is intended to be used for data collection and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's introduction page (page 1) to understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Fill out your First name and Last name in the required fields on page 2.
3. Enter your Email address in the required Email address field on page 3.
4. Optional: Fill out your Phone number, Company name, Job title, and Address.
5. Check the box on page 9 to Subscribe to newsletter (if desired).
6. Select one or more areas of interest from the list on page 10.
7. Add any Additional notes on page 12 if necessary.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your given name (page 2).
* **Last name**: Enter your family or surname (page 2).
* **Email address**: Enter your preferred email address for communication (page 3).
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code (page 4). (Optional)
* **Company name**: Enter your employer or organization name (page 6). (Optional)
* **Job title**: Enter your role at the organization (page 6). (Optional)
* **Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code (page 8). (Optional)
* **Subscribe to newsletter**: Check the box to indicate your consent to receive periodic communications (page 9).
* **Areas of interest**: Select one or more topics that interest you (page 10).
* **How did you hear about us**: Enter any information about how you were referred (page 11). (Optional)
* **Additional notes**: Enter any other information you would like to provide (page 12). (Optional)

## Tips
* Ensure that the Email address is correctly formatted (e.g., username@domain.com).
* Be accurate when entering your Phone number and Address.
* Select all relevant areas of interest to ensure you receive desired communications.
* Enter a valid referral source if you were referred by someone.
* Use Additional notes to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful.
