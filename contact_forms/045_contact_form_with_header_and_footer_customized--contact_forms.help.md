# Contact Form With Header And Footer (Customized) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details from visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your full name and email address, which are both required fields.
2. Provide your phone number, if you wish (it is not required but can be helpful for contact).
3. Enter a brief subject line for your inquiry.
4. Add a detailed message about your inquiry.
5. Select your preferred contact method: Email or Phone Call.
6. Finally, choose "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`visitor_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Your Email Address** (`visitor_email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for contact.
* **Your Phone Number** (`visitor_phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your mobile or home phone number for contact.
* **Subject** (`inquiry_subject`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Detailed Message** (`detailed_message`, `text`, required): Add a detailed message about your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted back (Email or Phone Call).
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): The date you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Be as detailed as possible in your message.
* Select the preferred contact method that best suits your needs.
* Review your filled form before submission to ensure accuracy.
