# scd_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The scd_contact_form is designed to collect contact information and comments from users. It is intended for general contact purposes and does not require any specific authentication or login credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it out to ensure that your information is collected correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your first name in this field. This will be the first part of your name that will be collected.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name in this field. This will be the last part of your name that will be collected.
- **Address** (`address1`, text, optional): Enter your address in this field. This will be used to contact you.
- **Address 2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address information in this field. This will be used to contact you further.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in this field. This will be used to contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in this field. This will be used to contact you.
- **Type of comment** (`type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of comment you would like to make from the options provided.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter your comments or feedback in this field. This is where you can provide any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to ensure that your information is collected accurately.
