# Business Contact Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Contact Booking Form is designed to collect contact information from potential customers or partners who would like to schedule a meeting or appointment with our business. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your company name and job title (if applicable).
3. Select your preferred appointment date and time from the available options.
4. Choose the preferred duration of your meeting and format (in person, phone call, or video conference).
5. Briefly describe the purpose of your appointment and the key topics you'd like to cover.
6. Optionally, provide any background information that would be helpful for us to know.
7. Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up communications (email, phone, or text message).
8. If you'd like, opt-in to receive follow-up communications after your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): This is the name you'd like to be addressed as.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please provide your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, required): If you represent a business or organization, please provide its name.
* **Job Title** (`position`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your job title, please do so.
* **Industry Type** (`industry`, text, optional): If applicable, please describe your industry or business type.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select a date and time that works best for you.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Appointment Duration Needed** (`appointment_duration`, select_one, required): How long would you like your meeting to last?
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, required): Would you prefer to meet in person, via phone call, or video conference?
* **Purpose of Appointment** (`appointment_purpose`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for your meeting.
* **Key Topics to Discuss** (`key_topics`, select_multiple, optional): If you'd like, select the topics you'd like to cover.
* **Background Information** (`background_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any relevant background information that would be helpful for us to know.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special accessibility or requirements, please let us know.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): How would you like us to confirm our appointment?
* **Opt-In to Follow-Up Communications** (`follow_up_opt_in`, select_one, optional): Would you like us to follow up with you after the appointment?
