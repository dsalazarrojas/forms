# Contact Form With Round Edges - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users who are interested in inquiring about a company or organization's services. The form is intended to be used by people who want to learn more about the organization's offerings and possibly schedule a follow-up call to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Name** field with your name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide a brief description of why you are inquiring about our services in the **How can we help you?** field.
4. If you have a specific project budget in mind, select the corresponding option from the **Estimated Project Budget** field.
5. If you've heard about us through a specific source, select the corresponding option from the **How did you hear about us?** field.
6. If you are interested in a follow-up call, select **Yes** from the **Would you like a follow-up call?** field and enter the phone number where you can be reached.
7. Finally, select the type of service you are inquiring about from the **Type of Inquiry** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (`contact_name`, `text`, required)
This field is where you should enter your name to help us know who you are.

### Email (`contact_email`, `email`, required)
Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.

### Company / Organization (`company_name`, `text`, optional)
If you are contacting us on behalf of a company or organization, you can enter its name here.

### Type of Inquiry (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of service you are inquiring about from the following options:
* **Partnership**
* **Media**
* **Support**
* **Careers**
* **General**

### Estimated Project Budget (`project_budget`, `select_one`, optional)
If you have a specific budget in mind for your project, select the corresponding option:
* **Under $1**
* **$1**
* **$000 - $5**
* **$5**
* **$000 - $10**
* **Over $10**

### How can we help you? (`details`, `text`, required)
Please provide a brief description of why you are inquiring about our services.

### How did you hear about us? (`reference_source`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the source where you heard about us:
* **Social Media**
* **Search Engine**
* **Referral**
* **Advertisement**
* **Other**

### Would you like a follow-up call? (`callback_requested`, `select_one`, optional)
If you are interested in a follow-up call, select **Yes** and provide the phone number where you can be reached.
 
### Phone Number for Callback (`callback_number`, `text`, optional)
Enter the phone number where you can be reached for a follow-up call.

This guide will help you complete the **Contact Form With Round Edges** efficiently and effectively. Please don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields.
