# Residential Community Newsletter Subscription - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable users to subscribe to the residential community newsletter, which will send regular updates to their email address. This form will check for duplicate subscriptions to prevent sending unwanted emails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form submission field**: Enter a brief description of why you want to subscribe to the newsletter.
2. **Enter your name**: Type your name as you would like it to appear in the newsletter.
3. **Enter your email address**: Provide a valid email address where you want to receive the newsletter.
4. **Enter your phone number**: Optionally, enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission (text)**: 
	+ Type a brief description of why you want to subscribe to the newsletter.
* **Name (text)**: 
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to appear in the newsletter.
* **Email (email)**: 
	+ Provide a valid email address where you want to receive the newsletter.
* **Phone (text)**: 
	+ Optionally, enter a phone number where you can be reached, which will help us get in touch with you if needed.
