# Concerned Stakeholder Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concerned Stakeholder Contact Form is a platform for stakeholders to share their concerns or issues with us. It helps us to understand the nature of their concerns, gather more information, and provide a resolution or answer to their query.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in your contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
* Select your stakeholder category (e.g., Investor, Customer, Employee, etc.).
* Describe your concern in detail, including the category and subject.
* Specify the urgency level and potential impact of your concern.
* Indicate if you have contacted us before and the date of your previous contact.
* Select your preferred response method and indicate if you want the conversation to be kept confidential.
* Finally, provide a desired outcome and any supporting documents you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact ID**: A unique identifier for your contact.
	+ Please enter a unique identifier for your contact, which will help us track your concerns.
* **Submission Date**: Date of submission.
	+ Please enter the date you are submitting your concern.
* **Stakeholder Name**: Your full name.
	+ Please enter your full name as the concerned stakeholder.
* **Organization**: Name of your organization.
	+ If applicable, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Stakeholder Type**: Select your stakeholder category.
	+ Choose the category that best fits your affiliation with us (e.g., Investor, Customer, Employee, etc.).
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
	+ Please enter your valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number.
	+ Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Complete mailing address.
	+ If applicable, please enter your complete mailing address.
* **Concern Category**: Select the category of your concern.
	+ Choose the category that best fits your concern (e.g., Financial Performance, Corporate Governance, etc.).
* **Concern Subject**: Brief subject line.
	+ Please enter a brief subject line for your concern.
* **Concern Description**: Describe your concern in detail.
	+ Provide a detailed description of your concern.
* **Urgency Level**: How urgent is your concern?
	+ Select the urgency level that best fits your situation (e.g., Low, Medium, High, etc.).
* **Impact Assessment**: Potential impact of your concern.
	+ Choose the potential impact of your concern (e.g., Minimal, Moderate, Significant, etc.).
* **Affected Parties**: Who else is affected?
	+ If applicable, please indicate who else is affected by your concern.
* **Previous Contact**: Have you contacted us before?
	+ Indicate if you have contacted us previously about this concern.
* **Previous Contact Date**: When did you last contact us?
	+ If applicable, enter the date of your last contact with us.
* **Supporting Documents**: Will you attach documents?
	+ Indicate if you will be attaching supporting documents.
* **Desired Outcome**: What resolution do you seek?
	+ Please specify the desired outcome for your concern.
* **Preferred Response Method**: How should we respond to you?
	+ Choose the preferred method of response (e.g., Email, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Availability**: When are you available?
	+ If applicable, indicate when you are available for a response.
* **Confidentiality Request**: Should this be kept confidential?
	+ Indicate if you want the conversation to be kept confidential.
* **Follow Up Permission**: May we contact you for more information?
	+ Indicate if you are available for follow-up contact.
* **Contact Status**: Current status.
	+ Select the current status of your concern (e.g., Submitted, Under Review, etc.).
