# thanksgiving_message_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and messages from users who want to get in touch, likely for a Thanksgiving-related purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contact Us" option (Page 1).
2. Enter your name in the text field (Page 2).
3. Type your message in the text field (Page 3).
4. Enter your email address in the email field (Page 4).
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the text field (Page 5).
6. If you're comfortable sharing, provide your date of birth in the date field (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us (Page 1)**:
	+ Select this option to begin the contact form submission process.
* **name (Page 2)**:
	+ Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **message (Page 3)**:
	+ Type in the message or question you'd like to discuss.
* **email (Page 4)**:
	+ Enter your email address so we can reach out if needed.
* **phone (Page 5)**:
	+ If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you by phone (if necessary).
* **date_of_birth (Page 6)**:
	+ If you're comfortable sharing, enter your date of birth to help us better understand your relationship with our organization.
