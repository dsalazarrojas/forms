# Submit Your Question For Q+A - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to submit questions, concerns, or inquiries to be addressed by a support team or team member. It is designed to provide a structured and efficient way of communicating with the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with the requested information.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the "question_text" field.
* Select the relevant option for "question_type" that best describes your question or concern.
* Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number, and date and time) so that the support team can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **question_text** (Text, Required): Enter a clear and concise description of your question or concern. This field is where you can provide as much detail as possible about your query.
* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name for the support team to address you by.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so that the support team can get back to you via email.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): If you have an alternative phone number where you can be reached, please enter it here.
* **date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you would like to receive the support.
* **time** (Time, Required): Enter the time you would like to receive the support.
* **question_type** (Select One, Required): Select the option that best describes your question or concern from the provided options.
* **question_text_2** (Text, Optional): If you have additional information to provide that is not included in the main "question_text" field, please enter it here.
* **description** (Note, Optional): If you want to add more context or details about your question, please use this field to type in your notes.
*   **phone**: This field is not mandatory and is meant for users who would prefer to be contacted by phone. If you have an alternative phone number, you can enter it here.
* **question_type**: This field is used to categorize your question or concern into one of the provided options. Choose the option that best describes your inquiry.
*   **question_text_2**: This field is meant for users who have additional information or thoughts that are not captured in the main "question_text" field. If you have something to add, please use this field.
