# gdpr contact form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR contact form is a tool used to handle sensitive data related to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other privacy concerns. This form is intended for customers to report or inquire about their personal data, request corrections, or provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional but recommended for further clarification).
4. Type your message in the "Message" field, clearly stating your reason for contacting us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)** (`text`, required): Please enter your name as you want it to be displayed in our response.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further discussions or clarifications.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, optional): If you provide your phone number, it will help us contact you for more detailed conversations, but it is not mandatory for us to process your request.
* **Message (Message)** (`text`, required): Describe the reason for your contact or inquiry, including any specific details or attachments if relevant.
