# Travel Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and travel-related details from users. This form will help our travel agency or tourism board understand your preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and preferences. The form is divided into sections, each with its own fields. Fill in each section as prompted, and don't hesitate to ask if you need any assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Travel Info** (`travel_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your travel plans, including destination and dates.
* **Home Info** (`home_info`, text, optional): Share your home address and any other relevant information about your home.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your travel plans.
* **Travel Arrangement** (`travel_arrangement`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred mode of transportation for your trip (Air, Train, Bus).
* **Hotel** (`hotel`, select one, optional): Select your preferred type of accommodation for your stay (Hotel, Resort, Hostel).
* **Flight** (`flight`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you require flight assistance (True or False).
* **Tips:** Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date, and that your travel preferences are clearly stated. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
