# Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Review Form is used to collect and track information about contracts, including customer details, contract terms, and review status. This form helps streamline the review process and keeps track of agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the customer's name, email, phone number, and organization (if applicable).
2. Select the type of contract (e.g., Service agreement, Product purchase, etc.).
3. Enter the contract title, description, value, and duration.
4. Choose the start and end dates (if applicable).
5. Select the payment terms (e.g., Upfront payment, Net 15, etc.).
6. Enter the deliverables and any special requirements.
7. Specify the customer's expectations.
8. Review and update the status as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email (2)**: Enter the customer's contact email.
* **Customer Phone (3)**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Customer Organization (4)**: Enter the customer's organization name (if applicable).
* **Customer Address (5)**: Enter the customer's full address (if applicable).
* **Submission Date (6)**: Enter the date the contract was submitted.
* **Sales Representative Name (7)**: Enter the sales representative's full name.
* **Sales Representative Email (8)**: Enter the sales representative's contact email.
* **Sales Representative Phone (9)**: Enter the sales representative's contact number.
* **Contract Type (10)**: Select the type of contract (e.g., Service agreement, Product purchase, etc.).
* **Contract Title (11)**: Enter the contract title.
* **Contract Description (12)**: Enter a brief summary of the contract.
* **Contract Value (13)**: Enter the dollar amount of the contract.
* **Contract Duration (14)**: Select the length of the contract (e.g., One-time, Less than 1 year, etc.).
* **Start Date (15)**: Enter the date when the contract is to begin.
* **End Date (16)**: Enter the date when the contract is to end (if applicable).
* **Payment Terms (17)**: Select the payment terms (e.g., Upfront payment, Net 15, etc.).
* **Deliverables (18)**: Enter what will be delivered as part of the contract.
* **Special Requirements (19)**: Enter any unique needs or requirements of the customer.
* **Customer Expectations (20)**: Enter what the customer expects from the contract.
* **Review Status (21)**: Select the current review status (e.g., Pending review, In review, etc.).
* **Reviewer Comments (22)**: Enter any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Follow-up Date (23)**: Enter the date to follow up on the review (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments (24)**: Enter any other relevant information.
