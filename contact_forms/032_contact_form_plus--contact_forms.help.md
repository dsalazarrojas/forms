# Contact Form Plus - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Form Plus is used for users to send inquiries about a website's content, functionality, or any other matters that require attention from the website owners or administrators. Fill out this form to get in touch with our team for assistance or information.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the required fields

- **Full Name** (text): Enter your name as it should be represented.
- **Email Address** (text): Enter a valid email address that we can respond to.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your mobile or landline number.
- **Message Subject** (text): Briefly summarize the nature of your inquiry.
- **Preferred Response Method** (select): Choose how you would like us to contact you (Email, Phone Call, or No Response Needed).
- **Date of Submission** (date): This field will automatically display today's date.

### Step 2: Choose your Website Inquiry Type (Select One)

- **Type of Website Inquiry** (select): Select the best match from the provided options:
    - Content Suggestion
    - Technical Issue/Bug Report
    - Advertising Inquiry
    - Partnership Opportunity
    - Privacy/Terms Inquiry
    - Other

### Step 3: Add more details and submit

- **Your Detailed Message** (text): Provide more information about your inquiry.
- **Website URL (if applicable)** (text, optional): Enter the URL of the website you are inquiring about.

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): A field to enter your full name as it should be represented.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, required): A field to enter a valid email address that we can respond to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): A field to enter your mobile or landline number.
* **Website URL (if applicable)** (`website_url`, text, optional): A field to enter the URL of the website you are inquiring about.

* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field will automatically display today's date.

## Tips

- Please fill out all required fields (marked as required).
- Make sure to enter a valid email address in the Email Address field.
- You can choose one option for the Type of Website Inquiry field.
- If you have any attachments, you can attach them in an email or provide the link to the attachment.
- Our team will review your message and respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
- If you have any further questions or clarifications, feel free to ask.
