# Request For Additional Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Additional Information Form is used to gather more details from clients or customers on an existing request or inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Client Name**.
2. Clearly state the **Question** you need more information on.
3. Provide your **Contact Number** if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
4. Add your **Email** address if you'd like to receive further updates or responses.
5. In the **Message** field, provide your query or any additional information you'd like to know more about.
6. Finally, select a **Requested Date**, indicating when you'd like to receive more information or an update on your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This is the name of the client or customer submitting this form.
* **Question** (`question`, text, required): Clearly state the specific question you need more information on.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted directly, provide your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive updates or responses via email, add your email address here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): In this field, explain your query or any additional information you'd like to know more about.
* **Requested Date** (`requested_date`, date, required): Choose a date when you'd like to receive more information or an update on your inquiry.
