# coronavirus_self_declaration_borthwick_castle_travel_allowed - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare travel history and contact details for COVID-19 related measures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Travel Allowed" option to indicate if you are allowed to travel.
2. If "yes", select the reason for travel from the list provided.
3. If you have other travel destinations, select "other" and provide the alternative destinations.
4. Enter your arrival date and time in the format "DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM".
5. Enter your departure date and time in the format "DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM".
6. Enter your contact number.
7. Select the type of accommodation you will be staying in from the list provided.
8. Enter your contact email address.
9. Enter your contact phone number.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about your travel history and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Allowed** (`travel_allowed`): Select "yes" to indicate that you are allowed to travel.
* **Covid Form** (`covid_form`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your travel history.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Select the reason for your travel from the list provided.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`): Select the destinations you will be visiting from the list provided. If you have other destinations, select "other" and provide the alternative destinations.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`): Enter your arrival date in the format "DD/MM/YYYY".
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Enter your arrival time in the format "HH:MM".
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`): Enter your departure date in the format "DD/MM/YYYY".
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Select the type of accommodation you will be staying in from the list provided.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Note** (`note`): Provide any additional notes or comments about your travel history and contact details.
* **Contact Form Submitted** (`contact_form_submitted`): Select "yes" to indicate that you have submitted the contact form.
* **Travel Reason Other** (`travel_reason_other`): Provide an alternative reason for travel.
* **Contact Number Other** (`contact_number_other`): Enter an alternative contact number.
* **Travel Destination Other** (`travel_destination_other`): Provide an alternative travel destination.
* **Contact Address Other** (`contact_address_other`): Enter an alternative contact address.
* **Note Other** (`note_other`): Provide an additional note or comment.
* **Note Note** (`note_note`): Enter an additional note or comment.
* **Contact Phone Other** (`contact_phone_other`): Enter an alternative contact phone number.
* **Contact Email Other** (`contact_email_other`): Enter an alternative contact email address.
