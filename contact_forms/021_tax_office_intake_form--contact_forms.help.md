# Tax Office Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Office Intake Form is a tool for submitting information to the Tax Office for tax-related purposes. This guide will walk you through the completion process and help you understand each field's purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* For the "Select One" field, choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your response.
* For the "Select Multiple" field, choose one or multiple options from the list.
* Enter a date in the "Date" field using the MM/DD/YY format.
* Enter a time in the "Time" field using the HH:MM format.
* In the "Note" field, provide any additional information you would like to share with the Tax Office.
* If you need to provide a required field, enter your information in the "Required Field" field. Please note that this field is not currently required for any purpose, despite its label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your response.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a date in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a time in the HH:MM format.
* **Note** (note, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with the Tax Office.
* **Required Field** (text, optional): Please note that this field is not currently required for any purpose, despite its label. If you have information to provide, enter it here.
* **Required Field (hidden note)**: This field is intended to be used by the Tax Office for internal purposes only, and it is currently not required for any purpose.
