# Bridal Show Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your wedding plans and preferences. It will help us understand your needs and provide you with the best services and support for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and truthfully to ensure we have all the necessary information.
2. Be sure to provide your contact information so we can get back to you.
3. If you have any special requests or questions, please don't hesitate to include them in the "Special Requests or Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride Name** (`couple_primary_name`, text, Required): Please enter your full name.
* **Groom Name** (`couple_secondary_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, Required): Provide your primary email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, Optional): If you'd like to share your street address for further communication.
* **City** (`city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where you reside or will be holding your wedding.
* **State** (`state`, text, Optional): If you'd like to share your state or province of residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, Optional): Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **Wedding Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, date, Required): Provide the date of your wedding ceremony.
* **Ceremony Time** (`ceremony_time`, time, Optional): If you'd like to share the approximate time of your ceremony.
* **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, text, Required): Enter the location of your wedding ceremony.
* **Venue City** (`venue_city`, text, Optional): If you'd like to share the city where your ceremony will be held.
* **Venue State** (`venue_state`, text, Optional): If you'd like to share the state where your ceremony will be held.
* **Reception Venue** (`reception_venue`, text, Optional): Enter the location of your wedding reception.
* **Estimated Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, Optional): If you have an approximate number of guests attending.
* **Wedding Style** (`wedding_style`, select_one, Optional): Select your desired wedding style from the list provided.
* **Bridal Party Size** (`bridal_party_size`, number, Optional): Enter the number of attendants you'll be having.
* **Vendors You Are Interested In** (`vendors_interested_in`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the types of services you're interested in.
* **Overall Wedding Budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, Optional): Provide your estimated total budget.
* **How Did You Hear About This Show** (`how_heard_about_show`, text, Optional): Share with us how you found out about this bridal show.
* **Special Requests or Information** (`special_requests`, text, Optional): If you have any additional notes or requests, please share them here.
