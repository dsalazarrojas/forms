# Press Comment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a comment or request for press coverage to the organization. This form is intended for press representatives and is used to collect relevant information to facilitate the request process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a legitimate purpose for submitting a press comment request.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Select relevant options for press type and frequency.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be used for communication purposes.
* **What is your job title?** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it relates to your press representative role.
* **What is your press type?** (`press_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Newspaper
	+ Magazine
	+ Online news site
* **What is your press frequency?** (`press_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Do you have a comment or request?** (`comment_request`, text, required): Enter a brief statement of your comment or request.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (not required but recommended).
* **What is your company?** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name as it should be used for communication purposes.
* **Where are you located?** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your location for geographical context (not required but recommended).
* **What is your department?** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name for further context (not required but recommended).
* **Do you have a comment or press comment?** (`comment_request_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or context (not required but recommended).
* **Do you have any additional notes?** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or context for your request (not required but recommended).
