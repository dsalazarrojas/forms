# Real Estate Visitor Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about visitors when they check out of a property. Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact name and email address in the "Contact Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. If an agent or representative accompanied you, enter their name in the "Agent Name" field.
3. Select the type of visit from the "Visitor Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the check-out time in the "Check Out Time" field.
5. Enter the name of the property you are checking out of in the "Property Name" field.
6. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
7. If you have any comments or feedback, you can add them in the "Visitor Comments" field (optional).
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name (text, required)**: Please enter your full name as it should be displayed for check-out purposes.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address as it should be associated with your name.
* **Phone Number (text, optional)**: This field is used to store your contact phone number, but it's not required.
* **Visitor Type (select_one, required)**: Select "Agent" if you are visiting on behalf of an agent or representative, or "Client" if you are a client or visitor.
* **Check Out Time (time, required)**: Enter the time you are checking out of the property.
* **Property Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the property you are checking out of.
* **Property Address (text, required)**: Enter the address of the property you are checking out of.
* **Visitor Comments (text, optional)**: If you have any feedback or comments about your visit, you can add them here.
* **Agent Name (text, required)**: If an agent or representative accompanied you, enter their name here.
* **Agent Phone Number (text, optional)**: If an agent or representative has a phone number, you can enter it here, but it's not required.
