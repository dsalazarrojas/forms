# Target Client List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Target Client List Form is a tool to collect and store client contact information and notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Form**: This is the first section of the form. It is used to collect the client's basic contact information.
2. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name in this field.
3. **Email**: Enter the client's email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
4. **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number without dashes or parentheses.
5. **Address**: Enter the client's address in free-form text.
6. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
7. **Date**: Select the date of the client's visit or call.
8. **Time**: Select the time of the client's visit or call.
9. **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
