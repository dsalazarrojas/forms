# Buyer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Information Form is a tool for businesses to gather essential information from potential buyers, including contact details, purchase preferences, and demographic data. This form helps us understand your needs, preferences, and interests, enabling us to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
3. Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`buyer_first_name`, Text, \*)
Your first name is the first name by which you are known.
* **Last Name** (`buyer_last_name`, Text, \*)
Your last name is the last name by which you are known.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, Email, \*)
Your primary email address is how we will contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, Text, \*)
Your contact phone number is how we can reach you.
* **Street Address** (`buyer_address`, Text, \*)
Your home or business address is where we can send you information or send products.
* **City** (`buyer_city`, Text, \*)
The city where you reside or do business.
* **State or Province** (`buyer_state`, Text, \*)
The state or province where you reside or do business.
* **Postal Code** (`buyer_postal_code`, Text, \*)
The zip code or postal code of your location.
* **Country** (`buyer_country`, Text, \*)
The country where you reside or do business.
* **Type of Buyer** (`buyer_type`, Select One, \*)
Select your category from the following options:
	+ Individual consumer
	+ Business or company
	+ Government or institution
	+ Non-profit organization
	+ Reseller or distributor
	+ Contractor
	+ Other
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, Text, Optional)
If you are a business or organization, provide your company name.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, Select One, Optional)
Select your company size from the following options:
	+ Solo or freelance
	+ 2-10 employees
	+ 11-50 employees
	+ 51-200 employees
	+ 200+ employees
	+ N/A
* **Industry or Business Type** (`industry`, Text, Optional)
If applicable, select your industry or business type.
* **Products or Services of Interest** (`product_interest`, Text, \*)
Describe the products or services you are looking to purchase.
* **Budget Range** (`budget`, Select One, Optional)
Select your budget range from the following options:
	+ Under $500
	+ $500-$2500
	+ $2500-$10000
	+ $10000-$50000
	+ $50000-$100000
	+ Over $100000
	+ Not determined
* **Purchase Timeline** (`purchase_timeline`, Select One, \*)
Select your expected purchase timeline from the following options:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ No specific timeline
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard`, Select One, Optional)
Select how you heard about us from the following options:
	+ Search engine
	+ Social media
	+ Referral from friend
	+ Advertisement
	+ Industry publication
	+ Trade show
	+ Direct mail
	+ Website
	+ Other
* **Subscribe to Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, Select One, Optional)
Select whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter:
	+ Yes, subscribe me
	+ No, do not subscribe
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Select One, Optional)
Select your preferred contact method from the following options:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Mail
	+ No preference
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, Text, Optional)
Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
