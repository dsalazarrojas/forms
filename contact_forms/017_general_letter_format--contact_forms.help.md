# General Letter Format - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Letter Format form is used to collect basic contact information for official correspondence purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Enter your name in the First Name field (ID: 1).
3. Enter your last name in the Last Name field (ID: 2).
4. Enter your email address in the Email field (ID: 3).
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field (ID: 4).
6. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field (ID: 5), in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter your address in the Address field (ID: 6), using a note format for any additional details.
8. If applicable, enter your company name in the Company field (ID: 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (ID: 1, text, required): Enter your given name as it appears on official records.
- **Last Name** (ID: 2, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on official records.
- **Email** (ID: 3, email, required): Enter your official email address for communication.
- **Phone** (ID: 4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (ID: 5, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address** (ID: 6, note, optional): Enter any additional details about your address.
- **Company** (ID: 7, text, optional): If applicable, enter your company name for official correspondence purposes.

## Tips
- Please use the format specified for date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
- If any of the fields are not applicable to you, feel free to leave them blank.
- Use this form for official correspondence only and not for any other purpose.
