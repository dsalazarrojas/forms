# Better Health With Nutrition Learn More Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal data from individuals interested in learning more about a nutrition-related program. It aims to gather information about your health goals, interests, and preferences to provide tailored guidance and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred time for contact.
3. Choose your primary health goals.
4. Select any specific dietary preferences you have.
5. Indicate if you are interested in a personal nutrition plan.
6. Choose the products or services you would like to learn more about.
7. Describe how you heard about our nutrition program.
8. Disclose any existing health conditions.
9. Specify any questions or topics you would like to discuss with our nutritionists.
10. Consent to receive health and nutrition updates.
11. Finally, provide your date of inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`visitor_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Your Email Address** (`visitor_email_addr`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further guidance.
* **Your Phone Number** (`visitor_phone_number`, text, required): Provide your phone number for potential follow-up calls.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`contact_time_preference`, select_one, required): Select the time of day when you are most likely to be available for contact.
* **What are your primary health goals** (`primary_health_goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the health goals that apply to you (e.g., Weight Management, Muscle Gain and Athleticism, etc.).
* **Any specific dietary preferences** (`dietary_preferences_select`, select_multiple, required): Select any specific dietary preferences you have (e.g., Vegan or Plant-based, Vegetarian, etc.).
* **Are you interested in a personal nutrition plan** (`plan_interest_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are interested in a personal nutrition plan tailored to your needs.
* **Which products or services would you like to learn more about** (`inquiry_interests_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose the products or services you are interested in learning more about (e.g., Nutritional Supplements, Personalized Meal Plans, etc.).
* **How did you hear about our nutrition program** (`referral_source_desc`, text, required): Describe how you heard about our nutrition program.
* **Any existing health conditions we should be aware of** (`health_conditions_disclosure`, text, required): Disclose any existing health conditions that may impact our guidance.
* **Any specific questions for our nutritionists** (`nutritionist_questions_text`, text, required): Specify any questions or topics you would like to discuss with our nutritionists.
* **Consent to receive health and nutrition updates** (`update_consent_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you consent to receive health and nutrition updates.
* **Date of Inquiry** (`inquiry_final_date_stamp`, date, required): Provide your date of inquiry.
