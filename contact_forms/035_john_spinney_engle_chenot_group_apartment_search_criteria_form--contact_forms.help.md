# john_spinney_engle_chenot_group_apartment_search_criteria_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide search criteria for apartments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the relevant fields with your desired search criteria. You can leave any field blank if you don't have a specific preference for it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Apartment Address** (`apartment_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the apartment you're looking for.
* **Apartment Number** (`apartment_number`, number, optional): Enter the apartment number (if applicable).
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of unit you're interested in (Apartment, Condo, or Townhouse).
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms you're looking for.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`baths`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms you're looking for.
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the apartment you're looking for.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rent you're willing to pay.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the lease duration you're interested in (6 months, 12 months, 18 months, 24 months, 36 months, or Other).
* **Pet Friendly** (`pet_friendly`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes if you have pets and are looking for a pet-friendly apartment.
* **Smoking Policies** (`smoking_policies`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes if you're looking for a non-smoking building.
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes for the amenities you're interested in (e.g., "Yes" for a pool, "No" for no pool).
* **Neighborhood** (`neighborhood`, text, optional): Enter the neighborhood you're looking for.
* **Map URL** (`map_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the apartment's location on a map.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your search.
* **Move In** (`move_in`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move Out** (`move_out`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to move out.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the apartment you're looking for.
* **Sq Ft** (`sqft`, number, optional): Enter the square footage of the apartment you're looking for.
* **Beds** (`beds`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms you're looking for.
* **Baths** (`baths`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms you're looking for.
* **Stories** (`stories`, number, optional): Enter the number of stories (floors) in the building.
* **Parking** (`parking`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes if you need parking.
