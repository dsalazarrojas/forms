# enquiry_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "enquiry_form_template" form is designed to collect general enquiries from users. It is typically used for customers, partners, or other stakeholders to reach out to our team for various reasons such as asking questions, providing feedback, or inquiring about something else. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your contact type by choosing one or more of the available options (Question, Feedback, or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of what you are inquiring about or the purpose of your contact.
3. If you have any questions, you can provide them in the "Questions" field. This is an optional field.
4. If you have any additional information to provide, you can enter it in the "Submitter Info" and "Submitter Info 2" fields. 
5. Enter your email address in the "Contact Info" field so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Type**: Select your contact type by choosing one or more of the available options (Question, Feedback, or Other). This helps us understand the nature of your enquiry.
* **What Regards**: Provide a brief description of what you are inquiring about or the purpose of your contact. This helps us understand the context of your enquiry.
* **Questions**: If you have any questions, you can provide them in this field. This is an optional field, and you can enter as many questions as you need.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you. Please make sure it's a valid email address.
* **Submitter Info** and **Submitter Info 2**: If you have any additional information to provide, you can enter it in these fields.
* **Questions 2**: This field is identical in purpose to "Questions" and should only be used if you have questions that are distinct from those already entered in "Questions". 

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if applicable) before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
