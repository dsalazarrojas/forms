# STL Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather comprehensive information about a client's medical history, allergies, and other relevant details. It helps healthcare professionals provide personalized and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you for further information or clarification.
3. Provide your Contact Number so we can reach you if needed.
4. In the Medical Notes field, briefly describe any relevant medical information you'd like to share.
5. Enter your Birth Date for accurate identification.
6. Describe your Medical History in the provided field.
7. Enter any Medical Conditions you'd like to disclose.
8. Outline your Treatment Plan, including any ongoing or upcoming treatments.
9. List any medications you're currently taking, including their dosages.
10. Indicate any allergies or sensitivities you have.
11. Note any medication allergies or sensitivities.
12. Mention any recent or upcoming medical tests you've undergone.
13. If you've been referred to a doctor, please provide their name.
14. If you're aware of any patient referrals, please provide their name.
15. In Medical History 2, describe any additional medical information not already mentioned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Provide your contact number for easy reachability.
* **Medical Notes (Medical Notes)** (`medical_notes`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any relevant medical information.
* **Birth Date (Birth Date)** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Enter your birth date for accurate identification.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe your medical history.
* **Medical Condition (Medical Condition)** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Treatment Plan (Treatment Plan)** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, optional): Outline your treatment plan, including ongoing or upcoming treatments.
* **Medication (Medication)** (`medication`, `text`, optional): List any medications you're currently taking, including their dosages.
* **Medication Dose (Medication Dose)** (`medication_dose`, `text`, optional): Note the dosage of your medications.
* **Medical Specialist (Medical Specialist)** (`medical_specialist`, `text`, optional): Indicate any medical specialist you've consulted or currently see.
* **Allergies (Allergies)** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medication Allergies (Medication Allergies)** (`medication_allergies`, `text`, optional): Note any medication allergies or sensitivities.
