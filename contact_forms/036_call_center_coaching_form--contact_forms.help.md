# Call Center Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide coaching feedback to agents on their performance in call center roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the call details: Enter the date and time of the call.
2. Provide agent information: Enter the agent's name and ID.
3. Evaluate call resolution: Select whether the call was resolved or not.
4. Assess agent performance: Evaluate the agent's communication skills, product knowledge, customer empathy, problem-solving ability, and other relevant aspects.
5. Provide specific feedback and suggestions: Enter any specific feedback or suggestions for the agent's development.
6. Create an action plan: Summarize the areas for improvement and suggestions for the agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Call Date** (`call_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the call.
* **Call Time** (`call_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the call.
* **Call Recording ID** (`call_recording_id`, text, optional): Enter the call recording ID (if available).
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agent.
* **Agent ID** (`agent_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the agent.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the team the agent belongs to.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Call Type** (`call_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of call (e.g., Inbound - Sales, Inbound - Support, etc.).
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Evaluate the customer's satisfaction level (e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Was the Call Resolved?** (`call_resolution`, select_one, required): Select whether the call was resolved or not.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Evaluate the agent's communication skills (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Product Knowledge** (`product_knowledge`, select_one, required): Evaluate the agent's product knowledge (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Customer Empathy** (`customer_empathy`, select_one, required): Evaluate the customer's empathy (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Problem Solving Ability** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Evaluate the agent's problem-solving ability (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Enter areas where the agent needs improvement.
* **Specific Feedback and Examples** (`specific_feedback`, text, optional): Enter specific feedback and examples for the agent's development.
* **Action Plan for Development** (`action_plan`, text, required): Enter a summary of the areas for improvement and suggestions for the agent's development.
