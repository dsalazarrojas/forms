# Contact Us Form Pin Theme With Captcha - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a secure and easy way for users to contact us, allowing them to ask general questions, seek technical support, or provide feedback. It helps us better understand their needs and preferences, enabling us to offer personalized assistance and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" at the bottom of the page.
2. Select "General Question", "Technical Support", "Sales", "Feedback", or "Other" from the "Type of Inquiry" dropdown menu.
3. Fill in your "Name" and "Email Address" fields.
4. Enter a valid "Email Address" for us to respond to your inquiry.
5. Provide your "Phone Number" (optional).
6. Enter a brief description of the purpose of your message in the "Subject" field.
7. Type your message in the "Your Message" field.
8. Enter the security code displayed in the "Security Verification" field to confirm you are human.
9. Check the box in "Privacy Consent" to agree to our terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us**: This field is the header of the form and does not need to be filled in.
* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Subject**: Briefly describe the purpose of your message.
* **Type of Inquiry**: Select the category that best fits your type of inquiry.
	+ General Question: For general questions or concerns.
	+ Technical Support: For technical support or issues with our services.
	+ Sales: For sales or business inquiries.
	+ Feedback: To provide feedback or suggestions.
	+ Other: For any other type of inquiry not listed above.
* **Your Message**: Type a detailed description of your message.
* **Security Verification**: Enter the code displayed in the image to confirm you are human.
* **Privacy Consent**: Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send your message to us.
