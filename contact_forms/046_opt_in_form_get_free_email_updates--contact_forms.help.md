# Opt In Form Get Free Email Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors interested in receiving free email updates. The purpose of this form is to capture contact information and preferences to provide relevant email content to the submitter. The form will not be duplicated if you have already submitted this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Get Free Email Updates" link and access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **email** (email, required/false): Enter your email address.
This field is where you can enter your email address to receive free email updates.
### 2. **mailing list** (mailing_list, select_one, required/false): Select your preferred mailing list(s).
Select the type of content you would like to receive, such as newsletters or specific mailing lists.
### 3. **subscribe** (subscribe, text, required/false): This field is not required for submission. However, if you enter any content here, it will be included in your email updates.
You can enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide to us.
### 4. **name** (name, text, required/false): Enter your name.
This field is optional, but we would appreciate knowing your name for personalization purposes.
### 5. **organization** (organization, text, required/false): Enter your organization or company name.
This field is optional, but it helps us understand your affiliation.
### 6. **comments** (comments, text, required/false): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
This field is optional, but it helps us tailor our content to your needs.
### 7. **company name** (company_name, text, required/false): Enter your company name.
This field is optional, but it helps us understand your company affiliation.
### 8. **company website** (company_website, text, required/false): Enter your company website URL.
This field is optional, but it helps us verify your company information.
### 9. **update frequency** (update_frequency, select_one, required/false): Select the frequency of email updates you would like to receive.
Choose from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly updates.
### 10. **email updates** (email_updates, text, required/false): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about email updates.
You can enter any information you would like to provide us about your email update preferences.
### 11. **mailing list 2** (mailing_list_2, select_one, required/false): Select a second mailing list option.
### 12. **mailing list 3** (mailing_list_3, select_multiple, required/false): Select multiple mailing list options.
### 13. **mailing lists** (mailing_lists, select_multiple, required/false): Select multiple mailing list options.
### 14. **company phone** (company_phone, text, required/false): Enter your company phone number.
This field is optional, but it helps us verify your company information.
### 15. **company address** (company_address, text, required/false): Enter your company address.
This field is optional, but it helps us verify your company information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct email address and mailing list options.
* Enter any relevant information in the optional fields to help us provide the most relevant updates.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at <insert contact email>.
