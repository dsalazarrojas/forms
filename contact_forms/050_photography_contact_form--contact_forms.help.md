# Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Contact Form is used to collect contact information from clients or customers for events, weddings, or commercial photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the necessary fields with accurate information about the client or customer, such as their name and contact details.
2. Provide the required information as specified in the form, ensuring that all necessary fields are completed.
3. Review your input before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text Field): This is the client's name that will be used for contact purposes.
* **spouse** (Text Field): If the client has a spouse, enter their name here.
* **due_date** (Date Field): Enter the date by which you would like the photography services to be completed. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **address** (Text Field): Provide the client's address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **phone_number** (Text Field): Enter the client's phone number for easy contact.
* **number_of_siblings** (Number Field): If the client has siblings, enter the number for contact purposes.
* **signature** (Text Field): Enter a brief description or note regarding the client's photography request.
* **email** (Email Field): Enter the client's email address for contact and communication purposes.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the form owner or service provider for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are completed to ensure proper processing of the form.
