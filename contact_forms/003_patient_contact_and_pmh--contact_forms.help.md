# Patient Contact And PMH - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient contact information and medical history (PMH) to support patient care and future communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with the patient's details.
2. Ensure that the required fields are filled out correctly.
3. If a field is optional, use it to provide additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's first name. For example, John.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's last name. For example, Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter the patient's email address. For example, johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's phone number. For example, 1234567890.
* **PMH** (`pmh`, select_one, required/optional): This field indicates whether the patient has a medical history or not. Select 'Yes' if the patient has a medical history, and 'No' if not.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's address for correspondence and communication.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Please enter the patient's state of residence.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Use clear and correct spelling for all fields.
* If you are unsure about a field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for patient contact and medical history purposes only. Do not use it for other purposes without approval from healthcare staff.
