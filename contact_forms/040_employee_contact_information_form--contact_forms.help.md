# Employee Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee contact information, which is essential for HR management, payroll, and benefits administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information as follows:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
3. **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the company.
4. **Department**: Enter the department you are currently working in.
5. **Email**: Enter your company email address.
6. **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
7. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
8. **Job Status**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Active**: You are currently employed and active in your role.
	* **Inactive**: You are no longer employed with the company.
	* **On Leave**: You are currently on leave or temporary leave of absence.
9. **Work Location**: Enter your current work location.
10. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
11. **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, sibling).
12. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
13. **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
