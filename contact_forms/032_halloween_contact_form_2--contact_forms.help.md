# Halloween Contact Form 2 - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
The Halloween Contact Form 2 is designed to collect user input on various aspects of the Halloween event. It is a simple and efficient way to allow users to send messages, inquiries, or suggestions.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
 
1.  Enter your first name.
2.  Enter your last name.
3.  Enter a valid email address if you want to receive a response.
4.  Enter a subject describing the purpose of your message.
5.  Enter a message or question you'd like to address.
6.  (Optional) Enter an alternative name if you want to provide a different name.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact and response purposes. If you don't provide an email address, you may not receive a response.
* **Subject** (Subject, text, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for your message.
* **Message** (Message, note, required): Enter your message or question you'd like to address.
* **Alternative Name** (Alternative Name, text, optional): Enter a different name, if you want to use one.
