# Startup Investor Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for startups to inquire about potential investment or partnership opportunities. It allows you to share your startup's information, including your contact details and the nature of your inquiry, so that our team can follow up and assist you further.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Enter your Phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
3. Select the type of inquiry you are interested in (Investment, Partnership, or Other).
4. Enter your Company name (if applicable).
5. If you have a website, please provide it.
6. Write a brief Description of your startup or the reason for your inquiry.
7. Click the Submit button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your business phone number or mobile number where we can contact you.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Select from Investment, Partnership, or Other to indicate the type of inquiry you have.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company's name (if you have one).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): If you have a business website, please provide the URL.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Briefly describe your startup, its focus, or the specific reason for your inquiry.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all mandatory fields.
* Double-check your contact details for accuracy before submitting.
* Please note that non-disclosure agreements may apply depending on the nature of your inquiry. We will review and contact you based on your provided details.
