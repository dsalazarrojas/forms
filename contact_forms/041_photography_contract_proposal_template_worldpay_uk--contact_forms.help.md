Photography Contract & Proposal Template WorldPay UK - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for photography businesses to create a contract with their clients for photo shoots. It collects information from the client, payment details, and other relevant data. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you need to fill out the required information on each field. Each field has a label that explains its purpose. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, contact details, and any other relevant information. 
* **Session Details**: Provide details about the photo session, including the date and time.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Photo Session**: Describe the photo session you are proposing.
* **Photo Purchase**: Select the photo package you want to propose.
* **Payment Information**: Enter the payment details, including the amount and payment method.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether a payment method is required.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the payment time.
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status.
* **Photo Package**: Select the photo package you want to propose.
* **Contract Signature**: Select whether the client has signed the contract.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Contract Signature Yes**: Select whether you have signed the contract.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter any terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Copyright Information**: Enter copyright information for the photos.
* **Photo Description**: Describe the photo session.
* **Payment Note**: Enter any payment notes.
* **Client Signature**: Select whether the client has signed the contract.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the signature date.
* **Photo Release**: Select whether you have released the photo.
* **Release Date**: Enter the release date.
* **Photo Portfolio**: Select whether you want to include the photo in your portfolio.

Note: Some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not required.
