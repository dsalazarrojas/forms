# New Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Referral Form is used to collect information from a referrer about a candidate they are referring to your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the corresponding information about the candidate and their referrer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter the first name of the candidate.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter the last name of the candidate.
* **Company** (`company`, text, false): Enter the name of the company the candidate is referring from.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): Enter the job title or position of the candidate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter the email of the candidate.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of the candidate.
* **Referrer Company** (`company_name`, text, false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Referrer Representative** (`company_representative`, text, false): Enter the name of the representative at your organization completing this form.
* **Referrer Representative Email** (`company_representative_email`, email, false): Enter the email of the representative at your organization completing this form.
* **Referrer Representative Phone** (`company_representative_phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of the representative at your organization completing this form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, false): Enter the date of birth of the candidate.
