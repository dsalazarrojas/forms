# real_estate_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_referral_form is a tool for real estate agents to document and track information about their referrals. This form allows agents to record details about the referred clients, properties, and their interactions, providing a comprehensive history for future reference and growth of their business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the real estate agent (page 1, **real_estate_agent**).
2. Provide a brief description of the property being referred (page 1, **real_estate_property**).
3. Explain why you are referring the client to this property (page 1, **referral_reason**).
4. Enter any relevant client information (page 1, **client_info**).
5. Indicate if you have referred the client to other agents in the past (page 1, **agent_referrals**).
6. Confirm if the property is a real estate property (page 1, **real_estate_property_type**).
7. Enter the property address (page 1, **real_estate_address**).
8. Enter the client's email address (page 1, **email**).
9. Enter the client's phone number (page 1, **phone**).
10. Add any additional notes about the client (page 1, **note**).
11. Confirm if you have assigned this referral to another agent (page 1, **assigned_to**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **real_estate_agent** (Page 1): Enter the name of the real estate agent who is referring the client.
* **referral_reason** (Page 1): Explain the reason for the referral, such as the client's specific needs or preferences.
* **client_info** (Page 1): Enter any relevant information about the client, such as their contact details or requirements.
* **agent_referrals** (Page 1): Check if you have referred this client to other agents in the past.
* **real_estate_property_type** (Page 1): Confirm if the property being referred is a real estate property.
* **real_estate_address** (Page 1): Enter the address of the property being referred.
* **email** (Page 1): Enter the client's email address.
* **phone** (Page 1): Enter the client's phone number.
* **note** (Page 1): Add any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **assigned_to** (Page 1): Confirm if you have assigned this referral to another agent.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields for accurate and thorough documentation.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the referral reason and notes fields.
* Double-check the property address for accuracy.
* Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the referral status or interactions.
