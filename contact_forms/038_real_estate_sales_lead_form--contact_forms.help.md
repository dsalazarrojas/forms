# real_estate_sales_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_sales_lead_form is used to collect contact information from potential clients interested in scheduling a showing for a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email" fields with your contact information.
2. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
3. In the "Message" field, provide a brief description of the property you are interested in and any specific needs or requirements you have for the showing.
4. If you are available to meet at a specific date and time for the showing, select "Yes" in the "Showings Scheduling" field and provide the desired date and time in the "Showings Scheduling Options" and "Time Scheduling" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name.
* **Email** (email): Your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number (optional).
* **Message** (text): A brief description of the property you are interested in and any specific needs or requirements you have for the showing.
* **Showings Scheduling** (select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate that you are available to meet at a specific date and time for the showing.
* **Showings Scheduling Options** (date): The date you are available for the showing (if "Yes" is selected in "Showings Scheduling").
* **Time Scheduling** (time): The time you are available for the showing (if "Yes" is selected in "Showings Scheduling")
