# email_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to provide their contact information and preferences for communication with a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the required field.
2. Enter your email address in the required field.
3. Confirm your email address in the required field.
4. If you have a company, enter it in the "Company" field.
5. If you have a job title, enter it in the "Job Title" field.
6. If you have a department, enter it in the "Department" field.
7. If you have a position, enter it in the "Position" field.
8. Select how you would like to be contacted (By Phone, By Email, or By Mail).
9. Select any updates you would like to receive (Newsletters, Promotions, or Surveys).
10. Review and agree to the terms and conditions, privacy policy, and anti-spam policy.

* **Enter your name**: <small>Enter your full name, as it appears on official documents.</small>
* **Enter your email**: <small>Enter your active email address. This will be the primary contact method.</small>
* **Confirm your email**: <small>This is to ensure email address accuracy. Enter the same email address as above.</small>
* **Company**: <small>Enter your company name, if applicable.</small>
* **Job Title**: <small>Enter your job title or role within the company.</small>
* **Department**: <small>Enter your department within the company, if applicable.</small>
* **Position**: <small>Enter your specific position within the company, if applicable.</small>
* **How would you like to be contacted?**: <small>Select one: By Phone, By Email, or By Mail. This determines the preferred communication method.</small>
* **Do you want to receive updates?**: <small>Select any: Newsletters, Promotions, or Surveys. You can choose multiple options.</small>
* **I agree to terms and conditions**: <small>This must be accepted to complete the form. Review the terms and conditions before proceeding.</small>
* **I agree to privacy policy**: <small>This must be accepted to complete the form. Review the privacy policy before proceeding.</small>
