# real_estate_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential leads on real estate properties. It helps us understand the type of property, location, and contact information of the lead.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Contact Information** section with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose the **Real Estate Type** of the property you are inquiring about (Single Family Home, Condo, or Townhouse).
3. Enter the **Location** of the property.
4. Specify the **Price Range** of the property (optional).
5. Choose your **Intention** for inquiring about the property (Buying, Selling, or Renting).
6. Provide your **Contact Number** and **Contact Email** (if applicable).
7. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** (Phone, Email, or Message).
8. Add any additional **Lead Notes** (optional).
9. Type in your **Contact Message** (if you'd like to provide more information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (id: 1)** (`text`, optional): Please fill in your name, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Real Estate Type (id: 2)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of property you are inquiring about (Single Family Home, Condo, or Townhouse).
* **Location (id: 3)** (`text`, optional): Enter the location of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Price Range (id: 4)** (`text`, optional): If you know the price range of the property, enter it here.
* **Intention (id: 5)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your intention for inquiring about the property (Buying, Selling, or Renting).
* **Contact Number (id: 6)** (`text`, optional): Please provide your contact number.
* **Contact Email (id: 7)** (`email`, optional): Please enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Preferred Contact Method (id: 8)** (`select_one`, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Message).
* **Lead Notes (id: 9)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional information about your inquiry.
* **Contact Message (id: 10)** (`text`, optional): Type in your message to us (if you have any).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if applicable).
- Please be accurate with the information you provide.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us.
- You can leave optional fields blank if you don't have the information ready.
- For any additional questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
