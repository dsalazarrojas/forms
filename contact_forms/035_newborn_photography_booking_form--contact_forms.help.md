# Newborn Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a newborn photography session and provide necessary information to the photographer for planning and preparation.
## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow the instructions below and provide the required information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you would like to be contacted on.
* **Session Date**: Choose the date for the photography session from the available dates.
* **Consent to Use Images**: Choose how you would like your images to be used. Please select all that apply.
	+ Yes, use my images in social media
	+ Yes, use my images for marketing purposes
* **Birth Date of Child**: Enter the birth date of your child.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your address where the photographer will visit for the session.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your session, such as special requests or allergies.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that the photographer has the necessary information to provide excellent service.
* Please choose the correct session date to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
