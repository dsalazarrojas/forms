# Contact Us - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Contact Us" form is designed to gather information from users for contact purposes, allowing you to get in touch with our company for sales, support, billing, or general inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field, which is required.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field, which is also required.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you have one.
4. Choose a subject that best describes the reason for your contact in the "Subject" field, which is required.
5. Type out your message in the "Message" field, which is required.
6. Select the department you're contacting us about (e.g., Sales, Support, Billing, or General) in the "Department" field.
7. Choose your preferred urgency level (Low, Normal, or High) in the "Priority" field.
8. Select your preferred contact method (Email or Phone) in the "Preferred Contact" field, which is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address so we can respond accordingly.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Subject (Text, Required)**: Describe the reason for your contact, briefly and clearly.
* **Message (Text, Required)**: Type out your message, including any additional details or questions.
* **Department (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the department you'd like to contact (e.g., Sales, Support, Billing, or General).
* **Priority (Select One, Optional)**: Choose your preferred urgency level (Low, Normal, or High).
* **Preferred Contact (Select One, Required)**: Select how you'd like us to respond (Email or Phone).
