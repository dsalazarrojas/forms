# Multiple Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multiple Contact Form is used to send contact requests to a designated contact person, providing multiple options for the type of contact form that needs to be filled out. This form is intended to be filled out by users who want to initiate contact with the contact person for various reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of contact form you need to fill out from the options provided in the "Select Form" field.
2.  Fill out the fields in the selected form, providing the necessary details as required.
3.  Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send the contact request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Form** (`Select Form`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose the type of contact form you need to fill out from the options provided. Select the option that best suits your needs.
* **Form 1 Fields** (`Form 1 Fields`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input text information for Form 1. Please fill out the field with the necessary details.
* **Form 2 Fields** (`Form 2 Fields`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input text information for Form 2. Please fill out the field with the necessary details.
* **Contact Fields** (`Contact Fields`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input any additional contact information you would like to provide. Please fill out the field with the necessary details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your contact request is handled efficiently.
* If you are unsure about the type of contact form you need to fill out, select the "Form 1" option and fill out the fields as needed.
* The contact person will respond to your contact request as soon as possible after receiving it.
