# Send Free Sample Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in receiving free samples of our product. Fill out the form to send us a request, and we'll get back to you with further instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is where we'll contact you about your sample request.
4. If you have a company or organization, please provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If applicable, fill in your company details in the "Company" field.
6. Choose the type of sample you're interested in from the "Sample" field (multiple options available).
7. In the "Sample Type" field, select the types of samples you'd like to receive (multiple options available).
8. If you'd like to receive the sample, select "True" in the "Send Sample" field.
9. Finally, choose a date and time for when you'd like to receive the sample in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can write them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to provide your first name.
* **Last Name**: A field to provide your last name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone**: A field to provide your phone number if you have a business or organization.
* **Company**: A field to enter your company details if applicable.
* **Sample**: A field to choose the type of sample you're interested in.
* **Sample Type**: A field to select the types of samples you'd like to receive.
* **Send Sample**: A field to indicate if you'd like to receive the sample.
* **Date**: A field to choose a date for when you'd like to receive the sample.
* **Time**: A field to select a time for when you'd like to receive the sample.
* **Note**: A field to add any additional comments or notes about your sample request.
