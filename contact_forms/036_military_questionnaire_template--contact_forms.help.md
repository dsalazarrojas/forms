# military_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect multiple contact information fields for military or defense-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the relevant fields with your contact information.
2.  Review the form to ensure you have filled in all required and optional fields.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter contact information.
    - **Service Member Info** (`service_member_info`, `text`, required: false): Another field to enter contact information.
    - **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required: false): A field for further contact information.
    - **Contact Info 3** (`contact_info_3`, `text`, required: false): A field for additional contact information.
    - **Contact Info 4** (`contact_info_4`, `text`, required: false): A field for more contact information.
    - **Contact Info 5** (`contact_info_5`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter further contact details.
    - **Contact Info 6** (`contact_info_6`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter more contact information.
    - **Contact Info 7** (`contact_info_7`, `text`, required: false): A field for extra contact information.
    - **Contact Info 8** (`contact_info_8`, `text`, required: false): A field for more contact details.
    - **Contact Info 9** (`contact_info_9`, `text`, required: false): The final field to enter contact information.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all fields that you have the necessary information for.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
