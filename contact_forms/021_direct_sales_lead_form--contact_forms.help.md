# Direct Sales Lead Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers who are interested in purchasing a product or service directly from a sales representative. The form will help the sales team understand the customer's needs and preferences, enabling them to provide a more personalized and effective sales experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For any optional fields, choose the options that best fit your needs or interests.
3. If you have any additional information or comments, you can use the note field at the end of the form.
