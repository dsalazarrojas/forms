# Hospice Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Referral Form is used to gather information about a patient's referral to a hospice program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you are capturing all the required and optional fields relevant to the patient's situation.
2. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, referring provider, reason for referral, and phone number into the respective fields.
3. Enter the patient's email address and address if available.
4. Select the patient's status, hospice level of care, referral source, and service area as appropriate for the patient's situation.
5. Enter the hospice name, address, phone number, and email address into the respective fields.
6. Enter any referral notes or comments about the patient's situation.
7. Select the date and time for the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
- **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Referring Provider** (`referring_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional referring the patient.
- **Reason for Referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the referral.
- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's home address if available.
- **Hospice Name** (`hospice_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospice program.
- **Hospice Address** (`hospice_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the hospice program.
- **Hospice Phone** (`hospice_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the hospice program.
- **Hospice Email** (`hospice_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the hospice program.
- **Referral Notes** (`referral_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's situation.
- **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, required): Select the patient's status as Active, Inpatient, or Outpatient.
- **Hospice Level of Care** (`hospice_level_of_care`, select_one, optional): Select the level of care the patient requires, such as Standard, Special, or Hospice Care.
- **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of the referral, such as Home Health, Nursing Facility, or Hospital.
- **Hospice Service Area** (`hospice_service_area`, select_multiple, optional): Select the service area where the patient resides.
- **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the referral in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Hospice Service Location** (`hospice_service_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the hospice services will be provided.
- **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the referral in the format HH:MM.
- **Referral Source Other** (`referral_source_other`, text, optional): Enter any other referral source that does not appear on the list.
- **Hospice Provider** (`hospice_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider that will be providing care.
- **Hospice ID** (`hospice_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID number for the hospice program.
- **NID** (`hospice_nid`, text, optional): Enter the National ID number for the hospice program.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare professional assigned to the patient.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information to support the patient's referral.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the hospice program for clarification.
