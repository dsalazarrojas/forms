## Purpose
This form allows users to join our mailing list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address in the "join_our_mailing_list_now" field.
2. Choose the type of mailing list you wish to join.
3. Confirm your email address in the "confirm_email" field.

* **join_our_mailing_list_now** (`join_our_mailing_list_now`, text, optional): Enter your email address to begin the registration process.

* **mailing_list_type** (`mailing_list_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of mailing list you wish to join (General Mailing List, Special Interest Mailing List, Event Mailing List, Mailing List with Newsletter).
* **Confirm your email address** (`mailing_list_input_2`, email, optional): Confirm that the entered email address is correct.
