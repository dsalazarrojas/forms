# email_confirmation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an email confirmation for a user account. It is a crucial step for users to confirm their email address and receive a verification email from the system. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Confirm whether the email address you entered is correct by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Email" field.
4. If you wish to accept or decline the system's terms and conditions, select the corresponding option in the "Accept Terms" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.
6. Finally, click the "Confirm" button to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the verification email.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive the verification email.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, `select one`, required/optional): Confirm whether the email address you entered is correct.
* **Accept Terms** (`Accept Terms`, `select multiple`, required/optional): Select whether you accept or decline the system's terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click this button to submit your request to the system.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your submission to the system.
* **Error Message** (`Error Message`, `note`, required/optional): This field displays any error messages from the system.
* **Accept** (`Accept`, `select multiple`, required/optional): Select whether you accept or decline the system's terms and conditions. **Note:** This field is used for tracking user response to terms and conditions.
* **Decline** (`Decline`, `select multiple`, required/optional): Select whether you decline the system's terms and conditions.
* **Undetermined** (`Undetermined`, `select multiple`, required/optional): This option is not used in the form but is present in the options for the "Accept Terms" field for tracking purpose.
