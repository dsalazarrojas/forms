# hotel_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hotel_contact_form" is a simple form designed to enable guests to get in touch with hotel staff for assistance, feedback, or general inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Guest Name if you have one. If not, feel free to leave it blank.
2. Provide your Email address so we can get back to you.
3. If you want to be reached quickly, leave your Phone number.
4. Type in your Message, and we'll do our best to respond promptly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (guest_name, Text, Optional): A field to input your name (if applicable). It's okay to leave it blank.
* **Email** (email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (phone, Text, Optional): If you want to be reached quickly, provide your phone number.
* **Message** (message, Text, Optional): Type in your message and we'll do our best to respond promptly.
