# pet_emergency_contact_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet emergency contact information form is designed to collect crucial contact and health information related to your pet in case of an emergency. This form is intended to ensure that you, as the pet owner, can be quickly contacted and informed if your pet's health or well-being is at risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the pet emergency contact information form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so that we can reach out to you in case of an emergency.
2. Provide details about your veterinarian, such as their name, address, and phone number.
3. List any medical conditions or allergies your pet may have.
4. Include any relevant medical information, such as medical supplies or devices used.
5. Add any photos or medical records that may be helpful for veterinary care.
6. Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact, in case you are unable to be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Information** (1): Enter your contact details so we can reach out to you in case of an emergency.
* **Vet Details** (2): Provide your veterinarian's name, address, and phone number, so we can reach out to them if needed.
* **Emergency Contacts** (3): List any additional contact information for emergency contacts, such as family members or friends.
* **Health Information** (4): Enter any medical conditions or allergies your pet may have.
* **Pet Name** (5): Enter your pet's name, which will help identify them in case of an emergency.
* **Pet Type** (6): Select 'Yes' if your pet is a specific type (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
* **Owner Name** (7): Enter your name, so we know who is submitting this form.
* **Owner Email** (8): Enter your email address, which will be used for correspondence.
* **Owner Phone** (9): Enter your phone number, which will be used for contact.
* **Veterinarian Name** (10): Enter your veterinarian's name.
* **Veterinarian Address** (11): Enter your veterinarian's address.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (12): Enter your veterinarian's phone number.
* **Veterinarian Email** (13): Enter your veterinarian's email address.
* **Medication** (14): Enter any medical medications your pet is currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (15): Enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (16): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (17): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (18): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and you.
* **Notes** (19): Enter any additional notes or comments about your pet's health or care.
* **Pet Photo** (20): Upload a photo of your pet, which can be helpful for identification.
* **Medical Information** (21): Enter any relevant medical information about your pet.
* **Medical Condition** (22): Enter any medical conditions or health issues your pet may have.
* **Allergy** (23): Enter any allergies your pet may have.
* **Medical Supplies** (24): Enter any medical supplies or devices used for your pet's care.
* **Medical Device** (25): Enter any medical devices used for your pet's care.
