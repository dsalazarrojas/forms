# Credit Consultation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit a message to the credit consultation service, requesting to schedule a meeting or discuss something.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
3. If you want to provide a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. You don't need to provide a credit score, so leave the "Credit Score" field blank.
5. Leave the "FICO" field blank.
6. In the "Submission Details" field, briefly describe the reason for your message.
7. If you want to add any additional comments, type them in the "Submission Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (Contact Form, text, optional): A text box to type your message requesting a credit consultation.
* **Submitter Name** (Submitter Name, text, optional): This is where you enter your name, this will help us to contact you back.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you want to provide a phone number, enter it here.
* **Credit Score** (Credit Score, number, optional): This field is not required and is meant for users who want to provide their credit score, but it's not necessary.
* **FICO** (FICO, number, optional): This field is not required and is meant for users who want to provide their FICO score, but it's not necessary.
* **Submission Details** (Submission Details, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your message.
* **Submission Comments** (Submission Comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, type them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out your contact form correctly.
* Double-check the information you are entering.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your message and get back to you as soon as possible.
