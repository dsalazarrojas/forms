# Home Remodeling Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a contract for a home remodeling project, outlining the details of the work to be done, payment terms, and other important information for both the contractor and client.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Required Information
Before starting the form, ensure you have all the necessary information for the contractor and client sections. This includes their name, contact information, and other relevant details.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

#### Contractor Information
* **Contractor Information** (`contractor_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contractor's name, address, and contact information.
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, address, and contact information.

#### Project Dates
* **Date of Contract** (`date_of_contract`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the contract was created.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected start date of the project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected end date of the project.

#### Project Details
* **Location of Property** (`location_of_property`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property being remodeled.
* **Description of Work** (`description_of_work`, `text`, required: false): Describe the scope of work to be done.
* **Contract Price** (`contract_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of the project.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Specify any payment terms or conditions.

#### Additional Information
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contractor's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contractor's phone number.
* **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the contract.

## Tips
* Ensure that both the contractor and client have reviewed and agreed on the terms of the contract before signing.
* Keep a copy of the contract for your records and ensure that it is stored securely.
* If you need to make changes to the contract, update the relevant fields and save the changes.
