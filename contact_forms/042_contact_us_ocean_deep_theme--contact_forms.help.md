# Contact Us Ocean Deep Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contact Us" form is a channel for users to communicate with the Ocean Deep Theme team for any questions, suggestions, or concerns they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number (if available) in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the reason for contacting us from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide a brief description of your message in the "Message Details" field.
4. If applicable, select the level of urgency and the best time to contact you.
5. Decide whether you want to subscribe to updates from us.
6. Indicate how you heard about us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dive Into Contact** (header): This is the title of the form.
* **Your Name** (visitor_name, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (visitor_email, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (visitor_phone, text, optional): If available, enter your contact phone number.
* **Your Location** (location, text, optional): Enter your city and state.
* **Reason for Contact** (reason, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate the reason for your contact.
* **Message Details** (details, text, required): Provide a brief description of your message.
* **Urgency** (urgency, select_one, required): Indicate the level of urgency you have regarding your message.
* **Best Time to Contact** (best_time, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the time you are available to be contacted.
* **Subscribe to Updates** (newsletter, select_one, optional): Check this box if you want to receive updates from us.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (referral, select_one, optional): Indicate how you heard about us.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your message to us.
