# Contact Us Grid Frame Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and project details from users who are interested in contacting the company for services such as web design, development, marketing, consulting, or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Get In Touch" page to begin.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
3. Select your preferred service interest from the provided options.
4. Choose your estimated budget range.
5. Select your project timeline.
6. Enter your inquiry message.
7. If you've already been in contact with the company, select the referral source.
8. Finally, select your preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be known.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter its name.
- **Service of Interest** (`service_interest`, select_one, required): Select the service you're interested in, such as Web Design, Development, Marketing, Consulting, or Support.
- **Budget Range** (`budget`, select_one, optional): Select your estimated budget range for the project.
- **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Choose your project timeline, such as Immediately, 1-2 months, 2-3 months, or 3-6 months.
- **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise message about your inquiry.
- **How Did You Find Us** (`how_found`, select_one, optional): If you've already been in contact with the company, select the referral source that led you here.
- **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted, such as Email, Phone, or Video Call.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a timely response.
* Be clear and concise in your message to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, attach them to your email for further reference.
