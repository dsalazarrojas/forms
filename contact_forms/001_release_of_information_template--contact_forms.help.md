# release_of_information_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage release of information documents. It is intended for individuals or organizations that need to release sensitive or confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email.
3. Provide information about the non-profit organization, including its name and address.
4. Select the release specifics, such as the date and time of release, and the method of release (e.g. in-person, by mail, or online).
5. Specify the release status (e.g. released, pending, approved, or denied).
6. Add any release notes or comments.
7. Indicate whether the release form has been completed.
8. Confirm whether an agreement has been made for release.
9. If necessary, provide additional information about other agreements or contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First page** (`first_page`, `text`, required): This page is the starting point for the release of information document.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Contact form** (`contact_form`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional contact information.
* **Non-profit info** (`non_profit_info`, `text`, required): Provide information about the non-profit organization.
* **Non-profit name** (`non_profit_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the non-profit organization.
* **Non-profit address** (`non_profit_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the non-profit organization.
* **Phone numbers** (`phone_numbers`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number(s) of the non-profit organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the non-profit organization.
* **Date of publication** (`date_of_publication`, `date`, required): Enter the date of publication of the release of information document.
* **Release date** (`release_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of release of the information.
* **Release time** (`release_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of release of the information.
* **Release method** (`release_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of release (e.g. in-person, by mail, or online).
* **Release type** (`release_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of release (e.g. confidential, public, restricted, or nonpublic).
* **Release status** (`release_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the release status (e.g. released, pending, approved, or denied).
* **Release notes** (`release_notes`, `note`, required): Add any notes or comments about the release of information.
* **Release comments** (`release_comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments about the release of information.
* **Release form** (`release_form`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the release form has been completed.
* **Release agreement** (`release_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether an agreement has been made for release.
* **Other agreement** (`other_agreement`, `text`, optional): If necessary, provide additional information about other agreements or contacts.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, optional): If necessary, provide any other relevant information.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or guidance.
* Use the form for legitimate purposes only.
