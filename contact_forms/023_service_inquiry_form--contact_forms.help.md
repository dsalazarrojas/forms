# Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users about their service inquiries, which will be used to provide a more efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of service you are inquiring about from the options provided.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Provide a brief message describing your inquiry.
5. Optionally, add any additional information you might want to provide.
6. If you have selected a service type, you can also select any additional services you are interested in.
7. Submit the form to send your inquiry to the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type**: 
  (Service Type, select_one, required) 
  This field allows you to select the type of service you are inquiring about. 
  Please choose one of the options provided:
  - General Service
  - Home Cleaning
  - Yard Service
* **Name**: 
  (Name, text, required) 
  Enter your name to help us identify you and contact you.
* **Email**:
  (Email, email, required) 
  Enter your email address so we can contact you about your inquiry.
* **Phone**: 
  (Phone, text, required) 
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Message**: 
  (Message, text, required) 
  Please provide a brief description of your inquiry.
* **Additional Information**: 
  (Additional Information, text, optional) 
  This field is optional, but you can use it to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Select a Service**:
  (Select a Service, select_multiple, optional) 
  If you have selected a service type, you can also select any additional services you are interested in.
  Please choose one of the options provided:
  - General Service
  - Yard Service
  - Home Cleaning
  - Other

## Tips
- Please make sure to select the correct service type and any additional services you are interested in to ensure we can provide the most accurate service.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact our support team.
