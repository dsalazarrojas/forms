# Popup Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Popup Contact Form is a simple and easy-to-use form that allows users to quickly get in touch with the organization. Fill out the form with your details and concerns, and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please note that we will use this to respond to your query.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
5. In the **Message** field, type in your query or concern.
6. If you're contacting us regarding a specific department, select it from the **Department** dropdown menu.
7. Enter a brief summary of your query in the **Subject** field, if applicable.
8. If you know your date and time of visit or appointment, you can enter it in the **Date** and **Time** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to respond to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Type in your query or concern in this field.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you're contacting us regarding (e.g., Customer Service, Sales, Marketing).
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): Enter a brief summary of your query.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit or appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit or appointment.
