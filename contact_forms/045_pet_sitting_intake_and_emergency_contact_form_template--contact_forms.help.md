**Pet Sitting Intake And Emergency Contact Form Template - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Pet Sitting Intake And Emergency Contact Form Template is designed to gather essential information from pet owners to ensure the comfort and safety of their pets during sitting sessions. This form helps pet sitters and owners understand each other's contact details, pet requirements, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Info**: Enter your name and contact information.
2. Select your pet type (e.g., Cat, Dog, or Other) and enter your pet's age.
3. Provide any veterinary information, including address, name, contact details, and medical notes.
4. Describe your pet's behavioral observations.
5. Enter emergency medical information, including conditions and treatments.
6. Provide contact information for emergency situations.
7. Enter any additional notes.
