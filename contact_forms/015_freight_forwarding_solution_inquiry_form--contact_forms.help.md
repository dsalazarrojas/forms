# ประก Freight Forwarding Solution Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers or users who are interested in exploring the freight forwarding solution offered by the company. The form will help the freight team to understand the customer's needs and preferences, such as the origin and destination cities, freight type, delivery timeframe, shipping rate, service options, and any additional notes or contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the origin city where the goods are being shipped from.
2. Enter the destination city where the goods are being shipped to.
3. Indicate whether you need a freight forwarding solution (Yes/No).
4. Select the delivery timeframe for the shipment (1-5 days).
5. Choose one or more service options for your shipment.
6. Enter any additional notes or instructions about your shipment.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Enter the name of the user who will be assigned to handle your shipment.
10. Enter your name or company name for the submittor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Origin City** (origin_city, text, required: false): Enter the city or location from where the goods are being shipped.
* **Destination City** (destination_city, text, required: false): Enter the city or location where the goods are being shipped to.
* **Freight Type** (freight_type, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you need a freight forwarding solution (Yes/No).
* **Delivery Timeframe** (delivery_timeframe, select_one, required: false): Select the delivery timeframe for the shipment (1-5 days).
* **Shipping Rate** (shipping_rate, number, required: false): Enter the shipping rate (no specific instructions for this field).
* **Service Option** (service_option, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more service options for your shipment.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or instructions about your shipment.
* **Contact E-mail** (contact_email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Assigned User** (assigned_user, text, required: false): Enter the name of the user who will be assigned to handle your shipment.
* **Submitter** (submitted, text, required: false): Enter your name or company name.
