# Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from customers, potential customers, or partners for general contact and event registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**. This is your complete name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your **Email Address**. This is the valid email address we will use to contact you.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**. This is the contact phone number you would like us to use.
4. If you are registering for an event, please answer **Event Participation**. If this is an event registration form, select 'True' if you are attending the event.
5. Enter the name of the **Event** you are registering for (if applicable).
6. If you have any **Dietary Requirements**, please specify them in the text field.
7. If you would like to provide an **Emergency Contact Name** and phone number, please fill in the relevant fields.
8. Finally, if you have any **Additional Information**, please share it with us in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): This is your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter your valid email address. We will use this to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (organization, text, optional): If you are representing an organization or company, please enter its name.
* **Position or Title** (position, text, optional): Enter your role or title within your organization.
* **Mailing Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (city, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (state, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (zip_code, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (country, select_one, required): Select your country from the dropdown list.
* **Event Participation** (event_participation, select_one, required): If this is an event registration form, select 'True' to indicate you are attending the event.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, optional): If you are registering for an event, enter the name of the event.
* **Dietary Requirements** (dietary_requirements, text, optional): If you have any special dietary requirements, please specify them in this field.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact, text, optional): If you would like to provide an emergency contact name, please enter it.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_phone, text, optional): If you would like to provide an emergency contact phone number, please enter it.
* **Additional Information** (additional_info, text, optional): If you have any additional information to share, please enter it in this field.
