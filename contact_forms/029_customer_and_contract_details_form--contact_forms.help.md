# Customer And Contract Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and update customer contract details, including personal information, contract dates, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your address in the physical location field.
5. Select your city from the options in the `city` field.
6. Select your country from the options in the `country` field.
7. If applicable, enter your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field.
8. Enter the date of your contract in the `contract_date` field.
9. Select the term of your contract from the options in the `contract_term` field.
10. Enter the amount of your contract in the `contract_amount` field.
11. Enter the start date of your contract in the `contract_start_date` field.
12. Enter the end date of your contract in the `contract_end_date` field.
13. Select the status of your contract from the options in the `contract_status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your street address or physical location.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Select your city from the provided options.
* **Country** (`country`, Select One, Required): Select your country from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): If you have a date of birth, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your contract in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, Select One, Required): Select the term of your contract from the provided options.
* **Contract Amount** (`contract_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the amount of your contract.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the start date of your contract in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the end date of your contract in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of your contract from the provided options.
