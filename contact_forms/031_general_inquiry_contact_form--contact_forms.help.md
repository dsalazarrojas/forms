# General Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Inquiry Contact Form is used for sending general inquiries or messages to our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the General Inquiry Contact Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field to identify yourself.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, which will be used to respond to your inquiry.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, which will be used to contact you if needed.
4. Enter your message in the **Message** field, detailing your inquiry or comment.
5. Optionally, provide the **Company** field if you are representing an organization and would like to be addressed accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Inquiry** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for your message, e.g., "Order Inquiry" or "Feedback".
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to respond to your inquiry.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person inquiring on behalf of their organization.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for follow-up if needed.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Enter a detailed message explaining your inquiry or comment.
* **Company** (`form_company`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the company or organization you are contacting us on behalf of.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter valid email and phone numbers for proper communication.
- Be clear and concise in your message to ensure a prompt response.
- If you're unsure about the purpose of this form, please contact our support team for clarification.
