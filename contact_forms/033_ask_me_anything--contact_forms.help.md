# Ask Me Anything - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ask Me Anything" form is used to submit questions for live sessions or discussions with a specific person or personality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Who is this question for?" field with the name of the person or personality you are asking the question about.
2.  Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
3.  If you want to receive a direct reply, you can optionally enter your email address in the "Your Email Address" field.
4.  Select the category of your question from the "Question Category" dropdown menu.
5.  Type your question in the "Your Question" field, being respectful and engaging.
6.  Choose whether you want to remain anonymous by selecting "yes" or "no" in the "Is this an anonymous question?" field.
7.  If you've followed the person for a while, you can optionally enter the length of time in the "How long have you followed this person?" field.
8.  If you're from a specific location, you can optionally enter your city or country in the "Where are you from" field.
9.  If you have a website or social media link, you can optionally enter it in the "Your Website or Social Media Link" field.
10.  Confirm that you consent to your question being shared publicly by selecting "I Consent" in the "I consent to my question being shared publicly?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Who is this question for** (AMA Target): Enter the name of the person or personality you are asking the question about.
* **Your Name**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the person asking the question.
* **Your Email Address** (optional): If you want to receive a direct reply, enter your email address.
* **Question Category**: Select the category of your question from the dropdown menu.
* **Your Question**: Type your question in a respectful and engaging manner.
* **Is this an anonymous question?** (yes/no): Choose whether you want to remain anonymous or not.
* **How long have you followed this person?** (optional): If you've followed the person for a while, enter the length of time.
* **Where are you from** (optional): If you're from a specific location, enter your city or country.
* **Your Website or Social Media Link** (optional): If you have a website or social media link, enter it.
* **I consent to my question being shared publicly?** (yes/no): Confirm that you consent to your question being shared publicly.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your real name and a valid email address (if applicable) to ensure you receive a response.
* Be respectful and engaging when asking your question.
* If you want to remain anonymous, select "yes" in the "Is this an anonymous question?" field.
* If you've followed the person for a while, provide the length of time in the "How long have you followed this person?" field.
* Confirm that you consent to your question being shared publicly to avoid any issues.
