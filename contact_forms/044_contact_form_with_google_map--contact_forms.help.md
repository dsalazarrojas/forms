# Contact Form With Google Map - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Contact Form With Google Map" is intended for clients or customers to provide their contact information, send messages or inquiries to the company, and get assistance or information without a phone call or email.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Your Full Name" field with your full name.
2. Enter your primary email address in the "Your Email Address" field.
3. If you wish, you may provide your phone number in the "Your Phone Number" field.
4. Select the store location that this inquiry is about from the "Preferred Store Location" dropdown list.
5. Indicate if the Google Map was helpful in finding our location by selecting one of the provided options.
6. Provide a brief summary of your inquiry in the "Inquiry Subject" field.
7. Enter a detailed message in the "Your Detailed Message" field.
8. Choose how you would like the company to reach out to you ("Email", "Phone Call", or other).
9. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Your Email Address** (email, required): Enter your primary email address where the company can contact you.
* **Your Phone Number** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here to allow the company to contact you by phone.
* **Preferred Store Location** (select_one, required): Select the store location that your inquiry is about.
* **Was the map helpful in finding our location?** (select_one, not required): Indicate whether the Google Map was helpful in finding our location.
* **Inquiry Subject** (text, required): Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Detailed Message** (text, required): Enter a detailed message about your inquiry.
* **Preferred Response Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like the company to contact you back ("Email", "Phone Call", or other).
* **Date of Submission** (date, required): Automatically populated with today's date; please do not modify.
* **Was the Google Map helpful in finding our location?** (select_one, not required): Indicate whether the Google Map was helpful in finding our location.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate information in all fields.
* Double-check your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the company directly or use the provided contact information.
* The form may ask for information that is not immediately relevant to your specific inquiry; please provide as much detail as you can.
* The company will contact you according to your preferred response method.
