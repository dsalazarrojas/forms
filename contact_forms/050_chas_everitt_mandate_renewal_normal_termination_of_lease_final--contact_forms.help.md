# Chas Everitt Mandate Renewal and Lease Termination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chas Everitt Mandate Renewal and Lease Termination form is used to facilitate the renewal or termination of a real estate mandate between a landlord and Chas Everitt. It gathers necessary information to update or end the lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Landlord/Property Owner Full Name, the Physical Address of Property, and the Current Tenant Name.
2. Provide the Date of Normal Lease Termination.
3. Choose whether to Renew Mandate with Chas Everitt or Terminate Mandate upon Lease Expiry.
4. If renewing, enter the Requested Renewal Period and Proposed New Monthly Rental Amount.
5. Schedule the Date for the Final Exit Inspection.
6. Acknowledge and agree to the revised mandate terms.
7. Decide if permission is granted to begin marketing for new tenant immediately.
8. Confirm that the security deposit is being handled in a compliant account.
9. Add any Special Instructions for the Agent, if necessary.
10. Sign with your Landlord Digital Signature.
11. Enter the Date of Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord/Property Owner Full Name**: Enter the full name of the landlord or property owner. This is a required field.
* **Physical Address of Property**: Provide the physical address of the property. This is crucial for accurate documentation and is also a required field.
* **Current Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the current tenant. This information helps in tracking the tenant's identity and is a required field.
* **Date of Normal Lease Termination**: Enter the date when the lease is set to terminate. This is a required field.
* **Landlord's Decision on Mandate Renewal**: Choose between renewing the mandate with Chas Everitt or terminating it. This decision affects the future course of the lease and is a required field.
* **Requested Renewal Period (if renewing)**: If you are renewing the mandate, enter the new period of the lease. This field is optional but only applies if you've chosen to renew.
* **Proposed New Monthly Rental Amount (if renewing)**: If renewing, enter the new proposed rent amount for the renewed period. This field is optional and only applicable if you've chosen to renew.
* **Scheduled Date for Final Exit Inspection**: Schedule a date for the final exit inspection. This is a required field.
* **I acknowledge and agree to the revised mandate terms**: Confirm your understanding and agreement with the revised mandate terms. This is a required field.
* **Permission to begin marketing for new tenant immediately (if renewing)**: Decide if permission to begin marketing for a new tenant can be granted immediately. If renewing, this field is optional but relevant if you've chosen to renew.
* **I confirm the security deposit is being handled in a compliant account**: Confirm that the security deposit is handled in a compliant manner. This field is a required confirmation.
* **Special Instructions for the Agent (if any)**: If there are any special instructions for the agent, enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Landlord Digital Signature**: Enter your digital signature to authenticate your agreement. This is a required field.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting the form. This is a required field.

**Tips**
- Ensure all required fields are filled.
- Be accurate with the information provided.
- If renewing, consider the implications of the new rent amount and renewal period on the future of the lease.
- Review the revised mandate terms carefully before acknowledging.
- Confirm the security deposit handling to ensure compliance.
- If special instructions for the agent are provided, keep them clear and concise.
