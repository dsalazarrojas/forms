# customer_service_appointment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture customer service requests, including their appointment preferences and availability. It helps customers communicate their needs to your company's customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if you would like to schedule an appointment with our customer service team. If yes, select "Yes" from the first dropdown menu.
2. Select a date for the appointment from the calendar.
3. Choose a preferred time for the appointment.
4. Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you.
5. Describe your service request in detail.
6. Let us know if you have any preferred hours of operation for the appointment.
7. If you prefer a specific time, select "Yes" for preferred hours and enter the preferred time.
8. Enter your email address and any additional message or comments.
9. Click the submit button to send in your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Form**: Select "Yes" if you would like to schedule an appointment with our customer service team, and "No" if you just have a question or need general information.
* **appointment_date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment. The calendar will show available dates.
* **appointment_time**: Choose the time you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **service_request**: Describe the service you need in as much detail as possible.
* **available_hours**: If you are available to schedule an appointment outside of regular business hours, select "Yes".
* **preferred_hours**: If you have a preferred time of day to schedule the appointment, select "Yes" and enter the preferred time.
* **preferred_time**: If you have a specific time you would like to schedule the appointment, enter it here.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **customer_message**: Enter any additional comments or messages you would like to include with your request.
* **service_request_description**: Enter any additional details about your service request.
* **assigned_tool**: This field is not currently used, so you do not need to enter anything here.
