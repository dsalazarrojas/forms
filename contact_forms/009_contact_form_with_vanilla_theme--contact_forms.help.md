# Contact Form With Vanilla Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users in a simple and clear way. It's intended for users who want to get in touch with the organization and need help or want to ask a question or provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the reason you are contacting us from the "Reason for Contact" dropdown menu.
5. Type your message in the "Message" field.
6. If you want to subscribe to updates, select the "Subscribe to updates?" checkbox.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name. Please fill in your real name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This field is where you enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): This field is optional, but if you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Reason for Contact** (`Reason for Contact`, select_one, required): This field is a dropdown menu where you select the reason you are contacting us. You can choose from three options: General Question, Feedback, or Problem Report.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, required): This field is where you type your message. Please be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Subscribe to updates?** (`Subscribe to updates?`, select_one, optional): This checkbox is where you decide if you want to subscribe to updates from us.
* **Today's Date** (`Today's Date`, date, required): This field is automatically filled in with the current date.
* **Today's Date** is not editable by the user and is only used for internal purposes.
