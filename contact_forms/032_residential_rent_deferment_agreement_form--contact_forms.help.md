# Residential Rent Deferment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Rent Deferment Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms of a temporary suspension or reduction in rent payments between a landlord and a tenant. It's essential for both parties to agree on the deforment start and end dates, reasons for the deferment, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the landlord's details in the "Landlord Details" field.
2. Provide your own details as the tenant in the "Tenant Details" field.
3. Select the "Deforment Start Date" and "Deforment End Date" for the agreement.
4. Specify the "Rent Deforment Period" and the reason for the deferment in the "Reason for Deforment" field.
5. Enter the "Amount Deforment" (the percentage or amount of rent to be deferred).
6. Read and agree to the "Agreement Terms" (optional).
7. Sign and date the "Signoff Date" field to confirm your agreement.
8. Indicate your agreement status in the "Tenant Agreed" and "Landlord Agreed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Details** (`landlord_details`, text, required): Enter the landlord's name and contact information.
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Deforment Start Date** (`deforment_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the rent deferment period.
* **Deforment End Date** (`deforment_end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the rent deferment period.
* **Rent Deforment Period** (`rent_deforment_period`, text, required): Specify the reason for the rent deferment.
* **Reason for Deforment** (`reason_for_deforment`, text, required): Enter the specific reason for the rent deferment.
* **Amount Deforment** (`amount_deforment`, number, required): Enter the percentage or amount of rent to be deferred.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, note, optional): Read and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Signoff Date** (`signoff_date`, date, required): Date and sign the agreement to confirm your agreement.
* **Tenant Agreed** (`tenant_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Landlord Agreed** (`landlord_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Check if you, as the landlord, have agreed to the terms of the agreement.
