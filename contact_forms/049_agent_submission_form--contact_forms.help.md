# Agent Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Submission Form is designed for agents to provide their professional credentials and contact information to the organization. This form allows us to verify and establish communication with agents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your Full Name, Professional Title, and Email Address.
2. Enter your Phone Number for us to contact you.
3. Select the Broker Name and Broker Office Address.
4. Choose your preferred method of communication, such as Phone Call, SMS / Text, or Email.
5. Enter your License Number (if applicable).
6. Indicate whether you are a licensed professional (Active, Inactive, or Not).
7. Enter the Submission Date.
8. Optionally, include a Brief Message or Reason for Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Professional Title**: Enter your professional title, e.g., Senior Broker, Sales Associate.
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you.
* **Broker Name**: Enter the name of the managing broker or firm.
* **Broker Office Address**: Enter the address of the managing broker or firm's office.
* **Preferred Communication Channel**: Choose how you would like us to contact you, e.g., Phone Call, SMS / Text, Email.
* **License Number**: If you are a licensed professional, enter your license number. This is optional, but recommended for verification purposes.
* **Are you a licensed professional?**: Indicate whether you are a licensed professional (Active, Inactive, or Not).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Brief Message or Reason for Submission**: Optionally, include a brief message or reason for your submission. This can be a few lines of text explaining why you are contacting us today.
