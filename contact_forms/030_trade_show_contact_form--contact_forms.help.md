# Trade Show Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Contact Form is used to collect information from attendees of trade shows or events for contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Contact Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Contact Email address in the field provided.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the field provided.
4. Select the Date of the trade show you attended or will be attending.
5. If applicable, select the Time you plan to attend.
6. Add any additional notes about your contact reason or inquiry in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (Text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your name tag or business card.
* **Contact Email** (Email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, required): Enter your mobile or work phone number.
* **Date** (Date, required): Select the date of the trade show you attended or will be attending.
* **Time** (Time, optional): If you want to specify a particular time slot, select it here. Leave blank if not applicable.
* **Note** (Note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the trade show or event.
