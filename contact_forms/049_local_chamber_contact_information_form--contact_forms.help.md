# Local Chamber Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from visitors to the local chamber. The form is designed to gather essential details such as the person's name, contact email, phone number, and job title to ensure that our team can effectively communicate with them. To use this form, please ensure that you have not already provided this information in a previous form submission. Duplicate submissions can cause confusion and may not be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the `Phone Number` field.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the `Job Title` field.
6. Enter your **Department** name in the `Department` field.
7. Enter your **Job Function** in the `Job Function` field.
8. Enter your **Work Phone** number in the `Work Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, **not required**): Enter your first name in the required field.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, **not required**): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, **not required**): Enter your email address in the format `user@domain.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, **not required**): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXXX-XXXX`.
* **Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, text, **not required**): Enter your current job title in this field.
* **Department** (`contact_department`, text, **not required**): Enter the name of the department you work in.
* **Job Function** (`contact_job_function`, text, **not required**): Enter your current job function or role.
* **Work Phone** (`contact_work_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter your work phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
