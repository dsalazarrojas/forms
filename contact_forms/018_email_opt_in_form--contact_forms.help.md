# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect email addresses from users for contact purposes. You can use this form to send newsletters, promotional materials, or other communications to users who have opted-in to receive emails from you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. The form has multiple pages, and each page has its own section of fields. You can navigate between pages using the page navigation buttons at the top of the form. Fill out each page completely before proceeding to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Email Form 
* **Email Form** (`email_form`, text, required): Enter your email address to receive communications from us.
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, optional): Enter your name or any additional information you'd like to provide.
### Email Address 
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter a contact number or any other relevant information.
### Email Confirmation 
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm your email address to prevent errors.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Re-enter your email address for verification.
### Duplicate Email 
* **Duplicate Email** (`duplicate_email`, text, optional): Enter a duplicate email address (if you'd like to receive emails on multiple devices).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
### Page 8 
* **Page 8** (`page_8`, text, optional): This field is currently not used. You can leave it blank.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, text, optional): Another confirmation of your email address to prevent errors.
* **Page 11** (`page_11`, text, optional): This field is currently not used. You can leave it blank.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
### Page 11 
* **Page 11** (`page_11`, text, optional): This field is currently not used. You can leave it blank.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
### Contact Info 
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.

## Tips
Please make sure to enter a valid email address in the **Email Address** field. This is a required field to prevent spam and abuse. The other fields are optional and can be used to provide additional information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
