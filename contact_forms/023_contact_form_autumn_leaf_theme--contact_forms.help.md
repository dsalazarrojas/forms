# Contact Form Autumn Leaf Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Form is a tool for users to communicate with the organization about seasonal events, vendor opportunities, or provide feedback. This form is intended to facilitate internal communication and event coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to open it in a new tab or window.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your full name.
	* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your valid email address.
	* **Subject** (`message_subject`): Select a category for your message or inquiry (e.g. Event Inquiry, Vendor Opportunity, Feedback, Other).
	* **Message** (`message_content`): Type your message or feedback.
	* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`): The current date will be automatically populated.
3. If applicable, select one or more seasonal events that interest you from the **Interest in Autumn Events** field.
4. If you'd like to specify a preferred visit date, enter it in the **Preferred Visit Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your mobile or home phone number, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
- **Interest in Autumn Events**: Select one or more seasonal events that interest you (e.g. Harvest Festival, Pumpkin Carving Workshop, Autumn Hike, Cider Tasting, Community Potluck).
- **Preferred Visit Date** (`preferred_visit_date`): Enter a date when you'd like to visit (not required).
- **Subject** (`message_subject`): Select a category for your message or inquiry (e.g. Event Inquiry, Vendor Opportunity, Feedback, Other).
- **Message** (`message_content`): Enter your message or feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you'd like to be contacted back, ensure your email and phone number are correctly entered.
* The form will automatically populate the Date of Submission field with today's date.
* You can select multiple events in the **Interest in Autumn Events** field if you're interested in more than one.
* If you have any specific questions or concerns, you can choose the **Other** category for your message subject.
