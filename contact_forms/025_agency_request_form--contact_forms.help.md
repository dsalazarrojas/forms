# Agency Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agency Request Form is designed to help us understand your agency's needs and interests in partnering with us. Please fill out this form to provide us with detailed information about your agency and your partnership goals.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
- If you are unsure about a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
- You can always save your progress and come back to it later by clicking the "Save and Continue Later" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name of Representative** (Representative Name): Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Organization Name** (Organization Name): Enter the name of your organization.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter your agency's contact phone number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Email Address** (Email Address): Enter your agency's email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true

- **Interest Level** (Interest Level - Immediate Need, Information Gathering, Budget Planning):
  - Select one of the available options (Immediate Need, Information Gathering, Budget Planning, Partner Program Inquiry).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true

- **Brief Description of Business Model** (Brief description of your business model):
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Specific Challenges** (Specific challenges you are looking to solve):
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Preferred Method of Contact** (Preferred method of contact):
  - Select one of the available options (Email, Phone Call, Video Conference (Zoom/Teams)).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true

- **Follow-up Call Time** (Best time for a follow-up call):
  - Select a time that works for you.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false

- **Materials to Provide** (Materials you can provide - Brand Guidelines, Market Research, etc.):
  - Select all applicable options.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false

- **Inquiry Details** (Inquiry Details):
  - This field is not required, but please provide any additional comments or details about your inquiry.
  - Type: note
  - Required: false
