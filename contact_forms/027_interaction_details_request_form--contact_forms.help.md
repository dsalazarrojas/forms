# Interaction Details Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect interaction details from users, helping us to tailor our experience and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. (Optional) Provide the date you would like to interact with us.
5. (Optional) Select a time for your interaction.
6. Fill in the name of your company (if applicable).
7. Write a brief message about your interaction details.
8. Choose your preferred contact method: Phone, Email, or Mail.
9. If you have a phone number, fill it in (not required but recommended for phone calls).
10. Click submit to send your interaction details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your interaction, provide it here.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred time for your interaction, select it here.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you represent a company, enter its name.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of your interaction details.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or Mail.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any contact methods you would prefer to be contacted through (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.

## Tips

* Please ensure your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* If you have any specific requests or preferences, include them in the message field.
* If you select Phone as your preferred contact method, please fill in your phone number.
* This form is designed to help us tailor our experience to better support you. Please be specific about your needs and preferences.
