# Antic Dom Kontakt - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Antic Dom Kontakt" form is a contact form designed to collect basic contact information from users. The form serves to facilitate communication and provide a convenient way for users to reach out to the relevant authorities or departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Antic Dom Kontakt" form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Complete" button to start filling in the form.
2. Enter your **Full Name** in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the "Email address" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your **Phone Number** if you prefer contact through this medium.
5. In the "Message" field, type your **Inquiry or Message**.
6. (Optional) If you have a preferred time to be contacted, select your local time zone in the "Preferred time to contact" field.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)** (`name`, `text`, Required): Enter your full name to help us identify you correctly.
* **Email address (Email address)** (`email`, `email`, Required): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for replies and correspondence.
* **Phone number (Phone number)** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): If you prefer to be contacted through this medium, enter your phone number.
* **Message (Message)** (`message`, `text`, Required): Type your inquiry or message in this field.
* **Preferred time to contact (Preferred time to contact)** (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, Optional): Select your local time zone if you have a specific preferred time to be contacted.
