# Contact Us To Sell Your House - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you contact a service to get a free home valuation for your house. Please fill out the form below with your information and we'll be in touch to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide additional information if you have it, but it's not required.
3. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Name (`homeowner_name`, text, required):** This is your full name as the homeowner. Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required):** This is your contact email address. We'll use this to follow up on your submission and provide further information.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, text, required):** This is your phone number. We'll use this to contact you for additional information or questions.
* **Property Address (`property_address`, text, required):** This is the street address of your property. Include the house number, street name, and any additional information that will help us find your property.
* **City (`property_city`, text, required):** This is the city where your property is located.
* **State (`property_state`, text, required):** This is the state where your property is located.
* **ZIP Code (`property_zip`, text, required):** This is the zip code of your property.
* **Property Type (`property_type`, select_one, required):** Please select the type of property you want to sell (e.g. Single Family Home, Condo, etc.).
* **Number of Bedrooms (`bedrooms`, number, required):** This is the number of bedrooms in your property.
* **Number of Bathrooms (`bathrooms`, number, required):** This is the number of bathrooms in your property.
* **Square Footage (`square_footage`, number, optional):** If you know the approximate size of your property, please enter it here.
* **Year Built (`year_built`, number, optional):** If you know the construction year of your property, please enter it here.
* **When Do You Want to Sell (`timeline`, select_one, required):** Please select when you'd like to sell your property (e.g. Immediately, Within 30 days, etc.).
* **Mortgage Status (`mortgage_status`, select_one, optional):** If you have a mortgage, please select your current mortgage status.
* **Estimated Home Value (`estimated_value`, select_one, optional):** Please estimate the value of your property. You can select from the provided options or enter your own value.
* **Reason for Selling (`reason_selling`, select_one, required):** Please select the reason why you want to sell your property (e.g. Relocating, Financial Reasons, etc.).
* **Property Condition (`condition`, select_one, required):** Please select the overall condition of your property (e.g. Excellent, Needs Repairs, etc.).
* **Recent Renovations (`renovations`, text, optional):** If you've made any updates or renovations to your property, please describe them here.
* **Availability for Showings (`showing_availability`, select_one, optional):** Please select when you're available for showings (e.g. Weekdays, Weekends, etc.).
* **Additional Information (`additional_info`, text, optional):** If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please enter it here.

Note that all required fields are marked as "required" in the form. Please fill out these fields accurately and completely for a smooth experience.
