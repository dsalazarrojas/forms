# Headshot Session Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headshot Session Inquiry Form is used to collect information from users for a headshot photography session. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements for the session, ensuring we can tailor the experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your preferred Phone Number and Date for the session.
3. Select your preferred Session Type (e.g., Wedding, Family, etc.).
4. Choose your preferred Start Time and End Time for the session.
5. Provide any additional Message or Notes about your session requirements.
6. (Optional) Select one or more individuals to be assigned to from the list of options.
7. Once you've completed all the fields, submit the form to proceed with your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your given name at birth.
* **Last Name**: Your surname (family name).
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: A phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date**: The date you would like to schedule the session.
* **Session Type**: The type of session you are interested in (e.g., Wedding, Family, etc.).
* **Start Time**: The time you would like the session to begin.
* **End Time**: The time you would like the session to end.
* **Message**: Any additional information or requests you have for the session.
* **Notes**: Any further details or reminders you'd like to share.
* **Assigned To**: Select one or more individuals from the list to be assigned to for your session (e.g., photographer, assistant, etc.).
* **Form Submitted**: The date and time you submitted this form.
