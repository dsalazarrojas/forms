# Distributor Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Contact Information Form is used to collect and store contact information from distributors. This information is essential for our team to communicate effectively and efficiently with our distributors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Company Name: This is the name of your company.
2. Enter the Contact Person: This is the name of the person to contact within your company.
3. Enter the Job Title: This is the job title of the contact person (optional).
4. Enter the Company Address: This is the address of your company.
5. Enter the Email: This is the email of your company.
6. Enter the Phone: This is the phone number of your company.
7. Enter the Company Website (optional): This is the website of your company.
8. Enter the Fax (optional): This is the fax number of your company.
9. Enter the Contact Email (optional): This is the email of the contact person.
10. Enter the Contact Phone (optional): This is the phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter Notes (optional): This is any additional information you'd like to provide about your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact within your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of your company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your company.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of your company.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Notes** (`company_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your company.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any changes to your company's information, please update this form accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
