# safari_villa_guest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "safari_villa_guest_registration_form" is a form for guests to provide information about their stay at the safari villa. This form helps the guest's management team to prepare for their arrival and stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as `true` in the form.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of Arrival and Date of Departure.
5. Select your preferred Accommodation Type.
6. Enter your Room Number.
7. If you have any Special Requests, you can enter them in the Special Requests field.
8. If you have any Comments, you can enter them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, `date`, required): Please select the date when you arrive at the safari villa.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, `date`, required): Please select the date when you leave the safari villa.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of accommodation you prefer (Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, Safari Villa).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, `number`, required): Please enter the room number you have been assigned.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them here.
