# Request A Free Demo 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a free demo of a product or service. Fill out the form to provide your details and schedule a meeting at your convenience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address to receive communication about your demo request.
3. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your demo request.
4. Select your location where you'd like to receive your demo.
5. Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the demo.
6. If scheduled, enter the date and time for the demo. If not scheduled, leave blank and we'll contact you to arrange a suitable date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (required): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* **Last name** (required): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address to receive communication about your demo request and any follow-up questions.
* **Phone number** (required): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you about your demo request.
* **Location** (required): Select your location where you'd like to receive the demo. This will help us tailor the demo according to your local needs.
* **Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the demo. This helps us understand your requirements and interests.
* **Date** (required): If scheduled, enter the date for the demo. This should be the date you are available to receive the demo. If not scheduled, leave blank and we'll contact you to arrange a suitable date.
* **Time** (optional): If scheduled, enter the time for the demo, otherwise leave blank and we'll contact you to arrange a suitable time.
