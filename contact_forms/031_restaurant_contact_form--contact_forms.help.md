The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from users, including their name, phone number, email, and a message. It is intended to be used by restaurant staff to communicate with customers.

The form consists of several fields that require different types of information:

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the restaurant you are contacting.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Describe the reason for your contact.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you are requesting a specific time for your contact, enter it here.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you are requesting a specific date for your contact, enter it here.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click to submit your contact information.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2.  Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3.  Enter the name of the restaurant you are contacting in the **Restaurant Name** field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6.  Enter a description of the reason for your contact in the **Message** field.
7.  If necessary, enter the specific time you are requesting in the **Time** field.
8.  If necessary, enter the specific date you are requesting in the **Date** field.
9.  Click the **Submit** button to send your contact information.

Tips:

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the instructions above for clarification.
*   The **Restaurant Name** field is required to be filled in for the form to be valid.
*   The **Phone Number** and **Email** fields require a valid phone number and email address respectively.
*   The **Message** field should contain a clear and concise description of your reason for contacting the restaurant.
