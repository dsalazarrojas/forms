# Simple Vendor Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from vendors for the purpose of establishing or maintaining a business relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
2. Enter your vendor's address in the "Address" field.
3. Enter your vendor's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided in the "Contact Method" field.
5. If you have a specific contact preference (e.g., "Other"), select it from the "Contact Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name (text)**: Enter the name of the vendor you are contacting.
* **Address (text)**: Enter the address of the vendor you are contacting.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the vendor you are contacting.
* **Contact Type (select_one)**: Select the type of contact method you have with the vendor (e.g., "Website", "Phone", "Email", "Other").
* **Contact Method (select_multiple)**: Select the methods you prefer to contact the vendor (e.g., "Call", "Text", "Email")
