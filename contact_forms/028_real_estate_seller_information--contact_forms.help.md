# real_estate_seller_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting seller information and property details in the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the seller's name in the **real_estate_seller_name** field.
2. Enter the seller's contact number in the **contact_number** field.
3. Enter the seller's email address in the **seller_email** field.
4. Enter the property address in the **property_address** field.
5. Select the type of property from the **property_type** dropdown menu.
6. Select the status of the property from the **property_status** dropdown menu.
7. Describe the property in the **property_description** field.
8. Enter the date of sale in the **date_of_sale** field.
9. Enter the sale price in the **sale_price** field.
10. Enter the seller's agreement in the **seller_agreement** field.
11. Enter the contact person's name in the **contact_person** field.
12. Enter the contact person's title in the **person_title** field.
13. Enter the contact number of the contact person in the **contact_number2** field.
14. Enter the contact email of the contact person in the **email2** field.
15. Enter the address of the contact person in the **property_address2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **real_estate_seller_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the seller.
* **contact_number** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the contact number of the seller.
* **seller_email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the seller.
* **property_address** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **property_type** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Mixed Use).
* **property_status** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the status of the property (For Sale, Rented, Leased).
* **property_description** (Text, Required/Optional): Describe the property.
* **date_of_sale** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date of sale.
* **sale_price** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the sale price.
* **seller_agreement** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the seller's agreement.
* **contact_person** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **person_title** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the contact person's title.
* **contact_number2** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **email2** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **property_address2** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the relevant authorities in your real estate agency or office.
