# Contact Form With Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or visitors to send messages and share files with the organization or company.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Nome Completo" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
3. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Telefone" field.
4. Enter the subject of your message in the "Assunto" field.
5. Enter your message in the "Mensagem" field.
6. Confirm if you have files to upload by selecting "Sim" or "Nao" in the "Voce tem arquivos para enviar?" field.
7. If you have files to upload, describe them in the "Descricao do Arquivo" field.
8. Select the type of file you are uploading (Image, Video, Documento, or Outro) in the "Tipo de Arquivo" field.
9. Enter the date of submission in the "Data" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nome Completo (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **E-mail (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Telefone (text)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Assunto (text)**: Enter the subject of your message.
* **Mensagem (text)**: Enter your message.
* **Voce tem arquivos para enviar? (select_one)**: Confirm if you have files to upload.
	+ Select "Sim" (Yes) if you have files to upload.
	+ Select "Nao" (No) if you don't have files to upload.
* **Descricao do Arquivo (text)**: If you selected "Sim" in the previous field, describe the files you are uploading.
* **Tipo de Arquivo (select_one)**: If you selected "Sim" in the previous field, select the type of file you are uploading.
	+ Select "Imagem" (Image) for image files.
	+ Select "Video" for video files.
	+ Select "Documento" for document files.
	+ Select "Outro" for other types of files.
* **Data (date)**: Enter the date of submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have files to upload, make sure to describe them in the "Descricao do Arquivo" field.
* Select the correct type of file you are uploading to ensure it is processed correctly.
* Enter the correct date of submission to ensure accurate record-keeping.
