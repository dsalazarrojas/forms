# Request Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit a complaint or feedback about our services. This form will help us to understand your concerns and provide a resolution in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Complaint field with a clear and concise description of your issue.
2. Provide a detailed description of your complaint in the complaint_description field.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu to indicate whether you would like to be contacted in regards to your complaint.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field, this will allow us to contact you directly.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, optional.
6. Select the Category related to your complaint from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Complaint** (`user_complaint`, `text`, required): Please describe the issue or problem you are experiencing in this field. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your complaint, including any relevant details or background information that will help us to understand your issue.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you would like to be contacted directly regarding your complaint, or "No" if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, optional.
* **Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category related to your complaint from the list.

## Tips
- Please be as clear and concise in your complaint description to ensure we can address your issue efficiently.
- If you have any supporting documentation or evidence related to your complaint, please attach it to your submission.
- If you have already submitted a complaint, please do not re-submit if the issue has already been resolved.
