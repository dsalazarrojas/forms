# Commercial: Rent Deferment (Payment Holiday) Agreement, Incorporating Deed Of Surety - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a Commercial Rent Deferment (Payment Holiday) Agreement, which is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a rent deferment between a landlord and a tenant. The agreement includes details about the parties involved, the leased premises, the deferment terms, and the deed of surety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Enter the full legal name of the landlord and tenant.
3. Provide the address and email of the landlord for formal notifications.
4. Enter the tenant's company registration number if applicable.
5. Specify the full address of the leased premises as per the original lease agreement.
6. Enter the original lease agreement reference number if available.
7. Enter the regular monthly rent amount, excluding utilities and extras.
8. Set the start and end dates of the payment holiday.
9. Enter the date when the deferred rent starts being repaid.
10. Enter the number of repayment installments.
11. Provide the full name of the surety/guarantor.
12. Enter the surety's ID or registration number.
13. Enter the surety's physical address.
14. Enter the contact phone number of the surety.
15. The landlord and tenant must formally agree to the terms of the deferment agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name of Landlord** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the landlord as per their identification documents.
* **Landlord Contact Address and Email** (`landlord_contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter the address and email of the landlord for formal notifications.
* **Full Legal Name of Tenant** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the tenant as per their identification documents.
* **Tenant Company Registration Number** (`tenant_registration_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the tenant's company registration number if applicable.
* **Full Address of the Leased Premises** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full address of the leased premises as per the original lease agreement.
* **Original Lease Agreement Reference Number** (`lease_reference_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the original lease agreement reference number if available.
* **Regular Monthly Rent Amount** (`monthly_rent_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the regular monthly rent amount, excluding utilities and extras.
* **Payment Holiday Start Date** (`holiday_start_date`, `date`, required): Set the start date of the payment holiday.
* **Payment Holiday End Date** (`holiday_end_date`, `date`, required): Set the end date of the payment holiday.
* **Repayment Commencement Date** (`deferred_rent_repayment_start`, `date`, required): Set the date when the deferred rent starts being repaid.
* **Number of Repayment Installments** (`repayment_installments`, `number`, required): Enter the number of repayment installments.
* **Full Legal Name of the Surety (Guarantor)** (`surety_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the surety/guarantor.
* **Surety ID or Registration Number** (`surety_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the surety's ID or registration number.
* **Surety Physical Address** (`surety_physical_address`, `text`, required): Enter the surety's physical address.
* **Surety Contact Phone Number** (`surety_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the surety.
* **Landlord - I agree to the terms of this deferment agreement** (`landlord_acceptance_check`, `select_one`, required): The landlord must formally agree to the terms of the deferment agreement.
* **Tenant - I agree to the terms of this deferment agreement** (`tenant_acceptance_check`, `select_one`, required): The tenant must formally agree to the terms of the deferment agreement.
* **Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
