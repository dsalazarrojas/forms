# Contact Me TRD - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request resume services from The Resume Dude (TRD). Fill out this form to provide necessary information about yourself and your career goals, and a member of the TRD team will be in touch to discuss your resume needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Contact Me TRD" form.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field (optional).
5. Select the type of service you are requesting from the "Service needed" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the approximate number of years you have been working in your field in the "Years of experience" field.
7. Enter the role or industry you are aiming for in the "Target role or industry" field (optional).
8. If you would like, provide a preferred availability for consultation in the "Availability for consultation" field.
9. Enter any additional information you would like to provide in the "Additional notes" field (optional).
10. Click "Submit" to send your request to TRD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Your full legal name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: A valid email address where you can be reached for correspondence about your resume services.
* **Phone number**: An optional field to provide a contact number for phone calls or messages.
* **Service needed**: Select one of the following services:
	+ Resume review
	+ Resume writing
	+ Cover letter
	+ LinkedIn profile
	+ Career coaching
* **Years of experience**: Enter the approximate total number of years you have been working in your field.
* **Target role or industry**: Enter the role or industry you are aiming for (e.g. software engineer, marketing manager).
* **Availability for consultation**: Provide your preferred days or times for a consultation with a TRD representative.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any other information that would be helpful for the TRD team to prepare for your resume services.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to get the most out of TRD's services.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Additional notes" field.
- A member of the TRD team will contact you to discuss your resume services and schedule a consultation if needed.
