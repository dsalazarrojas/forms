# Emergency Contact Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect emergency contact information for employees or individuals, which can be useful in situations such as natural disasters, accidents, or other critical events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with your information, or ask the person to fill in the form for you.
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency in the "Emergency contact name" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact number" field.
8. Select the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact person from the "Emergency contact relationship" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the address of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact address" field.
10. Enter the city of the emergency contact person's address in the "City" field.
11. Enter the state of the emergency contact person's address in the "State" field.
12. Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person's address in the "Zip" field.
13. Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact person from the provided options.
* **Emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the emergency contact person's address.
* **State** (`emergency_contact_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the emergency contact person's address.
* **Zip** (`emergency_contact_zip`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency contact notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person.
