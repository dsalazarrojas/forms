# Customer Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store customer information for customer profiles. The data collected will be used to maintain customer records and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in all the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Customer Profile Form 1** (`customer_profile_form_1`, text, false)
This field is used for a brief description of the customer's profile, such as a company name or a personal introduction.

### **Name** (name, text, false)
Please enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification or documents.

### **Email** (email, email, false)
Enter the customer's email address to facilitate communication and contact.

### **Phone** (phone, text, false)
Provide the customer's phone number for easier contact and communication.

### **Address** (address, text, false)
Enter the customer's address to ensure accurate delivery of services or correspondence.

### **City** (city, text, false)
Specify the city where the customer is located to provide regional services or correspondence.

### **State** (state, select_one, false)
Select the state from the list where the customer is located.

### **Zip** (zip, text, false)
Enter the customer's postal code for efficient postal services or correspondence.

### **Birthday** (birthday, date, false)
Specify the customer's birthday for demographic purposes or special events.

### **Anniversary** (anniversary, date, false)
Enter the customer's anniversary for special occasions or reminders.

### **Note** (note, text, false)
Add any additional comments or notes about the customer's profile.

### **Contact Note** (contact_note, text, false)
Provide any contact information for the customer's representative or point of contact.

### **Contact Person Note** (contact_person_note, text, false)
Add any notes about the customer's contact person or representative.

### **Customer Type** (customer_type, text, false)
Indicate the customer's type, such as individual or business.

### **Customer Type Other** (customer_type_other, text, false)
Enter any other customer type not listed above.

Tips:
- Fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate customer data.
- Use correct format for dates (DD-MM-YYYY or MM/DD/YY).
- Select the correct state from the options list.
- If you have any additional information, use the "Note" field to add it.
