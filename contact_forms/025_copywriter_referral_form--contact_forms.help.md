# Copywriter Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from copywriters who are interested in collaborating with our company. We use this form to gather context about their background and how they were referred to us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed. Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information about yourself, so we can better understand your qualifications and potential fit for future projects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Copywriter Referral Form** (`copywriter_referral_form_title`, text, not required): This is a title field that you can fill in to provide a brief description of your work or a reference to your portfolio.
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Please enter your job title or current position.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, not required): If you are currently employed or have worked with a company in the past, please enter their name.
* **How did you know us?** (`how_did_you_know_us`, text, not required): If you have had previous interactions with our company, please describe how we met.
* **Referred by** (`referred_by`, text, not required): If someone from our company referred you to us, please enter their name.
* **Why referred** (`why_referred`, text, not required): Please provide context on why you were referred to us.
* **How did we meet?** (`how_did_we_meet`, text, not required): If you have had previous interactions with our company, please describe how we met.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about yourself.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* If you have any attachments or portfolio links, please provide them separately.
