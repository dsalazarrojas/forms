# Massage Therapy Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Inquiry Form is designed to allow clients to express their interests and preferences for a massage therapy appointment. This form helps the massage therapist understand the client's needs and schedule a suitable appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Select your preferred massage type from the options listed (e.g. Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports).
*   Enter your email address if you prefer to be contacted via email.
*   Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, text).
*   Specify a date and time for your preferred appointment.
*   If you have any additional comments or preferences, enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
*   If you have a preferred therapist, you can add that information in the "Therapist preferences" field.
*   Confirm your email address for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Preferences** (Client Preferences, Note, No): This field is where you can provide any information that you think will help the massage therapist understand your needs and preferences.
* **Preferred Massage Type** (preferred massage type, Select Multiple, No): Choose from the options listed (Swedish, Deep Tissue, Sports) to express your interest in a specific type of massage.
* **Contact Details** (contact details, Email, No): If you prefer to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (preferred contact method, Select One, No): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, text).
* **Request an appointment** (appointment request, Date, No): Specify a date and time for your preferred appointment.
* **Therapist Preferences** (therapist preferences, Note, No): If you have a preferred therapist, you can provide that information here.
* **Preferred Time** (preferred time, Time, No): If you have a preferred time for your appointment, you can specify that here.
*   **Additional Comments** (additional comments, Note, No): This field is for any additional comments or preferences you may have.
* **Email Confirmation** (Email Confirmation, Email, No): Confirm your email address to ensure accurate communication with you.
