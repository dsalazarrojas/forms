# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) form is used to gather information from customers about their contact preferences and other related inquiries. This form is designed to be a simple and efficient way to capture relevant details that will help our team better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your contact form preferences using the "Contact Forms" dropdown menu.
2. Choose whether you have "Business Hours" or not.
3. If you have "Frequently Asked Questions", select the option that best describes your experience.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide any additional information in the "Description" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Forms** (`contact_forms`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the types of contact forms you are interested in (e.g. online form, email, phone, or in-person).
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time, optional):
	+ Choose a time that best describes your business hours, if any.
* **Frequently Asked Questions** (`frequently_asked_questions`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have frequently asked questions about our services/products, 'No' otherwise.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about your inquiry or question.
