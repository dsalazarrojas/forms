# Help Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Help Request Form" is designed to help our support team understand your needs and provide assistance in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Request Type" that best describes your need.
2. Enter a detailed description of your request in the "description" field.
3. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the "contact_number" field.
4. If you have an email, enter it in the "email" field.
5. If you have an attachment or document that provides more information, enter the details in the "attachment" field.
6. Submit your request by clicking the "Submit Request" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Type** (select_one): Choose the type of request you need help with. This will help us understand the nature of your request.
* **Submit Request** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your request, including any relevant details that will help us assist you.
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your request, including any relevant information that will help us provide the best support.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you would like to be contacted directly.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Attachment** (text, optional): If you have an attachment or document that provides more information, enter the details in this field.
