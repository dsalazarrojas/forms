# Simple Business Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Business Contact Form is a tool used to collect contact information from customers or clients for a business. It is usually placed on a website and is intended to provide a straightforward way for visitors to contact the business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the 'Contact Form' page.
2. Fill out the required fields with your contact information.
   - **Name (name)**: Enter your name.
   - **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
   - **Message (message)**: Enter your message.
3. If desired, you can also enter your **Phone (phone)**, but it is not necessary.
4. Click the 'Submit' button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Form (contact_form)**: This field is not editable and is used to display the page title.
- **Name (name)**: Enter your name to be included in the message that is sent to us.
- **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone (phone)**: If you have a phone number, you can enter it here.
- **Message (message)**: Enter the message or question you have for us.
   - Note: This is the main area for you to ask your question or provide your feedback.
   - Try to be as clear and concise as possible.
- **Submit**: Click this button to send your message to us.
