# Contact Us Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Contact Us Template" form is used for customers or users to send messages to the team, providing them with an opportunity to inquire about sales, support, or other general information. This form is essential for effective communication and helps the team respond to inquiries in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contact Us" page from the contact options.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Provide a subject for your message in the "Subject" field. This is a required field.
6. Choose the category of your inquiry from the "Category" dropdown menu. This is a required field.
7. Type your message in the "Message" field. This is a required field.
8. Select the urgency level of your inquiry by choosing from the "Priority" dropdown menu (optional).
9. If you want to attach a file to your message, you can do so in the "Attachment" field (optional).
10. Choose how you would like to be contacted in response to your inquiry from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu. This is a required field.
11. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service by selecting "I Agree" in the "Consent to Terms" field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)**: This is the field where you enter your full name. It is required to be filled in order for the team to properly address you.
* **Email (Email)**: This is the field where you enter your email address. It is required to be filled in order for the team to contact you directly.
* **Phone (Phone)**: This field is optional, but it's a good idea to fill it in so the team can contact you via phone if needed.
* **Subject (Subject)**: This is the field where you provide a brief subject for your message. It is required to be filled in.
* **Category (Category)**: This is the field where you select the type of inquiry you have. It is required to be filled in.
* **Message (Message)**: This is the field where you type your message to the team. It is required to be filled in.
* **Priority (Priority)**: This field is optional, and it's a way for you to indicate the urgency level of your inquiry.
* **Attachment (Attachment)**: This field is optional, and it's a way for you to attach a file to your message if needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred Contact Method)**: This field is required, and it's a way for you to choose how you would like to be contacted in response to your inquiry.
* **Consent to Terms (Consent to Terms)**: This field is required, and it's a way for you to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
