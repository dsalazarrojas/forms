# Ask A Question Black Responsive Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to send a question or message to the contact team, who will typically respond within 24 hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Contact Us" tab to start the process.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Select a priority level for your inquiry from the "Inquiry Priority" dropdown.
4. Choose a preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown.
5. If you provide a best time to call, you can specify it in the "Best Time to Call" field.
6. Check the "Sign up for our monthly updates" checkbox if you wish to receive regular updates from us.
7. Complete the security check at the end to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Contact Us --** (contact_header_black)
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Your Full Name** (full_name_black)
	+ This is the field where you enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (email_addr_black)
	+ This is the field where you enter your email address.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (subject_inquiry_black)
	+ This is the field where you enter the subject of your question or issue.
* **Your Question or Message** (message_text_black)
	+ This is the field where you enter the full text of your question or message.
* **Inquiry Priority** (priority_level_black)
	+ This is a dropdown menu where you select the priority level of your question or issue (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (preferred_contact_method_black)
	+ This is a dropdown menu where you select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone Call, or Text Message).
* **Best Time to Call** (best_time_to_call_black)
	+ This is an optional field where you specify a best time to call.
* **Sign up for our monthly updates** (newsletter_opt_in_black)
	+ This is an optional checkbox where you choose to receive regular updates from us.
* **Please complete the security check below** (captcha_note_black)
	+ This is a security check that must be completed before submitting the form.
