# Interest Inquiry Phone Call Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interest Inquiry Phone Call Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in scheduling a phone call with a sales or support representative. This form helps the representative quickly and efficiently gather the necessary details to follow up with the user and address their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email (if applicable) to ensure we can reach out to you for further discussions.
3. Enter your Phone number so we can contact you at a convenient time.
4. Write a message describing your interest or inquiry.
5. Select the preferred time of the follow-up call (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
6. Optionally, enter a specific time for the follow-up call and date for the follow-up call.
7. If you have any specific note or comment about the follow-up call, enter them in the "Follow up note" field.
8. Finally, submit the form and a representative will get in touch with you soon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email, optional): This is your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Message** (text, optional): Describe your interest or inquiry here.
* **Preferred** (select_one, required): Select the preferred time of the follow-up call (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Follow up time** (time, optional): If you select a specific time for the follow-up call, enter it here.
* **Follow up date** (date, optional): If you select a specific date for the follow-up call, enter it here.
* **Follow up note** (text, optional): Enter any specific notes or comments about the follow-up call here.
* **Follow up phone** (text, optional): Enter any other phone number here (if applicable).
* **Assign to** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Assigned time** (time, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Assigned date** (date, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Assigned user** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted** (date, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted by** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted time** (time, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted date** (date, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Submitted user** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Status** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.
* **Follow up status** (text, optional): This field is for internal use only.

Note: This guide is for the user's information only, and not for internal fields. The form fields that are not relevant to the user should not be filled in.
