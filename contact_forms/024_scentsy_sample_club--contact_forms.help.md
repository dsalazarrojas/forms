# scentsy_sample_club - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a simple contact method for users to get in touch with your organization. This form is used to collect contact information and messages from users, which will be reviewed and responded to by the organization's staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Us" link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (`_contact_form_`, **Contact Us**, optional): 
This is the main section of the form. Click on the "Contact Us" link to enter a message or provide feedback.
* **Name** (`_name_`, **Name**, optional): Enter your name to help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **Email** (`_email_`, **Email, optional): Enter your email address so we can respond to your message or contact you directly.
* **Message** (_message_, **Message, optional): Write your message or feedback in the text area.
* **Phone** (_phone_, **Phone, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any) to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* Be as clear and concise as possible when writing your message.
* If you are contacting us for a specific reason, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
