# Bridal Makeup Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The bridal makeup contact form is designed to gather information about the bride's contact, preferences, and service requests for her special day. This form will help the bridal makeup service provider understand the bride's needs and ensure a customized and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the best possible service.
2. Start by entering the bride's contact information.
3. Select the relevant options for the bride's details and preferences.
4. Provide any additional notes or requests.
5. Review and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bride Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the bride's name as it appears on her identification documents.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, optional): Select the bride's age range.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the bride's primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the bride's contact phone number.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required): Enter the approximate date of the wedding.
* **Days Until Wedding** (`days_until_wedding`, number, optional): Enter the number of days until the wedding date.
* **Groom Name** (`groom_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the groom, if applicable.
* **Type of Wedding** (`wedding_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of wedding.
* **Wedding Location** (`wedding_location`, text, required): Enter the city or venue location of the wedding.
* **Bridal Party Size** (`bridal_party_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people getting makeup.
* **Bride Makeup Service Requested** (`bride_makeup_service`, select_one, required): Select if the bride has a makeup service request.
* **Attendants Needing Makeup** (`attendants_services`, number, optional): Enter the number of attendants needing makeup.
* **Desired Makeup Style** (`makeup_style`, select_one, required): Select the bride's desired makeup style.
* **Your Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, select_one, optional): Select the skin tone for color matching purposes.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, optional): Select the skin type.
* **Makeup Allergies or Sensitivities** (`makeup_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Eye Color and Preferences** (`color_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select the eye color and preferences.
* **Hair Color and Style** (`hair_color`, text, optional): Enter hair color and style for coordination purposes.
* **Wedding Gown Color** (`gown_color`, text, optional): Enter the wedding gown color for coordination purposes.
* **Makeup Color Theme** (`makeup_theme`, select_multiple, optional): Select the preferred color theme.
* **Trial Session Requested** (`trial_needed`, select_one, required): Select if a trial makeup session is requested.
* **Preferred Trial Date** (`trial_date`, date, optional): Enter the preferred trial date.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select the budget range.
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the service location preference.
* **Additional Services of Interest** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services of interest.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements or notes.
