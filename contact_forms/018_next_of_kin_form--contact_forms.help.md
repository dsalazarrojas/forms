# Next Of Kin Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Next Of Kin Form is used to collect contact information of a family member or close relative who can be contacted in emergency situations or for other purposes such as insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:

1. Fill out the **First Name** field with the first name of the next of kin.
2. Fill out the **Last Name** field with the last name of the next of kin.
3. For the **Relationship Type**, select the type of relationship between you and the next of kin (e.g. 'Yes' or 'No').
4. If the answer to the **Relationship Type** question is 'Yes', fill out the **Name** field with the full name of the next of kin.
5. Fill out the **Date Of Birth** field with the birth date of the next of kin.
6. For the **Contact Phone** field, fill out the phone number of the next of kin.
7. Fill out the **Address** field with the address of the next of kin.
8. Fill out the **Email** field with the email address of the next of kin.
9. Fill out the **Contact Name** field with the full name of the person who will be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): The first name of the next of kin.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): The last name of the next of kin.
* **Relationship Type** (`relationship_type`, select_one, required): The type of relationship between you and the next of kin.
	+ Select 'Yes' to indicate that the person listed is a family member.
	+ Select 'No' if the person is not a family member.
* **Name** (`relationship_name`, text, optional): The full name of the next of kin.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): The birth date of the next of kin.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the next of kin.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): The address of the next of kin.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address of the next of kin.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): The full name of the person who will be contacted.
