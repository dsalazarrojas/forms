# Contact The Webmaster Form 3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for users to report website issues, request content updates, or provide design feedback to the webmaster.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the category that best fits your request**: Choose from "Bug report", "Content update", "Design request", or "Other" to help the webmaster understand the nature of your issue.
2. **Provide the page URL (if applicable)**: If your issue is related to a specific page, enter the URL to help the webmaster locate the problem.
3. **Describe the issue or request**: Be as detailed as possible so the webmaster can reproduce or implement changes.
4. **Specify the severity level**: Choose from "Low", "Medium", "High", or "Critical" to help prioritize the webmaster's response.
5. **Optional: Attach screenshots or specify a recommended action**: If you have screenshots or a suggested solution, you can attach them or describe them in this field.
6. **Enter your contact email**: This is where the webmaster will reach out to you to discuss or follow up on your issue.
7. **Add any additional comments**: Provide any other information that might be helpful for the web team to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry type** (`Inquiry type`, `select_one`): Select the category that best fits your request from the dropdown menu.
	+ Helps the webmaster understand the nature of your issue.
* **Page URL affected** (`Page URL`, `text`): Provide the page address if your issue is related to a specific page.
	+ Helps the webmaster locate the problem.
* **Description of issue or request** (`Description`, `text`): Be as detailed as possible so the webmaster can reproduce or implement changes.
	+ Provides essential information for the webmaster to resolve the issue.
* **Browser and device used** (`Browser and device used`, `text`): Specify the browser and device you are using to help with debugging responsive issues.
	+ Helps the webmaster understand the context of your issue.
* **Severity level** (`Severity level`, `select_one`): Choose from "Low", "Medium", "High", or "Critical" to help prioritize the webmaster's response.
	+ Allows the webmaster to understand the urgency of your issue.
* **Attach screenshots via email** (`Attach screenshots via email`, `note`): Specify if you have screenshots that can be attached via email.
	+ Helps with debugging large images.
* **Recommended action** (`Recommended action`, `text`): Suggest a solution if you have one.
	+ Provides a potential fix for the webmaster to consider.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`): Enter your email so the webmaster can follow up with you.
	+ Ensures the webmaster can reach out to you for further information.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, `text`): Provide any other information that might be helpful for the web team.
	+ Gives the web team a better understanding of your issue.
