# Sustainability Transformation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Transformation Contact Form is a tool for collecting contact information and details about users' interests or projects related to sustainability. This form allows users to get in touch with the Sustainability Transformation team and share their thoughts, questions, or project ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. On the first page, enter your contact information in the "Contact Form" section.
3. Select one of the options for "Sustainability Details" that best describes your current level of interest or project status.
4. Fill out the "Organization Details" field with any relevant information about the organization you're representing.
5. Choose the relevant options for "Lead Management" to indicate how you'd like to be contacted or informed about project updates.
6. Enter any specific "Project Inquiries" or questions you have in the "Project Inquiries" field.
7. Select the type of "Sustainability Project" that aligns with your current interests or projects.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. If applicable, enter the date and time you are available to discuss in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
11. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
12. Finally, provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Sustainability Details** (`form_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Green" if you're interested in learning more about sustainability, "Red" if you're actively working on a sustainability project, or "Blue" if you're not sure yet.
* **Organization Details** (`form_3`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant information about the organization you're representing, such as its name and location.
* **Lead Management** (`form_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how you'd like to be contacted or informed about project updates, such as email or phone call.
* **Project Inquiries** (`form_5`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific questions or project inquiries you have.
* **Sustainability Projects** (`form_6`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of sustainability project you're interested in or working on.
* **Email** (`form_7`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`form_8`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to call you back.
* **Date** (`form_9`, date, required/optional): If you have specific date preferences for discussing your project, enter them here.
* **Time** (`form_10`, time, required/optional): If you have specific time preferences for discussing your project, enter them here.
* **Name** (`form_11`, text, required/optional): Enter your name to help us associate it with your contact information.
* **Comments** (`form_12`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when entering your contact information and project details.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask and we'll do our best to help.
* We'll respond to your inquiry as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
