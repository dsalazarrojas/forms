# Photography Portfolio Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users interested in inquiring about different photography services. It helps us understand their needs and preferences for photography services.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and details to complete this form.
2. Review and understand the purpose of the form.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: This is your first name, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to receive further communication about your inquiry.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for any additional communication or scheduling.
* **Type of photography service**: Select the type of photography service you are interested in (e.g., Portrait, Event, Commercial, etc.).
* **Services you're interested in**: Select all the services you are interested in (e.g., Family portraits, Wedding, Headshots, Product photography, etc.).
* **Date of inquiry**: Enter the date you are inquiring about (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Inquire about**: Provide a brief description of your inquiry (e.g., inquire about services offered by our photographer).
* **Additional information**: Share any additional information that can help us understand your needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate while filling out the form.
* Ensure you select all relevant services and provide clear descriptions when inquiring about.
* We will get back to you within 24-48 hours after submission.
