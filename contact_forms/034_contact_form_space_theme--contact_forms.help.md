# Contact Form Space Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contact Form Space Theme" is designed to collect information from space travelers for contact and mission inquiry purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the `Your Full Name` and `Primary email for contact` fields.
2. Select your `Planet of Origin` from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the primary purpose of your mission in the `Mission Category` field.
4. Provide a brief summary of your mission in the `Mission Subject` field.
5. Type in a detailed description of your mission in the `Detailed Mission Message` field.
6. Enter your `Stardate` (current date in a galactic calendar).
7. Submit your completed form.

### 1. Your Full Name (`traveler_full_name`)
*   **Your Full Name**: (`traveler_full_name`, `text`, required)

### 2. Your Starbase Email (`traveler_email`)
*   **Your Starbase Email**: (`traveler_email`, `email`, required)

### 3. Planet of Origin (`planet_of_origin`)

### 4. Mission Category (`mission_category`)
*   **Mission Category**: (`mission_category`, `select_one`, required)
    Choose the primary category of your mission.

### 5. Mission Subject (`mission_subject`)
*   **Mission Subject**: (`mission_subject`, `text`, required)
    Provide a brief description of your mission.

### 6. Detailed Mission Message (`detailed_mission_message`)
*   **Detailed Mission Message**: (`detailed_mission_message`, `text`, required)

### 7. Stardate (`submission_date`)
