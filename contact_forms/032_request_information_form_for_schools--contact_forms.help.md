# Request Information Form (For Schools) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for schools to request information from a central point of contact. It is designed to allow administrators to submit requests for information that will be reviewed and responded to by the central contact point.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information and submit. You can find the form fields and their explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Request Information** (request_information, text, optional)
This field is where you can enter a detailed description of the information you are requesting. Please provide as much detail as possible to help the central contact point understand your request.

### 2. **Contact Method** (contact_forms, select_one, optional)
Select the method by which you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or address).

### 3. **Show Interest in Schools** (show_interest_in_schools, text, optional)
Use this field to express your interest in a particular program or service offered by the school. Please provide any relevant details or questions you have about the program or service.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much of the form as possible to ensure that the central contact point understands your request.
* If you have any additional information or attachments that support your request, you can upload them separately.
* The central contact point will review your request and respond at their earliest opportunity.
