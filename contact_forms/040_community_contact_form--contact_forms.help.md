# Community Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Community Contact Form is a channel for residents to connect with the community center. It helps collect essential information for follow-up and results delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your personal contact information and details.
2. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in contacting the community.
4. If you have any previous community engagement experience, describe it in the "Describe any previous community engagement experience" field.
5. If you have any specific skills or expertise, mention them in the "What key skills or expertise would you bring to the community" field.
6. Sign the form digitally to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Resident (resident_full_name_cta)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address (resident_email_cta)**: Enter your email address for follow-up and results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number (resident_phone_cta)**: Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth (resident_dob_cta)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Residential Address (resident_address_cta)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Which contact topic applies to you (primary_contact_topic_cta)**: Choose the reason for your contact from the options provided:
	+ General Inquiry
	+ Membership/Registration
	+ Event Coordination
	+ Support/Assistance
	+ Feedback/Suggestions
	+ Community Advocacy
* **What is your preferred contact method (preferred_contact_method_cta)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted from the options provided:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ Text Message
	+ In-person Visit
* **What key skills or expertise would you bring to the community (key_skills_expertise_cta)**: Enter your key skills or expertise.
* **Describe any previous community engagement experience (previous_community_experience_cta)**: Provide a brief description of your previous community engagement experience.
* **Why are you interested in contacting the community (reason_for_contacting_community_cta)**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in contacting the community.
* **Digital Signature (resident_signature_digital_cta)**: Type your full name as a digital signature to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Date of Contact (submission_date_final_cta)**: Enter the date and time of contact.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter (newsletter_opt_in_cta)**: Choose whether you would like to receive the newsletter from the options provided:
	+ Yes, sign me up!
	+ No, thank you
* **How did you hear about this contact form (referral_source_cta)**: Choose how you heard about the contact form from the options provided:
	+ Email
	+ Social Media
	+ Website
	+ Friend/Colleague
	+ Poster/Flyer
* **Emergency Contact Person (emergency_contact_full_name_cta)**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number (emergency_contact_phone_cta)**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and we will be happy to assist you with any further communication.
