# Quick Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Emergency Contact Form is a simple form designed to gather basic contact information from users, which can be useful in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) or skip if not required.
2. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Choose your department (if applicable) from the "Department" field.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
5. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Enter your contact address in the "Contact Address" field.
8. Enter your emergency contact's number in the "Emergency Contact Number" field.
9. Enter any additional message in the "Message" field (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the emergency contact form (e.g., "In case of an emergency, contact my family").
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the department to which you are affiliated (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the number to contact your emergency contact person.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your emergency contact (if needed).
