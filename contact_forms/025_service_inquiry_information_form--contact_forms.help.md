# Service Inquiry Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect service inquiry information from users. It consists of several fields that gather contact details, service inquiry type, preferred contact method, email address, and service inquiry details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Specify the type of service inquiry you have in mind in the "Service Inquiry" field.
3. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Inquiry Type" dropdown menu.
4. Select the type of service you are inquiring about from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Provide any additional service inquiry details in the "Service Inquiry Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: This is a text field where you can provide your contact details, such as name, address, and phone number.
* **Service Inquiry**: This field is where you can describe the service you are inquiring about, such as a consultation, booking, or quote.
* **Inquiry Type**: This dropdown menu allows you to choose how you would like to be contacted, whether it's by phone, email, or other methods.
* **Service Type**: This dropdown menu enables you to select the type of service you are inquiring about, such as consultation, booking, or quote.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This dropdown menu allows you to choose your preferred contact method, including phone, email, mail, or text.
* **Email**: This field is where you can enter your email address.
* **Service Inquiry Details**: This text field is where you can provide any additional details about your service inquiry.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible to ensure that your service inquiry is properly handled.
* You can choose which fields to fill out based on your needs and preferences.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
