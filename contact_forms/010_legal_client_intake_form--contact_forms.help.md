# Legal Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Client Intake Form is a document used to collect relevant information from legal clients, such as their contact details, legal concerns, and agreements. This form helps legal professionals to prepare for meetings and discussions with clients and ensure that all necessary information is collected in a single document.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
2. Provide any relevant information about the client in the `Client Info` field.
3. Select the contact method(s) that the client prefers to use for communication.
4. Enter the date and time of the meeting or discussion.
5. Describe any legal concerns or issues that the client may have.
6. Enter the client's email address.
7. Enter the client's phone number.
8. If necessary, enter any agreements or terms that have been established with the client.
9. Add any additional notes about the client and the meeting or discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter the name of the legal client.
* **Client Info (client_info)**: Describe any relevant information about the client, such as their business or personal background.
* **Contact Method (contact_method)**: Select the method(s) the client prefers to use for communication, such as email, phone, or in-person meetings.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the meeting or discussion.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the meeting or discussion.
* **Legal Concerns (legal_concerns)**: Describe any legal concerns or issues that the client may have.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Agreement (agreement)**: If necessary, enter any agreements or terms that have been established with the client.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes about the client and the meeting or discussion.
