# Package Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients request a consultation with a service provider. The goal is to collect essential information about the client's needs and preferences to match them with the best-suited provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the Client Name field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the Client Email field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the Client Phone field.
4. Select the services the client is interested in receiving from the Provider Services field.
5. If applicable, enter the client's desired appointment date and time in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time fields.
6. Type any additional comments or notes in the Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Client Name, text, required: false)**: Please enter the name of the client who is requesting the consultation.
* **Client Email (Client Email, email, required: false)**: Enter the email address of the client so that the provider can contact them.
* **Client Phone (Client Phone, text, required: false)**: If the client prefers to be contacted by phone, please enter their phone number.
* **Provider Services (Provider Services, select_multiple, required: true)**: Please select the services the client is interested in receiving (Consultation, Package Selection, or Other).
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, date, required: false)**: If the client has a preferred date for the consultation, please enter it in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time, time, required: false)**: If the client has a preferred time for the consultation, please enter it in hh:mm AM/PM format.
* **Message (Message, text, required: false)**: This is an optional field for the client to provide any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to ensure a successful submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your local support team for assistance.
