# Contact A Person - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to contact different departments or people within the organization for various reasons. It helps the support team to quickly identify the intended recipient and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the person or department you would like to contact from the options provided.
2. Fill in your details, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Specify the subject of your inquiry and a brief description of your message.
4. Choose the priority level of your inquiry (Low, Normal, High, or Urgent).
5. Select your preferred response method (Email, Phone Call, or Either).
6. If you've chosen Phone Call as your preferred response method, select the best time to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Select Who to Contact
* **Select Who to Contact** (`contact_person`, `select_one`, required): Choose the person or department you want to contact from the options provided.

### Your Name
* **Your Name** (`your_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for the support team to address you properly.

### Your Email
* **Your Email** (`your_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address where the support team can reach you for response.

### Your Phone Number
* **Your Phone Number** (`your_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.

### Subject
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Enter a brief subject line to help the support team quickly identify your inquiry.

### Message
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Type a clear and concise message to describe your inquiry or issue.

### Priority Level
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, `select_one`, required): Select the urgency level of your inquiry (Low, Normal, High, or Urgent).

### Preferred Response Method
* **Preferred Response Method** (`preferred_response`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'd like to be responded to (Email, Phone Call, or Either).

### Best Time to Contact
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time`, `select_one`, optional): If you've chosen Phone Call as your preferred response method, select the best time to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
