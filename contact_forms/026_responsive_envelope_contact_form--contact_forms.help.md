# Responsive Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Responsive Envelope Contact Form" is a contact form designed to collect information from end-users, allowing them to send messages, provide contact details, or inquire about services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Page 1" to begin the form.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant contact details in the text field on "Page 1" (Note: This field is optional but recommended if you want to be reached back).
3. If you want to send a message or inquire about services, type it in the text area on "Page 2".
4. Review "Page 3" for any additional instructions or messages.
5. Finally, review "Page 4" for any additional information or confirmations before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1 (Page 1)** (`page_1`, `text`, optional):
    - Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Page 2 (Page 2)** (`page_2`, `text`, optional):
    - Type in your message or inquiry about our services.
* **Page 3 (Page 3)** (`page_3`, `text`, optional):
    - Check for any additional instructions or messages.
* **Page 4 (Page 4)** (`page_4`, `text`, optional):
    - Review and confirm before submission.

## Tips
* Be sure to type in a clear and concise message on "Page 2" for the best chance of response.
* Double-check "Page 4" before submission for any errors or missing information.
* If you encounter any issues with the form, please contact us directly for assistance.
