## join_our_mailing_list_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows you to join our mailing list. By filling out the form, you will be added to our email list and receive occasional updates about our latest news and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license). 
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you own or have access to. This is the address we will use to send you updates about our latest news and events.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact or confirmation purposes.

## Tips
Please fill in all the required fields, if any. We will use the information provided in this form to add you to our mailing list and send you updates about our latest news and events. If you have any questions or need help with completing the form, contact us and we will assist you. 

(Note: The fields are not currently marked as required in the YAML, but I assumed that it was a mistake. If the fields are indeed not required, the form can be completed without any of the fields.)
