# Retirement Planning Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who are interested in planning their retirement and would like to get in touch with our team for guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and submit your information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current age** (`retirement_planning_interest_form`): This field is used to determine your current age and will help us provide you with relevant information and guidance.
* **What is your annual income** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_income`): This field is used to understand your income level, which will help us provide you with accurate information and resources.
* **What are your assets** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_assets`): This field is used to understand your assets and help us provide you with tailored guidance.
* **Do you have investments?** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_investments`): Please select 'Yes' if you have investments and we will provide you with guidance on how to incorporate them into your retirement plan.
* **Other investments** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_investments_other`): If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, please specify your investments in this field.
* **What are your retirement goals** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_retirement_goals`): This field is used to understand your retirement goals and provide you with relevant information and resources.
* **How do you want us to contact you** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_contact`): Please select how you would like us to contact you (Email, Phone, Text, or Other).
* **Additional notes** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_notes`): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in understanding your situation.
* **What is your email** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_email`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with further guidance and support.
* **What is your phone number** (`retirement_planning_interest_form_phone`): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with further guidance and support.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
