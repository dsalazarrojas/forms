# joint_venture_agreement_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is the joint venture agreement form. It is intended for collecting general information, partner details, venture details, contact information, and agreement terms from parties involved in joint ventures. Please note that this form is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the **General Information** page.
   - Enter any general details or comments that you would like to share.
2. Proceed to the **Partner Information** page.
   - Add details about your partner(s).
3. Move on to the **Venture Details** page.
   - Enter details about the joint venture.
4. Visit the **Contact Information** page.
   - Enter your contact details.
5. Review the **Agreement Terms** page.
   - Add any additional agreement terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information (page 1)** (`general_info`, required: false): Enter general details or comments about the joint venture.

### Partner Information
* **Partner Information (page 2)** (`partner_info`, required: false): Add details about your partner(s).

### Venture Details
* **Venture Details (page 3)** (`venture_details`, required: false): Enter details about the joint venture.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (page 4)** (`contact_info`, required: false): Enter your contact details.

### Agreement Terms
* **Agreement Terms (page 5)** (`agreement_terms`, required: false): Add any additional agreement terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all relevant fields to provide thorough information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form purpose or contact the relevant authorities.
* The form is designed to be flexible, so feel free to add or remove pages as needed.
* If you need help with the form, don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
