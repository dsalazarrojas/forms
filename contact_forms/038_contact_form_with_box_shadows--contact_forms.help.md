# Contact Form With Box Shadows - Help Guide
## Purpose
The contact form is intended for users to submit information and inquiries to the site administrator or support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are necessary for us to process your submission.
2. Enter your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
3. Enter your primary email address in the "Your Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Your Phone Number" field.
5. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
6. Enter a brief summary of your inquiry or message in the "Inquiry Subject" field.
7. Enter your detailed message in the "Your Message" field.
8. Select the current date in the "Date of Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`user_full_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Your Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/true): Enter your primary email address in this field.
* **Your Phone Number** (`user_phone`, `text`, required/true): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Inquiry Subject** (`inquiry_subject`, `text`, required/true): Enter a brief summary of your inquiry in this field.
* **Your Message** (`detailed_message`, `text`, required/true): Enter your detailed message in this field.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email or Phone Call).
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/true): Select the current date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete.
* If you have any attachments or additional information, you can attach them to your message.
* Our support team will respond to your submission as soon as possible, usually within 24-48 hours.
