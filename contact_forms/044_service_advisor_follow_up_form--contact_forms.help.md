# Service Advisor Follow Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide feedback and suggestions on their service visit experience. It helps our service advisors to follow up on the issues and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin. You will be taken to the first page of the form.
2. Read each question carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
3. Select your answers for each question from the provided options.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can type them in the text fields.
5. Scroll through the pages and answer all the questions.
6. Once you have completed the form, click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_1`, `note`, required/optional): This is a text area where you can provide any feedback or comments about your visit.
* **What was the reason for your visit today?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_2`, `select_one`, required): Please select the reason why you visited our service today. You can choose from the options: "Service issue", "Consultation", or "Other".
* **What was the issue with your service today?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_3`, `text`, required): This is a text field where you can describe the issue you experienced with your service.
* **Did you find our service helpful?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select whether our service was helpful or not.
* **How can we improve?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_5`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service, please type them in this field.
* **Any additional comments?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_6`, `text`, optional): This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Would you like to book another appointment?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_7`, `select_one`, optional): If you would like to book another appointment, please select "True".
* **Would you like to book a service advisor follow-up call?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_8`, `select_one`, optional): If you would like to receive a follow-up call from our service advisor, please select "True".
* **What is your preferred contact method?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_9`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **What is your contact information?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_10`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted (Phone Number, Email Address).
* **Would you like to be contacted via Phone/Email?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_11`, `select_one`, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please select how (Phone, Email, or Neither).
* **Additional comments?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_12`, `text`, optional): This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Information?** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_13`, `select_multiple`, optional): This is similar to the previous question, please select how you would like to be contacted (Phone Number, Email Address).
* **Service Advisor Follow Up Form Page 14** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_14`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Phone** (`service_advisor_follow_up_form_page_15`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide your phone number.

Note: The required fields are marked with **required** in the form YAML, which means the end user must complete those fields before submitting the form.

Tips:

* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any issues with our service, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please let us know.
* If you would like to be contacted, please select the contact method and provide your contact information.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
