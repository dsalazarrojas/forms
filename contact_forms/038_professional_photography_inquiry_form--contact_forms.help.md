# Professional Photography Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients or customers interested in professional photography services. It gathers contact details and inquiry messages to facilitate communication and potential collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field. This is the first step in getting in touch with you.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This helps us address you properly.
3. Provide your **Email** in the third field. This is the most efficient way to get in touch with you.
4. Enter your **Phone** in the fourth field. This allows us to quickly call or text you if needed.
5. In the final field, type your **Message**. This is where you can ask your question, share your project ideas, or inquire about our services. Be as detailed as you'd like, but keep it concise and clear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here. This is the first part of your name that will help us get in touch with you.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here. This helps us address you properly and make sure we don't confuse you with someone else.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is your email address where you can be reached. It's the best way to get in touch with you directly and efficiently.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number here. We might use it to quickly get in touch with you if needed.
- **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): This is the space to type in any questions, project ideas, or inquiries about our services. Be as detailed as you need, but keep it clear and concise.
