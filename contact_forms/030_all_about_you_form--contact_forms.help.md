# All About You Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your preferences, habits, and interests. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to help us provide you with more relevant information and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through each page and fill out each section one by one.
2. Use the hints provided to guide you through each question.
3. If you're unsure about a question, please leave it blank or provide the best effort you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1) (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (2) (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3) (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Date of Birth** (4) (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Favorite Color** (5) (`favorite_color`, select_one, required): Select your favorite color from the list.
* **Favorite Season** (6) (`favorite_season`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite season.
* **Favorite Day of Week** (7) (`favorite_day_of_week`, select_one, required): Select your favorite day of the week.
* **Favorite Meal of Day** (8) (`favorite_meal`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite meal of the day.
* **Favorite Cuisine** (9) (`favorite_cuisine`, select_one, required): Select your favorite cuisine from the list.
* **Favorite Hobby** (10) (`favorite_hobby`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite hobby.
* **Favorite Music Genre** (11) (`favorite_music_genre`, select_one, required): Select your favorite music genre.
* **Favorite Movie Genre** (12) (`favorite_movie_genre`, select_one, required): Choose your favorite movie genre.
* **Favorite Animal** (13) (`favorite_animal`, select_one, required): Select your favorite animal.
* **Pet Owner** (14) (`pet_owner`, select_one, required): Choose whether you're a current pet owner or not.
* **Favorite Sport** (15) (`favorite_sport`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite sport.
* **Exercise Frequency** (16) (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you exercise.
* **Favorite Book Genre** (17) (`favorite_book_genre`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite book genre.
* **Morning Person or Night Owl** (18) (`morning_or_night`, select_one, required): Choose whether you're a morning person or a night owl.
* **Coffee or Tea** (19) (`coffee_or_tea`, select_one, required): Select your preference.
* **Sweet or Savory** (20) (`sweet_or_savory`, select_one, required): Choose your preference.
* **Beach or Mountains** (21) (`beach_or_mountains`, select_one, required): Select your vacation preference.
* **City or Countryside** (22) (`city_or_countryside`, select_one, required): Choose where you'd like to live.
* **Introvert or Extrovert** (23) (`introvert_or_extrovert`, select_one, optional): Choose your personality type.
* **Biggest Goal** (24) (`biggest_goal`, text, optional): Enter your current biggest goal.
* **Biggest Fear** (25) (`biggest_fear`, text, optional): Enter your biggest fear.
* **Proudest Achievement** (26) (`proudest_achievement`, text, optional): Enter something you're proud of.
* **Bucket List Item** (27) (`bucket_list_item`, text, optional): Enter something on your bucket list.
* **Favorite Quote or Saying** (28) (`favorite_quote`, text, optional): Enter a favorite quote or saying.
* **Role Model** (29) (`role_model`, text, optional): Enter someone who inspires you.
* **Additional Comments** (30) (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you're unsure about a question, please leave it blank or provide the best effort you can.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask us for help.
* Have fun completing the form!
