# Send Group Messages Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to send group messages. Please only submit this form for relevant and non-duplication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have a valid Group Id.
2. Enter a message in the message field.
3. Select the recipient(s) to whom you want to send the message by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' in the recipient field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Id** (`group_id`, text, required: false): 
    This is a unique identifier for the group you want to send the message to. Please enter the correct Id from your records.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): 
    Enter the message you want to send to the group. Keep it concise and clear.
* **Recipient(s)** (`recipient_id`, select_multiple, required: false): 
    Choose who you want to send the message to by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each recipient. This field is meant to filter the recipients based on your selection.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter a valid Group Id to avoid errors.
* Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each recipient to filter the recipients based on your selection.
* Keep the message concise to make it easier to read and understand.
* Use this form for non-redundant and relevant purposes to avoid duplication of effort.
