# Contact Page Red And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contact Page Red And Responsive" form is a contact form used to gather information from visitors for communication with the team. It is designed to be user-friendly and accessible on various devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Page" heading to access the form.
2. Enter your full name in the "Visitor name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Enter a brief subject of your message in the "Subject" field (optional).
5. Type your message in the "Message" field.
6. If you want to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field (optional).
7. You can also add any additional information to help our response in the "Additional information" field (optional).
8. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred contact method" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Contact Page --**: This heading is the title of the form section.
* **Visitor name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Subject**: Briefly describe the topic or subject of your message.
* **Message**: Type your detailed message to our team.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone (optional).
* **Additional information**: Add any extra context or details that might help us respond to your message.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or No preference).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (Visitor name, Email address, Message).
- If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
- The "Preferred contact method" field is optional, but it helps us tailor our response to your preference.
- You can add any additional information that might be helpful for our response.
- Please ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
