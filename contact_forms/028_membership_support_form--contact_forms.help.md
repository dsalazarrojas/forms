# Membership Support Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Membership Support Form is designed to provide support and guidance to our members who need help with their membership-related issues. This form helps our support team understand the nature of the problem and address it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select "Yes" or "No" as your contact status**: Choose whether you are currently in contact with us or not.
2. Fill in your question or concern in the "member-question" field.
3. Provide any additional information or answers that may help us understand your issue in the "answers" field.
4. Indicate whether you have any other questions or concerns in the "support-questions" field.
5. Choose whether you are ready to submit your questions or concerns in the "submit-questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact-info (Yes/No)**: This field asks whether you are currently in contact with us. Please select "Yes" if you are currently in contact, and "No" if you are not.
* **member-question**: This field is where you can provide your question or concern about your membership. Please be as detailed as possible so that our support team can better understand your issue.
* **answers**: If you have any additional information or answers that may help us understand your issue, please provide them here.
* **support-questions (Yes/No)**: This field asks whether you have any other questions or concerns. Please select "Yes" if you have additional questions, and "No" if you do not.
* **submit-questions (Yes/No)**: This field indicates whether you are ready to submit your questions or concerns. Please select "Yes" if you are ready, and "No" if you are not.
