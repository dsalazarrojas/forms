# Simple Flat Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to contact the form owner with inquiries, questions, or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name in the "Contact Form" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Describe the purpose of your contact in the "Subject" field.
* Type your message in the "Message" field.
* Click the "Submit" button to send your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form** (form_name): This is the field where you enter your name.
* **Email** (form_email): This is the field where you enter your email address.
* **Subject** (form_subject): This is the field where you describe the purpose of your contact.
* **Message** (form_message): This is the field where you type your message.
* **Submit** (form_submit): This is the button that sends your contact information when clicked.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) to ensure that your contact information is complete and accurate.
* Be concise and clear in your message to help the form owner respond to your inquiry.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
