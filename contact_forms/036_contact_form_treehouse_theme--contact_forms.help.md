# Contact Form Treehouse Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Form Treehouse Theme is designed to gather information from users who visit or explore the treehouse. The form collects basic contact details, the user's forest of origin, and the primary purpose of their visit. The form helps the treehouse team to respond to user inquiries and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Type your primary email address in the "Your Forest Email" field.
3. Select the forest you are currently exploring from the "Home Forest" dropdown menu.
4. In the "Inquiry Category" field, choose the primary purpose of your visit to the treehouse.
5. Provide a brief summary of your inquiry in the "Inquiry Subject" field.
6. Enter a more detailed message about your visit in the "Detailed Forest Message" field.
7. Select the current date in the "Date of Exploration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name to help us know who is reaching out.
* **Your Forest Email**: Enter your primary email address for us to contact you.
* **Home Forest**: Select where you are exploring from. This will help us tailor our response.
* **Inquiry Category**: Choose the primary purpose of your visit. This helps us understand your needs.
* **Inquiry Subject**: Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Detailed Forest Message**: Give us more information about your visit.
* **Date of Exploration**: Select the current date to help us track your visit.
