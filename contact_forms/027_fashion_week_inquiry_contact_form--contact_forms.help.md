# Fashion Week Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to send messages to Fashion Week regarding sponsorship, press, or guest inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Click on the "Fashion Week Inquiry Form" label to begin.
* Fill in your **Name**, which should be a brief introduction.
* Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you back.
* Input your **Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
* In the **Message** field, write a clear and concise message explaining your inquiry.
* Select the **Sponsor Category** relevant to your inquiry (Sponsor, Press, Designer, or Guest).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fashion Week Inquiry Form**:
	+ This is the form title and introduction to your message.
* **Name** (`contact_form_1`, text, required: false):
	+ A brief introduction to your message. Please provide your name.
* **Email** (email, required: false):
	+ The email address where you can be reached back at.
* **Phone** (text, required: false):
	+ A phone number where you can be contacted directly.
* **Message** (text, required: false):
	+ A clear and concise description of your inquiry.
* **Sponsor Category** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more categories relevant to your inquiry.
