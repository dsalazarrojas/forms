# Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opt In Form is a tool used to collect basic information from users interested in opting in to a program or service. This form is intended for users who have expressed a genuine interest in participating in a particular service or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Indicate your interests by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "User Interests" field.
5. Enter your address in the "User Address" field.
6. Specify your city in the "User City" field.
7. Enter your state in the "User State" field.
8. Select your country in the "User Country" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "User Zip" field.
10. Add any additional comments or information in the "User Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your interest in our program or service.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your name to allow us to address you personally.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you via phone.
* **User Interests** (`user_interests`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Indicate your interests by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your address to allow us to send you information or packages related to our program or service.
* **User City** (`user_city`, text, Required/Optional): Specify your city to allow us to better understand your location.
* **User State** (`user_state`, text, Required/Optional): Enter your state to allow us to tailor our information or service to your location.
* **User Country** (`user_country`, text, Required/Optional): Select your country to allow us to ensure our service is relevant to you.
* **User Zip** (`user_zip`, number, Required/Optional): Enter your zip code to allow us to better understand your location.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, Required/Optional): Add any additional comments or information about your interest in our program or service.
