# Ask A Question Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to ask a question or provide feedback about our organization. It helps us understand your query and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name**: Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field.
2. **Enter your email address**: Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is also a required field.
3. **Select your industry**: Choose the category that best describes your field of work or interest. This is optional, but helps us understand your context.
4. **Choose the topic of interest**: Select the category that best relates to your question or feedback. This is a required field.
5. **Describe your question or feedback**: In the "Your Question or Feedback" field, please provide a detailed description of your query or feedback. This is a required field.
6. **Add any additional information (optional)**: If you have a screenshot or file related to your question or feedback, use the "If you have a screenshot or file, please describe it here" field to provide more context.
7. **How did you find us?**: Select how you found our website or organization. This is optional.
8. **Agree to our privacy policy and data handling terms**: Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and understood our privacy policy and data handling terms. This is a required field.
9. **Submit your request**: Click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (requester_name_at)**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address (requester_email_at)**: Enter your email address so we can respond to your question or feedback.
* **Company or Organization (organization_at)**: If you're representing a company or organization, enter its name. This field is optional.
* **Industry (industry_at)**: Select the category that best describes your field of work or interest.
	+ Technology: Related to technology products or services.
	+ Healthcare: Related to medical or healthcare services.
	+ Education: Related to educational institutions or services.
	+ Finance: Related to financial services or products.
	+ Manufacturing: Related to manufacturing or production processes.
	+ Other: Not listed above.
* **Topic of Interest (topic_interest_at)**: Select the category that best relates to your question or feedback.
	+ Product Features: Questions or feedback about specific features or products.
	+ Pricing and Licensing: Questions or feedback about pricing or licensing.
	+ Technical Support: Questions or feedback about technical support or services.
	+ Partnership Opportunities: Questions or feedback about partnership or collaboration opportunities.
	+ General Feedback: Unspecific feedback or questions.
* **Your Question or Feedback (question_feedback_at)**: Describe your query or feedback in as much detail as possible.
* **If you have a screenshot or file, please describe it here (attachment_notice_at)**: If you have a screenshot or file related to your question or feedback, provide a brief description here. This field is optional.
* **How did you find our website? (referral_source_at)**: Select how you found our website or organization.
	+ Google Search: Found through Google search engine.
	+ Social Media: Found through social media platforms.
	+ Professional Network: Found through professional network or connections.
	+ Advertisement: Found through advertisements.
	+ Word of Mouth: Found through personal recommendations.
* **I agree to the privacy policy and data handling terms (privacy_agreement_at)**: Select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and understood our privacy policy and data handling terms. This is a required field.
* **Date (submission_date_at)**: Enter the date when you submitted your request.
* **Is this an urgent request (urgent_flag_at)**: Select "True" if your question or feedback requires urgent attention, otherwise select "False". This field is optional.
