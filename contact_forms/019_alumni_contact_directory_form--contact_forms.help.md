# Alumni Contact Directory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering contact and biographical information from alumni to create a directory that showcases their achievements and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, such as your first name, last name, class year, and school or college.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and current city and state.
3. If applicable, fill out any additional contact information, such as alternate email and phone numbers, and current address and occupation.
4. Indicate your areas of interest for volunteering and willingness to participate in alumni events.
5. Choose whether you want to subscribe to the alumni newsletter and allow your information to be shared in the directory.
6. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`id: 1`): Enter your first name. 
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your last name. 
* **Maiden Name (if applicable)** (`id: 3`): If you have used your maiden name or former surname, enter it here. 
* **Class Year** (`id: 4`): Enter the year you graduated from the institution. 
* **School or College** (`id: 5`): Enter the institution you attended. 
* **Major Field of Study** (`id: 6`): Enter your area of study. 
* **Email Address** (`id: 7`): Enter your primary contact email. 
* **Alternate Email** (`id: 8`): If you have a secondary email, enter it here. 
* **Phone Number** (`id: 9`): Enter your primary phone number. 
* **Alternate Phone Number** (`id: 10`): If you have a secondary phone number, enter it here. 
* **Current City** (`id: 11`): Enter the city where you currently reside. 
* **Current State** (`id: 12`): Enter the state or province where you currently reside. 
* **Current Country** (`id: 13`): Enter the country where you currently reside. 
* **Current Address** (`id: 14`): Enter your current address, if applicable. 
* **Zip Code** (`id: 15`): Enter the zip code of your current address, if applicable. 
* **Current Occupation** (`id: 16`): Enter your current job title. 
* **Employer Name** (`id: 17`): Enter the name of your company or organization. 
* **Business Website** (`id: 18`): Enter your business website, if applicable. 
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`id: 19`): Enter your LinkedIn profile link, if applicable. 
* **Professional Title or Credentials** (`id: 20`): Enter any professional titles or certifications you hold. 
* **Willing to Volunteer for Alumni Events** (`id: 21`): Choose whether you are willing to volunteer for alumni events. 
* **Areas of Interest for Volunteering** (`id: 22`): Specify the types of volunteer work you are interested in. 
* **Subscribe to Alumni Newsletter** (`id: 23`): Choose whether you want to receive regular updates. 
* **Allow Information to be Shared** (`id: 24`): Choose whether you are willing to share your information in the directory.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to review your entries before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or support team for clarification.
