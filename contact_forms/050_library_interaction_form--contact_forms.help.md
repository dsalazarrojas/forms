# Library Interaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Interaction Form is designed to collect information from users who interact with the library. This form helps library staff to efficiently process and respond to user inquiries, suggestions, and requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. If you have a **Suggestion**, you can enter it in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** if you have any questions or need to be contacted.
6. Answer **Yes** or **No** to whether your interaction is for a specific **Department** (choose one department only).
7. Select all relevant **Departments** (choose multiple departments, if applicable) from the options provided.
8. Enter your **School ID** (a required field) to identify your school affiliation.
9. Optionally, enter your **School Name** for further reference.
10. If a **Librarian Name** is required or recommended for your interaction, enter it in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Suggestion**: Any ideas, thoughts, or feedback you have about our library services.
* **Contact Number**: A phone number where we can contact you, if needed.
* **Department**: Please choose the department you are interacting with (if required).
* **Departments**: Select all applicable departments for your interaction (if required).
* **School ID**: A required field to identify your school affiliation.
* **School Name**: Optional field for further reference.
* **Librarian Name**: Enter the name of the librarian who referred you, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields (marked as required).
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Be specific when entering your suggestion for our library services.
* If you have any doubts or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you need help with a specific department, please select the corresponding department.
