# Personal Introduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Introduction Form is a brief questionnaire designed to provide your colleagues with a snapshot of who you are and what you do. This information will help us get to know each other better and facilitate more efficient collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information before filling it out.
2. Fill out each field with the required information for your profile.
3. Use the provided options as a reference for your answers, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the company directory.
* **email**: Enter your work email address.
* **phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **background**: Briefly describe your background and experience.
* **interests**: List your hobbies or interests outside of work.
* **job title**: Enter your job title within the company.
* **job company**: Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **job location**: Enter the location of your workplace.
* **minimalist profile**: Choose 'Yes' if you'd like to keep your profile minimalist, which will only show your name and job title.
* **social media**: Choose 'Yes' if you have a social media presence, and provide the relevant links below.
* **about**: Briefly describe yourself in a few sentences.
* **contact info**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **linkedin**: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **bio**: Enter a short biography or description of yourself.
* **notes**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.

# Tips
- Make sure to keep your answers concise and relevant.
- Use proper grammar and spelling.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your colleagues or reach out to us.
- Once you've filled out this form, it will be visible to all your colleagues within the company.
