# Red Banner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to send a contact message to the website administrator. It should be used for general inquiries, feedback, or to request information that is not addressed in the FAQs or documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. Choose a suitable style for your message.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **EMail** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message** (note, required): Enter a clear and concise description of your request or inquiry.
* **Image URL** (text, optional): If you have an image to share, enter the URL of the image (e.g., a screenshot or a photo).
* **Style** (select multiple, optional): Choose the tone of your message. Options are:
	+ Formal: Use this style for professional or formal requests.
	+ Informal: Use this style for casual or friendly requests.
	+ Casual: Use this style for relaxed or non-official requests.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to send your message.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use clear and concise language in your message to help us understand your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the above explanations for guidance.
