# Simple Business Contact Form Freelancer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Business Contact Form Freelancer is a form designed to gather contact information and status updates from freelancers. It allows potential clients to easily contact freelancers to discuss projects or collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Name in the designated text field.
2. Provide your Email address in the correct format (e.g., "user@example.com").
3. If you have a Website, enter the URL in the text field.
4. Select your current Status from the dropdown menu, indicating whether you are Active, On vacation, On sick leave, On business trip, or Not available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the contact list.
* **Email**: Enter your business or contact email address. This will be used for future communication.
* **Website**: If you have a website or online presence, enter the URL here. This is an optional field.
* **Status**: Select your current status from the dropdown menu. This helps potential clients understand your availability for new projects.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely to ensure that potential clients can easily contact you.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submission.
* If you're unsure about your status, select "Not available" to avoid miscommunication.
