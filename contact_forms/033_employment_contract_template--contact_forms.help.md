# Employment Contract Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive template for creating employment contracts between an employer and an employee. It helps you define the terms and conditions of employment, including job titles, descriptions, company names, and employee information such as name and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "job_title" field, which is the name of the job position.
2. In the "job_description" field, enter a brief description of the job duties and responsibilities.
3. Enter the name of the company in the "company_name" field.
4. Fill in the "employee_name" field with the name of the employee.
5. Enter the email address of the employee in the "employee_email" field.
6. Finally, enter the phone number of the employee in the "employee_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the name of the job position.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are filled in correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with HR or the relevant department before submitting the form.
* This form is a template and can be customized as needed to fit specific employment contract requirements.
