# Lease Renewal Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Renewal Information Update Form is a document that helps landlords and tenants update and confirm lease renewal details. This form ensures that accurate and up-to-date information is collected for a seamless transition during the lease renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being collected.
2. Enter the required information in the designated fields.
3. Double-check for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lease Renewal Information Update (Page 1)
* **Lease Renewal Information Update** (`lease_renewal_information_update_form`, note, not required): This field is a header for the form and serves as a placeholder for page content. Please ignore this field as it is not editable.
* **Landlord** (`landlord`, text, not required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager responsible for the lease.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the property being leased.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the tenant or renter.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues during the lease renewal process.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact the relevant parties or seek further clarification.
* Do not submit duplicate or outdated information as it may cause confusion or delay the renewal process.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
