# Career Fair Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Fair Information Form is designed to collect essential details from exhibitors who wish to participate in the Career Fair event. This form helps us understand your needs and prepare for a successful event. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Submit the form when you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name (Organization name)**: Enter the name of your organization, which will be displayed at your booth during the event.
* **Contact person (Contact person)**: Enter the primary contact person's name for event communications.
* **Contact email (Contact email)**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person for event communications.
* **Number of representatives (Number of representatives)**: Indicate the number of staff members who will be representing your organization at the event.
* **Internet needs (Internet needs)**: Indicate whether you require internet access at your booth. You can choose 'True' if you need Wi-Fi or 'False' if you don't require any internet access.
* **Promotional materials (Promotional materials to bring)**: List any promotional materials you plan to bring to the event, such as brochures, swag, or demo equipment.
* **Special requests (Special requests)**: Mention any special requests you have for event accommodations, such as specific power needs or other requirements.
* **Agree to exhibitor terms and rules (Agree to exhibitor terms and rules)**: This is a required field that requires you to agree to the exhibitor terms and conditions to participate in the event. Please select 'I agree' if you understand and accept the terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your email address and contact person's name for correct spelling and formatting.
- If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
