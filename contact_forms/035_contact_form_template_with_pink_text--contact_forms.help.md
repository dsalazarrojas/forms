# Contact Form Template With Pink Text - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for visitors to contact the company with general inquiries, style consultations, or other questions. Fill out this form to provide your details and a brief description of your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: **Your Full Name**, **Your Email Address**, **Inquiry Category**, **Subject**, and **Date of Submission**.
2. If you have a **Phone Number**, you can provide it for easier contact.
3. If you'd like to provide more context, you can also fill out the **Detailed Message** field.
4. Select your preferred **Inquiry Category** from the options: **Style Consultation**, **Product Inquiry**, **Feedback/Love**, **Partnership Opportunity**, or **Other**.
5. If you'd like to let us know your favorite shade of pink, feel free to select one from the list or add your own.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself for our records.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your primary email for our team to get in touch with you.
* **Your Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Inquiry Category**: Choose the primary reason for your contact. This helps us direct your question to the right team member.
* **What's your favorite shade of pink?**: This is an optional question, and you can answer it if you like pink!
* **Subject**: Provide a brief summary of your inquiry, so we know what you're reaching out for.
* **Your Detailed Message**: If you'd like to add more context or details, this is the place to do so.
* **Date of Submission**: This will automatically fill in today's date, but you can change it if you need to.
