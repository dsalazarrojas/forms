# Tree Removal Services Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers request a quote for tree removal services.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "Tree Removal Service Requester" field.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the "phone_number" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the address where the tree removal service is required in the "tree_removal_service_address" field.
5. Select the specific services you need from the list provided in the "services_needed" field.
6. If you have a preferred date for the service, enter it in the "tree_removal_service_date" field.
7. Indicate the preferred time for the service in the "tree_removal_service_time" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the service you need in the "tree_removal_service_notes" field.
9. If you have an estimate of the number of trees to be removed, enter it in the "tree_removal_service_estimate" field.
10. If you have accepted the quote, enter "yes" in the "tree_removal_service_accept" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tree Removal Service Requester** (`tree_removal_service_requester`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information in this field.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Tree Removal Service Address** (`tree_removal_service_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the tree removal service is needed.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_one, required): Select the specific services you require from the available options.
* **Tree Removal Service Date** (`tree_removal_service_date`, date, optional): If you have a preferred date for the service, enter it here.
* **Tree Removal Service Time** (`tree_removal_service_time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for the service, enter it here.
* **Tree Removal Service Notes** (`tree_removal_service_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the service you need.
* **Tree Removal Service Estimate** (`tree_removal_service_estimate`, number, optional): If you have an estimate of the number of trees to be removed, enter it here.
* **Tree Removal Service Quote** (`tree_removal_service_quote`, number, optional): This field is not intended for user input, but it will be used to display the quote.
* **Tree Removal Service Accept** (`tree_removal_service_accept`, text, optional): Enter "yes" if you have accepted the quote. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us to discuss your needs.
- Our team will review your submission and provide a quote for the services you require. 

Please note: The field-by-field explanation uses a more formal tone to match the tone of the form, which is likely used for professional purposes.
