# Tagline Contact Form Noise Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tagline Contact Form Noise Theme is designed for users to provide contact information and category selection. Please use this form for noise-related inquiries or to request more information about the theme.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address to allow us to get back to you.
3. Enter your Phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
4. Type your Message with details about your inquiry.
5. Select one or more Category that apply to your message.
6. Choose a tool from the list of options for further assistance.
7. Optionally, you can provide the Form ID for more specific information.
8. Click "Submit" to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form Noise Theme** (contact_form_noise_theme): This is the title of the form, providing a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your Email address to allow us to get back to you.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your Phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Message (Message)**: Type your message with details about your inquiry.
* **Category (Category)**: Select one or more Category that apply to your message (Noise, Sound, Theme).
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: Choose a tool from the list of options for further assistance.
* **Form ID (Form ID)**: Optionally, you can provide the Form ID for more specific information.
