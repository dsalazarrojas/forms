# Contact Page Styled Textbox And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a contact page for users to reach out to the website or application owners or administrators. It allows users to input their full name, email address, subject, message, phone number, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill out the "Full name" field with your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This field is for the best email address to contact you.
4. Type in a short subject for your message in the "Subject" field.
5. In the "Message" field, describe your request or feedback in detail.
6. If you have a phone number you'd like to provide, enter it in the "Phone number" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or information, you can add it in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This is the field where you enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the field where you enter your email address. This is where the website or application owners or administrators will contact you.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, optional): This is the field where you type in a short subject for your message.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): This is the field where you describe your request or feedback in detail.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is the field where you enter your phone number if you'd like to provide it.
* **Preferred contact time** (`contact_time`, `time`, optional): This is the field where you select a time that's best to reach you.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This is the field where you add any other relevant information.
