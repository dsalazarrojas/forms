# Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you update student or participant contact records. Use it to provide current information for contact, guardian/parent name, phone number, address, and any other relevant changes to the record.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the record ID available to update the contact record.
2. Enter the current record ID.
3. Enter the guardian or parent name.
4. Enter the updated email address.
5. Enter the updated phone number.
6. Enter the updated home address (if applicable).
7. Enter the emergency contact name (if applicable).
8. Enter the emergency contact phone number (if applicable).
9. Select the preferred contact method (email, phone, or text).
10. Provide any additional updates (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Update Contact Information --**: This is the form's header, providing a clear indication of its purpose.
* **Record identifier**: Enter the student or account ID for the contact record you want to update.
* **Guardian or parent name**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Email address**: Enter the updated email address for contact.
* **Phone number**: Enter the updated phone number.
* **Home address**: Enter the updated street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone, or Text.
* **Additional updates**: Enter any other relevant changes to the contact record.
 
## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to update the contact record.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Use this form for updating student or participant contact records only.
