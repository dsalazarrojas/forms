# Customer Inquiry Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Inquiry Log form is used to record and manage customer inquiries, allowing our customer support team to respond and resolve customer issues efficiently. To fill out this form, please ensure that it is a new inquiry and not a follow-up to an existing issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. **Customer Inquiry Log**
	* This is the main form title, which should be filled in with the inquiry's title.
2. **Customer Name**
	* Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account or contact information.
3. **Inquiry Details**
	* Describe the customer's inquiry in detail, including any relevant context or background information.
4. **Customer Email**
	* Enter the customer's email address associated with their account or contact information.
5. **Customer Phone**
	* Enter the customer's phone number, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Inquiry Log**: Enter a brief title for the inquiry, making it easy to identify and manage.
* **Customer Name**: Ensure you enter the customer's name correctly, as it will help our team recognize them and address their issue.
* **Inquiry Details**: Provide as much context as possible, so our support team can understand the customer's concern.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address for easy communication and follow-up.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number, if available, for potential follow-up calls.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled out for a new inquiry and not a follow-up.
* If the inquiry requires attachments or additional information, please provide them separately.
* For any further assistance, our support team is here to help.
