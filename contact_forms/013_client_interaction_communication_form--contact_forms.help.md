# Client Interaction Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Interaction Communication Form is used to record and analyze the details of interactions between staff members and clients. It is essential to fill out this form for every client interaction to ensure accurate and consistent recording of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the staff member's name in the "Staff Name" field.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Select the date of the interaction from the calendar dropdown.
4. Choose the method of interaction (e.g., phone call, email, in-person, video call, chat).
5. Enter the subject of the discussion.
6. Enter a summary of the discussion.
7. If required, identify the key action items for follow-up.
8. Select the client's sentiment (e.g., very positive, positive, neutral, negative, very negative).
9. If follow-up is required, select 'True' and assign a follow-up task and a deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Staff Name** (text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the staff member who interacted with the client.
- **Client Name** (text, required): This field is used to enter the client's name.
- **Date of Interaction** (date, required): This field is used to select the date of the interaction.
- **Interaction Method** (select_one, required): This field allows you to choose the method of interaction (e.g., phone call, email, in-person, video call, chat).
- **Subject of Interaction** (text, required): This field is used to enter the subject of the discussion.
- **Summary of Discussion** (text, required): This field is used to enter a brief summary of the discussion.
- **Key Action Items Identified** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any key action items that need follow-up.
- **Observed Client Sentiment** (select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the client's sentiment during the interaction (e.g., very positive, positive, neutral, negative, very negative).
- **Is Follow-up Required?** (select_one, required): This field allows you to select whether follow-up is required.
- **Assigned Follow-up Task** (text, optional): This field is used to enter a follow-up task assigned to the staff member.
- **Follow-up Deadline** (date, optional): This field is used to select a follow-up deadline.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible when entering the subject and summary of the discussion.
* Use the client's sentiment field to provide context for the interaction.
* If follow-up is required, ensure that a follow-up task and deadline are assigned to the staff member.
* Review the form regularly to ensure that all interactions are accurately recorded and followed up on.
