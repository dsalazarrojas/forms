# Vendor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Information Form is a form used to collect information about a vendor from their contact person. This form is essential for our internal records and communication with the vendor. Please ensure that each vendor has only one active entry in this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the vendor's name in the "vendor-name" field.
2. Enter the vendor's contact email in the "contact-email" field.
3. Enter the vendor's contact phone number in the "contact-phone" field.
4. Enter the vendor's physical address in the "vendor-address" field.
5. Enter the vendor's website in the "website" field.
6. Add any notes about the vendor in the "vendor-notes" field.
7. Upload a reference image for the vendor in the "vendor-image" field.
8. If applicable, select whether the vendor is a vendor category in the "vendor-category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name (vendor-name)**: Enter the name of the vendor, including any relevant details such as the company name or trade name.
* **Contact Email (contact-email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Contact Phone (contact-phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Vendor Address (vendor-address)**: Enter the physical address of the vendor, including any relevant details such as suite or floor number.
* **Website (website)**: Enter the vendor's website, if applicable.
* **Vendor Notes (vendor-notes)**: Add any additional information about the vendor, such as their products or services.
* **Vendor Image (vendor-image)**: Upload a reference image for the vendor, such as their logo.
* **Vendor Category (vendor-category)**: Select whether the vendor is a vendor category if it applies.
