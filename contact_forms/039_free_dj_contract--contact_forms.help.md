# free_dj_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_dj_contract form is used to collect and document essential information for a legal agreement between a client and a DJ service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
* Enter the required information for each field. Note that some fields are optional, but filling them out will help provide a more comprehensive record.
* Review and adjust the form as needed to ensure all required information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **DJ Name**: (`dj_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the DJ providing the service.
* **Client Name**: (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client hiring the DJ service.
* **Event Date**: (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event the DJ will be performing at.
* **Start Time**: (`event_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: (`event_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Venue**: (`event_venue`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Client Phone**: (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Event Details**: (`event_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **DJ**: (`dj`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the DJ or the company providing the service.
* **Contract Terms**: (`contract_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any specific terms or conditions agreed upon in the contract.
