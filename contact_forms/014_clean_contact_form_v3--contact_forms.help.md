# Clean Contact Form V3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Clean Contact Form V3 is designed for customers, partners, or potential employees to contact us with inquiries, questions, or requests. This form helps us understand your needs and provide assistance efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the welcome message above to understand our team's purpose and how to proceed.
2. Fill out the form with your contact information and nature of inquiry.
3. Provide detailed message details about your request.
4. Select your urgency level and how you heard about us (if applicable).
5. Review our privacy policy and terms of service and agree to them.
6. Click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter your last name here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Please enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Company or Organization** (`organization`, text, **Optional**): If applicable, please enter your company or organization name.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, **Optional**): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here.
* **Nature of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, **Required**): Select the purpose of your contact (Sales, Support, Media/Press, Jobs, or Other).
* **Message Details** (`message_details`, text, **Required**): Please provide detailed information about your request.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, **Required**): Select the level of urgency for your request (Normal, High, or Critical).
* **How did you hear about us?** (`find_us`, select_one, **Optional**): If you'd like to share, please select how you heard about us (Google Search, Social Media, Word of Mouth, Advertisement, or Event).
* **I agree to the privacy policy and terms of service** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, **Required**): Review our privacy policy and terms of service and select the agreement option.
* **Internal Request ID** (`request_id`, text, **Optional**): If we ask, you can provide a unique request ID here.
* **Referral Code** (`referral_code`, text, **Optional**): If you have a referral code, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when entering your message details.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our team for clarification.
- We'll review and respond to your request as soon as possible.
