# Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for check-in purposes, such as dining experiences or appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reason for visit (e.g., dinner, lunch, meeting).
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Enter your reservation ID (if applicable).
5. Enter any instructions or special requests.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your expected time of arrival.
8. Enter your expected date of arrival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reasonForVisit**: Choose the reason for your visit (e.g., dinner, lunch, meeting, or other).
* **customerName**: Enter your full name for check-in purposes.
* **phoneNumber**: Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **reservationID**: Enter your reservation ID if you have one.
* **instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests you may have.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **time**: Enter your expected time of arrival.
