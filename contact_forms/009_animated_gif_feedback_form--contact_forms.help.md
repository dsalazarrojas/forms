# Animated GIF Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users on various aspects of our product or service. Your input will help us to understand what works and what can be improved, and will enable us to make data-driven decisions to provide better services to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Select the feedback topic that best describes your feedback.
3. Fill out the required fields (marked as *Required*).
4. Save your progress by clicking on the "Save Progress" button, if you need to complete the form later.
5. Review and confirm your submission before sending it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (Required)
Full name is your full name as you would like to be addressed.

### 2. **Email Address** (Required)
Enter your valid email address so we can respond to your feedback and follow up on any questions or concerns.

### 3. **Phone Number** (Optional)
You can provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you about your feedback or for urgent matters.

### 4. **Feedback Topic** (Required)
Select the category that best describes your feedback. This will help us understand what you are trying to communicate.

### 5. **Your Feedback** (Required)
Please provide us with your thoughts, concerns, and suggestions about your experience with our product or service.

### 6. **Overall Satisfaction** (Required)
Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience.

### 7. **Detailed Ratings** (Optional)
You can rate specific aspects of your experience, such as product quality, customer service, and value for money.

### 8. **Most Liked** (Optional)
Tell us what impressed you about your experience.

### 9. **Least Liked** (Optional)
Tell us what did not meet your expectations.

### 10. **Specific Suggestions for Improvement** (Optional)
If you have any ideas on how we can improve, please let us know.

### 11. **Would Recommend** (Required)
Let us know if you would recommend us to others.

### 12. **Recommendation Strength** (Optional)
Rate how likely you are to recommend us.

### 13. **Contact About Feedback** (Required)
Let us know if you would like us to contact you about this feedback.

### 14. **Subscribe to Newsletter** (Optional)
Subscribe to our newsletter to get updates about new products and offers.

### 15. **Any Final Thoughts** (Optional)
You can provide any additional comments or feedback.

### 16. **Thank You**

We appreciate your valuable feedback and will use it to improve our services to you.
