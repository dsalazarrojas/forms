# Buyer Information Form Home Buyers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Information Form Home Buyers is designed to gather essential details from potential home buyers to facilitate their home buying journey. Completing this form will help us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your primary contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If you are buying with another person, enter their name in the "Co-Buyer Name" field.
5. If you have a co-buyer, enter their contact phone number in the "Co-Buyer Phone" field.
6. Select the type of residence you currently live in from the "Current Residence Type" dropdown menu.
7. Indicate whether you are a first-time home buyer or not.
8. Choose a timeframe for when you are looking to buy a home.
9. Select your preferred budget range for the home.
10. Choose the type of property you are interested in buying (e.g. single family home, townhouse, condo).
11. If applicable, select the specific features you must have in your new home (e.g. garage, backyard, modern kitchen).
12. Enter your approximate desired square footage for the new home.
13. Enter the number of bedrooms you need.
14. Enter the number of bathrooms you need.
15. Indicate the importance of school district quality in your new home location.
16. Confirm whether you have pre-arranged financing or not.
17. Let us know if you are interested in open houses.
18. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, text, in person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your complete name to identify you as the buyer.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Co-Buyer Name** (text, optional): If buying with another person, enter their name.
* **Co-Buyer Phone** (text, optional): If buying with another person, enter their contact phone number.
* **Current Residence Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of residence you currently live in (single family home, apartment, townhouse, condo, other).
* **First Time Home Buyer** (select_one, required): Indicate whether you are a first-time home buyer.
* **Purchase Timeframe** (select_one, required): Choose when you are looking to buy a home (within 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, more than 1 year).
* **Budget Range** (select_one, required): Select your preferred budget range for the home.
* **Preferred Property Type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the type of property you are interested in buying (single family home, townhouse, condo, new construction, fixer upper, multi-unit property).
* **Must Have Features** (select_multiple, optional): Select the essential features or amenities you must have in your new home (garage, backyard, modern kitchen, basement, fireplace, large lot, other).
* **Preferred Square Footage** (text, optional): Enter the approximate size of the new home you are looking for.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms you need.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms you need.
* **School District Important** (select_one, required): Indicate the importance of school district quality in your new home location.
* **Financing Arranged** (select_one, required): Confirm whether you have pre-arranged financing or not.
* **Interested in Open Houses** (select_one, required): Let us know if you are interested in open houses.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, text, in person).
