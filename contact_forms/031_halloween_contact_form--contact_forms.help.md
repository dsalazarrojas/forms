# Halloween Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users who are interested in attending a Halloween event. It helps event organizers gather necessary contact information and preferences from attendees to plan and execute the event smoothly.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Let us know if you plan to RSVP (attend) or not.
3. Choose a preferred date and time for the event.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can reach out to you about event details and updates.
* **RSVP (rsvp)**: Choose "yes" if you plan to attend the event, or "no" if you don't.
* **Date (date)**: Select a date from the list when you are available to attend the event.
* **Time (time)**: Choose a time of day when you are available to attend the event.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or information you want to share with us about the event.
