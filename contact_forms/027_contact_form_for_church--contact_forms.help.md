# Contact Form For Church - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather contact information and inquiries from visitors, first-time attendees, or members of the church.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
*   Provide your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
*   Enter your primary email address in the "Your Email Address" field.
*   Select your current status with the church in the "Your Current Status with the Church" field.
*   Choose your interests in ministries in the "Interest in Ministries" field (if applicable).
*   Specify the type of inquiry in the "Type of Inquiry" field.
*   Provide a detailed message in the "Your Detailed Message" field.
*   Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
*   Fill out the "Submission Date" field with today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`full_name`, text, \*): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Your Email Address** (`email_address`, email, \*): Enter the email address you check most frequently.
* **Your Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, ): This is the number we will use to contact you (optional).
* **Your Current Status with the Church** (`visitor_status`, select\_one, \*): Select the status that best describes your relationship with the church.
* **Interest in Ministries** (`interest_in_ministries`, select\_multiple, ): Select any ministries you are interested in for further involvement.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`type_of_inquiry`, select\_one, \*): Choose the primary reason for your contact.
* **Your Detailed Message** (`detailed_message`, text, \*): Provide a clear and concise description of your message.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select\_one, \*): Choose the preferred method of contact.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, \*): This will be automatically filled out with today's date.

Note: The asterisk (\*) indicates a required field.
