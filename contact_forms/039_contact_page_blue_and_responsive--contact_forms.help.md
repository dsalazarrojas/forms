# Contact Page Blue And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contact page for users to send their queries, questions, or feedback to your organization. It's essential to use this form for issues that don't require immediate attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields:
*   Name (Required): Enter your full name.
*   Email address (Required): Enter the email address you want to use for reply.
*   Message (Required): Describe your query, question, or feedback clearly.
*   Phone number (Optional): If you want to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number. This is not required.
*   Preferred contact time (Optional): If you have a preferred time window for follow-up, select it from the available options.
*   Additional notes (Optional): If you have any other information that can help our response, please provide it in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name (Name, text, Required)**: Enter your full name.
*   **Email address (Email address, email, Required)**: Enter the email address you want to use for reply.
*   **Message (Message, text, Required)**: Describe your query, question, or feedback clearly.
*   **Phone number (Phone number, text, Optional)**: If you want to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
*   **Preferred contact time (Preferred contact time, time, Optional)**: Select the time window that you are available for follow-up.
*   **Additional notes (Additional notes, text, Optional)**: If you have any other information that can help our response, please provide it in this field.
