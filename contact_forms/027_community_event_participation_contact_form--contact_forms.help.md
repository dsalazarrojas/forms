# Community Event Participation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Event Participation Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in participating in community events. By filling out this form, you will help event organizers create a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form carefully, ensuring accuracy to avoid any issues during event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. Select the event of interest (e.g., Community Festival, Town Hall Meeting, etc.).
3. Indicate your participation type (e.g., Attendee, Volunteer, Vendor, etc.).
4. If you have a group, specify the number of people with you.
5. If you require accommodations, let us know.
6. If food is provided, mention any dietary restrictions.
7. If a T-Shirt is provided, select your size.
8. Let us know how you heard about the event.
9. Confirm if you've attended the event before.
10. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Event of Interest** (Required): Select the event that interests you.
* **Type of Participation** (Required): Choose how you want to participate in the event.
* **Number in Your Group** (Optional): If you're attending with others, specify the number of people.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Optional): If you require any accommodations, let us know.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): If food is provided, mention any dietary restrictions.
* **T-Shirt Size** (Optional): If a T-Shirt is provided, select your size.
* **How Did You Hear About the Event** (Optional): Let us know where you learned about the event.
* **Have You Attended Before** (Required): Confirm if you've attended the event before.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Add any additional information or notes about your participation.

By following this guide, you'll complete the form accurately and contribute to creating a successful and enjoyable community event experience for everyone involved.
