# Customer Search Filter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to search for customers by various criteria such as name, email, or phone number. It is designed to help users quickly locate specific customers in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the search criteria you wish to use. Each field is optional, so you can fill in as many or as few fields as you need.
2. Click the "Search" button to execute the search.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Filter (search_filter, text, optional)**: This is the search filter field. It allows you to type in a keyword or phrase to narrow down the search results. For example, you can type in a company name or a location to get results related to that search filter.
* **Customer Name (customer_name, text, optional)**: Fill in the customer's name to search for customers by their name. This field is useful for searching customers by their personal name or business name.
* **Email (email, email, optional)**: This field is used to search for customers by their email address. Be sure to enter a valid email address to get accurate search results.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone, text, optional)**: Fill in the customer's phone number to search for customers by their phone number. This field is useful for searching customers by their contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in valid information in the email field to get accurate search results.
* Use this form to refine existing records, not to collect new customer information.
* You can use this form to search for customers by multiple criteria. For example, you can search for customers by both name and email.
