# Send Us An E Mail Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to send us an email with their inquiry or feedback. It is a simple and user-friendly way to get in touch with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the form fields according to your needs.
- Make sure to choose the correct language for your message.
- If you want to upload a file, you can do so by typing a name and path to the file in the "Upload" field.
- Review the form to ensure you have filled out all the required fields (email, subject, and body).
- Click the "Submit" button to send us your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Send us an email**: This is the title of the form. It's a descriptive label to help you understand what the form is for.
* **Your email**: This field is where you enter your email address so we can respond to your message.
* **subject**: This field is where you enter a short description of the purpose or topic of your email.
* **Your message**: This field is where you can type in your message to us.
* **Upload**: This field is where you can upload a file if needed. Please type a name and path to the file you want to upload.
* **Language**: This field allows you to choose the language in which you would like to communicate with us.
* **Required fields**: This field allows you to select which language you are comfortable communicating in. If you select all the languages you are comfortable with, it means we will respond in all those languages.
* **Submit**: This button sends your completed form to us when clicked.
