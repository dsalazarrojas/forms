# Responsive Contact Form By Ismail - Help Guide
## Purpose
The contact form is designed to collect user's contact information and other details that will be used by the department to reply to the user in the preferred method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your name in the field marked "Name".
- Enter your email address in the field marked "Email".
- Select a subject for your inquiry.
- Type your message in the field marked "Message".
- Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or other).
- Enter your email address if you select "Email" as the contact method.
- Enter your phone number if you select "Phone" as the contact method.
- Enter any other information you wish to provide if you select "Other" as the contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A field to type in your contact information.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Type in your name to help us know who you are.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Subject** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Type in a brief subject of your inquiry.
* **Message** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Type in your message so that we can better understand your query.
* **How will you send?** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how you prefer to be contacted (email, phone, or other).
* **Email Address** (`7`, `email`, required: false): If you selected "Email" as the contact method, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Phone" as the contact method, enter your phone number here.
* **Other Information** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Other" as the contact method, enter any additional information you wish to provide.
