# Contact I Reviews - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to contact the organization about reviews or privacy policy-related inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the required fields.
2. If your inquiry is related to a specific form or review, provide the related form or review ID.
3. Select the type of inquiry you're making (Privacy question, Review issue, General question, or Other).
4. Provide a detailed message describing your inquiry.
5. If you have any supporting documents or files to attach, please use the email option at the bottom.
6. Indicate your consent to be contacted for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you in our response.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can respond to your inquiry.
* **Related form or reference** (`related_form_id`, `text`, optional): If your inquiry is related to a specific form or review, provide its ID here.
* **Inquiry type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the nature of your question or request (Privacy question, Review issue, General question, or Other).
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Provide detailed information so we can respond accurately.
* **Attach supporting documents via email** (`attach_files_note`, `note`, optional): If you have any supporting documents or files to attach, please use your email to send them to us.
* **Consent for follow up** (`consent_for_follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Check if you consent to be contacted for further information.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other details you want to add to your inquiry.
