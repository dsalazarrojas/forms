# Email Santa Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact Santa Claus's workshop for various purposes, such as requesting gifts or providing additional information for an existing contact. It's a standalone form created using public metadata and is not affiliated with any official holiday organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact information on the first page. Please fill out the fields as accurately as possible so we can better understand your needs.
2. If you have any gift requests, you can provide details on the second page.
3. If you have any additional contact information, you can provide it on the third page.
4. Please do not repeat any information already provided on the previous pages.
5. Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (text)**: Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.

### Gift Information
* **Gift Information (text)**: If you are requesting a gift, please describe your desired gift and any specific details you would like the elves to consider.

### Additional Contact Information
* **Additional Contact Information (text)**: If you have any additional contact information, such as a family member's name or address, please provide it here.

### Repeated Information
* **Repeated Information (text)**: Do not fill out this section if you have already provided the same information on the previous pages. If you have additional information that is not already provided, you can enter it here.

### Submit
* **Submit (text)**: This is the final step to send your request to Santa's workshop. Please review all the fields before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form to help Santa's workshop process your request efficiently.
* Do not hesitate to provide any additional information that might be helpful in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
