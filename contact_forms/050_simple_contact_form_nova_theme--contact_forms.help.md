# simple_contact_form_nova_theme - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a basic contact form designed to collect information from users, which will be sent to the site administrators or support team for assistance or inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Message** fields with your relevant information.
2. Optionally, enter a brief **Subject** to describe your message.
3. Click the **Send** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: A field to enter your first name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Message**: A field to enter your message or description of your query.
* **Subject**: A field to enter a brief subject or topic of your message.
* **Send**: A button to submit your form.
* **Reset**: A button to reset all fields.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete in your submissions to ensure the site administrators or support team can respond efficiently.
* If you're looking for specific help with a product or service, please contact the product support directly.
* The form does not save any information you input, and each submission will be treated as a new, individual query.
