# Personal Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather emergency contact information from users to keep on file for situations where immediate contact is necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct relationship type from the options provided.
3. Make sure to enter valid phone numbers and email addresses for your emergency contact.
4. Use the "Other Contact Info" field for any additional information you want to share with your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_form`): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person you are providing as an emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`): Choose how you are related to this contact, such as "Mother/Father", "Spouse/Partner", "Brother/Sister", "Friend", or "Other".
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other_contact_info`): Provide any additional information you want to share with the emergency contact, such as their schedule or availability.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to review and update your emergency contact information regularly.
- Use this form to provide information for emergency contacts that you are close to, such as family members or partners.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
