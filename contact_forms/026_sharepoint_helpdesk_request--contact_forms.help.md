# SharePoint Helpdesk Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SharePoint Helpdesk Request form is used for users to submit requests for assistance with SharePoint-related issues. This form allows users to provide contact information, issue details, and other relevant information to help support teams efficiently address their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email, and phone number so that the support team can get in touch with you.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing in the issue details field.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes as needed in the issue description field.
4. Let the support team know if this is a recurring problem by selecting "Yes" or "No" under Group Request.
5. Specify the priority level of your request under Request Priority.
6. Choose the status of your request under Request Status.
7. Select the type of request you are making under Request Type.
8. If applicable, select "Yes" under Request Source.
9. Fill out the Request Date and Request Time fields to track the timeline of your request.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the Request Notes field.
11. Update the status of your request under Update Status (if you are a support team member).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): Fill out your name, email, and phone number so that the support team can get in touch with you.
* **Issue Details** (Issue Details, text, required/optional): Describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Request Update** (Request Update, number, required/optional): Not applicable, but if you need to add numbers, you can enter them here.
* **Group Request** (Group Request, select_multiple, required/optional): Let the support team know if this is a recurring problem by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Issue Description** (Issue Description, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your issue.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name so that the support team can reference it for context.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email so that the support team can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the support team can reach you.
* **Request Priority** (Request Priority, select_one, required/optional): Select a priority level (not provided in the form schema) for your request (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Request Status** (Request Status, select_one, required/optional): Choose a status for your request (e.g., Active, Inactive, Not Started).
* **Request Type** (Request Type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of request you are making (e.g., Bug, Feature Request, Task).
* **Request Source** (Request Source, select_one, required/optional): If applicable, select "Yes" under Request Source.
* **Request Date** (Request Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your request was made.
* **Request Time** (Request Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time when your request was made.
* **Request Notes** (Request Notes, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Update Status** (Update Status, select_one, required/optional): As a support team member, you can update the status of your requests here.
