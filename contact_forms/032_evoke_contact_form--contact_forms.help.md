# evoke_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the `evoke_contact_form` is to collect contact information from users, which includes their name and email, along with a brief message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the **name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Type in your message in the **message** field.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name, as we would like to know who you are.
* **email** (email, required): Provide your email address so we can reply or communicate with you.
* **message** (text, required): This is a text field where you can type in your message or question. Please let us know what's on your mind!

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
- Double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Keep your message concise and clear.
- If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask!
