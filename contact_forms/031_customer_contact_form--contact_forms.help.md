# Customer Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture customer information for customer support or sales purposes. Please complete this form to provide us with a clear understanding of your needs, and our team will be in touch with you soon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Enter the customer's contact information in the "contact_number" field.
3. Provide the customer's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "address" field.
5. Write a brief message to us in the "customer_message" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the customer in the "customer_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the customer's name, e.g., John Doe.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter the customer's phone number or other contact information, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the customer's email address, e.g., johndoe@example.com.
* **Address (address)**: Enter the customer's home or office address, e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA.
* **Customer Message (customer_message)**: Write a brief description of your inquiry, question, or issue, e.g., "I have a question about our product."
* **Customer Note (customer_note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the customer, e.g., "Has a pet cat named Whiskers."
