# Graphic Designer Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to contact a graphic designer and provide details about their projects. It is intended to help designers receive messages from potential clients and understand their project requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name in the **Name** field.
2. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. If you have a Phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field.
4. Enter your website in the **Website** field, if applicable.
5. In the **Project Description** field, describe your project in detail, including any specific requirements or goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name to help the designer get back to you more efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Provide a valid email address to which the designer can respond.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): If you prefer, you can also provide a phone number for direct communication.
* **Website** (`text`, optional): If you have a website, it can help the designer get more information about your work.
* **Project Description** (`note`, optional): Briefly describe your project, highlighting its key requirements, deadlines, and any other relevant details.
