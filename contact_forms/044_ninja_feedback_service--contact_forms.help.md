# ninja_feedback_service - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to provide feedback on their experience with the company's ninja-themed service. This feedback will help the company improve and refine its services to better meet customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact name and email address in the respective fields.
2.  Select the type of feedback you want to provide: "Bug", "Feature Request", or "Other".
3.  Provide a detailed description of your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4.  If applicable, input the date and time of your visit to the site.
5.  You can also input your preferred date and time for the feedback.
6.  If you have any comments, type them in the "Comments" field.
7.  If you have attached any relevant files, upload them in the "Attach File" field.
8.  If you have a contact phone number, input it in the "Contact Phone" field.
9.  Select the status of your feedback: "Open", "In Progress", or "Closed".
10. Determine the priority of your feedback: "High", "Low", or "Medium".
11. If you want to assign a user to your feedback, select "Yes" or "No".
12. You can also leave any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
13. Optionally, if you have an email address, input it in the "Email" field.
