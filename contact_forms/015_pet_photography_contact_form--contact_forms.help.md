# Pet Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Photography Contact Form is a tool for users to get in touch with a pet photography service provider to inquire or book a session for their pet. It helps to gather information about the user's pet and their desired photography service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. If you are not comfortable with your first name being shared, you can leave it optional.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field. Please ensure it's accurate to receive updates and communications from us.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the "Phone" field. This will help us contact you for further inquiries or bookings.
4. Select your pet's **Type** from the dropdown menu. Choose from "Cat", "Dog", or "Other" if your pet doesn't fit into these categories.
5. Provide a brief description of your pet in the **Pet Details** field. This will help us understand your pet's personality and characteristics.
6. Choose your preferred **Photography Session** type. You can select from "In-Studio Session", "Outdoor Session", or "Both Sessions".
7. Enter the name of the **Contact Person** responsible for the inquiry or booking.
8. Enter any additional **Contact Info** for the contact person.
9. Select a date and time for the photography session using the **Date and Time** field.
10. Finally, provide any additional **Message** about your inquiry or booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, optional)**: Enter your first name to help us address you personally.
* **email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for further inquiries or bookings.
* **Pet Type (select_one, optional)**: Choose your pet's type from "Cat", "Dog", or "Other".
* **Pet Details (text, optional)**: Provide a brief description of your pet to help us understand them better.
* **Photography Session (select_one, optional)**: Select your preferred photography session type.
* **Contact Person (text, optional)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the inquiry or booking.
* **Contact Info (text, optional)**: Enter any additional contact information for the contact person.
* **Date and Time (date, optional)**: Select a date and time for the photography session.
* **Message (text, optional)**: Provide any additional information about your inquiry or booking.
