# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and organize information about event photography contracts. It ensures that all necessary details are covered, including event specifics, client and photographer information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event name.
2. Provide the client's name.
3. Enter the event date.
4. Specify the event location.
5. Enter the event time.
6. Mention the contract term (if any).
7. Enter the photographer's name.
8. Input the photographer's email address.
9. Enter the photographer's phone number.
10. Provide the client's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the event, such as a party or wedding.

### 2. **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false)
Please enter the name of the client or person booking the event photography services.

### 3. **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the event in the correct format (e.g., 2024-02-15).

### 4. **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false)
Specify the location of the event, such as a venue or hotel.

### 5. **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the time of the event in the correct format (e.g., 14:30).

### 6. **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, text, required: false)
Mention any specific contract term or agreement between the client and photographer.

### 7. **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the photographer assigned to the event.

### 8. **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, email, required: false)
Input the email address of the photographer.

### 9. **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the photographer.

### 10. **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, required: false)
Provide the client's contact information, such as their phone number or email address.
