# Weebly Business Model Option 2 Health Issues Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect interest from individuals who would like to discuss potential health issues related to the Weebly Business Model Option 2. The form helps us understand who is interested in this topic and what their needs and concerns are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- Select all the options that apply to you from "Topic Interest".
- Enter your email address in "Email Address" if you would like to be contacted about this topic.
- If you have a phone number, enter it in "Phone Number" to allow us to contact you more easily.
- In "Notes", provide any additional information you would like to share about your interest in this topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Topic Interest** (`topic_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the topics that interest you regarding Weebly Business Model Option 2.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about this topic.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you more easily.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information you would like to share about your interest in this topic.
