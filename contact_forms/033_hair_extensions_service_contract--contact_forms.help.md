# Hair Extensions Service Contract - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Extensions Service Contract form is used to create and manage service contracts between clients and providers. This form ensures that all required information is captured accurately and securely, facilitating a transparent and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Service Contract Details** field with the agreed-upon terms of the service contract.
2. Enter the name of the person who signed the contract in the **Signed By** field.
3. Add the client's signature in the **Client Signature** field.
4. Add the provider's signature in the **Provider Signature** field.
5. Select the contract signing date in the **Contract Date** field.
6. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
7. Enter the provider's name in the **Provider Name** field.
8. Optionally, add contract terms and conditions in the **Contract Terms and Conditions** field.
9. Optionally, specify the contract version and number if applicable.
10. Optionally, enter the client and provider contact information for future reference.
11. Select the contract status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected) from the **Contract Status** dropdown menu.
12. Add any comments about the contract in the **Comments** field.
13. Finally, select the contract signing date in the **Contract Date** field and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Contract Details** (`service_contract_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the service contract, including any agreed-upon terms.
* **Signed By** (`service_contract_signed_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who signed the contract.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Add the client's signature.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, required): Add the provider's signature.
* **Contract Date** (`service_contract_signed_date`, date, required): Select the date the contract was signed.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the provider's name.
* **Contract Terms and Conditions** (`contract_terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Optionally, add any contract terms and conditions.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, number, optional): Optionally, specify the contract version.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the contract number.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Optionally, select the contract signing date.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the client's contact information.
* **Provider Contact Info** (`provider_contact_info`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the provider's contact information.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_one, required): Select the contract status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Comments** (`contract_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the contract.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Optionally, select the contract signing date. (Note: This field is duplicated, please ignore or delete it for consistency's sake.)
