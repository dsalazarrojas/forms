# Dealer Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dealer Signup Form is used to collect information from potential dealers who are interested in signing up for our service or program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out each page of the form with the required information.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.
4. Click on "Submit" again to complete the form.

* **Dealer Information** (`dealer_signup_form_page_4`, text, optional): Enter any relevant dealer information such as your company name, location, or website.
	+ This field is also optional, and you can enter any relevant dealer information.
