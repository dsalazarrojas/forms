# Gift Card Giveaway Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gift Card Giveaway Form is used to collect information from users for the purpose of giving away gift cards. This form is meant for internal use and is not intended for external marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone Number (if applicable).
4. Select the type(s) of gift card you would like to receive by choosing the relevant option(s).
5. If you have any additional information or requests, you can add it in the Additional Information field.
6. Specify the value of the gift card you would like to receive in the Gift Card Value field.
7. If assigned to a specific tool or activity, enter the tool name in the Assigned Tool field.
8. Review your information and click Submit to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **What type of gift card would you like to receive?**: Choose from the available gift card types (Apple, Amazon, Other).
* **Which type of gift card would you like to receive? (Select multiple)**: Choose multiple gift card types from the available options.
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional information or requests you may have.
* **Gift Card Value**: Enter the value of the gift card you would like to receive.
* **Assigned Tool**: If assigned to a specific tool or activity, enter the tool name.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your form information.

## Tips

* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the form administrators.
* The form will be reviewed and responded to in a timely manner.
* Gift card values are limited, and selection is subject to availability.
