# it_service_request_form_2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for IT service requests. This information helps our team quickly understand the issue and assign it to the correct category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select the category of the problem you are experiencing.
4. Enter the date of the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your service request.
- **Category of the problem** (`problem_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the problem you are experiencing from the options provided.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
- **Further Explanation** (`further_explanation`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the problem you are experiencing.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the issue.
- **Problem Category** (`problem_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the problem you are experiencing from the options provided. This field is similar to the "Category of the problem" field above.
- **Date of the issue** (`issue_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the issue.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter your email address correctly.
- If you select "Yes" for a category, you will be contacted regarding the issue.
- If you select "No" for a category, the issue will not be escalated to that category.
- The "Further Explanation" field can be used to provide any additional information about the problem.
- The "Comments" field can be used to add any comments or notes about the issue.
- The "Date of the issue" field is required to ensure that the issue can be tracked and resolved correctly.
