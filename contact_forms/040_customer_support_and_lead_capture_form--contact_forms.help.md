# Customer Support And Lead Capture Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback, support requests, and lead generation information from customers. It allows customers to contact the support team and provides a channel for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Contact Us** title.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** (if you'd like to be recognized as a customer).
3. Provide your **Email** address so we can respond to your inquiry.
4. Share your **Phone** number (if you have one).
5. Describe your **Description** of the issue or your request.
6. Decide whether you want to capture your **Lead** or not.
7. If you choose to capture your **Lead**, select the **Lead Capture Option** that best describes your interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us**: This is the title that will be displayed on the form submission. You can keep it as is, or add a brief summary of your issue or request.
* **Customer Name**: This field is optional but helps us understand who you are contacting us about.
* **Email**: Please ensure you enter a valid email address so we can get back to you quickly and efficiently.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please provide it for our support team to contact you more conveniently.
* **Description**: Describe your issue, question, or request in detail. This will help us understand your concern better.
* **Lead Capture**: Choose "Yes" if you're interested in being contacted by our marketing team for potential leads. This will help us identify potential customers.
* **Lead Capture Option**: If you choose "Yes" for **Lead Capture**, select the option that best describes your interest (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4). This will allow us to tailor our follow-up communication.

## Tips
- Please double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- Be as clear and concise in your description as possible.
- Choose "No" for **Lead Capture** if you're not interested in being contacted by our marketing team.
