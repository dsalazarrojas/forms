# General Inquiry Contact Form With Dark Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit general inquiries or requests to your company. Fill out the form with your contact information and a brief message, and choose a category for your inquiry. Our team will respond to your message as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title (optional).
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your email address (please use a valid email format).
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Type a brief message describing your inquiry.
6. Select a category for your message.
7. Click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text, optional): Enter a title for your message, if you'd like to give it a specific subject.
* **customer_name** (text, required): Please provide your name to help us identify who is contacting us.
* **customer_email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can reply to you directly.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **customer_message** (text, required): Type a brief description of your inquiry or request.
* **category** (select_one, required): Choose a category that best describes the nature of your inquiry (e.g., Sales, Support, Feedback, etc.).
* **submit** (text, required): Click this button to send your message to our team.
