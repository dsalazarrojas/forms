# start_with_more_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "start_with_more_information" form is a general contact or information form designed to collect data from individuals for business or personal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter a Date in the "Date" field.
7. Enter a Time in the "Time" field.
8. Enter any additional Note or comment in the "Note" field.
9. Select one option in the "Select One" field.
10. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple", "Select Multiple 2" fields.
11. Enter a Decimal or Integer value in the "Decimal" and "Integer" fields.
12. Select a Time Zone from the "Time Zone" and "Time Zone 2" fields.
13. Enter a Date and Time in the "Date Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your First Name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your Last Name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, Optional): Enter your Company name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your Phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, Optional): Enter a valid Date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, Optional): Enter a valid Time.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional Note or comment.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, Optional): Select one option.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select multiple options.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select multiple options.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, `number`, Optional): Enter a Decimal value.
* **Integer** (`integer`, `number`, Optional): Enter an Integer value.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, Optional): Select a Time Zone.
* **Time Zone 2** (`time_zone_2`, `select_one`, Optional): Select a Time Zone.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, `date`, Optional): Enter a Date and Time.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field label for clarification.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the form owner or administrator.
