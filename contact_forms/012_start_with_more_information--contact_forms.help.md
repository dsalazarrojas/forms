# start_with_more_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "start_with_more_information" form is a general contact or information form designed to collect data from individuals for business or personal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter a Date in the "Date" field.
7. Enter a Time in the "Time" field.
8. Enter any additional Note or comment in the "Note" field.
9. Select one option in the "Select One" field.
10. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple", "Select Multiple 2" fields.
11. Enter a Decimal or Integer value in the "Decimal" and "Integer" fields.
12. Select a Time Zone from the "Time Zone" and "Time Zone 2" fields.
13. Enter a Date and Time in the "Date Time" field.
