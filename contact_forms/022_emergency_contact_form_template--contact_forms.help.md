# Emergency Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Form Template is designed to collect contact information for emergency purposes, such as out-of-town or international travel, medical conditions, or other situations where timely contact is crucial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form if you are an individual who needs to provide contact information or if you are the contact person for someone else.
2. Fill out all the required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
3. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name or the first name of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name or the last name of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Relation Name** (`relation_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are providing contact information for someone else, enter their relation to you (e.g., spouse, child, parent, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address or the address of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email or the email of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacted in emergency situations.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you are providing contact information for.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Enter the relationship between you and the person you are providing contact information for (e.g., colleague, neighbor, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Method** (`emergency_contact_method`, `text`, required: false): Enter the preferred method of emergency contact (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
