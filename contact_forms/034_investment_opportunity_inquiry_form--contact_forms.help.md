# investmentOpportunityInquiryForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for inquiring about investment opportunities. Please use it to share your interest in potential investment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Select all relevant fields as needed.
4. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: This is the main contact information section of the form. Please fill out this section with your contact information.
* **Email**: This is the email address associated with your contact information. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Financial Advisor**: Choose a financial advisor from the provided options.
* **Date**: This field is not required, but if you need to specify a date, please enter it here.
* **Time**: This field is not required, but if you need to specify a time, please enter it here.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number associated with your contact information. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Investment Interest**: This is an optional field to share your interest in potential investment opportunities.
* **Investment Opportunity**: This is an optional field to share any specific investment opportunities you are interested in.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form once all required fields are filled out.
* **Phone Number**: Please note that this field is not required, but if you enter a phone number, it will be associated with your contact information.
