# Travel Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information about travel-related activities or inquiries. Please provide your personal details, contact information, and travel-related information to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: All required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) are necessary for us to process your request.
2. **Use the correct format for dates**: For date fields, use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-07-25).
3. **Be accurate with membership information**: If you're a member, please provide your membership ID and type for our records.
4. **Select travel dates and type carefully**: Choose the correct travel dates and type from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)**: Enter your personal details, including [name, surname, etc.]. This is for our records.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your contact information, including [email address and phone number]. We'll use this to get in touch with you.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date Of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Date Of Arrival (6)**: Enter your expected date of arrival in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Travel Dates (7)**: Select the travel dates you're interested in.
* **Travel Type (8)**: Choose the type of travel you're interested in.
* **Membership Details (9)**: If you're a member, provide a brief description of your membership status.
* **Membership ID (10)**: Enter your membership ID number.
* **Membership Type (11)**: Choose your membership type (if applicable).
* **Membership Terms (12)**: Describe your membership terms (if applicable).
* **Membership Status (13)**: Select your current membership status.
* **Travel Agency (14)**: If you're working with a travel agency, enter their name.
* **Travel Agency Address (15)**: Enter the travel agency's address.

Note: If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
