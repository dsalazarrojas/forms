# FamilyChild Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects necessary information for a family child photography contract.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your photographer details.
2. Enter your client details.
3. Select the session date.
4. Choose the session time.
5. Enter any photographer or client notes.
6. Enter your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information as the photographer.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information as the client.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter the details of the session you want to book with the client.
* **Contract Agreement** (`contract_agreement`, select_one, optional): Choose whether or not you agree to the contract terms.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the session.
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or instructions for the photographer.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or instructions for the client.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure that all mandatory fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Please make sure to review and agree to the contract terms before submitting the form.
