# real_estate_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_communication_form is used to collect contact information and schedule appointments with potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **New Inquiry** field with a brief description of your inquiry.
2. Select a desired **Schedule an Appointment** time from the available options.
3. Choose your preferred **Preferred Contact Method** by selecting one or more of the available options.
4. Enter your **Message** to the real estate agent.
5. If applicable, enter your **Real Estate Information** and **Real Estate License Number**.
6. Provide any **Feedback** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Inquiry** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your inquiry.
* **Schedule an Appointment** (select_one, required/optional): Select a desired time for the appointment from the available options.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more of the available contact methods for communication.
* **Message** (note, required/optional): Enter a message for the real estate agent.
* **Real Estate Information** (email, required/optional): If applicable, provide your real estate agent information.
* **Real Estate License Number** (number, required/optional): If applicable, enter your real estate license number.
* **Feedback** (text, required/optional): Provide any feedback you may have.
