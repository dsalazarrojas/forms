# designer_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The designer_contact_form is a contact form for designers to provide their contact information and category selection for communication and collaboration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the text field marked "first_name". This is the first field on the form.
2. Enter your **last name** in the text field marked "last_name". This is the second field on the form.
3. Provide your **email address** in the text field marked "email". This is the third field on the form.
4. Enter your **phone number** in the text field marked "phone". This is the fourth field on the form.
5. Leave a **message** in the text field marked "message". This is the fifth field on the form.
6. Select the **category** for which you are reaching out. This is the sixth field on the form, and you can choose from the options "category a", "category b", or "category c".
7. If applicable, specify the **assigned tool** you are using in the text field marked "assigned_tool". This is the seventh field on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **last_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **message** (text, required/optional): Leave a message for the recipient.
- **category** (select_one, required/optional): Select the category for which you are reaching out (e.g., "category a", "category b", or "category c").
- **assigned_tool** (text, required/optional): If applicable, specify the assigned tool you are using.
