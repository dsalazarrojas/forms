# lime_theme_contact_us_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lime_theme_contact_us_form" is a contact form designed for users to reach out to the support team or team leaders with questions, feedback, or suggestions. The form is used to gather essential information from users, including their name, email, phone number, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form link to access the contact page.
2. Enter your name in the `name` field. Please provide your name as it appears on official identification.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field. This should be a valid email that you have access to.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field. This should be a valid phone number that you have access to.
5. If you have a website, enter it in the `website` field. This is optional.
6. In the `comments` field, provide any additional information or questions you have. This field is optional and not required.
7. If you are contacting us at a specific time, you can enter the date and time in the `date` and `time` fields. These fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number that you have access to.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information or questions you have.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are contacting us, if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are contacting us, if applicable.
