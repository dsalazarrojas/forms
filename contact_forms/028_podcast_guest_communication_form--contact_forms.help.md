# Podcast Guest Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a way for podcast guests to communicate with the team about their submissions, including their name, contact information, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Write your message in the "Message" field.
5. Select the category of your submission (audio, video, photo, or text) from the "Category" field.
6. Choose your guest type (host, guest, or both) from the "Guest Type" field.
7. Enter the date you submitted your content in the "Submission Date" field.
8. Enter the time you submitted your content in the "Submission Time" field.
9. If you have a specific tool assigned to you, enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. If you need to upload an output file, enter the file path in the "Output File" field.
11. Enter the category ID of your submission in the "Category ID" field (this is only required if you are a host).
12. Enter the form ID of your submission in the "Form ID" field (this is only required if you are a host).
13. Enter the title of your submission in the "Form Title" field.
14. Provide a brief description of your submission in the "Form Description" field.
15. Enter the category of your submission in the "Form Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: This is the name of the guest submitting content.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Email**: This is the email address of the guest submitting content.
  * Type: email
  * Required: No
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the guest submitting content.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Message**: This is a field for the guest to write any additional information about their submission.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Category**: Choose the category of your submission (audio, video, photo, or text).
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: No
* **Guest Type**: Choose your guest type (host, guest, or both).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: No
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your content.
  * Type: date
  * Required: No
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted your content.
  * Type: time
  * Required: No
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool assigned to you, enter it here.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Output File**: If you need to upload an output file, enter the file path here.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Category ID**: Enter the category ID of your submission (only required if you are a host).
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID of your submission (only required if you are a host).
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Form Title**: Enter the title of your submission.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Form Description**: Provide a brief description of your submission.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Form Category**: Enter the category of your submission.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
