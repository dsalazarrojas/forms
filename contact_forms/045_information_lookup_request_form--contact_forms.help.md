# InformationLookupRequestForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "InformationLookupRequestForm" is a form used to collect information from users to facilitate internal or external information lookup requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please use the correct format for your email address (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Provide a brief description of the information you are looking for in the "Description" field.
* Select the category of the information you are looking for from the "Category" dropdown menu.
* Choose the priority level of your request from the "Priority" dropdown menu.
* Select the status of your request from the "Status" dropdown menu.
* Optional: If you want to track the date and time your request was created, you can provide the "Date Created" and "Time Created" fields. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy for dates and hh:mm:ss for times.
* Optional: If you want to track the date and time your request was updated, you can provide the "Date Updated" and "Time Updated" fields. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy for dates and hh:mm:ss for times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Description** (5, text, required): Provide a brief description of the information you are looking for.
* **Category** (6, select_one, required): Select the category of the information you are looking for:
	+ Information
	+ Inquiry
	+ Request
* **Priority** (7, select_one, required): Choose the priority level of your request:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Status** (8, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your request:
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Cancelled
* **Date Created** (9, date, optional): If you want to track the date your request was created, use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date Updated** (10, date, optional): If you want to track the date your request was updated, use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Created** (11, time, optional): If you want to track the time your request was created, use the format hh:mm:ss.
* **Time Updated** (12, time, optional): If you want to track the time your request was updated, use the format hh:mm:ss.
