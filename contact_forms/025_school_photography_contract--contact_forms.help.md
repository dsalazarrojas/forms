# School Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photography Contract form is a tool used to gather necessary information from clients for the school's photography services. The form collects details such as client information, payment information, and payment terms to ensure clear communication and understanding between the client and the school administration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by ensuring you have all necessary information and authorization to fill out the form.
2. Begin by providing your client's information on the "Client Information" page.
3. Proceed to the "Payment Information" page to specify how you plan to make payments.
4. On the "Signature" page, indicate whether you agree to the photography contract terms.
5. Finally, choose your preferred payment gateways and methods on the "Payment Gateway" and "Payment Method" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (Page 1)
- **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, Required): Enter your name and contact details to allow us to communicate with you effectively.

### Payment Information (Page 2)
- **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, Required): Specify the details of your payment plan, including the amount and frequency of payments.

### Signature (Page 3)
- **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your agreement to the photography contract terms by selecting either "Yes" or "No".

### Payment Gateway (Page 4)
- **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose one or more payment gateways that you would like to use for payments.

### Payment Method (Page 5)
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more payment methods that you prefer for making payments.

### Payment Terms (Page 6)
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, Optional): Specify the terms of your payment, including details on payment due dates and any other relevant conditions.
 
 ## Tips
- Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the photography services and payment terms before filling out the form.
- Review the form thoroughly and ensure that all necessary information is provided.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
