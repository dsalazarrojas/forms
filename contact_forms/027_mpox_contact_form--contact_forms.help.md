# Mpox Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Contact Form is a tool to collect information from individuals who have had contact with Mpox, a virus that has gained attention due to recent outbreaks. Your responses will help health professionals understand how the virus spreads and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Contact Form**: 
   - Click on the "Contact Information" section, and provide a brief description of your exposure to Mpox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_form`, `text`, required):
  - This is the initial section where you provide a brief description of your exposure to Mpox.
  - Please include as much detail as you can about your situation.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required):
  - Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
  - Provide a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
  - Enter a working phone number that we can use to contact you if necessary.
* **Exposure Date** (`exposure_date`, `date`, required):
  - Select the date you think you were exposed to Mpox.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required):
  - Describe any symptoms you have experienced since being exposed to Mpox.
* **Exposure Location** (`exposure_location`, `text`, required):
  - Indicate where you were exposed to Mpox.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, optional):
  - If you have a healthcare provider who treated your Mpox symptoms, please provide their name or facility name.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, `text`, optional):
  - If you have visited a medical facility for Mpox treatment, enter their name or location.
* **Exposure Type** (`exposure_type`, `text`, required):
  - Describe how you think you got exposed to Mpox.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, `select_one`, required):
  - Select the type of contact:
    - Direct
    - Indirect
    - Household
    - Unknown
* **Exposure Duration** (`exposure_duration`, `text`, required):
  - Estimate how long you were exposed to Mpox.
* **Start Date** (`exposure_start_date`, `date`, required):
  - Enter the date you believe you started experiencing symptoms.
* **End Date** (`exposure_end_date`, `date`, optional):
  - If applicable, provide the date your symptoms ended.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, `select_multiple`, required):
  - Select all relevant travel history:
    - Within the US
    - Out of the US
    - Within the US state/region
    - Out of the US state/region
    - Unknown
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required):
  - Enter the date you traveled to or from an affected area.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, `text`, required):
  - Provide the location you visited or traveled from.
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, `select_one`, required):
  - Select how you traveled:
    - Airplane
    - Bus
    - Train
    - Boat
    - Other
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, `text`, required):
  - Estimate how long you spent traveling.
* **Travel Country** (`travel_country`, `text`, required):
  - Enter the country you visited or traveled from.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required):
  - Provide a working phone number for us to contact you at.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required):
  - Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you prefer to be known.

## Tips
- Ensure your responses are accurate and thorough.
- If you need help or have questions, contact us for assistance.
- Remember, this form is for reporting Mpox exposure.
