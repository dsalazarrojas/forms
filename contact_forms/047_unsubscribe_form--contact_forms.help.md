# Unsubscribe Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Unsubscribe Form is a tool used to confirm the removal of a user's email address from our email list. It is intended to be filled out by users who wish to stop receiving emails and notifications from us. This form is an AI-generated adaptation of a public survey on the topic of email management and is released under CC BY 4.0 license.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Unsubscribe Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "email" field.
2. Confirm that you are sure you want to unsubscribe by selecting "True" in the "are you sure" field.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Subscribe to our email list** (`subscribe_to_our_email_list`, Text, Optional)
This field is for you to enter the email address associated with your current subscription. If you've already submitted a new email address to subscribe, you can enter it here to unsubscribe from our list.

### **Email** (`email`, Email, Required)
Enter your email address to confirm your identity and allow us to update our records.

### **Are you sure?** (`are_you_sure`, Select One, Required)
Confirm that you are sure you want to unsubscribe from our email list. Please select "True" to confirm.

## Tips

* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're not sure why you're getting emails from us, please contact our support team to help resolve the issue.
* After submitting the form, you may receive a confirmation email to verify your request.
