# date_selection_and_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about preferred and end dates for booking purposes. It is used by customers to confirm their preferred dates and provide a contact number for communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred date from the date field.
2. If you have a preferred date in mind, enter it in the "Preferred Date" field. If you do not have a preferred date, you can leave this field blank.
3. Enter your end date for the booking, if applicable.
4. Confirm your booking by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Booking Confirmation" field.
5. If you have a contact number, enter it in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred date for the booking. If you do not have a preferred date, you can leave this field blank.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for the booking, if applicable.
- **Booking Confirmation** (`booking_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm your booking by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for communication purposes.
