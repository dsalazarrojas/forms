# Business Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Emergency Contact Form is used to gather contact information and emergency details from employees in case of business-related emergencies. This form helps the company maintain up-to-date emergency contact records, ensuring that important contacts are informed in the event of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the form link to access the Business Emergency Contact Form.
2. Fill in the required fields: Full Name, Position or Title, Work Phone Number, Mobile Phone Number, Work Email, Next of Kin Name, Relationship, and Next of Kin Phone.
3. Provide your Department and Home Address if applicable.
4. Enter your Current Medications and Health Insurance Provider if necessary.
5. Add any Additional Emergency Contacts if required.
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Position or Title (`position`, text, required): Enter your job title or position at the company.
* **Work Phone Number (`work_phone`, text, required): Enter your office phone number for official communication.
* **Mobile Phone Number (`mobile_phone`, text, required): Enter your personal mobile phone number for urgent contact.
* **Work Email (`work_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Personal Email (Optional) (`personal_email`, email, optional): Enter a personal email for backup contact.
* **Department (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your department or team name.
* **Home Address (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your street address for emergency contact.
* **City (`city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State or Province (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **Zip or Postal Code (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Next of Kin Name (`next_of_kin`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship (`kin_relationship`, text, required): Specify your relationship with the next of kin.
* **Next of Kin Phone (`kin_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Second Emergency Contact (Optional) (`second_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your backup emergency contact.
* **Second Contact Phone (Optional) (`second_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your backup emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any relevant health information or allergies.
* **Current Medications (`medications`, text, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Health Insurance Provider (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your health insurance provider.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional" but it's highly recommended to fill them in if possible.
