# Business Enrollment And Industry Classification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about a business's identity, address, industry, and contact details for categorization and registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form sections as prompted.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Be accurate when providing business and contact information.
4. Select the correct industry classification for your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Doing Business As (DBA)** (`business_dba`, optional): If your business operates under an assumed name, enter it here.
* **Business Structure** (`business_type`, required): Select the type of entity your business represents (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, required): Enter your business's Federal tax identification number or Employer Identification Number (EIN) if applicable.
* **State License Number** (`state_license`, optional): If your business has a state license, enter the number here.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, required): Enter your business's physical location.
* **City** (`city`, required): Enter your business's city name.
* **State** (`state`, required): Enter your business's state code.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, required): Enter your business's postal code.
* **Primary Industry** (`primary_industry`, required): Select the main type of business your company represents (e.g., Agriculture, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **NAICS Code** (`naics_code`, optional): If you know it, enter your NAICS code.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, required): Describe what your business does.
* **Primary Products or Services** (`primary_products`, required): Describe the main products or services your business offers.
* **Owner or Principal Name** (`owner_name`, required): Enter the name of your business's owner or principal.
* **Business Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your business's primary phone number.
* **Business Email** (`email`, required): Enter your business's primary email.
* **Website** (`website`, optional): Enter your business's website if it exists.
* **Number of Employees** (`employees`, required): Enter the total number of employees.
* **Business Start Date** (`founding_date`, required): Enter the date your business started operating.

## Tips

* Double-check your accuracy while filling out the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you're unsure about a field, contact your business registration officer or seek assistance.
