# Christmas Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Christmas Contact Form is designed for users to provide their contact information and inquiry details to businesses offering holiday-related services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your contact information: Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose your inquiry type: Select the subject of your inquiry from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief message: Describe your inquiry or needs in the message field.
4. Choose your preferred contact method: Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or either).
5. Optionally, provide your company name and phone number.
6. Choose your preferred time to contact you: Enter the best time for the contact team to reach you.
7. Subscribe to our holiday newsletter: Select whether you would like to receive our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone. If not, you can skip this field.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you represent a company, enter your company name.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, note, optional): Choose the subject of your inquiry.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (`subject`, select_one, required): Select the subject of your inquiry from the options provided.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Describe your inquiry or needs in this field.
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority level of your inquiry (high, medium, or low).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or either).
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_time_contact`, text, optional): Enter the best time for the contact team to reach you.
* **Newsletter Subscription** (`subscribe_newsletter`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our holiday newsletter.
