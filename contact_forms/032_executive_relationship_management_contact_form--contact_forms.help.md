# Executive Relationship Management Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from executives or individuals who need to be managed or kept in touch with. This includes their job title, email address, and other relevant details. The collected data will help your team to establish and maintain relationships, facilitate communication, and stay up-to-date with important contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the **Contact Information** section.
2. Provide your **Job Title** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
4. Provide your **Email Address** in the required format (e.g., "user@example.com").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**:
	+ **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your official job title at your organization.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., "user@example.com").

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check your company name and email address for correct formatting.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact [support email] (form@gic.mx) for assistance.
