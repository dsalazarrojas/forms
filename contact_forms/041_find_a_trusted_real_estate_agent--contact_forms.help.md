# Find A Trusted Real Estate Agent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form "Find A Trusted Real Estate Agent" is designed to collect information from users to find a suitable real estate agent that meets their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their relevance to finding a trusted real estate agent.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by *) to ensure you provide enough information for the form's purpose.
3. Fill in your client preferences, such as whether you want to contact a real estate agency or not, and what services you require.
4. Provide accurate contact details, including agency phone number and email address.
5. Select the agency location, preferred service(s), and agency phone number.
6. Complete the agency address, description, and license information, if available.
7. Add any additional comments or messages to the user message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Preferences (1) (text, required: false)**: This field is used to gather your client preferences, such as whether you want to contact a real estate agency or not. You can indicate your preference here.
* **Contact Details (2) (email, required: true)**: This field requires your contact email address so that the real estate agency can reach out to you.
* **Real Estate Agency (3) (select_one, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you are looking to contact a real estate agency.
* **Agency Location (4) (select_one, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you want to filter agencies by location.
* **Preferred Service (5) (select_multiple, required: true)**: Choose the services you require from the list provided. This will help the real estate agency understand your needs.
* **Agency Phone (6) (text, required: true)**: Provide the real estate agency's phone number so that they can contact you.
* **Agency Email (7) (email, required: false)**: If the real estate agency has an email address, provide it here.
* **Agency Website (8) (text, required: false)**: Provide the real estate agency's website address, if available.
* **Agency Fax (9) (text, required: false)**: Provide the real estate agency's fax number, if available.
* **Agency Hours (10) (time, required: false)**: Provide the real estate agency's hours of operation, if available.
* **Agency Address (11) (text, required: false)**: Provide the real estate agency's address.
* **Agency Description (12) (text, required: false)**: Provide a brief description of the real estate agency.
* **Agency License (13) (text, required: false)**: Provide the real estate agency's license number, if available.
* **Agency Rating (14) (note, required: false)**: Note down any rating or review you have for the real estate agency.
* **User Message (15) (text, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or messages to the real estate agent.
