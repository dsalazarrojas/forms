# recently_contacted_list - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recently_contacted_list form is used to record information about patients that healthcare professionals have recently contacted. This data helps in contact tracing and quality improvement processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's last name in the "patient_last_contacted" field.
2. Select or enter information about who contacted the patient ("contacted_person").
3. Choose the date of the last contact ("date_last_contacted").
4. Choose the time of the last contact ("time_last_contacted").
5. If necessary, enter any additional information about stop spreading ("stop_spreading").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_last_contacted** (text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **contacted_person** (select multiple, optional): Select all the people who contacted the patient. Possible options are "Yes" and "No".
* **date_last_contacted** (date, required): Choose the date of the last contact with the patient.
* **time_last_contacted** (time, required): Choose the time of the last contact with the patient.
* **stop_spreading** (text, optional): If a stop spreading order was given, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for each patient.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting it.
* Consider using the "contacted_person" field only if there were multiple people involved in the contact.
* Be precise when choosing the date and time of the last contact.
* If a stop spreading order was given, clearly enter it in the "stop_spreading" field.
