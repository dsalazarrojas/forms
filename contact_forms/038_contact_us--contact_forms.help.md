# Contact Us - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact the company with inquiries, questions, or feedback. Fill out this form to send a message to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide a valid email address and phone number if you have one.
3. Select one of the given categories for your inquiry.
4. Clearly explain what you want to talk about and when you need the response.
5. Choose how you found us, if you want to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **We Would Love to Hear From You** (`greeting`, note, required: false): This is a note field where you can add a brief introduction or greeting.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: true): Enter your full name, as we would like to address you by your name in our response.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: true): Provide your email address to ensure we can get back to you quickly and efficiently.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you want to be reached by phone, please provide your contact number. This is not required.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): If you're contacting us on behalf of your company, please provide your company name.
* **Inquiry Category** (`inquiry_category`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the given categories that best describes your inquiry.
* **Topic** (`topic`, text, required: true): Briefly describe the topic of your inquiry.
* **Details** (`details`, text, required: true): Provide as much detail as possible about your inquiry.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required: false): Choose when you need the response, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`referral`, select_one, required: false): Select how you found us, so we can track our marketing efforts.
* **Join Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required: false): If you're interested in staying updated, choose yes to receive our newsletter.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Be clear and concise in your message.
* Check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any attachments or files, please mention them in your message.
