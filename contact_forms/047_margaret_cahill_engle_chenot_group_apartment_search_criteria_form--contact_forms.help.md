# Margaret Cahill Engle Chenot Group Apartment Search Criteria Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users search for apartments based on their specific criteria. By filling out this form, you will be able to filter your search and find the perfect apartment that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your search criteria in the text fields, such as search criteria and search criteria 2.
2. Select your preferred location from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date range or a specific date for your search.
4. Select your preferred price range.
5. Choose an available space type.
6. Select amenities that you are interested in.
7. Choose a lease term.
8. Select your preferred floor plan.
9. Select a available date.
10. Select a preferred date.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes.
12. List search results and search results 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Criteria** (`search_criteria`, `text`, optional): Enter your search criteria to help us narrow down your search results. This can be anything you want, such as a specific neighborhood or feature you are looking for.
* **Date Range** (`date_range`, `date`, optional): Select a date range to filter your search results. This can be a specific date range or a month/year range.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred location from the dropdown menu. This will help us narrow down your search results.
* **Preferred Location 2** (`preferred_location_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred location from the dropdown menu. This will help us narrow down your search results.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, `number`, optional): Enter a price range to filter your search results. This can be a specific price range or a range with a minimum and maximum price.
* **Available Space** (`available_space`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the available space type that you are looking for. This can be studio, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, or 3-bedroom.
* **Amenities** (`amenities`, `checkbox`, optional): Select the amenities that you are interested in. This can be anything from a pool to a gym.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a lease term that suits your needs. This can be 1 year, 2 year, or 3 year.
* **Preferred Floor Plan** (`preferred_floor_plan`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred floor plan. This can be 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, or 3-bedroom.
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, `date`, optional): Select a available date to filter your search results. This can be a specific date or a month/year range.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): Select a preferred date to filter your search results. This can be a specific date or a month/year range.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your search.
* **Search Results** (`search_results`, `select_multiple`, optional): List search results that match your criteria.
* **Search Criteria 2** (`search_criteria_2`, `text`, optional): Enter additional search criteria.
* **Search Results 2** (`search_results_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): List additional search results that match your criteria.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your search criteria.
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, don't hesitate to enter them in the "Note" field.
* Once you have completed the form, click submit to generate your search results.
