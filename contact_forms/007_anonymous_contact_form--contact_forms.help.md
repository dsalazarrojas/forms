# Anonymous Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anonymous Contact Form is a secure and confidential channel for users to submit their inquiries, suggestions, and complaints without revealing their identity. This form is designed to provide a safe and private way for users to contact the relevant department or team within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Subject of Inquiry from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the department that this message is related to.
3. Select the type of submission you are making (e.g., question, suggestion, complaint, etc.).
4. Provide a detailed message in the text box, including as much information as possible while maintaining your anonymity.
5. Optionally, select the priority level of your message if you need a prompt response.
6. If applicable, provide the date of the incident or inquiry.
7. If relevant, provide a description of any evidence or documents attached.
8. Select whether you need a response to your message.
9. Finally, choose how you heard about this reporting channel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subject of Inquiry**: This field allows you to specify the subject of your message. Please select the most relevant option from the dropdown menu.
* **Department**: This field enables you to choose which department within the organization your message is related to. Select the most relevant option from the dropdown menu.
* **Type of Submission**: This field is used to describe the nature of your message (e.g., a question, suggestion, complaint, etc.). Choose the most relevant option from the dropdown menu.
* **Your Message**: This is the main text box for your message. Please provide as much detail as possible while maintaining your anonymity.
* **Priority Level**: This field is optional, but if you need a prompt response, select the priority level that best suits your needs. Please note that priority levels are not guarantees of response times.
* **Date of Incident or Inquiry (if applicable)**: If your message is related to a specific incident or inquiry, please provide the date of the incident or inquiry.
* **Description of any evidence or documents available**: If you have attached any evidence or documents related to your message, please provide a description of these in this field.
* **Do you require a response?**: Indicate whether you need a response to your message. If you choose "Yes", we will respond directly. If you choose "No", this message will be for information purposes only.
* **How did you hear about this reporting channel?**: This field is optional, but it helps us understand how users are aware of this reporting channel. Please provide any relevant information in this field.

Note: All submitted messages are reviewed and processed by the relevant department or team. Please be assured that your message will be treated confidentially and securely.
