# easy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "easy_contact_form" to help you understand its purpose and how to fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
   - Zip Code
   - City
   - State
   - Country

2. Fill out the optional fields:
   - Message (if you have any comments or questions)
   - Promotion
   - Giveaway (select as many options as apply)
   - Date (if relevant to your response)
   - Time (if relevant to your response)
   - Confirm Email (to confirm your email address)
   - Confirm Phone (if you want to confirm your phone number, but not required)
   - Agree to Terms (select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions)
   - Agree to Cookies (select "Yes" to agree to the cookies policy)
   - Agreement (if you have any additional comments or agreements)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required): Enter your country.
* **Message** (`Message`, note, optional): If you have any comments or questions, fill out this field.
* **Promotion** (`Promotion`, select_one, optional): Select the promotional option that applies to you.
* **Giveaway** (`Giveaway`, select_multiple, optional): Select as many giveaway options as apply.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): If relevant to your response, enter the date.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If relevant to your response, enter the time.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, email, required): Enter your email address to confirm it.
* **Confirm Phone** (`Confirm Phone`, text, optional): If you want to confirm your phone number, fill out this field.
* **Agree to Terms** (`Agree to Terms`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Cookies** (`Agree to Cookies`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to agree to the cookies policy.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or agreements, fill out this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your email and phone number confirmations.
- Review the terms and conditions and agree to them.
- Review the cookies policy and agree to it if necessary.
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If unsure, ask your administrator for assistance.
 
This guide is created for your convenience and understanding. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to your administrator.
