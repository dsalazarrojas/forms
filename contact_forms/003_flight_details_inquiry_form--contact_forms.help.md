# flight_details_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather details about flights for travelers or travel agencies. Please fill out the form correctly to ensure accurate and relevant information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the flight you are inquiring about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, optional): This field is for describing the flight you are inquiring about. Please provide a brief description of the flight.
* **Flight ID** (`flight_id`, number, optional): This field is for entering the flight identification number.
* **Departure City** (`departure_city`, text, optional): This field is for entering the city of departure for the flight.
* **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`, text, optional): This field is for entering the city of arrival for the flight.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date of travel.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the travel time.
* **Passengers** (`passengers`, number, optional): This field is for entering the number of passengers on the flight.
* **Flight Class** (`flight_class`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the class of service for the flight: Economy, First Class, or Business Class.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, number, optional): This field is for entering the flight number.
* **Flight Duration** (`flight_duration`, number, optional): This field is for entering the length of the flight in hours.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the departure time.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the arrival time.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): This field is for entering the travel agency associated with the flight.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is for entering the contact email of the person inquiring about the flight.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering the contact phone number of the person inquiring about the flight.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* Fill out the fields as accurately and concisely as possible to prevent form duplication.
* If any fields are not required, feel free to skip them, but ensure to fill out the other fields correctly.
