# Sales Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Inquiry Form is a tool that allows customers to get in touch with our sales team with questions or requests. To ensure that your inquiry is handled correctly, please provide all required and relevant information in the form fields below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Customer Email** field. Please ensure that your email is accurate, as this is how our sales team will contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
4. Describe your sales inquiry or question in the **Customer Request** field. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your needs.
5. Select how you would like to be contacted in the **Contact Type** field. You can choose between **Phone**, **Email**, or **Chat**.
6. If you have any additional questions or information to provide, enter it in the **Additional Questions** field.
7. Enter the name of the salesperson you would like to contact, or leave this field blank if you don't have one in mind.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so that our sales team knows who to contact.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that our sales team can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like for us to contact you by phone.
* **Customer Request** (`customer_request`, text, optional): Describe your sales inquiry or question in as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted: **Phone**, **Email**, or **Chat**.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or questions, enter them here.
* **Salesperson** (`salesperson`, text, optional): Enter the name of the salesperson you would like to contact, or leave blank to let us assign someone.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed, as this will help us to efficiently route your inquiry to the correct salesperson.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
* Our sales team will contact you within 24 hours of receiving your inquiry.
