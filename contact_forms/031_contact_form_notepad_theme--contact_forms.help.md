# Contact Form Notepad Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather contact information, type of inquiry, and other relevant details from users to facilitate efficient communication and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Describe your inquiry: Select the type of inquiry you have, such as General Question, Support Request, Sales Inquiry, or Feedback/Suggestion.
3. Summarize your inquiry: Provide a brief summary of your inquiry in the "Subject" field.
4. Share more details: Write a more detailed message explaining your inquiry in the "Your Message" field.
5. Choose your preferred contact method: Select how you would like to be contacted, such as via email or phone call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your mobile or landline phone number for additional contact.
* **Type of Inquiry** (Required): Select the primary reason for your contact from the available options.
* **Subject** (Required): Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Message** (Required): Write a more detailed message explaining your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email or phone call).
* **Date of Submission** (Required): Automatically populated with today's date for tracking purposes.
