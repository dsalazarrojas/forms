# Nsdf 18 Incident First Aid Reporting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that require first aid or other relevant information for incident management and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the respective fields.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4.  Select the type of incident from the options provided in the "Incident Type" field.
5.  If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred.
6.  Enter the name, phone number, and email of the person reporting the incident.
7.  Enter the name of the reporting officer (if applicable).
8.  Optionally, add any additional information or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details that might be relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Report Name** (`report_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Report Phone** (`report_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Report Email** (`report_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the officer who is handling the incident report.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the report in YYYY-MM-DD format.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form design.
