# Conveyancing Forms Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal and property-related details. It is used to gather essential data for conveyancing services, such as property addresses, financial information, and contact preferences. Completing this form helps our team to assess your requirements and provide tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your phone number and mobile number in the "Phone Number" and "Mobile Number" fields, respectively.
3. Provide your current address in the "Current Address" field.
4. Choose the type of address you are currently occupying from the "Address Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Owned, Rented, Living with Family).
5. Select the length of time you have been living at your current address from the "How long have you lived at this address" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the type of transaction you require from the "Type of Transaction" dropdown menu (e.g., Buying, Selling, Buying and Selling).
7. Provide the address of the property related to the transaction in the "Property Address" field.
8. Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Detached House, Semi-Detached House).
9. If applicable, enter the purchase price and deposit amount.
10. Specify the source of funds for the transaction from the "Source of Funds" dropdown menu (e.g., Savings, Sale of Property).
11. Choose how you would like to be contacted from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu (e.g., Email, Phone).
12. Select the best time to contact you from the "Best Time to Contact" dropdown menu (e.g., Morning, Afternoon).
13. Check the boxes to indicate that you have accepted our terms of business and privacy policy.
14. Sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance.
15. Add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: This is the legal name of the client.
* **Email Address**: This is the primary email address used for communication.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number for emergency contact.
* **Mobile Number**: This is the mobile phone number for urgent matters.
* **Mobile Number**: This is an optional field for an alternative mobile number.
* **Current Address**: This is the full mailing address of the client.
* **Address Type**: This determines the type of address the client is currently occupying.
* **How long have you lived at this address**: This specifies the length of time the client has been living at their current address.
* **Type of Transaction**: This selects the type of transaction the client requires (e.g., Buying, Selling).
* **Property Address**: This is the address of the property being bought or sold.
* **Property Type**: This determines the type of property (e.g., Detached House, Flat).
* **Purchase Price**: This is the agreed or expected price of the property.
* **Expected Completion Date**: This specifies the desired completion date.
* **Date of Birth**: This is the date of birth of the client.
* **Nationality**: This is the client's nationality.
* **Occupation**: This is the client's profession.
* **Employer Name**: This is the name of the client's employer (if applicable).
* **ID Type**: This selects the type of ID provided (e.g., Passport, Driving License).
* **ID Number**: This is the ID number or passport number.
* **Proof of Address Provided**: This specifies the proof of address provided (e.g., Utility Bill, Bank Statement).
* **Do you need a mortgage**: This indicates whether the client requires a mortgage.
* **Do you have a mortgage agreement in principle**: This indicates whether the client has a mortgage agreement in principle.
* **Lender Name**: This is the name of the mortgage lender (if applicable).
* **Deposit Amount**: This is the available deposit amount.
* **Source of Funds**: This specifies the source of funds for the transaction.
* **Are you a first time buyer**: This indicates whether the client is a first-time buyer.
* **Is this a buy to let property**: This indicates whether the property is for letting.
* **Is this shared ownership**: This indicates whether the property is shared ownership.
* **Are you using Help to Buy scheme**: This indicates whether the client is using Help to Buy scheme.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This is the preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone).
* **Best Time to Contact**: This is the best time to contact the client (e.g., Morning, Afternoon).
* **Terms Accepted**: This indicates whether the client accepts the terms of business.
* **Privacy Policy Accepted**: This indicates whether the client accepts the privacy policy.
* **Signature Date**: This is the date of acceptance.
* **Additional Comments**: This is for any additional comments or information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your contact preferences.
* Read through the terms of business and privacy policy before accepting.
