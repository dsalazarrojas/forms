# Contact Us Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contact Us Form is a tool for users to get in touch with your organization. It allows users to provide their contact information, select a category for their inquiry, and specify their message.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by entering your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Optionally, provide your phone number, organization, and role or title.
3. Choose a category for your inquiry from the provided list:
	* Information Request
	* Support
	* Feedback
	* Partnership
	* Media Inquiry
	* Other
4. Enter your message, which should include the subject and details about your inquiry.
5. If you have an optional attachment, you can upload it.
6. Specify how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or either).
7. Select the best time for you to be contacted (morning, afternoon, evening, or anytime).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, you can enter it for additional contact options (optional).
* **Organization**: If you represent an organization, enter your company name (optional).
* **Role or Title**: Enter your position or title within the organization (optional).
* **Subject**: Enter a brief subject for your message.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your inquiry from the provided list.
* **Message**: Enter a detailed message with the subject and your inquiry.
* **Attachment**: If you have an optional attachment, you can upload it to support your message.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or either).
* **Availability**: Select the best time for you to be contacted (morning, afternoon, evening, or anytime).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with \*).
- Be as specific as possible when selecting the category and subject.
- If you have an attachment, ensure it's relevant to your message.
- Be clear and concise in your message.
- Choose the best contact preference and availability based on your schedule and preferences.
