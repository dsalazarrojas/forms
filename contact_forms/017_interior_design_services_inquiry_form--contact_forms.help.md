# Interior Design Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their interior design project needs, allowing our interior designers to provide accurate and relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the project title and details on the **Project Details** field.
- Write a brief description of your project in the **Project Description** field.
- Select your preferred **Design Style** from the available options (e.g., Modern, Traditional, Rustic, Contemporary, Mid-Century).
- Enter your approximate **Project Budget** (if known).
- Specify your **Project Location** where the interior design services are needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: A brief description of your project, including the type of project (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Project Description**: A more detailed description of your project, including any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Preferred Design Style**: The aesthetic style you're looking for in your project (e.g., Modern, Traditional, etc.). You can select one or more of the available options.
* **Project Budget**: Your approximate budget for the project. If you're unsure, leave it blank.
* **Project Location**: The exact location of your project, including the address or city.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling in the **Project Description** field, as it will help our designers understand your project better.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask us for clarification.
- Make sure to proofread your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and consistency.
