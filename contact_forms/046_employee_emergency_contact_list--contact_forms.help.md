# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and maintain a list of emergency contact information for employees in case of an emergency. This form requires information such as the employee's name, phone number, email, and relation to the employee, as well as the type of contact and contact number, and extension.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's details in the corresponding fields.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee** (`employee`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's name. This field should contain the employee's last name, first name, or any other identifier.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person. This field should contain the contact person's last name, first name, or any other identifier.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person. This field should contain the contact person's phone number, including any extensions or other relevant details.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the emergency contact person. This field should contain the contact person's email address.
*   **Relation** (`relation`, `text`, required: false): Choose the type of relation between the contact person and the employee. Select "Emergency Contact" or "Family Member".
*   **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    +   Choose the type of contact person, such as an emergency contact or family member.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional phone numbers of the contact person.
*   **Extension** (`extension`, `text`, required: false): Enter any extension numbers associated with the contact person's phone number.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Verify that the contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If a contact person has multiple phone numbers, enter each number in the "Contact Number" field.
*   If a contact person has multiple extensions, enter each extension in the "Extension" field.
*   This form is for emergency contact information only. Do not use it for any other purpose.
