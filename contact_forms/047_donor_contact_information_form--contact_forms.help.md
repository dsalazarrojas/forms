# Donor Contact Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store donor contact information, which is essential for communicating with our donors, sharing updates, and keeping them informed about our organization's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or mail).
3. Choose your preferred communication frequency (once a month, weekly, or monthly).
4. If you'd like, you can opt-out of receiving email communications.
5. Add any additional notes about your contact information (e.g., your work address or special instructions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive updates and communications from our organization.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive phone calls or SMS from us.
* **Email Opt-out**: If you want to opt-out of receiving email communications from our organization, select "Yes" here.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select one or more methods you'd like us to use to contact you. You can choose phone, email, or mail.
* **Communication Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to hear from us. You can select "Never", "Once a month", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
* **Additional Notes**: Use this space to provide any additional information about your contact preferences, such as your work address or special instructions.

Note: Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that our organization can effectively communicate with you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
