# Child Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Information Form is designed to collect essential details about your child to ensure their safety and well-being while in our care. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in the Child Full Name field with your child's name as it is registered for our activities.
2. Select the Date of Birth of your child to help us track their age and developmental stage.
3. Provide the Parent or Guardian Name, which should be the primary point of contact for your child.
4. Enter your Contact Phone Number so we can reach you easily during activity hours.
5. If your child has any known allergies, please select all that apply from the list provided.
6. If your child takes any medications, please provide details about the dosage and schedule.
7. Grant permission for your child to participate in outdoor activities, including playground and field activities.
8. Finally, select the current date to ensure we have a record of the form submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, Text, Required): Enter your child's name as it is registered for our activities.
* **Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, Date, Required): Select your child's birthday to help us track their age and developmental stage.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, Text, Required): Provide your name, which should be the primary point of contact for your child.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you easily during activity hours.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all known allergies your child has, such as Peanuts and Tree Nuts, Dairy and Lactose, Gluten or Wheat, Latex Sensitivity, or Bee Stings.
* **Medication Information** (`medication_info`, Text, Optional): If your child takes any medications, enter details about the dosage and schedule.
* **I grant permission for my child to participate in outdoor activities** (`outdoor_permission`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you grant permission for your child to participate in outdoor activities, including playground and field activities.
* **Today's Date** (`today_date`, Date, Required): Select the current date to ensure we have a record of the form submission date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* We appreciate your attention to detail to ensure your child's safety and well-being while in our care.
