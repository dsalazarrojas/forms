# Child Contact Details Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record the contact details of a child, including their name, date of birth, home address, and contact information for primary and secondary contacts. The collected information will be used for internal purposes such as communication and identification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for primary child information (Child Full Name, Date of Birth, Home Street Address, City and Postal Code, Guardian Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Primary Email Address).
2. Optionally, provide additional contact information for the primary contact person (e.g., Secondary Contact Name and Phone).
3. Fill out the remaining fields only if you have additional contact information for a secondary contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Child Full Name
Child Full Name (`child_name`, text, required) 
Enter the full legal name of the child, including any middle name or initials.

### Date of Birth
Date of Birth (`child_dob`, date, required) 
Enter the child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Home Street Address
Home Street Address (`home_address`, text, required) 
Enter the child's home address, including the street number and name.

### City and Postal Code
City and Postal Code (`city_postal_code`, text, required) 
Enter the city and postal code of the child's home address, e.g., "Seattle, 98101".

### Guardian Full Name
Guardian Full Name (`guardian_name`, text, required) 
Enter the full name of the child's primary guardian or contact person.

### Mobile Phone Number
Mobile Phone Number (`mobile_phone`, text, required) 
Enter the mobile phone number of the child's primary guardian or contact person, for urgent communication.

### Primary Email Address
Primary Email Address (`primary_email`, email, required) 
Enter the primary email address of the child's guardian or contact person, for regular correspondence.

### Secondary Contact Name and Phone
Secondary Contact Name and Phone (`secondary_info`, text, optional) 
Enter the full name and phone number of the child's secondary contact person, if applicable.

Note: This form is used to collect and record contact information for a child. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or department.
