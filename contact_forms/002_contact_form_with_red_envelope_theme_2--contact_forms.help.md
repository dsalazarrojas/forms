# Contact Form With Red Envelope Theme 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to communicate with our team regarding delivery status, technical support, general inquiries, or to provide feedback. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information to ensure our team can assist you effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the label and hint text for guidance.
- Ensure each field is filled out according to its label and required/optional status.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Send Us a Message --** (`contact_header`, note, false): This is an optional header note that introduces the form. Feel free to add a brief message to explain the purpose of your inquiry.
* **Your Full Name** (`full_name`, text, true): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, true): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): If you have a contact number, include it for our team to reach you.
* **Message Subject** (`subject`, select_one, true): Select a subject that describes your inquiry, such as "Delivery Status" or "Technical Support".
* **Your Message** (`message_content`, text, true): Provide a detailed description of your issue or inquiry.
* **How should we contact you?** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, false): If you have a preferred method of contact, such as email, phone, or SMS, select it.
* **Do you have a photo of your envelope or package?** (`attachment_check`, select_one, false): If you have a relevant photo or attachment, select "True".
* **Date of Inquiry** (`submission_date`, date, true): Enter the date you submitted this form.
## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Your Message" field.
- Ensure your email address is correct and up-to-date.
- If you have any attachments, please mention it in the "Do you have a photo of your envelope or package?" field.
- If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please contact us after submitting this form.
