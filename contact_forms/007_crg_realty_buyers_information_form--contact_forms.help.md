# CRG_Realty Buyers Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential real estate buyers, helping real estate agents and brokers to identify their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Current Residential Address**: Enter your current home address.
3. **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you.
4. **Primary Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
5. **Buyer Status**: Select your buyer status from the provided options (First Time Buyer, Investor, Relocating, Looking for Larger Home, or Downsizing).
6. **Preferred City or Neighborhood**: Enter the city or neighborhood where you are interested in buying a property.
7. **Property Type Interest**: Select the types of properties you are interested in (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, etc.).
8. **Minimum Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you require.
9. **Minimum Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you require.
10. **Price Range - Minimum**: Enter the minimum price range you are willing to pay (leave blank if not applicable).
11. **Price Range - Maximum**: Enter the maximum price range you are willing to pay.
12. **Are you pre-approved for a mortgage?**: Select your mortgage status from the provided options (Yes - Letter available, No - Need assistance, or Cash Buyer).
13. **Estimated Down Payment Percentage**: Enter the estimated down payment percentage (leave blank if not applicable).
14. **Desired Closing Timeframe**: Select the timeframe for closing the deal from the provided options (Within 30 Days, 1 to 3 Months, 3 to 6 Months, or Just Browsing).
