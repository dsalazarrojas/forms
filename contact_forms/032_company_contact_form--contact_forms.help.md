# Company Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Company Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from visitors to our company, enabling us to better understand their inquiries and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information.
* For optional fields, provide any relevant information you feel will help us understand your inquiry.
* Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you prefer to be reached by phone.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization if it's different from your personal name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your professional title within your organization.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, optional): Select the industry category that best describes your organization:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Education
	+ Government
	+ Nonprofit
	+ Other
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your organization:
	+ 1-10 employees
	+ 11-50 employees
	+ 51-200 employees
	+ 201-500 employees
	+ 501-1000 employees
	+ 1000+ employees
* **What is this regarding?** (`subject`, select_one, required): Choose the type of inquiry you are making:
	+ General Inquiry
	+ Product Information
	+ Sales Question
	+ Pricing Request
	+ Support Request
	+ Partnership Opportunity
	+ Media Inquiry
	+ Investor Relations
	+ Careers
	+ Feedback
	+ Other
* **Your Message** (`message`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your inquiry or question.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Either
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the best time for us to contact you:
	+ Morning (8am-12pm)
	+ Afternoon (12pm-5pm)
	+ Evening (5pm-8pm)
	+ Anytime
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard_about`, select_one, optional): Choose the source of your visit:
	+ Website
	+ Search Engine
	+ Social Media
	+ Friend or Colleague
	+ Advertisement
	+ Trade Show
	+ Email Campaign
	+ Other
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter?** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to receive our marketing updates:
	+ Yes, please
	+ No, thank you

Note: The **Submission Date** field is automatically populated with the current date, so you do not need to fill it out.
