# Daily Call Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Call Log Form is a tool used to track and report on daily calls. It helps users keep a record of their interactions and provides a convenient way to analyze and report on call activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Call Log Form, follow these steps:

1. **Daily Call Log Form**: This is the title of the form. It is not a required field.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the call, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. **Time**: Enter the time of the call, in the format 12:00 AM.
4. **Duration**: Enter the length of the call in minutes.
5. **Call Type**: Select whether the call was a 'Yes' or 'No' (this field is not well-defined, please contact the owner for clarification).
6. **Purpose**: Enter the reason for the call.
7. **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the call.
8. **Assigned Tool**: Select whether the call was related to an assigned tool.
9. **User**: Select whether the call was with a user.
10. **Team Member**: Select which team members were present during the call.
11. **Team Member**: (Note: This field seems to be a duplicate of field 10, please review the form for consistency)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Call Log Form**: This title is used to identify the form and its purpose.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the call, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the call, in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of the call in minutes.
* **Call Type**: Select whether the call was a 'Yes' or 'No', indicating that the call was either yes or no in some way (please contact the owner for further clarification).
* **Purpose**: Enter the reason for the call, providing context and insight into the call's purpose.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the call, such as notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select whether the call was related to an assigned tool, indicating that the call was related to a specific tool or resource.
* **User**: Select whether the call was with a user, indicating that the call was with a person or customer.
* **Team Member**: Select which team members were present during the call, providing a list of participants.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field, as this will help ensure the data is accurate and useful.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please contact the owner for clarification.
* Review and report on your call activity regularly to gain valuable insights and improve your process.
