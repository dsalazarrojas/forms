# Animated Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animated Contact Form is designed to collect contact information from users to facilitate communication and provide support. It's intended for customers, partners, or anyone looking to inquire about our services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your contact information, including your full name, email address, and preferred method of contact.
2. Select a subject that best describes the purpose of your inquiry.
3. Enter a brief message describing your question or concern.
4. If applicable, provide additional information about your company or industry.
5. Indicate your preferred time for us to contact you and whether you'd like us to follow up with you.
6. Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email or phone) and whether you'd like to be added to our newsletter.
7. Agree to our privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address where you can be reached for responses.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for quick responses.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `select_one`, required): Choose a subject that best describes your inquiry (e.g., General Inquiry, Product Question, etc.).
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Type a brief message describing your question or concern.
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, `select_one`, optional): Select the urgency level of your inquiry (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your position or role (if applicable).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the industry you're associated with (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, etc.).
* **Communication Preferences** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or Either method).
* **Best Time to Contact** (`contact_time`, `select_one`, optional): Select a time slot when it's best for us to reach out to you.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you'd like us to follow up with you (Yes, No, or Only if necessary).
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you'd like to be added to our newsletter (Yes, No).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any extra details you'd like us to know.
* **File Attachments** (`attachment_note`, `note`, optional): Attach relevant documents if needed.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to our privacy policy (Yes, No).
