# Contact Form With Wooden Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and inquiries from guests to provide them with the best possible experience. Please answer the following questions to ensure we can respond to your needs efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Rustic Inquiry Form" section to begin.
2. Enter your Guest Name in the "Guest Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your Guest Email in the "Guest Email" field. This is a required field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Guest Phone" field. This field is optional.
5. Select the "Nature of Inquiry" that best describes your reason for contacting us.
6. In the "Message Details" field, provide a detailed description of your inquiry.
7. If you prefer to be contacted during a specific time of day, select your preferred time in the "Best time to reach you" field. This field is optional.
8. If applicable, enter the estimated number of people attending in the "Estimated Attendance" field.
9. Check the "Require follow up?" field if you would like to be contacted again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Rustic Inquiry Form** (`note`, not required): This section serves as an introduction to the form and sets the tone for the inquiry process.
* **Guest Name** (`text`, required): Please enter your name to help us understand who is contacting us.
* **Guest Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can respond to you efficiently.
* **Guest Phone** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it to ensure we can contact you.
* **Nature of Inquiry** (`select_one`, required): Select the reason you are contacting us.
	+ **Reservation**: You are inquiring about making a reservation.
	+ **Event Planning**: You are interested in planning an event with us.
	+ **Store Inquiry**: You have a question or concern about our store.
	+ **Other**: Select this if your inquiry does not fit into the above categories.
* **Message Details** (`text`, required): Describe your inquiry in detail, and we will do our best to assist you.
* **Best time to reach you** (`select_one`, optional): Select your preferred time of day to be contacted.
	+ **Morning**: Between 9 AM and 12 PM.
	+ **Afternoon**: Between 1 PM and 4 PM.
	+ **Evening**: Between 5 PM and 8 PM.
* **Date of Interest** (`date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date you are inquiring about.
* **Estimated Attendance** (`number`, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated number of people attending.
* **Require follow up?** (`select_one`, required): Check this field if you would like to be contacted again after your inquiry.
