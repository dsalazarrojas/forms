# Fellow Program Alumni Contact Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fellow Program Alumni Contact Update Form is used to update the contact information of a fellow program alumnus. The form will ask for details about the alumnus, their organization, role, department, job title, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your fellow program name
2. Enter your name as it appears on official documents
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your phone number (optional)
5. Enter your organization name
6. Select your current role
7. Enter your department (if applicable)
8. Enter your job title (if applicable)
9. Enter your address
10. Enter your address 2 (if applicable)
11. Enter your city
12. Enter your state
13. Enter your country
14. Enter your graduation month and year

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fellow Program** (`fellow_program`): Enter the name of the fellow program you participated in.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter the name of your current or previous organization.
* **Role** (`role`): Select your current role in your organization. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your current address.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`): Enter any additional address information (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter your country.
* **Graduation Month** (`graduation_month`): Enter the month you graduated.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Enter the date your information was last updated (if applicable).
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Enter the person who last updated your information (if applicable).
* **Updated At 2** (`updated_at_2`): Enter the date your information was last updated (if applicable).
* **Updated By 2** (`updated_by_2`): Enter the person who last updated your information (if applicable).
* **Updated At 3** (`updated_at_3`): Enter the date your information was last updated (if applicable).
* **Updated By 3** (`updated_by_3`): Enter the person who last updated your information (if applicable).
* **Updated At 4** (`updated_at_4`): Enter the date your information was last updated (if applicable).
* **Updated By 4** (`updated_by_4`): Enter the person who last updated your information (if applicable).

Note: The `Updated At` and `Updated By` fields are for internal use only and are not required for submission.
