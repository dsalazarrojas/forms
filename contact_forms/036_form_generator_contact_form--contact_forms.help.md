# Form Generator Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form Generator Contact Form is used to collect user contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the `Name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3.  Select one or more of the contact options that best fit your contact needs.
4.  Type in any additional information or message in the `Message` field.
5.  Click the `Submit` button to send your contact information to the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text, required: false)**: Please fill in your name as you would like it to appear on the contact form response.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your valid email address to receive the form response.
*   **Contact (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select one or more of the contact options. These options include:
  • Option 1 
  • Option 2 
  • Option 3
*   **Message (text, required: false)**: Type in any additional information or message you would like to send along with your contact information.
*   **Submit (text, required: false)**: Click this button to send your contact information to the form administrator. 

## Tips
- Please fill in as much information as possible for the form administrator to effectively process your contact information.
- The form administrator will use your contact information to respond to your query.
