# Embedded Opt In Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for embedded opt-in purposes. The collected data will be used to send promotional emails to the users who opt in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **name** in the first field. This is a text field where you can input your name.
2. Enter your **email** address in the second field. This is a required field to receive promotional emails.
3. Select your **opt-in preference** in the third field. You can choose between "Yes" or "No" to opt in or out of promotional emails.
4. If you have a **phone number**, you can input it in the fourth field. This field is optional.
5. If you have any additional **notes**, you can input them in the last field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, Optional): A field to input your name.
* **email** (Email, Required): A field to input your email address, which is required to receive promotional emails.
* **opt in for promotional emails** (Select One, Optional): A field to choose whether you want to opt in or out of promotional emails.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): A field to input your phone number, which is not required.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): A field to input any additional notes you may have. This field is not required.
