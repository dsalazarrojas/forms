# emergency_contact_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "emergency_contact_sheet" form is designed to collect contact information for staff and their emergency contacts. This form helps us to quickly and efficiently gather essential details in case of an emergency. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we have the right contact information.

## How to Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
- Select the correct options for the "Relationship" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields.
- Enter the staff member's emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, email, address, and school.
- Enter your own contact information, including your name, phone number, email, address, and school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or passport. 
* **Relationship**: Select how you are related to the staff member (Father, Mother, Other).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Select your state (e.g., AL, AK, AZ, etc.).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school you attend or are affiliated with.
* **Role**: Enter your role (e.g., student, parent, staff, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select how you are related to the emergency contact (Father, Mother, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the emergency contact's physical address.
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact State**: Select the state of the emergency contact's address (e.g., AL, AK, AZ, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the school the emergency contact is affiliated with.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select how the emergency contact is related to the emergency contact (e.g., Father, Mother, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact (optional).
* **Staff Note**: Enter any additional information about yourself (optional).
* **Staff Name**: Enter your name as a staff member.
* **Staff Number**: Enter your staff ID number.
* **Staff Email**: Enter your email address as a staff member.

Note that this form has many fields, so please make sure to fill out every required field. If you need any assistance or clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
