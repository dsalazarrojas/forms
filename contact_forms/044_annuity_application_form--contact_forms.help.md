# Annuity Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about you and your financial situation to determine which annuity option is best suited for you. Please provide accurate and honest answers to ensure that we can assess your eligibility and provide the right guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields. If you're unsure about a particular question, you can ask for clarification or guidance.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to all questions. This will help us to assess your eligibility and provide the right guidance.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): This is the name you are applying for, as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, text, required): This is your social security number, which is used to identify you in government records.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Choose your gender from the options provided.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required): Choose your marital status from the options provided.
* **Spouse Name** (`spouse_name`, text, optional): If married, please enter your spouse's name.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, including house number and name of the street.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a phone number where we can contact you.
* **Current Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your current job title or occupation.
* **Type of Annuity Requested** (`annuity_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of annuity you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Initial Premium Amount** (`premium_amount`, number, required): Enter the lump sum you wish to invest in the annuity.
* **Source of Premium Funds** (`premium_source`, select_one, required): Choose where the premium funds come from.
* **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, select_one, required): Choose your investment objective from the options provided.
* **Desired Income Start Date** (`income_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to begin receiving payments.
* **Preferred Payment Option** (`payment_option`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment option from the options provided.
* **Primary Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity that will receive benefits if you pass away.
* **Beneficiary Relationship** (`beneficiary_relationship`, text, optional): If applicable, describe your relationship with the beneficiary.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current health status.
* **Do you use tobacco products** (`tobacco_use`, select_one, required): Choose yes, no, or if you're a former user.
* **Are you working with a financial advisor** (`financial_advisor`, select_one, optional): Choose yes, no, or unsure.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your financial advisor.
