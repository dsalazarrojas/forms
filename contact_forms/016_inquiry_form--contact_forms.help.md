# Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from end-users for internal purposes, such as gathering feedback, questions, or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Form Name" field to describe the purpose of your inquiry.
2. Write a brief description of your inquiry in the "Form Description" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure that we can contact you with a response.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
5. Provide your address in the "Address" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your name in the "Name" field, so we can address you personally.
7. Describe the subject of your inquiry in the "Subject" field.
8. Finally, type a message to provide more details about your inquiry in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to give a brief name to your inquiry. You can provide any relevant information about the purpose of your request.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, `note`, required: false): This is an opportunity to give a more detailed description of your inquiry.
* **Email** (`form_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address so that we can contact you with a response or further information.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it here so that we can contact you via phone if needed.
* **Address** (`form_address`, `text`, required: false): If you have a specific address, enter it here so that we can understand your location better.
* **Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name so that we can address you personally in our response.
* **Subject** (`form_subject`, `text`, required: false): Describe the main topic or theme of your inquiry.
* **Message** (`form_message`, `text`, required: false): This is the main content area where you can provide more details about your inquiry.
