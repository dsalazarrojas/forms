# Consultancy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultancy Registration Form is designed to collect information from clients who are interested in seeking consultancy services from our organization. It helps us to understand their needs and preferences, and enables us to provide better services to them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in their respective sections.
2. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
3. If you have already registered with us in the past, please do not fill out this form again.
4. You can submit the form by clicking on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Your first name is the first name you are known by. Please enter your first name in the format "John" or "Jane".
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Your last name is the last name you are known by. Please enter your last name in the format "Smith" or "Doe".
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, *): Your primary email address that we will use to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, *): Your best contact phone number that we can use to reach you.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, ): If you are a company, please enter your company name. If you are an individual, you can leave this field blank.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, ): Your position or role within your organization.
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, ): Your mailing address.
- **City** (`city`, text, ): The city of your residence.
- **State or Province** (`state`, text, ): The state or province of your residence.
- **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, ): The postal code of your residence.
- **Country** (`country`, text, ): The country of your residence.
- **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of consultancy services you are interested in. Options include:
	+ General Inquiry
	+ Management Consulting
	+ Financial Consulting
	+ IT Consulting
	+ HR Consulting
	+ Marketing Consulting
	+ Strategy Consulting
	+ Other
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, *): Select how you would like to be contacted. Options include:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Either
- **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, ): Select when you are available to be contacted. Options include:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Anytime
- **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, *): Select when you need the consultancy services. Options include:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 Month
	+ 1-3 Months
	+ 3-6 Months
	+ 6+ Months
	+ Just Exploring
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, ): Select your estimated budget for the consultancy services. Options include:
	+ Under $5,000
	+ $5,000 - $10,000
	+ $10,000 - $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $50,000
	+ $50,000 - $100,000
	+ Over $100,000
	+ Not Sure
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, *): Describe your project or needs in brief.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, ): Select how you heard about us. Options include:
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Advertisement
	+ Conference or Event
	+ Search Engine
	+ Other
- **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, ): If you were referred by someone, please enter their name or contact information.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, ): If you have any other information you would like to share with us, please enter it here.
- **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, ): Select if you would like to receive updates and insights from us. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, *): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
