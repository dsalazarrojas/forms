# Clubhouse Question Submit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clubhouse Question Submit Form is designed for members to submit questions to the community. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way for submitters to provide context and details about their questions, making it easier for moderators and intended speakers or panelists to understand and address the questions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and email address on the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields to ensure that you can be reached for follow-up communications.
2.  Enter your Clubhouse username on the "Clubhouse Username" field if you wish to be associated with your question.
3.  Provide the date you submitted the question and the name of the room where the question is related to.
4.  Choose the category of the question from the "Type of question" field to help the community understand the nature of your inquiry.
5.  Enter the question itself in the "What would you like to ask" field.
6.  If necessary, provide additional context about the question.
7.  Decide how urgently you need an answer and if you want your question to be shared publicly.
8.  Indicate if you want to receive follow-up responses or if you want the answer to be sent to you via email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name (1, text, required)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the submitter of the question.
*   **Email Address (2, email, required)**: Enter a valid email address that moderators can use to contact you.
*   **Clubhouse Username (3, text, required)**: Enter your Clubhouse username if you wish to be associated with your question.
*   **Date of Question Submission (4, date, required)**: Select the date you are submitting the question.
*   **Room Name (5, text, required)**: Enter the name of the room where the question is related to.
*   **Session Date (6, date, required)**: Select the date of the session the question is related to.
*   **Session Time (7, time, required)**: Select the time of the session the question is related to.
*   **Type of Question (8, select_one, required)**: Choose the category of the question from the provided options.
*   **Your Question (9, text, required)**: Enter the question itself.
*   **Additional Context (10, text, required)**: If necessary, provide background information about the question.
*   **Question Priority (11, select_one, required)**: Choose the urgency level of the question.
*   **Intended Speaker or Panelist (12, text, required)**: Enter the name of the intended speaker or panelist.
*   **Follow-up Question (13, select_one, required)**: Indicate if this is a follow-up question.
*   **Share Question Publicly in Room (14, select_one, required)**: Decide if you want your question to be shared publicly in the room.
*   **Want Follow-Up Response (15, select_one, required)**: Indicate if you want follow-up responses or if you want the answer to be sent to you via email.
