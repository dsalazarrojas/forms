# Prospect Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential prospects for sales or business opportunities. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details to help us understand the prospect's background, needs, and preferences, which will aid in creating a suitable proposal or follow-up strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter the prospect's first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter the prospect's last name.
3. **Email**: Enter the prospect's email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter the prospect's phone number.
5. **Prospect Title**: Enter the title of the prospect (e.g., CEO, Manager, etc.).
6. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company the prospect is representing.
7. **Job Title**: Enter the prospect's job title.
8. **Job Function**: Enter the prospect's job function or role.
9. **Company URL**: Enter the URL of the prospect's company website.
10. **Location**: Enter the location of the prospect's company or office.
11. **Industry**: Enter the industry the prospect's company operates in.
12. **About the Company**: Provide a brief description of the prospect's company (optional).
13. **Why we should follow up with you**: Enter a short explanation of why we should follow up with the prospect (optional).
14. **Prospect Description**: Provide a brief description of the prospect (optional).
15. **Job Level**: Select the job level that best describes the prospect's position.
16. **Preferred method of contact**: Choose how you would prefer to be contacted (Email or Phone).
17. **Best time of day to contact**: Choose a time of day when you are most likely to be available.
18. **Best day of week to contact**: Choose a day of the week when you are most likely to be available.
19. **Best time of day to contact in range**: Choose a time range when you are most likely to be available.
20. **Job opening status**: Select the status of the job opening (Open, Not Specified).
21. **Job opening type**: Select the type of job opening (Temporary, Other).
22. **Job opening urgency**: Select the urgency level of the job opening (High, Medium, Low, Not Specified).
23. **Job opening frequency**: Select how frequently the job opening occurs (Ongoing, As needed, Other).
24. **How did you find us**: Select how you found our organization.
25. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about the prospect.
