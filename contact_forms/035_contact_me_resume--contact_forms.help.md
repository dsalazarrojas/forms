# Contact Me Resume - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to share their resumes and request follow-up on their applications. It's a convenient way for them to provide their contact information and relevant details about their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the required fields, such as your full name, email address, and the position you are applying for.
2. Optionally, you can add your phone number, LinkedIn profile URL, and any additional information you think would be relevant to your application.
3. If you have a resume that's too large to attach, you can provide a link to it or a note for the contact person to follow up.
4. You can also provide a brief cover letter summary and any availability for an interview.
5. Finally, add any additional information you think would be helpful for the contact person to review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Resume Contact --**: This is the header of the form, providing a clear and concise label for the form's purpose.
* **Full Name**: This field is a required field where you need to enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact and correspondence related to the application.
* **Email Address**: This field is required to ensure that the contact person can reach you efficiently.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional, but it's a good idea to include your best phone number for the contact person to contact you.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: If you have a professional profile on LinkedIn, you can share it with the contact person to review your work experience and skills.
* **Position Applied For**: Enter the job title you are applying for, as this will help the contact person to prioritize and review your application.
* **Attach Resume via email or upload link**: If your resume is too large to attach, you can provide a note with a link to it or instructions for the contact person to follow up.
* **Cover Letter Summary**: This field is optional, but a brief summary of your qualifications and experience can be helpful for the contact person to review.
* **Availability for Interview**: If you are available for an interview, enter the dates or times you are available, and the contact person will try to schedule it accordingly.
* **Additional Information**: This field is optional, and you can add any other details that you think are relevant to your application and will help the contact person to review your qualifications.
