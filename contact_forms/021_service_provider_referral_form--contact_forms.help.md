# service_provider_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Provider Referral Form is used to gather information from a referrer and the service provider when referring a new client or partner to a service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the referrer's First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter the Service Provider's Name, Description, and Business Name.
3. Provide the Business Address, Phone, and Email of the service provider.
4. Identify the Contact Person for the service provider.
5. Select the Referral Type and assign the referral to a specific user (e.g., chatjimmy).
6. Add any Notes about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Description** (`service_provider_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the service provider.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business associated with the service provider.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the business associated with the service provider.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business associated with the service provider.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the business associated with the service provider.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person associated with the service provider.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of referral (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the referral.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required): Select the user to whom the referral will be assigned (e.g., chatjimmy).
