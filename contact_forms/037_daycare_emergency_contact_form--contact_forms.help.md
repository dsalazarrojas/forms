# Daycare Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect emergency contact information from parents or guardians of children enrolled in the daycare program. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the child's safety and well-being in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the name of the daycare provider (such as the center or school).
*   Provide the name and title of the contact person.
*   Select the relationship of the contact person to the child (e.g., Emergency Contact, Parent/Guardian, Sibling, etc.).
*   Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   Enter the contact person's email address.
*   Enter the contact person's address.
*   Provide any additional contact information (e.g., work hours, availability).
*   Select the emergency contacts you wish to list for the child (e.g., parent, grandparent, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Daycare Provider** (`daycare_provider`, `text`, required): This field should contain the name of the daycare center or school the child is currently enrolled in.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): This field should contain the name of the contact person, ideally a parent or guardian of the child.
*   **Relationship** (`relationship`, `select_one`, optional): This field allows you to specify the relationship of the contact person to the child. Select from the provided options to indicate their role.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact person's email address for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact person's address for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Additional Contact Info** (`additional_contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional contact information for the contact person, such as their work hours or availability.
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any emergency contacts you wish to list for the child, such as other parents, grandparents, or friends.
    > If you have any other questions, feel free to reach out to us at [contact email or phone number].
