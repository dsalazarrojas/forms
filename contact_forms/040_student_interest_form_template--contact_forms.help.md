# Student Interest Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Interest Form Template is a survey form designed to capture students' interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all fields you think best apply to you. You can choose from multiple options in fields "Student Field of Study", and "Student Interests".
2. Type or select your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field if you have one.
3. If you are in a certain grade level, select the correct grade level from the "Student Grade Level" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Field of Study
* **Student Field of Study** (`student_field_of_study`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all fields that best describe your field of study. Choose from "Computer Science", "Art", "Math", "Music", "Science", or "Other".

### Student Interests
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all interests that best describe your interests. Choose from "Art", "Dance", "Music", "Science", "Sports", or "Theater".

### Teacher Name
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a teacher, type or select their name here. If you don't have a teacher, leave this field blank.

### Student Grade Level
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current grade level from the options 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. If you are unsure, please select the grade level you are currently in.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when selecting your field of study and interests.
* If you are unsure about a field, choose the option that best fits your current situation.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you want to express multiple interests.
