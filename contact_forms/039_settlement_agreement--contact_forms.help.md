# Settlement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to a settlement agreement in a legal context. It is intended for use by lawyers, paralegals, or other legal professionals to document settlement agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "Settlement Agreement" form, which consists of 13 fields divided into various sections.
2. Start by providing the Plaintiff Name and Defendant Name in the designated text fields.
3. In the "reasons for settlement" field, please provide a clear and concise reason for the settlement.
4. If required, disclose any relevant income and assets in the corresponding fields.
5. Specify the terms of the Settlement Property Agreement if necessary.
6. Describe the Disposition of Dispute.
7. Enter the Effective Date of the settlement.
8. Sign and date the agreement with both the Plaintiff Signature and Defendant Signature.
9. Verify the form by entering the Date Signed and Time Signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plaintiff Name** (`settlement_agreement_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Name** (`defendant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the defendant.
* **Reasons for Settlement** (`reasons_for_settlement`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the settlement.
* **Disclosures of Income** (`disclosures_income`, text, required): Disclose any relevant income related to the settlement.
* **Disclosures of Assets** (`disclosures_assets`, text, required): Disclose any relevant assets related to the settlement.
* **Settlement Property Agreement** (`settlement_property_agreement`, text, required): Specify the terms of the Settlement Property Agreement if necessary.
* **Disposition of Dispute** (`disposition_dispute`, text, required): Describe the Disposition of Dispute.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the effective date of the settlement.
* **Signatures** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the agreement with both the Plaintiff Signature and Defendant Signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Verify the date signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Verify the time signed.
* **Plaintiff Signature** (`plaintiff_signature`, text, required): This field is a redundant field. Please sign in the "Signatures" field.
* **Defendant Signature** (`defendant_signature`, text, required): This field is a redundant field. Please sign in the "Signatures" field. 

# Tips
- Always provide clear and concise answers.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy.
- Verify that all required fields are filled out to prevent form submission issues.
