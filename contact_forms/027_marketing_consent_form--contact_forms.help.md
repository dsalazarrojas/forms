# Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect marketing consent from customers or potential customers to receive marketing materials such as emails, phone calls, and other forms of contact. It is essential to fill out this form to sign up for marketing materials and to coordinate communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or other).
3. Indicate your contact frequency (never, occasionally, frequently).
4. Provide your marketing materials preferences.
5. Sign the consent section to confirm your agreement.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is the first name of the contact person.
* **Email**: This is the email address of the contact person. Please provide a valid email address to receive marketing materials.
* **Company**: This is the company name of the contact person or organization.
* **Role**: This is the role or position of the contact person (e.g., CEO, CMO, customer service).
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person.
* **Fax**: This is the fax number of the contact person.
* **Email Type**: This is the type of email you have (personal, work, or other).
* **Email Frequency**: This is how often you want to receive marketing materials (never, occasionally, frequently).
* **Phone Type**: This is the type of phone you have (mobile, landline, or other).
* **Phone Frequency**: This is how often you want to receive phone calls (never, occasionally, frequently).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This is the preferred way you want to be contacted (email, phone, or other).
* **Contact Methods**: This is a list of contact methods you prefer (phone, email, in-person).
* **Marketing Materials**: This is where you can provide additional information about your marketing materials preferences.
* **Consent**: Please sign below to confirm your agreement to receive marketing materials.
* **Time Zone**: This field is not essential, but it's useful if you're scheduling calls or meetings across different time zones.
* **Time of Day**: This field is not essential, but it's useful if you're scheduling calls or meetings across different time zones.
* **Time Zone Offset**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
* **Time of Year**: This is the time of year you're most active (January, February, March).
* **Year**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
* **Month**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
* **Month End Day**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
* **Month Day**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
* **Month Day of Week**: This field is not essential and can be skipped.
