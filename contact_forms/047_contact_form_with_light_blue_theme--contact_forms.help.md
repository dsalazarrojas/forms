# Contact Form With Light Blue Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The contact form is used to gather contact information and inquiry details from users. It is meant for general inquiries and not for emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Form With Light Blue Theme" to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields with your contact information.
3. In the "Subject" field, provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
4. In the "Your Detailed Message" field, provide more information about your inquiry.
5. Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown list.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for contact.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **Subject**: Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Detailed Message**: Provide more information about your inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like us to contact you back (Email or Phone Call).
* **Date of Submission**: The date and time you are submitting this form will be automatically filled in as the current date and time.
