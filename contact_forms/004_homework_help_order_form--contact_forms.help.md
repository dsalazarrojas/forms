# Homework Help Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students order homework assistance from designated help resources. It gathers essential details to assign the most suitable helper and resource for their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request for homework help, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student name** (required) to indicate who is making the request.
2. Enter your **Contact number** (required) so we can reach out to you for further assistance.
3. Provide your **Email** (optional) for easy communication.
4. Describe your **Description** of the homework task, including any relevant details or attachments.
5. Choose the **Assigned helper** from the available options (required).
6. Select the **Assigned tool** from the available options (required).
7. Set the **Due date** (required) for the homework submission.
8. Optionally, select the **Due time** for the homework submission.
9. Indicate if your homework has already been **Submitted** (required).
10. Enter your **Comments** (optional) regarding the submission.
11. Choose your **Student class** (required) from the list.
12. Select the **Subject** of your homework (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name (Required)**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Contact number (Required)**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further clarification or updates.
* **Email (Optional)**: Enter your email address for easy communication with our team.
* **Description (Required)**: Describe your homework task in detail, including any attachments or supporting materials.
* **Assigned helper (Required)**: Choose the helper who will assist you with your homework. Please select from the available options.
* **Assigned tool (Required)**: Select the tool your helper will use to assist you with your homework. Please select from the available options.
* **Due date (Required)**: Set the date by which you expect to submit your homework.
* **Due time (Optional)**: If applicable, select the time by which you expect to submit your homework.
* **Submitted (Required)**: Indicate if your homework has already been submitted.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any comments or feedback you have about your submission.
* **Student class (Required)**: Select your class from the list to help us tailor our assistance to your needs.
* **Subject (Required)**: Choose the subject of your homework from the list to ensure accurate assistance.
*   **Tips**: Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact our support team.
