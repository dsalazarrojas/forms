# lets_crush_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lets_crush_2021" form is designed to collect personal information from users for contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill out your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type a message in the "Message" field.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): This field is used to collect a message for us to respond to.
