# Scentsy Party Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scentsy Party Form is a contact form designed to collect information about a Scentsy party.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Scentsy Party Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Party Name** in the "Party Name" field. This is a required field that should be filled out with the name of the party you are planning.
2. Select the **Party Date** from the calendar in the "Party Date" field. This is a required field that should be filled out with the date of the party you are planning.
3. Enter the **Party Time** in the "Party Time" field. This is a required field that should be filled out with the time of the party you are planning.
4. Enter the number of **Guests** attending the party in the "Guests" field. This is a required field that will help you determine the number of invitations to print.
5. Enter the **Contact Name** in the "Contact Name" field. This is the person who will be in charge of the party and should be contacted for more information.
6. Enter the **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field. This is the phone number of the person who will be contacted for more information about the party.
7. Enter the **Contact Email** in the "Contact Email" field. This is the email address of the person who will be contacted for more information about the party.
8. The **Note** field is optional and can be filled out for any additional notes or comments about the party.
9. You can optionally enter the **Party Location** in the "Party Location" field. If left blank, the default location will be used.
10. Finally, select the **Assigned Tool** from the dropdown list. This is the tool that will be assigned to the party host or coordinator.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Name** (`party_name`, text, required):
  Enter the name of the party you are organizing.
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, date, required):
  Select the date of the party from the calendar.
* **Party Time** (`party_time`, time, required):
  Enter the time of the party in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Guests** (`guests`, text, required):
  Enter the number of guests attending the party.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required):
  Enter the name of the person in charge of the party.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required):
  Enter the phone number of the person in charge of the party.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required):
  Enter the email address of the person in charge of the party.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional):
  Enter any additional notes or comments about the party.
* **Party Location** (`party_location`, text, optional):
  Enter the location of the party, if different from the default.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required):
  Select the tool that will be assigned to the party host or coordinator from the dropdown list.
  Options: Chatjimmy, Other.
