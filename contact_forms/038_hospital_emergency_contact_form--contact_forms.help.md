# Hospital Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather contact information in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name by which you would like to be identified in case of an emergency.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is the name by which you would like to be identified in case of an emergency.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is your contact email address, which will be used to contact you in case of an emergency.
4. Select your preferred **Contact Method** from the dropdown menu. This can be **Phone**, **Email**, or **In-Person**.
5. If you have selected **Phone** as your contact method, enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. This is your address, which will be used to locate you in case of an emergency.
7. If you have an **Emergency Contact**, enter their **Name** in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
8. If you have an **Emergency Contact**, enter their **Phone** number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
9. Describe your **Relationship** with the emergency contact in the "Relationship" field.
10. Enter the **Address** of your **Emergency Contact** in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
11. Enter your **Emergency Contact's Occupation** in the "Emergency Contact Occupation" field.
12. Enter a **Message** describing your situation or needs in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Your first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Your last name.
* **Email (3)**: Your contact email address.
* **Contact Method (4)**: Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, In-Person).
* **Phone (5)**: If you selected Phone as your contact method, enter your phone number here.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your address, which will be used to locate you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name (7)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (8)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Relationship (9)**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address (10)**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Occupation (11)**: Enter your emergency contact's occupation.
* **Message (12)**: Enter a brief message describing your situation or needs.
