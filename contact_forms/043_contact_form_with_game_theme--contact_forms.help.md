# Contact Form With Game Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for players to contact the game developers with inquiries, feedback, or general comments about the games. Fill out the form to provide your details and the specific reason for your contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2.  Select your preferred gaming platform from the options.
3.  Choose the category for your inquiry (e.g., Bug Report, Feature Suggestion, etc.).
4.  Select the game title that your inquiry is about.
5.  Write a detailed message about your inquiry or feedback.
6.  Choose the level of urgency you need for a response.
7.  Submit the form to send your message to the game developers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Gamertag/Name** (`player_gamertag`, `text`, required): This is the name that other players know you by in-game. It's how the game developers will address you.
* **Player Email Address** (`player_email_address`, `email`, required): This is your primary email address for contact and communication. Ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Preferred Gaming Platform** (`preferred_gaming_platform`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more platforms you prefer to play on. This information helps the game developers understand your preferences.
* **Inquiry Category** (`inquiry_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary reason for your contact (e.g., Bug Report, Feature Suggestion, etc.). This helps the game developers quickly identify and prioritize your inquiry.
* **Game Title of Interest** (`game_title_of_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the game title that your inquiry is about. This helps the game developers understand the context of your message.
* **Your Detailed Message** (`detailed_game_message`, `text`, required): Write a clear and detailed message about your inquiry or feedback. Be concise and provide as much information as possible.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically filled out with the current date. However, you can manually change it if needed.
* **Level of Urgency** (`level_of_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how quickly you need a response from the game developers. This helps them prioritize your inquiry based on your needs.
