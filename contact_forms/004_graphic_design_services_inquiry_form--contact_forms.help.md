# Graphic Design Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit inquiries for graphic design services, including requests for information on specific services, budgets, timelines, and contact details. This form will help our team understand your needs and provide accurate quotes or consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief description of your request in the `Request Details` field.
2. If applicable, share your estimated budget for the project.
3. Choose one or more from the provided timelines for your project's duration.
4. If you have any contact information, please provide it in the `Contact Info` field.
5. Let us know how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
6. Finally, leave a message with any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your project or request, including any relevant details or context that will help us better understand your needs.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): If you have an estimated budget for the project, please provide it so we can better understand your expectations.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more from the provided options for your project's expected duration. 
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): If you have any contact information (e.g., phone number, email, or physical address), please provide it so we can get in touch with you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select one, required: false): Let us know how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): If you have any additional information or questions, please leave a brief message in this field.
