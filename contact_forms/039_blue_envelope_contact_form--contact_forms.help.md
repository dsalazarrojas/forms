# Blue Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Blue Envelope Contact Form is a contact form for sending messages, business proposals, support requests, feedback, and partnership inquiries to the Blue Envelope team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Get In Touch" button to open the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and correctly.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`sender_name`, **required**): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`sender_email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`sender_phone`, **optional**): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, **optional**): If you represent a company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Subject** (`subject`, **required**): Briefly describe the purpose of your message or inquiry.
* **Message Type** (`message_type`, **required**): Select the type of message you are sending (e.g., General inquiry, Business proposal, Support request, Feedback, Partnership opportunity, Other).
* **Message** (`message_body`, **required**): Type your message or describe your inquiry in detail.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, **optional**): Indicate the urgency level of your message (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Urgent).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, **required**): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Video call).
* **Best Time To Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, **optional**): Indicate the best time to contact you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Flexible).
* **Attachments** (`attachments_note`, **optional**): Indicate if you have attachments to send with your message.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, **optional**): If applicable, please provide an estimated budget range for your message or proposal.
* **Submission Date** (`contact_date`, **required**): Select the date you are submitting your message.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form completely and correctly before submitting it.
* If you have attachments, please ensure they are relevant to your message.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
