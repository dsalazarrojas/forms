# Electric Vehicle Charging Station Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Vehicle Charging Station Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from users regarding electric vehicle charging stations. This form is intended for users who have questions or need assistance related to charging stations, such as inquiries about station availability, charging options, or other related inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of inquiry you have: Choose from the options provided in the "Inquiry Type" field.
2. Enter your name: Type your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address: Type your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your contact number: Type your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Describe your location: Type your location in the "Location" field.
6. Share your inquiry message: Type your message in the "Inquiry Message" field.
7. Provide vehicle information: Select relevant vehicle information from the options in the "Vehicle Info" field.
8. Specify your preferred contact time: Select your preferred contact time from the options in the "Preferred Contact Time" field.
9. Provide additional context (optional): Type any additional information you would like to share about your inquiry in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiriy Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required: false): 
  - Select the type of inquiry you have (e.g., Electricity, Hybrid, Other). This field helps us understand the nature of your inquiry.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): 
  - Type your name as you would like to be referred to. This field is optional but helps us personalize our response to your inquiry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
  - Enter your valid email address so we can contact you about your inquiry.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): 
  - Type your contact number so we can reach out to you about your inquiry.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): 
  - Provide your location so we can better understand your context.
* **Inquiry Message** (`inquiry_message`, note, required: false): 
  - Share your inquiry message in as much detail as you can. This field is where you can provide any additional information about your inquiry.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  - Select from the options provided to describe your vehicle (e.g., Type, Make, Model, Year, Color).
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, time, required: false): 
  - Specify your preferred contact time to ensure we reach out to you at a suitable time.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): 
  - If you have any specific context or information related to your inquiry, you can provide it here.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed the FAQs and understood the purpose of this form before submitting an inquiry.
* Be as descriptive as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can provide the best possible assistance.
* If you need assistance with anything, don't hesitate to contact us through other means.
