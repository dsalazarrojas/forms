# Contact Form 18 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Contact Form 18" is a simple contact form designed to collect user information and feedback. This form is meant to be used for general inquiries, support requests, and other non-urgent matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information and details.
2. Select a suitable subject for your message from the provided options.
3. Enter your message in the text area.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (optional).
5. If you have a phone number, provide it for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for us to get back to you.
* **Message Subject** (`message_subject`, select_one, required): Choose a subject from the options provided to help us better understand the purpose of your message.
* **Your Message** (`message_content`, text, required): Type out your message in the text area. This is where you can provide more details about your inquiry or request.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number and prefer to be contacted by phone, enter it here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field will automatically be populated with today's date. You don't need to fill it out manually.
* **Note:** The "Submission Date" field is automatically populated with the current date when the form is submitted.
