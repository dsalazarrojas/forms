# Simple Email Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to send a simple email message to a recipient. It collects the user's name and email address, and a brief message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Type your message in the "Message" field.
5. Click the "Send" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (user_input_1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (user_input_2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (user_input_3, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where the message will be sent.
* **Message** (user_input_4, text, required: false): Type your message to be sent to the recipient. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field to ensure the message is delivered correctly.
* Keep your message brief and clear, as it will be sent as is to the recipient.
