# Contact Form With Rounded Theme Blue Button - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contact Form With Rounded Theme Blue Button is designed for users to send a contact inquiry to your organization. This form is used to collect contact information and details about the user's service interest, allowing you to provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the 'Contact Us' button at the top of the page.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*), in each section. These fields are:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Service of Interest
	* Message
	* Date
3. If applicable, you may fill out other fields for additional information:
	+ Phone (if you have a phone number to share)
	+ Website URL (if you have a website)
4. Select your inquiry priority:
	+ Low
	+ Normal
	+ High
5. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the 'Submit' button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us** (Note): This field is a heading that introduces the contact form. Please fill out the fields below for your inquiry.
* **Full Name** (text, *): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, *): Please enter your business email address. Make sure it matches your official email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Website URL** (text, optional): If you have a personal or business website, enter the URL here.
* **Service of Interest** (select_one, *): Select one or more services you are interested in.
	+ Web Design
	+ SEO
	+ Marketing
	+ Consulting
* **Message** (text, *): Please write a brief message describing your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Priority** (select_one, optional): Select the priority of your inquiry.
	+ Low
	+ Normal
	+ High
* **Date** (date, *): Please enter the date for your inquiry.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Please enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Website URL** (text, optional): Please enter your website URL for additional contact information.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required fields.
