# Dental Checkup Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients about their dental checkup inquiries, including appointment requests, contact methods, and insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the "Patient Inquiry" page.
2.  In the "Patient Inquiry" page, enter any information about the patient's inquiry.
3.  Select the "Appointment Request" page.
4.  Choose the preferred appointment time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
5.  Select the "Contact Method" page.
6.  Choose the desired contact methods (Phone, Email, or Chat).
7.  Select the "Email and Phone" page.
8.  Enter the patient's email address.
9.  Enter the patient's phone number.
10. Select the "Insurance and Follow-up" page.
11.  Indicate whether the patient has dental insurance or not.
12.  If applicable, enter the patient's insurance provider information.
13.  Select whether the patient wants follow-up information or not.
14.  Select the "Appointment Details" page.
15.  Choose the preferred appointment date and time.
16.  Select the "Additional Information" page.
17.  Enter any additional information about the patient or the appointment.
18.  Submit the form to complete the patient's inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Inquiry (Page 1)** (`patient_inquiry`, `text`, not required): Enter any information about the patient's inquiry.
*   **Appointment Request (Page 2)** (`appointment_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred appointment time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
*   **Contact Method (Page 3)** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the desired contact methods (Phone, Email, or Chat).
*   **Email (Page 4)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Phone (Page 4)** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Note (Page 5)** (`note`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional information about the patient.
*   **Patient Information (Page 5)** (`patient_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any information about the patient.
*   **Dental Insurance (Page 5)** (`dental_insurance`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether the patient has dental insurance or not.
*   **Insurance Provider (Page 5)** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the patient's insurance provider information.
*   **Follow-up (Page 5)** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select whether the patient wants follow-up information or not.
*   **Appointment Date (Page 6)** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Choose the preferred appointment date.
*   **Appointment Time (Page 6)** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required): Choose the preferred appointment time.
*   **Next Visit (Page 6)** (`next_visit`, `date`, not required): Choose the preferred next visit date.
*   **Dentist (Page 7)** (`dentist`, `text`, not required): Enter any information about the dentist.
*   **Preferred Contact Method (Page 7)** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Chat).
*   **Dental Insurance (Page 7)** (`dental_insurance`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate whether the patient has dental insurance or not.
*   **Phone (Page 7)** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter any phone number information.
*   **Follow-up (Page 7)** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select whether the patient wants follow-up information or not.
