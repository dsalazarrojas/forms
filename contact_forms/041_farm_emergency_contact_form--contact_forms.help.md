# Farm Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Emergency Contact Form is used to gather contact information from farm owners or operators in the event of an emergency. This form helps emergency responders quickly and accurately contact relevant individuals to provide assistance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the emergency contact information for the farm. Please check the box next to "Yes" if you have any emergency contacts.
2.  Provide the name of the contact person.
3.  Enter the contact person's phone number.
4.  Enter the contact person's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Information**: (1, select_multiple, required)
	+ This field allows you to confirm if you have any emergency contacts.
* **Contact Name** (2, text, optional): 
	+ Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (3, text, optional): 
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (4, email, optional): 
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* If you have any emergency contacts, please check the box next to "Emergency Contact Information" to confirm.
* If you need help with this form, or have any questions, please contact your farm's administration or management team.
