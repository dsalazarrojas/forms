# Player Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Player Emergency Contact Form is used to provide critical contact information in case of an emergency. Please fill out this form to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information to contact you or your emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form on one page at a time.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding text fields as prompted.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Relationship" field.
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information (<player_info>, text, optional)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Information (<contact_info>, text, optional)**: Enter the contact information for the person who will be handling your emergency contact.
* **Contact Relationships (<contact_relationships>, text, optional)**: Select "Yes" if you have an established relationship (e.g. family member, friend) with your contact.
* **Emergency Contact (<emergency_contact>, text, optional)**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Name (<emergency_name>, text, optional)**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Phone (<emergency_phone>, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Address (<emergency_address>, text, optional)**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship (<emergency_relationships>, select_one, optional)**: Select "Yes" if your emergency contact is someone you trust to handle your emergency needs.
* **Notes (<emergency_notes>, text, optional)**: Enter any additional information or notes about your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our staff for assistance.
* Please only fill out this form once.
