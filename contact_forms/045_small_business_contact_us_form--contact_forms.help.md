# small_business_contact_us_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple contact form designed for users to contact a small business. It allows users to provide basic contact information and a message, which can be used to address any questions, inquiries, or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name (if applicable).
3. Enter your Company Name (if applicable).
4. Provide your Phone Number (if applicable).
5. Enter your Email Address.
6. Select the reason for your inquiry by choosing one of the options for the "Subject" field.
7. Write your message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name (if you want to provide it).
* **Company Name** (`text`, required: false): If you are contacting us on behalf of your company, enter your company name here.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Subject** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for your inquiry from the options provided:
	+ Company Inquiry
	+ Other
* **Message** (`note`, required: false): Write your message in this field, explaining your inquiry or question.
