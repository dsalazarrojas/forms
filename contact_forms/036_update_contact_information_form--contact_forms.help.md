# Update Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general contact form used for users to update their information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email (if applicable).
4. Enter your Phone (if applicable).
5. Enter your Address.
6. Enter your City.
7. Enter your Region.
8. Enter your Postal Code.
9. If you have any comments, enter them here (optional).
10. If you have an Assigned Tool, enter the name of the tool here (optional).
11. Enter who submitted this form (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your last name.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): This is the field where you enter your email address, following the standard email format (e.g. example@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your phone number (e.g. 123-456-7890).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your city.
* **Region** (`region`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your region.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your postal code (e.g. A1A 1A1).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): This is the field where you enter any additional comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This is the field where you enter the name of a tool you may be using.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): This is the field where you enter who submitted this form.
