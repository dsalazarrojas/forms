# Color Street Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a product at Color Street.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Requester name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
3. Enter the name or code of the product you want to order in the "Product requested" field.
4. Specify the quantity of the product you want to order in the "Quantity requested" field.
5. Select a payment method from the list (Credit card, Bank transfer, or PayPal).
6. Fill in your billing address (if different from shipping) in the "Billing address" field.
7. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping address" field (if different from billing).
8. Enter any special requests or customizations in the "Special requests" field (if applicable).
9. If desired, enter a date for delivery in the "Desired delivery date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Order Request --** (Header, None, required/optional): This is a header indicating the purpose of the form.
* **Requester name** (requester_name, text, **required**): Enter your full name.
* **Contact email** (contact_email, email, **required**): Enter your contact email address for order confirmation.
* **Product requested** (product_requested, text, **required**): Enter the name or code of the product you want to order.
* **Quantity requested** (quantity, number, **required**): Enter the number of units you want to order.
* **Billing address** (billing_address, text, optional): Enter your billing address (if different from shipping).
* **Shipping address** (shipping_address, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Payment method** (payment_method, select_one, **required**): Select one of the payment options (Credit card, Bank transfer, or PayPal).
* **Desired delivery date** (desired_delivery_date, date, optional): Enter the date for desired delivery (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Special requests** (special_requests, text, optional): Enter any special requests or customizations for your order.
