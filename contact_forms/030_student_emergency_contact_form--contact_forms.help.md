# Student Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your emergency contact in case of an unexpected situation. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to help us ensure your well-being and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. If applicable, indicate your emergency contact's relationship to you (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. If you have specified a relationship other than those listed, provide a brief description in the "Other (Specify)" field.
4. Enter your contact type (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Provide your contact's phone number in the format XXX-XXXX.
6. Enter your contact's email address in the format name@domain.com.
7. Enter your contact's address.
8. Enter your contact's city.
9. Enter your contact's state.
10. Enter your contact's zip code in the format XXXX.
11. Enter your emergency contact's name.
12. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the format XXX-XXXX.
13. If applicable, indicate your emergency contact's relationship to you (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
14. Enter your emergency contact's type or description.
15. Enter your emergency contact's address.
16. Enter your emergency contact's city.
17. Enter your emergency contact's state.
18. Enter your emergency contact's zip code in the format XXXX.
19. If applicable, provide a brief description of your emergency contact's relationship to you.
20. Enter your emergency contact's phone number 2 in the format XXX-XXXX.
21. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the format name@domain.com.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your student ID or identification card.
* **Relationship**: Select "Yes" if you have a designated emergency contact, or "No" if you do not.
* **Other (Specify)**: If you have a relationship other than those listed, enter a brief description of your emergency contact's relationship to you.
* **Contact Type**: Select "Yes" if your emergency contact is a family member, or "No" if not.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact's phone number in the format XXX-XXXX.
* **Email**: Enter your contact's email address in the format name@domain.com.
* **Address**: Enter your contact's address in the format: 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345
* **City**: Enter your contact's city.
* **State**: Enter your contact's state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your contact's zip code in the format XXXX.
* **Emergency Name**: Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone Number**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the format XXX-XXXX.
* **Emergency Relationship**: Select "Yes" if your emergency contact has a relationship with you, or "No" if not.
* **Type**: Enter your emergency contact's type or description.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency City**: Enter your emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency State**: Enter your emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Zip**: Enter your emergency contact's zip code in the format XXXX.
* **Emergency Relationship Other**: If you have an emergency contact with a relationship other than those listed, enter a brief description of your emergency contact's relationship to you.
* **Emergency Phone Number 2**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number 2 in the format XXX-XXXX.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter your emergency contact's email address in the format name@domain.com.
