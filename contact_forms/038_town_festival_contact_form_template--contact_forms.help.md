# town_festival_contact_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The town_festival_contact_form_template is a contact form designed to collect information from attendees and visitors of a town festival. This form can be used to gather general feedback, questions, or other non-sensitive information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, simply fill in the form with the required and optional fields as needed.
2. The form is organized into a single page for ease of use.
3. You can submit the form once all necessary fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Town Festival Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, required: false): This is the main title of the form, and is only for reference.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please provide your name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you provide your email, we'll be able to contact you back.
* **Question/Feedback** (`question`, text, required: false): Please use this field to provide any feedback, questions, or comments you may have about the town festival.
* **Event** (`event_name`, text, required: false): If this form is used for a specific event, please provide the name of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all necessary fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* This form is for non-sensitive information only, so feel free to provide any general feedback or questions you may have.
