# Online Booking Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Booking Enquiry Form is a contact form that helps customers inquire about booking an online appointment or service. It is used to gather information about the customer's preferences and requirements for their online booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the first name field.
2. Enter your Last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the phone number field.
5. Specify the Number of people attending the online appointment or service.
6. Select the Arrival date for your online appointment or service.
7. Select the Departure date for your online appointment or service.
8. Provide any feedback or additional information in the feedback field.
9. If you are satisfied with the service, select how we did from the how did we do field.
10. If you have any special requests, enter them in the special requests field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Number of people** (`number_of_people`, number, required: false): Please specify the number of people attending the online appointment or service.
* **Arrival date** (`arrival_date`, date, required: false): Select the arrival date for your online appointment or service.
* **Departure date** (`departure_date`, date, required: false): Select the departure date for your online appointment or service.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Please provide any feedback or additional information.
* **How did we do?** (`how_did_we_do`, select_one, required: false): If you are satisfied with the service, select how we did.
* **Special requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to ensure that the business can efficiently handle your enquiry.
* If you have already submitted a booking form, please do not submit this form again.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, please contact the business directly.
