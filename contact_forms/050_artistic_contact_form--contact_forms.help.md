# Artistic Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artistic Contact Form is designed to gather contact information from potential clients or collaborators for artistic services. It allows artists to receive inquiries, project commissions, or feedback while understanding the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for response and communication.
3. If applicable, provide your **Phone Number** for quicker response.
4. Enter your **Organization or Business Name** if you represent a company or collective.
5. Select the **Reason for Contacting** you are reaching out for (e.g., General Inquiry, Commission, Collaboration Opportunity, etc.).
6. Provide a **Subject** for your message.
7. In the **Message** field, describe your inquiry or request.
8. If relevant, select the **Type of Project or Commission** and provide a brief **Project or Commission Description**.
9. Choose your **Budget or Budget Range** and **Timeline or Deadline** for the project.
10. Optionally, provide **Style or Aesthetic Preference**, **Reference Images or Links**, and your **Budget or Budget Range** and **Timeline or Deadline**.
11. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** (Email, Phone, or Text Message).
12. Choose your **Best Time to Contact You** (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
13. If you would like to receive updates and news from us, select "Yes, send updates."
14. Confirm that you are **Submitting Your Inquiry** by selecting "Yes, Submit My Inquiry."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name for us to address you.
* **Email Address**: Your email address will be used for response and communication. Please ensure it is valid and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number**: Optional - providing your phone number will help us respond more quickly.
* **Organization or Business Name**: If you represent a company or collective, enter your business name here.
* **Reason for Contacting**: Select the reason for your inquiry, and we'll guide you through the process.
* **Subject**: Briefly describe the subject of your message.
* **Message**: In this field, describe your inquiry or request in detail.
* **Type of Project or Commission**: Choose the type of artistic service you require (e.g., painting, sculpture, digital art, etc.).
* **Project or Commission Description**: Provide a brief description of your project or commission.
* **Budget or Budget Range**: Indicate your budget range for the project.
* **Timeline or Deadline**: Select the desired timeline or deadline for completion.
* **Style or Aesthetic Preference**: If you have a specific style or aesthetic in mind, describe it here.
* **Reference Images or Links**: Share reference images or links to help us understand your vision.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text Message).
* **Best Time to Contact You**: Select the most convenient time for us to contact you.
* **Marketing Consent**: If you would like to receive updates and news from us, select "Yes, send updates."
* **I Agree to Submit This Contact Form**: Confirm that you are submitting your inquiry by selecting "Yes, Submit My Inquiry."
