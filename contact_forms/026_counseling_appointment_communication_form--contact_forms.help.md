# counseling_appointment_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to communicate with therapists regarding their counseling appointments. It provides an efficient way to share appointment details, purposes, and any concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title to describe the reason for the communication.
2. Provide your name as the client.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Select the appointment date and time.
5. Specify the purpose of the visit.
6. Share any additional information or concerns through the message field.
7. Enter your contact phone number.
8. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Text Message) to receive updates or reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **counseling_appointment_communication_form**:
	+ This is the form title that should describe the reason for the communication.
* **client_name**:
	+ Enter your name as the client.
* **email**:
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further communication.
* **appointment_date**:
	+ Select the date of your appointment using the date picker.
* **time**:
	+ Select the time of your appointment using the time picker.
* **purpose_of_visit**:
	+ Explain the reason for your visit, helping us to prepare for your session.
* **message**:
	+ Share any additional information or concerns you have regarding your appointment.
* **contact_phone**:
	+ Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **preferred_contact**:
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Text Message) for updates or reminders.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in processing your request.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information for the purpose of your visit.
- Use the preferred contact method to receive updates or reminders from us.
