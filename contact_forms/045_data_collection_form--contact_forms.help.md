# Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Collection Form is a tool used to gather essential information from users, including name, email, and category, for the purpose of providing a clear and efficient way to collect data from customers or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter a brief description of your request or inquiry in the "Description" field.
5. Select the category that best describes your request or inquiry from the "Category" dropdown menu.
6. Optionally, provide any additional notes or information in the "Notes" field.
7. Select the "Submit" button to send your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Data Collection Form (form_title)**: This is the title of the form, which is already filled out for your convenience.
* **First Name (form_first_name)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (form_last_name)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (form_email)**: Enter your email address in this field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone (form_phone)**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Notes (form_notes)**: Enter any additional notes or information about your request or inquiry.
* **Category (form_category)**: Select the category that best describes your request or inquiry. The options are:
	+ Contact Forms
	+ Service Requests
	+ Other
* **Category Other (form_category_other)**: If you select "Other", you will be able to enter a custom category description in this field.
* **Description (form_description)**: Enter a brief description of your request or inquiry.
* **Submit (form_submit)**: Select this button to send your submission and complete the form.
