# Contact Form Busy City - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and event inquiries from users interested in attending or participating in events hosted by Busy City.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Attendee Information" section.
2. Select the event you are inquiring about from the "Specific Event Interest" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date you would prefer to attend the event in the "Preferred Event Date" field (if applicable).
4. Enter the number of people attending with you in the "Estimated Number of Attendees" field (if applicable).
5. Select the purpose of your message in the "Inquiry Subject" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your message or feedback in the "Your Detailed Message" field.
7. Finally, select the source where you found out about Busy City from the "How did you find us?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Specific Event Interest**: Choose the event you are inquiring about from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your interest and tailor our response accordingly.
* **Preferred Event Date**: If you have a preferred date to attend the event, enter it here. Note that this field is optional.
* **Estimated Number of Attendees**: If you plan to bring friends or family, enter the number here.
* **Inquiry Subject**: Choose the reason for your message or inquiry from the dropdown menu.
* **Your Detailed Message**: Provide a clear and detailed description of your message or feedback.
* **How did you find us?**: Let us know where you found out about Busy City from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the email address and phone number you provide are valid and can be reached for follow-up communication.
* If you are inquiring about multiple events, please fill out the form for each event separately.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please upload them along with your message.
* If you need a response to your message, please ensure that you have provided a valid contact email address.
