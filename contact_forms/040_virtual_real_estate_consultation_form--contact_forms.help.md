# virtual real estate consultation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients seeking a virtual real estate consultation. It will help real estate agents provide personalized services and advice to clients, allowing them to better understand their needs and provide effective guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive communication from the real estate agent.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for follow-up communications.
4. Choose the **Date of Consultation** and **Time of Consultation** that suits you best.
5. Select the **Reason for Consultation** to help the agent understand your needs.
6. Specify the type of **Property Type** you are interested in (e.g., Apartment, House, Condo, Townhouse, Land, or Other).
7. Enter the **Property Location** where you are looking to buy or sell.
8. If applicable, provide the **Real Estate Agent** name and their **Agent Contact Info**.
9. Add any **Client Notes** about your requirements or concerns.
10. The **Real Estate Agent Notes** and **Follow Up Notes** fields are for the agent to document their discussions and follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
### Email
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communications.
### Phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or updates.
### Date of Consultation
* **Date of Consultation** (date): Choose the date you are available for a consultation.
### Time of Consultation
* **Time of Consultation** (time): Select the time that suits you best for the consultation.
### Reason for Consultation
* **Reason for Consultation** (text): Explain the purpose of your consultation (e.g., buying, selling, or getting advice).
### Property Type
* **Property Type** (select multiple): Choose one or more types of properties you are interested in (e.g., Apartment, House, Condo, etc.).
### Property Location
* **Property Location** (text): Enter the location where you are looking to buy or sell a property.
### Real Estate Agent
* **Real Estate Agent** (text): If you have a preferred real estate agent, enter their name.
### Agent Contact Info
* **Agent Contact Info** (text): If you have a preferred real estate agent, enter their contact information.
### Client Notes
* **Client Notes** (note): Add any specific requirements or concerns you have about the property.
### Real Estate Agent Notes
* **Real Estate Agent Notes** (note): This field is for the agent to document their discussions and follow-up actions.
### Follow Up Notes
* **Follow Up Notes** (note): This field is for the agent to record any follow-up actions or discussions.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure the real estate agent can best serve your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the agent for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
