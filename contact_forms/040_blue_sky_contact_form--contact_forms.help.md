# Blue Sky Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Blue Sky Contact Form" is designed to allow you to contact us with any questions, feedback, or requests. Please fill out this form to send a message to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Us" link to open the form.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk `*`) with your details.
3. Select relevant options from the dropdown lists.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your message to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`)
* First name of the person contacting us

### Last Name (`last_name`)
* Last name of the person contacting us

### Email Address (`email_address`)
* Your email address where we can respond to your message

### Phone Number (`phone_number`)
* Optional: Your phone number for follow-up calls

### Company or Organization (`company_or_organization`)
* Your company or organization name (if you're contacting us on behalf of a company or organization)

### Reason for Contacting (`contact_reason`)
* Select one of the following reasons for contacting us:
	+ Sales inquiry
	+ Technical support
	+ Partnership
	+ Job inquiry
	+ Media request
	+ Feedback
	+ Other

### Subject Line (`subject_line`)
* Brief description of your message or inquiry

### Message (`message_content`)
* Your detailed message or inquiry

### Product or Service Interest (`inquiry_product_service`)
* If you're interested in our product or service, describe which one

### Industry (`industry`)
* Select your industry from the list:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Retail
	+ Education
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Other

### Company Size (`company_size`)
* Select your company size from the list:
	+ Startup
	+ Small (1-50)
	+ Medium (50-500)
	+ Large (500+)
	+ Individual/Freelance

### Response Timeframe (`response_timeframe`)
* Select when you need a response from us:
	+ Within 24 hours
	+ Within 3 days
	+ Within 1 week
	+ No rush

### How did you find us (`how_found_us`)
* Select how you found us:
	+ Search engine
	+ Social media
	+ Referral
	+ Advertisement
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Other

### Subscribe to our newsletter (`subscribe_newsletter`)
* Select yes or no to subscribe to our newsletter

### Submission Date (`submission_timestamp`)
* Date and time of submission, automatically generated

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure we can respond to your message effectively.
* If you have any attachments or files to include with your message, you can email them to us separately.
* We'll get back to you within the selected response timeframe.
