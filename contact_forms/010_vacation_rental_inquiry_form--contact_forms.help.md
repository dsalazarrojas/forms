# Vacation Rental Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Inquiry Form is a tool to gather information from users who are interested in inquiring about vacation rentals. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact details, specify your desired stay dates, share information about your guests, and include any additional questions or requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the **User Input** field.
2. Select your desired stay dates in the **Dates** field.
3. Provide details about your guests in the **Guest Details** field.
4. Type any additional messages or questions you have in the **Inquiry Message** field.
5. Review and submit your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Input** (text, optional)
Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information you want to share with the rental provider.

### **Dates** (date, optional)
Specify the dates you are interested in staying at the vacation rental.

### **Guest Details** (text, optional)
Include information about the number of guests, their ages, and any special requirements or needs they may have.

### **Inquiry Message** (text, optional)
Type any additional questions, requests, or information you think would be helpful for the rental provider to know about your inquiry.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when entering your contact information and desired dates.
- Include any necessary details about your guests to ensure the rental provider can provide the best possible accommodations.
- If you have any specific questions or requests, make sure to include them in the **Inquiry Message** field.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out accurately.
