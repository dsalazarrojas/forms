# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect emergency contact information from employees. It is intended to be used to identify a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency. This information will only be shared with authorized personnel in the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Choose the relationship between the emergency contact person and you.
3. Enter the name and relationship of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_info):
    - This field is optional and allows you to enter your contact information for the purpose of this form.
* **Name** (name):
    - Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (phone):
    - Enter your phone number that can be reached at in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (email):
    - Enter your email address that can be reached at in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship):
    - Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and you (e.g. Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Contact Name** (contact_name):
    - Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (contact_relationship):
    - Describe the relationship between you and the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone):
    - Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you need to update your contact information, please submit this form again with the new information.
