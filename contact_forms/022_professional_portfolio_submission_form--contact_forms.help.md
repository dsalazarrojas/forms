# Professional Portfolio Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Professional Portfolio Submission Form is designed to collect information about your professional portfolio submission. This form is meant to be filled out by individuals submitting their professional portfolio for review and consideration. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the `first_name` field. This field is for your first name, which will be used to address you in our communication.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `last_name` field. This field is for your last name, which will be used for official purposes.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the `email` field. This is the email address we will use to communicate with you about your submission.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the `phone` field. This is the number we will use to reach you if needed.
5. Check the **Have you worked with us before?** field. If you have worked with us in the past, please select "Yes".
6. Check the **Do you have work experience?** field. If you have work experience, please select "Yes".
7. Enter the **Link to your portfolio** in the `portfolio_link` field. This should be a link to your online portfolio or website.
8. Attach any relevant **Portfolio Attachments** in the `portfolio_attachment` field. This is optional, but please attach any relevant files or documents to support your submission.
9. Select your **Portfolio Type**. Please choose "Yes" if your portfolio meets our requirements.
10. Enter your **Submission ID** in the `submitted_by` field. This is a unique number assigned to you by the system, please enter the number exactly as it is.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Have you worked with us before?**: Check "Yes" if you have worked with us in the past.
* **Do you have work experience?**: Check "Yes" if you have work experience.
* **Link to your portfolio**: Enter a link to your online portfolio or website.
* **Portfolio Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents to support your submission. This is optional.
* **Portfolio Type**: Check "Yes" if your portfolio meets our requirements.
* **Submission ID**: Enter your unique submission ID exactly as it is assigned to you by the system.
