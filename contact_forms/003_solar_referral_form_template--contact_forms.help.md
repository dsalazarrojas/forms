# Solar Referral Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Solar Referral Form is designed to collect information about a referral that someone would like to make to our solar services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, following the correct format for an email address.
3. Type in your phone number, without any symbols or spaces.
4. Describe the reason for your referral, including any personal notes if necessary.
5. Choose the assigned tool that you would like to receive for your solar service.
6. If desired, add any additional comments about your referral.
7. Finally, choose whether you submitted this form as a User or if it was submitted by a System generated referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or personal documents.
  - This is a text field where you can input your first name.

- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or personal documents.
  - This is a text field where you can input your last name.

- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
  - This is a field to input your email address for contact purposes.

- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number without any symbols or spaces.
  - This is a text field for inputting your phone number.

- **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, `note`, required/optional): Explain why you are referring this individual to our solar services.
  - This is a text field for any personal notes about your referral.

- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the tool that best suits your solar service needs.
  - This dropdown menu lists available tools to select from for your referral.

- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or context about your referral.
  - This is a text field for extra notes or comments.

- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you submitted this form as a User or if it was a System generated referral.
  - This dropdown menu determines who submitted this form, whether a user or the system.
