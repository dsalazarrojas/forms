# Address Change Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a change in your address. It requires providing information about the old address, new address, and other relevant details like the effective date of change, contact phone number, and reason for change.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "Declarant information" section, which includes the "Full name" field. This is the name as currently recorded in our system.
2.  Next, enter your "Old address" in the "Old address" field. This is the previous address associated with your record.
3.  Enter your "New address" in the "New address" field. This is the new address you wish to update.
4.  Choose the "Effective date of change" for when the address change should take effect.
5.  If you have a different contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact phone" field. This can be useful for our team's verification process.
6.  If applicable, enter the type and number of your identification you used to prove the change.
7.  Optionally, provide a reason for the address change in the "Reason for change" field.
8.  Sign your name in the "Signature name" field.
9.  Date your declaration in the "Date signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Declarant information (declarant_information)**: This is the section where you can confirm your current identity.
*   *Full name (full_name):* This is your full name as recorded in the system. Please enter your current name as it is currently on file.
*   *Old address (old_address):* This is the previous address that was associated with your record. Enter this address as it was before the change.
*   *New address (new_address):* This is the new address you wish to update as your current address.
*   *Effective date of change (effective_date):* This is the date the change should take effect. Please note that the system may have specific requirements for the format of this date.
*   *Contact phone (contact_phone):* This is an optional field for you to enter a contact phone number. This can be helpful for our team's verification process.
*   *Identification provided (identification):* This is an optional field for you to provide identification, if applicable. If you have identification to prove the change, enter the type and number here.
*   *Reason for change (reason_for_change):* This is an optional field for you to provide a reason for the address change.
*   *Signature name (signature):* This is your typed name, which should match the signature you provide below.
*   *Date signed (date_signed):* This is the date you sign the declaration. Make sure to date it accurately.
