# Makeup Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users request a makeup appointment at a specified location. The form is used to collect information about the user's name and preferred appointment date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Select a date for your appointment by clicking on the calendar icon in the "Appointment Date" field.
3. Choose a time for your appointment by selecting from the available options in the "Appointment Time" field.
4. Optionally, enter a location for your appointment in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (required, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Appointment Date** (required, date): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (required, time): Select a time for your appointment. Please note that some locations may have limited availability, so be sure to choose a time that works for you.
* **Location** (optional, text): Enter a specific location where you would like to have your appointment (e.g., "Salon A", "Makeup Studio B", etc.). If left blank, the default location will be used.
