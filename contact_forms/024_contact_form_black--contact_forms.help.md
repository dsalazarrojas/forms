# Contact Form Black - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and inquiry details from users, enabling the organization to respond to their questions and requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Contact Form Black" to initiate the contact process.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* Your Full Name
	* Your Email Address
	* Preferred Time for Contact
	* Preferred Contact Method
	* Subject of Inquiry
	* Inquiry Category
	* Urgency Level
3.  Optionally, you may provide your Phone Number.
4.  Enter a detailed Message to elaborate on your inquiry.
5.  Finally, choose your preferred Date of Submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your personal name that you would like to be addressed with.
* **Your Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Provide your primary email address for communication.
* **Your Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Preferred Time for Contact** (`preferred_contact_time`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more times of the day for us to contact you, such as Morning (9 AM - 12 PM) or Afternoon (12 PM - 5 PM).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of contact, whether it's Email, Phone Call, or SMS/Text.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (`message_subject`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Category** (`inquiry_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the best match for your inquiry category, such as Sales Inquiry or Support Request.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_of_inquiry`, `select_one`, required): Specify how quickly you need a response, whether it's Urgent - High, Normal - Medium, or Low - Informational.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your submission, which defaults to today's date.
* **Message** (`detailed_message`, `text`, required): Write a more detailed message elaborating on your inquiry.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your inquiry.
