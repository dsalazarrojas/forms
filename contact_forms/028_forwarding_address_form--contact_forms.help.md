# Forwarding Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forwarding Address Form is used to collect contact information from users, specifically for communication or delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal contact information.
2. Choose the address type that best describes the address you are providing.
3. Verify that your email address is formatted correctly and is the one you want to use for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the complete address you would like to use for communication or delivery.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you would like to receive mail or messages.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address you would like to use for communication.
* **Address Type** (`select_one_address_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of address you are providing (Home, Work, Other).
* **Multiple Address Types** (`select_multiple_address_types`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional types of addresses you would like to use (Home, Work, Other).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication or delivery.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from a supervisor or system administrator.
* This form is intended for general contact purposes, so only provide information that you are comfortable sharing.
