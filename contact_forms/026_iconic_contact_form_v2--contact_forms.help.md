# Iconic Contact Form V2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Iconic Contact Form V2 is designed to be a comprehensive and adaptable contact form for various use cases. It allows users to provide personal information, feedback, and preferences in a flexible and user-friendly manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Provide feedback in the Feedback Box.
6. Select your Favorite Color from the Favorite Colors field.
7. Optionally, select multiple colors or provide feedback in the Feedback Box.
8. If required, choose a Date and Time for your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Feedback** (5, note, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback you may have.
* **Favorite Color** (6, select_one, required/optional): Select a color from the dropdown list.
* **Favorite Colors** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple colors from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (8, date, required/optional): Enter a date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (9, time, required/optional): Enter a time in the format `HH:MM` (24-hour).
* **Feedback Box** (10, select_one, required/optional): Select an option from the dropdown list.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
* Use the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD) and times (HH:MM).
* When selecting colors, choose one or multiple options depending on your preference.
* For feedback, be as detailed as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
