# Subscribe To My List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from users who want to subscribe to a mailing list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address for the "email" field.
3. Select the correct category for your subscription (Contact Forms or Marketing Forms).
4. Confirm your subscription by selecting "Yes, subscribe to my list" in the "confirm_subscription" field.
5. Double-check that your email address is correct in the "confirm_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **subscribe-to-my-list** (`text`, **required: false**): This is a text input field where you can provide a brief message or note about your subscription. You can leave this field blank if you don't want to leave a note.
* **name** (`text`, **required: false**): This is the field where you can enter your name to identify yourself.
* **email** (`email`, **required: false**): This is the field where you should enter a valid email address associated with your subscription. Please make sure to double-check that the address is correct.
* **confirm_email** (`email`, **required: false**): This is a field to confirm your email address, which should match the one you entered in the previous field. Make sure to type the same email address as before.
* **confirm_subscription** (`select_one`, **required: false**): This field is to confirm your subscription. Select "Yes, subscribe to my list" to confirm your interest in subscribing.
* **category** (`select_one`, **required: false**): This field is to select the category for your subscription. Choose "Contact Forms" or "Marketing Forms" to indicate the type of content you're interested in.
