# Contact Form With Fancy Header And Footer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This contact form is designed to gather visitor information and inquiry details for further communication and evaluation of user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter your phone number and website URL (if applicable).
3. Provide a brief summary of your inquiry in the subject field.
4. Describe your inquiry in more detail in the detailed message field.
5. Rate our website's overall performance (optional).
6. Finally, select the current date for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`visitor_full_name`, required): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Your Email Address** (`visitor_email_address`, required): Enter your primary email address for contact.
* **Your Phone Number** (`visitor_phone_number`, optional): Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **Your Website URL** (`visitor_website_url`, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Subject** (`inquiry_subject`, required): Provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Detailed Message** (`detailed_message`, required): Describe your inquiry in more detail.
* **Overall Rating of Our Website** (`overall_rating_of_website`, optional): Rate our website's overall performance, if you wish.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, required): Select the current date for submission.

Note: The form is divided into sections for easier navigation.
