# Simple Dark Themed Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information from users. It is intended for general purposes such as feedback, inquiry, or other non-mandatory communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as needed. Each field is optional, and you can choose to fill in any or none of them. If a field is marked as "required", you will not be able to submit the form until you fill it in. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or any other relevant information you'd like to share with us.

### Message
* **Message** (`form_message`, text, optional): Type in a brief message for us.

### Email
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to help us get in touch with you.

### Website
* **Website** (`website_input`, text, optional): If you have a website or blog, please share it with us.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

### Date
* **Date** (`date_picker`, date, optional): Select the date you prefer for us to get in touch with you.

### Time
* **Time** (`time_picker`, time, optional): Select the time you prefer for us to get in touch with you.

### Select One
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list to let us know your preference: 
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
