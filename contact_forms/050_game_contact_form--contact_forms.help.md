# Game Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Contact Form is a simple, user-friendly contact form designed to enable players to reach out to the game owners or developers with their thoughts, questions, or feedback about the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (name and email) in the corresponding fields.
2. Type in your message in the message field, describing your thoughts, questions, or feedback about the game.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your message to the game owners or developers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Contact Form** (`contact_form_1`, text, optional): This is the main title of the form, which is not a field you need to fill in.
* **Name** (`contact_form_2`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed by us.
* **Email** (`contact_form_3`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Message** (`contact_form_4`, text, optional): Describe your thoughts, questions, or feedback about the game. This is the space for you to share your opinions or suggestions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in your contact information and message fields accurately and clearly.
* Be as concise as possible when writing your message, but feel free to elaborate if needed.
* If you have any attachments or screenshots to support your message, you can include them with your email, but please don't attach them to this form.
