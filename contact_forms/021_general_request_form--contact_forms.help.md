# General Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Request Form is a contact form designed to collect information from customers, clients, or stakeholders. It helps the organization to understand their needs and respond accordingly. Please use this form for your general requests, and do not use it for duplicate requests or non-relevant purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Name.
2. Describe the Type of Request you are submitting.
3. Indicate your Preferred Contact Method (either Yes or No).
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Provide your Phone Number (optional, but highly recommended for faster response times).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (general_request_form_name, text, required): Enter your name to ensure that we can address the request personally.
* **Type of Request** (general_request_form_type_of_request, text, required): Describe the type of request you are submitting, e.g., "Service inquiry", "Product inquiry", or "Feedback".
* **Preferred Contact Method** (general_request_form_preferred_contact_method, select_one, true): Select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted via phone or email, and "No" otherwise.
* **Email** (general_request_form_email, email, true): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (general_request_form_phone_number, text, false): Enter your phone number, which is optional but recommended for quicker response times.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information in the Type of Request field.
* If you have any attachments or additional files to support your request, please attach them separately.
* For faster response times, please include your Phone Number.
* The organization may contact you via your preferred contact method or email if necessary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us directly.
