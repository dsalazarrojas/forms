# sidebar_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sidebar_contact_form is a contact form that enables users to get in touch with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate fields and fill out the required information. You can submit the form without any required fields if you prefer. Here's what each field is for:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name)** (`text`, Optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name (Last name)** (`text`, Optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email address (Email address)** (`email`, Optional): Enter your email address to receive a response.
* **Phone number (Phone number)** (`text`, Optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Message (Message)** (`text`, Optional): Write a brief message to describe your inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address if you provide one, to ensure you receive a response.
* You can submit the form without any required fields if you wish.
* Be clear and concise in your message, and we'll do our best to get back to you.
