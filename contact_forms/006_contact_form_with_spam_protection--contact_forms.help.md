# Contact Form With Spam Protection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The contact form is used to provide a secure and spam-protected way for end users to send inquiries, feedback, or general information. This form ensures that user-submitted content is not used for spam purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field (required).
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (required).
3. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select the **Subject** of your inquiry (required):
	* General
	* Technical
	* Billing
	* Feedback
5. Type your message in the **Message** field (required).
6. Answer the anti-spam question: **What is 5 plus 2?** (required).
7. Read and agree to the **Privacy Policy** by selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Secure Contact Form**: This is the header for the form, which is not a field to fill in, but rather a label to indicate that this form is secure and protected against spam.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed in the response.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use to receive notifications and communication.
* **Phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Subject**: Select the category that best describes your inquiry or message. This will help the recipient understand the context of your message.
	+ **General**: For general inquiries or information requests.
	+ **Technical**: For technical issues or technical support questions.
	+ **Billing**: For billing or payment-related inquiries.
	+ **Feedback**: For feedback or suggestions on our service or product.
* **Message**: Type a detailed description of your message or question in this text field.
* **What is 5 plus 2?**: This is an anti-spam question to prevent automated submissions. Answer the math question correctly to verify that you are a real person.
* **I agree to the privacy policy**: Select the correct option from the dropdown menu to confirm that you have read and agreed to our privacy policy.
