# Seller Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Information Form is designed to collect essential details from sellers who are listing their properties for sale. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements, enabling us to tailor our services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the fields with your personal information and property details.
2. Select the relevant options for the Type of Property, Reason to Sell, Best Time to Contact, Soon to Move Out, and Best Contact Method fields.
3. Submit the form to initiate the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Seller Personal Information** (`seller_personal_information`, text, required/optional): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Type of Property** (`type_of_property`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable types of property you are selling (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Reason to Sell** (`reason_to_sell`, select_one, optional): Select the primary reason you are selling your property (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Indicate the best time to contact you (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Soon to Move Out** (`soon_to_move_out`, select_one, optional): Check if you need to move out soon (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Best Contact Method** (`best_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
