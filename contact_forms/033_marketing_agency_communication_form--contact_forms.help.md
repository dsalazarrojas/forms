# Marketing Agency Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Agency Communication Form is designed to facilitate communication between the marketing agency and its clients. It serves as a platform for clients to submit their requests, provide feedback, and share information about their upcoming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the **Client Request** field with a brief description of your client request.
*   In the **Customer Request** field, describe any specific requests or concerns you have for the customer.
*   Select **Services Previous** to indicate whether you have used any services offered by the agency in the past. If yes, proceed to provide a brief description of your experience in **Services Previous Comments**.
*   Select **Services Upcoming** to indicate whether you are planning to use any services offered by the agency in the near future. If yes, describe your plans in **Services Upcoming Comments**.
*   In the **Comments** field, add any additional comments or feedback you have.
*   Finally, type any **Feedback** you have regarding the agency's services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Request**: Enter a brief description of your client request.
*   **Customer Request**: Provide a description of any specific requests or concerns you have for the customer.
*   **Services Previous**: Select 'Yes' if you have used any services offered by the agency in the past.
*   **Services Previous Comments**: Enter a brief description of your experience with previous services if you selected 'Yes'.
*   **Services Upcoming**: Select 'Yes' if you are planning to use any services offered by the agency in the near future.
*   **Services Upcoming Comments**: Describe your plans for upcoming services if you selected 'Yes'.
*   **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have.
*   **Feedback**: Enter any thoughts or opinions you have regarding the agency's services.
