# Sponsorship Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Collection Form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors, investors, or donors who are interested in supporting the organization. This form helps to gather data on their contact preferences and relationship type, ensuring that the organization can effectively communicate with them and categorize their level of support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your preferred Sponsor Type from the "Sponsor Type" dropdown menu, which can be Sponsor, Investor, or Donor.
5. Choose your desired Sponsor Level from the "Sponsor Level" dropdown menu, which can be Bronze, Silver, or Gold.
6. Enter your Sponsor Amount in the "Sponsor Amount" field. Note that this field accepts numerical values.
7. Select your contact method(s) from the "Contact Method" dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options, such as Phone, Email, or Mail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The "First Name" field is where you should input your first name.
* **Last Name**: In the "Last Name" field, enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field to ensure easy communication.
* **Sponsor Type**: Select from the options Sponsor, Investor, or Donor to describe your type of support.
* **Sponsor Level**: Choose your desired level of support from Bronze, Silver, or Gold.
* **Sponsor Amount**: Enter a numerical value to represent your sponsorship amount.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method(s), such as Phone, Email, or Mail.
