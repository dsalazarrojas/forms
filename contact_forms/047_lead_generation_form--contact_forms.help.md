# Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to help our team better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Choose the correct lead source (e.g., Marketing, Advertising, or Design).
* Enter your name in the `lead_name` field.
* Enter your email address in the `lead_email` field.
* Enter your phone number in the `lead_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Source (lead_source)**: Select the type of marketing channel that brought you to our website or business (e.g., Marketing, Advertising, or Design). This will help our team understand how you found out about us.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps us tailor our communication to your interests.
* **Lead Name (lead_name)**: Enter your name in this field. This will allow us to address you personally and build a relationship.
* **Lead Email (lead_email)**: Enter your email address in this field. This is how we'll communicate with you about our products or services.
* **Lead Phone (lead_phone)**: Enter your phone number in this field. This can be useful for follow-up calls or to discuss your interests further.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have already submitted this form previously, you can skip filling it out again.
* Our team will use the information you provide to better understand your needs and preferences.
