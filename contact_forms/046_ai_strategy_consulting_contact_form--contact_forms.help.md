# AI Strategy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather basic contact information from clients and prospects interested in AI strategy consulting services. It helps us understand their needs and provide relevant information to aid their decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Click "Submit"** to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Select the options for the select fields (e.g., preferred way to be reached, current AI adoption stage, areas of interest).
4. Upload any supporting documents or files as needed (not applicable for this form).
5. **Click "Submit" again** to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Full Name (required)
• **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.

### Job Title (required)
• **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.

### Organization Name (required)
• **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.

### Work Email Address (required)
• **Work Email Address** (`work_email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.

### Best way to reach you (required)
• **Best way to reach you** (`preferred_reach_out`, `select_one`, required): Select the best way we can reach you (Email, Phone Call, LinkedIn Message).

### Current stage of AI adoption (required)
• **Current stage of AI adoption** (`ai_adoption_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select where you are in the AI adoption process (Initial Researching / Education, Pilot / Proof of Concept Stage, Scaling AI across departments, Mature AI / Optimization Phase).

### Areas of Interest (required)
• **Areas of Interest** (`consulting_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas you are interested in (AI Roadmap & Strategy Development, Generative AI Opportunity Assessment, Build vs. Buy (Vendor Analysis), AI Governance, Risk & Ethics, Data Infrastructure & MLOps Strategy, AI Training & Talent Development).

### What is the primary challenge you are looking to solve with AI? (required)
• **What is the primary challenge you are looking to solve with AI?** (`primary_challenge`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the main challenge you are trying to address using AI.

### Expected Project Timeline (required)
• **Expected Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select the expected project timeline (Immediate (Within 1 month), Near-term (1 to 3 months), Mid-term (3 to 6 months), Long-term / Planning for next year).

### Budget Allocated for Consulting (required)
• **Budget allocated for consulting** (`budget_allocation`, `select_one`, required): Select the budget allocated for our consulting services (Under $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $200,000, $200,000+).

### Preferred Date and Time for Discovery Call (optional)
• **Preferred Date for Discovery Call** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your preferred date for the discovery call.
• **Preferred Time for Discovery Call** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter your preferred time for the discovery call.
• **Your Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, required): Select your time zone (Eastern Time (ET), Central Time (CT), Mountain Time (MT), Pacific Time (PT), GMT / UTC, Other).

### How did you hear about our consultancy? (optional)
• **How did you hear about our consultancy?** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe how you heard about us.

### I consent to my data being processed for contact purposes. (required)
• **I consent to my data being processed for contact purposes.** (`data_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you consent to data processing for contact purposes.
