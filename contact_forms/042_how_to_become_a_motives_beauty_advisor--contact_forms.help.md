# how_to_become_a_motives_beauty_advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow potential beauty advisors to submit their information and schedule a follow-up with a Motives representative for further discussion about becoming a Motives beauty advisor.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Contact Form**: Please enter your contact information in the "Contact Form" section.
2. **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
5. **Schedule Follow up**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for a follow-up.
6. **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate (if different from yours).
7. **Candidate Email**: Enter the email address of the candidate.
8. **Candidate Phone**: Enter the phone number of the candidate.
9. **Follow up Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted for a follow-up (phone, email, mail, or in-person).
10. **Follow up Message**: Enter any additional message or information you would like to include for the follow-up.
