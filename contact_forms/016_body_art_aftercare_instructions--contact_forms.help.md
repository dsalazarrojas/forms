# Body Art Aftercare Instructions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a client's understanding of the aftercare instructions provided by the artist or technician after a body art procedure, such as a tattoo, piercing, or microblading. The client is asked about their comprehension of general aftercare instructions, what type of products to use, and specific behaviors to avoid during the healing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Client Name.
2. Enter your Email Address.
3. Enter your Phone Number.
4. Select the Date of the Procedure.
5. Choose the Type of Body Art Procedure you underwent.
6. Enter the Location of the Procedure on your body.
7. Enter the Artist or Technician's Name (if applicable).
8. Answer each question about your understanding of aftercare instructions, selecting the correct response from the given options.
9. Select any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
10. If you have any questions or concerns, enter them here.
11. Finally, acknowledge receiving the aftercare instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Procedure**: Select the date of your body art procedure.
* **Type of Body Art Procedure**: Choose the type of procedure you underwent, such as Tattoo, Piercing, Microblading, etc.
* **Location on Body**: Describe the location on your body where the procedure was performed.
* **Artist or Technician Name**: Enter the name of the artist or technician who performed the procedure (if applicable).
* **I understand the general aftercare instructions**: Select "Yes, understood" if you have a clear understanding of the aftercare instructions provided.
* **Format of Instructions Provided**: Select the format in which you received the aftercare instructions, such as Written Paper, Digital Copy, Verbal Explanation, or Video Tutorial.
* **I will keep the area clean and dry**: Select "True" if you understand the importance of keeping the area clean and dry.
* **I understand to limit water exposure**: Select "True" if you understand that exposure to water may slow down the healing process.
* **I will avoid fragrances and scented products**: Select "True" if you understand the importance of avoiding fragrances and scented products during the healing process.
* **I will avoid direct sunlight**: Select "True" if you understand the importance of avoiding direct sunlight during the healing process.
* **I understand what lotion to use**: Select "Yes, clear instructions" if you have a clear understanding of what lotion to use during the healing process.
* **How often to apply lotion**: Select "True" if you understand the correct frequency of applying lotion during the healing process.
* **I understand not to pick or scratch**: Select "True" if you understand the importance of not picking or scratching the area during the healing process.
* **I understand the healing timeline**: Select "True" if you have a clear understanding of the expected healing timeline.
* **I understand what to expect as it heals**: Select "True" if you have a clear understanding of what to expect during the healing process, such as scabbing, color changes, etc.
* **I know the signs of infection**: Select "True" if you have a clear understanding of the signs of infection, such as excessive redness, swelling, discharge, etc.
* **When to contact the artist or doctor**: Select "True" if you have a clear understanding of when to contact the artist or doctor for concerns or issues.
* **I have the studio contact information**: Select "True" if you have the contact information of the studio where the procedure was performed.
* **Allergies or sensitivities**: If you have any allergies or sensitivities, enter them here.
* **Questions or Concerns**: Enter any questions or concerns you may have about the aftercare instructions.
* **I Acknowledge Receiving Aftercare Instructions**: Select "I Acknowledge" if you have received and understood the aftercare instructions.
