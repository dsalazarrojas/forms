# GhostHouse Haunted Tours - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GhostHouse Haunted Tours form is designed to collect contact information from customers interested in visiting a haunted tour. This form may be used for marketing, sales, or customer service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Follow the instructions below for each field.
* Fill out the form with the required and optional information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<name> first_name, <type> text): Enter your first name as you would like to be referred to by us.
* **Email** (<name> email, <type> email): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (<name> phone, <type> text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date** (<name> date, <type> date): Select the date you would like to visit the haunted tour.
* **Time** (<name> time, <type> time): Select the time you would like to visit the haunted tour.
* **Message** (<name> message, <type> note): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the Message field.
* If you need any help with your tour booking, contact us at [insert contact information].
