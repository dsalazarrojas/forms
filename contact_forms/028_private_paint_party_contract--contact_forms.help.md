# Private Paint Party Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Paint Party Contract is a form designed to capture the essential details of your private paint party event. This form helps you create a comprehensive contract that outlines the terms and conditions of the event, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Contract Title**, a brief description of your event.
2. Describe the **Event Description**, providing a more detailed account of what to expect at the event.
3. Fill in the **Event Date** when the paint party will take place.
4. Specify the **Event Time** when the paint party will start and end.
5. Enter the **Event Location**, where the paint party will be held.
6. Indicate the **Painting Style** of the event, such as acrylic, watercolor, or mixed media.
7. Provide the name of the **Artist** who will be leading the paint party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Title** (`contract_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your paint party event.
* **Description** (`contract_description`, text, optional): Give a detailed description of your paint party event, including what to expect and any notable details.
* **Event Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the paint party will take place.
* **Event Time** (`contract_time`, time, optional): Fill in the start and end time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`contract_location`, text, optional): Specify the venue or location where the paint party will be held.
* **Painting Style** (`contract_painting_style`, text, optional): Choose a style for the paint party, such as acrylic, watercolor, or mixed media.
* **Artist** (`contract_artist`, text, optional): Enter the name of the artist leading the paint party.
