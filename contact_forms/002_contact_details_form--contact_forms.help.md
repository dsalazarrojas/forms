# Contact Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Details Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from users, enabling them to be added to a contact list for the organization. This form is essential for establishing communication channels and ensuring that users receive relevant updates and information about the organization's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field, including your country code if applicable.
4. If you have an employer or company, enter your organization's name in the "Organization name" field.
5. Describe your role or job title within your organization in the "Role or job title" field.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, text message, or no preference) from the "Preferred contact method" dropdown list.
7. Select a preferred time window for us to contact you in the "Best time to contact" field.
8. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing address" field, including your street address, city, state, and postal code.
9. Choose the areas you consent to receive communications about (newsletters, product updates, promotions, or events) from the "Contact list preferences" dropdown list.
10. Finally, provide any additional information or details in the "Additional notes" field that we should know about you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your first and last name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address, whether it's for business or personal use.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including your country code if applicable.
* **Organization name**: If you have an employer or company, enter the name here.
* **Role or job title**: Describe your position within your organization.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, text message, or no preference).
* **Best time to contact**: Select a preferred time window for us to contact you.
* **Mailing address**: Enter your full mailing address, including your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Contact list preferences**: Select the areas you consent to receive communications about (newsletters, product updates, promotions, or events).
* **Additional notes**: Provide any other details we should know about you.
