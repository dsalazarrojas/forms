# Woodshop Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Woodshop Contact Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in placing custom orders or have general inquiries. This form is meant to be used for customers who want to get in touch with Woodshop directly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with your full name.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select a "Subject" that best describes the purpose of your contact.
4. Type out your message in the "Message" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email Address" field. (Optional)
7. If you have a website, enter it in the "Website" field. (Optional)
8. Select a date for your custom order from the "Date" field. (Optional)
9. Select a time for your custom order from the "Time" field. (Optional)
10. You can add a "Note" to your order in the "Note" field. (Optional)
11. Read and select the "Contact Form Agreement" to confirm you agree with the terms and conditions. (Required)
11. Provide any custom order details in the "Custom Order Details" field.
12. If you have any additional information to include with your order, use the "Attachment" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name for Woodshop to address you properly.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so Woodshop can contact you back.
* **Subject**: Choose a brief description of the purpose of your contact.
* **Message**: Type out your message to Woodshop.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so Woodshop can contact you if needed.
* **Email Address**: If you have an email address, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Website**: If you have a website, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Date**: Choose a date for your custom order. (Optional)
* **Time**: Choose a time for your custom order. (Optional)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes to your order. (Optional)
* **Contact Form Agreement**: Agree with the terms and conditions of Woodshop. (Required)
* **Custom Order Details**: Provide any additional information for your custom order.
* **Attachment**: Upload any additional information to include with your order. (Optional)
