# dietitian_contact_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dietitian contact information form is designed to collect and store contact information for clients, facilitating better communication and record-keeping for dietician services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Add any relevant client notes in the "Client notes" field.
6. Add any relevant dietician notes in the "Dietician notes" field.
7. Schedule a follow-up date and time in the "Follow up date" and "Follow up time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Client notes** (`text`, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about the client.
* **Dietician notes** (`text`, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about the client's care.
* **Follow up date** (`date`, required: false): Schedule a follow-up appointment date.
* **Follow up time** (`time`, required: false): Schedule a follow-up appointment time.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use the form for the intended purpose only, for better record-keeping and client relationship management.
