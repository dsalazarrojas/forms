# Tell Me Anything Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a simple and open-ended template for users to share their thoughts, questions, or concerns about any subject. It's meant to be a straightforward and user-friendly way to collect feedback or information without requiring too much personal or sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by writing in the "Tell Me Anything" field anything that's on your mind. This can be a question, a concern, or just a general thought.
2.  If you want to share your identity, fill in your Name and Email fields.
3.  If you have a specific question or concern, select one or more from the "Question or Concern" dropdown list.
4.  If you'd like to include more information, use the Message field.
5.  If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can type in the Anonymous Submission field.
6.  Finally, if you want to specify a teacher or manager name related to this form, fill in the Teacher/Manager Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tell Me Anything** (Tell Me Anything, text, required/optional): A free-text field for users to express their thoughts or questions.
- **Name** (Name, text, required/optional): A field to optionally share your name and contact information.
- **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): An optional field to share your email address, if you'd like to be contacted.
- **Question or Concern** (Question or Concern, select_multiple, required/optional): A dropdown list of questions or concerns to help guide your response, or simply select "Other" to provide your own text.
- **Message** (Message, text, required/optional): A field to include more information or context about your thoughts or question.
- **Anonymous Submission** (Anonymous Submission, text, required/optional): A field to type in if you want to remain anonymous.
- **Teacher/Manager Name** (Teacher/Manager Name, text, required/optional): A field to optionally specify a teacher or manager name related to this form response.
