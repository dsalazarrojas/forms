# Contact Form Rock Background - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for visitors to contact the website administrators with their thoughts, questions, or messages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Your Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Your Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select how you found us from the "How did you find us?" dropdown list.
5. In the "Subject" field, provide a brief summary of your inquiry.
6. Write a detailed message in the "Your Message" field, explaining your question or thought.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (`visitor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Your Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your mobile or home phone number where we can reach you.
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from the list how you found us:
	+ Social Media
	+ Search Engine
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Other
* **Subject** (`inquiry_subject`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of your message or question.
* **Your Message** (`detailed_message`, `text`, required): Write a detailed explanation of your thoughts or inquiry.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This will be automatically filled in with the current date.
