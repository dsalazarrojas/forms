# Contact Form With Round Corners - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to get in touch with our team, whether it's to inquire about a product or service, ask a question, or report an issue. Fill out this form to reach out to us and we'll respond as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through this help guide to understand the purpose of each field.
* Fill out each field accordingly, making sure to follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name (`First Name`, text, required)
* This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. Last Name (`Last Name`, text, required)
* This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 3. Electronic Mail Address (`user_email`, email, required)
* This field is for your electronic mail address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
### 4. Department to Contact (`department`, select_one, required)
* Select the department you'd like to contact: Sales, Customer Service, Billing, or Management.
### 5. Urgency of Request (`urgency_level`, select_one, required)
* Indicate the urgency of your request: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
### 6. Write your message here (`message_body`, text, required)
* This field is for the body of your message. Please provide a clear and concise description of your inquiry or question.
### 7. Preferred Time for Callback (`best_time_to_call`, time, optional)
* If applicable, choose a preferred time for a callback. If not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
### 8. Subscribe to our newsletter (`newsletter_opt_in`, select_one, optional)
* Check to indicate whether you'd like to subscribe to our newsletter for monthly updates and news.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can properly address your inquiry.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* We'll do our best to respond to your message as soon as possible.
