# cruise_passenger_vital_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cruise_passenger_vital_information_form is designed to collect essential information from cruise passengers, including their personal details, contact information, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are filling out the correct information for the intended passenger.
2. Fill out the Passenger Information section with the passenger's personal details.
3. Provide contact information for the passenger, including their address, email, and phone number.
4. List the passenger's emergency contact information, including the contact person's name, relationship, and contact information.
5. If the emergency contact relationship is not listed, provide a brief description of the relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Information** (`passenger_info`, text, required): Enter the passenger's name, date of birth, and passport number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the passenger's address, email, and phone number.
* **Travel Information** (`travel_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional travel-related information, such as flight numbers or travel dates.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): List the emergency contact person's name.
* **Next Of Kin** (`next_of_kin`, text, optional): List the passenger's next of kin's name.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's address, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Choose the relationship between the passenger and the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, text, optional): If the emergency contact relationship is not listed, provide a brief description of the relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (`emergency_contact_address_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate address for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_email_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate email for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_number_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type_other`, text, optional): If the emergency contact relationship is not listed, enter a brief description.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type Other Info** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type_other_info`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type Other Info Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type_other_info_other`, text, optional): Enter further details about the emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type Other Info Other Info 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type_other_info_other_info_2`, text, optional): Enter even more details about the emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (`emergency_contact_address_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate address for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (`emergency_contact_email_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate email for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`emergency_contact_number_2`, text, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need to provide additional emergency contact information, please create a new field by selecting the "Emergency Contact Relationship Type Other" field and entering your description.
* If you need to provide an alternate address, email, or phone number for the emergency contact person, select the "Emergency Contact Address", "Emergency Contact Email", or "Emergency Contact Phone" field and enter the alternate information.
