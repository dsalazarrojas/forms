# Landscaping Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients who are interested in requesting landscaping services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
2. Provide accurate and clear information for each field.
3. Submit the form to initiate the service request process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, `text`, required): This field is where you should describe the service you are interested in, such as lawn care, plant installation, or yard maintenance.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_informations`, `text`, required): Please provide your full name, address, phone number, and/or any other relevant contact information.
* **Service Confirmation** (`service_confirmation`, `text`, optional): If you would like to confirm your service request or provide further details, please do so in this field.
* **Service Schedule** (`service_schedule`, `text`, required): Please indicate your preferred schedule for the service appointment, including any specific dates or time windows you are available.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review and respond to your service request as soon as possible.
* You will receive a confirmation email with details on your service appointment once we have reviewed your form.
