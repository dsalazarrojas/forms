# Export Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Export Declaration Form is a tool for declaring the export of products from your organization. It is used to provide detailed information about the export, including product name, quantity, and cost, as well as the export date and shipping method. This form helps in ensuring that all required information is accurately recorded and reported for compliance and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in the required fields, such as product name, quantity, unit price, and export date.
2. For each product being exported, indicate whether the declaration is 'Yes' or 'No' in the Declaration Details field.
3. Enter the product name in the Product Name field, as it is an essential piece of information.
4. Provide the total cost of the export in the Total Cost field.
5. Indicate the shipping method used for the export in the Shipping Method field.
6. You may optionally provide additional shipping details in the Shipping Details field.
7. Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declaration Details** (`Yes/No`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether the declaration is 'Yes' or 'No'. A 'Yes' value indicates that a declaration is being made, while a 'No' value indicates that no declaration is being made.
* **Product Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product being exported here.
* **Quantity** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the product being exported.
* **Unit Price** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the unit price of the product being exported.
* **Total Cost** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the export, which is the product of the quantity and unit price.
* **Export Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date on which the export took place.
* **Shipping Method** (`Yes/No`, required/optional): Indicate the shipping method used for the export, either 'Yes' for a yes value or 'No' for a no value.
* **Shipping Details** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional shipping details here.
