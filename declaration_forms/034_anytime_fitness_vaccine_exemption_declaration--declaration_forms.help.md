# Anytime Fitness Vaccine Exemption Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare exemptions for members who wish to opt-out of certain safety protocols or procedures at the fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields. 
2. If you are declaring a medical exemption, please ensure that you have attached the required documentation to support your claim.
3. If you are declaring a religious or philosophical exemption, please ensure that you have attached the required documentation to support your claim.
4. Please select the correct exemption duration for your exemption.
5. If you are declaring a temporary exemption, please select the end date for your exemption.
6. Please acknowledge that you understand the safety measures and protocols in place at the fitness center.
7. Please acknowledge that you will comply with additional requirements that may be imposed upon you.
8. Please review and agree to the testing procedures, if required for your exemption.
9. Please agree to the liability waiver.
10. Finally, please sign and date this form to confirm your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your membership card or identification.
* **Member ID (Required)**: Enter your membership number.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Home Club Location (Required)**: Enter the name of your home club location.
* **Membership Status (Required)**: Select your current membership status: Active, On hold, New member, or Returning member.
* **Type of Exemption Requested (Required)**: Select the basis for your exemption: Medical exemption, Religious exemption, Philosophical or personal belief, or Other.
* **Medical Basis for Exemption (Optional)**: If selecting medical exemption, please describe your medical basis.
* **Medical Condition Details (Optional)**: If describing a medical condition, provide additional details about your condition.
* **Religious Basis for Exemption (Optional)**: If selecting religious exemption, please describe your religious basis.
* **Religious Belief Details (Optional)**: If describing a religious belief, provide additional details about your belief.
* **Personal Belief Details (Optional)**: If claiming a personal belief, provide additional details about your belief.
* **Healthcare Provider Name (Optional)**: If medical exemption, enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact (Optional)**: If medical exemption, enter the contact information for your healthcare provider.
* **Documentation Attached (Required)**: Indicate if you are attaching supporting documentation.
* **Type of Documentation (Optional)**: Select the type of documentation you are attaching: Physician letter, Medical records, Religious leader letter, Personal statement, or Other.
* **Exemption Duration (Required)**: Select the duration of your exemption.
* **Temporary Exemption End Date (Optional)**: If selecting temporary exemption, enter the end date for your exemption.
* **Safety Measures Acknowledgment (Required)**: Acknowledge that you understand the safety measures and protocols in place at the fitness center.
* **Additional Requirements Acknowledgment (Required)**: Acknowledge that you will comply with additional requirements that may be imposed upon you.
* **Agreement to Regular Testing (Optional)**: If required for exemption, agree to regular testing procedures.
* **Liability Waiver (Required)**: Agree to the liability waiver.
* **Accuracy Statement (Required)**: Ensure that the information provided is accurate.
* **Legal Consequences Acknowledgment (Required)**: Acknowledge that you understand the legal consequences of providing false statements.
* **Member Signature (Required)**: Sign your name to confirm your declaration.
* **Signature Date (Required)**: Date and sign your declaration.
* **Witness Name (Optional)**: If required, enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Signature (Optional)**: If required, enter the signature of the witness.
* **Club Manager Review (Optional)**: If required, enter the name of the club manager who reviewed your declaration.
* **Review Date (Optional)**: If required, enter the date of the review.
* **Exemption Status (Optional)**: Select the status of your exemption: Approved, Denied, Pending review, or Requires additional documentation.

Please review this form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting it to your club manager.
