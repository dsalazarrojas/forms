# Customs Value Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to customs value declarations for import shipments. It requires accurate and detailed data to ensure smooth customs clearance and avoid any potential issues or delays.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2.  Fill in the shipment information (page 1).
3.  Provide a detailed description of the goods being shipped (page 2).
4.  Indicate the value of the goods being shipped (page 3).
5.  Select the country of origin for the goods (page 4).
6.  Check the customs declaration options that apply (page 5).
7.  Enter the shipment date (page 6).
8.  Add contact information for the shipper, importer, and logistics contact (pages 7-10).
9.  If necessary, list other parties involved in the shipment (page 11).
10. Check the terms of service and add any additional comments (pages 12-14).
11. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipments Information** (Page 1, id: 1): Enter the shipment details, including shipment ID, tracking number, or any other identifying information.
* **Goods Description** (Page 2, id: 2): Provide a detailed description of the goods being shipped, including their type, quantity, and weight.
* **Goods Value** (Page 3, id: 3): Enter the total value of the goods being shipped in the currency required.
* **Country of Origin** (Page 4, id: 4): Select the country where the goods were produced or manufactured.
* **Customs Declaration** (Page 5, id: 5): Check the customs declarations that apply to the shipment.
* **Shipment Date** (Page 6, id: 6): Enter the date of the shipment.
* **Contact Information** (Pages 7-8, id: 7): Enter the contact information for the shipper.
*   *Shipper Name* (id: 8): Enter the full name of the shipper.
*   *Shipper Email* (id: 9): Enter the email address of the shipper.
*   *Shipper Phone* (id: 10): Enter the phone number of the shipper.
*   *Importer Name* (id: 11): Enter the full name of the importer.
*   *Importer Email* (id: 12): Enter the email address of the importer.
*   *Importer Phone* (id: 13): Enter the phone number of the importer.
*   *Logistics Contact* (id: 14): Enter the name of the logistics contact.
*   *Logistics Name* (id: 15): Enter the full name of the logistics contact.
*   *Logistics Email* (id: 16): Enter the email address of the logistics contact.
*   *Logistics Phone* (id: 17): Enter the phone number of the logistics contact.
*   *Other Parties* (id: 18): Enter any other parties involved in the shipment.
*   *Other Party Name* (id: 19): Enter the full name of the other party.
*   *Other Party Email* (id: 20): Enter the email address of the other party.
*   *Other Party Phone* (id: 21): Enter the phone number of the other party.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 22, id: 22): Enter any additional comments or information about the shipment.
* **Terms of Service** (Page 23, id: 23): Check the terms of service that apply to the shipment.
* **Notes** (Page 24, id: 24): Enter any notes or additional information.
* **Submit** (Page 25, id: 25): Review and submit the form.
