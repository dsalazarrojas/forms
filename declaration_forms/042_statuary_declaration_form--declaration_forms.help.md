# Statuary Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting personal information from individuals for statutory purposes, such as declarations or official documentation. It is designed to be a basic and efficient way to collect contact and identification data.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill out your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
4. Fill out your Address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name here. It is a good idea to enter your name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name here. Similar to your first name, it is best to enter it exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID or official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY. For example, if you were born on January 1, 1990, you would enter "01/01/1990".
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your address here, including the street number, street name, apartment or suite number, city, region, and postal code. It is recommended to double-check the formatting for accuracy.
