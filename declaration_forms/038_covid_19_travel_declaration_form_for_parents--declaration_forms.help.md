# COVID 19 Travel Declaration Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to inform the school about travel history and health status of your child. It helps the school take necessary precautions to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any field, contact the school administration for guidance.
3. Please sign and date the form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declaration Date** (1): Enter the date you are filling out this form.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (2): Enter your legal first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (3): Enter your legal last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (4): Enter your contact number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (5): Enter your contact email.
* **Home Address** (6): Enter your street address.
* **City** (7): Enter the city you reside in.
* **State** (8): Enter the state you reside in.
* **ZIP Code** (9): Enter your postal code.
* **Child First Name** (10): Enter your child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name** (11): Enter your child's legal last name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (12): Enter your child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Child Gender** (13): Select your child's gender from the dropdown menu.
* **School Name** (14): Enter your child's school name.
* **Child Grade Level** (15): Select your child's current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Purpose of Travel** (16): Select the reason for your travel from the dropdown menu.
* **Travel Destination** (17): Enter the destination of your travel.
* **Departure Date** (18): Enter the date you are leaving.
* **Return Date** (19): Enter the date your child will return to school.
* **Method of Travel** (20): Select the transportation method from the dropdown menu.
* **Is this international travel** (21): Select Yes if your travel is international, otherwise select No domestic only.
* **List Countries Visited** (22): If you have traveled internationally, enter the countries you visited.
* **Is your child currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms** (23): Select True if your child has symptoms, otherwise select False.
* **Which symptoms** (24): If your child has symptoms, select all applicable symptoms.
* **Has anyone in your household tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days** (25): Select True if anyone in your household has tested positive, otherwise select False.
* **What is your child's COVID-19 vaccination status** (26): Select the vaccination status from the dropdown menu.
* **I agree to follow all quarantine and testing requirements upon return** (27): Select Yes I agree to follow all requirements, otherwise select No I do not agree.
* **I will notify the school if we test positive for COVID-19 during or after travel** (28): Select Yes I will notify, otherwise select No I will not notify.
* **Expected Return to School Date** (29): Enter the date your child is expected to return to school.
* **Additional Comments** (30): Enter any additional information about your child's travel or health status.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature Date** (31): Enter the date you signed this form.
