# Student Government Association Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare interest in joining the Student Government Association. Please fill out this form to express your interest and provide necessary information about yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide the date you wish to declare your interest in the Student Government Association.
4. Enter any additional information about yourself that you would like to share in the "Candidate Details" field.
5. If applicable, enter the name of your school and your major.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Your name as it appears on your student ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (email, required): Your student email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Declaration Date** (date, required): The date on which you are declaring your interest in the Student Government Association.
* **Candidate Details** (note, optional): Any additional information you would like to share about yourself or your qualifications.
* **Name of School** (text, optional): The name of your school.
* **Major** (text, optional): Your major or area of study.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
* Be honest and truthful in the "Candidate Details" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
