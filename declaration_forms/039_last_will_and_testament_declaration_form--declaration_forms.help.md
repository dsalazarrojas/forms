# last_will_and_testament_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare an individual's wishes for the distribution of their assets after death. It is an essential document in estate planning and ensures that an individual's wishes are respected in the event of their passing.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill out your personal information

- **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

### Step 2: Provide details about your assets

- **Occupation** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: Manager, Lawyer, Engineer, or Doctor.
- **Assets Owned** (`6`, `text`, required): Describe the assets you own, such as property, investments, or personal effects.
- **Assets Value** (`9`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the value of your assets.

### Step 3: Identify your beneficiaries

- **Beneficiaries** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all of the following options that apply: Yes, No.
- **Assets Description** (`8`, `text`, optional): Describe the assets you are distributing to your beneficiaries.

### Step 4: Sign and witness the document

- **Will Signatures** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: Self, Witness 1, or Witness 2.
- **Notary Seal** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: Yes, No.
- **Notary Seal Location** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the notary seal is affixed.
- **Notary Seal Number** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the number of the notary seal.
- **Declaration Statement** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter a statement declaring the truth of the declaration.
- **Sign Off** (`17`, `date`, required): Sign the document and date it.

### Step 5: Review and submit

Review your form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly. Once complete, submit the form for further processing.
