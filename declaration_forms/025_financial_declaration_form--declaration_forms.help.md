# Financial Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Declaration Form is a document used to collect information about a user's financial situation. This form is typically used in situations where a person is making a declaration or statement about their financial status, such as in the context of a loan application or a financial aid request. The form is designed to be straightforward and simple, making it easy to understand and fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your information in the text fields, such as "User Input" and "Financial Assistance".
3. Choose the correct answer for "Required Fields" by selecting one of the provided options.
4. Enter the date and time of the declaration in the "Declaration Date" and "Declaration Time" fields, respectively.
5. Leave a note in the "Declaration Note" field for any additional information you'd like to include.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (Text)**: A field for you to enter any relevant information about yourself that is not covered by other fields.
* **Financial Assistance (Text)**: A field for you to describe any financial assistance you are seeking or have received.
* **Required Fields (Select Multiple)**: Choose all that apply from the options provided, indicating what fields are required for your declaration.
* **Declaration Date (Date)**: Enter the date on which you are making this declaration.
* **Declaration Time (Time)**: Enter the time on which you are making this declaration.
* **Declaration Note (Note)**: A field for you to include any additional information you'd like to provide about your declaration.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your declaration.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your declaration.
