# Event Attendance Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Attendance Declaration Form is a simple form used to gather basic information about an event, including the event name, date, time, location, and contact email for attendees. This form is meant to be filled out by event attendees to provide a record of their participation and any necessary contact information for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
2. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
3. Select the date and time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
4. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Event Details" field.
5. Optionally, enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you after the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Type the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): If the event is held at a specific location, enter it here.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Briefly describe the event, such as its purpose, speakers, or notable features.
* **Event Attendance Declaration Form** (`event_attendance_declaration`, text, required): This field is not a typical field for filling out, but it is a placeholder that should not be filled out. It is an auto-generated label for the first page of the form, and you should just ignore it. 

Tips:

* Be accurate when entering event details to ensure proper record-keeping.
* If you are unable to attend, please let us know by replying to this form with a cancelation message.
* For events with multiple dates or times, separate them with commas or enter them in separate rows.
* You can use this form to report your attendance for multiple events at once, just fill out a new row for each event. 

Note: The event details field is a free text field for brief descriptions, so feel free to include any relevant details, such as speakers, topics, or sponsors. This will help the event organizers keep track of event specifics. 

Please contact us at form@gic.mx if you have any further questions or concerns about this form.
