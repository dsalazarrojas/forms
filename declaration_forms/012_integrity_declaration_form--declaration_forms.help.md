# Integrity Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Integrity Declaration Form is designed to collect information about a company's declarations and statements. This form will help users provide accurate and reliable data.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before filling it out to understand what information is required.
- Fill in your company name and declaration information on the "Company Declaration" page.
- If the company has a subsidiary, fill in the "Subsidiary Declaration" page.
- Provide information about significant shareholders on the "Significant Shareholder Declaration" page.
- Write a declaration statement in the "Declaration Statement" page.
- Enter the date the form was submitted on the "Date Submitted" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Declaration (Company Declaration)**: A text field to fill in your company's declaration information.
* **Subsidiary Declaration (Subsidiary Declaration)**: A text field to fill in your subsidiary's declaration information. (Note: If you don't have a subsidiary, you can leave this field blank.)
* **Significant Shareholder Declaration (Significant Shareholder Declaration)**: A text field to provide information about significant shareholders.
* **Declaration Statement (Declaration Statement)**: A text field to write a declaration statement.
* **Date Submitted (Date Submitted)**: A date field to enter the date the form was submitted. (Note: This field will only be visible if filled in.)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and reliable data.
- If you're unsure about any information, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- Double-check your work before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
