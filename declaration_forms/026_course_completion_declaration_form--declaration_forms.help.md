# Course Completion Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your course completion from an employee. It's used to track and document their training history and program participation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your relevant information.
- Ensure all fields are filled out, especially if they are marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **course_title** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the course you're declaring completion for.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **course_completion_date** (`course_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **training_program_title** (`training_program_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the training program you're declaring completion for.
* **declaration_date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're declaring completion of the training program.
* **training_program_name** (`training_program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training program.
* **training_program_start_date** (`training_program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the training program you're declaring completion for.
* **training_program_end_date** (`training_program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training program. Only fill out this field if the program has ended.
* **training_program_hours** (`training_program_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours of training completed as part of this program. Only fill out this field if your program hours are tracked.

## Tips
- Review your input carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your HR or training department for assistance.
