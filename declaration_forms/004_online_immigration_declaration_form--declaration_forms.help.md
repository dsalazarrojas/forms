# Online Immigration Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Immigration Declaration Form is used to declare information about a team's travel plans and other relevant details for immigration purposes. This form is intended to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth and hassle-free immigration processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your travel team by choosing the correct option from the list.
2. Enter the date of your planned travel in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/15/2022).
3. Indicate whether you have a border entry coordinator by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Choose the type of travel you are undertaking (e.g., 'Yes' for work, 'Yes' for pleasure).
5. Enter your passport number in the required format (e.g., 123456789).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Team**: Select your travel team from the list. This field is not required.
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date of your planned travel in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is not required, but it is essential for accurate processing.
* **Border Entry Coordinator**: Indicate whether you have a border entry coordinator by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is not required.
* **Travel Type**: Choose the type of travel you are undertaking (e.g., 'Yes' for work, 'Yes' for pleasure). Select all that apply.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number in the required format. This field is not required, but it is essential for identification purposes.
