# Hazardous Material Handling Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Material Handling Declaration Form is designed to collect information about the handling and declaration of hazardous materials in a business or industrial setting. This form helps to ensure that the handling and declaration of these materials are done in a safe and controlled manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name and address in the respective fields.
2. Provide the date of the declaration.
3. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the declaration.
4. Enter the contact email and phone number of the contact person.
5. Describe the material(s) being handled in the "Material Description" field.
6. Select the hazardous materials list that apply to the material(s) being handled. You can select multiple options from the list.
7. Select the list of injuries associated with handling the material(s) from the "Handling Injuries List" field. You can select multiple options from the list.
8. Select the list of procedures in place for handling the material(s) from the "Handling Procedures List" field. You can select multiple options from the list.
9. Select the list of safety measures taken to prevent injuries from the "Safety Measures List" field. You can select multiple options from the list.
10. Enter any storage conditions for the material(s) in the "Storage Conditions" field.
11. Describe the plan for disposing of the material(s) in the "Disposal Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: This field is for entering the name of your business.
* **Address**: Enter the address of your business where the hazardous materials are being handled.
* **Declaration Date**: This field is for entering the date of the declaration.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the declaration.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Material Description**: Describe the material(s) being handled, including their type, quantity, and any other relevant details.
* **Hazardous Materials List**: Select the list of hazardous materials that apply to the material(s) being handled. Options include:
	+ Hazardous Material 1
	+ Hazardous Material 2
	+ Hazardous Material 3
* **Handling Injuries List**: Select the list of injuries that may be associated with handling the material(s). Options include:
	+ Injury 1
	+ Injury 2
	+ Injury 3
* **Handling Procedures List**: Select the list of procedures in place for handling the material(s). Options include:
	+ Procedure 1
	+ Procedure 2
	+ Procedure 3
* **Safety Measures List**: Select the list of safety measures taken to prevent injuries from the material(s). Options include:
	+ Measure 1
	+ Measure 2
	+ Measure 3
* **Storage Conditions**: Enter any specific conditions for storing the material(s), such as temperature or humidity requirements.
* **Disposal Plan**: Describe the plan for disposing of the material(s), including any applicable regulations or guidelines.
