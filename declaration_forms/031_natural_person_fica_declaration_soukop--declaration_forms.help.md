# Natural Person FICA Declaration Soukop - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Person FICA Declaration Soukop form is used to collect identity and demographic information from natural persons, specifically for FICA declaration purposes. This form will help you provide the necessary data required for administrative and legal procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Select your **Birth Date** from the calendar or enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your **Social Security Number** in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birth Date**: Select or enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security number as it appears on your identification documents.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays or complications in the process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support team for assistance.
- Double-check your information for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
