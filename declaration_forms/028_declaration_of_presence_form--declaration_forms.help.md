# declaration_of_presence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "declaration_of_presence_form" is used to declare the presence of a person at a specific event or location. This form is completed by the individual in attendance to provide information about their visit, including details about their mode of transportation, location, and purpose of visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the date and time of your visit.
3. Select the mode of transportation you used to get to the location (e.g., Walk, Drive, Bike, Other).
4. If you used a different mode of transportation, provide additional details about it in the "Mode of transport other" field.
5. Choose the location(s) you visited from the provided options or enter a custom location in the "Other location" field.
6. Select the purpose of your visit from the provided options or enter a custom purpose in the "Other purpose of visit" field.
7. Sign the form with your signature to confirm your presence.
