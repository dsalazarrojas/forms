# Conflict Of Interest Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conflict Of Interest Declaration Form is used to gather information about potential conflicts of interest that may impact your professional judgment and objectivity in your role. This includes relationships with suppliers, customers, competitors, business partners, and family members.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete this form honestly and accurately, as it is a legal requirement.
2.  If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
3.  If you have any conflicts or interests that are not listed on this form, please include them in the "Any Other Potential Conflicts" section.
4.  Please sign and date the form to confirm your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`): Enter the date you are completing this form.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee identification number.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter your current job title.
*   **Department** (`department`): Enter your department or division.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
*   **Type of Declaration** (`declaration_type`): Select the reason for this declaration (Annual, New Employment, Change in Circumstances, or Ad Hoc).
*   **Do You Have Outside Business Interests?** (`outside_business_interests`): Answer "True" if you have any business ownership or partnership outside the company.
*   **Outside Business Details** (`outside_business_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the nature of your business interest.
*   **Do You Have Outside Employment?** (`outside_employment`): Answer "True" if you have any secondary employment or consulting outside the company.
*   **Outside Employment Details** (`outside_employment_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe your employer and the nature of your work.
*   **Do You Have Relationship with Competitors?** (`competitor_relationship`): Answer "True" if you have any connection to competing organizations.
*   **Competitor Relationship Details** (`competitor_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the nature of your relationship.
*   **Do You Have Interest in Suppliers or Customers?** (`supplier_customer_interest`): Answer "True" if you have financial interest in business partners.
*   **Supplier or Customer Interest Details** (`supplier_customer_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the nature and extent of your interest.
*   **Do You Hold Board Positions?** (`board_positions`): Answer "True" if you have any board of directors memberships.
*   **Board Position Details** (`board_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, list the organizations and your role.
*   **Do Family Members Have Potential Conflicts?** (`family_conflicts`): Answer "True" if immediate family members have conflicts.
*   **Family Conflict Details** (`family_conflict_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the circumstances.
*   **Have You Received Significant Gifts?** (`gifts_received`): Answer "True" if you have received gifts from vendors, clients, or partners over $100.
*   **Gifts Received Details** (`gifts_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the gifts and source.
*   **Have You Shared Confidential Information?** (`confidential_information`): Answer "True" if you have disclosed company confidential data.
*   **Confidential Information Details** (`confidential_info_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, explain the circumstances.
*   **Have You Used Company Resources for Personal Gain?** (`company_resources_personal_use`): Answer "True" if you have used company assets for personal benefit.
*   **Company Resources Use Details** (`resources_details`): If you answered "True" to the previous question, describe the circumstances.
*   **Any Other Potential Conflicts?** (`other_conflicts`): If you have any other conflicts or interests not listed on this form, describe them.
*   **I Understand the Conflict of Interest Policy?** (`understanding_policy`): Answer "True" if you understand and agree to the conflict of interest policy.
*   **I Declare This Information is True and Complete?** (`information_accuracy`): Answer "Yes - I Confirm" if the information is accurate and complete.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Manager Review Status** (`manager_review`): For manager use only, this field is used to track the review status of this form.
