# Film Crew COVID 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for film crew members to declare their health status and provide information about their travel history, medical conditions, and potential exposure to COVID-19. This form helps the production team to assess potential risks and take necessary precautions to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Answer the question "Have you been exposed to COVID-19?".
6. Provide any relevant travel history.
7. Describe any exposure history.
8. If you have any medical conditions, select them from the list provided.
9. If you have a medical certificate, indicate whether you have it.
10. Decide whether to upload a medical certificate file.
11. Confirm your willingness to submit the declaration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Have you been exposed to COVID-19? (select_one)**: Answer with "No" if you have not been exposed to COVID-19, "Yes - Mild" if you have mild symptoms, or "Yes - Severe" if you have severe symptoms.
* **Travel History (text)**: Describe any travel history that may be relevant to your declaration.
* **Exposure History (text)**: Describe any exposure history that may be relevant to your declaration.
* **Medical Condition (select_multiple)**: Select any medical conditions you have from the list provided (None, Respiratory issues, Heart issues, Diabetes, Other).
* **Do you have a medical certificate? (select_one)**: Answer with "No" if you do not have a medical certificate, or "Yes" if you do.
* **Upload Medical Certificate (select_multiple)**: Decide whether to upload a medical certificate file.
* **Upload Certificate File (select_multiple)**: If you have decided to upload a medical certificate file, select "Yes" to proceed.
* **Submit (select_one)**: Confirm your willingness to submit the declaration form by selecting "Yes" or "No".
