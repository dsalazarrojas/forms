# Society Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Society Declaration Form is a document used to collect information from society members regarding their details, including their authorized representatives and confirmation of their participation. The form is designed to ensure the accuracy of the submitted information and to help the relevant authorities in making necessary decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. If you need help or have questions, contact the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Society Details (society_details)** (`text`, Optional): A brief description of the society, such as its name, purpose, or objectives.
* **Society Abbreviation (society_abbreviation)** (`text`, Optional): A brief abbreviation or acronym of the society's name.
* **Authorized Representatives (authorized_representatives)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Select one or more authorized representatives of the society from the given options.
* **Society Confirmation (society_confirmation)** (`select_one`, Optional): Confirm your participation in the society by selecting one of the given options.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, Optional): A contact email address for the society or a representative.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, Optional): A contact phone number for the society or a representative.
* **Note (note)** (`note`, Optional): A field for any additional comments or notes regarding the society.
* **Submit (submit)** (`text`, Optional): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Review each field before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
