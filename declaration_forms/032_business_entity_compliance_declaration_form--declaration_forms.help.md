# Business Entity Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help business entities declare their compliance status with various laws and regulations. This form ensures the entity is operating within the legal framework and highlights areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the business entity in the "Entity Name" field.
2. Select the type of business structure from the "Entity Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the federal tax ID number in the "Tax ID" field.
4. Enter the address of the principal place of business in the "Business Address" field.
5. Select the compliance status for each of the following: Tax Compliance, Regulatory Compliance, Financial Reporting Compliance, Employment Law Compliance, Environmental Compliance, and Safety Compliance. Choose "Fully Compliant", "Substantially Compliant", "Not Compliant" or "Not Applicable" as needed.
6. If you have areas of non-compliance, describe them in the "Areas of Non-Compliance" field.
7. Provide a remediation plan to address any non-compliance issues in the "Remediation Plan" field.
8. Select the date when compliance will be achieved in the "Remediation Timeline" field (optional).
9. Finally, confirm that you have read and understand the potential penalties for making a false declaration in the "Penalties Disclaimar" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Entity Name** (`entity_name`, required, text): Enter the name of the business entity.
- **Entity Type** (`entity_type`, required, select_one): Choose the type of business structure from the dropdown menu.
- **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, required, text): Enter the federal tax ID number of the entity.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`, required, text): Enter the address of the principal place of business.
- **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, required, date): Enter the date of this declaration.
- **Declaring Officer** (`declaring_officer`, required, text): Enter the name of the authorized person making the declaration.
- **Tax Compliance** (`tax_compliance`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for tax compliance.
- **Regulatory Compliance** (`regulatory_compliance`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for regulatory compliance.
- **Financial Reporting Compliance** (`financial_reporting`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for financial reporting.
- **Employment Law Compliance** (`employee_compliance`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for employment law.
- **Environmental Compliance** (`environmental_compliance`, optional, select_one): Select the compliance status for environmental regulations (if applicable).
- **Health and Safety Compliance** (`safety_compliance`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for health and safety standards.
- **Licensing Requirements** (`licensing`, required, select_one): Select the compliance status for licensing requirements.
- **Areas of Non-Compliance** (`non_compliant_areas`, optional, text): If you have areas of non-compliance, describe them in this field.
- **Remediation Plan** (`remediation_plan`, optional, text): Provide a plan to address any non-compliance issues.
- **Remediation Timeline** (`remediation_timeline`, optional, date): Enter the date when compliance will be achieved (if applicable).
- **I Declare That This Entity is in Compliance** (`declare_compliance`, required, select_one): Confirm that you have made a true declaration.
- **False Declaration Penalties Understood** (`penalties_disclaimer`, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand the penalties for making a false declaration.
