# Student Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who are traveling for official business or conferences. Please ensure that you are eligible to complete this form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), as accurately as possible.
*   Choose the correct options for each field from the provided choices.
*   Double-check your answers to ensure they are complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Travel Declaration Form** (`student_travel_declaration_form`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for your travel from the provided options. Please choose only one option that accurately reflects your travel purpose.
*   **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, text, optional): Enter the dates of your travel in the format "From DD/MM/YY to DD/MM/YY".
*   **Student Information** (`student_info`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information about yourself as a student. Please use this field if you need to provide extra details about your travel.
*   **Country Visited** (`country_visited`, select_one, optional): Choose the country you are visiting from the provided options.
*   **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current student status from the options.
*   **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your travel in a brief and clear statement.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you need to provide any contact information, please enter it here. This could be your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact method.

## Tips
*   Always ensure that your answers are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Double-check your form for any errors before submitting it.
