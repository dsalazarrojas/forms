# Policy Acceptance Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are accepting a policy. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities and obligations under the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address in the required fields.
2. If you are acting on behalf of a client, enter their name.
3. Attach the relevant policy document and ensure it is properly signed.
4. Specify the date and time of your acceptance of the policy.
5. Indicate if there are any risks associated with the policy.
6. Sign and date the declaration to confirm your understanding of the policy.
7. Enter your signature and other relevant details.
8. Provide the name and contact details of the attorney who is representing you or your client.
9. Add any additional notes about the declaration if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name or the name of the client you are acting on behalf of.
* **Client Address**: Enter your address or the address of the client you are representing.
* **Policy Document**: Attach a copy of the policy document that you are accepting.
* **Policy Acceptance Date and Time**: Specify the date and time you are accepting the policy.
* **Risks Associated with Policy**: If there are any risks associated with the policy, describe them briefly.
* **Declaration Is Signed**: Indicate if you have signed the declaration in good faith.
* **Declaration Signature Date and Time**: Date and time you signed the declaration.
* **Declaration Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your understanding of the policy.
* **Attorney Name**: Enter the name of the attorney who is representing you or your client.
* **Attorney Title**: Enter the title of the attorney (optional).
* **Attorney Firm**: Enter the name of the attorney's firm (optional).
* **Attorney Address**: Enter the address of the attorney's firm (optional).
* **Declaration Notes**: Add any additional notes about the declaration if necessary.
* **Attesting To**: Enter your name or the name of the client you are acting on behalf of.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the declaration.

Please note that all fields marked as 'required' are mandatory and must be completed to complete the form successfully. 

Tips:
* Ensure that all fields are completed correctly before submitting the form.
* Attach a copy of the policy document and ensure it is properly signed.
* Review the risks associated with the policy carefully to ensure you understand your responsibilities.
* Sign and date the declaration to confirm your understanding of the policy.
* Provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the declaration is valid and legally binding.
