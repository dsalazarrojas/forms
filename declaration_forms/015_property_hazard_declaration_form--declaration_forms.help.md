# Property Hazard Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Hazard Declaration Form is designed to report and document hazards present on a property. This form is used to identify potential risks to occupants, visitors, and the environment, and to provide information necessary for mitigating or addressing the hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the Property Address: Enter the address of the property where the hazard is located.
2. Select Property Type: Choose the type of property (Apartment, House, Office, or Other).
3. Enter Property Name: Provide the name of the property.
4. Enter Property Owner: Name of the property owner.
5. Enter Property Manager: Name of the person responsible for managing the property.
6. Enter Facility: Name of the facility or organization managing the property.
7. Select Hazard Type: Choose one or more hazards present (Fire, Gas, Electrical, Water, or Other).
8. Describe the Hazard Location: Provide a clear description of where the hazard is located within the property.
9. Describe the Hazard: Provide a detailed description of the hazard.
10. Submit Date and Time: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
11. Add Comments (optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the hazard.
12. Attach Relevant Files (optional): Upload any relevant attachments or documents related to the hazard.
13. Enter Contact Information: Enter the contact information for the person submitting the form.
14. Enter Additional Information: Provide any additional information about the hazard or property.
15. Specify Submission User: Choose who submitted the form (Owner, Manager, or Facility Team).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property where the hazard is located. This is the physical address of the property.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are declaring the hazard for, such as an apartment, house, office, or other.
* **Property Name**: Enter the name of the property.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Manager**: Enter the name of the person responsible for managing the property.
* **Facility**: Enter the name of the facility or organization managing the property.
* **Hazard Type**: Choose one or more hazards present, such as fire, gas, electrical, or water.
* **Hazard Location**: Describe where the hazard is located within the property.
* **Hazard Description**: Provide a detailed description of the hazard.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the hazard.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents related to the hazard.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about the hazard or property.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form being submitted.
* **Submission User**: Choose who submitted the form (Owner, Manager, or Facility Team).
* **Submission Date (UTC)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted in UTC time zone.
* **IP**: Enter the IP address of the device used to submit the form.
* **Submission User Agent**: Enter the user agent string of the device used to submit the form.
* **Browser**: Enter the browser used to submit the form.
* **Operating System**: Enter the operating system of the device used to submit the form.
* **Device**: Enter the type of device used to submit the form.
* **User Agent Details**: Enter any additional details about the device used to submit the form.
