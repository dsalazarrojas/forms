# COVID 19 Travel Declaration Form For Visitors - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your travel history and health status as a visitor. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe and enjoyable stay at your destination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, using the options provided for each question.
3. Make sure to provide your signature and date at the end of the form.
