# Lab Equipment Use Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by researchers, staff, and users to declare the use and status of lab equipment. This form ensures that equipment is properly maintained, and that users are aware of safety precautions and guidelines for handling and using the equipment.


## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your answers to each section. 
2. If you selected "Yes" to using equipment, please fill in the equipment list with the equipment you used. 
3. If you declared that the equipment was used, you must also indicate the date you used the equipment. 
4. Write your declaration, making sure to include any safety precautions or conditions you experienced while using the equipment. 
5. Sign the declaration to indicate that you have read, understand, and agreed to the form's guidelines.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, select_multiple, optional): List the equipment you used for your research or work. If you selected "No" to using equipment, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date of Use** (`date_of_use`, date, optional): If you used equipment, please enter the date you used it.
* **User Declaration** (`user_declaration`, text, required): Write a detailed declaration of your use of equipment, including any safety precautions or conditions you experienced.
* **Laboratory Supervision** (`laboratory_supervision`, select_one, optional): If you are supervised by a principal investigator or laboratory supervisor, indicate "Yes" here. If you are not, indicate "No".
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the condition of the equipment at the time of use. Choose one or more from the options: Good, Slightly Damaged, Damaged, or Not Working.
* **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, optional): If you experienced any safety precautions or issues while using the equipment, please list them here.
* **Declaration Signature** (`declaration_signature`, note, optional): This is a signature field where you can sign to indicate that you have read, understand, and agreed to the form's guidelines.
* **Equipment Return** (`equipment_return`, text, optional): If you need to return the equipment, please indicate here the reason for return.
