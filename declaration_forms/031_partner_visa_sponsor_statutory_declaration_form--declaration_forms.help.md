# Partner Visa Sponsor Statutory Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a partner's visa sponsorship and declaration. It captures the necessary information from both the partner and the sponsor, including partner and sponsor details, declaration, and signature. The form is intended for official use only, and all information provided will be treated as confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the Partner Details section with the partner's information.
3. Fill in the Sponsor Details section with the sponsor's information.
4. Provide sponsor identification in the Sponsor Identification section.
5. Read and agree to the declaration in the Declaration section.
6. Sign the Signatures section with your signature.
7. Enter the Date of Declaration and Declaration Time in their respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Controls** (`form_control`, `note`, optional):
	+ This section is for any additional information or comments related to the form. You can add any notes or comments you want to include in this section.
* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the partner's name, address, and any other relevant details in this section.
* **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the sponsor's name, address, and any other relevant details in this section.
* **Sponsor Identification** (`sponsor_identification`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide identification information for the sponsor, such as a passport number or ID card number.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, `text`, optional):
	+ Read the declaration carefully and agree to it by signing in the Signatures section.
* **Date of Declaration** (`date_of_declaration`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date on which the declaration is made.
* **Signatures** (`signature`, `text`, optional):
	+ Sign the declaration in this section with your signature.
* **Partner Signature** (`partner_signature`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the number of the partner's signature.
* **Sponsor Signature** (`sponsor_signature`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the number of the sponsor's signature.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date on which the declaration is made.
* **Declaration Time** (`declaration_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time on which the declaration is made.
