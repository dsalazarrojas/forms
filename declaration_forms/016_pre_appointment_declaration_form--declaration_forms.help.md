# pre_appointment_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-appointment declaration form is used to collect information from patients before an appointment. This form gathers details about the patient's medical history, travel history, symptoms, and other relevant information to help healthcare professionals prepare for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Patient ID, which is usually provided by your healthcare provider.
2. Provide your name as it should be listed on your medical records.
3. Enter the date of your appointment.
4. Specify the time of your appointment.
5. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
6. Provide any travel history, including recent travels.
7. List any contact history, including recent contacts with other people.
8. Check if you have any known medical conditions.
9. Select any current medications you are taking.
10. Indicate any allergies you have.
11. Choose any medical devices you use.
12. List any medical conditions you may have but are not aware of.
13. Select any contact preferences for your appointment.
14. Indicate any travel restrictions you have.
15. Describe any other symptoms you are experiencing.
16. List any other medical history you may have.
17. Describe any other medical devices you use.
18. List any other symptoms you are experiencing.
19. Describe any other contacts you have had recently.
20. List any other medical history you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`Patient ID`, text, required): Enter your unique Patient ID, usually provided by your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you'd like it to be listed on your medical records.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your appointment, usually provided by your healthcare provider.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your appointment, usually provided by your healthcare provider.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing or have experienced recently.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required): List any travel you've had recently, including any destinations or locations you've visited.
* **Contact History** (`contact_history`, text, required): List any recent contacts with other people, such as family members or friends.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have any known medical conditions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_one, required): Select any current medications you are taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required): Indicate any allergies you have.
* **Medical Devices** (`medical_devices`, select_multiple, required): Choose any medical devices you use, such as hearing aids or oxygen tanks.
* **Medical Conditions Other** (`medical_history_other`, text, required): List any other medical conditions you may have that are not listed above.
* **Medical Devices Other** (`medical_devices_other`, select_multiple, required): Select any other medical devices you use.
* **Symptoms Other 1** (`symptoms_other_1`, text, required): Describe any other symptoms you are experiencing.

* **Travel History Other** (`travel_history_other`, text, required): List any other travel history you may have.
* **Contact History Other** (`contact_history_other`, text, required): List any other contacts you have had recently.
