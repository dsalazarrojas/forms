# Coronavirus Self Declaration Borthwick Castle (Travel Allowed) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for travelers to declare their travel history and other relevant information in order for the relevant authorities to assess the risk of potential coronavirus transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your travel details, including the destinations you have visited or intend to visit.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Add any additional notes or comments you think are relevant to your travel history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_details** (`travel_details`, text, required: no): Please provide a brief description of your travel history, including the places you have visited or intend to visit.
* **declaration** (`declaration`, text, required: no): This field is intended to be a declaration of your travel history. Please type out a clear and accurate account of your travels.
* **contact** (`contact`, text, required: no): Please provide your contact information, including your name.
* **travel_date** (`travel_date`, date, required: no): If you have traveled recently, please provide the date of your travel.
* **travel_destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required: no): Please specify the destination(s) you have visited or intend to visit.
* **travel_reason** (`travel_reason`, text, required: no): Please provide the reason for your travel.
* **return_date** (`return_date`, date, required: no): If you have recently returned from travel, please provide the date of your return.
* **return_destination** (`return_destination`, text, required: no): Please specify the destination(s) you returned from.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: no): Please provide your phone number.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, text, required: no): Please provide your email address.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, required: no): Please add any additional comments or information you think are relevant to your travel history.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
* This form is designed to be completed once, and you will not need to fill it out multiple times.
