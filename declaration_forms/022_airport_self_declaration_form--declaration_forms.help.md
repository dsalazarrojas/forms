# Airport Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from travelers for airport security and immigration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your traveler full name as it appears on your passport.
2. Enter your passport or national ID number.
3. Select your nationality.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Move on to the next section "Flight and Travel Details."
7. Select the relevant option for your flight number.
8. Enter the name of the airline you are traveling with.
9. Enter the departure and arrival airport codes (e.g., IAD for Washington Dulles).
10. Choose the purpose of your travel from the available options.
11. If you have any additional comments or declarations, please add them in the comments section.
12. Finally, confirm that all information provided is accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your passport. Please enter your name exactly as it is printed on your passport.
* **Passport or National ID Number**: This is the number on your passport or national ID. Ensure you have this number easily accessible.
* **Nationality**: Select your country of origin or nationality.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted by airport officials, if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Flight Number**: Select the type of flight you are taking (e.g., international, domestic, etc.).
* **Airline Name**: Enter the name of the airline you are traveling with.
* **Departure Airport**: Enter the code of the airport you are departing from (e.g., IAD for Washington Dulles).
* **Arrival Airport**: Enter the code of the airport you are arriving at.
* **Purpose of Travel**: Select the reason for your travel (e.g., Business, Tourism, etc.).
* **Additional Declaration Comments**: If you need to provide any additional information, use this field to explain.
* **I Declare**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate and true. Providing false information can lead to legal actions.
