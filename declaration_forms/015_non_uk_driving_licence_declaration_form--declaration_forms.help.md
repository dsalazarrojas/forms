# Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a non-UK driving licence status. If you are a user who does not hold a UK driving licence, please use this form to declare your status for internal purposes such as insurance or administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Make sure to complete all necessary fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Form** (`1`, text, required): This is the main field where you will declare your non-UK driving licence status.
- **Declaration Form** (`2`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' if you do not hold a UK driving licence.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Form Fields 1** (`3`, number, optional): This field is not currently used. Please ignore it.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Form Fields 2** (`4`, number, optional): This field is not currently used. Please ignore it.
- **Date of Last Non-UK Driving Licence Renewal** (`5`, date, optional): If you have renewed your non-UK driving licence, please enter the date of renewal.
- **Time of Last Non-UK Driving Licence Renewal** (`6`, time, optional): This field is not currently used. Please ignore it.
- **Contact Email** (`7`, email, optional): Please enter your email address where we can contact you regarding your non-UK driving licence declaration.
- **Other Non-UK Driving Licence Details** (`8`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your non-UK driving licence.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Options 1** (`9`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply:
    - 'Yes'
    - 'No'
    - This field is for declaring any additional non-UK driving licence details.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Options 2** (`10`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have any additional non-UK driving licence details.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Options 3** (`11`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for declaring any additional non-UK driving licence details.
- **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Options 4** (`12`, select_one, optional): This field is not currently used. Please ignore it.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us.
- We will review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains accurate and relevant.
