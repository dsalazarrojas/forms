# Inheritance Rights Waiver Affidavit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inheritance Rights Waiver Affidavit" form is used to waive certain rights related to an estate. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to avoid any potential issues or disputes related to the estate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of declaration form you are using in the "Declaration Forms" section.
2. Select the type of legal form you are using in the "Legal Forms" section.
3. Enter any additional information about the estate settlement in the "Estate Settlement" section.
4. Indicate if there are any waivers related to the estate in the "Waivers" section.
5. Enter the collection date and time in the "Collection Information" section.
6. Provide your information as the collector in the "Collector's Information" section.
7. Enter the estate owner's information in the "Estate Information" section.
8. Enter any other information related to the declaration in the "Other Information" section.
9. Provide the information of the person submitting the declaration in the "Declaration Submitter's Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Declaration Forms**: (`declaraforms`, select_multiple) - Choose the type of declaration form you are using (Law Offices, Estate Administrators, Families, or Other).
* **Legal Forms**: (`legalforms`, select_multiple) - Select the type of legal form you are using (Affidavit, Agreement, Document, or Other).
* **Estate Settlement**: (`estate_settlement`, text) - Enter any additional information about the estate settlement (optional).
* **Waivers**: (`waiverns`, select_one) - Indicate if there are any waivers related to the estate (Yes or No).
* **Collection Information**: (`collatinformation`, date) - Enter the collection date and time of the declaration.
* **Collector's Name**: (`collatname`, text) - Enter your name as the collector.
* **Collector's Address**: (`collataddress`, text) - Enter your address as the collector.
* **Collector's Phone**: (`collatphone`, text) - Enter your phone number as the collector.
* **Collector's Email**: (`collatemail`, email) - Enter your email as the collector.
* **Estate Owner's Name**: (`estateownername`, text) - Enter the estate owner's name.
* **Estate Address**: (`estateaddressest`, text) - Enter the estate's address.
* **Estate Owner's Phone**: (`estateownerphonen`, text) - Enter the estate owner's phone number (optional).
* **Estate Owner's Email**: (`estateowneremail`, email) - Enter the estate owner's email (optional).
* **Other Information**: (`otherinform`, text) - Enter any other information related to the declaration (optional).
* **Other's Name**: (`othername`, text) - Enter the name of other person involved (optional).
* **Other's Phone**: (`otherphone`, text) - Enter the phone number of other person involved (optional).
* **Other's Email**: (`otheremail`, email) - Enter the email of other person involved (optional).
* **Declaration Submitted**: (`declarationsubmitted`, date) - Enter the date and time the declaration was submitted.
* **Declaration Submitter's Name**: (`declaformsubmittername`, text) - Enter the name of the person who submitted the declaration.
* **Declaration Submitter's Phone**: (`declaformsubmitterphonen`, text) - Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the declaration.
* **Declaration Submitter's Email**: (`declaformsubmitteremail`, email) - Enter the email of the person who submitted the declaration.
* **Declaration Signed By**: (`declarationsignedby`, text) - Enter the name of the person who signed the declaration.
* **Declaration Date**: (`declaformdate`, date) - Enter the date the declaration was signed.
* **Declaration Time**: (`declaformtime`, time) - Enter the time the declaration was signed.
