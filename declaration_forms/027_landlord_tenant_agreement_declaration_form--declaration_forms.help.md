# Landlord Tenant Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a rental agreement between a landlord and tenant. It outlines the terms of the rental, including lease length, rent amount, and payment schedule. The form is completed by both the landlord and tenant to ensure a clear understanding of the rental terms and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Rental Terms page with the relevant details about the rental, including lease length, rent amount, and payment schedule.
2.  On the Tenant Details page, enter the tenant's information, including their name and contact information.
3.  On the Landlord Details page, enter the landlord's information, including their name and contact information.
4.  On the Lease Length page, select the length of the lease.
5.  On the Move-in Date page, enter the date the tenant will move in.
6.  On the Rent Frequency page, enter how often the rent is paid.
7.  On the Rent Due Date page, enter the date the rent is due.
8.  On the Security Deposit page, enter the security deposit amount.
9.  On the Lease Start Date page, enter the start date of the lease.
10. On the Lease End Date page, enter the end date of the lease.
11. On the Lease Terms page, enter any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
12. On the Tenant Signature page, select the tenant's signature option.
13. On the Landlord Signature page, select the landlord's signature option.
14. On the Lease Date page, enter the date the lease is signed.
15. On the Move-in Date page, enter the date the tenant moves in.
16. On the Rent Frequency page, enter how often the rent is paid.
17. On the Rent Due Date page, enter the date the rent is due.
18. On the Security Deposit Return page, enter the date the security deposit is returned.
19. On the Lease End Date page, enter the end date of the lease.
20. On the Lease Extension page, enter any lease extension terms or conditions.
