# Boat Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about boat ownership from the declarant, ensuring that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details as the declarant.
2. Enter your boat's details, including make, model, year, and length.
3. Select the correct boat type and ownership type.
4. Confirm your ownership percentage and declaration statement.
5. Enter your signature and date of declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Declarant Name**: Enter your full legal name as the owner of the boat. 
* **Declarant Email**: Enter your contact email address. 
* **Declarant Phone**: Enter your contact phone number. 
* **Street Address**: Enter your mailing address. 
* **City**: Enter the city where the boat is registered. 
* **State**: Enter the state where the boat is registered. 
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code where the boat is registered. 
* **Boat Make**: Enter the manufacturer's name of the boat. 
* **Boat Model**: Enter the model name of the boat. 
* **Boat Year**: Enter the year of manufacture of the boat. 
* **Boat Hull ID (HIN)**: Enter the hull identification number of the boat. 
* **Boat Length**: Enter the length of the boat in feet. 
* **Boat Type**: Select the correct category of the boat (e.g., Bowrider, Cabin Cruiser, etc.). 
* **Boat Color**: Enter the primary color of the boat (optional). 
* **Engine Make**: Enter the manufacturer's name of the engine (optional). 
* **Engine Model**: Enter the model name of the engine (optional). 
* **Engine Serial Number**: Enter the engine identification number (optional). 
* **Ownership Type**: Select the type of ownership (e.g., Sole Owner, Joint Owners, etc.). 
* **Co-Owner Name**: Enter the name of the co-owner (if applicable). 
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you acquired the boat. 
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the amount paid for the boat (optional). 
* **Title Number**: Enter the title number (if titled). 
* **Registration Number**: Enter the current registration number (if registered). 
* **Lien Holder**: Enter the name of the lien holder (if there is a loan). 
* **Ownership Percentage**: Select your percentage of ownership. 
* **Declaration Statement**: Confirm that you are the legal owner of the boat. 
* **Declarant Signature**: Enter your full name as signature. 
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the declaration. 
* **Notary Required**: Check if notarization is required (optional).
