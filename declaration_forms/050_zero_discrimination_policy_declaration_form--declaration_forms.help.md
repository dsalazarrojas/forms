# zero_discrimination_policy_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the zero discrimination policy in your organization. It is a crucial document that ensures a fair and inclusive environment for all employees, clients, or customers. By completing this form, you are acknowledging that you understand and will adhere to this policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the zero discrimination policy carefully to ensure you understand its contents.
2.  Answer "yes" to "i_agree" to confirm that you agree with the policy.
3.  In "i_declare", briefly describe in your own words what you declare about discrimination.
4.  Choose one or more options in "i_would_prefer" to indicate your preferred methods or channels for receiving information and updates.
5.  Select the option that best describes your understanding of the policy in "i_understand".
6.  Choose one or more options in "i_will" to indicate your willingness to adhere to the policy.
7.  Select the option that best describes your level of understanding in "i_have_understood".
8.  Finally, choose the option that best describes when you will have understood the policy in "i_will_have_understood".
9.  In "comments", you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **i_agree (Number)**: Confirm that you agree with the zero discrimination policy.
* **i_declare (Text)**: Briefly describe what you declare about discrimination.
* **do_prefer (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more options for your preferred methods or channels for receiving information and updates.
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Text message
	+ Option 3: In-app notification
* **do_understand (Select One)**: Select the option that best describes your understanding of the policy.
	+ Option 1: I understand the policy.
	+ Option 2: I have some understanding.
	+ Option 3: I do not understand the policy.
* **do_will (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more options to indicate your willingness to adhere to the policy.
	+ Option 1: Yes, I will follow the policy.
	+ Option 2: Yes, I will try to follow the policy.
	+ Option 3: No, I will not follow the policy.
* **have_understood (Select One)**: Select the option that best describes your level of understanding.
	+ Option 1: I have fully understood the policy.
	+ Option 2: I have partially understood the policy.
	+ Option 3: I have not understood the policy.
* **will_have_understood (Select One)**: Select the option that best describes when you will have understood the policy.
	+ Option 1: I have already understood the policy.
	+ Option 2: I will understand the policy by [insert date].
	+ Option 3: I am still unclear about the policy.
* **comments (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the policy.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by individuals who will be adhering to the zero discrimination policy. It is a critical document that helps ensure a fair and inclusive environment for all.
