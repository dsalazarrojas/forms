# Payment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Declaration Form is a tool used for declaring payments and transactions. It serves as a record-keeping mechanism for tracking financial transactions, providing a clear overview of payments made and received.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the payment method used for the transaction (Payment Method).
2. Indicate whether the transaction is associated with a specific account type (Account Type).
3. Enter the date of the payment declaration (Declaration Date).
4. Enter the amount of the payment (Amount).
5. Confirm the payment amount (Payment Amount).
6. Determine if the transaction is active or inactive (Payment Status).
7. Select whether the transaction status is active or inactive (Transaction Status).
8. Identify the type of transaction (Transaction Type).
9. Enter the amount of the transaction (Transaction Amount).
10. Indicate whether the transaction currency is involved (Transaction Currency).
11. Enter any payment details (Payment Details).
12. Enter the payment date (Payment Date).
13. Choose whether the second account type is relevant (Account Type 2).
14. Enter account details for the second account (Account Details 2).
15. Determine the payment status for the second account (Payment Status 2).
16. Enter the payment amount for the second account (Payment Amount 2).
17. Enter the declaration date for the second account (Declaration Date 2).
18. Enter any transaction details (Transaction Details).
19. Choose whether the payment currency is involved (Payment Currency).
20. Choose whether the third account type is relevant (Account Type 3).
21. Enter account details for the third account (Account Details 2).
22. Enter the payment ID (Payment ID).
23. Enter declaration details (Declaration Details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, required): Select the method used for the payment.
* **Account Type** (account_type, select_one, required): Determine whether the transaction is associated with a specific account type.
* **Declaration Date** (declaration_date, date, required): Enter the date of the payment declaration.
* **Amount** (amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required): Confirm the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status, select_one, required): Determine if the transaction is active or inactive.
* **Transaction Status** (transaction_status, select_one, required): Select whether the transaction status is active or inactive.
* **Transaction Type** (transaction_type, select_multiple, required): Identify the type of transaction.
* **Transaction Amount** (transaction_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Transaction Currency** (transaction_currency, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the transaction currency is involved.
* **Transaction Details** (transaction_details, note, required): Enter any transaction details.
* **Payment Date** (payment_date, date, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Account Type 2** (account_type_2, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the second account type is relevant.
* **Account Details 2** (account_details_2, note, required): Enter account details for the second account.
* **Payment Status 2** (payment_status_2, select_one, required): Determine the payment status for the second account.
* **Payment Amount 2** (payment_amount_2, number, required): Enter the payment amount for the second account.
* **Declaration Date 2** (declaration_date_2, date, required): Enter the declaration date for the second account.
* **Transaction Details** (transaction_details, note, required): Enter any transaction details.
* **Payment Currency** (payment_currency, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the payment currency is involved.
* **Account Type 3** (account_type_3, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the third account type is relevant.
* **Account Details 2** (account_details_2, note, required): Enter account details for the third account.
* **Payment ID** (payment_id, select_one, required): Enter the payment ID.
* **Declaration Details** (declaration_details, note, required): Enter declaration details.

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid errors.
- Ensure you're authorized to access and complete this form.
- Review your answers for clarity and correctness before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or a trusted resource.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency.
- Keep your declaration up to date to maintain a clear financial record.
- Use this form for its intended purpose only; avoid using it for personal transactions.
