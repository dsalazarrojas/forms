# Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Declaration Form is used to collect information from travelers about their travel plans and health status. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded properly for travel purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your traveler information, including your name, travel dates, and contact information.
2. Select your current health status, including any symptoms or medical conditions you may have.
3. Provide travel details, such as your destination and the purpose of your travel.
4. Choose your returning vehicle and returning mode.
5. Enter your returning date and time.
6. Provide any additional travel documents or medical certificates.
7. Fill in any medical history or travel status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Information** (`traveler_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect basic information about the traveler, including their name and contact details.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the dates of your travel.
* **Current Health Status** (`health_status`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select any symptoms or medical conditions you may have.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about your travel.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide contact information in case of emergencies.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any travel documents or certificates you may have.
* **Medical Certificates** (`medical_certificates`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any medical certificates or documents.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any travel history or relevant information.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, required/optional): This field is used to specify the purpose of your travel.
* **Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the destination of your travel.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the number of days you will stay.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the frequency of your travel.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to choose the type of travel (Work, Leisure, Business, or Others).
* **Health Certificates** (`health_certificates`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any health certificates or documents.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide contact information in case of emergencies.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any medical history or relevant information.
* **Travel Status** (`travel_status`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the status of your travel.
* **Travel Documents Issued By** (`travel_documents_issued_by`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the issuer of your travel documents.
* **Travel Documents Issued Date** (`travel_documents_issued_date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of your travel documents.
* **Returning Date** (`travel_returning_date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the returning date of your travel.
* **Returning Time** (`travel_returning_time`, time, required/optional): This field is used to enter the returning time of your travel.
* **Returning Mode** (`travel_returning_mode`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the mode of return travel (Car, Bus, Train, or Others).
* **Returning Vehicle** (`travel_returning_vehicle`, select_one, required): This field is used to choose the vehicle you will return with.
* **Returning Location** (`travel_returning_location`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the location where you will return.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional remarks or comments about your travel.

Note: Some fields may be marked as required/optional, depending on the specific requirements of the airline or authority. Please fill in all required fields according to your travel information and circumstances.
