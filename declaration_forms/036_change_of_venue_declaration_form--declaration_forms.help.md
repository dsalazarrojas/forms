# Change Of Venue Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change Of Venue Declaration Form is used to notify the relevant authorities of a change in venue for an event. This form helps ensure that the event organizers and attendees are informed and prepared for the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the declaration date field with the date of submission.
2. Enter the name of the event in the event name field.
3. Provide the original date of the event in the original event date field.
4. Enter the name of the person responsible for the event in the event organizer name field.
5. Enter the contact information of the event organizer in the organizer contact information field.
6. Enter the name of the original venue in the original venue name field.
7. Enter the address of the original venue in the original venue address field.
8. Enter the contact information of the original venue in the original venue contact field.
9. If applicable, enter the original capacity of the venue in the original venue capacity field.
10. Enter the reason for the change of venue in the reason for venue change field.
11. Enter the name of the new venue in the new venue name field.
12. Enter the address of the new venue in the new venue address field.
13. Enter the contact information of the new venue in the new venue contact field.
14. If applicable, enter the new capacity of the venue in the new venue capacity field.
15. Enter the date when the venue change takes effect in the change effective date field.
16. Indicate if the parking situation changed in the parking situation changed field.
17. Indicate if the accessibility features changed in the accessibility features changed field.
18. Indicate if the attendees have been notified of the change in the attendees have been notified field.
19. If applicable, indicate if the insurance and permits were updated in the insurance and permits updated field.
20. Finally, enter any additional comments or information in the additional notes or comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declaration Date** (required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Event Name** (required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Original Event Date** (required): Enter the date of the original event schedule.
* **Event Organizer Name** (required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Event Organizer Contact Information** (required): Enter the contact information of the event organizer.
* **Original Venue Name** (required): Enter the name of the original venue.
* **Original Venue Address** (required): Enter the full address of the original venue.
* **Original Venue Contact** (optional): Enter the contact information of the original venue (if available).
* **Original Venue Capacity** (optional): Enter the capacity of the original venue (if applicable).
* **Reason for Venue Change** (required): Enter the reason for the change of venue.
* **New Venue Name** (required): Enter the name of the new venue.
* **New Venue Address** (required): Enter the full address of the new venue.
* **New Venue Contact** (optional): Enter the contact information of the new venue (if available).
* **New Venue Capacity** (optional): Enter the capacity of the new venue (if applicable).
* **Change Effective Date** (required): Enter the date when the venue change takes effect.
* **Parking Situation Changed** (optional): If the parking situation has changed, select "Yes".
* **Accessibility Features Changed** (optional): If the accessibility features have changed, select "Better", "Same", or "Worse".
* **Attendees Have Been Notified** (required): Select if the attendees have been informed of the change.
* **Insurance and Permits Updated** (optional): If the insurance and permits were updated, select "Yes".
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (optional): Enter any other relevant information.
