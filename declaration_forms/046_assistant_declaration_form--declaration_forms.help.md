# Assistant Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare your understanding and commitment to complete an assignment, including your duties, responsibilities, and commitments. It's essential to review and fill out this form carefully to ensure clarity and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date you are declaring your intention to complete the assignment.
	+ Type: date (mm/dd/yyyy)
	+ Example: 02/01/2024
* **Assistant Name**: Enter your full legal name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Assistant ID**: Enter your employee or staff number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: ABC123
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
	+ Type: email (username@domain.com)
	+ Example: johndoe@company.com
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: 123-456-7890
* **Department**: Enter your work department.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: Sales
* **Position Title**: Enter your job title.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: Sales Representative
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: Jane Smith
* **Assignment Name**: Enter the name of the assignment.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: Sales Project
* **Assignment Description**: Enter a brief description of the assignment.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: To complete a sales project for a new product launch.
* **Assignment Start Date**: Enter the date the assignment is scheduled to begin.
	+ Type: date (mm/dd/yyyy)
	+ Example: 02/15/2024
* **Assignment End Date**: Enter the date the assignment is scheduled to end.
	+ Type: date (mm/dd/yyyy)
	+ Example: 03/15/2024
* **Declaration Type**: Select the type of declaration you are making.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: New Assignment Declaration, Role Change Declaration, Responsibility Acknowledgment, Conflict of Interest Declaration, Confidentiality Declaration, or Confidentiality Declaration
* **Do You Understand Your Duties**: Select whether you understand your duties.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes - Fully Understood, Mostly - Some Clarification Needed, or No - Need Full Explanation
* **Are Necessary Resources Available**: Select whether you have the necessary resources.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes - All Available, Some - Need Additional, or No - Need Full Support
* **Is Assignment Timeline Feasible**: Select whether the assignment timeline is realistic.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes - Realistic, Yes - With Adjustments, or No - Need Extension
* **Are There Potential Conflicts**: Select whether there are potential conflicts.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: No Conflicts, Minor Conflicts, or Major Conflicts
* **Describe Any Conflicts**: Enter a description of any conflicts.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: I have a conflicting project meeting on the same day.
* **Do You Agree to Maintain Confidentiality**: Select whether you agree to maintain confidentiality.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes I Agree, or No
* **Do You Agree to Uphold Ethical Standards**: Select whether you agree to uphold ethical standards.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes I Agree, or No
* **Do You Understand Reporting Requirements**: Select whether you understand reporting requirements.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes, or No
* **Have You Completed Required Training**: Select whether you have completed required training.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes - All Training, Some - In Progress, or No - Need Training
* **Do You Have Questions or Concerns**: Select whether you have questions or concerns.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: No - All Clear, Yes - Minor Questions, or Yes - Major Concerns
* **Describe Questions or Concerns**: Enter a description of any questions or concerns.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: I am unsure about the assignment deadline.
* **I Commit to Fulfilling This Assignment**: Select whether you commit to fulfilling the assignment.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes I Commit, or No
* **I Declare This Information Is Accurate**: Select whether you declare the information as accurate.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Yes I Declare, or No
* **Assistant Signature**: Enter your name as it appears on your signature.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Supervisor Signature**: Enter your supervisor's name as it appears on their signature.
	+ Type: text
	+ Example: Jane Smith
* **HR Acknowledgment**: Select whether HR has acknowledged the declaration.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Acknowledged, Pending, or Not Required
