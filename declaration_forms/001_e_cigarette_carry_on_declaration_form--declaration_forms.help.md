# e Cigarette Carry On Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from users who are carrying electronic cigarettes on a flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
3. Declare your possession of electronic cigarettes, if applicable.
4. Provide your travel information, including flight number and travel documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (id: 1)**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **E Cigarette Information (id: 2)**: If you are carrying electronic cigarettes, please describe the type and quantity of devices you have.
* **Confirmation (id: 3)**: Please confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **E Cigarette Declaration (id: 4)**: Please declare whether you are carrying electronic cigarettes, and if so, the quantity.
* **Signature (id: 5)**: Please sign this form to confirm your declaration.
* **Date (id: 6)**: Please enter the date of travel.
* **Airline Signature (id: 7)**: If required by the airline, please sign to confirm that you have declared your possession of electronic cigarettes.
* **Flight Number (id: 8)**: Please enter the flight number or confirmation number for your travel.
* **Travel Documentation (id: 9)**: If you are required to provide travel documentation, please select the type of documentation you have.
* **Travel Agent (id: 10)**: If you are represented by a travel agent, please confirm this.

Note: All fields marked "required: false" are optional and can be skipped if not applicable.
