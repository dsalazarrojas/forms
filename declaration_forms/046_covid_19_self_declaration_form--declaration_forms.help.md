# COVID 19 Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients on their demographics, vaccination status, and declaration details to ensure accurate and efficient vaccination processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, one page at a time, to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
- If you have any issues or need assistance, feel free to reach out to the healthcare staff for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Full Name**: A required field where you are to enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **ID or Passport Number**: A required field where you enter your ID or passport number to identify yourself.
- **Contact Phone Number**: A required field where you enter your contact phone number for efficient communication.
- **Email Address**: A required field where you enter your email address to receive notifications and updates.
- **Residence Type**: Please select the type of residence category that applies to you.
  * Permanent Resident
  * Temporary Resident
  * Visitor
  * Student
  * Other (Please specify in the field below)
- **I declare my interest and intent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine**: Please select one of the options to express your interest in receiving the vaccine.
  * True
  * I am interested
  * False (if you are undecided)
- **Previous Vaccine Dose Status**: Please select your previous vaccine dose status.
  * None
  * One dose received
  * Fully vaccinated
  * Booster received
- **Do you belong to any of these priority groups?**: Please select one or more of the priority groups that apply to you.
  * Healthcare Worker
  * Elderly (65+)
  * Underlying Condition
  * Essential Worker
  * None
- **I certify that the information provided in this declaration is true**: Please select the truth status of your declaration.
  * True
  * I certify
- **Date of Declaration**: Enter the date on which you are filling out this form.
- **I agree to my data being processed for vaccination scheduling**: Please select your agreement status.
  * True
  * I agree
- **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)**: A required field where you enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents for signature purposes.

Please note that this form is a critical part of the vaccination process. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to avoid delays or issues with your vaccination scheduling. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to the healthcare staff.
