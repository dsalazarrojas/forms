# Funeral Planning Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a deceased person's funeral arrangements, next of kin, and other relevant details. It is meant to be a general guide for users to provide information about a deceased person's funeral plans and contact details in case of an emergency or to contact them about funeral arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your answer for the funeral home question.
2. Provide the name of the next of kin.
3. Enter the name of the deceased person.
4. Enter the date and time of death.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Funeral Home**: Choose whether or not a funeral home is involved in the deceased person's funeral arrangements.
* **Next Of Kin**: Provide the name and contact information of the next of kin.
* **Name Of Deceased**: Enter the full name of the deceased person.
* **Date Of Death**: Enter the date of death in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Death**: Enter the time of death in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the asterisk symbol) to ensure that the form is complete.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always reach out to the relevant authorities or the funeral home for clarification.
