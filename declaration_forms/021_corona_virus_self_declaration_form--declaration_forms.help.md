# Corona Virus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their recent travel history, contact details, and potential exposure to COVID-19. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the safety and health of all individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
3. Make sure to provide your correct contact information, including phone and email.
4. If you have traveled recently, please provide details about your trips.
5. If you have been in contact with someone who has COVID-19 or has symptoms of COVID-19, please indicate this.
6. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the introductory page of the form. Please provide your name and any other identifying information that is relevant to your recent travel history or potential exposure to COVID-19.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your name, phone number, and email address. This information will be used to contact you with any further instructions or concerns.
* **Travel History**: This section is designed to capture your recent travel history. Please indicate if you have traveled within the last week, either within your country or outside of it.
* **Symptoms**: If you are experiencing any symptoms of COVID-19, please indicate them here.
* **Exposure Risk**: This section is to capture your potential exposure to COVID-19. Please select the options that apply to you.
	+ **Have you had contact with someone confirmed with COVID-19?**: If you have had contact with someone who has been confirmed with COVID-19, please select this option.
	+ **Have you had contact with someone with symptoms of COVID-19 in the last 7 days?**: If you have had contact with someone who has symptoms of COVID-19 in the last 7 days, please select this option.
	+ **Have you traveled with someone confirmed with COVID-19?**: If you have traveled with someone who has been confirmed with COVID-19, please select this option.
