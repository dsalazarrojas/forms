# small_estate_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The small_estate_declaration_form is used to collect personal and estate-related information from individuals or entities making a declaration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Family Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the Estate Address where the estate is located.
3. Provide the Date of Death of the individual or entity making the declaration.
4. Select the Date of Declaration, which is the date this form is being completed.
5. Enter any additional Estate Details.
6. Select the Supporting Documents relevant to the declaration.
7. Enter the required Document 1, Document 2, and Document 3 details.
8. Enter any additional Document 4 to 16 details if applicable.
9. Enter the Contact Name and Contact Email of the representative or contact person.
10. Enter the Contact Address of the representative or contact person.
11. (Optional) Enter any Follow up details if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Family Name** (`family_name`, `text`, required): Enter your family name.

* **Date of Declaration** (`date_of_declaration`, `date`, required): Select the date this form is being completed.
* **Estate Details** (`estate_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the estate.
