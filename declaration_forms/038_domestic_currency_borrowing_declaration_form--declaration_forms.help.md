# Domestic Currency Borrowing Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domestic Currency Borrowing Declaration Form is used to document and track domestic currency borrowing and lending between individuals or entities. This form helps to ensure accurate and up-to-date information about borrowing and lending activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Answer each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure you have the necessary approvals or signatures before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Details** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_details`): This field should contain a brief description of the borrowing or lending activity, including the purpose and scope of the transaction.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Amount** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_amount`): Enter the exact amount of domestic currency borrowed or lent.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Date** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_date`): Enter the date the borrowing or lending took place.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Source** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_source`): Indicate whether the borrowing or lending is from or to an individual or entity.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Reason** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_reason`): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the borrowing or lending.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Details Note** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_details_note`): This field can be used for any additional comments or notes related to the borrowing or lending activity.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Declared By** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_declared_by`): Confirm whether the borrowing or lending was declared by the borrower or lender.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Declaration Form Signed** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_declaration_form_signed`): Confirm whether the form has been signed by both the borrower and lender.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Declaration Form Date** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_declaration_form_date`): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Borrower Declaration Form** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_borrower_declaration_form`): Enter the borrower's declaration form number or reference.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Lender Declaration Form** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_lender_declaration_form`): Enter the lender's declaration form number or reference.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Currency** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_currency`): Enter the type of domestic currency used for the borrowing or lending.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Currency Code** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_currency_code`): Enter a code for the domestic currency used (if applicable).
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Form Borrower Declaration Form Signed By** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_form_borrower_declaration_form_signed_by`): Confirm whether the form was signed by the borrower.
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Form Borrower Declaration Form Signed By Signed By** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_form_borrower_declaration_form_signed_by_signed_by`): Confirm whether the form was signed by the borrower's representative (if applicable).
* **Domestic Currency Borrowing Form Borrower Declaration Form Signed By Signed By** (`domestic_currency_borrowing_form_borrower_declaration_form_signed_by_signed_by`): Repeat of the above field for redundancy and consistency.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the borrower and lender, and both parties should sign and date the form to confirm their involvement.
