# vehicle_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vehicle_declaration_form" is a form used to declare information about a vehicle. This includes its make, model, year, color, registration, owner, driver, insurance, and license.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (1, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle, such as "Toyota" or "Ford".
* **Vehicle Model** (2, text, required): Enter the model of the vehicle, such as "Corolla" or "F-150".
* **Vehicle Year** (3, number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (4, text, required): Enter the color of the vehicle, such as "Red" or "Blue".
* **Vehicle Registration** (5, text, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Owner** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
* **Vehicle Driver** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the vehicle's driver.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (8, text, required): Enter the vehicle's insurance information.
* **Vehicle License** (9, text, required): Enter the vehicle's license information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If a field is not relevant to your vehicle, you can leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek clarification from the administrator.
* This form is for new vehicle declarations. To update a vehicle's record, please use a separate form or process.
