# independent_contractor_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from independent contractors for their declaration purposes. It's a crucial document that needs to be filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and the fields required.
2. Fill out the form with the correct information for the company, contact person, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Provide the contract start and end dates, if applicable.
4. Specify the payment terms and method.
5. Choose the correct contract status and type.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, required: false): This is a placeholder for the form title, and it's not a required field.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the company you're representing.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for this declaration.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: true): Enter the address where business operations will take place.
- **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a contract start date, enter it here.
- **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a contract end date, enter it here.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Describe the payment terms and conditions, if applicable.
- **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current contract status from the options: Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
- **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of contract from the options: Hourly, Project, or Both.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment method from the options: Invoice, Hourly Pay, or Retainer.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues.
- Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
