# Melbourne Metro Daily Health Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Melbourne Metro Daily Health Declaration form is designed to collect information about your health status, travel history, and other relevant details for health monitoring and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Provide information about your travel history, including any recent travel to Melbourne or other relevant locations.
3. Indicate your current health status, choosing from the options provided.
4. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
5. If you have a travel companion, provide their contact information.
6. Specify your location.
7. If you have any other symptoms or concerns, please describe them.
8. Select your declaration status, indicating whether you have declared healthy, unhealthy, or have declined to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (1)**: Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information. This is the first step in completing this form. 
* **Travel History (2)**: Check all locations you have recently visited, including Melbourne or other relevant locations.
* **Health Status (3)**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Healthy: You are feeling well and do not have any symptoms.
	+ Unwell: You are experiencing symptoms or feel unwell.
	+ Hospitalized: You are currently in the hospital.
	+ Isolated: You are self-isolating for health reasons.
* **Symptoms (4)**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing. This can include physical or mental health issues.
* **Contact Details (5)**: If you have a travel companion, provide their phone number or email address.
* **Location (6)**: Specify your current location.
* **Travel Companion Details (7)**: If you have a travel companion, describe their travel history and symptoms.
* **Other Symptoms (8)**: If you have any other symptoms or concerns, please describe them.
* **Declaration Status (9)**: Select whether you have declared healthy, unhealthy, or have declined to answer. 

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure that you are filling out this form for the intended purpose and that your submission is genuine.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to contact us for support.
