# Custom Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Declaration Form is a document used to record shipment details for regulatory compliance and internal management. It ensures accurate declaration of shipment contents, weight, and value. This form is essential for each unique shipment, and users must fill it out for every cargo shipment, even if it's not their first or last time.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before starting.
- Ensure you fill in the form for each unique shipment.
- Provide accurate information for each field as it applies to the shipment.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipment Number** (shipment_number, number, required: false): Enter the unique identification number of the shipment.
* **Shipment Contents** (shipment_contents, text, required: false): Describe the items or goods being transported.
* **Shipper Company** (shipper_company, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company shipping the cargo.
* **Shipper Name** (shipper_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual shipping the cargo.
* **Shipper Address** (shipper_address, text, required: false): Enter the address where the shipper is located.
* **Shipper Phone** (shipper_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the shipper.
* **Consignee Name** (consignee_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or company receiving the cargo.
* **Consignee Address** (consignee_address, text, required: false): Enter the address where the consignee is located.
* **Consignee Phone** (consignee_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the consignee.
* **Cargo Weight** (cargo_weight, number, required: false): Enter the weight of the cargo in units suitable for the shipment (e.g., kilograms or pounds).
* **Cargo Value** (cargo_value, number, required: false): Enter the value of the cargo.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and consistency in filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- Use the same format for each unique shipment to maintain uniformity and ease of comparison.
