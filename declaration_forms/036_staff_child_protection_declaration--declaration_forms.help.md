# Staff Child Protection Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Child Protection Declaration form is a mandatory document that ensures staff members of the organization are aware of and acknowledge the child protection policies and procedures of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read and understand the organization's child protection policies and procedures.
* Select "Yes" or "No" to acknowledge that you are aware of the policies and procedures.
* If you have any concerns or suspicions of child abuse or neglect, report them on the "Report Concerns" field.
* Declare if you have already reported any concerns or suspicions of child abuse or neglect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Acknowledge Policies** (`acknowledge_policies`, select_multiple, required: false): This field requires you to select all the policies you understand and agree to. Please note that this is not a comprehensive list of policies; it is a declaration of your understanding of the general principles.
* **Declare Protection Declaration** (`declare_protection_declaration`, select_one, required: false): This field requires you to declare if you are aware of the organization's child protection policies and procedures.
* **Report Concerns** (`report_concerns`, text, required: false): Please report any concerns or suspicions of child abuse or neglect here. This is a confidential channel for reporting sensitive information.
* **Declared** (`declare_declared`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to declare if you have already reported any concerns or suspicions of child abuse or neglect.
* **Staff Child Protection Declaration Form** (`staff_child_protection_declaration_form`, text, required: false): This is the main field where you can enter your declaration. Please provide a clear and concise statement explaining your declaration.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for reporting incidents of child abuse or neglect; staff members are expected to report such incidents promptly and confidentially through the organization's reporting mechanisms.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please reach out to the organization's Child Protection Officer for guidance.
* Please complete this form carefully and truthfully, as it is an essential document that promotes a safe and supportive work environment for children.
