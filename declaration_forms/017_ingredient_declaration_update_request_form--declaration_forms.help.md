# Ingredient Declaration Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update existing ingredient declarations. Please use this form to submit changes to your ingredient declaration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the updated information.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form to request the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ingredient Declarations** (Page 1): Describe the ingredients that you are updating.
    - Please provide a clear and concise description of the ingredients that you are updating.
    - If there are multiple ingredients, list them separately.
- **Reason for Update** (Page 2): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the update.
    - Explain why the declaration needs to be updated (e.g., change in formulation, new regulations, etc.).
- **Documentation URL** (Page 3): Provide a URL or link to any supporting documentation.
    - If you have additional information that supports your update, please provide a URL or link to it.
- **Updated Statement** (Page 4): Describe the updated information.
    - Clearly and concisely state the changes you are making to the declaration.
- **Updated Reason** (Page 5): Explain the reason for the updated statement.
    - Elaborate on the reason for the update, if necessary.
- **Contact Information** (Page 6): Provide contact information.
    - Include your name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Assigned To** (Page 7): Identify who will be responsible for reviewing and processing the update.
    - Ensure that the correct person or team receives the update request.

## Tips
- Please be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are complete.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please reach out to the contact person mentioned in the contact information field.
