# ownership_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ownership declaration form is a tool used to gather information about the owner of a pet. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the owner's details are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* First Name: Your first name.
	* Last Name: Your last name.
	* Email: Your email address.
	* Phone Number: Your phone number.
	* Address: Your address.
	* Pet Type: Choose the type of pet you own (Cat, Dog, Other).
	* Pet Name: Enter the name of your pet.
	* Owner Since: The date you started owning your pet.
	* Declaration Date: The date you're declaring ownership.
	* Declaration Time: The time you're declaring ownership.
	* Signed By: Your signature.
	* Signed Position: Your position or title.
	* Signed Date: The date you signed the form.
	* Signed Time: The time you signed the form.
	* Goals: Enter any additional goals or comments.
	* prophets (x8): This field is for adding additional prophets, but feel free to ignore it or leave blank.
