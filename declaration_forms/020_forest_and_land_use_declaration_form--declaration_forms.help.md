# Forest And Land Use Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forest And Land Use Declaration Form is a document that collects information about land use and land ownership, and is intended to be used in conjunction with relevant regulations and laws regarding land use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please start by entering your landowner information in the **Landowner Information** section. This includes providing your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. In the **Contact Details** section, please provide your contact information to ensure that you can be reached for further clarification or questions about your land use declaration.
3. In the **Land Use** section, please select one of the following options:
	* **Forest**: Select this option if your land is currently being used for forestry purposes.
	* **Agricultural**: Select this option if your land is being used for agriculture.
	* **Urban**: Select this option if your land is located within an urban area.
	* **Rural**: Select this option if your land is located outside of urban areas.
4. In the **Project Details** section, please provide any additional information about your project, including its location and description.
5. In the **Land Use Declaration** section, please enter a brief description of how your land is being used.
6. In the **Declaration Date** section, please enter the date on which you are declaring your land use.
7. In the **Declaration Time** section, please enter the time at which you are declaring your land use.
8. In the **Land Area** section, please enter the total area of your land.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landowner Information** (Landowner Information, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about the landowner.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Land Use** (Land Use, select one, required/optional): This field allows you to select the type of land use for your project. Please select the option that best describes your land use.
* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter any additional information about your project, including its location and description.
* **Land Use Declaration** (Land Use Declaration, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter a brief description of how your land is being used.
* **Declaration Date** (Declaration Date, date, required/optional): This section is where you can enter the date on which you are declaring your land use.
* **Declaration Time** (Declaration Time, time, required/optional): This section is where you can enter the time at which you are declaring your land use.
* **Land Area** (Land Area, number, required/optional): This section is where you can enter the total area of your land.
