# labor_rights_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The labor rights compliance declaration form is used to collect information about an employee's employment status, hours worked, and other relevant details for labor law purposes. This form helps to ensure compliance with labor regulations and provides an accurate record of employee employment information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Company Name
	* Employee Id
	* Employment Status
	* Start Date
	* End Date
	* Work Hours
	* Labor Law Version
	* Declaration Status
2. If applicable, select the employment type(s) you are employed in:
	* Full-time
	* Part-time
	* Contract
	* Intern
3. You can add any additional comments about your employment status in the Employee Comment field (optional).
4. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's official name.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status:
	+ Active
	+ Non-active
	+ Terminated
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your employment type(s):
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contract
	+ Intern
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you ended working with the company.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, required): Enter your total work hours per week.
* **Labor Law Version** (`labor_law_version`, select_one, required): Choose the relevant labor law version:
	+ Version 1
	+ Version 2
	+ Version 3
* **Declaration Status** (`declaration_status`, select_one, required): Choose your declaration status:
	+ Declared
	+ Declined
	+ In process
* **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about your employment status (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative or supervisor for assistance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
