# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the individual's health status and potential exposure risks to help determine if they can safely enter the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly, including providing details when requested (e.g., travel locations).
3. For select_one questions, choose the option that best matches your current status.
4. For text fields, provide a brief but clear explanation or answer.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID or Visitor** (Optional): If applicable, enter your employee ID or visitor ID number.
* **Date** (Required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Time of Screening** (Required): Enter the time you are completing this form.
* **Department or Purpose of Visit** (Required): Enter the department or reason for your visit to the facility.
* **Current Temperature** (Required): Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Do You Have a Fever of 100.4F or Higher?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you currently have a fever of 100.4F or higher.
* **Do You Have a New or Worsening Cough?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have a new or worsening cough.
* **Do You Have Shortness of Breath or Difficulty Breathing?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.
* **Do You Have a Sore Throat?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have a sore throat.
* **Do You Have Chills?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have chills.
* **Do You Have Muscle Pain or Body Aches?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have muscle pain or body aches.
* **Do You Have a Headache?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have a headache.
* **Have You Lost Your Sense of Taste?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have lost your sense of taste.
* **Have You Lost Your Sense of Smell?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have lost your sense of smell.
* **Do You Have Unusual Fatigue?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have unusual fatigue.
* **Do You Have Nausea or Vomiting?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have nausea or vomiting.
* **Do You Have Diarrhea?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have diarrhea.
* **Have You Been in Close Contact with a Confirmed COVID-19 Case Within the Past 14 Days?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case within the past 14 days.
* **Have You Traveled Outside the Country Recently Within the Past 14 Days?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have recently traveled outside the country within the past 14 days.
* **Travel Locations** (Optional): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, list the travel locations.
* **Have You Tested Positive for COVID-19 Within the Past 14 Days?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19 within the past 14 days.
* **Are You Awaiting COVID-19 Test Results?** (Required): Choose 'True' if you are waiting for COVID-19 test results.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (Optional): Choose your vaccination status: Fully Vaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Not Vaccinated, or Prefer Not to Say.
* **Have You Received a Booster Dose?** (Optional): If applicable, choose whether you have received a booster dose.
* **Based on Your Answers, Are You Clear to Enter?** (Required): Choose the corresponding option based on your answers to previous questions.
* **Reason for Denial** (Optional): If you are not cleared to enter, provide a brief explanation for the denial.
* **Your Signature** (Required): Sign your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (Required): Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Screener Name** (Optional): If applicable, enter the name of the screener.
* **Screener Signature** (Optional): If applicable, provide the signature of the screener.
