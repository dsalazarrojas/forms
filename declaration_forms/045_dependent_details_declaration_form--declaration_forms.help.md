# Dependent Details Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dependent Details Declaration Form is a document used to declare and verify the information of a dependent for official purposes. This form ensures that the dependent's details are accurate and complete, and it's essential to fill out this form correctly to avoid duplication or misrepresentation of the declaration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the fields below and fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your dependent.
2.  Ensure that you have the necessary documents and proof of the dependent's information before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately represent your dependent's details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dependent Info** (`dependent_info`, text, required: false): This field is for entering a brief description or details about your dependent.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about the relationship between you and your dependent. Please select the correct relationship status from the options provided:
	+ Spouse
	+ Parent
	+ Child
	+ Partner
	+ Other
* **Dependent Name** (`dependent_name`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's full name.
* **Dependent Date of Birth** (`dependent_dob`, date, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's date of birth.
* **Dependent SSN** (`dependent_ssn`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's Social Security Number.
* **Dependent Occupation** (`dependent_occupation`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's occupation.
* **Dependent Education** (`dependent_education`, text, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's level of education.
* **Dependent Education Level** (`dependent_education_level`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about your dependent's education level. Please select the correct level from the options provided:
	+ High School
	+ College
	+ University
* **Dependent Employment Status** (`dependent_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about your dependent's employment status. Please select the correct status from the options provided:
	+ Working
	+ Not Working
	+ Disabled
* **Dependent Income** (`dependent_income`, number, required: false): This field is for entering your dependent's income.
* **Document Upload 1** (`document_upload_1`, note, required: false): This field is for uploading a document that supports your dependent's information.
* **Document Upload 2** (`document_upload_2`, note, required: false): This field is for uploading another document that supports your dependent's information.
* **Electronic Signature 1** (`e_signature_1`, text, required: false): This field is for signing this form electronically.
* **Electronic Signature 2** (`e_signature_2`, text, required: false): This field is for signing this form electronically.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): This field is for submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or consult the relevant documents for clarification.
* Double-check that all uploaded documents are legible and complete.
* Electronic signatures must be valid and legible.
