# environmentalimpactdisclosurereportform - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Impact Disclosure Report Form is used to document and report on the environmental impact of a specific project. This form is required for projects that involve certain activities such as construction, mining, agriculture, manufacturing, transportation, energy, waste management, or other projects that have a potential environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select the project type from the drop-down menu.
2.  Enter the project start and end dates.
3.  Provide details about the project and its location.
4.  Select the measures you have taken or will take to mitigate the environmental impact.
5.  Enter any comments or observations about the project.
6.  Select the current project status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are reporting on from the list provided (construction, mining, agriculture, manufacturing, transportation, energy, waste management, or other).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the project started.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the project ended or is expected to end.
* **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of the project, including its location and any relevant information.
* **Company Information**: Enter the name and address of the company involved in the project.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact person's name and email address or phone number.
* **Project Location**: Enter the specific location of the project.
* **Environmental Impact**: Describe the environmental impact of the project, including any potential effects on the environment.
* **Measures**: If applicable, indicate what measures you have taken or will take to mitigate the environmental impact.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the project.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of the project (active, completed, or cancelled).

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information about the project.
- Be sure to complete all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or a designated representative.
