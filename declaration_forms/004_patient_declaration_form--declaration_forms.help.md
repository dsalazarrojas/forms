# Patient Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients regarding their medical history, contact details, and other relevant information. The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive understanding of the patient's situation, ensuring that healthcare providers have the necessary information to deliver high-quality care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that your medical history and contact details are up-to-date.
2. If you have any medical history or concerns that are not listed, please provide more information in the "Medical History" field.
3. If you have any medical history that does not fit into the provided categories, please use the "Other Medical History" field to provide more details.
4. Answer "yes" or "no" to the "Consent to Treatment" question. This is an important question that will allow us to provide you with the best possible care.
5. Please provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
6. If you have any notes or comments about your medical history, you can add them in the "Patient Declaration Form Notes" field.
7. Finally, please sign and date the form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Declaration Form**: This field is the title of the form, which is meant to be completed by you, the patient.
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Birth Date**: Please enter your date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Medical History**: This field is meant for you to describe any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Other Medical History**: If you have any medical history that does not fit into the provided categories, please use this field to provide more details.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Please answer "yes" if you consent to treatment and "no" otherwise.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number in the standard format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Patient Declaration Form Notes**: If you have any notes or comments about your medical history, you can add them here.
* **Patient Declaration Form Signature**: Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have provided accurate information. 
* **Patient Declaration Form Dated**: This field is meant to be completed by the healthcare provider who is collecting your information.
