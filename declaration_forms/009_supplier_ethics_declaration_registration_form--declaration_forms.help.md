# Supplier Ethics Declaration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Ethics Declaration Registration Form is designed to gather information from suppliers about their ethics declaration registration. This form is a mandatory requirement for suppliers to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title to indicate the purpose of your submission.
2. Enter the supplier's name as it appears on their official business registration documents.
3. Provide the supplier's company name and address.
4. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number.
6. Select the supplier's category and subcategory from the provided options.
7. Select the supplier's status from the provided options.
8. Enter any additional notes about the supplier.
9. Declare the supplier's ethics and conformity level based on the provided options.
10. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which should indicate the purpose of your submission.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's name as it appears on their official business registration documents.
* **Supplier Company** (`supplier_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's company name.
* **Supplier Contact Person** (`supplier_contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the declaration.
* **Declaration Time** (`declaration_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the declaration.
* **Declaration Details** (`declaration_details`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the declaration.
* **Supplier Category** (`supplier_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the supplier's category from the provided options.
* **Supplier Subcategory** (`supplier_subcategory`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the supplier's subcategory from the provided options.
* **Supplier Status** (`supplier_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the supplier's status from the provided options.
* **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the supplier.
* **Supplier Ethics** (`supplier_ethics`, `select_one`, required: false): Declare the supplier's ethics level based on the provided options.
* **Supplier Conformity** (`supplier_conformity`, `select_one`, required: false): Declare the supplier's conformity level based on the provided options.
