# COVID 19 Guest Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Guest Declaration Form is a critical document that ensures all guests visiting or staying at our location comply with COVID-19 protocols and regulations. This form collects essential information about the guest's personal and travel details to ensure their safety and the safety of others.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review each field carefully and ensure that the information provided is true and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **National ID or Passport Number** (`national_id`, text, required): Enter your national ID or passport number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your permanent address.
* **City** (`home_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you are staying.
* **Country** (`home_country`, text, required): Enter the country where you are staying.
* **Accommodation Name** (`accommodation_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your accommodation.
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check-in.
* **Check-Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check-out.
* **Place of Origin** (`travel_origin`, text, required): Enter the place of origin for your travel.
* **Mode of Transportation** (`travel_method`, select_one, required): Select how you traveled here (Air, Train, Bus, Car, Ship, Other).
* **Flight or Vehicle Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): Enter your flight or vehicle number if applicable.
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis in Past 14 Days** (`covid_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Declare if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms Present** (`covid_symptoms`, select_one, required): Declare if you are experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Declare your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated without booster, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated).
* **Consent to Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing_consent`, select_one, required): Declare if you consent to contact tracing.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission of this form.

Please note that all fields are required, and failure to complete the form accurately may result in your visit or stay being denied. Ensure that you complete the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our staff for assistance.
