# Cafe Agapita Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cafe Agapita Declaration Form is a mandatory form for all visitors and staff to complete before entering the cafe. It ensures a safe environment by gathering necessary information about your health and safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your name and contact phone number accurately and completely.
* Select your category (customer, delivery staff, contractor, job applicant, or vendor).
* Answer the health screening questions truthfully, including any current symptoms and travel history.
* Agree to the safety protocols, such as hand sanitizing, wearing a mask, and maintaining physical distancing.
* Consent to data privacy for contact tracing purposes.
* Certify that the information provided is accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please fill out your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for contact purposes.
* **I am a...** (`visitor_type`, select_one, required): Select your category from the options provided.
* **Are you currently experiencing any fever, cough, or shortness of breath** (`current_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer truthfully about any current health symptoms.
* **Current Body Temperature** (`temperature_reading`, number, required): Enter your current body temperature as recorded at the entrance station.
* **Have you traveled internationally in the last 14 days** (`travel_history`, select_one, required): Answer honestly about your recent travel history.
* **Have you been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case** (`contact_history`, select_one, required): Respond truthfully about any close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case.
* **I agree to sanitize my hands upon entry** (`hand_sanitizing_ack`, select_one, required): Check "True" to confirm that you will sanitize your hands.
* **I agree to wear a mask when not eating or drinking** (`mask_policy_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will wear a mask when not consuming food or drinks.
* **I will maintain 1.5 meters distance from other parties** (`physical_distancing_ack`, select_one, required): Choose "True" to indicate your commitment to physical distancing.
* **I consent to the use of this data for contact tracing if necessary** (`data_privacy_ack`, select_one, required): Check "True" to consent to data use for contact tracing.
* **I certify that the information provided is true and correct** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips
Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately. Failure to do so may result in denial of entry or other consequences.
