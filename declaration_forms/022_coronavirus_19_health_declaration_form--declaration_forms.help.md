# Coronavirus 19 Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare providers assess their health status and provide appropriate care. Please ensure you complete this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, date of birth, contact number, and email address.
2. If you have traveled recently, provide a brief history of your travel, including dates and destinations.
3. If you have experienced any symptoms related to COVID-19, describe them in the provided text field.
4. If you have a healthcare provider, provide their name, phone number, email address, and address.
5. Identify your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, phone number, and address.
6. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand its contents.
