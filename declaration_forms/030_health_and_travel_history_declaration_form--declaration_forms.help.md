# health_and_travel_history_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_and_travel_history_declaration_form is a form used to collect information about an individual's travel history, medical conditions, and contact information for travel purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact authorized personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel History**: Enter a brief description of your travel history, including any relevant information about your recent travel, such as destinations visited, dates traveled, and modes of transportation used.
* **Contact Info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any contact information available.
	+ If you select 'Yes', please provide your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your health while traveling.
	+ If you select 'Yes', please describe your medical conditions in the space provided.
* **Travel Destination**: Select the country or region you are traveling to.
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return from your trip.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the length of your trip in days.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of travel you are undertaking (Business, Pleasure, Other).
* **Travel Reason**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of your travel.
* **Health Insurance**: Enter your health insurance information, including your insurance provider and policy number.
* **Symptoms**: If you are experiencing any symptoms, please describe them in the space provided.
* **Travel Participants**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are traveling with others.
	+ If you select 'Yes', please list the names and contact information of your travel companions.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have an emergency contact person.
	+ If you select 'Yes', please provide their name, phone number, and address.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Contact Person**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a contact person.
* **Contact Relationship**: Enter a brief description of your relationship with your contact person.
* **Travel Documents**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any travel documents, such as a passport.
* **Travel Doc Number**: Enter the number on your travel documents, if applicable.
* **Travel Doc Type**: Select the type of travel documents you have (e.g., passport, visa).
* **Travel Doc Date**: Enter the date your travel documents expire, if applicable.
* **Passport Exp. Date**: Enter the expiration date of your passport, if applicable.
* **Travel Vaccinations**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have received any travel vaccinations.
* **Vaccinations Received**: Enter any vaccinations you have received, if applicable.
* **Health Declaration**: This is a note field for you to provide any additional health information or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Please complete all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* Please review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact authorized personnel.
