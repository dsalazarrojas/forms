# coronavirus_self_declaration_form_viva_toyota - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to a coronavirus self-declaration. It may be used by employees or suppliers to provide details about their work-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your company information in the "Company" field.
4. If you are an employee, enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
5. If you are a supplier, enter the Supplier Name in the "Supplier Name" field.
6. Select your Declaration Date from the calendar.
7. Choose your Employee Position from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
11. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
12. Select your options from the "Select Multiple 1" to "Select Multiple 9" fields as required.
13. Select your Employee Position 2 from the dropdown menu.
14. Select your Employee Position 3 from the dropdown menu.
15. Select your Time for the "Time" field.
16. Finally, submit the form.
