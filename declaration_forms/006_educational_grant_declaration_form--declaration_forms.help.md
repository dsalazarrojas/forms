# Educational Grant Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to declare educational grants for students. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your grant is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student name, email, and student identifier.
2. Select your educational institution from the options provided.
3. Choose your student status (Active, Inactive, or Withdrawn).
4. Enter the grant amount, start date, end date, and grant duration.
5. Sign the form with your student signature, parent signature, and institutional signature.
6. Enter the institutional date and identifier.
7. Enter the institutional name, email, phone number, address, post code, and institutional designation.
8. Enter the institutional URL (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: This is the student's name.
* **Email**: This is the student's email address.
* **Student Identifier**: This is the student's unique identifier number.
* **Declaration Date**: This is the date you are declaring the educational grant.
* **Resources**: This is a field for any additional resources or comments.
* **Educational Institution**: This is the name of the educational institution you are affiliated with.
* **Student Status**: This is your current status as a student (Active, Inactive, or Withdrawn).
* **Grant Amount**: This is the amount of the educational grant being declared.
* **Grant Start Date**: This is the start date of the grant.
* **Grant End Date**: This is the end date of the grant.
* **Grant Duration**: This is the duration of the grant in months or years.
* **Student Signature**: This is where the student signs the declaration.
* **Parent Signature**: This is where the parent signs the declaration (if applicable).
* **Institutional Signature**: This is where the institutional representative signs the declaration.
* **Institutional Date**: This is the date the institutional representative signs the declaration.
* **Institutional Identifier**: This is the unique identifier of the institution.
* **Institutional Name**: This is the name of the institution.
* **Institutional Email**: This is the email address of the institution.
* **Institutional Phone**: This is the phone number of the institution.
* **Institutional Address**: This is the address of the institution.
* **Institutional Post Code**: This is the postal code of the institution.
* **Institutional Designation**: This is a field for any additional information about the institution.
* **Institutional Abbreviation**: This is a field for any institutional abbreviation.
* **Institutional URL**: This is the URL of the institution (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Check that all date and number fields are in the correct format.
- Use the provided options for select fields to ensure accuracy.
- Sign the form with a genuine signature or a digital equivalent.
- If you need help with this form, contact your institution or support team for assistance.
