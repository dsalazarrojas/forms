# Stock Options Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for declaring stock options, stock grants, and stock awards for employees or individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter the Declaration Date.
3. Select the Stock Option Type (Stock Options, Stock Grants, or Stock Awards).
4. Select the Option Type (Stock Options, Stock Grants, or Stock Awards, or choose multiple options).
5. Select the Option Status (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
6. Enter the Option Amount.
7. Enter the Option Value.
8. Enter the Option Start Date.
9. Enter the Option End Date.
10. Enter the Option Exercise Date.
11. Enter the Option Exercise Price.
12. Enter the Option Vesting Date.
13. Enter the Option Expiration Date.
14. Enter the Option Termination Date.
15. Enter the Option Termination Reason.
16. Enter the Option Reissue Date.
17. Enter the Option Reissue Price.
18. Enter the Option Revesting Date.
19. Enter the Option IQO Date.
20. Select the Option IQO Status (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
21. Enter the Option IQO Price.
22. Enter the Option IPO Date.
23. Enter the Option IQO Termination Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): The date you are declaring the stock option, grant, or award.
* **Stock Option Type** (`stock_option_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of stock option, grant, or award you are declaring (Stock Options, Stock Grants, or Stock Awards).
* **Option Type** (`option_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of stock option, grant, or award you are declaring (Stock Options, Stock Grants, or Stock Awards, or choose multiple options).
* **Option Status** (`option_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the stock option, grant, or award (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Option Amount** (`option_amount`, number, optional): Enter the number of stock options, grants, or awards being declared.
* **Option Value** (`option_value`, number, optional): Enter the monetary value of the stock option, grant, or award.
* **Option Start Date** (`option_start_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award became active or vested.
* **Option End Date** (`option_end_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award ended or expired.
* **Option Exercise Date** (`option_exercise_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award was exercised.
* **Option Exercise Price** (`option_exercise_price`, number, optional): The price at which the option, grant, or award was exercised.
* **Option Vesting Date** (`option_vesting_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award vested.
* **Option Expiration Date** (`option_expiration_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award expired.
* **Option Termination Date** (`option_termination_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award was terminated.
* **Option Termination Reason** (`option_termination_reason`, text, optional): The reason for the termination of the option, grant, or award.
* **Option Reissue Date** (`option_reissue_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award was reissued.
* **Option Reissue Price** (`option_reissue_price`, number, optional): The price at which the option, grant, or award was reissued.
* **Option Revesting Date** (`option_revesting_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option, grant, or award re-vested.
* **Option IQO Date** (`option_iqo_date`, date, optional): The date on which the IQO (Incentive Plan) became active or vested.
* **Option IQO Status** (`option_iqo_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the IQO (Incentive Plan) (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Option IQO Price** (`option_iqo_price`, number, optional): The price at which the IQO (Incentive Plan) was exercised.
* **Option IPO Date** (`option_ipo_date`, date, optional): The date on which the option or grant was issued or offered.
* **Option IQO Termination Date** (`option_iqo_termination_date`, date, optional): The date on which the IQO (Incentive Plan) was terminated.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding this form, please contact the relevant administrative or HR department.
* Before submitting the form, review it carefully to ensure all required and optional fields are filled in correctly.
* If you have already submitted an entry for the same stock option, grant, or award, do not resubmit.
* If the form's fields are unclear or you need further guidance, seek assistance from the relevant administrative or HR department.
