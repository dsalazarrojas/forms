# covid-19-vaccine-exemption-declaration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document a patient's exemption from receiving a COVID-19 vaccine due to a medical reason. It should only be used for patients who have consulted with their doctor and have a valid medical reason for exemption.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete this form in collaboration with the patient's doctor to ensure that the information is accurate and complete.
2. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including their name and any relevant medical history.
3. Check the 'Exemption Status' field to indicate if the patient is currently active or inactive for vaccination due to their medical condition.
4. Select the option 'Yes' if the patient has a doctor or 'No' if they do not.
5. If necessary, add a date for the last vaccination received by the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Practitioner** (`Medical Practitioner`, text, not required): This field is for the name and title of the medical practitioner who is completing this form.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, not required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID or medical records.
* **Reason for Exemption** (`Reason for Exemption`, text, not required): Describe the medical reason why the patient is exempt from receiving a COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Exemption Status** (`Exemption Status`, select_multiple, not required): Select 'Active' if the patient is currently exempt from vaccination due to their medical condition, and 'Inactive' if they are no longer exempt.
* **Date of Last Vax** (`Date of Last Vax`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last time the patient received a COVID-19 vaccine, if applicable.
* **Medical Note** (`Medical Note`, note, not required): Add any additional medical information or notes that are relevant to the patient's exemption status.
* **Patient Doctor** (`Patient Doctor`, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' if the patient has a doctor who they can consult with, and 'No' if they do not.
* **Doctor Signature** (`Doctor Signature`, text, not required): This field is for the doctor's signature, confirming that they have reviewed and agreed to the patient's exemption status.

## Tips

* This form should only be used for patients who have a valid medical reason for exemption and have consulted with their doctor.
* Please ensure that you have the patient's doctor's signature before submitting this form.
* If the patient's exemption status changes, please update the 'Exemption Status' field accordingly.
