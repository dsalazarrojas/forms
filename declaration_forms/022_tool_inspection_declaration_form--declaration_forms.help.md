# Tool Inspection Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tool Inspection Declaration Form is designed to collect information about tool inspections. This data is used to maintain records of tool inspections and ensure that tools are properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the tool's type in the "Tool Type" field.
3. Enter the tool's description in the "Tool Description" field.
4. Check the tool's condition in the "Tool Condition" field.
5. Enter the location of the tool in the "Tool Location" field.
6. Enter the inspector's name in the "Inspector Name" field.
7. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspector Date" field.
8. Enter the time of the inspection in the "Inspector Time" field.
9. Enter the inspector's email address in the "Inspector E-mail" field.
10. Enter the inspector's phone number in the "Inspector Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tool Type** (`tool_type`, required: false): This field is used to identify the type of tool being inspected.
* **Tool Description** (`tool_description`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the tool.
* **Tool Condition** (`tool_condition`, required: false): Check the tool's condition (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* **Tool Location** (`tool_location`, required: false): Enter the location where the tool is currently stored.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who inspected the tool.
* **Inspector Date** (`inspector_date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Inspector Time** (`inspector_time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Inspector E-mail** (`inspector_email`, required: false): Enter the inspector's email address.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`, required: false): Enter the inspector's phone number.
