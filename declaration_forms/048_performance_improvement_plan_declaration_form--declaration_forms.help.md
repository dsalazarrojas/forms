# Performance Improvement Plan Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a tool to declare your performance improvement plan, which is a crucial step in addressing areas of improvement in your work. Completing this form will help your manager or HR representative understand your goals and strategies to enhance your performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Specify your department in the "Employee Department" field.
3. Choose a date for the "Date of Declaration".
4. Provide details about your improvement plan in the "Improvement Plan Details" field.
5. Outline your next steps in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Type the department where you are currently working.
* **Date of Declaration** (`date_of_declaration`, date, optional): Choose the date you are submitting this declaration.
* **Improvement Plan Details** (`improvement_plan_details`, note, optional): Describe the specific steps you will take to improve your performance.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): List the actions you will take following this declaration to achieve your improvement goals.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have been formally notified and have the necessary information before completing this form.
* Review your improvement plan with your manager or HR representative to ensure it aligns with company expectations.
* Update this form as needed to reflect changes in your improvement plan.
* Keep this form as a record of your progress and discussion with your manager or HR representative.
