# Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health status, medical history, and contact details. It's crucial to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure you receive proper medical care and diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, making sure to provide truthful information about your health status.
3.  Use a pen or a digital tool to fill out the form, and avoid using abbreviations or leaving fields blank if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, required/optional): This section is for basic information about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact details.
* **Travel History** (Travel History, text, required/optional): Please describe your travel history, including any recent travel or upcoming travel plans.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions, diseases, or allergies you have.
* **Health Risk Assessment** (Health Risk Assessment, text, required/optional): Describe any health risks or concerns you have, such as high blood pressure or diabetes.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and emergency contact details.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts, text, required/optional): List the names, phone numbers, and relationships of your emergency contacts.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or surgeries.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, required/optional): List any allergies you have, including any medications or substances to which you are allergic.
* **Health Declaration** (Health Declaration, text, required/optional): Describe your current health status, including any symptoms or concerns you are experiencing.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not leave it blank; instead, ask your medical provider for guidance.
* If you have any difficulties filling out the form, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
