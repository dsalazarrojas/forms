# Gift Aid Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Gift Aid Declaration Form is used to declare gifts or donations given to charities. This form captures information about the taxpayer and the charity, including the value of the gift, its date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your Taxpayer ID, Name, and Address. 
2. Enter the Charity Name and Charity Registration.
3. Enter the Gift Value and select the Gift Value (currency) in which the gift was made.
4. Enter the Gift Value Date.
5. Enter the Total Gift Amount.
6. Enter your Donor Declaration.
7. Sign and date the form with your Taxpayer Signature.
8. If required, select the Declaration Status and Charity Declaration Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Aid Declaration** (gift_aid_declaration_form): This is the title of the form, and it is not a field to be filled in.
* **Taxpayer ID** (taxpayer_id): Your tax identifier number, which is used to identify you as a taxpayer.
* **Taxpayer Name** (taxpayer_name): Your name as it appears on your tax records.
* **Address** (taxpayer_address): Your address as it appears on your tax records.
* **Charity Name** (charity_name): The name of the charity that received the gift.
* **Charity Registration** (charity_registration): The registration number of the charity.
* **Gift Value** (gift_value): The value of the gift made to the charity.
* **Gift Value (currency)** (gift_value_currency): The currency in which the gift was made (e.g., GBP, EUR, USD).
* **Gift Value Date** (gift_value_date): The date on which the gift was made.
* **Total Gift Amount** (total_gift_amount): The total value of the gift made to the charity.
* **Donor Declaration** (donor_declaration): A statement declaring that the gift was made by you.
* **Taxpayer Signature** (taxpayer_signature): Your signature, which confirms that the information provided is accurate.
* **Declaration Status** (declaration_status): The status of the declaration (e.g., completed, pending).
* **Charity Declaration** (charity_declaration): A statement declaring that the gift was made to the charity.
* **Charity Declaration Date** (charity_declaration_date): The date on which the charity received the gift.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* Only complete this form once for each gift or donation.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
