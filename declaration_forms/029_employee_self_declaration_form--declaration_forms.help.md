# Employee Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to provide information about themselves. Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by entering your name in the "name" field.
2.  Next, enter your email address in the "email" field.
3.  Then, input your phone number in the "phone" field.
4.  After that, select the date of your declaration in the "declaration_date" field.
5.  Finally, choose the time of your declaration in the "declaration_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents or personnel files.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
*   **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can contact you with.
*   **Declaration Date** (date, required): Select the date you are declaring this information.
*   **Declaration Time** (time, required): Choose the time you are declaring this information.
