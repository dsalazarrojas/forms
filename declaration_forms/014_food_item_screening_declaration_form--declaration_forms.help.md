# Food Item Screening Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a food item, its details, and the user's declaration regarding its status and category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant event type from the provided options.
2. Provide a brief description of the food item and its details.
3. Enter the item's number, if applicable.
4. Choose the item's status from the options provided.
5. Select the category that best describes the item.
6. Enter the date and time of the declaration.
7. Provide any supporting documentation or evidence related to the item.
8. Enter any relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event that this declaration is related to, such as "events" or "other".
  * This field determines the context in which the declaration is being made.
* **Item Details**: A brief description of the food item, including its key characteristics.
  * This field helps to provide a clear understanding of what the item is and what it entails.
* **Item Number**: The unique identifier or number assigned to the food item.
  * This field helps to track and reference the item accurately.
* **Item Description**: A detailed description of the food item.
  * This field provides additional information about the item, such as its composition or ingredients.
* **Item Status**: Choose the status of the item from the provided options, such as "Active", "inactive", or "cancelled".
  * This field indicates the current state of the item, which can be important for tracking and decision-making purposes.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the food item.
  * This field helps to classify the item and make it easier to find and reference.
* **Declaration Date**: The date on which the declaration is made.
  * This field helps to track when the declaration was made and when it becomes effective.
* **Declaration Time**: The time at which the declaration is made.
  * This field helps to track when the declaration is made and for audit purposes.
* **Supporting Documentation**: Any additional evidence or documentation that supports the declaration.
  * This field provides context and proof of the declaration, if necessary.
* **Contact Information**: The relevant contact information for the person or organization making the declaration.
  * This field helps to facilitate communication and follow-up actions.
