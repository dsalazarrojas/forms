# Statutory Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statutory Declaration Form is a personal information collection form used for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your first name, middle name, last name in the corresponding fields.
- Enter your city of residence.
- Select your country of origin if applicable.
- Provide your date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY format).
- Enter your occupation (if applicable).
- Enter your email address (if applicable).
- Enter your phone number (if applicable).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Middle name** (`middle_name`, text, optional): Enter your middle name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Address line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **City** (`city`, select_one, optional): Select your city of residence.
* **Country** (`country`, select_multiple, optional): Select your country of origin if applicable.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check your details before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If a field is required, ensure you have filled it in before submitting.
