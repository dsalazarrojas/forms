# Plagiarism Free Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to declare work as plagiarism-free, ensuring originality and authenticity. It's essential to provide accurate information to avoid any potential issues or misinterpretations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your details accurately and truthfully.
- Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals required for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submitter Name**: (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information to be associated with this declaration.
- **Original Work Details**: (`original_work_details`, text, optional): Provide a description of the original work, including its title, date, or other relevant details.
- **Declaration Date**: (`declaration_date`, date, optional): Select the date you're declaring the originality of the work.
- **Declaration Time**: (`declaration_time`, time, optional): Select the time of day you're making this declaration.
- **Plagiarism-Free Declaration**: (`plagiarism_free_declaration`, text, optional): Declare that your work is original and free from plagiarism.
- **Submitter Email**: (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Submitter Phone**: (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Confirm Submit**: (`confirm_submit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more options to confirm your understanding of the submission process.
  - Option 1: I understand that I'm declaring the originality of my work.
  - Option 2: I confirm that I have the necessary permissions or approvals for this submission.
  - Option 3: I acknowledge that I'll be contacted if any issues arise.
- **Other Declaration Details**: (`other_declaration_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information that's relevant to your declaration.
- **Submitter Note**: (`submitter_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to your submission.
- **Submission ID**: (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter any unique identifier for this submission, if applicable.
