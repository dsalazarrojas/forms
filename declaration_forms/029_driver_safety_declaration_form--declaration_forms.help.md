# driver_safety_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Driver Safety Declaration Form is a tool used to gather information from drivers regarding their safety concerns, incidents, or events that occurred during a journey or activity. This form helps in collecting data to better understand and address potential safety risks and improve the overall safety of drivers and vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the safety declaration you want to make (e.g., "Declaration Forms" or "Tracking Forms").
2. Provide a brief description of the incident or safety concern you want to report.
3. Fill in the description of the incident or safety concern in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the category of the safety declaration you want to make. This field is optional, but selecting the correct category will help us better understand the nature of your report.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed account of the incident or safety concern you want to report. This field is also optional, but it will help us better understand the context and severity of the issue.
* **Declaration Form/Tracking Form**: This field is a dropdown selection to categorize your declaration form into either "Declaration Forms" or "Tracking Forms". 

## Tips
Remember to be as detailed as possible when providing your description, and make sure to include any relevant information such as date, time, location, and any other context that may be helpful for us to better understand the incident or safety concern.
