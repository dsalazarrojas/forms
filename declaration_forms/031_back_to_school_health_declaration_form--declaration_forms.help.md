# Back To School Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a requirement for the back-to-school health declaration for a student. It provides the school administration with accurate and up-to-date information about the student's health status, allergies, medications, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide the student's legal name, parent or guardian's name, contact information, and health insurance details.
3. List the student's date of birth, grade level, and school name.
4. Provide the student's current health status, medical conditions, and allergies.
5. List the student's current medications and any physical activity restrictions.
6. Identify the student's emergency contacts and their contact information.
7. Declare your consent for the school to provide health services to your child.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Enter the legal name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the primary contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the contact email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address**: Enter the student's full street address.
* **Student Full Name**: Enter the legal name of the student.
* **Student Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's current or upcoming grade level.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the health insurance provider.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the student's health insurance policy or member ID number.
* **Primary Care Physician**: Enter the name and practice of the student's primary care physician.
* **Physician Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the primary care physician.
* **Immunization Status**: Select the student's immunization status.
* **Known Medical Conditions**: List any known medical conditions the student has.
* **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies the student has, including food, medication, insect, or environmental allergies.
* **Current Medications**: List all the student's current medications with their dosages.
* **Medication Administration Authorization**: Select whether the school staff can administer medication to the student.
* **Physical Activity Restrictions**: List any physical activity restrictions the student has.
* **Emergency Contact One Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of the student's primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Two Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of the student's secondary emergency contact.
* **Consent for School Health Services**: Select whether you consent to the school providing health services to your child.
* **Information Accuracy Declaration**: Select whether the information provided is accurate.
* **Date Signed**: Date and sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

# Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the school administration for clarification.
* Keep the form up-to-date and notify the school administration if any information changes.
* Review the form before signing to ensure accuracy and completeness.
