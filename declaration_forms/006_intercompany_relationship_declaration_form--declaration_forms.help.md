# Intercompany Relationship Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a relationship between an individual and your company. It captures information about the individual, such as their name, contact details, and relationship type, to verify their affiliation with your company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the individual's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the individual's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your position within the company in the "Position" field.
4. Enter the individual's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the individual's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Select the type of relationship between the individual and the company from the "Relationship Type" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the individual's relationship with the company in the "Related Party" field.
8. Enter the percentage of ownership or control held by the individual in the "Percentage Owned" field.
9. Select the date on which the declaration was made in the "Declaration Date" field.
10. Select the status of the submission from the "Submission Status" field.
11. Select the type of submitter from the "Submitter" field.
12. Enter any comments or notes about the submission in the "Comments" field.
13. Enter any notes about the declaration in the "Declaration Notes" field.
14. Enter any notes about the submission in the "Submission Notes" field.
15. Indicate whether the declaration has been signed by the individual in the "Declaration Signed?" field.
16. Enter the email address of the submitter in the "Submitter Email" field.
17. Enter the phone number of the submitter in the "Submitter Phone" field.
18. Select the type of relationship between the submitter and the company from the "Submitter Relationship Type" field.
19. Enter the email address of the related party in the "Related Party Email" field.
20. Enter the phone number of the related party in the "Related Party Phone" field.
21. Select the date on which the submission was made in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the individual's first name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter the individual's last name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Position**: Enter your position within the company.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the individual's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter the individual's email address.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company with which the individual is related.
* **Job Title**: Enter the individual's job title.
* **Relationship Type**: Select the type of relationship between the individual and the company (Business Partner, Client, Colleague, Other).
* **Related Party**: Briefly describe the individual's relationship with the company.
* **Percentage Owned**: Enter the percentage of ownership or control held by the individual.
* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date on which the declaration was made.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date on which the submission was made.
* **Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submitter**: Select the type of submitter (User, Manager, Other).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the submission.
* **Declaration Notes**: Enter any notes about the declaration.
* **Submission Notes**: Enter any notes about the submission.
* **Declaration Signed?**: Indicate whether the declaration has been signed by the individual.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Relationship Type**: Select the type of relationship between the submitter and the company (Business Partner, Client, Colleague, Other).
* **Related Party Email**: Enter the email address of the related party.
* **Related Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the related party.
* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date on which the declaration was made.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date on which the submission was made.
