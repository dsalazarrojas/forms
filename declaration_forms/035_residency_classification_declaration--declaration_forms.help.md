# Residency Classification Declaration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Residency Classification Declaration" form is a document used to declare an individual's residency status. This form is essential for various purposes, including tax, immigration, and government benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Name** in the text box provided.
2.  Enter your **Email** address in the text box.
3.  Fill in your **Phone** number in the text box.
4.  If necessary, add any additional **Contact Note** information.
5.  Select your **Residency Status** from the dropdown list, which includes options such as **Student**, **Worker**, or **Other**.
6.  Fill in the **Purpose of Stay** to describe your reason for declaration.
7.  Attach your **Proof of Address**, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (Name)**: A text field where you must type your full name.
*   **Email (Email)**: A field where you should enter your email address.
*   **Phone (Phone)**: A text field to type your phone number.
*   **Contact Note (Contact Note)**: A text field for any additional contact information you would like to provide.
*   **Residency Status (Residency Status)**: A dropdown list with options: **Student**, **Worker**, or **Other**.
*   **Purpose of Stay (Purpose of Stay)**: A text field to describe your reason for declaration.
*   **Proof of Address (Proof of Address)**: A field to attach your proof of address document (if required).

## Tips

*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have correctly selected your residency status from the dropdown list.
*   Provide a clear and concise purpose of stay for the declaration.
*   Attach proof of address, if required. 

Please note that the "Residency Classification Declaration" form is a critical document that requires attention to detail and accuracy. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the relevant authorities or contact the relevant department for assistance.
