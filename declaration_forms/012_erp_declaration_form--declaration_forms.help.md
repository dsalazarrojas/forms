# ERP Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to declare their involvement with ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) systems. It gathers information about the user's declaration status, declaration details, and related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field.
2. Answer the questions accordingly to provide accurate information.
3. Complete all required fields (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the introductory page of the form. You can write anything you like here.
* **Declaration Details** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether you declare 'Yes' or 'No' about your involvement with ERP systems.
* **Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **ERP System** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter any information related to the ERP system you are declaring about.
