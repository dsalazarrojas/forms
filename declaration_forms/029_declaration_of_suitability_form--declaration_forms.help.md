# Declaration Of Suitability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Declaration Of Suitability Form is used to gather information about a user's suitability for certain activities or roles.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "Declaration Of Suitability Form", follow the instructions below:

* Please review the form and make sure you are completing it for a relevant activity or role.
* Fill out all the relevant fields according to your knowledge and experience.
* Be honest about your roles and activities.
* Make sure to sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. declaration_of_suitability_form (Note)
* This field is a note field where you can write a brief description of why you are suitable for the activity or role.
### 2. roles (Select Multiple)
* Choose all the roles that you have experience or qualifications in.
### 3. placement (Select One)
* Choose the placement you are most familiar with.
### 4. activities (Select Multiple)
* Choose all the activities you are involved in or have experience with.
### 5. signed_at (Date)
* Fill out the date you signed this form.
### 6. signed_by (Text)
* Fill out your name as it appears on official documents.
### 7. role_ids (Select Multiple)
* This field is not currently used. Please ignore it.
### 8. role (Text)
* Fill out your role or job title.
