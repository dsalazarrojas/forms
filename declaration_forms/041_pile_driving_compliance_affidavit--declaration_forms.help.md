# Pile Driving Compliance Affidavit - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the presence of pile driving, a specific type of construction activity that involves the use of large piles of material to build structures, foundations, or roads. It is used for informational purposes only and does not replace any other necessary compliance documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out completely.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pile driving work details** (Pile driving work details, text, not required): This field is a brief description of the pile driving work being reported. Please provide a concise and accurate summary of the work performed.
* **Pile driving compliance affidavit** (Pile driving compliance affidavit, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the pile driving work complies with relevant regulations and laws. Select 'No' if the work does not comply.
* **Signed attestation** (Signed attestation, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable options to indicate that the attestation has been signed by the relevant personnel. Please note that this field is not required but recommended for completeness.
