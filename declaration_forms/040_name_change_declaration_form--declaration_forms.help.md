# Name Change Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Name Change Declaration Form is a document used to update a user's name information. This form is used to submit a name change request, providing details such as the old name, new name, reason for change, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your current name in the "current_name" field.
2. Enter your new name in the "new_name" field.
3. Provide a reason for the name change in the "reason_for_change" field.
4. Enter your current address in the "current_address" field.
5. Enter your new address in the "new_address" field.
6. Enter your current date of birth (DOB) in the "current_dob" field.
7. Enter your new DOB in the "new_dob" field.
8. If the reason for the name change is not listed, select the corresponding option from the "reason_for_change_other" field.
9. Choose the assigned tool from the "assigned_tool" field.
10. Click the "submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **prophets (Text Field)**: Enter your current name.
* **declaration_form_1 (Number Field)**: This field is not currently used.
* **current_name (Text Field)**: Enter your current name.
* **new_name (Text Field)**: Enter your new name.
* **reason_for_change (Text Field)**: Provide a reason for the name change.
* **current_address (Text Field)**: Enter your current address.
* **new_address (Text Field)**: Enter your new address.
* **current_dob (Date Field)**: Enter your current date of birth.
* **new_dob (Date Field)**: Enter your new date of birth.
* **reason_for_change_other (Text Field)**: If the reason for the name change is not listed, select "Other" and describe the reason.
* **assigned_tool (Select One Field)**: Choose the assigned tool for the name change.
* **output_file (Text Field)**: This field is not currently used.
