# freight_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The freight declaration form is used to collect information about a shipment, which is essential for customs clearance, taxation, or other regulatory purposes. This form helps to ensure that shipments are properly documented and cleared through customs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the Shipment Details section with the relevant information about the shipment you are declaring.
2. In the Goods Info section, enter a brief description of the goods being transported.
3. In the Shipment Party Info section, provide information about the party responsible for the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipment Details (shipment_details)**: This is the first section of the form where you should enter information about the shipment, such as its reference number, mode of transport, and other relevant details.
* **Goods Info (goods_info)**: Please enter a brief description of the goods being transported. This will help the authorities understand the contents of the shipment.
* **Shipment Party Info (shipment_party_info)**: This section requires information about the party responsible for the shipment, including their name, contact information, and other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each section accurately and completely to avoid delays in the clearance process.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please reach out to the relevant authorities or contact the form authors at form@gic.mx.
