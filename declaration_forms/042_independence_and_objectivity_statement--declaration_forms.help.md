# Independence And Objectivity Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to declare their independence and objectivity in a given context, such as audit or review engagements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Fill in the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I am independent** (`<name>`, text, optional): This field is meant to affirm your independence status. Please type 'yes' or 'no' to indicate whether you are independent or not.
* **My role in the organisation is** (`<name>`, text, optional): Describe your role within the organization to provide context to your independence declaration.
* **I have no conflicts of interest** (`<name>`, select multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to describe your potential conflicts of interest. Options include:
	+ No conflicts of interest
	+ Potential conflicts of interest
	+ Actual conflicts of interest
* **I am involved in this audit and/or review engagement** (`<name>`, select one, optional): Select 'yes' or 'no' to indicate your involvement in the audit or review engagement.
* **Date of independence** (`<name>`, date, optional): Enter the date of your independence, if applicable.
* **Time of independence** (`<name>`, time, optional): Enter the time of your independence, if applicable.
* **Team** (`<name>`, text, optional): Describe the team you are working with, if any.
* **Declaration statement** (`<name>`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise statement declaring your independence and objectivity.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to maintain the integrity of the declaration.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or experts.
* Please review and submit the form carefully before finalizing.
