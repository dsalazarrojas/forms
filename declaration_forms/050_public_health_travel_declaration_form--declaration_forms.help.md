# Public Health Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Travel Declaration Form is used to collect information from travelers about their travel history and any potential health risks associated with their trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the origin of your travel from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the destination city of your travel from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date you traveled in the `Travel Date` field.
4. Enter the length of your travel (in days) in the `Travel Length` field.
5. Select the reason for your travel from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the type of travel from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the frequency of your travel from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter any additional information about your travel origin in the `Travel Origin Details` field.
9. Enter any additional information about your travel destination in the `Travel Destination Details` field.
10. Enter any information about your travel accommodation in the `Travel Accommodation` field.
11. Enter the date you will return from your travel in the `Return Date` field.
12. Make a health declaration in the `Health Declaration` field.
13. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Origin** (Select One): Select the country or state you traveled from.
* **Destination City** (Select Multiple): Choose the city you traveled to.
* **Travel Date** (Date): Enter the date you traveled (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Travel Length** (Number of days): Enter the number of days you traveled.
* **Reason for Travel** (Text): Enter the reason for your travel.
* **Travel Type** (Select One): Select the type of travel you undertook (e.g. business, leisure).
* **Travel Frequency** (Select One): Select the frequency of your travel (e.g. regular, occasional).
* **Travel Purpose** (Select Multiple): Select the purpose of your travel (e.g. work, education).
* **Travel Origin Details** (Text): Enter any additional information about your travel origin.
* **Travel Destination Details** (Text): Enter any additional information about your travel destination.
* **Travel Accommodation** (Text): Enter any information about your travel accommodation.
* **Return Date** (Date): Enter the date you will return from your travel (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Health Declaration** (Text): Make a health declaration, including any health conditions you have or medications you are taking.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
