# coronavirus_self_declaration_form_queens_park_villas - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus self-declaration form is a crucial tool in the fight against the COVID-19 pandemic. It is designed to collect information about your health and travel history, which will help health officials take necessary measures to prevent the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Enter your information in the required fields.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **coronavirus_self_declaration_form_queens_park_villas** (Text Input): This is the title of the form. You do not need to fill this field.
* **declaration_form** (Text Input): This field is used to provide a brief description of your situation, if necessary.
* **have_covid_symptoms** (Select One):
	+ Yes: You are experiencing symptoms of COVID-19.
	+ No: You are not experiencing symptoms of COVID-19.
* **travel_history** (Text Input): Please provide a detailed account of your travel history, including dates and destinations.
* **fever** (Number Input): This field is intended for individuals who are experiencing fever. Please enter the number of days you have had a fever.
* **contact** (Select Multiple):
	+ No contacts: You have no close contacts.
	+ Has some contacts: You have a few close contacts.
	+ Many contacts: You have many close contacts.
* **contact_info** (Text Input): If you have contacts, provide their names and phone numbers or email addresses.
* **travel_date** (Date Input): If you have traveled recently, please enter the date you arrived.
* **return_date** (Date Input): If you have traveled recently, please enter the date you plan to return.
* **travel_country** (Select One):
	+ USA: You are from or are traveling in the United States.
	+ Other: You are from another country.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, and if you need more information or assistance, don't hesitate to ask for help.
