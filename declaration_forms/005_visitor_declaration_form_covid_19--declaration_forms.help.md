# visitor_declaration_form_covid_19 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from visitors about their health status, contact details, and other relevant information during the COVID-19 pandemic. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure the health and safety of all individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, including your contact details, visit reason, health declaration, and other relevant fields.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **visitor_info** (Text): Enter your personal information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **contact_info** (Text): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **contact_details** (Text): Enter your contact details, such as your address and emergency contact person's name.
* **visit_reason** (Text): State the reason for your visit.
* **health_declaration** (Select One): Indicate if you have been tested positive for COVID-19 or if you have been in contact with someone who has been tested positive.
* **health_decl_1** (Select Multiple): If you have symptoms, select all that apply (Fever, Fatigue, Cough, etc.).
* **health_decl_2** (Select Multiple): If you have been in contact with someone who has COVID-19, select all that apply (Close contact, Indirect contact, No contact, etc.).
* **contact_people** (Select One): Choose the number of people you have been in contact with.
* **last_contact** (Date): Enter the date of your last contact with the person who has COVID-19.
* **return_date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to return to work or your normal activities.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (Text): Enter your address.
* **emergency_contact** (Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_phone** (Text): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **emergency_address** (Text): Enter your emergency contact person's address.
* **signature** (Text): Provide your signature as proof of your identity.
* **signature_stamp** (Text): This field is not used for this form.
* **date** (Date): Enter the current date.
* **time** (Time): Enter the current time.
* **location** (Select One): Choose the location you are currently at.
* **employee** (Select One): Indicate if you are an employee.
* **employee_name** (Text): Enter your name if you are an employee.
* **employee_department** (Text): Enter your department if you are an employee.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
