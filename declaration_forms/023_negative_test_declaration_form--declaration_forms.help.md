# Negative Test Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a negative test for travel-related activities, ensuring passengers have the correct documentation for smooth travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and make sure you have the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, and make sure to provide accurate and relevant details.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they are complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Passenger Information
* **passenger_info** (`passenger_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the passenger.
  Please provide the passenger's name and relevant contact details, such as their email or phone number.

### Test Results
* **test_results** (`test_results`, select_one, optional): Indicate the test results.
  Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to answer whether the passenger has undergone a test.

### Test Type
* **test_type** (`test_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of test taken.
  Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to specify the type of test conducted on the passenger.

### Travel Date
* **travel_date** (date, optional): Enter the travel date.
  Enter the date of the intended travel, if applicable.

### Travel Time
* **travel_time** (time, optional): Enter the travel time.
  Enter the time of the intended travel, if applicable.

### Note
* **note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
  Enter any relevant comments or notes about the passenger's situation.

### Email
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the passenger's email address.
  Enter the passenger's email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter the passenger's phone number.
  Enter the passenger's phone number for communication purposes.

### Passenger Count
* **passenger_count** (number, optional): Enter the number of passengers.
  Enter the number of passengers traveling with you, if applicable.
