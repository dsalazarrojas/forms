# Beekeeping Hive Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beekeeping Hive Declaration Form is a form used to declare and report information about your beekeeping activities, including beekeeper information, apiary location, hive health and conditions, and certification. This form helps authorities track and monitor beekeeping activities in the area to prevent the spread of diseases and ensure public safety. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions asked in the form, and the information required to be entered.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Beekeeper Name** (`beekeeper_full_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. **Beekeeper Registration Number** (`registration_number`, text, required)
Enter the registration number issued by the Department of Agriculture as a beekeeper.

### 3. **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required)
Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding your beekeeping activities.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required)
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted regarding your beekeeping activities.

### 5. **Apiary Site Physical Address or GPS Coordinates** (`apiary_site_address`, text, required)
Enter the physical address or GPS coordinates of your apiary site.

### 6. **Number of Active Hives** (`number_of_active_hives`, number, required)
Enter the number of active hives you have at your apiary site.

### 7. **Primary Hive Type** (`hive_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of hive you primarily keep, including:

*   Langstroth
*   Top Bar
*   Warre
*   Flow Hive
*   Other

### 8. **Property Owner Consent** (`property_owner_consent`, select_one, required)
Indicate if you own the property where hives are located, or if you have obtained consent from the owner.

### 9. **Diseases Observed** (`disease_observed`, select_multiple, required)
Select all diseases you have observed in the last 6 months, including:

*   American Foulbrood (AFB)
*   European Foulbrood (EFB)
*   Varroa Mite Infestation
*   Nosema
*   Chalkbrood
*   None Observed

### 10. **Summary of Recent Treatments** (`treatment_history_summary`, text, optional)
Provide a brief summary of recent treatments applied to your hives, such as mite treatments or feeding.

### 11. **Significant Swarm Activity Noted** (`swarm_activity_noted`, select_one, optional)
Indicate if there has been significant swarm activity noted this season.

### 12. **Certification and Signature** (`declaration_agreement`, select_one, required)
Certify that this declaration is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

### 13. **Date of Declaration** (`declaration_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of declaration in the required format.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
