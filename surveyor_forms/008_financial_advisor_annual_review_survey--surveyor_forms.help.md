# Financial Advisor Annual Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisor Annual Review Survey is designed to collect demographic information and data about the client's financial situation to help advisors provide personalized and effective services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your name, email, phone number, and other requested information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your financial situation.
3. Review and update your information as necessary each year to ensure the advisor has the most current data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last review in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Last Review Score** (`last_review_score`, number, optional): Enter the score from your last review.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current financial institution.
