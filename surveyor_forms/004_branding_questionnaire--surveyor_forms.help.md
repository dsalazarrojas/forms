# Branding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Branding Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information about a client's brand identity and preferences. This information will be used to create a tailored brand identity that meets the client's goals and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Use specific examples and descriptions when possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client or Company Name** (`client_name`, text, required): The full name of the client or company.
* **What type of project is this** (`project_type`, select_one, required): The type of branding project you are looking to create. Select from the options: New brand creation, Logo redesign, Brand refresh, Product branding, Service branding, Rebranding campaign.
* **Current Brand Status** (`current_brand_status`, text, optional): A description of the current state of the brand (e.g., existing, new, rebranding).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): A brief explanation of what the business does.
* **Target Market and Demographics** (`target_market`, text, required): Describe your ideal customer.
* **How should your brand be positioned** (`brand_positioning`, text, required): Market positioning and differentiation.
* **Brand Archetype** (`brand_archetype`, select_one, optional): Select the archetype that best fits your brand. Options include: The Hero, The Innocent, The Sage, The Explorer, The Everyman, The Lover, The Creator, The Jester, The Magician, The Ruler, The Caregiver, The Outlaw.
* **Key Brand Attributes** (`key_brand_attributes`, text, required): List the primary characteristics of your brand.
* **Preferred Visual Style** (`visual_style`, select_multiple, optional): Select the visual style you prefer. Options include: Modern and minimalist, Classic and traditional, Playful and fun, Bold and dramatic, Elegant and sophisticated, Trendy and contemporary.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, text, optional): Any preferred colors or color schemes.
* **Font Style Preference** (`font_style`, select_one, optional): Choose the typography style you prefer. Options include: Sans-serif (modern), Serif (traditional), Handwritten (personal), Display (bold), Geometric (clean).
* **Imagery and Graphics Style** (`imagery_style`, select_one, optional): Describe your preferred visual elements. Options include: Photography, Illustrations, Icons and symbols, Abstract, Minimal elements.
* **Logo Design Preference** (`logo_style`, select_multiple, optional): Select the logo style you prefer. Options include: Word mark (text-based), Icon mark (symbol), Combination mark, Mascot, Lettermark, Abstract.
* **Competitor Brands to Reference** (`competitor_analysis`, text, optional): List competitor brands you know.
* **Design Inspiration Sources** (`inspiration_sources`, text, optional): Where the designer should draw inspiration from.
* **Core Brand Message** (`core_message`, text, required): What your brand should communicate.
* **Brand Personality** (`brand_personality`, text, optional): Describe how your brand should feel.
* **Must-Have Elements** (`must_haves`, text, optional): Non-negotiable elements to include.
* **Elements to Avoid** (`avoid_elements`, text, optional): What the designer should not include.
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when the branding is needed. Options include: ASAP, 2-4 weeks, 4-8 weeks, 8-12 weeks, Flexible.

Note that some fields are optional but still provide valuable insights for the designer. Ensure all required fields are completed to get the best results for your brand identity. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
