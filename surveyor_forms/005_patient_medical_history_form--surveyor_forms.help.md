# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Medical History Form is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather essential information about a patient's health background. This form helps medical professionals assess patient health and provide accurate treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the patient's consent before completing this form.
2. Fill in the full name of the patient.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. If applicable, provide the patient's email address.
5. Record the patient's weight.
6. List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
7. List any current medical conditions the patient is experiencing.
8. Note any surgeries the patient has undergone.
9. Describe the patient's healthy habits (e.g., regular exercise, balanced diet).
10. Describe the patient's unhealthy habits (e.g., smoking, excessive sugar intake).
11. Provide any additional medical history not already listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Required, Text): Enter the patient's full name, including their first name, last name, and any middle name(s).
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (Optional, Email): Enter the patient's email address, if applicable.
* **Weight** (Required, Number): Enter the patient's weight in kg or lbs.
* **Allergies** (Optional, Text): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient has, separated by commas.
* **Medical Conditions** (Optional, Text): List any current medical conditions the patient is experiencing, separated by commas.
* **Surgeries** (Optional, Text): List any surgeries the patient has undergone, separated by commas.
* **Healthy Habits** (Optional, Text): Describe the patient's healthy habits, such as regular exercise or a balanced diet.
* **Unhealthy Habits** (Optional, Text): Describe the patient's unhealthy habits, such as smoking or excessive sugar intake.
* **Medical History** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional medical history not already listed, separated by commas.
