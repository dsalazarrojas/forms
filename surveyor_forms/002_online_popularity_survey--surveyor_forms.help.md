# online_popularity_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' preferences and opinions on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and fill out the questions or fields as required or optionally.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one option from the list provided.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, note, not required): This section is an introduction to the survey. Read through it carefully before proceeding.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name, if you choose to provide it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Favorite Website** (`favorite_website`, text, not required): Select your favorite website from the list or enter a custom option.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, text, not required): Select your favorite color from the list or enter a custom option.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, text, not required): Select your favorite food from the list or enter a custom option.
* **Favorite Animal** (`favorite_animal`, text, not required): Select your favorite animal from the list or enter a custom option.
* **Agree With Statement** (`agree_with_statement`, select_one, not required): Select one of the three options provided that you agree with the most.
* **Disagree With Statement** (`disagree_with_statement`, select_one, not required): Select one of the three options provided that you disagree with the most.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the survey.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate with your responses.
* You can fill out this form as many times as you like, but each submission will be treated as a separate response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
