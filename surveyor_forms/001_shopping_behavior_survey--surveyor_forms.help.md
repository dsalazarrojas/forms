# Shopping Behavior Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your shopping behavior, habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all the mandatory questions (marked as required: true) to the best of your ability.
2. Answer the optional questions if you feel they are relevant to your shopping behavior.
3. Use the provided options for select questions to select one of the available answers.
4. Enter text responses in the boxes provided for text questions.
5. Make sure to fill out all the questions on this form.
