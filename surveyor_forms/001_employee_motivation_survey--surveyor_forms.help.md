# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Motivation Survey is designed to help your organization understand the level of motivation among its employees and identify areas of improvement. Please answer the questions honestly to provide valuable insights that can lead to a more positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any question, please leave the field blank.
3. For the "Motivation Level" question, choose the option that best describes your current level of motivation.
4. For the "Reasons for Motivation" question, be as specific as possible and provide details about what motivates you the most.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title within the organization.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been with the organization.
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your current level of motivation:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Motivation Level Other** (`motivation_level_other`, text, required): If you chose "Other" for the "Motivation Level" question, please provide details here.
* **Reasons for Motivation** (`reasons_for_motivation`, text, required): Please specify the reasons why you are motivated to work for the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where you work (e.g., office, remote, etc.).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, text, required): Enter a contact method for your manager (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Office Location** (`manager_office_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your manager's office.
* **Manager Work Hours** (`manager_work_hours`, time, required): Enter your manager's work hours (e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, etc.).
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your manager.
* **Questions** (`employee_survey_questions_0`, select_multiple, required): Choose the questions that best describe the areas you are motivated by:
	+ What
	+ When
	+ Where
* **Additional Comments** (`employee_survey_questions_1`, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about your motivation.
* **Surveyor Notes** (`surveyor_notes`, text, required): Enter any comments or notes about the survey.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required): Enter the name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Updated Time** (`updated_time`, time, required): Enter the time the form was last updated.
