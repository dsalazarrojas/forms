# Community Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about your awareness, usage, and experience with community services in the area. Your honest answers will help us understand the community's needs and perceptions, allowing us to better serve you and the community as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section, following the instructions provided for each field.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or skip it and move on.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they're accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_info`, `note`, optional): This section is a brief introduction to your identity. Please enter your name, email address, and ZIP code. This information is for internal purposes only and won't be shared publicly.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code where you live.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, optional): Select the age group that best fits you.
* **Household Type** (`household_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of household you belong to.
* **Awareness of Community Services** (`services_awareness`, `note`, optional): A brief description of community services you're aware of. This field is here to help you remember what services are available.
* **Which Services Are You Aware Of** (`known_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the services you're aware of. Select all that apply.
* **How Do You Learn About Services** (`information_source`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you learn about community services. Select all that apply.
* **Services Usage** (`services_usage`, `note`, optional): A brief description of your experience with community services.
* **Which Services Have You Used** (`used_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the services you've used. Select all that apply.
* **How Often Do You Use Services** (`frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you use community services.
* **Satisfaction Ratings** (`satisfaction_ratings`, `note`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with community services. Be honest about your feelings.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Community Services** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your overall satisfaction level.
* **How Easy Is It to Access Services** (`accessibility`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how easy it is to access community services.
* **How Helpful Is the Staff** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how helpful the staff is.
* **How Are the Wait Times** (`wait_time`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how long you have to wait for services.
* **How Is the Quality of Services** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the quality of services provided.
* **Barriers to Accessing Services** (`barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the barriers you face when accessing community services. Select all that apply.
* **Do You Have Unmet Needs** (`unmet_needs`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you have unmet needs.
* **What Services Do You Need** (`unmet_needs_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the services you need but cannot access.
* **What Improvements Would You Suggest** (`improvements`, `text`, optional): Share your ideas for improvements.
* **What Additional Services Would Help** (`additional_services`, `text`, optional): Suggest additional services that would be helpful.
* **Would You Recommend Our Services** (`recommend_services`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would recommend our services.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share anything else you want to say.

Please let us know if you have any questions or need help with any field. We'll be happy to assist you!
