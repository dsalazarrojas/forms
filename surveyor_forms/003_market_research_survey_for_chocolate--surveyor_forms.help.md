# Market Research Survey For Chocolate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This market research survey for chocolate is a customer feedback form designed to gather information about your purchasing habits, contact preferences, and demographic details. Your responses will help us improve the customer experience and inform product development.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Answer the questions in each page to the best of your ability.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Use the available fields as guidance to provide the most accurate and complete responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This page provides an overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Demographics** (`demographics_page`, text, required: false): This page asks for your basic demographic details, such as your date of birth and time zone. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Purchasing Habits** (`purchasing_habits_page`, text, required: false): This page is designed to gather information about your purchasing habits and behavior.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info_page`, text, required: false): This page requires your contact information, including your name and email or phone number. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date details.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method_page`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted by us in the future.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences_page`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred contact methods (e.g., text message, phone call, email, social media, or in-person).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth_page`, date, required: false): Please provide your date of birth accurately.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone_page`, time, required: false): This field is not currently supported by the form. Please ignore it.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page`, note, required: false): This page is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with our chocolate products.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion_page`, note, required: false): This is a note field to acknowledge that you have completed the survey.

## Tips
If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us through the contact information provided above. We will do our best to assist you.
