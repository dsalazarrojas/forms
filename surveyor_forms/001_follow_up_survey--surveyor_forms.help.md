# Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Follow Up Survey form is designed to gather information from customers after a follow-up action has been taken. This form helps to ensure that the follow-up process is efficient and effective, allowing us to stay in touch with customers and gather feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct rating and follow-up type options that apply to your situation.
3. Provide accurate contact information if necessary.
4. Enter any additional comments or details about the follow-up action.
5. Ensure that the follow-up survey date and time are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required): Please enter the customer's name.
* **Rating** (`rating`, required): Select the rating that best describes your experience with our follow-up action.
* **Comments** (`comments`, required): Enter any additional comments or details about the follow-up action.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, required): If you would like to provide your contact information, please do so.
* **Follow Up Type** (`follow_up_type`, required): Select the type of follow-up action that was taken.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, required): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, required): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Details** (`follow_up_details`, optional): Enter any additional details about the follow-up action.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): If you would like to provide your email address, please enter it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it.
* **Address** (`address`, optional): If you would like to provide your address, please enter it.
* **Follow Up Survey Date** (`follow_up_survey_date`, required): Enter the date of the follow-up survey.
* **Follow Up Survey Time** (`follow_up_survey_time`, required): Enter the time of the follow-up survey.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct rating and follow-up type options that apply to your situation.
* If you have any additional comments or details, please enter them in the comments field.
* If you would like to provide contact information, please do so accurately and completely.
* Double-check the follow-up survey date and time for accuracy.
