# Artist Recovery Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Recovery Survey Form is designed to help artists who are experiencing difficulties or challenges in their artistic endeavors. This form is a tool to gather information on the nature of these challenges, their duration, and the impact on their artistic practice. The data collected will be used to inform support services and resources that can help artists recover from these challenges.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the correct response for each field and provide any relevant information. All fields are marked as required or optional, depending on the field's label. Please read each field carefully and select the most accurate response to ensure that your answers are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on any follow-up materials or correspondence.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact and communication.
* **Primary Art Medium or Discipline** (`art_medium`, `text`, required): Select your primary art medium or discipline, such as painting, sculpture, music, or dance.
* **Years Practicing Your Art** (`years_practicing`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have been practicing your art.
* **Area of Recovery or Challenge** (`recovery_focus`, `select_one`, required): Select the area where you are experiencing difficulties or challenges, such as Mental Health, Financial Stability, or Creative Block.
* **Duration of Current Challenge - Months** (`problem_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of months you have been experiencing the current challenge.
* **Description of the Challenge** (`problem_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the challenge you are facing.
* **Impact on Artistic Practice** (`impact_on_art`, `text`, required): Describe how the current challenge is affecting your artistic practice.
* **Sources or Root Causes** (`sources_of_challenge`, `text`, optional): Identify what contributed to this situation, such as a specific event or person.
* **Support or Resources Already Accessessed** (`support_received`, `text`, optional): Mention any support or resources you have already accessed, such as therapy or groups.
* **Current Recovery Stage** (`recovery_stage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current stage of your recovery, such as Just Beginning, Early Stage, or Maintenance.
* **Main Barriers to Recovery** (`recovery_barriers`, `text`, optional): List any main barriers you are facing in your recovery process.
* **Types of Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of support or resources you need to aid in your recovery, such as Professional Counseling or Peer Support Groups.
* **Personal Recovery Goals** (`recovery_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your personal recovery goals, such as regaining confidence or finding new inspiration.
* **Strengths and Assets to Build On** (`strengths_assets`, `text`, optional): List any strengths or assets that you can use to support your recovery.
* **Willing to Share Experience with Others** (`willing_to_share`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you are willing to share your experience with others.
* **Willing to be Contacted for Follow-up** (`follow_up_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are willing to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful in your recovery process.

## Tips

Please answer each question honestly and accurately, and provide as much detail as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to support you in your recovery process.
