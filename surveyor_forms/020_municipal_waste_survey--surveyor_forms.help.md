# municipal_waste_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a resident's waste management practices for a survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your municipal information.
2. Select the type of household you live in (Home, Commercial, Other).
3. Choose the number of people living in your household.
4. Select the methods you use for waste disposal (Recycling, Composting, Landfill).
5. Select how often you dispose of waste (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
6. Choose the type of waste you generate (Organic, Inorganic, Glass, Other).
7. Select where you dispose of waste (Home, Curbside, Landfill, Other).
8. Describe the type of waste you generate.
9. Choose how often you generate waste (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
10. Confirm if you have completed the survey.
11. Provide any additional contact information for follow-up queries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Municipal Information**: Please enter your municipal information for accurate survey results.
* **Household Type**: Select the type of household you live in (Home, Commercial, Other).
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Waste Disposal**: Choose the methods you use for waste disposal (Recycling, Composting, Landfill).
* **Recycling Frequency**: Select how often you dispose of waste (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
* **Collection Type**: Choose where you dispose of waste (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Other).
* **Waste Type**: Select the type of waste you generate (Organic, Inorganic, Glass, Other).
* **Disposal Location**: Select where you dispose of waste (Home, Curbside, Landfill, Other).
* **Waste Description**: Please describe the type of waste you generate.
* **Waste Frequency**: Choose how often you generate waste (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Survey Completed**: Confirm if you have completed the survey.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact information for follow-up queries.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the provided contact information.
* The survey will help us improve waste management practices in our community. Thank you for your participation.
