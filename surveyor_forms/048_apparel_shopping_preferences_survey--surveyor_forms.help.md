# Apparel Shopping Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your preferences when it comes to shopping for apparel, helping businesses better understand your needs and tailor their product offerings to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Gender preference for apparel** from the options provided. This will help us understand which type of products you are interested in.
2. Choose your **Price sensitivity** by selecting the price range you typically spend on each purchase.
3. Select your **Preferred shopping channel** from the options provided, as this will influence how we recommend products to you.
4. For **Size consistency experiences**, select whether sizes match your expectations across brands.
5. Describe your **Favorite styles or brands** to us. Feel free to provide as many examples as you'd like.
6. Indicate how often you **Shop for apparel**, to help us understand your purchase frequency.
7. Finally, add any **Additional comments** you may have about your shopping experiences or any unmet needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gender preference for apparel**: Helps us understand which type of products you are interested in. Select one of the following options:
	+ Womens
	+ Mens
	+ Unisex
	+ No preference
* **Price sensitivity**: This question helps us understand how much you're willing to spend on each purchase. Select one of the following options:
	+ Budget under 25
	+ Mid 25 to 75
	+ Premium over 75
* **Preferred shopping channel**: This question helps us understand how you prefer to shop. Select one of the following options:
	+ Online
	+ In store
	+ Mobile app
	+ Catalog
* **Size consistency experiences**: This question helps us understand if sizes match your expectations across brands. Select one of the following options:
	+ Consistent
	+ Variable
	+ Unreliable
* **Favorite styles or brands**: Describe your favorite styles or brands to us. You can provide as many examples as you'd like.
* **Shopping frequency**: This question helps us understand how often you shop for apparel. Select one of the following options:
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ A few times a year
	+ Rarely
* **Additional comments**: Share any additional comments or preferences you may have about your shopping experiences or any unmet needs.

## Tips
- Be honest with your answers, as this will help us provide the best possible experience for you.
- You can provide multiple examples of your favorite styles or brands.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them with us.
- This form should take you about 5-10 minutes to complete. If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
