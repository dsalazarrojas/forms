# Student Well Being Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Well Being Survey is a tool designed to collect data on students' emotional states, sleep patterns, and leisure time habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

*   Start on the first page, which is an introduction to the survey.
*   On the subsequent pages, select your answers to each question using the options provided in each field.
*   You can choose one response from the options for each question.
*   Questions are labeled with numbers (e.g., question1, question2, etc.) for easy reference.
*   Each question provides options in the format of adjectives (e.g., Very Happy, Anxious, etc.).
*   For time fields (questions 4, 5, 6, and 7), simply enter the time you go to bed or wake up.
*   You can fill out the form at your own pace, and you can revisit any question at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (1, **Introduction**, note, none): This is a general introduction to the survey. It does not require any response.
*   **How often do you feel happy** (2, question1, select\_one, Very Happy, Happy, Not Very Happy, Not Happy): Answer this question based on your emotional state, with options ranging from Very Happy to Not Happy.
*   **How often do you feel anxious** (3, question2, select\_one, Very Anxious, Anxious, Not Very Anxious, Not Anxious): Choose how often you feel anxious, with options from Very Anxious to Not Anxious.
*   **How often do you feel stressed** (4, question3, select\_one, Very Stressed, Stressed, Not Very Stressed, Not Stressed): Select how often you feel stressed, with options from Very Stressed to Not Stressed.
*   **What time do you go to bed** (5, question4, time, none): Enter the time you go to bed.
*   **What time do you wake up** (6, question5, time, none): Enter the time you wake up.
