# Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from employees about their job satisfaction, work-life balance, and overall satisfaction with their job. This feedback will help management understand what employees like and dislike about their job, which will enable us to make informed decisions to improve the work environment and increase employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2.  Answer all questions that apply to you. You can skip questions that do not relate to your experience.
3.  Use clear and concise language to answer each question.
4.  Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Satisfaction Survey (1)**: Answer this question with a brief description of your overall satisfaction with your job.
* **Job Satisfaction (2)**: Answer this question with a brief description of your level of satisfaction with your job.
* **Work-Life Balance (3)**: Choose one of the options to indicate if you feel you have a good balance between your work and personal life.
* **Company Values (4)**: Select all that apply, to indicate which company values you strongly agree with.
* **Feedback (5)**: Write a brief description of any feedback or suggestions you have about your job or work environment.
* **Employee Satisfaction Survey 2 (6)**: Answer this question with a brief description of your overall satisfaction with your job (similar to question 1).
* **Job Satisfaction 2 (7)**: Answer this question with a brief description of your level of satisfaction with your job (similar to question 2).
* **Work-Life Balance 2 (8)**: Choose one of the options to indicate if you feel you have a good balance between your work and personal life (similar to question 3).
* **Employee Satisfaction Survey 3 (9)**: Answer this question with a brief description of your overall satisfaction with your job (similar to question 1).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use clear and concise language.
* Answer all questions that apply to you.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, use the feedback field to provide them.
