## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **category**: This field is used to select the type of farming category, which can be either "Organic Farming" or "Non-Organic Farming". Please choose the option that best describes your farm.
*   **farm_size**: This field is used to select the size of your farm, which can be "Small", "Medium", "Large", or "Extra Large". Please choose the option that best describes your farm.
*   **farm_type**: This field is used to select the type of farm, which can be "Crop Farm" or "Livestock Farm". Please choose the option that best describes your farm.
*   **crop_type**: This field is used to select the type of crops your farm produces, which can be "Grains", "Fruits", or "Vegetables". Please choose the option that best describes your farm's crops.
*   **market_target**: This field is used to select the target market for your farm's produce, which can be "Local", "Wholesale", or "Retail". Please choose the option that best describes your farm's market.
*   **farm_location**: This field is used to select the geographical location of your farm, which can be "Mountainous" or "Flatland". Please choose the option that best describes your farm's location.
*   **market_size**: This field is used to select the size of your farm's market, which can be "Small", "Medium", or "Large". Please choose the option that best describes your farm's market.
*   **farm_products**: This field is used to describe the products your farm produces, which can be any type of produce or livestock. Please enter your farm's products in this field.
*   **farm_income**: This field is used to report your farm's income, which can be a numerical value. Please enter your farm's income in this field.
*   **farm_profit**: This field is used to report your farm's profit, which can be a numerical value. Please enter your farm's profit in this field.
*   **farm_value**: This field is used to report your farm's value, which can be a numerical value. Please enter your farm's value in this field.
*   **market_share**: This field is used to report your farm's market share, which can be a numerical value. Please enter your farm's market share in this field.
*   **market_trend**: This field is used to describe the trend in your farm's market, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your observations about your farm's market trend.
*   **market_growth**: This field is used to report your farm's market growth, which can be a numerical value. Please enter your farm's market growth in this field.
*   **market_challenges**: This field is used to describe the challenges you face in your farm's market, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your observations about your farm's market challenges.
*   **market_opportunities**: This field is used to describe the opportunities you see in your farm's market, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your observations about your farm's market opportunities.
*   **customer_satisfaction**: This field is used to report your customer satisfaction, which can be "High", "Medium", or "Low". Please choose the option that best describes your customer satisfaction.
*   **market_competition**: This field is used to report the level of competition in your farm's market, which can be "High", "Medium", or "Low". Please choose the option that best describes your market competition.
*   **market_potential**: This field is used to report your farm's market potential, which can be "High", "Medium", or "Low". Please choose the option that best describes your farm's market potential.
*   **market_threats**: This field is used to report the threats you face in your farm's market, which can be "High", "Medium", or "Low". Please choose the option that best describes your market threats.
*   **market_trends**: This field is used to describe the trends you observe in your farm's market, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your observations about your farm's market trends.
*   **customer_feedback**: This field is used to gather feedback from your customers, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your customers' feedback.
*   **customer_expectations**: This field is used to describe the expectations of your customers, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your customers' expectations.
*   **customer_demands**: This field is used to describe the demands of your customers, which can be a free-text field. Please enter your customers' demands.

By following these steps and providing accurate information, you will be able to complete the Organic Farming Market Assessment Survey efficiently and effectively.
