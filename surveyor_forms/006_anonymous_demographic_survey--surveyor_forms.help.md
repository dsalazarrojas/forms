# Anonymous Demographic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for collecting anonymous demographic information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your relevant information. You will need to select the corresponding options for each field, and you can leave the optional fields blank if you prefer. Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately, and do not hesitate to provide any additional comments if you feel it's necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select the age group that best represents your age. This field helps us understand the age distribution of our respondents.
* **Gender identity** (`gender_identity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best represents your gender identity. This field helps us understand the gender distribution of our respondents.
* **Education level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the highest level of education you have achieved. This field helps us understand the educational background of our respondents.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your current employment status. This field helps us understand the employment status of our respondents.
* **Household income range** (`household_income_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the income range that best represents your household income. This field is optional and helps us understand the income distribution of our respondents.
* **Geographic region** (`geographic_region`, `text`, optional): Enter the city, state, or region where you are located. This field is optional and helps us understand the geographic distribution of our respondents.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional feedback or context about your responses, you can provide it here. This field is optional and helps us understand any other relevant information about our respondents.
 
**Tips:**
* Please answer truthfully and accurately.
* You can leave optional fields blank if you prefer.
* Do not answer if you are not comfortable sharing any information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
