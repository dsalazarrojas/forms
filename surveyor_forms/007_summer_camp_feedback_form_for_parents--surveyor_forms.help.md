# Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their child's experience at summer camp. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, please provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: What did the camper enjoy most about camp?
* **What did the camper enjoy most about camp** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_page_1`, text, required/optional): This question is asking about your child's favorite experience or activity during their time at summer camp. Please describe what they enjoyed most, whether it was a specific activity, a game, a counselor, or something else.

### Page 2: What would you like to see improved at camp?
* **What would you like to see improved** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_page_2`, text, required/optional): This question is asking for your thoughts on how the summer camp could be improved. Please share your ideas and suggestions for what you think would make the camp better for your child and other campers.

### Page 3: Would you recommend this camp to a friend?
* **Would you recommend this camp to a friend?** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_page_3`, select_one, required/optional): Please select "True" if you would recommend the summer camp to a friend, and "False" otherwise. This will help us understand how we're doing and help us make improvements.

### Page 4: What date did the camper attend summer camp?
* **What date did the camper attend summer camp?** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_page_4`, date, required/optional): Enter the date (MM/DD/YYYY) when your child attended summer camp. This will help us keep track of your child's attendance.

### Page 5: What time did the camper start at summer camp?
* **What time did the camper start at summer camp?** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_page_5`, time, required/optional): Enter the time (HH:MM AM/PM) when your child started at summer camp. This will help us understand their schedule.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the "What would you like to see improved" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and providing us with your valuable feedback!
