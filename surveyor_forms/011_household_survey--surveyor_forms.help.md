# Household Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects household information from respondents, including demographics, employment status, income, education level, language, and other related details, for research or statistical analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the form carefully to understand the questions and options.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately based on the household's characteristics.
3. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account the household's demographics and employment status.
4. If the household has multiple members with different attributes, select the attribute that best represents the majority or primary household characteristics.
5. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Information**: This field is not visible on the form as it is not required.
* **Household Demographics**: Select the correct option that best describes the household's residential area (Urban, Rural, Not Specified).
* **Employed Members**: Enter the number of employed members in the household.
* **Employed Household**: Enter the number of employed members in the household.
* **Income**: Enter the household's total income (optional, but recommended for financial analysis).
* **Employed Education**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (High School, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Occupation**: Select the type of occupation that best represents the employed members in the household (Service, White Collar, Blue Collar, Not Specified).
* **Employed Family**: Indicate whether the household has employed family members (True, False).
* **Employed Hobbies**: Select the hobbies or interests of the employed members in the household (Sports, Music, Reading, Traveling, Not Specified).
* **Employed Housing**: Indicate whether the household has employed members who own their own housing (True, False).
* **Employed Marital Status**: Select the marital status of the employed members in the household (Single, Married, Divorced, Widow, Not Specified).
* **Employed Marital Status 2**: Select the gender of the employed members in the household (Male, Female, Not Specified).
* **Employed Education Level**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (Less than High School, High School, Some College, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Income Range**: Select the income range of the employed members in the household (Under 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-20000, 20000-100000, Not Specified).
* **Employed Income Range 2**: Select the income range of the employed members in the household (Less than 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-20000, 20000-100000, Not Specified).
* **Employed Income Range 3**: Select the income range of the employed members in the household (Less than 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-20000, Not Specified).
* **Employed Income Range 4**: Select the income range of the employed members in the household (Under 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-10000, 10000-50000, 50000-100000, Not Specified).
* **Employed Education Level 2**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (Less than High School, High School, Some College, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Education Level 3**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (Less than High School, High School, Some College, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Education Level 4**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (Less than High School, High School, Some College, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Education Level 5**: Select the highest education level of the employed members in the household (Less than High School, High School, Some College, College, Not Specified).
* **Employed Language**: Select the language spoken by the employed members in the household (English, Spanish, Not Specified).
* **Employed Language 2**: Select the language spoken by the employed members in the household (English, Spanish, Not Specified).
* **Employed Language 3**: Select the language spoken by the employed members in the household (English, Not Specified).
* **Employed Language 4**: Select the language spoken by the employed members in the household (English, Not Specified).

## Tips

* Make sure to answer the questions truthfully and accurately based on the household's characteristics.
* If there are multiple members in the household with different attributes, select the attribute that best represents the majority or primary household characteristics.
* If you are unsure about any question or option, please contact the survey administrator for clarification.
