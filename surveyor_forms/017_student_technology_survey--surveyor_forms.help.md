# Student Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Student Technology Survey is designed to gather information about students' and teachers' use and attitudes towards technology in educational settings. The survey aims to provide insights that can inform decision-making about technology integration in the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and understand what is being asked.
2. Choose the answer that best describes your situation or attitude.
3. Select one option from the options provided for each question.
4. Ensure that you have answered all questions before submitting the form.
