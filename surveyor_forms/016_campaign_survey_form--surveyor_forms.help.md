# Campaign Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Campaign Survey Form is designed to gather information from respondents on their demographic profile, opinions on the campaign, and their likelihood of voting in the next election. The form helps us understand the campaign's target audience and tailor our efforts to best serve their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Select the corresponding options for each multiple-choice question.
3. Type in your answers to the open-ended questions.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required\*: Provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required\*: Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, required\*: Enter your phone number.
- **Street Address** (`respondent_address`, text, required\*: Enter your street address.
- **City** (`respondent_city`, text, required\*: Enter your city.
- **State** (`respondent_state`, text, required\*: Enter your state.
- **Zip Code** (`respondent_zip`, text, required\*: Enter your zip code.
- **Age Group** (`age_group`, select\_one, required\*: Choose your age group from the options below:
  - 18-25
  - 26-35
  - 36-45
  - 46-55
  - 56-65
  - 65 and older
  - Prefer not to say
- **Are you a registered voter** (`voter_registration`, select\_one, required\*: Indicate if you are a registered voter.
  - True
  - False
  - Unsure
- **Party Affiliation** (`party_affiliation`, select\_one, optional\*: Choose your party affiliation from the options below:
  - Democratic
  - Republican
  - Independent
  - Other
  - Prefer not to say
- **How familiar are you with our candidate** (`candidate_familiarity`, select\_one, required\*: Indicate your level of familiarity with the candidate.
  - Very Familiar
  - Somewhat Familiar
  - Not Very Familiar
  - Not Familiar at All
- **Would you support our candidate** (`candidate_support`, select\_one, required\*: Indicate if you would support our candidate in the upcoming election.
  - Definitely Yes
  - Probably Yes
  - Undecided
  - Probably No
  - Definitely No
- **Which issues are most important to you** (`important_issues`, select\_multiple, optional\*: Choose the issues that are most important to you.
  - Economy
  - Healthcare
  - Education
  - Environment
  - Public Safety
  - Taxes
  - Jobs
  - Other
- **Do you agree with the candidate's position on key issues** (`candidate_position_agreement`, select\_one, optional\*: Indicate if you agree or disagree with the candidate's position on key issues.
  - Strongly Agree
  - Agree
  - Neutral
  - Disagree
  - Strongly Disagree
- **What are your main concerns about current leadership** (`main_concerns`, text, optional\*: Share your concerns about current leadership.
- **What solutions are most important to you** (`solutions_important`, text, optional\*: Share the solutions that are most important to you.
- **Are you interested in volunteering for the campaign** (`volunteer_interest`, select\_one, optional\*: Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for the campaign.
  - True
  - False
  - Maybe
- **How likely are you to vote in the next election** (`likely_to_vote`, select\_one, required\*: Indicate how likely you are to vote in the next election.
  - Very Likely
  - Likely
  - Undecided
  - Unlikely
  - Very Unlikely
- **Would you persuade friends and family to support our candidate** (`friend_persuade`, select\_one, optional\*: Indicate if you would persuade friends and family to support our candidate.
  - True
  - False
  - Maybe
- **Additional comments or feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, optional\*: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the campaign.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling in your information.
* Please review your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
