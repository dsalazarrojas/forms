# post_festival_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and information from attendees after a festival or event. Your answers will help us better understand your experience and preferences. This will aid in improving future events and making sure that they are tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name**, which will help us address any communication with you regarding your feedback.
2. Provide your **Email** address so we can contact you if needed.
3. Enter the **Date** and **Time** you attended the festival.
4. Share any **Note** or comment you might have about the event.
5. Optionally, provide your **Phone Number** for further contact.
6. For the **Favorite Food** question, select your preferred food from the options: Pineapple, Orange, Pizza, or Pears.
7. For the **Favorite Color** question, select all the colors that you like from the options: red, blue, green, or purple.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text): Enter your first name for us to address any communication with you.
* **Email** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`Date`, date): Enter the date you attended the festival.
* **Time** (`Time`, time): Enter the time you attended the festival.
* **Note** (`Note`, note): Share any note or comment about your experience.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text): Optionally, provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Favorite Food** (`Favorite Food`, select_one): Select your preferred food from the options: Pineapple, Orange, Pizza, or Pears.
* **Favorite Color** (`Favorite Color`, select_multiple): Select all the colors you like from the options: red, blue, green, or purple.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields for us to better understand your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the **Note** field to share them.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the provided email or phone number.
