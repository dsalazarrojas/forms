# Subscriber Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Subscriber Survey" form is used to collect demographic and contact information from our subscribers. This information will help us understand our subscribers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as requested.
2. For the "First name" field, enter your given name.
3. For the "Last name" field, enter your last name.
4. For the "Email" field, enter your valid email address.
5. For the "Phone" field, enter your phone number.
6. For the "Date of birth" field, enter the date of your birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Once you've filled out all the fields, click "Submit" to save your survey responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter the date of your birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
