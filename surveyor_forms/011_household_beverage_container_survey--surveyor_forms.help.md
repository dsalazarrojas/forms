# Household Beverage Container Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Beverage Container Survey is a form designed to collect information about household beverage containers. This data will help us better understand household habits and consumption patterns related to beverage containers.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your current household situation.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which is indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. Make sure to check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Beverage Container Survey Page 1** (`household_beverage_container_survey_page1`): This is the first page of the form. Please start your answers from here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info_page`): Select the option that best represents your contact information. This information is optional.
  * Contact Info Option 1
  * Contact Info Option 2
  * Contact Info Option 3
* **Household Beverage Container Type** (`household_beverage_container_type_page`): Choose the type of household beverage container you are most frequently using:
  * Household Beverage Container Type 1
  * Household Beverage Container Type 2
  * Household Beverage Container Type 3
* **Household Beverage Container Size** (`household_beverage_container_size_page`): If you have multiple containers of the same type, estimate the average size you use:
  * (Note: This field should accept a numerical value, but it's not provided in the YAML)
* **Household Beverage Container Content** (`household_beverage_container_content_page`): Select the type of content or liquid you are storing in your containers:
  * Household Beverage Container Content 1
  * Household Beverage Container Content 2
  * Household Beverage Container Content 3
* **Household Beverage Container Shape** (`household_beverage_container_shape_page`): Choose the shape of your household beverage container:
  * Household Beverage Container Shape 1
  * Household Beverage Container Shape 2
  * Household Beverage Container Shape 3
* **Household Beverage Container Material** (`household_beverage_container_material_page`): Select the type of material of your household beverage container:
  * Household Beverage Container Material 1
  * Household Beverage Container Material 2
  * Household Beverage Container Material 3
* **Surveyor Comments** (`surveyor_comments_page`): If you have any additional comments or information about your household beverage container, please provide them here.
* **Surveyor Signature** (`surveyor_signature_page`): This will store the date and time of your submission.
* **Household Beverage Container Surveyor Name** (`household_beverage_container_surveyor_name`): Please enter your name as the surveyor who conducted this survey.
* **Household Beverage Container Surveyor Phone** (`household_beverage_container_surveyor_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Household Beverage Container Surveyor Email** (`household_beverage_container_surveyor_email`): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`): This will store the date of your submission.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory and are optional for the user to fill out.
