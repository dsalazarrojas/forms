# Mood Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mood Tracking Form is designed to help you monitor and track your mental health and well-being over time. The form collects information on various aspects of your emotional state, physical health, and social support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) if required.
2. Select or enter your answers for each field.
3. Scroll down to the next page to access additional fields.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 until you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mood Tracking Form (\*):** This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank.
* **Mood Rating (\*):** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current mood. A higher number represents a better mood.
* **Emotional State:** Describe your current emotional state in a few words or sentences.
* **Physical Energy:** Describe your current physical energy level, e.g., high, low, or moderate.
* **Sleep Quality (\*):** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current sleep quality. A higher number represents better sleep quality.
* **Pain Level (\*):** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current pain level. A higher number represents less pain.
* **Medication Usage:** List any medications you are currently taking, e.g., antidepressants, painkillers, etc.
* **Medication Side Effects:** Describe any side effects you are experiencing from medications.
* **Medical Conditions:** List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing, e.g., diabetes, arthritis, etc.
* **Social Support:** Describe your current level of social support, e.g., friends, family, etc.
* **Anxiety Level:** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current anxiety level. A higher number represents less anxiety.
* **Depression Level:** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current depression level. A higher number represents less depression.
* **Stress Level:** Select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your current stress level. A higher number represents less stress.
* **Self-Care:** Describe any self-care activities you are currently engaging in, e.g., exercise, meditation, etc.
* **Emotional State Change:** Describe any changes or improvements you've noticed in your emotional state.
