# Electrical Services Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have received electrical services from our company. We value your opinion and want to ensure that you are satisfied with the quality of work done.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
2. Select the date you received our services:
	* **Service Date**: Select the date you received our services.
3. Choose the type of service you received:
	* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you received (Installation, Repair, or Maintenance).
4. Rate your satisfaction:
	* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service you received (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Share your experience:
	* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback about your experience with our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Your phone number (optional). If you provide your phone number, we may use it to contact you about your service or to provide updates.
* **Service Date**: The date you received our services. This will help us track when we provided service to you.
* **Service Type**: The type of service you received from us. This will help us understand what type of service to focus on improving.
* **Rating**: Your rating of our service. We use this information to monitor our performance and make changes as needed.
* **Technician Name**: This is the name of the technician who provided service to you. If you don't provide this information, we will use our records to try to identify the technician.
* **Question**: This field is for you to share any questions or concerns you have about our services.
* **Comments**: This is where you can share any comments or feedback about your experience with our services.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields, as they will help us better understand your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your service, please contact us through our email address provided on the form.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this form.
