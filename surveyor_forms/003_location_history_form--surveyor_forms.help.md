# Location History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather location information for contact history, which is vital for public health interventions and outbreak management. It helps track the spread of a virus or disease through human contact or other means.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have the necessary information about the location in question.
- Review the form sections and ensure you have not previously submitted data on this location.
- Complete each section according to the instructions provided for each field.
- Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Submit the form to help track the spread of the virus or disease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contacted Person** (`user_contacted`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who was contacted at the location in question. This helps track individual interactions.
* **Last Location Visited** (`location_visited`, text, optional): Record the specific location where the person was last known to be at. This helps identify potential hotspots.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last known visit. This helps establish a timeline of events.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, optional): Record the time of the last known visit. This adds detail to the timeline.
* **Location Affected** (`location_affected`, select_one, optional): Identify the category of the location (Home, Work, Other).
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of contact that occurred (Contacted Person, Shared Equipment, Shared Vehicle, Shared Food).
* **Contact Duration** (`contact_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the contact. This helps quantify the contact.
* **Frequency of Contact** (`contact_frequency`, select_one, optional): Record the frequency of contact (Once, Multiple Times, Regularly).
* **Contact Distance** (`contact_distance`, number, optional): Record the physical distance of contact. This can be a measure of proximity.
* **Contact Medium** (`contact_medium`, select_one, optional): Identify the medium of contact (Air, Water, Ground).
* **Contact Location** (`contact_location`, select_one, optional): Identify the specific location of contact (Home, Work, School, Other).
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the contact.
* **Infected Person** (`user_surveyed`, select_one, optional): Identify whether the person was infected (Affected, Not Affected).
* **Location of Exposure** (`contact_other_location`, text, optional): Record the location where the exposure occurred.

## Tips

- Be thorough and detailed in your entry, as accurate data helps in public health interventions.
- If you are unsure of any information, consult with others to ensure accuracy.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
