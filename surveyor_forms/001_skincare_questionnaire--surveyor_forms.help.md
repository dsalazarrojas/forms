# Skincare Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their skincare habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. Use a specific date format for the "On what date did you last use a skincare product?" question.
4. For the "What is your primary phone number?" and "What is your secondary phone number?" questions, provide your phone numbers if you have them, but it's not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`user_input1`, text, required): This is the first question in the form. Please enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **How often do you use skincare products?** (`user_input2`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available options: Daily, Weekly, or Rarely.
* **What is your age?** (`user_input3`, number, required): Enter your age to help us better understand your demographics.
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions?** (`email_input1`, email, required): Provide your email address where we can reach you for follow-up questions.
* **Which skincare routine is most important to you?** (`select_input1`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the available options: Cleansing, Moisturizing, Exfoliating, and Masking.
* **On what date did you last use a skincare product?** (`date_input1`, date, required): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **At what time of day do you prefer to use skincare products?** (`time_input1`, time, required): Choose the time of day when you prefer to use skincare products.
* **What is your current skincare note?** (`note_input1`, note, required): Write a brief description of your current skincare note.
* **What is your primary phone number?** (`phone_input1`, text, optional): If you have a primary phone number, provide it.
* **What is your secondary phone number?** (`phone_input2`, text, optional): If you have a secondary phone number, provide it.
* **How do you rate your current skin condition?** (`user_input4`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`user_input5`, note, optional): Write any additional comments or notes about your skincare preferences or experiences.
