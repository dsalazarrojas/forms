# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a student survey, designed to gather feedback and information from students. Its main purpose is to collect data on students' preferences, perceptions, and experiences. The survey helps us understand your thoughts, opinions, and feelings, providing valuable insights for improving our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Use the provided options for select questions, such as "how do you feel" or "what facilities do you prefer."
3. Enter your answers in the text fields, such as "facilities_2" or "facilities_10."
4. Select all relevant options for multiple-choice questions, like "facilities_5" or "facilities_16."
5. Finally, click "Submit" to send your completed survey.
