# needs_assessments_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "needs_assessments_survey" form is designed to collect contact information from users for the purpose of creating a comprehensive contact list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields: first name, last name, email, phone number, and manager's contact information (if applicable).
2.  If you have additional contact information, you can optionally provide more details in the subsequent pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
*   First Name (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
*   Last Name (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.

### Email
*   Email (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.

### Phone
*   Phone (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number.

### Organization
*   Organization (`organization`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your organization's name.

### Job Title
*   Job Title (`job_title`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your job title.

### Department
*   Department (`department`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your department name.

### Manager Name
*   Manager Name (`manager_name`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a manager, enter their name.

### Manager Title
*   Manager Title (`manager_title`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's job title.

### Manager Email
*   Manager Email (`manager_email`, `email`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's email address.

### Manager Phone
*   Manager Phone (`manager_phone`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's phone number.

### Manager Organization
*   Manager Organization (`manager_organization`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's organization name.

### Manager Job Title
*   Manager Job Title (`manager_job_title`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your manager's job title.

### Contact Information 2 (Address)
*   Address 2 (`contact_information_2_1`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address 2.
*   City 2 (`contact_information_2_2`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the city of your address 2.
*   State 2 (`contact_information_2_3`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the state of your address 2.
*   Zip Code 2 (`contact_information_2_4`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the zip code of your address 2.
*   Country 2 (`contact_information_2_5`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the country of your address 2.

### Contact Information 3 (Address 2)
*   Address 2 (`contact_information_3_1`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address 2.

### Contact Information 4 (Address 3)
*   Address 3 (`contact_information_4_1`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address 3.

### Contact Information 5 (Address 4)
*   Address 4 (`contact_information_5_1`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address 4.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
