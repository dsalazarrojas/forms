# Net Promoter Score Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure customer satisfaction and loyalty towards this company. The Net Promoter Score is a widely used metric to evaluate the likelihood of customers to recommend a company to others, with scores ranging from 0-100.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and answer based on your recent interactions with the company. You can skip questions that you are not sure or do not have an opinion for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Net Promoter Score (1)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate how likely you are to recommend this company to others.
* **What do you usually do when you think about this company? (2)**: Share your typical behavior or action when thinking about this company.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend this company? (3)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate how likely you are to recommend this company.
* **Referrals - how often do you usually refer a friend to this company? (4)**: Select how often you refer friends to this company.
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Often
	+ Almost always
	+ Always
* **How likely are you to refer a friend to this company? (5)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate how likely you are to refer a friend.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how satisfied are you with this company? (6)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the company.
* **How likely are you to be a loyal customer? (7)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate your likelihood of continued loyalty.
* **How likely are you to continue using this company? (8)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate your level of commitment.
* **How likely are you to recommend this company? (9)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate your likelihood to recommend.
* **How often do you usually recommend this company? (10)**: Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate how often you refer others to this company.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and based on your personal experience.
- You can refer to your previous interactions with the company while answering.
- If you are unsure or do not have an opinion, you can skip questions that are not relevant to you.
