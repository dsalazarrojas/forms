# Christmas Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about users' holiday season plans, shopping habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, and Email Address).
2. Choose how much you plan to spend on Christmas (Under $200, $200-$500, $500-$1,000, $1,000-$2,000, or Over $2,000).
3. Decide when you plan to do your Christmas shopping (Before November, November, Early December, Mid-December, Last minute (Dec 20-24), or After Christmas).
4. Select whether you prefer shopping in-store or online (In-store, Online, Both equally, or Don't shop).
5. Tell us about your gift preferences (Experiences, Gift cards, Clothing, Electronics, Home goods, Subscription services, or Other).
6. Choose your preferred gift price range (Under $25, $25-$50, $50-$100, $100-$200, or Over $200).
7. Share how you prefer to celebrate Christmas (Select all that apply: With family, With friends, At church or religious gathering, Charitable activities, Vacationing, Quiet and relaxing, or Party and socializing).
8. Let us know if you'll host or attend holiday parties (Yes, host, Yes, attend, Both, or None).
9. Indicate which holiday activities interest you (Select all that apply: Holiday movies, Holiday music, Outdoor decorations, Holiday baking, Gift wrapping, Caroling, Games and puzzles, or Holiday shows).
10. Tell us about your technology usage for holiday shopping (Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never).
11. Share your stance on sustainable Christmas practices (Very concerned, Somewhat concerned, Not concerned, or Undecided).
12. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **How much do you plan to spend on Christmas?** (`holiday_spending`, `select_one`, required): Choose a range that suits your holiday budget.
* **When do you plan to do your Christmas shopping?** (`shopping_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Tell us when you plan to do your holiday shopping.
* **Do you prefer to shop in-store or online?** (`shopping_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to shop for Christmas.
* **What type of gifts do you prefer to receive?** (`preferred_gift_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of gifts you like to receive.
* **Preferred Gift Price Range** (`gift_budget_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the price range you prefer for gifts.
* **How do you prefer to celebrate Christmas?** (`celebration_style`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you prefer to celebrate Christmas.
* **Will you host or attend holiday parties?** (`entertaining`, `select_one`, optional): Tell us if you'll be hosting or attending holiday parties.
* **Which holiday activities interest you?** (`holiday_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the holiday activities that interest you.
* **Do you use technology for holiday shopping?** (`technology_usage`, `select_one`, optional): Tell us how often you use technology for holiday shopping.
* **Are you concerned about sustainable Christmas practices?** (`sustainability`, `select_one`, optional): Share your stance on sustainable holiday practices.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.
