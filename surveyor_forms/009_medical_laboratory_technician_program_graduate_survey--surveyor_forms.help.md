# Medical Laboratory Technician Program Graduate Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information about the Medical Laboratory Technician (MLT) program graduates. The purpose of this form is to collect data on the graduates' experiences, feedback, and evaluations of the program, which will help us improve the program's quality and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the program you graduated from (Medical Laboratory Technician, Diagnostic Services, or Other).
2. Enter your name, and any other required details if applicable (program name, graduation date, location, and contact information).
3. Provide feedback on your experience with the program, including any comments or evaluations you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category**: The category of the program you graduated from (Medical Laboratory Technician, Diagnostic Services, or Other). This is a required field and must be selected from the dropdown options.
* **program_name**: A field where you can enter the name of the program you graduated from, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **graduation_date**: Enter the date you graduated from the program. This field is optional.
* **program_location**: Select the location where you graduated from the program (Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, or Other). This field is required.
* **graduation_institution**: Select all institutions that apply where you graduated from. This field is required.
* **comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the program. This field is optional.
* **program_evaluation**: Select all that apply to evaluate the program (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5). This field is required.
* **program_evaluation_other**: Enter any additional comments about your evaluation of the program. This field is optional.
* **program_evaluation_comments**: Enter any comments about your evaluation of the program. This field is optional.
* **program_evaluation_other_comments**: Enter any additional comments about your evaluation of the program. This field is optional.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name, so we can contact you if necessary. This field is optional.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary. This field is optional.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary. This field is optional.
* **contact_department**: Select the department of your workplace or contact (Laboratory, Other). This field is required.
* **contact_title**: Enter your title or position. This field is optional.
* **contact_extension**: Enter any additional details about your contact information. This field is optional.
* **surveyor**: Enter the name of the surveyor or person who assigned you this survey. This field is optional.
* **assigned**: Enter any additional details about who assigned you this survey. This field is optional.
* **assigned_tool**: Enter any additional details about the tool used to assign this survey. This field is optional.
* **form_id**: Enter any unique identifier for the form. This field is optional.
* **output_file**: Enter any additional details about the output file. This field is optional.
* **form_category**: Enter the category of the form (surveyor_forms). This field is optional.
* **form_description**: Enter a brief description of the form. This field is optional.
* **form_title**: Enter the title of the form (Medical Laboratory Technician Program Graduate Survey). This field is optional.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by Medical Laboratory Technician program graduates, and the fields are organized to gather relevant information about your experience with the program. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve the program.
