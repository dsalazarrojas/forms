# Vertical Selling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vertical Selling Survey is a form designed to collect information about your company and industry. The form is used to categorize and target companies based on their specific industry. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that the data is used correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page, where you will be asked to enter your company's name.
2. On the second page, select the industry that your company operates in from the provided list.
3. On the third page, enter your email address to confirm your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your company** (Required: False): This field is where you will enter your company's name.
* **Industry** (Required: True): Select the industry that your company operates in from the list below:
	+ Advertising
	+ Automotive
	+ Financial
	+ Food
	+ Health
	+ Insurance
	+ Retail
	+ Technology
	+ Transportation
	+ Utilities
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Real Estate
* **Email** (Required: True): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the company's name to ensure that the data is used correctly.
- Select the industry that best represents your company's operations.
- Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
