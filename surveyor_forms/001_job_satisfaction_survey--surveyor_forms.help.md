# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your job satisfaction and identify areas for improvement. Your feedback is valuable in helping us create a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply fill out the following pages and answer each question to the best of your ability. You can answer all or some of the questions, and you're welcome to provide any additional comments or feedback in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was your overall job satisfaction**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your job.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Options:** None
  - **Hint:** No explanation provided, simply answer on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **How would you rate your job satisfaction**: This question also asks about your satisfaction with your job, on a scale of 1-5.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Options:** 1-5
  - **Hint:** Rate your satisfaction on this scale.
- **How satisfied are you with your salary**: This question is about your satisfaction with your salary. Choose between "Yes" and "No" to answer this question.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Options:** Yes, No
  - **Hint:** Simply choose "Yes" if you're satisfied or "No" otherwise.
- **How satisfied are you with your benefits**: This question is about your satisfaction with your benefits. Choose between "Yes", "No" (multiple options), to answer this question.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Options:** Yes, No
  - **Hint:** Choose all that apply.
- **Do you receive enough time off**: This question is about whether you feel you get enough time off.
  - **Type:** time
  - **Options:** None (answer as a date or time)
  - **Hint:** Simply answer with a date or time (e.g., "Yes, 2 weeks per year").
- **How satisfied are you with your work-life balance**: This question is about your satisfaction with your work-life balance.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Options:** None (answer on a scale, e.g., 1-5)
  - **Hint:** Rate your satisfaction on this scale.
- **How would you rate your job satisfaction with your manager**: This question is about your satisfaction with your job satisfaction with your manager.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Options:** 1-5
  - **Hint:** Rate your satisfaction on this scale.
- **How would you rate your job satisfaction with your team**: This question is about your satisfaction with your job satisfaction with your team.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Options:** 1-5
  - **Hint:** Rate your satisfaction on this scale.
- **Do you feel secure in your job**: This question is about whether you feel secure in your job.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Options:** Yes, No
  - **Hint:** Choose between "Yes" and "No".
- **Do you have job growth opportunities**: This question is about whether you have job growth opportunities.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Options:** Yes, No
  - **Hint:** Choose all that apply.
- **Have your job satisfaction changed**: This question is about whether your job satisfaction has changed.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Options:** Satisfied, Dissatisfied
  - **Hint:** Choose between "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
- **Do you want to provide feedback**: This question is about whether you want to provide feedback.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Options:** Yes, No
  - **Hint:** Choose between "Yes" and "No".
