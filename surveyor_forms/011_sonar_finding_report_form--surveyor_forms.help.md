# Sonar Finding Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sonar Finding Report Form is used to capture information on sonar findings from marine surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select "Marine Surveys" page**: Select the "Marine Surveys" page from the form.
2. Complete the **Sonar findings** section by selecting one or more of the "Yes" or "No" options.
3. **Enter Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the survey using the date field. 
4. **Enter Location**: Enter the location where the sonar findings were observed in the text field.
5. **Enter Depth**: Enter the depth of the survey in the number field.
6. **Select Sonar**: Select one or more of the "Yes" or "No" options for sonar readings.
7. **Attachments**: Use the text area to add any attachments or notes related to the survey.
8. **Enter File**: Enter any relevant files related to the survey in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marine Surveys**: This page allows you to select whether any sonar findings were observed during the survey. 
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the survey using the date field. 
* **Location**: Enter the location where the sonar findings were observed in the text field.
* **Time**: This field is not currently used, as the date field already includes time. 
* **Depth**: Enter the depth of the survey in the number field. 
* **Sonar**: This page allows you to select whether any sonar findings were observed during the survey. 
* **Attachment**: Use this text area to add any attachments or notes related to the survey.
* **File**: Enter any relevant files related to the survey in the text field.
