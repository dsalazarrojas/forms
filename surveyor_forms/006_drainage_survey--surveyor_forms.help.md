# Drainage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the status of the drainage system, including its condition and performance. It will help identify areas of concern and suggest potential improvements to maintain or optimize the drainage system.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct option for "Drainage System Status" to indicate the overall state of the system. You can choose from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on your assessment.
2.  Enter the flow rate (measured in units, e.g., liters per minute or gallons per minute) to provide more detailed information about the system's capacity.
3.  Describe the water quality in a few words or sentences, providing details about any concerns or issues you've observed.
4.  Ensure you answer all questions to the best of your ability, and do not hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drainage System Status** (drainage_system_status, select_one, required/optional): Indicate the overall state of the drainage system by selecting "Good", "Fair", or "Poor". This will help in understanding if the system is functioning properly or if there are areas of concern.
* **Flow Rates** (flow_rates, number, required/optional): Enter the rate at which water flows through the system, providing more information about its capacity.
* **Water Quality** (water_quality, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the quality of the water in the system, including any issues or concerns you've observed.
* **Form Questions** (form_questions, text, required/optional): This field is not actually a field but a placeholder for other questions that might be added in the future.
