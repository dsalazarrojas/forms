# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on our products. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance our product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Rate your experience with our product using the "Rating" field. You can select a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
5. Enter any specific product ID number if you have it.
6. In the "Review" field, provide a brief description of your experience with our product.
7. Select the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Name
**Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.

### Customer Name
**Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.

### Customer Email
**Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Customer Phone
**Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Rating
**Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with our product using a number from 1 to 5.

### Product Id
**Product Id** (`product_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter any product ID number if you have it.

### Review
**Review** (`review`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with our product in the "Review" field.

### Submit
**Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required: false): This is the button to submit your form.

### Feedback
**Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional feedback or comments about our product.

### Category
**Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all categories that apply to your experience with our product.

### Product Type
**Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you consider this product to be a "Yes" or "No" type product.

### Customer Service
**Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience with our customer service, selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Product Quality
**Product Quality** (`product_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of our product, selecting "Good" or "Poor".

### Overall
**Overall** (`overall`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our product, selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Recommend
**Recommend** (`recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Would you recommend our product to others, selecting "Yes" or "No".
