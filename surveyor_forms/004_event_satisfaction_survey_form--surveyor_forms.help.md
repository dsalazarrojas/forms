# Event Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from event participants about their satisfaction with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in each field with your honest opinion. You don't need to answer every question, as all fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Location** (text): Describe the location where the event took place.
- **Content** (text): Share your thoughts about the event content, such as the quality of the presentations, workshops, or activities.
- **Price** (text): Express your opinion on the pricing of the event, if applicable.
- **Speakers** (text): Mention any speakers or presenters that impressed or disappointed you.
- **Organization** (text): Provide any feedback about the event organization, including the efficiency of the event management.
- **Tips** 
Remember, all fields are optional, and you can answer as many or as few questions as you like. Your feedback will help us improve future events.
