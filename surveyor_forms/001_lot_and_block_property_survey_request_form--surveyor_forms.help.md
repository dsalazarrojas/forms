# Lot And Block Property Survey Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a property survey for a specific property. You can use this form to request a survey for a lot, block, or mixed property type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type (Lot, Block, or Mixed) that corresponds to the property you are requesting a survey for.
2. Enter the surveyor's name in the "Surveyor Name" field.
3. Enter the surveyor's email in the "Email" field.
4. Select a "Request Date" for when you would like the survey to be completed.
5. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
6. Enter the property size (if applicable) in the "Property Size" field.
7. Enter any additional information about the property in the "Additional Information" field.
8. Select the "Request Status" of the survey (New, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
9. If you would like to schedule a date for the survey, enter the date in the "Schedule Date" field.
10. Enter the surveyor's phone number in the "Surveyor Phone" field.
11. Enter the time of the survey in the "Schedule Time" field (if applicable).
12. Enter any notes about the survey in the "Notes" field.
13. Enter the submitter's name in the "Submitter" field.
14. Enter the submitter's phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
15. Enter the submitter's email in the "Submitter Email" field.
16. Enter the submitter's company in the "Submitter Company" field.
17. Enter the submitter's position in the "Submitter Position" field.
18. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted Date" field.
19. Enter the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
20. Enter any additional notes from the submitter in the "Submitter Notes" field.
21. Enter the submitter's address in the "Submitter Address" field.
22. Enter the submitter's zip code in the "Submitter Zip" field.
23. Enter the submitter's city in the "Submitter City" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lot and Block Property Survey Request Form** (`select_multiple`, 1, false): Select the type of property that corresponds to the property you are requesting a survey for.
* **Surveyor Name** (`text`, 2, true): Enter the name of the person who will be conducting the survey.
* **Email** (`email`, 3, true): Enter the email of the person who will be conducting the survey.
* **Request Date** (`date`, 4, false): Select the date when you would like the survey to be completed.
* **Property Address** (`text`, 5, true): Enter the address of the property that you are requesting a survey for.
* **Property Type** (`select_one`, 6, true): Select the type of property that corresponds to the property you are requesting a survey for (Lot, Block, or Mixed).
* **Property Size** (`number`, 7, false): Enter the size of the property (if applicable).
* **Additional Information** (`text`, 8, false): Enter any additional information about the property.
* **Request Status** (`select_one`, 9, true): Select the status of the survey request (New, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **Schedule Date** (`date`, 10, false): Select the date when you would like the survey to be completed (if applicable).
* **Surveyor Phone** (`text`, 11, false): Enter the phone number of the person who will be conducting the survey.
* **Schedule Time** (`time`, 13, false): Select the time when you would like the survey to be completed (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`text`, 14, false): Enter any notes about the survey.
* **Submitter** (`text`, 15, false): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (`text`, 16, false): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email** (`email`, 17, false): Enter the email of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Company** (`text`, 18, false): Enter the company of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Position** (`text`, 19, false): Enter the position of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted Date** (`date`, 20, false): Select the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`text`, 21, false): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Notes** (`text`, 22, false): Enter any additional notes from the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Address** (`text`, 23, false): Enter the address of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Zip** (`text`, 24, false): Enter the zip code of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter City** (`text`, 25, false): Enter the city of the person submitting the request.
