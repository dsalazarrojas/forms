# Youth Mentoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Mentoring Survey is designed to collect data on youth mentoring programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the title of your program in the "Program Title" field.
3. Describe your program in the "Program Description" field.
4. Enter your program's start and end dates in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Select the evaluation scale that best describes your program's quality from the "Evaluation Scale" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the number of participants in your program in the "Number of Participants" field.
7. Enter the number of male and female participants in the "Number of Male Participants" and "Number of Female Participants" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the age range of participants in the "Age Range" field.
9. Select the ethnicity of participants in the "Ethnicity" dropdown menu.
10. Select the gender of participants in the "Participants Gender" dropdown menu.
11. Enter any other ethnicity not listed in the "Participants Ethnicity Other" field.
12. Enter any other gender not listed in the "Participants Gender Other" field.
13. Enter the location of your program in the "Program Location" field.
14. Enter any other location not listed in the "Program Location Other" field.
15. Enter the funding sources of your program in the "Program Funding" field.
16. Enter any other funding sources not listed in the "Program Funding Other" field.
17. Enter any comments on your program's evaluation scale and other fields in the corresponding "Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your youth mentoring program.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization running the program.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Describe your program's goals and objectives.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, text, optional): Enter your program's goals.
* **Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your program.
* **End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the evaluation scale that best describes your program's quality.
* **Evaluation Scale Other** (`evaluation_scale_other`, text, optional): Enter any other evaluation scale not listed.
* **Evaluation Scale Other Comments** (`evaluation_scale_other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments on the evaluation scale you selected.
* **Number of Participants** (`program_participants`, number, optional): Enter the number of participants in your program.
* **Number of Male Participants** (`program_participants_male`, number, optional): Enter the number of male participants in your program.
* **Number of Female Participants** (`program_participants_female`, number, optional): Enter the number of female participants in your program.
* **Age Range** (`program_participants_age_range`, text, optional): Enter the age range of participants in your program.
* **Ethnicity** (`program_participants_ethnicity`, select_one, optional): Select the ethnicity of participants.
* **Participants Gender** (`program_participants_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the gender of participants.
* **Participants Gender Other** (`program_participants_gender_other`, text, optional): Enter any other gender not listed.
* **Participants Gender Other Comments** (`program_participants_gender_other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments on the participants' gender.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your program.
* **Program Location Other** (`program_location_other`, text, optional): Enter any other location not listed.
* **Program Location Other Comments** (`program_location_other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments on the program location.
* **Program Funding** (`program_funding`, text, optional): Enter the funding sources of your program.
* **Program Funding Other** (`program_funding_other`, text, optional): Enter any other funding sources not listed.
* **Program Funding Other Comments** (`program_funding_other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments on the program funding.
