# Consumer Spending Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Spending Survey is designed to collect information on how consumers allocate their income, shop, and make purchasing decisions. This data will help businesses and organizations better understand consumer behavior and develop targeted marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select your age group from the options provided in the "What is your age group?" field.
* Choose your annual household income from the options provided in the "Annual Household Income" field.
* In the "Average Monthly Grocery Spending" field, enter the amount you spend on groceries per month, in USD.
* Select how often you dine out per week from the options provided in the "How often do you dine out?" field.
* In the "Monthly Entertainment Budget" field, enter the amount you spend on entertainment per month, in USD.
* Select all categories you spend the most on from the options provided in the "Categories you spend the most on" field.
* Choose where you do most of your shopping from the options provided in the "Where do you do most of your shopping?" field.
* Answer whether you've made a purchase over $500 in the last 3 months from the options provided in the "Have you made a purchase over $500 in the last 3 months?" field.
* Select the primary factor influencing your purchases from the options provided in the "Primary factor influencing your purchases" field.
* If you'd like to save a percentage of your income, enter it in the "Percentage of monthly income saved" field.
* Finally, you may add any additional comments on your spending habits in the "Any additional comments on your spending habits" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select the age group that best applies to you.
* **Annual Household Income** (`household_income`, `select_one`, required): Choose your annual household income from the provided options.
* **Average Monthly Grocery Spending** (`monthly_grocery_spend`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you spend on groceries per month, in USD.
* **How often do you dine out?** (`dining_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you dine out per week.
* **Monthly Entertainment Budget** (`entertainment_spend`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you spend on entertainment per month, in USD.
* **Categories you spend the most on** (`shopping_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all categories you spend the most on.
* **Where do you do most of your shopping?** (`online_vs_instore`, `select_one`, required): Choose where you do most of your shopping.
* **Have you made a purchase over $500 in the last 3 months?** (`recent_major_purchase`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you've made a purchase over $500 in the last 3 months.
* **Primary factor influencing your purchases** (`purchase_influence`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary factor influencing your purchases.
* **Percentage of monthly income saved** (`saving_habits`, `number`, required): Enter the percentage of your monthly income you save.
* **Any additional comments on your spending habits** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments on your spending habits, if desired.
