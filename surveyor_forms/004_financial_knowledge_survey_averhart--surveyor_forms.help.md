# Financial Knowledge Survey Averhart - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on individuals' financial knowledge, literacy, and investment habits for the Averhart financial education program. The purpose of this form is to gather data that will be used to identify areas of improvement in financial education for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title for the financial survey in the "Financial Knowledge Survey Averhart" field.
2. Answer the questions on your financial knowledge and literacy level, using the numerical value provided.
3. Indicate whether or not you are currently invested in financial markets, by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Provide the date of your last financial transaction.
5. Record the time of your last financial transaction.
6. Add any additional notes about your financial situation.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Knowledge Survey Averhart**: This field is where you enter your title for the financial survey.
* **Financial Knowledge**: This field is where you report your current level of financial knowledge, using a numerical value.
* **Financial Literacy**: This field is where you report your current level of financial literacy, using a numerical value.
* **Financial Knowledge Level**: This field is where you select your level of financial knowledge, either "High" or "Low".
* **Financial Investment**: This field is where you indicate whether or not you are currently invested in financial markets, by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Date**: This field is where you report the date of your last financial transaction.
* **Time**: This field is where you report the time of your last financial transaction.
* **Notes**: This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes about your financial situation.
* **Email**: This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is where you enter your phone number.
