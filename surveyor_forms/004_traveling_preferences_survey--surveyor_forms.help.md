# Traveling Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your preferences and needs for traveling. Your input will help us provide better services and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question with your preferred choices or select all that apply.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety to ensure accurate collection of your preferences.
3. Double-check your answers before submission to avoid errors.
4. If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the field explanations below.
