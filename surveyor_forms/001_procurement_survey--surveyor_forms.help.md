# procurement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their business or organization's offerings. It is likely used for internal purposes, such as sales or marketing initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. On the first page, "what_services_do_you_sell", enter a brief description of the services your business or organization offers.
3. On the second page, "what_is_your_email", enter your business or organization's email address.
4. On the third page, "what_is_your_phone", enter your business or organization's phone number.
5. On the fourth page, "what_services_do_you_sell", enter a brief description of the services your business or organization offers (this page is likely a duplicate of the first page, but you may need to fill it out for verification purposes).
6. On the fifth page, "what_is_your_phone", enter your business or organization's phone number (this page is likely a duplicate of the third page, but you may need to fill it out for verification purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_services_do_you_sell (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the services your business or organization offers.
* **what_is_your_email (Email)**: Enter your business or organization's email address.
* **what_is_your_phone (Text)**: Enter your business or organization's phone number.
* **what_services_do_you_sell (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the services your business or organization offers (this is a duplicate field, please make sure the information matches what you entered on the first page).
* **what_is_your_phone (Text)**: Enter your business or organization's phone number (this is a duplicate field, please make sure the information matches what you entered on the third page).

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about your business or organization.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is intended for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of your organization.
