# Online Social Networking Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their online social networking activities and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title, which will be displayed as a header for your survey response.
2. Select whether you have an online presence in the category you are interested in.
3. Provide a brief description of your online activities.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have questions about online social networking.
5. Enter your email address (in the format "example@example.com").
6. Enter your website (if you have one).
7. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
8. Select a date and time when you added your online social networking presence.
9. Add any additional notes about your online activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_title** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your survey response.
* **category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all categories that apply to your online social networking presence.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your online activities.
* **questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have questions about online social networking.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (in the format "example@example.com").
* **website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website (if you have one).
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **date_added** (`date_added`, date, optional): Select a date when you added your online social networking presence.
* **time_added** (`time_added`, time, optional): Select a time when you added your online social networking presence.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your online activities.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have multiple online presences, you can submit multiple responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
