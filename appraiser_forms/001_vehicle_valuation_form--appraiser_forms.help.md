# Vehicle Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Valuation Form is designed to collect accurate and detailed information about a vehicle for valuation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Vehicle Make" of your vehicle from the list of options.
2. Enter the "Vehicle Year" in numerical format (e.g., 2001, 2002, etc.).
3. Input the "Vehicle Model" or the specific model of your vehicle.
4. Select the "Year Model" from the dropdown options (if applicable).
5. Choose the "Body Style" of your vehicle from the options (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Hatchback, Convertible, etc.).
6. Select the "Vehicle Condition" as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on the vehicle's condition.
7. Choose any applicable "Vehicle Damage" options (Front, Back, Side, Rear).
8. Enter the "Vehicle Color" in text format (e.g., red, blue, black, etc.).
9. Input the "Engine Type" or the specific type of engine your vehicle has.
10. Select the "Transmission Type" from the options (Automatic, Manual, CVT, DCT, Semi-Automatic, Dual Clutch).
11. Enter the "Vehicle Mileage" in numerical format (e.g., 100,000, etc.).
12. Input the "Engine Capacity" or the specific engine capacity of your vehicle.
13. Select the number of "Vehicle Doors" your vehicle has.
14. Choose the number of "Vehicle Seats" your vehicle has.
15. Select the "Drive Type" from the options (FWD, RWD, AWD, RWD with 2WD, 2WD, FWD with RWD).
16. Choose the "Wheel Type" from the options (RWD, FWD, 4WD, 2WD).
17. Input the "Vehicle Engine Type ID" if your vehicle has a specific engine type ID.
18. Input the "Vehicle Transmission ID" if your vehicle has a specific transmission type ID.
19. Check if the vehicle has a "Body Style ID" (Yes or No).
20. Input the "Vehicle Year ID" if your vehicle has a specific year ID.
21. Input the "Make ID" of your vehicle.
22. Input the "Model ID" of your vehicle.
23. Input the "Trim ID" of your vehicle.
24. Input the "Color ID" of your vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make**: The make of your vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year of your vehicle, e.g., 2001, 2002, etc.
* **Vehicle Model**: The specific model of your vehicle, e.g., Corolla, Mustang, etc.
* **Year Model**: If your vehicle has a specific year model, choose the corresponding option (e.g., 2001 model, 2002 model, etc.).
* **Body Style**: The body style of your vehicle, e.g., Sedan, SUV, Truck, Hatchback, Convertible, etc.
* **Vehicle Condition**: The condition of your vehicle, choose from "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Vehicle Damage**: Any damage your vehicle may have, choose from "Front", "Back", "Side", "Rear".
* **Vehicle Color**: The color of your vehicle, e.g., red, blue, black, etc.
* **Engine Type**: The type of engine your vehicle has, e.g., gasoline, diesel, etc.
* **Transmission Type**: The transmission type of your vehicle, e.g., Automatic, Manual, CVT, DCT, Semi-Automatic, Dual Clutch.
* **Vehicle Mileage**: The mileage of your vehicle, e.g., 100,000 etc.
* **Engine Capacity**: The engine capacity of your vehicle, e.g., 2L, 3L, etc.
* **Vehicle Doors**: The number of doors your vehicle has.
* **Vehicle Seats**: The number of seats your vehicle has.
* **Drive Type**: The drive type of your vehicle, e.g., FWD, RWD, AWD, RWD with 2WD, 2WD, FWD with RWD.
* **Wheel Type**: The type of wheel your vehicle has, e.g., RWD, FWD, 4WD, 2WD.
* **Vehicle Engine Type ID**: If your vehicle has a specific engine type ID, enter it here.
* **Vehicle Transmission ID**: If your vehicle has a specific transmission type ID, enter it here.
* **Body Style ID**: If your vehicle has a specific body style ID, choose "Yes" or "No".
* **Vehicle Year ID**: If your vehicle has a specific year ID, enter it here.
* **Make ID**: The ID of your vehicle's make, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Model ID**: The ID of your vehicle's model, e.g., Corolla, Mustang, etc.
* **Trim ID**: The ID of your vehicle's trim, e.g., L, X, etc.
* **Color ID**: The ID of your vehicle's color, e.g., red, blue, black, etc.

By following these steps and explanations, you should be able to complete the Vehicle Valuation Form accurately and help ensure that the provided information is correct and consistent.
