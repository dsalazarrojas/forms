# Antique Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for appraisers to collect detailed information about antique objects. It helps to facilitate accurate and precise appraisals by gathering a comprehensive set of data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "true" in the form.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurately and thoroughly filled out.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of appraisal** (`appraisal_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the appraisal in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Appraiser name** (`appraiser_name`, text, required): Fill out your full name as a professional appraiser.
* **Appraiser license number** (`appraiser_license`, text, optional): Enter your appraiser license number if applicable.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the object owner or client.
* **Client contact information** (`client_contact`, text, optional): Provide the client's phone number or email for further communication.
* **Description of object** (`object_description`, text, required): Describe the object being appraised in detail.
* **Type of antique** (`object_type`, select_one, required): Select the primary category of the antique from the list.
* **Estimated age or time period** (`estimated_age`, text, required): Enter the approximate century or decade of the object.
* **Period style** (`period_style`, text, optional): Describe the period style of the object if applicable.
* **Overall condition** (`condition`, select_one, required): Select the overall condition of the object from the list.
* **Describe any damage or repairs** (`damage_description`, text, optional): Describe any damage or repairs to the object.
* **Primary material composition** (`material_composition`, text, required): Enter the primary material composition of the object.
* **Dimensions** (`dimensions`, text, required): Enter the dimensions of the object in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, text, optional): Enter the approximate weight of the object if applicable.
* **Maker or artist signature present?** (`maker_signature`, select_one, required): Select whether a maker or artist signature is present on the object.
* **Maker or artist name if known** (`maker_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the maker or artist if known.
* **Rarity assessment** (`rarity_assessment`, select_one, required): Select the rarity assessment of the object from the list.
* **Recent comparable sales reviewed?** (`comparable_sales`, select_one, required): Select whether recent comparable sales have been reviewed.
* **Appraised fair market value** (`appraised_value`, number, required): Enter the appraised fair market value in dollars.
* **Purpose of appraisal** (`appraisal_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the appraisal from the list.
* **Special characteristics or provenance** (`special_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special characteristics or provenance of the object.
* **Recommendations for care or preservation** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for the care or preservation of the object.
