# Art Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and track information about individual artworks or art pieces within an inventory system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:
1. Enter the **Title or Name of Artwork**, which is the formal title of the piece.
2. Enter the **Artist Name**, which is the full name of the artist or creator.
3. If available, provide the **Artist Birth Year**, which is an approximate year if the exact date is unknown.
4. If applicable, enter the **Date of Creation**, which is the year or date range when the artwork was created.
5. Select the **Type of Artwork**, which categorizes the piece into different categories such as Painting, Sculpture, Drawing, etc.
6. Specify the **Medium or Materials** used in creating the artwork.
7. Enter the **Height, Width, and Depth** of the artwork if it's a three-dimensional piece.
8. Assess the **Current Condition** of the artwork, which is a subjective measure of its overall condition.
9. Provide **Condition Details** if there are any damages, repairs, or special considerations.
10. Document the **Provenance and Ownership History**, which includes previous owners or exhibition history.
11. Check if the **Artwork is Insured**, and if so, provide the insurance status.
12. Specify the **Storage or Display Location** where the artwork is currently stored.
13. Check if the **Artwork has a Certificate of Authenticity**, and if so, document it.
14. Check if **High-Quality Images** are available, and if so, document them.
15. Enter the **Date of This Record**, which is when the inventory entry was created.
16. Enter the **Recorded By**, which is the name of the person creating this inventory record.
17. Add any **Additional Notes** about the artwork if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title or Name of Artwork**: Enter the formal title of the piece.
- **Artist Name**: Enter the full name of the artist or creator.
- **Artist Birth Year**: Enter the approximate year if the exact date is unknown.
- **Date of Creation**: Enter the year or date range when the artwork was created.
- **Type of Artwork**: Select the category of artwork from the provided options.
- **Medium or Materials**: Specify the materials used in creating the artwork.
- **Height**: Enter the height of the artwork in inches if it's applicable.
- **Width**: Enter the width of the artwork in inches if it's applicable.
- **Depth**: Enter the depth of the artwork in inches if it's a three-dimensional piece.
- **Current Condition**: Assess the overall condition of the artwork from the provided options.
- **Condition Details**: Provide any details about damages, repairs, or special considerations.
- **Provenance and Ownership History**: Document previous owners or exhibition history.
- **Artwork is Insured**: Check if the artwork is insured, and if so, document the insurance status.
- **Storage or Display Location**: Specify where the artwork is currently stored or displayed.
- **Certificate of Authenticity**: Check if the artwork has authentication documentation.
- **High-Quality Images**: Check if high-quality images are documented.
- **Date of This Record**: Enter when the inventory entry was created.
- **Recorded By**: Enter the name of the person creating this inventory record.
- **Additional Notes**: Add any other relevant information about the artwork.

## Tips
- Ensure that each art piece is properly documented to prevent duplication or omission of vital information.
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to maintain organized records.
- Use the form to track and record information about individual art pieces.
- Update the form as necessary to reflect changes or new information about the artwork.
