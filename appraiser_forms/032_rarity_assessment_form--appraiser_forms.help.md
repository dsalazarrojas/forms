# Rarity Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the rarity of an item submitted for appraisal. It collects information about the item, including its description, condition, year produced, and age, as well as contact information for the appraiser and collector. The form is intended for internal use by appraisers to evaluate the rarity of items brought in for valuation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Item Description` field with a brief description of the item.
2. Select the item's `Item Condition` from the options provided (Good, Fair, or Poor).
3. Enter the `Item Year Produced` if known.
4. Enter the `Item Age` if known.
5. Fill in the `Appraiser Name` field with your name as the appraiser.
6. Select your country of licensure from the `Appraiser License` field (US, Canada, or UK).
7. Enter your phone number in the `Appraiser Phone` field.
8. Enter your email address in the `Appraiser Email` field.
9. Fill in the `Collector Name` field with the name of the collector.
10. Enter the collector's phone number in the `Collector Phone` field.
11. Enter the collector's email address in the `Collector Email` field.
12. Enter the resale price of the item in the `Resale Price` field.
13. Select the item's `Rarity Rating` from the options provided (1 - Common, 2 - Uncommon, 3 - Rare, 4 - Very Rare, or 5 - Extremely Rare).
14. Enter any additional notes about the item in the `Notes` field.
15. Select the `Submission Date` using the calendar picker.
16. Enter the item's value in the `Item Value` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission**: This is a placeholder field for the form submission itself. Please fill in the required information above.
* **Item Description**: A brief description of the item being assessed.
* **Item Condition**: The overall condition of the item, with options for Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Item Year Produced**: The year the item was produced, if known.
* **Item Age**: The age of the item, if known.
* **Appraiser Name**: Your name as the appraiser completing this form.
* **Appraiser License**: Your country of licensure, with options for US, Canada, and UK.
* **Appraiser Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Appraiser Email**: Your email address.
* **Collector Name**: The name of the collector.
* **Collector Phone**: The phone number of the collector.
* **Collector Email**: The email address of the collector.
* **Resale Price**: The price at which the item is being resold or would be resold.
* **Rarity Rating**: The rarity rating of the item, with options for 1 - Common, 2 - Uncommon, 3 - Rare, 4 - Very Rare, or 5 - Extremely Rare.
* **Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about the item.
* **Submission Date**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Item Value**: The estimated value of the item.
