# Manufactured Home Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a manufactured home's characteristics, features, and value.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions where applicable.
2. Enter the address of the manufactured home in the "Address" field.
3. Provide any additional details about the home's type in the "home_type_details" field.
4. Specify the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.) in the "property_type" field.
5. Enter the floor area of the home in the "floor_area" field.
6. Enter the year the home was built in the "year_built" field.
7. Enter the square footage of the home in the "square_footage" field.
8. Enter the size of the lot in the "lot_size" field.
9. Enter the frontage of the lot in the "lot_frontage" field.
10. Specify the neighborhood in which the home is located in the "neighborhood" field.
11. Enter any zoning restrictions that apply to the home in the "zoning_restrictions" field.
12. Describe the exterior finish of the home in the "exterior_finish" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" for any interior finishes (e.g., flooring, lighting, etc.) in the "interior_finish" field.
14. Describe the heating systems used in the home in the "heating_systems" field.
15. Describe the electrical systems used in the home in the "electrical_systems" field.
16. Describe the plumbing systems used in the home in the "plumbing_systems" field.
17. Describe the water heating system used in the home in the "water_heating_system" field.
18. Describe the septic system used in the home in the "septic_system" field.
19. Describe any property features (e.g., pool, deck, etc.) in the "property_features" field.
20. Specify the purpose of the lot in the "lot_purpose" field.
21. Specify the use of the lot in the "lot_use" field.

* **Heating Systems**: Describe the heating systems used in the home.
* **Electrical Systems**: Describe the electrical systems used in the home.
* **Plumbing Systems**: Describe the plumbing systems used in the home.
