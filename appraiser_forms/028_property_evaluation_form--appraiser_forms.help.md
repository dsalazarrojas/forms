# Property Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Evaluation Form is a comprehensive tool used to gather and document information about a property for the purpose of evaluation or inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with the necessary details about the property.
2. Provide the Property Details, including the type and status of the property.
3. Enter the Property Address and Seller Information.
4. Specify the Evaluation Date and Inspector Signature.
5. Add any Additional Notes or Report Details if necessary.
6. Review and submit the form for completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This section is used to gather general information about the property. Please provide a brief description of the property.

### Property Details
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Enter the details of the property, including its unique characteristics or features.

### Property Address
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address where the evaluation or inspection took place.

### Property Type
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property being evaluated or inspected, such as residential, commercial, or industrial.

### Property Status
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the property, such as occupied, vacant, or under renovation.

### Seller Information
* **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the seller, such as their name, contact number, or email.

### Evaluation Date
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the evaluation or inspection was conducted.

### Inspector Signature
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the inspector's signature and title.

### Report Details
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about the inspection or evaluation report.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments that might be relevant to the property evaluation or inspection.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If a field is not relevant, you can leave it blank.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
