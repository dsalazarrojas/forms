# Boat Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Appraisal Form is used to gather information about a boat's condition, features, and value for various purposes, such as sale/purchase, insurance, financing, estate planning, tax assessment, or other.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with a "required" label.
2. Ensure that each section is complete before proceeding to the next one.
3. For fields with a "select_one" type, select only one option from the list.
4. If a field is not required, you can skip it.
5. For fields with a "text" type, provide a clear and concise answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the boat's owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the boat's owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the boat's owner.
* **Boat Make** (`boat_make`, `text`, required): Enter the manufacturer name of the boat.
* **Boat Model** (`boat_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model name or number of the boat.
* **Boat Year** (`boat_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of manufacture.
* **Boat Length** (`boat_length`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the boat in feet.
* **Boat Type** (`boat_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the boat (e.g. Bowrider, Cabin Cruiser, Center Console, etc.).
* **Hull Material** (`hull_material`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary material of the hull (e.g. Fiberglass, Aluminum, Steel, Wood, etc.).
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of propulsion (e.g. Outboard, Inboard, Sterndrive (I/O), Jet Drive, Sail, Electric, etc.).
* **Engine Hours** (`engine_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours on the engine.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of fuel used (e.g. Gasoline, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid, etc.).
* **Overall Condition** (`overall_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the boat's overall condition (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Hull Condition** (`hull_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the hull (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the engine (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the interior/cabin (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the condition of the exterior surfaces (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Electronics Condition** (`electronics_condition`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, rate the condition of the navigation electronics (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Known Defects** (`known_defects`, `text`, not required): List any known issues with the boat (if applicable).
* **Recent Upgrades or Repairs** (`recent_upgrades`, `text`, not required): List recent improvements or repairs made to the boat (if applicable).
* **Included Equipment** (`included_equipment`, `text`, not required): List any equipment included with the boat (if applicable).
* **Trailer Included** (`trailer_included`, `select_one`, required): Select whether a trailer is included (e.g. True, False).
* **Trailer Condition** (`trailer_condition`, `select_one`, not required): If applicable, rate the condition of the trailer (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Appraisal Purpose** (`appraisal_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason why this appraisal is needed (e.g. Sale/Purchase, Insurance, Financing, Estate Planning, Tax Assessment, Other).
* **Estimated Appraised Value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, not required): Enter the estimated appraised value (if required).
* **Appraiser Name** (`appraiser_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the certified appraiser (if required).
* **Appraisal Date** (`appraisal_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the appraisal (if required).

## Tips
1. Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the label and hint for guidance.
