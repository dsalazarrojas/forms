# Jewelry Gemologist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer a piece of jewelry for evaluation by a gemologist. It is used to collect information about the jewelry piece, the gem type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, but only the required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) after the field label.
2. Select the correct Gem Type for the jewelry piece you are referring.
3. Enter any relevant details about the jewelry piece in the Jewelry Info field.
4. Enter the name of the jewelry item, if different from the main piece being referred.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the gemologist you are referring the jewelry to.
6. Select the evaluation method for the gemstone.
7. Enter the email and phone number of the appraiser or gemologist you are referring the jewelry to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info Form 1**: Enter your name, company name, and contact information to identify yourself and your business.
* **Gem Type**: Select the type of gemstone that the jewelry piece contains, such as diamond or ruby.
* **Jewelry Info**: Enter any relevant details about the jewelry piece, such as its condition, carat weight, or other distinguishing features.
* **Jewelry Item**: If the jewelry piece is part of a set or collection, enter the name of the item, otherwise leave blank.
* **Gemologist Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the gemologist you are referring the jewelry piece to, if different from the appraiser or gemologist providing the referral.
* **Gemologist**: Select the name of the gemologist you are referring the jewelry piece to, from the list of available options.
* **Evaluation**: Select the evaluation method for the gemstone, such as gemstone grading or authentication.
* **Appraiser Email**: Enter the email address of the appraiser or gemologist providing the referral, to whom the form will be sent.
* **Appraiser Phone**: Enter the phone number of the appraiser or gemologist providing the referral, to whom the form will be sent.
* **Jewelry Referral**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the jewelry piece, such as its provenance or history.
