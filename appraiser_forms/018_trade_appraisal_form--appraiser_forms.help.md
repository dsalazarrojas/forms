# Trade Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trade appraisal form is used to collect information about trade-in vehicles and make informed decisions about their value and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`): Fill in the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`): Provide a brief description of the vehicle.
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`): Enter the owner's name, address, and contact details.
* **Vehicle Warranty** (`vehicle_warranty`): Enter the warranty start and end dates.
* **Trade In Info** (`trade_in_info`): Specify the trade-in date and reason.
* **Trade Value** (`trade_value`): Enter the trade value of the vehicle.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`): Enter the purchase price of the vehicle.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`): Enter the sale price of the vehicle.
* **Trade In Value** (`trade_in_value`): Enter the trade-in value of the vehicle.
* **Current Vehicles** (`current_vehicles`): Select the current vehicle year.
* **Year Range** (`year_range`): Select the year range of the trade-in vehicle.
* **Trade Status** (`trade_status`): Select the trade status (Active or Inactive).
* **Trade Date** (`trade_date`): Enter the trade date.
* **Sale Date** (`sale_date`): Enter the sale date.
* **Trade Value Date** (`trade_value_date`): Enter the trade value date.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Contact** (`contact`): Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Trade Info** (`trade_info`): Select the trade type and trade-in reason.
* **Trade Type** (`trade_type`): Select the trade type (Cash or Trade).
* **Trade In Reason** (`trade_in_reason`): Enter the reason for the trade-in.
* **Appraiser Name** (`appraiser_name`): Enter the appraiser's name.
* **Appraiser Company** (`appraiser_company`): Enter the appraiser's company.
* **Appraiser License Number** (`appraiser_license`): Enter the appraiser's license number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Trade In Date** (`trade_in_date`): Enter the trade-in date.
* **Trade Status Date** (`trade_status_date`): Enter the trade status date.
* **Trade In Info** (`trade_in_info`): Select the trade-in information options.
