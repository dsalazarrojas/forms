# Vehicle Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for appraisers to evaluate and provide an objective assessment of a vehicle's condition and value. The appraisal form gathers information about the vehicle's year, make, model, color, mileage, engine and interior conditions, transmission type, drive type, purchase and sale prices, and condition of various components. This information will help appraisers create a comprehensive report that can be used for insurance claims, financing, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's year in the "vehicle_year" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's age and potential value.
2. Enter the vehicle's make in the "vehicle_make" field. This will help the appraiser identify the vehicle's manufacturer and model.
3. Enter the vehicle's model in the "vehicle_model" field. This will help the appraiser further identify the vehicle's specifications.
4. Enter the vehicle's color in the "vehicle_color" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's appearance and value.
5. Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "vehicle_mileage" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's condition and wear.
6. Select the engine condition in the "vehicle_engine_condition" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's engine health.
7. Select the interior condition in the "vehicle_interior_condition" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's interior condition.
8. Select the exterior condition in the "vehicle_exterior_condition" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's exterior condition.
9. Select the engine condition in the "engine_condition" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's engine health.
10. Select the vehicle's body type in the "vehicle_body_type" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's shape and size.
11. Select the vehicle's transmission type in the "vehicle_transmission" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's powertrain.
12. Select the vehicle's drive type in the "vehicle_drive_type" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's drive system.
13. Enter the vehicle's purchase price in the "vehicle_purchase_price" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's original value.
14. Enter the vehicle's sale price in the "vehicle_sale_price" field. This will help the appraiser understand the vehicle's current market value.
15. Enter any additional notes about the vehicle in the "vehicle_notes" field. This will help the appraiser understand any special features or defects.
16. Upload up to 10 photos of the vehicle to provide visual evidence of its condition. The "vehicle_photo" fields will help the appraiser assess the vehicle's overall condition and value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_year**: Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **vehicle_color**: Enter the vehicle's color.
* **vehicle_mileage**: Enter the vehicle's mileage.
* **vehicle_engine_condition**: Select the engine condition (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **vehicle_interior_condition**: Select the interior condition (Good, Fair, Poor, or Bad).
* **vehicle_exterior_condition**: Select the exterior condition (Good, Fair, Poor, or Bad).
* **engine_condition**: Select the engine condition (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **vehicle_body_type**: Select the vehicle's body type (Sedan, Hatchback, Wagon, Coupe, SUV, or Minivan).
* **vehicle_transmission**: Select the vehicle's transmission type (Automatic or Manual).
* **vehicle_drive_type**: Select the vehicle's drive type (Rear-wheel drive, All-wheel drive, or Four-wheel drive).
* **vehicle_purchase_price**: Enter the vehicle's original purchase price.
* **vehicle_sale_price**: Enter the vehicle's current sale price.
* **vehicle_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the vehicle.
* **vehicle_photo1** to **vehicle_photo10**: Upload up to 10 photos of the vehicle to provide visual evidence of its condition.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information to ensure a reliable appraisal.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the vehicle's condition.
* Attach relevant photos to support your assessment.
* Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing your appraisal.
