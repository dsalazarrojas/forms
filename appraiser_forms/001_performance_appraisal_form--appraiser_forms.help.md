# Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Form is used to conduct regular evaluations of an employee's performance, covering their skills, abilities, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant performance period from the "Performance Period" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee" field.
3. In the "Appraisal Form" fields, provide a brief overview of the employee's performance, highlighting their strengths and areas for improvement.
4. Choose the specific areas of evaluation that are relevant to the employee's job and performance period from the "Areas Of Evaluation" dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
6. Finally, sign and date the "Appraiser Signature" field to confirm that you've completed and reviewed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appraisal Form (1)**: Enter a brief description of the employee's performance, focusing on their strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Employee (2)**: Enter the employee's name for whom you're conducting the performance appraisal.
* **Performance Period (3)**: Select the performance period being evaluated (e.g., Q1 2022).
* **Appraisal Form (4)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Employee Evaluation (5)**: Enter any comments or feedback on the employee's overall performance.
* **Areas Of Evaluation (6)**: Select the specific areas of evaluation that are relevant to the employee's job and performance period. This could include skills like communication, problem-solving, leadership, or others.
* **Comments (7)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Appraiser Signature (8)**: Sign and date your name to confirm that you've completed and reviewed this form.

## Tips

* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback and comments.
* Focus on objective, fact-based evaluations and avoid personal opinions or biases.
* Use the performance period and job description to guide your evaluation and feedback.
* Keep the "Areas Of Evaluation" field relevant to the employee's job and performance period.
* Ensure that your comments and feedback are constructive and actionable, providing the employee with clear guidance for growth and improvement.
