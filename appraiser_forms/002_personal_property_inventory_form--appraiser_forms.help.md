# Personal Property Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Property Inventory Form is a tool for creating and maintaining a detailed inventory of personal properties. It is intended for personal use or for use within a business setting, such as a real estate agency or insurance company, to provide a comprehensive picture of properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Personal Property Inventory Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Category** field with the category of the property (e.g. "Residence", "Vehicle", "Jewelry").
2. Enter the **Location** of the property, where it is situated.
3. Fill in the **Item Number** field with the unique identifier or serial number of the property (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief **Description** of the property, including its make, model, and any other relevant details.
5. Enter the **Value** of the property.
6. Enter the **Acquired Date** of the property, using the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
7. Select the **Acquired Time** of the property, using the 24-hour format (hh:mm).
8. Choose whether the property is **Insured** or not.
9. Select the **Property Type** of the property (e.g. "Real Estate", "Vehicle", "Artwork").
10. Choose the **Category 1** of the property (if applicable).
11. Choose the **Category 2** of the property (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, false): Select the category of the property (e.g. "Residence", "Vehicle", "Jewelry").
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, false): Enter the location of the property.
* **Item Number** (`Item Number`, `text`, false): Enter the unique identifier or serial number of the property (if applicable).
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, false): Provide a brief description of the property, including its make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Value** (`Value`, `text`, false): Enter the value of the property.
* **Acquired Date** (`Acquired Date`, `date`, false): Enter the date the property was acquired, using the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Acquired Time** (`Acquired Time`, `time`, false): Enter the time the property was acquired, using the 24-hour format (hh:mm).
* **Insured** (`Insured`, `select_one`, false): Choose whether the property is insured or not.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `select_multiple`, false): Select the type of property (e.g. "Real Estate", "Vehicle", "Artwork").
* **Category 1** (`Category 1`, `select_one`, false): Select the first category of the property (if applicable).
* **Category 2** (`Category 2`, `select_one`, false): Select the second category of the property (if applicable).
