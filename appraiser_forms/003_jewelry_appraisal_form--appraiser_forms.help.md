# jewelry-appraisal-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for appraisals of jewelry items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the relevant information about the gemstone being appraised. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item information** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the gemstone being appraised.
* **description** (2, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the gemstone, including its characteristics and any notable features.
* **weight** (3, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the gemstone.
* **gemstone quality** (4, select_one, required/optional): Select the quality of the gemstone (Excellent, Good, or Fair).
* **appraisal date** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the appraisal was conducted.
* **appraised value** (6, number, required/optional): Enter the appraised value of the gemstone.
* **assessor name** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the appraisal.
* **assessor note** (8, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the appraisal.
* **certification** (9, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the gemstone is certified (Yes or No).
* **gemstone type** (10, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of gemstone (Yes or No).
* **appraisal report file** (11, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or files associated with the appraisal.
* **certification document** (12, text, required/optional): Enter any additional certification or documentation associated with the appraisal.
* **assessor signature** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the signature of the person conducting the appraisal.
* **assessor name 2** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the name of an additional assessor, if applicable.
* **assessor title** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the title or position of the additional assessor.
* **gemstone carat** (16, number, required/optional): Enter the carat weight of the gemstone.
* **gemstone weight unit** (17, select_one, required/optional): Select the unit of weight (Yes or No).
* **gemstone weight** (18, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the gemstone.
* **appraiser form type** (19, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of form being used (Yes or No).
* **gemstone clarity** (20, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the clarity of the gemstone (Yes or No).
* **Appraisal Date Time** (21, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the appraisal.
* **Assessor Note 2** (22, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the second assessor.
* **gemstone color** (23, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the color of the gemstone (Yes or No).
* **assessor signature 2** (24, text, required/optional): Enter the signature of the second assessor.
* **assessor telephone** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the second assessor.
