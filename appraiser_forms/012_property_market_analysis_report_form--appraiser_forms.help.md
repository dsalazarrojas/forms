# Property Market Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather market analysis data for a property, providing a comprehensive overview of its attributes and trends.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the property address field with the exact location of the property.
- Select the correct property type (Office, Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Other).
- Choose the property status (Active, Under Contract, Sold, Leased, or Vacant).
- Select the property age category (New, 1-5, 6-15, 16-25, 26-50).
- Enter the property size in square feet.
- Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
- Enter the number of floors.
- Enter the property lot size in square feet.
- Enter the market value and rental value of the property.
- Provide any relevant appraiser comments.
- Enter the user's email address and submit time.
- Finally, provide any notable market trends and take a note on any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the exact address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property (Office, Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Other).
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of the property (Active, Under Contract, Sold, Leased, or Vacant).
* **Property Age** (`property_age`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age of the property (New, 1-5, 6-15, 16-25, 26-50).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, number, optional): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Bedroom Count** (`property_bedroom_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Bathroom Count** (`property_bathroom_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Floor Count** (`property_floor_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of floors in the property.
* **Lot Size** (`property_lot_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the property lot.
* **Market Value** (`property_market_value`, number, optional): Enter the market value of the property.
* **Rental Value** (`property_rental_value`, number, optional): Enter the rental value of the property.
* **Appraiser Comments** (`appraiser_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes from the appraiser.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`form_submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Property Market Trends** (`property_market_trends`, text, optional): Enter any notable market trends.
* **Property Market Trends Note** (`property_market_trends_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments on market trends.
