# create-account - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for user account creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your first name.
2. Fill out the "Last Name" field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field will be verified by a confirmation step.
4. Choose "Verify email" in the "Verify Email" field to receive an email with a confirmation link.
5. Enter and confirm a password in the respective fields.
6. Choose your role within the platform by selecting "Regular user" or "Admin" in the "Role" field.
7. Confirm your chosen role by selecting the same option in the "Confirm User Role" field.
8. Answer the confirmatory questions by selecting "True" or "False" accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This field will be verified by a confirmation step.
- **Verify Email** (`email_verification`, select_one, required): Choose "Verify email" to receive a confirmation email with a link.
- **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter a password of your choice.
- **Verify Password** (`password_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm the password you entered.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role within the platform ("Regular user" or "Admin").
- **Confirm User Role** (`confirm_user_role`, select_one, required): Confirm your chosen role.
- **Other Confirmatory Question 1** (`other_confirmatory_question_1`, select_one, optional): Answer with "True" or "False" according to your knowledge.
- **Other Confirmatory Question 2** (`other_confirmatory_question_2`, select_one, optional): Answer with "True" or "False" according to your knowledge.
- **Other Confirmatory Question 3** (`other_confirmatory_question_3`, select_one, optional): Answer with "True" or "False" according to your knowledge.
- **Other Confirmatory Question 4** (`other_confirmatory_question_4`, select_one, optional): Answer with "True" or "False" according to your knowledge.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy, especially the email verification step.
- Be cautious with the password you choose and confirm it correctly.
- Choose your role according to the platform's policies and your expected responsibilities.
- Answer the confirmatory questions truthfully according to your knowledge.
