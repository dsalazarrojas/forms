# support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The support_request_form is designed to collect information about support requests from employees. It's a crucial tool for the support team to understand the issue and provide timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate "Request Type" that best describes your issue. You can choose from:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
2. Provide a detailed description of your problem in the "Description" field.
3. Enter your "Contact Email" if you want the support team to reach out to you directly.
4. If you have a specific "Contact Phone" number, please enter it.
5. Choose the "Support Group" that best fits your issue. You can select multiple options if applicable.
6. Select the "Assigned Agent" who you would like to handle your case. This will help us ensure that the right support team member is assigned to your issue.
7. Finally, add any additional "Comments" about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Support Request (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   A short and clear description of the issue you are experiencing.

### Request Type (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   The category of your support request (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, etc.)

### Description (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   A detailed description of the issue, including any relevant details.

### Contact Email (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   The email address where the support team can contact you.

### Contact Phone (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   The phone number where the support team can contact you.

### Support Group (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   A list of support groups related to the issue (you can select multiple options).

### Assigned Agent (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   The support team member you would like to handle your case.

### Comments (<id>, <label>, required: false)
*   Additional information or context about your support request.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible in the description field to help the support team understand your issue.
*   Make sure to check the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields if you want the support team to be able to contact you directly.
*   Selecting the right "Support Group" will help us assign the best support team member for your case.
*   Finally, add any additional comments or context that might help us resolve your issue efficiently.
