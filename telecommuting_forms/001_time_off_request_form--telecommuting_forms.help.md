# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for an employee. This includes requesting a leave of absence from work for a specified period. Before submitting a request, please ensure that you have discussed and received approval from your manager or HR representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the `employee_name` field.
2.  Select the start and end dates for your time off.
3.  Choose the time interval (e.g., half-day, full day, or multiple days).
4.  Enter the start and end times if applicable.
5.  Provide a brief explanation for your reason for time off.
6.  Add any additional comments or details about your time off request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
* **start_date** (`start date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to start your time off from.
* **end_date** (`end date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to end your time off by.
* **time_interval** (`time interval`, text, required/optional): Choose the length of time off you are requesting (e.g., half-day, full day, multiple days).
* **start_time** (`start time`, time, required/optional): If your time off is not a full day, enter the start time.
* **end_time** (`end time`, time, required/optional): If your time off is not a full day, enter the end time.
* **reason** (`reason`, note, required/optional): Briefly explain the reason for your time off request.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your time off request.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Check the company's time off policy for any specific requirements or restrictions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative.
