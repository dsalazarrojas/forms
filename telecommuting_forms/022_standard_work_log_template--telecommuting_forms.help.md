# Standard Work Log Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a work log template designed to record and track employee work activities. It captures essential details such as dates, times, work categories, statuses, and contact information to facilitate accurate record-keeping and communication between employees and managers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required to complete the form accurately.
2. Start by filling out the "date_of_log" field with the date the work was performed.
3. Enter the "start_time_of_work" and "end_time_of_work" fields to record the time worked.
4. Select the "work_category" field to indicate whether the work was performed from home, on-site, or remotely.
5. Choose the "work_status" field to update the status of the work, such as "In Progress," "Completed," or "On Hold."
6. Select the "work_priority" field to indicate the priority level of the work, such as "Low," "High," or "Medium."
7. Enter the "email" and "phone" fields to provide contact information.
8. If applicable, add additional comments or notes in the "work_description" and "work_description_additional" fields.
9. Finally, submit the form by entering the "submit_log" field with a brief description of the work performed.

## Tips

* Always fill out the "submit_log" field with a brief description of the work performed.
* Ensure that the "date_of_log," "start_time_of_work," and "end_time_of_work" fields are filled out accurately.
* Be clear and concise when entering comments or additional details in the "work_description," "work_description_additional," and "submit_comment" fields.
* If applicable, fill out the "assigned_to" field with the person to whom the work was assigned.
* Use the "work_category" field to select the correct category of work performed.
* Select the correct "work_status" and "work_priority" fields based on the nature of the work.
* Enter accurate contact information in the "email" and "phone" fields.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
