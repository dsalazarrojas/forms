# Work From Home Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to request work from home status. It allows you to provide your employee information, select a start and end date, and request approval from a manager. The form also requires approval from an approver and allows for a status to be assigned and comments to be added.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Employee Info section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date for your work from home request.
3. In the E-Signature section, type your signature in the provided field.
4. If you have a manager that you would like to approve your request, fill in their Manager Name, Manager Email, and Manager Phone.
5. In the Approver Name, Approver Email, and Approver Phone sections, fill in the information for the person you would like to be your approver.
6. Select the Status of your request from the drop down menu.
7. If you have any comments regarding your request, type them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, required): This section is for your personal details. Fill in your name and any other relevant information.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to start working from home.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to end working from home.
* **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, text, required): Type your electronic signature in this field.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you have a manager that you would like to approve your request, fill in their name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): If you have a manager that you would like to approve your request, fill in their email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): If you have a manager that you would like to approve your request, fill in their phone number.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to share with your manager.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person you would like to be your approver.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, required): Fill in the email address of your approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, required): Fill in the phone number of your approver.
* **Approver Note** (`approver_note`, note, required): This field is for any additional comments you would like to include for your approver.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your request from the drop down menu.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments you would like to include with your request.

Note: The form requires that the employee, approver, and manager information be filled in for approval to be granted.
