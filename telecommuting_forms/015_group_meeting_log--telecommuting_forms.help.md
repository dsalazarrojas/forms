# Group Meeting Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Meeting Log form is used to record and report details about group meetings. It helps managers and team leaders track who attended meetings, meeting status, and other important meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Who Attended" field by choosing the relevant team members from the list.
2. Choose the "Meeting Status" field by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive".
3. Enter the "Meeting Date" and "Meeting Time" fields using the provided date and time input fields.
4. Optionally, add notes about the meeting in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Log**: This is a text area where you can add any additional information about the meeting.
* **Who Attended**: This field allows you to select which team members attended the meeting. Please choose all team members who attended.
* **Meeting Status**: This field indicates the status of the meeting. Please select either "Active" or "Inactive" based on your team's current status.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in the provided date input field.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in the provided time input field.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional notes about the meeting.
