# Telecommuting Productivity Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the impact of telecommuting on productivity and work-related experiences. Your responses will help us better understand the effects of telecommuting on work-life balance, job satisfaction, communication, and other aspects of work life. Please answer honestly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by selecting the correct answer for each question.
2. Be truthful and provide as much detail as possible in text fields.
3. If a question is not applicable to you, simply select "Prefer not to say" in the relevant field.
4. You can provide comments or recommendations for improvement in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number at the end to facilitate follow-up communication if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Remote work arrangement** (1, text): This field is meant to be a brief description of your current or most common remote work arrangement. For example, "Full-time remote work", "Flexible schedule", or "Rarely works from home".
  
- **Work location** (2, text): Please indicate the most common location where you perform your work tasks. For example, "Home", "Office", "Co-working space", or "Multiple locations".
  
- **Do you work from home?** (3, select_one): If you work from home, select "True". If you do not, select "False". If you prefer not to answer, select "Prefer not to say".
  
- **Working hours flexibility** (4, time): Indicate how flexible your working hours are. Use the time input field to select a time that best represents your work hours flexibility, for example, 8:00-16:00 or 09:00-17:00.
  
- **Job satisfaction** (5, number): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means you are very dissatisfied, and 10 means you are very satisfied.
  
- **Work-life balance** (6, select_one): Assess how telecommuting has affected your work-life balance. Select "Slightly better", "Much better", "Much worse", or "Not affected".
  
- **Productivity** (7, number): Rate your productivity while telecommuting on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means you are much less productive, and 10 means you are much more productive.
  
- **Communication satisfaction** (8, number): Rate how satisfied you are with communication while telecommuting on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means you are very dissatisfied, and 10 means you are very satisfied.
  
- **Collaboration** (9, number): Rate how satisfied you are with collaboration while telecommuting on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means you are very dissatisfied, and 10 means you are very satisfied.
  
- **Work arrangement adequacy** (10, number): Rate how adequate your current work arrangement is on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means it is not adequate, and 10 means it is very adequate.
  
- **Overall satisfaction** (11, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with telecommuting on a scale from 0 to 10. A 0 means you are very dissatisfied, and 10 means you are very satisfied.
  
- **Recommendations for improvement** (12, text): If you have suggestions on how we can improve telecommuting, write them down here.
  
- **Comments** (13, note): This is an optional field to provide any additional comments or feedback.
  
- **Email** (14, email): Enter your email address to facilitate follow-up communication if needed.
  
- **Phone** (15, text): Enter your phone number to facilitate follow-up communication if needed.
