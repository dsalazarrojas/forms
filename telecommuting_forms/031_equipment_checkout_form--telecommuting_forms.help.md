# Equipment Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request equipment checkout for work-related activities. It helps the company to track and manage equipment usage, assign equipment to employees, and monitor the status of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name to identify your workplace.
2. Enter your name to confirm your identity as the equipment user.
3. Specify the type of equipment you need.
4. Set the checkout and return dates for the equipment.
5. If you've been assigned to work on specific equipment or tools, enter the assigned tool or tool name in the 'Assigned To' field.
6. Leave a note about your work on the equipment or your request.
7. Enter your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
8. If applicable, select the category or categories related to your work or equipment usage.
9. Confirm if you need any additional tools or support by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' under the 'Tags' field.
10. If relevant, select the applications you plan to use on your equipment from the 'Sync Applications' list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (company_name)**: Enter your company name for identification purposes.
* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter your name to confirm your identity as the equipment user.
* **Equipment Type (equipment_type)**: Specify the type of equipment you need, e.g., laptop, printer, or smartphone.
* **Checkout Date (checkout_date)**: Select the date you plan to start using the equipment.
* **Return Date (return_date)**: Set the date you plan to return the equipment to the company.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: If you've been assigned specific equipment or tools, enter the assigned tool or tool name here.
* **Notes (notes)**: Leave any notes about your work or equipment usage, such as the task you're working on or the purpose of the equipment.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your work phone number for easy contact.
* **Category (category)**: Select the category or categories related to your work or equipment usage. This helps the company to track usage by department or project.
* **Tags (tags)**: Confirm if you need any additional support or tools by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Tags Options (tags_options)**: Select the categories related to your work or equipment usage for more detailed tracking.
* **Comments (comments)**: Leave any comments about your work or equipment usage, such as updates on the project status.
* **Custom (custom)**: Enter any additional information relevant to your equipment request.
* **Sync Applications (sync_applications)**: Select the applications you plan to use on the equipment, if applicable.

By following these steps, you'll be able to complete the Equipment Checkout Form with ease and ensure a smooth equipment usage process for yourself and your team!
