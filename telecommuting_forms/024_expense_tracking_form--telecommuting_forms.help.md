# expense_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about individual expenses submitted by employees for reimbursement or tracking purposes in a remote work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" to start the form.
2. Fill in the required information for each field.
3. Click on "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Expense Info** (`expense_tracking_form.transaction_info`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the transaction, including the type of expense (e.g., meals, travel, etc.) and any other relevant details.

### **Date Of Expense** (`expense_tracking_form.date_of_expense`, required: false)
Enter the date the expense occurred.

### **Amount Of Expense** (`expense_tracking_form.amount_of_expense`, required: false)
Enter the exact amount of the expense.

## Tips
- Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate expense tracking.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your manager or administrator.
- Review the submitted form for any errors before clicking "Submit".
