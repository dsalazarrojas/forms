# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple report form for employees to document their work hours spent, breaks, and tasks completed at the end of the day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the work hours spent field with your total hours worked.
2. Enter your work location.
3. Optionally, add any notes about your workday.
4. Select the tasks you completed during your workday from the dropdown options.
5. Enter your email address so that your report can be sent to your manager or HR representative.
6. Optionally, enter a valid phone number.
7. Select the date you completed the form.
8. Select the person who approved or submitted the report (if applicable).
9. Submit the form to complete the report.
