### Simple Time Sheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the time spent on various projects and tasks. It captures information on the hours worked, the project being worked on, and the work performed. This form is meant to be a simple and straightforward way to document and report time spent on tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title to access the form.
2. Start by entering the number of hours worked for the period in the "Hours Worked" field.
3. Select the relevant project from the "Project" dropdown list.
4. For the "Work Performed" field, select all the tasks that you have performed during the period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of hours worked during the period. If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank, but please note that this may affect the accuracy of your report.
* **Project** (`project`, select_one, required: false): Select the name of the project you were working on. This is an important field as it helps us identify which project your hours are associated with.
* **Work Performed** (`work_performed`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the tasks that you have performed during the period. This field helps us understand the specific work you were doing, and is an opportunity for you to reflect on your activities.

## Tips
* Please note that the "Hours Worked" field is not required, but it is recommended for accurate reporting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended to be a simple and efficient way to document and report your time. If you have any difficulties or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
