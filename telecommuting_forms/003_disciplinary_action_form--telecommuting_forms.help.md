# Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The disciplinary action form is used to document and manage employee disciplinary actions, such as incidents, investigations, and subsequent actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's information in the **Employee Info** field.
2. Provide a reason for the disciplinary action in the **Employee Reasoning** field.
3. Describe the disciplinary action taken in the **Manager Reasoning** field.
4. Select one of the options in the **Disciplinary Action** field to indicate whether the disciplinary action was taken.
5. Describe the next steps or follow-up actions in the **Next Steps** field.
6. If applicable, the employee's **Employee Signature**, **Manager Signature**, and **Supervisor Signature** fields should be filled in.
7. Finally, enter the current date in the **Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (Required): Enter the employee's name, job title, and department.
* **Employee Reasoning** (Required): Explain the reason for the disciplinary action, including any details about the incident or behavior that led to it.
* **Manager Reasoning** (Required): Describe the disciplinary action taken, including any steps taken or actions implemented.
* **Disciplinary Action** (Required): Select one of the two options, "Yes" or "No," to indicate whether the disciplinary action was taken.
* **Next Steps** (Required): Describe any follow-up actions or steps taken as a result of the disciplinary action.
* **Employee Signature** (Optional): Enter the employee's signature, typically after they have agreed to the disciplinary action.
* **Manager Signature** (Optional): Enter the manager's signature, typically after they have agreed to the disciplinary action.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Optional): Enter the supervisor's signature, typically after they have reviewed and approved the disciplinary action.
* **Date** (Required): Enter the current date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD."
