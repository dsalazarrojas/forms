# Issue Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Issue Tracking Form is used to collect and manage information about issues that have occurred within a team or company. This form helps to identify, categorize, and prioritize issues to facilitate prompt resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, navigate to the Issue Tracking Form page.
2.  Select the category of the issue from the "Category" dropdown.
3.  Provide a clear description of the issue in the "Issue description" field.
4.  Choose the status of the issue from the "Issue Status" dropdown.
5.  Choose the priority of the issue from the "Issue priority" dropdown.
6.  Identify who reported the issue from the "Who reported the issue" dropdown.
7.  Enter the date the issue was reported in the "Date Reported" field.
8.  Enter the time the issue was reported in the "Time Reported" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the issue from the dropdown. This helps in identifying and grouping similar issues.
*   **Issue description** (`issue_description`, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
*   **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the issue, which can be Active or Inactive.
*   **Issue priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one, required: false): Select a priority level for the issue, which can be 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Who reported the issue** (`issue_reporter`, select_one, required: false): Identify who reported the issue.
*   **Date Reported** (`issue_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the issue was reported.
*   **Time Reported** (`issue_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the issue was reported.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you provide accurate and clear information to help the team or company address the issue efficiently.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please ask for assistance or clarification.
*   If the issue is already being tracked, please do not fill out this form again.
