# leave_or_remote_work_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate leave or remote work requests from employees. It helps employees submit their requests in a clear and organized manner, and allows managers to review and approve them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification card or personnel file.
2. **Choose your Leave Type**: Select the type of leave you're requesting, which can be "Remote Work", "Leave", or "Other" (if your reason doesn't fit into the first two categories).
3. **Specify your Leave Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your leave or remote work period.
4. **Select your Work Time**: Choose your start and end times for your leave or remote work period.
5. **Provide Reason (Optional)**: If you're requesting leave, you can optionally provide a reason for your request.
6. **Choose your Employee Status**: Select your current status as an employee, which can be "On Leave", "Remote Work", or "Not Available".
7. **Enter Manager's Name**: If your request is being reviewed by a manager, please enter their name.
8. **Enter Team Lead's Name**: If your request is being reviewed by a team lead, please enter their name.
9. **Select Department**: If you're requesting leave or remote work for a specific department, please enter the department name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Required)**: Your employee name as it appears on your employee identification card or personnel file.
* **Leave Start Date (Required)**: Enter the start date of your leave or remote work period.
* **Leave End Date (Required)**: Enter the end date of your leave or remote work period.
* **Leave Type (Required)**: Select the type of leave you're requesting, which can be "Remote Work", "Leave", or "Other".
* **Work Location (Optional)**: If you're requesting remote work, enter the location where you'll be working from.
* **Start Time (Required)**: Choose your start time for your leave or remote work period.
* **End Time (Required)**: Choose your end time for your leave or remote work period.
* **Reason (Optional)**: If you're requesting leave, you can optionally provide a reason for your request.
* **Approval Status (Required)**: This field will be automatically populated once your request is reviewed and approved.
* **Approval Date (Optional)**: If your request is approved, this field will be automatically populated with the date of approval.
* **Employee Status (Optional)**: Select your current status as an employee, which can be "On Leave", "Remote Work", or "Not Available".
* **Manager Name (Optional)**: If your request is being reviewed by a manager, enter their name.
* **Team Lead Name (Optional)**: If your request is being reviewed by a team lead, enter their name.
* **Department (Optional)**: If you're requesting leave or remote work for a specific department, enter the department name.
