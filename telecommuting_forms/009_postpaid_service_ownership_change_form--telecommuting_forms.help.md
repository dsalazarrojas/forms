# Postpaid Service Ownership Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to change the ownership of a postpaid service from the current owner to a new owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the option to transfer or not transfer ownership in section 1 (`account_transfer_request`).
2. Provide details about the new owner in section 2 (`new_owner_details`).
3. Enter the date of transfer in section 3 (`transfer_date`).
4. Enter any relevant information about the account in section 4 (`account_info`).
5. Enter the account number in section 5 (`account_number`).
6. Provide details about the current owner in section 6 (`current_owner_details`).
7. Enter the reason for transfer in section 7 (`transfer_reason`).
8. Add any additional notes or comments in section 8 (`notes`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Account Transfer Request**: This field is used to indicate whether you want to transfer ownership or not. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to transfer the ownership and 'No' otherwise.
* **2. New Owner Details**: This field is used to provide information about the new owner. Please enter the name and contact details of the new owner.
* **3. Transfer Date**: This field is used to enter the date when you plan to transfer the ownership. Please use the format 'DD/MM/YYYY'.
* **4. Account Info**: This field is used to provide any additional information about the account. Please enter any relevant details about the account.
* **5. Account Number**: This field is used to enter the account number of the postpaid service.
* **6. Current Owner Details**: This field is used to provide information about the current owner. Please enter the name and contact details of the current owner.
* **7. Transfer Reason**: This field is used to enter the reason for transferring the ownership. Please be specific and clear about your reason.
* **8. Notes**: This field is used to add any additional comments or notes about the ownership transfer process. Please use this field to provide any further information or clarification.
