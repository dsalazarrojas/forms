# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact Form is a crucial document that collects essential information from employees about their emergency contacts. This form is used to ensure that the company can quickly get in touch with a designated contact person in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
2. Indicate whether you have an emergency contact by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Relationship" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
5. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field. This will allow the company to contact you in case of an emergency.
6. If you have an email address for your emergency contact, enter it in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
7. Indicate whether your emergency contact has an email address by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
8. Provide any additional notes about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact, such as a family member or friend.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, **Required**): Indicate whether you have an emergency contact. Select "Yes" if you have someone you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency. Select "No" if you do not have an emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Optional**): If you have an email address, enter it so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, **Optional**): If your emergency contact has an email address, enter it.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, **Required**): Indicate whether your emergency contact has an email address. Select "Yes" if they do, select "No" if they do not.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, **Optional**): Provide any additional notes about your emergency contact, such as their address or any other information that might be helpful in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to update your emergency contact information regularly to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information.
* Double-check the accuracy of your phone and email numbers entered in the form.
* If your emergency contact does not speak English, please provide additional information to help us communicate with them effectively.
