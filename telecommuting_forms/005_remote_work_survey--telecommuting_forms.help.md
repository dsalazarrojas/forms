# Remote Work Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about employees who are working remotely. The goal is to gather data on the number of employees working remotely, their job titles, departments, and other relevant details. This information will help us understand the current state of remote work within the company and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your company email address in the "company_email" field.
2. Enter your company phone number in the "company_phone" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
4. Enter your manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
5. Enter your manager's phone number in the "manager_phone" field.
6. Enter your manager's email address in the "manager_email" field.
7. Enter your name in the "employee_name" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "job_type" field to indicate whether you are a full-time employee or not.
9. Enter the number of hours you work in the "work_hours" field.
10. Enter the start date of your remote work in the "start_date" field.
11. Enter the end date of your remote work in the "end_date" field.
12. Enter the time you start working in the "start_time" field.
13. Enter the time you finish working in the "end_time" field.
14. Enter the location where you work remotely in the "work_location" field.
15. Select "Active" or "Inactive" in the "work_status" field to indicate your current remote work status.
16. Enter the reason you are working remotely in the "reason_for_remote_work" field.
17. Enter any comments from your manager in the "manager_comments" field.
18. Enter any comments you have as an employee in the "employee_comments" field.
19. Enter any company policies related to remote work in the "company_policies" field.
20. Enter any company equipment you use for remote work in the "company_equipment" field.
21. Enter any employee-provided equipment you use for remote work in the "employee_provision" field.
22. Enter any work equipment you use for remote work in the "work_equipment" field.
23. Enter any manager-provided equipment you use for remote work in the "manager_equipment" field.
24. Enter any company policies related to remote work in the "remote_work_policies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): This is the email address of your company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): This is your job title within the company.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): This is the name of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): This is the email address of your manager.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is your name.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are a full-time employee, "No" otherwise.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you work remotely.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your remote work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your remote work.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you start working remotely.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the time you finish working remotely.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you work remotely.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Select "Active" if you are currently working remotely, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Reason for Remote Work** (`reason_for_remote_work`, text, required): Enter the reason you are working remotely.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from your manager.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments you have as an employee.
* **Company Policies** (`company_policies`, text, required): Enter any company policies related to remote work.
* **Company Equipment** (`company_equipment`, text, required): Enter any company equipment you use for remote work.
* **Employee Provision** (`employee_provision`, text, required): Enter any employee-provided equipment you use for remote work.
* **Work Equipment** (`work_equipment`, text, required): Enter any work equipment you use for remote work.
* **Manager Equipment** (`manager_equipment`, text, required): Enter any manager-provided equipment you use for remote work.
* **Remote Work Policies** (`remote_work_policies`, text, required): Enter any company policies related to remote work.
