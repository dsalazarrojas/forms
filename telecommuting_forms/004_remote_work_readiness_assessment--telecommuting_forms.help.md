# Remote Work Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees assess their readiness for remote work. It collects information about your daily habits, work-life balance, and work-related habits to evaluate your potential success in a remote work setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions truthfully, to the best of your ability.
2. Complete the form in your personal time, and not during work hours.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
