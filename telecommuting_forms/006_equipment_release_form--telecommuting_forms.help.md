# Equipment Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Equipment Release Form is a crucial document used to release company equipment that has been borrowed by employees for work purposes. This form ensures that all relevant information is collected and documented accurately, facilitating a smooth equipment release process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as the user.
2. Select the category of the equipment from the options provided (Laptop, Mobile Phone, or Tablet).
3. Enter the name of the equipment being released.
4. Enter the equipment number (if available).
5. Provide a brief description of the equipment.
6. Specify the reason for releasing the equipment.
7. Sign your name to confirm the release.
8. Enter the date the equipment was requested for release.
9. Enter the date the equipment is expected to be returned.
10. Add any additional notes about the release or return of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Your name as the user submitting the form.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Your work email address.
* **Category** (`equipment_category`, select_one, required: false): The category of the equipment being released. Choose from Laptop, Mobile Phone, or Tablet.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, required: false): The name of the equipment being released.
* **Equipment Number** (`equipment_number`, text, required: false): The unique number or ID of the equipment (if available).
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of the equipment being released.
* **Borrow Reason** (`borrow_reason`, text, required: false): The reason the equipment was borrowed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Your signature to confirm the release.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, required: false): The date the equipment was requested for release.
* **Equipment Return Date** (`equipment_return_date`, date, required: false): The date the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Any additional comments or notes about the release or return of the equipment.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have checked with your supervisor or the IT department before filling out this form.
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered, especially for the equipment category and equipment number.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your identity and confirm the release.
* Add any relevant notes to help facilitate the return of the equipment.
