# Remote Work Accommodation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request remote work arrangements for employees, such as flexible work hours or work from home options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the start date of the remote work arrangement in the "Start Date" field.
2.  Enter the end date of the remote work arrangement in the "End Date" field.
3.  Enter the location where you will be working remotely in the "Location" field.
4.  If you have a specific reason for requesting the remote work arrangement, enter it in the "Reason" field.
5.  Enter the terms of your remote work arrangement in the "Work Arrangement Terms" field.
6.  Provide the address and details of your accommodation in the "Accommodation Details" field.
7.  Select "Approved" or "Not Approved" for "Terms Approval" and enter any comments in the "Approval Comments" field.
8.  Select "Approved" or "Not Approved" for "Work Arrangement Terms Approval" and enter any comments in the "Approval Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Start Date**: Enter the date when you want to start your remote work arrangement.
*   **End Date**: Enter the date when you want to end your remote work arrangement.
*   **Location**: Enter the location where you will be working remotely.
*   **Reason**: Enter a reason for your request if you have one.
*   **Work Arrangement Terms**: Enter the terms of your remote work arrangement.
*   **Accommodation Details**: Enter the address and details of your accommodation.
*   **Terms Approval**: Select "Approved" if you approve the terms, "Not Approved" if you don't, and "Pending" if you need more time to review.
*   **Work Arrangement Terms Approval**: Select "Approved" if you approve the terms, "Not Approved" if you don't, and "Pending" if you need more time to review.
*   **Approval Comments**: Enter any comments you want to provide to the manager.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields to ensure the manager can make an informed decision.
*   Enter as much detail as possible in the "Accommodation Details" field to help the manager review your request.
*   Be clear and concise in the "Approval Comments" field to help the manager understand your thoughts.
