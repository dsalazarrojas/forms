# Computer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Computer Request Form is used to collect information from employees regarding their computer and equipment needs for work purposes. This form helps to ensure that the right equipment is provided to employees in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your request ID, date, and other personal details in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your department and job title from the provided options.
3. Choose the type of equipment you need and the primary use it will be used for.
4. Specify any current equipment you have and any issues with it.
5. If necessary, provide technical specifications and software requirements for the equipment.
6. Explain why you need the equipment and how long you will need it for.
7. Specify where you would like the equipment to be delivered and when you need it by.
8. If you need help with setting up the equipment, choose the relevant option.
9. If the equipment is temporary, specify when you expect to return it.
10. Confirm that your manager has approved this request.
11. Finally, select the current status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request ID** (1, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for your request.
* **Request Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this request.
* **Employee Name** (3, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (4, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (5, select_one, required): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Job Title** (6, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email Address** (7, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (8, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Manager Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Work Arrangement** (10, select_one, required): Select your work arrangement from the provided options.
* **Equipment Requested** (11, select_multiple, required): Select the equipment you need.
* **Primary Use** (12, select_one, required): Specify the primary use of the equipment.
* **Current Equipment** (13, select_one, required): Specify if you have current equipment and if it's sufficient.
* **Current Equipment Issues** (14, text, optional): Describe any issues with your current equipment.
* **Specifications Needed** (15, text, optional): Provide any specific technical requirements for the equipment.
* **Software Requirements** (16, text, optional): List any software you need to run on the equipment.
* **Request Justification** (17, text, required): Explain why you need the equipment.
* **Duration Needed** (18, select_one, required): Specify how long you will need the equipment for.
* **Delivery Address** (19, text, required): Enter the address where you would like the equipment to be delivered.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (20, date, required): Enter the date you would like the equipment to be delivered.
* **Setup Assistance Needed** (21, select_one, required): Specify if you need help with setting up the equipment.
* **Expected Return Date** (22, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to return the equipment if it's temporary.
* **Manager Approval** (23, select_one, required): Confirm that your manager has approved this request.
* **Request Status** (24, select_one, required): Select the current status of your request from the provided options.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your equipment needs and requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your manager or IT department for guidance.
* Please review your request carefully before submitting it to ensure it's accurate and complete.
