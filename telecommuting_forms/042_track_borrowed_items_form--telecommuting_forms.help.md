# track_borrowed_items_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The track_borrowed_items_form is a form used to track items that have been borrowed from the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the ID of the classroom where the item was borrowed from.
2. Enter the name of the item that was borrowed.
3. Enter your name or ID as the user who borrowed the item.
4. Enter the date the item was checked out.
5. Enter the date the item is expected to be returned.
6. Provide a reason for borrowing the item (optional).
7. Add any additional comments (optional).
8. Enter your email address (optional).
9. Enter your phone number (optional).
10. (Optional) Repeat the process for additional items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **classroom_id** (1, number, required): Enter the ID of the classroom where the item was borrowed from.
- **item_id** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the item that was borrowed.
