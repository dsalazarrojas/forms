# Office Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Supply Order Form is used to collect information about customer orders for office supplies, including order details, customer information, and payment information. This form is designed to be completed by customers to place orders for office supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your order details, including any specific items you would like to order.
2. Enter your customer information, including your name and contact details.
3. Enter your payment information, including the payment method and amount.
4. Review your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (order_details)** (`Order Details`, text, optional): Please enter the specific items you would like to order, including the quantity if applicable. For example, "10 pens" or "100 sheets of paper".
* **Customer Info (customer_info)** (`Customer Info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information. This will help us get in touch with you to confirm your order.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`Payment Info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method, such as "Credit Card" or "PayPal", and the amount you would like to pay. Please ensure that your payment information is accurate and complete to avoid any issues with your order.
