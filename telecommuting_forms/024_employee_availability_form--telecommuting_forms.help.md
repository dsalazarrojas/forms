# employee_availability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_availability_form is used to record and update an employee's availability for telecommuting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure that you can be reached for work-related purposes.
3. Select the Start Date and End Date of your telecommuting period.
4. Choose your Start Time and End Time for the work period.
5. Enter the total number of work hours you will be available during the period in the Work Hours field.
6. Select the days you will be available to work (Monday to Sunday).
7. Enter specific work hours for each day of the week (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to identify yourself accurately.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to identify yourself accurately.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that you can be reached for work-related purposes.
* **Start Date**: Select the date from which you will start working from home or remotely.
* **End Date**: Select the date on which your telecommuting period ends.
* **Start Time**: Choose the time from which you will start your work from home or remotely.
* **End Time**: Choose the time at which you will end your work from home or remotely.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you will work during the period.
* **Work Days**: Choose the days you will be available to work (Monday to Sunday).
* **Work Hours 7**: Enter the specific work hours for Saturday.
* **Work Hours 8**: Enter the specific work hours for Sunday.
* **Work Hours 9**: Enter the specific work hours for the rest of the week (Monday to Friday, Monday to Thursday, etc.).
* **Work Hours 10**: Enter the specific work hours for the rest of the week (Monday to Friday, Monday to Thursday, etc.).
