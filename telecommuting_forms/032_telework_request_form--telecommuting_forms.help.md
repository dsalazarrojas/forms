# Telework Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Telework Request Form is used to request authorization for employees to work remotely. It helps employees provide essential information about their work arrangement, including the start and end dates, work location, and relevant details about their manager and team lead.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit a successful telework request, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk *).
2. Select 'Manager Approval' to choose the status of your request: Approved, Denied, Pending, or Unknown.
3. Enter your Work From Hours, Work From Days, and Work From Weekdays and Weekends as required.
4. Provide the Manager Name, Manager Email, Manager Phone, Team Lead Name, Team Lead Email, and Team Lead Phone.
5. Enter your Employee Name, Department, Job Title, and Supervisor Name (if applicable).
6. Add any comments or notes if necessary.
7. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Request** (<id: 1>): Enter a brief description of your work request, including the purpose and duration of your telework arrangement.
* **Reason for Request** (<id: 2>): Explain why you're requesting telework, including any relevant details that support your request.
* **Start Date** (<id: 3>): Enter the date you plan to start working remotely.
* **End Date** (<id: 4>): Enter the date you plan to end working remotely.
* **Work From Location** (<id: 5>): Specify the location where you'll be working remotely.
* **Manager Approval** (<id: 6>): Choose the status of your request from the options: Approved, Denied, Pending, or Unknown.
* **Comments** (<id: 7>): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Work From Hours** (<id: 8>): Enter the number of hours you plan to work from home.
* **Work From Days** (<id: 9>): Enter the number of days you plan to work from home.
* **Work From Weekdays** (<id: 10>): Select whether you'll work from home on weekdays.
* **Work From Weekends** (<id: 11>): Select whether you'll work from home on weekends.
* **Manager Name** (<id: 12>): Enter your manager's name.

* **Employee Name** (<id: 20>): Enter your name.
