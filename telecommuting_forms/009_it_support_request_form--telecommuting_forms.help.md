# IT Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Request Form is designed to help employees submit requests for assistance with issues they are experiencing with their work devices or systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely, including your contact information and a clear description of the problem.
2. Select the correct operating system from the list.
3. Choose the type of assistance you are requesting from the options provided.
4. Fill in your phone number and email address so we can contact you for further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for any additional information you may want to provide about your issue or yourself.
* **Internet Connection** (`internet_connection`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a description of your internet connection issues, if any.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose the operating system you are using (Windows, macOS, or Linux). If you are unsure, just pick one that might be related to your issue.
* **Problem Details** (`problem_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Briefly describe the problem you are experiencing.
* **Assistance Requested** (`assistance_requested`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more of the options that best describes the type of assistance you are requesting. If you need something not listed, please choose "Other" and provide further details.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you for further details.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you via email if needed.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: `false`): Indicate who can we contact regarding the issue (e.g., team lead, manager, etc.).
