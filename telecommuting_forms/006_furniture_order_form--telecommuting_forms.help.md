# Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Order Form is used to capture detailed information about a customer's furniture order. It includes fields for customer contact and delivery information, specifics about the furniture ordered, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the purpose of the order (moving, removing, or redesigning).
2. Choose the customer's information from the options provided.
3. Select the specific furniture to be ordered from the available options.
4. Enter the quantity of furniture to be ordered.
5. Choose the color options for the selected furniture.
6. Enter the delivery date and delivery time for the order.
7. Enter the additional contact information for the customer.
8. Enter any additional comments or information about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moving, Removing, or Redesigning** (`moving_removing_or_redesigning`, not required): Choose the reason for the order from the options provided.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, not required): Select the customer's information from the options. This might be a contact person's name, phone number, or email address.
* **Select Furniture** (`select_furniture`, not required): Choose the specific furniture item to be ordered from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`quantity_furniture`, not required): Enter the number of items to be ordered.
* **Color Choice** (`color_furniture`, not required): Select the color(s) for the ordered furniture.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, not required): Enter the date for the delivery of the order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, not required): Enter the time for the delivery of the order.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, not required): Enter any additional contact information for the customer.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, not required): Enter any other information about the order that was not covered in other fields.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, not required): Enter any comments or questions you have about the order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information about the order.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact the relevant department or person.
* This form is for internal use only, please be mindful of any sensitive or confidential information you enter.
* If you need to duplicate the form, make sure to follow any applicable business rules or procedures.
