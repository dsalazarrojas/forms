# Internet Speed Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about users' internet experiences to help us improve our infrastructure and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Please answer all fields, as indicated by the required/optional labels.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_input`, `note`, required: false): This section is for providing any additional information about yourself, such as your name, email, or phone number. It's an optional field, but it helps us get in touch with you if we need more details.
* **Internet Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the internet service provider you're currently using. Our options are: AT&T, Verizon, Spectrum, Comcast, Xfinity.
* **Location** (`user_place`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you're located in an area that receives service from your internet service provider. Options are: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Download Speed** (`download_speed`, `number`, required: false): Enter your estimated download speed. This is your average download speed from your service provider.
* **Upload Speed** (`upload_speed`, `number`, required: false): Enter your estimated upload speed. This is your average upload speed from your service provider.
* **Router Type** (`router_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of router you're using to connect to the internet. Our options are: Wi-Fi, Ethernet, MOBILE.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional comments or concerns you may have about your internet connectivity experience.
* **Rating** (`user_rating`, `number`, required: false): Provide a number rating of your overall satisfaction with your internet connectivity from 1-5, where 1 is 'very poor' and 5 is 'excellent'.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're reporting issues with your internet service, contact our support team directly.
* Your input will help us improve our services and infrastructure.
