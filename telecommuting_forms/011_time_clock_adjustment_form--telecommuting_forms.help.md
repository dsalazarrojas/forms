# Time Clock Adjustment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to adjust an employee's time clock for accurate time tracking and payroll calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review and ensure that the form is filled out correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time <required: false>**: Enter the start time of the adjustment.
* **End Time <required: false>**: Enter the end time of the adjustment.
* **Duration <required: false>**: Enter the duration of the adjustment in hours.
* **Break in Hours <required: false>**: Enter the break time in hours.
* **Hours Worked <required: false>**: Enter the total hours worked during the adjustment.
* **Notes <required: false>**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the adjustment.
* **Date Adjusted <required: false>**: Enter the date of the adjustment.
* **Submitted By <required: false>**: Enter your email address or username.
* **Submitted By ID <required: false>**: Enter your employee ID or username.
