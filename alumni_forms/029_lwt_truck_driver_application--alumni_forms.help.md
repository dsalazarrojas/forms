# LWT Truck Driver Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential LWT truck drivers for business-to-business logistics or delivery services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User First Name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Enter your driver's license number in the "Driver License" field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User First Name** (User First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you, e.g., John.
* **Driver License** (Driver License, text, required): Enter your driver's license number or ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 01/01/1990.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication and account creation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, feel free to contact our support team.
* Note: This form will only be used to collect information for new driver applications. If you are an existing driver, please do not fill out this form again.
