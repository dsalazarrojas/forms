# Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from alumni who are attending or not attending the reunion. It helps us gather contact details and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. If you're a graduate, enter the year you graduated in the **Graduation Year** field.
4. Select your **Attending** status by choosing the correct option.
5. If you're attending, you can also select the status of your family/friends in the **Reunion Status** field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the **Email** field, which we'll use to stay in touch with you.
7. If you want to provide your phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field.
8. Enter your address in the **Address** field, which we'll use to send you updates.
9. If you have any comments or suggestions for the reunion, write them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Graduation Year**: If you are a graduate, enter the year you graduated. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Attending**: Select the correct option to indicate if you're attending or not.
*   *Attending* - You will be attending the reunion.
   *Not Attending* - You will not be attending the reunion.
   *Undecided* - You're still deciding or unsure.
* **Reunion Status**: If you're attending, select how you'll be attending (with Family, with Friends, or Alone).
*   *Attending with Family* - You'll be attending with family members.
   *Attending with Friends* - You'll be attending with friends.
   *Not Attending with Family* - You'll be attending without family members.
   *Not Attending with Friends* - You'll be attending without friends.
   *Not Attending* - You'll be attending alone.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach you about reunion updates and details.
* **Phone**: If you want to provide your phone number, enter it here. We'll use it to contact you about the reunion.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can send you updates and information about the reunion.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the reunion.
