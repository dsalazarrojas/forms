# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form" is designed to help create and track invitations for graduation ceremonies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form Details" section with the following information:
	* Graduation Ceremony Invitation Date: Enter the date of the graduation ceremony.
	* Graduation Ceremony Time: Enter the time of the graduation ceremony.
	* Invitation To Address: Enter the address where the graduation ceremony will take place.
	* Graduation Ceremony Invitation Message: Enter a message for the invitation.
2. Go to the "Invitation Options" section and fill in the following:
	* Invitation Status: Select the status of the invitation.
	* Graduation Date In Invitation: If required, enter the graduation date.
	* Recipient Name: Enter the name of the recipient of the invitation.
	* Recipient Email: Enter the email address of the recipient.
	* Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form Comments: Enter any additional comments.
	* Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form Attachments: Upload any attachments for the invitation.
