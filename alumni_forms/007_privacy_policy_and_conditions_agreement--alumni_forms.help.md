# Privacy Policy And Conditions Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for collecting information from alumni, which includes personal data and a signature for consent or agreement. The exact purpose may vary depending on the context and organization using the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the personal data fields: name, surname, email, and phone (if applicable)
2. Read and understand the provided Privacy Policy and Conditions
3. Agree to the terms by signing the signature field

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, text, optional): Please fill in a brief summary of the privacy policy.
* **Conditions** (`conditions`, text, optional): Describe the conditions you are agreeing to.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as per your preferred format.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): Enter your surname as per your preference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Fill in your contact email address. Please use a format that is likely to be recognized by your email service provider.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign below to indicate your agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand the provided privacy policy and conditions before agreeing.
- Enter accurate and valid contact information.
- Sign below the agreement with your preferred name format.
- Submit the form to complete the agreement process.
