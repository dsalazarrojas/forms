# Picnic - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist you in completing the Picnic form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: 
   - Picnic (Text Field)
   - Required (Text Field)
   - Team (Text Field)
   - Management (Text Field)

2. If you want to provide additional information, you can fill out the optional fields:
   - Category (Multiple selection)
   - Date (Date Field)
   - Time (Time Field)
   - School (Text Field)
   - Email (Email Field)
   - Phone (Text Field)
   - Moments (Text Field)
   - Activities (Text Field)
   - Regards (Text Field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Picnic** (`picnic_title`, Text, Required): Enter the title of the picnic event you attended.
* **Category** (`category`, Multiple selection, Required: False): Choose the category that best fits the picnic event. Please select "Yes" in the category list if the event is related to sports, games, or team activities.
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Required): Describe your experience at the picnic event.
* **Date** (`date`, Date Field, Required: False): Enter the date of the picnic event if it occurred in the past or if it's a future date for the picnic.
* **Time** (`time`, Time Field, Required: False): Enter the time of the picnic event if it occurred in the past or if it's a future date for the picnic.
* **School** (`school`, Text, Required: False): If you attended a school-related picnic event, enter the name of the school.
* **Required** (`required_feedback`, Text, Required): Provide any other information that the organizers of the picnic event need to know.
* **Email** (`email`, Email Field, Required: False): Enter your email if you want to stay updated about future events.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number for the organizers to contact you.
* **Moments** (`moments`, Text, Required: False): Share a memorable moment from the picnic event.
* **Activities** (`activities`, Text, Required: False): List any activities you participated in during the picnic event.
* **Regards** (`regards`, Text, Required: False): Express your gratitude to the organizers of the picnic event.
* **Team** (`team`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the team that organized the picnic event.
* **Management** (`management`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the management team that organized the picnic event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the correct category and providing accurate information.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the picnic event organizers.
