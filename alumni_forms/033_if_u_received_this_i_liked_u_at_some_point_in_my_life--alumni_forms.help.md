# if_u_received_this_i_liked_u_at_some_point_in_my_life - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and share memories and stories about recognizing someone in the past.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email in the Email field.
3. Select your Graduation Year from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone field.
5. Select how and why you recognized the person from the provided options.
6. If you'd like to share additional details, enter them in the "Why I Recognized You" field.
7. If you're an old classmate, select your grade from the dropdown list.
8. Answer the question "If I Knew You" with a brief description.
9. Answer the question "If I Recognized You Before" with a brief description.
10. If you'd like to share any additional information, use the "Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Graduation Date** (date, required): Select the year you graduated.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **If I Knew You At** (select_one, required): Select how you knew the person, e.g. "I was in school", "I met online", etc.
* **If You Recognized Me At** (select_multiple, required): Select the locations where you recognized the person, e.g. "school", "at a party", etc.
* **Why I Recognized You At** (text, required): Briefly describe why you recognized the person at that location.
* **If I Was In Your Class** (select_one, required): Select the grade level you were in when you knew the person.
* **If I Knew You** (text, required): Briefly describe how you knew the person.
* **If I Recognized You Before** (select_multiple, required): Select whether you recognized the person before or after a certain event or milestone.
* **If I Recognized You Before Other** (text, required): Briefly describe why you recognized the person before a certain event or milestone.
* **If I Recognized You Before Other Details** (text, required): Provide any additional information about how you recognized the person before a certain event or milestone.
* **Why I Recognized You Before** (select_one, required): Select the reason you recognized the person before a certain event or milestone.
* **If I Recognized You Before Other** (text, required): Briefly describe why you recognized the person before a certain event or milestone.
