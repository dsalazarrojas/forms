# Alumni Contact Directory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Contact Directory Form is a collection of personal and professional details from our esteemed alumni community. This information will be displayed in a publicly accessible directory, allowing current students and staff to connect with alumni more easily.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Graduation Major**. These details will help us verify your identity and display your affiliation with the institution.
2.  Enter your **Graduation Year** to ensure that we accurately display your graduation year in the directory.
3.  Provide your **Current Profession** (if applicable) to give others a better understanding of your work experience and expertise.
4.  Enter your **Employer or Company** (if applicable) to provide context about your professional background.
5.  If you have a **LinkedIn Profile URL**, please share it with us to help others connect with you more easily.
6.  Fill in your **City of Residence** and **Country** where you currently reside or work.
7.  Enter your **Contact Email**, which will be used to communicate with you about alumni events and updates.
8.  Choose to **Display Email in Directory** if you're okay with sharing your email with others. If not, it will be hidden from public view.
9.  Finally, **Consent to receive alumni newsletters** to stay updated on campus and alumni events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Graduation Major** (`graduation_major`, text, required): Your major during your time at our institution.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): The year you graduated from our institution.
* **Current Profession** (`current_profession`, text, optional): Briefly describe your current job title or field of work.
* **Employer or Company** (`employer_company`, text, optional): Provide the name of your current employer or company.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, text, optional): Share your linkedin profile to make it easier for others to connect with you.
* **City of Residence** (`city_of_residence`, text, required): Where you currently reside or work.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): The country where you currently live or work.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Your email address that we'll use to contact you.
* **Display Email in Directory** (`display_email_permission`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you're okay with sharing your email with others; choose 'False' to keep it hidden.
* **Consent to receive alumni newsletters** (`profile_update_consent`, select_one, optional): Check 'True' to stay updated on campus and alumni events; check 'False' to opt-out.
