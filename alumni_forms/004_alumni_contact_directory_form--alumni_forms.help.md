# Alumni Contact Directory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store alumni information in a centralized directory, enabling alumni to connect with each other and the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. Make sure to enter your email address (if you want to receive updates and notifications).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the directory.
* **Nickname at School** (`student_nickname`, `text`, optional): If you had a nickname during your time at school, feel free to share it here.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you graduated from the university.
* **Current Mailing Address** (`current_mailing_address`, `text`, optional): Provide your current home address for physical mailings and university magazine.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Professional Biography** (`professional_biography`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your career journey since graduation.
* **Favorite School Memory** (`favorite_memory`, `text`, optional): Share a cherished memory from your time at school.
* **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, `text`, optional): Provide your social media handle (e.g., Twitter, Instagram).
* **Alumni Interests** (`alumni_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select areas where you'd like to be involved in as an alumni (e.g., mentoring, professional networking, class reunions).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., email, phone, post).

Note: If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification!
