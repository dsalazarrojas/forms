# Alumni Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey aimed at gathering information from alumni about their experiences and engagement with the institution. The goal is to understand alumni's demographics, interests, and level of engagement, which will help us improve our services and activities for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the correct options for each question.
2. Enter your full name and graduation year if you choose to do so.
3. Describe your current role and how often you engage with alumni activities.
4. Indicate your primary interest in the alumni community.
5. If you wish to be contacted by the institution, select the "True" option.
## Tips
- If you are unsure about any questions, please ask yourself how you would have liked the alumni association to engage with you when you were an alumnus/alumna.
- This form should not take more than 5 minutes to complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: This field is for you to enter your full name. Please provide your name as you would like it to be known in the alumni community.
* **Graduation year**: This field is for you to specify the year you graduated. If you do not remember your graduation year, please leave this field blank.
* **Current role**: This field is for you to describe your current occupation or job title. This will help us understand your current status in the workforce.
* **How often do you engage with alumni activities?**: This question has three options: Never, Occasionally, and Frequently. Please select the one that best describes your level of engagement.
* **Primary interest in the alumni community**: You can select one or more of the options: Events, Mentoring, Giving, and Networking. Please choose the options that align with your interests and activities.
* **Feedback and suggestions**: This field is for you to provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the alumni association or its activities.
* **Permission to contact**: If you wish to be contacted by the institution, please select the "True" option. This will allow us to follow up with you about future events or activities.
