# Group Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from users about a group's performance, management, and other aspects. This form is meant to be used for internal evaluations within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately according to your evaluation of the group.
2. Select the correct manager from the list provided in the "Group Manager" field.
3. Enter the group manager's email address in the "Group Manager Email" field.
4. Provide any comments or additional feedback in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the evaluation date and time in the "Evaluation Date" and "Evaluation Time" fields.
6. Enter your name and the date you submitted the evaluation in the "Submitted By" and "Submitted On" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Group Manager (`group_manager`, select_one, required)
* Select the correct manager from the list to associate with the group being evaluated.

### Group Name (`group_name`, text, not required)
* Enter the name of the group being evaluated.

### Group Manager Email (`group_manager_email`, email, not required)
* Enter the email address of the group manager.

### Comments (`comments`, text, not required)
* Provide any additional comments or feedback about the group.

### Evaluation Date (`evaluation_date`, date, not required)
* Enter the date you evaluated the group.

### Evaluation Time (`evaluation_time`, time, not required)
* Enter the time you evaluated the group.

### Submitted By (`submitted_by`, text, not required)
* Enter your name.

### Submitted On (`submitted_on`, date, not required)
* Enter the date you submitted this evaluation form.

Tips:
* Be honest and thorough in your evaluation.
* Only submit one evaluation form per group.
* Make sure to use accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
