# Alumni Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information about alumni, including contact details, academic records, employment status, and preferences for updates and events. Providing accurate and up-to-date information will help us keep in touch with you and provide relevant communication.

**Before filling out this form, please ensure you have not already completed it previously.**

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out all required fields marked as **required**.
* Choose your preferred method of communication for updates and events (Yes - Email, Yes - Postal Mail, or No - Thank you).
* Select your employment status (e.g., Employed Full-time, Unemployed - Seeking, etc.).
* Choose whether to include your information in the alumni directory.
* Answer any additional questions that may have been included at the time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your first name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name (id: 4)**: Enter your last name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Maiden Name (id: 5)**: If you have a maiden name, please enter it. This is useful for cross-referencing purposes.
* **Primary Email Address (id: 6)**: Enter your primary email address that you check frequently.
* **Secondary Email Address (id: 7)**: Enter an optional backup email address in case you cannot be reached through your primary email.
* **Mobile Phone Number (id: 8)**: Enter your mobile phone number for urgent matters.
* **Home Phone Number (id: 9)**: Enter your home phone number, if applicable.
* **Street Address (id: 10)**: Enter your street address, including street number and name.
* **City (id: 11)**: Enter your city or town of residence.
* **State/Province (id: 12)**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code (id: 13)**: Enter your postal code, without dashes or parentheses.
* **Country (id: 14)**: Select your country of residence from the provided list (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, etc.).
* **Graduation Year (id: 16)**: Enter the year you graduated from our institution.
* **Degree Type (id: 17)**: Select your highest degree earned from our institution (e.g., Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, etc.).
* **Primary Major (id: 18)**: Enter your primary major field of study.
* **Secondary Major/Minor (id: 19)**: Enter any additional majors or minors you pursued.
* **Student ID Number (id: 20)**: If you have a student ID number, please enter it.
* **Marital Status (id: 22)**: Select your current marital status (Single, Married, Domestic Partnership, etc.).
* **Spouse/Partner Name (id: 23)**: Enter your spouse/partner's name, if applicable.
* **Is Spouse/Partner Also an Alumnus (id: 24)**: Check if your spouse/partner is also an alumnus.
* **Number of Children (id: 25)**: Enter the number of children you have.
* **Current Employment Status (id: 27)**: Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed Full-time, Unemployed - Seeking, etc.).
* **Employer/Organization Name (id: 28)**: Enter your current or previous employer's name.
* **Job Title/Position (id: 29)**: Enter your job title or current position.
* **Industry/Field (id: 30)**: Enter your industry or field of work.
* **Work Email Address (id: 31)**: Enter your work email address, if applicable.
* **Work Phone Number (id: 32)**: Enter your work phone number, if applicable.
* **Would you like to receive updates and news (id: 34)**: Select your preferred method of communication (Yes - Email, Yes - Postal Mail, etc.).
* **Notify about upcoming events (id: 35)**: Select whether you want to be notified about events (True or False).
* **Include in Alumni Directory (id: 36)**: Check if you want to be included in the alumni directory.
* **Additional Information or Updates (id: 37)**: Share any other information you think we should know about you.
