# Chapter Annual Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Chapter Annual Report form is used to collect information about the activities and achievements of an alumni chapter. This report helps the chapter's national organization track and evaluate the chapter's progress and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submit a report for each year**. This form is designed to be filled out annually, so please ensure that you are reporting on the most recent year's activities.
2. **Fill out all required fields**. The fields marked as "required" must be completed to ensure that the report is accurate and complete.
3. **Double-check your information**. Please review your report carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Year**: This field indicates the year being reported on.
* **Report Date**: This field indicates the date when the report was submitted.
* **Chapter Name**: Enter the name of your alumni chapter.
* **Chapter Location**: Enter the city or region where your chapter is located.
* **Chapter President Name**: Enter the name of your chapter's current president.
* **President Email**: Enter the email address of your chapter's president.
* **President Phone**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of your chapter's president.
* **Number of Active Members**: Enter the number of active members in your chapter.
* **New Members Acquired**: (Optional) Enter the number of new members acquired this year.
* **Total Events Held**: Enter the total number of events held by your chapter this year.
* **Types of Events Held**: Select all the types of events that were held by your chapter this year. Options include:
	+ Social Gatherings
	+ Educational Programs
	+ Service Projects
	+ Volunteer Work
	+ Fundraising Events
	+ Mentoring Programs
* **Total Event Attendance**: (Optional) Enter the total number of attendees across all events.
* **Total Fundraising Amount**: (Optional) Enter the total amount of money raised by your chapter this year.
* **Fundraising Purpose**: (Optional) Enter the purpose for which the funds were raised.
* **Scholarship or Grant Awarded**: (Optional) Enter the amount awarded to a scholarship or grant recipient.
* **Total Volunteer Hours**: (Optional) Enter the total number of service hours contributed by chapter members.
* **Community Projects Supported**: (Optional) Enter a brief description of any community projects supported by your chapter this year.
* **Challenges Faced**: (Optional) Enter any challenges or obstacles that your chapter faced this year.
* **Goals for Next Year**: (Optional) Enter any goals or objectives that your chapter aims to achieve next year.
* **Additional Comments**: (Optional) Enter any other relevant information that you feel is important for the national organization to know.
* **This Report is Accurate and Complete**: Confirm that your report is accurate and complete by selecting "Yes, I Confirm".
