# Driver Knowledge Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Knowledge Questionnaire is a form designed to assess a driver's knowledge and understanding of essential traffic rules, road signs, safe driving practices, and driver's license details. This information is critical for driving safely and following regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the text fields provided below, one at a time. Each field is a question or a topic that requires a short text response. Follow these guidelines for each field:

* Be as detailed and concise as possible in your response.
* Ensure your answer is relevant to the question or topic.
* Do not exceed the character limit of each field (typically around 1-2 paragraphs).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Traffic Rules 
* **Traffic Rules** (`traffic_rules`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the traffic rules you know, such as right-of-way rules, speed limits, and traffic signal rules.
### 2. Road Signs 
* **Road Signs** (`road_signs`, `text`, required/optional): Explain the road signs you are familiar with, including their meanings and usage.
### 3. Safe Driving Practices 
* **Safe Driving Practices** (`safe_driving_practices`, `text`, required/optional): Share your safe driving habits, such as wearing a seatbelt, following distance, and avoiding distractions while driving.
### 4. Driver License 
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your driver's license details, including license type (e.g., state, national), license number, and expiration date, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
* If unsure or do not know, please indicate so in your response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
