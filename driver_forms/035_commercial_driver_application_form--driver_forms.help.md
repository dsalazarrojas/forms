# Commercial Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in becoming commercial drivers for our transportation company. It assesses your qualifications, experience, and background to ensure you're a suitable fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out all required fields**:
    - Enter your full name as it appears on your license.
    - Provide your primary contact email address.
    - Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
    - Enter your home address.
    - Enter your date of birth for age verification.
    - Complete the required fields for CDL license information.
    - Choose your CDL class and select all applicable endorsements.
    - Provide your CDL expiration date.
    - List your previous trucking employers.
    - Enter the number of accidents and violations you've had in the past 3 years.
    - Indicate whether you've had a DUI or license suspension in the past 10 years.
    - Specify your preferred route type and home time preference.
    - Choose your available start date.
    - List any professional references.
    - Provide additional information about your experience, qualifications, and certifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your license.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **CDL Number** (`cdl_number`, `text`, required): Enter your commercial driver's license number.
* **CDL State** (`cdl_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state that issued your CDL.
* **CDL Class** (`cdl_class`, `select_one`, required): Choose your CDL class (Class A, B, or C).
* **CDL Endorsements** (`endorsements`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable endorsements (Hazardous Materials, Tank Vehicle, etc.).
* **CDL Expiration Date** (`cdl_expiration`, `date`, required): Enter the date your CDL expires.
* **Accidents in Past 3 Years** (`accidents_past_3_years`, `number`, required): Enter the number of accidents you've had in the past 3 years.
* **Violations in Past 3 Years** (`violations_past_3_years`, `number`, required): Enter the number of traffic violations you've had in the past 3 years.
* **DUI Conviction** (`dui_conviction`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you've had a DUI in the past 10 years (True or False).
* **License Suspension** (`license_suspension`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you've had a license suspension in the past 10 years (True or False).
* **Preferred Route Type** (`preferred_route_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred route type (Local, Regional, OTR, or Dedicated).
* **Home Time Preference** (`home_time_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you prefer to be home (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or As Scheduled).
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date_availability`, `date`, required): Choose your available start date.
* **Professional Reference 1** (`reference_1_name`, `text`, required): List the name of your professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of your reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship with your reference.
* **Why Do You Want to Work Here** (`why_work_here`, `text`, required): Explain why you're interested in working for our company.
* **Additional Certifications** (`additional_certifications`, `text`, optional): List any additional certifications you have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself.

## Tips

* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your background and qualifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
