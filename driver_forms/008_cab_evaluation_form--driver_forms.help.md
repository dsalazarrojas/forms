# Cab Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cab Evaluation Form is designed to help drivers assess the condition of cabs after a thorough inspection. The form collects information about the cab's overall state, including its exterior and interior condition, safety equipment, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the required fields**: Please enter all required fields, including Vehicle ID, License Plate Number, Evaluation Date, Evaluator Name, Vehicle Make and Model, Current Mileage, Exterior Condition, Interior Condition, Tire Condition, Brake System, Engine Performance, Lights and Signals, AC and Heating, Safety Equipment, Taximeter Condition, and Vehicle Status After Evaluation.
2. **Choose the correct condition**: For fields like Exterior Condition, Interior Condition, Tire Condition, Brake System, Engine Performance, Lights and Signals, AC and Heating, and Safety Equipment, select the correct condition from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, etc.).
3. **List issues and repairs**: If any issues or repairs are required, please enter them in the Issues Found and Repairs Needed fields.
4. **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations in the Additional Comments field.
5. **Next evaluation date (optional)**: If applicable, enter the next evaluation date in the Next Evaluation Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle ID (Vehicle ID)**: Enter the taxi or cab identification number.
- **License Plate Number (License Plate Number)**: Enter the vehicle's registration plate number.
- **Evaluation Date (Evaluation Date)**: Enter the date of this inspection.
- **Evaluator Name (Evaluator Name)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
- **Vehicle Make and Model (Vehicle Make and Model)**: Enter the vehicle's year, make, and model.
- **Current Mileage (Current Mileage)**: Enter the odometer reading.
- **Exterior Condition (Exterior Condition)**: Select the overall state of the vehicle's exterior (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs repair).
- **Interior Condition (Interior Condition)**: Select the overall state of the vehicle's interior (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Needs cleaning).
- **Tire Condition (Tire Condition)**: Select the state of all tires, including the spare (Excellent, Good, Fair, Fair - monitor, Poor - replace soon, Critical - replace immediately).
- **Brake System (Brake System)**: Select the condition of the braking system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs service, Unsafe - do not operate).
- **Engine Performance (Engine Performance)**: Select the engine condition and performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs service, Critical).
- **Lights and Signals (Lights and Signals)**: Select the status of all exterior lights (All working, Minor issues, Multiple issues, Critical - needs immediate repair).
- **AC and Heating (AC and Heating)**: Select the status of the climate control system (Both working, AC only, Heat only, Neither working, Not applicable).
- **Safety Equipment (Safety Equipment)**: Select the status of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits (All present and valid, Present but expired, Missing items, Not inspected).
- **Taximeter Condition (Taximeter Condition)**: Select the condition of the taximeter (Working properly, Minor issues, Needs calibration, Not working).
- **Issues Found (Issues Found)**: List any problems discovered during the inspection.
- **Repairs Needed (Repairs Needed)**: Describe any required repairs.
- **Vehicle Status After Evaluation (Vehicle Status After Evaluation)**: Select whether the vehicle is approved for service (Approved for service, Approved with minor repairs, Restricted service, Out of service - requires repair).
- **Next Evaluation Date (Next Evaluation Date)**: Enter the date of the next inspection (if applicable).
- **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Provide any additional comments or observations.
