# Chauffeurs Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chauffeurs Survey is designed to collect feedback from chauffeurs on their experiences, job satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement. This form is used to gather information to better understand the chauffeur experience and make informed decisions to improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For questions that ask for a specific number or rating, please provide a number that reflects your level of agreement or satisfaction.
3. For text fields, please provide clear and concise answers that reflect your thoughts and opinions.
4. For questions with multiple options, select the option that best reflects your answer.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. Chauffeur Full Name
**Chauffeur Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
 
### 3. Years of Chauffeur Experience
**Years of Chauffeur Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been driving.
 
### 4. Current Employment Status
**Current Employment Status**: Select the status that best reflects your current employment situation.
 
### 6. Overall Job Satisfaction Rating
**Overall Job Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your level of satisfaction with your job on a scale from 1 (Not Satisfied) to 5 (Highly Satisfied).
 
### 7. Satisfaction with Vehicle Maintenance
**Satisfaction with Vehicle Maintenance**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the vehicle maintenance provided.
 
### 8. Compensation Satisfaction
**Compensation Satisfaction**: Indicate whether you feel your compensation is fair, somewhat fair, or not fair.
 
### 9. Satisfaction with Work-Life Balance
**Satisfaction with Work-Life Balance**: Rate your level of satisfaction with your work-life balance on a scale from 1 (Very Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
 
### 11. Quality of Communication with Dispatch
**Quality of Communication with Dispatch**: Rate the quality of communication with dispatch as Excellent, Above Average, Average, or Below Average.
 
### 13. Typical Client Professionalism Rating
**Typical Client Professionalism Rating**: Rate the typical professionalism of your clients as Very Professional, Professional, Sometimes Unprofessional, or Often Unprofessional.
 
### 15. Training or Development Desired
**Training or Development Desired**: If you could choose, what type of training or development would you like to receive?
 
### 17. Date of Submission
**Date of Submission**: Please enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
 
Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed to submit the form.
