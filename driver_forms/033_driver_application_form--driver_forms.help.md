# Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Application Form is used for users to submit their application for a driver's license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field in the format of `example@email.com`.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your driver's license number in the "Driver License Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`. This field is required.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license`, number, optional): Enter your driver's license number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your application is processed correctly.
* Use a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Double-check your phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.
