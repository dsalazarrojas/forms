# Taxicab Driver Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for taxicab driver permit application. It is used by transportation authorities to collect information about the applicant and their vehicle to ensure that it meets safety and regulatory standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your first name, last name, email, phone number, date of birth, and address.
2. Provide vehicle information: Select your vehicle type, enter your vehicle license number, make, model, year, color, registration number, insurance provider, and insurance number.
3. Enter vehicle characteristics: Enter the weight class (Light, Medium, or Heavy), length, width, height, number of seats, number of engines, number of prophets (likely a typo, assume it's "wheels"), and number of axles (likely a typo, assume it's "wheels").
4. Review and submit: Review the form for accuracy and submit it to the transportation authority.
