# Daily Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Form is designed to collect information on the condition of a vehicle's various parts and overall status. This form helps to ensure that vehicles are properly maintained and inspected on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Vehicle ID**: Enter the vehicle's ID number to identify the vehicle being inspected.
2. **Driver ID**: Enter the ID number of the driver who is completing the inspection.
3. **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the vehicle from the options provided (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
4. **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
5. **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
6. **Mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
7. **Engine Condition**: Choose the condition of the engine from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
8. **Transmission Condition**: Choose the condition of the transmission from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
9. **Brakes Condition**: Choose the condition of the brakes from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
10. **Suspension Condition**: Choose the condition of the suspension from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
11. **Steering Condition**: Choose the condition of the steering from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
12. **Tires Condition**: Choose the condition of the tires from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
13. **Lights Condition**: Choose the condition of the lights from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
14. **Battery Condition**: Choose the condition of the battery from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
15. **Body Condition**: Choose the condition of the body from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
16. **Glass Condition**: Choose the condition of the glass from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
17. **Seat Condition**: Choose the condition of the seat from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
18. **Accessories Condition**: Choose the condition of the accessories from the options provided (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
19. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
20. **Vehicle Status**: Choose the current status of the vehicle from the options provided (e.g., Available, Unavailable, Off Road).
21. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
22. **Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed the inspection.
23. **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the inspection.
24. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the vehicle.
25. **Vehicle Inspection Form Completed**: Choose whether the vehicle inspection form is completed or not.
