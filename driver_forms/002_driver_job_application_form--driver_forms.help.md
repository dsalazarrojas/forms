# Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Job Application Form is designed for drivers to submit their information and qualifications for a job with our company. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Data section with any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide information about yourself in the Driver Info section.
3. Enter your address in the Address section.
4. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number section.
5. Enter your email address in the Email section.
6. Enter your license number in the License Number section.
7. Select your vehicle type in the Vehicle Type section.
8. Enter the year your vehicle was made in the Vehicle Year section.
9. Enter your vehicle model in the Vehicle Model section.
10. Enter your vehicle number in the Vehicle Number section.
11. Select your insurance provider in the Insurance Provider section.
12. Enter the start date of your insurance policy in the Insurance Policy Start section.
13. Enter the end date of your insurance policy in the Insurance Policy End section.
14. Select the condition of your vehicle in the Vehicle Condition section.
15. Enter your driving experience in the Driver Experience section.
16. Enter the weight of your vehicle in the Vehicle Weight section.
17. Enter the height of your vehicle in the Vehicle Height section.
18. Enter the length of your vehicle in the Vehicle Length section.
19. Enter the width of your vehicle in the Vehicle Width section.
20. Enter the length of your vehicle in feet in the Vehicle Length in Feet section.
21. Enter the wheel base of your vehicle in the Vehicle Wheel Base section.
22. Enter the track of your vehicle in the Vehicle Track section.
23. Enter the overall length of your vehicle in the Vehicle Overall Length section.
24. Enter the gross weight of your vehicle in the Vehicle Gross Weight section.
25. Finally, select 'Save and Submit' to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (text, optional): A free-text field for any additional information about yourself.
* **Driver Info** (text, optional): A field to provide information about yourself.
* **Address** (text, optional): Your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): A valid contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email, required): A valid email address where you can be reached.
* **License Number** (number, required): A valid driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Type** (select_one, optional): The type of vehicle you drive (e.g., License, Non-Licence).
* **Vehicle Year** (number, required): The year your vehicle was made.
* **Vehicle Model** (text, required): The make and model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Number** (number, required): The vehicle identification number.
* **Insurance Provider** (select_multiple, optional): The insurance provider for your vehicle (e.g., Allstate, Geico, State Farm).
* **Insurance Policy Start** (date, required): The start date of your insurance policy.
* **Insurance Policy End** (date, required): The end date of your insurance policy.
* **Vehicle Condition** (select_one, optional): The condition of your vehicle (e.g., New, Used, Old).
* **Driver Experience** (time, required): Your driving experience.
* **Vehicle Weight** (number, required): The weight of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Height** (number, required): The height of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length** (number, required): The length of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Width** (number, required): The width of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length in Feet** (number, required): The length of your vehicle in feet.
* **Vehicle Wheel Base** (number, required): The wheel base of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Track** (number, required): The track of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Overall Length** (number, required): The overall length of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Gross Weight** (number, required): The gross weight of your vehicle.
* **Submit** (select_one, optional): Select 'Save and Submit' to submit your application or 'Cancel' to cancel your application.
