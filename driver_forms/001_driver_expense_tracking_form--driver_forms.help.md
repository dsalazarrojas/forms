# Driver Expense Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Expense Tracking Form is designed to collect detailed information about a driver's expenses related to their trips. This includes mileage, fuel costs, and other expenses for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the trip frequency from the options provided (e.g., Fuel Efficient, Less Than Once a Week, 2-3 Times a Week, etc.).
2. Choose the trip length from the options provided (e.g., 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-8 hours, etc.).
3. Enter any mileage, fuel costs, tolls costs, parking costs, food costs, and lodging costs associated with the trip.
4. Enter any other expenses not listed above.
5. Enter the trip dates (start and end date).
6. Enter the trip times (start and end time).
7. Enter the driver's name, manager's name, company name, and department name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Frequency** (`trip_frequency`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the frequency of the trip from the options provided.
* **Trip Length** (`trip_length`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose the duration of the trip from the options provided.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, number, optional):
	+ Enter any mileage associated with the trip.
* **Fuel Cost** (`fuel_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the cost of fuel for the trip.
* **Tolls Cost** (`tolls_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the cost of tolls for the trip.
* **Parking Cost** (`parking_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the cost of parking for the trip.
* **Food Cost** (`food_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the cost of food for the trip.
* **Lodging Cost** (`lodging_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the cost of lodging for the trip.
* **Other Cost** (`other_cost`, number, optional):
	+ Enter any other expenses not listed above.
* **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start and end dates of the trip.
* **Trip Times** (`trip_times`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the start and end times of the trip.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the driver.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the manager.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the company.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the department.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all relevant information to provide a complete and accurate report for your manager and accountant.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your manager or HR representative.
* This form is intended for personal use only, and all information will be kept confidential.
