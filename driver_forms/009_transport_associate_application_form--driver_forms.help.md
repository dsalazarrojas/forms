# Transport Associate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Associate Application Form is a crucial document used to collect information from transport associates applying for a position. It is designed to ensure accurate and comprehensive data collection, which aids in the efficient evaluation and processing of applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready. This includes your category, description, and other specific details.
2. Start by filling out the "Category" field, selecting the correct category from the provided options (Driver Forms or Other Forms).
3. Proceed to fill out the "Description" field with a brief summary of your application.
4. If applicable, fill out the "Can Open On Any Device" field with the relevant information.
5. If applicable, fill out the "Assigned Tool" field with the specific tool assigned to you.
6. Fill out the "Transport Associate Position" field with the position you are applying for.
7. If you have relevant experience, fill out the "Years of Experience" field with your relevant experience.
8. Fill out the "Expected Start Date" field with the date you expect to start work.
9. Fill out the "Expected Termination Date" field with the date you expect to terminate work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`transport_associate_application_form`, text, required/optional): This is a title for your application. Please fill it out accurately and concisely.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the correct category for your application. Options are "Driver Forms" and "Other Forms".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief summary of your application.
* **Can Open On Any Device** (`can_open_on_any_device`, text, required/optional): If you have access to multiple devices, please specify which one you can open the form on.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): If you are assigned a specific tool, please fill it out.
* **Transport Associate Position** (`transport_associate_position`, text, required/optional): Fill out the position you are applying for.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required/optional): If you have relevant experience, fill out the number of years.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date, required/optional): Fill out the date you expect to start work.
* **Expected Termination Date** (`expected_termination_date`, date, required/optional): Fill out the date you expect to terminate work.

Note: Fields marked as required/optional may or may not be required depending on the specific situation. Make sure to fill out all the necessary fields accurately to ensure efficient processing of your application.
