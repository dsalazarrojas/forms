# Transport Driver Safety Excellence Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Transport Driver Safety Excellence Award Nomination Form is used to submit nominations for drivers who have demonstrated outstanding safety performance. This form is intended for drivers, team members, or external partners. The purpose of this form is to formally recognize and reward individuals who have made significant contributions to safety within the transport industry.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out this form, please review the guidelines below to ensure you provide accurate and complete information. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's or team member's or partner's name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the driver's or team member's or partner's name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the email address of the driver's or team member's or partner's email in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the driver's or team member's or partner's phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the reason for nomination in the **Award Criterion** field. You can choose from:
	* Nominate driver for outstanding performance
	* Nominate driver for safe work environment
	* Nominate driver for safety initiative
6. Select the type of nominee in the **Nominee** field. You can choose from:
	* Select a driver
	* Select a team member
	* Select an external partner
7. Enter the name of the person nominating the driver in the **Nominator** field.
8. Enter the name of the company the nominee is affiliated with in the **Company** field.
9. Enter the year the award is being given in the **Award Year** field.
10. Select the type of award recipient in the **Award Recipient** field. You can choose from:
	* Select one of our drivers
	* Select a team
	* Select an external partner
11. Indicate the current review status of the nomination in the **Review Status** field. You can choose from:
	* In progress
	* Approved
	* Rejected
12. Enter any additional comments or notes about the nomination in the **Comments** field.
13. Indicate the submission status of the nomination in the **Submission Status** field. You can choose from:
	* Not submitted
	* Submitted
	* Approved

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form**: This is the main section where you can enter information about the nomination.
* **First Name**: Enter the driver's or team member's or partner's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the driver’s or team member’s or partner’s last name.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the driver’s or team member’s or partner’s email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the driver's or team member's or partner's phone number.
* **Safety Rating**: This field is not used in this form, but is left for future reference.
* **Award Criterion**: Select the reason for nominating the driver. This will help us to understand why you are nominating the driver.
* **Nominee**: Select the type of nominee (driver, team member, or external partner).
* **Nominator**: Enter the name of the person nominating the driver.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company the nominee is affiliated with.
* **Award Year**: Enter the year the award is being given.
* **Award Recipient**: Select the type of award recipient (driver, team, or external partner).
* **Review Status**: Indicate the current review status of the nomination (in progress, approved, or rejected).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the nomination.
* **Submission Status**: Indicate the submission status of the nomination (not submitted, submitted, or approved).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is only for nominating drivers or team members or external partners for the Transport Driver Safety Excellence Award.
* Please ensure that the information you enter is correct to avoid any issues with the submission.
