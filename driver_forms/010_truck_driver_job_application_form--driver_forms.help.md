# Truck Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Driver Job Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential truck drivers who are interested in working with our company. This form is designed to help us understand the candidate's qualifications and experience in the field of truck driving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form in its entirety. Each field is important and will be evaluated as part of the application process.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to enter your contact information correctly, including your name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name here. This is a required field, but it is optional.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name here. This is a required field, but it is optional.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address here. This is a required field, but it is optional. Make sure to enter a valid email address that you regularly check.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number here. This is a required field, but it is optional.

### License Number
* **License Number** (`license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your driver's license number here. This is a required field, but it is optional.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly, and double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact our support team.
* We appreciate your interest in this job position and look forward to reviewing your application!
