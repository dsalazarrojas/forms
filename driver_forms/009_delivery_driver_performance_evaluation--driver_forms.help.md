# Delivery Driver Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of a delivery driver, including their shift details, route information, and overall efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields based on your role and responsibilities within the organization. Review the fields carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Enter the required information, such as driver ID and shift hours, if applicable.
3. Select the correct answers for fields like "delivery_location" and "route_type" based on your knowledge or records.
4. Fill out the shift details, including start and end times, total miles, average speed, stops, breaks, and drops.
5. Provide comments or feedback for the driver, if necessary.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shift_details**: This field is a text box where you can enter any relevant details about the driver's shift, such as specific challenges or accomplishments.
* **delivery_shift_id**: Enter the ID number of the delivery shift, if required.
* **route_details**: Describe the route taken by the driver, including any notable events or issues.
* **delivery_location**: Select "Yes" if the delivery location was visited during the shift, "No" otherwise.
* **performance_rating**: Rate the driver's performance as "Good" or "Fair" based on your evaluation.
* **time_spent**: Enter the total hours spent on the shift, if required.
* **comments**: Provide any comments or feedback for the driver, if necessary.
* **supervisor_signature**: Sign and date to confirm submission and review.
* **driver_signature**: Sign and date to confirm receipt of feedback.
* **route_plan**: Enter any specific details about the route, such as changes or challenges.
* **route_id**: Enter the ID number of the route, if required.
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date of the delivery, if required.
* **shift_start_time**: Enter the start time of the shift, if required.
* **shift_end_time**: Enter the end time of the shift, if required.
* **total_miles**: Enter the total miles driven during the shift, if required.
* **average_speed**: Enter the average speed of the driver during the shift, if required.
* **total_stops**: Enter the total number of stops made during the shift, if required.
* **total_breaks**: Enter the total number of breaks taken during the shift, if required.
* **total_drops**: Enter the total number of drops (deliveries) made during the shift, if required.
* **route_type**: Select "Yes" if the route was a regular delivery route, "No" otherwise.
* **driver_id**: Enter the ID number of the driver, if required.
* **delivery_type**: Select "Yes" if the delivery was a regular delivery, "No" otherwise.
* **shift_hours**: Enter the total hours spent on the shift, if required.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the driver, if necessary.
* **form_submitted**: The date the form was submitted, automatically populated if applicable.

Please note that some fields may only be relevant based on specific roles or requirements within your organization, so review and fill out only the necessary fields to ensure accuracy.
