# Driver Evaluation Road Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Evaluation Road Test Form is used to assess a driver's skills and knowledge during a road test. Instructors use this form to provide feedback to drivers on their performance, while students can also provide their own comments and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's name in the "Driver Name" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Select the date of the evaluation in the "Date" field.
4. Choose the type of vehicle being evaluated in the "Vehicle Type" field.
5. Enter the student's score out of 10 in the "Student Score" field.
6. Enter the instructor's score out of 10 in the "Instructor Score" field.
7. Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
8. Select the evaluation status from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the date the evaluation was completed in the "Completed On" field.
10. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
11. Enter any comments from the instructor in the "Instructor Comments" field.
12. Enter any comments from the student in the "Student Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name**: Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student being evaluated.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle being evaluated (e.g. Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Bus).
* **Student Score**: Enter the student's score out of 10, with 10 being the highest score.
* **Instructor Score**: Enter the instructor's score out of 10, with 10 being the highest score.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the instructor or student.
* **Evaluation Status**: Select the status of the evaluation (e.g. Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Completed On**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Instructor Comments**: Enter any comments from the instructor.
* **Student Comments**: Enter any comments from the student.
