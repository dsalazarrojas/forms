To create the Driver Scoring Form - Help Guide, we'll start by explaining the purpose of this form and its relevance. This form is used for scoring drivers based on their performance in tests and assessments, and the goal is to evaluate their skills and incidents during the evaluation process.

To ensure that each driver is evaluated fairly and accurately, please review the form carefully before completing it.

The Driver Scoring Form has two pages: Driver Scoring Form Page 1 and Driver Test Incidents.

## Purpose
The Driver Scoring Form is used to score drivers based on their performance in tests and assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
*   Select the relevant options for each field based on your assessment of the driver's performance.
*   Provide detailed comments and ratings for each field as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Driver Scoring Form Page 1
*   **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of the driver's performance.
*   **Driver Test Incidents** (`driver_test_incidents`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all incidents that occurred during the test.
*   **Driver Skills Assessment** (`driver_skills_assessment`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the driver's skills from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
*   **Driver Comments** (`driver_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the driver.
*   **Driver Rating** (`driver_rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the driver's overall performance from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
*   **Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainee who was assessed.
*   **Trainee ID** (`trainee_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the trainee who was assessed.
*   **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the assessor.

## Tips
*   Make sure to select all relevant incidents that occurred during the test.
*   Provide specific and detailed comments for the driver's performance.
*   Be fair and unbiased when rating the driver's performance.
*   Enter accurate and correct information for the trainee's name and ID.
*   Enter your name as the assessor to ensure accountability. 

Please review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing it.
