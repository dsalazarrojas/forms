# Bus Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from external applicants for the bus driver position. Please fill in your application details and supporting documents to be considered for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
2. Enter a valid email address and phone number that we can use to contact you about your application.
3. Provide your driver's license number, including the issuing state or country.
4. Enter the total number of years of driving experience you have.
5. Indicate whether you are able to work nights.
6. Consent to undergo a background check.
7. Provide the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
8. If required, attach your resume as per the resume upload instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): 
  Please enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): 
  Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you about your application.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): 
  Provide a phone number we can use to reach you.
* **Driving license number** (`license_number`, `text`, required): 
  Enter your driver's license number, including the issuing state or country.
* **Years of driving experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): 
  Enter the total number of years you have been driving.
* **Able to work nights** (`can_work_nights`, `select_one`, required): 
  Indicate whether you are able to work nights.
* **Willing to undergo background check** (`willing_to_undergo_background_check`, `select_one`, required): 
  Consent to undergo a background check.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): 
  Provide the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Resume upload instructions** (`resume_upload_instructions`, `note`, optional): 
  Please attach your resume through the employer portal after submitting this form.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact HR.
