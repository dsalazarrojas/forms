# daily_driver_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "daily_driver_checklist" form is designed to collect information about vehicle registration, maintenance, driver information, condition, location, status, and other relevant details for tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "daily_driver_checklist" form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter the vehicle registration details in the "Vehicle Registration" field.
3. Provide maintenance information in the "Vehicle Maintenance" field.
4. Select the driver's role from the options (Driver, Rider, or Passenger) in the "Driver Information" field.
5. Evaluate the vehicle's condition and select from the options (Good, Fair, or Poor) in the "Vehicle Condition" field.
6. Enter or select the vehicle's location in the "Vehicle Location" field.
7. Choose the driver's status from the options (Active or Inactive) in the "Driver Status" field.
8. Select the vehicle's status from the options (Active or Inactive) in the "Vehicle Status" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter the driver's email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the driver's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration (text)**: Enter the vehicle's registration details (e.g., license plate number, make, and model).
* **Vehicle Maintenance (text)**: Provide any recent maintenance or issues with the vehicle.
* **Driver Information (select_one)**: Choose the driver's role from the options: Driver, Rider, or Passenger.
* **Vehicle Condition (select_multiple)**: Select the vehicle's condition from the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Vehicle Location (text)**: Enter the vehicle's current location.
* **Driver Status (select_multiple)**: Choose the driver's status from the options: Active or Inactive.
* **Vehicle Status (select_multiple)**: Select the vehicle's status from the options: Active or Inactive.
* **Notes (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the driver's email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the driver's phone number.

## Tips
