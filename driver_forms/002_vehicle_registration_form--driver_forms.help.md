# Vehicle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the correct vehicle type from the "vehicle_type" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's license number in the "license_number" field.
4. Select the correct vehicle model and make from the "vehicle_model" and "vehicle_make" fields.
5. Enter the vehicle's year of purchase in the "vehicle_year_purchased" field.
6. Select the registration status from the "vehicle_registration_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_owners** (`vehicle_owners`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct vehicle owner from the provided options.
* **license_number** (`license_number`, select_multiple, required: false): Enter your driver's license number. This is the number that appears on your driver's license.
* **vehicle_type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of vehicle you are registering (e.g., Car, Motorcycle, Truck).
* **vehicle_model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false): Enter the make and model of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota Camry).
* **vehicle_year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required: false): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_color** (`vehicle_color`, select_one, required: false): Select the color of the vehicle (e.g., Red, Blue, Green).
* **vehicle_condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the condition of the vehicle (e.g., Excellent, Fair, Good).
* **vehicle_registration_number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required: false): Enter the registration number of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_VIN** (`vehicle_VIN`, text, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_plate** (`vehicle_plate`, text, required: false): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_year_purchased** (`vehicle_year_purchased`, date, required: false): Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
* **vehicle_purchase_date** (`vehicle_purchase_date`, time, required: false): Enter the date and time the vehicle was purchased.
* **vehicle_license_expiration_date** (`vehicle_license_expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the expiration date of the vehicle's license.
* **vehicle_registration_status** (`vehicle_registration_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the registration status of the vehicle (e.g., Registered, Unregistered, Pending).
* **vehicle_registration_number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required: false): Enter the registration number of the vehicle.

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary documentation and supporting documents before completing the form.
