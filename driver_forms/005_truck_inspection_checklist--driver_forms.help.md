# Truck Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a thorough inspection of a truck and gather information about its condition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of fields below and answer each question based on the actual condition of the truck.
2. For fields with a "select_one" type, choose one of the options that best describes the condition of the truck.
3. For fields with a "text" type, enter a brief description of the condition of the truck.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields. If a field is not required, you can still complete it if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truck Inspection Checklist Form** (`truck_inspection_checklist_form`, text, not required): This is the title of the form.
* **Tires Condition** (`tires_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the tires. Choose one of the options:
	+ Braking System: The tires are in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Normal: The tires are in good condition but may need to be replaced soon.
	+ Abnormal: The tires are worn or damaged.
	+ Not Present: There are no tires on the truck.
* **Brakes Condition** (`brakes_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the brakes. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The brakes are in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The brakes are worn or damaged.
	+ Not Present: There are no brakes on the truck.
* **Lights Condition** (`lights_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the lights. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The lights are in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The lights are worn or damaged.
	+ Not Present: There are no lights on the truck.
* **Mirrors Condition** (`mirrors_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the mirrors. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The mirrors are in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The mirrors are worn or damaged.
	+ Not Present: There are no mirrors on the truck.
* **Fluids Condition** (`fluids_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the fluids. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The fluids are at the recommended level and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The fluids are low or leaking.
	+ Not Present: There are no fluids on the truck.
* **Fuel Condition** (`fuel_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the fuel. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The fuel is at the recommended level and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The fuel is low or leaking.
	+ Not Present: There is no fuel on the truck.
* **Mechanical Condition** (`mechanical_condition`, select_one, not required): Check the condition of the mechanical parts. Choose one of the options:
	+ Normal: The mechanical parts are in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Abnormal: The mechanical parts are worn or damaged.
	+ Not Present: There are no mechanical parts on the truck.
* **Other Condition** (`other_condition`, text, not required): If there are any other conditions that are not listed, describe them here.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, text, not required): If you have any other comments or notes, enter them here.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, email, not required): If this report is to be assigned to a specific user, enter their email address here.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields.
* If a field is not required, you can still complete it if you wish.
* Make sure to be thorough and honest in your answers.
* If you are unsure of the condition of a truck, consult with a mechanic or supervisor before completing the form.
