# school_bus_driver_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation of a school bus driver's performance. It is used by supervisors to assess the driver's skills, behavior, and overall effectiveness on the job.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the driver receives a fair and comprehensive evaluation.
2. For each section, select the most appropriate answer or provide a written response.
3. Be specific and provide examples or details when answering open-ended questions.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the driver to ensure that all necessary information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### School Bus Driver Evaluation

* **School Bus Driver Evaluation**: A brief introduction to the evaluation form.
*   **Driver Information**: Select one of the following ratings for the driver's overall performance:
	+ Highly Effective
	+ Effective
	+ Not Effective
*   **Supervision**: Select all applicable ratings for the driver's supervision:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
*   **Communication Skills**: A written response to the driver's communication skills.
*   **Vehicle Operation**: A written response to the driver's vehicle operation skills.
*   **Safety Procedures**: A written response to the driver's safety procedures.
*   **Driver Rating**: A numerical rating of the driver's overall performance.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions for the driver.
*   **Supervisor Info**: A written response to the supervisor's information.
*   **Date**: The date of the evaluation.

## Tips

*   Be honest and fair when completing the form.
*   Use specific examples and details when answering open-ended questions.
*   Keep the tone professional and constructive in your feedback.
