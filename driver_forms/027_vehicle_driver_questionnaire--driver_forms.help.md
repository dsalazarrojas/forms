# Vehicle Driver Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle and its driver's history for a specific use case.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the vehicle make from the list of available options.
2. Enter the vehicle year (if applicable).
3. Provide the vehicle model and color.
4. Enter the driver's license number.
5. Choose any applicable driver's history or violations.
6. Enter the date and time of any incidents involving the vehicle.
7. Provide any additional information about the vehicle's history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the make of the vehicle from the list of available options.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter the driver's license number.
* **Violations** (`violations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose any applicable driver's history or violations.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of any incidents involving the vehicle.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of any incidents involving the vehicle.
* **Driver History** (`driver_history`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the vehicle's history.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required: false): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Of Production** (`vehicle_year_of_production`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle's production (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check the accuracy of the data entered.
* Enter only relevant and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to authorized personnel for assistance.
