# Driver Safety Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Driver Safety Quiz" form is designed to assess your knowledge of road safety rules and regulations. It is a self-assessment tool that helps drivers identify areas where they may need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Be honest and accurate in your answers, as this form is intended to help you assess your knowledge, not penalize you.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for help.
