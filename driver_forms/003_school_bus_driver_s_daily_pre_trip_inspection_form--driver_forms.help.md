# School Bus Driver’s Daily Pre Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Bus Driver’s Daily Pre Trip Inspection Form is used to collect information about the vehicle's condition before a trip, to ensure it's safe and roadworthy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the pre-trip inspections to be performed (Bus, Tire, Mirrors, Brake, Horn, Lights, Wipers, Seatbelts).
2. Choose the condition of the vehicle (Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Enter the vehicle's mileage.
4. Enter the vehicle's year.
5. Enter the vehicle's make.
6. Enter the vehicle's model.
7. Enter the vehicle's color.
8. Record the date of the last maintenance performed on the vehicle.
9. Record the date of the last inspection performed on the vehicle.
10. Record the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
11. Enter the vehicle's weight.
12. Enter the vehicle's length.
13. Enter the vehicle's width.
14. Enter the vehicle's height.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre Trip Inspections** (`pre_trip_inspections`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the pre-trip inspections to be performed on the vehicle, such as Bus, Tire, Mirrors, Brake, Horn, Lights, Wipers, and Seatbelts.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the overall condition of the vehicle, which can be Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Vehicle Miles** (`vehicle_miles`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of miles on the vehicle's odometer.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required/optional): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the last maintenance performed on the vehicle.
* **Last Inspection** (`last_inspection`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the last inspection performed on the vehicle.
* **Next Scheduled Maintenance** (`next_scheduled_maintenance`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length** (`vehicle_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Width** (`vehicle_width`, number, required/optional): Enter the width of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Height** (`vehicle_height`, number, required/optional): Enter the height of the vehicle.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant pre-trip inspections for the vehicle.
* Enter the vehicle's condition accurately based on its condition.
* Enter accurate vehicle details such as mileage, year, make, model, color, weight, length, and width.
* Record the dates of last maintenance and last inspection accurately.
* Update the next scheduled maintenance date as needed.
