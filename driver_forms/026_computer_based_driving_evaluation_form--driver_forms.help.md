# Computer Based Driving Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Computer Based Driving Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about a participant's driving performance in various categories. The form is used by evaluators to record the results of a driving evaluation, including scores for reaction time, hazard perception, decision making, vehicle control, and traffic law compliance. The form also captures information about the participant's license number, license state, and license expiry date, as well as the type of evaluation and simulation module used.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the Evaluation ID and Evaluation Date.
2. Fill in the Evaluator Name, Evaluator Certification, and Evaluator Signature.
3. Enter the Participant Name and Participant ID.
4. Record the Participant's Date of Birth, License Number, License State, and License Expiry Date.
5. Select the Evaluation Type and Simulation Module.
6. Rate the participant's performance in each category (Reaction Time, Hazard Perception, Decision Making, Vehicle Control, and Traffic Law Compliance).
7. Enter the Total Score, Errors Committed, and Critical Errors (if any).
8. Select the Evaluation Result (Pass - No Restrictions, Pass - With Recommendations, Conditional Pass - Further Assessment Needed, Fail - Not Recommended, Incomplete - Retest Required).
9. Enter any recommendations for the participant and record any driving restrictions.
10. Determine if follow up evaluation is needed and select the required follow-up interval.
11. Record the participant's acknowledgment of the results and sign the form.

By following these steps, you will be able to complete the Computer Based Driving Evaluation Form accurately and effectively.
