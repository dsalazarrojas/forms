# Driver's Eye Test Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from drivers to assess their visual acuity. It consists of 10 fields, each focused on a specific aspect of a driver's vision, including license information, visual fields, comments, and signature verification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
4. Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - Driver Information

* **Driver Information** (`driver_form_page_1`, text, optional): Please provide your name and any other identifying information as requested.

### Page 2 - Driver License

* **License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **License State** (`driver_license_state`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a valid driver's license in the state where you reside.
* **License Expire Date** (`driver_license_expire_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your driver's license will expire.

### Page 3 - Eye Visual Fields

* **Eye Visual Field 1** (`driver_eye_visual_field_1`, number, optional): Rate your visual acuity in this field (1-10).
* **Eye Visual Field 2** (`driver_eye_visual_field_2`, number, optional): Rate your visual acuity in this field (1-10).
* **Eye Visual Field 3** (`driver_eye_visual_field_3`, number, optional): Rate your visual acuity in this field (1-10).

### Page 4 - Additional Comments

* **Comments** (`driver_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns about your vision, please include them here.

### Signature Verification

* **Signature** (`driver_form_signature`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have provided a signature on the form.
* **Signature Date** (`driver_signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering all questions.
* Use clear and legible handwriting when signing the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or administrators.
