# Driver Route Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Route Check In Form is designed to be a quick and easy way for drivers to report their status on a specific route. This form will help drivers to keep the fleet management system updated with their current location and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the Date of your check-in.
3. Provide the Route ID associated with your current location.
4. Enter the Location of your vehicle (e.g. City, State, Address).
5. Enter your Vehicle ID for identification.
6. Select your current Status from the options provided (Check In, Check Out, On Route, Off Route).
7. Add any additional Notes about your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name to associate your identity with your check-in.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name to associate your identity with your check-in.
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date of your check-in in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Route ID** (text, required): Enter the ID of the route you are currently on.
* **Location** (text, required): Enter the city, state, or address where you are currently located.
* **Vehicle ID** (text, required): Enter your vehicle's identification number.
* **Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status that best describes your current situation (Check In, Check Out, On Route, Off Route).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information or comments you'd like to provide about your current status.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the fleet management system has the correct data.
* If you are unsure about the correct Route ID or Vehicle ID, contact your supervisor or operations team for assistance.
* Use the Notes field to provide any relevant context or information that may help with your check-in.
* If you experience any issues or need help with the form, contact your administrator or operations team.
