# Box Truck Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential box truck drivers applying for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your Street Address in the "Street Address" field.
6. Select your Driver License Number in the "Driver License Number" field.
7. Choose your Driver License Class from the dropdown menu in the "License Class" field.
8. Enter your License Expiration Date in the "License Expiration Date" field.
9. Enter the number of Years of Experience Driving Box Trucks in the "Years of Experience Driving Box Trucks" field.
10. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your most recent trucking employer in the "Most Recent Trucking Employer" field.
11. If applicable, enter the name and phone number of your previous supervisor in the "Supervisor Name and Phone" field.
12. Answer the question about traffic violations in the past 3 years in the "Do you have any traffic violations in the past 3 years?" field.
13. Answer the question "Do you authorize a background check?" in the "Do you authorize a background check?" field.
14. Enter your availability date to start in the "Available Start Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address**: Enter your residential address.
* **Driver License Number**: Enter your driver's license number as issued by the state or government agency.
* **License Class**: Select the type of license you hold (Class A, Class B, Class C, or Standard/Other).
* **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your license expires.
* **Years of Experience Driving Box Trucks**: Enter the number of years you have been driving box trucks.
* **Most Recent Trucking Employer**: If you have worked for a trucking company recently, enter the name and contact information of that company.
* **Supervisor Name and Phone**: If you have had a supervisor in your most recent trucking employment, enter their name and phone number.
* **Do you have any traffic violations in the past 3 years?**: Answer this question with "False", "Yes - 1", "Yes - 2", or "Yes - 3 or more".
* **Do you authorize a background check?**: Answer this question with "True" or "False".
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
