# Driver Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Check In Form is a critical document for tracking and recording driver information for pickups and dropoffs. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure correct and complete records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct dates and times for pickup and dropoff.
2. Choose the correct driver from the options provided.
3. Enter the vehicle details accurately.
4. Provide any additional remarks or comments as necessary.
5. Select the correct vehicle status and type.
6. Optionally, enter any additional information such as email, phone number, address, city, state, zip code, country, vehicle number, vehicle plate number, and vehicle type comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup date** (`pickup_date`): Enter the date of the pickup in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Pickup time** (`pickup_time`): Enter the time of the pickup in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Dropoff date** (`dropoff_date`): Enter the date of the dropoff in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Dropoff time** (`dropoff_time`): Enter the time of the dropoff in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Driver** (`driver`): Select the correct driver from the provided options.
* **Vehicle make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Provide any additional remarks or comments about the driver or vehicle.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the driver or vehicle.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the driver or contact person.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the driver or contact person.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the driver or contact person.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of the driver or contact person.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state of the driver or contact person.
* **Zip code** (`zip_code`): Enter the zip code of the driver or contact person.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country of the driver or contact person.
* **Vehicle number** (`vehicle_number`): Enter the vehicle number (plate number).
* **Vehicle plate number** (`vehicle_plate_number`): Enter the vehicle plate number.
* **Vehicle type** (`vehicle_type`): Select the type of vehicle (Van, Car, or Truck).
* **Vehicle status** (`vehicle_status`): Select the status of the vehicle (Available or Not available).
* **Assigned user** (`assigned_user`): Select the user assigned to the vehicle.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about the driver or vehicle.

Note: Some fields are optional, so fill out only what is necessary for your specific situation.
