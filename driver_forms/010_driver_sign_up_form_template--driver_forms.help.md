# driver_sign_up_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver_sign_up_form_template is a form designed to collect information from drivers to facilitate the onboarding process for them to access the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as **required** in the form) with your accurate information.
2. Review each field to ensure you are providing the correct type of information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (1):
	* This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your documents.
- **Last Name** (2):
	* This field is used to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your documents.
- **Email** (3):
	* This field is used to collect your company email address. Please ensure that the email address is valid and not a personal email.
- **Phone** (4):
	* This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number without any special characters.
- **Date of Birth** (5):
	* This field is used to collect your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **License Number** (6):
	* This field is used to collect your driver license number. Please enter the number as it appears on your driver license.
- **Address** (7):
	* This field is used to collect your address. Please enter your address as it appears on your driver license.
- **Resume** (8):
	* This field is used to upload your resume. Please upload a valid resume file in the supported format.
- **Driving License Number** (9):
	* This field is used to collect your driving license number. Please enter the number as it appears on your driver license.
- **Vehicle Type** (10):
	* This field is used to select the type of vehicle you drive. Please select from the available options: Car, Truck, Tractor, or Motorcycle.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form's hints for guidance.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
