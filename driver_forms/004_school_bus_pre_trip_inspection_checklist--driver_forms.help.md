# school_bus_pre_trip_inspection_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a pre-trip inspection checklist for school buses. It helps drivers ensure that their vehicle is in good condition and ready for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the vehicle's details, such as its make, model, year, and any relevant documentation.
2. In the safety equipment section, check the functionality of seatbelts, airbags, and fire extinguishers.
3. In the vehicle condition section, inspect the vehicle for any signs of wear or damage.
4. In the driver conduct section, report your hours of service, fatigue levels, and any medical conditions that may affect your ability to drive.
5. In the vehicle pre-trips section, review the route, expected duration, and any potential hazards or concerns.
6. Familiarize yourself with emergency procedures, including evacuation routes and emergency exits.
7. In the vehicle equipment section, check the functionality of lights, sirens, and any other essential tools.
8. In the driver vehicle information section, provide your name, contact information, and any relevant certifications or training.
9. In the vehicle status section, report the current fuel level, temperature, and any other relevant indicators.
10. Finally, sign the driver signature section to confirm that you have completed the pre-trip inspection and that the vehicle is ready for the trip.
