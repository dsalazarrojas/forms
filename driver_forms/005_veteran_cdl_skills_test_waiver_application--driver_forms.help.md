# Veteran CDL Skills Test Waiver Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for veteran CDL skills test waivers. It collects information to process and evaluate your waiver request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD for date fields.
5. Ensure all email and phone fields are in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`): Please enter the date you're submitting this waiver application.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD
* **Veteran Name** (`veteran_name`): Please enter your full name as a veteran.
* **Military ID Number** (`military_id_number`): Enter your military ID number if available.
* **Military Unit** (`military_unit`): Enter the name of the military unit you served with.
* **Military Rank** (`military_rank`): Enter your military rank or title.
* **Date of Discharge** (`military_date_of_discharge`): Please enter the date you were discharged from the military.
* **Military Branch** (`military_branch`): Enter the branch of the military you served in.
* **Years of Service** (`military_years_of_service`): Enter the number of years you served in the military.
* **Military License Number** (`military_license_number`): Enter your military driver's license number if available.
* **Civilian License Number** (`civilian_license_number`): Enter your civilian driver's license number if available.
* **Driving License Class** (`driving_license_class`): Select the type of CDL you have or want to obtain.
* **Commercial Driver License** (`commercial_veteran_license`): Check if you have a commercial driver's license.
* **Reason for Waiver** (`waiver_reason`): Explain the reason for your waiver request.
* **Military Vehicle Operated** (`military_vehicle_operated`): Select all types of vehicles you drove during your military service.
* **Military Vehicle Class** (`military_vehicle_class`): Select the classes of military vehicles you drove during your service.
* **Driving Tests Taken** (`driving_tests_taken`): Enter the number of driving tests you've taken.
* **Driving Tests Passed** (`driving_tests_passed`): Enter the number of driving tests you've passed.
* **Waiver Approved** (`waiver_approved`): Check if this waiver has been approved.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about your waiver request.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`): Enter your title or position as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
