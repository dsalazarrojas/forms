# Van Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Van Maintenance Checklist form, which is designed to aid in maintaining your van's condition and ensuring its overall performance and safety. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure that all necessary areas are checked and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the page with the corresponding section of your van's systems you want to check.
2. For each page, carefully review and select the options that apply to your van's condition.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by a *.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Van Maintenance Checklist 1 (Note)
* Van Maintenance Checklist 1: This is an overview of the entire form. It serves as a general introduction and reminder to check all areas of your van's condition.

### 2. Oil Level (Number)
* Check Oil Level: This field is used to monitor the oil level in your van. Enter the level in units (e.g., "3/4").

### 3. Tire Tread Depth (Select One)
* Check Tire Tread Depth: Select the appropriate level of tread depth for your tires. Options include:
	+ 1/4: Indicates the tread is less than 1/4 inch deep.
	+ 1/8: Indicates the tread is between 1/4 and 1/8 inch deep.
	+ 1/16: Indicates the tread is between 1/8 and 1/16 inch deep.

### 4. Check Air Filter (Select Multiple)
* Check Air Filter: Select all applicable air filters that need replacement or maintenance.
	+ air filter 1
	+ air filter 2
	+ air filter 3

### 5. Battery Health (Select One)
* Check Battery Health: Select the condition of your van's battery.
	+ good: The battery is functioning properly.
	+ fair: The battery is not functioning optimally but still usable.
	+ poor: The battery is not functioning and needs replacement.

### 6. Brake System (Select Multiple)
* Check Brake System: Select all applicable brake components that need maintenance or replacement.
	+ brake pads
	+ brake rods
	+ brake lines

### 7. Suspension Systems (Select Multiple)
* Check Suspension Systems: Select all applicable suspension components that need maintenance or replacement.
	+ front bushings
	+ front struts
	+ rear shocks

### 8. Steering System (Select One)
* Check Steering System: Select the condition of your van's steering.
	+ good: The steering is functioning properly.
	+ loose: The steering is loose and wobbly.
	+ faulty: The steering is not functioning and needs repair.

### 9. Transmission System (Select Multiple)
* Check Transmission System: Select all applicable transmission components that need maintenance or replacement.
	+ transmission oil
	+ transmission filters
	+ transmission flywheel

### 10. Electrical System (Select One)
* Check Electrical System: Select the condition of your van's electrical system.
	+ good: The electrical system is functioning properly.
	+ faulty: The electrical system is not functioning and needs repair.
	+ not connected: The electrical system is not connected or functioning.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is best to err on the side of caution and select the most conservative option.
* This form is designed to be a comprehensive checklist for van maintenance, so make sure to complete all fields for accurate results.
