# School Bus Pre-trip Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by school bus drivers to report on the results of a pre-trip inspection, which is conducted before a school bus departs for a trip. The information collected helps the school administration and maintenance teams to identify potential safety issues and schedule necessary repairs or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Be sure to list any safety deficiencies or issues with the vehicle.
3. Describe any corrective actions planned or taken to address safety issues.
4. Provide the date and time of the pre-trip inspection.
5. Specify the location where the pre-trip inspection was conducted.
6. Enter the vehicle's type, mileage, year, make, and model.
7. List the safety equipment on board the vehicle.
8. Enter the vehicle's registration number.
9. Select the frequency of vehicle inspections.
10. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the pre-trip inspection, including the date and time.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Driver Comments** (`driver_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes from the driver, including any actions planned or taken.
* **Safety Deficiencies** (`safety_deficiencies`, text, optional): List any safety-related issues or defects found during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, optional): Describe any actions planned or taken to address safety deficiencies.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the pre-trip inspection.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the pre-trip inspection was conducted.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select, optional): Select the type of vehicle (e.g. School Bus, Minivan, Van).
* **Number of Students** (`number_of_students`, number, optional): Enter the number of students on board.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the driver.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the driver's license number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school.
* **Route** (`route`, text, optional): Enter the school route.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's mileage.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select multiple, optional): Select any safety equipment on board (e.g. Fire Extinguisher, First Aid Kit, etc.).
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Frequency** (`vehicle_inspection_frequency`, select, optional): Select the frequency of vehicle inspections (e.g. Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool or equipment.
