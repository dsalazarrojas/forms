# Uber Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's make, model, year, and other relevant details for inspection and assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to provide the vehicle's make, year, and model accurately.
3. Select the vehicle's status from the options provided.
4. Enter the mechanic's name and number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make, e.g., Toyota, Ford, or Honda.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's year, e.g., 2015, 2018, or 2020.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model, e.g., Corolla, Mustang, or Camry.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle type, e.g., sedan, SUV, or truck.
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's current odometer reading.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Year Model** (`vehicle_year_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's year model.
* **Vehicle Type Details** (`vehicle_type_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the vehicle type.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the vehicle's status from the options provided:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Mechanic Name** (`mechanic_name`, text, optional): Enter the mechanic's name (if applicable).
* **Mechanic Number** (`mechanic_number`, text, optional): Enter the mechanic's number (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
- If a field is not applicable, enter "N/A" or "Not Applicable."
- If you need help or clarification, contact your supervisor or the vehicle inspection team.
