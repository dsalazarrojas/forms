# Driver Safety Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Safety Declaration Form is a critical document that helps us ensure the safety and well-being of all drivers. This form collects personal and vehicle information to help us provide the best possible services and services to our drivers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Enter your Vehicle Registration information in the "Vehicle Registration" field.
6. Select your License Class from the dropdown menu in the "License Class" field.
7. Enter your Insurance information in the "Insurance Info" field.
8. Provide any relevant information about your Accident History in the "Accident History" field.
9. Enter your Driver License Number in the "Driver License Number" field.
10. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number with the country code (e.g., +1 for the US).
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's registration number or details.
* **License Class** (`license_class`, select_one, required): Select your license class from the dropdown menu.
	+ Class 1: Select this option if you are a Class 1 driver.
	+ Class 2: Select this option if you are a Class 2 driver.
	+ Class 3: Select this option if you are a Class 3 driver.
	+ Class 4: Select this option if you are a Class 4 driver.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information.
* **Accident History** (`accident_history`, note, optional): Provide any information about your past accidents or incidents.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
