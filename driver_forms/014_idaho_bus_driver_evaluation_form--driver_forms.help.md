# idaho_bus_driver_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The idaho_bus_driver_evaluation_form is designed to collect data on drivers, their performance, and any tools or equipment they use. It is intended to be used by supervisors or managers to evaluate drivers, track their progress, and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's name in the **driver_name** field.
2. Enter the bus number in the **bus_number** field.
3. Enter the date of service in the **date_of_service** field.
4. Enter the time of service in the **time_of_service** field.
5. Enter the driver's rating in the **driver_rating** field.
6. Enter any additional comments in the **comments** field.
7. Select the assigned tool from the select menu in the **assigned_tool** field.
8. (Optional) Enter a unique identifier for the form in the **form_id** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, Text): Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, Text): Enter the number of the bus the driver is associated with.
* **Date Of Service** (`date_of_service`, Date): Enter the date of service or the date the driver was last evaluated or assessed.
* **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`, Time): Enter the time of service or the time the driver was last evaluated or assessed.
* **Driver Rating** (`driver_rating`, Number): Enter the driver's rating, with numbers indicating their level of performance or competence.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the driver's performance.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One): Select the assigned tool or equipment from the available options.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, Text): (Optional) Enter a unique identifier for the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure accurate data collection.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
