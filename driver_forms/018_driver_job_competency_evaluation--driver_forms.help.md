# Driver Job Competency Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Job Competency Evaluation form is designed to assess a driver's performance and skills. This form helps to identify strengths and areas for improvement for a driver's job competency evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field. If you don't know the date, you can leave this field empty.
2.  Fill in the "Driver Name" field with the name of the driver being evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false):
    *   Please enter the date of the evaluation in the format "YYYY-MM-DD." If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty.
### 2. **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required: false):
    *   Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.

Note: As there were only two fields provided in the YAML file, I've assumed they are the only ones relevant to the form's purpose. If more fields are needed, please provide them, and I will update the help guide accordingly.
