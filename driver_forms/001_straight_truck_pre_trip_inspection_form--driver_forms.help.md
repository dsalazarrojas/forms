# straight_truck_pre_trip_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Straight Truck Pre-Trip Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's condition, status, safety equipment, insurance, and other essential details before starting a trip. This form helps ensure that the vehicle is ready for safe operation and minimizes the risk of accidents or equipment failures during transit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's year of manufacture
2. Enter the vehicle's make and model
3. Provide your driver's license number
4. Enter your full name
5. Specify your level of driving experience
6. Check the vehicle's status (ready or not ready)
7. Select all safety equipment that is currently installed on the vehicle
8. Confirm if the vehicle has insurance coverage
9. Describe any known defects or issues with the vehicle
10. Add any comments or remarks about the vehicle or your experience as a driver
11. Describe the vehicle's current condition
12. Add any additional notes or comments
13. Sign and date your signature (driver) and the vehicle's signature (if applicable)
14. Record the date and time of the inspection
15. Choose the inspection location (on the job, pre-trip, or post trip)
16. Enter the route number and description
17. Add any additional notes or comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of manufacture for the vehicle.
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., "Ford" or "General Motors".
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., "F-150" or "Chevy 3500".
* **driver_license**: Enter your driver's license number.
* **driver_name**: Enter your full name as a driver.
* **driver_experience**: Specify your level of driving experience, e.g., "less than 1 year", "1-5 years", "more than 5 years".
* **vehicle_status**: Check if the vehicle is "ready to go" or "not ready to go".
* **safety_equipment**: Select all safety equipment currently installed on the vehicle, e.g., "Safety chains", "Safety straps", "Safety nets".
* **vehicle_insurance**: Confirm if the vehicle has active insurance coverage.
* **vehicle_defects**: Describe any known defects or issues with the vehicle.
* **driver_comments**: Add any comments or remarks about the vehicle or your experience as a driver.
* **vehicle_comments**: Add any comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition.
* **vehicle_condition**: Describe the vehicle's current condition, e.g., "in good condition", "in poor condition".
* **vehicle_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
* **driver_signature**: Sign and date your signature as the driver.
* **vehicle_signature**: Sign and date the vehicle's signature (if applicable).
* **inspection_date**: Record the date of the inspection.
* **inspection_time**: Record the time of the inspection.
* **vehicle_location**: Choose the location of the inspection, e.g., "on the job", "pre-trip", "post trip".
* **route_number**: Enter the route number.
* **route_description**: Enter a brief description of the route.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **signature**: Sign and date your signature (if required).
