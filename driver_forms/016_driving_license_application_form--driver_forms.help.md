# Driving License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driving License Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for the purpose of issuing a driving license. This form will ask for demographic details such as name, contact information, and a unique identifier (email address or phone number).

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready. The form is split into separate sections, each with its own purpose:

### First Page:
1. **First Name**: Enter your given first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your given last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Second Page:
1. **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you check frequently. This will be used for communication and verification.
### Third Page:
1. **Phone**: Enter your valid phone number that you can be contacted on.
### Fourth Page:
1. **Address**: Enter your valid address for delivery purposes.
### Fifth Page:
1. **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: A required field to enter your given first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: A required field to enter your given last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: An optional field to enter your valid email address that you check frequently. This will be used for communication and verification.
* **Phone (text)**: An optional field to enter your valid phone number that you can be contacted on.
* **Address (text)**: An optional field to enter your valid address for delivery purposes.
* **Date Of Birth (date)**: A required field to enter your date of birth in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your application.
- Use the same email address or phone number for all submissions to avoid duplication checks.
- If you encounter any issues or need help, contact the support team.
