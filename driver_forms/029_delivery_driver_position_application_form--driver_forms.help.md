# Delivery Driver Position Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Driver Position Application Form is used by transportation companies and delivery services to collect information from delivery drivers applying for a position. This form helps to assess the driver's qualifications, experience, and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and professional information.
2. Provide details about your experience as a driver, including your availability for work and preferred working hours.
3. Enter your emergency contact information, including the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
4. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform the job, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`name_of_applicant`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Address of Residence** (`address_of_residence`, text, required): Enter your current address, including street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, required): Enter the number of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been driving.
* **Available For Work** (`available_for_work`, select_one, required): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, select_multiple, required): Select the hours you are available to work.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, note, required): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform the job, enter a brief description.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
* We appreciate your honesty and transparency in filling out this form.
