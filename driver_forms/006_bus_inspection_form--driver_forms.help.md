# Bus Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Inspection Form is designed to collect data on the condition and status of a vehicle (specifically, a bus) after an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Inspection Date** field with the date the inspection was performed.
2.  Next, enter the **Inspector Name** and **Inspector ID** fields with the relevant details.
3.  Move on to the **Vehicle Information** section, where you will find fields for **Bus Number**, **License Plate**, **Vehicle Year**, **Make and Model**, and **Odometer Reading**. These fields provide essential details about the vehicle.
4.  Proceed to the **Exterior Inspection** section and evaluate the **Body Condition**, **Paint and Graphics**, **Windows and Glass**, **Lights Operational**, **Tires Condition**, and **Safety Equipment Present** fields based on your inspection findings.
5.  Move on to the **Interior Inspection** section, which includes fields for **Seats and Upholstery** and **Flooring Condition**.
6.  Evaluate the **Engine Condition**, **Transmission**, **Brakes System**, and **Mechanical Systems** fields in the **Mechanical Systems** section.
7.  Finally, provide any additional **Issues Found and Notes** in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inspection Date** (Field ID: 2): `Inspection Date` is the date when the vehicle inspection was performed.
*   **Inspector Name** (Field ID: 3): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
*   **Inspector ID** (Field ID: 4): Enter the employee or certification ID of the person conducting the inspection.
*   **Bus Number** (Field ID: 6): Enter the fleet identification number of the vehicle.
*   **License Plate** (Field ID: 7): Enter the vehicle registration plate number.
*   **Vehicle Year** (Field ID: 8): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
*   **Make and Model** (Field ID: 9): Enter the manufacturer and model name of the vehicle.
*   **Odometer Reading** (Field ID: 10): Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
*   **Body Condition** (Field ID: 12): Evaluate the overall body condition of the vehicle as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Needs Repair**, or **Poor**.
*   **Paint and Graphics** (Field ID: 13): Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's paint and graphics as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Needs Touch-up**, or **Poor**.
*   **Windows and Glass** (Field ID: 14): Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's windows and glass as **Functional**, **Minor Issues**, **Major Issues**, or **Not Acceptable**.
*   **Lights Operational** (Field ID: 15): Evaluate the operation status of the vehicle's lights as **All Operational**, **Some Non-functional**, **Multiple Issues**, or **Not Acceptable**.
*   **Tires Condition** (Field ID: 16): Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's tires as **Good**, **Acceptable**, **Needs Replacement Soon**, or **Needs Immediate Replacement**.
*   **Seats and Upholstery** (Field ID: 18): Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's seats and upholstery as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Needs Repair**, or **Poor**.
*   **Flooring Condition** (Field ID: 19): Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's interior flooring as **Clean and Good**, **Acceptable**, **Needs Cleaning**, **Needs Repair**, or **Poor**.
*   **Safety Equipment Present** (Field ID: 20): Evaluate the presence and condition of the vehicle's safety equipment as **All Present and Functional**, **Present but Needs Inspection**, **Missing Items**, or **Not Acceptable**.
*   **Engine Condition** (Field ID: 22): Evaluate the overall condition of the vehicle's engine as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Needs Service**, or **Poor**.
*   **Transmission** (Field ID: 23): Evaluate the status of the vehicle's transmission as **Smooth**, **Acceptable**, **Needs Adjustment**, **Needs Repair**, or **Poor**.
*   **Brakes System** (Field ID: 24): Evaluate the condition and performance of the vehicle's brake system as **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Needs Service**, or **Needs Urgent Repair**.
*   **Mechanical Systems** (Field ID: 21): This field is a note section where you can add any additional comments or descriptions about the vehicle's mechanical systems.
*   **Issues Found and Notes** (Field ID: 25): In this field, provide any additional notes or observations about the vehicle's condition or any maintenance needs.
*   **Interior Inspection** (Field ID: 17): This field is a note section where you can add any comments or descriptions about the vehicle's interior condition.
*   **Vehicle Information** (Field ID: 5): This field is a note section where you can add any additional information about the vehicle.
*   **Exterior Inspection** (Field ID: 11): This field is a note section where you can add any comments or descriptions about the vehicle's exterior condition.
