# Driver Qualification DOT Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Qualification DOT Form is a crucial document used to collect essential information about a driver's qualifications, license, and vehicle details. This form helps ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and facilitates accurate driver evaluation. 

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2.  Select your license type (Class A, B, or C) from the provided options.
3.  Specify your years of driving experience and the type of driving license you possess (Commercial, Private, or Non-Commercial).
4.  Enter your vehicle details, including license number, type, make, model, and year of registration.
5.  Provide your vehicle's registration number and color.
6.  Select the expiration date of your vehicle license.
7.  Enter any additional information about your vehicle's type (if not listed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your driver's license number.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of license you possess (Class A, B, or C).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been driving.
* **Driving License** (`driving_license`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of license you have (Commercial, Private, or Non-Commercial).
* **Driver Experience** (`driver_experience`, text, required): Describe your driving experience and skills.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, text, required): Describe your medical status as a driver.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, text, required): Attach a copy of your medical certificate, if applicable.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's license number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_license`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's license type.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, text, required): Enter the year of your vehicle's registration.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle License Expiration Date** (`vehicle_license_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your vehicle's license.
* **Vehicle Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_vehicle_type`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's type (if not listed).
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, text, required): Enter the insurance provider for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider** (`vehicle_insurance_provider`, select_multiple, required): Select your vehicle's insurance provider (GEICO, State Farm, Progressive, or Travelers).
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider Other** (`vehicle_insurance_provider_other`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle insurance provider.
* **Vehicle Make Other** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle make.
* **Vehicle Model Other** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle model.
* **Vehicle Year Other** (`vehicle_year`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle year.
* **Vehicle Registration Number Other** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Color Other** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle color.
* **Vehicle Type Other** (`vehicle_vehicle_type`, text, optional): Enter any other vehicle type.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in.
- If unsure about any field, refer to your license or vehicle documents.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us for assistance.
