# online-taxi-booking-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This online taxi booking form is designed to gather necessary information from customers to book a taxi service that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address in the address field.
2. Select your preferred fare type (Free or Paid) in the select-fare field.
3. Choose the type of trip you want to book (One way or Round trip) in the choose-trip field.
4. Select your desired pickup time in the pick-up-time field.
5. Select your desired dropoff time in the dropoff-time field.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
8. Enter your name in the name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **address (address)**: Enter your current address, including your house number and any additional details that might help the taxi service provider reach you.
* **select-fare (select-fare)**: Choose between "Free" and "Paid" fares. If you want to pay for your trip, select "Paid". If you want to use a free fare, select "Free".
* **choose-trip (choose-trip)**: Select "One way" for a single trip or "Round trip" for multiple trips.
* **pick-up-time (pick-up-time)**: Select your desired pick-up time.
* **dropoff-time (dropoff-time)**: Select your desired dropoff time.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates on your booking status.
* **phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates on your booking status.
* **name (name)**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to by the taxi service provider.
