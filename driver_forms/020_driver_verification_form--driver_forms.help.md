# Driver Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Verification Form is designed to collect information about a driver for verification purposes. Please fill out this form to provide accurate details about the driver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the driver name field with the driver's full name.
2. Enter the driver's license number in the driver license field.
3. Type in the driver's phone number in the phone number field.
4. Enter the driver's email address in the email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **driver name** (text, optional): Enter the driver's full name as it appears on their license.
* **driver_license** (text, optional): Enter the driver's license number.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter the driver's phone number.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the driver's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields for a more thorough verification.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form.
* If you've already completed this form, you can skip it and proceed to the next step.
