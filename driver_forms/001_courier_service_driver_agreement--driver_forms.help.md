# Courier Service Driver Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Service Driver Agreement form is used to collect information from drivers and get their agreement to work with the company. It is a mandatory step in the onboarding process for new drivers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your driver information, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
3. Sign the Agreement Date and Driver Signature fields to confirm you have read and agree to the terms.
4. If you have a manager, have them sign the Manager Signature field to confirm they have approved your agreement.
5. Enter your company information, including name and address.
6. Enter your driver license number (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, text, required): Enter your driver name, phone number, and email address.
- **Agreement Information** (`agreement_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your agreement with the company.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms of service by typing your name.
- **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date you agree to the terms.
- **Driver Signature** (`driver_signature`, note, required): Sign your name to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms.
- **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): If you have a manager, have them sign to confirm they have approved your agreement.
- **Company** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
- **Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company address.
- **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a driver.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Driver License** (`driver_license`, text, required): Enter your driver license number (if required).

## Tips
- Make sure to read and agree to the terms carefully before signing.
- If you have a manager, have them sign to confirm they have approved your agreement.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
