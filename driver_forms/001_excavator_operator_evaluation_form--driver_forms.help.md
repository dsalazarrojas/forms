# excavator_operator_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an excavator operator, including their personal details and work experience. It is meant to be used for evaluation purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field.
3. Select your **Date of birth** in the format MM/DD/YY in the third field.
4. Enter your **Date of evaluation** in the format MM/DD/YY in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Work experience** in years in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1st field): Your personal name, as it appears on official documents.
	+ Type: Text input
	+ Required: No
* **Last name** (2nd field): Your surname or family name.
	+ Type: Text input
	+ Required: No
* **Date of birth** (3rd field): The date on which you were born, in the format MM/DD/YY.
	+ Type: Date input
	+ Required: No
* **Date of evaluation** (4th field): The date on which you were evaluated, in the format MM/DD/YY. This field is optional and only required for specific evaluation purposes.
	+ Type: Date input
	+ Required: No
* **Work experience** (5th field): The number of years you have worked as an excavator operator.
	+ Type: Number input
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter your name and date of birth correctly to ensure accurate identification.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the relevant authorities or personnel for clarification.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only and does not affect your actual work status or benefits.
