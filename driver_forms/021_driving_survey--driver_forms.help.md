# driving_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your driving habits and vehicle usage. Please fill it out to help us understand how you drive and what you do on the road.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section at your own pace, and don't worry if you don't know something. You can leave some fields blank if you're not sure or don't want to answer.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own questions or fields.
3. Take a few minutes to fill out the form, and let us know how much time you spend driving and when.
4. Use the options provided for select fields (e.g. driving habits, vehicle model, frequency), and feel free to select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driving Survey (Introduction)** (`note`, required: false): This is just a title to let you know what the form is about.
* **vehicle_info** (`text`, required: false): Describe your vehicle, including its make, model, and year.
* **driving_habits** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the times of day when you drive, such as:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Night
	+ Weekday
	+ Weekend
* **driving_habits_time** (`time`, required: false): If you selected "driving_habits" and want to specify the exact time you usually drive, you can fill out this field. Please use the format "HH:MM".
* **vehicle_year** (`number`, required: false): If you know the year of your vehicle, please enter it.
* **driving_hours** (`number`, required: false): If you know the average hours you drive per day, please enter it.
* **vehicle_mileage** (`number`, required: false): If you know the mileage of your vehicle, please enter it.
* **vehicle_model** (`select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if your vehicle is a model with a certain feature (e.g. automatic transmission), "No" otherwise.
* **driving_frequency** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the frequencies at which you drive, such as:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **driving_route** (`text`, required: false): Describe your usual driving route or path, if you feel comfortable sharing that information.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as you feel comfortable providing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your input will help us make the driving experience safer and more efficient for everyone.
