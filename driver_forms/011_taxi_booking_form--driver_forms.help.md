# Taxi Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a taxi service. It collects essential information to facilitate a smooth and efficient booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your pickup location.
2. Select the start and end times of your booking.
3. Specify the number of passengers.
4. Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
5. Choose a payment method (Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
6. If necessary, provide any special instructions for your booking.
7. Select the status of your booking (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Location** (Pickup Location, text, required/optional): Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required/optional): Choose the time you would like your taxi to start its journey.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required/optional): Select the time you would like your taxi ride to end.
* **Passenger Count** (Passenger Count, number, required/optional): Specify the number of passengers you will be traveling with.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): Enter your email address and phone number so that the taxi service can contact you.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your taxi service (Card, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, note, required/optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, such as picking up items or traveling with pets, provide details in this field.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the taxi service can contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the taxi service can contact you.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required/optional): This field is not currently relevant to the form's purpose.
* **Assigned Driver** (Assigned Driver, text, required/optional): This field is not currently relevant to the form's purpose.
* **Status** (Status, select multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your booking (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).
