# Van Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Safety Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to evaluate the van's safety status before and during a trip. It covers essential aspects of the van's condition, such as tires, brakes, lights, and emergency equipment. This form is intended to help the operator identify potential safety issues and take necessary steps to ensure the van is in a safe condition for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully and answer each question based on your best judgment or actual inspection results.
2.  For each question, select the corresponding option:
   *   "Yes" if the item is in good condition.
   *   "No" if the item is not in good condition or requires maintenance.
   *   "Unknown" if you are unsure or unable to determine.
3.  Provide a brief explanation or reason for your answer, if applicable.
4.  Complete the form for each van that you will be operating during the trip.

## Tips
*   Always complete the form before the trip, and review it carefully to ensure accuracy.
*   If you are unsure or unable to determine the status of an item, answer "Unknown" and document your reason for the answer.
*   Report any issues or concerns to the relevant authorities or maintenance personnel as soon as possible.
*   Regularly inspect and maintain the van to prevent safety issues and ensure a safe and enjoyable trip.
