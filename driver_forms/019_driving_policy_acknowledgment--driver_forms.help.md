# driving-policy-acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge and agree to a company's driving policies and procedures. It is intended for employees or third-party contractors who need to confirm their understanding of the policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Company Name.
2. Enter your Title.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Enter your Company Name (if different from above).
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Enter your Department.
7. Enter your Location.
8. Select a Date.
9. Select a Time.
10. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
11. Enter your phone number (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name as it is known to you.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name_2`, text, required: false): If you are representing a company other than your own, enter its name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position within the company you are representing.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter your department or team within the company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter your current work location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select a date to acknowledge the policy on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time to acknowledge the policy on.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your acknowledgment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if required).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Review the policy carefully before submitting the form.
* Keep in mind that this form is for official use and will be stored in your company's records.
