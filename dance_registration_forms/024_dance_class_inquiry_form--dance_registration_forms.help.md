# Dance Class Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Class Inquiry Form is a simple inquiry form to gather information about dance classes offered by a dance studio or institution. Fill out this form to get more details about a specific class or to express interest in joining one.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the dance class you are inquiring about in the **Class Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the instructor teaching the class in the **Instructor Name** field.
3. Select the date you are interested in taking the class on in the **Date** field.
4. Choose the time slot you prefer for the class in the **Time** field.
5. Select which days of the week you are available to attend the class in the **Days** field.
6. Indicate your skill level as a dancer by selecting one or more options in the **Level** field.
7. Provide any additional information or questions you have about the class in the **Inquiry Message** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field to allow us to contact you about the class.
9. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field to enable us to reach you directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the dance class you are inquiring about.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor teaching the class.
* **Date**: Select the date you are interested in taking the class on.
* **Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the class.
* **Days**: Select which days of the week you are available to attend the class (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday).
* **Level**: Indicate your skill level as a dancer (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Inquiry Message**: Provide any additional information or questions you have about the class.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the class.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to reach you directly.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the **Inquiry Message** field to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
