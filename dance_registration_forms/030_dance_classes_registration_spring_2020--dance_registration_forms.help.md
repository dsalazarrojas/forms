# Dance Classes Registration Spring 2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students for dance classes offered during Spring 2020. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email" fields with your correct information.
2. Select your preferred class type(s) from the "Class Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your registration date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM formats for these fields.
4. Confirm that you are registered for the classes you have selected in the "Registered Classes" field.
5. Add any additional comments or questions in the "Comments" field (optional).
6. Finally, review your entries for accuracy and click "Form Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address to be contacted about your registration and any updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more class types that interest you. Please choose from:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you wish to register for the class in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2020-03-16).
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Enter the time you wish to register for the class in the HH:MM format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Registered Classes** (`registered_classes`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you are registered for the classes you have selected.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your registration.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, Text, Required): Review and submit your registration form.
