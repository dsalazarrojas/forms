# dance_classes_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for dance classes. It collects information about their student details, contact information, payment method, and other relevant details for the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Details** (Field 1) with your name, student ID, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** (Field 2) to ensure we can reach out to you about your registration.
3. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Field 3) from the options provided.
4. Enter the **Registration Date** (Field 4) when you plan to start classes.
5. Select the **Registration Time** (Field 5) for your classes.
6. Choose your preferred **Dance Style** (Field 6) from the options.
7. Add any **Student Notes** (Field 7) that you think we should know about.
8. Select your **Payment Status** (Field 8) from the options.
9. Enter any additional **Class Details** (Field 9) that we need to know.
10. Provide **Instructor Details** (Field 10) about the instructor leading the class.
11. Add any **Course Details** (Field 11) that we need to know.
12. Enter the **Payment Terms** (Field 12) that apply to your registration.
13. If applicable, add any **Class Schedule** (Field 13) information.
14. If applicable, add any **Instructor Qualification** (Field 14) information.
15. If applicable, add any **Other Services** (Field 15) information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Student Details** (Student Details): Enter your name and any other relevant student information.
* **2. Contact Information** (Contact Information): Provide your name, phone number, and email address.
* **3. Payment Method** (Payment Method): Choose how you plan to pay for the classes (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **4. Registration Date** (Registration Date): Enter the date you plan to start classes.
* **5. Registration Time** (Registration Time): Choose the time of day you plan to start classes.
* **6. Dance Style** (Dance Style): Select the type of dance class you're interested in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **7. Student Notes** (Student Notes): Add any important notes or comments about your registration.
* **8. Payment Status** (Payment Status): Choose the status of your payment (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **9. Class Details** (Class Details): Enter any additional information about the class you're registering for.
* **10. Instructor Details** (Instructor Details): Provide information about the instructor leading the class.
* **11. Course Details** (Course Details): Enter any other details about the course.
* **12. Payment Terms** (Payment Terms): Enter any payment terms or conditions that apply to your registration.
* **13. Class Schedule** (Class Schedule): Enter any schedule information for the class (if applicable).
* **14. Instructor Qualification** (Instructor Qualification): Add any information about the instructor's qualification (if applicable).
* **15. Other Services** (Other Services): Add any other services you'd like to request (if applicable).
