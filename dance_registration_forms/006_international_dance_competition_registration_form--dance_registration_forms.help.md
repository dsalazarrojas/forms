# International Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and their emergency contacts for an international dance competition. The information will be used to register participants and assign them to suitable competitions based on their skills and age groups.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately. All required fields must be filled out for the submission to be accepted.
- Ensure that all fields relevant to the participant are filled out correctly and truthfully.
- If a participant has any dietary restrictions, allergies, or other medical conditions, it is recommended that this information is also provided in the emergency contact section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name, age, and contact information. This will be used as the main contact point for communication.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the adult who will be responsible for you during the event.
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, text, required: false): Enter your dance studio or training location.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact number of the adult who will be responsible for you during the event.
* **Dancer Info** (`dancer_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about yourself and your experience in the relevant style of dance.
* **Style Preferences** (`style_preferences`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all styles that you are proficient in.
* **Competition Preferences** (`competition_preferences`, select_multiple, required: true): Select which dance style(s) you will be competing in at the event.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to perform.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to perform.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, number, required: false): Enter your age group. If unsure, please contact your studio or the event organizers.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Dancer ID** (`dancer_id`, text, required: false): Enter your ID number or dancer ID (if applicable).
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, required: false): Enter your participant ID number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Double check that you have filled out all required fields correctly and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your dance studio or event organizers for clarification.
- Please submit this form by the designated deadline to ensure your participation in the competition.
