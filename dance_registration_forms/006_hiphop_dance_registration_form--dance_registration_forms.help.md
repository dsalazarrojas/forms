# Hiphop Dance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiphop Dance Registration Form is designed to collect registration information for a Hiphop dance event or competition. It is intended to be used by crew teams to provide their member and manager details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Member Name" field with the name of the member participating in the event.
2. In the "Crew Members" field, select all the members who are part of the crew (up to 10 members).
3. Fill in the "Manager Name" field with the name of the team's manager.
4. In the "Manager Email" field, enter the email address of the team's manager.
5. In the "Manager Phone" field, enter the phone number of the team's manager.
6. If necessary, add any additional information or agreement terms in the "Agreement" field.
7. Confirm the crew's agreement with the event terms and conditions by selecting "True" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Enter the name of the member participating in the event.
* **Crew Members**: Select all the members who are part of the crew (up to 10 members).
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the team's manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the team's manager.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team's manager.
* **Agreement**: Add any additional information or agreement terms if necessary.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm the crew's agreement with the event terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Click to complete and submit the form.
