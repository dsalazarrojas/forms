# Dance Association Annual Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Association Annual Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect general information from teams for the annual dance association tournament entry. This form is used to register teams for the tournament and gather relevant information about the team and its participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your team's general information, including any relevant details about your team.
2.  Enter your team's contact information, including the team's name and contact person's details.
3.  Indicate if any team participants are not part of the team but would like to be considered for registration.
4.  Enter participant information for all team participants.
5.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter any general information about your team that you would like to share with the tournament organizers. This can include the team's name, description, or any other relevant details.
* **Team Info** (`team_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your team's name and any other relevant information. This can include the team's logo, colors, or motto.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your team's contact person's name, phone number, and email. The contact person will be the primary point of contact for the team.
* **Team Participants** (`team_participants`, select_multiple, required: false): If any team participants are not part of the team but would like to be considered for registration, select "Yes" next to their name. This will allow us to include them in the team's registration.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter information about each team participant, including their name and any other relevant details. Each participant should have their own entry in this field.
