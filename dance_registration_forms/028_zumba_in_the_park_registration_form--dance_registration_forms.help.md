# Zumba In The Park Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from users interested in the Zumba In The Park program. The form is for informational purposes and does not require mandatory submissions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the fields as per your preference and requirements. Each field has a label that describes its purpose, and you can fill in as much or as little information as you'd like. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please fill in your first name as you prefer to be known. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Fill in your last name as you prefer to be known. 
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address, if you'd like to share it. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, fill in a valid email address. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to share. 
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, date, optional): Choose a class date that you're available for, if you'd like. 
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, optional): If you'd like to attend a specific time, choose it here. 
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you're affiliated with an organization, feel free to mention it. 
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field might be used internally for tracking purposes, so it's optional. 
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category that best describes your interest: Dance, Music, Sports, or Theater. 
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): This field might be used for outputting results, but it's not required. 

## Tips
- You can fill in as much or as little information as you'd like, depending on your comfort level. 
- If a field is marked as "required", it means that information is necessary for further processing or communication. 
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask an administrator for clarification. 
- This form is a placeholder for demonstration purposes only, so your information will not be used for actual processing.
