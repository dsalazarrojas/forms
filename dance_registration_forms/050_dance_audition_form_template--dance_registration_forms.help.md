# Dance Audition Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Audition Form Template is designed to gather essential information from dancers interested in auditioning for various dance performances, rehearsals, or events. It helps event organizers and dance instructors understand the dancer's background, experience, and availability, making it easier to schedule auditions and assign roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Begin by filling in the "Dancer Info" section, providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. Proceed to the "Contact Info" section, where you can add emergency contact information in case we need to reach someone else about your audition.
4. In the "Dance Experience" section, select your dance experience level: "True" if you have prior experience, "False" if you don't, or "Prefer not to answer" if you don't want to disclose your experience.
5. The "Dance Background" section is an open field where you can provide any relevant background information about your dance experience or training.
6. In the "Emergency Contact Info" section, provide the name, phone number, and any other essential contact details for your emergency contact person.
7. In the "Emergency Name" section, provide the name of the emergency contact person.
8. The "Emergency Phone" section is for the phone number of your emergency contact person.
9. In the "Dance Audition Experience" section, provide any relevant experience you have with dance auditions or performances.
10. For "Available Dance Days", list all the days you are available to audition, using a comma-separated format (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
11. In the "Preferred Dance Days" section, select all the days you prefer to audition.
12. In the "Available Time Slots" section, list all the time slots you are available to audition, using a comma-separated format (e.g., 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM).
13. For "Preferred Time Slots", select all the time slots you prefer to audition.
14. In the "Available Dance Styles" section, list all the dance styles you are interested in or proficient in, using a comma-separated format (e.g., Ballet, Tap, Jazz).
15. For "Preferred Dance Styles", select all the dance styles you prefer to audition for.
16. The "Audition Date" and "Audition Time" fields will be provided by the event organizers and are not required to be filled by the dancer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dancer Info (id: 1, type: text, required: false)**: This is the section where you can provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a dancer.

* **Dance Background (id: 4, type: text, required: false)**: This is a field where you can provide any relevant background information about your dance experience or training.

* **Dance Audition Experience (id: 8, type: text, required: false)**: This section is for providing any relevant experience you have with dance auditions or performances.
* **Available Dance Days (id: 9, type: text, required: false)**: List all days you are available to audition using a comma-separated format (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
* **Preferred Dance Days (id: 10, type: select_multiple, required: true)**: Select all the days you prefer to audition using the given options.
* **Available Time Slots (id: 11, type: text, required: false)**: List all time slots you are available to audition using a comma-separated format (e.g., 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM).
