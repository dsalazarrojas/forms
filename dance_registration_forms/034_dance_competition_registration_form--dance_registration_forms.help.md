**Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Registration Form is designed to collect information from dancers and dance groups for a dance competition. This form will help us to gather performance details, contact information, and other relevant details for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the competition details section, including the dance style and competition type.
2. Provide your dancer's details, including name, age group, and performance name.
3. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and name.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments about your performance.
5. Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
