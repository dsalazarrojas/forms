# Dance Class Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Class Participation Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from parents and students to ensure a smooth and secure dance class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the required fields carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Review the terms and conditions before proceeding to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent Information** (Page 1, required): Provide your contact information and any relevant details about yourself.
  * Please fill out your name and address.
  * Ensure your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
  * If you're filling on behalf of a student, please ensure you have permission to do so.

- **Emergency Contact** (Page 1, optional): If you're filling on behalf of a student, provide the contact information of an emergency contact.
  * Please fill out the contact person's name, address, and phone number.

- **Medical Conditions** (Page 1, optional): If your student has any medical conditions, please describe them here.
  * Please provide any relevant medical information that may impact their dance class experience.

- **Consent** (Page 2, required): Select the options that apply to you and your student's dance class experience.
  * Please choose the options that best represent your understanding of the class rules and expectations.

- **Class Information** (Page 2, optional): Provide any additional information about your student's class schedule and preferences.
  * Please note any special needs or requirements your student may have.

- **Class Schedule** (Page 2, required): Select the class schedule that best suits your student's needs.
  * Please choose the schedule that aligns with your student's availability and your own.

- **Payment Information** (Page 3, optional): If you're paying for the class, please provide payment information.
  * Please fill out your payment method, amount, and payment date.

- **Terms and Conditions** (Page 3, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the dance class.
  * Please carefully review the terms before proceeding to submit your form.

- **Payment Gateway** (Page 3, required): Select the payment method you'd like to use.
  * Please choose the payment option that best suits your needs.

- **Notes** (Page 3, optional): If you have any further comments or questions, please note them here.
  * Please use this section to provide any additional information you'd like to share.

- **Payment Method 2** (Page 3, optional): If you have multiple payment methods, please select the one you'd like to use.
  * Please choose the payment method that best suits your needs.

- **Payment Status** (Page 3, optional): If you have any payment status updates, please note them here.
  * Please keep us informed about any payment status changes.

- **Payment Amount** (Page 3, required): Please fill out the payment amount for your student's dance class fees.
  * Please ensure the amount is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Payment Date** (Page 3, required): Please select the payment date for your student's dance class fees.
  * Please choose the date you'd like to make the payment.

- **Payment Time** (Page 3, required): Please select the payment time for your student's dance class fees.
  * Please choose the time you'd like to make the payment.

- **Parent Phone** (Page 4, required): Please provide your phone number for any urgent communication.
  * Please ensure your phone number is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Class Type** (Page 4, optional): If you'd like your student to participate in a specific class type, please select it here.
  * Please choose the class type that best suits your student's needs.

- **Class Level** (Page 4, optional): If you'd like your student to participate in a specific class level, please select it here.
  * Please choose the class level that best suits your student's needs.

- **Student ID** (Page 4, required): Please provide your student's unique ID number.
  * Please ensure the ID number is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Student Name** (Page 4, required): Please fill out your student's name.
  * Please ensure the name is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Student DOB** (Page 4, required): Please provide your student's date of birth.
  * Please ensure the DOB is accurate and up-to-date.

- **Payment Method 2** (Page 5, optional): If you have multiple payment methods, please select the one you'd like to use.
  * Please choose the payment method that best suits your needs.

- **Terms** (Page 5, optional): If you have any comments or questions, please note them here.
  * Please use this section to provide any additional information you'd like to share.
