# Dance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from dance registrants to process their registration for a dance event or class. This form is used to collect contact, dance category, and other relevant details from the registrants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the "Registrant Info" page with your name and email address.
2. In the "Dance Category" page, select the type of dance category you are interested in (e.g., Ballet, Ballroom, Contemporary, etc.).
3. Fill in your first and last name in the respective fields.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Add any additional comments or remarks in the "Comments" field.
6. In the "Course Academy" page, select the type of course or academy you are interested in (e.g., Course, Academy, Contest, Festival).
7. Enter the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
8. Provide any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
9. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code in the respective fields.
10. Select your country of residence.
11. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
12. Provide the course level, instructor, course duration, class time, class days, and class hours you are interested in.
13. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Info**: This page is used to collect your name and email address.
	+ **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Dance Category**: Select the type of dance category you are interested in.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Course Academy**: Select the type of course or academy you are interested in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event or class.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event or class.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your registration.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Course Level**: Enter the level of the course you are interested in.
* **Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Course Duration**: Enter the duration of the course.
* **Class Time**: Enter the time of the class.
* **Class Days**: Enter the days of the class.
* **Class Hours**: Enter the hours of the class.
* **Class Frequency**: Enter the frequency of the class.
* **Submission Date**: This field is not applicable as it is a readonly field.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct dance category and course academy.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please add them in the "Comments" field.
* You can contact us if you have any questions or concerns about the form or the registration process.
