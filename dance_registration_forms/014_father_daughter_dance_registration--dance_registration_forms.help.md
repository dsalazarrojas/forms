# Father Daughter Dance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from fathers and daughters to register them for the Father Daughter Dance. The purpose of this form is to ensure that we have accurate and complete information about each attendee, including their contact details and any special considerations for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Enter your Father's name and any additional details about him.
3. Enter your daughter's name and age.
4. Provide your father's work information, if applicable.
5. Enter emergency contact information.
6. Add any comments or special requests, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Father Name** (`father_name`, text, required): Enter your father's full name.
* **Father Email** (`father_email`, email, optional): Enter your father's email address.
* **Father Phone** (`father_phone`, text, optional): Enter your father's phone number.
* **Daughter Name** (`daughter_name`, text, required): Enter your daughter's full name.
* **Daughter Age** (`daughter_age`, number, required): Enter your daughter's age.
* **Father Works At** (`father_work`, text, optional): If applicable, enter where your father works.
* **Father Work Phone** (`father_work_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your father's work phone number.
* **Father Work Email** (`father_work_email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter your father's work email address.
* **Father Works** (`father_work`, text, optional): If applicable, enter what your father does for work.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please complete the form carefully, as it will help us ensure a wonderful experience for all attendees.
