# Studio A Dance Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Studio A Dance Center Registration Form is designed to collect information from students or registrants who wish to join dance classes at the center. This form helps the center to keep track of the student's contact information, class preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Fill out the required fields as indicated.
2. Select the class type (Ballet, Jazz, Tap, or Modern) that you prefer.
3. Choose the frequency of classes (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Select the day of the week when you'd like to attend classes.
5. Enter your preferred start and end dates for the class.
6. You can add any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
7. If you'd like, you can also provide the name of the person submitting the form.
8. Enter a submitted date and time for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your name.
* **Last Name**: Your surname or last name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Student ID**: A unique student ID provided by the center or a student number.
* **Class Type**: Select one class type from the options: Ballet, Jazz, Tap, or Modern.
* **Class Frequency**: Select one or more frequencies from the options: Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Class Day**: Select one day of the week for the class (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
* **Start Date**: Enter your preferred start date for the class in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date**: Enter your preferred end date for the class in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the class or your preferences.
* **Submitted By**: The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: The date and time of submission.
* **Assigned Tool**: A text field for additional information.
* **Output File**: A text field for additional information.
* **Category**: A text field for categorization purposes.

Please note that the form includes fields for submission and record-keeping purposes. Fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further assistance.
