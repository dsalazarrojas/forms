# dance_performance_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from dancers for the efficient check-out process after a performance. It is required for all dancers to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. (Optional) Enter any special requests or notes in the Special Requests field.
6. Enter the Date of Check Out.
7. Enter the Time of Check Out.
8. (Optional) Enter any Special Notes for the check-out process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name is required to complete this form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name is required to complete this form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): This field is optional, but if you have any special requests, please let us know.
* **Date of Check Out** (`date_of_check_out`, `date`, required): Enter the date you will be checking out.
* **Time of Check Out** (`time_of_check_out`, `time`, required): Enter the time you will be checking out.
* **Special Notes** (`special_notes`, `note`, optional): This field is optional, and any notes you provide will be reviewed by the management.
