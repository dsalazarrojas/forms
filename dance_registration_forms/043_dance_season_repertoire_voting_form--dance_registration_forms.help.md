# Dance Season Repertoire Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Season Repertoire Voting Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on the repertoire for a dance season. This form allows users to express their preferences and suggestions for the upcoming season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the repertoire you would like to vote on.
2. Enter the studio associated with the repertoire.
3. Select the date of the performance you would like to vote on.
4. Select the time of the performance you would like to vote on.
5. Indicate whether or not you would like to perform the repertoire.
6. Enter your vote for the repertoire (Yes or No).
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the repertoire.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Repertoire Title** (`repertoire_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the repertoire you would like to vote on.
* **Studio** (`studio`, text, optional): Enter the studio associated with the repertoire.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the performance you would like to vote on.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the performance you would like to vote on.
* **Repertoire** (`repertoire`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you would like to perform the repertoire (Yes or No).
* **Director** (`director`, text, optional): Enter the director associated with the repertoire.
* **Vote** (`vote`, select_one, optional): Enter your vote for the repertoire (Yes or No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the repertoire.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much or as little information as you like, as all fields are optional.
* You can use this form to vote on multiple repertoires, just fill out each field for each repertoire.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the notes field to provide them.
