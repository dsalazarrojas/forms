# dance_festival_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dance festival entry form is a collection of fields meant to gather information from students, participants, and emergency contacts for the dance festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the user information fields, including name, email, and phone number.
* Select the dance school and dance style that you are associated with.
* Provide contact information for a parent or guardian, including name, phone number, and email.
* Select the relationship between the student/participant and the emergency contact.
* If applicable, provide medical information and emergency contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (1, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Personal Info** (2, text, required: false): Provide any additional personal information that you feel is relevant.
* **Contact Info** (3, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the person filling out the form.
* **Parent Info** (4, text, required: false): Enter the name, phone number, and email of the parent or guardian.
* **Dance School** (5, select_one, required: false): Select the dance school that you are associated with.
* **Name** (6, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (7, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (8, number, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (9, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **School Year** (10, number, required: false): Enter your school year.
* **Dance Style** (11, select_multiple, required: false): Select the dance styles you are interested in or associated with (e.g. Hip Hop, Tap, Ballet, etc.).
* **Parent Name** (12, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (13, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (14, email, required: false): Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Name** (15, text, required: false): Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (16, number, required: false): Enter the phone number of the guardian.
* **Guardian Email** (17, email, required: false): Enter the email of the guardian.
* **Emergency Info** (18, note, required: false): Provide any relevant medical or emergency information.
* **Emergency Name** (19, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (20, email, required: false): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (21, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (22, select_multiple, required: false): Select the relationship between the student/participant and the emergency contact (e.g. Father, Mother, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Phone 2** (23, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact.
* **Medical Info** (24, note, required: false): Provide any relevant medical information.
* **Medical Info 2** (25, note, required: false): Provide any additional medical information.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or support.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
