# Dance Class Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students and parents/guardians for the dance class program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "Required".
2. Enter your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
3. Select your preferred language of communication and dance style.
4. Choose your dance level and preferred day of availability.
5. Provide any relevant medical history.
6. Confirm consent from parents/guardians for minors.
7. Enter any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (6, text): If the student's emergency contact information is required, enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (7, text): If the student's emergency contact information is required, enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (8, select_one): Choose the relationship between the student and the emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, Spouse, Grandparent, Sister, Brother, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (9, text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Preferred Language** (10, select_one): Select the language in which you would like to be contacted (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
* **Dance Style** (11, select_one): Choose the dance style you are interested in (Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, Contemporary).
* **Dance Level** (12, select_one): Choose your dance level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Pre-Professional).
* **Dance Experience** (13, text): If applicable, describe your dance experience.
* **Availability** (14, select_multiple): Choose the days you are available for the dance class (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Note** (15, text): Enter any additional notes about your preferences or requirements.
* **Student Notes** (16, text): Enter any relevant student notes.
* **Guardian Consent** (17, select_one): Confirm that you are a guardian (True) or not (False).
* **Parent Consent** (18, select_one): Confirm that you are a parent (True) or not (False).
* **Student Medical History** (19, text): If applicable, describe any relevant medical history.

Note: This form requires consent from both parents/guardians and students. Ensure that all required fields are filled out for proper processing.
