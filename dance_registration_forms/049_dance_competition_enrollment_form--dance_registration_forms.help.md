# Dance Competition Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Competition Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from participants to enroll in a dance competition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with \*\* to ensure that your registration is processed correctly.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
3. Select the correct dance style and competition division that suits your skill level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Please enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Age** (`age`, Number, Optional): Please enter your age (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Please fill in your current address for contact purposes.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Optional): Please fill in the city where you currently reside.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): Please provide a contact number that we can reach you at.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Please provide an email address that we can contact you at.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, Text, Required): Please fill in the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, Email, Optional): Please provide an email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, Select One, Required): Please select the relation of your emergency contact person (e.g. parent, sibling, spouse).
* **Dance Style** (`dance_style`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select the dance style(s) you are interested in (e.g. ballet, contemporary, hip hop, tap, jazz).
* **Competition Division** (`competition_division`, Select One, Required): Please select the competition division that suits your skill level (e.g. junior, senior, adult).
* **Competition Level** (`competition_level`, Select One, Required): Please select the competition level that suits your skill level (e.g. beginner, intermediate, advanced).

Note: This form is designed to collect information to help us process your registration efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
