# Irish Dance Camp Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Irish Dance Camp Registration Form Template is designed to collect information from participants and their parents for the purpose of registering for an Irish dance camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Then, provide your parents' information in the "Parent Information" section.
3. Next, fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
4. If applicable, provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5. Answer any medical information questions in the "Medical Information" section.
6. Check the "Consent" box to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the camp.
7. Finally, provide any relevant class signups in the "Class Signups" section.
8. If you need to, provide payment information in the "Payment Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information)
	+ This section is where you will enter your information as a participant of the camp.
	+ Enter your name and any other identifying details as required.
* **Parent Information** (Parent Information)
	+ If you are a parent or guardian of a participant, please enter your information here.
	+ This is an opportunity to provide any necessary details about yourself that are relevant to the participant's care while at camp.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details)
	+ Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about the camp or participant.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact)
	+ In case of an emergency, please list an alternative contact person we can reach out to.
* **Medical Information** (Medical Information)
	+ Answer any questions about any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs we should be aware of.
* **Consent** (Consent)
	+ By checking this box, you are agreeing to the camp's policies and procedures.
* **Class Signups** (Class Signups)
	+ List any classes you would like to sign up for during the camp.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info)
	+ If you need to, please provide any additional payment information for your registration. 

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to seeing you at the Irish Dance Camp!
