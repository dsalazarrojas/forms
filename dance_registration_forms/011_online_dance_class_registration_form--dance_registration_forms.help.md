# Online Dance Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a registration tool for participants interested in enrolling in dance classes. Please fill out the form to provide your details and choose your preferred class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Choose your preferred dance class from the "Interested in" dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field, if necessary.
4. Select the dance class schedule you are available for in the "Class Schedule" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name* (Text):** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name* (Text):** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth* (Date):** Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Email* (Email):** Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone* (Text):** Enter a contact phone number for the organization to reach you.
* **Interested in (Select One):** Select the dance class you are interested in enrolling in (Dance Class 1, Dance Class 2, or Dance Class 3).
* **Additional Comments (Text):** Enter any additional comments or notes about your enrollment.
* **Class Schedule (Text):** Enter your availability for the dance class schedule (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any special requirements or accommodations, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" field.
- Once submitted, the form will be reviewed, and you will be notified about the next steps.
