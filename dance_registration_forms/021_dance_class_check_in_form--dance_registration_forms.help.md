# Dance Class Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the attendance and details of students participating in a dance class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your first name.
2. Enter your "Last Name" in the next field.
3. Provide your "Email" address if you'd like to be contacted about any important class updates or changes.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field so we can contact you about any last-minute changes.
5. Choose the "Date" of the class you are currently attending.
6. Select the "Time" you arrived at the class.
7. Leave a "Note" if you need to be aware of any important information about your participation or have a special request.
8. If you are a dance style specialist, select the appropriate dance style from the list.
9. Indicate your current dance skill level from the provided options.
10. Check in with your "Check In Status" as either "Active" or "Inactive".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about the class.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about any last-minute changes.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Choose the date you are currently attending the class.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Select the time you arrived at the class.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Leave a note if you need to be aware of any important information about your participation or have a special request.
* **Dance Style** (`select_one`, required): Choose the dance style you specialize in.
* **Dance Level** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate your current dance skill level.
* **Check In Status** (`select_one`, required): Check in with your status, either "Active" or "Inactive".
