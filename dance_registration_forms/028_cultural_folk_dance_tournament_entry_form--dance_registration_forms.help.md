# cultural_folk_dance_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The cultural_folk_dance_tournament_entry_form is a form used to collect information and multimedia content related to a dance team's entry for a cultural folk dance tournament. This form is used to gather essential data about the team, including their entry details, and to upload supporting media files such as dance videos and images.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and multimedia content required for the team's entry.
2. Fill in the "Team Data" section with the required information about the team.
3. In the "Entry Data" section, provide a brief description of the team's dance routine.
4. Upload a dance file, if required, to support the team's entry.
5. If applicable, upload a dance video to showcase the team's performance.
6. Add an entry image, if required, to accompany the team's dance routine.
7. If applicable, add an entry audio file to support the team's entry.
8. In the "Entry Other Media" section, choose the types of media you have uploaded (video, image, or audio) and confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Team Data** (`form_data`, text, required: false): Fill in this field with the team's name, members' names, and any other relevant details about the team.
- **Entry Data** (`entry_data`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the dance routine that the team will be performing.
- **Upload Dance File** (`upload_dance_file`, text, required: false): Upload a file that provides additional information about the team's dance routine, such as a score sheet or other relevant documents.
- **Upload Dance Video** (`upload_dance_video`, text, required: false): Upload a video that showcases the team's dance performance, if available.
- **Entry Image** (`entry_image`, text, required: false): Add an image that accompanies the team's dance routine, if required.
- **Entry Audio** (`entry_audio`, text, required: false): Upload an audio file that supports the team's entry, if applicable.
- **Entry Other Media** (`entry_other_media`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of media you have uploaded (video, image, or audio).
- **Entry Other Media** (`entry_other_media`, text, required: false): Confirm the type of media you have uploaded, as chosen in the previous field.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have uploaded the correct types of media for the team's entry.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the tournament's rules and regulations or seek guidance from the organizers.
