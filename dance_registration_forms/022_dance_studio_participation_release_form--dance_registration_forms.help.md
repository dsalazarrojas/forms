# Dance Studio Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants who wish to participate in dance studio activities. The form helps the dance studio to gather necessary contact and emergency contact information and obtain participant consent for activities that may involve physical activity or risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the first field.
2. Enter the guardian's name if the participant is a minor.
3. Enter the participant's email address.
4. Enter the participant's phone number.
5. Select the types of activities or services that the participant consents to, from the "Waiver and Consent" field.
6. Select the emergency contact from the "Emergency Contact" field.
7. Enter any relevant medical information in the "Medical Info" field.
8. Indicate the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
9. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
10. Enter the emergency contact's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Guardian Name** (text, optional): If the participant is a minor, enter the guardian's name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Waiver and Consent** (select_multiple, optional): Select the activities or services that the participant consents to.
	+ Options: Label 1, Label 2
* **Emergency Contact** (select_one, optional): Select the emergency contact from the list.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Consent** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the participant's consent.
* **Emergency Info** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Medical Info** (text, optional): Enter any relevant medical information about the participant.
* **Relationship** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your information is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the dance studio staff.
