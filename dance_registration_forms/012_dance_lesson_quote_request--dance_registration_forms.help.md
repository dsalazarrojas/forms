# Dance Lesson Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Lesson Quote Request form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in dance lessons. By filling out this form, customers can get a quote for the lessons they're interested in and help the studio provide the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Select one or more dance styles you're interested in from the **Lesson Type** dropdown menu.
3. Choose the duration of your desired lesson from the available options.
4. If you have any special requests or information, use the **Additional Info** field to provide details.
5. Enter your email address in the **Your Email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Your Phone** field.
7. Enter the name of the studio in the **Studio Name** field.
8. Enter the name of your instructor in the **Instructor Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Required): Enter your name as it is spelled correctly.
* **Lesson Type** (Required, Select one or more): Choose the type(s) of lessons you're interested in. Options include:
	+ Ballet
	+ Jazz
	+ Tap
	+ Hip Hop
	+ Pilates
	+ Ballroom
	+ Tap/Jazz
	+ Jazz/Hip Hop
* **Lesson Duration** (Required): Select the duration of your desired lesson.
* **Lesson Frequency** (Optional): If you have a preferred lesson frequency, you can enter it here.
* **Inquiry Date** (Required): Enter the date you're interested in taking the lesson.
* **Time** (Optional): If you have a preferred time for your lesson, you can enter it here.
* **Additional Info** (Optional): Use this field to provide any additional information about your lesson request.
* **Your Email** (Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your quote.
* **Your Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss your quote.
* **Studio Name** (Required): Enter the name of the studio you're interested in.
* **Instructor Name** (Required): Enter the name of your preferred instructor.
