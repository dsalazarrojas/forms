# hip_hop_festival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hip_hop_festival_registration_form is used to register attendees for the hip-hop music festival. Please provide accurate information about yourself to ensure a smooth festival experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select all applicable interests to help festival organizers tailor the event to your taste.
3. Enter a valid email address and phone number if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Info** (1): Provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (2): Enter your contact details, including email and phone number (if available).
* **Area Of Interest** (3): Select all the genres of music that interest you, such as Hip-Hop, Electronic, or Rock. This will help festival organizers understand your musical preferences.
* **Rating** (4): If applicable, rate your musical preference on a scale (e.g., 1-5). This optional field helps festival organizers get a sense of your taste in music.
* **Note** (5): Leave a note about yourself, your musical experience, or anything else you'd like to share with festival organizers.
* **Email** (6): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for festival updates and information.
* **Phone** (7): Provide your phone number (if available) for festival organizers to contact you about event changes or important announcements.
* **Event Date** (8): Although not typically required, you can enter the event date if you know it.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about a field, please ask festival organizers for clarification.
- Make sure to review festival rules and guidelines before attending the event.
