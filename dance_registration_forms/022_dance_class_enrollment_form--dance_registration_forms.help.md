# dance-class-enrollment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dance-class-enrollment-form is used to collect information for general dance class enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by entering your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Contact Email, and Contact Phone.
2. Select your preferred Payment Method and Payment Amount.
3. Choose your preferred Dance Style and Schedule for your classes.
4. Add any Notes or comments for the instructor.
5. Confirm your Student Agreement and Teacher Confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant payment information here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your dance classes (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to make your payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you plan to pay for your dance classes.
* **Dance Class** (`dance_class`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the dance class you are enrolling in.
* **Dance Style** (`dance_style`, `text`, required): Choose your preferred dance style.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days and times you are available to attend your dance classes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes for your instructor here.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm that you agree to the student agreement terms and conditions.
* **Teacher Agreement** (`teacher_agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm that your teacher agreement terms and conditions.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Teacher Availability** (`teacher_availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the availability of your teacher (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Teacher Special Qualifications** (`teacher_special_qualifications`, `text`, required): Enter any special qualifications your teacher may have.
* **Student Special Qualifications** (`student_special_qualifications`, `text`, optional): Enter any special qualifications you may have.
* **Other Instructors** (`other_instructors`, `text`, optional): List any other instructors you would like to have on your class.
* **Class Level** (`class_level`, `text`, optional): Enter the level of your dance class (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to enroll in your dance class.
* **Enrollment Time** (`enrollment_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to enroll in your dance class.
* **Student Confirmation** (`student_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have been contacted by the student confirmation.
* **Teacher Confirmation** (`teacher_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have been contacted by the teacher confirmation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your enrollment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for general dance class enrollment purposes only. If you have specific questions or concerns related to a particular class, please contact the relevant teacher or department.
