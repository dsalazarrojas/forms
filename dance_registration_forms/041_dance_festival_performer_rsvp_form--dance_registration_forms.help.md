# Dance Festival Performer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from dance festival performers to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants. It covers RSVP details, performance specifics, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your RSVP details, including any necessary information about your dance performance.
2. Share your expected arrival details to help us plan logistics.
3. If applicable, choose the agreements that pertain to your situation.
4. Write any additional notes you'd like to share with us.
5. Provide your contact name and phone number in case we need to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **RSVP Details** (`rsvp_details`, text, required: false): Please provide any specific details about your RSVP, such as your group's name, performance time, or any special requirements.
- **Arrival Details** (`arrival_details`, text, required: false): Share your expected arrival time and any other relevant arrival information to help us plan logistics.
- **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, required: false): Enter any production-specific information, such as equipment requirements or special requests.
- **Agreements** (`agreements`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any applicable agreements or permissions you have granted. This might include contract permissions or other relevant agreements.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name, which we'll use as a contact point in case we need to reach out.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can quickly contact you in case of an emergency.
