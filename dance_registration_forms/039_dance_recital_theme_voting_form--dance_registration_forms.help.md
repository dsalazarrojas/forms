# Dance Recital Theme Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Recital Theme Voting Form is a tool for students and staff to submit their theme suggestions for the annual dance recital. This form helps the recital committee gather feedback and ideas from various categories and dance styles to decide on a theme for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category that best describes the theme you are suggesting.
2. Choose the dance styles that relate to your theme.
3. Provide a brief description of your theme.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
5. Set a submission date for your theme, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the category that best describes your theme. This could be "Dance", "School", or "Other" if it doesn't fit into one of the first two categories.
* **Dance Style** (`Dance Style`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all the dance styles that relate to your theme. For example, if your theme is a ballet-inspired theme, select "Ballet" and "Jazz" if the dance recital will feature both styles.
* **Theme** (`Theme`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of your theme. Keep it short and concise, but clear and descriptive.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required: false)
	+ If you want to set a specific date for your theme submission, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the theme.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a category and dance styles that accurately represent your theme.
* Keep your description concise and clear, but feel free to elaborate on any details you'd like to share.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us!
