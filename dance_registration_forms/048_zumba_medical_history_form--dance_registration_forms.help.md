# Zumba Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical history information from patients prior to participating in Zumba classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the patient's information, including their name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. Provide information about the patient's Zumba experience, including how long they have been dancing and any previous injuries or health issues related to dancing.
3. Report any other activities that may be relevant to the patient's dancing experience, such as previous sports or exercise history.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Page 1)**:
  **Name (Text)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID or driver's license.
  **Contact Info (Text)**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
  **Patient ID (Text)**: Enter the patient's ID number or other identifying information.
* **Zumba Info (Page 1)**:
  **Years of Zumba Experience (Text)**: Describe how long the patient has been taking Zumba classes.
  **Previous Injuries or Health Issues (Text)**: Report any previous injuries or health issues related to dancing.
* **Injury Info (Page 2)**:
  **Injury Details (Text)**: Describe any previous injuries or health issues related to dancing.
* **Other Activities (Page 3)**:
  **Other Activities (Text)**: Report any other activities that may be relevant to the patient's dancing experience, such as previous sports or exercise history.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your instructor or dance studio staff.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required information is included.
* This form should not be used for any other purpose than as a medical history form for Zumba patients.
