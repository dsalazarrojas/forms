# Dance Class Participant Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from dance class participants, ensuring their safety and well-being during the class. It's a quick and easy way to confirm their emergency contact details and acknowledge their understanding of the dance class rules and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Welcome To The Dance Class" page to begin.
2. Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
3. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
4. Confirm your safety check-in by checking the box "Participant Safety Check-in".
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to acknowledge that you, as a participant, understand the safety procedures and rules of the dance class.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you, as an instructor, have checked the participant's safety and emergency contact details.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to acknowledge that the dance studio has checked your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome To The Dance Class**: This is the introductory page of the form. Please read and understand the safety procedures and rules of the dance class before proceeding.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the person you've designated as your emergency contact. Enter the name as it appears on their identification.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of your emergency contact. Enter the number in the format ###-###-####.
* **Participant Safety Check-in**: This is a confirmation box that you, as a participant, confirm that you have read and understand the safety procedures and rules of the dance class.
* **Acknowledgement - Participant**: Please answer "Yes" if you've understood the safety procedures and rules of the dance class.
* **Acknowledgement - Instructor**: Please answer "Yes" if you've checked the participant's safety and emergency contact details.
* **Acknowledgement - Dance Studio**: Please answer "Yes" if the dance studio has checked your emergency contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the safety procedures and rules of the dance class before proceeding.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date emergency contact information.
* Confirm your safety check-in and emergency contact information to ensure a smooth and safe dance class experience.
