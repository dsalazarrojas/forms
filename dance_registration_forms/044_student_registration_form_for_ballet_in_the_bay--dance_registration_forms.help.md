# Student Registration Form For Ballet In The Bay - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student registration information for the Ballet In The Bay program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- Fill out the First Name field with your given name.
- Fill out the Last Name field with your surname.
- Enter your Email address in the Email field.
- Fill out the Phone field with your contact number.
- Specify your level of participation (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced) in the Level field.
- Enter your Student ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your given name.
  - This field collects your name as it is commonly known.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your surname.
  - This field collects your family name as it is commonly known.
- **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
  - This field is for the contact email address we'll use to communicate with you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your contact phone number.
  - This field is for your phone number we can use to reach you.
- **Level** (`level`, text, false): Select your dance level.
  - This field is to indicate your skill level in ballet (e.g. beginner, intermediate, advanced).
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, false): Enter your student ID number.
  - This field collects your student ID number for registration purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are completed for proper submission.
