# Dance Studio Space Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Studio Space Change Request Form is used to document and track changes to the studio space, allowing the management team to efficiently process and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the change you are requesting in the **Request Details** field.
2. Enter your **Name**, which will be used for communication and reference purposes.
3. Enter the **Space ID** of the studio space that will be affected by the change.
4. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the change.
5. Enter a brief explanation for the **Reason** for the change.
6. Provide your **Contact Info** so the management team can reach out to you with updates or questions.
7. Enter your **Email**, which will be used for official communication.
8. Leave any additional comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
9. Finally, select the desired **Submit** option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): A brief description of the change you are requesting.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Your name for communication and reference purposes.
* **Space ID** (`space_id`, text, required: false): The ID of the studio space that will be affected by the change.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): The date of the change.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): The time of the change.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): A brief explanation for the change.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Your contact information for the management team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): The email address that will be used for official communication.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Any additional notes or comments about the request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Select the option to submit the form for processing.
