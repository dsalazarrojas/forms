# Summer Dance Camp Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Dance Camp Application Form is an essential tool for registering your child(ren) into the summer dance camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with Page 2: Parent/Guardian Information.
3. Proceed to Page 3: Contact Info to provide your contact details.
4. On Page 4: Payment, enter the payment information to complete the application.
5. Review and finalize your application on Page 5: Submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Summer Dance Camp Application Form (Page 1)
**Title** (Required/Optional): This page is not required to be filled out but provides a general description of the summer dance camp.

### Parent/Guardian Information (Page 2)
* **Parent/Guardian** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select the relationship between you and the child. Choose from the options provided.

### Contact Info (Page 3)
* **Contact Info** (Text, Required): Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach you with any updates or concerns about your child's application.

### Payment (Page 4)
* **Payment** (Text, Required): Enter your payment information, including any necessary payment details. Ensure you have all necessary payment information before proceeding.

### Submit (Page 5)
* **Submit** (Text, Required): This is the final step to finalize your application. Review your information carefully and click submit to complete your registration.
