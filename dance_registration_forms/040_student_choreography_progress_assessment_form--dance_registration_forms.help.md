# Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form is a tool used by teachers and students to assess student performance, identify areas for improvement, and set goals for further growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field explanation below to understand the intended use.
2. Fill out the form based on your understanding of each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Choreography Progress Assessment** (id: 1): This is the main form title. Provide a brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Progress Assessment Date** (id: 2): Enter the date you assessed the student's progress.
* **Teacher Comments** (id: 3): Provide comments or feedback from the teacher regarding the student's progress, strengths, and areas for improvement.
* **Parent Comments** (id: 4): Enter any comments or feedback from parents regarding the student's progress.
* **Level of Difficulty** (id: 5): Select the level of difficulty the student is experiencing. This could be related to technical skills, physical demands, or other factors.
	+ Option 1: Very easy
	+ Option 2: Easy
	+ Option 3: Average
	+ Option 4: Challenging
	+ Option 5: Very challenging
* **Student Level of Difficulty** (id: 6): Select the level of difficulty you are experiencing as a student. This could be related to technical skills, physical demands, or other factors.
	+ Option 1: Very easy
	+ Option 2: Easy
	+ Option 3: Average
	+ Option 4: Challenging
	+ Option 5: Very challenging
* **Teacher Level of Difficulty** (id: 7): Select the level of difficulty you believe the student is experiencing. This could be related to technical skills, physical demands, or other factors.
	+ Option 1: Very easy
	+ Option 2: Easy
	+ Option 3: Average
	+ Option 4: Challenging
	+ Option 5: Very challenging
* **Teacher Level of Difficulty Reason** (id: 8): If you chose Option 4 or 5 for Teacher Level of Difficulty, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Student Feedback** (id: 9): Enter any feedback or comments from the student regarding their progress.
* **Teacher Feedback** (id: 10): Provide feedback or comments from the teacher regarding the student's progress.
* **Student Recommendation** (id: 11): Select the areas where you, as a student, see yourself improving or need help.
	+ Option 1: Technique and skills
	+ Option 2: Physical fitness and endurance
	+ Option 3: Performance and presentation
	+ Option 4: Creativity and expression
	+ Option 5: Overall development
* **Teacher Recommendation** (id: 12): Select the areas where you, as a teacher, see the student improving or need help.
	+ Option 1: Technique and skills
	+ Option 2: Physical fitness and endurance
	+ Option 3: Performance and presentation
	+ Option 4: Creativity and expression
	+ Option 5: Overall development
* **Level of Satisfaction** (id: 13): Select your level of satisfaction with your current level of progress.
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Student Level of Satisfaction** (id: 14): Select your level of satisfaction with your current level of progress.
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Teacher Level of Satisfaction** (id: 15): Select your level of satisfaction with the student's current level of progress.
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Overall Progress** (id: 16): Select your overall progress in the class.
	+ Option 1: Very advanced
	+ Option 2: Advanced
	+ Option 3: Intermediate
* **Teacher Overall Progress** (id: 17): Select your overall progress in the class.
	+ Option 1: Very advanced
	+ Option 2: Advanced
	+ Option 3: Intermediate
* **Student Overall Progress** (id: 18): Select your overall progress in the class.
	+ Option 1: Very advanced
	+ Option 2: Advanced
	+ Option 3: Intermediate
* **Teacher Recommendation for Next Level** (id: 19): Select whether you recommend the student moving to the next level.
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **Student Recommendation for Next Level** (id: 20): Select whether you recommend yourself moving to the next level.
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **Teacher Suggestions** (id: 21): Enter any suggestions or recommendations from the teacher for the student to improve.
* **Student Suggestions** (id: 22): Enter any suggestions or recommendations from the student for themselves to improve.
* **Comments** (id: 23): Enter any additional comments or feedback you want to share.
* **Teacher Signature** (id: 24): Enter your signature as the teacher.
* **Student Signature** (id: 25): Enter your signature as the student.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback or comments.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure you have covered all relevant areas.
* Discuss the form with the student and teacher to ensure everyone is on the same page.

By following this guide, you should be able to fill out the Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form effectively and accurately.
