# Dance Team Member Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Team Member Application Form is designed for dancers who wish to join or become a part of a dance team. It is used to gather information about the applicant, including their contact details, dance experience, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the **Dancer Details** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Select your **Dancer Type** (Dancer, Team Member, or Both).
3.  Indicate your dance experience and special skills in the **Dancer Experience** field.
4.  Choose your available time slots in the **Availability** field.
5.  Fill out the **Emergency Contact** section with the necessary information.
6.  If you have multiple emergency contacts, please list them in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
7.  Enter the address, city, state, and country of your emergency contact's location.
8.  Fill out any additional emergency contact information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Dancer Details** (Page 1):
    *   **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
    *   **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Dancer Type** (Page 2):
    *   **Dancer Type** (Select, Required): Select your type (Dancer, Team Member, or Both).
*   **Dancer Experience** (Page 3):
    *   **dancer_experience** (Number, Optional): Indicate your dance experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
*   **Special Skills** (Page 4):
    *   **special_skills** (Text, Optional): List any special skills you have.
*   **Availability** (Page 5):
    *   **availability** (Time, Optional): Choose your available time slots.
*   **Emergency Contact** (Page 6):
    *   **emergency_contact** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
    *   **emergency_contact_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
    *   **emergency_contact_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
*   **Dance Style** (Page 7):
    *   **dance_style** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your dance style (Ballet, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Jazz, Tap, Ballroom, Latin, or Other).
*   **Dance Levels** (Page 8):
    *   **dance_levels** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your dance levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Advanced-Advanced).
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Page 9):
    *   **emergency_contact_relationship** (Select Multiple, Optional): List your relationship with the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, or Other).
*   **Address** (Page 10):
    *   **emergency_contact_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
*   **City** (Page 11):
    *   **emergency_contact_city** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of your emergency contact.
*   **State** (Page 12):
    *   **emergency_contact_state** (Text, Optional): Enter the state of your emergency contact.
*   **Country** (Page 13):
    *   **emergency_contact_country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country of your emergency contact.
*   **Zip** (Page 14):
    *   **emergency_contact_zip** (Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (Page 15):
    *   **emergency_contact_phone_2** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number 2.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (Page 16):
    *   **emergency_contact_relationship_2** (Select Multiple, Optional): List your relationship with the emergency contact 2 (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, or Other).
*   **Organisation** (Page 17):
    *   **emergency_contact_organisation** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's organisation.
*   **Job** (Page 18):
    *   **emergency_contact_job** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's job.
*   **Notes** (Page 19):
    *   **emergency_contact_notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields.
*   Please fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your application.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this help guide for further clarification.
