# Dance Studio Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dance Studio Enquiry Form" is used to express interest in the dance studio's programs or services. Complete this form to allow the studio to understand your needs and respond with tailored information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, simply follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.**
2. **Make sure to complete all mandatory fields (marked as required).**
3. **Double-check your contact information for accuracy.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name for our records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for further discussion. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for more information. (Optional)
* **Program name**: If you have a specific program or service in mind, enter the name here. (Optional)
* **Enquiry message**: Share any additional information about your interests or questions about our programs. (Optional)

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your enquiry message to help us understand your needs better.
* If you have any attachments or files to support your enquiry, please contact us separately.
* A member of our team will respond to your enquiry within a short period of time.

Note: This guide is intended to be concise and easy to understand, allowing end-users to quickly and accurately complete the form.
