# Dance Class Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension for a student to join a dance class that they are late for. Fill out this form to ensure that the student can catch up on any missed class material and still participate in the dance class.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly:
  1. Fill in your student ID number to select your student from the dropdown menu.
  2. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
  3. Select the dance class you are requesting an extension for.
  4. Choose the date and time you would like to attend the dance class.
  5. Enter any additional extension details (optional).
  6. Choose the instructor teaching your dance class.
  7. Select the date and time you would like to attend the class extension (optional).
  8. Enter any notes or comments for the instructor (optional).
  9. Confirm with the instructor that they have received your request (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dance Class Extension**:
	+ This is the main form section where you will enter your extension request.
* **Student**: 
	+ Select your student ID number from the dropdown menu.
* **Name**:
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Dance Class**:
	+ Choose the dance class you are requesting an extension for.
* **Date**:
	+ Enter the date you would like to attend the class.
* **Time**:
	+ Enter the time you would like to attend the class.
* **Extension Details**:
	+ Enter any additional details for your extension request (optional).
* **Instructor**:
	+ Choose the instructor teaching your dance class.
* **Student Extension Date**:
	+ Select the date you would like to attend the class extension (optional).
* **Student Extension Time**:
	+ Select the time you would like to attend the class extension (optional).
* **Instructor Confirmation**:
	+ Enter any comments or notes for the instructor (optional).
* **Student Confirmation**:
	+ Enter any comments or notes for the student (optional).
* **Dance Class Extension Form Submission**:
	+ Enter your email address where you would like to receive confirmation of your request (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form with accurate information to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- Enter any additional comments or notes for the instructor or student to ensure they are aware of any special circumstances.
- Check your email for confirmation of your request after submitting the form.
