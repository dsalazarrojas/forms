# Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler form is designed to help dental offices manage and schedule meetings with employees for dental open enrollment, including information about the employer, employee, and meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Employer Information" page.
2. Enter the relevant information for the employer (e.g., company name, contact details).
3. Proceed to the "Employee Information" page and enter the employee's details (e.g., name, date of birth).
4. Choose the meeting request type: Broker/Agent, Employee, or Employer.
5. Select the meeting date and time.
6. Choose the meeting location: Office, Home, or Clinic.
7. Enter the meeting duration and details.
8. Enter the assigned contact and follow-up information.
9. Select the follow-up status and add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Information (1)**: Enter the employer's company name, address, and contact details.
* **Employee Information (2)**: Enter the employee's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
* **Meeting Request (3)**: Select from three options: Broker/Agent, Employee, or Employer. This indicates the type of meeting requested.
* **Meeting Date (4)**: Select the date for the meeting.
* **Meeting Time (5)**: Select the time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (6)**: Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Location (7)**: Select from three options: Office, Home, or Clinic.
* **Meeting Details (8)**: Enter any additional details about the meeting.
* **Follow Up Information (9)**: Enter any follow-up information for the meeting.
* **Follow Up Contact (10)**: Enter the contact person's name and information.
* **Follow Up Message (11)**: Enter a follow-up message to be sent to the employee.
* **Assigned Contact (12)**: Enter the name of the person assigned to follow up with the employee.
* **Assigned Date (13)**: Select the date when the meeting was assigned to the contact person.
* **Assigned Time (14)**: Select the time when the meeting was assigned to the contact person.
* **Assigned Duration (15)**: Enter the duration of the meeting assigned to the contact person.
* **Assigned Location (16)**: Select the location where the meeting was assigned to the contact person.
* **Assigned Meeting Details (17)**: Enter any additional details about the assigned meeting.
* **Follow Up Status (18)**: Select from three options: Open, Closed, or Rescheduled. This indicates the current status of the follow-up meeting.
* **Follow Up Note (19)**: Enter any additional notes about the follow-up meeting.

**Tips**

* Make sure to update the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion.
* Double-check the meeting details and assigned meeting details to ensure correct communication.
* Review the follow-up status and notes to ensure the meeting is properly documented.
* Use the form as a reference guide for future meetings and follow-ups.
