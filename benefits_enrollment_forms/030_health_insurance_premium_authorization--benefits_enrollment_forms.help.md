# health insurance premium authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and authorize health insurance premium payments. It is an essential part of the process to ensure that premium payments are accurate and up-to-date. Please complete the form carefully to provide the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information on the "User Information" page.
2. Select the type of policy you are authorizing (Individual, Group, or Family).
3. Provide payment information on the "Payment Information" page.
4. Add your signature, which will be used as proof of authorization.
5. Enter the date and time of the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)** (`user_info`, required: false): Enter your name, employee ID, and other relevant details as per your organization's policies.
* **Policy Details (2)** (`policy_details`, required: false): Choose the type of policy you are authorizing (Individual, Group, or Family).
* **Payment Information (3)** (`payment_info`, required: false): Enter the details of the payment, including the amount and payment method.
* **Signature (4)** (`signature`, required: false): Provide your digital signature as proof of authorization.
* **Date (5)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the authorization.
* **Time (6)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the authorization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your HR or Benefits Department.
* This form is only for authorizing health insurance premium payments. If you need to make other types of payments, please use the relevant form for that purpose.
