# Retirement And Health Savings Contributions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information on retirement and health savings contributions for employees. It's used for internal purposes and not for benefits eligibility or claim processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading the instructions and explanations provided in each field.
2. Answer each field truthfully and accurately, using the provided options or free-text fields.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information before completing the form.
4. If unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your HR or benefits representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (1)**: This field is for general employee information. Please provide your name, ID number, and any other relevant details as prompted.
* **Retirement Plan (2)**: Enter the number of years you've contributed to your retirement plan or the expected contributions for the next few years.
* **Health Savings Contributions (3)**: Enter the amount you've contributed to your health savings plan or the expected contributions for the next few years.
* **Select One (4)**: Choose one of the options that best suits your situation. This field is for optional information.
* **Select Multiple (5)**: Choose all the relevant options that apply to your situation.
* **Date (6)**: Enter your expected retirement date or a relevant date for your current contributions.
* **Time (7)**: Enter your expected retirement time or a relevant time for your current contributions.
* **Note (8)**: If you have any additional comments or information, use this field to provide them.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your work email address or any other contact information for follow-up.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your work phone number or any other contact information for follow-up.
