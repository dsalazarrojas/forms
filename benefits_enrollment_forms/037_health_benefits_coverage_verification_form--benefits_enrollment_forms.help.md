# Health Benefits Coverage Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify an individual's health benefits coverage. Complete this form to provide information about your coverage status as an employee or dependent of our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as required).
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, SSN, Date of Birth, Phone, Email, Date of Last Exam, Medical Group, Medical Group 2, Coverage Type, Plan ID, and Submitted By.
3. Select Medical Group 1 from the Medical Group select field.
4. Choose the Start Date and End Date if applicable.
5. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
6. Ensure that the form is submitted correctly, including selecting the correct Assigned Tool and Assigned User.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **SSN** (`ssn`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your address (not required but recommended for communication purposes).
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter your additional address information (not required but recommended for communication purposes).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Date of Last Exam** (`date_of_last_exam`, date, required): Enter the date of your last medical examination.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, required): Select your primary medical group from the options provided.
* **Medical Group 2** (`medical_group_2`, select_multiple, required): Select your secondary medical groups from the options provided.
* **Medical Group 3** (`medical_group_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select your additional medical groups (not required but recommended).
* **Coverage Type** (`coverage_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of coverage you have.
* **Plan ID** (`plan_id`, text, required): Enter your plan ID.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your coverage (if applicable).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your coverage (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your coverage.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool or system used to assign coverage.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required): Enter the user responsible for your coverage.
