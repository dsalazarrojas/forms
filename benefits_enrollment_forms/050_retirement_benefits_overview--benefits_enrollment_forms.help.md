# Retirement Benefits Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retirement Benefits Overview" form is designed to collect information about an employee's retirement benefits. This includes whether they have enrolled in benefits, the type of benefits they have, and their plan details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Benefits Form Type" with the type of benefits you have (Yes, No, or Other).
2. Select your "Plan Type" (Yes, No, or Other).
3. Enter your "Date of Birth".
4. Provide your "Retirement Date" (if applicable).
5. Describe your "Benefits Enrollment Process" in the text field.
6. Select "Additional Benefits" you have (Yes, No, or Other).
7. Fill out "Contact Information" with any relevant details (this field is optional).
8. Enter your "Email Address".
9. Enter your "Phone Number".
10. Identify your emergency contact (Yes, No, or Other).
11. If applicable, provide details about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship.
12. If applicable, identify your emergency contact's job title, department, and supervisor status.
13. If applicable, provide details about your emergency contact's job title 2.
14. If applicable, provide details about your emergency contact's employer, job title, and department.

* **Benefits Form Type** (`retirement_benefits_form_type`, optional): Select the type of benefits you have.
* **Plan Type** (`retirement_plan_type`, optional): Select the type of plan you have.

* **Retirement Date** (`retirement_date`, optional): Enter your retirement date.
* **Benefits Enrollment Process** (`benefits_enrollment_process`, optional): Describe the process you went through for benefits enrollment.
* **Additional Benefits** (`additional_benefits`, optional): Select additional benefits you have.

* **Email Address** (`email_address`, optional): Enter your email address.

* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, optional): Select the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`, optional): Select the relationship with your emergency contact 2.
