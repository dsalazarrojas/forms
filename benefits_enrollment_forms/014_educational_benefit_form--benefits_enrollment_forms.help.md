# Educational Benefit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Benefit Form is designed to capture information about educational benefits received by students. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that benefits are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Student Name
	* Student ID
	* Student Email
	* Student Phone
	* Financial Aid Amount
	* Financial Aid Type
	* Date of Benefit
	* Date of Disbursement
	* Benefit Amount
	* Benefit Status
2. If applicable, fill out the optional fields:
	* Student Address (not required)
	* Reason for Denial (if benefit status is Denied)
	* Notes (for any additional information)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Student Name
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification documents.

### 2. Student ID
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their identification documents.

### 3. Student Email
* **Student Email** (`Student Email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.

### 4. Student Phone
* **Student Phone** (`Student Phone`, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.

### 5. Student Address
* **Student Address** (`Student Address`, note, optional): Enter the student's address. (Note: Not required)

### 6. Financial Aid Amount
* **Financial Aid Amount** (`Financial Aid Amount`, number, required): Enter the financial aid amount received.

### 7. Financial Aid Type
* **Financial Aid Type** (`Financial Aid Type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Federal
	+ Institutional
	+ Private

### 8. Date of Benefit
* **Date of Benefit** (`Date of Benefit`, date, required): Enter the date the benefit was received in mm/dd/yyyy format.

### 9. Date of Disbursement
* **Date of Disbursement** (`Date of Disbursement`, date, required): Enter the date the benefit was disbursed in mm/dd/yyyy format.

### 10. Benefit Amount
* **Benefit Amount** (`Benefit Amount`, number, required): Enter the total benefit amount received.

### 11. Benefit Status
* **Benefit Status** (`Benefit Status`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Denied

### 12. Reason for Denial
* **Reason for Denial** (`Reason for Denial`, note, optional): If the benefit status is Denied, provide a reason for denial.

### 13. Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the benefit.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your representative for assistance.
