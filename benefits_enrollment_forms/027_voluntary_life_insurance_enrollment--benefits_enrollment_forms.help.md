# Voluntary Life Insurance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voluntary Life Insurance Enrollment form is used for employees to choose and enroll in various life insurance plans that provide additional financial protection for themselves and their loved ones in case of an unexpected event. This form helps employees select the right plan that suits their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the plan that best suits your needs from the "Plan Selections" page.
2. Fill in your employee information (name, etc.) on the "Employee Information" page.
3. Provide details about your beneficiary(ies) on the "Beneficiary Information" page.
4. Add any additional details about yourself on the "Employee Details" page.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
* **Plan Selections** (`plan_selections`, select_one, optional): Choose the life insurance plan that you want to enroll in from the options provided. These options are:
	+ Basic Life Insurance
	+ Enhanced Life Insurance
	+ No Life Insurance
* **Beneficiary Information** (`beneficiary_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the person or people you would like to designate as your beneficiary(ies). This could be a spouse, partner, child, or other family member.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about yourself that is not already on file with HR or your ID card.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date this section to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the selected plan.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date this section to confirm that you have reviewed and approve the selected plan for your employee.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date this section to confirm that you have reviewed and approve the selected plan for your employee.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are completing this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask HR or your supervisor for clarification.
- Double-check your selections and information before submitting the form.
