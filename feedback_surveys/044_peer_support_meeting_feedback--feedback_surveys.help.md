# Peer Support Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a peer support meeting. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing us to make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Email to help us contact you for follow-up or to send you any updates.
3. Provide your Feedback about the meeting. Be honest and specific about what you liked, what you didn't like, and what could be improved.
4. Select the Category that best describes the meeting's level of supportiveness. Choose from "Supportive" or "Not Supportive".
5. If you have a specific Follow-up request, please select one from the list provided.
6. If you have a suggestion for an improvement, please choose a Program from the list provided.
7. If you'd like to provide additional comments or feedback about the facilitator, type it in the Facilitator text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is spelled out in full.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is spelled out in full.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up or updates.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Provide detailed, specific feedback about the meeting. Be honest and concise.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting you're providing feedback for.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting you're providing feedback for.
* **Category** (Category, select_multiple, required): Select the level of supportiveness you experienced during the meeting. Choose from "Supportive" or "Not Supportive".
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, select_one, required): If you have a specific follow-up request, choose one from the list.
* **Program** (Program, select_one, required): Suggest an improvement or change for a future meeting from the list provided.
* **Facilitator** (Facilitator, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the facilitator.
