# Book Publishing Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who have used our book publishing services. We value your input and want to understand your experience with our team. Your responses will help us improve our services and provide better results for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select the services you used from the list (if applicable).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction and experience with our services.
4. Provide specific feedback or comments if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Which Service Did You Use?** (`service_used`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the service you received (e.g., Editing, Cover Design, etc.).
* **When Did You Use Our Services?** (`project_date`, `date`, required: false): Approximate date of service.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, required: false): Title of your book.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
* **Quality of Work Rating** (`quality_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the quality of work (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate our communication (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Timeliness Rating** (`timeliness_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how well we met deadlines (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Value for Money Rating** (`value_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the value received (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Professionalism Rating** (`professionalism_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate our professionalism (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Team Responsiveness** (`team_responsiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how responsive was our team (Very Responsive, Responsive, Neutral, Slow to Respond, or Unresponsive).
* **Were Your Expectations Met?** (`expectations_met`, `select_one`, required: true): Did we meet your expectations (Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Mostly Met, Below Expectations, or Far Below Expectations).
* **Would You Recommend Us?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required: true): Would you recommend our services (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **Would You Use Our Services Again?** (`would_use_again`, `select_one`, required: true): Would you work with us again (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, or Definitely No).
* **What Did You Like Best?** (`best_aspect`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the best aspect of our service (Quality of Work, Customer Service, Turnaround Time, Pricing, Communication, or Expertise).
* **What Could We Improve?** (`improvement_area`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the area for improvement (Communication, Turnaround Time, Pricing, Quality, Customer Service, or Nothing to Improve).
* **Specific Feedback** (`specific_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any specific feedback or comments.
* **Any Team Member to Recognize?** (`team_member_mention`, `text`, required: false): Name anyone who was exceptional in their service.
* **Interested in Additional Services?** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select additional services you might be interested in using (e.g., Editing, Cover Design, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, required: false): How did you find our services (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, Website, Advertisement, or Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Any other feedback to share.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required: true): Date of survey completion.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any specific feedback or comments, use the "Specific Feedback" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
