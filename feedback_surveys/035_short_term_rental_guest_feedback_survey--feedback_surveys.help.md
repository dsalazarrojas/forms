## short-term-rental-guest-feedback-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey for guests who have stayed at a short-term rental property. It is designed to gather feedback on their experience, helping us to understand what we do well and where we can improve. Your responses will help us to make informed decisions about our services and provide a better experience for future guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in all required fields (denoted by <code>required</code> in the field descriptions).
3. Select all applicable answers for multiple-choice questions.
4. Submit your feedback once you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Short Term Rental Guest Feedback Survey** (`title`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your name so we can address you in our response.
* **Stay Date** (`stay_date`, `date`, not required): If you're not sure what date you stayed, you can leave this field blank.
* **Rating** (`rating_1`, `number`, not required): This question allows you to rate your overall experience from 1-5.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, `number`, not required): This question allows you to rate another aspect of your experience from 1-5.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, `number`, not required): This question allows you to rate another aspect of your experience from 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_1`, `text`, not required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your stay.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, `text`, not required): Additional feedback is always welcome.
* **Type of Stay** (`stay_type`, `select_one`, not required): Please select the type of stay you experienced (e.g. Vacation Rental, Serviced Apartment, etc.).
* **Location** (`stay_location`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you visited multiple locations, select all that apply.
* **Room Quality** (`room_quality`, `select_one`, not required): This question allows you to rate the quality of the room.
* **Cleanliness Quality** (`cleanliness_quality`, `select_one`, not required): This question allows you to rate the cleanliness quality of the property.
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, not required): This question allows you to rate the communication quality of the property.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, not required): This question allows you to rate the value for money of the property.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, not required): This question allows you to rate your overall experience.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `select_multiple`, not required): We would like to know if you would recommend this property to others or not.
