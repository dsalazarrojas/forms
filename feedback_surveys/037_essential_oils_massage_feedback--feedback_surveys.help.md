# Essential Oils Massage Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have received an essential oils massage. It aims to gather information about the quality of the massage experience, the therapist's performance, and the client's satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, providing ratings and comments where applicable.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the massage.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How would you rate your overall experience? (massage_feedback)** 
* **Label:** How would you rate your overall experience?
* **Type:** Number
* **Description:** Please rate your overall experience with the massage, from 1 (Not Satisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).

### 2. **Therapist**
* **Label:** Therapist
* **Type:** Select One
* **Description:** Rate the performance of the massage therapist. You can choose from the options provided:
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied

### 3. **The massage quality of your therapist**
* **Label:** The massage quality of your therapist
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Description:** Rate the massage quality of your therapist. You can choose one or more options from the list:
	+ Too Hard
	+ Too Soft
	+ Just right

### 4. **Which essential oil was used during the massage? (oil_selection)**
* **Label:** Which essential oil was used during the massage?
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Description:** Please select all the essential oils that were used during the massage. You can choose one or more options from the list:
	+ Lavender
	+ Eucalyptus
	+ Frankincense

### 5. **Comments (comments)**
* **Label:** Comments
* **Type:** Note
* **Description:** If you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience, please type them in here.

### 6. **Email**
* **Label:** Email
* **Type:** Email
* **Description:** This is an optional field to provide your email address for follow-up or future communication.

### 7. **Phone**
* **Label:** Phone
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** This is an optional field to provide your phone number for follow-up or future communication.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
