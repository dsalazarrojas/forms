# Vendor Feedback Accuracy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Feedback Accuracy Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with vendors. The survey aims to assess the accuracy of the vendor's products or services in relation to the customer's expectations. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, click on "Submit" or "Next" at the end of each page.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information, and click "Save" or "Next" to proceed to the next page.
3. When you have completed all the pages, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information 1** (`vendor_info_1`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select the level of accuracy of the vendor's information. Choose from Very Accurate, Somewhat Accurate, Somewhat Inaccurate, or Very Inaccurate.
* **Performance 1** (`performance_1`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to select up to three areas where the vendor has performed well. Choose from Quality of Products/Services, Pricing, Communication, Timeliness, or Value for Money.
* **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to express your overall feedback on the vendor. Choose from Positive or Negative.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or feedback you have about the vendor.
* **Vendor Rating** (`vendor_rating`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to rate the vendor's overall performance. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Rating 1-16** (`rating_1-rating_16`, number, optional): These fields ask you to rate specific aspects of the vendor's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments 1** (`comments_1`, text, optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or feedback you have about the vendor's performance.

Note that some fields may not be visible or required, and you can always ask for help if you are unsure about any field.
