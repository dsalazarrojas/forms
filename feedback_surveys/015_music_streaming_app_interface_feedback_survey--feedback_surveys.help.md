# music_streaming_app_interface_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_streaming_app_interface_feedback_survey form is designed to gather feedback on the music streaming app's interface, allowing us to understand user experiences and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form link to open it.
2. Answer each question on the page, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Move to the next page by clicking the "Next" button.
4. Review your answers and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Page** (`home_page`, text, optional): Describe your recent interactions with the music streaming app's home page. This will help us understand how you navigate the app and what changes you'd like to see.
* **Search Results** (`search_results`, text, optional): Share your experiences with the music streaming app's search results page. What do you like or dislike about it?
* **Profile** (`profile`, select_one, required): Select one of the following profiles:
	+ Profile info: Share more details about yourself.
	+ Profile settings: Discuss your profile settings.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Suggest three things about the music streaming app:
	+ Music recommendations: Recommend songs, artists, or playlists.
	+ Interface changes: Share ideas for improvements to the app's interface.
	+ Bug report: Report any technical issues you've encountered.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the music streaming app (1-5).
* **Rate Your Experience** (`rating_scale`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the following aspects:
	+ Music discovery
	+ Navigation
	+ Overall satisfaction
* **Additional Comments** (`rating_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up contact (if desired).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact (if desired).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* Feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email or phone number.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback!
