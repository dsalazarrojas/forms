# Wear Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wear Experience Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their wear experience, helping us understand their satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your answers for each field as accurately as possible.
2. For select_one fields, choose one option from the available options.
3. For number fields, enter a numerical value.
4. For text fields, enter your thoughts and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Comfort** (1, number): Rate your comfort level when wearing the product (1-10).
* **Fit** (2, number): Rate your fit level when wearing the product (1-10).
* **Usage** (3, text): Describe how you used the product (e.g., daily, occasional, or special occasions).
* **Comfort Level** (4, text): Describe your comfort level with the product (e.g., comfortable, too tight, too loose).
* **Fit Level** (5, select_one): Choose how the product fits (Perfect, Too Small, Too Large).
* **Size** (6, number): Enter the size of the product.
* **Color** (7, select_one): Choose the color of the product (Black, White, Gray).
* **Style** (8, select_one): Choose the style of the product (Sport, Casual, Formal).
* **Price** (9, number): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Purchase Date** (10, date): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Amount** (11, number): Enter the amount you paid for the product.
* **Purchase Platform** (12, select_one): Choose where you purchased the product (Online, Offline, Store).
* **Purchase Method** (13, select_one): Choose how you paid for the product (Credit Card, Cash, Other).
* **Purchase Reason** (14, select_one): Choose why you purchased the product (Recommended, Ad, Social, Other).
* **Feedback** (15, text): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (16, text): Suggest any improvements or changes you'd like to see in the product.
* **Product Name** (17, text): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Price** (18, number): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Color** (19, select_one): Choose the color of the product (None, Other).
* **Product Style** (20, select_one): Choose the style of the product (None, Other).
* **Product Size** (21, number): Enter the size of the product.
* **Product Category** (22, select_one): Choose the category of the product (None, Other).
* **Product Brand** (23, text): Enter the brand of the product.
* **Comments** (24, text): Enter any additional comments about your experience.
