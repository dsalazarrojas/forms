# Property Staff Responsiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on the responsiveness and quality of service provided by property staff. Your input will help us improve our services and ensure that your property needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your most recent interaction with the property staff.
2. Rate the options according to your experience, where:
   * 1 = Very Poor
   * 2 = Poor
   * 3 = Average
   * 4 = Good
   * 5 = Excellent

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How responsive is your property staff** (`property_response_time`, number, required: false):
    • This question measures how quickly the property staff responded to your queries or requests.
* **How responsive is your property staff with communication** (`communication`, number, required: false):
    • This question rates the level of communication from the property staff, including the ease of getting in touch with them and their ability to address your concerns.
* **Overall quality of property staff** (`staff_quality`, number, required: false):
    • This question assesses the overall quality of service provided by the property staff, including their professionalism, efficiency, and effectiveness.
