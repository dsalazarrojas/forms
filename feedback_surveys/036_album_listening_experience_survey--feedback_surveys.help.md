# Album Listening Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from listeners about their experience with an album. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand how our music is being received.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in each section to provide a comprehensive picture of your experience.
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to add them in the "Final Thoughts or Messages for the Artist" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name** (text, required/optional): Please provide your name, which will help us understand who is providing feedback.
- **How did you listen to this album?** (select_one, required): Choose how you listened to the album (e.g. Vinyl, CD, Digital Stream, etc.). This will help us understand the context in which you experienced the album.
- **Where did you primarily listen to it?** (select_one, required): Choose the location where you spent most of your time listening to the album (e.g. home, commuting, working/studying, etc.).
- **Overall Album Rating** (number, required): Rate the album as a whole on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "not enjoyable at all" and 5 is "exceptionally enjoyable".
- **Production and Sound Quality** (number, required): Rate the production and sound quality of the album on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "not well-produced" and 5 is "exceptionally well-produced".
- **Lyricism and Songwriting** (number, required): Rate the lyrics and composition of the album on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "not well-written" and 5 is "exceptionally well-written".
- **Album Flow and Sequencing** (number, required): Rate the flow and sequencing of the album on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "not well-sequenced" and 5 is "exceptionally well-sequenced".
- **Favorite Track(s)** (select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 tracks that you enjoyed the most from the album.
- **Least Favorite Track** (text, optional): If you have a least favorite track, feel free to mention it.
- **What emotions did this album evoke?** (text, required): Describe how the album made you feel or what emotions it evoked.
- **Standout Moment or Lyric** (text, optional): If there's a particular moment or lyric that stood out to you, feel free to share.
- **Will you listen to this album again?** (select_one, required): Choose how often you'll listen to the album again (e.g. regularly, occasionally, rarely, etc.).
- **Would you recommend this album to a friend?** (select_one, required): Choose if you'd recommend the album to a friend (e.g. definitely, likely, unlikely, etc.).
- **Final Thoughts or Messages for the Artist** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with the artist.
