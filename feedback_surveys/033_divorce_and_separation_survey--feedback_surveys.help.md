# Divorce And Separation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have gone through a divorce or separation. The goal is to understand their experiences, feelings, and expectations after the separation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Choose one or more options for each question where applicable.
3. Provide as much detail as you feel comfortable with for open-ended questions.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please feel free to skip this question.
* **How long have you and your spouse been married?** (`2`, `number`, not required): Please enter the number of years you and your spouse were married.
* **How many years do you estimate you have been in a toxic environment?** (`3`, `number`, not required): Estimate the number of years you have been in a toxic environment.
* **What type of toxic environment?** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following:
	+ Abusive
	+ Emotionally neglective
	+ Emotionally and Physically Abusive
	+ Physically Abusive
	+ Narcissistic
	+ Gaslighting
	+ Financial Abusive
	+ Emotional Blackmail
	+ Parental Alienation
	+ Other
* **What was the most difficult part of your marriage?** (`5`, `text`, not required): Please describe the most challenging aspect of your marriage.
* **What were some of the circumstances that led to your decision to divorce?** (`6`, `text`, not required): Provide details about the events that led to your separation.
* **Do you think your spouse is at fault?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How would you rate your level of agreement on certain issues?** (`8`, `number`, not required): Rate your level of agreement on a scale of 1-10.
* **Do you feel your spouse is still in your life?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of the following:

	+ No

* **What do you think is the most difficult part for your children?** (`17`, `text`, not required): Describe the most challenging aspect for your children.
