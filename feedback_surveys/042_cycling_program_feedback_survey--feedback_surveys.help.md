# Cycling Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cycling Program Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in a cycling class. It will help us understand how the class and instructor met or fell short of your expectations and provide valuable insights to improve future classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions and understand what is being asked.
2. For each question, provide your honest and detailed response.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.
4. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a one-time note field for you to write any general comments about your experience in the class.
* **What rating do you give to this class?** (Number): Rate the class from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the instructor?** (Number): Rate the instructor from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What did you like or dislike about this class?** (Text): Please describe your positive and negative experiences with the class, including any specific events or interactions that stood out.
* **Which of the following best describes your experience in this class?** (Select One):
	+ Great
	+ Not what I expected
	+ Average
	+ Below average
* **Email address** (Email): If you'd like to receive updates or follow-up questions, please provide your email address.
* **Contact number** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your contact number.
* **Additional comments** (Text): If you have any other thoughts or suggestions about the class, please share them here.
* **Overall rating** (Number): Rate the class from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Instructor comments** (Note): This is a one-time note field for the instructor to respond to your comments.
* **Contact number** (Text): Please provide your contact number if you'd like the instructor to follow up with you.
* **Additional comments** (Text): If you have any other thoughts or suggestions about the class, please share them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve future classes!
