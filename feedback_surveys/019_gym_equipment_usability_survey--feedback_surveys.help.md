# Gym Equipment Usability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the usability of gym equipment. The survey is intended to gather information on the availability, usability, and facilities of gym equipment to improve the overall gym experience for members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the Home Page, which serves as an introduction to the survey.
2. Select the equipment types that are available at the gym (Free Weight Machines, Free Cardio Machines, or Free Strength Machines).
3. Rate the usability of each equipment type on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being very good.
4. Choose the days on which the equipment is available (e.g., Mon-Fri, Mon-Thu, etc.).
5. Select the hours when the equipment is available (e.g., 7am-6pm, 7am-5pm, etc.).
6. Provide your suggestions for improving the facilities of the gym.
7. Add any additional comments you may have about the equipment or gym facilities.
8. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
9. Enter your email address, phone number, location, and department (if applicable).
10. Enter your contact person's name and email address, phone number, and department (if applicable).
11. Finally, add any departmental comments and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Availability** (`equipment_availability`): Select all equipment types that are available at the gym. This could be Free Weight Machines, Free Cardio Machines, or Free Strength Machines.
* **How Usable** (`usability`): Rate the usability of the selected equipment on a scale of 1-5. A higher rating indicates better usability.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`): Choose the days on which the equipment is available. For example, if the equipment is available on weekdays, select "Mon-Fri".
* **Availability Hours** (`availability_hours`): Select the hours when the equipment is available. For example, if the equipment is available from 7am to 6pm, select "7am-6pm".
* **Facility Improvements** (`facilities_improvements`): Provide suggestions on how the gym facilities can be improved.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the equipment or facilities.
* **Member Survey Suggestion** (`member_survey_suggestion`): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the gym facilities.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter your location.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Shift** (`shift`): Enter your shift.
* **Day of Week** (`day_of_week`): Enter the day of the week on which you are working.
* **Comments Departmental** (`comments_departmental`): Add any departmental comments.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Email Departmental** (`email_departmental`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone Departmental** (`phone_departmental`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Availability Days Departmental** (`availability_days_departmental`): Choose the days on which the department is available.
* **Shift Departmental** (`shift_departmental`): Enter your departmental shift.
* **Departmental Comments** (`departmental_comments`): Add any departmental comments.
* **Departmental Contact Person** (`departmental_contact_person`): Enter the departmental contact person's name.
* **Departmental Email** (`departmental_email`): Enter the departmental contact person's email address.
* **Departmental Phone** (`departmental_phone`): Enter the departmental contact person's phone number.
* **Departmental Comments 2** (`departmental_comments_2`): Add any additional departmental comments.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the space provided.
* If you have any departmental comments or contact information, please provide it in the respective fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at [your contact information].
