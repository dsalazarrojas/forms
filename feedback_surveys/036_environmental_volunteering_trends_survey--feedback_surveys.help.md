# Environmental Volunteering Trends Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on your volunteering behavior and preferences. Your participation is voluntary and all responses will be kept confidential. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that all questions are completed, even if you're not sure about certain answers.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): A brief welcome message explaining the purpose of this survey and how your input will be used.
* **Volunteer Behavior** (`volunteer_behavior`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current volunteering behavior, including any specific activities or tasks you've participated in.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Indicate how often you volunteer, using the following scale:
	+ 1-2 times a month
	+ 3-4 times a month
	+ 5-6 times a month
	+ Weekly
	+ Less than once a month
* **Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_duration`, `number`, required: false): Indicate how long you typically volunteer for, using the following scale:
	+ Less than 1 hour
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-4 hours
	+ 4-6 hours
	+ More than 6 hours
* **Volunteer Location** (`volunteer_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a location where you usually volunteer:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Volunteer Purpose** (`volunteer_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to describe your motivations for volunteering:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Volunteer Frequency 2** (`volunteer_frequency_2`, `number`, required: false): Repeat the volunteer frequency question from above.
* **Volunteer Duration 2** (`volunteer_duration_2`, `number`, required: false): Repeat the volunteer duration question from above.
* **Volunteer Location 2** (`volunteer_location_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Repeat the volunteer location question from above.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Indicate the date you're currently participating in or plan to participate in a volunteering activity.

Please note that all fields are optional and you can leave any field blank if you're unsure or don't want to answer. If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, don't hesitate to contact us. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey.
