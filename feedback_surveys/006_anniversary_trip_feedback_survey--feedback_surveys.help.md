# Anniversary Trip Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have taken an anniversary trip to help us improve our services and create better experiences for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For questions that are not applicable to your experience, select "Not Applicable" or "No Plans Currently" as the appropriate option.
3. Provide specific details and anecdotes in the fields where possible.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name, or leave it blank if you'd like to remain anonymous.
* **Email Address (Optional)**: If you'd like us to contact you for follow-up regarding your feedback, please enter your email address here. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates you took your anniversary trip.
* **Destination**: Enter the city or location you visited.
* **Type of Trip**: Select the type of anniversary trip you took (e.g. Romantic Getaway, Cruise, etc.).
* **Number of Travelers**: Enter the number of people who traveled with you.
* **How Did You Book?**: Select the method you used to book your trip (e.g. Travel Agency, Online Booking Site, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you were with your overall experience.
* **Accommodation Rating**: Rate the quality of your accommodations.
* **Destination Rating**: Rate the quality of the destination.
* **Value for Money**: Select how you felt about the value for money of your trip.
* **Trip Highlights**: Select the best parts of your trip (e.g. accommodations, dining, activities, etc.).
* **Areas for Improvement**: Select areas that could be improved (e.g. accommodations, food, activities, etc.).
* **Service Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of service you received.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of your accommodations.
* **Anniversary Special Touches**: Select if the provider acknowledged your anniversary celebration.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Select how likely you are to recommend this destination to others.
* **Likelihood to Return**: Select how likely you are to visit this destination again.
* **Best Memory**: Share your favorite moment from the trip (optional).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any suggestions you have for how we can improve our services.
* **Share Photos**: Select if you would be willing to share photos of your trip for marketing purposes (optional).
* **Future Travel Plans**: Select if you are planning another anniversary trip.
