# Donation Decision Making Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information on your donation habits, motivations, and preferences. Your responses will help us better understand what drives your decision to donate, which will inform our organizational strategy to engage with you more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through each page carefully. Each page will ask you a question or prompt you to choose an option.
2. When prompted with a question, select the most accurate option from the provided choices. If none of the options match your thoughts, please use the text box for the "barrier_donate" and/or "comments" fields to provide your own response.
3. Fill out the "email_address" field with your email address. This is the only field that requires a specific format.
4. Optionally, provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field, but this is not required.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`title`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.

### Page 2: How often do you donate?
* **How often do you donate?** (`how_often_donate`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer this question by selecting one of the provided options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.

### Page 3: What motivates you to donate?
* **What motivates you to donate?** (`motivation_donate`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Answer this question by selecting one or more options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4.

### Page 4: What are some barriers you face when donating?
* **What are some barriers you face when donating?** (`barrier_donate`, `text`, required: true): If you have any challenges or difficulties that prevent you from donating, please provide a brief description in this text box.

### Page 5: What is your preferred way to donate?
* **What is your preferred way to donate?** (`preference_donate`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer this question by selecting one of the provided options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Page 6: Contact Information
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address in this field. This is the only field that requires a specific format.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to provide your contact number, please do so in this field. However, this is not required.

### Page 7: Additional Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments related to your donation habits or preferences, please use this text box to provide them.
