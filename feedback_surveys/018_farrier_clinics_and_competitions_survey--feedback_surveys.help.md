# Farrier Clinics And Competitions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from participants in Farrier Clinics And Competitions. Your responses will help us understand your experience with the event, allowing us to improve and refine our future events. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your time in completing this survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review each page carefully and select the corresponding fields that apply to your experience.
2. For rating fields, rate the event on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best reflect your experience.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional feedback" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes (optional).
