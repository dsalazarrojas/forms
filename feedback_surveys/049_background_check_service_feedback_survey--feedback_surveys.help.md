# Background Check Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their experience with the background check service. It helps the organization understand areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name if you wish to provide it.
2. Enter the date when you received service from the background check service.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the service using the 1-5 scale.
4. Answer the following questions about the service:
	* Did you find the turnaround time of the report to be satisfactory? (Good, Fair, Poor)
	* How accurate were the results? (Good, Fair, Poor)
	* How would you rate the communication quality? (Good, Fair, Poor)
5. Indicate if you would recommend this service to others.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the text box.
7. Let us know if you give permission to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1, text, optional): If you wish to provide your name, please enter it here.
* **Date of Service** (2, date, optional): When you received service from us.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (3, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Turnaround Time Satisfaction** (4, select_one, optional): Did you find the turnaround time of the report to be satisfactory? (Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Accuracy of Results** (5, select_one, optional): How accurate were the results? (Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Communication Quality** (6, select_one, optional): How would you rate the communication quality? (Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Would You Recommend This Service** (7, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend this service to others.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (8, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here.
* **Permission to Follow Up** (9, select_one, optional): May we contact you about your feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (10, text, optional): If you have any other feedback or comments, please enter them here.
