# Compassion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compassion Feedback Form is a tool for sharing observations about compassionate behavior, with the goal of promoting positive and supportive actions within the organization. By filling out this form, you can help highlight the impact of kindness and empathy on individuals and situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
2. Enter your name and role/relationship to the subject, if applicable.
3. Describe the date of the event you observed or experienced.
4. Provide a brief summary of what happened, including any context or details that may be helpful.
5. Rate the impact of the compassionate act from 1 to 5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
6. Share any positive outcomes or effects you observed as a result of the compassionate act.
7. If you have any suggestions or recommendations for promoting similar compassionate behavior, feel free to share them.
8. Finally, indicate your permission to share your feedback internally, and provide any contact information if follow-up is needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Feedback Form --**:
	+ This section serves as a header for the form and is not a required field.
* **your name** (id: 2, text, optional):
	+ Your name is an optional field for your identity. You can choose to remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **your role or relationship to subject** (id: 3, text, optional):
	+ Describe your relationship to the person or situation you are providing feedback about.
* **date of observed event** (id: 4, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the event you observed or experienced.
* **description of compassionate act** (id: 5, text, required):
	+ Provide a brief summary of what happened, including any context or details that may be helpful.
* **impact rating** (id: 6, number, required):
	+ Rate the impact of the compassionate act from 1 to 5, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **observed outcomes** (id: 7, text, optional):
	+ Share any positive outcomes or effects you observed as a result of the compassionate act.
* **suggestions to promote compassion** (id: 8, text, optional):
	+ If you have any ideas or recommendations for promoting similar compassionate behavior, share them here.
* **permission to share feedback internally** (id: 9, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate your permission to share your feedback internally, with names removed if requested.
* **contact phone or email** (id: 10, text, optional):
	+ Provide any contact information if follow-up is needed.

# Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing the compassionate act and its impact.
* If you have trouble determining the correct date of the event, please enter the closest date or approximate time period.
* If you wish to remain anonymous, you can do so by leaving your name field blank.
* Remember to keep your suggestions constructive and respectful, and focus on promoting positive behaviors.
* If you have any concerns or questions about this form, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
