# Document Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to understand your experiences and opinions on document management within the company. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and create a more efficient document management system that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. You can select only one option for questions with "select_one" types.
3. For "select_multiple" types, select all applicable options.
4. Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY for questions with "date" type.
5. You can leave any question blank if it's not applicable to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Document Management Suggestion
 * **Document Management Suggestion** (`document_management_survey_form_1`, text, required/optional): Share your general thoughts and ideas on what you think would improve our document management system.

### 2. Document Management Process
 * **Document Management Process** (`document_management_survey_form_2`, text, required/optional): Describe your current document management process and how it works for you.

### 3. Document Management Effectiveness
 * **Document Management Effectiveness** (`document_management_survey_form_3`, select_one, required/optional): How effective is our current document management system for you?
 	+ 'Yes' = Very effective
 	+ 'No' = Not very effective

### 4. Document Management Time Saving
 * **Document Management Time Saving** (`document_management_survey_form_4`, select_one, required/optional): Does our document management system save you time?
 	+ 'Yes' = Yes, it does
 	+ 'No' = Not much

### 5. Document Management Clarity
 * **Document Management Clarity** (`document_management_survey_form_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): How clear is our document management system?
 	+ 'Yes' = Very clear
 	+ 'No' = Not clear

### 6. Document Management Satisfaction
 * **Document Management Satisfaction** (`document_management_survey_form_6`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the document management system (0-10).

### 7. Document Management Frequency
 * **Document Management Frequency** (`document_management_survey_form_7`, date, required/optional): How often do you use our document management system?
 	+ Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY, e.g., 05/01/2022
