# organization_culture_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on various aspects of your organization's culture, climate, and policies. This feedback will help identify areas of strength and areas for improvement, enabling positive change and growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your honest perception of your organization's culture, climate, and policies.
2. Select the options that best reflect your views on each question.
3. You can skip questions if you do not have an opinion or if the question does not apply to your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief introduction or explanation about your feedback.
* **culture_climate**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "I feel comfortable and supported in my work environment."
* **leadership**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "Our leadership is effective and supportive."
* **communication**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "Information is openly and effectively communicated throughout the organization."
* **trust**: (Select multiple options) How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statements:
	+ "Our leaders demonstrate a high level of trustworthiness."
	+ "Our leaders are transparent in their decision-making processes."
* **collaboration**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "We collaborate effectively as a team to achieve our goals."
* **feedback**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "I receive regular and constructive feedback that helps me grow and develop."
* **work_life**: (Select multiple options) How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statements:
	+ "Our work-life balance is supported by the organization."
	+ "I feel that my work-life balance is respected by the organization."
* **growth**: (Select multiple options) How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statements:
	+ "I have opportunities for growth and development within the organization."
	+ "I am able to pursue opportunities for career growth and development."
* **recognition**: (Select multiple options) How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statements:
	+ "I receive recognition for my achievements and contributions."
	+ "I feel valued and appreciated for my contributions."
* **feedback_mechanism**: How strongly do you agree or disagree with the statement "We have a system in place for providing feedback and addressing concerns."
* **open_ended**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience in the organization.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input is valuable and will help create positive change.
* Select the options that best reflect your genuine opinion.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please include them in the "open_ended" field.
