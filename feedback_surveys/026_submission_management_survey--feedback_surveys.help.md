# Submission Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Submission Management Survey is designed to gather feedback on the submission management process, helping us understand your experiences and suggestions for improvement. This form is not required for everyone and is intended to be filled out by those who have submitted content in our system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button, which will guide you through the questions.
2. Fill in the necessary fields with your honest answers and feedback.
3. Once you've filled in all the required fields (User Name, Email, and one of the select fields), click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification or the name you would like to be associated with this feedback. This helps us identify you in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address where we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have any concerns or issues that are best addressed over the phone, please provide your contact number.
* **Select User Type** (`select_user_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of user you are (User, Team Member, or Stakeholder) to help us better understand your perspective on the submission management process.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the submission management process (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied Nor Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, optional): Indicate how easy or hard it was for you to use the submission management process (Very Easy, Somewhat Easy, Neither Easy Nor Hard, Somewhat Hard, Very Hard).
* **Submission Procedure** (`submission_procedure`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you experienced the submission process (Current Procedure, Improved Procedure, New Procedure).
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have on how to improve the submission management process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to finalize your submission.
