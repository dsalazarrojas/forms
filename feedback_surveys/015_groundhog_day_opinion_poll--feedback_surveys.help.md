# Groundhog Day Opinion Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Groundhog Day Opinion Poll is designed to understand your feelings and habits regarding Groundhog Day. By answering this form, you'll help us improve the way we share and celebrate this holiday.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and consider your experiences with Groundhog Day.
2. Fill in the required fields with your honest answers.
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions where possible.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How familiar are you with Groundhog Day?** (`groundhog_day_familiarity`, number, optional): Please rate your level of familiarity with Groundhog Day, from 0 to 100.
- **What are your beliefs about Groundhog Day?** (`groundhog_day_beliefs`, text, optional): Share with us your thoughts about Groundhog Day in a brief message.
- **How much interest do you have in Groundhog Day?** (`groundhog_day_interest`, number, optional): Rate your level of interest in Groundhog Day, from 0 to 100.
- **How much do you enjoy Groundhog Day puns?** (`groundhog_day_pun_intention`, select_one, optional): Choose how much you enjoy Groundhog Day puns.
	* None
	* A little
	* A lot
- **How often do you consume Groundhog Day content?** (`groundhog_day_media_consumption`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate your consumption habits:
	* Never
	* Rarely
	* Occasionally
	* Often
- **Have you learned something about Groundhog Day in school?** (`groundhog_day_education`, select_one, optional): Choose your response.
	* Yes
	* No
- **Have you ever participated in Groundhog Day events?** (`groundhog_day_participation`, select_one, optional): Choose your response.
	* Yes
	* No
- **Do you use Groundhog Day in your media work?** (`groundhog_day_media_usage`, select_one, optional): Choose your response.
	* Yes
	* No
- **Do you organize Groundhog Day events?** (`groundhog_day_organizing`, select_one, optional): Choose your response.
	* Yes
	* No
- **Would you recommend Groundhog Day to others?** (`groundhog_day_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Choose your response.
	* Yes
	* No

Note that this form is designed to be optional and does not require any personal information. Your responses will be kept anonymous and help us improve the way we share and celebrate Groundhog Day.
