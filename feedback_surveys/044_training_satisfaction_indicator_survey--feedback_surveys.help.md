* Gather information about the participant's overall experience during the training
* Identify areas of improvement for future training sessions
* Provide an opportunity for participants to rate their satisfaction with the training

The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the correct rating for your overall experience**: In the "Training Survey Participant Rating" field, choose a rating that best describes your overall experience with the training session. You can select one of the following options:
	* Highly Satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very Dissatisfied
2. **Provide a brief comment about your experience**: In the "Training Survey Comments" field, provide a brief comment about your experience during the training session. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
3. **Select all that apply for satisfaction scale**: In the "Training Satisfaction Scale" field, select all the items that apply to your experience with the training session. You can choose one or more of the following options:

	* The training session fell short of my expectations
	* The training session was average
	* I did not attend the training session
4. **Provide your contact information**: In the "Contact Info" field, enter your name, job title, and department (if applicable).
