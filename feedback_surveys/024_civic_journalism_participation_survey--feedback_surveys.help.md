# Civic Journalism Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in civic journalism initiatives. Civic journalism is a collaborative and innovative approach to journalism that involves citizens and journalists working together to produce high-quality, local reporting. This form helps us understand your role, areas of interest, and preferences for support, allowing us to better understand your needs and provide tailored assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your role as a respondent (Journalist, Citizen, Student, Researcher, or Other) in the "Respondent role" section.
* Choose the media outlet or independent affiliation you are associated with, if applicable, in the "Publication affiliation" section.
* Select up to three areas of reporting interest from the list provided in the "Areas of reporting interest" section.
* Indicate any barriers to participation in civic journalism in the "Barriers to participation" section.
* Specify areas where you would like to receive training or capacity building, if applicable, in the "Training needs" section.
* Answer yes or no to the question regarding interest in collaboration with organizations in the "Interest in collaboration" section.
* Choose any support types you are interested in from the list provided in the "Preferred support types" section, if applicable.
* Provide any feedback on available resources and platforms in the "Feedback on resources" section.
* Answer yes or no to the question regarding consent for follow-up contact in the "Consent for follow-up" section.
* Finally, ensure that the submission date is accurately filled in with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role (Respondent role, select_one)**: Choose how you are involved in civic journalism (Journalist, Citizen, Student, Researcher, or Other).
* **Publication affiliation (Publication affiliation, text)**: If you are associated with a media outlet or are an independent, enter your affiliation here.
* **Areas of reporting interest (Areas of reporting interest, select_multiple)**: Select up to three areas where you would like to report on (Local government, Public safety, Education, Environment, Culture).
* **Barriers to participation (Barriers to participation, text)**: Describe any challenges or obstacles you face when participating in civic journalism.
* **Training needs (Training needs, text)**: Specify areas where you would like to receive training or capacity building, if applicable.
* **Interest in collaboration (Interest in collaboration, select_one)**: Answer yes or no if you are interested in collaborating with organizations.
* **Preferred support types (Preferred support types, select_multiple)**: Choose any support types you are interested in (Training, Grants, Legal assistance, Mentorship).
* **Feedback on resources (Feedback on resources, text)**: Provide any comments on the available resources and platforms.
* **Consent for follow-up (Consent for follow-up, select_one)**: Answer yes or no if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Submission date (Submission date, date)**: Ensure that this field is filled in with the current date.
