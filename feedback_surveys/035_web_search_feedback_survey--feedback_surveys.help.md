# Web Search Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Search Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our web search results. This feedback is crucial in helping us understand how to improve the search functionality and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your satisfaction with our web search results using the scale of 1-5.
2. Choose how often you use our website (if applicable).
3. Share any feedback you have with us.
4. Provide your email address for us to follow up with you (if applicable).
5. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
6. Share any suggestions you have for improving our website.
7. Rate your overall experience with our web search results.
8. Select the device you used to search (e.g., desktop, mobile, etc.).
9. Select your current location (if applicable).
10. Suggest areas of improvement for our web search results.
11. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
12. If you have already provided feedback, you can skip this step. If you want to contact us for follow-up, please use the "Contact us" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with our web search results?** (`user_satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Rate your satisfaction with our web search results on a scale of 1-5.
- **How often do you use our website?** (`search_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Choose how often you use our website, if applicable.
- **Do you have any feedback for us?** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Share any feedback you have with us.
- **What is your email address?** (`email`, Email, Optional): Provide your email address so we can follow up with you (if applicable).
- **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Provide your phone number (if applicable).
- **Do you have any suggestions for improving our website?** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving our website.
- **Please rate your overall experience** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Rate your overall experience with our web search results.
- **What device are you using to search?** (`search_device`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the device you used to search (e.g., desktop, mobile, etc.).
- **What is your current location?** (`search_location`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your current location (if applicable).
- **What areas of improvement would you suggest?** (`improvement`, Select Multiple, Optional): Suggest areas of improvement for our web search results.
- **Additional comments?** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Contact us to follow up on your feedback** (`contact_us`, Note, Optional): If you have already provided feedback, you can skip this step. If you want to contact us for follow-up, use this field.
