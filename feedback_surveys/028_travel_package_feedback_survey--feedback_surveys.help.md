# Travel Package Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from travelers regarding their recent travel packages. The goal is to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement to help us provide better services in the future.

<HR>

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Package Details, Rating, Satisfaction, and Contact Details.
2. Select your preferred answers for the multiple-choice questions, such as Improvement Areas, Service Areas, Tour Operator, Tour Operator 1, Staff, Staff 1.
3. Enter your travel details, such as dates, travel places, travel places 1, and travel people.
4. Select your preferred answers for the multiple-choice questions, such as Tour Operator, Tour Operator 1, and Staff.
5. Fill in the remaining fields with any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Details** (`package_details`, text, required): Enter the name of the travel package you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Select the rating of your experience (Good or Fair).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your experience, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas that made you satisfied.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Specify the areas that need improvement.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our services.
* **Service Areas** (`service_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of service you received during your travel package.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other contact information.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Other Contact Information** (`contact_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): Enter the dates of your travel package.
* **Travel Dates 1** (`travel_dates_1`, date, required): Enter the dates of your travel package 1.
* **Travel Dates 2** (`travel_dates_2`, date, required): Enter the dates of your travel package 2.
* **Travel Places** (`travel_places`, text, required): Enter the travel places you visited during your package.
* **Travel Places 1** (`travel_places_1`, text, required): Enter the travel places 1 you visited during your package.
* **Travel Places 2** (`travel_places_2`, text, required): Enter the travel places 2 you visited during your package.
* **Travel People** (`travel_people`, text, required): Enter the travel people you met during your package.
* **Travel People 1** (`travel_people_1`, text, required): Enter the travel people 1 you met during your package.
* **Travel People 2** (`travel_people_2`, text, required): Enter the travel people 2 you met during your package.
* **Tour Operator** (`tour_operator`, select_one, required): Select whether you had a tour operator during your package.
* **Tour Operator 1** (`tour_operator_1`, select_one, required): Select whether you had a tour operator 1 during your package.
* **Staff** (`staff`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you had a staff member during your package.
* **Staff 1** (`staff_1`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you had a staff member 1 during your package.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select whether you are submitting this feedback form.
