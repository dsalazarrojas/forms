# Grilled Wings Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Grilled Wings Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers on the quality and service of our grilled wings product. This form will help us understand how our customers perceive our product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, providing your feedback on the taste, quality, appearance, and service of our grilled wings.
2. Select one of the options for each question (e.g., "Very Satisfied" for "Are You Satisfied").
3. If you would like to recommend our product, select "True" or "False" accordingly.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Suggestion" field.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

• **Rate The Taste** (`rating_taste`, `number`, optional): Rate the taste of the grilled wing on a scale of 1-5.
