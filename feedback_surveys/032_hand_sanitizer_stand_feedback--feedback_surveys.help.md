# Hand Sanitizer Stand Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hand Sanitizer Stand Feedback form is designed to collect user feedback on the hand sanitizer stand in the facility. This information will help us understand how to improve the services and experience for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Next" and "Back" buttons. Enter your feedback in each field, and don't forget to review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How Often Is It Used
* **How Often Is It Used** (`how_usualy_used`, `number`, optional): This field is used to gauge the frequency of use of the hand sanitizer stand. Please enter a numerical value to indicate how often the stand is used.
### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the hand sanitizer stand. Enter a brief description of your feelings.
### Improvement Suggestions
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the hand sanitizer stand, enter them here.
### Hygiene Management
* **Hygiene Management** (`hygiene_management`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the hand sanitizer stand's hygiene management from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your feedback.
* You can navigate through the form using the "Next" and "Back" buttons.
* All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form.
