# Disability Friendly Community Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from community members on accessibility needs, challenges, and suggestions for improvement to create a more inclusive and accessible environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the space provided at the end of the form.
4. Make sure to save your progress as you go through the form, as you will not be able to go back and change your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed. This can be your name, email address, or phone number.
* **What type of accessibility needs do you have in this city or community?** (`access_needs`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand the types of accessibility needs you may have.
* **Are there any barriers that prevent you from accessing community facilities or services?** (`access_barriers`, text, not required): Describe any barriers you have experienced or expect when accessing community facilities or services.
* **What challenges have you experienced or do you expect when accessing community facilities or services?** (`access_challenges`, text, not required): Share your experiences or concerns about accessing community facilities or services.
* **Do you have any ideas for improving accessibility in our city or community?** (`ideas_for_improvement`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving accessibility, please share them with us.
* **Which facilities or services do you use in our city or community?** (`community_facilities_services`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand which services you use and how we can improve them.
* **What is your experience with accessibility in our city or community?** (`access_experience`, text, not required): Share your overall experience with accessibility in our city or community.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback for us?** (`feedback`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use this space to share them with us.
