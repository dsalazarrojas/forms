# Newsletter Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the satisfaction of the newsletter and its components. Your responses will help us understand how to improve the newsletter and provide a better experience for our subscribers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your satisfaction with the newsletter, its content, design, and overall experience.
2. Choose your preferred method of receiving the newsletter.
3. Provide your contact information (email, phone, and name) if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
4. Answer any additional questions about your experience with the newsletter.
5. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How Satisfying is Your Newsletter** (question_one): Rate your satisfaction with the newsletter on a scale of very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **What is the Main Reason for Your Score** (question_two): Provide a brief explanation of why you gave your score.
- **Would You Like to Continue Receiving Newsletter** (question_three): Indicate if you would like to continue receiving the newsletter and why.
- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the newsletter.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
- **Name** (name): Provide your name.
- **Company** (company): Provide your company name (if applicable).
- **Job Title** (job_title): Provide your job title (if applicable).
- **Department** (department): Provide your department name (if applicable).
- **Job Description** (job_description): Provide a brief description of your job (if applicable).
- **How Did You Get the Newsletter** (how_did_you_get_the_newsletter): Choose how you got the newsletter (email, social media, print, online search, or other).
- **What is the Source of Your Newsletter** (what_is_the_source_of_your_newsletter): Choose where you got the newsletter (company newsletter, online source, online search, or other).
- **When Were You Subscribed** (when_were_you_subscribed): Enter the date you subscribed to the newsletter.
- **Frequency of Newsletter Reciepts** (frequency_of_newsletter_reciepts): Choose how often you receive the newsletter (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, never, rarely).
- **How Many Newsletters Have You Received** (how_many_newsletters_have_you_received): Enter the number of newsletters you have received.
- **How Satisfying is the Content** (how_satisfying_is_the_content): Rate your satisfaction with the content of the newsletter on a scale of very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **How Satisfying is the Design** (how_satisfying_is_the_design): Rate your satisfaction with the design of the newsletter on a scale of very satisfying, somewhat satisfying, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **How Satisfying is the Frequency** (how_satisfying_is_the_frequency): Rate your satisfaction with the frequency of the newsletter on a scale of very satisfying, somewhat satisfying, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **How Satisfying is the Newsletter** (how_satisfying_is_the_newsletter): Rate your overall satisfaction with the newsletter on a scale of very satisfying, somewhat satisfying, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **How Satisfying is Your Relationship with the Newsletter** (how_satisfying_is_your_relationship_with_the_newsletter): Rate your satisfaction with the relationship you have with the newsletter on a scale of very satisfying, somewhat satisfying, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
- **How Usful is Your Time** (how_usful_is_your_time): Rate how useful you find the time spent reading the newsletter on a scale of very usful, somewhat usful, not usful, or not at all usful.
- **How Usful is Your Investment** (how_usful_is_your_investiment): Rate how useful you find your investment in the newsletter on a scale of very usful, somewhat usful, not usful, or not at all usful.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction): Rate your overall satisfaction with the newsletter on a scale of very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied, or not at all satisfied.
