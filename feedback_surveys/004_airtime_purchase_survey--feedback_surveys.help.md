# Airtime Purchase Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers who have purchased airtime. The goal is to understand customer experiences, preferences, and satisfaction with the purchase process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select one option for each question, even if you are unsure.
3. Be honest and provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
