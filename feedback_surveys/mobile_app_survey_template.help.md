# mobile_app_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on the mobile app experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select how satisfied you are with the mobile app on the "How satisfied are you with the mobile app?" field.
2. Choose how often you use the mobile app on the "How often do you use the mobile app?" field.
3. Select all that apply from the list to describe your experience with the app on the "Which of the following describes your experience with the app?" field.
4. Choose the primary reason you use the mobile app on the "What is the primary reason you use the mobile app?" field.
5. Type any additional comments or improvements you'd like to suggest on the "What would you improve or change about the mobile app?" field.
6. If you have any further comments or feedback, please type them on the "Do you have any additional comments or feedback?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the mobile app?** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): This field measures your overall satisfaction with the app. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How often do you use the mobile app?** (Daily, Multiple times a week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never): This field asks about your usage frequency. Select the option that reflects how often you use the app.
* **Which of the following describes your experience with the app?** (Select all that apply):
  * **Easy to use**: The app feels effortless to use.
  * **Fast**: The app loads quickly and responds promptly.
  * **Reliable**: The app works consistently without issues.
  * **Intuitive**: The app is easy to understand and navigate.
  * **Lacks features**: The app is missing important features.
  * **Confusing**: The app is hard to understand.
  * **Buggy**: The app has technical issues.
  Choose all that apply if your experience with the app falls under any of these categories.
* **What is the primary reason you use the mobile app?** (Convenience, Specific feature, Notifications, Habit, Other): This field asks about your primary reason for using the app. Select the option that best describes your motivation.
* **What would you improve or change about the mobile app?** (Text field): Type any specific features or functionalities you'd like to see added or improved in the app.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (Text field): If you have any further comments or suggestions, type them here.
