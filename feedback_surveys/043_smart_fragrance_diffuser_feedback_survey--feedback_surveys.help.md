# Smart Fragrance Diffuser Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Fragrance Diffuser Feedback Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Smart Fragrance Diffuser product. This form helps us understand your satisfaction with the product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your experience with the Smart Fragrance Diffuser.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "feedback_comments" field.
4. Enter your email address and contact number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Satisfied** (`user_satisfaction`): How satisfied are you with the product? 
  • Satisfied: You are satisfied with the product.
  • Dissatisfied: You are not satisfied with the product.

* **Feature Usage** (`feature_usage`): Which features have you used on the product? 
  • Yes: You have used this feature.
  • No: You have not used this feature.

* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`): Do you have any ideas for improvements? 
  • Yes: You have ideas for improvement.
  • No: You do not have ideas for improvement.

* **Product Development** (`product_development`): Are you interested in product development? 
  • Yes: You are interested in product development.
  • No: You are not interested in product development.

* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`): How would you rate your experience with the product?
  • Yes: You are satisfied with your experience.
  • No: You are not satisfied with your experience.

* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product. 
  • Satisfied: Satisfied.
  • Dissatisfied: Dissatisfied.

* **Time Used** (`time_used`): How much time did you spend using the product?
  • Yes: You used a lot of time with the product.
  • No: You did not use a lot of time with the product.

* **Time Saved** (`time_saved`): How much time did you save using the product?
  • Yes: You saved a lot of time.
  • No: You did not save a lot of time.

* **Product Features** (`product_features`): Which product features have you used? 
  • Yes: You used this feature.
  • No: You did not use this feature.

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `product_name2`, `product_name3`, `product_name4`, `product_name5`, `product_name6`, `product_name7`, `product_name8`, `product_name9`, `product_name10`): This field is for entering the name of the product you are providing feedback on. Please enter the name of the product.

* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.

* **Email Address** (`email_address`): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, please enter your email address.

* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, please enter your contact number.

* **Product Category** (`product_category`): Please select the category of the product you are providing feedback on. 
  • Yes: You are familiar with the product category.
  • No: You are not familiar with the product category.

* **Product Type** (`product_type`): Please select the type of product you are providing feedback on. 
  • Yes: You are familiar with the product type.
  • No: You are not familiar with the product type.

* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`): Please select the customer segment you belong to. 
  • Yes: You belong to this customer segment.
  • No: You do not belong to this customer segment.
