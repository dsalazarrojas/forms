# Tilling Specialist Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients regarding their experiences with our Tilling Specialists. It helps us understand what we do well and what we can improve on to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name, which is the name of the person completing the form.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you if we need more information.
3. Provide your Phone number, which will be used to contact you about your feedback.
4. Choose a Date of Service from the calendar to indicate when you received our services.
5. Select the type of service you received from us: Landscaping, Construction, or Both.
6. Choose the location where we worked for you: Residential, Commercial, or Both.
7. Select the status of your job: Completed, Ongoing, or Pending.
8. If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our services, type them in the box. (Optional)
9. If you have any additional comments, type them in the box. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (contact_name)**: A field for you to enter your full name.
* **Email (contact_email)**: A field for you to enter your email address.
* **Phone (contact_phone)**: A field for you to enter your phone number.
* **Date of Service (service_date)**: A field for you to select the date of service from the calendar.
* **Service Type (service_type)**: A field to select the type of service you received from us.
* **Work Location (work_location)**: A field to select the location where we worked for you.
* **Job Status (job_status)**: A field to select the status of your job.
* **What can we improve on? (feedback_question)**: A field for you to suggest improvements to our services (Optional).
* **Additional Comments (comment)**: A field for you to provide any additional comments about your experience (Optional).
