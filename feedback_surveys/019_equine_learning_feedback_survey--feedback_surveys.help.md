# Equine Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect feedback from participants to help us improve our equine learning program. Your input will help us identify areas of success and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant options for each question based on your experience.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, especially in the text fields.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional comments" field.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Was the equine learning session helpful** (`first_session`):
	+ Choose "Session was helpful" if the session met your expectations.
	+ Choose "Session was somewhat helpful" if the session met some of your expectations.
	+ Choose "Session was not helpful" if the session did not meet your expectations.
* **Instructor's performance** (`instructor_performance`):
	+ Choose "Excellent" if the instructor was very competent and effective.
	+ Choose "Good" if the instructor was somewhat competent and effective.
	+ Choose "Poor" if the instructor was not very effective.
	+ Choose "Very poor" if the instructor was not effective at all.
* **Quality of instruction** (`instruction`):
	+ Enter a numerical value to rate the quality of instruction.
* **Facilities and equipment** (`facilities`):
	+ Enter a numerical value to rate the facilities and equipment.
* **Materials and resources** (`materials`):
	+ Choose "Excellent" if the materials and resources were very effective.
	+ Choose "Good" if the materials and resources were somewhat effective.
	+ Choose "Poor" if the materials and resources were not very effective.
	+ Choose "Very poor" if the materials and resources were not effective at all.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`):
	+ Choose "Very good" if the overall experience was very positive.
	+ Choose "Somewhat good" if the overall experience was somewhat positive.
	+ Choose "Neutral" if the overall experience was neutral.
	+ Choose "Somewhat bad" if the overall experience was somewhat negative.
	+ Choose "Very bad" if the overall experience was very negative.
* **Will you recommend this equine learning session** (`will_recommend`):
	+ Choose "True" if you would recommend this session to others.
	+ Choose "False" if you would not recommend this session to others.
* **Session date** (`session_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the equine learning session.
* **Session time** (`session_time`):
	+ Enter the time of the equine learning session.
* **Session duration** (`session_duration`):
	+ Enter the duration of the equine learning session.
* **Additional comments** (`note`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about the session.
* **Participant email** (`email`):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant name** (`name`):
	+ Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Program name** (`program`):
	+ Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **Session number** (`session_number`):
	+ Enter the number of the session you attended.
* **How often do you attend equine learning sessions** (`feedback_frequency`):
	+ Choose "Once or twice" if you have attended equine learning sessions one or two times.
	+ Choose "More than once" if you have attended equine learning sessions more than two times.
	+ Choose "Never" if you have not attended equine learning sessions.
	+ Choose "Do not know" if you are not sure about your attendance frequency.
* **Have you attended other equine learning sessions** (`attended_other_sessions`):
	+ Choose "True" if you have attended other equine learning sessions.
	+ Choose "False" if you have not attended other equine learning sessions.
	+ Choose "Do not know" if you are not sure about your attendance.
* **Will you attend other equine learning sessions** (`will_attend_other_sessions`):
	+ Choose "True" if you plan to attend other equine learning sessions.
	+ Choose "False" if you do not plan to attend other equine learning sessions.
	+ Choose "Do not know" if you are not sure about your future attendance.
* **Why do you plan to attend other equine learning sessions** (`other_sessions`):
	+ Enter the reasons you plan to attend other equine learning sessions.
* **Instructor qualifications** (`instructor_qualifications`):
	+ Enter any comments about the instructor's qualifications.
* **Comments on the instructor** (`instructor_feedback`):
	+ Enter any comments about the instructor.
* **Facility condition** (`facility_condition`):
	+ Enter any comments about the facility's condition.
* **Facility location** (`facility_location`):
	+ Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility rating** (`facility_rating`):
	+ Enter a numerical value to rate the facility.
