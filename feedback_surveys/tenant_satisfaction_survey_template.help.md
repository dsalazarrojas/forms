# Tenant_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from tenants to help property management or landlords improve the living experience and address any concerns or issues they may be facing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions by selecting one of the provided options.
2. Select all that apply for the "Which aspects of the property or community are most important to you?" question.
3. Write your answers to the open-ended questions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your living experience as a tenant?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with your living experience.
* **How would you rate communication from property management/landlord?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This question assesses your satisfaction with the communication you receive from property management or the landlord.
* **How satisfied are you with the responsiveness to maintenance requests?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`, `Not applicable`): This question helps us gauge the effectiveness of maintenance request handling.
* **Which aspects of the property or community are most important to you?** (`Cleanliness`, `Safety`, `Quietness`, `Amenities` (gym, pool), `Parking`, `Location`, `Neighborliness`): Select all that apply to indicate which aspects of the property or community are most important to you.
* **How likely are you to recommend this property to a friend or colleague?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): This question helps us understand how likely you are to recommend the property to others.
* **What could significantly improve your living experience here?** (text field): Write your thoughts on what could improve your living experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (text field): Share any other ideas or concerns you may have.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select all that apply for the "Which aspects of the property or community are most important to you?" question.
* Keep your answers clear and concise in the text fields.
