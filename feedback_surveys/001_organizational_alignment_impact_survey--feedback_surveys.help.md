# Organizational Alignment Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from respondents about their perceptions of team structure, leadership, and other factors that impact performance. The information gathered through this form will help identify areas of strength and weakness within the organization and inform future development and improvement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction (page 1) to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Answer each question in the best way you can, based on your honest opinions and experiences.
3. Select the most appropriate option for each question, or enter a numerical value if required.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (`intro`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form. Read it carefully before proceeding.

- **Team-Structure** (`team_structure`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No". This question asks about your perception of the current team structure.

- **Alignment Leads to Performance** (`alignment_leads_to_performance`, number, optional): This question asks for a numerical value that indicates the degree to which you believe alignment impacts performance.

- **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience with the team.

- **Organizational Culture** (`organizational_culture`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the options: "Yes" or "No". This question asks about your perception of the organization's culture.

- **Communication** (`communication`, time, optional): This question asks for an estimated time value that indicates how well you feel communication is happening within the team.

- **Leadership** (`leadership`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options: "Yes" or "No". This question asks about your perception of leadership within the team.

- **Performance Goals** (`performance_goals`, number, optional): This question asks for a numerical value that indicates how well you feel the team's performance goals are aligned.

- **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the options: "Yes" or "No". This question asks about the positive and negative impacts you feel on your work or experience.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your answers.
- Take your time to answer each question carefully.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
