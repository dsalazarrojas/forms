# Toolbox Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback about the toolbox meeting from attendees. This feedback will help the meeting organizers and safety leaders to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your meeting experience using the Meeting Rating field.
2. If you have any safety concerns, please type them in the Safety Concerns field.
3. Share your safety suggestions for improvement in the Safety Suggestions field.
4. Indicate whether you think the meeting has improvement areas by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each area in the Improvement Areas field.
5. Write any additional meeting notes you want to share in the Meeting Notes field.
6. Tell us whether you think there were safety leaders present at the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Safety Leaders field.
7. Let us know whether the safety equipment was used during the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each item in the Safety Equipment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Rating** (`meeting_rating`, number, required): Rate your meeting experience from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, optional): If you have any safety concerns, please type them here.
* **Safety Suggestions** (`safety_suggestion`, text, optional): Share your ideas for improvement.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas that need improvement:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional meeting notes.
* **Safety Leaders** (`safety_leaders`, select_one, optional): Were there safety leaders present?:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Were the following safety equipment used during the meeting?
	+ Yes
	+ No
