# Senior Home Delivery Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Home Delivery Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from recipients about their experience with the home delivery service. This feedback is crucial for the delivery service management to understand their strengths and weaknesses, allowing them to make data-driven decisions to improve their service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant option that best matches your experience and feelings about the delivery service.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Feedback from Recipient
* **Feedback from recipient** (`Recipient feedback`): Provide feedback about your experience with the delivery service.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Not required

### 2. Satisfaction with Delivery Service
* **Satisfaction with delivery service** (`Delivery service satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the delivery service.
	+ Type: Select one from the options
	+ Required: Not required

### 3. Satisfaction with Meal Quality
* **Satisfaction with meal quality** (`Meal quality satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the meal quality.
	+ Type: Select multiple from the options
	+ Required: Not required

### 4. Additional Comments
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the delivery service.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Not required

### 5. Recipient Information
* **Recipient name** (`Recipient name`): Enter your name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Not required
* **Recipient email** (`Recipient email`): Enter your email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Not required

### 6. Caregiver Information

### 7. Delivery Information
