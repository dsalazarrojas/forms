# Exam Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from examinees about their experience with the exam process. Your response will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure a better experience for all future examinees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select one of the options provided for the "overall_satisfaction" field.
3. You can leave the "category_id" field blank if it's not applicable.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
5. You can add any comments you'd like to share in the "exam_comments" field.
6. Finally, please enter your phone number in the "phone" field if you wish to be contacted via phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exam_process_feedback_survey** (text, required): Please describe your experience with the exam process, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **overall_satisfaction** (select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your overall experience with the exam process? Choose one of the options: "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Somewhat dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
* **category_id** (number, optional): If applicable, please enter your category ID number.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **exam_comments** (note, optional): Please leave any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience with the exam process.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your experience with the exam process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback will help us improve the exam process for future examinees, so please take a few minutes to complete this survey honestly.
