# Microfinance Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on microfinance awareness, interest, and understanding from end users. It's meant to gauge how well users are informed about microfinance services and their benefits. The collected data will help us better understand our audience's needs and improve our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Full Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field.
3. Select all that apply from the **Microfinance Awareness** field, which asks about the services you are aware of.
4. Answer how **aware you are of microfinance services** by selecting a score between 0 and 100 (0 being not aware at all, 100 being very aware).
5. Choose the **benefits** you think microfinance services provide from the list (you can select one).
6. Rate your **interest in using microfinance services** by selecting a score between 0 and 100 (0 being not interested at all, 100 being very interested).
7. Let us know how you **came across this form** by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
- **Microfinance Awareness**: Select all the microfinance services you are aware of. These services include access to credit for small business owners, mobile money, and microfinance services.
- **How aware are you of microfinance services?**: Answer this question by selecting a score between 0 and 100, where 0 means you are not aware at all and 100 means you are very aware.
- **What benefits do you think microfinance services provide?**: Choose one benefit you think microfinance services offer. You can select only one benefit from the list.
- **How interested are you in using microfinance services?**: Rate your interest in microfinance services by selecting a score between 0 and 100, where 0 means you are not interested at all and 100 means you are very interested.
- **How did you come across this form?**: Select the correct option to let us know how you found this form.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date to ensure you receive any follow-up communication.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and feedback!
