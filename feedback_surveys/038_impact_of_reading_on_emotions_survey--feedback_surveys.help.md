# Impact Of Reading On Emotions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your reading habits and the emotional impact of reading on you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Select the correct options for each question.
- If you're unsure about a question, use the provided options as a guide.
- You can add any additional comments or questions you have in the "Additional Comments" and "Additional Questions" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **introduction**: This is a note field where you can add any relevant information about your reading habits before answering the actual questions.
- **reading-habits** (ID 2):
	* Label: reading-habits
	* Select "Yes" if you regularly read for pleasure or for work.
	* Select "No" if you don't read regularly.
- **reading-impact** (ID 3):
	* Label: reading-impact
	* Select "Yes" if you feel that reading has a positive impact on your emotions.
	* Select "No" if you don't feel a significant impact.
- **reading-frequency** (ID 4):
	* Label: reading-frequency
	* Enter the number of times you read per week.
- **reading-duration** (ID 5):
	* Label: reading-duration
	* Enter the number of minutes you typically spend reading per session.
- **reading-motivation** (ID 6):
	* Label: reading-motivation
	* Select "Yes" if you read to relax, escape or explore knowledge.
	* Select "No" if you read for other reasons.
- **emotional-motivation** (ID 7):
	* Label: emotional-motivation
	* Select "Yes" if you read to manage or regulate your emotions.
	* Select "No" if you don't read for this reason.
- **reading-emotional-effect** (ID 8):
	* Label: reading-emotional-effect
	* Select "Yes" if you experience a positive emotional impact from reading (e.g., feeling happier, less stressed).
	* Select "No" if you don't experience a positive emotional impact.
- **reading-emotional-balance** (ID 9):
	* Label: reading-emotional-balance
	* Select "Yes" if you feel reading helps you balance your emotions.
	* Select "No" if you don't feel reading helps you balance your emotions.
- **reading-frequency-amount** (ID 10):
	* Label: reading-frequency-amount
	* Enter the number of minutes you read per session.
- **overall-satisfaction** (ID 11):
	* Label: overall-satisfaction
	* Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with reading.
- **additional-comments** (ID 12):
	* Label: additional-comments
	* Enter any additional comments or thoughts about your reading habits and experience.
- **additional-questions** (ID 13):
	* Label: additional-questions
	* Enter any questions or concerns you have about the survey or reading in general.

## Tips
- Please respond honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
- This survey is designed to gather anonymous and aggregated data, so your responses will not be traced back to you.
- Thank you for participating in this survey!
