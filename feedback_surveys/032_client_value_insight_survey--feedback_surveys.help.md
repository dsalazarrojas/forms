# Client Value Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience with our services, gather insights on the value you've received, and identify areas for improvement. Please answer the questions honestly, and your input will contribute to making our services better tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with your information and answer each question truthfully.
2. For questions asking for ratings or select options, choose the best fitting answer from the provided options.
3. For questions asking for free-form text, provide specific and detailed answers.
4. Make sure to submit the form to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name or Company (3)**: Enter your company name or the name of the client you're representing.
* **Your Name (4)**: Enter your name to be associated with the submitted feedback.
* **Email Address (5)**: Provide your email address for any follow-up communication or surveys.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion. (Optional)
* **How long have you been a client? (8)**: Choose the duration of your relationship with us to give us context on your experience.
* **How frequently do you use our services? (9)**: Select the frequency of your interaction with our services to help us understand your usage patterns.
* **Overall Perceived Value (11)**: Choose how valuable you think our services are to you.
* **Value Rating (12)**: Rate the value you receive from our services on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Value Received vs. Expectations (13)**: Select how well your expectations are met by our services.
* **Value Justifies the Investment (14)**: Choose how strongly you agree or disagree that our services justify the investment or cost.
* **Overall Satisfaction (16)**: Choose how satisfied you are with our services.
* **Satisfaction Rating (17)**: Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Services Meet Expectations (18)**: Select how well our services meet or exceed your expectations.
* **Solution Effectiveness (19)**: Choose how effective our solutions are for your needs.
* **Quality of Service (21)**: Rate the quality of our service or product.
* **Consistency (22)**: Select how consistent our quality is across all interactions.
* **Support Quality (23)**: Choose the quality of support you received from us.
* **Primary Benefits (25)**: Select all the benefits you've received from our services. (You can select multiple options.)
* **Measurable Results (26)**: Provide specific numbers or details on any measurable results or improvements achieved.
* **Business Impact (27)**: Describe how this has impacted your business or goals.
* **Feedback and Improvements (29)**: Provide any feedback or suggestions for areas we can improve.
* **Loyalty (30)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend us on a scale from 0 to 10.
