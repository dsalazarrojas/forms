# Coaching Session Client Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients after a coaching session. The feedback will be used by the coach to understand the client's experience and improve their practice.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the field labeled "Your Name".
2. Enter the name of your coach in the field labeled "Coach Name".
3. Enter the date of the session in the field labeled "Session Date".
4. Answer the questions on the session's quality, the coach's performance, and other aspects of the session.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the field labeled "Additional Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, required): This is where you enter your name. Please fill it in with your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, required): This is where you enter the name of your coach. Please fill it in with the coach's name as you know it.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, required): This is where you enter the date of the session. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Overall Session Rating** (`overall_session_rating`, required): This is where you rate the session overall. Please choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Coach Preparation** (`coach_preparation`, required): This is where you rate the coach's preparation. Please choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Relevance to Your Goals** (`relevance_to_goals`, required): This is where you rate the relevance of the session to your goals. Please choose one of the following options: Very relevant, Relevant, Somewhat relevant, Not relevant.
* **Session Pace** (`session_pace`, required): This is where you rate the pace of the session. Please choose one of the following options: Too fast, Good pace, Too slow.
* **Coach Listening Skills** (`listening_skills`, required): This is where you rate the coach's listening skills. Please choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Clarity of Instruction** (`clarity_of_instruction`, required): This is where you rate the clarity of the coach's instructions. Please choose one of the following options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear.
* **Coach Empathy** (`coach_empathy`, required): This is where you rate the coach's empathy. Please choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Value for Your Time** (`value_for_time`, required): This is where you rate the value of the session for your time. Please choose one of the following options: Excellent value, Good value, Adequate value, Poor value.
* **New Skills or Insights Learned** (`new_skills_learned`, optional): This is an optional field where you can list any new skills or insights you learned during the session.
* **Clear Action Items** (`action_items`, required): This is where you rate the clarity of the next steps. Please choose one of the following options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear.
* **What could the Coach Improve** (`coach_improvements`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide constructive feedback on how the coach could improve.
* **What could Improve the Session** (`session_improvements`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide suggestions for better sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
