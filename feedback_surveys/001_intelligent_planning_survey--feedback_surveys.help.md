# Intelligent Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intelligent Planning Survey is designed to gather user feedback and insights to help the organization improve its services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to answer the following questions. Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise response.
3. If a question requires a numerical value, please enter a whole number.
4. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
5. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What**: What would you like to share with us about your experience or thoughts? (text)
	+ This field is for any general comments or questions you may have.
* **When**: When would you like to schedule your appointment with us? (date)
	+ Please enter the date you are available to schedule an appointment.
* **Where**: Where would you like to receive our services? (select one)
	+ Select where you would prefer to receive our services.
* **Why**: Why do you think our service is important to you? (text)
	+ Please share your thoughts on why our service is important to you.
* **How**: How would rate your satisfaction with our service? (number)
	+ Rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-10.
* **What is your opinion**: What is your opinion about our service? (number)
	+ Please rate your opinion about our service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Select all that apply**: Select all the options that apply to your experience with us. (select multiple)
	+ Check all the options that are relevant to your experience.
* **Time**: When would you be available to schedule an appointment? (time)
	+ Please enter the time you are available to schedule an appointment.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted. (email)
	+ This field is optional and only used to contact you for follow-up questions.
