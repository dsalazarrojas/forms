# AI Tutor Feedback And Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback about the tutoring session, which will help us improve the quality of our services. It also gives you the opportunity to enter a raffle with a chance to win a prize.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, the date of the tutoring session, and rate your overall satisfaction, perceived learning progress, and usefulness of the feedback provided.
2. If you have any suggestions for the tutor's improvement, you can provide them in the "Suggestions for tutor improvement" field.
3. Select whether you want to enter the raffle by choosing "Yes, enter me" or "No, do not enter me" from the "Consent to enter raffle" dropdown menu.
4. If you are consenting to enter the raffle, you will be asked to provide your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Finally, if you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner name** (`learner_name`, text, optional): This field is for providing your name.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the tutoring session.
* **Tutor name** (`tutor_name`, text, optional): This field is optional, but if you feel it's relevant to your feedback, you can fill it in.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the tutor, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Usefulness of feedback** (`usefulness_of_feedback`, number, required): Rate the usefulness of the feedback provided, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Perceived learning progress** (`learning_progress`, number, required): Rate your perceived learning progress, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Suggestions for tutor improvement** (`suggestions_for_tutor`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for the tutor's improvement, you can provide them here.
* **Consent to enter raffle** (`raffle_entry_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you want to enter the raffle by choosing "Yes, enter me" or "No, do not enter me".
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you are consenting to enter the raffle, provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
