# Retail Promotional Campaign Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for gathering feedback on retail promotional campaigns. It is designed to gauge the effectiveness of the campaign's performance, customer preference, and overall awareness among the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page and read through each question carefully.
2. For the text fields (1, 6, and 14), provide a clear and concise answer in the space provided.
3. For the select_one fields (2, 4, 7, and 13), select the best answer from the provided options.
4. For the select_multiple fields (3, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12), select all that apply.
5. Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Promotional Performance** (`1`, `text`, `Optional`): Please express your opinion about the campaign's overall performance.
* **Promotional Awareness** (`2`, `select_one`, `Optional`): How would you rate the level of awareness about this campaign among our target audience? Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Customer Preference** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What are the top preferences for our customers for this campaign? Select Price, Discount, Quality, and/or Style.
* **Awareness Of Campaign** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): How would you rate the level of awareness among the target audience? Select Very High, High, Medium, and/or Low.
* **Performance Of Campaign** (`5`, `number`, `Optional`): What is your opinion about the campaign's performance? Enter a number value.
* **Target Audience** (`6`, `text`, `Optional`): Describe the target audience demographics.
* **Customer Service** (`7`, `select_one`, `Optional`): How would you rate the quality of customer service provided? Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Campaign Duration** (`8`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What is the ideal duration for this campaign? Select 1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 4-6 weeks, and/or Over 6 weeks.
* **Campaign Frequency** (`9`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What is the optimal frequency for this campaign? Select Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, and/or Monthly.
* **Retail Store Location** (`10`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What is the best retail store location? Select Online, Physical Store, and/or Both.
* **Retail Store Hours** (`11`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What are the best retail store hours? Select 24/7, Monday - Friday, Saturday - Sunday, and/or Weekdays - Weekends.
* **Retail Store Staff** (`12`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): What is the ideal number of retail store staff? Select Few, Medium, and/or A lot.
* **Customer Feedback** (`13`, `email`, `Optional`): Please enter your email address to receive any feedback or updates about this campaign.
* **Additional Comments** (`14`, `text`, `Optional`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about this campaign.
