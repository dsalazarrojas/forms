# travel_website_customer_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer feedback survey designed to collect information about your travel experience on our website. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and make your future visits more enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. You will be directed to the first page of the form, titled "Feedback Form".
3. On the "Feedback Form" page, provide any comments or suggestions you have about your travel experience.
4. When finished, click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
5. On the "Travel Booking Details" page, enter any relevant details about your booking, such as dates, destinations, and travel agency used.
6. When finished, click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
7. On the "Satisfaction Rating" page, rate your satisfaction with our services, using the available rating options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (page_1, text, required): This field is where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have about your travel experience. Please be as specific as possible to help us improve our services.
* **Travel Booking Details** (page_2, text, required): This field is where you can enter any relevant details about your booking, such as dates, destinations, and travel agency used. Please make sure to include as much information as possible to help us understand your experience.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (page_3, text, required): This field is where you can rate your satisfaction with our services. Please select one of the available rating options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
