# Arrival Confirmation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the check-in process, overall experience, and identify areas for improvement to enhance the visitor experience.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability.
*   Please select the options that best match your experience.
*   Be honest and provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (Name)**: Enter your name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
*   **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive a follow-up email with your results.
*   **Survey Date (Survey Date)**: Enter the date of your visit.
*   **Arrival Location (Arrival Location)**: Enter the location where you arrived (e.g., office, facility, etc.)
*   **How Punctual was your Arrival (How punctual was your arrival)**: Choose how punctual you were when you arrived.
    +   **On time**: You arrived exactly when you were expected.
    +   **Early**: You arrived earlier than expected.
    +   **Slightly late - under 10 minutes**: You arrived a bit late, but less than 10 minutes ahead of schedule.
    +   **Late - over 10 minutes**: You arrived more than 10 minutes later than expected.
*   **Check-In Process Ease (Check-In Process Ease)**: Rate the ease of the check-in process.
    +   **Very easy**: The process was quick and effortless.
    +   **Easy**: The process was straightforward, but not extremely quick.
    +   **Neutral**: The process was average and not remarkable either way.
    +   **Difficult**: The process was challenging and took some effort.
    +   **Very difficult**: The process was extremely difficult and time-consuming.
*   **Time Spent on Check-In (Time Spent on Check-In)**: Choose the time spent on check-in.
    +   **Less than 5 minutes**: You completed the check-in process in under 5 minutes.
    +   **5-10 minutes**: You spent between 5-10 minutes on check-in.
    +   **10-15 minutes**: You spent between 10-15 minutes on check-in.
    +   **More than 15 minutes**: You spent over 15 minutes on check-in.
*   **Staff Helpfulness (Staff Helpfulness During Check-In)**: Rate the helpfulness of staff during check-in.
    +   **Very helpful**: The staff were extremely helpful and assisted you promptly.
    +   **Helpful**: The staff were helpful and assisted you.
    +   **Neutral**: The staff were not particularly helpful or unhelpful.
    +   **Unhelpful**: The staff were not helpful.
    +   **Very unhelpful**: The staff were unhelpful and did not assist you.
*   **Wayfinding and Signage Clarity (Ease of finding your way)**: Rate the ease of finding your way around the facility.
    +   **Very clear**: The wayfinding was very easy and clear.
    +   **Clear**: The wayfinding was easy and straightforward.
    +   **Neutral**: The wayfinding was average.
    +   **Unclear**: The wayfinding was not easy to follow.
    +   **Very unclear**: The wayfinding was extremely difficult to follow.
*   **Facility Cleanliness (Facility Cleanliness)**: Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
    +   **Excellent**: The facility was extremely clean and well-maintained.
    +   **Good**: The facility was clean, but not spotless.
    +   **Fair**: The facility was average in cleanliness.
    +   **Poor**: The facility was not clean.
*   **Facility Appearance (Facility Appearance and Condition)**: Rate the overall appearance and condition of the facility.
    +   **Excellent**: The facility looked very well-maintained and impressive.
    +   **Good**: The facility looked clean and well-maintained.
    +   **Fair**: The facility looked average and not particularly impressive.
    +   **Poor**: The facility looked unkept and run-down.
*   **Welcome Feeling (Did you feel welcomed)**: Rate how welcomed you felt upon arrival.
    +   **Very welcomed**: You felt extremely welcomed and accommodated.
    +   **Welcomed**: You felt welcomed and comfortable.
    +   **Neutral**: You felt neither welcomed nor unwelcome.
    +   **Not welcomed**: You felt unwelcome.
*   **Temperature and Comfort (Temperature and Comfort Level)**: Rate your temperature and comfort level.
    +   **Very comfortable**: The temperature was comfortable, and you felt at ease.
    +   **Comfortable**: The temperature was pleasant.
    +   **Neutral**: The temperature was average.
    +   **Uncomfortable**: The temperature was not comfortable.
    +   **Very uncomfortable**: The temperature was extremely uncomfortable.
*   **Areas for Improvement (Areas for Improvement)**: Select all areas where you think the facility can improve.
*   **Additional Feedback or Comments (Additional Feedback or Comments)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
*   **Likelihood of Returning (Likelihood of Returning)**: Rate how likely you are to return to the facility.
    +   **Very likely**: You are extremely likely to return.
    +   **Likely**: You are likely to return.
    +   **Neutral**: You are neither likely nor unlikely to return.
    +   **Unlikely**: You are unlikely to return.
    +   **Very unlikely**: You are extremely unlikely to return.

## Tips

*   Please answer truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
*   If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Feedback or Comments" field.
*   Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the visitor experience.
