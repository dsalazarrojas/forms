# Sailing Club Racing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience and opinions about our sailing club racing events. Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us improve our events for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, and take as much time as you need.
2. Please select the options that best fit your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field.
4. Enter your contact information, so we can get back to you about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Race Schedule (<name>: race_schedule)
* This question asks about your experience with our race schedule.
* If you have any issues or concerns with our schedule, please let us know.

### Schedule Quality (<name>: schedule_quality)
* This question asks you to rate your overall experience with our race schedule, with 1 being "very poor" and 5 being "excellent".
