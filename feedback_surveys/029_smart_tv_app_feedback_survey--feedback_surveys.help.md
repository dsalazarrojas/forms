# Smart TV App Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart TV App Feedback Survey is designed to collect user feedback and experiences to improve the app's performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To ensure your feedback is considered, please take a few minutes to fill out this form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
2.  Please answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
3.  This form should take approximately 5-7 minutes to complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **What do you use Smart TV app for? (1)**: This is the opening question that aims to understand the primary use case for the Smart TV app. Please provide a brief description of what you use the app for.
*  **How would you rate your overall experience? (2)**: This question seeks to understand how you perceive your overall experience with the Smart TV app. Choose a rating from 1-5 stars to express your satisfaction level.
*  **Would you recommend Smart TV app? (3)**: This question is to gauge whether you would recommend the app to others. Choose one of the provided options to indicate your preference.
*  **Please provide feedback about Smart TV app (4)**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the app. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
*  **Have you faced any issues while using Smart TV app? (5)**: In this question, you can mention any problems or difficulties you encountered while using the app. Please select all that apply from the provided options.
*  **When did you start using Smart TV app? (6)**: This question aims to understand when you first started using the app. Please enter the date you began using the app.
*  **Contact Number (7)**: This field is optional and asks for your contact number in case we need to reach out to you for further clarification or to follow up on your feedback.
*  **Email (8)**: This field is also optional and asks for your email address for any future correspondence or to follow up on your feedback.

**Tips**

*  Please answer these questions truthfully to help us improve the Smart TV app.
*  If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
*  Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and input.
