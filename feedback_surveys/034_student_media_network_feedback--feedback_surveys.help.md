# Student Media Network Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Media Network Feedback form is designed to collect valuable information about your experience with our service. Your feedback will help us understand what you like and dislike about our content and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction with the Student Media Network by choosing the corresponding number on the first page.
2. Choose the media channels you use to consume our content by selecting all that apply from the second page.
3. Provide any suggestions for improvement you have in mind on the third page.
4. Enter your email address on the fourth page.
5. Enter your phone number on the fifth page.
6. Optionally, you can also provide the date and time you submitted this form by selecting the corresponding fields on the sixth and seventh pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_satisfaction (1)**: This is a single-choice question where you can select your level of satisfaction with the Student Media Network. Please choose the number that best represents your experience.
* **content_preferences (2)**: This is a multi-choice question where you can select all the media channels you use to consume our content. You can select multiple options by holding down the Control key or Shift key while clicking on them.
* **improvement_suggestions (3)**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any suggestions for improvement you have in mind.
* **email (4)**: This is a field to enter your email address.
* **phone (5)**: This is a field to enter your phone number.
* **date_submitted (6)**: This is the date you submitted this form. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank.
* **time_submitted (7)**: This is the time you submitted this form. This field is also not required, so you can leave it blank.
