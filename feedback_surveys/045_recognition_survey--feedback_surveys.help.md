# Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an outstanding individual or team's achievements and contributions. It will be used to recognize and appreciate their efforts and provide feedback to help improve performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Nomination title**, a brief description of the recognition or achievement being recognized.
2. Provide the **Nominee's name**, email address, and phone number (if available).
3. Select the **Type of recognition**, choosing from the following options: Team member, Individual contribution, or Project lead.
4. Enter the **Date recognized**, using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Share the **Recognition story**, a detailed account of the nominee's achievement or actions.
6. Rate the **Impact**, indicating the level of impact achieved by the nominee, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.
7. Provide the **Reason for impact**, explaining why the nominee's actions had a specific impact.
8. Finally, specify the **Recognition program experience** of the nominee, if they have received recognition through this program before.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the recognition or achievement being awarded.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person being recognized.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person being recognized. This will help us contact them for more information or to schedule recognition.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, optional): If you know the nominee's phone number, please provide it for us to reach out to them.
* **Type of recognition** (`recognition_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Team member, Individual contribution, or Project lead to indicate the nature of the recognition.
* **Date Recognized** (`recognition_date`, date, required): Enter the date the recognition took place, using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Recognition Story** (`recognition_story`, text, required): Share a detailed account of the nominee's achievement or actions.
* **Recognition Program Experience** (`recognition_program_experience`, number, required): Check if the nominee has received recognition through this program before.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, required): Choose from High, Medium, or Low to indicate the level of impact achieved by the nominee.
* **Reason for Impact** (`impact_reason`, text, required): Explain why the nominee's actions had a specific impact.
* **Recognition Program Experience** (`recognition_program_experience`, number, required): Check if the nominee has received recognition through this program before. 

# Tips

* Please make sure to provide clear and concise answers to all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* Remember to submit the form completely and accurately to ensure we can process the recognition effectively.
