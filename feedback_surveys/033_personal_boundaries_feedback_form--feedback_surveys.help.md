# Personal Boundaries Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals about their personal boundaries and how they are respected within the organization. It is essential to understand how your boundaries are being met and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the option that best answers who was present during the incident or situation.
2.  Provide a brief explanation of why you felt your boundaries were not respected.
3.  Select the options that best describe when your boundaries are being respected.
4.  Select the option that best describes how often your boundaries are being respected.
5.  This section is optional. If you have any additional comments or insights about your boundaries, please share them here.
6.  Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Who Was Present** (`feedback_who_was_present`, select_one, `required`): This field is used to specify who was present during the incident or situation.
	+ Select one of the options: Admin, Manager, Coworker, Other.
* **Why Were Not Respected** (`feedback_why`, note, `required`): This field is used to provide a brief explanation of why you felt your boundaries were not respected. Please keep your answer concise and to the point.
	+ This field is optional, but please use it to provide valuable feedback.
* **Boundary Being Respected** (`boundary_beings_respected`, select_multiple, `required`): This field is used to select the options that best describe when your boundaries are being respected.
	+ Select all that apply: True, False, Maybe.
* **Boundary Being Respected Frequency** (`boundary_being_respected_frequency`, select_one, `required`): This field is used to select the option that best describes how often your boundaries are being respected.
	+ Select one of the options: Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
* **Why Not Respected** (`why_not_respected`, note, `required`): This field is used to provide additional information about why you felt your boundaries were not respected. Please keep your answer concise and focused on the specific incident or situation.
	+ This field is optional, but please use it to provide valuable feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, `required`): This field is used to provide any additional comments or insights about your boundaries. Please keep your answer concise and focused on the specific incident or situation.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, `required`): This is the final step to submit your feedback.
	+ Select the option: Submit.
* **Form Title** (`title`, text, `required`): This is the title of the form, displayed to the user.
	+ This field is not editable and is used as a placeholder.
	+ Please ignore this field and focus on the other fields to provide your feedback.
