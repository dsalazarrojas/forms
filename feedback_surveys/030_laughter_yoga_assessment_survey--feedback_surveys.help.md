# Laughter Yoga Assessment Survey -- Feedback Surveys - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from participants in a Laughter Yoga session. It's meant to assess how well the participants connected with each other, how they felt before and after the session, how much they laughed, and how well they connected with the instructor. Your feedback will help us improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Choose the options that best match your experiences during the session.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction (text)**: This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experience during the session.

### 2. Did participants connect with each other?
* **how well did participants connect with each other (number)**: Rate how well you think the participants connected with each other on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not at all well" and 5 is "Extremely well".

### 3. How did participants participate?
* **how well did participants participate (text)**: Describe how active and engaged you think the participants were during the session.

### 4. How did participants feel before the session?
* **how did participants feel before (select one)**: Choose one of the options that best describes how you felt before the session:
	+ Very relaxed
	+ Somewhat relaxed
	+ Not at all relaxed
	+ Not sure
	+ Don't know
	+ Rather tense
	+ Somewhat tense
	+ Very tense

### 5. How did participants feel after the session?
* **how did participants feel after (select multiple)**: Select all the options that apply to describe how you felt after the session:
	+ Very Happy
	+ Somewhat Happy
	+ Not at all happy
	+ Not sure
	+ Don't know
	+ Rather Happy
	+ Neutral

### 6. How much did participants laugh?
* **how well did participants laugh (select one)**: Choose one of the options that best describes how much you think the participants laughed:
	+ Extremely well
	+ Quite well
	+ Somewhat well
	+ Not at all well
	+ Rather poorly
	+ Neutral
	+ Don't know

### 7. How long was the session?
* **how long was session (text)**: Provide an estimate of how long the session lasted.

### 8. Did participants laugh together?
* **did participants laugh together (select one)**: Choose yes or no to indicate if you think the participants laughed together.

### 9. Comments
* **comments (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.

### 10. Suggestions
* **suggestions (text)**: This is another text field where you can provide any suggestions for improvement.

### 11. Email
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or to receive future updates.

### 12. Phone
* **phone (text)**: This is a text field where you can enter your phone number, but it's optional.

### 13. Did participants attend a previous session?
* **did participants attend a previous session (select one)**: Choose one of the options that best describes if you think the participants attended a previous session:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Rather
	+ Rather not
	+ Don't know
	+ Not sure
	+ Neutral

### 14. Would participants recommend a session?
* **would participants recommend a session (select one)**: Choose one of the options that best describes if you think the participants would recommend a session:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Rather
	+ Rather not
	+ Don't know
	+ Not sure
	+ Neutral

### 15. How many previous sessions?
* **how many previous sessions (number)**: If you know, enter the number of previous sessions the participants attended.

### 16. Did participants connect with the instructor?
* **did participants connect with instructor (select multiple)**: Select all the options that apply to describe how well you think the participants connected with the instructor:
	+ Extremely well
	+ Somewhat well
	+ Not at all
	+ Neutral
	+ Rather poorly
	+ Not sure
	+ Don't know

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us improve future sessions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you'd like to be contacted for future updates or follow-up, please enter your email address.
