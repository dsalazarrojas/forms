# Response Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Response Content Feedback Survey is a tool to gather feedback about response content from users, helping to improve the response quality and overall interaction experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the response that you are providing feedback about from the "Response 1-25" dropdown list.
2. Evaluate the content quality of the selected response, choosing from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
3. Indicate your sentiment about the content, selecting one or more of the following: "Positive", "Negative", or "Neutral".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Response 1-25** (`Select Multiple`):
	+ This field allows you to select the response content you are providing feedback about. Choose the number that corresponds to the specific response you are commenting on.
* **Content Quality** (`Select One`):
	+ Evaluate the overall quality of the response content, using one of the following options:
		- **Excellent**: The content is well-written, informative, and meets your expectations.
		- **Good**: The content is adequate, but may have some minor issues or room for improvement.
		- **Fair**: The content is satisfactory, but has noticeable issues or limitations.
		- **Poor**: The content is significantly lacking or unhelpful.
* **Sentiment** (`Select Multiple`):
	+ Indicate how you feel about the content, selecting one or more of the following:
		- **Positive**: You feel happy, satisfied, or content about the response.
		- **Negative**: You feel unhappy, dissatisfied, or concerned about the response.
		- **Neutral**: You neither feel strongly positive nor negative about the response.

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest when providing feedback, as it will help improve the response quality and overall interaction experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field (not shown in this help guide, as it's not part of the provided form structure).
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback! Your input will help us improve the response content and overall interaction experience.
