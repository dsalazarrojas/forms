# Cancellation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on why you're canceling your subscription or service. Your input will help us understand your experience and improve our service for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your level of satisfaction with the service/product before canceling.
2. Choose how often you used the service/product.
3. Select all the reasons that apply for why you're canceling the service/product.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied were you with the service/product before deciding to cancel?**
  * (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
  This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with the service/product before canceling. Please choose the answer that best reflects your experience.
* **How often did you use the service/product?**
  * (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never)
  This question helps us understand how often you interacted with the service/product. Please choose the answer that best reflects your usage frequency.
* **Which of the following best describes your primary reason for canceling? (Select all that apply)**
  * (Cost too high, Missing features, Poor service/support, Found an alternative, Not needed anymore, Technical issues)
  This question helps us understand the main reason for your cancellation. Please select all the reasons that apply to you.
* **What could we improve about the service/product?**
  * A text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
  This question is an open-ended space for you to provide any thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve the service/product.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?**
  * A text field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
  This question is an opportunity for you to provide any final thoughts or suggestions that may not have been covered in the previous questions.
