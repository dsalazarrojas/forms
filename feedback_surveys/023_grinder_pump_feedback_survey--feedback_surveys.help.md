# grinder_pump_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects feedback from users on their experience with grinder pumps.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Click on each page tab to navigate through the form.
*   Answer each field according to the explanations below.
*   Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **grinder_pump_feedback_survey** (`form_1`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your experience with the grinder pump.
*   **contact_info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of contact information you have (e.g., Utilities, Property Manager, Pump Manufacturers).
*   **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
*   **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
*   **pump_model** (`pump_model`, `text`, optional): Enter your grinder pump model number or description.
*   **pump_type** (`pump_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of grinder pump you are using (e.g., Grinder Pump, Jet Pump, Centrally Located Pump, Subsurface Pump, Surface Pump).
*   **location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Describe the location where the grinder pump is installed or used.
*   **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the grinder pump on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
*   **issues** (`issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you have encountered any issues with the grinder pump (e.g., yes or no).
*   **improvement_suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required): Suggest improvements you would like to see in the grinder pump or related products (e.g., yes or no).
*   **comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your grinder pump or experience.
*   **pump_location** (`pump_location`, `select_one`, required): Select where your grinder pump is located (e.g., Inside, Outside, Underground).
*   **pump_visibility** (`pump_visibility`, `select_one`, required): Select how visible is your grinder pump (e.g., Inside, Outside, Underground).
*   **pump_accessiblity** (`pump_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Rate the accessibility of your grinder pump (e.g., Easy, Moderate, Difficult).
*   **pump_size** (`pump_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the size of your grinder pump.
*   **pump_condition** (`pump_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of your grinder pump.
*   **pump_location_type** (`pump_location_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of location where your grinder pump is located (e.g., Underground, Inside, Outside).
*   **pump_location_visibility** (`pump_location_visibility`, `select_one`, required): Select how visible is your pump location (e.g., Inside, Outside).
*   **pump_location_size** (`pump_location_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your pump location (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
*   **pump_location_depth** (`pump_location_depth`, `select_one`, required): Select the depth of your pump location (e.g., Less Than 20, 20-40, 40-60, More Than 60).
*   **pump_location_elevation** (`pump_location_elevation`, `select_one`, required): Select the elevation of your pump location (e.g., Lowest Point, Middle, Highest Point).
*   **pump_location_type_description** (`pump_location_type_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of location where your grinder pump is installed or used.
*   **pump_location_comments** (`pump_location_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your pump location.
*   **pump_condition_comments** (`pump_condition_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the condition of your grinder pump.
*   **contact_comments** (`contact_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments about your contact information.
