# Family Faith Formation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Faith Formation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from families who participate in the program. This feedback will be used to improve and enhance the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the Family Name field**: Enter the name of the family you are submitting feedback for.
2. **Enter your Feedback Suggestions**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the program.
3. **Select your Role**: Choose your role within the family (Parent, Teacher, or Leader).
4. **(Optional) Enter your Role Other**: If your role does not match any of the options, provide a brief description of your role.
5. **Rate your Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
6. **Enter your Email (optional)**: If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, enter your email address.
7. **Enter your Phone (optional)**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
8. **Provide Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback about the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Name**: A field to enter the name of the family you are submitting feedback for.
* **Feedback Suggestions**: A field to share any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the program.
* **Role**: A field to select your role within the family (Parent, Teacher, or Leader).
* **Role Other**: An optional field to describe a role not listed in the previous field.
* **Role Other**: A field to enter a brief description of your role.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: A field to rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Feedback**: A field to share any additional comments or feedback about the program.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in the Feedback and Feedback fields.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the program, please mention them in the Feedback field.
* If you have suggestions or ideas to improve the program, please share them in the Feedback Suggestions field.
* If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please enter your email address in the Email field.
* If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number in the Phone field.
