## Purpose
The Workforce Voice Participation Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their level of engagement and communication within the company. Your input is valuable, and your participation will help us create a more positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your thoughts and feelings.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible in your answer.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to [contact email/phone number].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Intro** (text)
This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide any additional context about your feedback.

### 2. **Engagement** (select_one)
How would you rate your level of engagement at work?

* Option 1: High
* Option 2: Medium
* Option 3: Low

Please select the option that best reflects your current level of engagement.

### 3. **Communication** (select_multiple)
Which of the following options describe your experience with communication at work?

* Option 1: Clear and timely
* Option 2: Mostly clear, but sometimes unclear
* Option 3: Mostly unclear, but sometimes clear
* Option 4: Unclear and unhelpful

Please select all the options that apply to you.

### 4. **Improvements** (text)
What areas of improvement would you like to see at work? Please provide specific examples and suggestions.

### 5. **Feedback** (text)
What do you want to tell us about your experience? Please be as detailed as possible.

### 6. **Email** (email)
This is your email address. Please enter it so we can contact you if needed.

### 7. **Phone** (text)
This is your phone number. Please enter it so we can contact you if needed.

### 8. **Note** (note)
This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your feedback.
