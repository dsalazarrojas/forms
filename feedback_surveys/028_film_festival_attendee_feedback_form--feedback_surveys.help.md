# Film Festival Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a film festival event. Your responses will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form to provide feedback on your recent film festival attendance.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- You can save your answers and come back later if needed.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please provide your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our feedback records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please provide your last name as you would like it to be displayed in our feedback records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed (e.g., for future events or surveys).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Provide the date you attended the film festival.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Report the time you attended the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the event took place.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Why did you rate this event as...** (`rating_reason`, text, optional): If you've chosen a rating, provide a brief explanation for your response.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Would you recommend this event to a friend?** (`would_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Yes/No, indicate if you'd recommend the event to a friend.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide as much detail as you can.
- You can use the 'Rating' field to express a general opinion about the event, while the 'Rating Reason' field can help us better understand your feedback.
- You can save this form and return to it later to make sure you've answered everything.
