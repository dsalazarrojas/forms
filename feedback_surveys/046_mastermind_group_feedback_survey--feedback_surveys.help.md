# Mastermind Group Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in a Mindful Group. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, and will inform future group improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can type your responses in the text fields, or select an option from the dropdown menus.
- You can skip any questions that don't apply to your experience.
- Be as honest and detailed as possible in your responses, as your feedback is valuable to us.
