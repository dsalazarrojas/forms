## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Please provide your basic information to help us understand who you are and what you experienced during the festival.
* **Favorite Festival Activities**: Select all the activities you enjoyed during the festival, such as concerts, food vendors, or art installations. This will help us understand what made the festival enjoyable for you.
* **Food And Drink Preferences**: Select all the food and drink options you enjoyed or would like to see more of at future events. This will help us understand your culinary preferences and make sure we have enough variety for everyone.
* **Favorite Food**: Select your favorite food from the list. This will help us understand what types of cuisine you enjoy most.
* **Festival Preferences**: Select all the festival preferences that best describe your experience. This could include aspects like crowd size, stage schedules, or overall atmosphere.
* **Favorite Artist**: Select your favorite artist from the list. This will help us understand who you enjoyed most and why.
* **Music Parks**: Select all the music parks you visited during the festival. This will help us understand where you spent most of your time.
* **Food Preference**: Select your favorite type of food from the list. This will help us understand what type of cuisine you enjoy most.
* **Artist Preferences**: Select all the artist preferences that best describe your experience. This could include things like genre, energy, or crowd size.
