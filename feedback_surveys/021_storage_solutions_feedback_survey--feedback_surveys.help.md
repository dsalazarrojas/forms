# Storage Solutions Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Storage Solutions Feedback Survey form, which is used to gather feedback on storage solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into two sections: **Form Start** and **Form End**. Each section has a text field where you can enter your feedback.
2. In the **Form Start** section, simply enter your name, if available.
3. In the **Storage Type** section, select the type of storage solution you are providing feedback on. The options are:
	* File Server
	* NAS
	* SAN
	* Other (please specify)
4. In the **Storage Location** section, select all locations where your storage solution is located. The options are:
	* On-premise
	* Cloud
	* Hybrid
	* Other (please specify)
5. In the **Satisfaction** section, rate your level of satisfaction with the storage solution on a scale of 1-5.
6. In the **Comments** section, enter any additional comments about your storage solution.
7. In the **Storage Product** section, select the type of storage product you are using. The options are:
	* Server
	* Storage
	* Rack
	* Other (please specify)
8. In the **Product Rating** section, rate your storage product on a scale of 1-5.
9. In the **Storage Service** section, select the type of storage service you are using. The options are:
	* On-site
	* Off-site
	* Both
10. In the **Service Rating** section, rate your storage service on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start** (text): Enter your name, if available.
* **Storage Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of storage solution you are providing feedback on. This helps the organization understand the specific solution you are using.
* **Storage Location** (select_multiple, required: false): Select all locations where your storage solution is located. This helps the organization understand the scope of your storage infrastructure.
* **Satisfaction** (number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with the storage solution on a scale of 1-5. This helps the organization understand your overall satisfaction with the solution.
* **Comments** (note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your storage solution. This is an opportunity to provide more details about your experience with the solution.
* **Storage Product** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of storage product you are using. This helps the organization understand the specific product you are using.
* **Product Rating** (number, required: false): Rate your storage product on a scale of 1-5. This helps the organization understand your satisfaction with the product.
* **Storage Service** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of storage service you are using. This helps the organization understand the specific service you are using.
* **Service Rating** (number, required: false): Rate your storage service on a scale of 1-5. This helps the organization understand your satisfaction with the service.
* **Form End** (text): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the storage solution.
* **Form End** (text): This marks the end of the form. Please review your feedback before submitting.
