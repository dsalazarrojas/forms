# Night Market Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have attended a Night Market event. It will help event organizers understand the success and feasibility of the event, allowing for future improvements and planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Event ID**, which is a unique identifier for the event you participated in.
2. Enter your **Participant ID**, which is your personal identifier for the survey.
3. Provide the **Event Name**, which is the title of the event you attended.
4. Rate the event's overall **Rating** on a scale of 1-5.
5. Optional: You can provide **Comment** and **Suggestions** for the event organizers to improve future events.
6. Enter your **Contact Number**, which will be used to contact you for any further questions or follow-ups.
7. Enter your **Email**, which will be used to contact you for event updates or announcements.
8. Select the **Date** you attended the event (YYYY-MM-DD format).
9. If applicable, enter the **Time** you attended the event (HH:MM format).
10. Enter the **Location** where the event took place.
11. If you have any **Additional Comments** about the event, please share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID**: A unique identifier for the event, used to track individual events. (Required)
* **Participant ID**: Your personal identifier for the survey, used to track your responses. (Required)
* **Event Name**: The title of the event you attended. (Required)
* **Rating**: Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. (Required)
* **Comment**: Optional field for providing feedback or suggestions for the event. (Optional)
* **Suggestions**: Optional field for providing ideas for future events. (Optional)
* **Contact Number**: Your contact information for any follow-ups or questions. (Required)
* **Email**: Your email address for event updates or announcements. (Optional)
* **Date**: The date you attended the event (YYYY-MM-DD format). (Required)
* **Time**: The time you attended the event (HH:MM format). (Optional)
* **Location**: The venue or location where the event took place. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Any further feedback or comments about the event. (Optional)
