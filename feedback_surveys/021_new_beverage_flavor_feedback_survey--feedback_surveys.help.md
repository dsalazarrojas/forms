# new_beverage_flavor_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about the flavor of a new beverage from customers. Your input will help us understand their experiences and preferences, enabling us to improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the intro page.
2. Rate your experience with the new beverage on the rating page.
3. Select the flavor you experienced on the flavor page.
4. Optionally, add any comments about your experience on the comments page.
5. Choose whether you have a favorite flavor or not on the favorite page.
6. Provide your contact email address (this is mandatory) on the contact_email page.
7. Optionally, add your contact phone number on the contact_phone page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Rating** (`rating`, required): Rate your overall experience with the new beverage. Please select a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high).
### **Flavor** (`flavor`, required): Select the flavor you experienced while drinking the new beverage. Please choose from Apple, Berry, or Citrus.
### **Comments** (`comments`, optional): If you'd like to share more details about your experience, please type your comments here.
### **Favorite** (`favorite`, required): Indicate whether you have a favorite flavor or not.
### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): If you'd like, provide your phone number to be contacted.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all mandatory fields.
- Be as specific as possible when rating and describing your experience.
- If you have a phone number, please ensure it's up-to-date.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your honesty.
