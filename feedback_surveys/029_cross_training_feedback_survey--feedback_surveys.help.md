# Cross Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the cross-training sessions. Your input will help us to identify areas of improvement and provide better training experiences for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answers for each question based on your experience with the cross-training session.
2. For questions with "select_one" type, choose only one option that best describes your experience.
3. For questions with "select_multiple" type, select all applicable options.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, type them in the corresponding text fields.
