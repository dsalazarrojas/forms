# primary_care_speaker_presentation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback on the primary care speaker's presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the required information. You can skip any fields that are not applicable or relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the session you attended.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the speaker.
* **Topic** (`topic`, text, optional): Please provide the topic of the session.
* **Speaker Rating** (`speaker_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the speaker's performance, from 1 to 5.
* **Presentation Rating** (`presentation_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the presentation's quality, from 1 to 5.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, select_one, optional): Please choose how relevant you found the presentation's topic, from 1 (not relevant) to 5 (very relevant).
* **Relevance Rating** (`relevance_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the relevance of the presentation, from 1 to 5.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the session's overall quality, from 1 to 5.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose whether you strongly recommend, probably recommend, don't recommend, or have no opinion on the presentation.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please provide any additional feedback you have on the presentation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address here, so we can contact you if we need further information.
