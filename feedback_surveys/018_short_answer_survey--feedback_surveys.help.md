# Short Answer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our product or service. Your input is valuable to us, and this form will help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the introduction section.
2. Provide your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. In the "Feedback" field, share your feedback about your experience with our product or service.
5. Answer the "Improvement Areas" question by selecting all the areas where you think we can improve.
6. If you have any suggestions, enter them in the "Suggestions" field.
7. Fill in your contact email in the "Contact E-mail" field.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. If you'd like to specify the time you spent on the product or service, enter it in the "Time" field.
10. Select the date you experienced our product or service in the "Date" field.
11. Evaluate our product by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Product" field.
12. Evaluate our service by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Service" field.
13. Choose the department you interacted with by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Department" field.
14. Select all the team members you interacted with by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Team Members" field.
15. Evaluate if you had a team lead by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Team Leads" field.
16. Choose your preferred contact method by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Method" field.
17. Select the contact person you interacted with by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Person" field.
18. Select the role of the contact person by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Person Role" field.
19. Choose the department of the contact person by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Department" field.
20. Enter the contact location by filling in the "Contact Location" field.
21. Enter the contact phone number by filling in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
22. Enter the contact email by filling in the "Contact E-mail" field.
23. Enter the contact Facebook by filling in the "Contact Facebook" field.
24. Enter the contact Twitter by filling in the "Contact Twitter" field.
