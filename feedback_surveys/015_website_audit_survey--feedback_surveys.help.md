# Website Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Audit Survey is a form designed to collect your honest and constructive feedback on our website. Your input will help us improve your experience and make necessary changes to enhance our website's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best match your experience.
2. Answer each question honestly, and be specific in your responses.
3. You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback).
5. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall website performance?** (`website_performance`, select_one): Rate your overall experience on our website. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Poor.
* **How would you rate website features?** (`website_features`, select_multiple): Indicate which features you found most or least helpful on our website. Choose from: Navigation, Content, Aesthetics, Usability.
* **What is your primary reason for visiting our website?** (`website_content`, select_one): Select the reason you visited our website. Choose from: To learn more about our services, To get in touch with us, For entertainment, Other.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`user_experience`, select_one): Rate your overall experience on our website. Choose from: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
* **What do you think about our website navigation?** (`website_navigation`, select_one): Select your opinion on our website's navigation. Choose from: Easy to navigate, Difficult to navigate, Not navigated at all.
* **How much time did you spend on our website?** (`time_spent_on_site`, number): Estimate the total time you spent on our website.
* **How likely are you to return to our website?** (`return_visit`, number): Rate how likely you are to visit our website again. Use a number from 1-5, where 5 is very likely.
* **Do you have any feedback on our website?** (`feedback`, note): Add any specific comments or suggestions you may have about our website.
* **What is your email?** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **What is your phone number?** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about our website.
