# Hiking Safety Guidelines Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Safety Guidelines Survey aims to collect information from experienced hikers about their hiking practices, safety habits, and emergency procedures. This will help improve the overall safety of hiking trails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **What do you do most often when on the trail?**: Please select the options that best describe your typical behavior when on the trail.
2. **Describe your typical trail conditions**: Please provide a detailed description of the conditions you typically encounter on your hikes.
3. **What level of hiking experience do you have?**: Choose your level of experience from Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert.
4. **Which of the following safety habits do you usually follow?**: Select the options that best describe your safety habits.
5. **What is your emergency procedure?**: Please provide a detailed description of your emergency procedure in case of an accident or injury.
6. **How many people are in your hiking group?**: Choose the number of people in your group from 1 to 5.
7. **Have you ever received safety training from a guide or guide company?**: If you have received training, please select the options that best describe your experience.
8. **What gear do you typically bring on a hike?**: Select the options that best describe the gear you usually bring.
9. **Provide emergency contact information**: Please enter your email address or phone number where we can contact you in case of an emergency.
10. **Thanks for taking the time to complete our survey!**: Please review your answers and submit the form.
