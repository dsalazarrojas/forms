# Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey is a form used to collect feedback and opinions from youth on climate actions. By filling out this form, you will be helping to gather valuable insights on the climate concerns and priorities of young people.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey, if desired. This field is optional.
2. Select the category of feedback you are providing. Please choose from the options: "Feedback Surveys" or "Other".
3. If you want to add a description for the survey, enter your comments in the text box. This field is optional.
4. Choose the correct category for the form. Please select from the options: "Feedback Surveys" or "Other".
5. Indicate if you have a form description. Please answer with "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter your name or the person submitting the form.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Select whether you have a phone number. Please answer with "Yes" or "No".
9. Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for the survey, if desired.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of feedback you are providing.
* **The Youth Climate Action Attitudes Survey is a Jotform Form Builder template...** (`description_input`, text, optional): A detailed description of the survey, if desired.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Choose the correct category for the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have a form description. Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Enter the name or person submitting the form.
* **E-Mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you have a phone number. Answer with "Yes" or "No".
* **Date Submitted** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
