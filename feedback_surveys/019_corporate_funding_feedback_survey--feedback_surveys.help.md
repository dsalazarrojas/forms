# Corporate Funding Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Funding Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from funders about their experiences and perceptions regarding the corporate funding process. This form aims to collect information on the funders' satisfaction levels, areas of improvement, and rating of the funding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the "Funder Name" and "Funder Email" fields, respectively.
2.  Select the type of funding you received (e.g., "Grant," "Loan," or "Other") in the "Funding Type" field.
3.  Indicate your level of satisfaction with the funding experience by selecting one of the options in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
4.  Choose the specific area(s) where the funding had a significant impact on (e.g., "Education," "Health," "Environment," "Arts") in the "Impact Area" field.
5.  Provide any improvement ideas you have for the funding process in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
6.  Enter the date of when you received the funding in the "Date" field.
7.  Select the time of day when you received the funding in the "Time" field.
8.  Enter any additional comments about the funding experience in the "Comments" field.
9.  Rate your overall experience with the funding on a scale of 1-5 in the "Rating" field.

*   **Improvement Ideas (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the funding process.
*   **Date (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter the date you received the funding.
*   **Time (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Select the time of day you received the funding.
*   **Comments (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments about the funding experience.
