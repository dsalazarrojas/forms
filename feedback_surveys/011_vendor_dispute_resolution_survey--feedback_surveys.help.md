# Vendor Dispute Resolution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Dispute Resolution Survey is a tool for resolving disputes between vendors and their service providers. It's used by vendors to submit their issues and concerns to be reviewed and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the form and start by entering a brief description of the dispute in the "vendor_dispute_details" field.
2. If you have any supporting documents that are relevant to your case, select the "supporting_documents" option that best describes your situation.
3. Indicate your current satisfaction level with the services provided by selecting one of the options under "satisfaction_level".
4. Finally, share any additional feedback about your experience with the services provider in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Dispute Details (vendor_dispute_details)**: Enter a short description of the issue you are experiencing with your service provider.
* **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Select the option that best describes any supporting documents you have for your case. This could be a document type or if you have an actual document to upload.
* **Satisfaction Level (satisfaction_level)**: Choose your current level of satisfaction with the services provided by your provider. This will help us understand your overall experience.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Share any additional thoughts or concerns you have about your experience with the services provider. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback that will help us improve our services.
