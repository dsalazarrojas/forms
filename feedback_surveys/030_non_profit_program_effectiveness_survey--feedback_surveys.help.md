# Non Profit Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with our non-profit program. The goal is to understand how effective our program was in achieving its intended outcomes and what we can improve for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Please complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) after their label.
3. You can skip questions that are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your experience with our program? (program_effectiveness)**: This field is a text-based question where you can describe your overall experience with our program. This is an optional field, but your feedback is valuable to us.
* **Measure of Success (program_outcome)**: This is a multiple-choice question where you can select one of the options that best describes your experience. Please choose one of the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Date of Participation (date_of_participation)**: This field is used to record the date you participated in our program. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Participation (time_of_participation)**: This field is used to record the time you participated in our program. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **What did you learn or gain from participating in our program? (feedback)**: This field is another text-based question where you can describe what you learned or gained from participating in our program. This is an optional field, but your feedback is valuable to us.
* **What suggestions do you have for us? (recommendations)**: This is a text-based field where you can provide any suggestions or recommendations you have for our program. This is an optional field, but your input is valuable to us.
