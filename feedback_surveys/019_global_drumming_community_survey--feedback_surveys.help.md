# Global Drumming Community Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Drumming Community Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from the global drumming community. This form will help us understand your experiences, preferences, and suggestions for our community, allowing us to improve and provide better services for drummers of all levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which includes questions about your experience with our community, your favorite types of drumming, and more.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question, using the options provided.
3. For questions with multiple options, you can select one or multiple answers depending on your preferences.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, please type them in the "Suggestions" field at the end of the form.
5. Review your responses to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall experience with our community?** (`user_experience`, number, optional):
	+ This question asks about your general impression of our community. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "not satisfied" and 10 being "very satisfied".
* **What types of drumming do you enjoy the most?** (`drum_styles`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose one or multiple options that best describe the types of drumming you enjoy. This will help us understand your preferences.
* **What is your level of involvement in online drumming communities?** (`community_involvement`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the option that best describes your level of involvement in online drumming communities. Options include "Active", "Occasional", and "Rarely".
* **Which of the following programs do you participate in?** (`program_participation`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or multiple options that you participate in. This will help us understand your involvement in different programs.
* **How do you usually connect with other drummers?** (`style_connection`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the option that best describes how you usually connect with other drummers. Options include "In-person events", "Online forums", "Social media", and "Private messaging".
* **Do you have any suggestions for our community?** (`suggestions`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any suggestions or comments for our community, please type them in this field.
* **How many years have you been drumming?** (`experience_years`, number, optional):
	+ If you know, please provide the number of years you have been drumming.
* **How often do you practice drumming?** (`practice_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the option that best describes how often you practice drumming. Options include "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", and "Rarely".
* **What is your connection to drummers of other styles?** (`drumming_style_connection`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your connection to drummers of other styles. Options include "Close", "Distant", and "No connection".
* **How can we improve our programs for you?** (`program_improvement`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our programs for you, please type them in this field.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* Your input will help us improve our community and provide better services for drummers of all levels.
