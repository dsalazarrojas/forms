## Shift Patterns And Mental Fatigue Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for questions that have multiple options.
3. Enter numerical values for questions that require numerical values.
4. Answer all questions that are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **This is a survey about shift work** (intro_message, text, required): This is the introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose.
* **What kind of shift work do you do?** (mental_fatigue, select_multiple, required): Select all that applies from the options listed (Morning shifts, Afternoon shifts, Night shifts, Rotating shifts, On call shifts).
* **What is your sleep duration?** (sleep_duration, number, required): Enter the number of hours you usually sleep in a 24-hour period.
* **How many hours do you work per day?** (work_duration, number, required): Enter the number of hours you usually work in a 24-hour period.
* **Do you get regular rest breaks?** (rest_breaks, select_one, required): Select whether you get regular rest breaks (Yes, No).
* **What are your mental health concerns?** (mental_health, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options listed (Stressful, Anxious, Depressed, Happy, Content).
* **Does your supervisor support your well-being?** (supervisor_support, select_one, required): Select whether your supervisor supports your well-being (Yes, No).
* **What would improve your well-being?** (suggestions, text, required): Enter any suggestions or suggestions you have to improve your well-being.
* **Any final comments?** (final_message, text, required): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **What kind of shift work do you do?** (mental_fatigue, select_multiple, required): This field is repeated for emphasis.
