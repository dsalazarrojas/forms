# Field Directors Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Field Directors Feedback Survey form. Please read this carefully to ensure you complete the form accurately and provide the best possible feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into two pages. Start by answering the questions on the first page.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you need to answer it truthfully.
3. Use the provided options for select questions (e.g., select_one, select_multiple).
4. You can type your feedback in the text boxes for open-ended questions.
5. Once you've answered all questions on the first page, click on the "Next" button to move to the second page.
6. Continue to the second page and complete the remaining questions.
7. When you're finished, click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`)
* Label: Overall Satisfaction
* Type: Number
* Description: Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10.

### **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`)
* Label: Job Satisfaction
* Type: Number
* Description: How satisfied are you with your job? Rate it on a scale of 1-10.

### **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`)
* Label: Suggestions for Improvement
* Type: Text
* Description: Provide any suggestions you have for improving your role or the organization.

### **Challenges Confronted** (`challenges_confronted`)
* Label: Challenges Confronted
* Type: Text
* Description: Describe the challenges you're currently facing in your role.

### **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`)
* Label: Improvement Ideas
* Type: Text
* Description: Share any ideas you have for improving your role or the organization.

### **Satisfaction with Manager** (`satisfaction_with_manager`)
* Label: Satisfaction with Manager
* Type: Select One
* Description: Rate your satisfaction with your manager. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### **Feedback about Manager** (`feedback_about_manager`)
* Label: Feedback about Manager
* Type: Text
* Description: Share your thoughts about your manager.

### **Select Multiple Challenges** (`select_multiple_challenges`)
* Label: Select Multiple Challenges
* Type: Select Multiple
* Description: Choose from: Lack of Resources, Communication issues, Poor Leadership, Other (please specify).

### **Submit** (`submit`)
* Label: Submit
* Type: Text
* Description: This is the final step to submit your feedback. Please ensure you've answered all questions truthfully and provided detailed feedback.
 
**Tips**

* Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us improve.
* Use the available space to elaborate on your answers.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to us.
