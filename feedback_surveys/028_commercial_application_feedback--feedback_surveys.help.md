# Commercial Application Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end users on their experience with the commercial application. It will help the development team understand what works and what doesn't, allowing them to improve the application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your actual experience and honest feedback.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best describes your experience.
4. For text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
