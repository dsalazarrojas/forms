# Workplace Inclusivity Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Inclusivity Index Survey aims to assess the current state of diversity, equity, and inclusion in the workplace. This anonymous survey will help us understand your experiences and perceptions of our work environment and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Please review each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Choose the options that best fit your experience.
3. For select multiple questions, you can select all the options that apply.
4. Make sure to provide your demographic data so we can get a better understanding of the overall demographics.
5. Click the 'Submit' button once you've completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
 	* (`introduction`, `note`, not required): A brief introduction to the survey purpose and objectives.
* **Workplace Culture**:
 	* (`workplace_culture`, `select_one`, not required): This question assesses your perception of the current state of diversity and inclusion in the workplace. Please choose between 'Diverse and Inclusive' or 'Not Diverse and Inclusive'.
* **Leadership Support**:
 	* (`leadership_support`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question evaluates the support provided by leadership for diversity and inclusion initiatives. Select all the options that apply from the list:
	+ Provide clear goals and objectives
	+ Foster open and honest communication
	+ Encourage diversity and inclusion
	+ Recognize and reward diverse behaviors
* **Workplace Climate**:
 	* (`workplace_climate`, `select_one`, not required): This question measures your perception of the overall work environment. Please choose between 'Positive' or 'Negative'.
* **Inclusivity Index**:
 	* (`inclusivity_index`, `number`, not required): This is a numerical rating system to rate your experience of inclusivity in the workplace. Please rate on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Demographic Data**:
 	* (`demographic_data`, `select_multiple`, not required): This section is to collect demographic information about yourself. Select all the options that apply:
	+ Age
	+ Sex/Gender
	+ Ethnicity
	+ Disability Status
* **Contact Information**:
 	* (`contact_information`, `text`, not required): This is an optional field to provide any additional comments or suggestions. Please keep it brief and professional.
* **Submit**:
 	* (`submit_form`, `text`, not required): Click this button to submit your survey once you've completed all the pages. 

Note: All questions are optional, and you can skip any section if you don't feel comfortable answering. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.
