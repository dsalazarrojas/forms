# Net User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with a network service provider. It helps the provider understand user interactions, issues encountered, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your email address in the "user_email" field.
2.  Select your experience level with the network service provider in the "Experience Level" field.
3.  Describe the issue you encountered in the "Issue Encountered" field.
4.  Suggest improvements for the network service provider in the "Suggestion" field.
5.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
6.  Select areas where the network service provider needs improvement in the "Net Service Improvement Area" field.
7.  Choose the priority of the improvement area in the "Net Service Improvement Priority" field.
8.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
9.  Select how often you encounter issues with the network service provider in the "Issue Frequency" field.
10.  Choose the type of issue you encountered in the "Issue Type" field.
11.  Select the cause of the issue in the "Issue Cause" field.
12.  Provide contact information for the network service provider in the "Net Service Provider Contact Info" field.
13.  Submit the form by selecting "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your feedback.
*   **Net Service Provider** (`net_service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your network service provider.
*   **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with the network service provider, from "Very Dissatisfied" to "Very Satisfied".
*   **Issue Encountered** (`issue_encountered`, text, optional): Describe the issue you encountered with the network service provider.
*   **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for the network service provider.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the network service provider.
*   **Net Service Improvement Area** (`net_service_improvement_area`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas where the network service provider needs improvement.
*   **Net Service Improvement Priority** (`net_service_improvement_priority`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the priority of the improvement area.
*   **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, note, optional): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to submit your feedback.
*   **Thank You** (`thank_you_message`, text, optional): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you about your feedback.
*   **Net Service Provider Email** (`net_service_provider_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of your network service provider.
*   **Issue Frequency** (`issue_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you encounter issues with the network service provider.
*   **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of issue you encountered.
*   **Issue Cause** (`issue_cause`, select_multiple, optional): Select the cause of the issue.
*   **Net Service Provider Contact Info** (`net_service_provider_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your network service provider.
