## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions on climate policy from respondents. It is likely intended for research or public engagement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to answer each question carefully.
- Select the options that best match your thoughts and opinions.
- If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the relevant fields.
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

- **Introduction** (`climate_change_introduction`, note, optional): This is the introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
- **Climate Change Awareness** (`climate_changeawareness`, select_one, required): How aware are you of climate change? Choose the option that best reflects your current level of awareness.
- **Climate Policies** (`climate_policies`, select_multiple, optional): What are your opinions on climate policies? Select all the options that apply to you.
- **Policy Ideas** (`policy_ideas`, text, optional): Do you have any ideas for climate policies? Share your thoughts in this field.
- **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, select_one, required): Please provide your demographic information by selecting the relevant option.
- **Age Range** (`age_range`, number, required): What is your age range? Choose a number that matches your age.
- **Education Level** (`education_level`, number, required): What is your education level? Choose a number that represents your level of education.
- **Region** (`region_range`, text, required): Which region do you live in? Provide the name of the region.
- **Language** (`language`, text, required): What language do you prefer to communicate? Choose the language that you are most comfortable with.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Please provide your contact number.
- **Submission** (`submission`, select_one, required): Please choose how you would like to submit your feedback.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Do you have any comments or feedback? Share them here.
- **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Do you have any other comments or feedback? Share them here.
