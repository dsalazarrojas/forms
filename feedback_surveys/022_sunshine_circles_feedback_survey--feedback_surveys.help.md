# Sunshine Circles Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunshine Circles feedback survey is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the program. Your input will help us improve the program and provide better services to our participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your feedback about your participation in the Sunshine Circles program.
2.  Provide your contact information so we can get back to you.
3.  Make sure to answer the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Rating (1)**: Rate your overall experience with the Sunshine Circles program. This will help us understand how well we met your expectations.
* **Overall Experience (2)**: Share your overall experience with the program. This will give us insight into what you liked and disliked about the program.
* **Suggestions (3)**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the program. This will help us make the program better for future participants.
* **Facilitator Feedback (4)**: Choose the facilitators who provided the best support for your group. Select all that apply.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you about your feedback.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number so we can get back to you about your feedback.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date you participated in the program.
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time you participated in the program.
* **How Often Do You Participate (9)**: Tell us how often you participate in the Sunshine Circles program. This will help us understand your engagement with the program.
* **Facilitator Engagement (10)**: Rate the engagement level of the facilitator during your program. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Materials Quality (11)**: Rate the quality of the materials provided for your program. This will help us understand how effective our materials are.
* **More Suggestions (12)**: Provide any additional suggestions you have for improving the program. Select all that apply.
* **Additional Comments (13)**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Participant Name (14)**: Enter your name so we can identify you in our feedback database.
* **School Name (15)**: Enter the name of the school you participated in the program.
* **Program Leader (16)**: Enter the name of the program leader who facilitated your group.
* **School Email (17)**: Enter the email address of your school so we can get back to them about your feedback.
* **School Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of your school so we can get back to them about your feedback.
