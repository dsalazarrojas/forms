# Peanut Brittle Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peanut Brittle Feedback Survey is designed to collect information from customers about their purchase experiences with our products. Your feedback will help us improve our offerings and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow the instructions below:

1. Start at the first page of the form, where you will be asked to enter your name.
2. Select the product you purchased (peanut brittle or other).
3. Choose how you purchased the product (grocery store, other, specialty store, online, farmer market, or restaurant).
4. (Optional) Select how often you purchased the product (if you wish to provide this information).
5. Rate your experience with the product (if you wish to provide this rating).
6. Suggest areas where we can improve (packaging, taste, quality, price, or availability).
7. Add any comments or suggestions you may have.
8. Finally, provide your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?**: Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in your feedback.
* **Product Puchased**: Select the type of product you purchased (Peanut Brittle or Other).
	+ Peanut Brittle: Choose this option if you purchased a peanut brittle product.
	+ Other: Choose this option if you purchased a different product or had a different experience.
* **How did you purchase?**: Select where you bought the product (grocery store, other, specialty store, online, farmer market, or restaurant).
	+ Grocery store: Choose this option if you purchased the product from a local grocery store.
	+ Other: Choose this option if you purchased the product from a different location or source.
	+ Specialty store: Choose this option if you purchased the product from a specialty store.
	+ Online: Choose this option if you purchased the product from our website or a different online retailer.
	+ Farmer market: Choose this option if you purchased the product from a farmer market or local market.
	+ Restaurant: Choose this option if you purchased the product from a restaurant or food establishment.
* **How often do you purchase?**: (Optional) Select how often you purchase the product (if you wish to provide this information).
	+ This field is optional, and you can select any frequency of purchase that applies to you.
* **Rate Your Experience**: (Optional) Select your rating of the product (if you wish to provide this rating).
	+ Please rate your experience with the product from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Areas to Improve**: (Optional) Suggest areas where we can improve (packaging, taste, quality, price, or availability).
	+ Packaging: Choose this option if you have suggestions for improving our product packaging.
	+ Taste: Choose this option if you have suggestions for improving the taste of our products.
	+ Quality: Choose this option if you have suggestions for improving the quality of our products.
	+ Price: Choose this option if you have suggestions for improving the price of our products.
	+ Availability: Choose this option if you have suggestions for improving the availability of our products.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Contact Us**: Provide your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
