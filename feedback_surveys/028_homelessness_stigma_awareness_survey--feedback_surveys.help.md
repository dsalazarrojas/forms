# Homelessness Stigma Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homelessness Stigma Awareness Survey is designed to gather information from various individuals and organizations about their experiences, comfort levels, and perceptions regarding interacting with homeless individuals or families. This data will help raise awareness and potentially reduce homelessness stigma.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2.  Select the appropriate option for each question.
3.  Fill in any required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4.  If you're unsure about any question, choose the closest option that reflects your feelings or experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role or position within an organization that interacts with homeless individuals or families?** (`role`, select_one, required)
    This question helps us understand your role or position within an organization that interacts with homeless individuals or families. Please select the option that best describes your job or position.
* **How would you rate your level of comfort in approaching someone on the street who is homeless?** (`comfort`, select_one, required)
    This question measures your comfort level when interacting with someone experiencing homelessness. Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **Have you or a family member/friend experienced homelessness in the past?** (`experienced_homelessness`, select_one, required)
    This question asks about your personal experience or that of a family member/friend with homelessness. Please select the option that applies to you.
* **What is your understanding of what causes homelessness?** (`causes_homelessness`, select_one, required)
    This question aims to gather your knowledge and understanding of the causes of homelessness. Choose the options that you believe contribute to homelessness.
* **Causes Homelessness** (`causes_homelessness_2`, select_one, required)
    This is a duplicate question of `causes_homelessness` and is meant to gather more information about what causes homelessness. Select the options that you believe contribute to homelessness.
* **How often do you interact with homeless individuals or families?** (`interaction_frequency`, select_one, required)
    This question helps us understand how often you interact with homeless individuals or families. Please choose the option that best reflects your frequency of interaction.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how confident are you in your ability to provide effective services to someone experiencing homelessness?** (`services_confidence`, number, required)
    This question evaluates your confidence in providing services to individuals or families experiencing homelessness. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to best reflect your feelings.

## Tips
- Answer questions truthfully and without bias.
- Choose the options that best reflect your feelings, experiences, or understanding.
- If unsure, choose the closest option that applies to you.
