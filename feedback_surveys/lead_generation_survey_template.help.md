# lead_generation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the lead generation process, aiming to optimize and improve the quality and quantity of leads generated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with the quality of leads generated?" by selecting one of the five options provided.
2. Answer the question "Is the current volume of leads sufficient for your needs?" by selecting one of the three options provided.
3. Select all the characteristics that best describe the leads you receive from the options provided (e.g., Highly qualified, Good fit, Engaged, Timely, Early stage, Not relevant).
4. Provide specific suggestions for improvement in the text field.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the lead generation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the quality of leads generated?** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
	+ This question helps us understand your level of satisfaction with the quality of leads generated. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
* **Is the current volume of leads sufficient for your needs?** (Too high, Just right, Too low)
	+ This question assesses whether the volume of leads meets your expectations. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Which characteristics best describe the leads you receive?** ( Highly qualified, Good fit, Engaged, Timely, Early stage, Not relevant)
	+ Select all the characteristics that apply to the leads you receive. This will help us understand what types of leads are being generated.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for our lead generation process?**
	+ Use this text field to provide detailed suggestions on how we can improve the lead generation process.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding lead generation?**
	+ Share any additional thoughts, observations, or ideas you have about the lead generation process. This will help us understand your concerns and preferences.
