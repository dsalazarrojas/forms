# Justice And Dignity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback about your experience with a service, focusing on how you felt about being treated with justice and dignity during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out the form completely and truthfully.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can always ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you feel when using our service?** (`service_felt_welcoming`, text, required: no): Answer this question by describing your feelings about your experience with our service. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Were you treated with respect?** (`service_felt_respectful`, select_one, required: no): Select one of the options to indicate how you felt about being treated with respect. You can choose from: "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Did you feel our service was fair?** (`service_felt_fair`, select_multiple, required: no): Select all that apply to indicate how you felt about the fairness of the service. You can choose from: "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Date of visit**: (`date`, date, required: no): If you know the date you visited the service, enter it here in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of visit**: (`time`, time, required: no): If you know the time of your visit, enter it here in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Your Email**: (`contact_email`, email, required: no): Enter your email if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Your Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required: no): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your number.
* **Additional information**: (`additional_info`, note, required: no): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here.
* **Your Name**: (`name`, text, required: no): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Was a team leader present during your visit?** (`team_leader`, select_one, required: no): Select one of the options to indicate if a team leader was present during your visit. You can choose from: "True", "False".
* **Additional feedback**: (`feedback`, text, required: no): If you have any other feedback or comments, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is for the purpose of improving our service, so any feedback you provide will be kept confidential and will only be reviewed by authorized personnel.
