# Camper Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from campers about their experience at the camp. It consists of multiple questions that aim to understand their satisfaction levels with various aspects of the camp, such as food, activities, counselors, and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions with rating scales (e.g., "5 - Excellent"), select the number that best represents your opinion.
3. For questions with text fields, provide a brief description or answer in your own words.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, but if a question is not applicable to your experience, please mark it as "N/A" (Not Applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`camper_name`, text, optional): This is the first question on the form where you can enter your name.
* **Your Age** (`age`, number, optional): Please enter your age, but if it's not applicable, just mark it as "N/A".
* **Which Camp Session Did You Attend** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the camp session you attended (e.g., Session 1, Session 2, Session 3, Session 4, Full summer).
* **How Much Did You Enjoy Camp Overall** (`overall_enjoyment`, select_one, required): Rate your overall enjoyment of the camp on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Did not enjoy at all" and 5 is "Loved it".
* **How Would You Rate Your Counselor** (`counselor_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your counselor on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Was Your Counselor Friendly and Helpful** (`counselor_friendliness`, select_one, required): Choose from the options how friendly and helpful your counselor was.
* **How Would You Rate the Activities** (`activities_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the activities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What Was Your Favorite Activity** (`favorite_activity`, select_one, required): Select your favorite activity from the options provided.
* **What Was Your Least Favorite Activity** (`least_favorite_activity`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the activity you least enjoyed.
* **How Would You Rate the Food** (`food_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the food on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What Was Your Favorite Meal** (`favorite_meal`, text, optional): Describe your favorite meal, if you have one.
* **How Would You Rate Your Cabin** (`cabin_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your cabin on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Did You Make New Friends at Camp** (`made_friends`, select_one, required): Choose from the options how many friends you made at camp.
* **Did You Feel Safe at Camp** (`felt_safe`, select_one, required): Choose from the options how safe you felt at camp.
* **How Would You Rate the Camp Facilities** (`camp_facilities`, select_one, required): Rate the facilities on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How Clean Was the Camp** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of the camp on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Dirty" and 5 is "Very clean".
* **Did You Learn New Skills at Camp** (`learned_new_skills`, select_one, required): Choose from the options how many new skills you learned at camp.
* **What Skills Did You Learn** (`skills_learned`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the skills you learned, if any.
* **Would You Recommend Camp to a Friend** (`recommend_to_friend`, select_one, required): Choose from the options whether you would recommend the camp to a friend.
* **Would You Like to Come Back Next Year** (`return_to_camp`, select_one, required): Choose from the options whether you would like to come back next year.
* **What Is Your Best Memory from Camp** (`best_memory`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your favorite memory from camp.
* **What Could We Improve** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the camp.
* **What Activities Would You Like to See Added** (`additional_activities`, text, optional): Suggest any new activities you would like to see added to the camp.
* **Any Other Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any other thoughts or comments about your experience at camp.
