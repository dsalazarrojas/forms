# Post event feedback survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from event participants to improve future events by evaluating their overall satisfaction, the value they derived from event activities, and their likelihood of attending similar events.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Respond to the questions truthfully and concisely. This will help ensure that the data collected is accurate and actionable.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions. This means you can choose as many options as relevant to your experience.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for text questions. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the provided text box.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied were you with the event?** (`satisfaction`, `single`, required): Please rate your satisfaction with the event overall, from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Which sessions or activities did you find most valuable? (Select all that apply)** (`sessions`, `multiple`, optional): Choose all the activities that you found most valuable, such as keynote presentations, workshops, or networking opportunities.
* **Was the event well organized and managed?** (`organization`, `single`, required): Rate your experience with the event's organization and management, from true to false.
* **How likely are you to attend a similar event in the future?** (`attendance_likelihood`, `single`, required): Rate your likelihood of attending a similar event in the future, from Very likely to Not likely.
* **What was the most impactful aspect of the event for you?** (`impactful_aspect`, `text`, optional): Describe the most impactful aspect of the event for you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement?** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions for improving future events.
