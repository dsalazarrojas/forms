# Civic Engagement In Youth Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather insights from young individuals about their civic engagement and interest in community service to inform initiatives and programs to support youth leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as you can.
2. If you are under 13, please have your parent or guardian complete this form with you.
3. Select only the options that apply to you.
4. For questions with a "Select All" option, select as many options as apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current age?** (`youth_age`, `number`, required): This question helps us understand your demographic information.
* **Current School Status** (`current_school_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your current school status to help us understand your education level.
* **Have you ever volunteered for a community project or nonprofit?** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, required): Share your experience with community service to help us understand your civic engagement.
* **How often do you participate in community service?** (`service_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you participate in community service to help us understand your level of engagement.
* **Which causes do you care about most?** (`preferred_causes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the causes that you are passionate about, to help us understand your interests.
* **Have you voted in the last local election?** (`voting_attitude`, `select_one`, required): If you are 18 or older, please answer this question truthfully. If under 18, please indicate if you plan to vote when you turn 18.
* **What stops you from being more involved in your community?** (`engagement_barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Share your thoughts on what prevents you from being more involved in your community.
* **Do you use social media to advocate for causes you care about?** (`digital_engagement`, `select_one`, required): Please select your level of social media engagement with your preferred causes.
* **Do you believe young people can make a real difference in the community?** (`community_impact_belief`, `select_one`, required): Share your thoughts on the impact of young people.
* **Would you be interested in joining a Youth Advisory Council?** (`youth_council_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you are interested in joining a Youth Advisory Council.
* **Name a young person you admire for their civic leadership** (`leader_nomination`, `text`, optional): Share a name of a young person who you admire for their civic leadership.
* **What is one thing the city could do to better support youth?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts on how the city could support youth.
* **Email address (if you want to be notified of youth events)** (`contact_email_opt`, `email`, optional): If you want to receive updates on youth events, please provide your email address.
* **If under 13 - I have my parent's permission to fill this out** (`parent_consent_if_minor`, `select_one`, required): If you are under 13, please confirm that you have your parent's permission to complete this form.
* **Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the date you complete this survey.
