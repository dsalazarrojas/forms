# Art And Color Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art And Color Evaluation Survey is designed to gather information about the viewer's opinion on artistic and color evaluation of a given artwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information (name and email) in the designated fields.
2. Evaluate the artwork based on its visual impact, emotional response, and understanding of the message in the corresponding sections.
3. Answer questions about color harmony, balance, and preference in the designated fields.
4. Provide an overall assessment of the artwork, including your rating and recommendation in the final sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, `text`, required/optional): This is your name, which will be used to identify you in the survey results.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required/optional): This is your email address, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Age Group** (`Age Group`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select your age group from the provided options.
* **Date of Survey** (`Date of Survey`, `date`, required/true): This is the date you are completing the survey.
* **Title of Artwork Being Evaluated** (`Title of Artwork Being Evaluated`, `text`, required/true): This is the title of the artwork you are evaluating.
* **Visual Impact of the Artwork** (`Visual Impact of the Artwork`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select the strength of the artwork's visual impact.
* **Emotional Response to the Artwork** (`Emotional Response to the Artwork`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Please select all the emotions that you feel when viewing the artwork.
* **Understanding of the Artwork's Message** (`Understanding of the Artwork's Message`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how clear you understand the message of the artwork.
* **Primary Color or Color Palette** (`Primary Color or Color Palette`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe the dominant colors in the artwork.
* **Color Harmony and Balance** (`Color Harmony and Balance`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how well the colors work together in the artwork.
* **How do the colors affect the mood** (`How do the colors affect the mood`, `text`, required/optional): This is an open-ended question where you can describe the effect of the colors on your mood.
* **Preference for Color Choices** (`Preference for Color Choices`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how much you like or dislike the colors in the artwork.
* **Technical Skill or Artistic Technique** (`Technical Skill or Artistic Technique`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how skilled or unskilled you think the artwork's technique is.
* **Originality and Uniqueness** (`Originality and Uniqueness`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how original or unoriginal you think the artwork is.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select your overall rating of the artwork.
* **Would you recommend this artwork to others** (`Would you recommend this artwork to others`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select whether you would recommend the artwork to others.
* **Additional Comments or Observations** (`Additional Comments or Observations`, `text`, required/true): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or observations about the artwork.
* **Suggestions for Artist Improvement** (`Suggestions for Artist Improvement`, `text`, required/optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any suggestions for improvement for the artist.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* You can refer to the artwork while completing the form for more accurate answers.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the questions, you can reach out to us for support.
