# NGO Collaboration Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the frequency and effectiveness of partnerships between non-profit organizations and other types of organizations. Your responses will help us gauge the current state of non-profit collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your current experiences and practices.
2. Select all options that apply for each question.
3. Provide any additional information that may help us better understand your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nonprofit partnership frequency**: This question asks about how often you partner with other organizations. Please select all types of partnerships that apply to you.

  • Nonprofit partnership frequency (private sector)
