# Children Storytelling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions aimed at understanding children's thoughts and feelings about a recent storytelling session. Your responses will help us improve our sessions and create a better experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the form one at a time, following the instructions for each question.
2. Fill out your information accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to submit your responses before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the child or parent answering the questions. If you prefer not to provide your name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, **required**): Choose the age group that best represents the child's age:
	+ 3 to 5
	+ 6 to 8
	+ 9 to 11
* **Favorite Genre** (`favorite_genre`, select_one, **required**): Which story genre did you enjoy most:
	+ Fantasy
	+ Adventure
	+ Humor
	+ Educational
* **Did you enjoy the storytelling session?** (`participation_enjoyment`, select_one, **required**): How much did you enjoy the session?
	+ Loved it
	+ Liked it
	+ It was okay
	+ Did not like it
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If there's anything you'd like to see changed or added in future sessions, please let us know.
* **Would you recommend this to a friend?** (`recommend_to_friend`, select_one, **required**): Would you suggest this storytelling session to a friend?
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other feedback or thoughts, please share them with us.
