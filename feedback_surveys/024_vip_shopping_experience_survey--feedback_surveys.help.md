# vip_shopping_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **VIP Shopping Experience Survey** is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience on the Vip Shopping platform. This form helps us understand how users interact with our platform and provides valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Each question is optional, but we highly encourage you to provide your feedback to help us improve.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the last field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to Vip Shopping Experience Survey** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **How did you get here?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the options to answer how you found out about our platform:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Shopping environment** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe your shopping environment, e.g., online, in-store, etc.
* **Product quality** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Product price** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price of the product you purchased (if applicable).
* **Purchase experience** (`6`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the time it took to make a purchase on our platform.
* **Purchase experience hint** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions about your purchase experience, please enter them here.
* **Contact information** (`8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion (if needed).
* **Contact info hint** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other contact information you'd like to share, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the user experience on our platform.
