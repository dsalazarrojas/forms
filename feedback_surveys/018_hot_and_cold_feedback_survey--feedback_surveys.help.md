# Hot And Cold Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their hot and cold (positive and negative) experiences with our services. Please take a few minutes to share your honest feedback. Your input will help us understand areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can skip any field that you are not comfortable sharing. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hot Feedback** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): Describe your positive experience with our services. Share your successes, what you liked, or anything else that went well.
* **Cold Feedback** (`form_2`, text, required/optional): Share your negative experience with our services. Describe any issues or problems you faced while interacting with us.
* **Customer Experience** (`form_3`, text, required/optional): Describe your overall experience with our services. What made it good or bad? Share your thoughts.
* **Service Improvement** (`form_4`, text, required/optional): Suggest ways we can improve our services. Be as specific as possible. Your input will help us better serve you in the future.
* **Time Of Feedback** (`form_5`, time, required/optional): Indicate when you had your experience with our services. Please select the correct time of day or date of experience.
* **Feedback Date** (`form_6`, date, required/optional): Select the date of your experience with our services.
* **Feedback Time** (`form_7`, time, required/optional): Select the time of day of your experience with our services.
* **Email** (`form_8`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you and discuss your feedback in more detail if needed.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- Share as much detail as you can about your experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
