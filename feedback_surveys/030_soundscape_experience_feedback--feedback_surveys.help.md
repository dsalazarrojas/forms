# Soundscape Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soundscape Experience Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with a soundscape. This feedback will help us improve and refine the soundscape, making it more enjoyable and engaging for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Rate your experience with the participant rating field, using a number to express your level of enjoyment.
3. Express your emotional response to the soundscape with the emotion rating field, also using a number.
4. If you liked the soundscape, select your favorite sound effect from the options provided. If you did not, you can skip this field.
5. If you have any feedback or comments about your experience, use the participant feedback field for a more detailed response.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, use the suggestion field for a more detailed response.
7. Finally, if you want to contact us for any reason, use the contact info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Rating (Participant Rating)**: Rate your overall experience with a number.
* **Emotion Rating (Emotion Rating)**: Express your emotional response to the soundscape using a number.
* **Did you like it (Favorite Part)**: If you enjoyed the soundscape, select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Favorite Sound Effect (Favorite Sound Effect)**: If you liked the soundscape, select your favorite sound effect from the options provided.
* **Participant Feedback (Participant Feedback)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Suggestion (Suggestion)**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, provide them in this field.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: If you want to contact us, provide your name and any other relevant information. 

## Tips
* Be honest and truthful in your responses.
* Provide specific feedback whenever possible.
* Remember that your input will help us improve the soundscape.
