# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback and evaluation on the Educational Kit, used to assess the effectiveness and usability of the kit in meeting its intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed:

1. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields to ensure we can contact you regarding your feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your experience with the Educational Kit, being as specific and honest as possible.
3. Use the options provided for each question, and if none of the options fit your experience, please let us know in the comments field.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the Educational Kit you are evaluating.
* **Learning Kit Name**: Enter the name of the learning kit you used during your evaluation.
* **Usefulness**: Select how useful you found the Educational Kit in meeting your needs (Very useful, Somewhat useful, Not very useful, Not useful at all).
* **Content Quality**: Select all that apply regarding the quality of the content provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Ease of Use**: Select how easy or difficult you found the Educational Kit to use (Very easy, Easy, Somewhat difficult, Difficult).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the Educational Kit (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not satisfied).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding the Educational Kit.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Program ID**: Enter the ID of the program you are evaluating.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to you for the evaluation.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form you are filling out.
* **Created Date**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when your evaluation was created.
* **Updated Date**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when your evaluation was last updated.
* **Submitted Date**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when your evaluation was submitted.
* **Created By**: This field is automatically populated and indicates who created the evaluation form.
* **Updated By**: This field is automatically populated and indicates who last updated the evaluation form.
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated and indicates who submitted the evaluation form.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback regarding your experience with the Educational Kit.
* **Learning Kit ID**: Enter the ID of the learning kit you used during your evaluation.
* **Program Name 2**: Select if you have used the Educational Kit in a program (Yes, No).
* **Program Type**: Select if you have used the Educational Kit in a program (Yes, No).
* **Program Length**: Select if you have used the Educational Kit in a program (Yes, No).
* **Location**: Select if you have used the Educational Kit in a location (Yes, No).
* **Evaluation Form**: Select if you have evaluated the Educational Kit (Yes, No).

Remember, your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the Educational Kit and its associated services. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
