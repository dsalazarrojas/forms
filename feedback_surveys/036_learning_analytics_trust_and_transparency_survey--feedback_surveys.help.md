# Learning Analytics Trust and Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Analytics Trust and Transparency Survey is designed to gather feedback on the trust and transparency of learning analytics tools and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form carefully to understand each section and field.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Be sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Surveys** (`id: 1`): This field is where you can provide general feedback on your experience with learning analytics tools and services.
  - Type: Text field for free-form feedback.
- **Survey Title** (`id: 2`): This field is for title of the survey you are providing feedback on.
  - Type: Text field for title of the survey.
- **Learning Analytics Questions** (`id: 3`): This field is a note field where you can provide additional questions or concerns related to learning analytics.

  - Type: Select_multiple field with options 'Yes' and 'No'.
