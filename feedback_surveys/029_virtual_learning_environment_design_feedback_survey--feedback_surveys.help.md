# Virtual Learning Environment Design Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback on the design of virtual learning environments, helping us understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that ask for multiple options, choose the one that best represents your opinion.
3. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers, and feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Finally, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
