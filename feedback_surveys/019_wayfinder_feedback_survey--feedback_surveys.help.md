# Wayfinder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with Wayfinder, a navigation system used in a specific industry or category. Your feedback will help us improve the system and make it more user-friendly for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Be honest and concise in your responses.
3. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to leave it blank or indicate that it doesn't apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Navigation** (`navigation`, `text`, required/optional: false): This page is a general feedback field. Please provide your thoughts on how you navigated the system.

* **Signage Clarity** (`signage_clarity`, `text`, required/optional: false): How clear were the signs and instructions while navigating the system?

* **Wayfinding Experience** (`wayfinding_experience`, `text`, required/optional: false): Please describe your overall experience with the wayfinding system.

* **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, `text`, required/optional: false): We use this information to improve your next visit. Please suggest any changes or improvements you would like to see.
