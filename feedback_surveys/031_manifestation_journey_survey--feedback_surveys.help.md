# Manifestation Journey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your manifestation journey, helping us understand your progress and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select all that apply for questions that have multiple options.
3. Enter your details in the relevant fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Journey Goals**: This field is a text box where you can describe your goals for the manifestation journey. Be as specific as possible.
* **Progress Updates**: This question allows you to choose all the progress updates that you've experienced during your journey. Select all options that apply to your situation.
* **Challenges Conquered**: Here, you can choose one option that best describes whether you've conquered challenges during your journey.
* **Wins Achieved**: Similar to the previous question, select all options that describe your wins during the journey.
* **Current Status**: This field is a date field where you can input your current status in the manifestation journey. This is optional, but it helps us track your progress.
* **Next Steps**: You can input the next steps you plan to take in the journey. This field is a time field where you can select a date and time.
* **Coach/Mentor Notes**: This is a text box where your coach or mentor can leave notes about your progress.
* **Client Notes**: Similarly, this field is for clients to leave notes about your journey.
* **Client Name**: Please input your name as it appears on the client list.
* **Email**: This is a field to input your email address.
* **Phone**: You can input your phone number in this field.
* **Submission**: This field is not required but can be used for additional comments or information.
* **Confirmation**: This field is for you to confirm that you've submitted the form successfully.
* **Survey Completed**: This field is used to confirm that you've completed the survey.
* **Form ID**: This field is not required but can be used for internal purposes.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not required but can be used for internal purposes.

Note: The fields with `required: true` are mandatory and must be filled out before submission. The fields with `required: false` are optional and can be filled out at your discretion. 

**Tips**: Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us improve the tool or process.
