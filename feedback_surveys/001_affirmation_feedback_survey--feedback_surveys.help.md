# Affirmation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affirmation Feedback Survey is designed to gather information on employee satisfaction with the affirmation process in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your official company email address.
2. Select the level of satisfaction you feel with the overall workplace culture.
3. Indicate how often you receive affirmations or praise.
4. Identify the source of most frequent affirmations you receive.
5. If you have a recent example, describe a time when you felt appreciated.
6. Select the impact of affirmations on your motivation.
7. Express your satisfaction with feedback from management.
8. Provide suggestions for improving the affirmation process (if applicable).
9. Select all methods you prefer to receive recognition.
10. Rate how valued you feel by your organization on a scale of 1-10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, required): Enter your official company email address.
  - This will help us identify you in our records and ensure you receive any follow-up communications.
  - Please note that we do not use this field for any other purpose.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Company Culture** (`culture_satisfaction`, required): Select the level of satisfaction you feel with the general workplace atmosphere.
  - Think about your overall feelings about coming to work and how valued you feel as an employee.
  - If you are unsure, try to reflect on your overall experience.
- **Frequency of Receiving Positive Recognition** (`affirmation_frequency`, required): Select how often you receive affirmations or praise.
  - Consider the times when you received positive feedback.
  - This helps us understand the scope of affirmations in the workplace.
- **Frequency of Affirmations** (`primary_source`, required): Identify the source of most frequent affirmations you receive.
  - Think about who usually gives you the most positive feedback.
  - You can select more than one source if applicable.
- **Recent Example of Affirmation** (`recent_example`, optional): Briefly describe a time when you felt appreciated.
  - Share a specific instance of positive feedback you received recently.
  - If you don't have a recent example, skip this field.
- **Impact of Affirmations on Motivation** (`motivation_impact`, required): Select the impact of affirmations on your work motivation.
  - Reflect on how positive recognition affects your productivity and drive.
  - Be honest about how much it affects you.
- **Satisfaction with Feedback from Management** (`management_feedback_satisfaction`, required): Select your level of satisfaction with feedback from your supervisor.
  - Think about how often you receive quality feedback from management.
  - This helps us understand the effectiveness of feedback in the workplace.
- **Suggestions for Improving Affirmation Process** (`improvement_suggestions`, optional): If you have suggestions, provide them here.
  - Share your ideas on how we can improve the affirmation process.
  - This is an opportunity for you to suggest improvements.
- **Preferred Methods of Receiving Recognition** (`preferred_methods`, optional): Select all methods you prefer to receive recognition.
  - Think about how you like to receive positive feedback.
  - You can choose more than one method.
- **Feeling of Being Valued** (`value_scale`, required): Rate how valued you feel by your organization on a scale of 1-10.
  - Reflect on how much you feel appreciated and valued in the organization.
  - Be honest, as this is a subjective measure.
