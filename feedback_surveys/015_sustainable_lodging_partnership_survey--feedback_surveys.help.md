# Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for partners to share their information and interests with us to help us better understand their needs and potential opportunities for collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Please answer each question according to your best knowledge and understanding.
- You can skip any field that is not applicable or relevant to your situation.
- If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey (survey_form_1)** (`survey_form_1`, `text`, optional): This field is an introductory question that helps us get to know you and your company better. Please provide a brief overview of your organization and its focus.
* **Partner Interests (partner_interests)** (`partner_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the areas of sustainability that interest you, such as True or False:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Sustainability Practices (sustainability_practices)** (`sustainability_practices`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to share your current practices and efforts related to sustainability.
* **Certifications (certifications)** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any certifications related to sustainability, please select them from the options:
	+ Certified
	+ Non-Certified
* **Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey (survey_form_2)** (`survey_form_2`, `text`, optional): This field is another opportunity for you to provide more information about your partnership goals and aspirations.
* **Partnership Opportunities (partnership_opportunities)** (`partnership_opportunities`, `text`, optional): Please share any potential collaboration or partnership opportunities you see with us.
* **Sustainable Lodging Partnership Survey (survey_form_3)** (`survey_form_3`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you may have about our sustainable lodging initiative.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about the survey or our sustainable lodging initiative.
