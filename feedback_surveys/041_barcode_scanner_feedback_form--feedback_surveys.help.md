# Barcode Scanner Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experiences with the barcode scanner. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve the performance and functionality of the device. Your input will help us create a better product that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing basic information about the device and user:
	* **Barcode Scanner Model / App Name**: Enter the name or model of the barcode scanner you're using (e.g., Zebra TC52, Honeywell Voyager, ScanApp v2).
	* **Device Serial Number (if known)**: If possible, provide the serial number found on the tag on the back or bottom of the device.
2. Select your **Primary Role** from the given options (e.g., Warehouse Picker/Packer, Retail Associate, Inventory Auditor, Delivery Driver, or IT Support).
3. Choose where the device is **Primarily Used** from the given options (e.g., Indoor Warehouse, Retail Floor, Outdoor/Loading Dock, On the Road, or Healthcare/Lab).
4. Rate the device's performance and functionality with the following questions:
	* **Scanning Speed and Responsiveness**: Rate how quickly the device captures codes from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* **Scanning Distance / Range**: Rate the device's ability to scan from far away or very close from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* **Performance in Low Light or Glare**: Rate the device's performance in low-light conditions from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* **Battery Life / Duration**: Rate whether the device lasts your entire shift from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
5. Indicate how often you experience technical issues with the device:
	* **How often do you experience technical glitches?**: Choose from Never, Rarely (Once a month), Occasionally (Once a week), or Frequently (Every shift).
6. Describe **any specific issues** you've encountered while using the device, including steps to reproduce them.
7. Select the **Connectivity Type** used for your device (e.g., Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular, or USB/Wired).
8. If you have any **suggested features or improvements** for the device, provide them in the text area below.
9. Finally, select the **Date of Feedback** when you're providing this feedback.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible with your feedback, especially when describing specific issues.
* Use the provided options for ratings and select options only if they apply to your experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
