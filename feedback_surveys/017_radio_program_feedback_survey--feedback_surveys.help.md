# Radio Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radio Program Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on our radio program. Your feedback is essential in helping us understand what works and what doesn't, allowing us to improve the show for future listeners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

1. Answer the first question about your feelings on today's show.
2. Select all the options that you liked most about today's show.
3. Suggest ways we could improve the show.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend this show to others.
5. Rate how likely you are to listen to our show again.
6. Choose why you decided to listen to our show (you can select all that apply).
7. Rate how likely you are to listen to our show again.
8. Choose the time of day you tuned in to our show.
9. Choose the date of the week you tuned in to our show.
10. Select types of content you would like to see more of in our show.
