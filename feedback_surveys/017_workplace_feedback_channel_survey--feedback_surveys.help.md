# Workplace Feedback Channel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your feedback about your experience with the workplace. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better service to our employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply fill out the form with your feedback and submit it. You can fill out the form online or print it out and submit it to the designated person in charge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Channel** (`feedback_channel`, text, optional): Describe the channel used to communicate with your team or colleagues, such as email, phone, or in-person meetings.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information or to receive updates about the results of this survey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further information or to receive updates about the results of this survey.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): This field will automatically display the date and time when you submit the form.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to share them.
- If you need any clarification or more information, please contact us.
