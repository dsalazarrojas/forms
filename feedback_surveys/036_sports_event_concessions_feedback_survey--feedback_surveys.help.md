# Sports Event Concessions Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback from attendees on their experience with concession services at sports events. The goal is to understand how attendees perceive the quality, services, and overall satisfaction with concessions during the event. Your input will help us improve the concession experience for future attendees.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions that apply to your experience.
2. Select the rating that best describes your opinion for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Provide your email address and/or phone number (optional) if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate concession quality** (`concession_quality`, select_one, required: false): 
  Rate the quality of the concession services you experienced during the event. This will help us understand how well the food and beverages met your expectations.
* **How would you rate concession services** (`concession_services`, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose how quickly the concession staff were able to serve you. This will help us understand the speed of service during the event.
* **How likely are you to purchase from concessions at this event** (`concession_value`, select_one, required: false): 
  Rate how likely you are to buy food and beverages from concessions in the future, based on your experience at this event.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall event experience** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): 
  Indicate how satisfied you are with the event overall, including concessions, entertainment, and other aspects.
* **How likely are you to attend this event again** (`purchase_intent`, select_one, required: false): 
  Rate how likely you are to attend this event in the future, based on your experience.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): 
  Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about your experience with concessions at this event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): 
  Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to share your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
  Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or to share your feedback.
