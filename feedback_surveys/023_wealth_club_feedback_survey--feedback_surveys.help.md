# Wealth Club Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on our Wealth Club service. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Enter your responses in the text fields. If a field is required, please make sure to fill it out.
3. Select the options that best match your answers for the select_multiple field.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think about our service?** (`introduction`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts and opinions on our service.
* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Do you have any suggestions to improve our service?** (`suggestion`, text, optional): We value your ideas and would love to hear any suggestions you might have.
* **Are you likely to refer our service to others?** (`referral_intent`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from "Very likely", "Somewhat likely", "Not very likely", or "Not at all likely".
* **What would you like to see improved in our service?** (`improvement`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what we can improve.
* **Rate this item 1/5** (`rating_1`, number, optional): Rate this item as 1/5.
* **Rate this item 2/5** (`rating_2`, number, optional): Rate this item as 2/5.
* **Rate this item 3/5** (`rating_3`, number, optional): Rate this item as 3/5.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, you can include them in the text fields.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It's invaluable to us.
