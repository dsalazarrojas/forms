# Nature Contact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their nature contact experiences. It is used in conjunction with other feedback mechanisms to gather more in-depth details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate category for your feedback.
2. Provide a brief description of your nature contact experience.
3. Choose the tool that is currently assigned to you for this task.
4. Select the type of output file you would like to receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category (Select One)**: Choose the category that best describes the type of feedback you are providing. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
  * Options: Feedback Surveys, Questionnaire Templates
* **description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of your nature contact experience. This can include details such as location, date, and any notable events or observations.
* **assigned_tool (Select One)**: Choose the tool that is currently assigned to you for this task. This helps us understand the tools and equipment used during your nature contact experience.
  * Options: chatjimmy, Other
* **output_file (Select One)**: Select the type of output file you would like to receive. This determines the format of the file that will contain your feedback.
  * Options: 008 nature contact questionnaire--feedback surveys.yaml, Other
