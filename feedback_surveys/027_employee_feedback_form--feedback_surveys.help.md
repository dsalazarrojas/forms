# Employee Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from employees about their work experience and suggestions for improvement. It is essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the relevant category for your feedback from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Provide your comments and thoughts in the "Comments" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the options provided in the "Satisfaction" field. You can select multiple options if applicable.
5. If you have a suggestion for improvement, type it in the "Suggestion" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
8. If applicable, select a date and time for your feedback.
9. You can add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your official company documentation.
* **Category** (select_one): Choose the category that best describes your feedback.
* **Comments** (text): Provide your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **Satisfaction** (select_multiple): Rate your overall satisfaction with the options provided.
* **Suggestion** (text): If you have a specific suggestion, type it here.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
* **Date** (date): Select the date of your feedback, if applicable.
* **Time** (time): Select the time of your feedback, if applicable.
* **Note** (note): Add any additional comments or notes here.
