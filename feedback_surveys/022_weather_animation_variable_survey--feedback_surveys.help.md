# Weather Animation Variable Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weather Animation Variable Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from users about their experiences and perceptions of weather animation variables. This form is used in research settings to collect user insights on how users engage with and respond to different weather animation options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, ensuring you understand the question or topic.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, or provide a detailed response in the text fields.
3. Be honest and provide specific examples when answering questions that ask for feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **weather-animations** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the weather animation options that apply to your current situation. Select all the options that you experience while using weather animation variables. This field is used to understand which animation types are most relevant or useful for you.
* **key-variables** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Which of the following key variables are most relevant to you? This field is meant to gauge your understanding and preference for key variables related to the weather animation variable.
* **weather-clarity** (`3`, `note`, required: false): Please provide a brief description or thought about the clarity of the weather animation variable.
* **desired-improvements** (`4`, `note`, required: false): Share your thoughts on how the weather animation variable can be improved. This could be related to functionality, user experience, or any other aspect you think needs improvement.
* **overall-clarity** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the weather animation variable, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **overall-scores** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the weather animation variable, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **meteorology-team-score** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the meteorology team related to the weather animation variable, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **app-developer-score** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the application developer related to the weather animation variable, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **researcher-score** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the researcher related to the weather animation variable, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **overall-improvements** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): How satisfied are you with the overall weather animation variable? Choose from "very-satisfied," "somewhat-satisfied," or "somewhat-unhappy."
* **comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or additional feedback, please share them here.
* **submit-form** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name or any additional comments you'd like to leave before submitting the form.
