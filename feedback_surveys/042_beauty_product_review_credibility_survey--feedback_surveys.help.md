# Beauty Product Review Credibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your behavior and preferences when interacting with beauty product reviews. Your responses will help us understand how you evaluate review credibility and what factors influence your purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your age group from the options provided.
2. Select how often you read beauty product reviews.
3. Indicate how often you read product reviews.
4. Choose how much you rely on reviews when making a purchase.
5. Select where you typically read reviews.
6. Indicate how you evaluate reviewer expertise.
7. Choose the importance of verified purchase badges.
8. Select the importance of detailed review length.
9. Indicate how you evaluate reviewer credibility based on their experience.
10. Choose how you evaluate the importance of checking multiple sources.
11. Select how much you are aware of sponsored reviews.
12. Indicate the importance of sponsorship disclosure.
13. Explain how you evaluate reviewer credibility.
14. Select the red flags that make you skeptical about a review.
15. Explain the red flags that make you skeptical about a review.
16. Indicate how you trust negative reviews.
17. Select how skeptical you are of overly positive reviews.
18. Choose whether you write product reviews.
19. Explain your motivation for writing reviews.

* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided.
