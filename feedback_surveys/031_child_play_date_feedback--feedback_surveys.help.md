# Child Play Date Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from parents or caregivers about their child's experience with a play date organized by our service. Your input will help us improve our services and create a more enjoyable and safe experience for children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the field labeled "Your Full Name" (field 6).
2. Enter the child's name in the field labeled "Child Name" (field 7).
3. Select the date of the play date from the calendar in the field labeled "Date of Play Date" (field 8).
4. Rate your overall experience by selecting one of the options in the field labeled "Overall experience rating" (field 9).
5. Choose all activities that your child enjoyed during the play date in the field labeled "Favorite activities during the play date" (field 10).
6. Indicate your interest in future play dates by selecting one of the options in the field labeled "Would your child like to play again?" (field 11).
7. Enter any suggestions or improvements for future play dates in the field labeled "Suggestions for next time" (field 12).
8. If you have any concerns or issues to report, please enter them in the field labeled "Any concerns or issues during the play date?" (field 13).
9. Finally, ensure that the "Today Date" field (field 14) is automatically populated with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person providing feedback.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child who participated in the play date.
* **Date of Play Date** (`play_date_date`, date, required): Select the date of the play date from the calendar.
* **Overall experience rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Choose how your experience with the play date was, using the provided options:
	+ Excellent - Everyone had fun
	+ Good - Mostly positive
	+ Fair - Some issues
	+ Poor - Not recommended
* **Favorite activities during the play date** (`favorite_activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all activities that your child enjoyed during the play date.
* **Would your child like to play again?** (`play_again`, select_one, required): Indicate your child's interest in future play dates by selecting one of the options:
	+ Yes - Definitely
	+ No - Not interested
	+ Unsure - Need to ask child
* **Suggestions for next time** (`suggestions_text`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or improvements for future play dates.
* **Any concerns or issues during the play date?** (`concerns_text`, text, optional): Report any safety or behavior concerns or issues you experienced during the play date.
* **Today Date** (`today_date`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date and should not be modified.
