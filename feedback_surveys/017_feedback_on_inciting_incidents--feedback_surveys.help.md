# Feedback On Inciting Incidents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on inciting incidents, which are significant events that may have a substantial impact on our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's questions carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Detailed Account Of Event" field.
3. Select the correct category for your feedback or suggestion from the available options.
4. If you have experienced multiple inciting incidents, you can select multiple options in the "Suggestion" field.
5. Enter the date of the incident using the calendar icon or by typing it in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback On Inciting Incidents Form (Introduction)** (`id: 1`): A brief introduction to the form.
* **Impact Rating (Impact Rating)** (`id: 2`): Rate the impact of the incident on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand the severity of the incident.
* **Detailed Account Of Event (Detailed Account)** (`id: 3`): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Suggestion (Suggestion)** (`id: 4`): Suggest how we can improve our services to prevent similar incidents in the future. You can select multiple options if you have multiple suggestions.
* **Category (Category)** (`id: 5`): Choose the category that best describes your feedback or suggestion.
* **Date Of Incident (Date Of Incident)** (`id: 6`): Enter the date of the incident using the calendar icon or by typing it in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback or suggestions.
* Use the "Detailed Account Of Event" field to describe the incident in detail, including the circumstances leading up to it.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask and we will be happy to assist you.
* We will review and analyze your feedback to improve our services.
