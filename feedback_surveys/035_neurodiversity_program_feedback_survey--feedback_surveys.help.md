# Neurodiversity Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neurodiversity Program Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants in the Neurodiversity Program. It helps us understand your experience with the program, your level of satisfaction, and any suggestions you may have. By providing feedback, you help us improve the program to better serve the needs of its participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session from the list that you participated in. You can choose from Session 1, Session 2, or Session 3.
2. Provide your overall satisfaction with the program on a numerical scale. You can choose any number from 0 to 100.
3. If you have any suggestions or feedback, please share them with us in the "Do you have any suggestions?" field. You can type your suggestions freely, and we appreciate any detail or ideas you may have.
4. If you wish to contact us, please provide your email address or phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Evaluation**: This field allows you to select which session you participated in. Please choose the session that best matches your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a numerical scale. You can choose any number from 0 to 100.
* **Do you have any suggestions?**: This field is for providing any feedback or suggestions you may have about the program. You can type freely and share any idea you may have.
* **Email**: This field is for you to provide your email address if you want to contact us for any reason.
* **Phone Number**: This field is for you to provide your phone number if you want to contact us by phone. Please note that you can leave this field blank if you do not wish to be contacted by phone.
