# Lymphedema Device Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lymphedema Device Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with the device. Your feedback will help us improve the device and make it more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in the following pages:
	* Introduction: This is a brief introduction to the survey.
	* Usage Patterns: Choose how often you use the device.
	* Satisfaction Scores: Rate your satisfaction with the device.
	* Improvement Ideas: Share any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
	* Comments: Any additional comments about your experience with the device.
	* Usage Frequency: Estimate how many times you use the device per week.
	* Usage Duration: Estimate how long you use the device per session.
	* Goals: Share any personal goals related to using the device.
	* Goals (2): Share any additional personal goals related to using the device.
2. For each page, follow the instructions and choose the relevant options or enter the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it to understand the purpose of the survey and what to expect.
* **Usage Patterns**: This question asks how often you use the device. Choose the option that best describes your usage frequency.
	+ 1-2 times a week: You use the device once or twice a week.
	+ 3-4 times a week: You use the device three or four times a week.
	+ 5-6 times a week: You use the device five or six times a week.
	+ Daily: You use the device every day.
* **Satisfaction Scores**: This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the device. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the device.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the device.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the device.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the device.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the device.
* **Improvement Ideas**: Share any suggestions or ideas for improvement. This is a text field where you can type your thoughts.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the device. This is a text field where you can type your thoughts.
* **Usage Frequency**: Estimate how many times you use the device per week. This is a numerical field where you can enter a number.
* **Usage Duration**: Estimate how long you use the device per session. This is a numerical field where you can enter a number.
* **Goals**: Share any personal goals related to using the device. This is a text field where you can type your thoughts.
* **Goals (2)**: Share any additional personal goals related to using the device. This is a text field where you can type your thoughts.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for help.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve the device.
