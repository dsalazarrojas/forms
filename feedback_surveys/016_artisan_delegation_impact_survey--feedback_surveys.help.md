# Artisan Delegation Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Artisan Delegation Impact Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from respondents about the Artisan Delegation Program. The survey assesses the program's overall impact, identifies areas of improvement, and gathers suggestions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please answer each section carefully, and ensure you provide as much detail as possible**.
2. **Use the provided options and fields as guidance**.
3. **Take as much time as you need, but try to complete the survey in one session**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information**
  * **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
  * **Your Role** (`respondent_role`): Select your role within the artisan organization, e.g., Artisan Member, Team Leader, Coordinator.
* **Respondent's Organization** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of your artisan network or group.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`): Indicate when you are completing this survey. This will help us track progress and identify trends over time.
* **Delegation Program Details**
  * **Delegation Name** (`delegation_name`): Describe the delegation initiative you are referring to.
  * **Duration of Delegation Program** (`delegation_duration`): Select the duration of the delegation program from the provided options.
  * **Delegation Start Date** (`delegation_start_date`): If applicable, enter the start date of the delegation program.
* **Impact Assessment**
  * **Overall Impact** (`overall_impact`): Rate the overall impact of the delegation program on artisans.
  * **Skills Development Impact** (`skills_development`): Assess how the delegation program has affected skills development among artisans.
  * **Economic Impact on Artisans** (`economic_impact`): Evaluate the economic impact of the delegation program on artisans.
* **Challenges and Opportunities**
  * **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`): Describe the main obstacles you faced during the delegation program.
  * **Challenge Categories** (`challenge_categories`): Select all relevant categories of challenges you faced, e.g., Time management, Resource constraints, Skills gaps.
  * **Positive Outcomes** (`positive_outcomes`): Share successful results or achievements from the delegation program.
* **Recommendations and Feedback**
  * **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`): Suggest areas that could be improved or done better.
  * **Support Needs** (`support_needs`): Specify resources, training, or assistance required by your organization.
  * **Program Continuation** (`program_continuation`): Decide whether you think the delegation program should continue, with modifications, or discontinue.
* **Recommendations**
  * **Scaling Potential** (`scaling_potential`): Assess the potential for scaling the delegation model.
  * **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_feedback`): Provide any other insights or observations you would like to share.
  * **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`): Indicate your willingness to participate in follow-up discussions.

## Tips

* **Answer honestly and thoroughly**, reflecting on your experiences with the delegation program.
* **Check each field carefully** before submitting your answers to ensure accuracy.
* **If you have any questions or concerns**, contact your organization's administrator or reach out to us directly.
