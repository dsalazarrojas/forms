# We Want To Hear You - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction ratings from users, as well as allow them to upload files. It is meant to be filled out once per user, and should not be repeated by the same user.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Feedback Survey" page.
2. Answer the questions and rating as accurately and honestly as possible.
3. Select a satisfaction rating (if applicable) to indicate your level of satisfaction.
4. If you have a file you would like to share with us, you can upload it using the "File Upload" field.
5. Double-check your answers and ratings before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey (1, feedback_survey)**: This is where you will answer our questions about your experience. Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
* **Satisfaction Rating (2, satisfaction_rating)**: This is where you can rate your level of satisfaction with the service you received.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **File Upload (3, file_upload)**: This is where you can upload a file to share with us. This can be a document, image, or any other type of file that is relevant to your feedback.
