# Repair Request Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Repair Request Survey" is a form used to collect feedback and information from customers about their repair experience. This helps us understand how we can improve our repair services and ensure that our customers are satisfied with the work we do.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any comments or details in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Repair Request Survey** (Repair Request Survey, text, optional)
Please enter a brief description of the issue you brought your item in for repair.

### 2. **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional)
Share any thoughts or comments about your experience with us.

### 3. **Issue Details** (Issue Details, note, optional)
Provide any details about the issue you brought in for repair.

### 4. **Service Team** (Service Team, select_one, optional)
Select the team that assisted you with your repair.

### 5. **Customer Contact** (Customer Contact, email, optional)
Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.

### 6. **Repair Time** (Repair Time, time, optional)
Enter the time it took to complete the repair.

### 7. **Customer Rating** (Customer Rating, number, optional)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the repair service.

### 8. **Comments** (Comments, text, optional)
Share any additional comments or details about your experience.

### 9. **Issue Resolution** (Issue Resolution, select_one, optional)
Indicate whether the issue was resolved or not.

### 10. **Repair Date** (Repair Date, date, optional)
Enter the date the repair was completed.

### 11. **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, optional)
Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

### 12. **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes, text, optional)
Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.

### 13. **Technician Rating** (Technician Rating, number, optional)
Rate the performance of the technician who assisted you.

### 14. **Technician Name** (Technician Name, select_one, optional)
Select the name of the technician who assisted you.

### 15. **Location** (Location, select_one, optional)
Select the location where the repair was performed.

### 16. **Visit Type** (Visit Type, select_one, optional)
Select the type of visit (e.g. repair, maintenance, etc.).

### 17. **Visit Date** (Visit Date, date, optional)
Enter the date of the visit.

### 18. **Visit Time** (Visit Time, time, optional)
Enter the time of the visit.

### 19. **Visit Duration** (Visit Duration, time, optional)
Enter the length of the visit.

### 20. **Customer Satisfaction** (Customer Satisfaction, select_one, optional)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.

### 21. **Issue Reopen** (Issue Reopen, select_one, optional)
Indicate whether the issue was reopened or not.

### 22. **Technician Comments** (Technician Comments, text, optional)
Enter any comments or feedback for the technician.

### 23. **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, text, optional)
Enter your signature to confirm the information provided.

### 24. **Technician Signature** (Technician Signature, text, optional)
Enter the technician's signature to confirm the information provided.

### 25. **Review** (Review, text, optional)
Enter a review or comment about your experience.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional", which means you can skip them if you don't have any information to provide. However, your feedback and comments are always appreciated and help us improve our services.
