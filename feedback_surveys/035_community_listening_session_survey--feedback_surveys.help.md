# Community Listening Session Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of community listening sessions, allowing us to understand their experiences, opinions, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee name (optional) if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions.
2. Enter the date of the session you attended.
3. List the key topics that were discussed during the session.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the session (Very effective, Effective, Somewhat effective, Not effective).
5. Share any additional feedback or comments you have about the session.
6. Check if you are willing to participate in future sessions.
7. Check if you agree to have your anonymized quotes published for future reference.
8. Optionally, enter your contact email if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Your name as the attendee of the session. This helps us track your feedback and contact you for follow-up discussions.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, required): The date of the session you attended.
* **Topics discussed** (`topics_discussed`, text, required): List the key topics that were discussed during the session.
* **Effectiveness of session** (`effectiveness_of_session`, select_one, required): Rate how effective the session was, with options from Very effective to Not effective.
* **Suggestions for future sessions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments you have about the session.
* **Willing to participate in future sessions** (`willingness_to_participate`, select_one, optional): Check if you are willing to participate in future sessions.
* **Permission to publish anonymized quotes** (`permission_to_publish`, select_one, optional): Check if you agree to have your anonymized quotes published for future reference.
* **Contact email for follow-up** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions.
* **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Share any other comments or suggestions you have about the session.
