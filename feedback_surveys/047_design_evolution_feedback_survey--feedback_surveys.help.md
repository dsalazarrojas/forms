# Design Evolution Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a feedback survey aimed at gathering end-user input on the design evolution of our product. It's essential to provide feedback that helps us understand what you like about the new design, what you don't, and how we can improve. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions honestly, and we'll use your input to shape the future of our design.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Read through the questions and answer them to the best of your ability.
*   Provide as much detail as you're comfortable sharing in the open text fields.
*   Select the aspects of the design you're providing feedback on from the "What aspect of the design is this feedback about?" dropdown menu.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the design by selecting a score in the "How would you rate this design?" field.
*   Finally, choose whether you'd like to submit or cancel your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think about this new design? (1)**: Please share your thoughts on the design, including your likes, dislikes, and any suggestions you may have. 
* **What aspect of the design is this feedback about? (3)**: Select all the aspects of the design you'd like to provide feedback on, such as Layout, Visual style, Functionality, or User experience. 
* **What is your feedback? (4)**: This is where you can provide specific, detailed feedback on the design. Be as concrete as possible so we can understand your thoughts.
* **How would you rate this design? (5)**: Rate your satisfaction with the design on a scale, with higher scores indicating higher satisfaction. 
* **Submit (6)**: Select this option to submit your feedback. If you're unsure or want to cancel, choose the "Cancel" option. 
* **Email/Phone (7, 8)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, enter your email or phone number here.
