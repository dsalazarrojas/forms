# crime_control_support_fund_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and survey responses from users about their experiences with crime control support services. We want to hear about your experiences and preferences to improve the support services provided by us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Details" field with your name and contact information.
2. In "Support Level", describe the level of support you have received or would like to receive from the crime control support service.
3. In "Engagement Preferences", tell us about your preferred method of engagement for support services (e.g., email, phone, in-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
* **Support Level** (Support Level, text, optional): Describe the level of support you have received or would like to receive from the crime control support service. For example, if you have received adequate support, you can write "I have received the support I needed." If you would like to receive more support, you can write "I would like to receive more support."
* **Engagement Preferences** (Engagement Preferences, text, optional): Tell us about your preferred method of engagement for support services. For example, if you would like to receive support via email, you can write "I would like to receive email support."
