# Decoy Development Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decoy Development Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users on their experience with our development team. This form helps us understand what you liked or disliked about our development process and what we can improve on. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide us with your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title that describes your feedback in the "Feedback Form" field.
2. Rate your experience from 1 to 5 in the "Form Rating" field.
3. Add any comments you have about your experience in the "Comment" field.
4. If applicable, provide any images or files that demonstrate your experience in the "Image" field.
5. Select any fields that you thought were well-executed or in need of improvement in the "Rated Fields" field.
6. Finally, add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your feedback.
* **Form Rating** (`form_rating`, number, required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
* **Comment** (`form_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or observations about your experience.
* **Image** (`form_image`, text, optional): Provide any images or files that demonstrate your experience.
* **Rated Fields** (`form_rated_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Select any fields that you felt were well-executed or in need of improvement.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Be specific and honest in your feedback.
* Try to keep your comments brief and to the point.
* If you have multiple images or files, you can upload them all in the "Image" field.
* You can select multiple fields in the "Rated Fields" field.
