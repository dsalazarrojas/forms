# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your daily experiences, mood, and stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through each page of the form, answering each question in as much detail as possible.
2. For text fields, simply type your responses into the provided input boxes.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your current state.
4. You can add notes to the note fields to provide additional context or insights.
5. Once you've completed all pages, click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did your morning go** (Morning Mood, text): This question is asking about your overall feeling and state of mind when waking up.
* **How was your day** (Day Mood, text): This question is asking about your emotional state throughout the day.
* **Prophets** (Prophets, text): This question is intended to gather any thoughts or ideas that have been on your mind.
* **How are you feeling now** (Evening Mood, text): This question is asking about your current emotional state at the end of the day.
* **How stressed do you feel** (Stress Level, text): This question is asking about your level of stress or anxiety at the moment.
* **Reflection time** (Reflection Time, note): This field is for any additional thoughts or insights you'd like to share.
* **End of Day** (End of Day, select_one): Choose how stressed you feel at the end of the day.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (Suggestions, select_multiple): Select any activities that might help you feel better, such as getting outside, exercising, or listening to music.
* **Goals** (Goals, select_multiple): Choose activities that you would like to do tomorrow or in the near future, such as getting enough sleep, getting outside, or exercising.
* **Notes** (Notes, note): This field is for any additional comments or insights you'd like to share.
* **Submitted by** (Submitted by, text): This field is for your name or username, if you'd like to provide it.
