# Motivational Speaker Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about the motivational speaker's performance. It helps us understand how well the speaker met the expectations of the audience and provides valuable insights to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Evaluate the speaker's overall performance by rating them on a scale of 1-5.
2.  Choose how well the speaker met your expectations by selecting "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the options.
3.  Rate the talk using the same scale as above.
4.  Select how well the talk met your expectations in terms of content, engagement, and presentation.
5.  Provide any comments about the speaker's performance.
6.  Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
7.  Enter your name so we can address you in future communications.
8.  Type the name of your company or organization, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Speaker Rating** (1, number, optional): Rate the speaker's overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Speaker Evaluation** (select_one, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, optional): Choose how well the speaker met your expectations.
*   **Talk Rating** (1, number, optional): Rate the talk on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Talk Evaluation** (select_one, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, optional): Select how well the talk met your expectations.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Enter any comments about the speaker's performance.
*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
*   **Name** (text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you in future communications.
*   **Company** (text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization, if applicable.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can submit the form without completing any of the fields if you'd like. However, providing feedback is valuable to us and helps us improve future events!
