# Best Radio Journalist Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and survey responses from radio journalists. It is used to evaluate the service's quality, effectiveness, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2.  Fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3.  Rate your experience with the service by selecting "Primary selection" and providing a whole number for "Quantity or rating".
4.  Select "Agreement" options that best apply to your experience with the service.
5.  If required, provide "Estimated value" in decimal format.
6.  Enter any additional comments or details you'd like to share.
7.  Finally, provide any closing remarks in the "Final comments or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: This field collects your legal full name.
*   **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if outside the local region.
*   **Date**: Select the relevant date from the available options.
*   **Primary selection**: Choose the option that best reflects your experience with the service.
*   **Quantity or rating**: Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
*   **Agreement**: Select any options that apply to your experience with the service.
*   **Estimated value**: Provide an estimated value in decimal format if required.
*   **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required.
*   **Final comments or notes**: Provide any closing remarks or additional information.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields with 'required' are filled in correctly.
*   Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and relevance.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
