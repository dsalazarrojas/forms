# Winter Apparel Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on our winter clothing items. It covers various aspects of our winter clothing, including quality, features, relevance, and overall satisfaction. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to your feedback on the winter clothing.
2. Rate the quality and features of the clothing on a scale of 1-10.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Enter your age group.
5. Provide your email address and other relevant information.
