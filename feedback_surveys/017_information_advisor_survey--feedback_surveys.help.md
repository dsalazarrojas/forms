# Information Advisor Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their interactions with a prophet's advisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your interaction with the advisor, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Rate the advisor's quality, communication skills, and satisfaction level using the provided scales.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the "Do you have any additional feedback" field.
4. If you would like to provide contact information, you can enter your email address and/or phone number in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What was the prophets advisor interaction like?
• **What was the prophets advisor interaction like** (text, optional): This field allows you to describe your interaction with the advisor. Provide a brief summary of your experience, including any positive or negative comments.

### How satisfied were you with your advisor?
• **How satisfied were you with your advisor** (number, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the advisor, using the scale: 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).

### How good was the advisor quality?
• **How good was the advisor quality** (number, optional): Rate the advisor's quality, using the scale: 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).

### How would you rate the advisors communication skills?
• **How would you rate the advisors communication skills** (select_one, optional): Rate the advisor's communication skills as: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### Do you have any additional feedback?
• **Do you have any additional feedback** (note, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use this field to provide them.

### Email
• **Email** (email, optional): If you would like to provide your email address, enter it here. This will help us contact you for further questions or clarification.

### Phone
• **Phone** (text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number, enter it here. This will help us contact you for further questions or clarification.

### Date
• **Date** (date, optional): If you would like to provide the date of your interaction, enter it here. This will help us track the timing of your interaction.

### Time
• **Time** (time, optional): If you would like to provide the time of your interaction, enter it here. This will help us track the timing of your interaction.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as honestly as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Do you have any additional feedback" field.
* If you would like to provide contact information, enter your email address and/or phone number.
