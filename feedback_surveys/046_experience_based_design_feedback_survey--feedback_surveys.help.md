# Experience Based Design Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Experience Based Design Feedback Survey" is designed to gather user feedback on their experience with the product or service. This survey is used to help the design team understand user satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the design.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the page you want to provide feedback on. This survey is divided into multiple pages, and each page is designed to address a specific aspect of your experience.
2. Read each question carefully and answer according to your genuine experience.
3. For questions with options, choose the one that best describes your response.
4. If a question is not applicable or you don't have an opinion, please select the "No" option or leave it blank.
5. Enter your email address and phone number if you are willing to provide them.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the title of the survey, which is "Experience Based Design Feedback Survey".
* **User Experience (page 2)**: This question asks about your overall experience with the product or service. Please select "Yes" if you are satisfied with your experience, or "No" if you are not.
* **Second Page (page 3)**: This question is about specific aspects of your experience. Select "Yes" for each aspect that applies to you.
* **Feedback (page 4)**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email (page 5)**: If you are willing to provide your email address, please enter it so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone (page 6)**: If you are willing to provide your phone number, please enter it so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Date (page 7)**: This question is about the date you experienced the product or service. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time (page 8)**: This question is about the time you experienced the product or service. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Note (page 9)**: This is an additional note field for any further comments or feedback.
* **Note (page 10)**: This is another note field for any additional comments or feedback.
