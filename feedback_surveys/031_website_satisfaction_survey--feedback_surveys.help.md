# Website Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback and satisfaction ratings about your experience on the website. Your responses will help us improve the website's functionality and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. You may skip questions that you do not have an opinion about.
3. Use the options provided for select type fields to choose your response.
4. Submit your survey after completing all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Satisfaction** (`page_one`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your overall satisfaction with the website.
* **What could we improve?** (`page_two`, `text`, required/optional): Share your thoughts on how we can improve the website.
* **Would you like to leave feedback?** (`page_three`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **How would you rate your experience?** (`page_four`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **What is your contact information?** (`page_five`, `text`, required/optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or questions, please provide your contact information.
* **What would you like to see improved?** (`page_six`, `text`, required/optional): Suggest areas for improvement on the website.
* **How would you rate this experience?** (`page_seven`, `number`, required/optional): Rate this experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Would you recommend this website?** (`page_eight`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you would recommend the website to others, or 'No' if you would not.
* **Would you like to participate in further surveys?** (`page_nine`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the ways you would like to participate in further surveys (Yes, phone, Yes, email, Yes, both, No, do not contact me).
* **Do you want to be contacted?** (`page_ten`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the ways you would like to be contacted (Yes, phone, Yes, email, Yes, both, No, do not contact me).

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Remember, your feedback is confidential and will help us improve the website.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
