# e_commerce_shipping_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their shipping experiences with an e-commerce platform. The information collected will help the platform improve its shipping processes and provide better services to its customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the customer.
2. Choose the status of your order (delivered, not delivered, partially delivered, not yet delivered, or cancelled).
3. Provide any additional shipping details in the text field.
4. Select the carrier (UPS, FedEx, USPS, or other) used for the shipment (if applicable).
5. Rate your delivery experience (delivered on time, early, late, or unknown).
6. Choose how you were contacted (phone, email, in person, or online).
7. Rate the performance of the carrier (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
8. Rate the packaging quality (satisfactory, good, fair, or poor).
9. Check any damage to your order (yes or no).
10. If there were damages, select the type (minor or major).
11. Rate your overall satisfaction with the order (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, or unknown).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This is the name of the customer who is submitting the feedback.
* **Order Details**: Please select the status of your order, which will help us understand the situation.
	+ Order Delivered: The order was received as expected.
	+ Order Not Delivered: The order was not received at all.
	+ Order Partially Delivered: The order was received, but not entirely as expected.
	+ Order Not Yet Delivered: The order is still en route or has not been processed.
	+ Order Cancelled: The order was cancelled by the customer or platform.
* **Shipping Details**: If you have any additional information about the shipping experience, please provide it here.
* **Carrier Used**: If you know which carrier was used for the shipment, please select it from the options. Note that this field is optional.
* **Delivery Experience**: Rate your delivery experience.
	+ Delivered On Time: The delivery was made according to the expected schedule.
	+ Early: The delivery was made before the expected time.
	+ Late: The delivery was made after the expected time.
	+ Unknown: You are not sure about the delivery time.
* **Communication**: How you were contacted about the order.
	+ Phone: You were contacted by phone.
	+ Email: You were contacted by email.
	+ In Person: You were contacted in person.
	+ Online: You were contacted online.
* **Carrier Performance**: Rate the performance of the carrier used.
	+ Excellent: The carrier performed well and met expectations.
	+ Good: The carrier performed adequately.
	+ Fair: The carrier performed below expectations.
	+ Poor: The carrier performed poorly.
* **Packaging**: Rate the quality of the packaging used.
	+ Satisfactory: The packaging was satisfactory and adequate.
	+ Good: The packaging was good and above expectations.
	+ Fair: The packaging was below expectations.
	+ Poor: The packaging was poor.
* **Order Damages**: If there were any damages to the order, please check yes and select the type of damages (minor or major).
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the order.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the order.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the order.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the order.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the order.
	+ Unknown: You are not sure about your satisfaction.
* **Customer Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback about the order.
