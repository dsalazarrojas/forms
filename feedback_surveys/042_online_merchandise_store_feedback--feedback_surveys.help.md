# online_merchandise_store_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and comments from customers about their experience with our online store's merchandise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Rate your experience by selecting a number in the "Rating" field (1-5).
3.  Enter the date of your purchase in the "Purchase Date" field.
4.  Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience with us in the "Comments" field.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you made your purchase from our store. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your experience with our store.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you have one associated with your purchase, so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
