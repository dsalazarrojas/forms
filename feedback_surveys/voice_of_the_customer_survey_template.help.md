# voice_of_the_customer_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback and opinions on our Voice of the Customer initiative, which aims to improve our listening and responding processes. This survey will help us understand your experiences, satisfaction levels, and perception of our feedback process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the options that best describe your experiences and opinions.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply if your answer falls under multiple categories.
3. Write any additional comments or suggestions in the designated text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Satisfaction Level** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction level with participating in our Voice of the Customer initiatives. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
- **Frequency of Engagement** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Quarterly`, `Rarely`, `Never`): This question asks about how often you typically engage with Voice of the Customer activities such as surveys and feedback forms. Select the option that best describes your frequency of interaction.
- **Feedback Process Perceptions** (`Easy to use`, `Fast`, `Reliable`, `Actionable`, `Transparent`, `Confusing`, `Time-consuming`): This question asks for your perceptions of the Voice of the Customer feedback process. Choose all options that apply to describe your experience.
- **Feedback Motivation** (`Positive experience`, `Negative experience`, `Specific issue`, `General improvement`, `Requested feedback`): This question asks what motivated you to provide feedback today. Select the option that best describes your motivation.
- **Feedback Heard and Acted Upon** (`Always`, `Often`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`): This question asks how you feel about whether your feedback is heard and acted upon effectively. Select the option that best describes your experience.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`text`): Write any suggestions or ideas you have to improve our Voice of the Customer feedback mechanisms in this text field.
- **Additional Comments** (`text`): Write any additional comments or suggestions related to our Voice of the Customer program in this text field.

## Tips

- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
- You can select multiple options for the "Feedback Process Perceptions" question if your experience falls under multiple categories.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
