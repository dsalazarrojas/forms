# Personal Organizer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Organizer Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from end-users about their experience with the Personal Organizer. This form is intended to gauge user satisfaction, understand their thoughts on how to improve the tool, and gather any additional comments or concerns they may have. This form is optional and can be filled out by users who want to provide input on their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as follows:

* Select one of the provided options for each question (e.g., "Very positive", "Somewhat positive", etc.)
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* Write any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
