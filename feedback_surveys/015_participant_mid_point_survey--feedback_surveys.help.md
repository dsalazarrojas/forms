# participant_mid_point_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants at the midpoint of a program or initiative. It's used to monitor and evaluate the progress of participants, identify areas that need improvement, and inform future program planning and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field. This is a required field that should be filled in accurately to identify you as the participant.
2. Enter your **email_address** in the second field. This is a required field that will be used to communicate with you about your participation and any updates or reminders.
3. Fill in your **phone_number** in the third field. This is a required field that will be used to contact you about your progress and any questions or concerns.
4. Provide an answer to the **Feedback Question** in the fourth field. This field is optional but helps us understand your thoughts and opinions about the program.
5. Rate your progress in the **Rating** field (fifth). This is a required field that will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the program.
6. Type in your **Comments** (sixth) if you have any additional thoughts or feedback about the program. This field is optional but helpful in providing us with more detailed information.
7. If applicable, enter the **Date Submitted** (seventh) and **Time Submitted** (eighth) fields to record the time of submission. These fields are optional.
8. You can provide a **Participant Survey Response** (ninth) if you have any comments or feedback about the program. This field is optional.
9. Indicate your **Program Participation** (tenth) status by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'. This is a required field that helps us understand your participation status in the program.
10. Select the **Program Outcomes** (eleventh) that you have experienced. These are the outcomes that the program aimed to achieve, and selecting them will help us understand your progress. This is a required field.
11. Provide any additional **Additional Feedback** (twelfth) in this field. This is optional but helpful in providing more detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your name as the participant in the program.
* **email_address** (`email`, `text`, required): Your email address that will be used for communication.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your phone number that will be used for contact.
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, `text`, optional): Answer the question to provide us with your thoughts and opinions about the program.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your progress and satisfaction with the program (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, optional): Record the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, optional): Record the time you submitted this form.
* **Participant Survey Response** (`participant_survey_response`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the program.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your participation status in the program (Yes or No).
* **Program Outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the program outcomes you have experienced (Yes for each outcome).
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the program.
