# operational_strategy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your position and responsibilities within the organization, allowing us to better understand your role in the operational strategy. Your input will help us refine and improve the strategy to better suit the needs of our team members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Page 1: What is Your Position?**
	* This field is for you to describe your current position within the organization.
2. **Page 2: What are Your Main Responsibilities?**
	* This field is for you to list your primary job duties and responsibilities.
3. **Page 3: Which Department?**
	* This field asks you to indicate which department you belong to, to help us understand the scope of your role within the organization.
	* Select all departments that apply to your position.
4. **Page 4: What are Your Goals?**
	* This field is for you to describe your personal and professional goals related to the operational strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is Your Position?**
	+ (Page 1, text field)
	+ Please enter a brief description of your current position within the organization.
* **What are Your Main Responsibilities?**
	+ (Page 2, text field)
	+ List your primary job duties and responsibilities.
* **Which Department?**
	+ (Page 3, select multiple field)
	+ Indicate all departments that you belong to, to help us understand your scope of work.
* **What are Your Goals?**
	+ (Page 4, text field)
	+ Describe your personal and professional goals related to the operational strategy.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your position and responsibilities.
* If you have multiple departments, select all that apply.
* Remember to focus on your goals related to the operational strategy, and avoid vague or unrelated goals.
