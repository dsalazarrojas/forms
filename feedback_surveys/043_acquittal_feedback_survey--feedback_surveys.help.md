# Acquittal Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients or customers on their experience with the legal team during the acquittal process. The feedback will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights to enhance the quality of service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your Case Reference Number, which is an internal reference number for matching your responses with the relevant case.
2. Enter the Date of Acquittal, the date of the actual acquittal.
3. Choose your response for Overall Experience with Legal Team, rating your satisfaction with the service provided.
4. Choose your response for Clarity of Communication, indicating how well the team explained the process.
5. Choose your response for Support Received During Trial, describing the level of emotional and logistical support you received.
6. (Optional) If you would like to provide specific Key Strengths of the Defense, please enter them here.
7. (Optional) If you have suggestions for areas where the legal process could be improved, please enter them here.
8. Choose how likely you are to Recommend Counsel, on a scale of 1 (Not likely) to 10 (Very Likely).
9. If you have Additional Comments on the Outcome, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Reference Number** (`case_reference_number`, `text`, required): Enter the internal reference number for the case, to help us match your feedback with the relevant case.
* **Date of Acquittal** (`date_of_acquittal`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the actual acquittal.
* **Overall Experience with Legal Team** (`overall_experience_with_legal_team`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the service provided, using the following options: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Clarity of Communication** (`clarity_of_communication`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well the team explained the process, using the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent.
* **Support Received During Trial** (`support_received_during_trial`, `select_one`, required): Describe the level of emotional and logistical support you received, using the following options: Insufficient, Adequate, Outstanding.
* **Key Strengths of the Defense** (`key_strengths_of_the_defense`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to highlight specific strengths of the defense, please enter them here.
* **Areas for Legal Process Improvement** (`areas_for_legal_process_improvement`, `text`, optional): If you have suggestions for areas where the legal process could be improved, please enter them here.
* **Likelihood to Recommend Counsel** (`likelihood_to_recommend_counsel`, `select_one`, required): Choose how likely you are to recommend the counsel, on a scale of 1 (Not likely) to 10 (Very Likely).
* **Additional Comments on the Outcome** (`additional_comments_on_the_outcome`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide additional comments on the outcome, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, you can refer to your experience with the legal team and provide a response that reflects your feelings.
* If you've completed the form, please review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your experience.

Note: The information provided in this form will help improve our service, and your responses will remain confidential.
