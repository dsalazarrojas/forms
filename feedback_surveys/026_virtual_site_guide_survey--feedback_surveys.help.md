### Virtual Site Guide Survey - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Virtual Site Guide Survey is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience using our virtual site guide. This feedback will help us improve and make the site more user-friendly.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the questions.
2. For each question, provide your honest feedback and answers.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome Message (1)**: This is a text field where you can leave a brief message about your experience with the virtual site guide.
* **How would you rate your virtual tour experience? (2)**: Choose a numerical rating from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) to rate your experience.
* **What are your suggestions for us? (3)**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any suggestions or comments about the site.
* **Contact Information (Email Address) (4)**: If you have any questions or want us to follow up with you, please provide your email address.

#### Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly, and feel free to provide as much detail as you like in the free-text fields.
* If you have trouble understanding any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is highly valued and will help us improve the site for everyone!
