# Group Participation Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a group's availability and preferences to facilitate scheduling and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by selecting your available days and time slots.
2. Choose your preferred days and time slots.
3. Select the activities you are interested in participating in.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
5. Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Participation Poll Form** (`group_participation_poll_form`, text, required): This is the main form section where you can select your available and preferred days and time slots.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Choose the days of the week you are available to participate in activities. Select all days that apply.
* **Available Time Slots** (`available_time_slots`, select_multiple, required): Select the time slots you are available to participate in activities.
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, select_multiple, required): Choose the days you prefer to participate in activities.
* **Preferred Time Slots** (`preferred_time_slots`, select_multiple, required): Select the time slots you prefer to participate in activities.
* **Interested Activities** (`interested_activities`, select_one, required): Choose one activity you are interested in participating in.
* **Available Activities** (`available_activities`, select_multiple, required): Select all activities you are available to participate in.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that others can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that others can reach you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional information or comments about your availability and preferences.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting your available and preferred days and time slots.
* Select all activities you are interested in and available to participate in.
* Enter your email address and phone number if you want others to be able to contact you.
