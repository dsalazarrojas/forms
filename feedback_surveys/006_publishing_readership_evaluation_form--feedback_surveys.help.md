# Publishing Readership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the readership of publishing content, gathering feedback from users on their satisfaction, format preferences, and content quality.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter a title for the form in the "Publishing Readership Evaluation Form" field.
2. Select the relevant category for the feedback in the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Write a brief description of your experience with the content in the "Description" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the content in the "Satisfaction" field by selecting from the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, or Not satisfied.
5. Choose your preferred format for reading the content in the "Format preference" field by selecting from: Print, Digital, or Both.
6. Evaluate the quality of the content in the "Content quality" field by selecting from: High, Medium, or Low.
7. Rate your engagement with the content in the "Engagement" field by selecting from: High, Medium, or Low.
8. Provide any additional feedback about the content in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a short title for the form to identify it.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the form, which helps us understand the type of feedback you're providing.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of your experience with the content.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the content on a scale from Very satisfied to Not satisfied.
* **Format preference** (`format_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred format for reading the content, which can be Print, Digital, or Both.
* **Content quality** (`content_quality`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of the content, with options from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your engagement with the content, from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the content.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
- You can select multiple options in the "Satisfaction" field if applicable.
- If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
