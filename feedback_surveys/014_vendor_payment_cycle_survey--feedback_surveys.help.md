# Vendor Payment Cycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Cycle Survey is designed to collect feedback from end-users on the vendor payment cycle process. This information will help the finance team improve and refine the payment cycle process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the process used for the vendor payment cycle.
2. Choose the payment terms that apply to your experience.
3. Enter the payment cycle length in the format 'X months/years'.
4. Select the frequency of the payment cycle.
5. Choose the type of payment cycle.
6. Select the status of the payment cycle.
7. Provide a brief description of the payment terms (optional).
8. Select the frequency of payment terms.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment cycle.
10. Enter your email address to contact you in case we need more information.
11. Enter your phone number for emergency contact (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **payment_cycle_process**: Select the process used for the vendor payment cycle.
* **payment_terms**: Choose the payment terms that apply to your experience.
* **payment_cycle_length**: Enter the payment cycle length in the format 'X months/years'.
* **payment_cycle_frequency**: Select the frequency of the payment cycle.
* **payment_cycle_type**: Choose the type of payment cycle.
* **payment_cycle_status**: Select the status of the payment cycle.
* **payment_terms_description**: Briefly describe the payment terms.
* **payment_terms_frequency**: Select the frequency of payment terms.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment cycle.
* **email**: Enter your email address to contact you in case we need more information.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact (optional).
* **payment_cycle_comment**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the payment cycle.
