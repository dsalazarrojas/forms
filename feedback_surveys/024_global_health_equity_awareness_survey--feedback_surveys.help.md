# Global Health Equity Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Equity Awareness Survey is designed to collect feedback and survey data from users to raise awareness about global health equity issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Fill in your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Select your gender from the `gender` dropdown menu.
5. Enter your age in the `age` field.
6. Fill in your phone number in the `phone` field.
7. Select a date from the calendar in the `date` field.
8. Select a time from the clock in the `time` field.
9. Write any additional comments or thoughts in the `survey_comments` field.
10. Select any relevant options from the checkbox groups in the `survey_comments_checkbox_1` to `survey_comments_checkbox_12` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or subject of your survey response.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for demographic purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for demographic purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender to help us understand your perspective.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age to help us understand your demographics.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date from the calendar for timing purposes.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time from the clock for timing purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`survey_comments`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to provide additional comments.
* **Other Comments** (`survey_comments_other`, text, optional): Write any other comments you may have.
* **Notes** (`survey_comments_note`, note, optional): Write any additional notes you may have.
* **Survey Comments Checkboxes** (`survey_comments_checkbox_1` to `survey_comments_checkbox_12`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant options to provide more specific comments.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* This survey is optional and anonymous.
* All responses will be treated confidentially.
* If you need more time to complete the survey, save your progress and come back later.
