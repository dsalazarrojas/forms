# Militarism And Conflict Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Militarism And Conflict Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from respondents about their thoughts and opinions on militarism and conflict. The form aims to gather qualitative data on respondents' perspectives on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each text prompt carefully and understand what information is being requested.
2. Provide a comprehensive response to each question or prompt in the corresponding field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the last field.
4. If you want to provide additional feedback or comments, you can use the optional field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro_text_1** (Required): This field asks for your initial thoughts on the topic of militarism and conflict.
* **intro_text_2** (Required): This field seeks your understanding of the relationship between militarism and conflict.
* **intro_text_3** (Required): Please provide your perspective on how militarism has contributed to conflict.
* **intro_text_4** (Required): Share your thoughts on the impact of militarism on society.
* **intro_text_5** (Required): Reflect on your understanding of the causes of militarism.
* **intro_text_6** (Optional): If you have any additional comments or insights on militarism and conflict, please share them here.
* **intro_text_7** (Required): This field asks for your opinions on the effectiveness of conflict resolution strategies in addressing militarism.
* **intro_text_8** (Required): Please provide your thoughts on the role of international organizations in addressing conflict.
* **intro_text_9** (Required): Share your perspective on how militarism has influenced your personal values or attitudes.
* **intro_text_10** (Required): This field seeks your understanding of the relationship between militarism and economic interests.
* **intro_text_11** (Optional): If you have any further thoughts or comments on the topic, please use this field to provide them.

## Tips
Please answer each question or prompt honestly and thoroughly. Your responses will help us understand your perspectives on militarism and conflict.
