# Residential Lighting Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Lighting Quality Survey is designed to gather feedback from residents about their experiences with their lighting systems. This survey aims to assess the performance, energy efficiency, and overall satisfaction of their lighting fixtures and systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your experiences with your lighting system.
2. For the "Lighting Satisfaction" question, choose one of the two options: "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
3. If you are not satisfied with your lighting system, provide additional feedback in the "Why Not Satisfied" question by selecting all that apply.
4. You may also provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lighting Performance** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Lighting Performance, text, no): This field asks for your subjective assessment of the lighting performance in your home, such as brightness, clarity, and overall satisfaction with the lighting. Please provide a brief description.
* **Energy Efficiency** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Energy Efficiency, number, no): This field asks you to estimate your energy bill for lighting in your home. Please input a numerical value in the format of "XX dollars" or "YY kWh".
* **Energy Usage** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Energy Usage, number, no): This field asks you to estimate how much you think your lighting system consumes in energy. Please input a numerical value in the format of "XX kWh" or "YY kWh".
* **Lighting Satisfaction** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Lighting Satisfaction, select_one, no): This question asks how satisfied you are with your lighting system. Choose "Satisfied" if you are happy with the current lighting, or "Dissatisfied" if you are not.
* **Why Not Satisfied** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Why Not Satisfied, select_multiple, no): If you are not satisfied with your lighting system, please select all the reasons that apply to you. This will help us understand what we can improve.
* **Comments** (<name>, <type>, required/optional:  Comments, note, no): This field is a space for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your lighting system.
