# Henna Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have experienced a henna session. The feedback will help the event planner and henna artist improve their services, ensuring that future clients have an exceptional experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields.
2. Provide feedback on your overall level of client satisfaction.
3. Share any comments about your experience with the henna session.
4. Rate your experience by selecting one of the provided options.
5. Indicate your level of recommendation for the artist and the salon.
6. Enter a valid email address (if you wish to receive updates or special offers from us).
7. Provide contact information (mobile number or alternative contact information).
8. Share information about the event planner (if relevant).
9. Share information about the henna artist (if relevant).
10. Share details about the session you attended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_satisfaction**: This is a numerical rating of your experience. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **comments**: Use this field to share any comments or feedback about your experience. Try to keep it brief and to the point.
* **rating**: Select one of the provided options to rate your experience (5, 4, 3, 2, or 1).
* **recommendation**: Choose all that apply to indicate your level of recommendation for the artist and the salon.
* **email**: If you wish to receive updates, special offers, or notifications from us, enter a valid email address here.
* **contact_number**: This is your contact information. Please provide your mobile number or alternative contact information.
* **event_planner_info**: Share information about the event planner you worked with (if applicable).
* **artist_info**: Share information about the henna artist you worked with (if applicable).
* **session_info**: Share details about the session you attended (if you had one). This can include the name of the session, date, and time.
* **date_of_session**: Enter the date of your session.
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of your session.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of your session.
* **henna_artist**: Share information about the henna artist who worked with you (if different from the one mentioned above).
* **salon_info**: Share information about the salon where you had your session (if applicable).
