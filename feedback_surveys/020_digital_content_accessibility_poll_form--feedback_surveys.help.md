# Digital Content Accessibility Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback or opinions from users about the accessibility of digital content on a website or application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand its purpose and questions.
2. Answer the questions and select the relevant options for each field.
3. Provide your feedback and comments in the "feedback" field.
4. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter any additional information in the "phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Digital Content Accessibility Poll Form** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): A title for the form, which is already filled out.
* **Accessibility Features** (`accessibility_features`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the two options to answer this question. Unfortunately, the options are not clearly labeled, so you may need to guess or assume what they are asking about.
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the three options. Again, the options are not clearly labeled, so you may need to guess or assume what they are asking about.
* **Accessibility Barriers** (`accessibility_barriers`, `note`, required/optional): This field is not used in the form (all options are empty), but it's a free-form text field where you can enter any additional information or comments about the content's accessibility.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the three options to answer this question. The options are "Easy to navigate", "Difficult to navigate", and an unspecified option. 
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your comments or feedback about the content's accessibility in this text field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number in this field (optional).

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your feedback and answers.
* Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
* If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in the "phone" field.
* Take your time to review the form and answer the questions carefully.
