# Expense Governance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your understanding of our organization's expense policies and procedures. Your responses will help us identify areas where we can improve communication and support for expense management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate answer.
2. For fields marked as "select_multiple" or "select_one," choose one or more options that apply to you.
3. Enter your comments or suggestions in the "suggestion_comments" field.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finance and Compliance Teams**: This question asks about your departmental affiliation within our organization.
* **Do you understand our expense policies?**: This question assesses your level of familiarity with our company's expense policies.
* **What type of training would be helpful to improve your understanding of expense policies?**: This question is meant to gather suggestions on how we can better support you in understanding and applying our policies.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving our expense policies?**: This question invites you to provide any ideas or recommendations for enhancing our expense policies.
* **Finance Department**: This question is about your relationship with our Finance Department.
* **Compliance Department**: This question is about your relationship with our Compliance Department.
* **HR Department**: This question is about your relationship with our HR Department.
* **Please describe any suggestions**: This is the field where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving our expense policies.
