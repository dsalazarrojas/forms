# Organizational Evolution Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from employees on various aspects of the organization, including employee feedback, change initiatives, team performance, leadership support, communication, and more. This feedback will help inform organizational changes and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Provide detailed and honest feedback where possible.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers that explain your thoughts and opinions.
5. Ensure all required fields are completed.
6. Submit the form once you have reviewed and completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Feedback (1)
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required/optional): This field allows you to provide feedback on your overall experience working in the organization.
*   Please provide any thoughts or comments on how you feel about the organization.

### 2. Change Initiatives (2)
* **Change Initiatives** (`change_initiatives`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks about the organization's change initiatives.
*   Please select the options that apply to your situation.

### 3. Feedback on Change (3)
* **Feedback on Change** (`feedback_on_change`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing feedback on specific changes within the organization.
*   Provide any comments or suggestions on the changes you have experienced.

### 4. Organizational Structure (4)
* **Organizational Structure** (`organizational_structure`, text, required/optional): This field is for commenting on the organization's structure.
*   Please provide any comments or suggestions on the organization's structure.

### 5. Team Performance (5)
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, text, required/optional): This field is for feedback on team performance.
*   Provide any thoughts or comments on team performance.

### 6. Leadership Support (6)
* **Leadership Support** (`leadership_support`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing feedback on leadership support.
*   Please comment on the level of support you have received from leadership.

### 7. Communication (7)
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required/optional): This field is for feedback on communication within the organization.
*   Comment on the organization's communication style and effectiveness.

### 8. Employee Suggestions (8)
* **Employee Suggestions** (`employee_suggestions`, text, required/optional): This field is for sharing any suggestions you may have for improving the organization.
*   Provide any ideas or comments on suggestions you think would benefit the organization.

### 9. Next Steps (9)
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required/optional): This field is for indicating any next steps you would recommend for the organization.
*   Provide any suggestions on what the organization should do next to improve.

### 10-21. Employee Feedback 2-15 (10)
* **Employee Feedback 2** (`employee_feedback_2`, text, required/optional): These fields are for providing feedback on specific aspects of the organization.
*   Please provide any thoughts or comments on each field.

### 22. Submission Status (22)
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field indicates the status of your submission.
*   Choose the options that apply to your submission status.

### 23. Employee Rating (23)
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, number, required/optional): This field is for rating your overall experience with the organization.
*   Provide a numerical rating between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

Note: This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees. Please answer honestly and provide concrete examples whenever possible. Your feedback is valuable to the organization's growth and improvement.
