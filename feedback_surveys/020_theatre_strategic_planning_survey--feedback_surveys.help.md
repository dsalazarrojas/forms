# Theatre Strategic Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
Theatre Strategic Planning Survey is a form designed to collect information from theatre organizations about their current challenges, priorities, and resource allocation. This will help us better understand your needs and provide more relevant guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and concisely, providing specific details about your theatre organization.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For free-text questions, provide clear and detailed responses.
4. If you have an "Other" option for an audience segment, please specify the segment in the text field.
