# Operations Transformation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operations Transformation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from users on the operations transformation process. This feedback is essential for the assigned team to make improvements and adjustments to the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions that apply to your experience with the operations transformation process.
2. Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
3. Be honest and objective in your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_outcomes** (1): Describe the outcomes of the operations transformation process. This may include benefits, achievements, or results.
* **what_challenges** (2): Identify the challenges you faced during the operations transformation process. This may include difficulties, obstacles, or limitations.
* **suggestions_for_improvement** (3): Suggest ways to improve the operations transformation process. Be specific and provide concrete ideas.
* **comments** (4): Add any additional comments or remarks about the operations transformation process.
* **assigned_team** (5): Select the team that was assigned to the operations transformation process.
* **email** (6): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion.
* **phone** (7): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion.
* **date** (8): Enter the date of the operations transformation process completion or the date when you first became aware of it.
* **time** (9): Enter the time of the operations transformation process completion or the time when you first became aware of it.
* **notes** (10): Add any additional notes or comments about the operations transformation process.
* **assigned_tool** (11): Select the tool used in the operations transformation process (e.g., chatjimmy).
* **form_id** (12): Enter your form ID (if you know it).
* **category** (13): Enter the category of the operations transformation process (e.g., team, project, etc.).
* **description** (14): Enter a brief description of the operations transformation process.
* **category_label** (15): Enter a label for the category of the operations transformation process.
* **description_label** (16): Enter a label for the description of the operations transformation process.
* **form_id_label** (17): Enter a label for the form ID.
* **category_label_label** (18): Enter a label for the category label.
* **description_label_label** (19): Enter a label for the description label.
* **form_id_label_label** (20): Enter a label for the form ID label.
* **category_label_label_label** (21): Enter a label for the category label label.
* **description_label_label_label** (22): Enter a label for the description label label.
* **operations_transformation_survey_title_label** (23): Enter a label for the operations transformation survey title.
* **operations_transformation_survey_title** (24): Enter a title for the operations transformation survey.
* **operations_transformation_survey_title_label_label** (25): Enter a label for the operations transformation survey title label.

Note: Fields with an *asterisk* are optional and should be used at your discretion.
