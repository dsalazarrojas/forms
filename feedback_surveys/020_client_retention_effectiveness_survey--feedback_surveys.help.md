**Client Retention Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The **Client Retention Effectiveness Survey** aims to understand your thoughts and experiences with our company. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and better serve you and other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions carefully, and feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and opinions.
3. If you have no preference or experience, select "N/A" (Not Applicable) for that field.
4. You can submit your responses when you've completed the entire form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name or Organization** (`client_name`, text, required)
Your name or organization name, so we can recognize you and personalize our response.

### 2. **How Long Have You Been a Customer?** (`customer_tenure`, select_one, required)
Choose how long you've been our customer: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or 10+ years.

### 3. **How Loyal Are You to Our Brand?** (`loyalty_rating`, select_one, required)
Rate your level of loyalty: Extremely loyal, Very loyal, Somewhat loyal, Not very loyal, Not at all loyal.

### 4. **What Are Your Main Reasons for Staying With Us?** (`primary_reasons_staying`, select_multiple, required)
Select all that apply: Quality of service, Competitive pricing, Customer service, Brand reputation, Loyalty rewards, Convenience, Other (please specify).

### 5. **Overall Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required)
Rate your overall satisfaction: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

### 6. **Quality of Products and Services** (`product_service_quality`, select_one, required)
Rate our product and service quality: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor.

### 7. **Perceived Value for Money** (`value_perception`, select_one, required)
Rate your perceived value: Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, Very poor value.

### 8. **Customer Support Experience** (`customer_support_experience`, select_one, required)
Rate our customer support: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very poor.

### 9. **Areas Needing Improvement** (`areas_needing_improvement`, text, optional)
Tell us what we could do better.

### 10. **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, number, required)
Rate how likely you are to recommend us: 0 (not at all) to 10 (extremely likely).

### 11. **What Factors Could Cause You to Leave?** (`factors_affecting_retention`, text, optional)
Tell us what might make you leave us.

### 12. **Are You Considering Alternatives?** (`competitive_alternatives`, select_one, required)
Are you researching or evaluating alternatives: False, Occasionally researching, Actively evaluating options, Close to switching, Already have an alternative.

### 13. **Interest in Loyalty or Engagement Programs** (`engagement_programs`, select_one, optional)
How interested are you in loyalty or engagement programs: Very interested, Interested, Somewhat interested, Not interested.

### 14. **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional)
Share any additional thoughts or ideas.

Please answer honestly, and we'll use your feedback to improve our services. Thank you for participating!
