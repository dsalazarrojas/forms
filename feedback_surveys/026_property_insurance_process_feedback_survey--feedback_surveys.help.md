# Property Insurance Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from property insurance customers on their experiences with the current property insurance process. Your input will help us improve the process to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2. Use the scale provided for numeric questions (e.g., Overall Experience).
3. Select the relevant options for multiple choice questions (e.g., Timeliness, Service Quality, Resolution).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments and Additional Comments sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience (Overall Experience, number, required)**: Rate your overall experience with our property insurance process, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication (Communication, text, optional)**: Describe your interactions with our communication channels, such as phone, email, or in-person meetings.
* **Timeliness (Timeliness, select_one, required)**: Indicate whether our response was timely (within 24 hours) or not.
* **Service Quality (Service Quality, select_multiple, required)**: Choose all the ways in which our service was good or poor (e.g., Good: Responsive, Friendly, Knowledgeable).
* **Resolution (Resolution, select_multiple, required)**: Choose all the ways in which your issue was resolved (e.g., Yes: Fully Resolved, Partially Resolved).
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Share any additional thoughts or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: Share any further details or feedback you would like to express.

## Tips

* Please answer questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Use specific examples to illustrate your points.
* If you have any trouble completing a section, feel free to ask for assistance.
