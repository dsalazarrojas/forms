# Quarterly Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarterly Meeting Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the effectiveness of recent meetings. This form is meant to provide a snapshot of the meeting's quality and any notable discussions or takeaways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Meeting Feedback section with your thoughts on the meeting's effectiveness.
2. Select which attendees you interacted with during the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Attendee Names section.
3. Rate your overall experience with the meeting on a scale of 1-10 in the Rating section.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you have in the Comments section.
5. If you wish to provide additional feedback, you can do so in the Additional Feedback section.
6. Enter your Email and Phone numbers to allow us to contact you if needed.
7. Select your department and team leader's attendance status in the Department and Team Leader sections.
8. Enter the date and time of the meeting in the Meeting Date and Meeting Time sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Feedback**: In this section, please provide your thoughts on the meeting's effectiveness. This can include what you enjoyed, what you didn't enjoy, or any suggestions for future meetings.
* **Attendee Names**: In this section, please select which attendees you interacted with during the meeting. This will help us understand who was present and engaged during the meeting.
* **Rating**: Please rate your overall experience with the meeting on a scale of 1-10. This will help us gauge the meeting's quality and effectiveness.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in this section.
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please enter them in this section.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Department**: In this section, please select your department to help us understand which areas of the organization are represented.
* **Team Leader**: In this section, please select your team leader's attendance status.
* **Meeting Date**: Please enter the date of the meeting to help us keep track of meeting schedules.
* **Meeting Time**: Please enter the time of the meeting to help us keep track of meeting schedules.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, suggestions, and ideas.
* Please keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you have any difficulty completing this form, please reach out to us for assistance.
