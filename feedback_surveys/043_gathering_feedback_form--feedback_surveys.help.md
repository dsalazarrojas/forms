# Gathering Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on various aspects of our product or service. The feedback collected will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our offerings. Please fill out this form only if your feedback is new and hasn't been submitted before. Duplicate submissions will not be accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully.
2. Select your response for each question.
3. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`form_page_1`, Note, Optional): This page introduces the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Feedback** (`form_page_2`, Select Multiple, Optional): Here, you can choose how we've done in different aspects of our product or service. Select 'Yes' if you're satisfied, and 'No' if there's room for improvement.
* **Comments** (`form_page_3`, Text, Optional): If you have any detailed thoughts or suggestions, add them here.
* **Email** (`form_page_4`, Email, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address.
