# Fusion Intensive Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather your feedback on a session you attended. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the session's overall quality.
2. Describe what you expected to gain from the session.
3. Indicate what you actually gained from the session.
4. Select the most valuable aspect of the session.
5. Express your level of satisfaction with the session.
6. Identify what you found most challenging.
7. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
8. Explain why you didn't participate more in the session.
9. Share any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate this session** (`session_rating`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Rate the session's quality using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **What did you expect to gain from this session** (`rating_1_what_did_you_expect_to_gain`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe what you anticipated gaining from the session. Be as specific as possible.
* **What did you gain from this session** (`rating_2_what_did_you_gain`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Indicate what you actually gained from the session. Select all that apply.
* **What did you find most valuable** (`rating_3_what_did_you_find_most_valuable`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Identify the most valuable aspect of the session for you.
* **How satisfied are you with the session** (`rating_4_how_satisfied`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Express your level of satisfaction on a scale: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **What did you find most challenging** (`rating_5_what_did_you_find_most_challenging`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Describe the aspect of the session that you found most challenging.
* **Do you have any suggestions for us** (`rating_6_any_suggestions`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any ideas or recommendations for improvement.
* **Why didn't you participate more** (`rating_8_why_didnt_you_participate_more`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the reason why you didn't participate more in the session.
* **Any other comments** (`rating_9_any_other_comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Share any additional feedback or thoughts.

Note: This form is for feedback purposes only and is not associated with any particular platform or service. Contact form@gic.mx for any questions or concerns.
