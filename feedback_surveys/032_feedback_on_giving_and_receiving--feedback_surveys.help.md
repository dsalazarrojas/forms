# feedback_on_giving_and_receiving - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Feedback on Giving and Receiving" form is designed to gather information on how individuals perceive the quality of feedback they receive and give in their work-related interactions. This form will help identify areas where feedback may be improving and understand the effectiveness of different channels used for giving and receiving feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Choose how frequently you give and receive feedback**: Select one of the options for how often you give and receive feedback (e.g., Less frequently than every 3 months, Every 1 month, Every 2 months, or Every 6 months).
2. **Select your preferred channels for giving and receiving feedback**: Choose one or more from the options (e.g., Phone calls, Video calls, In-person, Email, Chat).
3. **Identify your difficulties in giving and receiving feedback**: Select one from the options (e.g., I am not sure, Sometimes, Often, Very often).
4. **Rate the challenges in giving and receiving feedback**: Select one from the options (e.g., Not challenging, A little challenging, Moderately challenging, Very challenging).
5. **Rate the challenges in receiving feedback**: Select one from the options (e.g., Not challenging, A little challenging, Moderately challenging, Very challenging).
6. **Submit any suggestions for improvement**: Type a brief suggestion or comment.
7. **Add any comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
8. **Fill in your contact information**: Enter your name, email address, and department (if applicable).
9. **Submit your response**: Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Frequency** (`Feedback Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you give and receive feedback. This will help us understand the frequency of feedback in your work-related interactions.
* **Feedback Channels** (`Feedback Channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more from the options (e.g., Phone calls, Video calls, In-person, Email, Chat) to indicate your preferred channels for giving and receiving feedback.
* **Preferred Feedback Channels** (`Preferred Feedback Channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more from the options (e.g., Phone calls, Video calls, In-person, Email, Chat) as your preferred channels for giving and receiving feedback.
* **Difficulties** (`Difficulties`, `select_one`, required): Select one from the options (e.g., I am not sure, Sometimes, Often, Very often) to describe your level of difficulty in giving and receiving feedback.
* **Challenges in Giving** (`Challenges in Giving`, `select_one`, required): Select one from the options (e.g., Not challenging, A little challenging, Moderately challenging, Very challenging) to rate the challenges you face in giving feedback.
* **Challenges in Receiving** (`Challenges in Receiving`, `select_one`, required): Select one from the options (e.g., Not challenging, A little challenging, Moderately challenging, Very challenging) to rate the challenges you face in receiving feedback.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, `text`, optional): Type a brief suggestion or comment on how to improve feedback in your work-related interactions.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to associate with your feedback.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Submitter Department** (`Submitter Department`, `text`, required): Enter the department you belong to (if applicable).
* **Submitter Manager** (`Submitter Manager`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Supervisor** (`Submitter Supervisor`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Coach** (`Submitter Coach`, `text`, optional): Enter your coach's name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Colleague** (`Submitter Colleague`, `text`, optional): Enter your colleague's name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Other** (`Submitter Other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about who you are submitting feedback on behalf of.
* **Submitter Other Other** (`Submitter Other Other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
* **Submitter Comments** (`Submitter Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your submission.
* **Submitter Submitting Date** (`Submitter Submitting Date`, `date`, required): Select the date on which you are submitting your feedback.
