# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their purchasing experiences with our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in the required fields with your actual experience and data.
2. Choose the options that best fit your answers.
3. Submit your responses once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name (`product_name`, text, required):** Enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **Rating (`rating`, number, optional):** Rate your overall satisfaction with the product, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review (`review`, text, optional):** Provide a brief description of your experience with the product.
* **Recommendation (`recommendation`, select_multiple, optional):** Select all that apply from the list of recommendations.
* **Purchase Location (`purchase_location`, select_one, optional):** Indicate where you made the purchase (Online, In-store, Both).
* **Purchase Date (`purchase_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you made the purchase.
* **Product Rating (`product_rating`, number, optional):** Rate your satisfaction with the product, on a scale of 1-5.
* **How Relevant (`product_relevance`, select_one, optional):** Select how relevant the product was to your needs.
* **Satisfaction (`satisfaction`, number, optional):** Rate your satisfaction with the product, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Purchase Frequency (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, optional):** Indicate how often you purchase similar products.
* **Price Range (`price_range`, select_one, optional):** Select the price range of the product.
* **Age (`age`, number, optional):** Enter your age.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, optional):** Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional):** Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name (`contact_name`, text, optional):** Enter your name.
* **Contact Title (`contact_title`, text, optional):** Enter your title or position.
* **Contact Company (`contact_company`, text, optional):** Enter your company name.
* **Purchase Time (`purchase_time`, select_one, optional):** Indicate when you made the purchase (Online, In-store, Both).
* **Purchase Method (`purchase_method`, select_one, optional):** Select the method of purchase (Card, Cash, Mobile Wallet, Other).
* **Purchase Source (`purchase_source`, select_one, optional):** Indicate the source of purchase (Social Media, Search Engine, Email, Word of Mouth).
* **Purchase Influencer (`purchase_influencer`, select_one, optional):** Select the influencer of purchase (Influencer, Social Media, Recommendation, Online Advertisment).
* **Purchase Channel (`purchase_channel`, select_one, optional):** Select the channel of purchase (Online, In-store, Mobile, Other).
* **Purchase Medium (`purchase_medium`, select_one, optional):** Select the medium of purchase (Online, In-store, Print, TV).
* **Purchase Reason (`purchase_reason`, select_one, optional):** Select the reason for purchase (Convenience, Quality, Price, Recommendation).
* **Purchase Device (`purchase_device`, select_one, optional):** Select the device used for purchase (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, Other).
