The "Online Activism Behavior Study" form is designed to gather information about individuals' online activism behavior, including their motivations, perceptions, and demographics. This form will help researchers and analysts understand online activism trends and patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page, where you will be asked to select a category for your online activism behavior.
2. Choose the most relevant category that best describes your online activism activities.
3. Move to the next page, where you will be asked about your demographics, including basic information about yourself.
4. Provide your answers to the demographics questions.
5. Continue to the next page, where you will be asked to provide more information about your motivations for online activism.
6. Answer the questions about your motivations for online activism.
7. On the final page, you will be asked about your perceptions of different categories and yourself.
