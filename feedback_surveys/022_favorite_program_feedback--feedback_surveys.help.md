# Favorite Program Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Favorite Program Feedback" form is designed to help us understand your thoughts and opinions about our programs. Your feedback will help us to improve and refine our programs to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a program name from the text box.
2. Rate the program on a scale from 0 to 5.
3. Provide an overall rating for the program.
4. Enter your comments about the program.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them below.
6. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the program on a scale from 0 to 5, where 0 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Enter an overall rating for the program, also on a scale from 0 to 5.
* **Comments** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Provide any comments or thoughts about the program you'd like to share.
* **Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Describe why you liked or disliked the program.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions you have for improving the program.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have a suggestion for improvement, try to be as detailed as possible.
* Your feedback will help us to improve our programs and better serve you.
