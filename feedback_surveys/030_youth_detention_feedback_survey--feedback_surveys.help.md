# Youth Detention Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Detention Feedback Survey is designed to collect your thoughts and experiences about your time at the facility. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the services we provide.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Carefully read each question and its corresponding options.
* Select the answer that best represents your feelings or experiences for each question.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Any additional feedback or comments?" field at the end.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How safe do you feel when you are in the facility?**: This question asks about your overall sense of safety while at the facility. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
	+ Very safe: You feel very safe and secure in the facility.
	+ Somewhat safe: You feel somewhat safe, but there are some concerns.
	+ Not very safe: You feel not very safe or uncomfortable in the facility.
	+ Not at all safe: You feel not safe or threatened in the facility.
* **How would you rate the quality of the staff support you received?**: This question assesses your experience with the staff at the facility. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
	+ Excellent: The staff was very helpful and supportive.
	+ Good: The staff was generally helpful, but with some limitations.
	+ Fair: The staff was somewhat helpful, but with some significant issues.
	+ Poor: The staff was not helpful or unresponsive.
* **How satisfied are you with the services provided by the facility?**: This question evaluates your overall satisfaction with the services offered by the facility. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
	+ Very satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with the services provided.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with the services provided.
	+ Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied: You are neutral about the services provided.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the services provided.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are extremely dissatisfied with the services provided.
* **Any additional feedback or comments?**: This field is for any extra thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you might have. Please use this space to provide any additional information that might be helpful in improving the facility.
* **Please provide your contact information**: This field is for your email address or any other contact information you'd like to provide. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
