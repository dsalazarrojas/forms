# Arts Public Relations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the arts organization's public relations efforts. The goal is to understand how well the organization is communicating with its audience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your answer for each field based on your actual experience with our arts organization.
2.  Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
3.  If a question does not apply to you, choose "N/A" or "Does not apply" as your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you first learn about our arts organization?**
	+ This question helps us understand the primary source of information about our organization.
	+ Select the option that best describes how you first heard about us.
* **Rate our social media presence**
	+ This question assesses how well we are engaging with you through social media.
	+ Rate your experience from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Rate the clarity of our recent press releases**
	+ This question evaluates how clear and effective our press releases are.
	+ Rate your experience from 1 (confusing) to 5 (very clear).
* **Rate the quality of our local media coverage**
	+ This question assesses the quality of our media coverage.
	+ Rate your experience from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Do you feel you have a clear understanding of our mission?**
	+ This question helps us understand how well we are communicating our mission.
	+ Select the option that best describes your understanding.
* **Which communication channel do you prefer?**
	+ This question helps us understand your preferred way of receiving information about our organization.
	+ Select the option that best describes your preference.
* **How often do you engage with our PR materials?**
	+ This question assesses how frequently you interact with our public relations materials.
	+ Select the option that best describes your engagement frequency.
* **Suggestions for improving our public engagement**
	+ This question provides an opportunity to suggest ways we can improve our public engagement.
	+ Please provide your suggestions in the text field.
* **Rate the ease of finding information on our website**
	+ This question evaluates how easy or difficult it is to find information on our website.
	+ Rate your experience from 1 (difficult) to 5 (very easy).
* **What is your relationship to our organization?**
	+ This question helps us understand your connection to our organization.
	+ Select the option that best describes your relationship.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Please provide specific examples or details to support your answers whenever possible.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the text field.
