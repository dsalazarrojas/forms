# feedback_on_overcoming_challenges - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on how individuals overcome challenges in their personal and professional lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide detailed answers where possible.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. Use clear and concise language to ensure that your feedback is easy to understand.
4. Provide specific examples or stories whenever possible to make your feedback more valuable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional):
	+ This is a brief section where you can introduce yourself and the challenge you faced.
	+ Please keep it short and to the point.
* **Challenge Description** (`challenge_description`, text, required):
	+ Briefly describe the difficulty you faced.
	+ Provide as much detail as possible about the challenge.
	+ Be honest and transparent about your experience.
* **Strategies** (`strategies`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all the strategies you used to overcome the challenge.
	+ Choose from the options provided:
		- Self-reflection
		- Goal setting
		- Support systems
		- Self-care
		- Mindfulness
		- Physical activity
		- Seeking help
* **Advice** (`advice`, text, required):
	+ Provide advice or insights gained from the challenge.
	+ Share your expertise and experiences to help others.
	+ Be honest and accurate in your advice.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you felt about the experience:
		- Positive
		- Negative
		- Neutral
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required):
	+ Provide recommendations for others based on your experience.
	+ Be specific and detailed in your recommendations.
* **Reflective Story** (`reflective_story`, text, required):
	+ Tell a story about how you overcame the difficulty.
	+ Use specific examples and details to make your story more engaging.
* **Physical Care** (`physical_care`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how important physical health was to overcoming the difficulty:
		- Very Important
		- Somewhat Important
		- Not very Important
		- Not at all Important
* **Mental Care** (`mental_care`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how important mental health was to overcoming the difficulty:
		- Very Important
		- Somewhat Important
		- Not very Important
		- Not at all Important
* **Emotional Care** (`emotional_care`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how important emotional support was to overcoming the difficulty:
		- Very Important
		- Somewhat Important
		- Not very Important
		- Not at all Important
* **Social Care** (`social_care`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how important social support was to overcoming the difficulty:
		- Very Important
		- Somewhat Important
		- Not very Important
		- Not at all Important
* **Other** (`other_care`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.

## Tips
Remember to:

* Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples and stories.
* Use clear and concise language.
* Follow the instructions carefully to ensure that you complete the form correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
