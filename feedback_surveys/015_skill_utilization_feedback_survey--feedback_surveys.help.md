# Skill Utilization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the utilization of skills within our team. Your input will help us understand how well we're using our skills and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your perspective.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What skills are you responsible for?** (`what-skills-are-you-responsible-for`, text, required):
	+ Please describe the skills you are currently responsible for in our team.
* **How well are these skills utilized?** (`how-well-are-these-skills-utilized`, number, required):
	+ Rate how well you think these skills are being used within our team on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **How important is it to use these skills?** (`how-important-is-it-to-use-these-skills`, number, required):
	+ Rate how important it is to use these skills within our team on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **What areas could improve?** (`what-areas-could-improve`, text, required):
	+ Provide specific areas where you think we could improve the utilization of these skills.
* **How would you rate?** (`how-would-you-rate`, text, required):
	+ Rate your overall opinion on the utilization of these skills within our team (e.g., 1-5).
* **Additional comments** (`additional-comments`, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the utilization of these skills.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to the form owner for clarification.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we get a complete picture of your feedback.
