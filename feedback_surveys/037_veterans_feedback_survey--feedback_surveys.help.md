# Veterans Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from veterans on their experiences with a particular program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your satisfaction rating for the program.
2. Enter your comments regarding your experience with the program.
3. Provide your email address so we can contact you for any further clarification.
4. Enter your name.
5. Enter the date you served in the military.
6. Optional fields:
   * Enter any additional comments regarding the program.
   * Select the method you would prefer to be contacted for any further communication.
   * Enter your contact name and phone number (if you choose to be contacted by phone).
   * Enter your contact email address (if you choose to be contacted by email).
   * Enter the name of the program you participated in.
   * Indicate if you are a veteran (yes/no).
   * Select your branch of service.
   * Select the rating scale range you used.
   * Select your rating for the program.
   * Enter your overall satisfaction level.
   * Select your rating scale comments (if any).
   * Select the range of your rating scale.
   * Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Survey Form**: This field is not visible to the user but is the main container for the survey questions.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Select your satisfaction level with the program from the provided scale (1-5).
* **Comments**: Enter your comments regarding your experience with the program.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further clarification.
* **Veteran Name**: Enter your name.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date you served in the military.
* **Rating Comments**: Enter any comments regarding your rating selection.
* **Program Evaluation**: Select your evaluation of the program from the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Program Improvement**: Enter any suggestions for improvement for the program.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale range you used.
* **Contact Information**: This field is only visible if you chose to be contacted via phone or email. 
   + **Contact Name**: Enter your name in case we need to contact you.
   + **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number if you chose to be contacted by phone.
   + **Contact Email Address**: Enter your contact email address if you chose to be contacted by email.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **Veteran Status**: Indicate if you are a veteran (yes/no).
* **Branch of Service**: Select your branch of service.
* **Rating Scale Range**: Select the range of your rating scale.
* **Program Rating**: Select your rating for the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Enter your overall satisfaction level with the program.
* **Rating Scale Comments**: Enter any comments regarding your rating scale selection.
* **Rating Scale Range**: Select the range of your rating scale.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding the program.
* **Contact Method**: Select the method you prefer to be contacted (phone/email).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments.

# Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any comments, please provide them in the designated field.
* If you need to be contacted, ensure that the provided contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
