# Phrase Elimination Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Phrase Elimination Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions for phrases that should be retired or replaced in our language usage. This form helps us understand which phrases are no longer relevant, outdated, or no longer useful, and provides us with suggestions for alternative phrases that can be used instead.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a phrase from the list of phrases provided.
2. Choose one or more alternatives for the selected phrase.
3. Provide feedback on why the phrase should be retired.
4. Suggest a date and time for the phrase to be retired.
5. Confirm that you want to retire the phrase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a phrase (phrase_list)** (`Select a phrase`, `Select Multiple`, Optional): Choose a phrase from the list that you think should be retired.
* **Why should this phrase be retired? (retirement_reason)** (`Text`, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you think the phrase should be retired.
* **Suggested alternatives (alternative_phrase)** (`Select Multiple`, Optional): Choose one or more alternative phrases that can be used instead of the selected phrase.
* **Add feedback (feedback)** (`Select One`, Optional): Choose a feedback option to provide additional context or suggestions for the phrase to be retired.
* **How might we avoid miscommunication with this phrase in the future? (communication_concerns)** (`Text`, Optional): Provide suggestions on how to avoid miscommunication when using the phrase in the future.
* **Additional comments (additional_comments)** (`Text`, Optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts on the phrase to be retired.
* **Suggested retire date (retire_date)** (`Date`, Optional): Suggest a date for the phrase to be retired.
* **Suggested retire time (retire_time)** (`Time`, Optional): Suggest a time for the phrase to be retired.
* **Reason for suggested retire date (retire_reason_date)** (`Date`, Optional): Provide the reason for suggesting a specific date for the phrase to be retired.
* **Reason for suggested retire time (retire_reason_time)** (`Time`, Optional): Provide the reason for suggesting a specific time for the phrase to be retired.
* **Are you sure you want to retire this phrase (confirm_retire)** (`Select One`, Optional): Confirm that you want to retire the phrase.
