# Startup Beta Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Beta Product Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with our beta product. We value your feedback and would like to know what you think about the product's performance, features, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us make informed decisions to improve our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you need to help us understand your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Beta Product Experience
* **Beta Product Experience** (`beta_product_experience`, `text`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can describe your overall experience with the beta product. Please provide any details about what worked well and what didn't.

### Beta Product Satisfaction
* **Beta Product Satisfaction** (`beta_product_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): We ask you to rate your overall satisfaction with the beta product on a scale of 1 to 10. This will help us understand how well we met your expectations.

### Identified Issues
* **Identified Issues** (`identified_issues`, `text`, optional): If you encountered any issues during your experience, please describe them in detail here. This will help us identify areas for improvement.

### Suggested Improvements
* **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for how we can improve the beta product, please provide them here.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to stay informed about updates and improvements to the beta product, please provide your email address here.

### Beta Product Satisfaction Score
* **Beta Product Satisfaction Score** (`beta_product_satisfaction_score`, `number`, optional): If you'd like to provide a numerical satisfaction score (out of 10), please do so here. This will help us gauge your overall satisfaction.

### Suggested Features
* **Suggested Features** (`suggested_features`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas for new features you'd like to see in the beta product, please describe them here.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can include any additional comments or suggestions you have about the beta product.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): We use this field to track which tool is associated with your feedback. Please leave this field blank unless instructed otherwise.

### Form ID
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): This field is used internally to track form submission data. Please leave this field blank unless instructed otherwise.
