# iptv_service_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "iptv_service_feedback_survey" form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with IPTV services. It aims to collect information on various aspects of the service, including channel quality, content, and service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page and fill in the required fields.
2. Select the relevant options that best match your experience with IPTV services.
3. Provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" field, if you have any.

* **Introduction** (Page 1)
	+ This is the introduction page with a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **How often do you watch TV channels?** (Page 2)
	+ This field asks about your watching frequency of TV channels. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ Options: Channel A, Channel B, Channel C
* **How would you rate the quality of the channels?** (Page 3)
	+ This field asks you to rate the quality of the channels. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **How would you rate the content of the channels?** (Page 4)
	+ This field asks you to rate the content of the channels. Select the option that best matches your experience.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **How would you rate the service of the IPTV provider?** (Page 5)
	+ This field asks you to rate the service provided by the IPTV company. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Additional comments** (Page 6)
	+ This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the IPTV service.
* **Do you have any ideas for improvement?** (Page 7)
	+ This field asks about any ideas you have for improving the IPTV service. Select all the options that apply.
	+ Options: Option A, Option B, Option C
* **How many hours do you watch IPTV per day?** (Page 8)
	+ This field asks about your watching frequency of IPTV. Enter a number that best reflects your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with IPTV?** (Page 9)
	+ This field asks about your satisfaction level with IPTV. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied
* **Why are you not watching IPTV?** (Page 10)
	+ This field asks about your reasons for not watching IPTV. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
	+ Options: Option A, Option B, Option C
* **What device do you use to watch IPTV?** (Page 11)
	+ This field asks about the device you use to watch IPTV. Select all the options that apply.
	+ Options: Option A, Option B, Option C
* **Other (please specify)** (Page 12)
	+ This field is for any other comments or suggestions you have about the IPTV service.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing feedback.
- Select only the options that best match your experience.
