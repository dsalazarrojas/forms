# Video Synthesis Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on video synthesis, helping us improve our video content and provide better experiences for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate the video's realism, audio syncing, and overall quality using the provided rating scale.
2. Add any additional comments you may have about the video.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate Video Realism** (`video_synthesis_rating`, number, Required): Rate the video's realism on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Rate Audio Sync** (`audio_sync_rating`, number, Required): Rate the audio syncing of the video on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Rate Overall Quality** (`overall_quality_rating`, number, Required): Rate the video's overall quality on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Comment** (`video_synthesis_comment`, text, Optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the video.

## Tips

* Please be honest and objective in your feedback.
* Keep your comments concise and relevant to the video.
* Your feedback will help us improve our video content and provide better experiences for our users.
