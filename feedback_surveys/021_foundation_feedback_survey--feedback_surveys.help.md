# Foundation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Foundation. The collected data will be used to identify areas for improvement and provide better experiences for users.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form with a brief description of the survey title.
* Rate your satisfaction with the survey using the "Satisfaction Score (1-5)" field.
* Provide an email address in the "Your Email" field if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* Enter your phone number in the "Your Phone Number" field if you want to be contacted about your feedback. (Note: This is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Foundation Feedback Survey (required)**:
	+ This field is the title of the survey, which should be a brief description of the survey.
* **What was your experience like? (required)**:
	+ Describe your experience with the Foundation.
* **Satisfaction Score (1-5) (required)**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with your experience, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Your Email (required)**:
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Your Phone Number (optional)**:
	+ Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Suggestions for improvement (optional)**:
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for us to improve our services.
