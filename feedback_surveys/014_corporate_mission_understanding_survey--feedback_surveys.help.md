# Corporate Mission Understanding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Mission Understanding Survey is a form designed to assess the level of understanding among corporate employees about the company's mission, values, and goals. This survey aims to gather feedback on the current state of communication channels, mission understanding, and opportunities for improvement. The survey will help management to identify areas of strength and weakness, allowing for targeted interventions to enhance corporate mission understanding.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select the appropriate answers for each question based on your understanding.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the text fields.

* **Introduction** (`1` , `note`): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Mission Understanding** (`2`, required/optional): This question asks if you understand the company's mission statement. Please select "Yes" if you feel you have a good understanding, and "No" if you don't.

* **Communication Channels** (`4`, required/optional): Select the communication channels you use to stay informed about company updates and news. This will help us understand how information is shared within the company.
* **Internal Communication** (`5`, required/optional): Describe how internal communication is conducted within the company. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **Opportunities for Improvement** (`6`, required/optional): Select areas where you think the company can improve in terms of mission understanding.
* **Next Steps** (`7`, required/optional): Share your thoughts on what steps the company can take to improve mission understanding.
* **Corporate Mission Understanding** (`8`, required/optional): Rate the company's understanding of its mission. Select "Good" if you think the understanding is satisfactory, and "Fair" if it's not.
* **Company Mission Understanding** (`9`, required/optional): Select whether you understand the company's mission statement.
