# Voice Of The Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and data from customers regarding their experience with the company. It aims to improve customer satisfaction, identify areas of improvement, and build stronger relationships with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of feedback you would like to provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
2. Provide a brief description of your feedback in the text field.
3. If you have an email address, provide it so we can contact you for further discussion.
4. If you have a phone number, provide it so we can contact you for further discussion.
5. If you have any additional feedback, please provide it in the text field.
6. Select your level of satisfaction with our services: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
7. If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address and phone number.
8. Provide your employee's name, email address, phone number, and supervisor's contact information (if applicable).
9. If you have any feedback or comments about our services, please provide it in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of feedback you would like to provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your feedback in this text field.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Time of Call** (`time_of_call`, time, required): If applicable, provide the time of your call or interaction with our company.
* **Date of Call** (`date_of_call`, date, required): If applicable, provide the date of your call or interaction with our company.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about our services.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with our services: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Provide your employee's name.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Provide your employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Provide your employee's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Provide your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Provide your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Provide your supervisor's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Provide the department you interacted with.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required): Provide the team you interacted with.
* **Customer Survey Link** (`customer_survey_link`, text, required): Provide the link to the customer survey.
* **Customer Survey Text** (`customer_survey_text`, text, required): Provide any additional text about the survey.
* **Customer Survey Email** (`customer_survey_email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Customer Survey Phone** (`customer_survey_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Employee Survey Link** (`employee_survey_link`, text, required): Provide the link to the employee survey.
