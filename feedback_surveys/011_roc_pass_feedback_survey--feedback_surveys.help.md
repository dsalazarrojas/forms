# ROC Pass Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ROC Pass Feedback Survey is used to collect feedback from customers, operational team members, and management personnel on their experience with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the format of `example@example.com` to allow us to contact you if necessary.
3. Choose your **Rating** by selecting a number from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction with the service.
4. Select your **Role** in your organization from the dropdown menu.
5. Answer **Yes** or **No** if you **Do you use the service?**
6. In the text field, **What is your like most about the service?** Be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
7. In the text field, **What do you dislike most about the service?** If you have nothing to comment, you can leave this field blank.
8. Answer **Yes** or **No** if you **Have you experienced any issues with the service?**
9. If you answered **Yes** in the previous question, please describe the issue you experienced in the text field.
10. Choose how **likely are you to recommend the service to a friend or colleague?** from the dropdown menu.
11. Finally, please submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (optional, text): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (optional, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Rating** (optional, number): Select a number from 1 to 5 to express your level of satisfaction.
* **Role** (optional, select_one): Choose your role in your organization from the dropdown menu.
* **Do you use the service?** (optional, select_one): Answer **Yes** if you have used the service, **No** otherwise.
* **What is your like most about the service?** (optional, text): Be specific about what you like most about the service.
* **What do you dislike most about the service?** (optional, text): If you have nothing to comment, leave this field blank.
* **Have you experienced any issues with the service?** (optional, select_one): Answer **Yes** if you have experienced any issues.
* **If yes, what issue did you experience?** (optional, text): If you answered **Yes** in the previous question, describe the issue you experienced.
* **How likely are you to recommend the service to a friend or colleague?** (optional, select_one): Choose from **Not at all**, **Not very likely**, **Somewhat likely**, **Very likely**, or **Absolutely likely**.
