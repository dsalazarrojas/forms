# sports_merchandise_vendor_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with sports merchandise vendors. The information collected will help us understand our customers' satisfaction and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your experience with the sports merchandise vendor you are providing feedback about. Make sure to fill out all required fields and provide as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How was your experience with us?
* **How was your experience with us?** (Note): A text field where you can describe your overall experience with the sports merchandise vendor.
* **Overall experience (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if your experience was overall positive or negative.
* **Communication (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the communication with the vendor was good or bad.
* **Vendor performance (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the vendor's performance was good or bad.
* **Product quality (Good/Poor)** (Required): Check if the product quality was good or poor.
* **Event prophets (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the event prophets were good or bad.
* **Product selection (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the product selection was good or bad.
* **Event date** (Required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event time** (Required): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Event duration** (Required): Enter the duration of the event you attended.
* **Staff attendance (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the staff attendance was good or bad.
* **Staff performance (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the staff performance was good or bad.
* **Vendor rating (Good/Fair)** (Required): Check if the vendor rating was good or fair.
* **Vendor recommendation (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if you would recommend the vendor.
* **Vendor (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the vendor you interacted with was good or bad.
* **Sales person (Yes/No)** (Required): Check if the sales person was good or bad.
* **Sales person name** (Required): Enter the name of the sales person you interacted with.
* **Email address** (Optional): Enter the email address of the sales person.
* **Phone number** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the sales person.
* **Additional comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Report date** (Required): Enter the date you are reporting this feedback.
* **Report time** (Required): Enter the time you are reporting this feedback.
* **Sales person email** (Required): Enter the email address of the sales person.
* **Sales person phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of the sales person.
* **Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.

Note: Required fields are marked as "Required". Make sure to fill out all required fields.
