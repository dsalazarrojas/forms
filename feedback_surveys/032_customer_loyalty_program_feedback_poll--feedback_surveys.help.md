# Customer Loyalty Program Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide helps customers complete the Customer Loyalty Program Feedback Poll, a tool used to gather valuable feedback and insights from our valued customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the designated "Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Write your program name (e.g. "Customer Loyalty") in the "Program Name" field.
4. Select one of the options for "Rating" to provide your assessment of our program.
5. Leave any comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
6. Select a date for when you joined the program in the "Date Joined" field.
7. Choose how much time you last spent on a survey in the "Time Last Survey" field.
8. Select all that apply for your preferred contact methods in the "Contact Preferred" field.
9. Leave any suggestions in the "Suggestions" field.
10. Select your preferred email contact method in the "Email Preferred" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (this is a required field).
12. Enter your address in the "Address" field (optional).
13. Select your age range in the "Age Range" field (optional).
14. Select your gender in the "Gender" field.
15. Record the time you last completed a survey in the "Survey Completed" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Program Name (3)** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are referring to (e.g. "Customer Loyalty").
* **Rating (4)** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to rate our program.
* **Comments (5)** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about our customer loyalty program.
* **Date Joined (6)** (`date_joined`, date, optional): Select the date you joined the program.
* **Time Last Survey (7)** (`time_last_survey`, time, optional): Record the time you last spent on a survey.
* **Contact Preferred (8)** (`contact_preferred`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for your preferred contact methods.
* **Suggestions (9)** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Leave any suggestions for our program.
* **Email Preferred (10)** (`email_preferred`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred email contact method.
* **Phone Number (11)** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address (12)** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Age Range (13)** (`age_range`, select_multiple, optional): Select your age range.
* **Gender (14)** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Survey Completed (15)** (`survey_completed`, time, optional): Record the time you last completed a survey.

## Tips

- Be accurate and honest in your responses to help us understand your needs and preferences better.
- Ensure the information provided is up-to-date and correct.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Please do not complete this form if you have already done so previously to avoid duplicate responses.
