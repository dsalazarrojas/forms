# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about your views and opinions on foreign conflicts. This form will help us understand your level of awareness, sources of information, and opinions on conflict resolution, prevention, and impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Select the options that best represent your opinions and views.
3. Provide specific information on sources and potential solutions.
4. If unsure, you can provide additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your level of awareness about foreign conflicts?** (`foreign_conflict_awareness_survey`, select_one, required) - This question helps us understand how much you know about foreign conflicts. Choose 'High' if you feel confident in your knowledge, or 'Low' if you are not as informed.
* **What are your primary sources of information about foreign conflicts?** (`information_sources`, select_multiple, required) - Select all sources that you regularly use to gather information on foreign conflicts, such as news, social media, or academic research.
* **How do you perceive the severity of the conflict?** (`perception_of_conflict`, number, required) - Rate the severity of the conflict on a scale of 1-10.
* **How much do you discuss foreign conflicts in public with family and friends?** (`public_discussion`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you frequently discuss foreign conflicts, or 'No' if you do not discuss them much.
* **How would you rate the impact of foreign conflict on local councils?** (`impact_on_local_council`, number, required) - Rate the impact on a scale of 1-10.
* **Do you believe the conflict can be resolved without international intervention?** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you think the conflict can be resolved without external help.
* **How do you think the conflict can be resolved?** (`resolution_method`, select_one, required) - Select the method you believe would be most effective.
* **What is your role in resolving foreign conflicts?** (`role_in_conflict_resolution`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you have a role, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Do you think the conflict could have been prevented?** (`conflict_prevention`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you believe the conflict could have been avoided.
* **Do you think the conflict has global implications?** (`global_conflict`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you believe the conflict affects the world as a whole.
* **Do you think the conflict affects your community?** (`local_conflict`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you believe the conflict affects your community directly.
* **How do you think the conflict impacts your community?** (`local_impact`, number, required) - Rate the impact on a scale of 1-10.
* **How do you feel about the conflict?** (`personal_feeling`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you have a positive or negative view.
* **Would you support international intervention to resolve the conflict?** (`resolution_support`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you support external help.
* **Would you support global action to end the conflict?** (`global_support`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you support action to address the conflict.
* **Would you support local action to end the conflict?** (`local_support`, select_one, required) - Choose 'Yes' if you support local efforts to address the conflict.
* **Do you have any information about the conflict that you think others should know?** (`conflict_info`, text, required) - Provide any information you think would be helpful to others.
* **Other information about the conflict?** (`conflict_info_other`, text, required) - Provide any additional information not covered in previous questions.
* **What is your source of information about the conflict?** (`conflict_info_source`, text, required) - Specify where you get your information.
* **Other information source about the conflict?** (`conflict_info_source_other`, text, required) - Specify any other sources.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, required) - Share any further thoughts or opinions.
* **Details of the other information source** (`conflict_info_source_other_details`, text, required) - Provide details about the source.
* **What information or knowledge would help you make a more informed judgment on conflict prevention?** (`conflict_prevention_info`, text, required) - Specify what knowledge you lack to make a more informed decision.
* **What information or knowledge would help you make a more informed judgment on conflict resolution?** (`conflict_resolution_info`, text, required) - Specify what knowledge you lack to make a more informed decision.
* **What information or knowledge would help you make a more informed judgment on the impact of the conflict?** (`conflict_info_impact`, text, required) - Specify what knowledge you lack to make a more informed decision.
