# Baggage Offload Process Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants in the baggage offload process to help us improve its efficiency, safety, and overall effectiveness. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make the process better for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, answering each question truthfully.
2. Use the provided options for each question, as they are designed to help us understand your perspective.
3. If you have any safety concerns or incidents, please describe them in the "incident_description" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, select "Survey Complete" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your badge or identification.
* **Your Role**: Select your role in the baggage offload process, such as Baggage Handler, Supervisor, or Manager.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **How Often Do You Participate in Baggage Offload?**: Choose how frequently you participate in the offloading process.
* **Baggage Offload Process Instructions Are Clear**: Rate how clear the instructions for the offloading process are to you.
* **Equipment Is Adequate for Offloading**: Rate how adequate the equipment is for offloading.
* **Time Allocated for Baggage Offload Is Sufficient**: Rate how sufficient the time allocated for offloading is.
* **Safety Protocols Are Clear and Followed**: Rate how clear and followed safety protocols are.
* **Have You Witnessed Safety Issues or Incidents?**: Choose if you've witnessed any safety issues or incidents.
* **Safety Concerns**: Describe any safety concerns or incidents you've witnessed.
* **Training Received for Baggage Offload**: Rate the training you've received for offloading.
* **Ongoing Support and Feedback Provided**: Rate the ongoing support and feedback you've received.
* **Main Bottlenecks or Inefficiencies**: Describe any main bottlenecks or inefficiencies you've experienced.
* **Suggestions for Process Improvement**: Suggest any improvements for the offloading process.
* **Technology Tools Help Efficiency**: Rate how helpful technology tools are for offloading.
* **Technical Issues Encountered**: Describe any technical issues you've encountered.
* **Overall Satisfaction With Baggage Offload Process**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the process.
* **Thank You for Completing This Survey**: Select "Survey Complete" to submit your feedback.
