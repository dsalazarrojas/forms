# Economic Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Feedback Survey Form is a tool used to gather opinions and feedback from end users about the current economic situation. This form helps us understand their perceptions and concerns, which informs our decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please read the form carefully and answer all questions relevant to your current economic situation.
* Select your preferred options for each question, if applicable.
* Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction about your current economic situation. This section is optional, but it helps us get a better understanding of your background.
* **How are you feeling about the current economic situation?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your feelings about the current economic situation:
	+ Option 1: Very concerned
	+ Option 2: Somewhat concerned
	+ Option 3: Not concerned
* **What are your top priorities?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Option 1: Financial security
	+ Option 2: Economic growth
	+ Option 3: Environmental sustainability
* **What is your outlook on the economy?** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your expectations or concerns about the future economic situation.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or insights, feel free to provide them in the introduction field.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your current situation.

This guide is designed to be concise and practical, helping end users understand each field's purpose and how to complete it effectively.
