# Beauty App Interface Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty App Interface Usability Survey is designed to collect feedback about the user's experience with the app. We aim to gather insights that will help us improve the app's usability and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address (optional).
2. Select your age group.
3. Rate your technology proficiency.
4. Indicate how often you use the app.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the app.
6. Rate the ease of navigation.
7. Evaluate the visual design of the app.
8. Assess the loading speed of the app.
9. Rate the discoverability of features.
10. Rate your checkout experience.
11. Evaluate the search functionality of the app.
12. Select your favorite features.
13. Identify areas where the app needs improvement.
14. Describe any bugs or issues you've encountered.
15. Indicate if you would recommend the app to others.
16. Provide additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your name is not required, but it helps us understand who is taking the survey.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This is the email address you used to create your account or the one you want to use for follow-up.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Please select your age group from the options provided.
* **Technology Proficiency** (`tech_proficiency`, select_one, required): Choose how tech savvy you are, from very tech savvy to not very tech savvy.
* **How Often Do You Use the App** (`app_usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you use the app, from multiple times daily to rarely.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app, from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Ease of Navigation** (`ease_of_navigation`, select_one, required): Choose how easy it is to navigate the app, from very easy to very difficult.
* **Visual Design Rating** (`visual_design`, select_one, required): Rate the visual design of the app, from excellent to very poor.
* **Loading Speed** (`loading_speed`, select_one, required): Choose how fast the app loads, from very fast to very slow.
* **Feature Discoverability** (`feature_discoverability`, select_one, required): Select how easy it is to find features, from very easy to very difficult.
* **Checkout Experience** (`checkout_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with the checkout process, from very smooth to very frustrating.
* **Search Functionality** (`search_functionality`, select_one, required): Evaluate how well the search function works, from excellent to very poor.
* **Favorite Features** (`favorite_features`, select_multiple, required): Choose the features you like most about the app.
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify areas where the app needs improvement.
* **Have You Encountered Bugs** (`bug_encountered`, select_one, required): Indicate if you've encountered bugs, from yes - frequently to no - never.
* **Bug Description** (`bug_description`, text, optional): Describe any bugs or issues you've encountered.
* **Would You Recommend This App** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend the app to others, from definitely yes to definitely no.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the app.
