# Static Question Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users on their experiences with your product or service. The goal is to understand user opinions on various aspects of your product/service, including what they like and dislike about it. This information will help you to improve and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Answer all the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
2. **Save your responses**: You can save your responses as you go, or fill out the form in one session.
3. **Review before submission**: Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately represent your thoughts and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Static Question Survey**: This is the title of the survey, which helps us to identify the survey for future reference. 
* **Category**: This is the category where your survey is stored. If you know the category, please select it from the dropdown. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Description**: This field allows you to provide a brief description of your experience with our product/service.
* **Question**: This is the list of questions that we have prepared to get your valuable feedback. Please select one or multiple questions that apply to your experience.
* **What do you like most about our product/service?**: Please describe the things you like most about our product/service.
* **What do you dislike about our product/service?**: Please describe the things you dislike most about our product/service.
* **Would you recommend our product/service?**: This question helps us understand if you would recommend or not. You can select "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".
* **How likely are you to purchase from us again?**: This question helps us understand how likely you are to purchase from us again. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend or family member?**: This question helps us understand how likely you are to recommend our product/service to a friend or family member. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **What would make you more likely to purchase from us again?**: This question helps us understand what would make you more likely to purchase from us again. Please describe your thoughts.
* **What would make you less likely to purchase from us again?**: This question helps us understand what would make you less likely to purchase from us again. Please describe your thoughts.
* **What would make you more likely to recommend us to a friend or family member?**: This question helps us understand what would make you more likely to recommend us to a friend or family member. Please describe your thoughts.
* **What would make you less likely to recommend us to a friend or family member?**: This question helps us understand what would make you less likely to recommend us to a friend or family member. Please describe your thoughts.
* **Is there anything else you would like to tell us?**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
