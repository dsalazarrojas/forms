**Geology Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide**

**Purpose**

The Geology Course Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with our geology course. We value your opinion and your suggestions, and we will use this information to make improvements to the course.

**How To Complete This Form**

1.  Please select the rating options that best reflect your overall experience with the course.
2.  For the Instructor Evaluation field, please provide a brief description of your experience with the instructor.
3.  If you have any comments or suggestions for the course, please enter them in the Student Suggestion field.
4.  Select the ratings that best reflect your opinion on the course materials, instructor help, and other aspects of the course.
5.  Finally, please provide your email address, name, and department (if applicable), so we can contact you if needed.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

*   **Survey Rating** (`survey_rating`, number, required):
    *   This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the course on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Instructor Evaluation** (`instructor_evaluation`, text, required):
    *   Please provide a brief description of your experience with the instructor.
*   **Student Suggestion** (`student_suggestion`, text, optional):
    *   Any comments or suggestions you have for the course.
*   **Course Feedback** (`course_feedback`, note, optional):
    *   Any additional comments or feedback about the course.
*   **Rating: Instructor** (`rating_instructor`, select_one, optional):
    *   Please rate the instructor's performance from 1 to 5.
*   **Materials** (`rating_materials`, select_multiple, optional):
    *   Select the options that best describe your opinion on the course materials.
*   **Instructor Help** (`rating_instructor_help`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate the instructor's help from 1 to 5.
*   **Clarity** (`rating_clarity`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate the clarity of the course materials from 1 to 5.
*   **Interest** (`rating_interest`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate how interesting the course is from 1 to 5.
*   **Value** (`rating_value`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate the value of the course from 1 to 5.
*   **Relevance** (`rating_relevance`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate how relevant the course materials are from 1 to 5.
*   **Difficulty** (`rating_difficulty`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate the difficulty level of the course from 1 to 5.
*   **Satisfaction** (`rating_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
    *   Rate your overall satisfaction with the course from 1 to 5.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
    *   Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
    *   Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
    *   Please enter your name for our records.
*   **Department** (`department_name`, text, optional):
    *   Please enter your department (if applicable) for our records.

Please note that some fields are optional, but we appreciate any feedback or suggestions you may have. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve the geology course!
