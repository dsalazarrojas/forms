# EdTech App Feature Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather your feedback and suggestions about the EdTech App features. Your input will help us identify areas that need improvement and create a more enjoyable and functional user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to provide feedback on our EdTech App features.
- Select one of the categories when asked, as it will help us understand the context of your feedback.
- Answer questions truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible about your experience and suggestions.
- Don't forget to include your contact information so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a text field where you can provide a brief introduction about why you are filling out this form.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the feature or issue you are providing feedback on. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **What is your satisfaction with our app?**: Choose how satisfied you are with the app, based on your experience.
* **Suggest a feature**: Enter a feature name, explain the benefit and importance of the feature, and suggest ways to improve it.
* **How satisfied are you with each of these features?**: Select all the features that you are satisfied or not satisfied with, based on your experience.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our app?**: Choose how satisfied you are with the app overall.
* **Additional feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the app.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Website**: Enter our website, if you have one.
* **Social Media**: Enter our social media profiles, if you have any.
* **Comments (1)**: Enter any comments you have about the app.
* **Comments (2)**: Enter any additional comments you have about the app.

Note: Some fields are optional and only required if you want to provide feedback or suggestions on a specific feature or category.
