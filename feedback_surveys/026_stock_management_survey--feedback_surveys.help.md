# Stock Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on stock management, likely for the purpose of monitoring, analyzing, or improving stock levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each page of the form in the order presented.
2.  Complete all required fields.
3.  For select fields, choose an option that best reflects your answer.
4.  Use the provided hints for guidance, if available.
5.  Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_role** (Text, required): Please answer this question to describe your role within the stock management process.
* **what_are_your_goals** (Text, required): Briefly state your goals related to stock management.
* **items_in_stock** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all items that you currently have in stock.
* **stock_management_survey_page_4** (Date, required): Please enter the date related to the stock management process.
* **stock_management_survey_page_5** (Select One, required): Choose one of the provided options for your stock management process.
* **email** (Email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **stock_management_survey_page_9** (Time, non-required): If you have a specific time constraint for stock management, enter it here.
* **stock_management_survey_page_10** (Select One, required): Choose one of the provided options for stock management.
* **stock_management_survey_page_12** (Number, required): Enter a numerical value related to stock management.
* **stock_management_survey_page_13** (Select Multiple, required): Choose all options that apply to your stock management process.
* **stock_management_survey_page_14** (Text, non-required): Enter any additional information or notes about stock management.
* **stock_management_survey_page_15** (Email, non-required): Enter your email address for non-essential contact.
* **stock_management_survey_page_16** (Text, non-required): Enter any other information or notes about stock management.
* **stock_management_survey_page_17** (Time, non-required): If you have a specific time constraint for stock management, enter it here.
* **stock_management_survey_page_18** (Select One, required): Choose one of the provided options for stock management.
