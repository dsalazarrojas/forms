# Data Driven Turf Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from turf management professionals about their current practices, data use, and challenges. Your input will help us improve turf management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best match your current practices and preferences.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) if applicable.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Practices** (`current_practices`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a free-text input where you can describe your current practices related to turf management. Please provide a brief description of your current methods, techniques, or approaches used to manage your turf.
* **Data Use** (`data_use`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field asks about the types of data you use in your turf management practices. Select all that apply:
		- Turf Condition
		- Game Schedule
		- Turf Quality
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, optional):
	+ This field is another free-text input where you can describe the challenges you face in your current turf management practices. Please provide a brief description of the difficulties you encounter.
* **Interest in Data-Driven Solutions** (`interest`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field asks about your level of interest in data-driven solutions for turf management. Select one of the following options:
		- Highly Interested
		- Somewhat Interested
		- Not Interested
* **How often do you conduct turf practices?** (`practice_frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field asks about the frequency of your turf practices. Select one of the following options:
		- Every Day
		- Several times a week
		- Rarely
