# Salad Kit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with salad kits. It aims to gather information on their satisfaction level, product quality, value for money, and other relevant details to help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read the introduction carefully to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Choose the correct answers from the options provided for each question.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field to provide more information.
5. If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, use the "Contact Us" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Welcome to our survey** (`Introduction` , `note` , not required)
This page introduces the purpose of the form and explains what to expect.

### 2. **What is your meal kit name?** (`meal_kit_name` , `text` , not required)
Please type in the name of the meal kit you are referring to.

### 3. **How would you rate your satisfaction level with this meal kit?** (`satisfaction_level` , `select_one` , not required)
Choose one of the following satisfaction levels:
* Very Satisfied
* Somewhat Satisfied
* Neutral
* Somewhat Dissatisfied
* Very Dissatisfied

### 4. **What did you think of the quality of the products in this meal kit?** (`product_quality` , `select_multiple` , not required)
Select all that apply:
* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### 5. **How would you rate the value for money of this meal kit?** (`value_for_money` , `select_one` , not required)
Choose one of the following:
* Good
* Fair
* Bad

### 6. **How much did you pay for this meal kit?** (`price` , `number` , not required)
Please type in the amount you paid for the meal kit.

### 7. **How did you get in touch with us?** (`contact_method` , `select_one` , not required)
Choose one of the following:
* Phone
* Email
* Online chat
* In-person

### 8. **Do you have any comments about this meal kit?** (`comments` , `text` , not required)
If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use this field to provide more information.

### 9. **Would you like to be contacted for further discussion?** (`contact_us` , `select_one` , not required)
Choose one of the following:
* Yes
* No

### 10. **What is your email address?** (`email` , `email` , not required)
Please type in your email address if you would like to be contacted.

### 11. **What is your phone number?** (`phone` , `text` , not required)
Please type in your phone number if you would like to be contacted.

### 12. **Which social media do you use?** (`social_media` , `select_multiple` , not required)
Select all that apply:
* Facebook
* Twitter
* Instagram
* LinkedIn
* Other

### 13. **Would you like to be contacted for future product and marketing decisions?** (`follow_up` , `select_one` , not required)
Choose one of the following:
* Yes
* No

### 14. **How often would you like to be contacted?** (`follow_up_frequency` , `select_multiple` , not required)
Select all that apply:
* Every day
* Every week
* Every month
* Every 3 months
* Never
