# Badging Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from participants who have earned badges through the Badging Program. The purpose of this form is to gather information on the participant's experience with the program, including their satisfaction with the program's overall value, recognition, and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Feedback Submitted" in the "Thank You for Your Valuable Feedback" field at the end of the form, once you have completed all the other fields.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with your personal details and moving on to the program experience and overall satisfaction.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience with each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your badge.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Badge Name(s) Earned** (`badge_earned`, text, required): Please list the badge(s) you have earned through the program.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, optional): If applicable, please enter the date you completed the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction With Badge Program** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express your overall satisfaction with the program:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Value for Career Development** (`value_for_career`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express how valuable the program was for your career development:
	+ Highly Valuable
	+ Valuable
	+ Somewhat Valuable
	+ Not Valuable
	+ Unsure
* **Importance of Badge Recognition** (`recognition_importance`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express how important badge recognition was:
	+ Very Important
	+ Important
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Perceived Value** (`section_value`, note, optional): This is a note section where you can add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Program Requirements Were Clear** (`program_clarity`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express how clear the program requirements were:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Content Quality and Relevance** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express the quality and relevance of the program content:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Difficulty Level Was Appropriate** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express how difficult you found the program:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Reasonable Time to Complete** (`completion_timeframe`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express how reasonable the time it took to complete the program:
	+ Too Short
	+ About Right
	+ Too Long
* **Quality of Support Received** (`support_quality`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express the quality of support you received:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ No Support Received
* **Resources Were Adequate** (`resource_adequacy`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express how adequate the resources were:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Skill Improvement From Program** (`skill_improvement`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express how much you improved your skills through the program:
	+ Significant Improvement
	+ Moderate Improvement
	+ Some Improvement
	+ Minimal Improvement
	+ No Improvement
* **Increase in Confidence** (`confidence_increase`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express how much your confidence increased:
	+ Significant Increase
	+ Moderate Increase
	+ Small Increase
	+ No Change
	+ Decreased
* **Impact on Employment or Opportunities** (`employment_impact`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to express the impact of the program on your employment or opportunities:
	+ Very Positive
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
	+ Unsure
* **Suggestions for Program Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions for program improvement.
* **Would You Recommend This Badge Program to Others?** (`recommend_program`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to express whether you would recommend the program to others:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Thank You for Your Valuable Feedback** (`feedback_submission`, select_one, required): Select "Feedback Submitted" to confirm that you have completed the form.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any trouble filling out the form, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the Badging Program.
