NGO Internal Culture Diversity Survey - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on the internal culture and diversity within our organization. This survey aims to understand the current state of our culture and identify areas for improvement. Your input will help us create a more inclusive and welcoming work environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for select and multiple choice questions.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Page (Page 1)**: This is an introduction page to the survey. Please read it carefully and proceed to the next page.
* **How do you describe yourself (Page 2)**: This question asks for your personal description or self-identification. Please provide a brief answer that accurately reflects your identity.
* **How did we do with inclusion (Page 3)**: This question asks about your experiences with inclusion in the workplace. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ a
	+ b
	+ c
* **How did you feel in the last 6 months (Page 4)**: This question asks about your emotional state or feelings during the last 6 months. Please select one of the options:
	+ very strongly disagree
	+ somewhat disagree
	+ neither
	+ somewhat agree
	+ strongly agree
* **What do you believe is our strengths (Page 5)**: This question asks about your perceptions of our organization's strengths. Please provide a brief description or answer.
* **What do you want to improve (Page 6)**: This question asks about areas you think we can improve in our culture and diversity. Please provide a brief description or answer.
* **Email (Page 7)**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (Page 8)**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date (Page 9)**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Time (Page 10)**: This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Additional Comments (Page 11)**: This is a space for you to add any additional comments or suggestions. Please use this section to provide any further feedback or ideas.

Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" section.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your input will help us create a more inclusive and welcoming work environment for everyone.
