# Property Damage Recovery Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about the property damage recovery process. The purpose of this survey is to understand their experiences and concerns regarding the recovery process, which will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select all that apply to your experience with the property damage recovery process.
2. Type in your response to the questions in the text fields.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the "Required" tag.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey (Select Multiple Options)** (`survey_page_1`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting all that apply to your experience with the property damage recovery process:
	+ "Yes" if you received satisfactory support.
	+ "No" if you did not receive satisfactory support.
* **Feedback Survey (Text Response)** (`survey_page_2`, required: false): Provide your detailed feedback about your experience in this text field.
* **Contact Information (Text)** (`survey_page_3`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your contact information in this text field.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help us improve our services.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact information].
