# Multilingual Support Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users on their experience with multilingual support services. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement for future support enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select your native language from the options provided.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and provide specific examples when possible.
4. If there's anything you'd like to suggest for improvement, please type it in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Title** (`survey_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of your feedback. Please enter a brief description of your experience with our multilingual support services.
* **Select Language** (`language`, select_one, required: false): Choose your native language from the list of options provided.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our support?** (`question_1`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Was your language support experience good?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your experience was positive or not.
* **Can we improve anything about your experience?** (`question_3`, text, required: false): If there's anything you'd like us to improve, please type it in.
* **How can we improve our language support?** (`question_4`, text, required: false): Provide specific suggestions on how we can improve our multilingual support services.
* **Suggest an improvement or suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): If you have any additional suggestions or ideas for improvement, please type them in here.
