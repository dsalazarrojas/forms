# Flagship Store Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have visited Flagship Store. It will help us understand how we can improve and provide a better customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and submit your feedback. You can skip any questions you don't feel comfortable answering or don't have an opinion on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your experience at Flagship Store?** (`Introduction`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of your recent visit to Flagship Store.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate our customer service?** (`rating_scale`, `number`, optional): Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate our customer service.
* **What date did you visit Flagship Store?** (`date`, `date`, optional): Use the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-03-25).
* **What time did you visit Flagship Store?** (`time`, `time`, optional): Use the format h:mm a (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **What did you like about Flagship Store?** (`note`, `note`, optional): Tell us about what you liked most during your visit.
* **What's your email?** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **What's your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **How can we improve?** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve your experience at Flagship Store.
