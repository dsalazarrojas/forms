# Boat Trade Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Trade Evaluation Survey is a feedback form designed to gather information about your experience when trading in your boat at a dealership or broker. Your feedback will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses, enabling us to improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question carefully, as it will help us better understand your experience.
2.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
3.  You can submit your feedback anonymously, and all answers will be treated confidentially.
4.  If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (1): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback or questions.
*   **Phone Number** (3): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
*   **Name of Dealership or Broker** (4): Please enter the name of the dealership or broker where you traded in your boat.
*   **Date of Trade Evaluation** (5): Enter the date when you had your boat evaluated.
*   **Boat Traded - Manufacturer** (6): Enter the manufacturer of the boat you traded in.
*   **Boat Traded - Model** (7): Enter the model of the boat you traded in.
*   **Boat Traded - Year** (8): Enter the year of the boat you traded in.
*   **Trade-In Value Offered** (9): Please enter the amount offered for your traded-in boat.
*   **Trade Value Assessment** (10): How fair was the trade-in value you received? Please select from the options below:
    + Very Fair
    + Fair
    + Somewhat Fair
    + Unfair
    + Very Unfair
*   **Was the evaluation thorough and detailed** (11): How thorough and detailed was the evaluation process? Please select from the options below:
    + Very Thorough
    + Thorough
    + Somewhat Thorough
    + Not Thorough
    + Very Insufficient
*   **Professionalism of the Inspector** (12): How professional was the inspector? Please select from the options below:
    + Excellent
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
    + Very Poor
*   **Did they explain the evaluation process clearly** (13): How clear was the explanation of the evaluation process? Please select from the options below:
    + Yes, very clear
    + Yes, mostly clear
    + Somewhat clear
    + Not very clear
    + Not at all clear
*   **Accuracy of Condition Assessment** (14): How accurate was the condition assessment? Please select from the options below:
    + Very Accurate
    + Mostly Accurate
    + Somewhat Accurate
    + Not Very Accurate
    + Inaccurate
*   **Time taken for evaluation** (15): How long did the evaluation process take? Please select from the options below:
    + Less than 15 minutes
    + 15-30 minutes
    + 30-60 minutes
    + 1-2 hours
    + More than 2 hours
*   **Did they research comparable boat values** (16): Did they research comparable boat values? Please select from the options below:
    + Yes, thoroughly researched
    + Yes, some research
    + Minimal research
    + No research done
    + Unsure
*   **Did you purchase a boat from them** (17): Have you made a new boat purchase from this dealer or broker? Please select from the options below:
    + Yes, purchased
    + No, purchased elsewhere
    + Still deciding
    + Did not purchase
*   **Satisfaction with new boat purchase** (18): If you made a new boat purchase, how satisfied are you with the purchase? Please select from the options below:
    + Very Satisfied
    + Satisfied
    + Neutral
    + Dissatisfied
    + Very Dissatisfied
    + Not Applicable
*   **Overall Trade-In Experience Rating** (19): What do you think about your overall trade-in experience? Please select from the options below:
    + Excellent
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
    + Very Poor
*   **Would you recommend this dealer** (20): Would you recommend this dealership or broker to friends and family? Please select from the options below:
    + Definitely Yes
    + Probably Yes
    + Maybe
    + Probably No
    + Definitely No
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (21): If you have any suggestions for how we can improve our services, please enter them here.
*   **Additional Comments** (22): If you have any other comments or feedback, please share them here.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and accurate when answering each question.
*   If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Your feedback is invaluable and will help us improve our services.
