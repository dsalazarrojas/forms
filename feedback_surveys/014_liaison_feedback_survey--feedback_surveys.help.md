# Liaison Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their interactions with Liaisons.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a specific interaction or experience with a Liaison for which you're providing feedback.
2. Fill out each section thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For rating questions, select a number between 1 and 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
4. For open-ended questions, provide your honest and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Liaison Communication** (Text): Describe how well the Liaison communicated with you during the interaction.
* **Professionalism** (Text, Required): Rate how professional the Liaison was during the interaction. (Required)
* **Responsiveness** (Text): Describe how responsive the Liaison was during the interaction.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Text, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction. (Required)
* **Liaison Name** (Text, Required): Provide the name of the Liaison you interacted with. (Required)
* **Liaison Email** (Text, Required): Provide the email address of the Liaison. (Required)
* **Liaison Phone** (Text, Required): Provide the phone number of the Liaison. (Required)
* **Select One 1** (Select One, Required): Choose one option from the list. (Required)
* **Select Multiple 1** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or more options from the list. (Required)
* **Select Multiple 2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more options from the list. (Optional)
* **Select One 2** (Select One, Optional): Choose one option from the list. (Optional)
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Date of the interaction (if applicable).
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Time of the interaction (if applicable).
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Any additional comments or notes about the interaction.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Provide your phone number. (Required)
* **Liaison Email** (Email, Optional): Provide the email address of the Liaison. (Optional)
* **Liaison Phone** (Text, Optional): Provide the phone number of the Liaison. (Optional)
