# Community Information Exhibition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Information Exhibition Survey is a tool to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at exhibitions. The data collected will help event organizers understand what went well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and provide honest answers.
2. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. Fill in the fields as prompted, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Submit your answers when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exhibit 1: Event Feedback
* **Exhibition Feedback** (`-- Exhibition Feedback --`, note, not required): This is a header section for feedback on the exhibition.
* **Attendee name** (`Attendee name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Event date** (`Event date`, date, required): The date of the exhibition you attended.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience at the exhibition.
	+ Choose Very Satisfied for an exceptional experience.
	+ Choose Satisfied for a positive experience.
	+ Choose Neutral for an average experience.
	+ Choose Dissatisfied for an unsatisfactory experience.
	+ Choose Very Dissatisfied for a highly unsatisfactory experience.
* **Most useful information obtained** (`Most useful information obtained`, text, optional): Provide a short description of the most useful information you obtained during the exhibition.
* **Clarity of presentations** (`Clarity of presentations`, select_one, not required): Rate the clarity of the presentations at the exhibition.
	+ Choose Excellent for clear and well-structured presentations.
	+ Choose Good for generally clear, but some room for improvement.
	+ Choose Fair for somewhat unclear or unorganized presentations.
	+ Choose Poor for difficult to follow or unclear presentations.
* **Suggestions for future exhibitions** (`Suggestions for future exhibitions`, text, optional): Share your ideas on how to improve exhibitions.
* **Would recommend** (`Would recommend`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether you would recommend the exhibition to others.
	+ Choose True for yes, you would recommend.
	+ Choose False for no, you would not recommend.
* **Contact email for updates** (`Contact email for updates`, email, not required): If you want to be contacted for updates, enter your email address.
* **Consent for photos** (`Consent for photos`, select_one, not required): Agree to allow photos of you to be used in exhibition reporting.
	+ Choose True for yes, you agree.
	+ Choose False for no, you do not agree.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Check your answers carefully before submitting.
