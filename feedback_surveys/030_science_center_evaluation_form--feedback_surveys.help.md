# Science Center Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Center Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback and insights from visitors about their experience at the science center. Please take a few minutes to provide your honest feedback to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. You may skip any questions that are not relevant to your visit.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please feel free to write them in the "What did you think about your overall experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How did you find our science center** (`science_center_evaluation_form`, `text`, required: false): We would love to hear about your overall impression of our science center. Please describe your experience in a few sentences.
* **Which exhibits did you visit** (`exhibits`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the exhibits you visited during your stay. This will help us understand which exhibits are popular and which ones need improvement.
* **Which program did you take part in** (`program`, `select_one`, required: false): If you participated in any of our programs, please select the one that resonated with you the most.
* **How much time did you spend here** (`time_spent`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the amount of time you spent at our science center.
* **What did you think about your overall experience** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Share any feedback, suggestions, or comments about your visit. We value your opinion and it will help us to improve.
* **What is your email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): We would like to keep in touch with you. Please provide your email address if you would like us to send you updates or special offers.
* **What is your phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have any questions or would like to discuss your experience, please let us know your phone number.
* **When did you visit** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Please provide the date of your visit to help us track your experience over time.
