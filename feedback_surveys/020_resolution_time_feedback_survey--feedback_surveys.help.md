# Resolution Time Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the resolution time for support tickets. Support team leaders can use this form to provide feedback on their team's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Resolution Time** for each ticket, which is the time it took for your team to resolve the issue.
2. Enter the **Resolution Date** for each ticket, which is the date the ticket was resolved.
3. Select the **Resolution Type** for each ticket, which is how the ticket was resolved (On Time, Late, or No Response).
4. Enter any **Comments** you have on the ticket resolution.
5. Enter the **Support Team Lead Name**, **Support Team Lead Email**, and **Support Team Lead Phone** for the team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
6. Enter the **Resolution Status** for each ticket, which is the current status of the ticket (On Hold, Done, Pending, In Progress, or Unknown).
7. Enter any **Resolution Status Comment** you have on the ticket status.
8. Enter the **Support Team Lead Title** and **Support Team Lead Designation** for the team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
9. Enter any **Support Team Lead Comments** you have on the ticket resolution.
10. Enter any **Resolution Time Range Comment** you have on the resolution time.
10. Enter any **Resolution Time Comment** you have on the ticket resolution.
11. Enter the **Support Team Lead 2 Name**, **Support Team Lead 2 Email**, and **Support Team Lead 2 Phone** for the second team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, date): Enter the time it took for your team to resolve the issue.
* **Support Team Name** (`support_team_name`, text): Enter the name of the support team that resolved the ticket.
* **Support Team Lead Name** (`support_team_lead_name`, text): Enter the name of the support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, date): Enter the date the ticket was resolved.
* **Resolution Date Range** (`resolution_date_range`, date): Enter the range of dates for the ticket resolution.
* **Resolution Type** (`resolution_type`, select_one): Select how the ticket was resolved (On Time, Late, or No Response).
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, text): Enter the time it took for the team to respond to the ticket.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one): Select the current status of the ticket (On Hold, Done, Pending, In Progress, or Unknown).
* **Resolution Status Comment** (`resolution_status_comment`, text): Enter any comments on the ticket status.
* **Support Team Lead Email** (`support_team_lead_email`, email): Enter the email of the support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead Phone** (`support_team_lead_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead Title** (`support_team_lead_title`, text): Enter the title of the support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead Designation** (`support_team_lead_designation`, text): Enter the designation of the support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead Comments** (`support_team_lead_comments`, text): Enter any comments on the support team lead's performance.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Name** (`support_team_lead_2`, text): Enter the name of the second support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Email** (`support_team_lead_2_email`, email): Enter the email of the second support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Phone** (`support_team_lead_2_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the second support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Resolution Time Range** (`resolution_time_range`, text): Enter the range of time it took to resolve the ticket.
* **Resolution Time Comment** (`resolution_time_comment`, text): Enter any comments on the resolution time.
* **Resolution Type Comment** (`resolution_type_comment`, text): Enter any comments on the ticket resolution type.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Title** (`support_team_lead_2_title`, text): Enter the title of the second support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Designation** (`support_team_lead_2_designation`, text): Enter the designation of the second support team lead who was responsible for the ticket resolution.
* **Support Team Lead 2 Comments** (`support_team_lead_2_comments`, text): Enter any comments on the second support team lead's performance.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by support team leaders to provide feedback on their team's performance. Please enter accurate and thorough information to help improve the support team's performance.
