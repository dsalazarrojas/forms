# Arboretum Planning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arboretum Planning Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience at the arboretum. Your feedback will help us improve and enhance the overall experience for all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name, email address, and date of visit if you want to be contacted for any follow-up.
2. Answer the questions about your experience at the arboretum, including your satisfaction with the grounds maintenance, plant variety, and facilities.
3. Select any areas that need improvement.
4. Share any additional suggestions or comments you may have.
5. Finally, let us know if you would recommend this arboretum to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Visitor Information
- **Your Name** (`visitor_name`, text, Optional): This is your name. If you provide this information, you can be contacted for any follow-up or to receive updates about the arboretum.
- **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, Optional): This is your email address. You can provide this if you want to be contacted for any follow-up or to receive updates about the arboretum.
- **Date of Your Visit** (`visit_date`, date, Required): Please select the date of your visit to the arboretum.
- **How often do you visit the arboretum?** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, Required): Select how often you visit the arboretum.
  - **First time**
  - **Once a year**
  - **Few times a year**
  - **Monthly**
  - **Weekly**

### Overall Experience
- **Overall satisfaction with your visit** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.

  - **Dissatisfied**
  - **Neutral**

  - **Very Satisfied**

### Improvements and Suggestions
- **Areas most needing improvement** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, Optional): Select any areas that need improvement.

- **Additional suggestions or comments** (`suggestions`, text, Optional): Share any additional suggestions or comments you may have.
