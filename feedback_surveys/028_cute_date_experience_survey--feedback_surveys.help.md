# Cute Date Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experiences and opinions on dating. Your feedback will help us improve the dating service.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date to which this form applies.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
4. Use the options provided to select your responses.
5. Don't forget to rate your experience and say if you would recommend the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are referring to.
* **Who was the date with?** (`partner`, select_one, optional): Choose the person you went on the date with.
* **Was there a memorable moment?** (`memorable_moment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply if there were any memorable moments.
* **What was your feedback?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any comments or thoughts you have about the date.
* **What type of date was this?** (`date_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of date (First Date, Repeat Date, or Casual Date).
* **Was the date fun?** (`fun`, select_one, optional): Select if the date was fun.
* **What makes it memorable?** (`memorable_moment_details`, text, optional): Share what made the date special.
* **Would you like to do this again?** (`would_you_like`, select_one, optional): Select if you would like to repeat the experience.
* **Why or why not?** (`why`, text, optional): Share your reasoning for why you would or wouldn't like to repeat the date.
* **Rate your experience** (`rating`, number, optional): Give your rating of the date.
* **Would you recommend this?** (`recommend`, select_one, optional): Select if you would recommend the service.
* **Why or why not?** (`would_recommend_reason`, text, optional): Share your reasoning for why you would or wouldn't recommend the service.
