# Lease Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lease Policy Understanding Survey" is designed to gain insight into the current state of lease policy management in your organization. It aims to help you identify areas of improvement and provide a clear understanding of lease policy management best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant option for each question based on your role in the company/organization responsible for managing the property.
2. Choose the frequency of your review and update of the lease policy.
3. Select the primary concerns you experience when reviewing or updating the lease policy.
4. Choose the methods you use to communicate changes to the lease policy to stakeholders.
5. Select the methods you use to store and manage documents related to the lease policy.
6. Enter the number of hours you spend on lease-related tasks per week.
7. Select the challenges you face when it comes to understanding the lease policy.
8. Answer whether you have a dedicated team for lease policy management or if it's handled individually.
9. Select the frequency of reviewing lease policy-related data and reports.
10. Describe a recent situation where the lease policy was unclear or confusing.
11. Rate the overall clarity of the lease policy document.
12. Provide suggestions for improving the lease policy document's clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the company/organization responsible for managing the property?** (`role`, select_one): This question helps us understand who is responsible for managing the property within your organization.
* **How often do you review and update the lease policy?** (`review_frequency`, select_one): This question aims to determine how frequently you review and update the lease policy.
* **What is your primary concern when reviewing or updating the lease policy?** (`primary_concern`, select_multiple): This question helps us identify the main challenges you face when reviewing or updating the lease policy.
* **How do you currently communicate changes to the lease policy to stakeholders?** (`communication`, select_multiple): This question seeks to understand how you inform stakeholders about changes to the lease policy.
* **What method do you use to store and manage documents related to the lease policy?** (`document_storage`, select_multiple): This question helps us understand how you store and manage lease policy documents.
* **How many hours do you spend on lease-related tasks?** (`lease_hours`, number): This question aims to determine the amount of time you dedicate to lease-related tasks.
* **What is your biggest challenge when it comes to understanding the lease policy?** (`biggest_challenge`, select_multiple): This question helps us understand the main obstacles you face when comprehending the lease policy.
* **Do you have a dedicated team for lease policy management or is it handled individually?** (`dedicated_team`, select_one): This question determines whether you have a specific team for lease policy management.
* **How often do you review lease policy-related data and reports?** (`data_review`, select_one): This question aims to understand how frequently you review lease policy-related data and reports.
* **Can you describe a recent situation where the lease policy was unclear or confusing?** (`recent_unclear`, text): This question seeks to understand a specific situation where the lease policy was unclear or confusing.
* **How would you rate the overall clarity of the lease policy document?** (`clarity_level`, select_one): This question asks you to evaluate the clarity of the lease policy document.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the lease policy document's clarity?** (`suggestions`, text): This question provides an opportunity to offer suggestions for enhancing the lease policy document's clarity.
