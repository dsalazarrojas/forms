# manager_feedback_responsiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees regarding their manager's responsiveness, which is essential for improving work relationships and fostering a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please navigate through the pages listed below.
2. On each page, select the relevant options or enter your text responses according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure your feedback is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Strongly Disagree** (`feedback_1`, `select_one`, required): Select this option if you strongly disagree with your manager's responsiveness.
* **Neutral** (`feedback_1`, `select_one`, required): Select this option if you are neutral about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Agree** (`feedback_1`, `select_one`, required): Select this option if you agree with your manager's responsiveness.
* **Positive** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for positive feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Negative** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for negative feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Date** (`page_8_label`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the most recent instance of your manager's responsiveness.
* **Time** (`page_9_label`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the most recent instance of your manager's responsiveness.
* **Email** (`page_10_label`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up or additional feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_11_label`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Daily** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the frequency of your manager's responsiveness (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never).
* **Manager Feedback 1** (`employee_feedback_1`, `select_one`, required): Select this option if you have feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Manager Feedback 2** (`employee_feedback_2`, `select_one`, required): Select this option if you have feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your manager's responsiveness.
