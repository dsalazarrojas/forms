# Accounting System Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback from users about their experience with the accounting system. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance the system's overall usability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your primary role in the organization from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the accounting software system you primarily use.
3. Estimate how frequently you interact with the system.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the system.
5. Rate how easy it is to use the system.
6. If you've experienced performance issues, select all that apply.
7. Rate your satisfaction with specific features (reporting, data entry, integrations).
8. Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with reporting capabilities.
9. Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with data entry process.
10. Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with system integrations.
11. Rate the adequacy of the training provided.
12. Rate the quality of technical support provided.
13. Select all improvements you would like to see in the system.
14. Enter a numeric value to rate how likely you are to recommend this system to others.
15. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role in the Organization** (`user_role`, select_one): Select your primary role in the organization, such as Accountant, Bookkeeper, Finance Manager, CFO, Data Analyst, Administrator, or Other.
* **Accounting System Used** (`system_used`, select_one): Choose the accounting software system you primarily use, such as QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Sage, SAP, Oracle, FreshBooks, or Other.
* **How Often Do You Use the System** (`usage_frequency`, select_one): Estimate how frequently you interact with the system, such as Daily, Several times per week, Weekly, Several times per month, Monthly.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the System** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the system, such as Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Ease of Use Rating** (`ease_of_use_rating`, select_one): Rate how easy it is to use the system, such as Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very difficult.
* **Have You Experienced Performance Issues** (`system_performance_issues`, select_multiple): Select all performance issues you've encountered, such as Slow loading times, Frequent crashes, Login problems, Data sync delays, Report generation delays, None of the above.
* **Satisfaction with Key Features** (`feature_satisfaction`, note): Rate your satisfaction with specific features, such as reporting, data entry, integrations.
* **Reporting Feature Satisfaction** (`reporting_feature_satisfaction`, number): Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with reporting capabilities.
* **Data Entry Satisfaction** (`data_entry_satisfaction`, number): Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with data entry process.
* **Integration Capability Satisfaction** (`integration_capability_satisfaction`, number): Enter a numeric value to rate your satisfaction with system integrations.
* **Adequacy of Training Provided** (`training_adequacy`, select_one): Rate the adequacy of the training provided, such as More than adequate, Adequate, Somewhat adequate, Inadequate, Very inadequate.
* **Quality of Technical Support** (`support_quality`, select_one): Rate the quality of technical support provided, such as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
* **What Improvements Would You Most Like to See** (`desired_improvements`, select_multiple): Select all improvements you would like to see in the system, such as Better user interface, Faster performance, Enhanced reporting, Better mobile app, Improved integrations, More automation features, Better customer support.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend This System** (`recommendation_likelihood`, number): Enter a numeric value to rate how likely you are to recommend this system to others, such as 0-10.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, text): Enter any other comments about the accounting system.
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, number): Enter a numeric value to rate how likely you are to recommend this system to others, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, text): Enter any other comments about the accounting system.
