# Pre Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their pre-experience with the program, allowing the organization to understand their expectations, background, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Select the relevant options for the select_multiple and select_one fields.
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields, even if you're not sure what to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the title of the program?** (`program_title`, text, optional): Please enter the title of the program you are participating in.
* **What is a brief description of the program?** (`program_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the program to help us understand your expectations.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the program materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the program.
* **What are your pre-program expectations?** (`program_expectations`, text, optional): Describe your expectations for the program in 3-4 sentences.
* **Background Information** (`background_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief background story.
* **What type of program is it?** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of program you are participating in (e.g. Workshop, Webinar, Course).
* **What is your primary interest in the program?** (`participant_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the primary interest that drives your participation (e.g. Personal, Professional, Both).
* **What is your primary goal for this program?** (`participant_goal`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the primary goal you hope to achieve through the program (e.g. Skill acquisition, Networking, Networking and Skill Acquisition).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are willing to commit to the program.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are participating in the program.
* **Date format** (`date_format`, select_one, optional): Choose the format of the date you entered (e.g. YYYY-MM-DD, MMMM/DD/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time format** (`time_format`, select_one, optional): Choose the format of the time you entered (e.g. 12:00, 12:00 PM, 12:00 AM).
* **How long is the program?** (`program_length`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the length of the program (e.g. Half day, Full day, Multiple days).
* **What is your level of experience with our organization?** (`participant_experience`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of experience with the organization (e.g. Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **In what language will the program be presented?** (`program_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the language of the program (e.g. English, French, Spanish).
* **Are there any accessibility requirements?** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs or restrictions, please describe them.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted?** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone, Mail).
* **Is there an emergency contact we should know?** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If there is an emergency contact we should know, please enter their name, phone number, and email.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may be relevant?** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
* **Are you taking any medication?** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medication, please describe it.
* **Are there any emergency procedures we should know?** (`emergency_procedures`, text, optional): If there are any emergency procedures we should know, please describe them.
* **Do you have a preferred mode of transportation?** (`participant_vehicles`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred mode of transportation (e.g. Car, Taxi, Public Transportation).
* **How will you arrive at the location?** (`participant_arrival`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you will arrive at the location (e.g. Driving, Walking, Other).
* **What is your preferred parking option?** (`participant_parking`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred parking option (e.g. On site, Street parking, Other).
