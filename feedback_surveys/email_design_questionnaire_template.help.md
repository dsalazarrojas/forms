# email_design_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email design questionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback on the design and effectiveness of our emails, aiming to improve user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
2. Answer all questions to provide a comprehensive feedback.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with the overall design of our emails?**
	+ **Satisfaction Level**: Select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How often do you find yourself paying attention to our email designs?**
	+ **Frequency**: Choose how often you pay attention to our email designs: Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Which of the following best describes our email designs? (Select all that apply)**
	+ **Design Style**: Check all the elements that apply to our email designs, including Visually appealing, Easy to read, Clear Call-to-action, Mobile-friendly, Engaging, and Too busy.
* **What elements do you find most important in an email's design? (Select all that apply)**
	+ **Design Elements**: Choose the design elements that you find most important, including Images, Headings, CTA buttons, Layout, Brand colors, and Personalization.
* **What style of email design do you generally prefer?**
	+ **Preferred Style**: Select one of four options: Minimalist, Bold and vibrant, Classic and elegant, or Information-rich.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for our email designs?**
	+ **Improvement Suggestions**: Provide detailed and specific suggestions for our email designs.
* **Additional comments or feedback?**
	+ **Comments and Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have regarding our email designs.

## Tips
* Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our email designs.
* Keep in mind that your responses will be kept confidential and only used to improve our email design process.
* If you have already filled out this form, please do not resubmit it. Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will be taken into consideration.
