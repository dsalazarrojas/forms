# Post Workshop Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Post Workshop Engagement Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from participants who have attended a workshop. It helps us understand how participants perceive the workshop, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions for future workshops. Please take about 5-10 minutes to complete this survey to help us better understand your engagement and experiences with the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the corresponding answers based on your experiences and perceptions about the workshop. For numerical fields like "workshop_satisfaction" and "overall_rating", please enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. For select_multiple fields like "key_takeaways" and "areas_for_improvement", please select all the options that apply to you. For text fields, please provide as much detail as you can, but keep it concise. For select_one fields like "will_recommend" and "recommend_workshop", please select one of the options that best reflect your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Workshop Satisfaction** (`workshop_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate how satisfied you are with the workshop, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 2. **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that best describe the key takeaways you got from the workshop:
* What was the most valuable takeaway from the workshop?
* What did you find most interesting or surprising?
* What would you like to apply in your work or personal projects?

### 3. **Workshop Organization** (`workshop_organization`, `text`, required: false)
Please provide any comments or feedback about the workshop organization.

### 4. **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required: false)
Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your overall experience with the workshop, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 5. **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that you think could be improved:
* Time Management
* Content
* Venue
* Organization
* Materials

### 6. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
Please provide any feedback about your experience with the workshop.

### 7. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false)
Do you have any suggestions for future workshops?

### 8. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussions.

### 9. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)

### 10. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)

### 11. **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false)
