# Industry Insights Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Industry Insights Feedback Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from industry experts on various aspects of a particular industry. This form helps us understand the current trends, challenges, and opportunities in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form and what we are looking for.
2. Select all the options that apply to the questions listed on page 2.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text box on page 3.
4. Choose the category and industry that best represent your area of expertise.
5. Fill in your email address and phone number (optional) for further contact.
6. Submit your feedback by selecting the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`page 1`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
* **Questions** (`page 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that apply to your area of expertise.
* **Comments** (`page 3`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have. This is an optional field, but your thoughts are valuable to us.
* **Feedback** (`page 4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the feedback option that best represents your opinion.
* **Additional Feedback** (`page 5`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback you have. This is an optional field, but your thoughts are valuable to us.
* **Category** (`page 6`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the category that best represents your area of expertise.
* **Industry** (`page 7`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the industry that best represents your area of expertise.
* **Email** (`page 8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`page 9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date Submitted** (`page 10`, `date`, required: false): The date and time you submitted your feedback will be displayed here.
