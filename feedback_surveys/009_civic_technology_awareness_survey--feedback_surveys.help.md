# Civic Technology Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your awareness, usage, and preferences regarding civic technology. The data collected will help us understand how people interact with government apps, websites, and other digital tools, and what features they would like to see in these technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page and answer the questions accordingly. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions, and provide a brief answer in the text field for open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): This is your full name, which will be used to identify you for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): This is your email address, which will be used to send you survey results or follow-up information if you consent.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Please select your age group from the options provided.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Please select your highest completed education level from the options provided.
* **Technology Proficiency** (`tech_proficiency`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your overall technology proficiency from the options provided.
* **Civic Technology Awareness** (`civic_tech_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your familiarity with civic technology from the options provided.
* **Civic Technology Tools** (`civic_tech_tools_known`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the civic technology tools you have used from the options provided.
* **Government App Usage** (`government_app_usage`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you use government apps or websites from the options provided.
* **Digital Participation** (`digital_participation`, `select_one`, required): Please select how frequently you participate in digital civic activities from the options provided.
* **Trust in Government Technology** (`trust_government_tech`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your trust in government technology from the options provided.
* **Privacy Concerns** (`privacy_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your concerns about privacy with civic apps from the options provided.
* **Accessibility of Civic Tools** (`accessibility_rating`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the accessibility of current civic technology tools from the options provided.
* **Digital Divide** (`digital_divide`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you think the digital divide affects civic participation in your community.
* **Preferred Channels** (`preferred_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your preferred channels for engaging with government.
* **Feature Requests** (`feature_requests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all features you would like to see in civic technology.
* **Civic Tech Training Interest** (`training_interest`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your interest in civic tech training from the options provided.
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Please rate how likely you are to recommend civic tech tools to friends or family.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions on how civic technology can be improved.
