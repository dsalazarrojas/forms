# Headphone Purchase Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headphone Purchase Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experiences with purchasing our headphones. This form helps us understand what we do well and where we can improve, ultimately allowing us to provide better products and services to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us understand your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase Experience** (`purchase_experience`, `text`, required: false): This field is a brief description of your experience with purchasing our headphones. Please describe your purchase process, any difficulties you faced, and any positive or negative feelings you associate with the process.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): How satisfied are you with our product? Please select one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Not Satisfied at All.
* **Purchase Intent** (`purchase_intent`, `text`, required: false): This field is an optional field for you to express your motivation for making the purchase. If you selected "Very Satisfied" in the "Product Satisfaction" field, you can elaborate on what made you happy with the purchase.
* **Price Value** (`price_value`, `number`, required: false): If you feel that our product's price is not a good representation of its value, please provide a numerical value to express your thoughts.
* **Purchase Experience Rating** (`purchase_experience_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall purchase experience from Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor, or Bad.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Would you recommend our product to others? Please select True if you would recommend, False if you wouldn't.
* **Product Reliability** (`product_reliability`, `time`, required: false): If you have any thoughts on the reliability of our product, please provide a specific time frame or duration that reflects your experience.
* **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`, `date`, required: false): This field is to record the time you spent on the purchase process. While not required, it will help us understand the efficiency of our checkout process.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have any concerns or need to discuss your experience further, you can provide your phone number and we'll get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to receive further communication about your feedback, please provide your email address and we'll keep you updated on any changes or progress related to your experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `note`, required: false): This is an open-ended field for you to suggest any improvements or features you'd like to see added to our product or service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any further thoughts or feelings about your experience, please share them here.
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, `select_one`, required: false): Would you like to receive an email notification when we implement changes based on your feedback? Select True if you'd like to be notified, False otherwise.
