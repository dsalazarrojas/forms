# Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from participants in our training programs. Your responses will help us understand what's working well and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text area provided for that purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey for?**: This is an introductory page that explains the purpose of the survey and its importance.
* **What training programs have you attended?**:
  * Select all the training programs you have attended from the list below:
    - New Employee Orientation
    - Continuing Education
    - Certification
* **How would you rate your overall effectiveness of staff training?**: Rate your overall effectiveness of staff training on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "Not effective at all" and 10 is "Very effective".
* **How satisfied are you with the training program?**: Rate your satisfaction with the training program on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "Not satisfied at all" and 10 is "Very satisfied".
* **How relevant do you think the training programs are to your work?**: Rate how relevant you think the training programs are to your work on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "Not relevant at all" and 10 is "Very relevant".
* **How would you rate the communication with your training instructor?**: Rate the communication with your training instructor on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".
* **Do you have any feedback about our staff training programs?**: Use this text area to provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving our training programs.
* **Is there anything else you think we can do to improve our training programs?**: Use this text area to share any additional thoughts or ideas on how we can enhance our training programs.
