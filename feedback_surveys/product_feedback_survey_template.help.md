# product_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather product feedback from users, aiming to identify areas for improvement and assess overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with our product overall?" by selecting one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
2. For the question "Which of the following features do you use most often?", select all the features that apply from the list: Feature A, Feature B, Feature C, Feature D, Feature E.
3. For the question "What aspects most influenced your opinion of the product?", select all the aspects that apply from the list: Design, Price, Performance, Features, Support, Ease of Use.
4. Answer the question "Would you recommend this product to others?" by selecting one of the three options: true, false, Maybe.
5. Enter your answer to the question "What is the primary reason you use our product?" in the text field.
6. Enter your answer to the question "What is one thing you would improve or change about the product?" in the text field.
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our product overall?** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Which of the following features do you use most often?** (Feature A, Feature B, Feature C, Feature D, Feature E): Select all the features that apply to your experience with the product.
* **What aspects most influenced your opinion of the product?** (Design, Price, Performance, Features, Support, Ease of Use): Select all the aspects that influenced your opinion of the product.
* **Would you recommend this product to others?** (true, false, Maybe): Answer with one of the three options provided.
* **What is the primary reason you use our product?**: Enter a brief description of why you use the product.
* **What is one thing you would improve or change about the product?**: Enter a brief description of one thing you would improve or change about the product.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.
