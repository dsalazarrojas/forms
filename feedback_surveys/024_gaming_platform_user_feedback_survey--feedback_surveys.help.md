# gaming_platform_user_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from players of the gaming platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections, each representing a different aspect of the player's experience.
2. Please answer all required fields to submit your feedback.
3. If a field is not marked as required, you can skip it, but be aware that submitting incomplete data might affect the accuracy of the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Platform Rating** (platform_rating, number, required):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform. The scale ranges from -5 (Very dissatisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required):
	+ Choose one of the predefined options (Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied) that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the platform.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (improvement_opportunities, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all areas that you feel need improvement on the platform (Bugs, Performance, User interface, Content).
* **Recommendations** (recommendations, text, optional):
	+ If you have any suggestions or recommendations for the platform, enter them here.
* **Player Experience** (player_experience, text, optional):
	+ Describe your personal experience playing on the platform, including any memorable events or notable features.
* **Player ID** (player_id, text, optional):
	+ Enter your unique player ID, if you have one.
* **Player Name** (player_name, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as a player.
* **Player Email** (player_email, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Player Phone** (player_phone, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, if you wish for us to contact you by phone.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us to improve the platform for all players.
* If you have any questions or need help with submitting your feedback, feel free to contact us.
