# Audit Resource Utilization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback about the availability and effectiveness of resources within an organization to help improve the overall efficiency and productivity of teams.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the date when this survey is being completed.
2.  Enter the name of the department being surveyed.
3.  Enter your name.
4.  Enter your employee identification number (if applicable).
5.  Rate your satisfaction with resource access on a scale from very unsatisfied to very satisfied.
6.  Choose whether resources are adequate for your role on a scale from definitely not to definitely yes.
7.  Select all that apply to describe your tools and equipment status.
8.  Rate how effectively you are using technology on a scale from very low to very high.
9.  Rate the quality of training you received.
10. Rate how much budget constraints impact your work on a scale from no impact to severe impact.
11. Choose whether current staffing levels are adequate on a scale from severely understaffed to overstaffed.
12. Rate the fairness of resource allocation across teams on a scale from very unfair to very fair.
13. If applicable, select the biggest resource gap you face.
14. Rate how likely it is that efficiency could improve with better resources on a scale from very unlikely to very likely.
15. Provide specific improvement suggestions.
16. Rate how well resources are communicated to teams on a scale from very poor to excellent.
17. Choose whether support resources are available when needed on a scale from never available to always available.
18. Rate your overall satisfaction with resources on a scale from very unsatisfied to very satisfied.
19. Provide any additional comments about resource utilization.
20. Enter an email address if you would like to discuss the results of this survey further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): When is this survey being completed.
*   **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Name of department being surveyed.
*   **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Your name.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Your employee identification number.
*   **Satisfaction with Resource Access** (`resource_access_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with access to needed resources.
*   **Are Resources Adequate for Your Role** (`resource_adequacy`, select_one, required): Do you have sufficient resources to perform your duties.
*   **Tools and Equipment Status** (`tools_and_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to your situation.
*   **Technology Utilization Level** (`technology_utilization`, select_one, required): How effectively are you using technology.
*   **Quality of Training Received** (`training_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the training you received.
*   **Budget Constraints Impact** (`budget_constraints`, select_one, required): How much do budget limits affect your work.
*   **Staffing Level Adequacy** (`staffing_levels`, select_one, required): Are current staffing levels adequate.
*   **Resource Allocation Fairness** (`resource_allocation_fairness`, select_one, optional): Is resource allocation fair across teams.
*   **Biggest Resource Gap** (`biggest_resource_gap`, text, optional): What resource do you need most.
*   **Efficiency Improvement Potential** (`efficiency_improvement_potential`, select_one, required): How likely is it that efficiency could improve with better resources.
*   **Specific Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): What changes would help most.
*   **Resource Information Communication** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): How well are resources communicated to teams.
*   **IT and Support Team Availability** (`support_availability`, select_one, required): Are support resources available when needed.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): General satisfaction rating.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback about resource utilization.
*   **Email for Follow-Up** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you would like to discuss further.

## Tips

*   Please answer every question honestly to provide accurate data.
*   This form is designed to help the organization improve resource utilization and efficiency.
*   If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
*   If you would like to discuss the results further, enter your email address in the "Email for Follow-Up" field.
