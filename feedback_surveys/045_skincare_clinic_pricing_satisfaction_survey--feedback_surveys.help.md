# Skincare Clinic Pricing Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback on the pricing of our Skincare Clinic services, helping us to better understand your expectations and satisfaction with our prices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully before selecting an answer.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question.
3. Fill out the entire form, as some questions are required for data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Time Visitor** (`First Time Visitor`, `select_one`, required): This question determines if you are visiting the Skincare Clinic for the first time. Please select "Yes" if this is your first visit, or "No" otherwise.
* **How often do you visit the clinic?** (`visit_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your visit frequency to the Skincare Clinic. You can select one or multiple options from the list.
* **What is your price sensitivity?** (`price_sensitivity`, `select_one`, required): This question measures how sensitive you are to price changes. Select the option that best describes your price sensitivity.
* **Have you visited us in the past 3 months?** (`price_range`, `select_one`, required): This question asks if you have visited the Skincare Clinic within the past 3 months. Choose the correct option from the list.
* **What is the price of our service compared to your expectations?** (`price_range2`, `select_one`, required): This question compares the price of our service to your expectations. Select "Yes" if the price is in line with your expectations, or "No" otherwise.
* **How would you rate the value for money?** (`price_range3`, `number`, required): This question asks you to rate how valuable you think the Skincare Clinic service is for the price. You can enter a numerical value between 0 and 100.
* **What is your expectation for the service price?** (`price_range4`, `number`, required): This question asks about your expected price range for the Skincare Clinic services. Enter a numerical value.
* **How much would you be willing to pay for our service?** (`price_range5`, `number`, required): This question determines how much you are willing to pay for the Skincare Clinic services. Enter a numerical value.
* **Would you be willing to pay more for the service?** (`price_range6`, `select_one`, required): This question asks if you would be willing to pay more for the Skincare Clinic services. Choose "Yes" or "No" from the list.
* **Have you considered visiting another clinic for the service?** (`price_range7`, `select_one`, optional): This question inquires about considering alternative clinics for service. Select "Yes" or "No" from the list.
* **Would you recommend our service to a friend or colleague?** (`price_range8`, `select_one`, required): This question asks if you would recommend our service to someone else. Choose the correct option from the list.
