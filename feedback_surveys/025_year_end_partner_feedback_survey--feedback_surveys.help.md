# Year End Partner Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from partners at the end of the year, providing valuable insights into your experience with the partner. Your feedback will help us identify areas of strength and weakness in our partnership and inform future collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your partner's name in the first field.
2. Select the partner's title in the second field.
3. Answer the three open-ended questions (What was your overall experience with this partner, What did this partner do well, and What could this partner improve on).
4. Rate the partner's collaboration, communication, and support using the number scales.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the last field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the partner you are providing feedback on.
* **Partner Title** (`partner_title`, text, required: false): Select the title of the partner's representative or contact person.
* **What was your overall experience with this partner?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required: false): Describe your overall experience with the partner in a brief but concise manner.
* **What did this partner do well?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required: false): Identify specific areas where the partner excelled or demonstrated strengths.
* **What could this partner improve on?** (`feedback_question_3`, text, required: false): Provide constructive criticism or areas for improvement.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the partner's level of collaboration on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication** (`communication_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the partner's level of communication on a scale of 1-10.
* **Support** (`support_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the partner's level of support on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the partner on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact information.
