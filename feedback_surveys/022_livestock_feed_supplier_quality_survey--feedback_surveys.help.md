# Livestock Feed Supplier Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from farmers on their experiences with livestock feed suppliers, aiming to improve services and better cater to customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant options from the drop-down menus for each field.
2. Enter your numerical ratings (1-5) in the number fields.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your entries before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Information** (`farmer_information`, text, required: false): Please provide your name and contact details to ensure we can get back to you with any further questions or clarifications.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information to get in touch with you if we need more details.
* **Farm Details** (`farm_details`, text, required: false): Describe your farm details, including the type of animals you raise and feed them with.
* **Feed Info** (`feed_info`, text, required: false): Provide information about the feed you purchased from us, including product name and quantity.
* **Quality Ratings** (`quality_ratings`, text, required: false): Rate the quality of the products or services you received from us on a scale of 1-5.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, required: false): Describe any additional information about your supplier, such as their contact details.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, required: false): Share any experiences you've had with our team, such as support or communication.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, required: false): Evaluate our customer service, including communication and responsiveness.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, number, required: false): Enter the delivery time of the product or service you received.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required: false): Rate the shipping cost of the product or service you received.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the product or service you received.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Rate the price of the product or service you received.
* **Product Presentation** (`product_presentation`, number, required: false): Rate the presentation of the product or service you received.
* **Supplier Rating** (`supplier_rating`, select_one, required: false): Choose the level of satisfaction you have with the supplier, with options "Very Satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Customer Support** (`customer_support`, select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate the availability of customer support from us.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate the quality of communication from us.
* **Delivery** (`delivery`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the timely delivery of the product or service you received.
* **Product Availability** (`product_availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the availability of the product you received.
* **Customer Service 2** (`customer_service_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate the responsiveness of our customer service team.
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the resolution of any issues you had with our team or services.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_multiple, required: false): Evaluate the availability of a contact person for your queries.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the ease of contact with our team.
* **Shipping Cost 2** (`shipping_cost_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the cost of shipping the product or service you received.
* **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality`, number, required: false): Rate the overall quality of the product or service you received.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
* If you have any queries or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your cooperation and participation are highly valued and appreciated.
