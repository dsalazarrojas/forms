# Athlete Mentorship Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from athletes who have participated in the Athlete Mentorship Program. The feedback is meant to help the program's administrators evaluate the effectiveness of the program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple choice options, select the one that best represents your experience.
3. For open-ended questions (text fields), provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to be used in communication.
	+ This field is required and will help us know who is providing feedback.
* **Mentor or Mentee Name**: Enter the name of the person you were paired with in the program.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the dynamics of your partnership.
* **Your Role in the Program**: Select whether you were a Mentor or Mentee in the program.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand your perspective.
* **Duration of Participation**: Select the duration of your participation in the program.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the scope of your experience.
* **Frequency of Interaction**: Select how often you met or communicated with your partner.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the level of collaboration.
* **Quality of Communication**: Select how good or bad your communication was with your partner.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the effectiveness of the partnership.
* **Ease of Scheduling Meetings**: Select how easy or difficult it was to schedule meetings with your partner.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand any logistical challenges.
* **Feeling of Support**: Select how much support you felt from your partner.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the level of support provided.
* **Achievement of Initial Program Goals**: Select how much progress you made towards your initial goals.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the impact of the program.
* **Improvement in Athletic Performance**: Select how much improvement you saw in your athletic performance.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand any gains made through the program.
* **Improvement in Personal Development**: Select how much improvement you saw in your personal development.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand any personal benefits of the program.
* **Most Valuable Aspect of the Program**: Share what you found most helpful or valuable about the program.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand what made the program successful.
* **Challenges Faced**: Provide any challenges you faced during the program.
	+ This field is optional, but can help us understand any difficulties experienced.
* **Satisfaction with Initial Training and Orientation**: Select how satisfied you were with the initial training and orientation provided.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the quality of onboarding.
* **Satisfaction with Administrative Support**: Select how satisfied you were with the administrative support provided.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the level of support provided by staff.
* **Suggestions for Program Improvement**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the program.
	+ This field is optional, but can help us make the program better.
* **Recommend the Program**: Select whether you would recommend this program to other athletes.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the program's reputation.
* **Future Participation Interest**: Select whether you would participate again in a future cycle.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand interest in future programs.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or feedback about the program.
	+ This field is optional, but can help us understand any other thoughts or concerns.
