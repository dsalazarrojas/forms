# prepaid_package_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prepaid_package_feedback_survey is designed to collect feedback on customer experiences with prepaid packages. This form helps us understand customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides context and background information on the form.
2. Rate your overall experience with the prepaid package on a scale of 1-5.
3. Check why you purchased the prepaid package, selecting all that apply from the options.
4. Provide a suggestion for improvement, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, providing a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Rate** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the prepaid package on a scale of 1-5. This helps us understand your level of satisfaction.
* **Why Purchased** (`reason`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to indicate why you purchased the prepaid package. This helps us understand your motivations and preferences.
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the prepaid package. This can be a positive or negative comment.
