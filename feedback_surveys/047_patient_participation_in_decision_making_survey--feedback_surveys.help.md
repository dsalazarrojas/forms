# Patient Participation In Decision Making Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Patient Participation In Decision Making Survey is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience with healthcare providers. This survey aims to measure the level of participation and involvement patients have in decisions made about their care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**. This survey is intended to gather your feedback and experiences.
2. **Select one answer option per question**. You may choose from the provided options for each question.
3. **Be as specific as possible** when answering the questions. This will help healthcare providers understand your experience better.
4. **Submit the form** once you have completed all questions.
