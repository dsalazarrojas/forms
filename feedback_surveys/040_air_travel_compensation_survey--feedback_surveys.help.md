# Air Travel Compensation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from passengers about their experiences with air travel compensation. We use this information to understand how we can improve our processes and provide better service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately. Required questions are marked with a *.
2. For optional questions, you can choose from the provided options or provide additional information in the text field.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, optional): 
This is the name of the passenger who experienced the issue. Please enter your name as it appears on your boarding pass or ID.

### 2. **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): 
Enter the flight number of the trip that experienced the issue. This helps us identify the specific flight.

### 3. **Disruption Type** (`disruption_type`, select_one, *): 
Choose the type of disruption that occurred, such as:
*   Delay
*   Cancellation
*   Baggage loss
*   Other

### 4. **Compensation Received** (`compensation_received`, select_one, *): 
If you received compensation, please select:
*   'True'
*   'False'
*   Offered but declined

### 5. **Satisfaction with Resolution** (`satisfaction_with_resolution`, select_one, optional): 
Rate your satisfaction with how the issue was resolved:
*   Very satisfied
*   Satisfied
*   Neutral
*   Dissatisfied

### 6. **Time to Resolution (hours)** (`time_to_resolution_hours`, number, optional): 
If you reported the issue, please estimate the time taken to resolve it in hours.

### 7. **Refund or Voucher** (`refund_or_voucher`, select_one, optional): 
If you received a refund or voucher, select:
*   Refund
*   Voucher
*   Cash
*   None

### 8. **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): 
If you have any suggestions or comments about how we can improve our compensation process, please provide them here.
