# Gym Facility Renovation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your feedback about the gym facility's renovation. Your input will help us understand your opinions and suggestions on the new layout and amenities, ensuring we can make necessary improvements to enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer the questions in this form to provide your honest feedback.
* Each question is designed to gather specific information about your experience with the gym facility.
* You can select one or multiple answers for questions that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Facility Feedback** (Page 1: main_page)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the gym facility's renovation.
* **Renovation Type** (Page 2: renovation_type)
	+ Select the renovation type that best describes your experience:
		- Upgrade: We've noticed improvements in the facility.
		- Maintenance: We've noticed areas that need improvement.
		- Other: Please explain your experience in the text field.
		- None: You haven't noticed any changes.
* **Feedback on Upgrade** (Page 3: feedback_on_upgrade)
	+ Share your thoughts on what you've liked or disliked about the upgrade.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 4: overall_satisfaction)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the renovated facility on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Page 5: suggestions_for_improvement)
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for improving the gym facility.
* **Email** (Page 6: email)
	+ Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about future improvements.
* **Phone** (Page 7: phone)
	+ Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Date of Last Visit** (Page 8: date_of_last_visit)
	+ Enter the date of your last visit to the gym facility.
* **Time of Last Visit** (Page 9: time_of_last_visit)
	+ Enter the time of your last visit to the gym facility.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and provide specific examples to help us understand your experience.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular question, feel free to contact us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to make improvements to the gym facility.
