# SPL Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SPL Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers after visiting the SPL facility. This form helps to gauge customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: name, email, feedback, rating, date of visit, time of visit, rating of facility, and name of facility.
2. If you have additional comments or ratings, fill out the corresponding fields: comment, rating2, comment2, select_one2, date2, time2, rating3, and comment3.
3. In the select fields, choose the options that apply to your experience.
4. If applicable, select multiple options in the select multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **email** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **feedback** (`<feedback>`, `text`, required): Provide your feedback on the SPL.
* **rating** (`<rating>`, `number`, required): Rate your experience (1-5).
* **comment** (`<comment>`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **name_of_spl** (`<name_of_spl>`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the SPL.
* **rating_of_spl** (`<rating_of_spl>`, `number`, required): Rate the SPL (1-5).
* **date** (`<date>`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **time** (`<time>`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
* **select_one** (`<select_one>`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that apply to your experience.
* **select_multiple** (`<select_multiple>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **date_of_visit** (`<date_of_visit>`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **time_of_visit** (`<time_of_visit>`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
* **rating_of_facility** (`<rating_of_facility>`, `number`, required): Rate the facility (1-5).
* **name_of_facility** (`<name_of_facility>`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the facility.
* **select_multiple2** (`<select_multiple2>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **rating2** (`<rating2>`, `number`, required): Rate your experience (1-5).
* **comment2** (`<comment2>`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **select_one2** (`<select_one2>`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that apply to your experience.
* **date2** (`<date2>`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **time2** (`<time2>`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
* **rating3** (`<rating3>`, `number`, required): Rate your experience (1-5).
* **comment3** (`<comment3>`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **select_multiple3** (`<select_multiple3>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple options that apply to your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is submitted correctly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
