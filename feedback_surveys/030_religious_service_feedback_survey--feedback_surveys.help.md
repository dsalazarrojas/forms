# Religious Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the worship service provided. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the quality of the worship service you attended.
2. Indicate how helpful the sermon was.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
4. Choose the quality of the service.
5. Select the quality of the music played during the service.
6. Choose the quality of the speaker's delivery.
7. (Optional) If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the text field.
8. (Optional) If you have any other comments, please type them in the text field.
9. Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need to follow up.
10. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if we need to discuss any matters related to your feedback.
11. Add any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worship Service Rating**: Please rate the quality of the service from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Was the sermon helpful?**: Choose all that apply to the level of helpfulness you felt from the sermon:
	+ Not helpful
	+ Helpful
	+ Somewhat helpful
	+ Very helpful
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with the service?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Service quality**: Choose the quality of the service:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Music quality**: Choose all that apply to the level of quality you felt from the music:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
	+ Did not play music
	+ Too loud
* **Speaker quality**: Choose the quality of the speaker's delivery:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **What can be improved?** (Optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in this text field.
* **Other** (Optional): If you have any other comments, please type them in this text field.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if we need to discuss any matters related to your feedback.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if we need to discuss any matters related to your feedback.
* **Name**: Please enter your name so we can address your feedback.
* **Comments**: Please add any additional comments about your experience.
* **How often do you attend the worship service?**: Choose how often you attend the worship service:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Always

Note: The form fields marked as "required" are mandatory and need to be filled in order to submit the form. The non-mandatory fields are optional and can be filled or left blank.
