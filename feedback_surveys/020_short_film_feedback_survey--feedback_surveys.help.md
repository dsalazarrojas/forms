# Short Film Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have watched a short film. The purpose of this form is to gather their thoughts and opinions about the film. This information will be used to improve the content and make it more engaging for future viewers.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form on your device (phone, tablet, or computer).
* Start by clicking on the "Feedback" page to begin filling out the form.
* On the "Feedback" page, provide your thoughts and opinions about the film. This is your chance to share your honest feedback.
* On the "Contact Information" page, please provide your contact information (phone number) so we can get in touch with you if we need more information.
* On the "Email" page, please enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* On the "Phone" page, please enter your phone number (optional).
* On the "Submission Type" page, select how you watched the film (e.g., online, in theaters, etc.).
* On the "Watched Online" and "Watched In Theaters" pages, select whether you watched the film online or in a theater.
* On the "Comments" page, select any additional comments you have about the film.
* Finally, on the "page_10" page (Comments), you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (text, required): This is the main feedback section where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the film.
* **Contact Information** (phone number, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (email address, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Phone** (phone number, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for future contact.
* **Submission Type** (select_one, required): Choose how you watched the film (e.g., online, in theaters, etc.).
* **Watched Online** (select_one, optional): Select whether you watched the film online.
* **Watched In Theaters** (select_one, optional): Select whether you watched the film in a theater.
* **Comments** (select_multiple, optional): Select any additional comments or suggestions you have about the film.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions on this page.
