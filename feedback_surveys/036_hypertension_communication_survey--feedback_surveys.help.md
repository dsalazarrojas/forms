# Hypertension Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hypertension Communication Survey is designed to collect information about patients' demographics and communication preferences related to hypertensive conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Select your gender from the available options in the "Gender" field.
4. If you have an occupation, enter it in the "Occupation" field. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
5. Choose your education level from the provided options in the "Education Level" field.
6. Enter your income in the "Income" field, if applicable.
7. Answer the questions about your relationship with a doctor, family members, friends, media, and other sources in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age** (number): Enter your age in this field.
* **Gender** (select_one): Select your gender from the available options:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Occupation** (text): If you have a job or occupation, enter it here. You can leave this field blank if you do not have an occupation.
* **Education Level** (select_multiple): Select your education level from the available options:
	+ No High school
	+ High school
	+ Some college
	+ College
	+ Post-Graduate Degree
* **Income** (number): If you want to share your income, enter it here. This field is optional.
* **Doctor** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your relationship with a doctor:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Family Member** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your relationship with family members:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Media** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your preference for getting medical information from media:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Friend** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your preference for getting medical information from friends:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Other Source** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your preference for getting medical information from other sources:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Internet** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your preference for getting medical information from the Internet:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Other** (select_multiple): Answer the question about your preference for getting medical information from other sources:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips

* Make sure to review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
* Take your time to answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance.
* The form is meant to be completed by individuals who are willing to participate and provide honest answers. If you do not wish to answer any question, you can skip it.
