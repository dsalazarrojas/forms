# skate_park_customer_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience at the skate parks. Your responses will help us understand your needs and make improvements to provide a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer.
* Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* Fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you visited this skate park before** (1, park_experience):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your previous visits.
* **How safe did you feel during your visit** (2, safety_feeling):
	+ Choose how safe you felt during your visit.
* **How clean were the restrooms and surrounding facilities** (3, facilities):
	+ Select how clean you found the facilities to be.
* **How helpful were the staff on duty** (4, staff_helpfulness):
	+ Choose how helpful you found the staff to be.
* **Were the amenities and services available what you needed** (5, amenities):
	+ Select if the amenities and services were available to you.
* **How satisfied are you with your visit** (6, overall_satisfaction):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your visit.
* **Any additional comments about your visit** (7, comments):
	+ Write any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Your contact information** (8, contact_details):
	+ Fill in your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **What is the name of this park** (9, park_name):
	+ If you know the name of the park, please let us know.
* **Date of visit** (10, park_visit_date):
	+ Select the date of your visit.
* **Name of staff member you interacted with** (11, park_staff_name):
	+ If you interacted with a staff member, please enter their name.
* **Email of staff member you interacted with** (12, park_staff_email):
	+ If you interacted with a staff member, please enter their email.
* **Phone number of staff member you interacted with** (13, park_staff_phone):
	+ If you interacted with a staff member, please enter their phone number.
* **How often do you visit this skate park** (14, skatepark_id):
	+ Select how often you visit the skate park.
* **Which location did you visit?** (15, sk8_park_location):
	+ Select all the locations you visited.
* **Age** (16, age):
	+ Select your age group.
