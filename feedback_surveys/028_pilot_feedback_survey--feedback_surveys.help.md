# Pilot Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from pilots on their experiences and perceptions related to flight operations. It aims to collect data on the quality of service, safety, and communication. The feedback will be used to identify areas of improvement for future flight operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For the **safety_perception** and **communication_quality** fields, select all relevant options that apply.
3. For the **flight_experience** field, enter the date of the flight you are providing feedback on.
4. Enter any additional feedback or comments in the **feedback** field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them in the **improvement_opportunities** field.
6. If you have any comments or notes, you can add them in the **submitted** field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number, if you provide them.
8. Finally, describe your experience in the training you received in the **training_experience** field, and select the quality of the training in the **training_quality** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **intro_text**: A brief text box to introduce your feedback.
- **pilot_name**: Your name as a pilot.
- **flight_experience**: The date of the flight you are providing feedback on.
- **safety_perception**: This field allows you to select all options that apply related to your perception of the safety of the flight. Select from:
  • Option 1: The flight was very safe.
  • Option 2: The flight was mostly safe.
  • Option 3: The flight was somewhat safe.
  • Option 4: The flight was not safe.
  • Option 5: The flight was very unsafe.
- **communication_quality**: This field allows you to select all options that apply related to the quality of communication during the flight. Select from:
  • Option 1: Communication was excellent.
  • Option 2: Communication was good.
  • Option 3: Communication was fair.
  • Option 4: Communication was poor.
- **flight_experience_2**: Not to be confused with the previous **flight_experience**, this field is only for entering the date of the flight you are providing feedback on, if different from the previous one.
- **feedback**: A text field to provide any additional feedback or comments.
- **improvement_opportunities**: A text field to suggest areas for improvement.
- **submitted**: A field for any comments or notes.
- **email**: Your email address.
- **phone_number**: Your phone number.
- **training_experience**: A text field to describe your experience in the training you received.
- **training_quality**: This field allows you to select the quality of the training you received. Select from:
  • Option 1: The training was excellent.
  • Option 2: The training was good.
  • Option 3: The training was fair.
  • Option 4: The training was poor.
- **communication_quality_2**: This field allows you to select the quality of communication during the training. Select from:
  • Option 1: Communication was excellent.
  • Option 2: Communication was good.
  • Option 3: Communication was fair.
  • Option 4: Communication was poor.

Note: This form is not a survey, but rather a feedback form. Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help us improve our service.
