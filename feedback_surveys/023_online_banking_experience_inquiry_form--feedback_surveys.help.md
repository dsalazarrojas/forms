# online_banking_experience_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from customers about their online banking experience. It helps us understand your satisfaction with our services, identify areas of improvement, and provide you with an opportunity to report any security concerns you may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Select the relevant options for each question, if applicable.
3.  Provide clear and concise comments in the text fields where required.
4.  Make sure to enter your contact information correctly, as this will enable us to get back to you for follow-up questions or surveys.
5.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your experience and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall satisfaction with our online banking experience?** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): This question allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with our online banking services. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **Was there ever a moment you felt that your security was compromised?** (`security_risk`, select_one, optional): This question helps us identify potential security concerns. If you select "Yes", please provide details in the field below.
* **If yes, what was it?** (`security_risk_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a description of the security incident you experienced.
* **On a scale of 1-5 how would you rate your satisfaction with online banking?** (`satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks for your overall satisfaction with our online banking services. Please rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **On a scale of 1-5 how would you rate our online security measures?** (`security_score`, number, optional): This question asks for your opinion on the effectiveness of our online security measures. Please rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **What could we do to improve online banking services?** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide suggestions on how we can improve our online banking services.
* **How can we contact you for follow-up questions or surveys?** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, optional): You can select one or more methods of contact (e.g., phone, email, mail) that we can use to reach out to you.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Additional comments or information?** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know.
* **How can you be contacted for any questions or concerns?** (`contact_us`, text, optional): Enter any preferred contact method(s) for any questions or concerns.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys?** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are open to be contacted for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **If yes, how often would you like to be contacted?** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, please choose a contact frequency that suits you (e.g., after every question, monthly, quarterly, annually).
