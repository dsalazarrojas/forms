# media-content-subscriber-opinion-poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and opinions from media content subscribers. It aims to gather information on their preferences, experience, and suggestions to improve the media content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, simply navigate to the media-content-subscriber-opinion-poll form.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding.
3. For fields with multiple options (e.g., "media content", "tag", "category"), select the most relevant one(s) that apply to you.
4. For fields without options (e.g., "feedback", "suggestions", "comments"), provide a brief and accurate response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### media content
*   **media content** (Select One/Many, required/optional): 
    Select the media content that you have experience with (e.g., TV show, movie, podcast, article). If no options apply, select "Other" and provide your own response.

### feedback
*   **feedback** (Text, optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments you have about the media content.

### rating
*   **rating** (Number, optional): 
    Rate your experience with the media content on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### preferences
*   **preferences** (Select One/Many, optional): 
    Indicate the media content preferences you have, such as "Yes" for "Yes, I like" or "No" for "No, I don't like".

### experience
*   **experience** (Text, optional): 
    Describe your experience with the media content, including any relevant details.

### suggestions
*   **suggestions** (Text, optional): 
    Provide any suggestions you have for improving the media content.

### contact info
*   **contact info** (Text, optional): 
    If you have any contact information you'd like to share, such as a phone number or website, please enter it here.

### email
*   **email** (Email, optional): 
    Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### comments
*   **comments** (Note, optional): 
    Provide any additional comments or notes about your feedback.

### date
*   **date** (Date, optional): 
    The date you filled out this form.

### time
*   **time** (Time, optional): 
    The time you filled out this form.

### category
*   **category** (Select One, optional): 
    Indicate the category of the media content you have experience with (e.g., "Yes" for "Yes, I like").

### tag
*   **tag** (Select One/Many, optional): 
    Select the tags you associate with the media content.

### Comments 2
*   **Comments 2** (Text, optional): 
    Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the media content.

### Email Opt
*   **Email Opt** (Email, optional): 
    Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### phone_opt
*   **phone** (Text, optional): 
    Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### website
*   **website** (Text, optional): 
    Enter your website if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### Category Opt
*   **Category Opt** (Select One, optional): 
    Indicate the category of the media content you have experience with (e.g., "Yes" for "Yes, I like").

### Tag Opt
*   **Tag Opt** (Select One/Many, optional): 
    Select the tags you associate with the media content.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions that apply to you.
*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible in your responses.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
