# Organizational Growth Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from employees on their perception of the organization's growth and development. It is not intended to be a formal evaluation or performance review, but rather a tool to help us understand your thoughts and feelings about your work environment, colleagues, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and make sure you understand the questions before answering.
2. Answer each question in the space provided.
3. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as "required".
4. If you have any trouble completing any part of the form, don't hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey. Please take a moment to read through it before proceeding.
* **Name**: This field is where you should enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge or other official records.
* **Department**: Please select the department in which you work (Sales, Marketing, Finance, etc.).
* **Role**: Describe your role within the organization (e.g., Manager, Engineer, etc.).
* **Feedback**: If you have any general feedback or comments about your work experience, please enter them here. If you have already provided feedback, you can skip this question.
* **Rating**: Please rate your experience with our organization on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied". 
*   **Comment**: If you have any specific feedback or comments about your experience, please enter them here. If you have already provided feedback, you can skip this question.
*   **Email**: Please enter your official email address associated with your employee ID.
*   **Phone**: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your office phone number.

# Tips
* Please take a few minutes to think carefully about each question before answering.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* You can skip questions that are not marked as "required" if you are unsure or do not have the information.
* You can take a break if you need to - the survey is meant to be completed in a way that feels comfortable for you.
