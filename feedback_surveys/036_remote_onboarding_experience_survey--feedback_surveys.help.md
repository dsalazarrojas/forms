# Remote Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the remote onboarding experience of new hires.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
3. Provide detailed comments for any open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Onboarding Experience**: Enter the number that represents your onboarding experience. For example, 5 out of 5.
* **2. New Hire ID**: Enter your new hire ID, which was provided to you by your HR representative.
*   **3. Department**: Check all the departments you were part of during your onboarding. You can select multiple options.
*   **4. Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager, as it appears in your email or company directory.
*   **5. Onboarding Start Date**: Enter the date when your onboarding started. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **6. Onboarding Start Time**: Enter the time when your onboarding started. Format: HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **7. Onboarding End Date**: Enter the date when your onboarding ended. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **8. Onboarding End Time**: Enter the time when your onboarding ended. Format: HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **9. Onboarding Location**: Enter the location where you completed your onboarding.
*   **10. Onboarding Platform**: Enter the platform used for onboarding (e.g., email, phone, video conference).
*   **11. Onboarding Process**: Describe your onboarding experience in a few words.
*   **12. Onboarding Satisfaction**: Rate your onboarding satisfaction. Use a scale of 1-5.
*   **13. Onboarding Support**: Describe the support you received during your onboarding.
*   **14. Onboarding Feedback**: Share any feedback or concerns you have about your onboarding experience.
*   **15. Onboarding Recommendation**: Share any recommendations you have for improving the onboarding process.
*   **16. Onboarding Did It Work**: Describe whether the onboarding process worked for you.
*   **17. Onboarding Improve**: Describe how the onboarding process could be improved.
*   **18. Onboarding What Didn't Work**: Describe what didn't work during your onboarding experience.
*   **19. Onboarding Suggestion**: Share any suggestions you have for the onboarding process.
*   **20. Onboarding Other Comments**: Share any other comments about your onboarding experience.
*   **21. Manager Rating**: Rate your manager's support and guidance during the onboarding process. Select one of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ N/A
*   **22. Manager Comment**: Enter a comment about your manager's support and guidance during the onboarding process.
 

Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
