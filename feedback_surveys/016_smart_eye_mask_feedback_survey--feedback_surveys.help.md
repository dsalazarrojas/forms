# smart_eye_mask_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback survey to gather information from users on our product's performance, user experience, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by reading the "Welcome to the Survey" page, which provides a brief introduction to the form.
2. On the next page, rate our product by selecting the corresponding rating option for "How satisfied are you with our product?"
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "What do you think we could improve?" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field. This will be verified for accuracy.
5. Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field. This will be verified for correctness.
6. If you have any other comments or questions, type them in the "Do you have any other comments?" field.
7. If you want to provide additional information, you can fill out the "What is your age?", "What is your gender?", "What is your occupation?", "When did you first start using our product?", "What time do you prefer to use our product?" fields.
8. Verify the correctness of your email address, phone number, date of birth, preferred time, and correct gender in the corresponding fields.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

* **Welcome to the Survey**: This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **Rate our product** (product_rating): Rate our product by selecting one of the options provided.
* **What do you think we could improve?** (product_suggestions): Enter any suggestions or comments on how we can improve.
* **How satisfied are you with our product?** (satisfaction): Select the correct rating that best reflects your satisfaction level.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (contact_us): Enter any other comments or questions you may have.
* **What is your email address?** (email): Enter your email address. This will be verified for accuracy.
* **What is your phone number?** (phone): Enter your phone number. This will be verified for correctness.
* **What is your age?** (demographics): Enter your age.
* **What is your gender?** (gender): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **What is your occupation?** (occupation): Select your occupation from the provided options.
* **When did you first start using our product?** (date): Enter the date when you first started using our product.
* **What time do you prefer to use our product?** (time): Enter the time you prefer to use our product.
* **Is this your email address?** (email_confirmation): Verify the correctness of your email address.
* **Is this your phone number?** (phone_confirmation): Verify the correctness of your phone number.
* **Is this a valid email address?** (email_validation): Verify that the email address you provided matches the one we have on file.
* **Is this a valid phone number?** (phone_validation): Verify that the phone number you provided matches the one we have on file.
* **Is this a valid time?** (time_validation): Verify that the time you entered is correct.
* **Is this a valid date?** (date_validation): Verify that the date you entered is correct.
* **Is this your correct gender?** (gender_confirmation): Verify the correctness of your gender.
