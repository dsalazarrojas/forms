# Building Maintenance Scheduling Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the building maintenance scheduling process. Your input will help us improve the scheduling process and provide better services to you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you are completing this form in the "Survey Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the property or building you are providing feedback on in the "Property or Building Name" field.
3. Provide your specific unit or floor number in the "Unit or Floor Number" field (if applicable).
4. If you have recently received maintenance service, provide the date of the maintenance request in the "Date of Recent Maintenance Request" field.
5. For each question, select the response that best fits your experience:
	* "How Easy Was It to Schedule the Appointment" - rate your experience on a scale of Very Easy to Very Difficult.
	* "Was the Appointment Timing Convenient" - rate your experience on a scale of Very Convenient to Very Inconvenient.
	* "Were Available Time Slots Adequate" - rate your experience on a scale of Excellent to None.
	* "Preferred Scheduling Method" - select how you prefer to schedule appointments.
	* "Adequate Advance Notice Provided" - rate your experience on a scale of More than 2 weeks to Less than 1 day.
	* "Effectiveness of Appointment Reminders" - rate your experience on a scale of Very Effective to No reminders provided.
	* "Ability to Reschedule If Needed" - rate your experience on a scale of Very Flexible to Not Possible.
	* "Communication Quality About Appointment" - rate your experience on a scale of Excellent to Poor.
	* "Coordination With Tenant or Occupant Needs" - rate your experience on a scale of Excellent to Poor.
	* "Clear Documentation of Appointment" - rate your experience on a scale of Very Clear to Not Provided.
6. If you have suggestions for improvement, you can provide them in the "How Can We Improve Scheduling" field.
7. Finally, rate your overall satisfaction with the scheduling process on a scale of Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
