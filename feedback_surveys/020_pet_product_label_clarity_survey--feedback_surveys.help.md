# Pet Product Label Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on the clarity of product labels for pet products. The purpose of this survey is to understand how well our customers can easily identify and understand the information presented on our product labels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by selecting the first page.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Answer each question as accurately as possible.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Product Name** (`form_first_page_product_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the pet product you are providing feedback on.
* **Pet Product Category** (`form_first_page_product_category`, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field asks whether the pet product category is clear on the label.
* **Product Description** (`form_first_page_product_description`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of the pet product you are commenting on.
* **Label Effectiveness** (`form_first_page_label_effectiveness`, select one, optional):
	+ This field asks how clear the label is for the product.
* **Label Clarity** (`form_first_page_label_clarity`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide specific feedback about what you think makes the label clear or unclear.
* **Label Compliance** (`form_first_page_label_compliance`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide feedback about whether the label complies with your expectations.
* **Next Steps** (`form_first_page_next_steps`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any additional suggestions or ideas on how to improve the label's clarity, enter them here.
* **Pet Label Clarity** (`form_first_page`, text, optional):

* **Yes/No** (`form_first_page_product_category`, select multiple, optional):

* **Very Clear/Somewhat Clear/Not Clear** (`form_first_page_label_effectiveness`, select one, optional):
	+ This field asks how clear the label is for the product.
	+ Select either "Very Clear", "Somewhat Clear" or "Not Clear" to indicate your answer.
