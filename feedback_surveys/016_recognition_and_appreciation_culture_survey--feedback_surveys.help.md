# Recognition And Appreciation Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and insights from employees about their recognition and appreciation culture within the organization. This survey is designed to help management understand the level of recognition and appreciation felt by employees, identify areas of improvement, and provide a platform for open communication and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Scroll through each page and fill out the relevant questions.
* For questions with a select_one or select_multiple type, choose one of the provided options that best match your experience.
* For questions with a select_one type, you can only choose one option from the list.
* For questions with a select_multiple type, choose as many options as apply to you.
* For questions with a text type, type in your answer in the provided text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you feel recognized at work?**: This question asks about how often you feel that your work and efforts are recognized and appreciated by others in the organization.
* **How often do you feel appreciated at work?**: This question inquires about how often you feel that your work and contributions are valued and appreciated by your colleagues and superiors.
* **How often do you feel your work is fairly paid?**: This question asks about how often you think your compensation is fair and reflects the value of your work.
* **What is the main reason you feel underpaid/undervalued?**: This question is asking you to select all the reasons why you feel underpaid or undervalued, such as lack of appreciation from manager or lack of opportunities for growth.
* **Suggest an idea to improve our appreciation culture**: This question asks for any suggestions or ideas you have on how the organization can improve the recognition and appreciation culture.
* **How often do you feel that your work is valued at work?**: This question inquires about how often you feel that your work and contributions are valued by others in the organization.
* **Additional feedback**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the recognition and appreciation culture within the organization.
* **Rating for Recognition Culture**: This question asks you to rate the level of recognition and appreciation culture within the organization, with options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rating for Appreciation Culture**: This question asks you to rate the level of appreciation and gratitude within the organization, with options of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How often do you feel that your contributions are valued?**: This question inquires about how often you feel that your contributions and ideas are valued and recognized by others in the organization.

Note: All answers will be reviewed internally to gather insights and improve the work environment.
