# Nonprofit Social Impact Reporting Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your reporting practices and experiences. Please answer the questions honestly, and let us know if you have any questions or concerns.
## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each question to access its respective options.
- Select the options that best fit your answers.
- You can choose more than one option for multiple-choice questions.
- Enter your email address and/or phone number if you're interested in providing feedback on this form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you use reporting methods?** (reporting_methods_1)
	+ This question asks about your frequency of using reporting methods, such as daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, bi-annually, or annually.
* **At which level do you report your impact?** (reporting_frequency_2)
	+ This question asks whether you report impact at the grantor, grantee, or both levels.
* **Which reporting format is used?** (reporting_format_3)
	+ This question asks about the format used for reporting, such as financial, narrative, data, or other.
* **What methods do you use to measure impact?** (impact_measurement_4)
	+ This question is about the methods used to measure impact, like logical framework, outcome mapping, theory of change, balanced scorecard, or other.
* **How often do you report your impact?** (reporting_frequency_5)
	+ This question is a repeat of the first one, asking about the frequency of reporting impact.
* **What common challenges do you face when reporting?** (challenges_6)
	+ This question lists some common challenges, such as lack of data, inadequate resources, insufficient time, or other.
* **What support do you need?** (support_needs_7)
	+ This question asks about the types of support you need, like financial, logistical, technical, or other.
* **Are you interested in providing feedback on our form?** (form_submission_8)
	+ This question offers an opportunity to provide feedback on the form.
* **Email** (email_9)
	+ This question asks for your email address if you're interested in providing feedback or future correspondence.
* **Phone** (phone_10)
	+ This question asks for your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
Note: This guide is a basic explanation of the form's content. Please let us know if you have any further questions or concerns.
