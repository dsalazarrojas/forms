# Fun Group Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fun Group Dynamics Survey is designed to gather information about a group's collaboration, communication, and leadership dynamics. This survey will help us better understand how well your group works together, communicates with each other, and functions as a team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best describes your group's dynamics.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_does_your_group_collaborate**: Describe how your group collaborates on tasks and projects. This will help us understand how well your group works together. (Text field)
* **how_effective_is_your_groups_communication**: Rate how effectively your group communicates with each other. Choose from the following options: 
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Fair
	+ Very fair (Select one)
* **how_well_do_your_group_members_respect_each_other**: Select all the ways in which your group members respect each other. Choose from the following options:
	+ Always
	+ Mostly
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never (Select multiple)
* **how_often_do_you_collaborate_as_a_group**: Describe how often you collaborate as a group. This can be daily, weekly, or less frequently. (Number field)
* **collaboration_frequency_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe how often you collaborate as a group.
* **how_frequently_do_you_communicate_with_your_group**: Describe how frequently you communicate with your group. (Text field)
* **communication_frequency_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe how frequently you communicate with your group.
* **how_fun_do_you_find_your_group**: Rate how fun you find your group to be. Choose from the following options:
	+ Very funny
	+ Somewhat funny
	+ Not fun (Select one)
* **fun_level_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please rate how fun you find your group to be.
* **how_many_people_in_your_group**: Describe how many people are in your group. (Number field)
* **team_size_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe how many people are in your group.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_group**: Describe how you feel about your group. (Text field)
* **group_dynamics_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe how you feel about your group.
* **how_do_you_choose_your_group_members**: Select how you choose your group members. Choose from the following options:
	+ By random
	+ By skill
	+ By interest (Select one)
* **collaboration_type_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please select how you choose your group members.
* **how_do_you_communicate_with_your_group**: Rate how openly you communicate with your group. Choose from the following options:
	+ Very open
	+ Somewhat open
	+ Not open (Select one)
* **communication_level_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please rate how openly you communicate with your group.
* **what_s_is_the_name_of_your_group**: Describe the name of your group. (Text field)
* **group_name_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe the name of your group.
* **what_s_the_name_of_the_team_leader**: Describe the name of your team's leader. (Text field)
* **team_leader_hint**: This is a hint to help you answer the previous question. Please describe the name of your team's leader.

## Tips

* Be truthful and accurate in your answers.
* Answer all questions thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
