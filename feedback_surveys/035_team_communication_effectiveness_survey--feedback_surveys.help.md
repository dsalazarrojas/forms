# Team Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Communication Effectiveness Survey is designed to collect feedback on how well the team is communicating with each other and external stakeholders. This form will help identify areas of strength and weakness in the team's communication strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction page.
2. Answer the questions by selecting the relevant options.
3. Provide any additional comments in the comments field.
4. Enter a numerical rating to evaluate the team's overall performance.
5. Enter your email address for contact information.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction**
* (`intro_page`, note, required): This page provides background information and context for the survey. Please read through this page before proceeding.

### **Questions**
* (`questions_page`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that best describe your experiences with team communication. You can choose one or multiple options.

### **Answers**
* (`answers_page`, text, required): Write your thoughts and answers to the questions asked on the previous page.

### **Comments**
* (`comments_page`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the team communication.

### **Rating**
* (`rating_page`, select_one, required): Rate the team's communication effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being not effective and 5 being very effective.

### **Team Member**
* (`team_member_page`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a team member or not.

### **Team Member List**
* (`team_member_list_page`, select_multiple, required): Select all the team members you have interacted with during the survey period.

### **Email**
* (`email_page`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### **Phone**
* (`phone_page`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### **Rating Score**
* (`rating_score_page`, number, required): Enter a numerical score to evaluate the team's performance, with 0 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### **Overall Rating**
* (`overall_rating_page`, number, required): Enter a numerical score to evaluate the team's overall performance, with 0 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### **Submission**
* (`submission_page`, select_one, required): Select whether you have submitted your answers or not.
