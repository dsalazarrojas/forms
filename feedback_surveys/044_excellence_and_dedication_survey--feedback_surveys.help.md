# excellence_and_dedication_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their experiences, strengths, and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Survey Start** page, which is simply a confirmation page to begin the survey.
2. On the **Employee Name** page, enter your employee name to provide your identity.
3. On the **Employee Title** page, enter your job title or position.
4. On the **Department** page, enter your department or team you belong to.
5. On the **Manager Name** page, enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
6. On the **Team Leader Name** page, enter the name of your team leader.
7. On the **Team Leader Contact** page, enter the contact information of your team leader.
8. On the **How Do You Know Me** page, choose how you know the person filling out the form from the options provided.
9. On the **What Is Your Role** page, choose your role within the organization from the options provided.
10. On the **How Long Have You Worked Here** page, choose how long you have worked at the organization from the options provided.
11. On the **What Do You Like** page, choose what you like about your experience at the organization from the options provided.
12. On the **What Do You Dislike** page, choose what you dislike about your experience at the organization from the options provided.
13. On the **Why Did You Leave** page, choose why you left the organization from the options provided. (Note: This question is only required if you are no longer employed by the organization.)
14. On the **Feedback** page, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience at the organization.
15. Finally, on the **Survey End** page, confirm that you have completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as an employee of the organization.
* **Employee Title**: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team you belong to within the organization.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Team Leader Name**: Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Team Leader Contact**: Enter the contact information of your team leader.
* **How Do You Know Me**: Choose how you know the person filling out the form from the options provided. (e.g., through work, social media, etc.)
* **What Is Your Role**: Choose your role within the organization from the options provided.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here**: Choose how long you have worked at the organization from the options provided.
* **What Do You Like**: Choose what you like about your experience at the organization from the options provided.
* **What Do You Dislike**: Choose what you dislike about your experience at the organization from the options provided.
* **Why Did You Leave**: Choose why you left the organization from the options provided. (Note: This question is only required if you are no longer employed by the organization.)
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience at the organization.
* **Survey End**: Confirm that you have completed the survey.

## Tips

* Ensure you have permission from your manager or supervisor before completing this form.
* Be honest and provide accurate information to help the organization understand your experiences.
* If you are no longer employed by the organization, please answer the questions accordingly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the **Feedback** field.
