# Watched Content Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Watched Content Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback on content that has been watched. This form helps us understand your thoughts and opinions on the content, allowing us to improve and make it more valuable for you and others in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content you watched (Video, Webinar, or Other).
2. If you have any thoughts or comments about the content you watched, type them in the "Rating Comment" field.
3. If you'd like, you can also rate the content by entering a number in the "Video Rating" field.
4. If you'd like to provide any additional feedback, you can type it in the "Feedback" field.
5. Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a copy of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Rating (video_rating)** (`<number>`, optional): Rate the content with a number from 1-5.
* **Rating Comment (rating_comment)** (`<text>`, optional): Provide any comments or thoughts about the content you watched.
* **Watched Content Type (watched_content_type)** (`<select>`, required): Select the type of content you watched (Video, Webinar, or Other).
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`<text>`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the content.
* **Email (email)** (`<email>`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a copy of your submission.
* **Phone (phone)** (`<text>`, optional): (This field is not currently used. Please do not enter any information here.)

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
