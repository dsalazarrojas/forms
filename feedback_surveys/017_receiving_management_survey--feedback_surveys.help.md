# Receiving Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Management Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from individuals on their experiences with receiving management practices within your organization. This form is designed to gather information on the quality of service, performance metrics, and contact information to help identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions. Please fill out this form to provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to your experience with receiving management practices within your organization.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any concerns or comments, use the "What do you think could be improved?" field to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Receiving Management Survey (Page 1)
* **Receiving Management Survey** (`text`, required/optional): This is the main survey question, where you can provide your overall experience with receiving management practices. Please answer this question in a few sentences.

* **What do you think could be improved?** (`note`, required/optional): If you have any concerns or suggestions for improvement, please use this field to provide more detailed feedback. There is no character limit, so feel free to share as much as you need.

### Performance Metrics (Page 2)
* **Metrics** (`number`, optional): This field is for you to input any numbers related to your performance metrics. Please enter a specific number.

* **Metrics** (`number`, optional): This is another field for you to input numbers related to your performance metrics. Again, please enter a specific number.

* **Metrics** (`select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options provided to rate your performance metrics.

### Date and Time (Page 3)
* **Date and Time** (`date`, optional): Input the date and time of your experience with receiving management practices.

### Time of Day (Page 4)
* **Time of Day** (`time`, optional): Select the time of day when you received the management.

### Contact Info (Page 5)
* **Contact Info** (`note`, optional): If you have any contact information you'd like to share, such as a phone number or email address, use this field.

* **Email** (`email`, optional): If you have an email address you'd like to share, enter it here.

* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it here.

* **Contact Info** (`note`, optional): This field is a second opportunity to provide any additional contact information or comments.
