# Wireless Earbud Noise Issue Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on issues related to wireless earbuds, specifically noise issues. This form is intended to gather information about the model of earbuds used, the level of noise experienced, and any problems or suggestions related to the earbuds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the model of your wireless earbuds from the list provided.
2. Indicate the level of noise you experienced with the earbuds.
3. Choose the problems you are experiencing with the earbuds.
4. If you are willing, provide a specific time when you experienced the issue.
5. Enter any comments or suggestions related to the earbuds.
6. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further follow-up).
7. Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wireless Earbud Model** (Required): This field asks you to select the model of your wireless earbuds from the provided list. This information will help us identify specific issues with certain models.
* **Wireless Earbud Noise Level** (Optional): This field asks you to rate the level of noise you experienced with your earbuds. You can select one of the options provided.
* **Wireless Earbud Noise Problem** (Required): This field asks you to choose the problems you are experiencing with your earbuds. Select all that apply.
* **Wireless Earbud Use Time** (Optional): This field asks you to provide a specific time when you experienced the issue. This information will help us understand when the issue occurs.
* **Wireless Earbud Comments** (Optional): This field allows you to enter any additional comments or suggestions related to your earbuds.
* **Wireless Earbud Suggestion** (Optional): This field allows you to enter any specific suggestions you may have for improving the earbuds.
* **Wireless Earbud Email** (Optional): This field asks you to enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for further follow-up.
* **Wireless Earbud Phone** (Optional): This field asks you to enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted for further follow-up.
