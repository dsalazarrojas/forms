# Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Feedback Survey is designed to collect user feedback about a specific event. Your responses will help us understand the event's success and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the event you are providing feedback about from the event list.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Id** (`event_id`, number, required): This field is not currently used for anything in this example, it appears to be a placeholder. Please leave it blank.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback about. This will help us identify which event your feedback is about.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the event. This is your chance to tell us what worked well, what didn't, and what we could improve on.

## Tips
* Please be specific and concise in your feedback.
* If you have any supporting evidence (e.g. photos, videos), feel free to attach them to your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, we'd love to hear them!

Note: The "Event Id" field is not currently used, so you can leave it blank.
