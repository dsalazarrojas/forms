# Gym Member Community Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and engagement data to help us improve your overall gym experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the events that interest you from the list.
2. Choose your favorite activity among the options provided.
3. Select your preferred communication method(s) for important updates and news.
4. Rate the quality of our events on a scale of 1-10.
5. Rate the quality of our communication methods on a scale of 1-10.
6. Choose which programs you are interested in learning more about.
7. Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
8. Enter your member ID (if you have one).
9. Enter your email address (if you have one).
10. Enter your phone number (if you have one).
11. Choose how you would like to receive event reminders.
12. Select whether you want event reminders or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Interest**: This field allows you to select all the events that interest you. Please choose one or multiple events that you are interested in attending. For example, cardio, weights, or classes.
* **Favorite Activity**: This field asks you to select your favorite activity. Choose the one that you enjoy doing the most.
* **Communication Preference**: This field allows you to select your preferred method(s) of communication. You can choose one or multiple methods, such as email, text, phone, or in-app notifications.
* **Feedback on Events**: This field asks you to rate the quality of our events on a scale of 1-10. Rate your experience with our events, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Feedback on Communication**: This field asks you to rate the quality of our communication methods on a scale of 1-10. Rate your experience with our communication methods, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Program Interest**: This field allows you to select which programs you are interested in learning more about. Choose one or multiple programs that interest you.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have for us. You can use this space to share your thoughts on how we can improve your gym experience.
* **Member ID**: This field is for your unique member ID. If you have one, please enter it.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. If you have an email address associated with your account, please enter it.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. If you have a phone number associated with your account, please enter it.
* **In App**: This field allows you to select which methods you would like to receive notifications through our app.
* **Event Reminders**: This field asks you to select whether you want to receive event reminders or not. You can choose from three options: True, False, or not preferred.

Tips:

* Be honest in your ratings and responses, as this will help us make informed decisions to improve your gym experience.
* If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please use the comments field to share them.
* Make sure to choose your preferred communication methods to receive important updates and news.
