# Call Lights And Floor Mats Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Call Lights And Floor Mats Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from hospital staff regarding the functionality, reliability, and maintenance of call lights and floor mats. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and ensure patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Your Name**: Please enter your name for identification purposes.
2.  **Department or Unit**: Select your department or unit from the provided options.
3.  **Years Employed at Facility**: Select the number of years you have been employed at the facility.
4.  **Call Lights Assessment**: 
    *   **Call lights functionality**: Rate the functionality of call lights as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Non-functional.
    *   **Call lights are consistently reliable**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that call lights are reliable.
    *   **Response time to call lights is adequate**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that the response time to call lights is adequate.
    *   **How often do call lights malfunction**: Select how often call lights malfunction.
    *   **Describe any call light issues you experience**: Provide a description of any issues you experience with call lights.
5.  **Floor Mats Assessment**: 
    *   **Floor mats effectively prevent slips and falls**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that floor mats prevent slips and falls.
    *   **Overall condition of floor mats**: Rate the overall condition of floor mats as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Replace Needed.
    *   **Floor mats are strategically placed**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that floor mats are strategically placed.
    *   **Floor mats are kept clean and maintained**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that floor mats are kept clean and maintained.
    *   **Describe any floor mat hazards or concerns**: Provide a description of any hazards or concerns you experience with floor mats.
6.  **Overall Safety Feedback**: 
    *   **Safety equipment is adequate for patient care**: Rate your agreement or disagreement with the statement that safety equipment is adequate for patient care.
    *   **Suggestions for improving safety equipment**: Provide suggestions for improving safety equipment.
