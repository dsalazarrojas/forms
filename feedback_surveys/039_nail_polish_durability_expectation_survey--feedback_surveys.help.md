# Nail Polish Durability Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect your expectations and experiences regarding nail polish durability. Your feedback will help us improve our product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the provided fields according to your preferences and experiences. You may skip any questions that are not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Polish Color (Polish Color, select_one, required: false)**: Select the color of the nail polish you are evaluating. This will help us understand your color preferences.
* **How long do you wear your nail polish? (How long do you wear your nail polish?, number, required: false)**: Indicate how long you typically wear your nail polish. This will help us understand your usage habits.
* **Quality of the nail polish (Quality of the nail polish, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose one or more of the provided options to describe your experience with the quality of the nail polish. This will help us gauge your satisfaction with the polish's quality.
* **How durable do you feel the polish is? (How durable do you feel the polish is?, select_one, required: false)**: Select how durable you feel the polish is, based on your experience. This will help us understand the expectations we need to meet for our product.
* **How does the polish wear on your nails? (How does the polish wear on your nails?, text, required: false)**: Describe how the polish wears on your nails. This will provide us with more detailed insights into your experiences with the polish.
* **Additional comments (Additional comments, note, required: false)**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the polish, please share them here. This will help us better understand your thoughts and expectations.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as accurately as possible based on your actual experiences.
* You may skip any questions that do not apply to you.
* Your participation is greatly valued, and your feedback will help us improve our product offerings.
