# Satisfaction Survey For Eco Friendly Products - Help Guide

## Purpose
This satisfaction survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with our eco-friendly products. It will help us understand how satisfied our customers are with the quality, price, and availability of our products, as well as their overall perception of our customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this satisfaction survey, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the questions and selecting the best responses that apply to your experience.
2. For multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best describes your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, type your answer in the provided text field.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, but don't worry if some questions don't apply to your experience.
5. When you're finished, submit the survey to help us improve our services.
