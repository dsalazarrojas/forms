> **Audit Scheduling Experience Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with scheduling audits. Your input will help us improve our processes and provide better services to our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the survey completion date to indicate when you completed this survey.
2. Enter your full name as the respondent.
3. Enter the name of your organization.
4. Choose the date when your audit was scheduled.
5. Rate the ease of scheduling the audit with our team.
6. Choose the method used to schedule the audit.
7. Rate the quality of communication about scheduling.
8. Rate the responsiveness of the audit team.
9. Rate the flexibility of the audit team.
10. Rate the adequacy of the audit timeline.
11. Indicate the number of days advance notice given for the audit.
12. Rate the clarity of instructions provided about the audit.
13. Rate the clarity of document requirements.
14. Rate the quality of confirmation details received.
15. Choose whether a single point of contact was available.
16. Rate your overall satisfaction with the scheduling process.
17. Suggest areas for improvement in the scheduling process.
18. Indicate willingness to schedule another audit with our team.
19. Recommend our audit team to others.
20. Enter your email address for any follow-up questions.
