# Public Awareness Of Preventive Medicine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Awareness of Preventive Medicine Survey is a form designed to collect information on public awareness and feedback on preventive medicine practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Medical History**: Select the correct medical history that best describes your situation.
2.  **Doctor's Recommendation**: Select all the doctor's recommendations that apply to you.
3.  **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information to allow us to follow up with you.
4.  **Medical Issues**: Confirm if you have any medical issues.
5.  **Preventive Medicine Knowledge**: Confirm your knowledge level on preventive medicine.
6.  **Community Knowledge**: Select all the areas of community knowledge that apply to you.
7.  **Submit Feedback**: Enter your feedback on the survey.
8.  **Contact Info (Again)**: Enter your contact information to ensure we can follow up with you.
9.  **Submit Form**: Click to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your experience with preventive medicine.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions you have about preventive medicine.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required): Select the medical history that best describes your situation.
	+ Option 1: This option describes your medical history.
	+ Option 2: This option describes your medical history.
* **Doctor’s Recommendation** (`doctor_recommendation`, select_multiple, required): Select all the doctor's recommendations that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: This option describes a doctor’s recommendation.
	+ Option 2: This option describes a doctor’s recommendation.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Select the contact information you prefer to be reached with.
	+ Option 1: This option describes a preferred contact method.
	+ Option 2: This option describes a preferred contact method.
* **Medical Issues** (`medical_issues`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have any medical issues.
	+ ‘Yes’: You have medical issues.
	+ ‘No’: You don’t have medical issues.
* **Preventive Medicine Knowledge** (`preventive_medicine_knowledge`, select_one, required): Confirm your knowledge level on preventive medicine.
	+ ‘Yes’: You have knowledge on preventive medicine.
	+ ‘No’: You don’t have knowledge on preventive medicine.
* **Community Knowledge** (`community_knowledg`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of community knowledge that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: This option describes a community knowledge area.
	+ Option 2: This option describes a community knowledge area.
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, email, required): Enter your email address to submit your feedback.
* **Contact Info (Again)** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information to ensure we can follow up with you.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Click to submit your form.
