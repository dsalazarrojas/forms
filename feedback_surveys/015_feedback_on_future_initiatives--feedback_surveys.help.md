# Feedback On Future Initiatives - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on future initiatives from respondents. It's used to gather information on proposed programs, compare priorities, and suggest services or community projects. This form helps to identify the best courses of action for future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Proposed Programs**: Select the programs you have in mind for the future. You can choose one or multiple programs that you think would be beneficial.
2. **Compare Priorities**: Rank your priorities for the selected programs. This will help us understand what's most important to you.
3. **Roadmap Planning**: Choose the roadmap planning options that best fit your needs.
4. **Services**: Select the services that you think would be helpful for the future initiatives.
5. **Community Projects**: Suggest community projects that you think would be beneficial.
6. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Proposed Programs (Required)
 * **Proposed Programs** (`proposed_programs`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or multiple programs you have in mind for the future. This will help us understand your thoughts on what should be included in future initiatives.

### Compare Priorities (Required)
 * **Compare Priorities** (`compare_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required): Rank your priorities for the selected programs. This will help us understand what's most important to you.

### Roadmap Planning (Required)
 * **Roadmap Planning** (`roadmap_planning`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the roadmap planning options that best fit your needs. This will help us understand how to plan for future initiatives.

### Services (Optional)
 * **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services that you think would be helpful for future initiatives. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any suggestions.

### Community Projects (Optional)
 * **Community Projects** (`community_projects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Suggest community projects that you think would be beneficial. This is also an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any suggestions.

### Additional Comments (Optional)
 * **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have any comments.
