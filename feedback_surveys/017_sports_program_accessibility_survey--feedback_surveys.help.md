# Sports Program Accessibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Program Accessibility Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback and insights from users about the accessibility of our sports programs. Your input will help us understand how we can improve the accessibility of our sports programs and provide better services to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you have understood the questions.
2. Fill in all the required fields (if any) with accurate and clear information.
3. Select all applicable options for each question (if any).
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to add them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Sports Program Accessibility Survey (Introduction)
* **Sports Program Accessibility Survey** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is an introduction to the form, please provide a brief description about your experience with our sports programs.

### 2. Program Name
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the sports program you are referring to.

### 3. How do you feel about the accessibility of our facilities?
* **How do you feel about the accessibility of our facilities?** (`facility_access`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Very accessible
	+ Option 2: Somewhat accessible
	+ Option 3: Not very accessible

### 4. What experiences have you had in our sports programs?
* **What experiences have you had in our sports programs?** (`experiences`, `text`, required: false): Describe any experiences you have had with our sports programs, including any positive or negative experiences.

### 5. Additional Comments
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about our sports programs, please feel free to add them here.

### 6. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if you provide it, we may contact you to follow up on your feedback).

### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you provide it, we may contact you to follow up on your feedback).
