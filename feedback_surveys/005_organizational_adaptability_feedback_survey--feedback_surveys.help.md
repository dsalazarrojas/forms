# organizational_adaptability_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the organizational adaptability of your company, gathering feedback on how employees perceive the company's ability to adjust to changes, challenges, and uncertainties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond to the best of your ability.
2. For text fields, provide a short description or answer in the space provided.
3. If you're unsure or need more information, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative for clarification.
