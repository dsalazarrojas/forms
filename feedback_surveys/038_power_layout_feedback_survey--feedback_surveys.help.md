# Power Layout Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Layout Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the layout of a product or service. This form aims to understand how users perceive the layout, its usability, and their overall satisfaction. The survey helps the product team to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing your honest feedback.
2. Rate the layout on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Not at all important" and 5 means "Very important".
3. Provide suggestions for improvements, if any.
4. Answer questions about your usage frequency and habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Layout Rated** (1-5, required): Rate how important is the layout to you.
* **Usability Rated** (1-5, required): Rate how user-friendly the layout is to you.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (text, optional): Share your ideas for improving the layout.
* **Used Layout** (1-5, optional): How often do you use the layout.
* **Layout Liked** (1-5, optional): Rate how much you like the current layout.
* **Layout Liked Most** (1-5, optional): Rate how much you like the layout among the options available.
* **Layout Liked Least** (1-5, optional): Rate how much you like the least among the options available.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (1-5, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the layout.
* **Why Do You Use The Layout?** (text, optional): Explain why you use the layout.
* **How Often Do You Use The Layout?** (1-5, optional): Rate how often you use the layout.
* **How Important Is The Layout For You?** (1-5, optional): Rate how important the layout is to you.
* **Most Important Feature Of The Layout** (text, optional): Choose the most important feature of the layout.
* **Most Important Feature Of The Layout** (text, optional): Choose the most important feature of the layout among the options.
* **Least Important Feature Of The Layout** (text, optional): Choose the least important feature of the layout among the options.
* **None Of The Above** (text, optional): If none of the above options are correct.
* **Used Layout** (1-5, optional): Rate how often you use the layout.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing detailed and honest answers will help the product team create a better user experience for you and others.
