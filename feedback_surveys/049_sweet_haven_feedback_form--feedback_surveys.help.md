# Sweet Haven Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sweet Haven Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience at Sweet Haven. This form helps Sweet Haven understand customer needs, preferences, and suggestions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is a required field to help Sweet Haven know who's giving feedback.
2.  Provide your **Email** in the second field. This will also be required for Sweet Haven to contact you in case they need more information.
3.  Choose one of the following **Feedback Types**: **Sweet**, **Savory**, or **Salty**. This will guide Sweet Haven's staff on how to categorize and process your feedback.
4.  Rate your **Rating** on a scale. This will help Sweet Haven assess their services.
5.  Enter any **Comment** you may have about your experience. This is optional but helps Sweet Haven understand your thoughts.
6.  Select a **Date and Time** for the feedback submission. This will help Sweet Haven track the timing of your feedback.
7.  Provide your **Phone** number. This is a required field for Sweet Haven to contact you if needed.
8.  Choose whether you have any **Email** for Sweet Haven staff to contact you. This is optional.
9.  Select a **Submission Date** for your feedback. This is a required field.
10.  Choose a **Submission Time** for your feedback. This is a required field.
11.  Select the **Sweet Type** of your experience. This helps Sweet Haven understand what type of sweet treat you enjoyed or didn't enjoy.
12.  Select the **Salty Type** of your experience. This helps Sweet Haven understand what type of salty treat you enjoyed or didn't enjoy.
13.  Select the **Savory Type** of your experience. This helps Sweet Haven understand what type of savory treat you enjoyed or didn't enjoy.
14.  Leave any additional **Submission Comments** about your experience.
15.  Provide any **Sweet Haven Comments**.
16.  Select the **Sweet Haven Staff** who interacted with you. This helps Sweet Haven understand their service quality.
17.  Choose a **Sweet Haven Branch** where you visited or interacted with. This helps Sweet Haven understand their service reach.
18.  Select the **Sweet Haven Location** where you visited or interacted with. This helps Sweet Haven understand their location-specific services.
19.  Choose a **Sweet Haven Hours** that you visited or interacted with. This helps Sweet Haven understand their operating hours.
20.  Select whether you have any **Sweet Haven Special Requests**.
21.  Leave any **Sweet Haven Additional Comments** about your experience.
22.  Leave any **Sweet Haven Feedback** about your experience.
23.  Leave any **Sweet Haven Suggestion**.
24.  Leave any **Sweet Haven Improvement** suggestions.
25.  Leave any **Sweet Haven Idea** for Sweet Haven.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Below is a breakdown of the form's fields and what to expect from each.

- **Customer Name (1)**: Enter your full name to help Sweet Haven understand who's giving feedback.
  - **Type: Text**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Email (2)**: Enter your email to allow Sweet Haven to contact you if needed.
  - **Type: Email**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Feedback Type (3)**: Choose the type of experience you had with Sweet Haven.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: Sweet, Savory, Salty**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Rating (4)**: Rate your experience on a scale.
  - **Type: Number**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Comment (5)**: Leave any comments about your experience.
  - **Type: Note**
  - **Required: No**

- **Date and Time (6)**: Select a date and time for your feedback submission.
  - **Type: Date**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number for Sweet Haven to contact you if needed.
  - **Type: Text**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Email (8)**: Choose whether you want Sweet Haven to contact you via email.
  - **Type: Select Multiple**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Submission Date (9)**: Select a date for your feedback submission.
  - **Type: Date**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Submission Time (10)**: Choose a time for your feedback submission.
  - **Type: Time**
  - **Required: Yes**

- **Sweet Type (11)**: Select the type of sweet treat you enjoyed or didn't enjoy.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Salty Type (12)**: Select the type of salty treat you enjoyed or didn’t enjoy.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Savory Type (13)**: Select the type of savory treat you enjoyed or didn’t enjoy.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Submission Comments (14)**: Leave any comments about your experience.
  - **Type: Note**
  - **Required: No**

- **Sweet Haven Comments (15)**: Leave any comments about your experience.
  - **Type: Note**
  - **Required: No**

- **Sweet Haven Staff (16)**: Choose the Sweet Haven staff member who interacted with you.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Sweet Haven Branch (17)**: Choose the Sweet Haven branch where you visited or interacted with.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
  - **Required: No**

- **Sweet Haven Location (18)**: Choose the Sweet Haven location where you visited or interacted with.
  - **Type: Select One**
  - **Options: None, Other**
