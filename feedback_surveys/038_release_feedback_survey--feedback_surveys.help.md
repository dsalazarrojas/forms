# Release Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Release Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users about a specific product release. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future releases.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to provide detailed feedback about the product release.
2. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or discussions.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comment" field.
4. Provide any feedback or suggestions you have, and don't forget to include your contact information if you'd like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (user_feedback, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your experience with the product release, including any positive or negative feedback you'd like to share.
* **Email** (user_email, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Product Launch Date** (product_launch_date, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you received the product release (if applicable).
* **Product Name** (product_name, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback about.
* **Rating** (rating, number, required: false):
	+ Rate your experience with the product release (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **Comment** (comment, text, required: false):
	+ Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the product release.
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for improving the product release or future releases.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Product Category** (product_category, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the category of the product you are providing feedback about.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comment" field.
* If you'd like us to follow up with you, please provide your email address or phone number.
