# Class Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Class Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from students on their experiences and perceptions of a class they have taken. The survey is used to collect information on various aspects of the class, including the student's engagement, understanding, and interaction with the instructor. This feedback is essential for the instructor to improve their teaching practices and make necessary adjustments to create a more effective learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your name and email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussions).
2.  Select the class you are providing feedback on.
3.  Select the semester or term you took the class.
4.  Choose how often you attended class, how engaged you felt, and how well you understood the content.
5.  Rate the workload, difficulty of the class, and the instructor's clarity in explanations.
6.  Assess the instructor's availability and helpfulness of feedback provided.
7.  Evaluate the quality of class materials and what you learned most from the class.
8.  Identify what you liked most and least about the class.
9.  Provide any additional suggestions for improvement.
10.  Finally, decide whether you would recommend the class to other students and if you would take another class with this instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This is the field for you to enter your name, which will help us understand who is providing feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up discussions, please enter your email address here.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Choose the class you are providing feedback on.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Choose the instructor who led your class.
* **Semester or Term** (`semester_term`, select_one, required): Select the term when you took the class.
* **How Often Did You Attend Class** (`attendance_frequency`, select_one, required): This question helps us understand your attendance rate during the class.
* **How Engaged Did You Feel** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of engagement during class to help us understand your interaction with the instructor and the content.
* **How Well Do You Understand the Content** (`content_understanding`, select_one, required): Assess how well you understood the class materials.
* **How Would You Rate the Workload** (`workload_rating`, select_one, required): Choose how challenging you found the workload in the class.
* **How Difficult Was the Class** (`difficulty_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how difficult you found the class.
* **How Clear Was the Instructor** (`instructor_clarity`, select_one, required): Assess the instructor's clarity in explanations.
* **How Available Was the Instructor** (`instructor_availability`, select_one, required): Choose how available the instructor was for questions and help.
* **How Helpful Was the Feedback** (`feedback_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Assess the helpfulness of feedback received on assignments and exams.
* **Quality of Class Materials** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Choose the quality of class materials (e.g., textbooks, slides, handouts).
* **What Did You Learn Most** (`what_learned`, text, required): This is a field to identify the most important takeaways from the class.
* **What Did You Like Most** (`liked_most`, text, required): Choose the best aspects of the class.
* **What Did You Like Least** (`liked_least`, text, required): Identify areas for improvement in the class.
* **What Suggestions Do You Have** (`suggestions`, text, optional): This is a field for you to provide any additional suggestions for improvement.
* **Would You Recommend This Class** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Decide whether you would recommend this class to other students.
* **Would You Take Another Class With This Instructor** (`would_take_again`, select_one, required): Determine whether you would take another class with this instructor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you might have.
