# Payroll Helpdesk Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from employees on their experience when interacting with the payroll helpdesk. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and provide a better service. Your input will help us understand how we can make the process more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the form completely, but feel free to skip questions that don't apply to your experience.
* Make sure to answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can type them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How long it takes for your request to be responded to?** (`response_time`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks about the duration of time it takes for your request to be fulfilled. Please select an approximate duration from the options, or type in the text box if it's not listed.
* **How professional were the staff you interacted with?** (`professionalism`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question assesses the level of professionalism displayed by the staff you interacted with. Choose one of the options or type in your own evaluation.
* **How well was your issue resolved?** (`issue_resolution`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question evaluates how well your issue was addressed by the staff. Please select one of the options to give us an understanding of your experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): This is your chance to provide any other comments or suggestions you think might be useful in improving our service. Feel free to type whatever comes to mind.
* **Who was assigned to support your request?** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us identify who was responsible for handling your request. If unsure, you can choose the "Unknown" option.
* **Would you like to provide a feedback email?** (`feedback_email`, `email`, required/optional): If you'd like to share your feedback via email, please enter your email address here. We'll get in touch with you to follow up on your comments.
* **Would you like to provide a feedback phone number?** (`feedback_phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you'd rather provide feedback via phone, please enter your phone number here. We'll get in touch with you to discuss your comments.
* **Additional comments** (`feedback_comments`, `note`, required/optional): This is your last chance to add any final comments or suggestions before submitting your feedback.
