# Facilities Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the facilities they have used. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their experiences, satisfaction levels, and comments regarding various aspects of the facilities, such as services, staff, equipment, and overall condition. The collected feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance the facilities' quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the facility you used (Field 1: Facility Used).
2. Provide the date and time of your visit (Field 2: Date and Time).
3. Select your satisfaction level with the facilities' condition, services, and staff (Field 3: Satisfaction Level).
4. Share your comments about the facilities, including their condition, services, and staff (Fields 4-10: Comments about facilities).
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Used** (`Facility Used`, text, optional): Please enter the name or number of the facility you used. This will help us understand which facility you are providing feedback about.
* **Date and Time** (`Date and Time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time you visited the facility. This is helpful to track any changes over time.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`Satisfaction Level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the facilities' condition, services, and staff. Choose from:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Comments about facilities** (`Comments about facilities`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the facilities, including their condition, services, and staff. This is a space for you to elaborate on your previous answers.
* **Comments about services** (`Comments about services`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the services provided by the facility.
* **Comments about facility staff** (`Comments about facility staff`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the facility staff's performance and behavior.
* **Comments about facility condition** (`Comments about facility condition`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the overall condition and maintenance of the facility.
* **Comments about facility location** (`Comments about facility location`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the location and accessibility of the facility.
* **Comments about facility equipment** (`Comments about facility equipment`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the equipment available at the facility.
* **Comments about facility services** (`Comments about facility services`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the services provided by the facility.
* **Comments about facility parking** (`Comments about facility parking`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the parking facilities and services.
* **Comments about facility access** (`Comments about facility access`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the accessibility and availability of the facility for people with disabilities.

## Tips
Remember to answer honestly and thoroughly. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our facilities. If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them in the comments sections provided.
