# Organizational Feedback Maturity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Feedback Maturity Survey is designed to gauge the effectiveness of your organization's feedback process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose.
2. Answer the questions honestly, selecting the response that best matches your experience.
3. Use the provided options as a guide, but feel free to add any additional comments or details in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey Questions** (`feedback_survey_questions`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the frequency of feedback given and received within your organization. Select all the ways in which feedback is provided and received in your organization.
* **Feedback Categories** (`feedback_categories`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your organization's feedback culture.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, text, required): Describe how often feedback is provided and received in your organization (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Feedback Methods** (`feedback_methods`, text, required): List the channels or methods through which feedback is typically provided and received in your organization (e.g., email, phone, in-person, etc.).
* **Feedback Channels** (`feedback_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose the channels through which feedback is typically provided and received in your organization.
* **Feedback Recipients** (`feedback_recipients`, select_multiple, required): Select all individuals or groups within your organization that receive feedback.
* **Organizational Level** (`organizational_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your organization that best describes the frequency and effectiveness of feedback.
* **Frequency of Feedback** (`frequency_of_feedback`, date, required): Enter the date of the last feedback provided or received in your organization.
* **Feedback on Issues** (`feedback_on_issues`, text, required): Describe any recent issues or concerns that have been addressed through feedback in your organization.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose the level of satisfaction among employees regarding feedback provided and received.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select the channels used for communication within your organization.
* **Feedback on Actions** (`feedback_on_actions`, text, required): Describe any actions taken as a result of feedback provided or received in your organization.
* **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, select_one, required): Choose the level of engagement among employees regarding feedback provided and received.
* **Organizational Culture** (`organizational_culture`, select_multiple, required): Select the characteristics of your organization's culture that influence feedback provided and received.

Note: This guide is intended to be a helpful reference, but if you have any doubts or concerns, it's always best to clarify with the form creator or relevant stakeholders.
