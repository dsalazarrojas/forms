# mobile_app_customer_experience_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and survey data from customers about their experience with the mobile app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question based on your experience with the app.
2. Be honest and detailed in your responses.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please provide them in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### app_name (Required)
* **App Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of the app that you used.
* Please enter the exact name of the app as it appears on your device.

### how_did_you_find_us (Required)
* **How did you find us**: This field asks how you discovered the app.
* Select all the ways you found the app, such as social media, online search, or word of mouth.

### what_do_you_like (Required)
* **What do you like**: This field asks what you like about the app.
* Select one option that best describes your favorite feature or aspect of the app.

### how_did_you_feel (Required)
* **How did you feel**: This field is for you to describe your overall feeling about the app.
* Please provide a detailed description of your experience.

### how_did_you_like (Required)
* **How did you like**: This field asks about the level of satisfaction you had with the app.
* Enter the date of when you used the app and how long you used it.

### app_store (Optional)
* **App Store**: This field is for you to enter the store where you downloaded the app.
* If you have used the app from a store, please enter the name of the store.

### what_do_you_recommend (Required)
* **What do you recommend**: This field asks if you would recommend the app to others.
* Select one option that best describes your recommendation.

### have_you_used (Required)
* **Have you used**: This field asks if you have used the app before.
* Select all the times you have used the app, such as before, after, or never.

### how_long_did_you (Required)
* **How long did you use**: This field asks about the duration of your app usage.
* Select one option that best describes how long you used the app.

### will_you_use (Required)
* **Will you use**: This field asks about your future plans to use the app.
* Select one option that best describes your intention to use the app again.

### how_much_time (Optional)
* **How much time**: This field is for you to describe how much time you spent using the app.
* Please provide a detailed description of your experience.

### what_did_we_miss (Optional)
* **What did we miss**: This field asks about features or aspects of the app that you missed.
* Select all the features or aspects that you missed.

### what_do_we_miss (Optional)
* **What do we miss**: This field is for you to enter any features or aspects of the app that you missed.
* Please provide a detailed description of what you missed.

### what_we_miss (Optional)
* **What we miss**: This field is similar to the previous one.
* Please provide a detailed description of what you missed.

### feedback (Optional)
* **Feedback**: This field is for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the app.
* Please provide a detailed description of your experience or suggestions.

### rating (Required)

* Select one option that best describes your rating.

### how_would_rate (Optional)
