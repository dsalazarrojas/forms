# Laptop Rental Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers who have used our laptop rental service to share their feedback and opinions. Your input helps us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your comments and thoughts in the text fields as you would in a regular text message.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Leave optional comments in the "additional_comments" field if you wish to share more details.
4. Click submit once you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Laptop Rental Service Feedback Form** (`title`, `text`, required): Please enter your comments and thoughts about our laptop rental service.
* **Support Contact Info** (`support_contact_info`, `text`, required): This is your name and contact information so we can reach out to you to clarify or follow up on your feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, feel free to share them here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with our laptop rental service. What did you like or dislike? What could we improve? Be as detailed as possible.
