# Condo Automation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form was designed by an AI as a survey to gather feedback from residents about their experiences with automation in their condo or apartment building. This feedback will help the management and staff of the building understand how well the automation is meeting the residents' needs and identify areas for improvement.

<Thinking>
To ensure that your feedback is useful, please be honest in your responses and consider the following:
* Only provide feedback about the condo automation system, not about other building services or amenities.
* Focus on your personal experience and avoid general statements.
* Select one answer for questions that require it, and multiple answers for questions that allow it.
* Be specific and detailed in your answers, especially in the "Any other feedback for the management" field.
This guide is for informational purposes and does not guarantee that your feedback will be acted upon. However, your input is essential for improving the condo automation system.
</Thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

The form consists of 9 questions that gather information about your experience with the automation system. Please follow these steps:

1. **Please enter your name**: Type your name to let us know who is providing feedback. This is optional, so you can skip it if you prefer.
2. **Enter your unit number**: Provide your apartment or unit identifier to help us understand which part of the building you are experiencing the automation.
3. **Rate the system's usability**: Choose how easy or difficult you find the automation system to use.
4. **Select the most useful features**: Pick up to three features that you find most helpful or useful in the automation system.
5. **Rate the system's reliability**: Choose how often you experience failures or issues with the automation system.
6. **Rate your experience with support**: Choose how well you think the building staff or vendor supports the automation system.
7. **Suggest improvements**: Type any features or fixes you would like to see in the automation system.
8. **Share any privacy concerns**: Describe any data or privacy concerns you have about the automation system.
9. **Will you recommend the system?**: Choose how likely you are to recommend the automation system to others.
10. **Additional comments**: Share any other feedback or suggestions you have for the management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident name** (`Resident name`, text, optional): Your name is optional, but it helps us understand who is providing feedback.
* **Unit number** (`Unit number`, text, optional): Enter your apartment or unit identifier to help us understand which part of the building you are experiencing the automation.
* **System usability rating** (`System usability rating`, select_one, required): Choose how easy or difficult you find the automation system to use.
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
* **Most useful features** (`Most useful features`, select_multiple, optional): Pick up to three features that you find most helpful in the automation system.
	+ Lighting control
	+ Climate control
	+ Security monitoring
	+ Energy reports
	+ Remote access
* **Reliability rating** (`Reliability rating`, select_one, required): Choose how often you experience failures or issues with the automation system.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Support experience rating** (`Support experience rating`, select_one, optional): Choose how well you think the building staff or vendor supports the automation system.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Desired improvements** (`Desired improvements`, text, optional): Type any features or fixes you would like to see in the automation system.
* **Any privacy concerns** (`Any privacy concerns`, text, optional): Describe any data or privacy concerns you have about the automation system.
* **Likelihood to recommend** (`Likelihood to recommend`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend the automation system to others.
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Share any other feedback or suggestions you have for the management.
