# Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey form, ensuring you understand what information is collected and how to submit it.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the category as "Feedback Surveys" from the dropdown list.
2.  Fill in the description field to provide a brief explanation of the incident or issue you are reporting.
3.  Enter your email address (if you wish to receive updates) and your phone number (if you wish to be contacted).
4.  Select the date and time of the incident or issue.
5.  Provide any notes about the situation.
6.  Choose the gender you identify with.
7.  Select the type of violence you have experienced or witnessed (e.g., Emotional, Physical, or Sexual).
8.  Finally, submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Gender** (Gender): This field allows you to select the gender you identify with, which will help us understand the context of the violence you are reporting.
*   **type_of_violence** (type of violence): This field is a multiple-select option, allowing you to select the type of violence you have experienced or witnessed (e.g., Emotional, Physical, or Sexual). You can select more than one type if applicable.
*   **category** (category): This field allows you to select the category for the feedback you are providing, which will help in organizing the responses and providing aggregate reporting.
*   **description** (description): A field to provide a brief explanation of the incident or issue you are reporting.
*   **email** (Email): This is an optional field to provide your email address if you wish to receive updates or notifications.
*   **phone** (Phone): This is an optional field to provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
*   **date** (Date): This field allows you to select the date of the incident or issue.
*   **time** (Time): This field allows you to select the time of the incident or issue.
*   **note** (Note): A field for any additional notes or comments you would like to provide.
*   **submit** (Submit): This is the final step in submitting your response and providing us with the information we need.
*   **category** (category): This field allows you to select the category for the feedback you are providing, which will help in organizing the responses and providing aggregate reporting.

## Tips
- If you need to report multiple incidents, you can submit multiple responses using this form.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us at [insert contact info].
- Please provide accurate and honest information to help us better understand and address the issue of gender violence.
- All responses will be treated with confidentiality and anonymity will be maintained.
- If you are concerned about your safety or well-being, please reach out to a trusted authority figure or organization for support.
