# Ethical Consumerism Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your thoughts and behaviors regarding the impact of your purchasing decisions on the environment. Your input will help us improve our understanding of consumerism and make informed decisions for a more sustainable future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. If you have any technical issues, let us know in the "Did you experience any technical issues while completing this survey?" question.
4. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Would you like to provide additional comments about this survey?" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Have you ever thought about the impact of your purchasing decisions on the environment?** (`question1`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question to let us know if you've ever considered the environmental impact of your purchases.
* **What motivates you to make environmentally friendly purchasing decisions?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all that apply to let us know what drives you to make eco-friendly choices.
* **How important is the impact of your purchasing decisions on the environment to you?** (`question3`, `number`, optional): Answer this question to rate the importance of environmental impact in your purchasing decisions.
* **Have you ever made a purchasing decision based on a brand's environmental reputation?** (`question4`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you've ever chosen a product or brand because of its environmental reputation.
* **How do you currently think you make environmentally friendly purchasing decisions?** (`question5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your current approach to eco-friendly purchasing.
* **Which of the following best describes your current level of environmental awareness?** (`question6`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question to let us know how aware you are of environmental issues.
* **Have you ever avoided a product or brand that you believed was unethical or irresponsible?** (`question7`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you've ever chosen not to buy a product or brand due to its environmental or social impact.
* **How do you think you will make environmentally friendly purchasing decisions in the next 6 months?** (`question8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your future approach to eco-friendly purchasing.
* **How likely are you to recommend this type of survey to a friend or colleague?** (`question9`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question to let us know if you'd recommend this survey to others.
* **How long did it take you to complete this survey?** (`question10`, `time`, optional): Please report any technical issues you experienced while completing this survey.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `select_one`, optional): This is a dropdown menu to help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Other** (`Other`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to contact us through another method, provide your preferred method of contact here.
* **How did you hear about this survey?** (`question11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to help us understand how you were aware of this survey.
* **How did you find this survey?** (`question12`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question to let us know about your experience with the survey.
* **Would you like to provide additional comments about this survey?** (`question13`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions here.
* **Did you experience any technical issues while completing this survey?** (`question14`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you experienced any technical issues during the survey.
* **Would you like to take this survey again?** (`question15`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question to let us know if you'd be willing to participate in a future survey.
* **Awareness** (`Awareness`, `number`, optional): This is a scale to rate your current level of environmental awareness.
* **Awareness source** (`Awareness source`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe where you learned about environmental issues.
* **Education** (`Education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your educational background.
* **Income** (`Income`, `number`, optional): Enter your income level to help us understand your purchasing power.
* **Location** (`Location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your location.
