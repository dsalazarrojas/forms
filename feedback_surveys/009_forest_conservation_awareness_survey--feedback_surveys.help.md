# Forest Conservation Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your knowledge, feelings, and experiences regarding forest conservation practices and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
3. You can complete the form in one go, or save your progress and return later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief description of what we're trying to accomplish with this survey. Please provide a short paragraph explaining what you know or feel about forest conservation.
* **Knowledge**: In this section, please answer the question using a number (1, 2, 3) to describe your level of knowledge about forest conservation practices.
	+ 1: I don't know much about forest conservation.
	+ 2: I have some knowledge about forest conservation, but I'd like to learn more.
	+ 3: I have a good understanding of forest conservation practices.
* **Feelings**: Please select one of the options that best describes how you feel about forest conservation practices.
	+ Option 1: Concerned
	+ Option 2: Interested
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Actions**: In this section, please select all the actions that you have taken or would like to take to support forest conservation practices.
	+ Option 1: Plant trees
	+ Option 2: Participate in local conservation events
	+ Option 3: Support organizations that promote forest conservation
* **Comments**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about forest conservation.
* **Email** and **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted for further information or to receive updates about forest conservation efforts, please provide your email and phone number. Your contact information will be used only for this purpose.
* **Date** and **Time**: Please provide the date and time you're completing this form. This will help us to track the completion time of the survey.
* **Time**: Please enter the current time you're accessing this form.
* **Actions (continued)**: This is the same as the **Actions** field above. Please select all the actions that you have taken or would like to take to support forest conservation practices.

Note: The **Actions** field appears twice in the form, but it is only required once, on page 4.

Tips: Please be honest and provide accurate information to help us better understand your views on forest conservation practices. Your feedback is valuable to us!
