# Tenant Retention Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the motivation behind tenant retention, aiming to understand what drives tenants to renew their contracts or continue their relationship with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and their purpose.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. For optional fields, provide as much information as you feel is relevant and helpful.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Satisfied (\*_how\_satisfied\_\)**: How would you rate your satisfaction with our services? Please select one of the options that best describes your feelings.
	+ Option 1: Very Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Satisfied
* **Likely to Renew (\*_likely_to_renew\)**: Which of the following best describes your likelihood to renew your contract with us?
	+ Option 1: Very Likely
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Likely
	+ Option 3: Not Very Likely
* **Renewal Intent (\*_renewal_intent\)**: How likely are you to renew your contract with us?
	+ Option 1: Very Likely
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Likely
	+ Option 2: Not Very Likely
* **Improvement Ideas (\*_improvement_ideas\)**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them here. This will help us to better understand your needs and improve our services.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (\*_suggestions_for_improvement\)**: If you have any additional suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
* **Tenant Satisfaction (\*_tenant_satisfaction\)**: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our services? Please enter a number between 0 and 10.
* **Contact Info (\*_contact_info\)**: Which of the following best describes your preferred method of contact?
	+ Option 1: Email
	+ Option 2: Phone
	+ Option 3: In-person
