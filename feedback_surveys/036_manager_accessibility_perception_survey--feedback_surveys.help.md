# Manager Accessibility Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Accessibility Perception Survey is a tool used by employees to rate their manager's approachability, responsiveness, and communication style, and other attributes that contribute to a positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your experience with your manager's approachability, responsiveness, communication style, and other attributes.
2. Answer each field honestly, considering your experiences and perceptions.
3. If a field is not applicable or you are unsure, select "No" or "Unknown" as an option.
4. Submit the form once all fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Approachability (1)**: Rate your manager's approachability by selecting one of the two options: "Yes" or "No". This field assesses your manager's willingness to listen to your ideas and opinions.
* **Responsiveness (2)**: Choose from the two options, "Yes" or "No", to describe your manager's promptness and timeliness in responding to your needs and requests.
* **Communication Style (3)**: Select all the options that apply to your manager's communication style:
	+ "Yes" for managers who frequently initiate conversations and meetings.
	+ "No" for managers who infrequently initiate conversations and meetings.
* **Feedback (4)**: Describe your manager's feedback, if any, using this text field. This field is optional and not required.
* **Manager Response (5)**: If you have received a manager response to your request or question, select the amount of time it took for them to respond:
	+ Select a time range from the options provided.
* **Communication Frequency (6)**: Choose from the two options, "Yes" or "No", to describe your manager's communication frequency:
	+ "Yes" for managers who frequently initiate conversations.
	+ "No" for managers who infrequently initiate conversations.
* **Manager Availability (7)**: Select one option to describe your manager's availability:
	+ "Yes" for managers who are always or sometimes available.
	+ "No" for managers who are never available.
* **Email Frequency (8)**: Choose from the two options, "Yes" or "No", to describe your manager's email frequency:
	+ "Yes" for managers who frequently send emails.
	+ "No" for managers who infrequently send emails.

## Tips
* Please answer each field truthfully, based on your real experiences and perceptions.
* If you're unsure or have no experience with your manager, it's okay to select "No" or "Unknown".
