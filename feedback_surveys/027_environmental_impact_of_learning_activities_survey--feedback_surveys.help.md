# environmental_impact_of_learning_activities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback about the environmental impact of learning activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction page to understand the context and purpose of the survey.
2. On the "What type of learning activities do you perform most frequently?" page, select all the activities that apply to you.
3. On the "How do you think the impact of your activities is on the environment?" page, choose your response from the provided options.
4. If you have additional comments or insights, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Provide your rating of the environmental impact of your activities in the "Rating of the environmental impact" field.
6. If you would like to share your email or phone number (optional), you can do so on the respective fields.
7. Finally, type your name for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Environmental Impact of Learning Activities**: This is an introduction page providing context to the survey.
* **What type of learning activities do you perform most frequently?**: Select all the activities that you frequently engage in, such as reading books or articles, watching videos, or participating in online communities.
* **How do you think the impact of your activities is on the environment?**: Choose one of the provided options: Positive, Negative, Neutral, or No impact.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional insights or comments about your learning activities, you can type them in this field.
* **Rating of the environmental impact**: Provide a rating on a scale that is not specified, but is assumed to be a numerical value ( likely 1-10 or 1-5, but this is not explicit in the provided YAML fields).
* **Email**: This is an optional field to share your email address, which can be used for follow-up communications (if desired).
* **Phone**: This is also an optional field to share your phone number.
* **Name**: Type your name for identification purposes.
