# Wash Bay And Paint Bay Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users on the cleanliness and overall experience of the Wash Bay And Paint Bay services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the comments fields.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Cleanliness (1) 
Cleanliness: 
This field asks about your overall impression of the cleanliness of the service.
• Required: No
• Type: Number
• Options: None

### Equipment (2) 
Equipment: 
This field asks about your impression of the equipment provided for the service.
• Required: No
• Type: Number
• Options: None

### Staff (3) 
Staff: 
This field asks about your impression of the staff that provided the service.
• Required: No
• Type: Number
• Options: None

### Comments (4) 
Comments: 
This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
• Required: No
• Type: Text
• Options: None

### Overall Experience (5) 
Overall Experience: 
This field asks about your general impression of the service.
• Required: No
• Type: Number
• Options: None

### Rating (6) 
Rating: 
This field is not a numerical value but a rating on a scale.
• Required: No
• Type: Number
• Options: None

### Rating Scale (7) 
Rating Scale: 
This field allows you to rate the service on a scale of:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
• Required: No
• Type: Select one
• Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor

### Cleanliness Frequency (8) 
Cleanliness Frequency: 
This field asks about how often the service met your expectations for cleanliness.
• Required: No
• Type: Select multiple
• Options: Always, Most of the time, Sometimes, Rarely

### Equipment Frequency (9) 
Equipment Frequency: 
This field asks about how often the service met your expectations for equipment.
• Required: No
• Type: Select multiple
• Options: Always, Most of the time, Sometimes, Rarely

### Comments on Services (10) 
Comments on Services: 
This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have on the services.
• Required: No
• Type: Text
• Options: None
