# Kandi Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kandi Product Feedback Survey form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about the product. This feedback will help us understand how to improve the product and better serve our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the order of the pages, from page 1 to page 10.
2. On page 1, enter your feedback in the text field "Feedback Survey".
3. On page 2, select the type of feedback you are providing in the "Feedback Type" select_one field.
4. If you have any issues or concerns, enter them in the note field "Issue Report".
5. Provide detailed feedback in the note field "Feedback Details".
6. On pages 3-9, provide ratings for the product in the number fields labeled as 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, respectively.
7. Finally, share any improvement ideas you have in the text field "Improvement Idea".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey** (1, text, required): This field is where you can enter your overall feedback about the product.
* **Feedback Type** (2, select_one, optional): Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., feature request, bug report, etc.).
* **Feedback Details** (3, note, optional): Provide detailed feedback about your experience with the product.
* **Issue Report** (4, note, optional): Enter any issues or concerns you've encountered with the product.
* **Rating 1** (5, number, optional): Rate the product's overall quality.
* **Rating 2** (6, number, optional): Rate the product's ease of use.
* **Rating 3** (7, number, optional): Rate the product's features and functionality.
* **Rating 4** (8, number, optional): Rate the product's performance and speed.
* **Rating 5** (9, number, optional): Rate the product's overall satisfaction.
* **Improvement Idea** (10, text, optional): Share any improvement ideas you have for the product.
