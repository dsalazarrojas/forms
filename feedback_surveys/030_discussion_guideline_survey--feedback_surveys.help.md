# discussion_guideline_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on discussion guidelines, allowing you to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of your guidelines over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Start by reading the introduction to get a sense of the purpose of this form.
2. **Rating Clarity, Usefulness, Quality, and Satisfaction**: Rate the clarity, usefulness, quality, and satisfaction of the discussion guidelines on a scale from 1-10.
3. **Select One**: Choose one option from the list for "Select one 10".
4. **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options for "Select Multiple 10".
5. **Date**: Enter the date you are completing this form.
6. **Time**: Enter the time you are completing this form.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.
8. **Phone**: Enter your phone number to help us contact you if needed.
9. **Rating Time**: Rate how much time you spent on the discussion guidelines.
10. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to send your feedback.
