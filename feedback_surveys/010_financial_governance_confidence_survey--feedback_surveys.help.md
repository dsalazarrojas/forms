# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on the level of confidence end-users have in the financial governance practices of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by reading through the introduction page.
2. Fill out the required fields (name, email).
3. Answer the select-type questions (Financial Performance, Financial Risk, Financial Control, Financial Transparency, Financial Governance) with options that best fit your perception.
4. Provide your confidence level in the financial governance of the organization in the 'Confidence' field.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the 'Comments' field.
6. Fill out the date and time of submission.
7. Enter your name, which will be displayed as 'Submitted By'.
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name so we can associate your responses with you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the results of this survey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional/optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about this survey.
* **Financial Performance** (`financial_performance`, `select_multiple`, optional/optional): Choose all options that best describe your perception of our financial performance. 
  * Option 1: We are meeting our financial targets and achieving our goals.
  * Option 2: We are not meeting some of our financial targets, but overall financial performance is satisfactory.
  * Option 3: We are not meeting our financial targets and are experiencing difficulties.
* **Financial Risk** (`financial_risk`, `select_one`, optional/optional): Choose one option that best describes your perception of our financial risk.
  * Risk 1: We are taking calculated risks to achieve our financial goals.
  * Risk 2: We are taking excessive risks, which may affect our financial performance.
  * Risk 3: We are not taking enough risks to achieve our financial goals.
* **Financial Control** (`financial_control`, `select_one`, optional/optional): Choose one option that best describes your perception of our financial control.
  * Control 1: We have effective financial control mechanisms in place.
  * Control 2: We have some financial control, but there is room for improvement.
  * Control 3: We do not have sufficient financial control mechanisms.
* **Financial Transparency** (`financial_transparency`, `select_multiple`, optional/optional): Choose all options that best describe your perception of our financial transparency.
  * Option 1: We are transparent about our financial dealings and decisions.
  * Option 2: We have some financial transparency, but there is room for improvement.
  * Option 3: We do not have sufficient financial transparency.
* **Financial Governance** (`financial_governance`, `select_multiple`, optional/optional): Choose all options that best describe your perception of our financial governance practices.
  * Option 1: We have effective financial governance practices in place.
  * Option 2: We have some financial governance, but there is room for improvement.
  * Option 3: We do not have sufficient financial governance practices.
* **Confidence** (`confidence`, `note`, optional/optional): Describe your confidence level in our financial governance practices.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about this survey.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it will be displayed as 'Submitted By'.
