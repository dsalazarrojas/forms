# Termination Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting feedback from employees regarding the termination process. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the employee's experience and perceptions of the termination process, including the reasons for leaving, notice period, and any comments from the employee or manager. This feedback will help to identify areas for improvement in the termination process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the Employee Number, which is a unique identifier for the employee.
*   Choose the reason for leaving from the provided options or type in an "Other" reason.
*   Select the notice period and date (if applicable).
*   Enter the notice period length (if applicable).
*   Provide a brief description of the reason for leaving (if the "Other" option is selected).
*   Add any manager comments regarding the termination process.
*   Enter a brief description of any comments or concerns from the employee.
*   Select the termination method (Voluntary or Involuntary).
*   Note any Prophets that are relevant to the termination process (not applicable, this field is of type "note").
*   Select any Managers that were involved in the termination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Termination Process Feedback** (`termination_process_form`, text, required): A text field for entering the employee's feedback regarding the termination process.
* **Employee Number (`employee_number`, number, optional): A field for entering the employee's unique identifier.
* **Reason for Leaving (`reason_for_leaving`, select_one, optional): A dropdown menu to select the reason for leaving from the provided options or an "Other" option for entering a custom reason.
* **Notice Period (`notice_period`, select_multiple, optional): A dropdown menu to select the notice period.
* **Notice Date (`notice_date`, date, optional): A date field for entering the notice date.
* **Termination Date (`termination_date`, date, optional): A date field for entering the termination date.
* **Notice Period Length (`notice_period_length`, number, optional): A field for entering the notice period length.
* **Reason for Leaving Other (`reason_for_leaving_other`, text, optional): A field for entering a custom reason for leaving if the "Other" option is selected.
* **Manager Comments (`manager_comments`, text, optional): A field for entering any comments from the manager regarding the termination process.
* **Employee Comments (`employee_comments`, text, optional): A field for entering any comments or concerns from the employee.
* **Termination Method (`termination_method`, select_one, optional): A dropdown menu to select the termination method (Voluntary or Involuntary).
* **Prophets (`prophets`, note, optional): A field of type "note" that is not applicable and therefore has no explanation.
* **Managers (`managers`, select_multiple, optional): A dropdown menu to select any managers involved in the termination process.
