# Retail Challenge Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their shopping experience, specifically regarding sales incentives offered during the retail challenge. This survey aims to gather information on the sales incentive type and amount offered, product purchased, overall rating, and any additional comments or feedback. The collected data will help us to understand customer behavior and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions carefully and answer them according to your shopping experience.
2.  For questions with options, select the answer that best describes your experience.
3.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field to share your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Challenge Feedback Survey** (`title`, `note`): This is the title of the form, indicating that this is a feedback survey.
* **sales_incentive** (`sales_incentive`, `text`, optional): Please describe the sales incentive that was offered to you during the retail challenge.
* **product_purchased** (`product`, `select_one`, optional): If you purchased a product during the retail challenge, select 'Yes' to indicate that you bought something.
* **Overall_Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with the sales incentive, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional_comments** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **category_purchased** (`category`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you purchased multiple products during the retail challenge, select all categories that apply.
* **sales_incentive_amount** (`sales_incentive_amount`, `number`, optional): If the sales incentive was monetary, estimate the amount offered.
* **sales_incentive_type** (`sales_incentive_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of sales incentive that was offered (e.g., percentage off, free gift, etc.).
* **Contact_Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): Share your contact information for us to follow up with you.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Additional_feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us to improve our services.
* Please answer all questions that apply to your shopping experience.
* If you need help or have any issues, contact us through the provided contact information.
