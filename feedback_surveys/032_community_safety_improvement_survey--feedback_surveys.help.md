# Community Safety Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information on safety concerns and potential improvements within the community. Your input will help us better understand the current safety landscape and identify areas for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and ZIP Code).
2. Answer questions about your experience with safety in your neighborhood, including how safe you feel, concerns, and whether you've been a victim of crime.
3. Share your thoughts on specific locations where you feel unsafe.
4. Indicate whether you would volunteer for safety programs.
5. Choose your preferred method for receiving safety updates.
6. Express your level of trust in local authorities.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information**: Please provide your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and ZIP Code for contact purposes and follow-up communication.
* **First Name**: Your first name (optional).
* **Last Name**: Your last name (optional).
* **Email Address**: Your email address for future communication (optional).
* **Your Neighborhood or Area**: Your neighborhood or area of residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Your ZIP code for location identification.
* **How Long Have You Lived Here**: Indicate how long you've lived in your neighborhood.
* **Safety Perception**: Briefly express how safe you feel in your neighborhood.
* **How Safe Do You Feel in Your Neighborhood**: Choose how safe you feel in your neighborhood on a scale of Very Safe to Very Unsafe.
* **How Safe Do You Feel During the Day**: Choose how safe you feel during the day on a scale of Very Safe to Very Unsafe.
* **How Safe Do You Feel at Night**: Choose how safe you feel at night on a scale of Very Safe to Very Unsafe.
* **Has Safety Improved or Declined**: Indicate whether you feel the safety in your neighborhood has improved, stayed the same, or declined over the past year.
* **Safety Concerns**: List your main safety concerns, choosing all that apply.
* **What Are Your Main Safety Concerns**: Select all specific locations where you feel most unsafe (e.g., abandoned buildings, loitering).
* **Have You Been a Victim of Crime**: Indicate whether you've been a victim of crime in the past 2 years.
* **Incident Details**: If you're comfortable sharing, provide details about your experience.
* **Safety Improvements**: Share your thoughts on what would help improve safety in your neighborhood.
* **What Improvements Would Help Most**: Choose up to three improvements that would most benefit your neighborhood (e.g., more police patrols, better street lighting).
* **Is Street Lighting Adequate**: Indicate whether you feel street lighting is adequate.
* **Is Police Presence Adequate**: Indicate whether you feel the police presence is adequate.
* **Are You Involved in Community Safety**: Indicate your level of involvement in neighborhood watch or other community safety initiatives.
* **Would You Volunteer for Safety Programs**: Indicate whether you would volunteer for safety programs.
* **How Do You Prefer to Receive Safety Updates**: Choose how you prefer to receive safety updates (e.g., email, text messages, social media).
* **How Much Do You Trust Local Authorities**: Indicate your level of trust in local authorities.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have for improving safety in your neighborhood.
