# studio_usage_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "studio_usage_feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our studio service. This form will help us identify areas of improvement, understand what we're doing right, and provide a platform for users to share their thoughts and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what questions we're asking.
2. For each question, answer honestly and provide as much detail as you'd like.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Rating (`session_rating`, Number, Optional)
* A rating from 1 to 5 to express how you felt about your session.

### Overall Rating (`overall_rating`, Number, Optional)
* A rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall experience with our studio.

### Session Comments (`session_comments`, Text, Optional)
* Please share any thoughts or feedback you have about your session. This is a chance to provide more detailed feedback and suggestions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback. We value your input and use it to improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know in the session comments field.
* We're here to listen and improve, so don't hesitate to share your thoughts and suggestions.
