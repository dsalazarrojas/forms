# Online Program Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the online program purchase process to help us identify areas of improvement and ensure a seamless user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with your answers. You can skip any field that is not relevant to your situation. The form consists of several pages with fields that you need to fill out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Purchase Survey** (`program_purchase_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of your feedback. Please enter a brief description of your experience with the online program purchase process.
* **Category** (`category`, select, optional): Please select 'Yes' if the category of your feedback is relevant to this form. You can select 'No' if it's not.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific program, please enter the name of the program here.
* **Purchase Type** (`purchase_type`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the types that apply to your purchase experience:
	+ 'Yes' for purchasing online programs
	+ 'No' for not purchasing online programs
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific purchase date, please enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Purchased From** (`purchased_from`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback on where you purchased the online program, please enter the source (e.g., website, sales channel, etc.).
