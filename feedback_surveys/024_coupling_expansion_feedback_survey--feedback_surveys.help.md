# Coupling Expansion Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coupling Expansion Feedback Survey is designed to gather customer feedback on their experience with a product. This feedback will help us understand how customers perceive our product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Coupling Expansion Feedback Survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly about your experience with the product.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question from the provided choices.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field to share them.
4. Enter your email address and phone number if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
*   **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is a general introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and then start answering the questions.

### What is your overall satisfaction with the product?
*   **What is your overall satisfaction with the product?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product using the provided options:
    *   Very Dissatisfied
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Very Satisfied

### How likely are you to recommend the product?
*   **How likely are you to recommend the product?** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options:
    *   Not Likely
    *   Somewhat Likely
    *   Neutral
    *   Very Likely

### What is your experience with the product?
*   **What is your experience with the product?** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following:
    *   Very Poor
    *   Somewhat Poor
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Good
    *   Very Good

### Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (`5`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns about your experience with the product, please share them here.

### Email
*   **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your email address.

### Phone Number
*   **Phone Number** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.

### What would you like to see improved?
*   **What would you like to see improved?** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on what you would like to see improved in the product.
