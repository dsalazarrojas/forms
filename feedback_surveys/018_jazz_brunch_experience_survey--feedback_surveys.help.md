# Jazz Brunch Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Jazz Brunch event. It is intended to gather information on the quality of service, music, and overall experience of the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you have attended the Jazz Brunch event before completing this form.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Skip any optional fields that are not relevant to your experience.
4. Use the provided text boxes to provide detailed answers to any questions that require more information.
5. Submit your form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is the title section of the form, which provides a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Guest Name**: Please enter your name, as it will appear on the form submission.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Venue**: Enter the venue where the event took place (optional).
* **Server Rating**: Rate the quality of service you received during the event (1/5 to 5/5).
* **Food**: Rate the quality of the food served at the event (1/5 to 5/5).
* **Music**: Rate the quality of the music played at the event (1/5 to 5/5).
* **Overall**: Rate your overall experience at the event (1/5 to 5/5).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any contact information you would like us to use to follow up with you (optional).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you attended (optional).
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional details about the event (optional).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any further comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Submitted From**: Enter where you submitted this form from (optional).
* **Submitted At**: Enter the time you submitted this form (optional).
* **Form Header 2**: This is another title section of the form, which is not currently being used.
* **Event Attended**: Indicate whether you actually attended the event (yes/no).
* **Age**: Enter your age (not required, but optional).
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Female, Male, Non-binary, Prefer not to say).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Submitter**: Enter your name (optional).
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form (optional).
* **Submitted**: Enter your name (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have answered all required questions before submitting the form.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties or have questions about the form, please contact us for assistance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us to improve our events.
