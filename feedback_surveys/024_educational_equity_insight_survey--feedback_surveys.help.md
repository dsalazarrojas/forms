# Educational Equity Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Equity Insight Survey is a self-reporting tool to gather feedback and insights from school districts and educational institutions on their perspectives on equity and inclusion. The survey is meant to be a collaborative platform for improvement, fostering better relationships between schools, districts, and communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what information it collects.
2. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your knowledge.
3. Use the provided options for select fields.
4. If a field is optional, feel free to provide additional information if you wish.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. You can use it to provide context or any other relevant information.
* **Survey questions** (`survey_questions`, select_multiple, optional): This field collects multiple answers from you. Select all options that apply to your situation.
	+ Barriers to equity are a major obstacle.
	+ Equity and inclusion are a top priority.
	+ Our school/district is very diverse.
* **Demographic info** (`demographic_info`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes the organization or institution you represent.
	+ Schools
	+ Districts
	+ Organizations
	+ Other
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Submit** (`submit`, date, optional): This field is not used in the current form, but it seems to be a leftover field.
* **Submit time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): This field is not used in the current form, but it seems to be a leftover field.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information or feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Select when you would like to be followed up.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, select_one, optional): Select who the contact person should be.
	+ School Principal/Assistant Principal
	+ School District Superintendent/Assistant Superintendent
	+ District Office/Person
	+ Other
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide the contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide the contact phone number.
