# subscription_box_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from subscribers on their overall experience with subscription boxes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied are you with your overall subscription box experience?" by selecting one of the options provided.
2. Choose how long you have been subscribed to this box by selecting one of the time period options.
3. Select all the options that best describe your experience with the box from the "Which of the following best describe your experience with the box?" question.
4. In the text field "What types of products or themes would you like to see more of?", type in any specific types of products or themes you would like to see more of.
5. Type your response to the question "What is one thing you would improve about your subscription box?" in the provided text field.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your overall subscription box experience?** (`text`, `select`, required): 
This question measures your level of satisfaction with your subscription box experience. Select one of the options provided to indicate your level of satisfaction.

* **How long have you been subscribed to this box?** (`text`, `select`, required): 
This question helps us understand how long you've been enjoying the benefits of your subscription box.

* **Which of the following best describe your experience with the box?** (`text`, `multiple`, required): 
Select all the options that best describe your experience with the box. You can select multiple answers if applicable.

* **What types of products or themes would you like to see more of?** (`text`, `text`, optional): 
Type in any specific types of products or themes you would like to see more of. This helps us understand what you're interested in and can improve our offerings.

* **What is one thing you would improve about your subscription box?** (`text`, `text`, optional): 
Type one thing that you think could be improved about your subscription box experience.

* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`text`, `text`, optional): 
If you have any other thoughts or ideas, feel free to share them in this text field.
