# Greenhouse Grower Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from growers on their experience with our greenhouse operations. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve on.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on each page and fill out the requested information. You can skip any questions that are not applicable to your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page serves as a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Operation Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with our operations. Options are: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Operations**: Select all the operations that apply to your experience. Options are: Yes, No.
* **Challenges**: Select all the operations that you have faced challenges with. Options are: Yes, No.
* **Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with each operation. Options are: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Operator Operations Hours**: Enter the number of hours you worked with the operator.
* **Operator Operations Value**: Enter the number of hours you felt the operator was effective in your experience.
* **Operator Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with the operator's performance. Options are: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Operations Challenges**: Select all the operations that you have faced challenges with. Options are: Yes, No.
* **Challenges Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with the operations you faced challenges with. Options are: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with the overall experience. Options are: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Suggestions**: Please provide any suggestions you have for improving our operations.
* **Operator Name**: Please enter the name of the operator who assisted you.
* **Operator Email**: Please enter the email address of the operator who assisted you.
* **Operator Phone**: Please enter the phone number of the operator who assisted you.
* **Operator Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or feedback about the operator's performance.
* **Operator Department**: Please enter the department that the operator belongs to.
Note: All fields are optional, and you can skip any questions that are not applicable to your experience.
