# Therapeutic Garden Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have visited the therapeutic garden. Your responses will help us understand the positive and negative impacts of the garden on your overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For the "How many visits" section, select the number of times you have visited the garden.
3. For the "How often" section, select the frequency with which you visit the garden.
4. For the "Well-being" sections, select a rating that best describes your feelings before and after visiting the garden.
5. For the "Why visit" section, select all reasons that apply for why you visit the garden.
6. For the "Other" fields, if you have any additional comments or reasons not listed, please provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many visits to the garden have you had? (1)**: Answer this question to tell us how many times you have visited the garden.
* **Other visits to the garden (2)**: Check all reasons that apply for why your visits are not listed above (e.g., other family/friends, other healthcare providers, etc.).
* **How old are you? (3)**: Select the age range that best describes your age.
* **How long do you visit the garden for? (4)**: Answer this question to tell us how long you typically visit the garden.
* **How often do you visit the garden? (5)**: Select the frequency with which you visit the garden.
* **Other (6)**: If your visit frequency is not listed above, select this option and provide additional details in the text field below.
* **How would you rate your overall well-being before visiting the garden? (7)**: Rate your well-being as:
	+ 1-2: Much worse
	+ 3-4: A little worse
	+ 5: No change
	+ 6-7: A little better
	+ 8-10: Much better
* **How would you rate your overall well-being after visiting the garden? (8)**: Rate your well-being as:
	+ 1-2: Much worse
	+ 3-4: A little worse
	+ 5: No change
	+ 6-7: A little better
	+ 8-10: Much better
* **Has your well-being changed after visiting the garden? (9)**: Select all reasons that apply.
* **Other (10)**: If your well-being has changed for reasons not listed above, provide additional details in the text field.
* **Why do you visit the garden? (11)**: Check all reasons that apply for why you visit the garden.
* **How long do you typically visit the garden for? (12)**: Answer this question to tell us how long you typically visit the garden.
