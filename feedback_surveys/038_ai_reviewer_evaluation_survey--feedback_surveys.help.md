# AI Reviewer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an AI reviewer, specifically focusing on its accuracy, relevance, clarity of feedback, tone, and usefulness. This feedback will help stakeholders understand areas for improvement for the AI reviewer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the AI reviewer version or model name you are evaluating.
2.  Choose the date the AI review was performed.
3.  Rate how accurate the AI review was on a scale of 1-5.
4.  Select any errors observed during the review (factual errors, logical inconsistencies, etc.).
5.  Rate how well the AI captured the main points of the review.
6.  Rate how clear the AI feedback was on a scale of 1-5.
7.  Choose the tone of the AI reviewer (professional and helpful, neutral, etc.).
8.  Rate how useful the review was for your improvements.
9.  Choose how likely you are to trust this AI reviewer for future evaluations.
10.  Provide any specific suggestions for improving the AI reviewer (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **AI Reviewer Version or Model Name** (`ai_model_name`): Enter the specific version or name of the AI reviewer you are evaluating.
*   **Date of Review** (`review_date`): Select the date the AI review was performed.
*   **Overall Accuracy of the AI Review** (`overall_accuracy`): Rate how accurate the AI review was on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Types of Errors Observed** (`error_types`): Select all that apply if you noticed any issues during the review.
*   **Did the AI Capture the Main Points Correctly?** (`captured_main_points`): Rate how well the AI captured the main points of the review.
*   **Clarity of AI Feedback** (`clarity_of_feedback`): Rate how clear the AI feedback was on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Tone of the AI Reviewer** (`ai_tone`): Choose the tone of the AI reviewer (professional and helpful, neutral, etc.).
*   **How Useful Was the Review for Your Improvements?** (`review_usefulness`): Rate how useful the review was for your improvements.
*   **Would You Trust This AI Reviewer for Future Evaluations?** (`trust_for_future`): Choose how likely you are to trust this AI reviewer for future evaluations.
*   **Specific Suggestions for Improving the AI Reviewer** (`improvement_suggestions`): Provide any specific suggestions for improving the AI reviewer (optional).
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address (optional).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact number (optional).

**Tips**

*   Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback on the AI reviewer's performance.
*   If you are unsure about any of the ratings or selections, please choose the closest option that reflects your opinion.
*   If you have any specific suggestions for improving the AI reviewer, please provide them in the "Specific Suggestions for Improving the AI Reviewer" field.
