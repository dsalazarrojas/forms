## Vendor Cooperation Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors about their experience with our company. Your input will help us improve our collaboration and communication with you and other partners.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Main Page" button to proceed to the form's main section.
2. Answer each question in a way that best describes your experience.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your level of satisfaction with the current partnership? (Very High, High, Medium, Low, Very Low)**: This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the current partnership.
* **What is your level of satisfaction with communication? (Effective, Neutral, Ineffective)**: This question gauges your satisfaction with the level of communication between our teams.
* **How would you rate the level of collaboration with our team? (Strong, Moderate, None)**: This question evaluates the level of collaboration between our teams.
* **What suggestions do you have for our team?**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or comments you have for our team.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Attachment**: If you have any attachment or document to provide with your feedback, please upload it here.
* **Submission confirmation**: After submitting the form, you'll receive a confirmation email with the details of your feedback.
* **Back**: If you want to go back to the main page and make changes, click on this button.

Remember, your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your honesty and openness. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
