# Marina Scheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Scheduling Survey is designed to gather information from marina users about their scheduling preferences, amenities, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select your role as a "Boater" or "Visitor" from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the marina services you are interested in, such as "Boat Wash Station" or "Parking".
4. Select the marina amenities you would like to have, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
5. Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
6. Complete the "Marina Scheduling" section with a date.
7. Enter the time of your visit.
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Boater/Visitor Preferences** (`boater_preferences`): Select your role as a "Boater" or "Visitor" to help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Marina Access Preferences** (`marina_access_preferences`): Choose the marina services you are interested in, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Marina Services** (`marina_services`): Select the marina amenities you would like to have, such as "Boat Wash Station", "Parking", etc.
* **Marina Amenities** (`marina_amenities`): Choose the marina amenities you would like to have, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Marina Safety** (`marina_safety`): Select the marina safety options you would like to have, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Marina Comments** (`marina_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the marina.
* **Boater Comments** (`boater_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience as a boater.
* **Marina Scheduling** (`marina_scheduling`): Enter the date of your visit to the marina.
* **Marina Scheduling Time** (`marina_scheduling_time`): Enter the time of your visit to the marina.
* **Marina Email** (`marina_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Marina Phone** (`marina_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Marina Address** (`marina_address`): Enter your address.
* **Marina City** (`marina_city`): Enter the city of your location.
* **Marina State** (`marina_state`): Enter the state of your location.
* **Marina Zip** (`marina_zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Marina Latitude** (`marina_latitude`): Enter the latitude of your location.
* **Marina Longitude** (`marina_longitude`): Enter the longitude of your location.
* **Marina Altitude** (`marina_altitude`): Enter the altitude of your location.
* **Boater Altitude** (`boater_altitude`): Enter the altitude of your boat.
* **Marina Rating** (`marina_rating`): Rate your experience with the marina.
* **Marina Survey Completed** (`marina_survey_completed`): Enter the date this survey was completed.
* **Boater Survey Completed** (`boater_survey_completed`): Enter the date this survey was completed.
* **Marina Submission** (`marina_submission`): Enter any additional information or feedback.
