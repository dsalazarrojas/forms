# Customer Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our service. By completing this survey, you will be helping us improve our service quality and ensure that we are meeting your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go through each page and answer the questions or select options as instructed.
2. Fill in all the required fields, if any.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have in the "Feedback Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page: Intro
* **Intro** (`intro_page`, text, required: false): This is the introduction page that explains the purpose of the form and encourages the customer to complete it.

### Page: Service
* **Service** (`service_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of the service provided. Select "Yes" if the service met your expectations, "No" if it did not, and "Other" if you have a different opinion.
* **Communication** (`communication_skills`, number, required: false): Rate the communication skills of the service provider. You can enter a numerical value to express your opinion.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the resolution of the service issue. Select "Yes" if the issue was resolved, "No" if it was not, and "Other" if you have a different opinion.

### Page: Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to contact us:
	+ **Phone**: We will contact you via phone.
	+ **Email**: We will contact you via email.
	+ **Online**: We will contact you online.
	+ **Other**: Please specify your preferred contact method.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Customer Online** (`customer_online`, select_one, required: false): If you would like to be contacted online, choose this option.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): If you would like to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Customer Online** (`customer_online`, select_one, required: false): If you would like to be contacted online, choose this option.
* **Customer Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but please provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our service.
