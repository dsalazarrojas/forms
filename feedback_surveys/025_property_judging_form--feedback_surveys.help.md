# property_judging_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and feedback on properties, including location, contact details, property type, status, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the property location in the "property_location" field.
2.  Provide your contact information in the "contact_name", "contact_number", and "contact_email" fields.
3.  Select the type of property from the "property_type" dropdown list.
4.  Choose the current status of the property from the "property_status" dropdown list.
5.  Enter the property value and any notable features in the "property_value", "property_features", and "property_notes" fields.
6.  Provide any additional feedback or recommendation on the property in the "property_score" and "property_recommendation" fields.
7.  Enter the property address, city, and zip in the "property_address", "property_city", and "property_zip" fields.
8.  Upload an image of the property in the "property_image" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_location**: Enter the address of the property, including city and state.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name and title.
* **contact_number**: Enter your phone number.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address.
* **property_type**: Select the type of property from the dropdown list (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
* **property_status**: Choose the current status of the property from the dropdown list (Active, Sold, Vacant, or Under Contract).
* **property_value**: Enter the value of the property.
* **property_features**: Enter any notable features of the property.
* **property_notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **property_score**: Enter a score for the property (optional).
* **property_recommendation**: Select any recommendations for the property (Highly Recommended, Recommended, Not Recommended, or Not Sure).
* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **property_city**: Enter the city of the property.
* **property_state**: Enter the state of the property.
* **property_zip**: Enter the zip code of the property.
* **property_image**: Upload an image of the property, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If you are unsure about the type or status of the property, please consult with the property agent or owner.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "property_notes" field.
