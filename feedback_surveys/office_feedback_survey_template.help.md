# office_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the current office environment and facilities to help identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for the "Which of the following best describe your current office experience?" question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your overall office environment?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the office environment.
* **How often do you typically work from the office?** (`Daily`, `3-4 times a week`, `1-2 times a week`, `Rarely`, `Never`): Indicate how frequently you visit or work from the office.
* **Which of the following best describe your current office experience?** (`Productive`, `Collaborative`, `Comfortable`, `Distracting`, `Lacking amenities`, `Well-equipped`): Select all that apply to describe your office experience.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the office space?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, `Very poor`): Rate the cleanliness of the office space from excellent to very poor.
* **What is your primary mode of commute to the office?** (`Car`, `Public transport`, `Walk`, `Cycle`, `Other`): Choose your main way of traveling to the office.
* **What one thing would you most improve about the office environment?** (text): Share your thoughts on how you think the office could be improved.
* **Additional comments or suggestions?** (text): Feel free to add any other comments or suggestions you might have about the office environment.
