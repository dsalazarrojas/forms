# Concept Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a concept or idea, providing an opportunity to assess its strengths, weaknesses, and potential areas for improvement. The survey aims to understand how well the concept aligns with the respondent's expectations and identify potential blind spots or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your survey ID, which is a unique identifier for this particular survey.
2. Enter the date of the survey you are providing feedback on.
3. Provide your name and email address (optional).
4. Indicate your role in relation to the concept (Customer, Employee, Stakeholder, Partner, Investor, or Other).
5. Enter the name of the concept being reviewed.
6. Select the category of the concept (Product Idea, Service Idea, Feature Enhancement, Marketing Campaign, Process Improvement, Business Model, or Other).
7. Rate your first impression of the concept (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
8. Rate how clear the concept is (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, or Very Unclear).
9. Rate the perceived value of the concept (Very High, High, Moderate, Low, or No Value).
10. Rate how unique the concept is (Very Unique, Unique, Somewhat Unique, Common, or Not Unique).
11. Rate how feasible the concept is to implement (Very Feasible, Feasible, Somewhat Feasible, Difficult, or Not Feasible).
12. Rate the market appeal of the concept (Very High, High, Moderate, Low, or No Appeal).
13. Rate how likely you would use this concept (Definitely Would Use, Probably Would Use, Might Use, Probably Would Not Use, or Definitely Would Not Use).
14. Rate how likely you would recommend this concept (Definitely Would Recommend, Probably Would Recommend, Might Recommend, Probably Would Not Recommend, or Definitely Would Not Recommend).
15. List any strengths of the concept.
16. List any weaknesses of the concept.
17. List any concerns or risks associated with the concept (optional).
18. Provide suggestions for improvement.
19. Identify the target audience for the concept (optional).
20. Provide feedback on a fair price for the concept (optional).
21. Compare the concept to alternatives (optional).
22. Provide an overall rating for the concept (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
23. Add any additional comments about the concept.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter a unique identifier for this survey, which will help us identify your feedback.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the survey you are providing feedback on.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, **optional**): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **optional**): This is an optional field. If you'd like to provide feedback, please feel free to do so.
* **Respondent Type** (`respondent_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your role in relation to the concept:
	+ Customer
	+ Employee
	+ Stakeholder
	+ Partner
	+ Investor
	+ Other
* **Concept Name** (`concept_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter the name of the concept being reviewed.
* **Concept Category** (`concept_category`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select the category of the concept:
	+ Product Idea
	+ Service Idea
	+ Feature Enhancement
	+ Marketing Campaign
	+ Process Improvement
	+ Business Model
	+ Other
* **First Impression** (`first_impression`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate your first impression of the concept:
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 1 - Very Poor
* **Concept Clarity** (`concept_clarity`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate how clear the concept is:
	+ 5 - Very Clear
	+ 4 - Clear
	+ 3 - Somewhat Clear
	+ 2 - Unclear
	+ 1 - Very Unclear
* **Perceived Value** (`perceived_value`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the perceived value of the concept:
	+ 5 - Very High
	+ 4 - High
	+ 3 - Moderate
	+ 2 - Low
	+ 1 - No Value
* **Uniqueness Rating** (`uniqueness_rating`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate how unique the concept is:
	+ 5 - Very Unique
	+ 4 - Unique
	+ 3 - Somewhat Unique
	+ 2 - Common
	+ 1 - Not Unique
* **Feasibility Rating** (`feasibility_rating`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate how feasible the concept is to implement:
	+ 5 - Very Feasible
	+ 4 - Feasible
	+ 3 - Somewhat Feasible
	+ 2 - Difficult
	+ 1 - Not Feasible
* **Market Appeal** (`market_appeal`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the market appeal of the concept:
	+ 5 - Very High
	+ 4 - High
	+ 3 - Moderate
	+ 2 - Low
	+ 1 - No Appeal
* **Likelihood to Use** (`likelihood_to_use`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate how likely you would use this concept:
	+ 5 - Definitely Would Use
	+ 4 - Probably Would Use
	+ 3 - Might Use
	+ 2 - Probably Would Not Use
	+ 1 - Definitely Would Not Use
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate how likely you would recommend this concept:
	+ 5 - Definitely Would Recommend
	+ 4 - Probably Would Recommend
	+ 3 - Might Recommend
	+ 2 - Probably Would Not Recommend
	+ 1 - Definitely Would Not Recommend
* **Concept Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, **required**): List any strengths of the concept.
* **Concept Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, `text`, **required**): List any weaknesses of the concept.
* **Concerns or Risks** (`concerns`, `text`, **optional**): List any concerns or risks associated with the concept (if any).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, **required**): Provide suggestions for improvement of the concept.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any feedback on who the target audience for the concept is.
* **Pricing Feedback** (`pricing_feedback`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any feedback on a fair price for the concept.
* **Competitive Comparison** (`competitive_comparison`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any feedback on how the concept compares to alternatives.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate your overall impression of the concept:
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 1 - Very Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional comments about the concept.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, try your best to answer based on your initial impression.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" section.
