# Social Exposure Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback data from users about their social exposure experience on a website or application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each question.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, text, required/optional): This question is about your current social exposure experience. Please describe your experience.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, text, required/optional): What do you think are the main reasons for your current social exposure experience?
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, text, required/optional): How did you feel during your recent social exposure experience?
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, text, required/optional): How often do you engage in social exposure activities?
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, text, required/optional): What are your primary sources of social exposure (e.g., social media, online forums, etc.)?
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, text, required/optional): How much time do you spend on social exposure activities?
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, text, required/optional): How do you perceive the impact of social exposure on your mental health?
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, text, required/optional): How do you think your social exposure affects your relationships with others?
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, text, required/optional): Have you experienced any negative effects from social exposure (e.g., FOMO, anxiety, etc.)?
* **Question 10** (`question_10`, text, required/optional): If yes, describe the negative effects you've experienced.
* **Question 11** (`question_11`, text, required/optional): Have you noticed any positive effects from social exposure (e.g., connections, inspiration, etc.)?
* **Question 12** (`question_12`, text, required/optional): If yes, describe the positive effects you've experienced.
* **Question 13** (`question_13`, text, required/optional): How often do you use social media for social exposure?
* **Question 14** (`question_14`, text, required/optional): How often do you use online forums for social exposure?
* **Question 15** (`question_15`, text, required/optional): How often do you engage in online communities for social exposure?
* **Question 16** (`question_16`, text, required/optional): How often do you attend social events for social exposure?
* **Question 17** (`question_17`, text, required/optional): How often do you participate in social gatherings for social exposure?
* **Question 18** (`question_18`, text, required/optional): How often do you engage in video conferencing for social exposure?
* **Question 19** (`question_19`, text, required/optional): How often do you use social media for social exposure during work hours?
* **Question 20** (`question_20`, text, required/optional): How often do you use social media for social exposure during leisure time?
* **Question 21** (`question_21`, text, required/optional): How often do you check social media for social exposure updates?
* **Question 22** (`question_22`, text, required/optional): Do you prefer using social exposure activities during the following time of day: morning, afternoon, evening, or any time?
* **Question 23** (`question_23`, text, required/optional): What do you think is the most challenging aspect of social exposure?
* **Question 24** (`question_24`, text, required/optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving social exposure on our website/application?
* **End of Survey** (`survey_end`, note, required/optional): You have completed the social exposure impact survey. Please provide your contact email if you'd like to receive any updates or follow-up questions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Please provide your email address to contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.
