# Lifelong Learning Motivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lifelong Learning Motivation Survey is designed to assess an individual's motivation levels and preferences when it comes to learning new things. This survey aims to understand what drives individuals to engage in lifelong learning, what motivates them to learn new skills, and what challenges they face when doing so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, selecting the options that best fit your thoughts and feelings about learning new things.
2. Make sure to fill out each page carefully, as some questions are required to ensure we get a complete picture of your motivation levels and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What motivates you to learn new things (page 1)
* **Always** (Always)
	+ Select this option if you are always motivated to learn new things.

### How important is learning new things (page 2)
* **Very important** (Very important)
	+ Select this option if you consider learning new things as very crucial for your personal and professional growth.

### Current level of motivation (page 4)
* Enter your level of motivation on a scale of 1-10.
	+ This question helps us understand where you stand in terms of motivation, whether you feel highly motivated (1-5) or not (6-10).

### What holds you back from learning new things (page 5)
* **Lack of time** (Lack of time)
	+ Select this option if a lack of time is the primary reason you struggle to learn new things.

### How do you feel about learning new things (page 6)
* **Excited** (Excited)
	+ Select this option if you generally feel enthusiastic and eager to learn new things.

### What motivates you the most (page 7)
* **Curiosity** (Curiosity)
	+ Select this option if curiosity is the driving force behind your desire to learn new things.

### How do you feel about challenges when learning new things (page 8)
* **Encouraged** (Encouraged)
	+ Select this option if you feel supported and motivated by the presence of challenges when learning new things.

### What do you enjoy learning the most (page 10)
* **New skills** (New skills)
	+ Select this option if you enjoy acquiring new skills when learning new things.

### How do you feel about time constraints when learning new things (page 11)
* **Flexible** (Flexible)
	+ Select this option if you feel you have the flexibility to learn new things at your own pace.

### What is your motivation level (on a scale of 1-10) (page 12)
* Enter your level of motivation on a scale of 1-10.
	+ This question helps us understand your self-assessed motivation level.
