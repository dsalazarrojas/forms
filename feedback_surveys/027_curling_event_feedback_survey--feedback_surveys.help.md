# Curling Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction from attendees of a curling event. Your responses will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have attended the event, select 'Yes' in the 'Event Attended' question.
3. If you have not attended the event, select 'No' in the 'Event Attended' question.
4. Choose your preferred team by selecting all the teams you support.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
6. Select the area you would like us to improve in the 'What can we improve?' field.
7. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the 'Any additional comments?' field.
8. Fill in your email address and phone number for us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was the event?** (`curling_event_feedback_survey`): Describe your experience with the event in a few sentences.
- **Rating (out of 10)** (`event_rating`): Rate your overall experience with the event on a scale of 1-10.
- **Favorite part** (`favorite_part`): Select one of the options to indicate your favorite part of the event.
  - The ice resurfacing machine
  - The curling stones
  - The competition
- **How many times have you attended a curling event?** (`curling_experience`): Select one of the options to indicate how many times you have attended a curling event.
  - Never
  - Less than 5 times
  - 5-10 times
  - 10+ times
- **Which team do you support?** (`curling_team`): Select all the teams you support.
  - Team A
  - Team B
  - Team C
  - Team D
- **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
- **What can we improve?** (`improvement_area`): Select one of the options to indicate the area you would like us to improve.
- **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Event attended** (`event_attended`): Select 'Yes' if you have attended the event, 'No' otherwise.
- **Attended with** (`attended_with`): Select all the people you attended with.
  - Family
  - Friends
  - Club
  - Solo
- **Attended other curling events?** (`attended_other_events`): Select 'Yes' if you have attended other curling events, 'No' otherwise.
