# core-medical-news-feedback-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from medical professionals on various medical-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select your medical field of expertise.
2.  Provide feedback on medical news you've recently encountered.
3.  Answer any optional questions, such as "Feedback Question 1" and "Feedback Question 2."
4.  Select your preferred update cadence (optional).
5.  Evaluate the content quality and relevance of the medical news you've seen.
6.  Share your email address (optional).
7.  Enter your phone number (optional).
8.  Choose the date and time you completed this form (optional).
9.  Add any additional comments or notes (optional).
10. Select multiple or single medical fields for various options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical Field 1**: Select your primary field of expertise.
    *   This is the field you primarily work in or have experience with.
*   **Medical News**: Provide feedback on a recent medical news article.
    *   Share your thoughts on the news article's quality and relevance.
*   **Feedback Question 1**: Answer this question to share additional feedback.
    *   This is an opportunity to add any further comments or suggestions.
*   **Update Cadence**: Select how often you'd like to receive updates (optional).
    *   This is how often you'd like to receive information on medical news.
*   **Content Quality**: Evaluate the quality of the medical news you've seen.
    *   This will help us assess the relevance and accuracy of the content.
*   **Relevance**: Assess the relevance of the medical news you've seen.
    *   This will help us refine our content to better match your needs.
*   **Email**: Share your email address (optional).
    *   This will allow us to contact you for further feedback or questions.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
    *   This is an optional way for us to contact you, especially if we need to follow up on a specific question or concern.
*   **Date**: Choose the date you completed this form.
    *   This will help us keep track of when you've completed the survey.
*   **Time**: Select the time you completed this form.
    *   This will help us keep a record of when you finished the survey.
*   **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes.
    *   This is a space for you to share any extra information that might be helpful for us.
*   **Feedback**: Share any overall feedback.
    *   This is your opportunity to give us feedback on the survey itself and any other comments you have.
