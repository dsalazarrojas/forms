# Organizational Culture Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Organizational Culture Alignment Survey is designed to collect data on the current state of your organization's culture and identify areas for improvement. It's a crucial tool for leaders and managers to gauge the alignment between your organization's values, goals, and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the options that best describe your perspective on the current state of your organization.
3. Ensure you're answering honestly and accurately.
4. You can choose more than one option for questions that allow multiple selections.
