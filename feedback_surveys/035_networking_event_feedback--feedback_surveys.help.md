# Networking Event Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees after a networking event. The feedback collected will help event organizers evaluate the event's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information: "Event Name" and "Event Date" are optional, but if you want to provide feedback on a specific event, please fill them in.
2. Describe the positive connections you made at the event in the "What were the positive connections you made at this event?" field.
3. Select all the outcomes you attribute to this event from the list in the "What outcome(s) do you attribute to this event?" field.
4. Choose how satisfied you were with the event from the list in the "How satisfied were you with this event?" field.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the "Any suggestions to improve this event?" field.
6. Add any additional feedback about your experience at this event in the "Any additional feedback about your experience at this event?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **What were the positive connections you made at this event?** (`connection_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the positive connections you made at the event, such as meeting new contacts or partners.
* **What outcome(s) do you attribute to this event?** (`event_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the outcomes you attribute to this event, such as "Connection to a potential business partner" or "Connection to an existing business contact".
* **How satisfied were you with this event?** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you were with the event, from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Any suggestions to improve this event?** (`suggest_improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in this field.
* **Any additional feedback about your experience at this event?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback about your experience at this event.
