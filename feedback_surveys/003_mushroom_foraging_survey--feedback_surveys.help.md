# Mushroom Foraging Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mushroom Foraging Survey is designed to gather information from individuals who practice mushroom foraging, a hobby that involves searching for and harvesting wild mushrooms. This survey aims to understand various aspects of foraging experiences, locations, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide detailed answers for text-based questions.
4. If you have any additional comments or information, use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Foraging Experience** (`foraging_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your experience with mushroom foraging, including your level of experience, any notable successes or failures, and any memorable moments.
* **Locations Visited** (`locations_visited`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where you have foraged for mushrooms.
	+ Options: Field 1, Field 2, Field 3
* **Mushroom Types** (`mushroom_types`, select_one, optional): Select the type(s) of mushrooms you are familiar with.
	+ Options: Field 1, Field 2, Field 3
* **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, text, optional): Describe your approach to safety practices when foraging for mushrooms, including any precautions or guidelines you follow.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share, provide them here.
* **Foraging Locations** (`foraging_locations`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where you have foraged for mushrooms.
	+ Options: Field 1, Field 2, Field 3
* **Foraging Frequency** (`foraging_frequency`, text, optional): Describe how frequently you forage for mushrooms, including how often you go on foraging trips and any specific times of year or day.
* **Other Locations** (`other_mushroom_locations`, text, optional): If you have any other locations where you have foraged for mushrooms, describe them here.
* **Other Mushroom Types** (`other_mushroom_types`, text, optional): If you have encountered any other types of mushrooms, describe them here.
