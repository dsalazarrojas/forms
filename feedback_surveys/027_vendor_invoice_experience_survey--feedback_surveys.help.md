# Vendor Invoice Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors on their experience with the invoice process. The goal is to gather information that can be used to improve the vendor invoice experience and ultimately enhance the vendor relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options for each question to provide your feedback.
2. Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience in the "Notes" field.
3. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Invoicing Experience** (`vendor_invoicing_experience`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can describe your overall experience with the invoicing process. Please provide a brief summary of your thoughts.
* **Invoice Process** (`invoice_process`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to rate your experience with the invoice process. Select "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", "Bad", or "Very Bad" to provide your feedback.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is for you to select the payment terms you use (e.g., Net 30, Net 60, Net 90, On receipt).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to select how you typically pay for your invoices (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Online Payment, Other).
* **Payment Terms Date** (`payment_terms_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to provide the payment terms date, if applicable.
* **Payment Terms Time** (`payment_terms_time`, `time`, optional): This field is for you to provide the payment terms time, if applicable.
* **Vendor Contact Info** (`vendor_contact_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional vendor contact information.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, `email`, optional): This field is for you to provide your vendor email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide your vendor phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the invoicing process.
