# European Union Institutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about the European Union institutions. Your participation will help us understand your perception of these institutions and identify areas that need improvement. Your responses will be kept anonymous to ensure a safe and open environment for feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer the questions according to your genuine feelings and thoughts.
2. Choose your answers carefully, as they will help us understand your perspective.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Do you trust the European Union institutions?** (`eu_trust`, select_one, required: false): 
    This question asks about your general trust towards the European Union institutions. You can choose between 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Which of the following EU institutions do you trust?** (`eu_bodies`, select_multiple, required: false): 
    This question asks you to select from the list of European Union institutions that you trust. You can choose multiple options.
* **What are your reasons for trusting or not trusting the European Union?** (`eu_reasons`, text, required: false): 
    This text field is for you to explain why you trust or do not trust the European Union. Be as specific as possible.
* **Which institution has earned your trust?** (`eu_trust_earned`, select_one, required: false): 
    This question asks about the institution that you trust the most. You can choose one option.
* **Other comments about the European Union institutions?** (`other_comments`, text, required: false): 
    This field is for any additional comments or feedback you might have about the European Union institutions.
* **How do you think the EU institutions could improve?** (`eu_improve`, text, required: false): 
    This field is for suggestions on how you think the European Union institutions could be improved.
* **Other comments** (`eu_comments`, text, required: false): 
    This field is for any other comments or feedback you might have.
* **Your suggestions** (`eu_suggestions`, text, required: false): 
    This field is for any suggestions you might have for the European Union institutions.
* **Eu Improvement** (`eu_improvement`, text, required: false): 
    This field is for suggestions on how you think the European Union institutions could be improved. Please note that this question is a duplicate and is not necessary to fill in.
* **Eu Improvement 2** (`eu_improvement_2`, text, required: false): 
    This field is also a duplicate of the previous question.
* **Eu Improvement 3** (`eu_improvement_3`, text, required: false): 
    Another duplicate question.
* **Eu Improvement 4** (`eu_improvement_4`, text, required: false): 
    Another duplicate question.

Note: Please do not fill out the duplicate questions.
