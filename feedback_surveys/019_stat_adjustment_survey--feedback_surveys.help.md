# Stat Adjustment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Stat Adjustment Survey form is used to collect feedback from players and game administrators about the balance and stats of games in the platform. This form is designed to gather information about a player's experience and help game developers improve the game.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the correct options for each select fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
