# Hy Flex Learning Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide helps you complete the Hy Flex Learning Feedback Survey form efficiently and accurately. Please read this guide carefully before filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your course code (required).
2. Choose the section number (required).
3. Answer the three questions about your experience with the course (question 1, question 2, and question 3).
4. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
5. Evaluate the course overall (select one option: "Yes" or "No").
6. Provide feedback on the instructor's performance (select one or more options: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor").
7. Enter your email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hy Flex Learning Feedback Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your feedback, if desired.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required): Enter the code for the course you took, as provided by the course administrators.
* **Section Number** (`section_number`, number, required): Select the number of the section you took, as assigned by the course instructors.
* **What did you like most about this course?** (`question_1`, text, required): Describe what you enjoyed about the course. Be specific and provide details that will help us improve the course.
* **What did you dislike most about this course?** (`question_2`, text, required): Describe what you disliked about the course. Be specific and provide details that will help us improve the course.
* **What did you learn in this course that you can apply in your life?** (`question_3`, text, optional): Share how the course helped you in your life outside of class. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand the practical impact of the course.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any further feedback, suggestions, or comments, type them here. This field is optional, but it's a great way to provide more context.
* **Course Evaluation** (`course_evaluation`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you would recommend the course to others, or "No" if you wouldn't. This helps us evaluate the course's overall value.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would rate the instructor's performance: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor". This helps us understand how to improve the instructor's teaching methods.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you, if needed. This is optional, but it helps us reach out to you for follow-up questions or to send you any course updates.
