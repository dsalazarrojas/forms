# Surgical Room Monitoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from medical staff and patients about the quality of care and services provided in surgical rooms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting.
*   Read each field carefully to understand what information is required.
*   Provide honest and constructive feedback.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Surgical Room Id** (`surgical_room_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the surgical room.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient who received care in the surgical room.
*   **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, optional): Rate the performance of the surgical team. Use the rating scale provided to choose a score that best reflects your opinion.
*   **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience in the surgical room.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, email, optional): Enter your contact information (email address or phone number) so that hospital staff can reach out to you for follow-up discussions.
*   **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, optional): Select the date when you received feedback.
*   **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, time, optional): Select the time when you received feedback.
*   **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the surgical room is located.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the hospital where the surgical room is located.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and objective when providing feedback.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   Make sure to provide all required information to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
