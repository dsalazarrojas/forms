# Home Staging Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to gather feedback from customers after visiting a home that has been staged to help us improve our home staging services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You can submit your feedback at any time, but please note that duplicate feedback for the same home will not be processed.
3. If you have already submitted feedback for a home, do not fill out this form again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Rating** (`property_rating`, number, optional): Rate the property's presentation and condition. You can use a number between 1 and 5.
* **Property Rating Scale** (`property_rating_scale`, select one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Unlikely
	+ Neutral
	+ Very Likely
* **Property Rating Score** (`property_rating_score`, number, optional): If you have already completed the Property Rating, this field is not necessary to fill out. Please use this field to provide more detailed feedback on the property's condition.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments?** (`suggestions`, note, optional): If you have any suggestions or comments about the home, please share them here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Your feedback will help us improve our home staging services and provide better experiences for our customers.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us improve!
