# gear_kit_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about gear kits. It helps us understand your experience and opinions about the gear kits you have used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Provide your feedback about the gear kit in the `feedback` field.
5. Select the type of equipment you used from the `equipment_type` field.
6. Select the condition of your experience with the gear kit from the `condition` field.
7. Select your performance with the gear kit from the `performance` field.
8. Finally, select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide your feedback about the gear kit.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of equipment you used.
	+ GearKit
	+ Other
* **Condition** (`condition`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the condition of your experience with the gear kit.
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Performance** (`performance`, Select One, Required): Select your performance with the gear kit.
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Submit** (`submit`, Select One, Required): Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our gear kits.
* If you have any issues or problems with the gear kit, please describe them in the `feedback` field.
* If you have any other equipment not listed under `equipment_type`, select "Other" and describe it in the `feedback` field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback about our gear kits!
