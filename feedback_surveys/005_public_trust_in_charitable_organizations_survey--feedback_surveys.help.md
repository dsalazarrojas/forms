# Public Trust In Charitable Organizations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and input on the public trust in charitable organizations. It will help in evaluating the public trust in charitable organizations and inform decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the Nonprofit Name field with the name of the charitable organization you are providing input for.
- Provide the Email and Phone fields with the contact information of the organization.
- Choose the level of trust you have in the nonprofit organization (Low, Medium, or High).
- Select the Nonprofit Website, Nonprofit Address, and Contact Address fields if they are applicable.
- Identify the nonprofit type and size (number of employees) from the provided options.
- Choose the charitable organizations you are familiar with from the options provided.
- Answer whether you have trust in the nonprofit organization (True or False).
- If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the Feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Name (Nonprofit Name, text, required)**: Fill in the name of the charitable organization you are providing input for.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Provide the contact email of the organization.
* **Trust Level (Trust Level, select_one, required)**: Choose the level of trust you have in the nonprofit organization (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Provide the contact phone number of the organization.
* **Nonprofit Website (Nonprofit Website, text, optional)**: If the organization has a website, provide the URL.
* **Nonprofit Address (Nonprofit Address, text, optional)**: If the organization has a physical address, provide it here.
* **Contact Address (Contact Address, text, optional)**: If you have contact information for the organization, provide it here.
* **Trust Drivers (Trust Drivers, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the factors that contribute to your trust in the nonprofit organization (Governance and Transparency, Financial Accountability, Communication, Community Engagement, None of the above).
* **Contact (Contact, text, optional)**: If you have any additional contact information or comments, provide them here.
* **Feedback (Feedback, note, optional)**: Provide any feedback or comments you have about the nonprofit organization.
* **Public Trust (Public Trust, number, required)**: Provide a numerical value representing your level of trust in the nonprofit organization (1-5, where 1 means Low and 5 means High).
* **Charitable Organizations (Charitable Organizations, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the charitable organizations you are familiar with from the options provided.
* **Nonprofit Employees (Nonprofit Employees, select_multiple, required)**: Select the size of the nonprofit organization (Fewer than 10, 10-50, 51-100, 101-500, More than 501).
* **Nonprofit Type (Nonprofit Type, select_one, required)**: Choose the type of nonprofit organization (Public Benefit Organization (PBO), 501(c)(3), Other, None).
