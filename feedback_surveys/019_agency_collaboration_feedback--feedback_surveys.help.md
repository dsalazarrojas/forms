# Agency Collaboration Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their experience with agency collaborations. It's a tool to help agencies evaluate and improve their performance, and to provide valuable insights for future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the project and agency before starting.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
3. Provide honest and constructive feedback in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required)
Client name is the title you use to identify the organization or person with whom you collaborated.

### 2. **Agency name** (`agency_name`, text, required)
Agency name is the title you use to identify the external agency you collaborated with.

### 3. **Project duration** (`project_duration`, text, optional)
Project duration is the timeframe of the engagement, including start and end dates.

### 4. **Communication quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required)
Rate the clarity and responsiveness of the agency's communication. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 5. **Quality of deliverables** (`deliverables_quality`, select_one, required)
Rate the overall quality of deliverables provided by the agency. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 6. **Adherence to deadlines** (`adherence_to_deadlines`, select_one, required)
Indicate how well the agency met agreed deadlines. Choose from: Always, Mostly, Sometimes, Rarely.

### 7. **Value for money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required)
Rate the cost-effectiveness of the agency's services. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 8. **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional)
Provide practical suggestions for the agency to improve their performance and collaboration.

### 9. **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required)
Rate your overall experience with the agency. Choose from: Highly recommended, Recommended, Neutral, Not recommended.
