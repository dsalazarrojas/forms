# Event Security And Safety Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on the security and safety aspects of an event. It is intended to help identify areas of improvement and ensure that events are conducted in a secure and safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you attended (e.g. conference, workshop, seminar).
2. Choose the security measures that were taken to ensure the security of the event.
3. Choose the safety measures that were taken to ensure the safety of the event.
4. Rate the security and safety of the event on a scale of 1-5.
5. Provide any additional feedback about your event experience.
6. Enter your contact information to allow us to follow up with you if necessary.
7. Enter the event details (name, date, location, and time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of event you attended (e.g. conference, workshop, seminar).
* **Security Measures** (`security_measures`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to choose what measures were taken to ensure the security of the event. Examples include cameras, security personnel, and barriers.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to choose what measures were taken to ensure the safety of the event. Examples include first aid kits, emergency exits, and emergency contact information.
* **Event Security Rating** (`event_security_rating`, number, required: true): Rate the security of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Event Safety Rating** (`event_safety_rating`, number, required: true): Rate the safety of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional feedback about your event experience.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your name and email address.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: true): Enter your name.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: true): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: true): Enter the time of the event.
