# P2P Crypto Trading Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your P2P crypto trading experiences. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of trade you participated in (P2P, Escrow, or Hybrid).
2. Choose the type of product involved (Binance, Huobi, or FTX).
3. Rate your experience (Good, Bad, or Neutral).
4. Provide a brief description of your experience.
5. Select the security level you felt during the trade (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Choose the platforms you used (Discord, Telegram, or Reddit).
7. Select the exchange platform you used (Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken).
8. Specify the status of your trade (Completed, Failed, or Pending).
9. Enter the amount of the trade (a numerical value).
10. Enter the fees involved (a numerical value).
11. Choose the type of fees (Fixed, Percentage, or Variable).
12. Select the currency of the fees (USD, EUR, or BTC).
13. Specify the support methods you used (Phone, Email, or Live Chat).
14. Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
15. Rate your experience again to help us better understand your satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade** (`trade_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of your trade experience.
* **Rate** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience as Good, Bad, or Neutral.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Type of Trade** (`trade_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of trade you participated in (P2P, Escrow, or Hybrid).
* **Trade Time** (`trade_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your trade (in 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00, 14:30).
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Security** (`security`, select_one, optional): Rate the security level of your experience (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Product** (`product`, select_multiple, optional): Select the product(s) involved in your trade (Binance, Huobi, or FTX).
* **Community** (`community`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the communities you participated in (Discord, Telegram, or Reddit).
* **Platform** (`platform`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platform(s) you used (Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken).
* **Status** (`trade_status`, select_one, optional): Specify the status of your trade (Completed, Failed, or Pending).
* **Amount** (`trade_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of your trade (a numerical value).
* **Fees** (`trade_fees`, number, optional): Enter the fees involved in your trade (a numerical value).
* **Fees Type** (`trade_fees_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of fees (Fixed, Percentage, or Variable).
* **Fees Currency** (`trade_fees_currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency of the fees (USD, EUR, or BTC).
* **Support** (`support`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the support methods you used (Phone, Email, or Live Chat).
* **Notes** (`trade_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Rating 1** (`trade_rating_1`, number, optional): Rate your experience again to help us better understand your satisfaction.
* **Rating 2** (`trade_rating_2`, number, optional): Rate your experience again to help us better understand your satisfaction.
* **Rating 3** (`trade_rating_3`, number, optional): Rate your experience again to help us better understand your satisfaction.
