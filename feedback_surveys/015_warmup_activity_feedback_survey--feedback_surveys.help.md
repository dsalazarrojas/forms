# Warmup Activity Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants after a warmup activity. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, and we'll use this information to make future sessions more enjoyable for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. You can skip questions that are not required or are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warmup Survey** (`warmup_survey_1`, note, required: false): This is a placeholder page that serves as a transition to the next section.
* **How did you feel about the session?** (`warmup_survey_2`, text, required: false): Describe how you felt during the session. Be as specific as possible.
* **What did you enjoy most about the session?** (`warmup_survey_3`, text, required: false): Think about what parts of the session stood out to you and what you appreciated the most.
* **What would you improve?** (`warmup_survey_4`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for making future sessions better.
* **How likely are you to recommend this activity?** (`warmup_survey_5`, select_one, required: false): Select how likely you are to recommend this activity to others.
* **Do you want to participate again in a warmup session?** (`warmup_survey_6`, select_one, required: false): Let us know if you'd like to participate in another warmup session in the future.
