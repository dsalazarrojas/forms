# Customer Satisfaction Survey Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Survey Verification Form is a tool to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our company. This form is not a survey in the classical sense but rather a verification form to ensure that the customer's information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Select the Date you took the survey in the field labeled "Date Survey".
6. Select the Time you took the survey in the field labeled "Time Survey".
7. Choose one option from the field labeled "Select One".
8. Select multiple options from the field labeled "Select Multiple".
9. Enter any additional notes in the field labeled "Notes".
10. Rate your satisfaction with our company on a scale of 1-5 in the field labeled "Customer Satisfaction".
11. Rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5 in the field labeled "Rating".
12. Enter a decimal rating (e.g., 3.5) in the field labeled "Decimal Rating".
13. Enter your Date of Birth in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
14. Enter your Time of Birth in the field labeled "Time of Birth".
15. Enter your Address in the field labeled "Address".
16. Enter your City in the field labeled "City".
17. Enter your State in the field labeled "State".
18. Enter your Zip Code in the field labeled "Zip Code".
19. Enter your Country in the field labeled "Country".
20. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you would like to receive communications from us.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Survey** (`date_survey`, `date`, required): Select the date you took the survey.
* **Time Survey** (`time_survey`, `time`, required): Select the time you took the survey.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Notes** (`note`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes about your experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `text`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our company on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your Date of Birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required): Enter your Time of Birth.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your City.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your State.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your Zip Code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your Country.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to complete the form.
