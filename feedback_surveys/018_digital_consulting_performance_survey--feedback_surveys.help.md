# Digital Consulting Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on our digital consulting services. Your input will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through each page of the form, and review each question carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your honest feedback and experience with our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Rating** (`project_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the project, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of our services, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Provide a general rating for your overall experience with our digital consulting services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about our services.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any specific comments about a particular project or service, please mention it when providing feedback.
* You can skip any question if you don't have a comment or rating for that specific area.
