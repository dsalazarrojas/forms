# Academic Recognition Fairness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Recognition Fairness Survey is designed to collect feedback from individuals within the institution on the fairness of recognition processes. This survey aims to gather information on the respondent's perception of fairness, their role within the institution, and their experiences with recognition processes. The gathered feedback will help identify areas where recognition processes may be unfair and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully, as each page corresponds to a different section of the survey.
2. Select your role within the institution from the list provided in the "Role within institution" field.
3. Rate your perception of fairness in recognition processes using the options provided in the "Perception of fairness in recognition" field.
4. If you have examples of unfair practices observed, describe them in the "Examples of unfair practices observed" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the impact of perceived unfairness on morale in the "Impact of perceived unfairness on morale" field.
6. Offer recommendations for improving fairness in recognition processes in the "Recommendations to improve fairness" field.
7. Indicate your willingness to participate in fairness review groups in the "Willingness to participate" field.
8. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address in the "Contact email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Role within institution
* **Role within institution** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the institution from the options provided. This will help us understand your perspective on recognition processes.

### Perception of fairness in recognition
* **Perception of fairness in recognition** (`perception_of_fairness`, select_one, required): Rate how fair recognition processes are. This will help us understand your perception of fairness in recognition processes.

### Examples of unfair practices observed
* **Examples of unfair practices observed** (`examples_of_unfairness`, text, required): Describe incidents or patterns of unfair practices you have observed. Please provide specific examples, if possible.

### Impact of perceived unfairness on morale
* **Impact of perceived unfairness on morale** (`impact_on_morale`, text, required): Briefly describe the effects of perceived unfairness on morale within your institution. This will help us understand the impact of fairness on employee morale.

### Recommendations to improve fairness
* **Recommendations to improve fairness** (`recommendations`, text, required): Provide practical suggestions for improving fairness in recognition processes. This will help us understand potential solutions to address unfairness.

### Willingness to participate
* **Willingness to participate** (`willingness_to_participate`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to participate in fairness review groups. This will help us understand your interest in fairness review processes.

### Contact email
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up. This is optional, but it will help us stay in touch with you regarding your feedback.
