# Humanization Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Humanization Specialist Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users regarding their experience with Humanization Specialists. This form helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of our services, allowing us to improve and refine our approach to providing high-quality support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have understood its purpose.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Fill in the required fields. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page**: This is a blank page where you can provide any additional comments or information not covered in the other fields.

### Specialist Info
* **Specialist Info**: Please provide some basic information about the specialist you are providing feedback on, such as their name, title, or department. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.

### Feedback Details
* **Feedback Details**: This is the main section for providing detailed feedback on your experience with the specialist. Please be specific about what you liked or disliked, what worked well or didn't work well, and any suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them to the First Page field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
