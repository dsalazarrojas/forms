# Post Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Training Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from trainees after completing a training session. This feedback is used to help us improve our training programs and provide better services to our trainees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers to the required fields as they are marked.
2. Fill in the "Training Session ID" field with the ID of the training session you attended.
3. Select the correct rating scale for your overall rating and for each rating scale question.
4. Provide your feedback and suggestions in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session ID** (`training_session_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the training session you attended.
* **Trainers** (`trainer_id`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the trainers who were present during the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the day when the training session took place.
* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a trainee.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide constructive and specific feedback about your experience with the training session.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Provide any suggestions or ideas for future training sessions.
* **Trainee Rating** (`trainee_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Trainers Feedback** (`trainer_feedback`, `text`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the trainers' performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Rating 2** (`trainee_rating_2`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session again.
* **Trainers Feedback 2** (`trainer_feedback_2`, `text`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the trainers' performance again.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Session Satisfaction** (`session_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate how satisfied you are with the session.
* **Trainer Attentiveness** (`trainer_attentiveness`, `number`, required): Rate how attentive the trainers were during the session.
* **Trainee Satisfaction** (`trainee_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate how satisfied you are with the session.
* **Rating Scale 1** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, required): Select the rating scale for your overall rating.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for your satisfaction with the trainers.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the trainers' feedback.
* **Rating Scale 4** (`rating_scale_4`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the rating scale for the rating of the session.
* **Rating Scale 5** (`rating_scale_5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the trainers' feedback 2.
* **Rating Scale 6** (`rating_scale_6`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the rating scale for the overall rating.
* **Rating Scale 7** (`rating_scale_7`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the session satisfaction.
* **Rating Scale 8** (`rating_scale_8`, `select_one`, optional): Select the rating scale for the trainer's attentiveness.

Note: Please provide feedback and comments in a constructive and specific manner.
