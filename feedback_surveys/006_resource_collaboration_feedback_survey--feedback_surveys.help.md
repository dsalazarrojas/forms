# Resource Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Collaboration Feedback Survey is a tool to gather information about your experiences with resource collaboration. Your input will help us improve our resource collaboration processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and enter your responses in the corresponding sections.
2. For each question, provide your genuine feedback.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you don't have a response.
4. Submit your completed form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): 
Please provide a brief description of the project you are giving feedback on.
- **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, optional): 
Rate your experience with resource collaboration for this project, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **Collaboration Outcomes** (`collaboration_outcomes`, text, optional): 
Describe the outcomes of the collaboration, e.g., what was achieved, what was learned.
- **Collaboration Outcomes Rating** (`collaboration_outcome_rating`, number, optional): 
Rate the collaboration outcomes, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **Shared Resources** (`shared_resources`, select_multiple, optional): 
Select the resources that were shared with you during the project.
  - Resource A
  - Resource B
  - Resource C
- **Team Feedback** (`team_feedback`, text, optional): 
Share any comments or suggestions about the team's performance.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): 
Select the status of the project.
  - Active
  - Inactive
- **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one, optional): 
Select the project manager who supervised your project.
  - John
  - Jane
- **Project Manager Email** (`project_manager_email`, email, optional): 
Enter the email address of the project manager (not required if project manager was selected above).
