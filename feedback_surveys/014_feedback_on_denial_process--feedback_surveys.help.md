# Feedback On Denial Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide feedback on the Denial Process used to deny their service request. The purpose of this form is to gather information on what the reason for denial was and how the Denial Process was experienced.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the reason for denial on page 2.
3. Provide feedback on the Denial Process on pages 3-9.
4. Fill out contact information on pages 10-20.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Denial Decision** (page 2):
	+ This field asks for the reason for denial. Please select the reason from the options provided.
* **Feedback** (page 3):
	+ This field is for providing feedback on the Denial Process. Please provide your thoughts, suggestions, or comments on the process.
* **Denial Process** (page 4):
	+ This field asks how you found the Denial Process. Please select from the options provided.
* **Contact Information 1** (page 10):
	+ This field asks for your name and email address. Please provide your name and a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Information 2** (page 11):
	+ This field asks for your phone number and any other contact information. Please provide your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information 3** (page 12):
	+ This field asks you to select the best contact method to contact you with. Please select from the options provided.
* **Contact Information 4** (page 13):
	+ This field asks for your preferred email address for contact. Please provide your preferred email address.
* **Contact Information 5** (page 14):
	+ This field asks you to select the preferred contact method. Please select from the options provided.
* **Contact Information 6** (page 15):
	+ This field asks for your preferred phone number for contact. Please provide your preferred phone number.
* **Contact Information 7** (page 16):
	+ This field asks for your preferred time zone for contact. Please select from the options provided.
* **Contact Information 8** (page 17):
	+ This field asks for your preferred email address for contact. Please provide your preferred email address.
* **Contact Information 9** (page 18):
	+ This field asks for your preferred contact method. Please select from the options provided.
* **Contact Information 10** (page 19):
	+ This field asks for your contact information. Please provide your contact information.
* **Contact Information 11** (page 20):
	+ This field asks for your email address for contact. Please provide your email address.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and detailed information in each field to help us improve the Denial Process.
* If you have any attachments or files to include with your feedback, please make sure to attach them to this form.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
