# volunteer_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to gather feedback from volunteers on their experience with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer all questions to provide your feedback.
2.  Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3.  Select all relevant options for the multiple-choice questions.
4.  Type your answers in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your overall volunteer experience?** (`question1`, `multiple`, required): This question measures your overall satisfaction level with your volunteer experience. Please choose one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How often do you typically participate in volunteer activities?** (`question2`, `multiple`, required): This question helps us understand how frequently you engage with our organization's volunteer activities. Choose one of the five frequency options: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, A few times a year, or Once a year or less.
* **Which of the following best describe your volunteer experience?** (`question3`, `multiple`, required): This question allows you to select all the attributes that describe your experience. Choose from the six options: Rewarding, Well-organized, Impactful, Challenging, Underutilized skills, or Good support.
* **What improvements would you suggest for our volunteer program or specific activities?** (`question4`, `text`, required): This open-ended question is an opportunity for you to provide suggestions on how we can improve our volunteer program or specific activities.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`question5`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your experience.

## Tips

* Be honest in your responses.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended questions.
* Select all relevant options for the multiple-choice questions.

Note: This help guide is a concrete, concise, and practical explanation of the form purpose and fields. The explanations are based on the provided YAML fields and aim to assist end-users in understanding the form's requirements and usage.
