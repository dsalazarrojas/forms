# Teamwork Project Advancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teamwork Project Advancement Survey aims to collect information about your team's collaboration and project advancement. Your input will help us understand your experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the best answer for each question based on your genuine feelings and experiences.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly to provide valuable insights.
3. Use the provided options for select_multiple questions.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork_collaboration`, select_multiple, required): How often do you collaborate with your team members?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you frequently collaborate with your team members.
	+ Select 'No' if you rarely or never collaborate with your team members.
* **Current Project Status** (`current_project_status`, select_multiple, required): What is the current status of your project?
	+ Select 'Active' if the project is currently ongoing and active.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the project is paused or completed.
* **Project Milestone Satisfaction** (`project_milestone_satisfaction`, number, required): How satisfied are you with the current project milestones?
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest satisfaction.
* **Project Milestone Importance** (`project_milestone_importance`, number, required): How important do you think the project milestones are for your team's advancement?
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest importance.
* **Collaboration Level** (`collaboration_level`, select_one, required): What is your perception of the collaboration level within the team?
	+ Select 'Collaboration Level' for high-level collaboration.
	+ Select 'High' for frequent and effective collaboration.
	+ Select 'Medium' for moderate collaboration.
	+ Select 'Low' for rare or minimal collaboration.
* **Collaboration Comments** (`collaboration_comments`, note, required): What are your comments about the collaboration within the team?
	+ Provide detailed comments about your experiences and suggestions for improvement.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, required): Are there any challenges hindering the project's progress?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you face challenges.
	+ Select 'No' if you don't face challenges.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, select_multiple, required): Do you have any suggestions for improving the project or process?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have suggestions.
	+ Select 'No' if you don't have suggestions.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Do you have any feedback or comments about the project or process?
	+ Provide detailed feedback and comments.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Select the best option for each question based on your experiences.
* Provide detailed comments where possible.
