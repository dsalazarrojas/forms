# Coffee Chat Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees on their experience with the coffee chat event, focusing on evaluating the effectiveness of the format, the quality of the speakers, and the overall satisfaction with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Provide your company or organization (if applicable).
3. Enter the date and location of the event.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
5. Indicate your willingness to attend similar events in the future.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of the event format.
7. Rate the quality of the speakers or facilitators.
8. Assess the relevance of the topics discussed.
9. Choose your level of satisfaction with the venue.
10. Rate the value of the networking opportunities.
11. If you had any meaningful connections, please specify the number.
12. Evaluate the quality of the coffee and refreshments.
13. Rate your overall satisfaction with the refreshments.
14. If you wish to provide more detailed feedback, please do so in the "Detailed Feedback" field.
15. Finally, if you have any specific suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Suggestions for Future Events" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Company or Organization** (`company`): If you're representing a company or organization, enter your affiliation.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Event** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, using the scale:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Would You Attend a Similar Event Again?** (`would_attend_again`): Indicate your willingness to attend similar events in the future, using the scale:
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably no
	+ Definitely no
* **Effectiveness of the Event Format** (`format_effectiveness`): Evaluate the effectiveness of the event format, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Quality of Speakers or Facilitators** (`speaker_quality`): Rate the quality of the speakers or facilitators, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Relevance of Topics Discussed** (`topic_relevance`): Assess the relevance of the topics discussed, using the scale:
	+ Very relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Somewhat relevant
	+ Not relevant
* **Satisfaction with Venue** (`location_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the venue, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Value of Networking Opportunities** (`networking_value`): Evaluate the value of the networking opportunities, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ None
* **Number of Meaningful Connections Made** (`connections_made`): If you had any meaningful connections, specify the number.
* **Coffee Quality** (`coffee_quality`): Rate the quality of the coffee, using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **Satisfaction with Refreshments** (`refreshments_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the refreshments, using the scale:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What Worked Well About This Event?** (`what_worked_well`): If you had any positive experiences, describe them.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): If you had any issues or room for improvement, describe them.
* **Suggestions for Future Events** (`future_suggestions`): If you have any ideas for future events, provide them.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
