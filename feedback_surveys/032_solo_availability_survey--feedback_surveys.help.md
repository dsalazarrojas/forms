# Solo Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solo Availability Survey form is designed to collect information about an individual's availability to work solo on specific days and time slots. This data will be used to optimize solo work assignments and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your activity name, which is the name of the task or project you are referring to when answering the questions.
2. Select your time slots for each day of the week, indicating when you are available to work solo.
3. Choose your start and end times for your solo work sessions.
4. Select the days you are available to work solo each week.
5. Indicate your flexibility in case you need to adjust your solo work schedule.
6. Add any comments or notes about your availability, if needed.
7. Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **activity_name** (`activity_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the task or project you are referring to when answering the questions.
* **Time Slots** (`time_slots`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on each day of the week. Options are Yes or No.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of your solo work session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of your solo work session.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you are available to work solo each week. Options are Yes or No.
* **Monday** (`monday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Monday.
* **Tuesday** (`tuesday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Tuesday.
* **Wednesday** (`wednesday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Wednesday.
* **Thursday** (`thursday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Thursday.
* **Friday** (`friday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Friday.
* **Saturday** (`saturday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Saturday.
* **Sunday** (`sunday`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to work solo on Sunday.
* **Time Slot 1** (`time_slot_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Monday.
* **Time Slot 2** (`time_slot_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Tuesday.
* **Time Slot 3** (`time_slot_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Wednesday.
* **Time Slot 4** (`time_slot_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Thursday.
* **Time Slot 5** (`time_slot_5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Friday.
* **Time Slot 6** (`time_slot_6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Saturday.
* **Time Slot 7** (`time_slot_7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Sunday.
* **Time Slot 8** (`time_slot_8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Monday.
* **Time Slot 9** (`time_slot_9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Tuesday.
* **Time Slot 10** (`time_slot_10`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the time slots you are available to work solo on Wednesday.
* **Flexibility** (`flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available to adjust your solo work schedule.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Add any comments or notes about your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any changes in your availability, please update your responses accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, feel free to contact us.
