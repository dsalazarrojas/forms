# Branding questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the branding questionnaire form. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability. 
- For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply. 
- For the open-ended question, provide as much detail as possible. 
- Make sure to review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with our current brand identity?** (`<name>`: `satisfaction`, `<type>`: `single`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to gauge your current perception of our brand's identity. 
  Please select how you feel about our current brand.
- **Which of the following best describe your perception of our brand?** (`<name>`: `perception`, `<type>`: `multiple`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to see which attributes you associate with our brand. 
  Please select all that apply.
- **How likely are you to recommend our brand to others?** (`<name>`: `recommendation`, `<type>`: `single`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to understand how much you trust our brand. 
  Please answer truthfully.
- **What aspects of our brand could be improved or changed?** (`<name>`: `improvement`, `<type>`: `text`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to collect feedback on areas that need improvement. 
  Please provide specific and concrete examples or reasons.
- **Which emotions does our brand evoke?** (`<name>`: `brand_emotions`, `<type>`: `multiple`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to see which feelings our brand typically triggers. 
  Please select all that apply.
- **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding our brand?** (`<name>`: `comments`, `<type>`: `text`, required/optional: required)
  This question is to gather any other thoughts or ideas you may have. 
  Please provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question carefully.
- Be honest and specific in your answers.
- Review your answers before submitting.
- If unsure, answer to the best of your ability.
