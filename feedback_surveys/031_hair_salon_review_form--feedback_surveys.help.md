# Hair Salon Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and reviews from customers who have visited a hair salon. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their experience, including their name, the date of their visit, the salon's name, a rating of their experience, and a comment about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your visit to the salon in the "Review Date" field.
3. Type in the name of the salon you visited in the "Salon Name" field.
4. Rate your experience at the salon in the "Rating" field (on a scale from 1 to 10).
5. Enter your review or comment about your visit in the "Review Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name (text, required/optional):** Enter your name to help us identify you as a reviewer.
- **Review Date (date, optional):** Enter the date of your visit to the salon. This will help us track when reviews are submitted.
- **Salon Name (text, optional):** Enter the name of the salon where you had your hair styled.
- **Rating (number, optional):** Rate your experience from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- **Review Comment (text, optional):** Share your thoughts about your visit to the salon. Is there anything you particularly enjoyed or disliked?
