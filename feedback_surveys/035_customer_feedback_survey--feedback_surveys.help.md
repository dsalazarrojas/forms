## Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather customer feedback on our services, allowing us to understand your experiences and preferences. Your input will help us to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what it entails.
2. Select the category that best describes the nature of your feedback (General, Technical, or Billing).
3. Provide a brief description of your experience with our services.
4. Enter your email address (if you would like to receive a copy of your feedback).
5. Share your feedback on your experience with our services.
6. Rate your satisfaction level with our services on a scale of 1-5.
7. Specify the area of our service that you are providing feedback on (Front Desk, Back Office, or Both).
8. Provide any additional comments you would like to share with us.
9. If applicable, specify the tool you were using when interacting with our service (ChatJIMMY or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Please select the category that best describes the nature of your feedback. This could be General, Technical, or Billing-related.
* **description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with our services. Please keep your description concise and focused on your experience.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you would like to receive a copy of your feedback, please enter your email address here.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your feedback on your experience with our services. Be as detailed as possible.
* **rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with our services on a scale of 1-5.
* **service area** (`service_area`, select_one, required: false): Specify the area of our service that you are providing feedback on. This could be Front Desk, Back Office, or Both.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: true): If applicable, please specify the tool you were using when interacting with our service. This could be ChatJIMMY or Other.
* **form_id** (`form_id`, number, required: false): This field is currently not visible to users and is not required for completion.
* **output_file** (`output_file`, text, required: false): This field is currently not visible to users and is not required for completion.

## Tips

* Please keep your feedback brief and focused on your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "comments" field.
* If you were using a tool when interacting with our service, please specify it in the "assigned tool" field.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time in completing this survey!
