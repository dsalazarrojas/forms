# Employee Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience as an employee, specifically your relationship with your manager and your overall satisfaction with your role.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and helpful feedback, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience and feelings.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "feedback" field to provide them.
4. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What Department?**: This is the department you currently work in. Please enter the name of your department.
- **Job Title**: This is the title of your current role. Please enter your job title.
- **How Often Do You Interact with Your Manager?**: Select the frequency with which you interact with your manager. This can be never, rarely, occasionally, regularly, or daily.
- **Manager Communication Style**: Select all that apply from the list of communication styles that best describe your experience with your manager.
- **Manager Style**: Select a style that best describes your manager's behavior. This can be supportive, helpful, distant, micromanaging, or unhelpful.
- **Overall Satisfaction**: Select all that apply from the list of satisfaction levels that best describe your overall experience with your job. This can be very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
- **Manager Satisfaction**: Select a satisfaction level that best describes your experience with your manager. This can be very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
- **Feedback**: This is an open field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
- **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Employee ID**: This is your employee ID number. Please enter the number.
