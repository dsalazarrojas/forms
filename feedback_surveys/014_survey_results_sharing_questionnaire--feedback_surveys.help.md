# Survey Results Sharing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about sharing survey results with various groups such as employees, customers, students, and community stakeholders. It will help you provide the right information to the right people at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to give a brief description of the survey results.
2. Provide instructions for the people receiving the survey results.
3. Choose the group(s) you want to share the results with (e.g., Employees, Customers, Students, Community stakeholders).
4. Decide how often you want to share the results (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
5. Choose when you want to share the results (Immediately, After each survey, At end of survey).
6. Select the medium you want to use for sharing (Email, Text message, In person, Other - specify).
7. If you choose "Other (please specify)" for the medium, provide the email address for sharing.
8. Enter the contact number for sharing (optional).
9. Add any comments you have about the survey results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Give a brief description of the survey results.
* **Instructions** (`form_instructions`, text, optional): Provide additional information for the people receiving the survey results.
* **Share Findings To** (`share_findings_to`, select one, optional): Choose the group(s) you want to share the survey results with (e.g., Employees, Customers, Students, Community stakeholders).
* **Share Frequency** (`share_frequency`, select multiple, optional): Decide how often you want to share the survey results (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Share Timing** (`share_timing`, select one, optional): Choose when you want to share the results (Immediately, After each survey, At end of survey).
* **Share Medium** (`share_medium`, select one, required): Select the medium you want to use for sharing (Email, Text message, In person, Other - specify).
* **Other Share Medium** (`other_share_medium`, text, optional): If you choose "Other (please specify)" for the medium, provide the specific medium used.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address for sharing (only required if "Other (please specify)" is chosen).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for sharing (optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments you have about the survey results.
