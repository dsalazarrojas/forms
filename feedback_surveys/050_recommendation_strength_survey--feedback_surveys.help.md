# Recommendation Strength Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers who have interacted with us recently. We are seeking to understand their perceptions of our product or service and to identify areas where we may be able to improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page and make sure you understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with us.
3. Enter your ratings and comments in the required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.
