# Non Payment Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers who do not pay for our services. The survey aims to understand the reasons behind non-payment and identify areas for improvement. It will help us to better understand our customers' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Any additional comments?" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you pay for services online?** (`non_payment_survey`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand your payment habits and frequency. Select all the answers that apply to you, such as "Yes", "No", or "Other" (if none of the options fit).
* **What is your experience with our service?** (`service_quality`, select_one, required: false): This question helps us evaluate our service's quality and identify areas for improvement. Select your response from the options: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **What do you think is the main reason you do not pay?** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended and allows you to provide a detailed explanation for your non-payment. Be as specific as possible with your answer.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required: false): This question helps us understand how you would like to be contacted for future communication. Select all the contact methods that you prefer, such as "Email", "Phone", or "Social Media".
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any further feedback or suggestions you may have. Use it to share your thoughts and ideas on how we can improve our service.
