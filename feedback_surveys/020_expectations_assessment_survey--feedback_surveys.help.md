# Expectations Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expectations Assessment Survey is a form used to collect feedback on your expectations and experiences. It helps us understand how often these expectations are met or not, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
2. Be honest and accurate when answering the ratings and expectations questions.
3. Use the text fields (first_name, last_name, department, position, expectations, comments, and notes) to provide any additional information you feel is relevant.
4. Choose the correct ratings for each question, based on how often you experience each expectation.
