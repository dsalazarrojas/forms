# IT Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback on the quality, speed, and communication of our IT services. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support for you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the pages that you have interacted with our IT services. This form contains multiple pages, each with its own questions.
* Choose one of the options for each question on the page you are completing.
* For questions that allow rating or scaling, please select the rating that best reflects your experience.
* For questions that ask if you are likely to recommend us, please select 'Yes' if you would definitely recommend our services to others, and 'No' otherwise.
