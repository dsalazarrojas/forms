# Client Equipment Charge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Equipment Charge Survey is designed to help us understand your experiences with equipment charges. Please provide your honest feedback on the clarity, accuracy, and value of the charges, as well as any issues or areas for improvement in the billing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name and last name as they appear on your invoice.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. If you have an account number, please enter it.
5. Select the type of equipment that was billed.
6. Provide a brief description of the equipment.
7. Enter the total charge amount.
8. Select the type of charge (e.g. rental fee, lease payment, etc.).
9. Rate the clarity of the charge description on the invoice.
10. Rate the accuracy of the charge.
11. Indicate whether you expected this charge.
12. Rate the value of the charge.
13. If you experienced any issues with the billing process, select all that apply.
14. If you experienced any issues, provide a brief description.
15. Rate your satisfaction with the issue resolution.
16. If applicable, rate how well you understand your equipment charges.
17. Provide any suggestions for improving the billing process.
18. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them.
19. Finally, date and time you completed this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your invoice. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your invoice. 
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email. 
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number. 
* **Account Number**: If you have an account number, please enter it (optional). 
* **Company Name**: If you have a company name, please enter it (optional). 
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the reference number from your invoice. 
* **Invoice Date**: Enter the date on the invoice. 
* **Type of Equipment Charged**: Select the type of equipment that was billed (e.g. Computer Hardware, Network Equipment, etc.). 
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment. 
* **Charge Amount**: Enter the total charge amount. 
* **Type of Charge**: Select the type of charge (e.g. rental fee, lease payment, etc.). 
* **Charge Clarity**: Rate how clear the charge description on the invoice was. 
* **Charge Accuracy**: Rate how accurate the charge was. 
* **Expected Charge**: Indicate whether you expected this charge. 
* **Charge Value**: Rate the value of the charge. 
* **Billing Issues**: Select all issues you experienced with the billing process. 
* **Issue Description**: If you experienced issues, provide a brief description. 
* **Issue Resolution**: Rate your satisfaction with the issue resolution. 
* **Invoice Understanding**: Rate how well you understand your equipment charges. 
* **Billing Improvements**: Provide any suggestions for improving the billing process. 
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other feedback you may have. 
* **Survey Completion Date**: Date and time you completed this survey.
