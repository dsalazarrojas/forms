# Devotional Book Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions on a devotional book. Your input will help authors and publishers understand what works and what doesn't, allowing them to create better content for their readers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your overall impression of the book on the **What do you think of this book?** text field.
2. Rate the book using the **Rate this book** field, which requires you to select a number from 1-5.
3. If you have a favorite part of the book, select it from the **Which part did you like the most?** dropdown menu. This could be the cover, story, characters, or message.
4. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, type them in the **Any feedback or suggestions?** text field.
5. Finally, provide your **Email address** so we can get in touch with you, and optionally, your **Phone number** for further discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think of this book?** (text, required): Share your overall opinion about the book. Try to be as specific as possible, and include any notable moments or quotes that resonated with you.
* **Rate this book** (number, required): Rate the book on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Which part did you like the most?** (select_one, optional): Choose the part of the book that you enjoyed the most. You can select from:
	+ Cover
	+ Story
	+ Characters
	+ Message
* **Any feedback or suggestions?** (text, optional): Do you have any additional thoughts or ideas that you think could improve the book or its content? Share them with us.
* **Email address** (email, required): Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you and discuss your feedback further (if needed).
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Your phone number (if you're comfortable sharing it) can help us have a more personal discussion about your feedback.
