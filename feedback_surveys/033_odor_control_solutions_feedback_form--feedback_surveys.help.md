# Odor Control Solutions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on odor control solutions, helping us understand their experiences, performance, and suggestions for improvement. The goal is to gather valuable insights to refine and develop more effective odor control solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Leave blank fields that are not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (first_page)**: This is the introduction page of the form. You can enter any general feedback or comments about your experience with odor control solutions.
* **Product Performance (product_performance)**: Please describe your experience with the product's performance, including any successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.
* **User Experience (user_experience)**: Share your thoughts on how easy or difficult it was to use the product, including any positive or negative aspects of the user interface.
* **User Experience 2 (user_experience_2)**: This field is similar to the previous one, but feel free to add any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the product.
 
## Tips

* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, feel free to add them to your feedback.
* Don't hesitate to suggest new ideas or features that would enhance the product.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
