# Healthcare Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Service Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about our healthcare service. Your feedback will help us understand your experience and suggestions for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question with your thoughts about our healthcare service.
2. Choose a rating for our service from 1 to 5.
3. Select any additional ratings that apply to your experience.
4. Provide any suggestions or additional comments about our service.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Agree or disagree to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think about our service?** (`healthcare_service_feedback_questionnaire`, text, required): Please share your thoughts and opinions about our healthcare service. Be as detailed as possible, and explain your reasons for your opinions.
* **Overall rating (1-5)** (`healthcare_service_feedback`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **What would you rate our service?** (`healthcare_service_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Select any ratings that apply to your experience with our service.
* **Do you have any suggestions for us?** (`healthcare_service_suggestion`, text, required): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have about how we can improve our service.
* **Your email** (`healthcare_service_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Your phone number** (`healthcare_service_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Agree to share your feedback** (`healthcare_service_agree_to_share`, select_multiple, required): Please indicate if you agree or disagree to share your feedback with us.
