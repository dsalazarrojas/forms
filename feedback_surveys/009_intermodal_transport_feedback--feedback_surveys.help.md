# intermodal_transport_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about intermodal transportation. It is meant to gather details about transfers, travel times, modes of transportation, and user satisfaction levels. This feedback will be used to identify areas for improvement in the intermodal transportation service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant options for each field, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Fill in any text fields, such as `Origin Location`, `Destination Location`, `Comments`, and `Improvement Areas`.
3. Choose your answers from the provided options for the select fields, such as `Transfer Type`, `Transfer Mode`, `Travel Mode`, `Mode Of Transport`, and `Satisfaction Level`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Type**: Select all applicable transfer types that apply to your experience. This could include walking, public transportation, private transportation, etc.
* **Date Of Transfer**: Enter the date of the transfer, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time Of Transfer**: Enter the time of the transfer, in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Origin Location**: Enter the location where the transfer began.
* **Destination Location**: Enter the location where the transfer ended.
* **Transfer Mode**: Select the mode of transfer that occurred. This could be a bus, train, airplane, etc.
* **Travel Mode**: Select the mode of travel that was used during the transfer. This could be on foot, by bicycle, etc.
* **Travel Time**: Enter the time taken for the transfer, in hours and minutes.
* **Mode Of Transport**: Select all applicable modes of transportation that were used during the transfer. This could include buses, trains, etc.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the transfer.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Select your level of satisfaction with the transfer, on a scale from 1 to 3.
* **Improvement Areas**: Provide any areas for improvement for the intermodal transportation service.
