# Remote Work Feedback Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Feedback Pulse Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experiences with remote work. This information helps us understand what's working and what areas need improvement, enabling us to develop and refine our remote work policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
	* Phone number
	* Job title
	* Department
2. For the remaining fields, select the relevant options or type in your answers:
	* Team members (yes/no)
	* Work location (type in your current location)
	* Challenges faced during remote work (type in your experiences)
	* Suggestions for improvement (type in your ideas)
	* Satisfaction with remote work (select one of the options)
	* Productivity (select one of the options)
	* Collaboration (select one of the options)
	* Communication (select one of the options)
	* Work life balance (select one of the options)
	* Well-being (select one of the options)
	* Engagement (select one of the options)
	* Job satisfaction (select one of the options)
	* Remote work experience (select one of the options)
	* Work life satisfaction (select one of the options)
	* Overall satisfaction (select one of the options)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Team members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Are you working with other team members during remote work?
	+ Select "Yes" if you're working with others
	+ Select "No" if you're working alone
* **Work location** (`work_location`, text, optional): What's your current work location?
* **Challenges faced during remote work** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you've faced while working remotely.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving our remote work policies and practices.
* **Satisfaction with remote work** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with remote work?
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **Productivity** (`productivity`, select_one, optional): How productive do you feel while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very productive
	+ Option 2: Productive
	+ Option 3: Low productivity
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your collaboration experience while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very effective
	+ Option 2: Effective
	+ Option 3: Not effective
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your communication experience while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very effective
	+ Option 2: Effective
	+ Option 3: Not effective
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional): How do you balance your work and life while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very good balance
	+ Option 2: Good balance
	+ Option 3: Poor balance
* **Well-being** (`well_being`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall well-being while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very well
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, select_one, optional): How engaged do you feel in your work while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very engaged
	+ Option 2: Engaged
	+ Option 3: Not engaged
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your job while working remotely?
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **Remote work experience** (`remote_work_experience`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall remote work experience?
	+ Option 1: Very positive
	+ Option 2: Positive
	+ Option 3: Negative
* **Work life satisfaction** (`work_life_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your work life balance?
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your overall work experience?
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* You can select "No" or "Not applicable" for questions that don't apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
