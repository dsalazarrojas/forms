# Academic Communication Openness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the openness of communication within your academic institution. Your input will help us understand the current state of communication and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.
3. Rate your answers on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very closed and 10 is very open.
4. If you have suggestions for improving communication openness, please share them in the box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name of your institution**: Enter the full name of your college or university.
- **Department or faculty**: Enter the name of your department or faculty.
- **Your role at the institution**: Select your role within the institution (Faculty Member, Administrative Staff, Graduate Student, Undergraduate Student, Department Chair, Adjunct Instructor).
- **How many years have you been at this institution?**: Select the number of years you have been working at this institution.
- **How comfortable do you feel sharing new ideas with colleagues?**: Rate your comfort level with sharing new ideas.
- **How receptive is your department to constructive feedback?**: Rate your department's receptiveness to constructive feedback.
- **Which communication channels are most used in your department?**: Select all the channels you commonly use for communication (Email, In-person meetings, Video conferencing, Messaging platforms, Department newsletters, Shared documents or portals).
- **Rate the overall openness of communication in your department**: Rate your department's communication openness on a scale of 1-10.
- **How transparent is the leadership in sharing institutional decisions?**: Rate the leadership's transparency in sharing decisions.
- **What barriers to open communication have you experienced?**: Select all the barriers you have encountered (Fear of retaliation, Hierarchical culture, Lack of anonymous channels, Language or cultural differences, Time constraints, No clear feedback mechanisms).
* **What suggestions do you have for improving communication openness?**: Share your ideas for improving communication openness in your institution.
* **Would you be willing to participate in a follow-up discussion?**: Indicate your willingness to participate in a follow-up discussion.
* **Email address for follow-up (optional)**: Provide your email address if you have selected "Yes" for the previous question. 

Note: If you have any questions or concerns about this survey, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
