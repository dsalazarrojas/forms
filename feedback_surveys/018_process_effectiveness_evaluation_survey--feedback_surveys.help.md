# Process Effectiveness Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Effectiveness Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback on the overall effectiveness of a project process. It aims to assess how clear, efficient, and collaborative the process was. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make our processes better for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the overall project effectiveness by selecting a number from 1-100.
2. Provide the name of the team involved in the project.
3. List the team members who were involved in the project.
4. Assess the overall process clarity by selecting one or more of the provided options.
5. Evaluate the overall process efficiency by selecting one or more of the provided options.
6. Rate the communication among team members by selecting one or more of the provided options.
7. Rate the collaboration among team members by selecting one or more of the provided options.
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the project process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall project effectiveness** (1): How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the project?
	+ This field is used to rate the overall effectiveness of the project on a scale of 1-100.
* **Team name** (2): What is the name of the team involved in the project?
	+ This is a text field where you can enter the name of the team involved in the project.
* **Team members** (3): Who were the team members involved in the project?
	+ This is also a text field where you can list the team members involved in the project.
* **Overall process clarity** (4): How clear was the project process?
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Clear and concise
		+ Difficult to understand
		+ Somewhat unclear
		+ Very unclear
* **Overall process efficiency** (5): How efficient was the project process?
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Very efficient
		+ Somewhat efficient
		+ Not very efficient
		+ Not at all efficient
* **Communication among team members** (6): How effective was communication among team members?
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Average
		+ Fair
		+ Poor
* **Collaboration among team members** (7): How effective was collaboration among team members?
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Excellent
		+ Good
		+ Average
		+ Fair
		+ Poor
* **Additional comments** (8): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about the project process?
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the project process.
