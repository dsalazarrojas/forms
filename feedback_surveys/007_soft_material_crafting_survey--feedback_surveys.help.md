# Soft Material Crafting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your soft material crafting projects. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and we will use the provided data to better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand each field and its purpose.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, such as fabric type and project scale.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your project, including any necessary details about the project image.
4. Add any additional comments or questions you have about the form or your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Fabric Type** (`fabric_type`, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of fabric you are working with, such as Cotton, Polyester, or Other.

### 2. **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the type of project you are undertaking, such as Yarn Project or Soft Craft Project.

### 3. **Yarn Color** (`yarn_color`, select_one, required: false)
Select the color of the yarn or material you are using for your project.

### 4. **Project Scale** (`project_scale`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the scale of your project, e.g., small, medium, or large.

### 5. **Project Description** (`project_description`, note, required: false)
Write a short description of your project, including any relevant details about the project's scope and goals.

### 6. **Project Image URL** (`project_image_url`, text, required: false)
If you have an image related to your project, provide a URL where we can find it.

### 7. **Project Image URL (again)** (`project_image_url_url`, text, required: false)
This field is likely a duplicate of the previous one. Please disregard this field and focus on providing the correct image URL in the previous field.

### 8. **Soft Crafting Survey Comments** (`soft_crafting_survey_comments`, text, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or questions about the form or your project, please provide them here.

### 9. **Fabric Weight** (`fabric_weight`, number, required: false)
This field seems to be intended for numerical input, possibly for measuring the weight of the fabric. If this is not relevant to your project, please leave it blank.

Note that this form is meant to be a guide, so please feel free to skip or modify any field as needed to suit your specific project needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
