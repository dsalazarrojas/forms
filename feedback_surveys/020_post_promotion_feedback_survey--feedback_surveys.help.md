# Post-Promotion Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post-Promotion Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience after a promotion. Your feedback will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on for future promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **feedback** (Text, Optional): This is the main feedback section where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the promotion. Please be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **contact_info** (Text, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further information or to provide feedback, please provide your contact details here.
* **feedback_details** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments about the promotion, please share them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* Don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
