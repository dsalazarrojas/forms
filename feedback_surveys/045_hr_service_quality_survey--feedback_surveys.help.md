# HR Service Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees about the quality of HR services provided to them.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill in your name
* **Employee Name**: A field to enter your name.

### Step 2: Select your department
* **Department**: A dropdown list of departments to select from. Please choose the department you interacted with HR services from.

### Step 3: Provide comments about your experience
* **HR Service Quality Survey Form Comments**: A text field to share any comments or thoughts about your experience with HR services.

### Step 4: Rate your overall satisfaction
* **Overall Rating**: A scale to rate your overall satisfaction with HR services, using the options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent.

### Step 5: Rate your satisfaction with HR services
* **Satisfaction with HR Services**: A field to rate your overall satisfaction with HR services, using a scale of 1-10.

### Step 6: Provide additional feedback
* **Comments**: A text field to provide any additional comments or feedback about HR services.

### Step 7: Share further feedback and suggestions
* **Feedback**: A text field to share any further feedback or suggestions you may have about HR services.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and as concisely as possible.
* You can provide feedback about any HR service you have interacted with, such as submitting a request, seeking guidance, or receiving support.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please provide them in the comments field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback!
