# Metalectri Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metalectri Feedback Form is a tool for you to provide your thoughts and opinions about your experience with Metalectri. Your feedback will help us understand how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions that apply to you.
2. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please provide them on the "How can we improve?" page.
3. If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or to share your contact information, please select the relevant option on the "Contact Information" page.
4. If you have specific date and time concerns, please provide them on the "What date and time?" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (Page 1)
	+ This is the first page where you can provide any general feedback about your experience with Metalectri.
* **How can we improve?** (Page 2)
	+ On this page, you can select all the ways in which you think Metalectri can improve. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Contact Information** (Page 3)
	+ If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or would like to share your contact information, please select the relevant option from the dropdown list.
* **What date and time?** (Page 4)
	+ If you have any specific date and time concerns, please enter them in the format "DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM:SS".
