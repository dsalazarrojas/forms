# Streaming Support Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our streaming service. We appreciate your input, which will help us improve our streaming experience for you and our other users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "What was the issue?" field that best describes your problem.
2. Choose how you rate your support experience by selecting one or more options from the "How do you rate the support experience?" field.
3. If you would like to provide more information about your issue, you can include a date and time in the "When did the issue occur?" field.
4. You can also provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. If you have a streaming support email address, you can include it in the "Email" field.
6. Finally, please provide a brief description of the issue in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Streaming Support Feedback**: This is the title of the form that you are currently viewing.
- **What was the issue?** (Select One): Please choose the option that best describes your problem with our streaming service.
	+ Option 1: This option is not specified in the form fields.
	+ Option 2: This option is not specified in the form fields.
	+ Option 3: This option is not specified in the form fields.
- **How do you rate the support experience?** (Select One/Many): Choose one or more options that describe your experience with our support team.
	+ Option 4: This option is not specified in the form fields.
	+ Option 5: This option is not specified in the form fields.
	+ Option 6: This option is not specified in the form fields.
- **When did the issue occur?**: If you would like to provide more information about your issue, please enter the date and time it occurred.
- **Notes**: Please provide any additional comments or notes about your issue in this field.
- **Email**: If you have a streaming support email address, please enter it here.
- **Text 1-10**: These fields are for additional comments or information about your issue. Please provide as much detail as you need in these fields.
