# Healthcare Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Satisfaction Poll is a form designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from patients after visiting a healthcare provider. This form helps us understand how well we're meeting our patients' needs and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, which includes a series of questions and ratings that will help us understand your experience.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as you can.
3. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): This page is a free-text field where you can share any feedback or comments about your experience with our healthcare provider.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your overall satisfaction with your visit:
	+ Option 1: Very satisfied
	+ Option 2: Somewhat satisfied
	+ Option 3: Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
* **Rating** (`page_3`, `time`, optional): Please rate your visit on a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".
* **Patient Name** (`page_4`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Date Visited** (`page_5`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date of your visit.
* **Comments** (`page_6`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`page_7`, `select_one`, optional): Which healthcare provider did you visit?
	+ Option 1: Doctor 1
	+ Option 2: Doctor 2
	+ Option 3: Doctor 3
	+ Option 4: Doctor 4
	+ Option 5: Doctor 5
* **Department** (`page_8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Which department did you visit?
	+ Option 1: Department 1
	+ Option 2: Department 2
* **Email (for feedback only)** (`page_9`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to provide feedback via email.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_10`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please let us know here.

## Tips

* Please take a few minutes to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.
* We value your feedback and appreciate your honesty.
* All information collected will be kept confidential and used to improve our services.
