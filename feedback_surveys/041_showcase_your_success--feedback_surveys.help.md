# Showcase Your Success - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Showcase Your Success" form is designed to collect and showcase success stories, experiences, and achievements from individuals or organizations in a specific industry or category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Title** field with a brief title of your success story.
2. In the **Story Title** field, provide a concise and descriptive title for your success story.
3. In the **Story Description** field, write a detailed description of your success story, highlighting your achievements and experiences.
4. If you have any **Supporting Files** related to your success story, upload them in this field.
5. Select how you would like your story to be published: **Yes, with photo** to include a photo with your story, **No, without photo** to exclude a photo, or **No** to opt-out from publishing altogether.
6. Choose the **Recognition Programs** that you have been part of or achieved in your success story. This will help us categorize and showcase your story in the relevant industry or category.
7. Optionally, provide a detailed description for the **Community Spotlight** field if you feel that your story is relevant and worth highlighting in this section.
8. Finally, select a **Date** for your success story to be showcased.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief title of your success story. This should be a one-liner that captures the essence of your achievement.
* **Story Title**: A concise and descriptive title for your success story. This will be used as a headline for your story.
* **Story Description**: A detailed description of your success story, highlighting your achievements and experiences.
* **Supporting Files**: If you have any supporting files or documents related to your success story, upload them in this field. This could be images, videos, or other relevant attachments.
* **Publishing Consent**: Select how you would like your story to be published. You can choose to include or exclude a photo, or opt-out from publishing altogether.
* **Recognition Programs**: Choose the recognition programs that you have been part of or achieved in your success story. This will help us categorize and showcase your story in the relevant industry or category.
* **Community Spotlight**: If you feel that your story is relevant and worth highlighting in this section, provide a detailed description. This is optional and not required.
* **Success Date**: Select a date for your success story to be showcased. This should be the date when your achievement was recognized or accomplished.
