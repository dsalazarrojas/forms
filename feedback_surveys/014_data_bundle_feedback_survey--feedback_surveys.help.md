# Data Bundle Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about data bundles. It is meant to gather information about the customer's experience and perception of data bundles, allowing us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review this help guide to understand the purpose and structure of the form.
2. Read the instructions for each page carefully.
3. Answer all questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, optional):** This field is where you can provide your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Email (`email`, email, optional):** This field is where you can enter your email address. Please ensure that it is accurate and active.
* **Rating (`rating`, number, optional):** This field is a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. Please rate your experience with data bundles based on your perception.
* **Feedback (`feedback`, text, optional):** This field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about data bundles. Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
* **Category (`category`, select_one, optional):** This field is to select the category that best describes your experience with data bundles. Please choose from the options:
	+ Telecom
	+ Connectivity
