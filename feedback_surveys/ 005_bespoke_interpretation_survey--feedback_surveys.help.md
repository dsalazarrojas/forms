# Bespoke Interpretation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bespoke Interpretation Survey form is designed to gather information from users for internal feedback and analysis. It is intended to capture personal details, selections, and comments to better understand the user's perspective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: The first section of the form requires your full name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure your email address is valid and your phone number includes any necessary country code.
2. Select the relevant date: Choose the date that best applies to your situation.
3. Choose your primary selection: Select the option that best describes your experience or opinion (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. Provide a quantity or rating (if applicable): If relevant, enter a whole number to provide more detail about your experience.
5. Add any additional details: If you have any further comments or remarks, please enter them here.
6. Confirm your agreement: Select any that apply from the provided options.
7. Enter any estimated value (if applicable): If relevant, enter the estimated value in decimal format.
8. Schedule a preferred time (if necessary): If scheduling is required, enter your preferred time.
9. Leave any final comments or notes: If you have anything you'd like to say or any last-minute remarks, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name to identify yourself.
* **Email address**: Provide a valid email for internal contact.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number including any necessary country code.
* **Date**: Choose the date that best applies to your situation.
* **Primary selection**: Select the option that best describes your experience or opinion (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Quantity or rating**: Enter a whole number if providing more detail about your experience is necessary.
* **Additional details**: Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
* **Agreement**: Select any that apply from the provided options.
* **Estimated value**: Enter the estimated value in decimal format if necessary.
* **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes**: Leave any last-minute remarks or comments here.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any section, please refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Only submit the form once per session to avoid duplication.
