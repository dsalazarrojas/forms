# training_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on recent training sessions to improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, and choose the best response for you.
2. Select all that apply for the "Which aspects of the training did you find most valuable?" question.
3. Enter your thoughts and suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied were you with the training program?** (`Overall, how satisfied were you with the training program?`, single, required)
	+ Please rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **How would you rate the clarity and presentation of the training content?** (`How would you rate the clarity and presentation of the training content?`, single, required)
	+ Indicate how clear and well-presented you found the course materials.
* **Did the training meet your expectations for learning new skills/knowledge?** (`Did the training meet your expectations for learning new skills/knowledge?`, single, required)
	+ Choose whether the training met your expectations for new skills/knowledge.
* **Which aspects of the training did you find most valuable?** (`Which aspects of the training did you find most valuable?`, multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply for the aspects that were most valuable to you (e.g., instructor's expertise, hands-on exercises, course materials, peer discussions, relevance to job).
* **Do you feel confident applying what you learned in your role?** (`Do you feel confident applying what you learned in your role?`, single, required)
	+ Choose how confident you feel about applying the new skills and knowledge.
* **What specific improvements could be made to future training sessions?** (`What specific improvements could be made to future training sessions?`, text, optional)
	+ Share your thoughts on areas that could be improved.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?`, text, optional)
	+ Add any other feedback or suggestions you may have.
