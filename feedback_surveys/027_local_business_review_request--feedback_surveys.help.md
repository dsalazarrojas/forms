# local_business_review_request - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback and reviews from customers about local businesses. It's essential to provide your honest opinion about your experience with a specific shop or service provider. Your input will help us improve and provide better services to our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields (marked as *required*) with the requested information.
2.  Select your rating for the shop or service provider.
3.  Choose your preferred way to contact us (email or other).
4.  Review your experience with the shop or service provider.
5.  Complete any additional optional fields that apply to your experience.
6.  Click "Submit" to send your feedback and review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **shop_name**: Enter the name of the shop or service provider you are reviewing.
*   **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
*   **rating**: Rate your experience with the shop or service provider (Excellent, Good, Bad, Neutral, Very Bad).
*   **review**: Describe your experience with the shop or service provider in detail.
*   **experience**: Choose how you felt about your experience (Positive, Negative).
*   **service_quality**: Select how you felt about the service quality (Fast, Average, Slow).
*   **communication**: Rate how well the shop or service provider communicated with you (Good, Bad).
*   **price_range**: Select how you felt about the prices (Low, Average, High).
*   **recommended**: Tell us if you would recommend the shop or service provider to others (True, False).
*   **visit_time**: Enter the time you visited the shop or service provider.
*   **date**: Enter the date you visited the shop or service provider.
*   **note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
*   **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
*   **email_2**: Enter your email address for further contact.
*   **email_hint**: Enter any additional information you want to provide about your email address.
*   **service_providers**: Tell us if the shop or service provider was a service provider (Yes, No).
*   **shop_type**: Choose the type of shop or service provider (Restaurant, Shop, Salon, Other).
*   **shop**: Choose the type of shop or service provider (Local, Online).
*   **location**: Enter the location of the shop or service provider.
*   **review_location**: Choose where you reviewed the shop or service provider (Google, Yelp, Facebook, Instagram).
*   **rating_score**: Enter a rating score for the shop or service provider.
*   **review_score**: Enter a review score for the shop or service provider.
*   **Review 2**: Enter a detailed review of your experience.
*   **Rating 2**: Rate your experience with the shop or service provider (Excellent, Good, Bad, Neutral, Very Bad).
*   **submit**: Enter the text to be submitted with the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers who have experienced a local business or service provider. Please answer all questions truthfully and in as much detail as possible. Your feedback is invaluable to us and will help us improve our services.
