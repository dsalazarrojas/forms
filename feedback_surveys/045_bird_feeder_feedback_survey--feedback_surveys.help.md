# Bird Feeder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Bird Feeder Feedback Survey. Please take a few minutes to provide detailed and honest feedback about your bird feeder experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your demographic details in the Participant Information section.
2. Describe your bird feeder, including its type, brand, and model.
3. Answer questions about your usage patterns, such as how long you've used the feeder and how often you refill it.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction and experience with the feeder, including its durability, ease of use, and design appeal.
5. Select all that apply for any issues you've encountered with the feeder.
6. Provide feedback on any problems or difficulties you've faced with the feeder.
7. Share any photos of your feeder, if possible.
8. Let us know if you'd like us to follow up with you.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`): This section is for your demographic details. Please enter your first name, last name, and email address.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **General Location** (`location`): Enter the city and state where you reside.
* **Feeder Information** (`feeder_information`): Describe your bird feeder in this section.
* **Type of Bird Feeder** (`feeder_type`): Select the type of feeder you have (e.g., Tube Feeder, Hopper Feeder, etc.).
* **Feeder Brand** (`feeder_brand`): Enter the brand name of your feeder.
* **Feeder Model** (`feeder_model`): Enter the model number or name of your feeder.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): Enter the date you purchased the feeder.
* **How Long Have You Used It** (`usage_duration`): Select how long you've used the feeder.
* **Feeder Location** (`feeder_location`): Select where the feeder is placed.
* **Feeder Height** (`feeder_height`): Select the height at which the feeder is mounted.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the feeder.
* **Product Ratings** (`product_ratings`): This section is for any additional comments about the product.
* **Durability Rating** (`durability_rating`): Rate the feeder's durability.
* **Ease of Cleaning** (`ease_of_cleaning`): Rate how easy it is to clean the feeder.
* **Ease of Refilling** (`ease_of_refilling`): Rate how easy it is to refill the feeder.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): Rate the value you get for the price.
* **Design Appeal** (`design_appeal`): Rate the feeder's design appeal.
* **Weather Resistance** (`weather_resistance`): Rate the feeder's resistance to weather conditions.
* **Squirrel Resistance** (`squirrel_resistance`): Rate how well the feeder deters squirrels.
* **Seed Capacity** (`seed_capacity`): Select how much seed the feeder holds.
* **Bird Visits** (`bird_visits`): Describe your bird visits in this section.
* **Frequency of Bird Visits** (`frequency_of_visits`): Select how often birds visit the feeder.
* **Number of Bird Species Observed** (`number_of_species`): Select the approximate number of bird species you've observed.
* **Bird Species Observed** (`bird_species_observed`): List the bird species you've observed.
* **Favorite Visiting Birds** (`favorite_visiting_birds`): Choose your favorite bird species that visits.
* **Type of Seed Used** (`seed_type_used`): Select the type of seed you use.
* **Seed Waste Level** (`seed_waste`): Rate the level of seed waste.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`): Select all that apply for any issues you've faced with the feeder.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Describe any issues you've encountered.
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance`): This section is for any comments about feeder maintenance.
* **How Often Do You Clean** (`cleaning_frequency`): Select how often you clean the feeder.
* **Maintenance Difficulty** (`maintenance_difficulty`): Rate how difficult it is to maintain the feeder.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements_suggested`): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Best Features** (`best_features`): List the features you like most about the feeder.
* **Worst Features** (`worst_features`): List the features you like least about the feeder.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`): Rate whether you would recommend the feeder to others.
* **Repurchase** (`repurchase`): Rate whether you would repurchase the feeder.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Photos Available** (`photos_available`): Select whether you have photos to share.
* **Follow-up Permission** (`follow_up_permission`): Select whether we can contact you for follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve the product.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the `Additional Comments` field.
* If you have any photos to share, please select the `Photos Available` option and attach them to your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify it for you.
