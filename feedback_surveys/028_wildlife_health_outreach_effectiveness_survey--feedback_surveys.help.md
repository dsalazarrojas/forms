# Wildlife Health Outreach Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness of wildlife health outreach sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. Each question is designed to gather specific information about your experience with the wildlife health outreach session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wildlife Health Outreach Survey** (`wildlife_health_outreach_survey`, select_multiple, required): Please rate whether you found the wildlife health outreach survey informative (Yes) or not (No).
* **How do you rate your knowledge on Wildlife Health Outreach before this session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_effectiveness_survey`, number): Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high, how much you knew about wildlife health before this session.
* **What did you learn about wildlife health during this session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_survey`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the topics that you learned about wildlife health during this session.
* **What type of wildlife health topics were covered during this session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_session`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the topics that were covered during the session.
* **What outcomes or results do you think will come from this outreach session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the outcomes or results you think will come from this session.
* **What are the next steps or follow-up actions you would like to see after this session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_next_steps`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the next steps or follow-up actions you would like to see after this session.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of this wildlife health outreach session?** (`wildlife_health_outreach_integrity_rating`, select_multiple, required): Please rate the effectiveness of this session as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Please add any additional comments or feedback you have about the session.
