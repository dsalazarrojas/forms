# Cylinder Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cylinder Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from users about their experiences with a product or service. This form helps the development team to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Rating** (`user_rating`, select_one): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the cylinder. You can choose between Very dissatisfied, Neutral, and Very satisfied.
2. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the cylinder. You can select between Very dissatisfied, Neutral, and Very satisfied.
3. **Performance** (`performance`, select_multiple): Rate the cylinder's performance. You can select between Poor, Average, and Excellent.
4. **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number): This field is used to rate the maintenance cost of the cylinder. Please enter a numerical value between 0 and 100.
5. **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_one): Rate the quality of the product. You can select between Poor, Average, and Excellent.
6. **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, note): Share any improvement suggestions you have for the product or service.
7. **Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback.
8. **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
9. **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
10. **Department** (`department`, text): If you're contacting a specific department, please let us know which one.
11. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title.
12. **Shift** (`shift`, text): Enter your shift.
13. **Date** (`date`, date): Enter the date you used the product or service.
14. **Time** (`time`, time): Enter the time you used the product or service.
15. **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, text): Enter the time zone you were in when using the product or service.
16. **Type of Feedback** (`feedback_type`, text): Describe the type of feedback you're providing.
17. **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text): Describe the feedback you have about the product or service.
18. **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple): Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 0-100. You can select between multiple options.
19. **Rating Scale Comment** (`rating_scale_comment`, text): Enter your comments about the rating scale.
20. **Product ID** (`product_id`, text): If you're familiar with our product ID system, enter the ID of the product you're providing feedback on.
21. **Product Name** (`product_name`, text): Enter the name of the product you're providing feedback on.
22. **Product Description** (`product_description`, text): Enter a brief description of the product you're providing feedback on.
23. **Product Status** (`product_status`, select_one): If the product is active, inactive, or archived.
24. **Comments** (`user_comments`, text): Share any additional comments you have about your feedback.
25. **Submit** (`submit`, text): Once you've filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
- Keep your comments concise and clear.
- Check your contact information to ensure we can contact you if needed.
