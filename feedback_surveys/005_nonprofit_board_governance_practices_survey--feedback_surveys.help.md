# Nonprofit Board Governance Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the board governance practices of a nonprofit organization. The goal is to assess the current state of the board's composition, meeting frequency and length, documentation, and attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and fill in the required information. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Non Profit Organizational Chart** (1): Provide a brief description of your non-profit organization's chart, including its mission, purpose, and main programs and services.
- **Board Size** (2): Select the size of your board. Options are:
  * Small: less than 5 members
  * Medium: between 5-11 members
  * Large: more than 11 members
  * Other (Specify): if your board size is not listed here, select this option and provide details in the "Specify" field.
- **Board Composition** (3): Please describe your board composition, including information about its diversity, member terms, and any special committees or subcommittees.
- **Board Terms** (4): If you have a board with terms, please describe their length and the process for renewal.
- **Board Meeting Frequency** (5): Describe the frequency of your board meetings.
- **Board Meeting Length** (6): Describe the average length of your board meetings.
- **Executive Committee Meeting Length** (7): Describe the average length of your executive committee meetings.
- **Board Meeting Agenda** (8): Describe the main topics or items on your board meeting agenda.
- **Board Meeting Minutes** (9): Describe how your board meeting minutes are recorded and kept.
- **Board Meeting Attendance** (10): Describe the typical number of attendees at your board meetings.
- **Board Meeting Location** (11): Describe the location of your board meetings.
- **Board Meeting Start Time** (12): Enter the typical start time of your board meetings.
- **Board Meeting End Time** (13): Enter the typical end time of your board meetings.
- **Board Meeting Agenda Item** (14): Describe the main topic of discussion during the last board meeting.
- **Executive Committee Meeting Agenda Item** (15): Describe the main topic of discussion during the last executive committee meeting.
- **Board Meeting Minutes Attendance** (16): Enter the number of attendees who signed the meeting minutes.
- **Board Meeting Documentation** (17): Select whether you keep board meeting minutes, and if so, how often:
  * True: you keep meeting minutes
  * False: you do not keep meeting minutes
  * N/A: you do not have a board
- **Board Meeting Documentation Method** (18): If you keep meeting minutes, describe your method (e.g., paper, digital, or a combination).
- **Board Meeting Documentation Format** (19): If you keep meeting minutes, describe the format (e.g., detailed, brief, or a combination).
- **Board Meeting Attendance Comments** (20): Describe any comments or observations you have about board meeting attendance.
- **Board Meeting Documentation Comments** (21): If you keep meeting minutes, describe any comments or observations you have about the documentation process.
- **Executive Committee Meeting Documentation** (22): Select whether you keep executive committee meeting minutes, and if so, how often:
  * True: you keep meeting minutes
  * False: you do not keep meeting minutes
  * N/A: you do not have an executive committee
- **Executive Committee Meeting Documentation Format** (23): If you keep executive committee meeting minutes, describe the format (e.g., paper, digital, or a combination).
- **Executive Committee Meeting Attendance Comments** (24): Describe any comments or observations you have about executive committee meeting attendance.
- **Executive Committee Meeting Documentation Comments** (25): If you keep executive committee meeting minutes, describe any comments or observations you have about the documentation process.
- **Board Committee Meetings** (26): Select whether your board has committee meetings, and if so, how often:
  * True: you have committee meetings
  * False: you do not have committee meetings
  * N/A: you do not have a board with committees
- **Executive Committee Meetings** (27): Select whether your executive committee has meetings, and if so, how often:
  * True: you have committee meetings
  * False: you do not have committee meetings
  * N/A: you do not have an executive committee
- **Executive Committee Meeting Frequency** (28): If you have executive committee meetings, describe their frequency.
- **Board Committee Meeting Frequency** (29): If you have board committee meetings, describe their frequency.
- **Board Committee Meeting Attendance** (30): Describe the typical number of attendees at your board committee meetings.
- **Executive Committee Meeting Agenda** (31): Describe the main topics or items on your executive committee meeting agenda.
- **Board Committee Meeting Agenda** (32): Describe the main topics or items on your board committee meeting agenda.
- **Board Committee Meeting Minutes Attendance** (33): Enter the number of attendees who signed the board committee meeting minutes.
- **Board Committee Meeting Documentation** (34): Select whether your board committee keeps meeting minutes, and if so, how often:
  * True: you keep meeting minutes
  * False: you do not keep meeting minutes
  * N/A: you do not have board committees
- **Board Committee Meeting Documentation Format** (35): If you keep board committee meeting minutes, describe the format (e.g., paper, digital, or a combination).
- **Board Committee Meeting Documentation Comments** (36): If you keep board committee meeting minutes, describe any comments or observations you have about the documentation process.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or observations that aren't addressed by these questions, please include them in the appropriate fields.
