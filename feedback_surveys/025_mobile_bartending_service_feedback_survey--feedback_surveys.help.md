# Mobile Bartending Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Bartending Service Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers who have used the service. This form will help us improve the service quality and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the first field.
2. Choose the type of service you used (Bartender, Event Planner, or Venue).
3. Enter the date of the event in the date field.
4. Enter the time of the event in the time field.
5. Enter the name of the event.
6. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
7. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
8. Share any suggestions you have for how we can improve the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required: false): Please enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Service Type** (select_one, required: false): Choose the type of service you used (Bartender, Event Planner, or Venue).
* **Date of Event** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time of Event** (time, required: false): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Event Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the event.
* **Rating** (number, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (text, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for how we can improve the service.
