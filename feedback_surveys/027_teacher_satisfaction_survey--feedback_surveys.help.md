# Teacher Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your overall experience with the school, including your satisfaction with teacher support, resources, communication, and other aspects of your interactions with the school's administration.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
- Fill out the form in its entirety, providing your email address and phone number to help us reach out to you if needed.
- You can use the "Other Comments" field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How was your overall experience with the school?** (`introduction`, `text`, required)
	+ Please describe your overall experience with the school, including any positive or negative aspects.
- **How would you rate the support you receive from your teachers?** (`teacher_support`, `number`, required)
	+ Rate your experience with the support you receive from your teachers on a scale.
- **How would you rate the quality of school resources provided?** (`school_resources`, `number`, required)
	+ Rate the quality of resources provided by the school.
- **How would you rate the communication from your teachers and school administration?** (`communication`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Rate the communication from teachers and school administration on one of the provided options.
- **What suggestions do you have for improving the school or teacher support?** (`feedback`, `note`, optional)
	+ Share any suggestions you have for improving the school or teacher support.
- **What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, required)
	+ Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, required)
	+ Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Is there anything else you would like to share with us?** (`other_comments`, `text`, optional)
	+ Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
- **How would you rate the teacher you have interacted with?** (`teacher_rating`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Rate the teacher you have interacted with on multiple options.
