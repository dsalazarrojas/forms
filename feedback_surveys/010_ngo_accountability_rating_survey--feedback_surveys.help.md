# NGO Accountability Rating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Accountability Rating Survey is a tool designed to help stakeholders evaluate the effectiveness and performance of non-governmental organizations (NGOs). The survey collects feedback on various aspects of the organization, including accountability, transparency, and overall quality. The data collected will inform decisions and improve the management and governance of NGOs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the Non-Government Organization that you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter the Name of the organization.
3. Rate the organization's performance on a scale of 1-5 for the following aspects:
   * Engagement
   * Transparency
   * Accountability
   * Governance
4. Indicate how often you review this organization:
   * Never
   * Rarely
   * Quarterly
   * Monthly
   * Weekly
   * Daily
5. Provide any additional Comments about the organization (optional).
6. Enter the Email address associated with your account (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non-Government Organization**: This field is used to select the non-governmental organization that you are providing feedback on. Please ensure that you select the correct organization from the list.
* **Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the organization. Please enter the name as it is known to you.
* **Rating**: This field is used to rate the organization's performance on a scale of 1-5. A rating of 1 indicates a low performance, while a rating of 5 indicates a high performance.
* **What is your overall rating for this organization?**: This field is used to rate the organization's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Engagement**: This field is used to rate the organization's engagement with its stakeholders on a scale of 1-5.
* **Transparency**: This field is used to rate the organization's transparency on a scale of 1-5.
* **Accountability**: This field is used to rate the organization's accountability on a scale of 1-5.
* **Governance**: This field is used to rate the organization's governance on a scale of 1-5.
* **How often do you review this organization?**: This field is used to indicate how often you review the organization on a scale of 1-6 options.
* **Comments**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback about the organization.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter the email address associated with your account. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address.
