# Volunteer Skill Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information from volunteers about their skills and availability for future opportunities. By completing this survey, you will help us better match volunteers with suitable positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email Address.
4. Choose your Volunteer Role.
5. Select the skills you have experience with.
6. Indicate your availability for future opportunities.
7. Provide any suggestions or ideas for future surveys, if desired.
8. Finally, consent to our terms by indicating your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for future opportunities.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, Required): Choose the role you hold or aspire to hold in the organization, whether Volunteer or Non-Volunteer.
* **What skills do you have?** (`skills_list`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the skills you have experience with from the list below:
	+ Leadership
	+ Communication
	+ Team Management
	+ Time Management
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Organization
	+ Public Speaking
	+ Project Management
	+ Event Planning
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Financial Management
	+ Fundraising
	+ Grant Writing
	+ Marketing
	+ Social Media
	+ Volunteering
	+ Grant Research
	+ Community Outreach
	+ Grant Management
	+ Budgeting
	+ Reporting
	+ Research
	+ Writing
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, Required): Select how you are available for future opportunities:
	+ Full-Time
	+ Part-Time
	+ On-Demand
* **Do you have any suggestions or ideas for future surveys?** (`volunteer_suggestions`, note, Optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions for future surveys, please provide them in the text box below.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, Required): Choose your consent to our terms:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree
