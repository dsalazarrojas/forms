# Poverty Awareness Education Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from individuals who have participated in a poverty awareness education session or training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read each question and provide your answers accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal info** (`demographic_info`): This is your personal information, including your name, email, or any other identifying details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Income** (`income`): Please report your current income, if applicable. You can select from the following options:
  - Less than 1,000
  - 1,000 to 2,500
  - 2,500 to 3,000
  - More than 3,000
* **Household size** (`household_size`): Report the number of individuals in your household.
* **Educational level** (`education_level`): Please select the highest educational level you have achieved:
  - High
  - Low
* **Current occupation** (`current_occupation`): Report your current occupation or work description.
* **Occupation other (optional)** (`current_occupation_other`): If your occupation is not listed, please specify it here.
* **Income category** (`income_category`): Select the income category that best suits your current income:
  - Less than 1,000
  - 1,000 to 2,500
  - 2,500 to 3,000
  - More than 3,000
* **Session quality** (`session_quality`): Rate your overall experience with the session:
  - Very good
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
* **Behavior intentions** (`behavior_intentions`): Please select the reasons why you attended the session:
  - To learn more
  - To share with others
  - To improve skills
  - To help others
* **Additional information** (`note`): If you have any comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Please submit your answers to help us analyze the effectiveness of our education initiatives.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any comments or suggestions about the session, please add them here.
* **Session notes** (`session_notes`): Please add any notes or observations about the session you attended.
* **Session attendance** (`session_attendance`): Report the number of people you attended the session with.
* **Session length** (`session_length`): Report the length of the session:
* **Session date** (`session_date`): Report the date of the session:
* **Session time** (`session_time`): Report the session time:
* **Training participants** (`training_participants`): Report the number of people who attended the session with you.
* **Training facilitator** (`training_facilitator`): If you have a facilitator for the session, please report their name.
* **Training location** (`training_location`): Report the location of the training session:
* **Training topic** (`training_topic`): Report the topic of the session:
* **Training notes** (`training_notes`): Add any notes or observations about the session.

Note: Some questions may seem redundant or have multiple answers that seem similar. In such cases, please answer based on your actual experience and understanding.

Tips: Please answer each question accurately and honestly to help us better understand the effectiveness of our education initiatives.
