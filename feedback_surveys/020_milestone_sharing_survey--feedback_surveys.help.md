# Milestone Sharing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Milestone Sharing Survey" is a form used to collect feedback on project milestones from project team members. This form helps to ensure that all team members' contributions are captured and acknowledged, fostering a culture of collaboration and shared responsibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the project you are providing feedback on (Milestone Sharing, Project Management, etc.).
2. Enter the project name and description (Project).
3. Choose the project phase (Initiation, Planning, etc.) and provide a brief description (Project Phase).
4. Select the wins achieved (Project Wins, Departmental Wins, etc.).
5. Describe the wins in detail (Wins Details).
6. Add any comments or suggestions (Comments).
7. Choose the project lead and team member responsible (Project Lead, Team Member).
8. Select the department (Department).
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Attach any relevant files (File, Additional File).
11. Add any additional comments or suggestions (Additional Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter the project name and description.
* **Project Phase** (`name`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the project phase (Initiation, Planning, etc.).
* **Project Phase Details** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter a brief description of the project phase.
* **Wins** (`name`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select the wins achieved (Project Wins, Departmental Wins, etc.).
* **Wins Details** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Describe the wins in detail.
* **Comments** (`name`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments** (`name`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Satisfied** (`name`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how satisfied you are with the current state of the project.
* **Date Submitted** (`name`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted** (`name`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time of submission.
* **Email** (`name`, `email`, **optional**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number.
* **File** (`name`, `text`, **optional**): Attach any relevant files.
* **Additional File** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Attach an additional file.
* **Project Lead** (`name`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the project lead.
* **Team Member** (`name`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select the team members involved.
* **Department** (`name`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the department responsible.
* **Date** (`name`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (`name`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time of submission.
