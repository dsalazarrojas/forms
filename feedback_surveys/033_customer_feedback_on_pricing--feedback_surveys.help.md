# Customer Feedback On Pricing - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on the pricing of our products or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of pricing.
2. Select the options that best fit your perception for each question.
3. For questions with a "number" type field, please provide a numerical answer between 1-10, where 1 is "not at all" and 10 is "very much".
4. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction with our price** (Overall satisfaction, Number, Required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our price on a scale of 1-10.
* **How do you perceive our price?** (Price perception, Select one, Required: No): Choose one of the options that best describes your perception of our price.
* **How does it compare to your expected value?** (Value for money, Select one, Required: No): Select one of the options that best describes how our price compares to your expected value.
* **How clear is our pricing information?** (Pricing clarity, Select one, Required: No): Choose one of the options that best describes the clarity of our pricing information.
* **How does it compare to other prices?** (Price range, Select one, Required: No): Select one of the options that best describes how our price compares to other prices in the market.
* **Are you more or less likely to buy because of our price?** (Price change, Select one, Required: No): Choose one of the options that best describes how our price influences your purchasing decision.
* **How important is price in your purchasing decision?** (Price influence, Number, Required): Rate the importance of price in your purchasing decision on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional notes** (Not provided, Textarea, Required: No): This is an optional text field for you to provide any additional feedback or thoughts you may have about our price. 

Note: If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us. 

# Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Select the options that best fit your perception or experience.
- If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please use the "Additional notes" field.
