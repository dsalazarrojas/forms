# Medical Centre Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users regarding their experience at a medical centre. This feedback is crucial in helping the centre identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to better serve their patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction and review the feedback questions provided.
2.  Choose your rating options for various aspects of the medical centre, such as the nurse/staff, clinician, environment, services, and facilities.
3.  Provide an overall rating of your satisfaction with your visit.
4.  Leave any comments or suggestions for improvement.
5.  Enter contact information if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the feedback form. Read it to understand the purpose and context of the feedback questions.
*   **Rate Our Medical Centre**: This section allows you to rate the medical centre on various aspects, such as the nurse/staff, clinician, environment, services, and facilities. Choose one of the provided options to rate your experience.
*   **Visit Date**: Record the date of your visit to the medical centre. This helps the centre track your visit and reference it in their records.
*   **What Can We Improve On?**: Provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement you have regarding the medical centre. Be as specific as possible to help the centre understand your concerns.
*   **Rating of Nurse/Staff**: Rate the quality of care provided by the nurse or staff at the medical centre.
*   **Rating of Clinician**: Rate the quality of care provided by the clinician.
*   **Rating of Environment**: Rate the condition and ambiance of the medical centre's environment.
*   **Rating of Services**: Rate the quality of services provided by the medical centre.
*   **Rating of Facilities**: Rate the quality of facilities at the medical centre.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: Provide an overall rating of your satisfaction with your visit.
*   **Comments**: Leave any comments or suggestions for improvement.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.
*   **Medical Centre Address**: Enter the address of the medical centre for reference purposes.

Please take a few minutes to review each field carefully and provide accurate information to help the medical centre better understand your experience.
