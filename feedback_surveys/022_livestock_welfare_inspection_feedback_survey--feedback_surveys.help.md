# Livestock Welfare Inspection Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about the overall welfare of livestock on farms. It's used by inspectors to gather detailed observations and assessments on various aspects of livestock care. Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately to provide valuable insights for improving farm management and animal care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer all required questions**. The required fields are denoted by an asterisk (\*) on the right side of each field label.
2. **Provide specific details**. For fields like "Overall Animal Condition", please select all options that apply.
3. **Double-check your answers** before submitting. Ensure that your responses are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, optional): This field is for recording the name of the farm or location where the livestock inspection took place.
* **Livestock Farm ID** (`farm_id`, number, required): This is a unique identifier for the farm, which helps us track and reference specific farm data.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Record the date of the inspection, including the calendar year, month, and day.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection, including the hour and minute.
* **Overall Animal Condition** (`animal_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for the overall condition of the animals, using the provided dropdown menu. Examples:
	+ Very Healthy
	+ Somewhat Healthy
	+ Unhealthy
	+ Very Unhealthy
* **Food/Water Supplies** (`food_water_supplies`, text, optional): Briefly describe the availability and quality of food and water supplies for the livestock.
* **Shelter Provided** (`shelter_provided`, text, optional): Describe the type and condition of shelter provided for the livestock.
* **Handling and Harshness** (`handling_and_harshness`, text, optional): Report any instances of harsh handling or stress observed during the inspection.
* **Overall Conditions** (`overall_conditions`, text, optional): Summarize the general condition of the farm and livestock, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements or changes for the farm to consider, based on your observations and findings.
