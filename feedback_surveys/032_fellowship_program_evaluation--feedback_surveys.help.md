# Fellowship Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fellowship Program Evaluation form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness and impact of a fellowship program. It collects feedback and ratings on various aspects of the program, helping us to identify areas of improvement and areas of success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the submission option that best describes your role in the fellowship program. You can choose from Participant Feedback and Rating, Organizer Feedback and Rating, or Overall Evaluation.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* `participantName`, `participantTitle`, `participantOrganization`, `participantEmail`, `participantPhone`, `participantRating`, `participantComments`
	* `organizerTitle`, `organizerOrganization`, `organizerEmail`, `organizerPhone`, `organizerRating`
	* `overallSatisfaction`, `overallImpact`, `overallValue`, `additionalComments`
3. Provide your feedback and ratings on the following fields:
	* `programImpact`, `programQuality`, `programValue`
	* `participantSatisfaction`
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the `additionalComments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **OverallRating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **programImpact**: Rate the impact of the fellowship program on your work or organization on a scale of 1-5.
* **programQuality**: Rate the quality of the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **programValue**: Rate the value of the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **participantSatisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments on your experience with the fellowship program.
* **improvementSuggestions**: Suggest any improvements for the fellowship program.
* **evaluationDate**: The date you are evaluating the program.
* **participantName**: Your name.
* **participantTitle**: Your title.
* **participantOrganization**: Your organization.
* **participantEmail**: Your email.
* **participantPhone**: Your phone number.
* **participantRating**: Your rating of the program.
* **participantComments**: Your comments about the program.
* **organizerTitle**: The title of the organizer.
* **organizerOrganization**: The organization of the organizer.
* **organizerEmail**: The email of the organizer.
* **organizerPhone**: The phone number of the organizer.
* **organizerRating**: The rating of the organizer.
* **overallSatisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **overallImpact**: Rate the overall impact of the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **overallValue**: Rate the overall value of the fellowship program on a scale of 1-5.
* **additionalComments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all the required fields to ensure that your feedback is complete and accurate.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this guide for clarification.
* The ratings should be based on your honest opinion and experience with the fellowship program.
* Please be specific and provide concrete examples when providing feedback and comments.
