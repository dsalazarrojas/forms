# CI/CD Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the CI/CD (Continuous Integration / Continuous Deployment) process, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to you and your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your **Your Name** (optional but recommended) in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
3. Select your **primary role** from the list provided.
4. Indicate how often you **Use CI/CD Pipeline** (e.g., multiple times daily, daily, etc.).
5. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction**, **Build Speed Satisfaction**, **Deployment Reliability**, **Error Reporting Clarity**, **Pipeline Stability**, **Ease of Use**, and **Documentation Quality**.
6. Select all that apply from the list of **Common Issues Encountered**.
7. If you have a **biggest pain point**, type it in the text field.
8. If there are **features you would like to request**, type them in the text field.
9. Select the **areas where you need more training**.
10. Indicate whether you would **recommend this CI/CD pipeline** to others.
11. Provide any **additional feedback** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is a field where you can optionally fill in your name, so we can address you in follow-up communications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is for your email address, in case we need to contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Your Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Please select your primary role within your organization.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): This field is for your team name, if you'd like to specify it.
* **How Often Do You Use CI/CD Pipeline** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use the CI/CD pipeline.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the CI/CD process.
* **Build Speed Satisfaction** (`build_speed`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the build speed.
* **Deployment Reliability** (`deployment_reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the deployment reliability.
* **Error Reporting Clarity** (`error_reporting`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of error messages.
* **Pipeline Stability** (`pipeline_stability`, select_one, required): Rate the pipeline stability.
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of use.
* **Documentation Quality** (`documentation_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of documentation provided.
* **Common Issues Encountered** (`common_issues`, select_multiple, required): Select all issues you've encountered with the CI/CD pipeline.
* **Biggest Pain Point** (`biggest_pain_point`, text, optional): If there's one issue that frustrates you the most, type it in this field.
* **Requested Features** (`feature_requests`, text, optional): If there are features you'd like to see, type them in this field.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select areas where you need more training or education.
* **Would You Recommend This Pipeline** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this CI/CD pipeline to others.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Any other comments or suggestions you have, please provide them in this field.
