# Neurodiversity In Learning Environments Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather opinions and feedback on the support for neurodivergent learners in learning environments. It is intended to be a subjective and informal assessment tool for stakeholders interested in understanding how to best support these learners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers to help improve support for neurodivergent learners.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is neurodiversity?**: This is an open-ended question that asks you to briefly explain your understanding of neurodiversity.
* **What is neurodiversity in learning environments?**: This question seeks to understand your perspective on how neurodiversity applies to learning settings.
* **How do you feel about the overall support for neurodivergent learners in your school/university?**: This question gauges your opinion on the current support system for neurodivergent learners.
* **What specific aspects of support do you feel are lacking?**: In this question, you will identify which areas of support are insufficient or require improvement.
* **What specific aspects of support would you like to see improved?**: This question is an opportunity for you to suggest specific areas of support that would benefit neurodivergent learners.
* **Which of the following areas would benefit from improvements?**: Select all areas you believe require improvement.
	+ **Communication**
	+ **Lesson planning**
	+ **Resources**
	+ **Staff training**
	+ **Facilities**
* **What specific resources do you think would be helpful to support neurodivergent learners?**: Identify all resources that could assist in supporting neurodivergent learners.
	+ **Text-based communication tools**
	+ **Visual aids for learning**
	+ **Physical accommodations**
	+ **Emotional support staff**
	+ **Assistive technology**
* **What specific suggestions do you have for how to improve support for neurodivergent learners?**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions you have on how to enhance support for neurodivergent learners.
