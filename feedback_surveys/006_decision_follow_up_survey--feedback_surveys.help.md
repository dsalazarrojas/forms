# Decision Follow Up Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to follow up on the decision made by a decision leader on a project. The form collects information on the decision leader's satisfaction with the decision outcome, the project's impact, and any suggestions for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to save your responses when you have completed filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Decision Leader** (`decision_leader`, text, not required): Please enter the name or title of the decision leader who made the decision on this project.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the project that was decided on.
- **Decision Outcome** (`decision_outcome`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
  - Positive: The outcome was favorable.
  - Negative: The outcome was not favorable.
  - Neutral: The outcome was neither favorable nor not favorable.
- **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the name or title of the project manager who oversaw the project.
- **Date of Decision** (`date_of_decision`, date, not required): Enter the date when the decision was made.
- **Time of Decision** (`time_of_decision`, time, not required): Enter the time when the decision was made.
- **Decision Satisfied** (`decision_satisfied`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply:
  - True
  - False
- **Impact on Project** (`impact_on_project`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply:
  - High
  - Low
- **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
- **Other (if needed)** (`suggestion_for_improvement_other`, text, not required): If the previous field does not apply, enter any other suggestions for improvement here.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your project manager or decision leader for guidance.
- The form is only for collecting information and does not affect the decision itself.
