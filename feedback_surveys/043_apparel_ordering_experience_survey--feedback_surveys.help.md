# Apparel Ordering Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apparel Ordering Experience Survey is designed to gather detailed feedback from customers on their experience with ordering apparel from the site. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid order number ready to reference.
2. Select the corresponding options for each section based on your personal experience.
3. Fill in the text fields with any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order Number (Order Number)
 * **Order Number** (Order Number, required/optional): Please enter your valid order number for reference. 

### How easy was it to find what you were looking for? (Browsing Ease)
 * **How easy was it to find what you were looking for?** (How easy was it, required/optional): Rate your experience on a 1 to 5 scale, where 1 means very difficult and 5 means very easy.

### Rating for the Checkout and Payment Process (Checkout Experience)
 * **Rating for the Checkout and Payment Process** (Rating for the Checkout and Payment Process, required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience with the checkout and payment process: Seamless, Average, Difficult, Failed several times.

### Was the product information and sizing guide clear? (Product Info Clarity)
 * **Was the product information and sizing guide clear?** (Was the product information and sizing guide clear, required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience: Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, Confusing.

### Satisfaction with Delivery Time (Delivery Time Satisfaction)
 * **Satisfaction with Delivery Time** (Satisfaction with Delivery Time, required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience: Faster than expected, On time, Late, Much later than expected.

### Quality of Packaging Upon Arrival (Packaging Quality)
 * **Quality of Packaging Upon Arrival** (Quality of Packaging Upon Arrival, required/optional): Please rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 means very low quality and 5 means very high quality.

### Did the item match the online description and photos? (Product Accuracy)
 * **Did the item match the online description and photos?** (Did the item match the online description and photos, required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience: Yes, exactly, Mostly, No, it was different.

### Accuracy of the Sizing (Size Fit Rating)
 * **Accuracy of the Sizing (How did it fit?)** (Accuracy of the Sizing (How did it fit?), required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience: Fits perfectly, Runs small, Runs large.

### Satisfaction with Customer Support (Support Satisfaction)
 * **Satisfaction with Customer Support (if contacted)** (Satisfaction with Customer Support, optional/optional): Select one of the options to rate your experience: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Did not contact.

### How likely are you to purchase from us again? (Repurchase Intent)
 * **How likely are you to purchase from us again?** (How likely are you to purchase from us again, required/optional): Please rate your experience on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 means not likely at all and 10 means very likely.

### Any other comments or suggestions for us? (General Feedback)
 * **Any other comments or suggestions for us?** (Any other comments or suggestions for us, optional/optional): Please use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
