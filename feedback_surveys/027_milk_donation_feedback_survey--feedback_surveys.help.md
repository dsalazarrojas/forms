# Milk Donation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from milk donors about their experience with the milk donation system. The information collected will help us understand how donors perceive and interact with the system, informing improvements to the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all options that apply to your experience:
	* If you donated milk, select "Yes" to indicate that your donation experience was positive.
	* If you experienced issues with your donation, select "No" and be prepared to provide more details in the "Comments" field.
2.  Indicate your overall satisfaction with the donation process by selecting a number from 1 to 10.
3.  Provide your name in the "What is your name?" field.
4.  Select the frequency with which you plan to donate milk in the "How frequently do you plan to donate milk?" dropdown.
5.  Indicate where you donated milk by selecting all applicable options in the "Where did you donate milk?" dropdown.
6.  (Optional) If you have any additional contact information or feedback, you can provide it in the "What is your contact info?" and "Additional feedback notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your donation experience?** (donation_experience)
	+ Select all options that apply to your experience with the milk donation process.
	+ This field is not required, but providing more information can help us understand your perspective.
* **Overall satisfaction with the donation process** (satisfaction)
	+ Select a number from 1 to 10 to indicate your overall satisfaction.
	+ This field is not required, but providing this information can help us understand your level of satisfaction.
* **What is your name?** (donor_name)
	+ Enter your name for our records.
	+ This field is not required, but providing your name can help us better understand your feedback.
* **How frequently do you plan to donate milk?** (donation_frequency)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your plan for future donations.
	+ This field is required and is used to understand your commitment to the milk donation process.
* **Where did you donate milk?** (donation_location)
	+ Select all applicable options to indicate where you donated milk.
	+ This field is required and is used to understand where you donated milk.
* **What is your contact info?** (contact_info)
	+ Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with us.
	+ This field is not required, but providing your contact info can help us stay in touch.
* **Additional feedback notes** (feedback_notes)
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, provide them here.
	+ This field is not required, but your feedback is valued.
* **Comments** (comments)
	+ If you have any additional comments or concerns, provide them here.
	+ This field is not required, but providing your comments can help us understand your perspective.
