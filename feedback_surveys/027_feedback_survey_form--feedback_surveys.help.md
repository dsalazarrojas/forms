# Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users to help us improve our service. Your responses will be used to identify areas for growth and development, and will inform our future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the correct option from the dropdown menu on page 2.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey** (`Feedback Survey`, text, optional): This is the main feedback section where you can provide your thoughts and suggestions. You can type in as much or as little as you like.
* **How can we improve?** (`Satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to let us know how we can improve:
	+ **Satisfaction**: You're happy with our service as it is.
	+ **Services**: We could improve our services or offer more services.
	+ **Support**: Our support team or resources could be better.
* **Additional Feedback** (`Additional Feedback`, note, optional): This is an open text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments. This is a great place to share your thoughts on things that didn't quite fit into the previous questions, or to suggest new ideas.
