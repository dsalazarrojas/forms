# love celebration feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Love Celebration Feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees. This form helps us understand your experience, provide a space for you to rate our event, and gather your suggestions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers.
3. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and share a little about your background.
* **Guest Experience** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Share your experience at the event. What did you enjoy? What could be improved?
* **Event Type** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable event types that apply to your experience:
	+ Wedding
	+ Love theme party
	+ Love theme wedding anniversary
* **Rating** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5. 1 = Not Satisfied, 5 = Very Satisfied
* **Suggestions** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for future events.
* **Feedback** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`id: 7`, `type: email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Date of Event** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, required: false): This is where you can enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time of Event** (`id: 10`, `type: time`, required: false): This is where you can enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Event Location** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Share the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): A brief description of the event.
* **Event Host** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Share a little about the event host.
* **Event Host Email** (`id: 14`, `type: email`, required: false): If you'd like to contact the event host, please provide their email address.
* **Event Host Phone** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): If you'd like to contact the event host via phone, please provide their phone number.
* **Event Host Notes** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Any additional notes or comments about the event host.
* **Event Venue** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Share the name of the venue where the event took place.
* **Event Venue Notes** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Any additional notes or comments about the venue.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers.
* If a field is not applicable, you can leave it blank.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the event host or email the event organizers.
