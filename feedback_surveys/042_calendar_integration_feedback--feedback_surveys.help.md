# Calendar Integration Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about their experience with calendar integrations.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, make sure you have the following information:

* A stable internet connection
* Familiarity with the integration being used

1. **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for follow-up or communication purposes. (Optional)
3. **Company Name**: Enter your organization name, if applicable. (Optional)
4. **Your Role**: Select your job title or position within the organization.
5. **Calendar Platform Used**: Choose the calendar platform you are using (e.g., Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, etc.).
6. **Integration Used**: Select the specific integration being used (e.g., CRM, Project management, etc.).
7. **How Long Have You Used This**: Select the duration of use for the integration.
8. **Usage Frequency**: Choose how often you use the integration.
9. **Ease of Use**: Rate your experience with the ease of use of the integration.
10. **Reliability Rating**: Rate the reliability of the integration.
11. **Sync Accuracy**: Rate the accuracy of calendar syncs.
12. **Speed and Performance**: Rate the performance speed of the integration.
13. **Features Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with available features.
14. **Issues Encountered**: Select any issues you have experienced with the integration.
15. **Issue Description**: Describe any issues you've experienced in detail.
16. **Missing Features**: List any features you feel are missing from the integration.
17. **Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest ways to improve the integration.
18. **Would You Recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend the integration to others.
19. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the integration.
20. **Additional Feedback**: Provide any other comments or feedback.
