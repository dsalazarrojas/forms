# Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey is designed to measure customer satisfaction and gather demographic information about our customers. The survey consists of several questions that ask about your experience with our company, its products or services, and your willingness to recommend it to others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate response from the options provided.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with our products or services?** (Customer Satisfaction) (`1`, `number`, optional): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with our products or services. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means not satisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **How likely are you to recommend our company?** (Likelihood to Recommend) (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your willingness to recommend our company to others. Select one of the options: Not Likely, Somewhat Likely, Very Likely, or Extremely Likely.
* **Reason for not recommending** (Reason for Not Recommending) (`3`, `text`, optional): If you answered "Not Likely" or "Somewhat Likely" in the previous question, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** (Customer Satisfaction Score) (`4`, `number`, optional): This question is not applicable, as the NPS is calculated based on your responses to the previous questions.
* **Demographic Questions** (Demographic Information) (`5`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional demographic information you feel is relevant to your response.
* **What is your job title?** (Job Title) (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Which team or department do you belong to?** (Team or Department) (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the teams or departments that apply to your role.
* **How many years have you been with our company?** (Years with Company) (`8`, `number`, optional): Please provide the number of years you have been with our company.
* **What is your level of experience with our company?** (Level of Experience) (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your level of experience.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments) (`10`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our company.
* **What is your score?** (Score) (`11`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options: Yes or No.
* **Reason for score** (Reason for Score) (`12`, `text`, optional): If you answered "No" in the previous question, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Open Comments 2** (Open Comments 2) (`13`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our company.
* **What is your role in the company?** (Role in Company) (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the roles that apply to your position within the company.
* **How long have you been a customer?** (Time as a Customer) (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to how long you have been a customer of our company.
* **Open Comments 3** (Open Comments 3) (`16`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our company.
* **How long would you use our products or services?** (Product/Service Frequency) (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to how often you use our products or services.
* **What is your level of interest in our company?** (Level of Interest) (`18`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your level of interest in our company.
* **What is your level of satisfaction with our product or service?** (Satisfaction Level) (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your level of satisfaction with our product or service.
* **How would you rate our customer service?** (Customer Service Rating) (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to how you rate our customer service.
* **Open Comments 4** (Open Comments 4) (`21`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our company.
* **How likely is it that you would switch to a competitor?** (Switching Likelihood) (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to how likely you are to switch to a competitor.
* **Open Comments 5** (Open Comments 5) (`23`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our company.
* **What is the likelihood of recommending our company to a friend or family member?** (Recommendation Likelihood) (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options: Not Likely, Somewhat Likely, Very Likely, or Extremely Likely.
* **Demographic Questions 13** (Demographic Questions 13) (`25`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional demographic information you feel is relevant to your response.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" sections.
* If you answered "Not Likely" or "Somewhat Likely" to the "How likely are you to recommend our company?" question, please provide a brief explanation.
* If you answered "No" to the "What is your score?" question, please provide a brief explanation.
* Please select all the options that apply to your answers, if necessary.
