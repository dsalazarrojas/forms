# Label Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Label Management Survey aims to collect feedback from different teams about their needs, challenges, and suggestions for improvement. Your input is crucial in helping us understand your team's requirements and make informed decisions about label management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each question regarding the availability of specific teams.
2. Provide a brief description or explanation for each question that asks for text input.
3. If you have any comments or additional information regarding label design or workflows, please use the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Operations Team

* **operations_team**: 
  Please select "Yes" if the operations team is available for label management, "No" otherwise.

### Page 2: Label Design

* **label_design**: Please provide any specific comments or suggestions about the label design for your team.

### Page 3: Manufacturing Challenges

* **manufacturing_challenges**: Describe any challenges you face with manufacturing that are related to label management.

### Page 4: Logistics Needs

* **logistics_needs**: Please select the team that best represents the logistics needs of your team. Options are Operations Team, Logistics Team, and Manufacturing Team.

### Page 5: Feature Requests

* **feature_requests**: Provide any specific feature requests or suggestions for label management improvements.

### Page 6: Challenges

* **challenges**: Describe any general challenges your team faces with label management.

### Page 7: Workflows

* **workflows**: Describe the current state of your team's workflows related to label management.

### Page 8: Label Workflows

* **label_workflows**: Describe the current state of your team's label management workflows.

### Page 9: Current Label Challenges

* **current_label_challenges**: Describe any current challenges you face with label management.

### Page 10: Feature Needs

* **feature_needs**: Provide any specific feature requests or suggestions for label management improvements.

### Page 11: Comments

* **comments**: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving label management.

### Page 12: Label Design Comments

* **label_design_comments**: Please provide any specific comments or suggestions about the label design.

### Page 13: Manufacturing Needs

* **manufacturing_needs**: Describe any specific needs your team has regarding manufacturing and label management.

### Page 14: Workflows Comments

* **workflows_comments**: Please provide any comments or suggestions about the current state of your team's workflows.

### Page 15: Logistics Needs

* **logistics_needs**: Describe any specific needs your team has regarding logistics and label management.
