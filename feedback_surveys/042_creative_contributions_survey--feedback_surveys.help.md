# Creative Contributions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Creative Contributions Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on how to improve and expand our creative projects. This form helps us understand what works well and what doesn't in our current offerings, and what exciting new ideas users would like to see implemented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a category**: Choose the category that best describes the feedback or suggestion you are providing. You can choose from "Feedback Surveys", "General Feedback", or "Product Feedback".
2. **Provide a description**: In the description field, briefly explain your thought on how to improve or expand our creative projects. Be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples or ideas.
3. **Enter a form ID**: Please enter a unique ID for your feedback or suggestion, so we can easily reference it later.
4. **Choose an assigned tool**: If you have a preferred tool or platform in mind for implementing your feedback or suggestion, please let us know.
5. **Upload an output file**: If you have a file related to your feedback or suggestion, please upload it to help us better understand your idea.
6. **Optional: Jotform form ID**: If you have a Jotform form related to your feedback or suggestion, please enter its ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: This is the category that best describes the feedback or suggestion you are providing. Choose from "Feedback Surveys", "General Feedback", or "Product Feedback".
* **Description**: In this field, please provide a brief description of your feedback or suggestion. Be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples or ideas.
* **Form ID**: Please enter a unique ID for your feedback or suggestion, so we can easily reference it later.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a preferred tool or platform in mind for implementing your feedback or suggestion, please let us know. This will help us understand how to best utilize your idea.
* **Output File**: If you have a file related to your feedback or suggestion, please upload it to help us better understand your idea.
* **Jotform Form ID**: This field is optional, but if you have a Jotform form related to your feedback or suggestion, please enter its ID.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your feedback or suggestion.
* If you have multiple ideas, feel free to submit multiple forms.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
