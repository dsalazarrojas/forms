# Wildlife Park Visitor Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and expectations when visiting the Wildlife Park. Your feedback will help us improve the services and facilities we offer to make your visits more enjoyable and memorable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Choose your preferred park visit plan from the options provided.
* Select the frequency of your visits to the park.
* Choose your age group to help us tailor our services.
* Select the purpose of your visit to help us understand your interests.
* If you have any special experience at the park, you can enter it in the provided text field.
* If you have a different visit frequency, please specify in the provided text field.
* You can add any additional comments or notes about your visit in the provided text field.
* Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Park Visit Plan** (`park_visit_plan`): Select the type of visit plan you would like to have for your visit to the park. Choose from: 
  + Wildlife Park Map: A self-guided visit where you can explore the park at your own pace.
  + Self-Guided: A guided visit where you will be provided with a guide to help you explore the park.
  + Guided Tour: A guided tour where you will be accompanied by a guide throughout the visit.
  + Audio Guide: A guided tour where you will be provided with an audio guide to help you explore the park.

* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`): Select the frequency of your visits to the park from: 
  + Rarely: You rarely visit the park.
  + Occasionally: You occasionally visit the park.
  + Often: You often visit the park.
  + Every time I visit: You visit the park every time you are in the area.

* **Age Group** (`age_group`): Select your age group from: 
  + Adult: You are an adult.
  + Children: You are a child.
  + Senior: You are a senior citizen.
  + Group: You are visiting with a group.

* **Visit Purpose** (`visit_purpose`): Select the purpose of your visit to the park from: 
  + General Admission: You are visiting the park for general admission.
  + Special Event: You are visiting the park for a special event.
  + Group Visit: You are visiting the park with a group.
  + School Group: You are visiting the park with a school group.

* **Park Experience** (`park_experience`): This field is for any special experience you have had at the park, such as seeing a certain animal or participating in a specific activity.

* **Visit Frequency Other** (`visit_frequency_other`): If you don't see your visit frequency in the previous options, please specify it here.

* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit to the park.

* **Email** (`email`): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address here.

* **Phone** (`phone`): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your phone number here.
