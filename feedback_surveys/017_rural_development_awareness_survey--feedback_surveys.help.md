# Rural Development Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about rural development awareness from individuals who live or work in rural areas. It aims to collect data on known development programs, contact details, and other relevant information that can help understand the needs and challenges of rural communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Enter your name (optional).
3. Provide your contact details (optional).
4. Mention your location (optional).
5. Select all the development programs you are aware of (multiple selection allowed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets_name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name, if you want to be identified in the survey results.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any contact information you'd like, such as phone numbers or email addresses. If you don't want to share your contact details, you can leave this field blank.
* **location** (`location`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your location or the community you are from.
* **known_development_programs** (`known_development_programs`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to select all the development programs you are aware of. Please choose from the list below:
	+ National Rural Development Agency
	+ State Government Program
	+ Local Community Development Program

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are not sure about a particular program, you can select "Unknown" or "Other" and provide more information in the "contact_details" field.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use the "contact_details" field to provide them.
