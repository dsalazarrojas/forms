# Feedback On Tracking Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the tracking setup process. It allows users to provide their thoughts on how to improve the process, enabling us to make informed decisions to enhance user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the analytics implementations that you have experience with.
2. Choose the type of tracking setup that best describes your current setup.
3. Provide a brief explanation of why you are filling out this form (optional).
4. Enter any feedback or comments you have on the tracking setup process.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Analytics Implementations** (`analytics_implementations`, multiple choice, required): Choose all the analytics implementations that you have experience with, such as Google Analytics, Facebook Ads, Google Ads, Amazon Ads, TikTok Ads, or Other.
* **Tracking Setup Type** (`tracking_setup_type`, single choice, optional): Select the type of tracking setup that best describes your current setup. This will help us understand the level of complexity of your tracking setup.
* **Why Are You Asking This?** (`why_are_you_asking_this`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why you are filling out this form. This helps us understand your motivation for providing feedback.
* **Feedback On Tracking Setup** (`feedback_on_tracking_setup`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments you have on the tracking setup process. Be as detailed as possible, providing specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
* **How Can We Improve?** (`how_can_we_improve`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions for how we can improve the tracking setup process.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other comments or suggestions you have, no matter how big or small.
* **Feedback On Tracking Setup Form** (`feedback_on_tracking_setup_form`, text, optional): This field is not actually used for anything, so you can ignore it.
