# habilitation_program_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Habilitation Program Satisfaction Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the habilitation program. This survey aims to evaluate the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options for the program's instructors, facilities, and overall experience from the provided list.
2. Enter your participant details, including name, email, phone number, and any additional comments.
3. Rate the program, instructors, and facilities using the provided rating system (1-5).
4. Provide your date of visit and time of visit.
5. Answer the question about whether you will participate again in the program.
