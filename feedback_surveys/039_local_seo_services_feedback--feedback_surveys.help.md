# Local SEO Services Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Local SEO Services Feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from users on the local SEO services provided by the organization. This form is used to gather information on user satisfaction, services used, and any comments or feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose one of the services that you used from the options in the "Services Used" field.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the service in the "Rating" field.
3. Type in any comments or feedback you have in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
6. Provide a testimonial in the "Testimonial" field (if applicable).
7. If you were assigned a specific tool, select it from the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. If you would like to generate an output file with your feedback, enter a name for the file in the "Output File Name" field.
9. Finally, enter your feedback in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main form where you can enter your feedback. Please keep your response clear and concise.
* **Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction with the service, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Services Used** (`services_used`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more services that you used, such as Local SEO, Local Listings, On-Page Optimization, Link Building, Content Marketing, or Other.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Type in any comments or feedback you have on the service.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here (if applicable).
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief testimonial on the service you received.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): If you were assigned a specific tool, select it here.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required/optional): Enter the file name you would like to use for the output file with your feedback.
* **Output File Name** (`output_file_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a name for the output file with your feedback.
