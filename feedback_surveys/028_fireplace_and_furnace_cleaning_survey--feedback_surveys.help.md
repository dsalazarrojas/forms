# # Fireplace And Furnace Cleaning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireplace And Furnace Cleaning Survey is designed to gather customer feedback and survey data about our fireplace and furnace cleaning services. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and let us know about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Select the options that apply to your experience with our services.
* Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* Provide your satisfaction level with our services.
* Enter your service rating (1-5 stars).
* Choose how often you would like us to follow up with you (if at all).
* Select your preferred method of follow-up (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* Indicate if you would like us to follow up with you after the service (yes or no).
* Finally, provide any follow-up notes if you would like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_section_1** (Select Multiple): This field asks if our technicians were on-time for your scheduled appointment.
	+ Choose "Yes" if our technicians were on-time.
	+ Choose "No" if our technicians were not on-time.
* **service_type** (Select One): This field asks about the type of service you received.
	+ Choose "Heating" for services related to heating.
	+ Choose "Plumbing" for services related to plumbing.
	+ Choose "Electrical" for services related to electrical.
	+ Choose "Carpentry" for services related to carpentry.
	+ Choose "Other" if the service is not listed.
* **date_of_service** (Date): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **time_of_visit** (Time): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **technician_name** (Text): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **contact_name** (Text): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **contact_number** (Text): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **contact_email** (Email): This field is not currently being used in this form, so please ignore it.
* **satisfaction_level** (Select One): This field asks about your satisfaction level with our services.
	+ Choose "Excellent" if you were extremely satisfied.
	+ Choose "Good" if you were somewhat satisfied.
	+ Choose "Neutral" if you were neutral.
	+ Choose "Poor" if you were dissatisfied.
	+ Choose "Very Poor" if you were very dissatisfied.
* **additional_comments** (Text): This field is for any additional comments you may have about your experience with our services.
* **form_section_2** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our services.
* **service_rating** (Number): This field asks you to rate our services on a scale of 1-5 stars.
* **will_rebook** (Select One): This field asks if you would like us to follow up with you after the service.
	+ Choose "True" if you would like us to follow up.
	+ Choose "False" if you do not want us to follow up.
* **rebook_reason** (Text): This field is for any reasons you may have for not wanting us to follow up with you after the service.
* **form_section_3** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our services.
* **contact_method** (Select One): This field asks about your preferred method of follow-up communication.
	+ Choose "Email" if you prefer email.
	+ Choose "Phone" if you prefer phone.
	+ Choose "Social Media" if you prefer social media.
	+ Choose "Text Message" if you prefer text message.
* **follow_up** (Select One): This field asks if you would like us to follow up with you after the service.
	+ Choose "True" if you would like us to follow up.
	+ Choose "False" if you do not want us to follow up.
* **follow_up_method** (Select One): This field asks about your preferred method of follow-up communication.
	+ Choose "Phone" if you prefer phone.
	+ Choose "Email" if you prefer email.
	+ Choose "In-person" if you prefer in-person.
	+ Choose "Other" if you have another method in mind.
* **follow_up_frequency** (Select One): This field asks about how often you would like us to follow up with you.
	+ Choose "Weekly" if you would like follow-up weekly.
	+ Choose "Monthly" if you would like follow-up monthly.
	+ Choose "Quarterly" if you would like follow-up quarterly.
	+ Choose "Yearly" if you would like follow-up yearly.
* **follow_up_note** (Text): This field is for any additional notes you may have about your follow-up preferences.
