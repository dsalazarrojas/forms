# Inquest Session Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their experience in an inquest session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in their label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about this session** (`participant_feedback`): This question asks for your overall feeling or emotion about the session. Please rate your feeling on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate your overall experience in this session** (`participant_experience`): This question asks for your evaluation of the session's overall quality. Please rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **What did you think about the inquest session** (`inquest_session`): This open-ended question allows you to provide any thoughts or comments about the session.
* **How would you rate the facilitator** (`facilitator_performance`): This question asks for your evaluation of the facilitator's performance. Please select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not at all.
* **What would you value about this inquest session** (`inquest_session_value`): This question asks for what aspects of the session you found valuable or useful. You can select multiple options.
* **What could be improved in this session** (`session_improvement`): This open-ended question allows you to provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **How satisfied are you with this session** (`participant_satisfaction`): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the session. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Would you recommend this session** (`participant_recommend`): This question asks if you would recommend this session to others. Please select one of the following options: True, False, Not sure.
* **Additional feedback about inquest session** (`inquest_session_improvement`): This open-ended question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Additional comments about this session** (`additional_comments`): This open-ended question allows you to provide any additional thoughts or comments about the session.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`): This is your name as a participant in the session. Please enter your name accurately.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`): This is your email address. Please enter your email address accurately.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`): This is your phone number. Please enter your phone number accurately.
* **Participant organization** (`participant_organization`): This is your organization or company name. Please enter your organization name accurately.
* **Participant job title** (`participant_job_title`): This is your job title. Please enter your job title accurately.
* **Participant location** (`participant_location`): This is your location or workplace. Please enter your location accurately.
* **Facilitator name** (`facilitator_name`): This is the facilitator's name. Please enter their name accurately.
* **Facilitator email** (`facilitator_email`): This is the facilitator's email address. Please enter their email address accurately.
* **Facilitator phone** (`facilitator_phone`): This is the facilitator's phone number. Please enter their phone number accurately.
* **Facilitator job title** (`facilitator_job_title`): This is the facilitator's job title. Please enter their job title accurately.
* **Date of the inquest session** (`inquest_session_date`): This is the date of the inquest session. Please enter the date accurately.
* **Time of the inquest session** (`inquest_session_time`): This is the time of the inquest session. Please enter the time accurately.
