# Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members on their experiences with team leads, collaboration frequency, communication style, and overall collaboration quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant option for each question, using the dropdown menus for "Team Lead", "Collaboration Frequency", "Communication Style", and "Collaboration Quality". These questions are not required.
2. Answer the "Comments" question with a brief text description if you have any thoughts or experiences you'd like to share.
3. If you are a team lead, answer the "Team Lead" question truthfully about yourself.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Team Members" to indicate whether you collaborate with the team.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Collaboration Frequency" to indicate how often you collaborate with this team.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Communication Style Frequency" to indicate how often you feel you communicate effectively with this team.
7. Select "High" or "Low" for "Trust Level" to indicate your level of trust with the team.
8. Answer the "Comments" question with a brief text description if you have any additional thoughts or experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, not required): Select the person who leads your team.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, not required): Check "Yes" if you collaborate with this team, "No" otherwise.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, select_one, not required): Indicate how often you collaborate with this team, choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, text, not required): Enter your thoughts about the team's communication style.
* **Communication Style Frequency** (`communication_style_frequency`, select_one, not required): Indicate how often you feel you communicate effectively with this team, choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`communication_style_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the team's communication style.
* **Trust Level** (`trust_level`, select_one, not required): Choose "High" or "Low" to indicate your level of trust with the team.
* **Collaboration Quality** (`collaboration_quality`, select_one, not required): Indicate the quality of your collaboration experience with the team, choosing "Good" or "Poor".
