# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your perception of trust and accountability within our organization. Your responses will help us understand our strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, which will be verified for validity.
4. Select your Department from the "Department" field.
5. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Choose your level of Trust Level, using the scale (e.g., 1-10).
7. Choose your level of Accountability Level, using the same scale as Trust Level.
8. Provide a brief answer in the "Trust and Accountability" field to describe what matters most to you about trust and accountability in the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)** (`first_name`, Optional): Your given name as you would like it to appear on our reports.
* **Last Name (text)** (`last_name`, Optional): Your surname.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, Optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your work account.
* **Department (select_one)** (`department`, Optional): Select your department from the list to provide context for your feedback.
* **Job Title (text)** (`job_title`, Optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Trust Level (number)** (`trust_level`, Optional): Choose a level of trust in the organization on a scale from 1-10.
* **Accountability Level (number)** (`accountability_level`, Optional): Choose a level of accountability in the organization on a same scale as Trust Level.
* **Trust and Accountability (text)** (`trust_and_accountability`, Optional): Briefly describe what matters most to you about trust and accountability in the organization.

## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
* You can skip any questions that you feel are not relevant to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
