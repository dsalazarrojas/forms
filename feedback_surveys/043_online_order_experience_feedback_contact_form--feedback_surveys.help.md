# online_order_experience_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their online order experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the feedback form section with your thoughts and comments about your online order experience.
2. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or other) to receive a response from us.
3. If you select "other" as your contact method, please provide the contact information in the "other" field.
4. In the "details" field, provide any additional information about your experience that you think is relevant.
5. If you have any feedback or comments, please fill in the "feedback" field.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with your online order experience using the "rating" field. You can select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor, or Bad.
7. If you want to provide more details about your rating, you can fill in the "rating_details" field.
8. If you want to report your order ID, you can fill in the "order_id" field.
