# Clients Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from clients about their experience with our company. This feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement, understand our customers' needs and expectations, and make necessary adjustments to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
4. **Type of Customer**: Choose the category that best describes your relationship with our company.
5. **How Long Have You Been a Customer?**: Choose the time period you've been a customer.
6. **Purchase Frequency**: Indicate how often you make purchases with our company.
7. **Quality of Products or Services**: Rate the quality of our products or services.
8. **Value for Money**: Rate the value you feel you get for the money you spend with us.
9. **Speed of Delivery or Service**: Rate the speed of our delivery or service.
10. **Quality of Customer Service**: Rate the quality of our customer service.
11. **Communication and Responsiveness**: Rate how responsive and communicative our team members have been.
12. **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with our company.
13. **Did We Meet Your Expectations?**: Choose whether we met or exceeded your expectations.
14. **Likelihood to Recommend**: Rate how likely you are to recommend our company to others.
15. **What Did You Like Most About Us?**: Share what you liked most about your experience with us.
16. **What Could We Improve?**: Share any areas where you feel we can improve.
17. **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

* **Speed of Delivery or Service** (`delivery_speed`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the speed of our delivery or service.
* **Quality of Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of our customer service.
* **Communication and Responsiveness** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate how responsive and communicative our team members have been.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose how satisfied you are with our company.

## Tips

* Please be honest and open in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or incidents that you'd like to share.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please don't hesitate to share them.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you'd like.
