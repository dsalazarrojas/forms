# impression_review_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **impression_review_survey** form is a feedback survey aimed at gathering opinions and ratings about a product or service. It is intended to provide valuable insights for improvement and to measure customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have permission to complete this form.
2. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A short text field where you can write any comments or observations about the product or service you are reviewing.
* **Product or Service**: Select the type of product or service you are reviewing (Product, Service, Event, or Other).
* **Product/Service Name**: Enter the name of the product or service you are reviewing.
* **First Impression**: Select the time frame of your first impression (e.g., immediately, after a few minutes, after a few hours).
* **Onboarding Process**: Choose how easy or difficult it was to use the onboarding process (Easy to use, Not easy to use, N/A).
* **Onboarding Process Details**: Add any additional comments about the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Process Rating**: Rate the onboarding process (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the product or service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Improvement Priorities**: Select the areas where you think improvements are necessary (Product/Service Development, User Experience, Onboarding Process, Other).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions about the product or service.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Department**: Enter the department or team you are associated with.
* **Name**: Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Position**: Enter your position for follow-up purposes.
* **Role**: Enter your role for follow-up purposes.
* **Role/Department**: Enter your role and department for follow-up purposes.
* **Survey Comments**: Add any additional comments about your overall experience.
* **Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up comments or details.
* **Follow-up Rating**: Rate the follow-up interaction (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Follow-up Improvement**: Enter any suggestions for follow-up improvement.
* **Follow-up Improvement Details**: Add any additional comments about follow-up improvement.

Please submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
