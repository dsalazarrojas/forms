# survey_design_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The survey_design_feedback_form is used to gather internal feedback from staff members on the design of a survey. This feedback will help us improve the survey's usability and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Click on the survey_design_feedback_form link.
* Choose a category for the feedback (e.g., Feedback Surveys).
* Provide a description of your experience with the survey.
* Enter your participant ID.
* List any survey methodologies you used.
* Identify the tool you were assigned for the survey.
* Enter a name for the output file.
* Attach the output file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (id: 2)**: Select a category to associate your feedback with.
* **Description (id: 3)**: Share your thoughts on the survey's design and experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Participant ID (id: 4)**: Enter your internal participant ID, if applicable.
* **Survey Methodologies (id: 5)**: If applicable, list any survey methodologies you used (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **Assigned Tool (id: 6)**: Identify the tool you were assigned to use for the survey.
* **Output File Name (id: 7)**: Enter a name for the output file.
* **Output File (id: 8)**: Attach the output file, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or screenshots to support your feedback.
* Keep your feedback concise and to the point.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is intended for internal use only, and the feedback collected will not be shared with participants.
