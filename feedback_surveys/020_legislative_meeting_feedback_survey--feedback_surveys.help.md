# legislative_meeting_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "legislative_meeting_feedback_survey" form is used to gather feedback from participants after a legislative meeting. It helps to evaluate the meeting's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Select the meeting date when you attended the meeting.
3. Choose the type of session you participated in.
4. Provide your feedback in the "feedback" field.
5. Rate your experience using the rating scale.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant in the meeting.
* **meeting_date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): The date when you attended the meeting.
* **session_type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session you participated in.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide your thoughts and opinions about the meeting.
* **rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **participant_id** (`participant_id`, text, required): Enter your participant ID or ID number.
* **department** (`department`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose the department you belong to.
* **role** (`role`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose your role in the meeting.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact for further information.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact for further information.
* **meeting_attendance** (`meeting_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reasons for your attendance.
* **feedback_comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the meeting.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
