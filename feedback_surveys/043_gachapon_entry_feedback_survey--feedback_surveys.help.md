# gachapon_entry_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gachapon Entry Feedback Survey is a simple, optional form designed to collect your thoughts on your experience with our entry. It will help us improve the user experience and overall services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant fields and provide a brief description of your experience. You don't have to fill out all the fields, but feel free to do so if you have more to say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gachapon Entry Feedback Survey** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your title for your entry, and we'll use it for our reference.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your entry (Feedback Surveys, Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your entry and why you're taking the survey.
* **Player Feedback** (`player_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on the player experience, what worked well, and what didn't.
* **Drag and Drop Interface** (`drag_and_drop_interface`, text, optional): Share your experience with our drag and drop interface.
* **Easy Data Collection and Organized Submission** (`easy_data_collection_organized_submission`, text, optional): Let us know if you found our data collection and submission process easy and organized.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, select_one, optional): Select the event organizer that best describes your experience (Event Organizer, Player, Other).
* **Jotform Form Builder** (`jotform_form_builder`, text, optional): Tell us about your experience with Jotform Form Builder.
* **Easy Data Collection via Jotform** (`easy_data_collection_submitted_via_jotform`, text, optional): Share your experience with easy data collection via Jotform.
