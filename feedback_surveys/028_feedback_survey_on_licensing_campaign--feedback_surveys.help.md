# Feedback Survey On Licensing Campaign - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users on a licensing campaign, providing a platform for them to express their thoughts and opinions on the campaign's performance, quality, and effectiveness. The collected feedback will be used by internal stakeholders to improve and refine future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by reviewing each question carefully and responding truthfully to the best of your ability.
*   Enter your name, email, and phone number as prompted.
*   Choose the appropriate options for the "select_one" question that best reflect your opinion.
*   Select all applicable options for the "select_multiple" question that best match your preferences.
*   Enter numerical values for the "integer" and "decimal" questions as required.
*   Respond to the "text" question with a clear and concise answer.
*   Make sure to complete all questions as required, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is for your name, which will be associated with your feedback.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your email address, which will be used to contact you regarding your feedback.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for the date field.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time for the time field.
*   **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option that best reflects your opinion.
*   **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options that best match your preferences.
*   **Integer** (`integer`, number, required): Enter a numerical value for this field.
*   **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required): Enter a decimal value for this field.
*   **Text** (`text`, text, required): Respond to this question with a clear and concise answer.

## Tips
*   Please be as accurate and honest as possible when responding to each question.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
