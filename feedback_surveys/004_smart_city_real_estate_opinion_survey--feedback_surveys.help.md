# Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions on the Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey. We appreciate your input to help us better understand your thoughts and opinions on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the relevant options for each question.
2. Make sure to complete all the required fields (none are required in this form, but it's always good practice to provide as much information as possible).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Smart City Features** (`smart_city_features`, select_multiple): 
Select all the features you think are important for a smart city. You can choose more than one option.
* **Satisfaction Levels** (`satisfaction_levels`, number): 
Rate your satisfaction levels with the realty projects in our city. Please use a number between 1 and 10.
* **Improvements for Local Realty Projects** (`improvements_local_realty_projects`, select_one): 
Choose one option that you think can improve our local realty projects.
* **Resident Feedback** (`resident_feedback`, text): 
Provide your text feedback on our city's realty projects.
* **Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey** (`smart_city_real_estate_opinion_survey`, select_one): 
Select one option that you think is true about this survey.
* **Planners, Developers and Authorities** (`planners_developers_authorities`, select_one): 
Choose one option that you think best describes the role of planners, developers, and authorities in our city.
