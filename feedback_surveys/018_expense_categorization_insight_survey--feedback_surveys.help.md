# Expense Categorization Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on your current categorization of expenses and to identify potential areas for improvement in the category system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. For questions that ask about multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you currently categorize expenses?**: This question is asking about your current categorization approach for expenses. You have two options: "True" and "False". Please select one or multiple options that apply to you.
* **Can you walk us through your review process?**: This question is asking about your current process for reviewing and approving expenses. Please provide a detailed description of your current process.
* **What do you think is the current state of our category system?**: This question is asking about your perception of the current category system's strengths and weaknesses. Please provide an honest and constructive feedback on your experience.
* **What ideas do you have to improve the category system?**: This is an open-ended question asking for ideas on how to improve the category system. Please share any thoughts or suggestions you have on how we can better categorize expenses.
* **What types of expenses are you currently categorizing under "Expenses"?**: This question is asking about the specific expenses you typically categorize under the "Expenses" category. Please provide a detailed description of the types of expenses you usually categorize.
* **How can we simplify the categorization of expenses?**: This question is asking for ideas on how to make the categorization process more efficient and user-friendly. Please provide suggestions on how we can simplify the process.
