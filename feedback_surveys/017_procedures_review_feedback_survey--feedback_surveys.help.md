# Procedures Review Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Procedures Review Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from staff on the procedures in place. This feedback will help us understand what is working well and what areas need improvement. The survey is essential in refining our procedures to make them more effective and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the procedures you are evaluating and think about what you think is the main area of improvement.
2. Provide your staff response in the staff response field.
3. Choose the option that best describes how effective you think the procedures are.
4. Select all that apply for the urgency of the procedures (High, Medium, Low).
5. Choose the level of satisfaction you have with the procedures.
6. Provide any reason why you think the procedures have low urgency.
7. Lastly, describe any improvement opportunities for the procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Response (Staff Response)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or observations about the procedure. Please be specific and focused on the procedure you are evaluating.
* **What do you think is the main area of improvement? (What do you think is the main area of improvement?)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you can identify the main area of improvement for the procedure. Be specific and provide a concise answer.
* **Date (Date)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is not required, but it is used to track when the feedback was provided.
* **Time (Time)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is not required, but it is used to track when the feedback was provided.
* **How effective are these procedures? (How effective are these procedures?)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you can select how effective you think the procedures are. Choose the option that best describes your opinion.
* **Urgency (Urgency)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you can select the urgency of the procedures. You can choose all that apply (High, Medium, Low).
* **Satisfaction (Satisfaction)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you choose the level of satisfaction you have with the procedures. Choose the option that best describes your opinion.
* **Urgency Reason (Urgency Reason)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is not required, but it is used to track any reason why the procedures have low urgency.
* **Improvement Opportunities (Improvement Opportunities)** (<name>, <type>, required>): This field is where you can describe any improvement opportunities for the procedures. Please be specific and provide a concise answer.
