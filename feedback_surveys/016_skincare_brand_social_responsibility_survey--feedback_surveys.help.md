# Skincare Brand Social Responsibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the skincare brand's social responsibility. The goal is to help the brand understand how customers perceive their social responsibility efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction sections to understand the purpose of this form.
2. Answer the questions about your awareness of the brand's social responsibility efforts.
3. Indicate whether or not you prioritize the brand's social responsibility initiatives.
4. Select the values you align with.
5. Provide feedback on the brand's social responsibility efforts.
6. Enter your contact information to help us reach out to you for any further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, required: false): This section provides an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Awareness** (`2`, number, required: false): This question asks about your awareness of the brand's social responsibility efforts. Please respond with the number of initiatives you are aware of.
* **Prioritization** (`3`, select_one, required: false): This question asks whether or not you prioritize the brand's social responsibility initiatives. Select "Yes" if you do prioritize them, "No" otherwise.
* **Values** (`4`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks which values you align with. Select all the values that apply to you.
* **Feedback** (`5`, time, required: false): This question asks for your feedback on the brand's social responsibility efforts. Please respond with a brief message.
* **Email** (`6`, email, required: false): This is your email address for us to reach out to you for any further discussion.
* **Phone** (`7`, text, required: false): This is your phone number for us to reach out to you for any further discussion.
* **Introduction 2** (`8`, text, required: false): This section is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

* Please respond to all the questions to help us get a comprehensive view of the brand's social responsibility.
* If you have any further feedback or comments, please use the "Introduction 2" section.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
