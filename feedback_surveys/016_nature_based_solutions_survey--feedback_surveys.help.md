# Nature Based Solutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about Nature Based Solutions (NBS) awareness, benefits, and barriers from local communities. This form is designed to help us understand the current state of NBS adoption and how to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the best answer that applies to your situation.
2. Fill in the answers according to the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, as this will help us to get a complete picture of your perspective on NBS.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **nature_based_solutions_survey** (`nature_based_solutions_survey`, text, required: false)
This is the title of the form, and you don't need to fill it in. It's just a heading to identify the form.

### 2. **awareness_level** (`awareness_level`, select_one, required: false)
This question asks about your current level of awareness about NBS. Choose one of the provided options that best describes your understanding.

### 3. **benefits_aware** (`benefits_aware`, number, required: false)
This question is about how much you know about the benefits of NBS. Choose the number that corresponds to your understanding.

### 4. **barriers** (`barriers`, text, required: false)
Describe any barriers you face when it comes to NBS.

### 5. **interest_in_nature** (`interest_in_nature`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the reasons why you have an interest in NBS.

### 6. **awareness_source** (`awareness_source`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the sources from which you got your NBS awareness.

### 7. **benefits_source** (`benefits_source`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the sources from which you got your information about NBS benefits.

### 8. **barriers_source** (`barriers_source`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the sources from which you got your information about NBS barriers.

### 9. **nature_based_solution_type** (`nature_based_solution_type`, select_one, required: false)
This question is about the type of NBS you are interested in. Choose one of the provided options.

### 10. **location** (`location`, select_one, required: false)
This question is about whether you are familiar with NBS in your area. Choose 'Yes' if you are, and 'No' otherwise.

### 11. **local_authorities** (`local_authorities`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the local authorities involved in NBS in your area.

### 12. **nbs_focal_area** (`nbs_focal_area`, text, required: false)
This field is for you to describe the area you are referring to when answering the previous question.

### 13. **nbs_awareness_level** (`nbs_awareness_level`, select_one, required: false)
This question is about your awareness level of NBS in the specified area. Choose one of the provided options.

### 14. **benefits_level** (`benefits_level`, number, required: false)
This question is about your understanding of NBS benefits in the specified area. Choose the number that corresponds to your understanding.

### 15. **nbs_focal_area_other** (`nbs_focal_area_other`, text, required: false)
This field is for you to describe any other area you are referring to when answering the previous question.

### 16. **barriers_level** (`barriers_level`, number, required: false)
This question is about your understanding of NBS barriers in the specified area. Choose the number that corresponds to your understanding.

### 17. **nature_based_solution** (`nature_based_solution`, text, required: false)
Describe a NBS you are interested in or familiar with.

### 18. **nbs_focal_area_aware** (`nbs_focal_area_aware`, select_one, required: false)
This question is about your awareness of NBS in the specified area. Choose one of the provided options.

### 19. **nbs_focal_area_barriers** (`nbs_focal_area_barriers`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the barriers you face when implementing NBS in the specified area.

### 20. **nbs_focal_area_benefits** (`nbs_focal_area_benefits`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the benefits you have seen or heard about NBS in the specified area.
