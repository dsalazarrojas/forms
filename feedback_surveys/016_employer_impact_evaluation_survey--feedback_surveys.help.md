# Employer Impact Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Impact Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from employers about their initiatives. This form is intended to help employers evaluate the effectiveness and impact of their initiatives and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information and authority to complete it.
2. Select all the initiatives that apply to your employer from the list on page 2.
3. Provide a brief description of your feedback for each initiative on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form, and it serves as an introduction to the survey. You can type a brief explanation of the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Initiatives (Page 2)**: On this page, select all the initiatives that apply to your employer from the list. You can choose multiple initiatives by selecting the checkboxes next to each one.
	+ **initiative1**: Select this option if you are involved in initiative1.
	+ **initiative2**: Select this option if you are involved in initiative2.
	+ **initiative3**: Select this option if you are involved in initiative3.
* **Initiatives Feedback (Page 3)**: On this page, provide a brief description of your feedback for each initiative you selected on the previous page. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and insights about the effectiveness and impact of each initiative.
