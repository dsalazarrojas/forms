# Urban Air Quality Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of air quality in urban areas. Your input will help us understand how people in urban areas think and feel about air quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, follow the instructions below.
2.  Please fill out all fields that are marked as "required".
3.  For the fields that are marked as "select one" or "select multiple", choose one or multiple options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
4.  If you are not sure about a question, you can leave it blank.
5.  Please be as honest and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
6.  After completing all the fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This field is to collect your basic contact information for follow up purposes.
	+ **Name (required)**: Please provide your full name.
	+ **Email (required)**: Please provide your valid email address.
	+ **Phone (optional)**: Please provide your phone number if you want to be reached by phone.
* **Air Quality Perceptions**:
	+ **Excellent**: You perceive the air quality as excellent.
	+ **Good**: You perceive the air quality as good.
	+ **Fair**: You perceive the air quality as fair.
* **Behavioral Changes**:
	+ **True**: You have made behavioral changes due to air quality concerns.
	+ **False**: You have not made behavioral changes due to air quality concerns.
* **Behaviors**:
	+ **Use electric vehicle**: You use an electric vehicle.
	+ **Use public transportation**: You use public transportation.
	+ **Reduce vehicle usage**: You have reduced your vehicle usage.
* **Time Spent Outdoors**:
	+ **Date**: Please select the date you spent time outdoors.
* **Air Quality**:
	+ **Number**: Please provide a number that best describes your perception of air quality (e.g., 0-10).
* **Air Quality Score**:
	+ **Number**: Please provide a number that represents your score for air quality (e.g., 0-10).
* **Comments**:
	+ **Text**: Please provide any additional comments you have about air quality in urban areas.
* **Submitted By**:
	+ **User**: You are completing the form as a registered user.
	+ **Guest**: You are completing the form as a guest.
* **Submitted At**:
	+ **Date**: Please select the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted**:
	+ **Time**: Please select the time you submitted the form.
* **Location**:
	+ **Urban**: You are located in an urban area.
	+ **Rural**: You are located in a rural area.
