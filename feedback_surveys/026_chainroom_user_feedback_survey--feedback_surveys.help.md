# Chainroom User Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on the Chainroom application. It is used to understand your experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement. Your responses will help us to make Chainroom a better tool for you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as `required: true`).
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with Chainroom.
3. Provide detailed answers to the text fields (e.g., `missing_features`, `best_feature`, `improvement_areas`, `additional_feedback`).
4. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
