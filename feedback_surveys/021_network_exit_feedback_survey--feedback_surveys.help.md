# Network Exit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who are leaving the network to understand their experience and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in each answer.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How did you feel about our network?** (`network_exit_survey`, text, required): Please describe your overall feelings and impressions about the network.
- **What reasons did you have for leaving?** (`reasons_for_leaving`, text, required): Please list the reasons why you decided to leave the network.
- **What would improve your experience here?** (`what_would_improve_your_experience`, text, required): Suggest changes or improvements that would make your experience with the network better.
- **How did we do in terms of communication?** (`how_did_we_do`, text, required): Please assess our communication with you during your time with the network.
- **How did we do in terms of support?** (`how_did_we_do_other`, text, required): Evaluate our support services and how we responded to your needs.
- **How did we do in terms of resources?** (`how_did_we_do_other_2`, text, required): Assess the availability and quality of resources provided by the network.
- **What would improve your experience here other?** (`what_would_improve_your_experience_other`, text, required): Suggest any other changes or improvements that would make your experience better.
- **Overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, text, required): Rate your overall experience with the network.
- **Suggest something else?** (`suggestions`, text, required): Suggest any other ideas or suggestions for improvement.
- **What are your next steps?** (`next_steps`, text, required): Please describe your future plans and how you plan to use the information gathered in this survey.
