# Internal Process Friction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on internal process friction experienced by team members, which can hinder productivity, efficiency, and employee satisfaction. The goal is to identify pain points and areas for improvement within our internal processes.
## How To Complete This Form
To provide useful feedback, please answer the questions in the order of the form. If you have trouble or need help, please don't hesitate to ask.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`text`, required): Start by reading this brief introduction to understand the purpose and context of this survey.
* **Team Members** (`select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the team members who you have experience with, such as Team Lead, Senior, or Other.
* **Role** (`select_one`, optional): If you have a specific role within the team, please select it. This will help us understand your perspective.
* **How often do you experience friction** (`number`, required): On a scale of 1-10, rate how often you experience friction within our internal processes.
* **What are your top 3 pain points** (`text`, required): In the format 1. pain point 1, 2. pain point 2, 3. pain point 3, please list your top 3 pain points.
* **Proposed solution** (`text`, optional): If you have a proposed solution to address your pain points, please describe it.
* **Feedback** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please share them.
* **Action Plan** (`text`, optional): If you have a suggested action plan to address your pain points, please describe it.
* **Follow-up** (`text`, optional): If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please mention them.
* **Additional Feedback** (`text`, optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please share them.
