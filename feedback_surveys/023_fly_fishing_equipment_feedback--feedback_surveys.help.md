# Fly Fishing Equipment Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with fly fishing equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Info**: Enter your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. **Fly Fishing Gear**: Select the type of fly fishing gear you used during your trip (e.g., Gear, Fly Rod, Reel, Waders, Flies).
3. **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your satisfaction level with the equipment, on a scale of 1-10.
4. **Issue**: Describe any issues you encountered with the equipment.
5. **Improvement Suggestion**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
6. **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share with us.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
8. **Contact Method**: Select the method by which you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail).
9. **Feedback Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience.
10. **User Notes**: Add any additional notes or information you'd like to share with us.
