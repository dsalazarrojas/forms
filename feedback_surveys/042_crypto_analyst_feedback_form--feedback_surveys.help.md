# Crypto Analyst Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from crypto analysts regarding their experience and satisfaction with certain tools and services used in their work. The feedback will help us improve our services and tools to better support our analysts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your rating for the analysis quality using the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the challenges you encountered during the analysis process.
3. Indicate if there are any improvement areas for our tools or services.
4. Rate the importance of certain tools for your work.
5. Provide any specific tool recommendations.
6. Add any additional comments about your experience.
7. Enter your email address for contact information.
8. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Analysis Quality**: Select one or more of the following options to rate the quality of your analysis:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Challenges**: Choose the challenges you encountered during the analysis process:
	+ Major
	+ Minor
	+ None
* **Improvement Areas**: Indicate if there are any areas that need improvement:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Tools Use**: Rate the importance of our tools for your work:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Tools Recommendation**: Provide any specific tool recommendations or suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
