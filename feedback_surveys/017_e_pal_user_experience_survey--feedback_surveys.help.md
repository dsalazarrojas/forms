# E Pal User Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Pal User Experience Survey form is designed to gather feedback and insights on the E Pal platform. This form helps us understand how our users perceive and interact with the platform, enabling us to make data-driven decisions to improve the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select the category that best describes your feedback or comments about E Pal (Category field)
* Rate your level of satisfaction with the platform (Satisfaction fields)
* Choose how you use E Pal's features (Feature usage field)
* Provide your email address (Email field)
* Rate the features of E Pal (Feature fields with options)
* Choose how to categorize your feedback or comments about E Pal's features (Category fields with options)
* Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`form_category`): Select the category that best describes your feedback or comments about E Pal. This can be Feedback, Surveys, or Other.
* **Satisfaction** (`form_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction level with the platform using one of the following options: Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied.
* **Feature usage** (`form_feature_usage`): Choose how you use E Pal's features, including Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3, Product, Service, and Other.
* **Email** (`form_email`): Provide your email address to allow us to contact you with further questions or to send you a confirmation email.
* **Feature 1** (`form_comments_1`): Rate Feature 1 using the options provided.
* **Feature 2** (`form_comments_2`): Rate Feature 2 using the options provided.
* **Feature 3** (`form_comments_3`): Rate Feature 3 using the options provided.
* **Feature 4** (`form_comments_4`): Choose how to categorize Product using the options provided.
* **Feature 5** (`form_comments_5`): Choose how to categorize Service using the options provided.
* **Feature 6** (`form_comments_6`): Choose how to categorize Other using the options provided.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`): Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback and comments to our team.
