# Lease Flexibility Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Flexibility Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from tenants on the flexibility of a lease agreement. This survey aims to understand the comfort level of the tenant regarding various aspects of the lease agreement, including the agreement term length, flexibility of questions, and satisfaction with the manager and the agreement itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply select the relevant options for each field and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction** (`survey_intro`, text, optional): This field is a brief introduction to the survey. You can use this space to provide any additional information about your experience with the lease agreement.
* **Term Length** (`agreement_term_length`, number, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the term length of the lease agreement. If you select "1" or "2", you may want to provide additional comments in the "Comments" field to elaborate on your response.
* **Flexibility Questions** (`flexibility_questions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your experience with the flexibility of the lease agreement.
	+ Option 1: The agreement was very flexible.
	+ Option 2: The agreement was somewhat flexible.
	+ Option 3: The agreement was not flexible at all.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the lease agreement, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up questions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Tenant Satisfaction** (`tenant_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with your experience as a tenant.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with your experience with your manager.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Agreement Satisfaction** (`agreement_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with the lease agreement itself.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
