# Civic Education Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your knowledge and understanding of civic education in our local community. Your answers will help us understand what we can do to improve civic education and increase awareness among residents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, choosing the best option that applies to you.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select only the options that apply to you.
3. For the question "How important is it for citizens to vote in local elections?", please rate your answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Essential".
4. For the question "Where do you primarily get your civic and political information?", select up to 3 options that apply to you.
5. Please provide a clear and concise answer for the question "What is the biggest barrier to learning more about civic responsibilities?".
6. For the question "Would you attend a free community workshop on local governance?", select only one option.
7. If you have any suggestions on how the city can better inform residents of their rights, please provide a brief answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Age Range
* **Age Range** (`age_bracket`, `select_one`, required): Please select the age range that best describes you.
### Highest Level of Education Completed
* **Highest Level of Education Completed** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Please select the highest level of education you have completed.
### How many branches of government are there in the United States?
* **How many branches of government are there in the United States?** (`govt_branches_count`, `number`, required): Please select the correct number of branches of government in the United States.
### Which document contains the first ten amendments to the Constitution?
* **Which document contains the first ten amendments to the Constitution?** (`bill_of_rights_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Please select the correct document that contains the first ten amendments to the Constitution.
### Do you know the name of your local city council representative?
* **Do you know the name of your local city council representative?** (`local_rep_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that applies to you.
### How important is it for citizens to vote in local elections?
* **How important is it for citizens to vote in local elections?** (`civic_duty_perception`, `number`, required): Please rate how important it is for citizens to vote in local elections on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Essential".
### Level of interest in local community issues
* **Level of interest in local community issues** (`interest_level_politics`, `select_one`, required): Please select the level of interest you have in local community issues.
### Where do you primarily get your civic and political information?
* **Where do you primarily get your civic and political information?** (`information_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select up to 3 options that apply to you.
### What is the biggest barrier to learning more about civic responsibilities?
* **What is the biggest barrier to learning more about civic responsibilities?** (`barriers_to_education`, `text`, required): Please provide a clear and concise answer.
### Would you attend a free community workshop on local governance?
* **Would you attend a free community workshop on local governance?** (`program_interest`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that applies to you.
### One way the city could better inform residents of their rights
* **One way the city could better inform residents of their rights** (`improvement_suggestion`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief answer.
### Email for results or program updates (Optional)
* **Email for results or program updates (Optional)** (`follow_up_email`, `email`, required): If you would like to receive updates on your survey results, please provide your email address.

This form is designed to help us understand your knowledge and attitudes towards civic education and governance. Your answers will help us identify areas for improvement and inform our initiatives to increase awareness and engagement among residents.
