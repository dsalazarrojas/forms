# Civic Education Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your knowledge and understanding of civic education in our local community. Your answers will help us understand what we can do to improve civic education and increase awareness among residents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, choosing the best option that applies to you.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select only the options that apply to you.
3. For the question "How important is it for citizens to vote in local elections?", please rate your answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Important" and 5 is "Essential".
4. For the question "Where do you primarily get your civic and political information?", select up to 3 options that apply to you.
5. Please provide a clear and concise answer for the question "What is the biggest barrier to learning more about civic responsibilities?".
6. For the question "Would you attend a free community workshop on local governance?", select only one option.
7. If you have any suggestions on how the city can better inform residents of their rights, please provide a brief answer.
