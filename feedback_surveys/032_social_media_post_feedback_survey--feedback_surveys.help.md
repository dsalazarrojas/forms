# Social Media Post Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about social media posts. We want to know what you think about the post and any comments you have. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the ID of the social media post you are providing feedback about.
2. Share your feedback about the post.
3. Rate the post on a scale of 1 to 5.
4. If you have more comments about the post, you can add them in the "Comments" field.
5. If you want to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address.
6. If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
7. Finally, please select a date for when you submitted your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Post ID**: Enter the ID of the social media post you are providing feedback about.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and comments about the post. This is your opportunity to tell us what you liked or disliked about the post.
* **Rating**: Rate the post on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not good" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comments**: If you have more specific comments or thoughts about the post, add them in this field.
* **Email**: If you want to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address. We'll get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Phone**: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Submitted**: Select a date for when you submitted your feedback. Please note that this will not affect the date your feedback is received.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically generated and is not editable. It shows when your feedback was received.

## Tips
* Please note that you can only submit one response per social media post.
* If you are having trouble with this form, please contact us and we'll be happy to help.
