# Investment Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Investment Policy Awareness Survey is designed to gather feedback and information from employees regarding investment policies, ensuring that these policies are clear, accessible, and relevant to the organization's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field and its options to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. For fields with numerical values (e.g., employee understanding, policy update frequency, policy awareness level), use numbers to express your feedback.
3. For fields with text or open-ended options (e.g., feedback, employee suggestions, comments), provide clear and concise answers.
4. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (indicated by a star (*) in the field label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Policy Awareness Survey** (1): This is the title of the form, which you do not need to fill out.
* **Employee Understanding** (2): This field is used to gauge your understanding of the investment policies. Please answer on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 indicating you have little to no understanding and 10 indicating you have a good understanding.
* **Feedback** (3): This field is for providing any feedback or comments you might have about the investment policies. Be specific and concise in your answer.
* **Training Needs** (4): Select all areas where you require training on investment policies (Finance, Compliance, HR, Risk Management, or Other).
* **Policy Changes** (6): Indicate whether you believe there should be a new policy, an update to an existing policy, no policy changes, or that policy changes are not applicable.
* **Reporting Channels** (9): Select the channel through which you would prefer to receive policy updates (Email, Web portal, Phone, In-person, or Other).
* **Reporting Frequency** (10): This field is related to how often you would like to receive policy updates.
* **Reporting Deadline** (11): If you prefer a specific deadline for receiving policy updates, enter the date you would prefer.
* **Policy Review Frequency** (12): Indicate how frequently you think policy reviews should be conducted (Monthly, Quarterly, Biannually, or Annually).
* **Reporting Responsible** (13): Select who you believe is responsible for reporting policy updates (Manager, Department Head, Risk Management, or Policy Owner).
* **Policy Awareness Program** (14): Indicate whether you believe a policy awareness program is in place or not.
* **Awareness Level** (15): This field is similar to Employee Understanding but focuses on the overall awareness level of investment policies within the organization.
* **Employee Concerns** (16): This field is for expressing any concerns you might have about investment policies.
* **Policy Awareness Score** (17): This field is similar to Awareness Level but might be used to gauge the overall awareness level of investment policies.
* **Policy Awareness Level** (18): Select the level of awareness you believe the organization has about investment policies (High, Moderate, or Low).
* **Comments** (19): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you might have.
* **Form Completed By** (20): This field is automatically filled out and not to be filled by the user.
* **Form Completed Date** (21): This field is also automatically filled out and not to be filled by the user.
* **Policy Change Request** (22): If you have any specific suggestions for changing an investment policy, enter them here.
* **Policy Change Request Reason** (23): If you have any reason for requesting a policy change, enter it here.
* **Employee Signature** (24): This field is for signing off on the completion of the form.
* **Manager Comments** (25): If you have any comments or feedback for your manager, enter them here.
