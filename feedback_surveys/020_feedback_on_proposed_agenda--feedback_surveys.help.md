# feedback_on_proposed_agenda - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and comments on a proposed agenda for an event. It is designed to gauge the overall satisfaction with the proposed agenda and gather additional comments and suggestions from users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Provide your honest and constructive feedback on the proposed agenda.
*   Make sure to provide your email address so that we can get in touch with you if we need further clarification.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, optional): The name of the event that the proposed agenda is for.
*   **Agenda Date** (`agenda_date`, `date`, optional): The date of the proposed agenda.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): The time of the proposed agenda.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): The start date of the event.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): The end date of the event.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): The start time of the event.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): The end time of the event.
*   **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the attendees that you are providing feedback for.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide your comments and suggestions on the proposed agenda.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide your overall feedback on the proposed agenda.
*   **Proposed Agenda** (`proposed_agenda`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you agree or disagree with the proposed agenda.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the proposed agenda on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide your comments on the proposed agenda.
*   **Proposed Agenda Comments** (`proposed_agenda_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide your comments on the proposed agenda.
*   **Proposed Agenda Rating** (`proposed_agenda_rating`, `text`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the proposed agenda on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Proposed Agenda Rating 2** (`proposed_agenda_rating_2`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the proposed agenda on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Proposed Agenda Comments** (`proposed_agenda_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide your comments on the proposed agenda.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide your overall feedback on the proposed agenda.
*   **Proposed Agenda Rating 3** (`proposed_agenda_rating_3`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the proposed agenda on a scale of 1-5.
