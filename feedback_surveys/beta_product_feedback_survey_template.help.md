# beta_product_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The beta product feedback survey template is designed to gather information from users about their experience with the beta product. This feedback will help the development team understand areas that need improvement, identify user preferences, and determine the likelihood of users recommending the product to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Answer the first question about your level of satisfaction with your experience using the beta product.
2. Select all the features or aspects of the product that you used or interacted with.
3. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the beta product to a colleague.
4. Provide the single most important thing you would improve or change in the product.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Very satisfied** (Satisfaction Level)
* (`very_satisfied`, `text`, required): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with your experience using the beta product. Select the option that best reflects your feelings.

### **Which features or aspects of the product did you use or interact with?** (Feature Selection)
* (`features`, `multiple`, required): This question asks you to select all the features or aspects of the product that you used or interacted with. You can select multiple options by checking the boxes next to each one.

### **Very likely** (Recommendation Likelihood)
* (`recommend`, `text`, required): This question asks about how likely you are to recommend this beta product to a colleague. Select the option that best reflects your feelings.

### **What is the single most important thing you would improve or change in the product?**
* (`improvement`, `text`, required): This question asks you to think about what you would improve or change in the beta product. Please provide a specific answer to this open-ended question.

### **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**
* (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the beta product, please share them here. This is an optional question, but your input will be valuable to the development team.
