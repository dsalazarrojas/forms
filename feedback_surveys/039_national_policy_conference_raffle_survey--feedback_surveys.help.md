# National Policy Conference Raffle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Policy Conference Raffle Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and opinions from attendees of the National Policy Conference. This form is intended to help us understand attendees' experiences and preferences, and to provide valuable input for future conference planning and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (those marked as "required").
3. Select your preferences and opinions from the available options.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, optional): This field is a text input for any feedback or comments about the sessions you attended at the conference. Please share any thoughts, suggestions, or ideas you have about your experience.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for any further discussion or follow-up, please provide your contact information here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is the email address we will use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number here.
* **Favorite Session?** (`favorite_session`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you had a favorite session, and "No" if you did not.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you would like to share.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the sessions you attended as "Good", "Fair", or leave it blank for no rating.
* **Raffle Entry?** (`raffle_entry`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to be entered into the raffle, and "No" if you are not interested.
* **Your Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name if you want to be contacted for any further discussion or follow-up.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any difficulty or questions, contact [your support email/phone number].
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving this survey, let us know!
