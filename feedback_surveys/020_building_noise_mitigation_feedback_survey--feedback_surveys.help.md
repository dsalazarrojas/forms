# Building Noise Mitigation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from building occupants on noise levels and mitigation efforts in their work or living spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
* For multiple-select fields, choose all applicable options.
* For text fields, provide detailed descriptions whenever possible.
* Submit your completed form at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (1): This is the date when you are completing this survey.
* **Building Location** (2): Provide the name or address of the building where you are currently located.
* **Unit or Room Number** (3): If applicable, provide the number of your unit or room.
* **Occupancy Duration** (4): Choose how long you have been occupying this space.
* **Current Noise Level Perception** (5): Choose how loud or quiet you perceive the noise levels in the building.
* **Primary Noise Sources** (6): Select all noise sources that affect you the most (e.g., traffic, neighboring units, etc.).
* **Peak Noise Times** (7): Choose the times when the noise is most noticeable.
* **Noise Impact on Your Activities** (8): Choose how much the noise affects your daily activities.
* **Sleep Disturbance** (9): Choose how often noise keeps you awake or disrupts your sleep.
* **Noise Impact on Work or Study** (10): Choose how much the noise affects your work or study.
* **Previous Complaints** (11): Check if you have reported noise issues before.
* **Response to Complaints** (12): Choose how your previous complaints were handled.
* **Awareness of Noise Mitigation Efforts** (13): Choose how aware you are of efforts to improve noise levels.
* **Effectiveness of Mitigation Efforts** (14): Choose how effective you feel the noise mitigation efforts have been.
* **Suggested Improvements** (15): Provide additional suggestions for noise reduction in the building.
* **Willing to Contribute to Solutions** (16): Check if you are willing to help address noise issues.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Noise Levels** (17): Choose how satisfied you are with the current noise levels.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
