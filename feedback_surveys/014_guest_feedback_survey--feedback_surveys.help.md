# Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from guests about their stay. Your responses will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Select all the options that apply to you.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
- You can rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Stay Date** (`stay_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your stay.
  This field is optional, but it helps us track your feedback by stay date.

* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of your room.
  This field is optional, but it helps us understand which room you stayed in.

* **Feedback on Services** (`feedback_on_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you experienced during your stay.
  Please choose from the options below:
  - Option 1: Housekeeping
  - Option 2: Food and Beverage
  - Option 3: Front Desk

* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about your stay.
  Use this field to provide a more detailed account of your experience.

* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
  Please rate your stay as:
  1. Poor
  2. Fair
  3. Good
  4. Very Good
  5. Excellent

* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  This field is optional, but it helps us keep in touch with you for any follow-up.

Tips:

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please contact our guest services team.
* We appreciate your input and suggestions, and we will use them to improve our services.
