# Employee Trust And Loyalty Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from employees on their trust and loyalty towards the company, as well as demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the options in each of the following sections: 
    *   "Employee Survey"
    *   "Trust Questions"
    *   "Engagement Questions" (this field is a date field where you can input any date in the format MM/DD/YYYY)
    *   "Job Title"
    *   "Reporting Line"
2.  Enter your email address in the "Employee Info" field.
3.  Enter the name of your department in the "Department Info" field.
4.  Enter any information regarding your manager in the "Manager Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey** (`Employee Survey`, dropdown, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you feel you can trust your colleagues. This question aims to gauge the level of trust you feel among your coworkers.
* **Trust Questions** (`Trust Questions`, text, required/optional): Please provide any questions that you have regarding trust with your colleagues or company policies.
* **Loyalty Questions** (`Loyalty Questions`, dropdown, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you feel you can be loyal to the company. This question aims to gauge the level of loyalty you feel towards the organization.
* **Engagement Questions** (`Engagement Questions`, date, required/optional): Please provide the date of your last engagement activity (such as a training session or a meeting).
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, dropdown, required/optional): Please select your current job title from the list provided.
* **Reporting Line** (`Reporting Line`, text, required/optional): Please specify who you report to directly.
* **Employee Info** (`Employee Info`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Department Info** (`Department Info`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your department.
* **Manager Info** (`Manager Info`, text, required/optional): Please enter information regarding your manager.
* **Company Info** (`Company Info`, text, required/optional): Please enter any additional company information that you want to share.
