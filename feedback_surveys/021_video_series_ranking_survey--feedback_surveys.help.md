# Video Series Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and ratings about video series. Your input will help us understand user engagement and improve our content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** and select the **Video Series** you are providing feedback on.
2. Provide the **Episode Number** you watched.
3. Rate the video series using the **Rating** field.
4. If you have any comments, please provide them in the **Comments** field.
5. Select which episodes you have **Watched Episodes** (if any).
6. Choose if you have watched the entire **Watched Series** (yes/no).
7. Leave any additional feedback in the **Feedback** field.
8. Provide your **Email** (if you would like us to follow up with you about your feedback).
9. Optionally, enter your **Phone** number for further contact (if you prefer).
10. Enter the time and date you watched the series in the **Watch Time** and **Watch Date** fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Your name as it appears on your profile.
* **Video Series**: The name of the video series you are providing feedback on.
* **Episode Number**: The number of the episode you watched.
* **Rating**: Rate the video series from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments you have about the video series.
* **Watched Episodes**: Select which episodes you have watched (if any).
* **Watched Series**: Choose if you have watched the entire series (yes/no).
* **Feedback**: Leave any additional feedback about the video series.
* **Email**: Your email address (if you would like us to follow up with you about your feedback).
* **Phone**: Your phone number (if you prefer).
* **Watch Time**: Enter the time you spent watching the video series.
* **Watch Date**: Enter the date you watched the video series.

## Tips
* Be specific when providing feedback, as it helps us understand your experience.
* If you have any technical issues, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
