# Service Non Renewal Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Non Renewal Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers who have chosen not to renew their service with the company. This information will help us understand why customers decided not to renew and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the most accurate option for each question.
3. If you select "Other (please specify)", provide a brief description of your reason for not renewing.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Open-Ended Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Renewing Customer**: Select the reason why you decided not to renew with us.
	+ Why did you decide not to renew with us?
	+ What was the main reason for not renewing?
	+ Other (please specify): If you select this option, please provide a brief description of your reason for not renewing.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Suggest how we could improve**: Provide any suggestions or ideas on how we could improve to retain customers in the future.
* **Main Reason for Not Renewing**: Select all that apply for the main reason you had for not renewing with us.
	+ Lack of interest
	+ Not satisfied with service
	+ Too expensive
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Open-Ended Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method if you would like us to get in touch with you.
* **Non Renewing Customer Email**: If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your email address.
* **Customer Service**: Rate your experience with our customer service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Last Service Used**: Select the time frame when you last used our service.
	+ Less than 1 month
	+ 1 month to 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ More than 1 year
	+ Never
