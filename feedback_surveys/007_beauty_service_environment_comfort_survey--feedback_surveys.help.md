# Beauty Service Environment Comfort Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to assess your experience and comfort level during your visit to our salon. The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on various aspects of our environment, including comfort, temperature, seating, and overall ambiance. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance your experience in our salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question according to your genuine experience. Most fields require a selection from the given options, while some fields allow for free text responses. You can choose one of the provided options that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name (`client_name`, text, optional)
* This is an optional field where you can provide your name if you wish to do so. If not, just leave it blank.

### 2. Date of Visit (`visit_date`, date, required)
* Please select the date of your visit to our salon.

### 3. Service Received (`service_received`, select_one, required)
* Choose the service you received during your visit.

### 4. Overall Comfort Level (`overall_comfort`, select_one, required)
* Rate your overall comfort level during your visit.

### 5. Temperature Comfort (`temperature_comfort`, select_one, required)
* Choose how comfortable you felt with the temperature in the salon.

### 6. Seating Comfort (`seating_comfort`, select_one, required)
* Rate the comfort of your seating during your visit.

### 7. Lighting Comfort (`lighting_comfort`, select_one, required)
* Select how you felt about the lighting in the salon.

### 8. Noise Level (`noise_level`, select_one, required)
* Choose how you would describe the noise level in the salon.

### 9. Music Preference (`music_preference`, select_one, optional)
* If you listened to the background music, choose how you liked it.

### 10. Cleanliness Rating (`cleanliness_rating`, select_one, required)
* Rate the cleanliness of the facility.

### 11. Waiting Area Comfort (`waiting_area_comfort`, select_one, required)
* Rate the comfort of the waiting area.

### 12. Privacy Level (`privacy_level`, select_one, required)
* Select how private you felt during your service.

### 13. Aroma and Scent (`aroma_scent`, select_one, optional)
* If you noticed any scents in the salon, rate their pleasantness.

### 14. Bathroom Cleanliness (`bathroom_cleanliness`, select_one, required)
* Rate the cleanliness of the bathroom.

### 15. Equipment and Tools Condition (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required)
* Rate the condition of equipment and tools.

### 16. Ventilation and Air Quality (`ventilation`, select_one, required)
* Select how you felt about the air quality and ventilation.

### 17. Accessibility (`accessibility`, select_one, optional)
* If you had any mobility issues, rate how accessible you found the facility.

### 18. Favorite Feature of Our Environment (`favorite_feature`, text, optional)
* If you have a favorite feature, describe it in this text field.

### 19. Suggestions for Improvement (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional)
* If you have any suggestions, type them in this field.

### 20. Will You Return (`will_return`, select_one, required)
* Choose how likely you are to return to our salon based on your experience.
