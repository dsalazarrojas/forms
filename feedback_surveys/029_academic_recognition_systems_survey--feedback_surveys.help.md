# Academic Recognition Systems Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your experiences and opinions about academic recognition systems at our institution. Your feedback will help us improve these systems to better support and recognize our community members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your role in the institution from the options provided.
2. Answer questions about your awareness and participation in recognition programs.
3. Rate your satisfaction with various aspects of recognition systems, such as the variety of recognition types, frequency of opportunities, and transparency of selection processes.
4. Share any challenges you face with current recognition systems and suggest improvements.
5. Indicate your preferences for additional recognition methods.
6. Rate the impact of recognition on your motivation and decision to stay at the institution.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
8. Lastly, rate your overall satisfaction with recognition at our institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Role in the Institution (2)**: Select your primary role within the institution, which will help us understand how different roles interact with recognition systems.
* **Years at This Institution (3)**: Please provide the number of years you have been affiliated with this institution.
* **Department or College (4)**: Identify your primary department or college affiliation.
* **Awareness of Recognition Programs (5)**: Indicate your level of awareness of recognition programs available at your institution.
* **Participation in Recognition Programs (6)**: Share your experience with recognition programs, whether as a recipient, nominator, or evaluator.
* **Variety of Recognition Types (7)**: Rate your satisfaction with the variety of recognition types offered.
* **Frequency of Recognition Opportunities (8)**: Rate your satisfaction with the frequency of recognition opportunities.
* **Transparency of Selection Process (9)**: Rate your perception of the transparency of the selection process for recognition.
* **Adequacy of Recognition Rewards (10)**: Rate your satisfaction with the adequacy of recognition rewards.
* **Impact of Recognition on Motivation (11)**: Rate the impact of recognition on your motivation.
* **Challenges with Recognition Systems (12)**: Select all challenges you face with current recognition systems.
* **Preferred Recognition Methods (13)**: Select all preferred methods of recognition.
* **Recognition Impact on Retention (14)**: Rate the impact of recognition on your decision to stay at the institution.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (15)**: Provide any suggestions for improving recognition systems.
* **Additional Recognition Programs Desired (16)**: Share any additional recognition programs you would like to see.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Recognition (17)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with recognition at your institution.
