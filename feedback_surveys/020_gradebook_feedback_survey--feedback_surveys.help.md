# Gradebook Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Gradebook Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback and survey responses from students and staff about their interactions with teachers. The survey aims to collect data on the effectiveness of teaching methods, student engagement, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, click on the link provided to access the survey.
2.  Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields. The required fields are marked as such.
3.  For each question, provide detailed and honest feedback.
4.  Select the correct options for each field as required.
5.  Once you have completed all the pages, click submit to save your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher you are providing feedback for.
*   **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): Provide a question or area of improvement you would like to address.
*   **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or comments from the staff member you are working with.
*   **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or comments from the student you are working with.
*   **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will follow up with the teacher or staff member.
*   **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, required): Select how you will follow up (Email, Phone, Email and Phone).
*   **Term End Date** (`term_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the term ends.
*   **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, select_one, required): Select how you would rate the teacher (Excellent, Very good, Average, Below Average).
*   **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts from the staff member.
*   **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts from the student.
*   **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you will follow up with the teacher or staff member.
*   **Next Follow-up Method** (`next_follow_up_method`, select_one, required): Select how you will follow up (Email, Phone, Email and Phone).
*   **Term End Date** (`term_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the term ends.
*   **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts from the teacher.
*   **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, optional): Enter a rating of the staff member's performance (1-5).
*   **Student Rating** (`student_rating`, number, optional): Enter a rating of the student's performance (1-5).
*   **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Select the overall rating (Excellent, Very good, Average, Below Average).
*   **Staff Feedback Question** (`staff_feedback_question`, text, optional): Provide any questions or areas of improvement for the staff member.
*   **Staff Suggestions** (`staff_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for the staff member.
*   **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Select the overall rating (Excellent, Very good, Average, Below Average).

## Tips

*   Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
*   Select the correct options for each field.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
