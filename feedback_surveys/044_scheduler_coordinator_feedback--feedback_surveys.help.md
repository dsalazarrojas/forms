# Scheduler Coordinator Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide feedback on the performance, communication, and professionalism of the scheduler coordinator. It allows you to give input on how well the coordinator is doing their job and suggest improvements for continuous growth. The form is intended for use by staff members, managers, and supervisors who need to evaluate the coordinator's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully, taking note of any specific questions or prompts that you want to answer or comment on.
2. Answer each question or prompt as honestly and thoroughly as possible, providing concrete examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
3. Consider using the "Feedback on Performance," "Feedback on Communication," and "Feedback on Professionalism" sections to give specific examples or examples of strengths and weaknesses.
4. In the "Continuous Improvement" section, select one or more options that indicate areas where the coordinator needs to improve.
5. If you have specific suggestions or comments that don't fit into the existing sections, use the "Coordinator Suggestions" fields to provide additional feedback.
6. Finally, use the "Feedback To" fields to indicate to whom you want to direct your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coordinator Performance** (`coordinator_performance`, text, required/optional): Describe the coordinator's overall performance, including their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Staff Communication** (`staff_communication`, text, required/optional): Comment on the coordinator's communication skills, including their ability to keep staff informed and respond to questions and concerns.
* **Scheduling Professionalism** (`scheduling_professionalism`, text, required/optional): Evaluate the coordinator's scheduling skills, including their ability to manage schedules, prioritize tasks, and coordinate with other teams.
* **Feedback Form Helps** (`feedback_form_helps`, select one, required/optional): Indicate how helpful the feedback form has been, with options for "Very much," "Somewhat," and "Not very much."
* **Feedback on Performance** (`feedback_on_performance`, text, required/optional): Provide specific feedback on the coordinator's performance, including strengths and weaknesses.
* **Feedback on Communication** (`feedback_on_communication`, text, required/optional): Comment on the coordinator's communication skills, including their ability to communicate clearly and effectively.
* **Feedback on Professionalism** (`feedback_on_professionalism`, text, required/optional): Evaluate the coordinator's professionalism, including their ability to maintain confidentiality and follow procedures.
* **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate areas where the coordinator needs to improve, with options for "True," "False," and "Unsure."
* **Coordinator Suggestions** (`coordinator_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Provide additional feedback or suggestions for the coordinator's growth and improvement.
* **Feedback To** (`feedback_to`, text, required/optional): Direct your feedback to the coordinator, manager, or supervisor, as appropriate.
* **Manager Suggestions** (`manager_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback or suggestions for the manager's growth and improvement.
* **Supervisor Suggestions** (`supervisor_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback or suggestions for the supervisor's growth and improvement.
* **Staff Suggestions** (`staff_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Suggest improvements for the staff, including changes to processes or procedures.
* **Feedback To Coordinator** (`feedback_to_coordinator`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback to the coordinator directly.
* **Feedback To Manager** (`feedback_to_manager`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback to the manager directly.
* **Feedback To Supervisor** (`feedback_to_supervisor`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback to the supervisor directly.

Note: Please provide feedback that is specific, concrete, and actionable. Avoid general statements or comments that do not provide useful insight.
