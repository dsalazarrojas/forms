# youtube_brand_presence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youtube_brand_presence_survey is a form designed to collect feedback on the brand presence and engagement of certain tech giants in the market, specifically YouTube.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For Page 2, select all the brands you are familiar with from the list (Apple, Google, Microsoft, Facebook, Amazon, Netflix, Other).
3. For Page 3, select the level of engagement you have with these brands (Low, Medium, High).
4. For Page 4, select all the content types you are familiar with from the list (Music, Video, Live Streaming, Live Streaming and Music, Other).
5. You can skip Pages 5 and 6 if you don't have a specific date or time in mind.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Familiarity** (`Brand Familiarity`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the brands you are familiar with from the list to help us gauge your level of awareness and recognition of these brands.
* **Engagement** (`Engagement`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of engagement you have with these brands, with Low, Medium, or High representing the intensity of your interaction with them.
* **Content Preference** (`Content Preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the content types you are familiar with from the list to help us understand your preferences when it comes to content consumption.
* **Page 5** (`Page 5`, `date`, optional): Enter a specific date if you have a particular event or milestone related to your engagement with these brands.
* **Page 6** (`Page 6`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of day you usually engage with these brands.
