# Campus Sustainability Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Campus Sustainability Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from students, faculty, and staff about their experiences and perceptions of sustainability initiatives on campus. Your input will help us better understand what works and what doesn't, and inform future projects to improve campus sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose your role at the campus from the provided options (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Alumnus).
2.  Rate your overall sustainability awareness, knowledge of recycling programs, and knowledge of energy conservation efforts on a scale from Very Low to Very High.
3.  Rate your satisfaction with waste management, green spaces, and sustainable dining options on a scale from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
4.  Indicate how often you participate in recycling and campus green events.
5.  Express your willingness to participate in future sustainability initiatives.
6.  Identify areas where you think we can improve (e.g., Waste, Energy, Water, Transport, Food, or Other).
7.  Suggest a new project or initiative for sustainability.
8.  Provide an overall rating for campus sustainability on a scale from 1 to 10.
9.  Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role at Campus (1)** (`role_at_campus`, Select One, **Required**): Choose your primary affiliation with the campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Alumnus).
* **Department or Major (2)** (`department_major`, Text, **Required**): Enter your department or major.
* **Overall sustainability awareness (4)** (`overall_awareness`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your overall awareness of campus sustainability from Very Low to Very High.
* **Recycling knowledge (5)** (`recycling_knowledge`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your knowledge of campus recycling programs from Very Low to Very High.
* **Energy knowledge (6)** (`energy_knowledge`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your knowledge of energy conservation efforts on campus from Very Low to Very High.
* **Waste satisfaction (8)** (`waste_satisfaction`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your satisfaction with waste management from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Green spaces satisfaction (9)** (`green_spaces_satisfaction`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your satisfaction with green spaces on campus from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Sustainable dining satisfaction (10)** (`dining_satisfaction`, Select One, **Required**): Rate your satisfaction with sustainable dining options from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Recycling frequency (12)** (`recycling_frequency`, Select One, **Required**): Indicate how often you use recycling bins (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
* **Green event participation (13)** (`green_event_participation`, Select One, **Required**): Indicate how often you participate in campus green events (Regularly, Occasionally, Never).
* **Willingness to participate (14)** (`willingness_to_participate`, Select One, **Required**): Express your willingness to participate in future sustainability initiatives (Very Willing, Willing, Neutral, Unwilling, Very Unwilling).
* **Areas for improvement (15)** (`improvement_areas`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Select all areas where you think we can improve (Waste, Energy, Water, Transport, Food, Other).
* **New project suggestion (16)** (`new_project_suggestion`, Text, **Optional**): Suggest a new project or initiative for sustainability.
* **Overall campus rating (17)** (`overall_rating`, Number, **Required**): Rate your overall campus sustainability on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Additional comments (18)** (`additional_comments`, Text, **Optional**): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
