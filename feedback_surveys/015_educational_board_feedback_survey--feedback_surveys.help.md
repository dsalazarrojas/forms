# Educational Board Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users on the Educational Board's performance and services. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields with accurate and honest information.
*   Answer the questions in the provided fields.
*   Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
*   Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School District Name** (`school_district_name`, required/optional): This is the name of the school district you are associated with.
*   **What is your feedback on our educational board?** (`feedback_questions`, required/optional): Please provide your honest feedback on our educational board's performance.
*   **How satisfied are you with our educational board?** (`satisfaction_rating`, required/optional): Please rate your satisfaction with our educational board on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Do you have any suggestions for our educational board?** (`suggestions`, required/optional): If you have any suggestions or improvements for our board, please provide them in this field.
*   **Which school district are you associated with?** (`school_districts`, required/optional): Please select all the school districts you are associated with.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, optional/optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional/optional): If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
*   **Date** (`date`, optional/optional): If you want to date your feedback, please provide the date.
*   **Time** (`time`, optional/optional): If you want to date and time your feedback, please provide the time.
*   **Educational Board Rating** (`educational_board_rating`, required/optional): Please select one of the provided options to rate our educational board.
*   **Rating** (`rating`, required/optional): Please rate your experience with our educational board on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Category** (`category`, required/optional): Please select one of the provided categories that best describe your feedback.
*   **Category Other** (`category_other`, optional/optional): If none of the provided categories fit your feedback, please describe the category in this field.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, required/optional): Please submit your feedback to help us improve our services.
*   **Name** (`name`, required/optional): Please fill out your name, so we can associate it with your feedback.
*   **Department** (`department`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your department, please fill it out.
*   **Role** (`role`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your role, please fill it out.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your job title, please fill it out.
*   **Location** (`location`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your location, please fill it out.
*   **Job Function** (`job_function`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your job function, please fill it out.
*   **Company** (`company`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your company, please fill it out.
*   **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your job title 2, please fill it out.
*   **Department 2** (`department_2`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your department 2, please fill it out.
*   **Role 2** (`role_2`, optional/optional): If you want to provide your role 2, please fill it out.
