# Coaching Session Client Feedback Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from coaching session clients, helping coaches improve their skills and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, as it will help coaches understand your experience and improve future sessions.
2. For each field, select the most accurate option that best describes your opinion.
3. You can skip fields that are not applicable to your session.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, not required): Your full name. This is an optional field, but please provide your name if you want to be contacted for any follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, not required): Your email address. This is optional, but it will help us contact you for any further communication.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): The name of your coach. Please enter the name of the coach who conducted your session.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, `date`, required): The date of this feedback. Please select the date of your coaching session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): The date of your coaching session. Please select the date of your session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of session you had (Initial Consultation, Regular Session, Follow-Up, Intensive, Group Session).
* **Session Format** (`session_format`, `select_one`, required): Select the format of your session (In Person, Video Call, Phone Call).
* **Overall Session Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall session (5 Stars Excellent, 4 Stars Very Good, 3 Stars Good, 2 Stars Fair, 1 Star Poor).
* **Value for Time Invested** (`value_for_time`, `select_one`, required): Rate the value of the time invested (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Not Worth It).
* **Coach Preparation** (`coach_preparation`, `select_one`, required): Rate the coach's preparation (Very Well Prepared, Prepared, Somewhat Prepared, Not Well Prepared, Unprepared).
* **Coach Presence and Attention** (`coach_presence`, `select_one`, required): Rate the coach's presence and attention (Fully Present, Mostly Present, Somewhat Present, Distracted, Not Present).
* **Did You Feel Safe to Share** (`safe_space`, `select_one`, required): Rate how safe you felt to share (Very Safe, Safe, Neutral, Unsafe, Very Unsafe).
* **Did You Feel Heard and Understood** (`heard_and_understood`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well you felt heard and understood (Completely, Mostly, Somewhat, Not Really, Not At All).
* **Did You Gain Useful Insights** (`useful_insights`, `select_one`, required): Rate how valuable the session was (Yes Significant, Yes Some, Neutral, Not Really, Not At All).
* **Did You Identify Actionable Steps** (`actionable_steps`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear the actionable steps were (Yes Clear Steps, Yes Some Ideas, Neutral, Not Really, Not At All).
* **Coach Strengths** (`coach_strengths`, `text`, not required): If you have any comments on your coach's strengths.
* **Areas for Coach Improvement** (`coach_improvements`, `text`, not required): If you have any suggestions for areas of improvement.
* **Session Highlights** (`session_highlights`, `text`, not required): If there were any specific moments that stood out to you.
* **Any Challenges in Session** (`session_challenges`, `text`, not required): If you faced any challenges during the session.
* **Relevance to Your Goals** (`goal_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate how relevant the session was to your goals (Very Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Not Relevant).
* **Energy Level After Session** (`energy_level_after`, `select_one`, required): Rate your energy level after the session (Energized, Positive, Neutral, Drained, Negative).
* **Clarity Level After Session** (`clarity_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear you felt after the session (Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Confused).
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Rate your likelihood to recommend the coach (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Plan to Continue Coaching** (`continue_coaching`, `select_one`, required): Rate your plan to continue coaching (Definitely, Probably, Not Sure, Probably Not, Definitely Not).
* **Testimonial Permission** (`testimonial_permission`, `select_one`, not required): If you are okay with your feedback being shared, select "Yes Use My Name" or "Yes Anonymously". If not, select "No Do Not Share".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
