# 360 Degree Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Degree Feedback Survey is a form used to gather feedback on an employee's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement from multiple perspectives, including management, peers, and self-assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid email address and department name.
2. Choose whether you wish to remain anonymous.
3. Select your job title and date of hire.
4. Rate your satisfaction and overall experience.
5. Answer questions about your strengths, areas for improvement, desired training topics, work-life balance, and overall role satisfaction.
6. Provide specific suggestions for improvement.
7. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (Required): Enter your work email address. This will be used for survey summary only.
* **Department** (Required): Enter your department name, e.g., Marketing, Finance.
* **Job title** (Required): Enter your job title, e.g., Senior Analyst.
* **Date of hire** (Optional): Enter the date you were hired in the format yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Years employed** (Optional): Enter the approximate number of full years you have been employed.
* **Survey date** (Required): Enter the date you complete this survey.
* **Anonymous** (Required): Choose whether you wish to remain anonymous. If you choose yes, your responses will be anonymized.
* **Satisfaction** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the 360 Degree Feedback Survey on a scale of Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Management rating** (Required): Rate the communication from management on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Supervisor rating** (Required): Rate your supervisor's support on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Peer rating** (Required): Rate the collaboration with peers on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Self-assessment** (Required): Self-assess your performance on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Skills assessment** (Optional): Select all skills that you feel strongest in. You can select more than one skill.
* **Strengths** (Optional): List up to 3 of your key strengths.
* **Areas for improvement** (Optional): List up to 3 areas where you seek improvement.
* **Training needs** (Optional): List desired training or development topics, e.g., Advanced Excel, Leadership Coaching.
* **Work-life balance** (Required): Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Overall rating** (Required): Rate your overall experience on a role on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Improvement suggestions** (Optional): Provide specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
