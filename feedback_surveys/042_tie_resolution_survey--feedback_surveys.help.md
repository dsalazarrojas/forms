# Tie Resolution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tie Resolution Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users regarding their experience with the tie resolution process. This form helps us understand users' satisfaction levels with the tie resolution process, tie breakers, and decision-makers, and gather their improvement ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. If you have any improvement ideas, feel free to share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Details** (1): A text field where you can provide any additional information about the tie resolution process you participated in.
* **Satisfaction Level** (2): A numeric field where you can rate your level of satisfaction with the tie resolution process, from 0 to 100.
* **Improvement Ideas** (3): A text field where you can share any ideas you have to improve the tie resolution process.
* **Decision Process** (4): A text field where you can describe your experience with the decision-making process during the tie resolution.
* **Satisfaction with Tie Breaker** (5): A numeric field where you can rate your level of satisfaction with the tie breaker used, from 0 to 100.
* **Satisfaction with Decision Maker** (6): A text field where you can describe your experience with the decision-maker.
* **Tie Breaker Methods** (7): A multiple-choice field where you can select all applicable tie breaker methods used.
