# unss_functionality_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the usability and overall satisfaction of a system or application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out the survey questions in the order they appear.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you need to answer it truthfully.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience with the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, text, optional): This is the header for the form, and you can leave it as is or modify it as per your preference.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the system:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If you have any feedback or comments that do not fit into any other question, please provide a rating from 1-5 to show your level of satisfaction.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how to improve the system, please share them here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the system.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for any further questions or discussions, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the system.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please do not hesitate to reach out.
* Your feedback will help us improve the system, thank you for taking the time to provide it!
