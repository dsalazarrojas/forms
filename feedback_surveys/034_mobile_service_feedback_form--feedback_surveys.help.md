# Mobile Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or users about their experience with the mobile service. It is a crucial tool for understanding customer satisfaction and preferences, which helps us improve our service to better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer all the required questions, marked with **(*)**.
2. Select the relevant options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the text fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, *Required*): This field is a numerical rating that represents your overall experience with the mobile service. Please enter a number from 1 to 10 to indicate how satisfied you are with our service.
* **Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `number`, *Required*): This field is a numerical rating that gauges your level of satisfaction with the service. Please enter a number from 1 to 10 to express your satisfaction.
* **Comments** (`feedback`, `text`, *Required*): This field is a text area where you can provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the service. Please write your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **How likely are you to recommend?** (`rating_scale`, `select_multiple`, *Required*): This field asks about your likelihood of recommending the service to others. Please select from the options: Very likely, Not likely, or Somewhat likely.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *Required*): This field asks for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *Required*): This field asks for your phone number. Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **How would you rate the service?** (`rating_service`, `select_one`, *Required*): This field asks you to rate the service, and you can choose from Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the app?** (`rating_app`, `select_one`, *Required*): This field asks you to rate the app, and you can choose from Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Any additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `note`, *Optional*): This field is a text area where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience. Please use this field to provide more details.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, *Required*): This field asks for your name. Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Any additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, *Optional*): This field is a text area where you can provide any additional comments about your experience. Please use this field to provide more details.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, `select_one`, *Required*): This field asks if you want to receive emails from us. If you select "True", we will send you updates about our service.
* **Email** (`email_domain`, `text`, *Optional*): This field asks for your email domain or address. If you enter an email address, we will use it to contact you if needed.
* **Contact us** (`contact_us`, `select_one`, *Required*): This field asks if you are willing to contact us if needed. Please select "Yes" if you are available for contact.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Mobile Service Feedback Form accurately and provide valuable feedback that can help us improve our service.
