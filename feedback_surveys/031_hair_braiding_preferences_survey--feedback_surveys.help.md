# Hair Braiding Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about clients' preferences for hair braiding styles, salon experiences, and other relevant details. This information will help us understand our clients' needs and improve our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the braiding styles that you are interested in.
2. Enter your previous salon experiences, if any.
3. Choose your name from the options provided.
4. Enter your hair length in inches.
5. Select your hair color.
6. Choose how often you get braided.
7. Select your hair type.
8. Enter the date and time when you were surveyed.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number (optional).
11. Select the user who was assigned to you.
12. Enter the category and description of the form.
13. Enter the form ID and title.
14. Enter the form category.
15. Enter the form description.
16. Enter the tool assigned to you.
17. Enter the name of the output file.
18. Enter who created this form.
19. Enter the date and time when this form was created.
20. Enter who last updated this form.
21. Enter the date and time when this form was last updated.
