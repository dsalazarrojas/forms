# Live Stream Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Stream Content Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on our live stream content. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our content and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, navigate to the survey and select the "Live Stream Content Feedback" page.
2. Please select the relevant options for "Event Title", "Event Date", and "Event Type" that best describe the live stream you are providing feedback on.
3. Choose how likely you were to be engaged throughout the stream from the options provided.
4. Indicate how much you enjoyed the live stream using the options provided.
5. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please feel free to share them in the text box.
6. Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need further information.
7. Enter your phone number and name to ensure we can reach you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Live Stream Content Feedback** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please take a moment to read it before proceeding.
* **Event Title**, **Event Date**, **Event Type** (`event_details`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the event title, date, and type that best describe the live stream you are providing feedback on. These options will help us identify the specific stream you are referring to.
* **How likely were you to be engaged throughout the stream?** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the option that best describes your level of engagement throughout the stream. Please select one of the options: 1 - Not at all, 2 - A little, 3 - Somewhat, 4 - Very, 5 - Extremely.
* **Did you enjoy the live stream?** (`feedback_question_2`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the option that best describes your level of enjoyment for the live stream. Please select one of the options: 1 - Not at all, 2 - A little, 3 - Somewhat, 4 - Very, 5 - Extremely.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the live stream, please share them here. This is an optional but helpful section for us to get a better understanding of your experience.
* **Your email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if we need further information.
* **Your phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name so we can address you properly.
