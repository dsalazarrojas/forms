# leadership_network_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "leadership_network_feedback" form is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions about the leadership network. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and provide feedback on the questions that apply to you.
2. For each question, provide a clear and concise response in the space provided.
3. Skip questions that don't apply to you, as you're not required to answer every question.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional comments" field to share them.
5. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is an introduction to the feedback form. It's not a required field and just a way to get you started.
* **How was your experience with our leadership network?**: We want to know about your personal experience with our leadership network. This is your chance to share any positive or negative experiences you've had.
* **How would you rate the communication of our leadership network?**: Rate your experience with the communication from our leadership network. Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Have you attended any events hosted by our network?**: Check all the events you've attended, separated by commas. For example, "True, True, False"
* **Additional comments**: Use this field to share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have. You can be as detailed as you'd like.
* **Email address**: If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address. We'll only use it for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone number**: If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your phone number. We'll only use it for follow-up purposes.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click this button to submit your feedback.
