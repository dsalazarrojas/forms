# # Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer To Peer Relationship Survey is designed to collect information about relationships between two individuals. It helps in evaluating the nature, quality, and dynamics of the relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and select the response that best describes your relationship with the person in question.
2.  Answer all questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3.  Select the options that best fit your experiences and feelings about the relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey** (`survey_header`, `note`): This is a title and introduction to the form. Read through the questions carefully and understand what each one aims to assess.
*   **Do you currently have a relationship with this person?** (`relationship_type`, `select_one`): This question seeks to identify the nature of the relationship, such as friend, family member, or colleague.
*   **How long have you known them?** (`how_long`, `select_multiple`): Choose the duration of the relationship from the provided options.
*   **How often do you contact them?** (`frequency_of_contact`, `select_one`): Indicate the frequency of your interactions with the person.
*   **What method of communication do you use to stay in touch?** (`communication_method`, `select_multiple`): Select all applicable methods of communication you use with the person.
*   **How often do you experience conflicts with them?** (`conflict_frequency`, `select_one`): Answer this question to gauge the frequency of conflicts in your relationship.
*   **What type of conflicts do you have with them?** (`conflict_topic`, `select_multiple`): Identify the types of conflicts that occur in your relationship.
*   **How do you usually resolve conflicts with them?** (`conflict_resolution`, `select_one`): Describe your typical approach to resolving conflicts with the person.
*   **How often do you support them?** (`support_frequency`, `select_one`): Assess the frequency of your support for the person.
*   **What type of support do you give them?** (`support_type`, `select_multiple`): Indicate the types of support you offer.
*   **What level of support do you give them?** (`support_level`, `select_one`): Rate the level of support you provide.
*   **What level of emotional attachment do you have towards them?** (`emotional_level`, `select_one`): Measure your emotional connection with the person.
*   **What level of trust do you have towards them?** (`trust_level`, `select_one`): Indicate your level of trust in the person.
*   **What level of intimacy do you have with them?** (`intimacy_level`, `select_one`): Assess your closeness with the person.
*   **How satisfied are you with your relationship with them?** (`relationship_satisfaction`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the relationship.
*   **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your relationship with them?** (`overall_impact`, `select_one`): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the relationship.
*   **How would you rate the quality of your relationship with them?** (`relationship_quality`, `select_one`): Measure the quality of the relationship.
*   **How important is this relationship to you?** (`relationship_importance`, `select_one`): Rate the importance of the relationship.
*   **What stage are you and this person currently in?** (`relationship_stages`, `select_multiple`): Identify the current stage of the relationship.
*   **What is the status of your relationship with them?** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`): Specify the status of the relationship.
*   **How long have you been in a relationship with them?** (`relationship_length`, `select_multiple`): Choose the duration of the relationship.
*   **What stage of the relationship are you currently in?** (`relationship_stage`, `select_multiple`): Determine your current position in the relationship.
*   **What is your current communication style with this person?** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`): Indicate the communication style you use with the person.
*   **How often do you communicate with this person?** (`communication_frequencies`, `select_one`): Indicate the frequency of your communication with the person.
*   **How satisfied are you overall with your relationship with them?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the relationship.

By following these instructions and understanding each field's purpose, you will be able to provide accurate and insightful data about your relationship with the individual. Remember to answer honestly and select the options that best fit your experiences and feelings about the relationship.
