# Store Feedback Collection Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and comments from customers or visitors about their visit to the store. It will help the store management team understand customer experiences, identify areas of improvement, and make necessary adjustments to enhance the store's services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with as much or as little information as you'd like.
2.  Be as specific as possible when describing your visit and providing feedback.
3.  If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
4.  If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_form_data`): This field is for providing any additional information about your visit or feedback. Please describe your experience and any details you'd like to share.
* **Visit Details** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_visit_details`): This field is for providing information about your visit, such as date and time. Please use YYYY-MM-DD format for the date and 24h format (00:00 - 23:59) for the time.
* **Rate Our Store** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_rated_1` and `store_feedback_collection_contact_form_rated_2`): These fields are for rating your overall experience at the store. Please select one of the five rating options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Terrible.
* **Comments** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_comments`): This field is for providing more comments or suggestions about your visit.
* **Phone** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_phone`): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_email`): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please enter your email address.
* **Date** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_date`): This field is for providing the date of your visit. Please use YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_time`): This field is for providing the time of your visit. Please use 24h format (00:00 - 23:59).
* **Comments** (`store_feedback_collection_contact_form_comments`): This field is for providing more comments or suggestions about your visit.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback and help us improve our services!
