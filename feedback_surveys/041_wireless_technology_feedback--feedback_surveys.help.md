# Wireless Technology Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on wireless technology services, including ratings and suggestions for improvement. Your input will help the wireless service provider identify areas of improvement and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Rate the wireless service (optional)
2. Describe any issues or problems you've encountered
3. Suggest ways to improve the service
4. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your name and email address

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wireless Service Rating** (`wireless_service_rating`, number, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to rate the quality of the wireless service. Please rate it on a scale of 0-10, with 0 being poor service and 10 being excellent service.
* **Issues or Problems Encountered** (`issues_issuer`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any issues or problems you've had with the wireless service. This will help the provider understand what they need to improve.
* **Description of Issues** (`issue_description`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem you've had. This will help the provider understand the context of your issue.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`wireless_suggestions`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have any suggestions on how to improve the wireless service, please enter them here.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your name here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or for more information on the improvements to the service. Please enter a valid email address.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible in your feedback, so the provider can understand your concerns and make necessary improvements.
* If you're unsure about anything, just let us know and we'll be happy to help.
