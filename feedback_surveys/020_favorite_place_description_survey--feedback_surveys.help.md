# Favorite Place Description Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your favorite place, including what makes it special, how often you visit, and your preferences for engaging with it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing honest answers to each question.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them in the text fields.
