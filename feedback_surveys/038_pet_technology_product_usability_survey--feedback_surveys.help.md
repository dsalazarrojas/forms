# Pet Technology Product Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions and feedback on the usability of Pet Technology (PetTech) products. Your input will help us understand what you like and dislike about our products and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by providing answers to the questions and selecting the options that best match your thoughts and opinions.
- Answer each question honestly, and feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like.
- You can select multiple features for the question about PetTech product usability features.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **pet_company** (`pet_company`, text, optional): This is your company name, which will help us understand your association with PetTech.
- **pet_company_website** (`pet_company_website`, text, optional): If you have a website for your company, please provide the URL.
- **pet_usability_features** (`pet_usability_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the features that you think are most important for PetTech product usability. You can choose from:
  - PetTech product features
  - User interface clarity
  - Navigation efficiency
  - Customizability
  - Support
  - User feedback
- **pet_usability_survey_satisfaction** (`pet_usability_survey_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the PetTech product usability? You can select from:
  - Very satisfied
  - Somewhat satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Somewhat dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied
- **pet_usability_survey_feature_ideas** (`pet_usability_survey_feature_ideas`, text, optional): Please share any feature ideas you think would enhance the PetTech product usability.
- **pet_product_improvement_ideas** (`pet_product_improvement_ideas`, text, optional): If you could suggest improvements to the existing PetTech products, please type them here.
- **pet_survey_completion** (`pet_survey_completion`, select_one, optional): Would you be willing to participate in future surveys about PetTech products?
  - 'True'
  - 'False'
- **pet_survey_completion_reason** (`pet_survey_completion_reason`, text, optional): If you selected 'False', please explain why.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as you'd like.
- Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve PetTech products.
- You can exit the form at any time and save your progress if needed.
