# Individual Impact On Marine Environment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about the impact of human activities on the marine environment. It aims to understand the respondents' habits, marine environmental awareness, and suggestions for sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the most relevant options for the questions about your ocean habits, such as diving, swimming, surfing, or fishing/aquaculture.
2. Provide your level of marine environmental awareness on a scale from 0 to 10.
3. Choose one of the provided sustainability ideas that you think is most relevant.
4. (Optional) If you have additional comments or insights about the marine environment, you can provide them here.
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ocean Habits** (select multiple):
	+ This field asks about your activities related to the ocean, such as diving, swimming, surfing, or fishing/aquaculture.
* **Marine Environmental Awareness** (number):
	+ This field measures your level of awareness about the marine environment. Please respond on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Sustainability Ideas** (select one):
	+ This field allows you to suggest one way to improve the sustainability of our marine environment.
* **Marine Impact** (date):
	+ This field is not currently used in the form and is optional. It seems to be a field for future implementation.
* **Marine Impact Awareness** (number):
	+ This field is also not currently used in the form and is optional.
* **Marine Habits Frequency** (select one):
	+ This field asks about how often you engage in ocean-related activities.
* **Marine Environmental Protection** (select one):
	+ This field measures your level of agreement with marine environmental protection.
* **Marine Habits Other** (text):
	+ If you have any other ocean-related activities not listed in the previous field, you can describe them here.
* **Feedback** (text):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback about the marine environment.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text):
	+ Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Other Feedback** (text):
	+ This field is similar to the feedback field and is also not necessary for most users.
* **Email 2**, **Phone Number 2**, and **Other Feedback 2**, ..., **Email 9** and **Phone Number 9, **Other Feedback 9**:
	+ These fields seem to be duplicates of the previous fields and are not currently used in the form. Please ignore them.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at [insert contact information].
* This form is designed to be completed by one person at a time. If you are filling this form as part of a group, please ensure each person completes their own instance.
