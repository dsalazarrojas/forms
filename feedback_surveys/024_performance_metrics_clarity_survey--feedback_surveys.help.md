## Performance Metrics Clarity Survey - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the text fields (1, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11) with a brief description or response. These fields are used to gather more detailed information about your thoughts and opinions.
2. For the select fields (2, 5, 7), choose one of the provided options (Very clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear at all, True, False, Not make an impact, Somewhat make an impact, Make a significant impact).
3. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

- **What do you know about the performance metrics and targets?** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is asking you to describe what you understand about performance metrics and targets. Please provide a brief answer that reflects your current knowledge.
- **How clearly do you understand the performance metrics and targets?** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options: Very clear, Somewhat clear, or Not clear at all, to indicate your level of understanding.
- **What impact will you make on the organization if you don't understand the performance metrics and targets?** (`3`, `text`, optional): If you think about the consequences of not understanding performance metrics and targets, write a brief description of your thoughts.
- **Can you think of any feedback or suggestions about performance metrics and targets?** (`4`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas you have about how to improve performance metrics and targets.
- **Do you understand the performance metrics and targets?** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options: True or False, to confirm your understanding.
- **How will the performance metrics and targets affect your work?** (`6`, `text`, required): Describe how you think the performance metrics and targets will influence your work.
- **How do you see the impact on the organization if you don't understand the performance metrics and targets?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the options that apply to you: Not make an impact, Somewhat make an impact, or Make a significant impact.

- **What are performance metrics and targets?** (`11`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of what you understand by performance metrics and targets.
