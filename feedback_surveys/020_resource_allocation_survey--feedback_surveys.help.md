# Resource Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Allocation Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from users about their resource access, availability, and distribution within the organization. This form aims to identify areas for improvement and help optimize resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select one answer for each question from the available options.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing answers, especially in the note fields.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Take your time to complete the form, as it is designed to be user-friendly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many resources do you currently have access to? (`resources_access`)** (`1-5`, `6-10`, `11-15`, `More than 15`): Please select the range that best describes the number of resources you currently have access to.
* **How many hours do you have available per week? (`hours_available`)** (`Less than 10 hours`, `10-20 hours`, `21-30 hours`, `More than 30 hours`): Choose the option that represents the average number of hours you have available per week.
* **Do you think resource distribution is working well? (`resource_distribution`)** (`Yes`, `No`, `Undecided`): Select one of the provided options to express your opinion on resource distribution.
* **Can you access all necessary resources? (`resource_availability`)** (`Yes`, `No`): Choose whether you can access all necessary resources.
* **Are there any additional resources you'd like to request? (`additional_resources`)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional resources you would like to request.
* **What is your role in the organization? (`role`)** (`Team leader`, `Team member`, `Other`): Select the option that best describes your role within the organization.
* **What are your thoughts on resource distribution? (`thoughts_on_resource_distribution`)**: Please provide any thoughts or suggestions you have about resource distribution.
* **How do you currently use your available hours? (`available_hours_use`)** (`Working on core tasks`, `Collaborating with team members`, `Learning/Professional development`, `Other`): Choose how you currently use your available hours.
* **Do you feel you have the necessary skills to perform your job? (`skills_to_perform_job`)** (`Yes`, `No`): Select whether you feel you have the necessary skills to perform your job.
* **What are some challenges you face when accessing resources? (`resource_accessing_challenges`)**: This is a text field where you can provide any challenges you face when accessing resources.
* **How do you think resource distribution could be improved? (`improve_resource_distribution`)**: Please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving resource distribution.
* **Are there any other comments or suggestions you'd like to share? (`additional_comments`)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
