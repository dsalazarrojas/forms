# NICU Resuscitation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from staff members about the resuscitation process in the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU). The goal is to collect information on the resuscitation method used, any feedback about the care team, and overall ratings of the experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Fill out the form completely, including selecting options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your feedback and comments in the text fields.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`title`, `text`, not required):
This field is a brief introduction to the form.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required):
Select the type of event you are providing feedback on. Please choose from the available options, or select "None" if it is not applicable.
* **Resuscitation Method** (`resuscitation_method`, select_multiple, not required):
Select all methods that were used during the resuscitation process. Please choose from the available options or select "Other" if none of the listed methods were used.
* **Feedback on Resuscitation** (`feedback_resuscitation`, text, required):
Enter your feedback on the resuscitation process. This is an opportunity to provide specific comments or suggestions about the care team's performance.
* **Feedback on NICU Care Team** (`feedback_nicu_care_team`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the care team's performance or actions during the resuscitation process.
* **Feedback on QI Staff** (`feedback_qi_staff`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the Quality Improvement (QI) staff's performance or actions during the resuscitation process.
* **Feedback on Facility** (`feedback_facility`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the facility's performance or environment during the resuscitation process.
* **Feedback on NICU Care Team Training** (`feedback_nicu_care_team_training`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the NICU care team's training or education received during the resuscitation process.
* **Feedback on QI Care Team Training** (`feedback_qi_care_team_training`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the QI care team's training or education received during the resuscitation process.
* **Feedback on Facility** (`feedback_facility`, text, not required):
Enter any feedback about the facility's performance or environment during the resuscitation process.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, not required):
Rate your overall experience with the resuscitation process, selecting from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required):
Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the experience.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required):
Click the "Submit" button to finalize your feedback submission.
