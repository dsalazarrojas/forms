# Animal Rescue Infrastructure Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from animal rescue personnel on the condition and infrastructure of animal shelters. The goal of this survey is to identify areas of improvement and provide suggestions for budget prioritization. Your input will help us better understand the needs of these facilities and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate option for each field, or type in your response if there is no selection available.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially in the "Description of Identified Safety Hazards" field.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification or leave a brief note in the field for further explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Facility or Shelter Name (<name>, <type>: text, required)
This field asks for the name of the animal shelter or facility you are providing feedback on.
### Your Role at this Facility (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Please select the role you play at the facility, such as "Shelter Manager", "Veterinarian", or "Volunteer".
### Condition of Kennels and Enclosures (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Select one of the options:
* Excellent - No repairs needed
* Good - Minor cosmetic issues
* Fair - Functional but needs repair
* Poor - Immediate safety concern
### Ventilation and Air Quality Rating (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Select one of the options:
* Good - Well ventilated
* Acceptable - Average
* Poor - Needs improvement
### Lighting and Natural Light Access (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Select one of the options:
* Good - Sufficient light
* Acceptable - Functional
* Poor - Inadequate light
### Adequacy of Medical Treatment Areas (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Select one of the options:
* Sufficient - Meets all needs
* Insufficient - Too small or lacking
* Not Applicable - No medical area
### Facility Cleanliness and Sanitation Levels (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
Select one of the options:
* Always Clean - Highly maintained
* Mostly Clean - Standard maintenance
* Often Dirty - Substandard
### Description of Identified Safety Hazards (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Please describe any safety hazards you have identified, such as exposed wires or broken fences.
### What is the Most Urgent Infrastructure Need? (<name>, <type>: text, required)
Please select the most pressing infrastructure need you have identified.
### Suggestions for Budget Prioritization (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
Share any suggestions for budget prioritization, such as repairing a specific area or upgrading equipment.
