# love_sync_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "love_sync_feedback_survey" form is a feedback and rating survey used to collect information about the user's experience with the product or service. This form is designed to help the product team understand user satisfaction, feature preferences, and improvement ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the required questions and select all applicable options for each question.
2.  Fill in any additional comments or text fields with your thoughts and suggestions.
3.  Submit the form to send your feedback to the product team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Satisfaction**: (`user_satisfaction`, number, optional)
  This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service. Answer with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Feature Preferences**: (`feature_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select all the features you would like to see in future updates or versions of the product or service. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Improvement Ideas**: (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional)
  Share any ideas you have for improving the product or service. This could be suggestions for new features or ways to make the product more user-friendly.
* **User Satisfaction Rating**: (`user_satisfaction_rating`, number, optional)
  This question asks for a numerical rating of your overall satisfaction with the product or service. Answer with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Feedback Message**: (`feedback_message`, text, optional)
  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them here.
* **Feature Requests**: (`feature_requests`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select all the features you would like to see in future updates or versions of the product or service. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Improvement Ideas Rating**: (`improvement_ideas_rating`, number, optional)
  This question asks for a numerical rating of the quality of your improvement ideas. Answer with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Love Sync Features**: (`love_sync_features`, select_one, optional)
  Select the feature from the provided options that you think would be most valuable or useful to you. You can only choose one option.
* **User Preferences**: (`user_preferences`, text, optional)
  Share any preferences you have for the product or service, such as specific settings or options you would like to see added.
* **Love Sync Features Ratings**: (`love_sync_features_ratings`, number, optional)
  This question asks for a numerical rating of the selected feature from the "Love Sync Features" question. Answer with a numerical value between 1 and 10.
