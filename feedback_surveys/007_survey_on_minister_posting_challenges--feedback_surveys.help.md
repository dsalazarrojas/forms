# Survey On Minister Posting Challenges - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from ministers about their posting experiences, challenges, and feedback. The purpose of this form is to gather insights and identify areas for improvement in the minister posting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with the "Introduction" page, where you will find a brief overview of the form.
2. On the "Posting Experiences" page, select the number of posting experiences you have had.
3. On the "Challenges" page, select all the challenges you have faced during the posting process.
4. On the "Needed Support" page, select the type of support you needed during the posting process.
5. On the "Posting Experiences 2" page, describe your posting experiences in more detail.
6. On the "Comments" page, add any additional comments about your posting experiences.
7. On the "Date of Posting" page, enter the date of your posting (if applicable).
8. On the "Time of Posting" page, enter the time of your posting (if applicable).
9. On the "Email" page, enter your email address (required for follow-up contact).
10. On the "Phone" page, enter your phone number (optional).
11. On the "Additional Comments" page, add any additional comments about your posting experience.
12. On the "Onboarding Decision" page, select your decision regarding onboarding.
13. On the "Onboarding Plan" page, describe your onboarding plan.
14. On the "Follow-up Action" page, suggest any follow-up actions that can be taken.
15. On the "Assigned Staff" page, select the staff member to be assigned to your posting.
16. On the "Posting Status" page, select the status of your posting.
17. On the "Additional Posting Info" page, add any additional information about your posting.
18. On the "Posting Experience" page, describe your posting experience.
19. On the "Comments on Staff" page, add any comments about the staff member assigned to your posting.
20. On the "Follow-up Status" page, select the status of your follow-up action.
21. On the "Follow-up Comments" page, add any additional comments about your follow-up action.
22. On the "Next Steps" page, suggest any next steps to be taken.
23. On the "Follow-up Action Plan" page, describe your follow-up action plan.
24. On the "Follow-up Staff" page, select the staff member to be assigned to your follow-up action.
25. On the "Follow-up Status" page, select the status of your follow-up action.
26. On the "Follow-up Comments" page, add any additional comments about your follow-up action.
27. On the "Last Page" page, add any final comments or observations about your posting experience.
