# gearbox_design_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gearbox_design_feedback_form is a form used to collect feedback on the design quality of a gearbox. This form is intended for internal use to gather data on the design's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the design's overall quality by selecting a rating on the scale provided.
2. Provide any comments or thoughts you have about the design.
3. If you've conducted a trial with the design, select the outcome of the trial.
4. If you have any suggestions for continuous improvement, type them in the text field.
5. If you want to attach any relevant files or documents, you can do so using the Attachment field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
7. If you've completed the trial, provide the date and time it was finished.
8. Select all the rating scales that apply to the design's quality.
9. Add any notes from the trial.
10. If you'd like to rate the trial outcome, provide a number from 1 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Design Rating**: Rate the design's overall quality on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments**: Share any thoughts or comments about the design.
* **Trial Outcome**: Select the outcome of the trial, either Good or Poor.
* **Continuous Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improving the design, type them here.
* **Attachment**: If you want to attach any relevant files or documents, click here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date Completed**: If you've completed the trial, enter the date it was finished.
* **Time Completed**: If you've completed the trial, enter the time it was finished.
* **Rating Scale**: Select all the rating scales that apply to the design's quality.
* **Trial Notes**: Add any notes from the trial.
* **Trial Rating**: Rate the trial outcome from 1 to 5.
* **Trial Outcome Rating**: Rate the trial outcome from 1 to 5.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and ratings.
- If you've attached files, make sure they are relevant to the design being reviewed.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us at your email address provided.
