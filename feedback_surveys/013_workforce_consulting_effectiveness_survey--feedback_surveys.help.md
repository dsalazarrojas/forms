# Workforce Consulting Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the workforce consulting services provided by a company. The goal is to understand how the services are perceived by employees who have interacted with the team in the past month.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant feedback.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the options provided for select questions, or type in your own response if no option matches your answer.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise comments.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the HR department for assistance.
