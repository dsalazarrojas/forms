# Sales Strategy Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales strategy feedback survey form is designed to collect feedback from sales team members on their current sales strategy. It aims to gather insights on which sales channels are considered important or not by the team members, as well as areas of focus for the sales strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the importance of various sales channels (e.g. sales, marketing, etc.)
2. Identify the areas that you consider important or less important for the sales strategy
3. Provide demographic information (email address, etc.) if required

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sales_strategy_survey_form (Text, text, Not Required)**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a short description of your thoughts on the current sales strategy.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_2 (Text, select_one, Not Required)**: Please select the sales channels that you consider important or not important. Choose "Very Important" if you consider the channel very important, "Somewhat Important" if it's somewhat important, or "Not Very Important" if it's not very important.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_3 (Text, select_multiple, Not Required)**: Choose the sales channels that you would focus on if you were in charge of sales strategy. You can select multiple channels.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_4 (Text, date, Not Required)**: This is a date picker field. You can enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_5 (Text, time, Not Required)**: This is a time picker field. You can enter a time in the format HH:MM.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_6 (Text, note, Not Required)**: This is a text area for any additional comments you have.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_7 (Text, number, Not Required)**: Please enter a number that represents your years of experience in sales.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_8 (Text, number, Not Required)**: Please enter a number that represents your current sales performance rating (out of 10).
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_9 (Text, text, Not Required)**: Please enter your thoughts on what you think is missing from the current sales strategy.
* **sales_strategy_survey_form_10 (Text, email, Not Required)**: Please enter your email address (if you are willing to share it).

## Tips
Please be honest and provide genuine feedback. Each field is designed to be simple and easy to fill out. You can always skip fields that you feel are not relevant or do not apply to you. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
