# Transformative Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect transformative feedback from users, which is feedback that has the potential to bring about significant and positive change. The goal is to ensure that users feel heard and their feedback is taken seriously.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please take a moment to answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Start on **Page 1** and provide your **full_name** and **email** address.
3. On **Page 2**, provide your **comments** about your experience.
4. On **Page 3**, select your **how_did_you_feel** options that best describe your overall satisfaction.
5. On **Page 4**, add any additional **suggestions** you may have.
6. On **Page 5**, provide your **contact_info** if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **full_name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name.
* **email** (`email`, text, required): Please provide your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **how_did_you_feel** (`how_did_you_feel`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options that best describe your overall satisfaction.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required): Please provide your feedback and comments.
* **suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide more information about how to contact you.
* **contact_info_2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Add more contact information if needed.
* **contact_info_3** (`contact_info_3`, text, optional): Add even more contact information if needed.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have more to say, please add it in the **suggestions** or **comments** fields.
* If you have more contact information, please provide it in the **contact_info** fields.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
