# Floor Film Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floor Film Opinion Survey is a form designed to collect opinions and preferences from respondents on various aspects of floor film products and services. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and develop new products and services that meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the category that best represents your role or department:
	* Floor Film
	* Print Providers
	* Facilities Manager
2. Enter your product ID number (if applicable).
3. Describe how you use floor film products and services.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the current state of floor film products and services (1-5).
5. Select all that apply for your key preferences:
	* Product teams
	* Print providers
	* Facilities managers
6. Enter the number of hours you use floor film products and services per week.
7. Enter the number of days you use floor film products and services per week.
8. Describe your preferences for floor film products and services.
9. Indicate if you have:
	* Yes or No: Facilities
	* Yes or No: Print providers
10. Indicate if you have:
	* Yes or No: Facilities manager
11. Select all that apply for your product team:
	* Yes or No

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Floor Film Opinion Survey Form**:
	+ A text input field where you can enter any additional comments or observations about floor film products and services.
* **Category**:
	+ Please select the category that best represents your role or department. This will help us understand who is using floor film products and services and what their needs are.
* **Product ID**:
	+ Enter your product ID number if you have one. This will help us track your specific product usage and preferences.
* **Usage Patterns**:
	+ Describe how you use floor film products and services. This will help us understand your usage habits and preferences.
* **Satisfaction**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the current state of floor film products and services using a scale of 1-5 (1-5).
* **Key Preferences**:
	+ Select all that apply for your key preferences regarding floor film products and services.
* **Usage Hours**:
	+ Enter the number of hours you use floor film products and services per week.
* **Usage Days**:
	+ Enter the number of days you use floor film products and services per week.
* **Preferences**:
	+ Describe your preferences for floor film products and services.
* **Facilities**:
	+ Indicate if you have facilities that use floor film products and services. 
* **Print Providers**:
	+ Indicate if you have print providers that use floor film products and services.
* **Facilities Manager**:
	+ Indicate if you have a facilities manager that use floor film products and services.
* **Product Team**:
	+ Select all that apply for your product team that use floor film products and services.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please describe your thoughts and we will do our best to help clarify.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Floor Film Opinion Survey Form" text input field.
