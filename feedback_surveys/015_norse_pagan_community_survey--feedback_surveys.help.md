# Norse Pagan Community Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Norse Pagan Community Survey is designed to gather information from individuals involved in Norse Pagan practices. The data collected will help us understand the diversity of the community, its interests, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you understand each question before answering.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best fit your situation from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Intro** (`form_intro`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Pagan Practices** (`pagan_practices`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your primary Pagan practice. Select one of the options that best represents your primary practice:
	+ Asatru: A Germanic Paganism practice inspired by pre-Christian northern European cultures.
	+ Wicca: A modern Pagan practice based on the Witchcraft of 18th century Europe.
	+ Heathen: A modern Pagan practice based on the pre-Christian cultures of northern Europe.
	+ Druidry: A modern Pagan practice based on Celtic Druidry and other European cultures.
	+ Feri: A modern Pagan practice based on Feri tradition.
	+ Other (please describe): If none of the above options fit your practice, describe it here.
* **How often do you practice?** (`practice_frequency`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about how often you engage in Pagan practices:
	+ Daily: You practice Pagan rituals and activities every day.
	+ Several times a week: You practice Pagan rituals and activities several times a week.
	+ Weekly: You practice Pagan rituals and activities once a week.
	+ Monthly: You practice Pagan rituals and activities once a month.
	+ Rarely: You practice Pagan rituals and activities very rarely.
	+ Never: You do not practice Pagan rituals and activities.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your demographics:
	+ Age: Select your age group.
	+ Sex: Select your sex.
	+ Orientation: Select your orientation.
	+ Race: Select your racial background.
	+ Ethnicity: Select your ethnic background.
* **Community Size** (`community_size`, number, required: false): This question asks about the size of the community you are part of:
	+ Enter the number of people in your community.
* **Group Type** (`group_type`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the type of group you are part of:
	+ Local group: A local group with members from the same area.
	+ Regional group: A regional group with members from different areas.
	+ Local group + Local group: Multiple local groups.
	+ Local group + Regional group: Multiple local and regional groups.
	+ None: You do not belong to any group.
* **Interests** (`pagan_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your interests:
	+ Community service: Providing services to the community.
	+ Education: Learning about Paganism and related topics.
	+ Artisan skills: Practicing artisanal skills such as crafting and art.
	+ Ritual and magic: Practicing Pagan rituals and magic.
	+ Music: Engaging in Pagan-related music activities.
	+ Other: Select other interests that are not listed.
* **Resources** (`needs_resources`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the resources you would like to see:
	+ Community space: A physical space for Pagan community events and activities.
	+ Events: Organized events for Pagan community members.
	+ Community outreach: Outreach to other communities and groups.
	+ Online support: Online resources and support for Pagan community members.
	+ Other: Select other resources you would like to see.
* **Form Outro** (`form_outro`, note, required: false): This is a final note to complete the form.
