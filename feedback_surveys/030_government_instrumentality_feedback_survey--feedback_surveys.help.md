# Government Instrumentality Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their experiences with government instrumentalities, such as agencies or departments that provide services to the public. This feedback will help the instrumentalities to improve their services and better serve the public.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the services provided by the government instrumentalities that you have experience with.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the services provided.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrumentality Survey** (`government_instrumentality_survey_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can provide feedback about the government instrumentalities you have experience with. Please select all the instrumentalities that apply.
* **Rating** (`rating_1`): Rate your satisfaction with the services provided by the instrumentalities you selected. Please select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Agency Service** (`service_1`): Select one agency providing service. This is used to help the instrumentality understand which agency you are referring to.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the services provided. Please select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number. This is optional and will help the instrumentality contact you if needed.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the services provided. This is optional but will help the instrumentality understand your concerns or suggestions.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date in dd/mm/yy format for the feedback you are providing.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address. This is optional and will help the instrumentality contact you if needed.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Select the follow-up period for your feedback. This is optional and will help the instrumentality know when to follow-up with you.
* **Prophets** (`prophets_1`): Rate the prophets or effectiveness of the services provided. Please select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`): Select the reporting period for your feedback. This is optional and will help the instrumentality understand when the feedback was provided.
* **Prophets (2)** (`prophets`): Rate the second part of the prophets or effectiveness of the services provided. Please select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments 1** (`additional_comments_1`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the services provided. This is optional but will help the instrumentality understand your concerns or suggestions.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the services provided. This is optional but will help the instrumentality understand your concerns or suggestions.

Note: Some fields have hints or help text to guide you in filling out the form.
