# Cost Efficiency Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights on cost efficiency strategies from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides more information on the purpose and scope of this form.
2. For each page, read the instructions and questions carefully before providing your input.
3. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, review your input for accuracy and completeness.
5. Click submit to send your feedback and insights to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Note): This page provides an overview of the purpose and scope of this form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Operational Costs
* **Operational costs** (Number): This field is used to collect information about the operational costs you have encountered. Please enter a number that represents the amount.

### Strategies to Implement
* **Strategies to implement** (Select multiple): This field allows you to select multiple strategies that you plan or have implemented to optimize operational costs. Please choose from the options provided.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have that are not included in the previous questions. Please use this field to provide any additional information that you think is relevant to this form.

### Email
* **Email** (Email): This field is for your email address, which will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to send you a summary of your feedback. Please enter a valid email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Text): This field is for your phone number, which will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback. Please enter a valid phone number.

### File Upload
* **File upload** (Text): This field is for uploading any supporting files or documents that you think are relevant to your feedback. Please use a file type that is supported by our system.

### Date
* **Date** (Date): This field is for the date of your feedback. Please enter a date in the format MM/DD/YY.

### Time
* **Time** (Time): This field is for the time of your feedback. Please enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Category
* **Category** (Text): This field is for any additional category or sub-category that is not already provided. Please enter a category that is relevant to your feedback.

### Sub Category 1 to 13
* **Sub category 1 to 13** (Text): These fields are for providing sub-categories or additional information for each strategy you selected. Please enter as much detail as possible.

Note: The sub-category fields are only for providing additional information and not required fields.
