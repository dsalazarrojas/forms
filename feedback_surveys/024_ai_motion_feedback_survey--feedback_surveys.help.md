# AI Motion Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Motion Feedback Survey is designed to gather your insights on the performance of our AI Motion system during your session. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the system's accuracy, ease of use, and overall usefulness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on each page to navigate through the form.
2. For each field, provide the requested information. If a field is required, please fill it in; if not, you can skip it if you like.
3. Use the provided hints to guide your responses.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Information** (`--session_information--`, `note`, required: false): This page is a brief introduction to the feedback survey. Please take a moment to review the information before proceeding.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to provide your name for feedback purposes, enter it here. This is optional, and you can skip it if you prefer.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of your trial or session. This is a required field to help us match your feedback with your session.
* **Activity type** (`activity_type`, `text`, required: true): Describe the type of activity you performed during the session, such as running, lifting, or yoga.
* **Perceived accuracy of motion feedback** (`perceived_accuracy`, `number`, required: true): Rate the accuracy of the motion feedback you received during the session on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Ease of use of system** (`ease_of_use`, `number`, required: true): Rate the ease of use of the system on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Usefulness of feedback for improvement** (`usefulness`, `number`, required: true): Rate the usefulness of the feedback you received for improvement on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Any bugs or issues observed** (`bugs_or_issues`, `text`, required: false): If you encountered any issues or bugs during the session, describe them briefly in this field. This is optional.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any ideas or suggestions for improvement, enter them here. This is optional.
* **Consent to share anonymized data for research** (`consent_to_share`, `select_one`, required: true): Select whether you consent to share your anonymized data for research purposes. Please select 'True' if you are willing to share, and 'False' otherwise.
