# Community Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Community Needs Survey is designed to collect information about the needs and priorities of the community. This form is meant to gather feedback from community members to better understand their needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the **Respondent location** field to indicate the neighborhood or postal code where you reside.
2. In the **Top needs in your household**, select up to three needs that you currently face. These options include:
	* Food
	* Housing
	* Healthcare
	* Employment
	* Education
3. For the **Difficulty accessing services**, rate the level of difficulty you face in accessing services. This can include services such as food, housing, or healthcare.
4. If applicable, select the **Priority areas for community support** that you believe are the top priorities for your community. These options include:
	* Food security
	* Affordable housing
	* Health services
	* Job training
	* Childcare
5. Choose your availability for programs, selecting one of the following options:
	* Weekdays
	* Evenings
	* Weekends
	* Flexible
6. In the **Preferred communication channel**, select how you would like to receive information. This can include:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Community bulletin
	* Social media
7. If you are willing to participate in programs, please select **True**.
8. Finally, provide your **Contact email for follow up** (if applicable) and any additional information in the **Additional information** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent location**: This field asks you to indicate the neighborhood or postal code where you reside.
* **Top needs in your household**: Select up to three needs that you currently face in your household.
* **Difficulty accessing services**: Rate the level of difficulty you face in accessing services.
* **Priority areas for community support**: Select the top priorities for your community support.
* **Availability for programs**: Choose your availability for programs.
* **Preferred communication channel**: Select how you would like to receive information.
* **Willing to participate in programs**: If you are willing to participate, select **True**.
* **Contact email for follow up**: Provide your email address for follow up (if applicable).
* **Additional information**: Provide any other context or notes you would like to share.
