# Art For Social Change Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used to measure the social impact of Art For Social Change projects, focusing on awareness increase, community connection, skill development, and overall project quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For questions with multiple options, select the most appropriate answer for your experience.
3. If no option accurately represents your experience, please select 'Other' and provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of the Art for Social Change Project* (Project Title):** Select the title of the project you participated in from the provided list.
* **Project Location - Community**: Enter the community where the project took place.
* **To what extent did this project increase your awareness of the social issue addressed? (Awareness Increase):** Select the level of increase in awareness, where 1 represents 'Not at all' and 5 represents 'Significantly'.
* **Did you feel more connected to your community after participating? (Community Connection):** Select the level of connection to your community after the project.
* **What skills did you develop through this project? (Skill Development):** Select all the skills you developed through the project, such as Artistic Skills, Communication Skills, Leadership Skills, and others.
* **How did participation affect your emotional well-being? (Emotional Well-being Impact):** Provide a brief description of how participation affected your emotional well-being.
* **As a result of this project, do you intend to take further action on this social issue? (Behavior Change Intent):** Select the level of your intention to take further action on the social issue, where 'Yes - Definitely' represents a strong commitment.
* **How would you rate the quality of project facilitation? (Project Quality):** Select the rating for the quality of project facilitation.
* **How accessible was the project to everyone in the community? (Accessibility):** Select the level of accessibility for the project.
* **What was your single biggest takeaway from this project? (Key Takeaway):** Provide a brief description of your single biggest takeaway.
* **How can we make this project more impactful in the future? (Improvement Feedback):** Provide a brief description of how the project can be improved.
* **Date of Survey Completion:** Enter the date on which you completed this survey.

This guide will help you complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
