# customer_success_story_questionnaire_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather key insights and feedback from customers about their experience with our product/service. The goal is to understand what challenges they faced before using our solution, which features were most impactful, and how they measure their overall satisfaction and likelihood to recommend.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions in a way that best reflects your experience with our product/service. You can select from pre-defined options or type in your own answer if needed. Make sure to provide specific examples or details where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **How satisfied are you with our product/service overall?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`)
  This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction level with our product/service. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
- **What primary challenge were you facing before using our solution?** (`Inefficiency`, `High costs`, `Limited functionality`, `Data silos`, `No specific challenge`)
  This question aims to identify the main issue you were experiencing before using our solution. Please choose the challenge that best fits your situation.
- **Which of our features or aspects have been most impactful for you?** (Select all that apply: `Ease of use`, `Performance`, `Customer support`, `Integration capabilities`, `Reporting & analytics`)
  Here, you can select multiple features that have been particularly helpful or beneficial for you. This will help us understand which aspects of our product/service are most valuable to you.
- **What measurable results or improvements have you achieved?** (text field)
  This is an opportunity to provide specific examples or numbers of how our product/service has helped you. Please describe any tangible benefits, such as cost savings, time reduction, or process improvements.
- **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to others?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`)
  This question helps us gauge your enthusiasm for sharing our product/service with others. Please select your honest answer.
- **Would you like to share a quote or testimonial for your success story?** (text field)
  If you'd like to share a testimonial or a quote about your experience, please enter it in this field. This can be a powerful endorsement for our product/service.
