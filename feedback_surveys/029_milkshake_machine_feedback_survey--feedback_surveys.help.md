# Milkshake Machine Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the "Milkshake Machine". Your input will help us understand how we can improve the machine and provide better service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the "customer service" question, select all that apply.
3. For the "improvement" question, feel free to provide any thoughts on how we can improve the machine or our service.
4. If you have any additional comments, please use the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the machine's performance?** (`machine_performance`, number, optional)
	+ This question asks for your honest opinion about how well the machine performed during your use.
* **How would you rate the machine's quality?** (`machine_quality`, number, optional)
	+ This question is about the overall quality of the machine, including its durability and reliability.
* **How would you rate the customer service experience?** (`customer_service`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This question gives you the opportunity to rate our customer service, from Option 1 to Option 3.
* **How would you rate the quality of the milkshake?** (`product_quality`, number, optional)
	+ This question is about the taste, texture, and overall quality of the milkshake.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with this experience?** (`satisfaction`, number, optional)
	+ This question asks for your overall satisfaction with your experience, from 1 to 5.
* **What can be improved?** (`improvement`, text, optional)
	+ This question is a free-form text box where you can provide any thoughts on how we can improve the machine or our service.
* **Any other comments?** (`comments`, note, optional)
	+ This question allows you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Please enter a valid email address, so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ If you have a phone number, please enter it, so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
	+ Please enter your name, so we can address you personally in our follow-up.
* **Phone Number and Name**: Please ensure that the phone number you enter is your personal number, not a business number.
* This form is designed to be completed by customers who have used the machine, so please only fill out this form if you have had a real experience with the machine. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is designed to be filled out by end-users, not employees of the company.
