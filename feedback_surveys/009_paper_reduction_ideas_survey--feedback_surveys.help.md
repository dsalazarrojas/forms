# Paper Reduction Ideas Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect ideas and suggestions from employees on ways to reduce paper usage and improve office efficiency. It is intended to gather information on potential areas to focus on to reduce waste and increase productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2. Make sure to submit the form before leaving the page, as your ideas and suggestions will not be saved if you navigate away.
3. If you need help with any of the form fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, so we can address you in our response to your submission.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your department from the list, so we can route your idea to the relevant team.
* **Ideas** (`ideas`, `text`, required: false): Describe your idea for reducing paper usage.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional suggestions you have for improving office efficiency.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments you have about your submission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, so we can contact you for follow-up questions or clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you for follow-up questions or clarification.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click to submit your form.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us better understand and address your suggestions.
