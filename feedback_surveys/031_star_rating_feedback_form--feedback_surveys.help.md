# Star Rating Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Star Rating Feedback Form is a simple survey designed to gather users' opinions about their experience with our service. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make our service better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your rating for each of the experiences (1-5 stars) by selecting one of the options for `Star rating 1`, `Star rating 2`, and `Star rating 3`.
2. Enter your feedback in the `Feedback` field about your experience.
3. Add any additional feedback you have in the `Additional feedback` field.
4. If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, provide your `Contact E-mail` and `Your Name`.
5. Optionally, add a `Comment` about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Star Rating Form** (`star_rating_form`): Select one of the three experience ratings (e.g., Good, Fair).
- **Feedback** (`feedback`): Describe your overall experience in your own words.
- **Star rating 1** (`star_rating_1`): Rate the first experience with 1 to 5 stars (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
  *Note: This field is not required, so you can skip it if it's not relevant.*
- **Star rating 2** (`star_rating_2`): Rate the second experience with 1 to 5 stars (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
  *Note: This field is not required, so you can skip it if it's not relevant.*
- **Star rating 3** (`star_rating_3`): Rate the third experience with 1 to 5 stars (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
  *Note: This field is not required, so you can skip it if it's not relevant.*
- **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`): Share more details about your experience.
- **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
- **Your Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
- **Comment** (`comment`): Leave an optional comment about your experience.
- **Rating** (`rating`): Enter an average rating of 1-5 stars (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
  *Note: This field is not required, so you can skip it if it's not relevant.*
