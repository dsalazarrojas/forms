# BOD Demo Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback survey designed to gather information about your experience with the demo. It will help us understand your thoughts on the overall quality, content relevance, and other aspects of the demo. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our demo presentations and better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter the date of the demo in the "Date of Demo" field.
4. Describe the demo topic or product being demonstrated in the "Demo Topic or Product" field.
5. If you wish to, provide the name of the presenter.
6. Answer the questions regarding the overall quality, content relevance, presentation clarity, and other aspects of the demo.
7. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Your Name
Your Name (`attendee_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your full name for identification purposes.

### 2. Email Address
Email Address (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address to enable us to contact you regarding your feedback.

### 3. Date of Demo
Date of Demo (`demo_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the demo you attended.

### 4. Demo Topic or Product
Demo Topic or Product (`demo_topic`, text, required): Describe the demo topic or product being demonstrated.

### 5. Presenter Name
Presenter Name (`presenter_name`, text, optional): If you wish to acknowledge the presenter, please enter their name.

### 6. Overall Demo Quality
Overall Demo Quality (`overall_quality`, number, required): Rate the quality of the demo from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).

### 7. Relevance of Content to Your Needs
Relevance of Content to Your Needs (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Choose the level of relevance of the content to your needs:

* Highly relevant
* Relevant
* Somewhat relevant
* Not relevant
* Unsure

### 8. Clarity of Presentation
Clarity of Presentation (`presentation_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the presentation:

* Very clear
* Clear
* Somewhat clear
* Unclear
* Very unclear

### 9. Pacing of the Demo
Pacing of the Demo (`pacing`, select_one, optional): Rate the pacing of the demo:

* Too fast
* Just right
* Too slow
* Inconsistent

### 10. Quality of Visuals and Slides
Quality of Visuals and Slides (`visuals_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of visuals and slides:

* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### 11. Time Allocation
Time Allocation (`time_allocation`, select_one, optional): Rate how well time was allocated between different topics:

* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### 12. Quality of Q&A Session
Quality of Q&A Session (`q_and_a_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the Q&A session:

* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor
* No Q&A session

### 13. Key Learnings or Takeaways
Key Learnings or Takeaways (`key_learnings`, text, optional): Describe any key learnings or takeaways from the demo.

### 14. Areas for Improvement
Areas for Improvement (`improvements_needed`, text, optional): Describe any areas for improvement.

### 15. Likelihood of Purchase or Implementation
Likelihood of Purchase or Implementation (`likely_to_purchase`, select_one, optional): Rate your likelihood of purchase or implementation:

* Very Likely
* Likely
* Neutral
* Unlikely
* Very Unlikely

### 16. Would You Attend a Similar Demo?
Would You Attend a Similar Demo? (`would_attend_again`, select_one, optional): Rate how likely you are to attend a similar demo:

* Definitely Yes
* Probably Yes
* Unsure
* Probably No
* Definitely No

### 17. Additional Feedback
Additional Feedback (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
