# Local Attractions Tourist Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about your experience at local attractions. The information you provide will help us understand what you enjoyed or didn't enjoy about your visit and will aid us in making improvements for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each field carefully and accurately based on your experience.
- Take note that some fields are required to be filled in order to complete the form.
- If you're unsure about a field, please ask for clarification from the staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional)
This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance. You can read it carefully and then proceed to the next field.

### **Museum** (`museum`, `select_one`, required)
- This field asks if you visited a museum during your tour.
- Please select "Yes" if you visited a museum, and "No" otherwise.

### **Park** (`park`, `select_multiple`, optional)
- This field asks about your experience with parks during the tour.
- Select all parks that you visited during your tour.

### **Landmark** (`landmark`, `select_one`, required)
- This field asks if you visited a landmark during your tour.
- Please select "Yes" if you visited a landmark, and "No" otherwise.

### **Museum Experience** (`museum_experience`, `number`, required)
- This field is about your experience at the museum. 
- Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".

### **Park Experience** (`park_experience`, `number`, required)
- This field is about your experience at the park. 
- Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".

### **Landmark Experience** (`landmark_experience`, `number`, required)
- This field is about your experience at the landmark. 
- Rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".

### **Museum Services** (`museum_services`, `select_multiple`, optional)
- This field asks if you used any services offered by the museum during your visit.
- Select all services that you used, if any.

### **Park Services** (`park_services`, `select_multiple`, optional)
- This field asks if you used any services offered by the park during your visit.
- Select all services that you used, if any.

### **Landmark Services** (`landmark_services`, `select_multiple`, optional)
- This field asks if you used any services offered by the landmark during your visit.
- Select all services that you used, if any.

## Tips
- Take a moment to carefully read each field and its label.
- If you need help or clarification about any field, please ask the staff.
- Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
