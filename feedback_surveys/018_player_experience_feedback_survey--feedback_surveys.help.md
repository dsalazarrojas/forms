# Player Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather feedback from players about their experience with our game, allowing us to understand their likes and dislikes and make informed decisions to improve the game.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select a rating for your game experience (e.g., Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative).
2. Indicate if you have played our game (True or False).
3. If you have any suggestions for our team, you can type them in the text area.
4. Rate your satisfaction with our game (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
5. Describe what you liked or disliked about our game.
6. Provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you.
7. Select any features of our game that you particularly enjoyed (Graphics, Music, Story, Gameplay).
8. Share any improvement suggestions you have for our game.
9. Submit your feedback by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Experience** (`game_experience`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select how you feel about your experience with our game. This will help us understand your overall sentiment.
* **Have you played our game?** (`playtested_games`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check yes if you have played our game, and no if not.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our team?** (`suggestions`, note, optional):
	+ If you have any ideas on how to improve our game, type them in this text area.
* **How satisfied are you with our game?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with our game, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **What did you like or dislike about our game?** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your experience with our game, including what you liked or disliked.
* **What is your email address or phone number?** (`contact_info`, email, optional):
	+ Provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you.
* **What are your favorite features of our game?** (`favorite_features`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select any features of our game that you particularly enjoyed (e.g., Graphics, Music, Story, Gameplay).
* **What would you like to see improved in our game?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional):
	+ Share any suggestions you have on how to improve our game.
