## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect feedback from participants of our internal audit process. The purpose of this survey is to gather information on your experience with the audit team's preparation, communication, and overall performance. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our internal audit process is efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Rate each question using the scale provided (On a scale of 1-5).
3. You may skip questions that do not apply to your experience or do not make sense based on your understanding.
4. When finished, review and submit your survey.
