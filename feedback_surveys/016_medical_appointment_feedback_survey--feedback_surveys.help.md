# Medical Appointment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with medical appointments. Your responses will help us understand your thoughts on the care and service you received.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and constructive feedback about your medical appointment. You can select one or multiple answers depending on the question type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Appointment Feedback** (`1`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the appointment.
* **What was your overall experience at this appointment?** (`2`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 means very poor and 5 means excellent.
* **How would you rate the care you received from the provider?** (`3`, `number`, optional): Rate the provider's care from 1 to 5, where 1 means very poor and 5 means excellent.
* **How satisfied were you with this appointment?** (`4`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **How would you rate the provider's communication with you?** (`5`, `number`, optional): Rate the provider's communication from 1 to 5, where 1 means very poor and 5 means excellent.
* **How long did your appointment take?** (`6`, `number`, optional): Estimate the length of time you spent with the provider.
* **How long did you wait in the waiting area?** (`7`, `number`, optional): Estimate the time you waited in the waiting area.
* **Was the provider on time for your appointment?** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether the provider was On Time, Late, or Early.
* **Was your appointment started on time?** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether your appointment was On Time, Late, or Early.
* **Additional comments or feedback about your appointment?** (`10`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
* **Would you recommend this provider?** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Please select Yes or No.
* **Would you recommend this appointment?** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Please select Yes or No.
* **What type of appointment was this?** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select one or multiple types of appointments that apply to your experience.

Note to end-users: Please answer all questions that apply, and do not worry about the optional fields unless they directly relate to your experience.
