# Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from kitchen users about their experience with the kitchen. This feedback will help us understand areas of improvement and make necessary changes to enhance the kitchen collaboration experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, fill out the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. If you have already submitted feedback within the last 30 days, you will be notified that you cannot submit this form again.
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Overall rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the kitchen from 1 to 5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Describe your thoughts and feelings about your experience with the kitchen.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.
* **Submit time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted at** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This field will display the time when you submitted this form.
