# Healthcare Visitor Policy Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Visitor Policy Feedback Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from visitors about their experiences during visits to healthcare facilities. This form is meant to collect information on how well visitors were greeted, the effectiveness of staff communication, and any issues they faced during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information in each field. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each section to help us improve the visitor experience. If a section is not applicable or not relevant, feel free to leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, text, optional): Please describe your overall experience with our healthcare facility. Share any thoughts or feelings you had during your visit.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, text, optional): Share any comments or concerns you had about the staff you interacted with during your visit. This can include praise or constructive feedback.
* **Communication Rules** (`communication_rules`, select one): Rate the quality of communication you experienced during your visit. Choose from:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Issues Faced** (`issues_faced`, select multiple): Select all issues you faced during your visit, if any. Choose from:
	+ None
	+ Communication Issues
	+ Parking Issues
	+ Access Issues
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your visit.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you were assigned a specific tool or equipment during your visit, please list it here.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): If you have an output file from your visit, please attach it here.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): If you wish to specify a category for your feedback, please select it from the list below. (Note: this field is not currently populated with options)

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise stated as required.
