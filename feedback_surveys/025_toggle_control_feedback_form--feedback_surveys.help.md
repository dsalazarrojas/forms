# Toggle Control Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toggle Control Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback on the usability, functionality, and overall satisfaction of our toggle controls.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the platform you're using to access our product (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, or Web).
2. Choose the device you're using (Desktop or Mobile).
3. Select the toggle control you're providing feedback on (Toggle 1, Toggle 2, or Toggle 3).
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with each control using the numbers provided.
5. If you've encountered any issues or have suggestions, please describe them in the text fields.
6. If you'd like to provide additional comments, you can add them in the text fields.
7. If you have a screenshot related to your feedback, you can upload it as a note.
8. Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rating_1** (number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the first toggle control, using the numbers 1-10.
* **control_rating** (number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the toggle control, using the numbers 1-10.
* **bug_reports** (text, optional): If you've encountered any issues, describe them in this field.
* **improvement_suggestions** (text, optional): If you have suggestions for improvement, add them here.
* **feedback_comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience.
* **screenshot** (note, optional): Upload a screenshot related to your feedback.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **which_platform** (select_one, required): Select the platform you're using.
* **what_device** (select_one, required): Choose the device you're using.
* **toggle_control_id** (select_one, required): Select the toggle control you're providing feedback on.
* **platform_version** (text, required): Enter the platform version you're using.
* **rating_4**, **rating_5**, **rating_6**, **rating_7**, **rating_8**, **rating_9**, **rating_10** (number, required): Rate your satisfaction with each toggle control, using the numbers 1-10.
* **screenshot_2**, **screenshot_3** (time, optional): If you have a screenshot related to your feedback, upload it as a note.
* **bug_report_2** (text, optional): If you've encountered any issues, describe them in this field.
* **feedback_comments_2** (text, optional): Add any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience.
* **email_2** (email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, enter your email address.
