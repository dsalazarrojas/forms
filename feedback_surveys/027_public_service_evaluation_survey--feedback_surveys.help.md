# public_service_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on public services, allowing users to rate and comment on their experience. It is likely to be used by customers or users to express their opinions on how well a service was delivered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can add any general comments or questions you might have.
2. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale (though the scale is not specified in this example).
3. **Service Quality**: Choose how you would rate the service quality (good or poor).
4. **Communication**: Check if you received clear communication from the service staff.
5. **Staff Helpfulness**: Check if the staff was helpful during your interaction.
6. **Timeliness**: Check if the service was provided on time.
7. **How would you rate the service quality?**: Rate the service quality on a numerical scale (though the scale is not specified in this example).
8. **Overall Score**: Rate your overall experience on a numerical scale (though the scale is not specified in this example).
9. **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience.
10. **Email**: Input your email address for contact purposes.
11. **Phone**: Input your phone number for contact purposes.
12. **Other Contact Info**: Provide any other contact information you might have.

**Tips**: Please fill out this form as accurately as possible to provide valuable feedback. If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us at your email or phone number provided above.
