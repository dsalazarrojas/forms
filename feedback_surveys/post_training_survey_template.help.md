# post_training_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-training survey is designed to collect feedback from trainees after a training program. The feedback will help us improve future programs by identifying areas of strength and weakness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How satisfied were you with the overall training program?" by selecting one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Choose all the sessions or topics that you found most valuable by selecting all that apply from the list below:
	* Core Concepts
	* Advanced Techniques
	* Practical Exercises
	* Q&A and Discussion
	* Networking Opportunities
3. Respond to the question "Was the training program well organized and structured?" by selecting one of the four options: true, Mostly, Not really, or false.
4. Indicate how likely you are to recommend this training program to a colleague by selecting one of the five options: Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very unlikely.
5. Provide one suggestion on how to improve future training programs in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with the overall training program?** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied):
	+ Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Which sessions or topics did you find most valuable?** (Core Concepts, Advanced Techniques, Practical Exercises, Q&A and Discussion, Networking Opportunities):
	+ Choose all that apply.
* **Was the training program well organized and structured?** (true, Mostly, Not really, false):
	+ Select true if the program was well organized and structured, otherwise choose one of the other options.
* **How likely are you to recommend this training program to a colleague?** (Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely):
	+ Select one of the five options.
* **What is one thing that could significantly improve future training programs?**:
	+ Provide a brief and specific suggestion.
