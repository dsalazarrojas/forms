# Monthly Dialogue Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on the content and topics covered during the monthly dialogue sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely, if all fields are not required, you can skip them if you are unable to provide the information.
- Save your progress and come back to the form when you are ready to submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Monthly form** (`monthly_dialogue_form_1`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any thoughts or comments about the current month's dialogue session. Keep your entry concise and to the point.
- **ratings** (`monthly_ratings_1`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the rating you give to the current month's dialogue session, on a scale of 1-5.
- **participant_reflection** (`monthly_participant_reflection_1`, note, optional): This field is for providing any personal reflections or insights you gained from the dialogue session.
- **topic_suggestions** (`monthly_topic_suggestions_1`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for suggesting any new topics or areas you think should be covered in future dialogue sessions.
- **additional_comments** (`monthly_additional_comments_1`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or suggestions you have that do not fit in the other fields.

## Tips
- Please keep your feedback constructive and respectful.
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
- If you are unable to provide a rating, please explain why in the ratings field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the facilitator of the dialogue session.
