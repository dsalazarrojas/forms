# Global Human Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Human Rights Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about individuals' perceptions and knowledge of human rights. This survey aims to assess awareness, attitudes, and education levels related to human rights, which are essential for promoting understanding, respect, and protection of human rights worldwide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Please provide your responses based on your personal experiences and knowledge.
3. If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
4. Review the form carefully and submit it once you have completed all the required sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Human Rights Survey** (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
	+ This section is designed to provide space for you to share any thoughts, comments, or experiences related to human rights. It is not required to answer this question, but it will help us better understand your perspectives.
* **Human Rights Awareness** (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
	+ This section is meant for you to write about your current level of awareness or understanding of human rights. Please provide your honest thoughts and insights.
* **Human Rights Attitudes** (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, required)
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided, and choose the attitudes that you generally hold or believe in regarding human rights. This includes your feelings, values, or perspectives on human rights.
* **Human Rights Knowledge** (<name>, <type>: number, optional)
	+ In this section, please indicate your level of knowledge about human rights on a scale of 0-5. A 0-5 scale means 0 = No knowledge, 5 = Extensive knowledge.
* **Human Rights Education** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
	+ Select the level of education you have received about human rights. Please choose the option that best describes your educational background in this area.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help.
* This survey is designed to be completed by individuals who are 18 years or older.
* The survey should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the survey, please contact us for assistance.
