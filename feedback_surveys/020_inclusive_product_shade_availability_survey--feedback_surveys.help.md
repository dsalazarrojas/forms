# Inclusive Product Shade Availability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Inclusive Product Shade Availability Survey" is a tool designed to help us better understand the availability of shade options for different products. This survey aims to gather information about the products you have in stock and their corresponding shade availability. Your input will help us improve our inventory management and ensure that we have a more comprehensive selection of products with different shade options for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you want to provide information about from the options listed.
2. Enter the name of the product.
3. Choose the type of product from the available options.
4. Indicate the availability of shade options for the product by entering a numerical value.
5. Enter the price of the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inclusive Product Shade Availability** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the title of the product that you are providing information about. Please enter the name of the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product as it appears on the label or packaging.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of product from the options provided (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Shade Availability** (`shade_availability`, number, optional): Indicate the availability of shade options for the product by entering a numerical value.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information about the product you are providing data about.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If the product is not listed as an option in the "Product Type" field, select "Option 1" as a default choice.
* This survey is completely anonymous, and your input will be kept confidential.
* Your feedback is invaluable, and we appreciate your contribution to improving our inventory management.
