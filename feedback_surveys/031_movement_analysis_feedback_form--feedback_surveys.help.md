# movement_analysis_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "movement_analysis_feedback_form" is a feedback form designed to collect participant information and session details after a movement analysis session. This form is intended to gather your thoughts and feedback about your experience during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Participant Information page with your details.
2. Provide information about your session in the Movement Analysis Session page.
3. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 in the Overall Experience page.
4. Leave any additional comments or notes in the Session Notes and Participant Comments pages.
5. Optionally, provide ratings and comments for each session in the Ratings and Ratings Comments pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required/optional): Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Movement Analysis Session** (`Movement Analysis Session`, text, optional): Describe your experience during the session, including any notable events or moments that stood out to you.
* **Ratings** (`Ratings`, select multiple, optional): Rate your experience as:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Ratings Comments** (`Ratings Comments`, text, optional): If you rated your experience as "Poor", "Fair", or "Good", provide more details about your reasons for choosing that rating.
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, select one, optional): Rate your overall experience as:
	+ 1 - Not Satisfied
	+ 2 - Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ 5 - Very Satisfied
* **Session Notes** (`Session Notes`, text, optional): Leave any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Participant Comments** (`Participant Comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing your feedback.
* Keep your responses concise and clear.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.
* You can complete this form in any language.
