# Crisis Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Crisis Feedback Survey is a form used to collect feedback from individuals who have experienced or participated in a crisis response situation. This form aims to gather valuable insights and suggestions to improve response efforts and emergency planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `feedback_survey_form` field with your personal response to the crisis.
2. Rate your `response_effort` by selecting a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
3. Provide detailed `feedback` with your thoughts and experiences.
4. If you have any `suggestions`, enter them in the corresponding field.
5. Rate the `response_effort` of the crisis response team again, using the same scale as step 2.
6. Rate the `emergency_planning` of the crisis response team, using the same scale as step 2.
7. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can enter your `anonymous` response, but this will not be displayed.
8. Enter your `email` address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Survey Form** (`feedback_survey_form`, text, required: false): Enter your personal response to the crisis.
* **Response Effort** (`response_effort`, number, required: false): Rate the response effort from 1-5, where 1 is "Very poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide detailed feedback on your experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Enter any suggestions you have for improving response efforts.
* **Rating for Response Effort** (`rating_response_effort`, number, required: false): Rate the response effort again, using the same scale as above.
* **Rating for Emergency Planning** (`rating_emergency_planning`, number, required: false): Rate the emergency planning from 1-5, using the same scale as above.
* **Anonymous** (`anonymous`, text, required: false): If you wish to remain anonymous, enter your response here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
