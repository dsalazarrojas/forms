# Medication Dispensing Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from patients about their experience with medication dispensing during a visit to a pharmacy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Visit** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_1`, Select multiple, Required: False): This question asks if it was your first visit to the pharmacy. Please select "Yes" if it was your first visit, and "No" if you have visited before.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of care provided during your visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_2`, Select one, Required: False): Rate your experience with the overall quality of care provided during your visit, using the options very good, good, neutral, bad, or very bad.
* **Were medications dispensed as expected?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_3`, Select one, Required: False): This question asks if the medications were dispensed as you expected. Select "True" if they were, "False" if not, or "partially" if some were not.
* **Overall experience during visit** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_4`, Text, Required: False): Share your thoughts about your overall experience during the visit. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How would you rate the friendliness of staff during visit** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_5`, Select multiple, Required: False): Rate the friendliness of the staff during your visit, using the options Good, Fair.
* **How would you rate the helpfulness of staff during visit** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_6`, Select multiple, Required: False): Rate the helpfulness of the staff during your visit, using the options Good, Fair.
* **Was your medication needs met during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_7`, Select one, Required: False): This question asks if your medication needs were met during the visit. Select "True" if they were, "False" if not.
* **How long did it take for medication to be dispensed?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_8`, Select one, Required: False): Rate the time it took for medication to be dispensed, using the options less than 5 minutes, 5 to 10 minutes, 11 to 15 minutes, or more than 15 minutes.
* **Would you recommend this pharmacy?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_9`, Select multiple, Required: False): Let us know if you would recommend this pharmacy to others. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **How would you rate the knowledge of staff about your medication?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_10`, Select multiple, Required: False): Rate the knowledge of the staff about your medication, using the options Good, Fair.
* **Was information about medication provided during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_11`, Select one, Required: False): This question asks if information about your medication was provided during the visit. Select "True" if it was, "False" if not.
* **Were there any issues with your medication during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_12`, Select multiple, Required: False): Let us know if you experienced any issues with your medication during the visit. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **What issues were with your medication during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_13`, Text, Required: False): Share any issues you experienced with your medication during the visit. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Did you experience any issues with payment during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_14`, Select multiple, Required: False): Let us know if you experienced any issues with payment during the visit. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_15`, Text, Required: False): Share any additional feedback or comments you may have about your visit.
* **Was the visit during regular business hours?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_16`, Select one, Required: False): This question asks if the visit was during regular business hours. Select "True" if it was, "False" if not.
* **What time was visit during?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_17`, Time, Required: False): Share the time when you visited the pharmacy.
* **Date of visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_18`, Date, Required: False): Share the date of your visit.
* **Time of visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_19`, Time, Required: False): Share the time of your visit.
* **Date of visit** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_20`, Date, Required: False): This field is a duplicate of the previous question. Please skip it.
* **What type of medication was dispensed during the visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_21`, Text, Required: False): Share the type of medication you received during the visit.
* **Would you like to provide your medication name during visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_22`, Text, Required: False): Share the name of your medication.
* **How would you rate overall satisfaction with this visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_23`, Select one, Required: False): Rate your overall satisfaction with the visit, using the options very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **Would you like to provide feedback on this visit?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_24`, Text, Required: False): Share any feedback or comments about your visit.
* **Do you consent to use your feedback for improvement?** (`medication_dispensing_feedback_survey_25`, Select one, Required: False): This question asks if you consent to use your feedback to improve our services. Select "True" if you do, "False" if not.
