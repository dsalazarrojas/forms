# inhaler_technique_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with a specific inhaler technique.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by answering the questions on each page. The questions are designed to be easy to understand and will take about 5-10 minutes to complete.
2. Be honest and complete as much information as you can. If you are unsure about an answer, try your best to provide your best estimate.
3. Use the drop-down menus to select the most accurate answer for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How often do you use your inhaler?** (`question_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
* **What triggers your inhaler use?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply:
	+ Cough
	+ Wheezing
	+ Bronchitis
	+ Asthma
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How much medication do you take per inhaler?** (`question_3`, number, required: false): Enter a whole number.
* **What are your symptoms when using your inhaler?** (`question_4`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following:
	+ No symptoms
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Cough
	+ Tremors
* **How would you rate your comfort level with using your inhaler?** (`question_5`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the following:
	+ Not comfortable at all
	+ Somewhat uncomfortable
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Very uncomfortable
* **Any other comments or concerns you may have about using your inhaler?** (`question_6`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Submission** (`submission`, note, required: false): Please review your answers before submitting the form.
