# Institutional Sustainability Goals Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Institutional Sustainability Goals Awareness Survey" is designed to gauge employees' understanding of their institution's sustainability goals, particularly in relation to communication channels and feedback mechanisms. This survey aims to gather input on awareness, engagement, and areas for improvement regarding sustainability efforts within the institution. The information gathered will help in tailoring strategies to enhance awareness and engagement with sustainability goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This page provides a brief background on the survey's purpose and importance. Please read and understand the context before proceeding.
2. **Sustainability Focus**: Select all areas of sustainability that you are aware of, which might include environmental, operational, and institutional sustainability.
3. **Awareness Channels**: Choose the channels through which you receive information about sustainability efforts, such as social media, newsletters, campus events, or through your department meetings.
4. **Engagement Channels**: Indicate the channels you use to engage in sustainability efforts, including volunteer opportunities, course content, campus activities, or department meetings.
5. **Communication Channels**: Select all the channels you use or think are most effective for communication on sustainability-related activities.
6. **Feedback Frequency**: Tell us how often you usually receive feedback on sustainability efforts, whether daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or never.
7. **Feedback Channels**: Mention the channels you usually use to receive feedback, if at all.
8. **Open-ended Questions**: Answer each of the open-ended questions in the space provided. These are designed to gather more specific and detailed feedback on your perceptions and experiences with the institution's sustainability efforts.
9. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the previous steps, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainability Focus**: This question asks about your awareness of the institution's sustainability efforts. Please choose all that apply.
	+ **Environmental Sustainability**: Concerns related to environmental conservation and sustainability.
	+ **Operational Sustainability**: Concerns related to operational practices, such as energy efficiency and waste reduction.
	+ **Institutional Sustainability**: Concerns related to the institution's overall approach to sustainability, including policies and procedures.
* **Awareness Channels**: This question asks about how you receive information on sustainability efforts.
	+ **Social Media**: Information shared on social media platforms.
	+ **Newsletters**: Newsletters or email updates from the institution.
	+ **Campus Events**: Events, workshops, or seminars organized by the institution.
	+ **Department Meetings**: Meetings within your department or team.
	+ **Faculty Meetings**: Meetings with faculty members.
	+ **Posters**: Posters or printed materials displayed around campus.
	+ **Other**: Specify in the comments section.
* **Engagement Channels**: This question asks about how you participate in sustainability efforts.
	+ **Volunteer Opportunities**: Opportunities to participate in volunteer work related to sustainability.
	+ **Course Content**: Course materials or activities that incorporate sustainability.
	+ **Campus Activities**: Events, activities, or initiatives on campus that promote sustainability.
	+ **Department Meetings**: Meetings within your department or team.
	+ **Faculty Meetings**: Meetings with faculty members.
	+ **Posters**: Posters or printed materials displayed around campus.
	+ **Other**: Specify in the comments section.
* **Communication Channels**: This question asks about the channels you think are most effective for communication.
	+ **Email**: Email updates or newsletters.
	+ **Text Message**: Text messages or alerts.
	+ **Social Media**: Information shared on social media.
	+ **Newsletters**: Newsletters or regular updates.
	+ **Campus Portal**: Institutional or departmental websites or portals.
	+ **Department Meetings**: Meetings within your department or team.
	+ **Posters**: Posters or printed materials displayed around campus.
	+ **Other**: Specify in the comments section.
* **Feedback Frequency**: This question asks about how often you receive feedback on your institution's sustainability efforts. You might answer:
	+ **Daily**: Every day or on a daily basis.
	+ **Weekly**: Weekly or on a regular basis.
	+ **Monthly**: Feedback once a month or on a specific schedule.
	+ **Quarterly**: Feedback every quarter or on a set timeline.
	+ **Never**: No regular feedback is received.
* **Feedback Channels**: This question asks about the channels through which you usually receive feedback. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Email**: Feedback received via email.
	+ **Text Message**: Feedback received via text message.
	+ **Social Media**: Feedback received through social media.
	+ **Campus Portal**: Feedback received through the institutional or departmental website.
