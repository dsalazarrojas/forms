# Public Service Announcement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Service Announcement Survey is a tool to gather feedback and opinions from users on public service announcements. It is used to understand how effective these announcements are in reaching users and to gather information on how users can be contacted in case of a public service issue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "True" or "False" for the question "Recall".
2. For "message_quality", select a number to rate the quality of the announcement.
3. Choose one or more options from "Watch video", "Visit website", "Call/Text/Visit in person", and "Social Media" for "intended_actions".
4. For "contact_method", select "Phone", "Email", "Text", "Visit in person", or "Other (please specify)".
5. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "contact_email" field.
7. For "message_quality_rating", select "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Not Good" to rate the quality of the announcement.
8. For "message_quality_relevant", choose "Very Relevant", "Somewhat Relevant", "Not Relevant", or "Not Relevant at all".
9. For "message_quality_clear", select "Very Clear", "Somewhat Clear", "Not Clear", or "Not Clear at All".
10. Finally, click the submit button to submit your survey response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recall** (`Recall`, `select_one`, required): Please select "True" if you remember seeing or hearing the public service announcement, or "False" if you do not remember.
* **message_quality** (`message_quality`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the announcement on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **intended_actions** (`intended_actions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more ways you would like to be contacted in case of a public service issue, such as "Watch video", "Visit website", "Call/Text/Visit in person", or "Social Media".
* **contact_method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted in case of a public service issue, such as "Phone", "Email", "Text", "Visit in person", or "Other (please specify)".
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **message_quality_rating** (`message_quality_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate the quality of the announcement on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **message_quality_important** (`message_quality_important`, `select_one`, required): Choose how important the announcement was to you, such as "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Not Good".
* **message_quality_relevant** (`message_quality_relevant`, `select_one`, required): Choose how relevant the announcement was to you, such as "Very Relevant", "Somewhat Relevant", "Not Relevant", or "Not Relevant at all".
* **message_quality_clear** (`message_quality_clear`, `select_one`, required): Choose how clear the announcement was, such as "Very Clear", "Somewhat Clear", "Not Clear", or "Not Clear at All".
