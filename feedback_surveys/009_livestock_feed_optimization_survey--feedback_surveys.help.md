# Livestock Feed Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Feed Optimization Survey is designed to collect information about your farm's current feeding practices, habits, and challenges. This form will help you assess your feeding strategies and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. You will be asked about various aspects of your farm's feeding practices, including:
* Your farm's name
* Number of animals
* Frequency of feeding
* Types of feed used
* Challenges with feeding
* Methods for determining feeding schedules
* Health monitoring methods
* External data used
* Frequency of plan updates
* Satisfaction with the current plan
* Important factors considered when making decisions
* Benefits from the current plan

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the name of your farm?** (`page_1`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter the name of your farm, as it appears on your farm's identification or marketing materials.
* **How many animals do you have on your farm?** (`page_2`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Answer this question by providing a numerical value, as the total number of animals on your farm.
* **How often do you feed your livestock?** (`page_3`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Describe the frequency with which you feed your animals, e.g., daily, three times a week, etc.
* **What is the primary feed type used on your farm?** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select all applicable options from the list (Wheat, Corn, Soybean, Alfalfa, Other).
* **What are your top three challenges when it comes to feeding your livestock?** (`page_5`, `text`, required: false)
	+ List three challenges you face when feeding your animals, e.g., cost, time, etc.
* **What is your current method for determining when to start and stop feeding your livestock?** (`page_6`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the method you currently use, e.g., Visual inspection, Weight check, Schedule.
* **What type of data do you currently use to monitor your animal's health?** (`page_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select all applicable methods from the list (Body condition scores, Growth rate, Health status, Other).
* **Are you currently using any external data or information to guide your feeding decisions?** (`page_8`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer with a simple "True" or "False".
* **How often do you review and update your feeding plan?** (`page_9`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer with one of the options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Never).
* **How satisfied are you with the current feeding plan on your farm?** (`page_10`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Provide a numerical value, on a scale of 0 to 10, representing your level of satisfaction.
* **What is the most important factor to consider when making feeding decisions?** (`page_11`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select all applicable options from the list (Feed costs, Animal health, Production efficiency, Other).
* **What are some of the biggest benefits you've seen from using your current feeding plan?** (`page_12`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Describe the benefits you've experienced from your current feeding plan, e.g., cost savings, improved animal health, etc.
