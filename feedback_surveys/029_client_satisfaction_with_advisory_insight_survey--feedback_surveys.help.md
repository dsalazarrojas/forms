# Client Satisfaction With Advisory Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of the advisory services provided by our organization. It helps us to identify areas of strength and weakness, and make improvements to better meet the needs of our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2. For select_one fields, choose one option from the provided list.
3. For text fields (like `improvement_suggestions` and `additional_feedback`), provide your thoughts and comments in the space provided.
4. If you have already provided feedback through other channels, please do not repeat the same responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on the contact information provided to us.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for us to follow up with you if needed.
* **Length of Engagement** (`engagement_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how long you have worked with us from the provided options.
* **Quality of Advisory Services** (`advisory_quality`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the quality of our advisory services from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Relevance of Insights Provided** (`insights_relevance`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how applicable the insights are to your situation.
* **Actionability of Recommendations** (`actionability`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how actionable the recommendations are.
* **Business Impact of Recommendations** (`business_impact`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the positive impact of the recommendations.
* **Advisor Expertise and Knowledge** (`advisor_expertise`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the advisor's expertise and knowledge from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate the communication quality from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Responsiveness to Your Needs** (`responsiveness`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate our responsiveness to your needs from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Value Delivered for Investment** (`value_delivery`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how much value you received from our services (Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, No value).
* **Would You Continue or Expand Engagement?** (`future_engagement`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your likelihood to continue or expand engagement with our services.
* **Likelihood to Recommend to Others** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `number`, **required**): Enter a number (not an option) indicating how likely you are to recommend our services to others.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any suggestions on how we can improve our services.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please share them here.
