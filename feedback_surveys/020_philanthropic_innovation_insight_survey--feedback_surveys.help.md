# Philanthropic Innovation Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Philanthropic Innovation Insight Survey is designed to gather information about various aspects related to philanthropic innovation. This survey aims to collect data on programs, categories, questions, dates, times, and contact information. The data collected will help organizations understand the needs and trends in the philanthropic innovation space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Program Name in the first field.
2. Select one or more categories that best describe the program.
3. Answer Question 1 with a brief note.
4. Choose an option for Question 2.
5. Enter the Date and Time of the program.
6. Enter your Email address.
7. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name (program_name)**: Enter the name of the program you are submitting for review.
* **Category (category)**: Select one or more categories that best describe the program. This will help us understand the specific area of focus.
* **Question 1 (question_1)**: Please provide a brief description or answer to this question. This will help us understand the program's objectives and goals.
* **Question 2 (question_2)**: Choose the option that best answers this question. This will help us gauge the level of complexity and feasibility of the program.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the program.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the program.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Phone (phone)**: If applicable, enter your phone number so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
