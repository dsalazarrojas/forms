# Box Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Box Truck Inspection Form is used to assess the condition of a box truck before it is approved or rented out. The form captures information about the vehicle's overall condition, including exterior and interior checks, to ensure it is safe and reliable for use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field as instructed, using the provided fields to guide your inspection.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "required" label.
3. Select the most accurate option for each field based on your assessment.
4. If you need to list repairs or issues, use the text field at the end of the form.
5. Sign and date the form to authenticate your inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: Date of inspection, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Inspection Time**: Time of inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: Full name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector ID**: Employee identification number of the inspector.
* **Truck ID**: Vehicle identification number or fleet number.
* **Truck Make**: Manufacturer of the truck.
* **Truck Model**: Model name of the truck.
* **Truck Year**: Year of manufacture.
* **Odometer Reading**: Current mileage of the truck.
* **License Plate Number**: Plate number of the truck.
* **Inspection Type**: Type of inspection being performed (e.g., Pre-rental inspection, Post-rental inspection, etc.).
* **Exterior Condition**: Overall condition of the exterior of the truck (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Body Damage**: Any dents, scratches, or other damage found on the body of the truck (None, Minor, Moderate, Severe).
* **Damage Description**: Describe any damage found on the body of the truck.
* **Tire Condition**: Overall condition of the tires (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor - needs replacement).
* **Tire Pressure**: Is tire pressure adequate (All tires adequate, Some tires low, Multiple tires low).
* **Lights Functional**: Are all lights working (All lights working, Some lights out, Multiple lights out).
* **Brakes Condition**: Condition of the brake system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs attention).
* **Fluid Levels**: Are fluid levels adequate (All fluids adequate, Some fluids low, Multiple fluids low).
* **Cargo Area Condition**: Condition of the cargo area (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Cargo Area Cleanliness**: Is the cargo area clean (Clean, Moderately clean, Dirty, Very dirty).
* **Lift Gate Operation**: Does the lift gate work properly (Working properly, Minor issues, Not working, Not applicable).
* **Doors Operation**: Do all doors operate properly (All doors working, Some issues, Multiple issues).
* **Cab Interior Condition**: Condition of the driver area (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Safety Equipment**: Is safety equipment present (Fire extinguisher, First aid kit, Warning triangles, Reflective vests, All present, Some missing).
* **Overall Vehicle Rating**: Overall assessment of the vehicle's condition (Excellent - ready for use, Good - minor issues noted, Fair - needs attention, Poor - not recommended for use).
* **Repairs Needed**: List any repairs required.
* **Vehicle Approved for Use**: Is the vehicle approved for use (Yes, approved, Yes, with noted issues, No, not approved).
* **Inspector Signature**: Electronic signature of the inspector.
* **Driver Acknowledgment**: Driver signature if applicable.

Note: The driver's acknowledgment may be optional, but it is recommended that the driver signs the form to confirm they have reviewed the inspection report.
