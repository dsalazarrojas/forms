# Dot Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dot Inspection Form is designed to collect basic information about a vehicle's condition, inspection results, and other relevant details. This form is intended to be a quick and simple way to document vehicle inspections, but it's not a substitute for a comprehensive inspection report.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the vehicle you're inspecting.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your observations and findings.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "vehicle_inspection_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info (vehicle_info)**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Year (vehicle_year)**: Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Make (vehicle_make)**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model (vehicle_model)**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year (vehicle_year)**: Another field for the vehicle's year of manufacture. Please ensure consistency with the "Vehicle Year (vehicle_year)" field above.
* **Vehicle Condition (vehicle_condition)**: Select the condition of the vehicle from the options: "New", "Used", "Certified", or "Other".
* **Vehicle Type (vehicle_type)**: Select the type of vehicle from the options: "Car", "Truck", "Bus", "Motorcycle", or "Bicycle".
* **Vehicle Mileage (vehicle_mileage)**: Enter the vehicle's mileage, if applicable.
* **Inspection Item (vehicle_inspection_item)**: Select the specific inspection area(s) from the options: "Brake", "Steering", "Electrical", "Engine", "Frame", or "Other".
* **Inspection Result (vehicle_inspection_result)**: Select the result of the inspection from the options: "Pass", "Fail", "Repair", or "Other".
* **Inspection Date (vehicle_inspection_date)**: Enter the date of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Inspection Time (vehicle_inspection_time)**: Enter the time of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Inspector Name (vehicle_inspector_name)**: Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Contact (vehicle_inspector_contact)**: Enter the contact information of the inspector.
* **Inspection Notes (vehicle_inspection_notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Additional Info (vehicle_additional_info)**: Enter any other relevant information about the vehicle.

Remember to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure the vehicle inspection process is complete and effective. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or support team.
