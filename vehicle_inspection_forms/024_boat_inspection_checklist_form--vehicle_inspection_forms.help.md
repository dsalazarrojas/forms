# Boat Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Boat Inspection Checklist Form is used to evaluate the condition and safety of a boat. This form helps inspectors identify areas of concern and provide a final assessment of the overall inspection result.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the Inspection Date field. This date is required to keep track of when the inspection was performed.
2. **Inspector Name**: Type the full name of the person performing the inspection in the Inspector Name field. This is a required field to identify the person who conducted the inspection.
3. **Inspector Certification**: If applicable, enter the certification number or type in the Inspector Certification field.
4. **Boat Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the boat's owner in the Boat Owner Name field.
5. **Boat Owner Contact**: Enter the contact information (phone or email) of the boat's owner in the Boat Owner Contact field.
6. **Boat Make**: Enter the manufacturer's name of the boat in the Boat Model field.
7. **Boat Model**: Enter the model name of the boat in the Boat Model field.
8. **Boat Year**: Enter the year the boat was manufactured in the Boat Year field.
9. **Boat Hull ID**: Enter the Hull ID (HIN) number of the boat in the Boat Hull ID field.
10. **Engine Serial Number**: If applicable, enter the engine's serial number in the Engine Serial Number field.
11. **Inspection Type**: Select the type of inspection being performed from the Inspection Type field. Options include Pre-Purchase Inspection, Annual Safety Inspection, Insurance Inspection, Damage Assessment, Routine Maintenance, and Other.
12. **Hull Exterior Condition**: Evaluate the hull's exterior condition by selecting one of the options in the Hull Exterior Condition field. Choose Pass if there are no issues, Minor issues noted, Major issues found, or Fail if there is critical damage.
13. **Hull Structural Integrity**: Evaluate the hull's structural integrity by selecting one of the options in the Hull Structural Integrity field. Choose Pass, Minor concerns, Major concerns, or Fail.
14. **Deck Condition**: Check the deck's condition by selecting one of the options in the Deck Condition field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
15. **Engine External Condition**: Evaluate the engine's external condition by selecting one of the options in the Engine External Condition field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
16. **Engine Oil Level**: Check the engine's oil level and condition by selecting one of the options in the Engine Oil Level field. Choose Pass - Good level and condition, Low - Needs topping up, Contaminated - Needs change, or Unable to check.
17. **Engine Cooling System**: Check the engine's cooling system by selecting one of the options in the Engine Cooling System field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
18. **Fuel System Condition**: Evaluate the fuel system condition by selecting one of the options in the Fuel System Condition field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
19. **Electrical System**: Check the electrical system by selecting one of the options in the Electrical System field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
20. **Steering System**: Check the steering system by selecting one of the options in the Steering System field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
21. **Propulsion System**: Check the propulsion system by selecting one of the options in the Propulsion System field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
22. **Safety Equipment**: Evaluate the safety equipment by selecting one of the options in the Safety Equipment field. Choose Pass - All present and valid, Minor issues - Some replacement needed, Major issues - Missing equipment, or Fail - Inadequate safety gear.
23. **Navigation Lights**: Check the navigation lights by selecting one of the options in the Navigation Lights field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
24. **Bilge Condition**: Evaluate the bilge condition by selecting one of the options in the Bilge Condition field. Choose Pass - Clean and dry, Minor water present, Major water or oil, or Fail - Significant issues.
25. **Interior Condition**: Check the interior condition by selecting one of the options in the Interior Condition field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
26. **Through-Hull Fittings**: Check the through-hull fittings by selecting one of the options in the Through-Hull Fittings field. Choose Pass, Minor issues, Major issues, or Fail.
27. **Defects Found**: List any defects or issues discovered during the inspection in the Defects Found field. This field is optional and not required.
28. **Recommendations**: Suggest any repairs or actions needed to address the issues found during the inspection in the Recommendations field. This field is optional and not required.
29. **Overall Result**: Select the overall result of the inspection from the Overall Inspection Result field. Choose Pass - No significant issues, Pass with Minor Deficiencies, Fail - Major Deficiencies, or Fail - Critical Safety Issues.
30. **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name in the Inspector Signature field. This is a required field to confirm that the inspection was performed.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure a thorough inspection.
- Check the boat's owner contact information is correct in case follow-up communication is needed.
- Take clear notes and photographs during the inspection to support your findings.
- Use the form to document any defects or issues found and suggest recommended repairs or actions.
- Use the Overall Result field to clearly indicate the overall assessment of the boat's condition.
- Sign your name in the Inspector Signature field to confirm that the form has been completed honestly and accurately.
