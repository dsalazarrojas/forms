# Van Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Van Maintenance Checklist is designed to help you track and manage the regular maintenance activities required for your van. This form guides you through various aspects of van maintenance, including vehicle checks, maintenance frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form, which covers different aspects of van maintenance.
2. Select the relevant maintenance activities for your van from the options provided for each page.
3. Fill in any required or optional fields, such as dates, vehicle details, and comments.
4. Review your answers carefully to ensure you have covered all necessary maintenance activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`select_multiple`, id: 1): Select all maintenance activities that need to be performed on your van, such as Engine Oil Check, Air Filter Check, Brake Pads Check, etc.
* **Brake Pads and Fluid** (`select_one`, id: 2): Check if your van's brake pads and brake fluid are in good condition. Select 'True' if they are, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Tire Maintenance** (`select_multiple`, id: 3): Choose all necessary tire-related maintenance activities for your van, such as Tire Rotation, Tire Balance, and Tire Alignment.
* **Battery and Electrical** (`select_one`, id: 4): Check the condition of your van's battery and electrical system. Select 'True' if they are in good condition, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Vehicle Inspection** (`text`, id: 5): Provide a detailed description of the vehicle inspection performed on your van.
* **Notes** (`note`, id: 6): Add any additional comments or notes related to your van's maintenance.
* **Brake Fluid Level** (`number`, id: 7): Enter the current level of your van's brake fluid.
* **Brake Fluid Type** (`select_one`, id: 8): Select the type of brake fluid used in your van, such as Mineral Oil or Synthetic Oil.
* **Vehicle Age** (`date`, id: 9): Enter the age of your van.
* **Vehicle Type** (`select_one`, id: 10): Select the type of your van, such as Van, Truck, or SUV.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`select_multiple`, id: 11): Choose the frequency of maintenance for your van, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Last Service Date** (`date`, id: 12): Enter the date of your van's last service.
* **Van Maintenance Checklist Page 13** (`note`, id: 13): Add any additional comments or notes related to your van's maintenance.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, id: 14): Provide any additional comments or information about your van's maintenance.
* **Next Service Date** (`date`, id: 15): Enter the date of your van's next service.
* **Vehicle Identification** (`select_one`, id: 16): Select how you identify your van, such as Make, Model, or Year.
