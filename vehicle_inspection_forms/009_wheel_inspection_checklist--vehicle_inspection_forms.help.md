# Wheel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheel Inspection Checklist is a tool used to evaluate the condition of a vehicle's wheels and related equipment. It helps ensure that the vehicle is in good working condition and identifies potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and identify the sections that need to be completed based on the vehicle's specific needs.
2. For each section, select the appropriate options based on the vehicle's condition.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments as needed.
4. Enter the vehicle's details, including its number, equipment number, and the person responsible for submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle and equipment** (`vehicle_and_equipment`): This field is for recording the vehicle's make, model, and year, as well as any relevant equipment details.
* **Wheel condition** (`wheel_condition`): Select the condition of the vehicle's wheels, such as "Bald tires" or "Proper inflation".
* **Wheel alignment** (`wheel_alignment`): Choose whether the vehicle's wheels are "Aligned", "Misaligned", or "Loose".
* **Bearing condition** (`bearing_condition`): Check the condition of the vehicle's bearings, selecting "Normal", "Damaged", or "Worn".
* **Bearing temperature** (`bearing_temperature`): This field is for recording the temperature of the vehicle's bearings.
* **Bearing lubrication** (`bearing_lubrication`): Select whether the vehicle's bearings are "Lubricated", "Not lubricated", or "Not checked".
* **Bearing seal condition** (`bearing_seal`): Check the condition of the vehicle's bearing seals.
* **Hub condition** (`hub_condition`): Select the condition of the vehicle's hubs, such as "Normal", "Worn", or "Damaged".
* **Hub seal condition** (`hub_seal`): Check the condition of the vehicle's hub seals.
* **Wheel fasteners** (`wheel_fasteners`): Choose whether the vehicle's wheel fasteners are "Tight", "Loose", or "Not checked".
* **Wheel fastener seal condition** (`wheel_fastener_seal`): Check the condition of the vehicle's wheel fastener seals.
* **Brake pad condition** (`brake_pad`): Select the condition of the vehicle's brake pads.
* **Brake disc condition** (`brake_disc`): Check the condition of the vehicle's brake discs.
* **Steering box condition** (`steering_box`): Choose whether the vehicle's steering box is "Normal", "Worn", or "Not checked".
* **Power steering condition** (`power_steering`): Select whether the vehicle's power steering is "Normal", "Not working", or "Not checked".
* **Suspension system condition** (`suspension_system`): Check the condition of the vehicle's suspension system.
* **Steering system condition** (`steering_system`): Select the condition of the vehicle's steering system.
* **Brake lines condition** (`brake_lines`): Choose whether the vehicle's brake lines are "Normal", "Worn", or "Not checked".
* **Wheel rotation** (`wheel_rotation`): Record the date of the last wheel rotation.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's condition.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`): Enter the person responsible for the vehicle or equipment.
* **Vehicle number** (`vehicle_number`): Enter the vehicle's number.
* **Equipment number** (`equipment_number`): Enter the equipment's number.
* **Asset number** (`asset_number`): Enter the asset's number.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`): Enter the person submitting the form.
