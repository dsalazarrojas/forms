# Daily Vehicle Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect detailed information about vehicle inspections. Please fill it out carefully to track and evaluate the condition of the vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Description" field.
2. Select the correct "Vehicle Type" from the available options.
3. Enter the "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model" details.
4. (Optional) Enter the "Vehicle Color" and "Vehicle Year" if applicable.
5. Record the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
6. Evaluate the vehicle's overall condition by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Vehicle Condition" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" for each of the inspection items (1-10) based on the vehicle's condition.
8. Provide any additional comments about the vehicle in the "Vehicle Comments" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Description**: A brief description of the vehicle.
	+ Enter a concise and accurate description of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle (e.g., car, truck, motorcycle).
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Corolla, F-150, Accord).
* **Vehicle Color**: (Optional) Enter the vehicle's color (e.g., red, blue, white).
* **Vehicle Year**: (Optional) Enter the vehicle's year of production.
* **Vehicle Mileage**: Record the vehicle's current mileage.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Evaluate the vehicle's overall condition by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Vehicle Inspection Item 1-10**: Answer "Yes" or "No" for each inspection item based on the vehicle's condition.
	+ These items assess various aspects of the vehicle's condition, such as its tires, brakes, etc.
* **Vehicle Comments**: (Optional) Provide any additional comments about the vehicle.
