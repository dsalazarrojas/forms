# Excavation Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The excavation safety checklist is a critical document used to ensure that the excavation site meets the required safety standards and regulations. This form is designed to guide workers, site managers, and inspectors in identifying potential hazards and implementing necessary measures to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully to understand the specific safety requirements.
2.  Answer the questions according to your site-specific conditions and circumstances.
3.  Ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
4.  If unsure or unclear about any field, refer to the provided guidelines or seek guidance from a qualified expert.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions`, Select One, Required: False): This field asks whether the excavation site is clear of all debris and obstacles. Choose the most accurate option that describes the current state of the site.
* **Trench Safety** (`trench_safety`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This section assesses the availability of safety measures such as hard hats, scaffolding, safety harnesses, and netting. Choose the options that apply to your site.
* **Fall Protection** (`fall_protection`, Text, Required: False): Describe the use of fall protection measures such as harnesses or hard hats.
* **Trench Inspection** (`trench_inspection`, Time, Required: False): Enter the duration of the trench inspection performed.
* **Trench Depth** (`trench_depth`, Select One, Required: False): Choose the depth of the trench from 1-2 meters, 2-4 meters, 4-6 meters, or 6 meters or deeper.
* **Trench Width** (`trench_width`, Text, Required: False): Describe the width of the trench.
* **Slope Angle** (`slope_angle`, Select One, Required: False): Choose the slope angle from 0-20 degrees, 20-40 degrees, 40-60 degrees, or 60 degrees or more.
* **Trench Type** (`trench_type`, Select One, Required: False): Select the type of trench from vertical, near vertical, inclined, or other.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, Date, Required: False): Enter the current weather conditions (date).
* **Site Access** (`site_access`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This field assesses the accessibility of all areas of the site. Choose the options that apply.
* **Confined Spaces** (`confined_spaces`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Identify whether there are confined spaces on the site and their level of hazard.
* **Trench Area** (`trench_area`, Text, Required: False): Describe the area of the trench.
* **Trench Orientation** (`trench_orientation`, Text, Required: False): Describe the orientation of the trench.
* **Trench Location** (`trench_location`, Text, Required: False): Describe the location of the trench.
* **Trench Elevation** (`trench_elevation`, Text, Required: False): Describe the elevation of the trench.
* **Trench Direction** (`trench_direction`, Text, Required: False): Describe the direction of the trench.
* **Trench Size** (`trench_size`, Select One, Required: False): Choose the size of the trench from less than 1 meter, 1-3 meters, 3-6 meters, or 6 meters or deeper.
* **Trench Shape** (`trench_shape`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Identify the shape of the trench, which can be rectangular, circular, or U-shaped.
* **Trench Materials** (`trench_materials`, Select Multiple, Required: False): This field assesses the materials used in the trench. Choose the options that apply.
* **Trench Ventilation** (`trench_ventilation`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Evaluate the quality of ventilation in the trench. Choose good, fair, or poor.
* **Trench Access** (`trench_access`, Text, Required: False): Describe the accessibility of the trench.
* **Trench Comments** (`trench_comments`, Text, Required: False): Provide any additional comments about the trench.
* **Site Manager** (`site_manager`, Select One, Required: False): Confirm the availability of a site manager.
* **Site Inspector** (`site_inspector`, Select One, Required: False): Confirm the availability of a site inspector.
* **Inspector Comments** (`inspector_comments`, Text, Required: False): Provide any additional comments from the site inspector.
