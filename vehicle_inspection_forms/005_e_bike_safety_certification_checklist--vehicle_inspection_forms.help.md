# E Bike Safety Certification Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Bike Safety Certification Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to help inspectors evaluate the safety of an e-bike, ensuring it meets the necessary standards and regulations for safe operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the e-bike's manual or documentation to understand its features and specifications.
2. Complete the General Information section with the e-bike's basic details.
3. Review the Safety Checklist section and evaluate each item, checking off or noting any safety hazards or issues found.
4. Complete the Inspector Information section with your details as the designated inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section is used to provide basic details about the e-bike, such as its make and model.
* **Safety Checklist** (`Safety Checklist`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section includes a list of specific safety features and components that require evaluation, such as:
		- Brakes: Check that the brakes are functioning properly and are adequate for the e-bike's size and weight.
		- Lights: Ensure the e-bike is equipped with adequate lighting, such as headlight, taillight, and reflectors.
		- Batteries: Check that the batteries are properly secured and meet the necessary standards for safety and functionality.
* **Inspector Information** (`Inspector Information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section is used to record the inspector's details, including:
		- Name: Enter your name as the designated inspector.
		- Certification Number: Enter your certification number, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be thorough and methodical when evaluating the e-bike's safety features and components.
* Pay attention to any potential hazards or issues found during the inspection.
* Ensure that all necessary safety checks are completed before certifying the e-bike as safe for operation.
