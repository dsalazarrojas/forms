# Vehicle Check Engine Light Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting issues related to a vehicle's engine light coming on, indicating a problem with the engine or emissions system.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the make of your vehicle from the list.
2.  Enter the year of your vehicle.
3.  Enter the odometer reading of your vehicle.
4.  Select the engine size of your vehicle.
5.  Select the transmission type of your vehicle.
6.  Select the engine problem you are experiencing.
7.  Select the type of engine problem you are experiencing.
8.  Enter the fuel level of your vehicle.
9.  Select the type of fuel you are using.
10.  Enter the fuel capacity of your vehicle.
11.  Select the units of measurement for the fuel capacity.
12.  Enter the fuel economy of your vehicle.
13.  Select the units of measurement for the fuel economy.
14.  Select the type of air filter you are using.
15.  Select the type of oil filter you are using.
16.  Enter the oil level of your vehicle.
17.  Select the type of oil you are using.
18.  Enter the oil capacity of your vehicle.
19.  Select the units of measurement for the oil capacity.
20.  Select the transmission fluid type you are using.
21.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Make**: Select the make of your vehicle from the list to help identify the specific vehicle model.
* **Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle to help track maintenance and repair history.
* **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle to help track mileage and maintenance history.
* **Engine Size**: Select the engine size of your vehicle to help identify potential issues with engine performance.
* **Transmission**: Select the transmission type of your vehicle to help identify potential issues with transmission performance.
* **Engine Problem**: Select the type of engine problem you are experiencing (e.g. low oil level, high oil level, etc.).
* **Engine Problem Description**: Enter a brief description of the engine problem you are experiencing.
* **Fuel Level**: Enter the current fuel level of your vehicle.
* **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel you are using (e.g. regular, premium, synthetic, etc.).
* **Fuel Capacity**: Enter the current fuel capacity of your vehicle.
* **Fuel Capacity Units**: Select the units of measurement for the fuel capacity (e.g. gallons, liters, etc.).
* **Fuel Economy**: Enter the current fuel economy of your vehicle.
* **Fuel Economy Units**: Select the units of measurement for the fuel economy (e.g. miles, kilometers, etc.).
* **Air Filter**: Select the type of air filter you are using (e.g. disposable, cartridge, etc.).
* **Air Filter Type**: Select the type of air filter you are using (e.g. disposable, cartridge, etc.).
* **Oil Level**: Enter the current oil level of your vehicle.
* **Oil Type**: Select the type of oil you are using (e.g. synthetic, regular, etc.).
* **Oil Capacity**: Enter the current oil capacity of your vehicle.
* **Oil Capacity Units**: Select the units of measurement for the oil capacity (e.g. gallons, liters, etc.).
* **Transmission Fluid**: Select the type of transmission fluid you are using (e.g. automatic, manual, etc.).
* **Transmission Fluid Type**: Select the type of transmission fluid you are using (e.g. disposable, cartridge, etc.).
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and thorough reporting.
* If you are unsure about the options in a particular field, please select "Other" and provide a brief description in the "engine_problem_description" field.
* Please be as detailed as possible when completing the form to help identify and troubleshoot issues with your vehicle's engine and emissions system.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
