# Weekly Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Vehicle Inspection Form is used to record the details of a vehicle's condition, including its make, year, and other relevant characteristics. This form is designed to help fleet managers and administrators keep track of the vehicle's condition over time and identify any potential issues that may arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk \*.
2. Select the correct options for the "Vehicle Makes" and "Engine Type" fields.
3. Enter the vehicle's "Odometer Reading" using the numeric keypad.
4. Enter the vehicle's "Vehicle Year" using the numeric keypad.
5. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
6. Date and time stamp the form by filling in the "Submitted Date" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Makes**: Select all the makes of the vehicle, such as Toyota, Ford, or Chevrolet.
* **Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Model**: Enter the specific model of the vehicle, such as Camry or Focus.
* **VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Odometer Reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle using the numeric keypad.
* **Vehicle Body Style**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the vehicle has a sedan, truck, or SUV body style.
* **Engine Type**: Select all the types of engines the vehicle has, such as gasoline or diesel.
* **Transmission**: Select all the types of transmissions the vehicle has, such as manual or automatic.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the vehicle is in good condition or not.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or issues with the vehicle.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name.
* **Submitted Date**: Date and time stamp the form using the date field.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
