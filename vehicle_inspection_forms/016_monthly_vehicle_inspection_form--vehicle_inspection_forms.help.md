# Monthly Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly vehicle inspection form is a tool for tracking and documenting the condition and status of a vehicle on a regular basis. It's intended to be used by fleet managers, mechanics, or other vehicle owners to keep a record of the vehicle's maintenance and any issues that arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's license plate number in the "Vehicle license plate" field.
2. Select the correct vehicle year, make, and model from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter the vehicle's color and mileage.
4. Provide information about the engine condition, engine type, transmission type, and vehicle condition.
5. Select the vehicle's use (Private, Commercial, or Personal).
6. Indicate the vehicle year made.
7. Enter any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. Sign and date the form with your name and contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle License Plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number to identify the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Select the correct year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make Model**: Select the correct make and model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Mileage**: Enter the vehicle's current mileage.
* **Engine Condition**: Describe the condition of the engine.
* **Engine Type**: Select the correct engine type (Inline 2.0L, Inline 3.0L, Inline 4.0L).
* **Transmission Type**: Select the correct transmission type (Manual or Automatic).
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the vehicle's condition (New, Used, or Certified).
* **Vehicle Use**: Select the correct use of the vehicle (Private, Commercial, or Personal).
* **Vehicle Year Made**: Select the correct year the vehicle was made.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form with your name and contact information (email and phone number).
