# Cab Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cab Evaluation Form is a tool used by inspectors to document the results of a vehicle safety evaluation. It captures essential information about the vehicle's condition, including the inspector's details, vehicle registration, inspection date, safety checks passed, mileage, any defects found, recommended maintenance actions, and next inspection date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Provide your name and contact information**: Enter your name and contact details in the "Inspector name" field.
2. **Enter vehicle details**: Provide the vehicle's registration number or fleet ID in the "Vehicle registration number" field.
3. **Schedule the inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection date" field.
4. **Evaluate safety checks**: Check if the safety checks (seat belts, lights, brakes) were passed or failed in the "Safety checks passed" field.
5. **Record vehicle mileage**: Enter the current odometer reading in the "Odometer reading" field (if applicable).
6. **Document defects**: If any issues were found during the inspection, describe them in the "Defects found" field.
7. **Recommend maintenance**: Provide any suggested maintenance actions in the "Recommended maintenance actions" field.
8. **Schedule next inspection**: Enter the recommended date for the next inspection in the "Next inspection date" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector name (Person performing evaluation)**: Enter your name and contact details.
* **Vehicle registration number (Plate or fleet id)**: Provide the vehicle's registration number or fleet ID.
* **Inspection date (Date of inspection)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Safety checks passed (Seat belts, lights, brakes)**: Indicate if the safety checks were passed or failed.
* **Odometer reading (Current mileage)**: Enter the current odometer reading (if applicable).
* **Defects found (Describe any issues)**: Document any defects or issues found during the inspection.
* **Recommended maintenance actions (Repair schedule or parts)**: Suggest any maintenance actions for the vehicle.
* **Next inspection date (Suggested follow-up date)**: Enter the recommended date for the next inspection (if applicable).
