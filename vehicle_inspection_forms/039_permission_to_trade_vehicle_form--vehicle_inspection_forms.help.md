# Permission To Trade Vehicle Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Permission To Trade Vehicle Form" is used to gather information about a vehicle and its owner to facilitate the trading process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the vehicle's details.
2. Provide vehicle details, including the vehicle's VIN verification status.
3. Enter the odometer reading and trade price of the vehicle.
4. Indicate whether the VIN has been verified and select all applicable owner information and contact methods.
5. Enter the owner's date and time of birth.
6. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)** (required): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Vehicle Details (2)** (required): Enter the vehicle's details, including its current condition and any relevant notes.
* **Trade Information (3)** (required): Enter the trade price and any relevant notes.
* **Vehicle Inspection Information (4)** (required): Enter the vehicle inspection results.
* **Odometer Reading (5)** (required, numeric): Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **Trade Price (6)** (required, numeric): Enter the trade price of the vehicle.
* **VIN Verification (7)** (required): Select 'Yes' if the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) has been verified, 'No' otherwise.
* **VIN Verification Options (8)** (optional): Select all VIN verification options that apply.
* **Owner Information (9)** (required): Select the owner's sex.
* **Contact Information (10)** (required): Select all contact methods the owner has (e.g., phone, email, other).
* **Address Information (11)** (required): Select all address types the owner has (e.g., current, last known).
* **Date of Birth (12)** (required): Enter the date of birth of the owner.
* **Time of Birth (13)** (required): Enter the time of birth of the owner.
* **Notes (14)** (optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Email (15)** (required): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Phone (16)** (required): Enter the owner's phone number.
