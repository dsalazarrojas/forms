# Car Show Judging Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help car show judges evaluate a vehicle's condition during a competition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* Judge's name and vehicle entry number.
	* Vehicle's make, model, and year.
2. Evaluate the vehicle's condition using the provided ratings for each section:
	* Paint finish, body condition, and exterior condition.
	* Upholstery condition, interior condition, and dashboard condition.
	* Engine appearance and cleanliness, undercarriage condition, and overall impression.
3. Choose the rating that best describes the vehicle's condition for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Judge name (2)**: Enter your name as a judge.
* **Judge ID number (3)**: Enter your Judge ID number, if applicable.
* **Judging category (4)**: Select the category this judge is evaluating.
* **Entry number (6)**: Enter the vehicle's entry number in the competition.
* **Vehicle owner name (7)**: Enter the vehicle's owner's name.
* **Vehicle year (8)**: Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle make (9)**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle model (10)**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle color (11)**: Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Paint finish quality (13)**: Rate the vehicle's paint finish quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Body condition and alignment (14)**: Rate the vehicle's body condition and alignment (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Chrome, trim, and moldings (15)**: Rate the vehicle's chrome, trim, and moldings condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Wheels and tires condition (16)**: Rate the vehicle's wheels and tires condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Glass and lights condition (17)**: Rate the vehicle's glass and lights condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Upholstery condition (19)**: Rate the vehicle's upholstery condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Dashboard and panel condition (20)**: Rate the vehicle's dashboard and panel condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Carpets and floor mats (21)**: Rate the vehicle's carpets and floor mats condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Engine appearance and cleanliness (23)**: Rate the vehicle's engine appearance and cleanliness (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Undercarriage condition (24)**: Rate the vehicle's undercarriage condition (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall impression and scoring (25)**: Enter a score between 1 and 10 to rate the vehicle's overall impression and condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to evaluate the vehicle's condition based on its original and intended condition for its make and year.
* Use the provided ratings to accurately reflect the vehicle's condition.
* Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive evaluation.
