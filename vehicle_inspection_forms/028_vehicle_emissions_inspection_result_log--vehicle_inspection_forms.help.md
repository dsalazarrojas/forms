# Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the results of a vehicle emissions inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vehicle's inspection station.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. Provide the vehicle's model.
4. Record the odometer reading.
5. Enter the vehicle's ID (if applicable).
6. Describe the inspection results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Inspection Station** (`emissions_inspection_station`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the inspection station where the vehicle was inspected.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a valid date for the inspection.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make and model.
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, required/optional): Record the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle ID** (`vehicle_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's unique ID (if applicable).
* **Emissions Inspection Results** (`emissions_inspection_results`, text, required/optional): Describe the results of the emissions inspection.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the inspection station's documentation or consult with the inspector.
