# Trailer Towing Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trailer Towing Safety Checklist is a comprehensive tool used by drivers to inspect their vehicle and trailer for potential safety risks before towing. It helps to identify any issues and prevent accidents or damage to the vehicle and trailer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the pre-inspection checklist to ensure the vehicle and trailer are in good condition before starting your trip.
2.  Perform a road-side inspection by checking the vehicle and trailer conditions, weather conditions, and weight distribution.
3.  Check the trailer's condition and perform any necessary maintenance.
4.  Verify the driver's experience and qualifications for towing.
5.  Review and update the form as necessary during your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-Inspection** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist`, `note`, not required): Check the vehicle and trailer condition before the trip to prevent any issues that may arise during towing.
* **Roadside Checklist** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_roadside`, `text`, not required): This is a note for drivers to record their findings during a road-side inspection, such as checking the vehicle and trailer conditions, weather conditions, and weight distribution.
* **Regular Maintenance** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_maintenance`, `select_one`, not required): Check if regular maintenance is necessary for the trailer, such as lubricating the trailer's moving parts, checking the electrical connections, and more.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_vehicle_condition`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the vehicle condition from Good, Fair, Poor, or N/A if it's not applicable.
* **Driver Experience** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_driver_experience`, `select_one`, not required): Check the driver experience and qualifications by selecting Experienced, Inexperienced, or N/A if it's not applicable.
* **Weather Conditions** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_weather_conditions`, `date`, not required): Note the weather conditions before towing, such as temperature, wind, or rain.
* **Trailer Condition** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_trailer_condition`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check the trailer condition from Good, Fair, Poor, or N/A if it's not applicable.
* **Traction** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_traction`, `text`, not required): Record your observations about the vehicle's traction before towing, such as whether it has enough traction or not.
* **Weight Distribution** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_weight_distribution`, `select_one`, not required): Check if the weight distribution is within the limits or not.
* **Brakes** (`trailer_towing_safety_checklist_brakes`, `text`, not required): Check the brake functionality before towing, such as whether they are functioning properly or not.

## Tips
- Always review and update this form during your trip to ensure the safety of the vehicle and trailer.
- If any issues are found, address them before continuing to tow.
- This form is a critical tool for drivers to ensure their safety and the safety of others on the road.
