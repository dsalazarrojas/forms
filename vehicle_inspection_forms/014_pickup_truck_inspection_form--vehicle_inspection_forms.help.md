# pickup_truck_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the inspection of a pickup truck. It's designed to gather details about the vehicle's engine, transmission, brakes, tires, safety features, and body damage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your knowledge about the pickup truck you're inspecting.
2. Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle you're inspecting.
* **Engine** (`engine_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the engine's condition, including any issues or concerns.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, `text`, optional): Report on the transmission's condition, including any problems or needed repairs.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, `text`, optional): Assess the condition of the brakes, including any issues or concerns.
* **Tires** (`tires`, `text`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the tires, including any needed replacements or repairs.
* **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, `text`, optional): Inspect and report on the vehicle's safety features, including any issues or concerns.
* **Body Damage** (`body_damage`, `text`, optional): Look for any visible damage to the vehicle's body and report on it.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to provide the best information you can, or ask for clarification if needed.
* This form is for general inspection purposes only. If you're looking for a comprehensive inspection report, please use a more detailed form or consult a professional mechanic.
