# Truck Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect a truck for any potential issues, ensuring that all critical components are in good working condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the corresponding sections as you find them. Each page represents a different area of the truck to be inspected. You will be asked to report any issues you find with the truck's tires, brakes, lights, mirrors, and fluid check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Tires
• **Vehicle Tires** (`vehicle_tires`, `text`, not required): This is the first section of the form where you need to report any issues with the truck's tires. Check the condition of the tires, including any signs of wear, damage, or uneven wear. You don't need to take a photo or provide a detailed explanation, just mark any issues you find.

### Vehicle Brakes
• **Vehicle Brakes** (`vehicle_brakes`, `text`, not required): This section is for reporting any issues with the truck's brakes. Check the condition of the brake pads, drums, or discs for any signs of wear, scoring, or damage. Like the previous section, you don't need to provide any supporting documentation, just mark any issues you find.

### Vehicle Lights
• **Vehicle Lights** (`vehicle_lights`, `text`, not required): Here, you will report any issues with the truck's lights, including headlights, taillights, brake lights, and turn signals. Check for any damage, wear, or malfunctioning lights. Again, just mark any issues you find.

### Vehicle Mirrors
• **Vehicle Mirrors** (`vehicle_mirrors`, `text`, not required): This section is for reporting any issues with the truck's mirrors. Check for any damage, wear, or malfunctioning mirrors. Just like the previous sections, mark any issues you find.

### Fluid Check
• **Fluid Check** (`vehicle_fluid_check`, `text`, not required): In this last section, you will report any issues with the truck's fluid levels, including engine oil, transmission fluid, and brake fluid. Check the dipstick or the gauge to ensure the levels are at the recommended ranges. Just mark any issues you find.

## Tips
- Be thorough in checking each section to ensure a comprehensive inspection.
- Take your time to carefully check each area of the truck.
- If you are unsure about any issue, mark it and take a photo or note for further reference.
