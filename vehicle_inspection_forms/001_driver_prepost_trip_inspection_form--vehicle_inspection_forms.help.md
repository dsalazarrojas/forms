# Driver PrePost Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on a driver's pre-post trip inspection, which is essential for identifying potential risks and issues that may arise during a trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the following questions in the designated fields:

1. **Driver Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details that may be required by your organization or fleet manager.
2. **Vehicle Condition**: Describe the overall condition of your vehicle, including any pre-existing issues or defects that may impact its performance or safety.
3. **Vehicle Status**: Report any recent changes or updates to your vehicle's status, such as maintenance, repairs, or modifications.
4. **Trip Details**: Provide information about your planned route, including destination, expected duration, and any notable features or hazards along the way.
5. **Notes**: Record any additional comments or observations about your trip, including weather conditions, road conditions, or other factors that may impact your journey.
