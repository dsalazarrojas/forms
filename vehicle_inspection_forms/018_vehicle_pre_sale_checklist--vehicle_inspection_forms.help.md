Vehicle Pre Sale Checklist - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
The purpose of the Vehicle Pre Sale Checklist form is to gather information about a vehicle before it is sold, which will help the seller or inspector to verify the vehicle's details and ensure a smooth sales process. This form aims to collect data on the vehicle's make, model year, mileage, condition, any damages, notes, price, seller, sold date, and inspector's details. This information is crucial for the vehicle's sale, as it helps to identify potential issues and ensures that the vehicle meets the necessary standards for sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Steps to Follow

*   Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
*   Select the vehicle's year from the year input field.
*   Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
*   Choose the vehicle's condition from the provided options.
*   Select any existing damages or issues from the "Vehicle Damage" field.
*   Enter any notes or comments about the vehicle in the "Vehicle Notes" field.
*   Enter the vehicle's price in the "Vehicle Price" field.
*   Select the person or organization selling the vehicle from the provided options.
*   Enter the date the vehicle was sold in the "Vehicle Sold Date" field.
*   Enter the name and any other details of the vehicle inspector in the "Vehicle Inspector" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda.
*   **Vehicle Year**: Select the year of the vehicle, e.g., 2015, 2010, 2018.
*   **Vehicle Mileage**: Enter the total mileage of the vehicle in miles.
*   **Vehicle Condition**: Choose from the options to describe the vehicle's condition, e.g., New, Used, Certified.
*   **Vehicle Damage**: Select any damage or issues the vehicle has, e.g., dented bumper, oil leak, engine issue.
*   **Vehicle Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the vehicle, e.g., previous accidents, recent maintenance.
*   **Vehicle Price**: Enter the vehicle's price in the specified format.
*   **Vehicle Sold By**: Select the person or organization selling the vehicle, e.g., private seller, dealership.
*   **Vehicle Sold Date**: Enter the date the vehicle was sold.
*   **Vehicle Inspector**: Enter the name and any other details of the person inspecting the vehicle.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and honest when completing this form to avoid any potential issues during the sales process.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled to avoid incomplete or missing information.
*   If any field is not relevant or not applicable, leave it blank or indicate it as such.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to verify that all information is accurate and complete.
