# Vehicle Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Valuation Form is used to estimate the value of a vehicle based on its characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Vehicle Year of the vehicle you want to value.
2.  Enter the Vehicle Make of the vehicle.
3.  Enter the Vehicle Model of the vehicle.
4.  Enter the Vehicle VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) of the vehicle.
5.  Enter the Vehicle Engine of the vehicle.
6.  Check any Vehicle Damage that exists on the vehicle.
7.  Provide a rating for the Vehicle Inspector's assessment of the vehicle's condition.
8.  Enter the date of the Vehicle Inspection.
9.  Enter the Vehicle Body Location where the inspection took place.
10.  Describe any Other Damage present on the vehicle.
11.  Enter the Vehicle Engine Number (if applicable).
12.  Enter the vehicle's Year (note: this field is likely a duplicate of the first field, but it is included here for consistency)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, `false`): Enter the year of the vehicle you are valuing.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, `false`): Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g. Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, `false`): Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g. Corolla, Mustang, etc.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, `false`): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle. This is a unique identifier for the vehicle and is usually found on the driver's side dashboard.
* **Vehicle Engine** (`vehicle_engine`, `text`, `false`): Enter the type or description of the vehicle's engine, e.g. 2.0L, V6, etc.
* **Vehicle Damage** (`vehicle_damage`, `text`, `false`): Check any existing damage on the vehicle, e.g. scratches, dents, etc.
* **Vehicle Inspector Rating** (`vehicle_inspector_rating`, `number`, `false`): Provide a rating of the vehicle's condition, e.g. 1-5.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_date`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date of the vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Body Location** (`vehicle_body_location`, `text`, `false`): Enter the location where the vehicle inspection took place, e.g. dealership, private garage, etc.
* **Other Damage** (`vehicle_other_damage`, `text`, `false`): Describe any other damage on the vehicle that is not already listed.
* **Vehicle Engine Number** (`vehicle_engine_number`, `text`, `false`): Enter the engine number or description of the vehicle's engine, if applicable.
* **Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, `false`): Enter the year of the vehicle again, this is likely a duplicate field and may not be necessary to fill out.
