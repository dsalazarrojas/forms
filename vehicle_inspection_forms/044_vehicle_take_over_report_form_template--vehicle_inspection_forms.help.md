# Vehicle Take Over Report Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for a vehicle inspection report, used to monitor and record the condition and status of vehicles under company ownership or use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's mileage, if applicable.
2. Select the current fuel level of the vehicle (Low, Adequate, or High).
3. Check the engine oil level and select the appropriate status (Low, Adequate, or High).
4. Check the coolant fuel level and select the corresponding status (Low, Empty, or Not Checked).
5. Inspect the windshield wipers and select their condition (Clean, Dirty, or Not Applicable).
6. Check the vehicle for any damages and select the status (True or False).
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
8. If applicable, select the status of the vehicle (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
9. Enter the vehicle's make, model, color, year, odometer reading, engine size, transmission, drive type, and any other relevant information.
10. Check the vehicle's insurance status (True or False).
11. Enter any additional vehicle notes or comments.
12. Enter the manager's comments (if any).
13. Sign and date the form with your name and employee name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Mileage** (`1`, `number`, optional): Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Fuel** (`2`, `number`, optional): Enter the current fuel level of the vehicle.
* **Engine Oil** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Check the engine oil level and select the corresponding status (Low, Adequate, or High).
* **Coolant Fuel** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the coolant fuel level and select the corresponding status (Low, Empty, or Not Checked).
* **Windshield Wipers** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Check the condition of the windshield wipers and select their condition (Clean, Dirty, or Not Applicable).
* **Vehicle Damages** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the vehicle for any damages and select the status (True or False).
* **Vehicle Comment** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Employee Name** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the employee's name who is completing the form.
* **Employee Position** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's position if applicable.
* **Employee Department** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the employee's department if applicable.
* **Vehicle Status** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the vehicle (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Vehicle Make** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Color** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Year** (`15`, `number`, optional): Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Odometer** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter the vehicle's odometer reading.
* **Vehicle Engine Size** (`17`, `number`, optional): Enter the vehicle's engine size.
* **Vehicle Transmission** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's transmission type.
* **Vehicle Drive Type** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's drive type.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the vehicle's insurance status (True or False).
* **Vehicle Notes** (`21`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional vehicle notes or comments.
* **Manager Comment** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Signature** (`23`, `text`, optional): Sign the form with your name and employee name.
* **Employee Date** (`24`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Vehicle Tag Number** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's tag number if applicable.
