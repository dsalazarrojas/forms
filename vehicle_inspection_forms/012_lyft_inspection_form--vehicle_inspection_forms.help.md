# Lyft Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lyft Inspection Form is a vehicle inspection form used for inspecting and reporting on vehicle safety and condition. It's likely used for maintenance, repair, or pre-purchase inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1. Fill in the vehicle's information:
        - **vehicle_info**: Enter a brief description of the vehicle, e.g., "Sedan", "SUV", etc.
        - **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
        - **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., "Toyota", "Ford", etc.
        - **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., "Corolla", "Mustang", etc.
    2. Enter the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number):
        - **vin**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number for the vehicle.
    3. Schedule and perform the inspection:
        - **inspection_date**: Select the date of the inspection.
        - **odometer_reading**: Enter the current odometer reading.
    4. Evaluate vehicle safety:
        - **safety_items**: Check all safety items that apply, such as:
            - Yes, No
        5. Provide additional comments or notes:
        - **safety_items_other**: Enter any other safety items not listed above.
        - **inspection_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_info**: A brief description of the vehicle, e.g., "Sedan", "SUV", etc.
* **vehicle_year**: The year of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_make**: The make of the vehicle, e.g., "Toyota", "Ford", etc.
* **vehicle_model**: The model of the vehicle, e.g., "Corolla", "Mustang", etc.
* **vin**: The Vehicle Identification Number for the vehicle.
* **inspection_date**: The date of the inspection.
* **odometer_reading**: The current odometer reading.
* **safety_items**: Check all safety items that apply, such as:
    + Yes, No
* **safety_items_other**: Enter any other safety items not listed above.
* **inspection_notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
