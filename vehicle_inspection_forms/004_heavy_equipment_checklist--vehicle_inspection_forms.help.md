# Heavy Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect inspection data for heavy equipment, including condition, maintenance, and safety aspects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the inspection date for the equipment.
2. Identify the type of equipment being inspected.
3. Report the operating hours of the equipment.
4. Indicate the overall condition of the equipment.
5. Provide information about the equipment's maintenance history.
6. Check if the equipment requires maintenance.
7. Report the last maintenance done on the equipment.
8. Check if the equipment has safety equipment.
9. Check the availability of safety signs on the equipment.
10. Verify the presence of a fire hydrant.
11. Check the fuel level in the equipment.
12. Check the oil level in the equipment.
13. Check the hydraulic fluid level in the equipment.
14. Check the condition of the transmission.
15. Check the condition of the differential.
16. Check the condition of the brakes.
17. Check the condition of the tires.
18. Check the presence and condition of seatbelts.
19. Check the location of safety labels on the equipment.
20. Check the last update of safety labels on the equipment.
21. Check the location of a fire hydrant.
22. Identify the fuel type used by the equipment.
23. Identify the oil type used by the equipment.
24. Report the location of the hydraulic fluid level.
25. Report the last update of the hydraulic fluid level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the equipment inspection.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of equipment being inspected.
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, `number`, optional): Report the operating hours of the equipment.
* **Condition** (`condition`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the overall condition of the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Provide information about the equipment's maintenance history (Yes, No).
* **Maintenance Last** (`maintenance_last`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the equipment requires maintenance (Yes, No).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the equipment has safety equipment (Yes, No).
* **Safety Signs** (`safety_signs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the availability of safety signs on the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Fire Hydrant** (`fire_hydrant`, `select_multiple`, optional): Verify the presence of a fire hydrant (Yes, No).
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, `number`, optional): Check the fuel level in the equipment.
* **Oil Level** (`oil_level`, `number`, optional): Check the oil level in the equipment.
* **Hydraulic Fluid Level** (`hydraulic_fluid_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Report the hydraulic fluid level (High, Low).
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, `select_one`, optional): Check the condition of the transmission (Yes, No).
* **Differential** (`differential`, `select_one`, optional): Check the condition of the differential (Yes, No).
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, `select_one`, optional): Check the condition of the brakes (Yes, No).
* **Tires** (`tires`, `select_one`, optional): Check the condition of the tires (Yes, No).
* **Seatbelts** (`seatbelts`, `select_one`, optional): Check the presence and condition of seatbelts (Yes, No).
* **Safety Labels** (`safety_labels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the location of safety labels on the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Safety Labels Last** (`safety_labels_last`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the last update of safety labels on the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Fire Hydrant Location** (`fire_hydrant_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the location of a fire hydrant (Yes, No).
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the fuel type used by the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Oil Type** (`oil_type`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the oil type used by the equipment (Yes, No).
* **Hydraulic Fluid Level Location** (`hydraulic_fuild_level_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Report the location of the hydraulic fluid level (Yes, No).
* **Hydraulic Fluid Level Last** (`hydraulic_fuild_last`, `select_multiple`, optional): Report the last update of the hydraulic fluid level (Yes, No).
