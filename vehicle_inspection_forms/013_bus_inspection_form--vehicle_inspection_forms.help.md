# Bus Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Inspection Form is designed to collect data on the condition and status of buses during inspections. It is intended for internal use within the organization and is not for external purposes such as publishing or sharing information publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the inspection date and time in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter your name and employee ID in the "Inspector Name" and "Inspector ID" fields.
3. Enter the bus number, model, and license plate number in the "Bus Number", "Bus Model", and "License Plate" fields.
4. Describe the current odometer reading in the "Odometer Reading" field.
5. Select the type of inspection being conducted (e.g., Pre-Trip Inspection, Post-Trip Inspection, etc.) in the "Inspection Type" field.
6. Assess the condition of the bus's exterior, including windows, mirrors, lights, and tires, and select the corresponding option from the drop-down menu.
7. Perform an internal inspection and assess the condition of the bus's interior, including seats, floor condition, and safety equipment.
8. Enter any additional notes or observations in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Header** (`inspection_header`, note, optional): This field is a note field for basic inspection details.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional vehicle information.
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, text, required): Enter the bus number.
* **Bus Model** (`bus_model`, text, required): Enter the bus model.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, text, required): Enter the license plate number.
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, required): Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Inspection Type** (`inspection_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of inspection being conducted.
* **Exterior Check** (`exterior_check`, note, optional): This field is for external vehicle inspection notes.
* **Body Condition** (`body_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the overall body condition of the bus.
* **Windows and Mirrors** (`windows_mirrors`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of windows and mirrors.
* **Lights** (`lights`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of lights.
* **Tires** (`tires`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of tires.
* **Interior Check** (`interior_check`, note, optional): This field is for internal vehicle inspection notes.
* **Seats Condition** (`seats_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of passenger seats.
* **Floor Condition** (`floor_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of the floor and aisles.
* **Emergency Exits** (`emergency_exits`, select_one, required): Assess whether the emergency exits are functional.
* **HVAC System** (`hvac_system`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of the HVAC system.
* **Mechanical Check** (`mechanical_check`, note, optional): This field is for mechanical systems inspection notes.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the engine performance.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of the brakes.
* **Steering** (`steering`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of the steering system.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of safety equipment.
* **Defects Found** (`defects_found`, select_one, required): Select whether any defects were found.
* **Defect Description** (`defect_description`, text, optional): Describe any defects found.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one, required): Assess the vehicle's status (approved for service, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or observations.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If any defects are found, please describe them in detail in the "Defect Description" field.
* If there are any additional notes or observations, please enter them in the "Additional Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or refer to the organization's inspection guidelines.
