# Missing Loyalty Points Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missing Loyalty Points Claim Form is used to gather information from customers who are experiencing issues with their loyalty points. This form helps to verify the customer's claim and resolve the issue in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the store information where you made your purchase.
2. Select the loyalty program you are associated with from the provided options.
3. Enter the date of your purchase.
4. Provide details about the product you purchased.
5. Enter the number of points you believe you earned.
6. Upload a proof of purchase or provide a description of the purchase.
7. Select the status of your claim (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about your claim.
9. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
10. Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Information** (`store_info`, text, required): Enter the store where you made your purchase. This will help us locate your purchase and verify your claim.
* **Loyalty Program** (`loyalty_program`, select_one, required): Select the loyalty program you are associated with from the options provided. This will help us determine which program's points you are claiming.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date you made your purchase. This will help us verify the purchase date and ensure you are eligible for the points.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, required): Provide details about the product you purchased, such as the product name and quantity.
* **Points Earned** (`points_earned`, number, required): Enter the number of points you believe you earned. This will help us verify the points balance.
* **Proof of Purchase** (`purchase_proof`, text, required): Upload a photo or description of your proof of purchase. This will help us verify your purchase and award points accordingly.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your claim (Pending, Approved, or Rejected). This will help us track the progress of your claim.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about your claim. This will help us understand your situation and resolve the issue.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your claim.
