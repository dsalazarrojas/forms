# Toll Fee Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toll Fee Claim Form is a tool used to report toll fee discrepancies or issues. It allows users to provide detailed information about the incident, including the category of the claim, description of the incident, and any relevant input fields that may support their claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of your claim (e.g., parking, toll gate, or highway tolls).
2. Provide a brief description of the incident.
3. Fill out the input fields as required to support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Toll Fee Claim Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your claim.
- **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your claim (e.g., Claim Forms).
- **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
- **Input 1** (`form_input_1`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the incident.
- **Input 2** (`form_input_2`, text, required): Enter any additional relevant information about the incident.
- **Input 3** (`form_input_3`, text, required): Enter any further details about the incident.
- **Input 4** (`form_input_4`, text, required): Enter any subsequent details about the incident.
- **Input 5** (`form_input_5`, text, required): Enter any other important information about the incident.
- **Input 6** (`form_input_6`, text, required): Enter any final details about the incident.
- **Input 7** (`form_input_7`, text, required): Enter any last details about the incident.
- **Input 8** (`form_input_8`, text, required): Enter any final information about the incident.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
