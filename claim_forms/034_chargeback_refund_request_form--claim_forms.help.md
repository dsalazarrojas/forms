# Chargeback Refund Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help merchants capture information to support a chargeback refund request. Please provide accurate and complete details to support your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact email address so we can keep you informed about the status of your request.
2.  Provide the transaction ID to help us identify the original transaction.
3.  Enter the date of the original transaction.
4.  Specify the total amount to be refunded.
5.  Clearly state the reason why you are disputing the charge.
6.  Attach any supporting documents that back up your claim.
7.  Indicate your preferred resolution outcome.
8.  Sign and date your declaration to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name (requester_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Transaction ID (transaction_id)**: Enter the exact ID of the original transaction.
* **Transaction Date (transaction_date)**: Enter the date of the original transaction (if known).
* **Amount Disputed (amount_disputed)**: Enter the total amount you are seeking to be refunded.
* **Reason for Dispute (reason_for_dispute)**: Clearly state the reason why you are disputing the charge (e.g., incorrect charge, duplicate charge, or other).
* **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Attach any receipts or evidence that support your claim.
* **Preferred Resolution (preferred_resolution)**: Select your desired outcome for the chargeback (Full refund, Partial refund, Credit, or Other).
* **Declaration Signature (declaration_signature)**: Sign and date your declaration to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
