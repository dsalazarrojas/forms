# Car Accident Claim Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for claiming damages or losses related to a car accident. It is required to be filled out accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the claimant information (fields 4-10), providing your accurate and complete contact information.
2.  Fill out the vehicle information (fields 14-18), providing accurate details about the vehicle involved in the accident.
3.  Describe the accident in detail (fields 20-26), including date, time, location, and type of accident.
4.  Provide information about any other vehicle involved (fields 27-31), if applicable.
5.  Describe any damage or injuries (fields 33-35), including vehicle damage description and injury details.
6.  Confirm that you have filed a police report (fields 36-37), if applicable.
7.  Declare under oath that the information provided is true and accurate (field 38).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Your first name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Your last name
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Your birthday
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license`): License number
* **License State/Province** (`license_state`): Issuing state
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Your email
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Contact number
* **Insurance Company Name** (`insurance_info`): Insurance provider
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Policy number
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Year manufactured
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Brand
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Model
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vehicle_vin`): VIN
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate`): Plate number
* **Date of Accident** (`accident_date`): When the accident occurred
* **Time of Accident** (`accident_time`): Approximate time
* **Location of Accident** (`accident_location`): Street address or description
* **City** (`city`): City
* **State/Province** (`state`): State or province
* **Type of Accident** (`accident_type`): Select one of the provided options
* **Detailed Description of Accident** (`accident_description`): What happened
* **Other Vehicle Involved?** (`other_vehicle_involved`): Select yes or no
* **Other Driver Name** (`other_driver_name`): If applicable
* **Other Vehicle Make, Model, Year** (`other_vehicle_info`): Description
* **Other Vehicle License Plate** (`other_license_plate`): Plate number
* **Vehicle Damage Description** (`vehicle_damage`): Describe the damage
* **Any Injuries to Persons?** (`injuries`): Select yes or no
* **Injury Details** (`injury_details`): Describe the injuries
* **Police Report Filed?** (`police_report`): Select yes, no, or in progress
* **Police Report Number (if filed)** (`police_report_number`): Report number
* **I Declare Under Oath** (`affidavit_declaration`): Select yes or no
