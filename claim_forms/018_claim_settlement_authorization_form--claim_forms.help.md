# Claim Settlement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for policyholders to grant authorization for a representative to act on their behalf in settlement claims.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your information as the policyholder:
	* Policyholder Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your policy.
	* Policy Number: Enter your insurance policy number.
	* Mailing Address: Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
	* Email Address: Enter a contact email for correspondence.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact number.
	* Date of Loss or Incident: Enter the date when the loss or incident occurred.
	* Type of Loss: Select the type of claim from the list.
	* Date of Signature: Enter the date of your signature.
2. Fill in your representative's information:
	* Authorized Representative Name: Enter the full name of the representative you are authorizing.
	* Relationship to Policyholder: Select the relationship to the policyholder from the list.
	* Representative's Address: Enter the representative's mailing address (if applicable).
	* Representative Email: Enter the representative's contact email.
	* Representative Phone: Enter the representative's contact number (if applicable).
3. Specify the scope of authorization for the representative:
	* Select the powers of authorization you are granting from the list.
4. Confirm authorization details:
	* Can Representative Receive Payments: Select whether the representative can receive payments on your behalf.
	* Authorization Start Date: Enter the start date of the authorization.
	* Authorization End Date: Enter the end date of the authorization (optional).
5. Confirm understanding and verification:
	* Do You Understand Authorization Can Be Revoked: Select whether you understand that the authorization can be revoked in writing at any time.
	* Have You Provided Identification: Select whether you have provided identification or will provide it separately.
6. Optional fields:
	* Witness Name: Enter the name of a witness (if applicable).
	* Witness Signature Date: Enter the date of the witness's signature (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your policy.
* **Policy Number (2)**: Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Claim Number (3)**: Enter the claim number (if already assigned).
* **Mailing Address (4)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter a contact email for correspondence.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Date of Loss or Incident (7)**: Enter the date when the loss or incident occurred.
* **Type of Loss (8)**: Select the type of claim from the list.
* **Authorized Representative Name (9)**: Enter the full name of the representative you are authorizing.
* **Relationship to Policyholder (10)**: Select the relationship to the policyholder from the list.
* **Representative Address (11)**: Enter the representative's mailing address (if applicable).
* **Representative Email (12)**: Enter the representative's contact email.
* **Representative Phone (13)**: Enter the representative's contact number (if applicable).
* **Scope of Authorization (14)**: Select the powers of authorization you are granting.
* **Can Representative Receive Payments (15)**: Select whether the representative can receive payments on your behalf.
* **Authorization Start Date (16)**: Enter the start date of the authorization.
* **Authorization End Date (17)**: Enter the end date of the authorization (optional).
* **Do You Understand Authorization Can Be Revoked (18)**: Select whether you understand that the authorization can be revoked in writing at any time.
* **Have You Provided Identification (19)**: Select whether you have provided identification or will provide it separately.
* **Witness Name (20)**: Enter the name of a witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Signature Date (21)**: Enter the date of the witness's signature (if applicable).
* **Policyholder Signature (22)**: Sign the form by submitting this, you are authorizing the representative to act on your behalf.
* **Date of Signature (23)**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Additional Comments or Restrictions (24)**: Enter any limitations on the authorization.

## Tips
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure proper submission.
- Make sure to review and understand each field before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact our customer support for assistance.
