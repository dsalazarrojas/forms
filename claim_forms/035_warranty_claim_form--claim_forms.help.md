# Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Claim Form is used to gather information from customers regarding warranty claims for products they have purchased. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure efficient processing of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Customer Info** details, including any relevant information about yourself and your purchase.
2. Provide a description of your **Product Details** to help us understand the issue.
3. Select the **Claim Type** that best describes the problem you're experiencing.
4. Describe the **Product Condition** as accurately as possible.
5. Enter the **Date of Purchase**, if known.
6. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** for us to reach out to you.
7. Enter your **Email** address or other contact information.
8. Provide your **Phone number** for any follow-up communication.
9. Enter your **Address** for any potential delivery purposes.
10. Write a detailed **Message** describing the issue and any supporting details.
11. Sign your **Customer Signature** to confirm your submission.
12. Date and sign the form by entering your **Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required): This section is for you to provide your name, address, and contact information.
* **Product Details** (Product Details, text, required): Describe the product you are claiming, including the product name, model number, and any other relevant details.
* **Claim Type** (Claim Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of claim you are submitting, which can be:
	+ Faulty or damaged product
	+ Incorrect or missing instructions
	+ Not as described
	+ Other (please describe)
* **Product Condition** (Product Condition, text, optional): Describe the condition of the product at the time of purchase, including any pre-existing issues or defects.
* **Date of Purchase** (Date of Purchase, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the product, if known.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted regarding your claim, which can be:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can reach out to you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address for any potential delivery purposes.
* **Message** (Message, note, optional): Write a detailed description of the issue and any supporting details.
* **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your submission.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Date and sign the form by entering your date of signature.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure efficient processing of your claim.
- Ensure you have reviewed our warranty policy and that your claim falls within the scope of our warranty terms.
- If you have already submitted a claim for the same issue, please do not submit another one. Duplicate submissions will not be accepted.
