## Real Estate Settlement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Settlement Claim Form is used to collect information for real estate settlement claims.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Claim Details section with a brief description of the claim.
2. Provide the seller's and buyer's information in the respective sections.
3. Enter the property information, including the address and loan details.
4. Select the supporting documents that are relevant to the claim.
5. Sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): This section is for a brief description of the claim.
* **Seller** (`seller_info`, text, required): Enter the seller's name and contact information.
* **Buyer** (`buyer_info`, text, required): Enter the buyer's name and contact information.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, required): Enter the property address and any other relevant property information.
* **Closing Details** (`closing_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the closing, including the date and any other relevant information.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required): Select the supporting documents that are relevant to the claim.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, text, optional): This section is for any additional signatures required.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional): Enter any review notes.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, text, required): The seller must sign and date this section.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, text, required): The buyer must sign and date this section.
* **Broker Signature** (`broker_signature`, text, required): The broker must sign and date this section.
* **Settlement Company Signature** (`settlement_company_signature`, text, required): The settlement company must sign and date this section.
* **Property Manager Signature** (`property_manager_signature`, text, required): The property manager must sign and date this section.
* **Other Party** (`other_party`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of any other party involved in the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount claimed.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of claim (Real Estate Settlement or Other).
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the property address.
* **Loan Number** (`loan_number`, text, required): Enter the loan number.
* **Loan Date** (`loan_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the loan.
* **Other Party Info** (`other_party_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of any other party involved in the claim.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the claim (Pending, Closed, or Denied).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of supporting document (Proof of Loss, Proof of Damage, or Proof of Repair).
