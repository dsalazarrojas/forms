# windshield_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a windshield damage event in order to process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the following pages and fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **windshield_claim_form**: This is the main heading of the form and should be filled out with the title of the windshield claim.
	+ Required: No
* **date_of_crash**: Enter the date of the windshield crash in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
	+ Required: No
* **time_of_crash**: Enter the time of the windshield crash in the format of 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
	+ Required: No
* **location_of_crash**: Enter the location where the windshield crash occurred.
	+ Required: No
* **number_of_victims**: Enter the number of people involved in the windshield crash.
	+ Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team.
- If you have any additional information about the windshield damage, please include it in the comments section (not shown here).
