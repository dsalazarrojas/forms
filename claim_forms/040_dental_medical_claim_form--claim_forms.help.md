# Dental Medical Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Medical Claim Form is used to collect information for dental medical claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including their name and contact details.
2. Select the type of dental professional who treated the patient (Dentist, Orthodontist, or Pediatrician).
3. Enter the claimant's name.
4. Provide details about the claim, such as the claim status and date.
5. Enter the claim amount.
6. Provide the claimant's address, phone number, and email.
7. Specify the insurance company, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name and contact details to ensure proper communication and record-keeping.
* **Dental Information** (`dental_information`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of dental professional who treated the patient, such as Dentist, Orthodontist, or Pediatrician.
* **Claimant** (`claimant`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person making the claim.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the claim, including any relevant details about the treatment or service provided.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the claim, such as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the claim was made or the date the service was provided.
* **Claim Time** (`claim_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount claimed.
* **Claim Address** (`claim_address`, text, required: false): Enter the claimant's address.
* **Claim Phone** (`claim_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claim Email** (`claim_email`, email, required: false): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, required: false): If the claim is related to an insurance company, enter the name of the insurance company.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays or errors in the processing of the claim.
* Double-check the information for spelling and formatting errors before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or healthcare provider for clarification.
