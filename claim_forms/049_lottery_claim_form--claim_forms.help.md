# Lottery Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lottery Claim Form is a tool used to gather information from claimants who wish to claim a prize in a lottery drawing. This form is designed to collect the necessary details from the claimant and confirm their declaration to ensure the accuracy of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the designated fields, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the ticket numbers you wish to claim.
3. Confirm your name and ticket numbers to ensure accuracy.
4. Confirm that you understand the terms and conditions of this form.
5. Review your entries carefully and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Details** (`claimant_details`, text, optional): Enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication regarding your claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you regarding your claim.
* **Ticket Numbers** (`ticket_numbers`, number, optional): Enter the ticket numbers you wish to claim.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Confirm Ticket Numbers** (`confirm_ticket_numbers`, select_one, optional):
	+ TICKET NUMBER 1
	+ TICKET NUMBER 2
	+ TICKET NUMBER 3
Select the ticket numbers you wish to confirm.
* **Confirm Claimant Name** (`confirm_claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name again to confirm its accuracy.
* **Claimant Declaration** (`claimant_declaration`, text, optional): Enter a brief statement declaring your claim.
* **Confirm Claimant Declaration** (`confirm_claimant_declaration`, select_one, optional):
	+ I understand the terms and conditions of this form.
	+ I do not understand the terms and conditions of this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Review your entries carefully to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
* Submission of this form does not guarantee the accuracy of your claim.
* You will be contacted by our team to confirm your claim and verify your identity.
