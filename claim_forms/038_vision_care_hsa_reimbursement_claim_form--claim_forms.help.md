# Vision Care HSA Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim Vision Care Health Savings Account (HSA) reimbursement benefits.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: <p>A text box where you can enter any additional information you want to provide for your claim.</p>
* **Claim Type**: <p>Select one of the following:
  + Claim for Medical Expenses: For claims related to medical expenses.
  + Claim for Prescription Eyewear: For claims related to prescription eyewear.
  + Claim for Vision Care Benefits: For claims related to Vision Care benefits.</p>
* **Date of Service**: <p>The date on which the Vision Care services were rendered.</p>
* **Claim Amount**: <p>The total amount of the claim.</p>
* **Submitted By**: <p>A text box where you can enter the name of the person submitting the claim.</p>
* **Claim Status**: <p>Select one of the following:
  + Pending: The claim is currently pending review.
  + Approved: The claim has been approved.
  + Denied: The claim has been denied.
  + Resubmitted: The claim has been resubmitted.</p>
* **Claim Notes**: <p>A text box where you can enter any additional comments or information about the claim.</p>
* **Email**: <p>The email address of the person submitting the claim.</p>
* **Phone**: <p>The phone number of the person submitting the claim.</p>
* **Medical Record Number**: <p>The ID number of the medical record associated with the claim.</p>
* **Medical Provider**: <p>The name of the medical provider who rendered the services.</p>
* **Doctor Signature**: <p>A text box where the doctor can enter their signature.</p>
* **Claimant Signature**: <p>A text box where the claimant can enter their signature.</p>
* **Claimant Name**: <p>The name of the claimant.</p>
* **Claimant Address**: <p>The address of the claimant.</p>
* **Claimant Phone**: <p>The phone number of the claimant.</p>
* **Claimant Email**: <p>The email address of the claimant.</p>
* **Claimant Date of Birth**: <p>The date of birth of the claimant.</p>
* **Claimant Relationship**: <p>The relationship between the claimant and the employee.</p>
* **Claimant Employee Number**: <p>The ID number of the claimant's employee.</p>
* **Claimant Department**: <p>The department of the claimant's employee.</p>
