# Homeowners Insurance Claim Premium Increase Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to dispute claims of premium increases in a homeowners insurance policy. Fill out the form to inform the agency of your concerns and provide necessary information about your claim.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your concerns are addressed.
2. Ensure you have the necessary documents and information to support your claim.
3. Submit the form to the agency for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`): Enter your name as it is listed on your policy.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your dispute.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`): Select the type of claim you are disputing:
	+ Fire
	+ Theft
	+ Vandalism
	+ Other
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`): Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`): Enter the amount of the premium increase you are disputing.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter your policy number for reference.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`): Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`): Enter the end date of your policy.
* **Annual Premium** (`premium`): Enter your current annual premium.
* **Original Premium** (`original_premium`): Enter your original annual premium.
* **Current Premium** (`current_premium`): Enter the premium you are disputing.
* **Increase Amount** (`increase_amount`): Enter the amount of the premium increase you are disputing.
* **Supporting Document 1** (`docs1`): Attach any supporting documents related to your claim (e.g. proof of damage).
* **Supporting Document 2** (`docs2`): Attach any additional supporting documents related to your claim.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or details about your claim.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Select the status of your submission:
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Canceled
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`): Enter the name of the agency handling your claim.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`): Enter the email address of the agency handling your claim.
* **Agency Phone** (`agency_phone`): Enter the phone number of the agency handling your claim.

Note: This form is for internal agency use, and submissions will be reviewed by agency personnel to determine the validity of the claim. Ensure your submission is accurate and complete for a thorough review.
