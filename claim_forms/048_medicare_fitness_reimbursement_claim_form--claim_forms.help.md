# Medicare Fitness Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicare Fitness Reimbursement Claim Form is used to submit claims for medical services rendered to Medicare members. This form should be completed by the member or plan administrator to initiate the reimbursement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Medicaid Plan ID in the `medicaid_plan_id` field.
2. Provide your name in the `member_name` field.
3. Enter your plan administrator's name in the `plan_administrators_name` field.
4. Select the type of medical service provided in the `medical_service_type` field.
5. Enter the date of service in the `date_of_service` field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
6. Upload a receipt for the medical service provided in the `receipt_upload` field.
7. Upload any relevant medical documents in the `medical_document_upload` field.
8. Enter the amount claimed in the `claim_amount` field.
9. Select who the claim is being submitted by, either the Member, Plan Administrator, or Both in the `submit` field.
10. Optionally, provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the `email` and `phone` fields.
11. Enter your address in the `address` field.
12. Add any additional comments or notes in the `notes` field.
13. Enter your fax number in the `fax` field.
14. Enter your name in the `submitter_name` field.
15. Enter your phone number in the `submitter_phone` field.
16. Enter your email in the `submitter_email` field.
17. Enter your address in the `submitter_address` field.
18. Enter the plan administrator's email in the `plan_administrators_email` field.
19. Enter the plan administrator's phone number in the `plan_administrators_phone` field.
20. Enter the plan administrator's address in the `plan_administrators_address` field.
21. Enter the plan administrator's name in the `plan_admin_name` field.
22. Enter your plan ID in the `plan_id` field.
23. Finally, click the submit button to initiate the reimbursement process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **medicaid_plan_id** (id: 1, required): Enter your Medicaid plan ID.
* **member_name** (id: 2, required): Enter your name.
* **plan_administrators_name** (id: 3, required): Enter your plan administrator's name.
* **medical_service_type** (id: 4, required): Select the type of medical service provided.
* **date_of_service** (id: 5, required): Enter the date of service in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **receipt_upload** (id: 6, required): Upload a receipt for the medical service provided.
* **medical_document_upload** (id: 7, required): Upload any relevant medical documents.
* **claim_amount** (id: 8, required): Enter the amount claimed.
* **submit** (id: 9, required): Select who is submitting the claim, either the Member, Plan Administrator, or Both.
* **email** (id: 10, optional): Enter your email.
* **phone** (id: 11, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (id: 12, optional): Enter your address.
* **note** (id: 13, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **fax** (id: 14, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **comments** (id: 15, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **submitter_name** (id: 16, optional): Enter your name.
* **submitter_phone** (id: 17, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_email** (id: 18, optional): Enter your email.
* **submitter_address** (id: 19, optional): Enter your address.
* **plan_administrators_email** (id: 20, optional): Enter the plan administrator's email.
* **plan_administrators_phone** (id: 21, optional): Enter the plan administrator's phone number.
* **plan_administrators_address** (id: 22, optional): Enter the plan administrator's address.
* **plan_admin_name** (id: 23, optional): Enter the plan administrator's name.
* **plan_id** (id: 24, optional): Enter your plan ID.
* **submit** (id: 25, required): Select who is submitting the claim, either the Member, Plan Administrator, or Both.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information and plan administrator's contact information for accuracy.
* Ensure that you have all necessary documents and receipts uploaded before submitting the claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your plan administrator for assistance.
