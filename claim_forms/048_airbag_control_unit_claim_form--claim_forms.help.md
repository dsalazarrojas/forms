# Airbag Control Unit Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airbag Control Unit Claim Form is designed to collect information related to an incident involving an airbag deployment in a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Claimant Name", "Mailing Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Provide your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) and Make and Model.
3. Specify the date of the incident and whether the airbag deployed.
4. Describe what happened in the "Description of Issue" field.
5. If applicable, specify where the vehicle is currently, and upload any supporting documents.
6. Sign your claim with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the owner of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Mailing Address** (text, required): Provide your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (text, required): A 17-character code unique to your vehicle. Enter it here.
* **Make and Model** (text, required): Specify your vehicle's make and model.
* **Model Year** (number, required): Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Date of Incident** (date, required): Specify the date of the incident.
* **Did Airbag Deploy?** (select_one, required): Indicate whether the airbag deployed during the incident. 
* **Was SRS Light On Prior?** (select_one, required): If the airbag deployed, indicate whether the warning light on the dashboard was on prior to the incident.
* **Description of Issue** (text, required): Describe what happened during the incident.
* **Repair Shop** (text, optional): If the vehicle is at a repair shop, enter the shop's name.
* **Document Uploads** (note, optional): You can upload documents to support your claim, or email them to claims@auto.com.
* **Signature** (text, required): Type your full name to sign the claim.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a claim for the same incident.
* If you are unsure about any information, please seek guidance from relevant authorities or departments.
