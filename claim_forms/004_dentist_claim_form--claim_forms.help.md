# Dentist Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Dentist Claim Form** is a document that allows dentists to submit claims for reimbursement for services rendered to patients. This form is used to capture important information related to patient care, treatment, and claim details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Patient Information** page, providing details about the patient, such as their name and contact information.
2. Move on to the **Treatment Plan** page, where you will list the services provided to the patient, including procedures and diagnosis.
3. On the **Treatment Costs** page, enter the total cost of the services provided, including any applicable taxes and fees.
4. In the **Payment Preferences** section, specify the preferred payment method and any other relevant payment information.
5. The **Claim Information** page is where you will provide details about the claim, including the claim date and any additional supporting documentation.
6. On the **Claim Details** page, provide a brief description of the claim and any other relevant details.
7. In the **Dentist Information** section, enter your practice's details, such as name and address.
8. Sign and date the **Patient Signature** page, confirming that the information provided is accurate and true.
9. Finally, sign and date the **Dentist Signature** page, confirming that the claim is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Info)** (`patient_info`, Text, **Optional**): This is the first step in completing the form, where you will enter the patient's details, including their name, address, and contact information.
* **Treatment Plan (Treatment Plan)** (`treatment_plan`, Text, **Optional**): On this page, list the services provided to the patient, including procedures and diagnosis.
* **Treatment Costs (Treatment Costs)** (`treatment_costs`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the total cost of the services provided, including any applicable taxes and fees.
* **Payment Preferences (Payment Preferences)** (`payment_preferences`, Text, **Optional**): Specify the preferred payment method and any other relevant payment information.
* **Claim Information (Claim Information)** (`claim_info`, Text, **Optional**): Provide details about the claim, including the claim date and any additional supporting documentation.
* **Claim Details (Claim Details)** (`claim_details`, Text, **Optional**): A brief description of the claim and any other relevant details.
* **Dentist Information (Dentist Info)** (`dentist_info`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your practice's details, such as name and address.
* **Patient Signature (Patient Signature)** (`patient_signature`, Text, **Optional**): Sign and date this page, confirming that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Dentist Signature (Dentist Signature)** (`dentist_signature`, Text, **Optional**): Sign and date this page, confirming that the claim is accurate and complete.
* **Claim Status (Claim Status)** (`claim_status`, Text, **Optional**): This page is for tracking the status of the claim, which will be updated by the claims processor.
