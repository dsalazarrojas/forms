# Unemployment Claim Termination Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for claimants to appeal the termination of their unemployment claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each page in its entirety.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information.
3. Attach any necessary documentation to support your appeal.
4. Fill out the form in a well-structured and clear manner.
5. Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information**: Provide your personal information for verification purposes.
* **Claimant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Claimant Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter your current phone number.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter your current email address.
* **Claim**: Enter a brief description of your unemployment claim.
* **Claim Start Date**: Enter the date your unemployment claim started.
* **Claim Termination Date**: Enter the date your unemployment claim was terminated.
* **Reason for Termination**: Select one of the provided options or describe the reason for your termination in the text field.
* **Reason for Termination (Other)**: Provide a brief explanation if you selected "Other" as the reason for your termination.
* **Claim Number**: Enter the number of your original unemployment claim.
* **Appeal Reason**: Enter a brief description of the reason for your appeal.
* **Appeal Date**: Enter the date you are filing this appeal.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form (your representative or attorney).
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or company that the person submitting this form represents.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter the address of the person or organization submitting this form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide to support your appeal.
* **Review Status**: This field will be updated by our team after reviewing your appeal.
* **Review Comments**: Our team will provide feedback and comments on your appeal after reviewing it.
* **Review Status**: This field will be populated once your appeal has been reviewed.
* **Submitter Notes**: This field is for the submitter to provide any additional information they would like to share with the reviewer.
