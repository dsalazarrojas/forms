# Digital Wallet Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for a digital wallet. Please fill out this form with your information so we can process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address to help us get in touch with you about your claim.
4. Enter your **Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted for more information or to discuss your claim.
5. Enter the **Date** of the event related to your claim.
6. Enter the **Time** of the event related to your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<required>)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (<required>)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (<required>)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Date (<required>)**: Enter the date of the event related to your claim.
* **Time (<required>)**: Enter the time of the event related to your claim.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the contact information provided.
* We'll be in touch with you soon to discuss your claim further.
