# post_office_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from claimants about lost or damaged parcels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **claimant details** section with the claimant's name, contact number, and email.
2. Select the type of claim from the **claim type** dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, provide details about the claim in the **claim details** field.
4. Add any relevant **supporting documents**.
5. Fill in the **post office** details with the name of the post office where the claim was made.
6. Fill in the contact information for the post office **staff**.
7. Sign and date the form with the **signature date** and **signature** fields.
8. Review the form and add any **reviewer notes** if necessary.
9. Sign and date the form with the **reviewer signature** and **reviewer date** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant details**: Enter the claimant's name and contact information.
* **Claim type**: Select the type of claim from the dropdown menu.
* **Claim details**: Provide any additional information about the claim.
* **Supporting documents**: Attach any relevant documents to support the claim.
* **Post office**: Enter the name of the post office where the claim was made.
* **Staff**: Enter the contact information for the post office staff.
* **Staff signature**: Sign and date the form with the post office staff's signature.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the contact information for the reviewer.
* **Reviewer signature**: Sign and date the form with the reviewer's signature.
* **Submitted on**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
