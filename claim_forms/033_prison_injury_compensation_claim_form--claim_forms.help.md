# Prison Injury Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from claimants who have experienced an injury while in prison. The purpose of this form is to assist in the processing of their injury compensation claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the incident in the `Incident Date` field.
2.  Provide the location where the incident occurred in the `Incident Location` field.
3.  Briefly describe the incident in the `Incident Description` field.
4.  List any supporting documents related to the incident in the `Supporting Documents` field.
5.  Provide follow-up information related to the incident in the `Follow Up Information` field.
6.  Enter the name and contact information of the claimant in the `Claimant` fields.
7.  Specify the claimant's relationship to the person reporting the incident in the `Relationship to Claimant` field.
8.  Provide the signature of the claimant in the `Claimant Signature` field.
9.  Enter the name of the claimant in the `Claimant Name` field.
10.  Provide the address of the claimant in the `Claimant Address` field.
11.  Provide the signature of the person reporting the incident in the `Incident Reporter Signature` field.
12.  Enter the name of the person reporting the incident in the `Incident Reporter Name` field.
13.  Enter the job title of the person reporting the incident in the `Incident Reporter Title` field.
14.  Provide contact information for the person reporting the incident in the `Incident Reporter Contact` field.
15.  Specify the relationship between the person reporting the incident and the claimant in the `Incident Reporter Relationship` field.
16.  Enter the name and contact information of the person reporting the incident's supervisor in the `Incident Reporter Supervisor` fields.
17.  Enter any additional notes related to the incident in the `Incident Reporter Notes` field.
18.  If applicable, select the type of injuries experienced by the claimant in the `Claimant Injuries` field.
19.  If not listed, provide a brief description of any other injuries in the `Claimant Injuries Other` field.
