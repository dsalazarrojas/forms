# Carols St Matt's Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim expenses related to church business activities. Please note that you must be a staff member or volunteer to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the Claimant Details section.
2. Select your role within the church in the Role or Position in Church section.
3. Choose the relevant ministry area or department that the expenses relate to.
4. Complete the Expense Summary section by entering the total amount claimed.
5. Enter the date of the claim and the currency in which the expenses were incurred.
6. Complete the Expense Details section by providing a description of each expense, its category, and the date it was incurred.
7. If claiming travel expenses, provide the purpose and total miles traveled.
8. Choose your preferred method of payment and provide the required bank account details if applicable.
9. Declare that these expenses were incurred for church business and have not been previously claimed.
10. Submit the form with any required documentation and supporting evidence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Claimant Details --** (`Claimant Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on our church's records.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address that is registered with our church.
* **Role or Position in Church** (`Role or Position in Church`, select_one, required): Select your role within the church (e.g. Staff, Volunteer, Minister, Committee Member, Other).
* **Ministry Area or Department** (`Ministry Area or Department`, select_one, required): Select the area or department that the expenses relate to (e.g. Worship, Youth, Children, Outreach, Facilities, Administration).
* **-- Expense Summary --** (`Total Amount Claimed`, number, required): Enter the total amount of all expenses claimed.
* **Date of Claim** (`Date of Claim`, date, required): Enter the date that this claim is being made.
* **Currency** (`Currency`, select_one, required): Select the currency in which the expenses were incurred (e.g. GBP, USD, AUD, Other).
* **-- Expense Details --** (`Description of Expense`, text, required): Provide a clear description of each expense, including its category and date.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the expense (e.g. Supplies and Materials, Travel and Mileage, Catering and Refreshments, etc.).
* **Date Expense Incurred** (`Date Expense Incurred`, date, required): Enter the date the expense was incurred as shown on the receipt.
* **Have you attached digital receipts?** (`Have you attached digital receipts?`, select_one, required): If you have uploaded digital receipts, select True; otherwise, select False or N/A.
* **-- Travel and Mileage --** (`Purpose of Travel`, text, required for travel expenses): Provide a clear explanation of the reason for travel.
* **Total Miles Traveled** (`Total Miles Traveled`, number, required for travel expenses): Enter the total miles traveled for the claim.
* **Reimbursement Rate per mile** (`Reimbursement Rate per mile`, number, required for travel expenses): Enter the reimbursement rate per mile as per church policy.
* **-- Payment Method --** (`Preferred Reimbursement Method`, select_one, required): Select the preferred method of payment (e.g. Bank Transfer, Check, Cash).
* **Bank Account Number and Sort Code** (`Bank Account Number and Sort Code`, text, required for bank transfers): Enter your bank account details if applicable.
* **-- Authorization --** (`I declare these expenses were incurred for church business and have not been previously claimed`, select_one, required): Confirm that these expenses were incurred for church business and have not been previously claimed.
* **Treasurer Use Only - Approval Status** (`Treasurer Use Only - Approval Status`, select_one, not required): This field is for office use only.
* **Today's Date** (`Today's Date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
