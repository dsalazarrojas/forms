# Car Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for your car insurance policy. Please fill out the form with your policy information, details about the incident, and any other required documents or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your policyholder information in the "Policyholder Information" section.
2. Provide your policy number and email address in the "Policy Number" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date and time of the incident in the "Date of Incident" and "Time of Incident" fields.
5. Choose the type of incident that occurred in the "Type of Incident" field.
6. Describe the location of the incident in the "Location of Incident" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the damage to your vehicle in the "Description of Damage" field.
8. Indicate if your vehicle is drivable or not in the "Is the vehicle drivable?" field.
9. Check if a police report was filed in the "Was a police report filed?" field.
10. Provide the name of the police precinct or station (if applicable) in the "Police Precinct or Station" field.
11. Select "Yes, I agree" to acknowledge the claim terms in the "I acknowledge the claim terms" field.
12. Enter your name for claim confirmation in the "Type your name for claim confirmation" field.
13. Add any additional comments or requests in the "Any additional comments or requests?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Full Name (full_name_policyholder)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your policy.
* **Policy Number (policy_number_claim)**: Enter your insurance policy ID number.
* **Email Address (email_address_claim)**: Enter your email address for claim status updates.
* **Phone Number (phone_number_claim)**: Enter your primary contact phone number for the claim.
* **Date of Incident (date_of_incident)**: Select the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident (time_of_incident)**: Select the time the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident (incident_type)**: Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g. collision with another vehicle, collision with stationary object, etc.).
* **Location of Incident (location_of_incident)**: Enter the address or description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Damage (damage_description)**: Briefly describe the damage to your vehicle.
* **Is the vehicle drivable? (drivability_status)**: Indicate if your vehicle is currently drivable or not.
* **Was a police report filed? (police_report_filed_claim)**: Check if a police report was filed regarding the incident.
* **Police Precinct or Station (police_precinct_name)**: Enter the name of the police precinct or station (if applicable).
* **I acknowledge the claim terms (claim_acknowledgement)**: Select "Yes, I agree" to acknowledge the claim terms.
* **Type your name for claim confirmation (signature_confirmation_claim)**: Enter your name for claim confirmation.
* **Any additional comments or requests (additional_claim_comments)**: Provide any additional comments or requests regarding the claim.
