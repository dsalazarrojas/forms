# travel-insurance-claim-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for travelers who want to submit claims for travel-related expenses they have incurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your traveler info on **[page 1]**.
2. Provide a brief description of your claim on **[page 2]**.
3. Share your trip details on **[page 3]**.
4. Enter the claim date and claim value on **[page 4]**.
5. Submit the form to complete your claim request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler info** (`traveler_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as a traveler.
* **Claim description** (`claim_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe your claim in a few sentences, including the nature of your claim and any supporting documentation you have.
* **Trip details** (`trip_details`, `text`, required: false): Share information about your trip, such as the destination, dates, and mode of transportation.
* **Claim date** (`claim_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you incurred your claim-related expense.
* **Claim value** (`claim_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the monetary value of your claim, including any receipts or proof of payment.
