# municipal-camera-reimbursement-claim-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process municipal camera reimbursement claims. It's intended for claimants who have incurred expenses related to a city or local government camera and need to request reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Claimant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification document.
*   **Municipal ID**: Enter your municipal ID number or any other identification number assigned to you by the city or local government.
*   **Date of Expense**: Enter the date on which you incurred the expenses related to the camera.
*   **Expense Description**: Provide a detailed description of the expenses incurred related to the camera, including any supporting documentation or proof.
*   **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the total amount of reimbursement you are requesting, to the nearest dollar.
*   **Claim Status**: This field will show the status of your claim if it's been previously submitted. If it's empty or shows "Active", it means your claim is still in progress. If it's "Inactive", your claim has been processed.
*   **Supporting Documents**: If you have any documents or proof related to your claim, enter a description of them here.
*   **Claimant Signature**: Sign your name as a claimant to confirm your identity and claim.
*   **Date of Submission**: Enter the date on which you submitted this form.
*   **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person or department submitting your claim.
*   **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person or department submitting your claim.
*   **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Supporting Document Description**: Enter a description of any supporting documents or proofs you have submitted with your claim.

Note: All fields are case sensitive. Please be accurate and thorough when filling out this form. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult the municipal camera reimbursement policy or contact the relevant municipal authorities for clarification.
