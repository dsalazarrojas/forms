# Supermarket Customer Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about any issues or complaints they have experienced while shopping at our supermarket. The form will be reviewed and used to address any concerns or problems, and to improve the service quality of our store.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your contact information**: On page 1, enter your store location and contact information in the **store_information** field.
2. **Provide your customer information**: On page 1, enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself in the **customer_info** field.
3. **Describe the issue**: On page 1, provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing in the **claim_details** field.
4. **Select the issue type**: On page 2, choose the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g. Issue with Product, Issue with Store, Lost or Stolen Item, etc.) in the **issue_type** field.
5. **Provide more details about the issue**: On page 2, enter any additional details about the issue you selected in the **issue_description** field.
6. **Attach any supporting evidence (optional)**: On page 3, you can attach any images, videos, or audio recordings that support your claim, in the **issue_image**, **issue_video**, and **issue_audio** fields.
7. **Sign and submit**: On page 3, sign and submit the form by entering your signature in the **customer_signature** field, and the store's signature in the **store_signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_information** (Page 1, Text)
	+ Label: store_information
	+ Description: Please enter the store location where you experienced the issue (e.g. store number, address, etc.).
* **customer_info** (Page 1, Text)
	+ Label: customer_info
	+ Description: Please enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself (e.g. contact details).
* **claim_details** (Page 1, Text)
	+ Label: claim_details
	+ Description: Please provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **issue_type** (Page 2, Select One)
	+ Label: issue_type
	+ Description: Please choose the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g. Issue with Product, Issue with Store, Lost or Stolen Item, etc.).
* **issue_description** (Page 2, Text)
	+ Label: issue_description
	+ Description: Please enter any additional details about the issue you selected in the **issue_type** field.
* **issue_image** (Page 3, Text)
	+ Label: issue_image
	+ Description: Please attach any images that support your claim.
* **issue_video** (Page 3, Text)
	+ Label: issue_video
	+ Description: Please attach any videos that support your claim.
* **issue_audio** (Page 3, Text)
	+ Label: issue_audio
	+ Description: Please attach any audio recordings that support your claim.
* **customer_signature** (Page 3, Note)
	+ Label: customer_signature
	+ Description: Please enter your signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **store_signature** (Page 3, Note)
	+ Label: store_signature
	+ Description: Please enter the store representative's signature to acknowledge receipt of the claim.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the issue you are experiencing.
* If you have any supporting evidence (images, videos, or audio recordings), please attach them to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for customer use only. Any unauthorized use of this form will be subject to legal action.
