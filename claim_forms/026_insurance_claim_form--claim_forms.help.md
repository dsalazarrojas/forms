# Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Claim Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals who wish to file an insurance claim. This form will help insurance providers collect the necessary details to process and address the claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please navigate through the pages below. Each page is designed to gather specific information about the claim.
2. Begin with "Claim Details" (page 1), where you will provide the general information about the claim, such as the type of claim and the date it occurred.
3. Move on to "Claimant Info" (page 2), where you will provide your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
4. Next, go to "Claimant Contact" (page 3), where you will provide more information about how to contact you in case the insurance provider needs to reach out.
5. Finally, provide details about the specific type of claim in the "Claims Type" (page 4) section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details (claim_details)**: 
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will provide the general information about the claim.
	+ Please enter the type of claim you are making (e.g., accident, theft, etc.).
	+ Enter the date when the claim occurred.
* **Claimant Info (claimant_info)**: 
	+ This section is for your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
	+ Please enter your name.
	+ Enter your address.
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Contact (claimant_contact)**: 
	+ This section is for more information about how to contact you in case the insurance provider needs to reach out.
	+ Please enter the email address where you can be reached.
	+ Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Claims Type (claims_type)**: 
	+ In this section, please select the specific type of claim you are making (e.g., accident, theft, etc.).

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your claim details to the insurance provider for processing.
