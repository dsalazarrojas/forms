# Contact Lens Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate an insurance claim for contact lenses, ensuring all necessary information is gathered to process the claim correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Insurance Claim For Contact Lenses --**: This is a heading that introduces the form and its purpose.
- **Claimant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your policy or ID.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Insurance policy number**: Enter your insurance policy or member number as provided by your insurance provider.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Insurer name**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: No.
- **Date of purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the contact lenses.
  * Type: Date input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Purchase amount**: Enter the total amount paid for the contact lenses.
  * Type: Number input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Vendor name**: Enter the name of the optical retailer or clinic where you purchased the contact lenses.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: No.
- **Reason for claim**: Describe why you are filing a claim.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Attach receipts and prescriptions via email**: Send scanned receipts or prescriptions to claims email as supporting documents.
  * Type: Note field.
  * Required: No.
- **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for claim follow up.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Contact email**: Enter your email for claim correspondence.
  * Type: Email input field.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Declaration of accuracy**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
  * Type: Select one option.
  * Required: Yes.
- **Additional comments**: Enter any other information supporting the claim.
  * Type: Text input field.
  * Required: No.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Send supporting documents via email if required.
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
