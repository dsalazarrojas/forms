# Critical Illness Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Critical Illness Claim Form is used to file a claim for benefits related to a critical illness. This form collects relevant information from the claimant or their representative to help process the claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read this help guide carefully before starting the form.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before proceeding.
3. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (`claim_form_1`, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional comments or context about your claim.
	+ Please keep your answer concise and relevant to your claim.
* **Date of Claim** (`claim_form_2`, Number, Optional):
	+ This field is used to capture the date when your critical illness began.
	+ Please use the format YYYYMMDD (e.g., 20220101 for January 1, 2022).
* **Yes/No to Claim** (`claim_form_3`, Select Multiple, False):
	+ This field asks if you are making a claim for the critical illness itself or if it was a result of another condition or event.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you are making a claim for the critical illness, and 'No' if it is not related.
* **Event or Condition Date** (`claim_form_4`, Date, False):
	+ This field is used to capture the date when the event or condition that led to the critical illness occurred.
	+ Please use the format YYYYMMDD (e.g., 20220101 for January 1, 2022).
* **Time of Event/Condition** (`claim_form_5`, Time, False):
	+ This field is used to capture the time when the event or condition that led to the critical illness occurred.
	+ Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Additional Comments** (`claim_form_6`, Note, False):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional comments or context about your claim.
	+ Please keep your answer concise and relevant to your claim.
* **Contact Information** (`claim_form_7`, Email, False):
	+ This field is where you can provide your contact information for follow-up questions or requests.
	+ Please use the format `name@email.com`.
* **Claimant's Name** (`claim_form_8`, Text, False):
	+ This field is where you can enter your full name as the claimant or representative.
	+ Please capitalize each name correctly.
* **Claimant's Contact** (`claim_form_9`, Text, False):
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional contact information for the claimant or representative.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have all necessary documentation and supporting information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
