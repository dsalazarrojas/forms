# Guest Lecturer Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for Guest Lecturers to claim reimbursement for expenses related to their work. Please use this form to provide accurate information about your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your contact information on page 1.
2. Provide the necessary claim details on page 2.
3. Select the claim currency and provide any additional information required.
4. Upload any supporting documents if necessary.
5. Complete the claim information fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* **Claim Info** (`claim_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe your claim, including the nature of the expense and why it's related to your work.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, `text`, required: false): Select the type of claim you are making (e.g., accommodation, meals, etc.).
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of your claim.
* **Claim Currency** (`claim_currency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the currency of your claim (AUD or USD).
* **Other Currency** (`claim_currency_other`, `text`, required: false): If you selected 'other' as your claim currency, provide a description of the currency you are using.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting documents for your claim.
* **Claim Info 1** (`claim_info_1`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional claim information.
