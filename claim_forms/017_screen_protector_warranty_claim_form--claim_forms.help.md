# Screen Protector Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for your Screen Protector warranty. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your claim.
3. Provide your Screen Protector serial number, which can be found on the packaging or in your user manual.
4. Attach any supporting documentation that will help us assess your claim.

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your claim.

* **Screen Protector Serial Number** (`screen_protector_serial_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the serial number of your Screen Protector, which can be found on the packaging or in your user manual.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any supporting documentation that will help us assess your claim, please attach it here. This can include pictures, videos, or other relevant files.
