# Worker Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report an incident or injury that may require worker's compensation benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required information as accurately as possible to ensure a timely review and approval of your claim.
- Ensure all required fields (marked as "required") are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
- **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, **required**): Enter your job title as it is currently assigned to you.
- **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, **not required**): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you with any updates or requests for additional information.
- **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you with any updates or requests for additional information.
- **Injury Date** (`injury_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the incident or injury.
- **Injury Time** (`injury_time`, time, **required**): Enter the time of the incident or injury.
- **Injury Location** (`injury_location`, text, **required**): Enter the location of where the incident or injury occurred.
- **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the incident or injury.
- **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, **required**): Enter the total amount of worker's compensation you are claiming.
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your current employer.
- **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, **not required**): Enter the date the claim was made (if known).
- **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the name of any witnesses to the incident or injury.
- **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter the phone number of any witnesses to the incident or injury.
- **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, **required**): Select who is submitting the claim (e.g. employee, manager, HR representative).
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, **not required**): Enter the email address of the submitter.
- **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
- **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the name of the submitter.
- **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, **not required**): Enter the job title of the submitter.
- **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, **not required**): Enter the company of the submitter.
- **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, **not required**): Enter the department of the submitter.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your HR or relevant department for guidance.
- Please fill out the form in a timely manner to avoid delays in the process.
