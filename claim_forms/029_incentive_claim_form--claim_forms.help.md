# Incentive Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incentive Claim Form is used to submit claims for bonuses, awards, or other incentives from your manager. This form helps track and process such claims within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Company** details.
2. Enter the **Claim Date**, which is the date you are claiming the incentive.
3. Provide your **Employee ID** and **Department ID** if applicable.
4. Enter a brief explanation for your **Reason** for claiming the incentive.
5. Select the type of **Claim Type**: Incentive Claim, Other Claim, Reimbursement Claim, or Expense Report Claim.
6. If necessary, provide a brief explanation for your **Manager Approval** status (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
7. Enter your **Notes** for the claim, if any.
8. Add your **Email**, **Phone Number**, **Employee Email**, and **Employee Phone Number** for contact purposes.
9. Provide your **Manager Email** and **Manager Phone Number** if applicable.
10. Click submit to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date you are claiming the incentive.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID, if applicable.
* **Department ID**: Enter the ID of your department, if applicable.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for your claim.
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim (Incentive Claim, Other Claim, Reimbursement Claim, or Expense Report Claim).
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address for contact purposes.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Manager Note**: Add any additional notes for your manager, if necessary.
* **Manager Approval**: Select the status of your manager's approval (Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes for the claim, if necessary.
* **Claim Status**: Select the status of your claim (Approved, Pending, Rejected, In Progress).
