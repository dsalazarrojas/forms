This form is designed to collect information about retail rebates you have earned or are seeking to earn. To ensure that your receipts are processed efficiently, please complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

2. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready before beginning.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the red asterisk \*) carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Receipt** (`upload_receipt`, 1, required): Please attach a photo or scan of your retail rebate receipt.
* **Receipt Details** (`receipt_details`, 2, required): Provide a brief description of the receipt, including any relevant details about the purchase or promotion.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, 3, required): Enter the name of the product or service for which you are seeking a rebate.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, 4, required): Enter the date you purchased the product or service.
* **Rebate Amount** (`rebate_amount`, 5, required): Enter the amount of the rebate you earned.
* **Email** (`email`, 6, required): Enter your email address associated with your retail account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, 7, required): Enter your contact phone number associated with your retail account.
* **Retail Rebate Program** (`retail_rebate_program`, 8, required): Select the retail rebate program you are participating in from the drop-down menu.
