# out_of_network_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for out-of-network claims. It's meant for individuals or organizations to submit claims for reimbursement or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Claim Date of the out-of-network claim.
2. Enter the name of the claim provider.
3. Enter the NPI (National Provider Identifier) of the claim provider.
4. Enter the CPT (Current Procedural Terminology) code associated with the claim.
5. Describe the claim in brief.
6. Enter the claim amount.
7. Enter your name as the submitter.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date when the claim was submitted or is due to be submitted.
* **Claim Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who submitted the claim.
* **Claim Provider NPI**: Enter the 10-digit National Provider Identifier (NPI) of the claim provider.
* **Claim CPT Code**: Enter the Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) code associated with the claim.
* **Claim Description**: Briefly describe the services provided or the reason for the claim.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the total amount claimed for reimbursement.
* **Claim Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the individual or organization submitting the form on behalf of the claim provider.
* **Claim Submitter Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Claim Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Claimant Address**: Enter the address of the claimant, if applicable.
