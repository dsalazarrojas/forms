# Event Sponsorship Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sponsorship Claim Form is a submission form that helps us track event sponsorship claims. If your event has been sponsored by us, please fill out this form to help us verify your attendance and the details of your event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill out all required fields (Name and Email).
*   Enter the start and end dates of your event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   Provide a description of your event sponsorship in the "Event Sponsorship Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (3)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Email (4)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you to confirm your claim.
*   **Event Sponsorship Details (2)**: Please provide a brief description of your event and how we sponsored it.
*   **Event Start Date (6)**: Enter the start date of your event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Event End Date (7)**: Enter the end date of your event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Event Start Time (8)**: Enter the start time of your event, if applicable.
*   **Event End Time (9)**: Enter the end time of your event, if applicable.
*   **Event Sponsorship Description (10)**: Tell us about your event sponsorship, including any details about our organization's involvement.
