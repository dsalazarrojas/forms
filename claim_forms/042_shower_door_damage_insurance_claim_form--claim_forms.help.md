# Shower Door Damage Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers submit a claim for damage to their shower door. Please only fill out this form if you have experienced damage to your shower door and wish to file an insurance claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your name and contact information.
2. Provide your property address.
3. Describe the damage to your shower door.
4. Specify the date and time of the incident.
5. Choose the cause of the damage.
6. Indicate if there are any witness statements.
7. Provide details about any witnesses.
8. Specify the value of the claim.
9. (Optional) Attach images or notes about the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shower Door Damage Insurance Claim Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. Please leave this field blank.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about your claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX (e.g. 123-456-7890).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property where the damage occurred.
* **Description of Damage** (`description_of_damage`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage to your shower door.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): Enter the date of the damage in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour format (e.g. 01:30 PM).
* **Damage Cause** (`damage_cause`, select_one, optional): Choose the cause of the damage from the options:
	+ Accident
	+ Wear and Tear
	+ Natural Disaster
	+ Vandalism
	+ Other
* **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if there are any witness statements:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): If there are witness statements, enter the name of the witness.
* **Claim Value** (`claim_value`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Claim Images** (`claim_images`, text, optional): (Optional) Attach images or notes about the claim.
* **Claim Notes** (`claim_notes`, note, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the claim.

## Tips
* Please provide detailed and accurate information to ensure a smooth claims process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your insurance representative for assistance.
