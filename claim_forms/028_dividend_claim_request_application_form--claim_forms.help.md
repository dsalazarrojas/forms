# Dividend Claim Request Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a dividend claim request. Please fill out the form with your required information to initiate the claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for your claim.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id (`user_id`):** Enter your User ID (if you have one).
* **Claim Date (`claim_date`):** Enter the date you are claiming for.
* **Claim Amount (`claim_amount`):** Enter the amount you are claiming for.
* **Claim Reason (`claim_reason`):** Enter a brief description of the reason for your claim.
* **Contact Name (`contact_name`):** Enter your name (or the name of the contact person).
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`):** Enter your email address or the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`):** Enter your phone number or the phone number of the contact person.
* **Claim Notes (`claim_notes`):** Enter any additional information regarding your claim.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`):** Choose the tool assigned to process this claim.
*   *   Select a tool from the provided options:
    - Jimmy
    - John
    - Jane
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`):** Choose who submitted the claim:
  - User 1
  - User 2
  - User 3

## Tips
* Ensure you have a valid reason for your claim before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant department.
* This form is only for submitting new claims; do not resubmit claims already submitted.
