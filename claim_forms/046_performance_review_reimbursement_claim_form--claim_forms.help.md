# Performance Review Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for reimbursement for expenses incurred during a performance review period. The form is designed to gather information about the employee's expenses, including the period of the performance review, the reimbursement amount, and the approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Performance Review Period** field with the dates of the performance review period for which you are claiming reimbursement.
2. Enter the **Reimbursement Amount** field with the total amount you are claiming as reimbursement for your expenses.
3. Select the **Approved By** field to indicate who has approved your claim. This can be your Manager, Director, or Department Head.
4. Choose the **Expense Type** field to select the type of expense you are claiming reimbursement for. This can be Travel, Meal, or Hotel.
5. You can attach a **Receipt Attachment** if you have a receipt for your expense.
6. Enter your **Employee ID** number in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Review Period**: Enter the dates of the performance review period for which you are claiming reimbursement. For example, "Jan 2023 - Jun 2023".
* **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the total amount you are claiming as reimbursement for your expenses. This should be a numerical value (e.g., 100.00).
* **Approved By**: Select who has approved your claim. Options are Manager, Director, or Department Head.
* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense you are claiming reimbursement for. Options are Travel, Meal, or Hotel.
* **Receipt Attachment**: Attach a receipt for your expense if you have one. This is optional but recommended.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
