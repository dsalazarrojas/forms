# Personal Accident Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who have experienced a personal accident and wish to submit a claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to seek guidance from a designated representative or supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Details (Page 1)** (`claimant_details`, text, optional): Provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Accident Details (Page 2)** (`accident_details`, text, optional): Describe the circumstances of your accident, including the date, time, and location where it occurred.
* **Claimant Contact (Page 3)** (`claimant_contact`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Claimant Injuries (Page 4)** (`claimant_injuries`, text, optional): Describe any injuries you experienced as a result of the accident.
* **Claimant Medical (Page 5)** (`claimant_medical`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information related to your accident.
* **Claimant Occupation (Page 6)** (`claimant_occupation`, text, optional): Indicate your occupation at the time of the accident.
* **Claimant Income (Page 7)** (`claimant_income`, number, optional): Report your current income, including any relevant financial information.
* **Claimant Claim (Page 8)** (`claimant_claim`, text, optional): Describe the specific claim you are making regarding your accident.
* **Claimant Documents (Page 9)** (`claimant_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents related to your claim.
* **Claimant Other Parties (Page 10)** (`claimant_other_parties`, text, optional): List any other parties involved in the accident.
* **Claimant Additional Info (Page 11)** (`claimant_additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your claim.

Note: The "Claimant Income" field is a number field, so it is recommended that you enter your income in numerical format, e.g., 50000, 25000, etc. 

Tips:
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to seek guidance from a designated representative or supervisor.
* Please attach any supporting documents as relevant to your claim.
