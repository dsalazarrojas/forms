# Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form is used to claim reimbursement for business-related mileage expenses. Fill out this form accurately to ensure you receive the correct reimbursement amount.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have already been authorized to use your personal vehicle for business purposes.
2. Carefully fill out each field as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Trip** (`Business Trip`, number, required): Enter the number of business trips you made during the period in question.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Enter the date you began your business trip.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Enter the date you finished your business trip.
* **Purpose** (`Purpose`, text, required): Describe the purpose of your business trip.
* **Odometer Reading** (`Odometer Reading`, number, required): Enter the odometer reading from your vehicle before your trip.
* **Fuel Expenses** (`Fuel Expenses`, number, required): Enter the total cost of fuel expenses for your trip.
* **Personal Use Miles** (`Personal Use Miles`, number, required): Enter the miles driven for personal use during the trip.
* **Business Use Miles** (`Business Use Miles`, number, required): Enter the miles driven for business use during the trip.
* **Total Miles** (`Total Miles`, number, required): This field is automatically calculated and should not be filled out.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (`Reimbursement Amount`, number, required): This field is automatically calculated and should not be filled out.

## Tips

* Ensure you keep accurate records of your odometer reading before and after your trip.
* Attach supporting documentation, such as receipts for fuel expenses, to your claim.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or human resources department.
