# Prospective Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Reimbursement Claim Form is used to collect information from claimants who are requesting reimbursement for expenses incurred in connection with a claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your claimant's name in the "Claimant Name" field.
2. Enter your claimant's email in the "Claimant E-mail" field.
3. Enter your claimant's phone number in the "Claimant Phone" field.
4. Enter the date of the claim in the "Date" field.
5. Enter your claimant's company in the "Claimant Company" field.
6. Enter your claimant's job title in the "Claimant Job Title" field.
7. Enter your claimant's department in the "Claimant Department" field.
8. Upload a receipt related to the claim in the "Receipt Upload" field.
9. Enter a brief description of your claim in the "Claimant Reimbursement Request" field.
10. Enter any additional expenses that you would like to be reimbursed in the "Additional Expenses" field.
11. Enter a summary of your request in the "Claimant Request" field.
12. Enter any additional details about your request in the "Claimant Request Details" field.
13. Check the box in the "Claimant Agreed Terms" field to agree to the terms and conditions.
14. Enter your name and contact information in the "Reviewer Name", "Reviewer E-mail", and "Reviewer Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Name** (1, text, required): This is the name of the claimant submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Date** (2, date, required): This is the date of the claim.
* **Claimant E-mail** (4, email, required): This is the email address of the claimant submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Claimant Phone** (5, text, required): This is the phone number of the claimant submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Claimant Company** (7, text, required): This is the name of the claimant's company.
* **Claimant Job Title** (8, text, required): This is the job title of the claimant submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Claimant Department** (9, text, required): This is the department of the claimant submitting the reimbursement request.
* **Receipt Upload** (10, note, required): This is a field where the claimant can upload a receipt related to the claim.
* **Claimant Reimbursement Request** (11, text, required): This is a brief description of the claimant's reimbursement request.
* **Additional Expenses** (12, text, required): This is a field where the claimant can enter any additional expenses they would like to be reimbursed.
* **Claimant Request** (13, text, required): This is a summary of the claimant's request.
* **Claimant Request Details** (14, text, required): This is a field where the claimant can enter any additional details about their request.
* **Claimant Agreed Terms** (15, text, required): This is a field where the claimant can agree to the terms and conditions of the reimbursement process.
* **Claimant Terms and Conditions** (16, text, required): This is a field where the claimant can enter any additional terms and conditions related to the reimbursement process.
* **Reviewer Name** (17, text, required): This is the name of the reviewer reviewing the claim.
* **Reviewer E-mail** (18, email, required): This is the email address of the reviewer reviewing the claim.
* **Reviewer Phone** (19, text, required): This is the phone number of the reviewer reviewing the claim.
* **Reviewer Notes** (20, note, required): This is a field where the reviewer can enter any additional notes about the claim.
