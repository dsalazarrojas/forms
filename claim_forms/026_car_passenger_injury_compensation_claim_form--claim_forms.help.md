# Car Passenger Injury Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for passengers who have been involved in a motor vehicle accident to submit a claim for compensation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, date of birth, email address, and phone number as requested.
2. Provide details about the accident, including the date and location of the accident.
3. Describe the injuries you sustained in the accident.
4. If you have sought medical treatment, provide the name of the medical facility and the name of your medical provider.
5. If you are claiming lost wages, provide the estimated amount.
6. Sign and confirm your agreement to the terms of service.
7. Add any additional comments or requests you have for your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for claim status updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number for the claim.
* **Date of Accident**: Enter the date the accident occurred.
* **Accident Location**: Enter the address or description of where the accident occurred.
* **Description of the Accident**: Briefly describe how the accident happened.
* **Description of Injuries**: Describe the injuries you sustained in the accident.
* **Have you Sought Medical Treatment?**: Select 'True' if you have sought medical treatment, 'False' otherwise.
* **Medical Facility Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the medical facility you visited.
* **Medical Provider Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Compensation Amount Requested**: If known, enter the amount of compensation you are requesting.
* **Are you Claiming Lost Wages?**: Select 'True' if you are claiming lost wages, 'False' otherwise.
* **Estimated Lost Wages Amount**: If applicable, enter the estimated amount of lost wages.
* **I Acknowledge the Compensation Claim Terms**: Select 'Yes, I agree' to confirm your agreement to the terms of service.
* **Type your Name for Compensation Claim Confirmation**: Enter your name to confirm your claim.
* **Any Additional Comments or Requests?**: Provide any additional comments or requests for your claim.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to process your claim efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Please note that this form is only for passengers who have been involved in a motor vehicle accident.
