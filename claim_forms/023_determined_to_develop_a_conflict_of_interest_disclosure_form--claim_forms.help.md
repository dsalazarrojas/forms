# Determined To Develop A Conflict Of Interest Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information about potential conflicts of interest that may impact a person's ability to perform their job duties impartially. It is used to identify and disclose any relationships that may influence a person's decision-making or actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Answer each question accurately and completely.
3.  Select "None of the Above" if none of the relationships listed apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Conflict of Interest Disclosure** (`id: 8`): This field is a multiple-choice question that asks you to select any conflicts of interest that apply to you. Options include Affiliated Business Partner, Affiliated Company, Board Member, Business Partner, Family Member, None of the Above, Other, Self, and Other Affiliations. 
*   **Relationship** (`id: 5`): This field asks you to select how you are related to any conflicts of interest selected in the previous field. Options include Affiliated Business Partner, Affiliated Company, Board Member, Business Partner, Family Member, None of the Above, Other, Self, and Other. 
*   **Disclosure** (`id: 9`): This field is a single-choice question that asks you to disclose any potential conflicts of interest that you have. Options include "Yes" and "No". 
*   **Name** (`id: 2`): This field is a text box where you can enter your name. 
*   **Job Title** (`id: 3`): This field is a text box where you can enter your job title. 
*   **Organization** (`id: 4`): This field is a text box where you can enter the name of your organization. 
*   **Other Relationship** (`id: 6`): This field is a text box where you can enter any additional relationships that are not listed in the options. 
*   **Other Organization** (`id: 7`): This field is a text box where you can enter any other organizations that you are related to. 
*   **Signature** (`id: 11`): This field is a text box where you can enter your signature. 
*   **Signature Date** (`id: 12`): This field is a date field where you can enter the date you signed the form. 
*   **Submited By** (`id: 13`): This field is a text box where you can enter your name that is submitting this form. 
*   **Submited By Title** (`id: 14`): This field is a text box where you can enter your job title that is submitting this form. 
*   **Submited By Phone** (`id: 15`): This field is a text box where you can enter your phone number that is submitting this form.
