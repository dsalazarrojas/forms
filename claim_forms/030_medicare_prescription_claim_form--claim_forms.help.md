# medicare_prescription_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting Medicare prescription claims on behalf of patients. It ensures accurate and complete information is collected to process claims correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient information, including their name and address.
2. Select the claim status and type.
3. Enter the medication information, including the start and end dates.
4. Enter the claim amount and frequency.
5. Provide the provider's information, including their NPI and facility.
6. Enter the claimant's information, including their name, address, and date of birth.
7. Enter any additional claimant information as required.
