# HRA Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HRA Claim Form is used to submit claims for reimbursement, including Health Reimbursement Account (HRA), Dollars-off Claim, and Other Claim types. It captures relevant details such as claimant information, claim amount, claim date, and claim status. This form is essential for submitting claims to be reviewed and approved by the designated reviewer and approver users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Claimant Name, Claimant Email, and Claimant Phone fields with the relevant information of the person submitting the claim.
2. Select the Claim Type from the options provided, which include Health Reimbursement Account, Dollars-off Claim, and Other Claim.
3. Enter the Claim Amount, Claim Date, and Submission Date in the respective fields.
4. Provide any additional Claim Details in the Note field.
5. Upload any relevant Attachments if required.
6. Select the Status of the claim, which can be Pending, Approved, or Rejected, and provide any Reviewer Notes and Approval Notes.
7. Identify the Submission User, Reviewer User, and Approver User.
8. Enter any additional Notes and Reviewer Notes.
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (`hra_claim_form`): This is the main form title.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`): Enter the name of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`): Select the type of claim from the options provided, which include Health Reimbursement Account, Dollars-off Claim, and Other Claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`): Enter the date of the claim.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`): Provide any additional details about the claim in this Note field.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`): Upload any supporting attachments for the claim.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date the claim was reviewed.
* **Status** (`status`): Select the status of the claim from the options provided, which include Pending, Approved, and Rejected.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the claim.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`): Enter any reviewer comments or notes.
* **Approval Notes** (`approval_notes`): Enter any approval comments or notes.
* **Submission User** (`submission_user`): Enter the user who submitted the claim.
* **Reviewer User** (`reviewer_user`): Enter the user who reviewed the claim.
* **Approver User** (`approver_user`): Enter the user who approved the claim.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the user who originally submitted the claim.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the user who created the claim.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Enter the user who last updated the claim.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Enter the date the claim was last updated.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Enter the date the claim was created.
