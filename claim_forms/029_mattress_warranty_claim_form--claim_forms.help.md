# Mattress Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mattress Warranty Claim Form is used to gather information to process a warranty claim on a mattress. This form is intended for customers who have purchased a mattress from our company and are experiencing issues with it. Please ensure you are filling out this form with accurate and relevant information to help us process your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your customer information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
3. Describe the issue with your mattress, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Estimate the value of your claim, if applicable.
5. Select the date your issue started and the date you noticed the problem.
6. Choose the type of claim you are making (Mattress or Other).
7. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your phone number and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): Describe the issue with your mattress, including any steps you've taken to resolve the problem.
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue with your mattress, including any relevant dates or events.
* **Claim Value** (`claim_value`, text, required): Estimate the value of your claim, if applicable.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you noticed the problem with your mattress.
* **Claim Date Range** (`claim_date_range`, date, optional): If the problem started on a specific date range, enter that here.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of claim you are making (Mattress or Other).
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, or if you would like to speak to a representative, enter them here.
