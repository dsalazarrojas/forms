# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is a document used to gather information about an emergency incident, accident, or situation that requires reporting and filing. This form helps to collect relevant details, contact information, and supporting documentation to assist in the claims process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your contact information**: Fill in your name, contact number, and any other relevant details in the first field.
2. **Choose your claim type**: Select the type of claim you are filing, whether it's Auto Claim, Health Claim, or Other Claim.
3. **Select your contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by email, phone, or text message.
4. **Enter claim details**: Provide a brief description of the incident or situation that requires filing a claim.
5. **Enter contact method details**: If you selected a contact method, provide more information about how you can be contacted at this chosen method.
6. **Enter vehicle information**: Provide any relevant vehicle-related details, such as make and model or license plate number.
7. **Enter accident information**: Describe the accident or incident in detail, including any injuries or damages sustained.
8. **Enter witness names**: List the names of any witnesses who witnessed the incident.
9. **Enter witness information**: Provide a brief description of any witnesses who were present during the incident.
10. **Upload supporting documentation**: If available, attach copies of police reports, medical reports, or other relevant documents that support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`):
	+ Type: Text field to input your name and contact information.
	+ Use this field to provide your full name and any relevant contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`):
	+ Type: Select one option to specify the type of claim you are filing.
	+ Choose from Auto Claim, Health Claim, or Other Claim.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`):
	+ Type: Date field to input the date of the incident or accident.
	+ Enter the date in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`):
	+ Type: Note field to input a description of the incident or situation.
	+ Provide a brief and concise description of what happened.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`):
	+ Type: Select one option to specify how you would like to be contacted.
	+ Choose from Email, Phone, Text Message, or In Person.
* **Contact Method Details** (`contact_method_details`):
	+ Type: Text field to provide more information about your contact method of choice.
	+ Use this field to provide more details about how you can be contacted, such as your preferred contact hours or a good time to call.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`):
	+ Type: Text field to input vehicle-related information.
	+ Provide details about the vehicle involved in the incident, such as make and model or license plate number.
* **Accident Info** (`accident_info`):
	+ Type: Text field to input information about the accident or incident.
	+ Describe what happened, including any injuries or damages sustained.
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`):
	+ Type: Text field to input the names of any witnesses who were present during the incident.
	+ List the names of all witnesses, separated by commas or line breaks.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`):
	+ Type: Text field to input more information about the witnesses.
	+ Describe what you know about the witnesses, such as their involvement in the incident.
* **Police Report** (`police_report`):
	+ Type: Text field to input a copy of the police report.
	+ Attach a copy of the police report or describe the key points of the report.
* **Medical Report** (`medical_report`):
	+ Type: Text field to input a copy of the medical report.
	+ Attach a copy of the medical report or describe the key points of the report.
