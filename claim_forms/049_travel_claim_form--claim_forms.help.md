# Travel Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Claim Form is a tool for employees to submit their travel claims for reimbursement. This form is used to capture information about the claim, including the type of claim, date of the claim, amount claimed, reason for the claim, and details about the expense.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Travel Claim Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of claim you are submitting (Expense Claim or Reimbursement Claim).
2. Enter the date of the claim.
3. Enter the amount of the claim.
4. Provide a brief reason for the claim.
5. Select the type of expense related to the claim (Airfare, Hotel, Restaurant, or Rental Car).
6. Enter the value of the expense.
7. Enter a description of the expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of claim you are submitting (Expense Claim or Reimbursement Claim).
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the claim in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Claim Reason** (`claim_reason`, text, required): Provide a brief reason for the claim.
* **Expense Type** (`claim_expense_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of expense related to the claim (Airfare, Hotel, Restaurant, or Rental Car).
* **Expense Value** (`claim_expense_value`, number, required): Enter the value of the expense.
* **Expense Description** (`claim_expense_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the expense.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct claim type and expense type for your claim.
* Enter accurate dates and amounts to avoid delays in processing your claim.
* Provide a clear and concise reason for your claim to ensure that your request is processed quickly and efficiently.
