# Car Wash Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for damages incurred during a car wash service. Please fill out this form with accurate and detailed information to help us process your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the claimant information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
*   Enter the car wash business name, location, and phone number where the incident occurred.
*   Provide the date and time of the incident.
*   Select the type of service being performed at the time of the incident.
*   Enter your vehicle's year, make, model, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
*   Describe the location and type of damage on your vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
*   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
*   **Street address**: Enter your street address where the incident occurred.
*   **City, state, zip code**: Enter the city, state, and zip code where the incident occurred.
*   **Insurance company name**: If you have insurance, enter the name of your insurance company (optional).
*   **Insurance policy number**: If you have insurance, enter your policy number (optional).
*   **Car wash business name**: Enter the name of the car wash business where the incident occurred.
*   **Car wash location**: Enter the street address and city of the car wash business where the incident occurred.
*   **Car wash phone**: Enter the phone number of the car wash business where you can be reached for further communication.
*   **Date of incident**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of incident**: Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Type of service being performed**: Select the type of service being performed at the time of the incident.
*   **Vehicle year**: Enter your vehicle's year.
*   **Vehicle make**: Enter your vehicle's make.
*   **Vehicle model**: Enter your vehicle's model.
*   **Vehicle VIN**: Enter your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
*   **License plate number**: If available, enter your vehicle's license plate number.
*   **Damage location**: Describe the location of the damage on your vehicle.
*   **Type of damage**: Select the type of damage that occurred to your vehicle.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any supporting documentation or photos of the damage, please attach them to your claim.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
