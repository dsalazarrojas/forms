## Rehabilitation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Claim Form is a document used to submit claims for rehabilitation services to healthcare providers. This form is required for all rehabilitation claims and must be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure the claim is processed efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Provider Name field with the name of the healthcare provider who is providing the rehabilitation services.
2. Enter the Patient Name of the individual receiving the services in the Patient Name field.
3. Indicate the type of claim (Yes or No) in the Claim Type field, based on the type of services provided.
4. If applicable, select the date when the claim was made in the Claim Date field.
5. Describe any rehabilitative service benefits provided in the Rehabilitative Service Benefits field.
6. Enter the Provider License Number of the healthcare provider in the Provider License Number field.
7. Sign and date the form in the Signature field to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name** (Required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is providing the services.
* **Patient Name** (Required): Enter the name of the individual receiving the services.
* **Claim Type** (Required): Check 'Yes' if the claim is for a specific type of service, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Claim Date** (Optional): If applicable, enter the date when the claim was made (in date format, e.g. MM/DD/YY).
* **Rehabilitative Service Benefits** (Required): Provide a description of any services provided, including benefits or outcomes.
* **Provider License Number** (Required): Enter the provider's license number, as issued by the relevant regulatory authority.
* **Signature** (Required): Sign and date the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
