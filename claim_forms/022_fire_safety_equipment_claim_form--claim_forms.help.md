> # Fire Safety Equipment Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report a claim for fire safety equipment damage. Please fill out this form with the required information to help us process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields with accurate and clear information.
*   If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Street** (3, Text, Optional): Enter the street address where the fire safety equipment was located.
*   Note: Street, not building name or location code.
* **Equipment Location** (4, Text, Optional): Describe the location where the fire safety equipment was situated, including the building or facility if necessary.
* **Damage Description** (5, Text, Required): Please describe what happened with the fire safety equipment, including any details about the damage or loss.
* **Claim Date** (6, Date, Required): Enter the date of the incident when the fire safety equipment was damaged or lost.
* **Claimed By** (7, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or entity that claimed the damage or loss.
* **Claimed On** (8, Time, Optional): Enter the time of day when the damage occurred.
*   Note: This field should be in 24-hour time format (e.g., 12:00 or 13:30).
*   If no specific time is known, leave this field blank.
* **Claimed On Date** (9, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the damage or loss occurred.
*   Note: If not known, leave this field blank.
* **Claimed By Email** (10, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person or entity making the claim.
