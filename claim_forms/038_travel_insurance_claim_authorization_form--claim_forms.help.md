# travel_insurance_claim_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a travel insurance claim. Please fill out the form to provide information about the incident and supporting documentation to support your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review each field to ensure you are providing all required information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your travel when the incident occurred.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the insurance company you are associated with.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a clear description of the incident, including any supporting documentation.
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, required: false): Describe the claim you are submitting.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, required: false): Enter policy number related to your insurance policy.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of incident that occurred. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact information.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of your claim. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total claim amount.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, required: false): Please upload any supporting documents for the claim.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise descriptions in the text fields.
* If you are uploading supporting documents, make sure they are relevant to your claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
