# welcome_box_claim_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The welcome_box_claim_request_form is used to request a welcome box for someone else. This form requires specific details to ensure accurate and efficient delivery of the welcome box.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the program ID associated with the welcome box claim.
2. Provide the name of the person receiving the welcome box.
3. Enter the email address of the person receiving the welcome box.
4. Enter the contact number of the person or their representative.
5. Select a delivery date for the welcome box.
6. Choose a delivery time for the welcome box.
7. Enter the delivery address.
8. Enter the city where the welcome box will be delivered.
9. Enter the state where the welcome box will be delivered.
10. Enter the zip code of the delivery location.
11. If necessary, provide special delivery instructions.
12. Indicate if the person receiving the welcome box is eligible for the program.
12. Select the preferred method of delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program ID**: Enter the associated program ID for the welcome box claim.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the welcome box.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the welcome box.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person or their representative.
* **Delivery Date**: Select a date for the welcome box delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose a delivery time for the welcome box.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the delivery address of the welcome box.
* **City**: Enter the city where the welcome box will be delivered.
* **State**: Enter the state where the welcome box will be delivered.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the delivery location.
* **Delivery Instructions**: If necessary, provide special delivery instructions.
* **Program Eligibility**: Indicate if the person receiving the welcome box is eligible for the program.
* **Delivery Preference**: Select the preferred method of delivery.
