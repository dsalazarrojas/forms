# Property Claim Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge of a property claim, which is a process that signifies the resolution of a claim, typically after an adjuster has reviewed and approved the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the correct relationship between the claimant and the policyholder from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter the date the claim was completed.
*   Enter the claim amount.
*   Enter the claim description.
*   Select the current claim status.
*   Enter the claimant's contact information.
*   Enter the policyholder's contact information.
*   Enter the assigned adjuster's contact information.
*   Enter the insurance company's signature.
*   Enter the assigned adjuster's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder** (`Policyholder`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct relationship between the claimant and the policyholder from the dropdown menu. This field will typically be either "Claimant" or "Policyholder".
* **Claim Details** (`claim details`, `date`, required): Enter the date the claim was completed.
* **Policy Holder Details** (`policy holder details`, `number`, required): Enter the claim amount.
* **Claim Status** (`claim status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the claim from the dropdown menu. This will typically be one of the following options: "Pending", "Approved", "Declined", or "Discontinued".
* **Claim Description** (`claim description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **Policy Number** (`policy number`, `number`, required): Enter the policy number related to the claim.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the claim was discharged.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the claimant.
* **Policyholder Signature** (`policyholder signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the policyholder.
* **Claimant Contact Information** (`claimant contact info`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the claimant.
* **Policyholder Contact Information** (`policyholder contact info`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the policyholder.
* **Assigned Adjuster Contact Information** (`assigned adjuster info`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the assigned adjuster.
*   **Insurance Company Signature** (`insurance company signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the insurance company.
* **Assigned Adjuster Signature** (`assigned adjuster signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the assigned adjuster.
*   **Completed Date** (`date completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the claim was completed.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the claimant.
*   **Policyholder Address** (`policyholder address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the policyholder.
*   **Assigned Adjuster Address** (`assigned adjuster address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the assigned adjuster.
*   **Claimant Phone** (`claimant phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the claimant.
*   **Policyholder Phone** (`policyholder phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the policyholder.
*   **Claimant Email** (`claimant email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the claimant.
*   **Policyholder Email** (`policyholder email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the policyholder.
*   **Assigned Adjuster Phone** (`assigned adjuster phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the assigned adjuster.

Note: The required fields have been marked as such in the form, however the actual fields which are required are `Policyholder`, `claim_details`, `claim_status`, `claim_description`, `policy_number`, `discharge_date`, `claimant_signature`, `policyholder_signature`, `assigned_adjuster_contact_info`, `insurance_company_signature`, `assigned_adjuster_signature`, `date_completed`, `claimant_address`, `policyholder_address`, `assigned_adjuster_address`, `claimant_phone`, `claimant_email`, `policyholder_phone`, `policyholder_email`, and `assigned_adjuster_phone`
