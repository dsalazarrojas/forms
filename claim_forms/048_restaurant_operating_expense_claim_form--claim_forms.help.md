# Restaurant Operating Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about claims related to restaurant operating expenses from restaurant operators. This information will be used to track and process these claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. Choose a category for your claim from the options in the "Claim Form Category" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your claim in the "Claim Form Description" field.
3. Enter the date you are claiming for in the "Claim Form Date" field.
4. Enter the time you are claiming for in the "Claim Form Time" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your claim in the "Claim Form Note" field.
6. Enter a valid email address in the "Claim Form Email" field to ensure we can contact you.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Claim Form Phone" field, so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Claim Form Category**: Choose the category that best describes your claim. This will help us process it quickly.
*   **Claim Form Description**: Enter a brief description of your claim, including any relevant details.
*   **Claim Form Date**: Enter the date you are claiming for. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Claim Form Time**: Enter the time you are claiming for. This should be in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Claim Form Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your claim.
*   **Claim Form Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you about your claim.
*   **Claim Form Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can contact you if needed.

Note: The fields may not be displayed on the form as described, as the actual field labels and positions are determined by the form's design and layout.
