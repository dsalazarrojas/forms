# medicare_claim_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medicare_claim_form_template is a form used to collect information about Medicare claims. It is intended for healthcare providers to submit claims to the Medicare program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `claim_provider` field with your healthcare provider information.
2. Enter the `claim_date` field with the date of the claim.
3. Fill in the `patient_name` field with the patient's name.
4. Enter the `medicare_number` field with the patient's Medicare number.
5. Fill in the `claim_amount` field with the amount of the claim.
6. Select the type of claim in the `claim_type` field (e.g., Workers' compensation, Auto, Home).
7. Fill in the `submitter_name` field with your name as the submitter.
8. Fill in the `claim_status` field with the current status of the claim (e.g., Pending, Denied).
9. Select whether the claim was submitted by 'Yes' or 'No' in the `claim_submitter_type` field.
10. Fill in the `claim_note` field with any additional notes about the claim.
11. Enter the patient's email address in the `claim_email` field.
12. Fill in the `claim_phone` field with the patient's phone number.
13. Fill in the `claim_address` field with the patient's address.
14. Fill in the `claim_city` field with the patient's city.
15. Fill in the `claim_state` field with the patient's state.
16. Fill in the `claim_zip` field with the patient's zip code.
17. Fill in the `claim_country` field with the country where the patient resides.
18. Enter the date of the claim in the `claim_claim_date` field.
19. Fill in the `claim_submitter_name` field with your name as the submitter.
20. Select the status of the claim in the `claim_claim_status` field (e.g., Open, Denied, Pending).
21. Select whether the claim is 'Yes' or 'No' in the `claim_claim_type` field.
22. Fill in the `claim_claim_amount` field with the amount of the claim.
23. Fill in the `claim_claim_submitter_name` field with your name as the submitter.
24. Fill in the `claim_claim_note` field with any additional notes about the claim.
25. Enter the submitter's email address in the `claim_claim_email` field.
