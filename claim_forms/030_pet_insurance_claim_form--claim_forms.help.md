# Pet Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for your pet's insurance coverage. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your claim is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in each section as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "Yes" or "optional" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Insurance Claim Form** (`pet_insurance_claim_form`, text, not required):
	+ This is the title of the form. You can leave this blank if you'd like.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, not required):
	+ Answer "Yes" if your pet is an animal of a species that is covered under your insurance policy.
* **Pet's Veterinary Bill** (`pet_vet_bill`, number, not required):
	+ Enter the total amount of the veterinary bill related to your pet's care.
* **Medical Expenses** (`medical_expenses`, number, not required):
	+ Enter the amount of medical expenses you are claiming for your pet.
* **Vet Care** (`vet_care`, text, not required):
	+ Describe the services or treatments your pet has received.
* **Pet Medication** (`pet_medication`, select_one, not required):
	+ Answer "Yes" if your pet requires ongoing medication.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your pet's name.
## Tips

* Please make sure to attach any supporting documentation, such as veterinary bills or receipts, to your claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your insurance provider for assistance.
* This form is intended for internal use only, please do not share your claim information with others.
