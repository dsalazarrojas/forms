# Mesothelioma Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from claimants who are seeking compensation for mesothelioma. Mesothelioma is a type of cancer caused by asbestos exposure, often leading to respiratory problems and various health issues. This form helps us understand the claimant's situation, allowing us to provide accurate and timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form. These fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are essential for processing your claim.
2. For each field, provide accurate and clear information about the claimant's situation.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, number, optional): This field is used to collect the claimant's contact information. Please provide your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Claimant Date of Birth** (`claimant_date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to capture the claimant's date of birth. This helps us understand their age and medical history.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, optional): Enter the claimant's current or most recent address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): This field is required to contact the claimant for further assistance or questions. Please provide the claimant's phone number.
* **Mesothelioma Type** (`mesothelioma_type`, select one, required): Select the type of mesothelioma the claimant has been diagnosed with. Please choose from the options: Pleural, Peritoneal, Pericardial, Cystic, Am JCO, or Recurrent.
* **Claimant Date of Diagnosis** (`claimant_date_of_diagnosis`, date, required): Enter the date the claimant was diagnosed with mesothelioma.
* **Claimant Insurance Provider** (`claimant_insurance_provider`, text, optional): If the claimant has an insurance provider, please enter their name and policy number.
* **Claimant Insurance Number** (`claimant_insurance_number`, text, required): This field is required to verify the claimant's insurance information. Please enter their insurance policy number.
* **Claimant Date of Death** (`claimant_date_of_death`, date, optional): If the claimant has passed away, enter the date of death.
* **Claimant Date of Last Contact** (`claimant_date_of_last_contact`, date, optional): Enter the date of the claimant's last contact with the healthcare provider or medical facility.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for guidance.
- If you have any supporting documents or evidence, please attach them to the form or provide a link to download them.
