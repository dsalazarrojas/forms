# Insurance Claim Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Claim Discharge Form is used to document the closure of an insurance claim. This form is used to confirm that a claim has been resolved and the insurance provider has been released from further liability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the claim form number.
2.  Enter the customer's name.
3.  Enter the claimant's name.
4.  Enter the claimant's address.
5.  Enter the date of the claim.
6.  Enter the date of discharge.
7.  Select the type of claim.
8.  Enter the cause of the claim (if applicable).
9.  Select the status of the claim (if applicable).
10. Enter the insurance provider's name.
11. Enter the adjuster's name.
12. Enter the adjuster's phone number.
13. Enter the adjuster's email.
14. Enter the adjuster's address.
15. Enter the customer's phone number.
16. Enter the customer's email.
17. Enter the customer's address.
18. Add any additional note about the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Step 1: Insurance Claim Discharge Form Step 1 - Claim Discharge
* **claim_form_number**: Enter the number of the claim form. This is the unique identifier for the claim.
* **claimant_name**: Enter the name of the person filing the claim.
* **claimant_address**: Enter the address of the person filing the claim.
* **claim_date**: Enter the date the claim was originally filed.
* **discharge_date**: Enter the date the claim was discharged.
* **claim_type**: Select the type of claim (e.g., Auto Accident, Other Claim, Claim Discharge).

### Step 2: Insurance Claim Discharge Form Step 2 - Claim Details
* **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer (if different from the claimant).
* **customer_address**: Enter the address of the customer (if different from the claimant).
* **cause_of_claim**: Enter the reason or cause of the claim (if applicable).
* **claim_status**: Select the status of the claim (e.g., Pending, Active, Discharged).

### Step 3: Insurance Claim Discharge Form Step 3 - Adjuster Information
* **insurance_provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **adjuster**: Enter the name of the adjuster handling the claim.
* **adjuster_phone**: Enter the phone number of the adjuster.
* **adjuster_email**: Enter the email of the adjuster.
* **adjuster_address**: Enter the address of the adjuster.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **customer_email**: Enter the email of the customer.
* **customer_address**: Enter the address of the customer.

### Step 4: Notes and Additional Information
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the claim.
