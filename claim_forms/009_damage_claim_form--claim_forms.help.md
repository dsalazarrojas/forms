# Damage Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Damage Claim Form is used to collect and document information related to damages or losses incurred by individuals or vehicles. This form is used for claims management and investigation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Claimant Info section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide your vehicle info in the Vehicle Info section.
3. Describe the claim details in the Claim Details section.
4. Specify the type of damage in the Damage Type section.
5. Enter the date when the damage occurred in the Damage Date section (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the Claimant Signature section to confirm your identity and authenticity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Info (claimant_info)**: Enter your name and contact information to help us contact you about your claim.
* **Claimant Contact (claimant_contact)**: Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you with updates or questions.
* **Vehicle Info (vehicle_info)**: Describe your vehicle, including make, model, and license plate number.
* **Claim Details (claim_details)**: Give a detailed description of the damage, including any relevant circumstances.
* **Damage Description (damage_description)**: Describe the damage in more detail, including any injuries or losses.
* **Damage Type (damage_type)**: Choose from one of the following options to describe the type of damage:
	+ 1: Collision
	+ 2: Theft
	+ 3: Fire
	+ 4: Water damage
	+ Other (please select this option if none of the above apply)
* **Damage Date (damage_date)**: Enter the date when the damage occurred (if applicable).
* **Claimant Signature (claimant_signature)**: Sign and date this section to confirm your identity and authenticity.
