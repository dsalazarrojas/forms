# Workers' Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about workers' compensation claims from employees. It is used to gather essential details about workplace injuries, benefits claimed, employer-employee information, and claim status. Fill this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth processing and resolution of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment with minimal interruptions.
2. Read each field carefully and enter your answers accurately.
3. If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to contact your HR representative or the claims administrator.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Injuries** (`workplace_injuries`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a description of the injury that occurred at work.
* **Benefits Claimed** (`benefits_claimed`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose one or multiple benefits that you are claiming (e.g., wages, medical expenses, etc.).
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your employer (company name).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your full name (first and last).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location where the injury occurred (office, factory, store, etc.).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the incident or injury.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the incident or injury (HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the incident or circumstances surrounding the injury.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the status of your claim (e.g., pending, approved, denied, etc.).
