# Retail Store Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a retail store insurance claim. It will help you provide a detailed account of the incident, including the date, time, and description of what happened, as well as any supporting documents and store insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the date and time the incident occurred.
2. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
3. Decide if you have any supporting documents to upload.
4. Select the insurance company for your store.
5. Enter your store's insurance policy number.
6. Enter your name, contact number, and email address.
7. Enter your store's name, address, contact number, and email address.
8. Provide any additional comments or information about your submission.
