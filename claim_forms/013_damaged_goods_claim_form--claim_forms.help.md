# Damaged Goods Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Damaged Goods Claim Form is used to report damaged or defective goods. This form helps us gather information about the product and the circumstances surrounding the damage, which will be used to investigate and process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Check if you have already submitted a claim for this product. If you have, do not resubmit this form.
2. Ensure that you have already checked your product warranty or return policy to see if the damage is covered.
3. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to answer all the questions accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter** (`1`, number, required/optional): Enter your submitter ID number. This is a unique identifier assigned to you by our system.
* **Product Details** (`2`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the product has been damaged or become defective.
* **Description** (`3`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage or defect, including photos or videos if possible.
* **Claim Status** (`4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the current status of your claim (Active or Inactive).
* **Claim Details** (`5`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the claim, such as the date of purchase or the specific issue with the product.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as detailed as possible when describing the damage or defect.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to contact our customer support team for assistance.
* We'll review your form submission and get in touch with you as soon as possible to discuss the next steps in the claim process.
