# Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Claim Form is a tool used to document and process expense claims within an organization. It collects information about the claim, including details about the claim, date, time, location, amount, currency, and more. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all required fields to ensure your claim is processed correctly.
2.  Fill in the form title and description to provide a clear and concise summary of your claim.
3.  Choose the relevant claim type, category, and assigned tool to associate with your claim.
4.  Select the claim status to reflect the current state of your claim.
5.  Enter any additional comments or notes related to your claim.
6.  Provide your email and phone number for further contact.
7.  Upload any supporting files for your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (Text, Required): Enter a brief title for your claim.
* **form_description** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of your claim.
* **claim_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of claim (e.g., Claim Forms, Expense Tracking Forms, Accountant Forms).
* **category** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the category for your claim (e.g., Claim Forms, Expense Tracking Forms, Accountant Forms).
* **claim_forms** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the relevant claim forms associated with your claim.
* **form_category** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the category for your claim.
* **form_output_file** (Text, Required): Provide the path to the supporting file for your claim.
* **form_id** (Number, Required): Enter a unique ID for your claim.
* **claim_ids** (Text, Required): Enter a list of IDs associated with your claim.
* **form_assigned_tool** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the tool assigned to process your claim.
* **form_note** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to your claim.
* **form_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your claim.
* **form_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your claim.
* **claim_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your claim (if different from form_date).
* **claim_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your claim (if different from form_time).
* **claim_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where your claim was made.
* **claim_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of your claim.
* **claim_currency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the currency for your claim (e.g., USD, EUR, JPY).
* **claim_note** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your claim.
* **claim_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **claim_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **claim_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your claim (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **assigned_tool** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the tool assigned to process your claim.
* **claim_ids** (Text, Optional): Enter a list of IDs associated with your claim.
* **claim_details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details related to your claim.

Note: The optional fields are not required but are useful for providing further context and information about your claim.
