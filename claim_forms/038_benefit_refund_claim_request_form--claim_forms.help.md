# Benefit Refund Claim Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide you through the process of submitting a refund claim. It will help you provide all necessary information to process your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out.
2. Complete the Claimant Information section with your name, employee ID, and email address.
3. In the Claim Details section, choose the type of refund claim and provide the date of the original transaction and the requested refund amount.
4. In the Supporting Documentation section, select the supporting documents you have provided.
5. In the Refund Method section, choose how you would like to receive the refund.
6. Finally, verify that all information is accurate and authorize the processing of your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claimant Full Name**: Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as per your employment records.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  * Type: `email`
  * Required: `true`
- **Phone Number**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`
- **Department**: (Optional) Enter the department you belong to.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`
- **Claim Date**: Select the date when you requested the claim.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required: `true`
- **Type of Refund Claim**: Choose the reason for your refund claim.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - Overpayment Refund
    - Premium Refund
    - Deductible Refund
    - Co-pay Refund
    - Administrative Error
    - Termination Refund
    - Other
- **Date of Original Transaction**: Select the date of the original transaction.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required: `true`
- **Refund Amount Requested**: Enter the amount you are requesting for refund.
  * Type: `number`
  * Required: `true`
- **Reason for Refund**: Enter a detailed explanation for your claim.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `true`
- **Supporting Documents**: Select the documents you have provided for your claim.
  * Type: `select_multiple`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - Original Receipt
    - Invoice
    - Bank Statement
    - Proof of Payment
    - Correspondence
    - Medical Records
    - Insurance Explanation
    - Other
- **Description of Documentation**: Briefly describe the documentation you have provided.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`
- **Preferred Refund Method**: Choose how you would like to receive the refund.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - Original Payment Method
    - Direct Deposit
    - Check
    - Credit Back to Account
    - Other
- **Bank Account Information**: Enter your bank account information if direct deposit is selected.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: `false`
- **Verify Accuracy**: Ensure all information is accurate.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - `True`
    - `False`
- **Authorize Claim**: Authorize the processing of your claim.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - I Agree
    - I Do Not Agree
- **Claim Deadline**: Confirm if this claim is within the deadline.
  * Type: `select_one`
  * Required: `true`
  * Options:
    - `True`
    - `False`
- **Date of Submission**: Select the date when you submitted this form.
  * Type: `date`
  * Required: `true`

Note: Please ensure all fields are filled out carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
