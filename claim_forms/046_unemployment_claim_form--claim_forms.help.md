# Unemployment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unemployment Claim Form is a tool used to process unemployment claims for individuals who have been laid off or quit their job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information: This includes your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, postal code, birth date, and social security number.
* Provide employment details: This includes information about your last employment, such as the date of last employment, last day worked, hours worked, and weekly hours worked.
* Specify pay periods and benefits: This includes information about the pay periods you are claiming, the pay per week, notice period, weeks benefits, and weeks paid.
* Confirm your claim: Finally, confirm your identity as the claimant by providing your name and other identifying information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: This section is where you enter your personal contact information, including your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, postal code, birth date, and social security number. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* **Personal Info**: Enter your last name, first name, and date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-dd.
* **Employment Details**: Enter the date of last employment and the last day worked in the format YYYY-MM-dd. You will also be asked about your hours worked, and weekly hours worked.
* **Previous Employment**: If you have previous employment history, you will be asked to provide details about your last employment.
* **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief explanation for why you left your job, such as layoffs or quitting.
* **Date of Last Employment**: Enter the date of last employment in the format YYYY-MM-dd.
* **Last Day Worked**: Enter the last day you worked in the format YYYY-MM-dd.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours you worked in the last employment.
* **Weekly Hours Worked**: Enter the total weekly hours worked in the last employment.
* **Pay Periods**: Enter the number of pay periods you are claiming.
* **Pay per Week**: Enter the pay per week for the last employment.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the notice period you received from your employer.
* **Weeks Benefits**: Enter the total weeks benefits for your claim.
* **Weeks Paid**: Enter the total weeks paid for your claim.
* **Claimant**: This section is used to confirm your identity as the claimant. 
* **Claimant Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Claimant City**: Enter your city.
* **Claimant State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Claimant Zip**: Enter your postal code.
* **Claimant Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Claimant Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-dd.
* **Claimant Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number.
