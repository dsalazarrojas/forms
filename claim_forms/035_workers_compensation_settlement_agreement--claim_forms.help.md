# Workers Compensation Settlement Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workers Compensation Settlement Agreement form is designed to capture key information related to workers' compensation settlements between employers and employees. This form helps to establish clear terms and conditions for the settlement, ensuring a smooth and fair resolution to the workers' compensation case.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide settlement data in the first section.
2. Enter employer information in the second section.
3. Enter employee information in the third section.
4. Outline the terms of the agreement in the fourth section.
5. Summarize the agreement in the fifth section.
6. Include signatures and dates to confirm the agreement.
7. Specify payment details for the settlement, including payment schedule and status.
8. Add any relevant notes or comments about the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Settlement data** (id: 1): Enter the necessary details about the settlement, such as the settlement amount and any relevant dates.
* **Employer information** (id: 2): Provide contact and identification details for the employer.
* **Employee information** (id: 3): Provide contact and identification details for the employee.
* **Agreement terms** (id: 4): Specify the terms and conditions of the settlement, including payment terms and any other relevant agreements.
* **Agreement summary** (id: 5): Summarize the agreement in a brief paragraph.
* **Agreement signatures** (id: 6): Include signatures from both the employer and the employee to confirm the agreement.
* **Settlement payment** (id: 7): Specify the payment details, including amount and payment method.
* **Settlement details** (id: 8): Enter any additional settlement details, such as medical expenses or payment schedules.
* **Medical expenses** (id: 9): Record medical expenses related to the workers' compensation case.
* **Medical bills** (id: 10): Attach or upload medical bills related to the workers' compensation case.
* **Payment plan** (id: 11): Specify the payment plan for the settlement, including payment frequency and amounts.
* **Employer concessions** (id: 12): Enter any concessions or discounts made by the employer.
* **Employee concessions** (id: 13): Enter any concessions or discounts made by the employee.
* **Payment schedule** (id: 14): Specify the payment schedule for the settlement.
* **Payment status** (id: 15): Record the status of the payment (e.g., paid, unpaid, etc.).
* **Agreement status** (id: 16): Record the status of the agreement (e.g., agreed, disputed, etc.).
* **Agreement notes** (id: 17): Add any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Payment terms** (id: 18): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment terms 2** (id: 19): Enter any further payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment terms 3** (id: 20): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.

Note: This form is for general guidance purposes only and should be reviewed and approved by a relevant authority before use.
