# Workplace Mentorship Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who have participated in mentorship programs and need to claim expenses related to these programs for reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Determine if you participated in a mentorship program and incurred expenses related to it.
2. Select the correct answer for "Expense Program" to indicate whether you participated in a mentorship program.
3. Enter the date when the expense occurred.
4. Enter the amount of the expense.
5. Enter your name and email address to allow us to contact you regarding your claim.
6. Enter your phone number and a note to provide any additional information about your claim.
7. Briefly describe the expense you're claiming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Program** (`expense_program`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you participated in a mentorship program.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the expense occurred.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Note** (`claimant_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the expense you're claiming.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
- Attach any supporting documentation for your claim, if required.
