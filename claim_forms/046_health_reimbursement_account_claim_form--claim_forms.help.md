# Health Reimbursement Account Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for claiming reimbursement for expenses related to health-related activities, such as medical treatments, procedures, or other health-related services. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the claimant's details, including name, job title, department, and contact information. 
2. Choose the claim date type (e.g., Claim Date, Claim Period, Claim Amount)
3. Enter the claim amount
4. Enter the claim reason
5. Select the claim status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled)
6. Add any additional notes about the claim

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Form** (Claim Form, required): Enter a brief description of the claim
* **Claimant Details** (Claimant Details, required): Enter the claimant's name, job title, department, and contact information
* **Claimant Address** (Claimant Address, required): Enter the claimant's address
* **Claimant Phone** (Claimant Phone, required): Enter the claimant's phone number
* **Claimant Email** (Claimant Email, required): Enter the claimant's email address
* **Claimant Job Title** (Claimant Job Title, required): Enter the claimant's job title
* **Claimant Department** (Claimant Department, required): Enter the claimant's department
* **Claim Date** (Claim Date, required): Enter the date of the claim
* **Claim Date Type** (Claim Date Type, select_one, required): Select the type of claim date (e.g., Claim Date, Claim Period, Claim Amount)
* **Claim Amount** (Claim Amount, required): Enter the amount claimed
* **Claim Reason** (Claim Reason, required): Enter a brief reason for the claim
* **Claim Status** (Claim Status, select_one, required): Select the status of the claim (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled)
* **Claim Status Type** (Claim Status Type, select_one, required): Select the type of claim status (e.g., Claim Date, Claim Period, Claim Amount)
* **Claim Note** (Claim Note, required): Enter any additional notes about the claim
* **Claim Reimbursement** (Claim Reimbursement, required): Enter the reimbursement amount
* **Claim Total Claim** (Claim Total Claim, required): Enter the total claim amount
* **Claimant Reimbursement Plan Provider** (Claimant Reimbursement Plan Provider, required): Enter the provider of the reimbursement plan
* **Claimant Reimbursement Plan Effective Date** (Claimant Reimbursement Plan Effective Date, required): Enter the effective date of the reimbursement plan
* **Claimant Reimbursement Plan End Date** (Claimant Reimbursement Plan End Date, required): Enter the end date of the reimbursement plan
* **Claimant Reimbursement Plan Status** (Claimant Reimbursement Plan Status, required): Select the status of the reimbursement plan (e.g., Active, Inactive, Cancelled)
* **Claimant Reimbursement Plan Notes** (Claimant Reimbursement Plan Notes, required): Enter any additional notes about the reimbursement plan 
* **Claimant Reimbursement Request** (Claimant Reimbursement Request, required): Enter the reimbursement request details
* **Claimant Reimbursement Request Details** (Claimant Reimbursement Request Details, required): Enter any additional details about the reimbursement request

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Review the form for any specific requirements or instructions before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, contact the relevant department or representative for assistance.
* Double-check for typos and accuracy before submitting.
