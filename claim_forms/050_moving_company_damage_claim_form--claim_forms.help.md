# Moving Company Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Company Damage Claim Form is a form designed for customers to report damages or issues with a moving company's services. This form should be used when customers need to submit a claim for damages sustained during a move.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Moving Company Damage Claim Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the field marked "Customer Name".
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the field labeled "email".
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "phone_number" field.
4. Describe the damage or issue you encountered in the "description" field.
5. Select the **Type of Damage** you experienced from the dropdown options in the "type_of_damage" field.
6. Check all the boxes corresponding to the **Select Multiple** options that apply to your situation in the "select_multiple" field.
7. If you have any supporting documents, upload them in the "uploaded_documents" field.
8. Upload any relevant files related to your claim in the "upload_file" field.
9. Upload any receipts or invoices related to your claim in the "uploaded_receipt" field.
10. Click the "submit" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding your claim.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **description**: Explain in detail the damage or issue you experienced during the move.
* **type_of_damage**: Select the type of damage that occurred from the dropdown options.
* **select_multiple**: Check all the boxes that apply to your situation to help us understand the scope of the damage.
* **uploaded_documents**: Attach any documents that support your claim, such as photos or receipts.
* **upload_file**: Upload any relevant files related to your claim.
* **uploaded_receipt**: Attach any receipts or invoices related to your claim.
* **submit**: Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **confirm**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **uploaded_signature**: If necessary, upload a signature for verification purposes.
* **confirm_signature**: Confirm that the signature is authentic and matches your identity.
* **submit_all**: Submit your completed form to initiate the claim process.
* **uploaded_document2**, **uploaded_document3**, **uploaded_document4**, **uploaded_document5**, **uploaded_document6**, **uploaded_document7**, **uploaded_document8**, **uploaded_document9**: These fields are for attaching supporting documents related to your claim. You can upload up to 9 additional documents if needed.
