# TCPA Violation Claim Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from claimants who have experienced potential TCPA violations, such as unwanted or robocalls.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the following fields:
	* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
	* **Claim type**: Select the type of claim you are making (Text Message, Auto-dialed call, Live call, or Robocall).
	* **Date and time of claim**: Enter the date and time of when you experienced the alleged violation. Use the YYYY-MM-dd format for the date and HH:mm for the time.
	* **Description of claim**: Provide a detailed description of what happened, including the alleged violation details.
	* **Supporting document**: If you have any supporting documents, such as receipts or logs, upload them here.
	* **File name**: Enter the name of the supporting document.
	* **File description**: Briefly describe the file you are uploading.
	* **File size**: Enter the size of the uploaded file in bytes.
	* **Report**: Enter any additional information or comments about the claim.
	* **Signature**: If available, sign the form with your current date.
	* **Claimant signature**: If you have an attorney representing you, their signature is required.
	* **Attorney signature**: Enter the date when you sign this form.
	* **Date of claim**: Enter the date of when you experienced the alleged violation. Use the YYYY-MM-dd format.
	* **Time of claim**: Enter the time of when you experienced the alleged violation.
	* **Time of call**: Enter the time you were contacted.
	* **Location**: Enter the location where you were contacted.
	* **Time zone**: Enter your time zone.
	* **Claimant phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Upload supporting document**: Upload any supporting documents, such as receipts or logs.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out this form.
* Ensure that the supporting documents uploaded are clear and relevant to your claim.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
