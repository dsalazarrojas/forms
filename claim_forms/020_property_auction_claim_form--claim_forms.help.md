# Property Auction Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property being put up for auction. It's meant for authorized representatives of the property's owner to provide essential details for the auction process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct option for each field.
3. Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Representative** (property_auction_claim_form_representative): Check if you are representing the property's owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
* **Property Details** (property_auction_claim_form_property_details): Provide a brief description of the property.
* **Buyer Details** (property_auction_claim_form_buyer_details): Enter the buyer's contact information.
* **Auction Details** (property_auction_claim_form_auction_details): Enter the auction's date, time, and location.
* **Claim Status** (property_auction_claim_form_claim_status): Select the status of the claim (Claim Status, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Attachments** (property_auction_claim_form_attachments): Upload any relevant documents or images supporting your claim.
* **Email** (property_auction_claim_form_email): Enter the representative's or buyer's email address.
* **Phone** (property_auction_claim_form_phone): Enter the representative's or buyer's phone number.
* **Property Type** (property_auction_claim_form_property_type): Describe the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Address** (property_auction_claim_form_property_address): Enter the property's address.
* **Auction Date** (property_auction_claim_form_auction_date): Enter the date of the auction.
* **Auction Time** (property_auction_claim_form_auction_time): Enter the time of the auction.
* **Auction Location** (property_auction_claim_form_auction_location): Enter the location of the auction.
* **Auction Price** (property_auction_claim_form_auction_price): Enter the price of the auction.
* **Representative Signature** (property_auction_claim_form_representative_signature): Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Buyer Signature** (property_auction_claim_form_buyer_signature): Sign the form to confirm the buyer's acceptance of the claim.
* **Representative Name** (property_auction_claim_form_representative_name): Enter the representative's name.
* **Representative Position** (property_auction_claim_form_representative_position): Enter the representative's position or role.
* **Representative Phone** (property_auction_claim_form_representative_phone): Enter the representative's phone number.
* **Buyer Name** (property_auction_claim_form_buyer_name): Enter the buyer's name.
* **Buyer Position** (property_auction_claim_form_buyer_position): Enter the buyer's position or role.
* **Buyer Phone** (property_auction_claim_form_buyer_phone): Enter the buyer's phone number.
