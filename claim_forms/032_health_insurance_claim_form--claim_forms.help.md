# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Form is a document used to submit claims for reimbursement to your health insurance provider. It is used to provide detailed information about a claim, including the claimant's and patient's contact information, claim details, and claim status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the claim date**: Select the date on which the claim is being submitted.
2. **Enter the claimant's name and contact information**: Provide the claimant's name, phone number, and email address.
3. **Enter the patient's name and date of birth**: Provide the patient's name and date of birth.
4. **Enter the provider's name and contact information**: Provide the provider's name and contact information.
5. **Enter a description of the claim**: Provide a brief description of the claim.
6. **Select the claim status**: Choose from the following options: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
7. **Enter the claim amount and details**: Provide the claim total, reimbursement, deductible, out-of-pocket amount, and any other relevant financial details.
8. **Enter the date last updated**: Select the date the claim was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Claim Date (claim_date)**: Enter the date on which the claim is being submitted.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Claimant Name (claimant_name)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the claim.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the claimant's full name.
- **Claimant Phone (claimant_phone)**: Enter the claimant's phone number.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter a valid phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX-XXX-XXXX-XXXX.
- **Claimant Email (claimant_email)**: Enter the claimant's email address.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter a valid email address.
- **Provider Name (provider_name)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the provider's full name.
- **Patient Name (patient_name)**: Enter the patient's name.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the patient's full name.
- **Patient Date of Birth (patient_dob)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Provider Contact (provider_contact)**: Enter the provider's contact information.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the provider's name, address, or phone number.
- **Claim Description (claim_description)**: Enter a brief description of the claim.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter a short description of the claim.
- **Claim Status (claim_status)**: Choose from the following options: Pending, Approved, or Denied.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Select the current status of the claim.
- **Claim Total (claim_total)**: Enter the total claim amount.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the claim total in numeric format.
- **Reimbursement (claim_reimbursement)**: Enter the reimbursement amount.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the reimbursement amount in numeric format.
- **Deductible (claim_deductible)**: Enter the deductible amount.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the deductible amount in numeric format.
- **Out of Pocket (claim_out_of_pocket)**: Enter the out-of-pocket amount.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the out-of-pocket amount in numeric format.
- **Date Last Updated (claim_date_last_update)**: Select the date the claim was last updated.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips

* Please note that all fields are optional unless specified otherwise.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the claimant or provider for clarification.
* Ensure that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
