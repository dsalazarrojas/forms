# Insurance Claim Distance Declaration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Insurance Claim Distance Declaration form is used to collect information related to an insurance claim for distance traveled. This form is used to capture the necessary details for processing and verifying an insurance claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the Claim Distance Form page.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Distance Form** (`insurance_claim_distance_declaration`, text, required):
	+ This field is the main form name, and it's a required field to fill.
* **Claimant Details** (`claimant_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of travel in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional):
	+ This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Distance in Kms** (`distance_km`, number, required):
	+ Enter the distance traveled in kilometers.
* **Distance in miles** (`distance_miles`, number, optional):
	+ This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the mode of travel used, such as car, flight, train, or other.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional):
	+ This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required):
	+ Enter the amount claimed for the insurance claim.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the current status of the claim, such as pending, approved, or rejected.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to avoid delays or errors in the claim process.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided to ensure a smooth processing of the claim.
