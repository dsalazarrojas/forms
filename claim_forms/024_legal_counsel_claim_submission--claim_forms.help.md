# Legal Counsel Claim Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Counsel Claim Submission form is used to collect information from claimants to support their cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client information in the Client Information section.
2. Provide a detailed description of your claim in the Claim Details section.
3. Enter your and your lawyer's information in the Legal Counsel Information section.
4. Upload any relevant supporting documents in the Supporting Documents section.
5. Enter your phone number and email address in the Claimant Phone and Claimant Email sections.
6. Enter your date of birth in the Claimant Date of Birth section.
7. Enter your time of birth in the Claimant Time of Birth section (if applicable).
8. Enter your case number in the Case Number section (if applicable).
9. Enter your address in the Claimant Address section.
10. Enter your lawyer's information in the Defense Counsel Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (client_info)**: Enter your name, company, or any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Claim Details (claim_details)**: Provide a clear and detailed description of your claim, including the reason for your claim and any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Legal Counsel Information (legal_counsel_info)**: Enter your lawyer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about your representation.
* **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Upload any relevant documents that support your claim, such as contracts, invoices, or witness statements.
* **Claimant Phone (claimant_phone)**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your case.
* **Claimant Email (claimant_email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your case.
* **Claimant Date of Birth (claimant_date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth, as required for official records.
* **Claimant Time of Birth (claimant_time_of_birth)**: Enter your time of birth, if applicable.
* **Case Number (case_number)**: Enter your case number, if applicable, to help us locate your case.
* **Claimant Address (claimant_address)**: Enter your address to ensure we can contact you about your case.
* **Defense Counsel Info (defense_counsel_info)**: Enter your lawyer's contact information, so we can get in touch with them about your case.
