# fire_damage_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire damage claim form is used to collect information related to a fire damage claim from a claimant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the **Claimant Information** section, including your name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Provide your insurance provider's name, contact information, and policy number.
3. Enter the date and time of the fire incident.
4. Describe the fire damage in the **Fire Damage Description** section.
5. Enter the total damage cost and claim amount.
6. Add any supporting documents or files as needed.
7. Update the claim status as necessary.
8. Enter any additional comments or remarks from the insurance provider and claimant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information**:
	+ **Claimant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Claimant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
	+ **Claimant Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
	+ **Claimant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Insurance Provider**:
	+ **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
	+ **Insurance Provider Contact**: Enter the contact information of your insurance provider.
	+ **Insurance Provider Policy Number**: Enter your policy number.
* **Fire Incident**:
	+ **Fire Incident Date**: Enter the date and time of the fire incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`.
* **Fire Damage Description**:
	+ Describe the fire damage in detail, including any damage to property or personal belongings.
* **Claim Amount and Cost**:
	+ **Total Damage Cost**: Enter the total cost of the damage.
	+ **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Supporting Documents**:
	+ Upload any supporting documents or files related to the claim.
* **Claim Status**:
	+ Select the current status of the claim from the dropdown menu (Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or remarks from the insurance provider or claimant.
* **Claimant Signature**:
	+ Sign the claim form to confirm your identity.
* **Insurance Provider Signature**:
	+ Sign the claim form to confirm acceptance of the claim.
* **Claimant Date**:
	+ Enter the date of completion of the claim form.
* **Claimant Time**:
	+ Enter the time of completion of the claim form.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the insurance provider has all necessary information to process your claim.
- Make sure to attach any supporting documents or files as required.
- Review and update the claim status as necessary.
- Keep track of any comments or remarks from the insurance provider and claimant.
- Sign the claim form to confirm your identity and acceptance of the claim.
