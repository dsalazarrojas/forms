# Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about emergency medical services negligence claims. It's intended to gather essential details about the claimant, the incident, medical history, treatment, claim status, medical expert testimony, and claimant's signature. The form serves as an initial intake process for insurance companies, law firms, or medical malpractice attorneys handling such claims.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's information, including name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the claim details, including the incident date, location, and any involved parties.
3. Share your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
4. Describe the treatment or procedures received during the incident.
5. Specify the current claim status, whether it's pending, approved, or denied.
6. If applicable, provide medical expert testimony or supporting evidence.
7. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details such as phone number and address.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the claim details, including the incident date, location, and any involved parties.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share your medical history, including any pre-existing conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
* **Treatment Procedure** (`treatment_procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the treatment or procedures received during the incident.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the current claim status, whether it's pending, approved, or denied.
* **Medical Expert Testimony** (`medical_expert_testimony`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, provide medical expert testimony or supporting evidence.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the information provided.
