# Student Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Insurance Claim Form is a document used to report accidents or injuries that occurred during a student's academic activities, such as while on campus or traveling for academic purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Begin by filling out the first page, providing any general information about the incident.
2. On the second page, enter your email address and other relevant details.
3. Select your category (Student or Faculty) from the drop-down menu on the third page.
4. Enter the date and time of the accident on the fourth and fifth pages.
5. Provide a brief description of the incident on the sixth page.
6. Attach any supporting documents or photos to the seventh page.
7. On the eighth page, provide additional information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (Claim Form Page 1)**: This is the start of the form where you can enter any general information about the incident.
* **Email Address (Claim Form Page 2)**: Enter your email address so that the insurance provider can contact you with updates or further instructions.
* **Category (Claim Form Page 3)**: Select 'Student' or 'Faculty' to indicate your category.
* **Date of Accident (Claim Form Page 4)**: Enter the date the accident occurred.
* **Time of Accident (Claim Form Page 5)**: Enter the time the accident happened.
* **Claim Description (Claim Form Page 6)**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Supporting Documents (Claim Form Page 7)**: Attach any supporting documents or photos that can help with the claim.
* **Claimant Information (Claim Form Page 8)**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
