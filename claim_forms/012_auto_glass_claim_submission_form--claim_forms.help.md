# Auto Glass Claim Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit claims for auto glass damage repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out your contact information:**
	* Policyholder Name: Enter the full name as it appears on your insurance policy.
	* Policy Number: Enter your auto insurance policy ID number.
	* Contact Email: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your claim.
	* Contact Phone: Enter your best phone number for us to contact you.
2. **Describe the damage:**
	* Vehicle Make, Model, and Year: Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
	* VIN Number: Enter your vehicle's 17-character ID number.
	* Date of Incident: Enter the date when the damage occurred.
	* Type of Damage: Select the type of glass damage (e.g. windshield chip, crack, etc.).
	* Cause of Damage: Select the cause of the damage (e.g. road debris, vandalism, etc.).
	* Location of Damage on Glass: Describe the exact location of the damage on your vehicle's glass.
3. **Choose your repair option:**
	* Preferred Repair Provider: Select from our list of authorized repair providers.
	* Authorize Direct Billing: Select whether you authorize direct payment to the repair provider.
4. **Submit your claim:**
	* Review all the information you've entered and submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder Name (Policy Number)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your insurance policy and your auto insurance policy ID number.
* **Contact Email (Contact Phone)**: Enter your email address and best phone number for us to contact you about your claim.
* **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year**: Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
* **VIN Number**: Enter your vehicle's 17-character ID number.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Type of Damage**: Select the type of glass damage (e.g. windshield chip, crack, etc.).
* **Cause of Damage**: Select the cause of the damage (e.g. road debris, vandalism, etc.).
* **Location of Damage on Glass**: Describe the exact location of the damage on your vehicle's glass.
* **Preferred Repair Provider**: Select from our list of authorized repair providers.
* **Authorize Direct Billing**: Select whether you authorize direct payment to the repair provider.
* **Authorize Direct Billing**: This option allows the repair provider to bill you directly for the repairs. If selected, you will not need to pay out-of-pocket for the repairs.
