# Administrative Claim Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Administrative Claim Submission Form is designed to gather necessary information from claimants to process their claims efficiently. Please fill this form to submit claims related to any incidents or issues you have experienced with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your personal details:
	* Fill in your **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`) accurately.
	* Enter your **Claimant ID** (`claimant_id`) as per the provided hint.
	* Provide your **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`) for communication purposes.
	* If applicable, fill in your **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`) number for contact.
2.  Enter claim-related details:
	* Select the **Date of Claim Submission** (`claim_date`) when you submitted this claim.
	* Enter the **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`) when the incident occurred.
	* Specify the **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`) where the incident took place.
3.  Choose the type of claim you are submitting:
	* Select the **Type of Claim** (`claim_type`) from the given options (Property Damage, Personal Injury, Administrative Error, Financial Loss, Service Complaint, Policy Violation, Other).
4.  Identify the department involved:
	* Select the **Department Involved** (`department_involved`) from the given options (Administration, Human Resources, Finance, Marketing, Operations, IT, Sales, Customer Service, Executive, Other).
5.  Provide a detailed description of your claim:
	* Write a **Description of Claim** (`claim_description`) in a clear and concise manner.
6.  Specify damages:
	* Mention any **Damages Incurred** (`damages_incurred`).
7.  Monetary amount (if applicable):
	* Fill in the **Monetary Amount Requested** (`monetary_amount`) if it applies.
8.  Add any witnesses:
	* Provide the names of any **Witnesses** (`witnesses`) involved.
9.  Attach supporting documents:
	* Mention any **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`) attached.
10.  Claim status and resolution:
	* Fill in the **Current Claim Status** (`claim_status`) as per the options provided.
	* Specify your **Resolution Requested** (`resolution_requested`) as per your needs.
10.  Set a claim priority:
	* Choose the claim priority from the options **High**, **Medium**, **Low**.
11.  Add any additional information:
	* Attach any **Supporting Documents** (`evidence_attached`) and describe them.
12.  Acknowledge previous claims:
	* Indicate if you have filed **Previous Similar Claims** (`previous_claims`) before.
13.  Electronic signature:
	* Sign your claim with your **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`).

By filling out this form, you will provide the necessary information to process your claim efficiently. Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any potential delays or issues with your claim submission.
