# mental_health_service_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from claimants for mental health services claims. It's meant to be used by individuals or organizations providing these services to gather necessary details for processing and resolving these claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Claimant Information**: Enter the claimant's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
2. **Claim**: Briefly describe the mental health service claim being made.
3. **Date of Claim**: Specify the date when the claim was made.
4. **Claim Amount**: Enter the amount claimed for the mental health service.
5. **Claimant Provider**: Enter the name of the claimant's service provider.
6. **Claimant Doctor**: Enter the claimant's doctor's name.
7. **Claimant Phone Number**: Enter the claimant's phone number.
8. **Claimant Email**: Enter the claimant's email address.
9. **Claimant Address**: Enter the claimant's address.
10. **Claim Description**: Briefly describe the claim in more detail.
11. **Claimant Signature**: Sign the claimant's section to confirm their agreement.
12. **Claim Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person or organization reviewing the claim.
13. **Claim Reviewed Date**: Specify the date when the claim was reviewed.
14. **Claim Reviewed By Provider**: Enter the name of the person or organization reviewing the claim.
15. **Claim Approved Amount**: Enter the amount approved for the mental health service.
16. **Claim Status**: Choose the status of the claim (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
17. **Claim Status Comment**: Add any additional comments about the claim status.
18. **Claim Status Date**: Specify the date when the claim status was updated.
19. **Claim Status Provider**: Enter the name of the person or organization updating the claim status.
20. **Claim Notes**: Add any additional notes about the claim.
