# Sport Trading Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sport Trading Warranty Claim Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to file a warranty claim related to sports trading activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide details about the product you are claiming, including any relevant product codes or serial numbers.
3. Select the claim you wish to file, choosing from the provided options.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Describe the issue with your product in the provided description field.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the notes field.
7. Assign the claim to a specific person or choose "other" as needed.
8. Verify your form ID, which will be automatically generated and displayed on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you regarding your claim.
* **Product Details (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the product details, including any relevant product codes or serial numbers.
* **Claim (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the claim you wish to file, choosing from the provided options.
* **Claim Date (id: 4)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Claim Time (id: 5)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Description (id: 6)** (`text`, required: false): Describe the issue with your product in a clear and concise manner.
* **Email (id: 7)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your claim.
* **Phone (id: 8)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your claim.
* **Notes (id: 9)** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes you think are relevant to your claim.
* **Assigned To (id: 10)** (`select_one`, required: false): Assign the claim to a specific person or choose "other" as needed.
* **Form ID (id: 11)** (`number`, required: false): Verify your form ID, which will be automatically generated and displayed on the form.
