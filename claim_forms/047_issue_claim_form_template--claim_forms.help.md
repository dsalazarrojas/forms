# Issue Claim Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Issue Claim Form Template" is a standard template used to collect and document workplace issues. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the issue is properly reported and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by entering your claimant's name and contact information in the "Claimant's Name," "Email Address," and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Select the correct "Issue Type" from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the "Date" and "Time" of the issue occurred in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Claim Description" field.
5. If necessary, upload any supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Enter the claimant's supervisor and manager's name in the "Claimant's Supervisor" and "Claimant's Manager" fields.
7. Enter the name of the issue manager in the "Issue Manager" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Claimant Information

* **Claimant's Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the claimant.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claimant's Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, required): This field is not used in this template.

### 2. Issue Information

* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of issue from the dropdown list.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, required): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (`issue_time`, time, required): Enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Issue Details 1**, 2, and 3** (`issue_details_1`, `issue_details_2`, `issue_details_3`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the issue.

### 3. Supporting Information

* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Claimant's Supervisor** (`claimant_supervisor`, text, required): Enter the name of the claimant's supervisor.
* **Claimant's Manager** (`claimant_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the claimant's manager.
* **Issue Manager** (`issue_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the issue manager.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_docs`, text, required): Upload any supporting documents related to the issue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the issue.

This help guide should provide you with a clear understanding of how to complete the "Issue Claim Form Template" accurately and efficiently.
