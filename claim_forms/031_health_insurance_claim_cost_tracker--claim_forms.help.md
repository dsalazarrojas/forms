# Health Insurance Claim Cost Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Cost Tracker form is designed to provide an overview of health insurance claims, including claim status, type, and related costs. This form helps insurance companies and claimants to quickly and easily access the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the claim information, including claimant details, insurance provider, and claim details.
* Select the claim date and type (e.g., Medical, Dental, Vision).
* Choose the claim status (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending).
* Enter any additional claim notes, contact email, and contact phone number.
* Select any relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claim Information** (`claim_info`): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **Claimant** (`claimant`): Enter the name and any other relevant details of the claimant.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`): Enter any additional details about the claim.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`): Enter the cost of the claim.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`): Select the current status of the claim (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending).
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`): Select the type of claim (e.g., Medical, Dental, Vision).
* **Claim Notes** (`claim_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the claim.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter any additional contact information.

Note: All fields are optional, but some may be required for the form to be submitted correctly.
