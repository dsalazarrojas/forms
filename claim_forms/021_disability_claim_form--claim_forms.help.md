# Disability Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Claim Form is used by claimants to provide essential information about their disability claims. This form helps gather details required for the processing and evaluation of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're in the right context and not duplicating any existing claims.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your claim.
3. Review each field carefully and make sure to complete every required field.
4. Sign and date the form, as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Form** (1, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your claim. This is a general field for you to provide any information about your claim that doesn't fit into other categories.
* **Member Information** (2, `text`, optional): If you're a member of an organization or group, provide your member information here. This could include your membership number, member ID, or any other relevant details.
* **Claim Details** (3, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your claim, including any relevant dates, events, or circumstances surrounding your claim.
* **Employer Section** (4, `text`, optional): If you're claiming against an employer, provide their name, address, and contact information here.
* **Claimant Signature** (5, `text`, optional): Sign and date this section to confirm that you're the claimant and have reviewed the information provided.
* **Physician Signature** (6, `text`, optional): Sign and date this section if you're including medical documentation or certification from a physician.
* **Employer Signature** (7, `text`, optional): If you're claiming against an employer, have them sign and date this section to confirm their involvement.
* **Claim Number** (8, `number`, optional): If you have a claim number, enter it here.
* **Claim Date** (9, `date`, optional): Enter the date you're filing this claim, or the date of the incident that relates to your claim.
* **Claim Status** (10, `select_one`, optional): Choose an option from the dropdown list to indicate the current status of your claim.
* **Employer Name** (11, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your employer's name and any relevant details.
* **Employer Address** (12, `text`, optional): Enter your employer's address if applicable.
* **Employer Phone** (13, `text`, optional): Enter your employer's phone number if applicable.
* **Employer Email** (14, `email`, optional): Enter your employer's email address if applicable.
* **Claimant Name** (15, `text`, optional): Enter your full name as it should be spelled.
* **Claimant Phone** (16, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Email** (17, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Claimant DOB** (18, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Claimant Address** (19, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Claimant Employer** (20, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your current or previous employer's name.
* **Reason for Disability** (21, `text`, optional): Describe the reason for your disability.
* **Doctor Name** (22, `text`, optional): If you're including medical documentation, enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor Specialty** (23, `text`, optional): Enter the specialty of your doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (24, `text`, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Signature** (25, `text`, optional): Sign and date this section if you're including medical documentation or certification from a doctor.

Please take your time to review each field carefully and complete the form accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, refer back to this guide for clarification or reach out to the relevant authorities for further assistance.
