# Receipt Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Receipt Claim Form is used by employees to submit receipts for business expenses, allowing for accurate tracking and processing in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all required information and receipts before filling out the form.
2. Click on the "Claim a Receipt" button to access the form.
3. Select the relevant receipt date and time from the calendar or clock icon.
4. Enter the amount of the expense in the "Amount" field.
5. Choose the expense type from the dropdown options (e.g., Business Meal, Business Trip, Business Meeting, or Other).
6. Select the expense date from the calendar or clock icon.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Receipt Date** (`receipt_date`): Enter the date you received the expense.
* **Receipt Time** (`receipt_time`): Enter the time you received the expense (if applicable).
* **Amount** (`amount`): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Expense Type** (`expense_type`): Select the type of expense you are claiming (e.g., Business Meal, Business Trip, Business Meeting, or Other).
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`): Enter the date the expense was incurred. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Attach all necessary receipts and supporting documentation to the form.
- Submit each receipt separately to avoid duplication.
- Review and update your submission before finalizing the form.
