# Medical Travel Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and reimburse medical travel expenses incurred by employees of your organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the medical travel expense.
2. Select the type of medical travel expense (Medical, Non-Medical, or Other).
3. Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
4. Provide the name and address of the medical provider.
5. Enter the start and end dates of the medical travel.
6. Describe the purpose of the medical travel.
7. Enter the destination of the medical travel (if applicable).
8. Enter the total medical expense amount.
9. Enter the name of the person who incurred the expense.
10. Enter the employee ID of the person who incurred the expense (if applicable).
11. If applicable, upload medical receipts.
12. Select the medical travel expense items (e.g. Transportation, Lodging, Meals, Parking, Other).
13. Enter the amount for each medical expense item.
14. Enter the quantity for each medical expense item.
15. Enter a description for each medical expense item.
16. Enter the subtotal for each medical expense item.
17. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
18. (Optional) Upload medical receipts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Travel Employer Information**: Enter your employer's information as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Medical Travel Expense Type**: Select the type of medical travel expense (Medical, Non-Medical, or Other).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
* **Medical Provider Name**: Enter the name of the medical provider who treated you.
* **Medical Provider Address**: Enter the address of the medical provider.
* **Medical Travel Start Date**: Enter the start date of the medical travel.
* **Medical Travel End Date**: Enter the end date of the medical travel.
* **Medical Travel Purpose**: Describe the purpose of the medical travel.
* **Medical Travel Destination**: Enter the destination of the medical travel (if applicable).
* **Medical Expense Amount**: Enter the total medical expense amount.
* **Medical Expense Claimant Name**: Enter the name of the person who incurred the expense.
* **Medical Expense Claimant Employee ID**: Enter the employee ID of the person who incurred the expense (if applicable).
* **Medical Travel Receipts**: (Optional) Upload medical receipts.
* **Medical Expense Items**: Select the medical expense items (e.g. Transportation, Lodging, Meals, Parking, Other).
* **Medical Expense Item Amount**: Enter the amount for each medical expense item.
* **Medical Expense Item Quantity**: Enter the quantity for each medical expense item.
* **Medical Expense Item Description**: Enter a description for each medical expense item.
* **Medical Expense Item Subtotal**: Enter the subtotal for each medical expense item.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: This form is for internal use only and all information provided will be reviewed and validated before reimbursement is processed.
