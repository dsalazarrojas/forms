# Nail Technician Team Review Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback from the nail technician team on an appointment with a client. It's essential to provide a thorough and accurate review to ensure that the client's needs are met and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant options that best describe the appointment from the "Review Type" and "Review Frequency" dropdown menus.
2. Enter the date and start and end times of the meeting in the "Meeting Date" and "Start Time" fields respectively.
3. Enter the meeting location and the name of the salon where the meeting took place in the "Meeting Location" and "Salon Name" fields respectively.
4. Enter the name of the nail technician who conducted the meeting in the "Technician Name" field.
5. Provide feedback and comments on the appointment in the "Review Comments", "Review Comments 2", "Review Comments 3", "Review Comments 4", "Review Comments 5", "Review Comments 6", "Review Comments 7", "Review Comments 8", and "Review Comments 9" fields.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Review Comments 2", "Review Comments 3", "Review Comments 4", "Review Comments 5", "Review Comments 6", "Review Comments 7", "Review Comments 8", and "Review Comments 9" fields.
7. Enter any next steps that need to be taken in the "Next Steps" field.
8. Provide the email and phone number of the client in the "Email" and "Phone" fields respectively.
9. Select the "Review Status" from the dropdown menu to indicate if the review is "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started".
