# Hair Specialist Review Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Specialist Review Appointment Form is used by patients to schedule an appointment with a hair specialist and by hair specialists to record and review appointment details, including patient feedback and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email, and contact number to ensure that we can reach you for any appointment reminders or follow-up.
2. Select the reason for your visit to let us know what you need from the hair specialist.
3. Choose a date and time for your appointment, and make sure to select the correct follow-up date and time if necessary.
4. Enter your reviewer name, contact information, and title to let us know how you will be reviewing the appointment.
5. Select the review status to indicate if the review is pending, complete, or in progress.
6. Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share about your experience.
7. Finally, click submit to send your completed form.
