# uk-rpm-magazine-review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather reviews from users about their appointment experiences with the RPM (Rapid Prototyping Method) team. This includes feedback about the appointment type, date, time, and overall review of the experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). This includes providing your email address and selecting the appointment type.
* Enter your email address in the field labeled "User Email".
* Select either "Yes" or "No" for the field labeled "Appointment Type" to indicate whether you had an appointment with the RPM team.
* If applicable, enter the date of your appointment in the "Date" field.
* If applicable, enter the start and end times of your appointment in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
* If applicable, enter the location of your appointment in the "Venue" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (user_email, text, \***): Enter your email address associated with your appointment with the RPM team.
* **Appointment Type** (appointment_type, select_one, ): Select either "Yes" to indicate you had an appointment or "No" to indicate you did not.
* **Date** (date, date, ): Enter the date of your appointment if you had one.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, ): Enter the start time of your appointment if you had one.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, ): Enter the end time of your appointment if you had one.
* **Venue** (venue, text, ): Enter the location of your appointment if you had one.
* **Review Notes** (reviewer_notes, text, ): Enter any additional notes about your experience.
* **Review** (review_text, text, ): Enter your overall review of your experience with the RPM team.
