# Contract Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Review Appointment Form is used to schedule and collect information about client appointments for review. This form helps ensure accurate and relevant data collection for efficient review processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's **Client Name**, **Client Email**, and **Client Phone Number**. These are required fields for communication and appointment scheduling.
2. Provide the **Client's Company Name** if applicable.
3. Select a preferred **Appointment Date** and **Time** that suits you best.
4. Choose the **Appointment Type** (In-Person Meeting, Video Conference, or Phone Call) for the review.
5. Enter the **Type of Contract** (e.g., Employment Contract, Service Agreement, etc.) and estimated **Contract Value** if applicable.
6. Select the **Urgency Level** of the review (Urgent, High, Normal, or Low) to prioritize the appointment.
7. Answer **Yes/No** to whether the contract has been **reviewed before**.
8. Enter the number of **Other Parties Involved** in the contract.
9. Provide any **Special Requirements** or **Additional Notes** for the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Full legal name of the client.
* **Client Email**: Primary email address for communication.
* **Client Phone Number**: Best contact number for the client.
* **Company Name**: Name of the client's organization if applicable.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select your preferred date for the review.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Select your preferred time slot for the review.
* **Appointment Type**: How would you like to meet for the review (In-Person Meeting, Video Conference, or Phone Call).
* **Type of Contract**: What type of contract needs review (e.g., Employment Contract, Service Agreement, etc.).
* **Contract Value**: Estimated value of the contract if applicable.
* **Contract Duration**: Length of the contract term.
* **Urgency Level**: Prioritize the review by selecting the urgency level (Urgent, High, Normal, or Low).
* **Has this contract been reviewed before?**: Answer yes or no.
* **Number of Other Parties Involved**: How many other parties are part of this contract.
* **Special Requirements**: Any special accommodations or requirements.
* **How did you hear about us**: Referral, Online Search, Social Media, Advertisement, Previous Client, or Other.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other information we should know for the review.
