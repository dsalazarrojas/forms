# Fitness Progress Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for reviewing the progress of a client's fitness program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary information for the client's appointment. Please follow these steps:

1. Choose the client from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the appointment date and time.
3. Fill in the training program details.
4. Write any progress notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: Select the client's name from the dropdown menu (e.g. Client A, Client B, or Client C).
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the appointment (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Training Program**: Enter a brief description of the training program being reviewed.
* **Progress Notes**: Provide any notes or comments about the client's progress.
