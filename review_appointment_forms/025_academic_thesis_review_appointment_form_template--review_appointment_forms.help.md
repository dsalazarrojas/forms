# Academic Thesis Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help students schedule a review meeting with their thesis advisor in an efficient and organized manner. By filling out this form, students can ensure that all necessary information is provided, and their advisor is well-prepared for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
*   Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
*   Provide your email address in the "Student email address" field.
*   Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID number" field.
*   Select your degree program from the "Degree program" dropdown menu.
*   Enter your department in the "Department" field.
*   Enter the title of your thesis or dissertation in the "Thesis or dissertation title" field.
*   Enter your advisor's name in the "Thesis advisor name" field.
*   Enter your advisor's email address in the "Thesis advisor email" field.
*   Select the type of review you are requesting from the "Type of review requested" dropdown menu.
*   Choose a preferred appointment date in the "Preferred appointment date" field.
*   Select a preferred appointment time in the "Preferred appointment time" field.
*   If the preferred appointment date is unavailable, choose an alternative date in the "Alternative date (if first choice is unavailable)" field.
*   Select your preferred meeting format from the "Preferred meeting format" dropdown menu.
*   Enter any specific topics or questions you want to discuss during the review in the "Specific topics or questions you want to discuss during the review" field.
*   Provide any additional notes for your advisor in the "Any additional notes for your advisor" field.
