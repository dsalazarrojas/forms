# Compliance Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a compliance review with the organization's compliance office.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Be realistic with your preferred dates and times.
4. List key personnel who will be present during the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name**: Your full name as the person or entity requesting the review.
* **Organization or Entity Name**: The full legal name of the entity being reviewed.
* **Primary Contact Email**: The email address of the primary contact person for the review.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: The phone number of the primary contact person for the review (optional).
* **Review Category**: Select the type of compliance review required.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (Option 1)**: Select your first choice for the review date.
* **Preferred Time Slot (Option 1)**: Select your first choice for the review time.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (Option 2)**: Select your second choice for the review date (if available).
* **Preferred Time Slot (Option 2)**: Select your second choice for the review time (if available).
* **Review Location or Method**: Select whether you prefer the review to be on-site, virtual, or off-site.
* **Key Personnel Attending**: List the names and roles of key personnel who will be present during the review.
* **Documentation Readiness**: Indicate whether all necessary documents are prepared and available for the review.
* **Special Instructions or Concerns**: Provide any specific areas of focus or concerns for the review (optional).
* **Appointment Confirmation Consent**: Confirm that this request is subject to confirmation by the compliance office.

## Tips
* Be realistic with your preferred dates and times.
* Ensure that all necessary documents are prepared and available before scheduling the review.
* List key personnel who will be present during the review.
* Provide any specific areas of focus or concerns for the review.
* Confirm that this request is subject to confirmation by the compliance office.
