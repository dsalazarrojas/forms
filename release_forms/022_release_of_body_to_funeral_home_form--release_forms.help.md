# Release Of Body To Funeral Home Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release the body to a funeral home for further processing. You will be required to provide the name, address, and other relevant details of the funeral home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form. You can save and resume your progress at any time, and you can also print the form for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Funeral Home Name** (`funeral_home_name`, required, text): Enter the name of the funeral home where the body will be released.
* **Address** (`address`, required, text): Enter the address of the funeral home where the body will be released.
* **City** (`city`, required, text): Enter the city where the funeral home is located.
* **State** (`state`, required, text): Enter the state where the funeral home is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, required, number): Enter the zip code of the funeral home's address.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, required, text): Enter the phone number of the funeral home.
* **Email** (`email`, optional, email): Enter the email address of the funeral home (if available).
* **Date** (`signature_date`, required, date): Enter the date of the release of the body to the funeral home.
* **Signature** (`signature`, optional, text): Enter your signature to confirm the release of the body.
* **Funeral Director's Name** (`funeral_director_name`, optional, text): Enter the name of the funeral director (if available).

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your funeral home coordinator for assistance.
