# Safekeeping Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and formalize a safekeeping agreement between two parties, outlining the terms and conditions under which a person or organization is authorized to hold or store something on behalf of another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person or organization that will be storing or holding the item (depositor_name).
2. Provide the address of the person or organization storing or holding the item (depositor_address).
3. (Optional) Enter any relevant details about the custodian, such as their contact information or role.
4. Describe the item being stored, including its type and value (item_description).
5. Specify the terms and conditions under which the item will be stored (storage_terms).
6. Enter the date on which the agreement was made (agreement_date).
7. Enter the date on which the agreement will end (end_date).
8. (Optional) Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement (notes).
9. Enter any specific terms or conditions of the agreement (agreement_terms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **depositor_name** (Required): Enter the name of the person or organization that will be storing or holding the item.
* **depositor_address** (Required): Enter the address of the person or organization storing or holding the item.
* **Custodian Details** (Optional): Enter any relevant details about the custodian, such as their contact information or role.
* **Item Description** (Required): Describe the item being stored, including its type and value.
* **Storage Terms** (Optional): Specify the terms and conditions under which the item will be stored.
* **Agreement Date** (Required): Enter the date on which the agreement was made.
* **End Date** (Required): Enter the date on which the agreement will end.
* **Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Agreement Terms** (Optional): Enter any specific terms or conditions of the agreement.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all necessary information is included.
- Save the completed form for reference and future updates.
