# Cultural Heritage Interview Recording Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects recording permissions from cultural heritage interview subjects, ensuring they are aware of and consent to being recorded during the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understand the purpose and instructions for this form.
2. Complete each section as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cultural Heritage Interview Recording Consent** (`1`, `text`, required)
	+ This is the main title of the form, which explains its purpose.
* **Participant Information** (`2`, `text`, required)
	+ Please provide basic information about the participant, including their name, role, or position.
* **Participant Consent** (`3`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose "Yes, I consent to be recorded." to indicate that you agree to be recorded. Choose "No, I do not consent to be recorded." if you do not agree.
* **Audio Recording Consent** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you consent to being audio recorded. Select "No" to indicate that you do not consent.
* **Video Recording Consent** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you consent to being video recorded. Select "No" to indicate that you do not consent.
* **PhotoGRAPHIC Consent** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you consent to being photographed. Select "No" to indicate that you do not consent.
* **Recording Start Time** (`7`, `date`, required)
	+ Enter the expected start time of the recording. This will be used to schedule the recording.
* **Recording End Time** (`8`, `date`, required)
	+ Enter the expected end time of the recording.
* **Recording Duration** (`9`, `number`, required)
	+ Enter the expected duration of the recording.
* **Recording Location** (`10`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the location where the recording will take place.
* **Recording Date** (`11`, `date`, required)
	+ Enter the date of the recording.
* **Recording Time** (`12`, `time`, required)
	+ Enter the time of the recording.
* **Interviewer Name** (`13`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your name and title or position.
* **Recording Device** (`14`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the make and model of the recording device to be used.
* **Recording Conditions** (`15`, `text`, required)
	+ Provide any additional recording conditions or instructions.
* **Recording Conditions Yes/No** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you agree to the recording conditions. Select "No" to indicate that you do not agree.
* **Recording Conditions Notes** (`17`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the recording conditions.
* **Participant Comments** (`18`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the recording process.
* **Recording Terms and Conditions** (`19`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter any additional terms or conditions for the recording.
* **Participant Email** (`20`, `email`, required)
	+ Enter the participant's email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`21`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the participant's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Address** (`22`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the participant's address for contact purposes.

Note: The form requires the participant to consent to being recorded and provides options for them to agree or decline. It also collects basic information about the participant and recording details for the interviewer.
