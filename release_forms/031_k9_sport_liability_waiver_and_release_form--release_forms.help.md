# K9 Sport Liability Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K9 Sport Liability Waiver And Release Form is a document used to collect information and obtain waivers and releases from participants, hosts, and organizers for K9 sports events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1.  Fill in the general information section with your name, address, and other relevant details.
2.  Provide event details, including the date, time, and venue of the event.
3.  Complete the liability waiver section, which includes details about your understanding of the risks involved in the event and your agreement to waive liability.
4.  Sign the release agreement, which includes your agreement to release the event organizers and hosts from liability.
5.  Fill in the emergency contact information section with details of who to contact in case of an emergency.
6.  Complete the event host details section, which includes the name, address, and contact information of the event host.
7.  Fill in the version control section with the version numbers of the liability waiver and release agreement.
8.  Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement and understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Tips
*   Before completing this form, please ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the event.
*   Please complete all fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form carefully before signing.
*   Ensure that all necessary approvals and permissions have been obtained before proceeding with the event.
*   If you are unsure about any section of the form, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
