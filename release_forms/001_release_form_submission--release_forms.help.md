# Release Form Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Release Form Submission is a critical document used to record and manage participant releases for events and activities. This form captures essential details about the participant, including their contact information, event specifics, and consent details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Release Form Submission, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Release Form** section, providing a brief description of the activity or event.
2. Move on to the **Participant Details** section, where you'll enter participant contact information, such as their email address and phone number.
3. Next, specify the **Activity Info** section to provide details about the event, including the date and time it occurred.
4. Enter the **Consent Date** and **Consent Time** to document the participant's consent for the release.
5. In the **Participant Email** and **Participant Phone** fields, enter the participant's contact information.
6. If applicable, enter the **Organization Name** of the participant.
7. Specify the **Event Date** and **Event Time** for the activity or event.
8. Finally, add any additional **Notes** about the participant's release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Form**: Enter a brief description of the activity or event. (Required: No)
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the participant's contact information, such as their email address and phone number.
* **Activity Info** (`activity_info`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide details about the event, including the date and time it occurred.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date on which the participant consented for the release.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Enter the time at which the participant consented for the release.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ If applicable, enter the name of the organization associated with the participant.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the event or activity.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the participant's release.
