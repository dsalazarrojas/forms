# Minors COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability release waiver for minors, designed to inform parents or guardians of their legal rights and responsibilities regarding COVID-19 vaccinations for their children. It is intended to be completed by parents or guardians to acknowledge their understanding of the terms and conditions of the waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Begin" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the minor's name, date of birth, and vaccination date in the respective fields.
3. Select the vaccination status from the provided options (Completed, Partially Completed, Not Completed).
4. Enter the vaccination location where the vaccination was administered.
5. Enter the parent's name, contact information, and signature to acknowledge that they have read and understood the terms and conditions.
6. Enter the emergency contact information (name, phone number, email) and any other relevant details.
7. Review and select the terms of service that you have read and understood.
8. Finally, enter the date you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor's Name** (`minor_name`): Enter the name of the minor.
* **Minor's Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the minor's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`): Enter the date the minor received the COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Select the status of the vaccination (Completed, Partially Completed, Not Completed).
* **Vaccination Location** (`vaccination_location`): Enter the location where the vaccination was administered.
* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent's Contact Information** (`parent_contact`): Enter the contact information of the parent or guardian (phone number, email, etc.).
* **Parent's Signature** (`signature`): Select that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the waiver.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact information (name, phone number, email).
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`): Enter the emergency contact email.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the name of the doctor administering the vaccination.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`): Enter the name of the hospital or facility where the vaccination was administered.
* **Doctor's Address** (`doctor_address`): Enter the address of the doctor administering the vaccination.
* **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`): Enter the address of the hospital or facility where the vaccination was administered.
* **Insurance Name** (`insurance_name`): Enter the name of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`): Enter the insurance provider's number (if applicable).
* **Insurance Address** (`insurance_address`): Enter the address of the insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Other Information** (`other_info`): Enter any additional information regarding the vaccination.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`): Select that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the waiver.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you signed the form.
