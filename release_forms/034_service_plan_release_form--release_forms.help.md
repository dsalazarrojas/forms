# Service Plan Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Plan Release Form is a document used to record the release of a service from a service provider. This form captures essential details such as the service provider's name, reason for release, date and time of release, customer information, and the customer's decision on the release. It is a crucial step in the release process to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the release and can make necessary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the service provider in the "Service Provider Name" field. This is the individual or organization providing the service.
2. Provide a brief reason for the release in the "Reason for Release" field. Explain why the service is being released or terminated.
3. Enter the date of the release in the "Date of Release" field. Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the time of the release in the "Time of Release" field. Use the 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
5. If applicable, enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
7. If the customer has a phone number, enter it in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. Make a decision on whether to release the service in the "Decision" field. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your decision.
9. Optionally, include any additional comments regarding the release in the "Comments" field.
10. Finally, sign and date the form with your customer's signature in the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider_name`, Required): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, Optional): A brief explanation of why the service is being released.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, Optional): The date when the service will be released (use MM/DD/YYYY format, e.g., 01/15/2021).
* **Time of Release** (`time_of_release`, Optional): The time when the service will be released (use 12-hour clock format, e.g., 02:00 PM).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_nam`, Optional): The name of the customer associated with the service.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Optional): The email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phon`, Optional): The phone number of the customer (if applicable).
* **Decision** (`releas`, Required): Choose whether to release the service with 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Optional): Additional notes on the release.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Optional): A signature from the customer confirming their acceptance or approval of the release (if applicable).
