# Client Testimonial Advertising Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Client Testimonial Advertising Release Form is used to collect genuine and truthful testimonials from clients to be used in marketing materials, such as websites, social media, and email marketing. This form helps clients share their experience and grant permission for the business to use their testimonial.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled out. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your full name, job title, and email address in the respective fields.
3. Specify the service or product you used and the length of time you have been a client.
4. Write your testimonial in the provided text field. Please make sure it is accurate and truthful.
5. Choose the approximate length of your testimonial (Brief, Medium, or Detailed).
6. Choose how detailed you would like your testimonial to be.
7. Answer the question about the biggest benefit or result you experienced.
8. Grant permission to use your testimonial for marketing purposes.
9. Select which materials you would like to be used to showcase your testimonial.
10. Choose if you would like your name to be used in the testimonial.
11. Choose if you would like your company name to be used.
12. Choose if you would like a photo to be included with the testimonial.
13. Choose if you would like a video testimonial.
14. Choose if your testimonial is exclusive or not.
15. Choose how long you would like the testimonial to be used.
16. Choose if you want to review the testimonial before it is published.
17. Choose if you would like your testimonial to be edited for grammar or brevity.
18. Answer if you are interested in receiving an incentive for the testimonial.
19. Confirm that your testimonial is truthful and based on your genuine experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 3, required): Enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (id: 4, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title or Position** (id: 5, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (id: 6, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **City and State** (id: 8, optional): Enter your city and state.
* **Company Website** (id: 9, optional): Enter the website of your company.
* **What service or product did you use?** (id: 11, required): Specify the service or product you used.
* **How long have you been a client?** (id: 12, optional): Specify the length of time you have been a client.
* **Your Testimonial** (id: 13, required): Write your testimonial in your own words.
* **Approximate length of testimonial** (id: 14, optional): Choose the approximate length of your testimonial.
* **What was the biggest benefit or result you experienced?** (id: 15, optional): Answer the question about the biggest benefit or result you experienced.
* **I grant permission to use my testimonial for marketing purposes** (id: 17, required): Grant permission to use your testimonial for marketing purposes.
* **I grant permission to use my name in the testimonial** (id: 19, required): Choose if you would like your name to be used in the testimonial.
* **I grant permission to use my company name** (id: 20, optional): Choose if you would like your company name to be used.
* **May we include a photo of you in the testimonial materials?** (id: 21, optional): Choose if you would like a photo to be included.
* **May we use a video testimonial?** (id: 22, optional): Choose if you would like a video testimonial.
* **Is this testimonial exclusive or can you provide it to other companies?** (id: 23, optional): Choose if your testimonial is exclusive or not.
* **How long may we use this testimonial?** (id: 25, optional): Choose how long you would like the testimonial to be used.
* **I want to review the testimonial before it is published** (id: 26, optional): Choose if you would like to review the testimonial before it is published.
* **We may edit the testimonial for grammar or brevity** (id: 27, required): Choose if you would like your testimonial to be edited for grammar or brevity.
* **I am interested in receiving an incentive for this testimonial?** (id: 29, optional): Answer if you are interested in receiving an incentive.
* **Confirm that this testimonial is truthful and based on your genuine experience** (id: 30, required): Confirm that your testimonial is truthful and based on your genuine experience.

## Tips

* Answer all questions truthfully.
