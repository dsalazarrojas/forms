# Conference Speaker Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information from speakers to grant video release rights for their presentations or talks. It's crucial that speakers understand the terms of use, distribution scope, and other conditions related to the recording and release of their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete legal name as the speaker.
2. Enter your contact email address for communication.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your organization or company name.
5. Enter your professional title.
6. Fill in the session title and date for the presentation.
7. Select the type of recording you agree to (video and audio, audio only, slides only, or no recording).
8. Choose how you want the recording to be used (conference website, on-demand library, social media, educational purposes, marketing materials, internal archive, or public distribution).
9. Select how you want the recording to be distributed (public unlimited access, registered attendees only, internal use only, or no distribution).
10. Choose whether you want to edit the content (yes, minor edits only, no editing without approval, or must approve final version).
11. Select the duration of use for the recording (unlimited perpetual, 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, or event only).
12. Indicate whether the presentation includes third-party content (no original content, have permissions, need to verify, or unsure).
13. Describe and confirm the permissions for third-party content if applicable.
14. Select the compensation for recording (no additional compensation, included in speaking fee, separate fee required, or to be negotiated).
15. Specify how you want to be credited (text).
16. Choose whether you need to review and approve the recording before release (yes must approve, yes but not required, no trust organizers).
17. Select the withdrawal rights (may withdraw within 30 days, may withdraw anytime, cannot withdraw after signing).
18. Fill in the name and contact information for the person or organization granting permissions (if applicable).
19. Enter any special conditions or restrictions related to the presentation.
20. Indicate whether you have legal representation (yes represented, no self-represented).
21. If represented, fill in the name and contact information of your attorney.
22. Select the date of signing the agreement.
23. Enter the name and contact information of the witness (if applicable).
24. If applicable, sign the witness signature.
25. Finally, review and confirm that you grant video release rights as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Full Name**: Your complete legal name.
* **Email Address**: Contact email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for communication.
* **Organization**: Company or institution name.
* **Professional Title**: Your position or title.
* **Session Title**: Name of the presentation.
* **Session Date**: Date of the presentation.
* **Session Time**: Time of the presentation.
* **Recording Type**: Type of recording you agree to (video and audio, audio only, slides only, or no recording).
* **Usage Rights Granted**: How the recording can be used (conference website, on-demand library, social media, educational purposes, marketing materials, internal archive, or public distribution).
* **Distribution Scope**: Where the recording can be shared (public unlimited access, registered attendees only, internal use only, or no distribution).
* **Editing Rights**: Whether you want to edit the content (yes, minor edits only, no editing without approval, or must approve final version).
* **Duration of Use**: How long the recording can be used (unlimited perpetual, 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, or event only).
* **Third-Party Content**: Whether the presentation includes third-party content (no original content, have permissions, need to verify, or unsure).
* **Third-Party Content Details**: Describe and confirm permissions for third-party content if applicable.
* **Compensation**: Compensation for recording (no additional compensation, included in speaking fee, separate fee required, or to be negotiated).
* **Attribution Requirements**: How you want to be credited (text).
* **Review and Approval**: Whether you need to review and approve the recording before release (yes must approve, yes but not required, no trust organizers).
* **Withdrawal Rights**: Whether you can withdraw consent (may withdraw within 30 days, may withdraw anytime, cannot withdraw after signing).
* **Contact for Permissions**: Name and contact information for permission requests (if applicable).
* **Special Conditions**: Any restrictions or special conditions related to the presentation.
* **Legally Represented**: Whether you have legal representation (yes represented, no self-represented).
* **Attorney Name**: Name of your attorney (if applicable).
* **Attorney Contact**: Attorney contact information (if applicable).
* **Agreement Date**: Date of signing the agreement.
* **Witness Name**: Name of the witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Signature**: Witness signature (if applicable).
* **Release Confirmation**: Confirm that you grant video release rights as specified.
