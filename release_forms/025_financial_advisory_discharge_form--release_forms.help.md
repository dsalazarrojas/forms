This form is for financial advisory discharge. It's intended to be completed by an individual seeking to end their relationship with a financial advisor. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the client's financial situation, including their name, the date of the relationship end, and the reason for the discharge. To avoid duplication, this form should not be completed by anyone who has already been discharged from a financial advisory relationship.

## Financial Advisory Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisory Discharge Form is used to document the end of a financial advisory relationship. It ensures that all relevant details are captured for both the financial advisor and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**, providing all requested information.
2. **Ensure the "Client Name" field is completed with the most up-to-date and correct information**.
3. **Enter the "Relationship End Date" as the date the advisory relationship ended**.
4. **Specify the "Reason For Discharge" in a clear and concise manner**.
5. **Provide any additional "Notes" regarding the relationship termination**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (ID: 2)**: The client's name as they appear on record.
* **Relationship End Date (ID: 3)**: The date the financial advisory relationship ended.
* **Reason For Discharge (ID: 4)**: A clear explanation of why the client wants to end the relationship.
* **Notes (ID: 5)**: Any additional information regarding the relationship termination that may be helpful for future reference.

## Tips
* Ensure the form is filled out carefully and accurately to avoid any potential discrepancies.
* If the form is completed electronically, ensure that the submitted data is accurate and complete.
* For any issues or concerns, contact <form@gic.mx> for support.
