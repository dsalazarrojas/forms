# Client Release Form For Hair Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a client release form for hair services, used to gather information about clients' contact details, services received, and consent for treatment. It is intended to ensure clients are informed about the risks and benefits associated with hair treatments and to provide a clear release of liability for the salon and stylist.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Make sure to provide your phone number, email address, and date of service.
3. Select the services you received during the treatment.
4. Answer the questions regarding allergies, sensitivities, and medical conditions.
5. Sign the consent agreement and release clause.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`): Enter the date you received the hair services.
* **Hair Stylist Name** (`steward_name`): Enter the name of the stylist who provided you with service.
* **Salon Name** (`salon_name`): Enter the name of the salon where you received the service.
* **Services Received** (`services_received`): Select all services you received during the treatment.
* **Do you have any known allergies or sensitivities to hair products?** (`allergies_sensitivities`): Answer yes or no.
* **If yes, please describe** (`allergy_details`): Provide details if you answered yes to the previous question.
* **Any previous bad reactions to hair treatments?** (`previous_reactions`): Answer yes or no.
* **Please describe the reaction** (`reaction_details`): Provide details if you answered yes to the previous question.
* **Current Scalp Condition** (`scalp_condition`): Select your current scalp condition.
* **Any medical conditions affecting the scalp or hair?** (`medical_conditions`): Provide details if you have any medical conditions.
* **Any medications that may affect hair treatment?** (`medications`): Provide details of any medications.
* **Consent Agreement** (`consent_agreement`): Select whether you agree or disagree with the consent agreement.
* **I release the salon and stylist from any liability for adverse reactions** (`release_clause`): Select whether you agree or disagree with the release clause.
* **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to ask your stylist or salon manager.
* This form is designed to protect both you and the salon, so please take the time to review it carefully.
