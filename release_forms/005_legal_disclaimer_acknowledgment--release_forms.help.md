# Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather confirmation from users that they have read and understood the legal disclaimer text.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1.  Enter the legal disclaimer text in the "legal_notice" field.
2.  Enter the date on which you confirmed the legal disclaimer in the "confirmed_date" field.
3.  Select "True" in the "confirmation" field to confirm your understanding.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *legal_notice* (text, required): Enter the legal disclaimer text that you have read and understood.
*   *confirmed_date* (date, optional): Enter the date on which you confirmed the legal disclaimer.
*   *confirmation* (select-one, required): Select "True" to confirm that you have read and understood the legal disclaimer.
## Tips
*   Ensure that you enter the correct legal disclaimer text in the "legal_notice" field.
*   Enter the date on which you confirmed the legal disclaimer accurately in the "confirmed_date" field.
*   Select "True" in the "confirmation" field to confirm your understanding.
