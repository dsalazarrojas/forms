## Fan Interview Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fan Interview Release Consent Form is a document that aims to capture the consent of participants (and their parents/guardians) for interviews conducted by our organization. This form ensures that all participants are aware of the purpose, scope, and potential risks associated with the interview, and are willing to provide their consent before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the interview.
2. Check the "Participant Consent" field to confirm that the participant is willing to participate in the interview.
3. If the participant is under 18 years old, please check the "Parent Consent" field to ensure that their parent/guardian has provided consent.
4. Sign your name in the "Signature" field to confirm your agreement.
5. Provide your name and contact information in the "Participant Name", "Parent Name", and "Email" fields, if applicable.
6. Add any additional event information in the "Event Information" field, if necessary.
7. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Consent**: Please select "True" to indicate that the participant is willing to participate in the interview. This is a mandatory field.
* **Parent Consent**: If the participant is under 18 years old, please select all relevant options from this field to indicate that their parent/guardian has provided consent. This is a mandatory field.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name in this field to confirm that you are aware of the purpose and scope of the interview, and are willing to participate. This is a mandatory field.
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as the participant. This is a mandatory field.
* **Parent Name**: If you are a parent/guardian, please enter your name in this field. This is an optional field.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. This is an optional field.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. This is an optional field.
* **Event Information**: Please add any additional event information related to the interview. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the form carefully before proceeding.
