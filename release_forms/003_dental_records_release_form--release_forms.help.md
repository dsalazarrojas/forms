# Dental Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a patient's dental records release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information page, which includes the patient's name, date of birth, address, and any medical history.
2. Ensure to fill out the medical history field with any relevant information.
3. Select whether medical records are authorized to be released.
4. Sign the form to confirm that you are the authorized person to release the medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address where they can be reached.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical information for the patient, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Records**: This field is used to provide a reason for releasing the medical records, if necessary.
* **Medical Providers**: List any medical providers who have treated the patient in the past.
* **Authorization**: Select 'Yes' if you are authorized to release the medical records, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you are the authorized person to release the medical records.
