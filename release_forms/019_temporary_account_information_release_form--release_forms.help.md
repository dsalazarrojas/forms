# Temporary Account Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Account Information Release Form is used to release temporary accounts held by employees. This form helps to gather necessary information and document details for the account release process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth account release process.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid any mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's details as per your records.
    *   **Name (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's name.
    *   **Email (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's email address.
    *   **Phone (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
    *   **Company (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's company name.
    *   **Job Title (Page 1)**: Enter the customer's job title.
*   **Account Number (Page 2)**: Enter the account number associated with the customer.
*   **Employee Name (Page 2)**: Enter the employee's name.
*   **Address (Page 2)**: Enter the employee's address.
*   **Department (Page 2)**: Enter the employee's department.
*   **Date of Release (Page 3)**: Enter the date the account is to be released.
*   **Time of Release (Page 3)**: Enter the time the account is to be released.
*   **Notes (Page 3)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the account release.
*   **Employee Notes (Page 3)**: Enter any additional comments or details from the employee.
*   **Date of Birth (Page 4)**: Enter the employee's date of birth.
*   **Time of Hire (Page 4)**: Enter the time the employee was hired.
*   **Job Title (Page 4)**: Enter the employee's job title.
*   **Account Number (Page 4)**: Enter the account number associated with the employee.
*   **Temporary Account Release Information (Page 5)**: This is a summary page where all the information will be displayed for final review and verification.
*   **Confirm Release (Page 5)**: Confirm that the information is accurate and complete before proceeding with the release.
