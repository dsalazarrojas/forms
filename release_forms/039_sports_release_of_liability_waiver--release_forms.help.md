# sports_release_of_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Release of Liability Waiver form is designed for individuals to acknowledge and consent to participate in sports-related activities, releasing liability and medical information. This form helps ensure that participants are aware of the risks involved and are willing to take on personal responsibility for their involvement in sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Identify an emergency contact person and provide their name and phone number in the Emergency Contact section.
3. If a medical condition or procedure applies to you, describe it in the Medical Conditions section.
4. Choose whether you want to release liability and give medical information in the Release and Medical Release sections.
5. If you have an emergency contact, specify their relationship to you in the Emergency Contact Relationship section.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Emergency Contact Relationship Note section.
7. Sign and date the Witness Signature section, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
8. Sign and date the Witness Signature Date section with the current date.
9. Sign and time the Witness Signature Time section with the current time.
10. If applicable, provide information on any medical procedures in the Medical Procedure section.
11. Provide any additional comments or notes on the Medical Procedure Note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your full name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **emergency_contact**: Identify a person to contact in case of an emergency, and enter their name and phone number.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Witness Information**: Select a witness who will vouch for your participation in the activity (e.g., Witness 1, Witness 2, or Witness 3).
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions or disabilities you have, or any medical procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Conditions Note**: Provide any additional information related to your medical conditions or procedures.
* **Release**: Indicate whether you are willing to release liability and take on personal responsibility for your involvement in the activity (Yes/No).
* **Medical Release**: Indicate whether you are willing to give your medical information to authorized personnel (Yes/No).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship to your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes on your emergency contact relationship.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign and date the witness signature section, indicating that you have read and understood the form.
* **Witness Signature Date**: Sign and date the witness signature date section with the current date.
* **Witness Signature Time**: Sign and time the witness signature time section with the current time.
* **Medical Procedure**: Provide information on any medical procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Procedure Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes on your medical procedures.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter any additional comments or notes on your emergency contact relationship.
