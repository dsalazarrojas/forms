# Church Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Church Photo Release Form is designed to ensure that participants in events or activities at your church understand and agree to the use of their photographs in various ways. It protects both the church and the participant by setting clear expectations and boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please read each section of the form carefully and ensure that you understand the implications of granting photo release.
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are a parent or guardian, please complete the form on behalf of the participant if they are a minor.
* Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, required): This is the name of the person being photographed. Please type it as it appears on their identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, optional): If you have an email address, please provide it to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, required): This is where the photographs will be taken. Please type the complete address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Please type the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Is the Participant a Minor?** (`minor_status`, required): If the participant is under 18 years old, select 'Yes'.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, optional): If the participant is a minor, please provide the name of the parent or guardian who is completing this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, optional): If the participant is a minor, please provide the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (`guardian_phone`, optional): If the participant is a minor, please provide the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Description of Photographs** (`photo_description`, required): Please describe the type of photographs that will be taken.
* **Event or Activity Name** (`event_name`, required): Please type the name of the event or activity where the photographs will be taken.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Please type the date of the event or activity where the photographs will be taken.
* **Intended Use of Photographs** (`usage_purpose`, required): Select all the ways the photographs will be used, such as on the church website, social media, etc.
* **Duration of Usage Rights** (`usage_duration`, required): Please select the duration for which the photographs can be used.
* **Will You Receive Compensation?** (`compensation`, required): If you receive payment or a gift for participating in the event, select 'Yes'.
* **Any Usage Restrictions** (`restrictions`, required): If you have any restrictions on the use of photographs, select 'Yes' and type a description.
* **Please Specify Restrictions** (`restriction_details`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for restrictions, provide a detailed description.
* **Permission Grant** (`grant_permission`, required): Select 'Yes' to grant permission for the photographs to be used.
* **Revocation Understanding** (`revocation_understanding`, required): If you select 'No' for permission grant, you will be able to revoke permission in writing.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, required): Please sign and date the form with your full name as the participant.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, optional): If the participant is a minor, please sign and date the form with your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, required): Please type the date of your signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, optional): If you are an adult accompanying the participant, you can sign and provide your name as a witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, optional): If you are an adult accompanying the participant, you can sign and date the form as a witness.
