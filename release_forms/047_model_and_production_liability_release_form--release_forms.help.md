# Model And Production Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals who have provided feedback or participated in a project or production, allowing them to release liability for any model or content they may have contributed. This form ensures that the rights and responsibilities of all parties involved are clarified and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by providing details about your project, such as its title, description, and relevant images or files.
2. Next, enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
3. If applicable, describe your relationship with us, such as if you are a collaborator, freelancer, or a participant in the project.
4. Finally, include any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the project or production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (Project Details)** (`project_details`, text, optional):
This field is where you can enter a brief description of the project, including its title and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`contact_details`, text, optional):
Enter your name, email address, and any other contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Contact Relationship (Contact Relationship)** (`contact_relationship`, text, optional):
If you have a relationship with us (e.g., collaborator, freelancer, or participant), describe it briefly.
* **Participant Info (Participant Info)** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the project or production.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
