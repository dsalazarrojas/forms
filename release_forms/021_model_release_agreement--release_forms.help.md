# model_release_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model release agreement form is used to gather information about a model release agreement between a model and a business. The form collects details about the business name, contact address, contact information, release date, expiration date, business use, and statements from both the model and the business sides.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the business name in the first field.
2. Enter the contact address in the second field.
3. Provide the business contact information, including email and phone number, in the third field.
4. Select the release date and expiration date from the calendar picker.
5. Check the box if the business is using the model's likeness for commercial purposes.
6. Enter a statement from the model in the seventh field.
7. Enter a statement from the business in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (1, text, required): This is the name of the business that is releasing the model's likeness.
* **Contact Address** (2, text, optional): This is the contact address of the business.
* **Business Contact Info** (3, text, optional): This field is for providing the business contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Release Date** (4, date, required): This is the date when the model's likeness was released by the business.
* **Expiration Date** (5, date, required): This is the date when the model's likeness will expire after being released by the business.
* **Business Use** (6, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the purpose of use for the model's likeness. Check the box if the business is using the model's likeness for commercial purposes.
* **Model Release Agreement** (7, text, required): This is the model release agreement statement from the model.
* **Model Statement** (8, text, optional): This field is for entering a statement from the model.
* **Business Statement** (9, text, optional): This field is for entering a statement from the business.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields before proceeding.
* Ensure that the business use field is properly selected to reflect the business's intention.
* Proofread the statements from both the model and the business sides to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant party for clarification.
