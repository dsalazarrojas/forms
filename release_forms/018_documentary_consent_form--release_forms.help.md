# Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consents for a research project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the participant name.
2. Enter the participant's email address.
3. Enter the participant's phone number.
4. Provide a title for the project.
5. Describe the project in a brief summary.
6. Indicate whether you are a participant in the project.
7. Check the boxes to indicate your consent to the use of image, audio, or video recordings and location data.
8. Confirm whether you have given your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **project title** (`project_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for the project.
* **project description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief summary of the project.
* **participant role** (`participant_role`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **image, audio, or video consent** (`image_audio_video_consent`, note, required: false): Check the box to indicate your consent.
* **location data consent** (`location_data_consent`, select_one, required: false): Check one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **consent given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: false): Check one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
