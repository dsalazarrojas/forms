# visage_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a COVID-19 Liability Release Waiver designed to gather information about an individual's medical conditions, medical history, and consent for medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question accurately and in full.
2. Provide detailed information about your medical conditions, medical history, and consent for medical procedures.
3. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Release Waiver** (`release_waiver`, text, optional): 
This field is not applicable for this form, as it is a liability release waiver.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): 
Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you in case we need more information or clarification.

### **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): 
List any medical conditions you have, including any allergies, disabilities, or chronic illnesses that may affect your treatment or medical care.

### **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): 
Provide a detailed medical history, including any previous medical procedures, surgeries, or treatments you have undergone.

### **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, optional): 
Describe any medical procedures, treatments, or surgeries you have undergone or are scheduled to undergo.

### **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): 
Please indicate your willingness to undergo medical treatment or procedure by writing your initials in the provided space. This will serve as proof of your consent.
