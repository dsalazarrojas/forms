# Soda Flavor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new soda flavors and their release status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the new flavor.
2. Select the flavors that this release belongs to (if applicable).
3. Provide a brief description of the new flavor.
4. Specify the submission status (approved, pending, or rejected).
5. Select the team members involved.
6. Choose the submission date and time.
7. Add any additional comments if needed.
8. Enter the name of the owner of the form.
9. Enter the name of the team leader.
10. Enter the names of team members (if not already selected).
11. Choose the release date and time.
12. Enter any release notes.
13. Specify the release status (approved, pending, or rejected).
14. Specify the submission status (approved, pending, or rejected).
15. Specify the team status (approved, pending, or rejected).
16. Enter the name of the form being released.
17. Enter the email address of team members (if not already provided).
18. Enter the email address of the form owner (if not already provided).
19. Enter the email address of the submission (if not already provided).
20. Enter the phone number of the team (if not already provided).
21. Enter the phone number of the submission (if not already provided).
22. Add any additional comments from the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Form Name** (`release_form_name`): Enter a brief name for the release.
* **Flavors** (`flavor`): Select the flavor(s) that this release belongs to.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the new flavor.
* **Submission Permission** (`submission_permission`): Specify whether the submission is approved, pending, or rejected.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`): Select the team members involved in the release.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Choose the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Choose the time of the submission.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Form Owner** (`form_owner`): Enter the name of the owner of the form.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`): Enter the name of the team leader.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): Enter the names of team members (if not already selected).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): Choose the release date.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`): Choose the release time.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`): Enter any release notes or comments.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`): Specify whether the release is approved, pending, or rejected.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Specify whether the submission is approved, pending, or rejected.
* **Team Status** (`team_status`): Specify whether the team is approved, pending, or rejected.
* **Release Form** (`release_form`): Enter the name of the form being released.
* **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`): Enter the email address of team members (if not already provided).
* **Release Form Email** (`release_form_email`): Enter the email address of the form owner (if not already provided).
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`): Enter the email address of the submission (if not already provided).
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`): Enter the phone number of the team (if not already provided).
* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`): Enter the phone number of the submission (if not already provided).
* **Team Comments** (`team_comments`): Enter any additional comments from the team.
