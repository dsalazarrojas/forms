# Player Endorsement Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Player Endorsement Participation Consent Form" is a document used to gather consent from both the player and their parent or guardian for participation in a sports program, camp, or other sports-related activities. This form aims to ensure that both parties are aware of and consent to the terms and conditions of participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Player's Details**: In the "Player Details" section, provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information as requested.
2. **Enter the Parent Guardian's Details**: In the "Parent Guardian Details" section, provide your name and any other relevant information as requested.
3. **Provide Player Consent**: In the "Player Consent" section, confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of participation.
4. **Provide Guardian Consent**: In the "Guardian Consent" section, confirm that you, as the parent or guardian, have read and agree to the terms and conditions of participation for your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other requested information to confirm your identity as the participant.
* **Parent Guardian Details**: Enter your name, and any other requested information to confirm your identity as the parent or guardian.
* **Player Consent**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of participation by typing your name and date.
* **Guardian Consent**: Confirm that you, as the parent or guardian, have read and agree to the terms and conditions of participation for your child by typing your name and date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that both the player and parent or guardian fill out their respective sections completely and accurately.
* Please review the terms and conditions of participation carefully before providing consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
