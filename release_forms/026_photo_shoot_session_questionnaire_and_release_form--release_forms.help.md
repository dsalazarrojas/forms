# photo_shoot_session_questionnaire_and_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients before their photo shoot session. It is used to gather details about the session, client preferences, and obtain consent for the release of photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the session details**: In the "Photo Shoot Session Details" field, please provide a brief description of your photo shoot session, including any specific details or requirements.
2. **Specify client preferences**: In the "Client Preferences" field, please select the options that apply to you, such as whether you would like to be contacted for future photo shoots or if there are any specific photos you would like us to capture during the session.
3. **Confirm photo release**: In the "Photo Release" field, please select whether you agree to the release of photos taken during the session. This is an important step to ensure that you are aware of and consent to the use of your photos.
4. **Provide contact details**: In the "Contact Details" field, please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you after the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Photo Shoot Session Details
* **Photo Shoot Session Details** (`photo_shoot_session_details`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your photo shoot session. Please keep this field concise and include any specific details or requirements you have for the session.
### Client Preferences
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select the options that apply to you. You can select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted for future photo shoots or if there are specific photos you would like us to capture during the session. You can also select "No" if you do not want to be contacted or have any specific preferences.
### Photo Release
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to confirm whether you agree to the release of photos taken during the session. Please select "Yes" to grant permission for the use of your photos or "No" to decline.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and content of this form before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please ask your photographer for clarification.
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you after the session.
