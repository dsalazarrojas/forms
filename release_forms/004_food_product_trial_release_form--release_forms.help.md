# Food Product Trial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants who are part of a food product trial. It is intended to be completed in conjunction with other trial-related documentation and is not required for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Participant Information page with any relevant details about the participant.
2. Answer the Allergy Disclosures questions to indicate if the participant has any allergies or sensitivities related to the food product being tested.
3. Select your Follow-up Preferences to indicate if you would like to receive any follow-up communications or feedback from the trial organizers.
4. Provide your consent to participate in the trial by typing in your name on the Participant Consent field.
5. Enter any feedback or comments you have about the trial in the Feedback field.
6. Sign your name on the Participant Signature field to indicate that you have read and understand the form.
7. Provide any relevant information about the food product being tested on the Food Product Information field.
8. Sign the Release Form Signature field to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the trial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): This page allows you to provide any relevant details about yourself as a participant in the trial.
* **Allergy Disclosures** (`allergy_disclosures`, select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" if you have any allergies or sensitivities related to the food product being tested.
* **Follow-up Preferences** (`follow_up_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you would like to receive follow-up communications or feedback from the trial organizers.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, optional): Type your name to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the trial.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the trial.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to indicate that you have read and understand the form.
* **Food Product Information** (`food_product_information`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the food product being tested.
* **Release Form Signature** (`release_form_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the trial.
