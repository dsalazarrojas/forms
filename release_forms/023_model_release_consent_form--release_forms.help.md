# Model Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the consent of models (people appearing in photographs) for the use of their images. The form helps ensure that the model is aware of and agrees to the terms of use of their images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the first field, "Going public", with a brief description of how the photograph will be used.
2. Check the box next to "You need you to use consent in order to have your models sign off on it to use their photographs?" if you require the model's signature for use of their photograph.
3. If applicable, provide a template in the "Use our hassle-free template" field to facilitate the consent process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Going public** (`model_release_consent`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of how the photograph will be used. For example, "The photograph will be used for promotional purposes on our website and social media."
- **You need you to use consent in order to have your models sign off on it to use their photographs?** (`model_signature_consent`, text, optional): Check this box if you require the model's signature for use of their photograph.
- **Use our hassle-free template** (`model_release_consent_template`, text, optional): If required, provide a template to facilitate the consent process. This can be a pre-formatted agreement that the model can sign off on.
