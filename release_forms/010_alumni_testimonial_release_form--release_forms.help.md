# Alumni Testimonial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Testimonial Release Form is a necessary step for students and graduates to release their testimonials and media usage permissions to the institution for promotional purposes. It's essential to understand this form's requirements and implications before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Read the introduction to understand the purpose and implications of this form.
2. Follow the step-by-step instructions to complete each section:
3. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** in the corresponding fields. These are required fields to ensure we can contact you for follow-up.
4. **Graduation Year** and **Degree Earned** fields are used to track your academic progress and achievements.
5. Select your **Major/Field of Study** to ensure we know which program you were part of.
6. In the **Testimonial Content** field, share your genuine experience and how the institution impacted your life.
7. **Type of Testimonial** field allows you to select the format of your testimonial (written quote, video, or photo).
8. **Specific Programs or Experiences** (optional) to highlight any particular programs or professors that had a significant impact on your life.
9. **How did your education impact your career?** (optional) to describe the influence on your professional journey.
10. **Photo Usage Permission**, **Video Testimonial Permission**, and **Exclusive Rights** fields are used to determine how we may use your media. Please review these options carefully.
11. **Name Usage Permission** and **Professional Information Usage** fields allow you to decide how your name and professional information are used with your testimonial.
12. **Approved Media Channels** and **Duration of Usage Rights** fields decide where and how long we may use your testimonial.
13. **Availability for Follow-up** field allows you to choose when you're available for further contact.
14. **How did you hear about this opportunity?** (optional) to specify the source of your invitation.
15. **Additional Comments or Questions** (optional) field is for any further information or concerns.
16. **Release Agreement** and **Date of Agreement** fields confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms.
17. **Signature Date** is required to validate your release agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as used in your testimonial.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as used in your testimonial.
* **Email Address**: For follow-up communication about this testimonial.
* **Graduation Year**: The year you graduated from this institution.
* **Degree Earned**: The highest degree earned from this institution.
* **Major/Field of Study**: The field of study you were part of during your time at this institution.
* **Testimonial Content**: A genuine account of your experience and how the institution impacted your life.
* **Type of Testimonial**: The format of your testimonial (written quote, video, photo, or case study).
* **Specific Programs or Experiences**: Any particular programs or professors that had a significant impact on your life.
* **How did your education impact your career?**: A description of the influence on your professional journey.
* **Photo Usage Permission**: Grant permission to use your photo in promotional materials.
* **Video Testimonial Permission**: Grant permission to record and use video testimonial.
* **Exclusive Rights**: Understanding of the rights granted to the institution for using your testimonial.
* **Name Usage Permission**: Decide how your name is used with your testimonial (full name, first name and last initial, or anonymous).
* **Professional Information Usage**: Decide if your professional information (job title, employer, etc.) is included with your testimonial.
* **Approved Media Channels**: Where your testimonial may be used (institution website, social media, print materials, etc.).
* **Duration of Usage Rights**: How long the institution may use your testimonial (indefinite, specific years, etc.).
* **Availability for Follow-up**: Choose a time frame for follow-up communication.
* **How did you hear about this opportunity?**: The source of your invitation.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Any further information or concerns.
* **Release Agreement**: Confirm your understanding and agreement to the terms.
* **Signature Date**: Validate your release agreement.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Optional - for verification purposes.
* **Industry/Sector**: Optional - for categorization purposes.
* **Current Employer/Organization**: Optional - for follow-up purposes.
* **Current Job Title**: Optional - for follow-up purposes.
* **Availability Contact**: Optional - for follow-up purposes.
* **Referral Source**: Optional - for tracking purposes.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Optional - for further discussion. 

## Tips
* Be honest and genuine with your testimonial content.
* Review each field carefully before submitting your form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your testimonial release form will be reviewed and processed upon submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
