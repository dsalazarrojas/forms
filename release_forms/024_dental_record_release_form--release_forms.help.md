# Dental Record Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Record Release Form is used to provide a new dentist or practice with relevant patient information to facilitate a smooth transition of care. This form is designed to be completed by the patient or a representative on their behalf, and it will be used to share patient details with the new dentist or practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **New Dentist** field with the name of the new dentist or practice the patient will be visiting.
2. Provide the patient's **Patient Information**, which can include any relevant background or medical details that will help the new dentist or practice understand the patient's needs.
3. Enter the patient's **Name**, including their last name.
4. If applicable, provide the contact **Contact Person** responsible for the patient's care at the new dentist or practice.
5. Enter the patient's **Contact E-Mail** so that the new dentist or practice can reach out if needed.
6. In the **New Dentist's Practice** field, enter the name of the new dentist's practice where the patient will be visiting.
