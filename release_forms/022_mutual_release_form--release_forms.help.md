# Mutual Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for mutual release agreements, allowing two parties to exchange information and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: mutual_release_form_page_1
* **mutual_release_form_page_1** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the mutual release agreement.
* **mutual_release_form_page_2** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the second page of the mutual release agreement.
* **mutual_release_form_page_3** (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the third page of the mutual release agreement.

Note: Since all fields are optional, you can leave them blank if they do not apply to your mutual release agreement.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the form creator for further clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.
