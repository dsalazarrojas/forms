# Fitness Transformation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who have completed the fitness transformation program. It will be used to gather details on their progress, feedback, and challenges they faced during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
- Select all that apply for the "Training Challenges" field.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Provide your name, and any other relevant details you wish to share about yourself. 
* **Participant Signed Consent**: Confirm that you have signed the participant contract and agree to participate in future evaluations. 
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you. 
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of emergencies or further communication. 
* **Training Challenges**: Select all challenges you faced during the program (e.g., Challenge 1, Challenge 2, Challenge 3). 
* **Gym Details**: Provide any relevant details about your gym or training experience. 
* **Coach Details**: If you had a coach, provide their name and any additional information about them. 
* **Program Enrollment**: Provide any relevant details about your enrollment in the program. 

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
- Please proofread your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
