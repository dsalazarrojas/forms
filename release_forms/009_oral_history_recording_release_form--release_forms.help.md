# Oral History Recording Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the participant's release and consent for their oral history recording to be released.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill in the required fields with the participant's information.
3. Sign and date the participant's signature field.
4. Ensure that you, as the researcher, sign and date your own signature field.
5. Fill out the release date, time, and location fields with accurate information.
6. Check the participant's consent checkbox to indicate their agreement to release their oral history recording.
7. Fill out the release terms field with any additional information or conditions for the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
    This field is for the participant to provide their name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Recording Info** (`recording_info`, text, optional):
    This field is for the participant to describe their oral history recording.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_one, optional):
    Choose whether the oral history recording is being released as an "Oral History Recording" or "Other".
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional):
    The participant signs to confirm that they have read and agree with the release terms.
* **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, text, optional):
    You, as the researcher, sign to confirm that you have obtained the participant's consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional):
    The date on which the participant signed the form.
* **Date Recorded** (`date_recorded`, date, optional):
    The date on which the oral history recording was made.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional):
    The date on which the oral history recording is to be released.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional):
    The time on which the oral history recording is to be released.
* **Release Location** (`release_location`, text, optional):
    The location where the oral history recording is to be released.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`, text, optional):
    Any additional notes or instructions for the release of the oral history recording.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, optional):
    Check the box to indicate that the participant has given their consent for the oral history recording to be released.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, date, optional):
    The date on which the participant signed the form.
* **Participant Signature Time** (`participant_signature_time`, time, optional):
    The time on which the participant signed the form.
* **Participant Consent Note** (`participant_consent_note`, text, optional):
    Any additional comments or notes from the participant regarding their consent.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact_info`, text, optional):
    The participant's contact information.
* **Researcher Note** (`researcher_note`, text, optional):
    Any additional comments or notes from the researcher.
* **Release Terms** (`release_terms`, text, optional):
    Any additional conditions or terms for the release of the oral history recording.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, optional):
    The participant's agreement to the release terms.
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participant_agreement_date`, date, optional):
    The date on which the participant agreed to the release terms.
* **Participant Agreement Time** (`participant_agreement_time`, time, optional):
    The time on which the participant agreed to the release terms.
* **Release Agreement** (`release_agreement`, text, optional):
    The release agreement between the participant and the researcher.
* **Release Agreement Date** (`release_agreement_date`, date, optional):
    The date on which the release agreement was made.
* **Release Agreement Time** (`release_agreement_time`, time, optional):
    The time on which the release agreement was made.
