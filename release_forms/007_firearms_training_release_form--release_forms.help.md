# Firearms Training Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firearms Training Release Form is a comprehensive document that captures participant and emergency contact information, as well as release details for firearms training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the participant's information on page 1.
2.  Provide contact information for the participant's emergency contact on page 2.
3.  Fill in any additional participant information on page 3.
4.  Provide emergency contact information on page 4.
5.  Sign the participant signature on page 5.
6.  Sign the emergency contact signature on page 6 (if applicable).
7.  Enter the participant's date and time on page 7.
8.  Enter the release date and time on page 8 (if applicable).
9.  Fill in any notes on page 9.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1, <label>Participant Information</label>, <name>participant_information</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>true</strong>): Enter the participant's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (Page 2, <label>Contact Information</label>, <name>contact_information</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>true</strong>): Enter the participant's emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 3, <label>Emergency Contact</label>, <name>emergency_contact</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>false</strong>): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Participant Information** (Page 4, <label>Participant Information</label>, <name>participant_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>false</strong>): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Emergency Information** (Page 5, <label>Emergency Information</label>, <name>emergency_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>false</strong>): Enter any additional emergency information.
* **Participant Signature** (Page 6, <label>Participant Signature</label>, <name>participant_signature</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>true</strong>): Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (Page 7, <label>Emergency Contact Signature</label>, <name>emergency_signature</name>, <type>text</type>, required/optional: <strong>false</strong>): Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately (if applicable).

* **Notes** (Page 10, <label>Notes</label>, <name>participant_notes</name>, <type>note</type>, required/optional: <strong>false</strong>): Enter any additional participant notes.
