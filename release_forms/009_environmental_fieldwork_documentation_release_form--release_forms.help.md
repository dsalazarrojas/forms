# Environmental Fieldwork Documentation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document environmental fieldwork releases. It captures the details of the project, team members, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required") and provide as much information as possible for the other fields.
2. Enter the project title and category in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter a brief description of the project.
4. Enter the names, emails, and numbers of team members and project leads.
5. Select the start and end dates of the fieldwork.
6. Enter the duration of the fieldwork.
7. List the names, emails, and roles of team members.
8. Enter any project notes.
9. List any fieldwork documents.
10. Enter the name and email of the submitter.
11. Enter the submitter's phone number and notes.
12. Enter any review comments.
13. Select the release date and approval date.
14. Select the review status.
15. Enter the submitter's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title of the project.
* **category** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the category of the project.
* **description** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **research_project** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the research project.
* **team_members** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the names of team members.
* **team_leads** (`6`, `text`, required/true): Enter the names of team leads.
* **project_leads** (`7`, `text`, required/true): Enter the names of project leads.
* **fieldwork_start_date** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the fieldwork.
* **fieldwork_end_date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the fieldwork.
* **fieldwork_duration** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the fieldwork.
* **team_member_names** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the names of team members.
* **team_member_emails** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emails of team members.
* **team_member_numbers** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the numbers of team members.
* **team_member_roles** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the roles of team members.
* **project_notes** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any project notes.
* **fieldwork_documents** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any fieldwork documents.
* **submitter** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **submitter_email** (`18`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the submitter.
* **submitter_phone** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **submitter_notes** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes of the submitter.
* **review_comments** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any review comments.
* **release_date** (`22`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the release date.
* **approval_date** (`23`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the approval date.
* **review_status** (`24`, `text`, required/true): Select the review status.
* **submitter_signature** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the submitter's signature.
