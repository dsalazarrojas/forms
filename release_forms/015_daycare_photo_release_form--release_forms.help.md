# Daycare Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daycare Photo Release Form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for the release of their child's photos and videos by the daycare center or school. This form is required for all new photo and video media releases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Enter a valid phone number.
5. Enter the child's name.
6. Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
7. Enter the child's school or daycare center.
8. If applicable, select the grade level of the child.
9. Briefly describe the photo being released.
10. Select the media types (photos and/or videos) to be released.
11. Confirm your acceptance of the release of the child's photos and videos.
12. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Child's Name**: Enter the child's name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **School or Daycare Center**: Enter the child's school or daycare center.
* **Grade Level**: (Optional) Select the grade level of the child, if applicable.
* **Photo Description**: Briefly describe the photo being released.
* **Media Types**: Select the media types (photos and/or videos) to be released.
* **Acceptance**: Confirm your acceptance of the release of the child's photos and videos.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments you may have.
