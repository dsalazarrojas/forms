# waste_management_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for releasing waste management records. It is intended to capture essential information about the company, individual, or department being released, and to gather consent for the release. The form is usually used for official purposes such as reporting, auditing, or regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Company Name, Date of Signature, Time of Signature, Printed Name, and Reason For Release.
2. If applicable, select the Contact Person and Department from the provided options.
3. Enter your Phone Number and Email address.
4. Provide your consent for the release.
5. Sign the form digitally using the Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (required, text): Enter the name of the company.
* **Date Of Birth** (optional, date): This field is not applicable for this form, but it might be useful in other contexts.
* **Address** (required, text): Enter the address of the company.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Person** (optional, select_one): Select the contact person from the provided options.
* **Department** (optional, select_multiple): Select the department(s) from the provided options.
* **Phone Number** (required, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Consent** (optional, select_one): Select "True" if you agree with the release, otherwise select "False".
* **Reason For Release** (required, text): Enter the reason for releasing the waste management records.
* **Date of Signature** (required, date): Enter the date of signature.
* **Time of Signature** (required, time): Enter the time of signature.
* **Signature** (required, note): Sign the form digitally.
* **Printed Name** (required, text): Enter your printed name.
* **Title** (optional, text): Enter your title.
