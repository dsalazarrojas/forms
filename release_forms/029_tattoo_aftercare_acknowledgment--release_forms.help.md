# Tattoo Aftercare Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect acknowledgment from the client and studio/artist that they have reviewed and understood the aftercare instructions after a tattoo session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the aftercare instructions provided by the studio/artist.
2.  Select one of the two options for both the studio/artist and client signature fields.
3.  Sign and date the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Signature** (Studio, select_one, required): Choose "Studio" if you are the studio owner or manager, or "Artist" if you are the artist who performed the tattoo session. Selecting this option will confirm that the client has received and understood the aftercare instructions.
* **Client Signature** (Client, select_one, required): Choose "I have read and understood the aftercare instructions" if you have indeed reviewed the aftercare instructions provided by the studio/artist, or "I have not read or understood the aftercare instructions" if you have not.
*   The date and time of signature will be automatically populated.
