# Photography Copyright Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Copyright Release Form is a document that allows you to release the copyright ownership of a photograph and set the terms and conditions for its use. This form is used to grant permission for others to use your photograph, and by filling it out, you can specify how and when it can be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the **Photographer**.
2. Enter the **Copyright Owner Name**, if different from your own.
3. Specify the **Copyright Date** when the photograph was taken.
4. Choose the **Permission Type**: Exclusive (only you can use it) or Non-Exclusive (others can use it).
5. Select the **Permission Duration**: Forever, Until Cancelled, 1 Year, 5 Years, or 10 Years.
6. Choose the **Territory**: Worldwide, North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia, or South America.
7. Select the **Usage**: Print, Online, Billboard, Advertising, Merchandise, or Digital Use.
8. Fill in the **Signed Date** when you sign this form.
9. Sign your name as the **Photographer**.
10. If you're not the photographer, fill in the **Signed Position** (e.g., Photographer, Copyright Owner, Artist, Model, Assistant, or Other).
11. Specify your **Signed Position Other** if you're not one of the listed options.
12. Sign your name as the **Witness**.
13. Sign your name as the **Photographer's Signature**.
14. Fill in the **Date** when you sign this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`): Your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Name** (`copyright_owner_name`): Name of the copyright owner, if different from yours.
* **Copyright Date** (`copyright_date`): Date when the photograph was taken.
* **Permission Type** (`permission_type`):
	+ **Exclusive**: Only you can use it.
	+ **Non-Exclusive**: Others can use it.
* **Permission Duration** (`permission_duration`):
	+ **Forever**: Until revoked.
	+ **Until Cancelled**: Permission can be cancelled at any time.
	+ **1 Year**: Permission is granted for 1 year.
	+ **5 Years**: Permission is granted for 5 years.
	+ **10 Years**: Permission is granted for 10 years.
* **Territory** (`territory`):
	+ **Worldwide**: Everywhere in the world.
	+ **North America**: USA, Canada, Mexico, etc.
	+ **Europe**: European countries.
	+ **Asia**: Countries in Asia.
	+ **Africa**: Countries in Africa.
	+ **Australia**: Countries in Australia.
	+ **South America**: Countries in South America.
* **Usage** (`usage`):
	+ **Print**: For print media (newspapers, magazines, etc.).
	+ **Online**: For online use (websites, social media, etc.).
	+ **Billboard**: For billboard advertising.
	+ **Advertising**: For advertising purposes.
	+ **Merchandise**: For sale as merchandise.
	+ **Digital Use**: For digital use (e.g., video, audio, etc.).
* **Signed Date** (`signed_date`): Date when you sign this form.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`): Your name as the photographer or other party signing.
* **Signed Position** (`signed_position`):
	+ **Photographer**: You are the photographer.
	+ **Copyright Owner**: You are the copyright owner.
	+ **Artist**: You are an artist involved in the photograph.
	+ **Model**: You are a model in the photograph.
	+ **Assistant**: You are an assistant to the photographer.
	+ **Other (Specify Below)**: Specify your role in the photograph.
* **Signed Position Other** (`signed_position_other`): Specify your role if not listed above.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Your signature as the photographer or other party signing.
* **Witness** (`witness`): Name of the witness, if any.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): Signature of the witness.
* **Copyright Agreement** (`copyright_agreement`): A statement of the agreement between you and the other party.
* **Photographer's Signature** (`photographer_signature`): Your signature as the photographer.
* **Date** (`date`): Date when you sign this form.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. If you have specific questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
