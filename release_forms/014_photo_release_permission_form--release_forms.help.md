We're glad you're using our Photo Release Permission Form to request photo releases from your subjects. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the form, and what each field is for.

# photo_release_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo release permission form is used to collect the necessary information from a photo subject before taking or sharing their photo. It ensures that both you and the subject understand the terms of the photo release and can agree to them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Photo Release Form Fields**
	* **photo_release_form_fields (Text)**: This is a placeholder for the text describing the photo release form, which will be filled in by the subject upon completing the form.
2. Follow the instructions for each field below to ensure you complete the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: A placeholder for the name of the subject giving photo release permission.
* **Email (Text)**: A placeholder for the email address of the subject giving photo release permission.
* **Phone (Text)**: A placeholder for the phone number of the subject giving photo release permission.
* **Release Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of release that applies to the photo: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Photo Release Date (Date)**: The date the photo was taken or will be released.
* **Photo Release Time (Time)**: The time the photo was taken or will be released.
* **Photo Release Note (Note)**: A text field for any additional notes or comments related to the photo release.
* **Photo Usage (Select Multiple)**: Choose how the photo will be used: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Photo Usage Note (Note)**: A text field for any additional notes or comments related to photo usage.
