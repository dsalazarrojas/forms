# Chair Massage Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from clients for a safe and effective chair massage experience. It's essential to fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure the therapist understands your needs and can provide the best service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2.  Fill out all required fields (marked as `required`).
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please ask your therapist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): The date you're completing this form.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Your email address for record-keeping purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): The full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): List any medical conditions you may have (e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes).
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, select_one, required): Indicate if you're currently taking any medications.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any allergies.
* **Allergy Details** (`allergy_details`, text, optional): If you have allergies, please specify.
* **Previous Massage Experience** (`previous_massage_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had massage therapy before.
* **Massage Goals** (`massage_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select your goals for this massage experience (e.g., relaxation, stress relief).
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required): Indicate your consent to receive chair massage therapy.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with massage therapy.
* **Health Information Accuracy** (`health_accuracy`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provided health information is accurate.
* **Communication Agreement** (`communication_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to communicate any discomfort during the massage.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Release the therapist and facility from liability.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Consent to photos being taken for record-keeping purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other information the therapist should know.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name as your signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date of signing.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, text, optional): The therapist will sign and date this field.
