# Chemical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chemical Release Form is used to report and document chemical release or spill incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and contact phone number in the "Submitter Name" and "Contact Phone" fields.
*   Provide the street address of the incident site in the "Site Address" field.
*   Briefly describe the type of chemical involved in the "Chemical Type" field.
*   Enter the estimated volume of the release in numeric value and units in the "Estimated Volume" field.
*   Select the best match for the exposure potential of the incident from the dropdown menu in the "Exposure Potential" field.
*   Describe the observed environmental impact in the "Environmental Impact Observed" field.
*   Outline the steps to be taken to remediate the situation in the "Corrective Actions Planned" field.
*   Indicate if photos are available for review in the "Photos Attached" field.
*   Mark if further investigation is needed in the "Follow Up Needed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Submitter Name (Text)**: This field collects the name of the person submitting the report.
*   **Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for urgent follow up.
*   **Site Address (Text)**: Provide the street address of the incident site.
*   **Chemical Type (Text)**: Briefly describe the type of chemical substance involved.
*   **Estimated Volume (Number)**: Enter the numeric value of the release in the units specified in the comments section.
*   **Exposure Potential (Select)**:
    *   **None**: No exposure occurred.
    *   **Limited**: Exposure was possible but not significant.
    *   **Moderate**: Exposure was possible with potential health effects.
    *   **High**: Exposure was possible with significant health effects.
*   **Environmental Impact Observed (Text)**: Describe any observed environmental impact.
*   **Corrective Actions Planned (Text)**: Outline steps to be taken to remediate the situation.
*   **Photos Attached (Select)**:
    *   **True**: Photos are available for review.
    *   **False**: No photos are available.
    *   **Not Applicable**: Not applicable for this incident.
*   **Follow Up Needed (Select)**:
    *   **True**: Further investigation is required.
    *   **False**: Further investigation is not required.

## Tips

*   Be sure to review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints for guidance.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
