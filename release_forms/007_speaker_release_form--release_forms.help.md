# Speaker Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Release Form is a tool to collect speaker information and have them agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "speaker name" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of yourself in the "content details" field.
3.  If applicable, upload or enter the image of yourself in the "speaker image" field.
4.  Read through the "terms and conditions" and check the box to confirm you have read and understand it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name**: Your name is required to identify you as a speaker.
* **Speaker Info**: Briefly describe yourself and your background to help organizers better understand your qualifications.
* **Speaker Image** (Optional): If you have an image of yourself, please provide it. This can help organizers promote you more effectively.
* **Content Details**: Describe your presentation or topic in detail. This will help the audience understand what to expect from you.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event. This includes any expectations, rules, or guidelines for your participation.
