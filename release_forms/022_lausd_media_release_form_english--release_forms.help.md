# lausd-media-release-form-english - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal information for media release purposes related to Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name in the "Parent or Guardian First Name" and "Parent or Guardian Last Name" fields.
4.  Enter the address in the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name (`First Name`, text, optional):** Enter your name here.
*   **Last Name (`Last Name`, text, optional):** Enter your last name here.
*   **Parent or Guardian First Name (`Parent or Guardian First Name`, text, optional):** If you are a parent or guardian, enter your first name here.
*   **Parent or Guardian Last Name (`Parent or Guardian Last Name`, text, optional):** If you are a parent or guardian, enter your last name here.
*   **Address 1 (`Address 1`, text, optional):** Enter the first part of your address here.
*   **Address 2 (`Address 2`, text, optional):** Enter the second part of your address here.

## Tips
*   Make sure to complete all the required fields to ensure the form is completed correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the form owner for help.
*   The form is designed to be used as-is or modified according to your needs, just give credit to the form author.
