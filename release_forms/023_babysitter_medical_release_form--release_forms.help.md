# Babysitter Medical Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Babysitter Medical Release Form is a document that provides authorization for a babysitter to care for your child and to provide emergency medical treatment if needed. This form helps ensure that the babysitter is aware of your child's medical needs and conditions, and allows them to take necessary actions in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). The form is organized into sections that ask for your name and contact information, your child's name and birthdate, the babysitter's name and contact information, and information about your child's medical needs and conditions. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): This is your name and the name of the parent or guardian authorized to provide information and consent for this form.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): This is your primary phone number where the babysitter can contact you.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): This is your email address where the babysitter can contact you.
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, text, required): This is your child's full name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, required): This is your child's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Babysitter Name** (`babysitter_name`, text, required): This is the name of the authorized caregiver.
* **Dates of Care** (`care_dates`, text, required): This is when the babysitter will be caring for your child.
* **Primary Care Physician** (`medical_provider`, text, required): This is the name of your child's primary care physician and their practice.
* **Doctor Phone Number** (`doctor_phone`, text, required): This is the contact phone number for your child's primary care physician.
* **Preferred Hospital** (`preferred_hospital`, text, required): This is the name of the nearest preferred hospital to your location.
* **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`, text, required): This is the full address of the preferred hospital.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, required): This is the name of your child's health insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): This is your child's insurance policy or member ID number.
* **Group Number** (`group_number`, text, optional): This is your child's insurance group number (if applicable).
* **Known Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): This is a list of any chronic medical conditions your child has.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): This is a list of any allergies your child has, including food, medication, and environmental.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required): This is a list of your child's current medications and dosages.
* **Medical Treatment Authorization** (`authorization_statement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you authorize the babysitter to seek emergency medical treatment for your child.
* **Scope of Authorized Treatment** (`treatment_scope`, select_multiple, required): Choose the scope of treatment that the babysitter is authorized to provide.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact** (`alternate_contact`, text, required): This is the name and phone number of an alternate emergency contact.
* **Date Signed** (`parent_signature_date`, date, required): This is the date this form was signed by you.
* **Authorization Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): This is the date this form expires (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Make sure to review this form carefully before submitting it.
* This form is to be filled out once per child, and is not a recurring form.
* The babysitter should keep this form with them at all times while caring for your child.
