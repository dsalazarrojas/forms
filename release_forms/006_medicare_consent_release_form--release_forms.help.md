# medicare_consent_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of medical information from one healthcare provider to another, obtaining the patient's consent and ensuring the accuracy of the transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Practice Name**: Enter the name of the medical practice or facility that is transferring the patient's medical records.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields accurately.
3. **Date of Transfer**: Provide the date the patient's records were transferred.
4. **Healthcare Providers**: Check the boxes of all healthcare providers involved in the patient's care.
5. **Consent Status**: Confirm that the patient has consented to the transfer of their records.
6. **Comments**: Add any relevant comments or details about the transfer.
7. **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form to authenticate the transfer process.
8. **Signature**: Sign the form to authenticate your identity.
9. **Printed Name**: Write your printed name to further authenticate the form.
10. **Printed Date**: Print the date you signed the form.
11. **Printed Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the medical facility that printed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Name** (id: 1, name: practice_name): Enter the name of the medical practice or facility that is transferring the patient's records.
* **Patient Name** (id: 2, name: patient_name): Enter the patient's name as they are registered in the system.
* **Date of Transfer** (id: 3, name: date_of_transfer): Provide the date the patient's records were transferred.
* **Healthcare Providers** (id: 4, name: healthcare_providers): Check the boxes of all healthcare providers involved in the patient's care.
* **Consent Status** (id: 5, name: consent_status): Confirm that the patient has consented to the transfer of their records.
* **Comments** (id: 6, name: comments): Add any relevant comments or details about the transfer.
* **Signature Date** (id: 7, name: signature_date): Date and sign the form to authenticate the transfer process.
* **Signature** (id: 8, name: signature): Sign the form to authenticate your identity.
* **Printed Name** (id: 9, name: printed_name): Write your printed name to further authenticate the form.
* **Printed Date** (id: 10, name: printed_date): Print the date you signed the form.
* **Printed Hospital Name** (id: 11, name: printed_hospital_name): Enter the name of the medical facility that printed the form.
* **Prophets** (id: 12, name: patient_signature): Sign your signature as the patient or authorized representative.
* **Healthcare Organization** (id: 13, name: healthcare_organization): Check the boxes of all healthcare organizations involved in the patient's care.
* **Healthcare Provider** (id: 14, name: healthcare_provider): Confirm that the healthcare provider is authorized to access the patient's records.
