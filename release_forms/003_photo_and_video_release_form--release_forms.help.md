# Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from clients for the use of their photos, videos, and audio recordings. It's a crucial document to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the terms and conditions of the content's usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the client, which is required to identify yourself.
2. Provide a detailed description of the material you are releasing, which helps us understand what we are dealing with.
3. Specify how the material will be used, which ensures we respect your wishes.
4. Grant your permission for the use of the material, which is a critical part of this form.
5. Provide any necessary credits or acknowledgments for the material.
6. Add your email and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Sign and date the form, which confirms your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the client.
* **Description** (`release_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the material you are releasing.
* **Material Usage** (`material_usage`, text, optional): Specify how the material will be used, e.g., for marketing, educational, or promotional purposes.
* **Client Permission** (`client_permission`, select_one, optional): Grant your permission for the use of the material. Please select 'True' to give your permission.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person signing this form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): The date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): The time you signed this form.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the type of material being released, e.g., photo, video, audio, or none.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, text, optional): Provide any necessary credits or acknowledgments for photos.
* **Video Credit** (`video_credit`, text, optional): Provide any necessary credits or acknowledgments for videos.
* **Audio Credit** (`audio_credit`, text, optional): Provide any necessary credits or acknowledgments for audio recordings.
* **Other Materials** (`other_materials`, text, optional): If the material is not photo, video, or audio, describe it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signed With** (`signed_with`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person signing this form.
* **Signed Date** (`signed_date`, date, optional): The date you signed this form.
* **Signed Time** (`signed_time`, time, optional): The time you signed this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign this form to confirm your agreement.
* **Signed At** (`signed_at`, date, optional): The date you signed this form.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): The date of material release.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): The time of material release.
* **Client Permission 2** (`client_permission_2`, text, optional): This field is not described in the YAML file, so we'll assume it's for additional client permission. Please provide any necessary additional permission details.
