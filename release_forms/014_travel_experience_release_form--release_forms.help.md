# Travel Experience Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to release their travel experience and associated details to the travel agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, which includes:
	* Participant Details (page 1)
	* Email (page 2)
	* Contact Number (page 3)
	* Emergency Contact (page 4)
	* Emergency Phone (page 5)
	* Release Terms and Conditions (page 6)
	* Medical Conditions (page 7)
2. Answer the medical questions in sections 8-11:
	* Medications and Allergies (page 8)
	* Medical History (page 9)
	* Insurance (page 10)
	* Emergency Procedure (page 11)
3. Answer the release questions in sections 12-15:
	* Medical Release (page 12)
	* Emergency Release (page 13)
	* Travel Agency Acknowledgement (page 14)
	* Participant Acknowledgement (page 15)
	* Travel Agency Signature (page 16)
	* Participant Signature (page 17)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

• **Participant Details** (Page 1)
  • This field is used to capture your personal information.
  • Please enter your name and any other details as required.

• **Email** (Page 2)
  • Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.

• **Contact Number** (Page 3)
  • Enter your contact number, which will be used to reach you in case of emergencies.

• **Address** (Page 4)
  • This field is optional, but it's useful for the travel agency to know your address for emergency contact purposes.

• **Release Date** (Page 5)
  • Enter the date you are releasing your travel experience.

• **Travel Experience** (Page 6)
  • This field is optional, but it's used to capture any additional travel experience information.

• **Tour Leader Name** (Page 7)
  • Enter the name of the tour leader or guide, if applicable.

• **Travel Agency Name** (Page 8)
  • Enter the name of the travel agency, if applicable.

• **Travel Agency Email** (Page 9)
  • Enter the email address of the travel agency, if applicable.

• **Emergency Contact** (Page 10)
  • Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

• **Emergency Phone** (Page 11)
  • Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

• **Emergency Procedure** (Page 12)
  • This field is optional, but it's used to specify any emergency procedures.

• **Release Terms and Conditions** (Page 13)
  • Read and agree to the terms and conditions of your travel experience.

• **Medical Conditions** (Page 14)
  • List any medical conditions you have that may affect your travel.

• **Medication Allergies** (Page 15)
  • List any medications or allergies you have that may affect your travel.

• **Medical History** (Page 16)
  • Provide any relevant medical history, if applicable.

• **Insurance Company** (Page 17)
  • Enter the name of your insurance company, if applicable.

• **Insurance Policy Number** (Page 18)
  • Enter your insurance policy number, if applicable.

• **Medical Release** (Page 19)
  • Answer whether you are releasing your medical information for travel purposes.

• **Emergency Release** (Page 20)
  • Answer whether you are releasing your emergency contact information.

• **Travel Agency Acknowledgement** (Page 21)
  • Select whether you have received and acknowledged the travel agency's terms and conditions.

• **Participant Acknowledgement** (Page 22)
  • Select whether you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of your travel experience.

• **Travel Agency Signature** (Page 23)
  • Select whether you have obtained the travel agency's signature for your release.

• **Participant Signature** (Page 24)
  • Sign to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of your travel experience.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
