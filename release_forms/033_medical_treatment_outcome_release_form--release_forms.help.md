# medical_treatment_outcome_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical treatment outcome release form is a document used to share medical treatment information with authorized parties, such as patients or their representatives. This form confirms that the information has been released and provides a record of the authorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the authorized recipient in the "Recipient Name" field.
3. Record the date of authorization in the "Authorization Date" field.
4. Select the type of authorization in the "Authorization Type" field.
5. Enter the reason for authorization in the "Authorization Reason" field.
6. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
7. Sign the form to confirm authorization as the authorized party in the "Authorized Signature" field.
8. Sign the form to confirm receipt of the information as the patient in the "Patient Signature" field.
9. Enter the name of the authorized party in the "Authorized Party" field.
10. Record the type of authorized party in the "Authorized Party Type" field.
11. If desired, update the "Release Form Version" field to track changes to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name (`Patient Name`, **required**)
* Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.

### Recipient Name (`recipient_name`, **required**)
* Enter the name of the authorized party receiving the medical treatment information.

### Authorization Date (`Authorization Date`, **required**)
* Record the date when the authorization was made.

### Authorization Type (`Authorization Type`, **required**)
* Select the type of authorization, such as "Patient," "Guardian," or "Authorized Representative."

### Authorization Reason (`Authorization Reason`, **required**)
* Enter the reason for granting authorization.

### Patient ID (`Patient ID`, **required**)
* Enter the patient's ID number as recorded in medical records.

### Authorized Signature (`Authorized Signature`, **required**)
* Sign the form to confirm authorization as the authorized party.

### Patient Signature (`Patient Signature`, **required**)
* Sign the form to confirm receipt of the information as the patient.

### Authorized Party (`Authorized Party`, **required**)
* Enter the name of the authorized party.

### Authorized Party Type (`Authorized Party Type`, **required**)
* Record the type of authorized party.

### Release Form Version (`Release Form Version`, **optional**)
* Update the release form version to track changes to the form.

### Release Form Status (`Release Form Status`, **optional**)
* Select the status of the release form, such as "Active" or "Inactive."

### Release Form Note (`Release Form Note`, **optional**)
* Add any additional comments or notes about the release form.

Tips:

* Ensure that the authorized party is properly informed and consents to the release of information before completing this form.
* Store this form securely, as it contains sensitive medical information.
* Update the "Release Form Version" field to track changes to the form and ensure that all parties are aware of any updates.
