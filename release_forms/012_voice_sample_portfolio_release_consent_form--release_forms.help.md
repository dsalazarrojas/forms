# Voice Sample Portfolio Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "voice_sample_portfolio_release_consent_form" is used to release a voice sample from a portfolio. This form helps collect relevant information for the sample, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the first field.
2. Select the **Category** of the voice sample from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief **Description** of the sample (optional).
4. Select the **Assigned Tool** related to the sample.
5. Upload or enter the **Output File** associated with the sample.
6. Set the **Created At** and **Updated At** timestamps for the sample.
7. Enter the **Form ID** to associate the sample with a specific form.
8. Confirm the **Deleted At** timestamp if the sample has been deleted.
9. Finally, enter the **Created By** information to track who released the sample.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the voice sample.
* **Category** (select_one, required): Choose the category of the voice sample from the options: "Release Forms" or "Other".
* **Description** (note, optional): Provide a short description of the voice sample to give context.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Select the tool associated with the sample.
* **Output File** (text, required): Upload or enter the output file related to the sample.
* **Created At** (date, required): Set the timestamp when the sample was created.
* **Updated At** (date, required): Set the timestamp when the sample was last updated.
* **Form ID** (number, required): Enter the ID of the form associated with the sample.
* **Deleted At** (date, optional): Set the timestamp when the sample was deleted (if applicable).
* **Created By** (text, required): Enter the information of who released the sample.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to ensure the voice sample is properly released.
- Double-check the category and assigned tool selections to ensure they match the sample's characteristics.
- Be aware that the description field is optional, but it helps provide context to the sample.
