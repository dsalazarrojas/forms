# Disability Support Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disability Support Records Release Form is used to record and document an individual's consent to access and manage their disability-related records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date of birth to identify yourself as the individual requesting access to their records.
2. Indicate whether you have authorized a party (e.g., a healthcare provider, family member, or representative) to access your records. If so, select 'Yes' from the options provided.
3. Provide your phone number and email address to ensure that we can contact you for any further information or clarification.
4. Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate and that you consent to access and management of your records.
5. Select the reason for your request to access your records, if applicable.
6. Specify the date and time you would like your records to be released, if applicable.
7. Identify the party that will be accessing your records, if you have chosen to authorize someone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Authorized Party**: If you have authorized someone to access your records, select 'Yes' from the options provided. This indicates that you have given permission for someone to access your disability records on your behalf.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it is recorded in your official documents.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you for any further information or clarification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive updates or notifications regarding your records.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate and that you consent to access and management of your records.
* **Reason for Request**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a specific reason for requesting access to your records. If you have chosen 'Yes', provide a brief explanation in the space below.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date you would like your records to be released, if applicable.
* **Release Time**: Enter the time you would like your records to be released, if applicable.
* **Release Party**: If you have authorized someone to access your records, select 'Yes' from the options provided. This indicates that you have given permission for someone to access your records on your behalf.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are authorizing someone to access your records, make sure to provide their name and contact information.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
