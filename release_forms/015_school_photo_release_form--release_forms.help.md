# School Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photo Release Form is a document that allows parents or guardians to grant or deny permission for the school to use their child's photograph. This form is used to ensure that parents are aware of how their child's photograph will be used and to obtain their consent before it is used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
2. Enter the child's name.
3. Select whether you grant or deny permission for the school to use the child's photograph.
4. If you choose to grant permission, select how the school will use the photograph (e.g., for school events, school newsletter, or other purposes).
5. If you choose to deny permission, you may be able to specify an alternative reason for not granting permission (e.g., medical reason).
6. Sign the form to confirm your decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Photo Release Form** (`school_photo_release_form_title`, not required): This is the title of the form.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, not required): Please enter your last name.
* **Do you grant permission for the school to release the above child's photograph?** (`photo_release_consent`, not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ I Grant
	+ I Do Not Grant
* **How will you use the photograph?** (`school_use`, not required): Select all that apply:
	+ School Use
	+ School Newsletter
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)** (`other_use`, not required): If you selected "Other (please specify)", please enter a brief description of how you want the school to use the photograph.
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, not required): Please enter the child's first name.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, not required): Please enter the child's last name.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, not required): Sign your name to confirm your decision.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, not required): Sign the child's name to confirm their agreement to the terms of this form.
* **Other (please specify)** (`other_use`, not required): If you want to specify an alternative use for the photograph, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to carefully read and understand the terms of this form before granting permission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration.
* This form is a legal document and should be kept on file by the school.
