# Quality Assurance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Assurance Records Release Form is a crucial document used to record the release of Quality Assurance records. This form helps to ensure that the QA process is properly documented and communicated to all stakeholders. It is used to capture the necessary information regarding the release of records and provides a clear audit trail for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user input (name).
2. Enter the Organization Name where the records were generated or processed.
3. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the release.
4. Specify the Department responsible for the release.
5. Confirm if consent was given for the release.
6. Record the date of the release.
7. Provide your contact email address in case further information is needed.
8. Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
9. Add any additional comments about the release.
10. If applicable, select the assigned tool used for the release.
11. Select the category of the release (Release Forms, Other, or Other Forms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**: This field is for entering your name or ID.
* **Organization Name (2)**: Enter the name of the organization where the records were generated or processed.
* **Contact Name (3)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the release.
* **Department (4)**: Enter the Department responsible for the release.
* **Consent Given (5)**: Confirm if consent was given for the release. Select 'Yes' if consent was granted and 'No' otherwise.
* **Records Release Date (6)**: Enter the date the records were released.
* **Contact Email (7)**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact Phone (8)**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Comments (9)**: Add any additional comments about the release.
* **Assigned Tool (10)**: If an assigned tool was used for the release, select it from the list.
* **Category (11)**: Select the category of the release (Release Forms, Other, or Other Forms).

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to select the correct category for the release.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or team for guidance.
* This form is an official record of the release, so please ensure that the information entered is accurate and complete.
