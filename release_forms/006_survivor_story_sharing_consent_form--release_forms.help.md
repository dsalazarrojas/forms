# Survivor Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from survivors about their willingness to share their story with the organization, including the details of the story, the method of sharing, and the scope of sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). The optional fields can be left blank if you do not have the necessary information.
2. In the "social_media" field, select the social media platforms on which you would like to share your story.
3. In the "story_type" field, select the type of story you are willing to share (text, photo, or video).
4. In the "sharing_options" field, select the scope of sharing you are willing to allow (publicly, privately, or only with the organization).
5. In the "sharing_method" field, select the method(s) through which you would like to share your story (email, mailchimp, mailchimp groups, or social media).
6. Fill in the "name" field with your name.
7. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
8. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "phone_number" field.
9. If you have a file to upload, select the type of file in the "upload_file_type" field and upload the file in the "upload_file" field.
10. Enter the date of your birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
11. Enter the time of your story in the "time_of_story" field.
12. Enter the location of your story in the "location_of_story" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email_address** (email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **social_media** (select multiple, required): Select the social media platforms on which you would like to share your story.
* **story_type** (select one, required): Select the type of story you are willing to share (text, photo, or video).
* **story** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of your story.
* **sharing_options** (select multiple, required): Select the scope of sharing you are willing to allow (publicly, privately, or only with the organization).
* **sharing_method** (select multiple, required): Select the method(s) through which you would like to share your story (email, mailchimp, mailchimp groups, or social media).
* **signature** (text, optional): If you have a digital signature, enter it here.
* **upload_file** (text, optional): If you have a file to upload, enter the file name and path.
* **upload_file_type** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of file you are uploading (photo or video).
* **date_of_birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **time_of_story** (time, optional): Enter the time of your story.
* **location_of_story** (text, optional): Enter the location of your story.
