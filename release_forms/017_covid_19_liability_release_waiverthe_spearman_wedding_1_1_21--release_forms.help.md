# COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Spearman Wedding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the COVID-19 Liability Release Waiver form for the Spearman Wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "True" or "False" option for each question, based on your personal circumstances.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully, as this information will be used to manage the risk of COVID-19 transmission at the event.
3. Once you have completed the form, please sign and date it to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information (Section 1)
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to ensure you can be contacted with any updates or information related to the event.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number to enable easy communication in case of any emergencies.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to help the event organizers manage the risk of COVID-19 transmission.
* **Address**: Enter your residential address to help track any potential exposures.
* **City, State, ZIP**: Enter your city, state, and ZIP code to help the event organizers verify your location.

### Event Details (Section 2)
* **Event Name**: This is the name of the event, which is the Spearman Wedding. This field is pre-populated for your convenience.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event, which is pre-populated as January 1, 2021.

### Health Certification (Section 3)
* **I am free from COVID-19 symptoms**: Please select "True" if you are not experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms. Select "False" if you are experiencing any symptoms.
* **I have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days**: Select "True" if you have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Select "False" if you have been diagnosed.
* **I have not been exposed to anyone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days**: Select "True" if you have not been exposed to anyone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Select "False" if you have been exposed.

### Liability Release (Section 4)
* **I understand and assume the risk of COVID-19 transmission at this event**: Select "I agree" if you understand and assume the risk of COVID-19 transmission at this event.
* **I release the organizers from any liability related to COVID-19**: Select "I agree" to release the organizers from any liability related to COVID-19.
* **I agree to follow all health and safety guidelines**: Select "I agree" to follow all health and safety guidelines.

### Confirmation
* **I have read and understand this waiver**: Select "True" to confirm you have read and understand the terms of the waiver. Select "False" if you have not read or do not understand the waiver.

Please note that this guide is subject to change based on the event organizers' policies and procedures. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers directly.
