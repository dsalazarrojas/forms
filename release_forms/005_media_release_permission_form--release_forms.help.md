# Media Release Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Release Permission Form is used to obtain consent from individuals to use their media, such as images or videos, for a particular purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `Consent to Use`: Select the type of media that you are consenting to use (Images, Videos, or Both).
	* `Consent to Share`: Check the box to indicate if you consent to share your media (Yes or No).
	* `Consent to Annotate`: Check the box to indicate if you consent to have your media annotated (Yes or No).
2. Fill in the optional fields as needed:
	* `Contact Name`: Provide the name of the individual or organization providing the media.
	* `Contact Email`: Provide the email of the individual or organization providing the media.
	* `Contact Phone`: Provide the phone number of the individual or organization providing the media.
	* `Contact Address`: Provide the address of the individual or organization providing the media.
	* `Release Date`: Provide the date when the media can be released.
	* `Release Expiration Date`: Provide the date when the media can no longer be used.
	* `Media Type`: Select the type of media that you are consenting to use (Images or Videos).
	* `Usage Rights`: Describe the usage rights of the media.
	* `Usage Description`: Describe how the media will be used.
	* `Release By`: Describe who will release the media.
	* `Consent Version`: Provide the version of the consent form.
	* `Media Release ID`: Provide the ID of the media release.
	* `Contact Details`: Provide any additional contact details.
	* `Media Owner`: Provide the owner of the media.
	* `Contact Person`: Provide the person responsible for the media.
	* `Usage Purpose`: Describe the purpose of using the media.
	* `Consent Note`: Provide any additional consent notes.
	* `Usage Terms`: Describe the terms of use for the media.
	* `Signature Date`: Provide the date of the signature.
	* `Signature`: Provide a signature.
3. Review and submit the form.
