# Audience Camera Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audience Camera Appearance Consent Form is designed to capture participant information and obtain their consent for an audience camera appearance. This form ensures that participants understand how their likeness will be used and for what purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** to verify your age.
3. Select whether you are 18 years of age or older, or if you are a minor and need parental consent.
4. If you are a minor, fill in your **Parent or Guardian Name** and **Contact Email**.
5. Provide the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, and **Event Location**.
6. Select the types of **Recording Type** that will take place.
7. Select the **Usage Rights Granted** for your likeness, including promotional materials, social media posts, website content, broadcast television, streaming platforms, and print publications.
8. Choose the **Usage Duration** for your likeness, such as 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, or perpetual.
9. Indicate whether you received any **Compensation** for your appearance, such as a monetary payment or non-monetary compensation.
10. Grant **Likeness Release** by selecting whether you grant full permission, restricted permission, or decline to grant permission.
11. If you have **Usage Restrictions**, specify them in the text field.
12. Release **Liability** by selecting whether you release all claims or not.
13. Sign the form with your **Participant Signature** and provide the **Signature Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Provide your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age.
* **Age Verification**: Select whether you are 18 years of age or older, or a minor who requires parental consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event being recorded.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Recording Type**: Select the types of recording that will take place, such as photography, video, or audio.
* **Usage Rights Granted**: Select the rights granted for your likeness, including promotional materials, social media posts, website content, broadcast television, streaming platforms, or print publications.
* **Usage Duration**: Choose the duration of the granted rights for your likeness.
* **Compensation Received**: Indicate whether you received any compensation for your appearance.
* **Likeness Release**: Grant permission for the use of your likeness, with or without restrictions.
* **Usage Restrictions**: If you have any usage restrictions, specify them in this field.
* **Liability Release**: Release the organization from claims related to the recording.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign the form with your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Provide the date of your consent.
* **Parental Consent**: If you are a minor, your parent or guardian must sign below as well.
