# Hospital Patient Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Release Form is a document used to release a patient from a hospital or medical facility. This form is completed by the patient or their representative and is used to provide information about the patient's stay, including their name, date of birth, hospital name, and medical record number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
- Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
- Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was treated in the "Hospital Name" field.
- If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
- Record the date of the release in the "Signature Date" field.
- Enter the name of the physician or medical professional who is releasing the patient in the "Physician Name" field.
- Sign the form to confirm that the patient has been properly discharged in the "Patient Signature" field.
- Sign the form to confirm that the release is being made under hospital or medical facility protocols in the "Physician Signature" field.
- If a witness is present, sign the form to confirm that you are aware of the release in the "Witness Signature" field.
- Enter the name of the witness in the "Witness Name" field.
- Enter your relationship to the patient in the "Relationship To Patient" field.
- Enter your title or position in the "Witness Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Hospital Name**: Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was treated.
- **Medical Record Number**: If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number.
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the release, in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician or medical professional who is releasing the patient.
- **Patient Signature**: Sign to confirm that the patient has been properly discharged.
- **Physician Signature**: Sign to confirm that the release is being made under hospital or medical facility protocols.
- **Witness Signature**: If a witness is present, sign to confirm that you are aware of the release.
- **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness, if present.
- **Relationship To Patient**: Enter the relationship between the witness and the patient.
- **Witness Title**: Enter your title or position, if present.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Verify that the form is signed by the patient and all witnesses before releasing the patient.
- Keep a copy of the form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
- Ensure that the form is completed in a clear and legible handwriting or print.
- If any fields are left blank or not applicable, clearly mark them as such to avoid confusion.
