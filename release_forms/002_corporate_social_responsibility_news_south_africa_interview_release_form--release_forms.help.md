# corporate_social_responsibility_news_south_africa_interview_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal details and interview-related information from individuals for corporate social responsibility news purposes in South Africa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details such as name, email, and phone number on page 1.
2. Read and agree to the interview agreement on page 2.
3. Share details about the interview on page 3.
4. Enter your contact details on page 4.
5. Confirm your photo release on page 5.
6. Give your consent on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number for our records.
* **Interview Agreement** (`interview_agreement`, text, required/optional): Agree to the terms of the interview by typing "yes" or "I agree".
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant details about the interview, such as the location, date, or time.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, text, required/optional): Confirm if you are okay with sharing your photo with us.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Give your consent to participate in the corporate social responsibility news project in South Africa.
