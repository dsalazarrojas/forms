# Patient Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Photo Release Form is a document that is used to obtain consent from patients for the storage and release of their photos. This form is required by healthcare providers to ensure that patient's photos are handled and stored in accordance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure that you understand what is required.
2. Answer all required fields.
3. Enter your information in the relevant fields.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Consent**: This field is used to obtain the patient's consent for the storage and release of their photo.
* **Patient Photo**: This field is for the healthcare provider to note any information about the photo.
* **Healthcare Provider**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter their name and contact information.
* **Date Created**: This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
* **Date Updated**: This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
* **Healthcare Provider Info**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter any additional information about the photo release process.
* **Patient Name**: This field is for the patient to enter their name.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter their name.
* **Date of Photo**: This field is for the patient to enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Patient Age**: This field is for the patient to enter their age.
* **Photo Release**: This field allows the patient to confirm whether they have given consent for the photo release.
* **Signature**: This field is for the healthcare provider and patient to sign and date.
* **Date Signed**: This field is for the healthcare provider and patient to enter the date they signed the form.
* **Patient Signature**: This field is for the patient to sign and date.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: This field is for the healthcare provider to sign and date.
* **Date Provider Signed**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter the date they signed the form.
* **Patient Photo Release Version**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter the version of the photo release form.
* **Healthcare Provider Version**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter the version of the photo release form.
* **Patient Release Version**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter the version of the photo release form.
* **Healthcare Provider Relationship**: This field is for the healthcare provider to enter their relationship with the patient.
* **Patient Relationship**: This field is for the patient to enter their relationship with the healthcare provider.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional notes or information about the photo release process.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the content.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
