# Sports COVID 19 And Release Of Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a COVID-19 and release of liability waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your information in each field, making sure to fill in all required fields.
2. Review each field for accuracy before proceeding.
3. Once you have completed all fields, review the form for any errors or discrepancies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`): Enter a brief description of the patient's liability release.
* **Patient Informed** (`patient_informed`): Enter a brief description of the patient's informed consent.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1: I consent to the administration of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ Option 2: I do not consent to the administration of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ Option 3: I need further information before giving consent.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare organization.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`): Enter the address of the healthcare organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`): Enter the date of birth of the patient.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`vaccine_administered`): Enter a brief description of the vaccine administered.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`): Enter a brief description of the vaccine dose.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Enter a brief description of the medical condition.
* **Medical Condition Note** (`medical_condition_note`): Enter any additional information about the medical condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History Note** (`medical_history_note`): Enter any additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`): Enter any allergy information related to the patient.
* **Allergy Information Note** (`allergy_information_note`): Enter any additional information about the allergy.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Enter the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`): Enter the time of the consent.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`): Sign the consent with your signature.

Note: This form is not intended for general public consumption and is only for authorized healthcare professionals and their representatives.
