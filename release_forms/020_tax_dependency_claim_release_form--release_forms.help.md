# Tax Dependency Claim Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a tax dependency claim. Complete this form if you are a claimant wishing to terminate or modify a tax dependency relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields (if any).
2. Ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
3. Sign the claimant's signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Release Form (Page 1)** (<label: claim_release_form, <type: text, required: false>): This form serves as the primary input area for your tax dependency claim details. Please type your information here.
* **Co-Parent Name (Page 1)** (<label: co_parent_name, <type: text, required: false>): Enter the name of the co-parent whose tax dependency claim you are releasing.
* **Co-Parent Address (Page 1)** (<label: co_parents_address, <type: text, required: false>): Provide the address of the co-parent.
* **Co-Parent Phone (Page 1)** (<label: co_parents_phone, <type: text, required: false>): Enter the co-parent's phone number.
* **Tax Year (Page 1)** (<label: tax_year, <type: number, required: false>): Specify the tax year for which you are releasing the tax dependency claim.
* **Claimant Signature (Page 1)** (<label: claimant_signature, <type: text, required: false>): Please sign and date this section to confirm your identity and agreement to release the tax dependency claim.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or authorizations to release the tax dependency claim.
* If you are unsure about any field or process, consult with a tax professional or relevant authority.
