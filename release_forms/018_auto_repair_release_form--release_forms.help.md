# Auto Repair Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Auto Repair Release Form" is a crucial document that ensures customer understanding and agreement on the vehicle's condition, repairs, and warranty after the job is completed. Please take a few minutes to carefully review and complete this form to ensure a smooth handover of your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about your vehicle and the repair work completed.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, as they will be used to document the vehicle's condition and warranty.
3. If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, don't hesitate to ask your service advisor for clarification.
4. Sign the form with your signature to acknowledge that you've reviewed and agree to the terms outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name as it appears on your driver's license or identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name as it appears on your driver's license or identification.
* **Street Address**: Provide the exact address where you reside.
* **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State**: Select the state where you reside from the list provided.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the 5-digit ZIP code of your residence.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of manufacture for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model name of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color**: Select the exterior color of your vehicle from the list provided.
* **VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) for your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number**: Enter the current license plate number for your vehicle.
* **Mileage In**: Enter the odometer reading at the time of drop-off.
* **Description of Repairs**: Describe the work performed on your vehicle.
* **Date of Repair**: Enter the date when the repair work was completed.
* **Total Repair Cost**: Enter the total amount paid for the repair work.
* **Parts Used**: List the parts replaced during the repair work.
* **Parts Warranty**: Select the warranty period for the parts used.
* **Labor Warranty**: Select the warranty period for the labor performed.
* **Release Terms**: Carefully read and understand the terms outlined in this section before signing.
* **Work Completed Satisfactorily**: Select whether you're satisfied with the work completed.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the condition of your vehicle at the time of pickup.
* **Personal Items Removed**: Select if all personal items were removed from the vehicle.
* **All Keys Received**: Select if you received all keys to your vehicle.
* **Outstanding Issues**: If there are any issues not covered by the warranty, list them here.
* **Release Agreement**: Select whether you agree to the terms outlined.
* **Warranty Understanding**: Select whether you understand the warranty terms.
* **Future Issues Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that you understand that any issues not related to the repair are not covered by the warranty.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge your agreement to the terms.
* **Date of Signature**: Date and sign the form with the current date.
* **Witness Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the shop representative signing on your behalf.
* **Witness Signature**: If applicable, sign the form with your shop representative's signature.

Remember to sign the form carefully and ensure that you understand each section before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your service advisor.
