# Laser COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and signatures from clients and providers for liability release waivers related to COVID-19. It is essential to understand that this form is not a legal document and is intended for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the client has provided information and signed the waiver.
2. Enter the client's signature as required.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the provider has provided information and signed the waiver.
4. Enter the provider's signature as required.
5. Enter the date and time of the client's signature.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the client has released liability for the services provided.
7. Enter the date and time of the release.
8. Enter any additional notes for the client and provider.
9. Enter the client's and provider's email and phone numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`select_multiple`, **Yes/No**): This field allows you to confirm whether the client has provided information and signed the waiver.
* **Client Signature** (`text`, **Client Signature**): Enter the client's signature as required.
* **Provider Information** (`select_one`, **Yes/No**): This field allows you to confirm whether the provider has provided information and signed the waiver.
* **Provider Signature** (`number`, **Provider Signature**): Enter the provider's signature as required. Please note that this field is not a typical signature field and should be filled with a number that represents the provider's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, **Date**): Enter the date when the client signed the waiver.
* **Time** (`time`, **Time**): Enter the time when the client signed the waiver.
* **Waiver** (`text`, **Waiver**): Enter any additional notes for the client.
* **Terms** (`text`, **Terms**): Enter any additional terms for the client.
* **Signature Date** (`date`, **Signature Date**): Enter the date when the client signed the form.
* **Signature Time** (`time`, **Signature Time**): Enter the time when the client signed the form.
* **Waiver Date** (`date`, **Waiver Date**): Enter the date when the waiver was issued.
* **Waiver Time** (`time`, **Waiver Time**): Enter the time when the waiver was issued.
* **Release** (`select_one`, **Yes/No**): This field allows you to confirm whether the client has released liability for the services provided.
* **Release Date** (`date`, **Release Date**): Enter the date when the release was issued.
* **Release Time** (`time`, **Release Time**): Enter the time when the release was issued.
* **Signature** (`select_multiple`, **Yes/No**): This field allows you to confirm whether the provider has signed the form.
* **Signature Date 2** (`date`, **Signature Date 2**): Enter the date when the provider signed the form.
* **Signature Time 2** (`time`, **Signature Time 2**): Enter the time when the provider signed the form.
* **Notes** (`text`, **Notes**): Enter any additional notes for the client and provider.
* **Client Notes** (`text`, **Client Notes**): Enter any additional notes for the client.
* **Provider Notes** (`text`, **Provider Notes**): Enter any additional notes for the provider.
* **Email** (`email`, **E-mail**): Enter the client's email.
* **Phone** (`text`, **Phone**): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client E-mail** (`email`, **Client E-mail**): Enter the client's email.
* **Provider Phone** (`text`, **Provider Phone**): Enter the provider's phone number.
