# Audio Recording Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between a talent and a producer for an audio recording project. It outlines the rights and responsibilities of both parties, including the transfer of intellectual property rights, distribution rights, reproduction rights, and other related permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the agreement.
2. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your date of birth and confirm that you are 18 years of age or older.
4. Select the project title, type, and location from the provided options.
5. Specify the date of the recording session.
6. Indicate the rights and territories for which you are transferring rights.
7. Choose the duration of the agreement.
8. Select the compensation received for your work.
9. Enter the amount of compensation received, if applicable.
10. Sign and date the form, indicating that you release all claims related to the recording and waive moral rights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Talent Full Name** (`talent_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`talent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`talent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (`talent_address`, text, required): Enter your full address for correspondence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to confirm your age.
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are 18 years of age or older.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian, if applicable.
* **Producer or Company Name** (`producer_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the producer or production company.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the audio project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project this is (e.g., film, television, radio, etc.).
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recording session.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the recording was made.
* **Rights Transferred** (`rights_transferred`, select_multiple, required): Specify the rights being transferred (e.g., intellectual property, distribution, reproduction, etc.).
* **Territory** (`territory`, select_one, required): Select the geographic scope of the rights transfer (e.g., worldwide, specific region).
* **Duration** (`duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of time for which rights are transferred (e.g., in perpetuity, specific years).
* **Compensation Received** (`compensation_received`, select_one, required): Select the type of compensation received (e.g., monetary payment, royalty share, etc.).
* **Compensation Amount** (`compensation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of compensation received, if applicable.
* **Moral Rights Waiver** (`moral_rights_waiver`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you waive moral rights to the recording.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you release claims related to the recording.
* **Exclusivity Agreement** (`exclusivity_agreement`, select_one, required): Indicate whether this release is exclusive.
* **Talent Signature** (`talent_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the release.
