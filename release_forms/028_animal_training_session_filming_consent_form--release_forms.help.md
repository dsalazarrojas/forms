# Animal Training Session Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to obtain consent from clients for filming a training session with their animal. It captures the client's information, the animal's name, the training session details, and the usage permissions for the footage. The form ensures that both the client and animal are protected, and that the trainer and filming team have necessary permissions to record and use the footage as required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

* Enter your **Client Full Name** in the `Client Full Name` field.
* Enter the **Animal Name** in the `Animal Name` field.
* Enter the **Lead Trainer Name** in the `Lead Trainer Name` field.
* Select the **Date of Training Session** in the `Date of Training Session` field.
* Choose whether you **Grant Permission to Record Session** from the `Permission to Record Session` field.
* Indicate whether you **Allow Footage for Educational Purposes** from the `Use of Footage for Educational Purposes` field.
* Choose whether you **Allow Footage for Promotional Purposes** from the `Use of Footage for Promotional Purposes` field.
* Select whether you **Waive Liability for Filming Activities** from the `Liability Waiver for Filming Activities` field.
* If you have any specific **Restrictions on Footage Use**, enter them in the `Specific Restrictions on Footage Use` field (optional).
* Enter your **Digital Signature** in the `Digital Signature` field.
* Enter the **Date Signed** in the `Date Signed` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name (Client Full Name, text, required):** Please enter your name as the client.
* **Animal Name (Animal Name, text, required):** Please enter the name of the animal being trained.
* **Lead Trainer Name (Trainer Name, text, required):** Please enter the name of the lead trainer.
* **Date of Training Session (Session Date/Time, date, required):** Please select the date and time of the training session.
* **Permission to Record Session (Select One, required):** Choose whether you grant permission to film or record this training session.
* **Use of Footage for Educational Purposes (Select One, required):** Choose whether you allow the footage to be shown to other trainers or used in classes.
* **Use of Footage for Promotional Purposes (Select One, required):** Choose whether you allow footage to be used on social media or your website for promotional purposes.
* **Liability Waiver for Filming Activities (Select One, required):** Select whether you agree to waive liability for any issues related to filming.
* **Specific Restrictions on Footage Use (text, optional):** If you have any specific restrictions on how the footage can be used, please enter them here.
* **Digital Signature (Digital Signature, text, required):** Please enter your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date Signed (Date Signed, date, required):** Please enter the date you signed this form.
