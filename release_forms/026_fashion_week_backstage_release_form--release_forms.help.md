# Fashion Week Backstage Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fashion Week Backstage Release Form is a document that ensures participants, models, guests, staff, and crew have the necessary permissions and agreements in place to access the backstage areas during Fashion Week events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your role**: Choose your role from the options: Participant, Guest, Model, Crew, or Staff.
2. **Choose your backstage access**: Select the time slots you need access to the backstage area.
3. **Enter your expected arrival time**: Provide your expected arrival time for the backstage area.
4. **Provide participant details**: Enter your participant information.
5. **Enter participant contact information**: Enter your participant's email and phone number.
6. **Enter participant name**: Enter your participant's name.
7. **Add participant notes (optional)**: If you have any additional information about your participant, please add it here.
8. **Participant signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
9. **Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
10. **Agreement date**: Enter the date you sign the agreement.
11. **Agreement time**: Optional field for tracking the time of agreement (not required).
12. **Agreement status**: Select one of the options to indicate your agreement status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant**: Choose your role from the options.
    *   Participant: This is for participants who require access to the backstage area.
    *   Guest: This is for guests who need to enter the backstage area.
    *   Model: This is for models who require access to the backstage area.
    *   Crew: This is for crew members who need to access the backstage area.
    *   Staff: This is for staff members who need to access the backstage area.
* **Backstage Access**: Select the time slots you need access to the backstage area. 
    *   9am - 12pm: This is the first time slot.
    *   12pm - 3pm: This is the second time slot.
    *   3pm - 6pm: This is the third time slot.
    *   6pm - 9pm: This is the fourth time slot.
* **Expected Arrival Time**: Provide your expected arrival time for the backstage area.
* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant information, such as participant name and other relevant details.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your participant's email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your participant's phone number.
*   **Participant Name**: Enter your participant's name.
*   **Participant Notes (optional)**: If you have any additional information about your participant, please add it here.
*   **Participant Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
*   **Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
*   **Agreement Date**: Enter the date you sign the agreement.
*   **Agreement Time (optional)**: Optional field for tracking the time of agreement (not required).
*   **Agreement Status**: Select one of the options to indicate your agreement status.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any information, please reach out to the Fashion Week team for guidance.
* Don't submit incomplete or duplicate forms.
* Review the agreements carefully before signing.
