# Model Waiver & Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Waiver & Release Form is a document that helps you, as a model, to agree or disagree to the use of your images in photographs. This form is designed to be used by photographers, event organizers, or any entity that requires models to grant permissions for their image use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other required information in the first two fields, "Model Waiver" and "Release Form". These fields are not required, but providing them will help the organization understand your preferences.
2. Choose your consent option from the "Consent" dropdown menu. If you agree to the use of your images in photographs, select "I consent to the use of my images in photographs." If you don't agree, choose "I do not consent to the use of my images in photographs."
3. If you have a specific photographer or organization that you wish to work with, type their name in the "Photographer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Waiver (1)**: This field allows you to provide your name and any other relevant details for your participation in the photo shoot or event.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Release Form (2)**: This field is where you can provide any additional information related to your participation in the photo shoot or event. If you don't want to provide any additional details, you can leave this field blank.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Consent (3)**: This dropdown menu allows you to choose whether you consent to the use of your images in photographs.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Options: `I consent to the use of my images in photographs.` and `I do not consent to the use of my images in photographs.`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Photographer (4)**: If you have a specific photographer or organization that you wish to work with, type their name in this field.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your name and any other relevant details in the "Model Waiver" field to ensure that the organization has your correct contact information.
- Choose your consent option carefully, as it will determine how your images are used.
