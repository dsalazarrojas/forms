**Student Success Story Release Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Student Success Story Release Form is a critical document that ensures all necessary permissions and agreements are in place before sharing a student's story.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Succeeds**: This field is where you will enter a brief description of the student's success story.
* **Publication Permissions**: Select all the permissions that apply to the story, indicating who can publish and share the story.
* **School/Program**: Enter the name of the school and program involved in the story.
* **Date**: Enter the date the story was completed or published.
* **Time**: Enter the time the story was completed or published.
* **Publication Consent**: This field is where you will enter a statement indicating that you have obtained or will obtain the necessary permissions for publication.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number (optional).
* **Story**: Enter a brief description of the student's success story.
* **Publication Agreement**: Enter a statement indicating that you have obtained or will obtain the necessary agreements for publication.
* **School/Program Agreement**: Enter a statement indicating that you have obtained or will obtain the necessary agreements for the school or program involved.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **School/Program Instructor**: Enter the name of the school or program instructor involved in the story.
* **Student School/Program**: Enter the name of the school or program the student is attending.
* **Publication Agreement Date**: Enter the date the publication agreement was signed.
* **Story Agreement**: Enter a statement indicating that you have obtained or will obtain the necessary agreements for the story.
* **Story Agreement Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who signed the story agreement.
* **Publication Agreement Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who signed the publication agreement.
* **Publication Agreement Student**: Enter a statement indicating that you have obtained or will obtain the necessary agreements from the student.
* **Student Agreement Date**: Enter the date the student agreement was signed.
* **Student Agreement Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who signed the student agreement.
* **School/Program Agreement Date**: Enter the date the school or program agreement was signed.
* **School/Program Agreement Instructor**: Enter the name of the instructor who signed the school or program agreement.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
