# social_media_photo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media photo release form is used to release photos on social media platforms, ensuring that the necessary information is provided to accompany the photos. This form is intended for individuals who have photos they want to publish on social media and need to provide relevant details about the photos and their subjects.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each field according to your knowledge and the specific situation.
2. Provide the necessary information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photo Release Form** (`photo_1`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering a description or any relevant comments about the photos.
- **Who would you like to publish photos of?** (`person_1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who is the subject of the photos.
- **Do you have pictures of another person?** (`person_2`, select_one, optional): Check 'True' if you have photos of another person, and 'False' otherwise.
- **What platforms do you want to publish your photos on?** (`publish_on`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the social media platforms you want to publish your photos on (e.g. Twitter, Facebook, Instagram).
- **Have you signed a photo release?** (`signed_photo_release`, text, optional): Provide any signed photo release that pertains to the photos.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the photo release was signed.
- **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization that signed the photo release.
- **Contact Information** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the subject of the photos.
- **Signed Date** (`signed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the photo release was signed.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or details about the photo release.
- **Signed** (`signed`, text, optional): Provide a confirmation that the photo release is signed.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Other Contact Information** (`other_contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
- **Other Information** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any other information that may be relevant to the photo release.
