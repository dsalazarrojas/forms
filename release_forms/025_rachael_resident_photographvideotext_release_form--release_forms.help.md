# rachael_resident_photographvideotext_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rachael Resident Photograph/Video/Text Release Form is used by Rachael to release official photographs, videos, and text content. This form is used to document and confirm that Rachael has given permission for the release of certain content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that the form is signed and dated before submitting.
3. Submit the completed form to the intended recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a descriptive name for the form, such as a project title or a description of the content being released.
	+ This field is used to provide a reference for the form and its purpose.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes next to 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the photographer has been given permission to release the content.
	+ This field confirms whether the photographer has been given permission to release the content.
* **Photo/Video/Text Credit** (`photo_video_text_credit`, `text`, required: false): Enter a credit to be displayed with the released content.
	+ This field is used to specify the credit to be displayed with the released content, such as the photographer's name or a copyright notice.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address to contact the recipient.
	+ This field is used to contact the recipient with any questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number to contact the recipient.
	+ This field is used to contact the recipient with any questions or concerns.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate the type of release being requested.
	+ This field is used to determine the type of release being requested.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the release.
	+ This field is used to document the date of the release.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the release.
	+ This field is used to document the time of the release.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information regarding the release.
	+ This field is used to provide any additional information about the release.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required: false): This field is intended for the client to sign and date the form, confirming that they have received and understood the contents of the form.
	+ This field is used to confirm that the client has received and understood the contents of the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand the form before completing it.
- Complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- Submit the form to the intended recipient only.
