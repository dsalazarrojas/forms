# summer_camp_parental_release_medical_information_code_of_conduct - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information and consent from parents or guardians of children attending the summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general contact information section with your child's name, your name, and your contact information.
2. Provide emergency contact information for yourself and any other person who may need to contact in case of an emergency.
3. Answer the questions about your child's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions.
4. List any medications your child is taking.
5. Read and agree to the code of conduct and terms and conditions.
6. Add any additional medical history notes if necessary.
7. Confirm your emergency contact information and agree to the second set of terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Fill in your child's name, your name, and your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any contact information for yourself or another person who may need to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Parent Contact Information**: Fill in your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Student Information**: This section is for general information about your child. You can include your child's age, grade level, or any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contacts**: List any emergency contacts, including their name, phone number, and relationship to your child.
* **Medical History**: Provide any medical history information about your child, including allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Allergy and Medication**: List any medications your child is currently taking, including the name and dosage.
* **Consent**: Read and agree to the code of conduct, which outlines the camp's rules and expectations.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions, which outline the camp's policies and procedures.
* **Medical History Notes**: Add any additional medical history notes, such as medical procedures or equipment your child may need.
* **Emergency Contacts 2**: List any additional emergency contacts, including their name, phone number, and relationship to your child.
* **Consent 2**: Read and agree to the second set of terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your emergency contact information for accuracy.
* If your child has any medical conditions or allergies, be sure to list them.
* Read and understand the code of conduct and terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the summer camp staff.
