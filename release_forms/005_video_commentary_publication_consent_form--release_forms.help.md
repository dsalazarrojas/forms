# Video Commentary Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Video Commentary Publication Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please select all that apply**:
	* Fill in your user information, including name, email, and phone number.
	* Provide a title and description of the video.
	* Choose the desired date and time of publication.
	* Select all applicable publication methods (Print, Digital, Radio, Television, or Internet).
2. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, or driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (this will be used for further communication).
* **Video Title**: Enter the title of the video you are commenting on.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the video and your commentary.
* **Publication Date**: Choose the date you would like the video to be published.
* **Time of Publication**: Choose the time you would like the video to be published.
* **Consent**: Select all the ways you are willing to have your video commentary published (Print, Digital, Radio, Television, or Internet).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled out all required fields (marked with ***) before submitting the form.
* You can request that your video not be published if you are not comfortable with any of the selected publication methods.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
