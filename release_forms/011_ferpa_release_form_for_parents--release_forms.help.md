# FERPA Release Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for the release of student information, such as grades and disciplinary records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully.
3.  If you need help or clarification about any field, please ask a school representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **FERPA Release Form** (`release_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is for identification purposes only.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID.
- **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the child for whom you are providing consent.
- **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose how you are related to the child.
  •   Child
  •   Parent
  •   Guardian
  •   Other
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the child.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter your contact number where you can be reached.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): This is the date you are providing consent for.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form by typing your name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, text, optional): If you have a fax number, please enter it. If not, leave it blank.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
- **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the school your child attends.
- **District Name** (`district_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school district.
- **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, optional): If your child is in a specific grade level, please enter it. If not, leave it blank.
- **Teacher’s Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): If your child has a teacher, please enter their name. If not, leave it blank.
- **School** (`teacher_school`, text, optional): If your child attends this school, please enter the name. If not, leave it blank.
- **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are providing consent.
  •   True
  •   False
- **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_multiple, optional): If you are providing consent, choose how much access you want to give.
  •   Full
  •   Limited
  •   False
- **Date Start** (`consent_date_start`, date, optional): If you are providing limited consent, enter the start date.
- **Date End** (`consent_date_end`, date, optional): If you are providing limited consent, enter the end date.
- **Tips** 
This form is for parents and guardians to obtain consent for the release of their child's information. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately. If you need help or clarification, please ask a school representative.

Note: The form fields are optional, but please fill them in as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
