# Interview Quote Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release information about an interview to a specific individual or organization. It captures essential details about the participant, publication status, and consent given.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Main Page**: Enter the main details of the interview, including any relevant notes.
2. Complete the **Participant Name** field with the name of the person being interviewed.
3. Enter the **Participant Email** field with the email address of the participant.
4. Check the **Publication Status** field to indicate whether the interview will be published in any medium.
5. Select the **Consent Given** field to indicate whether consent was given by the participant.
6. Enter the date of **Consent Given** (if applicable).
7. Add any **Notes** about the interview.
8. Enter the **Submission Date** of the form.
9. Enter the **Form Owner**'s details.
10. Enter the **Form Owner Email**.
11. Enter the **Assigned Tool** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **Assigned Tool Email** (if applicable).
13. Select the **Submitted By** field to indicate who submitted the form.
14. Select the **Submitted To** field to indicate who submitted the form to.
15. Add any **Comments** about the submission.
16. Enter the date of **Submission**.
17. Enter the **Form ID**.
18. Enter the **Form Version**.
19. Enter the **Form Category** (if applicable).
20. Enter the **Form Description** (if applicable).
21. Enter the **Form Creator** (if applicable).
22. Enter the **Form Uploaded By** (if applicable).
23. Enter the date the **Form was updated** (if applicable).
