# Transcript Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transcript Release Authorization Form is used to obtain authorization from parents or guardians to release student records, likely for transcript purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the student(s) for whom you are submitting this form.
2. Fill in all required fields, including student name, grade level, date of birth, and other necessary details.
3. For each student section, repeat the process, ensuring you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, required): Enter the name of the student for whom you are submitting this form.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade`, required): Select the current grade level of the student.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, required): Indicate whether the student is currently in school or not.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number as the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, required): Enter your email address as the parent or guardian.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, required): Enter the school address of the student.
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, required): Indicate whether you are authorized to release the student's records.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, required): Enter the date you are signing this form.
* **Signature of Authorized** (`signature_of_authorized`, required): Sign this form to confirm your authorization.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Grade Level 2** (`student_grade_level_2`, required): Select the current grade level of the second student, if applicable.
* **School Year 2** (`school_year_2`, required): Indicate whether the second student is currently in school or not.
* **Student Name 2** (`student_name_2`, required): Enter the name of the second student.
* **Date of Birth 2** (`date_of_birth_2`, required): Enter the date of birth of the second student.
* **Guardian Phone 2** (`guardian_phone_2`, required): Enter your contact phone number as the parent or guardian of the second student.
* **Guardian Email 2** (`guardian_email_2`, required): Enter your email address as the parent or guardian of the second student.
* **School Address 2** (`school_address_2`, required): Enter the school address of the second student.
* **Authorized By 2** (`authorized_by_2`, required): Indicate whether you are authorized to release the second student's records.
* **Date of Signature 2** (`date_of_signature_2`, required): Enter the date you are signing for the second student.
* **Signature of Authorized 2** (`signature_of_authorized_2`, required): Sign this form to confirm your authorization for the second student.
* **Student ID 2** (`student_id_2`, required): Enter the student ID number of the second student.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to the student records.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* If you need to submit multiple students, repeat the process for each student.
* Verify all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority.
