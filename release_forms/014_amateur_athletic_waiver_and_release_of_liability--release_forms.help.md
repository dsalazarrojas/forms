# Amateur Athletic Waiver And Release Of Liability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and medical information from participants in a sports or athletic activity, and to obtain their signature of waiver and release of liability. This form aims to protect the participant, organization, coaches, staff, and facility owners from potential legal claims related to injuries or accidents that may occur during the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and review each section carefully before proceeding.
2. Enter your name and date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Fill in your contact information, including your street address, city, state/province, and ZIP/postal code.
4. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
5. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, select the relevant options and describe them in the text box.
6. If you take any regular medications, select "Yes" and list them in the text box.
7. Indicate whether you have any allergies and describe them in the text box.
8. Select your health insurance provider and policy number (if applicable).
9. Acknowledge your understanding of the risks involved in the activity.
10. Release the organization, coaches, staff, and facility owners from liability.
11. If you are a minor, obtain a parent/guardian's consent and signature.
12. Sign the form and date it.

## Tips

* Please read the form carefully before signing.
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Sign and date the form once completed.
* Return the form to the organization before participating in the activity.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, ask for clarification.

This document is intended to serve as a guide to assist you in completing the form. Please note that some fields may not be applicable in all cases, and some sections may not be required for your specific situation.
