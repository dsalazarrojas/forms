# telehealth-release-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from patients about the release of their medical records, including their medical information, consent date, and relationship with the healthcare provider.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information in the **patient-info** field.
2. Select the **consent-date** field and enter the date when you granted consent for the release of your medical records.
3. Enter the name and details of the **healthcare-provider** who will be accessing your medical records.
4. Choose the **release-date** when you want your records to be released.
5. Explain the **reason-for-release** in a brief paragraph.
6. Sign the **signed-date** field with the date when you signed the consent form.
7. Select the **signed-by** field and choose who signed the form (Doctor, Nurse, or Other).
8. Sign the form with your **signature** in the designated field.
9. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be releasing your records in the **healthcare-provider-signature** field.
10. Check the box to confirm that the form has been saved (**release-form-saved**).
11. Select your **patient-relationship** with the healthcare provider from the dropdown menu (Parent/Primary Caregiver, Partner, Spouse, Child, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient-info**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and contact information to identify yourself.
* **consent-date**: Enter the date when you granted consent for the release of your medical records.
* **healthcare-provider**: Enter the name and details of the healthcare provider who will be accessing your medical records.
* **release-date**: Choose the date when you want your records to be released.
* **reason-for-release**: Explain the reason for releasing your medical records in a brief paragraph.
* **signed-date**: Sign the date when you signed the consent form.
* **signed-by**: Select who signed the form (Doctor, Nurse, or Other).
* **signature**: Sign with your signature to confirm your consent.
* **doctor-signature**: Please do not complete this field unless instructed otherwise by a healthcare provider.
* **healthcare-provider-signature**: Please do not complete this field unless instructed otherwise by a healthcare provider.
* **release-form-saved**: Check this box to confirm that the form has been saved.
* **patient-relationship**: Select your relationship with the healthcare provider from the dropdown menu (Parent/Primary Caregiver, Partner, Spouse, Child, or Other).
