# Employee Feedback Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Feedback Release Form" is a tool to collect employee feedback, suggestions, and ratings to help identify areas of strength and areas for improvement in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID (number) in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Provide your department (optional).
3. Enter your role or position (optional).
4. Share your suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions For Improvement" field.
5. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field (optional).
6. Rate your overall experience in the "Employee Feedback Rating" field, using a number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`): Enter a brief description of your experience and thoughts.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department or team you belong to (optional).
* **Role** (`role`): Enter your current role or position in the company (optional).
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`): Provide concrete, actionable suggestions for improving our workplace.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience (optional).
* **Employee Feedback Rating** (`employee_feedback_rating`): Rate your overall experience, using a number. This will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): This field is for internal use only and should not be completed by employees. Please leave this field blank.
