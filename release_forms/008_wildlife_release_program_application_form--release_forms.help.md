# wildlife_release_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildlife_release_program_application_form is a form designed for applicants to provide information for the wildlife release program. This program aims to release animals back into the wild after rehabilitation or conservation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the species of animal being released.
2. Provide your weight if you are releasing an animal.
3. Describe the health status of the animal.
4. Schedule the release date for the animal.
5. Select the release location for the animal.
6. Enter your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Species** (`species_id`, `number`, required: false): Select or enter the species of animal being released.
* **Weight** (`animal_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the animal.
* **Health** (`animal_health`, `text`, required: false): Describe the health status of the animal.
* **Date** (`release_date`, `date`, required: false): Schedule the release date for the animal.
* **Location** (`release_location`, `text`, required: false): Choose the release location for the animal.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for program records.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for a successful application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the wildlife release program coordinators.
- Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.
