# Pediatrics Medical Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pediatrics Medical Release Form is used to collect information about a child's medical history, emergency contact, medical conditions, medications, and other relevant details. This form helps healthcare providers assess the child's health status and ensure that they receive appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please note**: This form is for pediatrics medical release purposes only. Please ensure that all the required information is accurate and up-to-date.

2. **Section 1: Medical History**
	* Enter the child's **Medical History** in the text field.
3. **Section 2: Emergency Contact**
	* Enter the **Emergency Contact**'s name and relationship with the child.
4. **Section 3: Medical Conditions**
	* List any **Medical Conditions** the child has, such as allergies or chronic diseases.
5. **Section 4: Medications**
	* List any **Medications** the child is currently taking.
6. **Section 5: Guardian**
	* Select the **Guardian** responsible for the child's care.
7. **Section 6: Date of Birth**
	* Enter the child's **Date of Birth** in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
8. **Section 7: Medical Staff**
	* Select the **Medical Staff** who will be treating the child.
9. **Section 8: Medical Facility**
	* Enter the name of the **Medical Facility** where the child will receive care.
10. **Section 9: Date and Time**
	* Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the child's appointment.
11. **Section 10: Emergency Contact Relationship**
	* Select the **Relationship** of the **Emergency Contact** (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, or Friend).
12. **Section 11: Signature**
	* Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
13. **Section 12: Printed Date**
	* Enter the date and time you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (Section 1):
	+ A brief description of the child's medical history, including any illnesses, injuries, or medical procedures.
* **Emergency Contact** (Section 2):
	+ The name and relationship of the person responsible for the child's care in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions** (Section 3):
	+ A list of any medical conditions, allergies, or health issues the child has.
* **Medications** (Section 4):
	+ A list of any medications the child is currently taking.
* **Guardian** (Section 5):
	+ The person responsible for the child's care. This is typically a parent or legal guardian.
* **Date of Birth** (Section 6):
	+ The child's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical Staff** (Section 7):
	+ The name of the healthcare provider(s) responsible for the child's care.
* **Medical Facility** (Section 8):
	+ The name of the medical facility where the child will receive care.
* **Date** (Section 9):
	+ The date of the child's appointment in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (Section 10):
	+ The time of the child's appointment in the HH:MM format.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Section 11):
	+ The relationship of the person responsible for the child's care in case of an emergency (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, or Friend).
* **Signature** (Section 12):
	+ A signature from the person responsible for the child's care, indicating they have read and understood the information provided.
* **Printed Date** (Section 13):
	+ The date and time the form was signed.
