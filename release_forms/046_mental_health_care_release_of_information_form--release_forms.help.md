# mental_health_care_release_of_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health care release of information form is a document that allows you to release your mental health care information to authorized parties. This form is used to obtain your consent to release your mental health care information and to ensure that it is handled appropriately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the questions accurately.
2. Fill in your name and date of birth.
3. Select your healthcare provider from the list.
4. Enter your patient number and medical record number (if applicable).
5. Choose the type of medical record you are releasing (electronic or paper).
6. Select who authorized you to sign this form (patient, family member, or healthcare provider).
7. Enter the date you are authorizing the release of your mental health care information.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent**: Provide a brief statement indicating your willingness to release your mental health care information.
* **Name of Individual**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Select the healthcare provider who has treated you.
* **Patient Number**: Enter your patient number if you have one.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter your medical record number if you have one.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your most recent visit to the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Facility**: Select the healthcare facility where you were treated.
* **Department**: Select the department where you were treated (e.g., cardiology, dermatology, etc.).
* **Physician**: Select the physician who treated you.
* **Medical Record Type**: Choose the type of medical record you are releasing (electronic or paper).
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date you are authorizing the release of your mental health care information.
* **Authorized By**: Select who is authorized to sign this form (patient, family member, or healthcare provider).
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are giving your consent.
* **Authorized By**: Select who is authorized to sign this form.
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date you are authorizing the release of your mental health care information.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
