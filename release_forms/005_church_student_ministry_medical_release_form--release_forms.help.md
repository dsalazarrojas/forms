# Church Student Ministry Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain medical information and emergency contact details from parents or guardians for students participating in the church student ministry programs. It helps us to ensure that we can provide proper care and support in case of any medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the student's name, date of birth, and parent or guardian's name in the corresponding fields.
*   Provide your emergency contact phone number, so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
*   If the student has any known medical conditions, please describe them in the "Known medical conditions" field.
*   If you have a primary physician, please provide their name and contact information.
*   If you have any insurance coverage, please provide the insurance provider's name and policy number.
*   For each question, please answer honestly and thoroughly.
*   If you do not consent to emergency treatment, please select "I do not consent" in the "Consent for emergency treatment" field.
*   If you have any special medical instructions or notes for us, please provide them in the "Special medical instructions" field.
*   Finally, sign the "Signature acknowledgement" field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the full legal name of the student.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format to enter the student's date of birth.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the parent or guardian who is filling out this form.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a best number to reach you in case of an emergency.
*   **Known Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): If the student has any known medical conditions, please describe them here.
*   **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required/optional): If the student has a primary physician, please provide their name.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required/optional): If the student has any insurance coverage, please provide the insurance provider's name and policy number.
*   **Consent for Emergency Treatment** (`consent_for_treatment`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one of the options to indicate your consent or non-consent for emergency treatment.
*   **Special Medical Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any special medical instructions or notes for us, please provide them here.
*   **Signature Acknowledgement** (`signature_acknowledgement`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you will sign to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
