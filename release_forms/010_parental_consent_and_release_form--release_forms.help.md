# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent And Release Form is designed to collect necessary information from a parent or guardian about their child's participation in a program or activity. This form will help establish clear communication and ensure that the child's involvement is with their permission.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2.  Choose the relationship between you (parent/guardian) and the child in the "Relationship To Child" field.
3.  Indicate your permission for the child's participation in the "Guardian Permission" field.
4.  Select the contact method you prefer for communication in the "Contact Method" field.
5.  Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
6.  Sign and date the form in the "Signature" field, indicating your agreement to the terms.
7.  Specify the release date for the child's participation in the "Release Date" field.
8.  Enter the start date of the program or activity in the "Program Start Date" field.
9.  Enter the end date of the program or activity in the "Program End Date" field.
10. Provide your consent for the child's participation in the "Parental Consent" field.
11. Enter the emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (`Child Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child participating in the program or activity.
* **Relationship To Child** (`Relationship To Child`, select_one, required): Choose how you are related to the child (Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
* **Guardian Permission** (`Guardian Permission`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your permission for the child's participation by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates or information (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required): Provide your contact details, such as phone number, email, or address.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, date, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Release Date** (`Release Date`, date, required): Specify the date of the child's release from the program or activity.
* **Program Start Date** (`Program Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program or activity.
* **Program End Date** (`Program End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program or activity.
* **Parental Consent** (`Parental Consent`, text, optional): Provide your consent for the child's participation in the program or activity.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact information for the child.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand all the questions before filling in the form.
* Fill in accurate and clear information to avoid any confusion.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you have given correct information.
* Return the completed form to the program or activity administrators for processing.
