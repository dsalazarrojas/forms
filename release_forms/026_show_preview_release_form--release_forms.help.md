# Show Preview Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Show Preview Release Form is used to collect information from participants and event organizers before showing a preview of a production or performance. This form helps to obtain consent from participants and gather contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Indicate your consent status for participating in the production or performance.
5. Provide the name of the production team.
6. Enter the name of the event organizer.
7. Enter the date of the event.
8. Enter the time of the event.
9. Enter the name of the theater where the event will take place.
10. Indicate your consent status for promotional recording.
11. Enter a unique identifier for this form (optional).
12. Date of participant consent (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent to participate in the production or performance.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the production team.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the event (in 12-hour format).
* **Theater Name** (`theater_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the theater where the event will take place.
* **Promotional Recording Consent** (`promotional_recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent for promotional recording.
* **Show Preview Release Form ID** (`show_preview_release_form_id`, `text`, optional): Enter a unique identifier for this form (if required).
* **Participant Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, `date`, optional): If you've given consent, enter the date of your consent.
