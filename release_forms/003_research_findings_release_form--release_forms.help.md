# Research Findings Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is for researchers to document and release their research findings to authorized parties. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the proper documentation of your research.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your research findings in the space provided under "Research Findings."
2. Ensure you are ready to share your research results by setting the "Release Status" to either "Pending", "Approved", or "Released" accordingly.
3. If necessary, add your institutional review information and the individual(s) who signed the consent.
4. Finally, ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Findings**: Describe the main findings of your research in this section.
  - Required: Optional
  - Type: Text
  - Use this space to provide a brief summary of your research results.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date you are releasing your research findings.
  - Required: False
  - Type: Date
  - This field is used to record the date when you are releasing your research findings.
* **Author Sign-off**: This field is not required.
  - Optional
  - Type: Text
  - Please add your name if you wish to sign off on your research findings.
* **Release Status**: 
  - Required: True
  - Type: Select One
  - Options: "Pending", "Approved", "Released"
  - Please select the release status of your research findings from the options provided.
* **Institutional Review**: If necessary, add any institutional review information.
  - Optional
  - Type: Text
  - Please provide any relevant institutional review information.
* **Consent Signed By**: This field is not required.
  - Optional
  - Type: Text
  - If consent was signed by someone, please enter their name here.
* **Date Signed Consent**: This field is not required.
  - Optional
  - Type: Date
  - If consent was signed, enter the date when it was signed.
