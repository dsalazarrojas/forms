# Distributor Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Agreement Release Form is a document used to officially release a distributor from their agreement with a company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the Distributor Name of the person or company being released.
3. Enter the Agreement Date of the last date the agreement was active.
4. Enter the Reason for Removal, stating the reason why the distributor is being released.
5. Enter the Effective Date, the date the release takes effect.
6. Optional: Enter any additional notes about the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distributor Name**: Enter the name of the person or company being released from the agreement. This field is required.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date the agreement was last active. This field is required and should be in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Reason for Removal**: Enter a brief explanation for why the distributor is being released from the agreement. This field is required.
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date the release takes effect. This field is required and should be in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the release. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the standard date format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact <form@gic.mx> for further assistance.
