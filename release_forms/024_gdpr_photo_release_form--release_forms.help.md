# GDPR Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of an individual's parents or guardians to use their child's photo. It is a legal requirement for various photo shoots, events, or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Subject Name** of the child.
2. Enter the **Subject Age** of the child.
3. Enter the **Address** of the child.
4. Check the box for **Parent Permission** if the parent/guardian is giving consent.
5. Check the box for **Release Type** if you wish to use the photo.
6. Enter a description of the **Image Description**.
7. Enter the **Date Taken** and **Time Taken** of the photo.
8. Enter the **Location** where the photo was taken.
9. (Optional) Add any **Additional Notes** about the photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subject Name** (`photo_release_form_subject_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child who is the subject of the photo.
* **Subject Age** (`photo_release_form_subject_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the child who is the subject of the photo.
* **Address** (`photo_release_form_subject_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the child's residence or the location where the photo was taken.
* **Parent Permission** (`photo_release_form_parent_permission`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate that the parent/guardian is giving consent for the use of the photo.
* **Subject Contact** (`photo_release_form_subject_contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the child or their parents/guardians.
* **Release Type** (`photo_release_form_release_type`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate whether you wish to use the photo.
* **Image Description** (`photo_release_form_image_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the photo.
* **Date Taken** (`photo_release_form_image_date_taken`, date, required): Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Time Taken** (`photo_release_form_image_time_taken`, time, required): Enter the time the photo was taken.
* **Location** (`photo_release_form_image_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Additional Notes** (`photo_release_form_additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the photo.
