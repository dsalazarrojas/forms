# Expansion Pack Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Expansion Pack Release Form" is a crucial step in the expansion pack release process for game teams. It allows you to provide essential information about the expansion pack, ensuring a smooth and organized release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections and understand what each field is asking for.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3.  Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Team** (Select One, required: false):
    This field asks you to confirm that you are a member of a game team. This is a select-one field with two options: 'Yes' or 'No'. Please choose the correct option to proceed.
* **Release Planner** (Text, required: false):
    In this text field, enter the name or identifier of the person responsible for the release planning process. This helps to track and coordinate release efforts within the team.
* **Release Date** (Date, required: false):
    Provide the date on which the expansion pack is planned to be released. This ensures that everyone is aware of the release timeline.
* **Promotional Assets** (Select Multiple, required: false):
    Here, you can choose the promotional assets required for the release, such as 'Yes' for promotional materials or 'No' if no assets are needed. This helps in preparing the necessary promotional materials for the release.

## Tips
* Always double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you're not duplicating work by confirming that you're not already releasing the same expansion pack.
* If you're unsure about any field or the release process, consult with your team or contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
