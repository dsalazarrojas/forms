# Permission To Use Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Permission To Use Photo Release Form" is a document used to obtain permission from a photographer to use their photos in various contexts. This form is used to ensure that the photographer agrees to the terms and conditions of using their work.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- Make sure to have the photographer sign and date the form.
- Once completed, the form should be returned to the photographer for their signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Permission To Use Photo Release** (`release_forms_1`, text, required): This field should include a detailed description of the purpose for which the photo will be used.
- **Name of Photographer** (`photographer_forms_3`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer who took the photos.
- **Company of Photographer** (`photographer_forms_4`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the company or organization associated with the photographer.
- **Date of Photo Taken** (`photographer_forms_5`, date, optional): If available, enter the date the photo was taken.
- **Purposes of Use** (`release_forms_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose which of the following the photo will be used for (e.g., "Yes" for each applicable category).
  * Yes
  * No
  * Other (optional)
- **Additional Uses** (`photographer_forms_7`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which additional uses the photo will have (e.g., "Yes" for each applicable category).
  * Yes
  * No
  * Other (optional)
- **Release Signature** (`photographer_forms_8`, number, required): The photographer must sign and date this form to approve the terms of use.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully and ensure all fields are complete and accurate before submitting it to the photographer.
- This form is a legally binding contract, so ensure to have it signed by the photographer before using the photo.
- Keep a copy of the signed form as proof of permission.
