# Energy Plan Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for releasing energy plans to ensure they are properly transferred and managed. It captures essential details about the energy plan, including the utility name, account number, and transfer dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Utility Name** field with the name of the utility company associated with the energy plan.
2. Enter the **Account Number** of the energy plan you want to release.
3. Select the correct **Account Number Type** from the options provided.
4. Enter the **Reason for Release** to ensure the release is documented correctly.
5. Enter the **Date of Transfer**, which marks the date the energy plan was transferred.
6. Enter the **Transfer Amount**, if applicable.
7. Enter the **Release Date**, which marks the date the energy plan was released.
8. Enter the **Transfer Date**, which marks the date the energy plan was transferred.
9. Enter the **Authorized Name** of the person authorizing the release.
10. Enter the **Authorization Date**, which marks the date of authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Utility Name** (`Utility Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the utility company.
- **Account Number** (`Account Number`, text, required): Enter the account number of the energy plan.
- **Account Number Type** (`Account Number Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of account number from the options provided.
  - Meter: Meter account number.
  - Service: Service account number.
  - Account: Account account number.
- **Reason for Release** (`Reason for Release`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the release.
- **Date of Transfer** (`Date of Transfer`, date, optional): Enter the date the energy plan was transferred.
- **Transfer Amount** (`Transfer Amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount transferred, if applicable.
- **Release Date** (`Release Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the energy plan was released.
- **Transfer Date** (`Transfer Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the energy plan was transferred.
- **Authorized Name** (`Authorized Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person authorizing the release.
- **Authorization Date** (`Authorization Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of authorization.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are correctly filled out to avoid any errors.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant documentation or consult with the relevant authority.
