# supreme_nails_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a liability release and waiver related to COVID-19. It is essential to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the necessary information for the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the release form at the top of the page. This section is optional and is used for any additional comments or information.
2. In the "Name" field, please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. In the "Address" field, please provide your address as it appears on your identification.
4. In the "Email" field, please provide your email address. Ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
5. In the "Phone" field, please provide your phone number. Ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
6. In the "Date Of Birth" field, please provide your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
7. In the "Emergency Contact" field, please provide the name of the contact person in case of an emergency.
8. In the "Emergency Contact Name" field, please provide the name of the emergency contact.
9. In the "Emergency Contact Phone" field, please provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
10. In the "Relationship" field, please select the relationship between you and the emergency contact. The options are:
	* Parent
	* Guardian
	* Spouse
	* Other (please specify in the next field)
11. If you select "Other", please provide a description of the relationship in the "Other" field.
