**Tournament Photography and Filming Consent Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to collect consent from participants for tournament photography and filming. It gathers information about the participant's details, photography and filming instruments, locations, and consents, which will be used to create a record of consent from participants for photography and filming at the tournament event. This form is designed to be completed by tournament participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select the instrument you used for photography (Camera, Drone, Phone, GoPro, or Other).
3. Select the instrument you used for filming (Camera, Drone, Phone, GoPro, or Other).
4. Provide the location where the photography took place.
5. Select a time for the photography session.
6. Sign your name in the Participant Signature section.
7. Enter your email address in the Participant Email section.
8. Check the Photo Consent box if you allow photography, and check the Filming Consent box if you allow filming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Provide your name and any other relevant details.
* **Photo Instrument**: Select the instrument you used for photography (e.g., Camera, Drone, Phone, GoPro, or Other).
* **Filming Instrument**: Select the instrument you used for filming (e.g., Camera, Drone, Phone, GoPro, or Other).
* **Photo Location**: Enter the location where the photography took place.
* **Photo Time**: Select a time for the photography session. You can use the 24-hour clock format.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Photo Consent**: Check the box if you allow photography. If you do not want to be photographed, do not check this box.
* **Filming Consent**: Check the box if you allow filming. If you do not want to be filmed, do not check this box.
