# Photo Print Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a release from a client for printed photos, allowing the photographer to use or share the images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Name**, **Photo Title**, **Photographer Name**, and **Date** fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the **Photo Number** assigned to the photo.
3. Select the **Release** option to indicate whether the photo is released or not.
4. Enter the **Signature Date** of the client or authorized representative.
5. If there are any print restrictions, enter them in the **Print Restriction** field (optional).
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the photo in the **Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Print Release Form Template** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should not be filled in.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the release.
* **Photo Title** (`photo_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the photograph or image.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer who took the picture.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the photograph was taken.
* **Photo Number** (`photo_number`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the photo.
* **Release** (`release`, select_one, required): Choose whether the photo is released or not.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the client or authorized representative signed the release.
* **Print Restriction** (`print_restriction`, text, optional): If there are any restrictions on printing the photo, enter them here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the photo.
