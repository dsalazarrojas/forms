# TV Series Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Series Release Form is designed to collect information from participants, allowing them to agree or disagree to the release of their content. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and secure data submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter your participant name**: This is the field to fill in your name.
2.  **Enter your participant email address**: Enter your email address as per your official communication.
3.  **Choose your consent**: Select one of the options to agree, disagree, or request to review and sign.
4.  **Enter your production company (if applicable)**: If you are a production company, provide your details here.
5.  **Select release date (if applicable)**: Enter the release date of your content.
6.  **Specify media use (if applicable)**: Choose how you want your content to be used, such as filming or media.
7.  **Add participant notes (if necessary)**: Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to add.
8.  **Sign this form**: Please sign this form digitally to confirm your agreement or disagreement.
9.  **Enter participant date (if applicable)**: Enter the date of your content release, if it is different from today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Name**
* **Participant Name**: This field is to enter your name. Please fill in your name accurately.

### **Participant Email**
* **Participant Email**: This field is to enter your email address for official communication. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.

### **Consent**
* **Consent**: Here, you can choose to agree, disagree, or ask to review and sign below to indicate your consent to the release of your content.

### **Production Company**
* **Production Company**: If you are a production company, enter your details here to be included in the release.

### **Release Date**
* **Release Date**: Enter the release date of your content if it differs from today.

### **Participant Media Use**
* **Participant Media Use**: Choose how you want your content to be used: filming, media, both, or neither.

### **Participant Notes**
* **Participant Notes**: Add any extra comments or information you'd like to include.

### **Participant Signature**
* **Participant Signature**: Please sign this form digitally to confirm your agreement or disagreement.

### **Participant Date**
* **Participant Date**: Enter the date of your content release. If different from today, enter it here.
