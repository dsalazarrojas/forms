**pet_nail_trim_liability_release_form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is a liability release form for pet nail trimming services. It is designed to be filled out by pet owners to provide information about their pet and their consent for the nail trimming service. The form helps to inform the pet owner about the risks and benefits associated with nail trimming and to obtain their explicit consent for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select the correct pet owner's consent from the options provided:
	* Full Release: The pet owner releases the liability for any injuries or damages that may occur during the nail trimming process.
	* Partial Release: The pet owner provides partial liability for some injuries or damages that may occur during the nail trimming process.
	* None: The pet owner declines to provide any liability release.
3. Enter the pet's name in the "pet name" field.
4. Select the pet's type from the options provided:
	* Cat
	* Dog
	* Other
5. Enter the pet's breed (if applicable).
6. Enter the pet's age.
7. Select the type of nail trimming service:
	* Front
	* Back
	* Both
8. Enter the owner's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Nail Trim Liability Release Form** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): A brief introduction to the form.
* **Pet Owner Consent** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the three options to indicate the level of liability release:
	+ Full Release: The pet owner releases the liability for any injuries or damages that may occur during the nail trimming process.
	+ Partial Release: The pet owner provides partial liability for some injuries or damages that may occur during the nail trimming process.
	+ None: The pet owner declines to provide any liability release.
* **Pet Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of your pet:
	+ Cat
	+ Dog
	+ Other
* **Pet Breed** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the breed of your pet (if applicable).
* **Pet Age** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your pet's age.
* **Nail Type** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of nail trimming service:
	+ Front
	+ Back
	+ Both
* **Owner Name** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the pet owner.

## Tips

* Make sure to read the form carefully and understand the terms of the liability release before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
* Complete the form honestly and accurately to ensure that you are fully aware of the risks and benefits associated with the nail trimming service.
