# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Photo Release Form is a tool used to collect photo release information from users who have taken or will take photos that may be published on social media platforms. This form is essential for ensuring that the photo release is valid and compliant with any applicable laws or regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your name and email address, which will be used to contact you about the photo release.
2.  Provide your phone number, which will be used for further communication (optional).
3.  Enter the date you plan to take or have taken the photo.
4.  Describe the location where the photo was taken or will be taken.
5.  Select the social media platforms where the photo will be published.
6.  Enter the date the photo will be published on social media.
7.  Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Photo Release** (`photo_release`): Enter the number of the photo you are releasing, as it will be used for reference.
*   **Name** (`name`): This is your name as it will be displayed on social media.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the photo release.
*   **Phone** (`phone`): Provide your phone number for further communication (optional).
*   **Photo Release Date** (`photo_release_date`): Enter the date you plan to take or have taken the photo.
*   **Photo Release Description** (`photo_release_description`): Briefly describe the photo and its context.
*   **Photo Release Location** (`photo_release_location`): Provide the location where the photo was taken or will be taken.
*   **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`): Select the social media platforms where the photo will be published (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok).
*   **Publish Date** (`publish_date`): Enter the date the photo will be published on social media.
