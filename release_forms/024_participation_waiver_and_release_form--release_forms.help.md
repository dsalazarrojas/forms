# participation_waiver_and_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture participant information and to obtain their agreement to the waiver and release terms. It is an essential document that helps to protect both the participant and the organization offering the program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct waiver agreement from the options provided. Please read the options carefully and choose the one that best describes your situation.
2. Enter your participant details, including your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, and country. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Select the correct release option. Please read the options carefully and choose the one that best describes your situation.
4. Enter the date and time of signing for the waiver and release.
5. Enter the date and time of signing for the participant.
6. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waiver Agreement** (Waiver Agreement, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the waiver agreement that best describes your situation. Please read the options carefully and choose the one that best describes your situation.
* **participant_details** (Participant Details, note, required/optional): This field is used to capture information about the participant. Please enter your name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, and country accurately.
* **Release of All Liability** (Release of All Liability, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select the release option. Please read the options carefully and choose the one that best describes your situation.
* **Waiver Sign Date** (Waiver Sign Date, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date on which the waiver was signed.
* **Waiver Sign Time** (Waiver Sign Time, time, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time on which the waiver was signed.
* **Release Sign Date** (Release Sign Date, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date on which the release was signed.
* **Release Sign Time** (Release Sign Time, time, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time on which the release was signed.
* **Participant Sign Name** (Participant Sign Name, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Sign Date** (Participant Sign Date, date, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date on which the participant signed.
* **Participant Sign Time** (Participant Sign Time, time, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time on which the participant signed.
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Participant Phone, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (Participant Address, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's address.
* **Participant City** (Participant City, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's city.
* **Participant State** (Participant State, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's state.
* **Participant Zip** (Participant Zip, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's zip code.
* **Participant Country** (Participant Country, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the participant's country.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organization offering the program or activity.
