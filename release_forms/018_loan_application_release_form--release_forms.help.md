# Loan Application Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Release Form is designed to collect information about an individual's loan application, including their personal details, employment status, and contact preferences. This form aims to gather all necessary information for the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Applicant Information" page with your personal details.
2. Move on to the "Loan Details" page to provide information about your loan application.
3. On the "Contact Preferences" page, let us know your preferred contact method and frequency.
4. In the "Credit Score" and "Employment Length" fields, enter the requested numerical values.
5. On the "Income Source" and "Employment Status" pages, select your relevant options.
6. Fill out the "Employment Type" and "Marital Status" pages according to your employment type and marital status.
7. Enter your "Date of Birth" and "Time" in the correct formats.
8. Complete the "Address 1" and "Address 2" fields with your physical address.
9. Enter the "City," "State," and "Zip" fields with your location details.
10. Provide the country of residence in the "Country" field.
11. Enter your "Email" and "Phone" contact details.
12. On the "Loan Status" page, select the relevant option.
13. Finally, add any notes about your loan application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information**: (id: 1)
	+ Name (text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the loan application process.
	+ Address 1 (text): Enter your current physical address.
	+ Phone (text): Enter your contact phone number.
	+ Email (email): Enter your email address.
* **Loan Details**: (id: 2)
	+ Loan Amount (number): Enter the amount you are applying for.
* **Contact Preferences**: (id: 3)
	+ Contact Frequency (text): Choose your preferred contact frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Communication Preferences**: (id: 4)
	+ Communication Method (text): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, SMS).
* **Credit Score**: (id: 5)
	+ Credit Score (number): Enter your credit score.
* **Income Source**: (id: 6)
	+ Income Source (text): Select your income source (e.g., employment, self-employment, pension).
* **Employment Length**: (id: 7)
	+ Employment Length (number): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Employment Status**: (id: 9)
	+ Employment Status (select_one): Select your current employment status (Active, Retired, Disabled, Unemployed).
* **Employment Type**: (id: 10)
	+ Employment Type (select_multiple): Select your employment type (Yes, No).
* **Marital Status**: (id: 11)
	+ Marital Status (select_one): Select your marital status (Active, Inactive).
* **Date of Birth**: (id: 12)
	+ Date of Birth (date): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Time**: (id: 13)
	+ Time (time): Enter the time you are available.
* **Address 1**: (id: 14)
	+ Address 1 (text): Enter your physical address.
* **Address 2**: (id: 15)
	+ Address 2 (text): Enter any additional address information.
* **City**: (id: 16)
	+ City (text): Enter the city of your physical address.
* **State**: (id: 17)
	+ State (text): Enter the state of your physical address.
* **Zip**: (id: 18)
	+ Zip (text): Enter the zip code of your physical address.
* **Country**: (id: 19)
	+ Country (text): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Loan Status**: (id: 22)
	+ Loan Status (select_one): Select the status of your loan (Active, Inactive).
* **Note**: (id: 23)
	+ Note (note): Add any additional comments or notes about your loan application.

Please fill out the form accurately and according to the provided instructions. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the loan administrators.
