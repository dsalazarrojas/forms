# Unconditional Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain an unconditional release from a client, allowing a subcontractor to work on a project without any legal or contractual restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the title of the project in the first field.
2.  As the subcontractor, select "Yes, I Accept" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.
3.  If you have declined the project as the subcontractor, provide a brief reason in the "Reason for Decline" field.
4.  As the client, select "Yes, I Accept" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.
5.  If you have declined the project as the client, provide a brief reason in the "Reason for Decline" field.
6.  Enter the date you are signing this form.
7.  Sign your name as the client.
8.  Sign your name as the subcontractor.
9.  Sign your name as the project manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Unconditional Release Form** (`title`, **text**, required): Enter the title of the project.
*   **Accept** (`subcontractor_accept`, **select_one**, required): If you are the subcontractor, choose "Yes, I Accept" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.
*   **Reason for Decline** (`subcontractor_decline_reason`, **text**, optional): If you are the subcontractor and have declined the project, provide a brief reason for your decline.
*   **Accept** (`client_accept`, **select_one**, required): If you are the client, choose "Yes, I Accept" to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.
*   **Reason for Decline** (`client_decline_reason`, **text**, optional): If you are the client and have declined the project, provide a brief reason for your decline.
*   **Date** (`signature_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date on which you are signing this form.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, **note**, required): Sign your name as the client.
*   **Subcontractor Signature** (`subcontractor_signature`, **note**, required): Sign your name as the subcontractor.
*   **Project Manager Signature** (`project_manager_signature`, **note**, required): Sign your name as the project manager.
