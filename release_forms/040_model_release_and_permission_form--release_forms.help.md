# Model Release And Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release And Permission Form is used to obtain permission from a subject to use their likeness or image in a project. This form ensures that the subject is aware of and consents to the use of their image in the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the subject's details.
2. Make sure to include the subject's signature and date for consent.
3. The model release date is optional, but if provided, it should be the date of the original image capture or shoot.
4. If you are a model (or their representative), fill out the form to express your consent.
5. Answer "True" or "False" to the two statements.
6. If you answer "False" to either of the statements, provide a brief explanation in the optional text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter the subject's full name.
* **Model Release Date** (2, date, optional): Enter the date of the original image capture or shoot, if applicable.
* **Photographer's Name** (3, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer or media representative responsible for capturing the image.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, optional): Enter a contact number where the subject can be reached for further information or clarification.
* **Email** (5, email, optional): Enter the subject's email address where they can be reached for further information or clarification.
* **Signature** (6, text, optional): This is where the subject signs the form to express their consent.
* **I Agree I am the model** (7, select_one, optional): Answer "True" if you are the model, otherwise answer "False".
* **I Agree to use my likeness** (8, select_one, optional): Answer "True" if you agree to use your likeness in the project, otherwise answer "False".
* **Why Did You Not Agree** (9, text, optional): If you answer "False" to either of the statements, provide a brief explanation in this text field.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to keep a record of the form and the subject's consent.
* The subject's consent is required for use of their likeness or image in the project.
* This form is only a guideline and may be subject to change or modification.
