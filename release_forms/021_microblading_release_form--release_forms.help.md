# Microblading Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microblading Release Form is used to collect information from clients after a microblading procedure. This form helps ensure that clients receive accurate and timely follow-up care and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the date of your microblading procedure.
2. Enter the time of your microblading procedure.
3. Add any additional notes or instructions related to your procedure.
4. Enter your email address so that your care team can contact you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your microblading procedure.
* **Time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your microblading procedure.
* **Note** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or instructions related to your procedure, such as any concerns or questions you may have for your care team.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that your care team can contact you for follow-up.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter the correct date and time of your procedure to ensure accurate follow-up.
- If you have any concerns or questions after completing this form, please reach out to your care team promptly.
- This form is intended for use in a clinical setting, such as a tattoo removal or medical clinic. If you need to use this form for a different purpose, please modify it carefully to reflect the new context.
