# Permanent Makeup Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Release Form is used to gather information about a client's permanent makeup experience. This form ensures that all relevant details are captured at the end of the process, helping to prevent errors and discrepancies during billing, follow-up, and other administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal and contact information in the "Client Information" field.
2. Confirm the permanent makeup technique used in the "Permanent Makeup Technique" field.
3. If applicable, provide additional details about the process in the "Release Form Details" field.
4. If you have any accompanying photos or videos, upload them in the "Photo and Video Details" field.
5. Submit the form to complete the permanent makeup release process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Required): Enter your name, address, and contact information to ensure we can reach you with any updates or follow-ups regarding your permanent makeup experience.
* **Release Form Details** (Required): Describe any notable events, injuries, or concerns that occurred during the permanent makeup process.
* **Permanent Makeup Technique** (Required): Choose the type of permanent makeup service you received:
	+ Brows
	+ Lash Extensions
	+ Micro-Blading
	+ Permanent Eyebrows
	+ Permanent Lash Line
* **Permanent Makeup Technique** (Required): Select the specific technique used:
	+ Eyebrow
	+ Eyelash
	+ Eyelash-Extension
	+ Full Face
	+ Lip
	+ Lash
	+ Lip-Extension
* **Photo and Video Details** (Optional): If you have photos or videos from the permanent makeup process, upload them here for reference and future use.
