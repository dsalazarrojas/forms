# Blueprint Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blueprint Records Release Form is a comprehensive document used to formalize agreements between record labels or publishers and artists regarding the release of their music recordings. This form captures essential details about the artist, recording, and release terms to ensure accurate and transparent documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the artist information fields, including their legal name, stage name (if applicable), email, phone number, and address.
2.  Select the correct recording title, date of recording, and location.
3.  Choose the type of release (e.g., single, EP, full album, etc.).
4.  Select the distribution territory for the release (e.g., worldwide, specific regions, etc.).
5.  Choose the distribution formats (e.g., digital streaming, physical CD, vinyl, etc.).
6.  Determine the rights granted to the artist (e.g., mechanical, performance, synchronization, etc.).
7.  Enter the royalty percentage and advance payment amount if applicable.
8.  Specify whether the agreement is exclusive or non-exclusive.
9.  Set the agreement term length.
10. Add any additional terms or notes.
11. Finally, sign and date the agreement by the artist, and include an optional witness name (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the artist's legal name.
* **Stage Name** (`artist_stage_name`, text, optional): Enter the artist's professional or stage name (if applicable).
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter the artist's contact email address.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter the artist's contact phone number.
* **Artist Address** (`artist_address`, text, required): Enter the artist's full mailing address.
* **Recording Title** (`recording_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the recording being released.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date the recording was made.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, optional): Enter the studio or venue where the recording took place.
* **Producer Name** (`producer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the producer involved.
* **Record Label Name** (`label_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the record label.
* **Type of Release** (`release_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of release (e.g., single, EP, album, etc.).
* **Distribution Territory** (`distribution_territory`, select_one, required): Select where the release will be distributed (e.g., worldwide, specific regions, etc.).
* **Distribution Format** (`distribution_format`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable distribution formats (e.g., digital streaming, physical CD, vinyl, etc.).
* **Rights Granted** (`rights_granted`, select_multiple, required): Select the rights granted to the artist (e.g., mechanical, performance, synchronization, etc.).
* **Royalty Percentage** (`royalty_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the artist's royalty percentage (if applicable).
* **Advance Payment Amount** (`advance_payment`, number, optional): Enter the advance payment amount (if applicable).
* **License Type** (`exclusive_or_nonexclusive`, select_one, required): Choose whether the agreement is exclusive or non-exclusive.
* **Agreement Term Length** (`term_length`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the agreement (e.g., 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, etc.).
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Add any special conditions or notes.
* **Artist Signature** (`artist_signature`, text, required): Type the artist's full legal name as their signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness (if required).

Note: This guide assumes that the form will be completed by the artist or their representative. Please refer to your company's internal guidelines for specific use cases and requirements.
