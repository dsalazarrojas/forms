# mvr_release_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of employees or applicants for the sharing of information related to their employment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by filling in the required information about the employee/applicant in the "Employee" field.
* If applicable, provide the employer's information in the "Employer" field.
* If relevant, fill in the "Driving History" field with details about the employee/applicant's driving record.
* Finally, in the "Release" field, select "True" if the employee/applicant consents to the sharing of their information, and "False" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Enter the name and any relevant details of the individual for whom you are obtaining consent.
* **Employer**: If this form is being filled out on behalf of an applicant, enter the name and address of the applicant's employer. Otherwise, enter the name and address of the company.
* **Driving History**: If the individual has a driving record, please provide a brief description of it.
* **Release**: Select "True" to indicate that the individual consents to the sharing of their information, and "False" to indicate that they do not.
