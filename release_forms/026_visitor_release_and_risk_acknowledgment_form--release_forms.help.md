# Visitor Release And Risk Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Visitor Release And Risk Acknowledgment Form is a document that aims to capture visitor information, acknowledge potential risks associated with visiting a particular location or facility, and gather contact information for emergency purposes. This form is typically used in situations where visitors are required to provide their details and sign off on the risks associated with their visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Visitor Details**: Please fill in the visitor's details, including their name, contact information, and any additional comments.
2. **Risk Acknowledgments**: Select the risks associated with the visit that you acknowledge and understand.
3. **Signature and Date Visited**: Sign your name and date your visit.
4. **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. **Emergency Contact**: If applicable, provide emergency contact information for a person to contact in case of an emergency.
6. **Employee Information**: If you are visiting as an employee, please provide your company, department, title, and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Details** (`visitor_details`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to capture the visitor's name and any additional comments.
* **Risk Acknowledgments** (`risk_acknowledgments`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field allows you to select the risks associated with the visit that you acknowledge and understand. Please select all relevant risks.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is used for the visitor to sign their name, indicating they have read and understood the risks associated with the visit.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, `date`, required: `false`): Please enter the date you visited the location or facility.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, `time`, required: `false`): Please enter the time you visited the location or facility.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is used to capture your contact information, including address.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: `false`): If applicable, provide emergency contact information for a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Please enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your address.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is used to capture any additional comments you may have.
* **Type of Visitor** (`visitor_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of visitor you are (e.g. employee, contractor, etc.).
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_sign`, `text`, required: `false`): If you are an employee, please sign your name in this field.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your title as an employee.
* **Date** (`employee_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Please enter the date you signed the form as an employee.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): If you are an employee, please sign your name in this field.
* **Employee Company** (`employee_company`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter the company you work for as an employee.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your phone number as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Please enter your email address as an employee.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your department as an employee.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the risk level associated with your visit.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, required: `false`): If applicable, provide the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Please enter the emergency contact person's email address.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and understand the risks associated with the visit before completing this form.
* If you are an employee, please sign and date the form as required.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the facility management or administration.
