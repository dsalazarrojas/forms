# University Research Study Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Study Release Form is a document used to record and share information about a university research study. The form is designed to provide a structured approach to recording project details, allowing for easy tracking and release of study results.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the participant's name (Page 1).
2. Enter the project's name (Page 1).
3. Enter a brief description of the project (Page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the participant involved in the research study. This field is not required.
* **project_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the research project. This field is not required.
* **project_description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the research project. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* Verify that you have permission to complete this form and submit the information.
* If you are unsure about any field or the form's purpose, contact your research supervisor or department administrator for guidance.
