# Vehicle Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's release, including owner details, vehicle characteristics, and release conditions.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle owner's name in the "Vehicle Owner Name" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's license number in the "Vehicle License Number" field.
3. Select the type of release from the options provided in the "Type Of Release" field.
4. Enter the date of release in the "Release Date" field.
5. Provide a reason for the release in the "Reason For Release" field.
6. Enter the name of the assignee (person or entity receiving the vehicle) in the "Name Of Assignee" field.
7. Describe the relationship between the assignee and the vehicle in the "Relationship Of Assignee" field.
8. Enter the assignee's address in the "Address Of Assignee" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner Name** (`vehicle_owner_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the vehicle owner.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's license number for identification purposes.
* **Type Of Release** (`release_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options for the type of release (e.g., "Sale", "Transfer", "Return").
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date the vehicle is being released.
* **Reason For Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of release.
* **Name Of Assignee** (`assignee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the vehicle.
* **Relationship Of Assignee** (`assignee_relationship`, text, required): Describe the relationship between the assignee and the vehicle (e.g., "Employee", "Customer", "Family Member").
* **Address Of Assignee** (`assignee_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the assignee.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., "Toyota", "Ford", "Honda").

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided options for guidance.
* This form is designed to be filled in by the vehicle owner or authorized personnel.
