# Student Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by schools to authorize the release of a student to return home or to another facility. It ensures that the student's departure is legitimate and documented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you are using this form for a legitimate reason and not for duplicate purposes.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records or identification.
* **Parent Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian signing this form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the student's ID number or student ID as it appears on their records.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are releasing the student.
* **Time of Release** (`time_of_release`, `time`, required): Enter the time of release in 24-hour format.
* **Location of Release** (`location_of_release`, `text`, required): Enter the location where you are releasing the student.
* **Purpose of Release** (`purpose_of_release`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the student's release, e.g., "To return home" or "To attend a family event."
* **Name of Responsible Official** (`name_of_responsible_official`, `text`, required): Enter the name and title of the person signing this form.
* **Signature of Responsible Official** (`signature_of_responsible_official`, `note`, required): Sign and date this form to authenticate the release.

## Tips
- Ensure that the form is signed and dated by the responsible official before releasing the student.
- Keep a copy of the form for record-keeping purposes.
- If you are unsure about any field or the purpose of this form, please consult with the school administration.
