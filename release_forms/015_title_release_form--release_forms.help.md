# Title Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Title Release Form is used to collect information about a vehicle being released. This form is essential for various reasons, including ensuring compliance with regulations, facilitating smooth vehicle transfer processes, and maintaining accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Vehicle Owner field with the correct information about the current owner of the vehicle.
2. Enter the Vehicle Year field with the year the vehicle was manufactured.
3. Provide the Vehicle Make and Vehicle Model fields with the correct information about the vehicle's make and model.
4. Enter the Vehicle VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) field with the vehicle's unique identifier.
5. Fill out the Release Owner field with the owner's name and contact information.
6. Enter the Release Date field with the date of the vehicle release.
7. Sign both Signature 1 and Signature 2 fields with your name and print your name and date.
8. Enter the date for Signature 1 and Signature 2 in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Owner** (`vehicle_owner`, text, optional): Enter the current owner's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Provide the correct make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the correct model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, etc.).
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, text, optional): Enter the unique vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Release Owner** (`release_owner`, text, optional): Enter the owner's name and contact information.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the vehicle release.
* **Signature 1** (`signature1`, note, optional): Sign your name in this field to confirm your involvement.
* **Signature 2** (`signature2`, note, optional): Sign your name in this field to confirm your involvement.
* **Signature 1 Date** (`signature1_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the first signature.
* **Signature 2 Date** (`signature2_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the second signature.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the correct information about the vehicle and its owner before filling out the form.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy to avoid errors.
* Sign and date both signatures to confirm your involvement in the release process.
* Keep a record of the signed and dated signatures for future reference.
