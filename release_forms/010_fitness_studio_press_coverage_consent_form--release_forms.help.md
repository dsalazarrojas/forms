# Fitness Studio Press Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Studio Press Coverage Consent Form" is a form designed to collect information about a member's consent to be photographed or filmed for press coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page of the form.
2. Review and understand the terms and conditions of photo release and consent dates.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (`member_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in activities, please describe them here.
* **Media Use** (`media_use`, required): Select "None" if you do not want your image to be used for press coverage.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, required): Select "None" if you do not want your image to be used for press coverage. If you do want to be photographed, select "Other" and describe below.
* **Photo Release Date** (`photo_release_date`, optional): If you select "Other" for Photo Release, enter the date when you are available for photography.
* **Photo Release Time** (`photo_release_time`, optional): If you select "Other" for Photo Release, enter the time when you are available for photography.
* **Photo Release Date End** (`photo_release_date_end`, optional): If you select "Other" for Photo Release, enter the date when your photography release will end.
* **Photo Release Time** (`photo_release_time_end`, optional): If you select "Other" for Photo Release, enter the time when your photography release will end.
* **Consent** (`consent_type`, required): Select the type of consent you are giving (e.g., photo release, video release, or media use).
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates about press coverage.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergency.
* **Address** (`address`, optional): Enter your address so we can contact you in case of emergency.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, describe them here.
* **Medical Conditions Description** (`medical_conditions_description`, optional): Describe your medical conditions in detail.
* **Medical Conditions Treatment** (`medical_conditions_treatment`, optional): Describe the treatment for your medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions Treatment Duration** (`medical_conditions_treatment_duration`, optional): Enter the duration of your medical conditions treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, optional): Enter the date when you gave your consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, optional): Enter the time when you gave your consent.
* **Consent Note** (`consent_note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your consent.

Please note that some fields are marked as required. This means that you must fill out these fields in order to complete the form.
