# Training Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide their information and consent for the release of training videos. It ensures that participants' details and consent are collected in a standardized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Participant Information" section with your details as required.
2. Provide details about the video in the "Video Information" section.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Release Consent" to indicate whether you consent to the release of the video.
4. If you have any additional recording information, type it in the "Recording Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your name, role, and any other identifying information as required.
* **Video Information**: Briefly describe the video, including its title, date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Release Consent**: Select "Yes" to consent to the release of the video, or "No" if you do not want it to be released. This field is optional, but it's crucial for the video release process.
* **Recording Information**: Provide any additional details about the recording, such as the audio or video equipment used, the software used, and any other relevant technical information.
