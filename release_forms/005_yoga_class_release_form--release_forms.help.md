# yoga_class_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "yoga_class_release_form" is used to gather participant information, medical status, and waiver/releases for yoga class participants. This form helps protect both the participant and the yoga class organizers from potential liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2.  Enter your date of release in the "Date" field.
3.  Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your release.
4.  If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5.  Choose one of the emergency contacts from the list in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
6.  If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medical devices, provide information in the "Medical Information" section.
7.  Sign the "Waiver Signature" field to confirm you have read and understand the waiver.
8.  Finally, sign the "Participant Signature" field to confirm your release and participation in the yoga class.
