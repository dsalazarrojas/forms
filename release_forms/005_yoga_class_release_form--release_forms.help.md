# yoga_class_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "yoga_class_release_form" is used to gather participant information, medical status, and waiver/releases for yoga class participants. This form helps protect both the participant and the yoga class organizers from potential liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2.  Enter your date of release in the "Date" field.
3.  Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your release.
4.  If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5.  Choose one of the emergency contacts from the list in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
6.  If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medical devices, provide information in the "Medical Information" section.
7.  Sign the "Waiver Signature" field to confirm you have read and understand the waiver.
8.  Finally, sign the "Participant Signature" field to confirm your release and participation in the yoga class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date you are releasing the yoga class from liability.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm your release.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required): Provide name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`Emergency Contact Signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm your emergency contact's release and responsibility.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, select_one, required): Choose one of the emergency contacts you listed.
* **Liability Consent** (`Liability Consent`, text, required): Sign to confirm you have read and understand the waiver.
* **Medical Information** (`Medical Information`, text, required): Provide any medical conditions, allergies, or medical devices you have.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, list them.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, list them.
* **Medical Devices** (`Medical Devices`, text, optional): If you have any medical devices, list them.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them.
* **Medical Procedures** (`Medical Procedures`, text, optional): If you have undergone any medical procedures, list them.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): If you have any medical history, list it.
* **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, text, optional): If you have any medical notes, list them.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, select_one, required): Choose one of the emergency contacts you listed.
* **Waiver** (`Waiver`, text, required): Read and understand the waiver.
* **Waiver Signature** (`Waiver Signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm you have read and understand the waiver.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, text, required): Sign to confirm your release and participation in the yoga class.
* **Medical Certificate** (`Medical Certificate`, select_one, required): Choose one of the medical certificate options.

## Tips

* Please be honest when filling out the form. If you are unsure about any information, consult with the yoga class organizers.
* Make sure to sign all required fields to confirm your release and participation in the yoga class.
* If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medical devices, provide clear and detailed information to ensure your safety during the yoga class.
