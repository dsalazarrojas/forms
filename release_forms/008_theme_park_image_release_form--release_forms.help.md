# Theme Park Image Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Image Release Form is used to collect information from guests who have provided images or videos of our theme park. This form is necessary to obtain their explicit consent to use their content in promotional materials and media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Guest Email" field.
3. Select one or more options for "Consent Given" and "Guest Video Consent".
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Guest Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
- **Consent Given**: Select all options that apply:
  - Option 1: You grant permission for us to use your image/video in our promotional materials.
  - Option 2: You grant permission for us to use your image/video on social media.
- **Guest Video Consent**: Select all options that apply:
  - Option 1: You grant permission for us to use your video in our promotional materials.
  - Option 2: You grant permission for us to use your video on social media.
  - Option 3: You grant permission for us to share your video on our website.
  - Option 4: You grant permission for us to use your video in any other media or promotional materials.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
