# Legal Case Details Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Case Details Release Form is used to collect and document the necessary information for the release of a legal case. This form ensures that all required details are accurately recorded and that the release is properly authorized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Case Details section with the relevant information about the case.
2. Provide the authorized party's details, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Select the method of sharing the release information (Email, Phone, or In Person).
4. Select the parties to share the release information with (Client, Attorney, or Both).
5. Enter the authorization date and expiration date for the release.
6. Add any additional notes or instructions for the authorized party.
7. Enter the case number and authorized party ID (if applicable).
8. Enter the release date (if applicable).
9. Enter the authorized party's date of birth (if applicable).
10. Enter the authorized party's country, state, city, and zip code (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Details**: This section is used to collect information about the legal case, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Authorized Party Details**: This section is used to collect information about the authorized party, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
	+ **Authorized Party Name**: Enter the name of the authorized party.
	+ **Authorized Party Address**: Enter the address of the authorized party.
	+ **Authorized Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the authorized party.
	+ **Authorized Party Email**: Enter the email address of the authorized party.
	+ **Authorized Party Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions for the authorized party.
* **Share Method**: Select the method of sharing the release information with the authorized party (Email, Phone, or In Person).
* **Share With**: Select the parties to share the release information with (Client, Attorney, or Both).
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date when the release was authorized.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the release will expire.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of the release (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party Country**: Enter the country of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party State**: Enter the state of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party City**: Enter the city of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party Zip**: Enter the zip code of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Case Number**: Enter the case number (if applicable).
* **Authorized Party ID**: Enter the ID of the authorized party (if applicable).
* **Release Reason**: Enter the reason for the release (if applicable).
