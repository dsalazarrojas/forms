# Art Residency Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your stay at the art residency program. This includes your contact details, residency duration, and condition of the space you occupied during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your correct contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the correct dates for your residency start and end dates.
3. Provide information about the space you occupied, including the studio space number and accommodation number if applicable.
4. Confirm that you have returned all keys, equipment, and materials used during your residency.
5. Report on the condition of the space at check-out, and describe any artwork left behind.
6. Indicate if all utilities have been turned off.
7. Confirm if the final utility reading has been taken if applicable.
8. Report if an inspection has been completed at check-out.
9. Provide any notes from inspection.
10. Rate your overall residency experience and provide feedback.
11. Indicate if you are interested in future collaboration.
12. Sign the form to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`): Date of discharge from residency.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`): Full name of resident artist.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`): Email address.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`): Contact phone number. (Optional)
* **Residency Program** (`residency_program`): Name of residency program.
* **Residency Start Date** (`residency_start_date`): When did residency begin.
* **Residency End Date** (`residency_end_date`): Scheduled end date.
* **Studio Space Number** (`studio_space_number`): Assigned studio space.
* **Accommodation Number** (`accommodation_number`): Assigned living space if applicable. (Optional)
* **Keys Returned** (`keys_returned`): Have all keys been returned?
  + Yes - All Keys Returned
  + No - Keys Still Outstanding
  + Not Applicable
* **Keys Details** (`keys_details`): List any missing keys.
* **Equipment Returned** (`equipment_returned`): Select all equipment returned.
* **Studio Condition** (`studio_condition`): Condition of studio space at checkout.
  + Excellent - Clean and Ready
  + Good - Minor Cleaning Needed
  + Fair - Cleaning Required
  + Poor - Significant Cleaning Needed
  + Damaged - Repairs Needed
* **Accommodation Condition** (`accommodation_condition`): Condition of living space.
  + Excellent
  + Good
  + Fair
  + Poor
  + Not Applicable
* **Materials Disposed** (`materials_disposed`): Have you properly disposed of materials?
  + Yes - All Materials Disposed
  + Yes - Some Materials Remaining
  + No - Materials Still Present
* **Materials Details** (`materials_details`): Describe remaining materials.
* **Artwork Removed** (`artwork_removed`): Has all artwork been removed?
  + Yes - All Artwork Removed
  + Partially - Some Artwork Remaining
  + No - Artwork Still Present
* **Artwork Left Behind** (`artwork_left_behind`): Describe any artwork left behind.
* **Utilities Turned Off** (`utilities_turned_off`): Have utilities been turned off?
  + True
  + False
  + Not Applicable
* **Final Utility Reading** (`final_utility_reading`): Final meter readings if applicable.
* **Checkout Inspection Completed** (`checkout_inspection_completed`): Has inspection been completed?
  + True
  + False
* **Inspection Notes** (`inspection_notes`): Any notes from inspection.
* **Residency Experience** (`residency_experience`): Rate your overall residency experience.
  + Excellent
  + Good
  + Average
  + Poor
  + Very Poor
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`): Would you recommend this residency?
  + Definitely Yes
  + Probably Yes
  + Unsure
  + Probably No
  + Definitely No
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`): Any feedback about your residency.
* **Future Collaboration** (`future_collaboration`): Interested in future collaboration?
  + True
  + False
  + Maybe
* **Artist Signature** (`artist_signature`): Type full name as signature.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`): Staff member completing checkout.

## Tips
- Please double-check your contact information before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask the residency staff for guidance.
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Attach any supporting documents if required.
- Review and submit the form once completed.
