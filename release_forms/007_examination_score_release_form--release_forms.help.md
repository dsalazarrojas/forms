# Examination Score Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from a school administration or instructor to release examination scores to the proper parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form correctly.
2. Start by entering the **School Name** in the corresponding text field.
3. Next, enter the **Exam Name** in the text field.
4. Select the **Grade Level** that corresponds to the exam and student.
5. Enter the **Student Name** in the text field.
6. Enter the **Parent Email** in the email field. Please enter the parent's email address that we should use for contact purposes.
7. Enter the **Student Email** in the email field. Please enter the student's email address that we should use for contact purposes.
8. Enter the **Teacher Name** in the text field.
9. Enter the **School Email** in the email field. Please enter the school's email address that we should use for contact purposes.
10. Select the appropriate **Department** from the dropdown menu. This will indicate whether the exam is for an Accounting, English, Finance, History, Math, or Science class.
11. Enter the **Teacher Email** in the email field. Please enter the teacher's email address that we should use for contact purposes.
12. Select the multiple options from the **Department** dropdown menu, but this field is not required.
13. Enter the **Authorized Recipient Name** in the text field. This is the person to whom we should release the scores to.
14. Enter the **Authorized Recipient Email** in the email field. Please enter the authorized recipient's email address that we should use for contact purposes.
15. Enter the **Authorized Recipient Phone** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: The name of the school where the exam was taken.
* **Exam Name**: The name of the exam that was taken by the student.
* **Grade Level**: The grade level of the student taking the exam.
* **Student Name**: The name of the student who took the exam.
* **Parent Email**: The email address of the parent/guardian of the student.
* **Student Email**: The email address of the student who took the exam.
* **Teacher Name**: The name of the teacher who administered the exam.
* **School Email**: The email address of the school where the exam was taken.
* **Department**: The department for which the exam was taken (Accounting, English, Finance, History, Math, or Science).
* **Teacher Email**: The email address of the teacher who administered the exam.
* **Department Options**: A dropdown menu of departments for which the exam was taken (Accounting, English, Finance, History, Math, or Science).
* **Authorized Recipient Name**: The name of the person to whom the scores should be released.
* **Authorized Recipient Email**: The email address of the person to whom the scores should be released.
* **Authorized Recipient Phone**: The phone number of the person to whom the scores should be released.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting this form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
