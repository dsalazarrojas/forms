# Energy Solutions Feedback Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Solutions Feedback Release Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with energy-related solutions. This form aims to understand their satisfaction, concerns, and preferences related to energy efficiency, renewable energy, and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please select each field that applies to your experience:
    *   **Customer Feedback**: A text field where you can describe your thoughts, feelings, or concerns about your energy solutions experience.
    *   **Can Reuse for Promotional or Informational Materials**: This field allows you to choose whether the company can use your feedback for promotional or informational purposes.
    *   **Permission**: Indicate whether you grant permission for your feedback to be used in such materials.
    *   **Comments**: This is a text field for additional comments or details you'd like to provide about your experience.
    *   **Date of Visit**: If you visited one of our energy solutions, please enter the date of your visit.
    *   **Time of Visit**: If applicable, enter the time of your visit to help us better understand your timing.
    *   **Energy Provider**: Select from the options (Utility, Energy company, Other) to specify who provided you the energy solution.
    *   **Sustainability Focus**: You may select one or multiple focus areas for the energy solution that interested you (Energy efficiency, Renewable energy, Sustainability focus).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Feedback** (customer_feedback):
    *   A field to enter your personal, detailed feedback about your energy solutions experience.
*   **Can Reuse for Promotional or Informational Materials** (reuse_permission):
    *   Decide if you permit the reuse of your feedback in any promotional or informational materials (such as brochures, videos, or articles).
*   **Permission** (customer_permission):
    *   Indicate if you agree to grant permission for your feedback's reuse.
*   **Comments** (comments):
    *   Enter any additional comments you wish about your experience.
*   **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit):
    *   Enter the exact date of your visit to better understand your visit specifics.
*   **Time of Visit** (time_of_visit):
    *   Enter the specific time of your visit, if applicable.
*   **Energy Provider** (energy_provider):
    *   Select the category under which you received the energy solution (Utility, Energy company, Other).
*   **Sustainability Focus** (sustainability_focus):
    *   You may choose one or more options that represent the key sustainability aspects of your energy solution experience (Energy efficiency, Renewable energy, Sustainability focus).
