# Risk Waiver Form And Publicity Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect risk waiver and publicity release information from participants in various activities or events. It is essential to fill out this form to understand and agree to the terms of the risk waiver and publicity release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Participant Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Participant Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
4. Enter the date of the activity or event in the `Date` field (if applicable).
5. Select the type of release you are granting (risk waiver or publicity release) in the `Release Type` field.
6. Indicate whether you are a parent or minor participant in the `Release Participant` field.
7. Enter your signature in the `Participant Signature` field.
8. Enter the date you signed the form in the `Date Signed` field.
9. Select your agreement to the risk waiver and publicity release terms in the `Risk Waiver Agreement` and `Publicity Release Agreement` fields.
10. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the `Risk Waiver Text` and `Publicity Release Text` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the activity or event (if applicable).
* **Release Type** (Required): Select the type of release you are granting (risk waiver or publicity release).
* **Release Participant** (Required): Indicate whether you are a parent or minor participant.
* **Participant Signature** (Required): Enter your signature.
* **Date Signed** (Required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Risk Waiver Agreement** (Required): Select your agreement to the risk waiver terms.
* **Publicity Release Agreement** (Required): Select your agreement to the publicity release terms.
* **Risk Waiver Text** (Required): Enter any comments or remarks related to the risk waiver.
* **Publicity Release Text** (Required): Enter any comments or remarks related to the publicity release.
* **Signature** (Required): Enter your signature.
* **Signature Date** (Required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Risk Waiver Agreement** (Required): Select your agreement to the risk waiver terms.
* **Publicity Release Agreement** (Required): Select your agreement to the publicity release terms.
