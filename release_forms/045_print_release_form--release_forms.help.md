# print_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The print_release_form is used to provide information for print and digital releases of a document or image. Fill out this form to specify details such as the print and digital release types, distribution methods, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of release you want to create (print, digital, or both).
2. Enter the **subject** (name) and **client** (name) of the release.
3. Choose a **photographer** who took the photos or captured the image.
4. Enter the **email** of the person or team who will receive the release.
5. Optionally, select the **print and distribute** options to specify how you want to distribute the release.
6. Enter the **quantity** and **size** of prints you want to make and the **quantity** and **size** of distributed copies.
7. Select the **release type** to specify if you want to release for print, digital, or both.
8. Specify the **distribution method** to choose how you want to distribute the release (print, social media, friends and family, or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Print and distribute**: Select the methods for distributing the print release (Print, Share on social media, Share with friends and family)
* **Subject**: The name of the subject in the release (required).
* **Email**: The email of the person or team who will receive the release (optional).
* **Signature**: A note for the photographer or person responsible for the release.
* **Date**: The date of the release (optional).
* **Time**: The time of the release (optional).
* **Photographer**: The name of the photographer who took the photos or captured the image (required).
* **Studio**: The studio where the photos or image were taken (optional).
* **Client**: The name of the client or person who commissioned the photos or image (required).
* **Release type**: The type of release desired (Print, Digital, Both).
* **Print quantity**: The number of prints to be made (optional).
* **Print size**: The size of the print to be made (optional).
* **Distribution quantity**: The number of copies to be distributed (optional).
* **Distribution size**: The size of the distributed copies (optional).
* **Distribution**: The method of distribution (Print, Share on social media, Share with friends and family, Other).
* **Release type**: The type of release (Print, Digital, Both).
