# Curriculum Content Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Curriculum Content Release Form is designed to collect necessary information for the release of a new or updated curriculum content. This includes the title, name of the organisation, email, and other relevant details about the content and its release.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "title" field with the title of the curriculum content.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your organisation's name in the "organisation" field.
5. Describe the "what" field is about.
6. Identify "who" the content is for or targeted at.
7. Specify "where" the content will be deployed.
8. If applicable, provide a "why" for the release.
9. Describe "what-is-the-content" being released.
10. If applicable, specify "what-is-the-version" of the content.
11. Identify "who-is-approving" the release.
12. Add any comments about the release in the "comments" field (optional).
13. Sign the form with your signature in the "signature" field.
14. Choose all that apply in the "terms" field and provide specific answers to each term in the "terms*" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_01_title** (Title): The title of the curriculum content.
* **form_01_name** (Name): Your name.
* **form_01_email** (Email): Your email address.
* **form_01_organisation** (Organisation): Your organisation's name.
* **form_01_what** (What): Describe what the content is about.
* **form_01_who** (Who): Identify who the content is for or targeted at.
* **form_01_where** (Where): Specify where the content will be deployed.
* **form_01_why** (Why): If applicable, provide a reason for the release.
* **form_01_what-is-the-content** (What is the content): Describe what content is being released.
* **form_01_what-is-the-version** (What is the version): If applicable, specify the version of the content.
* **form_01_who-is-approving** (Who is approving): Identify who is approving the release.
* **form_01_comments** (Comments): Add any comments about the release (optional).
* **form_01_signature** (Signature): Sign the form with your signature.
* **form_01_submit** (Submit): Submit the form to release the content.
