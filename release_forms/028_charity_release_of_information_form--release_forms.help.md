# Charity Release Of Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document an individual's authorization to share their personal information with a specific organization or entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you understand the purpose and scope of the information being released.
* Fill out each field accurately and truthfully, including your full legal name, date of birth, identification number, contact information (email and phone number), and current address.
* Select the type of information being released (e.g., medical records, financial information, etc.).
* Identify the organization or entity releasing and receiving the information.
* Specify the purpose of the information release.
* Confirm your understanding and consent to share the information, as well as your acknowledgment of the release duration and revocation rights.
* Sign the form, including the date and a witness name (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (`individual_name`): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Identification Number** (`identification_number`): Enter your identification number (e.g., ID card, social security number, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your current address.
* **Type of Information Being Released** (`information_type`): Select all types of information being released (e.g., medical records, financial information, etc.).
* **Organization Releasing Information** (`releasing_entity`): Enter the name of the organization sharing the information.
* **Organization Receiving Information** (`receiving_entity`): Enter the name of the organization receiving the information.
* **Contact Person at Receiving Organization** (`receiving_entity_contact`): Enter the name and title of the contact person at the receiving organization (if required).
* **Purpose of Information Release** (`purpose_of_release`): Explain why the information is being released.
* **I Authorize the Release of Information** (`consent_acknowledge`): Select "Yes, I Authorize" to confirm your consent.
* **Duration of Authorization** (`duration_of_release`): Select the duration of the authorization (e.g., one time only, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, indefinite until revoked).
* **Any Conditions or Restrictions** (`conditions`): Enter any special conditions or restrictions on the information release.
* **I Understand I Can Revoke This Authorization** (`revocation_right`): Select "Yes, I Understand" to confirm your understanding of revocation rights.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): Enter the name of a witness (if required).
* **I Confirm I Have Read and Understood This Form** (`confirmation`): Select "Yes, I Confirm" to finalize the form.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand all fields and the purpose of the form before signing.
* Sign the form only when you are certain that you understand and agree to the terms.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
