# Property Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Release Form is a document used to record the transfer of ownership of a property from one individual or entity to another. It captures essential details about the property, including its type, address, and sale price. This form ensures that all parties involved in the transfer are aware of the terms and conditions of the sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property details, including the current owner's name, the new owner's name, and the date and time of sale.
2. Provide a description of the property and its type.
3. Enter the sale price and the currency used for the transaction.
4. Enter the address of the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_release_form**: This is the title of the form that summarizes its purpose and content. You should review this section to understand the context and objectives of the form.
* **current_owner**: This field is used to record the name of the individual or entity that currently owns the property. 
* **new_owner**: This field is used to enter the name of the individual or entity that will become the new owner of the property.
* **date_of_sale**: This field captures the date of the sale or transfer of the property. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **time_of_sale**: This field captures the time of the sale or transfer of the property.
* **property_details**: This field provides a description of the property being released, including any relevant details that may be useful for the transfer process.
* **property_type**: This field is used to categorize the type of property being released (e.g., house, land, etc.).
* **sale_price**: This field is used to enter the monetary value of the sale or transfer of the property.
* **sale_price_currency**: This field captures the currency used for the sale or transfer of the property.
* **property_address**: This field is used to enter the address of the property being released.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or disputes during the transfer process.
* Please verify the information provided to ensure it is correct and consistent with other relevant records.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please reach out to the relevant authorities or contacts involved in the transfer process.
