# Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining work release from an employer to ensure a smooth and efficient communication process between the employer and the patient's doctor or physician.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the necessary information.
2. Ensure that all fields are correctly filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Employer Information**: Select whether the patient is an active, active with limitations, or not working. 
* **Employment Details**: Enter any relevant employment details.
* **Medical Cause**: Specify the reason for the work release.
* **Signature**: Enter your name and date for the patient and doctor/physician.
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your employer.
* **Employer Address**: Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer Phone**: Enter your employer's phone number.
* **Employer Email**: Enter your employer's email address.
* **Physician Information**: Enter the doctor/physician's name.
* **Doctor Address**: Enter the doctor/physician's address.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter the doctor/physician's phone number.
* **Doctor Email**: Enter the doctor/physician's email address.
* **Doctor Signature**: Enter the doctor/physician's signature.
* **Patient Signature**: Enter your signature as the patient.
* **Date of Discharge**: Enter the date the patient is to be discharged from work.
* **Date of Discharge**: This field is a duplicate of the previous field, it seems there might be a typo or a mistake. Please ignore it.
