# Photography Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain legal permission from clients to use their images in various capacities. This form is typically used for commercial photography shoots where the photographer is using the client's images for their own business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in accurately.
2. Enter the client's name and email address (if they have one).
3. Select the date the client is granting permission for the images to be used.
4. Specify the type of permission being granted (e.g. social media, advertising, etc.).
5. Read and agree to the release terms, which outline the usage and conditions of the images.
6. Indicate whether the client has signed and agreed to the terms.
7. Enter the number of images being released.
8. List all images being released (if multiple images are being used).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information** (required): This field is used to provide a brief description of the shoot or project.
* **Client Name** (text, optional): Enter the client's name as it appears on their business card or contact information.
* **Client Email** (email, required): Enter the client's email address if they have one.
* **Permission Date** (date, required): Enter the date the client is granting permission for the images to be used.
* **Permission Type** (text, required): Specify the type of permission being granted (e.g. social media, advertising, etc.).
* **Release Terms** (text, required): Read and agree to the release terms, which outline the usage and conditions of the images.
* **Release Form Signed** (select one, required): Indicate whether the client has signed and agreed to the release terms.
* **Number of Images** (number, required): Enter the total number of images being released.
* **List of Images** (note, optional): List all images being released (if multiple images are being used).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the release terms before granting permission.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* Make sure the client understands and agrees to the terms before signing. 
* Store the completed form securely, in a place where it can be easily accessed by the photographer and client. 
* This form should only be used for commercial photography shoots, not for personal or editorial purposes.
