# Photo Waiver Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Waiver Release Form is a document that allows individuals to grant permission for their photo to be used by an organization. This form is used to ensure that the subject is aware of and consents to the use of their photo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter the date and time the photo was taken in the "Release Date" and "Release Time" fields.
3. Describe the purpose of the photo in the "Purpose" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the photo in the "Waiver Text" field.
5. Mention the location where the photo was taken in the "Photo Location" field.
6. Identify who took the photo in the "Photo Taken By" field.
7. Describe the event or purpose for which the photo was taken in the "Photo Taken For" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: <user_name>: Enter your full name, as it will appear on the waiver document.
* **Release Date**: <release_date>: The date the photo was taken.
* **Release Time**: <release_time>: The time the photo was taken.
* **Purpose**: <purpose>: A brief description of the photo's purpose.
* **Waiver Text**: <waiver_text>: A brief description of the photo.
* **Photo Location**: <photo_location>: The location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Taken By**: <photo_taken_by>: The name of the person who took the photo.
* **Photo Taken For**: <photo_taken_for>: The event or purpose for which the photo was taken.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the organization that took your photo.
* This form is for your protection and ensures that your photo is used in accordance with your wishes.
* By signing this form, you are granting permission for your photo to be used by the organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organization directly.
