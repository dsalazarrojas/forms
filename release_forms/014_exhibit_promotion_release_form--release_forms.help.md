# Exhibit Promotion Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibit Promotion Release Form is used to collect and review information about an exhibitor's promotion and exhibition materials before they are shared or used. This form ensures that all promotion and exhibition materials are reviewed and approved, preventing unauthorized or misleading information from being distributed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibitor Name** field with the name of the exhibitor who is promoting their exhibition or event.
2. Enter the exhibitor's **Email** address in the **Exhibitor Email** field.
3. Select the name of the **Exhibition Name** from the drop-down options.
4. Choose whether the media is **Approved** or **Not Approved** from the **Approved Media** field.
5. If the media is **Approved**, you can add additional **Promotion Terms** in the **Promotion Terms** field.
6. Select whether you **Agree** or **Disagree** with the **Promotion Terms** from the **Promotion Terms Agreed** field.
7. Add any additional **Promotion Terms Additional** in the **Promotion Terms Additional** field if needed.
8. Choose whether you **Agree** or **Disagree** with the **Exhibition Terms** from the **Exhibition Terms Agreed** field.
9. Add any additional **Exhibition Terms Additional** in the **Exhibition Terms Additional** field if needed.
10. Fill in the **Date** and **Time** fields if required.
11. Add any **Note** if needed.
12. Select whether the exhibitor has **Media List** or not from the **Media List** field.
13. Fill in the **Promotion** field if needed.
14. Enter the **Exhibition Contact** name and **Email** and **Phone** and **Media** fields if required.
15. Add any additional **Exhibition Terms Additional** in the **Exhibition Terms Additional** field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization promoting the exhibition or event.
* **Exhibitor Email**: Enter the email address of the exhibitor.
* **Exhibition Name**: Select the name of the exhibition from the drop-down options.
* **Approved Media**: Choose whether the media is approved or not.
* **Promotion Terms**: Add any additional promotion terms if needed.
* **Promotion Terms Agreed**: Select whether you agree or disagree with the promotion terms.
* **Promotion Terms Additional**: Add any additional promotion terms.
* **Exhibition Terms Agreed**: Select whether you agree or disagree with the exhibition terms.
* **Exhibition Terms Additional**: Add any additional exhibition terms.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the exhibition or event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the exhibition or event.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Media List**: Select whether the exhibitor has media list or not.
* **Promotion**: Fill in any additional promotion materials if needed.
* **Exhibition Contact**: Enter the contact person's name, email, phone, and media for the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Terms Additional**: Add any additional exhibition terms.
* **Promotion Terms Agreed**: Select whether you agree or disagree with the promotion terms.
* **Promotion Terms Additional**: Add any additional promotion terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a colleague or contact the exhibition organizer for clarification.
