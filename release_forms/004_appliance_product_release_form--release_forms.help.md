# Appliance Product Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appliance Product Release Form is a critical document that ensures the quality and safety of products before they are released to the next stage of the production pipeline. This form helps to track the product's movement, ensures that all necessary checks and tests are completed, and keeps a record of the product's history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form for each product release.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Product Information 
* **Appliance Name (1)** (`appliance_name`, Text, required): Enter the product name, e.g., Smart Fridge X100.
* **Model Number (2)** (`model_number`, Text, required): Enter the product's model number.
* **Serial Number (3)** (`serial_number`, Text, required): Enter the product's serial number.
* **Production Batch ID (4)** (`production_batch_id`, Text, required): Enter the production batch ID.
* **Release Details**
* **Quality Inspection Status (7)** (`quality_check_status`, Select One, required): Choose whether the product has passed all quality checks (Passed) or has minor issues (Passed with Minor Issues). If it has failed, please explain the reason for failure.
* **Safety Certification Verified (8)** (`safety_certification_verified`, Select One, required): Choose whether the safety certification is verified (True) or not (False).
* **Firmware Version (9)** (`firmware_version`, Text, optional): Enter the firmware version installed on the product, if applicable.
* **Physical Condition Assessment (10)** (`physical_condition_check`, Select One, required): Choose the product's physical condition: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Release Approver Name (11)** (`release_approver_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the person releasing the product.
* **Release Date (12)** (`release_date`, Date, required): Enter the date of the release.
* **Warehouse Handoff**
* **Warehouse Storage Location (14)** (`warehouse_location`, Text, required): Enter the storage location in the warehouse.
* **Destination Distributor or Retailer (15)** (`destination_distributor`, Text, optional): Enter the destination distributor or retailer name, if applicable.
* **Shipment Tracking Number (16)** (`shipment_tracking_number`, Text, optional): Enter the shipment tracking number, if applicable.
* **Additional Release Notes (17)** (`additional_notes`, Text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the release.

### Tips
- Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, please consult with your supervisor or relevant stakeholders before submitting it.
- The form is an essential document for tracking the product's movement and ensuring its quality and safety.
