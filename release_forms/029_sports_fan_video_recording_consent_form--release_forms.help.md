# sports_fan_video_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for sports fans who are being recorded as part of a sports event or activity. It gathers information on their personal details, video recording settings, and consent for publication and sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Provide your personal details in the "Personal Details" field.
3. Indicate your release permission and provide additional details if necessary in the "Release Permission" field.
4. Specify the date and time of the video recording.
5. Choose your video capture settings (Video, Audio, or Both).
6. Indicate whether you are being recorded for image capture.
7. Choose your consent to publish and share the video.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Sign the "Consent Form Signature" field to confirm your consent.
10. Enter the release date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (page 1): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Personal Details** (page 2): Enter your name, age, and other relevant contact information.
* **Release Permission** (page 3):
	+ "Yes": Grant permission for video recording.
	+ "No": Do not grant permission for video recording.
	+ "Maybe": Indicate uncertainty or ambiguity about video recording.
	+ "Other": Enter other details if not in the previous options.
* **Release Permission Other (if applicable)** (page 4): Enter any other details related to your release permission.
* **Date and Time of Video Recording** (page 5): Enter the date and time of the video recording.
* **Recording Details** (page 6): Enter any other details about the video recording (e.g., location, event, activity).
* **Video Capture** (page 7):
	+ "Video": Record video only.
	+ "Audio": Record audio only.
	+ "Both": Record both video and audio.
* **Image Capture** (page 8):
	+ "No": Do not record images.
	+ "Yes": Record images.
	+ "Only if permitted": Record images if permitted.
* **Consent to Publish** (page 9):
	+ "No": Do not publish the video.
	+ "Yes": Publish the video.
* **Consent to Share** (page 10):
	+ "No": Do not share the video.
	+ "Yes": Share the video.
* **Additional Comments** (page 11): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Consent Form Signature** (page 12): Sign this field to confirm your consent.
* **Release Date** (page 13): Enter the release date.
* **Release Time** (page 14): Enter the release time.
* **Video Location** (page 15): Enter the location where the video recording took place.
* **Video Length** (page 16): Enter the length of the video recording.
* **Consent to Share On** (page 17):
	+ "No": Do not share the video.
	+ "Yes": Share the video.
* **Release Form Signature** (page 18): Sign this field to confirm your release.

Note: This help guide assumes the form is being used for sports event or activity video recording and sharing. If the form is used in a different context, the explanation may need to be adjusted accordingly.
