# Property Damage Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and verify the release of property between two parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the user's information (Page 1: User Information)
2. Provide the party's information (Page 2: Party Information)
3. Describe the damage summary (Page 3: Damage Summary)
4. Confirm the release agreement (Page 4: Release Agreement)
5. Sign and date the form (Page 5: Date and Time)
6. Have both parties sign the form (Page 6: Party Signature and Page 7: Representative Signature)
7. Sign the user's signature (Page 8: User Signature)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required/optional): Enter the user's name and contact information.
* **Party Information** (`Party Information`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the party releasing the property.
* **Damage Summary** (`Damage Summary`, text, required): Describe the damage to the property in detail.
* **Release Agreement** (`Release Agreement`, text, required): Confirm that the property has been inspected and released as agreed upon.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the release.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the release.
* **Party Signature** (`Party Signature`, text, required): Sign the release as the party releasing the property.
* **Representative Signature** (`Representative Signature`, text, required): Sign the release as a representative of the party releasing the property.
* **User Signature** (`User Signature`, text, required): Sign the release as the user receiving the property.

## Tips
* Make sure to have both parties sign the form to confirm the release.
* Date and time should be accurate and reflect the actual time of release.
* Use this form only for property releases between two parties.
