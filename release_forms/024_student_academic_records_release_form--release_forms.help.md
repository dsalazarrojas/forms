## Student Academic Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Academic Records Release Form is a document that enables students to grant or deny authorization to access their academic records. This form is intended for students to control who can access their records and for how long. By completing this form, students can provide or withhold access to their academic information from institutions, individuals, or entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter your student email address in the **student_email** field.
3. Answer the **institution_id** field by selecting 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if you authorize institution access to your records.
4. Choose the desired **authorization_level** to grant access. This includes 'High' or 'Low' options, with 'High' allowing complete access and 'Low' restricting access.
5. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for **authorized_individuals**, indicating if you wish to allow specific individuals access.
6. In the **academic_records_release** section, provide any additional information or comments you'd like to attach to your record release decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: Enter your full name as a student. This information will be associated with this record release request.
* **student_email**: Your email address associated with your student account.
* **institution_id**: Select 'Yes' if you wish to allow the institution to access your academic records, and 'No' otherwise. This field determines if and when the institution can access your records.
* **authorization_level**: Choose between 'High' and 'Low' to grant access. 'High' allows full access, while 'Low' restricts it.
* **authorized_individuals**: Select 'Yes' if you wish to grant access to specific individuals, and 'No' otherwise. This field allows you to specify who, besides the institution, should have access.
* **academic_records_release**: Enter any relevant comments or information related to your record release decision in this field.
* **academic_records_release**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your record release decision.
