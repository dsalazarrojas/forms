# Release And Waiver Of Liability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Release And Waiver Of Liability form is a general-purpose template used to obtain releasors' details, their consent, or other relevant information for various activities or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the releasor's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the releasor's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter a phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the relevant date in the "Date" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Release and Waiver of Liability" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_1`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_2`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_3`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_4`, text, **optional**): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_5`, date, **optional**): Select a date relevant to the activity or event.
* **Release and Waiver of Liability** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_6`, text, **optional**): Enter any comments or notes about your release and waiver.
* **Time** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability_form_7`, time, **optional**): Select a time relevant to the activity or event.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
