# Veterinary Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Release Form is designed to collect information about a pet's medical history and treatment plan when an owner is releasing their pet to a new caregiver or hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the owner's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the pet's name, species, and breed.
3. Provide any relevant medical information, such as medical conditions, medical notes, and medication lists.
4. Indicate the number of times medication has been administered and when.
5. Provide any additional treatment plan information, such as medication schedules and authorization details.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm authorization and provide any necessary notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. owner_name (Text, Optional)
* Enter the owner's name.

### 2. owner_email (Email, Required)
* Enter the owner's email address.

### 3. owner_phone (Text, Optional)
* Enter the owner's phone number.

### 4. pet_name (Text, Required)
* Enter the pet's name.

### 5. pet_species (Text, Required)
* Enter the pet's species (e.g. dog, cat, etc.).

### 6. pet_breed (Text, Required)
* Enter the pet's breed.

### 7. veterinary_name (Text, Optional)
* Enter the name of the veterinary clinic or hospital.

### 8. contact_number (Text, Required)
* Enter the contact number of the caregiver or hospital.

### 9. emergency_contact (Text, Optional)
* Enter the contact information of an emergency contact.

### 10. medical_condition (Text, Required)
* Enter any relevant medical conditions.

### 11. medical_notes (Text, Optional)
* Enter any additional medical notes.

### 12. medication_list (Select Multiple, Optional)
* Select any medications the pet is currently taking. (Aspirin, Benadryl, Ciprofloxacin, Ibuprofen)

### 13. medication_administered (Select One, Optional)
* Select how many times medication has been administered: once, twice, or three times.

### 14. treatment_plan (Text, Optional)
* Enter any additional treatment plan information.

### 15. medication_schedule (Select One, Optional)
* Select when medication is to be administered: immediately, later, or today.

### 16. authorization_type (Select One, Required)
* Select who is authorized to administer treatment: owner, veterinarian, or both.

### 17. authorization_date (Date, Required)
* Enter the date of authorization.

### 18. authorization_hours (Time, Required)
* Enter the hours of authorization.

### 19. authorization_notes (Text, Optional)
* Enter any additional authorization notes.

### 20. signature (Note, Required)
* Sign and date the form to confirm authorization.
