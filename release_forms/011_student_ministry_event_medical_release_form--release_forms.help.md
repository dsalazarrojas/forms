# Student Ministry Event Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to release their child for participation in ministry events, such as camps, retreats, or other activities. It is used to provide critical medical information and emergency contact details to event staff to ensure the child's safety and well-being.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
2.  Provide your child's name in the "Student Name" field.
3.  Enter your name and phone number in the "Parent Name" and "Parent Phone" fields.
4.  Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
5.  If your child has any medical conditions, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6.  If your child is taking any medications, list them in the "Meds" field.
7.  If your child has any allergies, list them in the "Allergies" field.
8.  Finally, provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date): Enter the date of the event your child will be participating in.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text): Enter your child's full name.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text): Enter your full name.
*   **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text): List any known medical conditions or disabilities your child may have.
*   **Meds** (`medications`, text): List any medications your child is currently taking.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, text): List any allergies or sensitivities your child may have.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): Provide any relevant medical history or health concerns.

## Tips
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   We are committed to providing a safe and nurturing environment for your child, and this form is an important part of our effort to do so.
