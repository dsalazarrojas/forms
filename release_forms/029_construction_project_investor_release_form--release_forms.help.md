# Construction Project Investor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an investor's investment and the terms of their release in a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Investor and Project Identification (Section 1)**: This section is used to identify the investor and the project. Fill out the `Investor Full Name or Legal Entity Name` with the investor's name, and select the correct `Investor Type` from the dropdown menu.
2. **Project Details (Section 2)**: In this section, fill out the `Project Name` and `Project Site Address` with the correct information.
3. **Release and Settlement Details (Section 3)**: This section outlines the terms of the release. Fill out the `Original Investment Amount ($)` and `Final Settlement or Release Amount ($)` with the correct figures.
4. **Release Category (Section 4)**: Select the primary reason for the release from the dropdown menu.
5. **Legal Acknowledgment and Release (Section 5)**: Review and agree to the release of liability and future claims.
6. **Payment and Distribution Logistics (Section 6)**: Choose your preferred method of payment and distribution.
7. **Final Submission and Date (Section 7)**: Fill out the title of the person signing for the investor and select the date of the release authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor Full Name or Legal Entity Name**: The full name or legal entity name of the investor.
* **Investor Type**: The type of investor, such as Individual Investor, Private Equity Firm, Institutional Investor, Joint Venture Partner, Lendor/Financial Institution, or Other.
* **Project Name**: The name of the project.
* **Project Site Address**: The address of the project site.
* **Original Investment Amount ($)**: The original amount invested by the investor.
* **Final Settlement or Release Amount ($)**: The total amount being released to the investor.
* **Primary Release Reason**: The reason for the release, such as Project Completion and Exit, Partial Exit/Divestiture, Investor Buyout, Re-capitalization/Refinancing, Loan Maturity/Payoff, or Early Termination/Release.
* **Release of Liability and Future Claims**: Review and agree to the release of liability and future claims.
* **Acknowledgment of Continuing Confidentiality Obligations**: Review and agree to the continuing confidentiality obligations.
* **Acknowledge and Agree (Tax Responsibility for Release)**: The investor is responsible for all tax filings related to this release.
* **Preferred Distribution Method**: Choose the preferred method of payment and distribution, such as Wire Transfer (Preferred), Physical Check via Courier, or Internal Account Transfer.
* **Wire/Payment Instructions Provided**: Confirm that wire/payment instructions are provided.
* **Title of Person Signing for Investor**: The title of the person signing for the investor.
* **Date of Release Authorization**: The date of the release authorization.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions**: Any additional comments or instructions.
