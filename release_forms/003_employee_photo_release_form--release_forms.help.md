# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Photo Release Form is used to obtain the necessary permissions and documentation for employees to have their photos taken. This includes photos for official company purposes, such as ID cards, company events, or other official company materials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the **Employee Photo Release Form** title to confirm the form name.
2.  Fill out your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields to identify yourself.
3.  Enter your valid **Email** and **Phone** numbers to make it easy for the photo taker or the photo processor to contact you.
4.  Provide the **Photo Location** where your photo was taken, or where it will be taken.
5.  Enter the date and time of the photo in the **Photo Date** and **Photo Time** fields.
6.  Briefly state the **Purpose** of the photo, to help the photo processor understand how it will be used.
7.  Sign the **Employee Signature** field to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
8.  A **Witness Signature** and **Witness Name** are also required for photo taking situations where a witness is present.
9.  The **Witness Title** field is used to provide more information about the witness if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Photo Release Form**
*   (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is used to confirm the form name.
### 2. **First Name**
*   (`first_name`, text, optional): This is your first name that will be used for official purposes.
### 3. **Last Name**
*   (`last_name`, text, optional): This is your last name that will be used for official purposes.
### 4. **Email**
*   (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure that you can be contacted regarding your photo.
### 5. **Phone**
*   (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to make it easy for the photo taker or the photo processor to contact you.
### 6. **Photo Location**
*   (`photo_location`, text, optional): Provide the location where your photo was taken or where it will be taken.
### 7. **Photo Date**
*   (`photo_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the photo in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 8. **Photo Time**
*   (`photo_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the photo in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
### 9. **Purpose**
*   (`purpose`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the photo, to help the photo processor understand how it will be used.
### 10. **Employee Signature**
*   (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name here to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
### 11. **Witness Signature**
*   (`witness_signature`, text, optional): If a witness was present, sign here to confirm that you have witnessed the photo taking or have given permission for it.
### 12. **Witness Name**
*   (`witness_name`, text, optional): If a witness was present, provide their name here.
### 13. **Witness Title**
*   (`witness_title`, text, optional): If a witness was present, provide their title here, such as their job title or position.

Tips:
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly, and ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to take or process your photo. If you are unsure about any field or process, do not hesitate to ask your team or supervisor for assistance.
