# Host Event Legal Release Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Host Event Legal Release Authorization form is a mandatory document that must be completed by event participants to grant the event host permission to use their image, likeness, or other identifying information for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your event name and date in the respective fields.
2. Enter your name as the photographer.
3. Select one of the options for the model agreement, indicating your understanding of the event host's rights and responsibilities.
4. Choose any applicable options for the release agreement, specifying the terms of use for your image, likeness, or other identifying information.
5. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
6. Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
7. Enter your emergency contact's phone number and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required false): Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, required false): Enter your name as the photographer or individual capturing your image or likeness.
* **Model Agreement** (`model_agreement`, `select_one`, required false): Choose one of the options below to indicate your understanding of the event host's rights and responsibilities:
	+ I am clear of all rights and responsibilities
	+ I am aware that all rights and responsibilities will be transferred to the event host
* **Release Agreement** (`release_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required false): Choose any applicable options below to specify the terms of use for your image, likeness, or other identifying information:
	+ I am clear of all rights and responsibilities
	+ I am aware that all rights and responsibilities will be transferred to the event host
	+ The event host has the right to use my services for advertising and future events
	+ I will not seek compensation for my appearance
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required false): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required false): Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required false): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required false): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Address** (`model_address`, `text`, required false): Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, required false): Enter your emergency contact's address.
