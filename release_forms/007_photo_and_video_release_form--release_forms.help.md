Before filling out the form, please review the following instructions to avoid duplication of effort and ensure the correct information is provided.

## Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photo And Video Release Form is a document that serves as a legal agreement between the client and the photographer/producer, allowing them to use their likeness, property, or materials for a specific purpose. This form is essential for obtaining the necessary permissions and agreements from clients before publishing, editing, or distributing the photographs or videos.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field. This is the person or entity granting permission for their likeness, property, or materials to be used.
2.  Enter the client's contact information in the **Contact Number** field. This will help with future communication regarding the project.
3.  Enter the client's email address in the **Email Address** field. This will be used for further communication and updates.
4.  Enter the name of the project in the **Project Name** field. This is the title of the project that the photographs or videos will be used for.
5.  Describe the type of materials used in the project in the **Materials** field. Be as detailed as possible.
6.  Describe the usage of the materials in the **Usage** field. This will help determine how the photographs or videos will be used.
7.  Select the **Permissions** option that best suits the client's needs:
    *   **All**: The client grants full permission for the use of their likeness, property, or materials without any restrictions.
    *   **Exclusive**: The client grants exclusive permission, meaning their likeness, property, or materials can only be used by the photographer/producer for this specific project.
    *   **Personal Use**: The client grants permission for personal use only, meaning the photographs or videos can only be used by the client for their personal purposes.
    *   **Non-Exclusive**: The client grants permission for the use of their likeness, property, or materials, but also allows other parties to use them.
    *   **Royalty Free**: The client grants permission for the use of their likeness, property, or materials, but also pays a fee for their use.
8.  Select the **Release Type** options that apply:
    *   **Full**: The client grants permission for the full use of their likeness, property, or materials.
    *   **Partial**: The client grants permission for a partial use of their likeness, property, or materials.
    *   **Exclusive**: The client grants exclusive permission for their likeness, property, or materials.
9.  Enter the date when the form is being signed in the **Release Date** field. This is the date when the client signed the form.
10. Sign the form with your **Client Signature** in the **Client Signature** field. This confirms that the client has reviewed and agreed to the terms of the form.
11. Sign the form with your **Photographer Signature** in the **Photographer Signature** field. This confirms that you have obtained the necessary permissions from the client.
12. Enter the time when the form is completed in the **Date** field.
13. For better clarity, you can add labels for each field. These labels help identify what each field represents.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to the client.
*   Ensure that the client understands the terms and conditions before obtaining their signature.
*   Keep the form up-to-date with any changes to the project or client information.
*   Store the signed form for future reference and compliance purposes.

By following these instructions, you will be able to complete the Photo And Video Release Form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that you have the necessary permissions and agreements from your clients.
