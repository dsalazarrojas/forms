# Security Deposit Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a security deposit for a tenant in a rental property. It requires information from both the landlord and tenant to confirm the deposit release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Tenant ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to the tenant.
2. Enter the Deposit Amount, which is the total amount of the security deposit.
3. Select the Deposit Return Date, which is the date the deposit will be returned.
4. Enter the Date of Return, which is the date the deposit will actually be returned to the tenant.
5. Sign with your Landlord Signature to confirm you are the property owner.
6. Sign with your Tenant Signature to confirm you are the tenant.
7. Enter the Landlord Name, which is the name of the property owner.
8. Enter the Landlord Phone number, which is the contact information for the property owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant ID** (`tenant_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier assigned to the tenant.
- **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the security deposit.
- **Deposit Return Date** (`deposit_return_date`, date, optional): Select the date the deposit will be returned.
- **Date of Return** (`date_of_return`, date, optional): Enter the actual date the deposit will be returned to the tenant.
- **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, optional): Sign this field with your property owner's signature.
- **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, optional): Sign this field with your tenant's signature.
- **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property owner.
- **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the property owner.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure the deposit is released correctly.
* Attach a copy of the signed form to any relevant documents.
* Keep a record of the completed form for your reference.
