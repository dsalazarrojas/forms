# visage_client_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect liability release and waiver information from clients, specifically in the context of COVID-19-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the "Client Name" field with the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
3. Provide the "Contact Info" field with the client's contact information, such as their email address or phone number.
4. Enter the client's "Date Of Birth" in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
5. Enter the client's "Address" in the format "Street Address, City, State ZIP Code".
6. Enter the client's "Contact Email" and "Phone Number" fields with their contact information.
7. For the emergency contact fields, enter the names and contact information of up to 15 emergency contacts. You can skip fields if no emergency contacts are required.
8. Enter the current "Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter the client's contact information, such as their email address or phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the client's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address in the format "Street Address, City, State ZIP Code".
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* For the emergency contact fields 3-15, enter the names and contact information of up to 15 emergency contacts. You can skip fields if no emergency contacts are required.

## Tips
Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding with this form. Double-check that you are not overwriting existing records. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant stakeholders for clarification.
