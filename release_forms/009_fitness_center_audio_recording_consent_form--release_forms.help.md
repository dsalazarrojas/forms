# Fitness Center Audio Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Center Audio Recording Consent Form" is a mandatory form that must be completed by every member before recording takes place. It ensures that you understand and agree to being audio recorded during classes, training sessions, or events held at the fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Member Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field, making sure to use the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the third field.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the fourth field, using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Select the type of recording you consent to by choosing one of the options in the **Event Type** field (e.g., Recording in Class, Recording in Training Sessions, Recording in Events).
6. Choose your consent for audio recording in the **Consent** field (e.g., I consent to the recording of my audio, I do not consent to the recording of my audio).
7. If you have any additional consents, you can provide them in the **Additional Consents** field.
8. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can type them in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of event where you consent to being recorded (e.g., Recording in Class, Recording in Training Sessions, Recording in Events).
* **Consent** (select_multiple, required): Choose whether you consent or do not consent to audio recording.
* **Additional Consents** (text, optional): If you have any additional consents, please provide them here.
* **Additional Notes** (note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes related to your consent.
