# X RAY Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The X RAY Release Form is a standard document used to request the release of a patient's medical records or other sensitive information. This form is designed to be completed by patients or their authorized representatives, ensuring that the necessary details are collected before releasing the information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are a patient or an authorized representative.
2. Enter your name as the patient or representative.
3. Fill in the patient's name.
4. If applicable, enter the medical condition that pertains to the release.
5. Enter the authorization date, which is the date the release was approved.
6. Enter the release date, which is the date the information is being released.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date**: The date on which the patient's medical record or information is being released.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient for whom the release is being made.
* **Medical Condition**: A brief description of the medical condition that pertains to the release (if applicable).
* **Authorization Date**: The date on which the release was approved.
* **Authorization Date**: Not used in this form - this field seems to be a duplicate of the "release_date" field.
