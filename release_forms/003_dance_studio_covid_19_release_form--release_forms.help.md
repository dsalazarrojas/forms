# Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from customers and their families regarding COVID-19 vaccination status, liability waiver, and emergency contact details to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience at the dance studio.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each field carefully and enter the required information.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as this information will be used to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about any field, feel free to ask the studio staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### i_Declare_covid_vaccination_status (ID: 1)
• **i_declare_covid_vaccination_status** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you'll declare your COVID-19 vaccination status. Please enter "Yes" if you've been vaccinated, "No" if you haven't, or "Decline to answer" if you prefer not to disclose this information.

### i_accept_liability (ID: 2)
• **i_accept_liability** (`note`, required/optional): This is a liability waiver, which confirms that you understand and agree to the dance studio's policies and procedures. Please read the waiver carefully and sign it to acknowledge your understanding and agreement.

### i_have_parental_consent (ID: 3)
• **i_have_parental_consent** (`number`, required/optional): This field is for parents or guardians to confirm that they have given consent for their child to participate in dance activities. Please select "1" if you are the parent or guardian, "0" if you are not.

### visiting_teachers (ID: 4)
• **visiting_teachers** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for selecting the visiting teachers or staff members you are working with. Please select all applicable options.

### studio_policies (ID: 5)
• **studio_policies_and_rules** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for describing the dance studio's policies and procedures. Please provide a clear and concise description of what you understand and agree to follow.

### i_have_emergency_contact_details (ID: 6)
• **i_have_emergency_contact_details** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field is for selecting an emergency contact person in case of an emergency. Please select one of the provided options.

## Tips
- Ensure you have read and understood all the fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the studio staff for help.
- This form is not a replacement for any medical advice or guidance. If you have specific medical concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
- The studio reserves the right to request additional information or documentation to ensure the safety and well-being of all participants.
