# Covid 19 Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about patients who have received the Covid-19 vaccine. Please only complete this form for patients who have already been vaccinated.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to review each section before proceeding.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**: Provide general information about the patient, such as their name and date of birth.
* **Vaccination Information (Page 2)**: Answer questions related to the patient's vaccination status, including when they received the vaccine.
* **Medical Condition (Page 3)**: Check if the patient has any medical conditions, such as diabetes or hypertension.
* **Medical History (Page 4)**: List any medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **Allergies (Page 5)**: Check if the patient has any allergies.
* **Medications (Page 6)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Vaccine Side Effects (Page 7)**: Note any side effects the patient may have experienced after receiving the vaccine.
* **Patient Release (Page 8)**: Confirm that the patient has been released from the medical facility.
* **Medical Provider (Page 9)**: Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Signature (Page 10)**: The patient must sign and date the form to confirm their release.
* **Provider Signature (Page 11)**: The healthcare provider must sign and time the form to confirm their agreement with the patient's release.
* **Release Date (Page 12)**: Enter the date the patient was released from the medical facility.
* **Medical Team Member (Page 13)**: Enter the name of the healthcare team member who is signing this form.
* **Patient Phone (Page 14)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Provider Phone (Page 15)**: Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Medical Record Number (Page 16)**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Email (Page 17)**: Enter the patient's email (if applicable).
* **Patient Release (Page 18)**: Confirm that the patient has been released from the medical facility and that you, as the healthcare provider, are releasing them.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to all patients, so please answer carefully based on each individual's situation. If you're unsure about any question, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
