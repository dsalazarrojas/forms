# Audio Recording Remix Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the necessary permissions and agreements from copyright holders for remixing original audio recordings. It helps to establish the terms of use, distribution, and credit requirements for the remix, ensuring that the original creators' rights are respected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Answer the questions carefully, taking into account the specifics of your remix project.
3. Review your answers to ensure they match your intentions and plans for the remix project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Copyright Holder Name** (`copyright_holder_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of the copyright holder or owner of the original recording.
* **Copyright Holder Email** (`copyright_holder_email`, email, required): Provide the contact email address of the copyright holder, allowing for efficient communication and potential follow-up queries.
* **Copyright Holder Phone** (`copyright_holder_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the copyright holder, in case of further questions or concerns.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If the original recording is owned by an organization, enter its name to provide more specific details about the ownership.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Record the date on which you obtained this consent agreement.
* **Original Work Title** (`original_work_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the original recording, which will be the basis for the remix.
* **Original Artist** (`original_artist`, text, required): Identify the name of the original artist or performer who created the original recording.
* **Original Release Date** (`original_release_date`, date, optional): If known, provide the date of the original release.
* **Original ISRC** (`original_isrc`, text, optional): Enter the ISRC code of the original recording, if applicable.
* **Remix Artist Name** (`remix_artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the artist creating the remix.
* **Remix Artist Email** (`remix_artist_email`, email, required): Provide your contact email or that of the artist creating the remix.
* **Remix Purpose** (`remix_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the remix, such as for commercial release, promotional use, personal project, or competition entry.
* **Remix Type** (`remix_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of remix you plan to create, such as full remix, radio edit, or extended mix.
* **Elements Used** (`elements_used`, select_multiple, required): Select the elements from the original recording you plan to use in your remix, such as vocals only, instrumental only, or full mix.
* **Distribution Plan** (`distribution_plan`, select_multiple, required): Determine how you plan to distribute the remix, including streaming platforms, digital download, physical release, radio, or other channels.
* **Royalty Agreement** (`royalty_agreement`, select_one, required): Define the royalty arrangement for the remix, such as original owner receives royalties, split royalties 50-50, or no royalties.
* **Credit Requirement** (`credit_requirement`, text, required): Describe how you intend to credit the original recording, taking into account the original owner's wishes.
* **Approval Required** (`approval_required`, select_one, required): Decide whether the remix requires full approval from the copyright holder or if partial approval is sufficient.
* **Exclusivity** (`exclusivity`, select_one, required): Determine whether the remix is exclusive to you or if it can be used by others without restriction.
* **Term Length** (`term_length`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the consent, such as perpetual, 5 years, or a single release only.
* **Copyright Holder Signature** (`copyright_holder_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature, acknowledging your commitment to these terms.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Record the date of this consent agreement.

## Tips

* Be honest and clear in your answers to ensure accurate representation of your plans for the remix.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to confirm all information is correct.
* Understand that these consents are agreements that should be respected to avoid conflicts and potential lawsuits.
