# Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model release form is used to obtain the model's consent to be photographed or filmed. It's a critical document that ensures the model's rights are respected and minimizes potential legal issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the photo shoot or project.
2. Enter the **Release Title** in the second field, which is a short description of the release itself.
3. Enter the **Model Release Date**, which is the date the model was photographed or filmed.
4. Enter the **Release Date**, which is the date the release is being submitted.
5. Fill in the **Client Name**, **Client Address**, **Client Phone**, and **Client Email** fields with the relevant information.
6. In the **Model Release Signature** field, have the model sign and date their name as proof of their consent.
7. In the **Client Signature** field, have the client sign and date their name as proof of their acceptance of the model's terms.
8. Fill in the **Release Notes** field with any additional information relevant to the release.
9. Enter the **Release Date/Time**, which is the date and time the photoshoot or recording took place.
10. In the **Release Location** field, enter the location where the photoshoot or recording took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title_input`, text, required): A brief description of the photo shoot or project.
* **Release Title** (`release_title_input`, text, required): A short description of the release itself.
* **Model Release Date** (`model_release_date_input`, date, required): The date the model was photographed or filmed.
* **Release Date** (`release_date_input`, date, required): The date the release is being submitted.
* **Client Name** (`client_name_input`, text, required): The name of the client who commissioned the photo shoot or recording.
* **Client Address** (`client_address_input`, text, required): The address of the client who commissioned the photo shoot or recording.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone_input`, text, required): The phone number of the client who commissioned the photo shoot or recording.
* **Client Email** (`client_email_input`, email, required): The email address of the client who commissioned the photo shoot or recording.
* **Model Release Signature** (`model_release_signature_input`, note, required): A note indicating the model's signature and date as proof of their consent.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature_input`, note, required): A note indicating the client's signature and date as proof of their acceptance of the model's terms.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes_input`, text, required): Additional information relevant to the release.
* **Release Date/Time** (`release_date_time_input`, time, required): The date and time the photoshoot or recording took place.
* **Release Location** (`release_location_input`, text, required): The location where the photoshoot or recording took place.
* **Location Note** (`location_note_input`, note, required): A note about the location of the photoshoot or recording.
