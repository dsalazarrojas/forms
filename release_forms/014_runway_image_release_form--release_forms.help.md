# Runway Image Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Runway Image Release Form is used to obtain a license agreement from a photographer for images used in runway shows. It ensures that the photographer grants permission to use the image and provides necessary details for image usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Complete the form by providing the requested information, including your name, contact number, email, and submitter's details.
2. **Specify image details**: Provide a description of the image, the event name, date and time, and photographer details.
3. **Select image license and release type**: Choose the correct image license and release type from the provided options.
4. **Add additional comments**: If necessary, add any additional comments about the image or event.
5. **Submit the form**: Once all required fields are filled out, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Fill in your name as the submitter of this form.
* **Contact Number** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide your contact number for any follow-up questions or requests.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Image Description** (`5`, `text`, required): Describe the image being released, including the event name, date, and time.
* **Image URL** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the URL where the image can be found.
* **Event Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Specify the name of the event where the image was taken.
* **Event Date** (`8`, `date`, required): Enter the date the event occurred.
* **Event Time** (`9`, `time`, required): Specify the time the event occurred.
* **Photographer Details** (`10`, `text`, required): Provide details about the photographer who took the image.
* **Model Details** (`11`, `text`, required): Describe the model in the image, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Image License** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct image license from the provided options.
* **Release Type** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the correct release type from the provided options.
* **Additional Comments** (`14`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the image or event.
* **Submitter** (`15`, `text`, required): Fill in the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email** (`16`, `email`, required): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`17`, `text`, required): Provide the submitter's contact number.
* **Submitter Comments** (`18`, `note`, required): Add any comments or notes from the submitter.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct submitter agreement from the provided options.
* **Submitter Agreement Terms** (`20`, `text`, required): Specify any terms of the submitter agreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Date** (`21`, `date`, required): Enter the date the submitter agreement was made.
* **Submitter Agreement Time** (`22`, `time`, required): Specify the time the submitter agreement was made.
* **Submitter Agreement Signature** (`23`, `text`, required): Include the submitter's signature.
* **Submitter Agreement Title** (`24`, `text`, required): Provide a title for the submitter agreement.
* **Submitter Agreement Notes** (`25`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes for the submitter agreement.
