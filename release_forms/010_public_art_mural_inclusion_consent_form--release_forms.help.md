# Public Art Mural Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information, mural participation details, and consent from individuals who wish to be featured in a public art mural. It is meant to be a simple and straightforward process to ensure that participants are aware of the project's context and implications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2.  Indicate your intended participation in the mural on the second page (e.g., as a subject, artist, or model).
3.  Provide any additional contact details you'd like to share on the third page.
4.  Optionally, you may provide your consent to be included in the mural's documentation on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
    This section asks for your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Mural Participation** (`mural_participation`, text, required: false)
    Please describe your intended participation in the mural, such as being a subject, artist, or model.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false)
    Share any additional contact information you'd like to be included in the mural's documentation.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false)
    This is an optional section where you can indicate your consent to being featured in the mural and its documentation. If you're unsure, you can simply leave this section blank.
