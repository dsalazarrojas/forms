# Article Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Article Release Form is used to obtain the necessary permissions and rights to release an article for use in various contexts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact information in the **Author Information** section.
* Enter the official title of the article in the **Article Title** field.
* Provide the original publication date and source in the **Publication Date** and **Publication Source** fields.
* Select the type of release requested (e.g., Full Rights Transfer, Republishing Rights, etc.) and specify the permitted uses of the article (e.g., online publication, print publication, educational distribution, etc.) in the **Release Scope** section.
* Specify the geographic scope of the release (e.g., worldwide, United States only, etc.) and the duration of the rights granted in the **Geographic Scope** and **Duration of Rights Grant** sections.
* If applicable, specify the compensation provided and the author credit required in the **Compensation and Terms** section.
* Check the box indicating that you have the legal authority to grant the requested rights in the **Confirmation and Signature** section.
* Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms of the release in the **Signature Agreement** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author Full Name** (Text): Your full name as the author of the article.
* **Author Email Address** (Email): Your primary contact email address.
* **Author Phone Number** (Text): Your contact phone number.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (Text): The company, publication, or institution with which you are associated.
* **Article Title** (Text): The official title of the article being released.
* **Original Publication Date** (Date): The date the article was originally published.
* **Original Publication Source** (Text): Where the article was originally published (e.g., journal, book, website, etc.).
* **Article Topic or Category** (Text): The main subject matter of the article.
* **Type of Release Requested** (Select One): The type of release requested (e.g., Full Rights Transfer, Republishing Rights, etc.).
* **Permitted Uses of Article** (Select Multiple): Select all authorized uses of the article (e.g., online publication, print publication, etc.).
* **Geographic Scope of Release** (Select One): Where the article can be used (e.g., worldwide, United States only, etc.).
* **Duration of Rights Grant** (Select One): How long the rights are granted (e.g., indefinite, 1 year, etc.).
* **Compensation for Rights** (Select One): The type of compensation provided (e.g., No Compensation, One-Time Payment, etc.).
* **Compensation Amount** (Number): The amount of compensation provided (if applicable).
* **Author Credit Required** (Select One): Whether the author credit is required (e.g., yes, byline required, yes, anonymous acceptable, etc.).
* **Explicitly Excluded Uses** (Text): Any uses that are not permitted.
* **Are Modifications to the Article Permitted?** (Select One): Whether modifications to the article are permitted (e.g., yes, with approval required, etc.).
* **I have the legal authority to grant these rights** (Select One): Confirm that you own or control the article rights.
* **I agree to the terms of this release** (Select One): Confirm that you agree to the terms of the release.
