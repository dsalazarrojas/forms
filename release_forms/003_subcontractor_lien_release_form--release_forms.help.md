# Subcontractor Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by a subcontractor to release a lien claim on a construction project. It informs relevant parties that the lien claim is being satisfied or released.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
2.  Review each field carefully to avoid errors or duplication.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or project manager for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lien Release Form** (id: 1, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should be left blank.
* **Contractor Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your name as the subcontractor.
* **Address** (id: 3, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (id: 5, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the state of the lien release.
* **Zip** (id: 6, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Completion** (id: 7, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Job Location** (id: 8, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job location.
* **Job Site Address** (id: 9, text, required): Enter the address of the job site.
* **Job Site City** (id: 10, text, required): Enter the city of the job site.
* **Job Site State** (id: 11, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the state of the job site.
* **Job Site Zip** (id: 12, text, required): Enter the zip code of the job site.
* **Lien Amount** (id: 13, number, required): Enter the amount of the lien.
* **General Contractor Name** (id: 14, text, required): Enter the name of the general contractor.
* **Phone** (id: 15, text, required): Enter the phone number of the general contractor.
* **Email** (id: 16, email, required): Enter the email address of the general contractor.
* **General Contractor Address** (id: 17, text, required): Enter the address of the general contractor.
* **Release** (id: 18, text, required): Enter the specific details of the lien release.
* **Signature** (id: 19, text, required): Provide a signature as verification of the release.
* **Date** (id: 20, date, required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Job Number** (id: 21, text, required): Enter the job number as reference.
* **Job Description** (id: 22, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Lien Type** (id: 23, text, required): Enter the type of lien release.
* **Job Site** (id: 24, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job site.

This guide is provided to assist in the accurate and thorough completion of the form to ensure a smooth and successful release of the lien claim.
