# yoga_consent_release_waiver_indemnity_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for students to provide their consent and release information for participating in yoga classes. It is used to collect demographic information, emergency contact details, medical history, insurance information, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your student information in the Student Information section.
2. Provide your medical history in the Medical History section, including any relevant medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in yoga.
3. In the Emergency Contact Information section, enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4. If you have medical insurance, select your insurance provider and enter your insurance ID number.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter any additional contact information, such as an email or address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in yoga.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select any relevant medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in yoga.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`): Select your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Information** (`insurance_provider_info`): Enter any additional insurance information.
* **Insurance ID Number** (`insurance_id_number`): Enter your insurance ID number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`): Sign the form to indicate your understanding and agreement.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`): Sign the form to indicate agreement with the student.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where you sign the form.
* **Class** (`class`): Enter the class you are participating in.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Student Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Instructor Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
