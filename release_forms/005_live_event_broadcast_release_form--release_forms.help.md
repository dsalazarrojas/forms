# Live Event Broadcast Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain release permission from participants before broadcasting their live event information on various channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, participants must first select the "Participant Information" page.
2. On this page, they can choose to provide any information they wish, as it is not mandatory.
3. The next page is where participants can grant broadcast permission.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if they grant permission for their live event to be broadcasted.
5. If they select "Yes", they will be able to choose which channels they want to grant permission for.
6. The "Release Date" and "Time" fields are for selecting the date and time of the live event.
7. Finally, they can submit their completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
	+ This is the first page where participants can provide any information they wish to share about themselves or their live event.
* **Do you grant permission for your participant to be broadcasted** (`broadcast_permission`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" to grant permission for your live event to be broadcasted, or "No" to decline.
* **Which channel(s) do you want to grant broadcast permission on** (`broadcast_channels`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ If you select "Yes" for broadcast permission, choose which channels you want to grant permission on (e.g. TV, Radio, Internet).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the live event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the live event.
* **Tips**
	+ Please note that this form is not mandatory, and participants can choose not to fill it out.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
