# Artifact Display Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artifact Display Release Form is used to request the release of an artifact for display in a specific venue or institution. This form gathers necessary information for the release process and ensures that the artifact is properly handled and displayed according to the owner's or lender's conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions below.
2. Provide the required information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artifact Title** (`Artifact Title`, text, required): Enter the title of the artifact being released.
* **Artifact Description** (`Artifact Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the artifact, including its type, material, and dimensions.
* **Artifact ID or Accession Number** (`Artifact ID or Accession Number`, text, optional): Enter the artifact's ID or accession number if available.
* **Owner or Lender Name** (`Owner or Lender Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the owner or lender of the artifact.
* **Owner Organization** (`Owner Organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization that owns or lends the artifact.
* **Owner Email Address** (`Owner Email Address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the owner or lender.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`Owner Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the owner or lender.
* **Display Venue or Institution** (`Display Venue or Institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue or institution where the artifact will be displayed.
* **Exhibit Name** (`Exhibit Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the exhibit or display.
* **Display Start Date** (`Display Start Date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the display.
* **Display End Date** (`Display End Date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the display.
* **Display Location Details** (`Display Location Details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the display location, such as the gallery or room number.
* **Insurance Coverage Required** (`Insurance Coverage Required`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' if insurance coverage is required for the artifact during display.
* **Handling Restrictions** (`Handling Restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any special care instructions for the artifact.
* **Photography and Documentation Permitted** (`Photography and Documentation Permitted`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' if photography and documentation are permitted for the artifact during display.
* **Artifact May Be Displayed in Public** (`Artifact May Be Displayed in Public`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' if the artifact may be displayed in public.
* **Date of Release** (`Date of Release`, date, required): Enter the date the artifact is released for display.
* **Primary Contact at Institution** (`Primary Contact at Institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact at the institution.
* **Additional Notes or Conditions** (`Additional Notes or Conditions`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or conditions for the artifact's display.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
