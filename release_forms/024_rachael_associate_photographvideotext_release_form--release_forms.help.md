# rachael_associate_photographvideotext_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a photograph, video, or text release, including details about the photographer, videographer, and client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the photographer's name in the "Photographer Name" field.
2. Enter the location where the photograph or video was taken in the "Photo Location" field.
3. Enter the photographer's address in the "Photographer Address" field.
4. Enter the photographer's city and state in the "Photographer City" and "Photographer State" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the photographer's phone number in the "Photographer Phone" field.
6. Enter a release statement in the "Photograph/Video/Text Release" field.
7. Enter the videographer's name in the "Videographer Name" field.
8. Enter the videographer's location in the "Videographer Location" field.
9. Enter the videographer's address in the "Videographer Address" field.
10. Enter the videographer's city and state in the "Videographer City" and "Videographer State" fields, respectively.
11. Enter the videographer's phone number in the "Videographer Phone" field.
12. Enter the date the photograph or video was taken in the "Date Taken" field.
13. Enter the time the photograph or video was taken in the "Time Taken" field.
14. Enter the release date in the "Release Date" field.
15. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
16. Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field.
17. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
18. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
19. Enter the client's city and state in the "Client City" and "Client State" fields, respectively.
20. Enter any other uses of the released material in the "Other Uses" field.
21. Enter any additional notes or comments about the release in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or company that took the photograph or video.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the photograph or video was taken.
* **Photographer Address** (`photographer_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the photographer.
* **Photographer City** (`photographer_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the photograph or video was taken.
* **Photographer State** (`photographer_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the photograph or video was taken.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the photographer.
* **Photograph/Video/Text Release** (`photographvideotext_release_text`, text, required): A statement releasing the rights of the photograph, video, or text.
* **Videographer Name** (`videographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or company that shot the video.
* **Videographer Location** (`videographer_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the video was shot.
* **Videographer Address** (`videographer_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the videographer.
* **Videographer City** (`videographer_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the video was shot.
* **Videographer State** (`videographer_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the video was shot.
* **Videographer Phone** (`videographer_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the videographer.
* **Date Taken** (`date_taken`, date, required): Enter the date the photograph or video was taken.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, time, required): Enter the time the photograph or video was taken.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date the release was made.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or company that commissioned the photograph or video.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Client City** (`client_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the client is located.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the client is located.
* **Other Uses** (`other_uses`, text, required): Enter any other uses of the released material.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or information about the release.
