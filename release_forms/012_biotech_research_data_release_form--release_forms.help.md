# Biotech Research Data Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotech Research Data Release Form is used by researchers requesting data from an internal data repository.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1: Data Release Requestor**
	* Please fill in your information as the person or entity requesting the data release.
	* **Data Release Requestor Name**: Enter your full name as the person or entity requesting the data release.
	* **Requestor Title**: Enter your professional title or position.
	* **Department or Lab**: Enter the name of your department or lab where the data was collected.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email for follow-up questions or communications.
	* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up questions or communications.
2. **Section 2: Data Information**
	* **Type of Data to be Released**: Select all types of data you are requesting (Raw Sequencing Data, Analysis Results, Patient Data, Genomic Data, Protein Data, Experimental Results, Clinical Trial Data, or Other).
	* **Description of Data Being Released**: Provide a detailed description of the dataset you are requesting.
	* **Volume of Data**: Describe the size or quantity of data you are requesting.
	* **Project ID or Identifier**: Enter any associated project number or identifier for the data.
3. **Section 3: Data Recipient**
	* **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the data.
	* **Recipient Title**: Enter the recipient's professional title or position.
	* **Recipient Organization**: Enter the name of the company or institution receiving the data.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the recipient.
4. **Section 4: Purpose and Use**
	* **Purpose for Data Release**: Select the reason for releasing the data (Continuing Research, Publication, Collaboration, Clinical Use, Regulatory Submission, Quality Control, or Other).
	* **Intended Use of Data**: Describe how you plan to use the data.
	* **Associated Research Protocol or Study**: If applicable, enter the name of the research protocol or study associated with the data.
5. **Section 5: Data Use Restrictions**
	* **Confidentiality Requirements**: Select whether the recipient needs to maintain confidentiality (Yes - Confidential, No - Public Use Allowed, or Limited Sharing Only).
	* **Publication Embargo Period**: Select how long before the data can be published (No Embargo - Can Publish Immediately, 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, or As Agreed).
	* **Secondary Analysis Allowed**: Select whether the recipient can use the data for other analyses (True, False, or With Permission Only).
	* **Data Can Be Shared with Third Parties**: Select whether the recipient can share the data with other researchers (True, False, or With Original Requestor Permission).
6. **Section 6: Compliance and Approvals**
	* **IRB Approval Status**: Select the IRB approval status (Approved, Pending, Not Required, or Exempt).
	* **HIPAA Compliance Required**: Select whether the recipient needs to comply with HIPAA (Yes - HIPAA Required, No - Not Required, or Unknown).
	* **Recipient Data Security Plan in Place**: Select whether the recipient has a data security plan (Yes - Confirmed, No - Not Provided, or Under Review).
7. **Section 7: Authorization and Signature**
	* **Principal Investigator Approval**: Enter the name of the principal investigator who approved this request.
	* **Date of Approval**: Enter the date of approval.
	* **Please review each section carefully and ensure accurate information is provided.**

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you need to make corrections, please contact the data repository administrator.
* For any questions or concerns, please contact the data release team.
