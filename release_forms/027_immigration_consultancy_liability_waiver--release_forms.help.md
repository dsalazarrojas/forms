# immigration consultancy liability waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a consultant's liability waiver for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of the consultant, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Fill in the details of the client, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. Fill in the signed date and time of the document, using the format mm/dd/yyyy and hh:mm for the time.
4. Sign the form with the consultant's and client's signatures.
5. If necessary, fill in the waiver terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consultant Details**: Enter the name of the consultant who is providing the services.
* **Client Details**: Enter the name of the client who is receiving the services.
* **Signed Date**: Enter the date on which the consultant and client signed this document, using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Signed Time**: Enter the time at which the consultant and client signed this document, using the format hh:mm.
* **Consultant Signature**: Enter the signature of the consultant who is providing the services.
* **Client Signature**: Enter the signature of the client who is receiving the services.
* **Waiver Terms**: If necessary, enter any additional terms or conditions that the consultant and client have agreed upon.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the consultant who is providing the services. 

Note: Please ensure that this form is used in a way that is consistent with its original purpose and intent. If you're unsure about the form's purpose or usage, please seek clarification before proceeding.
