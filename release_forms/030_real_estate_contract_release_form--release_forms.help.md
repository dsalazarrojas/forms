# Real Estate Contract Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a real estate contract, providing necessary information for the release process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in the following steps:
  1. Select the "Real Estate Agent" involved in the contract release.
  2. Provide the reason for the contract release.
  3. Specify the date and time of the contract release.
  4. Enter the location of the release.
  5. Input the contact number and email of the party to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Agent**: 
  * **Name:** real_estate_agent 
  * **Type:** Select multiple options 
  * **Purpose:** Select "Yes" or "No" indicating whether there is a real estate agent involved in the contract release. If "Yes" is selected, you will be asked to provide more information about the agent.
* **Reason for Release**: 
  * **Name:** reason_for_release 
  * **Type:** Text 
  * **Purpose:** Provide a clear reason for the release of the real estate contract.
* **Date of Release**: 
  * **Name:** date_of_release 
  * **Type:** Date 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the date of the contract release.
* **Release Date**: 
  * **Name:** release_date 
  * **Type:** Date 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the date of the contract release (Note: This field is likely a duplicate of "Date of Release")
* **Release Time**: 
  * **Name:** release_time 
  * **Type:** Time 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the time of the contract release.
* **Release Location**: 
  * **Name:** release_location 
  * **Type:** Text 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the location of the contract release.
* **Contact Number**: 
  * **Name:** contact_number 
  * **Type:** Text 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the contact number of the party to be contacted.
* **Contact Email**: 
  * **Name:** contact_email 
  * **Type:** Email 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the email of the party to be contacted.
* **Real Estate Contract Number**: 
  * **Name:** real_estate_contract_number 
  * **Type:** Text 
  * **Purpose:** Enter the number of the real estate contract.
