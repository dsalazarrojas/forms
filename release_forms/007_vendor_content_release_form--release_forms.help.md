# Vendor Content Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from external vendors about their content releases. The form helps track and manage content releases from external vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your vendor name in the **vendor_name** field.
2. Enter the title of the content you are releasing in the **content_title** field.
3. Select the type of content you are releasing (Type A, Type B, or Type C) in the **release_type** field.
4. Choose the location where the content will be used (Location A, Location B, or Location C) in the **content_location** field.
5. Enter the date when the content will start being released in the **start_date** field.
6. Enter the time when the content will start being released in the **start_time** field.
7. Enter the date when the content will end being released in the **end_date** field.
8. Enter the time when the content will end being released in the **end_time** field.
9. Enter your contact email address in the **contact_email** field.
10. Enter your contact phone number in the **contact_phone** field.
11. Add any additional notes you would like to provide to our team in the **vendor_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name (vendor_name)**: Enter your company name or the name of your business.
* **Content Title (content_title)**: Enter the title of the content you are releasing.
* **Release Type (release_type)**: Select the type of content you are releasing (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Content Location (content_location)**: Choose the location where the content will be used (Location A, Location B, or Location C).
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date when the content will start being released.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: Enter the time when the content will start being released.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date when the content will end being released.
* **End Time (end_time)**: Enter the time when the content will end being released.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Vendor Notes (vendor_notes)**: Add any additional notes you would like to provide to our team.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact our team for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation and timely submission of this form.
