# Performance Lighting Test Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the necessary consent and information for recording test lighting performance. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure the smooth running of the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the date of the test lighting performance from the calendar.
2. Enter your name as the participant in the test.
3. Choose the location where the test will be conducted (e.g., Studio A, Theater X, or Other).
4. If necessary, provide your contact information.
5. Select your consent to participate in the test lighting performance.
6. If you choose to participate, please sign and date the form below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Date**: Select the date of the test lighting performance.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant in the test.
* **Test Location**: Choose the location where the test will be conducted (e.g., Studio A, Theater X, or Other).
* **Contact Info**: If you need to provide your contact information for any reason, please enter it here.
* **Participant Consent**: Select whether you consent to participate in the test lighting performance.
	+ I consent to participate
	+ I do not consent to participate
* **Signature Date**: Select the date of your signature below, if you choose to participate.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name below, if you choose to participate.
* **Test Start Time**: Enter the start time of the test lighting performance.
* **Test End Time**: Enter the end time of the test lighting performance.

## Tips

* Be accurate when selecting the test date and times to ensure correct scheduling.
* Ensure your correct contact information is provided, if necessary.
* Clearly indicate your consent to participate in the test lighting performance.
* Sign and date the form if you choose to participate, and ensure your signature is legible.
* Enter the correct start and end times for the test lighting performance.
