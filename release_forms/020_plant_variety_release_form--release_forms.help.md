This form is designed to gather information for the release of a new plant variety. The purpose of this form is to collect data from users who want to release a plant variety, including its name, release date, location, description, breeder information, certification status, release reason, comments, and release requester information. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected in a structured format, making it easier to process and review the release request.

## Plant Variety Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plant Variety Release Form is a tool used to collect information for the release of a new plant variety. It is essential to have accurate and complete data to ensure that the release is processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Variety Name** field with the name of the plant variety you want to release.
2. Enter the **Release Date** when the variety will be released.
3. Provide the **Release Location** where the variety will be released.
4. Describe the **Variety Description** in a few sentences.
5. Add **Breeder Information**, including any relevant details about the breeder.
6. Select the **Certification** status of the variety (Certified, Not Certified, or Pending).
7. Enter the **Release Reason** for the variety.
8. Add any **Comments** about the release.
9. Provide **Release Requester Information**, including the name and contact details of the person requesting the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Release Form**: This field is the header for the form and is automatically filled in.
- **Variety Name**: Enter the name of the plant variety you want to release. This is an essential field and must be filled in.
  - Type: text
  - Required: yes
- **Release Date**: Enter the date when the variety will be released.
  - Type: date
  - Required: no
- **Release Location**: Enter the location where the variety will be released.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Variety Description**: Describe the variety in a few sentences.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Breeder Information**: Provide any relevant details about the breeder, including their name and contact information.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Certification**: Select the certification status of the variety (Certified, Not Certified, or Pending).
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: Certified, Not Certified, Pending
  - Required: no
- **Release Reason**: Enter the reason for releasing the variety.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the release.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Release Requester Information**: Provide the name and contact information of the person requesting the release.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, especially the Variety Name.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification.
- This form is for the release of new plant varieties only. Do not use it for other purposes.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
