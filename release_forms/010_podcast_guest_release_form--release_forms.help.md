# Podcast Guest Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and usage of the Podcast Guest Release Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the release date in the "Release Date" field.
5. Select the release time in the "Release Time" field.
6. Choose the content types that the guest is willing to have released (e.g. audio, video, written content, etc.) by selecting all applicable options in the "Content Permission" field.
7. If the guest has specified other content not listed in the "Other (Specify)" field.
8. Confirm that the form has been signed by selecting "True" in the "Release Form Signed" field.
9. You can add any notes or comments regarding the release in the "Notes" field.
10. Choose which tool is assigned to the guest from the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's name as it should be displayed on the release schedule.
- **Email**: Enter the guest's email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter the guest's phone number for contact purposes.
- **Release Date**: Select the date when the guest will be released from their commitments.
- **Release Time**: Select the time when the guest will be released from their commitments.
- **Content Permission**: Select all applicable content types that the guest is willing to have released. This can include:
  - All Content
  - Audio
  - Video
  - Written Content
  - Social Media Posts
  - Other (Specify below)
- **Other (Specify)**: If the guest has specified other content that is not listed, enter it here.
- **Release Form Signed**: Confirm that the guest has signed the release form.
  - Select "True" to confirm that the guest has signed the form.
- **Signature**: Note: This field is not editable by the end-user, it seems to be a note field for storing signature information.
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the release.
- **Assigned Tool**: Choose which tool is assigned to the guest from the available options.
- **Form ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the form.

Tips:

* Ensure that you have the guest's permission before filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the guest.
* Use the form in accordance with the organization's policies and procedures.
