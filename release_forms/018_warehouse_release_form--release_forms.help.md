# Warehouse Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Release Form is used to collect information from users for warehouse releases. This form is designed to gather necessary details from users for the release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input field with your relevant information.
2. Provide details about the goods in the Goods Details field.
3. Enter the release date in the Release Date field.
4. Specify the quantity of goods being released in the Quantity field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or any relevant information that is required for the release process.
* **Goods Details** (`goods_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the goods being released, such as product codes, descriptions, or any other relevant information.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the goods are to be released.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of goods being released. Please ensure this field is filled in with a numerical value.
