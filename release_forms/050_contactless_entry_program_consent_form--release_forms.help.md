# Contactless Entry Program Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contactless Entry Program Consent Form is used to collect participant information and consents for a contactless entry program, which allows individuals to enter a building without physical contact, reducing the risk of COVID-19 transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's details:
	* Enter your full name in the "Participant name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
2. Choose your consent choice:
	* Select "I consent" if you agree to participate in the contactless entry program.
	* Select "I do not consent" if you do not agree to participate.
3. Describe the data collection scope:
	* Enter the types of data that will be collected for access control.
4. Specify the data retention period:
	* Enter the duration for which data will be retained.
5. Optional:
	* If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
	* If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant name
• **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

### Organization
• **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing a company or building, enter its name.

### Email address
• **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for program updates and notifications.

### Phone number
• **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter an optional contact number for notifications.

### Consent choice
• **Consent choice** (`consent_choice`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" to participate, or "I do not consent" to decline participation.

### Data collection scope
• **Data collection scope** (`data_collection_scope`, text, required): Describe the types of data that will be collected for access control.

### Data retention period
• **Data retention period** (`retention_period`, text, required): Enter the duration for which data will be retained.

### Opt out instructions
• **Opt out instructions** (`opt_out_instructions`, note, optional): Describe how participants can withdraw their consent.

### Emergency contact
• **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of an optional emergency contact for program administrators.

### Additional comments
• **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other notes about your consent or participation.

Note: All fields with "optional" status can be left blank if not applicable.

Tips:
* Make sure to review the scope of data collection and retention before selecting "I consent" to participate.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to the program administrators.
* Remember to review the opt out instructions if you decide to withdraw your consent.
