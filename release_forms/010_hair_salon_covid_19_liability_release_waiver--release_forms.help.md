# Hair Salon Covid 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a waiver from customers that they understand and agree to the risks associated with receiving salon services during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form ensures that both the salon and customers are aware of their responsibilities and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your salon address**:
	* This is the address of the salon where you will be receiving services.
2. **Enter your customer address**:
	* This is the address where you can be contacted if needed.
3. **Select salon services**:
	* Choose the services you would like to receive, such as Haircut, Coloring, Chemical Treatments, or Styling.
4. **Describe your current health status**:
	* Select all the health statuses that apply to you, such as Healthy, Cough, Fever, or Recent travel.
5. **Customer Signature** (optional):
	* This is a note field for you to sign and date your name.
6. **Salon Signature** (optional):
	* This is a note field for the salon staff to sign and date their name.
7. **Date**:
	* Enter the date you visit the salon.
8. **Time**:
	* Enter the time you visit the salon.
9. **Email**:
	* Enter your contact email address.
10. **Phone Number**:
	* Enter your phone number.
11. **Customer Health Status Hint**:
	* This is a free-text field where you can describe your current health status in more detail.
12. **Salon Contact Info**:
	* Enter the contact information of the salon.
