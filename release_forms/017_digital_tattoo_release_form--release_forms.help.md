# Digital Tattoo Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Tattoo Release Form is a document used to formalize the release of a digital tattoo. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and successful tattoo removal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and sign all required fields.
3. Attach any supporting documents or additional information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide the client's current address.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the contact preferences for the client, such as "Yes" or "No" for contact by phone or email.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the digital tattoo was released.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the digital tattoo is expected to expire.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `text`, required): Include the name of the person signing the form, typically the client or their representative.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, `text`, required): Include the client's agreement statement, confirming they understand the terms of the release.
* **Release Agreement** (`release_agreement`, `text`, required): Include the release agreement statement, confirming the client's understanding of the release process.
* **Client Statement** (`client_statement`, `text`, required): Include any additional comments or statements from the client.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the client's contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Contact Cell** (`contact_cell`, `text`, optional): Provide the client's contact cell phone number, if applicable.
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, `text`, optional): Provide the client's contact fax number, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship between the client and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required): Sign the form with your client's signature, acknowledging the release.
* **Dated Signature** (`client_dated_signature`, `note`, required): Include the date signed.
* **Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, `text`, required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the emergency contact person's signature, acknowledging their support.
* **Emergency Contact Dated Signature** (`emergency_contact_dated_signature`, `note`, required): Include the date of the emergency contact person's signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form as a witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Dated Signature** (`witness_dated_signature`, `note`, required): Include the date of the witness's signature.
* **Witness Signature Date** (`witness_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the witness's signature.

By following this guide, you'll be able to accurately and efficiently complete the Digital Tattoo Release Form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
