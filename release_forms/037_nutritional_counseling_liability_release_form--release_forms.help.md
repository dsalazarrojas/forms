# Nutritional Counseling Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the consent and acknowledgment from clients before providing them with nutritional counseling services. It ensures that clients understand their limitations and the scope of the services they will receive.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Sign each section where required.
3. Date each section where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, text, optional): This is the client's acknowledgment of understanding the scope of services they will receive.
	+ Please write in your own words that you understand the scope of the services you will receive from the dietitian.
* **Counseling Limitations** (`counseling_limitations`, text, optional): This section is for you to acknowledge any limitations you have in your health history.
	+ Please list any medical conditions, medications, or other factors that may impact your ability to receive nutritional counseling services.
* **Client Acknowledgment** (`client_acknowledgment`, text, optional): This is your acknowledgment of understanding that nutritional counseling is not a substitute for medical treatment.
	+ Please acknowledge that you understand that nutritional counseling is not a replacement for medical treatment, and you will seek medical attention if needed.
* **Dietitian Acknowledgment** (`dietitian_acknowledgment`, text, optional): This section is for the dietitian to acknowledge that they will take your health information into consideration.
	+ Please ensure that the dietitian understands your health history and limitations.
* **Program Acknowledgment** (`program_acknowledgment`, text, optional): This is the program's acknowledgment that they will take into consideration your health information.
	+ Please ensure that the program understands your health history and limitations.
* **Client Consent Date** (`client_consent_date`, date, optional): This is the date you acknowledged understanding the scope of services.
	+ Please date and sign the acknowledgment.
* **Dietitian Signature** (`dietitian_signature`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether the dietitian has reviewed your health information and acknowledged their understanding.
* **Program Signature** (`program_signature`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether the program has reviewed your health information and acknowledged their understanding.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, select_one, optional): Please indicate whether you have reviewed the form carefully and acknowledged your understanding.
* **Release Form Signature** (`release_form_signature`, select_one, optional): Please indicate that you have reviewed the form and acknowledged that you are aware of your responsibilities.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill out each section thoroughly and accurately to ensure that you and the dietitian/program understand each other's expectations.
