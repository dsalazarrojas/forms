# Handyman Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of a handyman, including essential details such as name, customer contact information, and service details. It is intended to ensure that the release process is thorough and accurate, and it should only be used once per handyman and customer pair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Handyman Name field with the name of the handyman who performed the service.
2. Fill in the Customer Name field with the name of the customer who received the service.
3. Enter the Date of Service and Time of Service fields with the relevant dates and times.
4. Enter the Service Location field with the location where the service was performed.
5. Enter the Task Description field with a brief description of the service provided.
6. Enter the Injury During Service field with any injuries or issues that occurred during the service.
7. Enter the Release Date field with the date of release.
8. Enter the Signed By Handyman and Signed By Customer fields with the signatures of the handyman and customer, respectively.
9. Enter the Date field with the date the form was signed.
10. Enter the Reason for Release field with a brief reason for the release.
11. Enter the Handyman Address and Customer Address fields with the relevant addresses.
12. Enter the Contact Number field with a contact number for the customer.
13. Enter the Handyman Phone field with the handyman's phone number.
14. Enter the Email field with the handyman's email address.
15. Enter the Handyman License Number field with the handyman's license number.
16. Enter the Customer License Number field with the customer's license number.
17. Enter the Vehicle Number field with the vehicle number (if applicable).
18. Enter the Job Number field with the job number (if applicable).
19. Enter the Notes field with any additional comments or notes (optional).
20. Enter the Handyman Signature Date field with the date the form was signed by the handyman.
