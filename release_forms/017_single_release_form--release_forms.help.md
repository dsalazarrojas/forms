# Single Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Single Release Form is a tool used to collect information about a single release within a project. This form is intended to be completed by the project owner, providing essential details about the release, and ensuring that all necessary permissions and information are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contributor's permission information in the "Contributor Permission" field.
2. Sign and date the "Contributor Signature" field.
3. Enter the project owner's name in the "Project Owner Name" field.
4. Enter the project owner's email address in the "Project Owner Email" field.
5. Enter the project owner's phone number in the "Project Owner Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contributor Permission
* **Contributor Permission**: The field where you should enter the permission details of the contributor, such as any relevant permissions or approvals required for the release.

### Contributor Signature
* **Contributor Signature**: The field where the contributor should sign their name to confirm their permission and agreement.

### Project Owner Name
* **Project Owner Name**: The field where you should enter your name as the project owner, ensuring that you are the authorized individual responsible for the release.

### Project Owner Email
* **Project Owner Email**: The field where you should enter your email address as the project owner, allowing us to contact you for any further information or updates.

### Project Owner Phone
* **Project Owner Phone**: The field where you should enter your phone number as the project owner, providing a contact point for any urgent matters or concerns related to the release.
