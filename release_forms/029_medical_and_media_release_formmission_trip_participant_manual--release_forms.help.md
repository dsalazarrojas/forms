# Medical And Media Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical information and media release consent from patients. It is essential for healthcare providers to have accurate and up-to-date medical information to deliver the best possible care. By completing this form, you will be helping healthcare providers understand any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out any applicable medical information in the `Medical Information` field.
3. List any medical conditions in the `Medical Condition` field.
4. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name and relationship, in the `Emergency Contact` fields.
5. Specify the type of event this form is related to in the `On-Campus Events` or `Off-Campus Events` field.
6. Select whether you give your consent to share medical information in the `Consent` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Information**: A brief description of any pre-existing medical conditions that you may have.
* **Medical Condition**: A list of any medical conditions you wish to report.
* **Emergency Contact**: The name and relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: A description of your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Behavior Parameters**: A list of specific behaviors we should be aware of for this event.
* **Media Release**: The date you would like medical information to be released to media.
* **Media Release Explanation**: A note for the media release date.
* **Behavior Parameters Explanation**: A note for the behavior parameters list.
* **On-Campus Events**: Select 'Yes' if this form is related to an on-campus event.
* **Off-Campus Events**: Select 'Yes' if this form is related to an off-campus event.
* **Consent**: Select 'I give my consent' to allow healthcare providers to share your medical information.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure your medical information is up-to-date.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
- Ensure you understand the implications of sharing your medical information and media release consent.
