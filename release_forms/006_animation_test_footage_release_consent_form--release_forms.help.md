# Animation Test Footage Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Test Footage Release Consent Form is a one-page form used to collect consent and information from participants, contributors, or talent in animation projects. This form is used to ensure that all parties involved are aware of their rights and any terms of use for the project's footage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions on each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contributor full name** (`Contributor full name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name to be recorded in the release record.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, `email`, required): Provide the email address where release confirmations and further communication will be sent.
* **Role in footage** (`Role in footage`, `text`, optional): Specify the role you played in the project (e.g., animator, voice talent, actor).
* **Consent to use video and audio** (`Consent to use video and audio`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' to give consent for the project's footage to be used in marketing and portfolio purposes, or 'False' to decline.
* **Compensation terms** (`Compensation terms`, `text`, optional): If there was any agreed compensation, enter the details.
* **Date of recording** (`Date of recording`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the footage was created.
* **Typed name for signature** (`Typed name for signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name to indicate your agreement to the release terms.
* **Date signed** (`Date signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you agreed to the release terms.
* **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or restrictions on the use of the footage.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed the project's terms and conditions before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project lead or administrator.
* Once you have completed this form, you will receive a release confirmation to your contact email address.
