# photo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Form is a tool used to obtain consent from individuals to use their likeness or image in various media channels. It's designed to be filled out by the subjects of photo shoots, interviews, or other recordings, to grant permission for their photo or likeness to be used in publications, advertisements, or other promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and true information to the best of your knowledge.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask the person assisting you or the photographer for guidance.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Release Form**: This is the main section of the form. Please provide your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **FB Release**: Please indicate your consent to use your likeness or image in Facebook and other social media platforms.
* **Release Date**: If you're granting permission for the photo to be used on a specific date, please select that date from the calendar. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Release Time**: If you're granting permission for the photo to be used at a specific time, please select that time. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read each field before filling it out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* Remember to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.

Please note that this form is for your personal use and does not collect sensitive or protected information.
