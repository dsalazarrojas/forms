# Game Highlights Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Highlights Release Form is a tool used to collect and document participant permissions for sharing game-related content. It is essential for ensuring that participants' rights are respected and that their agreements are accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Ensure that you have the necessary permissions for the specified activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, `text`, optional): This field is for participants to confirm their consent for the specified activities regarding their game highlights.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Provide your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Media Distributor** (`Media Distributor`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the media outlet or entity you would like to share your game highlights with (Teams, leagues, schools, and event organizers, Event organizers, Leagues, Teams, Schools).
* **Participant Consent (Give)** (`participant_consent_give`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for others to give your game highlights.
* **Participant Consent (Publish)** (`participant_consent_publish`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for your game highlights to be published.
* **Participant Consent (Distribute)** (`participant_consent_distribute`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for others to distribute your game highlights.
* **Participant Consent (Share)** (`participant_consent_share`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for others to share your game highlights.
* **Participant Consent (Reprint)** (`participant_consent_reprint`, `text`, optional): Confirm your consent for others to reprint your game highlights.
* **Participant Consent (Other Rights)** (`participant_consent_other_rights`, `text`, optional): Specify other rights you would like to grant or deny.
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_consent_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Other (please specify)** (`participant_consent_other`, `text`, optional): Specify any other activities you are granting or denying permission for.

## Tips
Please review all fields carefully and ensure that you have the necessary permissions for the specified activities. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact <form@gic.mx> for further clarification.
