# Student Ministry Medical And Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Ministry Medical And Photo Release Form is used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for taking photos or handling medical situations involving a student. This form helps ensure that the student's medical and photo records are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your child's medical details in the **medical-details** field.
2. Enter your child's emergency contact information in the **emergency-contacts** field.
3. Specify your photo permissions in the **photo-permissions** field.
4. Enter your contact information in the **guardian-contact** field.
5. Enter your name in the **guardian-name** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **guardian-email** field.
7. Enter your child's emergency contact's name in the **emergency-contact-name** field.
8. Enter your child's emergency contact's email address in the **emergency-contact-email** field.
9. Enter your child's emergency contact phone number in the **emergency-phone-num** field.
10. Specify if you grant photo permission in the **photo-permission** field.
11. Enter the date when the photo was taken or can be taken in the **photo-release-date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **medical-details** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide medical information about your child.
* **emergency-contacts** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide contact information for your child's emergency contact.
* **photo-permissions** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to specify if you grant photo permission for your child.
* **guardian-contact** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your contact information.
* **guardian-name** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your name.
* **guardian-email** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your email address.
* **emergency-contact-name** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your child's emergency contact's name.
* **emergency-contact-email** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your child's emergency contact's email address.
* **emergency-phone-num** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to provide your child's emergency contact's phone number.
* **photo-permission** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to specify if you grant photo permission for your child.
* **photo-release-date** (required: false): 
	+ This field is used to enter the date when the photo was taken or can be taken.

## Tips

- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
- Make sure to provide up-to-date contact information.
- Check the photo release date to make sure it is accurate.
- Review this form before submitting.
