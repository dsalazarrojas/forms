# Tanning Salon Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tanning Salon Release Form is a template used for various purposes, including liability releases, client communication, and other general uses. It is designed to be filled out in sections, ensuring that each part is completed before moving on to the next.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections. Each section has its own specific fields that need to be filled out.
2.  Start by filling out the "Tanning Salon Information" section with the name of the tanning salon and other relevant details.
3.  Next, fill out the "Client Information" section with your name, phone number, and email address.
4.  In the "Tanning Session" section, enter the date and details of your tanning session.
5.  In the "Release" section, select the "Release of Liability" option to confirm you understand and accept the terms and conditions.
6.  Finally, review and fill out any additional sections as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tanning Salon Name**: The name of the tanning salon. Enter the name of the tanning salon you visited.
*   **Name**: Your full name.
*   **Phone**: Your phone number.
*   **Email**: Your email address.
*   **Date**: The date of your tanning session.
*   **Tanning Session Date**: The date of your tanning session.
*   **Tanning Session Details**: A brief description of your tanning session.
*   **Client Signature**: A space for you to sign your name, indicating you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
*   **Tanning Salon Liability Released**: Select "Release of Liability" to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
*   **Date Signed**: The date and time you signed the form.
*   **Tanning Salon Release Form Signed**: A field for the tanning salon to sign and date, indicating they have accepted the terms and conditions.
*   **Printed By**: A field for the person printing the form.
*   **Printed At**: The date and time the form was printed.
*   **Printed Count**: A count of the number of copies printed.
*   **Release Form ID**: A unique ID for the release form.
*   **Client Copy**: A copy of the release form for you to keep.
*   **Tanning Salon Copy**: A copy of the release form for the tanning salon.
*   **Signed**: A field indicating the form was signed.
*   **Printed**: A field indicating the form was printed.
*   **Release**: Select "Release of Liability" to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
*   **Notes**: Additional notes about your tanning session.
