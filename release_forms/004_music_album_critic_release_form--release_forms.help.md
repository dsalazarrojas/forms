# Music Album Critic Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Album Critic Release Form is used to share music album critic reviews with the public. It is a text-based form that collects information about a specific music album, including its title, artist, release date, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Music Album Critic Release Form** field with the title of the music album you want to review.
2. Enter the name of the **Artist** who created the music album.
3. Select the **Release Date** when the music album was released.
4. Choose a **Music Album Rating** out of 100.
5. Enter your **Reviewer** name or alias.
6. Write a brief **Review** of the music album.
7. Choose whether you grant **Publication Permission** to release the review publicly.
8. Enter your **Email** address.
9. Enter your **Phone** number (optional).
10. Add any additional **Note** about the review.
11. Enter the **Release Form ID** and **Release Form Version** (this will be provided by the system).
12. Date and time of when the form was **Created At** and **Updated At** (this will be populated automatically).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Music Album Critic Release Form** (id: 1): Enter the title of the music album you want to review.
- **Label** (id: 2): Enter a brief description or label for the music album review.
- **Artist** (id: 3): Enter the name of the artist who created the music album.
- **Release Date** (id: 4): Select the date when the music album was released.
- **Music Album Rating** (id: 5): Choose a rating out of 100 for the music album.
- **Reviewer** (id: 6): Enter your name or alias for the review.
- **Review** (id: 7): Write a brief review of the music album.
- **Publication Permission** (id: 8): Choose whether you grant permission to release the review publicly.
- **Email** (id: 9): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (id: 10): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Note** (id: 11): Add any additional note about the review.
- **Release Form ID** (id: 12): Enter the release form ID provided by the system.
- **Release Form Version** (id: 13): Enter the release form version provided by the system.
- **Created At** (id: 14): This field is populated automatically with the date and time the form was created.
- **Updated At** (id: 15): This field is populated automatically with the date and time the form was last updated.

Note: Please ensure that all mandatory fields are completed before submitting the form.
