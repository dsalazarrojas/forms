# Interview Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a liability release from participants in an interview or similar settings, ensuring they have a clear understanding of their rights and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Participant Info**: Enter your name, contact information, or other relevant details that will be used to contact you.
2.  **Participant Details**: Provide more in-depth information about yourself, such as your role or position in the organization you represent.
3.  **Release Form**: Add any additional information or context that may affect the release, such as specific dates or conditions.
4.  **Release Date and Time**: Record the exact date and time of the release. This will serve as a timestamp for the release agreement.
5.  **Participant Signature**: Sign your agreement to the release, confirming you understand your rights and obligations.
6.  **Release Version**: If the release agreement has been updated or revised, record the version number here.
