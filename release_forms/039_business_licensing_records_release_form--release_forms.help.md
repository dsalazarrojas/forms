# Business Licensing Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Licensing Records Release Form is designed to collect information from an individual or organization seeking access to business licensing records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your name as the requester.
3. Provide the name of the business associated with the records.
4. Specify the type of records you are requesting.
5. State the reason for requesting the records.
6. Identify the authorized recipient of the records.
7. Enter your email for coordination purposes.
8. Read and follow the signature instructions to complete the release process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (Required/Text): Enter your full name as the individual making the request.
* **Business name** (Optional/Text): Enter the name of the business whose records you are requesting.
* **Records type** (Required/Text): Specify the type of business licensing records you are seeking (e.g., permits, licenses, certificates).
* **Release purpose** (Required/Text): Briefly state the reason for requesting the records.
* **Authorized recipient** (Optional/Text): Identify the individual or entity authorized to receive the records.
* **Contact email** (Required/Email): Enter your email address for coordination purposes.
* **Signature instructions** (Optional/Text): Follow the instructions for providing an authorizing signature to release the records.
