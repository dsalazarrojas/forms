# Babysitting Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians and caregivers (babysitters) for babysitting arrangements. It ensures that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the care, including responsibilities and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Personal Information**: Fill in the required fields with the correct information about yourself and the children you are entrusting the caregiver with.
2. Enter details about the caregiver, including their name and phone number.
3. Specify the schedule and start date of care.
4. Check the boxes for your preferences on care arrangements, such as transportation, visitors, screens, and outings.
5. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your agreement and acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian One Name** (`parent_name_1`, text, required): Enter the legal name of the first parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (`parent_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the best contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the parent or guardian.
* **First Child Name** (`child_one_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the first child.
* **First Child Date of Birth** (`child_one_dob`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Second Child Name** (`child_two_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the second child, if applicable.
* **Second Child Date of Birth** (`child_two_dob`, date, optional): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format, if applicable.
* **Third Child Name** (`child_three_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the third child, if applicable.
* **Third Child Date of Birth** (`child_three_dob`, date, optional): Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format, if applicable.
* **Babysitter Name** (`babysitter_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the caregiver.
* **Babysitter Phone** (`babysitter_phone`, text, required): Enter the caregiver's contact number.
* **Regular Care Schedule** (`care_schedule`, text, required): Specify the days and times of care.
* **Care Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when care begins.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Check "Yes, I release" to release the babysitter from liability for ordinary negligence.
* **Emergency Medical Authorization** (`emergency_authorization`, select_one, required): Check "Yes, authorized" to authorize emergency medical treatment.
* **Transportation Permission** (`transportation_permission`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like the caregiver to transport children (with restrictions, no restrictions, or not permitted).
* **Visitor Permission** (`visitor_permission`, select_one, required): Check "True" if you're comfortable with visitors or guests.
* **Screen Time Permission** (`screen_time_rules`, select_one, required): Choose whether children can have screen time with or without limits.
* **Outing Permission** (`outing_permission`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like the caregiver to take children on outings (public places, with prior approval, or stay at home only).
* **Photo Permission** (`photo_permission`, select_one, required): Choose if you're okay with the caregiver taking and sharing photos of your child.
* **Termination Notice Period** (`termination_notice`, select_one, required): Choose how much notice you'd like to give the caregiver for ending the arrangement.
* **Date Signed** (`parent_signature_date`, date, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (`agreement_accepted`, select_one, required): Check "Yes, I accept" to confirm you've read and agree to the terms.
