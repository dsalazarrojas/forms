# Boudoir Model Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by photographers to obtain model releases from subjects in boudoir photography sessions. The form helps to establish clear communication and agreements between the photographer and the model regarding image usage, compensation, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please enter your legal first name in the "Model First Name" field.
2.  Next, enter your legal last name in the "Model Last Name" field.
3.  In the "Date of Birth" field, enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY. Please ensure you are 18 years of age or older.
4.  Confirm that you are 18 years of age or older by selecting the appropriate option in the "Age Verification" field.
5.  In the "Address" field, enter your full home address for contact and communication purposes.
6.  Enter your primary contact number in the "Phone Number" field.
7.  Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for communication regarding the images taken.
8.  Enter the full name of the photographer in the "Photographer Name" field.
9.  Enter the name of the photography business or studio in the "Photography Business Name" field.
10.  In the "Date of Photography Session" field, enter the date you had your session with the photographer.
11.  In the "Location of Photography Session" field, enter where the session took place.
12.  Provide a general description of the images created during the session in the "Image Description" field.
13.  Select how the images may be used by checking the corresponding options in the "Usage Rights Granted" field.
14.  If there are any restrictions on the use of the images, describe them in the "Usage Restrictions" field.
15.  Choose whether the images are exclusive to you or not in the "Exclusivity" field.
16.  Select the type of compensation provided to you for the session in the "Compensation" field.
17.  If you received monetary compensation, enter the amount in the "Compensation Amount" field.
18.  In the "Image Delivery" field, choose how you would like to receive your images.
19.  Confirm whether you approve of retouched images or if the decision lies with the photographer in the "Retouching Approval" field.
20.  Select if you grant permission to the photographer to license the images to third parties, with or without notification, in the "Third Party Usage" field.
21.  Sign your name in the "Model Signature" field, typing out your full legal name.
22.  Enter the date you sign this form in the "Signature Date" field.
23.  If applicable, provide the name of the witness in the "Witness Name" field.
24.  If a witness is present, have them sign in the "Witness Signature" field.
25.  Finally, sign your name as the photographer in the "Photographer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Model First Name**: Enter your legal first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Model Last Name**: Enter your legal last name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the date you were born in the MM/DD/YYYY format. Please ensure this date is correct to be 18 years or older.
*   **Age Verification**: Confirm you are 18 years of age or older.
*   **Address**: Enter your full home address for communication and contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Provide your primary contact phone number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter an email address for the photographer to contact you regarding your images.
*   **Photographer Name**: Enter the full name of the photographer who took your images.
*   **Photography Business Name**: Enter the name of the photography business or studio where your session took place.
*   **Date of Photography Session**: Enter the date of your photography session.
*   **Location of Photography Session**: Enter where the photography session took place.
*   **Image Description**: Describe the images taken during the session.
*   **Usage Rights Granted**: Indicate how the images may be used, such as in the photographer's portfolio, on their website, for social media, etc.
*   **Usage Restrictions**: List any conditions under which the images may not be used.
*   **Exclusivity**: Choose whether the images are exclusive to you or not.
*   **Compensation**: Select the type of compensation you received for the session.
*   **Compensation Amount**: If you received monetary compensation, enter the amount.
*   **Image Delivery**: Choose how you would like to receive your images.
*   **Retouching Approval**: Confirm whether you approve or disapprove of retouched images.
*   **Third Party Usage**: Choose whether you grant permission to the photographer to license images to third parties.
*   **Model Signature**: Sign your name as the model, typing out your full legal name.
*   **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
*   **Witness Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the witness who witnessed the signing.
*   **Witness Signature**: If applicable, have the witness sign this field.
*   **Photographer Signature**: Sign your name as the photographer.

Note: Please ensure to have a witness sign if required and to have the model and photographer sign the form. This form ensures a clear understanding and agreement between the parties involved.
