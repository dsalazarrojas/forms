# Precautionary Coronavirus Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide you with important information about the potential risks and liabilities associated with our services, particularly in relation to COVID-19. Please take a few minutes to carefully read and complete this form to ensure you understand your responsibilities and our precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Customer Information**, including your name and contact details, if you feel comfortable sharing them.
2. Enter your **Contact Details**, including any relevant contact information for yourself or your representative.
3. Review our **Service Terms**, which outline the potential risks and conditions associated with our services.
4. Acknowledge any **Precautionary Conditions** that apply to our services.
5. Indicate whether you've **signed a Waiver Agreement** (if required).
6. Declare any **Medical Conditions** that may impact your participation in our services.
7. **Sign** the form to indicate your understanding and agreement.
