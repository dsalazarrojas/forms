# Theme Park Ride Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests who have been selected for filming by a theme park ride. The form helps to ensure that the guest is aware of and consents to being filmed for the park's media and marketing activities. This form is usually filled out during the ride filming process, and it's essential to provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth and safe experience for both the guest and the film crew.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form to provide your information and consent to being filmed by the theme park ride filming team.
- Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately, as this information will be used for media and marketing purposes.
- If you have any additional comments or requests, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information**: This is the section where you can provide your personal details, such as your email address and phone number. This information will help the filming team to contact you if needed.
* **Guest Name**: This is where you can provide your name as it appears on your photo ID or driver's license. This will help the filming team to identify you in case of any issues.
* **Guest Email**: This is where you can provide your email address, which will be used for communication purposes only if needed.
* **Guest Phone**: This is where you can provide your phone number, which will be used for emergency contact purposes only.
* **Filming Date**: This is where you can select the date of the filming session, as specified by the theme park ride filming team.
* **Filming Time**: This is where you can select the time of the filming session, as specified by the theme park ride filming team.
* **Filming Location**: This is where you can specify the location where you will be filmed, as specified by the theme park ride filming team.
* **I Consent to being filmed**: This is a selection field where you can choose one of the four options:
	+ Option 1: Consent to be filmed
	+ Option 2: Refuse to be filmed
	+ Option 3: Consent to be filmed, but only if I'm wearing a mask
	+ Option 4: Refuse to be filmed, but only for a specific time period
* **Additional Comments**: This is where you can provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the filming team.
