# Urgent Care Work Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Urgent Care Work Release Form is used to document a patient's discharge from urgent care. This form captures essential information about the patient's details, release status, and witness information to ensure a smooth and safe discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select "True" or "False" for Hospital Release to indicate the patient's release status.
3. Enter the reason for the patient's release in the Reason for Release field.
4. Enter the date and time of the signature in the Signature Date and Time fields.
5. Sign the form with your official physician signature in the Physician Signature field.
6. Sign the form with the patient's signature in the Patient Signature field.
7. Sign the form with the witness's signature in the Witness Signature field.
8. Enter the witness's name and title in the Witness Name and Witness Title fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number as it appears on their identification or medical records.
* **Reason for Release**: Enter a brief explanation of why the patient is being released from urgent care.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date and time you sign this form.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign your official physician signature in this field. This is your acknowledgement of the patient's release.
* **Hospital Release**: Select "True" to indicate the patient's release from urgent care, or "False" if the patient is not being released.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the patient is released from urgent care.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that you, the patient, acknowledge your release from urgent care.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that you witnessed the patient's release.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the person who witnessed the patient's release.
* **Witness Title**: Enter the title of the person who witnessed the patient's release.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before signing the form.
* Make sure to date and time your signature in the Signature Date and Time fields.
* If the patient is being released, select "True" for Hospital Release.
* If the patient is not being released, select "False" for Hospital Release.
* Sign the form with your official signature and the patient's signature as required.
* Have the witness sign the form to confirm the release.
