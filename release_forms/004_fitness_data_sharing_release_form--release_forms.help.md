# Fitness Data Sharing Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Data Sharing Release Form is used to share individual's fitness data with a specific person or organization. This form ensures that the data shared is done so in a secure and informed manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understood the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the fitness data.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Explain the reason for sharing the fitness data.
* **Time Frame** (`time_frame`, select_one, optional):
	+ Immediate: Share the fitness data immediately.
	+ Ongoing: Share the fitness data on an ongoing basis.
	+ One-time: Share the fitness data only once.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a specific date for sharing the fitness data.
* **Fitness Data Sharing Agreement** (`fitness_data_sharing_agreement`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or details about the fitness data sharing agreement.
* **Recipient Details** (`recipient_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the recipient, such as their contact method and email or phone number.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Email: Share contact information via email.
	+ Phone: Share contact information via phone.
	+ None: Do not share contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional):
	+ Submit: Submit the form to share the fitness data.
	+ Cancel: Cancel the form and start again.
* **Release Agreement** (`release_agreement`, text, optional): Sign the release agreement by indicating that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Fitness Data Sharing Agreement Signed** (`fitness_data_sharing_agreement_signed`, select_one, optional):
	+ True: Sign the fitness data sharing agreement.
	+ False: Do not sign the fitness data sharing agreement.
