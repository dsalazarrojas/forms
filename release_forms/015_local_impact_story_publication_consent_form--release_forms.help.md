# local_impact_story_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from end-users about their local impact story publications. It is essential for tracking and monitoring the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have not filled out this form for the same publication previously.
2. Select your contact information from the list of options provided.
3. Enter the submission date of your publication.
4. Fill in the created by information, including your name and title.
5. If you are not the creator, fill in the updated by information, including your name and title.
6. Choose the publication details by selecting one or more options from the list.
7. Enter the publication date of your story.
8. Select the time at which your story was published.
9. Enter the name of the person who published your story.
10. If you have any additional information or notes, type them in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`form_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the contact information that best describes you or your organization.
* **Submission Date** (`form_2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your publication submission.
* **Created By** (`form_3`, `text`, required): Fill in your name and title as the creator of the publication.
* **Updated By** (`form_4`, `text`, optional): If you are not the creator, fill in your name and title as the updater.
* **Published** (`form_5`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more options that describe the publication details.
* **Publication Date** (`form_6`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your publication.
* **Published At** (`form_7`, `time`, required): Select the time at which your story was published.
* **Published By** (`form_8`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who published your story.
* **Notes** (`form_9`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your publication.
