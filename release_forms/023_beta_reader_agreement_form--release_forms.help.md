# Beta Reader Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Reader Agreement Form is designed to collect information from beta readers about their background, experience, and preferences in order to match them with manuscripts that suit their interests and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. We will use this address to contact you regarding the manuscript and any updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is optional, but it will help us contact you if needed.
4. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field. This is also optional, but it will help us know how to send you a physical copy of the manuscript, if needed.
5. Choose your level of beta reading experience in the "Beta Reading Experience" field.
6. Select your preferred genres from the list in the "Preferred Genres" field.
7. Enter the title of the manuscript you will be reading in the "Manuscript Title" field.
8. Enter the name of the author of the manuscript in the "Author Name" field.
9. Choose how you would like to receive the manuscript in the "Manuscript Format" field.
10. Choose when you expect to complete your review in the "Expected Completion Date" field.
11. Choose how you would like to provide your feedback in the "Preferred Feedback Format" field.
12. Select the areas of focus for your feedback in the "Areas of Focus" field.
13. Agree to keep the manuscript confidential in the "Confidentiality Agreement" field.
14. Agree not to share or distribute the manuscript in the "Non-Disclosure Terms" field.
15. Commit to the feedback deadline in the "Timeline Commitment" field.
16. Indicate whether you are expecting compensation for your work in the "Compensation Expectation" field.
17. Choose whether you would like to be acknowledged in the book in the "Acknowledgment Preference" field.
18. Enter any additional comments or questions you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.
19. Sign the form with your electronic signature in the "Electronic Signature" field.
20. Date the form in the "Date of Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your first and last name.
- **Email Address** (email, required): We will send manuscript and updates here.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Optional contact information.
- **Mailing Address** (text, optional): For potential physical copy delivery.
- **Beta Reading Experience** (select one, required): Choose your level of experience as a beta reader.
  - First Time Beta Reader
  - Some Experience
  - Experienced Beta Reader
  - Professional Beta Reader
- **Preferred Genres** (select multiple, required): Choose the genres you prefer to read.
  - Fiction
  - Mystery
  - Romance
  - Science Fiction
  - Fantasy
  - Thriller
  - Horror
  - Historical Fiction
  - Young Adult
  - Non-Fiction
  - Biography
  - Self-Help
- **Manuscript Title** (text, required): Title of the book you will be reading.
- **Author Name** (text, required): Name of the author.
- **Manuscript Format** (select one, required): Choose how you would like to receive the manuscript.
  - PDF
  - Word Document
  - Google Docs
  - ePub
  - Physical Copy
- **Expected Completion Date** (date, required): When do you expect to complete your review.
- **Preferred Feedback Format** (select one, required): Choose how you would like to provide feedback.
  - Written Report
  - Inline Comments
  - Video Call
  - Phone Call
  - Email Exchange
- **Areas of Focus** (select multiple, required): Choose the aspects you would like to focus on in your feedback.
  - Plot and Pacing
  - Character Development
  - Dialogue
  - Setting and World Building
  - Grammar and Spelling
  - Overall Readability
  - Emotional Impact
  - Consistency
  - Market Appeal
  - Other
- **Confidentiality Agreement** (select one, required): Agree to keep the manuscript confidential.
  - Yes, I Agree
  - No, I Do Not Agree
- **Non-Disclosure Terms** (select one, required): Agree not to share or distribute the manuscript.
  - Yes, I Agree
  - No, I Do Not Agree
- **Timeline Commitment** (select one, required): Commit to the feedback deadline.
  - Yes, I Can Commit
  - I Need More Time
  - Not Sure
- **Compensation Expectation** (select one, required): Indicate if you are expecting compensation for your work.
  - Volunteer, No Compensation
  - Open to Discussion
  - Yes, Paid Only
- **Acknowledgment Preference** (select one, optional): Choose whether you would like to be acknowledged in the book.
  - Yes, Please Acknowledge Me
  - No, I Prefer to Remain Anonymous
  - Not Sure
- **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any questions or information you would like to share.
- **Electronic Signature** (text, required): Type your full name as signature.
- **Date of Agreement** (date, required): Today date.
