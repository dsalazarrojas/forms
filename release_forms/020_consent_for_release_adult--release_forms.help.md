# Consent For Release Adult - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form serves as a consent mechanism for adults to give permission to organizations to access their personal data. This data may include academic records, medical history, employment information, or other relevant details. The form ensures that the individual is informed and willing to share their data with the authorized entity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   Provide your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2000).
*   Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
*   Identify the organization authorized to release your information (e.g., a healthcare provider, school, or employer).
*   Specify the person or organization to receive the information (e.g., The Youth Connection).
*   Select all the specific information you are willing to release (e.g., academic records, medical history, employment history, etc.).
*   Choose the purpose of this disclosure (e.g., enrollment in YIT services, employment screening, etc.).
*   If you want to set a specific expiration date for your authorization, enter it in the format month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2024). If left blank, this form will expire in 12 months from the date signed.
*   Confirm that you understand you have the right to revoke this consent at any time.
*   Sign your name electronically as it appears on your government-issued ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Legal Name**: This is the name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
    *   Type: Text, Required
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2000).
    *   Type: Date, Required
*   **Contact Phone Number**: This is your phone number where you can be reached.
    *   Type: Text, Required
*   **I authorize the following organization to release my records**: Identify the organization authorized to release your information.
    *   Type: Text, Required
*   **Person or Organization to receive the information**: Specify the person or organization to receive the information.
    *   Type: Text, Required
*   **Specific Information to be Released**: Select all the specific information you are willing to release (e.g., academic records, medical history, employment history, etc.).
    *   Type: Select Multiple, Required
*   **Purpose of this Disclosure**: Choose the purpose of this disclosure (e.g., enrollment in YIT services, employment screening, etc.).
    *   Type: Select One, Required
*   **This authorization will expire on**: If you want to set a specific expiration date, enter it in the format month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/2024). If left blank, this form will expire in 12 months from the date signed.
    *   Type: Date, Optional
*   **I understand I have the right to revoke this consent at any time**: Confirm that you understand your rights.
    *   Type: Select One, Required
*   **Applicant Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)**: Sign your name electronically as it appears on your government-issued ID.
    *   Type: Text, Required
*   **Date**: The date you sign this form.
    *   Type: Date, Required
*   **Witness Name (if applicable)**: If applicable, enter the name of a witness who can attest to your identity.
    *   Type: Text, Optional
