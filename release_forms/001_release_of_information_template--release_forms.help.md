# release_of_information_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "release_of_information_template" form is designed to collect basic contact and address information about non-profit organizations. It helps gather data on non-profits for public or internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the non-profit in the "NonProfit Name" field (field 2).
2. Type the address of the non-profit in the "Address" field (field 3).
3. Specify the city in which the non-profit is located in the "City" field (field 4).
4. Enter the state in which the non-profit is located in the "State" field (field 5).
5. (Optional) You can enter a title for the form in the "Release Of Information" field (field 1), but this is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Of Information (1)**: A general title for the form. Enter a brief title for the release of information if needed.
* **NonProfit Name (2)**: The name of the non-profit organization.
* **Address (3)**: The address of the non-profit organization's location.
* **City (4)**: The city where the non-profit organization is located.
* **State (5)**: The state where the non-profit organization is located.
* **Tips**: Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date. If the form is being used for official purposes, please verify the information with the non-profit before releasing it.
