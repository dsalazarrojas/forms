# Photo Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain photo releases from individuals or their representatives for the use of their photos. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure that the person releasing the photo has given their consent for its use and understands the terms of the release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **photo_release_consent** field with a brief description of the photo being released.
2. Select the **acquiring_party** field to indicate whether you are the individual in the photo or your representative.
3. Choose the **photo_owner** field to select the type of representative (e.g., Self or Representative).
4. In the **acquiring_party_purpose** field, enter the purpose for which you are acquiring the photo.
5. Enter the **acquiring_party_use** field to describe how you intend to use the photo.
6. Select the photo's intended use from the **photo_use** field (e.g., Social Media, Advertising, Print, Online, Video, or Other).
7. If applicable, enter the **date_of_release** field with the date the photo was taken or when the release is effective.
8. Enter the time of release in the **time_of_release** field, if applicable.
9. Sign and date the **signature_date** field to indicate when you have read and understood the terms of the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photo_release_consent_form** (Text Field): A brief description of the photo being released. 
* **acquiring_party** (Text Field): Indicate whether you are the individual in the photo or your representative. 
* **photo_owner** (Select One Field): Choose the type of representative (e.g., Self or Representative). 
* **photo_description** (Text Field): A description of the photo being released.
* **acquiring_party_purpose** (Text Field): Enter the purpose for which you are acquiring the photo.
* **acquiring_party_use** (Text Field): Describe how you intend to use the photo.
* **photo_use** (Select Multiple Field): Select all the ways you intend to use the photo (e.g., Social Media, Advertising, Print, Online, Video, or Other).
* **date_of_release** (Date Field): Enter the date the photo was taken or when the release is effective.
* **time_of_release** (Time Field): Enter the time of release, if applicable.
* **signature_date** (Date Field): Sign and date the release to indicate you have read and understood the terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate release.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to avoid any issues with the release.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
