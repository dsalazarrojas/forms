# Release And Waiver Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the start and end dates of services provided to an individual. It captures essential details about the user, contact information, service provider, service location, service type, start and end dates, and any additional service notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your User Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your Contact Information, so we can get in touch with you if needed.
3. Specify the Community Service Provider you are working with, if applicable.
4. Enter the Service Details, including any relevant notes or information.
5. Select the Service Type, which indicates whether or not the service is active.
6. Provide the Service Location, where the service was or will be provided.
7. Enter the Service Start Date and Service End Date, which are the dates the service started and ended.
8. Enter any Service Notes, which can be used to provide additional context or information about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required): This field captures your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This field is used to get in touch with you, so please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Community Service Provider** (`community_service_provider`, text, optional): If you are working with a specific service provider, please enter their name or details here.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional context about the service you are providing or receiving.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the service is active, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the service was or will be provided.
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the service started.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the service ended.
* **Release And Waiver Of Liability** (`release_and_waiver_of_liability`, text, optional): This field is used to document any release or waiver of liability related to the service.
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the service.
