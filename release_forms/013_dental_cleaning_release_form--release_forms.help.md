# dental_cleaning_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dental_cleaning_release_form" is a form designed to collect patient information and medical history for dental cleaning procedures. It is intended to ensure that patients are properly informed and consented before undergoing treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including name and contact details.
2. Fill in the medical history section with any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
3. Select the appropriate option for consent.
4. If necessary, provide additional information about the treatment plan.
5. Indicate any risk factors that may affect the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect general patient information.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's name.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is used to record any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **patient_contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the patient's contact details.
* **medical_provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the medical provider or doctor treating the patient.
* **dentist** (`dentist`, text, optional): This field is used to record the name of the dentist or treating professional.
* **date_and_time** (`date_and_time`, time, optional): This field is used to record the date and time of the dental cleaning procedure.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): This field is used to obtain the patient's consent for the procedure. The options are:
	+ Patient Information
	+ Medical History
	+ Treatment Plan
	+ Consent Form
* **treatment_plan** (`treatment_plan`, note, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the treatment plan.
* **risk_factor** (`risk_factor`, text, optional): This field is used to indicate any risk factors that may affect the treatment.
