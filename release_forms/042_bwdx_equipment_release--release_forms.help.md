# BWDX Equipment Release - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the release of BWDX equipment, ensuring the accurate tracking and management of company assets. This includes gathering information about the employee releasing the equipment, its condition and status, and the reasons for release. The form is used to record the release date, reason, and receiving party for auditing and inventory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Employee Information section with your personal details: provide your full name, employee ID, and department to which you belong.
2. In the Equipment Information section, select the type of equipment being released and describe it in detail.
3. Provide the current condition and operational status of the equipment.
4. Select the release date and reason for releasing the equipment.
5. Specify the receiving party and their signature or initials for confirmation.
6. Confirm if all items are included or missing.
7. If applicable, describe any missing items.
8. Provide any damage documentation or additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department from our company's list.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment from the provided options.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`): Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the equipment's serial number.
* **Asset Tag Number** (`asset_tag`): Enter the internal asset tag number.
* **Equipment Condition** (`condition`): Select the current condition of the equipment from the provided options.
* **Operational Status** (`operational_status`): Select the operational status of the equipment from the provided options.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): Enter the date when the equipment is being released.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`): Select the reason for releasing the equipment from the provided options.
* **Receiving Party** (`receiving_party`): Enter the name of the party receiving the equipment.
* **Receiver Signature or Initials** (`receiving_signature`): Provide their signature or initials for confirmation.
* **All Items Included?** (`items_included`): Confirm if all items are included.
* **Missing Items List** (`missing_items`): If applicable, describe the missing items.
* **Damage Documentation** (`damage_documentation`): Provide any photos or notes about damage.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any other relevant information.

## Tips
- Ensure the form is filled out accurately and completely.
- If the equipment is being released due to employee departure, make sure to update HR and IT accordingly.
- If the equipment requires special handling or disposal, note it in the release reason field.
- Keep a record of the release date for future reference.
- Verify the receiving party's signature or initials before completing the form.
