# IBSL Release Of Information_Brannon - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IBSL Release Of Information_Brannon form is used to capture relevant information from patients to be released to external organizations, such as insurance companies, healthcare providers, or other third parties. This form ensures that sensitive patient information is properly documented and released in accordance with regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Release Of Information** title to provide a brief description of the form and the purpose of the release.
2. Enter the **patients_name** of the individual about whom information is being released.
3. Provide the **date_of_birth**, which is the date of birth for the patient.
4. Enter the **social_security_number**, which is the patient's social security number (or other identifying number, as applicable).
5. Enter the **contact_phone**, which is the phone number of the individual or organization to contact for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Release Of Information** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form and the purpose of the release.
- **patients_name** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient about whom information is being released.
- **date_of_birth** (3, date, optional): Enter the date of birth for the patient.
- **social_security_number** (4, number, optional): Enter the patient's social security number (or other identifying number, as applicable).
- **contact_phone** (5, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or organization to contact for further information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure proper completion of the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant healthcare provider or organization for clarification.
- This form is not intended to be used for emergency situations. If you are requesting release of information in an emergency, please contact your healthcare provider immediately.
