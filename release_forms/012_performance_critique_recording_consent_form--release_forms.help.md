# Performance Critique Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the consent of prophets for performance critiquing activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Prophets name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Recording Consent" field, indicating your agreement to participate in performance critiquing activities.
5. Optional: Enter the date, time, and duration of the recording.
6. Provide the location where the recording will take place (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophets name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as a prophet, as it will appear in the recording consent log.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you with further instructions or updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can send you notifications or reminders.
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate your consent to participate in performance critiquing activities, or "No" if you do not wish to participate.
* **Date** (`recording_consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the recording (if you have selected "Yes" in the previous field).
* **Time** (`recording_consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the recording (if you have selected "Yes" in the previous field).
* **Duration** (`recording_consent_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the recording (if you have selected "Yes" in the previous field).
* **Location** (`recording_consent_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the recording will take place (if you have selected "Yes" in the previous field).

## Tips
* Please note that the recording consent is required to participate in performance critiquing activities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address or phone number.
