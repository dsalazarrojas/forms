# Donor Event Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Event Release Form is used to collect and release event details from donors. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured consistently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Details section with all relevant information about the event.
2. Provide the Donor Information, including their name, contact details, and consent status.
3. Enter the Release Date and Event Date of the event.
4. Add the Email, Phone, and Organization of the donor.
5. Select the Donor Consent status.
6. Complete the Event Organizer section with their name, phone, and email.
7. Enter the Form Submitter's name, phone, and email.
8. Provide additional Event Details as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter the name and description of the event.
* **Donor Information**: This section captures the details of the donor, including their name, contact information, and consent status.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the event was released.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Email**: Enter the donor's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization related to the event.
* **Donor Consent**: Indicate whether the donor has given their consent for their event to be released.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Form Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter Address**: Enter the address of the form submitter.
* **Event Details 1-7**: Enter any additional details about the event as required.
* **Form Submitter Phone, Email, and Address are not currently required fields and should only be filled in by the person submitting the form.**
