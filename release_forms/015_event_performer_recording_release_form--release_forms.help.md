# Event Performer Recording Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain the consent of event performers for their recording in various formats (video, live streaming, still photography, etc.). It's essential to complete this form carefully to ensure the performer's rights and consent are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the required fields, such as event name, performer name, event date, event location, and contact information for the event organizer and performer.
3.  Choose the types of recording you agree to in the "Consent" section, and make sure to check all boxes for the types of recording you consent to.
4.  Add any additional comments or notes, if necessary, in the "Additional Comments" field.
5.  Finally, place your signature on the "Signature" field to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (1): Enter the name of the event where the recording will take place.
* **Performer Name** (3): Provide your name as the event performer.
* **Event Date** (4): Input the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (5): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (6): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Organizer Name** (7): Enter your name if you are an event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Contact Email** (8): Enter your contact email for the event organizer.
* **Performer Contact Email** (9): Enter your email address for contact purposes as the performer.
* **Performer Contact Phone** (10): Enter your phone number for contact purposes as the performer.
* **Consent** (11): Choose the types of recording you agree to by checking the corresponding boxes. This includes video recording, live streaming, still photography, and more.
* **Additional Comments** (12): If you have any comments or additional information, enter it here.
* **Signature** (13): Sign and date this form to indicate your consent to the recording.

**Tips:**
- Be sure to review each field carefully before completing the form.
- Make sure to check all applicable boxes in the "Consent" section for accurate representation of your consent.
- Sign and date the "Signature" field to indicate your consent.
