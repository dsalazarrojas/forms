# employment_verification_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment verification release form is used to gather employment-related information from individuals. This form helps to document their job history, including job title, dates of employment, and reason for leaving.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you understand each field.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Fill in the required information, such as former employer's name and address, dates of employment, and reason for leaving.
4. If you're unsure about any field, ask for assistance.
5. Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
