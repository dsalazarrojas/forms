# Co Living Safety Awareness Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from residents to ensure their safety and compliance with the community's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Resident Name (Full Name)**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
4. **Unit or Room Number**: Enter your unit or room identifier.
5. **Move-In Date**: Enter the date you moved into the community or the date you plan to move in.
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
7. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
8. **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Spouse or Partner, Parent, Sibling, Child, Friend, or Other).
9. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that staff should be aware of, please list them here.
10. **Mobility Assistance**: Select whether you require mobility assistance due to any medical condition.
11. **I Have Read Safety Guidelines**: Select "Yes I Have Read" if you have read and understood the safety guidelines.
12. **Fire Safety Procedures Acknowledged**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the fire evacuation procedures.
13. **Do You Know Emergency Exit Locations**: Select whether you can locate all emergency exits.
14. **Do You Know Fire Extinguisher Locations**: Select whether you can locate fire extinguishers.
15. **Do You Know First Aid Kit Location**: Select whether you know where first aid kits are.
16. **Security Features Acknowledged**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the security systems in place.
17. **Guest Policy Acknowledged**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the guest policy.
18. **Noise Policy Acknowledged**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the noise policy.
19. **Common Area Rules Acknowledged**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the shared space etiquette.
20. **Incident Reporting Procedures Known**: Select "Yes I Know How" if you are aware of the incident reporting procedures.
21. **Willing to Use Safety Equipment**: Select "Yes I Agree" if you are willing to use the provided safety equipment.
22. **Participation in Safety Drills**: Select "Yes I Will" if you are willing to participate in scheduled safety drills.
23. **Consent to Contact Emergency Contact**: Select "Yes I Consent" if you agree to allow staff to contact your emergency contact if needed.
24. **Liability Terms Understood**: Select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the liability limitations.
25. **Date of Acknowledgment**: This field will be automatically filled out with the current date.
