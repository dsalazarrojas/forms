# Book Excerpt Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to use a book excerpt. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address.
2. Fill out the book information section with the full title of the book, the book's author, and the chapter or section you would like to use.
3. Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., magazine, online publication, educational institution).
4. Specify how you plan to use the excerpt and where it will be published.
5. Select the intended scope of distribution.
6. Indicate whether you request exclusive rights or not.
7. Confirm if compensation is offered.
8. If applicable, provide a compensation amount.
9. Suggest a credit line for the book.
10. Grant release permission for the excerpt.
11. Agree to the terms and conditions.
12. Add any additional notes or conditions.
13. Sign and date the form as the authorizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Full Name**: Your full name as the person requesting the excerpt.
* **Requestor Email**: Your email address associated with your name.
* **Requestor Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization Name**: Name of the organization or publication you represent.
* **Organization Type**: Type of organization you represent (e.g., magazine, online publication, educational institution).
* **Book Title**: Full title of the book you are requesting the excerpt from.
* **Book Author**: Author of the book.
* **Publisher**: Name of the publisher of the book.
* **Publication Year**: Year the book was published.
* **ISBN**: ISBN number if available.
* **Chapter or Section**: Chapter or section of the book you would like to use.
* **Length of Excerpt**: Approximate word count of the excerpt you are requesting.
* **Intended Use of Excerpt**: How you plan to use the excerpt (e.g., print publication, online article, marketing material).
* **Publication Name**: Where the excerpt will be published.
* **Expected Publication Date**: When the excerpt will be published.
* **Distribution Scope**: How widely the excerpt will be distributed (e.g., local, regional, national, international, online global).
* **Exclusive Rights**: Select "Yes Exclusive" if you need exclusive rights for your use.
* **Is Compensation Offered**: Check "True" if you are offering compensation for the excerpt rights.
* **Compensation Amount**: If applicable, provide the amount offered for the excerpt rights.
* **Proposed Credit Line**: Suggest a credit line for the book.
* **Release Permission**: Grant permission to use the excerpt.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Select "Yes I agree" to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Notes or Conditions**: Add any special conditions or notes related to your request.
* **Authorizer Full Name**: Full name of the person authorizing the release.
* **Authorizer Signature**: Sign your full name as the authorizer.
* **Authorization Date**: Date of authorization.
