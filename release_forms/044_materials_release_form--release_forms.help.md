# Materials Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Materials Release Form is used to document the release of materials in a structured and concise way for easy reference. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to avoid confusion and duplication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form after a meeting or discussion with a relevant team member to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Only fill out the form for materials that are not already released or scheduled to be released in the near future.
3. Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the material being released.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the material will be released. This should be a specific date, not a time range.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for the release.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select multiple, optional): Select all relevant material types (e.g., "Hardware," "Software," "Document," etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the release.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for further questions or follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter an email address for further communication.
* **Release Date Time** (`release_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the specific time when the material will be released. This field is optional and should only be filled out if the release time is important.
