# Tech User Testimonial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tech User Testimonial Release Form" is a document that collects feedback and testimonials from technical users about our products or services. This form is designed to gather information about their experience and opinions, which will be used to improve our technical services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "User Details" field with your name and contact information.
2. Type your testimonial in the "Testimonial" field. This is a required field, and it's essential to provide as much detail as possible about your experience with our services.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Publish Permission" to indicate whether you give us permission to use your testimonial for marketing purposes.
4. Choose the medium where you'd like your testimonial to be published (Website, Blog, Newsletter, Social Media, or Email Marketing).
5. If you want to add more details to your testimonial, you can do so in the "Testimonial Content" field.
6. Finally, confirm your "Agreement" by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you've read and agreed to the terms of use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you'll provide your name and contact information. Please fill out this field accurately to ensure that we can contact you if we need to discuss your testimonial further.

### Testimonial
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, `note`, required: true): This field is where you'll write your testimonial about our technical services. Try to be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.

### Publish Permission
* **Publish Permission** (`publish_permission`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Here, you'll select "Yes" if you give us permission to use your testimonial for marketing purposes. If you select "No", we will not use your testimonial for promotional purposes.

### Publish Medium
* **Publish Medium** (`publish_medium`, `select_one`, required: true): In this field, you'll choose where you'd like your testimonial to be published. Select all that apply from the options provided.

### Testimonial Content
* **Testimonial Content** (`testimonial_content`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide more details to your testimonial. If you have additional information to share, feel free to add it here.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Here, you'll confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms of use by selecting "Yes" or "No".
