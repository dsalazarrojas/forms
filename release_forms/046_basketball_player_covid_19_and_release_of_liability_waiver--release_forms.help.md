# Basketball Player COVID 19 And Release Of Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inform players and parents/guardians about the potential risks of COVID-19 transmission in basketball activities and to obtain their informed consent and acknowledgment of those risks. By filling out this form, players and parents/guardians confirm that they understand and accept the risks associated with participating in these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and read each section carefully.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely in the required fields (Player name, Date of birth, Email address, Phone number).
3. If you are under 18, enter your parent/guardian's information in the corresponding fields.
4. Answer the COVID-19 health screening questions honestly, as they may impact your participation in basketball activities.
5. Check the boxes to acknowledge the risks and release the organization from liability.
6. Confirm that you have read and understood the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player name** (`player_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-08-15).
* **Email address** (`player_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number** (`player_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Parent/Guardian name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent/guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent/Guardian phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Do you have any COVID-19 symptoms?** (`covid_symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Answer honestly about whether you have any COVID-19 symptoms such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Have you been exposed to COVID-19?** (`covid_exposure`, `select_one`, required): Answer honestly about whether you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
* **Are you currently in quarantine?** (`quarantine_status`, `select_one`, required): Answer honestly about whether you are currently in quarantine.
* **Vaccination status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, optional): Answer honestly about your vaccination status.
* **I understand the risks of COVID-19 transmission** (`covid_risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to acknowledge that you understand the risks of COVID-19 transmission.
* **I confirm I am in good health** (`health_condition_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to confirm that you are in good health to participate.
* **I release the organization from liability for COVID-19 related injuries** (`release_liability`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to release the organization from liability for COVID-19 related injuries.
* **I assume all risks of COVID-19 transmission** (`assumption_of_risk`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to assume all risks of COVID-19 transmission.
* **I release the organization from general liability claims** (`general_liability_release`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to release the organization from general liability claims.
* **Parent/Guardian consent** (`parent_consent`, `select_one`, optional): If you are under 18, check this box to confirm that your parent/guardian consents to your participation.
* **I confirm I have read and understood this waiver** (`form_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Check this box to confirm that you have read and understood this waiver.
