# Privacy Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Privacy Release Form is used to collect and authorize the release of personal information from employees. This form is an essential document for HR-related activities, such as background checks, employee records management, and data authorization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information for your specific case.
3. Double-check the form for any missing or incorrect fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, Number, Optional): Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and phone number of a person authorized to contact on your behalf in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your current address.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your current or past employer's name.
* **Release Authorization** (`release_authorization`, Text, Optional): I, _______________, authorize the release of my personal information.
* **Authorized Person/Department** (`authorized_person`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or department authorizing the release of your information.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the release.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason for the release of your information.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submission.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your HR representative or contact your supervisor for assistance.
