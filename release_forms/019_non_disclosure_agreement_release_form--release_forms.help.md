# Non Disclosure Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain a release from a non-disclosure agreement (NDA). It gathers information and confirms the release, ensuring that the individual is no longer bound by the agreement's terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your full first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your full last name.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth if required.
4. **Address**: Enter your address if required.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
7. **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number, but be aware that this field requires verification.
8. **Date of Death**: Not required.
9. **Place of Death**: Not required.
10. **Reason for Death**: Not required.
11. **Non-Disclosure Agreement**: Describe your Non-Disclosure Agreement.
12. **Release Date**: Enter the date of your release from the non-disclosure agreement.
13. **Release Time**: Not required.
14. **Signature**: Select 'True' if you are certain about releasing your non-disclosure agreement.
15. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
16. **Signature Time**: Enter the time of your signature.
17. **Non-Disclosure Agreement Date**: Not required.
18. **Non-Disclosure Agreement Time**: Not required.
19. **Reason for Release**: Not required.
20. **Release Agreement**: Select the relevant option if you wish to release your agreement.
21. **Non-Disclosure Agreement Release**: Select the relevant option if you wish to release your agreement.
22. **Reason for Non-Disclosure**: Not required.
23. **Agreement Date**: Not required.
24. **Agreement Time**: Not required.
25. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments.
