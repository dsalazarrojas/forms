# Third Party Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third Party Release Form is a document that grants permission for a client or third party to access certain information. It ensures that the user has understood and agreed to the terms of the release, including that they have received the form from their client and understand the scope of the information being released.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions of the release.
2. Complete the fields as follows:
	* **User Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
	* **Third Party Access**: Select the level of access you are granting to the third party (Client Name, Client Contact Number, or Client Date of Birth).
	* **Authorized Third Party**: Enter the number of authorized third parties.
	* **Release Form Signature**: Select the option that indicates you understand and agree to the terms of the release.
	* **Date**: Enter the date you are signing the form.
	* **Time**: Enter the time you are signing the form.
	* **Client Name**: Enter the name of your client (if not already selected in the Third Party Access field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to be associated with this release form.
* **Third Party Access (Third Party Access)**: Select the level of access you are granting to the third party. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Client Name: This grants access to the client's name.
	+ Client Contact Number: This grants access to the client's contact number.
	+ Client Date of Birth: This grants access to the client's date of birth.
* **Authorized Third Party (Authorized Third Party)**: Enter the number of authorized third parties.
* **Release Form Signature (Release Form Signature)**: Select the option that indicates you understand and agree to the terms of the release. You must select one of the provided options.
* **Date (Release Form Date)**: Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Time (Release Form Time)**: Enter the time you are signing the form.
* **Client Name (Release Form Client Name)**: Enter the name of your client (if not already selected in the Third Party Access field).

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure you understand the scope of information being released and the terms of the release before signing.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with your supervisor or the relevant department.
* Keep a record of the completed form for future reference.
