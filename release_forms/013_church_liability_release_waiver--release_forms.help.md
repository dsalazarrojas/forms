# Church Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Liability Release Waiver form is used to gather information about participants in various church activities, events, and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
3. Answer truthfully and accurately all questions.
4. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, Text, Required): Provide your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, Text, Required): Enter your full street address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Event or Activity** (`event_activity`, Select, Required): Select the specific activity you are participating in, such as Youth Retreat or Mission Trip.
* **Event Date(s)** (`event_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name and contact information of the person you want to be informed in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Select, Required): Choose how you're related to the emergency contact, such as Spouse or Sibling.
* **Do You Have Any Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Select, Required): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that the church should be aware of. 
* **Please Describe Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_detail`, Text, Optional): Provide further details about your medical conditions, such as allergies or medications.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Assumption of Risk Acknowledgment** (`assumption_of_risk`, Select, Required): Acknowledge understanding of risks involved in the activity.
* **Release of Liability Agreement** (`release_of_liability`, Select, Required): Agree to release the church from liability.
* **Consent for Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_consent`, Select, Required): Provide consent for medical treatment in case of emergencies.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`, Select, Required): Grant permission for photos or videos to be taken.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, Select, Required): Agree to follow the church's code of conduct.
* **Transportation Consent** (`transportation_consent`, Select, Required): Provide consent for church transportation or choose to arrange your own transportation.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (if minor)** (`guardian_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of a parent or guardian if you're a minor.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature (if minor)** (`guardian_signature`, Text, Optional): Type the name as a signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, Text, Required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, Date, Required): Date and time of your signature.
