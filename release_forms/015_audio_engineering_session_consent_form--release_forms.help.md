# Audio Engineering Session Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by participants to provide their consent and details for an audio engineering session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number as required by the "Participant Full Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is required for age verification.
3. Select "Yes, I am 18 or older" or "No, I am a minor" in the "Age Verification" field. If you are a minor, you must provide your parent or guardian's name and email address in the "Parent or Guardian Name" and "Parent or Guardian Email" fields.
4. Enter the name of the studio or facility where the session will take place in the "Studio Name" field.
5. Choose the date of the recording session in the "Session Date" field.
6. Select the type of recording session (e.g., music, voice over, podcast) in the "Session Type" field.
7. Enter the name or title of the project in the "Project Name" field.
8. Choose the intended use of the recording in the "Recording Purpose" field.
9. Select whether you consent to being recorded during the session in the "Recording Consent" field.
10. Choose how the recording may be used (e.g., commercial, streaming, social media) in the "Usage Rights" field.
11. Select the duration of usage (e.g., 1 year, 3 years, perpetual) in the "Usage Duration" field.
12. If applicable, select whether you have been compensated for the session in the "Compensation Agreement" field.
13. Select whether you release the studio from liability related to the session in the "Liability Release" field.
14. Select whether you understand and agree to the equipment damage policy in the "Equipment Damage Policy" field.
15. Select whether you agree to keep session content confidential if requested in the "Confidentiality Agreement" field.
16. Type your full name as electronic signature in the "Participant Signature" field.
17. Enter the date of consent in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (Text, Required): Your legal full name as a session participant.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Required for age verification.
* **Age Verification** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes, I am 18 or older" or "No, I am a minor".
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Text, Optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (Email, Optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Studio Name** (Text, Required): The name of the recording studio or facility.
* **Session Date** (Date, Required): The date of the recording session.
* **Session Type** (Select One, Required): The type of recording session (e.g., music, voice over, podcast).
* **Project Name** (Text, Required): The name or title of the project.
* **Recording Purpose** (Select One, Required): The intended use of the recording.
* **Recording Consent** (Select One, Required): Select whether you consent to being recorded during the session.
* **Usage Rights** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose how the recording may be used (e.g., commercial, streaming, social media).
* **Usage Duration** (Select One, Required): The duration of usage (e.g., 1 year, 3 years, perpetual).
* **Compensation Agreement** (Select One, Required): If applicable, select whether you have been compensated for the session.
* **Liability Release** (Select One, Required): Select whether you release the studio from liability related to the session.
* **Equipment Damage Policy** (Select One, Required): Select whether you understand and agree to the equipment damage policy.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Select One, Required): Select whether you agree to keep session content confidential if requested.
* **Participant Signature** (Text, Required): Type your full name as electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Required): The date of consent.
