# ten_syx_med_spa_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document patient information for medical release processes. It helps healthcare professionals gather essential details about patients, including their identity, medical history, and visit dates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**. Ensure you enter your correct name, date of birth, and visit information.
2. **Provide detailed medical history**, including any allergies and current medications.
3. **Specify your medical condition** if it's not already listed.
4. **Sign the form** to acknowledge your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit to the medical institution.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of your visit, if applicable.
* **Date of Release**: Enter the date you are scheduled to be released from the medical institution.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of your actual release from the medical institution.
* **Reason for Release**: Briefly explain the reason for your release.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history, including allergies and current medications.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, if applicable.
* **Current Meds**: List any current medications you are taking, if applicable.
* **Medical Condition**: Specify your medical condition, if not already listed.
* **Medical Condition Other**: Provide any additional information about your medical condition, if not already listed.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your participation and accuracy of the information provided.

Note: This form is not intended for patients to complete without medical staff guidance. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with medical staff for clarification.
