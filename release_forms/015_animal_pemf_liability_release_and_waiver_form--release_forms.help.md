# Animal PEMF Liability Release And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure that the animal's owner acknowledges and accepts the risks associated with PEMF therapy and releases the practitioner from any liability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully before signing.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Sign the form to acknowledge and accept the risks associated with PEMF therapy.
4. Have a witness (optional) sign the form as a witness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Legal Name** (`owner_legal_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as the owner of the animal.
* **Animal Information - Name and Species** (`animal_info_details`, text, required): Enter the name and species of the animal undergoing PEMF therapy.
* **Description of PEMF Service** (`pemf_service_desc`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the PEMF therapy services you are seeking.
* **Acknowledgment of Risks** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "I understand and accept the risks" or "Other" if you wish to provide additional information.
* **Release of Practitioner from Liability** (`practitioner_release`, select_one, required): Select "I agree to release all liability" from the practitioner.
* **Indemnification Clause** (`indemnification_clause`, select_one, required): Select "I agree to indemnify" and hold harmless the service provider.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Date of Signature** (`owner_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing the form.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If a witness is present, enter their name.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the practitioner providing PEMF therapy.

**Tips**

* Ensure you have read and understand the terms and conditions before signing.
* Sign the form in the presence of a witness, if possible.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
