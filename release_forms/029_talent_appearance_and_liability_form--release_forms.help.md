# Talent Appearance And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Appearance And Liability Form is a comprehensive form used to collect information about an individual's talent, including their appearance details, contact information, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Form Title" field with a brief and clear title of the form.
2. Provide a description of the form in the "Description" field.
3. Select the category of the event or form from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the type of talent from the "Talent Type" dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional event details in the "Event Details" text area.
6. Enter your appearance details in the "Appearance Details" text field.
7. Select the location of your appearance from the "Appearance Location" dropdown menu.
8. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
9. Enter the time of day in the "Time of Day" field in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
10. Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
11. Confirm your consent to the terms of service and agreement terms.
12. Upload a talent image (optional).
13. Sign the form with a digital signature.
14. Choose the type of agreement from the "Agreement Type" dropdown menu.
15. Add any additional event details in the "Event Details 1-6" text areas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): A brief and clear title of the form.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): A description of the form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): The category of the event or form.
* **Talent Type** (`talent_type`, select_one, required/optional): The type of talent.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required/optional): Additional event details.
* **Appearance Details** (`appearance_details`, text, required/optional): Information about your appearance.
* **Appearance Location** (`appearance_location`, select_one, required/optional): The location of your appearance.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required/optional): The time of day in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Email address.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Confirmation of terms of service and agreement terms.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, text, required/optional): Terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, text, required/optional): Privacy policy.
* **Talent Image** (`talent_image`, text, required/optional): A talent image.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): A digital signature.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required/optional): Agreement terms.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required/optional): The type of agreement.
* **Event Details 1-6** (`event_details_1-6`, note, required/optional): Additional event details.

# Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the category and talent type to ensure it is correct.
* Use proper formats for date and time fields.
* Be cautious with the upload of talent image.
* Sign the form digitally to confirm your commitment.
