# Relaxation Package Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relaxation Package Release Form is used to release a relaxation package, which is a collection of tasks or resources used to engage with customers or users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the required information for each field, and review your answers for accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details** (1, text, \*)
	+ Please enter the name of the client associated with this relaxation package.
### Email
* **Email** (2, email, \*)
	+ Enter the email address of the client or user associated with this relaxation package.
### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (3, select_one, \*)
	+ Select the contact method used to reach the client or user, such as phone, email, or in-person.
### Phone
* **Phone** (4, text, \*)
	+ Enter the phone number of the client or user associated with this relaxation package.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (5, date, \*)
	+ Enter the start date of the relaxation package, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### End Date
* **End Date** (6, date, \*)
	+ Enter the end date of the relaxation package, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Package Details
* **Package Details** (7, text, \*)
	+ Describe the relaxation package, including any specific tasks or resources involved.
### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (8, email, \*)
	+ Enter your email address, as this form will be submitted on your behalf.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy, especially the dates and contact information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or the package owner for clarification.
