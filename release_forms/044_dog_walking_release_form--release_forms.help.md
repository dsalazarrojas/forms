# Dog Walking Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their pet walking experience to ensure a smooth and safe experience for their pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form, answering the questions in order.
2. For each question, provide the necessary information as requested.
3. Be as accurate and detailed as possible in your responses.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Client Information
* **Client Information**: This is the first page of the form. It contains fields to collect your contact and identification details. Please fill in your name, address, and any other relevant information as requested.

### Page 2: Service Details
* **Service Details**: This page collects information about the dog walking service. Please describe the services you are requesting, including the date, time, and duration of the walk.

### Page 3: Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: In the event of an emergency, please provide the contact information of a person we can reach out to. This could be a family member, friend, or another trusted contact.

### Page 4: Payment Information
* **Payment Information**: This page is for payment details. Please provide the payment method and any other relevant financial information as requested.
