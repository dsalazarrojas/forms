# press_release_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Press Release Communication Form is designed to gather information about a press release, its details, and the distribution channels for the press release. This form helps us to communicate effectively with our press contacts and media partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Press Release Details section (id: 1) with a brief description of the press release.
2. Enter the Contact Information (id: 2) if you want to share your contact details with the press team.
3. Select the Release Date (id: 23) and Press Release Date (id: 24) for the press release.
4. Choose the distribution channels (id: 6, 7, 8, 18, 25) where you would like to have your press release distributed.
5. Set your contact preferences (id: 9) for the press team.
6. Fill in the Contact Person (id: 10), Company Name (id: 11), and other details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Press Release Details** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the press release.
* **Contact Information** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter your contact information if you want to share it with the press team.
* **Release Date** (id: 3, date, required: false): Select the release date for the press release.
* **Time of Release** (id: 4, time, required: false): Select the time of release for the press release.
* **News Story** (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter the news story related to the press release.
* **Images/Videos** (id: 6, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you want to include images or videos with the press release.
* **Press Coverage** (id: 7, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you want press coverage for the press release.
* **Distribution Channels** (id: 8, select_multiple, required: false): Select the distribution channels for the press release.
* **Contact Preferences** (id: 9, select_multiple, required: false): Set your contact preferences for the press team.
* **Contact Person** (id: 10, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Company Name** (id: 11, text, required: false): Enter the company name.
* **Company Email** (id: 12, email, required: false): Enter the company email.
* **Phone Number** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter the company phone number.
* **Company Address** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter the company address.
* **Contact Title** (id: 15, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's title.
* **Contact Department** (id: 16, text, required: false): Enter the contact department.
* **Contact Extension** (id: 17, text, required: false): Enter the contact extension.
* **Press Releases** (id: 18, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you want press releases for the press release.
* **Press Release Type** (id: 19, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of press release.
* **Press Release Status** (id: 20, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the press release.
* **Press Release Number** (id: 21, number, required: false): Enter the press release number.
* **Deadline** (id: 22, date, required: false): Enter the deadline for the press release.
* **Priority** (id: 23, number, required: false): Set the priority for the press release.
* **Press Release Date** (id: 24, date, required: false): Enter the press release date.
* **Distribution Channels 2** (id: 25, select_multiple, required: false): Select additional distribution channels for the press release.

Note: Required fields are marked as "required: false" to allow end-users to understand that these fields are not mandatory but are part of the form.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely for accurate communication.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Select the relevant options for distribution channels and contact preferences.
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
