# Media Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Participation Waiver Form is designed for participants to provide their consent and information required for media participation in events or productions. This form ensures that participants, their parents or guardians (if applicable), and other relevant parties are aware of and agree to the terms of media use, sharing, and consent for different types of media (photo, video, audio). It helps to maintain transparency and ensures that the participants' rights and interests are respected and protected throughout the media process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your Participant Name.
2.  Select your Parent/Guardian Permission (if applicable).
3.  Enter your Participant Age.
4.  Enter your Birthdate.
5.  Provide your Email address.
6.  Enter your Phone number.
7.  Specify your consent for Photo, Video, and Audio.
8.  Choose how you wish to share your information (Yes or No).
9.  Specify the type of media you wish to participate in (Photo, Video, or Audio).
10.  Specify the participant's school (if applicable).
11.  Specify the participant's class and grade (if applicable).
12.  Specify the participant's teacher (if applicable).
13.  Specify the media production team (if applicable).
14.  Enter the event date (if applicable).
15.  Enter the event time (if applicable).
16.  Specify the assigned user (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required): Please enter your name as it should be recorded for media purposes.
- **Parent/Guardian Permission** (`parent_permission`, required): Select whether you have a Parent/Guardian's permission for media participation. Choose "Parent/Guardian Permission" if you are under 18.
- **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, required): Enter your age for verification and media participation purposes.
- **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, required): Enter your birthdate to validate your age.
- **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address where media participants' information and updates will be sent.
- **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number where media participants' information and updates will be sent.
- **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, required): Specify whether you give consent for media participants' photos to be taken and shared. Choose 'Yes' to allow photo consent.
- **Video Consent** (`video_consent`, required): Specify whether you give consent for media participants' videos to be recorded and shared. Choose 'Yes' to allow video consent.
- **Audio Consent** (`audio_consent`, required): Specify whether you give consent for media participants' audio recordings to be shared. Choose 'Yes' to allow audio consent.
- **Media Types** (`media_types`, required): Specify the type(s) of media you wish to participate in by choosing one or more of the options: Photo, Video, or Audio.
- **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, required): Choose how you wish to share your information: 'Yes' for sharing or 'No' for not sharing.
- **Consent to Share (Email)** (`consent_to_share_email`, required): Specify whether you agree to share your email address with others. Choose 'Yes' for sharing or 'No' for not sharing.
- **Consent to Share (Video)** (`consent_to_share_video`, required): Specify whether you agree for sharing your video recordings with others. Choose 'Yes' for sharing or 'No' for not sharing.
- **Consent to Share (Audio)** (`consent_to_share_audio`, required): Specify whether you agree for sharing your audio recordings with others. Choose 'Yes' for sharing or 'No' for not sharing.
- **Media Participant** (`media_participant`, required): Please provide more specific information about the participant, such as their description.
- **Participant School** (`participant_school`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the school where you attend.
- **Participant Class** (`participant_class`, optional): If applicable, enter your class name.
- **Participant Grade** (`participant_grade`, optional): If applicable, enter your grade level.
- **Participant Teacher** (`participant_teacher`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your teacher.
- **Media Production Team** (`media_production_team`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the media production team.
- **Production Company** (`production_company`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the production company.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, optional): If applicable, enter the time of the event.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the assigned user.

Note: All fields marked as 'required' are necessary to be filled out to complete the form. Optional fields are for additional information that may be helpful in specific cases but are not mandatory.
