# Equipment Request And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and release equipment within facilities. It captures relevant information about the equipment, the requestor, and the facility manager involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number to identify yourself as the requestor.
2. Provide the date and time of the request.
3. Enter the name of the facility where the equipment is located.
4. Describe the equipment you are requesting or releasing, including any specific details about the device.
5. Enter any additional information about the device, such as its details.
6. Enter the facility manager's name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name (Requestor Name)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the requestor of the equipment.
* **Request Date (Request Date)**: Enter the date you are requesting or releasing the equipment.
* **Request Time (Request Time)**: Enter the time you are requesting or releasing the equipment.
* **Facility Name (Facility Name)**: Enter the name of the facility where the equipment is located.
* **Equipment Details (Equipment Details)**: Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting or releasing.
* **Device Details (Device Details)**: Enter any additional information about the device, such as its specifications.
* **Requestor Email (Requestor Email)**: Enter your email address, which will help the facility manager contact you if necessary.
* **Requestor Phone (Requestor Phone)**: Enter your phone number, which will help the facility manager contact you if needed.
* **Requestor Department (Requestor Department)**: Enter your department or section within the facility, if applicable.
* **Release Date (Release Date)**: Enter the date you are releasing the equipment.
* **Release Time (Release Time)**: Enter the time you are releasing the equipment.
* **Facility Manager Name (Facility Manager Name)**: Enter the name of the facility manager who is responsible for the equipment.
* **Facility Manager Phone (Facility Manager Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the facility manager, which will help you contact them if needed.
* **Facility Manager Email (Facility Manager Email)**: Enter the email address of the facility manager, which will help them contact you if necessary.
