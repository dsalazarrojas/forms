# News Interview TV Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The News Interview TV Release Form is used to gather essential information for TV releases. It captures participant consent, contact information, and release details to ensure a smooth and efficient release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant consent section to ensure the participant is aware of and agrees to the release terms.
2. Enter the participant's contact information in the contact info section.
3. Enter the date and time of the interview, location, and other relevant details in the interview section.
4. Choose the release status and reason for the release in the release status section.
5. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, note, required: false): This field captures the participant's consent to the release terms. Please have the participant review and sign this section to ensure their agreement to the release terms.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's name in this field.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's contact information in this field.
* **Date of Record Interview** (`date_of_recorded_interview`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Time of Record Interview** (`time_of_recorded_interview`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Location of Interview** (`location_of_recorded_interview`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the interview.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the release status from the options provided.
	+ Released
	+ Not Released
	+ Released with restrictions
	+ Not Released (pending review)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments about the participant.
* **Team Notes** (`team_notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments from the team.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required: false): Enter the release date.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, note, required: false): Add any production notes or comments.
* **Release Status Date** (`release_status_date`, date, required: false): Enter the release status date.
* **Release Status Reason** (`release_status_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the release status.
* **Shoot Name** (`shoot_name`, text, required: false): Enter the shoot name.
* **Shoot Number** (`shoot_number`, number, required: false): Enter the shoot number.
* **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, date, required: false): Enter the shoot date.
* **Shoot Time** (`shoot_time`, time, required: false): Enter the shoot time.
* **Shoot Location** (`shoot_location`, text, required: false): Enter the shoot location.
* **Participant Release Agreement** (`participant_release_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Choose the participant's release agreement from the options provided.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email.
* **Shoot Director** (`shoot_director`, text, required: false): Enter the shoot director's name.

Please note that some fields are not required, and users can choose to fill them out as needed. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately for efficient processing.
