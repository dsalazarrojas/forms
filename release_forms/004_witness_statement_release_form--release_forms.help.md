# Witness Statement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Witness Statement Release Form is designed to collect information from witnesses or individuals who have witnessed an event, incident, or accident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the witness's statement in the "witness_statement_form" field.
2. Provide more details about the statement in the "witness_statement_details" field.
3. Enter the date of the event in the "witness_statement_date" field.
4. Enter the time of the event in the "witness_statement_time" field.
5. Specify the location of the event in the "witness_statement_location" field.
6. Describe the event in the "witness_statement_description" field.
7. If applicable, enter the date of the event in the "witness_statement_event_date" field.
8. If applicable, enter the time of the event in the "witness_statement_event_time" field.
9. Specify the location of the event in the "witness_statement_event_location" field.
10. Describe the event in the "witness_statement_event_description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Witness Statement Form** (`witness_statement_form`): Enter a short statement describing what you witnessed.
* **Witness Statement Details** (`witness_statement_details`): Provide more details about your witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Date** (`witness_statement_date`): Enter the date of the event you witnessed.
* **Witness Statement Time** (`witness_statement_time`): Enter the time of the event you witnessed.
* **Witness Statement Location** (`witness_statement_location`): Enter the location where the event occurred.
* **Witness Statement Description** (`witness_statement_description`): Describe what happened during the event.
* **Event Date** (`witness_statement_event_date`): Enter the date of the event that was witnessed. (If applicable)
* **Event Time** (`witness_statement_event_time`): Enter the time of the event that was witnessed. (If applicable)
* **Event Location** (`witness_statement_event_location`): Enter the location where the event occurred. (If applicable)
* **Event Description** (`witness_statement_event_description`): Describe what happened during the event. (If applicable)

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the witness's account is recorded accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
