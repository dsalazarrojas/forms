# Sample Podcast Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the release status and release date of a podcast.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the guest's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the podcast's title, date, and time.
3. Choose the release status of the podcast (Released or Not Released).
4. If necessary, enter the release date for the podcast.
5. Click submit to save the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (`sample_podcast_release_form`, text, optional): This field is for collecting general information about the guest.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the guest's name.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): This field is for entering the guest's email address.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering the guest's phone number.
* **Podcast Title** (`podcast_title`, text, optional): This field is for entering the podcast's title.
* **Podcast Date** (`podcast_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the podcast's date.
* **Podcast Time** (`podcast_time`, time, optional): This field is for entering the podcast's time.
* **Podcast Length** (`podcast_length`, number, optional): This field is for entering the podcast's length.
* **Podcast Description** (`podcast_description`, text, optional): This field is for entering a description of the podcast.
* **Podcast Audio** (`podcast_audio`, text, optional): This field is for collecting any audio files related to the podcast.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`, select_one, required): This field is for choosing the release status of the podcast (Released or Not Released).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the release date of the podcast.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the guest's information and podcast details for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant team for clarification.
