# Podcast Listener Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect listener's personal details and liability information for the podcast. It's essential to have this information for maintaining records and ensuring listeners are aware of their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Listener Name.
2. Enter your Email Address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Contact Number for easy contact.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have given consent.
5. Optionally, enter the Date Signed if you're completing a physical copy of the form.
6. You do not need to fill in Form ID or Timestamp as these fields are not required.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, use the 'Additional Information' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Listener Name** (`listener_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate you have given consent, or 'No' to indicate otherwise.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): If you're completing a physical copy of the form, enter the date you signed.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field is not required and should not be completed.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, time, optional): This field is also not required and should not be completed.
* **Additional Information** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional details or comments you have, if necessary.
