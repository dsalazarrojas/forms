# GSWO Health Information And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The GSWO Health Information And Release Form is a vital document for healthcare providers to gather critical health information and obtain signatures from relevant parties. Completing this form ensures that healthcare professionals have the necessary information to provide quality care and treatment for the individual.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
4. Obtain signatures from all relevant parties, including parents, guardians, or adults.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent Name** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian completing this form.
- **Guardian Contact Info** (2, text, optional): Enter contact information for the guardian or responsible adult.
- **Health Information** (3, text, optional): Provide any relevant health information that may be useful for the healthcare provider.
- **Medical Conditions** (4, text, optional): List any known medical conditions affecting the individual.
- **Medications** (5, text, optional): List any medications the individual is currently taking.
- **Allergies** (6, text, optional): List any known allergies the individual has.
- **Medical Procedure** (7, text, optional): Explain any specific medical procedures or treatments the individual is currently undergoing.
- **Emergency Contacts** (8, text, optional): Provide contact information for emergency contacts.
- **Parent Signature** (9, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the parent or guardian has read and understood the form. Select 'Yes' if they have, otherwise select 'No'.
- **Guardian Signature** (10, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the guardian or responsible adult has read and understood the form. Select 'Yes' if they have, otherwise select 'No'.
- **Adult Signature** (11, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the adult or responsible individual has read and understood the form. Select 'Yes' if they have, otherwise select 'No'.

## Tips

- Double-check each field for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to the healthcare provider for guidance.
