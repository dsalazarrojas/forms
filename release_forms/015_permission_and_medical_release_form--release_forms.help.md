# Permission And Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purpose of this form is to obtain permission from parents or guardians to participate in medical, dental, or general activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the first section with your child's information:
	* **Release Form First Name**: Enter your child's first name.
	* **Release Form Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
	* **Release Form Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth.
	* **Release Form Date of Permission**: Enter the date of permission (if applicable).
2. Select the correct permissions for your child:
	* **Release Form Permission Type**: Choose the type of permission you are granting (General, Dental, or General and Dental).
	* **Release Form Medical Group**: Choose the type of medical group your child is associated with (None, Primary Care Provider, or Specialist).
3. Provide your child's medical group information:
	* **Release Form Medical Group Name**: Enter the name of your child's medical group.
	* **Release Form Medical Group Phone**: Enter the phone number of your child's medical group (if applicable).
	* **Release Form Medical Group Address**: Enter the address of your child's medical group (if applicable).
	* **Release Form Medical Group City**: Enter the city of your child's medical group (if applicable).
	* **Release Form Medical Group State**: Enter the state of your child's medical group (if applicable).
	* **Release Form Medical Group Zip**: Enter the zip code of your child's medical group (if applicable).
	* **Release Form Medical Group Email**: Enter the email of your child's medical group (if applicable).
4. Answer questions about your child's abilities:
	* **Release Form Authorized to Drive**: Check if your child is authorized to drive.
	* **Release Form Authorized to Participate**: Check if your child is authorized to participate in activities.
	* **Release Form Authorized to Take Pills**: Check if your child is authorized to take medication.
	* **Release Form Authorized to Drive License**: Check if your child has a valid driver's license.
5. Sign and date the form:
	* **Release Form Parent Signature**: Sign your name as the parent or guardian.
	* **Release Form Parent Date**: Date and sign the form.
