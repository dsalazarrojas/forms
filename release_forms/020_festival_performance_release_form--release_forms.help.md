# Festival Performance Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Performance Release Form is used to collect information from performers for festival performances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact the festival team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Information** (`festival_performance_release_form_1`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the performer and their performance details. 
* **Performer Data 2** (`performer_data_2`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the performer has performed previously at the festival. 
* **Performer Data 3** (`performer_data_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating the type of performance (e.g., solo, group, etc.). 
* **Release Permission 1** (`release_permission_1`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the performer has granted release permission for their performance. 
* **Release Permission 2** (`release_permission_2`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the performer has granted release permission for their performance. 
* **Release Permission 3** (`release_permission_3`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the performer has granted release permission for their performance. 
* **Release Media 1** (`release_media_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the media release is applicable. 
* **Release Media 2** (`release_media_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the media release is applicable. 
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, `date`, optional): The date of the performance. 
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, `time`, optional): The time of the performance. 
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, `number`, optional): The duration of the performance. 
* **Recording Format 1** (`recording_format_1`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording format is applicable. 
* **Recording Format 2** (`recording_format_2`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording format is applicable. 
* **Recording Format 3** (`recording_format_3`, `select_one`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording format is applicable. 
* **Recording Device 1** (`recording_device_1`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording device is applicable. 
* **Recording Device 2** (`recording_device_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording device is applicable. 
* **Recording Device 3** (`recording_device_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): A selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating whether the recording device is applicable. 
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`, `note`, optional): Additional notes about the release. 
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the submitter. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the submitter. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Additional comments about the submission. 
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Additional comments about the submission. 
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, optional): The name of the submitter. 
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the submitter. 
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the submitter. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the festival team for clarification.
- This form should not be duplicated or submitted on behalf of another person without their knowledge and consent.
