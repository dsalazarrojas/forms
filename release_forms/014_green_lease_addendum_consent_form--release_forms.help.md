# Green Lease Addendum Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Lease Addendum Consent Form is a critical document that ensures the accuracy of information collected between landlords and tenants. This form is essential for maintaining the integrity and trust between the two parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form in order from 1 to 25.
2. Start with section 1: Tenant Consent. Enter the tenant's name and any additional comments or information required.
3. Proceed to section 2: Landlord Consent. Enter the landlord's name and sign their name to confirm.
4. If applicable, fill out sections 3 and 4 for sustainability commitments and contact information.
5. Complete sections 5 to 9 for tenant contact information and landlord signature and note.
6. Enter the date signed and time signed in sections 10 and 11.
7. Finally, provide the completed date in section 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Consent (#1)**: 
  (Text, required, true): Enter your name to confirm your consent for the green lease addendum.
* **Landlord Consent (#2)**: 
  (Text, required, true): Enter your name and sign here to confirm your consent for the green lease addendum.
* **Sustainability Commitments (#3)**: 
  (Select multiple, false): Select 'Yes' if you consent to the green lease addendum's sustainability commitments.
* **Landlord Contact (#4)**: 
  (Select one, true): Select 'Yes' if you have the landlord's contact information.
* **Tenant Contact (#5)**: 
  (Select one, true): Select 'Yes' if you have the tenant's contact information.
* **Lease Terms (#6)**: 
  (Time, false): This field is not applicable.
* **Sustainability Commitments Description (#7)**: 
  (Text, false): Enter a description of the sustainability commitments.
* **Tenant Signature (#8)**: 
  (Date, true): Date and sign here to confirm your consent.
* **Landlord Signature (#9)**: 
  (Date, true): Date and sign here to confirm your consent.
* **Tenant Signature Note (#10)**:

* **Landlord Signature Note (#11)**:
