# Sale Proceeds Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sale Proceeds Release Form is used to document the release of sale proceeds from a transaction. This form is used to collect information about the client, payment method, and bank details for the purpose of releasing funds.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their address, city, postal code, province, country, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter the release amount and date.
3. Select the release method (Bank, Wire Transfer, or Certified Check).
4. Enter the bank name, account number, and bank routing number (if applicable).
5. If necessary, enter additional information about the release amount, payment method, and bank details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **client** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's name.

### 2. **client_address_1** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's address line 1.

### 3. **client_address_2** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's address line 2.

### 4. **client_city** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's city.

### 5. **client_postal_code** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's postal code.

### 6. **client_province** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's province.

### 7. **client_country** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's country.

### 8. **client_phone** (Text)
This field is for entering the client's phone number.

### 9. **client_email** (Email)
This field is for entering the client's email address.

### 10. **release_amount** (Number)
This field is for entering the amount to be released.

### 11. **release_date** (Date)
This field is for selecting the date of the release.

### 12. **release_method** (Select One)
This field is for selecting the release method (Bank, Wire Transfer, or Certified Check).

### 13. **bank_name** (Text)
This field is for entering the bank name.

### 14. **bank_account_number** (Text)
This field is for entering the bank account number.

### 15. **bank_account_number_two** (Text)
This field is for entering the second bank account number (if necessary).

### 16. **release_amount_two** (Number)
This field is for entering a second release amount (if necessary).

### 17. **payment_method** (Select One)
This field is for selecting whether a second payment method (Yes or No).

### 18. **payment_method_two** (Select One)
This field is for selecting the second payment method (Online Banking, Check, or Wire Transfer).

### 19. **bank_routing_number** (Text)
This field is for entering the bank routing number.

### 20. **bank_routing_number_two** (Text)
This field is for entering the second bank routing number (if necessary).

### 21. **account_name** (Text)
This field is for entering the account name.

### 22. **account_number** (Text)
This field is for entering the account number.

### 23. **bank_account_name** (Text)
This field is for entering the bank account name.

### 24. **account_two** (Text)
This field is a duplicate of the account field and is not used. Please do not enter information here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the client's information for accuracy.
* Ensure that the release amount and date are correct.
* Verify the bank details before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the relevant parties before proceeding.
