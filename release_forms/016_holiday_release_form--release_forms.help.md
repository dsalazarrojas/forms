# Holiday Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Release Form is a document used to record and manage a student's leave or release from school, especially for holiday purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `students_name` field.
2. Indicate the type of relationship between the student and the school representative in the `relationship_to_teacher` field.
3. If required, enter the student's ID in the `student_id` field.
4. Sign the `guardian_signature` field if you are a parent or guardian.
5. Sign the `parent_signature` field if you are a parent or guardian signing on behalf of your child.
6. Enter the date of the release in the `date` field.
7. Enter the time of the release in the `time` field.
8. Enter the name of the school in the `school_name` field.
9. Specify the year of the student's attendance in the `year` field.
10. Provide a reason for the release in the `reason` field.
11. Enter the date of the release in the `date_of_release` field.
12. Specify the school year the student is in in the `school_year` field.
13. Indicate the student's grade level in the `student_grade` field.
14. Include any comments from the teacher in the `teacher_comments` field.
15. Include any comments from parents in the `parent_comments` field.
16. Enter any comments from the school administrator in the `school_admin_comments` field.
17. Finally, sign the `student_signature` field with your dated signature as the student.
18. Verify and sign the `student_dated_signature` field with your dated signature as the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Form** (`release_form`): Enter a brief description or summary of the release reason or purpose.
* **Student's Name** (`students_name`): Enter the name of the student releasing or leaving the school.
* **Relationship to Teacher** (`relationship_to_teacher`): Specify your relationship to the student (e.g., parent, guardian, teacher, etc.).
* **Student ID** (`student_id`): If required, enter the student's ID number.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`): Sign and date this field if you are a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): Sign and date this field if you are a parent or guardian signing on behalf of your child.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the release or leave.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the release or leave.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school the student is releasing or leaving from.
* **Year** (`year`): Specify the year of the student's attendance.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Provide a reason for the student's release or leave.
* **Date of Release** (`date_of_release`): Enter the date of the release or leave.
* **School Year** (`school_year`): Specify the school year the student is in.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`): Indicate the student's grade level.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`): Include any comments from the teacher regarding the student's release or leave.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`): Include any comments from parents regarding the student's release or leave.
* **School Admin Comments** (`school_admin_comments`): Enter any comments from the school administrator.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`): Sign and date this field with your dated signature as the student.
* **Student Dated Signature** (`student_dated_signature`): Verify and sign with your dated signature as the student.
