# Good Hart February Release Pre Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Good Hart February Release Pre Order form is designed to gather information from customers interested in pre-ordering the February release. This information is used for internal processing and follow-up communication with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name as you would like it to appear on your order.
2. Enter your email address for internal communication.
3. Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
4. Add any additional notes or instructions for your pre-order.
5. Indicate whether you are interested in receiving the February release by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **February Pre Order** (`february_pre_order`, text, optional): Enter the title of the pre-order in a free-form text field.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for internal communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for your pre-order.
* **February Release** (`february_release`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you are interested in receiving the February release by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct email address to ensure you receive follow-up communication about your pre-order.
* If you have any specific requests or instructions, please add them in the "Note" field.
* If you are not interested in receiving the February release, please select "No" in the "February Release" field.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share your pre-order information with others.
