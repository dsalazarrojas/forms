# Audio Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Release Form is a document used to transfer ownership and rights related to an audio work from the Assignor to the Assignee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are the rightful owner of the rights being transferred.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
3. Sign the form electronically by typing your full name as the Assignor.
4. Enter the date of the release agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assignor Full Name** (`assignor_full_name`): Enter your full legal name as the party transferring the rights.
* **Email Address** (`assignor_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`assignor_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`assignor_address`): Enter your full address for correspondence.
* **Assignee Name** (`assignee_name`): Enter the name of the party receiving the rights.
* **Assignee Company** (`assignee_company`): Enter the name of the company (if applicable) receiving the rights.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): Enter the date of the release agreement.
* **Audio Work Title** (`audio_work_title`): Enter the title of the audio work being released.
* **Audio Work Description** (`audio_work_description`): Briefly describe the audio work.
* **Creation Date** (`creation_date`): Enter the date of creation of the audio work (optional).
* **Work Type** (`work_type`): Select the type of audio work (e.g., Musical composition, Sound recording, etc.).
* **Rights Transferred** (`rights_transferred`): Select the rights being transferred (e.g., Copyright ownership, Master recording rights, etc.).
* **Territory Scope** (`territory_scope`): Select the geographic scope of transfer (e.g., Worldwide, North America, etc.).
* **Duration of Transfer** (`duration_of_transfer`): Select the duration of rights transfer (e.g., In perpetuity, 99 years, etc.).
* **Consideration Amount** (`consideration_amount`): Enter the payment amount in USD.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Select the payment structure (e.g., Lump sum payment, Installment plan, etc.).
* **Writer Share Retained** (`writer_share_retained`): Select whether you are retaining any writer share.
* **Moral Rights Waived** (`moral_rights_waived`): Select whether moral rights are being waived.
* **Warranties Confirmed** (`warranties_confirmed`): Confirm that you own the rights and can transfer them.
* **Indemnification Agreement** (`indemnification_agreement`): Select whether you agree to indemnify against claims.
* **Assignor Signature** (`assignor_signature`): Type your full name as the electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of the release agreement.

## Tips
Please review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out accurately and consistently with the terms of the agreement. Consult with your legal representative if you are unsure about any of the terms or fields.
