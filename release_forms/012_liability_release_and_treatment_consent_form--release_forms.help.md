# Liability Release And Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a client or patient about their liability and treatment for medical or dental procedures. It is meant to be completed by the client or patient before treatment, to ensure they are aware of the risks and benefits of the treatment and to provide a legal release of liability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Review each field to ensure you understand the question and can answer it correctly.
3. If you need help, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Form** (`release_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can describe your understanding and consent to the treatment. Please provide as much detail as possible about your experience and expectations.
* **Signature Form** (`signature_form`, select_one, optional): Please select "I understand and agree to the terms" to confirm you have read and agreed to the treatment terms.
* **Acknowledgment Form** (`acknowledgment_form`, text, optional): This section is for any additional comments or acknowledgment you would like to make about your understanding and consent.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (`risk_acknowledgment`, select_one, optional): Please select "I have read and understand the risk" to confirm you have read and understood the risk involved with the treatment.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can describe your consent and understanding of the treatment.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Please enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Please enter the date this form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Please enter the time this form was completed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm your understanding and consent.
* **Name of Client** (`name_of_client`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it should be spelled on the form.
* **Name of Provider** (`name_of_provider`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the treatment.
