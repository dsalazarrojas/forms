# photo_waiver_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a photo waiver release form, which is a document that allows individuals to waive their rights to certain photographs or images. It is typically used in situations where photographs are taken for official purposes, such as for law enforcement, public events, or other organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with the relevant information, making sure to include the purpose of the waiver.
2. Enter the date the waiver was signed by the individual.
3. Enter the date the waiver is effective from.
4. Enter the date the waiver expires.
5. Review and submit the form once all fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photo Waiver** (1, text, required): This field is the main text section where you will enter the details of the photo waiver.
* **Waiver** (2, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the waiver, if necessary.
* **Purpose** (3, text, optional): Enter the purpose of the photo waiver, if applicable.
* **Date Signed** (4, date, required): Enter the date the individual signed the photo waiver.
* **Date Effective** (5, date, required): Enter the date the photo waiver is effective from.
* **Date Expiration** (6, date, required): Enter the date the photo waiver expires.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check dates for correct formatting.
* Review your entries before submission to ensure they meet your needs.
