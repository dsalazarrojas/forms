# Energy Credit Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to authorize energy credits for a customer's premises. It helps authorized personnel to accurately capture information required for billing and credit purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Energy Credit Authorization** information in the first field. This is the primary field where you can provide a brief description of the energy credit authorization.
2.  Select the **Credit Type** from the options provided. This field helps to identify the type of credit being authorized.
3.  Enter the **Usage Start Date** and **Usage End Date** in the respective fields. These dates help to establish the period of energy usage.
4.  Enter the **Billing Start Date** and **Billing End Date** in the respective fields. These dates help to establish the billing cycle for the energy credit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Energy Credit Authorization** (1):
    *   **Label:** Energy Credit Authorization
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** False
    *   This field is used to enter a brief description of the energy credit authorization for the premises.
*   **Credit Type** (2):
    *   **Label:** Credit Type
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Required:** False
    *   This field helps to identify the type of credit being authorized.
*   **Usage Start Date** (3):
    *   **Label:** Usage Start Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** False
    *   Enter the date when energy usage started for the premises.
*   **Usage End Date** (4):
    *   **Label:** Usage End Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** False
    *   Enter the date when energy usage ended for the premises.
*   **Billing Start Date** (5):
    *   **Label:** Billing Start Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** False
    *   Enter the date when billing started for the premises.
*   **Billing End Date** (6):
    *   **Label:** Billing End Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** False
    *   Enter the date when billing ended for the premises.

## Tips

*   Double-check the dates entered in the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Ensure that the credit type selected is correct.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
