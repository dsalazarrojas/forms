# Personal Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Credit Application Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals applying for credit. This form helps to collect necessary details about the applicant, their employment, income, credit history, and agreement terms for a credit decision. By providing accurate and honest information, applicants can help us make informed decisions about their credit application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form fields with the required and optional information requested.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date details about your employment, income, and credit history.
3. Review and agree to the credit agreement terms, which outline the terms and conditions of the credit application.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Credit Application Form**: This is the main title of the form.
  - **Applicant Info**: Please provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
  - **Contact Info**: Enter your contact details, such as phone number and email.
  - **Employment Info**: Share your employment history, including your job title, employer, and length of employment.
  - **Income Info**: Provide your income details, including your income amount and sources.
  - **Employment History**: Describe your employment history, including your previous employers and job titles.
  - **Credit History**: Share your credit history, including your credit score and any existing debts.
  - **Credit Score**: Enter your current credit score.
  - **Credit Limit**: Specify the desired credit limit.
  - **Credit Agreement**: Review and agree to the credit agreement terms.
  - **Credit Report**: This field will be used to display your credit report.
  - **Credit Decision**: This field will display the credit decision.
  - **User Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
  - **Credit Agreement Terms 1-10**: Review and agree to the credit agreement terms 1-10.

Note: All fields are required unless specified as optional. Please fill in all the fields accurately to ensure a smooth credit application process.
