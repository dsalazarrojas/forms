# Small Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from small business owners applying for credit. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to help us assess your creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the requested information. You can navigate between the different sections using the page buttons at the top and bottom of the page. If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information 
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required): This section is where you will provide some basic information about yourself, the owner of the business. Please fill out your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
### Contact Information 
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This section is where you can provide contact information for the business, including a phone number and email address.
### Credit Information 
* **Credit Information** (`credit_information`, text, required): In this section, you will need to provide some credit history for the business, including any credit scores or reports that are relevant to the application.
### Business Information 
* **Business Information** (`business_information`, text, required): Here, you will provide some information about the business, including its name, address, and industry.
### Loan Options 
* **Loan Options** (`loan_options`, text, required): This section is where you choose the type of loan you are interested in, such as a term loan or line of credit.
### Loan Amount 
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the exact amount of money you are seeking to borrow.
### Loan Term 
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, required): Here, you will choose the length of time you plan to take the loan for.
Tips:
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact us for help.
- This form should not be used for personal or residential purposes, but rather for small business credit applications only.
