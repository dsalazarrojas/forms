# small_business_fund_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about a small business applying for funding. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the business, including its type, name, contact information, and financial details. This information will be used to assess the business's eligibility for funding and to guide the funding decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business information section, including the business type, name, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide contact information for the business, including owner and business contact details.
3. Enter financial information, including funding details and tax ID.
4. Enter any additional details about the business, such as employee count, industry, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Type**: Select the type of business (Small Business, Medium Business, or Large Business)
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the business, including owner and business contact details.
* **Owner Information**: Enter the contact information for the owner of the business.
* **Business Details**: Enter any additional details about the business, such as employee count, industry, and address.
* **Funding Details**: Enter any additional details about the funding, such as the amount required.
* **Financial Information**: Enter the financial information about the business, including tax ID.
* **Tax ID**: Enter the tax ID of the business.
* **Date Opened**: Enter the date the business was opened.
* **Business License**: Enter the business license number.
* **Employee Count**: Enter the number of employees in the business.
* **Industry**: Enter the industry of the business.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the business.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the address line 1 of the business.
* **City**: Enter the city of the business.
* **State**: Enter the state of the business.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the business.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Owner Phone 2**: Enter the owner's secondary phone number.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the business phone number.
* **Business Email**: Enter the business email address.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **Website**: Enter the website of the business.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the business.
