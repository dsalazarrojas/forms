# Credit Reference Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request credit references for a credit application. It is intended for individuals to provide information about their current or past employment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your details in the relevant fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Credit Reference Request Form** (`credit_reference_request_form`, text, optional): This field is the title of the form. You can enter any relevant details about the request, such as the purpose of the credit application or the reason for the request.
* **Credit History** (`credit_history`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your credit history, including any relevant information about your employment or financial situations.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Name** (`contact_details_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be listed on any credit reports or references.
* **Address** (`contact_details_address`, text, optional): Enter your current address or previous address where you were employed.
* **Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Employer Since** (`employer_since`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been employed with your current or previous employer.
* **Job Title** (`employer_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your job title or position at your current or previous employer.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your current employment status (e.g., employed, unemployed, retired).
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of your employment.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of your employment.
* **Employment Termination Reason** (`employment_termination_reason`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the reason for your employment termination.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and share this information with the requesting party.
* Do not submit duplicate requests, as this may cause delays in the credit application process.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors or mistakes.
