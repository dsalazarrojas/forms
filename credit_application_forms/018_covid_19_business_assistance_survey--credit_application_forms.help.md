# COVID 19 Business Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Business Assistance Survey is designed to collect information about businesses affected by the pandemic. It will help us understand the challenges faced by businesses and provide essential data to inform support and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the **Business Name**, **Business Owner Name**, **Business Email**, and **Business Phone Number** fields with your business's contact information.
2. Select the **Type of Business** that best describes your business from the provided options.
3. Enter your **Business Location** in the text field.
4. Enter the **Number of Employees** in your business.
5. Provide an approximate value for **Annual Revenue Before COVID-19**.
6. Select the **Impact of COVID-19 on Business** that best describes your business's situation from the provided options.
7. Enter your estimated **Revenue Loss** percentage, if applicable.
8. Select all relevant **Operational Changes Required** due to the pandemic.
9. Choose the types of **Financial Assistance Needed** from the options provided.
10. If you have received any **pandemic assistance**, select 'Yes' or 'No'.
11. If you've received assistance, choose the **Type of Assistance Received** from the options.
12. Describe the **Main Challenges Faced** by your business due to the pandemic.
13. Select your **Expected Recovery Timeline** from the provided options.
14. (Optional) Enter the type of **Support Needed** if different from previous answers.
15. Finally, select your business's **Business Outlook** from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's name, e.g., 'ABC Corporation'.
* **Business Owner Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business, e.g., 'John Doe'.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter your business's email address, e.g., 'john.doe@example.com'.
* **Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's phone number, e.g., '123-456-7890'.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your business, e.g., 'Retail', 'Food and Beverage', etc.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required): Enter your business's location, e.g., '123 Main St, Anytown, USA'.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Annual Revenue Before COVID-19** (`annual_revenue_before_covid`, number, required): Enter an approximate value of your business's annual revenue before the pandemic.
* **Impact of COVID-19 on Business** (`covid_impact_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of impact the pandemic has had on your business.
* **Estimated Revenue Loss** (`revenue_loss_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the estimated revenue loss percentage, if applicable.
* **Operational Changes Required** (`operational_changes`, select_multiple, required): Select all operational changes made due to the pandemic.
* **Financial Assistance Needed** (`financial_assistance_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of financial assistance needed.
* **Have you received any pandemic assistance?** (`assistance_received`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you've received assistance.
* **Type of Assistance Received** (`assistance_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of assistance received, if different from previous answers.
* **Main Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, required): Describe the main challenges faced by your business due to the pandemic.
* **Expected Recovery Timeline** (`recovery_timeline`, select_one, required): Select your business's expected recovery timeline.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, text, optional): Enter the type of support needed if different from previous answers.
* **Business Outlook** (`future_outlook`, select_one, required): Select your business's outlook after the pandemic.
