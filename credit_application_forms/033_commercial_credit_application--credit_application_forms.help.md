# Commercial Credit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from businesses seeking commercial credit from the organization. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the credit application process proceeds smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2. Enter the business name and DBA (Doing Business As) name if applicable.
3. Choose the correct business type from the drop-down menu.
4. Fill in the federal tax ID (EIN), state of incorporation, and year established.
5. Provide the business address, phone number, and email address.
6. Enter the principal owner's name, title, and SSN.
7. Select the desired payment terms from the drop-down menu.
8. Complete the trade references (Company name, contact number, and account number) and bank references (Bank name, account number, and routing number).
9. Authorize credit checks and accept the terms and conditions.
10. Add any additional comments or information as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **DBA (Doing Business As)** (`dba`, `text`, optional): If your business has a trade name, enter it here.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct type of business from the drop-down menu.
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)** (`federal_tax_id`, `text`, required): Enter the Employer Identification Number.
* **State of Incorporation** (`state_of_incorporation`, `text`, required): Enter the state where your business is incorporated.
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, `number`, required): Enter the year your business started.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter the complete address of your business.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the primary phone number of your business.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your business.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the principal owner.
* **Owner Title** (`owner_title`, `text`, required): Enter the position held by the principal owner in the business.
* **Owner SSN** (`owner_ssn`, `text`, required): Enter the Social Security Number of the principal owner.
* **Owner Date of Birth** (`owner_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth of the principal owner.
* **Owner Home Address** (`owner_home_address`, `text`, required): Enter the personal address of the principal owner.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the personal phone number of the principal owner.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `number`, required): Enter the gross annual revenue of your business.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of employees in your business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct number of years your business has been operating.
* **Credit Amount Requested** (`credit_amount_requested`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of credit you are requesting.
* **Purpose of Credit** (`credit_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose the reason for credit request from the drop-down menu.
* **Desired Payment Terms** (`desired_payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred payment terms from the drop-down menu.
* **Trade Reference 1** (`reference_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the first trade reference.
* **Trade Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the first trade reference.
* **Trade Reference 1 Account** (`reference_1_account`, `text`, optional): Enter the account number of the first trade reference.
* **Trade Reference 2** (`reference_2_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the second trade reference.
* **Trade Reference 2 Phone** (`reference_2_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the second trade reference.
* **Trade Reference 2 Account** (`reference_2_account`, `text`, optional): Enter the account number of the second trade reference.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, `text`, required): Enter the primary banking institution.
* **Bank Account Number** (`bank_account_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the account number of your business with the bank.
* **Bank Routing Number** (`bank_routing_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the routing number of your business with the bank.
* **Credit Check Authorization** (`credit_check_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you authorize credit checks.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
