# credit_repair_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals applying for credit repair services. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide personalized recommendations for improving your credit score and reducing debt.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information as requested.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you regarding your application.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
- **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your annual income.
- **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are looking to repair your credit for.
- **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required): Enter your current credit score.
- **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, select_multiple, required): Choose the loan terms you are interested in (e.g., 30, 60, 90 days).
- **Credit Agency** (`credit_agency`, text, required): Enter the credit agency you have a relationship with.
- **Client ID** (`client_id`, text, optional): If you have a client ID with the credit agency, enter it here.
- **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required): Choose whether your account is for personal or business purposes.
- **Loan Terms Note** (`loan_terms_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your loan terms, enter it here.
- **Loan Amount Note** (`loan_amount_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your loan amount, enter it here.
- **Credit Score Note** (`credit_score_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your credit score, enter it here.
- **Credit Agency Note** (`credit_agency_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your credit agency, enter it here.
- **Employment Status Note** (`employment_status_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your employment status, enter it here.
- **Loan Terms Note** (`loan_terms_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your loan terms, enter it here.
- **Client ID Note** (`client_id_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your client ID, enter it here.
- **Loan Amount Note** (`loan_amount_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your loan amount, enter it here.
- **Credit Score Note** (`credit_score_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your credit score, enter it here.
- **Credit Agency Note** (`credit_agency_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your credit agency, enter it here.
