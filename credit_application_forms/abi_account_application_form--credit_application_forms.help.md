# ABI Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants seeking an ABI account, including personal details, employment status, income, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your official ID.
2. Enter your primary email address for correspondence.
3. Provide your phone number, including the country code.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Enter your national ID or passport number.
6. Provide your home address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
7. Enter the date of application.
8. Select the type of application (e.g., new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
9. Choose the primary purpose of the account (e.g., personal, business, or joint).
10. Select your current employment status (e.g., employed, self-employed, unemployed, retired, or student).
11. If applicable, enter the name of your employer or company.
12. Approximate annual income in local currency (optional).
13. If you have dependents, enter their number.
14. Select all supporting documents included with this application.
15. Confirm your consent to the terms and data use.
16. Provide any additional comments or supporting details.
17. Sign your name as a digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email address** (Required): Enter your primary contact email for correspondence.
* **Phone number** (Optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **National ID / Passport number** (Required): Enter your national ID or passport number.
* **Home address** (Required): Enter your home address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.
* **Application date** (Required): Enter the date of application.
* **Type of application** (Required): Select the type of application (e.g., new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
* **Desired account type** (Required): Choose the primary account purpose (e.g., personal, business, or joint).
* **Employment status** (Required): Select your current employment status (e.g., employed, self-employed, unemployed, retired, or student).
* **Employer / Company name** (Optional): Enter the name of your employer or company if applicable.
* **Approximate annual income** (Optional): Enter your approximate annual income in local currency.
* **Number of dependents** (Optional): Enter the number of dependents if you have any.
* **Supporting documents** (Optional): Select all documents included with this application (e.g., Proof of ID, Proof of Address, Income Statement, Bank Statement, Reference Letter, or Other).
* **Consent to terms and data use** (Required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and data use.
* **Additional comments or supporting details** (Optional): Provide any extra information helpful for processing.
* **Applicant signature** (Required): Sign your name as a digital signature.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the applicant, and the fields marked as "required" are essential for the application process.
