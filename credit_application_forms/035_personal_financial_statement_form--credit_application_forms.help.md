# Personal Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Personal Financial Statement Form is designed to gather information about your financial status, including your assets, liabilities, and income. This form will help us understand your current financial situation and provide accurate information to assess your eligibility for certain financial programs or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2.  For fields where you have multiple options, select the ones that best match your financial situation.
3.  If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
4.  Ensure that you have up-to-date information before filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Financial Status** (`personal_financial_statement`, text, required/optional):
    Please provide a brief overview of your current financial status, including any relevant details about your assets, liabilities, and income.
*   **Total Assets** (`assets_total`, number, required/optional):
    Enter the total value of your assets.
*   **Cash and Savings** (`assets_cash`, select_multiple, required):
    List all cash and savings accounts, such as:
    -   Cash
    -   Savings
*   **Real Estate** (`assets_real_estate`, select_multiple, required):
    List all real estate holdings, such as:
    -   Home
    -   Investment properties
*   **Other Assets** (`assets_other`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    List all other assets, such as:
    -   401(k)
    -   Stocks
    -   Bonds
    -   etc.
*   **Total Liabilities** (`liabilities_total`, number, required/optional):
    Enter the total value of your liabilities.
*   **Debt** (`liabilities_debt`, select_multiple, required):
    List all debt with a balance over $100,000.
*   **Other Liabilities** (`liabilities_other`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    List all other debt and liabilities, such as:
    -   Tax debt
    -   Utility bills
    -   etc.
*   **Date of Statement** (`date_statement`, date, required/optional):
    Enter the date of the current or most recent financial statement.
*   **Time of Year** (`time_of_year`, select_one, required):
    Select the time of year the financial statement is for:
    -   Q1
    -   Q2
    -   Q3
    -   Q4
*   **Annual Income** (`income`, number, required/optional):
    Enter your annual income for the year.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional):
    Enter the date of your signature.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional):
    Provide your signature.

**Tips**

- Please ensure that you answer each field accurately and honestly.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
- This form is for your use, and the information provided will be used to assess your eligibility for certain financial programs or services.
