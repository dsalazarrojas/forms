# Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business credit application information to help us evaluate your creditworthiness and provide you with the necessary funds to support your business growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Business Information**: Select the "Business Type" option that best describes your business structure. This can be Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other.
2. **Business Contact**: Enter your business's legal name, and ensure it matches the name on your tax ID or EIN.
3. **Address and Contact Information**: Provide your business's physical address, main phone number, and email address.
4. **Contact Information**: Enter the name of the primary contact person, their title in the company, and their personal email and phone number.
5. **Financial Information**: Enter your annual revenue, approximate total assets, and current total liabilities.
6. **Credit Application Details**: Select the amount of credit you need, choose the type of credit (e.g., Line of Credit, Term Loan, Equipment Financing, or Working Capital), and explain the purpose of the credit.
7. **Credit Repayment**: Select your preferred repayment period (6, 12, 24, 36, or 60 Months).
8. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details that are relevant to your credit application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: A brief, accurate representation of your business's legal name.
* **Business Type**: Select the structure of your business.
* **Tax ID or EIN**: A 9-digit Employer Identification Number (EIN) or 11-digit Tax ID number.
* **Years in Business**: How long your business has been established.
* **Business Address**: Your business's physical address.
* **Business Phone**: Your primary business phone number.
* **Business Email**: A valid email address associated with your business.
* **Owner or Manager Name**: The name of the primary contact person.
* **Title**: The contact person's position in the company.
* **Contact Email**: A valid email address associated with the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The contact person's direct phone number.
* **Annual Revenue**: Your business's revenue for the last 12 months.
* **Total Assets**: An approximate value of your business's total assets.
* **Total Liabilities**: Your current total debt.
* **Credit Amount Requested**: The amount of credit you need.
* **Type of Credit**: The type of credit you are applying for (e.g., Line of Credit, Term Loan, Equipment Financing).
* **Purpose of Credit**: A clear explanation of how you plan to use the credit funds.
* **Preferred Repayment Period**: Your preferred repayment duration.
* **Additional Information**: Any other relevant information that will help us evaluate your credit application.
