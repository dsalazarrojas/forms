# Auto Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a credit application process. The form collects personal details, employment information, and vehicle details in order to assess the applicant's creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the following pages:
	* Page 1: Applicant Information
	* Page 2: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth
	* Page 3: Employment Information (Employer Name, Job Title, Length of Employment)
	* Page 4: Vehicle Information (Year, Make, Model)
	* Page 5: Credit Agreement

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (Page 1)
	+ **First Name**: Your legal first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
	+ **Date of Birth**: MM-DD-YYYY
* **First Name**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: MM-DD-YYYY
* **Email Address**: A valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Primary contact number.
* **Street Address**: Your current residence address.
* **City**: City of residence.
* **State**: State of residence. Select from the list of options.
* **ZIP Code**: 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Residence Type**: Your current housing situation. Select from the list of options (Own, Rent, Live with family, Other).
* **Monthly Housing Payment**: Monthly rent or mortgage amount.
* **Employment Information** (Page 2)
	+ **Employer Name**: Current employer.
	+ **Job Title**: Your current position.
	+ **Length of Employment**: How long have you worked there. Select from the list of options (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years).
* **Annual Income**: Gross annual income before taxes.
* **Vehicle Information** (Page 3)
	+ **Vehicle Year**: Year of the vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Make**: Manufacturer of the vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Model**: Model of the vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Price**: Purchase price of the vehicle.
* **Do You Have a Trade-In**: Are you trading in a vehicle? Select from the list of options (True, False).
* **Down Payment Amount**: Amount you plan to put down.
* **Employment Information (Employer Name)**: Current employer.
* **Job Title**: Your current position.
* **Length of Employment**: How long have you worked there. Select from the list of options (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years).
* **Annual Income**: Gross annual income before taxes.
* **Credit References**: Provide a personal reference.
* **Authorization Agreement**: I authorize the verification of information provided. Select from the list of options (I Agree, I Do Not Agree).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

Note: Please note that the provided information may be used to generate a credit report or score.
