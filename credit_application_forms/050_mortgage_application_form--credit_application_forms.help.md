# Mortgage Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Application Form is used to gather information about the applicant's personal and financial details, property information, loan requirements, and employment history. This form helps the lender to assess the applicant's creditworthiness, loan eligibility, and loan terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Applicant Information section, which includes details such as name, contact details, and employment status.
2. Next, enter your contact details, including phone number and any other relevant contact information.
3. Provide information about your employment history, including length of employment and employment status.
4. Enter your income and asset information, including loan amount and property value.
5. Select the loan terms and payment frequency that suits your needs.
6. Finally, review all the sections and ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): Enter your full name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about the property you are applying for a loan on, including location and type.
* **Loan Information** (`loan_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about the loan you are applying for, including purpose and amount.
* **Income Information** (`income_info`, text, required: false): Enter your income details, including monthly income and any other relevant financial information.
* **Asset Information** (`asset_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about your assets, including value and type.
* **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, text, required: false): Enter your employment details, including job title and years of experience.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your employment history.
* **Loan Offer** (`loan_offer`, text, required: false): Enter any additional loan offer details, such as interest rate and loan term.
* **Applicant Employment Status** (`applicant_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Length of Employment** (`applicant_employment_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of your employment in years.
* **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any additional loan terms, such as interest rate and loan duration.
* **Applicant Income** (`applicant_income`, number, required: false): Enter your monthly income.
* **Asset Value** (`asset_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of your assets.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, required: false): Enter the value of the property you are applying for a loan on.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **Applicant Age** (`applicant_age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Employment Years** (`employment_years`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Loan Terms Frequency** (`loan_terms_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your loan payments from the options provided.
* **Loan Terms Payment** (`loan_terms_payment`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of loan payment you prefer from the options provided.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.

Note: All fields marked as "required: false" are optional, and you can choose to fill them out or not as per your preference.
