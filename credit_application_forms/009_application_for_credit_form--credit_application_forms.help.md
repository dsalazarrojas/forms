# Application For Credit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a credit application form used to collect information from individuals seeking credit. The information gathered will be used to assess creditworthiness and determine credit approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the terms and conditions before starting the form.
2. Complete each section carefully and accurately.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date on which the credit application was submitted.
* **Applicant Type** (`applicant_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of applicant:
	+ Individual: For personal credit applications.
	+ Business: For business credit applications.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter your SSN or tax ID number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal or zip code of residence.
* **Residence Status** (`residence_status`, select_one, required): Select your residence status:
	+ Own: You own the property.
	+ Rent: You rent the property.
	+ Live with Family: You live with family members.
	+ Other: Other (please specify).
* **Monthly Housing Payment** (`monthly_housing_payment`, number, required): Enter your monthly rent or mortgage payment.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status:
	+ Employed Full-Time: You are employed full-time.
	+ Employed Part-Time: You are employed part-time.
	+ Self-Employed: You are self-employed.
	+ Unemployed: You are unemployed.
	+ Retired: You are retired.
	+ Student: You are a student.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter your current employer's name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of employment:
	+ Less than 6 months: Less than 6 months.
	+ 6 months to 1 year: 6 months to 1 year.
	+ 1-3 years: 1-3 years.
	+ 3-5 years: 3-5 years.
	+ More than 5 years: More than 5 years.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, required): Enter your gross annual income.
* **Additional Income Sources** (`additional_income_sources`, text, optional): List any additional sources of income (if applicable).
* **Monthly Expenses** (`monthly_expenses`, number, required): Enter your total monthly expenses.
* **Credit Card Requested** (`credit_card_requested`, select_one, required): Select the type of credit card requested:
	+ Standard: Standard credit card.
	+ Gold: Gold credit card.
	+ Platinum: Platinum credit card.
	+ Premium: Premium credit card.
	+ Not Sure: Other (specify in the comments).
* **Credit Limit Requested** (`credit_limit_requested`, number, required): Enter the desired credit limit.
* **Purpose of Credit** (`purpose_of_credit`, select_one, required): Select the primary purpose of credit:
	+ Everyday Purchases: For everyday purchases.
	+ Building Credit: To build credit.
	+ Emergency Fund: For emergency funding.
	+ Large Purchases: For large purchases.
	+ Debt Consolidation: To consolidate debt.
	+ Other: Other (specify in the comments).
* **Existing Bank Accounts** (`existing_bank_accounts`, select_one, required): Select your bank account status:
	+ Checking: You have a checking account with us.
	+ Savings: You have a savings account with us.
	+ Both: You have both checking and savings accounts with us.
	+ None: You do not have an account with us.
* **Bankruptcies** (`bankruptcies`, select_one, required): Select your bankruptcy status:
	+ True: You have filed for bankruptcy.
	+ False: You have not filed for bankruptcy.
* **Bankruptcy Details** (`bankruptcy_details`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your bankruptcy (if applicable).
* **Outstanding Judgments** (`outstanding_judgments`, select_one, required): Select your outstanding judgment status:
	+ True: You have outstanding judgments.
	+ False: You do not have outstanding judgments.
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, select_one, required): Select your agreement to terms:
	+ True: You agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ False: You do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Authorize Credit Check** (`authorize_credit_check`, select_one, required): Select your authorization for credit check:
	+ True: You authorize a credit check.
	+ False: You do not authorize a credit check.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as the signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
1. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
2. Review the terms and conditions before starting the form.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
