# Cold Logistics Equipment Credit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by businesses to apply for credit to purchase logistics equipment. It is a crucial part of the credit application process, and it helps our credit committee assess your creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name in the "Applicant business name" field.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for credit correspondence in the "Contact person" and "Contact email" fields.
3. Specify the amount of credit you are requesting in the "Requested credit amount" field.
4. Describe the purpose of the credit in the "Purpose of credit" field.
5. If applicable, provide the number of years your business has been in operation in the "Years in business" field.
6. If available, provide credit references from banks or suppliers in the "Credit references" field.
7. Indicate whether financial statements are attached in the "Financial statements attached" field.
8. Add any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the credit committee in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant business name**: Enter your business's legal name.
* **Contact person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for credit correspondence.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address used for credit statements.
* **Requested credit amount**: Enter the numeric amount of credit you are requesting.
* **Purpose of credit**: Describe the type or description of equipment you are interested in purchasing.
* **Years in business**: If applicable, enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Credit references**: Provide references from banks or suppliers.
* **Financial statements attached**: Indicate if financial statements are attached.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional information that may be helpful for the credit committee.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Attach any supporting documents, such as financial statements, if applicable.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our credit department for assistance.
