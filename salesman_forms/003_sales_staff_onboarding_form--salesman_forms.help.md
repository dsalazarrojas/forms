# Sales Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Staff Onboarding Form is used to gather essential information about new sales staff members. This information helps us to understand their roles, regions, and manager's details, which is crucial for effective team management and sales performance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Select your Sales Role from the available options.
3. Choose the Sales Regions you operate in.
4. Enter your Date Available.
5. Select your Sales Manager from the available options.
6. Enter your Manager's Notes.
7. Enter your Manager's Phone Number.
8. Enter your Manager's Email.
9. Select your Manager's Sales Region.
10. Select your Sales Team.
11. Select your Manager Sales Rep.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Sales Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select the sales role you are assigned to.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the regions you operate in.
* **Date Available** (`date_available`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`, select_one, required): Select your sales manager from the available options.
* **Manager Notes** (`sales_manager_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your sales manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`sales_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`sales_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Sales Manager Region** (`sales_manager_sales_region`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your manager's sales region.
* **Manager Start Date** (`sales_manager_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your manager's employment.
* **Manager End Date** (`sales_manager_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your manager's employment.
* **Sales Team** (`sales_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sales team you belong to.
* **Manager Sales Rep** (`sales_manager_sales_rep`, select_one, optional): Select your manager's sales representative.
