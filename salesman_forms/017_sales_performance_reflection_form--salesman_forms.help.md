# Sales Performance Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Performance Reflection Form is designed to track and monitor sales performance across various sales periods. This form collects information about customer interactions, sales amounts, and sales strategies employed. It helps to analyze and improve sales performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the customer's name and the sales representative's name in the respective fields.
2. Fill in the sales period for which you are reflecting on.
3. Provide the total sales amount achieved during that period.
4. Describe any notable customer interactions that occurred during that period.
5. Write down your sales strategy used to reach the customer or secure the sale.
6. Briefly explain the sales result and outcome of the interaction or sale.
7. Add any additional notes about the sales interaction or sale (optional).
8. If applicable, enter the sales date and time of the interaction or sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer's Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer you interacted with or sold to.
* **Sales Representative's Name** (`sales_rep_name`, text, required): Enter your own name or the name of the sales representative who interacted with or sold to the customer.
* **Sales Period** (`sales_period`, date, required): Select the sales period for which you are providing feedback. For example, "Q1" or "February 2023".
* **Total Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, number, required): Enter the total sales amount achieved during the selected sales period.
* **Customer Interaction** (`customer_interaction`, text, required): Describe any notable interactions or conversations with the customer during that period.
* **Sales Strategy** (`sales_strategy`, text, required): Write down your sales strategy or approach used to reach the customer or secure the sale.
* **Sales Result** (`sales_result`, text, required): Briefly explain the sales result and outcome of the interaction or sale.
* **Sales Notes** (`sales_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the sales interaction or sale, such as follow-up tasks or next steps.
* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the sales interaction or sale (if different from the sales period).
* **Sales Time** (`sales_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the sales interaction or sale (if applicable).
* **Customer's Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if you have it).
* **Customer's Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address (if you have it).

## Tips
* Be specific and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
