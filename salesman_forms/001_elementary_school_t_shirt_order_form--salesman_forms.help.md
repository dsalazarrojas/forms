# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School T Shirt Order Form is designed for students and parents to purchase t-shirts with their school's logo or mascot on it. This form helps to track orders and ensure accurate payment and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the required fields.
2.  Fill out the required fields (T-Shirt Size, T-Shirt Name, Card Type, and Card CVV).
3.  Fill out the optional fields (T-Shirt Color, T-Shirt Quantity, Email, Card Type, Expiration Date, Expiration Year, School Name, and School Address) according to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the size of the t-shirt you want to order (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **T-Shirt Color** (`color`, `select_one`, required): Select the color of the t-shirt you want to order (Red, Blue, or Green).
* **T-Shirt Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): If you're ordering more than one t-shirt, enter the total quantity here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates and notifications.
* **T-Shirt Name** (`tshirt_name`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the t-shirt design you want to order (T-Shirt A, T-Shirt B, or T-Shirt C).
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of credit card you will be using for payment (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, optional): If you're paying with a credit card, enter the expiration date of the card.
* **Expiration Year** (`expiration_year`, `time`, optional): Enter the expiration year of the credit card.
* **Card CVV** (`card_cvv`, `number`, required): Enter the three-digit code on the back of your credit card.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): If you're ordering for a specific school, enter the school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the school's address for any special delivery instructions.

## Tips
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the school's administration office.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
