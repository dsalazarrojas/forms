# Email Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect email order information from customers to enable the sales team to process orders efficiently. This includes customer contact information, order details, and payment methods to facilitate smooth order management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required" in red text on the form.
2. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is the name of the person placing the order.
3. Enter your **Customer Email** in the "Customer Email" field. This is the email address associated with the customer's account.
4. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown list. You can choose between **Cash** and **Credit Card**.
5. Enter your **Order Total** in the "Order Total" field. This is the total cost of the ordered items.
6. If desired, check the box to receive a **Confirmation Email**. This will send a confirmation email to the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address associated with the customer's account.
* **Order Item**: Enter a brief description of the ordered item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the ordered item.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer. You can choose between **Cash** and **Credit Card**.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the ordered items.
* **Confirmation Email**: Check this box to receive a confirmation email from us. This is optional and helps us keep you updated on your order status.
* **Message**: If you have any specific comments or requests for your order, enter them here. This is optional.
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional details about your order. This is optional.
