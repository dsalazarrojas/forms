# Sales Appointment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Appointment Confirmation Form is designed to collect information and confirm sales appointments between a salesperson and a lead. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are documented and synced with the sales system and calendar. The form must be completed for each sales appointment to ensure accurate record-keeping and communication with the lead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the lead's information in the "Lead Information" section.
2.  Enter the date and time of the appointment.
3.  Choose the type of appointment (e.g., "Appointment", "Demo", or "Meeting").
4.  Select whether the salesperson will be present or not.
5.  Choose the lead status (e.g., "New Lead", "Existing Lead", or "Qualified Lead").
6.  Determine whether to sync the appointment with your calendar (e.g., "Zoom", "Google Calendar", or "iCal") and with the sales system (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7.  Add any custom information as needed in the "Custom Fields" section.
8.  Save the form to confirm the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lead Information** (`Lead Information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the lead's name, email, or any other relevant information in this field.
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the appointment in MM/DD/YY format.
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the appointment in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **Type of Appointment** (`Type of Appointment`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of appointment from the available options (e.g., "Appointment", "Demo", or "Meeting").
*   **Sales Person** (`Sales Person`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the salesperson will be present or not.
*   **Lead Status** (`Lead Status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the lead status from the available options (e.g., "New Lead", "Existing Lead", or "Qualified Lead").
*   **Sync with Calendar** (`Sync with Calendar`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how to sync the appointment with your calendar (e.g., "Zoom", "Google Calendar", or "iCal").
*   **Sync with Sales System** (`Sync with Sales System`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether to sync the appointment with the sales system.
*   **Custom Fields** (`Custom Fields`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information about the lead or appointment.
*   **Custom Field 1** (`Custom Field 1`, `text`, required: false): Enter custom information about the lead or appointment.
