# Commission Statement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a commission statement for a salesperson, showing the total sales for a given period, commission rate, and calculated commission earned. The form will guide you through a standard commission calculation process, which includes entering the statement period, agent's name, total sales, commission rate, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of the earnings period for which you want to generate the commission statement.
2. Select the end date of the earnings period.
3. Enter the name of the salesperson receiving the statement.
4. Enter the total sales for the period, as a whole number.
5. Enter the commission rate as a percentage.
6. The form will calculate the commission earned based on the total sales and commission rate.
7. Optionally, list any adjustments or deductions applied to the commission, if necessary.
8. Enter the net payable commission, which is the final amount after adjustments.
9. Optionally, select the date you plan to make the payment for the calculated commission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Statement Period Start Date (1)** (`date`, `Required`): Enter the start date of the earnings period for which you want to generate the commission statement.
* **Statement Period End Date (2)** (`date`, `Required`): Enter the end date of the earnings period.
* **Agent Name (3)** (`text`, `Required`): Enter the name of the salesperson receiving the statement.
* **Total Sales for Period (4)** (`number`, `Required`): Enter the total sales for the period, as a whole number.
* **Commission Rate Percent (5)** (`number`, `Required`): Enter the commission rate as a percentage used to calculate the commission earned.
* **Commission Earned (6)** (`number`, `Required`): This field will display the calculated commission earned based on the total sales and commission rate. You cannot edit this field.
* **Adjustments or Deductions (7)** (`text`, `Optional`): Optionally, list any adjustments or deductions applied to the commission, if necessary.
* **Net Payable (8)** (`number`, `Required`): Enter the final amount after adjustments, calculated based on the commission earned and any adjustments or deductions.
* **Payment Date (9)** (`date`, `Optional`): Optionally, select the date you plan to make the payment for the calculated commission.
