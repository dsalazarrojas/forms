# Game T-Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game T-Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information for a game T-shirt order. It gathers details such as size, color, quantity, payment method, and game details to process the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "game_tshirt_size" field, which is a multiple-choice field with four options: Small, Medium, Large, and X-Large.
2. Next, choose a "color" for your T-shirt from the list of available options: Blue, Red, Green, and Yellow.
3. Enter the "quantity" of T-shirts you would like to order.
4. Select the payment method from "payment_method", which is a multiple-choice field with three options: PayPal, Stripe, and Square.
5. Choose a "payment_app" from "payment_app", which is a multiple-choice field with three options: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay.
6. For "card_number", select the type of credit or debit card you would like to use.
7. Select the expiration month and year for your card from "exp_month" and "exp_year", respectively.
8. Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for your card in "cvv".
9. For "game_title", enter the title of the game you are ordering the T-shirt for.
10. Enter the "game_name" of the game you are ordering.
11. Choose the "game_logo" from the available options.
12. Enter the "order_date" and "exp_date" for the order and expiration date of the card, respectively.
13. Enter the "game_price" of the T-shirt.
14. Finally, select the "order_status" of the order, which is a multiple-choice field with three options: New, In Progress, and Complete.
