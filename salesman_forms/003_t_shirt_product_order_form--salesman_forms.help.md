# T Shirt Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their t-shirt product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information:
	* Fill in your order details in the `t_shirt_order_form` field.
	* If applicable, select your preferred t-shirt size in the `tshirt_size` field.
	* If applicable, select your preferred t-shirt color in the `tshirt_color` field.
2. Fill out your contact information:
	* Enter your name in the `customer_name` field.
	* Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.
	* Enter your phone number in the `customer_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **t_shirt_order_form**: A field for describing or adding any additional information about your order.
* **t_shirt_size**: Select one size: XS, S, M, L, or XL.
* **t_shirt_color**: Select one color: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black, or White.
* **customer_info**: A field for collecting general customer information.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information correctly to ensure that your order is fulfilled accurately.
* If you have any questions or issues, don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you've filled out this form before, you can simply click submit to place your order without needing to fill out the entire form again.
