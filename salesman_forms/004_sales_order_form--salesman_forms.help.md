# Sales Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Order Form is a tool used to collect customer information and order details for sales orders, ensuring accurate processing and excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer information (customer name, email, and phone number).
2. Select the sales person responsible for the order.
3. Enter the order date.
4. Add any comments or notes regarding the order.
5. Enter the total amount for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any other customer information not included in the other fields. Please keep it brief and relevant to the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as they want to be addressed.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are the sales person responsible for this order, 'No' otherwise.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address for correspondence.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Total** (`total`, number, optional): Enter the total amount for the order.

## Tips
* Always double-check the form for accuracy before submission.
* Use the customer's email and phone number for proper communication.
* Keep the order comments concise and focused on the order details.
