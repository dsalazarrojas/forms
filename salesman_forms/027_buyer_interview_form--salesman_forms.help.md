# Buyer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Interview Form is a tool used to gather information from buyers about their preferences and situation to provide a more personalized experience in the home buying process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself.
2. Choose the date of the interview.
3. Select your real estate agent or representative.
4. Choose your current living situation and whether you will be selling your current home.
5. Indicate if this is your first time buying a home.
6. Choose when you want to purchase your home.
7. Select your desired budget range.
8. Confirm your mortgage pre-approval status.
9. Choose the type of property you are interested in buying.
10. Select your preferred neighborhoods or areas.
11. (Optional) Choose any features you must have in your new home.
12. (Optional) Specify the minimum number of bedrooms and bathrooms you need.
13. (Optional) Mention any special needs or considerations.
14. (Optional) Add any concerns or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Select the date of the interview.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Select your real estate agent or representative.
* **Current Living Situation** (`current_home_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of home you currently live in. Options: Single Family Home, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse, Other.
* **Will You Be Selling Current Home** (`selling_current_home`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you will be selling your current home. Options: True, False, Unsure.
* **First Time Home Buyer** (`first_time_buyer`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is your first time buying a home. Options: True, False, Somewhat experienced.
* **Purchase Timeline** (`purchase_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you want to purchase your home. Options: Within 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, Over 1 year.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select your desired budget range. Options: Less than 200000, 200000 - 400000, 400000 - 600000, 600000 - 800000, Over 800000.
* **Mortgage Pre-Approved** (`preapproved`, select_one, required): Confirm your mortgage pre-approval status. Options: True, False, In process.
* **Desired Property Type** (`property_type_wanted`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of property you are interested in buying. Options: Single Family, Condo, Townhouse, Multi-family, Land.
* **Location Preferences** (`location_preferences`, text, required): Enter your preferred neighborhoods or areas.
* **Necessary Features** (`necessary_features`, select_multiple, required): Choose any features you must have in your new home. Options: Garage, Yard, Pool, Updated Kitchen, Fireplace, Basement, View, Other.
* **Bedrooms Needed** (`bedrooms_needed`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bedrooms you need.
* **Bathrooms Needed** (`bathrooms_needed`, number, required): Enter the minimum number of bathrooms you need.
* **Special Needs or Considerations** (`special_needs`, text, required): Enter any special needs or considerations.
* **Concerns or Questions** (`concerns_questions`, text, required): Enter any concerns or questions you have.

Note: Some fields are optional, indicated by the label "required: false".
