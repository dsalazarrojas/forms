# Lead Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Survey is a form designed to collect information about the lead generation process and strategies used by sales representatives and teams.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select Lead Generation Strategies** (id: 4): Select all the strategies that your team or organization uses to generate leads. These include Trade Show, Telemarketing, Cold Calling, Social Media, Referral, Online Advertising, Print Advertising, Other, Email Marketing, and Content Marketing.
2. **Current Sales Process Stage** (id: 2): Enter the current stage of the sales process.
3. **Lead Sources** (id: 3): Select all the sources where your team or organization gets leads from. These include Website, Social Media, Referral, Email, Print, and Other.
4. **Lead Channels** (id: 5): Select all the channels used to generate leads. These include LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Email, and Other.
5. **Lead Conversion Rate** (id: 6): Enter the conversion rate (in percentage) of your leads.
6. **Lead Follow-up** (id: 7): Enter the time frame for lead follow-up.
7. **Lead Value** (id: 8): Enter the value of the lead.
8. **Departmental Lead Generation Comments** (id: 18): Add any notes or comments about the lead generation process for your department.
9. **Sales Representative Information** (id: 10-16): Fill in the contact information for the sales representative, sales team, manager, and territory manager.
10. **Lead Generation Survey Comments** (id: 17): Add any additional comments or notes about the lead generation process.
11. **Lead Generation Survey Date** (id: 19): Enter the date the survey was completed.
12. **Territory** (id: 21): Select the territory for which the lead generation process is being reported.
13. **Status** (id: 22): Choose the status of the lead generation process, such as In Progress, Not Started, or Completed.
14. **Completed by** (id: 23): Enter the person or team that completed the survey.
15. **Completed On** (id: 24): Enter the date the survey was completed.
