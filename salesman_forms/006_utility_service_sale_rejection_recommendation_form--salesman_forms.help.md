# Utility Service Sale Rejection Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture the reasons why a utility service sale was not sold, and to provide recommendations for future sales efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to the reasons why the sale was not sold, and provide any additional comments in the text fields. You can select multiple options in the "Sales Form Section" fields, and one option in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Form Section**: Indicates whether the sales form section was filled out or not. Select "Yes" if the sales form was completed, "No" otherwise.
* **rejection_rationale**: Provide a brief explanation for why the sale was not sold.
* **next_steps**: Provide any next steps or recommended actions for the customer or sales team.
* **internal_recommendations**: Provide any internal recommendations or actions for the sales team.
* **customer_name**: Indicates whether the customer is a new or existing customer. Select "Yes" if the customer is a new customer, "No" otherwise.
* **sales_rep_name**: Enter the name of the sales representative who interacted with the customer.
