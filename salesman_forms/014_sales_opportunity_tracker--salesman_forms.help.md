# Sales Opportunity Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track sales opportunities and manage sales data. It's crucial to fill it out accurately and consistently to maintain a clear and up-to-date sales pipeline.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the lead source (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Referral, Social Media, Other).
2. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
3. Enter the contact phone number.
4. Select the current opportunity status (Open, In Progress, Won, Lost).
5. Choose the deal stage (Qualification, Needs Analysis, Proposal, Decision, Closed Won, Closed Lost).
6. Enter the sales channel (e.g., phone, email, in-person meeting).
7. Enter the sales representative's name.
8. Enter the lead date.
9. Enter the follow-up date.
10. Enter the close date.
11. Enter the close reason.
12. Enter the sales amount.
13. Enter any additional notes about the opportunity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, text, required: false): Enter the source of the lead (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Referral, Social Media, Other).
- **Lead Source Category** (`lead_source_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the lead source (Sales, Marketing, Referral, Social Media, Other).
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email address.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
- **Opportunity Status** (`opportunity_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current opportunity status (Open, In Progress, Won, Lost).
- **Deal Stage** (`deal_stage`, select_one, required: false): Choose the deal stage (Qualification, Needs Analysis, Proposal, Decision, Closed Won, Closed Lost).
- **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, text, required: false): Enter the sales channel (e.g., phone, email, in-person meeting).
- **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, text, required: false): Enter the sales representative's name.
- **Lead Date** (`lead_date`, date, required: false): Enter the lead date.
- **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required: false): Enter the follow-up date.
- **Close Date** (`close_date`, date, required: false): Enter the close date.
- **Close Reason** (`close_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the close reason.
- **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the sales amount.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the opportunity.
