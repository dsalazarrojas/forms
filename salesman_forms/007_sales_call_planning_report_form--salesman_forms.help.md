# Sales Call Planning Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Call Planning Report Form is a tool used to record and plan information related to sales calls with customers. It helps sales teams gather data and insights for sales planning, reporting, and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the week start and end dates for the sales call.
2. Select the sales person responsible for the call.
3. Choose the territory where the call took place.
4. Enter the product name being sold or discussed.
5. Enter the customer's name and phone number.
6. Add any additional notes about the call.
7. Enter the sales manager's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Week Start Date** (`week_start_date`, Date, required): Enter the start date of the sales call week (e.g., Monday of the call week).
  - This field is used to define the start date of the call week.
- **Week End Date** (`week_end_date`, Date, required): Enter the end date of the sales call week (e.g., Sunday of the call week).
  - This field is used to define the end date of the call week.
- **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, Text, required): Enter the name of the sales person who made the sales call.
  - This field is used to identify the sales person responsible for the call.
- **Territory** (`territory`, Text, required): Enter the name of the territory where the call took place.
  - This field is used to identify the territory for the sales call.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the product being sold or discussed.
  - This field is used to track the product related to the sales call.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the customer.
  - This field is used to record the customer's name.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
  - This field is used to store the customer's phone number.
- **Notes** (`notes`, Text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the sales call.
  - This field is used for recording additional information about the call.
- **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`, Text, required): Enter the name of the sales manager who oversaw the call.
  - This field is used to identify the sales manager responsible for the call.
- **Sales Manager Phone** (`sales_manager_phone`, Text, optional): Enter the sales manager's phone number (if applicable).
  - This field is used to store the sales manager's phone number.
- **Sales Manager Email** (`sales_manager_email`, Email, optional): Enter the sales manager's email (if applicable).
  - This field is used to store the sales manager's email.

## Tips
- Please make sure to complete the form for each sales call separately.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
