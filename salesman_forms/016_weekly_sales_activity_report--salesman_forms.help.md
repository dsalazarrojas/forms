# Weekly Sales Activity Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Activity Report is a form used to track sales performance and progress on a weekly basis. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and consistently to provide valuable insights into sales activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the relevant options for each field, ensuring you are honest and accurate in your responses.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are not all applicable.
3.  Double-check your responses for consistency and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rep** (Select One, Required: False)
    *This field asks if you are the representative filling out the form.*
- **Territory** (Select One, Required: False)
    *This field inquires about the territory you are associated with.*
- **Targets vs Actuals** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
    *Choose the targets and actuals that apply to your sales performance.*
- **Challenges** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
    *List any challenges you have faced during the week.*
- **Pipeline Progress** (Select One, Required: False)
    *Indicate your pipeline progress, whether you are making progress or facing obstacles.*
- **Week Start Date** (Date, Required: False)
    *Enter the date from which your week started.*

Note: This form is not intended to be duplicated, and it's best to fill it out once a week. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
