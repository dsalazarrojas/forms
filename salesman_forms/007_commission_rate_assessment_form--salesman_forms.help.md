# Commission Rate Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess and adjust commission rates for sales representatives. It is used to capture detailed information about the commission rate assessment, including the assessment date, rate type, proposed rate, and proposed effective date.

The form is intended to be filled out by sales managers or administrators to evaluate and adjust commission rates as needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide Assessment Information: Enter a brief description of the commission rate assessment.
2. Enter Assessment Date: Enter the date of the commission rate assessment.
3. Enter Assessment ID: Enter a unique reference number for the assessment.
4. Enter Sales Representative Information: Provide details about the sales representative, including their name and employee identification number.
5. Enter Territory Region: Enter the assigned sales territory or region for the sales representative.
6. Enter Current Rate Information: Enter details about the current commission structure and rate.
7. Enter Current Commission Rate: Enter the current commission percentage.
8. Enter Current Rate Effective Date: Enter the date when the current rate became effective.
9. Enter Sales Performance Data: Enter total sales volume last quarter and year-to-date in USD.
10. Evaluate Performance: Evaluate the sales representative's performance using the provided options (Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations, Needs Improvement).
11. Enter Proposed Rate Information: Enter details about the proposed commission rate change.
12. Enter Proposed Commission Rate: Enter the new proposed commission percentage.
13. Enter Proposed Effective Date: Enter the date when the new rate should take effect.
14. Enter Rate Change Type: Select the type of rate adjustment (Increase, Decrease, No Change, Restructure).
15. Enter Change Justification: Enter the reason for the proposed rate change.
16. Determine Tier Structure Change: Indicate if the tier structure is being modified.
17. Enter Approval Workflow: Enter the approval status and routing.
18. Enter Manager Name: Enter the name of the requesting manager.
19. Enter Approval Status: Select the current approval status (Pending Review, Approved, Denied, Requires Revision).
20. Enter Additional Notes: Enter any additional comments or considerations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Information**: A brief description of the commission rate assessment.
* **Assessment Date**: The date of the commission rate assessment.
* **Assessment ID**: A unique reference number for the assessment.
* **Sales Representative Information**: Details about the sales representative.
* **Sales Representative Name**: The full name of the sales representative.
* **Sales Representative ID**: The employee identification number of the sales representative.
* **Territory Region**: The assigned sales territory or region.
* **Current Rate Information**: Details about the current commission structure and rate.
* **Current Commission Rate**: The current commission percentage.
* **Current Rate Effective Date**: The date when the current rate became effective.
* **Sales Last Quarter**: The total sales volume last quarter in USD.
* **Sales Year to Date**: The total sales volume YTD in USD.
* **Quota Attainment**: The percentage of quota achieved.
* **Performance Rating**: An evaluation of the sales representative's performance.
* **Proposed Rate Information**: Details about the proposed commission rate change.
* **Proposed Commission Rate**: The new proposed commission percentage.
* **Proposed Effective Date**: The date when the new rate should take effect.
* **Rate Change Type**: The type of rate adjustment (Increase, Decrease, No Change, Restructure).
* **Change Justification**: The reason for the proposed rate change.
* **Tier Structure Change**: Whether the tier structure is being modified.
* **Approval Workflow**: The approval status and routing.
* **Manager Name**: The name of the requesting manager.
* **Approval Status**: The current approval status.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or considerations.
