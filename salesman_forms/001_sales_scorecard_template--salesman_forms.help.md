# Sales Scorecard Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Scorecard Template is a form designed to help sales teams track and measure progress, providing a comprehensive view of sales performance. It collects essential information for sales scorecards, enabling sales representatives to monitor their progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sales Scorecard Template, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for the sales scorecard from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the context of your sales activities.
2. Enter a brief description of the sales scorecard, providing context and background information.
3. Select the relevant sales scorecard options that apply to your situation.
4. Record the progress tracking dates for each sales scorecard.
5. Enter the progress tracking values for each scorecard.
6. Optional: Enter any additional notes or comments for each sales scorecard.
7. Review and submit the form to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: A dropdown menu where you select the category for the sales scorecard. This helps us understand the context and relevance of your sales activities.
  * Options: Salesman Forms, Other, Unknown
- **Description**: A text field where you enter a brief description of the sales scorecard, providing background information and context.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Sales Scorecard**: A dropdown menu where you select the relevant sales scorecards that apply to your situation.
  * Options: Option-1, Option-2, Option-3
  * Required: Yes
- **Progress Tracking**: A date field where you record the progress tracking dates for each sales scorecard.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Yes
- **Progress Tracking 2**: A time field where you record the progress tracking times for each sales scorecard.
  * Type: Time
  * Required: No
- **Sales Scorecard 2**: A note field where you enter any additional notes or comments for each sales scorecard.
  * Type: Note
  * Required: No
- **Progress Tracking 3**: A number field where you enter the progress tracking values for each scorecard.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
- **Progress Tracking 4**: Another number field for additional progress tracking values.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
- **Progress Tracking 5**: A text field where you enter any further progress tracking information.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Progress Tracking 6**: An email field where you enter progress tracking email addresses.
  * Type: Email
  * Required: No
- **Progress Tracking 7** to **Progress Tracking 20**: A series of text fields for additional progress tracking information.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No

**Tips**
- Make sure to select the correct category and sales scorecard options to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
- Be as specific as possible when entering progress tracking dates and values to help us understand your progress.
- Use the notes field to provide any additional context or explanations for each sales scorecard.
- Review and submit the form regularly to save your progress and receive updates.
