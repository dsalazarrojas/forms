# End Of Day Sales Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Day Sales Report Form is used for daily sales reporting, capturing sales data for each salesperson, region, district, customer, and product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "New Entry" button to start a new form entry.
*   Choose the date and time of the sales event from the calendar picker for the sales_date field.
*   Input the name of the region for which the sales was made, in the region field.
*   Input the district name for the sales event, in the district field.
*   Input the name of the customer for the sales event, in the customer field.
*   Enter the sales amount in dollars in the sales_amount field.
*   Input the name of the product sold, in the product_name field.
*   Input the product ID, if applicable, in the product_id field.
*   Select the product category from the options provided, in the product_category field.
*   Enter the selling price of the product in the product_price field.
*   Enter the quantity sold in the product_quantity field.
*   Select the sales status from the options provided, in the sales_status field.
*   Provide a reason for the sales status, if applicable, in the sales_status_reason field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **sales_date** (Date): Enter the date of the sales event.
*   **sales_time** (Time): Enter the time of the sales event.
*   **region** (Text): Input the name of the region for which the sales was made.
*   **district** (Text): Input the district name for the sales event.
*   **sales_person** (Text): Input the name of the salesperson who made the sale.
*   **territory** (Text): Input the name of the territory for the sales event.
*   **customer** (Text): Input the name of the customer for the sales event.
*   **sales_amount** (Text): Enter the sales amount in dollars.
*   **product_name** (Text): Input the name of the product sold.
*   **product_id** (Text): Input the product ID, if applicable.
*   **product_category** (Select One): Select the product category from the options provided.
*   **product_price** (Number): Enter the selling price of the product.
*   **product_quantity** (Number): Enter the quantity sold.
*   **sales_status** (Select One): Select the sales status from the options provided.
*   **sales_status_reason** (Text): Provide a reason for the sales status, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Ensure you input accurate and up-to-date sales data for each sales event.
*   Complete all required fields to ensure proper recording and reporting of sales performance.
*   Use the provided options for product category and sales status to select the correct values.
*   Enter the sales amount and product price in the correct format (e.g., dollars for sales amount and cents for product price).
