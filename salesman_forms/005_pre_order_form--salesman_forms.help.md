# Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Order Form is used for salesmen to collect contact information from customers interested in pre-ordering a product or service. It helps salesmen to gather essential details about the customer, enabling them to follow up with them and facilitate the pre-order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pre Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Pre Order Form" title field with a brief description of the pre-order opportunity.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "customer name" field.
3. Provide the customer's email address in the "customer email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre Order Form** (`title`, required/optional: title): Enter a brief description of the pre-order opportunity.
* **Customer** (`customer`, required/optional: no): Enter the customer's first and last name(s).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required/optional: no): Enter the customer’s full name as they would like to be referred to (e.g., John Doe).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, required/optional: no): Enter the customer's valid email address to contact them with updates and information.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, required/optional: no): Enter the customer's phone number to contact them with updates and information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective communication with the customer.
* Double-check the form fields before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your sales team for guidance.
