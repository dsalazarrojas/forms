# streetwear_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The streetwear_order_form_template is a form used to collect information for streetwear orders. This form is designed to gather essential details from customers to ensure accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, following the correct format (e.g., "user@example.com").
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your shirt size from the options provided in the "Shirt Size" field.
6. Enter the date you would like to place your order in the "Order Date" field.
7. Enter the time you would like to place your order in the "Order Time" field.
8. Enter any additional order notes or instructions in the "Order Notes" field.
9. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
10. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
11. Enter your city in the "City" field.
12. Enter your state or province in the "State/Province" field.
13. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
14. Enter your preferred store manager's name in the "Store Manager" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, which will help us get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will help us stay in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred shirt size from the options provided.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to place your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions about your order.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, text, optional): Enter your preferred store manager's name.
