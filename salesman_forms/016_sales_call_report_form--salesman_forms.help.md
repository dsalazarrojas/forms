# Sales Call Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on sales calls made by sales managers. The information collected in this form will be used to inform future sales strategies and improve the overall sales process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **sales call date** field with the date of the sales call.
2. Enter the **sales call time** in the format HH:MM:SS or 24-hour format.
3. Enter the **contact name** as the name of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
4. Enter the **contact phone** number as the phone number of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
5. Enter the **contact email** address of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
6. Enter the name of the **sales manager** who made the sales call.
7. Enter the name of the **customer company** involved in the sales call.
8. Enter a brief description of the **sales call notes**, including any notable points or issues discussed during the call.
9. Select the **follow up action** required for the sales call, either **Follow Up** or **No Follow Up**.
10. Enter the date for the **follow up date** if you have selected **Follow Up**.
11. Enter the time for the **follow up time** in the format HH:MM:SS or 24-hour format.
12. Select the **sales call outcome**, either **Successful**, **Unsuccessful**, or **No Sale**.
13. Enter any additional **sales call outcome notes**.
14. Enter the **next step** to take on the sales call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sales call date**: Enter the date of the sales call in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **sales call time**: Enter the time of the sales call in the format HH:MM:SS or 24-hour format.
* **contact name**: Enter the name of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
* **contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
* **contact email**: Enter the email address of the person you spoke with during the sales call.
* **sales manager**: Enter the name of the sales manager who made the sales call.
* **customer company**: Enter the name of the customer company involved in the sales call.
* **sales call notes**: Enter a brief description of the sales call, including any notable points or issues discussed during the call.
* **follow up action**: Select whether to **Follow Up** or **No Follow Up** is required for the sales call.
* **follow up date**: Enter the date for the follow up if **Follow Up** is selected.
* **follow up time**: Enter the time for the follow up in the format HH:MM:SS or 24-hour format.
* **sales call outcome**: Select the outcome of the sales call, either **Successful**, **Unsuccessful**, or **No Sale**.
* **sales call outcome notes**: Enter any additional notes on the sales call outcome.
* **next step**: Enter the next step to take on the sales call.
