# Discovery Call Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discovery Call Application Form is a tool designed to help sales team members record and track details about discovery calls, including schedules, contacts, and company information. This form aims to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the sales process by providing a centralized and organized way to gather and store data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form data section with a brief description of the discovery call.
2. Enter your name and contact details in the "Name," "Email," "Phone," and "Company Name" fields.
3. Choose a preferred time for the discovery call from the "Schedule Discovery" dropdown menu.
4. Select a schedule for the recurring discovery call from the "Schedule Discovery Recurring" dropdown menu.
5. Update the lead status and next steps as per the discovery call outcome.
6. Enter the company's contact person's name, email, phone, and other relevant details.
7. Select the lead source from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose the company's industry from the "Company Industry" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the company's address and website.
10. Add any additional notes about the discovery call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_data** (Form Data): A field for a brief description of the discovery call.
* **Name** (Name): Enter your name and contact details.
* **email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person you'll be contacting.
* **phone** (Phone): Enter the phone number of the person you'll be contacting.
* **Discovery Time** (Discovery Time): Select the time for the discovery call.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time): Select the preferred time for the discovery call.
* **Schedule Discovery** (Schedule Discovery): Choose a schedule for the discovery call from the dropdown menu.
* **Schedule Discovery Recurring** (Schedule Discovery Recurring): Select a schedule for the recurring discovery call from the dropdown menu.
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps): Enter any next steps or actions required from the discovery call.
* **Lead Status** (Lead Status): Update the lead status as per the discovery call outcome.
* **Lead Source** (Lead Source): Select the source of the lead from the dropdown menu.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To): Enter the name of the salesperson assigned to the lead.
* **Company Name** (Company Name): Enter the name of the company.
* **Company Size** (Company Size): Select the company size from the dropdown menu.
* **Company Industry** (Company Industry): Select the company's industry from the dropdown menu.
* **Company Address** (Company Address): Enter the company's address.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Website** (Company Website): Enter the company's website.
* **LinkedIn Link** (LinkedIn Link): Enter the link to the company's LinkedIn profile.
* **Twitter Link** (Twitter Link): Enter the link to the company's Twitter profile.
* **Facebook Link** (Facebook Link): Enter the link to the company's Facebook profile.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional notes about the discovery call.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned To): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the salesperson.
* **Lead Source** (Lead Source): Select the source of the lead from the dropdown menu.
