# Sales Advisor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Advisor Survey is a tool used to gather information from sales advisors about their sales strategies, performance, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate answers based on your knowledge.
2. For each question, select the most relevant answer from the available options or enter a text response as required.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information to share before completing the form.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your sales manager or a colleague for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Advisor Survey** (1, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled.
* **Sales Strategy** (2, select_one, required: false): Select one of the two options provided to indicate your current sales strategy. Please choose the one that best describes your approach.
* **Training Feedback** (3, text, required: false): Describe your experience with sales training programs. Provide any feedback or suggestions you have for improvements.
* **Deals Closed** (4, number, required: false): Enter the number of deals you've closed recently. This will help track your sales performance.
* **Sales Targets** (5, number, required: false): Enter your current sales targets or goals. This will help the sales team understand your performance expectations.
* **Meeting Frequency** (6, select_one, required: false): Select one of the two options to indicate how frequently you meet with clients or prospects. This will help the sales team plan meetings and follow-ups.
* **Sales Channel** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the two options to indicate your primary sales channel. This will help the sales team understand your sales approach.
* **Useful Tool** (8, select_one, required: false): Select one of the two options to indicate a useful tool or resource you've found helpful in your sales work. This will help the sales team identify valuable tools and materials.
* **Favorite Book** (9, text, required: false): Enter the title of a book that you've found helpful in your sales work. This is an optional field for sharing personal preferences.
* **Sales Experience** (10, number, required: false): Enter your number of years of sales experience. This will help the sales team understand your background and expertise.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your sales manager or a colleague for clarification.
* This form is intended to be completed once or multiple times, depending on the context and goals of the sales team.
