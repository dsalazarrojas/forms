# Lead Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Qualification Form is used to gather and track information about potential leads, ensuring that sales representatives have a clear understanding of the lead's status and requirements within the sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the lead's information, including the lead name, company name, contact phone number, and email address.
2. Select the lead's company type (small business, medium business, large business).
3. Choose the lead's source (trade show, online search, referral).
4. Determine the lead's company size (10 employees, 20 employees, 50 employees, 100 employees).
5. Select the lead's product interest (software, hardware, service).
6. Identify the lead's company stage (start up, growth stage, mature stage).
7. Select the lead's company location.
8. Choose the lead's decision maker and industry.
9. Determine the lead's sales stage (new, qualified, rejected).
10. Select the lead's sales reach (qualified, not reached).
11. If necessary, assign the lead to a sales representative and select the owner of the lead.
12. Finally, select the lead's status (new, qualified, rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Name**: (`lead_name`, text, required)
Enter the lead's name as it appears on file or in any communication with the lead.
* **Company Name**: (`company_name`, text, not required)
Enter the name of the company the lead represents.
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required)
Enter the lead's contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: (`email_address`, email, not required)
Enter the lead's email address.
* **Company Website**: (`company_website`, text, not required)
Enter the lead's company website.
* **Company Type**: (`company_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of company the lead represents (small business, medium business, large business).
* **Lead Source**: (`lead_source`, select_multiple, required)
Select the source of the lead (trade show, online search, referral).
* **Company Size**: (`company_size`, select_one, required)
Select the size of the company the lead represents (10 employees, 20 employees, 50 employees, 100 employees).
* **Product Interest**: (`product_interest`, select_multiple, not required)
Select the product(s) the lead is interested in (software, hardware, service).
* **Company Stage**: (`company_stage`, select_one, required)
Select the stage of the company the lead represents (start up, growth stage, mature stage).
* **Company Location**: (`company_location`, text, required)
Enter the location of the company.
* **Decision Making**: (`decision_making`, select_multiple, required)
Select who the lead's decision maker is (decision maker, department).
* **Company Industry**: (`company_industry`, select_multiple, required)
Select the industry of the company the lead represents (finance, marketing).
* **Sales Channel**: (`sales_channel`, select_multiple, not required)
Select the sales channel(s) for the lead (phone, email, mail).
* **Lead Qualify**: (`lead_qualify`, select_one, required)
Select whether the lead is qualified or unqualified.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, text, not required)
Enter any additional notes about the lead.
* **Lead Qualified**: (`lead_qualified`, select_one, required)
Select whether the lead has been qualified or not.
* **Lead Rejected**: (`lead_rejected`, select_one, required)
Select whether the lead has been rejected or not.
* **Lead Assigned To**: (`lead_assigned_to`, text, not required)
Select the sales representative assigned to the lead.
* **Lead Owner**: (`lead_owner`, text, not required)
Enter the owner of the lead.
* **Lead Status**: (`lead_status`, select_one, required)
Select the current status of the lead (new, qualified, rejected).
* **Lead Stage**: (`lead_stage`, select_one, required)
Select the current stage of the lead (new, qualified, rejected).
* **Sales Stage**: (`sales_stage`, select_one, required)
Select the sales stage of the lead (new, qualified, rejected).
* **Sales Reach**: (`sales_reach`, select_multiple, not required)
Select whether the lead has been reached or not by sales (qualified, not reached).
* **Lead Qualified**: (this is a duplicate field and will be removed from the form)

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure that leads are properly categorized and assigned.
- Only complete the form for leads that are new to you as a sales representative.
- If you're unsure about a field, leave it blank or consult with a colleague or supervisor.
- Review this guide regularly to stay up-to-date on the current status of the lead and make adjustments as necessary.
