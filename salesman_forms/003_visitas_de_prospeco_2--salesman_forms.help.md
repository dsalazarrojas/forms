# visitas_de_prospeco_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about sales visits, including services provided and overall evaluation of each visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "visitas_a_lojas" field with a description of the sales visit.
2. Evaluate the services provided during the visit in the "servicos" field.
3. Provide an overall evaluation of the visit in the "avaliacoes_de_parcializacoes" field.
4. Answer questions related to reports and partners in the remaining fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **visitas_a_lojas** (Text, Required): Provide a description of the sales visit.
* **avaliacoes_de_parcializacoes** (Text, Required): Evaluate the overall performance of the visit (e.g., good, bad, excellent).
* **servicos** (Text, Optional): List the services provided during the visit.
* **relatorios** (Text, Optional): Describe any reports generated or discussed during the visit.
* **parceios** (Text, Optional): Identify any partners or collaborators involved in the visit.
* **servicos_e_prospeco** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" if you provided services related to prospecting, "No" otherwise.
* **avaliacoes** (Number, Required): Use numbers to evaluate the quality of the services provided (e.g., 1-5).
* **visitas_e_servicos** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" if you visited the customer to discuss or demonstrate a service, "No" otherwise.
* **visita_1**, **visita_2**, **visita_3**, **visitas** (Text, Optional): Describe any specific visit events or details.
* **servico**, **parceio**, **servicos**, **relatorio** (Text, Optional): These fields are identical and seem to be duplicates, please use with caution.
* **visitas_de_prospeco_1**, **visitas_de_prospeco_2** (Text, Optional): These fields seem to be unused and can be ignored.
* **visita_e_servico** (Text, Optional): Describe any visits related to services.
* **parceio_1, **parceio_2, **parceio_3, **parceio_4, **parceio_5, **parceio_6** (Text, Optional): These fields seem to be redundant and can be ignored.

Note: The fields with optional labels are not required and can be filled if relevant.
