# sales_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales form is designed to collect information on sales performance and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that you have permission to access the form and understand its purpose within your organization.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Performance** (1, note, required/optional): A free-text field where you can briefly describe the sales performance.
* **Sales Target** (2, number, required/optional): Enter your sales target figure.
* **Sales Result** (3, select_one, required/optional): Select the outcome of your sales efforts from the options provided.
* **Sales Rating** (4, number, required/optional): Rate your sales performance on a scale suitable for your organization.
* **Sales Comments** (5, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments about your sales activities.
* **Sales Date** (6, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your sales activities.
* **Sales Time** (7, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your sales activities.
* **Email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the sales staff.
* **Phone** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the sales staff.
* **Sales Manager** (10, select_one, required/optional): Select the sales manager responsible for the sales activities.
* **Sales Region** (11, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the sales region where the activities took place.
* **Sales Channel** (12, select_one, required/optional): Select the sales channel used.
* **Sales Product** (13, select_one, required/optional): Select the product involved in the sales activities.
* **Sales Customer** (14, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the customers involved in the sales activities.
* **Sales Area** (15, select_one, required/optional): Select the sales area covered.
* **Sales Type** (16, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of sales activities.
* **Sales Location** (17, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the locations where sales activities took place.
* **Sales Status** (18, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the sales activities.
* **Sales Notes** (19, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the sales activities.
* **Sales Form Completed** (20, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the form was completed.
* **Sales Form Completed By** (21, select_one, required/optional): Select who completed the form.
* **Sales Form Completed Date** (22, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Sales Form Completed Time** (23, time, required/optional): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Sales Form Completed Time Ago** (24, time, required/optional): Indicate the time elapsed since form completion.
* **Form Version** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the version of the form.
