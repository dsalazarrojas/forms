# Field Day T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Day T Shirt Order Form is used to collect information from customers to process their orders for Field Day T Shirts. It is essential that the form is completed accurately to ensure that orders are fulfilled correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), as they are necessary to process your order.
2. Select your preferred T-shirt size from the list provided.
3. Enter your name as it should be displayed on your order receipt.
4. Fill in your email address in the correct format (example: john.doe@example.com).
5. Provide a valid phone number.
6. Select a payment method from the available options (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
7. Optionally, enter any notes or special instructions for your order.
8. Select "Submit" to complete and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Day T Shirt Order Form (\*)**: This field is used to display the title of the form. Please leave this field as is.
* **Name (\*)**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on your order receipt.
* **T-shirt Size (\*)**: Select the size of the T-shirt you would like to order. Please choose from the options: XS, S, M, L, XL.
* **Quantity (\*)**: Enter the number of T-shirts you would like to order.
* **Price (\*)**: Enter the price of the T-shirt you would like to order.
* **Email (\*)**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication and order updates.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number. This will be used for additional contact if needed.
* **Payment Method (\*)**: Select a payment method from the options: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order if needed.
* **Submit**: Select this option to complete and submit your order.
