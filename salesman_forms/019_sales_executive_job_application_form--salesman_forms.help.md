# Sales Executive Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for sales executives who wish to apply for a job within the organization's sales team. It gathers essential information to consider their application and potential fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Contact Name** field.
2. Choose the category of the job you are applying for from the **Job Category** dropdown menu.
3. If the job category is not listed, you can enter it in the **Other Job Category** field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, respectively.
5. You can attach your resume in the **Resume** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in communication with our team.
### Job Category
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of job you are applying for (Software Sales, Sales, or Service).
* **Other Job Category** (`job_category_other`, text, optional): If not listed above, enter your own job category.
### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Attachment
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach your resume to your application.
