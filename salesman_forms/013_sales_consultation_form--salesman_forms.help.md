# Sales Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Consultation Form is designed to help sales team members gather information for sales consultations. It is meant to be used for sales consultations that do not involve sensitive or proprietary information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First name** (Field 1) and **Last name** (Field 2) fields with your name.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** (Field 3) field.
3. Fill out your phone number in the **Phone** (Field 4) field.
4. Provide the date of the consultation in the **Consultation Date** (Field 5) field.
5. Select the time of the consultation in the **Consultation Time** (Field 6) field.
6. Describe your sales strategy in the **Sales Strategy** (Field 7) field.
7. Select the services you will offer during the consultation from the **Services Offered** (Field 8) options.
8. Provide any additional information about the consultation in the **Additional Info** (Field 9) field.
9. Select the sales team member to whom the consultation will be assigned from the **Assigned to** (Field 10) options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (Field 1)**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name (Field 2)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email (Field 3)**: Enter your work email address to ensure we can reach you.
* **Phone (Field 4)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Consultation Date (Field 5)**: Select the date of the consultation.
* **Consultation Time (Field 6)**: Select the time of the consultation.
* **Sales Strategy (Field 7)**: Describe your approach and strategy for the sale.
* **Services Offered (Field 8)**: Select the services you will offer during the consultation, such as Sales Advice and Sales Strategies.
* **Additional Info (Field 9)**: Provide any other relevant information about the consultation.
* **Assigned to (Field 10)**: Select the sales team member to whom the consultation will be assigned.
