# Used Car Sales Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about the vehicle they are interested in purchasing, including customer details, vehicle information, contact details, and sales specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information in the "Customer info" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's information in the "Vehicle info" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your salesperson's number from the "Salesperson" field.
7. Enter the sale date in the "Sale date" field.
8. Enter the sale time in the "Sale time" field.
9. Add any relevant notes about the sale in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales form**: This is the main field for entering customer comments or feedback about the vehicle.
* **Customer info**: Enter the customer's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle info**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact**: Enter your name, title, and any other contact information.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address so that the customer can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the customer can contact you.
* **Salesperson**: Select your salesperson's number to associate with the sale.
* **Sale date**: Enter the date of the sale in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Sale time**: Enter the time of the sale in the format hh:mm.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the sale, such as the customer's feedback or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the customer's data is recorded correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your sales team for guidance.
* The form is designed to be used in conjunction with other sales tools and systems to track customer interactions and vehicle sales.
