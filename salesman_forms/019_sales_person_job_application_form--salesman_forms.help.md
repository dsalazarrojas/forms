# sales_person_job_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in being hired as sales persons within our company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **contact information**, including your email address and phone number.
2. Provide a brief description of your **contact information address**.
3. Share your **work experience** relevant to sales roles.
4. List your **sales experience** and any notable achievements.
5. Specify your **sales goals** and any targets you've accomplished.
6. Choose your **education level**, from High school or equivalent to Some other.
7. Select the fields of study you've completed for your education level.
8. Briefly describe your **work history**, including any previous work experiences.
9. Indicate your **availability**, selecting the days you are available to work.
10. Choose the **sales tools** you are proficient with, such as CRM or Sales Navigator.
11. Select the languages you speak fluently.
12. Choose the specific **job title** you are applying for.
13. Provide the name of the **company** you are currently working with or interested in working with.
14. Enter your **work phone number** for contact purposes.
15. Enter your **work email** for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact details for us to reach out to you.
* **2. Contact Information Address** (`contact_information_address`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your contact address to help us reach you.
* **3. Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Share your relevant work experience that would help in a sales role.
* **4. Sales Experience** (`sales_experience`, text, required): List your sales experience, including any notable sales achievements.
* **5. Sales Goals** (`sales_goal`, text, required): Specify any sales targets or goals you've accomplished.
* **6. Education Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose your highest level of education, such as High school or equivalent, Some College, or Some other.
* **7. Fields of Study** (`education_fields_of_study`, text, required): Select the fields of study relevant to your education level.
* **8. Work History** (`work_history`, text, required): Briefly describe your previous work experiences that are relevant to sales roles.
* **9. Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Indicate the days you are available to work, such as Monday, Tuesday, or None.
* **10. Sales Tools** (`sales_tools`, select_multiple, required): Choose the sales tools you are proficient with, like CRM or Sales Navigator.
* **11. Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Select the languages you speak fluently, such as English, Spanish, or Chinese.
* **12. Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Specify the job title you are applying for.
* **13. Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are currently or interested in working with.
* **14. Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **15. Work Email** (`work_email`, email, required): Enter your work email for communication.
* **16. Home Phone** (`home_phone`, text, optional): Enter your home phone number for additional contact options.

This guide is designed to assist users in filling out the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that they provide the necessary information for a thorough consideration in the hiring process.
