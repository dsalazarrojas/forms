# Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify information about vendors for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address and business phone number.
3. Fill in all relevant fields, especially if there's a hint that you need to fill in for the form to be valid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **VENDOR REGISTRATION FORM (1)** (`text`, not required): This field is the title of the form.
* **first_name (2)** (`text`, not required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name (3)** (`text`, not required): Enter your last name.
* **email (4)** (`email`, not required): Enter your business email address. (Please enter a valid email address.)
* **business_phone (5)** (`text`, not required): Enter your business phone number.
* **business_email (6)** (`email`, not required): Enter your business email address (if different from your personal email).
* **company_name (7)** (`text`, not required): Enter your company name.
* **contact_person (8)** (`text`, not required): Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
* **address_line_1 (9)** (`text`, not required): Enter your company's address line 1.
* **address_line_2 (10)** (`text`, not required): Enter your company's address line 2.
* **city (11)** (`text`, not required): Enter your city.
* **state (12)** (`select_one`, not required): Check 'Yes' for your company's state status (it's unclear what this option is checking for).
* **zip (13)** (`text`, not required): Enter your zip code.
* **country (14)** (`text`, not required): Enter your company's country.
* **description (15)** (`note`, not required): A text area for any additional comments or notes about your company.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate vendor information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your sales manager or the system administrator for assistance.
* Be cautious with the "state" field as it may not be self-explanatory.
