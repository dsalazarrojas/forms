# Sales Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Onboarding Assessment Form is a tool used by the sales team to evaluate the skills and knowledge of new team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "onboarding_assessment_form" page.
2. Fill in the required fields as best as you can.
3. Review your answers and submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Assessment Form** (onboarding_assessment_form, Note, Required: False):
This is a placeholder page for the onboarding form.
* **Organisation Data** (organisation_data, Text, Required: False):
 Enter the details about your current or previous sales organisation (company).
* **Sales Data** (sales_data, Text, Required: False):
 Enter any relevant sales data that you think would be helpful for the assessment.
* **Product Data** (product_data, Select One, Required: False):
 Select the products you're familiar with:
	+ Product 1
	+ Product 2
	+ Product 3
* **Sales Process** (sales_process, Text, Required: False):
 Describe your experience with the sales process, including any areas where you need improvement.
* **Sales Strategy** (sales_strategy, Text, Required: False):
 Share your sales strategy and approach.
* **Sales Tactics** (sales_tactics, Text, Required: False):
 Describe your sales tactics and techniques.
* **Sales Tech Stack** (sales_tech_stack, Text, Required: False):
 List your experience with sales tech tools and platforms.

## Tips
* Please answer as accurately and truthfully as possible.
* This form is intended for internal use only. Do not share it with external parties.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
