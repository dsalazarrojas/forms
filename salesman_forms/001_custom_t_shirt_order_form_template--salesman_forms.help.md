# Custom T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for salesmen to take orders for custom T-Shirts. It collects customer information, order details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information fields, including name, email, phone, address, city, state, and zip.
2. Select the type of T-Shirt you want to order (Graphic T-Shirt, Hoodie, or Tank Top).
3. Choose the color of the T-Shirt from the options provided.
4. Select the size of the T-Shirt from the options provided.
5. Enter the quantity of T-Shirts you want to order.
6. Add any additional design information or notes about the order in the T-Shirt Design field.
7. Select the payment method you want to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
8. Enter any additional notes about the order in the Order Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (text): Enter the customer's name.
* **T-Shirt Type** (select): Choose the type of T-Shirt you want to order (Graphic T-Shirt, Hoodie, or Tank Top).
* **Color** (select): Choose the color of the T-Shirt from the options provided (Blue, Red, or Green).
* **Size** (select): Choose the size of the T-Shirt from the options provided (S, M, or L).
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of T-Shirts you want to order.
* **T-Shirt Design** (note): Add any additional design information or notes about the order.
* **Order Notes** (text): Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Payment Method** (select): Choose the payment method you want to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address** (text): Enter the customer's address.
* **City** (text): Enter the customer's city.
* **State** (text): Enter the customer's state.
* **Zip** (text): Enter the customer's zip code.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the order is complete.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sales team for assistance.
