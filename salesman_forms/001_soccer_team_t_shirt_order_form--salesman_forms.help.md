# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form is a sales tool for the soccer team to collect orders and payment details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Order Form" page to provide basic details about your order.
2. On the "Contact Information" page, fill in your name and contact information.
3. On the "Payment Information" page, enter your payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**:
	+ <label> (Order Form)</label> 
		- This is the main page to describe your order. You can enter any notes or comments related to your order.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ <label> (Contact Name)</label> 
		- Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	+ <label> (Contact Email)</label> 
		- Enter your email address where you can be reached.
	+ <label> (Contact Phone)</label> 
		- Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Payment Information**:
	+ <label> (Payment Method)</label> 
		- Select the payment method you want to use (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).
	+ <label> (Card Number)</label> 
		- Enter your credit card number for credit card payments.
	+ <label> (CVV)</label> 
		- Enter the security code (CVV) found on your credit card for credit card payments.
	+ <label> (Expiry Date)</label> 
		- Enter the expiration date of your credit card for credit card payments.
	+ <label> (Zip Code)</label> 
		- Enter your zip code if you're using a credit card.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* For any issues with this form, contact the soccer team's administration.
* Review your details carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
