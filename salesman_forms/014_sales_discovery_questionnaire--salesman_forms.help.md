# Sales Discovery Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales discovery questionnaire is a tool used to gain a better understanding of customer needs and preferences. Its primary goal is to gather information that will help drive sales and revenue growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing lead details, including the lead's name, contact information, and any relevant background data.
2. Evaluate the lead's priorities and categorize them as needed.
3. Determine the buying context, describing the circumstances surrounding the sale.
4. Indicate the lead status, whether it's active or inactive.
5. Confirm whether the lead is a source of new business or not.
6. Select the lead category, which may be a yes or no answer.
7. Record the details of the first call or interaction with the customer.
8. Enter a numerical value for the lead, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Details (Lead Details)**: Enter the name, contact information, and any relevant background data about the lead.
* **Lead Priorities (Lead Priorities)**: Indicate the priorities of the lead, such as urgent, low-priority, or high-priority.
* **Buying Context (Buying Context)**: Describe the circumstances surrounding the sale, including the customer's motivation and expectations.
* **Lead Status (Lead Status)**: Choose whether the lead is active or inactive.
* **Lead Source (Lead Source)**: Confirm whether the lead is a source of new business or not.
* **Lead Category (Lead Category)**: Choose whether the lead belongs to a specific category, such as yes or no.
* **First Call Details (First Call Details)**: Record the details of the first call or interaction with the customer.
* **Lead Value (Lead Value)**: Enter a numerical value for the lead, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete each field accurately and completely.
- Avoid duplicating efforts by verifying that the lead data doesn't already exist in the system.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, reach out to your sales team for guidance.
- Keep the form up to date by regularly reviewing and revising the fields and their explanations.
