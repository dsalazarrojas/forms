# Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shirt Order Form is a tool used by sales representatives to gather essential information from customers when placing orders for shirts. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately, improving the efficiency and quality of the sales process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form's fields to understand what information is required for each step.
*   Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Info (1)** (`text`, optional): This field is used to capture general information about the customer. Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
*   **Order Type (2)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of order being placed. Select from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the order type.
*   **Order Date (3)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date on which the order is to be placed.
*   **Order Time (4)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time on which the order is to be placed.
*   **Customer Name (5)** (`text`, optional): Enter the customer's name.
*   **Order Total (6)** (`number`, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
*   **Customer Email (7)** (`email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Phone (8)** (`text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Order Notes (9)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about the order.

## Tips

*   Review each field to ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately.
*   Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   Take your time to fill in the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
