# screen_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The screen_printing_order_form is designed to capture sales order information from customers for internal order tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and email.
2. Enter your phone number for contact information.
3. Select the T-Shirt type and color that you are interested in ordering.
4. Fill in your address details, including the city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter the order total.
6. Choose the payment method for this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required)
Your name is required for order tracking purposes.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
Please enter your email address accurately for order confirmation and communication purposes.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required)
Enter your phone number for sales team to contact you with order updates or inquiries.

### 4. **T-Shirt Type** (`tshirt_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the T-Shirt type you are ordering from the options: A, B, C, D.

### 5. **T-Shirt Color** (`tshirt_color`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the T-Shirt color you are interested in: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.

### 6. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required)
Please re-enter your name for order tracking purposes.

### 7. **Address** (`address`, `text`, required)
Enter your address for shipping and delivery purposes.

### 8. **City** (`city`, `text`, required)
Enter the city of your address.

### 9. **State** (`state`, `text`, required)
Enter the state of your address.

### 10. **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required)
Enter the zip code of your address.

### 11. **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required)
Enter the total amount of your order accurately.

### 12. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required)
Choose the payment method for this order: Credit, PayPal, Stripe.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* Double check your order total before submitting the form.
* Review your order details before payment to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact sales team for assistance.
