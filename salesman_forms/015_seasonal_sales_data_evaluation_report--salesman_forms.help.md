# Seasonal Sales Data Evaluation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Sales Data Evaluation Report is a form used to collect sales data from various sales channels and periods. This data will be used to evaluate sales performance during different seasons and sales channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Sales Period** for which you want to report sales data. You can choose from the following options: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.
2.  Select the **Sales Channels** through which sales were made. You can choose multiple channels.
3.  Provide the **Store Name** where sales were made.
4.  Select the **Sales Region** where sales were made. You can choose from the following options: North, South, East, West, Central.
5.  Enter the **Sales Amount** made during the selected period and sales channel.
6.  Select the **Sales Manager** responsible for the sales during the selected period and sales channel.
7.  Provide the **Store Location** where sales were made.
8.  Enter the date and time of the sales (**Sales Date and Sales Time**).
9.  Provide a **Sales Note** about the sales.
10.  Enter your **Email Address** for follow-up purposes.
11.  Enter the **Phone Number** of your work, home, or mobile number.
12.  Enter the **Sales Manager Email** and **Sales Manager Phone** for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Period** (`sales_period`): Select the sales period for which you want to report sales data.
* **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`): Select the sales channels through which sales were made.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`): Provide the name of the store where sales were made.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`): Select the sales region where sales were made.
* **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`): Enter the sales amount made during the selected period and sales channel.
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`): Select the sales manager responsible for the sales during the selected period and sales channel.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`): Provide the store location where sales were made.
* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`): Enter the date of the sales.
* **Sales Time** (`sales_time`): Enter the time of the sales.
* **Sales Note** (`sales_note`): Provide a note about the sales.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of your work, home, or mobile number.
* **Sales Manager Email** (`sales_manager_email`): Enter the sales manager email for follow-up purposes.
* **Sales Manager Phone** (`sales_manager_phone`): Enter the sales manager phone number for follow-up purposes.
