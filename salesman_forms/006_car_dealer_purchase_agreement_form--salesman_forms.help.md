# Car Dealer Purchase Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a crucial document for vehicle purchases. It ensures that all parties involved in the transaction have a clear understanding of the terms, details, and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the buyer's information section (fields 2-9) with your personal details.
2.  Provide accurate vehicle information (fields 16-20 and 22-25) to ensure that the purchase is accurately recorded.
3. Select the financing method (field 28) that best suits your needs.
4. If applicable, fill out the co-buyer information (fields 13-15).
5. Review and complete the purchase details section (field 23) for clarity on the agreement.
6. Indicate whether there is a trade-in (field 25) and, if so, fill out the trade-in value (field 26).
7. Enter the down payment (field 27) and confirm your financing method (field 28).
8. Provide the dealership's information (fields 31-32) for verification.
9. Confirm the dealer's agreement with you (field 36) to ensure both parties are in agreement.
10. Review and sign the agreement (field 35) to confirm your acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Buyer First Name** (`Buyer First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Buyer Last Name** (`Buyer Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Buyer Email** (`Buyer Email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication regarding the contract.
*   **Buyer Phone** (`Buyer Phone`, text, required): Enter a contact phone number for easy communication.
*   **Buyer Address** (`Buyer Address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
*   **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
*   **ZIP Code** (`ZIP Code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
*   **Driver's License Number** (`Driver's License Number`, text, required): Enter your government-issued driver's license number.
*   **License State** (`License State`, text, required): Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
*   **Co-Buyer Information** (`Co-Buyer Information`, note, false): This field is only applicable if there is a co-buyer involved in the purchase.
*   **Co-Buyer Name** (`Co-Buyer Name`, text, false): If applicable, fill out the co-buyer's full name.
*   **Co-Buyer Email** (`Co-Buyer Email`, email, false): If applicable, enter the co-buyer's email address.
*   **Vehicle Information** (`Vehicle Information`, note, false): This field is for additional details regarding the vehicle being purchased.
*   **Vehicle Year** (`Vehicle Year`, text, required): Enter the year of the vehicle being purchased.
*   **Vehicle Make** (`Vehicle Make`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's manufacturer.
*   **Vehicle Model** (`Vehicle Model`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's model name.
*   **VIN** (`VIN`, text, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Color** (`Vehicle Color`, text, required): Enter the exterior color of the vehicle.
*   **Current Mileage** (`Current Mileage`, number, required): Enter the odometer reading at the time of the sale.
*   **Stock Number** (`Stock Number`, text, required): Enter the dealer's stock number for the vehicle.
*   **Purchase Details** (`Purchase Details`, note, false): This field is for additional information about the purchase.
*   **Purchase Price** (`Purchase Price`, number, required): Enter the agreed-upon purchase price in dollars.
*   **Trade-In Vehicle** (`Trade-In Vehicle`, select_one, required): Indicate if there is a trade-in vehicle involved in the purchase.
*   **Trade-In Value** (`Trade-In Value`, number, false): If applicable, enter the agreed-upon trade-in value.
*   **Down Payment** (`Down Payment`, number, required): Enter the amount paid upfront.
*   **Financing Method** (`Financing Method`, select_one, required): Choose the method by which the vehicle will be financed.
*   **Additional Fees** (`Additional Fees`, select_multiple, false): Select any additional fees or add-ons.
*   **Dealer Information** (`Dealer Information`, note, false): This field is for dealership details.
*   **Dealership Name** (`Dealership Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the dealership.
*   **Salesperson Name** (`Salesperson Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the salesperson.
*   **Salesperson ID** (`Salesperson ID`, text, required): Enter the salesperson's identification number.
*   **Agreement Date** (`Agreement Date`, date, required): Enter the date of this agreement.
*   **Buyer Signature Confirmation** (`Buyer Signature Confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
*   **Dealer Signature Confirmation** (`Dealer Signature Confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm the dealership's authorization.

## Tips

- Always read and review the form carefully before completion.
- Double-check accuracy of information entered in fields 2-9.
- For trade-in options, ensure the value entered aligns with the agreed-upon terms.
- Review and confirm agreement terms before signing field 35.
- Ensure the salesperson and the dealership confirm their signatures in fields 36 and 35.
- This form is intended to be a comprehensive guide for both parties in the vehicle purchase process. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact your salesperson or dealership representative.
