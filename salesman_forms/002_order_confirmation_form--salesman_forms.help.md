# Order Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Order Confirmation Form is designed to review and confirm order details, ensuring accuracy and consistency before proceeding with further processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully, ensuring all fields are filled accurately.
2. For each field, select or input the relevant information as required.
3. Double-check the fields for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once all fields are filled and reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirm Order** (`order_confirmation_form`, text, required): Confirm the order by typing or selecting "Yes" to indicate approval.
* **Customer Shipping Info** (`customer_shipping_info`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm shipping information.
* **Customer Contact Info** (`customer_contact_info`, text, required): Enter or confirm the customer's contact information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter or confirm the order details.
* **Customer Order Number** (`customer_order_number`, number, required): Enter the customer's order number.
* **Order Date Dispatched** (`order_date_dispatched`, date, required): Enter the date the order was dispatched.
* **Order Status** (`customer_order_status`, select_one, required): Select the current order status (Active or Inactive).
* **Shipping Status** (`customer_shipping_status`, select_one, required): Select the current shipping status (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter or confirm the customer's name.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total value of the order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter or confirm the customer's phone number.
* **Order Ship Date** (`order_ship_date`, time, required): Enter the expected ship date and time.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter or confirm the customer's email address.
* **Customer Ship Name** (`customer_ship_name`, note, required): Enter or confirm the ship name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter or confirm the customer's address.
* **Order Ship Status** (`order_ship_status`, select_one, required): Select the current ship status (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Shipping Method** (`customer_shipping_method`, select_multiple, required): Select or confirm the shipping method (Yes or No).
* **Order Ship Date Time** (`order_ship_date_time`, time, required): Enter the expected ship date and time.
* **Customer Ship Address** (`customer_ship_address`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship address.
* **Customer Ship Country** (`customer_ship_country`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship country.
* **Customer Ship State** (`customer_ship_state`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship state.
* **Customer Ship City** (`customer_ship_city`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship city.
* **Customer Ship Zip** (`customer_ship_zip`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship zip code.
* **Customer Ship Country Code** (`customer_ship_country_code`, text, required): Enter or confirm the ship country code.

Note: The fields marked as "select_multiple" or "select_one" allow for multiple or single selection respectively, while "text" fields are for free-form input and "email" fields are for email address input. The "time" and "date" fields are for entering dates and times, respectively. All fields marked as "required" are essential for completing the form accurately.
