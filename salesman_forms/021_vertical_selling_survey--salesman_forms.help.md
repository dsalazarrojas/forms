# Vertical Selling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sales-related information from end-users. It is intended for use by sales teams to gather data about their customers or potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your company in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your industry in the "Industry" field.
7. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your job title (if applicable) in the "Job Title 2" field.
10. Enter your department (if applicable) in the "Department 2" field.
11. Enter your job title (if applicable) in the "Job Title 3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: no): Enter your first name here. 
* **Last Name** (text, required: no): Enter your last name here.
* **Company** (text, required: no): Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Department** (text, required: no): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (text, required: no): Enter your job title or role.
* **Industry** (text, required: no): Enter the industry you work in.
* **Email** (email, required: no): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: no): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title 2** (text, required: no): Enter any additional job titles you may have.
* **Department 2** (text, required: no): Enter any additional departments you may belong to.
* **Job Title 3** (text, required: no): Enter any additional job titles you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields, but only fill out fields that apply to you.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your sales representative for clarification.

Note: This help guide is based on the assumption that the form fields are intended to be filled out by sales representatives or sales-related personnel. If this is not the case, please let me know and I will modify the guide accordingly.
