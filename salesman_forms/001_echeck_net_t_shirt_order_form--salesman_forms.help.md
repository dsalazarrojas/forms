# ECheck.Net T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ECheck.Net T Shirt Order Form is a form used for customers to place orders for T shirts through the ECheck.Net platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
6. Specify the order item and quantity in the "Order Item" and "Order Quantity" fields respectively.
7. Enter the total price of the order in the "Total Price" field.
8. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
9. Confirm the payment details in the "Confirm Payment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ECheck.Net Section** (`note`, required/optional): This section provides a note about the ECheck.Net platform.
* **Customer Information** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your customer information here.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name here.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Customer Address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your address here.
* **Order Item** (`text`, required/optional): Specify the T shirt item you want to order.
* **Order Quantity** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the T shirt you want to order.
* **Total Price** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment method from the options provided (eCheck, PayPal).
* **Confirm Payment** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Confirm the payment details by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check the customer information and payment details before submitting the form.
