# Auto Sales Customer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Sales Customer Application Form is used to collect information from customers about their personal, employment, and vehicle preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information:
	* Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
	* Provide your Date of Birth.
	* Select your Driver License State.
2. Enter your current address:
	* Type in your Street Address.
	* Select your City.
	* Select your State.
	* Enter your Zip Code.
3. Provide employment information:
	* Select your employment status (Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled).
	* If employed, enter your Employer Name (optional).
	* Enter your Annual Income.
4. (Optional) If you have a trade-in vehicle:
	* Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade-in.
	* If yes, enter the Year, Make, and Model of your trade-in vehicle.
5. Choose your desired vehicle preferences:
	* Select the Vehicle Type of Interest (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Crossover, Van, Other).
	* Choose if you prefer a New or Used vehicle.
	* Choose your Budget Range (Under 15,000, 15,000-25,000, 25,000-40,000, 40,000-60,000, 60,000+).
	* Choose if Financing is required (Yes, No, Unsure).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license or identification.
* **Email Address** (Required): Provide your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your Date of Birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Driver License State** (Required): Select your state of residence.
* **Street Address** (Required): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (Required): Select your city of residence.
* **State** (Required): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip Code** (Required): Enter your zip code.
* **Employment Status** (Required): Select your current employment status.
* **Employer Name** (Optional): If employed, enter your employer's name.
* **Annual Income** (Required): Enter your gross annual income.
* **Do you have a vehicle to trade-in?** (Required): Select yes or no.
* **Trade-In Vehicle Year** (Optional): If trading in a vehicle, enter the year.
* **Trade-In Vehicle Make** (Optional): If trading in a vehicle, enter the make.
* **Trade-In Vehicle Model** (Optional): If trading in a vehicle, enter the model.
* **Vehicle Type of Interest** (Required): Select the type of vehicle you are interested in purchasing.
* **New or Used Vehicle** (Required): Choose if you prefer a new or used vehicle.
* **Budget Range** (Required): Select your preferred budget for the vehicle.
* **Financing Required** (Required): Choose if financing is required for the purchase.
