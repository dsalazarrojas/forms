# Weekly Sales Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Report Form is designed to help sales representatives submit their sales activity and performance on a weekly basis. This form provides a structured approach to report sales data, allowing for accurate and timely tracking of sales performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate sales regions you worked in during the week.
2. Enter your sales representative's ID.
3. Enter the total sales amount for the week.
4. Choose the relevant sales region(s) for the week's sales activity.
5. Ensure you have completed all required and optional fields, as indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, required: false): Enter your unique customer ID assigned by the sales team or management.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more sales regions where you worked during the week.
* **Weekly Sales Date** (`weekly_sales_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for which you are reporting sales activity.
* **Sales Rep Name** (`sales_rep_name`, text, required: false): Enter your sales representative name as assigned by management.
* **Total Sales** (`total_sales`, number, required: false): Enter the total sales amount for the week.
* **Sales Region 2** (`sales_region_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose one relevant sales region for the week's sales activity. (This field might seem redundant, but ensure to select the correct option according to your sales region assignments.)
* **Sales Region 3** (`sales_region_3`, select_one, required: false): Choose one relevant sales region for the week's sales activity. (Like Sales Region 2, make sure to choose the correct option according to your sales region assignments.)
* **Sales Region 4** (`sales_region_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more relevant sales regions for the week's sales activity. (This field serves as an alternative to Sales Region.)

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your sales manager or team lead for assistance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Report your sales activity according to the company's sales policy and guidelines.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the sales department.
