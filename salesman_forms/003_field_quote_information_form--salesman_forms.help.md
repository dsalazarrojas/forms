# Field Quote Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Quote Information Form is used to collect information about a customer's quote, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the `Customer Email` field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the `Customer Phone` field.
4. Enter the salesperson's name in the `Sales Person` field.
5. Enter the salesperson's email address in the `Sales Person Email` field.
6. Enter the salesperson's phone number in the `Sales Person Phone` field.
7. Enter the quote date in the `Quote Date` field.
8. Enter the quote time in the `Quote Time` field.
9. Enter the quote value in the `Quote Value` field.
10. Select the product or service being quoted in the `Product or Service` field.
11. Select the location where the service will be performed in the `Location` field.
12. Select the assigned tool in the `Assigned Tool` field.
13. Enter any additional information in the `Additional Info` field (optional).
14. Enter the sales manager's name in the `Sales Manager` field.
15. Enter the sales manager's email address in the `Sales Manager Email` field.
16. Enter the sales manager's phone number in the `Sales Manager Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, text, required): Enter the salesperson's name.
* **Sales Person Email** (`sales_person_email`, email, required): Enter the salesperson's email address.
* **Sales Person Phone** (`sales_person_phone`, text, required): Enter the salesperson's phone number.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, required): Enter the quote date.
* **Quote Time** (`quote_time`, time, required): Enter the quote time.
* **Quote Value** (`quote_value`, number, required): Enter the quote value.
* **Quote Notes** (`quote_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the quote (optional).
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more products or services being quoted.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select the location where the service will be performed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the assigned tool.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information (optional).
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`, text, required): Enter the sales manager's name.
* **Sales Manager Email** (`sales_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the sales manager's email address.
* **Sales Manager Phone** (`sales_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the sales manager's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter the customer's state.
* **Customer Postal Code** (`customer_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the customer's postal code.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, optional): Enter the customer's country.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Region** (`customer_region`, text, optional): Enter the customer's region.
* **Customer Region 2** (`customer_region_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's region 2.
