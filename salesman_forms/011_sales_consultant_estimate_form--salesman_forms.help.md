# Sales Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Consultant Estimate Form is used to generate an estimate for a sales consultant's services. This form collects information from the customer about their needs and requirements, which is then used to provide a detailed estimate for the sales consultant's services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the sales consultant's name in the "Sales Consultant Name" field.
3. Enter the sales consultant's email address in the "Sales Consultant Email" field.
4. Enter the sales consultant's phone number in the "Sales Consultant Phone" field.
5. Enter any additional customer messages or comments in the "Customer Message" field.
6. Select the estimated date for the services in the "Estimated Date" field.
7. Select the estimated time for the services in the "Estimated Time" field.
8. Select the services that will be provided, such as "minimalist analysis", "comprehensive analysis", "consulting", "strategy development", or "sales strategy", in the "Services" field.
9. Enter the price for the estimated services.
10. Select the assigned tool for the services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Sales Consultant Name**: Enter the sales consultant's name.
* **Sales Consultant Email (Sales Consultant Email)**: Enter the sales consultant's email address.
* **Sales Consultant Phone (Sales Consultant Phone)**: Enter the sales consultant's phone number.
* **Customer Message (Customer Message)**: Enter any additional customer messages or comments.
* **Estimated Date (Estimated Date)**: Select the estimated date for the services.
* **Estimated Time (Estimated Time)**: Select the estimated time for the services.
* **Services (Services)**: Select the services that will be provided, such as "minimalist analysis", "comprehensive analysis", "consulting", "strategy development", or "sales strategy".
* **Price (Price)**: Enter the price for the estimated services.
* **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: Enter the assigned tool for the services.
