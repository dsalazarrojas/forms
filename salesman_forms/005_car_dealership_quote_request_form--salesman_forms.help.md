# Car Dealership Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the dealership's sales team determine the best possible quote for your vehicle needs. It will ask for your contact information, preferences for the type of vehicle you're interested in, and other details to narrow down the best options for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer contact information fields (first name, last name, email, and phone number).
2. Select the type of vehicle that interests you from the list.
3. Choose your preferred vehicle make.
4. Enter your preferred vehicle model.
5. Indicate if you're interested in a new or used vehicle.
6. Provide your budget range for the vehicle purchase.
7. Select your desired features for the vehicle (such as leather interior, sunroof, etc.).
8. Choose your preferred fuel type and transmission.
9. Select your preferred color for the vehicle.
10. Choose your financing method (cash purchase, dealership financing, etc.).
11. Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade in.
12. Provide details about your trade-in vehicle (year, make, model, and condition).
13. Select how soon you need the vehicle.
14. Add any additional notes or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **What type of vehicle interests you?**: Select the type of vehicle you're interested in (Sedan, SUV, Truck, etc.).
* **Preferred vehicle make**: Enter the make of the vehicle you're interested in (e.g., Honda, Toyota, etc.).
* **Preferred vehicle model**: Enter the model of the vehicle you're interested in.
* **New or used vehicle?**: Select if you're interested in a new or used vehicle.
* **Budget range - low**: Enter the minimum amount you're willing to spend on the vehicle purchase.
* **Budget range - high**: Enter the maximum amount you're willing to spend on the vehicle purchase.
* **Desired features (select all that apply)**: Select any features that you would like to have in the vehicle (leather interior, sunroof, etc.).
* **Preferred fuel type**: Select your preferred fuel type (gasoline, diesel, etc.).
* **Transmission preference**: Select your preferred transmission type (manual, automatic, etc.).
* **Preferred color**: Enter your preferred color for the vehicle.
* **Financing method**: Select how you plan to finance the vehicle purchase (cash, dealership financing, etc.).
* **Do you have a vehicle to trade in?**: Select yes or no if you have a vehicle to trade in.
* **Trade-in vehicle details**: If you answered yes to the previous question, provide details about your trade-in vehicle (year, make, model, and condition).
* **How soon do you need a vehicle?**: Select how soon you need the vehicle (within 1 week, within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Additional notes or questions**: Add any additional information or questions you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us provide the best possible quote for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Additional notes or questions" field.
* We will review your form submission and contact you to discuss the best possible quote for your vehicle needs.
