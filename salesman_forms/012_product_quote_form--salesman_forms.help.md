# Product Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for clients to submit their product or service interest. Please provide as much information as possible to get a more accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the first field to identify yourself.
2. Provide your **Contact Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you for further questions or to schedule a meeting.
3. Enter your **Contact Email** to receive updates and confirmations.
4. Select the **Product Type** from the dropdown list that best describes the product or service you are interested in.
5. If applicable, select the **Color** of the product or service.
6. Enter the **Product Size**, **Weight**, **Length**, and **Width** to provide accurate measurements.
7. Enter the **Product Price**, which will help us get a better quote for you.
8. Choose the **Sales Agent** you would like to contact.
9. Select a **Quote Date** and **Quote Time** to schedule a meeting.
10. Enter any additional **Quote Note** to help us better understand your requirements.
11. If you are representing a company, enter the **Company Name**, **Company Address**, and **Contact Name**, **Contact Title**, **Contact Job Title**, **Contact Department**, and **Contact Job Function**.
12. Finally, add any **Custom Comments** you would like to include.
