# vehicle_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers regarding their vehicle purchase. It includes fields for customer details, vehicle information, payment details, additional vehicle information, salesman details, and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer information on page 1.
2. Continue to page 2 and fill in the vehicle details.
3. On page 3, enter payment details and any additional information about the vehicle.
4. Finally, enter salesman information and order details on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer** (Page 1, Text, Optional): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle** (Page 2, Text, Optional): Provide the vehicle's make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Details** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Enter the payment method and any additional payment information.
* **Additional Vehicle Info** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Add any additional details or features of the vehicle.
* **Salesman Info** (Page 4, Text, Optional): Enter the salesman's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (Page 4, Text, Optional): Enter any additional order information or notes.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling in customer and vehicle details to ensure accurate records.
* Double-check payment and order details to avoid errors.
* Use this form for creating vehicle purchase orders and other related purposes.
