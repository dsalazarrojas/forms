# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School T Shirt Order Form is a sales form designed to collect information from customers to place orders for school t-shirts. This form is intended to be used by sales team members to capture relevant details from customers and process their orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the date of your order in the **Order Date** field.
3. Select the **Quantity** of t-shirts you would like to order.
4. Choose your preferred **T Shirt Size** from the available options.
5. Select the **Color** of the t-shirts you would like to order (select as many options as apply).
6. Enter the price of your order in the **Price** field.
7. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
8. If you have any additional notes or instructions, you can type them in the **Order Notes** field.
9. Finally, enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your school ID or business card.
* **Order Date**: This field is not required, but it's helpful for our sales team to keep track of order history.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order.
* **T Shirt Size**: Choose from Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Color**: Select the colors you would like to order (e.g. Red, Blue, or Green).
* **Price**: Enter the total price of your order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Order Notes**: If you have any specific requests or instructions for your order, type them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact purposes.
