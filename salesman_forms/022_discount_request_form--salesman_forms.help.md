# Discount Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a discount from the sales team. Please use it to provide relevant information about yourself and the discount you are requesting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address.
3. Enter your **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
4. Enter **Date** (`date`): Select the date for which you are requesting the discount.
5. Enter **Time** (`time`): Select the time for which you are requesting the discount.
6. Enter **Discount** (`discount`): Provide a numerical value for the discount you are requesting.
7. Enter a brief **Description** (`description`): Explain the reason for your discount request.
8. Enter the **Salesman Name** (`salesman_name`): Enter the name of the salesperson you are working with.
9. Enter the **Salesman Email** (`salesman_email`): Enter the salesperson's email address.
10. Enter the salesperson's **Phone** (`salesman_phone`): Enter the salesperson's phone number.
11. Enter the salesperson's **Time** (`salesman_time`): Select the time the salesperson is available to talk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for which you are requesting the discount.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for which you are requesting the discount.
* **Discount** (`discount`, number, required/optional): Provide a numerical value for the discount you are requesting.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for your discount request.
* **Salesman Name** (`salesman_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the salesperson you are working with.
* **Salesman Email** (`salesman_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the salesperson's email address.
* **Salesman Phone** (`salesman_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the salesperson's phone number.
* **Salesman Time** (`salesman_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time the salesperson is available to talk.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* This form is intended to be used once, so double-check your information before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the sales team for assistance.
