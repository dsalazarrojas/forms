# product_enquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers and prospects about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Optionally, type any additional message in the "Enquiry Message" field.
7. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here. Please format it as per your country's phone number format.
* **Enquiry Message** (`enquiry_message`, `note`, required/optional): Type any additional message here if you have any questions or need further assistance.
