# Drain Problem Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Drain Problem Report Form" is used to collect and record information about drain problems reported by customers or users. This form helps the administration to identify, categorize, and prioritize drain problems effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Street Address where the problem is located.
2. Check if the problem is located at a private or public location.
3. Select all applicable Problem Type(s) that describe the problem.
4. Choose the priority of the problem.
5. Enter a detailed Description of the problem.
6. Optionally, enter the Date and Time when the problem started or was noticed.
7. Enter the Contact Name and Contact Number for the individual reporting the problem.
8. Optionally, enter a Contact E-mail address.
9. Finally, provide a brief Contact Message to the administration or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Street Address**:
	+ This is the address where the problem is located.
	+ Enter the full street address, including the house number and any additional information.
* **Location**:
	+ Check if the problem is located at a private or public location.
* **Problem Type**:
	+ Choose all applicable Problem Type(s) from the list to describe the type of problem.
	+ Common types are Water leak, Clogged, and Slow draining.
* **Priority**:
	+ Select the priority of the problem based on severity or urgency.
	+ High, Medium, and Low priorities are available.
* **Description**:
	+ Provide a detailed Description of the problem, including specific details about the issue.
	+ Please be as specific as possible.
* **Date**:
	+ Optionally, enter the Date when the problem started or was noticed.
* **Time**:
	+ Optionally, enter the Time when the problem started or was noticed.
* **Contact Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the person reporting the problem.
* **Contact Number**:
	+ Enter the contact number of the person reporting the problem.
* **Contact E-mail**:
	+ Optionally, enter a contact e-mail address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Message**:
	+ Provide a brief message to the administration or support team for any additional information or instructions.
