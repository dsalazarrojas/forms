# Gas Boiler Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for gas boiler replacement requests. It is intended for customers or users who need to replace their gas boiler and want to provide details about the replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the contact information on page 5.
2. Select the correct boiler type on page 6.
3. Choose the gas supplier on page 7.
4. Confirm the installation details on page 8.
5. Enter the phone number on page 9.
6. Provide the dates of installation and replacement on pages 10 and 11.
7. Enter the time of replacement on page 12.
8. Optionally, add any additional comments on page 13.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (page 5, required): Enter your name, address, and email to contact you for further communication.
* **Boiler Type** (page 6, required): Choose the type of boiler you have or want to replace, such as gas boiler or other types of boilers.
* **Gas Supplier** (page 7, required): Choose the gas supplier you currently use or would like to use for the replacement.
* **Submit** (page 8, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Phone** (page 9, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Date of Installation** (page 10, required): Enter the date the gas boiler was installed.
* **Date of Replacement** (page 11, required): Enter the date you want to replace the gas boiler.
* **Time of Replacement** (page 12, required): Enter the time you want to replace the gas boiler.
* **Comments** (page 13, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the replacement process.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your gas boiler replacement request is processed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
