# plumbing_services_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "plumbing_services_billing_form" is used to document and bill for plumbing services provided to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all relevant fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure all fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided (Drain Cleaning, Leaking Valve, Leaking Water Heater, or Leaking Faucet).
* **Payment Method**: Select all applicable payment methods (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Check).
* **Service Date**: Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Service Time**: Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Total Charges**: Enter the total charges for the service (if applicable).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* **Payment Method Other**: If a different payment method was used, enter it here.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the service.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the client's address.
* **Zip**: Enter the client's zip code.
* **City**: Enter the client's city.
* **State**: Enter the client's state.
* **Country**: Enter the client's country.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool used to complete the service (if applicable).
* **Assigned User**: Enter the plumber or staff member who completed the service.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the service (In Progress, Complete, or Overdue).
* **Due Date**: If applicable, enter the date the service is due to be completed by.
* **Completed Date**: If applicable, enter the date the service was completed by.
* **Assigned Date**: If applicable, enter the date the service was assigned to a user.
* **Service Type Other**: If a different service type was provided, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double check the information entered for accuracy.
* Ensure that all services are accounted for on the form.
