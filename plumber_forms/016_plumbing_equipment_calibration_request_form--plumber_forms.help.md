# Plumbing Equipment Calibration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request the calibration of plumbing equipment, ensuring that it operates within specified standards for optimal performance and safety. Please use this form to submit a calibration request for your plumbing equipment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Equipment Name** field with the name of the equipment that needs calibration.
2.  Choose the correct **Equipment Type** from the dropdown menu that matches your equipment.
3.  If you know the **Calibration Date**, enter it in the date field. If you don't know the exact date, you can leave this field blank.
4.  If you have a preferred date for the calibration, select it in the **Preferred Date** field.
5.  Enter the **Technician Name** who will perform the calibration, if known.
6.  In the **Contact Phone** field, enter the phone number of the person responsible for the equipment or the contact person for the calibration.
7.  Enter the **Contact Email** of the person responsible for the equipment or the contact person for the calibration.
8.  If you know the **Assigned Tool** used for the calibration, enter it in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment that needs calibration.
* **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment from the dropdown menu that best matches your equipment (e.g., Water Heater, Boiler, Pump, Faucet, Toilet, or Shower).
* **Calibration Date**: If you know the calibration date, enter it in the format MM/DD/YY. If you don't know the exact date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a preferred date for the calibration, if available.
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the person who will perform the calibration, if known.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the equipment or the contact person for the calibration.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the equipment or the contact person for the calibration.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you know the specific tool used for calibration, enter it in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in as much information as you can, as this will help us process your calibration request more efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* Please note that we will contact you to discuss and confirm your calibration request before proceeding with the process.
