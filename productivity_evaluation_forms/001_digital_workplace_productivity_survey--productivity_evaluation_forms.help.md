# digital_workplace_productivity_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about digital workplace productivity of employees. It helps the organization understand how employees spend their time and use tools to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Scroll through the form pages to answer each question.
3. For each question, select the relevant answer from the options provided.
4. Enter your answer in the text field or select the corresponding option.
5. When finished, click on the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this form about** (`intro`, `note`, `id: 1`): This is the introduction page that explains the purpose of the form.
* **User name** (`name`, `text`, `id: 2`): Please enter your name as it appears on your company directory or ID badge.
* **Company name** (`company`, `text`, `id: 3`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Department name** (`department`, `text`, `id: 4`): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **Job title** (`role`, `text`, `id: 5`): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `id: 6`): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `id: 7`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Tools used for work** (`workflow_tools`, `select_multiple`, `id: 8`): Select all the digital tools you use for work from the list:
	+ Email
	+ Chat
	+ Video conferencing
	+ Project management software
	+ Document management software
	+ Other (please specify in the next field)
* **Other tools** (`workflow_tools_other`, `text`, `id: 9`): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please specify the name of the tool.
* **Average work hours per day** (`work_hours`, `number`, `id: 10`): Estimate the number of hours you work on an average day.
* **Average break length in minutes** (`break_length`, `number`, `id: 11`): Estimate the average length of your break in minutes.
* **Number of work breaks** (`work_breaks`, `number`, `id: 12`): Enter the number of breaks you take during an average day.
* **Meetings per week** (`meetings_per_week`, `select_multiple`, `id: 13`): Select the frequency of meetings you have per week:
	+ 1-2 meetings
	+ 3-5 meetings
	+ 6 or more
* **Meeting length** (`meeting_length`, `number`, `id: 14`): Estimate the average length of your meetings.
* **Meeting frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, `id: 15`): Select the frequency of meetings you have:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As needed
* **Communication channel preference** (`communication_channel`, `select_multiple`, `id: 16`): Select your preferred method of communication:
	+ Email
	+ Chat
	+ Phone
	+ Video
* **Self-assessed productivity** (`productivity`, `number`, `id: 17`): Rate your self-assessed productivity on a scale from 1-5.
* **Work satisfaction** (`work_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `id: 18`): Rate your work satisfaction on a scale:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, `id: 19`): Rate your work-life balance on a scale:
	+ Work-life balance is good
	+ Work-life balance is fair
	+ Work-life balance is poor
* **Other comments** (`work_life_balance_other`, `text`, `id: 20`): If you have any other comments about your work-life balance, please enter them here.
