# Productive Systems Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the productivity and effectiveness of systems used in a team or organization. It is designed to gather information on various aspects of these systems, including tool usage, team communication, availability, engagement, impact, process clarity, satisfaction, data collection, form submission data, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For each section, choose the options that best describe your current situation.
4. For numerical scores, enter a value between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
5. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description.
6. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first-page**: This is the main page of the form where you will find questions about tool usage.
* **tool-usage**: Please select all the tools you use for your work. 
* **tool-score**: Rate your current tool usage from 1 to 5.
* **team-communication**: Please select all the methods you use for team communication. 
* **communication-score**: Rate your current team communication from 1 to 5.
* **team-satisfaction**: Please select how you feel about team communication. 
* **satisfaction-score**: Rate your satisfaction with team communication from 1 to 5.
* **tool-availability**: Please select the availability of tools for your team. 
* **availability-score**: Rate the availability of tools for your team from 1 to 5.
* **team-engagement**: Please select all the methods your team uses to stay engaged. 
* **engagement-score**: Rate your team engagement from 1 to 5.
* **tool-impact**: Please select all the impact you see from tools in your work. 
* **impact-score**: Rate the impact of tools on your work from 1 to 5.
* **process-clarification**: Please select all the clarity you have in your work processes. 
* **clarification-score**: Rate the clarity of your work processes from 1 to 5.
* **team-satisfaction-across-departments**: Please select where your team's satisfaction extends. 
* **satisfaction-score**: Rate your team's satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **data-collection**: Please select all the ways you collect data. 
* **collection-score**: Rate your data collection from 1 to 5.
* **form-submission-data**: Provide details about your form submission data. 
* **submission-data-score**: Rate your form submission data from 1 to 5.
* **submission-data-accuracy**: Please select how accurate your form submission data is. 
* **submission-data-accuracy-score**: Rate the accuracy of your form submission data from 1 to 5.
* **next-steps**: Please provide information about next steps. 
* **next-steps-score**: Rate your next steps from 1 to 5.
