# Hybrid Work Productivity Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Work Productivity Monitoring Form is designed to track employee productivity and work patterns during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name.
2. Select your role (Yes or No).
3. Choose whether you work at a client site (Yes or No).
4. Enter the number of hours you worked.
5. Enter the date you worked.
6. Select your work schedule for each day of the week (0-8 hours).
7. Choose whether you work from home (Yes or No).
8. Choose whether you work remotely (Yes or No).
9. Select whether you are a team lead (Yes or No).
10. Select whether you have a manager (Yes or No).
11. Select whether you report directly to someone (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name. This will help us identify you in our records.
* **Role**: Check if you have a role. This helps us understand your job responsibilities.
* **Work Location**: Check if you work at a client site. This helps us understand your work environment.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the number of hours you worked. Please use 24-hour format (0-8 hours per day).
* **Hours Worked Date**: Enter the date you worked. Please use the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Monday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Monday (0-8 hours).
* **Tuesday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Tuesday (0-8 hours).
* **Wednesday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Wednesday (0-8 hours).
* **Thursday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Thursday (0-8 hours).
* **Friday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Friday (0-8 hours).
* **Saturday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Saturday (0-8 hours).
* **Sunday**: Enter the number of hours you worked on Sunday (0-8 hours).
* **Work From Home**: Check if you work from home. This helps us understand your work environment.
* **Work In Office**: Check if you work in the office. This helps us understand your work environment.
* **Work In Client Site**: Check if you work at a client site. This helps us understand your work environment.
* **Work Remotely**: Check if you work remotely. This helps us understand your work environment.
* **Team Lead**: Check if you are a team lead. This helps us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Manager**: Check if you have a manager. This helps us understand your reporting structure.
* **Direct Report**: Check if you report directly to someone. This helps us understand your reporting structure.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
