# Employee Productivity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Productivity Evaluation Form is used to assess an employee's performance, quality of work, collaboration skills, and other relevant aspects. It helps to evaluate the employee's productivity and provide feedback for improvement. This form is meant to be filled out by a manager or supervisor, and is usually completed on a regular basis, such as on a quarterly or semi-annual basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, as applicable.
3. Fill out the employee's name, date, and time of evaluation.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Performance** (`performance`, text, optional): Describe the employee's performance, including their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Quality** (`quality`, text, optional): Discuss the quality of the employee's work, including their attention to detail and ability to deliver results.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, optional): Describe the employee's collaboration skills, including their ability to work with colleagues and management.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Record the time of the evaluation.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided, indicating the outcome of the evaluation.

### Tips
* Please be thorough and honest when evaluating the employee's performance.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your evaluation.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, you can always contact the HR department for guidance.
* Remember to save the form before submitting it.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accurate and fair feedback.
