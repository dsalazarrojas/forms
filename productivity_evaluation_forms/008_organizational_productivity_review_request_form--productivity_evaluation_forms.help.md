# Organizational Productivity Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information on the organizational productivity review. It's used to gather data on productivity metrics such as goals, focus areas, timeline, and manager's feedback. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to help your team or organization improve its productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Choose one of the options for each field.
- Do not leave any fields blank unless you have a valid reason for doing so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief introduction to your productivity review request.
- **Goals** (`goals`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate what kind of goals you are focusing on:
  - Short-term goals
  - Long-term goals
  - Both
  - None
- **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, `text`, optional): Describe the focus areas of your productivity review. For example, you can mention specific departments or teams.
- **Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate when you'd like to review your productivity:
  - Weekly
  - Bi-weekly
  - Monthly
  - Quarterly
- **Manager** (`manager`, `select_one`, optional): Select your manager from the list:
  - Manager 1
  - Manager 2
  - Manager 3
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so that your manager can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that your manager can contact you.
- **Hours** (`hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total number of hours spent on your productivity review.
- **Meetings** (`meetings`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of meetings you've had for your productivity review.
- **Targets** (`targets`, `select_one`, optional): Answer the following question:
  - 'True'
  - 'False'
  - Not sure
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit your form. Please make sure to review your form carefully before submitting.
- **Submit Button 2** (`submit_button_2`, `text`, optional): This is a secondary submit button for your form. Please do not fill it out unless instructed by your manager.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out this form completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your manager for guidance.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative.
