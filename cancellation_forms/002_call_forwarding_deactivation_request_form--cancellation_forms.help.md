# Call Forwarding Deactivation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help account holders deactivate call forwarding on their account. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for deactivating call forwarding. The reason should be one of the following:
	* No longer needed
	* Switching providers
	* Retiring number
	* Incorrect setup
	* Technical issues
	* Other (please specify in the "Additional Reason Details" field)
2. Select the type of call forwarding you want to deactivate:
	* Unconditional
	* Busy
	* No Answer
	* All Calls
3. Choose the reason for deactivating call forwarding:
	* No longer needed
	* Switching providers
	* Retiring number
	* Incorrect setup
	* Technical issues
	* Other (please specify in the "Additional Reason Details" field)
4. Provide additional details about your reason for deactivation in the "Additional Reason Details" field.
5. Choose the method of confirmation you would like to receive:
	* Email
	* Phone Call
	* SMS
6. Confirm that you authorize the deactivation of call forwarding on your account:
	* Yes, I authorize
	* False (if you are not sure or need further assistance, please contact us)
7. Add any additional comments or special instructions.
8. If you are requesting scheduled deactivation, select the date you would like the deactivation to occur.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Number (`account_number`, required):** Your service account number.
* **Phone Number with Call Forwarding (`phone_number`, required):** The number you want to deactivate call forwarding for.
* **Account Holder Name (`account_holder_name`, required):** Your name as it appears on the account.
* **Account Holder Email (`account_holder_email`, required):** Your email address associated with the account.
* **Contact Phone Number (`contact_phone`, required):** The best number to reach you.
* **Number Calls Are Currently Forwarded To (`current_forwarding_number`, required):** The number calls are currently forwarded to.
* **Date Forwarding Was Activated (`forwarding_activation_date`, not required):** The date call forwarding was activated. If you are unsure, you can leave this blank.
* **Type of Call Forwarding (`forwarding_type`, required):** The type of call forwarding you want to deactivate.
* **Reason for Deactivation (`reason_for_deactivation`, required):** The reason for deactivating call forwarding.
* **Additional Reason Details (`additional_reason_details`, not required):** Additional details about your reason for deactivation.
* **Request Details (`request_details`, not required):** Additional comments or instructions.
* **Deactivate Immediately (`immediate_deactivation`, required):** Whether you want to deactivate call forwarding immediately or schedule it for a later date.
* **Scheduled Deactivation Date (`scheduled_date`, not required):** If you selected "Schedule for later date", choose the date for deactivation.
* **How would you like to receive confirmation (`request_confirmation_method`, required):** The method you would like to receive confirmation of deactivation.
* **Authorize Deactivation (`authorization_confirmation`, required):** Confirm that you authorize the deactivation of call forwarding on your account.
* **Additional Comments or Special Instructions (`comments`, not required):** Any additional comments or special instructions you have.
