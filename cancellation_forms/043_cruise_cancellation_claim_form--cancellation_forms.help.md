# cruise_cancellation_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to report a cruise cancellation and request a refund. It's for passengers who experienced issues on their cruise and need to claim a refund.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the cancellation date when the cruise was cancelled.
2.  Choose the type of cancellation from the available options.
3.  Provide a reason for the cancellation.
4.  Select any relevant travel agencies involved in the cancellation.
5.  Enter the passenger's name.
6.  Enter the passenger's email.
7.  Enter the passenger's phone number.
8.  Enter the passenger's address.
9.  Select the cruise operator.
10.  Enter the amount claimed.
11.  Select the claim status.
12.  Enter any additional comments or notes.
13.  Select who the claim was assigned to.
14.  Enter the date assigned.
15.  Enter the date the claim was closed.
16.  Select if the issue was resolved.
17.  Enter any status notes.
18.  Select any additional travel agencies involved.
