# Debt Review Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the cancellation or continuation of debt review, providing an opportunity to dispute errors or discrepancies in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Cancel" or "Continue" in the first field to determine the outcome of your request.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for cancellation in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
3. If applicable, select one or multiple reasons for dispute in the "Dispute with Counselor" or "Dispute with Agency" fields.
4. Describe any errors or issues encountered during the debt review process in the "Error in Debt Review Enrollment" field.
5. Select the desired resolution in the "Resolution" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Debt Review Cancellation** (`debt_review_cancellation_form`, select_one, required): 
    Select "Cancel" to cancel the debt review process or "Continue" to continue it.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for cancelling the debt review process.
* **Dispute with Counselor** (`dispute_with_counselor`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any issues with your debt counselor, choose from the options listed or select "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Dispute with Agency** (`dispute_with_agency`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any issues with the agency handling your debt review, choose from the options listed or select "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Error in Debt Review Enrollment** (`error_in_enrollment`, text, required): Describe any errors or discrepancies encountered during the debt review process.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_one, required): Select the desired outcome of your request, either "Cancel debt review" or "Re-enroll in debt review".
