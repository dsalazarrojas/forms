# leave_cancellation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to provide information about their reason for cancellation of an event or appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Employee Information**: In the "employee_information" field, please enter your full name and any other relevant information as required.
2. **Provide Reason for Cancellation**: In the "reason_for_cancellation" field, describe the reason why you are cancelling the event or appointment.
3. **Select Date of Cancellation**: In the "date_of_cancellation" field, select the date you would like to cancel the event or appointment.
4. **Select Time of Cancellation**: In the "time_of_cancellation" field, select the time you would like to cancel the event or appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (Employee Information, Text, Required): Enter your full name and any other relevant information as required.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (Reason for Cancellation, Text, Not Required): Describe the reason why you are cancelling the event or appointment.
* **Date of Cancellation** (Date, Not Required): Select the date you would like to cancel the event or appointment.
* **Time of Cancellation** (Time, Not Required): Select the time you would like to cancel the event or appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form only if you are a registered employee of the organization.
* Verify that you have a valid reason for cancellation before proceeding.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating a previous cancellation request.
