# membership_cancellation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a member's intention to cancel their membership. It is a crucial part of the cancellation process, as it provides a formal request and confirmation from both the member and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your member name in the `member_name` field.
2.  Enter your email address in the `member_email` field.
3.  Enter your gym's name in the `gym_name` field.
4.  Enter your gym's address in the `gym_address` field.
5.  Select the reason for your membership cancellation from the `cancellation_details` field.
6.  Enter your cancellation request in the `cancellation_request` field.
7.  Confirm that you are submitting this form in the `confirmation` field.
8.  Enter the date of cancellation in the `date_of_cancellation` field.
9.  Select your current membership status from the `membership_status` field.
10.  Sign your name in the `signature` field.
11.  Add any additional comments in the `comments` field.
12.  Select the cancellation reason from the `cancellation_reason` field.
13.  If applicable, enter the reason for cancellation in the `other_reason` field and provide a description in the `other_reason_description` field.
14.  Enter the staff confirmation in the `staff_confirmation` field.
15.  Add any staff comments in the `staff_comment` field.
16.  Sign the staff member who confirmed the cancellation in the `staff_signature` field.
17.  Enter the admin confirmation in the `admin_confirmation` field.
18.  Add any admin comments in the `admin_comment` field.
19.  Sign the admin who confirmed the cancellation in the `admin_signature` field.
20.  If applicable, enter the reason for cancellation in the `reason_for_cancellation` field.
21.  Enter any comments from the member in the `member_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **member_name** (`member_name`, text, required): This is the member's name who is requesting to cancel their membership.
*   **member_email** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter the member's email address.
*   **gym_name** (`gym_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the gym from which the membership is to be cancelled.
*   **gym_address** (`gym_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the gym from which the membership is to be cancelled.
*   **cancellation_details** (Cancellation Reason, select_multiple, required): Select a reason for cancelling the membership. Possible options are Termination of membership agreement, Discontinue membership for personal reasons, Move to new gym, Other, and Other (please specify).
*   **cancellation_request** (Cancellation Request, text, required): A formal request to cancel the membership.
*   **confirmation** (Confirmation, select_one, required): Confirm that you are submitting this form.
*   **date_of_cancellation** (Date of Cancellation, date, required): Enter the date the membership is to be cancelled.
*   **membership_status** (Membership Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of your membership.
*   **signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign and date your signature to confirm your request.
*   **comments** (Comments, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the cancellation.
*   **cancellation_reason** (Cancellation Reason, select_one, required): Select a reason for cancelling the membership.
*   **other_reason** (Other Reason, text, required): If you selected "Other" as the reason for cancellation, enter a description of the reason.
*   **other_reason_description** (Other Reason Description, text, required): If you selected "Other" as the reason for cancellation, provide a description of the reason.
*   **staff_confirmation** (Staff Confirmation, text, required): Enter the staff member's confirmation of the cancellation.
*   **staff_comment** (Staff Comment, text, required): Add any comments from the staff member.
*   **staff_signature** (Staff Signature, text, required): Sign and date the staff member's signature.
*   **admin_confirmation** (Admin Confirmation, text, required): Enter the admin's confirmation of the cancellation.
*   **admin_comment** (Admin Comment, text, required): Add any comments from the admin.
*   **admin_signature** (Admin Signature, text, required): Sign and date the admin's signature.
*   **cancellation_date** (Cancellation Date, date, required): Enter the date the membership is to be cancelled.
*   **reason_for_cancellation** (Reason for Cancellation, text, required): If you selected "Other" as the reason for cancellation, enter a reason.
*   **member_comments** (Member Comments, text, required): Add any comments from the member.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing your request.
