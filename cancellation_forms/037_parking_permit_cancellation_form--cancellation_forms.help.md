# Parking Permit Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel a parking permit and gather information about the permit holder and reason for cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
- Start by filling in your name and contact information so the parking management team knows who is requesting the cancellation and how to contact you.
- Provide the permit number of the parking permit you are cancelling.
- Enter the reason for cancellation, being as detailed as possible so the parking management team understands why the permit was cancelled.
- If applicable, provide the date and time of the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **parking_permit_holder (parking permit holder)**:
	+ A field to enter the name of the permit holder.
* **reason_for_cancellation (reason for cancellation)**:
	+ A field to explain the reason for cancelling the parking permit.
* **contact_information (contact information)**:
	+ A field to enter your contact information, which will help the parking management team contact you if they need more details.
* **date_of_prophets (date of prophets)**:
	+ A field to enter the date of the cancellation. This field is likely a mistake and should be ignored.
* **time_of_prophet (time of prophet)**:
	+ A field to enter the time of the cancellation. This field is likely a mistake and should be ignored.
* **permit_number (permit number)**:
	+ A field to enter the number of the parking permit being cancelled.
