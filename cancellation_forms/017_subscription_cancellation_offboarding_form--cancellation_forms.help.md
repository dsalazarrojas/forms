# Subscription Cancellation Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from subscribers who wish to cancel their subscription. It helps to understand the reason for cancellation, provides additional details such as the cancellation date and time, and allows subscribers to provide feedback on how to improve services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for cancellation from the dropdown list (if applicable).
2. Enter the date of cancellation in the "when are you canceling" field.
3. Enter the time of cancellation using the 12-hour clock format.
4. Type a brief explanation for the cancellation reason.
5. If you have any feedback on how to improve services, type it in the "how can we improve" field.
6. Select any features you'd like to see improved in the "improved features" field (if applicable).
7. Provide your best contact information in the "what is your best contact information" field.
8. Type "yes" in the "have you agreed to the terms" field if you've accepted the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **why are you canceling** (cancellation_reason): This field is used to understand the reason for cancellation. Please select the correct reason from the dropdown list or type a brief explanation.
* **when are you canceling** (cancellation_date): Enter the date of cancellation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **at what time are you canceling** (cancellation_time): Enter the time of cancellation using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 01:30 PM).
* **what reason is this cancellation due to** (reason_for_cancellation): This field is used to provide more details about the cancellation reason. Select the correct reason from the dropdown list or type a brief explanation.
* **how can we improve** (how_can_improve): If you have any feedback on how to improve services, type it in this field.
* **are there any features youd like to see improved** (improved_features): Select any features you'd like to see improved in this field.
* **what is your best contact information** (contact_info): Provide your email address or any other contact information where you can be reached.
* **have you agreed to the terms** (agreed_terms): Type "yes" in this field if you've accepted the terms and conditions of the service.
