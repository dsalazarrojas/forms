# Cancellation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who have cancelled their service, allowing us to understand the reasons behind the cancellation and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and select the relevant sections that apply to your experience.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), and provide as much detail as possible.
3. Select all that apply for multi-select fields.
4. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Service Cancellation Survey --** (*): This is the header for the form.
* **We would like to understand your reasons for cancelling. Your feedback helps us improve our services.** (*): This section serves as a brief introduction to the purpose of the form.
* **-- Customer Information --** (*): This section collects your contact information, including:
	+ **Full Name**: Your full name as you would like to be addressed.
	+ **Email Address**: Your email address where we can contact you.
	+ **Customer or Account ID**: If you have a customer or account ID, please provide it.
* **-- Cancellation Details --** (*): This section gathers information about your cancellation:
	+ **What service are you cancelling?**: Select the service you are cancelling from the list provided.
	+ **When did you cancel?**: Provide the approximate date of cancellation.
	+ **How long were you a customer?**: Select the duration of your subscription.
* **-- Reasons for Cancellation --** (*): This section helps us understand the primary reason for your cancellation:
	+ **What is the primary reason for your cancellation?**: Select the main reason from the list provided.
* **If applicable, which issues frustrated you most?** (*): If you have experienced issues with our service, select all that apply.
* **-- Service Satisfaction --** (*): This section allows you to rate your satisfaction with our service:
	+ **How satisfied were you with our service overall?**: Rate your overall satisfaction.
	+ **How well did our service meet your needs?**: Rate your experience with our service.
	+ **How would you rate our customer support?**: Rate your experience with our customer support.
* **-- Feedback and Improvements --** (*): This section provides an opportunity to share your thoughts on how we can improve:
	+ **What could we have done better?**: Share your suggestions.
* **-- Follow-Up --** (*): This section allows us to follow up with you about your feedback:
	+ **May we follow up with you about this feedback?**: Select how you would like to be contacted.
	+ **Phone Number (if you want follow-up)**: Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and honest as possible when providing feedback.
* Select all that apply for multi-select fields.
* If you have already submitted feedback, please do not duplicate your response.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
