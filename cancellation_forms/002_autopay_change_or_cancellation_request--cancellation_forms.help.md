# Autopay Change Or Cancellation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to change or cancel an autopay payment method associated with your account. If you want to change or stop your recurring payments, please fill out this form with the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Account Holder Name, which is the name on the billing account.
2. Enter your Account Number, which is the billing account or customer number.
3. Type in your Email Address associated with the account.
4. If you have a preferred contact number, enter it in the Phone Number field.
5. Choose whether you want to change your payment method, cancel autopay, or pause it temporarily.
6. Select the date on which the change or cancellation should take effect.
7. If changing your payment method, provide the last four digits of the new method or a description.
8. Enter your authorization name to confirm the request.
9. Select the date of authorization.
10. If necessary, add any additional comments or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Holder Name** (account_holder_name, text, **Required**): This is the name of the person who owns the account.
* **Account Number** (account_number, text, **Required**): This is the billing account or customer number associated with your account.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, **Required**): This is the email address associated with your account.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, **Optional**): This is an optional preferred contact number.
* **Request Type** (request_type, select_one, **Required**): Choose whether you want to change your payment method, cancel autopay, or pause it temporarily.
* **Effective Date** (effective_date, date, **Required**): Select the date on which the change or cancellation should take effect.
* **New Payment Method** (new_payment_method, text, **Optional**): If changing your payment method, enter the last four digits of the new method or a description.
* **Authorization Name** (authorization_signature, text, **Required**): Enter your full name to confirm the request.
* **Authorization Date** (authorization_date, date, **Required**): Select the date of authorization.
* **Additional Notes** (additional_notes, text, **Optional**): Add any special instructions or comments if necessary.
