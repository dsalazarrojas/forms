# Subscription Cancellation Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Cancellation Claim Form is used to provide a structured way to submit a subscription cancellation request. This form helps the relevant department to process your cancellation request quickly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the form fields.
2.  Make sure to provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Select a valid reason for cancellation from the "Reason for Cancellation" dropdown.
4.  If you selected "Other" as the reason for cancellation, provide a detailed explanation in the "Other" field.
5.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Cancellation Claim Form**: This field is not a field you interact with directly. It is the title of the form, indicating the form's purpose.
* **Why are you cancelling**: A text field where you can explain in as much detail as necessary why you want to cancel your subscription.
* **Email**: A field where you provide your email address to contact you for any further action.
* **Date of Cancellation**: A field where you enter the date when your subscription will be cancelled. Please use the standard date format of your locale.
* **Time of Cancellation**: A field where you specify the time when your subscription will be cancelled. This is useful to ensure a coordinated cancellation process.
* **Reason for Cancellation**: A dropdown field with pre-defined options for common reasons for cancellations. Choose the reason that best describes your situation.
* **Other (please specify)**: A text field where you can provide a detailed explanation for your cancellation reason if you selected "Other" in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
* **Phone Number**: A field where you provide your phone number to contact you for any further action.
* **Other**: This field is only visible when you select "Other" as the reason for cancellation, and you must provide a detailed explanation in it.
