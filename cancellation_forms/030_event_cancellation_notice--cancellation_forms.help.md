# Event Cancellation Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Cancellation Notice form is used to inform stakeholders about an event cancellation. This form helps event planners, venue managers, school administrators, and organization representatives to notify stakeholders about the cancellation of an event in a timely and professional manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct Event Planner type from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the reason for cancellation in the Reason for Cancellation field.
3. If necessary, add additional comments in the Comments field.
4. Enter the date and time of cancellation in the Cancellation Date and Cancellation Time fields.
5. Enter the email addresses of stakeholders to be notified in the Notify Stakeholders field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, select_one, required): Select the correct title from the dropdown menu (Event Planner, Venue Manager, School Administrator, or Organization Representative) that best describes the person who is responsible for the event.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the reason for the cancellation.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the event that stakeholders may find useful.
* **Notify Stakeholders** (`notify_stakeholders`, email, optional): Enter the email addresses of stakeholders who need to be notified about the cancellation.
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of cancellation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Cancellation Time** (`cancellation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of cancellation in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for cancellation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the cancellation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide clear and concise information about the cancellation to avoid confusion.
* Use proper format for dates and times to avoid errors.
* Double-check that the correct stakeholders are notified about the cancellation.
* Save your completed form for future reference.
* Review and edit the form as necessary before finalizing the cancellation notice.
