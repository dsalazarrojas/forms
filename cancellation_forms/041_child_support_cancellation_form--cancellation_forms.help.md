# Child Support Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the cancellation of child support. Please ensure that you review the purpose of this form and the information it collects before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the required fields**: Complete all the fields marked as "required" in the form. These are:
   * Full Name of Petitioner
   * Case Reference Number
   * Requested Cancellation Date
   * Name and Birthdate of child
   * Brief explanation for the request
   * Have both parents agreed to this cancellation?
   * I understand this request is subject to court approval
   * Today Date

2. **Select the primary reason for cancellation**: Choose the correct reason for cancellation from the provided options under "Primary reason for cancellation".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Petitioner**: This field is for the person requesting the cancellation.
* **Case Reference Number**: This is the reference number listed on your court documents.
* **Requested Cancellation Date**: This is the date you would like the support to end.
* **Name and Birthdate of child**: This field captures information about the minor child.
* **Primary reason for cancellation**: Choose the reason for cancellation from the list below:
	+ Child has reached the age of majority
	+ Child has joined the military
	+ Change in legal custody status
	+ Mutual agreement between parents
* **Brief explanation for the request**: Provide any additional context or information related to the request.
* **Have both parents agreed to this cancellation?**: Choose "Yes" if both parents are in agreement, or "No" if the request is contested.
* **I understand this request is subject to court approval**: Confirm that you understand the request must be approved by a court.
* **Today Date**: This is the date on which you are submitting the form.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If you are unsure about any part of the process, please contact the relevant authorities or support staff for assistance.
