# Extended Warranty Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to cancel their extended warranties. If you're a customer looking to cancel your warranty, please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. The fields marked as "required" must be filled out before submitting the form.
2. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the product you would like to cancel the warranty for in the "Product Name" field.
4. Enter a valid reason for cancellation in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
5. Enter the date you purchased the product in the "Date of Purchase" field.
6. Enter the date you are canceling the warranty in the "Date of Cancellation" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Please ensure it's a valid 10-digit number.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
9. Enter the serial number of the product in the "Serial Number" field.
10. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
11. Confirm your warranty cancellation request in the "Confirmation of Cancellation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Extended Warranty Cancellation** (`extended_warranty_cancellation_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of why you're canceling your extended warranty.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you're canceling the warranty for.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, required): Enter a valid reason for cancellation.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, required): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`date_of_cancellation`, date, required): Enter the date you are canceling the warranty.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your valid 10-digit phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid serial number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes to your warranty cancellation request.
* **Confirmation of Cancellation** (`confirmation_of_cancellation`, text, required): Confirm that you're canceling your warranty.
