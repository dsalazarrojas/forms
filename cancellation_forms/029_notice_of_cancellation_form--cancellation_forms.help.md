# Notice Of Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the cancellation of an agreement between two parties. It is a formal notice that confirms the cancellation of the agreement and specifies the details of the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Agreement ID to identify the specific agreement being cancelled.
2. Provide the Reason For Cancellation to state the cause of the cancellation.
3. Enter the Effective Date of the cancellation, which is the date on which the cancellation becomes effective.
4. Specify the Notice Period to state how much notice was given before the effective date.
5. Enter the Cancellation Date to mark the date the agreement was cancelled.
6. Choose the Notice Method to indicate how the notice was given (e.g., email, letter, in-person).
7. Optionally, enter the Notice Period Days if the notice period is measured in days.
8. Provide a detailed Cancellation Reason to describe why the agreement was cancelled.
9. Select Notice Given To to indicate which party is giving the notice (Party A, Party B, or Both).
10. Select Notice Given By to indicate which party is receiving the notice (Individual, Business, or Other).
11. Enter the Signature Date to record the date the notice was signed.
11. Sign the form to confirm the notice of cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the agreement being cancelled.
* **Reason For Cancellation**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of why the agreement is being cancelled.
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date on which the cancellation becomes effective.
* **Notice Period**: Specify the amount of time given to the other party before the effective date.
* **Cancellation Date**: Enter the date the agreement was cancelled.
* **Notice Method**: Choose how the notice was given (e.g., email, letter, in-person).
* **Notice Period Days**: Optionally, enter the number of days given for notice.
* **Cancellation Reason**: Provide a detailed explanation of why the agreement was cancelled.
* **Notice Given To**: Select which party is giving the notice (Party A, Party B, or Both).
* **Notice Given By**: Select which party is receiving the notice (Individual, Business, or Other).
* **Signature Date**: Record the date the notice was signed.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the notice of cancellation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a complete and formal notice of cancellation.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
