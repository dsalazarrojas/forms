# Policy Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Cancellation Form is used to inform us of policy cancellations. This allows us to update our systems and ensure that you receive the most up-to-date information about your policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct cancellation option from the "Auto Vehicle Cancellation" dropdown.
2. Enter the reason for cancellation in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
3. Provide the date and time of cancellation.
4. Enter the cancellation message.
5. Optionally, provide your policy ID, policy type, contact email, contact phone number, and additional notes.
6. If applicable, select the assigned tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auto Vehicle Cancellation** (`auto_vehicle_cancellation`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the type of cancellation you are submitting. This will help us process the cancellation correctly.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of why your policy was cancelled.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`date_of_cancellation`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date the policy was cancelled.
* **Time of Cancellation** (`time_of_cancellation`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time the policy was cancelled.
* **Cancellation Message** (`cancellation_message`, text):
	+ Enter a message explaining the cancellation. This will help us communicate with you and update our systems.
* **Policy ID** (`policy_id`, number):
	+ Enter your policy ID if you have one.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one):
	+ Enter the type of policy you had (e.g. Auto, Home, Rent).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email):
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the cancellation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one):
	+ If applicable, select the tool assigned to the cancellation.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for assistance.
