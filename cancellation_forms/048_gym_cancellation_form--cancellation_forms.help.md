# Gym Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the cancellation of a gym membership. Please fill out this form to inform the gym management of your decision to cancel your membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. In the cancellation_reason field, describe why you are cancelling your membership.
5. Enter the date for the cancellation in the date field.
6. Submit the form to complete your cancellation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (first_name, text, Required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, Required): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (email, email, Required): Enter your email address for gym communication and correspondence.
* **Cancellation reason** (cancellation_reason, text, Required): Describe the reason for your cancellation, which helps us understand your concerns and address them accordingly.
* **Cancellation date** (date, date, Required): Enter the date you would like to cancel your membership.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for a smooth and efficient cancellation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* The cancellation date entered will be used as a reference for the cancellation process. If you need to make any changes, please contact the gym management directly.
