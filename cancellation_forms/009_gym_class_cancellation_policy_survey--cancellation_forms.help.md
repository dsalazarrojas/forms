# Gym Class Cancellation Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey to collect feedback from users who have experienced the cancellation of a gym class. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our current cancellation policy and make improvements to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form carefully and honestly.
2. Fill out all required fields (see below for required fields).
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or information that you would like to share about your experience with gym class cancellation.

* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Option 1: Select this if your class was a group fitness class.
	+ Option 2: Select this if your class was a specialized class (e.g., yoga, Pilates).
	+ Option 3: Select this if your class was a private coaching session.

* **Cancellation Rule** (`cancellation_rule`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Select this if you were not notified in advance about the cancellation.
	+ Option 2: Select this if the cancellation was not clear or confusing.
	+ Option 3: Select this if you were not able to reschedule the class.

* **Awareness Level** (`awareness_level`, number, optional): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate how aware you were of the cancellation policy before the class was cancelled.

* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the cancellation process.

* **Comments** (`comment`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share about your experience with gym class cancellation.

* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about this matter.

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.

* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional):
	+ Option 1: Submit your feedback.
	+ Option 2: Do not submit your feedback. 
	+ Option 3: Cancel.
