# Acord Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist policyholders in requesting the cancellation of their policy with a specific insurance provider. It is intended to capture relevant information for the cancellation process, ensuring that the policyholder's needs are accurately represented and their request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the policyholder's name as it appears on the policy.
2. Enter the policy number associated with the policy being cancelled.
3. Select the type of insurance policy that is being cancelled.
4. Provide the effective date and time of the cancellation (if applicable).
5. Specify the reason for cancellation, and provide any additional details if necessary.
6. Choose how any unearned premium should be handled.
7. If the policyholder's address has changed, provide the new address.
8. Sign the form to authorize the cancellation.
9. Enter the date the cancellation was requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Policyholder Name (`policyholder_name`, text, required)
• **Policyholder Name**: Enter the policyholder's name as it appears on the policy. This is a critical piece of information to ensure the cancellation request is accurately processed.

### Policy Number (`policy_number`, text, required)
• **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the policy being cancelled.

### Type of Insurance (`type_of_insurance`, select_one, required)
• **Type of Insurance**: Select the type of insurance policy being cancelled (e.g., Auto, Homeowners, Commercial General Liability, Workers Comp, Life).

### Effective Date of Cancellation (`effective_date_of_cancellation`, date, required)
• **Effective Date of Cancellation**: Enter the date on which the policy will be cancelled.

### Effective Time of Cancellation (optional) (`effective_time_of_cancellation`, time, optional)
• **Effective Time of Cancellation**: Enter the specific time the policy will be cancelled (if applicable).

### Reason for Cancellation (`reason_for_cancellation`, select_one, required)
• **Reason for Cancellation**: Specify the reason for cancelling the policy (e.g., Policy replaced, Property sold, Business closed, Premium too high, Other).

### Other Reason Details (optional) (`other_reason_details`, text, optional)
• **Other Reason Details**: If you selected "Other" as the reason for cancellation, provide additional details to explain why the policy is being cancelled.

### Return Premium Method (`return_premium_method`, select_one, required)
• **Return Premium Method**: Choose how any unearned premium should be handled (e.g., Mail check to policyholder, Apply to other policy, Send to agent).

### Forwarding Address (optional) (`forwarding_address_if_changed`, text, optional)
• **Forwarding Address**: If the policyholder's address has changed, enter the new address.

### Policyholder Signature (`policyholder_signature`, text, required)
• **Policyholder Signature**: Type your name to authorize the cancellation.

### Request Date (`request_date`, date, required)
• **Request Date**: Enter the date the cancellation was requested.

### Agent / Broker Name (optional) (`agent_broker_name`, text, optional)
• **Agent / Broker Name**: Enter the name of your insurance representative (if applicable).

This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to understand. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
