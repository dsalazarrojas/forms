# wedding_photography_contract_cancellation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and documentation for a wedding photography contract cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name and contact information in the "Client" field.
2. Select the reason for cancellation from the "Why are you cancelling?" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date of cancellation in the "Date of cancellation" field.
4. Enter the amount to be refunded in the "Payment Refund" field.
5. Enter the amount actually refunded in the "Amount Refund" field.
6. Select the payment method for the refund in the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
7. Check boxes indicating that the client and photographer have read and understood the cancellation policy.
8. Sign and date the document with the "Client's Signature", "Photographer's Signature", and "Date Signed" fields.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client** (Text): Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Why are you cancelling?** (Text): Select the reason for cancellation from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of cancellation** (Date): Enter the date of the cancellation.
* **Payment Refund** (Number): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Amount Refund** (Number): Enter the amount actually refunded.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Select the method of payment for the refund.
* **Photographer's response** (Text): Enter a message from the photographer.
* **Message from Photographer** (Text): Enter a message from the photographer.
* **Client's Consent** (Select One): Check the box indicating that the client has read and understood the cancellation policy.
* **Photographer's Consent** (Select One): Check the box indicating that the photographer has read and understood the cancellation policy.
* **Date of Original Booking** (Date): Enter the date of the original booking (optional).
* **Payment Status** (Select One): Check the box indicating the payment status.
* **Refund Status** (Select One): Check the box indicating the refund status.
* **Additional Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Photographer** (Text): Enter the photographer's name.
* **Contact Photographer** (Text): Enter the photographer's contact information.
* **Email Photographer** (Text): Enter the photographer's email address (optional).
* **Client's Signature** (Note): Sign and date the document with the client's signature.
* **Photographer's Signature** (Note): Sign and date the document with the photographer's signature.
* **Date Signed** (Date): Enter the date signed.
* **Cancellation policy** (Text): Enter the cancellation policy.
* **Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments from the photographer.
* **Client Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments from the client.
* **Cancellation fees** (Number): Enter any cancellation fees.
* **Payment Terms** (Text): Enter any payment terms.
* **Payment Status** (Select One): Check the box indicating the payment status.
* **Refund Status** (Select One): Check the box indicating the refund status.
* **Additional Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Photographer's contact** (Text): Enter the photographer's contact information.
* **Client's Consent** (Select One): Check the box indicating that the client has read and understood the cancellation policy.
* **Photographer's Consent** (Select One): Check the box indicating that the photographer has read and understood the cancellation policy.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review and understand the cancellation policy before proceeding with the cancellation process.
* Ensure that both the client and photographer have read and understood the cancellation policy.
* Use clear and concise language when entering additional notes or comments.
* Double-check the payment status and refund status to ensure accuracy.
* Keep a record of the cancellation process and communication with the client.
