# farmers_insurance_cancellation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for policyholders or their representatives to initiate the cancellation of their farmer's insurance policy. It will guide you through the process of providing necessary information to complete the cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the field labels.
- Select the reason for cancellation from the dropdown menu.
- Enter the policyholder's name, address, phone, and email if required.
- Optionally add any comments for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Number (Policy Number, text, optional)**: Enter the policy number to identify the policy to be cancelled.
* **Reason for Cancellation (reason_for_cancellation, select_multiple, optional)**: Select one or more from the list to provide a valid reason for cancelling the policy.
* **Policyholder Name (name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the policyholder who initiated the cancellation.
* **Policyholder Address (address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the policyholder to verify their information.
* **Policyholder Phone (phone, text, optional)**: Enter the contact phone number of the policyholder.
* **Policyholder Email (email, email, optional)**: Enter the policyholder's email address for communication purposes.
* **Reason (reason, text, optional)**: Enter any additional reason for cancellation if not already selected.
* **Comments (comments, note, optional)**: Add any comments or notes for further explanation or clarification.

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
