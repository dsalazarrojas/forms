# Pet Service Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Service Termination Request Form is used to request the termination of pet services. Please fill out this form to initiate the termination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the date of termination.
2.  Provide the effective date of termination.
3.  Enter your confirmation.
4.  Enter the reason for cancellation.
5.  Enter your name.
6.  Enter your phone number.
7.  Enter your email address.
8.  Enter your address.
9.  Enter the name of the pet.
10.  Select the type of pet.
11.  Enter the pet breed.
12.  Enter the age of the pet.
13.  Select the termination status.
14.  Select the follow-up status.
15.  Enter the follow-up date (if follow-up is required).
16.  Enter follow-up details (if follow-up is required).
17.  Enter any additional notes.
18.  Enter follow-up notes (if follow-up is required).
19.  Enter the next follow-up date (if follow-up is required).
20.  Enter next follow-up details (if follow-up is required).
21.  Select the next follow-up status (if follow-up is required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Date of Termination** (text, optional): The date the pet service will be terminated.
*   **Effective Date** (date, optional): The date the termination will take effect.
*   **Confirmation** (text, optional): Confirm that you wish to terminate the pet service.
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (text, optional): The reason for cancelling the pet service.
*   **Customer Name** (text, optional): Your name.
*   **Customer Phone** (text, optional): Your phone number.
*   **Customer Email** (email, optional): Your email address.
*   **Customer Address** (text, optional): Your address.
*   **Pet Name** (text, optional): The name of the pet.
*   **Pet Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of pet.
*   **Pet Breed** (text, optional): The breed of the pet.
*   **Pet Age** (number, optional): The age of the pet.
*   **Termination Status** (select_one, optional): Select the termination status.
*   **Follow-up Status** (select_one, optional): Select the follow-up status.
*   **Follow-up Date** (date, optional): The date of the follow-up (if follow-up is required).
*   **Follow-up Details** (text, optional): Details of the follow-up (if follow-up is required).
*   **Notes** (text, optional): Any additional notes.
*   **Follow-up Notes** (text, optional): Notes for the follow-up (if follow-up is required).
*   **Next Follow-up Date** (date, optional): The date of the next follow-up (if follow-up is required).
*   **Next Follow-up Details** (text, optional): Details of the next follow-up (if follow-up is required).
*   **Next Follow-up Status** (select_one, optional): Select the next follow-up status (if follow-up is required).
