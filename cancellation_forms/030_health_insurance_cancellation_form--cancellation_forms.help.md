# Health Insurance Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for an individual to cancel their health insurance policy. It captures essential information to process the cancellation request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your name as the policyholder.
2.  Enter your health insurance policy number.
3.  Select the date of the cancellation request.
4.  Choose the reason for cancellation (e.g., Cancellation of Policy, Change of Coverage, or Other).
5.  Enter the cancellation request date.
6.  Choose the status of the cancellation request (e.g., Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
7.  If necessary, enter a signature from the policyholder.
8.  If necessary, enter the authorized signer's name.
9.  Enter the effective date of cancellation.
10. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for cancellation.
11. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **policyholder name** (`policyholder_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the individual cancelling their health insurance policy.
* **policy number** (`policy_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the health insurance policy number associated with the policyholder.
* **date of cancellation request** (`date_of_cancellation_request`, date, required/optional): Select the date when the cancellation request was made.
* **reason for cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the reason for cancellation from the available options (Cancellation of Policy, Change of Coverage, or Other).
* **cancellation request date** (`cancellation_request_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the cancellation request was made.
* **cancellation date** (`cancellation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the cancellation will be effective.
* **cancellation request status** (`cancellation_request_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the cancellation request from the available options (Pending, Completed, Cancelled).
* **policyholder signature** (`policyholder_signature`, text, required/optional): If required, enter the signature of the policyholder.
* **authorized signer** (`authorized_signer`, text, required/optional): If necessary, enter the name of the authorized signer.
* **cancellation date effective** (`cancellation_date_effective`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the cancellation will be effective.
* **cancellation reason** (`cancellation_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief explanation of the reason for cancellation.
* **cancellation request details** (`cancellation_request_details`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about the cancellation request.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the cancellation request.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the provided instructions above.
- If you need help, contact us for assistance.
