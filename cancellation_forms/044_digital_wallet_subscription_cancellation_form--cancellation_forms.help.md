# Digital Wallet Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Wallet Subscription Cancellation Form is designed to help users cancel their digital wallet subscription. This form collects necessary information from users to facilitate a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general submission form on page 1 with your information.
2. Move to page 2 to provide subscription details.
3. On page 3, select at least one reason for cancellation from the multiple options provided.
4. On page 4, choose a specific reason for cancellation from the single option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Submission Form** (`form_page_1`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information you'd like to provide as a customer.
* **Subscription Information** (`page_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your subscription, such as subscription type or plan number.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to multiple reasons for cancelling your subscription. Select at least one reason to ensure your form is complete.
* **Cancellation Reason 2** (`cancellation_reason_2`, select_one, required): Select a specific reason for cancellation to complete the form.
