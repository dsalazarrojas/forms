# Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are cancelling their subscription. It aims to understand the reasons behind their cancellation and gather feedback on our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the reason for your subscription cancellation.
2. Provide some feedback about our services. You can write as much or as little as you like.
3. On a scale of 0-5, rate your satisfaction with our services.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend our services to others.
5. Enter the date you requested cancellation.
6. Add any additional notes about your cancellation (optional).
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Cancellation by Choice
* Select this option if you are cancelling your subscription by choice.
### Feedback
* Please provide some feedback about our services, such as things we could improve on or any positive experiences you've had.
### On a scale of 0-5, how satisfied were you with our services?
* Rate your satisfaction with our services, with 0 being extremely dissatisfied and 5 being extremely satisfied.
### Would you recommend our services to others?
* Select this option if you would recommend our services to others.
### Strongly
* Select this option if you strongly recommend our services to others.
### Somewhat
* Select this option if you somewhat recommend our services to others.
### Not at all
* Select this option if you do not recommend our services to others.
### What date did you request cancellation?
* Enter the date you requested cancellation of your subscription.
### Additional notes about cancellation?
* Add any additional notes or comments about your cancellation. (Optional)
### Email
* Enter your email address so we can contact you about your cancellation.
### Phone
* Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your cancellation.
