## Intent To Withdraw From Academy - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for students to notify the academy of their intent to withdraw. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth transition for both the student and the academy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student ID
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID number as provided by the academy. This helps the academy track your student record.

### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. This is used to identify you as a student in the academy's records.

### Student Email
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address as registered with the academy. This helps the academy contact you regarding your withdrawal.

### Reason for Withdrawal
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise explanation for your withdrawal. This helps the academy understand your reasons and process your withdrawal smoothly.

### Date of Last Attendance
* **Date of Last Attendance** (`date_of_last_attendance`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last attendance at the academy. This helps the academy track your attendance history.

### Expected Date of Exit
* **Expected Date of Exit** (`expected_date_of_exit`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to leave the academy. This helps the academy plan your departure.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information, such as phone numbers or addresses, that the academy can use to reach you.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for your digital signature, confirming that you have read and understood the withdrawal process.
