# Business Termination Notice Letter Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Termination Notice Letter Generator is a tool to help create a formal notice of termination for various business agreements and contracts.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's information, including name, address, and contact details.
2. Enter the name and contact information for the recipient company or individual.
3. Select the type of agreement being terminated (e.g., Service Agreement, Vendor Contract, Employment Agreement, etc.).
4. Choose the date when the original agreement was signed and/or when it should be terminated.
5. Select the reason for termination from the provided options or enter a custom reason.
6. Enter any additional details about the termination, such as notice period or final payments.
7. Review and confirm your input before generating the notice letter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Company Name** (`sender_company_name`): Enter your company's name to appear on the notice letter.
* **Your Company Address** (`sender_address`): Enter your company's complete mailing address.
* **City, State, Postal Code** (`sender_city_state`): Enter your city, state, and postal code.
* **Contact Person Name** (`sender_contact_person`): Enter the name of the person signing the letter.
* **Contact Person Title** (`sender_title`): Enter the contact person's title or position.
* **Contact Email Address** (`sender_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`sender_phone`): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Recipient Company or Vendor Name** (`recipient_company_name`): Enter the name of the company or vendor being notified.
* **Recipient Contact Person Name** (`recipient_contact_person`): Enter the name of the person to receive the notice.
* **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`): Enter the recipient's complete mailing address.
* **City, State, Postal Code** (`recipient_city_state`): Enter the recipient's city, state, and postal code.
* **Type of Agreement Being Terminated** (`agreement_type`): Select the type of agreement being terminated.
* **Original Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`): Enter the date when the original agreement was signed.
* **Agreement or Contract Number** (`agreement_number`): Enter a reference number for the agreement or contract (optional).
* **Termination Effective Date** (`termination_date`): Enter the date when the termination should take effect.
* **Notice Period Given** (`notice_period`): Choose the notice period given to the recipient.
* **Reason for Termination** (`termination_reason`): Select the reason for termination or enter a custom reason.
* **Additional Details About Termination** (`termination_reason_details`): Enter any further explanation or context about the termination.
* **Outstanding Payment Information** (`final_payment_details`): Enter any final payments or settlements needed.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions** (`additional_terms`): Enter any transition plans, return of materials, or other relevant details.
