# Pet Wellness Plan Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Wellness Plan Cancellation Form is used by pet owners to communicate their intent to cancel their pet's wellness plan. This form is used to gather necessary information for the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your pet's name in the "pet_name" field.
2. Provide a reason for cancellation in the "reason" field.
3. Select the species of your pet from the "pet_species" dropdown list.
4. Enter the name of the veterinarian who is currently caring for your pet in the "veterinarian" field.
5. Provide a reason for the cancellation in the "reason_for_cancellation" field.
6. Enter the date of cancellation in the "cancellation_date" field.
7. Enter any additional cancellation notes in the "cancellation_note" field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the person assigned to care for your pet in the "assigned_to" field.
9. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
11. Confirm that you have read and understood the cancellation by selecting "True" in the "confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name**: Enter the name of your pet as it is registered on their wellness plan.
* **reason**: Provide a brief reason for cancellation, if applicable.
* **pet_species**: Select the species of your pet from the dropdown list.
* **veterinarian**: Enter the name of your pet's current veterinarian.
* **reason_for_cancellation**: Provide a reason for cancellation, if applicable.
* **cancellation_date**: Enter the date of cancellation.
* **cancellation_reason**: Enter any additional cancellation notes or reasons.
* **cancellation_note**: Enter any additional information or comments about the cancellation.
* **assigned_to**: If applicable, enter the name of the person assigned to care for your pet.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **email**: Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understood the cancellation by selecting "True".
