# Registration Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the cancellation of registration in a system, likely for a user or participant in a course, event, or similar activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your information, including your email address and any other required details in the "User Info" field.
2. Select the reason for the cancellation from the list provided in the "reason" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the cancellation in the "Date of Cancellation" and "Time of Cancellation" fields.
4. Provide any additional notes about the cancellation in the "notes" field.
5. Confirm whether the cancellation is true by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm Cancellation" field.
6. Review your submission and click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and other contact information to identify yourself.
* **user_email** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address to contact you about the cancellation.
* **reason** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more reasons for cancelling the registration.
* **Date of Cancellation** (Date, Optional): Enter the date on which the cancellation takes effect.
* **Time of Cancellation** (Time, Optional): Enter the time at which the cancellation takes effect.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations about the cancellation.
* **Confirm Cancellation** (Select One, Optional): Select "True" to confirm the cancellation or "False" to indicate that it is not cancelled.
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Review and submit your cancellation information.
