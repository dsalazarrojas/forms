# Document Scanning Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to cancel their document scanning subscription service. Please complete it to let us know you're ending your agreement with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
2. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. Ensure you're providing accurate information to facilitate efficient processing and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field in this form has a specific purpose:

- **User Input**: A text field for entering additional information or comments.
  * (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations related to your cancellation.

- **Reason for Cancellation**: A text field for entering the reason for cancellation.
  * (`cancellation_reason`, text, optional): Please specify the reason for cancelling your subscription.

- **Date of Last Service**: A date field for specifying the date of the last service provided.
  * (`cancellation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last service you received from us.

- **Last Service Details**: A text field for providing more information about the last service.
  * (`last_service_details`, text, optional): Describe the last service you received and why you're canceling.

- **Customer Signature**: A text field for entering your signature.
  * (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm your cancellation.

- **Provider Feedback**: A select-one field for rating the service quality.
  * (`provider_feedback`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our service. Select one of the options:
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor

- **Request Date**: A date field for specifying the date you're canceling your subscription.
  * (`cancellation_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're canceling your subscription.

Note: Completing this form accurately and thoroughly will help us to process your cancellation efficiently and effectively.
