# Software License Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software License Termination Request Form is used to formally request the cancellation of a software license. When you fill out this form, you will be providing necessary information for the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Employee ID to associate your request with your account.
2. Enter the Software Name that you would like to cancel the license for.
3. Provide the License Number for the software license you would like to cancel.
4. Enter the Reason for Cancellation, explaining why you are requesting the cancellation.
5. Select the Date of Cancellation, using the calendar to ensure you are entering the correct date.
6. Confirm that your Manager has approved the cancellation request by selecting 'Yes' from the Manager Approval dropdown menu.
7. If you have any additional comments or information to provide, you can enter them in the Comments field (optional).
8. Enter your name and title to confirm that you submitted the request.
9. Enter the name of the Manager who approved or will approve the cancellation request.
10. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to begin the cancellation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (required): Enter your unique Employee ID to associate this request with your account.
* **Software Name** (required): Enter the name of the software that you would like to cancel the license for.
* **License Number** (required): Enter the License Number for the software you are canceling the license for.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (required): Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting the cancellation.
* **Date of Cancellation** (required): Select the date when you would like to cancel the software license. Using the calendar will ensure you enter the correct date.
* **Manager Approval** (required): Confirm that your Manager has approved the cancellation request by selecting 'Yes'.
* **Comments** (optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide, enter them here.
* **Submitted By** (optional): Enter your name and title to confirm that you submitted the request.
* **Submitted To** (optional): Enter the name of the Manager who approved or will approve the cancellation request.
* **Manager Name** (required): Enter the name of the Manager who approved or will approve the cancellation request.

**Tips**: Make sure to review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your Manager for guidance.
