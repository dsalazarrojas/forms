# Insurance Agency Resignation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help insurance agency employees or agents notify their intention to resign and provide a clear understanding of their reasons for resignation.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the following information:
    - Agency Name: Enter the name of the insurance agency you are resigning from.
    - Reason for Resignation: Describe the reason for your resignation. This could be due to a new opportunity, personal reasons, or other reasons you would like to share.
    - Date of Resignation: Enter the date you plan to officially leave the agency.
    - Reason for Resignation Details: Provide more details about your reason for resignation, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Name (Agency Name, text, required/optional)**: Enter the official name of the insurance agency you are resigning from.
* **Reason for Resignation (reason_for_resignation, text, required/optional)**: Describe the reason for your resignation. You can be as brief or detailed as you want to be.
* **Date of Resignation (date_of_resignation, date, required/optional)**: Enter the date you plan to officially leave the agency. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason for Resignation Details (reason_for_resignation_details, text, required/optional)**: Provide more details about your reason for resignation, if necessary. This is the space to elaborate on your previously entered reason for resignation.

## Tips
- Fill in the form fields as truthfully and completely as possible.
- Use a standard date format when filling out the Date of Resignation field.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your agency representative for assistance.
- Once you complete the form, make sure to submit it for final review and approval.
