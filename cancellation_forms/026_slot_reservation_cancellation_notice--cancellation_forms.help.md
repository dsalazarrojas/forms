# Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inform the management team about a slot cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for the cancellation.
3. Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice** (`slot_reservation_cancellation_notice`): This is the main field where you need to write a clear and concise notice about the cancellation.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Explain the reason for the cancellation. This could be due to any reason such as no-show, change of plans, or any other valid reason.
* **Scheduled For** (`scheduled_for`): Select the time when the slot was scheduled to take place. This is to help the management team understand when the cancellation occurred.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Provide a clear and concise reason for the cancellation.
* Only submit this form once for each cancellation. If you need to cancel multiple slots, fill out the form multiple times.
