# Digital Health App Subscription Cancellation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Health App Subscription Cancellation form is used to process customer requests for cancelling their subscription to the digital health app. This form gathers necessary information for the cancellation process, including the customer's details, reason for cancellation, and confirmation of the cancellation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Select the reason for cancellation from the options provided.
3. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your subscription cancellation.
4. Provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
5. Confirm the cancellation date for your subscription.
6. Provide a detailed reason for your cancellation.
7. Indicate whether you would like to be contacted by our customer service representative.
8. Confirm your cancellation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancellation Request** (`cancellation_request`, text, optional): Please describe your request for cancellation in a few words. This is a brief description of your reason for wanting to cancel your subscription.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, optional): Select the reason for your cancellation from the options provided. This will help us understand your situation better.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your account or in our records.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your subscription cancellation.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like your subscription to be cancelled.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, note, optional): Provide a detailed explanation for your cancellation request.
* **Customer Service Representative** (`customer_service_rep`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would like to be contacted by our customer service representative.
* **Cancellation Status** (`cancellation_status`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your cancellation status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
