# Adoption Cancellation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adoption Cancellation Agreement form is used for the adopter to cancel the adoption of a pet. This form is essential to document the reason for the cancellation and ensure a smooth transfer of ownership.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to select the correct reason for cancellation from the provided options.
- Enter the correct date of the cancellation.
- Indicate the current location of the pet.
- Check the refund policy and select whether a refund is requested or not.
- Sign the form electronically to confirm your identity and authenticate the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Original Adopter Name**: Enter your name as it appears on the original adoption contract.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Identifies the original adopter who is cancelling the adoption.
- **Pet Name and ID**: Enter the name and ID of the pet being cancelled.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Identifies the pet in question.
- **Original Adoption Date**: Enter the date the pet was originally adopted.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Records the original adoption date.
- **Cancellation Effective Date**: Enter the date the adoption will be cancelled.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Records the cancellation date.
- **Primary Reason**: Select the most accurate reason for cancellation from the options provided.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Provides a reason for cancellation.
- **Detailed Explanation**: Enter a more detailed explanation of the reason for cancellation.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Offers additional context for the decision.
- **Pet Return Status**: Select the current location of the pet.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Records the pet's current status.
- **Refund Requested**: Check if a refund is requested based on the 30-day refund policy.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Indicates if a refund is needed.
- **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name to confirm your identity.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Authenticate the cancellation.
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the electronic signature.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
  - Purpose: Records the date of the electronic signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Check the refund policy to ensure you are eligible for a refund.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the organization for assistance.
