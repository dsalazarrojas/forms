# performance_cancellation_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance_cancellation_claim_form is used to document the cancellation of an event due to unforeseen circumstances. This form helps to gather information for future reference and ensures that the cancellation process is completed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information as the performer in the "Performer Information" section.
2.  Provide event details in the "Event Information" section.
3.  Enter the claim details in the "Claim Details" section.
4.  Enter payment information, if applicable, in the "Payment Information" section.
5.  Select the "Date of Event" and "Time of Event" for the performance.
6.  Choose the type of performance you were scheduled to do (e.g., Live, Recording).
7.  Specify the reason for the cancellation in the "Reason for Cancellation" dropdown menu.
8.  Enter the date and time of cancellation.
9.  Enter any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" section, if applicable.
10. Enter contact information for the claimant and organizer, if applicable.
11. Confirm the claimant and organizer's agreements, if applicable.
12. Choose the reason for cancellation (if not already selected) and add any additional information.
13. Finally, upload proof or documents as required, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performer Information** (`performer_information`, text, required/optional): This section is used to capture the performer's information, including their name, contact number, and other relevant details.
*   **Event Information** (`event_information`, text, required/optional): Enter details about the event being cancelled, such as the event name and date.
*   **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the claim details, including any relevant information about the reason for the cancellation.
*   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter payment information related to the event.
*   **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event that is being cancelled.
*   **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event that is being cancelled.
*   **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, select_multiple, required/true): Choose the type of performance you were scheduled to do (e.g., Live, Recording).
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, select_one, required/true): Choose the reason for the cancellation from the dropdown menu.
*   **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, required/true): Enter the date of the cancellation.
*   **Cancellation Time** (`cancellation_time`, time, required/true): Enter the time of the cancellation.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or explanations.
*   **Claimant Contact Info** (`claimant_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's contact information.
*   **Organizer Contact Info** (`organizer_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the organizer's contact information.
*   **Claimant Agreement** (`claimant_agreement`, text, required/optional): Confirm the claimant's agreement.
*   **Organizer Agreement** (`organizer_agreement`, text, required/optional): Confirm the organizer's agreement.
*   **Other Reason** (`other_reason`, text, required/optional): Add any other reason for cancellation, if not already selected.
*   **Claimant's Signature** (`signature_claimant`, select_one, required/true): Select one of the two options, or type in your own reason.
*   **Organizer's Signature** (`signature_organizer`, select_one, required/true): Select one of the two options, or type in your own reason.
*   **Upload Proof** (`upload_proof`, text, required/optional): Upload proof or documents related to the cancellation.
*   **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, required/optional): Upload documents related to the cancellation.
