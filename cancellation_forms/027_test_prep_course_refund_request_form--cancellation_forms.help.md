# Test Prep Course Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who want to request a refund for a Test Prep Course. Fill out this form to initiate a refund request.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
* Click on the "Next" button to move through the pages of the form.
* Fill out the required fields on each page. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Refund Request**: 
  (Select Multiple, required)
  This field allows you to choose the reason for your refund request. Select one or more options from the list.
* **Student First Name:**
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the first name of the student submitting the refund request.
* **Student Last Name:**
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the last name of the student submitting the refund request.
* **Student Email:**
  (Email, Optional)
  Enter the student's email address associated with their registration.
* **Student Phone:**
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter the student's phone number associated with their registration.
* **Student Program:**
  (Select One, Optional)
  Select the student's program from the list. If your program is not listed, use the "Student Program (Other)" field below.
* **Student Program (Other):**
  (Text, Optional)
  If your program is not listed, enter the name of your program here.
* **Student Date of Birth:**
  (Date, Optional)
  Enter the date of birth of the student submitting the refund request.
* **Refund Date:**
  (Time, Optional)
  Enter the date and time when you would like to receive the refund.
* **Reason for Refund:**
  (Text, Optional)
  Enter a brief explanation for your refund request.
## Tips
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please contact our support team.
* Please ensure that you have attached any required documents or supporting information with your refund request.
* Refund requests will be processed as per our refund policy.
