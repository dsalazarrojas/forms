# Vitamin Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist customers in cancelling their vitamin subscription. Please note that this form is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform, and is released under CC BY 4.0.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Cancellation Form** field. This is the main section of the form where you can provide your reason for cancelling your subscription.
2.  Provide **Subscription Details**. Please enter your subscription information, including any relevant details about your account or subscription.
3.  Share your **Feedback**. This is an optional section where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions for us to improve our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancellation Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: optional)
	+ This is the main section of the form where you can provide your reason for cancelling your subscription. Please type in a clear and concise explanation of why you want to cancel your subscription.
* **Subscription Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: optional)
	+ Please provide any relevant details about your account or subscription, such as your account number or subscription plan.
* **Feedback** (`3`, `text`, required/optional: optional)
	+ This is an optional section where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions for us to improve our service. If you have any suggestions or ideas, please feel free to share them! 

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional, but we encourage you to fill out as much information as possible to help us better assist you with your cancellation request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Once you have completed the form, please submit it and we will be in touch with further instructions on how to proceed with your cancellation request.
