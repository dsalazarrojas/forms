The Corporate social responsibility (CSR) survey template was created to understand how employees perceive and engage with our company's initiatives. This form aims to gather valuable insights on their experience with our CSR program, including how satisfied they are with the process, their participation frequency, and what they'd like to improve about it. To avoid duplication of effort, please complete this form if you have not already participated in a CSR survey within the past 3 months.

# Corporate Social Responsibility CSR Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on your experience with the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) survey process. Your input will help us understand how to improve the process for future surveys.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions with the following considerations:
    * For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
    * For questions with a text field, type your answer in the provided field.
2.  Make sure to answer all required fields.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **How satisfied are you with the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) survey?**
    *   (`satisfied`, `single`, required)
        How satisfied you are with the CSR survey, with options ranging from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
*  **How often do you participate in surveys related to corporate social responsibility?**
    *   (`frequent`, `single`, required)
        This question asks about your participation frequency in CSR surveys, with options from Frequently to Never.
*  **Which of the following applied to your experience with this CSR survey? (Select all that apply)**
    *   (`clear`, `relevant`, `easy`, `confusing`, `too long`, `irrelevant`, multiple, optional)
        Choose all the descriptors that best fit your experience with the CSR survey. You can select multiple options.
*  **What would you improve or change about the CSR survey process?**
    *   (`text`, optional)
        Share your thoughts on how the CSR survey process could be improved.
*  **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding CSR surveys?**
    *   (`text`, optional)
        If you have any further thoughts or concerns about CSR surveys, this is the field to share them.
