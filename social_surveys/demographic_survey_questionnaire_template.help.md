# demographic_survey_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather demographic information for social research and analysis. The data collected will help researchers understand the characteristics of the population being surveyed and analyze the results in a more accurate and representative manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions according to your personal information.
2.  Select the age group that best represents your age.
3.  Choose the option that best describes your gender identity.
4.  Select all the options that apply to your current employment status.
5.  Choose the income range that best represents your approximate annual household income.
6.  You may optionally provide any additional demographic information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age group?** (`Under 18`, `18-29`, `30-44`, `45-59`, `60-74`, `75 or older`, `Required`)
	+ This field collects the age group of the respondent.
* **What is your gender identity?** (`Woman`, `Man`, `Non-binary`, `Prefer not to say`, `Required`)
	+ This field collects the gender identity of the respondent.
* **What is your highest level of education completed?** (`High School`, `Some College`, `Associate's Degree`, `Bachelor's Degree`, `Master's Degree`, `Doctoral Degree`, `Required`)
	+ This field collects the highest level of education completed by the respondent.
* **Which of the following best describes your current employment status?** (`Under $25K`, `$25K-$49K`, `$50K-$74K`, `$75K-$99K`, `$100K-$149K`, `$150K+`, `Required`)
	+ This field collects the respondent's current employment status.
* **Do you have any additional demographic information you'd like to share?** (`text`, `Optional`)
	+ This field is optional and allows respondents to provide any additional demographic information they'd like to share.
