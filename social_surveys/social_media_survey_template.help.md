# social_media_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback on their social media experience, including their habits, satisfaction, and perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select your satisfaction level with your overall social media experience in the "How satisfied are you with your overall social media experience?" field.
2. Indicate how often you interact with social media platforms by selecting the frequency that best describes your behavior in the "How often do you interact with social media platforms?" field.
3. Choose the reason(s) that best describe your main reason for using social media in the "Which of the following best describes your main reason for using social media?" field. You can select multiple options.
4. In the "Which of the following applies to your experience with social media? (select all that apply)" field, choose all that apply to your current experience.
5. Provide your thoughts on what you would like to improve or change about your social media experience in the "What would you most like to improve or change about your social media experience?" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or feedback regarding social media, you can provide them in the "Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding social media?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your overall social media experience?**: This question aims to gauge your satisfaction level with your social media experience. Please select the option that best reflects your feelings about your social media experience.
* **How often do you interact with social media platforms?**: This question is designed to understand how frequently you engage with social media platforms. Choose the frequency that best describes your behavior.
* **Which of the following best describes your main reason for using social media?**: This question is meant to identify your primary motivation for using social media. Select the reason(s) that best apply to you.
* **Which of the following applies to your experience with social media? (select all that apply)**: In this field, choose all the options that apply to your current experience with social media. This will help to identify any areas of concern or issues you face.
* **What would you most like to improve or change about your social media experience?**: Here, you can express your thoughts on what you would like to improve or change about your social media experience. Be as specific as possible.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding social media?**: If you have any further comments or suggestions about social media, you can provide them in this field.

## Tips
* Please be honest and genuine in your responses. Your feedback is crucial in helping us better understand your social media experience.
* Take a few minutes to carefully consider each question before selecting your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx for clarification.
