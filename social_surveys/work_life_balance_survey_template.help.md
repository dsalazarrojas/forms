# work_life_balance_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "work_life_balance_survey_template" is a social survey aimed at understanding current perceptions and experiences regarding work-life balance within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For the questions that allow multiple selections, choose all that apply.
3. Provide clear and concise comments in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your current work-life balance?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your current work-life balance. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Which of the following most impacts your work-life balance?** (`Workload`, `Working hours`, `Commuting time`, `Manager support`, `Company culture`, `Personal commitments`): This question is about the factors affecting your work-life balance. Choose the option that best describes the primary factor that impacts your balance.
* **Which company initiatives currently help your work-life balance? (Select all that apply)** (`Flexible hours`, `Remote work`, `Generous PTO`, `Wellness programs`, `Mental health support`, `None of these`): This question asks about company initiatives that support your work-life balance. Select all options that apply to your experiences.
* **How often do you feel you have enough time for personal activities outside of work?** (`Always`, `Often`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`): This question is about your personal time management. Choose the option that best describes your experiences.
* **What one change would most improve your work-life balance?**: This question asks for a specific change that would improve your work-life balance. Provide a clear and concise answer.
* **How important is work-life balance to your overall job satisfaction?** (`Extremely important`, `Very important`, `Moderately important`, `Slightly important`, `Not important`): This question assesses the importance of work-life balance for your overall job satisfaction. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions related to work-life balance?**: This text field is for any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have regarding work-life balance. Provide clear and concise comments.
