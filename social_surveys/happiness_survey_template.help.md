# happiness_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, titled "Happiness Survey", aims to gather information about an individual's general well-being and the factors that contribute to their happiness from a social perspective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions as accurately as possible. The first four questions ask about your current level of happiness, frequency of feeling joyful or content, and what factors contribute to your happiness.
2. Select all applicable answers for the multiple-choice question about factors contributing to happiness.
3. Respond to the question about the biggest challenge impacting your happiness with a single answer from the provided options or an "Other" option for an alternative reason.
4. Share a brief description or idea about one thing you would change in your life to increase your happiness.
5. If you have any additional thoughts on happiness or well-being, type them in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how would you rate your current level of happiness?**: This question helps gauge your current emotional state. Choose the response that best fits your feelings: Very happy, Happy, Neutral, Slightly unhappy, or Unhappy.
* **How often do you feel truly joyful or content in your daily life?**: This question assesses how frequently you experience positive emotions. Select the answer that best describes your daily experiences: Daily, Multiple times a week, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **Which of the following factors contribute most to your happiness? (Select all that apply)**: This question identifies the factors that have the greatest impact on your happiness. Choose from the options: Relationships, Hobbies/Leisure, Work/Career, Health, Financial security, Personal growth, or Spirituality/Beliefs.
* **What is the biggest challenge currently impacting your happiness?**: This question seeks to understand the primary obstacle to your happiness. Select from the provided options or choose "Other" if the reason is not listed.
* **What is one thing you would change in your life to increase your happiness?**: Share a brief idea or description of what action you would take to improve your happiness.
* **Do you have any additional thoughts on happiness or well-being?**: This is an opportunity to provide any further insights or reflections on happiness and well-being that you didn't mention earlier.
