# Online Community Needs Assessment Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and insights from the online community members to understand their needs and engagement with the community. This information will help us to make informed decisions and improve the community's value and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible, based on your personal experience and feelings.
2. Choose one or more answers for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide detailed and specific answers for text questions.
4. Don't worry if you don't see an option that perfectly matches your opinion; choose the one that best represents your feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your overall experience in our online community?**
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the community.
	+ Please select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How often do you actively participate or engage with content in the online community?**
	+ This question helps us understand your level of engagement with the community.
	+ Please select one of the options: Daily, Several times a week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
* **Which of the following benefits do you gain from participating in our community? (Select all that apply)**
	+ This question helps us understand the benefits you gain from the community.
	+ Please select all that apply:
		- Connect with others
		- Share knowledge
		- Learn new things
		- Find support
		- Stay informed
		- Entertainment
* **What types of content or activities would you like to see more of in the community?**
	+ This question helps us understand what content or activities you'd like to see more of.
	+ Please select all that apply:
		- Live events/Webinars
		- Discussion topics
		- Tutorials/Guides
		- Q&A sessions
		- Member spotlights
		- Social activities
* **How important is it for you to have a sense of belonging in our online community?**
	+ This question helps us understand the importance you place on belonging in the community.
	+ Please select one of the options: Very important, Somewhat important, Neutral, Not very important, or Not important at all.
* **What improvements or new features would you suggest for the online community?**
	+ This question helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions for improving the community.
	+ Please provide a detailed and specific answer.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback about the community?**
	+ This question helps us understand your overall thoughts and feelings about the community.
	+ Please provide a detailed and specific answer.
