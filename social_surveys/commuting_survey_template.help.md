# Commuting Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to understand people's daily commuting habits and experiences. It aims to collect data on their satisfaction levels, transportation modes, commute times, challenges, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your actual experiences.
2. Fill in the answers to the best of your ability, being honest and truthful.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless you are genuinely unsure or cannot provide an answer.
4. Take your time, and submit your responses when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How satisfied are you with your current daily commute?

* **Very satisfied** (labelled as "satisfied" in the form): Indicate that you are extremely pleased with your current daily commute.
* **Somewhat satisfied** (labelled as "satisfied" in the form): Indicate that you are somewhat pleased with your current daily commute.
* **Neutral** (labelled as "satisfied" in the form): Indicate that you are neutral or neither very pleased nor displeased with your current daily commute.
* **Somewhat dissatisfied** (labelled as "satisfied" in the form): Indicate that you are somewhat displeased with your current daily commute.
* **Very dissatisfied** (labelled as "satisfied" in the form): Indicate that you are extremely displeased with your current daily commute.

### What is your primary mode of transportation for commuting?

* **Car (driver)** (labelled as "car (driver)" in the form): Indicate that you primarily drive a car for your daily commute.
* **Car (passenger)** (labelled as "car (passenger)" in the form): Indicate that you primarily ride in a car as a passenger for your daily commute.
* **Public transport** (labelled as "public transport" in the form): Indicate that you use public transport, such as buses or trains, for your daily commute.
* **Cycling** (labelled as "cycling" in the form): Indicate that you commute by cycling.
* **Walking** (labelled as "walking" in the form): Indicate that you walk for your daily commute.
* **Motorcycle** (labelled as "motorcycle" in the form): Indicate that you use a motorcycle for your daily commute.
* **Rideshare/Taxi** (labelled as "rideshare/Taxi" in the form): Indicate that you use ridesharing or taxi services for your daily commute.

### How long does your typical one-way commute take?

* **Under 15 minutes** (labelled as "Under 15 minutes" in the form): Indicate that your typical one-way commute is less than 15 minutes.
* **15-30 minutes** (labelled as "15-30 minutes" in the form): Indicate that your typical one-way commute is between 15-30 minutes.
* **31-45 minutes** (labelled as "31-45 minutes" in the form): Indicate that your typical one-way commute is between 31-45 minutes.
* **46-60 minutes** (labelled as "46-60 minutes" in the form): Indicate that your typical one-way commute is between 46-60 minutes.
* **Over 60 minutes** (labelled as "Over 60 minutes" in the form): Indicate that your typical one-way commute is more than 60 minutes.

### What are the biggest challenges you face during your commute? (Select all that apply)

* **Traffic congestion** (labelled as "traffic congestion" in the form): Choose this option if you face traffic congestion during your commute.
* **Crowded transport** (labelled as "crowded transport" in the form): Choose this option if you face crowded transport during your commute.
* **High costs** (labelled as "high costs" in the form): Choose this option if you face high costs related to your commute.
* **Long delays** (labelled as "long delays" in the form): Choose this option if you experience long delays during your commute.
* **Safety concerns** (labelled as "safety concerns" in the form): Choose this option if you have safety concerns related to your commute.
* **Lack of convenient options** (labelled as "lack of convenient options" in the form): Choose this option if you face a lack of convenient options for your commute.
