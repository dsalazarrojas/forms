# member_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The member satisfaction survey template is designed to collect feedback from members of the organization to understand their overall satisfaction and experience. The goal is to identify areas of strength and weakness, and make data-driven decisions to enhance member satisfaction and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select one of the satisfaction options for the first question.
2. Choose an option or enter a grade for the second question.
3. Answer the yes or no question for the third question.
4. Type your thoughts and suggestions in the text field for the fourth question.
5. Write any additional comments or suggestions in the text field for the fifth question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `single`, required): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with your role at the organization. Select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Communication from Management** (`rate`, `single`, required): This question rates your level of satisfaction with communication from management. Choose one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Professional Development Support** (`support`, `single`, required): This question checks if you feel supported in your professional development. Choose true or false, or Somewhat.
* **Area for Improvement** (`comments`, `text`, required): This question asks you to describe what could improve your experience at work.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This question allows you to write any additional comments or suggestions you might have.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
- Take your time to think about each question before answering.
- You can write as much or as little as you need in the text fields.
