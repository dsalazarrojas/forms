# Hair Product Sample Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the testing team to collect information from testers about their availability and preferences for testing hair products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and fill in your information as prompted.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by *).
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Test** (`schedule_tester`, number, required: false): Select the date and time you are available to test hair products.
* **Tester Details** (`tester_hair_details`, note, required: false): Provide any details about your hair type, texture, or other factors that may impact the test results.
* **Hair Type** (`tester_hair_product`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of hair product you are available to test (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.).
* **Name** (`tester_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the test report.
* **Email** (`tester_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`tester_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address** (`tester_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Comment** (`tester_comment`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your testing experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact the testing team for clarification.
* The testing team will review your application and contact you to schedule a test if your qualifications and availability match the testing needs.
