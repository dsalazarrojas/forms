# Hair Salon Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Client Questionnaire is a form used to gather essential information from clients about their visit to the salon. This form helps the salon staff in scheduling appointments and managing client interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Phone Number.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Address.
4. Select the type of service you require (Haircut, Color, Style, etc.).
5. Choose a date and time for your service.
6. Select your preferred stylist.
7. Add any additional comments if you have any.
8. Enter your Contact Phone, Email, and Address.
9. Select a Submission Date and Submission Time.
10. Finally, confirm the user submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This field is where you will enter your email address.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): This field is where you will enter your address.
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select one, required): Select the type of service you require (Haircut, Color, Style, etc.).
* **Service Date** (`Service Date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to have your service.
* **Service Time** (`Service Time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to have your service.
* **Stylist** (`Stylist`, text, required): This field is where you will select your preferred stylist.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about your service.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Address** (`Contact Address`, text, required): Enter your contact address.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, time, required): Select the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission User** (`Submission User`, text, required): Enter the name of the user submitting this form.

Note: This form is intended for client usage, and it is expected that the information provided will be accurate and up-to-date.
