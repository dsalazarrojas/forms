# Hairdressing Assessment Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a client's hairdressing assessment, including their personal details, condition history, and treatment history. This form is designed to be completed by a hairdresser or stylist after conducting an assessment and is intended to be used for internal purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the client's details in the **Client Details** field.
2. Describe the client's condition history in the **Condition History** field.
3. Choose any relevant options from the **Stylist Recommendation** field (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional information about the client in the **Additional Client Details** field.
5. Record any relevant information about the client's treatment history in the **Treatment History** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`id: 1`, `label: Client Details`): This field is used to record the client's personal details, such as name, contact information, and any other relevant contact data.
* **Condition History** (`id: 2`, `label: Condition History`): Describe the client's current hair condition, including any relevant health issues or concerns.
* **Stylist Recommendation** (`id: 3`, `label: Stylist Recommendation`): This field provides suggestions for the client's hair care, such as advice on how to take care of their hair or recommending additional consultations.
* **Additional Client Details** (`id: 4`, `label: Additional Client Details`): Provide any additional information about the client that may be relevant to their hairdressing assessment.
* **Treatment History** (`id: 5`, `label: Treatment History`): Record any relevant information about the client's treatment history, including any previous appointments or services received.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
* This form is intended for internal use only, so please do not share any information gathered from this form with third parties without permission.
