# Hair Salon Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before providing them with services at the hair salon. It includes fields for client information, service preferences, and liability release.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the client information section with your personal details.
2. Select the services you would like to receive from the salon.
3. Provide additional service details, if necessary.
4. Release the salon from liability by selecting the "True" option in the liability release section.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: <u>Client Name, Address, and Contact Information.</u>
  Enter your name, address, and phone number or email in this section.
* **Services**: <u>Choose the services you want to receive from the salon.</u>
  Select all the services you would like to receive, such as haircuts, coloring, and styles.
* **Service Details**: <u>Provide additional information about your service preference.</u>
  Enter any additional details about your service choice, if necessary.
* **Service Name**: <u>Enter the name of the service you selected.</u>
  Type in the name of the service you selected from the previous field.
* **Service Time**: <u>Enter the duration of the service.</u>
  Type in the time you would like to spend on the service.
* **Liability Release**: <u>Release the salon from liability.</u>
  Select "True" to release the salon from liability.
* **Date**: <u>Enter the date you are signing the form.</u>
  Type in the date you are signing the form.
* **Time**: <u>Enter the time you are signing the form.</u>
  Type in the time you are signing the form.
* **Signature**: <u>Sign the form to confirm your agreement.</u>
  Sign your name to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Email**: <u>Enter your email address.</u>
  Type in your email address.
* **Phone Number**: <u>Enter your phone number.</u>
  Type in your phone number.
* **Note**: <u>Enter any additional comments or notes.</u>
  Type in any additional comments or notes you would like to make.
* **Email Confirmation**: <u>Confirm your email address.</u>
  Type in your email address again for confirmation.
* **Confirmation Message**: <u>Enter a confirmation message.</u>
  Type in a message confirming your agreement.
* **Email Sent**: <u>Record the email being sent.</u>
  Type in the email being sent status.
* **Status**: <u>Record the status of your confirmation email.</u>
  Select "Sent" to indicate that the confirmation email has been sent.
* **Note 1-5**: <u>Enter any additional comments or notes.</u>
  Type in any additional comments or notes you would like to make.
