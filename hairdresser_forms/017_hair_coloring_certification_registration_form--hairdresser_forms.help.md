# hair_coloring_certification_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for hairstylists who have obtained certifications in hair coloring. It collects information about the hairstylist's details, certification status, and renewal status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your hair colorist name in the "Hair Colorist Name" field.
2. Enter your salon address in the "Salon Address" field.
3. Type in your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select the time of your certification from the options in the "Time of Certification" field.
6. Choose your certification level from the options in the "Certification Level" field.
7. Type in your certification number in the "Certification Number" field.
8. Enter your certification date in the "Certification Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Select your certification status from the options in the "Certification Status" field.
10. Enter your certification expiration date in the "Certification Expiration Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
11. Enter your certification renewal date in the "Certification Renewal Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
12. Select your certification renewal status from the options in the "Certification Renewal Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a title for the form, if desired (optional).
* **Hair Colorist Name**: <emphasize>Enter your name as it appears on your certification</em>.
* **Salon Address**: Enter your salon's address where you work.
* **Contact Number**: Type in your contact number, including area code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Certification**: Select the time of your certification from the options.
* **Certification Level**: Choose your certification level from the options.
* **Certification Number**: Enter your certification number.
* **Certification Date**: Enter your certification date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Certification Status**: Select your certification status from the options.
* **Certification Expiration Date**: Enter your certification expiration date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Certification Renewal Date**: Enter your certification renewal date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Certification Renewal Status**: Select your certification renewal status from the options.
