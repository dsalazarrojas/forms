# hair_salon_new_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect new client information for the hair salon, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data entry.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
    2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
    3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
    4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
    5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
    6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
    7. Choose the hairdresser that recommended you by selecting an option in the "Name of hairdresser that recommended this client" field.
    8. Choose your appointment type by selecting an option in the "Appointment Type" field.
    9. Select your appointment time using the time input field.
    10. Choose your appointment date using the date input field.
    11. Provide any additional notes about your appointment in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (text, required): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)** (text, required): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (3)** (date, required): Provide your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email (4)** (email, required): Enter your active and valid email address.
* **Phone (5)** (text, required): Enter your phone number as per your phone identification documents.
* **Address (6)** (text, required): Enter your residential address as per your identification documents.
* **Name of hairdresser that recommended this client (7)** (select multiple, required): Choose the name of the hairdresser that recommended you.
* **Appointment Type (8)** (select one, required): Choose your appointment type.
* **Appointment Time (9)** (time, required): Select your appointment time.
* **Appointment Date (10)** (date, required): Choose your appointment date.
* **Notes (11)** (note, required): Provide any additional notes about your appointment.
 
## Tips
- Ensure that the email, phone number, and address you enter are accurate and up-to-date.
- Select the correct appointment time and date for your scheduled appointment.
- Provide clear and concise notes about your appointment to help the salon staff assist you better.
