# Hair Texture Relaxation Service Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and obtain consent from clients for a hair texture relaxation service. The service involves treating and modifying the chemical structure of hair, and this form helps to inform clients about potential health risks and obtain their consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's health details on page 1.
2. If applicable, select any health risks associated with the service on page 2.
3. Leave a note with your signature on page 3.
4. Select your response to the question about whether you have read and signed this form on page 4.
5. Enter your name as the stylist on page 5.
6. Choose the date of service on page 6.
7. Enter the name of the salon on page 7.
8. Select the type of service you will be providing on page 8.
9. If applicable, enter any additional services you will be providing on page 9.
10. Select your response to the consent question on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, text, optional): Enter your client's health details, such as any medical conditions or allergies they may have.
* **Health Risks** (`health_risks`, select_multiple, optional): Select any health risks associated with the service, such as high risk of burns or scarring.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Leave a note with your signature, indicating your acceptance of the service and understanding of the potential risks.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have read and signed this form, indicating your understanding of the service and potential risks.
* **Stylist Name** (`stylist_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the stylist providing the service.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the service.
* **Salon** (`salon_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the salon where the service will be provided.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you will be providing, such as chemical relaxing or haircut.
* **Other services provided** (`other_services`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any additional services you will be providing.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have read and understand the risks associated with the service, indicating your consent for the procedure.
