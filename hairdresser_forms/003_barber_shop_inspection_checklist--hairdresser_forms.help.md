# Barber Shop Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barber Shop Inspection Checklist is a form used by inspectors to assess the compliance of a barber shop with certain standards. This form is designed to be completed by one inspector at a time and is intended for official purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the form in order, section by section.
2. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
3. If a section has options, select the one that best describes your finding.
4. If you have no comment or issue, select "Not applicable" where applicable.
5. If a shop manager or supervisor is present, they may be asked to sign off on the form, especially for the overall assessment and any corrective actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date (1)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name (2)**: Enter your full name.
* **Shop Name (3)**: Enter the name of the barber shop being inspected.
* **Shop Address (4)**: Enter the complete street address of the shop.
* **Business License Displayed (5)**: Answer if the business license is visibly posted or not.
* **Barber Licenses Current (6)**: Answer if all barber licenses are up to date or not.
* **Overall Cleanliness Rating (7)**: Rate the general cleanliness of the shop from Excellent to Unacceptable.
* **Floor Condition (8)**: Rate the floor condition from Clean and well maintained to Unacceptable.
* **Workstation Cleanliness (9)**: Rate the cleanliness of the barber stations from Very clean to Unsanitary.
* **Tool Sanitization (10)**: Answer if tools are properly sanitized or not.
* **Equipment Condition (11)**: Rate the state of chairs, mirrors, and equipment from Excellent condition to Poor condition.
* **Ventilation System (12)**: Answer if there is adequate air circulation or not.
* **Lighting Quality (13)**: Rate the quality of lighting from Excellent to Poor.
* **Restroom Cleanliness (14)**: Rate the condition of customer restrooms from Very clean to Unsanitary.
* **Waste Disposal (15)**: Answer if trash containers are properly maintained or not.
* **Product Storage (16)**: Answer if products are stored properly and labeled or not.
* **Fire Safety Equipment (17)**: Rate the presence and condition of fire extinguishers.
* **Accessibility (18)**: Answer if the shop is fully accessible, partially accessible, or not accessible.
* **Violations Found (19)**: List any issues or findings.
* **Required Corrective Actions (20)**: List any actions needed to achieve compliance.
* **Follow Up Inspection Required (21)**: Answer if a follow-up inspection is needed or not.
* **Overall Assessment (22)**: Rate the final assessment of the shop from Pass - No issues to Fail - Immediate closure recommended.
* **Inspector Signature (23)**: Type your full name.
* **Manager Acknowledgment Signature (24)**: Shop manager signature if present.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
- Use the options provided to select the most accurate answer.
- If a shop manager or supervisor is present, ask them to sign off on the form where applicable.
- Take notes or photos if necessary to support your findings.
