# Custom Wig Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Wig Order Form is a tool for hairdressers to collect and process wig orders from clients. It helps ensure that all required and relevant information is gathered to efficiently fulfill the client's request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the **Client Email** field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the **Client Phone Number** field.
4. Specify the client's hair type in the **Hair Type** field.
5. Choose the desired hair color from the **Color Request** dropdown menu.
6. Select the size of the wig the client is requesting (if desired).
7. Choose the style of the wig from the **Style Request** dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional notes about the order in the **Notes** field (if necessary).
9. Indicate who submitted the order (Client, Saloon, or Stylist) in the **Submitted By** field.
10. Enter the date in the **Date** field.
11. Enter the time of submission in the **Time** field.
12. (Optional) Provide additional information about the tool assigned to the order in the **Assigned Tool** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email** (email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Hair Type** (text, required): Specify the client's hair type (e.g., natural, artificial, etc.).
* **Color Request** (select_one, required): Choose the desired hair color from the dropdown menu.
* **Size Request** (select_multiple, optional): Select the size of the wig the client is requesting (if desired).
* **Style Request** (select_one, required): Choose the style of the wig from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the order (if necessary).
* **Submitted By** (select_one, required): Indicate who submitted the order (Client, Saloon, or Stylist).
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Provide additional information about the tool assigned to the order (if necessary).
