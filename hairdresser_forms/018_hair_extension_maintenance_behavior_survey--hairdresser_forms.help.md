# Hair Extension Maintenance Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your habits and preferences when it comes to your hair extensions. Your feedback will help us better understand your needs and provide more effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers from the options below. Please answer as accurately as possible, and don't worry, you can skip this form if you're not comfortable sharing this information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Habits** (`client_habits`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the habits that apply to you:
	+ Yes: Do you visit a salon for hair extensions?
	+ No: Do you get hair extensions from a friend, family member, or online?
* **Salon Visit Frequency** (`salon_visit_frequency`, select_one, Optional): How often do you visit a salon for hair extensions?
	+ Yes: You visit a salon regularly.
	+ No: You rarely or never visit a salon for hair extensions.
* **Satisfaction with Hair Care** (`satisfaction_with_hair_care`, select_multiple, Optional): Which of the following statements best describe your level of satisfaction with your current hair care routine?
	+ Satisfied: You're happy with your current hair care routine.
	+ Dissatisfied: You're not satisfied with your current hair care routine.
