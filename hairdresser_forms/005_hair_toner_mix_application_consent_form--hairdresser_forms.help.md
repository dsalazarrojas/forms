# Hair Toner Mix Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from clients regarding their hair toner mix applications, ensuring a smooth and informed service experience. Please complete this form accurately to facilitate your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required and optional fields as prompted.
- Ensure you provide clear and legible handwriting or typed responses.
- Review each field carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, text, optional): Briefly express your consent and understanding of the hair toner mix application process and any risks or concerns you may have.
  • Please type your consent statement in the space provided.
  • If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your stylist.

- **Saloon Details** (`saloon_details`, text, optional): Provide your preferred saloon name and address.
  • Enter the name of the saloon where you wish to have your service.
  • Also, include the address of the saloon for reference.

- **Pre-Service Details** (`pre_service_details`, text, optional): Describe your pre-service requirements or concerns.
  • Share any details that might be important for a smooth service experience.
  • This could include medical conditions, allergies, or any other relevant information.

- **Toner Mix** (`toner_mix`, text, optional): Specify the toner mix you wish to apply.
  • Clearly write the toner mix you are interested in, including any specific ingredients or percentages if required.

- **Stylist Name** (`stylist_name`, text, optional): Specify the name of your preferred stylist.
  • Choose a stylist with whom you have a good rapport or a specific preference.

- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information for future reference.
  • Include your address, phone number, or email for easy communication.

- **Date And Time** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Select a date and time for your appointment.
  • Choose a date that suits you best, taking into consideration the availability of your preferred stylist.
  • Please note that appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled as needed.

## Tips
- Please complete this form with accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth service experience.
- If you have any concerns or need assistance, feel free to ask your stylist or contact the saloon directly.
- This form is a confidential document; please be honest and respectful in your responses.
