# Hair Texture Styling Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a hairdresser's comfort level with different hair textures, their styling habits, and personal details such as hair length, thickness, and color. It's intended to help hairdressers understand their customers' preferences and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field and provide any additional information requested. Make sure to fill in all the fields, especially the ones marked as "Name", "Email", and "Phone", as they are important for our records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Texture**: 
    * Curly
    * Wavy
    * Straight
* **How Often You Get Styled**: 
    * Never
    * Less than once a week
    * Once a week
    * More than once a week
* **Comfort Level**: 
    * Very Comfortable
    * Somewhat Comfortable
    * Neutral
    * Somewhat Uncomfortable
    * Very Uncomfortable
* **Hair Length**: If applicable, please specify your hair length.
* **Hair Thickness**: If applicable, please specify your hair thickness.
* **Hair Density**: If applicable, please specify your hair density.
* **Hair Color**: 
    * Black
    * Blonde
    * Brown
    * Gray
    * Red
    * Other
* **Favorite Styling Product**: If you have a preferred hair styling product, please specify.
* **How Do You Style**: Describe your usual hair styling habits.
* **Hair Care**: Share any hair care tips or routines you have.
* **Comments**: Any additional information or comments about your hair.
* **Email**: Your email address, please.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, please.
* **Name**: Your full name, please.
* **Age**: Your age, please.
* **Location**: Your location, please.
