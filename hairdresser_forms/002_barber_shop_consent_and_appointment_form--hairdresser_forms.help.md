# Barber Shop Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from clients before booking an appointment at the barber shop. It helps our barbers and management understand client preferences and requirements for a smooth and safe service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your personal details such as your full name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Select your preferred appointment date and time.
3. Choose the service you would like to book, such as a hair cut, shave, or full grooming package.
4. Estimate how long you would like your appointment to last.
5. If you have any skin conditions or allergies, please select them from the list and provide additional details in the text field.
6. Answer the question about any bleeding disorders or blood thinners.
7. Confirm that you have disclosed all relevant health information and release the barber and shop from liability for normal service-related risks.
8. Understand and agree to our cancellation policy, which requires 24 hours' notice for cancellations.
9. Consent to having your information stored for future appointments.
10. Finally, confirm your identity and agreement by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Preferred appointment date**: Select a date for your appointment. Please note that availability may vary depending on the chosen date.
* **Preferred appointment time**: Choose your preferred time slot for the appointment.
* **Service requested**: Select the type of service you would like to book, such as a haircut, shave, or full grooming package.
* **Estimated service duration preference**: Choose how long you would like your appointment to last.
* **Preferred barber**: If you have a preferred barber, you can specify their name here. If left blank, we will allocate you to the first available barber.
* **Do you have any skin conditions or allergies?**: Select any relevant skin conditions or allergies you may have.
* If you selected "Yes" to skin conditions or allergies, please describe them in the text field below.
* **Bleeding disorders or blood thinners**: Confirm if you have any bleeding disorders or take blood thinners.
* **I confirm that I have disclosed all relevant health information**: Select "I Agree" to confirm that you have provided accurate health information.
* **I release the barber and shop from liability for normal service-related risks**: Select "I Agree" to release the shop and barbers from liability for normal service-related risks.
* **I understand the cancellation policy and agree to provide 24 hours notice**: Select "I Agree" to understand and agree to our cancellation policy.
* **I consent to having my information stored for future appointments**: Select "I Agree" to consent to storing your information for future appointments.
* **By submitting this form, I confirm my identity and agreement**: Select "I Confirm" to confirm that you are the person submitting the form and agree to the terms.
