# Hair & Scalp Rejuvenation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair & Scalp Rejuvenation Report Form is used to document and track the progress of hair and scalp treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the *required* label).
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1** (`page_1`, `text`, not required): This page serves as a placeholder for any additional comments or information.
- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, `text`, not required): Please enter your hair color in this field.
- **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, `select_one`, not required): Select the length of your hair from the options provided.
- **Scalp Condition** (`scalp_condition`, `text`, not required): Please describe the condition of your scalp.
- **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Scalp/Hair Loss** (`scalp_hair_loss`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Prophets Length** (`prophets_length`, `number`, not required): Please enter the length of your prophets.
- **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `note`, not required): Use this field to describe any treatment plans or notes.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Use this field to add any additional comments.
- **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of your hair from the options provided.
- **Hair Thickness** (`hair_thickness`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Hair Porosity** (`hair_porosity`, `select_one`, not required): Select the porosity of your hair from the options provided.
- **Hair Porosity Grass** (`hair_porosity_grass`, `time`, not required): Please enter the porosity of your hair in time units.
- **Scalp Porosity Grass** (`scalp_porosity_grass`, `time`, not required): Please enter the porosity of your scalp in time units.
- **Prophets Grass** (`prophets_grass`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Treatment Grass** (`treatment_grass`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Use this field to add any additional notes.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Please enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Please enter your phone number.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, not required): Select the tool assigned to you.
- **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `text`, not required): Use this field to add any additional comments.
- **Comments 3** (`comments_3`, `text`, not required): Use this field to add any additional comments.
- **Comments 4** (`comments_4`, `text`, not required): Use this field to add any additional comments.

Note: The fields labeled as `note` type are used for free-text notes. The fields labeled as `select_one` and `select_multiple` allow selecting one or multiple options from the provided options. The fields labeled as `text` are for free-text entry.
