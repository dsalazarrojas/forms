# Hair Consultation Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our salon. It will help us understand what we're doing well and what areas we need to improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the options that best represent your experience.
3. Don't worry about formatting or grammar; just focus on providing clear and concise answers.
4. Submit your response when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What do you think is the main reason you didn't come to our salon?** (question 1): This question helps us understand the reasons why you didn't visit us. Please provide a brief explanation of your decision not to come to our salon.
- **How clear were the prices explained to you?** (question 2): We want to know if you were clear about the costs of our services. Choose Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, or Not Clear.
- **How satisfied are you with the service provided?** (question 3): This question allows you to rate your satisfaction with the service you received. Choose Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
- **How likely are you to recommend our salon to a friend or family member?** (question 4): This question helps us understand the likelihood of our customers recommending us to others. Please choose Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Neither Likely nor Unlikely, Somewhat Unlikely, or Very Unlikely.
- **What can we improve on our service?** (question 5): Share any thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve our service.
- **What can we improve on our service?** (question 6) **(optional)**: This question is similar to question 5, but it's optional.
- **Was the staff friendly and welcoming?** (question 7): We want to know if our staff made you feel welcome and comfortable.
- **Were the facilities clean and well-maintained?** (question 8): This question helps us ensure our facilities meet your expectations.
- **Was the consultation time sufficient?** (question 9): We want to know if our consultation time was sufficient for your needs.
- **Was the consultation time sufficient for the price?** (question 10): This question helps us understand the value of our services.
- **Was the communication clear and easy to understand?** (question 11): We want to know if our communication was clear and easy to follow.
- **Were you given enough time to discuss your hair requirements?** (question 12): This question helps us understand if we gave you enough time to discuss your needs.
- **Was the consultation conducted in a clean and professional environment?** (question 13): We want to know if our consultation area was clean and professional.
- **Was there any other staff member who attended to our conversation?** (question 14): This question helps us understand if you had any interaction with other staff members.
- **Did you feel comfortable sharing your expectations and concerns with our staff?** (question 15): We want to know if you felt at ease sharing your thoughts with us.
- **Did you feel that your concerns were heard and addressed?** (question 16): This question helps us understand if we responded to your concerns effectively.
- **Did we meet your expectations on the consultation?** (question 17): We want to know if we met your expectations for the consultation.
- **How likely are you to return to our salon?** (question 18): This question helps us understand your likelihood of returning to our salon.
- **What can we improve on our services?** (question 19): Share any thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve our services.
- **How satisfied are you with our customer service?** (question 20): We want to know if our customer service met your expectations.
- **Would you like to schedule another appointment or consultation?** (question 21): This question helps us understand if you'd like to schedule another appointment.
- **Would you like to share your thoughts with our staff member?** (question 22): This question allows you to share any further thoughts or feedback with our staff.
- **Do you have any other comments, suggestions, or feedback?** (question 23): Share any additional thoughts or feedback.
- **What do you think we could have done better?** (question 24) **(optional)**: Share any other comments or suggestions on how we could have improved our service.
