# Hair Frizz Control Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect client information for the hair frizz control treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your details as a client.
2. Select your hair type.
3. Specify your hair dryness level.
4. Check any allergies you may have.
5. Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
6. Choose whether you consent to treatment with chemical frizz control.
7. Confirm your consent.
8. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
9. Enter the date and time of the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details that you'd like to share with us.
  - This field is not required but it's helpful to know who you are and how to contact you.
- **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select one, required: false): Select the type of hair you have, such as All, Bald, Curly, Fine, Medium, Thick, or Very Thick.
  - Please choose the best description that suits your hair type.
- **Hair Dryness** (`hair_dryness`, select multiple, required: false): Select the level of dryness you experience, including Dry, Very Dry, or Somewhat Dry.
  - Be as specific as possible to help us understand your needs.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, required: false): If you have any allergies, list them here.
  - This is an optional field, but it's important to inform us about any allergies you may have.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select one, required: false): Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
  - This field is also optional, but it helps us tailor the treatment to your needs.
- **Treatment Consent** (`treatment_consent`, select one, required: false): Choose whether you consent to treatment with chemical frizz control.
  - Please select the option that best reflects your decision.
- **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, select one, required: false): Confirm your consent to the treatment.
  - This is a required field to ensure we have your agreement.
- **Client Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign this form to confirm your consent.
  - Write your signature here, and make sure to write clearly and legibly.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the treatment.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the treatment.
  - Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
