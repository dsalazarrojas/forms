# Hair Iron Protection Spray Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information and consent from clients for the use of hair iron protection spray during a hair treatment or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out each field as accurately and completely as possible.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have been informed about the use of hair iron protection spray and have agreed to its application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Authorization**: 
    This field is for the client to confirm that they have been informed about the use of hair iron protection spray and have agreed to its application.
* **Sign**:
    This field is to capture the client's signature, indicating their consent for the application of hair iron protection spray.
    Please date it with the current date.
* **Contact Details**: 
    Select the most convenient way you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or 1-2 business days prior).
* **Name**:
    Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**:
    Enter your email address as it appears in official documents.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact details are up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the hairdresser or receptionist.
* The completion of this form is required for the safe and proper application of hair iron protection spray.
