# Hair Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Consultation Form is designed to collect information from users to schedule a hair consultation, including their preferred hair style, color, texture, length, and language for communication. This information will help the service provider to understand the user's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Hair Consultation Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone number and Email for contact purposes.
3. Select your Preferred hair style from the provided options (Short, Long, Ponytail).
4. Choose your Preferred hair color from the options (Brown, Blonde, Red).
5. Select your Preferred hair texture from the options (Fine, Medium, Coarse).
6. Choose your Preferred hair length from the options (Short, Medium, Long).
7. Select a Scheduling date and time for your consultation.
8. Enter any Special requirements you may have.
9. Explain why you want to consult, if applicable.
10. Provide any Additional comments.
11. Choose your Preferred language for communication (English, Spanish, French).
12. Select your Preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Message).
13. Confirm your agreement to schedule the consultation (Yes, No).
14. Indicate if you have declined the consultation (Yes, No).
15. Confirm if you have any pending appointments or consultations (Yes, No).
16. Indicate if you have refused the consultation (Yes, No).
17. Confirm if you are unavailable for the consultation (Yes, No).
18. Confirm if the consultation was canceled (Yes, No).
19. Indicate if you had a No show (Yes, No).
20. Confirm if you arrived late for the consultation (Yes, No).
21. Confirm if you were satisfied with the consultation (Yes, No).
22. Indicate if you were not satisfied with the consultation (Yes, No).

* **Scheduling date** (hair_consultation_form_scheduling_date, date, required): Select a date for your consultation.
* **Scheduling time** (hair_consultation_form_scheduling_time, time, required): Select a time for your consultation.
* **Special requirements** (hair_consultation_form_special_requirements, text, required): Enter any special requirements you may have.

* **Agreed** (hair_consultation_form_agreed, select_multiple, required): Confirm your agreement to schedule the consultation (Yes, No).

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate information is collected.
* If you have any special requirements, be specific and provide details.
* Choose your preferred language and contact method for effective communication.
* Confirm your agreement to schedule the consultation and respond accordingly to any subsequent notifications.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule, please do so well in advance.
* Provide feedback on your consultation to help us improve our services.
