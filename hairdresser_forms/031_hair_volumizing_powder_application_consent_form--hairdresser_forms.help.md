# Hair Volumizing Powder Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before undergoing a hair volumizing treatment. It helps to document client preferences, treatment options, and consent, ensuring that both the stylist and client are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2.  Select the correct options for each field from the provided choices.
3.  Ensure you understand each question and provide accurate information.

*   **PowderVolume** (`Light`, `Medium`, `Heavy`, `select_one`): Select the desired powder volume for your treatment.

*   **Consent** (`note`): Please sign below to indicate that you have read and understood the treatment plan.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your stylist.
