# Hair Foiling Highlight Retouch Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients who are requesting to have their hair retouched with a foiling highlight technique. It helps the hairdresser to understand the client's hair history, preferences, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your client approvals (yes/no) for the retouching procedure.
2. Provide a brief description of your hair history, including any previous chemical treatments or color applications.
3. Choose your preferred hair type (Blonde, Brunette, Highlight, Redhead, Curly, Wavy, or Other).
4. Select the retouch method you would like (Foiling, Foiling with Glitter, Foiling with Pearls, or Foiling with Pearls and Glitter).
5. Leave any additional notes for the hairdresser.
6. Fill in your contact information (if applicable).
7. Submit your form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Approvals** (id: 1, type: select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your approval for the retouching procedure.
* **Hair History** (id: 2, type: text, required: true):
	+ Provide a brief description of your hair history, including any previous chemical treatments or color applications.
* **Risks** (id: 3, type: text, required: false):
	+ Provide any concerns or risks you have regarding the retouching procedure.
* **Hair Type** (id: 4, type: select_one, required: true):
	+ Choose the type of hair you have (Blonde, Brunette, Highlight, Redhead, Curly, Wavy, or Other).
* **Retouch Method** (id: 5, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the method of retouching you would like (Foiling, Foiling with Glitter, Foiling with Pearls, or Foiling with Pearls and Glitter).
* **Notes** (id: 6, type: note, required: false):
	+ Leave any additional notes for the hairdresser.
* **Contact Info** (id: 7, type: text, required: false):
	+ Provide any contact information you would like to be used in case of emergencies.
* **Email** (id: 8, type: email, required: false):
	+ Provide your email address (if applicable).

	+ Select 'Submit' to confirm your submission.
* **Submission Date** (id: 10, type: date, required: true):
	+ Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (id: 11, type: time, required: true):
