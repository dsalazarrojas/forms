# Client Release Form For Hair Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their hair condition, sensitivities, and planned services. It helps the hairdresser understand the client's needs and ensures a safe and effective hair service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as you go through the form.
2. Select the correct options for your hair type, hair condition, and scalp sensitivity.
3. If applicable, list any known allergies or sensitivities.
4. Select all services you are requesting.
5. Confirm that you understand and accept the risks associated with the hair services.
6. Confirm that you agree that the salon is not responsible for any adverse reactions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, *required*): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, *required*): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, *required*): Enter the date of your hair service appointment.
* **Current hair type** (`hair_type`, `select_one`, *required*): Select your hair type from the options: Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, or Combination.
* **Current hair condition** (`hair_condition`, `select_one`, *required*): Select your hair condition from the options: Healthy, Dry, Oily, Damaged, or Colored/Processed.
* **Scalp sensitivity** (`scalp_sensitivity`, `select_one`, *required*): Select your scalp sensitivity from the options: None, Mild, Moderate, Severe.
* **Known allergies** (`known_allergies`, `text`, *optional*): List any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Allergic reactions** (`allergic_reactions`, `select_one`, *required*): Select whether you have had allergic reactions to hair services before.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_one`, *optional*): Select if you are taking medications that may affect hair services.
* **Services you are requesting** (`planned_services`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Select all services you are requesting from the list: Cut, Color, Perm, Relaxer, Straightening, Extensions, Styling, Other.
* **First time service** (`first_time_service`, `select_one`, *required*): Confirm if this is your first time getting this service.
* **I understand and accept the risks** (`release_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, *required*): Confirm that you understand and accept the risks associated with these hair services.
* **Waiver agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, *required*): Confirm that you agree that the salon is not responsible for any adverse reactions.
* **Additional notes or concerns** (`additional_notes`, `text`, *optional*): Add any additional notes or concerns you have.
