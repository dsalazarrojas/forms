# Walk In Haircut Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Walk In Haircut Appointment Request form is designed for users to request a walk-in haircut appointment at a salon or barbershop. This form helps to streamline the appointment process and reduce no-shows by collecting necessary information in advance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your desired appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.
2. Select the start and end times for your appointment in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
3. Choose a preferred stylist from the list of available stylists by typing their name in the "Stylist Name" field.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes or information in the "Notes" field, such as specific haircut requests or special instructions.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`<name>`, `<type>`: text, required): This field is not a standard label, but it seems to be a placeholder for user-provided information. Please type in any relevant details about your appointment, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Appointment Date** (`<name>`, `<type>`: date, required): Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to ensure correct parsing.
* **Start Time** (`<name>`, `<type>`: time, required): Choose the start time for your appointment. Please select a time that works best for you and your schedule.
* **End Time** (`<name>`, `<type>`: time, required): Select the end time for your appointment. Ensure it's long enough for a proper haircut service.
* **Stylist Name** (`<name>`, `<type>`: text, required): Type in the name of your preferred stylist. Please double-check their availability before submitting this form.
* **Notes** (`<name>`, `<type>`: note, optional): Add any additional information or special requests you have for your appointment. This is a good opportunity to mention any specific haircut requests, such as beard trim or hair coloring.
* **Confirm** (`<name>`, `<type>`: select_one, required): Please confirm that the information you provided is accurate and complete. Select "Yes" to proceed with your appointment request.
* **Additional Info** (`<name>`, `<type>`: text, optional): Provide any additional details or information about your appointment. This could be used to inform the salon staff about any relevant circumstances or requirements.
