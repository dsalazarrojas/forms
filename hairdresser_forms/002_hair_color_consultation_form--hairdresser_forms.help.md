# Hair Color Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the client's hair color preferences, appointment schedule, and contact details. It will help the hairstylist or salon staff to better understand the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the correct hair color and type from the provided options.
3. Choose a suitable appointment time that suits your schedule.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for easy communication with the salon staff.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments about your hair color preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Color** (`hair_color_consultation_form_hair_color`): Indicate whether you want a hair color or no.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_color_consultation_form_hair_type`): Choose one or more hair types that you're interested in.
* **Appointment** (`hair_color_consultation_form_appointment`): Schedule a meeting with the salon staff to discuss your hair color preferences.
* **Time** (`hair_color_consultation_form_time`): Select a suitable time for your appointment.
* **Note** (`hair_color_consultation_form_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about your hair color preferences.
* **Email** (`hair_color_consultation_form_email`): Enter your email address for easy communication with the salon staff.
* **Phone** (`hair_color_consultation_form_phone`): Enter your phone number for easy communication with the salon staff.
* **Category** (`hair_color_consultation_form_category`): Indicate whether this is a regular or one-off appointment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`hair_color_consultation_form_assigned_tool`): Choose whether a specific tool or equipment is required for your appointment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields to ensure that the salon staff can provide you with the best service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the salon staff.
* You can always adjust your preferences or cancel your appointment if needed.
