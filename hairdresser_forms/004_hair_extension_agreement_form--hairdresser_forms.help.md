# Hair Extension Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Extension Agreement Form is designed to collect information from clients about their hair extension services. This includes their contact details, service type, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*), with the relevant information.
2. For the `client_name` field, enter the client's name.
3. For the `service_type` field, select the type of service the client is interested in.
4. For the `service_description` field, provide a brief description of the service the client is interested in.
5. For the `client_contact` field, enter the client's contact information.
6. For the `technician` field, enter the name of the hairdresser assigned to the client.
7. For the `service_start_date` and `service_end_date` fields, enter the start and end dates of the service.
8. For the `extension_type` field, select the type of extension service.
9. For the `hair_color` field, enter the client's hair color.
10. For the `notes` field, provide any additional notes about the client's hair extension service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_name**: The client's name. Enter the client's full name, as they would like to be referred to.
* **service_type**: The type of service the client is interested in. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the service type.
* **service_description**: A brief description of the service the client is interested in. This will help the hairdresser understand the client's needs.
* **client_contact**: The client's contact information. Enter the client's email address, phone number, or other relevant contact details.
* **technician**: The name of the hairdresser assigned to the client. Enter the hairdresser's name or ID number.
* **service_start_date**: The start date of the service. Enter the date the service is scheduled to begin.
* **service_end_date**: The end date of the service. Enter the date the service is scheduled to end.
* **extension_type**: The type of extension service. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the service type.
* **hair_color**: The client's hair color. Enter the client's natural hair color.
* **notes**: Any additional notes about the client's hair extension service. Provide any relevant information the hairdresser may need to know.
