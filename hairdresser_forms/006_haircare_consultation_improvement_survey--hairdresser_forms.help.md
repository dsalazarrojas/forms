# Haircare Consultation Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for users who are completing the Haircare Consultation Improvement Survey form. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from clients on how the haircare service can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the survey questions on Page 1.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the services provided on Page 2.
3. Write any additional comments or notes on Page 3.
4. Provide an email address for follow-up on Page 4.
5. Record the date and time of the consultation on Page 5.
6. Add any final comments or notes on Page 6.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback on Page 7.
8. Complete the form by submitting it on Page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions (Page 1)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any thoughts or comments about your experience with the haircare consultation.
	+ Please type your answers in the space provided.
* **Rating Questions (Page 2)**:
	+ This is a multiple-choice field where you can rate your satisfaction with different aspects of the service.
	+ Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Note to Self (Page 3)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can add any personal notes or reminders about your consultation.
	+ Please type your answers in the space provided.
* **Email for Follow up (Page 4)**:
	+ This is a field where you can provide an email address for the salon to contact you for follow-up.
	+ Please type your email address exactly as it appears in your email account.
* **Phone Number (Page 5)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide your phone number for the salon to contact you.
	+ Please type your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date of Consultation (Page 6)**:
	+ This is a date field where you can select the date of your consultation.
	+ Please use the calendar to select the correct date.
* **Time of Consultation (Page 7)**:
	+ This is a time field where you can select the time of your consultation.
	+ Please use the clock to select the correct time.
* **Client Feedback (Page 8)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
	+ Please type your answers in the space provided.
* **Note to Salon (Page 9)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can add any notes or reminders for the salon staff.
	+ Please type your answers in the space provided.
* **Additional Comments (Page 10)**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
	+ Please type your answers in the space provided.

## Tips
Please note that all fields are optional, but providing feedback is encouraged to help the salon improve its services.
