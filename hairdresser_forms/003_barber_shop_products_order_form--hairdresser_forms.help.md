# Barber Shop Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from barbershops for ordering products such as hair care tools, shaving products, and other related items. It helps us track and fulfill orders accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the order date.
2. Provide your barber shop's name, phone number, and email address.
3. Enter the shipping address, including city, state/province, and ZIP/postal code.
4. Choose the product categories needed, selecting all that apply.
5. (Optional) Specify any specific hair care product brands or clipper guard sizes required.
6. Estimate the total quantity of items ordered.
7. If applicable, enter any special requests or notes for the order.
8. Choose a preferred delivery method (if not choosing local pickup).
9. Select an approximate budget range for the order.
10. Confirm that the order is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Date** (Order date): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Order ID (optional)**: A unique identifier for tracking purposes. You can leave this blank.
* **Barber Shop Name** (Barber shop name): Enter your barber shop's name as it appears on our records.
* **Business Phone Number** (Business phone number): Enter your barber shop's phone number.
* **Business Email** (Business email): Enter your barber shop's email address.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping address): Enter the address where the order will be shipped to.
* **City** (City): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **State/Province** (State/Province): Enter the state or province for the shipping address.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (ZIP/Postal code): Enter the ZIP or postal code for the shipping address.
* **Product Categories** (Product categories needed): Choose all the product categories that you need, such as Hair Clippers, Shaving Products, etc.
* **Specific Hair Care Products** (Specific hair care product brands): If applicable, enter specific hair care product brands you require.
* **Clipper Guard Sizes Needed** (Clipper guard sizes needed): If applicable, enter any clipper guard sizes you require.
* **Estimated Total Quantity** (Estimated total quantity of items): Estimate the total quantity of items ordered.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (Special product requests or notes): If you have any special requests or notes for the order, enter them here.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (Preferred delivery method): Choose your preferred delivery method: Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Local Pickup, or Courier Service.
* **Approximate Budget Range** (Approximate budget range): Estimate the approximate budget for your order.
* **I Confirm This Order Is Accurate** (I confirm this order is accurate): Confirm that the order is accurate by selecting "I Confirm". If you need to review, select "I Need to Review".
