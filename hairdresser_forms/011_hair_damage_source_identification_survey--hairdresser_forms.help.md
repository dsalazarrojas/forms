# Hair Damage Source Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Hair Damage Source Identification Survey** is a form used to gather information about the possible sources of hair damage. This form helps us to identify the primary causes of hair damage and provide better services to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. Select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus for fields with multiple options.
3. Type your answers for the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Damage Source** (`hair_damage_source`): Choose the source of the hair damage from the available options: **Chemical Treatment**, **Physical Damage**, **Mechanical Stress**, or **Other**.

* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`): Select one or more of the following hair types: **Fine**, **Coarse**, **Medium**, **Curly**, or **Wavy**.

* **Chemical Treatment** (`chemical_treatment`): Select one or more of the following chemical treatments: **Perming, Hair Coloring, Hair Relaxing, Hair straightening, Hair Smoothing**.

* **Physical Stress** (`physical_stress`): Choose the cause of physical stress from the available options: **Tight Hats, Tight Hair Ties, Hot Water, Sun Exposure, or Other**.

* **Mechanical Stress** (`mechanical_stress`): Select one or more causes of mechanical stress: **Tight Ponytails, Hair Ties, Hair Clips, Other**.

* **Other Damage** (`other_damage`): Provide a description of any other damage not mentioned above.

* **Hair Damage Description** (`hair_damage_description`): Describe the hair damage you are experiencing.

* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Choose who submitted this form: **Salon Staff, Stylist, or Other**.

* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Enter the date submitted in **mm/dd/yyyy** format.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose "Other" and provide a description in the "Hair Damage Description" field.
* This form is designed to be completed by salon staff, stylists, or customers.
* Please submit the form only once per hair damage issue.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
