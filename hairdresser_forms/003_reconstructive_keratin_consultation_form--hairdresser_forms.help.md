# Reconstructive Keratin Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reconstructive Keratin Consultation Form is designed to collect information about a client's hair condition, goals, and treatment history to help salon professionals provide the best possible treatment plan for their keratin treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Complete all required fields.
3. If a field is not required, feel free to answer if you have any relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon History**: Please provide a brief history of your previous salon visits and treatments. This will help us understand your hair's condition and progress over time.
* **Client Goals**: Please describe your hair goals, such as hair length, volume, texture, color, or health.
* **Treatment Goals**: Select all applicable options for the treatment goals you are seeking, such as toning, smoothing, strengthening, straightening, or thinning.
* **Hair Length**: Please provide the length of your hair in inches.
* **Hair Condition**: Select the current state of your hair, such as dry, damaged, normal, or oily.
* **Hair Flexibility**: Please rate the flexibility of your hair, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Scalp Condition**: Select the current state of your scalp, such as normal, slightly dry, or very dry.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please provide any relevant medical conditions that may affect your hair care.
* **Previous Treatments**: Select any previous treatments you have received, such as keratin, chemical relaxer, hair straightening, or hair smoothing.
* **Hair Goals**: Select all applicable options for your hair goals, such as hair length, volume, texture, color, or health.
* **Hair Products Used**: Select all hair products you have used, such as shampoo, conditioner, hair spray, or hair serum.
* **Preferred Products**: Select your preferred hair products, such as shampoo, conditioner, hair serum, or hair mask.
* **Keratin Treatment Goals**: Select the desired outcome of the keratin treatment, such as repair and restore, smooth and straighten, strengthen, or add shine.
* **Keratin Treatment Concentration**: Select the desired concentration of the keratin treatment.
* **Keratin Treatment Duration**: Please provide the desired treatment duration in minutes.
* **Hair Color**: Select the current color of your hair, such as brown, blonde, red, grey, or a mix of colors.
* **Chemical Relaxer History**: Please provide a history of chemical relaxer treatments, including the frequency and duration of use.
* **Hair Length History**: Please provide a brief history of your hair length changes, including any significant changes or trends.
* **Chemical Process History**: Please provide a history of chemical processing, including any relevant treatments or products used.
* **Treatment Outcome**: Please describe the outcome of any previous treatments, including any successes or challenges.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and completed it accurately.

Note: Please complete the form carefully and honestly to ensure that the information gathered is accurate and useful for the treatment planning process. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your salon professional for clarification.
