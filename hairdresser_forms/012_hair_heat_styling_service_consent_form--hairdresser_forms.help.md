# Hair Heat Styling Service Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hair Heat Styling Service Consent Form is designed to gather information from clients before offering them hair heat styling services. The form will help the salon to ensure that the client is aware of the services they are receiving, and to gather any necessary information about the client's health and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide your Health Information, including any allergies or medical conditions you have.
3. Select your Consent to receive services, or indicate if you do not wish to receive services.
4. Choose the services you wish to receive from the salon, including hair cut, coloring, styling, perming, up-style, and hair relaxing.
5. Enter your Additional Services, including any hair treatment, face treatment, scalp treatment, hand treatment, and scalp and hair treatment you may require.
6. Select your Payment Method, or indicate if you do not wish to pay for services.
7. Enter your appointment details, including the date, time, and duration of your appointment.
8. Provide your Emergency Contact information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
9. Enter any Medical Notes that may be relevant to your appointment.
10. Enter your Additional Services, including any other services you may require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (text, required): Please enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Health Information** (text, required): Please provide any relevant health information, including allergies or medical conditions.
* **Consent** (select multiple, not required): Please select whether you have given your consent to receive services, or if you do not wish to receive services.
* **Appointment Details** (text, required): Please enter the details of your appointment, including the date, time, and duration.
* **Client Health** (text, required): Please provide any relevant health information, including allergies or medical conditions.
* **Stylist Details** (text, required): Please enter the details of your stylist, including their name and contact information.
* **Salon Details** (text, required): Please enter the details of the salon, including their name and contact information.
* **Contact Info** (email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (number, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (text, required): Please enter your address.
* **Services** (select multiple, not required): Please select the services you wish to receive from the salon, including hair cut, coloring, styling, perming, up-style, and hair relaxing.
* **Additional Services** (select multiple, not required): Please select any additional services you may require, including hair treatment, face treatment, scalp treatment, hand treatment, and scalp and hair treatment.
* **Allergies** (text, required): Please list any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Please enter the details of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship.
* **Medical Notes** (text, required): Please enter any medical notes that may be relevant to your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (time, required): Please enter the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (date, required): Please enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (number, required): Please enter the duration of your appointment.
* **Services 2** (select multiple, not required): This is a duplicate field of Services, please select the services you wish to receive from the salon.
* **Payment Method** (select multiple, not required): Please select your payment method, or indicate if you do not wish to pay for services.
* **Payment Method** (select multiple, not required): This is a duplicate field of Payment Method, please select your payment method, or indicate if you do not wish to pay for services.

Note: The form has two duplicate fields for services and payment method, please select one of the options to avoid confusion.
