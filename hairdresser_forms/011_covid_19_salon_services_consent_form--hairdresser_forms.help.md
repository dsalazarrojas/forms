# Covid 19 Salon Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their visit to a salon during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Information** (Salon Information, text, optional):
	+ This field is for general information about the salon you are visiting.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the salon.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, number, required):
	+ This field is for your contact information.
	+ Please enter your phone number or other contact information so we can reach you.
* **Screening Questionnaire** (Screening Questionnaire, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for screening questions about your health.
	+ Please select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
	+ Please use this space to provide any relevant information.
* **Email** (Email, email, required):
	+ This field is for your email address.
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required):
	+ This field is for your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit, date, required):
	+ This field is for the date of your visit.
	+ Please enter the date you plan to visit the salon.
* **Time of Visit** (Time of Visit, time, required):
	+ This field is for the time of your visit.
	+ Please enter the time you plan to visit the salon.
* **Screening Result** (Screening Result, select one, required):
	+ This field is for the screening results.
	+ Please select either 'True' or 'False' depending on your results.
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for follow-up information.
	+ Please select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Next Visit** (Next Visit, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for information about your next visit.
	+ Please select all that apply from the list of options.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields (*).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
