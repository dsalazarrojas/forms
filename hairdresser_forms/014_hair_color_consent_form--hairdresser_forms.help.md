# Hair Color Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by hairdressers or beauty professionals to obtain informed consent from clients or customers prior to providing hair color treatment. The form ensures that clients understand the type of treatment they are receiving and have given their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of hair color treatment you wish to receive.
2. Provide any additional details about the treatment in the "Color Treatment Details" field.
3. Enter the date you give your consent.
4. Add any notes about your hair color form in the "Hair Color Form Notes" field.
5. Sign the form in the "Hair Color Form Signature" field.
6. Confirm that you have given your consent by selecting "True" in the "Hair Color Consent Given" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Color Consent** (`hair_color_consent_form`, note, optional): A brief explanation of the form's purpose and importance.
* **Hair Color Treatment** (`hair_color_treatment`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of hair color treatment you wish to receive (e.g. Shamoox, Chromoluxe, Platinum, Silver).
* **Color Treatment Details** (`color_treatment_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the treatment, such as any specific instructions or allergies.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you give your consent for the hair color treatment.
* **Hair Color Form Notes** (`hair_color_form_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the hair color form.
* **Hair Color Form Signature** (`hair_color_form_signature`, time, optional): Sign the form to indicate you have given your consent.
* **Hair Color Consent Given** (`hair_color_consent_given`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm that you have given your consent by selecting "True". 

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read each field and understand its purpose before completing the form.
- Select the correct type of hair color treatment and provide any necessary details.
- Ensure you enter the correct date of consent.
- Sign the form to confirm your consent.
- Review the "Hair Color Form Notes" field for any additional information or instructions.
- Confirm your consent by selecting "True" in the "Hair Color Consent Given" field.
