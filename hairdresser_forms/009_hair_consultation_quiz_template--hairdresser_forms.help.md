# Hair Consultation Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients about their hair needs and preferences for a consultation with a stylist. This form helps stylists understand the client's expectations and provides a foundation for a productive and informed consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information (Page 1)**: This page is used to collect the client's basic information. Simply type in the client's first and last name, and their contact method (phone, email, or text).
2. **Hair Goals (Page 2)**: Choose the length of hair desired from the options provided: Short, Medium, or Long.
3. **Services Required (Page 3)**: Select all the hair services required from the options provided: Cut, Color, or Treatment.
4. **Hair Concerns (Page 4)**: Select all hair concerns that apply from the options provided: Dandruff, Itching, or Baldness.
5. **Appointment Time (Page 5)**: Enter the preferred appointment time for the client's consultation.
6. **Preferred Appointment Time (Page 6)**: Select the preferred appointment time from the options provided.
7. **Medical Conditions (Page 7)**: Enter any medical conditions that may affect the client's hair.
8. **Allergies (Page 8)**: Enter any allergies the client may have.
9. **Medications (Page 9)**: Enter any medications the client is currently taking.
10. **Previous Medical Conditions (Page 10)**: Enter any previous medical conditions that may affect the client's hair.
11. **Contact Method (Page 11)**: Select the contact method for follow-up with the client: Phone, Email, or Text.
12. **Additional Comments or Questions (Page 12)**: Enter any additional comments or questions for the client.
13. **Additional Questions (Page 13)**: Enter any additional questions for the client.
14. **Stylist Feedback (Page 14)**: Enter feedback for the client after the consultation.
15. **Page 15**: This page is left blank for any additional notes from the client or stylist.
16. **Stylist Comments (Page 16)**: Enter any comments or feedback for the client after the consultation.
17. **Client Feedback (Page 17)**: Enter feedback from the client after the consultation.
18. **Page 18**: This page is left blank for any additional comments or feedback.
19. **Quiz Summary (Page 19)**: Enter a summary of the consultation.
20. **Page 20**: This page is left blank for any additional comments or feedback.
21. **Stylist Notes (Page 21)**: Enter any notes or feedback from the stylist.
22. **Client Comments (Page 22)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the client.
