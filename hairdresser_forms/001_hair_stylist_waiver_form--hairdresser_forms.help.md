# Hair Stylist Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Stylist Waiver Form is used to collect information from clients and stylists to ensure that services are delivered safely and effectively. This form helps to document client and stylist information, service details, and any necessary waiver text, ensuring that all parties are aware of and consent to the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked as **Required** in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
2. Make sure to select the correct **Service Type** from the drop-down list, as this will help the stylist know what to do for the client.
3. Enter all other fields as requested in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter the client's full name.
- **Service Date** (`service_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date of the service provided.
- **Stylist Name** (`stylist_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the stylist who will be providing the service.
- **Service Time** (`service_time`, Time, **Required**): Enter the time of the service provided.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, Select One, **Required**): Select the type of service provided from the list:
	+ **Haircut**: A standard hair cut service.
	+ **Coloring**: A coloring service, including any chemical treatments.
	+ **Chemical Treatment**: A chemical treatment service, including perms, relaxers, etc.
- **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Client E-mail** (`client_email`, E-mail, **Required**): Enter the client's E-mail address.
- **Waiver Text** (`waiver_text`, Text, **Required**): Enter any important waiver text that should be signed by the client prior to the service being performed.

## Tips
* Please make sure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you need to make any changes to the form, contact your supervisor.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
