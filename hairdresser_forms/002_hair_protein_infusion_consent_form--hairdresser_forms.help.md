# Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form is a form designed to collect essential information from individuals seeking hair protein infusion treatment. This form helps us understand your medical history, allergies, and consent for the treatment. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and safe treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have any health information that may be relevant to your treatment, please provide it in the "Health Information" field.
5. If you have any allergies, please select them from the "Allergies" options.
6. Indicate your consent for the treatment by selecting "I consent to the treatment" in the "Treatment Consent" field.
7. You may enter your Date of Birth, but this field is optional.
8. Enter your Phone number (if applicable) in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Health Information**: If you have any health information that may be relevant to your treatment, provide it here.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies you have from the options provided.
* **Treatment Consent**: Indicate your consent for the treatment by selecting "I consent to the treatment".
* **Date of Birth**: (Optional) Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if applicable.
