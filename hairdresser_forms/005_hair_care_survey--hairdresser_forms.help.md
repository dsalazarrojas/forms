# Hair Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Care Survey is a form designed to gather information about a person's hair care habits and preferences. This data will be used to provide personalized recommendations for hair care products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, start by answering each question to the best of your ability.
- You can select more than one option for questions with multiple-choice options.
- Be as detailed as possible in your answers to ensure that you receive accurate and relevant recommendations.
- You can add any additional comments or responses about hair care in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your current hair care routine?** (hair_care_survey, text, optional): A brief description of your current hair care routine, including any products or methods you use.
- **What type is your hair?** (hair_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of hair you have, such as curly, straight, or wavy.
- **How long is your hair?** (hair_length, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: short, medium, long, extra long.
- **What is your hair color?** (hair_color, select_one, optional): Select the color of your hair from the following options: blonde, brunette, red, or other.
- **Which hair products do you use?** (hair_product_usage, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: shampoo, conditioner, hair oil, hair spray.
- **How often do you use hair care products?** (hair_care_frequency, select_one, optional): Select how often you use hair care products, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
- **How do you care for your hair?** (hair_care_method, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: wash, condition, style, other.
- **What hair issues do you face?** (hair_issues, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: dryness, frizz, oiliness, other.
- **What matters most when choosing hair care products?** (hair_preferences, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: quality, price, brand, other.
- **Do you have any other comments or responses about hair care?** (hair_survey_response, note, optional): Add any additional comments or responses about your hair care routine or preferences.
