# Haircare Business Audit Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients to help haircare businesses improve their services and client satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Answer all questions in the space provided.
2. Ensure you provide honest and detailed answers to help the business understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (Page 1)** (`title`, `text`, `not required`): 
This page is where you'll start by providing your name or a unique identifier to help the business associate your feedback with your appointment.
* **Review Page (Page 2)** (`title`, `text`, `not required`): 
Please share your overall impression of your experience with the business. This is an opportunity to provide detailed feedback on how you felt about your appointment.
* **Contact Page (Page 3)** (`title`, `text`, `not required`): 
If you'd like to be contacted by the business regarding your feedback, please provide your contact information in this page. This helps the business follow up on your comments.
* **Service Page (Page 4)** (`title`, `text`, `not required`): 
Share your thoughts on the services you received during your appointment, including the quality of service, communication, and overall experience. Your feedback will help the business understand how they can improve their services.
* **Agreement Page (Page 5)** (`title`, `text`, `not required`): 
Please review and agree to the terms and conditions of the form. This is a necessary step to ensure your feedback is processed accurately.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use specific examples and details to help the business understand your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please include them in your feedback.
