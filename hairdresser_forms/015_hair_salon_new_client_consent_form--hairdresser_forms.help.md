# Hair Salon New Client Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from new clients, including their contact details and appointment preferences. This information will be used to schedule appointments and communicate effectively with clients.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely to ensure accurate information is gathered.
- Review and confirm your appointment details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client Consent** (`new_client_consent`, `note`, required: false): Please provide your consent to receive communication from our salon.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to in our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive appointment reminders and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive appointment reminders and updates.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the services you would like to book at the salon.
