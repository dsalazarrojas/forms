# Hair Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help salon staff gather necessary information from clients visiting the salon. It's a one-time intake form used to collect client details, appointment preferences, contact information, service preferences, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "New Client Details" page with the client's basic information.
2. Proceed to the "Appointment Preferences" page to specify your appointment details.
3. On the "Contact Details" page, provide your contact information.
4. In the "Service Preferences" page, select the services you would like to receive during your visit.
5. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### New Client Details
* **Name** (`New Client Details`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Age** (`New Client Details`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's age.
* **Email** (`New Client Details`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`New Client Details`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`New Client Details`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's home address.

### Appointment Preferences
* **Time and Date** (`Appointment Preferences`, `text`, required): Specify the desired appointment date and time.

### Contact Details
* **Phone Number** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for salon contact.
* **Email** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address for salon contact.
* **Address** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your home address for salon contact.

### Service Preferences
* **Service** (`Service Preferences`, `text`, optional): Select the hair services you would like to receive during your visit (e.g., haircut, color treatment, etc.).

### Note
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure smooth communication between you and the salon.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them in the "Note" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the salon staff for assistance.
