# Beauty Salon Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Salon Job Application Form is a one-time form designed for potential employees to apply for a job at a beauty salon. This form collects personal and professional information from applicants, including their contact information, job application details, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the position you are applying for.
3. Choose your availability for work.
4. If applicable, provide your cosmetology license information.
5. Enter your years of experience in the beauty industry.
6. Select your areas of specialization.
7. Provide information about your previous salon experience.
8. Answer questions about your reason for leaving your previous position.
9. Indicate whether you have an existing client base.
10. Choose your preferred commission structure.
11. Provide professional references.
12. Link to your work portfolio or social media account (optional).
13. Select your available start date.
14. Consent to a background check.
15. List any additional certifications or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Provide your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Your best phone number for contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, `text`, required): Your full street address.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, `select_one`, required): Select the position you are applying for (Hairstylist, Senior Stylist, etc.).
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the days you are available to work (Monday Morning, etc.).
* **Cosmetology License Number** (`cosmetology_license`, `text`, optional): If you have a state cosmetology license, provide the number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, `text`, optional): The state where your license was issued.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiry`, `date`, optional): The date your license expires.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter your total years of experience in the beauty industry.
* **Areas of Specialization** (`specialties`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your areas of specialization (Hair Cutting, etc.).
* **Most Recent Salon Employer** (`previous_salon`, `text`, required): Name and location of your previous salon.
* **Previous Position** (`previous_position`, `text`, required): Your title at your previous salon.
* **Employment Dates** (`employment_dates`, `text`, required): Start and end dates at your previous salon.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_leaving`, `text`, required): Why you left your previous position.
* **Existing Client Base** (`client_base`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have an existing client base (True, False, Some).
* **Commission Structure Preference** (`commission_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred commission structure (Hourly plus tips, etc.).
* **Professional References** (`references`, `text`, required): Provide 2-3 professional references with contact information.
* **Portfolio or Social Media Link** (`portfolio_link`, `text`, optional): Link to your work portfolio or Instagram.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select your available start date.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check`, `select_one`, required): Consent to a background check (Yes I consent, No I do not consent).
* **Additional Certifications** (`additional_certifications`, `text`, optional): List any additional certifications or training.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* Attach any additional documents or materials that support your application.
* Review our salon's policies and procedures before applying.
* We look forward to reviewing your application!
