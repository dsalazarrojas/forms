# salon_equip_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "salon_equip_entry_form" is used to record the entry of new salon equipment. This form collects information about the equipment, its type, brand, and purchase details. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the information is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out all required fields.
* Make sure you have filled out the form correctly and accurately.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_id** (number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the equipment. This can be any number you choose, but it should be unique to each piece of equipment.
* **item_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the equipment.
* **equipment_type_id** (select one, optional): Choose whether the equipment is 'Yes' or 'No' for its type.
* **brand** (text, optional): Enter the brand of the equipment.
* **purchase_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **purchase_price** (number, optional): Enter the cost of the equipment purchase.

## Tips
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please ask your administrator for guidance.
* Make sure to check the system regularly for updates and changes to this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
