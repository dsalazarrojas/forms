# Christmas Photography Session Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Christmas photography session. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that we can provide the best service possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email Address" field for session confirmation.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for contact purposes.
4. Select your preferred session date in the "Preferred Session Date" field.
5. Choose an alternate date (if available) in the "Alternate Date" field.
6. In the "Contact Information" field, add any additional contact details if needed.
7. Select the type of session you want (e.g., Individual Portrait, Couple Session, etc.) in the "Session Type" field.
8. Choose the location for the session (e.g., Studio Session, Outdoor Park, etc.) in the "Session Location" field.
9. If applicable, enter the location address in the "Location Address" field.
10. Select your desired package (e.g., Mini Session, Standard Session, etc.) in the "Package Selection" field.
11. Choose the number of people to be photographed in the "Number of People" field.
12. If children will be included, enter their ages in the "Children Ages" field.
13. Decide if pets will be included in the photos in the "Pets Included" field.
14. In the "Outfit Changes" field, select how many outfit changes you'd like to make.
15. Choose your photography style preference (e.g., Traditional Posed, Candid Natural, etc.) in the "Style Preference" field.
16. Select the backgrounds you'd like for the photos in the "Background Preference" field.
17. Choose how you'd like to use the photos (e.g., personal use, social media, etc.) in the "Photo Usage" field.
18. Select if you'd like printed photos or not in the "Print Package" field.
19. In the "Special Requests" field, add any specific photo requests you may have.
20. In the "Inspiration Photos" field, share any reference photos that inspired you.
21. Let us know how you found out about us in the "How Did You Hear About Us" field.
22. Confirm that you understand the deposit is required to book the session in the "Deposit Required" field.
23. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy in the "Cancellation Policy" field.
24. Finally, grant us permission to use the photos in our portfolio if you select "Yes I consent" in the "Model Release" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for session confirmation. This will be used to send you session details and reminders.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select your preferred session date. Please note that we will try to accommodate your first choice, but we may have limited availability.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`, date, optional): Choose an alternate date in case your first choice is not available.
* **Contact Information** (`section_contact_info`, note, optional): Add any additional contact details if needed.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session you want (e.g., Individual Portrait, Couple Session, etc.).
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location for the session (e.g., Studio Session, Outdoor Park, etc.).
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, text, optional): If you've chosen a home or specific location, enter the address.
* **Package Selection** (`package_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your desired package (e.g., Mini Session, Standard Session, etc.).
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, select_one, required): Select the number of people to be photographed.
* **Children Ages** (`children_ages`, text, optional): If children will be included, enter their ages.
* **Pets Included** (`pets_included`, select_one, required): Decide if pets will be included in the photos.
* **Outfit Changes** (`outfit_changes`, select_one, required): Select how many outfit changes you'd like to make.
* **Style Preference** (`style_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your photography style preference (e.g., Traditional Posed, Candid Natural, etc.).
* **Background Preference** (`background_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select the backgrounds you'd like for the photos.
* **Photo Usage** (`photo_usage`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you'd like to use the photos (e.g., personal use, social media, etc.).
* **Print Package** (`print_package`, select_one, required): Select if you'd like printed photos or not.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Add any specific photo requests you may have.
* **Inspiration Photos** (`inspiration_photos`, text, optional): Share any reference photos that inspired you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you found out about us.
* **Deposit Required** (`deposit_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the deposit is required to book the session.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Model Release** (`model_release`, select_one, required): Grant us permission to use the photos in our portfolio if you select "Yes I consent".

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate information to avoid any issues with your session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Be aware that we will try to accommodate your first-choice session date, but we may have limited availability.
* Please review the cancellation policy to ensure you understand the terms.
* If you select "Yes I consent" for the model release, we will use the photos for our portfolio, but we will always ask for your permission first.
