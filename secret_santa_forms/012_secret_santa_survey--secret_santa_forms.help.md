# Secret Santa Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Secret Santa Survey is a form designed to gather information about participants in a Secret Santa gift exchange. It helps the gift coordinator to collect participant preferences, budget, and gift recipient details, ensuring a fun and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Secret Santa Survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your own participant details:
	* Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
	* Provide your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
2. Specify your budget for the gift:
	* Enter a numerical value in the "Participant Budget" field, indicating how much you are willing to spend on a gift.
3. Choose the recipient of the gift:
	* Select the type of relationship you have with the gift recipient from the "Gift Recipient" dropdown menu (e.g., Close Friend, Family Member, Colleague, Neighbor, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the gift exchange.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you about the gift exchange.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for easier communication, you can enter it here.
* **Participant Budget** (`participant_budget`, number, optional): Indicate how much you are comfortable spending on a gift, to help the gift coordinator allocate participants' budgets effectively.
* **Gift Recipient** (`gift_recipient`, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship you have with the gift recipient from the available options (Close Friend, Family Member, Colleague, Neighbor, Other).

## Tips
Remember to fill out the form accurately and honestly, as your input will help the gift coordinator to organize the gift exchange successfully. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the gift coordinator for assistance.
