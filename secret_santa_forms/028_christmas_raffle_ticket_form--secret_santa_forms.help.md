# Christmas Raffle Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for purchasing raffle tickets for the Christmas event. Please provide your information to select your tickets and proceed with your purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your details in the **Purchaser Information** section: 
	* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for ticket confirmation and winner notification.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for winner notification and any further communication.
2. Choose your **Ticket Package**: Select the number of tickets you'd like to purchase (Single Ticket 1, Strip of 5 Tickets, Strip of 10 Tickets, Strip of 20 Tickets, or Bulk 50 Tickets).
3. Select the **Number of Tickets**: Enter the total number of tickets you'd like to purchase.
4. Enter **Billing Address**: Provide your full address for billing purposes.
5. Choose your **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Cash at Event, Check, or Bank Transfer).
6. Select **Ticket Delivery**: Choose how you'd like to receive your tickets (Email E-tickets, Mail Physical Tickets, Pickup at Event, or Pickup at Office).
7. In the **Ticket Selection** section:
	* **Prize Preference**: Select the prize that interests you (Grand Prize, Second Prize, Third Prize, Any Prize, or No Preference).
	* **Prize Categories of Interest**: Select all categories that interest you (Electronics, Gift Baskets, Travel Vouchers, Cash Prizes, Home Goods, Jewelry, Experiences, Sports Memorabilia).
8. In the **Event Attendance** section:
	* **Event Attendance**: Select if you'll be attending the event (Yes I will attend, No I cannot attend, Maybe still deciding).
	* **Volunteer Interest**: Select if you'd like to help at the event (No thank you, Yes as helper, Yes as setup crew, Yes as cleanup crew).
9. In the **Additional Information** section:
	* **How Did You Hear About Raffle**: Select how you heard about the raffle (Social Media, Email, Website, Friend or Family, Previous Event, Advertisement, Other).
10. Agree to the **Terms Agreement** and **Privacy Consent**:
	* **Terms Agreement**: Confirm you've read and agree to the raffle terms and conditions.
	* **Privacy Consent**: Allow us to contact you about the raffle.
11. Finally, select if you'd like to be updated about **Additional Purchases** (Yes keep me informed, No this is one-time).
