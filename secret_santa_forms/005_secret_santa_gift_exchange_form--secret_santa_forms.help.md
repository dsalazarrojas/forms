## Purpose
The Secret Santa Gift Exchange Form is used to collect information from participants in a Secret Santa gift exchange program. This information helps the gift-giver choose a suitable gift for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all the required fields with your own information:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone Number
	* Favorite Food
	* Favorite Drink
	* Budget
	* Gift Interested

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be known in the Secret Santa program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive messages and gifts from other participants.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for other participants to contact you.
* **Favorite Food**: Enter your favorite food or dish you enjoy eating.
* **Favorite Drink**: Enter your favorite drink or beverage.
* **Budget**: Enter your budget for the gift you are willing to spend.
* **Gift Interested**: Enter a short description of what you are interested in receiving as a gift (e.g., book, experience, or a specific type of gift).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully to help other participants get a better understanding of your interests and preferences.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g., vegetarian, vegan), you can include them in the "Favorite Food" field.
* If you have any gift-giving ideas or restrictions, you can include them in the "Gift Interested" field.
* Be open-minded and flexible when receiving or giving gifts. Remember, the goal of the Secret Santa program is to have fun and be kind to each other!
