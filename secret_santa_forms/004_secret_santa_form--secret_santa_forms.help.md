# Secret Santa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form is used to collect information from participants to organize a Secret Santa gift exchange. It gathers data on individual contributors, such as first and last names, and their preferences for the gift exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **first name** in the **first_name** field. This will help us know your name.
2. Enter your **last name** in the **last_name** field. This will help us identify you correctly.
3. Decide on the **gift amount** you'd like to spend and input it in the **gift_amount** field. This will help us determine the gift range for the exchange.
4. Choose how many **recipients** you'll be buying gifts for in the **how_many_recipients** field. This will help us organize the gift distribution.
5. Indicate if you'll be giving a gift to **recipient_one** and **recipient_two**. This will help us know who will receive gifts from you.
6. Select when you'll be sending the gift in the **when_to_send** field. This will help us plan the gift delivery.
7. Write a **message** to your recipient(s) in the **message** field. This will help personalize the gift.
8. Provide an optional **assigned_tool** if you have a preferred way of giving or receiving gifts.
9. Enter a **form_id** (not recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **gift_amount** (`gift_amount`, number, Optional): Enter the amount you'd like to spend on the gift.
* **how_many_recipients** (`how_many_recipients`, Select One, Optional): Choose how many recipients you'll be buying gifts for.
* **recipient_one** (`recipient_one`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if you'll be giving a gift to recipient one.
* **recipient_two** (`recipient_two`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if you'll be giving a gift to recipient two.
* **when_to_send** (`when_to_send`, Select One, Optional): Select when you'll be sending the gift (Christmas Day or New Year's Day).
* **message** (`message`, text, Optional): Write a message to your recipient(s).
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, Optional): Enter an optional gift-giving preference.
* **form_id** (`form_id`, text, Optional): Not recommended, but you can enter it if you want.
