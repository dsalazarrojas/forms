# Christmas Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Potluck Invitation Form is designed to collect information from guests attending the event. This form helps the organizers manage the event efficiently and ensures that everyone has a enjoyable time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
3. Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help with any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional, but helpful for the organizers).
* **Will you be attending?** (`attendance`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending the potluck or not.
* **Number of People Attending** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (`guest_names`, text, optional): If you are bringing guests, list their names separated by commas.
* **Potluck Contribution** (`contribution`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your contribution.
* **Type of Dish You Will Bring** (`dish_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of dish you will bring (e.g., appetizer, main dish, etc.).
* **Name of Dish** (`dish_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the dish you will bring.
* **Description and Ingredients** (`dish_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description and ingredients of your dish.
* **Number of Servings** (`servings`, number, required): Enter the number of servings you will bring.
* **Allergen Information** (`allergen_info`, text, optional): Identify any major allergens in your dish.
* **Special Requests & Preferences** (`special_section`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, add them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, text, optional): If you have any seating preference, enter it here.
* **Will you participate in Secret Santa?** (`secret_santa_participation`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you will participate in Secret Santa or not.
* **Secret Santa Budget** (`secret_santa_budget`, select_one, optional): If you participate in Secret Santa, choose your budget range.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.

Please review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
