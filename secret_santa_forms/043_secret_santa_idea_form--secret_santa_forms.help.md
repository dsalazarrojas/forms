# secretSantaIdeaForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit ideas for the Secret Santa gift exchange. You will be asked to input information about yourself and ideas for gifts. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "person" field.
2. Enter your employee ID number in the "person_id" field.
3. Suggest gift ideas in the "gift_suggestion" field.
4. Choose which gift ideas you would like to give or receive in the "emacs" field, where you can select either "emacs" or ".reporter".
5. If you have any additional gift ideas, enter them in the "gift_suggestion_ideas" field. You can select multiple options.
6. Enter the details of your gift suggestion in the "gift_suggestion_details" field.
7. If you know the ID of the gift suggestion you are referencing, enter it in the "gift_suggestion_id" field.
8. Optionally, enter any additional gift ideas in the "gift_suggestion_2", "gift_suggestion_3", "gift_suggestion_4", "gift_suggestion_5", "gift_suggestion_6", "gift_suggestion_7", "gift_suggestion_8", "gift_suggestion_9", "gift_suggestion_10", "gift_suggestion_11", "gift_suggestion_12", "gift_suggestion_13", "gift_suggestion_14", and "gift_suggestion_15" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **person (text)**: Enter your name.
* **person_id (number)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **emacs (select_one)**: Choose between "emacs" and ".reporter".
* **notes (text)**: Enter any additional gift ideas or information.
* **email (email)**: Enter your work email address (optional).
* **phone (text)**: Enter your work phone number (optional).
* **gift_suggestion (text)**: Suggest a gift idea.
* **gift_suggestion_details (date)**: Enter the details of your gift suggestion.
* **gift_suggestion_ideas (select_multiple)**: Choose multiple gift ideas from the options provided.
* **gift_suggestion_id (text)**: Enter the ID of the gift suggestion you are referencing (if you know it).
* **gift_suggestion_2, gift_suggestion_3, ..., gift_suggestion_15 (text)**: Enter any additional gift ideas you have.

## Tips
- Please make sure to input accurate gift IDs if you know them.
- If you need help with gift idea generation, you can use the options provided in the "gift_suggestion_ideas" field.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department.
