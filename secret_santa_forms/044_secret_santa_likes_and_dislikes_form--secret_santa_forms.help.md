# Secret Santa Likes And Dislikes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the recipient of a Secret Santa gift to create a more personalized and thoughtful gift.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Be specific and detailed when answering questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gift_recipient** (Required): Enter your name.
* **recipient_interests** (Optional): List your interests, hobbies, or things you enjoy doing. This will help the gift-giver understand your tastes and preferences.
* **likes** (Optional): List things you enjoy or like.
* **dislikes** (Optional): List things you dislike or don't enjoy.
* **preferences** (Optional): Specify any preferences or dislikes you may have for the gift.
* **specific_likes_dislikes** (Optional): Provide specific examples of things you like or dislike.
* **gift_budget** (Optional): If you have a specific budget in mind for your gift, enter it here.
* **gift_frequency** (Optional): Indicate how often you would like to receive gifts (e.g., once a month, once a quarter, etc.).

## Tips

* Be honest and specific when answering questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator.
* Remember, the goal is to create a fun and enjoyable experience for everyone involved!
