# Secret Santa Form For Kids - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for children to fill out a Secret Santa form, where they can share their interests and preferences for the holiday season. This information will help their parents or guardians choose a suitable gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
	* Holiday Activity
	* Secret Santa Name
	* Favorite Food
	* Favorite Toy
	* Favorite Holiday Movie
	* Parent Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Gift Certificate
	* Date of Form Submission
	* Time of Submission
	* IP Address
	* User Agent
	* Browser
	* Operating System
	* Device
	* Screen Size
	* Resolution
	* Timezone
	* Language
2. Select your favorite food and toy from the provided options.
3. Choose one favorite holiday movie.
4. You can add a special request, but it's optional.
5. Fill in any other optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Activity** (`holiday_activity`, text, required): Tell us about your favorite holiday activity or tradition.
* **Secret Santa Name** (`secret_santa_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it will be used for the Secret Santa gift-giver.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options that you like to eat during the holidays.
* **Favorite Toy** (`favorite_toy`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the toys that you like to play with during the holidays.
* **Favorite Holiday Song** (`favorite_holiday_song`, text, optional): If you have a favorite holiday song, tell us about it.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Add any special requests you might have for your Secret Santa gift.
* **Favorite Holiday Movie** (`favorite_holiday_movie`, select_one, required): Choose one of the movies that you like to watch during the holidays.
* **Favorite Holiday Book** (`favorite_holiday_book`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the books that you like to read during the holidays.
* **Gift Suggestion** (`gift_suggestion`, text, optional): If you have a gift idea, tell us about it.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): Add any special instructions for the Secret Santa gift-giver.
* **Gift Certificate** (`gift_certificate`, number, required): Enter the gift certificate number.
* **Date of Form Submission** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): The date and time this form was submitted.
* **Time of Submission** (`submission_time`, time, required): The time this form was submitted.
* **IP Address** (`submission_ip_address`, text, required): The IP address of the device submitting this form.
* **User Agent** (`submission_user_agent`, text, required): The user agent of the device submitting this form.
* **Browser** (`submission_browser`, text, optional): The browser used to submit this form.
* **Operating System** (`submission_os`, text, optional): The operating system of the device submitting this form.
* **Device** (`submission_device`, text, optional): The device used to submit this form.
* **Screen Size** (`submission_screen_size`, text, optional): The size of the screen of the device submitting this form.
* **Resolution** (`submission_resolution`, text, optional): The resolution of the device submitting this form.
* **Timezone** (`submission_timezone`, text, optional): The timezone of the device submitting this form.
* **Language** (`submission_language`, text, optional): The language of the device submitting this form.
