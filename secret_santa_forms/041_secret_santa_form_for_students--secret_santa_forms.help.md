# Secret Santa Form For Students - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form For Students is designed to gather information about each student's preferences and requirements for the Secret Santa gift exchange. This form helps the Secret Santa organizers understand who to pair with whom, what to expect for gift-giving, and how to tailor gifts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address as they appear on your student ID.
2. If applicable, select your department from the list of available options (Yes/No).
3. Set your Gift Budget to guide the gifter on the gift's price range.
4. Write your Preferred Gift Suggestions to help the gifter choose a suitable gift.
5. Enter your Student ID and Student Number for identification and pairing purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your student email address for communication purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): If you're a member of a specific department, select Yes to help the gifter tailor their gift.
* **Gift Budget** (`gift_budget`, number, required): Set your budget to guide the gifter on the gift's price range.
* **Preferred Gift Suggestions** (`preferred_gift_suggestions`, note, optional): Write a brief note on your gift preferences to help the gifter choose a suitable gift.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID for identification and pairing purposes.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, optional): Enter your student number for identification and pairing purposes.
