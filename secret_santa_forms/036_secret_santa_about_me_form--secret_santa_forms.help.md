# secret_santa_about_me_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal information from users for Secret Santa gift exchange or internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your gift tag.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Date**: Choose a date for your preferences.
	* **Time**: Choose a time for your preferences.
2. Select your interests:
	* **Interests**: Choose one of the options that best describes your interest.
3. Optional fields:
	* **Hobbies**: Select multiple interests that you enjoy. (You can select as many as you like.)
	* **Favorite**: Choose your favorite beverage.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share.
	* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options that you like. (You can select as many as you like.)
	* **Select One**: Choose one of the options that best describes you.
	* **Date (again)**: Choose another date for your preferences.
	* **Time (again)**: Choose another time for your preferences.
	* **Email (again)**: This field is optional and not used in this form.
	* **Phone (again)**: This field is optional and not used in this form.
	* **Address (again)**: This field is optional and not used in this form.
	* **Select Multiple (again)**: Select multiple options that you like. (You can select as many as you like.)
	* **Select One (again)**: Choose one of the options that best describes you.
	* **Note**: Add any additional information you'd like to share.
	* **Select Multiple 4**: Select multiple options that you like. (You can select as many as you like.)
	* **Select One 5**: Choose one of the options that best describes you.
	* **Time 3**: Choose a time for your preferences.
	* **Date 3**: Choose a date for your preferences.
