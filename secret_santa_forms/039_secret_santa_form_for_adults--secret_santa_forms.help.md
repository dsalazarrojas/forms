# Secret Santa Form For Adults - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for a Secret Santa gift exchange among adults, where participants submit their name and other relevant details to collect information about each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
* Provide a gift exchange name, which is the name of the event or group you are participating in.
* Set a gift price limit and currency for the exchange.
* Set a gift price max amount for the exchange.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Exchange Name** (`gift_exchange_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the gift exchange event or group you are participating in.
* **Organizer** (`organizer`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the gift exchange.
* **Participants Name** (`participants_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you in the gift exchange.
* **Participants Email** (`participants_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Gift Limit** (`gift_limit`, number, optional): Set the maximum amount you are willing to spend on a gift.
* **Gift Price Limit** (`gift_price_limit`, number, optional): Set the maximum amount you are willing to spend on a gift, which will help determine the price of the gift you receive.
* **Gift Price Currency** (`gift_price_currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency of the gift price limit you set.
* **Gift Price Max** (`gift_price_max_amount`, text, optional): Enter the maximum amount of the gift price limit, which will help determine the price of the gift you receive.
