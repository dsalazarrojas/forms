# Secret Santa Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Secret Santa gifting within an organization or group of friends. It helps participants collect information about themselves and recipients to facilitate the Secret Santa gift exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your own information in the "Your Name" field.
2. For each recipient, provide their first name and last name in the respective fields.
3. If you'd like to include a personal message for the recipient, you can do so in the "Message for Recipient" field.
4. Once you have filled in all the required and optional fields, click submit to generate a list of participants and their information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`recipient_first_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for entering the recipient's first name. If you're unsure, leave it blank.
* **Last Name** (`recipient_last_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for entering the recipient's last name. If you're unsure, leave it blank.
* **Your Name** (`giver_first_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for entering your own name. Please fill this in accurately, as it will be used to match you with a recipient.
* **Message for Recipient** (`message_for_recipient`, Text, Optional): This field is for including a personal message for the recipient, if desired. Keep it short and sweet!

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in your own information and the recipient's information.
* Keep personal messages respectful and considerate of the recipient's interests.
* Use this form within a closed group to avoid sharing sensitive information.
* Review and adjust your participation settings to ensure you're comfortable with the Secret Santa exchange.
