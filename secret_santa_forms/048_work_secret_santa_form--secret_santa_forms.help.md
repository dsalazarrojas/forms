# Work Secret Santa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Secret Santa Form is designed to collect information from participants to assign them to a secret gift-giver group for a fun and anonymous gift exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **Form ID** (`1`). This is a unique identifier for the form.
2. Select the **Category** (`2`). Choose "Secret Santa Forms" to indicate that this is a secret gift-giver form.
3. Enter your **Name** (`3`). Provide your real name to ensure accurate gift assignment.
4. Select your **Assigned Tool** (`4`). Pick the tool you have access to, such as "chatjimmy".
5. Provide the **Output File** (`5`). Enter the name of the file to output the completed form data.
6. Enter the **Title** (`6`). Choose a title for the completed form.
7. Enter the **Description** (`7`). Provide a brief description of the form purpose.
8. Select your **Favorite Color** (`8`). Choose a color you like, such as "Red", "Blue", or "Green".
9. Check any **Allergies** (`9`). Select any food or environmental allergies you have.
10. Check any **Medical Conditions** (`10`). Select any medical conditions you have.
11. Select your **Coffee Preference** (`11`). Choose your preferred strength of coffee, such as "Strong", "Weak", or "None".
12. Select your **Favorite Food** (`12`). Choose one or more of your favorite foods, such as "Pizza", "Salad", or "Tacos".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (1): A unique identifier for the form.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Category** (2): Choose the type of form this is, in this case, "Secret Santa Forms".
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Name** (3): Enter your real name for accurate gift assignment.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Assigned Tool** (4): Select the tool you have access to, such as "chatjimmy".
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Output File** (5): Enter the name of the file to output the completed form data.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Title** (6): Choose a title for the completed form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Description** (7): Provide a brief description of the form purpose.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Favorite Color** (8): Choose a color you like, such as "Red", "Blue", or "Green".
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Allergies** (9): Select any food or environmental allergies you have.
	+ Type: checkbox
	+ Required: No
* **Medical Conditions** (10): Select any medical conditions you have.
	+ Type: checkbox
	+ Required: No
* **Coffee Preference** (11): Choose your preferred strength of coffee, such as "Strong", "Weak", or "None".
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
* **Favorite Food** (12): Choose one or more of your favorite foods, such as "Pizza", "Salad", or "Tacos".
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: Yes
## Tips

* Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Select "Other" options wisely, especially for Favorite Color and Food, to ensure a good fit.
* If unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for help.
* Enjoy the gift-giving activity!
