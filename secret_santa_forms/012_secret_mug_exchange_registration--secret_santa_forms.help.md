# Secret Mug Exchange Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Secret Mug Exchange Registration form. This form collects information from participants to facilitate the Secret Mug Exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details.
2. Select your gift preferences.
3. Choose your favorite color.
4. Enter your favorite thing.
5. Provide your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Add any notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter participant details (<participant_details>, text, required: false)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share with the organizers.
* **Gift preferences (<gift_preferences, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the types of gifts you'd like to receive during the Secret Mug Exchange. Select multiple options if applicable.
* **Favorite color (<favorite_color, select_one, required: false)**: Select a single color that represents your favorite color.
* **Favorite thing (<favorite_thing, text, required: false)**: Enter a favorite thing, hobby, or interest that you'd like to share.
* **E-mail (<participant_email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (<participant_phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note (<participant_note, note, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Tips
* Be honest when selecting your gift preferences, as this will help ensure you receive a gift you'll enjoy.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please include this information in the "Note" field.
* If you've already completed the form, please don't submit it again.
