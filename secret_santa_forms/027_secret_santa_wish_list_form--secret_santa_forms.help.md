# Secret Santa Wish List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your interests, preferences, and personality traits to help your Secret Santa participants gift you something you'll truly enjoy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred options in the following fields. You can skip any field you're not comfortable with or don't have information about.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Please enter your full name so that your Secret Santa participants can address you correctly.
  (`name`, text, `true`)
- **Email**: Enter your email address so that your Secret Santa participants can contact you if needed.
  (`email`, email, `true`)
- **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
  (`phone`, text, `true`)
- **Date of Birth**: If you're comfortable sharing, please enter your date of birth.
  (`date_of_birth`, date, `false`)
- **Favorite Hobbies**: List your favorite hobbies or activities you enjoy in your free time.
  (`favorite_hobbies`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Food**: What's your favorite food or cuisine?
  (`favorite_food`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Drinks**: What are your favorite beverages or types of food you enjoy?
  (`favorite_drinks`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Movies**: Select your favorite movies or film genres.
  (`favorite_movies`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Music**: What type of music do you enjoy listening to?
  (`favorite_music`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Books**: List your favorite books or authors.
  (`favorite_books`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Games**: What games do you enjoy playing?
  (`favorite_games`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Activities**: Select your favorite activities or hobbies.
  (`favorite_activities`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Holidays**: Which holidays do you enjoy celebrating?
  (`favorite_holidays`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Places**: Where's your favorite destination or place?
  (`favorite_places`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Things**: What are your favorite things or hobbies?
  (`favorite_things`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite People**: Who are your favorite people or characters?
  (`favorite_people`, text, `false`)
- **Favorite Events**: Select your favorite events or occasions.
  (`favorite_events`, text, `false`)
- **Gift Ideas**: If you have any specific gift ideas, feel free to share them.
  (`wish_list_ideas`, text, `false`)
- **Gift Group**: If you'd like to specify a gift group, please enter it here.
  (`gift_santa_group`, text, `false`)
- **Date of Exchange**: If you know the date of the Secret Santa event, please enter it.
  (`date_of_exchange`, text, `false`)
- **Gift Limit**: If you have a gift limit, please specify it here.
  (`gift_limit`, text, `false`)
- **Notes**: Any additional information or notes for your Secret Santa participants.
  (`notes`, text, `false`)
