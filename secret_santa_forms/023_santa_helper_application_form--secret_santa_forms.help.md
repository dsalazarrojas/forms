# Santa Helper Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Helper Application Form is used to collect contact information and skills of potential helpers for the Secret Santa gift-giving process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information:
	* Enter your name
	* Enter your email address
2. Fill out your contact details:
	* Provide your phone number
	* Select a date on the calendar when you are available to help
3. List your skills:
	* Select all the skills you have (e.g. wrapping, baking, photography)
4. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself
5. Confirm your selection
6. Enter your address details:
	* Enter your address
	* Select your city and state
	* Enter your zip code

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be known to the recipients.
* **Contact Information (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with further instructions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, date, optional): Select a date when you are available to help with the gift-giving process.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the skills you have that might be useful for the gift-giving process (e.g. wrapping, baking, photography).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about yourself that might be helpful.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, optional): Confirm your selection as a helper (Option 1: Yes, I'm in!).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address so we can send you gifts or instructions.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code so we can ensure accurate delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code again for verification purposes. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to increase your chances of being matched with a recipient.
* Be honest about your skills and availability to ensure the matching process is fair and effective.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
