# Secret Santa Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Sign Up Form is used to collect information from participants in the Secret Santa gift exchange. This form is used to determine whether participants want to be part of the gift exchange and to collect their name and gift preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Answer the questions carefully, as they will be used to determine your gift preferences.
3. Submit the form to participate in the Secret Santa gift exchange.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: This is the name of the participant. Please fill out your full name.
* **Participant Type** (`Yes/No`):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you want to be part of the Secret Santa gift exchange.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want to be part of the gift exchange.
* **Name to Gift** (`Yes/No`):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you want to receive a gift from the group.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want to receive a gift from the group.
* **Gift Limit**: This is the maximum value of the gift you are willing to give or receive. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 10, 20, 30).
* **Gift Limit**:
	+ Please note that the gift limit is optional, but it is used as a guideline for participants to follow. This field is only visible if you selected 'Yes' for 'Name to Gift'.
