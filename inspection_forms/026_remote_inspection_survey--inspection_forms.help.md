# Remote Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for conducting remote inspections of facilities, gathering essential data, and recording any additional comments or notes. 
## How To Complete This Form
To ensure you complete the form accurately and effectively, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Facility** (`remote_facility`, select_one, required: false): Select the facility you are inspecting from the dropdown list of options.
* **Remote Inspector** (`remote_inspector`, select_one, required: false): Choose the inspector who is conducting the remote inspection.
* **Remote Start Date** (`remote_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the inspection in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Remote Start Time** (`remote_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the inspection in the HH:MM:SS format.
* **Remote End Date** (`remote_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the inspection in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Remote End Time** (`remote_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the inspection in the HH:MM:SS format.
* **Remote Notes** (`remote_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Remote Comments** (`remote_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the facility or the inspection.
* **Remote Photos** (`remote_photos`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the photos to upload from your device.
* **Remote Signature** (`remote_signature`, text, required: false): Enter the inspector's signature for verification.
* **Remote Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Remote Facility Type** (`facility_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of facility being inspected.
* **Remote Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Remote Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the facility.
* **Remote Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Remote Facility Email** (`facility_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the facility.
* **Remote Facility Hours** (`facility_hours`, text, required: false): Enter the operating hours of the facility.
* **Remote Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **Remote Facility Inspector** (`facility_inspector`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility inspector.
* **Remote Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the facility.
* **Remote Facility Comments** (`facility_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the facility.
* **Remote Facility Photos** (`facility_photos`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the photos to upload from your device.
* **Remote Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, text, required: false): Enter the facility's signature for verification.
* **Remote Facility Review** (`facility_review`, text, required: false): Enter a review of the facility.
