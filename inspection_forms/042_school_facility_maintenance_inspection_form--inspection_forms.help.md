# School Facility Maintenance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Facility Maintenance Inspection Form is used to track and manage school facility maintenance requests and inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the school facility's ID in the `school_id` field.
2. Select the date of the inspection in the `date` field.
3. Choose the time of the inspection in the `time` field.
4. Describe the issue being inspected in the `issue_description` field.
5. Select the priority level of the issue in the `priority_level` field.
6. Enter the contact information of the assigned inspector in the `assigned_inspector` field.
7. Choose the status of the inspection in the `status` field.
8. Enter the completion date of the inspection in the `completed_date` field.
9. Enter any comments about the inspection in the `comments` field.
10. Enter any facility status updates in the `facility_status_update` field.
11. Select the follow-up date for the inspection in the `follow_up_date` field.
12. Choose the follow-up status in the `follow_up_status` field.
13. Enter any notes from the follow-up inspector in the `follow_up_inspector_note` field.
14. Enter the contact information of the follow-up inspector in the `follow_up_inspector_phone`, `follow_up_inspector_email`, and `follow_up_inspector_name` fields.
15. Enter any comments from the follow-up inspector in the `follow_up_inspector_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_id** (Number, Required): Enter the ID of the school facility being inspected.
* **date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the inspection.
* **time** (Time, Required): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **facility_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the school facility.
* **issue_description** (Text, Required): Describe the issue being inspected.
* **priority_level** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **assigned_inspector** (Email, Optional): Enter the contact information of the assigned inspector.
* **status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the inspection (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **completed_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the completion date of the inspection.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments about the inspection.
* **facility_status_update** (Note, Optional): Enter any facility status updates.
* **follow_up_date** (Date, Optional): Select the follow-up date for the inspection.
* **follow_up_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the follow-up status (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **follow_up_inspector_id** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments from the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the follow-up inspector (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **follow_up_inspector_note** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes from the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the follow-up inspection.
* **issue_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of issue being inspected (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Roofing, Flooring, Paint, Siding, Windows, Doors, Other).
* **school_id_2** (Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the school facility being inspected.
* **assigned_inspector_note** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes from the assigned inspector.
* **assigned_inspector_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the assigned inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_id_2** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_name_2** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_phone_2** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_email_2** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the follow-up inspector.
* **follow_up_inspector_comments_2** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments from the follow-up inspector.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information for the assigned and follow-up inspectors.
* Choose the correct status and priority level for the issue being inspected.
* Enter any relevant comments and notes to provide context for the inspection.
* Use this form to track and manage maintenance requests and inspections effectively.
