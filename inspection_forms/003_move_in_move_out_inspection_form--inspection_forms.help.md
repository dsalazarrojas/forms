# move-in-move-out-inspection-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of the Move In Move Out Form is to evaluate the overall condition of a property before or after a tenant moves in or out. This form helps ensure a thorough inspection and reduces potential disputes or issues during the move-in/move-out process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the inspection sections and assess the condition of the property. Select the options that best describe your observations for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exterior** (Select One - id: 2):
  Is the general condition of the property's exterior?
* **Move In Move Out Form** (Note - id: 1):
  A general note section for any additional comments or observations.
* **Is the property well-lit and well-maintained?** (Select Multiple - id: 3):
  Are the exterior lights functioning properly and is the property well-maintained in terms of cleanliness and repair?
* **Are there any broken windows or glass?** (Number - id: 4):
  Count the number of broken windows or glass found on the property.
* **Are the doors functional?** (Select One - id: 5):
  Are the exterior doors functional and able to open and close properly?
* **Interior** (Select One - id: 6):
  Is the overall condition of the interior of the property?
* **Is the kitchen well-equipped and clean?** (Select Multiple - id: 7):
  Is the kitchen well-equipped with necessary appliances and fixtures, and is it clean and free of any debris?
* **Are the appliances in good working order?** (Select Multiple - id: 8):
  Are all kitchen appliances functioning properly, or are there any issues with them?
* **Is the bathroom well-ventilated and clean?** (Select Multiple - id: 9):
  Is the bathroom well-ventilated and is it clean and free of any debris?
* **Are the heating and cooling systems operational?** (Select Multiple - id: 10):
  Are the heating and cooling systems functioning properly?
* **Fire Safety** (Select One - id: 11):
  Is the fire safety condition of the property?
* **Are the fire extinguishers well-marked and accessible?** (Select Multiple - id: 12):
  Are the fire extinguishers well-marked and easily accessible for use in case of an emergency?
* **Is the fire alarm functioning properly?** (Select Multiple - id: 13):
  Is the fire alarm functioning properly and alerting correctly in case of an emergency?
* **Security** (Select One - id: 14):
  Is the overall security condition of the property?
* **Security Section 1** (Select Multiple - id: 13):
  Are security features such as locks and alarms functioning properly?
* **Security Section 2** (Select Multiple - id: 10):
  Are security features such as cameras and motion detectors functioning properly?

Note: Please answer the questions as accurately and truthfully as possible. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please use the "Note" section to provide additional comments or explanations.
