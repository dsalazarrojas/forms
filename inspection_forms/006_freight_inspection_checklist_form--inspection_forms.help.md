# Freight Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Process** (`shipping_process`, text, optional)
	+ This is the starting page of the form, which serves as a heading for the shipping process.
* **Freight Damage** (`freight_damage`, text, optional)
	+ Describe any damage or issues encountered during shipping.
* **Packaging** (`packaging`, text, optional)
	+ Specify the type of packaging used for the goods being transported.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select, optional)
	+ Choose the delivery method used for shipping (Express, Standard, or Local).
* **Shipping Weight** (`shipping_weight`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the weight of the goods being shipped.
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date the goods were shipped.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time the goods were shipped.
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, select, optional)
	+ Choose the outcome of the inspection (Passed, Failed, or Not Evaluated).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or observations about the shipping process.
* **Tips**
	+ Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure that the information is reliable.
	+ If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
	+ This form is designed to be user-friendly; if you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.
