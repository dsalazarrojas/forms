# Telecom Network Infrastructure Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for field inspectors to record information about the condition and maintenance of telecom network infrastructure during site visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the site's general information (page 1).
2. Record the date of your site visit (page 2).
3. Report on the condition of the site (page 3).
4. Indicate if you found any network equipment (page 4).
5. Select the condition of the network (page 5).
6. Report maintenance actions taken or required (page 6).
7. Attach any supporting photos or observations (page 7).
8. Leave any comments or notes (page 8).
9. Sign and date the form (page 9).
10. Record the site visit date and your signature (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the site visited.
* **Site Visit (Page 2)**: Record the date and time of your site visit.
* **Site Survey (Page 3)**: Enter the site's condition using a number value (e.g., 0-10).
* **Network Information (Page 4)**: Select 'Yes' if the network equipment is present.
* **Network Equipment (Page 5)**: Select 'Yes' for each type of equipment found.
* **Condition Report (Page 6)**: Enter any observations or comments about the site's condition.
* **Maintenance Actions (Page 7)**: Select 'Yes' for each maintenance action taken.
* **Photos (Page 8)**: Upload any photos taken during the site visit.
* **Comments (Page 9)**: Leave any additional comments or notes.
* **Signature (Page 10)**: Sign and date the form to verify the inspection.
* **Date (Page 10)**: Record the date of the site visit.
* **Site Visit Date (Page 10)**: Record the date of the site visit.
