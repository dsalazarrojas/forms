# Walkability Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a walkability audit, which is an assessment of a property's or campus's accessibility for people with mobility impairments.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the options that apply to the walkability audit from the available choices in each section.
2. Enter your observations and comments about the audit in the corresponding text fields.
3. Optionally, you can provide a date and time of when the audit was conducted.
4. Identify the priority level of improvement needed for each section.
5. Assign the audit to a person or team for further review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Walkability Audit Checklist (Select Multiple)**: This section requires you to select all the options that apply to the walkability audit.
* **2. Street**: Please enter the street name of the property or campus being audited.
* **3. Campus**: If applicable, please enter the name of the campus being audited.
* **4. Property**: Enter the name of the property being audited.
* **5. Observations**: This field is for you to enter any observations or comments about the audit.
* **6. Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or information.
* **7. Priority Improvement**: Select 'Yes' if the area needs immediate improvement.
* **8. Date**: Enter the date the audit was conducted.
* **9. Time**: Enter the time the audit was conducted.
* **10. Assigned To**: Select 'Yes' if this audit is assigned to someone or some team for review and action.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- Review each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or team lead.
