# shelf_compliance_audit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about store conditions, shelf standards, and other essential details during an audit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter your answers in the designated fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Conditions** (`store_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the store's current conditions.
* **Shelf Standards** (`shelf_standards`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options from the provided list, indicating whether the store meets the standards in each area.
* **Checklist Items** (`checklist_items`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the applicable option that best describes the current status of the checklist items.
* **Photos** (`photos`, `note`, required: false): If applicable, attach any photos that support your answers in the other fields.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `text`, required: false): Describe any follow-up actions or recommendations you have for the store.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the store's ID number.
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the auditor performing the inspection.
* **Auditor Email** (`auditor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the auditor performing the inspection.

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission to access and use this form before proceeding.
* Review each field carefully and provide clear and concise answers.
* Use standard formatting and file types for any attached photos.
* Be thorough and honest in your assessment.
* If you are unsure about any field, seek clarification before submitting the form.
