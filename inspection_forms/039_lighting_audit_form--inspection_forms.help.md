# Lighting Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighting Audit Form is used to collect data about lighting fixtures and conditions. This form helps maintenance personnel or facilities managers record observations and notes about lighting installations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the lighting audit information in the designated fields.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate and honest information.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lighting Audit** (`lighting_audit_form`, text, optional):
This field is for entering the lighting audit information.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional):
Enter the location of the lighting fixture or area being audited.
* **Power Requirement** (`power_requirement`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following power requirements:
	+ 120
	+ 180
	+ 240
	+ 280
	+ 480
	+ 600
* **Power Type** (`power_type`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following power types:
	+ AC
	+ DC
* **Power Factor** (`power_factor`, text, optional):
Enter the power factor information.
* **Quality** (`quality`, text, optional):
Enter the quality information.
* **Lumens** (`lumens`, number, optional):
Enter the lumens information.
* **Lux** (`lux`, number, optional):
Enter the lux information.
* **Color Temperature** (`color_temperature`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following color temperatures:
	+ 2700
	+ 3000
	+ 3500
	+ 4000
	+ 6500
* **Color Rendering** (`color_rendering`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following color rendering options:
	+ warm
	+ cool
	+ warm-white
	+ cool-white
* **Fixture Type** (`fixture_type`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following fixture types:
	+ LED
	+ Halogen
	+ Fluorescent
	+ HID
* **Fixture Location** (`fixture_location`, text, optional):
Enter the location of the fixture.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
Enter any additional comments.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional):
Enter any additional notes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional):
Select one of the following:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other
