# Quality Control Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Inspection Form is designed to collect data on vehicle inspections for quality control purposes. This form will help you track inspection results, identify potential issues, and provide notes for further action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information for the vehicle being inspected.
* Ensure that each vehicle has a unique inspection record.
* If you are unsure about the purpose of a field, check the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (Page 1): This page is the first section of the form. It may be used to display additional information or instructions.
* **Inspection Date** (Page 2): Enter the date of the inspection.
	+ Type: `date` field
	+ Required: No
* **Inspector Name** (Page 3): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
	+ Type: `text` field
	+ Required: No
* **Vehicle Make** (Page 4): Enter the make of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Type: `text` field
	+ Required: No
* **Vehicle Model** (Page 5): Enter the model of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Type: `text` field
	+ Required: No
* **Vehicle Year** (Page 6): Enter the year of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Type: `number` field
	+ Required: No
* **Inspection Location** (Page 7): Enter the location of the inspection.
	+ Type: `text` field
	+ Required: No
* **Inspector Notes** (Page 8): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
	+ Type: `note` field
	+ Required: No
* **Vehicle Status** (Page 9): Select the current status of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: `Active` or `Inactive`
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Ensure that each vehicle has a unique inspection record.
* Review the fields carefully and enter accurate information to maintain the effectiveness of the form. 
* If a field is missing or unclear, check the field-by-field explanation again for more information.
