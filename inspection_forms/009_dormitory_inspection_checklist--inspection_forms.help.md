# Dormitory Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide the inspector through the process of inspecting a dormitory. The form is used to gather information about the condition and status of the dormitory, including its maintenance and any necessary repairs or issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
* Select the correct options for each field based on your observations and findings.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Dormitory Inspection** (`first_page`)
This field is a general heading for the inspection.
* This is the first page of the inspection form, where you will list all necessary items related to the dormitory inspection.

### 2. **Check List Items** (`checklist_items`)
Select all items that apply from the list:
* Yes, the item is in good condition.
* No, the item is not in good condition or is missing.
* This field is used to track the condition of various items in the dormitory, such as furniture, fixtures, and appliances.

### 3. **Maintenance Issues** (`maintenance_issues`)
Select "Yes" if you have identified any maintenance issues during the inspection:
* Yes, there are maintenance issues.
* No, there are no maintenance issues.
* This field is used to highlight any maintenance issues that need to be addressed in the dormitory.

### 4. **Other Maintenance Issues** (`maintenance_issues_other`)
If you have identified any maintenance issues not listed in the previous field, describe them here:
* This field is used to provide additional information about any maintenance issues not listed in the previous field.
* Please be as detailed as possible and include any relevant descriptions or photographs.

### 5. **Date** (`date`)
Enter the date of the inspection:
* Use the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* This field is used to record the date of the inspection.

### 6. **Time** (`time`)
Enter the time of the inspection (optional):
* Use the correct format for times (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
* This field is used to record the time of the inspection, but it is not required.

### 7. **Room Number** (`room_number`)
Enter the room number of the dormitory being inspected:
* Use the correct format for room numbers (e.g., 101, 102, etc.).
* This field is used to identify the specific room being inspected.

### 8. **Room Description** (`room_description`)
Describe the room being inspected:
* Be as detailed as possible and include any relevant notes or observations.
* This field is used to provide additional information about the room being inspected.

### 9. **Comments** (`comments`)
Add any additional comments or recommendations:
* This field is used to provide any additional information or recommendations that may not be covered in the other fields.
* Please be thorough and include any relevant details.

### 10. **Submit** (`submit`)
Submit the form to complete the inspection:
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* This field is used to complete and submit the inspection form.
