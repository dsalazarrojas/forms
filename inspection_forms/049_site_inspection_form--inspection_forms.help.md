# Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Inspection Form is a tool used for site inspections, allowing site inspectors to document and report on safety issues and compliance with regulations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the site's name and address.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. Select the safety issues observed during the inspection from the list.
4. Provide a brief description of the safety measures taken to address the issues.
5. Enter the name and any notes of the site inspector.
6. Select the assigned inspector's name.
7. Choose the safety certification that applies to the site.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name**: The name of the site being inspected.
* **Site Address**: The physical address of the site being inspected. This field is optional, but providing it helps with record-keeping and future reference.
* **Date of Inspection**: The date the inspection took place.
* **Safety Issues**: Select the safety issues observed during the inspection. Choose all applicable options.
* **Safety Measures**: Describe the safety measures taken to address the issues observed during the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: The name of the site inspector, as this will help in tracking who conducted the inspection.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations made during the inspection.
* **Assigned Inspector**: Select the person assigned to perform the inspection, or the one responsible for the site's safety.
* **Safety Certification**: Choose the applicable safety certification for the site being inspected.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when selecting safety issues and describing safety measures.
* Use the assigned inspector field to track who conducted the inspection, but also to note any specific responsibilities or contact information for that person.
* Review and understand the available options for the safety certification field before selecting one, as it may impact site's safety rating.
