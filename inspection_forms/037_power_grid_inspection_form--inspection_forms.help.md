# Power Grid Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Grid Inspection Form is designed to collect essential information about the state of power grids, helping inspectors to document and record their findings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Grid Location**: Enter the specific location where the power grid is located, including any relevant identifying information such as grid name, address, or coordinates.
2. **Date and Time**: Select the date and time of the inspection.
3. **Weather Conditions**: Describe the current weather conditions at the time of the inspection.
4. **Grid Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the grid using the options:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
5. **Asset Condition**: Describe the condition of the power grid assets, such as transmission lines, substations, or transformers.
6. **Asset Status**: Select the status of the power grid assets using the options:
	* Active
	* Inactive
	* Decommissioned
7. **Inspection Finding**: Describe any notable findings or observations made during the inspection.
8. **Photo Attachment 1**: Attach any relevant photos or images to support your findings.
9. **Notes**: Record any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
10. **Follow-up Actions**: Provide any necessary follow-up actions or recommendations for future inspections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grid Location** (Required): The location where the power grid is situated.
* **Date and Time** (Required): The date and time of the inspection.
* **Weather Conditions** (Required): A description of the weather at the time of the inspection.
* **Grid Condition** (Optional): A rating of the grid's overall condition.
* **Asset Condition** (Required): A description of the condition of the power grid assets.
* **Asset Status** (Optional): The status of the power grid assets.
* **Inspection Finding** (Required): A description of any notable findings or observations made during the inspection.
* **Photo Attachment 1** (Optional): An attachment for supporting photos or images.
* **Notes** (Optional): Additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Follow-up Actions** (Required): Recommendations or actions for future inspections.
