# technology_company_industry_standards_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help technology companies assess their compliance status with industry standards. The goal is to collect information about a company's technology standards, audit frequency, and compliance status, as well as other relevant details. This form will help companies track their progress in maintaining industry standards and identify areas for improvement.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Technology Company name in the "Technology Company" field (field 1).
2. Select the industry standards that apply to your company from the "Industry Standards" field (field 2).
3. Choose the date of the inspection from the calendar picker in the "Date of Inspection" field (field 3).
4. Select the time of the inspection from the time picker in the "Time of Inspection" field (field 4).
5. Enter the name of the vendor in the "Vendor Name" field (field 5).
6. Enter the name of the auditor in the "Auditor" field (field 6).
7. Select the technology standards used by your company from the "Technology Standards" field (field 7).
8. Indicate the compliance status of your company from the options in the "Compliance Status" field (field 8).
9. Enter any additional notes about the audit in the "Notes" field (field 9).
10. Enter the email address of your company in the "Email" field (field 10).
11. Enter the phone number of your company in the "Phone" field (field 11).
12. Select the technology standards used by your company from the "Technology Standards Used" field (field 12).
13. Choose the compliance level of your company from the options in the "Compliance Level" field (field 13).
