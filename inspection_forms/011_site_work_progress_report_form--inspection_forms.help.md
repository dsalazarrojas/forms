# site work progress report form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The site work progress report form is used to gather information about site work progress from clients and inspectors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your project progress in the "Progress Report" field (page 1).
2. Provide the site location (page 2).
3. Enter the inspection date and time (page 3 and 4).
4. Select the project status (page 5).
5. Enter the client's name and contact information (page 6-9).
6. Add any additional comments or notes (page 10).
7. Upload any relevant site photos, videos, or documents (page 11-13).
8. Enter any additional site notes (page 15).
9. Enter the project location and type (page 16 and 17).
10. Enter the site address (page 18).
11. List any site contacts (page 19).
12. Obtain the client's signature (page 20).
13. Obtain the inspector's signature (page 21).
14. Enter the client's date and time (page 22 and 23).
15. Enter the inspector's date and time (page 24 and 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Progress Report** (`Progress Report`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of your project progress.
* **Site Location** (`Site Location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the physical location of the site.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`Inspection Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Project Status** (`Project Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the project (Active, Pending, Complete).
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Project Manager Name** (`Project Manager Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Inspector Comments** (`Inspector Comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments from the inspector.
* **Site Work Status** (`Site Work Status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the site work (Active, Completed).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
* **Site Photo** (`Site Photo`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo of the site.
* **Site Video** (`Site Video`, `text`, required: false): Upload a video of the site.
* **Site Work Documents** (`Site Work Documents`, `text`, required: false): Upload any relevant documents.
* **Site Work Attachments** (`Site Work Attachments`, `text`, required: false): Upload any attachments related to the site work.
* **Site Notes** (`Site Notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any site notes.
* **Project Location** (`Project Location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Type** (`Project Type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of project.
* **Site Address** (`Site Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the site.
* **Site Contacts** (`Site Contacts`, `text`, required: false): List any site contacts.
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, `text`, required: false): Obtain the client's signature.
* **Inspector Signature** (`Inspector Signature`, `text`, required: false): Obtain the inspector's signature.
* **Client Date** (`Client Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the client's signature.
* **Client Time** (`Client Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the client's signature.
* **Inspector Date** (`Inspector Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the inspector's signature.
* **Inspector Time** (`Inspector Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the inspector's signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field correctly and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* Attach any relevant documents, photos, or videos to support your progress report.
* Obtain signatures from clients and inspectors as required.
* Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing your progress report.
