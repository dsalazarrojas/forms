# Parapet Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parapet Inspection Checklist is designed to document the condition of Parapets during an inspection. This form helps ensure that all relevant information is collected to make informed decisions about maintenance, repairs, or further actions needed to maintain the Parapet's integrity. This form should be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that any necessary actions are taken to prevent or address potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field thoroughly and accurately.
2. Answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. If you're unsure about the answers to any questions, use your best judgment and note any supporting evidence or references.
4. For the "Recommended" field, select the most appropriate action based on the Parapet's condition.
5. For the "Condition Description" field, describe the current condition of the Parapet.
6. For the "Findings" field, document any relevant information or observations about the Parapet's condition.
7. For the "Other Findings" field, include any additional information not captured in the "Findings" field.
8. For the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parapet Conditions** (`parapet_condition`): Describe the overall condition of the Parapet.
* **Photos** (`attached_photos`): Select the relevant photos that demonstrate the condition of the Parapet.
* **Condition Rating** (`condition_rating`): Rate the condition of the Parapet on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very poor and 10 being excellent.
* **Condition Description** (`condition_description`): Describe the condition of the Parapet in detail.
* **Findings** (`findings`): Document any notable findings or observations about the Parapet's condition.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended`): Select the most appropriate action to address any issues or concerns with the Parapet's condition.
* **Other Findings** (`other_findings`): Include any additional information not captured in the "Findings" field.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

This form is designed to be completed thoroughly and accurately to ensure that all necessary information is captured to make informed decisions about the Parapet's maintenance and upkeep.
