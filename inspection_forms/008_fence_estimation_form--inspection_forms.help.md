# Fence Estimation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Fence Estimation Form is a tool used by fence contractors and sales teams to gather information about a customer's property and fence requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the customer's name and contact information in the customer_name, customer_email, and customer_phone fields.
2.  Select the type of house that needs a fence (Home, Condo, Townhouse, Duplex, Single Family).
3.  Choose the type of fence you need (Wood, Vinyl, Chain link, Iron, Aluminum).
4.  Enter the height of the fence.
5.  Enter the width of the fence.
6.  Enter the depth of the fence.
7.  Fill out the material cost of the fence.
8.  Fill out the labor cost of the fence.
9.  Enter the labor hours required for the project.
10.  Enter the labor rate (per hour).
11.  Optionally, enter additional notes about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **customer_name** (Text, required): Enter the customer's name.
*  **email** (Email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
*  **phone** (Text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
*  **house_type** (Select One, required): Select the type of house that needs a fence.
*  **fence_type** (Select Multiple, required): Choose the type of fence you need.
*  **height** (Number, required): Enter the height of the fence.
*  **width** (Number, required): Enter the width of the fence.
*  **depth** (Number, required): Enter the depth of the fence.
*  **material_cost** (Number, required): Enter the material cost of the fence.
*  **labor_cost** (Number, required): Enter the labor cost of the fence.
*  **labor_hours** (Number, required): Enter the labor hours required for the project.
*  **labor_rate** (Number, required): Enter the labor rate (per hour).
*  **total_cost** (Number, required): Automatically calculated by the system.
*  **notes** (Note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the project.
