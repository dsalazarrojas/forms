# Stormwater Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stormwater inspection report form is used to assess the stormwater management practices and measures of a particular site. The form has 11 fields that need to be completed to evaluate the site's stormwater management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer for each field based on your observation and assessment of the site's stormwater management practices.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will be used to guide further action and decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stormwater Management Compliance** (`stormwater_inspection_report_page_1`): Assess the overall effectiveness of the stormwater management practices and measures in place at the site. Choose "Good" if the site has an effective stormwater management system in place, "Fair" if there are some issues, and "Poor" if the management is severely inadequate.
* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions_page_1`): Describe the site conditions, including any hazards, obstacles, or challenges that may affect stormwater management practices.
* **Pollution Prevention Measures** (`pollution_prevention_measures_page_1`): Select the pollution prevention measures in place at the site. Choose "Good" if the measures are effective, "Fair" if there are some issues, and "Poor" if the measures are ineffective.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page_1`): Provide any additional comments or information about the site's stormwater management practices.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection_page_1`): Record the date and time of the stormwater inspection.
* **Stormwater Inspection Report Form** (`stormwater_inspection_report_form_page_1`): Indicate whether the stormwater inspection report form was completed accurately and truthfully.
* **Photo** (`photo_page_1`): Take a photo of the stormwater management practices and measures in place at the site.
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption_page_1`): Provide a caption for the photo, describing what the photo shows and why it is relevant to the site's stormwater management practices.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection_page_2`): Record the date and time of the stormwater inspection on the second page.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page_2`): Provide any additional comments or information about the site's stormwater management practices on the second page.
* **Photo** (`photo_page_2`): Take a photo of the stormwater management practices and measures in place at the site on the second page.
