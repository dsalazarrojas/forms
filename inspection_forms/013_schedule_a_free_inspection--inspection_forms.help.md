# Schedule A Free Inspection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through filling out the Schedule A Free Inspection form, which is designed to gather information for a free inspection of a property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Schedule A Free Inspection" field, which is the main title of the form.
2. In the "provider_details" field, enter the details of the inspection provider or agency.
3. Enter the request details in the "request_details" field.
4. Select the desired appointment time from the "appointment_details" dropdown menu.
5. In the "contact_info" field, select how the client would like to be contacted (email, phone, or text).
6. Choose the type of property being inspected (residential, commercial, industrial) in the "property_details" field.
7. Enter the property address in the "property_address" field.
8. If necessary, enter any additional service requests in the "service_request" field.
9. Enter the date of the inspection in the "date" field.
10. Enter the time of the inspection in the "time" field.
11. Add any additional notes in the "note" field.
12. Enter the contact email in the "email" field.
13. Enter the contact phone number in the "phone" field.
14. If necessary, specify the type of property being inspected in the "property_type" field.
15. Finally, enter any information about the property manager in the "property_manager" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule A Free Inspection**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of the inspection.
* **provider_details**: Enter the details of the inspection provider or agency.
* **request_details**: Enter any additional information about the inspection request.
* **appointment_details**: Select a time slot for the inspection.
* **contact_info**: Choose how the client would like to be contacted (email, phone, or text).
* **property_details**: Choose the type of property being inspected (residential, commercial, industrial).
* **property_address**: Enter the property address.
* **service_request**: Enter any additional service requests.
* **date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **email**: Enter the contact email.
* **phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **property_type**: Enter the type of property being inspected.
* **property_manager**: Enter information about the property manager.

Note: This guide assumes that all fields are optional and can be filled out as needed, depending on the specific requirements of the inspection.
