# BBS Behavioral Based Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to observe and document the safety behavior of employees during work activities. It helps identify potential hazards and areas for improvement in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Inspection Date" field with the date of the observation.
2. Enter the "Inspector Name" in the field provided, along with their "Inspector ID or Badge Number" if applicable.
3. Select the "Department or Area Inspected" from the list of options provided.
4. Enter the "Employee Name" being observed, if applicable.
5. For each observation, select the time of the observation using the "Time of Observation" field.
6. Specify the duration of the observation in minutes using the "Duration of Observation" field.
7. Describe what the employee was doing during the observation in the "Activity Being Performed" field.
8. Select whether the employee was wearing proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and specify the details if they were not compliant.
9. Select whether equipment was used safely and specify the details if it was not used safely.
10. Evaluate the "Housekeeping and Order" of the work area, selecting from the options provided.
11. Identify any hazards present during the observation and select the type of hazard.
12. If hazards were identified, describe them in the "Hazard Description" field.
13. Specify the corrective action that should be taken to address the hazards.
14. Set a deadline for the corrective action to be completed using the "Action Deadline" field.
15. List any positive safety behaviors observed during the observation.
16. Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the safety observation.
* **Inspector ID or Badge Number** (`inspector_id`, optional): Enter the ID or badge number of the person conducting the safety observation, if applicable.
* **Department or Area Inspected** (`department`, required): Select the department or area of the work being inspected.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, optional): Enter the name of the employee being observed, if applicable.
* **Time of Observation** (`observation_time`, optional): Specify the time of the observation.
* **Duration of Observation** (`observation_duration`, optional): Specify the duration of the observation in minutes.
* **Activity Being Performed** (`activity_observed`, required): Describe what the employee was doing during the observation.
* **Proper PPE Being Worn?** (`ppe_compliance`, required): Select whether the employee was wearing proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
* **PPE Details** (`ppe_details`, optional): If the employee was not wearing proper PPE, specify the details.
* **Equipment Used Safely?** (`equipment_use`, required): Select whether equipment was used safely during the observation.
* **Housekeeping and Order** (`housekeeping_status`, required): Evaluate the condition of the work area, selecting from the options provided.
* **Any Hazards Identified?** (`hazards_identified`, required): Select whether any hazards were identified during the observation.
* **Hazard Description** (`hazard_description`, optional): If hazards were identified, describe them in this field.
* **Corrective Action Required** (`corrective_action`, optional): Specify the corrective action that should be taken to address the hazards.
* **Action Deadline** (`action_deadline`, optional): Set a deadline for the corrective action to be completed.
* **Positive Safety Behaviors Observed** (`positive_behaviors`, optional): List any positive safety behaviors observed during the observation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations.
