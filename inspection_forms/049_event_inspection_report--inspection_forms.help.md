# Event Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Inspection Report is used by inspectors to document and report on inspection activities after attending an event. This form helps to collect information on the event's key details and findings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Event Inspection Form section with the relevant event details.
2. Provide information on the inspector's personal details in the Inspector Information section.
3. Add photos to the Photo Gallery section, if applicable.
4. Document any findings in the Finding Section, including the finding status and priority.
5. Fill out the Event Summary and Comments sections with your observations and comments.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field for contact purposes.
7. Sign the Inspector Signature field to confirm your completion of the form.
8. Count and record the total number of photos uploaded in the Photo Count field.
9. Enter the Date and Time of Inspection for accuracy.
10. Select the Inspection Type and Frequency for the event.
11. Choose the status of each finding in the Finding Status field.
12. Choose the finding priority in the Finding Priority field.
13. Count the number of photos uploaded in the Photo Gallery Count field.
14. Enter any Inspection Comments or additional information.
15. Finally, confirm that you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Inspection Form** (`event_inspection_form`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event, including the name, location, and date.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional event details, such as the event category or type.
* **Inspector Information** (`inspector_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, contact number, and email for contact purposes.
* **Photo Gallery** (`photo_gallery`, text, optional): Upload photos relevant to the event inspection, including descriptions and URLs.
* **Finding Section** (`finding_section`, text, optional): Document any findings made during the inspection, including status and priority.
* **Event Summary** (`event_summary`, text, optional): Enter a brief summary of the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or additional information about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your completion of the form.
* **Photo Count** (`photo_count`, number, optional): Count the number of photos uploaded in the Photo Gallery.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Type** (`inspection_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of inspection performed (Yes/No).
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of inspection (Yes/No).
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Enter a photo description, if applicable.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the photo, if applicable.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the photo, if applicable.
* **Finding Type** (`finding_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of finding (Yes/No).
* **Finding Description** (`finding_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the finding.
* **Finding Frequency** (`finding_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of finding (Yes/No).
* **Finding Status** (`finding_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the finding (Active/Inactive).
* **Finding Priority** (`finding_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the priority of the finding (Yes/No).
* **Photo Gallery Count** (`photo_gallery_count`, number, optional): Count the number of photos uploaded in the Photo Gallery.
* **Inspection Comments** (`inspection_comments`, text, optional): Enter any inspection comments or additional information.
* **Form Complete** (`form_complete`, text, optional): Confirm that you have completed the form.
