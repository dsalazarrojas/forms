# Electrical Installation Periodic Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Installation Periodic Inspection Checklist is a tool used to inspect and document the status of electrical installations on a regular basis. This form helps ensure that electrical systems are functioning correctly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question or select the relevant option for your electrical installation.
3. Complete all required fields before proceeding.
4. Provide any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical Installation Details** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing any background information or details about the electrical installation being inspected, such as its location, date installed, or relevant documentation.
* **Electrical Equipment List** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all electrical equipment that is present at the location, including but not limited to circuit breakers, outlets, and switches.
* **Inspection Checklist** (`page_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate the status of the electrical installation's safety and functionality. If you select "No", please provide reasons for the failure in the "Notes" section.
* **Notes** (`page_4`, `note`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or explanations about the inspection, such as areas of concern or recommended repairs.
