# Energy Leak Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Leak Assessment Survey is a form designed to assess and document energy leaks in a building. It helps to gather information about the location, severity, and type of leak, as well as provide a space for inspectors to take notes and make recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the `leak_location` field, describe the location where the leak is occurring.
3. For the `leak_description` field, provide a detailed description of the leak, including any relevant details.
4. For the `leak_photo` field, upload a photo of the leak.
5. For the `leak_category` field, select the type of leak from the available options (Insulation, Electrical, HVAC, Plumbing, or Other).
6. For the `leak_severity` field, select the severity of the leak from the available options (Low, Moderate, or Severe).
7. For the `leak_recommendation` field, enter a recommendation for addressing the leak.
8. For the `inspector_comments` field, enter any additional comments or information for the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **leak_location** (Text): This field asks you to describe the location where the leak is occurring. This is an open-ended question, so provide as much detail as possible.
* **leak_description** (Text): This field is where you can provide a detailed description of the leak. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **leak_photo** (Text): You can upload a photo of the leak to help the inspector understand the issue.
* **leak_category** (Select One):
	+ Insulation: A leak related to insulation, such as a missing or damaged insulation material.
	+ Electrical: An electrical-related leak, such as a faulty electrical connection or wiring.
	+ HVAC: A heating, ventilation, or air conditioning-related leak.
	+ Plumbing: A leak related to plumbing, such as a leaky faucet or pipe.
	+ Other: Any other type of leak that doesn't fit into the above categories.
* **leak_severity** (Select One):
	+ Low: A minor leak that is not causing significant damage or safety issues.
	+ Moderate: A leak that requires attention but is not critical.
	+ Severe: A critical leak that requires immediate attention.
* **leak_recommendation** (Text): Suggest a course of action for addressing the leak, such as repairing or replacing a specific component.
* **inspector_comments** (Note): Any additional notes or comments for the inspector, such as any other issues or concerns.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each field, so the inspector can understand the issue accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* You can upload photos or documents to help support your answers.
