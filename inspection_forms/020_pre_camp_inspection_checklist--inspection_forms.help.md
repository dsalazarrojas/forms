## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate the readiness and quality of a campsite for camping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your knowledge of the campsite.
2. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate response.
3. Fill out all fields, even if you are unsure or lack information.
