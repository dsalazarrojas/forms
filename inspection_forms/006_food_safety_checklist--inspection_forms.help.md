# Food Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Checklist is a tool designed to help food establishments assess their level of compliance with food safety regulations. It is essential for identifying potential risks and ensuring the safe handling and storage of food.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question according to the guidelines provided.
2. Ensure that you have sufficient information about your food handling and storage practices.
3. Complete the form honestly and accurately, indicating any deficiencies or non-compliance with food safety regulations.
4. Review your answers to ensure that you have addressed all areas of concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Handling** (required/optional: optional)
• **Handling**: Please describe the procedures in place for handling raw food materials, including cleaning and sanitizing procedures for utensils and equipment.

### **Storage** (required/optional: optional)
• **Storage**: Describe the storage practices for raw and processed food materials, including proper refrigeration, temperature control, and labeling.

### **Labeling** (required/optional: optional)
• **Labeling**: Explain the labeling system used for raw and processed food materials, including ingredient information, date, and storage information.

## Tips
• Be thorough and detailed in your answers to ensure accuracy.
• Review and update your answers regularly to reflect any changes in your food handling and storage practices.
• Consult with a qualified food safety expert if you are unsure about any aspect of food safety regulations.

Note: This form is for guidance purposes only. Compliance with food safety regulations is the responsibility of food establishments, and it is essential to review and verify the accuracy of this form with a qualified food safety expert.
