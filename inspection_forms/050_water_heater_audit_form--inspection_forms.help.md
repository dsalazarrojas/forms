# Water Heater Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the water heater inspection, including the home address, customer details, and any issues found. It will help in maintaining accurate records and facilitating effective maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, first, ensure you are in the correct section (page 1) with the "Home Address" field.
- Fill in the "Home Address" field with the address where the water heater is located.
- Move to the next section (page 2) and fill in the "Customer Name" field with the name of the customer.
- In the "Water Heater Make" field, select 'Yes' if the water heater is functioning correctly, or 'No' if it is not.
- The "Water Heater Model" field is a text input for the specific model of the water heater.
- In the "Inspection Date" field, select the date of the inspection using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- The "Inspection Time" field is not required, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.
- In the "Issue Found" field, select 'False' if no issues were found, or 'True' if any issues were detected.
- The "Description Of Issue" field is where you can provide a detailed description of the issue.
- In the "Repair/Replace" field, select 'Repair' if the issue can be fixed, or 'Replace' if the water heater needs to be replaced.
- The "Maintenance Suggestions" field is optional and can be used to provide additional advice on how to maintain the water heater.
- In the "Technician Name" field, fill in the name of the technician who performed the inspection.
- The "Technician Company" field should be filled with the name of the company the technician works for.
- In the "Service Date" field, select the date of the last service, using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- The "Service Time" field is not required and should be left blank if not applicable.
- In the "Cost" field, enter the cost of the repair or replacement.
- The "Comments" field is optional and can be used to provide additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): This field is for entering the address of the location where the water heater is situated.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the customer.
* **Water Heater Make** (`water_heater_make`, select_one, optional): This field is used to indicate if the water heater is functioning correctly, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Water Heater Model** (`water_heater_model`, text, optional): This field is for entering the specific model of the water heater.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): This field is for entering the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): This field is for entering the time of the inspection, but it is not required.
* **Issue Found** (`issue_found`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate if any issues were found during the inspection, with options 'False' or 'True'.
* **Description Of Issue** (`description_of_issue`, text, required): This field is for providing a detailed description of the issue found.
* **Repair/Replace** (`repair_replacement`, select_one, required): This field is for indicating the recommended course of action, with options 'Repair' or 'Replace'.
* **Maintenance Suggestions** (`maintenance_suggestions`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional advice on how to maintain the water heater.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the technician who performed the inspection.
* **Technician Company** (`technician_company`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the company the technician works for.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): This field is for entering the date of the last service in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): This field is for entering the time of the last service, but it is not required.
* **Cost** (`cost`, number, required): This field is for entering the cost of the repair or replacement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional comments or notes.
