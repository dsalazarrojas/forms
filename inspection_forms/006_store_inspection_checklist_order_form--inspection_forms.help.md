# Store Inspection Checklist Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from store owners for store inspection purposes. It is intended for store owners to provide details about their store, such as contact information, preferred inspection date, and store ID, which will be used by inspectors to schedule and perform store inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as **Store ID** and **Contact Details**.
2. Enter your preferred date for the inspection in the **Preferred Date** field.
3. Once you have completed the required fields, review your answers carefully to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store ID** (`store_id`, number, required): This is a unique identifier for your store. Please enter the correct store ID as it will be used to identify your store for the inspection.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you have any contact information for the inspection, such as a phone number or email address, please enter it here. This will help us reach out to you with any questions or updates about your store inspection.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred date and time for the store inspection. Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your preferred date, but we may need to adjust it if our inspectors are not available.
* **Store Inspection Checklist Order Form** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form`, text, optional): This field is not currently in use.
