# Ship Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ship Inspection Form is a document used to record and document the condition and details of a ship during inspections. This form is used to gather information about the ship's hull condition, details, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions and fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Ensure you select the correct options from the provided dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional remarks or comments in the "Remarks" field.
4. Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hull Condition**: Describe the overall condition of the ship's hull, including any notable damages or issues.
* **Ship Details**: Select the correct options for the ship's port of loading, port of discharge, and type of voyage.
* **Cargo Details**: Enter any relevant information about the ship's cargo, including type and quantity.
* **Crew Details**: Enter information about the ship's crew, including number and qualifications.
* **Inspection Results**: Record the results of the inspection, including any notable findings or issues.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Ship Type**: Select the correct type of ship (Bulk, Cargo, or Passenger).
* **Ship Length**: Enter the ship's length in meters.
* **Ship Speed**: Enter the ship's speed in knots.
* **Ship Draft**: Enter the ship's draft in meters.
* **Ship Breadth**: Enter the ship's breadth in meters.
* **Ship Beam**: Enter the ship's beam in meters.
* **Ship Depth**: Enter the ship's depth in meters.
* **Ship Tonnage**: Enter the ship's tonnage in tons.
* **Ship Crew**: Enter the number of crew members.
* **Ship Tons**: Enter the ship's tons in tons.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Ship Type Id**: Select the correct type of ship (Bulk, Cargo, or Passenger).
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Ship Type Id 2**: Select the correct type of ship (Bulk, Cargo, or Passenger).
* **Inspection Date 2**: Enter the date of the second inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Ship Speed 2**: Enter the ship's speed in knots.
* **Ship Draft 2**: Enter the ship's draft in meters.
* **Ship Breadth 2**: Enter the ship's breadth in meters.
* **Ship Depth 2**: Enter the ship's depth in meters.
