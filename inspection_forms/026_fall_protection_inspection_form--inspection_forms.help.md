# Fall Protection Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Protection Inspection Form is designed to help inspect and document fall protection equipment, training, and other relevant safety measures in place to protect employees from falls from heights.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing general information, including your name and contact details.
2. Answer the questions about the inspection areas, such as which office spaces or stairways were inspected.
3. Provide information about the equipment available for fall protection, such as harnesses, nets, or other safety gear.
4. Specify the safety equipment used for fall protection, like harnesses, harnesses with shock absorbers, or other types of fall arrest equipment.
5. Describe the training programs provided to employees on fall protection, including certification status.
6. Document any records and reports related to fall protection, such as incident reports or maintenance records.
7. Confirm or deny certification for the inspection.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (General Information, text, optional)
	+ This field is for providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Equipment Information** (Equipment Information, text, optional)
	+ This field is for listing or describing the fall protection equipment available, such as harnesses, nets, or other safety gear.
- **Inspection Areas** (Inspection Areas, select multiple, optional)
	+ Choose which areas were inspected, such as office spaces or stairways.
- **Hazard Control Measures** (Hazard Control Measures, text, optional)
	+ Describe any hazard control measures in place, like guardrails, netting, or other safety systems.
- **Safety Equipment** (Safety Equipment, text, optional)
	+ List or describe the safety equipment used for fall protection, such as harnesses, harnesses with shock absorbers, or other types of fall arrest equipment.
- **Fall Protection Equipment** (Fall Protection Equipment, text, optional)
	+ Describe the fall protection equipment available, such as harnesses, nets, or other safety gear.
- **Personal Protection Equipment** (Personal Protection Equipment, text, optional)
	+ List or describe any personal protective equipment, like hard hats, gloves, or other safety gear.
- **Training Programs** (Training Programs, text, optional)
	+ Describe any training programs provided on fall protection, including certification status.
- **Records and Reports** (Records and Reports, text, optional)
	+ Document any records or reports related to fall protection, such as incident reports or maintenance records.
- **Certification** (Certification, select one, optional)
	+ Confirm or deny certification for the inspection.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
- **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about this inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all relevant fields to ensure that the inspection is thorough and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure that all safety measures are in place and employees are adequately protected.
