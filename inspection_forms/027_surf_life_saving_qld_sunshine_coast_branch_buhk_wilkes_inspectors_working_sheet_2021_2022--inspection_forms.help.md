# Surf Life Saving Qld – Sunshine Coast Branch Buhk Wilkes Inspectors Working Sheet 2021 2022 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to record details of patrols conducted by surf lifesavers on the Sunshine Coast, including observations and data relevant to the safety and effectiveness of lifesaving services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patrol Inspection page with any relevant details observed during the patrol.
2. Enter the patrol area in the Patrol Area field.
3. Record the patrol duration in the Patrol Duration field.
4. Enter the patrol distance in the Patrol Distance field.
5. Provide any additional notes or observations in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol Inspection** (`patrol_inspection`, text, required): Enter any relevant details observed during the patrol, such as weather conditions, beach hazards, or other notable observations.
* **Patrol Area** (`patrol_area`, text, required): Enter the specific area patrolled, e.g. "Main Beach".
* **Patrol Duration** (`patrol_duration`, time, required): Record the length of time spent on patrol in hours and minutes, e.g. "02:30".
* **Patrol Distance** (`patrol_distance`, number, required): Enter the total distance patrolled, e.g. "5 km".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or observations that may be relevant to the patrol, e.g. "Notable weather conditions: strong winds".
