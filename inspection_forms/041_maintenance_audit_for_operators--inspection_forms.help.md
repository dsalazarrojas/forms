# Maintenance Audit For Operators - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and report on maintenance activities for operators. It helps in tracking the status and history of maintenance, identifying potential issues, and scheduling next maintenance actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the relevant fields. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. For the "Operators Name" field, enter the name of the operator responsible for the maintenance activity.
3. For the "Equipment Id" field, enter the ID number of the equipment being maintained.
4. For the "Equipment Model" field, enter the model number or description of the equipment.
5. For the "Manufacturers Name" field, enter the name of the manufacturer of the equipment.
6. For the "Year Of Manufacture" field, enter the year the equipment was manufactured.
7. For the "Last Inspection Date" field, enter the date of the last maintenance inspection.
8. For the "Next Inspection Date" field, select the date for the next scheduled inspection.
9. For the "Inspection Period" field, choose the interval between inspections (e.g., Every 3 months, Every 6 months, Every 12 months).
10. For the "Inspection Status" field, select the current status of the maintenance activity (Not Started, In Progress, Done).
11. For the "Comments" field, add any additional comments or notes related to the maintenance activity.
