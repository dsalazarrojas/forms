# legionella_site_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legionella Site Audit Form is designed to help inspectors collect and document relevant information during site visits to assess potential Legionella contamination sites.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the form section by section, providing accurate and concise answers to each question.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed, marked as they are typically indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with date/time options, use the standard date and time format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Site Audit Form** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Provide a brief description of the site, including its location and any notable features.

*   This field helps the inspector to get an initial understanding of the site's context and layout, which may influence the inspection process.

### **Contact Information** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Enter the contact information for the site's representative or point of contact.

*   This field is for recording the site's contact person's details to facilitate communication and follow-up.

### **Water System Characteristics** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Describe the water system's type (e.g., cooling tower, hot and cold), flow rates, and other relevant characteristics.

*   This field is intended to gather information on the site's water system's specifics to help the inspector understand its operation and potential risks.

### **Sampling Plan** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Outline the sampling plan and procedures followed at the site, including the sampling locations and frequencies.

*   This field is designed for documenting the site's current sampling protocols to compare and assess their adequacy.

### **Inspection Results** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Provide the results of your inspection, including observations and any notable findings.

*   This field is intended for recording the results of your site visit, noting any areas of concern or compliance issues.

### **Corrective Action** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Describe any corrective actions taken or recommended for the site's water system.

*   This field is for documenting any steps taken to address any observed issues or risks.

### **Comments** (`<name>`, text, not required)
Include any additional comments or notes about the site visit, such as weather conditions or unusual occurrences.

*   This field is for providing additional context or notes that might be relevant to the site visit.

### **Date Visited** (`<name>`, date, not required)
Enter the date of the site visit.

*   This field records the date of the site visit for reference and historical tracking.

### **Time Visited** (`<name>`, time, not required)
Enter the time of the site visit.

*   This field records the time of the site visit for reference and historical tracking.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before proceeding with the site visit.
- Use the form as a guide, adapting it to suit your specific inspection needs and protocols.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect changing site conditions, regulations, or best practices.
