# Food Safety Record Keeping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps food safety inspectors collect and document critical information about food safety checks. It guides them in assessing food items, recording results, and tracking follow-up activities to maintain a clean and safe food handling environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all the items checked during the food safety inspection on Page 1.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection on Page 2.
3. Record the name of the shift leader and supervisor, if applicable.
4. Select the results of each item checked on Page 3 and 4.
5. Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection on Page 3 and 4.
6. If necessary, schedule a follow-up date and time on Page 5, and select the follow-up status.
7. Confirm the supervisor's signature, date, and time on Page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Safety Record** (`Page 1`, `select_multiple`): Select all the items checked during the food safety inspection. This may include equipment, storage areas, and other critical areas of concern.
* **Inspection Date** (`Page 2`, `date`): Enter the date of the food safety inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`Page 2`, `time`): Enter the time of the food safety inspection.
* **Shift Leader Name** (`Page 2`, `text`): Record the name of the shift leader who conducted the inspection.
* **Supervisor Name** (`Page 2`, `text`, **optional**): Record the name of the supervisor who was present during the inspection. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Item Checked** (`Page 3`, `select_multiple`): Select the item checked during the inspection.
* **Item Result** (`Page 3`, `select_one`): Select the result of the item checked (e.g., passed, failed, etc.).
* **Notes** (`Page 3`, `note`): Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Page 5`, `date`, **optional**): Schedule a follow-up date for any items that need further attention.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Page 5`, `time`, **optional**): Schedule a follow-up time for any items that need further attention.
* **Follow-up Status** (`Page 5`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the follow-up status (e.g., scheduled, pending, etc.).
* **Supervisor Signature** (`Page 6`, `text`): Confirm the supervisor's signature to indicate their agreement on the inspection results.
* **Supervisor Date** (`Page 6`, `date`): Enter the date of the supervisor's signature.
* **Supervisor Time** (`Page 6`, `time`): Enter the time of the supervisor's signature.
* **Item Checked 1** to **Item Checked 4** (`Page 7-10`, `select_multiple`): Select the items checked during the inspection (repeated for multiple pages).
* **Item Result 1** to **Item Result 4** (`Page 7-10`, `select_one`): Select the result of each item checked (repeated for multiple pages).
* **Notes 1** to **Notes 3** (`Page 7-10`, `note`): Enter any additional notes regarding the inspection (repeated for multiple pages).
