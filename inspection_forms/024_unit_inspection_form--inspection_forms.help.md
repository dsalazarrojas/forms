# Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Unit Inspection Form is used to collect information about unit inspections, allowing you to track the status of rentals and interactions between renter and landlord. This form helps you to ensure a smooth renting process and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the unit inspection date and status.
2. Provide details about the unit inspection, including conditions and any issues found.
3. Document any communication with the renter.
4. Enter any photos taken during the inspection.
5. Enter any relevant details about the renter, such as their name and contact information.
6. Confirm the move-in or move-out status of the unit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Inspection** (`unit_inspection_form`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter a brief description of the unit inspection, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Enter the date the unit was inspected for move-in.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Enter the date the unit was inspected for move-out.
* **Move In Condition** (`move_in_condition`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe the condition of the unit upon move-in, including any existing damages or issues.
* **Move Out Condition** (`move_out_condition`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe the condition of the unit upon move-out.
* **Move In Notes** (`move_in_notes`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter any notes or comments about the move-in inspection.
* **Move Out Notes** (`move_out_notes`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter any notes or comments about the move-out inspection.
* **Damages** (`damages`, `text`, **required: false**): Document any damages or existing issues with the unit.
* **Move In Photos** (`move_in_photos`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload photos taken during the move-in inspection.
* **Move Out Photos** (`move_out_photos`, `text`, **required: false**): Upload photos taken during the move-out inspection.
* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the renter's name.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the property address.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, `email`, **required: false**): Enter the landlord's email address.
* **Move In Status** (`move_in_status`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Select from the options: Moved In, No Move-In, or Unknown.
* **Move Out Status** (`move_out_status`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Select from the options: Moved Out, No Move-Out, or Unknown.

## Tips
- Always ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly, especially when documenting damages or issues.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Upload photos to support your documentation and provide evidence of the inspection.
- Communicate clearly with the renter and landlord to ensure a smooth renting process.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate records and compliance.
