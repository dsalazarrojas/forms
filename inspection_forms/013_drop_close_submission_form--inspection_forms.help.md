## How To Complete This Form

### How to complete this form

1. Review the form and understand the required fields.
2. Fill out the form according to your needs and circumstances.
3. Enter the submission date and time on the "submission_date" and "submission_time" fields.
4. Provide the facility name on the "facility_name" field.
5. Enter the reason for closure in the "close_reason" field.
6. Enter details about the closure in the "closure_details" field.
7. Select the facility type from the options provided on the "facility_type" field.
8. If applicable, select the facility area from the options provided on the "facility_area" field.
9. Confirm the involvement of operations and logistics teams by selecting "Yes" or "No" on the "operations_team" and "logistics_team" fields.
10. Indicate whether supporting documents are attached or not on the "supporting_documents" field.
11. Enter any comments or additional information in the "comments" field.
12. Sign the form with your name in the "signature" field.
13. Enter your name and email address in the "submitter" and "submitted_by_email" fields.
14. Enter the date and time of submission on the "submitted_by" and "submitted_time" fields.
15. Confirm the submission with your name on the "submitted_by_name" field.
16. Finally, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### submission_date
 * **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the submission event.
### submission_time
 * **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the submission event.
### facility_name
 * **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility that was closed.
### close_reason
 * **Close Reason** (`close_reason`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the facility closure.
### closure_details
 * **Closure Details** (`closure_details`, `text`, required): Enter detailed information about the closure event.
### facility_type
 * **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of facility that was closed.
### facility_area
 * **Facility Area** (`facility_area`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the area of the facility that was closed.
### operations_team
 * **Operations Team** (`operations_team`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm whether the operations team was involved in the closure event by selecting "Yes" or "No". You can also select "Other" if it's not applicable.
### logistics_team
 * **Logistics Team** (`logistics_team`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm whether the logistics team was involved in the closure event by selecting "Yes" or "No". You can also select "Other" if it's not applicable.
### supporting_documents
 * **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether supporting documents were attached to the submission.
### comments
 * **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or additional information about the closure event.
### signature
 * **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with your name.
### submitter
 * **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
### submitted_by
 * **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
### submitted_time
 * **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
### submitted_by_name
 * **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
### submitted_at_time
 * **Submitted At Time** (`submitted_at_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
### submitted_at_date
 * **Submitted At Date** (`submitted_at_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
### submitted_by_email
 * **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
### comments_2
 * **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
### submitter_2
 * **Submitter 2** (`submitter_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your name (if different from "Submitter").
### submitted_by_2
 * **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your name (if different from "Submitted By Name").
