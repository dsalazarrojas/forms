# Power Station Efficiency Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Station Efficiency Inspection Form is used to gather data about the power station's efficiency. This form is intended for power station staff to report their efficiency metrics, such as power consumption, efficiency rating, and power factor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Power Plant ID" field with the unique identifier of the power station being inspected.
2. Select the "Inspection Date" field to choose the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the "Inspector ID" field with the ID of the inspector performing the inspection.
4. In the "Power Prophets" field, enter any additional notes about the power station's prophets.
5. Choose the "Efficiency Review" field to select the efficiency rating of the power station.
6. In the "Findings" field, enter any notes or issues found during the inspection.
7. In the "Comments" field, enter any additional comments about the inspection.
8. Fill out the "Submitted By" field with the person who submitted the report.
9. In the "Prophets Count" field, enter the number of prophets found during the inspection.
10. Enter the "Power Consumption" field with the power consumption of the power station.
11. Enter the "Efficiency Rating" field with the efficiency rating of the power station.
12. Enter the "Efficiency Score" field with the efficiency score of the power station.
13. Enter the "Power Factor" field with the power factor of the power station.
14. In the "Power Factor Unit" field, enter the unit of the power factor (e.g., kWh).
15. In the "Power Factor Value" field, enter the value of the power factor.
16. In the "Power Factor Rating" field, choose the rating of the power factor (e.g., Good, Fair).
17. Enter the "Power Factor Rating Score" field with the rating score of the power factor.
18. In the "Notes" field, enter any additional notes about the inspection.
19. In the "Power Factor Notes" field, enter any notes about the power factor.
20. Fill out the "Submitted By Who" field with the person who submitted the report.
21. In the "Submitted By Email" field, enter the email of the person who submitted the report.
22. In the "Submitted By Date" field, select the date the report was submitted.
23. In the "Submitted By Time" field, select the time the report was submitted.
24. In the "Submitted By User Agent" field, enter any additional information about the user who submitted the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Power Plant ID** (`power_plant_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier of the power station being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **Power Prophets** (`power_prophets`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the power station's prophets.
* **Efficiency Review** (`efficiency_review`, select_one, optional): Choose the efficiency rating of the power station.
* **Findings** (`findings`, text, optional): Enter any notes or issues found during the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the report.
* **Prophets Count** (`prophets_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of prophets found during the inspection.
* **Power Consumption** (`power_consumption`, number, optional): Enter the power consumption of the power station.
* **Efficiency Rating** (`efficiency_rating`, number, optional): Enter the efficiency rating of the power station.
* **Efficiency Score** (`efficiency_score`, number, optional): Enter the efficiency score of the power station.
* **Power Factor** (`power_factor`, number, optional): Enter the power factor of the power station.
* **Power Factor Unit** (`power_factor_unit`, text, optional): Enter the unit of the power factor (e.g., kWh).
* **Power Factor Value** (`power_factor_value`, text, optional): Enter the value of the power factor.
* **Power Factor Rating** (`power_factor_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose the rating of the power factor (e.g., Good, Fair).
* **Power Factor Rating Score** (`power_factor_rating_score`, number, optional): Enter the rating score of the power factor.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Power Factor Notes** (`power_factor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the power factor.
* **Submitted By Who** (`submitted_by_who`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Date** (`submitted_by_date`, date, optional): Select the date the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By Time** (`submitted_by_time`, time, optional): Select the time the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By User Agent** (`submitted_by_user_agent`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the user who submitted the report.
