# Temperature Control Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and document the results of a temperature control audit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Temperature Control Audit Request Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Date of Inspection**: Record the date of the inspection.
* **Site Location**: Specify the location of the site inspected.
* **Temperature Range**: Enter the range of temperatures observed during the inspection.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID or name of the equipment.
* **Equipment Status**: Choose the status of the equipment (in good working condition, not in good working condition, or in need of maintenance).
* **Maintenance Notes**: Provide any additional maintenance notes.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name as the inspector.
* **Signature Date**: Record the date of the signature.
