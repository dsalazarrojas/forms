# municipal_urban_development_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The municipal urban development audit form is designed to collect information about urban development projects. It is intended for municipal officials and stakeholders to assess the progress and status of projects in urban areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Project Info" section with a brief description of the project.
2. Provide the location of the project in the "Project Location" field.
3. Enter the address of the project in the "Project Address" field.
4. In the "Inspection Info" field, enter any relevant details about the inspection.
5. Specify the current status of the project in the "Project Status" field.
6. If necessary, provide additional follow-up information in the "Follow-up Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the project, including its purpose and scope.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the city, state, or province where the project is located.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the exact address of the project, including the street address and postal code.
* **Inspection Info** (`inspection_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the inspection, such as the inspection date or the name of the inspector.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, optional):
	+ Choose the current status of the project from "Not Started", "In Progress", "On Hold", "Completed", or "Cancelled".
	+ If the project is completed, please specify the date of completion.
* **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information that may be needed for follow-up purposes, such as contact numbers or emails for further discussion.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure accurate data collection.
- If you need to provide any supporting documents or files, you can attach them separately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
