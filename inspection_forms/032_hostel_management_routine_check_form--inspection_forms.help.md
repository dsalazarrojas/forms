# Hostel Management Routine Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data on the daily management and upkeep of the hostel. It helps the management team to monitor and address any issues promptly, and to keep track of ongoing tasks and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and submit date in the corresponding fields.
2. Report on the status of facilities, cleanliness, and maintenance by entering a number from 0 to 5, where 0 represents "poor" and 5 represents "excellent".
3. Enter the number of staff present on duty, and select any staff members who were not present.
4. List any issues found, such as broken fixtures or appliances, overcrowding, or other issues.
5. Enter any comments or notes about the hostel's condition.
6. Enter the time of submission.
7. Enter the date of submission.
8. Enter the name of the person assigned to review and address the issues found.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (id: 1, type: text): Enter the general information about the hostel's condition at the time of submission.
* **Facilities** (id: 2, type: number): Rate the facilities from 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Cleanliness** (id: 3, type: number): Rate the cleanliness from 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Maintenance** (id: 4, type: number): Rate the maintenance from 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Shopping Routine** (id: 5, type: number): Rate the shopping routine from 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Staff** (id: 6, type: select_multiple): List the staff members present, including Manager, Supervisor, and Maintenance Team.
* **Issues Found** (id: 7, type: select_multiple): Select any issues found, such as Broken Fixtures, Broken Appliances, Overcrowding, or Other.
* **Comments** (id: 8, type: note): Enter any comments or notes about the hostel's condition.
* **Notes** (id: 9, type: note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Date** (id: 10, type: date): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time** (id: 11, type: time): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (id: 12, type: text): Enter your name.
* **Submitted To** (id: 13, type: text): Enter the name of the person to whom the form is submitted.
* **Submitted Date** (id: 14, type: date): Enter the date of submission.
* **Assigned To** (id: 15, type: text): Enter the name of the person assigned to review and address the issues found.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your reporting to ensure effective management and upkeep of the hostel.
* Complete this form at the end of each shift or on a daily basis, as required.
* Ensure that you submit the form to the correct person, as indicated in the "Submitted To" field.
