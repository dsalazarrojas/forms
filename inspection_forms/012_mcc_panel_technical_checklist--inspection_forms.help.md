# MCC PANEL TECHNICAL CHECKLIST - Help Guide

## Purpose
The MCC Panel Technical Checklist form is designed to record the results of a technical inspection of a Motor Control Center (MCC) panel. This form should be used to document the findings and observations of the technician performing the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the MCC panel and ensure it is in good working condition.
2. Complete the "main_circuit" field, selecting "Yes" if the panel's main circuit is functional and "No" otherwise.
3. Enter the panel's rating in the "panel_rating" field.
4. For each section of the panel, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the alarm system is functional or not.
5. Record the results of the inspection in the "panel_inspection_result" field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the "notes" field.
7. Record the technician's ID, name, and signature.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes from the technician in the "technician_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Panel Technical Checklist**: This is the title of the form.
* **main_circuit**: This field is used to indicate whether the panel's main circuit is functional or not. Select "Yes" if the panel's main circuit is working correctly, and "No" otherwise.
* **panel_rating**: This field is used to record the panel's rating.
* **alarm_system**: This field is used to select whether the alarm system is functional or not for each section of the panel.
* **Panel Inspection Result**: This field is used to record the results of the inspection.
* **Notes**: This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Technician ID**: This field is used to record the technician's ID.
* **Technician Name**: This field is used to record the technician's name.
* **Technician Signature**: This field is used to record the technician's signature.
* **Technician Comments**: This field is used to record any additional comments or notes from the technician.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If any sections of the panel have issues, indicate this in the corresponding fields.
* Take clear and legible photos or videos of any issues or areas of concern.
* Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "notes" field.
