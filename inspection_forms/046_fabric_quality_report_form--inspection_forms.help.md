# Fabric Quality Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help inspectors report on the quality of fabric samples. It is used to gather information on the fabric's physical and chemical properties, as well as its appearance and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the fabric sample carefully and observe its physical and chemical properties.
* Answer each question accurately and completely, referring to the fabric sample for guidance if necessary.
* Ensure that each question is answered only once, and that answers are consistent throughout the form.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, refer to the relevant section of the fabric sample or consult with a relevant expert.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Info** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide the name and contact information of the supplier of the fabric sample.

* **Inspection Results** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Summarize your findings from the inspection, including any notable observations or concerns.
* **Fabric Condition** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate if the fabric is in a good condition or if there are any notable defects.
* **Fabric Weight** (`6`, `number`, required: `false`): Report the weight of the fabric.
