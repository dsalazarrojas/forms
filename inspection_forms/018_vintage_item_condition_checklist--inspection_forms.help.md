# Vintage Item Condition Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vintage Item Condition Checklist" form is designed to collect detailed information about vintage items, including their condition, provenance, and other relevant attributes. This form helps you assess the item's value, authenticity, and overall condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information about the vintage item, including its title, condition, and provenance.
2. Provide a description of the item, noting any distinctive features or characteristics.
3. Specify the item's value, weight, and dimensions (width, length, height, and depth).
4. Choose the correct unit of measurement for each attribute (e.g., inches, meters, pounds).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the item's condition, provenance, or price.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vintage_item_info**: A brief text field for entering the vintage item's title or description.
* **vintage_item_condition**: A select-one field with options for assessing the item's condition (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_provenance**: A text field for providing information about the item's provenance (e.g., its origin, history, or ownership).
* **vintage_item_description**: A text field for describing the item in detail, noting any distinctive features or characteristics.
* **vintage_item_value**: A numerical field for entering the item's value.
* **vintage_item_price**: A numerical field for entering the item's price.
* **condition_scale_2**: A select-multiple field with options for further assessing the item's condition (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_weight**: A numerical field for entering the item's weight.
* **vintage_item_width**: A text field for entering the item's width.
* **vintage_item_length**: A text field for entering the item's length.
* **vintage_item_height**: A text field for entering the item's height.
* **vintage_item_depth**: A text field for entering the item's depth.
* **vintage_item_width_cm**: A text field for entering the item's width in centimeters (cm).
* **vintage_item_length_cm**: A text field for entering the item's length in centimeters (cm).
* **vintage_item_height_cm**: A text field for entering the item's height in centimeters (cm).
* **vintage_item_depth_cm**: A text field for entering the item's depth in centimeters (cm).
* **vintage_item_weight_unit**: A select-one field for choosing the unit of measurement for the item's weight (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_value_unit**: A select-one field for choosing the unit of measurement for the item's value (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_price_currency**: A select-one field for choosing the currency of the item's price (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_weight_currency**: A select-one field for choosing the currency of the item's weight (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **vintage_item_description_notes**: A text area for adding any additional comments or notes about the item's description.
* **vintage_item_condition_scale_notes**: A text area for adding any additional comments or notes about the item's condition scale.
* **vintage_item_provenance_notes**: A text area for adding any additional comments or notes about the item's provenance.
* **vintage_item_price_notes**: A text area for adding any additional comments or notes about the item's price.
* **vintage_item_status_notes**: A text field for entering any additional information about the item's status or condition.

Note: The "Note" type fields are for adding free-form comments or additional information about the item. You can use these fields to provide any additional context or details that might be helpful in assessing the item's value, provenance, or condition.
