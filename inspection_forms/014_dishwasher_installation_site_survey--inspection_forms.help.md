# Dishwasher Installation Site Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dishwasher Installation Site Survey form is a tool used to collect information and feedback from users after they have inspected a site for a potential dishwasher installation. This form is intended to be completed by authorized users, such as technicians and site administrators, to provide a comprehensive overview of the site conditions and any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input** field with your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about your site visit, including the date and time you visited the site.
3. Select the **Site Readiness** option that best describes the site's readiness for installation.
4. Add any additional notes about the site visit.
5. Enter the customer's email address and phone number.
6. Provide notes about the site visit.
7. Enter details about the appliance being installed.
8. Enter any comments from the customer.
9. Enter any comments from the technician.
10. Select the options that describe the site's access.
11. Enter any installation requirements.
12. Select the status of the installation site survey form completion.
13. Select the status of the installation.
14. Select the status of the site visit completion.
15. Enter any completion issues related to the site visit.
16. Enter completion comments for the site visit.
17. Enter completion comments for the technician visit.
18. Enter a rating for the site survey completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (id: 1, required: true): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Site Visit Details** (id: 2, required: false): Provide details about your site visit, including the date and time you visited the site.
* **Site Survey Date** (id: 3, required: false): Enter the date of your site visit.
* **Site Survey Time** (id: 4, required: false): Enter the time of your site visit.
* **Technician Visit Details** (id: 5, required: false): Provide details about your visit as a technician.
* **Site Readiness** (id: 6, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the option that best describes the site's readiness for installation.
* **Additional Notes** (id: 7, required: false): Add any additional notes about your site visit.
* **Customer Email** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (id: 9, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Site Visit Notes** (id: 10, required: false): Provide notes about your site visit.
* **Appliance Details** (id: 11, required: false): Enter details about the appliance being installed.
* **Customer Comments** (id: 12, required: false): Enter any comments from the customer.
* **Technician Comments** (id: 13, required: false): Enter any comments from the technician.
* **Site Access** (id: 14, select_multiple, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the options that describe the site's access.
* **Installation Requirements** (id: 15, required: false): Enter any installation requirements.
* **Site Survey Form Completion Status** (id: 16, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the status of the installation site survey form completion.
* **Installation Status** (id: 17, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the status of the installation.
* **Site Visit Completion Status** (id: 18, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the status of the site visit completion.
* **Site Visit Completion Comments** (id: 19, required: false): Enter completion comments for the site visit.
* **Site Visit Completion Issues** (id: 20, required: false): Enter any completion issues related to the site visit.
* **Installation Site Survey Form Submission Status** (id: 21, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the status of the installation site survey form submission.
* **Technician Visit Completion Status** (id: 22, select_one, options: [Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Select the status of the technician visit completion.
* **Technician Visit Completion Comments** (id: 23, required: false): Enter completion comments for the technician visit.
* **Site Survey Completion Rating** (id: 24, required: false): Enter a rating for the site survey completion.
* **Customer Signature** (id: 25, required: false): Enter the customer's signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are authorized to access this form.
* Complete the form as thoroughly as possible.
* Select the options that best describe the site conditions.
* Enter any relevant comments or notes for the site visit.
