# Food Safety Inspection Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and release food safety inspection records, providing relevant information to food safety authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the `business name` of the inspected business.
2. Enter the `inspector name` and `inspector title` who conducted the inspection.
3. Enter the `inspection date` of the inspection.
4. Select the `inspection result` from the provided options (e.g., "Pass" or "Fail").
5. Enter any additional `notes` regarding the inspection.
6. Enter the `email` and `phone number` of the contact person.
7. Enter the `business address` of the inspected business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): This field captures the name of the business being inspected.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Title** (`inspector_title`, text, optional): Enter the title or position of the inspector.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, select_one, optional): Select the outcome of the inspection from the provided options (e.g., "Pass" or "Fail").
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the inspection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the business or contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business or contact person.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the inspected business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for food safety at the business.
