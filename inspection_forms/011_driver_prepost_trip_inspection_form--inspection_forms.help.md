# Driver PrePost Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Driver PrePost Trip Inspection Form is used to document the pre- and post-trip conditions of a vehicle, including various aspects such as vehicle condition, fuel level, fuel type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's pre-trip condition by filling in the fields related to the vehicle's condition, fuel level, and other relevant details.
2. Complete the fields related to the vehicle's post-trip condition after the trip has been completed.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Condition**: Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any issues or concerns.
* **Engine Condition**: Describe the condition of the engine, including any issues or concerns.
* **Transmission Condition**: Describe the condition of the transmission, including any issues or concerns.
* **Brakes Condition**: Describe the condition of the brakes, including any issues or concerns.
* **Steering Condition**: Describe the condition of the steering, including any issues or concerns.
* **Tire Condition**: Describe the condition of the tires, including any issues or concerns.
* **Suspension Condition**: Describe the condition of the suspension, including any issues or concerns.
* **Electrical System Condition**: Describe the condition of the electrical system, including any issues or concerns.
* **Body Condition**: Describe the condition of the body, including any issues or concerns.
* **Cargo Condition**: Describe the condition of the cargo, including any issues or concerns.
* **Fuel Level**: Select the current fuel level of the vehicle, either "Empty" or "Full".
* **Fuel Type**: Describe the type of fuel used in the vehicle.
* **Odometer Reading**: Record the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **Fuel Consumption**: Describe the fuel consumption of the vehicle during the trip.
* **Vehicle Weight**: Record the weight of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Length**: Record the length of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Height**: Record the height of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Width**: Record the width of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Record the year the vehicle was made.
* **Vehicle Make**: Record the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Record the model of the vehicle.
* **Engine Type**: Record the type of engine used in the vehicle.
* **Engine Year**: Record the year the engine was made.
* **Engine Condition**: Describe the condition of the engine, including any issues or concerns.
* **Assigned To**: Record the person or department assigned to the vehicle for maintenance.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all relevant information is included.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the fleet manager or maintenance team for guidance.
