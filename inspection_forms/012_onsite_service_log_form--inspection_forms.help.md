# onsite_service_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the details of onsite service provided by a company's engineers to its clinics. It helps in tracking and analyzing the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Onsite Service Log` page with the relevant details.
2. Enter the name of the engineer who provided the service.
3. Enter the name of the clinic where the service was provided.
4. Fill in the `Assigned To` field with the responsible person or team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onsite Service Log** (Page 1, Text)
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter a brief description or log of the onsite service provided.
* **Engineer** (Page 2, Text)
	+ Enter the name of the engineer who provided the service.
* **Clinic** (Page 2, Text)
	+ Enter the name of the clinic where the service was provided.
* **Assigned To** (Page 3, Text)
	+ Fill in the name of the person or team responsible for managing the service provided.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all the relevant details accurately and consistently.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling in the form, contact form@gic.mx for support.
