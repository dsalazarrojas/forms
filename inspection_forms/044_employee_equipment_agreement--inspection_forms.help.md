# Employee Equipment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Equipment Agreement form is used to report on the status of equipment assigned to employees. This form helps the team to stay up-to-date on the equipment's condition and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of equipment assigned to you.
2. Check if the equipment is still available or if it has been replaced.
3. Select the correct status for each piece of equipment.
4. Enter the date the equipment was assigned to you.
5. Enter the time the equipment was assigned to you.
6. Make sure to submit the form with the correct information to avoid any discrepancies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment List** (Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to select all the equipment assigned to you that you want to report on. You can choose from the list of available options.
* **Equipment Details** (Select One, Optional): This field is used to report the details of each selected equipment. Choose the correct option from the list provided.
* **Date Assigned** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the equipment was assigned to you. This helps us track when the equipment was first assigned.
* **Time Assigned** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the equipment was assigned to you. This helps us track when the equipment was assigned to you during the day.
* **Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the correct status of the equipment (Active or Inactive) to keep the team informed of its availability.
