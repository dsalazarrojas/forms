# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information related to <string>, including facility location, safety inspector, inspection date, reported hazards, severity of hazards, actions taken, maintenance status, comments, robot number, and robot name. This form will be used to document and report on the inspection findings and provide information for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Facility Location** field with the location where the inspection was performed.
2. Enter the **Safety Inspector** name in the corresponding field.
3. Select the **Inspection Date** using the date field.
4. For each reported **Hazard**, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the **Hazard Severity** level based on the severity of the hazard.
6. Indicate the **Action Taken** for each hazard, choosing one or multiple options from the list.
7. Choose the **Maintenance Status** of the facility, equipment, or system being inspected.
8. Enter any additional **Comments** about the inspection in the text field.
9. If applicable, enter the **Robot Number** and **Robot Name** for the robot being inspected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Location**: Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
* **Safety Inspector**: Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date when the inspection was performed.
* **Hazard Reported**: Select from the dropdown menu the hazard that was reported during the inspection.
* **Hazard Severity**: Select the level of severity of the hazard from the dropdown menu.
* **Action Taken**: Choose one or multiple options from the list to indicate the action taken for each hazard.
* **Maintenance Status**: Choose one or multiple options from the list to indicate the maintenance status of the facility, equipment, or system being inspected.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection in the text field.
* **Robot Number**: Enter the number of the robot being inspected.
* **Robot Name**: Enter the name of the robot being inspected.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by inspection team members after completing an inspection, and the data collected will be used for future reference and reporting.
