# House Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Checklist Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about a house's status, systems, and components. It is used to assess the condition of each house in a neighborhood and ensure that it is filled out only once.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each house in the neighborhood only once.
2. Answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
3. Use the provided options for unsure answers.
4. Ensure that each section is completed completely and accurately.
5. Be aware of other people's input and agree on the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **House Status (1)** (`house_status`, note, optional): This section is used to provide a brief description of the house's status, such as vacant, occupied, or under construction.
* **Systems And Engines (2)** (`systems_and_engines`, select_multiple, optional): This section is used to select the systems and engines currently in the house, such as heating, cooling, and electrical systems.
* **Service Requirements (3)** (`service_requirements`, select_one, optional): This section is used to select the service requirements of the house, such as lawn care and maintenance.
* **Exteriors Information (4)** (`exteriors_info`, text, optional): This section is used to provide information about the house's exteriors, such as the condition of the roof, walls, and windows.
* **Security Systems (5)** (`security_systems`, select_multiple, optional): This section is used to select the security systems in the house, such as alarm systems and surveillance cameras.
* **Electrical Systems (6)** (`electrical_systems`, select_one, optional): This section is used to select the electrical systems in the house, such as the type of wiring and electrical panels.
