## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Fill out the name of the project.
* **Job ID / Permit** (`job_id`, text, required): Fill out the job ID or permit number.
* **Site Supervisor** (`supervisor`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the site supervisor.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter today's date.
* **Work Area Description** (`work_area`, text, required): Describe the work area, including whether it is confined or open.
* **Potential Air Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, required): Check all potential air hazards that apply.
* **Ventilation Plan** (`ventilation_plan`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate ventilation plan for the work area.
* **PPE Requirements** (`ppe_reqs`, select_one, required): Choose the necessary PPE requirements for the workers.
* **Tester Name** (`tester_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who performed the test.
* **Certification Statement** (`cert_statement`, select_one, required): Select "I Certify" to confirm that the air quality is safe for work.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your full name as the site supervisor.
