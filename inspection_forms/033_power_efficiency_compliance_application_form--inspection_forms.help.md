# power_efficiency_compliance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "power_efficiency_compliance_application_form" is designed to collect information about a facility's power efficiency compliance status, including facility details, energy efficiency details, supporting documents, and other relevant information. This form is intended for facilities that need to demonstrate their commitment to power efficiency and compliance with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Facility Details section, providing information about your facility's address, contact, and type (if applicable).
2. Enter your facility's energy efficiency details, including any relevant statistics or certifications.
3. Upload supporting documents as necessary, such as energy efficiency reports or certifications.
4. Provide information about your facility's compliance programs, including any relevant training or education programs.
5. Review and select relevant programs for your facility from the Review Programs section.
6. Enter any comments or additional information about your facility's compliance status.
7. Provide information about your facility's manager, including their name and contact information.
8. Select the facility type that best describes your facility.
9. Enter your facility's building square footage and total electricity consumption.
10. Enter your facility's energy efficiency score, if available.
11. Upload any necessary documents to support your facility's compliance status.
12. Upload more documents as necessary, if any.
13. Enter the date of compliance for your facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details**: This section collects basic facility information, including address and contact details. This field is required for all facilities.
* **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's address in the format: "Street Address, City, State, Zip".
* **Facility Contact**: Enter your facility's contact information, including name and email.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility you have, such as "Commercial" or "Industrial".
* **Energy Efficiency Details**: Enter any relevant energy efficiency statistics or certifications.
* **Supporting Documents**: Upload supporting documents, such as energy efficiency reports or certifications.
* **Compliance Programs**: List any relevant compliance programs or training programs.
* **Review Programs**: Select the programs that your facility has participated in or is applicable for.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about your facility's compliance status.
* **Facility Manager Info**: Enter the name and contact information of your facility manager.
* **Building SqFt**: Enter the square footage of your facility.
* **Total Electricity Consumption**: Enter your facility's total electricity consumption.
* **Energy Efficiency Score**: Enter your facility's energy efficiency score, if available.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Upload documents to support your facility's compliance status.
* **Upload More Documents**: Select more documents to upload as necessary.
* **Date of Compliance**: Enter the date of compliance for your facility.

Note: The fields with a red asterisk (*) are required fields.
