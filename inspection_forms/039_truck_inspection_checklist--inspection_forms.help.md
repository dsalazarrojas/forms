## Purpose
The Truck Inspection Checklist form is designed to help users inspect and evaluate the condition of a truck. This form is used to assess the vehicle's key components, such as tires, brakes, lights, mirrors, fluids, fuel, and other mechanical components, to ensure they are in good working order. The form is used for maintenance, repair, and inspection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, start by filling out the **Vehicle Details** page (Page 1). This section requires no specific information, so you can proceed to the next page.
2.  Next, move on to the **Tires** page (Page 2). Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's tires: **Perfect**, **Worn**, **Damaged**, or **Missing**.
3.  Then, inspect the **Brakes** page (Page 3). Select the condition of your truck's brakes: **Perfect**, **Worn**, **Damaged**, or **Missing**.
4.  After that, move on to the **Lights** page (Page 4). Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's lights: **Perfect**, **Worn**, **Damaged**, or **Missing**.
5.  Inspect the **Mirrors** page (Page 5). Select the condition of your truck's mirrors: **Perfect**, **Worn**, **Damaged**, or **Missing**.
6.  Next, assess the **Fluids** page (Page 6). Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's fluids: **Perfect**, **Low**, **High**, or **Missing**.
7.  Then, evaluate the **Fuel** page (Page 7). Select the condition of your truck's fuel: **Perfect**, **Low**, or **High**.
8.  After that, move on to the **Other Mechanical Components** page (Page 8). This page is for any other mechanical components that you want to add.
9.  Next, add any **Additional Comments** on Page 9.
10.  Finally, fill out the **Date**, **Time**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your contact information on Page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Details (Page 1)** (`vehicle_details`, text, not required): A brief description of the vehicle being inspected.
*   **Tires (Page 2)** (`tires`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's tires.
	+ Perfect: The tires are in excellent condition.
	+ Worn: The tires are worn out, but still functional.
	+ Damaged: The tires are damaged, but can be repaired.
	+ Missing: The tire is missing.
*   **Brakes (Page 3)** (`brakes`, select_one, not required): Select the condition of your truck's brakes.
	+ Perfect: The brakes are in excellent condition.
	+ Worn: The brakes are worn out, but still functional.
	+ Damaged: The brakes are damaged, but can be repaired.
	+ Missing: The brake is missing.
*   **Lights (Page 4)** (`lights`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's lights.
	+ Perfect: The lights are in excellent condition.
	+ Worn: The lights are worn out, but still functional.
	+ Damaged: The lights are damaged, but can be repaired.
	+ Missing: The light is missing.
*   **Mirrors (Page 5)** (`mirrors`, select_one, not required): Select the condition of your truck's mirrors.
	+ Perfect: The mirrors are in excellent condition.
	+ Worn: The mirrors are worn out, but still functional.
	+ Damaged: The mirrors are damaged, but can be repaired.
	+ Missing: The mirror is missing.
*   **Fluids (Page 6)** (`fluids`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the conditions that apply to your truck's fluids.
	+ Perfect: The fluids are in excellent condition.
	+ Low: The fluids are low or running low.
	+ High: The fluids are high or overfilled.
	+ Missing: The fluid is missing.
*   **Fuel (Page 7)** (`fuel`, select_one, not required): Select the condition of your truck's fuel.
	+ Perfect: The fuel is in excellent condition.
	+ Low: The fuel is low or running low.
	+ High: The fuel is high or overfilled.
	+ Missing: The fuel is missing.
*   **Other Mechanical Components (Page 8)** (`other_mechanical_components`, text, not required): Any other mechanical components that need to be inspected.
*   **Additional Comments (Page 9)** (`additional_comments`, note, not required): Any additional comments about the vehicle.
*   **Date (Page 10)** (`date`, date, not required): The date of the inspection.
*   **Time (Page 10)** (`time`, time, not required): The time of the inspection.
*   **Email (Page 10)** (`email`, email, not required): The contact email of the person performing the inspection.
*   **Phone (Page 10)** (`phone`, text, not required): The contact phone number of the person performing the inspection.
*   **Assignee (Page 10)** (`assignee`, select_one, not required): Select who performed the inspection.
	+ Inspector: The inspection was performed by an inspector.
	+ Vehicle Expert: The inspection was performed by a vehicle expert.
	+ Vehicle Repairmen: The inspection was performed by a vehicle repairman.
	+ Vehicle Seller: The inspection was performed by a vehicle seller.
