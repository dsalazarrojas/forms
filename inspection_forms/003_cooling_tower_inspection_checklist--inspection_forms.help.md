# Cooling Tower Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document the results of a cooling tower inspection. It will help maintain the tower's condition, identify potential issues, and ensure proper functioning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Tower ID: Enter the unique identifier of the cooling tower being inspected.
	* Inspection Date: Record the date of the inspection.
	* Technician Name: Enter the name and ID of the technician performing the inspection.
2. Provide additional details:
	* Notes: Add any additional comments or observations related to the inspection.
	* Temperature Range: If applicable, record the temperature range of the cooling tower.
	* Cooling Water Level: If applicable, record the level of the cooling water.
3. Check the temperature readings:
	* Motor Temperature: Record the temperature reading of the motor.
	* Bearing Temperature: Record the temperature reading of the bearing.
4. Assess the condition of critical components:
	* Motor Bearing Condition: Evaluate the condition of the motor bearing.
	* Fan Bearing Condition: Evaluate the condition of the fan bearing.
	* Motor Seal Condition: Evaluate the condition of the motor seal.
	* Fan Seal Condition: Evaluate the condition of the fan seal.
	* Sump Condition: Evaluate the condition of the sump.
	* Basin Condition: Evaluate the condition of the basin.
5. Record the height of critical components:
	* Sump Height: Record the height of the sump.
	* Motor Drain Height: Record the height of the motor drain.
6. Check the water level in the sump:
	* Sump Water Level: Record the water level in the sump.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **tower_id** (`tower_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier of the cooling tower being inspected.
- **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Record the date of the inspection.
- **technician_name** (`technician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name and ID of the technician performing the inspection.
- **notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations related to the inspection.
- **temperature_range** (`temperature_range`, `number`, optional): If applicable, record the temperature range of the cooling tower.
- **cooling_water_level** (`cooling_water_level`, `select multiple`, optional): If applicable, record the level of the cooling water.
- **motor_temperature** (`motor_temperature`, `select one`, required): Record the temperature reading of the motor.
- **bearing_temperature** (`bearing_temperature`, `select one`, optional): Record the temperature reading of the bearing.
- **motor_bearing_condition** (`motor_bearing_condition`, `select one`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the motor bearing.
- **fan_bearing_condition** (`fan_bearing_condition`, `select one`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the fan bearing.
- **motor_seal_condition** (`motor_seal_condition`, `select one`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the motor seal.
- **fan_seal_condition** (`fan_seal_condition`, `select one`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the fan seal.
- **sump_condition** (`sump_condition`, `select multiple`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the sump.
- **basin_condition** (`basin_condition`, `select one`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the basin.
- **sump_height** (`sump_height`, `select one`, required): Record the height of the sump.
- **motor_drain_height** (`motor_drain_height`, `select one`, required): Record the height of the motor drain.
- **sump_water_level** (`sump_water_level`, `select multiple`, optional): Record the water level in the sump.

## Tips
* Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the tower's optimal functioning.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Record any issues or concerns that require immediate attention.
* Keep a record of all inspections for future reference and to track any changes or improvements.
