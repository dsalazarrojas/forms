# Field Visit Documentation Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from individuals before a field visit, ensuring that they agree to have documentation, photos, videos, or other documents taken.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
* Choose your consent for photo, video, and other document taking.
* Ensure all consent fields are selected correctly to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Signature (Note, Optional)**: This field is not required but allows you to provide a physical or digital signature if needed.
* **Photo Consent (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have photos taken during the visit.
* **Video Consent (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have videos taken during the visit.
* **Notes Consent (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have notes taken during the visit.
* **Photos Consent (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have photos taken during the visit.
* **Videos Consent (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have videos taken during the visit.
* **Other Documents (Select One, Required)**: Choose 'True' if you agree to have other documents taken during the visit.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand what consent is required before selecting any of the consent fields.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the consent fields.
* Ask the field personnel for clarification if you have any doubts or questions about the form.
