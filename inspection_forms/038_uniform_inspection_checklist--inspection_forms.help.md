# Uniform Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Inspection Checklist form is a tool used to evaluate the condition and proper wear of a uniform. It ensures that uniforms are worn correctly and professionally, maintaining high standards of appearance and discipline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions for each page according to the instructions for that page.
2. Fill in the correct answer for each question, using the provided options for select fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is the uniform in good condition** (Page 1): Check the uniform for any damage, stains, or other issues that affect its appearance.
* **Proper fit** (Page 2): Evaluate the fit of the uniform, including whether it is loose or tight in any areas.
* **Is insignia properly in order?** (Page 3, Select Multiple): Check that the insignia is correctly aligned and follows the proper protocol for wear on your specific branch (Army, Marine Corps, Air Force).
* **Is the ribbon properly in order?** (Page 4, Select Multiple): Check that the ribbon is correctly aligned and follows the proper protocol for wear on your specific location (Center, Left, Right).
* **Inspection area** (Page 5, Select One): Choose the area of the uniform being inspected (Front, Back, Sleeve, Collar, Insignia).
* **Is the patch properly in order?** (Page 6, Select Multiple): Check that the patch is correctly aligned and follows the proper protocol for wear on your specific location (Front, Center, Rear).
* **Is the belt properly in order?** (Page 7, Select Multiple): Check that the belt is correctly aligned and follows the proper protocol for wear on your specific location (Front, Center, Back).
* **Inspection score** (Page 8): Enter a score from 1-5, indicating the overall condition of the uniform.
* **Inspector Name** (Page 9): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Rank** (Page 10): Enter the rank of the inspector.
* **Inspector ID** (Page 11): Enter the ID of the inspector.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Use the provided options for select fields to ensure accuracy.
* If unsure about any aspect of the uniform, consult with a superior or reference material.
