# Cleaning Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive assessment of a property's cleanliness and condition, identify any issues or concerns, and determine the necessary cleaning services required to address these concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Start by entering the client's name and property address on the first page.
2. Select the date of inspection on the second page.
3. Proceed to fill out the remaining fields, evaluating the condition of the property as you go along.
4. For each field, select the most accurate description of the current condition.
5. Be thorough and provide any supporting comments or observations.
6. Once you've completed all fields, review and submit the form for final review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the client or property owner.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, Text, Required): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, Date, Required): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the inspector.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of property being inspected (residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`square_footage`, Select One, Required): Choose the approximate size of the property in square footage.
* **Overall Cleanliness Level** (`overall_cleanliness`, Select One, Required): Rate the overall cleanliness level of the property (excellent, good, fair, poor, or extremely poor).
* **Floor Condition** (`floor_condition`, Select One, Required): Rate the condition of the floors (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Wall and Ceiling Condition** (`wall_condition`, Select One, Required): Rate the condition of the walls and ceilings (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Window and Glass Condition** (`window_condition`, Select One, Required): Rate the condition of the windows and glass surfaces (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Bathroom Cleanliness** (`bathroom_condition`, Select One, Required): Rate the cleanliness of the bathrooms (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Kitchen Cleanliness** (`kitchen_condition`, Select One, Required): Rate the cleanliness of the kitchen (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
* **Unpleasant Odors Present** (`odor_present`, Select One, Required): Indicate if there are any noticeable odors present (no odors, mild odors, moderate odors, strong odors, or severe odors).
* **Evidence of Pests** (`pest_evidence`, Select One, Required): Indicate if there is any evidence of pests present (none observed, possible signs, clear evidence, or active infestation).
* **Mold or Mildew Presence** (`mold_mildew`, Select One, Required): Rate the presence of mold or mildew (none observed, minor spots, moderate growth, or extensive growth).
* **Property Damage** (`damage_observed`, Text, Required): Describe any property damage or concerns.
* **Safety Hazards Identified** (`safety_hazards`, Text, Required): List any safety concerns or hazards identified during the inspection.
* **Cleaning Priority Level** (`cleaning_priority`, Select One, Required): Determine the priority level of the cleaning services required (routine, elevated, high, or urgent).
* **Areas Requiring Special Attention** (`special_attention_areas`, Text, Required): List any areas of the property that require special attention.
* **Recommended Cleaning Services** (`recommended_services`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all cleaning services recommended for the property.
* **Special Equipment Needed** (`special_equipment_needed`, Text, Optional): List any special equipment required for cleaning.
* **Access Restrictions or Notes** (`access_restrictions`, Text, Optional): Enter any access restrictions or special instructions for the cleaning services.
* **Estimated Cleaning Time Required** (`estimated_cleaning_time`, Select One, Required): Estimate the time required for cleaning (1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, 6-8 hours, or full day or more).
* **Recommended Crew Size** (`crew_size_recommended`, Select One, Required): Choose the recommended crew size (1 cleaner, 2 cleaners, 3-4 cleaners, or 5 or more cleaners).
* **Inspector Recommendations** (`inspector_recommendations`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional recommendations or comments.
* **Follow-Up Inspection Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, Select One, Required): Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required (yes, no, or periodic inspections).
* **Inspector Signature (Type Full Name)** (`inspector_signature`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the inspector.
