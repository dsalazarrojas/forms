# Water Damage Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Water Damage Assessment Form is used to capture detailed information about water damage incidents. This form helps in documenting and assessing the situation, enabling swift and effective resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Incident Date** (Step 1) to record the date when the water damage incident occurred.
2. Enter the **Incident Time** (Step 2) to specify the time when the incident took place.
3. Provide the **Incident Location** (Step 3) where the incident happened.
4. Select the **Cause of Incident** (Step 4) from the given options to specify the reason for the water damage.
5. Describe the **Damage Description** (Step 5) in detail, selecting multiple options as applicable.
6. Indicate the **Damage Severity** (Step 6) of the damage from the given options.
7. Choose the **Incident Reporter** (Step 7) from the available options.
8. Select the **Incident Status** (Step 8) from the options provided.
9. Optionally, you can provide an **Incident Status Update** (Step 9) if necessary.
10. Attach an **Incident Image** (Step 10) if it's relevant to the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Enter the date when the water damage incident occurred. This is a required field for accurate documentation.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time): Enter the time when the incident took place. This field is also required for precise timing.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text): Provide the specific location where the incident happened. This field is crucial for identifying the affected areas.
* **Cause of Incident** (`cause_of_incident`, select_one): Select one of the provided options to specify the reason for the water damage. This helps in identifying root causes.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, select_multiple): Select multiple options to describe the damage in detail. This field is useful for capturing comprehensive information.
* **Damage Severity** (`damage_severity`, select_one): Choose the severity of the damage from the options provided. This helps in assessing the extent of damage.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, select_one): Select the person or entity that reported the incident. This field is essential for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one): Select the status of the incident from the given options. This helps in tracking the progress and resolution of the incident.
* **Incident Status Update** (`incident_status_update`, note): Provide an optional update on the incident status if needed.
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`, text): Attach an image related to the incident if it's relevant to the case.
