# Site Visit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Site Visit Form is designed to collect contact information and site inspection data during a site visit.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Contact Information
Enter the name, email, and phone number of the visitor in the contact fields.

1. **Name**: Enter the full name of the person visiting the site.
2. **Email**: Enter the email address of the person visiting the site.
3. **Phone (Mobile or Landline)**: Enter the phone number of the person visiting the site.

### Step 2: Select Inspection Data
Select the relevant inspection data for the site visit.

1. **Checklist 1 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more options from the list to confirm or reject specific site inspections.
2. **Checkboxes 1-5 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more options from the list to confirm or reject specific site inspections.
3. **Checkboxes 6-10 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more options from the list to confirm or reject specific site inspections.
4. **Checkboxes 11-15 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more options from the list to confirm or reject specific site inspections.
5. **Checkboxes 16-20 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more options from the list to confirm or reject specific site inspections.

### Step 3: Enter Site Visit Details
Enter the site visit details in the text fields.

1. **Site Visit**: Enter the site visit description in the text field.
2. **Site Inspection 6**: Enter the site inspection 6 details in the text field.
3. **Site Inspection 7**: Enter the site inspection 7 details in the text field.
4. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the site visit in the text field.
5. **Additional Information 2**: Enter any additional information about the site visit in the text field.

### Step 4: Review and Submit
Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: Enter the full name of the person visiting the site.
* **Name (2) (Confirmation)**: Confirm the name entered in the previous field.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the email address of the person visiting the site.
* **Email (4) (Confirmation)**: Confirm the email entered in the previous field.
* **Phone (5) (Mobile or Landline)**: Enter the phone number of the person visiting the site.
* **Site Visit (6)**: Enter the site visit description in the text field.
* **Site Inspection 1 (7)**: Enter the site inspection 1 details in the text field.
* **Site Inspection 2 (8)**: Enter the site inspection 2 details in the text field.

* **Site Inspection 5 (12)**: Enter the site inspection 5 details in the text field.

## Tips

* Double-check the selected inspection data for accuracy.
