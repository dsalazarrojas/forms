# Spare Wheel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the spare wheel of a vehicle, including its type, condition, pressure, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of spare wheel.
2. Select the condition of the spare wheel.
3. Enter the pressure of the spare wheel in PSI.
4. Enter the tire tread depth of the spare wheel in mm.
5. Enter the year of the vehicle model.
6. (Optional) Enter the vehicle registration number.
7. Select the color of the vehicle.
8. Select the type of tire.
9. Select the type of spare wheel.
10. Select the condition of the spare wheel.
11. Enter the inspection date.
12. Enter the inspector's name.
13. Sign the inspection.
14. (Optional) Enter any additional notes.
15. (Optional) Enter the vehicle model.
16. (Optional) Enter the vehicle year.
17. Select the condition of the spare wheel's rim.
18. Select the status of the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What type of spare wheel is this?** (`wheel_type`, select_one): Select the type of spare wheel from the options provided.
  - Pneumatic
  - Semi Pneumatic
  - Non Pneumatic

- **What is the condition of this spare wheel?** (`wheel_condition`, select_multiple): Select the condition of the spare wheel from the options provided.
  - New
  - Used
  - Worn

- **What is the wheel pressure?** (`wheel_pressure`, number): Enter the pressure of the spare wheel in PSI.

- **What is the tire tread depth?** (`tire_tread_depth`, number): Enter the tire tread depth of the spare wheel in mm.

- **What is the vehicle model year?** (`vehicle_model_year`, number): Enter the year of the vehicle model.

- **What is the vehicle registration number?** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text): Enter the vehicle registration number. * (Not required)

- **What is the vehicle color?** (`vehicle_color`, select_one): Select the color of the vehicle from the options provided.
  - Red
  - Blue
  - Green

- **What type of tire is this?** (`tire_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of tire from the options provided.
  - Pneumatic
  - Semi Pneumatic
  - Non Pneumatic

- **What type of spare wheel is this?** (`spare_wheel_type`, select_one): Select the type of spare wheel from the options provided.
  - Pneumatic
  - Semi Pneumatic
  - Non Pneumatic

- **What is the condition of this spare wheel?** (`spare_wheel_condition`, select_multiple): Select the condition of the spare wheel from the options provided.
  - New
  - Used
  - Worn

- **What is the inspection date?** (`inspection_date`, date): Enter the inspection date.

- **What is the inspector's name?** (`inspector_name`, text): Enter the inspector's name. * (Required)

- **Inspector's signature** (`inspector_signature`, text): Sign the inspection. * (Required)

- **Additional notes?** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes. * (Not required)

- **What is the vehicle model?** (`vehicle_model`, text): Enter the vehicle model. * (Not required)

- **What is the vehicle year?** (`vehicle_year`, number): Enter the vehicle year. * (Not required)

- **What is the condition of this spare wheel's rim?** (`spare_wheel_rim_condition`, select_multiple): Select the condition of the spare wheel's rim from the options provided.
  - New
  - Used
  - Worn

- **What is the vehicle status?** (`vehicle_status`, select_one): Select the status of the vehicle from the options provided.
  - New
  - Used
  - Worn
