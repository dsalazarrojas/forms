# Spare Wheel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the spare wheel of a vehicle, including its type, condition, pressure, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of spare wheel.
2. Select the condition of the spare wheel.
3. Enter the pressure of the spare wheel in PSI.
4. Enter the tire tread depth of the spare wheel in mm.
5. Enter the year of the vehicle model.
6. (Optional) Enter the vehicle registration number.
7. Select the color of the vehicle.
8. Select the type of tire.
9. Select the type of spare wheel.
10. Select the condition of the spare wheel.
11. Enter the inspection date.
12. Enter the inspector's name.
13. Sign the inspection.
14. (Optional) Enter any additional notes.
15. (Optional) Enter the vehicle model.
16. (Optional) Enter the vehicle year.
17. Select the condition of the spare wheel's rim.
18. Select the status of the vehicle.
