## Care Facility Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Care Facility Evaluation Form is designed to provide a structured evaluation of a care facility's performance. This form assesses various aspects of the facility, including cleanliness, staff professionalism, safety and security, resident comfort and dignity, and other essential areas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a thorough evaluation.
2. Enter the facility's name in the "Facility name" field, which is required.
3. Provide your name in the "Evaluator name" field, which is also required.
4. Record the date of the inspection in the "Date of inspection" field.
5. Evaluate the facility's cleanliness, staff professionalism, safety and security, and resident comfort and dignity using the provided rating options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility name** (`Facility name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility being evaluated.
* **Evaluator name** (`Evaluator name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person conducting the assessment.
* **Date of inspection** (`Date of inspection`, `date`, required): Record the date you visited the facility.
* **Cleanliness rating** (`Cleanliness rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the facility's overall cleanliness:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Staff professionalism** (`Staff professionalism`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the interaction quality and responsiveness of the staff:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Safety and security** (`Safety and security`, `select_one`, required): Observe and evaluate the safety measures and protocols:
	+ Meets standards
	+ Needs improvement
	+ Does not meet standards
* **Resident comfort and dignity** (`Resident comfort and dignity`, `select_one`, required): Assess the living conditions and privacy:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any necessary follow-up or improvements.
* **Follow up required** (`Follow up required`, `select_one`, required): Flag for follow-up inspection:
	+ True
	+ False
