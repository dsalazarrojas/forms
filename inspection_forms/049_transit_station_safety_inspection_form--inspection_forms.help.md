# Transit Station Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a comprehensive safety inspection of a transit station, ensuring that it meets the necessary standards and protocols for safe operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the station's safety guidelines and protocols before beginning the inspection.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary equipment and authorization to complete the inspection.
3. Complete the form for each station, using the following fields:
	+ **Station ID**: Enter the station's unique identification number (if applicable).
	+ **Inspection Date**: Record the date of the inspection.
	+ **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information (if required).
	+ **Facility Name**: Enter the station's name and location.
	+ **Safety Issue**: Describe any safety issues or concerns found during the inspection.
	+ **Station Location**: Describe the station's location, including any notable features or hazards.
	+ **Issue Description**: Describe any issues or concerns related to the station's safety.
	+ **Photos**: Attach photos of any safety issues or concerns.
	+ **Signature**: Record your signature and date (if required).
	+ **Station Status**: Select the current status of the station (Active or Inactive).
	+ **Safety Checks**: Answer "Yes" or "No" (or N/A) for each safety check, as appropriate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Station ID**: Enter the station's unique identification number, if applicable.
* **Inspection Date**: Record the date of the inspection, using the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information (if required).
* **Facility Name**: Enter the station's name and location.
* **Safety Issue**: Describe any safety issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* **Station Location**: Describe the station's location, including any notable features or hazards.
* **Issue Description**: Describe any issues or concerns related to the station's safety.
* **Photos**: Attach photos of any safety issues or concerns.
* **Signature**: Record your signature and date (if required).
* **Station Status**: Select the current status of the station (Active or Inactive).
* **Safety Check 1-15**: Answer "Yes", "No", or "N/A" for each safety check, as appropriate. These questions assess the station's safety features and practices, such as emergency equipment, signage, and personnel training.
