# Roofing Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roofing Inspection Form is a comprehensive tool used to inspect and document the condition and status of roofing systems for buildings. It covers various aspects of the roofing system, including its materials, structure, and compliance with safety and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions being asked.
2. Select the correct options for each section based on your findings during the inspection.
3. Enter your answers for each field, and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Roofing Inspection (id: 1)
 * **Roofing Inspection** (`Roofing Inspection`, `note`, `optional`): This is an overview of the inspection area, where you can take note of any notable findings or observations.

### Exterior (id: 2)
 * **Exterior** (`Exterior`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Answer whether or not the exterior of the roof is inspectable.
 * **Roofing Materials** (`Roofing Materials`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select whether or not the roofing material is present.
 * **Exterior Inspect Check List** (`Exterior Inspect Check List`, `select_one`, `optional`): Checklist of exterior inspection items.
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'

### Interior (id: 3)
 * **Interior** (`Interior`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Answer whether or not the interior of the roof is inspectable.
 * **Roof Deck** (`Roof Deck`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select whether or not the roof deck is present.

### Structural (id: 4)
 * **Structural** (`Structural`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Answer whether or not the structure of the roof is inspectable.

### Permits (id: 5)

### Safety (id: 6)
