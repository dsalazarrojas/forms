# Range Boundaries Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Range Boundaries Assessment Form is a tool used to collect and document information about range boundaries. This form will help you gather data on the properties and boundaries of the area being assessed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Property Id (ID of the property or area being assessed).
2. Choose the type of boundary (Range, Boundary, or Other).
3. Provide a brief description of the boundary (optional).
4. Enter the length of the boundary (in meters).
5. Select the direction of the boundary (East, West, North, or South).
6. Indicate the status of the boundary (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
7. Add any additional notes about the boundary (optional).
8. Enter the date and time when the boundary was observed (optional).
9. Enter the latitude and longitude of the boundary (in decimal degrees).
10. Enter the elevation of the boundary (in meters).
11. Enter the User Id (ID of the user who observed the boundary).
12. Enter the User Name (name of the user who observed the boundary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Id** (1): The ID of the property or area being assessed.
* **boundary_type** (2): Select the type of boundary (Range, Boundary, or Other).
* **boundary_description** (3): Provide a brief description of the boundary (optional).
* **boundary_length** (4): Enter the length of the boundary in meters.
* **boundary_location** (5): Select the direction of the boundary (East, West, North, or South).
* **boundary_status** (6): Indicate the status of the boundary (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **boundary_notes** (7): Add any additional notes about the boundary (optional).
* **boundary_observation** (8): Enter any observations or comments about the boundary (optional).
* **date_observed** (9): Enter the date when the boundary was observed (optional).
* **time_observed** (10): Enter the time when the boundary was observed (optional).
* **latitude** (11): Enter the latitude of the boundary in decimal degrees.
* **longitude** (12): Enter the longitude of the boundary in decimal degrees.
* **elevation** (13): Enter the elevation of the boundary in meters.
* **user_id** (14): Enter the ID of the user who observed the boundary.
* **user_name** (15): Enter the name of the user who observed the boundary.

Tips:
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure the correctness of the assessment.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant authorities or experts in the field.
* This form is designed to be filled out by one person at a time, so please be thorough and accurate in your responses.
