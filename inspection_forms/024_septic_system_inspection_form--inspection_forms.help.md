# septic_system_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The <inspector_form> is designed to collect information on the septic system of a property. This includes general information about the property and septic system, as well as specific details on the system's components and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page to provide the necessary information. Please enter your responses accurately and thoroughly, as this information is used to inform further actions and decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (id: 2, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspector Info** (id: 3, required): Enter your name and contact information for the inspector.
* **Septic System Info** (id: 4, optional): Provide a brief description of the septic system, including any relevant details about its type and location.
* **Septic System Components** (id: 5, required): Select all components that are part of the septic system, including:
	+ Septic Tank
	+ Distribution Line
	+ Pump
	+ Filter
* **Component Condition** (id: 6, required): Select the condition of each selected component:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Inspection Results** (id: 7, optional): Enter any notable findings or observations from the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (id: 8, required): Enter your signature to confirm completion of the form.
* **Notes** (id: 9, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Email** (id: 10, optional): Enter your contact email for further communication.
* **Phone** (id: 11, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Inspector License** (id: 12, optional): If you are a licensed inspector, enter your license number for verification.
* **Inspector Notes** (id: 13, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspector.
* **Property Address** (id: 14, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Property Owner** (id: 15, required): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Owner Contact Info** (id: 16, optional): Enter the contact information for the property owner.
* **System Type** (id: 17, optional): Enter the type of septic system (e.g. conventional, mound, etc.).
* **Well and Drainfield Info** (id: 18, optional): Enter any relevant information about the well and drainfield.
* **Well and Drainfield Inspection** (id: 19, optional): Enter any notable findings or observations from the inspection of the well and drainfield.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the property owner or relevant authorities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for further assistance.
