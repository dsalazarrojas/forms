# Lyft Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lyft Inspection Form is used to inspect and evaluate the condition of a vehicle for potential use in the Lyft platform. The form is used to collect information about the vehicle, including its year, make, model, and overall condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lyft Inspection Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle year, make, and model information.
2. Select the options on the Safety Checklist that apply to the vehicle's condition.
3. Add any additional notes regarding the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Year
**Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required: false)

* Enter the year of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Make
**Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required: false)

* Enter the make of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Model
**Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required: false)

* Enter the model of the vehicle.

### Safety Checklist
**Safety Checklist** (`safety_checklist`, `select_one`, required: true)

* Select all options that apply to the vehicle's condition:
	+ Is there any evidence of wear and tear on the vehicle?
	+ Is the vehicle in good condition?
	+ Is the vehicle clean?
	+ Is the vehicle well-maintained?

### Additional Notes
**Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, required: true)

* Please describe any additional notes regarding the vehicle's condition. This is a free-text field where you can add any relevant information.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields, including the Safety Checklist and Additional Notes.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the Additional Notes field.
* If a vehicle is not in good condition, please clearly explain why it is not suitable for use on the Lyft platform.
