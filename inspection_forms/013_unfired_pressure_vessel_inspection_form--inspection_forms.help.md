# Unfired Pressure Vessel Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the inspection of unfired pressure vessels. It helps ensure the vessel's structural integrity and safety before it's fired or operated under pressure. The form gathers information about the vessel, its inspection, and completion details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as necessary based on the actual conditions of the pressure vessel being inspected.
2. Complete the **vessel_details** field with any relevant information about the vessel.
3. In the **safety_inspection** field, report any safety concerns or issues found during the inspection.
4. Add any additional notes about the inspection in the **technician_notes** field.
5. If applicable, sign and date the **supervisor_signature** and **inspectors_signature** fields to indicate approval or completion of the inspection.
6. Complete the **date_completed** field with the date the inspection was finished.
7. For each question in the **category**, **form_category**, and **form_subcategory** fields, choose either "Yes" or "No" based on whether the condition applies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vessel_details** (Text): Enter any relevant details about the pressure vessel being inspected.
* **safety_inspection** (Text): Report any safety concerns or issues found during the inspection.
* **technician_notes** (Text): Add any notes about the inspection that the technician would like to document.
* **supervisor_signature** (Date): Sign and date this field to indicate approval or completion of the inspection.
* **inspectors_signature** (Date): Sign and date this field to indicate completion of the inspection.
* **date_completed** (Date): Complete this field with the date the inspection was finished.
* **category** (Select Multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether the condition applies.
* **form_category** (Select Multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether the condition applies.
* **form_subcategory** (Select Multiple): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether the condition applies.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before finalizing the form.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as "No" to ensure accurate reporting.
* Review and revise the form before submitting it for approval.
* Keep a record of completed forms for future reference and inspection records.
