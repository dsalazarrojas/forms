# Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Equipment Inspection Request Form is used to report issues or concerns with equipment used by workers. This form helps ensure that equipment is inspected regularly, reducing downtime and preventing accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the equipment details in the "Equipment Details" field.
2. Select the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Choose the type of inspection required by selecting one of the options in the "Type" field.
4. Provide any supporting photos related to the inspection in the "Supporting Photos" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Details (Required/Optional)**: Enter a brief description of the equipment being inspected, including its location and any relevant details about the issue.
* **Inspection Date (Required/Optional)**: Enter the date on which the inspection is to be performed.
* **Type (Required/Optional)**: Select one of the following inspection types:
	+ General: A routine inspection to ensure all systems are functioning properly.
	+ Safety: An inspection to check for any safety-related issues.
	+ Maintenance: A check for wear and tear, or necessary maintenance.
* **Supporting Photos (Required/Optional)**: Upload any photos that can help support your inspection request. These should clearly show the issue or concern with the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when describing the issue with the equipment.
* If you have any supporting photos, make sure they are clear and well-lit to help with diagnosis.
* If you are unsure about which type of inspection to select, choose "General" to err on the side of caution.
