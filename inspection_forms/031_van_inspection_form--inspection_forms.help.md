# Van Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Inspection Form is a comprehensive document used to evaluate and report on the condition of a van. It is used to identify any damage or issues with the vehicle's exterior, safety equipment, tires, brakes, suspension systems, and other critical components.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you provide accurate information.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your observation of the van's condition.
3. Complete all required fields, marked as such.
4. If a field is not applicable, please enter "N/A" or a brief explanation.
5. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **van_inspection_form** (Text, Optional): Enter a description or title of the van inspection, if desired.
* **exterior_condition** (Text, Optional): Describe the van's exterior condition, including any notable scratches, dents, or other external damage.
* **body_damage** (Text, Optional): Report any body damage or issues with the van's body.
* **safety_equipment** (Text, Optional): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the van's safety equipment, such as seatbelts, airbags, and mirrors.
* **tires** (Text, Optional): Assess the tread depth, condition, and air pressure of the tires.
* **brakes** (Text, Optional): Check the condition and functionality of the brakes, including wear and tear.
* **suspension_systems** (Text, Optional): Inspect the van's suspension systems, including shock absorbers and springs.
* **steering_systems** (Text, Optional): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the steering systems, including the steering column and tie-rod ends.
* **engine** (Text, Optional): Report any issues or concerns with the engine, including oil leaks, performance, and unusual noises.
* **electrical_systems** (Text, Optional): Check the condition and functionality of the van's electrical systems, including the battery and starter motor.
* **transmission** (Text, Optional): Inspect the condition and functionality of the transmission.
* **brakes_warranty** (Text, Optional): Provide information on the status of the brake warranty or maintenance schedule.
* **tires_warranty** (Text, Optional): Report on the status of the tire warranty or maintenance schedule.
* **vehicle_identification** (Text, Optional): Provide information about the vehicle's make, model, year, and vehicle identification number (VIN).
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the van inspection.
* **assigned_user** (Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the user who performed the inspection.
* **assigned_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the inspection was performed.
* **inspection_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the inspection was completed.
* **vehicle_year** (Number, Optional): Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **vehicle_make** (Select, Optional): Select the vehicle's make (e.g., Dodge, Ford, Toyota, etc.).
* **vehicle_model** (Text, Optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **inspection_score** (Number, Optional): Enter a score from 1 to 100, based on the inspection results.
* **inspection_status** (Select, Optional): Select the inspection status (e.g., Pass, Fail, Pass - Conditional, etc.).
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection results.
* **form_id** (Text, Optional): Enter a unique ID or reference number for the form.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and use it as a reference for future maintenance or repair decisions.
