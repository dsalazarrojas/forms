# Stormwater Drainage Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition and performance of stormwater drainage systems, enabling facilities to identify areas of concern and inform maintenance and repair activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **General Information** section, providing an overview of the inspection location and any relevant context.
2. Select the correct **Drainage System** from the list provided.
3. Enter the **Inspector's Information** in the next field, including their name and any other relevant details.
4. Record the **Date of Inspection** and **Time of Inspection** using the respective fields.
5. In the **Notes** field, enter any observations, concerns, or issues that you have noted during the inspection.
6. Indicate whether **Photos** are available and relevant to the inspection.
7. Check whether **Signatures** from relevant personnel or stakeholders are provided.
8. Identify whether **Attachments** such as videos, notes, or other supporting materials are relevant and available.
9. Describe the **System Condition** in detail, including any issues or concerns observed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter a brief description of the inspection location and any relevant context.
* **System Info (2)**: Select the correct drainage system from the list provided. This will help in identifying the specific system being inspected.
* **Inspector Info (3)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details, such as your role or contact information.
* **Date of Inspection (4)**: Enter the date on which the inspection was performed.
* **Time of Inspection (5)**: Enter the time when the inspection was conducted.
* **Notes (6)**: Enter any observations, concerns, or issues that you have noted during the inspection.
* **Photos (7)**: Indicate whether photos are available and relevant to the inspection. If photos are provided, they can be attached or referenced in the notes.
* **Signatures (8)**: Check whether signatures from relevant personnel or stakeholders are available and attached.
* **Attachments (9)**: Identify whether any relevant videos, notes, or other supporting materials are available.
* **System Condition (10)**: Describe the condition of the stormwater drainage system in detail, including any issues or concerns observed. Be specific about any defects, damage, or malfunctions noted.
