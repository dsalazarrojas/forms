# CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CPR Chest Compression Feedback Device Inspection Checklist is a tool used to inspect CPR chest compression feedback devices, ensuring they are in good working order and meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully, ensuring you answer every required question.
2. Start with the first page, "Inspection Details", where you'll provide your name and the date of the inspection.
3. Move on to the "Device Details" page, where you'll enter the device's serial number and select the correct model type.
4. Next, perform the physical checks on the "Physical Condition Checks" page, evaluating the casing integrity and battery compartment status.
5. Conduct the functional tests on the "Functional Tests" page, checking the power-on self-test, display clarity, compression depth accuracy, and rate feedback accuracy.
6. Add any maintenance or repair notes, if necessary.
7. Finally, select the overall inspection result, indicating whether the device is certified for use, needs repair, or should be decommissioned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Details (Page 1)**
	+ **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and title, so we can keep track of who performed the inspection.
	+ **Date of Inspection**: Record the date when this inspection took place.
* **Device Serial Number (Page 2)**
	+ Locate the serial number on the back or bottom of the device.
* **Device Model (Page 2)**
	+ Select the correct model type from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the device's capabilities and limitations.
* **Physical Condition Checks (Page 3)**
	+ **Casing Integrity**: Check the device's casing for any cracks or damage. Select "Pass" if it's in good condition, "Fail" if there's an issue, or "N/A" if you couldn't inspect it.
	+ **Battery Compartment Status**: Check the battery compartment for signs of corrosion or loose contacts. Select "Clean" if it's okay, "Needs Attention" if it requires maintenance, or "Critical Error" if it's unusable.
	+ **Battery Charge Level**: Check the battery level, aiming for at least 25% charge or more. Enter the current level as a number.
* **Functional Tests (Page 4)**
	+ **Power-On Self-Test Successful**: Check if the device's self-test is successful after power-on.
	+ **Display Visibility and Clarity**: Ensure all LEDs or screen elements are functional and easy to read.
	+ **Compression Depth Accuracy**: Use a calibration manikin to verify the compression depth accuracy.
	+ **Rate Feedback Accuracy**: Test the rate feedback accuracy using a verification method.
* **Overall Inspection Result (Page 5)**
	+ **Overall Inspection Result**: Select one of three options: "Certified for Use", "Quarantine - Needs Repair", or "Decommissioned" based on your findings.

## Tips
Remember to be thorough and accurate when completing this form, as the results will impact the device's certification and usage. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or maintenance personnel for guidance.
