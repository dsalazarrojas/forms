# Welding Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Welding Inspection Form is designed to document the results of a welding inspection, which is an essential process to ensure the quality and safety of welded products. This form helps inspectors and welders record and track important details about the production process, welder information, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Production Process** field, describing the specific production process that was conducted.
2. Enter the **Welder ID** number to identify the welder who performed the welding task.
3. Record the **Date Completed** and **Time Completed** to document when the inspection took place.
4. Evaluate the **Weld Quality** by selecting one or more of the options (Good, Poor).
5. Indicate if the inspection was conducted at the **Inspection Location** where the welding took place.
6. Sign the **Welder Signature** to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
7. Sign the **Inspector Signature** to confirm that the inspection was conducted.
8. Enter the **Inspector Name** to identify the person who conducted the inspection.
9. Provide the **Company Name** of the organization where the welding was performed.
10. Record the **Production Lot** to identify the specific production batch.
11. Enter the **Date of Inspection** to document when the inspection occurred.
12. Enter the **Shift ID** to identify the specific shift during which the inspection took place.
13. Record the **Time Completed (Hrs)** to document the time spent on the inspection.
14. Select the **Inspection Result** based on the outcome of the inspection (Yes, No).
15. Enter the **Welder Name** to identify the person who performed the welding task.
16. Record the **Production Line** to identify the specific production line where the welding took place.
17. Enter the **Production Code** to identify the specific production code associated with the welding task.
18. Enter the **Production Number** to identify the specific production number.
19. Record the **Production Date** to document when the production took place.
20. Enter the **Shift** to identify the specific shift during which the inspection took place.
21. Record the **Welder Location** to identify the specific location where the welding took place.
22. Add any additional **Notes** about the inspection.
23. Record any **Welder Notes** about the inspection.
24. Enter the **Inspector Comments** to provide any additional information about the inspection.
