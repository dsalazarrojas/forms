# Wastewater Treatment Plant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the inspection of a wastewater treatment plant. It gathers data on various aspects of the facility, including inspection dates and times, inspector and facility information, critical and non-critical areas observations, regulatory compliance, and signatures. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are documented during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.
* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are:
	+ Inspection Date (page 1)
	+ Inspector Name (page 1)
	+ Facility Name (page 1)
	+ Notes (page 2)
* Complete the optional fields as needed, such as inspector role, facility address, and facility signature.
* Use the select multiple options to choose multiple critical and non-critical areas to observe.
* Enter your signature and date in the Inspector Sign Date and Inspector Signature fields.
* Verify that the facility signature and facility name title are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (1, date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (2, time): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (3, text): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Role** (4, select_one): Select your role as the inspector (Administrator, Manager, or Engineer).
* **Facility Name** (5, text): Enter the name of the wastewater treatment plant.
* **Facility Address** (6, text): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Observation Critical Areas** (7, select_multiple): Select the critical areas to observe.
*   *Primary Clarifier*: Select if this area is critical.
*   *Aeration Blower*: Select if this area is critical.
*   *Mixing Tank*: Select if this area is critical.
* **Observation Critical Comments** (8, text): Enter any comments about critical areas.
* **Observation Non-Critical Areas** (9, select_multiple): Select the non-critical areas to observe.
*   *Floor and Wall Joints*: Select if this area is non-critical.
*   *Roof and Wall Joints*: Select if this area is non-critical.
* **Observation Non-Critical Comments** (10, text): Enter any comments about non-critical areas.
* **Regulatory Compliance** (11, select_one): Select if the facility is in regulatory compliance.
* **Notes** (12, note): Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Inspector Sign Date** (13, date): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Inspector Sign Time** (14, time): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Inspector Signature** (15, text): Enter your signature.
* **Facility Signature** (16, text): Enter the facility's signature.
* **Facility Name Title** (17, text): Enter the title of the facility's name.
