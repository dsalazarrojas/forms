# Owner Builder Inspection Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Owner Builder Inspection Report" form is a document used to record details about a building project's inspection. This form captures information such as dates, times, weather conditions, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the inspection date (page 3).
2. Enter the start and end times of the inspection (pages 4 and 5).
3. Describe the weather conditions at the time of inspection (page 6).
4. Provide your information as the inspector (page 7).
5. Enter the project site address (page 9).
6. Provide contact information for the project contact person (pages 10, 11, and 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (page 3): Enter the date of the inspection in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Start Time** (page 4): Enter the start time of the inspection.
* **End Time** (page 5): Enter the end time of the inspection.
* **Weather Conditions** (page 6): Describe the weather conditions at the time of inspection.
* **Inspector Info** (page 7): Enter your name, title, or any other relevant information as the inspector.
* **Inspector Signature** (page 8): Sign your name as the inspector, indicating that you have inspected the project.
* **Project Site Address** (page 9): Enter the address of the project site.
* **Contact Person** (page 10): Enter the name of the person you contacted for the project.
* **Contact Email** (page 11): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (page 12): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all necessary fields to ensure accurate and complete inspection data.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* Use proper date and time formats.
* Ensure that the contact person's email is valid.
* Consider printing a copy of the inspection report for your records.
