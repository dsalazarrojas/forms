# walkthrough_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The walkthrough_inspection_form is used to inspect tanks and evaluate their condition and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources needed for the inspection.
2. Complete each field in the form, providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Save the completed form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tank_id** (Number, Required: false): Enter the unique ID of the tank being inspected.
* **tank_name** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name of the tank being inspected.
* **tank_status** (Select One, Required: false):
	+ Select the current status of the tank from the options:
		- Good
		- Bad
		- Critical
* **inspection_date** (Date, Required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **tank_condition** (Text, Required: false): Describe the overall condition of the tank.
* **notes** (Text, Required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the tank.
* **tank_capacity** (Number, Required: false): Enter the capacity of the tank.
* **tank_height** (Number, Required: false): Enter the height of the tank.
* **tank_width** (Number, Required: false): Enter the width of the tank.
* **tank_depth** (Number, Required: false): Enter the depth of the tank.
* **tank_length** (Number, Required: false): Enter the length of the tank.
* **tank_comments** (Text, Required: false): Enter any additional comments about the tank.
* **tank_image** (Text, Required: false): Enter a URL or description of any images related to the tank.
* **tank_video** (Text, Required: false): Enter a URL or description of any videos related to the tank.
* **tank_audio** (Text, Required: false): Enter a URL or description of any audio files related to the tank.
* **tank_file** (Text, Required: false): Enter a URL or description of any files related to the tank.
* **tank_status_critical** (Select Multiple, Required: false):
	+ Select any critical status issues from the options:
		- None
		- Critical

**Tips:**

* Use the tank_status field to report the overall status of the tank.
* Use the tank_status_critical field to report any critical status issues with the tank.
* Use the tank_condition and tank_comments fields to provide additional comments or notes about the tank.
* Use the tank_image, tank_video, tank_audio, and tank_file fields to attach any relevant media to the tank.
