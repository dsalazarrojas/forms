# Power Pole Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Pole Inspection Form is a data collection tool designed to gather information about the condition and maintenance needs of power poles in a utility system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the location of the power pole being inspected in the "Location" field.
2.  Describe the condition of the power pole in the "Condition" field.
3.  Provide any additional comments or notes about the power pole or inspection in the "Notes" field.
4.  Select the name(s) of the technician(s) assigned to inspect the power pole in the "Assigned Technician" field.
5.  Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Location** (`location_1`, text, optional): Enter the location of the power pole being inspected, such as a street address or a description of the location.
*   **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the power pole from the list: Normal, Abnormal, or Critical.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the power pole or inspection.
*   **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select_multiple, optional): Select the name(s) of the technician(s) assigned to inspect the power pole.
*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Power Pole Inspection** (`power_pole_inspection_form_1`, text, optional): Describe the power pole being inspected.
