# SWPPP Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SWPPP (Site Wide Pollution Prevention Program) Inspection Form is a tool used to collect information about site inspections, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and standards. This form is crucial for assessing the overall condition of the site and guiding future inspection schedules. Please ensure that this form is completed accurately and consistently to maintain a record of site inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Site Name, Address, City, State, and Zip Code of the location being inspected.
2. Provide the Date of Last Inspection and the Last Inspection Result (Yes/No).
3. Choose the Frequency of Inspection (Yes/No) and enter the Last Inspection Date.
4. Enter any Notes related to the Last Inspection Result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section A - Introduction
* **Site Name** (`site_name`): Enter the name of the site being inspected.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`): Enter the address of the site being inspected.
* **Site City** (`site_city`): Enter the city where the site is located.
* **Site State** (`site_state`): Enter the state where the site is located.
* **Site Zip** (`site_zip`): Enter the zip code of the site location.
* **Site Country** (`site_country`): Enter the country where the site is located.

### Site Details
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`swppp_date_of_last_inspection`): Enter the date of the last inspection, if applicable.
* **Frequency of Inspection** (`swppp_frequency_of_inspection`): Choose whether the site requires regular inspections (Yes/No).
* **Last Inspection Date** (`swppp_last_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the last scheduled or actual inspection.
* **Last Inspection Result** (`swppp_last_inspection_result`): Choose the outcome of the last inspection (Yes/No).
* **Last Inspection Result Notes** (`swppp_last_inspection_result_notes`): Enter any additional notes regarding the last inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled accurately and consistently.
- Review and update this form regularly to reflect site changes and inspection schedules.
- Store this form securely and confidentially to maintain site integrity.
