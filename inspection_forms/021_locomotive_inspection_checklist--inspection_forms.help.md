# Locomotive Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Locomotive Inspection Checklist is a comprehensive inspection form designed to help ensure the safe and efficient operation of locomotives. This guide will walk you through each field on the form and provide explanations to help you complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information and resources available.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, using the explanations below as a guide.
3. If you are unsure about any field or option, please refer to your training or contact your supervisor for guidance.
