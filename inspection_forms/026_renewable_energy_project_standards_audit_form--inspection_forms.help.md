# Renewable Energy Project Standards Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Project Standards Audit Form is a standardized tool for collecting project information and tracking project status, site inspections, and audit results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the current **project status** from the options provided.
2. Enter the **project location** where the project is located.
3. Enter the **project start** and **project finish dates**.
4. Fill in the **project owner** and **project contact** information.
5. Optionally, add any additional **project notes**.
6. As a reviewer, fill out the **reviewer comments**, and sign and provide your **reviewer name**, **reviewer title**, and **reviewer signature**.
7. Confirm the **site inspection date**, **site inspection time**, and **site inspection location**.
8. Enter the **site inspection notes**.
9. Select the **site inspection status** as Completed or Not Completed.
10. Enter the **site inspection reviewer**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_status**: Select the current status of the project from the dropdown menu (In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
* **project_location**: Enter the specific location of the project.
* **project_date**: Enter the date the project started.
* **project_start_date**: Enter the date the project started.
* **project_finish_date**: Enter the date the project finished.
* **project_owner**: Enter the name of the project owner.
* **project_contact**: Enter the contact information for the project.
* **project_notes**: Add any additional notes about the project (optional).
* **reviewer_comments**: Add any comments from the reviewer (optional).
* **reviewer_signature**: Sign as the reviewer to confirm the audit.
* **reviewer_name**: Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **reviewer_title**: Enter your title as the reviewer.
* **location_map**: Select whether or not to include a location map.
* **location_address**: Enter the physical address of the project location.
* **location_postal_code**: Enter the postal code of the project location.
* **location_city**: Enter the city where the project is located.
* **location_state**: Enter the state where the project is located.
* **location_country**: Enter the country where the project is located.
* **location_notes**: Add any additional notes about the project location (optional).
* **site_inspection_date**: Enter the date of the site inspection.
* **site_inspection_time**: Enter the time of the site inspection.
* **site_inspection_location**: Enter the location of the site inspection.
* **site_inspection_notes**: Add any notes from the site inspection.
* **site_inspection_status**: Select whether or not the site inspection was Completed or Not Completed.
* **site_inspection_reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who performed the site inspection.
