# Pickup Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pickup Truck Inspection Form is designed to gather detailed information about the condition and specifications of a pickup truck. It covers various attributes such as vehicle condition, dimensions, and engine details to aid in thorough assessment and inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Vehicle Condition** field (Field 1) with a brief description of the vehicle's overall condition.
2. Enter the **Vehicle Year** (Field 2) of the pickup truck.
3. Describe the **Engine Condition** (Field 3) in the provided text field.
4. Select the **Transmission Type** (Field 4) from the available options (Automatic, Manual, Semi-Automatic).
5. Enter the **Engine Year** (Field 5) of the engine.
6. Enter the **Vehicle Color** (Field 6) in the provided text field.
7. Enter the **VIN** (Field 7) for identification purposes.
8. Enter the **Mileage** (Field 8) of the vehicle in number format.
9. Enter the **Fuel Capacity** (Field 9) of the vehicle in number format.
10. Enter the **Fuel Efficiency** (Field 10) of the vehicle in number format.
11. Enter the **Vehicle Weight** (Field 11) of the vehicle in number format.
12. Enter the **Engine Size** (Field 12) of the vehicle in number format.
13. Describe the **Transmission Fuel** (Field 13) in the provided text field.
14. Enter the **Vehicle Length** (Field 14) of the vehicle in number format.
15. Enter the **Vehicle Width** (Field 15) of the vehicle in number format.
16. Enter the **Vehicle Height** (Field 16) of the vehicle in number format.
17. Enter the **Curb Weight** (Field 17) of the vehicle in number format.
18. Enter the **Wheelbase** (Field 18) of the vehicle in number format.
19. Enter the **Length Over Wheels** (Field 19) of the vehicle in number format.
20. Enter the **Width Over Wheels** (Field 20) of the vehicle in number format.
21. Enter the **Height Over Wheels** (Field 21) of the vehicle in number format.
22. Enter the **Track** (Field 22) of the vehicle in number format.
23. Enter the **Overall Length** (Field 23) of the vehicle in number format.
24. Enter the **Overall Width** (Field 24) of the vehicle in number format.
25. Enter the **Overall Height** (Field 25) of the vehicle in number format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Condition (1)**: Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any existing damage or repairs.
* **Vehicle Year (2)**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Engine Condition (3)**: Describe the engine's overall condition, including any issues or needed repairs.
* **Transmission Type (4)**: Select the type of transmission the vehicle has (Automatic, Manual, Semi-Automatic).
* **Engine Year (5)**: Enter the year the engine was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Color (6)**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **VIN (7)**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number for the vehicle.
* **Mileage (8)**: Enter the total miles driven on the vehicle.
* **Fuel Capacity (9)**: Enter the fuel tank capacity of the vehicle.
* **Fuel Efficiency (10)**: Enter the fuel efficiency of the vehicle (e.g., miles per gallon).
* **Vehicle Weight (11)**: Enter the weight of the vehicle in pounds.
* **Engine Size (12)**: Enter the engine size in cubic inches.
* **Transmission Fuel (13)**: Describe the fuel type used by the transmission.
* **Vehicle Length (14)**: Enter the length of the vehicle in feet.
* **Vehicle Width (15)**: Enter the width of the vehicle in feet.
* **Vehicle Height (16)**: Enter the height of the vehicle in feet.
* **Curb Weight (17)**: Enter the curb weight of the vehicle in pounds.
* **Wheelbase (18)**: Enter the wheelbase of the vehicle in feet.
* **Length Over Wheels (19)**: Enter the length of the vehicle over the wheels in feet.
* **Width Over Wheels (20)**: Enter the width of the vehicle over the wheels in feet.
* **Height Over Wheels (21)**: Enter the height of the vehicle over the wheels in feet.
* **Track (22)**: Enter the track of the vehicle in feet.
* **Overall Length (23)**: Enter the overall length of the vehicle in feet.
* **Overall Width (24)**: Enter the overall width of the vehicle in feet.
* **Overall Height (25)**: Enter the overall height of the vehicle in feet.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a thorough inspection and assessment of the vehicle. If you're unsure about any field, you can refer to the provided instructions or seek guidance from an expert.
