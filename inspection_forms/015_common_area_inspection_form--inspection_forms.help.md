# Common Area Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Common Area Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the inspection of common areas within a property or building. This form helps building managers and facility administrators to assess and maintain the cleanliness, condition, and functionality of these areas, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for occupants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Inspection Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
2. **Inspector Name and Title**: Enter your full name and job title.
3. **Property Information**: Provide details about the property being inspected.
4. **Property Name and Address**: Enter the name and full address of the property.
5. **Building Type**: Select the type of building or facility being inspected (e.g., Office Building, Apartment Complex, etc.).
6. **Areas Inspected**: Choose the common areas that were inspected (e.g., Lobby, Hallways, Restrooms, etc.).
7. **Cleanliness Assessment**: Evaluate the overall cleanliness of the common areas.
8. **Overall Cleanliness**: Rate the overall cleanliness of the area (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Unacceptable).
9. **Floor Condition, Wall Condition, and Lighting Condition**: Evaluate the condition of flooring, walls, and lighting in the common areas.
10. **Restroom Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of restroom facilities.
11. **Trash Management**: Evaluate the condition of trash and recycling areas.
12. **Safety Assessment**: Evaluate any safety-related issues or hazards.
13. **Safety Hazards**: Identify any safety hazards present.
14. **Hazard Details**: Describe any safety hazards found.
15. **Accessibility Compliance**: Evaluate the accessibility and compliance of the area.
16. **Maintenance Needed**: Identify any maintenance required for the common areas.
17. **Priority Level**: Select the priority level of the maintenance required (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Urgent).
18. **Follow-Up Required**: Indicate if follow-up inspection is needed.
19. **Follow-Up Date**: Enter a scheduled follow-up date (if applicable).
20. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or observations.
21. **Photos Taken**: Indicate if photos were taken during the inspection.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the inspection.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
