**inspector_qualification_assessment - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The **inspector_qualification_assessment** form is designed to assess the qualification and performance of an inspector. This form is used to collect information about the inspector's skills, knowledge, and experience. It is essential to fill out this form to evaluate the inspector's qualifications and provide them with a fair evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Title** field with a brief title that describes the purpose of the assessment.
2.  Select the appropriate **Category** from the options provided to indicate the type of assessment.
3.  Enter a detailed **Description** of the assessment in the provided text area.
4.  Choose the inspector's **Qualification** level from the options (Good, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory).
5.  Enter the **Review Date** and **Review Time** of the assessment.
6.  Enter the **Inspector Name** who performed the assessment.
7.  Add any additional **Review Notes** if necessary.
8.  Enter the **Email** address of the inspector.
9.  Enter the **Phone** number of the inspector (if applicable).
10. Finally, select the submission option from the **Submit** field (Save and Exit or Save and Continue).
11. If the form is submitted, indicate the **Assigned Tool** used for the assessment (Chatjimmy or Other).
12. Optionally, enter the **Review Time 2**, **Review Date 2**, and **Submitted Time 2** fields if necessary.
13. Indicate whether the form has been **Submitted** (True or False) and the **Submitted Time 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: Enter a brief title that describes the purpose of the assessment.
* **Category**: Select the category of the assessment (Inspection Forms, Maintenance Forms, or Other).
* **Description**: Enter a detailed description of the assessment.
* **Qualification**: Choose the inspector's qualification level (Good, Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the review.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the inspector.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the inspector (if applicable).
* **Submit**: Select whether to save and exit or save and continue.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used for the assessment (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Review Time 2**: Enter the review time for the second review.
* **Review Date 2**: Enter the review date for the second review.
* **Submitted**: Indicate whether the form has been submitted (True or False).
* **Submitted Time 2**: Enter the submitted time for the second submission.
* **Review Time 3**, **Review Time 4**, **Review Time 5**, and **Review Time 6**: Enter the review times for the subsequent reviews.
* **Review Date 3**, **Review Date 4**, **Review Date 5**, and **Review Date 6**: Enter the review dates for the subsequent reviews.
