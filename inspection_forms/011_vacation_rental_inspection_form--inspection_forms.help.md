# vacation_rental_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the condition of a vacation rental property, any maintenance requests, and photos during an inspection.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the following fields:

### Property Details (Page 1)

* **Property Details** (text): This field is for you to provide basic information about the property being inspected, such as the address, unit number, and any other relevant details.

### Property Inspection (Page 1)

* **Property Inspection** (text): Use this field to describe the overall condition of the property, including any issues found during the inspection.

### Maintenance Requests (Page 1)

* **Maintenance Requests** (text): If there are any maintenance or repair requests for the property, enter them in this field.

### Photos (Page 1)

* **Photos** (text): Upload or link to photos of the property that can be used to support your inspection report.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure accurate and thorough inspection reports.
* If you have any additional comments or notes about the property, feel free to include them in the "Property Details" field.
* Make sure to upload clear and relevant photos to support your inspection report.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
