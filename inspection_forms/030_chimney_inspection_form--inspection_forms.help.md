# Chimney Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a chimney inspection, including the inspector's name, the date of the inspection, the property address, the type of chimney, the level of creosote buildup, any structural issues observed, the recommended action, and the overall safety status of the chimney.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person completing the inspection.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter the address of the property where the chimney was inspected.
4. Select the type of chimney from the options: Brick masonry, Metal, Prefabricated, or Other.
5. Estimate the level of creosote buildup by selecting from the options: Low, Moderate, High.
6. Provide a description of any structural issues observed, such as cracks, spalling, or damage.
7. Suggest any recommended actions for maintenance or repair.
8. Assess the overall safety status of the chimney by selecting from the options: Safe, Repair recommended, or Unsafe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Inspector Name
• **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing the inspection.
### 2. Inspection Date
• **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
### 3. Property Address
• **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property where the chimney was inspected.
### 4. Chimney Type
• **Chimney Type** (`chimney_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of chimney from the options: Brick masonry, Metal, Prefabricated, or Other.
### 5. Creosote Level
• **Creosote Level** (`creosote_level`, select_one, required): Estimate the level of creosote buildup by selecting from the options: Low, Moderate, High.
### 6. Structural Issues
• **Structural Issues Observed** (`structural_issues`, text, optional): Provide a description of any structural issues observed, such as cracks, spalling, or damage.
### 7. Recommended Action
• **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, text, required): Suggest any recommended actions for maintenance or repair.
### 8. Safety Status
• **Safety Status** (`safety_status`, select_one, required): Assess the overall safety status of the chimney by selecting from the options: Safe, Repair recommended, or Unsafe.
