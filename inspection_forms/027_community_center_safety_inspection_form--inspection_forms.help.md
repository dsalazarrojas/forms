# Community Center Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for community center safety inspectors to conduct regular safety inspections and gather information on the current state of safety conditions within the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and job title in the "Name of Inspector" and "Job Title or Role of Inspector" fields.
2. Select the date and time of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" and "Time of Inspection" fields.
3. Identify the area or zone being inspected in the "Area or Zone Being Inspected" field.
4. Answer the questions in the "Fire Safety and Emergency Preparedness" section, checking the relevant boxes to indicate whether fire extinguishers are present and up to date, whether smoke detectors have been tested recently, and whether emergency exits are clear and unobstructed.
5. Answer the questions in the "General Facility Safety" section, checking the boxes to indicate whether floors are clear of tripping hazards, whether there is adequate lighting in all common areas, and whether walkways are clear and unobstructed.
6. In the "Inspection Findings and Recommendations" section, provide a detailed description of any issues or concerns identified during the inspection.
7. For any identified issues, suggest recommended corrective actions in the "Recommended Corrective Actions" field.
8. Indicate the priority level for the corrective actions in the "Priority Level for Corrective Action" field.
9. If applicable, provide a target date for completion of the corrective actions in the "Target Date for Completion" field.
10. Finally, sign the "Digital Signature of Inspector" field with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Inspector** (`inspector_full_name_saf`): Enter your full name as the inspector.
* **Job Title or Role of Inspector** (`inspector_title_saf`): Enter your job title or role as the inspector.
* **Date of Inspection** (`inspection_date_saf`): Select the date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Time of Inspection** (`inspection_time_saf`): Select the time on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Area or Zone Being Inspected** (`inspection_location_area_saf`): Identify the area or zone being inspected, e.g., Gymnasium, Kitchen, Outdoor Area.
* **Fire Extinguishers Present and Up to Date** (`fire_extuishers_present_saf`): Check if fire extinguishers are present and up to date.
* **Smoke Detectors Tested Recently** (`smoke_detectors_tested_saf`): Check if smoke detectors have been tested recently.
* **Are All Emergency Exits Clear and Unobstructed** (`emergency_exits_clear_saf`): Check if all emergency exits are clear and unobstructed.
* **Is Emergency Lighting Functional** (`emergency_lighting_functional_saf`): Check if emergency lighting is functional.
* **Are Floors Clear of Tripping Hazards** (`floors_clear_tripping_hazards_saf`): Check if floors are clear of tripping hazards.
* **Is There Adequate Lighting in All Common Areas** (`adequate_lighting_common_areas_saf`): Check if there is adequate lighting in all common areas.
* **Are Walkways Clear and Unobstructed** (`walkways_clear_unobstructed_saf`): Check if walkways are clear and unobstructed.
* **Are Electrical Cords in Good Condition** (`electrical_cords_safe_condition_saf`): Check if electrical cords are in good condition (no fraying or exposed wires).
* **Detailed Findings and Observations** (`detailed_inspection_findings_saf`): Provide a detailed description of any issues or concerns identified during the inspection.
* **Recommended Corrective Actions** (`recommended_corrective_actions_saf`): Suggest any corrective actions to address identified issues.
* **Priority Level for Corrective Action** (`priority_level_corrective_action_saf`): Indicate the priority level for the corrective actions (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Target Date for Completion** (`completion_date_corrective_action_saf`): If applicable, provide a target date for completion of the corrective actions.
* **Digital Signature of Inspector** (`inspector_signature_digital_saf`): Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date Report Submitted** (`report_submission_date_saf`): Enter the date the report is submitted.
