# Fan Coil Unit Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Coil Unit Inspection Survey is used to collect information about the Fan Coil Unit's condition, maintenance history, and upcoming service schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the Fan Coil Unit's maintenance history and ensure you are familiar with its model, size, and previous services.
2. Enter the Fan Coil Unit's model and size in the corresponding fields.
3. Check the unit's status and select the correct option from the list (Working, Not Working, or Needs Service).
4. If necessary, enter the notes or comments about the unit's condition.
5. Enter any relevant information about the last service, including date and cost.
6. Enter the expected next service date and cost.
7. If applicable, enter the mileage or other relevant metrics about the last and next service.
8. Finally, enter the name and title of the technician who performed the last service, and any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Coil Model** (`fan_coil_model`, text, optional): Enter the model number or name of the Fan Coil Unit.
* **Fan Coil Size** (`fan_coil_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the Fan Coil Unit.
* **Fan Coil Capacity** (`fan_coil_capacity`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the Fan Coil Unit's capacity.
* **Fan Coil Location** (`fan_coil_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the Fan Coil Unit.
* **Fan Coil Status** (`fan_coil_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the Fan Coil Unit (Working, Not Working, or Needs Service).
* **Fan Coil Remaks** (`fan_coil_remarks`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the Fan Coil Unit's condition.
* **Fan Coil Last Service Date** (`fan_coil_last_service_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last service.
* **Fan Coil Last Service Cost** (`fan_coil_last_service_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the last service.
* **Fan Coil Last Service Mileage** (`fan_coil_last_service_mileage`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the last service, such as mileage.
* **Fan Coil Next Service Date** (`fan_coil_next_service_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected next service date.
* **Fan Coil Next Service Cost** (`fan_coil_next_service_cost`, number, optional): Enter the expected cost of the next service.
* **Fan Coil Next Service Mileage** (`fan_coil_next_service_mileage`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the next service, such as mileage.
* **Fan Coil Last Service Technician** (`fan_coil_last_service_technician`, text, optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the last service.
* **Fan Coil Comments** (`fan_coil_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the Fan Coil Unit.
