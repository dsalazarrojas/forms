# Brake System Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the inspection of a vehicle's brake system. The goal is to record information about the vehicle's brakes, including the type, condition, and any issues found during inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Report Information** section. This includes providing the **Report Date**, which is the date of the inspection, and the **Report Number**, which is a unique identifier for the report.
2.  Move on to the **Vehicle Information** section. Here, you will provide details about the vehicle, including its **Vehicle Year**, **Vehicle Make**, and **Vehicle Model**.
3.  In the **Inspection Findings** section, assess the condition of the vehicle's brakes, including **Brake System Type**, **Brake Fluid Condition**, **Brake Pad Condition**, and **Rotor Condition**.
4.  If any issues were found during inspection, describe them in the **Issues Identified** section.
5.  Based on the findings, make any necessary **Required Repairs** and suggest any **Recommended Maintenance**.
6.  Finally, indicate the **Inspection Status** in the last section. This will reflect the outcome of the inspection based on the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Report Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the report.
*   **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
*   **Vehicle Year**: Enter the model year of the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make**: Enter the manufacturer of the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model name of the vehicle.
*   **VIN Number**: Enter the vehicle's identification number. (Not required)
*   **Brake System Type**: Select the type of brakes on the vehicle (e.g., Disc brakes all wheels, Disc and drum mix, etc.).
*   **Brake Fluid Condition**: Assess the condition of the brake fluid (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, etc.).
*   **Brake Pad Condition**: Assess the condition of the brake pads (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Worn, etc.).
*   **Rotor Condition**: Assess the condition of the rotors (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Warped, etc.).
*   **Issues Found**: Describe any problems found during the inspection.
*   **Required Repairs**: Enter any necessary repairs.
*   **Recommended Maintenance**: Suggest any preventative care.
*   **Inspection Status**: Select the final outcome of the inspection (e.g., Pass, Pass with advisories, Fail, Conditional pass, etc.).
