# Excavator Operator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavator Operator Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about the performance and proficiency of excavator operators during an inspection. This form helps assess their skills, identifies areas for improvement, and tracks the completion status of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Provide the name of your supervisor in the **Supervisor Name** field.
4. Enter your supervisor's email address in the **Supervisor Email** field (if applicable).
5. Enter your supervisor's phone number in the **Supervisor Phone** field (if applicable).
6. Specify the model of the excavator you are operating in the **Excavator Model** field.
7. Fill in the **Operating Hours** field with the actual operating hours of the excavator.
8. Choose the unit of measurement for the operating hours in the **Operating Hours Unit** field (e.g., hours, days, weeks).
9. Enter the total operating hours of the excavator in the **Operating Hours Total** field.
10. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar in the **Inspection Date** field.
11. Fill in the inspection time in the **Inspection Time** field (if applicable).
12. Add any relevant **Observations** about your performance.
13. List any **Action Items**, or suggestions for improvement.
14. Finally, confirm the completion status of the inspection by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Completed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as the excavator operator.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details for future reference.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address (if your supervisor has an email).
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number (if your supervisor has a phone number).
* **Excavator Model**: Specify the model of the excavator you are operating.
* **Operating Hours**: Record the operating hours of the excavator.
* **Operating Hours Unit**: Choose the unit of measurement for the operating hours (e.g., hours, days, weeks).
* **Operating Hours Total**: Enter the total operating hours of the excavator.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **Inspection Time**: Fill in the inspection time (if the inspection was conducted at a specific time).
* **Observations**: Provide any comments or observations about your performance.
* **Action Items**: List any action items or suggestions for improvement.
* **Completed**: Confirm the completion status of the inspection by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
