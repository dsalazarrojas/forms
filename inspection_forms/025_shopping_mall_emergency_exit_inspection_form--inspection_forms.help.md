# Shopping Mall Emergency Exit Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Shopping Mall Emergency Exit Inspection Form is a tool for documenting the result of an emergency exit inspection at a shopping mall. This form helps mall management or security personnel to identify any issues or concerns with emergency exits and ensure that they are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the emergency exits at the mall to identify any issues or concerns.
2. Enter the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Inspector" field.
4. Enter a description of the area you inspected in the "Mall Floor Area" field.
5. Click "Submit" to record your inspection results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Exit** (Number): This field is not used. The form is not intended for use with this field.
* **Inspection Date** (Date): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required.
* **Inspector** (Text): Enter your name as the person who performed the inspection. This field is not required, but it is recommended to ensure accountability and follow-up.
* **Mall Floor Area** (Text): Enter a brief description of the area you inspected, such as "First Floor Near Food Court" or "Second Floor Near Movie Theater". This field is not required, but it helps the reviewer understand the context of the inspection.

## Tips

* Be thorough in your inspection and description of any issues or concerns you find.
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you find any issues or concerns, take corrective action or recommend further action to the mall management or security team.
