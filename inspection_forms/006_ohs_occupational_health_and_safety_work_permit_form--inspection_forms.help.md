# OHS (Occupational Health and Safety) Work Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OHS (Occupational Health and Safety) Work Permit Form is a document that provides a standardized way for workers to report on any hazards they encounter during work activities. This form is designed to ensure that workers take necessary precautions and follow safety protocols to minimize risks and prevent accidents. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your site ID**: In this field, enter the ID of the site where you worked. This will help us track the location of your work activities.
2. **Select hazards checked**: Choose the hazards you encountered during your work. Select all that apply from the list:
   * Fire
   * Flood
   * Chemical
   * Physical
   * Electrical
   * Structural
   * Weather
   * Other (please specify)
3. **Describe hazard control measures**: If you took any measures to control the hazards you checked, describe them in this field.
4. **Describe actions taken**: In this field, describe the actions you took to mitigate any hazards.
5. **Name of site safety inspector**: Please enter the name of the site safety inspector who was present during your work.
6. **Site safety inspector signature**: If the site safety inspector was present, ask them to sign this field to confirm they reviewed your report.
7. **Date completed**: Enter the date when you completed this form.
8. **Time completed**: Enter the time when you completed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site ID (1)**: Enter the ID of the site where you worked.
* **Hazards Checked (2)**: Select all hazards you encountered during your work. This will help us identify potential risks and take necessary precautions.
* **Hazard Control Measures (3)**: Describe any measures you took to control the hazards you checked. This will help us understand what you did to minimize risks.
* **Actions Taken (4)**: Describe the actions you took to mitigate any hazards you encountered.
* **Site Safety Inspector (5)**: Please enter the name of the site safety inspector who was present during your work.
* **Site Safety Inspector Signature (6)**: If the site safety inspector was present, ask them to sign this field to confirm they reviewed your report.
* **Date Completed (7)**: Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Time Completed (8)**: Enter the time when you completed this form.
