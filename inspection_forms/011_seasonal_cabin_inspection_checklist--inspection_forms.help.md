# Seasonal Cabin Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Cabin Inspection Checklist form is designed to collect information and data related to the maintenance and upkeep of seasonal cabins. The form is intended to ensure that cabins are properly maintained and inspected on a regular basis to prevent accidents, ensure guest safety, and identify potential issues before they become major problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the cabin's address in the "Cabin Address" field.
2.  Enter the contact person's name and information in the "Contact Person" field.
3.  Select the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
4.  Enter the name of the person responsible for the cabin's maintenance in the "Maintenance Manager" field.
5.  Select the type of cabin from the "Cabin Type" field.
6.  Select the season of the year in which the inspection is performed from the "Season" field.
7.  Select the maintenance status of the cabin from the "Maintenance Status" field.
8.  Enter any additional comments or notes about the cabin's condition in the "Notes" field.
9.  Enter the contact person's email address in the "Email" field.
10.  Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Phone" field.
11.  Select whether a follow-up inspection is required or not from the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Cabin Address**: <small>Enter the physical address of the cabin, including the street number, city, and state.</small>
*   **Contact Person**: <small>Enter the name and information of the person responsible for the cabin's maintenance and upkeep.</small>
*   **Inspection Date**: <small>Enter the date of the inspection, which is essential for tracking maintenance and upkeep schedules.</small>
*   **Maintenance Manager**: <small>Enter the name of the person responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of the cabin.</small>
*   **Cabin Type**: <small>Select the type of cabin, which can be Cabin, Lodge, Chalet, or Cabinette.</small>
*   **Season**: <small>Select the season of the year in which the inspection is performed, which can be Winter, Spring, Summer, or Fall.</small>
*   **Maintenance Status**: <small>Select the status of the cabin's maintenance, which can be Active, Inactive, or both.</small>
*   **Notes**: <small>Enter any additional comments or notes about the cabin's condition.</small>
*   **Email**: <small>Enter the contact person's email address.</small>
*   **Phone**: <small>Enter the contact person's phone number.</small>
*   **Follow-up**: <small>Select whether a follow-up inspection is required or not.</small>
