# Facility Features Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the safety and emergency preparedness of a facility. It collects information on various features such as safety equipment, systems, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by entering the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers for each question.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Features** (`facility_features`): A text field for describing the facility's features.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`): Enter the name of the building or facility being assessed.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`date_last_inspection`): The date of the last inspection of the facility.
* **Last Inspection Result** (`last_inspection_result`): A text field for describing the result of the last inspection.
* **Fire Extinguishing System** (`fire_extinguishing_system`): Select one of the available options for the fire extinguishing system (Fire Alarm System, Fire Sprinkler System, or None).
* **Electrical Systems** (`electrical_systems`): Select multiple options from the list (Emergency Exit Lighting, Fire Alarm System, Generator, Power Distribution Box, Security System, or Other (Specify)).
* **Fire Safety System** (`fire_safety_system`): Select multiple options from the list (Fire Suppression System, Fire Alarm System, Fire Sprinkler System, Fire Extinguisher, Fire Exit Signs, Fire Resistant Materials, Fire Safety Training Program).
* **Water System** (`water_system`): Select one of the available options for the water system (Boiler System, Cooling Tower, Ductwork System, Pumps, Sewer, or Other (Specify)).
* **Sewage System** (`sewage_system`): Select one of the available options for the sewage system (Grease Trap, Pumps, Sewer, or Other (Specify)).
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`): Select multiple options from the list (Recycling Bins, Composting, Dustbin, Garbage Bins, Incinerator, Landfill).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`): Select multiple options from the list (First Aid Kit, Fire Extinguisher, Safety Harness, Safety Shoes, Respirator, or Other (Specify)).
* **Fire Extinguisher** (`fire_extinguisher`): Select multiple options from the list (Fire Extinguisher AFFF, Fire Extinguisher FE-24, Fire Extinguisher FE-24K, Fire Extinguisher K Class, Fire Extinguisher Other (Specify)).
* **Safety Hazards** (`safety_hazards`): Select multiple options from the list (Fall Hazards, Fire Hazards, Electrical Hazards, Chemical Hazards, or Other (Specify)).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (Spouse, Relative, Friend, Coworker, or Other (Specify)).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Facility Inspector** (`facility_inspector`): Enter the name of the facility inspector.
* **Facility Inspector Phone** (`facility_inspector_phone`): Enter the phone number of the facility inspector.
* **Facility Inspector Email** (`facility_inspector_email`): Enter the email of the facility inspector.
* **Facility Inspector Relationship** (`facility_inspector_relationship`): Select the relationship of the facility inspector (Spouse, Relative, Friend, Coworker, or Other (Specify)).
* **Facility Inspector Name** (`facility_inspector_name`): Enter the name of the facility inspector.
* **Comments** (`comments`): A text field for leaving additional comments or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Review each field carefully before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the facility inspector or manager for guidance.
