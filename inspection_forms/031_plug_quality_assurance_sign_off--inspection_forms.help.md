# Plug Quality Assurance Sign Off - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and sign off on the quality assurance process for a specific plug.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals in place before starting.
2. Fill in the plug quality assurance sign off details:
	* plugUnitNumber: Enter the unit number of the plug.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffNumber: Enter the unique quality assurance sign off number.
	* signOffDate: Enter the date the quality assurance was completed.
	* inspectionDate: (Optional) Enter the date of the inspection. If not required.
3. Select the plug quality assurance inspector:
	* inspectorName: Enter the name of the inspector responsible for the quality assurance.
	* inspectorId: Enter the ID of the inspector.
4. Confirm the sign off details:
	* signOffBy: Enter the name of the person signing off on the quality assurance.
	* approvedBy: Enter the name of the person approving the quality assurance.
5. Document the plug quality assurance:
	* plugType: Select the type of plug being assured.
	* qualityAssuranceResults: Select all relevant quality assurance results.
	* comments: (Optional) Enter any comments related to the quality assurance.
6. Document the outcome:
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcome: Select the outcome of the quality assurance.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffReason: (Optional) Enter the reason for the quality assurance outcome.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeDate: Enter the date of the quality assurance outcome.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeTime: (Optional) Enter the time of the quality assurance outcome.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeComments: (Optional) Enter any comments related to the quality assurance outcome.
7. Attach any relevant files:
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffAttachments: Select all relevant files attached.
8. Finalize the quality assurance sign off:
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOff: Select the status of the quality assurance sign off.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffDate: Enter the date of the quality assurance sign off.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffTime: (Optional) Enter the time of the quality assurance sign off.
	* plugQualityAssuranceSignOffNotes: (Optional) Enter any notes related to the quality assurance sign off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **plugUnitNumber** (Required, Number): Enter the unit number of the plug.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffNumber** (Required, Number): Enter the unique quality assurance sign off number.
* **signOffDate** (Required, Date): Enter the date the quality assurance was completed.
* **inspectionDate** (Optional, Date): (Optional) Enter the date of the inspection, if required.
* **inspectorName** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the inspector responsible for the quality assurance.
* **inspectorId** (Required, Number): Enter the ID of the inspector.
* **signOffBy** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the person signing off on the quality assurance.
* **approvedBy** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the person approving the quality assurance.
* **plugType** (Required, Select One): Select the type of plug being assured.
* **qualityAssuranceResults** (Required, Select Multiple): Select all relevant quality assurance results.
* **comments** (Optional, Note): Enter any comments related to the quality assurance.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffStatus** (Required, Select One): Select the status of the quality assurance sign off.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcome** (Required, Select One): Select the outcome of the quality assurance.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffReason** (Optional, Text): Enter the reason for the quality assurance outcome.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeDate** (Required, Date): Enter the date of the quality assurance outcome.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeTime** (Optional, Time): Enter the time of the quality assurance outcome, if required.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffOutcomeComments** (Optional, Note): Enter any comments related to the quality assurance outcome.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOffAttachments** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select all relevant files attached.
* **plugQualityAssuranceSignOff** (Required, Select One): Select the status of the quality assurance sign off.

## Tips

* Review the form before submitting.
* Save and print the form for future reference.
