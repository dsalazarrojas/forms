# crane_safety_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the safety and condition of cranes on your site. It helps identify potential hazards and schedule corrective actions to prevent accidents and ensure the crane's continued safe operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each crane on your site.
2. Select the correct crane type from the options provided.
3. Enter the location of the crane.
4. Select the date of the last inspection.
5. Identify any hazards encountered during the inspection.
6. Indicate whether any corrective actions were taken or if they are pending.
7. Enter the date for the next scheduled inspection.
8. Specify the interval for the next inspection.
9. (Optional) Enter any observations made during the inspection.
10. (Optional) Enter any notes about the crane.
11. Enter your signature.
12. Enter your email.
13. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
14. (Optional) Enter the assigned user responsible for the crane.
15. (Optional) Enter any comments about the crane.
16. (Optional) Enter the equipment ID.
17. (Optional) Enter the equipment name.
18. (Optional) Enter the equipment type.
19. (Optional) Enter the equipment model.
20. (Optional) Enter a description of the equipment.
21. (Optional) Enter the equipment condition.
22. (Optional) Enter the equipment manufacturer.
23. (Optional) Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
24. (Optional) Enter the date of the last inspection.
25. (Optional) Enter the location of the equipment.
26. (Optional) Enter the equipment serial number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crane type** (`crane_type`): Select the type of crane being inspected.
  * Choose from: Tower crane, Hooklift crane, or Other.
* **Crane location** (`crane_location`): Enter the location where the crane is being used.
* **Date of inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Hazards encountered** (`hazards_encountered`): Identify any hazards found during the inspection.
  * Choose from: None, Loose chains, Broken parts, Loose bolts, or Other.
* **Corrective actions** (`corrective_actions`): Indicate whether any corrective actions were taken.
  * Choose from: True, False, or Partially completed.
* **Next due date** (`next_due_date`): Enter the date for the next scheduled inspection.
* **Next inspection interval** (`next_inspection_interval`): Specify the interval for the next inspection in days.
* **Observations** (`observations`): (Optional) Enter any observations made during the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`): (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the crane.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Enter your signature to confirm the inspection.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): (Optional) Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned user** (`assigned_user`): (Optional) Enter the user responsible for the crane.
* **Comments** (`comments`): (Optional) Enter any comments about the crane.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`): (Optional) Enter the equipment ID.
* **Equipment name** (`equipment_name`): (Optional) Enter the equipment name.
* **Equipment type** (`equipment_type`): (Optional) Enter the equipment type.
* **Equipment model** (`equipment_model`): (Optional) Enter the equipment model.
* **Equipment description** (`equipment_description`): (Optional) Enter a description of the equipment.
* **Equipment condition** (`equipment_condition`): (Optional) Select the condition of the equipment.
  * Choose from: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Equipment manufacturer** (`equipment_manufacturer`): (Optional) Enter the equipment manufacturer.
* **Equipment purchase date** (`equipment_purchase_date`): (Optional) Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Last inspection date** (`equipment_last_inspection`): (Optional) Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Equipment location** (`equipment_location`): (Optional) Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Equipment serial number** (`equipment_serial_number`): (Optional) Enter the equipment serial number.
