# Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory form is used to collect information about the protected areas infrastructure in your organization. This form helps you to keep track of the location and asset category of your protected areas infrastructure, which is crucial for maintenance and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. On the first page, enter the location of the protected areas infrastructure in the `infrastructure_location` field. This is the physical address where the infrastructure is located.
3. On the second page, select the asset category of the protected areas infrastructure from the `asset_category` dropdown menu. You can choose from the options "Yes" and "No" to indicate whether the infrastructure is an asset or not.
4. Finally, on the second page, select the status of photos associated with the infrastructure from the `photos` dropdown menu. You can choose from the options "Yes" and "No" to indicate whether there are photos associated with the infrastructure or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **infrastructure_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the protected areas infrastructure, such as a address or a description of the location.
* **asset_category** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether the infrastructure is an asset or not. If it is an asset, choose "Yes". If not, choose "No".
* **photos** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the status of photos associated with the infrastructure. If there are photos, choose "Yes". If not, choose "No".
