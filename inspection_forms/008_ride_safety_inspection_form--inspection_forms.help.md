# Ride Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ride Safety Inspection Form is used to track the safety status of a vehicle, providing a snapshot of its condition and inspection history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you are completing this form for the correct vehicle by providing its correct Ride ID.
- Select the correct options for Ride Inspection, Ride Type, and Inspection Frequency based on the actual condition of the vehicle.
- If the Safety Device Status is Active, enter the Safety Device Serial Number, if applicable.
- Enter the Date and Time of the inspection accurately, as they are crucial for tracking and reporting.
- Enter your name as the Inspector conducting the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ride Inspection**: Select 'Yes' if the vehicle is currently in a safe state for operation, 'No' otherwise.
* **Ride ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the vehicle, which helps in tracking and identification.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Time**: Enter the time on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name, which is essential for documentation and accountability purposes.
* **Ride Type**: Select the type of vehicle being inspected (e.g., 'Yes' for a specific type).
* **Safety Device Status**: If the vehicle is equipped with a safety device (e.g., a seatbelt), select 'Active' if it is currently functioning correctly, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Safety Device Serial Number**: If the vehicle has a safety device, enter its serial number for reference and tracking.
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of inspections for this vehicle (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly).
