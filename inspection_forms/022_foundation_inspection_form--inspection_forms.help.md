# Foundation Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundation Inspection Form is a tool used to record the details of a foundation inspection. It is used to collect contact information and property-specific data to ensure that the inspection is conducted properly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Property Address field with the actual address of the property being inspected.
2. Select the Type of Property field to indicate whether the property is a residential or commercial property.
3. In the Describe the issue field, provide a detailed description of any issues found during the inspection.
4. Enter the Contact Name field with the name of the person to contact in case of any further information or action required.
5. Enter the Contact E-mail field with the email address of the contact person.
6. Enter the Contact Phone field with the phone number of the contact person.
7. Fill in the Date Of Inspection field with the actual date of the inspection.
8. Enter the Time Of Inspection field with the actual time of the inspection.
9. Enter the Inspector Name field with the name of the person conducting the inspection.
10. Sign and date the Inspector Signature field with the inspector's signature and the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (1, text, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Type of Property** (2, select_one, optional): Select whether the property is a residential, business, or other type of property.
* **Describe the issue** (3, text, required): Provide a detailed description of any issues found during the inspection.
* **Contact Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of further information or action required.
* **Contact E-mail** (5, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (6, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date Of Inspection** (7, date, required): Enter the actual date of the inspection.
* **Time Of Inspection** (8, time, required): Enter the actual time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (10, note, required): Sign and date the inspector's signature field with the inspector's signature and the date of the inspection.
