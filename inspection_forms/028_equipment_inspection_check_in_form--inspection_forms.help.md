# Equipment Inspection Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for inspecting and documenting the status of equipment in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the list of equipment and select the item you are inspecting.
2. Fill out the form fields as required or necessary.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Inspection Form (1)** (`Equipment Inspection Form`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the equipment being inspected.
* **Status (2)** (`Status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the equipment. Options include:
	+ Available
	+ Out of Order
	+ Maintenance Required
	+ Removed
* **Comments (3)** (`Comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the equipment inspection.
* **Condition (4)** (`Condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current condition of the equipment. Options include:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Needs Repair
* **Date Inspected (5)** (`Date Inspected`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was inspected.
* **Date Last Inspected (6)** (`Date Last Inspected`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was last inspected.
* **Last Inspected By (7)** (`Last Inspected By`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last inspected the equipment.
* **Notes (8)** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the equipment inspection.
* **Assigned User (9)** (`Assigned User`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to the equipment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.
* For each field, provide as much detail as necessary to accurately document the equipment status.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the equipment documentation or consult with the equipment owner.
* This form is intended for routine inspections and check-ins. For urgent or critical issues, please contact the maintenance team directly.
