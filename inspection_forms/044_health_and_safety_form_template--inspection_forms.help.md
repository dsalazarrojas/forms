# health_and_safety_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health and safety information, risk assessments, and training protocols. It is intended to be completed by users who need to provide detailed health and safety data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "health_safety_section" page to start.
2.  Enter any relevant health and safety information in the text field.
3.  Move to the "risk_assessment" page to assess any risks.
4.  Enter your risk assessment details in the text field.
5.  Move to the "training_protocol" page to provide information on training protocols.
6.  Select the relevant training protocols from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **health_safety_section** (`health_safety_section`, Note, Optional): This page allows you to enter any general health and safety information.
*   **risk_assessment** (`risk_assessment`, Text, Optional): This page is for gathering risk assessment details.
*   **training_protocol** (`training_protocol`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any relevant training protocols from the list provided.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible when completing this form.
*   Ensure that you provide enough information to allow the reviewer to make a fair evaluation.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.
*   Save your progress regularly to avoid losing data in case of system errors or interruptions.
