# property_condition_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting on the condition of a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the property type (residential, commercial, or other).
2. Choose the property status (active, inactive, or vacant).
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS AM/PM).
5. Enter the name and contact information of the person conducting the inspection.
6. Enter the address of the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Condition Report**: This is the main text field where you can enter any comments or observations about the property's condition.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property being inspected (residential, commercial, or other).
* **Property Status**: Choose all that apply from the list of status options (active, inactive, or vacant).
* **Condition Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Condition Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Contact**: Enter the contact information of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the property.
