# New Construction Final Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Construction Final Inspection Checklist is a form used to document the completion status of a construction project. This form is typically filled out by a construction inspector when they have completed the final inspection of a construction project. The form is used to provide a snapshot of the project's final condition and ensure that all necessary steps have been taken before final certification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the response for each item from the provided options.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Final Inspection Checklist** (`new_construction_final_inspection_checklist`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ A checklist of all necessary steps completed for the final inspection.
	+ Select "Yes" for each step completed.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional):
	+ The name of the person conducting the final inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional):
	+ The date of the final inspection.
* **Construction Manager Name** (`construction_manager_name`, text, optional):
	+ The name of the person responsible for the construction project.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out and select the correct options for the "Final Inspection Checklist" field.
