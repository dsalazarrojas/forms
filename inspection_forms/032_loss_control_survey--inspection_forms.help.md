# Loss Control Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loss Control Survey is a form used to assess and document potential risks and losses related to your work or activities. It's an essential tool for identifying and mitigating risks, and helping you and your team stay safe and productive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2.  For questions with multiple options, choose the answer that best describes your situation.
3.  Use clear and concise answers to explain your answers to questions with text fields.
4.  Don't worry if you're unsure about any question; just answer based on your best judgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Read it carefully to understand what the form is asking you to do.
*   **Risk Areas** (`risk_areas`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check all the risk areas that you are concerned about. These areas might include things like workplace hazards, equipment, or processes.
*   **What are the risk areas you are concerned about?** (`risk_areas_list_1`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the risk areas you are concerned about from the list provided. This is a repeat of the previous question, but with more options.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `number`, not required): Enter a number (0-10) to rate your risk assessment for each risk area.
*   **What is your risk assessment score?** (`risk_assessment_score`, `number`, not required): Enter a number (0-10) to rate your risk assessment score.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the risk level that best describes the situation (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **How often is this risk level present?** (`risk_level_frequency`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select how often the risk level is present (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
*   **Loss Control Plan** (`loss_control_plan`, `text`, not required): Provide a brief description of your loss control plan.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or concerns you have.
*   **Loss Control Plan 2** (`loss_control_plan_2`, `text`, not required): This field is similar to the previous one, just enter a brief description of your loss control plan again.
*   **Loss Control Plan 3** (`loss_control_plan_3`, `text`, not required): Another field to describe your loss control plan.
*   **Loss Control Plan 4** (`loss_control_plan_4`, `text`, not required): Another field to describe your loss control plan.
*   **Loss Control Plan 5** (`loss_control_plan_5`, `text`, not required): The final field to describe your loss control plan.

## Tips
*   Be honest and thorough in your answers.
*   If you're unsure about any question, just answer based on your best judgment.
*   Don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.
