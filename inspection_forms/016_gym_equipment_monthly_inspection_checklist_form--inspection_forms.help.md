# Gym Equipment Monthly Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a monthly inspection of gym equipment to ensure their proper functioning, safety, and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the equipment and its surroundings.
2. Answer each section of the form accordingly.
3. Fill out any necessary sections that need attention or maintenance.
4. Enter the next scheduled inspection date and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Equipment (1)**: Describe the condition of the general equipment, such as treadmills, ellipticals, and stationary bikes. 
* **Electrical Equipment (2)**: List any electrical equipment that requires attention or maintenance, such as electrical panels, circuit breakers, or wiring.
* **Electrical Cords (3)**: Inspect electrical cords and list any issues or concerns.
* **Electrical Systems (4)**: Describe the condition of the electrical systems, including circuit breakers, fuses, and other electrical components.
* **Fire Safety Equipment (5)**: Check fire safety equipment, such as fire alarms, fire extinguishers, or emergency exit signs.
* **Fire Safety Systems (6)**: Describe the condition of fire safety systems, including sprinkler systems, smoke detectors, or fire suppression systems.
* **Safety Equipment (7)**: List any safety equipment that requires maintenance, such as first aid kits, fire extinguishers, or safety signs.
* **Safety Systems (8)**: Describe the condition of safety systems, including alarm systems, security cameras, or access control systems.
* **Emergency Equipment (9)**: Identify emergency equipment that requires attention or maintenance, such as emergency exits, emergency phones, or first aid kits.
* **Emergency Equipment List (10)**: Select all emergency equipment that is currently functional and ready for use.
* **Maintenance Records (11)**: Keep a record of past and present maintenance activities.
* **Safety Maintenance Records (12)**: Keep a record of past and present safety maintenance activities.
* **Maintenance Status (13)**: Describe the current maintenance status of the equipment or area.
* **Equipment Condition (14)**: Describe the overall condition of the equipment.
* **Equipment Status (15)**: Describe the functional status of the equipment.
* **Last Inspection Date (16)**: Enter the date of the previous monthly inspection.
* **Next Inspection Date (17)**: Enter the date of the next scheduled monthly inspection.
* **Next Inspection Frequency (18)**: Enter the scheduled frequency of inspections (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Last Inspection Frequency (19)**: Enter the last known inspection frequency.
* **Inspection Frequency (20)**: Enter the recommended inspection frequency for the equipment.
* **Maintenance Frequency (21)**: Enter the recommended maintenance frequency for the equipment.
* **Maintenance Status (22)**: Describe the current maintenance status of the equipment.
* **Equipment Location (23)**: Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Notes (24)**: Enter any comments or observations about the equipment or area.
* **Comments (25)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
