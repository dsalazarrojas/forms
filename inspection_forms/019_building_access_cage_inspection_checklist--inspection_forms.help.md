# Building Access Cage Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Access Cage Inspection Checklist is a tool used by inspectors or maintenance personnel to document and report on the condition of an access cage, highlighting any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your information in the top section, including your name, ID, contact email, phone number, and date of inspection.
2. Select the type of inspection you're performing (e.g., routine, scheduled maintenance, incident response, etc.).
3. Enter the name and location of the building being inspected.
4. Record the cage's identifier and location within the building.
5. Assess the condition of the cage, using the provided checklists for structural integrity, locking mechanism, access control system, surveillance cameras, lighting, cleanliness, and safety hazards.
6. Describe any damage or issues noted during the inspection.
7. List any maintenance requirements or corrective actions needed.
8. Determine the priority level of any issues found (low, medium, high, or critical).
9. Specify if follow-up is required and, if so, schedule a date for the next inspection.
10. Attach any photos or documentation that support your findings.
11. Provide a final assessment of the cage's overall status (pass, pass with minor issues, fail, or fail with critical issues).
12. Certify that your inspection is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspector ID (2)**: Enter your employee or contractor ID number.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Inspection Date (5)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time (6)**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Building Name (7)**: Enter the name of the building being inspected.
* **Building Location (8)**: Enter the address or location of the building being inspected.
* **Cage Identifier (9)**: Enter the cage's identifier number or name.
* **Cage Location (10)**: Enter the location within the building where the cage is situated.
* **Inspection Type (11)**: Select the type of inspection being performed (e.g., routine, scheduled maintenance, incident response, etc.).
* **Structural Integrity (12)**: Assess the cage's structural integrity using the provided options (pass, minor issues, major issues, critical).
* **Locking Mechanism (13)**: Assess the condition of the locking mechanism using the provided options (functional, needs adjustment, needs repair, needs replacement).
* **Access Control System (14)**: Assess the functionality of the access control system using the provided options (fully operational, minor issues, malfunctioning, not applicable).
* **Surveillance Cameras (15)**: Assess the status of the surveillance cameras using the provided options (all functional, some issues, not functional, not installed).
* **Lighting Condition (16)**: Assess the lighting condition using the provided options (adequate, needs improvement, insufficient, not functional).
* **Cleanliness Status (17)**: Assess the cleanliness status using the provided options (clean, acceptable, needs cleaning, unsatisfactory).
* **Safety Hazards (18)**: List any safety hazards found during the inspection, using the provided options (none, exposed wiring, sharp edges, slippery surfaces, obstructed exit, fire hazards, structural damage, other).
* **Damage Noted (19)**: Describe any damage or issues noted during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Needed (20)**: List any maintenance requirements or corrective actions needed.
* **Corrective Actions Required (21)**: List any required actions for corrective maintenance.
* **Priority Level (22)**: Determine the priority level of any issues found (low, medium, high, critical).
* **Follow-up Required (23)**: Specify if follow-up is required and, if so, schedule a date for the next inspection.
* **Follow-up Date (24)**: Enter the date for the next inspection (optional).
* **Photos Attached (25)**: Specify if photos or documentation are attached to support your findings.
* **Overall Inspection Status (26)**: Provide a final assessment of the cage's overall status (pass, pass with minor issues, fail, fail with critical issues).
* **Inspector Acknowledgment (27)**: Certify that your inspection is accurate and complete.
