# Fountain Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fountain Inspection Checklist is a form designed to inspect various aspects of a fountain's functionality and maintenance needs. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question based on your inspection findings.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. If you're unsure about any question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ Choose "High" if the water level is sufficient for the plants.
	+ Choose "Low" if the water level is not sufficient.
