# Aquarium Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Aquarium Tank Inspection Form is a crucial tool used to document the status of an aquarium tank and its contents. It ensures that all inspections are recorded accurately and consistently, enabling aquarists to monitor tank conditions over time and take necessary actions to maintain tank health and water quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately for each inspection.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the `Inspection Date` field.
3. Enter the time of the inspection in the `Inspection Time` field.
4. Enter your name as the inspector in the `Inspector Name` field.
5. Enter the Tank ID or Location in the `Tank ID or Location` field.
6. Enter the tank's volume in gallons in the `Tank Volume (gallons)` field (optional).
7. Record the water temperature in Fahrenheit in the `Water Temperature (Fahrenheit)` field.
8. Assess the water clarity with the options:
	* Crystal Clear
	* Clear
	* Slightly Cloudy
	* Very Cloudy
9. Evaluate the filtration system's status with the options:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
	* Not Functioning
10. Check the status of the tank's equipment with the options:
	* Working
	* Not Working
	* Needs Service
11. Record any aquatic life observations in the `Aquatic Life Observations` field (optional).
12. Enter any concerns or issues with the tank in the `Any Concerns or Issues` field (optional).
13. Finally, note any recommended maintenance for the tank in the `Recommended Maintenance` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (e.g., `14:30`).
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Tank ID or Location**: Enter the tank's ID or location where the inspection was conducted.
* **Tank Volume (gallons)**: Enter the tank's volume in gallons (optional).
* **Water Temperature (Fahrenheit)**: Record the water temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Water Clarity Assessment**: Select the water clarity status from the provided options.
* **Filtration System Status**: Evaluate the filtration system's status from the provided options.
* **Equipment Condition Check**: Evaluate the status of the tank's equipment from the provided options.
* **Heater Status**: Evaluate the status of the tank's heater from the provided options.
* **Pump Status**: Evaluate the status of the tank's pump from the provided options.
* **Aquatic Life Observations**: Record any observations about the tank's aquatic life.
* **Any Concerns or Issues**: Enter any concerns or issues with the tank.
* **Recommended Maintenance**: Note any recommended maintenance for the tank.
