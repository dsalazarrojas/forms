# industrial_equipment_inspection_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in the inspection and review of industrial equipment, helping ensure that it is in good working condition and meets safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by ensuring you are in the correct location for the inspection. This may be a physical space where the equipment is located or a virtual environment where the form can be accessed.
2. Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and relevance to the inspection process.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
4. For fields with multiple options, select all applicable items.
5. Enter any additional comments or observations in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General (1)
* **General** (`General`, `note`, required: false): This field provides space for any additional comments or observations about the equipment or inspection process. It is not a required field.

### Critical areas (2)
* **Critical areas** (`Critical areas`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing any critical areas that require inspection. Enter the specific areas or components that need attention.

## Tips
* Be thorough in your inspection and ensure all relevant fields are completed.
* If an option is not applicable, mark it as "N/A" or "Not Applicable" to show that it does not apply.
* Save the completed form for future reference and to help track inspection history.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, refer to the equipment manual or manufacturer's instructions for guidance.
