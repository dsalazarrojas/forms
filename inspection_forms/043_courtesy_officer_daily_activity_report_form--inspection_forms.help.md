# Courtesy Officer Daily Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courtesy Officer Daily Activity Report Form is designed to collect information on daily activities and incident reports from Courtesy Officers. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Report Date" field and select the date of the report.
2.  Enter the name of the Courtesy Officer who completed the form in the "Courtesy Officer" field (if required).
3.  Provide a brief summary of the incident or activity in the "Activity Summary" field.
4.  Specify the location of the incident or activity in the "Location" field.
5.  Describe the incident or activity in the "Incident Description" field.
6.  Specify the action taken regarding the incident or activity in the "Action Taken" field.
7.  Check the box in the "Incident Status" field to indicate the status of the incident.
8.  Enter any contact information for the incident or activity in the "Contact Number", "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
9.  Specify the type of incident or activity in the "Incident Type" field.
10.  Check the box in the "Follow-Up Required" field to indicate if follow-up is required.
11.  Enter any follow-up notes in the "Follow-Up Notes" field (if required).
12.  Enter the date of the follow-up (if required) in the "Follow-Up Date" field.
13.  Select the follow-up method (e.g. phone call, email, etc.) in the "Follow-Up Method" field (if required).
14.  Enter the status of the follow-up (e.g. open, closed, etc.) in the "Follow-Up Status" field (if required).
15.  Enter any follow-up comments in the "Follow-Up Comment" field (if required).
16.  Enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field (if required).
17.  Enter the date the incident was reported in the "Incident Reporting Date" field (if required).
18.  Enter the name of the party responsible for the follow-up in the "Follow-Up Responsible Party" field (if required).
19.  Enter any comments regarding the closure of the follow-up in the "Follow-Up Closed Comments" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report.
* **Courtesy Officer**: Enter the name of the Courtesy Officer who completed the form.
* **Activity Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the incident or activity.
* **Location**: Specify the location of the incident or activity.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident or activity in detail.
* **Action Taken**: Specify the action taken regarding the incident or activity.
* **Incident Status**: Check the box to indicate the status of the incident.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number related to the incident or activity.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact related to the incident or activity.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact related to the incident or activity.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact related to the incident or activity.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident or activity (e.g. Other, Incident, Event, Meeting, Phone Call).
* **Incident Location**: Specify the location of the incident or activity (if required).
* **Follow-Up Required**: Check the box to indicate if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-Up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes (if required).
* **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-Up Method**: Select the method of the follow-up (e.g. phone call, email, etc.).
* **Follow-Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (e.g. open, closed, etc.).
* **Follow-Up Comment**: Enter any follow-up comments (if required).
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporting Date**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Follow-Up Responsible Party**: Enter the name of the party responsible for the follow-up (if required).
* **Follow-Up Closed Comments**: Enter any comments regarding the closure of the follow-up (if required).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and attachments ready before completing the form.
* The form can be submitted with attachments if needed.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
