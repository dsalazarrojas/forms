# Cottage Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cottage Safety Inspection Checklist is a form designed to help users conduct a thorough safety inspection of a cottage, covering various critical areas to identify potential risks and ensure the cottage meets safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing detailed descriptions of the conditions you observe in the cottage.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most appropriate response based on your observations.
3. If a condition is not present or not applicable, leave the field blank or select "0" or "None" where applicable.
4. Complete the form as thoroughly as possible, and be honest about any potential risks or hazards you find.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. cottage_address
**Cottage Address**: This field is the address where the cottage is located. Please enter the complete address, including street number and postcode, where applicable.

### 2. fire_safety
**Fire Safety**: Assess the fire safety features of the cottage, such as working smoke alarms and fire extinguishers. Rate the fire safety on a scale from 1 (Very Poor) to 3 (Excellent).

### 3. electrical_safety
**Electrical Safety**: Select one of the following options to rate the electrical safety of the cottage:
* 1: Electrical safety is very poor, with exposed wiring and/or faulty fuses.
* 2: Electrical safety is average, with some exposed wiring but no signs of faulty fuses.
* 3: Electrical safety is excellent, with no exposed wiring and no signs of faulty fuses.

### 4. gas_safety
**Gas Safety**: Select all applicable options for the type of gas sources in the cottage:
* Natural Gas
* Propane
* Electric (if applicable)

### 5. water_safety
**Water Safety**: Enter any observations about the water supply and quality in the cottage.

### 6. structural_integrity
**Structural Integrity**: Rate the structural integrity of the cottage on a scale from 1 (Very Poor) to 3 (Excellent).

### 7. safety_equipment
**Safety Equipment**: Select one of the following options to rate the safety equipment in the cottage:
* 1: No safety equipment available or in poor condition.
* 2: Safety equipment is available but not in good condition.
* 3: Safety equipment is in good condition and functioning properly.

### 8. electrical_hazards
**Electrical Hazards**: Select all applicable options that pose electrical hazards:
* Loose wiring
* Exposed wiring
* Faulty fuses

### 9. gas_leaks
**Gas Leaks**: Select one of the following options to report any gas leaks:
* 1: No gas leaks detected.
* 2: Gas leaks detected, but not severe.
* 3: Severe gas leaks detected.

### 10. water_leaks
**Water Leaks**: Select one of the following options to report any water leaks:
* 1: No water leaks detected.
* 2: Water leaks detected, but not severe.
* 3: Severe water leaks detected.
