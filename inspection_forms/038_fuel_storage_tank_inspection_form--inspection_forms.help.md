# Fuel Storage Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about the condition of fuel storage tanks, including tank condition, level, and any relevant notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the tank for which you are recording information.
2. Select the tank's current condition (e.g. Clean, Stained, Rusted).
3. Choose the date and time of the inspection.
4. Enter the tank's current level.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the tank's condition.
6. If applicable, provide additional information about Tank 1, 2, or 3.
7. Select the inspector who performed the inspection.
8. Select the person who completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tank Info** (`Tank 1`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the tank you are inspecting (e.g. Tank 1, Tank 2, Tank 3).
* **Tank Condition** (`Tank Condition`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the current condition of the tank (e.g. Clean, Stained, Rusted).
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date of the inspection in `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g. 2022-01-01).
* **Inspection Time** (`Inspection Time`, `time`, `false`): Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (e.g. 14:00).
* **Tank Level** (`Tank Level`, `number`, `false`): Enter the current level of the tank as a number (e.g. 50%).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, `false`): Enter any notes or comments about the tank's condition.
* **Tank Info 1** (`Tank Info 1`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional information about Tank 1.
* **Tank Info 2** (`Tank Info 2`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional information about Tank 2.
* **Tank Info 3** (`Tank Info 3`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional information about Tank 3.
* **Assigned Inspector** (`Assigned Inspector`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Completed By** (`Completed By`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the person who completed the form.
