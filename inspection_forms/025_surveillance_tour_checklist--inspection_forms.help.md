# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surveillance Tour Checklist is a form used to record and document the details of a surveillance tour. This form is used to gather information about the officer's tour, including patrol details, incident reports, and patrol frequency. It is designed to be completed by the officer during the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and select the relevant options for each field.
2. Complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Enter information in the fields as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol Details** (`patrol_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to select multiple options for patrol details. Select all the relevant options that apply to your tour.
* **Incident Reports** (`incident_reports`, `text`, optional): Enter any incident reports or observations made during the tour.
* **Checkpoint** (`checkpoint`, `text`, optional): Enter any checkpoint information or observations made during the tour.
* **Patrol Frequency** (`patrol_frequency`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of the patrol, if applicable.
* **Patrol Start Time** (`patrol_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the patrol.
* **Patrol End Time** (`patrol_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the patrol.
* **Patrol Duration** (`patrol_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the patrol.
* **Patrol Notes** (`patrol_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the officer conducting the patrol.
* **Officer ID** (`officer_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your ID number as the officer conducting the patrol.
* **Officer Role** (`officer_role`, `select_one`, optional): Select your role as the officer conducting the patrol.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of incident reported.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Patrol Frequency 2** (`patrol_frequency_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of the patrol 2 (if applicable).
* **Incident Frequency** (`incident_frequency`, `number`, optional): Enter the frequency of the incident (if applicable).
* **Checkpoint Frequency** (`checkpoint_frequency`, `text`, optional): Enter the frequency of the checkpoint (if applicable).
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patrol 2.
* **Patrol Start Time 2** (`patrol_start_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the patrol 2.
* **Patrol End Time 2** (`patrol_end_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the patrol 2.
* **Incident Start Time** (`incident_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the incident.
* **Incident End Time** (`incident_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the notes fields to enter any additional comments or observations.
* If unsure about any field, ask for assistance.
