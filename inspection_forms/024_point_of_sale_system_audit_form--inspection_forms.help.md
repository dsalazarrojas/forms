# Point Of Sale System Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Point of Sale System Audit Form is used to document and review point of sale system audits. This form helps maintain accuracy and reliability of the point of sale system and ensures that any issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Store ID: Enter the ID of the store being audited.
3. Date Submitted: Enter the date the audit was submitted.
4. Store Location: Enter the location of the store.
5. Auditor Name: Enter the name of the person who performed the audit.
6. Findings: Describe any issues or problems found during the audit.
7. Planned Action: Describe the actions to be taken to address any issues or problems.
8. Status: Select the status of the audit (Active or Inactive).
9. Assigned To: Select the personnel assigned to take action on the issues.
10. Follow-up: Select whether follow-up is required after the planned action.
11. Comments: Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_id** (store_id, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the store being audited.
* **date_submitted** (date_submitted, date, required: false): Enter the date the audit was submitted.
* **store_location** (store_location, text, required: false): Enter the location of the store being audited.
* **auditor_name** (auditor_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who performed the audit.
* **findings** (findings, text, required: false): Describe any issues or problems found during the audit.
* **action_planned** (action_planned, text, required: false): Describe the actions to be taken to address any issues or problems.
* **status** (status, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the audit (Active or Inactive).
* **assigned_to** (assigned_to, select_multiple, required: false): Select the personnel assigned to take action on the issues.
* **follow_up** (follow_up, select_one, required: false): Select whether follow-up is required after the planned action.
* **comments** (comments, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the audit.
