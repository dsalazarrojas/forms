# Patient Transfer Lift Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Transfer Lift Inspection Checklist is a critical tool used by healthcare workers to inspect patient transfer equipment and environment, ensuring patient safety during transfers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Go through each page and select the options that apply to your specific situation.
2. For fields with multiple options (e.g. select_multiple), select all that apply.
3. For text fields (e.g. checklist_transfer_lift), provide a detailed description of your findings.
4. Ensure you answer all required fields to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Transfer Lift Equipment Checklist** (transfer_lift_equipment_checklist)
* **Type:** Select One
* **Description:** Check if the lift equipment is in good working order and ready for use.
* **Options:** Yes, No

### 2. **Patient Receiving Area Checklist (patient_receiving_area_checklist)**
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Description:** Evaluate the patient receiving area, checking for any potential hazards or issues that may affect patient safety.
* **Options:** Yes, No

### 3. **Patient Lift Equipment Checklist** (patient_lift_equipment_checklist)
* **Type:** Select One
* **Description:** Check the condition and functionality of the patient lift equipment.
* **Options:** Yes, No

### 4. **Transfer Lift Checklist** (transfer_lift_checklist)
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** Describe any observations or issues with the patient lift equipment or environment that may affect patient safety.
* **Note:** Provide a detailed description of your findings.

### 5. **Patient Transfer Lift Checklist** (patient_transfer_lift_checklist)
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** Describe any observations or issues with the patient lift equipment or environment that may affect patient safety.
* **Note:** Provide a detailed description of your findings.

### 6. **Equipment Checklist** (equipment_checklist)
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Description:** Check the condition and functionality of other equipment that may be used during the patient transfer.
* **Options:** Yes, No

### 7. **Patient Transfer Lifting Equipment Checklist** (patient_transfer_lifting_equipment_checklist)
* **Type:** Select One
* **Description:** Check the condition and functionality of patient lifting equipment.
* **Options:** Yes, No

### 8. **Checklist-Transfer Lift** (checklist_transfer_lift)
* **Type:** Text
* **Description:** Describe any final observations or issues with the patient transfer lift equipment or environment that may affect patient safety.
* **Note:** Provide a detailed description of your findings.
