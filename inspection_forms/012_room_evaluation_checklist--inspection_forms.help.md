# Room Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Room Evaluation Checklist is a tool used to evaluate the condition of a room within a building or property. It helps identify areas that require attention, schedule follow-up actions, and record additional comments or observations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the Room Evaluation Checklist form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Complete the form by answering each question according to your observations and knowledge.
3.  Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., bathroom conditions, room type, status, and condition).
4.  For text fields (e.g., general area, kitchen area, maintenance items, and additional comments), provide a detailed description of your observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Area** (`general_area`, text, required: false): Describe the general condition of the room, including any notable features or issues.
*   **Bathroom** (`bathroom`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the condition of the bathroom, choosing from options such as Clean, Unclean, Partially Clean, or Not Clean.
*   **Kitchen Area** (`kitchen_area`, text, required: false): Describe the condition of the kitchen area, including any issues or concerns.
*   **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of room being evaluated, such as Room or Suite.
*   **Room Status** (`room_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the status of the room, choosing from options like Occupied, Vacant, or Under Renovation.
*   **General Condition** (`general_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Assess the overall condition of the room, choosing from options such as Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Needs Major Repair.
*   **Maintenance Items** (`maintenance_items`, text, required: false): List any maintenance items that require attention or action.
*   **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if follow-up actions are scheduled, not scheduled, or in progress.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or observations that may be relevant to the room evaluation.

Note: This form is intended to be a general guideline, and users should complete it according to their professional judgment and experience. The form's output should be tailored to their specific needs and circumstances.
