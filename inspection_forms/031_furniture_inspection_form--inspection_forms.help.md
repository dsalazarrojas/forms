# Furniture Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the condition and details of furniture during an inspection. It is designed to be a comprehensive tool for inspectors to document their findings and provide recommendations for maintenance or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form page by page, from page 1 to page 5.
2. On page 1, provide a brief description of the furniture being inspected.
3. On page 2, describe the furniture's details, such as its type and material.
4. On page 3, assess the condition of the furniture and list any defects or damages found.
5. On page 4, document any specific damages or defects that require attention.
6. On page 5, provide a final recommendation for the furniture's maintenance or repairs.
7. On pages 6-8, select the type of furniture being inspected from the options provided.
8. On pages 9-10, enter any numerical values or quantities that require assessment.
9. On page 11, select the date of the inspection.
10. On page 12, select the time of the inspection.
11. On page 13, take notes on any additional comments or observations.
12. On page 14, enter your email address for contact information.
13. On pages 15-17, select any additional options related to the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Furniture Inspection Form** (`inspection_form_page_1`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form and is not editable.
* **Furniture Details** (`inspection_form_page_2`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the furniture being inspected.
* **Condition and Defects** (`inspection_form_page_3`, text, required/optional): List any defects or damages found on the furniture.
* **Defects and Damages** (`inspection_form_page_4`, text, required/optional): Document any specific damages or defects that require attention.
* **Recommendation** (`inspection_form_page_5`, text, required/optional): Provide a final recommendation for the furniture's maintenance or repairs.
* **Property Type** (`inspection_form_page_6`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of furniture being inspected from the options provided.
* **Property Type Other** (`inspection_form_page_7`, text, required/optional): If the type of furniture is not listed in the previous option, provide a description of the type of furniture.
* **Property Type** (`inspection_form_page_8`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional types of furniture being inspected from the options provided.
* **Quantity** (`inspection_form_page_9`, number, required/optional): Enter any numerical values or quantities that require assessment.
* **Quantity** (`inspection_form_page_10`, number, required/optional): Enter any additional numerical values or quantities that require assessment.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_form_page_11`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_form_page_12`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`inspection_form_page_13`, note, required/optional): Take any additional comments or observations during the inspection.
* **Contact Email** (`inspection_form_page_14`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication.
* **Option 1** (`inspection_form_page_15`, select_one, required/optional): Select any additional options related to the inspection.
* **Option 2** (`inspection_form_page_16`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional options related to the inspection.
* **Option 3** (`inspection_form_page_17`, select_one, required/optional): Select any additional options related to the inspection.
* **Additional Comments** (`inspection_form_page_18`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or observations.
