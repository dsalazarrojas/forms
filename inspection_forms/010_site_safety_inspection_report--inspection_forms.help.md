# Site Safety Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Safety Inspection Report form is used to document and report on site conditions, safety concerns, and inspection outcomes after a site inspection. It helps site managers, supervisors, and safety experts to gather essential information for future reference and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `Site Name`: Enter the name of the site you are inspecting.
	* `Date of Inspection`: Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (e.g., 2023-03-01).
	* `Time of Inspection`: Enter the time of the inspection (e.g., 08:00).
	* `Inspection Team`: Enter the names of the individuals or teams conducting the inspection.
	* `Report Status`: Select the status of the report (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).
2. Provide optional information:
	* `Weather Conditions`: Describe the weather conditions during the inspection (e.g., sunny, rainy, etc.).
	* `Site Condition`: Describe the site conditions (e.g., clean, messy, etc.).
3. Add comments and contact information:
	* `Comments`: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
	* `Contact Information`: Enter the contact details of the person or organization conducting the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the site being inspected.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (e.g., 2023-03-01).
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection (e.g., 08:00).
* **Inspection Team** (`inspection_team`, text, required): Enter the names of the individuals or teams conducting the inspection.
* **Report Status** (`report_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the report (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the weather conditions during the inspection (e.g., sunny, rainy, etc.).
* **Site Condition** (`site_condition`, text, optional): Describe the site conditions (e.g., clean, messy, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter the contact details of the person or organization conducting the inspection.
