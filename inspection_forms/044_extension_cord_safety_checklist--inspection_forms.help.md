# extension_cord_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The extension cord safety checklist is a form used to inspect and evaluate the safety of extension cords. It is designed to help personnel identify potential electrical hazards and assess the condition of extension cords.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Check the appropriate boxes for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or observations in the "extension_cord_safety_checklist_comments" field.
4. Enter the date of the inspection in the "extension_cord_safety_checklist_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical Hazards** (`electrical_hazards`, select_multiple): This field requires you to indicate if there are any electrical hazards present. Select all that apply:
	+ no hazards
	+ hazards
	+ unknown
* **Cord Condition** (`cord_condition`, text): Describe the condition of the extension cord, including any signs of wear, damage, or deterioration.
* **Extension Cords Inspected** (`extension_cords_inspected`, number): Enter the number of extension cords inspected.
* **Extension Cord Safety Checklist Comments** (`extension_cord_safety_checklist_comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or observations about the extension cords.
* **Extension Cord Safety Checklist Date** (`extension_cord_safety_checklist_date`, date): Enter the date of the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to inspect all extension cords in the area.
* Take note of any hazards or issues found during the inspection.
* Document all findings and recommendations in the "extension_cord_safety_checklist_comments" field.
* Keep the "extension_cord_safety_checklist_date" field up to date.
