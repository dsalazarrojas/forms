# Internal Wall Insulation Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the risk level of internal wall insulation in a property and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by providing general information about the property.
2. Enter details about the property's internal walls.
3. Identify any risks associated with the internal wall insulation.
4. Select the type of project that this assessment is for (Insulation System, Wall Insulation System, or Roof Insulation).
5. Enter the wall area, height, and thickness in the corresponding fields.
6. Enter the type and value of the internal wall insulation material.
7. Enter the supplier name.
8. Provide a description of the risk level and recommended action.
9. Enter any additional notes about the property's internal wall insulation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General information** (1): This field is for entering general information about the property.
* **Property details** (2): This field is for entering specific details about the property's internal walls.
* **Risks** (3): This field is for identifying any risks associated with the internal wall insulation.
* **Wall Insulation** (4): This field is for entering information about the internal wall insulation type and material.
* **Project information** (5): This field is for selecting the type of project this assessment is for (Insulation System, Wall Insulation System, or Roof Insulation).
* **Project details** (6): This field is for selecting from a list of options, currently only (Insulation System, Wall Insulation System, and Roof Insulation).
* **Wall area** (7): This field is for entering the wall area in square meters.
* **Wall height** (8): This field is for entering the wall height in meters.
* **Wall thickness** (9): This field is for entering the wall thickness in millimeters.
* **Insulation type** (10): This field is for entering the type of internal wall insulation material.
* **Material** (11): This field is for entering the material used for the internal wall insulation.
* **Supplier** (12): This field is for entering the supplier's name.
* **Risk level** (13): This field is for entering the risk level associated with the internal wall insulation.
* **Risk description** (14): This field is for describing the risk associated with the internal wall insulation.
* **Recommended action** (15): This field is for entering a recommended action to mitigate the risk associated with the internal wall insulation.
* **Wall area units** (16): This field is for entering the unit of measurement for the wall area (e.g., sq m, sq ft).
* **Wall area value** (17): This field is for entering the wall area value.
* **Wall height units** (18): This field is for entering the unit of measurement for the wall height (e.g., m, ft).
* **Wall height value** (19): This field is for entering the wall height value.
* **Wall thickness units** (20): This field is for entering the unit of measurement for the wall thickness (e.g., mm, in).
* **Wall thickness value** (21): This field is for entering the wall thickness value.
* **Insulation type units** (22): This field is for entering the unit of measurement for the internal wall insulation type (e.g., type, material).
* **Insulation type value** (23): This field is for entering the internal wall insulation type value.
* **Notes** (24): This field is for entering any additional notes about the property's internal wall insulation.

## Tips

* Please fill in the fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a thorough risk assessment.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* This form is for internal wall insulation only, so please do not enter information about external wall insulation or other types of insulation.
* This form is for general guidance only and should not be used as a substitute for professional advice or assessment.
