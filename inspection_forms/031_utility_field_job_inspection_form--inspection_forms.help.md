# Utility Field Job Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help utility field inspectors record and document their inspection findings. It captures essential information about the inspector, location, and condition of the utility field, which is crucial for maintaining and troubleshooting the system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and title as the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* Select the date and time of the inspection from the calendar or clock.
* Choose the type of utility field being inspected (electric, gas, water, or sewer).
* Enter the location of the inspection (e.g., address, building number, or landmark).
* Take photos of the utility field, if possible, to provide visual evidence.
* Describe the condition of the utility field using the provided options (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
* Report the status of the inspection (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
* Sign your name as the inspector, indicating that you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector**: Enter your name and title as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection**: Select the date on which the inspection took place using the calendar or clock.
* **Time of Inspection**: Choose the time of the inspection from the clock.
* **Utility Type**: Choose the type of utility field being inspected (Electric, Gas, Water, or Sewer).
* **Location**: Enter the exact location of the inspection, including the address, building number, or a recognizable landmark.
* **Utility Photos**: Take and upload photos of the utility field to provide visual evidence.
* **Notes**: Record any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Inspection Condition**: Choose the condition of the inspected utility field using the provided options (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
* **Inspection Status**: Report the status of the inspection (Complete, Incomplete, or Not Started).
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name as the inspector, confirming that you have completed the form.
