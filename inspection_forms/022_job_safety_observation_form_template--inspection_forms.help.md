# Job Safety Observation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Safety Observation Form is used to collect and document site safety observations. These observations are important for identifying and mitigating potential hazards on the job site. By completing this form, site observers can report incidents, near misses, or observations that may not have resulted in an injury but could have potentially led to one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Site Location where the observation took place.
2. Select the Observer Location (e.g., office, yard, etc.).
3. Enter your Observer Name and Date.
4. Choose the Observed By option that best describes the situation (e.g., Employee, Supervisor, etc.).
5. Describe the Observation, including any actions taken.
6. Enter any Additional Information about the observation.
7. Choose the Observation Date for the incident or near miss.
8. Enter the Observed Item, if applicable.
9. Enter the Condition, if applicable.
10. Enter the Location of the incident or near miss.
11. Enter any Notes about the observation.
12. Choose if the Observation is Complete.
13. Select the person who completed the form.
14. Enter any location-specific notes for the observer.
15. Enter any notes for the person observed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Location**: This is the location where the observation took place (e.g., office, yard, etc.).
* **Observer Location**: This is the location of the observer (e.g., office, yard, etc.).
* **Observer Name**: This field is for the name of the person completing the observation.
* **Observer Date**: This is the date the observation was made.
* **Observed By**: This option selects who was observed (e.g., Employee, Supervisor, etc.).
* **Observation**: This is the description of the incident or near miss.
* **Action Taken**: This field is for any actions taken as a result of the observation.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional details that are relevant to the observation.
* **Observation Date**: This is the date of the observation.
* **Observed Item**: This field is for any items or equipment involved in the observation.
* **Condition**: This field is for any conditions that contributed to the incident or near miss.
* **Location**: This field is for the location of the incident or near miss.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the observation.
* **Observation Complete**: This option indicates if the observation is complete or not.
* **Form Completed By**: This option selects the person who completed the form.
* **Observer Location Note**: This field is for any notes specific to the observer's location.
* **Observed By Note**: This field is for any notes specific to the person observed.
* **Observed Item Note**: This field is for any notes specific to the observed item.
