# fire_extinguisher_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the inspection and maintenance of fire extinguishers in a facility or building. It ensures that fire extinguishers are functioning properly and meet safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are filled out accurately.
2. Enter the name of the fire extinguisher being inspected.
3. Select the correct model of the fire extinguisher.
4. Check the current status of the fire extinguisher (e.g., "Working", "Exhusted", or "Missing").
5. Enter the date of the last inspection and the next scheduled inspection.
6. Provide any additional notes about the fire extinguisher.
7. Select the "Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form" status (e.g., "Complete", "Not Started", or "In Progress").
8. Confirm if the form has been submitted.
9. Select the "Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form" status change (e.g., "True" or "False").
10. Check if the review is completed.
11. Select the "Fire Extinguisher Inspected" status.
12. Enter the date of the review completion.
13. Enter the date of the last inspection.
14. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
