# University Dining Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the state of the university's dining facilities. It should be used to conduct regular inspections and record any findings or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar provided.
2. Choose the time of the inspection.
3. Enter the name of the inspector.
4. Type any comments or observations about the facilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector** (`inspector`, required: false): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, required: false): Type any comments or observations about the facilities.
