# Recycling Plant Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and report any safety issues found during a safety inspection at a recycling plant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the ID of the facility being inspected.
3. Provide your name and ID.
4. Choose the type of equipment being inspected (e.g. Crusher, Shredder, Compactor, Baler).
5. Select all locations that were inspected (e.g. Main Plant, Waste Processing Area, Receiving Area, Other).
6. Indicate if any safety issues were found during the inspection.
7. Record any notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection.
* **Facility**: The ID of the facility being inspected.
* **Employee Name**: Your name.
* **Employee ID**: Your ID.
* **Equipment Type**: The type of equipment being inspected (e.g. Crusher, Shredder, Compactor, Baler).
* **Inspection Location**: The locations that were inspected (e.g. Main Plant, Waste Processing Area, Receiving Area, Other).
* **Safety Issue Found**: Indicate if any safety issues were found during the inspection.
* **Notes**: Record any notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use the correct equipment type and location options.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "Notes" field.
