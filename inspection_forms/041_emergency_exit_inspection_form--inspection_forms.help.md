# Emergency Exit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Exit Inspection Form is a template designed for inspecting emergency exits. This form is meant to guide users through an examination of emergency exits to identify any hazards or risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review your answers before submitting.
3. Make sure you've filled out all required fields.
4. Submit your completed form once you're satisfied with your answers.
