# Electrical Minor Works Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and certify the completion of electrical minor works. It is designed to be used by electrical inspectors or certifiers to provide a record of their findings and certification after inspecting electrical work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and questions.
2. Select the relevant options for the works completed.
3. Fill in the description of the minor works in a clear and concise manner.
4. Select the relevant options for the type of works and electrical certificate required.
5. Enter the date of completion in the correct format.
6. Enter the name of the certifier.
7. Add your signature to confirm your certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Minor Works Description**: This field is used to describe the minor works completed.
* **Works Type**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Electrical Certificate**: Select the relevant option for the electrical certificate required.
* **Date of Completion**: Enter the date when the minor works were completed in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Name of Certifier**: Enter the name of the certifier who is signing this form.
* **Signature**: Add your signature to confirm your certification.
* **Tips**:
	+ Ensure all fields are completed correctly before signing the form.
	+ Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
	+ Keep a record of all completed forms for future reference.
