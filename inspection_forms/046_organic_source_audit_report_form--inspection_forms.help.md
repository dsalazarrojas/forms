# Organic Source Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Source Audit Report Form is a structured form designed to collect information related to the organic source audit report. It's meant to be filled out by suppliers to report their organic sources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier information in the "supplier_info" field.
2. Provide details about the audit in the "audit_info" field.
3. Attach any supporting evidence in the "evidence" field.
4. Describe your findings in the "findings" field.
5. Offer recommendations in the "recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier_info** (`supplier_info`, text, required: false): Enter your supplier information, such as your business name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **audit_info** (`audit_info`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the audit you've conducted, including any relevant dates, locations, and results.
* **evidence** (`evidence`, text, required: false): Attach any supporting evidence, such as photos, videos, or documents, that demonstrate your organic sources.
* **findings** (`findings`, text, required: false): Describe your findings from the audit, including any successes, failures, or areas for improvement.
* **recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required: false): Offer suggestions or recommendations for improvement based on your audit findings.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are filling this form to report your own organic sources.
* Attach supporting evidence where applicable.
* Be as detailed as possible in your supplier information, audit description, findings, and recommendations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
