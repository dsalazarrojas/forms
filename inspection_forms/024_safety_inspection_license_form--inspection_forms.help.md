# Safety Inspection License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document safety inspections and record important details about the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name and contact information (page 1)
2. Enter the inspection date and time (page 1)
3. Answer the question about whether safety standards were met (page 1)
4. Enter any corrective actions taken as a result of the inspection (page 1)
5. Enter the name of the assigned inspector (page 1)
6. Sign and date the form (page 1)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business being inspected.
* **Enter Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the business.
* **Enter Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the business.
* **Enter Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the business.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Safety Standards Met** (`safety_standards_met`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if safety standards were met, 'No' if they were not.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text, optional): Enter any details about the inspection results.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, optional): Enter any corrective actions taken as a result of the inspection.
* **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned inspector.
* **Inspector Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
