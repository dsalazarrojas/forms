# Basement Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basement Inspection Survey is a form designed to collect information about the condition and characteristics of a basement, including its property address, owner's name, inspection date, and various aspects such as foundation, moisture, drainage, structural components, systems, and safety issues. This form is intended to aid inspectors in conducting thorough and accurate assessments of the basement's condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Basement Inspection Survey, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information and resources before starting the inspection.
* Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
* Answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required" tag.
* If unsure or unable to determine an answer, mark it accordingly (e.g., "Not Applicable" or "Unable to determine").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property address** (1: property_address, text, required): Enter the exact address of the property being inspected.
* **Property owner name** (2: property_owner, text, required): Enter the name of the property's owner.
* **Property owner phone** (3: property_phone, text, optional): Enter the property owner's phone number (if available).
* **Inspection date** (4: inspection_date, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector name** (5: inspector_name, text, required): Enter your name or identification as the inspector.
* **Foundation type** (6: foundation_type, select_one, required): Select the type of foundation for the basement.
	+ **Concrete Slab**: A foundation built using a concrete slab.
	+ **Pier and Beam**: A foundation built using piers and a beam system.
	+ **Full Basement**: A fully developed basement with walls and a floor.
	+ **Crawlspace**: A partially developed basement with limited headroom.
	+ **Other**: Choose this option if the foundation type is not listed.
* **Foundation condition** (7: foundation_condition, select_one, required): Assess the overall condition of the foundation.
	+ **Excellent**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Good**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Significant issues or damage.
	+ **Severely Damaged**: Critical issues or damage.
* **Are any cracks visible?** (8: cracks_visible, select_one, required): Determine if cracks are present in the foundation.
	+ **None**: No visible cracks.
	+ **Minor**: Minor cracks present.
	+ **Moderate**: Moderate cracks present.
	+ **Severe**: Severe cracks present.
* **Any signs of moisture or water damage?** (9: moisture_issues, select_one, required): Determine if there are any signs of moisture or water damage in the basement.
	+ **None**: No signs of moisture or water damage.
	+ **Minor Staining**: Minor signs of staining or discoloration.
	+ **Moderate Damage**: Moderate signs of moisture or water damage.
	+ **Severe Flooding History**: Severe signs of moisture or water damage.
* **Drainage system condition** (10: drainage_system, select_one, required): Assess the overall condition of the drainage system.
	+ **Good**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Adequate**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **None Visible**: No signs of damage.
* **Floor condition** (11: floors_condition, select_one, required): Assess the overall condition of the floors.
	+ **Excellent**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Good**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Significant issues or damage.
* **Wall condition** (12: walls_condition, select_one, required): Assess the overall condition of the walls.
	+ **Excellent**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Good**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Significant issues or damage.
* **Ceiling condition** (13: ceiling_condition, select_one, required): Assess the overall condition of the ceiling.
	+ **Excellent**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Good**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Significant issues or damage.
* **HVAC equipment present in basement?** (14: hvac_present, select_one, optional): Determine if HVAC equipment is present in the basement.
	+ **True**: HVAC equipment is present.
	+ **False**: HVAC equipment is not present.
* **HVAC condition (if applicable)** (15: hvac_condition, select_one, optional): Assess the condition of the HVAC equipment.
	+ **Good**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Not Applicable**: Not applicable as no HVAC equipment is present.
* **Electrical panel present?** (16: electrical_present, select_one, optional): Determine if an electrical panel is present in the basement.
	+ **True**: Electrical panel is present.
	+ **False**: Electrical panel is not present.
* **Electrical panel condition** (17: electrical_condition, select_one, optional): Assess the condition of the electrical panel.
	+ **Safe**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Issues Present**: Issues or damage present.
	+ **Not Applicable**: Not applicable as no electrical panel is present.
* **Plumbing present in basement?** (18: plumbing_present, select_one, optional): Determine if plumbing is present in the basement.
	+ **True**: Plumbing is present.
	+ **False**: Plumbing is not present.
* **Overall basement assessment** (19: overall_assessment, select_one, required): Provide an overall assessment of the basement's condition.
	+ **Excellent**: No issues or damage.
	+ **Good**: Minor issues or damage.
	+ **Fair**: Noticeable issues or damage.
	+ **Poor**: Significant issues or damage.
	+ **Requires Repairs**: Requires immediate repairs or attention.

# Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* If unsure or unable to determine an answer, mark it accordingly.
* Take your time and review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Remember to include any additional comments or observations in the designated fields.
