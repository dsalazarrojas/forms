# Structural Safety Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for requesting a structural safety review of a building or structure. It allows users to provide necessary information about the inspection request, building details, and related documents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Inspection Request page with any relevant information about the inspection you need.
2. On the Building Information page, enter details about the building, including its type and status.
3. On the Inspection Concerns page, list any notable issues with the building's structure.
4. If you have any supporting documents, upload them on the Uploaded Documents page.
5. On the Contact Information page, provide your contact details so the inspector can get in touch with you.
6. On the Manager Information page, enter the manager's name and any relevant details.
7. On the Property Type page, select the type of building being inspected.
8. On the Structural Status page, select the building's current structural status.
9. On the Uploaded Documents 2 page, select any additional documents relevant to the inspection.
10. If you have a preferred inspection date and time, enter them on their respective pages.
11. On the Assigned Inspector page, choose the inspector assigned to the job.
12. Add any notes about the inspection on the Notes page.
13. On the Inspection Status page, select the current status of the inspection.
14. On the Inspection Comments page, provide any comments or observations about the inspection.
15. If you have more documents to upload, go to the Uploaded Documents 3 page.
16. On the Submission Date and Time page, enter the date and time you submitted the request.
17. On the Manager Status page, notify the manager about the submission.
18. On the Uploaded Documents 4 page, select any additional documents related to the submission.
19. On the Assigned Tool page, choose the tool or equipment used for the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Request**: Provide a brief description of the inspection request. This will help the inspector understand what you need them to review.
* **building_info**: Enter details about the building, including its type and status.
* **inspection_concerns**: List any notable issues with the building's structure that you'd like the inspector to look into.
* **uploaded_documents**: This is for uploading relevant documents related to the inspection, such as photos, videos, or reports.
* **contact_info**: Provide your contact details so the inspector can get in touch with you.
* **manager_name**: Enter the manager's name and any relevant details.
* **manager_info**: Enter any additional manager information, such as their contact details.
* **property_type**: Select the type of building being inspected (e.g. residential, commercial, industrial).
* **structural_status**: Select the current structural status of the building (e.g. active, passive, active-passive, other).
* **uploaded_documents_2**: Select any additional documents relevant to the inspection.
* **inspection_date**: Enter the preferred date for the inspection.
* **inspection_time**: Enter the preferred time for the inspection.
* **assigned_inspector**: Choose the inspector assigned to the job.
* **notes**: Add any notes or observations about the inspection.
* **inspection_status**: Select the current status of the inspection (e.g. pending, in progress, completed).
* **inspection_comments**: Provide any comments or observations about the inspection.
* **uploaded_documents_3**: Select any additional documents related to the inspection.
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date and time you submitted the request.
* **submitted_by**: Choose who submitted the request (e.g. facility manager, property owner, maintenance personnel).
* **manager_status**: Notify the manager about the submission.
* **uploaded_documents_4**: Select any additional documents related to the submission.
* **assigned_tool**: Choose the tool or equipment used for the inspection.
* **submitted_time**: Enter the time you submitted the request.

Please note that this guide is based on the assumption that the form is for creating new requests, as indicated by the required fields. If this form is for editing existing requests, please adjust the guide accordingly.
