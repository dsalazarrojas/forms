# School Cafeteria Sanitation Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct regular inspections of the school cafeteria's sanitation and cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Date of Inspection: Enter the date of the inspection.
2. Inspector Name: Enter your name as the inspector.
3. Location: Enter the name of the location being inspected (e.g. "School Cafeteria").
4. Food Temperature: Enter the temperature of food items, if applicable.
5. Hand Washing Frequency: Enter the frequency of hand washing observed.
6. Trash Can Contents: Describe the contents of the trash cans.
7. Floor Cleanliness: Assess the floor cleanliness and choose the correct option.
8. Equipment Condition: Assess the condition of equipment and choose the correct option.
9. Food Handling: Describe the food handling practices observed.
10. Personal Hygiene: Describe the personal hygiene of staff members.
11. Pest Control: Describe the pest control measures in place.
12. Waste Disposal: Describe the waste disposal practices.
13. Food Storage: Describe the food storage practices.
14. Pest Control Method: Describe the method of pest control used.
15. Pest Control Frequency: Describe the frequency of pest control inspections.
16. Last Pest Control Inspection Date: Enter the date of the last pest control inspection.
17. Next Inspection Date: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
18. Inspector Signature: Sign your name as the inspector.
19. Comments: Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
20. Notes: Enter any additional notes or observations.
