# Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist is a form used to assess and document hydrocarbon storage site operations, responses to spills, staff training, observations, and corrective actions. This form ensures that all required checks are performed and recorded to improve site safety and operational efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Hydrocarbon Storage section (page 1) with any information about the hydrocarbon storage on site.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" for the Spill Response question to indicate whether a spill response was performed.
3. In the Staff Training section, describe any training provided to staff related to hydrocarbon safety.
4. In the Observations section, enter any relevant comments or notes about the site's hydrocarbon operations.
5. In the Corrective Actions section, describe any corrective actions taken or planned to address any identified hydrocarbon-related issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hydrocarbon Storage (Page 1)** (`hydrocarbon_storage`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the hydrocarbon storage site, including any relevant details such as storage type, size, and location.
* **Spill Response (Page 1)** (`spill_response`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the box if a spill response was performed, and choose all that apply: 
  - Yes
  - No
* **Staff Training (Page 1)** (`staff_training`, text, required: false): Describe any training provided to staff related to hydrocarbon safety, including the type of training and the date completed.
* **Observations (Page 1)** (`observations`, note, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the site's hydrocarbon operations, including any observations or concerns.
* **Corrective Actions (Page 1)** (`corrective_actions`, text, required: false): Describe any corrective actions taken or planned to address any identified hydrocarbon-related issues, including any actions taken or planned and their completion dates.
