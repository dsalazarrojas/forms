# Skid Steer Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skid Steer Equipment Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the condition and status of Skid Steer equipment. This form helps to ensure that the equipment is properly maintained and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the equipment, including its ID and location.
2. Report the Skid Steer's current condition, using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Indicate if any issues were found during inspection (True or False).
4. Add any specific issue details if applicable.
5. Record the date of the inspection.
6. Specify who inspected the equipment (Inspected By).
7. Identify the person assigned to address any issues (Assigned To).
8. Mention who assigned the task to address issues (Assigned By).
9. Update the inspection status (Open, Closed, Reported).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1, general_info, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its ID and location.
* **Skid Steer Information** (2, skid_steer_info, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the Skid Steer, such as its make, model, or serial number.
* **Condition** (3, condition, required/optional): Rate the condition of the Skid Steer using the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Issues Found** (4, issues_found, required/optional): Indicate if any issues were found during inspection (True or False).
* **Notes** (5, notes, required/optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Issue Details** (6, issue_details, required/optional): Provide more information about any issues found during inspection.
* **Date** (7, inspection_date, required/optional): Record the date of the inspection.
* **Inspected By** (8, inspected_by, required/optional): Specify who inspected the equipment (Inspected By).
* **Assigned To** (9, assigned_to, required/optional): Identify the person assigned to address any issues (Assigned To).
* **Assigned By** (10, assigned_by, required/optional): Mention who assigned the task to address issues (Assigned By).
* **Status** (11, inspection_status, required/optional): Update the inspection status (Open, Closed, Reported).
