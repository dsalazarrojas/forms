# Stormwater Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about stormwater system inspections. The form helps to gather details about the status of stormwater systems, including their functionality and any issues that may be present.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1**: Enter your name and title in the "Stormwater Inspection Form Submitted By" field. This is the field where you'll add your name and title.
- **Step 2**: Click on "Stormwater System Status" dropdown menu to select the status of the stormwater system (functioning well, functioning badly, or not functioning at all). 
- **Step 3**: Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date Of Inspection" field. 
- **Step 4**: Add any relevant notes or comments in the "Notes" field. 
- **Step 5**: Select the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field. 
- **Step 6**: Add any comments or notes related to the assigned tool used for the inspection in the "Assigned Tool" field. 
- **Step 7**: Enter your email address in the "Email" field. 
- **Step 8**: Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stormwater System Status** (`Stormwater System Status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the stormwater system. Options are: 
	+ Stormwater system is functioning well
	+ Stormwater system is functioning badly
	+ Stormwater system is not functioning at all
* **Date Of Inspection** (`Date Of Inspection`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes or comments related to the inspection.
* **Inspection Location** (`Inspection Location`, text, required: false): Select the location of the inspection.
* **Stormwater Inspection Form Submitted By** (`Stormwater Inspection Form Submitted By`, text, required: false): Enter your name and title.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required: false): Add any comments or notes related to the assigned tool used for the inspection.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required information accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options in the "Stormwater System Status" field to accurately describe the status of the system.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to relevant guidelines or resources.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
