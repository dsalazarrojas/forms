# Lawn Mower Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information on the condition and maintenance of a lawn mower, helping users identify potential issues and determine the need for maintenance or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your actual observation of the lawn mower's condition.
2. Be as accurate as possible when answering each question.
3. Fill out the form thoroughly to get an accurate assessment of the lawn mower.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mower Model** (`mower_model`, `text`, optional): Enter the model name of the lawn mower you are inspecting.
* **Blade Condition** (`blade_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the condition of the lawn mower blades, such as sharpness, rust, or damage.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, `text`, optional): Assess the engine's overall condition, including signs of oil leaks, unusual noises, or overheating.
* **Oil Level** (`oil_level`, `text`, optional): Check the oil level in the engine and note any discrepancies.
* **Air Filter** (`air_filter`, `text`, optional): Check the air filter's condition and note any signs of clogging or excessive dirt buildup.
* **Oil Filter** (`oil_filter`, `text`, optional): Check the oil filter's condition and note any signs of clogging or excessive dirt buildup.
* **Spark Plugs** (`spark_plugs`, `text`, optional): Inspect the spark plugs for any signs of wear or fouling.
* **Drive System** (`drive_system`, `text`, optional): Check the drive system's condition, including any signs of wear or unusual noises.
* **Drive Tires** (`drive_tires`, `text`, optional): Inspect the drive tires for any signs of wear or uneven wear patterns.
* **Mower Tires** (`mower_tires`, `text`, optional): Inspect the mower tires for any signs of wear or uneven wear patterns.
* **Cutting System** (`cutting_system`, `text`, optional): Check the cutting system's condition, including any signs of wear or unusual noises.
* **Mower Condition** (`mower_condition`, `text`, optional): Assess the overall condition of the lawn mower, including any signs of rust, dents, or other damage.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, `text`, optional): Record any scheduled maintenance or repairs for the lawn mower.
* **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`, `text`, optional): Note the date and details of the last maintenance or repair performed on the lawn mower.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, `text`, optional): Specify the recommended maintenance frequency for the lawn mower, based on the manufacturer's recommendations or your personal experience.

## Tips

* Be thorough in your inspection to get an accurate assessment of the lawn mower's condition.
* Take note of any issues or concerns you notice during the inspection.
* Use the information collected to determine the need for maintenance or repairs.
* Keep the form for future reference or share it with authorized personnel for further action.
