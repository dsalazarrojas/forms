# School Bus Pre Trip Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist school bus drivers in conducting a pre-trip inspection of their vehicle. It helps drivers identify potential safety issues and ensures that all necessary checks are completed before embarking on a trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the driver information section, providing your name, driver's license number, and contact information.
2. Provide details about the vehicle, including its make, model, and license plate number.
3. Go through each section of the form, inspecting the vehicle according to the specified checks.
4. Record your findings and observations in the corresponding text fields.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Information** (`driver_info`, text, required): Provide your name, driver's license number, and contact information to identify yourself as the driver.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and license plate number for easy identification.
* **Exterior** (`exterior`, text, required): Inspect the bus's exterior for any visible damage, dents, or scratches. Check for any obstructions, such as snow or debris, on the windshield and windows.
* **Interior** (`interior`, text, required): Inspect the interior of the bus, including seats, floors, and aisles. Check for any damage, cleanliness, or issues with the interior components.
* **Safety** (`safety`, text, required): Check the vehicle's safety features, including seatbelts, emergency exits, and safety equipment.
* **Fire Suppression** (`fire_suppression`, text, required): Inspect the fire suppression system, including the fire extinguisher, hoses, and nozzles.
* **Electrical** (`electrical`, text, required): Check the electrical systems, including lights, batteries, and charging systems.
* **Mechanical** (`mechanical`, text, required): Inspect the mechanical systems, including the engine, transmission, and axles.
* **Tires** (`tires`, text, required): Check the tire condition, tread depth, and pressure.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, text, required): Inspect the brake system, including the condition and functionality of the brake pads, lines, and rotors.
* **Lighting** (`lighting`, text, required): Check the vehicle's lighting systems, including headlights, taillights, and interior lights.
* **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, text, required): Inspect the emergency equipment, including first aid kits, fire extinguishers, and other safety gear.
* **Seatbelts** (`seatbelts`, text, required): Check the seatbelt condition and functionality.
* **First Aid** (`firstaid`, text, required): Inspect the first aid equipment, including kits, supplies, and equipment.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required): Check the communication equipment, including radios, sirens, and intercom systems.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, text, required): Review the vehicle's documentation, including maintenance records, inspection reports, and driver logs.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, text, required): Inspect the additional safety equipment, including harnesses, nets, and other gear.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, required): Inspect the fire extinguishers, including the condition and functionality.
* **First Aid Equipment** (`firstaid_equipment`, text, required): Inspect the first aid equipment, including supplies, kits, and equipment.
* **Communication Equipment** (`communication_equipment`, text, required): Inspect the communication equipment, including radios, sirens, and intercom systems.
* **Additional Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment2`, text, required): Check for any additional safety equipment, including harnesses, nets, and other gear.

## Tips
* Use this form as a guide to standardize the inspection process.
* Be thorough and detailed in your inspection, checking for any issues or concerns.
* Record your findings and observations in the corresponding text fields.
* If you find any issues or concerns, address them before embarking on the trip.
* Keep the form up to date and accurate by reviewing and updating it regularly.
