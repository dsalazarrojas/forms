# VOC Control Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the Voice of the Customer (VOC) control, which is a critical component of customer feedback and satisfaction assessment. The VOC control helps to identify areas where customers are satisfied or dissatisfied with our services. The form is designed to collect data from customers on their experiences, preferences, and expectations, allowing us to make informed decisions and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully** and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. **Select the relevant information** for each field from the options provided.
3. **Fill in the required fields**, which are marked as `required` in the form.
4. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **voc_control_form_data** (`voc_control_form_data`, text, required: false): This field collects any additional data or information that is not covered by the other fields. Please provide a brief description or explanation of the customer's experience or concern.
* **voc_type** (`voc_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of control that best describes the customer's experience. This will help us understand the nature of the control.
* **sources** (`sources`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the sources that contributed to the control. This will help us identify the root causes of the control.
* **controls** (`controls`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the controls that were involved in the control.
* **voc_control_status** (`voc_control_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the control, which is an important factor in determining our next steps.
* **compliance_status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the compliance status, which is also an important factor in determining our next steps.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the control.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each field.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
