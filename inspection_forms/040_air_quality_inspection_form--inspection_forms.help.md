# Air Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Quality Inspection Form is used to collect data on the ambient air quality conditions in a specific location. This form is designed for certified inspectors to conduct thorough inspections and identify potential air quality issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the inspector's name in the Inspector Name field.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the Inspection Date field.
3. Set the time of the inspection in the Time field.
4. Specify the location where the inspection was conducted in the Location / Room field.
5. Record the temperature reading in the Temperature (F) field.
6. Record the relative humidity reading in the Relative Humidity (%) field.
7. Record the CO2 level reading in the CO2 Level (ppm) field.
8. Record the TVOC level reading in the TVOC Level (ppb) field.
9. Record the PM2.5 and PM10 level readings in the respective fields.
10. Check the mold presence in the Mold Presence field.
11. Check the ventilation status in the Ventilation Status field.
12. Describe any odor detection in the Odor Detection field.
13. Enter any recommendations for improvement in the Recommendations field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name (`inspector_name`)**: Enter your name as a certified inspector.
* **Inspection Date (`inspection_date`)**: Enter the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time (`inspection_time`)**: Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format.
* **Location / Room (`location`)**: Specify the location where the inspection was conducted, including the specific room or area tested.
* **Temperature (F) (`temperature`)**: Record the ambient temperature reading in Fahrenheit.
* **Relative Humidity (%) (`humidity`)**: Record the relative humidity reading in percentage.
* **CO2 Level (ppm) (`co2_level`)**: Record the CO2 level reading in parts per million.
* **TVOC Level (ppb) (`voc_level`)**: Record the TVOC level reading in parts per billion.
* **PM2.5 Level (ug/m3) (`pm25_level`)**: Record the PM2.5 level reading in micrograms per cubic meter.
* **PM10 Level (ug/m3) (`pm10_level`)**: Record the PM10 level reading in micrograms per cubic meter.
* **Mold Presence (`mold_check`)**: Check if there is mold presence or absence.
* **Ventilation Status (`ventilation_status`)**: Check the ventilation status as Good / Adequate, Poor / Stagnant, or Blocked Vents.
* **Odor Detection (`odor_detection`)**: Describe any odor detection during the inspection.
* **Recommendations (`recommendations`)**: Enter any recommendations for improvement.
