# Department Condition Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for evaluating the condition of a department during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Department Condition Assessment form, follow these steps:

1. **Department Location**: Enter the department's location in the text box provided.
2. **Condition Assessment**: Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the department is in good working condition or not.
3. **Facilities Equipment**: Select any applicable facilities and equipment from the dropdown list.
4. **Operations**: Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the department's operations are in good working condition or not.
5. **Department Leads**: Select any applicable department leads from the dropdown list.
6. **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. **Time of Inspection**: If desired, enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
8. **Comments and Notes**: Use this field to record any comments or notes about the inspection.
9. **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the inspection results.
10. **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the inspection results.
11. **Submission Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the submission.
12. **Submitter**: Enter your name or title to identify yourself as the submitter.
13. **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
14. **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the review was completed in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
15. **Time Reviewed**: If desired, enter the time the review was completed in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
16. **Comments Reviewed**: Use this field to record any comments or notes from the reviewer.
17. **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the reviewer.
18. **Review Status**: Select either "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the review.
19. **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the submission.
20. **Submission Status**: Select either "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the submission.
21. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the submission.
22. **Reviewer ID**: Enter the ID of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
23. **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
24. **Department**: Enter the name of the department being assessed.
25. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the department's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department Location**: The location of the department, such as its floor, room, or office number.
* **Condition Assessment**: A yes/no indicator of the department's condition.
* **Facilities Equipment**: A list of facilities and equipment in the department, such as chairs, tables, or computers.
* **Operations**: A yes/no indicator of the department's operational condition.
* **Department Leads**: A list of department leads, such as managers or team members.
* **Date of Inspection**: The date the inspection was performed, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Inspection**: The time the inspection was performed, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Comments and Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Email**: Your email address if you want to be contacted about the inspection results.
* **Phone**: Your phone number if you want to be contacted about the inspection results.
* **Submission Comments**: Any additional comments about the submission.
* **Submitter**: Your name or title, to identify yourself as the submitter.
* **Reviewer**: The name of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
* **Date Reviewed**: The date the review was completed, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time Reviewed**: The time the review was completed, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Comments Reviewed**: Any comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Review Status**: The status of the review, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Submitter Comments**: Any additional comments about the submission.
* **Submission Status**: The status of the submission, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Comments**: Any additional comments about the submission.
* **Reviewer ID**: The ID of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
* **Reviewer Name**: The name of the reviewer who assessed the department's condition.
* **Department**: The name of the department being assessed.
* **Notes**: Any additional notes about the department's condition.
