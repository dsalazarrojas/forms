# Hygiene Compliance Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track any issues or non-compliance found during daily hygiene compliance checks in a specific area.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the area of the facility or location that was checked (Area Field).
2. Enter the time of the daily hygiene compliance check (Time of Day Field).
3. If any issues or non-compliance were found, describe the problem in the Issue Field.
4. If necessary, describe the corrective action taken to resolve the issue in the Corrective Action Field.
5. Indicate the status of the check, whether "On Schedule", "Off Schedule", or "Unannounced" (Time Check Field).
6. Record the date and time of the supervisor's verification in the Supervisor Verification Field.
7. Sign and date the form with your supervisor's signature and name (Supervisor Signature and Supervisor Name Fields).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Area**: Describe the area of the facility or location that was checked.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of the daily hygiene compliance check.
* **Issue**: Describe any issues or non-compliance found during the check.
* **Corrective Action**: Provide details on any actions taken to address the issue.
* **Time Check**: Indicate the status of the check: "On Schedule", "Off Schedule", or "Unannounced".
* **Supervisor Verification**: Record the date and time of the supervisor's verification.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the check was performed.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor who performed the check.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and comprehensively to maintain a reliable record of hygiene compliance checks.
* Double-check that the correct area and time are entered to ensure the correct information is recorded.
* If no issues or non-compliance were found, indicate this in the Issue Field.
* The Supervisor Verification, Supervisor Signature, and Supervisor Name fields are for the supervisor's validation and confirmation of the check.
