# industrial_hygiene_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of an industrial hygiene inspection. The form is designed to collect information on general conditions, chemical hazards, biological hazards, physical hazards, and control measures in place at the workplace. The form is intended for use by assigned inspectors to document their findings and observations during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant pages and questions for your inspection. Each page corresponds to a specific section of the form.
2. Answer each question based on your observations and findings during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Conditions** (`general_conditions`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the general conditions at the workplace. This includes observations on the overall condition of the facility, equipment, and work environment.
* **Chemical Hazards** (`chemical_hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Check all applicable options if you observe any chemical hazards present. This includes chemicals with known hazards, such as cleaning agents, paint fumes, or other substances that may pose a risk to employees.
* **Biological Hazards** (`biological_hazards`, text, optional): Describe any biological hazards observed during the inspection, such as bacteria, viruses, or other microorganisms.
* **Physical Hazards** (`physical_hazards`, select_one, optional): Check the option that best describes the level of physical hazard observed. This includes physical conditions such as slippery floors, uneven walkways, or other hazards that may cause injury.
* **Chemical Control Measures** (`chemical_control_measures`, text, optional): Describe any control measures in place to mitigate or mitigate chemical hazards.
* **Biological Control Measures** (`biological_control_measures`, text, optional): Describe any control measures in place to mitigate or mitigate biological hazards.
* **Physical Control Measures** (`physical_control_measures`, text, optional): Describe any control measures in place to mitigate or mitigate physical hazards.
* **General Comments** (`general_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection. This includes any concerns or recommendations you have for improvement.
* **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, select_one, optional): Select the person assigned to conduct this inspection from the list of available options.
