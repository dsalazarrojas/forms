# Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form is designed to facilitate regular inspections of fire extinguishers in your premises. It helps track the inspection results and provides a systematic approach to identifying any potential issues or maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the inspection frequency for the form (e.g., Every 6 months, Every 12 months, Every 24 months).
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Record the inspection result for each fire extinguisher (e.g., Pass, Fail, Partial).
4. Add any comments or notes for each fire extinguisher as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fire Extinguishers** (`Fire Extinguishers`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of fire extinguishers inspected on this form.
- **Inspection Frequency** (`Inspection Frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of the inspection (e.g., Every 6 months, Every 12 months, Every 24 months).
- **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Inspection Result** (`Inspection Result`, `select_multiple`, optional): Record the inspection result for each fire extinguisher (e.g., Pass, Fail, Partial). Choose all applicable results.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): Add any comments or notes for each fire extinguisher as needed.
