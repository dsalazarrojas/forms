# urban_fire_hydrant_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on urban fire hydrant inspections, allowing users to track and record the status of fire hydrants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location of the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Location` field.
2. Indicate the condition of the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Condition` field. You can select multiple conditions as needed.
3. Choose the inspection frequency for the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Inspection Frequency` field.
4. Enter the date of the inspection in the `Fire Hydrant Inspection Date` field.
5. Enter the time of the inspection in the `Fire Hydrant Inspection Time` field.
6. Indicate the status of the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Status` field.
7. Add any additional notes about the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Notes` field.
8. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection in the `Fire Hydrant Inspector` field.
9. Enter the department responsible for the fire hydrant in the `Fire Hydrant Department` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Urban Fire Hydrant Inspection**:
	+ This field is the title of the form and is not a field that needs to be filled in.
* **Fire Hydrant Location**:
	+ The location where the fire hydrant is situated.
* **Fire Hydrant Condition**:
	+ Check boxes to indicate the condition of the fire hydrant, such as whether it is working properly or if there are any issues.
* **Fire Hydrant Inspection Frequency**:
	+ Choose how often the fire hydrant is inspected, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Fire Hydrant Inspection Date**:
	+ Enter the date of the last inspection of the fire hydrant.
* **Fire Hydrant Inspection Time**:
	+ Enter the time of the last inspection of the fire hydrant.
* **Fire Hydrant Status**:
	+ Indicate the status of the fire hydrant, such as whether it is active or inactive.
* **Fire Hydrant Notes**:
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the fire hydrant.
* **Fire Hydrant Inspector**:
	+ Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.
* **Fire Hydrant Department**:
	+ Enter the department responsible for the fire hydrant.
