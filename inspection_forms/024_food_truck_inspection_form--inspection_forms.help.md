# Food Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from food truck operators for inspection purposes. It includes fields for vehicle details, status, location, equipment, hygiene practices, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Food Truck Information" section, providing the food truck's details such as its location and license status.
2. Select the current status of the food truck from the options provided in the "Food Truck Status" field.
3. Provide the name of the food truck's equipment in the "Food Truck Equipment" field.
4. Inspect the food truck's hygiene practices and select the corresponding option in the "Food Truck Hygiene" field.
5. Check the food truck's temperature control and select the corresponding option in the "Temperature Control" field.
6. Inspect the refrigeration units and select the corresponding option in the "Refrigeration" field.
7. Enter the name of the food truck inspector in the "Food Truck Inspector" field.
8. Provide the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the inspection in the "Food Truck Notes" field.
10. Provide the contact email and phone number of the food truck operator in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields respectively.
11. Enter the address of the food truck in the "Food Truck Address" field.
12. Enter any status updates about the food truck in the "Food Truck Status Update" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Truck Information (Text)**: Enter the food truck's details such as its location and license status.
* **Food Truck Status (Select One)**: Select the current status of the food truck from the options provided.
* **Food Truck Location (Text)**: Provide the name of the food truck's location.
* **Food Truck License (Select Multiple)**: Select the license status of the food truck from the options provided.
* **Food Truck Equipment (Text)**: List all the equipment used in the food truck.
* **Food Truck Hygiene (Select One)**: Inspect the food truck's hygiene practices and select the corresponding option.
* **Temperature Control (Select One)**: Check the food truck's temperature control and select the corresponding option.
* **Refrigeration (Select One)**: Inspect the food truck's refrigeration units and select the corresponding option.
* **Food Truck Inspector (Text)**: Enter the name of the food truck inspector.
* **Inspection Date (Date)**: Provide the date of the inspection.
* **Food Truck Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Contact Email (Email)**: Enter the email of the food truck operator.
* **Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the food truck operator.
* **Food Truck Address (Text)**: Provide the address of the food truck.
* **Food Truck Status Update (Text)**: Enter any status updates about the food truck.
