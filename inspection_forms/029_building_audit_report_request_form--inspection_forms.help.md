# Building Audit Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Building Audit Report Request Form is a comprehensive tool used to collect information from requestors for a building audit report. This form helps the audit team to plan and prepare for the audit process by gathering essential details about the building, the requestor, and the desired scope and format of the report.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2.  Provide your full name and contact information for the audit team to reach out to you.
3.  Indicate your job title and the name of the organization or company you represent.
4.  Specify your contact email address and phone number for the audit team to contact you.
5.  Provide the requested date of the audit and the date you prefer for the report delivery.
6.  Indicate the name of the building to be audited and its full address.
7.  Select the type of building (e.g., office, residential, industrial, etc.).
8.  Enter the year the building was constructed and its total square footage.
9.  Indicate the number of floors in the building.
10.  Select the type of audit required (e.g., energy, structural, safety, etc.).
11.  Specify the areas to be audited (e.g., building envelope, HVAC systems, etc.).
12.  Choose the reason for the audit (e.g., regulatory requirement, property transaction, etc.).
13.  Provide any specific concerns you have about the building (e.g., performance issues, etc.).
14.  Indicate when the building is accessible for the audit (e.g., during work hours, etc.).
15.  Specify who will be available on-site during the audit (e.g., key contact person).
16.  Provide the contact phone number of the on-site key contact person.
17.  Indicate whether you have had previous audits and when they were performed.
18.  Specify what documentation is available for the audit team to review.
19.  Choose the preferred format for the report (e.g., digital PDF, printed, etc.).
20.  Select the turnaround time for the report (e.g., standard 2 weeks, expedited 1 week, etc.).
21.  Choose an estimated budget range for the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Requestor Name** (1): Enter your full name.
*   **Job Title** (2): Provide your job title and position.
*   **Organization** (3): Enter your company or organization name.
*   **Email Address** (4): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (5): Provide your contact phone number.
*   **Request Date** (6): Enter the date of the request for the audit.
*   **Building Name** (7): Enter the name of the building to be audited.
*   **Building Address** (8): Enter the full address of the building.
*   **Building Type** (9): Select the type of building (e.g., office, residential, etc.).
*   **Year Built** (10): Enter the year the building was constructed (optional).
*   **Total Square Footage** (11): Enter the total square footage of the building.
*   **Number of Floors** (12): Indicate the total number of floors in the building.
*   **Audit Type** (13): Select the type of audit required (e.g., energy, structural, etc.).
*   **Audit Scope** (14): Specify the areas to be audited (e.g., building envelope, HVAC systems, etc.).
*   **Reason for Audit** (15): Choose the reason for the audit (e.g., regulatory requirement, property transaction, etc.).
*   **Specific Concerns** (16): Enter any specific concerns or issues about the building (optional).
*   **Preferred Date** (17): Enter the preferred date for the report delivery.
*   **Access Availability** (18): Indicate when the building is accessible (e.g., during work hours, etc.).
*   **On-site Key Contact** (19): Specify who will be available on-site during the audit.
*   **Key Contact Phone** (20): Enter the contact phone number of the on-site key contact person.
*   **Previous Audits** (21): Indicate whether you have had previous audits and when they were performed.
*   **Documentation Available** (22): Specify what documentation is available for the audit team to review.
*   **Report Format** (23): Choose the preferred format for the report (e.g., digital PDF, printed, etc.).
*   **Turnaround Time** (24): Select the turnaround time for the report (e.g., standard 2 weeks, expedited 1 week, etc.).
*   **Budget Range** (25): Choose an estimated budget range for the audit (optional).
*   **Additional Comments** (26): Enter any additional information or comments about the building or audit (optional).
