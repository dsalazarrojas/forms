# Fire Alarm Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide you through the inspection of a fire alarm system. It includes checks on the status of fire alarm connections, sensors, controllers, panels, wiring, maintenance, testing, standards compliance, inspection frequency, system inspection dates, and other relevant details. By filling out this form, you can ensure that your fire alarm system is functioning correctly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant status for each field from the given options. Be sure to check each field carefully as they are marked as required.
2. For each field, provide a brief explanation of your findings or any issues you've encountered.
3. Fill in the signature and date sections after completing the form.
4. Review and verify the information you've entered to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Fire Alarm Connection Status** (`fire_alarm_inspection_status`, select_one, required): Select whether the fire alarm connection is working or not working.
* **Check Fire Alarm Sensors Status** (`fire_alarm_sensors_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of each fire alarm sensor, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Controllers Status** (`fire_alarm_controllers_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the fire alarm controller, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Panels Status** (`fire_alarm_panels_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the fire alarm panel, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Controllers and Panels Connection Status** (`fire_alarm_controllers_panel_connection_status`, select_one, required): Select the connection status of the fire alarm controller and panel, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Wiring Status** (`fire_alarm_wiring_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the fire alarm wiring, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Maintenance Status** (`fire_alarm_maintenance_status`, select_one, required): Select the maintenance status of the fire alarm system, whether it's working, not working, or unknown.
* **Check Fire Alarm Testing Status** (`fire_alarm_testing_status`, select_one, required): Select the testing status of the fire alarm system, whether it's completed, in progress, or not tested.
* **Check Fire Alarm Standards Compliance Status** (`fire_alarm_standards_compliance_status`, select_one, required): Select the compliance status of the fire alarm system with standards, whether it's complete, partial, or not compliant.
* **Check Fire Alarm Inspection Frequency** (`fire_alarm_inspection_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the inspection frequency of the fire alarm system, whether it's monthly, quarterly, or semi-annually.
* **Check Fire Alarm System Last Inspection Date** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_last_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the last fire alarm system inspection.
* **Check Fire Alarm System Next Inspection Date** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_next_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the next fire alarm system inspection.
* **Check Fire Alarm System Inspection Duration** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_duration`, time, required): Enter the duration of the fire alarm system inspection.
* **Fire Alarm System Inspection Remark** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_remark`, text, required): Enter any remark or comments about the inspection.
* **Fire Alarm System Inspection Signature** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_signature`, text, required): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Fire Alarm System Inspection Signature Date** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Fire Alarm System Inspection Signature Time** (`fire_alarm_system_inspection_signature_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the signature.

By following this guide, you will be able to accurately and efficiently complete the fire alarm inspection checklist form.
