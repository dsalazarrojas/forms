# Public Park Playground Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Park Playground Equipment Inspection Form is used by park inspectors to record their observations and findings during regular inspections of public park playgrounds. The data collected will help park administrators identify maintenance needs, prioritize resource allocation, and improve the overall quality of the park's playgrounds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing general information about the playground being inspected, including its name and any relevant details.
2.  Describe the overall condition of the playground area, including any notable features or hazards.
3.  Report on the condition of specific equipment, including any issues or concerns.
4.  Assess the overall condition of equipment, selecting from the options provided.
5.  Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment or playground.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of the playground being inspected, or any other relevant details that will help identify it. For example, "Playground 'A' at Park 'X'".
* **Playground Area** (`playground_area`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the playground area, including any notable features or hazards you encountered.
* **Equipment Inspection** (`equipment_inspection`, text, optional): Write a brief description of the condition of the equipment at the playground, including any issues or concerns you found.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select the condition of the equipment, choosing from:
	+ **Damaged**: Equipment is in a state of disrepair, posing potential safety hazards.
	+ **Intact**: Equipment is in good condition, with no visible signs of wear or damage.
	+ **Not Present**: The equipment is missing or has been removed.
	+ **Needs Repair**: Equipment is in a state that requires maintenance or repair.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Use this field to capture any additional information you believe is relevant to the equipment or playground being inspected. This could include details on any repairs or improvements made, or any other observations you'd like to document.

## Tips

*   Make sure to be as detailed as possible when describing the condition of the equipment and playground area.
*   If a piece of equipment is missing or in a state that requires immediate attention, mark it as 'Not Present' or 'Needs Repair' respectively.
*   Use the 'Notes' field to record any additional information that may not fit into one of the other categories.
