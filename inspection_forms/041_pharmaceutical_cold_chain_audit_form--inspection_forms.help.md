# Pharmaceutical Cold Chain Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Cold Chain Audit Form is used to record and verify the storage conditions of pharmaceutical products. It ensures that the storage conditions meet the required standards for safe and effective storage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with the actual storage conditions.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Review and verify the information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Storage Conditions
* **Storage Conditions** (`storage_conditions`, text, required: false): Describe the storage conditions in detail, including the storage area, temperature, and humidity.
### 2. Storage Area
* **Storage Area** (`storage_area`, text, required: false): Enter the storage area where the product is kept.
### 3. Storage Temperature
* **Storage Temperature** (`storage_temperature`, number, required: false): Record the actual storage temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
### 4. Storage Humidity
* **Storage Humidity** (`storage_minimalist_assistant`, number, required: false): Note the storage humidity level.
### 5. Storage Area Conditioned
* **Storage Area Conditioned** (`storage_area_conditioned`, select_one, required: false): Select the appropriate option to indicate if the storage area is conditioned or not.
### 6. Storage Location
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, text, required: false): Enter the storage location where the product is kept.
### 7. Deviation Date
* **Deviation Date** (`deviation_date`, date, required: false): Record the date of any deviations or discrepancies found during storage.
### 8. Deviation Time
* **Deviation Time** (`deviation_time`, time, required: false): Record the time of any deviations or discrepancies found during storage.
### 9. Deviation Description
* **Deviation Description** (`deviation_description`, note, required: false): Describe any deviations or discrepancies found during storage.
### 10. Deviation Cause
* **Deviation Cause** (`deviation_cause`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the reasons for any deviations or discrepancies found during storage.
### 11. Deviation Resolution
* **Deviation Resolution** (`deviation_resolution`, text, required: false): Describe the resolution taken for any deviations or discrepancies found during storage.
### 12. Storage Personnel
* **Storage Personnel** (`storage_personnel`, select_one, required: false): Select the person responsible for storage.
### 13. Storage Equipment
* **Storage Equipment** (`storage_equipment`, text, required: false): Describe the equipment used for storage.
### 14. Storage Materials
* **Storage Materials** (`storage_materials`, text, required: false): Note any materials used for storage.
### 15. Storage Area Size
* **Storage Area Size** (`storage_area_size`, number, required: false): Record the size of the storage area.
### 16. Storage Area Conditioned Size
* **Storage Area Conditioned Size** (`storage_area_conditioned_size`, number, required: false): Record the size of the conditioned storage area.
### 17. Storage Location Size
* **Storage Location Size** (`storage_location_size`, number, required: false): Record the size of the storage location.
### 18. Storage Location Depth
* **Storage Location Depth** (`storage_location_depth`, number, required: false): Record the depth of the storage location.

## Tips
- Always fill out this form accurately and completely.
- Review and verify the information before submission.
- Keep a record of all storage conditions and deviations.
- Store the completed form securely and accessibly for future reference.
