# Church Background Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect background information from applicants for potential employment, volunteering, or other church-related activities. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure you have authorization to complete this form, as indicated in the internal instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (3, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Middle Name** (4, text, optional): Enter your middle name as it appears on your identification documents. (If you do not have a middle name, please leave this field blank.)
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (6, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Current Street Address** (9, text, required): Enter your current street address.
* **Current City** (10, text, required): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Current State or Province** (11, text, required): Enter the state or province where you currently reside.
* **Current ZIP or Postal Code** (12, text, required): Enter your current ZIP or postal code.
* **Years at Current Address** (13, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have resided at your current address.
* **Previous Address** (14, text, optional): Enter any previous address where you resided for 7+ years.
* **Position Title for Which You Are Applying** (16, text, required): Enter your current or desired position title.
* **Will You Have Direct Unsupervised Contact with Children** (17, select one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have direct contact with children, 'No' otherwise.
* **List Last Three Employers** (18, text, required): Enter your last three employers, including company name, position, and dates of employment.
* **Explain Any Employment Gaps** (19, text, optional): Enter any gaps in employment, if applicable.
* **Have You Ever Been Convicted of a Crime** (21, select one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have been convicted of a crime, 'No' otherwise.
* **Details of Any Convictions** (22, text, optional): Enter details of any convictions, if applicable.
* **Reference 1 Name** (24, text, required): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (25, text, required): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (26, text, optional): Enter the relationship between you and your first reference (e.g., former supervisor, colleague).
* **Reference 2 Name** (27, text, required): Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Phone** (28, text, required): Enter the phone number of your second reference.
* **I authorize the church to conduct a background check** (30, select one, required): Select 'Yes, I authorize' if you agree to the church conducting a background check.
* **I certify that all information provided is true and accurate** (31, select one, required): Select 'Yes, I certify' if all information is accurate and true. 

# Tips
1. Ensure you have authorization from the church administration before completing this form.
2. Answer all required fields to progress through the form.
3. Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
4. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact the church administration.
