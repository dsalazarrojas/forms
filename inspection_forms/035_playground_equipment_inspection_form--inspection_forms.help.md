# Playground Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playground Equipment Inspection Form is used to record the condition and inspection status of playground equipment. This form is completed by authorized personnel after inspecting the equipment and is used to determine its suitability for use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of equipment being inspected from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. Enter the name or initials of the person who conducted the inspection.
4. Enter any comments or notes from the inspection.
5. Enter the email address of the person who completed the inspection.
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person (if available).
7. Select the frequency at which the inspection is performed (e.g. Every 30 days).
8. Select the inspection status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
9. Indicate whether safety hazards were found (True or False).
10. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_one): Select the type of equipment being inspected (e.g. Playground Equipment, Playhouse, Swing Set, etc.).
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`, text): Enter the name or initials of the person who conducted the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Enter any comments or notes from the inspection.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the email address of the person who completed the inspection.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if available).
* **Equipment Count** (`equipment_count`, number): Enter the quantity of the inspected equipment.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, select_one): Select how often the inspection is performed (e.g. Every 30 days).
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, select_one): Select the inspection status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Safety Hazards Found** (`safety_hazards_found`, select_one): Indicate whether safety hazards were found during the inspection (True or False).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
