# Snow Removal Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to snow removal at a site for maintenance or inspection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3.  Enter your **Address** in the field provided.
4.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the field provided.
5.  Enter your **Email Address** in the field provided (optional).
6.  Enter your **Business Name** (if applicable).
7.  Select the **Date** of the inspection.
8.  Select the **Time** of the inspection.
9.  Enter any additional **Comments** you have.
10.  Select the **Snow Condition**:
    *   Light
    *   Medium
    *   Heavy
10.  Select the **Type of Snow Removal Needed**:
    *   Plowing
    *   Salting
    *   Both
    *   Neither
11.  Select the **Locations where Snow Clearing is Required**:
    *   Driveway
    *   Sidewalk
    *   Parking lot
    *   Sidewalk and Driveway
12.  Select if **Snow Clearing is Required**:
    *   True
    *   False

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: A field to enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: A field to enter your last name.
*   **Address**: A field to enter your address.
*   **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number (optional).
*   **Email**: A field to enter your email address (optional).
*   **Business Name**: A field to enter your business name (if applicable).
*   **Date**: A field to select the date of the inspection.
*   **Time**: A field to select the time of the inspection.
*   **Comments**: A field for any additional comments.
*   **Snow Condition**: A field to select the snow condition (Light, Medium, Heavy).
*   **Snow Type**: A field to select the type of snow removal needed (Plowing, Salting, Both, Neither).
*   **Snow Location**: A field to select the locations where snow clearing is required (Driveway, Sidewalk, Parking lot, Sidewalk and Driveway).
*   **Snow Clearing Required**: A field to select if snow clearing is required (True, False).
