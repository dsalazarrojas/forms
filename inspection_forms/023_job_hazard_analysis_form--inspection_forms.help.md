# Job Hazard Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Hazard Analysis Form is used to document and evaluate job hazards in the workplace. It is a tool for safety professionals to identify and mitigate potential hazards that could impact employees' well-being and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the category of the hazard from the provided options (Safety Hazard, Non-Safety Hazard, or Unknown).
2.  Describe the hazard in detail in the Hazard Description field.
3.  Identify the location where the hazard is present.
4.  Assess the risk of the hazard using the provided risk assessment scale (Low, Medium, or High).
5.  Document any corrective actions taken to mitigate the hazard.
6.  Identify any preventive actions that can be taken to reduce the likelihood of the hazard.
7.  Describe any control measures that are already in place to mitigate the hazard.
8.  Specify how frequently the hazard is present (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
9.  Identify the severity of the hazard (Low, Medium, or High).
10.  Assess the likelihood of the hazard (Low, Medium, or High).
11.  Specify the status of the hazard control measures (Implemented, Not Implemented, or Unknown).
12.  Specify the status of the hazard itself (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
13.  Add any additional notes about the hazard.
14.  Provide contact information for follow-up (Email and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`Category`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of hazard you are analyzing (Safety Hazard, Non-Safety Hazard, or Unknown).
    *   *Safety Hazard*: Hazards that could cause injury or harm to people.
    *   *Non-Safety Hazard*: Hazards that could cause non-injury related impacts (e.g., environmental, equipment related).
    *   *Unknown*: Hazards that are unknown or not yet identified.
*   **Hazard Description** (`Hazard Description`, Text, Optional): Describe the hazard in detail, including any relevant context or background information.
*   **Location** (`Location`, Text, Optional): Identify where the hazard is present, including specific locations or areas where it can be found.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`Risk Assessment`, Select Multiple, Optional): Assess the risk of the hazard using the provided scale (Low, Medium, High).
*   **Corrective Action** (`Corrective Action`, Text, Optional): Document any corrective actions taken to mitigate the hazard.
*   **Preventive Action** (`Preventive Action`, Text, Optional): Identify any preventive actions that can be taken to reduce the likelihood of the hazard.
*   **Job Hazard Control Measures** (`Job Hazard Control Measures`, Text, Optional): Describe any control measures that are already in place to mitigate the hazard.
*   **Job Hazard Frequency** (`Job Hazard Frequency`, Select One, Optional): Specify how frequently the hazard is present (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
*   **Job Hazard Severity** (`Job Hazard Severity`, Select One, Optional): Identify the severity of the hazard (Low, Medium, or High).
*   **Job Hazard Likelihood** (`Job Hazard Likelihood`, Select One, Optional): Assess the likelihood of the hazard (Low, Medium, or High).
*   **Job Hazard Control Status** (`Job Hazard Control Status`, Select One, Optional): Specify the status of the hazard control measures (Implemented, Not Implemented, or Unknown).
*   **Job Hazard Status** (`Job Hazard Status`, Select One, Optional): Specify the status of the hazard itself (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
*   **Notes** (`Notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the hazard.
*   **Email** (`Email`, Email, Optional): Provide contact information for follow-up (Email).
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, Optional): Provide contact information for follow-up (Phone).

## Tips

*   This form should be completed with the help of safety professionals.
*   The form is designed to be a tool for documenting and evaluating job hazards, not a comprehensive regulatory or legal requirement.
*   The fields should be completed in accordance with the provided instructions and guidelines. 
*   If you are unsure about how to complete a field or are unable to determine the correct information, contact the designated safety professional for assistance. 
*   This form should be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that it accurately reflects the current status of job hazards and control measures.
