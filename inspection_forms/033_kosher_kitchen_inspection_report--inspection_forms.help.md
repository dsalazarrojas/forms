# Kosher Kitchen Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the inspection of a kitchen, ensuring it meets kosher standards. It's an essential tool for tracking and monitoring kitchen compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you've provided all necessary information for each field.
2. For each field, follow the provided instructions and formatting guidelines to ensure accurate and valid data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kitchen Location** (text, optional):
  This field allows you to specify the exact location of the kitchen being inspected. This information will help in organizing and referencing the inspection report.
* **Inspection Date** (date, required):
  Enter the date of the inspection in the standard date format. This will help in tracking the timing and progression of inspections.
* **Inspector Name** (text, optional):
  Enter your name or title as the person conducting the inspection. This adds a personal touch and facilitates contact in case of further questions or inquiries.
* **Kitchen ID** (number, optional):
  Enter a unique identifier for the kitchen being inspected. This helps in tracking and referencing the kitchen's compliance history.
* **Kosher Observations** (select multiple, optional):
  Select all applicable observations from the provided list (Observed Issue, Corrective Action, Pass). These are key points to note during the inspection, indicating areas that need improvement or compliance.
* **Non-Kosher Items** (text, optional):
  Enter any non-kosher items found or observed during the inspection. This field is for free-form description or listing of non-compliant items.
* **Cleanliness Outcome** (select one, optional):
  Choose from the options Pass, Fail, or Not Applicable. This field indicates the overall cleanliness status of the kitchen based on the inspection findings.
* **Notes** (text, optional):
  Additional comments or remarks regarding the inspection. This can be used to provide extra context or clarification on specific points.
* **Kosher Standards** (text, optional):
  Enter any relevant information about the kosher standards applied or observed during the inspection.
* **Kosher Equipment** (text, optional):
  List or describe the equipment used or observed during the inspection, especially if it's related to kosher standards.
* **Kosher Labeling** (text, optional):
  Enter any information about kosher labeling found or observed during the inspection, such as labels or markings.
* **Additional Notes** (text, optional):
  Enter any additional comments or remarks about the kitchen or inspection process.
