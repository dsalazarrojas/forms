# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and report on inspections, training, and other events related to construction site activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the fields with the necessary information about the visitor, employee, and event details.
2. Select the relevant safety equipment available, worn, and hazards encountered during the event.
3. Record the supervisor and drill supervisor's contact information and signatures.
4. Document the date, time, and location of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Visitor Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the visitor or individual visiting the site.
* **Employee Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the employee performing the task or activity.
* **Employee Job Position (Text, Optional)**: Enter the job position of the employee performing the task or activity.
* **Date of Visit (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the visit or event.
* **Time of Arrival (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of arrival at the site.
* **Time of Departure (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of departure from the site.
* **Site Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the site or location.
* **Safety Equipment Available (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select any safety equipment available at the site, such as Hard hat, Safety vest, and Safety harness.
* **Safety Equipment Worn (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select any safety equipment worn by the employee, such as Hard hat, Safety vest, and Safety harness.
* **Hazards (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select any hazards encountered during the event, such as Falling objects, Fire, and Gas leaks.
* **Supervisor Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the supervisor in charge.
* **Supervisor Contact Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter the contact number of the supervisor.
* **Employee Signature (Note, Optional)**: Record the employee's signature.
* **Date of Training (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of training.
* **Goals (Text, Optional)**: Enter any goals or objectives.
* **Site Supervisor (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the supervisor responsible for the site.
* **Site Supervisor Contact Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter the contact number of the site supervisor.
* **Employee Induction (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the level of employee induction, such as Induction complete, Partial induction, or No induction.
* **Employee Induction Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of employee induction.
* **Drill Supervisor (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the supervisor responsible for the drill.
* **Drill Supervisor Contact Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter the contact number of the drill supervisor.
* **Date of Drill (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the drill.
* **Time of Drill (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of the drill.
* **Drill Location (Text, Optional)**: Enter the location of the drill.
* **Drill Supervisor Signature (Note, Optional)**: Record the drill supervisor's signature.
* **Site Inspector Signature (Note, Optional)**: Record the site inspector's signature.
