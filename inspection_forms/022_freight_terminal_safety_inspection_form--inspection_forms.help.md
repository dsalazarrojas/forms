# Freight Terminal Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Terminal Safety Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the safety conditions of a freight terminal. This form will help you identify potential safety hazards and facilitate corrective actions to prevent accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with details about the terminal, including its location and contact information.
2. Answer the specific questions about the terminal's safety features in the "General Information Questions" section.
3. Select all the safety-related questions that apply to your terminal in the "Questions 1" section.
4. Provide your answers to the selected questions in the "General Information Answers" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the terminal.
* **terminal_location** (`terminal_location`, text, optional): Enter the address or coordinates of the terminal.
* **terminal_address** (`terminal_address`, text, optional): Enter the address or postal code of the terminal.
* **terminal_contact** (`terminal_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the terminal, including names and phone numbers.

### General Information Questions
* **General Information Questions** (`general_info_questions`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the terminal that is not covered in the previous sections.

### Questions 1
* **Questions 1** (`questions_1`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ 1. Are all emergency exits clearly marked? 
	+ 2. Are all exits well lit? 
	+ 3. Are there fire extinguishers readily available? 
	+ 4. Are all walkways clear of debris? 
	+ 5. Are all walkways well maintained? 
	+ 6. Are all fire alarms in good working condition? 
	+ 7. Are all emergency exits well marked? 
	+ 8. Are all stairways well lit? 
	+ 9. Are all stairways clear of debris? 
	+ 10. Are all fire extinguishers well maintained? 
	+ 11. Are all first aid kits readily available? 
	+ 12. Are all fire suppression systems well maintained? 
	+ 13. Are all emergency phones available?

### General Information Answers
* **General Information Answers** (`general_info_answer`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the terminal's safety condition.
