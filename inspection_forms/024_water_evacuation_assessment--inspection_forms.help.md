The "Water Evacuation Assessment" form is designed to collect information about the water evacuation method and any associated photo attachments for a facility manager to assess and document. This form is intended to be completed by facility managers to assess and report on the effectiveness of the water evacuation system in their facility. The form should only be completed if the facility has a water evacuation system, as this form is not intended for facilities without such a system.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you are the designated facility manager or have authority to complete this form.
2.  Select the method used for water evacuation (if applicable) and provide any attachments or photos as necessary.
3.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required: false):
    Enter the name of the person responsible for managing the facility's water evacuation system.
*   **Evacuation Method** (`evacuation_method`, select_multiple, required: false):
    Select all the methods used for water evacuation in the facility. These options are:
    *   **Drain to floor**: Water is drained through the floor or drains.
    *   **Sump pump**: Water is pumped out using a sump pump.
    *   **Bilge pump**: Water is pumped out using a bilge pump.
    *   **Other**: Select this option if the facility uses an alternative method not listed above.
*   **Photo Attachments** (`photo_attachments`, note, required: false):
    Please attach any photos or images that support your response for reference.
*   **Photos** (`photos`, text, required: false):
    Enter any comments or descriptions for the photo attachments provided.

## Tips

*   This form is for facility managers to assess and document their water evacuation systems.
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
*   This form is not intended for facilities without a water evacuation system.

This form is a helpful tool for facility managers to assess and document their water evacuation systems, and by following these guidelines, you can ensure that you complete it accurately and effectively.
