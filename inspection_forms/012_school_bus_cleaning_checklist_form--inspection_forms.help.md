# School Bus Cleaning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Bus Cleaning Checklist Form is a tool for tracking and documenting the cleaning activities performed on school buses after each trip or excursion. This form ensures that buses are properly cleaned and maintained, which helps to create a safe and healthy environment for students, drivers, and other staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the type of trip**: Choose the type of trip or excursion that was made. Options are: School, Field, or Extracurricular.
2. **Enter the date of the trip**: Enter the date of the trip or excursion using the date field.
3. **Enter bus license number**: Enter the bus's license number if available.
4. **Enter bus license state**: Enter the state where the bus is licensed, if available.
5. **Enter odometer reading**: If applicable, enter the odometer reading after the trip.
6. **Choose the fuel level**: Check the fuel level after the trip. Options are: Empty, Full, or Half.
7. **Select interior cleaning**: Check which interior areas were cleaned. Options are: Floor Seats, Handrails, Arm Rests, Seatbelts, Headrests, or Other (Specify Below).
8. **Select exterior cleaning**: Check which exterior areas were cleaned. Options are: Front Side, Rear Side, Roof, or Other (Specify Below).
9. **Select interior and exterior cleaning**: If both interior and exterior areas were cleaned, check the corresponding options.
10. **Enter other cleaning**: If any other areas were cleaned, enter a brief description in the text field.
11. **Enter other cleaning specify**: If other areas were cleaned, enter a brief description in this field.
12. **Enter comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the cleaning activities performed.
13. **Enter completed by**: Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
14. **Enter completed date and time**: Enter the date and time the form was completed.
