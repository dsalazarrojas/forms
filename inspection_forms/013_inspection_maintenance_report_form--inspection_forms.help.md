# inspection_maintenance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inspection_maintenance_report_form is used to document the condition and maintenance status of assets or equipment in a warehouse or similar setting. It helps track and record information about asset inspections, including type, location, condition, and any repairs or maintenance performed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the asset type(s) that you are reporting on.
2. Enter the asset number of the asset being inspected.
3. Choose the location where the asset is situated.
4. Describe the condition of the asset (e.g., Good, Needs Inspection, Requires Repair).
5. Enter the date when the inspection was reported.
6. Enter the name of the person who reported the inspection.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the asset.
8. If applicable, enter the department responsible for the asset.
9. If applicable, enter any comments or notes about the asset.
10. Enter the date when the asset was inspected.
11. If applicable, enter the time when the asset was inspected.
12. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
13. Enter the ID number of the inspector who performed the inspection.
14. Describe the equipment condition (e.g., Good, Needs Maintenance, Requires Repair).
15. If applicable, enter the cost of any repairs performed.
16. If applicable, enter the maintenance plan for the asset.
17. Choose the inspection result (e.g., Good, Needs Inspection, Requires Repair).
18. If applicable, enter any comments or notes about the asset.
19. If applicable, enter the date when a follow-up action is planned.
20. If applicable, enter the name of the person who will perform the follow-up action.
21. Choose the follow-up status (e.g., True, False).
22. If applicable, enter the date when the next inspection is scheduled.
23. If applicable, enter the name of the inspector who will perform the next inspection.
24. Choose the next inspection status (e.g., Scheduled, Rescheduled, Missed).
25. If applicable, enter any additional comments or remarks about the asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Asset Type**: Select the type of asset being inspected (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Asset Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the asset.
* **Location**: Choose the location where the asset is situated (e.g., Warehouse 1, Warehouse 2, Warehouse 3).
* **Condition**: Describe the condition of the asset (e.g., Good, Needs Inspection, Requires Repair).
* **Date Reported**: Enter the date when the inspection was reported.
* **Reported By**: Enter the name of the person who reported the inspection.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter the department responsible for the asset.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the asset.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the asset.
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the date when the asset was inspected.
* **Time Inspected**: If applicable, enter the time when the asset was inspected.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Inspector ID**: Enter the ID number of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of the equipment (e.g., Good, Needs Maintenance, Requires Repair).
* **Repair Cost**: If applicable, enter the cost of any repairs performed.
* **Maintenance Plan**: If applicable, enter the maintenance plan for the asset.
* **Inspection Result**: Choose the inspection result (e.g., Good, Needs Inspection, Requires Repair).
* **Followup**: If applicable, enter the date when a follow-up action is planned.
* **Followup By**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who will perform the follow-up action.
* **Followup Status**: Choose the follow-up status (e.g., True, False).
* **Next Inspection**: If applicable, enter the date when the next inspection is scheduled.
* **Next Inspector**: If applicable, enter the name of the inspector who will perform the next inspection.
* **Next Inspection Status**: Choose the next inspection status (e.g., Scheduled, Rescheduled, Missed).
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the asset.
