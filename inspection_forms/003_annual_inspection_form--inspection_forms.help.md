# Annual Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for annual property inspections, gathering detailed information on the condition of a property's systems and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the inspection date on the "Inspection Date" field.
2.  Provide the complete address of the property on the "Property Address" field.
3.  Select the type of property being inspected on the "Property Type" field.
4.  Enter the full name of the property owner on the "Property Owner Name" field.
5.  Enter the contact information of the property owner on the "Owner Contact Information" field (optional).
6.  Enter the full name of the building inspector on the "Inspector Name" field.
7.  Enter the inspector's license or certification number on the "Inspector License or Certification Number" field (optional).
8.  Estimate the years since construction or last major renovation on the "Estimated Building Age" field (optional).
9.  Enter the total usable square feet of the property on the "Building Square Footage" field (optional).
10.  Assess the condition of the roof on the "Roof Condition" field.
11.  Evaluate the condition of the exterior walls on the "Exterior Walls Assessment" field.
12.  Check for foundation cracks or issues on the "Foundation Cracks or Issues" field.
13.  Inspect the condition of windows and doors on the "Windows and Doors Condition" field.
14.  Check the functionality and safety of the plumbing system on the "Plumbing System Assessment" field.
15.  Inspect the electrical system for safety compliance on the "Electrical System Inspection" field.
16.  Check the HVAC system's condition on the "HVAC and Ventilation System" field.
17.  Identify fire safety features present on the "Fire Safety Features Present" field.
18.  List any safety code violations noted on the "Safety Code Violations Identified" field (optional).
19.  Evaluate the property's ADA accessibility compliance on the "ADA Accessibility Compliance" field (optional).
20.  Rate the overall condition of the property on the "Overall Property Condition Rating" field.
21.  Specify any necessary follow-up actions on the "Required Follow-Up Actions" field (optional).
22.  Schedule the next inspection date on the "Date of Next Scheduled Inspection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Property Address**: Enter the complete address of the property being inspected.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property being inspected (e.g., Residential - Single Family, Residential - Multi-Family, Commercial, etc.).
* **Property Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the property owner.
* **Owner Contact Information**: Enter the phone number or email address of the property owner (optional).
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the full name of the building inspector.
* **Inspector License or Certification Number**: Enter the inspector's license or certification number (optional).
* **Estimated Building Age**: Estimate the years since construction or last major renovation.
* **Building Square Footage**: Enter the total usable square feet of the property.
* **Roof Condition**: Assess the condition of the roof (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Needs immediate repair).
* **Exterior Walls Assessment**: Evaluate the condition of the exterior walls (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Deteriorating).
* **Foundation Cracks or Issues**: Check for foundation cracks or issues (None detected, Minor cracks, Moderate cracks, Significant structural issues).
* **Windows and Doors Condition**: Assess the condition of windows and doors (All in good condition, Minor wear, Some repairs needed, Multiple issues).
* **Plumbing System Assessment**: Check the functionality and safety of the plumbing system (Adequate and functional, Minor issues noted, Repairs needed, System failing).
* **Electrical System Inspection**: Inspect the electrical system for safety compliance (Meets current code, Acceptable with minor upgrades, Upgrades recommended, Safety concerns present).
* **HVAC and Ventilation System**: Assess the condition of the HVAC and ventilation system (Excellent condition, Good working order, Repairs recommended, Replacement needed).
* **Fire Safety Features Present**: Identify fire safety features present (Fire extinguishers, Emergency exits clearly marked, Smoke detectors, Sprinkler system, Fire suppression system, None present).
* **Safety Code Violations Identified**: List any safety code violations noted during the inspection (optional).
* **ADA Accessibility Compliance**: Evaluate the property's ADA accessibility compliance (Compliant, Mostly compliant with minor issues, Non-compliant - upgrades required, Not applicable).
* **Overall Property Condition Rating**: Rate the overall condition of the property (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical - Immediate attention required).
* **Required Follow-Up Actions**: Specify any necessary follow-up actions (optional).
* **Date of Next Scheduled Inspection**: Schedule the next inspection date.

## Tips
* Be thorough in assessing each field, as the information collected will inform future inspections and property management.
* Ensure accuracy in entering dates and addresses to facilitate efficient data management.
* When evaluating the condition of a system or feature, select the option that best describes its current state.
* When listing safety code violations or follow-up actions, be detailed and provide clear instructions for resolution.
