# housing_inspection_site_notes_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect site notes from housing inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general notes about the inspection.
2. Enter the property information.
3. Select the inspection date.
4. Enter your name as the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Notes
* **General Notes** (`general_notes`, `text`, optional): Use this field to write any comments or observations you made during the inspection. This can include any issues you found, concerns, or recommendations.

### Property Information
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the details of the property, such as its address or any other relevant information.

### Inspection Date
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the inspection. This will help in tracking the progress and history of inspections.

### Inspector Name
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the inspector. This will help in identifying who conducted the inspection and for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- You can add any additional information or attachments as needed.
- Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
