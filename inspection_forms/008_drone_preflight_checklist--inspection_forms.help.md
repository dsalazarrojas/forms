# Drone Preflight Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive preflight checklist for drones, designed to ensure that all critical systems are checked before flight.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section of the form carefully and check off each item as you complete it.
2. Be thorough and methodical in your checks to reduce the risk of accidents and ensure a safe flight.
3. If you encounter any issues or doubts, flag them and bring them to the attention of your team or flight operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Preliminary Check**: Ensure that the drone is properly prepared and cleared for flight.
	+ This section checks that the drone is properly secured, batteries are connected, and all propellers are securely attached.
* **Propeller Check**: Verify the status of the propellers.
	+ Check if propellers are present, securely attached, and not damaged or worn.
* **Motor Check**: Check the status of the motors.
	+ Ensure that motors are properly secured, not overheating, and not damaged or malfunctioning.
* **Battery Check**: Check the status of the battery.
	+ Ensure that the battery is properly secured, at or above the recommended level (High or Low), and not damaged or malfunctioning.
* **Propeller Cover Check**: Check the status of the propeller covers.
	+ Ensure that propeller covers are securely attached and not damaged or malfunctioning.
* **Motor Mount Check**: Check the status of the motor mounts.
	+ Ensure that motor mounts are properly secured and not damaged or malfunctioning.
* **Drone Body Check**: Check the status of the drone body.
	+ Ensure that the drone body is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Electrical System Check**: Check the status of the electrical system.
	+ Ensure that all electrical connections are secure, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Flight Control System Check**: Check the status of the flight control system.
	+ Ensure that the flight control system is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Safety System Check**: Check the status of the safety system.
	+ Ensure that the safety system is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Navigation Check**: Check the status of the navigation system.
	+ Ensure that the navigation system is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Communication System Check**: Check the status of the communication system.
	+ Ensure that the communication system is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Battery Level Check**: Check the level of the battery.
	+ Ensure that the battery level is at or above the recommended level (High or Low).
* **Drone Weight Check**: Check the weight of the drone.
	+ Ensure that the drone weight is within the recommended range.
* **Flight Controller Check**: Check the status of the flight controller.
	+ Ensure that the flight controller is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Compass Check**: Check the status of the compass.
	+ Ensure that the compass is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.
* **Altimeter Check**: Check the status of the altimeter.
	+ Ensure that the altimeter is properly secured, not damaged, and not malfunctioning.

## Tips
- Always review and complete this form before each flight to ensure the safety of the drone and its operators.
- Be thorough and methodical in your checks to reduce the risk of accidents and ensure a safe flight.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, flag them and bring them to the attention of your team or flight operations.
