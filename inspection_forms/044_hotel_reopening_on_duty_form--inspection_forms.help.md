# hotel_reopening_on_duty_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report on the status of hotels during the COVID-19 pandemic. It helps track and monitor the safety measures and precautions taken by hotels to ensure the well-being of their guests and employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* `manager_name`
	* `reopening_date`
	* `employee_testing`
	* `contact_tracing`
	* `training_program`
	* `assigned_to`
	* `assigned_by`
	* `email`
	* `phone`
2. Select the relevant options for the following fields:
	* `social_distancing_measures` (select multiple options that apply)
	* `employee_testing` (select one option)
	* `contact_tracing` (select multiple options)
	* `training_program` (select one option)
3. Fill out the remaining fields as needed:
	* `safety_precautions`
	* `emergency_procedure`
	* `comments`
	* `completed_by`
	* `form_version`
	* `date_created`
	* `date_completed`

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Hotel Name (hotel_name) (*)
* **hotel_name**: This is the name of the hotel being reported on.
### 2. Manager Name (manager_name) (*)
* **manager_name**: This is the name of the hotel manager or contact person.
### 3. Reopening Date (reopening_date) (*)
* **reopening_date**: This is the date the hotel reopened.
### 4. Social Distancing Measures (social_distancing_measures) (*)
* **social_distancing_measures**: Select the social distancing measures the hotel is taking, such as reduced occupancy limits, regular cleaning, hand sanitizer, etc.
### 5. Safety Precautions (safety_precautions)
* **safety_precautions**: Describe any additional safety precautions the hotel is taking.
### 6. Employee Testing (employee_testing) (*)
* **employee_testing**: Select how often hotel employees are required to be tested.
### 7. Contact Tracing (contact_tracing) (*)
* **contact_tracing**: Select the level of contact tracing the hotel is conducting.
### 8. Training Program (training_program) (*)
* **training_program**: Select the level of training program the hotel is following.
### 9. Emergency Procedure (emergency_procedure)
* **emergency_procedure**: Describe the hotel's emergency procedure in case of an outbreak.
### 10. Comments (comments)
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the hotel's status.
### 11. Submitted By (submit)
* **submit**: This is a hidden field that is automatically filled by the system.
### 12. Email (*)
* **email**: This is the email address of the person submitting the form.
### 13. Phone (*)
* **phone**: This is the phone number of the person submitting the form.
### 14. Address (address)
* **address**: This is the address of the hotel.
### 15. Zip (zip)
* **zip**: This is the zip code of the hotel's address.
### 16. City (city)
* **city**: This is the city of the hotel's address.
### 17. State (state)
* **state**: This is the state of the hotel's address.
### 18. Country (country)
* **country**: This is the country of the hotel's address.
### 19. Assigned To (*)
* **assigned_to**: This is the person assigned to receive the report.
### 20. Assigned By (*)
* **assigned_by**: This is the person submitting the report.
### 21. Form Version (form_version)
* **form_version**: This is the version of the form being submitted.
### 22. Date Created (date_created)
* **date_created**: This is the date the form was created.
### 23. Date Completed (date_completed)
* **date_completed**: This is the date the form was completed.
### 24. Completed By (completed_by)
* **completed_by**: This is the person completing the form.
### 25. Comments (comments)
* **comments**: This is an optional field for additional comments.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.
