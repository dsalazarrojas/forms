# Customer Lounge Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Lounge Inspection Form is designed to help assess the cleanliness and comfort level of a customer lounge. It is intended to be filled out after a customer has visited the lounge, and the answers will be used to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments to provide a better experience for customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each of the sections below and provide your feedback according to the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Lounge Inspection Form Page 1** (`customer_lounge_inspection_form_page_1`, Text, Not required): Start by writing any comments or observations you have about your visit to the customer lounge.
* **Cleanliness Scale** (`cleanliness_scale`, Select One, Not required): How clean did you find the lounge? Choose from Very clean, Somewhat clean, or Not clean.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, Select One, Not required): How comfortable did you find the lounge? Choose from Very comfortable, Somewhat comfortable, or Not comfortable.
* **Furniture Condition** (`furniture_condition`, Select Multiple, Not required): What is the condition of the furniture in the lounge? Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Cleanliness Frequency** (`cleanliness_frequency`, Select One, Not required): How often do you think the lounge is cleaned? Choose from Daily, Several times a week, Once a week, or Rarely.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Not required): Is there anything else you'd like to mention about your visit?
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Not required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or comments.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Not required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or comments.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, Text, Not required): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about your visit?
* **Customer Comments 2** (`customer_comments_2`, Text, Not required): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions about your visit?

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and be as specific as possible with your comments.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please do not hesitate to mention them.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your help in making our customer lounge a better experience for everyone.
