# Heat Pump Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heat Pump Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect information related to heat pump systems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you are authorized to complete it.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your observation and knowledge of the system.
3. Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, including any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)**: 
	+ A general information section to capture overall impressions of the system.
* **System Settings (2)**: 
	+ Provide details about the heat pump system settings, such as operating conditions, or any relevant settings that may impact performance.
* **System Performance (3)**: 
	+ Describe the overall performance of the heat pump system, including any issues or problems encountered.
* **Inspection Areas (4)**: 
	+ List any areas of the system that were inspected.
* **Pump Type (5)**:
	+ Select the type of heat pump system being inspected.
* **Installed Year (6)**: 
	+ Record the year the heat pump system was installed.
* **Installed Capacity (7)**: 
	+ Record the installed capacity of the heat pump system.
* **Installed Location (8)**: 
	+ Record the location where the heat pump system was installed.
* **Inspection Date (9)**: 
	+ Record the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name (10)**: 
	+ Record the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Contact (11)**: 
	+ Record the contact information of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Property Owner Name (12)**: 
	+ Record the name of the property owner.
* **Property Owner Contact (13)**: 
	+ Record the contact information of the property owner.
* **Property Address (14)**: 
	+ Record the address of the property where the heat pump system is installed.
* **Comments (15)**: 
	+ Record any additional comments or notes about the system.
* **System Status (16)**: 
	+ Select the current status of the heat pump system (Working, Partially Working, Not Working, or Not Applicable).
* **System Performance Rating (17)**: 
	+ Rate the overall performance of the heat pump system on a scale of 1-10.
* **General Condition (18)**: 
	+ Select the general condition of the heat pump system (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Installed By (19)**: 
	+ Select who installed the heat pump system (HVAC Team, Service Company, or Property Manager).
* **Inspected By (20)**: 
	+ Select who inspected the heat pump system (HVAC Team, Service Company, or Property Manager).
* **Last Maintenance (21)**: 
	+ Record the date of the last maintenance performed on the heat pump system.
* **Next Maintenance (22)**: 
	+ Record the date of the next scheduled maintenance for the heat pump system.
* **Maintenance Frequency (23)**: 
	+ Record the recommended maintenance frequency for the heat pump system.
* **System Manufacturer (24)**: 
	+ Record the manufacturer of the heat pump system.
* **Model Number (25)**: 
	+ Record the model number of the heat pump system.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use the options that best describe the situation.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with an expert or leave the field blank.
* This form is intended to provide a general overview of the heat pump system's performance and condition.
* The data collected will help in tracking and analyzing the performance and maintenance needs of the heat pump system.
