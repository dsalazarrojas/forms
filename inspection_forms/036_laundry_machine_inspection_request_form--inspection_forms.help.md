# Laundry Machine Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Machine Inspection Request Form is used to report any issues or concerns with a laundry machine, such as leaks, odors, or malfunctioning equipment. This form helps the maintenance team to identify and address problems promptly, minimizing downtime and ensuring the safety of users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name and contact information, so the maintenance team can contact you for clarification or additional information.
2. Describe the current status of the laundry machine, including any issues or concerns you have observed.
3. Indicate whether you have encountered any issues with the machine, such as "Yes" or "No".
4. Attach any relevant photos or documents to support your report.
5. Specify your preferred dates for the inspection to be performed.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the machine or the issue.
7. Identify who submitted this form, so the maintenance team can verify the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information to facilitate communication with the maintenance team.
* **Machine Information** (`machine_info`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the machine's current status, including any issues or concerns.
* **Issues** (`issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have encountered any issues with the machine, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Photos** (`photos`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant photos or documents to support your report.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify your preferred dates for the inspection to be performed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the machine or the issue.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, optional): Attach any supporting documents or files to your report.
* **Submitter** (`submitters`, `select_one`, optional): Identify who submitted this form, so the maintenance team can verify the information provided.
