# Digital Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the completion of digital checklists, enabling users to record and track their progress in a systematic and organized manner. It captures information about tasks, checklists, and related activities to ensure thorough and accurate completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the title of the digital checklist form.
*   Enter a name for the task.
*   Add the item to be checked.
*   Choose the status of the item from the provided options.
*   Indicate the status of the task by selecting multiple options.
*   Provide the date of completion.
*   Enter the date of completion, if different from the previously mentioned date.
*   Enter the time of completion, if applicable.
*   Attach any relevant photo evidence or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Checklist Form Title
*   **Digital Checklist Form** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the digital checklist form.

### Task Name
*   **Task Name** (`task_name`, text, optional): Enter a name for the task to be checked.

### Checklist Item
*   **Checklist Item** (`checklist_item`, text, optional): Add the item to be checked.

### Item Status
*   **Item Status** (`item_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the item from the provided options.

### Task Status
*   **Task Status** (`task_status`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the status of the task by selecting multiple options.

### Date Completed
*   **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Provide the date of completion.

### Date Completed (Again)
*   **Date Completed (Again)** (`date_completed_date`, date, required): If the date of completion is different from the previously mentioned date, enter it here.

### Time Completed
*   **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time of completion, if applicable.

### Photo Evidence
*   **Photo Evidence** (`photo_evidence`, text, optional): Attach any relevant photo evidence or notes.

### Photo Notes
*   **Photo Notes** (`photo_notes`, text, optional): Attach any additional notes for the photo evidence.

### Notes
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes for the task.
