# Scaffold Handover Certificate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to certify that the scaffolding process has been completed on a construction site.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have checked the scaffolding carefully before completing this form.
2. Enter the date when the scaffolding was started.
3. Select the type of scaffolding used.
4. Choose the location where the scaffolding was erected.
5. Enter any details about the scaffolding.
6. Confirm acceptance of the scaffolding.
7. Enter your signature as the site manager.
8. Enter your signature as the client.
9. Enter the date when the scaffolding was completed.
10. Enter your name and title as the person completing the form.
11. Enter the date of the inspection.
12. Enter your name and title as the person conducting the inspection.
13. Enter your name and title as the site inspector.
14. Enter any additional notes about the scaffolding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is scaffolding completed?** (`scaffolding_completed`, number, **required**): This field is used to indicate whether the scaffolding process is complete or not. Please enter a number indicating the status.
* **Date started** (`date_started`, date, **required**): Enter the date when the scaffolding began.
* **Type of scaffolding** (`scaffolding_type`, select multiple, **required**): Select all the types of scaffolding used. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Location** (`scaffolding_location`, select one, **required**): Choose the location where the scaffolding was erected.
* **Scaffolding details** (`scaffolding_details`, text, **required**): Enter any details about the scaffolding, such as materials used or specific features.
* **Scaffolding acceptance** (`scaffolding_acceptance`, select one, **required**): Confirm that you have inspected the scaffolding and accept it as complete.
* **Site manager signature** (`site_manager_signature`, text, **required**): Enter your signature as the site manager.
* **Client signature** (`client_signature`, text, **required**): Enter your signature as the client.
* **Date completed** (`date_completed`, date, **required**): Enter the date when the scaffolding was completed.
* **Completed by** (`completed_by`, text, **required**): Enter your name and title as the person completing the form.
* **Inspection date** (`inspection_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection by** (`inspection_by`, text, **required**): Enter your name and title as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Site inspector** (`site_inspector`, text, **required**): Enter your name and title as the site inspector.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional notes about the scaffolding, if needed.
