# lifting_equipment_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and maintain the safety of lifting equipment. It gathers essential information about the equipment's condition, location, and inspection details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **What is this form for** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is a brief description of the form's purpose.
2. **Equipment Description** (`2`, `text`, required): Describe the equipment being inspected in detail.
3. **Inspect Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
4. **Location** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the equipment.
5. **Inspector Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
6. **Equipment ID** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the equipment's identification number or code.
7. **Condition** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the condition of the equipment from the available options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
8. **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
9. **Photo** (`9`, `text`, required): Upload a photo of the equipment.
10. **Signature Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the signature.
11. **Signature** (`11`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm the inspection.
12. **Assigned To** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the equipment.
13. **Assigned By** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned the equipment for inspection.
14. **Review Status** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select the review status from the available options: Review, Not Reviewed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the fields.
- Upload a clear and recognizable photo of the equipment.
- Double-check the review status after completing the inspection.
- Store this form securely and refer to it when needed.
