# Snow Plow Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the inspection and maintenance history of snow plows. It is essential to complete this form for each snow plow after every use and maintenance activity to ensure accurate tracking and recording of its condition and maintenance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Machine Name field with the name of the snow plow being inspected.
- Enter the Machine Model of the snow plow in the Machine Model field.
- Record the Date Last Used in the Date Last Used field.
- Enter the current mileage of the snow plow in the Mileage Last Inspection field.
- Check the Maintenance Last 30 Days field to record any maintenance activities performed within the last 30 days.
- Describe any maintenance activities performed Since Last Inspection in the Maintenance Since Last Inspection field.
- Enter the cost of the last maintenance activity in the Last Maintenance Dollar Value field.
- Record the Total Maintenance Cost, if any, in the Total Maintenance Cost field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Machine Name**: Enter the name of the snow plow being inspected. This field is used to identify the snow plow being inspected.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Machine Model**: Enter the model of the snow plow being inspected. This field is used to track the specific model of the snow plow.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Date Last Used**: Record the date the snow plow was last used. This field is used to track the usage history of the snow plow.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
- **Mileage Last Inspection**: Enter the current mileage of the snow plow. This field is used to track the mileage of the snow plow after each inspection.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
- **Maintenance Last 30 Days**: Check any boxes for maintenance activities performed within the last 30 days. This field is used to track recent maintenance activities.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Maintenance Since Last Inspection**: Describe any maintenance activities performed since the last inspection. This field is used to track all maintenance activities performed on the snow plow.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **Last Maintenance Dollar Value**: Enter the cost of the last maintenance activity. This field is used to track the cost of the last maintenance activity.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
- **Total Maintenance Cost**: Record the total maintenance cost for the snow plow. This field is used to track the total maintenance cost of the snow plow.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
