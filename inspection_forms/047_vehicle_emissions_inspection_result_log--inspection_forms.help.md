# Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a vehicle emissions inspection. It helps inspectors gather necessary data to document and track vehicle emissions inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's registration number, make, and year.
2. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
3. Choose the outcome of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
4. Provide any comments or additional information about the inspection.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log** (`vehicle_emissions_inspection_result_log`):
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`):
	+ Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`):
	+ Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`):
	+ Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, Civic).
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`):
	+ Enter the vehicle's registration number (required field).
* **Vehicle Engine Capacity** (`vehicle_engine_capacity`):
	+ Enter the engine capacity of the vehicle (optional field).
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_date`):
	+ Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **Vehicle Test Result** (`vehicle_test_result`):
	+ Choose the outcome of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
* **Vehicle Comments** (`vehicle_comments`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about the inspection (optional field).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information about the vehicle and the inspection outcome.
* If the vehicle has been inspected multiple times, fill out a separate form for each inspection.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
