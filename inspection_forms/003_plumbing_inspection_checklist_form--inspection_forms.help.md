# Plumbing Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data during a plumbing inspection and to record the status of the inspection. It gathers information about the inspection data, date, and other relevant details such as plumber's name, property address, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Inspection Data" field with any relevant details about the inspection.
2. Enter the "Inspection Date" in the specified format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Type in the "Plumber Name" of the person performing the inspection.
4. Enter the "Property Address" of the inspected property.
5. Fill in the "Checklist Status" field by selecting either "Active" or "Inactive".
6. Add any "Notes" about the inspection in the provided field.
7. Enter the "Email" and "Phone" of the person to contact if needed.
8. Provide any "Comments" about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Data** (`inspection_form_data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the inspection, such as the type of inspection and its scope.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Plumber Name** (`plumber_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the inspected property.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the person performing the inspection. (Note: This field may seem redundant with "Plumber Name", but it is intended to be the name of the person in charge of the inspection, not necessarily the plumber performing it.)
* **Checklist Status** (`checklist_status`, select_one, optional): Select either "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any comments or explanations about the inspection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the person to contact if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person to contact if needed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
