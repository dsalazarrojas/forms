# tow_truck_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help vehicle owners or operators perform a comprehensive inspection of a tow truck, identifying any potential issues or defects that may affect the vehicle's performance, safety, and reliability.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure all required fields are clearly marked.
2. Provide detailed information for each section, using the space provided for each field.
3. Be thorough and accurate when recording your observations and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This section is for recording the tow truck's make, model, and year, as well as the vehicle's current status (e.g., in-service, out-of-service, etc.).
- **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the tow truck, including any notable features or modifications.
- **Engine Info** (`engine_info`, text, optional): Record details about the engine, including its type, fuel type, and any known issues.
- **Transmission Info** (`transmission_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the transmission type, gear type, and any known issues.
- **Brakes Info** (`brakes_info`, text, optional): Describe the brake system, including its type (e.g., disc, drum) and any issues or concerns.
- **Steering Info** (`steering_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the steering system, including its type (e.g., manual, power) and any issues or concerns.
- **Tires Info** (`tires_info`, text, optional): Record information about the tires, including their type, size, and tread depth.
- **Suspension Info** (`suspension_info`, text, optional): Document any issues or concerns with the suspension system, including any worn-out parts or unusual noises.

## Tips
- Use clear and concise language when completing this form.
- Be thorough and accurate in your observations and notes.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and useful.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your organization's maintenance team or refer to your organization's policies and guidelines.
