# Anti Static Curtain Wall Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Anti-Static Curtain Wall Assessment form is designed to evaluate the condition and integrity of a curtain wall system in a building or facility. This form helps inspectors identify potential issues and provide recommendations for maintenance and repairs. The assessment covers various aspects of the curtain wall system, including its surface condition, sealant condition, electrical testing, and environmental factors.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill in Your Details

1. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the person conducting the assessment.
2. **Inspector Certification**: Enter your certification or license number.
3. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
4. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the building or facility being assessed.
5. **Facility Address**: Enter the full address of the building or facility.
6. **Curtain Wall Location**: Enter the specific area or section being assessed.

### Step 2: Evaluate the Curtain Wall System

1. **Building Type**: Choose the type of structure from the options provided (Office building, Industrial facility, Healthcare facility, Laboratory, Manufacturing plant, Data center, Other).
2. **Curtain Wall Age**: Choose how old the curtain wall system installation is (Less than 1 year, 1 to 5 years, 5 to 10 years, 10 to 20 years, More than 20 years).
3. **Manufacturer**: If known, enter the brand or manufacturer of the curtain wall system.
4. **Installation Date**: Enter when the curtain wall system was installed.
5. **Surface Condition**: Evaluate the overall surface state of the curtain wall system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical).
6. **Visible Damage**: Check for any observable damage on the curtain wall system (None, Minor scratches, Cracks or chips, Significant damage, Severe deterioration).
7. **Sealant Condition**: Evaluate the state of seals and gaskets on the curtain wall system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Failed).
8. **Contamination Present**: Check for signs of contamination on the curtain wall system (None, Dust accumulation, Oil or grease, Chemical residue, Mold or mildew, Other).
9. **Frame Condition**: Evaluate the state of the structural frame of the curtain wall system (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Compromised).
10. **Mounting Security**: Check how secure the mounting is (Very secure, Secure, Slightly loose, Loose, Unstable).
11. **Grounding Connection**: Check if the grounding is intact (Properly grounded, Grounded but worn, Questionable grounding, Not grounded, Unable to assess).
12. **Surface Resistance**: Enter the measured resistance in ohms.
13. **Meets Resistance Standards**: Check if the measured resistance is within the acceptable range (Yes, within range, Borderline, No, out of range, Not tested).
14. **Static Decay Test**: Enter the decay time measurement.
15. **Cleaning Schedule**: Check how often the curtain wall system is cleaned (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely or never).
16. **Maintenance History**: Check if maintenance records are kept (Yes, complete records, Partial records, No records).

### Step 3: Evaluate and Record Your Results

1. **Overall Assessment Rating**: Enter a final evaluation of the curtain wall system (Pass - No action needed, Pass with minor maintenance, Conditional pass - Repair needed, Fail - Replacement recommended, Fail - Immediate action required).
2. **Recommended Corrective Actions**: Enter any necessary actions to be taken.
3. **Priority Level**: Evaluate the urgency of repairs (Low - Routine maintenance, Medium - Schedule within 30 days, High - Schedule within 7 days, Critical - Immediate action).
4. **Recommended Re-inspection Date**: Enter when to reassess the curtain wall system.
5. **Inspector Signature**: Enter your digital signature as the inspector.
6. **Facility Representative**: Enter the name of the facility contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully evaluate each aspect of the curtain wall system to provide an accurate assessment.
* Take note of any corrective actions or recommendations for the facility representative.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review the overall assessment rating and recommended corrective actions carefully before finalizing the assessment.
