# Restaurant Opening Manager Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and review the performance of your restaurant. It's a crucial tool for restaurant managers and quality control teams to assess the overall quality of the restaurant's service, food, and cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the "New Restaurant Name" field with the name of the restaurant you're reviewing.
2. Identify the "Restaurant Manager" who is in charge of the restaurant.
3. Enter the "Date Opened" when the restaurant first started operating.
4. Rate the "Overall Rating" on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Poor" and 5 means "Excellent".
5. Rate the "Cleanliness" on a scale of 1-5.
6. Rate the "Service Quality" on a scale of 1-5.
7. Rate the "Food Quality" on a scale of 1-5.
8. Rate the "Value for Money" on a scale of 1-5.
9. Enter any "Overall Rating Comments" you have about the restaurant.
10. Add any "Additional Comments" you'd like to mention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Restaurant Name** (`new_restaurant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the restaurant being reviewed.
* **Restaurant Manager** (`restaurant_manager`, text, optional): Identify the restaurant manager in charge.
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, date, optional): Enter the date when the restaurant first opened.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the restaurant's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality_rating`, number, optional): Rate the service quality of the staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality_rating`, number, optional): Rate the food quality of the restaurant on a scale of 1-5.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money_rating`, number, optional): Rate the value for money on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rating Comments** (`overall_rating_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the restaurant's overall performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback you'd like to share about the restaurant.
