# Seal Intake Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seal Intake Inspection Form is used to collect information about a seal for inspection and further evaluation. This form is required for all seals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Seal Location** field with the exact location of the seal.
2. Select the **Seal Type** from the provided options.
3. Select the **Seal Status** from the provided options.
4. If necessary, specify **Next Steps** for further evaluation or action.
5. If there are any issues with the seal, select from the **Condition Checks** options.
6. Provide the date the seal was last inspected or sealed, in the **Seal Date** field.
7. Enter the time the seal was last inspected or sealed, in the **Seal Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seal Location (Required)**: Enter the exact location of the seal.
* **Seal Type**: Select the type of seal from the provided options (Yes or No).
* **Seal Status (Required)**: Select the status of the seal from the provided options (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Next Steps**: Provide any next steps or actions required for further evaluation or resolution.
* **Condition Checks (Optional)**: Select any issues with the seal (No issues, Minor issues, or Major issues).
* **Seal Date (Required)**: Enter the date the seal was last inspected or sealed.
* **Seal Time (Optional)**: Enter the time the seal was last inspected or sealed.
