# property_isolated_routine_inspection_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property isolated routine inspection report form is used to record the results of a property isolated routine inspection. The form captures information about the property, including general information, property details, maintenance issues, recommended actions, photos, notes, comments, who is assigned to, date and time of inspection, and the inspector's signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the general information about the property.
2. Enter the property details as needed.
3. Indicate the maintenance issues found during the inspection.
4. Suggest recommended actions for addressing the maintenance issues.
5. Take and attach photos of any issues found.
6. Add any notes or comments about the inspection.
7. Determine if there are any issues to be assigned to someone else.
8. Record the date and time of the inspection.
9. Sign the form to confirm completion of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (General Information, text, required: false): This field is for entering general information about the property.
* **Property Details** (Property Details, text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the property.
* **Maintenance Issues** (Maintenance Issues, select_multiple, required: true): Indicate any maintenance issues found during the inspection. Select multiple issues as needed.
* **Recommended Actions** (Recommended Actions, text, required: true): Suggest any recommended actions for addressing the maintenance issues.
* **Photos** (Photos, text, required: false): Attach photos of any issues found during the inspection.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required: false): Add any comments about the inspection.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, required: false): Indicate if there are any issues to be assigned to someone else.
* **Date Inspected** (Date Inspected, date, required: true): Record the date of the inspection.
* **Time Inspected** (Time Inspected, time, required: false): Record the time of the inspection.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required: true): Sign and date the form to confirm completion of the inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Attach any supporting documentation or photos as needed.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
