# Mining Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mining Equipment Inspection Form is used to gather information about the status and history of mining equipment, enabling regular inspections and maintenance to be planned and executed effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of several pages with different fields that require input from the user. Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Select the type of equipment from the dropdown menu in **Mining Equipment Type**. This will help identify the specific type of equipment being inspected.
2.  In **Equipment Location**, select all relevant locations where the equipment is or will be used.
3.  Enter the **Inspection Date** and **Inspection Time** to record the details of the current inspection.
4.  Enter the **Last Inspection Date** if it's relevant to the equipment's history.
5.  Select the **Next Inspection Due** date for the equipment's next scheduled inspection.
6.  In **Findings**, describe any problems or issues observed during the inspection.
7.  In **Actions Taken**, describe any actions taken to address the issues mentioned above.
8.  In **Comments**, add any additional comments about the equipment's status.
9.  Select the **Equipment Status** from the dropdown menu to indicate the current status of the equipment.
10. In **Assigned Inspector**, select all relevant personnel responsible for inspecting the equipment.
11. In **Inspection Frequency**, select the frequency of future inspections for the equipment.
12. In **Inspection Responsible Party**, select all relevant parties responsible for the equipment's maintenance and inspection.
13. Add any **Notes** that can be added here, such as additional information or comments about the equipment's history.

*   **Mining Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of equipment from the dropdown menu.
*   **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant locations where the equipment is or will be used.

*   **Findings** (`findings`, `text`, required: false): Describe any problems or issues observed during the inspection.
