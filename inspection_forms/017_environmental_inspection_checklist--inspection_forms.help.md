# Environmental Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Inspection Checklist is a form used to assess the environmental and physical conditions of a facility. This form helps inspectors evaluate the facility's compliance with safety and environmental standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Building Address**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
2. **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information as the inspector.
3. **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. **Building Name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
5. **Facility Status**: If the facility is in operation, choose **Active**. If not, choose **Inactive**.
6. **Environmental Standards**:
	* Select all the environmental standards that apply to the facility. You can select multiple options.
	* This field is required to ensure the facility is compliant with environmental regulations.
7. **Safety Standards**:
	* Select all the safety standards that apply to the facility. You can select multiple options.
	* This field is required to ensure the facility is compliant with safety regulations.
8. **Safety Concerns**:
	* This field is a note-taking area for any safety concerns you notice during the inspection.
9. **Inspector Notes**:
	* This field is a note-taking area for any comments or observations you have about the facility.
10. **Assigned Inspector**:
	* Enter the name of the inspector assigned to the facility.
11. **Facility Contact**:
	* Enter the contact information for the facility.
12. **Facility Email**:
	* Enter the email address of the facility.
13. **Facility Phone**:
	* Enter the phone number of the facility.
14. **Facility Address**:
	* Enter the address of the facility.
15. **Facility Location**:
	* If the facility is located on a specific site, choose **Yes**. If not, choose **No**.
16. **Building Condition**:
	* Enter the current condition of the building.
17. **Building Type**:
	* Enter the type of building (e.g., office, residential, commercial, etc.).
18. **Building Age**:
	* Enter the age of the building.
19. **Building Size**:
	* Enter the size of the building in square meters or feet.
20. **Building Height**:
	* Enter the height of the building.
21. **Building Design**:
	* Enter the design of the building.
22. **Building Materials**:
	* Enter the materials used to construct the building.
23. **Building Layout**:
	* Enter the layout of the building.
24. **Building Elevators**:
	* If the building has an elevator, choose **Yes**. If not, choose **No**.
25. **Building Elevators Count**:
	* If the building has an elevator, enter the number of elevators.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are consistent with the facility's actual conditions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the assigned inspector or facility management.
