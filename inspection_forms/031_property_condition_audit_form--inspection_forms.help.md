# Property Condition Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Condition Audit Form is used to assess the condition of a property, providing a score based on its condition. This score can be used for various purposes, such as determining the need for repairs or maintenance, evaluating the property's value, or tracking changes in the property's condition over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Property Condition Audit Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address in the `property_address` field. This field is required.
2. Select the type of property being audited in the `property_type` field. This field is required.
3. Enter a score for the property condition in the `condition_score` field. This field is required and should be a numerical value between 1 and 5.
4. Enter any additional notes about the property condition in the `note` field. This field is required.
5. Enter the contact person's name in the `contact_person` field. This field is required.
6. Enter the contact person's email address in the `contact_email` field. This field is required.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property being audited.
* **property_type** (select_multiple, required): Select the type of property being audited, such as residential, commercial, or industrial.
* **condition_score** (number, required): Enter a score for the property condition, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **note** (note, required): Enter any additional notes about the property condition.
* **contact_person** (text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
