# Desk Review Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Desk Review Appraisal Form" is a form designed to gather information about a property for further analysis and review. It collects data from the contact person and contains fields for general information, property specifics, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, make sure to have the necessary information ready to fill in the form accurately. Each field is crucial in ensuring the form is completed correctly.

1.  General Information: This section is for entering the property's address, type, and status.
2.  Property Information: This section is for entering the property's value, size, and year built.
3.  Contact Information: This section is for entering the contact person's name, phone number, and email.
4.  Appraisal and Review Information: This section is for entering the appraisal date, time, and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info

* **General Info**: Enter the property's general information, such as its address.
* **Property Info**: Enter the property's type, value, size, and year built.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Property Address**: Enter the property's address.
* **Property Type**: Select the property type, e.g., Single Family, Multi Family, etc.
* **Property Status**: Select the property status, e.g., Active, Sold, Leased, etc.
* **Property Value**: Enter the property's value.
* **Property Year Built**: Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Size**: Enter the property's size.
* **Property SqFt**: Enter the property's square footage.
* **Property Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the property.
* **General Comments**: Enter any general comments about the property.
* **Property Photos**: Choose whether to include property photos.
* **Appraisal Date**: Enter the date of the appraisal.
* **Appraisal Time**: Enter the time of the appraisal.
* **Appraisal Notes**: Enter any notes about the appraisal.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any notes about the review.
* **Review Signature**: Enter the signature of the reviewer.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

### Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the property's address for accuracy.
* Select the correct property type and status.
* Enter the contact person's contact information accurately.
* Ensure all appraisal and review information is filled in correctly.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the "Desk Review Appraisal Form" accurately and efficiently. Remember to review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are complete and correct.
