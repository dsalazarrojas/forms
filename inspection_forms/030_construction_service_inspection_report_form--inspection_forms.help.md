# Construction Service Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Service Inspection Report Form is a critical document used to record the findings of a construction service inspection. It helps ensure the quality and safety of construction projects by identifying any issues, deficiencies, or safety concerns and providing a basis for corrective actions to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the service inspection in the format of MM/DD/YY.
2. **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the service inspection in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
3. **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
4. **Inspector Company**: Enter the name of the company or department performing the inspection.
5. **Project Name**: Enter the name of the construction project being inspected.
6. **Project Address**: Enter the site location of the construction project.
7. **Service Type Being Inspected**: Select the type of construction service being inspected, such as Electrical Installation, Plumbing Installation, HVAC Installation, etc.
8. **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the company performing the construction service.
9. **Permit Number**: (Optional) Enter the associated permit number, if any.
10. **Installation Complete**: Indicate whether the service installation is finished or not.
11. **Work Quality**: Rate the quality of work as Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Below Standard, or Poor.
12. **Code Compliance**: Indicate whether the work meets applicable codes or not.
13. **Manufacturer Specifications**: Indicate whether the equipment was installed according to manufacturer specifications.
14. **Materials Used**: Indicate whether approved materials were used.
15. **Testing Performed**: Indicate whether required testing was performed.
16. **Test Results**: Provide a brief description of the test outcomes (Optional).
17. **Deficiencies Found**: Enter the number of issues identified during the inspection.
18. **Deficiency Description**: Describe each issue or deficiency found during the inspection.
19. **Safety Violations**: Describe any safety issues observed during the inspection.
20. **Corrective Actions Required**: Specify what actions must be taken to address any deficiencies or safety concerns.
21. **Reinspection Required**: Indicate whether a reinspection is required.
22. **Reinspection Date**: (Optional) Enter the scheduled date for reinspection if applicable.
23. **Inspection Result**: Indicate the final determination of the inspection, such as Approved, Approved with Conditions, Conditional Approval, Failed, or Incomplete.
24. **Photos Taken**: Indicate whether photos were taken during the inspection.
25. **Inspector Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspector.
26. **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date the form with your full name as the inspector.
27. **Contractor Signature**: (Optional) The contractor may sign and date the form if they have reviewed it.
