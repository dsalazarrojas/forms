# Ferris Wheel Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ferris wheel safety inspection form is used to collect essential information about a Ferris wheel inspection. This form helps identify and record any safety issues or concerns related to the Ferris wheel, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the inspector in the "Inspector Name" field.
3. If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Ride Operator" field to indicate if the ride operator was present during the inspection.
4. Enter the contact information of the ride operator in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Enter the address of the ride operator in the "Ride Operator Address" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the ride operator in the "Ride Operator Phone" field.
7. Enter the name of the ride in the "Ride Name" field.
8. Enter the ride's identification number in the "Ride ID Number" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Ride Type" field to indicate if the ride is a specific type.
10. Enter the capacity of the ride (e.g., number of passengers) in the "Ride Capacity" field.
11. Enter the location of the ride in the "Ride Location" field.
12. Enter a description of the ride's status in the "Ride Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Ride Operator**: Check if the ride operator was present during the inspection. If "Yes" is selected, provide additional contact information below.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact information (e.g., phone, email) of the ride operator.
* **Ride Operator Address**: Enter the address of the ride operator.
* **Ride Operator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the ride operator.
* **Ride Name**: Enter the name of the Ferris wheel.
* **Ride ID Number**: Enter the identification number assigned to the Ferris wheel.
* **Ride Type**: Select "Yes" if the ride is a specific type (e.g., water ride, roller coaster).
* **Ride Capacity**: Enter the maximum number of passengers the ride can hold.
* **Ride Location**: Enter the location where the ride is installed (e.g., theme park, carnival).
* **Ride Status**: Enter a description of the ride's current status (e.g., operational, closed, in maintenance).
