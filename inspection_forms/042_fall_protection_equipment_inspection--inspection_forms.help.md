# Fall Protection Equipment Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and record the results of fall protection equipment inspections on a construction site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the equipment used during the inspection.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the location of the inspection.
4. Record the results of inspections on safety equipment by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each item.
5. Provide details about the safety equipment inspections by typing in the text field.
6. Record the results of inspections on personal protection equipment by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each item.
7. Provide details about the personal protection equipment inspections by typing in the text field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the inspection is completed.
9. Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
10. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Used**: This field allows you to select which equipment was used during the inspection. Select all equipment that was used.
* **Date Inspected**: This field is where you enter the date of the inspection. Please format the date in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Location**: This field is where you enter the location of the inspection.
* **Safety Equipment Inspections**: This field is where you record the results of inspections on safety equipment. Select "Yes" for equipment that was inspected and "No" for equipment that was not inspected.
* **Safety Equipment Inspections Details**: This field is where you provide details about the safety equipment inspections.
* **Personal Protection Equipment Inspections**: This field is where you record the results of inspections on personal protection equipment. Select "Yes" for equipment that was inspected and "No" for equipment that was not inspected.
* **Personal Protection Equipment Inspections Details**: This field is where you provide details about the personal protection equipment inspections.
* **Inspection Completed**: This field is where you select "Yes" to indicate that the inspection is completed, or "No" if it is not completed.
* **Inspector Comments**: This field is where you enter any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is where you enter any additional comments or information about the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature**: This field is where you sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.
