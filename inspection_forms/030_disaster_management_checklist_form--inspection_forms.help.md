# Disaster Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The disaster management checklist form is used to document and report incidents that occur during a disaster response or recovery process. It captures basic information about the incident, including the affected category, subcategory, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the category of the incident from the dropdown menu. This will help identify the type of incident and aid in reporting and documentation.
2. If necessary, select one or more subcategories related to the incident.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the text field.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter the contact information for key stakeholders, including their name, email address, and any additional notes.
6. Add any additional notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Checklist**: This field is not used in this form. However, it may be used in other forms or systems to provide a summary of key points to review during the incident response process.
* **Category**: Select the category of the incident from the dropdown menu.
* **Subcategory**: Select one or more subcategories related to the incident from the list. These subcategories are used to provide more specific information about the incident.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details that may aid in the response process.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Contact**: Enter the name and email address of key stakeholders involved in the incident.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of key stakeholders involved in the incident.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the incident that may be useful for the response process.
