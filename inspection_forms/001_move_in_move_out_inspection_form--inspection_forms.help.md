# move_in_move_out_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for inspecting a building or house during a move-in or move-out scenario. It checks the condition and status of the property's exterior and interior features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2.  Check all the boxes marked "Yes" or "No" to indicate the condition of the property.
3.  Provide any additional comments or explanations in the text boxes if necessary.
