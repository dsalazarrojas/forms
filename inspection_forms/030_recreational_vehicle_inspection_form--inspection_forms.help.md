# Recreational Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreational Vehicle Inspection Form is used to record information about the condition, maintenance, and repairs of a recreational vehicle during an inspection. This form helps inspectors capture details such as vehicle make, model, year, and inspection date, as well as any repairs or issues identified.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the vehicle make and model from the provided options or choose "Other" if not listed.
2. Enter the vehicle's year and mileage.
3. Select the type of inspection (e.g., routine, pre-sale).
4. Enter the inspection date.
5. Fill in the inspector's name and any comments.
6. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
7. List any repaired items and estimated repair cost.
8. Select the payment method for the inspection.
9. Check if the vehicle's maintenance is up to date.
10. Enter the date of the next inspection.
11. Enter the date of the previous inspection.
12. Estimate the cost of any recommended repairs.
13. List any additional comments or observations from the inspector or service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the vehicle from the options. If not listed, choose "Other".
* **Vehicle Model**: Select the model of the vehicle from the options. If not listed, choose "Other".
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Mileage**: Enter the vehicle's mileage.
* **Vehicle Yearly Maintenance**: Select if the vehicle's maintenance is up to date.
* **Vehicle Inspection Type**: Select the type of inspection (e.g., routine, pre-sale).
* **Vehicle Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspector Name**: Enter the inspector's name.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Add any comments about the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Repaired Items**: List any items that were repaired during the inspection.
* **Estimated Repair Cost**: Estimate the cost of any recommended repairs.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the inspection (e.g., cash, credit).
* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next inspection.
* **Previous Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the previous inspection.
* **Estimated Repair Cost**: Estimate the cost of any recommended repairs.
* **Vehicle Inspector Comments**: Add any comments or observations from the inspector.
* **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider.
* **VIN Notes**: Add any additional notes about the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Service Provider Notes**: Add any comments or observations from the service provider.

Note that some fields may not be applicable or relevant to your specific use case. Please review each field carefully and use your best judgment when completing the form.
