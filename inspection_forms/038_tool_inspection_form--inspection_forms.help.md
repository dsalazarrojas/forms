# tool_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tool_inspection_form is designed to collect information about a tool's condition, description, assigned status, and additional details after inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the tool that is being inspected from the list of options in the "assigned_tool" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the tool in the "description" field.
3. Assess the tool's condition by selecting one of the three options in the "condition" field: Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "date" and "time" fields, respectively.
5. Add any additional notes about the inspection in the "notes" field.
6. Enter the email address of the person who performed the inspection in the "email" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the person who performed the inspection in the "phone" field.
8. Enter the name of the person who submitted the inspection report in the "submitted_by" field.
9. Enter the date when the inspection report was submitted in the "submitted_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page**: Enter a title or label for the inspection report.
* **assigned_tool**: Select the tool that is being inspected from the list of options. This field is used to associate the inspection report with the specific tool being examined.
* **description**: Enter a brief description of the tool being inspected. This field should provide context about the tool's function, usage, or any other relevant details.
* **condition**: Assess the tool's condition by selecting one of the three options: Good, Fair, or Poor. This field helps identify potential issues or areas for improvement.
* **date**: Enter the date when the inspection was performed.
* **time**: Enter the time when the inspection was performed.
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection that may not be captured in other fields.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the person who performed the inspection.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who performed the inspection.
* **submitted_by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the inspection report.
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date when the inspection report was submitted.

Note: Each field is optional, except for the "assigned_tool" field, which is required to associate the inspection report with a specific tool.
