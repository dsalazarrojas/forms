# Restaurant Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Inspection Checklist is a standardized form used to evaluate a restaurant's adherence to health and safety regulations, fire safety protocols, and other relevant codes and guidelines. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining the purpose and requirements of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the items being inspected.
2. Provide accurate and truthful responses to each question, based on your restaurant's actual practices and conditions.
3. Ensure you have all necessary documentation and evidence to support your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Restaurant Address (<input name="restaurant_address">)
* **Text field:** Enter the street address of your restaurant.
* **Hint:** This is the main address where the restaurant is located.

### Restaurant Name (<input name="restaurant_name">)
* **Text field:** Enter the name of your restaurant.
* **Hint:** This is the name displayed on your sign and marketing materials.

### Emergency Contact (<input name="emergency_contact">)
* **Text field:** Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Hint:** This is the person the inspector will contact in case of an emergency.

### Manager Name (<input name="manager_name">)
* **Text field:** Enter the name of your manager or the person responsible for daily operations.
* **Hint:** This person is likely to have knowledge of the restaurant's safety and health procedures.

### Phone (<input name="restaurant_phone">)
* **Text field:** Enter the phone number of your restaurant.
* **Hint:** This number is used for emergency contact and other official communications.

### Email (<input name="restaurant_email">)
* **Email field:** Enter the email address of your restaurant.
* **Hint:** This email is used for official communications and documentation.

### Business License (<input name="business_license">)
* **Text field:** Enter your business license or permit number.
* **Hint:** This is required by law and must be displayed prominently in the restaurant.

### Food Handling Certificate (<input name="food_handling_cert">)
* **Text field:** Enter your food handling certification or license number.
* **Hint:** This is required by law and must be displayed in the kitchen and dining area.

### Health Certificate (<input name="health_cert">)
* **Text field:** Enter your health inspection certificate or license number.
* **Hint:** This is required by law and must be displayed in the restaurant.

### Safety Equipment (<input name="safety_equipment">)
* **Text field:** Describe the safety equipment available at your restaurant, such as fire extinguishers and emergency exits.
* **Hint:** Ensure this equipment is easily accessible and well-maintained.

### Fire Suppression System (<input name="fire_suppression_system">)
* **Text field:** Describe your fire suppression system, including type and location.
* **Hint:** Ensure this system is properly inspected and maintained regularly.

### Fire Extinguishers (<input name="fire_extinguishers">)
* **Text field:** Count the number of fire extinguishers available and indicate location.
* **Hint:** Ensure they are easily accessible and well-maintained.

### Emergency Exit Signs (<input name="emergency_exit_signs">)
* **Text field:** Describe the location and number of emergency exit signs.
* **Hint:** Ensure they are easily visible and well-maintained.

### Emergency Phone (<input name="emergency_phone">)
* **Text field:** Enter the number of emergency phones available and indicate location.
* **Hint:** Ensure they are easily accessible and well-maintained.

### Emergency Contact Phone (<input name="emergency_contact_phone">)
* **Text field:** Enter the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Hint:** This person is the contact in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Name (<input name="emergency_contact_name">)
* **Text field:** Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Hint:** This person is the contact in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Email (<input name="emergency_contact_email">)
* **Text field:** Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Hint:** This email is used for official communications and documentation.

### Safety Equipment Type (<input name="safety_equipment_type">)
* **Text field:** Describe the type of safety equipment available at your restaurant, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.
* **Hint:** Ensure this equipment is easily accessible and well-maintained.

### Safety Equipment Count (<input name="safety_equipment_count">)
* **Number field:** Enter the number of safety equipment items available at your restaurant.
