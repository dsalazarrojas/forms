# Border Patrol Facility Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Border Patrol Facility Inspection Form is used to record the details of a border patrol facility inspection, including the date of inspection, the name of the inspector, and the facility's overall condition rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of inspection from the calendar provided.
2. Enter the full name of the inspecting officer.
3. Enter the internal facility or building ID.
4. Enter the address or GPS coordinates of the facility.
5. Select the overall condition rating of the facility (Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. If applicable, briefly describe any deficiencies or issues found during the inspection.
7. If applicable, provide suggested corrective actions and timeline.
8. Indicate if supporting photos are provided.
9. Mark if immediate repairs are needed.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes from the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection date**: Enter the date of the inspection, which is the day when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector name**: Enter the full name of the officer who conducted the inspection.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the internal facility or building ID, which is a unique identifier for the facility.
* **Facility location**: Enter the address or GPS coordinates of the facility's location.
* **Overall condition rating**: Select the most accurate rating for the facility's condition from the options provided.
* **Issues found summary**: Briefly describe any deficiencies or issues found during the inspection. If no issues were found, you can enter "None".
* **Recommended actions**: Suggest any corrective actions and timeline for addressing the issues found.
* **Photos attached**: Indicate if supporting photos are provided to illustrate the issues found.
* **Urgent repair required**: Mark if immediate repairs are needed to address critical issues.
* **Inspector comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the inspector.
