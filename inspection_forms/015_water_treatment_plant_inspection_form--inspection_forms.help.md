# Water Treatment Plant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the water treatment plant's maintenance schedule and inspections. It is intended to be used by plant operators or maintenance personnel to ensure that the plant is operating safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Facility Name** of the water treatment plant.
*   Select the **Inspection Date** of the visit.
*   Enter the name of the **Inspector** conducting the inspection.
*   Enter the **Plant ID** of the water treatment plant.
*   Update the **Maintenance Schedule Last Updated** date to reflect the last updated date of the maintenance schedule.
*   Select the **Last Updated** time for the maintenance schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the water treatment plant being inspected.
*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the inspection visit.
*   **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
*   **Plant ID** (`plant_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique ID of the water treatment plant.
*   **Maintenance Schedule Last Updated** (`maintenance_schedule_last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the last updated date of the maintenance schedule.
*   **Last Updated** (`maintenance_schedule_last_updated_date`, time, optional): Select the last updated time of the maintenance schedule.

## Tips

*   Make sure to update the maintenance schedule last updated date and time accurately.
*   Double-check the plant ID to ensure it is correct and unique.
*   If you are unsure about any information, contact the plant operator or maintenance personnel for clarification.
*   This form is a helpful tool for tracking and reporting on the water treatment plant's maintenance activities, ensuring a safe and efficient operation.
*   Use the form to identify areas for improvement and make recommendations for future maintenance activities.
