# Rental Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Inspection Report Form is used to document the findings from a rental inspection, gathering information on contact details, property specifics, issues discovered, and repair requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contact information of the property owner or manager.
2. Provide details about the inspected property.
3. Indicate if issues were found during the inspection.
4. Specify if repair requests are needed.
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Enter the name of the inspector.
7. Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter the contact name, address, phone number, and other relevant details of the property owner or manager.
* **Property Information** (property_info, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the property, including its address, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Issues Found** (issues_found, select_multiple, optional): Choose all issues that were discovered during the inspection from the list provided.
* **Repair Requests** (repair_requests, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any repairs or maintenance are needed for the issues found.
* **Date Inspected** (date_inspected, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time Inspected** (time_inspected, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection in 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Inspector Name** (inspector_name, text, optional): Enter your name and title/position as the inspector.
* **Signature** (signature, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm that the inspection was conducted and the information is accurate.
