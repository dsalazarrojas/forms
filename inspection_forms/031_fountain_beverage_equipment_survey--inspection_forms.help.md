# Fountain Beverage Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on the status of fountain equipment being inspected. It will help track and record details about the equipment, such as its type, location, condition, and maintenance history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment being inspected from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the Equipment ID, if applicable.
3. Enter the location of the equipment.
4. Select the condition of the equipment (Excellent, Fair, Poor).
5. Enter any relevant maintenance details.
6. Select the date of the last inspection using the date picker.
7. Choose the person who performed the last inspection.
8. Enter the date of the last inspection.
9. Add any comments or observations about the inspection.
10. Enter the email of the person completing the form (optional).
11. Enter the phone number of the person completing the form (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the type of equipment being inspected**: This field asks for the type of equipment being inspected, such as Equipment ID, Pump, Cooler, or Water Treatment System.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the identification number of the equipment, if applicable. This can be a serial number, model number, or any other unique identifier for the equipment.
* **Location of the equipment**: Enter the location where the equipment is installed or stored.
* **Condition of the equipment**: Select the condition of the equipment from the dropdown menu (Excellent, Fair, Poor).
* **Maintenance Details**: Enter any relevant details about the maintenance history of the equipment.
* **Date of Last Inspection**: Select the date of the last inspection using the date picker.
* **Last inspected by**: Choose the person who performed the last inspection from the dropdown menu.
* **Last Inspected Date**: Select the date of the last inspection using the date picker.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or observations about the inspection. This field is free text, so feel free to include as much information as needed.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person completing the form, if you'd like to receive updates or confirmations. This is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person completing the form, if you'd like to receive calls or updates. This is optional.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us better understand the status of the equipment and provide better service.
