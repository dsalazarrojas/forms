# Dealer Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a dealer site inspection. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to document and record observations during an inspection visit to a dealer site. This includes site address, name, date, and time of inspection, type of inspection, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Site Address**: Enter the physical address of the site being inspected.
2. **Site Name**: Enter the name of the site being inspected.
3. **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection visit.
4. **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection visit.
5. **Inspection Type**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether an inspection was conducted at the site.
6. **Site Condition**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the site's condition at the time of inspection.
7. **Notes**: Add any relevant notes about the site or inspection.
8. **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the inspection report.
9. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the inspection report.
10. **Files**: Attach any supporting files or documents related to the inspection.
11. **Submitted By**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the inspection was submitted by an authorized person.
12. **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the inspection report was submitted.
13. **Submitted Time**: Enter the time the inspection report was submitted.
14. **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the report was assigned to someone.
15. **Site Location**: Enter the location of the site being inspected.
16. **Latitude**: Enter the latitude of the site's location.
17. **Longitude**: Enter the longitude of the site's location.
18. **Location Notes**: Add any additional notes about the site's location.
19. **Photos**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if photos were taken during the inspection.
20. **Videos**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if videos were taken during the inspection.
21. **Audio Recording**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if an audio recording was made during the inspection.
22. **Site Elevation**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the site's elevation.
23. **Submitted By Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the inspection report.
24. **Inspector Comments**: Add any comments from the inspector.
25. **Site Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the site.
26. **Assigned To Name**: Enter the name of the person assigned to review the inspection report.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the site's address, name, and any previous inspections or notes before starting the inspection.
* Attach any supporting files or documents related to the inspection.
* Enter the correct date, time, and email of the person submitting the report.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that require a yes/no answer.
* Enter any relevant notes or comments about the site or inspection.
* Review and proofread your completed form carefully before submission.
