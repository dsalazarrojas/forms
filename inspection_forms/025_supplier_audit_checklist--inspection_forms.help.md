# supplier_audit_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The supplier audit checklist is a form used to gather information about a supplier's performance, products, and services as part of a systematic evaluation to ensure they meet company quality and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the supplier's recent performance, products, and services to determine the purpose of the audit.
2. Select the relevant options for each checklist section.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the audit in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Complete any other required fields as instructed by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Checklist** (`product_checklist`, select_one, required): Choose the level of compliance with company policies and procedures for the supplier's products.
	+ Select one of the three options:
		- 1: Fully compliant
		- 2: Partially compliant
		- 3: Non-compliant
* **Service Checklist** (`service_checklist`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of compliance with company policies and procedures for the supplier's services.
	+ Select all the options that apply:
		- 1: Fully compliant
		- 2: Partially compliant
		- 3: Non-compliant
* **Administration Checklist** (`administration_checklist`, select_one, required): Choose the level of compliance with company policies and procedures for the supplier's administration.
	+ Select one of the three options:
		- 1: Fully compliant
		- 2: Partially compliant
		- 3: Non-compliant
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or observations regarding the supplier's performance, products, or services.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit in the format HH:mm.
* **Supervisor's Comments** (none, none, not applicable): This field is not applicable, please ignore.
