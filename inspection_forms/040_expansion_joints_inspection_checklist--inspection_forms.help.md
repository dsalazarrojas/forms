# Expansion Joints Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Expansion Joints Inspection Checklist is used to collect information about the condition of expansion joints on a bridge or building. This form is a structured guide to ensure that all necessary details are documented during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please navigate through its multiple pages.
2.  Enter the required information in each field according to the labels below.
3.  Make sure to provide clear and concise comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant general information about the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the building or bridge being inspected.
* **Joint Condition** (`joint_condition`, select_one, required): Select the condition of the expansion joint from 1 to 5.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): If applicable, provide a link or description of a photo related to the inspection.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, optional): Enter recommended actions for the expansion joint based on the inspection result.
* **Bridge Type** (`bridge_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of bridge or building being inspected.
* **Joint Location** (`joint_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the expansion joint within the building or bridge.
* **Joint Size** (`joint_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the joint.
* **Joint Orientation** (`joint_orientation`, text, optional): Enter the orientation of the joint.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Building Location** (`building_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the building or bridge.
* **Bridge Material** (`bridge_material`, text, optional): Enter the material used for the bridge or building.
* **Recommended Actions Other** (`recommended_actions_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional recommended actions not listed.
* **Photos** (`photos`, note, optional): Attach photos of the expansion joint.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): Describe the photo attached.
* **Photo Tag** (`photo_tag`, text, optional): Tag the photo with a keyword or description.
* **Photo Comment** (`photo_comment`, text, optional): Enter a comment about the photo.
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption`, text, optional): Enter a caption for the photo.
* **Bridge Condition** (`bridge_condition`, text, optional): Enter the condition of the bridge or building.
* **Expansion Joint Size** (`expansion_joint_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the expansion joint.
* **Joint Movement** (`joint_movement`, text, optional): Enter the movement of the joint.
* **Recommended Action Date** (`recommended_action_date`, text, optional): Enter the date for the recommended action.
* **Bridge Type** (`bridge_type`, text, optional): Repeat of the bridge type.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it is still essential to provide as much information as possible for accurate record-keeping.
