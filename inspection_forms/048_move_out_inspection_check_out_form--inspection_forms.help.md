# Move Out Inspection Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Move Out Inspection Check Out Form is a document used to verify the condition of a property after a tenant has moved out. This form helps to ensure that the property is left in good condition, and any damages or issues are documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing detailed information about the condition of the property.
2. Fill in all required fields and any additional comments or notes that may be helpful for the owner or property manager.
3. If you are unsure about a particular item, check the corresponding checkbox or select an option that best represents your answer.
4. Review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_1** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the property location and date.
* **property_condition** (Text, Optional): Describe the overall condition of the property when you left it.
* **damage** (Number, Optional): If there is any damage, provide a numerical value to indicate its severity (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is minor and 5 is major).
* **property_damage** (Text, Optional): Describe any specific damage or issues with the property.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_6** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if there was any damage to the property (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_7** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any appliances or equipment that were missing or not functioning properly.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_8** (Date, Optional): Date and time you completed the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_9** (Time, Optional): Time of day you completed the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_10** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_11** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if any items were missing or not in good working order (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_12** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the property was left clean and tidy (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_13** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any appliances or equipment that were missing or not functioning properly.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_14** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the property was left in good condition (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_15** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_17** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the property was left in clean and tidy condition (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_18** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the property was free of pests and rodents (True or False).
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_19** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any appliances or equipment that were missing or not functioning properly.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_20** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_21** (Email, Optional): Email address of the tenant or property manager.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_22** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_23** (Note, Optional): Any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_24** (Text, Optional): Additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **move_out_inspection_check_out_form_25** (Time, Optional): Time of day you completed the inspection.
