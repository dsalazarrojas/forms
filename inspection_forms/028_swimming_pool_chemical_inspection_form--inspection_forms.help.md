# swimming_pool_chemical_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for pool inspectors to gather and document the chemical inspection of a swimming pool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of pool being inspected (heated or non-heated).
2. Enter the current water temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
3. Record the pH level of the pool water, within the range of 7.2-8.0.
4. Measure and record the chlorine level within the range of 0.5-5.0.
5. Determine the level of alkalinity, within the range of 0.0-10.0.
6. Indicate which maintenance actions have been taken or recommended for the pool (select all that apply).
7. Provide any additional maintenance notes.
8. Schedule and record the date of the next inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Type** (Pool with heater, Pool without heater): 
  * Select the type of pool being inspected. This will help inspectors tailor their evaluation according to pool type.
* **Water Temperature (°F)**: 
  * Enter the current water temperature in degrees Fahrenheit. This field is not required.
* **pH level**: 
  * Record the pH level of the pool water, within the range of 7.2-8.0. A pH level outside this range may indicate potential issues with pool maintenance or water quality.
* **Chlorine level**: 
  * Measure and record the chlorine level within the range of 0.5-5.0. This field is not required, but it will help inspectors identify potential issues with pool disinfection.
* **Alkalinity level**: 
  * Determine the level of alkalinity within the range of 0.0-10.0. This field is not required, but it will help inspectors evaluate pool water stability.
* **Maintenance Actions** (Water sample taken, Pump inspection, Equipment maintenance): 
  * Indicate which maintenance actions have been taken or recommended for the pool. Select all that apply.
* **Maintenance Notes**: 
  * Provide any additional notes on pool maintenance, such as repairs or adjustments made.
* **Next Inspection Date**: 
  * Schedule and record the date of the next inspection, following the YYYY-MM-YYYY format. This field is not required, but it will help pool owners stay on track with regular inspections.
