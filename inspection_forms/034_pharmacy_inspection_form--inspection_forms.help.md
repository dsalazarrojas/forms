# Pharmacy Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Inspection Form is a standard form used to collect information from pharmacies during an inspection. This form helps ensure that all pharmacies are operating in compliance with regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the required fields filled in.
2. Complete all required fields, including:
	* Section Five (Date and time)
	* Section Seven (Contact information, phone)
	* Section Eight (Additional contact information, phone)
3. For optional fields, choose the relevant information that applies to your pharmacy.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section One (First page)**: This is the first page of the form, which may contain an introduction or instructions.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Safety Section (Section Two)**: This section collects information about the safety protocols and procedures in place at the pharmacy.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Compliance Section (Section Three)**: This section collects information about the compliance status of the pharmacy with regulations and standards.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Additional Information (Section Four)**: This section is for any additional information that may be relevant to the inspection.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **Contact Information (Section Five)**: This section collects the contact information of the person filling out the form.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date and Time (Section Six)**: This section collects the date and time of the inspection.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Time (Section Seven)**: This section collects the time of the inspection.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: No
* **Contact Information (phone) (Section Eight)**: This section collects the contact information of the person filling out the form.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Additional Contact Information (phone) (Section Nine)**: This section is for any additional contact information that may be relevant.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
* If you are unsure about a specific field, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you need help, don't hesitate to reach out to the pharmacy team for assistance.
* Please review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
