# Food Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to aid food safety inspectors in conducting thorough inspections of facilities, food vehicles, and equipment to identify potential hazards and ensure compliance with food safety laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review this form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Fill in the details as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Make sure to include any observations, notes, and evidence collected during the inspection.
4. Ensure you answer all questions honestly and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter a brief description of the facility or food vehicle being inspected.
* **Facility Info**: Record any information about the facility or food vehicle, including its type and address.
* **Facility Equipment**: Check if the facility has any equipment that may pose a food safety risk. Select multiple options if applicable.
* **Facility Operations**: Specify the time of day when the inspection took place (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Date Arrived**: Enter the date you arrived at the facility or food vehicle.
* **Time Arrived**: Record the time you arrived at the facility or food vehicle.
* **Inspection Started**: Enter the time the inspection started.
* **Inspection Ended**: Enter the time the inspection ended.
* **Food Vehicle Present**: Check if a food vehicle was present at the facility.
* **Vehicle Present**: Select all food vehicles present at the facility.
* **Facility Owner Present**: Check if the facility owner was present during the inspection.
* **Facility Owner Name**: Record the name of the facility owner.
* **Facility Owner Contact**: Enter the contact information for the facility owner.
* **Notes**: Take any additional notes regarding the inspection, including observations, concerns, or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips

* Be thorough in your answers to ensure accurate and comprehensive information.
* Take photos, videos, or make notes to support your observations and findings.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, seek guidance from your supervisor or a colleague.
* Remember that this form is for official use only, and all information collected will be kept confidential.
