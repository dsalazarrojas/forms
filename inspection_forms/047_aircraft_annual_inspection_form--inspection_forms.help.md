# Aircraft Annual Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information for the annual inspection of an aircraft. The form helps to ensure that the aircraft is in compliance with regulatory requirements and identifies any issues that need to be addressed. Completing this form is an important step in maintaining the airworthiness of your aircraft.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter the tail number or registration identifier of the aircraft.
4. If applicable, enter the total hours flown since the last inspection.
5. List any discrepancies or defects found during the inspection.
6. Describe any required repairs or recommendations for the aircraft.
7. Provide the status of any life-limited parts, including their condition and remaining life.
8. Select whether the aircraft is compliant or non-compliant with airworthiness directives.
9. Sign and date the form, confirming that you have completed the inspection and are aware of any issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector name**: The name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection date**: The date of the annual inspection, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Aircraft registration**: The tail number or registration identifier of the aircraft.
* **Hours flown since last**: If applicable, enter the total hours flown since the last inspection. (Optional)
* **Discrepancies found**: List any defects or maintenance items that were found during the inspection.
* **Required repairs**: Describe any actions needed before the aircraft can be released.
* **Life limited parts status**: Provide the condition and remaining life of any life-limited parts.
* **Compliance status**: Select whether the aircraft is compliant or non-compliant with airworthiness directives.
* **Inspector signature acknowledgement**: Sign and date the form, confirming that you have completed the inspection and are aware of any issues.
