# Free Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and service request details from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the First Name field.
2. Fill in your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the Contact E-mail field. Please use a valid email address.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Contact Phone field.
5. Enter your contact address in the Contact Address field.
6. Select whether or not you are requesting a service by choosing one of the options for Service Type.
7. In the Service Details field, describe the specific service you are requesting.
8. (Optional) If you have a preferred tool or equipment, enter it in the Assigned Tool field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select Yes if you are requesting a service, No if you are not.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Describe the specific service you are requesting.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have a preferred tool or equipment, enter it here.
