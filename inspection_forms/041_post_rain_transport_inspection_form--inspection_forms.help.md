# Post Rain Transport Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Rain Transport Inspection Form is used to record information about a vehicle that has been inspected after exposure to rain. This form helps ensure that the vehicle has been properly inspected and cleared for transport.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct vehicle type from the options provided.
2. Enter the vehicle number if applicable.
3. Enter the vehicle's license number if applicable.
4. Enter the vehicle's color if known.
5. Enter the vehicle's weight if applicable.
6. Select the vehicle's condition as it pertains to its exposure to rain.
7. Enter the vehicle's location if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the vehicle's driver if known.
9. Enter the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (Select One):
	+ Choose the correct category for your vehicle, such as Vehicle, Motorcycle, Car, Truck, or Tractor.
* **Vehicle Number** (Text):
	+ If you are reporting on a specific vehicle, enter its identification number here.
* **License Number** (Text):
	+ If you are reporting on a registered vehicle, enter its license number here.
* **Vehicle Color** (Text):
	+ Enter the vehicle's primary color.
* **Vehicle Weight** (Number):
	+ Enter the total weight of the vehicle, including cargo.
* **Vehicle Condition** (Select One):
	+ Indicate the condition of the vehicle as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Vehicle Location** (Text):
	+ Enter the location where the vehicle was inspected.
* **Vehicle Driver** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the vehicle's driver.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the vehicle inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a successful inspection.
* If the vehicle has not been exposed to rain, do not fill out this form.
* Only complete this form if you are authorized to do so.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
