# Industrial Equipment Health Log Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Equipment Health Log Report is a form used to document the health and maintenance status of industrial equipment. It captures information about equipment details, inspection dates, issues, and maintenance history to help inform maintenance prioritization and resource allocation decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the equipment type and details in the **Equipment Details** field.
2. Enter dates of past inspections in the **Inspection Dates** field.
3. Record any issues or problems in the **Issue Log** field.
4. Indicate whether maintenance has been performed on the equipment by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Maintenance History** field.
5. Add photos of the equipment, if applicable, in the **Photos** field.
6. Enter any additional comments about the equipment in the **Comments** field.
7. Indicate which personnel are responsible for maintenance tasks by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Assigned To** field.
8. Confirm which person completed the inspection by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Completed By** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Details** (required): Enter the name, model number, or description of the equipment being inspected. This field is used to identify the specific equipment being reported on.
* **Inspection Dates** (optional): Enter the dates of past inspections, including start and end dates. This field is used to track inspection schedules and frequencies.
* **Issue Log** (optional): Record any issues or problems encountered during the inspection. This field is used to document problems that require maintenance attention.
* **Maintenance History** (optional): Indicate whether maintenance has been performed on the equipment by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This field helps track maintenance activities and schedules.
* **Photos** (optional): Attach photos of the equipment, if applicable, to support issue documentation or maintenance planning.
* **Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment. This field is for free-form comments or explanations.
* **Assigned To** (optional): Indicate which personnel are responsible for maintenance tasks by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This field helps track maintenance responsibilities and schedules.
* **Completed By** (optional): Confirm which person completed the inspection by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This field helps track completion status and ownership.
