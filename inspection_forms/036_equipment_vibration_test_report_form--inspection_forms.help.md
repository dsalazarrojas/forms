# Equipment Vibration Test Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the results of equipment vibration tests, including test conditions, equipment used, measurements, and any relevant remarks. It helps in monitoring and ensuring the proper functioning of equipment in a maintenance or quality control context.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and relevance to your specific situation.
- Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- For fields with multiple options, select the most applicable choice.
- Ensure you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Sign and date the form upon completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Conditions**: Describe the conditions under which the equipment was tested (e.g., temperature, humidity, etc.).
* **Test Equipment**: Indicate whether the equipment was tested (select all that apply) or not.
* **Measurements**: Record any relevant measurements taken during the test (e.g., frequency, amplitude, etc.). This field is optional but may be required depending on the specific test conditions.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks related to the test (e.g., notable observations, special circumstances).
* **Test Recommendations**: Indicate whether the test resulted in any recommendations for future tests or maintenance actions (select one of the provided options, typically "Yes" or "No").
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the test.
* **Date**: Record the date the test was performed.
* **Time**: Record the time the test was performed. This field is optional.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the form has been completed and reviewed.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name, as the inspector reviewing the form.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being tested.
