# Container Entry Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Container Entry Survey form is used to gather information about a container's inspection, including details about the inspector, the container, and the results of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your information as the inspector in the `Inspector Full Name`, `Inspector Identification Number`, and confirm that the form is for the correct container.
2. Enter the `Inspection Date` and `Inspection Time` fields to record the date and time of the inspection.
3. Select the `Container Type` from the available options (e.g., Standard (20ft), Standard (40ft), High Cube (40ft), Refrigerator (40ft), Open Top, or Other).
4. Rate the `Overall Container Condition` as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged.
5. Confirm the `Seal Integrity` as True or False.
6. If applicable, provide `Damage Details` about any damage to the container.
7. Rate the `Cleanliness Rating` as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Dirty.
8. Confirm that the inspection is completed by selecting `Inspection Completed?`.
9. Leave any additional comments or details in the `Any additional details or comments?` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Full Name**: Enter your full name as the inspector performing the survey.
* **Inspector Identification Number**: Enter your identification number as the inspector.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Container Identification Number**: Enter the identification number of the container being inspected.
* **Container Type**: Select the type of container from the options provided.
* **Overall Container Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the container as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged.
* **Seal Integrity**: Confirm the seal integrity of the container as True or False.
* **Damage Details**: If the container is damaged, provide details about the damage in this field.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the container as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Dirty.
* **Inspection Completed**: Confirm that the inspection is completed.
* **Any additional details or comments?**: Leave any additional comments or details about the inspection in this field.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate before submitting the form.

Note: This form is used for inspection purposes, so be accurate and thorough when filling it out.
