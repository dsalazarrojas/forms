# Property Inspection Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property inspection, which is a crucial process for maintaining or upkeeping residential or commercial properties. The collected data will help property managers, inspectors, and other stakeholders assess the condition and status of a property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the property type from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the property address and location in the respective text fields.
3. Provide the property size and year built.
4. Describe the property condition using the text field.
5. Specify the property status as Active or Inactive.
6. Enter the inspection date.
7. Provide your name, phone number, and email as the inspector.
8. If applicable, enter the property manager's name, phone number, and email.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type** (1):
	+ Select the type of property (Single Family Home, Condo, or Townhouse) from the dropdown menu.
* **Property Address** (3):
	+ Enter the address of the property, including street number and street name.
* **Property Location** (4):
	+ Enter the location or area where the property is situated.
* **Property Size** (5):
	+ Enter the size of the property in square feet or other relevant units.
* **Property Year Built** (6):
	+ Enter the year the property was built.
* **Property Condition** (7):
	+ Describe the current condition of the property, including any issues or concerns.
* **Property Status** (8):
	+ Select whether the property is Active or Inactive.
* **Inspection Date** (9):
	+ Enter the date of the property inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (10):
	+ Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone** (11):
	+ Enter your phone number as the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (12):
	+ Enter your email address as the inspector.
* **Property Manager Name** (13):
	+ Enter the name of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Phone** (14):
	+ Enter the phone number of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Email** (15):
	+ Enter the email address of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Notes** (16):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Comments** (17):
	+ Add any further comments or observations about the property inspection.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* Use the provided fields to capture all relevant information about the property.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, refer to the relevant authorities or systems for guidance.
