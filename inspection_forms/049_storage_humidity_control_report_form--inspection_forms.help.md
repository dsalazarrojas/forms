# Storage Humidity Control Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you report and track humidity control issues in storage facilities. It collects information on storage conditions, corrective actions taken, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact details (Facility Name, Location, Contact Person, and Email) in the corresponding fields.
2. Record the date when the report was filled.
3. Enter the temperature and humidity readings for the storage area.
4. Check the "Corrective Action Taken" field to select the corrective action taken to address the issue.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the storage area in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`Facility Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the storage facility where the report is being filled.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the storage facility.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the report is filled.
* **Temperature** (`Temperature`, number, required/optional): Record the temperature reading of the storage area.
* **Humidity** (`Humidity`, number, required/optional): Record the humidity reading of the storage area.
* **Corrective Action Taken** (`Corrective Action Taken`, select_one, required/optional): Select the corrective action taken to address the issue.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the storage area.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person responsible for the storage area.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
