# Industrial Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Equipment Inspection Form is used to collect data on industrial equipment inspections, including the type of equipment, inspection date, and any notes from the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct type of equipment from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the Inspection Date field.
3. Fill in the Equipment Location field with the location of the equipment.
4. Enter any notes about the inspection in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type (Type 3)** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of equipment being inspected from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the inspection process, such as any issues or areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are familiar with the type of equipment being inspected before completing this form.
* Enter dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Be thorough in your notes, as they will help inform future maintenance or repair decisions.
