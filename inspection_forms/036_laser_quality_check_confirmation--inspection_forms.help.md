# Laser Quality Check Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Quality Check Confirmation form is designed to ensure that all necessary steps for a laser quality check are completed correctly. It is intended for situations where a laser quality check is required but not related to other quality checks. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. Select "Pass", "Fail", or "Not Applicable" for the "Quality Control" field, depending on the outcome of the laser quality check.
3. If you selected "Fail", provide a reason for failure in the "Reason for Failure" field.
4. Enter any additional comments about the quality check in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Form** (`inspection_form_1`, text, required: false): This field is not currently used in this form.
* **Quality Control** (`inspection_form_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Pass" if the laser quality check was successful, "Fail" if it was not, or "Not Applicable" if the quality check was not performed.
* **Reason for Failure** (`inspection_form_3`, text, required: false): Enter a reason for the failure if you selected "Fail" in the "Quality Control" field.
* **Comments** (`inspection_form_4`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the laser quality check.
