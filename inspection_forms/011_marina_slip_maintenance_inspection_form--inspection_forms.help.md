# Marina Slip Maintenance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Slip Maintenance Inspection Form is used to assess the current maintenance status of a marina slip and ensure that it is in compliance with required safety and regulatory standards. This form is essential for identifying any maintenance needs and scheduling necessary repairs to prevent accidents and downtime. By using this form, marina managers can efficiently track maintenance progress and prioritize tasks based on the level of maintenance required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select the current maintenance status of the marina slip from the options provided.
* Enter the address of the marina where the slip is located.
* Enter the date of the most recent inspection or the date when the maintenance was performed.
* If you are the person performing the inspection, enter your name.
* If available, enter your contact phone number in case additional information is needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Status (maintenance_status)** 
  (Select One)
  • This field helps us understand the current maintenance status of the marina slip. Please select from the options:
  • **Needs Maintenance**: The marina slip requires immediate attention and repair.
  • **No Action Required**: The marina slip does not require maintenance at this time.
  • **Completed**: The maintenance for the marina slip has been completed.

* **Marina Address (marina_address)** 
  (Text)
  • Enter the address of the marina where the slip is located. This will help us track the location of the slip.

* **Inspection Date (inspection_date)** 
  (Date)
  • Enter the date of the most recent inspection or the date when the maintenance was performed. Use the standard date format: mm/dd/yyyy.

* **Inspector Name (inspector_name)** 
  (Text)
  • If you are the person performing the inspection, enter your name. This will help us identify who performed the inspection.

* **Inspector Phone (inspector_phone)** 
  (Text)
  • If available, enter your contact phone number in case additional information is needed. This will help us contact you with any further questions or concerns.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately select the maintenance status to reflect the actual condition of the marina slip.
* Double-check the address of the marina to ensure it is correct.
* Use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy) when entering the inspection date.
* If you are not the inspector, please ensure that the inspector's name and phone number are accurate.
* Please note that this form is used to document maintenance progress and prioritize tasks. Your input is invaluable in helping us maintain a safe and well-maintained marina slip.
