# School Bus Driver’s Daily Pre Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help school bus drivers conduct a daily pre-trip inspection to identify and document any issues or concerns with their vehicle before starting their route.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each section based on the actual condition of the vehicle.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
4. Complete all required fields.
5. Keep the form up-to-date and accurate.
