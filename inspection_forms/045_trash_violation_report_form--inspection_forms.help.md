# trash_violation_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reporting a trash violation incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Resident Name" field with the name of the resident who is involved in the incident.
2. Enter the address where the incident occurred in the "address" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Description" field.
4. If available, attach a photo as proof of the incident in the "Photo Proof" field.
5. Enter the date of the incident in the "Date of Violation" field.
6. If a witness is reporting the incident, enter their name in the "Witness Name" field.
7. If a witness is reporting the incident, enter their phone number in the "Witness Phone Number" field.
8. Select the person who the report is assigned to from the "Assigned To" field.
9. Select the type of report from the "Submitted For" field.
10. Enter any additional comments about the incident in the "Comments" field.
11. If a witness is reporting the incident, attach photos as proof of the incident in the "Witness Photos" field.
12. If a resident is reporting the incident, attach photos as proof of the incident in the "Resident Photos" field.
13. Enter the date when the report is submitted in the "Submitted On" field.
14. Enter the date when the resident's signature is obtained in the "Resident Signature Date" field.
15. Enter the date when the witness's signature is obtained in the "Witness Signature Date" field.
16. Enter the date when the report is submitted for review in the "Submitted For Date" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the resident who is involved in the incident.
* **Address (text, required)**: Enter the address where the incident occurred.
* **Phone Number (text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the resident.
* **Description (text, required)**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Photo Proof (text, optional)**: If available, attach a photo as proof of the incident.
* **Date of Violation (date, required)**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Witness Name (text, optional)**: If a witness is reporting the incident, enter their name.
* **Witness Phone Number (text, optional)**: If a witness is reporting the incident, enter their phone number.
* **Assigned To (select_one, required)**: Select the person who the report is assigned to.
* **Submitted By (select_one, required)**: Select whether the report is submitted by the resident or a witness.
* **Submitted On (date, required)**: Enter the date when the report is submitted.
* **Submitted For (select_one, required)**: Select the type of report.
* **Location Details (text, required)**: Enter any additional details about the location of the incident.
* **Signature (text, required)**: Enter your signature.
* **Witness Signature (text, optional)**: If a witness is reporting the incident, enter their signature.
* **Witness Signature Date (date, optional)**: If a witness is reporting the incident, enter the date of their signature.
* **Resident Signature (text, required)**: Enter the resident's signature.
* **Resident Signature Date (date, required)**: Enter the date when the resident's signature is obtained.
* **Submitted For Date (date, required)**: Enter the date when the report is submitted for review.
* **Notes (text, optional)**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Photos (text, optional)**: If a witness or resident is reporting the incident, attach photos as proof of the incident.
* **Witness Photos (text, optional)**: If a witness is reporting the incident, attach photos as proof of the incident.
* **Resident Photos (text, optional)**: If a resident is reporting the incident, attach photos as proof of the incident.
* **Submitted (text, required)**: Enter a confirmation that the report is submitted.
