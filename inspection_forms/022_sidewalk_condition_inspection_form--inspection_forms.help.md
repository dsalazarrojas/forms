# Sidewalk Condition Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect the condition of sidewalks and report any issues or observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the introduction page for any specific instructions or context.
* Select the condition of the sidewalk (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* Choose all relevant locations where the sidewalk is located (location1, location2, or location3).
* Enter the date of the inspection (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
* Provide any relevant photos or comments about the condition of the sidewalk.
* Enter the name of the user who is assigned to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### * **Sidewalk Condition** (`sidewalk_condition`, select_one, required)
Select the current condition of the sidewalk from the available options.

### * **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required)
Choose all locations where the sidewalk is currently located.

### * **Condition Date** (`condition_date`, date, required)
Enter the date when the inspection was completed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### * **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional)
Upload any photos that are relevant to the inspection.

### * **Comments** (`comments`, text, required)
Enter any comments or observations about the condition of the sidewalk.

### * **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, email, required)
Enter the email address of the user who is assigned to complete this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant locations where the sidewalk is located.
* Enter the correct date when completing the inspection.
* Provide clear and accurate comments about the condition of the sidewalk.
* Enter the correct email of the user who is assigned to complete this form.
