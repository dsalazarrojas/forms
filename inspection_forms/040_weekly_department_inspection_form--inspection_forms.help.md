# weekly_department_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Form

### Page 1

*   **department_name** (Required: false)
    *   *department_name (text)*
        + Enter the name of the department or area being inspected.
*   **supervisor_name** (Required: false)
    *   *supervisor_name (text)*
        + Enter the name of the supervisor or inspector performing the inspection.
*   **inspection_date** (Required: false)
    *   *inspection_date (date)*
        + Enter the date of the inspection using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **inspection_time** (Required: false)
    *   *inspection_time (time)*
        + If the inspection was conducted during a specific time, enter it in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **areas_of_interest** (Required: false)
    *   *areas_of_interest (text)*
        + Identify areas of interest for the inspection, such as equipment, cleanliness, or other specific aspects of the department or area.
*   **observations** (Required: false)
    *   *observations (text)*
        + Document any notable observations made during the inspection.
*   **corrective_actions** (Required: false)
    *   *corrective_actions (text)*
        + Describe any corrective actions taken or planned to address issues found during the inspection.
*   **notes** (Required: false)
    *   *notes (note)*
        + Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection, such as photos or other supporting information.
*   **signature** (Required: false)
    *   *signature (text)*
        + If required, add your signature to the form to signify that you have completed and reviewed the inspection form.
