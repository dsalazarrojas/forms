# Government Accessibility Standards Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Accessibility Standards Inspection Form is used to inspect facilities and determine their compliance with government accessibility standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility's address in the "Facility Address" field.
2. Enter the facility's name in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Select whether the facility is "Yes" or "No" in the "Facility Type" field.
4. Enter the name of the facility inspector in the "Facility Inspector" field.
5. Enter the contact information for the facility in the "Facility Contact" field.
6. Select whether the facility meets the accessibility standards version in the "Facility Accessibility Standards Version" field.
7. Select whether the facility meets the accessibility standards in the "Facility Accessibility Standards Compliance" field.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "Facility Notes" field.
9. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
10. Enter the time of the inspection in the "Inspection Time" field.
11. Enter the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field.
12. Enter any additional notes about the inspection in the "Inspection Notes" field.
13. Enter the facility's ID in the "Facility ID" field.
14. Enter your signature as the inspector in the "Inspector Signature" field.
15. Enter the name of the agency that conducted the inspection in the "Agency" field.
16. Enter the name of the department within the agency that conducted the inspection in the "Department" field.
17. Enter the contact person for the agency in the "Contact Person" field.
18. Enter the facility's phone number in the "Facility Phone" field (optional).
19. Enter the facility's email address in the "Facility Email" field (optional).
20. Enter the facility's fax number in the "Facility Fax" field (optional).
21. Enter the facility's website in the "Facility Website" field (optional).
22. Enter the second line of the facility's address in the "Facility Address Line 2" field (optional).
23. Enter the city of the facility's address in the "Facility City" field (optional).
24. Enter the state of the facility's address in the "Facility State" field (optional).
25. Enter the zip code of the facility's address in the "Facility Zip" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Type**: Select whether the facility is "Yes" or "No" to indicate its type.
* **Facility Inspector**: Enter the name of the facility inspector.
* **Facility Contact**: Enter the contact information for the facility.
* **Facility Accessibility Standards Version**: Select whether the facility meets the accessibility standards version.
* **Facility Accessibility Standards Compliance**: Select whether the facility meets the accessibility standards.
* **Facility Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the facility.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Location**: Enter the location of the inspection.
* **Inspection Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the facility's ID.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date your signature as the inspector.
* **Agency**: Enter the name of the agency that conducted the inspection.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department within the agency that conducted the inspection.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person for the agency.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the facility's phone number (optional).
* **Facility Email**: Enter the facility's email address (optional).
* **Facility Fax**: Enter the facility's fax number (optional).
* **Facility Website**: Enter the facility's website (optional).
* **Facility Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of the facility's address (optional).
* **Facility City**: Enter the city of the facility's address (optional).
* **Facility State**: Enter the state of the facility's address (optional).
* **Facility Zip**: Enter the zip code of the facility's address (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Check the "Facility Accessibility Standards Compliance" field carefully to ensure the facility meets the standards.
* Enter accurate and complete information about the facility and its inspection.
* Keep a record of all inspections conducted.
