# Shipping Dock Operational Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure the safety and security of your shipping dock operations. It's essential to fill out this form to identify potential hazards, inspect safety equipment, and document any actions required to maintain a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the inspection date and time of the form.
2. Enter your name as the inspector.
3. Enter the vessel ID and shipper ID as applicable.
4. Select all safety equipment that you have checked on the vessel or within the dock area.
5. Identify any hazards observed during the inspection.
6. Describe the hazards you've observed.
7. Specify the actions required to address the hazards.
8. Schedule a follow-up date and time for the next inspection.
9. Assign a team member to be responsible for following up on the actions required.
10. Add any additional notes or comments as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date (1)** (`inspection_date`, date, **required**): This is the date when the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspection Time (2)** (`inspection_time`, time, **required**): This is the time when the inspection was completed.
* **Inspector Name (3)** (`inspector_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your name as the inspector conducting the form.
* **Vessel ID (4)** (`vessel_id`, text, **required**): Enter the ID of the vessel that was inspected.
* **Shipper ID (5)** (`shipper_id`, text, **required**): Enter the ID of the shipper (if applicable).
* **Safety Equipment Checklist (6)** (`safety_equipment_checklist`, select_multiple, **required**):
	+ `Anchor winch`
	+ `Fire extinguisher`
	+ `Emergency telephone`
	+ `Safety harness`
* **Safety Equipment Inscope (7)** (`safety_equipment_inscope`, text, **required**): Describe the safety equipment inspected.
* **Hazards Observed (8)** (`hazards_observed`, select_multiple, **required**):
	+ `Electrical`
	+ `Fire`
	+ `Water`
	+ `Gas`
	+ `Other`
* **Description (9)** (`hazards_description`, text, **required**): Describe the hazards observed.
* **Action Required (10)** (`action_required`, text, **required**): Specify the actions needed to address the hazards.
* **Follow-up Date (11)** (`follow_up_date`, date, **required**): Schedule a follow-up date for the next inspection.
* **Follow-up Time (12)** (`follow_up_time`, time, **required**): Schedule a follow-up time for the next inspection.
* **Assigned To (13)** (`assigned_to`, select_one, **required**):
	+ `Manager`
	+ `Operations`
	+ `Supervisor`
	+ `Other`
* **Notes (14)** (`notes`, text, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Completed Date (15)** (`completed_date`, date, **optional**): This is an optional field for recording when the form was completed.
* **Completed Time (16)** (`completed_time`, time, **optional**): This is an optional field for recording the completion time.
* **Completed By (17)** (`completed_by`, text, **optional**): This is an optional field for recording who completed the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* This form is designed for your safety and security, so please take the necessary actions to address any hazards observed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
