# Sensor Inspection And Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about sensors, including their status, maintenance, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. First, select the sensors that are to be inspected and maintained from the "select_sensors" field.
2. In the "select_action" field, choose the maintenance actions that need to be performed on the selected sensors.
3. Enter the location where the sensors are installed in the "sensor_location" field.
4. In the "inspection_result" field, enter the results of the inspection and maintenance performed.
5. Select the inspection frequency for the sensors from the "sensor_inspection_frequency" field.
6. Enter the reorder frequency for the sensors from the "sensor_reorder_frequency" field.
7. Enter the total reorder cost for the sensors from the "sensor_reorder_total" field.
8. Choose the status of the sensor reorder from the "sensor_reorder_status" field.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "notes", "notes2", and "notes3" fields.
10. Select the date and time of the inspection and maintenance from the "select_date" and "select_time" fields.
11. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person in the "enter_email" and "enter_phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Select Sensors**: Select the sensors that are to be inspected and maintained from the list. This field is required.
* **2. Maintenance Actions**: In this field, choose the maintenance actions that need to be performed on the selected sensors. This field is required.
* **3. Sensor Location**: Enter the location where the sensors are installed. This field is required.
* **4. Inspection Result**: Enter the results of the inspection and maintenance performed. This field is required.
* **5. Inspection Frequency**: Select the inspection frequency for the sensors. This field is required.
* **6. Reorder Frequency**: Enter the reorder frequency for the sensors. This field is required.
* **7. Total Reorder Cost**: Enter the total reorder cost for the sensors. This field is required.
* **8. Sensor Reorder Status**: Choose the status of the sensor reorder. This field is required.
* **9. Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection and maintenance. This field is optional.
* **10. Notes 2**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the reorder. This field is optional.
* **11. Notes 3**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the reorder. This field is optional.
* **12. Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection and maintenance. This field is required.
* **13. Inspection Time**: Select the time of the inspection and maintenance. This field is required.
* **14. Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **15. Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **16. Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the inspection and maintenance. This field is optional.
