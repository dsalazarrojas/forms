# material_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material inspection checklist form is used to gather information about the condition of materials received at a facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the delivery condition of the material you are inspecting.
2.  Enter the date the material was delivered.
3.  Identify the source or supplier of the material.
4.  Indicate who inspected the material.
5.  Enter the date the inspection took place.
6.  Provide any comments about the condition of the material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Delivery Condition** (`delivery_condition`, text, not required):
    Please describe the condition of the material upon inspection, including any notable defects or issues.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, not required):
    Enter the date the material was delivered, which will help in tracking the timeline of material movement and usage.
*   **Received From** (`received_from`, text, not required):
    Provide the name or identifier of the supplier or source of the material.
*   **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`, text, not required):
    Indicate who performed the inspection, which may be important for accountability and quality control purposes.
*   **Inspected Date** (`inspected_date`, date, not required):
    Record the date the inspection took place, which helps in tracking the inspection and maintenance history of the material.
*   **Condition Comments** (`condition_comments`, text, not required):
    Enter any additional comments or notes about the material's condition, which can be useful for future reference and decision-making.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete the form accurately and consistently to maintain the integrity of the inspection data.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all required information is filled in.
- If unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
