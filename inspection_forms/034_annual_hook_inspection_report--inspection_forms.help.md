# Annual Hook Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Hook Inspection Report is a form designed to gather information about the condition of a hook during its annual inspection. It helps maintenance or quality control teams track the inspection history and identify potential issues with the hook, ensuring it remains safe and operational for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* **Inspector Full Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the contact email of the person conducting the inspection.
	* **Hook ID Number**: Enter the identification number of the hook being inspected.
	* **Hook Type**: Select the type of hook being inspected (e.g., Overhead Crane Hook, Hoist Hook, etc.).
	* **Hook Location**: Enter the location of the hook.
	* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
	* **Inspection Year**: Select the year this inspection is for.
	* **Overall Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the hook.
	* **Inspection Result**: Enter the result of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or Conditional).
	* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
	* **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name as the inspector.
2. If applicable, fill out the optional fields:
	* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or department responsible for the hook.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the person conducting the inspection.
	* **Manufacturer**: Enter the manufacturer of the hook.
	* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of the hook.
	* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the hook if available.
	* **Installation Date**: Enter the date the hook was installed.
	* **Rated Capacity**: Enter the maximum load capacity of the hook.
	* **Throat Opening Measurement**: Enter the current measurement of the hook's throat opening.
	* **Twist Check**: Check if there is any twist in the hook.
	* **Wear Check**: Check for excessive wear on the hook.
	* **Crack Inspection**: Check for any cracks in the hook.
	* **Deformation Check**: Check for any deformation in the hook.
	* **Latch Condition**: Check the condition of the safety latch.
	* **Latch Operation**: Check if the latch functions properly.
	* **Corrosion Check**: Check for any corrosion on the hook.
	* **Heat Damage**: Check for any heat damage on the hook.
	* **Deficiencies Found**: Describe any issues or deficiencies found during the inspection.
	* **Corrective Action Required**: Describe any action needed to address issues.
	* **Action Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the corrective action.
	* **Photos Attached**: Check if photos are attached to the form.
	* **Photo Details**: Describe the photos attached.
	* **Supervisor Review**: Check if the supervisor has reviewed the form.
	* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. If the form is incomplete or missing information, it will not be accepted.
