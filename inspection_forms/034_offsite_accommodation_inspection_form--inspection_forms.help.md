# offsite-accommodation-inspection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The offsite-accommodation-inspection-form is used to collect information for an offsite accommodation inspection. It is designed to be completed once for each property being inspected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the property name in the "property_name" field.
2. Enter the address of the property in the "address_1" field.
3. Enter the city of the property in the "city" field.
4. Enter the state of the property in the "state" field.
5. Enter the postal code of the property in the "postal_code" field.
6. Enter the contact name in the "contact_name" field.
7. Enter the contact email in the "contact_email" field.
8. Enter the contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
9. Select the date of the inspection in the "date_of_inspection" field.
10. Select the time of the inspection in the "time_of_inspection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **property_name**: Enter the name of the property being inspected.
* **address_1**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **city**: Enter the city where the property is located.
* **state**: Enter the state where the property is located.
* **postal_code**: Enter the postal code of the property.
* **contact_name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the property.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **date_of_inspection**: Select the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time_of_inspection**: Select the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
