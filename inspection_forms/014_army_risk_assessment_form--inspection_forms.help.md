# Army Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Risk Assessment Form is a crucial document that helps evaluate a soldier's risk level in various areas. This form is used to identify concerns and provide recommendations for improvement, ensuring the soldier's well-being and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the soldier's personal details, behavior, and performance.
2. Ensure that you answer all required questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure that you're capturing all relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Soldier Name**: Enter the soldier's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Rank**: Enter the soldier's rank within the army.
* **Service Number**: Enter the soldier's service number as it appears on their ID.
* **Unit or Department**: Enter the unit or department the soldier is assigned to.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessing Officer Name and Rank**: Enter the assessing officer's name and rank.
* **Discipline and Conduct Record**: Rate the soldier's discipline and conduct as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Number of Incidents in past 12 months**: Enter the number of incidents reported in the past 12 months.
* **Types of incidents or Violations**: Select all relevant types of incidents or violations, multiple options are allowed.
	+ Unauthorized absence
	+ Insubordination
	+ Alcohol-related
	+ Drug-related
	+ Violence or aggression
	+ Theft or theft-related
	+ Other
* **Current behavioral concerns or Red Flags**: Enter any current concerns or red flags related to the soldier's behavior.
* **Mental Health Screening Results**: Rate the soldier's recent mental health screening results as:
	+ Normal
	+ Minor concerns
	+ Significant concerns
	+ Requires immediate attention
* **Observable Stress or Emotional Level**: Rate the soldier's observable stress or emotional level as:
	+ Calm and composed
	+ Mildly stressed
	+ Moderately stressed
	+ Highly stressed
* **Substance Abuse Risk**: Rate the soldier's risk level for substance abuse as:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Job Performance Rating**: Rate the soldier's job performance as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below average
* **Physical Fitness for Duty**: Rate the soldier's physical fitness for duty as:
	+ Fit
	+ Questionable fitness
	+ Not fit
* **Reliability and Dependability**: Rate the soldier's reliability and dependability as:
	+ Highly reliable
	+ Generally reliable
	+ Questionable reliability
	+ Unreliable
* **Overall Risk Rating**: Rate the soldier's overall risk level as:
	+ Low risk
	+ Moderate risk
	+ High risk
	+ Critical risk
* **Recommended Actions or Interventions**: Enter any recommended actions or interventions for the soldier.
* **Monitoring or Follow-up Required**: Indicate if the soldier requires periodic, regular, or intensive monitoring.
