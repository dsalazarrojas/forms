# External Audit Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The External Audit Process Checklist is a form used to gather and track information about external audits, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and follow-up actions are taken as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant audit type from the options provided.
2. Choose the current audit status (Audit Complete, In Progress, In Review, or Not Started).
3. Enter the date and time of the audits (if applicable).
4. Provide auditor information (name, title, contact number, contact email, and contact person).
5. Enter location details (department, location details).
6. Summarize findings and any follow-up actions.
7. Add notes and comments as needed.
8. Schedule follow-ups and track status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Audit Type**: Choose the type of audit being conducted (e.g., internal, external, or third-party).
* **Audit Status**: Select the current status of the audit (Audit Complete, In Progress, In Review, or Not Started).
* **Date of Audits**: Enter the date of the audit (if applicable).
* **Time of Audits**: Enter the time of the audit (if applicable).
* **Auditor Information**: Provide the name, title, contact number, contact email, and contact person for the auditor.
* **Location**: Enter the location details (department, building, or other relevant details).
* **External Audits**: Enter any external audits that have been conducted.
* **Findings**: Summarize any findings or results from the audit.
* **Follow-up**: Schedule follow-up actions and track status.
* **Next Follow-up**: Schedule the next follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Status**: Track the status of the follow-up action (Complete, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Follow-up Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the follow-up action.
* **Review Comments**: Add any review comments or notes.
* **Review Status**: Track the status of the review action (Complete, In Progress, Not Started).

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the correct audit type and status for the specific audit.
- Enter accurate date and time for the audit (if applicable).
- Provide complete and accurate auditor information.
- Enter location details accurately.
- Summarize findings and follow-up actions clearly.
- Track follow-up actions and status accurately.
