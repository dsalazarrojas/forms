# Factory Infrastructure Integrity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the infrastructure integrity of a factory, including its condition, status, and any issues or concerns. This information is used for internal purposes to ensure the factory's safety, efficiency, and quality.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to each field's required status.
- Enter your factory's general information, including name and contact person.
- Specify the date and time of the inspection.
- Identify the location where the inspection was conducted.
- If necessary, add notes about any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
- Provide photos of any notable areas or issues.
- Confirm the factory's status and the type of inspection conducted.
- Add any additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `title`, required/true): This is the factory's general information. Enter the factory's name and any other relevant details.
* **Factory Name** (`2`, `factory_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the factory being inspected.
* **Contact Person** (`3`, `contact_person`, required/true): Enter the name and contact information of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection** (`4`, `date_of_inspection`, required/false): Enter the date of the inspection. If not specified, leave blank.
* **Time of Inspection** (`5`, `time_of_inspection`, required/false): Enter the time of the inspection. If not specified, leave blank.
* **Inspection Location** (`6`, `inspection_location`, required/true): Enter the location where the inspection was conducted.
* **Notes** (`7`, `notes`, required/false): Enter any additional comments or concerns found during the inspection.
* **Photos** (`8`, `photos`, required/true): Attach photos of any notable areas or issues found during the inspection.
* **Signature** (`9`, `signature`, required/false): This is not actually a field for a signature but rather a field for the date of a signature. You may want to correct or remove this field.
* **Factory Status** (`10`, `factory_status`, required/true): Choose the current status of the factory (Active, Inactive, or On Breakdown).
* **Inspection Type** (`11`, `inspection_type`, required/true): Choose the type of inspection (Routine, Preventive, or Special).
* **Comments** (`12`, `comments`, required/false): Enter any additional comments or concerns about the inspection.
