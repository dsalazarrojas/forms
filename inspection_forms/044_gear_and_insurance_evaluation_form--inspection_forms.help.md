# Gear And Insurance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gear And Insurance Evaluation Form is designed to collect information related to gears and insurance evaluations. This form helps assess the condition and insurance coverage of gears or equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **General Info** field with the basic information about the gear or equipment.
2. Rate the condition of the equipment using the **Equipment Condition** field by selecting one of the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
3. Provide insurance details in the **Insurance Info** field.
4. Enter the contact person's details in the **Contact Info** field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Additional Info** field.
6. Select the type of equipment from the **Equipment Type** field, which is categorized as Machinery, Equipment, or Other.
7. Choose the date the equipment condition was last assessed in the **Equipment Condition Date** field.
8. Enter a detailed assessment of the equipment condition in the **Equipment Condition Assessment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`id: 1`, `name: general_info`, `label: General Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field collects the basic information about the gear or equipment.
* **Equipment Condition** (`id: 2`, `name: equipment_condition`, `label: Equipment Condition`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Rate the condition of the equipment using one of the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Insurance Info** (`id: 3`, `name: insurance_info`, `label: Insurance Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This field is for providing insurance details related to the gear or equipment.
* **Contact Info** (`id: 4`, `name: contact_info`, `label: Contact Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the contact person's details here.
* **Additional Info** (`id: 5`, `name: additional_info`, `label: Additional Info`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes about the gear or equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`id: 6`, `name: equipment_type`, `label: Equipment Type`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the type of equipment from the options: Machinery, Equipment, or Other.
* **Equipment Condition Date** (`id: 7`, `name: equipment_condition_date`, `label: Equipment Condition Date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Choose the date the equipment condition was last assessed.
* **Equipment Condition Assessment** (`id: 8`, `name: equipment_condition_assessment`, `label: Equipment Condition Assessment`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter a detailed assessment of the equipment condition.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure the form is completed correctly.
- If a field is not applicable or not relevant, please leave it blank.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
