# property-management-inspection-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property-management-inspection-checklist form is designed to collect basic information about a property and its inspection details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of property being inspected from the "Property Type" dropdown menu. This will help us understand the context of the inspection.
2.  Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date Visited" field. This should be the date on which the inspection took place.
3.  Enter the time of the inspection in the "Time Visited" field. This should be the exact time of day when the inspection began.
4.  Enter the address of the property in the "Address" field. This should include the building number, street address, and any additional details such as unit or floor numbers.
5.  Enter the location of the property in the "Property Location" field. This can include any additional details about the property's location, such as its proximity to nearby landmarks or transportation options.
6.  Enter the name of the inspector or the person performing the inspection in the "Inspector Name" field.
7.  Add any additional notes about the inspection in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (Select One):
	+ Select the type of property being inspected from the dropdown menu.

	+ Enter the name of the inspector or the person performing the inspection.
* **Notes** (Note):
	+ Add any additional notes about the inspection.
