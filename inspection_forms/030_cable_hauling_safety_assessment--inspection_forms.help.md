# Cable Hauling Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to conduct a comprehensive safety assessment of cable hauling operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete the form as you would in a real-world scenario**. The form is designed to mimic the experience of conducting an assessment during a site visit.
2. **Fill in the required fields with accurate information**. Ensure that you are truthful and comprehensive in your assessment to provide an accurate picture of the current state of the cable hauling operation.
3. **Submit the form once you have completed all required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Assessor name (Page 1)
**Assessor name** (assessor_name, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing the assessment.

### Assessment date (Page 1)
**Assessment date** (assessment_date, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.

### Site location (Page 1)
**Site location** (site_location, text, required): Enter the address or location of the worksite.

### Equipment condition (Page 2)
**Equipment condition** (equipment_condition, select_one, required): Assess the condition of the winches, slings, and supports. Select from the options:
	* Satisfactory
	* Requires maintenance
	* Unsatisfactory

### Environmental hazards (Page 3)
**Environmental hazards present** (environmental_hazards, text, optional): Describe any environmental hazards present during the assessment, such as weather or wildlife.

### Personnel training compliance (Page 3)
**Personnel training compliance** (personnel_training_compliance, select_one, required): Indicate if all personnel involved in the cable hauling activity are certified or compliant with required training:
	* True
	* False

### Corrective measures (Page 4)
**Corrective measures required** (corrective_measures, text, optional): Describe any corrective actions needed to mitigate risks or address issues.

### Follow up inspection date (Page 4)
**Follow up inspection date** (follow_up_date, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up inspection, if necessary.
