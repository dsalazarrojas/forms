# Plumbing Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plumbing Inspection Checklist Form is used by inspectors to document the findings of a plumbing inspection on a property, ensuring that the system meets safety standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the house address** in the "house address" field. This should be the exact address where the inspection was conducted (e.g., 123 Main St).
2. **Choose the type of inspection** you performed (e.g., house inspection, well inspection, or other). Select one of the options from the dropdown menu.
3. **Sign your inspector signature** in the "inspector signature" field. This is your official signature as the inspector.
4. **Enter the date of inspection** in the "date of inspection" field. Format the date as it appears on your calendar (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. **Enter the time of inspection** in the "time of inspection" field. Format the time as it appears on your clock (e.g., 02:45 PM).
6. **Add any additional notes** about the inspection in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **house_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the exact address where the inspection was conducted.
* **type_of_inspection** (Select One, Optional): Choose from the options: House Inspection, Well Inspection, Other.
* **inspector_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign your name as the official inspector.
* **date_of_inspection** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time_of_inspection** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional information about the inspection that you'd like to note.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if applicable.
* Use the format specified for date and time fields to ensure accuracy.
* If you need to add more information, use the "notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
