# structural_development_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data for the structural development assessment of a site. This form is used to gather information about the site address, type, status, and other relevant details about the site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the site address, site type, and site status fields. If you are unsure of the site type or status, you can select "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" from the options.
2. Enter the site name and any other relevant details such as site area, structure type, and site location.
3. Enter the date and time of the visit to ensure accurate record keeping.
4. Describe the structural materials and condition of the site.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the site inspection.
6. Enter the email and phone number of the site inspector and assessor.
7. Select the name of the site inspector and assessor from the options.
8. Sign off with your inspector and assessor signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Address** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the address of the site. Please enter the exact address of the site where the structural development assessment was performed.
* **Site Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of site. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Site Status** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to select the status of the site. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Site Name** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the name of the site.
* **Date Of Visit** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the date of the site visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the time of the site visit.
* **Site Area** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the area of the site.
* **Structure Type** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of structure. Choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Site Location** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the location of the site.
* **Site Elevation** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the elevation of the site.
* **Site Condition** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the condition of the site.
* **Structure Height** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the height of the structure.
* **Structure Width** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the width of the structure.
* **Structure Length** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the length of the structure.
* **Structure Depth** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the depth of the structure.
* **Structural Material** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the structural material used for the site.
* **Structural Condition** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the condition of the structure.
* **Site Inspection** (`18`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any notes or comments about the site inspection.
* **Notes** (`19`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`20`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the email of the site inspector and assessor.
* **Phone Number** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the site inspector and assessor.
* **Site Inspector** (`22`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the name of the site inspector from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Site Assessor** (`23`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the name of the site assessor from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Inspector Signature** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the signature of the site inspector from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Assessor Signature** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to select the signature of the site assessor from the options provided in the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure of any field, please select "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" from the options.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
* If you need to contact the site inspector or assessor, please use the email or phone number provided.
