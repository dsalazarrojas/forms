# Earthquake Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a building's safety in the event of an earthquake. It helps assess the building's structural condition, evacuation plan, and other critical details that are essential for emergency response and mitigation plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the building and its location.
2. Review each page and fill out the relevant fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you are unsure about any question, contact the relevant authorities or building management for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A note to introduce the purpose and importance of the form.
* **Site Address**: Enter the physical address of the building, including the building number and street address.
* **Building Type**: Select the type of building from the options provided (Low-Rise Building, High-Rise Building, Other).
* **Building Height**: Enter the height of the building in meters or feet.
* **Structural Condition**: Select the structural condition of the building from the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Earthquake Resistance**: Select whether the building has earthquake resistance features or not.
* **Evacuation Plan**: Select whether the building has an evacuation plan in place or not.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Structural Intervention**: Describe any planned or ongoing structural interventions.
* **Structural Intervention Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of any structural interventions.
* **Structural Intervention Status**: Select the current status of any structural interventions from the options provided (Pending, In Progress, Complete).
* **Structural Intervention Priority**: Select the priority of any structural interventions from the options provided (Low, Medium, High).
* **Building Owner**: Select the current building owner from the options provided (Owner, Tenant, Other).
* **Building Owner Contact**: Enter the contact information for the building owner.
* **Building Location**: Enter the location of the building, including any notable geographical features.
* **Site Description**: Describe the site of the building, including its surroundings.
* **Structural Condition Rating**: Select the structural condition rating of the building from the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the building.
* **Building Area**: Enter the approximate area of the building.
* **Building Area Units**: Enter the units used to measure the building area.
* **Building Address**: Enter the physical address of the building, including the building number and street address.
* **Building Zip**: Enter the postal code of the building.
* **Building City**: Enter the city where the building is located.
