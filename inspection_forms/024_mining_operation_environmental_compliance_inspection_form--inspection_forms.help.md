# Mining Operation Environmental Compliance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record information about a mining operation's environmental compliance inspection. The form is used to track the details of the inspection, including the site's location, date and time of inspection, and environmental features observed. It also collects information about the inspection findings, actions taken, and follow-up requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page from the form, which is "Site Inspection".
2. Enter the site location in the `Site Location` field.
3. Enter the `Date Of Inspection` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter the `Time Of Inspection` in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
5. Enter the `Inspection Team` responsible for the inspection.
6. Describe the observed `Environmental Features` in the field provided.
7. Provide details about the `Inspection Finding`, including any notable issues or concerns.
8. Enter the `Action Taken` in response to the inspection finding.
9. Check the `Follow-up Required` checkbox if follow-up is necessary.
10. If follow-up is required, enter the `Follow-up Date` and `Follow-up Time` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM:SS` respectively.
11. Enter the `Next Inspection Date` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Inspection (Page 1)** (`site_inspection`, text, optional): Enter the details of the site inspection, including any notable features or observations.
* **Site Location (Page 1)** (`site_location`, text, optional): Enter the site's location, including the address or coordinates.
* **Date Of Inspection (Page 1)** (`date_of_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time Of Inspection (Page 1)** (`time_of_inspection`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Inspection Team (Page 1)** (`inspection_team`, text, optional): Enter the team responsible for the inspection.
* **Environmental Features (Page 1)** (`environmental_features`, text, optional): Describe the observed environmental features, including any notable aspects or concerns.
* **Inspection Finding (Page 1)** (`inspection_finding`, text, optional): Provide details about the inspection finding, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Action Taken (Page 1)** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Enter the action taken in response to the inspection finding.
* **Follow-up Required (Page 1)** (`follow_up_required`, text, optional, checkbox): Check this box if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up Date (Page 1)** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Follow-up Time (Page 1)** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Next Inspection Date (Page 1)** (`next_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the next inspection date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
