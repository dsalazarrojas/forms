# Fire Department Truck Check Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Department Truck Check Form Template is a tool used to gather essential information about fire department trucks during regular checks. It ensures that each vehicle is thoroughly inspected and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you're not duplicating checks on the same vehicle.
2. On the first page, enter the **truck number** and **vehicle identification**.
3. On the second page, fill in the remaining fields with the relevant information about the truck.
4. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truck Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the truck being checked.
* **Vehicle Identification**: Enter a brief description of the vehicle, such as its make and model.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year the truck was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the truck, e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, etc.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the truck, e.g., F-150, Silverado, etc.
* **Mileage**: Record the current mileage of the truck.
* **Driver's Name**: Enter the name of the driver assigned to the truck.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Describe the overall condition of the truck, including any defects or issues found during the check.
* **Safety Equipment**: List the safety equipment currently installed on the truck, such as fire extinguishers, sirens, etc.

## Tips
- Be sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- Use the vehicle year to determine the age of the vehicle.
- Check the vehicle identification for any missing or expired safety certification.
- Take note of any maintenance or repair recommendations made during the check.
- Update the vehicle's record with the new information after completing the form.
