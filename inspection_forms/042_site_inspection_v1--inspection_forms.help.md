# site_inspection_v1 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "site_inspection_v1" form is designed to collect information about site conditions and other related details during inspections. This form is intended to guide users in capturing essential data for effective site monitoring and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your responses in the designated fields.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated as "required" in the form).
4. Select the appropriate options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **site_inspection_form**:
	+ **(Page 1)**: A text field for capturing general site inspection information. Please provide a brief description of the site conditions.
* **site_inspection_form_2**:
	+ **(Page 2)**: A multiple-choice field where you can select one or more options. Please choose the applicable options from the available choices.
* **site_inspection_form_3**:
	+ **(Page 3)**: A text field for capturing additional site inspection information. Please provide any relevant details.
* **site_inspection_form_4**:
	+ **(Page 4)**: A multiple-choice field where you can select one option. Please choose the correct option from the available choices.
* **site_inspection_form_5**:
	+ **(Page 5)**: A date field for capturing the date of the site inspection. Please enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **site_inspection_form_6**:
	+ **(Page 6)**: A time field for capturing the time of the site inspection. Please enter the time in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM).
* **site_inspection_form_7**:
	+ **(Page 7)**: A numeric field for capturing specific site information. Please enter the numeric value as instructed.
* **site_inspection_form_8**:
	+ **(Page 8)**: A numeric field for capturing additional site information. Please enter the numeric value as instructed.

## Tips
- Always review the form before proceeding to ensure you understand the required information.
- Be accurate and precise when entering your responses.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or refer to the form's purpose and instructions.
- Save your progress regularly to avoid losing any entered information.
