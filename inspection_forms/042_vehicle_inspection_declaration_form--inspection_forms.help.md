# Vehicle Inspection Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Vehicle Inspection Declaration Form is used to document the inspection status of a vehicle, ensuring that it meets the required safety standards. This form is used by inspectors to declare the results of a physical inspection and provide assurance that the vehicle is safe and compliant for operation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's year, make, and model.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. Indicate the result of the inspection (Yes or No).
4. Provide any repair notes if necessary.
5. Indicate if the vehicle complies with safety standards (Yes or No).
6. Add any additional comments.
7. Enter the name of the assigned inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vehicle Year
* **Vehicle Year** (number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Make
* **Vehicle Make** (text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Model
* **Vehicle Model** (text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.

### Inspection Date
* **Inspection Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.

### Inspection Result
* **Inspection Result** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the vehicle meets the safety standards, "No" otherwise.

### Repair Notes
* **Repair Notes** (text, optional): Provide any repair notes if necessary.

### Safety Standards Compliance
* **Safety Standards Compliance** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the vehicle complies with safety standards, "No" otherwise.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments.

### Assigned Inspector
* **Assigned Inspector** (text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned inspector.
