# Sewer Testing Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of sewer testing activities. It captures information about the date and inspector conducting the test, the sewer being tested, the type of test conducted, and the results of the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the test in the "Inspector Name" field.
3. Enter the unique identifier for the sewer being tested in the "Sewer ID" field.
4. Select the type of test conducted from the "Test Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter any notes related to the test in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the outcome of the test (passing or failing) in the "Test Result" field.
7. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks" field.
8. Enter the location of the sewer in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspect_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the test was conducted.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the test.
* **Sewer ID** (`sewer_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the sewer being tested.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of test conducted (e.g., flow test, visual test).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or observations related to the test.
* **Test Result** (`result`, select_one, required: false): Select the outcome of the test (e.g., passing or failing).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or remarks related to the test.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the sewer being tested.
