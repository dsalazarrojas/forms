# excavator_daily_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The excavator daily inspection form is used to document the results of routine inspections of excavating equipment. This form helps track and maintain a record of the status of critical equipment and personnel, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information is kept.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following these steps:

1. Select the correct equipment operator performing the inspection.
2. Enter the excavator number, which is the unique identifier for the equipment being inspected.
3. Type in the location where the inspection was performed.
4. Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format.
5. Select the safety equipment used during the inspection from the provided options.
6. Choose the inspection status from the options provided.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes regarding the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the operator performing the inspection.
* **Excavator Number** (`excavator_number`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the unique identifier for the excavator being inspected.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, **Required**): Type in the location where the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the safety equipment used during the inspection from the options provided. Examples include Safety Glasses, Hard Hat, and Safety Vest.
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the inspection status from the options provided, which is either Passed or Failed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, **Optional**): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the inspection, if necessary.

## Tips
* Ensure you are using the correct excavator number for the specific equipment being inspected.
* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct date format for the inspection date.
* Clearly select the correct safety equipment used during the inspection.
* Choose the correct inspection status based on the findings.
