# Supervisory Visit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisory Visit Checklist form is designed to help supervisors document and track their site visits, including site status, supervisory visit standards, follow-ups, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by `*`) with the relevant information.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding field.
3. Use the options provided for each field to make your selection.
4. Review and submit the form to document your supervisory visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Date Of Visit* (`date`, required): Enter the date of the visit.
- **Site Name* (`text`, required): Enter the name of the site visited.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Templates Help Managers Document Site Visits* (`note`, required): Enter a brief summary or comments about the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Standards* (`select_one`, required): Choose whether the site met or did not meet the supervisory visit standards.
- **Supervisory Visit Followups* (`select_multiple`, required): Select the follow-up visits required for the site.
- **Site Status* (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the site (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
- **Supervisory Visit Comments* (`text`, required): Enter any comments or observations about the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Comments2* (`text`, required): Enter any additional comments or observations about the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Date* (`date`, required): Enter the date of the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Time* (`time`, required): Enter the time of the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Submitter* (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Followup Type* (`text`, required): Enter the type of follow-up visit required.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Followup Frequency* (`select_one`, required): Choose the frequency of the follow-up visit (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Submitter 2* (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Submission Time 2* (`time`, required): Enter the time of the submission.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Notes* (`note`, required): Enter any additional comments or observations about the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Date 2* (`date`, required): Enter the date of the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Time 2* (`time`, required): Enter the time of the visit.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Submitter 3* (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
- **Supervisory Visit Checklist Form Submission Time 3* (`time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
 
Note: The fields marked with `*` are required fields.
