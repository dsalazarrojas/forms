# Building Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to guide inspectors to conduct a thorough building inspection and assess various aspects of a building's condition. It helps inspectors identify issues, note minor and major concerns, and provide recommendations for repairs and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the sections of the form, each representing a critical area of inspection.
2.  For each page, select the most accurate answer to each question using the provided options.
3.  Take notes in the space provided for any observations, comments, or recommendations.
4.  Ensure that you sign and date the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Building Information

*   **-- Building Inspection Checklist --** (`inspection_header`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the form.
*   **Inspection date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): The date of the inspection.
*   **Inspector name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required): The name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
*   **Building address** (`building_address`, `text`, required): The address of the building being inspected.
*   **Building type** (`building_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of building being inspected, such as single-family home, multi-family apartment, office building, etc.

### Page 2: Exterior

*   **-- Exterior Inspection --** (`exterior_checklist`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the exterior inspection.
*   **Roof condition and integrity** (`roof_inspection`, `select_one`, required): Assess the roof's condition, with options including pass, minor defects noted, repair needed, replacement recommended, or not accessible.
*   **Roof inspection notes** (`roof_notes`, `text`, not required): Take notes on any damage, wear, or materials observed on the roof.
*   **Foundation condition** (`foundation_inspection`, `select_one`, required): Assess the foundation's condition, with options including pass, minor cracks noted, significant cracking, settlement observed, or repair needed.
*   **Foundation inspection notes** (`foundation_notes`, `text`, not required): Note any observations, comments, or recommendations on the foundation's condition.
*   **Exterior siding and walls** (`siding_inspection`, `select_one`, required): Assess the exterior siding and walls, with options including pass, minor wear or damage, moderate deterioration, significant damage, or repair needed.
*   **Windows and exterior doors** (`windows_doors_ext`, `select_one`, required): Assess the windows and exterior doors, with options including pass, minor issues, multiple damaged units, widespread problems, or replacement recommended.
*   **Gutters and downspouts** (`gutters_downspouts`, `select_one`, required): Assess the condition of the gutters and downspouts, with options including pass, minor cleaning needed, repair needed, missing or blocked, or replacement recommended.
*   **Drainage and grading** (`drainage_grading`, `select_one`, required): Assess the drainage and grading, with options including pass, minor pooling noted, water pooling areas, poor drainage, or immediate drainage repairs needed.

### Page 3: Interior

*   **-- Interior Inspection --** (`interior_checklist`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the interior inspection.
*   **Interior flooring condition** (`flooring_interior`, `select_one`, required): Assess the interior flooring condition, with options including pass, minor wear, moderate damage, significant damage, or replacement recommended.
*   **Interior walls and paint** (`walls_interior`, `select_one`, required): Assess the interior walls and paint, with options including pass, minor marks or stains, moderate damage, significant water damage, or major repairs needed.
*   **Ceilings condition** (`ceiling_interior`, `select_one`, required): Assess the ceilings condition, with options including pass, minor stains, water damage present, sagging or structural concern, or urgent repairs needed.
*   **Interior doors and operation** (`doors_interior`, `select_one`, required): Assess the interior doors and operation, with options including pass, minor adjustments needed, some stuck or damaged doors, multiple non-functional doors, or repairs needed.
*   **Stairs, railings, and safety** (`stairs_railings`, `select_one`, required): Assess the stairs, railings, and safety features, with options including pass, minor defects noted, safety concerns, code violations found, or immediate repairs required.
*   **Evidence of water damage or leaks** (`water_damage`, `select_one`, required): Assess the evidence of water damage or leaks, with options including none observed, minor stains, recent water damage, active leaks present, or severe water damage.
*   **Mold or mildew presence** (`mold_mildew`, `select_one`, required): Assess the presence of mold or mildew, with options including none observed, minor presence, moderate mold growth, extensive growth, or professional remediation needed.
*   **Pest evidence or damage** (`pest_damage`, `select_one`, required): Assess the presence of pest evidence or damage, with options including none observed, minor signs, moderate evidence, significant damage, or active infestation.

### Page 4: Systems

*   **-- Systems Inspection --** (`systems_checklist`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the systems inspection.
*   **Electrical system condition** (`electrical_system`, `select_one`, required): Assess the electrical system condition, with options including pass, safe and modern, adequate but older, concerns noted, or hazards present.
*   **Plumbing system condition** (`plumbing_system`, `select_one`, required): Assess the plumbing system condition, with options including pass, good condition, minor leaks, multiple leaks, or water pressure issues.
*   **HVAC system condition** (`hvac_system`, `select_one`, required): Assess the HVAC system condition, with options including pass, good working order, adequate condition, needs servicing, or replacement recommended.
*   **Water heater condition** (`water_heater`, `select_one`, required): Assess the water heater condition, with options including pass, recent or new, good condition, fair condition, poor condition, or replacement needed.

### Page 5: Safety Features

*   **-- Safety Features --** (`safety_features`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the safety features inspection.
*   **Fire extinguishers present and accessible** (`fire_extinguishers`, `select_one`, required): Assess the presence and accessibility of fire extinguishers, with options including yes, present and accessible, yes, present but inaccessible, no, none present, or partially.
*   **Smoke detectors operational** (`smoke_detectors`, `select_one`, required): Assess the smoke detector operation, with options including yes, all functioning, yes, most functional, no, none working, or not present.
*   **Emergency exits marked and accessible** (`emergency_exits`, `select_one`, required): Assess the emergency exits' marking and accessibility, with options including yes, clearly marked, yes, marked but not obvious, no, not marked, or blocked or inaccessible.

### Page 6: Overall Assessment

*   **-- Overall Assessment --** (`overall_assessment`, `note`, not required): This is a header section that serves as a title for the overall assessment.
*   **Overall building condition** (`overall_condition`, `select_one`, required): Assess the overall building condition, with options including excellent, well-maintained, good, minor issues only, fair, moderate repairs needed, poor, significant repairs needed, or critical, major issues present.
*   **Building safe for occupancy** (`occupancy_safe`, `select_one`, required): Assess whether the building is safe for occupancy, with options including yes, safe, yes, with minor improvements, conditional, repairs needed, no, safety concerns, or no, unsafe for occupancy.
*   **Priority repairs and recommendations** (`priority_repairs`, `text`, not required): Record any priority repairs and recommendations.
*   **Estimated repair costs** (`estimated_repairs_cost`, `number`, not required): Record the estimated repair costs.
*   **Follow-up needed** (`follow_up_needed`, `select_one`, required): Assess the need for follow-up actions, with options including no, pass inspection, yes, minor issues follow-up, yes, major issues follow-up, yes, professional specialist needed, or not applicable.
