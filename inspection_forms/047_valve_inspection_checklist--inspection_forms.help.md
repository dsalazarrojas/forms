# Valve Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Valve Inspection Checklist is a form used to document the inspection and maintenance of valves in a facility or organization. This form helps ensure that all valves are properly inspected, and necessary maintenance is recorded and scheduled.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page of the form based on the specific inspection being performed.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are indicated by a star (\*) in the table below.
3. Enter the relevant information for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Valve Inspection Checklist Page 1** (`valve_inspection_checklist_page_1`): This page is the first step in the inspection process.
* **Valve Condition Rating** (`valve_condition_rating`): Rate the condition of the valve as Good, Fair, or Poor. This helps determine the level of maintenance required.
* **Photo Evidence** (`photo_evidence`): If possible, upload a photo of the valve to provide additional context for the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or observations about the valve's condition.
* **Valve Inspection Details** (`valve_inspection_details`): Describe the specific details of the valve's inspection, including any issues found.
* **Valve Description** (`valve_description`): Describe the purpose and function of the valve.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`): Enter the date the valve was inspected.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`): Enter the time the valve was inspected.
* **Location** (`location`): Record the location where the valve is installed.
* **Asset Tag Number** (`asset_tag_number`): Record the asset tag number of the valve.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`): Describe the valve and its components.
* **Maintenance Actions Required** (`maintenance_actions_required`): Select the maintenance actions required for the valve, such as repair, replace, or inspect.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`): Record the date of the last maintenance performed on the valve.
* **Last Maintenance By** (`last_maintenance_by`): Record the person who performed the last maintenance on the valve.
* **Next Maintenance Due Date** (`next_maintenance_due_date`): Schedule the next maintenance date for the valve.
* **Next Maintenance By** (`next_maintenance_by`): Record the person responsible for the next maintenance on the valve.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`): Describe the recommended inspection frequency for the valve.
* **Inspection Frequency Unit** (`inspection_frequency_unit`): Select the unit of measurement for the inspection frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Inspection Frequency Value** (`inspection_frequency_value`): Enter the specific value for the inspection frequency.
* **Asset ID** (`asset_id`): Record the asset ID of the valve.
* **Asset Name** (`asset_name`): Record the name of the valve.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`): Describe the valve and its components.
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`): Record the current status of the valve.
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`): Describe the current condition of the valve.
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`): Describe the category of the valve (e.g., steam, water, air, etc.).
