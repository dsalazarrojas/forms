# uber_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The uber_inspection_form is designed to collect information related to vehicle inspections, providing a simple and straightforward way to document details about the vehicle, driver, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by providing general information about the vehicle, such as its make, model, and year.
* Then, enter relevant details about the driver, including their name and contact information.
* Next, describe the vehicle's current condition, including any notable issues or concerns.
* Provide information about the vehicle documentation, such as any relevant records or certifications.
* Enter the results of the inspection, detailing any findings or issues that were discovered.
* Finally, include any additional remarks or comments about the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Info
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the vehicle's basic information, such as make, model, and year.

### Driver Info
* **Driver Info** (`driver_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the driver's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Vehicle Condition
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the current condition of the vehicle, including any notable issues or concerns.

### Mechanic Info
* **Mechanic Info** (`mechanic_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the mechanic who performed the inspection, including their name and contact details.

### Vehicle Documentation
* **Vehicle Documentation** (`vehicle_documentation`, text, required/optional): Provide any relevant documentation or records about the vehicle, such as service records or certifications.

### Inspection Results
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text, required/optional): Enter the results of the inspection, detailing any findings or issues that were discovered.

### Vehicle Remarks
* **Vehicle Remarks** (`vehicle_remarks`, text, required/optional): Include any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle's condition, such as any notable features or concerns.

### Mechanic Signature
* **Mechanic Signature** (`mechanic_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature as the mechanic who performed the inspection, providing verification of the inspection's authenticity.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to support the vehicle's inspection and maintenance process.
* If you're unsure about any information or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
