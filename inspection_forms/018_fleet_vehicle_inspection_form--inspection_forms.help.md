# Fleet Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record details about a vehicle's status and condition during inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure the form is filled out accurately and completely.
2. Complete each field as required, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. Select the correct options for each field where options are available.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed in the "Inspection Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle, including make, model, and registration number.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the current status of the vehicle, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time the inspection was conducted.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`, text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the driver, such as their name and role.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate the current condition of the vehicle, selecting all that apply:
	+ Yes (for issues)
	+ No (for no issues)
* **Safety Regulations** (`safety_regulations`, select_one, Optional): Confirm if the vehicle meets safety regulations, selecting either:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Inspection Notes** (`inspection_notes`, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's inspection, such as issues found or actions taken.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or maintenance manual for guidance.
