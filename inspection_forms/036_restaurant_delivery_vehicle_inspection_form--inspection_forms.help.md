# restaurant_delivery_vehicle_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant_delivery_vehicle_inspection_form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's registration and its inspection details. This form is intended for restaurants or businesses that need to inspect and verify the authenticity of vehicles delivering food, beverages, or other goods. The form helps to ensure that vehicles are registered and comply with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the vehicle's registration number in the "Vehicle Registration Number" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
3. Provide details about the driver who is operating the vehicle in the "Driver Details" field.
4. Enter information about the inspector who is performing the inspection in the "Inspector Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`Vehicle Registration Number`, text, required/optional): The Vehicle Registration Number is the unique identifier assigned to the vehicle by the governing authority. Enter the exact registration number as printed on the vehicle's registration card.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): The Vehicle Make refers to the brand or manufacturer of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, or Honda.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the driver operating the vehicle, including their name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Inspector Details** (`inspector_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the information of the inspector performing the vehicle inspection, including their name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
