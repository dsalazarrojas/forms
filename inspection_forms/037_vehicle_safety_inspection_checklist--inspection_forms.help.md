# Vehicle Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the results of a vehicle safety inspection. It includes information on the condition, safety status, and other relevant details of the vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Vehicle Safety Inspection Checklist**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating that the vehicle's safety inspection has been completed.
- **Vehicle Type**: Select the correct type of vehicle being inspected.
- **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle being inspected.
- **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle being inspected.
- **Safety Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the safety inspection.
- **Inspection Result**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the inspection was successful or not.
- **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
- **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle being inspected.
- **Engine Type**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating the type of engine used in the vehicle.
- **Transmission Type**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating the type of transmission used in the vehicle.
- **Safety Rating**: Select one of three options, "Good", "Fair", or "Poor", indicating the vehicle's safety rating.
- **Vehicle Status**: Select one of two options, "Active" or "Inactive", indicating the current status of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Condition**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating the overall condition of the vehicle.
- **Inspection Result Details**: Enter additional comments or notes about the inspection results.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
- **Action Plan**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating whether an action plan is needed.
- **Next Step**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating the next step in the process.
- **Responsible Party**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating the party responsible for the vehicle.
- **Safety Inspection Report**: Enter the safety inspection report.
- **Signature**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating that the inspector has signed off on the inspection.
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature.
- **Signature Time**: Enter the time of the signature.
- **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
- **Review**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating that the results have been reviewed.
- **Submitted**: Select one of two options, "Yes" or "No", indicating that the form has been submitted.
