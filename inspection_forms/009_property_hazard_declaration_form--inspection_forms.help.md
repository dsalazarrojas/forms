# Property Hazard Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Hazard Declaration Form is used to report properties with potential hazards. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure accurate documentation and reporting of hazards on properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question of whether a hazard is present on the property. If you answer yes, you will need to provide further information about the hazard.
2. Select the type of hazard that exists.
3. Provide a description of the hazard.
4. Categorize the risk level of the hazard as Low, Moderate, or High.
5. Indicate the status of the hazard as Active, Passive, or Unknown.
6. If the hazard status is Active, provide a date and time when the hazard was last reported.
7. If the hazard status is Active, provide a time when the hazard was first reported.
8. Provide details about the status of the hazard.
9. Enter the name and contact information of the property manager or owner.
10. Upload any relevant files related to the hazard.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hazards Found** (`hazards_found`, Number, Required): This field is used to answer whether a hazard is present on the property. Answering yes or no to this question will determine how many further questions you will need to answer.
* **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`, Text, Required): This field is used to select the type of hazard that exists on the property. This information will help in identifying and understanding the hazard.
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Optional): This field is used to provide a description of the hazard. A brief and detailed description can be helpful in understanding the hazard better.
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, Select One, Required): This field is used to categorize the risk level of the hazard as Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Hazard Status** (`hazard_status`, Select Multiple, Required): This field is used to indicate the status of the hazard as Active, Passive, or Unknown.
* **Hazard Status Date** (`hazard_status_date`, Date, Optional): This field is used to provide the date when the hazard status was last reported.
* **Hazard Status Time** (`hazard_status_time`, Time, Optional): This field is used to provide the time when the hazard status was last reported.
* **Hazard Status Details** (`hazard_status_details`, Text, Optional): This field is used to provide additional details about the hazard status.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter the name of the property manager or owner.
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter the name of the property owner.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): This field is used to enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): This field is used to enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Hazard Reported Date** (`hazard_reported_date`, Date, Optional): This field is used to provide the date when the hazard was first reported.
* **Hazard Reported Time** (`hazard_reported_time`, Time, Optional): This field is used to provide the time when the hazard was first reported.
* **Hazard Reported Details** (`hazard_reported_details`, Text, Optional): This field is used to provide details about the hazard when it was first reported.
* **Hazard Remarks** (`hazard_remarks`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter any additional comments or remarks about the hazard.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, Text, Optional): This field is used to upload any relevant files related to the hazard.

Note: Fields marked as "Required" must be filled out.
