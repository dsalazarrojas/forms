# Groundwork Confirmation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to confirm the groundwork site preparation tasks, inspector name, inspection date, site prep approvals, notes, contact email, contact phone, and submission status. It helps to ensure that all necessary steps are completed before proceeding with the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Prep Tasks** (`site_prep_tasks`):
	+ A text field to confirm completion of groundwork site preparation tasks.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`):
	+ A field to verify the name of the inspector.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`):
	+ A field to confirm the inspection date.
* **Site Prep Approvals** (`site_prep_approvals`):
	+ A multiple selection field to confirm whether site prep approvals have been obtained or denied.
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	+ A field for taking notes of any concerns or issues.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	+ A field to provide the contact email of the relevant personnel.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ A field to provide the contact phone number of the relevant personnel.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`):
	+ A field to confirm whether this form has been submitted or not.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any issues.
