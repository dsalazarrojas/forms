# Surface Finish Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Surface Finish Evaluation Form is used to assess the quality of a surface finish on a material or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Evaluate the surface finish and provide an assessment of its quality.
2. Select the most accurate description of the surface finish from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional comments or observations about the surface finish in the comments field.
4. Attach any relevant images or documentation to support your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surface Finish Evaluation (Page 1)** (`surface_finish_evaluation`, `note`, optional): This field is used to capture a brief description of the overall appearance and quality of the surface finish.
* **Surface Quality Check 1 (Page 1)** (`surface_quality_check_1`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to assess the quality of the surface finish using the following options: Very good, Fair, Very poor, or N/A. Select the most accurate description.
* **Surface Quality Check 2 (Page 1)** (`surface_quality_check_2`, `select_one`, optional): This field is similar to Surface Quality Check 1, used to assess the quality of the surface finish.
* **Surface Quality Check 3 (Page 1)** (`surface_quality_check_3`, `select_one`, optional): This field is also similar to Surface Quality Check 1 and 2, used to assess the quality of the surface finish.
* **Surface Finish Evaluation (Page 2)** (`surface_finish_evaluation`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any additional comments or observations about the surface finish.
* **Surface Finish Evaluation Comments (Page 2)** (`surface_finish_evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter any additional comments or observations about the surface finish.
* **Surface Finish Evaluation Form Comments (Page 2)** (`surface_finish_evaluation_form_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter any additional comments or observations about the form itself.
* **Surface Finish Evaluation Image 1 (Page 2)** (`surface_finish_evaluation_image_1`, `text`, optional): This field is used to attach any relevant images or documentation to support your assessment.
