# Vehicle Inspection Protocol - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's inspection status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Use the available options for select fields (e.g., dropdown menus) to select the most appropriate response.
4. Enter free-form text in text fields as needed.
5. Use date and time fields to record relevant dates and times.
6. Use email fields to provide contact information.
7. Save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.
