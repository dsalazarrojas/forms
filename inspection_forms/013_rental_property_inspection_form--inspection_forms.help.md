# Rental Property Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information on rental property inspection dates, addresses, and conditions, along with the identity of the assigned agent and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: 
   - Inspection Date: Enter the date of the inspection, following the format of your local date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
   - Property Type: Select the type of property that was inspected (Landlord, Tenant, or Vacant).
   - Condition Rating: Rate the overall condition of the property as Excellent, Good, or Poor.
   - Assigned Agent: Select the agent(s) that were assigned to this inspection.
2. Enter optional fields:
   - Property Address: Provide the address of the inspected property.
   - Unit Number: Enter the unit number of the inspected property (if applicable).
   - Inspection Notes: Record any additional comments or observations from the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date (1)**: Enter the date of the inspection to ensure accurate tracking and record-keeping.
* **Property Address (2)**: Provide the address of the inspected property, including any relevant details (street, number, etc.).
* **Unit Number (3)**: Enter the unit number of the inspected property if applicable.
* **Property Type (4)**: Choose the type of property that was inspected (Landlord, Tenant, or Vacant).
* **Condition Rating (5)**: Rate the overall condition of the property as Excellent, Good, or Poor.
* **Inspection Notes (6)**: Record any additional comments or observations from the inspection.
* **Assigned Agent (7)**: Select the agent(s) that were assigned to this inspection, for proper delegation of tasks and responsibility.

## Tips
* Always review and approve each submission before submitting multiple forms for the same property to maintain consistency and accuracy.
* Use clear and concise descriptions in the Inspection Notes field to ensure clarity on findings and any next steps required.
