# Water Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about water safety checklists conducted by various teams. The form helps identify hazards, take action, schedule follow-ups, and record the location and remarks of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of water safety area that the inspection is for (e.g., Aquatic Facility, Beach, Lake, River, Pool, Pond, Spa, or Other).
2. Indicate if any hazards were found during the inspection.
3. Describe the actions taken to address the hazards found.
4. Choose the follow-up schedule for the identified hazards.
5. List the team members involved in the inspection.
6. Enter the date and time of the visit (optional).
7. Enter the location of the inspection.
8. Add any additional remarks about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water Safety Area** (`general_info`, `select_one`): Select the type of water safety area that the inspection is for. This helps the inspector focus on the specific area and hazards related to that area.
* **Hazards Found** (`hazards_found`, `select_multiple`): Indicate if any hazards were found during the inspection. This field allows you to choose multiple hazards that apply to the specific water safety area.
* **Actions Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`): Describe the actions taken to address the hazards found. This field helps track the steps taken to mitigate or resolve the hazards.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`): Choose the follow-up schedule for the identified hazards. This field ensures that follow-ups are scheduled for the necessary hazards.
* **Team Members Involved** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`): List the team members involved in the inspection. This field helps track the personnel who participated in the inspection.
* **Date and Time Visited** (`date_visited`, `date`): Enter the date and time of the visit (if required).
* **Location Visited** (`location_visited`, `text`): Enter the location of the inspection.
* **Additional Remarks** (`remarks`, `note`): Add any additional comments or remarks about the inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct type of water safety area and hazards found.
* Ensure that follow-ups are scheduled for the necessary hazards.
* List all team members involved in the inspection.
* Be detailed in the actions taken and location visited.
* Use this form to track and monitor water safety checklists for various areas.
