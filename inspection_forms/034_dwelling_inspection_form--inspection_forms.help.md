# Dwelling Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the condition of a dwelling, including its roof, exterior, and interior. The data collected will help identify potential issues and inform further inspection or repair actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

*   Read the form carefully and ensure you have access to the relevant property documentation and information.
*   Complete each field as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed descriptions and observations about the property's condition.
*   If you need to add or remove pages, please consult with a supervisor or relevant authority.
