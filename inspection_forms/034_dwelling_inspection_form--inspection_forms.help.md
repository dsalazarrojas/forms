# Dwelling Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the condition of a dwelling, including its roof, exterior, and interior. The data collected will help identify potential issues and inform further inspection or repair actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

*   Read the form carefully and ensure you have access to the relevant property documentation and information.
*   Complete each field as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed descriptions and observations about the property's condition.
*   If you need to add or remove pages, please consult with a supervisor or relevant authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the property and the purpose of the inspection. Please provide a concise summary of the inspection's goals and objectives.
*   **Property Details** (`Property Details`, `number`, required: false): Enter the property's identification number or any other relevant property-specific details.
*   **Roof** (`Roof`, `text`, required: false): Describe the roof's condition, including any noticeable defects or issues.
*   **Exterior** (`Exterior`, `text`, required: false): Describe the exterior's condition, including walls, windows, doors, and any notable features.
*   **Foundation** (`Foundation`, `text`, required: false): Describe the foundation's condition, including any signs of distress or defects.
*   **Interior** (`Interior`, `text`, required: false): Describe the interior's condition, including flooring, walls, ceilings, and any notable features.
