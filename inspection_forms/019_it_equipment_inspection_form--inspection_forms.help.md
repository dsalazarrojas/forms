# it_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for IT equipment inspection and maintenance record keeping. It helps IT staff to track the condition, issues, and repair status of equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide user information (name, contact info, and email).
2. Select the equipment type (Hardware or Software).
3. Choose the equipment condition (Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. Select any issues found during the inspection.
5. Choose the repair status (Repairing, Repaired, or Not Repairing).
6. Provide the next inspection date (if applicable).
7. Fill in any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your name, contact information, and email address.
* **Equipment List**: Select all the IT equipment that you are inspecting.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the current inspection.
* **Issues List**: Select any issues found during the inspection.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Repair Status**: Choose the repair status of the equipment (Repairing, Repaired, or Not Repairing).
* **Equipment Type**: Select whether the equipment is Hardware or Software.
* **Condition**: Choose the condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection (if applicable).
* **Last Repaired Date**: Enter the date of the last repair (if applicable).
* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next inspection (if applicable).
