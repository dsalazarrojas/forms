# Refrigeration Energy Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Refrigeration Energy Review Request Form is used to collect information about your facility's refrigeration systems and energy usage. This form is intended to help energy consultants assess and review your facility's energy consumption patterns and identify potential areas for improvement. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for a comprehensive energy review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **business_contact_info**: This field is for providing your facility's business contact information. Please enter the name, email, phone number, and address of the person responsible for this energy review.
* **facility_info**: This field is for indicating whether the facility is currently using refrigeration systems. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your facility has refrigeration systems.
* **system_info**: This field is for providing more information about the refrigeration systems in your facility. Please enter a brief description or details about your systems.
* **system_characteristics**: This field is for selecting which type of refrigeration system you have at your facility. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a central chiller plant, chiller water loop, or other specific system types.
* **records_and_documents**: If you have any relevant records or documents related to your facility's energy usage, please upload them here.
* **review_request**: This field is for submitting a review request for the energy consultant. Please briefly describe the purpose of the energy review.
* **assessment_plan**: If you have a specific assessment plan in mind, please describe it here.
* **energy_consultant_info**: This field is for providing information about the energy consultant you'd like to work with. Please enter their name and any relevant contact details.
* **submission**: This field is for indicating whether the facility is ready to submit the form and proceed with the energy review.
* **submit**: This field is for confirming that the submitted information is accurate and complete.
* **facility_owner**: This field is for indicating whether the facility owner is involved in the energy review process.
* **facility_manager**: This field is for indicating whether the facility manager is involved in the energy review process.
* **facility_director**: This field is for indicating whether the facility director is involved in the energy review process.
* **facility_general_manager**: This field is for indicating whether the facility general manager is involved in the energy review process.
* **business_contact**: This field is for providing more information about the business contact person.
* **facility_address**: This field is for providing the physical address of the facility.
* **facility_city**: This field is for providing the city where the facility is located.
* **facility_country**: This field is for providing the country where the facility is located.
* **facility_postal_code**: This field is for providing the postal code of the facility's location.
* **facility_phone**: This field is for providing the phone number of the facility.
* **facility_email**: This field is for providing the email address of the facility.
* **facility_comments**: This field is for providing any additional comments or information about the facility.
* **consultant**: This field is for providing more information about the energy consultant.
* **consultant_comments**: This field is for providing any additional comments or information about the energy consultant.
* **submission_date**: This field is for selecting the date you'd like to submit the form to the energy consultant.
* **business_contact_info_2**: This field is for providing more information about the business contact person. 

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please refer back to this help guide or consult with your facility's energy management team.
