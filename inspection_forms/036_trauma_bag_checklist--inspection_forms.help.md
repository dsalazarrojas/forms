# Trauma Bag Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma Bag Checklist is a form used to identify the contents of a medical trauma bag and gather information about the first responders and location associated with it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contents of the trauma bag from the list provided. You must select all applicable items.
2. Choose the first responder(s) who will be handling the trauma bag. You must select at least one responder.
3. Enter the location where the trauma bag will be used, if applicable.
4. Optionally, enter the date and time when the trauma bag is checked.
5. Select any missing items from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trauma Bag Contents** (`trauma_bag_contents`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable items from the list to ensure the trauma bag is fully stocked.
* **First Responder** (`first_responder`, select_one, required): Choose the first responder(s) who will be handling the trauma bag.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the trauma bag will be used, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the trauma bag is checked.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the trauma bag is checked.
* **Missing Items** (`missing_items`, select_multiple, required): Select any items that are missing from the list to ensure the trauma bag is fully stocked.

## Tips
* Please select all applicable items for the trauma bag contents and missing items.
* Ensure that at least one first responder is selected.
* The location field is optional, but it can be useful for tracking the location where the trauma bag is used.
* Use the correct date and time format when filling out the date and time fields.
