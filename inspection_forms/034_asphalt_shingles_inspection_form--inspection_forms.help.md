# Asphalt Shingles Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information about the condition and installation of asphalt shingles on a roof.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Inspector or Roofer Name: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
	* Date of Inspection: Enter the date of the inspection.
	* Client Property Address: Enter the address of the client's property.
	* Shingle Brand and Model: Enter the brand and model of the shingles used on the roof.
	* Shingle Color: Enter the color of the shingles.
	* Quantity of Bundles Installed: Enter the number of bundles installed.
	* Roof Slope or Pitch: Enter the slope or pitch of the roof.
2. Evaluate the condition of the underlayment:
	* Condition of Underlayment: Select the condition of the underlayment from the options provided.
3. Check the nailing pattern:
	* Nailing Pattern Accuracy: Check all that apply from the options provided.
4. Evaluate the flashing installation:
	* Flashing Installation Quality: Enter a number rating from 1-5.
5. Document any visible defects or damage:
	* Visible Defects or Damage Found: Enter a brief description of any issues found during the inspection.
6. Calculate the total roof area:
	* Total Roof Area Covered: Enter the total area of the roof in square feet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector or Roofer Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Client Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Shingle Brand and Model**: Enter the brand and model of the shingles used on the roof.
* **Shingle Color**: Enter the color of the shingles.
* **Quantity of Bundles Installed**: Enter the number of bundles installed.
* **Roof Slope or Pitch**: Enter the slope or pitch of the roof, e.g., 6/12.
* **Condition of Underlayment**: Select the condition of the underlayment from the options provided:
	+ Excellent - New and dry
	+ Good - Intact
	+ Fair - Some wear
	+ Poor - Replacement needed
* **Nailing Pattern Accuracy**: Check all that apply:
	+ Consistent Nailing Line
	+ Proper Number of Nails per Shingle
	+ Nails Driven Flush
	+ No Over-driven Nails
* **Flashing Installation Quality**: Enter a number rating from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Visible Defects or Damage Found**: Enter a brief description of any issues found during the inspection.
* **Total Roof Area Covered**: Enter the total area of the roof in square feet.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in your observations and documentation.
* Take clear photos or notes to support your findings.
* Review and update this form as needed to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
