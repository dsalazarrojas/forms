# Operational Punctuality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Punctuality Audit Form is used to document and track instances of delays or issues that affect the operational schedule of a team or process. It helps to identify reasons and causes behind delays, enabling the team to become more efficient and punctual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form sections and fields, which are listed below.
* Enter your information in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
* Leave any optional fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the scheduled start time of the delayed operation or process. If the delay started on a different day, enter the start time on the previous day.
* **Team Members Involved** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team members who were involved in the delayed operation or process. Choose from the following options:
	+ Team Lead
	+ Shift Manager
	+ Operations Supervisor
* **Delay Reasons** (`delay_reasons`, select_one, optional): Choose the reason behind the delay from the following options:
	+ Equipment failure
	+ Weather
	+ Manpower shortage
	+ Material shortage
	+ Communication issues
	+ Process issues
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Delay Duration** (`delay_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the delay in minutes.
* **Impact Level** (`impact_level`, select_one, optional): Assess the impact of the delay using the following options:
	+ Critical
	+ Minor
	+ None
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or context about the delay.

## Tips

* Always complete this form as soon as possible after the delay occurs.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the delay reason and impact level.
* Use the notes field to add any additional context or details about the delay.
