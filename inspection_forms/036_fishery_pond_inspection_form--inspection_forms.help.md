# Fishery Pond Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishery Pond Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a fishery pond's condition, water, and fish life. This form will help inspectors collect and record data that will aid in the assessment, management, and conservation of the pond.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page in a clear and concise manner.
2. Use the provided labels as a guide to ensure you capture all necessary information.
3. If you have any concerns or issues, take note of them in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing general information about the pond, such as its location, size, and any other relevant details.
* **Water Observation** (`water_observation`, text, optional): Describe the water's condition, including its clarity, temperature, and any notable features.
* **Fish Observation** (`fish_observation`, text, optional): Report any observations about the fish life in the pond, including species, numbers, and behavior.
* **Site Details** (`site_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the site, such as surrounding land use, access, or other relevant context.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Use this field to record any comments, concerns, or issues you encounter during the inspection.
