# inflatable_safety_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inflatable safety inspection checklist is a tool designed to ensure the safe use and operation of inflatables, such as pool inflatables or emergency response balloons. This form helps operators and safety inspectors verify that inflatables are in good condition and meet safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to begin the inspection process.
2. Review each field for completeness and accuracy.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspect Inflatable** (`inspect_inflatable`, text, required): Describe the inflatable you are inspecting.
* **Inflatable Structure** (`inspect_inflatable_structure`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the inflatable structure is intact and safe, or 'No' if it is damaged or compromised.
* **Inflatable Fastenings** (`inspect_inflatable_fastenings`, select_multiple, required): List the types of fastenings you have found on the inflatable (e.g., 'Yes', 'No' for each type of fastening found).
* **Inflatable Inflated** (`inspect_inflatable_inflated`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the inflatable is inflated as expected, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Inflatable Vent Valves** (`inspect_inflatable_vent_valves`, select_multiple, required): List the types of vent valves you have found on the inflatable (e.g., 'Yes', 'No' for each type of vent valve found).
* **Inflatable Deflating** (`inspect_inflatable_deflating`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the inflatable deflates as expected, or 'No' if it does not.
* **Inflatable Inflate Rate** (`inspect_inflatable_inflate_rate`, text, required): Report the time it takes for the inflatable to inflate fully.
* **Inflatable Inflate Time** (`inspect_inflatable_inflate_time`, time, required): Report the time of day the inflatable inflated.
* **Inflatable Deflate Time** (`inspect_inflatable_deflate_time`, time, required): Report the time of day the inflatable deflated.
* **Inflatable Notes** (`inspect_inflatable_notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or observations about the inflatable.
* **Inflatable Inspector** (`inspect_inflatable_inspector`, text, required): Enter your name and ID or other contact information.
* **Inflatable Inspection Date** (`inspect_inflatable_inspection_date`, date, required): Report the date of the inspection.
* **Inflatable Inspection Time** (`inspect_inflatable_inspection_time`, time, required): Report the time of the inspection.
* **Inflatable Completed** (`inspect_inflatable_completed`, text, required): Confirm that the inspection is complete and accurate.

## Tips
- Ensure the inflatable is in a safe and secure location before starting the inspection.
- If you are unsure about any part of the inspection, consult the inflatable's manufacturer or seek additional guidance.
- Keep the comments section for notes and observations about the inflatable's condition.
- Complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure the safety of all users.
