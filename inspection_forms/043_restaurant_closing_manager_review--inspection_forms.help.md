# Restaurant Closing Manager Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Closing Manager Review form is used to gather information about a restaurant's closing process, including the category of the review, a brief description, and the assigned tool used during the review. This form is designed to provide insight into the restaurant's closing operations and help the management team assess their performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by selecting the correct category for the review (e.g., Inspection Forms, Other).
2.  Type a brief description of the review in the "Description" field.
3.  Enter the name of the tool assigned for the review in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4.  Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category under which the review falls, such as Inspection Forms or Other.
*   **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the review, describing what was reviewed.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required): Enter the name of the tool used during the review, such as a checklist or report.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Description" field to provide a clear understanding of the review.
*   Ensure you have entered the correct "Category" and "Assigned Tool" information to facilitate accurate tracking and analysis.
*   Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
