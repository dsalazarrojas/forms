# Facility Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for facility safety inspections. It helps ensure that facilities comply with relevant safety standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and select the relevant safety aspects that apply to your facility.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your selections to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Safety Inspection Checklist**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and title for the facility.
* **Safety Laws**: Select all applicable safety laws that the facility needs to adhere to.
* **Safety Regulations**: Select all applicable safety regulations that the facility needs to follow.
* **Safety Policies**: Select all applicable safety policies that the facility needs to enforce.
* **Safety Equipment**: Enter the safety equipment available at the facility.
* **Safety Procedures**: Select all applicable safety procedures that the facility needs to follow.
* **Safety Training**: Select all applicable safety training programs for employees.
* **Safety Inspections**: Select all applicable safety inspections that the facility needs to perform.
* **Safety Records**: Select all applicable safety records that the facility needs to keep.
* **Safety Certification**: Select all applicable safety certifications that the facility needs to obtain.
* **Facility Name 1/Facility Name 2**: Enter a brief description of the facility (this field may be redundant).
* **Facility Owner 1/Facility Owner 2**: Enter the facility's owner's name and title.
* **Facility Phone Number 1/Facility Phone Number 2**: Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter the emergency contact's name, email, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2**: This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Facility Safety Equipment**: Enter a brief description of the safety equipment available at the facility.
* **Facility Address**: This field may be redundant and is not required.

Note: Ensure that you fill out all required fields and double-check your selections for accuracy.
