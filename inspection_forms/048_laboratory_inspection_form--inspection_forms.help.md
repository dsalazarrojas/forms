# laboratory_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The laboratory_inspection_form is used to document and record information about a facility's inspection. This form is designed to gather data about the facility's safety, equipment, and personnel, and is used to inform inspection decisions and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of each section.
2. Enter the facility's name in the "facility_name" field.
3. Enter the facility's address in the "facility_address" field.
4. Enter the facility's city in the "facility_city" field.
5. Select the facility's state in the "facility_state" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the facility has a region or zone designation.
7. Enter the facility's zip code in the "facility_zip" field.
8. Enter the facility's phone number in the "facility_phone" field.
9. Enter the names of the inspectors and supervisors in the "facility_inspectors" and "facility_supervisors" fields, respectively.
10. Enter any additional safety concerns or concerns in the "facility_safety" field.
11. Select the types of equipment present at the facility in the "facility_equipment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility_name** (text): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_address** (text): Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_city** (text): Enter the city of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_state** (select_one): Select the state where the facility is located.
* **facility_region** (text): Enter "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the facility has a region or zone designation.
* **facility_zip** (text): Enter the zip code of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the facility being inspected.
* **facility_inspectors** (text): Enter the names of the inspectors conducting the inspection.
* **facility_supervisors** (text): Enter the names of the supervisors responsible for the facility's operations.
* **facility_safety** (text): Enter any additional safety concerns or concerns about the facility's safety record.
* **facility_equipment** (select_multiple): Select the types of equipment present at the facility (e.g. "Yes" for "Yes, equipment is present", or "No" for "No, equipment is not present").
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the facility.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
