# Excavator Attachment Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavator Attachment Inspection Checklist is used to track the inspection status of excavator attachments. It ensures that all attachments are properly inspected and recorded, reducing the risk of equipment failure and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information about the attachment, including its type and serial number.
2. Select the type of attachment from the options provided.
3. Record the attachment's manufacturer information, if applicable.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
5. Select the inspection status (Complete, Incomplete, or Cancelled).
6. Add any comments about the inspection.
7. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This section provides a brief overview of the attachment being inspected.
* **Attachment Type**: Select the type of attachment being inspected from the options provided (Boom, Bucket, or Other).
* **Attachment Serial Number**: Record the serial number of the attachment for identification purposes.
* **Manufacturer Information**: If the attachment has a manufacturer, select the relevant information from the options provided (Caterpillar, Komatsu, or Terex).
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Inspection Status**: Select the status of the inspection (Complete, Incomplete, or Cancelled).
* **Comments**: Add any relevant comments about the inspection, including any issues or recommendations.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately record the attachment's information and inspection status to maintain accurate records.
* Complete all required fields to ensure the form is submitted successfully.
* Use the comments field to provide detailed descriptions of any issues or recommendations.
* If the attachment's inspection status is Cancelled, ensure that any associated tasks or activities are also cancelled accordingly.
