# electrical_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the inspection of electrical equipment, collecting information on its status, location, notes, inspection date and time, and the person responsible for the inspection and submission of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the `electrical_equipment_inspection_form_page` field with a brief description or any additional comments about the equipment.
* Enter the `electrical_equipment_model` in the next field.
* Type in the `electrical_equipment_location` to identify where the equipment is situated.
* Select the current `electrical_equipment_status` of the equipment from the provided options ("Active" or "Inactive").
* Add any notes or comments about the equipment's condition in the `electrical_equipment_notes` field.
* Enter the date of the inspection in the `electrical_equipment_inspection_date` field.
* Enter the time of the inspection in the `electrical_equipment_inspection_time` field.
* Indicate if the equipment was inspected by the person who submitted the form in the `electrical_equipment_inspection_user` field.
* Confirm if the form was submitted by the same person who inspected the equipment in the `electrical_equipment_inspection_form_submitted_by` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **electrical_equipment_inspection_form_page**: A text field for any additional comments about the electrical equipment.
- **electrical_equipment_model**: Enter the model number of the electrical equipment.
- **electrical_equipment_location**: Type in where the equipment is located.
- **electrical_equipment_status**: Select "Active" if the electrical equipment is currently in use or functioning correctly, and "Inactive" if it's not.
- **electrical_equipment_notes**: A free text field for any comments or notes about the equipment.
- **electrical_equipment_inspection_date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
- **electrical_equipment_inspection_time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
- **electrical_equipment_inspection_user**: Check "Yes" if the person filling this form was the one who inspected the equipment, or "No" otherwise.
- **electrical_equipment_inspection_form_submitted_by**: Check "Yes" if this form was submitted by the same person who inspected the equipment, or "No" otherwise.
