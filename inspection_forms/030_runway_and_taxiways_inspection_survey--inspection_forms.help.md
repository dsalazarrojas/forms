# Runway And Taxiways Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about inspections of runways and taxiways. This includes collecting data on the airport name, runways, and taxiways inspected, as well as any issues identified during the inspection. The form is used to ensure that all aspects of the airport's infrastructure are well-maintained and safe for aircraft operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Airport Name**, which should be the name of the airport that was inspected.
2. Select the **Runways** that were inspected, from the dropdown menu. This could include Runway A, B, or C.
3. Select the **Taxiways** that were inspected, from the dropdown menu. This could include Taxiway A, B, or C.
4. Enter any **Issue Comments** about the inspection, if applicable.
5. Upload **Photos** of any issues or defects observed during the inspection, if applicable.
6. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the inspection, if applicable.
7. Select the **AM/PM** for the time of the inspection.
8. Indicate whether the inspection was **Completed** or not.
9. Enter any **Comments** about the inspection.
10. Select the **Assigned To** person or team that completed the inspection.
11. Enter the **Completed By** person or team that completed the inspection.
12. Select the **Issue Priority** level, from High, Medium, or Low.
13. Select the **Issue Status**, from Active, Closed, or Resolved.
14. Choose the **Category** of the issue, from Runway, Taxiway, or Other.
15. Choose the **Location** of the issue, from Runway, Taxiway, or Other.
16. Select the **Type** of issue, from Crack, Debris, or Other.
17. Select the **Severity** of the issue, from Not Observed, Observed, or Critical.
18. Enter a brief **Description** of the issue.
19. Upload a **Photo** of the issue, if applicable.
20. Enter any additional **Comments** about the issue.
21. Enter any **Status Comments** about the issue.
22. Enter any **Remark** about the issue.
23. Enter the **Issue Date**, which should be the date the issue was discovered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Airport Name**: Enter the name of the airport that was inspected.
* **Runways**: Select the runways that were inspected during the survey.
* **Taxiways**: Select the taxiways that were inspected during the survey.
* **Issue Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the issues or defects observed during the inspection.
* **Photos**: Upload photos of any issues or defects observed during the inspection.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection, in the 24-hour format.
* **AM/PM**: Select the AM/PM for the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Completed**: Indicate whether the inspection was completed or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the person or team that completed the inspection.
* **Completed By**: Enter the person or team that completed the inspection.
* **Issue Priority**: Select the priority level of the issue, from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Issue Status**: Select the status of the issue, from Active, Closed, or Resolved.
* **Category**: Choose the category of the issue, from Runway, Taxiway, or Other.
* **Location**: Choose the location of the issue, from Runway, Taxiway, or Other.
* **Type**: Select the type of issue, from Crack, Debris, or Other.
* **Severity**: Select the severity of the issue, from Not Observed, Observed, or Critical.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the issue.
* **Photo**: Upload a photo of the issue, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the issue.
* **Status Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the issue status.
* **Remark**: Enter any additional remarks about the issue.
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date the issue was discovered, in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
