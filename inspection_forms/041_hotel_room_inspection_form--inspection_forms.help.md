# Hotel Room Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Room Inspection Form is designed to be used by hotel staff to report the condition of a hotel room, track maintenance needs, and assign tasks and deadlines for maintenance and repair.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Check the condition of the room carefully and select the appropriate status from the options provided.
3. If maintenance is needed, select the relevant category and describe the issue in detail.
4. Enter the room number and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Room Inspection** (1): Briefly describe the overall condition of the room.
* **Condition Checklist** (2): Select the current condition of the room from the options: Good, Poor, or N/A.
* **Maintenance Request** (3): If the room needs any maintenance, describe the issue and the maintenance requested in this field.
* **Room Status** (4): Select the status of the room from the options: Clean, Dirty, or N/A.
* **Maintenance Priority** (5): This field is intended for the hotel staff to enter the priority level of the maintenance request.
* **Category** (6): Select all the categories that apply to the maintenance need, from the options: Electrical, Plumbing, etc.
* **Description** (7): Enter any additional comments or details about the maintenance request.
* **Room Number** (8): Enter the room number of the inspected room.
* **Assigned By** (9): Enter the name of the person or department that assigned the task.
* **Assigned To** (10): Enter the name of the person or department that the task is assigned to.
* **Completed By** (11): Enter the name of the person that completed the task.
* **Completed On** (12): Enter the date the task was completed.

## Tips

* Please be accurate and thorough in your report to ensure that the correct information is collected.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure that the information is properly tracked.
* This form is used for internal purposes, please do not share it with guests or other external parties.
