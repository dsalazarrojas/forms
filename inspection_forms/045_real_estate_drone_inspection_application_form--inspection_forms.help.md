# Real Estate Drone Inspection Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the inspection of real estate properties using a drone. It captures the necessary information to document the inspection process and findings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Property ID and Property Address fields, which are required to identify the inspected property.
2. Select the Client Name from the dropdown menu, which is a required field.
3. Enter the Inspector Name, which is also a required field.
4. Input the Inspection Date, Start Time, and End Time to record the inspection details.
5. Select the Inspection Areas to be inspected (Front, Back, Side, Top).
6. Enter the Drone Equipment Model and Sensor Specifications to record the equipment used.
7. Fill in the Camera Batteries Level and Flight Time to record the drone's performance.
8. Upload or link to the Inspection Report, which is a required field.
9. Enter the Inspection Status (Completed, In Progress, Canceled).
10. Complete the Comments field, if necessary.
11. Provide your Email and Phone numbers for contact information.
12. Enter your signature to confirm you are the Client.
13. Enter your signature as the Inspector.
14. Enter the Drone Operator's signature, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property ID** (`1`, `number`, required): This is the unique ID of the property being inspected.
* **Property Address** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Client Name** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select the client from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspector Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Start Time** (`6`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the inspection.
* **End Time** (`7`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Status** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the inspection (Completed, In Progress, Canceled).
* **Property Inspector Comments** (`8`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments from the property inspector.
* **Drone Equipment Model** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the drone used for the inspection.
* **Camera Angle of View** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter the angle of view of the camera.
* **Sensor Specifications** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter the specifications of the sensor used.
* **Camera Batteries Level** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the level of the camera batteries during the inspection.
* **Flight Time** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the time spent flying the drone.
* **Inspection Report Link** (`16`, `text`, required): Upload or link to the inspection report.
* **Inspection Areas** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas inspected during the flight (Front, Back, Side, Top).
* **Drone Equipment Status** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the status of the drone equipment.
* **Client Signature** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter your signature as the client.
* **Inspector Signature** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter your signature as the inspector.
* **Drone Operator Signature** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the signature of the drone operator.
* **Email** (`19`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.

Note: This guide provides an understanding of the form's purpose and how to complete it, and explains each field in the context of the form provided.
