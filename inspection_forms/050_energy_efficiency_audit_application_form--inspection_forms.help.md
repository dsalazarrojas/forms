# Energy Efficiency Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Audit Application Form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of conducting energy efficiency audits. This form ensures that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide the schedule date for the audit, if known. If not, you can select a preferred date.
3. Enter details about the property being audited, including any relevant information.
4. Estimate your energy usage and energy savings.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
6. Finally, select the type of applicant (Energy Consultant, Resident, or Business) to proceed with your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required: false): If you know the date you would like to schedule the audit, please enter it here. If not, you can select a preferred date in the next field.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: false): If you are unable to schedule a specific date, please select a preferred date for the audit.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, required: false): Please enter any details about the property being audited, such as its size or location.
* **Energy Usage** (`energy_usage`, number, required: false): Estimate your current energy usage in units suitable for your country or region.
* **Energy Savings** (`energy_savings`, number, required: false): Estimate the potential energy savings from the audit, in units suitable for your country or region.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Please select whether you are an Energy Consultant, Resident, or Business to proceed with your submission.
