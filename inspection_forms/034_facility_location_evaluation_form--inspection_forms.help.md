# Facility Location Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Location Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate a facility's location, type, condition, safety, and other relevant factors. This form is completed by facility inspectors to gather information about a facility's suitability for a particular purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the "Facility Location" field, where you can enter a brief description of the facility's location. This field is optional.
2. Next, choose the "Facility Type" from the list of options provided, which can be "Facility", "Warehouse", or "Office".
3. If applicable, select the "Facility Condition" using the "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low" options.
4. The "Facility Equipments" field is meant for additional comments or descriptions of equipment found at the facility.
5. In the "Facility Safety" field, select the level of safety you observed during the inspection, which can be "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low".
6. The "Facility Location Rating" field is an optional numerical rating of the facility's location.
7. In the "Facility Comments" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the facility's location.
8. If applicable, add facility images, videos, or documents to provide further context about the facility.
9. Enter the date of the facility location rating in the "Facility Location Rating Date" field.
10. Select the facility inspector who performed the evaluation from the list of options provided.
11. Choose the "Prophets" field, which is meant for selecting the "Prophet 1", "Prophet 2", or "Prophet 3" options.
12. Select the facility's safety rating using the "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low" options.
13. Enter the date of the facility safety rating in the "Facility Safety Rating Date" field.
14. Select the facility safety inspector who performed the rating from the list of options provided.
15. In the "Facility Inspector Comments" field, provide any additional comments or observations from the facility inspector.
16. Enter the name of the facility inspector in the "Facility Inspector Name" field.
17. Select multiple facility inspectors who participated in the evaluation from the list of options provided.
18. In the "Facility Safety Rating Comments" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the facility's safety.
19. Enter the date of the facility safety rating 2 in the "Facility Safety Rating Date 2" field.
20. Select the facility safety inspector 2 who performed the rating from the list of options provided.
21. Select multiple facility inspectors who participated in the evaluation from the list of options provided.
22. In the "Facility Safety Rating Comments 2" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the facility's safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Location**: A brief description of the facility's location. (Optional)
* **Facility Type**: The type of facility being evaluated, which can be "Facility", "Warehouse", or "Office". (Optional)
* **Facility Condition**: The level of condition of the facility, which can be "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low". (Optional)
* **Facility Equipments**: Additional comments or descriptions of equipment found at the facility. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety**: The level of safety observed during the inspection, which can be "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low". (Optional)
* **Facility Location Rating**: A numerical rating of the facility's location, on a scale of 1-5. (Optional)
* **Facility Comments**: Additional comments or observations about the facility's location. (Optional)
* **Facility Images**: Attach facility images to provide further context. (Optional)
* **Facility Videos**: Attach facility videos to provide further context. (Optional)
* **Facility Documents**: Attach facility documents to provide further context. (Optional)
* **Facility Location Rating Date**: The date of the facility location rating. (Optional)
* **Facility Inspector**: The name of the facility inspector who performed the evaluation. (Optional)
* **Prophets**: Select the "Prophet 1", "Prophet 2", or "Prophet 3" options. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating**: The level of safety observed during the inspection, which can be "Strict", "Moderate", or "Low". (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Date**: The date of the facility safety rating. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Inspector**: The name of the facility safety inspector who performed the rating. (Optional)
* **Facility Inspector Comments**: Additional comments or observations from the facility inspector. (Optional)
* **Facility Inspector Name**: The name of the facility inspector. (Optional)
* **Facility Inspectors**: Select multiple facility inspectors who participated in the evaluation. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Comments**: Additional comments or observations about the facility's safety. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Date 2**: The date of the facility safety rating 2. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Inspector 2**: The name of the facility safety inspector 2 who performed the rating. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Inspectors 2**: Select multiple facility inspectors who participated in the evaluation. (Optional)
* **Facility Location Rating Date 2**: The date of the facility location rating 2. (Optional)
* **Facility Safety Rating Comments 2**: Additional comments or observations about the facility's safety. (Optional)
