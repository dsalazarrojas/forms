# Railway Track Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the safety inspection of railway tracks. It helps in documenting the inspection process, identifying potential safety issues, and taking corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the "Inspection Date" field with the date of the safety inspection.
2. Select the "Inspection Frequency" field with the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
3. Enter your "Inspector Name" in the field provided.
4. Describe the "Location" of the safety inspection in the field provided.
5. Check the status of the equipment in the "Equipment Status" field.
6. Select the "Inspection Result" field with the outcome of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or Deferred).
7. Write any comments about the inspection in the "Comments" field.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Attach photos of defects or issues in the "Photos" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`section_1_inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the safety inspection.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`section_1_inspection_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Inspector Name** (`section_1_inspector_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Location** (`section_1_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of the safety inspection.
* **Equipment Status** (`section_1_equipment_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the status of the equipment (Working, Not Working, or Needs Maintenance).
* **Inspection Result** (`section_2_inspection_result`, select_one, required: true): Select the outcome of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or Deferred).
* **Comments** (`section_2_comments`, text, required: false): Write any comments about the inspection.
* **Additional Comments** (`section_3_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Photos** (`section_3_photos`, text, required: false): Attach photos of defects or issues during the inspection.
* **Inspection Notes** (`section_4_inspection_notes`, text, required: false): Write any notes about the inspection process.

## Tips
* Ensure that the inspection frequency matches the actual frequency of the inspection.
* Check the equipment status correctly to ensure accurate reporting.
* Attach photos of defects or issues to support the inspection result.
* Be concise and clear when writing comments and additional comments.
