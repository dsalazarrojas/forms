## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., F-150, Silverado, etc.).
* **Vehicle Plate**: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Weight**: Enter the weight of the vehicle in kilograms.
* **Vehicle Length**: Enter the length of the vehicle in meters.
* **Vehicle Width**: Enter the width of the vehicle in meters.
* **Vehicle Height**: Enter the height of the vehicle in meters.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle (e.g., box truck, trailer, etc.).
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the condition of the vehicle (New, Used, Both).
* **Engine Type**: Select the type of engine the vehicle uses (e.g., Gasoline, Diesel, Dual).
* **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel the vehicle uses (e.g., Gas, Diesel, Both).
* **Vehicle Speed**: Enter the vehicle's speed in kilometers per hour.
* **Vehicle Location**: Enter the location where the vehicle is currently parked.
* **Driver License**: Enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Inspection**: Select the type of vehicle inspection performed (Pre-Trip, Post-Trip, Both).
* **Vehicle Defect**: Indicate if there are any vehicle defects (Yes, No).
* **Driver Comments**: Enter any comments about the vehicle or the inspection.
* **Vehicle Comments**: Enter any comments about the vehicle's condition.
