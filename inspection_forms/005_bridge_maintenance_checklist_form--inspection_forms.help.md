# Bridge Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Bridge Maintenance Checklist Form is used to document the maintenance work performed on a bridge. It helps track the progress of maintenance activities, identify areas that require further attention, and ensure that all necessary tasks are completed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Bridge Name, Bridge Location, Maintenance Date, Crew Leader Name, Number of Crew Members, Weather Conditions, and Total Hours Worked.
2. Answer the questions on the sections for Deck Maintenance and Structural Maintenance, indicating the status of each task as necessary.
3. In the Summary section, describe any issues found during maintenance and list any repairs that were completed.
4. If there are any additional actions needed, enter them in the Follow-up Actions Needed field.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Additional Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bridge Name** (`Bridge Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name or ID of the bridge being maintained.
* **Bridge Location** (`Bridge Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the bridge being maintained.
* **Maintenance Date** (`Maintenance Date`, `date`, required): Select the date maintenance was performed.
* **Crew Leader Name** (`Crew Leader Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the crew leader who performed the maintenance.
* **Number of Crew Members** (`Number of Crew Members`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of crew members who worked on the maintenance.
* **Weather Conditions** (`Weather Conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select the weather conditions during maintenance.
* **Deck Cleaning Performed** (`Deck Cleaning Performed`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if deck cleaning was performed.
* **Deck Crack Sealing** (`Deck Crack Sealing`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if crack sealing was performed.
* **Expansion Joint Inspection** (`Expansion Joint Inspection`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of expansion joint inspection.
* **Deck Drainage Check** (`Deck Drainage Check`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if drainage systems were checked.
* **Bearing Inspection** (`Bearing Inspection`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of bearing inspection.
* **Steel Member Inspection** (`Steel Member Inspection`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of steel member inspection.
* **Concrete Surface Repair** (`Concrete Surface Repair`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if concrete repairs were made.
* **Guardrail Inspection** (`Guardrail Inspection`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of guardrail inspection.
* **Approach Roadway Check** (`Approach Roadway Check`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of approach roadway inspection.
* **Signage Check** (`Signage Check`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if signage was checked and is adequate.
* **Lighting Inspection** (`Lighting Inspection`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of lighting inspection.
* **Issues Found** (`Issues Found`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues found during maintenance.
* **Repairs Completed** (`Repairs Completed`, `text`, optional): List any repairs that were completed.
* **Follow-up Actions Needed** (`Follow-up Actions Needed`, `text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions required.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`Total Hours Worked`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours spent on maintenance.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
