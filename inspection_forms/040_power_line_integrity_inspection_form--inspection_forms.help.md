# power_line_integrity_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Line Integrity Inspection Form is a supplementary tool for inspectors to gather information about power line inspections. It is designed to aid in the collection of data and ensure that inspections are performed thoroughly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorities to access and complete it.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. If necessary, attach any supporting documents or photos to provide additional context.
4. Save the form and submit it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Power Line Integrity Inspection Form** (`power_line_integrity_inspection_form`): This is the main form that contains all the relevant information about the power line inspection. Please fill in the form with accurate and concise information.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the inspection.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the inspection.
* **Power Line Location** (`power_line_location`): Enter the location of the power line.
* **Power Line Location Coordinates** (`power_line_location_coordinates`): Enter any additional coordinates or details about the power line location.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`): Enter your ID as the inspector (if applicable).
* **Inspection Type** (`inspection_type`): Select the type of inspection performed (e.g., routine, emergency).
* **Power Line Type** (`power_line_type`): Select the type of power line being inspected (e.g., live, dead).
* **Power Line Status** (`power_line_status`): Select the status of the power line (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Hazard Found** (`hazard_found`): Select if a hazard was found during the inspection.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Add any additional remarks or comments about the inspection.
* **Power Line Integrity Form Notes** (`power_line_integrity_form_notes`): Add any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Power Line Integrity Form Attachments** (`power_line_integrity_form_attachments`): Attach any supporting documents or photos to the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Use attachments to provide supporting evidence or photos of any findings.
* Review and proofread the form carefully before submitting it for review and approval.
