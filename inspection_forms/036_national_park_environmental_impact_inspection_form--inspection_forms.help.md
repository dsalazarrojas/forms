# national_park_environmental_impact_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The national park environmental impact inspection form is used to record data on the environmental condition of national parks during inspections. It helps park rangers and conservationists to assess the park's status and make informed decisions for its conservation and management.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, follow these steps:
    1.  Enter the park's name in the "park_name" field.
    2.  Select the date of the inspection in the "date_inspected" field.
    3.  Evaluate the environmental condition of the park and select the appropriate option from the "environmental_condition" field.
    4.  Assess the water quality and select the appropriate option from the "water_quality" field (if applicable).
    5.  Assess the air quality and select the appropriate option from the "air_quality" field (if applicable).
    6.  Evaluate the habitat condition and select the appropriate option from the "habitat_condition" field (if applicable).
    7.  Evaluate the vegetation status and select the appropriate option from the "vegetation_status" field (if applicable).
    8.  Assess the habitat health and select the appropriate option from the "habitat_health" field (if applicable).
    9.  Check for wildlife harassment and select the appropriate option from the "wildlife_harassment" field (if applicable).
    10. Enter any other comments or observations in the "other_comments" field (if applicable).
    11. Enter your name and contact information in the "inspector_name" and "inspector_contact_info" fields.
    12. Enter your title in the "inspector_title" field (if applicable).
    13. Enter notes about the inspection in the "inspector_notes" field (if applicable).
    14. Enter comments about the site visit in the "site_visit_comments" field (if applicable).
    15. Enter a photo of the site visit in the "site_visit_photo" field (if applicable).
    16. Enter a video of the site visit in the "site_visit_video" field (if applicable).
    17. Enter notes about the park visit in the "park_visit_notes" field (if applicable).
    18. Enter comments about the park visit in the "park_visit_comments" field (if applicable).
    19. Enter a photo of the park visit in the "park_visit_photo" field (if applicable).
    20. Enter a video of the park visit in the "park_visit_video" field (if applicable).
    21. Enter comments for follow-up actions in the "follow_up_comments" field (if applicable).
    22. Enter a photo for follow-up actions in the "follow_up_photo" field (if applicable).
    23. Enter a video for follow-up actions in the "follow_up_video" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Park Name (park_name)**: Enter the name of the national park being inspected.
*   **Date Inspected (date_inspected)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
*   **Environmental Condition (environmental_condition)**: Evaluate the park's environmental condition, choosing from the options: Clean, Moderate, Severe, or Extremely Severe.
*   **Water Quality (water_quality)**: Assess the water quality of the park, choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (if applicable).
*   **Air Quality (air_quality)**: Assess the air quality of the park, choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (if applicable).
*   **Habitat Condition (habitat_condition)**: Evaluate the habitat condition of the park, choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (if applicable).
*   **Vegetation Status (vegetation_status)**: Evaluate the vegetation status of the park, choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (if applicable).
*   **Habitat Health (habitat_health)**: Assess the habitat health of the park, choosing from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor (if applicable).
*   **Wildlife Harassment (wildlife_harassment)**: Check for wildlife harassment, choosing from the options: Yes or No (if applicable).
*   **Other Comments (other_comments)**: Enter any other comments or observations about the inspection.
*   **Inspector Name (inspector_name)**: Enter your name as the inspector.
*   **Inspector Contact Info (inspector_contact_info)**: Enter your contact information.
*   **Inspector Title (inspector_title)**: Enter your title (if applicable).
*   **Inspector Notes (inspector_notes)**: Enter notes about the inspection.
*   **Site Visit Comments (site_visit_comments)**: Enter comments about the site visit (if applicable).

*   **Site Visit Video (site_visit_video)**: Enter a video of the site visit (if applicable).
