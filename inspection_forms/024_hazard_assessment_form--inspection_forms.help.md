# Hazard Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hazard Assessment Form is used to identify, assess, and record hazards found in a workplace or facility. This form helps in taking corrective actions to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in the following pages:
   - Introduction: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to confirm that you understand the purpose and instructions of the form.
   - Hazards: This page will ask you about the presence of hazards in your workplace and their location.
   - Hazard Type: Here, you will be asked to select the type of hazard present.
   - Frequency and Severity: This page will ask you about the frequency and severity of the hazards.
   - Hazard Control Measure: Provide details about the control measures in place to mitigate the hazards.
   - Employee Details: Please fill out the employee details, including name, position, and contact information.
   - Supervisor Details: Provide the supervisor's name and position if available.
   - Safety Officer Details: Enter the safety officer's name and contact information.
   - Hazard Reporting: Confirm whether the hazard has been reported or not.
   - Hazard Description: Describe the hazard in detail.
   - Corrective Action: Provide details about any corrective actions taken or planned.
   - Follow-up Details: Enter follow-up dates and details if applicable.
   - Follow-up Person: Select the person responsible for follow-up actions.
   - Follow-up Status: Indicate the current follow-up status.
   - Follow-up Date: Enter the follow-up date.
   - Follow-up Description: Provide a detailed description of the follow-up action or status.
   - Submit: Review and submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (`title`, text, required):
  Please confirm that you understand the purpose and instructions of this form.

- **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, required):
  Please select all the hazards present in your workplace from the options provided.

- **Hazard Location** (`hazard_location`, text, optional):
  Provide the location of the hazard(s) identified.

- **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`, select_one, required):
  Select the type of hazard present.

- **Hazard Frequency** (`hazard_frequency`, number, required):
  Enter the frequency of the hazard (e.g., how often it occurs).

- **Hazard Severity** (`hazard_severity`, select_one, required):
  Select the severity of the hazard.

- **Hazard Control Measure** (`hazard_control_measure`, text, required):
  Describe the control measure(s) in place to mitigate the hazard.

- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required):
  Enter the name of the employee(s) involved in the hazard.

- **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, select_one, required):
  Select the position of the employee(s) involved.

- **Employee Date of Birth** (`employee_date_of_birth`, date, required):
  Enter the date of birth of the employee(s) involved.

- **Employee Contact Info** (`employee_contact_info`, text, required):
  Enter the contact information of the employee(s) involved.

- **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional):
  Enter the name of the supervisor if applicable.

- **Supervisor Position** (`supervisor_position`, text, optional):
  Enter the position of the supervisor if applicable.

- **Safety Officer Name** (`safety_officer_name`, text, required):
  Enter the name and contact information of the safety officer.

- **Hazard Reported** (`hazard_reported`, select_multiple, required):
  Confirm whether the hazard has been reported or not.

- **Hazard Description** (`hazard_description`, text, required):
  Describe the hazard in detail.

- **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, optional):
  Provide details about any corrective actions taken or planned.

- **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional):
  Enter the follow-up date if applicable.

- **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional):
  Enter follow-up details if applicable.

- **Follow-up Person** (`follow_up_person`, select_one, required):
  Select the person responsible for follow-up actions.

- **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required):
  Indicate the current follow-up status.

- **Follow-up Description** (`follow_up_description`, text, required):
  Provide a detailed description of the follow-up action or status.

- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required):
  Review and submit your assessment.
