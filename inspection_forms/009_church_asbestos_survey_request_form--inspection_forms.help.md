# Church Asbestos Survey Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for church administrators to provide information about their church property and the required survey details to asbestos surveyors. It helps in ensuring that the right information is collected and communicated effectively to conduct a successful survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and title in the "Requestor Information" section.
2. Provide the church's property name, address, city, state, and zip in the "Property Information" section.
3. Specify the type of survey needed and the areas of concern in the "Survey Details" section.
4. Enter the urgency level and preferred survey date in the "Survey Scheduling" section.
5. Provide contact information for the on-site contact person in the "Access and Contact Information" section.
6. Confirm whether previous asbestos surveys have been conducted and provide any additional information about them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Requestor**: Enter your name as the person requesting the survey. 
* **Title or Position**: Enter your official title or position within the church.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any updates or further information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Church Property Name**: Enter the name of the church property.
* **Property Address**: Enter the physical address of the church property.
* **City**: Enter the city where the church property is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where the church property is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of the church property.
* **Type of Asbestos Survey Needed**: Select the type of survey required:
	+ Management Survey
	+ Refurbishment Survey
	+ Demolition Survey
	+ Suspected Contamination Survey
	+ Routine Inspection Survey
	+ Other
* **Specific Areas of Concern**: Specify which areas or materials require assessment.
* **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level:
	+ Routine
	+ Within 30 days
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Urgent
	+ Emergency
* **Building Year Built (if known)**: Enter the year the church building was built if you know it.
* **Building Square Footage**: Enter the square footage of the church building if you know it.
* **Preferred Survey Date (or date range)**: Enter the preferred date for the survey or date range.
* **Are there occupancy restrictions needed during survey**: Select if there are any occupancy restrictions during the survey.
* **On-Site Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person on-site.
* **On-Site Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the on-site contact person.
* **Site Access Information**: Provide any special instructions for accessing the property.
* **Have Previous Asbestos Surveys Been Conducted**: Select if previous surveys have been conducted.
* **Details About Previous Surveys**: Provide any information about previous surveys conducted on the property.
