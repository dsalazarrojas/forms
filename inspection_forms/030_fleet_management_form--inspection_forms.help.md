# Fleet Management Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fleet Management Form is used to record and manage vehicle data, including vehicle details, status, and inspection dates. This form helps to maintain accurate and up-to-date information about the fleet, enabling efficient management and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the required vehicle information, including:
    *   Vehicle Number
    *   Vehicle Make
    *   Vehicle Year
    *   Vehicle Model
    *   Vehicle Registration Plate
    *   Vehicle Color
    *   Vehicle Year Purchased
    *   Vehicle Type
    *   Vehicle Status
    *   Vehicle Driver
    *   Vehicle Owner
*   Provide any additional notes or comments for each vehicle in the "Vehicle Notes" field.
*   If applicable, select the vehicle's status and provide a reason for the status change in the "Vehicle Status Note" field.
*   Enter the vehicle's last inspection date and next inspection date in the "Vehicle Last Inspection" and "Vehicle Next Inspection" fields.
*   Enter the vehicle's last mileage reading in the "Vehicle Last Mileage" field.
*   Indicate if the vehicle has been requisitioned or returned, and provide a reason for the action in the "Vehicle Requisitioned Reason" and "Vehicle Returned Reason" fields.
*   Finally, update the vehicle's status date in the "Vehicle Status Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
*   **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
*   **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Corolla, F-150, etc.
*   **Vehicle Registration Plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
*   **Vehicle Color**: Enter the vehicle's color.
*   **Vehicle Year Purchased**: Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
*   **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle, e.g., Car, Truck, Bus.
*   **Vehicle Status**: Select the current status of the vehicle, e.g., Active, Inactive, Sold.
*   **Vehicle Driver**: Enter the name of the vehicle's driver.
*   **Vehicle Owner**: Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
*   **Vehicle Inspected**: Enter the date of the vehicle's last inspection.
*   **Vehicle Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the vehicle's next inspection.
*   **Vehicle Last Mileage**: Enter the vehicle's last mileage reading.
*   **Vehicle Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Status Note**: Enter a note about the vehicle's status change.
*   **Vehicle Requisitioned**: Indicate if the vehicle has been requisitioned.
*   **Vehicle Requisitioned Reason**: Enter the reason for requisitioning the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Returned**: Indicate if the vehicle has been returned.
*   **Vehicle Returned Reason**: Enter the reason for returning the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Status Date**: Enter the date of the vehicle's status change.
*   **Vehicle Status Note**: Enter a note about the vehicle's status change.
*   **Vehicle Driver**: Enter the name of the vehicle's driver.
*   **Vehicle Owner**: Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
