# Move In Move Out Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Move In Move Out Inspection Form is a tool used to record the condition of a property at the beginning and end of a tenancy. It helps landlords, property owners, or facility managers to identify any damages, missing items, or issues with the property that may be present. This form is essential for future maintenance and repairs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the tenant move-in date.
2.  Provide the property address.
3.  Describe the condition of each of the property's components, such as units, common areas, equipment, appliances, fixtures, lighting, plumbing, electrical systems, heating and air conditioning systems, safety equipment, and fire safety equipment.
4.  Select the condition of the smoke detector for the property.
5.  Review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Move In Move Out Inspection Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Tenant Move In Date (tenant-move-in-date)**: Enter the date the tenant moved in.
    *   Type: date
*   **Property Address (property-address)**: Enter the address of the property.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Property (condition-of-property)**: Describe the overall condition of the property.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Units (condition-of-units)**: Describe the condition of each unit.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Common Areas (condition-of-common-areas)**: Describe the condition of common areas such as hallways, lobbies, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Equipment (condition-of-equipment)**: Describe the condition of equipment such as appliances, fixtures, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Appliances (condition-of-appliances)**: Describe the condition of appliances such as kitchen, bathroom, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Fixtures and Furniture (condition-of-fixtures-and-furniture)**: Describe the condition of fixtures and furniture such as lighting, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Lighting (condition-of-lighting)**: Describe the condition of lighting fixtures and fixtures.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Plumbing (condition-of-plumbing)**: Describe the condition of plumbing fixtures and systems.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Electrical (condition-of-electrical)**: Describe the condition of electrical systems and fixtures.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Heating and Air Conditioning (condition-of-heating-airconditioning)**: Describe the condition of heating and air conditioning systems.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Safety Equipment (condition-of-safety-equipment)**: Describe the condition of safety equipment such as fire extinguishers, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Fire Safety Equipment (condition-of-fire-safety-equipment)**: Describe the condition of fire safety equipment such as smoke detectors, etc.
    *   Type: text
*   **Condition of Smoke Detector (condition-of-smoke-detector)**: Select the condition of smoke detectors.
    *   Type: select multiple
*   **Condition of Smoke Detector**
        +   Smoke Detector is working
