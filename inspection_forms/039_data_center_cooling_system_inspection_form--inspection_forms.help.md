# Data Center Cooling System Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Center Cooling System Inspection Form is designed for facility maintenance personnel or data center operators to report their findings on the cooling system's condition, temperature, humidity, and airflow readings. This form helps to ensure that the data center's climate control systems are functioning properly, which is crucial for the health and safety of equipment and personnel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form, where you will find a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions on how to complete it.
2. **Cooling System Condition**: Check the condition of the cooling system and select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
3. **Temperature Readings**: Record the temperature readings (in numbers) for each of the two designated points (e.g., Temperature Reading 1 and Temperature Reading 2).
4. **Humidity Readings**: Record the humidity readings (in numbers) for each of the two designated points (e.g., Humidity Reading 1 and Humidity Reading 2).
5. **Airflow Readings**: Record the airflow readings (in numbers) for each of the two designated points (e.g., Airflow Reading 1 and Airflow Reading 2).
6. **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
7. **Assigned Person**: Enter the name and designation of the person responsible for the inspection.
8. **Submission Information**: Fill in the submission information (date, time, and by whom) for each of the four submission instances (e.g., Submitted By, Submitted Date, Submitted Time, and Submitted Time 24 Hour).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Cooling System Condition**: Check the condition of the cooling system. Select "Yes" if the system is functioning properly, and "No" otherwise.
* **Temperature Reading 1**: Record the temperature reading (in numbers) at a specific point.
* **Temperature Reading 2**: Record the temperature reading (in numbers) at another specific point.
* **Humidity Reading 1**: Record the humidity reading (in numbers) at a specific point.
* **Humidity Reading 2**: Record the humidity reading (in numbers) at another specific point.
* **Airflow Reading 1**: Record the airflow reading (in numbers) at a specific point.
* **Airflow Reading 2**: Record the airflow reading (in numbers) at another specific point.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Assigned Person**: Enter the name and designation of the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name and designation of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Record the date the form is submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Record the time the form is submitted.
* **Submitted Time 24 Hour**: Record the time in 24-hour format (if necessary).
* **Comments 2, 3, 4**: These fields seem to be identical to the "Comments" field above, but with different labels. You can ignore these fields as they are likely redundant.
