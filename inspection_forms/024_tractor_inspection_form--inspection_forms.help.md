# Tractor Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tractor Inspection Form is designed to collect information about tractors, including their engine conditions, types, models, years, and vehicle details. This form helps inspectors and users to assess and record the condition and specifications of tractors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Tractor Inspection Form` page with relevant information about the tractor.
2. Provide details about the tractor's engine condition on the next page.
3. Answer questions about the tractor's type and capacity.
4. Enter the tractor's model and year (if known).
5. Add any additional vehicle details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tractor Inspection Form
* **Tractor Inspection Form** (`tractor_inspection_form_1`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form.
### Tractor Year
* **Tractor Year** (`tractor_year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the tractor's manufacture.
### Engine Condition
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the engine condition from the options: new, used, damaged, or other.
### Tractor Type
* **Tractor Type** (`tractor_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of tractor from the options: standard, wide, compact, or other.
### Engine Capacity
* **Engine Capacity** (`engine_capacity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the engine capacity (not applicable if not known).
### Tractor Model
* **Tractor Model** (`tractor_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tractor model (if known).
### Tractor Year (2)
* **Tractor Year (2)** (`tractor_year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the tractor's manufacture (if different from the first year field).
### Vehicle Identification Number
* **Vehicle Identification Number** (`tractor_inspection_form_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle identification number (if known).
### Serial Number
* **Serial Number** (`tractor_inspection_form_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the serial number (if known).
### Vehicle Make
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle make (if known).
### Vehicle Model
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle model (if known).
### Vehicle Year
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the vehicle year (if known).
### Vehicle Inspection Form 6
* **Vehicle Inspection Form 6** (`vehicle_inspection_form_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional vehicle inspection form 6 details (if applicable).
### Vehicle Type
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_inspection_form_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the vehicle type from the options: standard, wide, compact, or other.

## Tips
- Double-check the information provided, especially for fields with options.
- If you are unsure about any field, try to provide as much information as possible to help the inspectors assess the tractor's condition and specifications.
- This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other inspection tools and procedures to ensure accurate and complete assessment of the tractors.
