# Landlord Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Inspection Form is designed to collect essential information about a landlord's property inspection. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are recorded accurately and consistently, facilitating efficient and effective property management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter the address** of the inspected property.
2.  **Enter the landlord's name** for the property.
3.  **Specify the property type**: Select 'Landlord Owned' if the property is owned by the landlord, 'Tenant Owned' if it is owned by the tenant, or 'Lease' if it is leased.
4.  **Select the date of inspection** in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY' or 'MM/DD/YY'.
5.  **Choose the time of inspection** in the format 'HH:MM:SS'.
6.  **Add any additional notes or comments** regarding the inspection.
7.  **Enter your email address**, which will be used for communication purposes.
8.  **Enter your phone number**, which will be used for communication and follow-up.
