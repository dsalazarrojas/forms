# Bicycle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bicycle Inspection Form is designed to collect data for bike inspections, ensuring consistency and thoroughness. It covers all critical aspects of a bike, from the frame and components to the drivetrain, brakes, and overall safety assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Start on the first page** with your contact information:
	* Enter the owner's name and contact details (phone or email).
2. **Enter bike details**:
	* Enter the bike brand and model.
	* Select the bike type (e.g., road bike, mountain bike, etc.).
3. **Check the frame and components**:
	* Assess the overall frame condition (excellent, good, acceptable, poor, or critical).
	* Check for any frame cracks or major damage (no cracks or damage, minor cracks, significant cracks, or major structural damage).
4. **Evaluate the wheels and tires**:
	* Check the front and rear tire conditions (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
	* Assess the tire pressure (properly inflated, slightly under, significantly under, over-inflated, or severely under).
	* Check the wheel alignment (perfectly aligned, minor wobble, noticeable wobble, significant bend, or severely bent).
5. **Inspect the brakes**:
	* Select the brake type (rim, disc, coaster, or mixed).
	* Assess the brake condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
	* Check the brake pad wear (like new, good condition, fair condition, minimal pad remaining, or need immediate replacement).
6. **Check the drivetrain and chain**:
	* Assess the chain condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
	* Check the chain tension (perfect tension, slightly loose, properly adjusted, slightly tight, or too loose or too tight).
7. **Inspect the handlebars and seat**:
	* Assess the handlebar condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or damaged).
	* Check the grip or bar tape condition (like new, good condition, minor wear, significant wear, or deteriorating).
8. **Check the overall safety assessment**:
	* Assess the overall safety of the bike (safe, safe with minor maintenance, conditionally safe, unsafe, or dangerous).
	* Provide any recommended services or repairs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name (2)**: Enter the owner's name.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter the owner's contact details (phone or email).
* **Bicycle Brand (4)**: Enter the bike's brand name.
* **Bicycle Model (5)**: Enter the bike's model number or name.
* **Bicycle Type (6)**: Select the type of bike (e.g., road, mountain, hybrid, etc.).
* **Inspection Date (7)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name (8)**: Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Frame Condition (10)**: Assess the overall frame condition (excellent, good, acceptable, poor, or critical).
* **Frame Cracks or Damage (11)**: Check for any frame cracks or major damage.
* **Paint or Finish Condition (12)**: Assess the cosmetic condition of the paint or finish.
* **Front Tire Condition (14)**: Assess the front tire condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
* **Rear Tire Condition (15)**: Assess the rear tire condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
* **Tire Pressure Check (16)**: Check if the tires are properly inflated.
* **Wheel Alignment (17)**: Check if the wheels are perfectly aligned.
* **Brake Type (19)**: Select the type of brakes (rim, disc, coaster, or mixed).
* **Brake Condition (20)**: Assess the overall brake condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
* **Brake Pad Wear (21)**: Assess the brake pad wear (like new, good condition, fair, minimal pad remaining, or need immediate replacement).
* **Brake Noise or Squealing (22)**: Check for any unusual brake noises.
* **Chain Condition (24)**: Assess the chain condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or critical).
* **Chain Tension (25)**: Check if the chain is properly tensioned.
* **Handlebar Condition (28)**: Assess the overall handlebar condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or damaged).
* **Grip or Bar Tape Condition (29)**: Assess the grip or bar tape condition (like new, good condition, minor wear, significant wear, or deteriorating).
* **Seat Condition (30)**: Assess the saddle condition (excellent, good, fair, poor, or cracked or broken).
* **Overall Safety Assessment (32)**: Assess the overall safety of the bike (safe, safe with minor maintenance, conditionally safe, unsafe, or dangerous).
* **Recommended Services or Repairs (33)**: Provide any recommended services or repairs.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each question carefully, as the form is designed to ensure a thorough inspection.
* Use the options provided to give a clear and concise assessment of each component.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the bike, make a note in the additional notes section (34).
* This form is meant to be a standardization tool for bike inspections, so please answer consistently and accurately.
