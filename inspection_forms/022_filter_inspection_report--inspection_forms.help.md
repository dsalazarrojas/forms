# Filter Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Filter Inspection Report" form is designed to assist inspectors in quickly searching for specific inspection reports based on their desired criteria. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1**: To complete this form, follow these steps:
  1. Review the options provided for "Filter Condition" and "Filter Result" fields and select the options that apply to your search.
  2. Choose the "Inspection Date" and "Inspection Time" to specify a particular time frame for the inspection.
  3. Add any relevant information about the "Maintenance Plan" if you are looking for specific maintenance-related reports.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Filter Condition** (`filter_condition`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are looking for reports with this condition, "No" if you are not.
- **Filter Result** (`filter_result`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant results you want to filter by.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection you are looking for.
- **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection you are looking for.
- **Maintenance Plan** (`maintenance_plan`, note, required: false): Provide any additional details about the maintenance plan you are searching for.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all relevant options for "Filter Condition" and "Filter Result" fields to get the most accurate results.
- Use the "Inspection Date" and "Inspection Time" fields to filter reports within a specific time frame.
- The "Maintenance Plan" field is optional, but providing detailed information about the maintenance plan can help narrow down your search results.
