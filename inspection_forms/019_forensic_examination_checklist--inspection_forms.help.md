# Forensic Examination Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Forensic Examination Checklist is a form used to document and collect information about a forensic examination process. This form provides a structured approach to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by typing in the requested information for each field.
2. For fields that have options, select the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: First Page
* **first_page** (`first_page`, text, required: false): This field is the title of the form. You can type in a brief title to help identify the examination.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your contact information for any questions or concerns.
* **case_info** (`case_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the case being examined.
* **evidence** (`evidence`, text, required: false): Describe the evidence being examined.
* **examination_type** (`examination_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of examination being conducted (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
* **examination_date** (`examination_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the examination.
* **examiner_name** (`examiner_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the examiner.
* **examiner_role** (`examiner_role`, text, required: false): Describe your role as the examiner.
* **laboratory_name** (`laboratory_name`, text, required: false): Identify the laboratory where the examination was conducted.
* **equipment_name** (`equipment_name`, text, required: false): Identify the equipment being examined.
* **examination_notes** (`examination_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the examination.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the examination.

### Page 2: Additional Information
* **examiner_id** (`examiner_id`, text, required: false): Enter your ID as the examiner.
* **examination_location** (`examination_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of the examination.
* **equipment_type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required: false): Identify the type of equipment being examined (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
* **equipment_serial_number** (`equipment_serial_number`, text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **examination_condition** (`examination_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Describe the condition of the examination (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
* **equipment_location** (`equipment_location`, text, required: false): Identify the location of the equipment.
* **examination_status** (`examination_status`, select_one, required: false): Describe the status of the examination (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **case_number** (`case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the case number.
* **examiner_phone** (`examiner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as the examiner.
* **examiner_email** (`examiner_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email as the examiner.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Provide your signature.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of completion.
* **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of completion.
* **examination_location** (`examination_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of the examination.
