# structure_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `structure_inspection_form` is a form used to collect information about the structural integrity of a building or structure. It helps to identify any potential issues or defects that may be present in the structure, allowing for swift and effective maintenance or repairs to be conducted. This form is intended for use by building owners, property managers, or maintenance personnel to assess the condition of a building's structure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate page for the structure you are inspecting.
2. Answer all the questions on the page, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If a question is not applicable to the structure, simply mark the "No" answer on the corresponding field.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **structure_inspection_form_section_1** (`structure_inspection_form`, text, optional): This field is not visible to the end-user as it is only used to identify the form.
* **structure_inspection_form_section_2** (`structure_inspection_form_section_2`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the structure has a defect or issue that requires attention, otherwise select "No".
* **structure_inspection_form_section_3** (`structure_inspection_form_section_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable "Yes" if multiple issues are present, otherwise select "No".
* **structure_inspection_form_section_4** (`structure_inspection_form_section_4`, date, optional): Enter the date when the structure inspection was last performed or when the next inspection is scheduled.
* **structure_inspection_form_section_5** (`structure_inspection_form_section_5`, time, optional): Enter the time of day or time of the next scheduled inspection. 

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions thoroughly and accurately to provide a comprehensive report.
* If you are unsure about any question, it is recommended to select "No" and include a brief explanation in the comments field (not shown in this form).
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the structure is inspected and maintained as needed.
