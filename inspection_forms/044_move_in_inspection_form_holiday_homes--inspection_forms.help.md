# Move In Inspection Form Holiday Homes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a property inspection, including property address, inspection details, and move-in date.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
2.  Ensure that you provide the correct property address.
3.  Enter the name of the inspection, which can be a brief description of the inspection event.
4.  Select the correct date of the inspection, which is when the inspection took place.
5.  Choose the correct time of the inspection, which is when the inspection occurred.
6.  If applicable, select the move-in date, which is when the tenant moved in.
7.  Choose the status of the move-in, which can be true or false, depending on the circumstances.
8.  Provide the reason for the inspection, which can be a brief explanation of the purpose of the inspection.
9.  Add any additional notes or photos that are relevant to the inspection.
10.  Finally, sign the move-in signature field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the property that was inspected.
*   **Name of Inspection**: Provide a brief description of the inspection event.
*   **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date on which the inspection took place.
*   **Time of Inspection**: Choose the time of the inspection, which is when it occurred.
*   **Move In Date**: If the tenant has moved in, enter the date of move-in. Otherwise, leave it blank.
*   **Move In Status**: Select true if the tenant has moved in, false if they have not, or unknown if the status is unclear.
*   **Reason for Inspection**: Provide a brief explanation of why the inspection was conducted.
*   **Notes and Photos**: Add any additional notes or photos that are relevant to the inspection.
*   **Move In Signature**: Sign and date the move-in signature field to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
