# GPS Field Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GPS Field Testing Form is a document used to collect and document the testing process and results of a GPS device.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Complete the form accurately and honestly to provide a thorough documentation of the testing process and results.
3. If you are unsure about any field or section, consult with a supervisor or a colleague for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Device Information** (`device_info`, text, optional): This section is used to capture general information about the GPS device being tested, such as its model, make, or any other relevant details.
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_num`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the GPS device being tested.
* **Device Model** (`device_model`, text, optional): Enter the model number of the GPS device being tested.
* **Location Information** (`location_info`, text, optional): Describe the location where the testing was performed, including any relevant details such as latitude, longitude, or elevation.
* **Longitude** (`location_long`, number, optional): Enter the longitude of the testing location, if available.
* **Latitude** (`location_lat`, number, optional): Enter the latitude of the testing location, if available.
* **Date Tested** (`date_tested`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the testing was performed.
* **Time Tested** (`time_tested`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the testing was performed.
* **Location Notes** (`location_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the location where the testing was performed.
* **Tester Information** (`tester_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself, the tester.
* **Tester Name** (`tester_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the tester.
* **Tester Email** (`tester_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for further questions or clarification.
* **Tester Phone** (`tester_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted for further questions or clarification.
* **Test Notes** (`test_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the testing process, including any issues or observations.
* **Test Status** (`test_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the test, either "Passed" or "Failed".
* **Test Status 2** (`test_status_2`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the test, either "Passed" or "Failed". (Duplicate field)
* **Test Status 3** (`test_status_3`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the test, either "Passed" or "Failed". (Duplicate field)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the testing process.

Note: The optional fields can be left blank if not applicable to the specific testing scenario.
