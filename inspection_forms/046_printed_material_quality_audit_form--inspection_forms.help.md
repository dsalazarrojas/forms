# Printed Material Quality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Printed Material Quality Audit Form is designed for inspecting printed materials. This form is used to record and evaluate the quality of printed materials, including their material type, color, size, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Printed Material Quality in the field labeled "Printed Material Quality".
2. Enter the Inspection Team Name in the field labeled "Inspection Team Name".
3. Enter the description of the printed material in the field labeled "Printed Material".
4. Enter the type of material being inspected in the field labeled "Material Type".
5. Enter the color of the material in the field labeled "Material Color".
6. Enter the size of the material in the field labeled "Material Size".
7. Enter the location where the inspection took place in the field labeled "Inspection Location".
8. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar in the field labeled "Inspection Date".
9. Select the time of the inspection in the field labeled "Inspection Time".
10. Enter any additional notes about the printed material quality in the field labeled "Printed Material Quality Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Printed Material Quality** (`Printed Material Quality`): This field is for recording the quality of the printed material. Enter a description of the material's quality, such as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Inspection Team Name** (`Inspection Team Name`): This field is for recording the name of the team responsible for the inspection.
* **Printed Material** (`Printed Material`): This field is for describing the printed material being inspected.
* **Material Type** (`Material Type`): This field is for entering the type of material being inspected (e.g., paper, plastic, etc.).
* **Material Color** (`Material Color`): This field is for entering the color of the material (e.g., white, blue, etc.).
* **Material Size** (`Material Size`): This field is for entering the size of the material (e.g., A4, A3, etc.).
* **Inspection Location** (`Inspection Location`): This field is for entering the location where the inspection took place.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`): This field is for selecting the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`Inspection Time`): This field is for selecting the time of the inspection.
* **Printed Material Quality Notes** (`Printed Material Quality Notes`): This field is for entering any additional notes about the printed material quality.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., MM/dd/yy for dates, HH:MM for times).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
