# Anti-Static Workstation Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Anti-Static Workstation Assessment form is used to evaluate and document the state of anti-static workstations in various departments to ensure a safe working environment. This guide will walk you through the form fields and their purpose.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to enter accurate and relevant details.
2. For fields with options, select the most accurate response based on your evaluation.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Assessor Information

* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or role.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you conducted the assessment.
* **Workstation ID** (`workstation_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the workstation being assessed.
* **Workstation Location** (`workstation_location`, text, required): Enter the building and room number where the workstation is located.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department that uses this workstation.
* **Workstation Type** (`workstation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ESD workstation being assessed (e.g., single operator bench, dual operator bench, assembly station, etc.).

### Wrist and Heel Straps

* **Wrist Strap Assessment** (`wrist_strap_section`, note): This section is not a field to fill in but provides space for you to take note of wrist strap issues, if any.
* **Wrist Strap Available** (`wrist_strap_available`, select_one, required): Check if wrist straps are provided at each position or shared among users.
* **Wrist Strap Condition** (`wrist_strap_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the state of the wrist straps (excellent, good, fair, poor, not applicable).
* **Wrist Strap Testing** (`wrist_strap_testing`, select_one, required): Check if wrist straps are tested regularly (daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, never).
* **Heel Straps Available** (`heel_straps_available`, select_one, required): Check if heel straps are available.

### Flooring

* **Flooring Assessment** (`flooring_section`, note): This section is not a field to fill in but provides space for you to take note of flooring issues, if any.
* **Flooring Type** (`flooring_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of flooring (ESD vinyl tile, ESD rubber flooring, ESD carpet, conductive epoxy, standard flooring, other).
* **Flooring Condition** (`flooring_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the state of the flooring (excellent, good, fair, poor, damaged).
* **Flooring Resistance** (`flooring_resistance`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the measured resistance of the flooring in ohms.

### Equipment and Ionizers

* **Equipment Assessment** (`equipment_section`, note): This section is not a field to fill in but provides space for you to take note of equipment issues, if any.
* **Equipment Grounding** (`equipment_grounding`, select_one, required): Check if equipment is properly grounded (all equipment grounded, most equipment grounded, some equipment grounded, no equipment grounded).
* **Ionizer Present** (`ionizer_present`, select_one, required): Check if an ionizer is present (yes, at workstation, yes, in room, no).
* **Ionizer Functional** (`ionizer_functional`, select_one, required): Evaluate if the ionizer is working properly (yes, verified, yes, not verified recently, false, not applicable).

### Signage and Documentation

* **ESD Warning Signage** (`esd_signage`, select_one, required): Check if ESD warning signs are posted (yes, clearly visible, yes, but worn, no signage).
* **ESD Procedures Documented** (`procedures_documented`, select_one, required): Check if ESD procedures are documented (yes, at workstation, yes, in department, no documentation).
* **Employee Training Records** (`training_records`, select_one, required): Check if employees are trained on ESD procedures (all employees trained, some employees trained, no training records).

### Compliance

* **ANSI/ESD Compliance** (`ansi_esd_compliance`, select_one, required): Evaluate if the workstation meets ANSI ESD standards (fully compliant, mostly compliant, partially compliant, non-compliant, not assessed).
* **Deficiencies Found** (`deficiencies_found`, text, required): Describe any issues or problems encountered during the assessment.
* **Recommended Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, required): Enter suggested actions to address any issues or problems identified.
* **Priority Rating** (`priority_rating`, select_one, required): Assess the urgency of addressing the issues (low - address during routine maintenance, medium - address within 30 days, high - address within 7 days, critical - address immediately).
* **Next Assessment Date** (`next_assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the next scheduled assessment.
* **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, text, required): Enter your digital signature to confirm your assessment.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, text, not required): This field is optional but requires the supervisor's name, if desired.

### Other

* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the workstation was installed.
* **Last Assessment Date** (`last_assessment_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the previous assessment.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Take your time to evaluate each field carefully and accurately.
* Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be completed in a systematic and organized manner to ensure a thorough assessment.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or a colleague for guidance.
* Please keep the form for future reference and use it as a guide for future assessments.
