# Operational Compliance Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Compliance Inspection Checklist is designed to collect data during on-site inspections to ensure the smooth operation of facilities, equipment, and teams. The form helps to monitor and report any issues or concerns that arise during inspections, enabling prompt corrective actions to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Inspection Date** field with the date of the inspection.
2. Select the **Inspection Location** field to enter the location where the inspection took place.
3. Choose the **Inspection Team** field to select the team that conducted the inspection. This could be Asset Management, Maintenance, or Operations.
4. For the **Equipment Status** field, choose the status of the equipment inspected. This could be Active, Inactive, or Missing.
5. In the **Findings** field, list any issues or concerns observed during the inspection. This could include anything from minor defects to critical hazards.
6. In the **Corrective Action** field, record any actions taken to address the findings or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Location** (`inspection_location`): Enter the location of the inspection.
* **Inspection Team** (`inspection_team`): Choose the team that conducted the inspection.
	+ *Asset Management*: Responsible for the maintenance and management of assets.
	+ *Maintenance*: Responsible for equipment maintenance and repair.
	+ *Operations*: Conducts the operational activities.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Choose the status of the equipment inspected.
	+ *Active*: The equipment is functional and in operation.
	+ *Inactive*: The equipment is not currently in use.
	+ *Missing*: The equipment is not present or has not been located.
* **Findings** (`findings`): List any issues or concerns observed during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`): Record any actions taken to address the findings or concerns.

## Tips
- Be thorough in your inspection and ensure that all relevant information is captured.
- Verify the accuracy of the information entered, especially for the **Findings** field.
- If there are multiple findings, use the **Equipment Status** field to select all relevant statuses.
- Remember to document all corrective actions taken to address the findings or concerns.
