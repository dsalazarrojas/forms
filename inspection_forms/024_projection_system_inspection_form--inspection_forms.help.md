# projection_system_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the projection system's status, location, and inspection details for record-keeping and reference purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the `system_id` number to identify the system being inspected.
2. Select the `system_type` category from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of projection system.
3. Provide the `system_location` where the system is installed or used.
4. Choose the `system_status` from the dropdown menu to reflect the system's current condition (Functional, Non-Functional, or Inactive).
5. Add any additional `system_notes` regarding the system's condition or issues.
6. Enter your `user_name` and `user_id` for identification purposes.
7. Select the `date_inspected` and `time_inspected` for the inspection date and time.
8. Enter the `technician_name` who conducted the inspection.
9. Provide your `inspector_signature` for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **system_id** (number, required): Enter the system's unique identifier number.
* **system_type** (select_one, required): Choose the category of projection system being inspected (e.g., Projection System).
* **system_location** (text, optional): Provide the location where the system is installed or used.
* **system_status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the system (Functional, Non-Functional, or Inactive).
* **system_notes** (note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the system's condition or issues.
* **user_name** (text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **user_id** (number, optional): Enter your unique identifier number.
* **date_inspected** (date, optional): Select the date of the inspection.
* **time_inspected** (time, optional): Select the time of the inspection.
* **technician_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the technician who conducted the inspection.
* **inspector_signature** (text, optional): Provide your signature for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have authorization to complete this form for the inspected system.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* Keep the `system_notes` field concise and focused on essential details.
* Ensure that your `inspector_signature` is legible and accurate.
