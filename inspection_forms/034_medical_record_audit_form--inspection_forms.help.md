# Medical Record Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Record Audit Form is designed to gather information about medical records, ensuring they are accurate and complete. This form helps healthcare professionals audit and review medical records, identifying any discrepancies or issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter it manually in the "date_of_visit" field.
3. Choose the medical specialty that was involved in the visit from the list of options.
4. Mark the status of the medical record as "Complete", "Incomplete", or "Partially" in the "medical_record_reviewed" field.
5. Select the date the medical record was reviewed in the "medical_record_reviewed_date" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the medical record in the "medical_record_reviewed_by" field.
7. Enter the time the medical record was reviewed in the "medical_record_reviewed_time" field (if applicable).
8. Sign and date the medical record with your signature in the "medical_record_reviewed_by_signature" field.
9. Choose the result of the audit from the options: "Pass", "Fail", or "Warning" in the "audit_result" field.
10. Enter any comments or feedback in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Name (patient_name)
* **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's full name.
  * Required: No

### Date of Visit (date_of_visit)
* **Date of Visit** (date): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
  * Required: No

### Medical Specialty (medical_specialty)
* **Medical Specialty** (select multiple): Choose the medical specialty involved in the visit.
  * Options: Cardiologist, Endocrinologist, Gastroenterologist, Neurologist, Pulmonologist, Urologist
  * Required: Yes

### Medical Record Reviewed (medical_record_reviewed)
* **Medical Record Reviewed** (select one): Mark the status of the medical record as "Complete", "Incomplete", or "Partially".
  * Required: Yes

### Medical Record Reviewed Date (medical_record_reviewed_date)
* **Medical Record Reviewed Date** (date): Select the date the medical record was reviewed (if applicable).
  * Required: No

### Medical Record Reviewed By (medical_record_reviewed_by)
* **Medical Record Reviewed By** (text): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the medical record.
  * Required: No

### Medical Record Reviewed Time (medical_record_reviewed_time)
* **Medical Record Reviewed Time** (time): Enter the time the medical record was reviewed (if applicable).
  * Required: No

### Medical Record Reviewed By Signature (medical_record_reviewed_by_signature)
* **Medical Record Reviewed By Signature** (note): Sign and date the medical record with your signature.
  * Required: No

### Audit Result (audit_result)
* **Audit Result** (select one): Choose the result of the audit: "Pass", "Fail", or "Warning".
  * Required: Yes

### Comments (comments)
* **Comments** (text): Enter any comments or feedback about the medical record.
  * Required: No
