# Heating System Filling Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to check and record the status of a heating system in a building or facility. It helps ensure that the system is working correctly and provides a way to track any issues that may need to be addressed. The form is designed to be completed by facility managers, maintenance staff, or other authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the status of the heating system from the options provided on page 1.
2. If there are issues with the system, choose the "Requires repair" option on page 1.
3. On page 2, check all fields that apply to the current status of the heating system.
4. On page 3, verify the temperature reading.
5. On page 4, you are not required to answer any questions.
6. On page 5, enter the temperature reading.
7. On page 6, enter the date of the inspection or check-in.
8. On page 7, enter the time of the inspection or check-in.
9. On page 8, you are not required to answer this field (timezone is an optional field and is not used in this form).
10. On page 9, enter your email address.
11. On page 10, enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Heating System Filling Checklist** (`page_1`, `select_one`): Select the current status of the heating system from the options provided. This will help you determine which sections of the form to fill out.
* **Checklist** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`): Check all fields that apply to the current status of the heating system, such as any issues or concerns you have found during the inspection.
* **Temperature Check** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`): This field is used to check the temperature of the system. Select the appropriate temperature reading from the options provided.
* **Temperature** (`page_5`, `number`): Enter the current temperature reading of the system.
* **Date** (`page_6`, `date`): Enter the date of the inspection or check-in.
* **Time** (`page_7`, `time`): Enter the time of the inspection or check-in.
* **Email** (`page_9`, `email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`page_10`, `text`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
