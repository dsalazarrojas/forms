# Monthly Facility Inspection Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the results of a monthly facility inspection. It ensures that facilities and their surroundings are properly maintained, and that any maintenance issues are promptly addressed. Completing this form helps facility managers keep track of inspection results, maintenance status, and assigned technicians.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1:** Select a facility and fill in its name in the "Facility Name" field. This is the first page of the form.
2. **Step 2:** Choose a date for the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field. This date should be the date of the inspection.
3. **Step 3:** Enter any maintenance issues found during the inspection in the "Maintenance Issues" field.
4. **Step 4:** Choose the maintenance status from the options: "Active", "Inactive", "Active" indicates that maintenance is being performed, while "Inactive" means it's not.
5. **Step 5:** Indicate whether a technician has been assigned to perform maintenance in the "Assigned Technician" field. Select "Yes" if a technician has been assigned, "No" otherwise.
6. **Step 6:** Enter any comments related to the inspection in the "Comments" field. This field is optional.
7. **Step 7:** Select the facility location from the options: "Yes" or "No". This field is optional.
8. **Step 8:** Indicate if maintenance has been completed in the "Maintenance Completed" field. Select "Yes" if maintenance has been completed, "No" otherwise.
9. **Step 9:** Take photos of any issues and upload them in the "Photos Taken" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Maintenance Issues**: List any maintenance issues found during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Status**: Choose the maintenance status of the facility. Options are "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Assigned Technician**: Indicate whether a technician has been assigned to perform maintenance. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments related to the inspection.
* **Facility Location**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the facility is located in the same place or not.
* **Maintenance Completed**: Indicate whether maintenance has been completed. Select "Yes" or "No".
* **Photos Taken**: Take photos of any issues and upload them in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the "Active", "Inactive" and "Yes", "No" options for "Maintenance Status" and "Facility Location" fields respectively.
* For "Maintenance Issues" and "Comments" fields, be concise and descriptive.
* For "Assigned Technician" and "Maintenance Completed" fields, select "Yes" or "No" accurately.
* Remember to take photos of any issues in "Photos Taken" field if necessary.
