# Spa Health Code Compliance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Health Code Compliance Inspection Form is a tool used to evaluate a spa's compliance with relevant health codes and regulations. It helps inspectors assess the facility's conditions, identify potential risks, and track corrective actions.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the general information at the beginning of the form, including the facility's name, address, and contact details.
* Choose the inspection date and time from the respective fields.
* Provide details about the facility, including the facility manager's name and contact person.
* Identify any areas that require inspection, such as specific rooms or sections within the spa.
* Evaluate the condition of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and emergency exit signs.
* Check the presence and condition of emergency exits, taking note of any issues or concerns.
* Determine the status of the spa's license, including whether it is active, inactive, or expired.
* Record any corrective actions taken or planned to address any deficiencies or concerns.
* Provide feedback on the facility's overall rating, including any total risks identified.
* Note any actions taken or planned to address these risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter the facility's name, address, and contact details.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the facility, such as its type and purpose.
* **Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the facility's address.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or observations about the inspection.
* **Inspection Areas** (`inspection_areas`, select_one, optional): Choose the specific areas of the facility that require inspection.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the condition of the facility's equipment, such as:
	+ Good: The equipment is in good condition and functioning properly.
	+ Fair: The equipment is in fair condition but requires maintenance or repair.
	+ Poor: The equipment is in poor condition or not functioning at all.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the presence and condition of safety equipment, such as:
	+ Present: The safety equipment is present and in good condition.
	+ Absent: The safety equipment is not present.
* **Emergency Exit** (`emergency_exit`, time, optional): Evaluate the condition of the emergency exits, taking note of any issues or concerns.
* **Emergency Exit Notes** (`emergency_exit_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or observations about the emergency exit.
* **Spa License** (`spa_license`, select_one, optional): Determine the status of the spa's license:
	+ Active: The license is active and up-to-date.
	+ Inactive: The license is inactive but not expired.
	+ Expired: The license is expired.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter the spa's license number.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, select_one, optional): Determine the status of the business license:
	+ Active: The license is active and up-to-date.
	+ Inactive: The license is inactive but not expired.
	+ Expired: The license is expired.
* **License Number 2** (`license_number_2`, text, optional): Enter the business license number.
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the facility's overall rating:
	+ Good: The facility is in good condition and meets health code requirements.
	+ Fair: The facility is in fair condition but requires maintenance or correction.
	+ Poor: The facility is in poor condition and does not meet health code requirements.
* **Total Risks** (`total_risks`, text, optional): Enter any total risks identified during the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, select_multiple, optional): Record any corrective actions taken or planned to address any deficiencies or concerns:
	+ Taken: The corrective action has been taken.
	+ Not Taken: The corrective action has not been taken.
* **Action Status** (`action_status`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the status of the corrective actions:
	+ Completed: The action has been completed.
	+ Due: The action is due but not completed.
	+ Not Due: The action is not due.
* **Next Review** (`next_review`, date, optional): Choose the date of the next review.
* **Next Review Time** (`next_review_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the next review.
* **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or observations about the facility.
