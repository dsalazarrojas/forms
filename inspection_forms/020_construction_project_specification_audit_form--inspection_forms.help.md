This form is used to conduct a construction project specification audit, which involves reviewing and verifying that a construction project meets the specified requirements and standards. The form is designed to be completed by an auditor who conducts the audit, and it captures key information about the project, including the auditor's name, the project's reference or ID, the date of the audit, and the overall compliance status. The form also allows the auditor to list non-conforming items and recommend corrective actions if needed. The follow-up date for re-inspection or verification is also included. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the construction project meets the specified requirements and standards, and to provide a record of the audit process.

# Construction Project Specification Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the results of a construction project specification audit, which is a process of reviewing and verifying that a construction project meets the specified requirements and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Auditor name" field. This is the person conducting the audit.
2. Enter the project's reference or ID in the "Project reference" field. This is the name or ID of the project being audited.
3. Enter the date of the audit in the "Audit date" field.
4. Select the overall compliance status of the project by choosing one of the options in the "Compliance overview" field.
5. If there are any non-conforming items, list them in the "Non conformance items" field.
6. If necessary, enter recommended corrective actions in the "Corrective actions required" field.
7. Enter the date for re-inspection or verification in the "Follow up date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor name** (`auditor_name`, `text`, required): The person conducting the audit.
* **Project reference** (`project_reference`, `text`, required): The name or ID of the project being audited.
* **Audit date** (`audit_date`, `date`, required): The date of the audit.
* **Compliance overview** (`compliance_overview`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to indicate the overall compliance status of the project.
	+ Fully compliant
	+ Partially compliant
	+ Non compliant
* **Non conformance items** (`non_conformance_items`, `text`, optional): A list of items not meeting specifications.
* **Corrective actions required** (`corrective_actions_required`, `text`, optional): Recommended corrective measures.
* **Follow up date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): The date for re-inspection or verification.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If there are any non-conforming items, list them in detail to help identify areas for improvement.
* If recommended corrective actions are required, enter them clearly and concisely to ensure that they are understandable and actionable.
