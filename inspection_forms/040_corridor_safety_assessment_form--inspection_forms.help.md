# Corridor Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corridor Safety Assessment Form is used to identify and document hazards in the facility's corridors, ensuring a safe working environment for all employees and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Select the risk severity level of the hazard from the dropdown menu.
3. Describe the hazard location in the text field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the hazard in the text field.
5. Identify the type of hazard from the dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, specify the correct actions to mitigate the risk in the text field.
7. Indicate if safety equipment is available and functioning correctly.
8. If safety equipment is used, describe the type and condition of the equipment.
9. Sign off as the facility manager to confirm the assessment.
10. Date and time of the assessment.
11. Enter your name and ID as the safety officer conducting the assessment.
12. Enter the name of the person assigned to address the hazard.
13. Sign off as the safety officer to confirm the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corridor Safety Assessment Form**: This is the title of the form. It serves as an identifier for the assessment.
* **Risk Severity**: Select the level of risk associated with the hazard:
	+ Low: A relatively minor risk with a low potential for harm.
	+ Medium: A moderate risk with a potential for moderate harm.
	+ High: A significant risk with a high potential for harm.
* **Hazard Location**: Describe the exact location of the hazard in the corridor.
* **Hazard Description**: Provide a detailed description of the hazard.
* **Hazard Type**: Identify the type of hazard:
	+ Physical Hazard: A physical object or condition that can cause harm.
	+ Environmental Hazard: An environmental condition that can cause harm.
	+ Other: Any other type of hazard not listed above.
* **Corrective Action**: Describe any corrective actions taken or proposed to mitigate the risk.
* **Safety Equipment**: Indicate if safety equipment is available and functioning correctly:
	+ Yes: Safety equipment is present and functional.
	+ No: Safety equipment is not available or not functional.
* **Type of Safety Equipment**: If safety equipment is used, describe the type and condition of the equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of the safety equipment.
* **Facility Manager Signature**: Sign off as the facility manager to confirm the assessment.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Safety Officer Name**: Enter your name as the safety officer conducting the assessment.
* **Safety Officer ID**: Enter your ID as the safety officer conducting the assessment.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to address the hazard.
* **Facility Manager Signature**: Sign off as the facility manager to confirm the assessment.
