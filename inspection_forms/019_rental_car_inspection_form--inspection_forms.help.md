# Rental Car Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Car Inspection Form is designed to assess the condition of a rental vehicle's various components to ensure they are properly maintained and any issues are addressed before they become major problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's condition carefully, paying attention to any issues or concerns.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. If you're unsure about any section, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Vehicle Condition
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any notable issues or concerns.

### 2. Exterior Body
* **Exterior Body** (`exterior_body`, `text`, optional): Report any exterior damage, dents, or scratches on the vehicle.

### 3. Engine
* **Engine** (`engine`, `text`, optional): Check the engine's condition, including the oil level, fuel level, and any leaks.

### 4. Interior Body
* **Interior Body** (`interior_body`, `text`, optional): Inspect the interior for any signs of wear, stains, or odors.

### 5. Tires
* **Tires** (`tires`, `text`, optional): Check the tire pressure, tread depth, and any signs of wear.

### 6. Brakes
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, `text`, optional): Inspect the brake pads, rotors, and any signs of wear.

### 7. Suspension
* **Suspension** (`suspension`, `text`, optional): Check the suspension system for any signs of wear or damage.

### 8. Electrical System
* **Electrical System** (`electrical_system`, `text`, optional): Inspect the electrical system, including the battery, lights, and any signs of wear.

## Tips
* Be thorough and detailed in your assessment to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you're unsure about any section, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* This form is designed to help you identify potential issues early on, so don't hesitate to report any concerns.
