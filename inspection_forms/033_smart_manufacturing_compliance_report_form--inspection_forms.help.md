# Smart Manufacturing Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Manufacturing Compliance Report Form is used to document and track non-compliance incidents in manufacturing facilities. It helps ensure that all relevant information is recorded and reported, enabling timely corrective actions to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Inspection Form** field with a brief description of the non-compliance incident.
2. Select the **Non Compliance Incident** field to describe the specific incident in more detail.
3. Enter the **Corrective Action** field to document any steps taken to correct the problem.
4. Choose the **Status** field to indicate whether the corrective action is **Complete**, **In Progress**, **Closed**, or **Pending**.
5. Enter the **Date Recorded** field to specify the date the incident was recorded.
6. Enter the **Time Recorded** field to specify the time the incident was recorded.
7. Select the **Assigned To** field to assign the incident to a specific person.
8. Enter any additional **Remarks** or comments about the incident in the **Remarks** field.
9. Sign the **Signature** field with your signature.
10. Enter the **Signature Date** field to specify the date of your signature.
11. Enter the **Signature Time** field to specify the time of your signature.
12. If you are updating the form, select the **Updated By** field to indicate who made the update.
13. If you are updating the form, enter the **Updated Date** and **Updated Time** fields to specify the date and time of the update.
14. If you are updating the form, enter the **Updated By ID** field to specify the ID of the person who made the update.
14. If you are updating the form, enter the **Updated At** field to specify the date and time of the update.
15. If you are deleting the form, enter the **Deleted At** field to specify the date and time the form was deleted.
