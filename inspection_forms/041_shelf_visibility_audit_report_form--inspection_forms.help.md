# Shelf Visibility Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Visibility Audit Report Form is designed to collect information about the condition and visibility of shelves in stores. This form will help identify any issues and facilitate follow-up actions to improve the shopping experience for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the store where the audit was performed.
2. Enter the name and title of the person performing the audit.
3. Enter the store's ID number.
4. Attach a photo of the shelf as evidence (optional).
5. Enter any notes or comments about the audit.
6. Select the category of the shelf (e.g., Shelf, Floor, or Wall).
7. Choose the location of the shelf on the floor (e.g., Top, Middle, or Bottom).
8. Select the condition of the shelf (e.g., Good, Bad, or Unknown).
9. Enter the name and contact information of the store manager.
10. Enter any follow-up action or plan to rectify the issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the store where the audit was performed.
* **auditor_name** (text, required): Enter the name and title of the person performing the audit.
* **store_id** (number, required): Enter the ID number of the store.
* **photo_evidence** (note, optional): Attach a photo of the shelf as evidence, if desired.
* **notes** (text, required): Enter any comments or notes about the audit.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date when the audit was performed.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time when the audit was performed.
* **category** (select_one, required): Select the category of the shelf (e.g., Shelf, Floor, or Wall).
* **shelf_location** (select_multiple, required): Choose the location of the shelf on the floor (e.g., Top, Middle, or Bottom).
* **shelf_condition** (select_one, required): Select the condition of the shelf (e.g., Good, Bad, or Unknown).
* **store_manager** (text, required): Enter the name of the store manager.
* **store_manager_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the store manager.
* **store_manager_email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the store manager.
* **follow_up** (note, required): Enter any follow-up action or plan to rectify the issues.
