# Start Of Shift Vehicle Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the condition of a vehicle at the start of a shift, ensuring it's in good working order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Vehicle Id number, if required.
2. Fill in the Vehicle Make.
3. Enter the Vehicle Year.
4. Enter the current Odometer Reading.
5. Check the Engine Temperature (select High, Low, or Normal).
6. Check the Engine Oil Level (select Low, Medium, or High).
7. Check the condition of the following:
	* Oil Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Tire Pressure (enter a number)
	* Tire Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Brake Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Steering Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Suspension Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Frame Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Wheel Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Battery Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
	* Transmission Condition (select Good, Fair, or Bad)
8. Enter the condition of the Electrical Systems (text field).
9. Check the Air Fuel Ratio (enter a number).
10. Check the Cooling System (select Good, Fair, or Bad).
11. Check if the Brakes are worn (select Good, Fair, or Bad).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Id (1)**: Enter the Vehicle's Id number.
* **Vehicle Make (2)**: Enter the Vehicle's Make.
* **Vehicle Year (3)**: Enter the Vehicle's Year.
* **Odometer Reading (4)**: Enter the current Odometer Reading.
* **Engine Temperature (5)**: Check if the Engine Temperature is High, Low, or Normal.
* **Engine Oil Level (6)**: Check if the Engine Oil Level is Low, Medium, or High.
* **Oil Condition (7)**: Check if the Oil Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Tire Pressure (8)**: Enter the Tire Pressure.
* **Tire Condition (9)**: Check if the Tire Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Brake Condition (10)**: Check if the Brake Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Steering Condition (11)**: Check if the Steering Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Suspension Condition (12)**: Check if the Suspension Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Frame Condition (13)**: Check if the Frame Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Wheel Condition (14)**: Check if the Wheel Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Battery Condition (15)**: Check if the Battery Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Transmission Condition (16)**: Check if the Transmission Condition is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Electrical Systems (17)**: Enter a brief description of the Electrical Systems' condition.
* **Air Fuel Ratio (18)**: Enter the Air Fuel Ratio.
* **Cooling System (19)**: Check if the Cooling System is Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Brakes Worn (20)**: Check if the Brakes are worn (Good, Fair, or Bad).
