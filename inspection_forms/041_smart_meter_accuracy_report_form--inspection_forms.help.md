# Smart Meter Accuracy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Meter Accuracy Report Form is used to report on the accuracy of smart meters inspected in homes and businesses. The form helps to collect information about the inspection results, which can be used to identify potential issues with the meter's accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page and fill out the "Meter Inspection Details" field with a brief description of the inspection.
2. In the second page, select the "Date of Inspection" field and choose the date the inspection was performed.
3. In the third page, enter the "Test Results" field with the results of the inspection.
4. In the fourth page, use the "Supporting Evidence" field to attach any relevant evidence or documentation related to the inspection.
5. On the fifth page, enter the name of the person who performed the inspection in the "Inspected By" field.
6. On the same page, enter the name of the team that performed the inspection in the "Utility Team" field.
7. On the sixth page, enter the model of the meter being inspected in the "Meter Model" field.
8. On the same page, enter the serial number of the meter being inspected in the "Meter Serial Number" field.
9. On the seventh and final page, enter the location of the meter being inspected in the "Meter Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meter Inspection Details (Page 1)**: A brief description of the inspection performed on the smart meter.
* **Date of Inspection (Page 2)**: The date on which the inspection was performed.
* **Test Results (Page 3)**: The results of the inspection, including any issues found.
* **Supporting Evidence (Page 4)**: Any relevant evidence or documentation related to the inspection, such as photos or videos.
* **Inspected By (Page 5)**: The name of the person who performed the inspection.
* **Utility Team (Page 5)**: The name of the team that performed the inspection.
* **Meter Model (Page 6)**: The model of the meter being inspected.
* **Meter Serial Number (Page 6)**: The serial number of the meter being inspected.
* **Meter Location (Page 7)**: The location where the meter is installed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the "Supporting Evidence" field to attach any relevant evidence or documentation.
* Double-check the "Date of Inspection" field to ensure it is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.

Note: The form can be completed in multiple pages, but it is designed to be simple and straightforward, with each field having a clear explanation and a specific purpose.
