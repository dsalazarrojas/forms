# Trade Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Appraisal Form is used to gather information about a vehicle's details, condition, and value for accurate trade-in or purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Vehicle Details**: Enter the make, model, and year of the vehicle being appraised.
2. **Vehicle Condition**: Select the current condition of the vehicle from the available options (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
3. **Trade In Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for why you are trading in the vehicle.
4. **Vehicle Value**: Enter the current value of the vehicle, taking into consideration any relevant data or documentation you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the make, model, and year of the vehicle being appraised.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current condition of the vehicle from the available options (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
* **Trade In Reason** (`trade_in_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you are trading in the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Value** (`vehicle_value`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the current value of the vehicle, taking into consideration any relevant data or documentation you may have.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and relevant to the vehicle being appraised.
* Avoid duplicating data entries, as this can lead to confusion and errors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from your supervisor.
