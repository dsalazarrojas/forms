The Daily Retail Inspection Form is a form used for inspectors to document their observations and findings during a daily inspection of a retail store. The form is intended to be completed by store managers or inspectors to provide a snapshot of the store's status and inventory levels at a specific time. The form will help identify any issues or areas for improvement within the store and aid in making data-driven decisions. To ensure accuracy, please note that this form should not be completed if the store has not been inspected yet. 

# Daily Retail Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Retail Inspection Form is used to document and gather information about a retail store's status and inventory levels during a daily inspection. This form will aid in identifying issues and areas for improvement within the store.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the store name to identify the retail location being inspected.
2. Enter the store location to specify the exact location of the store.
3. Enter the store manager's name to identify the responsible person for the store.
4. Select the date of the inspection.
5. Select the time of the inspection.
6. Enter the current temperature inside and outside the store.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the store's condition.
8. Select the inventory level (1-10) to indicate the amount of stock available.
9. Choose the store status (Open, Closed, or Vacant) to indicate the store's current state.
10. Select the person assigned to complete the form.
11. Record the date and time the form was created.
12. Record the date and time the form was last updated.
13. Record the date and time the form was deleted (if applicable).
14. Enter additional comments or notes about the store.
15. Select the store status (Open, Closed, or Vacant) to confirm the store's current status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (Store Name, text, required): Enter the name of the store being inspected.
* **Store Location** (store_location, text, required): Enter the exact location of the store.
* **Store Manager** (store_manager, text, required): Enter the name of the store manager responsible for the store.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the time of the inspection.
* **Temperature** (Temperature, number, required): Enter the current indoor and outdoor temperature of the store.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the store.
* **Inventory** (Inventory, select_multiple, optional): Select the amount of stock available at the store (1-10).
* **Store Status** (Store Status, select_one, optional): Choose the store status (Open, Closed, or Vacant).
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to complete the form.
* **Created At** (Created At, date, not available for end-users): This field is for internal use.
* **Updated At** (Updated At, date, not available for end-users): This field is for internal use.
* **Deleted At** (Deleted At, date, not available for end-users): This field is for internal use.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Enter additional comments or notes about the store.
* **Store Status 2** (Store Status 2, select_one, optional): Choose the store status (Open, Closed, or Vacant) to confirm the store status.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If the store status changes, update the store status field accordingly.
* If you need more information or clarification, contact the store manager or assigned user.
