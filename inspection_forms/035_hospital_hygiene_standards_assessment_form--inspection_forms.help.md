# Hospital Hygiene Standards Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Hygiene Standards Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the hygiene standards in hospital departments during visits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each page of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide the required information for each field.
3. For fields with multiple options, choose the most appropriate answer that best describes the situation.
4. If a field is not applicable or requires a custom answer, use the "Comments" field to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification** (`1`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, or patient ID number to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Date Of Visit** (`2`, date, required): Record the date of the visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspector Info** (`3`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are the authorized inspector for this department; 'No' if not.
* **Patient Condition** (`4`, text, optional): Briefly describe the patient's condition at the time of the visit.
* **Hospital Department** (`5`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable hospital departments that were inspected during the visit.
* **Assessment Type** (`6`, select_one, required): Select the type of assessment (e.g., 'Yes', 'No') performed during the visit.
* **Facility Rating** (`7`, number, required): Rate the facility on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Patient Rating** (`8`, number, optional): Rate the patient's experience on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`9`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns about the patient's visit.
* **Follow-up Recommendations** (`10`, text, optional): Suggest any recommendations for improvement or future visits.
* **Facility Score** (`11`, number, optional): Calculate the facility's total score based on the facility rating.
* **Score Explanation** (`12`, text, optional): Explain the facility's score and any factors that contributed to it.
