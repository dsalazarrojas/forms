# First Aid Kit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you inspect and document the contents of a first aid kit, ensuring it is properly maintained and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the **first_aid_kit_inspected_by** field.
2. Select the date of inspection in the **first_aid_kit_inspected_date** field.
3. If the first aid kit is not up to date or missing any contents, list them in the **first_aid_kit_contents** field.
4. Describe any missing or non-usable items in the **first_aid_kit_contents_list** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Aid Kit Name** (`first_aid_kit_name`): Enter the name of the first aid kit being inspected.
* **First Aid Kit Location** (`first_aid_kit_location`): Enter the location where the first aid kit is found.
* **First Aid Kit Contents** (`first_aid_kit_contents`): Describe the contents of the first aid kit, including any missing or expired items.
* **First Aid Kit Contents List** (`first_aid_kit_contents_list`): List any missing or non-usable items found in the first aid kit.
* **Inspected By** (`first_aid_kit_inspected_by`): Enter your name and title to indicate who inspected the first aid kit.
* **Inspected Date** (`first_aid_kit_inspected_date`): Select the date of inspection.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`first_aid_kit_last_inspection`): Enter the date of the previous inspection of this first aid kit.

## Tips

* Be detailed and specific when describing the contents of the first aid kit, including any missing or expired items.
* Use this form to ensure that the first aid kit is properly maintained and up-to-date.
* Update the **first_aid_kit_last_inspection** field with the date of the previous inspection.
