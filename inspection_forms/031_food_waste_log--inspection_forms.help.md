# Food Waste Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Food Waste Log" form is designed to record and manage food waste incidents within facilities. It helps facility management track and analyze food waste events, ensuring compliance with health and safety regulations and maintaining cleanliness standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Food Waste Log" form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the food waste incident in the "Food Waste Log Date" field.
2. Fill in your name as the inspector in the "inspector_name" field.
3. Enter the facility's ID in the "facility_id" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the food waste incident in the "Food Waste Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Waste Log Date**: Enter the date of the food waste incident in this field. This date will help track the timing of the incident.
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector who reported the food waste incident. This ensures accountability and allows for easy tracking of who reported the incident.
* **facility_id**: Enter the facility's ID or unique identifier. This helps in linking the incident with the facility's record.
* **Food Waste Description**: Provide a detailed description of the food waste incident, including any relevant details about the circumstances surrounding it. This helps in identifying potential root causes and developing strategies for improvement.
