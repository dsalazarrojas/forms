# Manhole Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manhole Inspection Form is used to document and track the inspection of manholes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Manhole ID, which is a unique identifier for the manhole.
2. Provide the date of inspection, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the condition of the manhole from the dropdown list:
	* 1: Condition 1
	* 2: Condition 2
	* 3: Condition 3
	* 4: Condition 4
	* 5: Condition 5
4. Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
5. If required, upload a photo of the manhole.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manhole ID** (1, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the manhole.
* **Manhole Date Inspected** (2, date, required): Enter the date of inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Condition** (3, select_one, required): Select the condition of the manhole from the dropdown list.
* **Notes** (4, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Manhole Photo 1** (5, text, required): Upload a photo of the manhole.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the correct Manhole ID before completing the form.
* Select the correct condition from the dropdown list.
* Enter accurate and complete notes about the inspection.
* If a photo is required, ensure it is a clear and recent image of the manhole.
