# Flooded Riser Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a flooded riser inspection, including the location and date of the inspection, findings, and recommendations for action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
2. Enter the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the time of the inspection.
4. Record the temperature of the flooded riser.
5. Enter any findings related to the inspection.
6. Enter any repetitive findings.
7. Enter any recommendations for action.
8. Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
9. Select the responsible person for the inspection.
10. Select any additional responsible groups.
11. Select who inspected the flooded riser.
12. Select who verified the inspection.
13. Enter the date the inspection was verified.
14. Indicate if the flooded riser has been closed.
15. Indicate if the flooded riser has been reopened.
16. Enter the date the flooded riser was reopened.
17. Enter the date the flooded riser was closed.
18. Enter any review comments.
19. Enter the date the review comments were made.
20. Select the status of the flooded riser.
21. Enter the last date the flooded riser was inspected.
22. Enter the next date the flooded riser is to be inspected.
