# Public Health Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a facility's compliance with public health regulations, including its previous inspection history. It's a crucial tool for public health authorities to conduct thorough assessments and ensure facilities are up to standard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **facility name** and **date of inspection** at the top of the form.
2. Answer **Yes/No** questions about the facility's previous inspection history, including:
	* Whether the facility has had previous inspections (**facilities_previous_inspection**).
	* Whether critical items were identified during the previous inspection (**facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items**).
3. Enter any comments or scores related to critical items from previous inspections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name (facilty_name)**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address (facilty_address)**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Phone (facilty_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Email (facilty_email)**: Enter the email address of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Type (facilty_type)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether this facility has had previous inspections.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection (facilities_previous_inspection)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the facility has had previous inspections.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Date (facilities_previous_inspection_date)**: Enter the date of the facility's previous inspection if the facility has had one.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Score (facilities_previous_inspection_score)**: Enter the score from the facility's previous inspection, if applicable.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Comments (facilities_previous_inspection_comments)**: Enter any comments from the facility's previous inspection, if applicable.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each critical item identified during the previous inspection.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items Score (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items_score)**: Enter the score for each critical item identified during the previous inspection.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items Comments (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items_comments)**: Enter any comments related to critical items from the previous inspection.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items Scores (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items_scores)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each critical item identified during the previous inspection.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items Scores Score (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items_scores_score)**: Enter the score for each critical item identified during the previous inspection.
* **Facilities Previous Inspection Critical Items Scores Comments (facilities_previous_inspection_critical_items_scores_comments)**: Enter any comments related to critical items from the previous inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately.
* If the facility has had previous inspections, ensure you enter the correct scores and comments.
* If the facility has not had previous inspections, select 'No' for all previous inspection fields.
* Be thorough and complete in your assessment to ensure accurate scoring and comments.
