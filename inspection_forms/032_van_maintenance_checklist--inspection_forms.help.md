# Van Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Maintenance Checklist form is a tool to help track and record the maintenance schedule for a van, ensuring that regular services are performed at the right time to prevent costly repairs and ensure the vehicle's longevity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant information for your van, including the year, make, and model.
2. Enter the last time the van was maintained, including the date.
3. Record the current mileage to track progress.
4. Set intervals for regular services such as oil changes, tire changes, and component replacements (e.g., brake pads, spark plugs, and air filters).
5. Save the form to update and track your van's maintenance schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Van Year** (`van_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the van.
* **Van Make** (`van_make`, select_one, optional): Choose the make of the van from the available options.
* **Van Model** (`van_model`, select_one, optional): Choose the model of the van from the available options.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, number, optional): Enter the current mileage of the van.
* **Oil Change Interval** (`oil_change_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for oil changes.
* **Tire Change Interval** (`tire_change_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for tire changes.
* **Battery Service Interval** (`battery_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for battery services.
* **Brakes Service Interval** (`brakes_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for brake services.
* **Suspension Service Interval** (`suspension_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for suspension services.
* **Alignment Service Interval** (`alignment_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for alignment services.
* **Steering Service Interval** (`steering_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for steering services.
* **Belt Service Interval** (`belt_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for belt services.
* **Spark Plug Service Interval** (`spark_plug_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for spark plug services.
* **Air Filter Service Interval** (`air_filter_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for air filter services.
* **Spark Plug Service Interval** (`spark_plug_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for spark plug services.
* **Transmission Service Interval** (`transmission_service_interval`, number, optional): Set the recommended interval for transmission services.

## Tips
- Regular maintenance is key to preventing costly repairs and ensuring the longevity of your van.
- Review and update this form regularly to stay on track with your van's maintenance schedule.
- Consult your owner's manual or manufacturer's guidelines for specific recommendations on maintenance intervals.
- This form is for tracking purposes only; consult with a mechanic or maintenance professional for actual servicing and repairs.
