# Daily Safety Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for daily safety inspections to ensure that a site is safe for work. The form collects information about the general site conditions, equipment, and other relevant details to help maintain a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the General Information questions on the first page.
2.  On the second page, select the correct answers for the Supervisor Checklist.
3.  On the third page, select the conditions of the site (Good or Bad).
4.  On the fourth page, enter the date for Equipment Conditioning.
5.  On the fifth page, provide a note about Safety Equipment (if necessary).
6.  On the sixth page, select the status of First Aid (Available or Not Available).
7.  On the seventh page, enter the number of Electrical Equipment items in use.
8.  On the eighth page, enter the number of Fire Equipment items in use.
9.  On the ninth page, enter the number of Gas Equipment items in use.
10. On the tenth page, select the status of Other Equipment (Available or Not Available).
11. On the eleventh page, enter the total time spent on General Equipment Conditioning.
12. On the twelfth page, enter any additional information about Other Equipment Conditioning (if necessary).
13. On the thirteenth page, enter any additional general information about the site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please enter any general information about the site.
* **Supervisor Checklist**: Select whether the supervisor has checked the site (Yes or No).
* **Site Conditions**: Select whether the site conditions are Good or Bad.
* **Equipment Conditioning**: Enter the date for equipment conditioning.
* **Safety Equipment**: Please provide any notes about safety equipment (if necessary).
* **First Aid**: Select whether First Aid is available or not.
* **Electrical Equipment**: Enter the number of electrical equipment items in use.
* **Fire Equipment**: Enter the number of fire equipment items in use.
* **Gas Equipment**: Enter the number of gas equipment items in use.
* **Other Equipment**: Select whether other equipment is available or not.
* **General Equipment Conditioning**: Enter the total time spent on general equipment conditioning.
* **Other Equipment Conditioning**: Enter any additional information about other equipment conditioning (if necessary).
* **General Info**: Enter any additional general information about the site.
* **Site Inspection Status**: Please provide any additional information about the site inspection status (if necessary).
