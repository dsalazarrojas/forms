# Municipal Water Facility Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Water Facility Information
* **Water Facility Name** (`water_facility_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the water facility being inspected. This could be the name of the facility, such as a water tower or treatment plant.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of facility being inspected, such as a reservoir, treatment plant, or water tower.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the facility, including the street number and any other relevant details.
* **Latitude** (`latitude`, number, not required): This field is optional and will be used for geographic referencing. If you have a GPS device or know the facility's latitude, please enter it here.
* **Longitude** (`longitude`, number, not required): This field is optional and will be used for geographic referencing. If you have a GPS device or know the facility's longitude, please enter it here.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`, text, required): Enter your name and title or position as the person inspecting the facility.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Enter any notes or comments about the facility or inspection process.

### Page 2: Facility Contact Information
* **Facility Contact Person** (`facility_contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the facility or contact information.
* **Facility Manager Name** (`facility_manager_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the facility manager or contact person.
* **Facility Manager Contact Number** (`facility_manager_contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`facility_manager_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Address** (`facility_manager_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the facility manager.

### Page 3: Facility Details
* **Water Source Type** (`water_source_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of water source for the facility, such as a well, river, or lake.
* **Water Source Capacity** (`water_source_capacity`, number, not required): Enter the capacity of the water source in liters or gallons.
* **Water Source Pressure** (`water_source_pressure`, number, not required): Enter the water pressure in pounds per square inch (PSI).
* **Facility Operating Status** (`facility_operating_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the current operating status of the facility, such as online or offline.
* **Facility Operating Capacity** (`facility_operating_capacity`, number, not required): Enter the operating capacity of the facility in liters or gallons.
* **Facility Operating Pressure** (`facility_operating_pressure`, number, not required): Enter the operating pressure in pounds per square inch (PSI).
* **Water Treatment Type** (`water_treatment_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of water treatment used by the facility, such as filtration or distillation.
* **Facility Condition** (`facility_condition`, select_one, not required): Choose the overall condition of the facility, such as good, fair, or poor.
* **Date of Last Inspection** (`date_of_last_inspection`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the facility.
* **Facility Image 1** (`facility_image1`, text, not required): Upload or enter the URL of an image of the facility.
* **Facility Image 2** (`facility_image2`, text, not required): Upload or enter the URL of an image of the facility.

### Page 4: Additional Information
* **Facility Image 1** and **Facility Image 2** are optional and can be used to provide additional images or evidence of the facility's condition.

Please note that this is not an exhaustive list of all fields, but rather a guide to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently.
