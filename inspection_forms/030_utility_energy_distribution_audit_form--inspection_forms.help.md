# Utility Energy Distribution Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about utility energy distribution audits, including company details, inspection dates, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Company Name field with the name of the company or organization being audited.
2. Select the Inspection Date from the date picker or enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection in the Inspector field.
4. Select the appropriate Utility from the options provided.
5. Enter a number in the Inspection Frequency field to indicate how often the inspection is performed (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
6. Enter the date of the last inspection in the Last Inspection Date field or select from the date picker.
7. Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection in the Next Inspection Date field or select from the date picker.
8. Enter the Facility ID in the field provided.
9. Enter any notes or comments related to the inspection in the Inspection Notes field.
10. Select the type of facility being inspected from the options provided.
11. Select the types of utility being distributed from the options provided.
12. Select the status of the inspection from the options provided.
13. Enter the next action or follow-up required in the Next Action field.
14. Enter the date by which the follow-up action is due in the Follow-up Due Date field.
15. Select the status of the follow-up action from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization being audited.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date the inspection was performed or will be performed.
* **Inspector**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Utility**: Select the utility being distributed (e.g., electricity, gas, water).
* **Inspection Frequency**: Enter a number to indicate how often the inspection is performed (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection or select from the date picker.
* **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection or select from the date picker.
* **Facility ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Notes**: Enter any notes or comments related to the inspection.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility being inspected (e.g., office building, residential, industrial).
* **Utility Type**: Select the types of utility being distributed (e.g., electricity, gas, water).
* **Inspection Status**: Select the status of the inspection (e.g., completed, pending, cancelled).
* **Next Action**: Enter the next action or follow-up required for the facility.
* **Follow-up Due Date**: Enter the date by which the follow-up action is due.
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up action (e.g., completed, pending, cancelled).

Note: The fields marked as "required" should be completed to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
