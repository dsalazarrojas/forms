# Post-session survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the post-session survey form to help you provide valuable feedback about your experience with the seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and select your responses. You can skip questions that are not applicable to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think of the seminar? (overall_opinion)**: Please share your overall impression of the seminar, including any positive or negative thoughts you have about it.
* **Most interesting part of the seminar (most_interesting)**: Choose the part that resonated with you the most, or that was most engaging or useful.
* **How likely are you to recommend this seminar? (recommend)**: Rate your likelihood of recommending this seminar to others based on your experience.
* **What did you not like about the seminar? (dislike)**: Share any specific aspects of the seminar that you found disappointing or frustrating.
* **Do you plan on taking another seminar from this speaker? (next_seminar)**: Indicate whether you plan to attend another seminar from the same speaker or presenter.
* **How long should the seminar be? (session_length)**: Suggest the ideal length of time for the seminar, taking into account your learning pace and engagement.
* **What were the most memorable moments from this seminar? (memorable_moments)**: Describe the most significant or impactful experiences you had during the seminar.
* **Do you have any comments or feedback about this seminar? (comments)**: Share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about the seminar.
* **How easy was the registration process? (registration_process)**: Rate the ease of registration process for the seminar.
* **How clear was the communication from the speaker or organizers? (communication)**: Rate the level of clarity in communication from the seminar organizers or presenter.
* **How engaging was the speaker? (speaker_engagement)**: Evaluate the presenter's ability to engage and keep you interested in the seminar content.
* **How well did the seminar meet your expectations? (expectations_met)**: Rate how closely the seminar met your expectations, based on what you were hoping for.

Tips:

* Please answer each question based on your genuine experience and opinions.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please feel free to share them in the comments section.
