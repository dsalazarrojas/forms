# Veterinary Inspections Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document information about animal inspections. It helps veterinarians, animal owners, and other veterinary staff to track and report on animal inspections efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal Name** (`animal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the animal being inspected.
* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of animal being inspected.
* **Owner Contact Info** (`owner_contact_info`, text, required): Provide the owner's contact information, including name and contact number.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the veterinarian who performed the inspection.
* **Inspection Location** (`inspection_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Optional field for adding any additional notes about the inspection. (Note: This field is for extra comments and details that don't fit in other fields.)
* **Examination Results** (`examination_results`, text, optional): Optional field for recording the results of any examination or testing performed during the inspection. 

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and thoroughly.
- Review and edit the form before submission.
- Keep the form for future reference or use it to track inspection data.
