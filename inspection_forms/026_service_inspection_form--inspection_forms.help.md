# Service Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Inspection Form is used to record and document service inspection information. This form captures details about the service inspection date, service provider, and inspection condition, as well as customer satisfaction and any comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Service Inspection Date and Service Inspection Time fields with the details of the inspection.
2. Enter the Service Provider Name, which should include their title or company name.
3. Choose the Inspection Condition to reflect the quality of the service provided.
4. Select multiple options for Customer Satisfaction to indicate the level of customer satisfaction.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Service Inspection Note field.
6. Enter the Service Inspection Email and Service Inspection Phone fields with relevant contact information.
7. Select who the form was submitted by from the list of available options.
8. Add any additional comments in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Inspection Form**:
	+ This is the overall title of the form.
* **Service Inspection Date**:
	+ This field is for the date of the service inspection. Please enter the date in a standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Service Inspection Time**:
	+ This field is for the time of the service inspection. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:30 for 1:30 PM).
* **Service Provider Name**:
	+ This field is for the name of the service provider, which may include their title or company name.
* **Service Inspection Condition**:
	+ Choose this field to reflect the quality of the service provided. Options include Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Customer Satisfaction**:
	+ Select one or more of the following options to indicate the customer's level of satisfaction: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Service Inspection Note**:
	+ This is an optional field for adding any additional notes or comments about the service inspection.
* **Service Inspection Email**:
	+ This field is for the email address of the service provider or contact person.
* **Service Inspection Phone**:
	+ This field is for the phone number of the service provider or contact person.
* **Submitted By**:
	+ This field is for indicating who submitted the form. Please select from the list of available options.
* **Comments**:
	+ This field is for adding any additional comments or feedback about the service inspection.
* **Service Provider Name (again)**:
	+ Please note that this field is not a duplicate of the Service Provider Name field, but rather a redundant field that was included in the original YAML.
