# Dormitory Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dormitory Safety Inspection Form is designed to help dormitory managers, administrators, or safety inspectors assess the safety and security of the dormitory environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. For select fields, choose the most accurate response from the given options.
3. If a field does not apply to your situation, select "Not Available".
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1. General Information`, `text`, required: false`)
	+ This section is for any additional information you would like to add or provide context to your inspection.
* **Safety Equipment** (`2. Safety Equipment`, `text`, required: false`)
	+ List any safety equipment available or present in the dormitory.
* **Electrical Outlets** (`3. Electrical Outlets`, `select_multiple`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate if the electrical outlets are:
		- Yes (available and functional)
		- No (unavailable or non-functional)
		- Not Available (not present)
* **Fire Safety** (`4. Fire Safety`, `select_one`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate the current fire safety condition:
		- Yes (safety measures in place)
		- No (safety measures not in place)
		- Not Available (not applicable or no measures present)
* **Emergency Exit Signs** (`5. Emergency Exit Signs`, `select_multiple`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate if emergency exit signs are:
		- Yes (present and clear)
		- No (absent or unclear)
		- Not Available (not applicable)
* **First Aid Kits** (`6. First Aid Kits`, `select_one`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate if first aid kits are:
		- Yes (available and stocked)
		- No (unavailable or not stocked)
		- Not Available (not present)
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`7. Fire Extinguishers`, `select_one`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate if fire extinguishers are:
		- Working (functional and easily accessible)
		- Not Working (non-functional)
		- Not Available (not present)
* **Emergency Phone Numbers** (`8. Emergency Phone Numbers`, `select_multiple`, required: false`)
	+ Indicate if emergency phone numbers are:
		- Available (clearly posted and accessible)
		- Not Available (not posted or not accessible)

Note: If you have any questions or concerns about the form or its purpose, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
