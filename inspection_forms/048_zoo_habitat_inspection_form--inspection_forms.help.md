# Zoo Habitat Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the inspection of a zoo habitat, including its status, condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the habitat being inspected (e.g., "Inspection Forms").
2. Enter the description of the habitat (e.g., the purpose of the inspection, any notable events or conditions observed during the inspection).
3. Enter your name and contact information as the inspector.
4. Record the date and time of the inspection.
5. Record any inspector notes or comments.
6. Select the habitat status (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
7. Record the facility's name, address, phone number, and email.
8. Record any facility notes or comments.
9. Select the habitat area where the inspection was conducted.
10. Take a photograph of the inspector (not required, but recommended).
11. Take a photograph of the facility (not required, but recommended).
12. Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Zoo Habitat Inspection Form**: Enter the title of the form, which is already pre-filled for your convenience.
* **Category**: Select the category of the habitat being inspected (e.g., "Inspection Forms").
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the habitat, including its purpose and any notable events or conditions observed during the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Inspector Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the facility's ID number (not applicable to all users).
* **Habitat Checked**: Enter any notes or comments about the habitat's condition (not applicable to all users).
* **Date Completed**: Record the date and time of the inspection.
* **Time Completed**: Record the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspector Phone**: Enter your phone number (not required, but recommended).
* **Inspector Email**: Enter your email address (not required, but recommended).
* **Facility Email**: Enter the facility's email address (not required, but recommended).
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the facility's phone number (not required, but recommended).
* **Facility Address**: Enter the facility's address.
* **Inspector Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Facility Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the facility.
* **Inspector Signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector.
* **Facility Signature**: Enter the facility's signature (not required, but recommended).
* **Facility Name**: Enter the facility's name.
* **Habitat Status**: Select the status of the habitat (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Habitat Area**: Record the area where the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Photo**: Take a photograph of yourself as the inspector (not required, but recommended).
* **Facility Photo**: Take a photograph of the facility (not required, but recommended).
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form, or "Cancel" to cancel the form and start over.
