# Framing Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Framing Inspection Checklist is a tool used to assess the condition and quality of a building's framing during an inspection. It helps ensure that the building's structural integrity is maintained and any necessary repairs or improvements are identified.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options from the "Framing Inspection Checklist" page to note any issues or defects found during the inspection.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Framing Inspection Date" field.
3. Enter the name and title of the inspector conducting the inspection in the "Framing Inspection Inspector" field.
4. Enter the address of the building being inspected in the "Framing Inspection Address" field.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the "Framing Inspection Notes" field.
6. Select any issues or problems identified during the inspection from the "Framing Inspection Issues" page.
7. Enter any recommendations for future repairs or improvements in the "Framing Inspection Recommendations" field.
8. Finally, enter your signature as the inspector in the "Framing Inspection Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Framing Inspection Checklist** (`framing_inspection_checklist`, select_multiple): 
	+ Select all relevant options that apply to the inspection, such as issues with the building's framing, defects, or necessary improvements.
* **Framing Inspection Date** (`framing_inspection_date`, date): 
	+ Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Framing Inspection Inspector** (`framing_inspection_inspector`, text): 
	+ Enter the name and title of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Framing Inspection Address** (`framing_inspection_address`, text): 
	+ Enter the address of the building being inspected.
* **Framing Inspection Notes** (`framing_inspection_notes`, note): 
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Framing Inspection Issues** (`framing_inspection_issues`, select_one): 
	+ Select the issue that best describes the problem found during the inspection.
* **Framing Inspection Recommendations** (`framing_inspection_recommendations`, text): 
	+ Enter any recommendations for future repairs or improvements.
* **Framing Inspection Signature** (`framing_inspection_signature`, text): 
	+ Enter your signature as the inspector conducting the inspection.
