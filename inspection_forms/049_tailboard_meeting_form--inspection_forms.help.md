# tailboard_meeting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tailboard_meeting_form is used to capture information about a meeting, including location, participants, any issues that arose, and details about who it was assigned to.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the location of the meeting in the "location" field.
2. Indicate whether anyone was present at the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "people_present" field.
3. Describe any issues that arose during the meeting in the "issue" field.
4. Enter the date of the meeting in the "date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the time of the meeting in the "time" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about the meeting in the "notes" field (optional).
7. Indicate whether the meeting was assigned to someone by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "assigned_to" field.
8. Enter the name of the person to whom the meeting was assigned in the "assigned_user" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location** (1, text, required): Enter the location where the meeting took place, such as a conference room or office.
* **People Present** (2, select_multiple, required): This field is used to indicate whether anyone was present at the meeting. Select "Yes" if you were present, and "No" otherwise.
* **Issue** (3, text, required): Describe any issues that arose during the meeting, such as problems or concerns that need to be addressed.
* **Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (5, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Notes** (6, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the meeting, such as follow-up actions or reminders.
* **Assigned To** (7, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate whether the meeting was assigned to someone. Select "Yes" if it was assigned, and "No" otherwise.
* **Assigned User** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the person to whom the meeting was assigned.
