# haccp_cleaning_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The haccp_cleaning_submission_form is used to collect and document information related to the HACCP cleaning process, ensuring a systematic and controlled approach to prevent or reduce risks to food safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review the form purpose, ensure it's not being duplicated, and follow the steps outlined in the internal reasoning block above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This field is used as a placeholder and does not require any input.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_schedule** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the schedule for the HACCP cleaning process.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_verification** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" if the cleaning has been properly verified, or "No" if it has not.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_responsible_person** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the person responsible for the cleaning schedule from the options provided.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_verification_date** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the cleaning verification.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_time** (`6`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the cleaning verification.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_note** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the cleaning process.
* **haccp_cleaning_submission_form_uploads** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Attach any relevant files or images for further reference.
