# Park Supervision Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Park Supervision Checklist is a form designed to help park authorities conduct a thorough inspection of park conditions. This form helps monitor and address potential hazards, take photos or notes for record-keeping, track staff presence and inspection dates, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select any hazards found during the inspection.
2. Provide any photos or notes taken during the inspection.
3. Indicate if staff members are present during the inspection.
4. Select the date and time of the inspection.
5. Select the staff member performing the inspection.
6. Select the staff member in charge during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Are there any hazards** (Are there any hazards) (required/optional): Check any hazards found during the inspection.
* **Photos** (Photos) (optional): Attach any photos taken during the inspection to provide visual evidence.
* **Notes** (Notes) (optional): Add any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Staff Present** (Staff Present) (optional): Indicate if staff members are present during the inspection.
* **Date** (Date) (optional): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Time** (Time) (optional): Select the time of the inspection.
* **Staff Member** (Staff Member) (optional): Select the staff member performing the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date) (optional): Select the date of the inspection (Note: This field is redundant and can be ignored).
* **Staff** (Staff) (optional): Select the staff member in charge during the inspection.
* **Date/Time** (Date/Time) (optional): This field is a duplicate of the Date and Time fields and can be ignored.

## Tips
* Ensure that you select the correct date and time of the inspection.
* Attach any photos or notes taken during the inspection.
* Be accurate when indicating staff presence and staff members performing the inspection.
