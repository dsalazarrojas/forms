# Agricultural Spray Records Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about agricultural spray applications, ensuring that all necessary safety measures and protocols are followed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following:
	* Operator name: Enter the name of the person who applied the spray.
	* Date of application: Enter the date when the spraying occurred.
	* Product used: Enter the trade or chemical name of the product used.
	* Application rate per hectare: Enter the amount of product used per hectare or acre.
	* Safety measures taken: Describe the safety measures taken, including any personal protective equipment (PPE) used and buffer zones implemented.
	* Nearby sensitive areas: Mention any nearby water bodies, schools, or residences that may be affected by the spray.
	* Record keeper signature: Enter your name as the person recording the details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator name** (`operator_name`, required): Enter the name of the person who applied the spray. This information helps identify the person responsible for the application.
* **Date of application** (`date_of_application`, required): Enter the date when the spraying occurred. This helps track the timing of the application and ensures that records are kept up-to-date.
* **Product used** (`product_used`, required): Enter the trade or chemical name of the product used. This ensures accurate tracking and inventorying of chemicals used.
* **Application rate per hectare** (`application_rate`, required): Enter the amount of product used per hectare or acre. This helps in calculating the correct dosage and application amount.
* **Active ingredient percent** (`active_ingredient_percent`, optional): If known, enter the concentration of the active ingredient in the product used. This information can be useful for future reference.
* **Weather conditions** (`weather_conditions`, optional): Describe the wind, temperature, and rainfall conditions at the time of application. This helps in understanding the environmental conditions under which the spray was applied.
* **Equipment used** (`equipment_used`, optional): Enter the make and model of the sprayer used. This helps in tracking the equipment used and its maintenance history.
* **Safety measures taken** (`safety_measures_taken`, required): Describe the PPE used and any buffer zones implemented to minimize risk to people and the environment. This is crucial for ensuring the safety of people and the environment.
* **Nearby sensitive areas** (`nearby_sensitive_areas`, optional): Mention any nearby water bodies, schools, or residences that may be affected by the spray. This helps in identifying potential risks and taking necessary precautions.
* **Record keeper signature** (`record_keeper_signature`, required): Enter your name as the person recording the details. This ensures accountability and accuracy in recording the data.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* Store the form securely to maintain confidentiality and prevent tampering.
