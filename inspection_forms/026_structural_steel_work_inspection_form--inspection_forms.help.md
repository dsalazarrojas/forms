# Structural Steel Work Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Structural Steel Work Inspection Form is a tool used to gather information and feedback from construction workers during structural steel work inspections on-site. This form helps ensure that work is completed correctly and efficiently by providing a structured way to document observations, notes, and any modifications made during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the General Information section with your name, ID, and the date of the inspection.
2.  In the Modifications section, select any modifications made during the inspection.
3.  In the Structural Steel Work section, provide a detailed description of the structural steel work completed during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is the section where you provide basic contact information and details about the inspection, such as your name, ID, and date. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Modifications**: This section allows you to select any modifications made during the inspection. Please review the options carefully and select all that apply. You can select multiple modifications as needed.
* **Structural Steel Work**: In this section, please provide a detailed description of the structural steel work completed during the inspection. Be sure to include any notes or observations relevant to the inspection.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the inspection process is completed efficiently.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
*   If you are unsure about any section or question, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
