# Field Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Inspection Form is a tool to record and document the inspection of fields in the field inspection area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required fields completed.
2. Fill out each page as follows:
	* Page 1: General Inspection (Note page)
		+ This page allows you to provide any general observations or comments regarding the field's condition.
	* Page 2: Observed Conditions (Select)
		+ Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you observed any conditions that require attention.
* Page 3: Field Type (Select)
		+ Choose the type of field from the options provided.
* Page 4: Field Status (Select)
		+ Choose the status of the field from the options provided.
* Page 5: Observed Damage (Select)
		+ Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you observed any damage to the field.
* Page 6: Field Location (Text)
		+ Enter the location of the field.
* Page 7: Field Condition (Text)
		+ Enter any specific conditions observed on the field.
* Page 8: Field Description (Select)
		+ Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the field has a description.
* Page 9: Field Color (Number)
		+ Enter the color of the field.
* Page 10: Date Inspected (Date)
		+ Enter the date the field was inspected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Inspection (Note)**: Provide any general observations or comments regarding the field's condition.

* **Field Status (Select)**: Choose the status of the field from the options provided.
* **Observed Damage (Select)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you observed any damage to the field.
* **Field Location (Text)**: Enter the location of the field.

* **Field Description (Select)**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the field has a description.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out each page completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the responsible personnel for assistance.
- The form requires completion for field data accuracy and efficiency.
