# Gym Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a thorough inspection of the gym's cleanliness, safety, and general state of repair. It helps to identify areas that need attention and ensures that the gym meets the highest standards of cleanliness, safety, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you have considered all areas of the gym.
2. For each section, select the answer that best reflects the state of the gym.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a red star symbol).
4. Use the provided hints to help guide your answers.
5. Complete the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the results are helpful to management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Cleaning
- **General Cleaning** (`General Cleaning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the overall state of the gym, including floors, walls, windows, and doors. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if everything is clean and well-maintained
  - `No` if any areas need attention

### Bathroom Cleaning
- **Bathroom Cleaning** (`Bathroom Cleaning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Inspect the bathroom area, including toilets, sinks, showers, and other fixtures. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if everything is clean and well-maintained
  - `No` if any areas need attention

### Locker Room Cleaning
- **Locker Room Cleaning** (`Locker Room Cleaning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the state of the locker room, including floors, walls, and facilities. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if everything is clean and well-maintained
  - `No` if any areas need attention

### Equipment Cleaning
- **Equipment Cleaning** (`Equipment Cleaning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Inspect exercise equipment, including machines, weights, and other machinery. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if everything is clean and well-maintained
  - `No` if any areas need attention

### Safety Procedures
- **Safety Procedures** (`Safety Procedures`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Review safety procedures and check for any hazards or risks. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if everything is in order and safe
  - `No` if any areas need attention

### First Aid Kit
- **First Aid Kit** (`First Aid Kit`, `note`, required/optional): Check the first aid kit for its condition, completeness, and availability. Answer: 
  - Write the date, condition, and any other notes about the first aid kit

### Emergency Contact
- **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `select_one`, required/optional): Identify the emergency contact person in case of an emergency. Answer: 
  - `Yes` if an emergency contact has been identified and notified
  - `No` if no emergency contact has been identified

### Review
- **Review** (`Review`, `note`, required/optional): Summarize your findings and observations from the inspection. Answer: 
  - Write a brief summary of your observations and any recommendations for improvement.
