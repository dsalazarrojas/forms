# emergency_lighting_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency lighting inspection form is designed to document the status of emergency lighting systems within a building or facility. This form helps identify any potential defects or deficiencies in the systems and ensures that they are functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on the actual status of the emergency lighting systems.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that the inspection has been conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_lighting_inspection_form** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form`, select_one, required): Select the overall status of the emergency lighting system (Yes/No).
* **emergency_lighting_type** (`emergency_lighting_type`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the type of emergency lighting system(s) installed (e.g., LED, fluorescent, incandescent).
* **installed_lighting_systems** (`installed_lighting_systems`, select_multiple, optional): List the emergency lighting systems currently installed (e.g., exit signs, stairwell lighting, etc.).
* **emergency_lighting_systems** (`emergency_lighting_systems`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the emergency lighting system is functional (Yes/No).
* **installed_lighting_systems_1** (`installed_lighting_systems_1`, select_multiple, optional): List additional emergency lighting systems installed (e.g., backup power systems).
* **emergency_exit_paths** (`emergency_exit_paths`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of emergency exit paths (Yes/No).
* **emergency_exit_paths_1** (`emergency_exit_paths_1`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of additional emergency exit paths.
* **emergency_exit_paths_2** (`emergency_exit_paths_2`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of further emergency exit paths.
* **emergency_exit_paths_3** (`emergency_exit_paths_3`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of more emergency exit paths.
* **emergency_exit_paths_4** (`emergency_exit_paths_4`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of even more emergency exit paths.
* **exit_signs** (`exit_signs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of exit signs (Yes/No).
* **exit_signs_1** (`exit_signs_1`, select_multiple, optional): List additional exit signs.
* **exit_signs_2** (`exit_signs_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of further exit signs.
* **emergency_exit_paths_5** (`emergency_exit_paths_5`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of even more emergency exit paths.
* **electrical_systems** (`electrical_systems`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of electrical systems powering the emergency lighting (Yes/No).
* **electrical_systems_1** (`electrical_systems_1`, select_multiple, optional): List additional electrical systems powering the emergency lighting.
* **electrical_systems_2** (`electrical_systems_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of further electrical systems powering the emergency lighting.
* **emergency_lighting_inspection_form_1** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_1`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of this inspection form.
* **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Date of inspection (not required, but recommended).
* **inspector_comments** (`inspector_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the inspector.
* **inspector_signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm that the inspection has been conducted.
* **emergency_inspection_form_version** (`emergency_inspection_form_version`, text, optional): Version of the inspection form used.
* **form_completed_by** (`form_completed_by`, text, optional): Name and title of the person completing the form.
* **emergency_inspection_status** (`emergency_inspection_status`, text, optional): Status of the emergency lighting inspection (e.g., passed, failed, pending).
