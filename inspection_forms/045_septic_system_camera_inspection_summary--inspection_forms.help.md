## septic_system_camera_inspection_summary - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document the inspection summary of a septic system's camera inspection, which is a crucial component of regular septic system maintenance. The inspection is used to evaluate the condition and status of the septic system, including its capacity, camera inspection date, and system status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the real estate transaction information in the "real_estate_transaction" field.
2.  Enter the property address in the "property_address" field (if applicable).
3.  Enter the inspector's name in the "inspector_name" field.
4.  Select the type of septic system from the "system_type" options.
5.  Enter the system capacity in the "system_capacity" field.
6.  Enter the date of the camera inspection in the "camera_inspection" field.
7.  Select the inspector's license from the "inspector_license" options (if applicable).
8.  Enter the septic system ID in the "septic_system_id" field.
9.  Enter any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field (if applicable).
10.  Upload up to 10 images of the septic system (camera_make, camera_model, camera_date, camera_notes, system_image1, system_image2, system_image3, system_image4, system_image5, system_image6, system_image7, system_image8, system_image9, system_image10).
