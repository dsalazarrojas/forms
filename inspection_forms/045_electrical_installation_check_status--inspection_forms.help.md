# Electrical Installation Check Status - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the status of electrical installations during an inspection. It helps to ensure that electrical systems, connections, and components are safe and functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the electrical systems checked during the inspection.
2. Select the electrical systems that were inspected (e.g. HVAC, electrical, plumbing).
3. Check the condition of each electrical system (e.g. good, fair, poor).
4. Assess the risk level of each electrical system (e.g. low, medium, high).
5. Check the condition of all electrical connections.
6. List all electrical components (e.g. outlets, switches, etc.).
7. Record the date and time of the electrical inspection.
8. Add any additional notes from the inspection.
9. Report the result of the electrical inspection (e.g. passed, failed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical Systems Checked**: Select all electrical systems checked during the inspection.
	+ This field allows you to document the electrical systems that were inspected.
* **Electrical Systems Condition**: Check the condition of each electrical system (e.g. good, fair, poor).
	+ This field assesses the condition of each electrical system.
* **Electrical Systems Risk**: Assess the risk level of each electrical system (e.g. low, medium, high).
	+ This field estimates the risk level of each electrical system.
* **Electrical Connections**: Check the condition of all electrical connections.
	+ This field evaluates the condition of all electrical connections.
* **Electrical Components**: List all electrical components (e.g. outlets, switches, etc.).
	+ This field is for listing all electrical components.
* **Electrical Systems Inspected**: Select the systems that were inspected (e.g. HVAC, electrical, plumbing).
	+ This field selects the systems inspected.
* **Electrical Inspection Date**: Record the date of the electrical inspection.
	+ This field records the date of the inspection.
* **Electrical Inspection Time**: Record the time of the electrical inspection.
	+ This field records the time of the inspection.
* **Electrical Inspection Notes**: Add any additional notes from the inspection.
	+ This field is for adding notes from the inspection.
* **Electrical Inspection Result**: Report the result of the electrical inspection (e.g. passed, failed).
	+ This field reports the outcome of the inspection.
