# Factory Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for factory inspectors to document the condition of equipment and any findings during an inspection. It can help improve maintenance and safety procedures in the factory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and make sure you have all necessary information before starting.
* For each section, read the label carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information, text, required: false)** 
This field is for any additional general information that the inspector wants to include in the report. You can provide any relevant details about the inspection, such as the date and time of the inspection, the inspector's name, or any other important information.

* **Equipment Condition (Equipment Condition, select_multiple, required: false)** 
This field asks about the condition of the equipment. You are to select all that apply from the following options:

	+ Crisp and clean
	+ Dull
	+ Dirty
	+ Worn
	+ Rusty

* **Inspection Notes (Inspection Notes, text, required: false)** 
This field is for any additional notes or comments about the inspection. You can provide any relevant details or observations that may be useful for future reference.

* **Findings (Findings, text, required: false)** 
This field is for any notable findings or observations made during the inspection. You can provide any important information that may be useful for future maintenance or repairs.

# Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions as accurately as possible.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when providing notes or findings.
