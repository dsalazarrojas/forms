# Wall Surface Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wall Surface Inspection Checklist is a form used for conducting inspections on the surface of walls in properties.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the property ID to identify the property being inspected.
2.  Enter the inspection date to record the date of the inspection.
3.  Select the wall surface type from the list of options provided.
4.  Select the wall material from the list of options provided.
5.  Choose the overall condition of the wall from the list of options provided.
6.  Enter any additional comments or observations in the notes field.
7.  Enter the last name and first name of the inspector, followed by their phone number and email address.
8.  Enter the last name and first name of the property manager, followed by their phone number and email address.
9.  Enter the follow-up date for any follow-up inspections.
10.  Enter any additional comments or observations from the follow-up inspection in the follow-up notes field.
11.  Check the "Completed" field to indicate that the form has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, required): Enter a numerical value to identify the property being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY/MM/DD.
* **Wall Surface Type** (`wall_surface_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of wall surface being inspected (Concrete, Drywall, Painted, Wood).
* **Wall Material** (`wall_material`, select_multiple, optional): Select the materials used for the wall surface (Concrete Block, Drywall, Painted, Plaster, Wood).
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, required): Choose the overall condition of the wall (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspector.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name and first name of the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the inspector.
* **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name and first name of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Phone** (`property_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Email** (`property_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the property manager.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date for any follow-up inspections.
* **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the follow-up inspection.
* **Completed** (`completed`, select_one, required): Check to indicate that the form has been completed.
