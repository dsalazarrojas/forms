The store display compliance audit form is designed to help store owners and managers evaluate the compliance of their store's visual merchandising standards. The form is used to assess whether the store's merchandise display meets the required standards, including product placement, pricing, shelf facing, and category correct placement. The form also helps to identify non-compliant items and provides a space for store owners to add notes and photos to support their audit findings.

This form is intended for use by store owners, managers, and other authorized personnel to inspect and audit store displays. It is not intended for customers or other non-authority personnel.

The form is divided into several sections, each designed to capture specific information relevant to the compliance audit. The form includes fields for selecting the correct merchandising standards, identifying non-compliant items, and adding notes and photos to support findings.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you have conducted the audit in person and have taken note of the store's current merchandising standards and non-compliant items.

# store_display_compliance_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store display compliance audit form is used to evaluate the compliance of a store's visual merchandising standards.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct merchandising standards for the store by choosing the options for "merchandising_standards", "merchandising_standards_1", and "product_availability".
2. Identify non-compliant items by selecting the options for "non_compliant_items".
3. Add photos and notes to support findings by typing in the text fields for "photo_1", "photo_2", "photo_3", and "notes".
4. Enter the store's name and location in the text fields for "store_name" and "store_location".
5. Enter the email and phone contact information in the text fields for "email" and "phone".
6. Record the date and time of the inspection in the "date_of_inspection" and "time_of_inspection" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store display compliance audit**: Select "True" if the store's merchandising standards are correct, and "False" otherwise.
* **non compliant items**: Select the items that are not meeting the merchandising standards.
* **merchandising standards**: Select the correct merchandising standards for the store.
* **merchandising standards 1**: Enter a number if you need to specify the first merchandising standard.
* **product availability**: Select "In Stock", "Low Stock", or "Out of Stock" to indicate the product availability.
* **store name**: Enter the store name.
* **store location**: Enter the store location.
* **photo 1**: Add a photo 1 to support your findings.
* **photo 2**: Add a photo 2 to support your findings.
* **photo 3**: Add a photo 3 to support your findings.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **email**: Enter the store's contact email.
* **phone**: Enter the store's contact phone number.
* **date of inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **time of inspection**: Enter the time of the inspection.
