# Bed Bug Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bed Bug Disclosure Form is a document that provides information about the presence, past history, and current status of bed bugs in a rental property. It is designed to be completed by landlords and property managers to inform tenants about potential bed bug issues and to ensure that all parties are aware of their responsibilities regarding bed bug infestations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill in the required fields:
	*   Property Address: Enter the address of the rental property.
	*   Unit Number: Enter the number of the unit you are disclosing information about.
3.  Answer the questions in the Infestation History section:
	*   To the best of your knowledge, has the unit had bed bug activity in the past year?
		+   Select "True" if the unit has had bed bugs in the past year.
		+   Select "False" if the unit has not had bed bugs in the past year.
4.  If the answer to the above question is "True," provide information about the date of the last known infestation or treatment.
5.  Answer the questions in the Recent Inspection section:
	*   Date of most recent bed bug inspection:
		+   Enter the date of the most recent inspection.
	*   Results of most recent inspection:
		+   Select "No Evidence of Bed Bugs" if there is no evidence of bed bugs.
		+   Select "Evidence Found - Remediation Required" if bed bugs were found but remediation is still needed.
		+   Select "Evidence Found - Remediation Completed" if bed bugs were found but remediation has already been completed.
6.  Enter the name of the inspector or company that conducted the inspection.
7.  In the Disclosure Acknowledgment section:
	*   Landlord - I certify the accuracy of this disclosure:
		+   Select "I Certify" to indicate that the information is accurate.
	*   Tenant - I acknowledge receipt of this bed bug history disclosure:
		+   Select "I Acknowledge" to indicate that the tenant has received and understands the disclosure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address (Property Address, text, required)**: Enter the address of the rental property.
*   **Unit Number (Unit Number, text, required)**: Enter the number of the unit you are disclosing information about.
*   **To the best of your knowledge, has the unit had bed bug activity in the past year? (history_check, select_one, required)**: Answer "True" if the unit has had bed bugs in the past year, "False" if it has not.
*   **Date of last known infestation or treatment (last_infestation_date, date, optional)**: Leave blank if none.
*   **If yes, was the unit professionally treated and cleared? (treatment_confirmation, select_one, optional)**: Select "Yes, by a licensed professional," "Treated but not professionally cleared," "False," or "N/A."
*   **Date of most recent bed bug inspection (recent_inspection_date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the most recent inspection.
*   **Results of most recent inspection (inspection_results, select_one, required)**: Select "No Evidence of Bed Bugs," "Evidence Found - Remediation Required," or "Evidence Found - Remediation Completed."
*   **Name of Inspector or Company (inspector_name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the inspector or company that conducted the inspection.
*   **Landlord - I certify the accuracy of this disclosure (landlord_disclosure_statement, select_one, required)**: Select "I Certify" to indicate that the information is accurate.
*   **Tenant - I acknowledge receipt of this bed bug history disclosure (tenant_acknowledgment_statement, select_one, required)**: Select "I Acknowledge" to indicate that the tenant has received and understands the disclosure.
*   **Date of Disclosure (disclosure_date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the disclosure.
*   **Witness Name (if applicable) (witness_name, text, optional)**: If applicable, enter the name of the witness.

Tips:

*   Be honest and accurate when filling in the form.
*   Make sure to provide correct dates and information.
*   If you are a tenant, make sure to read and understand the contents of the form carefully before signing it.
*   If you are a landlord, make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the property.
