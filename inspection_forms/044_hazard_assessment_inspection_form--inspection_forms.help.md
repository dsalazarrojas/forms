# hazard_assessment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hazard assessment inspection form is used to identify and document potential hazards in the workplace and to track the actions taken to mitigate or control these hazards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the workplace hazards listed on page 1 and select all the hazards that you have observed or identified during the inspection.
2. Complete the "Inspection Date" field with the date of the inspection.
3. Select the severity of each hazard in the "Hazard Risks" section.
4. Describe the control measures and corrective actions taken in the "Control Measures" section.
5. Document any additional comments or observations in the "Comments" section.
6. Complete the "Assigned Personnel" and "Reporting Personnel" fields with your name and contact information.
7. Sign and date the "Supervisor Signature" and "Safety Manager Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Hazards** (`workplace_hazards`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select all the hazards that you have observed or identified during the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Hazard Risks** (`hazard_risks`, select_one, **required**): Select the severity of each hazard as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Control Measures** (`control_measures`, note, **required**): Describe the control measures or corrective actions taken to mitigate or eliminate the hazards.
* **Risk Control Measures** (`risk_control_measures`, note, **required**): Document any additional risk control measures taken.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, note, **required**): Describe the actions taken to mitigate or eliminate the hazards.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **required**): Provide any additional comments or observations.
* **Assigned Personnel** (`assigned_personnel`, text, **optional**): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Reporting Personnel** (`reporting_personnel`, text, **optional**): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, **required**): Sign and date your supervisor's name and contact information.
* **Safety Manager Signature** (`safety_manager_signature`, text, **required**): Sign and date your safety manager's name and contact information.

## Tips

* Please make sure to complete all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing comments and descriptions.
* Review and sign the form before submitting it for official approval.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or safety manager.
