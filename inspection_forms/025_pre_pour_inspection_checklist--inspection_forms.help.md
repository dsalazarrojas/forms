# Pre Pour Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the outcome of a pre-pour inspection, which is crucial in ensuring the quality and integrity of the concrete pour. The inspection process assesses various aspects of the site, including foundation quality, concrete compatibility, concrete finish, surface quality, and other critical factors that impact the overall structure's stability and durability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the inspection guidelines and procedures to ensure you understand the expected outcomes and criteria.
2. Complete each field according to the guidelines, selecting the most accurate answers based on your observations.
3. Fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
4. If you're unsure or need clarification on any field, refer to the guidelines or consult with your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Foundation Quality (1)**: Select the level of foundation quality based on your observation. Options include:
	+ Very Satisfactory
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Not Satisfactory
	+ Not Acceptable
* **Concrete Compatibility (2)**: Choose whether the concrete is suitable for the pour or not.
	+ Suitable
	+ Not Suitable
	+ Unknown
* **Concrete Finish (3)**: Evaluate the concrete finish based on your observation.
	+ Smooth
	+ Rough
	+ Not Applicable
* **Surface Quality (4)**: Determine if the surface is free from defects.
	+ Free from Defects
	+ Not Free from Defects
	+ Not Applicable
* **Inspection Date (5)**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspection Time (6)**: Record the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM.
* **Pour Depth (7)**: Enter the depth of the pour.
* **Comments (8)**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Assignee (9)**: Enter the name of the person assigned to perform the inspection.
* **Assigned Tool (10)**: Enter the tool or equipment used during the inspection (if applicable).
* **Form ID (11)**: Enter a unique identifier for this form.
* **Category (12)**: Choose the category for this form.

Note: This guide is for general reference only. Refer to your organization's policies and procedures for specific guidance on completing this form accurately and effectively.
