# Greenhouse Crop Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Crop Inspection Form is used to inspect the crops in a controlled environment, evaluate their condition, and identify any issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information in the `Contact` field.
2. Provide the location where the crop was inspected in the `Location` field.
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the `Date Inspected` field.
4. Select the condition of the crop using the `Crop Condition` dropdown menu.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the inspection in the `Notes` field.
6. If applicable, choose a follow-up action in the `Follow Up Action` dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional comments or information in the `Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other contact information, such as phone number or address.
* **Location** (`text`, required/optional): Provide the name or address of the location where the crop was inspected.
* **Date Inspected** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time Inspected** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
* **Crop Condition** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the condition of the crop as Good, Bad, or Unknown.
* **Notes** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Contact** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other contact information, such as phone number or address.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Follow Up Action** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more follow-up actions as applicable.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the inspection to ensure a thorough assessment of the crop condition.
* If you are unsure about the condition of the crop, select "Unknown" from the `Crop Condition` dropdown menu.
* Use the `Notes` field to provide any additional context or information about the inspection that may not be addressed in other fields.
