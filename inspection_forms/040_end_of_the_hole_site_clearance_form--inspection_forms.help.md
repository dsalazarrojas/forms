# End Of The Hole Site Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial tool for documenting and ensuring the completion of a site clearance process. It helps in maintaining site safety and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Site Name** field with the name of the site being cleared.
2. Enter the **Site Address** in the corresponding field for easy identification and location tracking.
3. Select the **Site Location** from the provided options or fill out the text field for a specific location description.
4. Enter the **Clearance Date** when the site was cleared.
5. Choose the **Clearance Type** from the options provided (e.g., Full Clearance, Partial Clearance, etc.).
6. Select the **Clearance Status** from the options provided (e.g., Completed, In Progress, etc.).
7. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes in the **Remarks** field for further explanation.
8. Confirm the **Site Clearance Confirmation** by selecting the relevant option.
9. If additional comments or notes are required, they can be provided in the second **Remarks** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name**: The name of the site being cleared. This is a unique identifier for the site.
* **Site Address**: The address of the site to ensure easy tracking and identification.
* **Site Location**: A brief description of the site's location or a selection from the provided options.
* **Clearance Date**: The date when the site was cleared.
* **Clearance Type**: The type of clearance performed on the site (e.g., Full, Partial, etc.).
* **Clearance Status**: The current status of the clearance process.
* **Site Clearance Confirmation**: A confirmation that the site has been cleared.
* **Remarks** (1): Additional comments or notes about the clearance process.
* **Remarks** (2): More comments or notes if needed for further explanation.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Verify the **Clearance Status** and **Clearance Type** before confirming **Site Clearance Confirmation**.
* Use the **Remarks** fields for any additional information that may be required for future reference.
