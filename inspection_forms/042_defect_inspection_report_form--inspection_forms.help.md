# Defect Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Defect Inspection Report Form is used to document and report defects found during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the `user` field.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the `date` field.
3. Describe the defect in the `defect description` field.
4. Specify the location where the defect was found in the `inspection location` field.
5. Enter the product ID in the `product id` field.
6. Select the severity level of the defect (`Critical`, `Moderate`, or `Minor`) in the `defect severity` field.
7. Choose the status of the defect (`Active` or `Inactive`) in the `defect status` field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the `inspector notes` field.
9. Record the inspection result (`Pass` or `Fail`) in the `inspection result` field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and title.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **defect description** (text, required): Describe the defect found.
* **inspection location** (text, required): Specify the location where the defect was found.
* **product id** (number, required): Enter the product ID.
* **product category** (text, optional): Enter the category of the product.
* **product type** (text, optional): Enter the type of the product.
* **serial number** (text, optional): Enter the serial number of the product.
* **asset tag** (text, optional): Enter the asset tag of the product.
* **asset tag location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the asset tag.
* **equipment id** (number, optional): Enter the equipment ID.
* **equipment type** (text, optional): Enter the type of equipment.
* **defect location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the defect.
* **defect image** (text, optional): Enter a reference to a defect image.
* **additional comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **inspection result** (select one, required): Record the inspection result.
* **comments** (text, optional): Add any comments or notes.
* **defect description 2** (text, optional): Describe any additional information about the defect.
* **notes** (text, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **action plan** (text, optional): Enter an action plan to resolve the defect.
