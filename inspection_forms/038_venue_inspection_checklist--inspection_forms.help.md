# Venue Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for inspecting venue details such as event organizer, venue, event type, event dates, and event time. The information collected in this form will be used to ensure that the venue meets the necessary requirements for hosting an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Enter the name of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer" field.
3. Enter the name of the venue in the "Venue" field.
4. Select the type of event being held in the "Event Type" field.
5. Enter the event dates in the "Event Dates" field.
6. Enter the event time in the "Event Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name and contact information of the person or organization responsible for the event.
* **Venue**: Enter the name and address of the venue where the event will be held.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event being held, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Event Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the event in the "MM/DD/YY" format.
* **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the event in the "HH:MM AM/PM" format.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the venue meets the necessary requirements.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
