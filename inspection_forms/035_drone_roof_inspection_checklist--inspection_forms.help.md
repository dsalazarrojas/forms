# Drone Roof Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, read the field-by-field explanations below and fill out each field carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Roof Inspection Date** (`roof_inspection_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
*   **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
*   **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of roof that was inspected. Options include Asphalt Shingle, Clay Tile, and Cedar Shake.
*   **Inspector Contact Info** (`inspector_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information of the inspector, including name, email, or phone number.
*   **Inspector Company** (`inspector_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company that the inspector works for.
*   **Roof Area** (`roof_area`, `number`, required: false): Enter the size of the roof in square feet.
*   **Number of Roof Layers** (`number_of_roof_layers`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of layers on the roof.
*   **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the condition of the roof. Options include Excellent, Fair, Poor, and Good.
*   **Repair Recommendations** (`repair_recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Enter any recommended repairs or maintenance that should be done to the roof.
*   **Drone Pictures** (`drone_pictures`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select if drone pictures were taken during the inspection. Options include Yes and No.
*   **Roof Measurement** (`roof_measurement`, `number`, required: false): Enter any measurements taken during the inspection.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out each field carefully and accurately to ensure that the data is correct and helpful for future reference.
*   If you are not sure about any field, do not hesitate to consult with a supervisor or colleague.
