# Home Window And Door Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inspect and record the conditions of windows and doors in a residential or commercial setting. It can be used for maintenance, quality assurance, or housing inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions carefully and answer each field according to your observation and expertise.
2. Select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu or type a brief description in the text field.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (none of the fields are marked as required, so it's optional but recommended).
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Window Condition**: Describe the general condition of the windows, such as if they are intact, damaged, or not applicable.
* **Glass Type**: Select the type of glass used in the window, such as Broken, Intact, Damaged, or Not Applicable.
* **Door Condition**: Describe the condition of the door, such as its overall state or any notable issues.
* **Frame Condition**: Describe the condition of the door frame, including any signs of wear or damage.
* **Door Alignment**: Select the alignment of the door, such as if it is Center, Off-center, Misaligned, or Not Applicable.
* **Door Hinges**: Describe the condition of the door hinges, including if they are loose, stiff, or not functioning properly.
* **Door Sill**: Describe the condition of the door sill, including if it is rotten, damaged, or showing signs of wear.
* **Door Threshold**: Describe the condition of the threshold, including if it is level, uneven, or damaged.
* **Door Sweep**: Describe the condition of the door sweep, including if it is properly aligned, loose, or not functioning properly.
* **Door Frame Condition**: Describe the condition of the door frame, including if it is damaged, rotten, or showing signs of wear.
* **Window Type**: Describe the type of window, such as a window with a specific material or design.
* **Door Alignment**: Select the alignment of the door, such as if it is Center, Off-center, Misaligned, or Not Applicable.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the conditions of windows and doors.
* Take note of any issues or concerns that need further attention.
* This form is for general guidance and may not cover all possible scenarios, so use your discretion when completing it.
* Consult with experts or professionals if you are unsure about any of the conditions or issues you observe.
