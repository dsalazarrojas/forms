# Pediatric Medical Daycare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and evaluate the compliance of daycare centers with medical standards to ensure a safe and healthy environment for children.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Daycare Center Details form (Page 1) with the name and location of the daycare center.
- Select the Inspection Date (Page 2) of your visit or the date you are reporting on.
- Provide information on Staff Training (Page 3), including any relevant certifications or training received.
- Complete the Staff To Staff Ratio (Page 4) section by inputting the number of staff members present during the inspection.
- Check if Emergency Contact Info (Page 5) is available or not. Select "True" if the daycare center has emergency contact information, or "False" if it does not.
- Verify the availability of First Aid Kit (Page 6). Select "Available" if the first aid kit is present, or "Not Available" if it is not.
- Input information on Medical Records (Page 7) such as any relevant medical history, allergies, or health issues.
- List the available Medical Supplies (Page 8) such as bandages, antiseptics, etc.
- Report the Inspection Result (Page 9) as either "Pass" or "Fail" based on the findings.
- Provide an Action Plan (Page 10) for any issues or areas for improvement.
- Outline a Follow Up Plan (Page 11) for any issues that require further action.
- Add any additional Comments (Page 12) or observations.
- Finally, input the name of the user who submitted the form and the date it was submitted on (Pages 13-14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daycare Center Details**: Provide the name and location of the daycare center being inspected.
* **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection or the date you are reporting on.
* **Staff Training**: Provide information on any staff training or certifications received, such as CPR or First Aid certification.
* **Staff To Staff Ratio**: The number of staff members present during the inspection.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Check if the daycare center has emergency contact information available. 
* **First Aid Kit Availability**: Check if the first aid kit is available on site.
* **Medical Records**: Input any relevant medical history, allergies, or health issues of the children.
* **Medical Supplies**: List any available medical supplies on site such as bandages, antiseptics, etc.
* **Inspection Result**: Report the outcome of the inspection as "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Action Plan**: Outline any necessary action items or areas for improvement.
* **Follow Up Plan**: Outline a plan for follow up on any issues that require additional action.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or observations.
* **Submitted By**: The name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On**: The date the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure accurate reporting.
* Verify the availability of first aid kits and emergency contact information.
* Ensure all staff members are properly trained and certified.
* Report any issues or concerns immediately.
* Follow up on any necessary actions or areas for improvement.
* Keep a record of all inspections and submissions for future reference.
