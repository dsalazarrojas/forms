# Pool Chemicals Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the pool's chemical levels, water level, and other relevant details during a routine chemical check. This form should be used for pools that are not part of a larger facility or system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct pool water level from the "What is the pool water level?" dropdown menu.
2. Choose whether the pool uses a chemical feed type from the "Chemical Feed Type" dropdown menu.
3. Select the results of the chemical check from the "Chemicals Check Results" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of the chemical check in the "Check Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the chemical check in the "Check Time" field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
7. Enter any notes about the chemical check (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the pool water level?** (`pool_water_level`): Select the current water level of the pool, which can be Low, Normal, or High.
* **Chemical Feed Type** (`chemical_feed_type`): Choose whether the pool uses a chemical feed type. This field is optional.
* **Chemicals Check Results** (`chemicals_check_results`): Select the results of the chemical check. This field is required and should be filled out with the outcome of the check.
* **Check Date** (`check_date`): Enter the date of the chemical check. This field is required and should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Check Time** (`check_time`): Enter the time of the chemical check. This field is required and should be in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any notes about the chemical check (optional).
* **What is the chemical check frequency?** (`chemical_check_frequency`): Select the frequency of the chemical check. This field is optional.
* **What is the pool size?** (`pool_size`): Enter the size of the pool (optional).
* **What is the pool type?** (`pool_type`): Select the type of pool, which can be Saltwater or Freshwater. This field is optional.
