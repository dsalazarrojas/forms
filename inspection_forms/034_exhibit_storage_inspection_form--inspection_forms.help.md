# Exhibit Storage Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibit Storage Inspection Form is used to inspect exhibits for storage conditions and provide information about the storage process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information.
2.  Enter the exhibit name in the field labeled "Exhibit Name."
3.  Choose the storage location for the exhibit from the options provided.
4.  Check the storage condition of the exhibit.
5.  Enter the storage inspection date and time.
6.  Enter the storage inspection frequency.
7.  Enter the next storage inspection date and time (optional).
8.  Select the storage inspection status.
9.  Enter any storage inspection comments or recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Exhibit Name**: This is the name of the exhibit being inspected. Enter the name of the exhibit in this field.
    (<label>Exhibit Name`, `<type>`text`, required: true)
*   **Storage Inspection**: This is the storage inspection section where you will enter the inspection details. Enter any comments, concerns, or issues related to the storage of this exhibit.
    (<label>Storage Inspection`, `<type>`text`, required: false)
*   **Storage Location**: This is the storage location where the exhibit is kept. Choose the appropriate storage location from the options provided.
    (<label>Storage Location`, `<type>`select_multiple`, required: false)
*   **Storage Condition**: This is the storage condition of the exhibit. Check this field to indicate the condition of the storage area.
    (<label>Storage Condition`, `<type>`text`, required: false)
*   **Storage Inspection Date**: This is the date of the storage inspection. Enter the date of the storage inspection in this field.
    (<label>Storage Inspection Date`, `<type>`date`, required: true)
*   **Storage Inspection Time**: This is the time of the storage inspection. Enter the time of the storage inspection in this field.
    (<label>Storage Inspection Time`, `<type>`time`, required: false)
*   **Storage Inspection Frequency**: This is the frequency of the storage inspection. Enter the frequency of the storage inspection (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
    (<label>Storage Inspection Frequency`, `<type>`number`, required: true)
*   **Next Storage Inspection Date**: This is the next storage inspection date. Enter the date of the next storage inspection (optional).
    (<label>Next Storage Inspection Date`, `<type>`date`, required: false)
*   **Next Storage Inspection Time**: This is the next storage inspection time. Enter the time of the next storage inspection (optional).
    (<label>Next Storage Inspection Time`, `<type>`time`, required: false)
*   **Storage Inspection Frequency Frequency**: This is a note field for the storage inspection frequency. Enter any additional comments or information about the storage inspection frequency.
    (<label>Storage Inspection Frequency Frequency`, `<type>`text`, required: false)
*   **Storage Inspection Status**: This is the status of the storage inspection. Select the appropriate status from the options provided (Pass, Fail, Review).
    (<label>Storage Inspection Status`, `<type>`select_one`, required: true)
*   **Storage Inspection Comments**: This is a note field for any additional comments or recommendations related to the storage inspection.
    (<label>Storage Inspection Comments`, `<type>`text`, required: false)
*   **Storage Inspection Recommendations**: This is a note field for any additional recommendations related to the storage inspection.
    (<label>Storage Inspection Recommendations`, `<type>`text`, required: false)

Note: Please make sure to enter all the required fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
