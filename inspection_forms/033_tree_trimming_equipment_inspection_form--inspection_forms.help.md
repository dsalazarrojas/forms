# Tree Trimming Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inspectors to document the status and maintenance of tree trimming equipment. It ensures that all necessary information is captured during the inspection, including equipment identification, inspection dates, locations, and safety certifications. This form helps to maintain accurate records and facilitate compliance with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the Tree Trimming Equipment Id in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the Inspector Id to associate with the equipment.
3. Select the Inspection Date and Location from the available options.
4. Enter the Equipment Id to track the specific equipment being inspected.
5. Provide the Inspection Results in the text field.
6. Select the status of the Equipment Safety Certification (Certified or Not Certified).
7. Check if the equipment is certified by the Inspector (True or False).
8. Enter the last maintenance date for the equipment.
9. Enter the next maintenance due date for the equipment.
10. Enter the maintenance frequency for the equipment (number of days or hours).
11. Select if the equipment is operating properly (True or False).
12. Select if maintenance is required (True or False).
13. Select if the equipment has safety labels (True or False).
14. Select if the equipment has safety harnesses (True or False).
15. Select if the equipment has safety hats (True or False).
16. Select if the equipment has safety gloves (True or False).
17. Select if the equipment has safety goggles (True or False).
18. Select if the equipment has safety kneepads (True or False).
19. Select if the equipment has safety chaps (True or False).
20. Select if the equipment has safety vests (True or False).
21. Select if the equipment has safety boots (True or False).
22. Select if the equipment has safety gaiters (True or False).
23. Select if the equipment has safety spectacles (True or False).
24. Select the person assigned to the equipment from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tree Trimming Equipment Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the tree trimming equipment being inspected.
* **Inspector Id**: Enter the identifier of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection from the calendar picker.
* **Location Id**: Enter the location where the inspection took place.
* **Equipment Id**: Enter the identifier of the specific equipment being inspected.
* **Inspection Results**: Provide a brief description of the inspection results in the text field.
* **Is Equipment Safety Certified**: Select if the equipment is safety certified (Certified or Not Certified).
* **Certified By**: Check if the equipment is certified by the inspector (True or False).
* **Equipment Last Maintenance**: Enter the date of the last maintenance for the equipment.
* **Equipment Next Maintenance Due Date**: Enter the date when the next maintenance is due.
* **Equipment Maintenance Frequency**: Enter the frequency of maintenance for the equipment (number of days or hours).
* **Has Operating Properly**: Select if the equipment is operating properly (True or False).
* **Is Maintenance Required**: Select if maintenance is required for the equipment (True or False).
* **Has Safety Labels**: Select if the equipment has safety labels (True or False).
* **Has Safety Harnesses**: Select if the equipment has safety harnesses (True or False).
* **Has Safety Hats**: Select if the equipment has safety hats (True or False).
* **Has Safety Gloves**: Select if the equipment has safety gloves (True or False).
* **Has Safety Goggles**: Select if the equipment has safety goggles (True or False).
* **Has Safety Kneepads**: Select if the equipment has safety kneepads (True or False).
* **Has Safety Chaps**: Select if the equipment has safety chaps (True or False).
* **Has Safety Vests**: Select if the equipment has safety vests (True or False).
* **Has Safety Boots**: Select if the equipment has safety boots (True or False).
* **Has Safety Gaiters**: Select if the equipment has safety gaiters (True or False).
* **Has Safety Spectacles**: Select if the equipment has safety spectacles (True or False).
* **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to the equipment from the dropdown list.
