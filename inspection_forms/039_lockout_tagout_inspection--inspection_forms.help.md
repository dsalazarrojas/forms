# lockout-tagout-inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the process of conducting a lockout-tagout-inspection, which is a procedure used to prevent or control hazardous situations by ensuring that machinery or equipment is safely stopped or isolated before maintenance or repair work is performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the employee-name field with the name of the person performing the inspection.
2.  Enter the employee-id of the person performing the inspection in the employee-id field.
3.  Select the date of the inspection in the date-inspected field.
4.  Choose the time of the inspection in the time-inspected field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *employee-name* (`employee-name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
*   *employee-id* (`employee-id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the person performing the inspection.
*   *date-inspected* (`date`, required/optional): Select the date of the inspection.
*   *time-inspected* (`time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the inspection.
