# Elevated Work Platform Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elevated Work Platform Safety Checklist is designed to ensure the safety of operators working on elevated work platforms such as scaffolding, cranes, and aerial lifts. This form helps inspectors and operators identify potential hazards and document corrective actions taken to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the operator or responsible individual working on the elevated work platform.
2.  Select the date of the inspection or audit.
3.  Check if Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) has been checked and is present on the platform.
4.  List any hazards identified during the inspection.
5.  Describe the corrective actions taken to address these hazards.
6.  Select the assigned inspector who conducted the inspection.
7.  Sign and date the inspector's section to confirm they have reviewed the checklist.
8.  Sign and date the operator's section to confirm they have reviewed the checklist and understand their responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the responsible individual working on the elevated work platform.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection or audit.
*   **PPE Checked** (`ppe_checked`, select_one, required): Check if Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) has been checked and is present on the platform.
	+ Options: `PPE Checked`, `PPE Not Checked`
*   **Hazards Identified** (`hazards_identified`, select_multiple, required): Select any hazards identified during the inspection.
	+ Options: `Hazard 1`, `Hazard 2`, `Hazard 3`
*   **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, required): Describe the actions taken to address the hazards identified.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
*   **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, select_one, required): Select the assigned inspector who conducted the inspection.
	+ Options: `Assigned Inspector 1`, `Assigned Inspector 2`, `Assigned Inspector 3`
*   **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the inspector's section to confirm they have reviewed the checklist.
*   **Operator Signature** (`operator_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the operator's section to confirm they have reviewed the checklist and understand their responsibilities.
*   **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the form.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
*   **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
*   **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was created.
*   **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was last updated.
