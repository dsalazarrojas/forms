# Appliance Security Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appliance Security Inspection Checklist is a form used to inspect appliances for potential safety hazards and document any issues that need to be addressed. This form guides the inspector through a series of questions and checks to ensure a comprehensive and accurate inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have a pen and paper or a digital device to fill out the form.
2.  Complete each section of the form based on the observed conditions during the inspection.
3.  For fields that are required, you must provide an answer before proceeding.
4.  Review and correct any mistakes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required): Provide your full name as the inspector responsible for this form.
* **Inspection date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Specify the facility or address where the inspection took place.
* **Appliance model** (`appliance_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model or serial number of the appliance being inspected.
* **Tamper seals intact** (`tamper_seals_ok`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if the tamper seals are present and not broken.
* **Grounding and electrical safety** (`grounding_check`, `select_one`, required): Check 'Pass' if the appliance is properly grounded and wired, 'Fail' if there are issues, and 'Not applicable' if it's not applicable to this appliance.
* **Safety labels present** (`safety_labels_present`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if the required safety labels are present on the appliance, 'False' if they are missing.
* **Observed hazards** (`observed_hazards`, `text`, optional): Describe any hazards found during the inspection. If none are found, leave this section blank.
* **Corrective action required** (`corrective_action_required`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of any necessary corrective steps.
* **Follow up date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for a reinspection if corrective actions are required or if the issue persists.

Please ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
