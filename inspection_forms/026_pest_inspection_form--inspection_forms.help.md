# Pest Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in the documentation of pest inspection activities, such as recording treatment dates, customer information, and treatment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Treatment Date** in the date field (page 1).
2. Provide the **Customer** details in the text field (page 1).
3. Select the type of **Treatment** performed from the dropdown menu (page 1).
4. Choose the type of **Pest Type** present from the multiple-choice list (page 1).
5. Enter any **Notes** taken during the inspection in the text field (page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Treatment Date (date)**: The date the pest inspection treatment was performed.
* **Customer (text)**: Information about the customer, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Treatment (select_one)**: The type of treatment performed, e.g., Baiting, Trapping, or Chemical Application.
* **Pest Type (select_multiple)**: The type of pest present, such as Rats, Mice, Cockroaches, or Ants.
* **Notes (text)**: Any additional notes or comments taken during the inspection.
