# Building Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Inspection Checklist is used by inspectors to document their findings after inspecting a building. It helps them to identify potential issues and provide an overall assessment of the property's condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the inspection date.
2. Enter the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the type of building from the options provided.
4. Fill in the inspector's name and license number (if applicable).
5. For each section, choose the corresponding rating based on your assessment.
6. List any major deficiencies or recommended repairs in the "Deficiencies" field.
7. Select "True" if photos or documentation are attached to support your findings.
