# semi_truck_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The semi-truck inspection form is used to collect information about a semi-truck's inspection, including its identification details, inspection date, and notes about the condition of the truck and trailers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Truck Id** number of the semi-truck being inspected. This is a required field.
2. Select the **Date Of Inspection** when the inspection was performed. This is a required field.
3. Enter the **Inspection Date** when the inspection is scheduled or completed. This is a required field.
4. Choose the **Truck Type** of the semi-truck being inspected. This can be Single Axle, Double Axle, or Semi Tractor.
5. Enter the **Truck Number** of the semi-truck being inspected. This is a required field.
6. Enter the number of **Trailer Count** if applicable.
7. Choose the **Trailer Type** of the trailers being inspected. If not applicable, select "No Trailer".
8. Enter any **Notes** about the condition of the truck and trailers. This is an optional field.
9. Enter the **Email** of the contact person or person responsible for the inspection. This is an optional field.
10. Enter the **Phone** number of the contact person or person responsible for the inspection. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truck Id**: Enter the unique identifier of the semi-truck being inspected. 
* **Date Of Inspection**: Select the date when the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date when the inspection is scheduled or completed.
* **Truck Type**: Select the type of semi-truck being inspected (Single Axle, Double Axle, Semi Tractor).
* **Truck Number**: Enter the number of the semi-truck being inspected.
* **Trailer Count**: Enter the number of trailers being inspected if applicable.
* **Trailer Type**: Select the type of trailer being inspected (Single Axle, Double Axle, No Trailer).
* **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the condition of the truck and trailers.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the contact person or person responsible for the inspection.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or person responsible for the inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the correct semi-truck identification details before starting the inspection.
- Enter accurate dates for the inspection.
- Complete all required fields.
- Enter any relevant notes or comments about the inspection.
- Ensure the contact information is correct and up-to-date.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
