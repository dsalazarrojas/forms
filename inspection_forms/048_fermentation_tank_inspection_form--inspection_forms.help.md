# Fermentation Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fermentation Tank Inspection Form is a document used to record and assess the condition and status of fermentation tanks on a specific date. This form is used to collect crucial information about each tank, enabling efficient monitoring, inspection, and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the **Tank Number**, which is a unique identifier for the tank being inspected.
2. Select the **Tank Type** from the provided options, describing the tank's material or style.
3. In the **Tank Condition** field, select all applicable options that describe the tank's current state (e.g., Clean, Stained, Dirty, Leaking).
4. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of inspection, ensuring accuracy and precision.
5. Provide the **Temperature** reading in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
6. Enter the **Level** of the tank's contents.
7. Add any **Comments** or notes about the tank's condition, condition, or any issues that need attention.
8. Select the **Assigned To** option for the person responsible for monitoring the tank.
9. Optionally enter the **Assigned At** date for tracking progress.
10. Provide the **Email** and **Phone** contact information for the person assigned.
11. Finally, sign with a **Signature**, which serves as verification of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tank Number**: A unique identifier for the tank being inspected.
* **Tank Type**: The material or style of the tank (e.g., Stainless Steel, Glass).
* **Tank Condition**: Select all applicable options describing the tank's current state (e.g., Clean, Stained, Dirty, Leaking).
* **Date**: The date of inspection.
* **Temperature**: The temperature reading in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Level**: The level of the tank's contents.
* **Comments**: Any notes or issues about the tank's condition.
* **Assigned To**: The person responsible for monitoring the tank.
* **Assigned At**: The date assigned to for tracking progress.
* **Email**: The contact email of the person assigned.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number of the person assigned.
* **Signature**: A verification of the inspection.

## Tips
* Always complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Verify that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
* Use the contact information provided to reach out to the assigned person.
* Keep the comments section concise, focusing on key issues or observations.
* Ensure that the tank number matches the actual tank being inspected.
* Review the form for any inconsistencies or errors before signing.
