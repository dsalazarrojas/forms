# Dam Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dam Inspection Form is a tool used by maintenance teams and operators to gather data and record observations during dam inspections. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin the inspection. 
2.  Answer the questions on each page according to your observations and knowledge.
3.  Be sure to save your progress as you go, as the form can be saved and resumed later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Dam Type**: This field asks you to identify the type of dam you are inspecting. Please select the correct type from the options provided.
*   **Location**: This field is for you to enter the location of the dam. Please provide the exact coordinates or a detailed address.
*   **Inspection Date**: This field is for you to enter the date of the inspection. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for date entries.
*   **Inspection Time**: This field is for you to enter the time of the inspection. Please use the 24-hour format `HH:MM:SS`.
*   **Comments**: This field is for you to enter any comments or notes about the inspection. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Assigned To**: This field is for you to indicate if the inspection was assigned to someone else. Please select "Yes" if the inspection was assigned to another team member, or "No" if it was performed by you.
*   **Notes**: This field is a text area for you to enter any additional information about the inspection. Please use this field to record any observations that are not covered by other fields.
*   **Dam Height**: This field is for you to enter the height of the dam. Please use numerical values only.
*   **Water Level**: This field is for you to enter the water level of the dam. Please use numerical values only.
*   **Dam Condition**: This field asks you to identify the condition of the dam. Please select the correct condition from the options provided.
*   **Inspection Frequency**: This field is for you to indicate if the inspection was performed as part of a regular schedule. Please select "Yes" if the inspection was part of a scheduled inspection, or "No" if it was ad-hoc.

The fields marked as required are necessary to be filled. The others are optional and are meant to be filled as needed. Remember that this form can be saved and resumed later as you see fit. If you are unsure about any of the fields or their purpose, please consult with your team leader or supervisor before proceeding.
