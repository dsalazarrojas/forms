# iso_container_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iso_container_inspection_checklist form is used to inspect and record information about ISO containers. The form helps ensure that containers are properly managed and stored, reducing the risk of contamination and damage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the General Info page and enter any relevant details about the container.
2. Move on to the Container Info page and record any specific information about the container.
3. On the Seals page, select the correct status of the seals (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
4. Record the temperature reading for the container.
5. Enter the moisture level reading for the container.
6. Enter the humidity reading for the container.
7. On the Container Condition page, select any relevant conditions (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
8. Enter any comments about the container in the Comments field.
9. Record any notes about the container in the Notes field.
10. Set the date and time of the inspection on the Date and Time page.
11. Finally, set the time of the inspection on the Date and Time Time page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. General info
* **General Info (Text, Required)**: Enter a brief description or identification of the container.

### 2. container_info
* **Container Info (Text, Required)**: Enter specific details about the container, including its type, size, or other relevant information.

### 3. Seals
* **Seals (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the status of the seals (Good, Bad, or Unknown) to indicate their condition.

### 4. temperature
* **Temperature (Number, Required)**: Record the current temperature reading for the container.

### 5. moisture_level
* **Moisture Level (Number, Required)**: Enter the current moisture level reading for the container.

### 6. humidity
* **Humidity (Number, Required)**: Enter the current humidity reading for the container.

### 7. container_condition
* **Container Condition (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select any relevant conditions (Good, Bad, or Unknown) to describe the container's overall condition.

### 8. comments
* **Comments (Text, Required)**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the container.

### 9. notes
* **Notes (Text, Required)**: Enter any relevant notes or information about the container.

### 10. date_and_time
* **Date and Time (Date, Required)**: Set the date of the inspection.

### 11. date_and_time_time
* **Date and Time Time (Time, Required)**: Set the time of the inspection.

## Tips
* Always review and record accurate information about the container.
* Be thorough in your inspection to ensure the container is properly managed and stored.
* If you're unsure about any information, do not hesitate to seek guidance from a supervisor or expert.
