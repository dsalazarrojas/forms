# Building Occupancy Audit Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Building Occupancy Audit Registration form is designed to gather information from building owners or facility managers to register their building for an occupancy audit. This form helps the auditor understand the building's characteristics, occupancy details, and audit requirements to ensure a comprehensive and accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  For fields not required, you can leave them blank or provide additional information if necessary.
3.  Ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays or miscommunication during the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Manager/Contact Name** (`facility_manager`): Provide the name of the person responsible for managing the building or the contact person for the audit.
*   **Email Address** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of the facility manager or contact person.
*   **Phone Number** (`manager_phone`): Provide the phone number of the facility manager or contact person.
*   **Organization Name** (`organization`): Enter the name of the organization owning the building.
*   **Building Name** (`building_name`): Enter the name of the building.
*   **Building Address** (`building_address`): Provide the full address of the building.
*   **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`): Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of the building's location.
*   **Building Type** (`building_type`): Select the type of building (e.g., office, residential, educational, etc.).
*   **Total Square Footage** (`square_footage`): Enter the total square footage of the building.
*   **Number of Floors** (`number_floors`): Enter the number of floors in the building.
*   **Current Occupancy (persons)** (`current_occupancy`): Enter the daily average number of people occupying the building.
*   **Occupancy Type** (`occupancy_type`): Select the type of occupancy (e.g., owner-occupied, leased spaces, etc.).
*   **Type of Occupancy Audit Requested** (`audit_type`): Select the type of occupancy audit required (e.g., comprehensive, density, compliance, etc.).
*   **Focus Areas for Audit** (`focus_areas`): Select the areas to be audited (e.g., space utilization, occupancy counts, fire egress, etc.).
*   **Preferred Audit Dates** (`preferred_dates`): Enter the preferred dates for the audit.
*   **Flexibility with Scheduling** (`timing_flexibility`): Select the level of flexibility with scheduling (e.g., fixed date required, flexible within 2 weeks, etc.).
*   **Expected Audit Duration** (`audit_duration`): Select the expected duration of the audit (e.g., half-day, full-day, etc.).
*   **Building Access during Audit** (`access_arrangement`): Select the level of access to the building during the audit (e.g., full access, some restricted areas, etc.).
*   **Occupant and Staff Coordination** (`occupant_coordination`): Select the level of coordination needed with occupants and staff during the audit.
*   **Primary Purpose of Audit** (`audit_purpose`): Enter the primary purpose of the audit.
*   **Current Occupancy Data Available** (`occupancy_data`): Select whether current occupancy data is available.
*   **Occupancy Permits and Documentation** (`permits_documentation`): Select the level of documentation available for occupancy permits.
*   **Regulations and Standards** (`regulations_standards`): Select the regulations and standards to be assessed (e.g., local building codes, fire codes, etc.).
*   **Compliance Standards** (`compliance_standards`): Select the compliance standards to be assessed (e.g., local building codes, fire codes, etc.).
*   **Regulatory Authority Requiring Audit** (`regulatory_authority`): Enter the regulatory authority requiring the audit.
*   **Deliverables and Follow-up** (`deliverables`): Select the preferred report format and distribution options.
*   **Follow-up Meeting or Presentation Needed** (`follow_up_meeting`): Select whether a follow-up meeting or presentation is needed.
*   **Support for Remediation Planning** (`remediation_support`): Select the level of support needed for remediation planning.
*   **Additional Information or Special Requests** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information or special requests.
