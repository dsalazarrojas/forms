# Overcurrent Protection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and verify overcurrent protection checks performed on electrical equipment. Inspectors will use this form to gather information about the date of inspection, the inspector's name, and other relevant details to ensure accurate and timely maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
3. Provide the address of the equipment being inspected.
4. Describe the circuit being inspected.
5. Describe the equipment being inspected.
6. List the overcurrent protection devices used.
7. Record the result of the inspection.
8. Add any comments or notes about the inspection.
9. Enter the name and email address of the facility manager.
10. Enter the contact phone number for the facility manager.
11. Select the inspector assigned to the equipment.
12. Choose the inspection frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
13. Select the status of the inspection (e.g., completed, scheduled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (1, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the inspection was performed.
* **Inspector Name** (2, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the inspector performing the inspection.
* **Device Address** (3, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the equipment being inspected.
* **Circuit Description** (4, `text`, optional): Describe the circuit being inspected in detail.
* **Equipment Description** (5, `text`, optional): Describe the equipment being inspected.
* **Overcurrent Protection Devices** (6, `text`, optional): List any overcurrent protection devices used.
* **Inspection Result** (7, `select_one`, optional): Select the outcome of the inspection: Not Applicable, Passed, or Failed.
* **Comments** (8, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Facility Manager Name** (9, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Email** (10, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the facility manager.
* **Phone Number** (11, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the facility manager.
* **Assigned Inspector** (12, `select_one`, optional): Select the inspector assigned to the equipment (e.g., Jimmy or Jane).
* **Inspection Frequency** (13, `select_one`, optional): Choose the frequency of the inspection (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Inspection Status** (14, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the inspection (e.g., completed, scheduled).
