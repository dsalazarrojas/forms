# travel_movement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel movement declaration form is used to document and track employee travel for work purposes. It is required for employees who need to travel for work-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your travel date (page 1).
2. Enter your travel location (page 2).
3. Choose your current health status (page 3).
4. Enter your body temperature (page 4).
5. Provide a reason for your travel (page 5).
6. Enter the name of the contact person you will be meeting with (page 6).
7. Enter the contact number of the contact person (page 7).
8. If necessary, add a note for your signature (page 8).
9. Provide any additional travel details (page 9).
10. Add any comments (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Date** (`1`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your trip, in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Travel Location** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of your destination, including city and state or country.
* **Health Status** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current health status from the options:
	+ Normal
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ Critical
* **Temperature** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current body temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
* **Reason for Travel** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason for your travel, including any relevant information or details.
* **Contact Person** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you will be meeting with.
* **Contact Number** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Signature** (`8`, `note`, required: false): If necessary, add a note for your signature, for example, a printed name and date.
* **Travel Details** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional travel details, such as flight numbers or hotel information.
* **Comments** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your trip.
