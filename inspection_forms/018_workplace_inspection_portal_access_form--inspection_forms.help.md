# Workplace Inspection Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a way for administrators to grant access to the Workplace Inspection Portal for their organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the form, including the Organization ID, Contact Person, Email, and Phone number.
2. Select the correct Organization ID from the available options.
3. Enter the Contact Person's name and their role within the organization.
4. Enter the Contact Person's email address.
5. Enter the Contact Person's phone number.
6. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization ID** (`organization_id`, number, required): This field is used to select the organization that the contact person will be granted access to. Please enter the correct Organization ID from the available options.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the contact person who will be granted access to the Workplace Inspection Portal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the contact person's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the contact person to ensure they receive important notifications and updates from the Workplace Inspection Portal.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting it to avoid any delays or issues with granting access.
