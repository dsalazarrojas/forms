# Pre Project Site Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information about a site before a project starts. It helps project teams identify potential risks, issues, or hazards that may need attention during the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the site's details, including its address, location, or any other relevant information.
2. Check if there are any safety hazards on the site and select the relevant options.
3. Add any necessary site images or documents.
4. Take notes or comments about the site.
5. Add any contacts or individuals involved with the site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details** (`site_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the site, including its address, location, or any other relevant information.
* **Site Access Instructions** (`site_access_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any specific instructions for accessing the site, such as parking or entry procedures.
* **Safety Hazards** (`safety_hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable hazards present on the site, such as 'Yes' for hazards that exist and 'No' for areas where they do not.
* **Site Images** (`site_images`, text, optional): Add any images or photos of the site, which can be helpful for further reference.
* **Site Documents** (`site_documents`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents related to the site, such as permits, licenses, or contracts.
* **Site Notes** (`site_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the site.
* **Site Contacts** (`site_contacts`, text, optional): Enter the names and contact information of individuals involved with the site.
* **Site Comments** (`site_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or observations about the site.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed, if applicable.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about a field, please reach out to your project team or manager for guidance.
