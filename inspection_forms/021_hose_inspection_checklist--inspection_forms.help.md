# hose_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to perform routine inspections of hoses, ensuring compliance with regulations and maintaining a high level of safety and maintenance standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the relevant fields, and use the options provided to select the correct answers.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, as indicated by the "required" label on each field.
3. Use the space provided for additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Routine Hose Checks**: This field is used to record routine checks performed on the hose. Please enter the information in the text field.
* **Checklist Items**: Please select 'Yes' if the required checklist items are present, or 'No' if they are not. This will help in maintaining a comprehensive record of the hose's condition.
* **Maintenance Equipment**: Select 'Yes' if the necessary maintenance equipment is present, or 'No' if it is not. This field is crucial for ensuring the hose is in good working condition.
* **Safety Equipment**: Select 'Yes' if the required safety equipment is present, or 'No' if it is not. This field helps in identifying potential hazards and ensuring the hose is safe to use.
* **Photo Documentation**: This field is for attaching photo documentation of the hose's condition. Please provide a clear and descriptive photo.
* **Submission Records**: Record any submission records, such as certification or approval documents, related to the hose.
* **Assigned Team**: Select 'Yes' if the hose is assigned to a specific team, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Notes**: Use this field for any additional comments or notes related to the hose's condition or maintenance.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you in case of any further queries or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure you can be easily contacted in case of any emergencies or updates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant personnel or refer to the relevant documentation.
* This form is a comprehensive tool for performing routine inspections and maintaining accurate records.
