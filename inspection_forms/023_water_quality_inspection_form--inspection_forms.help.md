# Water Quality Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Quality Inspection Form is used to record and report water quality parameters from various water bodies. This form is intended for data collection, analysis, and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Site ID to identify the water body.
2. Enter the Site Latitude and Site Longitude to locate the water body.
3. Enter the Sample ID to track the sample's collection details.
4. Enter the Date Collected date in the correct format.
5. Choose the type of water body (Groundwater, Surface Water, Spring, Well, or Reservoir) from the Water Body Type dropdown menu.
6. Enter the pH Level reading.
7. Enter the Turbidity reading.
8. Enter the Dissolved Oxygen reading.
9. Enter the Temperature reading.
10. Enter the Conductivity reading.
11. Enter the Total Dissolved Solids reading.
12. Choose the units for Total Dissolved Solids.
13. Enter the Total Hardness reading.
14. Enter the Alkalinity reading.
15. Enter the Free Cyanide reading.
16. Enter the Fixed Cyanide reading.
17. Enter the Total Cyanide reading.
18. Enter the Free Ammonia reading.
19. Enter the Fixed Ammonia reading.
20. Enter the Total Ammonia reading.
21. Choose the units for Total Ammonia.
22. Enter any additional Notes about the sample.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site ID** (text, required): This field is used to identify the water body.
* **Site Latitude** (text, required): Enter the latitude coordinate of the water body location.
* **Site Longitude** (text, required): Enter the longitude coordinate of the water body location.
* **Sample ID** (text, required): This field is used to track the sample's collection details.
* **Date Collected** (date, required): Enter the date the sample was collected.
* **Water Body** (text, required): This field is used to select the type of water body (Groundwater, Surface Water, Spring, Well, or Reservoir).
* **Water Body Type** (select multiple, required): Choose the type of water body from the provided options.
* **pH Level** (number, required): Enter the pH reading of the water sample.
* **Turbidity** (number, required): Enter the turbidity reading of the water sample.
* **Dissolved Oxygen** (number, required): Enter the dissolved oxygen reading of the water sample.
* **Temperature** (number, required): Enter the temperature reading of the water sample.
* **Conductivity** (number, required): Enter the conductivity reading of the water sample.
* **Total Dissolved Solids** (number, required): Enter the total dissolved solids reading of the water sample.
* **Total Dissolved Solids Units** (text, optional): Choose the units for the total dissolved solids reading.
* **Total Hardness** (number, required): Enter the total hardness reading of the water sample.
* **Alkalinity** (number, required): Enter the alkalinity reading of the water sample.
* **Free Cyanide** (number, required): Enter the free cyanide reading of the water sample.
* **Fixed Cyanide** (number, required): Enter the fixed cyanide reading of the water sample.
* **Total Cyanide** (number, required): Enter the total cyanide reading of the water sample.
* **Free Ammonia** (number, required): Enter the free ammonia reading of the water sample.
* **Fixed Ammonia** (number, required): Enter the fixed ammonia reading of the water sample.
* **Total Ammonia** (number, required): Enter the total ammonia reading of the water sample.
* **Total Ammonia Units** (text, optional): Choose the units for the total ammonia reading.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the sample.
