# COVID 19 Private Inspections Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Private Inspections Disclosure Form is a critical tool for inspectors to capture essential information about a property's COVID-19-related status. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate response.
3. Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy.
4. Complete the form for each inspection or update as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name** (text, required): This field is used to record the name of the inspector conducting the inspection. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Inspector ID** (text, optional): This field is used to record your official inspector ID number or license number. If you don't have an ID, please leave this field blank.
* **Inspection Agency** (text, required): This field is used to record the name of the agency or company conducting the inspection. Please enter the official name as it appears on your business cards or stationery.
* **Email Address** (email, required): This field is used to record your official email address. Please ensure that this is your primary email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): This field is used to record your official phone number. Please ensure that this is your primary phone number where you can be reached.
* **Property Address** (text, required): This field is used to record the address of the property being inspected. Please enter the full address as it appears on the property's records.
* **Property Owner Name** (text, required): This field is used to record the name of the property owner or representative. Please ensure that this is the correct name as it appears on the property's records.
* **Property Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of property being inspected. Options include: Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Mixed use, or Other.
* **Inspection Date** (date, required): This field is used to record the date of the inspection. Please ensure that this is the correct date of the inspection.
* **Known COVID-19 cases at property?** (select_one, required): Select whether there have been known COVID-19 cases at the property. Options include: False, True.
* **Details of COVID-19 cases** (text, optional): If there have been known COVID-19 cases at the property, please provide detailed information about the cases.
* **Property or occupants currently in quarantine?** (select_one, required): Select whether the property or its occupants are currently in quarantine. Options include: False, True.
* **COVID-19 Safety Measures in Place** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable safety measures that are currently in place at the property. Options include: Sanitization protocol, Ventilation improvements, Isolation areas, PPE available, Hand sanitizer stations, Social distancing markers, Deep cleaning schedule, Mask requirements, Other.
* **COVID-19 Safety Compliance Status** (select_one, required): Select the COVID-19 safety compliance status of the property. Options include: Compliant, Needs improvement, Non-compliant, Unable to determine.
* **Detailed Findings** (text, required): This field is used to record the detailed findings of the inspection. Please provide a comprehensive report of the property's COVID-19-related status.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (text, optional): This field is used to record any recommendations for improvement for the property. Please provide detailed suggestions for addressing any COVID-19-related concerns.
* **Inspector Signature Confirmation** (select_one, required): Select whether you confirm the accuracy of this report. Options include: I confirm the accuracy of this report, I do not confirm this report.
* **Report Date** (date, required): This field is used to record the date of the report. Please ensure that this is the correct date of the report.
