# painting_inspection_record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "painting_inspection_record" form is a tool for documenting the details of a painting-related inspection. It captures information on inspection date and time, inspector and customer contact details, and the status of the inspection. This form ensures that all relevant information is recorded accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date and time of the inspection from the calendar or time picker provided.
2. Enter the name and contact details of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
3. Enter the customer's name and contact details.
4. Choose the method of contact used during the inspection.
5. Identify if the contact person was present during the inspection.
6. Select the status of the inspection (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
8. Specify the type of painting inspected.
9. Enter the color of the inspected area.
10. Provide notes on the color quality.
11. Indicate the condition of the inspected surface.
12. List any surface defects found during the inspection.
13. Enter the area inspected.
14. Specify the area covered by the inspector.
15. Choose whether the area inspected was fully covered or not.
16. Enter any color codes relevant to the inspected area.
17. Provide comments on the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspector Contact** (`inspector_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of the inspector.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, required/optional): Choose how you contacted the customer or area manager during the inspection.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate if the contact person was present during the inspection.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the customer or area manager.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of the customer or area manager.
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the inspection (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Painting Type** (`painting_type`, text, required/optional): Specify the type of painting inspected.
* **Color** (`color`, text, required/optional): Enter the color of the inspected area.
* **Color Notes** (`color_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes on the color quality.
* **Color Quality** (`color_quality`, select one, required/optional): Select the quality of the color.
* **Surface Condition** (`surface_condition`, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate the condition of the inspected surface.
* **Surface Defects** (`surface_defects`, select multiple, required/optional): List any surface defects found during the inspection.
* **Surface Area** (`surface_area`, text, required/optional): Enter the area inspected.
* **Area Covered** (`area_covered`, number, required/optional): Enter the area covered by the inspector.
* **Area Covered Unit** (`area_covered_unit`, select one, required/optional): Specify if the area inspected was fully covered or not.
* **Color Code** (`color_code`, text, required/optional): Enter any color codes relevant to the inspected area.
* **Inspection Comments** (`inspection_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments on the inspection.

Note: This help guide assumes that each field is optional unless otherwise specified as 'required'. Please ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure the form's integrity and usefulness.
