# Fishing Boat Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Boat Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the safety and regulatory compliance of a fishing boat. This form helps ensure that the boat meets the necessary standards for safe operation and compliance with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Provide detailed and accurate information about the fishing boat.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the required field label.
4. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect any general information about the fishing boat.
* **Captain and Crew** (`captain_and_crew`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the captain and crew of the fishing boat, including their names, positions, and contact details.
* **Boat Details** (`boat_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the boat's dimensions, type, and registration number.
* **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the safety features of the boat, such as life jackets, emergency beacons, and fire extinguishers.
* **Fire Fighting Equipment** (`fire_fighting_equipment`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the fire fighting equipment on board, including type, quantity, and location.
* **First Aid Equipment** (`first_aid_equipment`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the first aid equipment on board, including type, quantity, and location.
* **Navigation and Signalization** (`navigation_and_signalization`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the navigation and signalization equipment on board, including GPS, radio communication, and other navigation aids.
* **Communication Equipment** (`communication_equipment`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the communication equipment on board, including radios, satellite phones, and other communication devices.
* **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a summary of the inspection result, including any observations, comments, or recommendations.
* **Certification and Training** (`certification_and_training`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the captain's and crew's certifications and training, including any relevant certifications, licenses, and training hours.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional comments or observations about the fishing boat.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): This field is used for the inspector's digital signature, indicating that the inspection has been completed and reviewed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor.
* The completed form should be reviewed and signed by the inspector before being submitted.
* The form is intended for official use only and should not be used for recreational or non-regulatory purposes.
