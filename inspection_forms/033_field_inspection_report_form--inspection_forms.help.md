# Field Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Inspection Report Form is used by site inspectors to document the results of field inspections. This form collects information about the date, location, and status of the inspection, as well as other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `date_of_inspection` field with the actual date of the inspection.
2. Enter the `inspection_location` in the text field provided.
3. Select the `site_visit_type` that best describes the type of inspection performed (e.g., site visit, field visit, or other).
4. Select the `inspection_status` from the dropdown menu to indicate the status of the inspection (e.g., complete, incomplete, not started).
5. Enter any relevant `inspection_notes` about the inspection.
6. Fill in the `inspector_name` field with the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
7. Enter the `inspector_phone` and `inspector_email` fields with the contact information of the inspector.
8. If an inspection was assigned to another person, fill in the `assigned_inspector` fields with their name, phone, and email.
9. Enter any `assigned_inspector_comments` made by the assigned inspector.
10. If applicable, enter the `site_visit_id` number assigned to the inspection.
11. Fill in the `client` field with the name of the client or company being inspected.
12. Enter any comments from the inspector in the `inspector_comments` field.
13. Finally, click the submit button to save the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inspection_report_form**:
	+ This field is the title of the report.
* **date_of_inspection**:
	+ Enter the actual date of the inspection.
	+ Format: mm/dd/yyyy
* **inspector_name**:
	+ Enter the name of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **inspection_location**:
	+ Enter the location of the inspection (e.g., site address).
* **inspector_comments**:
	+ Enter any comments from the inspector about the inspection.
* **inspection_status**:
	+ Select the status of the inspection (e.g., complete, incomplete, not started).
	+ Options: complete, incomplete, not started
* **comments**:
	+ Enter any comments made by others about the inspection.
* **inspection_notes**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about the inspection.
* **inspection_date**:
	+ Enter the date of the inspection (format: dd/mm/yyyy).
* **inspector_phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **inspector_email**:
	+ Enter the email of the inspector performing the inspection.
* **assigned_inspector**:
	+ Enter the name of the inspector assigned to the inspection.
* **assigned_inspector_phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the inspector assigned to the inspection.
* **assigned_inspector_email**:
	+ Enter the email of the inspector assigned to the inspection.
* **assigned_inspector_comments**:
	+ Enter any comments from the assigned inspector.
* **site_visit_id**:
	+ Enter the site visit ID number assigned to the inspection.
* **site_visit_type**:
	+ Select the type of inspection performed (e.g., site visit, field visit, other).
	+ Options: site visit, field visit, other
* **client**:
	+ Enter the name of the client or company being inspected.
* **inspector_comments**:
	+ Enter any comments from the inspector.
* **assigned_inspector_comments**:
	+ Enter any comments from the assigned inspector.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please refer back to this help guide for clarification.
