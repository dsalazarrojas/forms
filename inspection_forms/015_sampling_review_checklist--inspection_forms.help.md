# Sampling Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sampling Review Checklist form is used to gather essential information about the sampling process, including sampling details, sampling steps, and sampling dates and times. This form helps to ensure that the sampling process is documented accurately and consistently, which is crucial for quality control and audit purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the sampling form details, which includes providing a brief description of the sampling process.
2. Select the sampling step(s) that was performed.
3. Enter the sampling date and time.
4. Provide a brief description of the sample details.
5. Document any review steps taken.
6. Enter any relevant sampling records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sampling Form Details** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_1`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the sampling process, such as the sampling method and purpose.
* **Sampling Step** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_2`, `text`, optional): The specific step or steps performed during the sampling process, such as inspection or testing.
* **Approvals** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable options that represent approval or endorsement of the sampling process.
* **Sampling Date** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_4`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the sampling process took place.
* **Sampling Time** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_5`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the sampling process took place.
* **Sample Details** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_6`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the sample or material being sampled.
* **Review Steps** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_7`, `text`, optional): A brief description of any review steps taken during the sampling process.
* **Sampling Records** (`sampling_review_checklist_form_8`, `text`, optional): Any additional information about the sampling records, such as documentation or certification.
