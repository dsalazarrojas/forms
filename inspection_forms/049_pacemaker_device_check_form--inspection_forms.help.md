# Pacemaker Device Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about pacemaker devices, allowing healthcare professionals to monitor and track their status. It's a one-time use form, intended for collecting data from a single patient at a time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please indicate if the patient has given their consent for the device follow-up.
2. Enter the date the device was last checked.
3. Enter the date for the next check.
4. Provide any comments about the device's condition.
5. Record the device model.
6. Enter the patient's name.
7. Provide the patient's birthday.
8. Enter the date when the device was implanted.
9. Specify the status of the device (Active, Inactive, Discontinued, Removed, or Scheduled for Removal).
10. Enter the serial number of the device.
11. Note any next device follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent** (`Patient Consent`, select_one, required): Check if the patient has given consent for the device follow-up.
* **Date Last Checked** (`Date Last Checked`, date, required): Enter the date the pacemaker device was last checked.
* **Date Next Check** (`Date Next Check`, date, required): Enter the date for the next check on the pacemaker device.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required): Provide any comments about the device's condition.
* **Device Model** (`Device Model`, text, required): Enter the model of the pacemaker device.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Birthday** (`Patient Birthday`, date, required): Enter the patient's birthday.
* **Device Implant Date** (`Device Implant Date`, date, required): Enter the date when the pacemaker device was implanted.
* **Device Status** (`Device Status`, select_multiple, required): Specify the status of the device (Active, Inactive, Discontinued, Removed, or Scheduled for Removal).
* **Device Serial Number** (`Device Serial Number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the pacemaker device.
* **Next Device Follow-up** (`Next Device Follow-up`, text, required): Note any next device follow-up information.
* **Check By** (`Check By`, date, required): Enter the date when the device was last checked.

## Tips

* Make sure to update the "Date Last Checked" and "Date Next Check" fields accurately to ensure proper monitoring of the pacemaker device.
* If the patient's birthday is not known, enter "Unknown" in the "Patient Birthday" field.
* When selecting the device status, choose the most accurate option to ensure correct tracking.
