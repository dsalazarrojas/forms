# Stop Box Measurement Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition of a stop box, its measurement data, and any comments or photos taken during an inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    1. Start by reviewing the stop box's condition and collecting any measurement data relevant to the stop box.
    2. Enter the condition of the stop box as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "condition_details" field.
    3. If you have taken photos during the inspection, upload them in the "photo_upload" field.
    4. Enter any comments or additional information about the stop box in the "comments" field.
    5. Enter your name and signature in the "inspector_signature", "inspector_name", and "inspector_phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Condition Details (condition_details)**: Select the condition of the stop box from the options "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Stop Box Measurements (stop_box_measurements)**: Enter any relevant measurement data about the stop box.
*   **Photo Upload (photo_upload)**: Upload any photos taken during the inspection that are relevant to the stop box.
*   **Comments (comments)**: Enter any additional information or comments about the stop box.
*   **Inspector Signature (inspector_signature)**: Enter your signature as the inspector.
*   **Inspector Name (inspector_name)**: Enter your name as the inspector.
*   **Inspector Phone (inspector_phone)**: Enter your phone number as the inspector.
