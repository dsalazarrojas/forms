# Aircraft Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aircraft Inspection Checklist is a form designed to assess the airworthiness of an aircraft after maintenance, repair, or inspection. This form helps inspectors ensure that all critical systems and components are functioning correctly and safely. It's a standard tool for verifying that an aircraft is airworthy, which means it's safe to fly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Inspector Name** in the first field. This is your name and contact information.
2. Enter the **Inspector ID** in the second field. This is your certificate or badge number.
3. Select the **Inspection Date** in the third field. This is the date and time of the inspection.
4. Choose the **Inspection Type** in the fourth field. Select the type of inspection you're performing (e.g., pre-flight, post-flight, annual, etc.).
5. Enter the **Aircraft Registration** in the fifth field. This is the aircraft's tail number (e.g., N12345).
6. Enter the **Aircraft Make** in the sixth field. This is the aircraft's manufacturer (e.g., Boeing, Cessna, etc.).
7. Enter the **Aircraft Model** in the seventh field. This is the specific model of the aircraft (e.g., 737, Cessna 172, etc.).
8. Enter the **Serial Number** in the eighth field. This is the aircraft's serial number.
9. Enter the total **Time Airframe** in the ninth field. This is the number of hours the aircraft has been in service.
10. Select the overall **Exterior Condition** of the aircraft in the tenth field. Choose one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
11. Check each **Fuselage Inspection** field (11) to assess the aircraft's fuselage structure. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
12. Check each **Wings Inspection** field (12) to assess the aircraft's wing structure and surfaces. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
13. Check each **Empennage Inspection** field (13) to assess the aircraft's empennage (tail) surfaces. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
14. Check each **Landing Gear Inspection** field (14) to assess the aircraft's landing gear and brakes. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
15. Select the overall **Tires Condition** in the fifteenth field. Choose one of the four options: Good, Fair, Needs replacement, or Unsatisfactory.
16. Select the **Brakes Condition** in the sixteenth field. Choose one of the four options: Good, Fair, Needs adjustment, or Unsatisfactory.
17. Check the **Engine Exterior** field (17) for leaks or damage. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
18. Check each **Propeller Inspection** field (18) for propeller blades and hub condition. Select one of the five options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, Unsatisfactory, or Not applicable.
19. Check each **Flight Controls** field (19) to ensure all control surfaces are functioning correctly. Select one of the four options: All functional, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
20. Check each **Lights Check** field (20) to ensure all lights are operational. Select one of the four options: All functional, Some out, Multiple failures, or Unsatisfactory.
21. Check the **Pitot-Static System** field (21) to ensure ports and covers are clear. Select one of the four options: Clear and uncovered, Covered, Removed, Damaged.
22. Select the **Fluid Levels** field (22) to check oil and hydraulic fluid levels. Choose one of the four options: All adequate, Some low, Needs service, or Unsatisfactory.
23. Check the **Cockpit Inspection** field (23) to ensure all instruments and controls are functional. Select one of the four options: Satisfactory, Minor issues, Major issues, or Unsatisfactory.
24. Check the **Avionics Check** field (24) to ensure all avionics equipment is functioning correctly. Select one of the four options: All functional, Some issues, Multiple failures, or Unsatisfactory.
25. Check the **Emergency Equipment** field (25) to ensure all emergency equipment is present. Select one of the four options: All present, Some missing, Multiple missing, or Unsatisfactory.
26. Check the **Required Documents** field (26) to ensure all required documents are on board. Select one of the four options: All present, Some missing, Expired, or Unsatisfactory.
27. Enter any **Discrepancies Found** in the twenty-seventh field. List all issues or problems encountered during the inspection.
28. Enter any **Corrective Action** taken or needed in the twenty-eighth field. Describe any actions taken or required to address issues found.
29. Select the final **Airworthiness Status** in the twenty-ninth field. Choose one of the three options: Airworthy, Airworthy with limitations, or Not airworthy.
30. Enter your **Inspector Signature** in the thirtieth field. This is your digital signature.
30. Select the **Inspection Approved** status in the thirty-first field. Choose one of the three options: Approved, Pending review, or Not approved.

Note: The form will automatically save and update as you fill it out. Make sure to review and update the form carefully to ensure accurate information.

## Tips
* Double-check each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Be thorough in your assessment of each system and component.
* Take clear photos or make notes of any issues or problems found.
* Address any discrepancies or issues promptly.
* Review and update the form carefully to ensure accurate information before finalizing the inspection.
