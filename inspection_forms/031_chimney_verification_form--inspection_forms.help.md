# Chimney Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from verifiers about the condition and safety of chimneys during inspections or maintenance checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Verifier Name**: This is the name of the person verifying the condition and safety of the chimney.
2. Select **Verification Date**: This is the date on which the verification occurred.
3. Enter **Site Address**: This is the location where the verification took place.
4. Select the **Purpose of Verification**: This is the reason for the verification, such as a sale inspection or safety check.
5. Report on the **Flue Condition**: This is the current state of the flue (e.g., good, marginal, poor).
6. Note the **Chimney Cap and Chase Condition**: If present, describe its condition. If not, indicate its absence.
7. Enter the **Verification Result**: This is the final determination of the chimney's safety.
8. Provide any additional **Notes**: Any further comments or observations about the verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Verifier Name** (`Verifier Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person verifying the chimney's condition and safety.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the verification occurred.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property where the verification took place.
* **Purpose of Verification** (`verification_purpose`, text, required): Select the reason for the verification, such as sale inspection or safety check.
* **Flue Condition** (`flue_condition`, select_one, required): Rate the condition of the flue as good, marginal, or poor.
* **Chimney Cap and Chase Condition** (`cap_and_chase`, text, optional): If present, describe the condition of the chimney cap and chase. If not present, indicate its absence.
* **Verification Result** (`verification_result`, select_one, required): Select the final verification outcome: Verified safe, Repairs required, or Not safe.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the verification.
