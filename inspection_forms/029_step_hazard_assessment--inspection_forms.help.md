# step_hazard_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Step Hazard Assessment form is used to identify and document potential hazards present on each step of a manufacturing process. This form helps in providing a structured way to assess and report on hazards, allowing for proactive risk management and improvement of workplace safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the step being assessed and identify any potential hazards.
2. Select the hazards present on the step using the options provided.
3. Rate the hazard using the hazard rating field.
4. Provide a brief description of your recommendation to mitigate or control the hazard.
5. Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the hazard.
6. Assign the assessment to a specific person or team.
7. Record the location of the step being assessed.
8. Provide the date and time of the assessment.
9. Identify the person performing the assessment.
10. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Step Hazard Assessment**: This is the main section of the form where you can review and assess the hazards present on the step.
	+ **Step Number**: Enter the number of the step being assessed.
	+ **Step Hazard**: Select the hazards present on the step from the options provided.
* **Hazard Rating**: Rate the hazard using a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendation**: Describe your recommendation to mitigate or control the hazard.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the hazard.
* **Assigned To**: Identify the person or team responsible for addressing the hazard.
* **Location**: Record the location of the step being assessed.
* **Date**: Record the date of the assessment.
* **Time**: Record the time of the assessment.
* **Inspector**: Identify the person performing the assessment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and assess each step carefully to identify potential hazards.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting and rating hazards.
* Provide clear and concise recommendations for mitigating or controlling hazards.
* Use the comments field for additional information or notes that may not fit in the recommendation field.
* Ensure that the person or team responsible for addressing the hazard is correctly identified.
* Review and update the assessment regularly to reflect any changes or updates.
