# Power Amplifier Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Amplifier Unit Inspection Form is used to inspect and track the condition and status of power amplifier units. It helps to ensure that these units are functioning correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the power amplifier unit being inspected.
2. Describe the inspection details, including any issues or findings.
3. Sign off the form once you've completed the inspection.
4. Record the results of the inspection, including any necessary comments or notes.
5. Add any necessary comments from the technician or facility team.
6. Select the status of the form, either Complete, Incomplete, or Deferred.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
8. Enter the power rating of the unit, if applicable.
9. Enter the voltage rating of the unit, if applicable.
10. Enter the current rating of the unit, if applicable.
11. Select the input and output select multiple options, as applicable.
12. Add any additional comments or notes.
13. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person.
14. Select the team responsible for the assigned task, either Tech Team, Facility Team, or Service Team.
15. Select the person or team that assigned the task, either Tech Team, Facility Team, or Service Team.
16. Enter the date of the inspection.
17. Enter the time of the inspection.
18. Select the type of equipment being inspected, either Power Transformer, Power Rectifier, or Power Regulator.
19. Enter the model number of the equipment.
20. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
21. Specify the inspection frequency, if applicable.
22. Add any additional comments or notes.
