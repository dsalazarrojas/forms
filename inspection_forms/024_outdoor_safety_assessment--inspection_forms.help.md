# Outdoor Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from field staff to report and record outdoor safety incidents.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page in the form. Each question is designed to help us gather as much information as possible about the incident.
2. Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked as such on the form.
3. If a question does not apply, please mark the corresponding box as "No" or "N/A".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What areas are checked?**: Please select all areas that were checked during the incident. This could include equipment, vehicles, or personnel. You can select multiple areas.
* **Hazards and Materials On Site**: Please select all hazards and materials that were on site during the incident. This could include chemicals, machinery, or other potential hazards. You can select multiple hazards.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who witnessed or reported the incident.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency phone number of the incident location.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Emergency Phone (again)**: This is a duplicate field. Please enter the same emergency phone number as in the previous field.
* **Incident Reported**: Date and time of when the incident was reported, not when it occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred. Please use the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred. This could include equipment failure, personnel injury, or other types of incidents.
* **Upload Photo**: This field is not required. If you have evidence of the incident, please upload a photo to support your report.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your supervisor.
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If the incident is still ongoing, please report it as soon as possible.
