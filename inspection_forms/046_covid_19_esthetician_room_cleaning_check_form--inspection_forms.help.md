## COVID 19 Esthetician Room Cleaning Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Esthetician Room Cleaning Check Form is designed to ensure that esthetician rooms are thoroughly cleaned and disinfected regularly to maintain a safe environment for clients and staff. This form helps inspectors check the cleanliness and organization of the room, including the condition of surfaces, linens, equipment, and other essential facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of the cleaning inspection in the "Cleaning Check Date" and "Time of Check" fields.
2. Enter the room number and esthetician name in the "Room Number" and "Esthetician Name" fields.
3. Check the boxes for the different areas of the room that require attention, such as hard surfaces, linens, equipment, and others.
4. Answer the questions about the condition of the room, including whether hard surfaces are disinfected, linens are changed, and equipment is sanitized.
5. Provide an overall cleanliness rating and list any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
6. Check the boxes for any corrective actions needed and whether a follow-up check is required.
7. Sign off on the room's approval for use, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Cleaning Check Date** (`check_date`, `date`, required)
Enter the date of the cleaning inspection.
### 2. **Time of Check** (`check_time`, `time`, optional)
Enter the time of the cleaning inspection.
### 3. **Room Number** (`room_number`, `text`, required)
Enter the room number to identify the esthetician room.
### 4. **Esthetician Name** (`esthetician_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the staff member using this room.
### 5. **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the person conducting the check.
### 6. **Cleaning Staff** (`cleaning_staff`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of the person who performed the cleaning.
### 7. **Hard Surfaces Disinfected** (`surfaces_disinfected`, `select_one`, required)
Check if hard surfaces such as tables, chairs, door handles, etc. are disinfected.
### 8. **Linens and Towels Changed** (`linens_changed`, `select_one`, required)
Check if linens and towels are clean and provided.
### 9. **Equipment Sanitized** (`equipment_sanitized`, `select_one`, required)
Check if equipment such as mirrors, lights, tools, and beds are sanitized.
### 10. **Facial Bed Clean and Protected** (`facial_bed_clean`, `select_one`, required)
Check if the facial bed is clean and has a protective covering.
### 11. **Floors Cleaned** (`floors_cleaned`, `select_one`, required)
Check if the floors are swept and mopped.
### 12. **Biohazard Waste Properly Disposed** (`waste_disposed`, `select_one`, required)
Check if biohazard waste is properly disposed of in containers.
### 13. **Sanitizer Supplies Stocked** (`sanitizer_supplies`, `select_one`, required)
Check if hand sanitizer and disinfectant supplies are sufficient.
### 14. **Ventilation/Air Circulation Working** (`air_ventilation`, `select_one`, optional)
Check if the ventilation system is working properly.
### 15. **Air Purifier or Filter Operating** (`air_purifier`, `select_one`, optional)
Check if the air purifier or filter is working (if applicable).
### 16. **Trash Bins Emptied** (`trash_emptied`, `select_one`, required)
Check if trash bins are emptied.
### 17. **Supplies Organized and Stored Properly** (`supplies_organized`, `select_one`, optional)
Check if supplies are organized and stored properly.
### 18. **Any Unpleasant Odors Detected** (`odors_present`, `select_one`, optional)
Check if any unpleasant odors are detected in the room.
### 19. **Overall Cleanliness Rating** (`overall_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the overall cleanliness of the room (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
### 20. **Issues or Concerns Found** (`issues_found`, `text`, optional)
List any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
### 21. **Corrective Actions Needed** (`corrective_actions`, `text`, optional)
Provide corrective actions needed to address any issues found.
### 22. **Follow-Up Check Required** (`follow_up_required`, `select_one`, required)
Check if a follow-up check is required for the room.
### 23. **Inspector Approval** (`signature_approval`, `select_one`, required)
Sign off on the room's approval for use (or not).
