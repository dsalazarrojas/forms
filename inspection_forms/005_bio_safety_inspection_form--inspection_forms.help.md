# Bio Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bio Safety Inspection Form is a tool used to inspect labs and identify potential biosafety issues. This form ensures that lab personnel are following biosafety guidelines and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Inspection Details** section with the relevant information about the inspection.
2. Enter your **Inspection Name** in the Inspector Name field.
3. Enter the **Inspection Date** in the date field.
4. Enter the **Lab Location or Room** number in the Lab Location or Room field.
5. Enter the **Area Inspected** in the Area Inspected field.
6. Select the applicable **Biosafety Level** from the dropdown list.
7. Select whether **PPE Compliance** is compliant, non-compliant, or not applicable.
8. Indicate if **Contamination Was Observed**, selecting one of the options.
9. If immediate actions were taken, enter them in the **Immediate Actions Taken** field. (Note: This field is optional.)
10. Indicate if **Corrective Action Is Required**, selecting one of the options.
11. If a follow-up inspection is required, enter the **Follow-up Date** in the date field. (Note: This field is optional.)
12. Enter any **Inspector Comments** in the Inspector Comments field. (Note: This field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name** (id 2): Enter your name as the inspector conducting the review.
* **Inspection Date** (id 3): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Lab Location or Room** (id 4): Enter the building and room number for the lab.
* **Area Inspected** (id 5): Enter the specific bench, hood, or storage area inspected.
* **Biosafety Level** (id 6): Select the applicable containment level from the dropdown list.
* **PPE Compliance** (id 7): Select whether personnel are following PPE requirements.
* **Contamination Observed** (id 8): Indicate if evidence of spills or contamination was observed.
* **Immediate Actions Taken** (id 9): Describe any immediate actions taken during the inspection. (Optional)
* **Corrective Action Required** (id 10): Indicate if follow-up or corrective work is needed.
* **Follow-up Date** (id 11): Enter the date for re-inspection or verification. (Optional)
* **Inspector Comments** (id 12): Enter any additional observations or recommendations. (Optional)
