# vehicle_inspection_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quiz designed to test users' knowledge about vehicle inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of vehicle you are inspecting from the **vehicle type** field.
2.  Enter the year of the vehicle in the **vehicle year** field.
3.  Select the engine type of the vehicle from the **engine type** field.
4.  Choose the transmission type of the vehicle from the **transmission type** field.
5.  Select the current status of the vehicle inspection from the **vehicle inspection status** field.
6.  Enter the date of the vehicle inspection in the **inspection date** field.
7.  Enter the time of the vehicle inspection in the **inspection time** field.
8.  You may add any additional comments in the **vehicle inspection knowledge quiz end** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **vehicle type** (select one): Select the type of vehicle you are inspecting.
*   **vehicle year** (number): Enter the year of the vehicle.
*   **engine type** (select one): Choose the engine type of the vehicle.
*   **transmission type** (select one): Select the transmission type of the vehicle.
*   **vehicle inspection status** (select multiple): Select the current status of the vehicle inspection.
*   **inspection date** (date): Enter the date of the vehicle inspection.
*   **inspection time** (time): Enter the time of the vehicle inspection.
*   **vehicle inspection knowledge quiz end** (note): Add any additional comments.

## Tips
This quiz is designed to test your knowledge about vehicle inspections, so ensure you answer accurately to the best of your abilities. If you're unsure about any field, refer to your vehicle inspection guidelines or seek clarification from your supervisor.
