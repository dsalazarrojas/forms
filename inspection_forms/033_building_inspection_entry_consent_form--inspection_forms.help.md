# Building Inspection Entry Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide necessary information and authorization for a scheduled building inspection, ensuring that all parties are aware of the inspection details and any potential risks or hazards present on the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the property owner or landlord's name and address.
2. Provide the tenant's information, if applicable.
3. Select the type of inspection (e.g., routine maintenance, annual, pre-lease, etc.).
4. Specify the purpose of the inspection.
5. Enter the inspector's name, email, and phone number.
6. Schedule the inspection by selecting a date and time.
7. Choose how the inspector will access the property (e.g., with a key, with the owner's presence, etc.).
8. Confirm that the property owner or tenant is aware of and authorizes the inspection.
9. Acknowledge that you have assumed responsibility for property access and potential risks.
10. Provide any additional information or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Building Inspection Entry Consent --**: This section serves as a title and introduction to the form.
* **Property owner or landlord name**: Enter the name of the property owner or landlord.
	+ Hint: This field is required to ensure the inspector knows who to contact for information or permission.
* **Property address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
	+ Hint: This field is required for location and identification purposes.
* **Unit number or apartment (if applicable)**: Enter the unit number or apartment number, if applicable.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the property owner or landlord.
	+ Hint: This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter the phone number of the property owner or landlord.
	+ Hint: This field is required for communication purposes.
* **Tenant name**: Enter the name of the tenant, if applicable.
	+ Hint: This field is only required if a tenant is present on the property.
* **Tenant contact information**: Enter the contact information of the tenant (e.g., phone number and email).
* **Inspection type**: Select the type of inspection (e.g., routine, annual, pre-lease, etc.).
	+ Hint: This field is required to determine the scope and purpose of the inspection.
* **Inspection purpose**: Describe the reason for the inspection (e.g., pre-lease, maintenance, etc.).
	+ Hint: This field is required to provide context for the inspection.
* **Inspector name**: Enter the name of the inspector or inspection company.
	+ Hint: This field is required to ensure the inspector knows who is conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector email**: Enter the email address of the inspector or inspection company.
* **Inspector phone number**: Enter the phone number of the inspector or inspection company.
* **Inspector license or credentials**: Enter the inspector's license or credentials, if applicable.
* **Inspection scheduling information**: Schedule the inspection by selecting a date and time.
	+ Hint: This field is required to ensure the inspection occurs at the correct time.
* **Preferred time window**: Select the preferred time window for the inspection (e.g., early morning, late morning, etc.).
* **Estimated inspection duration**: Select the estimated duration of the inspection (e.g., under 30 minutes, 1-2 hours, etc.).
* **How will inspector access the property?**: Select how the inspector will access the property (e.g., with a key, with the owner's presence, etc.).
* **Property owner/tenant presence required**: Confirm whether the property owner or tenant must be present during the inspection.
* **Restricted areas or access limitations**: Enter any restricted areas or access limitations on the property.
* **Pets present on property**: Indicate whether pets are present on the property.
* **Security system present**: Confirm whether a security system is present on the property.
* **Hazardous materials or special hazards**: Indicate whether there are hazardous materials or special hazards present on the property.
* **Inspector liability insurance**: Confirm whether the inspector has liability insurance.
* **Consent and Authorization**: Confirm that you authorize the inspection.
* **Photography authorization**: Confirm that you authorize photography during the inspection.
* **Report distribution**: Confirm how the report will be distributed.
* **Liability acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that you have assumed responsibility for property access and potential risks.

Note: The following fields are only required if they are relevant to the specific inspection: Unit number or apartment, Tenant name, Tenant contact information, Tenant phone number, Inspection purpose, Inspector license or credentials, Security system present, Hazardous materials or special hazards, Inspector liability insurance, and Additional information or special requests.
