# Final Walkthrough Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Walkthrough Checklist is a form used to perform a thorough review of a property's systems and components before completion or handover.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you understand the purpose and scope of the inspection.
2. Select the correct status for each system or component from the dropdown menus.
3. Answer each question truthfully, taking into account any relevant documentation or records.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ Inactive: The system is not currently in use or not fully functional.

* Be thorough and detailed in your answers.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

* This form should only be used for properties that have not been modified or changed significantly since the initial inspection.
