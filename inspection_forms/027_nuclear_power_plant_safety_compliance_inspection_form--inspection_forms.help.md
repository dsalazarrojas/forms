# Nuclear Power Plant Safety Compliance Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nuclear Power Plant Safety Compliance Inspection Form is used for documenting and tracking the safety compliance inspection of a nuclear power plant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete the General Information section with the required information about the inspection team, plant operator, and safety manager.
3. Enter the contact information for the Inspection Team, Plant Operator, and Safety Manager in their respective fields.
4. In the Findings and Recommendations section, list any observations, findings, or recommendations for the plant's safety.
5. In the Follow-Ups section, list any follow-ups or actions required for the findings.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
7. If applicable, rate the plant's safety compliance in the Safety Compliance Rating section.
8. Select the Follow-Up Status for each finding in the Follow-Up Status section.
9. Enter the due date for each follow-up in the Due Date section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Text)**: This section collects basic contact information about the inspection team, plant operator, and safety manager.
* **Inspection Team (Text)**: This field is for the inspector to enter their name and contact information.
* **Plant Operator (Text)**: This field is for the plant operator to enter their contact information.
* **Safety Manager (Text)**: This field is for the safety manager to enter their contact information.
* **Findings and Recommendations (Text)**: List any observations, findings, or recommendations for the plant's safety.
* **Follow-Ups (Text)**: List any follow-ups or actions required for the findings.
* **Notes (Text)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Safety Compliance Rating (Number)**: Rate the plant's safety compliance (though no scale is defined).
* **Follow-Up Status (Select One)**: Select the status of the follow-up for each finding.
* **Due Date (Date)**: Enter the due date for each follow-up.

## Tips
* Ensure that all sections are completed thoroughly and accurately.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes and comments.
* Be sure to rate the plant's safety compliance honestly and fairly.
* Select the correct follow-up status and due date for each finding.
* This form is designed to be completed in conjunction with other safety compliance records and protocols.
