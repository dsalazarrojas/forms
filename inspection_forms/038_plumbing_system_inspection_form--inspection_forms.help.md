# plumbing_system_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The plumbing system inspection form is used to conduct a thorough inspection of a plumbing system and document any issues or defects found. This form helps to identify potential problems and ensure the system is functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately based on your observations.
2. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. Use additional pages (pages 2-4) to provide more detailed information as needed.
4. Ensure that all comments and observations are clearly written and easy to read.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - plumbing_system_inspection_form_page_1
* **plumbing_system_inspection_form_page_1** (Text, Required): This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of the purpose of the inspection.
 
### Page 2 - system_inspection_details
* **system_inspection_details** (Text, Required): Please provide a detailed description of the system's condition, including any issues or defects observed.
 
### Page 3 - inspectors_comments
* **inspectors_comments** (Text, Optional): Use this page to provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
 
### Page 4 - contractors_comments
* **contractors_comments** (Text, Optional): This page is for the contractor to provide their comments or observations about the inspection.

## Tips
* Take care to complete all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when providing comments or observations.
* Ensure all pages are filled out completely and accurately.
* Store the completed form in a safe and secure location to maintain record of the inspection results.
