# Athletic Field Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Athletic Field Inspection Form is designed to help inspect the condition of athletic fields before they are used for play. This form helps inspectors collect crucial information about the field's state, which can include the playing surface, safety hazards, and maintenance requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields:
   - **Field Name or Number**: Enter the name or number of the field being inspected.
   - **Facility Location**: Enter the location of the facility where the field is located.
   - **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
   - **Date of Inspection**: Choose the date when the inspection was conducted.
   - **Time of Inspection**: Choose the time when the inspection was conducted.
   - **Current Weather Conditions**: Select the current weather conditions of the field (e.g., Sunny / Clear, Overcast, Raining, Snow or Ice Present, High Winds).
   - **Surface Type**: Choose the type of playing surface (e.g., Natural Grass, Synthetic Turf, Clay or Dirt, Hard Court Surface).
   - **Surface Evenness and Leveling**: Rate the evenness and leveling of the playing surface from 1 (Poor / Uneven) to 5 (Perfectly Level).
   - **Presence of Holes, Divots, or Tripping Hazards?**: Check if holes, divots, or tripping hazards are present on the field.
   - **Grass Height or Turf Fiber Quality**: Rate the grass height or turf fiber quality from Excellent, Satisfactory, Needs Mowing or Brushing, to Significant Repair Needed.
   - **Drainage Issues or Standing Water Noted?**: Check if drainage issues or standing water is present.
   - **Goal Posts, Nets, and Fixings Condition**: Rate the condition of goal posts, nets, and fixings from Secure and Safe, Minor Repair Needed, to Damaged / Unsafe.
   - **Perimeter Fencing and Gates Condition**: Rate the condition of perimeter fencing and gates from Secure and Safe, Minor Repair Needed, to Damaged / Unsafe.
   - **Condition of Player Benches and Dugouts**: Rate the condition of player benches and dugouts from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
   - **Lighting System Functionality**: Choose the status of the lighting system (e.g., All Lights Working, Partial Failure, Non-Functional, Not Applicable).
   - **Debris or Trash Present on the Field?**: Check if debris or trash is present on the field.
   - **Obstructions in Designated Safety Zones?**: Check if obstructions are present in safety zones.
   - **Visibility of Safety and Rule Signage**: Rate the visibility of safety and rule signage from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
   - **Required Maintenance Actions**: Detail any maintenance actions needed to reach safe status.
   - **Field Play Status**: Choose the overall status of the field for immediate play (e.g., Open - Safe for Play, Conditional - Minor Repairs Needed, Closed - Unsafe for Play).
   - **Inspector Electronic Signature**: Sign with your full name.

2. Review the additional questions and provide ratings or answers as needed:
   - **Playing Surface Condition**: Rate the condition of the playing surface from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
   - **Safety Hazards**: Check if any safety hazards are present on the field.
   - **Determination**: Note any final determination made after inspecting the field.

## Tips
- Always inspect the field carefully and thoroughly before filling out this form.
- Take photos or notes if you need to document any issues or hazards found on the field.
- If you are unsure about any field condition, consult with your supervisor or facilities staff.
- Fill out this form accurately and honestly, as it will help maintain the integrity of the inspection process.
