# Film Set Equipment Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the current status of film set equipment, including whether the equipment is active or inactive, and any issues or concerns that need to be addressed. It helps ensure that all equipment is safe and functioning properly for on-set use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the general information section to ensure it accurately reflects the current status of the equipment.
2. Select the correct equipment from the equipment list.
3. Choose whether the selected equipment is active or inactive.
4. Provide any inspection results, including any notes or issues found.
5. If necessary, note any action planned to address any issues.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
7. Assign the equipment to a specific person, if applicable.
8. Record the last inspected date and time, as well as who last inspected the equipment.
9. Note the date and time of the next scheduled inspection.
10. Identify the equipment location.
11. Record the current status of the equipment.
12. Update the date and time the status was last changed, if applicable.
13. Enter any notes about the last inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information)** (`1`, `text`, optional): 
Enter any general information relevant to the equipment, such as its description, purpose, or other relevant details.
* **Equipment List (Equipment List)** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Select all equipment that require inspection.
* **Inspection Results (Inspection Results)** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): 
Choose whether the inspection results are positive or negative.
* **Action Planned (Action Planned)** (`4`, `text`, optional): 
If any issues were found during the inspection, describe the action planned to address them.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`5`, `note`, optional): 
Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To)** (`6`, `text`, optional): 
Assign the equipment to a specific person, if applicable.
* **Equipment Checked (Equipment Checked)** (`7`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the equipment that was checked during the inspection.
* **Last Inspected At (Last Inspected At)** (`8`, `date`, optional): 
Record the date and time the equipment was last inspected.
* **Last Checked By (Last Checked By)** (`9`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the person or team that last inspected the equipment.
* **Next Inspection Due (Next Inspection Due)** (`10`, `date`, optional): 
Record the date and time of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Next Checked By (Next Checked By)** (`11`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the person or team that will perform the next inspection.
* **Equipment Location (Equipment Location)** (`12`, `text`, optional): 
Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Equipment Status (Equipment Status)** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): 
Choose whether the equipment is active or inactive.
* **Last Status Changed At (Last Status Changed At)** (`14`, `date`, optional): 
Record the date and time the equipment status was last changed.
* **Notes On Last Inspection (Notes On Last Inspection)** (`15`, `note`, optional): 
Enter any notes about the last inspection.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if applicable.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy and completeness.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes and comments.
* Review this form regularly to ensure all equipment is inspected and up-to-date.
