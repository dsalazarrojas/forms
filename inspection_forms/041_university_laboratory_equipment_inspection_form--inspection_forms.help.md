# University Laboratory Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the condition, maintenance, and inspection of university laboratory equipment. It's part of a maintenance management system to ensure equipment is properly maintained and functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the lab equipment from the options provided.
2.  Answer each field according to your inspection and findings.
3.  Be sure to note any maintenance needs or concerns.
4.  Record the last maintenance date and schedule next maintenance if applicable.
5.  Document who inspected the equipment and when.
6.  Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Equipment**: This field allows you to select which piece of equipment you're inspecting. Choose the correct option from the provided list.
* **Equipment Condition**: Provide a brief description of the equipment's current condition, i.e., working properly, requires maintenance, damaged, etc.
* **Maintenance Needed**: Check if any maintenance is required for the equipment. If yes, provide a brief explanation.
* **Equipment Description**: Describe the equipment in detail, including its make, model, and serial number if known.
* **Location**: Specify where the equipment is located within the lab or facility.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Record the last date the equipment was maintained.
* **Next Maintenance Date**: Schedule the next maintenance date if applicable.
* **Maintenance Due Date**: Record the date when the equipment is due for maintenance.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: Specify how often the equipment requires maintenance.
* **Maintenance History**: Document the maintenance history of the equipment, including repairs and notable issues.
* **Maintenance Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the equipment's maintenance status.
* **Equipment Cost**: Record the cost of the equipment if available.
* **Equipment Age**: Record the age of the equipment, which can be an estimate or exact years.
* **Last Inspected By**: Record who last inspected the equipment.
* **Last Inspected Date**: Record the date of the last inspection.
* **Next Inspected By**: Record who will inspect the equipment next.
* **Next Inspected Date**: Schedule the next inspection date.
* **Inspected By**: Check off who inspected the equipment. This field can be filled with multiple names or roles.
* **Inspection Frequency**: Determine how often the equipment should be inspected.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
* **Comments**: Record any additional comments or observations about the equipment.
* **Equipment Type**: Specify the type of equipment being inspected (e.g., microscope, spectrophotometer, etc.).
* **Equipment Status**: Check the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Lab Location**: Identify the location of the lab where the equipment is housed.
* **Assigned Technician**: Record the name of the technician assigned to the equipment.

This help guide should provide a clear understanding of the purpose and usage of the University Laboratory Equipment Inspection Form. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your laboratory management team.
