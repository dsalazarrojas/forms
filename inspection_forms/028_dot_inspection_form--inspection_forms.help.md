# Dot Inspectoin Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dot Inspectoin Form is a tool used to collect and store vehicle inspection information. It is used for various types of inspections, including safety, emissions, and maintenance checks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the vehicle make from the options provided.
* Select the vehicle model from the options provided.
* Select the vehicle year from the options provided.
* Choose the vehicle color from the options (multiple selections allowed).
* Choose the type of vehicle (Car, Motorcycle, or Bicycle).
* Select the type of inspection to be performed (Safety, Emissions, or Maintenance).
* Optionally, select a date and time for the inspection.
* Add any relevant inspection notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select the vehicle make**: Choose the make of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: Ford, Toyota, Honda
* **Select the vehicle model**: Choose the model of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: Ford F-150, Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic
* **Select the vehicle year**: Choose the year of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: 2010, 2015, 2020
* **Select the vehicle color**: Choose the color(s) of the vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: Red, Blue, Green
* **Select the vehicle type**: Choose the type of vehicle being inspected.
	+ Options: Car, Motorcycle, Bicycle
* **Select the inspection type**: Choose the type of inspection to be performed.
	+ Options: Safety, Emissions, Maintenance
* **Select the date**: Optionally, choose a date for the inspection.
* **Select the time**: Optionally, choose a time for the inspection.
* **Inspectoin notes**: Add any relevant notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all applicable fields for accurate results.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are complete.
