# fire_safety_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire safety inspection checklist is used to ensure that all necessary fire safety measures are in place within a facility. It is a comprehensive guide to help facility management or staff identify potential fire safety risks and inform corrective actions to improve fire safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility's name and address in the respective fields.
2. Answer each question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating whether the facility has the respective feature or system in place.
3. Enter the training date for fire safety training if it has been completed.

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
