# Artificial Turf Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Artificial Turf Inspection Checklist" is a comprehensive form designed for field inspectors to assess the condition of artificial turf fields. The form is divided into six sections: Inspection Details, Surface Condition, Seams and Joints, Drainage and Infill, Damage and Hazards, and Maintenance Needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully, understanding the purpose and requirements of each field.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. Use clear and concise language when entering text-based fields.
4. Select the most accurate option for each select_one field.
5. Select all applicable maintenance actions for each field.
6. Enter any additional notes or observations in the "Additional Notes or Observations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Inspection** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date on which the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Title** (`inspector_title`): Enter the position or role of the inspector.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the sports field or facility being inspected.
* **Overall Turf Condition** (`overall_condition`): Select the general assessment of the field's condition, using the options provided.
* **Turf Pile Height Measurement** (`turf_pile_height`): Enter the average pile height in millimeters.
* **Pile Wear Assessment** (`pile_wear`): Select the level of wear on the turf piles, using the options provided.
* **Color Fading or Discoloration** (`color_fading`): Select the level of color fading or discoloration observed.
* **Seam Integrity** (`seam_integrity`): Select the condition of the seams, using the provided options.
* **Seam Issues Identified** (`seam_issues`): Enter any seam problems found during the inspection.
* **Drainage System Status** (`drainage_status`): Select the condition of the drainage system, using the options provided.
* **Infill Compaction Level** (`infill_compaction`): Select the level of infill compaction, using the options provided.
* **Missing or Exposed Areas** (`infill_missing`): Select the extent of missing or exposed infill areas.
* **Tears or Puncture Damage** (`tears_damage`): Select the level of tears or puncture damage observed.
* **Debris or Hazard Removal Needed** (`hazard_debris`): Select the presence of debris or hazards.
* **Maintenance Actions Required** (`maintenance_type`): Select all applicable maintenance actions needed for the field.
* **Urgency of Repairs** (`maintenance_urgency`): Select the urgency of the maintenance actions needed.
* **Additional Notes or Observations** (`additional_notes`): Enter any other findings or recommendations.
* **Recommended Follow-up Inspection Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for the next inspection.

## Tips

* Ensure that each field is completed accurately and truthfully.
* If unsure about a field, consult the section heading or the provided options.
* Use clear and concise language when entering text-based fields.
* Select all applicable options for the "Maintenance Actions Required" field.
* Enter the recommended follow-up inspection date only if necessary.
