# Manufacturing Quality Control Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Quality Control Assessment form is used to collect information and data related to quality control issues that occur during the manufacturing process. It helps track and report issues, document evidence, and record resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Quality Control Processes" option to indicate whether the quality control process is in place or not.
2. Fill out the "Quality Control Parameters" field with relevant numbers or values.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
4. Attach an image or video related to the issue in the "Issue Image" or "Issue Video" field.
5. Select the severity of the issue (Minor, Major, Critical) in the "Issue Severity" field.
6. Enter a comment or action plan for resolution in the "Quality Control Action" field.
7. Update the status of the issue in the "Issue Status" field.
8. Add any relevant comments or additional information in the "Quality Control Comment" field.
9. Enter the date and time when the quality control was performed in the "Quality Control Date" and "Quality Control Time" fields.
10. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the issue in the "Note" field.
12. Provide a comment about the issue image in the "Issue Image Comment" field.
13. Attach any relevant documents or files to the issue in the "Issue Attachment" field.
14. Enter any relevant video information about the issue in the "Issue Video" field.
15. Enter any audio information about the issue in the "Issue Audio" field.
16. Enter the location of the issue in the "Issue Location" field.
17. Enter the location of the quality control check in the "Quality Control Location" field.
18. Enter the equipment used for quality control in the "Quality Control Equipment" field.
19. Enter any additional comments about the issue in the "Quality Control Comment 2" field.
20. Update the status of the quality control in the "Quality Control Status" field.
