# Cleaning Sanitizing Disinfecting Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Sanitizing Disinfecting Log is a comprehensive form for recording and tracking cleaning, sanitizing, and disinfecting activities across various facilities, departments, or areas within an organization. This form helps ensure that cleaning tasks are completed accurately and safely, and that any issues or concerns are documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name and department or area where the cleaning tasks were performed.
2. Record the date of the log entry.
3. Enter the staff member's name who performed the cleaning tasks.
4. Enter the staff member's ID number (if applicable).
5. Answer the questions for each shift (morning, afternoon, and evening) about the cleaning tasks completed, supplies used, and any issues or concerns encountered.
6. Indicate whether the proper contact time was followed for disinfectant products.
7. Confirm the use of personal protective equipment (PPE) during cleaning tasks.
8. Verify that chemical dilution ratios were followed.
9. Confirm that safety data sheets (SDS) are available for all products used.
10. Document any issues or concerns encountered during the cleaning process.
11. List any supplies used during the cleaning process.
12. List any supplies that need to be restocked.
13. Sign and date the form to indicate completion and accuracy.
14. Have the supervisor verify and sign the form to confirm accuracy and completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the cleaning tasks were performed.
* **Department or Area** (`facility_department`, text, required): Enter the department or area within the facility where the cleaning tasks were performed.
* **Log Date** (`log_date`, date, required): Record the date of the log entry.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the staff member's name who performed the cleaning tasks.
* **Staff ID Number** (`staff_id`, text, optional): Enter the staff member's ID number (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor who verified the log entry.

## Tips

* Document any issues or concerns encountered during the cleaning process to improve future cleaning tasks.
