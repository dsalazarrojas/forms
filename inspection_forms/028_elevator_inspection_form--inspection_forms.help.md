# Elevator Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for recording information about elevator inspections, including the maintenance team, inspection date and time, location, and result.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the maintenance team that performed the inspection.
3. Choose the elevator type.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
5. Enter the location of the elevator.
6. Enter the elevator ID.
7. Enter any issues found during the inspection.
8. Select the action items that need to be completed.
9. Enter the deadline for completing action items.
10. Select the action items that have been completed.
11. Enter the deadline for completed action items.
12. Enter comments about action items.
13. Select whether the maintenance team is safety certified.
14. Enter the date the maintenance team was safety certified.
15. Select the result of the inspection.
16. Select whether the completed checklist was completed.
17. Enter the date the completed checklist was completed.
18. Enter comments about the completed checklist.
19. Select the status of the completed checklist.
20. Enter the inspection frequency.
21. Enter the next inspection date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Elevator Inspection** (`elevator_inspection`, text, required): This is the main description field for the elevator inspection.
- **Maintenance Team** (`maintenance_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of the maintenance team that performed the inspection.
- **Maintenance Team Members** (`maintenance_team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the members of the maintenance team.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
- **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
- **Elevator Location** (`elevator_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the elevator.
- **Elevator ID** (`elevator_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the elevator.
- **Elevator Type** (`elevator_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of elevator.
- **Inspection Notes** (`inspection_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the inspection.
- **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, select_one, optional): Select whether any issues were found during the inspection.
- **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional): Enter any action items that need to be completed.
- **Deadline for Action Items** (`action_items_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for completing action items.
- **Completed Action Items** (`completed_action_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether action items have been completed.
- **Deadline for Completed Action Items** (`completed_action_items_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for completed action items.
- **Action Items Comments** (`action_items_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments about action items.
- **Safety Certified** (`safety_certified`, select_one, optional): Select whether the maintenance team is safety certified.
- **Date Certified** (`safety_certified_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the maintenance team was safety certified.
- **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, select_one, optional): Select the result of the inspection.
- **Completed Checklist** (`completed_checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the checklist was completed.
- **Completed Checklist Date** (`completed_checklist_completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the checklist was completed.
- **Checklist Comments** (`checklist_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments about the checklist.
- **Checklist Status** (`checklist_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the checklist.
- **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the inspection frequency.
- **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the next inspection date.
- **Next Inspection Time** (`next_inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the next inspection time.

## Tips

* Always fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Be sure to include any issues found during the inspection and the deadline for completing action items.
* Select the correct status of the checklist and enter the deadline for completed action items.
* Ensure that the maintenance team is safety certified and enter the date certified.
* Enter the result of the inspection and the next inspection date and time.
