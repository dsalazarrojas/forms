# Well Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Well Inspection Report form is used to document the findings and observations of a well inspection. This form is completed by the inspection team and is used to gather information about the well's condition, identify any issues or defects, and track recommendations for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the well's ID in the "well_id" field.
2. Enter your name in the "inspector_name" field.
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the "date_inspected" field.
4. Provide any comments or observations in the "inspector_comments" field.
5. Upload and store photos of the well's condition in the "photo_1", "photo_2", and "photo_3" fields.
6. For each photo, provide a brief description of what was observed.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "recommendation_1" and "recommendation_2" fields, as appropriate.
8. Enter your signature in the "inspector_signature" field.
9. Enter the date you signed the report in the "date_signed" field.
10. Enter the entity to which you are assigning the well in the "assigned_to" field.
11. Enter the date you assigned the well to the entity in the "assigned_date" field.
12. Provide any additional comments about the well in the "well_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **well_id** (`well_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique identifier of the well.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the inspector conducting the well inspection.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Comments** (`inspector_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any comments or observations about the inspection, such as any issues or defects found.
* **Photo 1** (`photo_1`, text, required: false): Upload and store a photo of the well's condition.
* **Photo 2** (`photo_2`, text, required: false): Upload and store another photo of the well's condition.
* **Photo 3** (`photo_3`, text, required: false): Upload and store a third photo of the well's condition.
* **Recommendation 1** (`recommendation_1`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the well requires any repairs or improvements.
* **Recommendation 2** (`recommendation_2`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the well requires any further actions or follow-ups.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, required: false): Enter your signature to confirm the inspection.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the report.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, required: false): Enter the entity to which you are assigning the well.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you assigned the well to the entity.
* **Well Comments** (`well_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments about the well's condition or inspection findings.
