# Taxi Stand Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Taxi Stand Inspection Form" is a crucial tool for evaluating taxi stands in the field, helping inspectors collect data on their operational status, vehicle compliance, and regulatory fines. This form ensures a thorough and accurate assessment of each taxi stand's compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Taxi Stand" option from the "Location" dropdown menu to identify the taxi stand being inspected.
2. Enter the address of the taxi stand in the "Address" field.
3. Provide the taxi stand's ID number in the "Taxi Stand ID" field.
4. Enter the name of the operator in the "Operator Name" field.
5. Select the vehicle license status as "Valid" or "Invalid" in the "Vehicle License" field.
6. Choose the type of vehicle(s) present at the taxi stand from the "Vessels" dropdown menu (e.g., "Bus", "Taxi").
7. Select the operational status as "Operational" or "Non-Operational" in the "Operational Status" field.
8. Indicate compliance or non-compliance with safety standards in the "Safety Standards Compliance" field.
9. Enter any applicable regulatory fines in the "Regulatory Fines" field.
10. Record any notable observations or comments in the "Observation" field.
11. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
12. Enter the inspector's ID number in the "Inspector ID" field.
13. Enter the inspector's name in the "Inspector Name" field.
14. Specify the inspector's department in the "Inspector Department" field.
15. Enter the date of inspection in the "Inspector Date" field.
16. Enter the time of inspection in the "Inspector Time" field.
17. Provide the inspector's email address in the "Inspector Email" field.
18. Enter the inspector's phone number in the "Inspector Phone" field.
19. Select the form status as "Complete" or "Incomplete" in the "Taxi Stand Inspection Form Status" field.
20. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Taxi Stand Inspection Form Comments" field.
21. Specify the type of vehicle as "Bus" or "Taxi" in the "Vehicle Type" field.
22. Enter the vehicle's capacity in the "Vehicle Capacity" field.
23. Enter the vehicle's length in the "Vehicle Length" field.
24. Enter the vehicle's weight in the "Vehicle Weight" field.
