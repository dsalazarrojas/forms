# Operational Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Operational Safety Checklist is used to ensure that equipment is in good condition and safe for use. It helps identify potential hazards and take corrective actions to mitigate risks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go to the relevant location where the form will be filled out.
* Review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, using the information you have gathered.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Condition** (`Equipment Condition`, text, required: false):
  Describe the overall condition of the equipment, including any defects, damage, or maintenance needs.
* **Inspection Areas** (`Inspection Areas`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select all areas to inspect, such as electrical, mechanical, or piping.
* **Safety Personnel** (`Safety Personnel`, text, required: false):
  List the personnel directly involved in the inspection, including their names and titles.
* **Equipment Maintenance** (`Equipment Maintenance`, date, required: false):
  Record the date of the most recent maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Last Inspection** (`Last Inspection`, time, required: false):
  Record the date and time of the last inspection, if known.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review the form with your supervisor or maintenance coordinator before submitting.
* If any issues are identified, take corrective actions to mitigate risks.
* Keep the form up to date and accessible for future reference.
