# emergency_exit_light_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The emergency_exit_light_inspection_form is used to collect information about the state of emergency exit lights in a facility or building. This includes their maintenance frequency, last performed maintenance date, last tested date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Enter the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_exit_light_inspection_form** (`emergency_exit_light_inspection_form`, text, required): Briefly describe the state of emergency exit lights in your facility or building.
* **emergency_exit_light_description** (`emergency_exit_light_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the emergency exit light(s) being inspected.
* **emergency_exit_light_maintenance_frequency** (`emergency_exit_light_maintenance_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of maintenance for the emergency exit light(s).
	+ Options: Regularly, As needed
* **emergency_exit_light_maintenance_lastperformeddate** (`emergency_exit_light_maintenance_lastperformed_date`, date, required): Enter the date of last performed maintenance on the emergency exit light(s).
* **emergency_exit_light_maintenance_lastperformedby** (`emergency_exit_light_maintenance_last_performed_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team who performed the last maintenance.
* **emergency_exit_light_tested_date** (`emergency_exit_light_tested_date`, date, required): Enter the date the emergency exit light(s) were last tested.
* **emergency_exit_light_tested_by** (`emergency_exit_light_tested_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team who performed the last test.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the emergency exit light(s).
