# Material Cutting Control Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track the control process of material cutting operations, ensuring accuracy and consistency in the recording and tracking of key information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form according to the specific information required for that page.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field label.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form for final review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Cutting Details** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the material cutting operation, including the specific details of the cut, such as dimensions, cutting method, and any other relevant information.
* **2. Quality Checks** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant quality check options that apply to the material cutting operation.
* **3. Sign-offs** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relevant sign-off option for the material cutting operation.
* **4. Date Cut** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_4`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the material was cut.
* **5. Time Cut** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_5`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the material was cut.
* **6. Production Run Number** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_6`, `number`, optional): Enter the production run number for the material cutting operation.
* **7. Material Description** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_7`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the material being cut.
* **8. Cut Operator** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_8`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the operator who performed the cut.
* **9. Quality Check Results** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_9`, `text`, optional): Enter the results of any quality checks performed on the material.
* **10. Sign-off Comments** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_10`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments related to the sign-off process.
* **11. Cutting Notes** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_11`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes related to the material cutting operation.
* **12. Reject Reason** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_12`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for rejecting the material if it does not meet quality standards.
* **13. Acceptance Criteria** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_13`, `text`, optional): Enter the acceptance criteria for the material cutting operation.
* **14. Date Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_14`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the material was accepted.
* **15. Time Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_15`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the material was accepted.
* **16. Production Run Number Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_16`, `number`, optional): Enter the production run number for the accepted material.
* **17. Material Description Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_17`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the material that was accepted.
* **18. Cut Operator Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_18`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the operator who accepted the material.
* **19. Quality Check Results Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_19`, `text`, optional): Enter the results of any quality checks performed on the accepted material.
* **20. Acceptance Comments** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_20`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments related to the acceptance process.
* **21. Acceptance Notes** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_21`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes related to the accepted material.
* **22. Reject Reason Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_22`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for rejecting the material if it does not meet quality standards after acceptance.
* **23. Acceptance Criteria Accepted** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_23`, `text`, optional): Enter the acceptance criteria for the accepted material.
* **24. Acceptance Date** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_24`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the material was accepted.
* **25. Acceptance Time** (`material_cutting_control_form_page_25`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the material was accepted.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate tracking and recording of material cutting operations.
