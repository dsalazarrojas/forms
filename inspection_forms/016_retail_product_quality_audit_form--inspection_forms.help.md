# Retail Product Quality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and store data related to retail product quality audits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Type** of audit being performed (Physical, Visual, or Documentation).
2. Enter the **Product Name**, **Product ID**, and **Location Name** for the product being inspected.
3. Enter the **Audit Date** and **Audit Time** of the inspection.
4. Select the relevant options from the **Questionnaire** section.
5. Enter any **Observation Notes** related to the product.
6. Upload any **Image**, **Video**, or other relevant files using the corresponding fields.
7. Save your progress and move to the next page.
