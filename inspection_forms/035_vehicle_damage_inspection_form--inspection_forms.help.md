# Vehicle Damage Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about vehicle damage after an incident. It helps to identify the severity and type of damage to the vehicle. This form is intended for use in situations where a vehicle has been involved in an accident, been involved in a collision, or has suffered wear and tear.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Vehicle Registration Plate** field with the license plate number of the vehicle being inspected.
2. Enter the **Vehicle Year Model** to specify the year and model of the vehicle.
3. Select the **Vehicle License Plate Color** from the dropdown list to indicate the color of the license plate.
4. Describe the **Vehicle Body Damage** in the text field, including any dents, scratches, or other damage you observe.
5. Describe the **Vehicle Tire Damage** in the text field, including any flat tires, uneven wear, or other tire-related issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration Plate** (Vehicle Registration Plate, text, required): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle being inspected.
* **Vehicle Year Model** (Vehicle Year Model, text, optional): Enter the year and model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Plate Color** (Vehicle License Plate Color, select_one, required): Choose the color of the license plate from the dropdown list.
* **Vehicle Body Damage Description** (Vehicle Body Damage Description, text, optional): Describe any dents, scratches, or other damage you observe on the vehicle body.
* **Vehicle Tire Damage Description** (Vehicle Tire Damage Description, text, optional): Describe any flat tires, uneven wear, or other tire-related issues you observe.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for the Vehicle Registration Plate to ensure that the correct vehicle is being inspected.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the Vehicle Body Damage and Vehicle Tire Damage to help the inspector accurately assess the damage.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask the inspector for guidance.

Note: The required fields are marked with a star (*) in the field labels.
