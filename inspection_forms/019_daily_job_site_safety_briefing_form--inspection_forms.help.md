# Daily Job Site Safety Briefing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a daily safety briefing on a job site, ensuring that all necessary safety measures are in place and that any potential hazards are identified and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Job Site Id number for the specific job site being inspected.
2. Enter the Job Site Name, which is the name of the job site.
3. Select the Date Of Inspection, which is the date on which the safety briefing is being conducted.
4. Select the Time Of Inspection, which is the time of day when the safety briefing is being conducted.
5. Choose the Weather Conditions on the day of the safety briefing.
6. Select the areas of the site that were inspected.
7. Choose the personal equipment that was available and inspected.
8. Choose the fire equipment that was available and inspected.
9. Choose the first aid equipment that was available and inspected.
10. Choose the electrical equipment that was available and inspected.
11. Choose the safety equipment that was available and inspected.
12. Choose the emergency contact for the site.
13. Choose the emergency procedure that was used or available.
14. Select the current site condition.
15. Select the condition of the safety equipment.
16. Select the condition of the personal safety equipment.
17. Select the personal hygiene of the personnel on site.
18. Select the condition of the vehicles on site.
19. Select whether a fire supervisor is present.
20. Select whether a first aid supervisor is present.
21. Select whether a safety supervisor is present.
22. Select whether a fire equipment supervisor is present.
23. Select whether an emergency equipment supervisor is present.
24. Select whether a vehicle equipment supervisor is present.
25. Select whether a site inspector is present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Site Id** (`job_site_id`, Number, Required): Enter the job site's unique identifier number.
* **Job Site Name** (`job_site_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the job site being inspected.
* **Date Of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, Date, Required): Enter the date on which the safety briefing is being conducted.
* **Time Of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, Time, Required): Enter the time of day when the safety briefing is being conducted.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, Select, Optional): Choose the current weather conditions on site.
	+ Clear
	+ Partly Cloudy
	+ Overcast
	+ Light Rain
	+ Heavy Rain
	+ Snow
	+ Fog
* **Inspection Area** (`inspection_area`, Multi-Select, Required): Select the areas of the site that were inspected.

* **Personal Equipment** (`personal_equipment`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the personal equipment that was available and inspected.

	+ Hard Hat

* **Fire Equipment** (`fire_equipment`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the fire equipment that was available and inspected.

* **First Aid** (`first_aid`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the first aid equipment that was available and inspected.
	+ First Aid Kit
	+ Fire Extinguisher

* **Electrical Equipment** (`electrical_equipment`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the electrical equipment that was available and inspected.

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the emergency contact for the site.
	+ Supervisor

* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, Multi-Select, Required): Choose the emergency procedure that was used or available.
	+ First Aid

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully read each field's explanation to understand what information is being requested.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* If a field is marked as "True" or "False", ensure that you are answering accordingly.
* If a field is marked as "Multi-Select", choose all relevant options that apply.
* If a field is marked as "Select", choose only one relevant option.
* If a field is marked as "Required", make sure to fill it out completely.
* If a field is marked as "Optional", it's up to your discretion whether or not to fill it out.
