# SWPPP Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SWPPP Inspection form is used to track and document the results of SWPPP inspections at construction sites. It ensures that site inspectors gather all necessary information to verify compliance with environmental regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Site Location** (Field 1) to identify the site being inspected.
2. Answer whether **Stormwater Runoff** (Field 2) is an issue at the site.
3. Choose the **Compliance Frequency** (Field 3) for the SWPPP inspection, such as Daily, Weekly, or Quarterly.
4. Select the **Responsible Party** (Field 4) who is responsible for SWPPP at the site, such as Owner, Operator, or Other.
5. Provide any **Comments** (Field 5) or notes relevant to the inspection.
6. Enter the **Inspection Date** (Field 6) of the last inspection.
7. Choose the **Inspection Status** (Field 7) as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
8. Enter the **Last Updated** (Field 8) date of the last update.
9. Enter the **Next Due Date** (Field 9) for the next inspection.
10. Enter the **Last Inspection Date** (Field 10) of the previous inspection.
11. Enter the **Last Inspector's Name** (Field 11) who performed the last inspection.
12. Enter the **Last Inspector's Signature** (Field 12) of the last inspection.
13. Enter the **Last Inspection Cost** (Field 13) in the specified currency.
14. Choose the **Last Inspection Cost Currency** (Field 14) from USD, EUR, or Other.
15. Enter the **Last Inspection Cost Amount** (Field 15) for the previous inspection.
16. Enter any **Last Inspector Notes** (Field 16) from the last inspector.
17. Enter the **Last Inspector's Signature** (Field 17) for the second inspector (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Location**: A brief description of the site location.
* **Stormwater Runoff Source**: Is stormwater runoff an issue at this site?
* **Compliance Frequency**: How often is the SWPPP inspected? (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually)
* **Responsible Party**: Who is responsible for SWPPP at this site? (Owner, Operator, Other)
* **Comments**: Additional comments or notes from the last inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Date of last inspection.
* **Inspection Status**: Is the inspection active or inactive? (Active, Inactive, Pending)
* **Last Updated**: Date of last update.
* **Next Due Date**: Date of next inspection.
* **Last Inspection Date**: Date of last inspection.
* **Last Inspector's Name**: Name of last inspector.
* **Last Inspector's Signature**: Last inspector's signature.
* **Last Inspection Cost**: Cost of last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Cost Currency**: Currency of last inspection cost.
* **Last Inspection Cost Amount**: Amount of last inspection cost.
* **Last Inspector Notes**: Notes from last inspector.
* **Last Inspector's Signature 2**: Last inspector's signature (if second inspector exists).
* **Last Inspector's Signature 2 2**: Last inspector's signature (if second inspector exists, not used in actual form).

Note: Fields with "2" in their name are not used in the actual form.
