# parking_lot_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parking lot inspection form is designed to collect information about the parking lot's condition and status. This form helps in tracking and analyzing the inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the correct status of the parking lot from the options provided in the "Inspection Area" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the parking lot's status in the "Parking Lot Status" field.
3. Fill in any additional details that are relevant to the inspection.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Inspection Area** (`inspection_area`, `select_one`, required: false)
The "Inspection Area" field is used to select the specific area of the parking lot that is being inspected. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide a clear and accurate description of the area being inspected.

*   Select 'Yes' if the inspection area is clean and free of debris.
*   Select 'No' if the inspection area is dirty or has debris.

**Parking Lot Status** (`parking_lot_status`, `text`, required: false)
This field is used to provide a detailed description of the parking lot's status.

*   Describe the overall condition of the parking lot, including any issues or concerns.
