# Home Inspection Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a home inspection order, which is a crucial step in various processes such as property management, real estate, or construction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **order_form** details in the field provided.
2. Provide the client's **name** in the designated field.
3. Enter the client's **contact_number** for effective communication.
4. Input the client's **email** address for further contact and communication.
5. Enter the client's **address** for accurate location and identification.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether **inspection_type** is required for this order.
7. Choose the type of **property_type** being inspected.
8. Enter the date of the intended **inspection_date**.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether the **inspector** is available for this order.
10. Choose the current **status** of the order (Active or Inactive).
11. Enter the priority level of the order in the **priority** field.
12. Provide any additional **comments** relevant to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (`order_form`, `text`, required/optional): A field to input any special instructions or details about the order.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the client for whom the inspection is ordered.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's contact number for effective communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Input the client's email address for further contact and communication.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the property to be inspected.
* **Inspection Type** (`inspection_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if an inspection is necessary for this order, and "No" otherwise.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of property being inspected.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the inspection is planned.
* **Inspector** (`inspector`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the inspector is available, and "No" otherwise.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the current status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Priority** (`priority`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the priority level of the order.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments relevant to the order.

This guide is intended to ensure that users understand the purpose and requirements of the form and can complete it accurately and efficiently.
