# Anonymous Bullying Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about bullying incidents, providing a safe and confidential channel for reporting and helping to create a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
3. If a question doesn't apply, you can skip it and move on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Bullying Incident (#1)**: Provide the date of the bullying incident.
* **Approximate Time (#2)**: (Optional) Specify the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident (#3)**: Describe the location where the bullying took place.
* **Type of Environment (#4)**: Choose from the list of environment options (e.g., School - classroom, School - online, etc.).
* **Type of Bullying (#5)**: Select all types of bullying that apply (e.g., Physical bullying, Verbal harassment, etc.).
* **Description of Target - Student or Person (#6)**: If safe to do so, provide a brief description of the target's grade level or role.
* **Description of Person Bullying (#7)**: If safe to do so, provide a brief description of the person doing the bullying (e.g., grade level, characteristics, or role).
* **Detailed Description of Incident (#8)**: Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Consequences Observed (#9)**: Describe how the bullying affected the target.
* **Were there Witnesses? (#10)**: Indicate if there were witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Information (#11)**: (Optional) If there were witnesses, provide a brief description of how many people saw the incident.
* **Is this a Repeated Pattern? (#12)**: Indicate if this is a repeated bullying incident or the first occurrence.
* **Has this been Reported Before? (#13)**: Indicate if the incident has been reported to school, workplace, or authorities before.
* **What Action was Taken Previously? (#14)**: (Optional) If this has been reported before, describe any actions taken previously.
* **Your Relationship to Situation (#15)**: Choose how you relate to the situation (e.g., Witness to the bullying, Friend of the target, etc.).
* **Is the Target Currently Safe? (#16)**: Indicate if the target is currently in danger or potentially in danger.
* **Does the Target Need Immediate Support? (#17)**: Indicate if the target requires immediate support or emergency help.
* **May Officials Contact You for More Information? (#18)**: If you're providing your contact information for follow-up, indicate that you're willing to be contacted.
* **Email for Follow-up (#19)**: (Optional) Provide an email address for officials to contact you for follow-up.
* **Additional Information or Evidence (#20)**: Provide any other details that can help in the investigation.
* **I Understand this Report will be Investigated (#21)**: Confirm that you understand the report will be investigated and acknowledge your understanding of the process.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about a question, it's best to choose "Unsure" or "I don't know" instead of leaving it blank.
* If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, please have their permission or consent before completing this form.
* You can choose to remain completely anonymous, and your identity will not be revealed. However, providing your contact information can help us follow up on the report and provide support to the target.
* If you're experiencing bullying, please don't hesitate to report it.
