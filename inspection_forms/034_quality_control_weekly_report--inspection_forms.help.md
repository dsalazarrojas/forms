# Quality Control Weekly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Weekly Report is a form used to collect data for the Quality Control department on a weekly basis. This form helps identify trends and areas for improvement in the quality control processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant department from the dropdown list.
2. Enter any issues found during the inspection.
3. Choose whether or not the issues found require corrective actions.
4. Indicate if escalation is needed for any of the issues.
5. Enter the average and lowest ratings received during the inspection.
6. Choose the highest rating received during the inspection.
7. Enter any comments regarding the inspection.
8. Indicate if any comments were provided by the inspectors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department inspected, e.g. "Quality Control" or "Production".
* **Weekly Ratings** (`weekly_ratings`, number, optional): Enter the average rating received during the inspection.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if issues were found during the inspection.
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, optional): Enter a list of corrective actions taken to address the issues found.
* **Escalation Needs** (`escalation_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if escalation is needed for any of the issues.
* **Ratings Average** (`ratings_average`, number, optional): Enter the average rating received during the inspection.
* **Ratings Lowest** (`ratings_lowest`, select_one, optional): Choose the lowest rating received during the inspection.
* **Ratings Highest** (`ratings_highest`, select_one, optional): Choose the highest rating received during the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments regarding the inspection.
* **Comments Comments** (`comments_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any comments were provided by the inspectors.
