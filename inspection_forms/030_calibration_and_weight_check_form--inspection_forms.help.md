# Calibration And Weight Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Calibration And Weight Check Form" is designed to collect accurate and detailed information about the calibration status of various equipment, such as scales, balances, and other precision instruments. This form ensures that the equipment is functioning correctly and within the specified tolerance range.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Equipment Information**: Enter the equipment's ID or serial number to identify the device.
2. **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment (e.g., balance scale, digital scale, weight scale, precision scale, laboratory scale, or other).
3. **Equipment Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is used.
4. **Inspection Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
5. **Inspector's Information**: Enter your name and credentials to ensure your identity.
6. **Last Calibration Date**: Enter the date of the last calibration of the equipment.
7. **Next Calibration Due Date**: Enter the next due date for calibration.
8. **Calibration Standard**: Choose the calibration standard used (e.g., NIST, ISO 9001, company standard, or other).
9. **Test Weights and Results**: Enter test weights and measured results for up to three weights (e.g., Test Weight 1, Measured Result 1, etc.).
10. **Accuracy Tolerance**: Enter the accuracy tolerance percentage.
11. **Calibration Status**: Choose the calibration status (within tolerance, outside tolerance, or needs recalibration).
12. **Corrective Action**: If the equipment is outside tolerance, enter the corrective action to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment ID or Serial Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment.
	+ Example: 123456
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment (e.g., balance scale, digital scale, weight scale, precision scale, laboratory scale, or other).
	+ Examples: Balance Scale, Digital Scale
* **Equipment Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is used.
	+ Example: Laboratory 1, Main Office
* **Inspection Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 2022-03-01 10:00 AM)
* **Inspector's Name and Credentials**: Enter your name and credentials to ensure your identity.
	+ Example: John Doe, Calibration Technician
* **Last Calibration Date**: Enter the date of the last calibration of the equipment.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01)
* **Next Calibration Due Date**: Enter the next due date for calibration.
	+ Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-06-01)
* **Calibration Standard**: Choose the calibration standard used (e.g., NIST, ISO 9001, company standard, or other).
	+ Examples: NIST, ISO 9001
* **Test Weights and Results**: Enter test weights and measured results for up to three weights.
	+ Format: Weight Value (e.g., 2.5 kg, 10 lbs)
	+ Example: Test Weight 1 (2.5 kg), Measured Result 1 (2.48 kg)
* **Accuracy Tolerance**: Enter the accuracy tolerance percentage.
	+ Example: 0.5%
* **Calibration Status**: Choose the calibration status (within tolerance, outside tolerance, or needs recalibration).
	+ Examples: Within tolerance, Outside tolerance
* **Corrective Action**: If the equipment is outside tolerance, enter the corrective action to be taken.
	+ Example: Replace the equipment's weights, Perform recalibration within 24 hours, etc.

Note: This guide is a general overview, and you should consult your company's specific calibration procedures and guidelines for any additional requirements or specific instructions.
