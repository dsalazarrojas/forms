# Heritage House Kids Online Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Heritage House Kids Online Class Registration Form" is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for an online class. This form is used to gather essential details, such as the child's name and contact information. By filling out this form, parents will be able to enroll their child in a fun and educational online class experience at Heritage House.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Review your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (1)** (`text`, Optional):
	+ This is the main registration section where you can provide any additional information about your child's enrollment.
* **Name (2)** (`text`, Optional):
	+ Please enter your child's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or birth certificate.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, Optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your child's class schedule and progress.
* **Phone (4)** (`text`, Optional):
	+ Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or notifications.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
