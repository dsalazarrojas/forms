# Family Friendly Dinner Reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to reserve a table at Family Friendly Dinner by providing necessary information about your reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a date for your reservation
2. Specify the number of guests attending
3. Select the time of your reservation
4. If you have any special requests, please let us know in the "Special Requests" field
5. Provide the contact person's name
6. Enter their phone number
7. Enter their email address (if available)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required: false): This is the date you would like to make your reservation. Please select a date that works for you.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required: false): Enter the number of people in your party. This will help us prepare for your group.
* **Time of Reservation** (`time_of_reservation`, time, required: false): Select the time of your reservation. Choose from our available time slots.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required: false): If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or special occasions, please let us know here. We'll do our best to accommodate them.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person making the reservation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number. This will help us contact you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address. We'll use this to send confirmation and details of your reservation.

## Tips
- Please double-check your reservation details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you need to make any changes to your reservation, please contact us directly.
