# Wedding Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Day Questionnaire is a form used to gather essential details for wedding ceremonies and receptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for each field based on the wedding details.
2.  Fill in all required fields, denoted by the asterisk symbol (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`, `note`, required: false): This field is for noting any important details about the ceremony.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the contact persons responsible for the wedding.
	+ Choose the Wedding Planner and Event Organizers involved.
* **Major Sponsors** (`major_sponsors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the major sponsors for the event.
* **Ceremony Time** (`ceremony_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the ceremony start time.
* **Reception Details** (`reception_details`, `note`, required: false): Note any important details about the reception.
* **Special Addons** (`special_addons`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any special addons for the event.
* **Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the ceremony date.
* **Wedding Planner** (`wedding_planner`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the wedding planner in charge.
* **Reception Time** (`reception_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the reception start time.
* **Event Organizers** (`event_organizers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the event organizers involved in the event.
* **Major Sponsor** (`major_sponsor`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the major sponsor for the event.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the wedding date.
* **Ceremony Location** (`ceremony_location`, `note`, required: false): Note the location of the ceremony.
* **Reception Location** (`reception_location`, `note`, required: false): Note the reception location.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the type of ceremony.
* **Special Addon** (`special_addon`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional features for the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact address.
* **Major Sponsor Name** (`major_sponsor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the major sponsor name.
* **Major Sponsor Email** (`major_sponsor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the major sponsor email.
* **Major Sponsor Phone** (`major_sponsor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the major sponsor phone number.
* **Major Sponsor Address** (`major_sponsor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the major sponsor address.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the event organizer name.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the event organizer email.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the event organizer phone number.
