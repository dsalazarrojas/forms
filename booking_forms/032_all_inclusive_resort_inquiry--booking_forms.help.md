# All Inclusive Resort Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for an all-inclusive resort inquiry. The form collects personal contact details, travel preferences, and special requests to provide a better experience for potential guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Select your preferred resort, travel dates, and flexible dates.
3. Choose the number of nights you plan to stay and the number of adults and children traveling with you.
4. Select your preferred room type and number of rooms required.
5. Choose your budget range per person and whether you want flights included.
6. If you have any special occasions or dietary needs, please select the relevant options.
7. Indicate your interests and activities you would like to experience during your stay.
8. If you have any accessibility needs, please provide more information in the "Additional Requests" field.
9. If you are interested in travel insurance, please select the relevant option.
10. Finally, let us know how you heard about us and any additional requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you directly.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your full mailing address for any further communication.
* **Preferred Resort**: Select the resort that interests you the most.
* **Preferred Travel Dates**: Enter your preferred travel dates.
* **Are Dates Flexible?**: Indicate if you are flexible with your travel dates or if you have any specific requirements.
* **Number of Nights**: Choose the number of nights you plan to stay at the resort.
* **Number of Adults**: Enter the number of adults traveling with you.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children traveling with you. (Optional)
* **Children Ages**: Enter the ages of the children traveling with you. (Optional)
* **Room Preference**: Select the type of room that suits your needs.
* **Number of Rooms Needed**: Enter the number of rooms required.
* **Budget Range Per Person**: Choose your budget range per person.
* **Departure Airport**: Enter the nearest airport for your arrival.
* **Flights Needed**: Indicate if you want flights included in your booking.
* **Special Occasion**: Select if you are traveling for a special occasion.
* **Dining Preferences**: Select any dietary needs or restrictions you may have.
* **Activities of Interest**: Choose the activities you would like to experience during your stay.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Travel Insurance Interest**: Select if you are interested in travel insurance.
* **Previous Visits to Area**: Select if you have visited the area before.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Let us know where you heard about us.
* **Additional Requests**: Provide any additional requests or information you would like to share with us.
* **Best Time to Contact**: Enter the best time to contact you for further inquiries.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your inquiry accurately.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible to provide you with personalized offers and responses.
