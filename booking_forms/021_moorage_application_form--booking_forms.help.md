# Moorage Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moorage Application Form is designed to collect necessary information for moorage applications. Please fill out this form with accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Info section with your basic contact information.
2. Provide detailed information about your vessel in the Vessel Info section.
3. Describe your request in the Request Details section.
4. Share specifics about the moorage you need in the Moorage Details section.
5. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info section.
6. Fill out the Submitter Info section with details about the person submitting this form.
7. Read and agree to the Submitter Terms Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (General Info, text, required/optional): This is the first part of the form where you will provide your basic contact information.
- **Vessel Info** (Vessel Info, text, optional): This section is meant for you to share detailed information about your vessel. Be as detailed as possible to ensure your moorage application is processed efficiently.
- **Request Details** (Request Details, text, optional): In this section, please provide detailed information about your request.
- **Moorage Details** (Moorage Details, text, optional): Here, you will provide specific details about the moorage you need.
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
- **Submitter Info** (Submitter Info, text, optional): In this section, please provide detailed information about the person submitting this form.
- **Submitter Terms Agreement** (Submitter Terms Agreement, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms of submission, which is a necessary step to complete the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields for your moorage application to be processed correctly.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to ensure easy understanding.
