# RV Park Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Park Check Out Form is used to document the details of a customer's checkout from an RV park. It is an essential tool for park management to keep track of customer transactions and ensure accurate financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your Reservation ID, which is the unique identifier assigned to your stay.
2.  Enter the name of the RV park you stayed at.
3.  Enter your campsite number.
4.  Enter your name as it appears on your payment receipt.
5.  Enter the date and time you checked out.
6.  Select your level of satisfaction with your stay.
7.  Enter any comments about your stay.
8.  Enter the amount charged to your account.
9.  Select the method of payment you used.
10. If you used a different payment method than listed, enter it here.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter any notes or comments for the park manager.
14. Select the level of satisfaction of the park manager with your stay.
15. Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
16. Enter any additional comments.
17. Enter the park manager's ID.
18. Enter the park reservation ID.
19. Enter the park manager's signature.
20. Enter the park manager's name.
21. Enter the date of check out.
22. Select the payment status.
23. Enter any comments about the payment status.
24. Enter the final amount charged.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Id**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to your stay. 
* **Park Name**: Enter the name of the RV park you stayed at. 
* **Campsite Number**: Enter the number of your campsite. 
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your payment receipt. 
* **Check Out Date**: Enter the date you checked out. 
*   Format: MM/DD/YY
* **Check Out Time**: Enter the time you checked out. 
*   Format: 12:00 AM/12:00 PM
*   **Guest Satisfaction**: Select one of the following options:
    + Very Satisfied
    + Somewhat Satisfied
    + Somewhat Dissatisfied
    + Very Dissatisfied
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about your stay.
*   **Charge Amount**: Enter the total amount charged to your account. 
*   **Payment Method**: Select one of the following options:
    + Credit Card
    + Cash
    + Other
*   **Other Payment Method**: If you used a different payment method, enter it here.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Manager Notes**: Enter any notes or comments for the park manager.
*   **Manager Satisfaction**: Select one of the following options:
    + Very Satisfied
    + Somewhat Satisfied
    + Somewhat Dissatisfied
    + Very Dissatisfied
*   **Follow Up**: Indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your stay.
*   **Park Manager ID**: Enter the park manager's ID.
*   **Park Reservation ID**: Enter the park reservation ID.
*   **Manager Signature**: Enter the park manager's signature.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter the park manager's name.
*   **Date of Check Out**: Enter the date of check out.
*   **Payment Status**: Select one of the following options:
    + Paid
    + Unpaid
    + Partially Paid
*   **Payment Status Comments**: Enter any comments about the payment status.
*   **Final Charges**: Enter the final amount charged.
