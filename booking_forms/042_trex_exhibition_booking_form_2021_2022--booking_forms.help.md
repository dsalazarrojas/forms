# TREX Exhibition Booking Form 2021 2022 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from event organizers or participants to facilitate planning and coordination for exhibitions. It collects details about the venue, event name, dates, duration, type, and description, as well as contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the exhibition venue from the available options.
2. Enter the name of the exhibition.
3. Choose the start and end times of the exhibition.
4. Select the dates of the exhibition from the available options.
5. Choose the duration of the exhibition from the available options.
6. Select the type of exhibition (Art, Music, or Drama).
7. Provide a detailed description of the exhibition.
8. (Optional) Select the contact method (Email or Phone) to receive updates or further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Exhibition Venue** (`exhibition_venue`, text, required): Enter the name of the location where the exhibition will take place.
- **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, text, required): Enter the title or name of the exhibition.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, date, required): Choose the start time of the exhibition.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Choose the end time of the exhibition.
- **Exhibition Dates** (`exhibition_dates`, select_one, required): Select the dates of the exhibition from the available options.
- **Exhibition Duration** (`exhibition_duration`, select_multiple, required): Choose the duration of the exhibition from the available options.
- **Exhibition Type** (`exhibition_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of exhibition (Art, Music, or Drama).
- **Exhibition Description** (`exhibition_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the exhibition.
- **Venue Contact Details** (`venue_contact_details`, select_multiple, optional): (Optional) Select the contact method (Email or Phone) to receive updates or further information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Select the correct exhibition dates and duration.
- Choose the correct contact method if you wish to receive updates or further information.
- Provide a clear and detailed description of the exhibition.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
