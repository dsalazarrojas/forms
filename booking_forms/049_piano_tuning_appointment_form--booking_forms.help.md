# Piano Tuning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a piano tuning appointment with a professional piano technician. To submit the form, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the customer information section with your name, contact number, email, and a brief description of your piano (if applicable).
2. Choose a suitable appointment time and date for your piano tuning session.
3. Select the technician you would like to work with.
4. Indicate your preferred visit duration.
5. Specify the service details, such as which piano needs tuning.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your appointment. Please note that the available dates may vary based on the technician's schedule.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your appointment. Ensure that you choose a time that fits within the available slots provided by the technician.
- **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, optional): Choose the name of the technician you would like to schedule with. Please note that not all technicians may be available at the time of your request.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so that we can easily reach you to confirm your appointment and provide any updates.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can send you appointment confirmation and any relevant information.
- **Piano Type** (`piano_type`, text, optional): If applicable, please provide details about the type of piano that needs tuning.
- **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Indicate the length of your appointment. Please note that this is an estimate and can be adjusted based on the technician’s recommendation.
- **Service Details** (`service_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the service you require, such as which parts of the piano need attention.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided how you plan to pay for your appointment. Please note that not all methods may be available for all appointments.
- **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Leave a message or any special requests you have for the technician or the appointment.

## Tips
* Please double-check your contact information and ensure it’s accurate to ensure we can reach you and confirm the appointment successfully.
* If you have any questions, concerns, or special requests, please don’t hesitate to include them in the "Customer Message" field.
* Available slots and technician schedules are subject to change. We will notify you with any updates regarding your appointment.
