# vacation rental inquiry form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to submit their inquiries for vacation rentals. It collects information about the guest, their contact details, travel dates, accommodation preferences, and any additional inquiries they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your guest information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
3. Choose your preferred check-in and check-out dates for the inquiry.
4. Select your desired stay length (short, long, or flexible).
5. Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in (house, apartment, or other).
6. If you have a preferred arrival time, select it from the options.
7. Enter any additional messages or questions you have about the rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact** (`contact`, number, optional): Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Date Inquiry** (`date_inquiry`, date, optional): Choose your preferred check-in and check-out dates for the inquiry.
* **Stay Length** (`stay_length`, select_one, optional): Select how long you plan to stay (short, long, or flexible).
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in (house, apartment, or other).
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred arrival time if applicable.
* **Inquiry Message** (`inquiry_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional messages or questions you have about the rental.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is complete and accurate.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, such as a preferred accommodation type, make sure to include them in the inquiry message.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
