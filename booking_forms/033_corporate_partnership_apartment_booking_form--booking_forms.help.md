# Corporate Partnership Apartment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a corporate partnership apartment booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the General Information page with your basic contact details.
* In the Company Details page, enter information about your company.
* On the Accommodation Preferences page, specify your preferences for the accommodation.
* In the Arrival and Check-in page, enter your arrival and departure dates and times.
* On the Accommodation Selection page, choose whether you would like to book an accommodation.
* In the Room Preferences page, enter any specific room preferences.
* In the Special Requests page, enter any additional requests you may have.
* In the Contact Information page, enter your contact details.
* On the Submit page, review your entries and click on "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**
	+ General Information (Text, Optional): This is where you can enter your basic contact details such as your name and email.

	+ Company Details (Text, Optional): Here, enter information about your company.

* **Room Preferences (Page 6)**

* **Special Requests (Page 7)**
	+ Special Requests (Text, Optional): Enter any additional requests you may have.
* **Contact Information (Page 8)**

* **Submit (Page 9)**

* **Terms of Service (Page 10)**

* **Accept Terms of Service (Page 11)**
	+ Accept Terms of Service (Select Multiple, Optional): Accept the terms of service.
* **Email (Page 12)**

* **Date of Arrival (Page 13)**

* **Time of Arrival (Page 14)**

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all the required fields.
* If a field is optional, fill it in if you think it's necessary.
