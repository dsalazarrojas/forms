# Music Festival Performer Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from music festival performers about their accommodation needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to be completed by music festival performers. Follow these steps:

* Fill in the form completely and honestly to ensure that the festival organizers can make the best possible arrangements for you.
* Each field is described in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer name** (Performer name, text, required: false): Please fill in your name as a performer.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach you quickly.
* **Accommodation type** (Accommodation type, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you need accommodation, "No" otherwise.
* **Accommodation location** (Accommodation location, text, required: false): If you need accommodation, enter the location you would like to be accommodated.
* **Accommodation details** (Accommodation details, text, required: false): If you have any specific accommodation requirements, enter them here.
* **Special requests** (Special requests, text, required: false): If you have any special requests, such as specific amenities or requirements, enter them here.
* **Event name** (Event name, text, required: false): If you have any specific details about the event, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields completely and honestly to ensure that the festival organizers can make the best possible arrangements for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will contact you for further information and clarification if needed.
* Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
