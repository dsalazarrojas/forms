# Meeting Space Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Space Request Form is used to request a meeting space for a specific meeting, training, or event. Fill out this form to provide information about your meeting, and the meeting space administrator will process and assign a suitable meeting space for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the space type that best suits your needs.
2. Choose the meeting start and end dates and times.
3. Enter the number of guests attending.
4. Select the meeting type (meeting, training, or event).
5. Choose your preferred meeting space options (e.g., Meeting Room, Training Room, Conference Room).
6. If needed, add any special requests for your meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Space Request Form** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_12`, text, optional): 
    This field is for any additional comments or requests related to your meeting space request.

* **Meeting Space Preference** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_9`, select_multiple, optional): 
    This field allows you to select multiple meeting space preferences for your meeting. Please choose the spaces that best fit your needs.

* **Meeting Space** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_10`, select_multiple, optional): 
    This field is similar to the meeting space preference field but with a different label. Please use it to select your preferred meeting spaces.

* **Special Requests** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_11`, text, optional): 
    This field is for any additional comments or requests related to your meeting space request.

* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_8`, select_one, optional): 
    This field is for selecting the type of meeting (meeting, training, or event).

* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_6`, time, optional): 
    Enter the end time of your meeting.

* **Meeting End Date** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_5`, date, optional): 
    Enter the end date of your meeting.

* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_4`, time, optional): 
    Enter the start time of your meeting.

* **Meeting Start Date** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_3`, date, optional): 
    Enter the start date of your meeting.

* **Number of Guests** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_7`, number, optional): 
    Enter the expected number of guests attending your meeting.

* **Select Space** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_2`, select_one, optional): 
    Choose a space type that best suits your needs (e.g., Meeting Room, Training Room, Conference Room).

* **Meeting Space Request** (`meeting_space_request_form_page_1`, text, optional): 
    This field is for entering a brief description or purpose of your meeting.
