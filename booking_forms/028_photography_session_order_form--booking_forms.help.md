# Photography Session Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Session Order Form is a form designed for customers to book a photography session with your company. The form allows customers to provide necessary information for the session, such as their contact details, session preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select a preferred Session Date and Session Time for your photography session.
4. Choose a Session Duration for your session.
5. Provide the Session Location where you would like the session to take place.
6. Choose how you would like to pay for your session (select all applicable methods).
7. Enter the payment Amount you wish to pay.
8. Add any relevant Notes for the session.
9. Select the tool to be assigned for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date, required): Select a date for your photography session from the calendar.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, time, required): Select a time for your photography session.
* **Session Duration** (`Session Duration`, number, required): Choose how long you would like your session to last (in minutes).
* **Session Location** (`Session Location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like the session to take place.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your session (select all applicable methods: Cash, Credit card, PayPal).
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to pay for your session.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information for the session.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required): Enter the tool that will be used for the session (e.g., Camera, Lenses, etc.).
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): Enter your name or title as a submitter (e.g., "John Doe, Photographer" for a photographer).
