# Ocean Freight Booking Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display essential information about ocean freight shipments, enabling users to track and manage these shipments effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing information about the ocean freight shipment you are tracking in the "Ocean Freight Booking Details" field.
2. Next, enter the relevant freight details in the "Freight Details" field.
3. Enter shipping details, including any relevant information about the shipment's origin and destination, in the "Shipping Details" field.
4. Provide a current status update for the shipment in the "Tracking Status" field.
5. Document any progress or updates related to the shipment in the "Shipment Progress" field.
6. Add any relevant notes or comments about the shipment in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ocean Freight Booking Details**: Enter a brief description of the ocean freight shipment you are tracking.
* **Freight Details**: Enter information about the freight being shipped, including the type and quantity.
* **Shipping Details**: Enter information about the shipping vessel, cargo type, and other relevant details.
* **Tracking Status**: Enter a brief update about the current status of the shipment, such as "Awaiting departure" or "Arrived at destination."
* **Shipment Progress**: Document any updates or progress related to the shipment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the shipment, such as contact information for the shipper or consignee.
