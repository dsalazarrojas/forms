# Mental Health Counseling Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and preferences for a counseling session. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your accurate and honest information. 
2. Make sure to review and correct any errors before submission. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your full name and contact information. Please make sure to provide accurate details so we can reach you back.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, note, required: false): This field is a space for you to add any specific preferences or notes you think are relevant to your session. Please use this field to share any information that might help us better understand your needs.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: false): Please select a date that you would prefer for your session. Keep in mind that we will do our best to accommodate your preferred date but may not always be able to do so.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time that works best for you. If you have any specific time constraints, please let us know in this field.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, required: false): Add any relevant notes about your session. This can include any information you think will help us understand you better.
* **Assigned Therapist** (`assigned_therapist`, select_one, required: false): Please choose whether you would like to be assigned a specific therapist. If you have a preference, please select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required: false): Indicate the preferred duration of your session. Please choose a number that represents the length of time you would like to spend with your assigned therapist.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly. 
* We will do our best to accommodate your preferences but may not always be able to do so.
