# time_slot_booking_form_usm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for booking a specific time slot for a student meeting or session.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name and ID in the "Student Name" and "Student ID" fields respectively.
2. Select the faculty member's name for the booking.
3. Choose the time slot from the given options or enter a specific time slot using the time format.
4. Specify the date of the booking in the "Date Of Booking" field.
5. Briefly describe the purpose of the visit in the "Purpose Of Visit" field.
6. Select the department related to the booking.
7. Enter the room number where the meeting will take place.
8. Select the staff member handling the booking.

• **Student ID** (`student_id`, number): Enter the student's ID.
• **Faculty** (`faculty_name`, text): Select the faculty member's name for the booking.
• **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, time): Choose the time slot from the given options or enter a specific time slot using the time format.

• **Purpose Of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, text): Briefly describe the purpose of the visit.
• **Department** (`department`, text): Select the department related to the booking.
• **Room Number** (`room_number`, text): Enter the room number where the meeting will take place.
