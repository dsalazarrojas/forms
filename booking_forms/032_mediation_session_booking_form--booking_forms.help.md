# Mediation Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Session Booking Form is designed to collect necessary information for booking a mediation session. This form is intended for individuals or organizations seeking conflict resolution services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter **General Information**, providing any relevant details about the session or mediation process.
2. Select a **Mediator** from the available options to facilitate the session.
3. Choose a **Date** and **Time** for the session, as specified in the dropdown list.
4. Enter the **Party Name**, **Party Email**, and other relevant contact details to ensure smooth communication.
5. Provide **Session Notes**, if necessary, to help the mediator understand the context and requirements of the session.
6. Submit the form to initiate the mediation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter a brief description of the session or mediation process, if applicable.
* **Mediator**: Select a mediator to facilitate the session.
* **Date**: Choose a date for the session from the provided options.
* **Time**: Select a time for the session from the provided options.
* **Party Name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization participating in the session.
* **Party Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or organization participating in the session.
* **Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or organization participating in the session.
* **Party Address**: Enter the physical address of the individual or organization participating in the session.
* **Party Other**: Enter any additional information about the party participating in the session.
* **Session Notes**: Provide any additional notes or context for the session.
* **Session Details**: Enter any additional session details.
* **Submit**: Confirm and submit the form to initiate the mediation process.
