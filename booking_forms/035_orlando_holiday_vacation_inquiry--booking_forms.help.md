# Orlando Holiday Vacation Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your planned visit to Orlando during the holiday season. Please provide accurate and complete information to facilitate our processing of your inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your arrival and departure dates in the "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" fields.
2. Select your preferred mode of transportation from the list.
3. Choose your preferred traveler type from the list.
4. If applicable, select your accommodation type.
5. Enter your preferred language of travel.
6. If you have any special needs, provide details in the "Special Needs" field.
7. Enter any additional information relevant to your trip.
8. If applicable, provide details of your travel agent.
9. Enter any special requests.
10. Enter your travel agency contact information (if applicable).
11. Check the "Terms and Conditions" field to acknowledge and agree to our terms and conditions.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to arrive in Orlando.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to depart from Orlando.
* **Number of Travelers** (`number_of_travelers`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your travel purpose.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Preferred Mode of Transportation** (`preferred_mode_of_transportation`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply: Yes/No.
* **Preferred Traveler Type** (`preferred_traveler_type`, select_one, required): Select your preferred traveler type (e.g. Yes/No).
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Select your accommodation type (if applicable).
* **Travel Language** (`travel_language`, select_one, optional): Select your travel language.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special needs.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your trip.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of your travel agent.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any special requests.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter payment details (if applicable).
* **Travel Insurance** (`travel_insurance`, text, optional): Enter travel insurance information (if applicable).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to submit your form.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). It is essential to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
