# Photography Studio Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Studio Booking Form is a crucial part of the booking process. It gathers essential information from clients and helps our team prepare for their photo shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the **Photographer Details** field.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** (email and phone number) so we can get in touch with you.
3. Fill in the **Booking Details** section with your requested photo shoot date and time.
4. Add any additional **Booking Notes** if you have specific requirements.
5. Choose the type of photo shoot you're interested in, either **In Studio** or **Outside**.
6. Select a payment status, either **Paid** or **Unpaid**.
7. If you have any **Client Notes**, feel free to add them.
8. Finally, select an **Assigned Agent** from the list to ensure we know who to contact you with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Details**: This field is for you to provide details about the photographer assigned to your shoot.
* **Contact Info**: This is where you enter your email address and phone number so our team can reach out to you about your booking.
* **Booking Details**: Enter the date and time you'd like your photo shoot to be scheduled.
* **Booking Notes**: Add any extra notes or requirements you have about your photo shoot.
* **Booking Type**: Choose between **In Studio** or **Outside** photo shoot options.
* **Payment Status**: Select whether you've already paid for your booking or not.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name for our records.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your photo shoot.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your photo shoot.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your photo shoot.
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date of your photo shoot.
* **Status**: Select the status of your booking.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment.
* **Booking Type**: Select the type of booking you're making.
* **Photo Session ID**: Enter the ID of your photo session.
* **Photo Session Type**: Enter the type of photo session.
* **Photo Session Date**: Enter the date of your photo session.
* **Photo Session Time**: Enter the time of your photo session.
* **Photo Session Duration**: Enter the duration of your photo session.
* **Client Notes**: Add any notes for your client.
* **Staff Notes**: Add any notes for our staff.
* **Assigned Agent**: Select the agent assigned to your booking.
