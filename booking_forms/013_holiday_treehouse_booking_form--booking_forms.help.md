# holiday_treehouse_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday treehouse booking form is used to book a holiday treehouse for a specific period of time. It asks for the start and end dates of the stay, details about the guests, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of your stay. This is the date you would like your holiday treehouse booking to commence.
2. Enter the end date of your stay. This is the date you would like your holiday treehouse booking to finish.
3. Provide details about your guests. This can include their names, ages, and any other relevant information you think is necessary.
4. Specify the length of your stay. This is the number of days you would like to stay in the holiday treehouse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### start_date
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you would like your holiday treehouse booking to start.

### end_date
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you would like your holiday treehouse booking to end.

### guest_details
* **Guest Details** (Text, Optional): Provide details about your guests, such as their names, ages, and any other relevant information.

### stay_length
* **Stay Length** (Number, Required): Specify the number of days you would like to stay in the holiday treehouse.

### special_requests
* **Special Requests** (Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or requirements for your stay, such as a crib or extra towels, please enter them here.
