# Photography Session Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a photography session with a photographer. Please fill in the form with your information to book a session with the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information:
   - Provide your **Customer Name** in the Customer Name field.
   - Enter your **Email** address in the Email field.
   - Enter your **Phone** number in the Phone field.
   - Provide a brief description of the **Session Details**, including the date, time, and location of the session.

2. Fill in the photographer's information:
   - If you are a photographer, provide your **Photographer Name** in the Photographer Name field.
   - Enter your **Email** address in the Photographer Email field.
   - Enter your **Phone** number in the Photographer Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your full name as a customer.
* **Email**: The email address associated with your booking, used for updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number, used for potential future communication.
* **Session Details**: A brief description of the photography session, including date, time, and location.
* **Agreement**: An optional field to provide any additional information or agreements you would like to make about the session.
* **Photographer Name**: Your name if you are the photographer.
* **Photographer Email**: The email address of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone**: The phone number of the photographer.
