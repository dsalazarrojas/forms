# Travel Itinerary Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for gathering and sharing travel-related information to facilitate effective travel coordination and communication between travel coordinators, attendees, and participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide travel dates in the **travel_dates** field.
2. Select your travel destinations from the **travel_destinations** options.
3. Add any relevant group members information in the **group_members** field.
4. Describe any travel activities in the **travel_activities** field.
5. Enter flight information in the **flight_info** field.
6. Add accommodation details in the **accommodation_info** field.
7. Include any travel-related notes in the **travel_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_dates** (`travel_dates`, text, required: false): Enter your travel dates, including the start and end dates of your trip.
* **travel_destinations** (`travel_destinations`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the destinations you plan to visit on your trip.
* **group_members** (`group_members`, text, required: false): List the names of the individuals or groups traveling with you.
* **travel_activities** (`travel_activities`, text, required: false): Describe the activities you plan to engage in during your trip.
* **flight_info** (`flight_info`, text, required: false): Enter your flight details, including flight numbers, departure and arrival times, and any other relevant information.
* **accommodation_info** (`accommodation_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about your accommodation, such as hotel or resort names, addresses, and contact information.
* **travel_notes** (`travel_notes`, text, required: true): Add any additional travel notes or information that may be helpful for your travel coordinator to know.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure your travel coordinator has a clear understanding of your travel plans.
- Use the **select_multiple** option for travel destinations to choose multiple locations.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the **travel_notes** field.
