# Surgery Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Surgery Reservation Form is designed to gather information from patients for a scheduled surgery. This form helps the medical staff to prepare for the procedure by capturing essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate information. Each field is described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false)
This field is for entering patient details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.

*   Please provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 2. **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, required: false)
This field is for entering the doctor's details such as name, specialty, and contact information.

*   Enter your name as it appears on your medical license.
*   Provide your specialty or area of expertise.

### 3. **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required: false)
This field is for entering appointment details such as date, time, and location.

*   Enter the date and time of your scheduled appointment.
*   Provide the location where the surgery will take place.

### 4. **Surgery Information** (`surgery_info`, text, required: false)
This field is for entering the specifics of the surgery such as procedure type and any pre/post-operative instructions.

*   Clearly describe the type of surgery you will undergo.
*   Enter any pre or post-operative instructions you have been provided.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the medical staff for assistance.
