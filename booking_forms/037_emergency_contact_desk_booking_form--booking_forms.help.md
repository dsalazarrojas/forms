# Emergency Contact Desk Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for emergency contact desk bookings. It will help us assist you in arranging a visit at your earliest convenience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure a smooth booking process.
- Use the booking form to enter your name and the details of your visit.
- Use the schedule form to provide your availability and preferred time slot.
- Use the contact details form to provide your contact information and any special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Form (`booking_form`, text, optional)**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during your visit. Please keep this field concise and accurate.
* **Schedule Form (`schedule_form`, text, optional)**: Provide your preferred time slot for the visit, taking into account your availability. You can enter this information in any format you like (e.g., 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, or any date/time range).
* **Contact Details (`contact_details`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional contact information or special requirements you may have for your visit, such as a phone number or email address.

## Tips
- Please double-check your details before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
- We will respond to your form submission at the earliest opportunity.
