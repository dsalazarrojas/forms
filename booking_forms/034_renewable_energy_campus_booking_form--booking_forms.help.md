# Renewable Energy Campus Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a visit to a renewable energy campus for groups.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required information.
- Make sure to submit the form before the visit date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your email or phone number].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your visit.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number.

### Group Information
* **Group Information** (`group_info`, text, optional): Provide a description of your group.

### Visit Purpose
* **Visit Purpose** (`visit_purpose`, select_one, optional): Choose the purpose of your visit (School, Nonprofit, Group).

### Group Size
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your group.

### Visit Date
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of your visit.

### Visit Time
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your visit.

### Preferred Visit Time
* **Preferred Visit Time** (`preferred_visit_time`, time, optional): Choose your preferred visit time.

### Preferred Visit Date
* **Preferred Visit Date** (`preferred_visit_date`, date, optional): Choose your preferred visit date.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your visit.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful visit.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your email or phone number].
