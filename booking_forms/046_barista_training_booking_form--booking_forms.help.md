# Barista Training Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of the Barista Training Booking Form is to book a spot in one of our training sessions for baristas interested in improving their skills. The form allows participants to select their preferred training course, provide their contact information, and schedule their preferred dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a training session, follow these steps:

1. Select the training course you wish to attend from the options provided.
2. Choose your current experience level to help us group you with similar peers.
3. Choose your preferred training date and time.
4. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
5. Mention any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
6. Select your preferred payment method.
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to our cancellation and safety policies.
8. Finally, indicate how you found our training center and confirm your understanding of the safety protocols.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Select Your Training Course --**: Choose the course that interests you from the list of available options.
* **Experience Level**: Select your current level of experience, helping us group you with similar peers to ensure optimal learning.
* **Preferred Training Date**: Select the date on which you would like to attend the training session.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Choose between Morning, Afternoon, or Evening session times.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Provide the email address where you will receive course materials and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Dietary Restrictions / Allergies**: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please mention them so we can accommodate your needs.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred method of payment.
* **Cancellation Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you understand our cancellation policy and are aware that cancellations within 24 hours of the session are non-refundable.
* **Safety and Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you understand and agree to follow all safety protocols when using machinery.
* **How did you find our training center?**: Indicate how you found our training center.
* **Date Signed**: This field will be populated automatically upon submission.
