# Fishing Permit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a reservation for a fishing permit. It will help you reserve a spot for a specific fishing trip by providing necessary information such as customer name, contact details, and type of fishing trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Select a date for the reservation using the "Date of Reservation" field.
6. Choose a time for the reservation using the "Time of Reservation" field.
7. Indicate the number of guests accompanying you on the trip in the "Number of Guests" field.
8. Select the type of fishing trip you want to go on (Fresh Water, Salt Water, or Both).
9. Enter the total number of people going on the trip in the "Number of People" field.
10. Provide your fishing license number if you have one.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
12. Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Reservation" field.
13. Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Reservation (Date of Reservation)**: Enter the date you want to go on the fishing trip in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time of Reservation (Time of Reservation)**: Select the time you want to go on the fishing trip.
* **Number of Guests (Number of Guests)**: Indicate the number of people accompanying you on the trip.
* **Type of Fishing Trip (Type of Fishing Trip)**: Choose the type of fishing trip you want to go on (Fresh Water, Salt Water, or Both).
* **Number of People (Number of People)**: Enter the total number of people going on the trip.
* **Fishing License Number (Fishing License Number)**: Enter your fishing license number if you have one.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
* **Confirm Reservation (Confirm Reservation)**: Select "Yes" to confirm your reservation.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Click this button to finalize your reservation.
* **Cancel (Cancel)**: Click this button to cancel your reservation.
