# Historic Walking Tour Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and agreement to terms and conditions for a historic walking tour.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide the tour date and start time you are interested in.
3. Enter the duration of the tour in minutes.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
5. Review the tour details and start location.
6. Confirm your agreement to the participant terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your personal details, including your name and email address.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with tour updates and reminders.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you directly.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to participate in the tour.
* **Tour Start Time** (`tour_start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the tour in minutes.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Tour Details** (`tour_details`, text, required): Enter any information or requests you have about the tour.
* **Tour Start Location** (`tour_start_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the tour will start.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your participation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the participant terms and conditions. Choose 'I agree' to confirm.
* **Participant Agreed** (`participant_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Confirm you have agreed to the participant terms and conditions. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Participant Terms Agreed** (`participant_terms_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Confirm you have agreed to the participant terms and conditions. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to participate in the tour. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, text, optional): Enter your declaration of participation.
* **Participant Terms Declaration** (`participant_terms_declaration`, text, optional): Enter your terms of participation.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature.
* **Participant Agreement Signed** (`participant_agreement_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the participant terms and conditions was signed. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Participant Declaration Signed** (`participant_declaration_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm your declaration of participation was signed. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Agreement Signed** (`participant_agreed`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the participant terms and conditions was signed. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Declaration Signed** (`participant_declaration_signed`, select_one, required): Confirm your declaration of participation was signed. Choose 'Yes' to confirm.
