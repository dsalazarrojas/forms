# rural_regeneration_residency_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a rural regeneration residency project. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, select the preferred dates for your project. This will help us determine the availability of the residency space.
- Next, provide details about your project, including project start date, project duration, project end date, project location, and project type (music, visual, or performance).
- Then, enter your artist information, including your name, email, and phone number.
- Finally, select the submission option that best suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Request** (`booking_request`, `text`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your project or request.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the dates you are interested in for your project. Please note that the availability of the dates will be determined by the residency space.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your project, including any relevant details that may help us understand your proposal.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your project on (if applicable).
* **Project Start Time** (`project_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to start your project on (if applicable).
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Select the duration of your project (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your project on (if applicable).
* **Project End Time** (`project_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to end your project on (if applicable).
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief statement about your project and its significance.
* **Project Image** (`project_image`, `text`, optional): Upload an image or video file to accompany your proposal.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the artist or submitter.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter your project budget (if applicable).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where your project will take place.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your project (approved, rejected, or pending).
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of your project (music, visual, or performance).
* **Project URL** (`project_url`, `text`, optional): Enter a URL related to your project (if applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the submission option that best suits your needs (submit, submit and edit, or submit and save draft).
* **Project Start Date 2** (`project_start_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your project on (if applicable).
* **Project Duration 2** (`project_duration_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the duration of your project (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Project End Date 2** (`project_end_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to end your project on (if applicable).
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.
