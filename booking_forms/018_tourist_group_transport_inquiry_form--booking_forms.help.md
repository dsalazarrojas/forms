# Tourist Group Transport Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourist Group Transport Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential details about a tourist group's transport preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine the group size and enter it in the `group_size` field.
2.  Select the preferred mode of transport in the `transport_preferences` field. You can choose one or multiple options, such as Air Travel, Ground Travel, or Water Travel.
3.  Enter the desired date and time of transport in the `transport_date` and `transport_time` fields. Note that these fields are not required, but including the information will help us make more accurate arrangements.
4.  Provide the arrival city and time in the `arrival_city` and `arrival_time` fields. This will help us arrange for the correct pick-up and drop-off points.
5.  Select the preferred method of transportation from the `transportation_method` field. You can choose from Bus, Taxi, Train, Flight, or Private Transport.
6.  If you have any special requests, such as extra luggage or equipment, you can mention them in the `special_requests` field.
7.  Enter the contact information of the group's representative in the `contact_information` field. This is not required but will help us reach out to you in case of any issues.
8.  Provide the email address of the group's representative in the `email` field. This is not required but will help us keep in touch with you.
9.  Enter the phone number of the group's representative in the `phone` field. This is not required but will help us contact you quickly.
10.  Add any additional details about the group in the `additional_details` field. This can include any specific requirements or needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required: false): Enter the number of people in your group.
*   **Transport Preferences** (`transport_preferences`, select multiple, required: true): Select the mode of transport you prefer. You can choose Air Travel, Ground Travel, or Water Travel.
*   **Transport Date** (`transport_date`, date, required: false): Enter the desired date for transport. This will help us arrange for the transport on the correct date.
*   **Transport Time** (`transport_time`, time, required: false): Enter the desired time for transport. This will help us arrange for the transport at the correct time.
*   **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of arrival. This will help us arrange for the correct pick-up and drop-off points.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of arrival. This will help us arrange for the transport at the correct time.
*   **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, select one, required: true): Select the preferred mode of transport. You can choose from Bus, Taxi, Train, Flight, or Private Transport.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests, such as extra luggage or equipment, enter them here.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, required: false): Enter the contact information of your representative. This will help us reach out to you in case of any issues.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your representative. This will help us keep in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your representative. This will help us contact you quickly.
*   **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about your group. This can include specific requirements or needs.
