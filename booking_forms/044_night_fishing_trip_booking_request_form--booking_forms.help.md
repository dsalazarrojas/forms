# night_fishing_trip_booking_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in booking a night fishing trip. It helps the trip organizers to understand user preferences and details, making the booking process more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the "User Details" page with your name, email, and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
* On the "Fishing Preferences" page, select your preferred fishing spot, gear, and any specific fishing style you're interested in.
* On the "Trip Preferences" page, choose your preferred trip duration, date, and time.
* On the "Payment Details" page, provide your payment information, including your credit card number and expiration date.
* On the "Confirmation" page, review and confirm your booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (User Details, text, optional):
	+ This page is where you can provide any additional information about yourself, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Fishing Preferences** (Fishing Preferences, text, optional):
	+ Here, you can specify your preferred fishing spot, gear, and any specific fishing style you're interested in.
* **Trip Preferences** (Trip Preferences, text, optional):
	+ Choose your preferred trip duration, date, and time.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional):
	+ Enter your credit card number and expiration date to complete your booking.
* **Confirmation** (Confirmation, text, optional):
	+ Review and confirm your booking details to ensure everything is accurate.
