# Property Photography Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Photography Service Registration form is designed to collect information from clients or customers interested in a property photography service. This form helps us understand their contact details, property requirements, and preferred service dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your contact name and email address in the "Contact Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter the address of the property you're interested in.
5. Select the type of property you're interested in (House, Condo, Townhouse, Apartment, or Other).
6. Add any additional property details in the "Property Details" field.
7. Indicate which services you're requesting (select one or more from the list).
8. Choose a preferred date and time for the service.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Property Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address of the property you're interested in.
* **Property Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of property (House, Condo, Townhouse, Apartment, or Other).
* **Property Details** (text, required/optional): Add any additional details about the property.
* **Services Requesting** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the services you're requesting (Yes/No).
* **Preferred Date** (date, required/optional): Choose a preferred date for the service.
* **Preferred Time** (time, required/optional): Choose a preferred time for the service.
* **Additional Comments** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with your submission.
- Double-check your contact email and phone number for correctness.
- If you're unsure about the property type, select "Other" and provide detailed information in the "Property Details" field.
- For services, you can select one or more options depending on your requirements.
- For date and time preferences, choose a date in the YYYY-MM-DD format and a time in the HH:MM AM/PM format.
- Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any further information about your request.
