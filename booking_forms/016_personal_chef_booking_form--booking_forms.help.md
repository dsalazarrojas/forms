# Prophets Personal Chef Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients book events with a personal chef. It captures necessary details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the client and the chef.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the "Event Type" from the dropdown menu that best describes your event.
3. Choose the "Event Capacity" that suits your needs.
4. Select your dietary preferences from the available options.
5. Enter the event details, including the event name and date.
6. Select the "Number of Guests" and "Booking Start Time" that fit your event.
7. Choose the "Booking Duration" and "Booking Date" for the event.
8. Select the payment method and terms that apply to your booking.
9. Finally, add any additional details about your event in the provided text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, date, required): Enter the name and date of your event. This information will help the chef prepare for your special occasion.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_one, required): Select your preferred event type, capacity, and dietary options to ensure the chef accommodates your needs.
* **Chef Details** (`chef_details`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions for the chef, please enter them here.
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the number of guests, booking start time, booking duration, and booking date for your event.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method and terms that apply to your booking.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Enter any extra information about your event that the chef should be aware of.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure the chef can meet your needs.
- Double-check your event details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with your booking, contact us at form@gic.mx.
