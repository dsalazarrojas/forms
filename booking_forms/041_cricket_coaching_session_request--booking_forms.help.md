# Cricket Coaching Session Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Coaching Session Request form is used to collect information from users who want to request a cricket coaching session. This form helps the coaching team schedule and organize sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a type of coaching session:
	* Cricket Academy (Academy X, Location Y)
	* Cricket Club (Club Z, Location W)
	* Private Coach (Coach A, Location B)
2. Select a date and time for the session:
	* Enter a date using the calendar picker or manually type in a date.
3. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter your name as it appears on your cricket team's registration or ID card.
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you about your session.
	* Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request a Session** (`session_request`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coaching session you want to attend. This will help us schedule the session with suitable coaches and resources.
* **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, date, required): Select a date and time for your coaching session. Please note that the system will display available coaching slots based on your selection.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional): This is your name as it appears on your cricket team's registration or ID card. If you're not sure what to enter here, please check with your team administrator.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address so we can contact you about your session. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number so we can reach you if needed. Please enter a number that we can contact you on.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional information you want to provide about your participation in the coaching session. You can use this to mention any specific areas you want to focus on during the session.
