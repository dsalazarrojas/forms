# Vacation Home Rental Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vacation Home Rental Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from guests inquiring about renting a vacation home. The form is used to gather necessary details to facilitate a smooth rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2.  Enter the guest's name and contact information in the first few fields.
3.  Select the start and end dates for the stay from the calendar or enter them manually.
4.  Choose the type of inquiry from the dropdown menu.
5.  Fill out the remaining fields according to the guest's needs and your property type preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Please enter the guest's full name.
* **Start Date** (`inquiry_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the guest's stay. 
* **End Date** (`inquiry_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the guest's stay. 
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Please enter the guest's email address.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Message** (`guest_message`, text, optional): Please enter a message to the property manager.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of inquiry. 
* **Stay Duration** (`stay_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you plan to stay.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Enter the arrival date.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional): Enter the departure date.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If the guest has any special requests, please enter them here.
* **Guest Country** (`guest_country`, select_one, optional): Select the guest's country.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property the guest is inquiring about.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are completed to ensure a smooth rental experience.
*   Check for any special requirements or restrictions before filling out the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the property manager for assistance.
