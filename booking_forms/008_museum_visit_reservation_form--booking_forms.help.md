# Museum Visit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you plan and prepare for your museum visit. Fill it out before your visit, and our staff will review it to ensure we're ready to welcome you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Select Date" field and choose the date you plan to visit the museum.
2. If you're bringing guests, select the correct number of visitors in the "Select Number of Visitors" field.
3. If applicable, choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Visitor Type" field.
4. Enter the visitor's name in the "Name" field.
5. Enter the visitor's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. If available, enter the visitor's email address in the "Email Address" field.
7. Select the time of your visit in the "Time of Visit" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. If you'd like, enter any relevant contact information for the visitor in the "Visitor's Name" and "Visitor's Contact Number" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Date**: Choose the date you plan to visit the museum.
* **Select Number of Visitors**: If you're bringing guests, select their number here.
* **Visitor Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you have a special request or specific needs, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Name**: Enter the visitor's name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Email Address**: If available, enter the visitor's email address.
* **Time of Visit**: Select the time you plan to visit the museum.
* **Additional Notes**: Write any important notes or comments here.
* **Visitor's Name**: Enter the visitor's name (if different from your own).
* **Visitor's Contact Number**: Enter the visitor's contact number (if different from their own).
