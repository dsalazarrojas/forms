# Dungeon Master Session Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players for a scheduled Dungeon Master (DM) session. The form aims to gather essential details for the DM to prepare and conduct an engaging and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the session details (page 1).
2. Provide player details (page 1).
3. Select the session time that suits your schedule (page 2).
4. Confirm your player status (page 2).
5. Choose the game system you will be using (page 2).
6. Add any session notes or remarks (optional, page 3).
7. Enter store details (optional, page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): 
  Enter the details of the session, such as the date, time, or any other relevant information.

- **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, optional): 
  Provide any relevant information about yourself as a player, such as your character's name or abilities.

- **Session Time** (`session_time`, select_one, required): 
  Please select the time for the session from the provided options.

- **Player Confirmation** (`player_confirmation`, select_multiple, required): 
  Confirm your player status by checking the corresponding boxes. This will help the DM confirm your attendance and prepare accordingly.

- **Game System** (`game_system`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Choose the game system you will be using for the session, such as D&D 5, Pathfinder, Shadowrun, or Call of Cthulhu.

- **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): 
  Add any additional remarks or notes about the session, such as specific rules or settings.

- **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, optional): 
  Enter any relevant store details, such as the location or any special instructions.
