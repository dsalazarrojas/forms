# Car Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for a car photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete all the required fields (\*) with accurate and clear information.
2.  Double-check your details before submitting the form.
3.  Ensure you have read and agreed to the booking terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Client Information --
* **Client Full Name**: \* Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: \* Enter your email address for booking confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number**: \* Enter your primary contact number for the booking.

### -- Session Details --
* **Preferred Session Date**: \* Select your preferred date for the session.
* **Preferred Session Time**: \* Select your preferred time for the session.
* **Preferred Session Location**: \* Choose the location for the session (e.g., Outdoor, Indoor, Studio, or a particular street).
* **Type of Photography Session**: \* Select the type of session you want (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, Event, or Video Production).

### -- Vehicle Information --
* **Car Make and Model**: \* Describe the make and model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: \* Enter the year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Color**: \* Enter the primary color of your vehicle.
* **Notable Modifications**: \* List any notable modifications to your vehicle, if applicable.

### -- Verification --
* **I Acknowledge the Booking Terms**: \* Confirm that you have read and agreed to the booking terms and conditions.

### -- Booking Confirmation --
* **Booking Confirmation**: \* Type your name to confirm your booking.
* **Additional Comments**: \* Provide any additional comments or requests for the booking.
* **Usage Preference**: \* Specify how you plan to use the photos (e.g., Personal, Social Media, Print/Framing, Business Marketing, or Editorial).

Note: The fields are divided into categories for easier understanding. Please fill out each section according to the provided guidance.
