# Airline Seat Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Airline Seat Reservation Form is a critical document used to collect important details about your flight reservation. It helps ensure that your seat is allocated correctly and that you have the best possible experience during your journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	* Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field. This is the name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
	* Enter your **Email Address** in the second field, ensuring it matches the email address used for your booking.
	* Provide your **Phone Number** in the third field for contact purposes.
	* Enter your **Booking Reference (PNR)**, also known as the flight number or reservation code.
	* In the next section, select your **Cabin Class** for your flight - choose from Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class.
	* Choose your preferred **Seat Number**, if available.
	* Select your **Seat Position Preference** as Window, Aisle, Middle, or Bulkhead (if available) or Exit Row (subject to requirements).
	* Answer truthfully about traveling with an infant - Yes or No, for bassinet seat allocation.
	* Confirm if you require a seat for medical or mobility reasons - Yes or No.
	* Finally, enter any additional comments about your seating preferences in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Required): Please fill in your name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
- **Email Address** (Required): Ensure this matches the email address used for your booking.
- **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact number for updates and inquiries.
- **Booking Reference (PNR)** (Required): Enter the flight number or reservation code provided to you.
- **-- Flight Information --** (Note): This section contains information related to your flight.
	+ **Flight Number** (Required): Enter your flight number or PNR for reference.
	+ **Date of Travel** (Required): Enter the date you're traveling.
	+ **Departure City** (Required): Enter the city of departure.
	+ **Arrival City** (Required): Enter the city of arrival.
- **-- Seating Selection --** (Note): This section contains information related to your seat preferences.
	+ **Cabin Class** (Required): Select your preferred cabin class.
	+ **Preferred Seat Number** (Optional): Choose your preferred seat number, if available.
	+ **Seat Position Preference** (Required): Select your preferred seat position (Window, Aisle, Middle, or Bulkhead).
	+ **Are you traveling with an infant?** (Required): Confirm if you're traveling with an infant.
	+ **Do you require a seat for medical or mobility reasons?** (Required): Answer truthfully if you need a specific seat due to health issues.
	+ **Additional Seating Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments about your seating preferences.

Note: Failure to fill the required fields may lead to delays or incorrect seat allocations. Ensure you answer all the required fields accurately and truthfully to guarantee a smooth and comfortable journey.
