# Yoga Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Class Reservation Form is designed for users to reserve a spot in a yoga class. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Details: Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide Class Details: Enter the Class Name, Start Date, End Date, and Duration of the class.
4. Check the Reservation Status of the class (if it is Available, Reserved, or Cancelled).
5. Add any relevant Class Notes.
6. Enter Payment Details: Amount, Method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer), and Status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
7. Add any notes regarding the payment.
8. Click Submit to finalize your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`User Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name and last name.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Class Details** (`Class Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the class.
* **Class Name** (`Class Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the class.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the class.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the class.
* **Duration** (`Duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the class.
* **Max Reservations** (`Max Reservations`, `number`, required: false): Enter the maximum number of reservations allowed for the class.
* **Reservation Status** (`Reservation Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reservation status of the class (Available, Reserved, Cancelled).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes regarding the class.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter payment details.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount paid for the class.
* **Method** (`Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes regarding the payment.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to finalize your reservation.
