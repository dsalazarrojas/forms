# cruise_booking_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the purpose of booking a cruise. It is intended to be used by customers to provide their personal and travel-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Select your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" dropdown menu.
* Select the type of cruise you are interested in from the "Type of Cruise" dropdown menu.
* Enter the date you plan to travel in the "Travel Date" field.
* If applicable, enter the duration of your travel in the "Travel Duration" field.
* Enter any special requests you may have in the "Special Requests" field.
* Select the type of accommodation you prefer from the "Accommodation Type" dropdown menu.
* Enter the time of your arrival in the "Arrival Time" field.
* If you are working with a travel agent, enter their name and phone number in the "Travel Agent" and "Travel Agent Phone" fields respectively.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the "Notes" field.
* If you are using a different tool, select it from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu and enter its name in the "Assigned Tool Other" field.
* If you are being assigned a location, enter it in the "Assigned Location" field.
* If you are submitting this form, enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
* Enter the date and time you submitted this form in the "Submitted Date" and "Submission Time" fields respectively.
* Finally, enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language.
* **Type of Cruise** (`Type of Cruise`, select_one, optional): Select the type of cruise you are interested in.
* **Travel Date** (`Travel Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **Travel Duration** (`Travel Duration`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the duration of your travel.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Accommodation Type** (`Accommodation Type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of accommodation you prefer.
* **Arrival Time** (`Arrival Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Travel Agent** (`Travel Agent`, text, optional): If you are working with a travel agent, enter their name.
* **Travel Agent Phone** (`Travel Agent Phone`, text, optional): If you are working with a travel agent, enter their phone number.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool you are using.
* **Assigned Tool Other** (`Assigned Tool Other`, text, optional): If you are using a different tool, enter its name.
* **Assigned Location** (`Assigned Location`, text, optional): Enter the assigned location.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): Enter your name if you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Assigned Time** (`Assigned Time`, time, optional): Enter the assigned time.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
