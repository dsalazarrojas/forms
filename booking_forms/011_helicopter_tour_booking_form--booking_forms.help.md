# Helicopter Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a helicopter tour. It requires customer information, contact details, tour details, and payment information to process the booking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer information (customer name, contact number, and contact email).
2. Provide tour details (tour date, tour time, and tour location).
3. Select the helicopter type, color, and model.
4. Enter the total number of people going on the tour.
5. Specify any special requests.
6. Confirm that the booking is complete.
7. Choose a payment method and amount.
8. Update the booking status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, required/optional): Enter the customer name.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, number, required/optional): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Contact E-mail** (contact_email, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Tour Details** (tour_details, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the tour.
* **Tour Date** (tour_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the tour.
* **Tour Time** (tour_time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the tour.
* **Payment Info** (payment_info, text, required/optional): Enter payment information.
* **Name of Guests** (name_of_guests, text, required/optional): Enter the names of the guests.
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, required/optional): Enter any special requests.
* **Helicopter Type** (helicopter_type, select_one, required/optional): Choose a helicopter type.
* **Helicopter Color** (helicopter_color, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more helicopter colors.
* **Helicopter Model** (helicopter_model, text, required/optional): Enter the helicopter model.
* **Total People** (total_people, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of people.
* **Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, required/optional): Enter any special instructions.
* **Confirm Booking** (confirm_booking, select_one, required): Confirm that the booking is complete.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose a payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Tour Cost** (tour_cost, number, required/optional): Enter the tour cost.
* **Booking Status** (booking_status, select_one, required/optional): Choose the booking status.
* **Tour Location** (tour_location, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more tour locations.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment status.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments.
* **Booking Date** (booking_date, date, required/optional): Enter the booking date.
* **Tour Time** (tour_time, time, required/optional): Enter the tour time.
* **Customer ID** (customer_id, text, required/optional): Enter the customer ID.
* **Confirm Booking** (confirm_booking, select_one, required): Confirm that the booking is complete.
* **Helicopter Type** (helicopter_type, select_one, required/optional): Choose a helicopter type.
* **Helicopter Color** (helicopter_color, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more helicopter colors.
* **Helicopter Model** (helicopter_model, text, required/optional): Enter the helicopter model.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the tour date and time are accurate to avoid any conflicts.
* Choose the correct helicopter type, color, and model for the tour.
* Enter the correct payment method and amount.
* Confirm the booking before proceeding.
* Enter any special requests or instructions to ensure a smooth tour experience.
