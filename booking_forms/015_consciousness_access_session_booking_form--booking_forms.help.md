# Consciousness Access Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book sessions for Consciousness Access, a type of meditation or mindfulness session. Fill out this form to schedule a session that suits your needs and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
- Follow each step-by-step guide below to complete the form correctly.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Type of Session Requested** (`session_type`, `select_one`): Choose the type of Consciousness Access session you are interested in:
    + Individual Consciousness Scan: A one-on-one session tailored to your specific needs.
    + Group Facilitation: A group session focused on shared goals and experiences.
    + Deep Dive Intensive: A more in-depth, extended session for deeper relaxation and growth.
    + Introductory Consultation: A brief consultation to discuss your goals and needs.
- **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`): Select a date for your session. Please note that session availability is subject to change, and this field will help us schedule you accordingly.
- **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`): Choose a time slot for your session:
    + Morning (8am - 12pm)
    + Afternoon (12pm - 5pm)
    + Evening (5pm - 8pm)
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`): Enter your full name for accurate booking and communication purposes.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`): Provide your email address for booking confirmation and reminder purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`): Enter your phone number for further contact and confirmation.
- **What is your primary focus for this session?** (`primary_focus_area`, `select_multiple`): Choose your main area of focus for the session:
    + Clarity and Decision Making
    + Stress Reduction
    + Spiritual Growth
    + Creative Blocks
    + Emotional Balancing
    + Other
- **Have you had a Consciousness Access session before?** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`): Answer whether you have had a session with this practitioner or another:
    + Yes, with this practitioner
    + Yes, with another practitioner
    + No, this is my first time
- **Any special requests or information the facilitator should know?** (`special_requests`, `text`): If you have any specific needs or requirements, please mention them here.
- **I agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy** (`cancellation_policy_agreement`, `select_one`): Confirm that you understand and agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.
- **I acknowledge the session fee and payment terms** (`session_fee_acknowledgment`, `select_one`): Confirm your understanding of the session fee and payment terms.
- After filling out the form, submit it for review and confirmation of your session booking.
