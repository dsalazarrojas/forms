# Dental Clinic Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental clinic booking form is designed to collect patient information and schedule preferences for an appointment at the dental clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first and last name as per your identification documents.
2. Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
3. Select a preferred date for the appointment.
4. Specify your available time for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address for communication and contact purposes.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, not required): Select a date for the appointment.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, text, not required): Specify the available time for the appointment.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in your name and email to ensure efficient communication.
* Select a date and time that works for you to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please mention them in the available time field.
