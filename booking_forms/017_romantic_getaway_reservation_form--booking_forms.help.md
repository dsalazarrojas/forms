# Romantic Getaway Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a romantic getaway reservation. It helps our staff prepare for a customized experience tailored to the user's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the first and second fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your reservation.
3. Add your phone number, including the country code, in the third field.
4. If you have any special requests or allergies, please list them in the fourth field.
5. Select your desired date of arrival.
6. Choose your time of arrival.
7. Select your arrival and departure times.
8. Choose the number of rooms you require.
9. Select your meal preference (Vegetarian or Non-vegetarian).
10. Choose your preferred transportation arrangement (Private car or Public transport).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or allergies, please list them here.
* **Date**: Select your desired date of arrival.
* **Time**: Select your time of arrival.
* **Arrival Time**: Select your time of arrival.
* **Departure Time**: Select your time of departure.
* **Rooms**: Select the number of rooms you require for your stay.
* **Meal**: Choose your meal preference (Vegetarian or Non-vegetarian).
* **Transportation**: Choose your preferred transportation arrangement (Private car or Public transport).

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for you.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please mention them in the Special Requests field.
* If you select public transportation, please be aware that we will need to check with our transportation provider for availability.
* We require at least 24 hours' notice for changes in your reservation.
