## RV Park Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for guests to book an RV park accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Park Name** (`park_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the RV park where you plan to stay.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the RV park.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your arrival at the RV park.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to leave the RV park.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, required): Specify the number of guests staying with you.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of accommodation you require (Motor Home, Trailer, Cabins, RV Park, Tent, Other).
* **Accommodation Type (Other)** (`accommodation_type_other`, text, optional): If you have any other type of accommodation in mind, please specify it here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Reservation Start** (`reservation_start`, date, required): Select the start date of your reservation.
* **Reservation End** (`reservation_end`, date, required): Select the end date of your reservation.
* **Reservation Notes** (`reservation_notes`, note, optional): Add any reservation notes you may have.
