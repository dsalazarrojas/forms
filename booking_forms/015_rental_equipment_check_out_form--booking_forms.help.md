# rental_equipment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental equipment check out form is used to request the use of equipment outside of work hours or for a specific period. This form helps the organization track and manage the equipment and its usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Make sure to provide any additional comments or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for the user to provide any additional information that may be required.
* **equipment** (`rental_equipment`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if the equipment is being rented or 'No' if it is not.
* **duration** (`rental_duration`, date, optional): Select a date for the duration of the rental period.
* **location** (`rental_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
* **equipment_details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the equipment being rented.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about the rental request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name or initials of the person assigned to use the equipment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or initials.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, text, optional): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of submission.
