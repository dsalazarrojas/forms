# extended_stay_accommodation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The extended stay accommodation form is used to collect information for booking a room for an extended period of time. It is crucial that you fill in the form accurately to ensure that all the information is correct and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the **guest_name** field.
2. Select your **check_in_date** and **check_out_date** in the respective fields.
3. Choose whether you have a preferred **accommodation_type** (yes or no).
4. Select your preferred **accommodation** (yes or no).
5. Choose whether you have a preferred **room_type** (yes or no).
6. Enter your **room_number**.
7. If you have any comments or requests, enter them in the **guest_comment** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
10. Enter your message in the **guest_message** field.
11. Choose your **payment_method** (yes or no).
12. If you have a credit card, enter your **payment_card_type** (yes or no).
13. Enter your credit card number in the **payment_card_number** field.
14. Choose whether you have a **cvv** (yes or no).
15. Choose your **cvv_type** (yes or no).
16. Enter your address in the **address** field.
17. Enter your zip code in the **zip** field.
18. Enter your city in the **city** field.
19. Enter your country in the **country** field.
20. Choose whether you have a **state** (yes or no).
21. Enter your country code in the **country_code** field.
22. Enter your state code in the **state_code** field.
23. Enter your zip code in the **zip_code** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **guest_name**: Enter your name.
* **check_in_date**: Select your check-in date.
* **check_in_time**: Enter your check-in time (if applicable).
* **check_out_date**: Select your check-out date.
* **accommodation_type**: Choose whether you have a preferred accommodation type.
* **accommodation**: Choose whether you have a preferred accommodation.
* **room_type**: Choose whether you have a preferred room type.
* **room_number**: Enter your room number.
* **guest_comment**: Enter any comments or requests you may have.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **guest_message**: Enter your message.
* **payment_method**: Choose your payment method.
* **payment_card_type**: If you have a credit card, choose your payment card type.
* **payment_card_number**: Enter your credit card number.
* **cvv**: Choose whether you have a CVC (card verification value).
* **cvv_type**: Choose your CVC type.
* **address**: Enter your address.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **city**: Enter your city.
* **country**: Enter your country.
* **state**: Choose whether you have a state.
* **country_code**: Enter your country code.
* **state_code**: Enter your state code.
* **zip_code**: Enter your zip code.
