# Voice Over Recording Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Voice Over Recording Reservation Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients for voice over recording sessions. This form helps us to schedule and manage these sessions efficiently, ensuring that you receive the desired voice over recording services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure the "Reserve Date" field is selected with the desired date for your voice over recording session.
3. Choose the "Session Time" field with the preferred time for your session.
4. In the "Project Details" field, provide a brief description of your project.
5. Enter your "Client Name" and "Client Email" as requested.
6. If you are the artist or voice over professional, fill in your "Artist Name".
7. In "Project Description", provide a detailed description of your project, including any specific requirements or needs.
8. Indicate the "Recording Location" where the session will take place.
9. Finally, select the "Project Type" field to specify the type of project you are booking (e.g. commercial, animation, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reserve Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your voice over recording session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the preferred time for your session.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your project, ensuring we have an accurate understanding of what we are working on.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the client or representative.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required/optional): If you are the artist or voice over professional, fill in your name.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required/optional): Describe your project in detail, including specific requirements or needs.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, required/optional): Indicate the location where the recording will take place.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, text, required/optional): Select the type of project you are booking (e.g. commercial, animation, etc.).

## Tips

* Please ensure the date and time selected are feasible for the voice over recording team.
* If you have any specific requirements or needs, do not hesitate to include them in the "Project Description" field.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for a smooth and efficient booking process.
