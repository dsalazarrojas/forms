# Hotel Amenties Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Amenties Access Form is a tool used by hotel guests to provide information about their stay, including access to rooms, required amenities, and payment details. This form helps hotel staff understand guest's needs and provide efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Guest Info" option and choose "Yes" or "No" if the guest will be arriving alone or with companions.
2. Enter any guest preferences if required.
3. Choose the "Access Type" option to indicate how you would like to access the hotel, e.g., "Yes" for check-in or "No" for no check-in.
4. Select the "Access Date" and "Access Time" fields to specify the date and time you would like to access the hotel.
5. Enter your "Room Number" and "Floor Number" if applicable.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Room Type" if you require a specific room type.
7. Select the "Amenities" you require, such as "Yes" for each option.
8. Select your "Payment Method", "Payment Card Type", and enter the "Payment Amount".
9. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Customer Service", "Room Service", "Housekeeping", "Food and Beverage", and "Room Cleaning" as per your requirements.
10. Finally, add any "Notes" and provide your "Email" and "Phone" number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Info (2)**: Enter "Yes" if you're arriving with companions, "No" if alone.
- **Guest Preferences (3)**: Enter any special requests or requirements, e.g., "Extra bed" or "Special diet".
- **Access Type (4)**: Choose "Yes" for check-in, "No" otherwise.
- **Access Date (5)**: Enter the date you would like to access the hotel in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Access Time (6)**: Enter the time you would like to access the hotel in 24-hour format.
- **Room Number (7)**: Enter your assigned room number if provided.
- **Floor Number (8)**: Enter the floor number of your room, if applicable.
- **Room Type (9)**: Choose "Yes" if you need a specific room type.
- **Amenities (10)**: Select the amenities you require, e.g., "Yes" for "Wi-Fi" or "Mini bar".
- **Payment Method (11)**: Choose "Yes" for cash, "No" for credit card, etc.
- **Payment Card Type (12)**: Choose "Yes" for specific payment card types.
- **Payment Amount (13)**: Enter the exact amount you're paying.
- **Customer Service (14)**: Choose "Yes" for extra customer service, e.g., "Welcome drink".
- **Room Service (15)**: Choose "Yes" if you require room service.
- **Housekeeping (16)**: Choose "Yes" for extra housekeeping services.
- **Food and Beverage (17)**: Choose "Yes" for extra food and beverage services.
- **Room Cleaning (18)**: Choose "Yes" for extra room cleaning services.
- **Notes (19)**: Add any additional comments or requests.
- **Email (20)**: Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone (21)**: Enter your contact phone number.
