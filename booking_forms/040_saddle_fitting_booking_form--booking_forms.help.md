# Saddle Fitting Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a time slot for a service such as a saddle fitting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you've chosen the correct services from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (1, text, required): Provide your full name as you would like to be addressed in your appointment.
  - **Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date on which you would like to book the service.
  - **Start Time** (3, time, required): Select the time at which you would like the service to begin.
  - **End Time** (4, time, required): Select the time at which you would like the service to end.
  - **Services** (5, select_multiple, required): Choose the service you would like to book, such as Saddle Fitting or Saddle Adjustment.
  - **Notes** (6, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your booking.
  - **Email** (7, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
  - **Phone** (8, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- Ensure that you've filled out all required fields to avoid missing out on your appointment.
