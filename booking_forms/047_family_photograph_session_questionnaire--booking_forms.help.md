# Family Photograph Session Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Family Photograph Session Questionnaire form, a tool used to collect necessary information from clients to book a family photograph session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Family Photograph Session Details page with a brief description of your family and any special requests.
2. Provide your contact details on the Contact Details page.
3. Select the preferred time slot for the photo session on the Preferred Time Slot page.
4. Fill out the Emergency Contact page with the necessary information about your emergency contact.
5. Review the Payment Terms and sign the Agreement page to confirm your booking.
6. Fill out the Terms and Conditions page with your signature, date, and time.
7. Provide additional comments or information as needed on the Additional Comments page.
8. If applicable, add emergency contact information for a second person on the Emergency Contact 2 page.
