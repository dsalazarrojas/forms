# University Lecture Hall Rental Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to rent university lecture halls for events. Users will need to provide details about their application, event, and rental requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with the required information.
* Provide accurate and complete answers to each question.
* Review and submit the form once you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to provide any additional details about their application.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to describe the event they're planning to hold in the lecture hall.
* **Rental Details** (`rental_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to provide any specific requirements for the rental, such as equipment needs or catering arrangements.
* **Review and Signature** (`review_and_signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to confirm their agreement or declination of terms and conditions. Please review the terms carefully before signing.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to provide any other information that might be relevant to the rental.
* **Review and Signature Agree/Decline** (`review_and_signature_agree_decline`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for users to confirm their agreement or declination of the terms and conditions. Please review the terms carefully before making a choice.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete in your answers to ensure a smooth rental process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* Make sure to review the terms and conditions carefully before making a signature decision.
