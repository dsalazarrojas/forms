# Guest Acknowledgement Form For Bookings And Short Term Rentals - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording guest information and acknowledgements for bookings and short term rentals. It provides a structured way to collect details such as guest name, stay date, payment amount, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the booking information in the "Booking Information" field.
2. Enter the guest name.
3. Enter the stay date.
4. Enter the payment amount.
5. Select the payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
6. Sign with your signature (if required).
7. Date and time of submission (automatically populated).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Information** (`booking_info`): Enter a brief description of the booking or short term rental, including the type of accommodation or service booked.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`): Enter the name of the guest or person staying at the accommodation.
* **Stay Date** (`stay_date`): Enter the date when the guest is staying at the accommodation.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount paid for the booking or short term rental.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select whether the payment has been made (`Paid`) or not (`Unpaid`).
* **Guest Signature** (`guest_signature`): Sign with your signature to acknowledge that the information provided is accurate.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`): Sign with your signature as the staff member handling the booking or short term rental.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): This field is automatically populated with the date and time of submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and correct information in each field.
* Double-check that you have entered the correct payment amount and payment status.
* Ensure that your signatures are clear and legible.
* Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.
