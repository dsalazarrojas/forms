# Cart Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to book a cart for a service. It gathers information to confirm their booking details and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your customer information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the date and time you would like to pick up your cart.
3. Choose the type of cart that best suits your needs, based on capacity.
4. Specify the purpose of your booking, such as golfing or resort transport.
5. Indicate the number of carts you need.
6. Choose whether you need a driver provided or not.
7. Select any additional equipment you may require, such as GPS navigation or a cooler.
8. Choose where you would like to collect your cart, such as the club house or main entrance.
9. If you need delivery to a specific site within the facility, indicate this and provide the site address.
10. Select your preferred payment method.
11. Confirm that you agree to the damage waiver and rental terms.
12. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (2, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can reach you for any last-minute updates.
* **Member ID** (5, text, optional): If you are a registered member, enter your member ID for priority services.
* **Reservation Date** (7, date, required): Select the date you would like to pick up your cart.
* **Preferred Pickup Time** (8, time, required): Choose your preferred pickup time for your cart.
* **Rental Duration (hours)** (9, number, required): Specify how long you need the cart for.
* **Preferred Cart Type** (10, select_one, required): Choose the cart type that suits your needs, based on capacity.
* **Primary Purpose** (11, select_one, required): Select the main reason for your booking, such as golfing or resort transport.
* **Total Number of Carts** (13, number, required): Indicate how many carts you need.
* **Driver Requirement** (14, select_one, required): Choose whether you need a driver or not.
* **Additional Equipment** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional equipment you may need, such as GPS navigation or a cooler.
* **Pickup Location** (17, select_one, required): Choose where you would like to collect your cart, such as the club house or main entrance.
* **Request Delivery** (18, select_one, required): Indicate if you need delivery to a specific site within the facility.
* **Site Address** (19, text, optional): If you need delivery, provide the site address for your cart.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (21, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method, such as credit card or cash.
* **Damage Waiver and Rental Terms** (22, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the damage waiver and rental terms.
* **Any Additional Comments or Requests** (24, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them below.
* **Booking Request Confirmation** (25, text, required): Type your name as a digital signature to confirm your booking details.
