# Hotel Booking Form Deep Blue Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a hotel room. Please fill out the required fields to complete your booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to proceed with your booking.
2. Enter your details in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the hotel you are interested in booking.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to book the hotel on.
* **Form Time** (`form_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you would like to book the hotel on.
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted regarding your booking.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted regarding your booking.
* **Form Address** (`form_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address if you would like to be contacted at your location.
* **Check-in Date** (`form_checkin`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to check-in to the hotel.
* **Check-out Date** (`form_checkout`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to check-out of the hotel.
* **Form Message** (`form_message`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or comments you may have regarding your booking.
* **Form Comment** (`form_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any other comments you would like to make regarding your booking.
* **Submit Options** (`form_submit`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred submission option.
* **Form Confirmation** (`form_confirmation`, `note`, not applicable): This field is not applicable as a note field cannot be used for user input.
  It seems like you have already completed the form. Thank you for booking with us!

Note: The explanation for the "Form Confirmation" field was removed as it was not applicable.
