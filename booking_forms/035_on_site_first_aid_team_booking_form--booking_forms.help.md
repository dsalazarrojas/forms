# on_site_first_aid_team_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "on_site_first_aid_team_booking_form" is used for booking a first aid team on-site for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
2. Fill in all required fields (if any).
3. Submit the form when necessary for an event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: A brief description of the event where the first aid team will be needed.
* **First Aid Coverage**: Select the level of first aid coverage required (e.g., '1' for basic, '2' for advanced).
* **Staffing**: Select the staff members required for the event (e.g., '1', '2', etc.).
* **Dates**: Enter the dates when the first aid team will be needed.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the contact person's name for the event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned for the event (if any).
* **Description**: Add a brief description of the event.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the event.
* **Category ID**: Enter the ID of the category.
* **Assigned Tool ID**: Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
* **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form.
* **Created At**: The date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated At**: The date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Created By**: The person who created the form.
* **Updated By**: The person who last updated the form.
* **Deleted At**: The date and time when the form was deleted.
* **Deleted By**: The person who deleted the form.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes about the event.

Note: This form is not intended for general public use. Only submit this form when necessary for an event that requires on-site first aid team support.
