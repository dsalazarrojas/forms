# Patient Contact And PMH - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather patient contact information and medical history for medical professionals to use for treatment and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name, last name, email, and phone fields with the patient's contact information.
2. Select the type of physical rehabilitation the patient requires (Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, or Speech Therapy).
3. Provide any relevant medical history, including any conditions or allergies the patient may have.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth and any medical record numbers they may have.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the patient's doctor.
6. Select the medical facility where the patient receives treatment (Hospital, Clinic, or Private Practice).
7. Enter the date and time of the physical rehabilitation appointment.
8. Select the location of the physical rehabilitation (In clinic, In office, or Home).
9. Select the doctor's degree and medical specialization.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Email** (`form_3`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`form_4`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Physical Rehab Type** (`form_5`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of physical rehabilitation the patient requires.
* **Medical History** (`form_6`, note, required/optional): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions the patient may have.
* **Medical Condition** (`form_7`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any medical conditions the patient has (e.g. Hypertension, Diabetes, Cancer).
* **Date of Birth** (`form_8`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Record Number** (`form_9`, text, required/optional): Enter any medical record numbers the patient may have.
* **Doctor Name** (`form_10`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone** (`form_11`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`form_12`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's doctor's email address.
* **Medical Facility** (`form_13`, select_one, required/optional): Select the medical facility where the patient receives treatment.
* **Physical Rehab Date** (`form_14`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the physical rehabilitation appointment.
* **Physical Rehab Time** (`form_15`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the physical rehabilitation appointment.
* **Medical Specialization** (`form_16`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's medical specialization.
* **Medical Condition Other** (`form_17`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition Other Text** (`form_18`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the patient's medical conditions.
* **Rehab Location** (`form_20`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the location of the physical rehabilitation (e.g. In clinic, In office, Home).
* **Doctor Degree** (`form_21`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the doctor's degree (e.g. MD, PhD, RN).
* **Doctor License** (`form_22`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Doctor License Number** (`form_23`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Doctor Specialization** (`form_24`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's medical specialization.
* **Patient Notes** (`form_25`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the patient.
