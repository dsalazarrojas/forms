# Schedule Your Fitness Testing Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule your fitness testing appointment with our team. It will ask for some basic information about you, your health status, and your preferred time slots to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to each page and fill out the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (page 1, required: false)
	+ This is the first page where you can provide some basic details about yourself. This is not a required field, but it will help us understand your needs better.
* **Health Information** (page 2, required: true)
	+ Please enter your current health information, including any medical conditions, allergies, or special requirements you may have.
* **Preferred Time Slots** (page 3, required: true)
	+ Select your preferred time slots for your appointment. Be as specific as possible to help us accommodate your schedule.
* **Submission** (page 4, required: true)
	+ This is where you can submit your form. Once you click the submit button, your information will be sent to our team, and we will contact you to confirm your appointment.
