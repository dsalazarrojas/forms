# Upcoming Flight Itinerary - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your upcoming flights, including passenger details, flight numbers, departure airports, and flight times. It helps to keep track of your travel schedule and ensures that you don't miss any important flights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Passenger Information**: Fill in your name, passenger number, and other relevant details in the form.
2. **Fill in Flight Details**: Enter the flight numbers, flight dates, departure airports, and flight times for each of your flights.
3. **Select Flight Arrival Time**: Choose the desired arrival time for each flight from the dropdown menu.
4. **Submit**: Once you have filled in all the required information, click on the submit button to save your data.
