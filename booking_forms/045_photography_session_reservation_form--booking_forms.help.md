# photography_session_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **photography_session_reservation_form** is a form designed to collect information for a client to book a photography session with a specific photographer. This form aims to streamline the booking process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected in one place.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your client information**: Fill out the **Client Information** page with your name, email address, and contact number.
2. **Select your session details**: Move to the **Session Details** page and choose the date and time for your photography session.
3. **Enter contact information**: Provide your contact details on the **Contact** page.
4. **Select a location**: Choose the studio location for your photography session on the **Studio Location** page.
5. **Provide photographer details**: Enter the name and contact information of the photographer assigned to your session on the **Photographer Details** page.
6. **Select services**: Choose the services you'd like to include in your photography session on the **Services** page.
7. **Add client notes**: If you have any specific requests or notes for your session, enter them on the **Client Notes** page.
8. **Add special instructions**: If you have any special instructions or requests for your session, enter them on the **Special Instructions** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)**: 
  * **Client Name** (`title`, text): Enter your name as the client.
  * **Email Address** (`title`, text): Enter your email address to be contacted about your session.
  * **Contact Number** (`title`, text): Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Session Details (Session Details)**: 
  * **Select Date** (`date`, date): Choose a date for your photography session.
  * **Select Time** (`time`, time): Choose a time for your photography session.
* **Contact (Contact)**: 
  * **Contact Name** (`title`, text): Enter the name of the contact person.
  * **Contact Email** (`title`, text): Enter the email address of the contact person.
  * **Contact Number** (`title`, text): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Studio Location (Studio Location)**: 
  * **Studio Location** (`location`, text): Choose the studio location for your session.
* **Photographer Details (Photographer Details)**: 
  * **Photographer Name** (`title`, text): Enter the name of the photographer assigned to your session.
  * **Photographer Contact Number** (`title`, text): Enter the contact number of the photographer.
  * **Photographer Email** (`title`, text): Enter the email address of the photographer.
* **Services (Services)**: 
  * **Yes** (`services`, select_multiple): Choose to include various services in your photography session.
* **Client Notes (Client Notes)**: 
  * **Client Notes** (`note`, text): Enter any notes or requests for your session.
* **Special Instructions (Special Instructions)**: 
  * **Special Instructions** (`title`, text): Enter any special instructions or requests for your session.
