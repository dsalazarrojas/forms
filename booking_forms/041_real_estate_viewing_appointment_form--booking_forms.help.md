# real_estate_viewing_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by real estate agents to schedule viewings of properties. It collects contact information and preferences from potential clients, helping agents to efficiently book appointments and provide a better experience for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Select your Preferred Contact Method (Phone, Email, or SMS).
5. Choose the type of Property you are interested in viewing (Residential, Commercial, or Agricultural).
6. Select a date and time for the appointment using the calendar picker.
7. Enter the name of the agent who will be handling the viewing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name to help us get to know you better.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can stay in touch with you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, or SMS).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property you are interested in viewing (Residential, Commercial, or Agricultural).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose a date and time for the appointment using the calendar picker.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select the time for the appointment using the time picker.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the agent who will be handling the viewing.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate to ensure a smooth viewing experience.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can adjust the form according to your preferences and needs.
* This form is for scheduling viewings only and is not intended for any other purpose.
