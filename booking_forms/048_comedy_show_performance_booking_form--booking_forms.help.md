# Comedy Show Performance Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comedy Show Performance Booking Form is designed to help event organizers book a comedy show performance. This form allows you to provide details about the event, such as the event date, venue, and compensation offered, to help the comedy show management team review and confirm your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review the form for any errors or missing information.
3. Submit the form to the comedy show management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event organizer name** (`event_organizer`, text, required): This field is for the name of the person organizing the event. Please provide the name of the contact person.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This field is for the email address of the contact person, for booking confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required): This field is for the date of the event, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Venue name** (`venue`, text, required): This field is for the name of the location where the show will take place.
* **Performance length in minutes** (`performance_length_minutes`, number, required): This field is for the total length of the performance, in minutes.
* **Compensation offered** (`compensation_offered`, text, required): This field is for details about the fee or revenue share offered for the performance.
* **Sound and stage requirements** (`sound_and_stage`, text, optional): This field is for any specific technical requirements or needs for the performance, such as sound and stage equipment.
* **Expected audience capacity** (`audience_capacity`, number, optional): This field is for an approximate number of people expected to attend the event.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the event that you would like to share with the performer.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact the comedy show management team.
