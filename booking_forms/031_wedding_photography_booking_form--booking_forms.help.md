# Wedding Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by individuals interested in booking wedding photography services. It collects necessary information to understand the client's event details, such as date, time, and type of event, as well as their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Full Name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Select a date for the event using the `Date of Event` field.
5. Choose a time for the event using the `Time of Event` field.
6. Select the type of event from the `Event Type` dropdown menu (e.g., `Wedding` or `Other`).
7. If applicable, enter the number of guests attending the event in the `Guests Count` field.
8. In the `Special Requests` field, provide any additional information or requests you have for your event.
9. Finally, confirm that you have read and agreed to the `Terms and Conditions` by selecting `True`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
  - **Type**: Text input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Email**: Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your event.
  - **Type**: Email input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
  - **Type**: Text input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Date of Event**: Select the date of your wedding or special event.
  - **Type**: Calendar date picker
  - **Required**: No
- **Time of Event**: Choose the start time of your event.
  - **Type**: Time input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (e.g., wedding, other).
  - **Type**: Dropdown menu with options
  - **Required**: No
- **Guests Count**: If you're hosting an event with many guests, enter the number of people attending.
  - **Type**: Number input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requests or requirements for your event, enter them here.
  - **Type**: Text input field
  - **Required**: No
- **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
  - **Type**: Dropdown menu with options
  - **Required**: No
- **Send**: This button will submit your form and send it to our team for review.
  - **Type**: Text input field
  - **Required**: No

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled correctly to ensure your event details are accurately recorded.
- You can always contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
- We will review and respond to your form submission as soon as possible.
