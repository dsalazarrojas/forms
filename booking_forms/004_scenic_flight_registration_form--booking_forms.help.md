# scenic_flight_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scenic_flight_registration_form is used to collect registration information from users for a scenic flight. This form gathers personal details, tour options, and other relevant information to prepare for the flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Select your preferred Tour Option (you can choose one or more options).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Select your Time of Arrival.
7. Enter any additional information in the Note field.
8. Provide your Phone number (this is likely for emergency contact purposes).
9. Confirm your Date and Time of arrival.
10. Select your preferred Tour Choice (you can choose multiple options).
11. Confirm any additional information you entered in the Note field.
12. Confirm your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Tour Option** (select_one, required: false): Choose a tour option from the list. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Arrival** (time, required: false): Select your time of arrival.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Enter any additional information or remarks about your tour.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number (likely for emergency contact purposes).
* **Confirm Date** (date, required: false): Verify the date you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Time** (time, required: false): Verify the time you selected earlier.
* **Tour Choice** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple tour options.
* **Confirm Note** (note, required: false): Verify any additional information you entered in the Note field.
* **Confirm Phone** (text, required: false): Verify your phone number.
