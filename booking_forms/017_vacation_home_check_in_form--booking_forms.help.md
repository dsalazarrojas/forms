# vacation_home_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vacation_home_check_in_form is a form used to collect information from guests checking in to a property. This form gathers essential details for a smooth check-in experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select the "Arrival Date" using the calendar tool or manually enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Enter the arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
4. Provide the guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the guest contact person in the "Arrival Contact Person" field.
6. Enter the guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.
7. Enter the property's keycode in the "Property Key Code" field.
8. Enter the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
9. Enter any additional notes for the property in the "Property Notes" field.
10. Enter any notes about the guest in the "Guest Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's full name as they will be checking in.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date the guest will be arriving.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the exact time the guest will be arriving.
* **Guest Phone**: Provide the guest's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Arrival Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person the guest will be meeting.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the guest's email address.
* **Property Key Code**: Enter the keycode for the property.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the property that are relevant to the guest's stay.
* **Guest Notes**: Enter any notes about the guest that are relevant to their stay.
* **Guest Signature**: This field is typically reserved for the guest's signature upon arrival, but it's not mandatory.
