# Climbing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for booking a climbing session, whether it's a drop-in climb, guided climb, or a course. The form asks for personal information, session details, and payment information to ensure a smooth and safe climbing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal information, including your first and last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Choose your preferred session date and time from the available options.
3. Select the type of session you want to book, such as guided or drop-in climbing.
4. Indicate your experience level, whether you're a beginner, intermediate, or advanced climber.
5. If you're booking for a group, provide the total number of participants and select the group composition.
6. If applicable, provide information about children, including their ages.
7. Indicate any medical conditions or fears you'd like to share with us.
8. Agree to the waiver and safety rules.
9. Choose your preferred payment method.
10. If needed, select any additional services or equipment rentals.
11. Finally, provide any special requests or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (4): Your first name, which we'll use to address you in our communications.
* **Last Name** (5): Your last name, which we'll use to confirm your identity.
* **Email Address** (5): Your email address, which we'll use to send you booking confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (6): Your phone number, which we'll use to contact you with last-minute updates or changes.
* **Date of Birth** (7): Your date of birth, which helps us understand your climbing experience.
* **Preferred Session Date** (9): Choose your preferred session date from the available options.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (10): Select the time slot that suits you best for your session.
* **Session Duration** (11): Choose the duration of your session, whether it's 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, or a full day.
* **Type of Session** (12): Select the type of session you want to book, such as guided or drop-in climbing.
* **Experience Level** (14): Indicate your climbing experience level, whether you're a beginner, intermediate, or advanced climber.
* **Number of Children** (21): If you're bringing children, provide the total number of children.
* **Children's Ages** (22): Provide comma-separated ages of the children, if applicable.
* **Any Medical Conditions** (24): If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please let us know so we can ensure your safety.
* **Fear of Heights** (25): If you have a fear of heights, please indicate the level of your fear, if any.
* **Agree to Waiver and Release of Liability** (27): Confirm you agree to the waiver and release of liability.
* **Agree to Safety Rules** (28): Confirm you understand and agree to follow all safety rules.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (30): Choose your preferred payment method, whether it's credit card, debit card, cash, or bank transfer.
* **Additional Services or Equipment Rental** (31): If needed, select any additional services or equipment rental you'd like to add to your session.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (32): Provide any special requests or notes for our staff to know about your session.

## Tips

- Please be honest and clear about your medical conditions or fears to ensure your safety and well-being during the session.
- If you have any doubts or questions about the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- We reserve the right to request additional information or verification of your medical conditions or identity.
- Please note that some session types or durations may have specific requirements or restrictions that we'll discuss with you before booking.
