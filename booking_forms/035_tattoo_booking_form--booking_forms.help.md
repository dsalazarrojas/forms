# Tattoo Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tattoo Booking Form" is a form designed to help customers book an appointment with a tattoo artist. It's a simple form that collects some basic information to schedule a booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Info" page with your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
2. On the "Appointment Details" page, provide any additional details about your booking, such as the type of tattoo you want to get, any design ideas you have, or any specific requests you may have.
3. In the "Tattoo Details" page, describe the tattoo you want to book, including the design, size, and location on your body.
4. Finally, provide your "Contact Info" page with any additional contact details, such as an email address or a messaging platform handle, where the artist can reach out to you to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Customer Info, text, required/optional)**: This field is where you can enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information so that the artist can get in touch with you.
* **Appointment Details (Appointment Details, text, required/optional)**: This field is where you can provide any additional details about your booking, such as the type of tattoo you want to get or any specific requests you may have.
* **Tattoo Details (Tattoo Details, text, required/optional)**: This field is where you can describe the tattoo you want to book, including the design, size, and location on your body.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required/optional)**: This field is where you can provide any additional contact details, such as an email address or a messaging platform handle, where the artist can reach out to you to confirm your booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any specific requests or questions, don't hesitate to include them in the "Appointment Details" page.
* If you have any questions or need to follow up on your booking, the artist will use the contact information you provide in the "Contact Info" page to get in touch with you.
