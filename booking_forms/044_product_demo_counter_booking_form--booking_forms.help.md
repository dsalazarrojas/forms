# Product Demo Counter Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a booking system designed for product demos. It gathers information from users to book a demo session with a product owner or representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the "User Info" page with your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* Select the desired "Product Demo" option from the dropdown menu.
* Choose your preferred location(s) for the demo session from the "Location Preferences" options.
* Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
* Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
* Click the submit button to send your demo request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
* **User Info** (text, optional): Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Demo Info
* **Demo Info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the demo you're requesting.

### Notes
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your demo request.

### Product Demo
* **Product Demo** (select one, required): Choose the specific demo session you want to book:
	+ Demo 1
	+ Demo 2
	+ Demo 3

### Location Preferences
* **Location Preferences** (select multiple, required): Select all your preferred locations for the demo session:
	+ Location 1
	+ Location 2
	+ Location 3

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): Enter your contact details, such as phone number and other relevant contact information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements for the demo session, please mention them in the "Notes" field.
* A representative will review your demo request and follow up with you to confirm the details.
