# Hostel Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hostel Accommodation Form is a booking form used to gather information from guests who want to stay at the hostel. It is used to capture the guest's personal details and preferences for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Guest Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Choose a Preferred Date for your stay.
5. Select a Preferred Time for your stay.
6. Choose your Booking Preferences from the list of options.
7. Select the type of Accommodation you require.
8. Enter any additional Payment Details you need to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid Email address that will be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that will be used to contact you.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to arrive at the hostel.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you would like to arrive at the hostel.
* **Booking Preferences** (`booking_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of room you would like to book from the options provided.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of accommodation you require from the options provided.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional payment details you need to provide.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately to ensure smooth communication and processing of your booking request.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Payment Details field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hostel staff for assistance.
