# Spa Relaxation Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a spa relaxation therapy appointment. It requires some basic information such as client's name, email address, address and phone number. You will also be asked about the therapy type, schedule time, therapy time and notes. The form will allow you to select a method of booking, such as online or in-person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have a valid email address to be used as your contact information.
*   Fill out the booking date field with the desired date of your appointment.
*   Enter your name and address in the respective fields.
*   Choose the type of therapy you wish to receive.
*   Select the time of your appointment from the available options.
*   Enter the duration of your appointment.
*   Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
*   Select the method of booking you prefer (online or in-person).
*   Submit the form once you have filled out all the required information.
