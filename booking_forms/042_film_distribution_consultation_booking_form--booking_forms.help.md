# film_distribution_consultation_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking a film distribution consultation. It captures various details related to the booking process, including contact information, booking forms, payment methods, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
2. Select the desired booking form from the options provided.
3. Choose your payment method and status.
4. Enter the booking date and time.
5. Select the desired booking time.
6. Enter any additional information if necessary.
7. Confirm your booking details and submit the form.
