# Hotel Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in hotel membership subscriptions. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your form information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred membership type, start date, and end date for your subscription.
3. Choose your membership duration and payment method.
4. Provide your address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Add any additional notes about your subscription (optional).
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information** (Text): Enter a brief description of your membership subscription.
* **More Information** (Text): Provide any additional details about your membership subscription.
* **Membership Type** (Select Multiple): Select the type of membership you are interested in.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of your membership subscription.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of your membership subscription.
* **Membership Duration** (Number): Specify the duration of your membership subscription.
* **Membership Plan** (Select One): Choose your preferred membership plan.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address where you would like to receive mail.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city where you would like to receive mail.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state where you would like to receive mail.
* **Zip** (Text): Enter your zip code where you would like to receive mail.
* **Country** (Text): Enter your country of residence.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your membership subscription (optional).
* **Submit** (Text): Click the submit button to complete and submit your form.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out.
