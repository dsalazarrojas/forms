# Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to book a photography session with a photographer. Fill out this form to schedule a session that meets your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you'd like to book a session for someone else, enter their phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional, but it ensures that the photographer can contact you or the person on the booking details.
5. Enter the number of people that you'd like to book the session for in the "Number of People" field.
6. Choose the date and time for the session in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields, respectively.
7. Select the location for the session in the "Location" field.
8. Choose the type of session you'd like to book in the "Session Type" field.
9. If you'd like to add any additional notes or agreements, enter them in the "Agreement" field.
10. Enter your payment method and any additional payment information in the "Payment Method" and "Payment Info" fields, respectively.
11. Finally, enter the deposit amount in the "Deposit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the person booking the session, or the person being photographed.
* **Number of People** (Required): Enter the number of people booking the session.
* **Session Date** (Required): Choose the date for the session.
* **Session Time** (Required): Choose the time for the session.
* **Location** (Required): Choose the location for the session.
* **Session Type** (Required): Choose the type of session you'd like to book.
* **Agreement** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or agreements.
* **Agreement Text** (Optional): Enter the text for the agreement.
* **Deposit** (Required): Enter the deposit amount for the session.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Choose the payment method for the session.
* **Payment Info** (Optional): Enter any additional payment information.
* **Payment Terms** (Optional): Enter the payment terms for the session.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer directly.
* The photographer will review your submission and get back to you with further instructions or confirmation of your booking.
