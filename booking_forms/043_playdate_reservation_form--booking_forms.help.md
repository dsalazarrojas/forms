# Playdate Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents to facilitate smooth playdate reservations with the childcare center or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your child's name and age range.
2. Select a date for the playdate.
3. Choose a time for the playdate.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
5. Add any additional notes about the playdate, if necessary.
6. Select any consents that apply to the playdate.
7. Choose the playdate schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info**: A text field where you can enter your contact information for the parent(s) accompanying the child.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the playdate in the "DD MMM YYYY" format (e.g., 12 Feb 2024).
* **Time**: Enter a 4-digit time in 24-hour format (e.g., 1200 for 12:00 PM).
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child.
* **Age Range**: Select the age range of the child from the options provided (e.g., 0-5, 6-12, 13-18).
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies or medical conditions the child has, if any.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent accompanying the child.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the playdate, if necessary.
* **Consent**: Select any consents that apply to the playdate, from the options provided (e.g., Consent 1, Consent 2, Consent 3).
* **Schedule**: Select the schedule for the playdate from the options provided (e.g., Schedule 1, Schedule 2, Schedule 3).
