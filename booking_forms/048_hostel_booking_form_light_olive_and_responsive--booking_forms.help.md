# Hostel Booking Form Light Olive And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hostel Booking Form is designed for users to provide information for their stay at a hostel. The form collects contact details, accommodation preferences, and other relevant information to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to access it.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking care to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "required" flag in the form instructions.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **check in** (`1`, `date`, `false`): This field is for selecting the check-in date. Please enter the date you plan to arrive at the hostel.
* **arrival time** (`2`, `time`, `false`): Select the time you plan to arrive at the hostel.
* **number of guests** (`3`, `number`, `false`): Indicate the number of guests in your group.
* **room type** (`4`, `select_one`, `false`): Choose the type of room you prefer.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **accommodation name** (`5`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the accommodation(s) you are interested in booking.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **departure date** (`6`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date you plan to leave the hostel.
* **email** (`7`, `email`, `false`): Provide your email address.
* **phone** (`8`, `text`, `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **notes** (`9`, `note`, `false`): Add any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to avoid delays in the booking process.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are complete.
