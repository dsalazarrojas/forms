# Museum Group Tour Pre Visit Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the museum with essential information about your visiting group before your arrival. This allows us to better prepare for your visit and ensure that you have a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following required fields:
	* Group Name: This is the name of your group, e.g., "Smith Family" or "Company Team".
	* Group Size: Enter the number of people in your group.
	* Visit Date: Select the date you plan to visit the museum.
	* Visit Time: Choose the time of your visit.
	* Tour Type: Select whether you will have a Guided Tour or a Self-Guided Tour.
2. If applicable, fill in the following optional fields:
	* Tour Guide (if you selected Guided Tour): This is the name of your tour guide.
	* Contact Email: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or information.
	* Contact Phone: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute updates.
	* Additional Comments: If you have any specific requests or requirements, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Name**: Enter the name of your group, as we will use this to welcome and greet you upon arrival.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your group so we can plan for adequate facilities and staff.
* **Visit Date**: Select the date you plan to visit the museum. This will help us confirm our availability and prepare for your arrival.
* **Visit Time**: Choose the time of your visit to ensure we are ready to accommodate you.
* **Tour Type**: Select whether you prefer a Guided Tour or a Self-Guided Tour. This will determine the type of experience we provide for you.
* **Tour Guide**: If you selected Guided Tour, enter the name of your tour guide so we can assign them to your group.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you updates, reminders, or any important information regarding your visit.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any specific requests or requirements, enter them here. This will help us tailor your experience to your group's needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us, and we will be happy to assist you.
* Please note that this form is for pre-visit planning purposes only. We will use the information to better prepare for your group's arrival.
