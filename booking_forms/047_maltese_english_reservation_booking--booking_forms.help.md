# maltese-english-reservation-booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a hotel reservation at an event that combines Maltese and English cultures. The form helps you provide the necessary details to ensure a smooth reservation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Event Date in the "Event Date" field.
2. Enter your Guest Name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Type the name of the hotel you wish to book in the "Hotel Name" field.
4. Indicate the number of people in your party using the "Party Size" field.
5. Choose the type of room you require from the "Room Type" field.
6. Provide the date of your reservation in the "Reservation Date" field.
7. Choose a time for your reservation using the "Reservation Time" field.
8. Enter the time of the event you are attending in the "Event Time" field.
9. Optionally, you may leave a Message for any additional information you would like to provide to the hotel staff.
10. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): This field is for specifying the date of the event you are attending.
- **Guest Name** (guest_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Hotel Name** (hotel_name, text, required): Type the name of the hotel where you wish to book your reservation.
- **Party Size** (party_size, select_multiple, required): Indicate the number of people in your party. You can select one or more options from the dropdown list.
- **Room Type** (room_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of room you need, selecting one option from the list.
- **Reservation Date** (reservation_date, date, required): Enter the date you wish to make your reservation.
- **Reservation Time** (reservation_time, time, required): Choose a time for your reservation from the list.
- **Event Time** (event_time, time, required): Enter the time of the event you are attending.
- **Message** (message, note, optional): If you have any additional information you would like to provide to the hotel staff, use this field to leave a message.
