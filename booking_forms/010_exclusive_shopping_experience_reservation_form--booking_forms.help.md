# Exclusive Shopping Experience Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for exclusive shopping experiences. It is intended for customers who are interested in booking a personalized shopping session with a sales associate.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out each page carefully, following the instructions for each field.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Please enter your name, address, and other relevant information to help our sales associate get in touch with you.
* **Shopping Info**: Provide details about the products or items you are interested in purchasing or experiencing.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time that suits you best for your exclusive shopping experience.
* **Preferred Contact**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates about your booking.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your booking or preferences.
* **Confirm Reservations**: Select a time slot that works best for you from the provided options. Please note that some time slots might not be available, so choose the ones that are offered.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will get in touch with you soon after you submit your form to confirm your booking.
