# Luxury Glamping Tent Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a luxury glamping tent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Guest Email" field.
3. Select the date of checkin using the calendar icon next to the "Checkin Date" field.
4. Select the time of checkin using the clock icon next to the "Checkin Time" field.
5. Select the date of checkout using the calendar icon next to the "Checkout Date" field.
6. Select the time of checkout using the clock icon next to the "Checkout Time" field.
7. Enter the booking duration in the "Booking Duration" field.
8. If you have any special room requirements, select the options in the "Select Room Type" field.
9. If you require parking, select the options in the "Select Parking Type" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person(s) booking the luxury glamping tent.
- **Guest Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your booking.
- **Checkin Date (Date)**: Select the date you want to checkin for your stay.
- **Checkin Time (Time)**: Select the time you want to checkin for your stay.
- **Checkout Date (Date)**: Select the date you want to checkout from your stay.
- **Checkout Time (Time)**: Select the time you want to checkout from your stay.
- **Booking Fee (Number)**: Enter the fee for your booking.
- **Booking Duration (Text)**: Enter the duration of your stay.
- **Select Room Type (Select)**: Select any special room requirements you have, such as smoking or non-smoking room.
- **Select Parking Type (Select)**: Select if you require parking during your stay.
- **Submit (Select)**: Click this button to send the form and complete your booking.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter your email address correctly so we can contact you to confirm your booking.
- If you have any special requests, please make sure to select them in the "Select Room Type" field.
- Please select the correct options in the "Select Parking Type" field if you require parking.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
