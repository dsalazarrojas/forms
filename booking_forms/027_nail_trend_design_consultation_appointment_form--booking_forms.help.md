# Nail Trend Design Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for nail trend consultations. It helps businesses like nail salons or spas to streamline the booking process and ensure all necessary details are collected and confirmed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Consultation Request** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Client Info** in the second field.
3. Select your desired **Service Request** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Nail Polish, Nail Art, or Nail Shaping).
4. Enter the **Service Details** in the text field.
5. Optionally, provide **Additional Info** if needed.
6. Confirm your **Service Request** by selecting it from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the **Appointment Details** (e.g., date, time, duration).
8. Choose your preferred **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time** using the date and time fields.
9. Select the **Appointment Duration** (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, or more).
10. Enter the **Appointment Location** where the consultation will take place.
11. Enter the **Appointment Notes** (e.g., any specific requirements or requests).
12. Select the **Payment Terms** (e.g., cash, credit card, or online payment).
13. Choose the **Payment Method** (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
14. Sign the **Client Signature** field.
15. Sign the **Staff Signature** field.
16. Confirm the **Confirmation Date** and **Confirmation Time**.
17. Select the **Appointment Status** (e.g., booked, cancelled, or rescheduled).
18. Select the **Staff Signature Status** (e.g., confirmed, declined, or pending).
19. Select the **Payment Status** (e.g., paid, pending, or declined).
20. Select the **Payment Terms Status** (e.g., accepted, declined, or pending).
