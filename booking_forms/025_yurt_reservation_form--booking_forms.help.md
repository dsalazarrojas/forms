# Yurt Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by customers who want to book a yurt. It will help our team to understand their personal details, stay preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Guest Name.
2. Provide your Contact Number.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Select your Checkin Date.
5. Select your Checkout Date.
6. Choose your Guest Type (Guest or Non-Guest).
7. Choose your Yurt Capacity (Single, Double, Triple, or Quad).
8. Add any additional Notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name (text, required: false)**: Please provide your name as you would like to be referred to for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number (text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your stay.
* **Email Address (email, required: false)**: Provide your email address so we can send you information about your stay and any updates.
* **Checkin Date (date, required: false)**: Choose the date when you would like to check in for your stay.
* **Checkout Date (date, required: false)**: Choose the date when you would like to check out from your stay.
* **Guest Type (select_one, required: false)**: Are you traveling with guests or do you live here?
* **Yurt Capacity (select_multiple, required: false)**: How many people will be occupying the yurt? Please select the capacity that fits your needs.
* **Notes (note, required: false)**: Is there anything specific you would like to mention about your stay?
