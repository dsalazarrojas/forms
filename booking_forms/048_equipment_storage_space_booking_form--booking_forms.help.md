# Equipment Storage Space Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Storage Space Booking Form is used to request and manage bookings of storage units for equipment and other assets. It's a crucial tool for staff members or authorized users to reserve a storage unit for a specific duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Information section with your personal details.
2. Select the Storage Unit you would like to book from the available options.
3. Enter the Start Date and End Date for your booking.
4. Specify the Booking Duration, which will determine how long you need the storage unit for.
5. Indicate the Storage Needs, which will help the storage administrator to allocate the correct unit.
6. Add any Follow Up information (optional).
7. Enter your Email and Phone contact information for easy communication.
8. Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, review your form for accuracy and submit it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This section is for your personal details. Please fill out your name, contact information, and any relevant notes about your booking request.
* **Storage Unit**: Select the storage unit you would like to book from the available options. Please ensure it's available for your requested duration.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you would like to start using the storage unit.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you would like to finish using the storage unit.
* **Booking Duration**: Specify how long you need the storage unit for (in days).
* **Storage Needs**: Indicate which storage units you need (you can select multiple units).
* **Follow Up**: Add any additional information or comments about your booking (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for easy communication with the storage administrator.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
