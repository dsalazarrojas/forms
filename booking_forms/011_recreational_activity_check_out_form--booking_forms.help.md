# Activity Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Activity Check Out Form is a standardized form designed to collect information from participants and staff when they return equipment after use. This form helps track check-out and check-in procedures, ensuring accurate records and facilitating efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Participant section with the participant's details, including their name, email, phone number, and any additional notes.
2.  In the Equipment section, select the type of equipment used and enter the equipment serial number.
3.  Choose the date and time when the participant returned the equipment.
4.  Enter any additional staff notes, participant notes, or any other relevant details.
5.  Finally, have both the staff member and participant sign the form, and date their signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant** (`text`, required): Enter the participant's name.
*   **Equipment Type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of equipment used. (Kayak, Bike, Skates, Other)
*   **Equipment Serial Number** (`text`, optional): Enter the equipment serial number (if applicable).
*   **Check-Out Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date when the participant returned the equipment.
*   **Check-Out Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time when the participant returned the equipment.
*   **Participant Name** (`text`, required): Enter the participant's name.
*   **Participant Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
*   **Participant Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional participant notes.
*   **Staff Notes** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional staff notes.
*   **Staff Signature** (`text`, required): Sign the form as a staff member.
*   **Participant Signature** (`text`, required): Sign the form as a participant.
*   **Equipment Signature** (`text`, optional): Sign the form as an equipment representative.
*   **Participant Photo** (`text`, optional): Enter a participant photo (if applicable).
*   **Staff Signature Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the staff signature.
*   **Equipment Signature Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the equipment signature.
*   **Equipment Serial Number 2** (`text`, optional): Enter the equipment serial number (if applicable, duplicate field).
*   **Participant Phone 2** (`text`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Notes 2** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional participant notes (if duplicate).
*   **Staff Notes 2** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional staff notes (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Name 2** (`text`, optional): Enter the participant's name (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Photo 2** (`text`, optional): Enter a participant photo (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Email 2** (`email`, optional): Enter the participant's email address (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Phone 3** (`text`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number (if duplicate).
*   **Participant Signature 2** (`text`, optional): Sign the form as a participant (if duplicate).

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy to ensure correct records.
*   Ensure that both staff and participant signatures are obtained and dated.
*   If any issues arise during the check-out process, consult with the staff member or participant responsible.
