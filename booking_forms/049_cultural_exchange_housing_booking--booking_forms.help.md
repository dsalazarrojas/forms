# Cultural Exchange Housing Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you fill out the "Cultural Exchange Housing Booking" form. The form is used to provide information for housing reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Start by selecting the appropriate option for "Housing Reservation" page.
*   On the "Guest Type" page, select the type of guest you are.
*   Enter your "Arrival Date" in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   Enter your "Departure Date" in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   Enter your "Email" address.
*   Enter your "Phone" number.
*   Enter your "Address".
*   Provide any additional information about your stay in "Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Housing Reservation** (`housing_reservation`, text, optional):
    This field is where you select the reservation option.
- **Guest Type** (`guest_type`, select_one, optional):
    Please select the type of guest you are (Individual, Group, or Couple).
- **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date you plan to arrive.
- **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date you plan to leave.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
    Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
    Enter your phone number for easier contact.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
    Enter your address for delivery/pickup purposes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
    Provide any additional information about your stay.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out properly.
*   If you have any questions or issues, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
