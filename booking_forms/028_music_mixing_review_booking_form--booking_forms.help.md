# music_mixing_review_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The music_mixing_review_booking_form is designed for booking a music mixing review session with a mix engineer or music coach. It helps facilitate communication between the client and the service provider by collecting essential details about the session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of session you want to book (Studio or Event) in the "Session Type" field.
2. Select the date of the session in the "Session Date" field, or leave this field blank if you don't know the exact date.
3. Choose the time of the session in the "Session Time" field, or leave this field blank if you don't know the exact time.
4. Enter the name of the session or track you want to book in the "Session Track" field.
5. If you're booking a session with a mix engineer, enter their name in the "Mix Engineer" field.
6. If you're booking a session with a music coach, enter their name in the "Music Coach" field.
7. Choose how you want to have the session review (Online or In-person) in the "Review Session" field.
8. Enter the date of the review session in the "Review Session Date" field, or leave this field blank if you don't know the exact date.
9. Choose the time of the review session in the "Review Session Time" field, or leave this field blank if you don't know the exact time.
10. Enter any notes or details about the review session in the "Review Session Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Type**: Choose the type of session you want to book (Studio or Event).
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session. If you don't know the exact date, leave this field blank.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session. If you don't know the exact time, leave this field blank.
* **Session Track**: Enter the name of the session or track you want to book.
* **Mix Engineer**: Enter the name of the mix engineer you want to book the session with. If you don't know their name, leave this field blank.
* **Music Coach**: Enter the name of the music coach you want to book the session with. If you don't know their name, leave this field blank.
* **Review Session**: Choose how you want to have the session review (Online or In-person).
* **Review Session Date**: Enter the date of the review session. If you don't know the exact date, leave this field blank.
* **Review Session Time**: Enter the time of the review session. If you don't know the exact time, leave this field blank.
* **Review Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes or details about the review session.
