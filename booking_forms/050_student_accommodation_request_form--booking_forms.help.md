# Student Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Accommodation Request Form is used by students to request accommodation for their stay. It provides a simple and straightforward way for students to submit their requests for booking student accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your student type from the list provided.
2. Choose your preferred start date and end date for the accommodation.
3. Fill in your student details (name, email, and phone number).
4. Select your preferred accommodation type (single room, shared room, flat, or house).
5. If you have a specific number of people requiring accommodation, enter the number.
6. If the accommodation status is not available, reserved or unvailable, select accordingly.
7. Add any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Type**: Select your student type from the list provided. This is to help us understand your status and provide relevant assistance.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your accommodation.

* **Student Email**: Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your student phone number.

* **Accommodation Number**: If you have a specific number of people requiring accommodation, enter the number.
