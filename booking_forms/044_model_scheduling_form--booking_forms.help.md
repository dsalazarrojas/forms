# Model Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Scheduling Form is a tool used to gather essential information from models for upcoming shoots and events. It is used to ensure accurate and efficient communication between models and clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Model Scheduling Form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact information to identify yourself as a model.
* Enter your availability for the shoot, including the date and time you are available.
* Provide your phone number to ensure easy communication.
* Enter the shoot details, such as the shoot type and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Model Name
* **Model Name** (`model_scheduling_form_model_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a model.

### Email
* **Email** (`model_scheduling_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for easy communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`model_scheduling_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to ensure easy communication.

### Date
* **Date** (`model_scheduling_form_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available for the shoot.

### Time
* **Time** (`model_scheduling_form_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are available for the shoot.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`model_scheduling_form_availability`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your availability.

### Portfolio
* **Portfolio** (`model_scheduling_form_portfolio`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about your portfolio, such as experience and skills.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`model_scheduling_form_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share about the shoot.

### Casting Team
* **Casting Team** (`model_scheduling_form_casting_team`, text, required): Enter the name of the casting team handling the shoot.

### Agency
* **Agency** (`model_scheduling_form_agency`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency handling the shoot.

### Shoot Details
* **Shoot Details** (`model_scheduling_form_shoot_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the shoot, such as location and equipment required.

### Shoot Type
* **Shoot Type** (`model_scheduling_form_shoot_type`, text, required): Enter the type of shoot, such as commercial or editorial.
