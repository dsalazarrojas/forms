# Family Friendly Villa Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a family-friendly villa. It collects information about the villa, family members, and other details to process the booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of villa you are interested in** (`family_members`): 
   - Choose whether the villa you are interested in is family-friendly or not.
2. **Choose the start and end dates for your stay** (`start_date`, `end_date`): 
   - Enter the date you would like to check-in and check-out from the villa.
3. **Enter your contact information** (`contact_name`, `phone`, `email`): 
   - Fill in the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
4. **Choose your payment method and amount** (`payment_method`, `payment_amount`): 
   - Select whether you will pay or not pay for the villa, and enter the amount if applicable.
5. **Review the terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): 
   - Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the booking.
