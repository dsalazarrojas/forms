# Movie Screening Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users reserve a movie screening. Users will be able to select their preferred movie, screening time, seats, and other options to make their reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the movie you are interested in by checking the box next to "Yes" under the "Select Movie" section.
2. Choose the screening time that suits you best by selecting the corresponding option under "Select Screening" section.
3. Select the seats you want to reserve by checking the box next to "Yes" under the "Select Seats" section.
4. Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes" under "Confirm Reservation".
5. If you want to provide any additional comments or information, you can do so in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Finally, enter your email address in the "Email" field to receive confirmation of your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Movie** (`Select Movie`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field allows you to choose the movie you are interested in.
* **Select Screening** (`Select Screening`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose the screening time that suits you best.
* **Select Seats** (`Select Seats`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Check the box next to "Yes" to select the seats you want to reserve.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`Confirm Reservation`, select_one, required: false):
  Confirm your reservation by selecting "Yes".
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional comments or information you want to add to your reservation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false):
  Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your reservation.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct movie and screening time to ensure that you can attend the screening.
- Be sure to select the correct seats to ensure that you can be seated comfortably during the screening.
- Double-check your email address to ensure that you receive the confirmation of your reservation.
