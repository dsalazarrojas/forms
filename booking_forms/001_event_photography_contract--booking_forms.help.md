# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by clients and event organizers to create a contract for event photography services. The form collects essential information such as event details and agreement terms to ensure a clear understanding between the client and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out all required fields**. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. **Enter event details**: Type in the name of the event, select the correct date and time, and enter the location of the event.
3. **Provide contact information**: Enter your email address and the photographer's name and contact information.
4. **Select contract duration**: Choose the length of time you wish to contract the photographer for.
5. **Check additional services**: Select any additional services you require, such as videography or live streaming services.
6. **Check contract terms**: Select all applicable terms you agree to, including payment terms, independent contractor status, and ownership of photographs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event, such as a wedding, conference, or birthday party.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event, such as a specific venue or address.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and billing purposes.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer you are contracting.
* **Photographer Contact**: Enter the contact information for the photographer, including phone number and email address.
* **Contract Duration**: Choose the length of time you wish to contract the photographer for (1 day, 2 days, or 3 days).
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you require, such as photography services, videography services, live streaming services, or other services.
* **Contract Terms**: Check all that apply, including:
	+ Client agrees to pay for photographer services at the contracted rate.
	+ Client understands that the photographer is an independent contractor and not an employee.
	+ Client acknowledges that the photographer retains ownership of all photographs taken.
	+ Client acknowledges that they are responsible for any damages or losses to equipment.
