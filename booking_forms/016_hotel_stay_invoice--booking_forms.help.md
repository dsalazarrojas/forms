# Hotel Stay Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create invoices for hotel stays, including billing information, client details, services rendered, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's billing information in the "Billing Information" section.
2. Provide the client's contact information in the "Client Information" section.
3. Select the services provided in the "Services" section.
4. Enter the details of the services provided in the "Services Details" section.
5. Select the room services provided in the "Room Services" section.
6. Enter the details of the room services provided in the "Room Services Details" section.
7. Enter the services charges, tax charges, and total services charges in the "Services Charges" section.
8. Enter the room rate and total rooms in the "Room Rate" and "Total Rooms" sections.
9. Enter the tax rate in the "Tax Rate" section.
10. Enter the departure and arrival dates, check-in and check-out times in the "Departure Date", "Arrival Date", "Check In Time", and "Check Out Time" sections.
11. Select if payment terms are included in the invoice and if "Yes" or "No" is chosen in the "Payment Terms" section.
12. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
13. Enter any additional details in the "Minimalist" section (optional).
14. Enter the hotel name in the "Hotel Name" section.
15. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Information** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the client's billing address, phone number, and email.
* **Client Information** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the client's name, company, and contact information.
* **Services** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the services provided to the client (Yes or No).
* **Services Details** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the details of the services provided.
* **Room Services** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the room services provided to the client (Yes or No).
* **Room Services Details** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the details of the room services provided.
* **Services Charges** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the services charges.
* **Tax Charges** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the tax charges.
* **Total Services Charges** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the total services charges.
* **Room Rate** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the room rate.
* **Tax Rate** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the tax rate.
* **Total Rooms** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the total rooms.
* **Total Nights** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the total nights.
* **Departure Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the departure date.
* **Arrival Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the arrival date.
* **Check In Time** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the check-in time.
* **Check Out Time** (Time, Required/Optional): Enter the check-out time.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One, Required/Optional): If "Yes" is chosen, include payment terms in the invoice; otherwise, "No" is chosen.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the payment method.
* **Minimalist** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional details.
* **Hotel Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the hotel name.
* **Client Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the client's name.
