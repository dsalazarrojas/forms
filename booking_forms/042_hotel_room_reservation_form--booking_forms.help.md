# hotel_room_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel room reservation form is designed to collect information from guests to facilitate their stay at the hotel. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure a smooth and comfortable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **hotel_room_form**: Enter a brief description or comment about your room reservation.
2. **name**: Enter your full name for hotel records and communication purposes.
3. **checkin_date**: Select your check-in date for your stay.
4. **checkout_date**: Select your check-out date for your stay.
5. **room_type**: Choose between "Apartment" or "Suite" for your room accommodation.
6. **adult_count**: Enter the number of adults staying with you.
7. **children_count**: Enter the number of children staying with you.
8. **email**: Enter your email address for communication from the hotel.
9. **phone**: Enter your phone number for any urgent contact from the hotel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hotel_room_form**: Enter a brief description or comment about your room reservation. This field allows you to provide additional information about your stay.
* **name**: Enter your full name for hotel records and communication purposes. Please ensure it's accurate to receive any important updates or messages from the hotel.
* **checkin_date**: Select your check-in date for your stay. This field is crucial for the hotel's scheduling and preparation purposes.
* **checkout_date**: Select your check-out date for your stay. This field helps the hotel plan for your departure.
* **room_type**: Choose between "Apartment" or "Suite" for your room accommodation. Select the one that suits your preferences.
* **adult_count**: Enter the number of adults staying with you. This field helps the hotel prepare for your group's needs.
* **children_count**: Enter the number of children staying with you. This is essential for the hotel's child-friendly services and preparations.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication from the hotel. The hotel will use this to send you updates and messages about your stay.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for urgent contact from the hotel. This is a quick and direct way to reach you during your stay.

## Tips

- Please ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date for smooth communication with the hotel.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them in the "hotel_room_form" field.
- If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to contact the hotel using the provided contact information.
