# wedding_dj_and_lighting_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request a quote for wedding DJ and lighting services. It gathers essential details to provide a tailored quote for your special day.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter event details such as date and location.
2. Choose the type of DJ service you require (Wedding Package, Reception Package, or Lighting Setup).
3. If you're interested in any additional services, select them from the list.
4. Provide the contact information of the person requesting the quote (Name, Email, and Phone number).
5. If there are any special requests, feel free to add them in the text box.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`id: 1`): Enter the date of your event and any other relevant details about the occasion.
* **DJS Service** (`id: 2`): Select one of the available options: **Wedding Package**, **Reception Package**, or **Lighting Setup**.
* **DJS Additional Services** (`id: 3`): If you want to add any extra services, such as photo booth or MC, choose them from this list.
* **Lighting Setup** (`id: 4`): Select the level of lighting setup you need (**Basic**, **Premium**, or **Custom**).
* **Event Date** (`id: 5`): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`id: 6`): Enter the start and end time of your event.
* **Event Location** (`id: 7`): Provide the location of your event, such as the venue or address.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 8`): Enter the name of the person requesting the quote.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 9`): Enter the email of the person requesting the quote.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the quote.
* **Wedding Details** (`id: 11`): Add any additional wedding details that might affect the quote.
* **Special Requests** (`id: 12`): If you have any special requests, such as specific equipment or setup requirements, add them here.
* **Quote Request** (`id: 13`): Enter any specific requirements or preferences for your quote.
* **Event Guests** (`id: 14`): Enter the estimated number of guests attending your event.
* **Event Services** (`id: 15`): Enter the services you're interested in, if not already chosen from **DJS Service**.
* **Other Services** (`id: 16`): If there are other services not listed, enter them here.
* **Other Services Description** (`id: 17`): If you've selected **Other Services**, provide a brief description of what you need.
* **DJS Service Price** (`id: 18`): Enter the price of the selected DJS service.
* **Lighting Setup Price** (`id: 19`): Enter the price of the selected lighting setup.
* **Other Services Price** (`id: 20`): Enter the price of the other services you've selected.
* **Services Included** (`id: 21`): Enter the services that are included in your quote.
* **DJS Service Duration** (`id: 22`): Enter the duration of the DJS service.
* **Lighting Setup Duration** (`id: 23`): Enter the duration of the lighting setup.
* **Other Services Duration** (`id: 24`): Enter the duration of the other services.
* **Additional Services Price** (`id: 25`): Enter the price of any additional services.
