# Equestrian Facility Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equestrian Facility Rental Application form is designed to collect essential information from potential clients or customers who wish to rent the equestrian facility. This form helps us understand your rental requirements, verify your contact information, and ensure a smooth rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** field with your basic details.
2. In the **Rental Request** field, describe your specific rental requirements, including dates, timeframes, and any special requests.
3. Provide any additional information in the **Additional Information** field, if necessary.
4. Select your contact method (First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone) in the **Contact Information** field.
5. Enter your payment details in the **Payment Information** field.
6. Describe the facility details in the **Facility Details** field.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the **Review and Agreement** field.
8. Sign and submit the form to complete your rental request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Information**
* **General Information**: Enter your basic contact details to help us identify you and verify your information.
### 2. **Rental Request**
* **Rental Request**: Provide a clear and concise description of your rental requirements, including dates, timeframes, and any special requests.
### 3. **Additional Information** (Optional)
* **Additional Information**: Add any further details or information about your rental request, if needed.
### 4. **Contact Information**
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
### 5. **Payment Information**
* **Payment Information**: Provide payment details to secure your booking. Please ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
### 6. **Facility Details**
* **Facility Details**: Describe the specific facility you require and any equipment you need during your rental period.
### 7. **Review and Agreement**
* **Review and Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined below the field.
### 8. **Signature**
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure your payment information is up-to-date and correct to avoid delays in processing your rental request.
* Please review the terms and conditions carefully before signing and submitting the form.
