# Documentary Preview Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a documentary preview session. It is intended for users to reserve a time slot for a preview of a specific documentary.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Select the "Session Details" page to enter the title of the documentary and other relevant information.
3. Choose a booking date and time in the "Booking Date" and "Booking Time" fields.
4. Select the screening session from the list provided in the "Screening Session" field.
5. Enter your contact number and email address in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
7. Once you have completed all the fields, click the submit button to save your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the title of the documentary you would like to preview.
* **Booking Date**: Select the date you would like to book the preview session.
* **Booking Time**: Select the time you would like to book the preview session.
* **Screening Session**: Choose the screening session you would like to attend from the list provided.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.
* **Submitted At**: This field will be automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted At**: This field will be automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
