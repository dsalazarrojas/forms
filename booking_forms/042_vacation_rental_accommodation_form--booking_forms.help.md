# Vacation Rental Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Accommodation Form is designed to gather essential information from guests about their upcoming stay. This form helps the property manager to better understand the guest's needs and preferences, ensuring a more personalized and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your name, phone number, and email address in the relevant fields.
*   Select your preferred arrival and departure dates for your stay.
*   Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in (Apartment, House, Suite, or Room).
*   If you have any specific accommodation in mind, enter the name in the Accommodation Name field.
*   Provide a comment about your stay, if you have any specific requests or preferences (optional).
*   If you have chosen a specific accommodation, please mention the Property Manager's Name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, not required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
*   **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with updates and information.
*   **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you about your stay.
*   **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, not required): Select the date you will be arriving at the accommodation.
*   **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, not required): Select the date you will be departing from the accommodation.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, not required): Please select the time you will be arriving at the accommodation. If you don't know the exact time, just select “Not Applicable”.
*   **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in (Apartment, House, Suite, Room).
*   **Accommodation Name** (`accommodation_name`, text, not required): If you have a specific accommodation in mind, please enter its name.
*   **Guest Comment** (`guest_comment`, text, not required): If you have any specific requests or preferences for your stay, please mention them here.
*   **Property Manager's Name** (`property_managers_name`, text, not required): If you have chosen a specific accommodation, please mention the Property Manager’s Name.
