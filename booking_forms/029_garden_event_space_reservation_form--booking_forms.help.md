# Garden Event Space Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Event Space Reservation Form is designed to collect information about events held in garden spaces. The form will help event organizers plan and manage their events effectively by gathering details about the event, contact information, and specific space information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your event.
2. Enter the details about your event in the **event_details** field.
3. Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** field.
4. Specify the date and time of your event in the **date_time** field.
5. Write a brief description of your event in the **event_description** field.
6. Enter the name of your event in the **event_name** field.
7. Describe the specific garden space you would like to reserve in the **space_details** field.
8. Specify the capacity of the space in the **space_capacity** field.
9. Provide the location of the space in the **space_location** field.
10. List the event organizers in the **event_organizers** field.
11. Enter the phone number of the event organizers in the **event_organizers_phone** field.
12. Provide the email address of the event organizers in the **event_organizers_email** field.
13. Add any additional notes about the event in the **event_organizers_note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_details**: Enter a brief description of your event.
* **contact_info**: Enter your name, title, and contact information (phone number and email address) to be reached regarding your event.
* **date_time**: Enter the date and time of your event.
* **event_description**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **event_name**: Enter the name of your event.
* **space_details**: Describe the specific garden space you would like to reserve.
* **space_capacity**: Enter the maximum number of attendees your event is expected to have.
* **space_location**: Enter the location of the garden space you would like to reserve.
* **event_organizers**: List the names of the event organizers.
* **event_organizers_phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizers.
* **event_organizers_email**: Enter the email address of the event organizers.
* **event_organizers_note**: Add any additional notes about your event.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure smooth event planning and execution.
* Use a clear and concise format for your event description to avoid confusion.
* Double-check the space capacity to ensure it aligns with your event needs.
* Verify the space location to ensure it matches your requirements.
* Keep the event organizers' contact information up-to-date in case of any last-minute changes.
* Review and update your event details regularly to keep stakeholders informed.
