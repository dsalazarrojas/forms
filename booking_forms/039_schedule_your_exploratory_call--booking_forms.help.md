# Schedule_Your_Exploratory_Call - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an exploratory call with a potential client or partner. Please use this form to input your details and preferences for the call.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as needed. You can skip any fields you don't feel are necessary for the call. Please make sure to input accurate information, especially for your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please input your name as you would like to be addressed during the call.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for the call.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Input your phone number where you can be reached during the call.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for the call.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time for the call.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your time zone from the options provided.
* **Schedule Call** (`exploratory_call_form_submit`, `text`, required: false): Confirm whether to schedule the call or not.
* **Cancel Call** (`exploratory_call_form_cancel`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether to cancel the call or not. (This field is for internal use only, please use "No" if you do not want to schedule the call.)
* **Additional Information** (`exploratory_call_form_additional_information`, `note`, required: false): You can add any additional information you would like to mention before or after the call.
* **Introduction** (`exploratory_call_form_introduction`, `note`, required: false): Add a brief introduction for the call.
* **Conclusion** (`exploratory_call_form_conclusion`, `note`, required: false): Add a brief conclusion for the call.
* **Notes** (`exploratory_call_form_notes`, `note`, required: false): You can add any notes or comments about the call.
* **Next Steps** (`exploratory_call_form_next_steps`, `note`, required: false): Add any next steps or follow-up actions after the call.
* **Additional Notes** (`exploratory_call_form_additional_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`exploratory_call_form_scheduling_preferences`, `note`, required: false): Add any preferences or requirements for scheduling the call.
