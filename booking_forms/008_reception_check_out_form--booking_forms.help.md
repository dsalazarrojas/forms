# Reception Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reception Check Out Form is designed to collect information from guests during their stay at your reception, ensuring they can provide feedback, specify their payment method and details, and indicate any special requests or requirements. This guide will walk you through each field and help you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your general information in the first field.
2. Provide feedback about your experience in the second field.
3. Enter billing details in the third field.
4. Specify your contact information in the fourth field.
5. Select your preferred payment method and enter the payment amount.
6. Choose the date and time of payment.
7. Add any comments or notes about your stay.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for contact.
9. Sign and date the form.
10. Choose whether you agree or decline to our policies.
11. Indicate if you have any special requests.
12. Select any extra services you require.
13. Add any additional text information.
14. Enter any extra numerical information.
15. Specify the extra date of any events or appointments.
16. Choose the extra time of any events or appointments.
17. Enter the extra payment amount.
18. Select whether you want extra services or not.
19. Specify any extra radio buttons.
20. Upload any extra file attachments.
