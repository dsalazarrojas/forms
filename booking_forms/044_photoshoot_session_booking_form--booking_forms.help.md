# Photoshoot Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a photoshoot session with the agency. Please fill out the form with your contact information, photoshoot details, and payment options to secure your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Details" section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the "Session Details" section, provide the date and time you would like to book the photoshoot for.
3. Select your preferred photo packages and choose the ones that interest you.
4. Select the payment options for your chosen photo packages.
5. Provide your photo package choices and price details.
6. Confirm your payment details.
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the contract terms.
8. Finalize your booking by submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This section is where you provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Session Details**: Here, you can choose the date and time you would like to book the photoshoot for.
* **Photo Packages**: Select the photo packages that interest you and whether you would like to include them in your photoshoot.
* **Payment Options**: Choose the payment options that apply to your photo package selections.
* **Photo Package Choices**: Specify which photo packages you have chosen and the price you are willing to pay for each.
* **Payment Details**: Confirm the total payment details for your selected photo packages.
* **Model Consent**: Select the model releases and contracts you agree to for your photoshoot.
* **Contract**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the contract terms for the photoshoot.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Select the photo packages that fit your specific needs and budget.
- Review your payment details carefully to avoid errors.
- Carefully read and agree to the contract terms before submitting the form.
