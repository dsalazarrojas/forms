# New Guest Consent Form (salon) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new guests visiting the salon. It's essential to understand the form's purpose and ensure you complete it correctly to avoid any issues during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully before starting.
- Enter your details accurately, especially your name, address, and email.
- Select the correct option for policy agreement.
- Check the "agree and sign" section to confirm your understanding of the salon's policies.
- Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Address** (text, required/optional): Provide your current address for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for salon communication.
- **Policy Agreed** (select one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the salon's policies. Please review the policy terms for more information.
  - Option 1: I have read and agree to the salon's policies.
  - Option 2: I have not read the salon's policies but am willing to accept them.
  - Option 3: I am not sure or need clarification on the policies.
- **Agree and Sign** (select multiple, required): Confirm your understanding of the salon's policies. Select 'Yes' for each policy term you agree with.
  - 'Yes'
  - 'No'
- **Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments related to your visit or understanding of the policies.
- **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for salon communication.
- **Policy Important** (text, required/optional): Provide any important details or questions related to the policies.
- **Policy Details** (text, required/optional): Describe any specific details about the policies you'd like to discuss.
- **Policy Terms** (text, required/optional): Review and understand the salon's policies before signing this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to sign the "agree and sign" section to confirm your understanding of the salon's policies.
- Review the policy terms carefully before selecting your options.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the salon staff.
