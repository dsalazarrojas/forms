# Tango Festival Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tango Festival Booking Form is designed to collect information from attendees about their personal details, tango preferences, and payment method, which will be used by festival organizers to manage the event and communicate with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Personal Info** page with your first and last name.
2. Choose your **Tango Category**, which will help us understand your level of experience in tango.
3. Select your **Tango Style**, which will help us tailor the event to your preferences.
4. Choose your **Payment Method**, which will be used for event logistics.
5. Fill in your **Event Details**, including date and time.
6. Provide an **Emergency Contact** and **Emergency Contact Number**, which will help us reach you in case of an emergency.
7. If you have any **Special Requests**, please let us know.
8. Finally, review the **Terms & Conditions** and select "Yes" if you agree to them.
9. Click **Submit** to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Tango Category**: Choose your level of experience in tango, from "Newcomer", "Improver", to "Advanced".
* **Tango Style**: Choose the style of tango you prefer.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the event.
* **Payment Details**: This is where you can fill in any additional payment information, such as a date or amount.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event you would like to attend.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event you would like to attend.
* **Tango Level**: Choose your current level of experience in tango.
* **Tango Experience**: Choose how long you have been practising tango.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: If you have an emergency contact number, please enter it.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requests, please let us know.
* **Note**: This is a free text field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Terms & Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Email Label**: Not a visible field, but might be used internally.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Email Hint**: Not a visible field, but might be used internally.
* **Email Subject**: Not a visible field, but might be used internally.
* **Email Body**: Not a visible field, but might be used internally.
* **Submit**: Click to complete your booking.
* **Cancel**: Click to cancel your booking.
