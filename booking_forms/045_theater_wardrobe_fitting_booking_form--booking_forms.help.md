# Theater Wardrobe Fitting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a wardrobe fitting for a theater production. Please fill out the form with accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
3. Select a suitable time slot from the available options (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM, 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM).
4. Indicate whether you will be requiring a service time for your fitting.
5. Check all the additional services you require for your fitting.
6. Enter your email address for internal communication.
7. Select a date for your appointment.
8. Add any additional notes that might be helpful for the production team to know.
9. Confirm your additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your full name?** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, `select_one`, required): Select a time that suits you best from the available options.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you will be requiring a service time for your fitting.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all that apply for your fitting.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for internal communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `select_multiple`, required): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any other relevant information.
* **Confirm Additional Notes** (`confirm_notes`, `note`, required): Enter your notes for confirmation.
