# urban_loft_suite_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Loft Suite Booking Form is designed to collect relevant information from guests to facilitate their stay in our accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date check-in and check-out dates.
3. Answer the question about whether you have any special requests.
4. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Check-in Date
* **Check-in Date (check_in_date)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to check-in to the Urban Loft Suite. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Check-out Date
* **Check-out Date (check_out_date)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to check-out from the Urban Loft Suite. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Guest Details
* **Guest Details (guest_details)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more of the following options to indicate your preferences:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests (special_requests)** (`text`, required/optional): If you have any specific requests, such as extra amenities or services, please enter them in this field.
