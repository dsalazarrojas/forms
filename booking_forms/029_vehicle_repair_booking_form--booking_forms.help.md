# Vehicle Repair Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for booking vehicle repair services. It helps our team to efficiently schedule and process your requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details (<vehicle_details>)**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and other relevant details.
* **Customer Details (<customer_details>)**: Provide your contact information and any relevant customer details.
* **Schedule Appointment (<schedule_appointment>)**: Select a date and time for your appointment.
* **Repair Option (<select_repair_option>)**: Choose one of the available repair options (Vehicle Service, Vehicle Maintenance, or Vehicle Inspection).
* **Select Service (<select_service>)**: Choose one or more services you'd like to have performed (Oil Change, Tire Rotation, or Brake Pads Replacement).
* **Customer Notes (<customer_notes>)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Phone (<customer_phone>)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email (<customer_email>)**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your appointment.
* **Service Cost (<service_cost>)**: Estimate the cost of the service, if known.
* **Service Time (<service_time>)**: Estimate the time it will take to complete the service, if known.
 
Note: Required fields are indicated by an asterisk (\*). Please fill these fields as a priority.
