# Headshot Session Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients interested in booking a headshot session. It collects essential details about the client, their preferred date and time, and their desired services. The form's data will be used to schedule and prepare for their headshot session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
3.  Choose a date for your headshot session in the "Inquiry Date" field.
4.  Select your desired time for the session in the "Inquiry Time" field.
5.  Choose the services you need from the "Services Needed" dropdown.
6.  Indicate how many sittings you require in the "Number of Sittings" field.
7.  If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
8.  Select your preferred date for the session in the "Preferred Date" field.
9.  Choose a time you are available for the session in the "Available Time" field.
10. Add any additional notes about your session in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name to help us identify you and contact you effectively.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you to confirm your session details.
* **Inquiry Date**: Select a date for your headshot session. Our system will automatically suggest dates based on your preference, but you can choose any date that works for you.
* **Inquiry Time**: Choose a time for your session. Please be as specific as possible to ensure we schedule the best time for you.
* **Services Needed**: Select all the services you require for your session, such as a Headshot, Full Day, Half Day, or Quarter Day.
* **Number of Sittings**: Indicate how many sittings you need. This will help us prepare for your session.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requirements or requests for your session, type them here.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a date that works best for you. We'll do our best to accommodate your preference.
* **Available Time**: Choose a time that you are available for your session. This helps us schedule your session accordingly.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or instructions you think are essential for your session.
