# Music Festival Vendor Booth Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from vendors who want to book a booth at the music festival. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information (email address and phone number) in the corresponding fields.
2.  Choose the preferred booth size that suits your needs.
3.  Select any additional services you require, such as food and beverages or marketing materials.
4.  Estimate the time it will take you to set up and tear down your booth.
5.  Provide an estimated staff count and their names.
6.  List any special requirements for your booth.
7.  Add any additional details that might be helpful for the festival organizers.
8.  Finally, indicate your agreement with the vendor agreement terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name of Vendor** (`name_of_vendor`, text, required: false)
*   This field is for you to enter your company or individual name.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false)
*   This is where you enter your official email address where you can be reached for communication.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
*   Provide your official phone number where you can be contacted during the festival.

### 4. **Booth Preferences** (`booth_preferences`, select_one, required: false)
*   Choose the size of the booth that fits your needs: 10 ft x 10 ft, 20 ft x 20 ft, or 30 ft x 30 ft.

### 5. **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   If you need any extra services, such as food and beverages or music equipment, select them here.

### 6. **Estimated Setup Time** (`estimated_setup_time`, time, required: false)
*   Estimate the time it will take you to set up your booth (in hours and minutes, with AM/PM time designation).

### 7. **Estimated Tear Down Time** (`estimated_tear_down_time`, time, required: false)
*   Estimate the time it will take you to take down your booth (in hours and minutes, with AM/PM time designation).

### 8. **Estimated Staff Count** (`estimated_staff_count`, text, required: false)
*   Estimate the number of staff members who will be manning your booth.

### 9. **Estimated Staff Names** (`estimated_staff_names`, text, required: false)
*   List the names of your staff members who will be working at the booth.

### 10. **Booth Requirements** (`booth_requirements`, text, required: false)
*   List any special requirements for your booth, such as electrical needs or specific equipment.

### 11. **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required: false)
*   Add any extra information that might be helpful for the festival organizers to know.

### 12. **Vendor Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, select_one, required: false)
*   Confirm your agreement with the vendor agreement terms.

### 13. **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select_multiple, required: false)
*   Indicate the status of your form submission: Accepted, Rejected, or In Process.

### 14. **Form Submission** (this is a duplicate label, text, required: false)
*   This field should be removed or renamed as it's not necessary, as the status is already covered in the previous field.
