# Boutique Hotel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boutique Hotel Booking Form is a user-friendly form designed for customers to book a room at a luxurious hotel. This form gathers essential information for the reservation, payment, and any special requests or restrictions the customer may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a room, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Check-in Date** field with your arrival date in the format of MM/DD/YY.
2. Fill out the **Check-out Date** field with your departure date in the format of MM/DD/YY.
3. Enter the number of **Adults** staying in your room in the text box.
4. If applicable, enter the number of **Children** staying in your room.
5. Select your **Preferred Room Type** from the available options (Luxury Suite, Deluxe Double, Garden View King, or Penthouse).
6. Enter your **Primary Guest Name** as it appears on your identification.
7. Enter your **Email Address** to receive any updates or confirmations from the hotel.
8. Provide your **Phone Number** for easy communication with hotel staff.
9. If you have any **Special Requests** such as additional amenities or services, select them in the list.
10. If you have any **Dietary Restrictions**, please mention them in the text box.
11. To confirm your booking, provide your **Name on Credit Card** as it appears on the card.
12. Finally, confirm that you agree to the hotel's **Cancellation and Booking Policies** by selecting "Yes, I Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-in Date**: Enter your arrival date in the format of MM/DD/YY to help us prepare for your stay.
* **Check-out Date**: Enter your departure date in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Number of Adults**: Type the number of adults staying in your room.
* **Number of Children**: Type the number of children staying in your room (if applicable).
* **Preferred Room Type**: Select the type of room you would like to book from the available options.
* **Primary Guest Name**: Enter the name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive any updates or confirmations from the hotel.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy communication with hotel staff.
* **Additional Amenities**: Select any additional amenities or services you would like to have during your stay.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any specific dietary needs, mention them here.
* **Name on Credit Card**: Enter the name as it appears on your credit card.
* **I agree to the hotel cancellation and booking policies**: Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm your understanding and consent to the hotel's terms.

Tips:

* Double-check your dates to ensure they match the hotel's room availability.
* Be specific about any special requests or restrictions you may have.
* Enter your contact information accurately to ensure smooth communication with hotel staff.
* Read and understand the hotel's cancellation and booking policies before confirming your booking.
