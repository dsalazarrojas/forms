# Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form is used to book a hair and makeup service. This form is intended for internal use by the backstage team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form info section with any relevant details.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
3. Provide the contact person's phone number.
4. Select the show time and show time ending.
5. Choose the hair services you require.
6. Choose the makeup services you require.
7. Enter the number of services you need.
8. Select the booking date.
9. Select the booking time.
10. Add any notes about your booking.
11. Click submit to submit your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Info (1)**: Enter any relevant information about your booking.
* **Contact Person (2)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your booking.
* **Contact Email (3)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Show Time (5)**: Select the time of your show.
* **Show Time Ending (6)**: Select the time your show will end.
* **Hair Services (7)**: Choose the hair services you require (e.g., Haircut, Color, Style).
* **Makeup Services (8)**: Choose the makeup services you require (e.g., Makeup, Airbrush, Henna).
* **Number of Services (9)**: Enter the number of services you need.
* **Booking Date (10)**: Select the date of your booking.
* **Booking Time (11)**: Select the time of your booking.
* **Notes (12)**: Add any additional notes about your booking.
* **Submission (13)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.
