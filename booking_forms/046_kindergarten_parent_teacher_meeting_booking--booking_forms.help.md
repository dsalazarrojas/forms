# Kindergarten Parent Teacher Meeting Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to book a meeting with their child's kindergarten teacher. The purpose of this form is to collect the necessary information from parents to schedule a meeting at a convenient time for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide Parent Information**: Enter your name, your child's name, and your child's ID number (if applicable) in the "Parent Information" section.
2. **Choose Meeting Preferences**: In the "Meeting Preferences" section, select your preferred date and time for the meeting. Please note that you can also leave this section blank if you don't have any specific preferences.
3. **Submit Your Preferences**: In the "Submission Preferences" section, specify how you would like to submit your booking confirmation (e.g., email or phone call).
4. **Submit Confirmation**: In the "Submission Confirmation" section, enter a confirmation code to confirm your booking.
5. **Sign and Submit**: Finally, sign your name in the "Parent Signature" section to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, your child's name, and your child's ID number (if applicable) to identify yourself as the parent.
- **Meeting Preferences** (`meeting_preferences`, text, optional): Select your preferred date and time for the meeting. You can leave this section blank if you don't have any specific preferences.
- **Submission Preferences** (`submission_preferences`, text, required): Specify how you would like to submit your booking confirmation (e.g., email or phone call).
- **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, text, required): Enter a confirmation code to confirm your booking.
- **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your booking.
