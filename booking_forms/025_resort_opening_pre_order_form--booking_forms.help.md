# Resort Opening Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pre-order form for guests interested in booking a stay at the resort during its opening. It allows guests to input their details, preferred stay dates, and package choices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your guest information in the "Guest Information" section.
2. Provide your first and last names in the respective fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred stay dates in the "Preferred stay dates" field.
6. Select your package choices in the "Package Choice" and "Package Choice 2" fields.
7. Optionally enter your stay duration in the "Stay Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information (Guest Information)**: This field allows you to enter your guest information.
* **first name (first name)**: Please enter your first name.
* **last name (last name)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred stay dates (Preferred stay dates)**: Choose the dates you prefer to stay at the resort.
* **Package Choice (Package Choice)**: Select one package choice from the available options.
* **Package Choice 2 (Package Choice 2)**: Select multiple package choices from the available options.
* **Stay Duration (Stay Duration)**: Optionally enter the duration of your stay.
