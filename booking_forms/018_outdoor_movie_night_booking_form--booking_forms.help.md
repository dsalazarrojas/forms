# Outdoor Movie Night Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Movie Night Booking Form is used for customers to book tickets for an outdoor movie night event. This form is designed to collect information from customers, such as their name, contact details, and preferences, to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address to receive any updates or notifications about the event.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field for any follow-up contacts.
4. Choose the type(s) of ticket(s) you want to purchase by selecting one or more options in the "Ticket Type" field.
5. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field for us to consider.
6. Choose the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
7. Select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
8. Enter the title of the movie you want to watch in the "Movie Title" field.
9. Rate the movie you want to watch in the "Movie Rating" field (note: this field is intended for your personal rating, not the event's rating).
10. Enter the number of tickets you want to purchase in the "Number of Tickets" field.
11. If you have any special instructions for us to know, type them in the "Special Instructions" field.
12. Enter the location where the event will take place in the "Event Location" field.
13. Choose the price of the ticket(s) you want to purchase in the "Ticket Price" field.
14. Check the ticket availability in the "Ticket Availability" field. Please select "Available" if you are sure of the ticket availability.
15. Enter the age of the attendee(s) in the "Attendee Age" field.
16. Choose the interests of the attendee(s) in the "Attendee Interests" field. Please select one or more options.
17. Enter the name of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer" field.
18. Enter the email address of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer Email" field.
19. Choose the channel through which you want to purchase tickets in the "Ticket Sales Channel" field. Please select "Online", "On-site", or "Both".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. (text, required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. (text, required)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive any updates or notifications about the event. (email, required)
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here for follow-up contacts. (text, optional)
* **Ticket Type**: Select one or more ticket types you want to purchase. (select multiple, optional)
* **Special Requests**: Type any special requests you have for us to consider. (note, optional)
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event. (date, optional)
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event. (time, optional)
* **Movie Title**: Enter the title of the movie you want to watch. (text, optional)
* **Movie Rating**: Rate the movie you want to watch (note: this field is intended for your personal rating, not the event's rating). (number, optional)
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you want to purchase. (number, optional)
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special instructions for us, type them here. (note, optional)
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place. (text, optional)
* **Ticket Price**: Choose the price of the ticket(s) you want to purchase. (number, optional)
* **Ticket Availability**: Check the ticket availability. Please select "Available" if you are sure of the ticket availability. (select one, optional)
* **Attendee Age**: Enter the age of the attendee(s). (number, optional)
* **Attendee Interests**: Choose the interests of the attendee(s). Please select one or more options. (select one, optional)
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer. (text, optional)
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email address of the event organizer. (email, optional)
* **Ticket Sales Channel**: Choose the channel through which you want to purchase tickets. Please select "Online", "On-site", or "Both". (select one, optional)

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email).
* If you are unsure about any field, it's best to ask the event organizer for clarification.
* You can always contact the event organizer at the email address provided in the "Event Organizer Email" field for any questions or concerns.
