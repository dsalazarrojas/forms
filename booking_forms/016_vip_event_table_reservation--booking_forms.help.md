# vip_event_table_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for VIP events, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for the successful execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. For optional fields, fill in only the necessary information for your event.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid duplication of efforts and miscommunication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event. This will help us track the event's schedule.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event. This will help us track the event's timing.
* **Event Table**: Choose the correct table for the event. Please select from the available options.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event. This will help us identify the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event. This will help us understand the event's details.
* **Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer. This will help us contact them.
* **Capacity**: Enter the number of attendees for the event. This will help us plan for the event's seating.
* **Occupancy**: Enter the occupancy rate for the event. This will help us track the event's usage.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the event organizer or attendees. This will help us communicate with them.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email for the event organizer or attendees. This will help us communicate with them.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the event. This will help us understand the event's requirements.
* **Required**: Check if any guests are required for the event. This will help us track the event's attendance.
* **Allergies**: Enter any allergies or restrictions for the guests. This will help us ensure their comfort and safety.
* **Diet**: Enter the guest's dietary restrictions. This will help us provide the right food and drinks.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the event. This will help us ensure its successful execution.
* **Table Reservation**: Select the date and time for the table reservation. This will help us track the event's scheduling.
* **Table Reservation Time**: Select the time for the table reservation. This will help us track the event's timing.
* **Table Reservation Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the table reservation. This will help us understand the event's requirements.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Private, Public, Both, or None). This will help us categorize the event.
* **Early Bird**: Enter the early bird discount for the event. This will help us track the event's pricing.
* **Discount**: Enter the discount for the event. This will help us track the event's pricing.
* **Total**: Enter the total cost for the event. This will help us track the event's revenue.
* **Tax**: Enter the tax for the event. This will help us track the event's tax liability.
* **Tip**: Enter the tip for the event. This will help us track the event's tip revenue.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the event (Cash or Card). This will help us track the event's payment method.
