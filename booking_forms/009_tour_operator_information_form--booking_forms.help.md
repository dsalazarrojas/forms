# Tour Operator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a tour operator's details for booking purposes.

<thinking>
The form is intended to ensure that the tour operator provides essential information for smooth and organized bookings. The fields cover various aspects of the tour, including tour type, start and end dates, duration, and payment methods. This form will help tour operators provide their clients with the necessary information and terms to ensure a successful and stress-free tour experience.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of tour you are offering: Walking Tour, Guided Tour, or Self-Guided Tour.
2. Enter the start and end dates for the tour.
3. Specify the start time for the tour.
4. Choose a suitable payment method for the booking (Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer).
5. Enter the tour operator's details, including name, URL, email, phone number, address, and license number.
6. Provide the client's information, such as name, phone number, email, and notes.
7. Review and confirm the terms and conditions of the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Type** (`tour_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of tour you are offering: Walking Tour, Guided Tour, or Self-Guided Tour.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`): Enter the start date of the tour.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`): Enter the end date of the tour.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`): Specify the start time for the tour.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`): Enter the duration of the tour.
* **Tour Operator** (`tour_operator`, `text`): Enter the name of the tour operator.
* **Tour Operator URL** (`tour_operator_url`, `text`): Enter the URL of the tour operator's website.
* **Tour Operator Email** (`tour_operator_email`, `email`): Enter the tour operator's email address.
* **Tour Operator Phone** (`tour_operator_phone`, `text`): Enter the tour operator's phone number.
* **Tour Operator Address** (`tour_operator_address`, `text`): Enter the tour operator's address.
* **Tour Operator License** (`tour_operator_license`, `text`): Enter the tour operator's license number.
* **Tour Operator Notes** (`tour_operator_notes`, `note`): Enter any additional notes about the tour operator.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `note`): Enter any additional notes about the client.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): Choose a payment method for the tour booking: Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`): Enter the client's signature.
* **Tour Terms & Conditions** (`tour_terms_and_conditions`, `text`): Enter the terms and conditions of the tour.
* **Tour Cancellation Policies** (`tour_cancellation_policies`, `text`): Enter the tour cancellation policies.
* **Custom** (`custom`, `text`): Enter any additional custom information.
