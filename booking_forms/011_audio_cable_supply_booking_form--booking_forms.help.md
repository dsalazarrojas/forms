# Audio Cable Supply Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking audio cables for events and productions. It will help you provide the required information to fulfill your cable needs for a specific date range, including the type and quantity of cables, delivery method, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your contact information: **Customer Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2.  Enter your **Organization Name** if applicable (e.g., company or institution).
3.  Choose a **Booking Date** when you need the cables.
4.  Select a start and end date for the **Rental Period**, ensuring the cables are available for the duration you specified.
5.  Choose the type and **Quantity** of specific audio cables you require, such as **XLR**, **TRS**, **TS**, **RCA**, and **Speakon**.
6.  Select your **Preferred Cable Length**, from options 3-6 feet, 10-15 feet, 20-25 feet, or 50+ feet, or Mixed lengths.
7.  Choose how you'd like to receive the cables: **Pickup at Warehouse**, **Delivery to Venue**, **Shipping to Address**, or **On-site setup included**.
8.  Enter a **Delivery Address** if applicable (for delivery method selected).
9.  Select the type of event: **Live Concert**, **Conference**, **Theater Production**, **Recording Session**, **Corporate Event**, **Wedding**, or **Other** (for event details).
10. Provide the **Venue Name** if applicable (for event details).
11. Enter any **Special Requirements** you might have.
12. Choose whether to add **Damage Insurance** for the rental period.
13. Select how you'll pay: **Credit Card**, **Debit Card**, **Invoice (approved accounts)**, or **Cash on Pickup**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name**: Enter the name of the person making the booking.
* **Email Address**: Provide contact email for booking confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter contact phone number.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your company or institution (if applicable).
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date you need the cables for.
* **Rental Start Date**: Enter the start date of the rental period.
* **Rental End Date**: Enter the end date of the rental period.
* **XLR Cables**: Enter the number of XLR cables you need.
* **TRS Cables**: Enter the number of TRS cables you need.
* **TS Cables**: Enter the number of TS cables you need.
* **RCA Cables**: Enter the number of RCA cables you need.
* **Speakon Cables**: Enter the number of Speakon cables you need.
* **Preferred Cable Length**: Select the cable length you prefer from the options.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you'd like to receive the cables.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the full address for delivery (if applicable).
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event this booking is for.
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the event venue (if applicable).
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any specific needs or requests you have.
* **Damage Insurance**: Choose whether to add insurance for the rental period.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you'll pay for the cables.
