# Appointment E Session Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a request for an appointment or session with our service provider. It is designed to ensure that we have all the necessary information to create a successful and enjoyable experience for both parties. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out all required fields (denoted by*)**: These fields are required to ensure that we have all the necessary information for the session. 
2. **Provide accurate and relevant information**: Be as specific as possible when answering the questions, this will help us create a better experience for you and your partner. 
3. **Choose your preferred options**: Select the options that best fit your needs and preferences. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride Name** (bride_name, text, *): This field is where you will enter your name. 
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, *): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details and session confirmations. 
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, *): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. 
* **Wedding Date** (wedding_date, date, *): Enter the date of your wedding. 
* **Wedding Location - City** (location_city, text, *): Enter the city where your wedding will take place. 
* **E-Session Type** (session_type, select_one, *): Choose the type of session you are interested in (Engagement session, Bridal session, Couples session, Full E-Session package, Other). 
* **Preferred Session Date** (preferred_session_date, date, *): Enter the date you prefer for the session. 
* **Preferred Session Duration** (session_duration, select_one, ): Choose the duration of the session (1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, Half day, Full day). 
* **Preferred Session Location** (session_location, text, *): Enter the location you prefer for the session (indoor, outdoor, studio, etc). 
* **Photography Style Preference** (photography_style, select_one, ): Choose the photography style that best fits your needs (Traditional, Modern, Artistic, Candid, Mix of styles). 
* **Styling Preferences** (styling_preferences, text, ): If you have any styling preferences, such as hair and makeup, enter them here. 
* **Wedding Dress Details** (dress_details, text, ): If you have any specific details about your dress, enter them here. 
* **Makeup and Hair Details** (makeup_hair, text, ): If you have any specific details about your makeup and hair, enter them here. 
* **Accessories and Jewelry** (accessories_jewelry, text, ): If you have any accessories or jewelry that you want to include in the session, enter them here. 
* **Will the groom be in some photos?** (groom_participation, select_one, ): Choose if the groom will be in some photos (Yes, for all session, Yes, for part of session, False, Undecided). 
* **Will anyone else be in the session?** (additional_people, text, ): If you have any other people attending the session, enter them here. 
* **Special requests or ideas** (special_requests, text, ): If you have any special requests or ideas for the session, enter them here. 
* **Budget or package confirmation** (budget_confirmation, text, ): If you have any questions or need clarification on the budget, enter them here.
