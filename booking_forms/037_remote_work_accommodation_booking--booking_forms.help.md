# Remote Work Accommodation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for booking a remote work accommodation for a company. It gathers information about the booking details, accommodation type, and status of the booking. It is essential for ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Company Id (if provided) in the **Company Id** field. If you don't have a Company Id, leave it blank.
2. Enter your User Id (if provided) in the **User Id** field. If you don't have a User Id, leave it blank.
3. Select the **Start Date** for your booking. This is the date when you plan to start your remote work.
4. Select the **End Date** for your booking. This is the date when you plan to end your remote work.
5. Select the **Checkin Date** for your booking. This is the date when you plan to start your remote work.
6. Select the **Checkout Date** for your booking. This is the date when you plan to end your remote work.
7. Choose the **Status** of your booking by selecting either **Active** or **Inactive**.
8. In the **Booking Terms** field, add any additional terms or conditions related to your booking (optional).
9. In the **Accommodation Type** field, select whether the accommodation has **Yes** or **No**.
10. In the **Accommodation Details** field, provide any additional details about the accommodation (optional).
11. Enter the **Accommodation Id** (if provided) in the **Accommodation Id** field. If you don't have an Accommodation Id, leave it blank.
12. Enter the **Accommodation Name** (if provided) in the **Accommodation Name** field. If you don't have an Accommodation Name, leave it blank.
13. Select the **Booking Status** by choosing either **Active** or **Inactive**.
14. If your booking has been approved, select **Yes** for **Approved By**, and enter the date and name of the user who approved it in the **Approved On** and **Approved By User** fields.
15. If your booking has been rejected, enter a reason for the rejection in the **Reason For Rejection** field (optional).
16. If you have agreed to the booking terms, select **Yes** for **Terms Agreed**.
17. Add any notes about your booking in the **Booking Notes** field (optional).
