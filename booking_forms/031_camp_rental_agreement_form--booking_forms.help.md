# Camp Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Rental Agreement Form is used to collect essential information for a camp rental agreement, ensuring a clear understanding between the renter and the camp management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name and the name of the organization (if applicable).
2.  Enter your contact phone number for booking coordination.
3.  Provide the date when the rental begins and ends.
4.  Briefly describe the purpose of the rental.
5.  Estimate the expected number of attendees for facility planning.
6.  Indicate if a security deposit is required.
7.  Check if the renter needs to provide insurance.
8.  Provide any additional terms and conditions specific to this rental.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Renter Full Name** (`renter_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person or organization renting the facility.
*   **Organization Name** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization if the rental is for a business or group purpose.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for booking coordination.
*   **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the rental begins.
*   **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the rental ends.
*   **Purpose of Rental** (`purpose_of_rental`, text, required): Briefly describe the intended use of the rental.
*   **Expected Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, optional): Estimate the number of people expected to be on site for the rental.
*   **Security Deposit Required** (`security_deposit_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if a security deposit is required.
*   **Insurance Required** (`insurance_required`, select_one, optional): Check if the renter needs to provide insurance.
*   **Additional Terms and Conditions** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Provide any special terms or conditions specific to this rental.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with the rental agreement.
- If you're unsure about any field, it's always best to consult with the camp management for clarification.
