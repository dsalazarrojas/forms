# Digital Signage Installation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather essential information for the installation of digital signage at a customer's location. The form is divided into sections to help guide you through the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Info**: Enter your name and contact information in this section.
2. **Contact Info**: Provide your contact details, including your name and contact number.
3. **Project Info**: Briefly describe the project and its requirements.
4. **Date**: Select the date for the installation.
5. **Time**: Choose the time for the installation.
6. **Installation Details**: Enter a detailed description of the installation requirements.
7. **Installation Address**: Enter the address where the installation will take place.
8. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes regarding the installation.
9. **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for the installation.
10. **Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
11. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
12. **Confirm**: Enter the confirmation details for the installation.
13. **Confirm Email**: Enter the email address to confirm the installation details.
14. **Confirm Phone**: Enter the phone number to confirm the installation details.
15. **Confirm Note**: Enter the confirmation note for the installation details.
16. **Confirm Installation Details**: Enter the confirmation details for the installation requirements.
17. **Installation Details 2**: Enter a detailed description of the installation requirements 2.
18. **Confirm Installation Details 2**: Enter the confirmation details for the installation requirements 2.
19. **Confirm Date**: Select the confirmed date for the installation.
20. **Confirm Time**: Choose the confirmed time for the installation.
21. **Confirm Note 2**: Enter the confirmation note for the installation details 2.
22. **Confirm Address**: Enter the confirmation address for the installation.
23. **Confirm Phone 2**: Enter the confirmation phone number for the installation.
24. **Confirm Email 2**: Enter the confirmation email address for the installation.
25. **Confirm Address 2**: Enter the confirmation address 2 for the installation.
