# report_outdoor_patio_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book or reserve outdoor patio areas or services for customers or patrons. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure successful booking and timely delivery of services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in as indicated by the asterisks (*) in the form's field labels.
3. Double-check the information entered to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`1`, text, required): Enter the name or identification of the person making the booking.
* **Date** (`2`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to book the outdoor patio area.
* **Time** (`3`, time, required): Enter the time slot you'd like to book the outdoor patio area for.
* **Contact Number** (`4`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`5`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Reservation Time** (`6`, time, required): Enter the exact time you'd like to start using the outdoor patio area.
* **Number of Guests** (`7`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Note** (`8`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have for your booking.
* **Confirm Date** (`9`, date, required): Confirm the date you'd like to book the outdoor patio area.
* **Confirm Time** (`10`, time, required): Confirm the time slot you'd like to book the outdoor patio area for.
* **Confirm Contact Number** (`11`, text, required): Confirm your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Confirm Email** (`12`, email, required): Confirm your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Confirm Number of Guests** (`13`, number, required): Confirm the number of people in your party.

Note: Ensure you review each field carefully and ensure accuracy before submission.
