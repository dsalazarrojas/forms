# Yacht Docking Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create new reservations for yacht docking at the marina.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **User Name** field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used as the primary contact method.
3. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact in case of any updates or questions.
4. Select the **Marina Name** where you would like to make the reservation from the provided list.
5. Choose the **Slip Size** that suits your yacht's dimensions from the available options (small, medium, large).
6. Enter the **Reservation Date** and **Reservation Time** when you would like to arrive at the marina.
7. Optionally, add any **Details** about your reservation or specific requirements.
8. In the **Message** field, you can include any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A field to enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Your primary contact email address for communication.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Marina Name**: Select the marina where you would like to make the reservation from the provided options.
* **Slip Size**: Choose the size of the slip that suits your yacht's dimensions.
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date when you would like to arrive at the marina.
* **Reservation Time**: Select the time when you would like to arrive at the marina.
* **Details**: A text area for any additional comments or specific requirements about your reservation.
* **Message**: A field to include any further comments or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Ensure that the **Marina Name** and **Slip Size** are chosen based on your yacht's dimensions and your specific needs.
- Enter accurate **Reservation Date** and **Reservation Time** to ensure timely confirmation of your reservation.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the **Details** field.
- Double-check your submission before confirming the form, as any mistakes may lead to incorrect or incomplete reservations.
