# Art Studio Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a reservation for an art studio session. It collects essential information such as date, time, session duration, and other relevant details required for a successful reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Reservation Date**: Enter the date of your reservation using the provided date picker.
2. **Customer Name**: Fill in your full name accurately.
3. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. **Customer Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
5. **Studio Type**: Choose the type of studio space you'd like to book (e.g., Painting Studio, Sculpture Studio, etc.).
6. **Session Date**: Enter the date of your studio session.
7. **Session Time**: Select your preferred time slot for the session (e.g., 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM, etc.).
8. **Duration**: Choose the length of your reservation (e.g., 1 Hour, 2 Hours, etc.).
9. **Number of Participants**: Enter the total number of people in your group.
10. **Participant Names**: List the names of all participants in your group (optional).
11. **Experience Level**: Indicate the skill level of your group (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
12. **Equipment Needed**: Select any equipment you require for the session.
13. **Materials Needed**: Select any materials you'll need for the session (optional).
14. **Instructor Requested**: Choose if you require an instructor, and if so, what type of guidance you need.
15. **Instructor Name**: If you've requested an instructor, provide their preferred name.
16. **Project Type**: Describe the type of project or activity you'll be doing during the session (optional).
17. **Special Requirements**: Mention any special needs or requests you have.
18. **Accessibility Needs**: Indicate if you need any accessibility accommodations.
19. **Accessibility Details**: Describe your accessibility needs if you've selected "True".
20. **Parking Needed**: Select if you need parking for your group.
21. **Session Cost**: Enter the cost of your reservation.
22. **Equipment Cost**: Enter the cost of any equipment rental.
23. **Materials Cost**: Enter the cost of materials.
24. **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of your reservation.
25. **Deposit Paid**: Confirm if you've paid the deposit.
26. **Payment Method**: Select how you'll pay for your reservation (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
27. **Cancellation Policy**: Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
28. **Liability Waiver**: Agree to the liability waiver.
29. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other information you'd like to share.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth reservation process. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the studio staff for assistance.
