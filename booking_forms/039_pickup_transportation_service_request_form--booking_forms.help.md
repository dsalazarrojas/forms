# pickup_transportation_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help users request and provide information for pickup transportation services. It collects details such as pickup date and time, transportation method, and special requests to facilitate efficient transportation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the pickup transportation service.
2. Fill in the pickup date and time fields with the desired pickup details.
3. Select the transportation type that suits your needs from the options provided.
4. Confirm if you need to provide a pickup address or have any special requests.
5. Enter your contact information and email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Request (text)**: Enter a brief description of your pickup request.
* **Pickup Date**: Enter the date you need to be picked up.
* **Pickup Time**: Enter the time you need to be picked up.
* **Transportation Type (select_one)**: Select the transportation type that suits your needs: Taxi, Uber, Lyft, or Public Transit.
* **Pickup Address (select_multiple)**: Confirm if you need to provide a pickup address and select the relevant option.
* **Drop-off Location (select_multiple)**: Confirm if you need to provide a drop-off location and select the relevant option.
* **Special Requests (text)**: Enter any special requests you might have for the pickup service.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields with a "required" status are completed.
* Review your form submission before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.
* If you have any special requests, be as specific as possible to ensure the service provider can meet your needs.
