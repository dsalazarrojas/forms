# City Tour Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to book a city tour experience for the user. The information collected in this form will be used to complete the booking process and will be reviewed by the tour staff before confirming the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Lead Traveler Name (Full name)
	* Contact Email (For booking confirmation)
	* Preferred Tour Date (Select date)
	* Number of Participants (Total people in booking)
	* Payment Option (Choose how you will pay)
	* Cancellation Policy Note (Review terms before booking)
	* Submission Date (Use today's date)
2. Optionally, fill in any additional information that is relevant to your booking:
	* Contact Phone (Mobile preferred)
	* Tour Package Selection (Choose package)
	* Special Requests (Accessibility or dietary needs)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Traveler Name**: Enter the full name of the person making the booking.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that the tour staff can use to contact you for confirmation.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid mobile phone number for the tour staff to contact you (optional).
* **Preferred Tour Date**: Select a date for the tour. If the selected date is unavailable, the tour staff will contact you to suggest an alternative.
* **Tour Package Selection**: Choose the type of tour you would like to book:
	+ Standard city tour
	+ Historical landmarks
	+ Food and culture
	+ Night tour
* **Number of Participants**: Enter the total number of people in your booking.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any accessibility or dietary needs, enter them here (e.g. wheelchair accessibility, vegetarian diet).
* **Payment Option**: Choose how you would like to pay for the tour:
	+ Online
	+ Pay on arrival
	+ Invoice
* **Cancellation Policy Note**: Review the cancellation policy before booking to ensure you understand the terms.
* **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated with today's date.
