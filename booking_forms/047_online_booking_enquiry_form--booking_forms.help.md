Online Booking Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information for booking purposes. It is meant to be used for making an online booking enquiry, and the submitted information will be used to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Visitor Contact Information" page. This includes your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the number of people you plan to book.
3. Enter your arrival and departure dates.
4. Choose whether you would like to leave feedback after your visit by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Want to leave us feedback?" field.
5. If you have any additional comments, you may enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Visitor Contact Information
* **Visitor Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required=false): This page is used to collect your contact information. Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant details.

### Number of people
* **Number of people** (`party_size`, `number`, required=false): Select the number of people you plan to book.

### Arrival Date
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required=false): Enter your expected arrival date.

### Departure Date
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required=false): Enter your expected departure date.

### Want to leave us feedback?
* **Want to leave us feedback?** (`feedback`, `select_one`, required=true): Choose whether you would like to leave feedback after your visit. Select "Yes" if you would like to provide feedback, "No" otherwise.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required=false): If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the "Comments" field.
* We will contact you to confirm your booking details after you submit this form.
