# Live Show Visual Effects Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Show Visual Effects Booking Form is designed to collect essential information from clients or production teams for booking live show visual effects services. The form helps in streamlining the booking process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Event Details page.
2. Enter the required information in the Event Details page, including the title of the event, the event requirements, and a brief description if necessary.
3. Proceed to the Event Requirements page and provide any specific requirements for your event, if applicable.
4. In the Contact Info page, enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
5. Choose a venue from the options provided.
6. Enter the date and time of the live show, if applicable.
7. In the Show Info page, provide any additional information about the show, such as a brief description or special requests.
8. Finally, review all the pages and verify that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the event.

### 2. Event Requirements
* **Event Requirements** (`event_requirements`, `text`, required): Provide any specific requirements for your event, such as unique equipment or technical specifications.

### 3. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.

### 4. Venue
* **Venue** (`venue`, `select_one`, required): Choose the venue where the event will take place from the provided options.

### 5. Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the live show, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

### 6. Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the live show, in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 7. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations about the booking.

### 8. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly for any further information or clarification.

### 9. Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event or booking process.

### 10. Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Not applicable in this context, this field is likely not relevant to the form purpose.

### 11. Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Not applicable in this context, this field is likely not relevant to the form purpose.

### 12. Email Sent
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, `text`, optional): This field is not relevant to the form purpose, and it's likely a confirmation flag.

### 13. Production Requirements
* **Production Requirements** (`production_requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific requirements for the production, such as unique equipment or technical specifications.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them in the "Note" field.
* We will contact you via your email address to confirm and discuss further details of your booking.
