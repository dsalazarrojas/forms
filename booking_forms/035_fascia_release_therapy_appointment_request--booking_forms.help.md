# Fascia Release Therapy Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fascia Release Therapy Appointment Request form is designed to collect information from patients for Fascia Release Therapy appointments. The information collected will be used to confirm the appointment and ensure the patient's treatment goals and needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text) in the Contact Method field.
4. Choose a preferred time for the appointment in the Preferred Time field.
5. Enter the date of the appointment in the Date field.
6. Enter the time of the appointment in the Time field.
7. Describe your treatment goals in the Treatment Goals field.
8. If applicable, enter any relevant medical history in the Medical History field.
9. If you have medical history that you would like to upload, select the Medical History Upload field.
10. Describe your preferred treatment options in the Treatment Options field.
11. Enter any additional notes about your appointment in the Appointment Notes field.
12. Select the payment method for your appointment (credit card, cash, insurance, or other).
13. If you have insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider in the Insurance Provider field.
14. If you have insurance, enter your insurance number in the Insurance Number field.
15. Select the status of your appointment (pending, confirmed, or rescheduled) in the Appointment Status field.
16. If your appointment is cancelled, enter the reason for cancellation in the Cancellation Reason field.
17. If your appointment is rescheduled, enter the reason for rescheduling in the Reschedule Reason field.
18. Finally, enter the name of the practitioner who will be attending to you, their title, and the address of the clinic where the appointment will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred way to be contacted (phone, email, or text).
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, time, required): Choose a time for your appointment that works best for you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, text, required): Describe your treatment goals and expectations.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical history that may be relevant to your treatment, enter it here.
* **Medical History Upload** (`medical_history_upload`, text, optional): If you have medical history that you would like to upload, select this field.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, text, required): Describe your preferred treatment options.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for your practitioner to know.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for your appointment (credit card, cash, insurance, or other).
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If you have insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, optional): If you have insurance, enter your insurance number.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Choose the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the clinic where your appointment will take place.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the practitioner who will be attending to you.
* **Practitioner Title** (`practitioner_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the practitioner who will be attending to you.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your appointment (pending, confirmed, or rescheduled).
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, text, optional): If your appointment is cancelled, enter the reason for cancellation.
* **Reschedule Reason** (`reschedule_reason`, text, optional): If your appointment is rescheduled, enter the reason for rescheduling.
* **Follow Up Appointment** (`follow_up_appointment`, text, optional): Enter any information about follow up appointments.
