# Hotel Reservation Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend a hotel reservation for a guest. It helps hotel staff gather the necessary information to process the reservation extension request correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select the "Check In Date" when the guest plans to arrive at the hotel.
3. Select the "Check Out Date" when the guest plans to check out of the hotel.
4. Enter the length of the reservation extension in days in the "Extension Length" field.
5. Choose the type of room the guest has booked in the "Room Type" field.
6. Provide a brief reason for the extension in the "Extension Reason" field (optional).
7. Enter the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Select the preferred method of contact for the guest (Phone, Email, or In Person) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
10. Enter the name and title of the contact person handling the extension request in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's full name as it appears on their booking or ID card.
* **Check In Date**: Select the date the guest plans to arrive at the hotel.
* **Check Out Date**: Select the date the guest plans to check out of the hotel.
* **Extension Length**: Enter the number of days the guest wants to extend their reservation.
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room the guest has booked (Single, Double, or Suite).
* **Extension Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the reservation extension request (optional).
* **Email**: Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how the guest would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the contact person handling the extension request.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
- Make sure to select the correct "Room Type" to ensure that the guest's booking is processed correctly.
- If the guest has any special requests or requirements, please note them in the "Extension Reason" field.
- Use the selected "Preferred Contact Method" to communicate with the guest regarding their extension request.
