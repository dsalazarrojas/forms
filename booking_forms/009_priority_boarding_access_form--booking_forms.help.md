# Priority Boarding Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Priority Boarding Access Form is used to gather information from passengers to optimize the boarding process. This form is not a replacement for other boarding processes, but rather a supplement to help ensure a smooth and efficient boarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **User Input** field with your relevant details. This field is optional.
2. Enter your valid **Email Address** in the format of `john.doe@example.com`.
3. Enter your valid **Phone Number** in the format of `123-456-7890`.
4. Select your preferred **Preferred Boarding Time** from the available options.
5. Select your preferred **Preferred Boarding Day** from the list of available options.
6. Enter your **Boarding Pass Number**.
7. Enter your **Flight Number**.
8. Enter your **Seat Number**.
9. Select your **Boarding Group** from the available options.
10. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Optional, Text): A field for you to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your boarding experience.
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter your email address in the format `john.doe@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (Optional, Text): Enter your valid phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Preferred Boarding Time** (Required, Time): Select your preferred boarding time to help us optimize the boarding process.
* **Preferred Boarding Day** (Required, Select One): Select your preferred boarding day from the available options.
* **Boarding Pass Number** (Required, Number): Enter your boarding pass number.
* **Flight Number** (Optional, Text): Enter your flight number.
* **Seat Number** (Optional, Number): Enter your seat number.
* **Boarding Group** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select your boarding group from the available options.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them here.
