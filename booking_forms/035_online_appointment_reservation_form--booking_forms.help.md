# Online Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to be used for booking appointments with a healthcare professional. It will collect information about the user's schedule preferences, such as their preferred date and time, and their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Select Date" field to choose a date for your appointment. Select a day that works for you from the calendar.
2. In the "Select Time" field, choose a time for your appointment. Select a time slot from the available options.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you wish to be contacted for further information.
4. In the "Additional Information" field, enter any other details you'd like to share with the healthcare professional.

* **Title** (`title`, not used, not required): This field is not actually part of the form, but rather a descriptive title for the form.
* **Select Date** (`page_2`, `label`, `type`, `options`, `required`: false): This is where you choose the date for your appointment. Select a date that works for you from the calendar. This field is optional, but recommended to ensure a smooth booking process.
* **Select Time** (`page_3`, `label`, `type`, `options`, `required`: false): Choose a time slot for your appointment from the available options. This field is also optional, but recommended for your convenience.
* **Phone** (`page_3`, `label`, `type`, `options`, `required`: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Additional Information** (`page_3`, `label`, `type`, `options`, `required`: false): This is a text field where you can enter any additional details about your appointment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to choose a date and time that works for you and the healthcare professional.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to enter them in the "Additional Information" field.
* We will contact you at the phone number you provide to confirm your appointment details.
* If you have any further questions or need to make changes to your appointment, please contact us at the email address you provided.
