# Maritime Simulator Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Maritime Simulator Reservation Form is designed for users to reserve a simulation session for training or testing purposes. This form allows you to submit a request for a simulator session, providing essential details for the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form submit date, if you want to schedule the reservation.
2. Provide your name for contact purposes.
3. Enter your organization's name if applicable.
4. Enter your contact email address.
5. Add your contact phone number for easier communication.
6. Select the status of the simulator session (Yes or No).
7. Specify the duration of the simulator session in minutes.
8. Enter the start and end times of the simulation session.
9. Choose the number of simulators required for the session.
10. Select the number of participants expected for the session.
11. Select the type of simulator required (Sim1, Sim2, etc.).
12. Provide any additional equipment needed for the session.
13. Add any notes or comments regarding the reservation.
14. Confirm the person submitting the form (Submitter).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Date** (`form_submit_date`, date): Enter the date you want to schedule the reservation.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Your name for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text): If you represent an organization, enter its name here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your valid email address for easy communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Your contact phone number for easier communication.
* **Simulator Session ID** (`simulator_session_id`, select_one): Select 'Yes' to schedule the simulator session or 'No' otherwise.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number): Enter the duration of the simulation session in minutes.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the simulation session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of the simulation session.
* **Number of Simulators** (`number_of_simulators`, number): Choose the number of simulators required for the session.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number): Select the expected number of participants for the session.
* **Simulator Type** (`simulator_type`, select_multiple): Choose the type of simulator (Sim1, Sim2, etc.) required for the session.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text): List any additional equipment needed for the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any comments or notes regarding the reservation.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text): Confirm the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
