# Artist Booking (Updated) - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking an artist for an event. Please fill out this form with your event details to help us understand your needs and provide the best possible artist match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate details, especially for dates and times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the person booking the artist.
* **Organizer Email Address** (`organizer_email`, email, required): Enter the primary contact email address for the event organizer.
* **Organizer Phone Number** (`organizer_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the event organizer.
* **Organization or Venue Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or venue hosting the event.
* **Event Name or Title** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the title or name of the event.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event (e.g., concert, wedding, corporate event).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Requested Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like the artist to perform.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, select_one, required): Select the desired performance duration (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue hosting the event.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the venue.
* **City** (`venue_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the venue is located.
* **Type of Artist Needed** (`artist_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of artist you are looking for (e.g., solo musician, band, comedian).
* **Preferred Genre or Style** (`genre_preference`, text, optional): Enter the preferred music style or performance type.
* **Budget for Performance (GBP)** (`budget`, number, required): Enter your budget for the performance.
* **Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`, text, optional): Enter the distance you are willing to travel to the venue (if applicable).
* **Additional Event Details or Requirements** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the event.
* **Is This Date Flexible?** (`date_confirmation`, select_one, optional): If the date is flexible, select one of the flexible options.
* **How Would You Like Us to Follow Up?** (`followup_instructions`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method for follow-up communication.

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please contact us.
* We will review your submission and provide the best possible artist match based on your requirements.
