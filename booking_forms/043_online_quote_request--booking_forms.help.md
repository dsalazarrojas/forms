# online_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather travel-related information from users for booking purposes. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed answers to help us better understand your travel needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct booking date for your travel.
2.  Enter your departure city or country (if applicable).
3.  Indicate whether you're traveling internationally or domestically.
4.  Choose the type(s) of travel you're planning (e.g., business, leisure).
5.  Enter the city or country you're arriving in (if different from departure).
6.  Select the type of travel class you're interested in (e.g., economy, business).
7.  Indicate your budget range for the trip.
8.  Select the duration of your travel (e.g., one day, multiple days).
9.  Indicate how frequently you plan to travel (e.g., rarely, frequently).
10. Select the type of return trip you're planning (e.g., return, one-way).
11. Enter the number of times you plan to travel in a year.
12. Provide any additional travel type details (if necessary).
13. Indicate which currency you'd like to use for your trip (e.g., USD, EUR).
14. Enter any additional currency symbols you'd like to use (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Booking Date (1)** (`booking_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you're booking your travel.
*   **Departure City** (`departure_city`, Text, Optional): Enter your departure city (if applicable).
*   **Departure Country** (`departure_country`, Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you're traveling internationally or domestically (e.g., yes or no).
*   **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type(s) of travel you're planning (e.g., business, leisure).
*   **Arrival City** (`arrival_city`, Text, Optional): Enter your arrival city (if different from departure).
*   **Arrival Country** (`arrival_country`, Select One, Optional): Indicate whether your arrival city is in your country or not (e.g., yes or no).
*   **Travel Class** (`travel_class`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of travel class you're interested in (e.g., economy, business).
*   **Budget** (`budget`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate your budget range for the trip.
*   **Travel Date Range** (`travel_date_range`, Date, Optional): Enter the duration of your travel (e.g., one day, multiple days).
*   **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, Select One, Optional): Indicate the duration of your travel (e.g., yes or no).
*   **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Indicate how frequently you plan to travel (e.g., rarely, frequently).
*   **Return Type** (`return_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of return trip you're planning (e.g., return, one-way).
*   **Travel Frequency Count** (`travel_frequency_count`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of times you plan to travel in a year.
*   **Travel Type Details** (`travel_type_details`, Note, Optional): Provide any additional travel type details (if necessary).
*   **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate which currency you'd like to use for your trip (e.g., USD, EUR).
*   **Travel Currency Symbol** (`travel_currency_symbol`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional currency symbols you'd like to use (if necessary).
