# senior-living-trial-stay-booking-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a trial stay in a senior living facility. It will help us to understand your needs and preferences to ensure a smooth and comfortable experience for you during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and fill in the required information. Don't worry if you're not sure about a particular field; use your best judgment and provide the most accurate information you can. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trial Stay Requests** (`trial_stay_requests`, `note`, **required: false**): This section is for providing any specific requests or requirements you have for your stay. If you have any requests or concerns, please list them here.

* **Guest Needs** (`guest_needs`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Please select any of the options that apply to you. You may select multiple options.

	+ Guest
	+ Other

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `email`, **required: false**): Please provide your email address where we can contact you for further information or clarification.

* **Follow Up Schedule** (`follow_up_schedule`, `date`, **required: false**): Please select a date for your follow-up schedule.

* **Phone Numbers** (`phone_numbers`, `text`, **required: false**): Please provide your phone number so we can reach you for further information or clarification.

* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Please select whether you have any special needs or not.

	+ True
	+ False

* **Stay Duration** (`stay_duration`, `number`, **required: false**): Please provide the number of days you plan to stay with us.

* **Guest Comments** (`guest_comments`, `text`, **required: false**): This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your stay.

## Tips
- Please fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your feedback and comments to help us improve our services.
