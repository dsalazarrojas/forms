# Booking Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our customers quickly and efficiently submit their booking enquiries to us. The form is divided into different sections, each with specific fields to capture relevant information. Please fill in the required fields to ensure we understand your needs correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections and understand what information each field requests.
2. Fill in your full name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact method.
3. Choose the type of booking you're interested in (e.g., Accommodation, Tour package, etc.).
4. Select a preferred date for the booking.
5. Specify the duration or time required for the booking.
6. Indicate the number of people involved in the booking.
7. If applicable, select a budget range for your booking.
8. Provide any specific requirements or preferences for the booking.
9. Write a detailed description of your booking needs in the "Details of Your Enquiry" field.
10. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
11. Finally, indicate your flexibility with dates for the booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): We will use this email address to respond to your enquiry.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Your best contact number for us to reach you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you're representing a business or organization, enter your company name here.
* **What would you like to book** (`booking_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options provided to select the type of booking you're interested in.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Enter a date when you'd like the booking to take place.
* **Duration or Time Required** (`duration_or_time`, `text`, optional): Specify the length of time or time frame needed for the booking.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, `number`, required): Indicate how many people will be involved in the booking.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select an approximate budget range for your booking (if applicable).
* **Specific Requirements or Preferences** (`specific_requirements`, `text`, optional): Add any special requests or requirements for the booking.
* **Details of Your Enquiry** (`enquiry_details`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your booking needs.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like us to contact you (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Availability Status** (`availability_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your flexibility with dates (e.g., Fixed dates, Some flexibility, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.

Remember to review the form carefully and fill in all required fields to ensure your booking enquiry is processed efficiently and effectively.
