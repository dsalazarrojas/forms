# Railway Lodge Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for booking accommodation at the Railway Lodge. By providing your check-in and check-out dates and your room preferences, you can ensure that we have enough space and resources to accommodate your stay comfortably. If you are booking for more than one person, please specify the total number of guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you wish to check-in to the Railway Lodge.
2. Choose the date you plan to check-out from the Railway Lodge.
3. Select the type of room you prefer, if you have specific preferences.
4. Indicate the total number of guests in your group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Check-in Date <checkin_date>
- **Check-in Date**: This field allows you to select the date you wish to check-in to the Railway Lodge.

### Check-out Date <checkout_date>
- **Check-out Date**: This field is used to choose the date you plan to check-out from the Railway Lodge.

### Room Preferences <room_preferences>
- **Room Preferences**: Select the type of room you prefer, such as King, Queen, Double, Twin, or Suite. If you have no specific preference, you can leave this field blank.

### Guests Count <guests_count>
- **Guests Count**: Please indicate the total number of guests in your group. This will help us to ensure we have enough space and resources to accommodate your stay.

## Tips
- If you are booking for a large group, please contact us in advance to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
- Please double-check your check-in and check-out dates to avoid any errors.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" section (not shown in this form).
