# Holiday Brunch Table Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Brunch Table Booking Form is used to book a table for a holiday brunch event. Guests can use this form to submit their booking requests, including their name, email, phone number, preferred date and time, party size, and any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the text field. This is the name of the person booking the table.
2. Enter your **Guest Email** in the email field. This is the email address where you would like to be contacted for updates.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field. This is the phone number where you can be reached if needed.
4. Select a **Date** for your booking from the date picker.
5. Choose a time for your booking from the time picker.
6. Select the **Party Size** of your group from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose the **Table** number where you would like to be seated.
8. Select any **Special Requests** you have from the dropdown menu.
9. Leave a **Note** if you have any additional comments or information you would like to share.
10. If you have a preferred **Assigned Tool**, you can choose from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: The name of the person booking the table.
* **Guest Email**: The email address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone**: The phone number where you can be reached if needed.
* **Date**: The date you would like to book the table for.
* **Time**: The time you would like to book the table for.
* **Party Size**: The number of people in your group.
* **Table**: The number of the table where you would like to be seated.
* **Special Requests**: Any special requests you have, such as food allergies or dietary restrictions.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool or device you would like to be assigned for your booking.

# Tips
* Please be sure to enter your contact information accurately to ensure we can reach you for updates and confirmations.
* If you have any special requests, please be sure to select them from the dropdown menu to ensure they are processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
