# Speaker Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book speakers for various presentations, events, or workshops. It collects crucial information about the speaker's details, presentation preferences, and submission dates to facilitate smooth event organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are thoroughly reviewed before submission.
3. Double-check the speaker's details for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Details (1)** (`speaker_details`, text, required): Enter a brief introduction or bio about the speaker.
* **Speaker Name (2)** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter the speaker's full name as it appears on their business card or public profiles.
* **Speaker Email (3)** (`speaker_email`, email, required): Enter the speaker's professional email address for communication.
* **Speaker Phone (4)** (`speaker_phone`, text, required): Enter the speaker's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Speaker Topic (5)** (`speaker_topic`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the speaker's topic or presentation title.
* **Presentation Date (6)** (`presentation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the presentation or event.
* **Presentation Time (7)** (`presentation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the presentation or event.
* **Presentation Duration (8)** (`presentation_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the presentation in minutes.
* **Presentation Type (9)** (`presentation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of presentation (Presentation, Workshop, Panel Discussion, Video Presentation, or Other).
* **Presentation Format (10)** (`presentation_format`, select_multiple, required): Select the formats for the presentation (Video and Slides, Handouts, Handouts and Slides, Other).
* **Speaker Bio (11)** (`speaker_bio`, text, optional): Enter a more in-depth bio about the speaker, if desired.
* **Speaker Interest (12)** (`speaker_interest`, text, optional): Enter any relevant speaker interests or areas of expertise.
* **Speaker Experience (13)** (`speaker_experience`, text, optional): Enter any relevant speaker experience or credentials.
* **Speaker Interest 2 (14)** (`speaker_interest_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional speaker interests or areas of expertise.
* **Speaker Experience 2 (15)** (`speaker_experience_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional speaker experience or credentials.
* **Presentation Notes (16)** (`presentation_notes`, text, optional): Enter any important notes about the presentation.
* **Speaker Image (17)** (`speaker_image`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of a speaker image or profile picture.
* **Speaker Video (18)** (`speaker_video`, text, optional): Upload or enter the URL of a speaker video presentation or demo.
* **Speaker Interest 3 (19)** (`speaker_interest_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional speaker interests or areas of expertise.
* **Speaker Experience 3 (20)** (`speaker_experience_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional speaker experience or credentials.
* **Presentation Format 2 (21)** (`presentation_format_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional formats for the presentation (Video and Slides, Handouts, Handouts and Slides, Other).
* **Presentation Notes 2 (22)** (`presentation_notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any important notes about the presentation.
* **Presentation Duration 2 (23)** (`presentation_duration_2`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the presentation in minutes.
* **Presentation Type 2 (24)** (`presentation_type_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of presentation (Presentation, Workshop, Panel Discussion, Video Presentation, Other).
* **Submission Date (25)** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission or deadline for the presentation.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If any field is not applicable, leave it blank instead of entering random or unnecessary information.
- Ensure that the speaker's details match their actual credentials and experience for a smooth event organization.
