# Voiceover Studio Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Voiceover Studio Session Booking Form, explaining each field and how to complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, fill out the **Studio Name** field with the name of the studio you wish to book a session with.
- Then, select a **Date** for your desired session. Note that this field is optional, so if you don't have a specific date in mind, you can leave it blank.
- Next, choose a **Time** for your session, again noting that this field is optional.
- Enter the name of the **Actor Name** for your session.
- Choose the **Booking Type** by selecting one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
- If you're booking multiple projects, enter the **Project Name** field with each project name separated by commas.
- In the **Project Description** field, provide a brief description of your project.
- Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** in the corresponding fields.
- If you have a specific **Project Deadline**, select it in the corresponding field. This is optional.
- Finally, select the **Project Status** of your project, which can be either **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Studio Name** (Required): Enter the name of the studio you wish to book a session with.
- **Date** (Optional): Select a date for your session.
- **Time** (Optional): Choose a time for your session.
- **Actor Name** (Required): Enter the name of the actor for your session.
- **Booking Type** (Optional): Select one or multiple booking types from the dropdown list.
- **Project Name** (Optional): Enter the name of your project, separating multiple projects with commas.
- **Project Description** (Optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
- **Contact Email** (Required): Enter your contact email.
- **Contact Phone** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Project Deadline** (Optional): Select a date for your project deadline.
- **Project Status** (Optional): Select the status of your project as either **Active** or **Inactive**.
