# Business Class Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book tickets for business class flights. Please fill it out carefully to ensure that your travel arrangements are made correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your passenger information:
	*   First Name
	*   Last Name
	*   Email Address
	*   Phone Number
	*   Passport or ID Number
2.  Provide travel details:
	*   Departure Date
	*   Departure City
	*   Destination City
	*   Return Date (optional)
3.  Select your preferences:
	*   Number of Passengers
	*   Preferred Business Class
	*   Seat Preference
	*   Meal Preferences
4.  Add baggage and special services information:
	*   Number of Checked Bags
	*   Number of Carry-On Bags
	*   Special Equipment or Needs
	*   Additional Services (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your ID.
*   **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your ID.
*   **Email Address**: Your business or personal email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
*   **Passport or ID Number**: Your government-issued ID number.
*   **Departure Date**: The date you plan to travel.
*   **Departure City**: The city or airport code of your departure location.
*   **Destination City**: The city or airport code of your destination location.
*   **Return Date**: The date you plan to return, if applicable.
*   **Number of Passengers**: The number of business class seats you need.
*   **Preferred Business Class**: The type of business class you prefer (First Class, Premium Business Class, Standard Business Class).
*   **Seat Preference**: The seat position you prefer (Window Seat, Aisle Seat, Center Seat, No Preference).
*   **Meat Preferences**: Select all the dietary meal preferences that apply to you (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Kosher, None).
*   **Number of Checked Bags**: The number of bags you plan to check in.
*   **Number of Carry-On Bags**: The number of bags you plan to bring on board.
*   **Special Equipment or Needs**: Any special equipment or needs you have, such as a wheelchair.
*   **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you need, such as Priority Boarding, Lounge Access, Ground Transportation, Hotel Accommodation, Travel Insurance.
*   **Company Name**: The name of your company, for business billing purposes.
*   **Billing Address**: The complete address for billing purposes.
*   **Payment Method**: How you plan to pay for your ticket (Corporate Credit Card, Personal Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Company Account).
