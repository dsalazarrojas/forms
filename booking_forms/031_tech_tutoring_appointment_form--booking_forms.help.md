# tech_tutoring_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments for tech tutoring sessions. Please fill out this form to book a session with a tutor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the appointment date.
2. Enter your student's name.
3. Provide the tutor's name.
4. Choose a start and end time for the session.
5. Specify the total session time.
6. Select the type of session (tech support or other).
7. Add any additional notes for the session.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Provide your phone number.
10. Enter your student's email address.
11. Enter your student's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **appointment_date** (1, date, required: false): Enter the date of the tech tutoring session.
* **student_name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your student's name.
* **tutor_name** (3, text, required: false): Enter your name as the tutor.
* **start_time** (4, time, required: false): Choose the start time for the session.
* **end_time** (5, time, required: false): Choose the end time for the session.
* **appointment_time** (6, number, required: false): Enter the total duration of the session.
* **session_type** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of session (tech support or other).
* **notes** (8, note, required: false): Add any additional notes for the session.
* **email** (9, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (10, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **student_email** (11, email, required: false): Enter your student's email address.
* **student_phone** (12, text, required: false): Enter your student's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out the form correctly to avoid duplicate bookings.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the tech tutoring team.
* Sessions are subject to tutor availability, so you may be contacted to confirm the session time and details.
