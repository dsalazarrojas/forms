# Exhibition Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for exhibition bookings, including exhibition title, name, company, email, booth size, and date/time slots. It will help us understand your exhibition requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a suitable exhibition option from the list.
2. Input your name and company (if applicable).
3. Provide your email address.
4. Choose your desired booth size.
5. Select the start and end dates for your exhibition.
6. Choose your preferred start and end time slots.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your exhibition.
8. If you have a specific tool assigned, you can input its name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Booking Form**: Select a suitable exhibition option from the list to determine the type of booking you wish to make.
* **Name**: Please input your full name as we'll use it for communication purposes.
* **Company** (if applicable): If you're representing a company, please input the name of the company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and details.
* **Booth Size**: Choose the booth size that suits your needs, such as 10x10 or 20x20.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date for your exhibition.
* **End Date**: Select the end date for your exhibition.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time for your exhibition.
* **End Time**: Select the end time for your exhibition.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional information or requests, please provide it in the comments field.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool assigned, please input its name.
