# Production Space Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to reserve a production space for specific events or activities. It helps to collect necessary information for efficient planning and management of production spaces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Reservation Request** (page 1) with a brief description of the event or activity you want to reserve the space for.
2. Provide your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** (pages 2-4) to contact you for any further questions or updates.
3. Specify the **Event Details** (page 5) of your event, including any necessary information.
4. Choose the **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Event Duration** (pages 6-8) for your event.
5. Indicate the **Event Time** (page 9) for your event.
6. Specify the number of **Number of Guests** (page 10) attending your event.
7. Choose the **Event Type** (page 11) of your event (e.g., Event, Meeting, Other).
8. Select the **Reservation Type** (page 12) of your event (e.g., Single, Recurring, One-time).
9. Add any additional **Notes** (page 13) about your event.
10. If applicable, specify the **Assigned Tool** and **Assigned To** (pages 14-15) for your event.
11. Finally, fill in the **Form ID** fields (pages 16-23) with any relevant IDs or references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Request**: A brief description of the event or activity you want to reserve the space for.
* **Name**: Your name, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.
* **Email**: Your email address, used for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, used for further questions or updates.
* **Event Details**: A detailed description of your event, including any necessary information.
* **Start Date**: The date you want to start your event.
* **End Date**: The date you want to end your event.
* **Event Duration**: The length of your event.
* **Event Time**: The time of your event.
* **Number of Guests**: The number of people attending your event.
* **Event Type**: The type of event (e.g., Event, Meeting, Other).
* **Reservation Type**: The type of reservation (e.g., Single, Recurring, One-time).
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your event.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, specify the tool assigned to your event.
* **Assigned To**: If applicable, specify the person or team assigned to your event.
* **Form ID**: These fields are used to store any relevant IDs or references, if applicable.
