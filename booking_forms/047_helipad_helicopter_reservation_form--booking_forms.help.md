# helipad_helicopter_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The helipad_helicopter_reservation_form is a form used for booking and managing helicopter services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Booking Details**: Enter the basic booking information, including the purpose and date of the helicopter flight.
2. **Arrival Details**: Provide arrival details, such as the arrival time and flight number.
3. **Departure Details**: Enter departure details, like departure time and flight number.
4. **Facilities/Venue**: Select the facilities or venue required for the booking.
5. **Booking Date**: Enter the date for the booking.
6. **Booking Time**: Select the time for the booking.
7. **Facilities**: If facilities are required, select "Yes".
8. **Venue**: If a specific venue is needed, select "Yes".
9. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
10. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
11. **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email.
12. **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that may be useful for the booking.
13. **Form Id**: Enter any unique identifier for the form submission.
14. **Submission Details**: Enter any relevant submission details.
15. **Submission Date**: Select the date of submission.
16. **Submission Time**: Select the time of submission.
17. **Notes**: Enter any notes about the submission.
18. **Submission Status**: Enter the status of the submission.
19. **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
20. **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
21. **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
22. **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of the person submitting the form.
23. **Submitter Comment**: Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
24. **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the person submitting the form.
25. **Submitter IP**: Enter the IP address of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Details**: Enter the basic details about the booking, including the purpose and date of the helicopter flight.
* **Arrival Details**: Provide information about the arrival time and flight number for the helicopter.
* **Departure Details**: Enter the departure time and flight number for the helicopter.
* **Facilities/Venue**: Select the facilities or venue required for the booking.
	+ "Facilities" and "Venue" are mutually exclusive options. Select "Yes" if you need either or both of these facilities.
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date for the booking. This will help in scheduling and planning the flight.
* **Booking Time**: Select the time for the booking. Ensure you are available and the helicopter is available at this time.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the booking.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the booking.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person responsible for the booking.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for the booking.
* **Form Id**: Enter any unique identifier for the form submission.
* **Submission Details**: Enter any relevant details about the submission.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Select the time of submission.
* **Submission Status**: Enter the current status of the submission.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Comment**: Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter IP**: Enter the IP address of the person submitting the form.
