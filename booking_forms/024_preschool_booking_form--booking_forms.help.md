**Preschool Booking Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Preschool Booking Form is a tool used to collect information from parents or guardians to schedule and manage bookings for their children at a preschool. This form will help the preschool staff to efficiently process and manage booking requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2. Select your address from the list of options in the "Address" field.
3. Enter your child's birth date and birth time (if applicable) in the "Birth Date" and "Birth Time" fields.
4. Enter your preschool ID number.
5. Enter your name as a parent or guardian in the "Parent Name" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Parent Contact" field.
7. Enter your child's name in the "Child Name" field.
8. Enter your child's age.
9. Select your child's grade level from the options in the "Child Grade" field.
10. Enter the start and end dates for the booking in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).
12. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
- **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`<phone>`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`<address>`, `select_one`, required): Select your address from the list of options.
- **Birth Date** (`<birthdate>`, `date`, required): Enter your child's birth date.
- **Birth Time** (`<birthtime>`, `time`, optional): Enter your child's birth time (if applicable).
- **Preschool ID** (`<preschoolid>`, `number`, required): Enter your preschool ID number.
- **Parent Name** (`<parentname>`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
- **Parent Contact** (`<parentcontact>`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information.
- **Child Name** (`<childname>`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name.
- **Child Age** (`<childage>`, `number`, required): Enter your child's age.
- **Child Grade** (`<childgrade>`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's grade level from the options.
- **Start Date** (`<startdate>`, `date`, required): Enter the start date for the booking.
- **End Date** (`<enddate>`, `date`, required): Enter the end date for the booking.
- **Notes** (`<notes>`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Parent Email** (`<parentemail>`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Notes 2** (`<notes2>`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
