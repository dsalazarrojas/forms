# Stage Setup Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Setup Inquiry Form is a simple form used to collect information about stage setup and event details. This form is designed to help our team prepare for your event by gathering essential information about the stage and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an *.
2. Enter the required information in each field.
3. Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
4. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stage Width** (`stage_width`, number, *): Enter the width of the stage in feet.
- **Stage Length** (`stage_length`, number, *): Enter the length of the stage in feet.
- **Stage Height** (`stage_height`, number, *): Enter the height of the stage in feet.
- **Stage Type** (`stage_type`, select_one, *): Select the size of the stage you require: small, medium, or large.
- **Stage Description** (`stage_description`, text, ): This field is for any additional notes or details about the stage setup.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, *): Enter the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, *): Enter the time of the event.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the contact person or team member responsible for the event.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, *): Enter the email address of the contact person or team member.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, ): Enter the phone number of the contact person or team member.
- **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, ): This field is for any additional notes or comments from the contact person or team member.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, ): This field is for any additional event-specific details or information.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- If you need to make changes to your submission, please contact us and we will assist you.
