# Holiday Portrait Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule a holiday portrait appointment with our studio. By filling out this form, you will be able to select a date, time, and photo session type that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date for your holiday portrait appointment. You can choose one or multiple dates from the list provided.
2. Choose a time for your appointment. Again, you can select one or multiple times from the list.
3. Select the type of photo session you prefer: family, couple, individual, or group.
4. Provide any additional details about your session in the "Holiday Portrait Session Details" text fields.
5. Select the duration of your photo session: 30, 45, or 60 minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Portrait Appointment** (`holiday_portrait_appointment_form`): This is the main field for your holiday portrait appointment request. It's a text field where you can briefly describe your needs.
* **Select Date** (`select_date`): This field allows you to choose one or multiple dates for your appointment. Select from the list of available dates.
* **Select Time** (`select_time`): This field allows you to choose one or multiple times for your appointment. Select from the list of available times.
* **Select Session Type** (`select_session_type`): This field lets you choose the type of photo session you prefer: family, couple, individual, or group.
* **Holiday Portrait Session Details** (`holiday_portrait_session_details`): Provide any additional details about your photo session in this text field.
* **Select Photo Package** (`select_photo_package`): This field allows you to choose the duration of your photo session: 30, 45, or 60 minutes.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a date and time that works for you and your family or group.
- Be as specific as possible when providing details about your session in the "Holiday Portrait Session Details" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
