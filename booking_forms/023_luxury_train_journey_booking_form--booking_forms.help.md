# Luxury Train Journey Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Train Journey Booking Form is used to book luxury train travel with the help of an authorized travel agent. To complete this form, please ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Traveler Name, Email, Phone number (if applicable), and other relevant details on page 1.
2. Confirm your Email address to ensure it matches the information you provided.
3. Choose your preferred Train Class (First Class, Second Class, or Third Class) on page 2.
4. Select your preferred Room Type (Single, Double, or Suite) on page 2.
5. Choose your preferred Train Type (Luxury Train or Standard Train) on page 3.
6. Enter your Travel Date and Travel Duration on page 4.
7. Confirm your Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) on page 5.
8. Enter your Payment Details on page 6.
9. Enter your Travel Agent's contact information (Phone, Email, and Name) on page 7.
10. Confirm the Payment Terms on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Name** (`traveler_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your email address to ensure it matches the information above.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about your payment method (e.g., credit card number, expiration date).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required: false): Enter your travel date.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the number of days you plan to travel.
* **Train Class** (`train_class`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred train class (First Class, Second Class, or Third Class).
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred room type (Single, Double, or Suite).
* **Train Type** (`train_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred train type (Luxury Train or Standard Train).
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required: false): Enter your travel agent's name.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your travel agent's phone number.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your travel agent's email address.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your travel agent's name.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, note, required: false): View and confirm payment terms with your travel agent.
