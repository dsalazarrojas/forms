# Resource Booking Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Booking Access Form is a tool used to request access to resources such as meeting rooms, equipment, or office spaces. It helps ensure that resources are used efficiently and are available for other users during the requested time period.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of resource you are booking (e.g., Meeting Room, Equipment, Office Space, or Other).
2. Enter the name of the resource you are booking.
3. Choose the start and end dates of your booking.
4. Select the start and end times of your booking.
5. Specify the duration of your booking.
6. If necessary, provide a reason for your booking.
7. Choose the available access slots for your booking (e.g., 8am-5pm, 9am-6pm, etc.).
8. Add any additional information relevant to your booking.
9. Finally, review and submit the form or cancel it if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, required): Choose the type of resource you wish to book (e.g., Meeting Room, Equipment, Office Space, or Other).
* **Resource Name** (`resource_name`, optional): Enter the specific resource you wish to book (e.g., Conference Room A, Projector 1, etc.).
* **Booking Start Date** (`booking_start_date`, required): Select the start date of your booking (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Booking Start Time** (`booking_start_time`, required): Choose the start time of your booking (HH:MM format, 24-hour clock).
* **Booking End Date** (`booking_end_date`, required): Select the end date of your booking (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Booking End Time** (`booking_end_time`, required): Choose the end time of your booking (HH:MM format, 24-hour clock).
* **Booking Duration** (`booking_duration`, required): Specify the duration of your booking in minutes.
* **Booking Reason** (`booking_reason`, optional): Provide a reason for your booking (e.g., Meeting, Training Session, etc.).
* **Resource Booking Access** (`resource_booking_access`, required): Select the available access slots for your booking.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, optional): Add any additional details relevant to your booking.
* **Submit** (`submit`, optional): Review and submit the form or cancel it if needed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are booking resources during their available time slots.
- Be accurate when providing dates and times to avoid conflicts with other bookings.
- Add any important details in the "Additional Information" field for clarity and context.
- Review your form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
