# Booking Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for guests to provide information related to their booking cancellation, including the reason for cancellation, original booking details, and refund preferences. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Booking Cancellation Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required" in the form, which include:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Booking Reference Number
	* Original Check-In Date
	* Original Check-Out Date
	* Reason for Cancellation
	* Date of Cancellation Request
	* Are You Requesting a Refund
	* I Understand the Cancellation Policy
	* I Agree to Cancellation Terms
2. Optional fields can be filled in if applicable, which include:
	* Original Payment Method
	* Preferred Refund Method
	* Do You Have Travel Insurance
	* Are You Interested in Rebooking
	* Preferred Rebooking Date
	* Feedback Comments
	* May We Contact You About Future Offers
3. Be sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Full Name** (Required)
Your full name as it appears on your identification or passport.

### 2. **Email Address** (Required)
Your contact email address, where we can reach you with any updates or further questions.

### 3. **Phone Number** (Required)
Your contact phone number, where we can contact you for any clarification or updates.

### 4. **Booking Reference Number** (Required)
Your booking confirmation number, as provided in your booking confirmation email or documents.

### 5. **Original Check-In Date** (Required)
The scheduled arrival date as per your original booking.

### 6. **Original Check-Out Date** (Required)
The scheduled departure date as per your original booking.

### 7. **Room Type Booked** (Optional)
The type of room you originally booked, if applicable.

### 8. **Number of Nights** (Optional)
The total number of nights you originally booked.

### 9. **Number of Guests** (Optional)
The total number of guests on your original booking.

### 10. **Reason for Cancellation** (Required)
Select the main reason for cancellation from the provided options:
	* Change of Plans
	* Medical Emergency
	* Family Emergency
	* Travel Restrictions
	* Weather Issues
	* Found Alternative
	* Financial Reasons
	* Work Commitment
	* Other

### 11. **Cancellation Details** (Required)
Please provide additional context or explanation for your reason for cancellation.

### 12. **Date of Cancellation Request** (Required)
The date you are submitting this cancellation request.

### 13. **Days Before Check-in** (Optional)
The number of days before arrival for cancellation.

### 14. **Are You Requesting a Refund** (Required)
Select if you are requesting a refund.

### 15. **Expected Refund Amount** (Optional)
The amount you expect as a refund, if applicable.

### 16. **Original Payment Method** (Optional)
The original payment method used for your booking.

### 17. **Preferred Refund Method** (Optional)
The preferred method for refund, if applicable.

### 18. **Do You Have Travel Insurance** (Optional)
Select if you have travel insurance.

### 19. **Are You Interested in Rebooking** (Optional)
Select if you are interested in rebooking.

### 20. **Preferred Rebooking Date** (Optional)
The date you would like to rebook, if rebooking is desired.

### 21. **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgement** (Required)
By selecting "Yes I understand", you acknowledge that you have read and understood the cancellation policy.

### 22. **Feedback Willingness** (Optional)
Select if you are willing to provide feedback.

### 23. **Feedback Comments** (Optional)
Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

### 24. **May We Contact You About Future Offers** (Optional)
Select if you are willing to receive future offers or communication from us.

### 25. **I Agree to Cancellation Terms** (Required)
By selecting "Yes I agree", you acknowledge that you have read and understood the cancellation terms.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out the required fields.
* Use the provided options for each select field to ensure correct selection.
* If you need assistance or clarification, contact us directly.
* Remember to review and submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
