# Property Inspection Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Inspection Scheduling Form is used to schedule a property inspection. Property managers can use this form to request inspections for specific properties, while inspectors can use it to receive notifications and prepare for their inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Inspection Type" to indicate the purpose of the inspection (e.g., General, Pest, or Fire Inspection).
2. Enter the "Property Address" where the inspection will take place.
3. Enter the "Inspection Date" in the specified format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Enter the "Inspector Name" and "Inspector Phone" (optional) for the inspector assigned to the property.
5. Enter the "Inspector Email" (optional) for communication purposes.
6. Enter any "Notes" (optional) for the inspector, such as specific areas to inspect or any special requests.
7. Click "Submit" to send the request to the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Property Manager** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_property_manager): This field is optional and allows the property manager to enter their name for the inspection request.
    * Type: text
    * Required: no
- **Inspection Date** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspection_date): Enter the date of the inspection in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
    * Type: date
    * Required: yes
- **Inspector Name** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspector_name): Enter the name of the inspector assigned to the property.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
- **Property Address** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_property_address): Enter the address of the property to be inspected.
    * Type: text
    * Required: yes
- **Notes** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_notes): Enter any additional notes for the inspector, such as specific areas to inspect or any special requests.
    * Type: text
    * Required: no
- **Inspection Time** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspection_time): Enter the time of the inspection in the specified format.
    * Type: time
    * Required: yes
- **Inspector Phone** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspector_phone): Enter the phone number of the inspector (optional).
    * Type: text
    * Required: no
- **Inspector Email** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspector_email): Enter the email of the inspector for communication purposes (optional).
    * Type: email
    * Required: no
- **Inspection Type** (property_inspection_scheduling_form_inspection_type): Select the type of inspection (General, Pest, or Fire Inspection).
    * Type: select_one
    * Required: yes

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with the inspection request.
* Double-check the inspection date and time to ensure they are correct.
* Make sure to enter the correct inspector name and contact information.
* Use the notes field to provide any additional information for the inspector.
