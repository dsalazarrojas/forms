# Swim Lane Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swim Lane Reservation Form is designed to collect information for swim lane bookings, allowing pool staff to manage swim lane availability and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address accurately to ensure that we can contact you.
2. Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
3. Select a date for your swim lane reservation.
4. Choose the time slot for your swim lane booking.
5. Specify the duration of your swim lane reservation.
6. Indicate whether you prefer a swim lane, and provide any additional notes if needed.
7. If necessary, add any additional information or notes to support your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name accurately for identification purposes.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: true
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Date**: Select the date for your swim lane reservation.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: true
* **Time**: Choose the time slot for your swim lane booking.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: true
* **Duration**: Specify the length of time for your swim lane reservation.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: true
* **Swim Lane**: Indicate whether you prefer a swim lane.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes to support your swim lane reservation.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
* **Additional Info**: Add any extra information that may be relevant to your booking.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes for your swim lane reservation.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false
