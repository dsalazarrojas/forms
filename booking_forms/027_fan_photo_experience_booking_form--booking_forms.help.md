# Fan Photo Experience Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Photo Experience Booking Form is a tool for fans to book a personalized photo experience with a photographer. This form helps us collect information about your preferences and requirements for the photo session, ensuring that your experience is tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred photographer from the list.
2. Choose the time and duration of your photo session.
3. Select the location for your photo session.
4. Add any additional notes or comments for the photographer.
5. Update the booking status as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fan Photo Experience** (`fan_photo_experience_1`): A brief description of your photo experience, describing what you are looking for.
* **Name** (`name_2`): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the photo.
* **Email** (`email_3`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates about your booking.
* **Phone** (`phone_4`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates about your booking.
* **Session Time** (`session_time_5`): Select the time for your photo session from the dropdown options.
* **Photographer** (`photographer_6`): Select your preferred photographer from the list.
* **Photo Session** (`photo_session_7`): Choose the duration of your photo session from the dropdown options.
* **Photo Session Date** (`photo_session_date_8`): Select the date for your photo session.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location_9`): Select the location for your photo session from the list.
* **Notes** (`notes_10`): Add any additional notes or comments for the photographer.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user_11`): Select the user who will be assigned to your photo session.
* **Photo Session Notes** (`photo_session_notes_12`): Add any additional notes or comments for the photo session.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status_13`): Update the booking status as completed, confirmed, or pending.
* **Booking Status Reason** (`booking_status_reason_14`): Add a reason for the current booking status.
* **Booking Status Date** (`booking_status_date_15`): Select the date for the booking status update.
* **Booking Status Time** (`booking_status_time_16`): Select the time for the booking status update.
* **Photo Session Start Time** (`photo_session_start_time_17`): Set the start time for your photo session.
* **Photo Session End Time** (`photo_session_end_time_18`): Set the end time for your photo session.
* **Photo Session Location** (`photo_session_location_19`): Select the location for your photo session from the list.
* **Photo Session Notes** (`photo_session_notes_20`): Add any additional notes or comments for the photo session.
* **Fan Experience** (`fan_experience_21`): Enter a brief description of your fan experience.
* **Fan Experience** (`fan_experience_22`): Choose the duration of your fan experience from the dropdown options.
* **Fan Experience Notes** (`fan_experience_notes_23`): Add any additional notes or comments for the fan experience.
* **Photo Session Start Date** (`photo_session_start_date_24`): Select the start date for your photo session.
* **Photo Session End Date** (`photo_session_end_date_25`): Select the end date for your photo session.

Note: This form is designed to be completed once per fan experience. If you need to make changes or updates, please contact us directly.
