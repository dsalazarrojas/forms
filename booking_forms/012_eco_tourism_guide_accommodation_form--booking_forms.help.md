# Eco Tourism Guide Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Tourism Guide Accommodation Form is designed to collect information from guests or customers who are planning to visit an eco-tourism guide. The form gathers necessary details such as contact information, travel plans, accommodation requirements, and special requests to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out as many or as few fields as you need based on the information you have.
2. For each field, provide accurate and complete information.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide any relevant personal details about yourself, such as your name, title, or nationality.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide your contact information, including your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide any additional payment information, such as the method of payment or any other relevant payment details.
* **Accommodation Information** (Accommodation Information, text, optional)
  This field is where you can specify any specific accommodation requirements or preferences, such as the type of room or special amenities.
* **Travel Plans** (Travel Plans, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide detailed information about your travel plans, including your destination, dates, or mode of transportation.
* **Arrival Information** (Arrival Information, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide information about your expected arrival time, mode of transportation, or any other relevant arrival details.
* **Booking Information** (Booking Information, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide any information about your booking, including your booking reference number or any other relevant booking details.
* **Travel Documents** (Travel Documents, text, optional)
  This field is where you can upload any necessary travel documents, such as your passport or ID, or provide a link to them.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, optional)
  This field is where you can provide any special requests or requirements, such as specific food preferences or any other relevant special requests.
