# Virtual Office Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Office Booking Form is a tool to help you schedule a meeting or event in our virtual office space. Fill out this form to provide us with the required details and we will do our best to accommodate your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your input in the User Input field.
2. Next, select a date for your event from the Date Input field.
3. Choose a time for your event in the Time Input field.
4. Select the desired room for your event from the Room Input field.
5. If you'd like to add any additional information or instructions, enter it in the Notes Input field.
6. Repeat the User Input field to provide any other details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, **required**): This field is for your personal input. Please enter any relevant information about yourself or your event.
- **Date Input** (`date_input`, `date`, **required**): Select a date for your event.
- **Time Input** (`time_input`, `time`, **required**): Choose a time for your event.
- **Room Input** (`room_input`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the desired room for your event.
  • Room 1
  • Room 2
  • Room 3
- **Notes Input** (`notes_input`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional information or instructions for your event.
- **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, `text`, **optional**): This field is for any other personal input you'd like to provide.
