# holiday_home_visit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a holiday home visit, allowing employees or staff members to plan and book their visits during periods of time off for holidays.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out all the required fields with accurate and valid information.
- Ensure that your visit date and accommodation details are accurate.
- If you have any special requests, provide them in the notes fields.
- Double-check that you have the necessary approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so that our team can reach you for further details.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to visit, ensuring it aligns with your approved holiday schedule.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you plan to visit, so we can plan accordingly.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requests or requirements, please describe them here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for us to reach you.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you have accommodation provided, 'No' otherwise.
* **Accommodation ID** (`accommodation_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your accommodation ID if you have one.
* **Accommodation Name** (`accommodation_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your accommodation.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your booking.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your booking.
* **Special Request 1** (`special_request_1`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special requests, please describe them here.
* **Special Request 2** (`special_request_2`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special requests, please describe them here.

Note: This form requires no approvals. It's intended for employees to self-serve and plan their visits.
