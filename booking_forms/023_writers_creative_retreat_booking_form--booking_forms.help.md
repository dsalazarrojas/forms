# Writers' Creative Retreat Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book a spot in a writers' creative retreat. Please fill out the form with your details to request a booking and provide any additional information that will help us accommodate your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting a date for your booking**. Choose a date that works best for you, and make sure it's within the dates specified for the retreat.
2. **Enter your name**. This is the name that will be associated with your booking.
3. **Provide a valid email address**. This will be the email we use to communicate with you about your booking.
4. **Enter your phone number**. This is the number we will use to contact you regarding your booking.
5. **Submit your request**. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send your booking request to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Date** (`retreat_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Please select a date for your booking in the format MM/DD/YYYY. Choose a date within the specified dates for the retreat.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the retreat materials and communications.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your booking.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable. This will be used for communication purposes only.
