# Buy Your Tickets Today With Moneris - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows you to purchase tickets for an event hosted by Moneris. The form is used to collect the necessary information to complete the ticket purchase process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the name of the event you are attending in the "Event Name" field.
5. Choose the date of the event from the calendar or enter the date manually in the "Event Date" field.
6. Select the start time of the event, if applicable, in the "Event Start Time" field.
7. Choose the type of ticket you want to purchase in the "Ticket Type" field.
8. Enter the number of tickets you need in the "Number of Tickets" field.
9. If applicable, select your seat preference from the options in the "Seat or Section Preference (if applicable)" field.
10. Enter the price per ticket in the "Price Per Ticket" field.
11. Calculate the total cost of your tickets, including any additional fees, in the "Total Cost (Before Fees)" field.
12. Indicate if parking is required in the "Parking or Permit Required" field.
13. If necessary, enter any special accommodations or requests in the "Special Accommodations or Requests" field.
14. If this is your first time attending, select "Yes, first time" in the "Is This Your First Time Attending?" field.
15. If purchasing multiple tickets, enter the names of the other attendees in the "Names of Other Attendees" field.
16. Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
17. Enter your city, state or province in the "City, State or Province" field.
18. Enter your postal or ZIP code in the "Postal Code" field.
19. Indicate whether you want to subscribe to event updates in the "Subscribe to Event Updates" field.
20. If you have a promotional or discount code, enter it in the "Promotional or Discount Code (if applicable)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (2)**: Provide your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Name (4)**: Select the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date (5)**: Choose the date of the event from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Event Start Time (6)**: Select the start time of the event, if applicable.
* **Ticket Type (7)**: Choose the type of ticket you want to purchase.
* **Number of Tickets (8)**: Enter the number of tickets you need to purchase.
* **Seat or Section Preference (if applicable) (9)**: Select your seat preference, if applicable.
* **Price Per Ticket (10)**: Enter the price per ticket.
* **Total Cost (Before Fees) (11)**: Calculate the total cost of your tickets, including any additional fees.
* **Parking or Permit Required (12)**: Indicate if parking is required for your event attendance.
* **Special Accommodations or Requests (13)**: Enter any special accommodations or requests you may have.
* **Is This Your First Time Attending? (14)**: Indicate if this is your first time attending the event.
* **Names of Other Attendees (15)**: Enter the names of other attendees if you are purchasing multiple tickets.
* **Billing Address (16)**: Enter your billing address.
* **City, State or Province (17)**: Enter your city, state or province.
* **Postal Code (18)**: Enter your postal or ZIP code.
* **Subscribe to Event Updates (19)**: Indicate whether you want to subscribe to event updates.
* **Promotional or Discount Code (if applicable) (20)**: Enter any promotional or discount code you may have.
