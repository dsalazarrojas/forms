# flooring_measurement_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flooring_measurement_appointment_form is a template for scheduling appointments for measuring different types of flooring. This form is used to collect customer information and select the services to be measured during the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Schedule Date** for the appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Provide your **Customer Name** and **Phone** number.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. Select the **Measurement Services** you require (Carpet, Hardwood, Vinyl, Tile, or Other).
5. Choose the **Measurement Date** and **Measurement Time** for the appointment.
6. Enter any additional **Notes** about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the measurement.
* **Customer Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Phone** (3, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Email** (4, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Measurement Services** (5, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you would like to have measured (e.g., Carpet, Hardwood, Vinyl, Tile, or Other).
* **Measurement Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to have the measurement done.
* **Measurement Time** (7, time, optional): Enter the time you would like the measurement to take place.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the measurement.
* **Measurement Services List** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you would like to have measured (e.g., Carpet, Hardwood, Vinyl, Tile, or Other).
