# online_taxi_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a taxi online, allowing users to input necessary information such as date, time, fare, and payment details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for your taxi booking on each page.
2. Fill out the required fields for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: The title of your taxi booking.
* **First Page**: Enter your pickup date and time.
* **Second Page**: Enter your pickup and drop-off locations.
* **Payment Page**: Enter your payment information.
	+ **Payment Information**: Enter a brief description of your payment details.
	+ **Payment Method**: Select whether you have a preferred payment method (Note: This field is currently a placeholder and requires further explanation).
	+ **Payment Gateway**: Select whether you have a preferred payment gateway (Note: This field is currently a placeholder and requires further explanation).
* **Payment Info**: Enter any additional payment information.
* **Payment Method Select**: Select multiple payment methods for your taxi booking.
* **Payment Gateway Select**: Select multiple payment gateways for your taxi booking.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your taxi booking.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your taxi booking.
* **Fare**: Enter the fare for your taxi booking.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount for your taxi booking.
* **Payment Currency**: Select the payment currency for your taxi booking.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
