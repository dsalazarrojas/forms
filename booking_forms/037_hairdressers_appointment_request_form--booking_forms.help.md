# Hairdressers Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for clients or users to request appointments with hairdressers. The form helps the user to provide contact information, select services needed, choose a preferred stylist, and schedule a date and time for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
2. Select the services you need from the **Services** field.
3. Choose a preferred stylist from the **Stylist** field.
4. Enter a date for your appointment in the **Date** field.
5. Enter a time for your appointment in the **Time** field.
6. If needed, add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
7. Review and edit your submission in the **Form Submission** field before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**:
	+ This field is for your name and contact information.
	+ Please fill it in accordingly.
* **Services**:
	+ Select the services you need, such as haircut, color, or style.
	+ You can select multiple services.
* **Stylist**:
	+ Choose a preferred stylist from the list.
* **Date**:
	+ Enter a date for your appointment.
	+ Format should be in DD/MM/YY (DD - day, MM - month, YY - year).
* **Time**:
	+ Enter a time for your appointment.
	+ Format should be in 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
* **Notes** (optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Form Submission**:
	+ This field is for reviewing and editing your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.
	+ Make sure to review all fields before submitting the form.
