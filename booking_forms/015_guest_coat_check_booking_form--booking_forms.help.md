# Guest Coat Check Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Coat Check Booking Form is used to collect information from guests about their coats, allowing event staff to efficiently manage coat check services during events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the **Guest Name** field.
2. Enter the guest's email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter the guest's contact number in the **Contact Number** field. This is required for SMS reminders.
4. Select the **Coat Size** from the dropdown menu.
5. Select one or more **Coat Color(s)** from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the **Coat Type** from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the guest's **Arrival Time** in the format of "HH:MM" (e.g., 14:00).
8. Enter the time you would like to **Collect Your Coat** in the format of "HH:MM" (e.g., 17:00).
9. If applicable, provide **Event Details** in the **Event Details** field.
10. Enter the **Event ID** number.
11. Enter the **Event Name** of the event.
12. Enter the **Event Date** in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., 2022-02-15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the guest's name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the guest's email address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the guest's contact number for SMS reminders.
- **Coat Size** (`coat_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the coat from the dropdown menu.
- **Coat Color** (`coat_color`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more colors of the coat from the dropdown menu.
- **Coat Type** (`coat_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coat from the dropdown menu.
- **Guest Arrival Time** (`guest_arrival_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the guest arrives in the format of "HH:MM".
- **Coat Collection Time** (`coat_collection_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to collect your coat in the format of "HH:MM".
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide any additional event details.
- **Event ID** (`event_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the Event ID number.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event in the format of "YYYY-MM-DD".
