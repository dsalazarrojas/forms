## travel_itinerary_planning_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The travel_itinerary_planning_form is a simple form that helps users provide information about their travel plans. It can be used by travel agents or travel agencies to get a better understanding of the user's preferences and offer tailored travel suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the form and select "Destination" to provide the location you plan to travel to.
2. Enter your "Departure Date" to specify the date you plan to leave for your trip.
3. Choose your accommodation type from the options provided (e.g., hotel, apartment, etc.).
4. Enter the name of your chosen accommodation.
5. Select the activities you want to participate in during your trip (multiple selections allowed).
6. Enter the "Start Date" of your trip.
7. Enter the "End Date" of your trip.
8. Select the "Return Date" of your trip.
9. Provide the name of the travel agency handling your trip.
10. Enter the name of your travel agent (if applicable).
11. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
12. Add any additional notes about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination** (`destination`, text, required/optional): Enter the city, country, or region you plan to visit.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to leave for your trip.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, text, required/optional): Choose a type of accommodation that suits your needs, such as hotel, apartment, or resort.
* **Accommodation Name** (`accommodation_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your chosen accommodation.
* **Activities** (`activities`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the activities you want to participate in during your trip, such as sightseeing, hiking, or museum visits.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to begin your trip.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to end your trip.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to return from your trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the travel agency handling your trip.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your travel agent (if applicable).
* **Customer** (`customer`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your trip.
