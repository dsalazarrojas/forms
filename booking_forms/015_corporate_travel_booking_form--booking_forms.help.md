# Corporate Travel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Travel Booking Form is used to request company approval for corporate travel bookings. It collects information about your travel plans, allowing the travel administrator to verify that your trip is company-approved and meets company guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately with the required information.
2. Review the form fields and their explanations below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_date (id: 1)**: Date of travel
  Enter the date of your corporate travel, following the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YYYY.
* **start_time (id: 2)**: Start time of travel
  Enter the start time of your travel. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **end_time (id: 3)**: End time of travel
  Enter the end time of your travel. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **travel_location (id: 4)**: Travel location
  Enter the location of your travel, including city, state, and country (if applicable).
* **travel_purpose (id: 5)**: Travel purpose
  Briefly describe the purpose of your travel, including any relevant details about the meeting or event.
* **travel_type (id: 6)**: Type of travel
  Select the type of travel that best describes your trip (e.g., business meeting, conference, etc.).
  Options:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
* **employee_name (id: 7)**: Employee name
  Enter your name as it appears on your company ID card.
* **employee_email (id: 8)**: Employee email
  Enter your company email address.
* **employee_phone (id: 9)**: Employee phone
  Enter your company phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure that your travel is company-approved before submitting this form.
* Review your company's travel policy for any specific requirements or restrictions.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact your travel administrator.
