# leadership_workshop_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book leadership workshops. It gathers information about the user and the workshop they wish to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information, which includes any details you'd like to share.
2. Provide details about the workshop you're booking.
3. Share any participant requests or special instructions.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information, text, optional)**: Fill in your basic contact information such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Workshop Information (Workshop Information, text, optional)**: Describe the leadership workshop you're interested in, including its title or any relevant details.
* **Booking Details (Booking Details, text, optional)**: Share any specific booking details, such as dates or times.
* **Participant Requests (Participant Requests, text, optional)**: If you have any requests or special requirements for participants, such as dietary needs or accessibility, please share them here.
