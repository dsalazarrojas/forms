# Historical Site Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Historical Site Tour Appointment Form is designed to be a comprehensive guide to booking historical site tours. This form will help you schedule a tour that suits your needs and preferences, while also assisting tour operators with confirming availability and preparing for the tour.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select your availability for the tour by choosing a time slot from the list of options.
2.  Next, select your preferred language for the tour. This will ensure that you can fully understand the tour content.
3.  Please fill in your contact information: first and last name, email, and phone number.
4.  If you have any special requests or additional information, please provide them in the notes field.
5.  Finally, confirm your booking by checking the "Booking Confirmation" box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Operator** (`tour-operator`): Choose the tour operator that will be leading your tour.
* **Visitor First Name** (`visitor-first-name`): Enter your first name.
* **Visitor Last Name** (`visitor-last-name`): Enter your last name.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor-email`): Enter your email address.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor-phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Tour Date** (`tour-date`): Select the date of your tour.
* **Tour Time** (`tour-time`): Select the time of your tour.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional information about your tour.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Choose a time slot for your tour.
* **Tour Language** (`tour-language`): Select your preferred language for the tour.
* **Notes 2** (`notes-2`): Add any additional information about your tour.
* **Tour Type** (`tour-type`): Choose the type of tour you're interested in (Walking, Group, or Private).
* **Number of Visitors** (`number-of-visitors`): Enter the number of visitors attending your tour.
* **Additional Notes 2** (`additional-notes-2`): Add any additional comments or questions about your tour.
* **Tour Operator Notes** (`tour-operator-notes`): Enter any notes or information for the tour operator.
* **Booking Confirmation** (`booking-confirmation`): Confirm your booking by checking this box.
* **Tour Date 2** (`tour-date-2`): Select the date of your tour.
* **Tour Time 2** (`tour-time-2`): Select the time of your tour.
* **Additional Notes 2** (`additional-notes-2`): Add any additional information about your tour.
* **Visitor Language** (`visitor-language`): Select your preferred language for the tour.
* **Visitor Phone 2** (`visitor-phone-2`): Enter your phone number.
* **Visitor Email 2** (`visitor-email-2`): Enter your email address.
* **Visitor First Name 2** (`visitor-first-name-2`): Enter your first name.

## Tips

* Please double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Ensure that you select your preferred language for the tour.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please provide them in the "Notes" field.
* Confirm your booking by checking the "Booking Confirmation" box.
