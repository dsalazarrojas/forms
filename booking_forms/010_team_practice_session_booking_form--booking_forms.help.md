# team_practice_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team practice session booking form is designed for teams to book and manage their practice sessions. This form helps the team and coaches to gather important information about the practice session, such as date, time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure you provide all required fields (indicated by *) are filled out.
3. Review your form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Practice Session Booking Form** (text, required): A text field for the team to provide a brief description of their practice session.
* **Start Date** (date, required): The date of the practice session.
* **Start Time** (time, required): The start time of the practice session (am or pm).
* **End Date** (date, required): The end date of the practice session.
* **End Time** (time, required): The end time of the practice session.
* **Duration in Minutes** (number, required): The length of the practice session in minutes.
* **Team Size** (number, optional): The number of team members attending the practice session.
* **Practice Facilities** (select multiple, optional): Select the facilities that the team will be using during the practice session (field, field, other).
* **Coach Name** (text, required): The name of the coach or instructor leading the practice session.
* **Coach Email** (email, required): The email address of the coach or instructor.
* **Coach Phone** (text, required): The phone number of the coach or instructor.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Additional notes or comments about the practice session.
* **Team Name** (text, optional): The name of the team.
* **Team Email** (email, optional): The email address of the team.
* **Team Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the team.
* **Practice Location** (text, optional): The location of the practice session.
* **Practice Level** (select one, optional): The skill level of the practice session (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Practice Description** (text, optional): A brief description of the practice session.
* **Practice Type** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of practice session (soccer, hockey, basketball).
* **Start Time Am/Pm** (select one, optional): Select the am or pm of the practice session start time.
* **Team Practice Time Zone** (select multiple, optional): Select the time zone of the practice session (field, Other).
* **Team Practice Time Zone Option** (text, optional): Additional time zone information.
* **Practice Days** (select multiple, optional): Select the days of the practice session (field, Other).
* **Practice Days Option 1** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.
* **Practice Days Option 2** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.
* **Practice Days Option 3** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.
* **Practice Days Option 4** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.
* **Practice Days Option 5** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.
* **Practice Days Option 6** (text, optional): Additional practice days information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your coach or instructor for guidance.
* This form is for team practice session booking purposes only. If you have any other booking requests, please use the appropriate form for that purpose.
