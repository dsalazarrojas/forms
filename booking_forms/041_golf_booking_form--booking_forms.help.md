# Golf Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from players who are booking a golf course. It is intended to capture essential details such as player name, email, phone number, and booking preferences to facilitate a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your player name
2. Select the golf course name
3. Choose a booking date
4. Select your gender
5. Choose a booking time
6. Enter the number of players in your group
7. Enter your email address
8. Enter your phone number
9. Add any additional message to the course administrators
10. Confirm your booking details

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself for the booking.
* **Golf Course Name** (`golf_course_name`, text, required): Select the name of the golf course you would like to book.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your golf booking.
* **Player Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender to help us better serve you.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, time, required): Select a time for your golf booking.
* **Number of Players** (`number_of_players`, number, required): Enter the number of players in your group.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required): Add any additional message to the course administrators.
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, select_one, required): Confirm your booking details.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you would like to book.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the course.
* **Course** (`course`, text, required): Enter any other course details.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, required): Enter a rating for the course.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required): Enter a description for the course.
* **Course Image URL** (`course_image_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the course image.
* **Course Video URL** (`course_video_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the course video.
* **Course Phone** (`course_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the course.
* **Course Email** (`course_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the course.
* **Course Website** (`course_website`, text, required): Enter the website of the course.
* **Course Facebook URL** (`course_facebook_url`, text, required): Enter the Facebook URL of the course.
* **Course Twitter URL** (`course_twitter_url`, text, required): Enter the Twitter URL of the course.
* **Course Instagram URL** (`course_instagram_url`, text, required): Enter the Instagram URL of the course.
* **Course Facebook Page** (`course_facebook_page`, text, required): Enter the Facebook page URL of the course.
* **Course Twitter Page** (`course_twitter_page`, text, required): Enter the Twitter page URL of the course.
