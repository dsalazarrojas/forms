# mountain_retreat_cabin_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mountain retreat cabin booking form is used to gather information for booking a cabin at a mountain retreat. Fill out this form to start the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your reservation in the **Reservation** field.
2. Choose the type of cabin you would like to book in the **Cabin Type** field.
3. Enter your start date of stay in the **Start Date** field.
4. Enter your end date of stay in the **End Date** field.
5. Provide the necessary guest details in the **Guest Details** field.
6. Enter your **First Name**.
7. Enter your **Last Name**.
8. Enter your **Email** address.
9. Enter your **Phone** number.
10. Enter the date you plan to check in in the **Checkin Date** field.
11. Enter the date you plan to check out in the **Checkout Date** field.
12. Read and agree to the **Terms Agreement**.
13. Provide a brief description of your agreement in the **Terms Agreement Text** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation** (`reservation`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your reservation.
* **Cabin Type** (`cabin_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of cabin you would like to book.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your start date of stay.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter your end date of stay.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, `text`, optional): Provide the necessary guest details.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to check in.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to check out.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `text`, optional): Read and agree to the terms of agreement.
* **Terms Agreement Text** (`terms_agreement_text`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your agreement to the terms of agreement.
