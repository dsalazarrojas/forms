# Event Invitation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for generating event invitations. It helps you gather the necessary information to create invitations for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your contact phone number.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Choose whether to RSVP or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Your name, as you want it to appear on the invitation.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Your phone number for event contacts or follow-up.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Your email address for event invitations or follow-up.
* **rsvp** (`rsvp`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check 'No' if you cannot attend the event, or select 'Yes' to confirm your attendance.
