# tattoo_booking_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who are interested in booking a tattoo session with our artists. This form helps us to understand their preferences and requirements, and it ensures that we can tailor our services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name: First and last name of the customer.
2. Enter your email: A valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number: A phone number that we can use to contact you.
4. Describe the tattoo design: Briefly describe the tattoo design you are interested in.
5. Choose a date and time: Select a date and time that work for you and our available artists.
6. Select an artist: Choose from a list of available artists.
7. Add any additional message: If you have any special requests or questions, add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Tattoo Design** (`tattoo_design`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the tattoo design you are interested in.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select a date for your tattoo session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for your tattoo session.
* **Artist** (`artist`, text, required: false): Choose from a list of available artists.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests or questions, add them here.
