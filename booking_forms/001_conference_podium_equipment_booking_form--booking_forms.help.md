# Conference Podium Equipment Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Podium Equipment Booking Form is a critical document for requesting equipment and services for conferences, presentations, or events. This form helps organizers and technical teams prepare and manage the necessary equipment and support for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, enter your **Requestor Full Name** in the "Requestor Full Name" field. This is your full legal name, as you will be the main contact person for this booking request.
2.  Next, select your **Department or Organization** from the provided options or enter it as a text input. This helps us understand your affiliation and departmental needs.
3.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will send confirmation and updates about your booking request.
4.  Provide your **Phone Number** for contact in the "Phone Number" field. This is crucial for us to get in touch with you in case of urgent matters.
5.  Enter the **Event Name** in the "Event Name" field. This should be the title of your conference or presentation.
6.  Choose the **Event Date** from the date picker. This is crucial for scheduling the equipment setup and availability.
7.  Select the **Start Time** and **End Time** for your event using the time picker. This helps us understand your event schedule.
8.  Enter the **Venue Location** where your event will take place. This is essential for us to deliver the equipment correctly.
9.  Choose the **Podium Type Required** from the provided options. This ensures we provide the suitable podium for your presentation.
10. Select all **Microphone Requirements** that apply to your event. This helps us prepare the necessary audio equipment.
11. Choose any **Additional AV Equipment** needed from the provided options. This allows us to include the necessary equipment for your event.
12.  If you need **Technical Support**, select the option that best suits your needs.
13. If you have any **Setup Time Required**, choose the time frame for the equipment setup.
14. Add any **Special Requirements or Notes** you may have in the text field. This is a chance to mention any specific needs or requests you have.
15.  Estimate the **Number of Attendees** for your event. This helps us prepare the equipment accordingly.
16.  Provide an alternative **Backup Contact Name** and **Phone Number** in case the main contact is unavailable.
17. Finally, confirm that you **agree to the equipment usage terms and conditions**. This is an essential step for ensuring the safe and responsible use of the equipment.
