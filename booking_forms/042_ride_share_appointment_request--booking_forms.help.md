# Ride Share Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a ride-sharing appointment request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Pickup Info field to select a pickup location and time.
2. Provide details about your ride request in the Ride Request field.
3. Add any additional notes in the Notes field if needed.
4. Select Submit to submit your request, or Cancel to exit without submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, required): Please fill in your information in this field to provide your details for the ride-sharing appointment request.
* **Pickup Info** (`Pickup Info`, `text`, required): Select a pickup location and time in this field.
* **Ride Request** (`Ride Request`, `text`, required): Provide details about the type of ride you are requesting, such as a pickup from a specific location or a specific type of vehicle.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your request, such as special requests or instructions.
* **Submit** (Select, required): Select Submit to submit your ride-sharing appointment request, or Cancel to exit without submitting.
