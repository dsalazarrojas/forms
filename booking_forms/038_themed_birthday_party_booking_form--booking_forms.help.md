# themed_birthday_party_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a themed birthday party. It captures the essential details of the party, including party date, time, title, theme, and guest list. It will help you provide your guests with a great birthday experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information on the first page, "Party Details".
2. Enter the date and time of the party in the "Party Date" and "Party Time" fields.
3. Add the title and theme of the party in the "Event Name" and "Party Theme" fields.
4. Specify the number of guests attending in the "Number of Guests" field.
5. Select the guests attending from the "Guest List" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Details** (Party Preferences, text, optional): This page allows you to enter any additional information or preferences for the party.
* **Party Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the party in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Party Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the party in the format "HH:MM".
* **Event Name** (text, optional): Enter a title for the party, such as "Jane's Birthday Party".
* **Party Theme** (text, optional): Choose a theme for the party, such as "Hollywood Glam" or "Retro Disco".
* **Number of Guests** (number, optional): Enter the number of guests attending the party.
* **Guest List** (select multiple, optional): Select the guests attending the party from the list.
