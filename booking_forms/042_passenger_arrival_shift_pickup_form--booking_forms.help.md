# Passenger Arrival Shift Pickup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from passengers who are arriving at a location and need to be picked up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Arrival Details** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Passenger Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** in the text field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@domain.com`.
5. Select your **Preferred Shift** by choosing "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter your **Arrival Time** in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
7. Choose your **Arrival Time Zone** from the list (e.g., EST, PST, CST, or MST).
8. Enter your **Flight Number** in the text field.
9. Describe your **Travel Document** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Arrival Details
* **Arrival Details** (`arrival_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your arrival circumstances.

### Passenger Name
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.

### Preferred Shift
* **Preferred Shift** (`preferred_shift`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if you have a preferred shift, "No" otherwise.

### Arrival Time
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you expect to arrive.

### Arrival Time Zone
* **Arrival Time Zone** (`arrival_time_zone`, select_one, not required): Choose your time zone from the list.

### Flight Number
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, not required): Enter your flight number.

### Travel Document
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, text, not required): Describe your travel document, such as a visa or passport.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our staff.
