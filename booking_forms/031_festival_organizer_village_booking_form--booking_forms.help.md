# Festival Organizer Village Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by village administrators to collect information from festival organizers for booking purposes. It helps to streamline the process of understanding the needs and preferences of festival organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. If you need help or have questions, contact the village administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Organizer Village Booking Form** (`booking_form`, Text, Required): This is the main title of the form.
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, Text, Optional): Please provide a brief description of the guests coming to the festival.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, Text, Optional): If you need to contact us, please provide your contact details here.
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional details about the booking.
* **Booking Dates** (`booking_dates`, Text, Optional): Please specify the dates of the booking.
* **Booking Hours** (`booking_hours`, Text, Optional): Indicate the hours of the booking.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, Text, Optional): Describe the type of accommodation required.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the options for the accommodation type.
* **Guests** (`guests`, Text, Optional): Provide the number of guests coming.
* **Accommodation Price** (`accommodation_price`, Number, Optional): Specify the price of the accommodation.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, Date, Optional): Specify the date of arrival.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, Date, Optional): Specify the date of departure.
* **Accommodation Price Range** (`accommodation_price_range`, Number, Optional): Specify the price range of the accommodation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Any additional notes or comments.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, Email, Optional): If you need to contact us for further information, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for contact.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Provide your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Provide your address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional): Click the submit button to send the form.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or need help, contact the village administrators.
