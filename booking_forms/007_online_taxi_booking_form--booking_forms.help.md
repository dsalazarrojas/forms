# online_taxi_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online taxi booking form is used to gather information from users to facilitate the taxi booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your address or pickup and drop-off points in the `Address` field.
2. Indicate whether you want to pay for the taxi fare in the `Taxi Fares` field.
3. Select whether the trip is for 'Yes' or 'No' in the `Trip` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
6. Select a time for the trip in the `Time` field.
7. Select a date for the trip in the `Date` field.
8. Enter a description of your request in the `Description` field.
9. If you have any additional information or clarification for the driver, you can enter it in the `Address Hint` field.
10. Finally, click the `Submit` button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (text): Enter your pickup and drop-off points, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Taxi Fares** (Select Multiple): Check 'Yes' if you want to pay for the taxi fare, or check 'No' if you are not planning to pay.
* **Trip** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if the trip is for a purpose other than personal use, or 'No' if it's for personal use.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or information about your booking.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or information about your booking.
* **Time** (Time): Select the time for your pickup, and our driver will do their best to arrive on time.
* **Date** (Date): Select the date for your trip, and our driver will be there to pick you up.
* **Description** (Text): Enter a description of your request, such as where you are going or what you need to carry.
* **Address Hint** (Text): This field is optional, but it's a good idea to enter any additional information about your pickup and drop-off points.
* **Address Hint** (Text): This field is similar to the above, but with a more prominent label for clarity.
* **Submit** (Note): This button is used to complete and submit your form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to guarantee that your booking is accepted.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
- Please enter your email address correctly, as we will use it to contact you with any updates or information.
- Please select the correct date and time for your trip to avoid any confusion with the driver. 
- Our driver will arrive at the selected time and date to pick you up. 
- If you have any additional information, enter it in the `Address Hint` field.
