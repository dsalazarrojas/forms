# Couples Wellness Getaway Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for couples to book a wellness getaway package. It consists of four pages, each with specific fields that need to be completed to ensure a successful booking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the first page, "Form".
2. You can enter any relevant details or comments on this page.
3. Proceed to page 2, "Booking Details" to enter specific details about the booking.
4. On page 3, "Booking Information", select whether you would like to receive a confirmation email.
5. Enter your email address on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form**: A brief introduction to the form. This page is a free-form text field where you can enter any additional information or comments about your booking.
* **Booking Details**: Enter any relevant details about your booking, such as dates, times, or special requests.
* **Booking Information**: Select whether you would like to receive a confirmation email about your booking. Choose "Yes" if you want to receive an email and "No" if you do not.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can receive the confirmation email if you selected "Yes" in the previous field.
