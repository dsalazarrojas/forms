## Purpose
This guide is intended for end-users to fill out the "Smart Shuttle Coordination Application" form, which is used to provide information about a trip's details, such as departure and arrival times, dates, and other relevant details.

To avoid duplication, please ensure that you only fill out this form once for each trip.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the trip details in the "Trip details" field.
2. Select the departure time from the calendar.
3. Select the arrival time from the calendar.
4. Enter the trip date in the "Trip date" field.
5. Choose the type of trip from the "Trip type" dropdown menu.
6. Select the number of riders and vehicle capacity.
7. If necessary, select any additional rider needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart shuttle form** (`smart_shuttle_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip in this field.
* **Trip details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter detailed information about the trip, such as the destination, reason for the trip, etc.
* **Departure time** (`departure_time`, time, optional): Select the departure time from the calendar to schedule the trip.
* **Arrival time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Select the arrival time from the calendar to schedule the trip.
* **Trip date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Trip type** (`trip_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of trip:
	+ Event
	+ Regular
	+ School
	+ Other
* **Rider capacity** (`rider_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the number of riders for the trip.
* **Vehicle capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the capacity of the vehicle for the trip.
* **Rider needs** (`rider_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any special needs for the riders:
	+ Wheelchair accessible
	+ Elderly assistance
	+ Child seat
	+ Other needs

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and detailed information to ensure the smooth execution of the trip.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
