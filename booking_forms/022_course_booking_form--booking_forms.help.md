# Course Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for booking a course. It captures information about the course, student, and booking details such as date, time, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, which are marked with a `*` symbol.
* Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
* Enter the date you would like to book the course in the "Date" field.
* Enter your name and any other relevant details in the "Student Name" field.
* Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
* Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
* Enter any additional message or notes in the "Message" field.
* Select your preferred booking option from the "Submit" dropdown list.
* Finally, submit the form to complete your course booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you would like to book. 
* **Date**: Choose the date you would like to book the course on. 
* **Student Name**: Enter your name and any other relevant details, such as your student ID. 
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date you would like to book the course. 
* **Number Of Places**: Enter the number of places you would like to book. 
* **Booking Time**: Choose the time you would like to book the course on. 
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your booking. 
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, which will be used to communicate with you about your booking. 
* **Message**: Enter any additional message or notes you would like to provide about your booking. 
* **Submit**: Select one of the available options to confirm your booking request. 
* **Course Description**: This field is not required, and it is intended for optional additional information about the course. However, you can enter any relevant details about the course if needed. 
* **Submit**: Please select one of the available options to confirm your booking request.
