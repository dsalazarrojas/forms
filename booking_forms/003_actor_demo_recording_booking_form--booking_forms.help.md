# Actor Demo Recording Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Demo Recording Booking Form is a crucial tool for actors to provide necessary information for booking a demo recording session. This form helps the studio to prepare and tailor the session according to the actor's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of demo reel you want to record.
3. Select your preferred recording package (e.g., Basic, Standard, or Premium) and session length.
4. Specify your preferred recording date and start time.
5. Choose your studio location preference.
6. Indicate whether you need script selection assistance.
7. If needed, provide any special technical requests (e.g., specific microphones or music beds).
8. Finally, let us know how you heard about us (e.g., social media, referral, search engine).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your name as the artist or actor.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the session details and schedules.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for easy communication and coordination.
* **Agency / Representation (if any)** (`agent_info`, `text`, optional): If you're represented by an agency, enter their name and contact information.
* **Type of Demo Reel** (`demo_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary focus of your demo reel (e.g., Voiceover, Singing, or Narration).
* **Recording Package** (`session_package`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred recording package (e.g., Basic, Standard, or Premium) and session length.
* **Preferred Recording Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred date for the recording session.
* **Preferred Start Time** (`booking_time`, `time`, required): Choose your preferred start time for the session.
* **Studio Location Preference** (`studio_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose between the main studio, west side annex, or remote/home studio options.
* **Do you need script selection assistance?** (`script_assistance`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you need our script assistance or have your own scripts.
* **Special Technical Requests** (`specific_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific technical requirements or requests you might have (e.g., microphones or music beds).
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know how you heard about us, so we can thank our referrers or improve our marketing efforts.
