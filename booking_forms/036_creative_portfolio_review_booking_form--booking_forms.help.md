# Creative Portfolio Review Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Portfolio Review Booking Form is a tool designed to help you book a review session for your creative portfolio. This form will gather necessary information from you to schedule an appointment with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information, and make sure to provide accurate details for a successful review session.
2. Review the provided options for the "Category" field and select one that best describes your portfolio.
3. Ensure you have filled out all the necessary fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Creative Portfolio Review Booking Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This is a title placeholder, and you can leave it blank or fill it out as you see fit.
  *Please note that this field is not mandatory.*
 
- **Full Name** (`form_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your full name, which will be used for communication purposes.
 
- **Email** (`form_email`, email, required: false): Please provide a valid email address where you can be reached for further instructions or updates.
 
- **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required: false): This is your contact phone number, where our team can reach you for any last-minute arrangements.
 
- **Portfolio Link** (`form_portfolio_link`, text, required: false): If you have a link to your online portfolio, please provide it here. This will help our team get familiar with your work before the review session.
 
- **Review Session** (`form_review_session`, date, required: false): This is the date and time you would like to schedule the review session.
 
- **Review Time** (`form_review_time`, time, required: false): Select the time slot you are available for the review session.
 
- **Category** (`form_category`, select one, required: false): Choose the best category that represents your creative work. Options: 
  - Photography
  - Graphic Design
  - Web Development
  - Video Production
 
- **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any specific comments or instructions for our team, please add them here.
 
- **Agreement** (`form_agreement`, select one, required: false): Please indicate your agreement for our review session. Options: 
  - 'True'
  - 'False'
 
- **Terms and Conditions** (`form_terms`, text, required: false): If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to our terms and conditions.

# Please submit this form after filling out all the required information.
