# Dog Training Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Class Reservation Form is used to book a spot in a dog training class. To use this form, fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. In the Select Class section, choose the class you are interested in taking by selecting the corresponding option.
3. Enter the Date for the class in the Date field. This should be the date you wish to attend the class.
4. Choose the Time for the class in the Time field. This should be the time slot you prefer.
5. If you have a preferred class duration, enter it in the Class Duration field.
6. Add any additional Notes about your booking.
7. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
8. Finally, enter your Phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information. This field is not required.
* **Select Class**: Choose the class you wish to book. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Date**: Enter the date you wish to attend the class. This field uses the standard date format (month/day/year).
* **Time**: Enter the time slot you prefer for the class.
* **Class Duration**: Enter the preferred class duration in minutes. This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your booking. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter your Phone number. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to successfully submit the form.
- If you encounter any issues, contact our support team for assistance.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form has been properly configured to display the fields in the correct order for the user to follow along.
