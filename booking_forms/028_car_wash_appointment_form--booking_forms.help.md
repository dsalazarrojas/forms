# Car Wash Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Wash Appointment Form is designed to help customers book a car wash appointment with the service provider's team. It helps the service provider understand the customer's vehicle details, preferred wash date and time, and other essential information to provide a quality service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. For optional fields, provide information if you have it, but don't hesitate to ask if you're unsure about anything.
4. If you have any special requests or concerns, please mention them in the "Any special requests or concerns?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): Please enter your name.
* **Email address** (Customer Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address to help us contact you efficiently.
* **Phone number** (Customer Phone, Text, Required): Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Preferred wash date** (Preferred Date, Date, Required): Choose the date you'd like to book your car wash appointment.
* **Preferred wash time** (Preferred Time, Time, Required): Select the time you'd like to book your car wash appointment.
* **How flexible is your time?** (Time Flexibility, Select One, Not Required): Choose how flexible you are with your time to help us schedule the appointment.
* **Vehicle make and model** (Vehicle Make Model, Text, Required): Enter your vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle color** (Vehicle Color, Text, Optional): If you'd like to provide your vehicle's color, please do so.
* **Vehicle size** (Vehicle Size, Select One, Required): Choose the size of your vehicle.
* **Current vehicle condition** (Vehicle Condition, Select One, Required): Select the current condition of your vehicle.
* **Wash package selection** (Wash Package, Select One, Required): Choose the wash package that suits your needs.
* **What's included in your chosen package?** (Wash Package Details, Text, Optional): If you have any specific requests or concerns about the package, please mention them here.
* **Additional services** (Additional Services, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any additional services you'd like to include in your wash.
* **Any special requests or concerns?** (Special Requests, Text, Optional): If you have any allergies or specific requirements, please mention them here.
* **Interior cleaning needed?** (Interior Cleaning, Select One, Optional): Check if you need interior cleaning services.
* **Window tinting to protect?** (Window Tinting, Select One, Optional): If you'd like us to apply window tinting, please select this option.
* **Service location preference** (Service Location, Select One, Required): Choose where you'd like to receive the service (at our facility or at your location).
* **Service address (if mobile service)** (Service Address, Text, Optional): If you've chosen to receive the service at your location, please provide the address.
* **Is this an express or standard duration appointment?** (Estimated Duration, Select One, Optional): Choose the duration of your appointment (30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, or 2+ hours).
* **Would you like a recurring appointment?** (Recurring Appointment, Select One, Optional): If you'd like to schedule regular appointments, select this option.
* **Are you a loyalty program member?** (Loyalty Program, Select One, Optional): If you're a member of our loyalty program, let us know.
* **Preferred payment method** (Payment Method, Select One, Required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your service.
* **Estimated wash cost** (Estimated Cost, Number, Optional): If you're unsure about the estimated cost, we'll provide you with more information.
* **Preferred contact method** (Contact Preference, Select One, Optional): Select how you'd like to be contacted for updates and information.
* **Any additional notes or requirements?** (Additional Notes, Text, Optional): If you have any further requests or requirements, please mention them here.
