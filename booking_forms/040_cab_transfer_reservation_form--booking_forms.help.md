# CAB Transfer Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CAB Transfer Reservation Form is used to book a private car service transfer for yourself or others. This form helps our drivers understand your specific needs and preferences for the transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your full name and contact information (phone number and email).
2. Provide the pickup and drop-off locations, including the street addresses and city names.
3. Select a date for the reservation and a preferred pickup time.
4. Specify your time flexibility, number of passengers, and any special requirements.
5. Choose a vehicle type that suits your needs.
6. Decide if you need a one-way or round-trip transfer.
7. If applicable, select a return trip date and time.
8. Choose your preferred payment method.
9. Add any special instructions for the driver, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you want to be referred to during the transfer.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number so our team can reach you.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want us to contact you.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Provide the name of the location where you want to be picked up.
* **Pickup Street Address** (`pickup_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address where you want to be picked up.
* **City** (`pickup_city`, text, required): Enter the city name of the pickup location.
* **Dropoff Location** (`dropoff_location`, text, required): Provide the name of the location where you want to be dropped off.
* **Dropoff Street Address** (`dropoff_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address where you want to be dropped off.
* **Dropoff City** (`dropoff_city`, text, required): Enter the city name of the drop-off location.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Select the date you need the transfer.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select the time you want to be picked up.
* **Time Flexibility** (`time_flexibility`, select_one, required): Choose how flexible you are with the timing.
* **Number of Passengers** (`number_of_passengers`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Luggage or Large Items** (`luggage_items`, number, optional): If you have any luggage or large items, please specify the number.
* **Vehicle Preference** (`vehicle_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle you need.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or special needs, provide details here.
* **Do You Need a Return Trip?** (`return_trip`, select_one, required): Decide if you need a one-way or round-trip transfer.
* **Return Trip Date and Time** (`return_date`, text, optional): If applicable, select a return trip date and time.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the transfer.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Add any additional notes for the driver, if necessary.
