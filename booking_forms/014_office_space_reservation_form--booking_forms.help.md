# office_space_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office space reservation form is used to reserve an office space for a specific date and time. This form is intended for authorized personnel or visitors with a legitimate reason for space reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you would like to reserve the space.
2. Select the time you would like to start and end your reservation.
3. Provide your name for identification purposes.
4. Enter your email address so that the space administrator can contact you.
5. Enter your contact number in case of any queries or emergencies.
6. Indicate if you require any additional equipment or tools during your reservation.
7. Specify if you need any specific space type.
8. Choose the capacity of the room you require.
9. Provide a brief description of your purpose for space reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **office_space_reservation**: 
  (text field, note, not required): This is the main field where you need to describe the purpose of your space reservation.
* **date**: 
  (date field, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to reserve the space.
* **time**: 
  (time field, time, not required): Select the time you would like to start and end your reservation.
* **name**: 
  (text field, text, not required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **email**: 
  (email field, email, not required): Enter your email address so that the space administrator can contact you.
* **contact_number**: 
  (text field, text, not required): Enter your contact number in case of any queries or emergencies.
* **space**: 
  (select multiple field, select multiple, not required): Indicate if you require any additional space or equipment during your reservation.
* **space_type**: 
  (select one field, select one, not required): Specify if you need any specific space type (e.g., meeting room, office, etc.).
* **room_capacity**: 
  (number field, number, not required): Choose the capacity of the room you require (e.g., single, double, etc.).
* **description**: 
  (text field, text, not required): Provide a brief description of your purpose for space reservation.
* **assigned_tool**: 
  (select one field, select one, not required): Indicate if you need any specific tools or equipment assigned to you during your reservation.
