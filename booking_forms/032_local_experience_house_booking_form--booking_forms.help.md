# local_experience_house_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to book a local experience house. It captures essential information such as guest details, stay dates, special requests, and booking status.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest details in the "Guest Details" field.
2. Specify your stay dates in the "Stay Dates" field.
3. Enter any special requests or notes about your stay in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Select your booking status from the options provided in the "Booking Status" field.
5. Select the type of booking (House, Room, or other) from the options in the "Booking Type" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Specify the start date of your stay in the "Stay Dates Start" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: A field for you to enter your name and any other personal details.
* **Stay Dates**: A field to specify the dates you plan to stay at the local experience house.
* **Special Requests**: A field to note any special requests or requests you have for your stay.
* **Booking Status**: A field to select whether your booking is new, already booked, or another status.
* **Booking Type**: A field to select the type of booking you are making (House, Room, or other).
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
* **Stay Dates Start**: A field to specify the start date of your stay.
