# Recording Studio Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to reserve time slots in a recording studio. Both the client and the recording studio staff will need to complete this form to ensure accurate and complete information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Recording Studio Reservation Form**: Enter a title for the reservation.
2. **Client Info**: Enter your contact information, including any additional details that are relevant.
3. **Date Time**: Select the date and time for the recording session.
4. **Studio Info**: Enter information about the studio, such as its location and amenities.
5. **Payment Info**: Enter any relevant payment details, such as the payment method and amount.
6. **Musician Preference**: Indicate if any musicians are involved in the recording session.
7. **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by phone or email.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address.
9. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
10. **Additional Details**: Enter any additional details that are relevant to the recording session.
11. **Studio Availability**: Select the studio's availability for the recording session.
12. **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requirements for the recording session.

* 2. **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
