# Bicycle Tune Up Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers to schedule a bike tune-up appointment. It helps the bike shop or repair center to understand the customer's needs and preferences, allowing them to provide an efficient and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide details about your bike, such as its brand, model, and type.
3. Specify any main concerns or issues with your bike.
4. Choose your preferred appointment time and date.
5. Select your desired service level and parts replacement options.
6. Provide any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment confirmation. This will help us keep you updated on your scheduled appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any updates or last-minute notifications.
* **Bicycle Brand**: Enter the manufacturer's name of your bike.
* **Bicycle Model**: Enter the model number or name of your bike.
* **Bicycle Type**: Select the type of bike you have (e.g., road, mountain, hybrid, etc.).
* **Main Concerns or Issues**: Select any main concerns or issues with your bike, such as brake adjustments or gear shifting problems.
* **Detailed Description**: Describe in detail any issues you're experiencing with your bike, such as brake or gear problems.
* **Brake Concern**: If you're experiencing issues with your brakes, select the specific concern you're facing.
* **Gear Concern**: If you're experiencing issues with your gears, select the specific concern you're facing.
* **Service Level Desired**: Choose the level of service you require, such as a basic tune-up or a full overhaul.
* **Parts Replacement**: Let us know if you're open to replacing parts if needed during the service.
* **Budget Range**: Select the budget range you're comfortable with for the service.
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date and time for your appointment.
* **Turnaround Time Needed**: Specify when you need your bike back, such as the same day or within a few days.
* **Last Service**: If you've had your bike serviced before, specify when it was last serviced.
* **Usage Frequency**: Select how often you ride your bike.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests or preferences for your appointment.
* **Bike Lockup Needed**: Indicate if you'll need to wait or pick up your bike later.
* **Contact Preference**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for updates or notifications.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional comments or questions you'd like to add.

## Tips
Remember to review each field carefully and fill it in accordingly to ensure we have the necessary information to provide the best possible service for your bike. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us. We're here to help!
