# prescreen_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prescreen_form is a tool used to collect and document prescreening information from clients or customers. This form gathers critical details about the client, including their first and last names, email address, phone number, reason for prescreening, screening information, date of screening, time of screening, and any additional prescreening comments. The goal is to have a comprehensive record of the prescreening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the prescreen_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with the client's first name.
2. Enter the client's last name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide the client's email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the client's phone number in the Phone field.
5. Describe the reason for the prescreening in the Reason For Screening field.
6. Include any additional screening information in the Screening Information field.
7. Enter the date of the screening in the Date Of Screening field.
8. Enter the time of the screening in the Time Of Screening field.
9. Add any comments or notes about the prescreening in the Prescreening Comments field.
10. Assign the screening to a specific person in the Assigned To field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required)**: Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required)**: Enter the client's last name.
* **Email (Email, text, required)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Reason For Screening (Reason For Screening, note, required)**: Describe the reason for the prescreening.
* **Screening Information (Screening Information, note, required)**: Include any additional screening information.
* **Date Of Screening (Date Of Screening, date, required)**: Enter the date of the screening.
* **Time Of Screening (Time Of Screening, time, required)**: Enter the time of the screening.
* **Prescreening Comments (Prescreening Comments, note, required)**: Add any comments or notes about the prescreening.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To, text, required)**: Assign the screening to a specific person.
