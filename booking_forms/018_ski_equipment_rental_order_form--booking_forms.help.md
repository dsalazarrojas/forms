# ski_equipment_rental_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to book ski equipment rentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment you would like to rent.
2. Choose the size of the equipment you would like to rent.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Choose a pickup date for the equipment.
5. Choose a dropoff date for the equipment.
6. Enter the location where you would like to pick up the equipment.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or instructions for the rental staff.
8. Enter your phone number for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment (Select Multiple)**: Select all types of equipment you would like to rent (e.g. skis, boots, etc.).
* **equipment_size (Select One)**: Choose the size of the equipment you selected above (e.g. small, medium, large).
* **customer_name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
* **customer_email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with rental information and confirmation.
* **pickup_date (Date)**: Choose a date for the equipment to be picked up.
* **dropoff_date (Date)**: Choose a date for the equipment to be dropped off.
* **pickup_location (Text)**: Enter the location where you would like to pick up the equipment.
* **comments (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the rental staff, if applicable.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
