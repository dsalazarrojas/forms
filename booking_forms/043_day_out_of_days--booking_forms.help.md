# Day Out Of Days - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and track days off or absences. It is intended for employees to provide dates when they will be out of the office. This form is not intended for recurring events or appointments, and should only be used for one-time events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the start and end dates for your absence.
2. Enter the start and end dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for both fields.
3. Enter any relevant notes or details for your absence in the "result" field, which will help management understand your reason for being out of the office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date (start_date)** (`date`, required/optional, not currently required): Please enter the date you will be starting your absence. Use the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **End Date (end_date)** (`date`, required/optional, not currently required): Please enter the date you will be ending your absence. Use the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Result (result)** (`number`, required/optional, not currently required): Please enter a code or description to indicate the reason for your absence. For example, "A" for "Annual Leave", "M" for "Medical Leave", etc. You can also enter a brief description for management to understand your reason for being out of the office.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete dates to avoid any scheduling conflicts.
* If you have multiple absences, please complete this form for each absence individually.
* You can also use this form to track multiple absences for different dates, just complete the form for each date separately.
