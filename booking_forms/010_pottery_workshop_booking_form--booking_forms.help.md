# Pottery Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pottery Workshop Booking Form is a digital form designed for booking pottery workshops. It captures essential details such as name, email, phone, and payment information to facilitate smooth workshop organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the designated field.
2.  Fill in your valid email address for easy communication.
3.  Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4.  Select the desired workshop date from the available options.
5.  Choose the preferred workshop time slot.
6.  Indicate the number of students attending the workshop.
7.  Enter the total fee for the workshop.
8.  Select your preferred payment method from the available options.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and initiate the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself with your booking.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for workshop communication and updates.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your contact number for workshop organization and any last-minute updates or cancellations.
* **Workshop Date** (date, required): Select the desired workshop date from the available schedule.
* **Workshop Time** (time, required): Choose the preferred workshop time slot.
* **Number of Students** (number, required): Indicate the number of students attending the workshop.
* **Fee** (number, required): Enter the total fee for the workshop.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select from the available payment methods, including Payment Card, Bank Transfer, or Pay Pal.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to complete and initiate the booking process.
* **Workshop Schedules** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more workshop days for your preferred schedule.
