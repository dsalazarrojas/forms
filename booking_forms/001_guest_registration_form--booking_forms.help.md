# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is a mandatory form that helps us collect essential information about each guest. This form is designed to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
* Enter your Guest Name, Email, Phone, and Address in the respective fields.
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, as you would like it to be displayed on your guest room key card and communication materials.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you. Please ensure it is spelled correctly to avoid any errors in our communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or issues.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the complete address where you will be staying with us. This will help us ensure that you receive your guest room key card and any other communications at the correct location.
