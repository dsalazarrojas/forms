# Nature Conservation Camp Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the nature conservation camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Nature Conservation Camp Booking Form** with your name and email address.
2. Enter your **Participant Details**, including any relevant information about your needs and preferences.
3. Select the **Date** and **End Date** of your camp stay.
4. Choose your **Booking Status** from the options provided.
5. Select your **Participant Status** from the options.
6. Enter your **Participant Name** and **Email**.
7. In the **Participant Details** section, enter any additional information about your participants, such as their name, date of birth, or other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **booking_form**: Enter your name and email address to complete the nature conservation camp booking form.
* **participant_details**: Enter your participant's personal details, such as name, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
* **date**: Enter the date you would like to attend the camp.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your camp stay.
* **booking_status**: Select the status of your booking from the options provided.
* **participant_status**: Select the status of your participant from the options.
* **participant_name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **participant_details**: Enter a list of participant details, such as name, date of birth, or other relevant information.
* **participant_details** (note): Enter a list of participant details, including any additional information about your participants.
