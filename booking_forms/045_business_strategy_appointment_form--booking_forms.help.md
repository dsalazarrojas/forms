# Business Strategy Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions meant to guide the consultant in understanding your business needs, goals, and expectations before your appointment. It is not a formal application or agreement, but rather a tool to help us prepare for our meeting and ensure we address your most pressing concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each question and answer each one accurately and completely.
2. Use the hint text as a guide for what to expect from the question.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially if you have any specific challenges or goals.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to your consultant before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please provide your name or the name of your company.
* **Title or Position** (`client_title`, text, required): This is your role or position within the company.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): This is your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): This is your business contact number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): This is the name of your organization.
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, text, required): This is the industry your company operates in.
* **Type of Strategy Session** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of strategy session you are looking for.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select a date and time that works best for you.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Select a time that works best for you.
* **Session Format** (`session_format`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to meet (in-person, phone call, video conference, or hybrid).
* **Estimated Duration Needed** (`appointment_duration`, select_one, optional): If you know how long the session will take, please select it here.
* **Business Stage** (`company_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the stage of your business.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, number, optional): If you'd like to share the number of employees you have.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, optional): If you'd like to share your approximate annual revenue.
* **Key Discussion Topics** (`key_discussion_topics`, select_multiple, required): Choose the topics you'd like to discuss during the session.
* **Specific Challenges or Goals** (`specific_challenges`, text, required): Share any specific challenges or goals you are facing.
* **Desired Outcomes from Session** (`desired_outcomes`, text, required): Share what you hope to achieve from the session.
* **Materials to Review Before Session** (`materials_to_review`, text, optional): If you have any documents you'd like to share with us before the session.
* **Any Prior Strategy Work** (`prior_strategy_work`, text, optional): If you have any previous strategy work with a consultant, please share it here.
* **Key Stakeholders to Involve** (`key_stakeholders`, text, optional): If there are any other stakeholders that should be involved in the session, please list them here.
