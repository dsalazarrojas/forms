The purpose of this form is to collect booking and payment information for holiday packages. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the booking process.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the booking details, including any relevant information such as dates, package name, and booking number.
2.  Provide payment details, including the package price and any applicable taxes and fees.
3.  Specify the shipping method and details, including the shipping price and any relevant information.
4.  Enter customer details, including contact information and any additional comments.
5.  Select the payment method and confirm the package price, tax rate, and shipping price.
6.  Review the total prices and details before submitting the form.
