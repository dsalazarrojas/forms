# Virtual Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist customers in booking virtual tours with providers. It requires information about the tour, such as its name, provider, start and end dates and times, type, duration, price, contact email and phone number, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Enter the tour name in the "Tour Name" field.
* Select the tour provider from the "Tour Provider" field.
* Enter the start date of the tour in the "Start Date" field.
* Select the start time of the tour from the "Start Time" field.
* Enter the end date of the tour in the "End Date" field.
* Select the end time of the tour from the "End Time" field.
* Choose the tour type from the "Tour Type" field.
* Enter the duration of the tour in the "Duration" field.
* Enter the price of the tour in the "Price" field.
* Enter the contact email of the customer in the "Email" field.
* Enter the contact phone number of the customer in the "Phone" field.
* Add any additional notes about the tour in the "Notes" field.
* If applicable, select the assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Name** (`tour_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the tour you want to book.
* **Tour Provider** (`tour_provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the provider of the tour from the list.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the tour.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the start time of the tour.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the tour.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the end time of the tour.
* **Tour Type** (`tour_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of tour you want to book.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the tour in minutes.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price of the tour.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the tour.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): If applicable, select the assigned tool for the tour from the list.
