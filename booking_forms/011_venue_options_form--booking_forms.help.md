# Venue Options Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect venue-related information for booking purposes. It gathers details about the venue's type, capacity, payment methods, indoor and outdoor spaces, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the venue's name or location in the `venue` field.
2.  Select the type of venue you are looking for from the `venue_type` field.
3.  Optionally, input the venue's capacity or rental fees in the `costs` field.
4.  Choose the payment methods accepted by the venue from the `payment_method` field.
5.  For `indoor` and `outdoor` fields, select the spaces that apply to the venue.
6.  Provide the contact person's name, phone number, email, and address in the respective `contact` fields.
7.  Optionally, add a description of the venue in the `description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **venue** (text, required): Enter the venue's name or location.
*   **venue_type** (select multiple, optional): Choose the type of venue (e.g., event space, conference room, restaurant, etc.).
*   **costs** (number, optional): Input the venue's capacity or rental fees.
*   **payment_method** (select one, optional): Select the payment methods accepted by the venue.
*   **indoor** (select multiple, optional): Choose the indoor spaces available at the venue.
*   **outdoor** (select multiple, optional): Select the outdoor spaces available at the venue.
*   **contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
*   **phone** (text, optional): Input the contact person's phone number.
*   **email** (email, optional): Enter the contact person's email.
*   **description** (text, optional): Add a description of the venue.
*   **contact_person** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
*   **contact_phone** (text, optional): Input the contact person's phone number.
*   **contact_email** (email, optional): Enter the contact person's email.
*   **contact_address** (text, optional): Input the contact person's address.
