# gallery_space_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a gallery space rental application. It is intended to be used by individuals or organizations looking to rent out a gallery space for various purposes such as exhibitions, events, or meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Form Details** section (page 1) with any information you want to provide about your application.
2.  Provide your **Applicant Information** (page 2) including your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
3.  In the **Planning Information** section (page 3), detail your event/exhibition/meeting plans, including dates, times, and any special requirements.
4.  In the **Approval Information** section (page 4), add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Details** (Page 1, Text, Not Required):
    This section is for providing any general information about your application.
*   **Applicant Information** (Page 2, Text, Not Required):
    This section is for providing your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
*   **Planning Information** (Page 3, Text, Not Required):
    This section is for providing details about your event, exhibition, or meeting, including dates, times, and any special requirements.
*   **Approval Information** (Page 4, Text, Not Required):
    This section is for providing any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields (none in this form).
*   Clearly provide all necessary information for your application.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
