# Aviation Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Aviation Appointment Scheduling form is designed to facilitate efficient and accurate appointment booking for clients and customers of the aviation services company. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Full Name**: This field requires your full name, which will be used to identify you and ensure accurate communication.
2. **Provide your Email Address**: Enter your email address, which will be used for booking confirmations and updates.
3. **Enter your Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for any additional communication or questions.
4. **Specify your Pilot License Number (if applicable)**: If you hold a pilot license, please enter the number for reference.
5. **Choose the Requested Service**: Select the primary purpose of your visit, which can be flight training, solo flight, aircraft maintenance, discovery flight, or charter services inquiry.
6. **Select your Preferred Aircraft Model**: If applicable, choose the aircraft model you prefer for your appointment.
7. **Specify your Preferred Date**: Choose a date for your appointment.
8. **Select your Preferred Start Time**: Choose a start time for your appointment.
9. **Specify the Duration of Your Appointment**: Select an approximate time needed for your appointment.
10. **Choose your Preferred Instructor or Mechanic**: If available, select the professional you would like to work with.
11. **Specify your Fueling Requirements**: Select whether you need fueling before or after your appointment.
12. **Add any Additional Comments or Requests**: Provide any extra details or special requests you may have.
13. **Agree to comply with Safety Regulations and School Policies**: Confirm your agreement to comply with all safety regulations and school policies.

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name for accurate communication and identification.

* **Preferred Start Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, **required**): Choose a start time for your appointment.
* **Duration of Appointment** (`appointment_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Select an approximate time needed for your appointment.
* **Instructor or Mechanic Preference** (`personnel_preference`, `text`, **optional**): If available, select the professional you would like to work with.
* **Fueling Requirements** (`fuel_service`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select whether you need fueling before or after your appointment.
