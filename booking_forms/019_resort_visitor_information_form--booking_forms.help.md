# Resort Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about visitors to the resort, including contact information, visit dates and times, and any notable notes or assigned tasks. It will help the staff efficiently manage visitor data and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the visitor's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide the visitor's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the visitor's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the date of visit from the calendar or manually enter it in the "Date of Visit" field.
5. Select the time of visit from the available options or manually enter it in the "Time of Visit" field.
6. Provide any notes or special instructions in the "Note" field.
7. If applicable, assign a tool to the visitor from the available options in the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Leave the "Form ID" field blank unless specifically instructed otherwise.
9. Enter any relevant category information in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the guest's name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Date of Visit** (date, required/optional): Select or enter the date of the visitor's visit.
* **Time of Visit** (time, required/optional): Select or enter the time of the visitor's visit.
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Provide any special instructions or notes about the visitor.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required/optional): Choose a tool from the available options that the visitor is assigned to use during their visit.
* **Form ID** (number, required/optional): Leave this field blank unless otherwise instructed.
* **Category** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant category information for the visitor.
