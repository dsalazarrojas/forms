# event_crew_rest_area_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a rest area for event crew members, allowing them to select their event category, team, and time slot, and provide relevant contact information for booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your event category from the provided options.
2. Choose your event team from the list of available teams.
3. Select one or more time slots that work best for you.
4. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
5. Provide your phone number for further contact.
6. Assign a contact person or department for your booking request.
7. Submit your booking request by clicking the "submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Category** (`event_category`, select_one): Choose the event location from the provided options to ensure that your crew receives the correct rest area booking.
* **Event Team** (`event_team`, select_multiple): Select one or more teams that will be attending the event to ensure that their rest area needs are met.
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, select_multiple): Choose one or more time slots that work best for your crew to book the rest area during that time.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note): Click the "submit" button to send your booking request.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for communication purposes, such as receiving confirmation of your booking or updates about rest area availability.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Provide your phone number for further contact and confirmation of your booking.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text): Assign a contact person or department for your booking request, ensuring that they receive notification about your request.
