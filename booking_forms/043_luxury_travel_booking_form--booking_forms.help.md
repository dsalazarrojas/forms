# Luxury Travel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The luxury travel booking form is designed to collect detailed information from customers about their travel preferences and requirements. This information is essential for booking their luxury travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Carefully select your travel agency from the provided options.
* Choose your destination from the available options.
* Select the type of travel that suits your needs.
* Enter the dates you would like to travel on.
* Specify the duration of your trip in days.
* Enter your budget for the trip.
* Select your preferred payment method.
* Provide your email address for communication.
* Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* If you have any special requests, please specify them.
* Select your payment status.
* Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* Select the booking status of your travel.
* If you have any customer notes, please specify them.
* If you want to add more customer notes, please do so.
* Enter any travel agency notes.
* If you want to add more travel agency notes, please do so.
* Enter the travel agency's phone number.
* Enter the travel agency's email address.
* If you want to add their address, please select the options.
* Select the travel agency you have chosen.
* Confirm that you have received a confirmation email.
* Select the type of travel you have chosen.
* Enter the agency's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the travel agency?** (travel_agency, select_one, required)
    The travel agency is the company that will handle your travel booking. Select the correct option that applies to you.
- **Destination** (destination, select_one, required)
    Choose the destination you would like to travel to.
- **Type of travel** (travel_type, select_one, required)
    Select the type of travel that suits your needs (e.g., honeymoon, business, leisure).
- **When do you want to travel?** (travel_dates, date, required)
    Enter the dates you would like to travel on (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **How long do you want to travel?** (travel_duration, number, required)
    Specify the duration of your trip in days.
- **Budget** (budget, number, required)
    Enter your budget for the trip.
- **How would you like to pay?** (payment_method, select_one, required)
    Select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
- **Email** (email, email, required)
    Enter your email address for communication.
- **Phone number** (phone_number, text, required)
    Enter your phone number for easy contact.
- **Special requests** (special_requests, select_multiple, optional)
    If you have any special requests, please specify them.
- **Payment status** (payment_status, select_one, required)
    Select your payment status.
- **Confirmation** (confirmation, select_one, required)
    Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
- **Booking status** (booking_status, select_one, required)
    Select the booking status of your travel.
- **Customer notes** (customer_notes, select_multiple, optional)
    If you have any customer notes, please specify them.
- **More customer notes** (customer_notes, note, optional)
    If you want to add more customer notes, please do so.
- **Travel agency notes** (travel_agency_notes, note, optional)
    Enter any travel agency notes.
- **More travel agency notes** (travel_agency_notes, note, optional)
    If you want to add more travel agency notes, please do so.
- **Travel agency phone number** (travel_agency_phone_number, text, optional)
    Enter the travel agency's phone number.
- **Travel agency email** (travel_agency_email, email, optional)
    Enter the travel agency's email address.
- **Travel agency address** (travel_agency_address, select_multiple, optional)
    If you want to add the travel agency's address, please select the options.
- **Travel agency** (travel_agency, select_one, optional)
    Select the travel agency you have chosen.
- **Confirmation email** (confirmation_email, select_one, optional)
    Confirm that you have received a confirmation email.
- **Type of travel** (travel_type, select_one, optional)
    Select the type of travel you have chosen.
- **Agency Phone** (travel_agency_phone_number, text, optional)
    Enter the agency's phone number.
