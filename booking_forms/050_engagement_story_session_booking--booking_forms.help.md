# Engagement Story Session Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Engagement Story Session Booking" form is used to book sessions with a photographer for clients. This form collects information such as session details, schedule, time, photographer, client, and session location.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on "Submit" after filling out all the required fields, which are marked with a *.
2. If you are unable to book a session date or time that fits your schedule, you can try selecting multiple dates and times to find a suitable alternative.
3. You can also add a message to the photographer if you have any special requests or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your session.
* **Schedule Session** (`session_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Select the dates for your session from the dropdown menu. You can select multiple dates if they are available.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Choose the time for your session from the available options.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the photographer that will be attending your session.
* **Client** (`client`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with the booking details.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add a personal message to the photographer if you have any specific requirements or requests.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you with further instructions or information.
* **Booking Notes** (`booking_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the photographer about your session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location for your session from the available options. You can choose multiple locations if they are suitable for your needs.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and understood the session details.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields (marked with a * in the field-by-field explanation) are filled in order to complete the form.
