# Book Club Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a spot in a book club meeting and provide information about your preferences and needs for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
*   Select the meeting you wish to reserve from the "Meeting to Reserve" dropdown menu.
*   Fill out your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
*   If you have guests attending with you, list their names in the "Guest Names" field.
*   Select the meeting location from the "Meeting Location" dropdown menu.
*   Indicate your reading progress on the current book being discussed.
*   If you have any dietary restrictions, select them from the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
*   If you need transportation assistance, select "Yes I need a ride" from the "Transportation Needed" field.
*   Indicate your preference for reminders, such as email or text message.
*   Finally, select the date of the reservation and acknowledge the cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (member\_name, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Email Address** (email\_address, email, required): We will send confirmation emails here.
*   **Phone Number** (phone\_number, text, optional): For reservation reminders.
*   **Membership ID** (membership\_id, text, optional): Your book club member ID.
*   **Meeting to Reserve** (meeting\_to_reserve, select\_one, required): Select the meeting you want to attend.
*   **Meeting Location** (meeting\_location, select\_one, required): Where the meeting will be held.
*   **Number of Guests** (number\_of\_guests, number, required): How many guests will accompany you.
*   **Guest Names** (guest\_names, text, optional): List names of guests if applicable.
*   **RSVP Status** (rsvp\_status, select\_one, required): Indicate if you will be attending.
*   **Current Book Title** (book\_title, text, required): Title of the book being discussed.
*   **Reading Progress** (reading\_progress, select\_one, required): Indicate how much of the book you have read.
*   **Will You Bring Snacks or Drinks** (bringing\_snacks, select\_one, optional): For potluck style meetings.
*   **What Will You Bring** (snack\_details, text, optional): Describe what you plan to bring.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary\_restrictions, select\_multiple, optional): Any dietary restrictions we should know.
*   **Topics You Want to Discuss** (discussion\_topic\_request, text, optional): Any specific topics or questions.
*   **Do You Need Transportation Assistance** (transportation\_needed, select\_one, optional): Select if you need a ride.
*   **Special Requests or Accommodations** (special\_requests, text, optional): Any special needs or requests.
*   **How Would You Like Reminders** (reminder\_preference, select\_one, required): Select your reminder preference.
*   **Reservation Date** (reservation\_date, date, required): Date of this reservation.
*   **I Understand the Cancellation Policy** (cancellation\_policy\_ack, select\_one, required): By selecting yes you acknowledge the policy.
