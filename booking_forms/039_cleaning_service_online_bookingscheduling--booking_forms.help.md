# Cleaning Service Online Booking Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for online booking and scheduling of cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section (fields 2-5) with your name, email address, phone number, and any other contact details.
2. Select the type of service you require (field 7) and approximate square footage of the property (field 8).
3. If applicable, select the number of bedrooms and bathrooms (fields 9-10).
4. Enter your preferred date and time for the service (fields 17-18).
5. Select any additional services you require (field 15).
6. Provide access instructions and parking information (fields 26-27).
7. Select your preferred payment method (field 31).
8. Review and agree to the terms and conditions (field 36).
9. Sign the form with your full name (field 37).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (field 2)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (field 3)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (field 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (field 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type (field 7)**: Select the type of service you require (e.g., residential or commercial).
* **Approximate Square Footage (field 8)**: Enter the approximate square footage of the property.
* **Bedrooms (field 9)**: Select the number of bedrooms if applicable.
* **Bathrooms (field 10)**: Select the number of bathrooms if applicable.
* **Full Service Address (field 11)**: Enter the full service address.
* **Cleaning Service Type (field 13)**: Select the type of cleaning service you require (e.g., standard, deep, or move-in/move-out).
* **Service Frequency (field 14)**: Select how often you require the service (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or one-time).
* **Additional Services (field 15)**: Select any additional services you require (e.g., interior windows, oven cleaning).
* **Preferred Date (field 17)**: Enter your preferred date for the service.
* **Preferred Time of Day (field 18)**: Select the preferred time of day for the service.
* **Are You Flexible with the Date (field 19)**: Indicate if you are flexible with the date or not.
* **Pets in Home (field 21)**: Indicate if you have any pets in the home.
* **Pet Instructions (field 22)**: Enter any special instructions about pets.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities (field 23)**: Enter any allergies or sensitivities in your household.
* **Cleaning Products Preference (field 24)**: Select the type of cleaning products you prefer.
* **How Will We Access Your Home (field 26)**: Select how you would like our team to access your home.
* **Access Details (field 27)**: Enter any specific access instructions.
* **Is Parking Available (field 28)**: Indicate if parking is available at your property.
* **Parking Details (field 29)**: Enter any parking instructions.
* **Payment Method (field 31)**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Promo Code (field 32)**: Enter any promo code you have.
* **Terms and Conditions (field 36)**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Customer Signature (field 37)**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Booking Date (field 38)**: This is automatically generated and not editable.

Note: This help guide is for reference only. The actual fields and their labels may vary depending on the specific implementation of the form.
