# wedding_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests about their accommodation requests for the wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name as they would like it to appear on the accommodation documents.
2. Enter the guest's email address so that we can contact them with accommodation information.
3. Enter the guest's phone number, if they have one.
4. Describe the type of accommodation the guest is requesting (e.g. Hotel, Airbnb, Hostel).
5. Enter the date and time the guest would like to arrive.
6. Enter the date the guest would like to depart.
7. Enter any additional information the guest would like to share with us.
8. If the guest has any special requests or needs, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's name as they would like it to appear on the accommodation documents. This will be used to address them personally.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the guest's email address so that we can contact them with accommodation information. This is the best way for us to reach them with any updates or changes.
* **Guest Phone**: This is an optional field for the guest to enter their phone number. If they do not have a phone, they can leave this field blank.
* **Accommodation Request**: Describe the type of accommodation the guest is requesting (e.g. Hotel, Airbnb, Hostel).
* **Accommodation Address**: Enter the address of the accommodation the guest has chosen.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date the guest would like to arrive. This is the date when they will start their stay.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time the guest would like to arrive. This will help us with check-in procedures.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date the guest would like to depart. This is the date when they will end their stay.
* **Special Request**: If the guest has any special requests or needs, enter them here. This could include dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or any other requirements.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information the guest would like to share with us. This is a free text field where they can provide more details about their stay.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us and we will be happy to help.
* We will do our best to accommodate the guest's requests, but some requests may not be possible due to logistical or financial constraints.
* We will keep the guest updated on any changes or updates to their accommodation arrangement.
* If the guest has any special requests or needs, please let us know as soon as possible so that we can arrange for it to be met.
