# Taxi Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers about their taxi service order, including pickup and drop-off locations, contact details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Name** field with your full name.
2. Enter the date of your pickup in the **Pick-up Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select a pickup time from the available options in the **Pickup Time** field.
4. Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up in the **Pickup Location** field.
5. Enter the address or location where you would like to be dropped off in the **Drop-Off Location** field.
6. Provide your **Phone Number** and **Email** for contact purposes.
7. If you have any special instructions or requests, type them in the **Special Instructions** field.
8. Choose your preferred **Taxi Type** from the options provided (Economy, Luxury, Sedan, or Truck).
9. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
- **Pick-up Date**: Enter the date of your pickup in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Pickup Time**: Select a time for your pickup from the available options.
- **Pickup Location**: Enter the address or location where you would like to be picked up.
- **Drop-Off Location**: Enter the address or location where you would like to be dropped off.
- **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions, type them here.
- **Taxi Type**: Choose the type of taxi that best suits your needs from the available options.
- **Payment Method**: Select the method by which you would like to pay for your taxi service.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth pickup and drop-off.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support for assistance.
