# Vacation Rental Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect vacation rental properties and collect data on their status, condition, and maintenance needs. It helps in documenting and tracking the progress of the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the property's status from the available options.
2. Choose the property's condition from the available options.
3. Indicate the level of maintenance needed for the property.
4. Add any comments or notes about the property's condition.
5. Enter the contact number of the property owner or manager.
6. Enter the email address of the property owner or manager.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
8. Select the AM/PM of the inspection time.
9. Enter your name as the inspector.
10. Select any notes or comments for the property.
11. Choose whether to upload photos or videos of the property.
12. Select the status of the inspection report.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes about the property.
14. Select the status of the inspection.
15. Choose the result of the inspection (Pass, Fail, or N/A).
16. Select any actions needed for the property.
17. Choose any additional comments or notes for the property.
18. Enter any comments or notes about the property.
19. Select whether the inspection report is attached or not.
20. Enter any additional comments or notes about the assigned tool.
21. Enter your name and the date and time when the form was completed.
22. Select the status of the inspection report.
