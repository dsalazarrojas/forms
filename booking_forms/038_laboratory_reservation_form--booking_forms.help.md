# Laboratory Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book laboratory time slots for experiments or activities. It is a non-mandatory step for users to provide more information, and they can choose to complete it at their discretion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more time slots that you would like to book.
2. Provide the intended use of your reservation.
3. Choose a scheduled date and time for your reservation.
4. (Optional) Specify the start and end dates of your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Time Slots** (Yes/No, Required: No): 
  Select one or more time slots that you would like to book.
* **Intended Use** (Text, Required: No): Provide a brief description of the purpose of your reservation.
* **Supporting Information** (Note, Required: No): Enter any additional information that might be helpful for laboratory staff to know about your reservation.
* **Scheduled Date** (Date, Required: No): Choose a date for your reservation.
* **Scheduled Time** (Time, Required: No): Choose a time for your reservation.
* **Requested Time** (Time, Required: No): If you have a specific time in mind that you would like to book, enter it here.
* **Reservation Start** (Date, Required: No): Choose the start date of your reservation.
* **Reservation End** (Date, Required: No): Choose the end date of your reservation.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is not a mandatory part of the lab's processes, so feel free to complete it if you want to provide more information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to laboratory staff for assistance.
* Remember to review your reservation details carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
