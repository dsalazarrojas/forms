# Student Housing Allocation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a student accommodation or room in a student housing facility. It collects personal details, accommodation preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Surname** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
3. Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** as per the format required.
4. Select your preferred **Accommodation Type** from the options provided (Shared Accommodation, Single Room, Double Room).
5. Enter your **Student ID** as a numeric value.
6. Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date** for your booking.
7. Enter the **Room Number** of the accommodation you are booking.
8. Provide the **Building Name** of the accommodation.
9. Add any additional **Room Details** you would like to provide.
10. Select the **Accommodation Manager** responsible for your booking from the options provided.
11. Select the **Accommodation Type Manager** from the options provided.
12. If necessary, add any additional **Comments** about your booking.
13. Confirm that you have signed the **Agreement** by selecting 'Yes' from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Surname**: Enter your surname.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format [email address].
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select your preferred accommodation type from the options provided.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID as a numeric value.
* **Start Date**: Choose the start date for your booking.
* **End Date**: Choose the end date for your booking.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number you are booking.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building you are booking.
* **Room Details**: Add any additional details about the room.
* **Accommodation Manager**: Select the person responsible for your accommodation booking.
* **Accommodation Type Manager**: Select the person who is in charge of accommodation type.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments about your booking, add them here.
* **Agreement Signed**: Confirm that you have signed the agreement by selecting 'Yes' from the options provided.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for dates and phone numbers.
* Select the correct accommodation type and accommodation manager.
* Review your booking details before submitting the form.
