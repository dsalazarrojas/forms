# Tent Lighting Installation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners book a tent lighting installation for their event. It will help us understand the event requirements to provide the right lighting solution for a successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Event Planner" field and enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the event.
2. Click on the "Event Date" field and select the date of the event.
3. Click on the "Event Start Time" field and select the start time of the event.
4. Click on the "Event End Time" field and select the end time of the event.
5. Click on the "Event Duration" field and enter the duration of the event.
6. Click on the "Event Location" field and enter the location of the event.
7. Click on the "Lighting System Requirements" field and describe the lighting system requirements for the event.
8. Click on the "Power Distribution" field and describe the power distribution requirements for the event.
9. Click on the "Special Requests" field and enter any special requests or additional requirements you have for the lighting installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planner**: Please enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the event.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Select the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Select the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Lighting System Requirements**: Describe the lighting system requirements for the event, including the number and type of lights needed.
* **Power Distribution**: Describe the power distribution requirements for the event, including the number of power outlets and their locations.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or additional requirements you have for the lighting installation.
