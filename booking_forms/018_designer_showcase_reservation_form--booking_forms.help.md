# Designer Showcase Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer Showcase Reservation Form is designed to allow attendees to reserve a time slot for a showcase during the Designer Showcase event. This form helps event organizers track attendee reservations and assign tools to each attendee accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Form Name: Enter a name for your reservation.
	* Showcase Date: Choose the date of the showcase.
	* Showcase Time: Choose the time slot for the showcase.
	* Assigned Tool: Select the tool assigned to you.
	* Form Status: Select the status of your form submission.
	* Submitted By: Choose the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
2. Optionally, you can provide:
	* Attendee Email: Enter your email address.
	* Attendee Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Attendee Note: Add any additional notes about your showcase reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required): Enter a name for your reservation.
* **Showcase Date** (`showcase_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the showcase.
* **Showcase Time** (`showcase_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time slot for the showcase.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Attendee Note** (`attendee_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your showcase reservation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your form submission.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your showcase reservation.
