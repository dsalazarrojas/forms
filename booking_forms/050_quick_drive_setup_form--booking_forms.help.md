# Quick Drive Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Drive Setup Form is designed to gather information required for scheduling a quick drive. This form asks for details such as pickup and drop off addresses, schedule frequency, vehicle type, and contact information, among others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that this form is not already loaded in your browser's local storage to avoid duplication.
2. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drive Request** (`1`, `note`, required: false): Briefly describe your drive request in the space provided.
* **Driver Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the driver you wish to schedule with.
* **Pickup Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for pickup of the items/packages.
* **Drop Off Date** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for drop off of the items/packages.
* **Schedule Time** (`5`, `time`, required: false): Enter the desired schedule time for the drive.
* **Pickup Address** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the items/packages will be picked up from.
* **Drop Off Address** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the items/packages will be dropped off.
* **Schedule Frequency** (`8`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the frequency of the drive schedule from Monday to Sunday.
* **Vehicle Type** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the type of vehicle required for the drive, such as Car, Truck, or Van.
* **Drive Details** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or details about the drive.
* **Contact Number** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the person scheduling the drive.
* **EMail** (`12`, `email`, required: false): Enter the EMail of the person scheduling the drive.
* **Notes** (`13`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the drive.
