# home_exchange_program_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for hosts and guests to provide essential information for a home exchange program booking. It gathers relevant details such as host and guest details, stay duration, and dates for a seamless experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1.  Enter the requested details for both the host and the guest in the `host_details` and `guest_details` fields, respectively.
2.  Provide the desired stay duration in the `stay_duration` field.
3.  Enter the preferred booking date in the `booking_date` field.
4.  Enter the date of the guest's arrival in the `guest_arrival_date` field.
5.  Enter the date of the guest's departure in the `guest_departure_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Host Details (`host_details`, Text, **Required**: No): This field is used to enter the host's name and any other relevant details.
*  **Guest Details (`guest_details`, Text, **Required**: No): This field is used to enter the guest's name and any other relevant details.
*  **Stay Duration (`stay_duration`, Text, **Required**: No): Enter the number of days the guest plans to stay at the host's home.
*  **Booking Date (`booking_date`, Date, **Required**: No): Select the desired date for the booking.
*  **Guest Arrival Date (`guest_arrival_date`, Date, **Required**: No): Enter the date the guest plans to arrive.
*  **Guest Departure Date (`guest_departure_date`, Date, **Required**: No): Enter the date the guest plans to leave.

## Tips
*  Double-check the entered details for accuracy to avoid any confusion or errors.
*  Make sure to communicate with the host and guest through the provided contact information.
*  Review the booking terms and conditions before completing the form to ensure a clear understanding of the exchange's expectations.
