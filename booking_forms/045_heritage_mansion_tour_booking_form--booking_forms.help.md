# Heritage Mansion Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heritage Mansion Tour Booking Form is a tool designed for visitors to provide their details and preferences for a guided tour of the heritage mansion. It collects essential information to ensure a seamless experience for both the visitors and the tour guides. This form will help you plan your tour effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Visitor Details** (page 1)
    * Enter your name and any other relevant details in the text field provided.
    * Ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid any issues with your booking.
2.  Enter your **Email** (page 2)
    * Type your email address accurately to receive any updates or notifications regarding your tour.
3.  Fill in your **Phone** (page 3)
    * Enter your phone number correctly to be contacted in case of any updates.
4.  Choose your **Tour Date** (page 4)
    * Select the date you wish to take the tour on.
5.  Enter the number of **Guest Count** (page 5)
    * Provide the exact number of people you will be bringing along for the tour.
6.  Choose your **Preferred Language** (page 6)
    * Select "Yes" if you prefer to be guided in your native language, or "No" if you don't have any language preferences.
7.  Choose your **Preferred Time** (page 7)
    * Select the time you would like the tour to take place at.
8.  If you have any **Special Requests** (page 8)
    * Use this text field to enter any special requests or requirements you might have.
9.  Choose your **Preferred Time Slot** (page 9)
    * Select "Yes" for any time slots you are available, or "No" for slots you are not available for the tour.
