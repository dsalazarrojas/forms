# Daycare Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to book services at a daycare facility. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth and personalized care experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your personal information: first name, email, and phone number.
2.  Select a booking date for your child's visit.
3.  Choose a pickup time that suits you best.
4.  Select the type of service you require (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
5.  If applicable, choose additional services you'd like to book.
6.  Add any relevant care notes for your child's caregivers.
7.  Provide detailed scheduling information.
8.  If needed, leave any comments or concerns in the comments section.
9.  Finally, review and submit your booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your child's name. (optional)
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached. (optional)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your booking. (optional)
*   **Booking Date**: Select a date for your child's visit. This is the date you'd like to book the service.
*   **Pickup Time**: Choose a time for your child to be picked up.
*   **Care Notes**: Add any important information about your child's needs or allergies. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Select Service**: Choose the type of service you require (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
*   **Select Services**: If you require multiple services, select all that apply. (optional)
*   **Parent Details**: Add any relevant information about yourself and your child. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Scheduling**: Provide detailed scheduling information for your child's visit. Please ensure this is accurate to avoid any confusion.
*   **Comments**: Leave any comments or concerns you have. This is an opportunity to share any additional information about your booking.
*   **Submit**: Once you've reviewed and confirmed your details, click to submit your booking.
*   **Cancel**: If you change your mind or need to cancel your booking, click this button.

Note that some fields are optional but providing them will help the daycare facility to better serve you and your child.
