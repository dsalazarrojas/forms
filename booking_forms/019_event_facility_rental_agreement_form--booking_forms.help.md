# Event Facility Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Facility Rental Agreement Form is a document used to gather information from clients about their event, including the facility rented, event details, and contact information. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you understand what to expect from the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the necessary details about your event. Make sure to answer every required field (marked with an asterisk \*).
2. For the "Agree" field, select "Yes" if you have read, understood, and agreed to the rental terms and conditions. If you are unsure or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Facility**: Describe the facility you have rented for your event (e.g., conference room, outdoor space, etc.).
* **Agree**: Confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to the rental terms and conditions. This is a critical field, as it ensures you are aware of your responsibilities and commitments.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event (e.g., 2023-02-20).
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of your event (e.g., 05:00 PM).
* **Event Duration**: Indicate the duration of your event in hours (e.g., 8 hours).
* **Event Capacity**: Estimate the number of people attending your event (e.g., 50).
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of your event (e.g., meeting, party, etc.).
* **Event Notes**: Include any additional notes or comments about your event.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person (e.g., Event Manager).
* **Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Event Facility Owners**: If you are unsure or need clarification about any of the above fields, please contact us at [insert contact information].
