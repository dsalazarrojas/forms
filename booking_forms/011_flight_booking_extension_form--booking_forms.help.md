# Flight Booking Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Flight Booking Extension Form is a comprehensive form designed for booking flights, capturing customer details, payment information, and other relevant data. This form is intended to streamline the booking process, allowing users to provide necessary information in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the appropriate page (1-5) from the provided options.
2. On each page, carefully review the instructions and available fields.
3. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
4. Ensure that you've completed all the fields, as some may be conditional or dependent on previous entries.
