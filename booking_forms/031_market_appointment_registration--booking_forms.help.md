# Market Appointment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Appointment Registration form is used to register vendors for a market appointment, gathering essential details about the vendor, their contact information, and the person submitting the registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Market Appointment Registration" page.
2. Enter the vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
3. Provide the vendor's email address in the "Vendor Email" field.
4. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Market Appointment Registration** (`market_appointment_registration`, text, required): This is the title of the form that describes the purpose and type of registration.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor you're registering for the market appointment.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the vendor. This will be used for communication and contact purposes.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the registration or the contact person for the vendor.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vendor.

## Tips
* Double-check the vendor's email address for accuracy to ensure you can contact them successfully.
* Make sure to provide the correct contact person's name and phone number to avoid any confusion.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the event coordinator for assistance.
