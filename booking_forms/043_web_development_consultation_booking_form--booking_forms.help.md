# web_development_consultation_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to book a web development consultation with our team. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to ensure a successful consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name, contact details, and preferences.
2. Select the services you require from the list provided.
3. Choose the tool assigned to your consultation.
4. Add any additional notes if needed.
5. Click "Submit" to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Select your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for the consultation.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_one`, required): Choose the services you require from the list provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your consultation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Choose the tool assigned to your consultation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click to confirm your booking.
 
## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth consultation process.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
