# Hotel Stay Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Stay Extension Form is used to collect information from guests about their stay extension requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "hotel_stay_extension" page.
2. Enter your check-out date and time (if different from the originally scheduled check-out date).
3. Provide the reason for the extension (required).
4. Enter your contact number (required).
5. If applicable, enter any comments or information about your stay.
6. Select the staff member you would like to be assigned to handle your stay extension (required).
7. If you are not assigning your stay to someone, enter their name in the "assigned_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hotel_stay_extension** (hotel_stay_extension_form, text, required): Enter a brief description of your stay extension request.
* **check_out_date** (check_out_date, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to check out.
* **check_out_time** (check_out_time, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to check out.
* **reason_for_extension** (reason_for_extension, text, required): Provide the reason for your stay extension request.
* **contact_number** (contact_number, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your stay.
* **comments_hint** (comments_hint, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or information about your stay.
* **assigned_to** (assigned_to, select_one, required): Select the staff member you would like to be assigned to handle your stay extension.
* **assigned_to_hint** (assigned_to_hint, text, optional): If you are not assigning your stay to someone, enter their name in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your stay extension request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask hotel staff for assistance.
