# moving-service-pre-screening-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from clients about their moving needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the client's contact details: 
   - **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
   - **Email**: Enter the client's email address that the moving company can use for communication.
   - **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for easy contact.
2. Move Details:
   - **Move In Date**: Select the date you plan to move into your new place.
   - **Move Out Date**: Select the date you plan to move out of your current place.
   - **Move In Time**: Select the time you plan to move into your new place. 
   - **Move Out Time**: Select the time you plan to move out of your current place.
3. Address Information:
   - **Address**: Enter the address you are moving from and to.
   - **Move In Time Est**: If you have an estimated move-in time, please enter it.
   - **Move Out Time Est**: If you have an estimated move-out time, please enter it.
   - **Move Duration**: Enter the duration of your move in hours.
4. Additional Details:
   - **Move Type**: Select the type of move you are doing (Apartment, House, Other).
   - **Move Distance**: If this is a move within your city or state, you can enter an estimated distance in miles.
   - **Move Weight**: If you have an estimated weight of your move, please enter it.
   - **Move Weight Est**: If you have an estimated weight, enter it.
   - **Move Duration Est**: If you have an estimated move duration, enter it.
   - **Move Distance Est**: If you have an estimated distance, enter it.
   - **Move Weight Unit**: Select the unit of your move weight (pounds, ounces, kilograms, grams).
   - **Notes**: Add any additional information or special requests.
   - **Questions**: If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them.
   - **Comments**: If you have any comments or feedback about your move, please enter them.
   - **References**: If you have any references or previous moves with our company, please enter them.
   - **Preferred Date**: If you have a preferred date for your move, please enter it.
   - **Estimated Move Date**: If you have an estimated move date, enter it.
   - **Reason for Move**: Enter the reason for your move.
