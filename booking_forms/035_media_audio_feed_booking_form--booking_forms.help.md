# media_audio_feed_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect booking details for media and audio feed events, including event specifics, schedules, requested audio details, dates and times, and production team information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the event, including title, schedule, and any relevant details.
2. Enter the requested audio details, such as audio types and requirements.
3. Select the dates and times for the event, ensuring that they are accurate and correct.
4. List the production teams involved in the event, including their names and contact information.
5. Add any additional notes about the event, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **event_details** (text): The event title, description, or any other relevant information about the event.
### 2. **event_schedule** (text): The event schedule, including start and end times, and any other relevant details.
### 3. **audio_requests** (text): The audio requirements or specifications for the event, such as audio types or formats.
### 4. **dates_and_times** (text): The dates and times for the event, ensuring that they are accurate and correct.
### 5. **production_teams** (text): The names and contact information of the production teams involved in the event.
### 6. **event_details_note** (note): Any additional notes or comments about the event, if necessary.
### 7. **event_details_date** (date): The date for the event, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided, especially for dates and times.
- Ensure that the event details and production team information are up-to-date and correct.
- Use the additional note field to add any further information or comments about the event.
