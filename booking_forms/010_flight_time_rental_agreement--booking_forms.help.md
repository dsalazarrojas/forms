# Flight Time Rental Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the booking process for flight time rentals. It helps us understand your needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. Enter your contact number and email address so we can reach out to you in case of any last-minute updates or questions.
3. Select the date when you want to rent the flight time, and the date when it will end.
4. Choose the type of aircraft you'd like to rent (Cessna 152, Cessna 172, or specify your own type).
5. Indicate the total number of hours you want to rent the flight.
6. Read and confirm the terms and conditions.
7. Confirm whether you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: <name> (`Customer Name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Customer Contact**: <contact_number> (`Customer Contact`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Email**: <email> (`Customer Email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and confirmations.
* **Start Date**: <start_date> (`Start Date`, date, **required**): Select the date when you want to rent the flight time.
* **End Date**: <end_date> (`End Date`, date, **required**): Select the date when the rented flight time will end.
* **Aircraft Type**: <aircraft_type> (`Aircraft Type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of aircraft you'd like to rent (Cessna 152, Cessna 172, or specify your own type).
* **Flight Duration**: <flight_duration> (`Flight Duration`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of hours you want to rent the flight time.
* **Terms and Conditions**: <terms> (`Terms and Conditions`, note, **required**): Read and understand the terms and conditions before proceeding.
* **Customer Acknowledgement**: <customer_terms> (`Customer Acknowledgement`, select_one, **required**): Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Submit**: <submit> (`Submit`, text, **required**): Click the submit button to complete the form.
