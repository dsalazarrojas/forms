# Health Retreat Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for clients who wish to rent a space for a health retreat. To ensure accurate and successful submissions, please read this guide carefully before filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form). If a field is marked as "Optional", you can fill it in or leave it blank, depending on your needs.
2. Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., "Special Requests" and "Payment Method") to choose the most suitable options for your group's needs.
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Details** (`group_details`, text, Optional): Please provide a brief description of your group, including its name, purpose, or any specific details that may be relevant to your retreat.
* **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date(s) you wish to rent the space for.
* **Rental Time** (`rental_time`, time, Optional): Select the time slot you prefer for your rental.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, Optional): Specify the number of people in your group.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Provide a contact number for your group, in case we need to get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for your group.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, select_one, Optional): Select any special requests your group may have, such as:
	+ Special Accommodations
	+ Special Meals
	+ Special Activities
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose how you plan to pay for your rental:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Cash
