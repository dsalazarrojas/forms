# sustainable_travel_cottage_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users book a stay at a sustainable travel cottage. The form collects essential information to facilitate a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Complete each field according to the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
    * This is the main booking information field. You can enter a brief description of your stay, including any special requests or requirements.
* **Guest Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
    * Provide information about yourself, such as your name and any other guests traveling with you.
* **Dates** (`3`, `date`, required: false):
    * Enter the dates of your stay, in the format of `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Stay Length** (`4`, `number`, required: false):
    * Enter the number of nights you plan to stay at the sustainable travel cottage.
* **Arrival Method** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false):
    * Choose how you would like to arrive at the cottage, such as 'Yes' for a guided tour or 'No' for self-guided arrival.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    * Select any accommodation preferences you have, such as 'Yes' for a private room or 'No' for a shared room.
* **Payment Method** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false):
    * Choose your preferred payment method, such as 'Yes' for credit card or 'No' for bank transfer.
* **Comments** (`8`, `note`, required: false):
    * Add any additional comments or notes about your stay, such as special requests or dietary restrictions.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false):
    * Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
    * Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
