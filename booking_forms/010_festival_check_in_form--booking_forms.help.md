# festival_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival_check_in_form is designed to collect essential information from festival attendees, enabling efficient event tracking and communication. This includes gathering their name, contact details, and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** in the designated text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address accurately, as it will be used for communication purposes.
4. Type your **Phone Number** to enable quick contact in case of emergencies or updates.
5. Mention the **Event** name or code you are attending for reference.
6. Enter your **Ticket Number**, if applicable, for access to event services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Please enter your first name to help us confirm your identity.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name to enable accurate attendance tracking.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Provide a valid email address to receive important event updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Type your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of event changes or emergencies.
* **Event** (Event, text, required: false): Mention the event name or code you are attending for reference.
* **Ticket Number** (Ticket Number, text, required: false): Enter your ticket number if you have one for easy access to event services.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure efficient event tracking.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the festival team for assistance.
- Review your information before submission to avoid errors.
