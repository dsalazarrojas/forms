# pet_boarding_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet_boarding_check_in_form is a critical component of our pet boarding process, designed to gather essential information from pet owners when checking in their pets for boarding services. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently, facilitating a smooth and safe boarding experience for both pets and their owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required and optional information ready.
2. Start by filling out the **pet_boarding_check_in_form** page, which serves as a general overview of the pet's information.
3. Move on to the **date** page, where you will provide the pet's check-in date.
4. Proceed to the **pet_type** page, where you will select the pet's type (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
5. Continue to the **pet_name**, **pet_age**, **phone**, **email**, **pet_weight**, **pet_breed**, **pet_color**, **pet_diet**, **pet_medication**, **pet_allergies**, and **pet_special_instructions** pages, where you will provide the relevant information about the pet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **pet_boarding_check_in_form**:
	+ (Page 1): This is the first page of the form, which serves as a general overview of the pet's information.
* **date**: 
	+ (Page 2): Enter the date the pet will be checked in for boarding services.
* **pet_type**:
	+ (Page 3): Select the type of pet you are checking in (e.g., dog, cat, etc.).
* **pet_name**:
	+ (Page 4): Enter the pet's name.
* **pet_age**:
	+ (Page 5): Enter the pet's age.
* **phone**:
	+ (Page 6): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **email**:
	+ (Page 7): Enter the owner's email address.
* **pet_weight**:
	+ (Page 8): Enter the pet's weight (in pounds or kilograms).
* **pet_breed**:
	+ (Page 9): Enter the pet's breed (e.g., Labrador, Persian, etc.).
* **pet_color**:
	+ (Page 10): Enter the pet's color.
* **pet_diet**:
	+ (Page 11): Select whether the pet has any special diet requirements.
* **pet_medication**:
	+ (Page 12): Enter any medication the pet is currently taking.
* **pet_allergies**:
	+ (Page 13): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the pet has.
* **pet_special_instructions**:
	+ (Page 14): Enter any special instructions or requests for the pet's care.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
* The information provided in this form will be used to tailor the boarding experience to your pet's specific needs.
