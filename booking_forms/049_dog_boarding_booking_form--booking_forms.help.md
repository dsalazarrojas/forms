# Dog Boarding Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to book dog boarding services. Please fill out the form to provide your contact and dog information, select the desired boarding dates and services, and provide payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your dog's information, such as its name and breed.
3. Select the desired boarding day and time for your dog.
4. Provide your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Card, or PayPal).
5. If applicable, select any special services required for your dog (e.g., walking, feeding, bathing, or training).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your dog's boarding needs.
7. Review your submission and make any necessary adjustments before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (text, required): Please enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Dog Info** (text, required): Provide details about your dog, such as its name and breed.
* **Prophets** (select_one, required): Select the desired boarding day. Choose from:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
* **Start Date** (date, required): Enter the date you'd like to drop off your dog.
* **End Date** (date, required): Enter the date you'd like to pick up your dog.
* **Contact Info** (text, required): Enter your phone number and any other relevant contact details.
* **Owner Info** (text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant owner information.
* **Payment Info** (text, required): Enter your payment method and details.
* **Special Services** (select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services required for your dog, such as:
	+ Walking
	+ Feeding
	+ Bathing
	+ Training
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your dog's boarding needs.
* **Notes 2** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your dog's boarding needs.
* **Notes 3** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your dog's boarding needs.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select your payment method:
	+ Cash
	+ Card
	+ PayPal
* **Payment Details** (select_multiple, required): Confirm your payment details:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
