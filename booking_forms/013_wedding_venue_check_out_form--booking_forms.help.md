# Wedding Venue Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Venue Check Out Form is used to collect information from the contact person in charge of a wedding venue rental. This form is essential for processing the booking and ensuring that all relevant details are recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask the booking coordinator or contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Name (<font size="2">text</font>, text)**: Enter the name of the venue that was rented for the event.
* **Contact Name (<font size="2">text</font>, text)**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the venue rental.
* **Contact Email (<font size="2">email</font>, email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (<font size="2">text</font>, text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Rental Items (<font size="2">text</font>, text)**: List the items that were rented from the venue.
* **Items Returned (<font size="2">text</font>, text)**: List the items that were returned to the venue.
* **Comments (<font size="2">note</font>, note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the rental or return process.
* **Date Returned (<font size="2">date</font>, date)**: Enter the date the items were returned to the venue.
