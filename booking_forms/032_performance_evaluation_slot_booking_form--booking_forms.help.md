# Performance Evaluation Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Slot Booking Form is a tool used to schedule and coordinate performance evaluation sessions between team members. It allows reviewers (managers/evaluators) to select reviewees and managers, choose a time and date for the evaluation, and provide notes and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reviewer and reviewee from the respective drop-down menus.
2. Choose the date and time for the evaluation session.
3. Select the duration and frequency of the slot.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments.
5. Provide the reviewee's and reviewer's contact information.
6. Review and submit the form to confirm your slot booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Evaluation Form** (`form_start`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or context about the evaluation session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date for the evaluation session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time for the evaluation session.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, optional): Select the manager who will be evaluating the reviewee.
* **Reviewee** (`reviewee`, select_one, optional): Select the reviewee who will be being evaluated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the evaluation session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the reviewer's and reviewee's email addresses.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's and reviewee's phone numbers.
* **Slot Duration** (`slot_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the evaluation session (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes).
* **Slot Frequency** (`slot_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often the evaluation session will occur (Once, Daily, or Weekly).
* **Slot Time of Day** (`slot_time_of_day`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day for the evaluation session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Slot Date** (`slot_date`, date, optional): Select the date for the evaluation session.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review and submit the form to confirm the slot booking.
* **Reviewee Phone** (`reviewee_phone`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the reviewee's phone numbers.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the manager's phone numbers.
* **Reviewee Email** (`reviewee_email`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the reviewee's email addresses.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the manager's email addresses.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the evaluation session.
* **Manager 2** (`manager_2`, select_one, optional): Select the manager who will be evaluating the reviewee.
* **Reviewee 2** (`reviewee_2`, select_one, optional): Select the reviewee who will be being evaluated.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter the reviewer's and reviewee's email addresses.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's and reviewee's phone numbers.
* **Slot Duration 2** (`slot_duration_2`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the evaluation session (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes).
* **Slot Frequency 2** (`slot_frequency_2`, select_one, optional): Select how often the evaluation session will occur (Once, Daily, or Weekly).
* **Slot Time of Day 2** (`slot_time_of_day_2`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day for the evaluation session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
