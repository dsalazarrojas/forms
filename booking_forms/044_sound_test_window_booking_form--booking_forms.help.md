# Sound Test Window Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of booking a sound test window. The form is typically used to gather the user's input, email address, and phone number for further processing and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* User Input: Type your desired sound test window configuration in this field.
	* User Email: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
	* User Phone: Provide your phone number for communication.
2. Review and submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (<type>text, required: false)**: Enter your desired sound test window configuration in this field. This is the primary input field for your booking request.
* **User Email (<type>email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications regarding your booking.
* **User Phone (<type>text, required: false)**: Provide your phone number for additional contact and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to ensure successful submission.
* If you are unable to complete a field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is for booking a sound test window; please only use it for that purpose.
