# Artist Collaboration Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artist Collaboration Session Booking Form is designed to facilitate collaboration between artists by providing a structured way to gather essential information about the collaborating artists and the session details. This form helps ensure that both artists are on the same page and can plan a successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist name, email address, and phone number in the "Primary Artist Information" section.
2. Select your primary artistic specialization.
3. In the "Collaborating Artist Information" section, enter the name and email address of the artist you wish to collaborate with.
4. Select the proposed session date and time in the "Session Details" section.
5. Choose the expected session duration and purpose.
6. In the "Project Details" section, enter the project title or concept and provide a description of the collaborative work.
7. Select the project scope and location for the session.
8. Confirm your schedule flexibility and agree to the collaboration terms.
9. Add any additional notes or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your professional or stage name.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Your Artistic Specialization** (`artist_specialization`, select_one, required): Select your primary art form or discipline.
* **Collaborating Artist Name** (`collaborator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist you wish to collaborate with.
* **Collaborator Email** (`collaborator_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the collaborating artist.
* **Collaborator Specialization** (`collaborator_specialization`, text, optional): Enter the primary art form of the collaborating artist.
* **Proposed Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date you propose for the session.
* **Proposed Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Select the time you propose for the session.
* **Expected Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the expected duration of the session.
* **Purpose of Collaboration** (`session_purpose`, text, required): Describe the goal of the collaboration.
* **Project Title or Concept** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter the name of the collaborative project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide an overview of the collaborative work.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, select_one, optional): Select the scope of the project.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location for the session.
* **Location Details or Address** (`location_details`, text, optional): Enter specific address or meeting details.
* **Schedule Flexibility** (`schedule_flexibility`, select_one, required): Select how flexible your schedule is.
* **Collaboration Agreement** (`collaboration_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the collaboration terms.
* **Additional Notes or Requirements** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or information.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Be clear and concise in your responses to ensure a successful collaboration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
