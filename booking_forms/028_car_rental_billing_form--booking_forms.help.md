# Car Rental Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental Billing Form is designed to collect billing information and details for car rentals from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's billing contact details, including their first name, last name, email address, phone number, street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
2. **Rental Information**: Enter the rental agreement details, including the reservation number, pickup date, return date, and total rental days.
3. **Vehicle Information**: Enter the vehicle details, including the vehicle category, make and model, VIN, license plate, starting and ending mileage, total miles driven, and daily rental rate.
4. **Billing Charges**: Enter the billing charges, including the base rental charge, mileage charge, additional driver fee, GPS navigation fee, child seat fee, additional equipment fee, insurance charges, fuel charges, toll charges, late return fee, cleaning fee, damage charges, and other charges.
5. **Payment Information**: Enter the payment details, including the amount paid, payment date, balance due, payment method, and billing notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`): Enter the customer's email address for billing communications.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Street Address** (`id: 6`): Enter the customer's billing address.
* **City** (`id: 7`): Enter the city of the customer's billing address.
* **State** (`id: 8`): Enter the state of the customer's billing address.
* **ZIP Code** (`id: 9`): Enter the customer's ZIP code.
* **Reservation Number** (`id: 11`): Enter the confirmation number for the reservation.
* **Rental Start Date** (`id: 12`): Enter the pickup date of the rental agreement.
* **Rental End Date** (`id: 13`): Enter the return date of the rental agreement.
* **Rental Days** (`id: 15`): Enter the number of days for the rental agreement.
* **Vehicle Category** (`id: 17`): Enter the class of the vehicle rented.
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (`id: 18`): Enter the specific vehicle make and model rented.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`id: 19`): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **License Plate** (`id: 20`): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Starting Mileage** (`id: 21`): Enter the odometer reading at the start of the rental.
* **Ending Mileage** (`id: 22`): Enter the odometer reading at the end of the rental.
* **Total Miles Driven** (`id: 23`): Enter the total distance driven during the rental.
* **Daily Rental Rate** (`id: 25`): Enter the rate per day for the rental.
* **Base Rental Charge** (`id: 26`): Enter the subtotal for the rental.
* **Mileage Charge** (`id: 27`): Enter the charge for the mileage driven.
* **Additional Driver Fee** (`id: 28`): Enter the fee for extra drivers.
* **GPS Navigation Fee** (`id: 29`): Enter the fee for GPS navigation.
* **Child Seat Fee** (`id: 30`): Enter the fee for child seat.
* **Additional Equipment Fee** (`id: 31`): Enter the fee for other equipment.
* **Insurance Charges** (`id: 32`): Enter the fees for insurance coverage.
* **Fuel Charges** (`id: 33`): Enter the charges for fuel.
* **Toll Charges** (`id: 34`): Enter the charges for tolls.
* **Late Return Fee** (`id: 35`): Enter the fee for late return.
* **Cleaning Fee** (`id: 36`): Enter the fee for cleaning.
* **Damage Charges** (`id: 37`): Enter the fees for damages.
* **Other Charges** (`id: 38`): Enter any other fees applied.
* **Other Charges Description** (`id: 39`): Enter a description of any other charges applied.
* **Subtotal** (`id: 40`): Enter the subtotal before taxes.
* **Tax Rate** (`id: 41`): Enter the tax percentage applied.
* **Taxes** (`id: 42`): Enter the tax amount applied.
* **Total Amount Due** (`id: 43`): Enter the final total amount due.
* **Payment Information** (`id: 44`): Enter payment details, including amount paid, payment date, balance due, and payment method.
* **Deposit Amount** (`id: 45`): Enter the security deposit amount.
* **Deposit Status** (`id: 46`): Select the deposit status, either Held, Released, or Applied to balance.
* **Amount Paid** (`id: 47`): Enter the amount already paid.
* **Payment Date** (`id: 48`): Enter the date payment was made.
* **Balance Due** (`id: 49`): Enter the remaining balance due.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 50`): Select the payment method used, such as Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Check, Corporate Account, or Pending.
* **Billing Notes** (`id: 51`): Enter any additional billing notes.
* **Billing Date** (`id: 52`): Enter the date of this invoice.
