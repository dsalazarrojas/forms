# Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Contract Form is a vital document that outlines the terms and conditions of a photography session. It provides a clear understanding of the client's and photographer's responsibilities, expectations, and obligations. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and fields to ensure you fill out the form accurately and make the most of your photography experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information in the **Client Information** section, including your name and contact details.
2.  In the **Contract Terms** and **Payment Terms** sections, review and agree to the terms presented.
3.  Specify the **Photography Coverage** details, such as the date and location of the shoot.
4.  Choose a cancellation policy from the provided options for the **Cancellation Policy** field.
5.  Fill in the **Shoot Details** section with relevant information about the session, such as the type of shoot and duration.
6.  Record the date you signed this contract in the **Date Signed** field.
7.  Specify the version of this contract in the **Version** field.
8.  In the **Photography Session Details** section, add any additional information that would be helpful for the photographer to know.
9.  Finally, select who will be signing this contract, either you as the **Client** or the photographer as the **Photographer**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information** (`photography_contract_form`, text, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you'll enter your basic contact information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details to ensure we can get in touch with you effectively.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number here. This is essential for us to get in touch with you in case of any last-minute details or changes.
*   **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, note, required: false): Here, you will find the terms and conditions of the contract. Read through carefully and agree to the terms presented.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, note, required: false): This section outlines the payment terms and conditions for the session. Please review these carefully to understand your obligations.
*   **Photography Coverage** (`photography_coverage`, text, required: false): In this field, provide details about the photography coverage, including the date and location of the shoot.
*   **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to determine the cancellation policy that best suits you and your needs.
*   **Shoot Details** (`shoot_details`, text, required: false): This is the section to provide any additional details about the session that would be helpful for the photographer to know.
*   **Date Signed** (`contract_date_signed`, date, required: false): Record the date you signed this contract.
*   **Version** (`contract_version`, text, required: false): Specify the version of this contract.
*   **Photography Session Details** (`photography_session_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional information that would be beneficial for the photographer to know.
*   **Contract Signed By** (`contract_signed_by`, select_multiple, required: false): You can select whether you will be signing this contract as the client or the photographer will be signing it for you.
