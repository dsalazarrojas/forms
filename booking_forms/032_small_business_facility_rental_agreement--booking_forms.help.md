# Small Business Facility Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for small businesses that wish to rent a facility. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including First name, Last name, Email, and Phone.
2. Enter your company name and select the company size.
3. Provide the rental start and end dates.
4. Choose the type of facility you require (Office space, Warehouse, Storage).
5. Select the facilities you need (Parking, Meeting rooms, Kitchen, Office equipment, Fiber optic internet, Utilities).
6. Specify the rental duration and select the lease term (Month, Quarter, Year).
7. Enter the security deposit and monthly rent amounts.
8. Specify any other charges.
9. Read and sign the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
- **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
- **Company size** (`company_size`, number, optional): Select the number of employees in your company.
- **Rental start date** (`rental_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to start renting the facility.
- **Rental end date** (`rental_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to end renting the facility.
- **Facility type** (`facility_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of facility you require (Office space, Warehouse, Storage).
- **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_multiple, optional): Select the facilities you need (Parking, Meeting rooms, Kitchen, Office equipment, Fiber optic internet, Utilities).
- **Rental duration** (`rental_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of months or years you wish to rent the facility.
- **Lease term** (`lease_term`, select_one, optional): Select the lease term (Month, Quarter, Year).
- **Security deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, optional): Enter the amount of security deposit.
- **Monthly rent** (`monthly_rent`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rent amount.
- **Other charges** (`other_charges`, number, optional): Enter any additional charges.
- **Terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Signed date** (`sign_date`, date, optional): Sign and date the agreement.
