# hvc_maintenance_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The hvc_maintenance_booking_form is used to schedule and plan maintenance activities for HVAC systems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
*   **HVAC System Details** (`hvc_system_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select the HVAC system(s) that require maintenance.
*   **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of maintenance activity (e.g., routine, emergency, etc.).
*   **Schedule Maintenance** (`schedule_maintenance`, date, optional): Enter the date when the maintenance activity is scheduled.
*   **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the maintenance activity is scheduled.
*   **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the maintenance activity is scheduled.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the maintenance activity.
*   **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If the maintenance activity is for a specific technician, select their name from the drop-down list.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to book and assign the maintenance activity.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct HVAC system(s) that require maintenance.
*   Choose the correct type of maintenance activity.
*   Enter the correct date and time for the maintenance activity.
*   Add any relevant notes or comments about the maintenance activity.
*   If assigning a technician, select their name from the drop-down list.
