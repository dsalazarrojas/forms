# Farm Volunteer Housing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Volunteer Housing Booking Form is a simple online form designed to collect information from potential volunteers about their accommodation preferences and requirements for a specific event or period. This form helps the event organizers to understand your needs and preferences and ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay for all volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Choose the correct answer from the dropdown list for "Accommodation Type".
3. Write a clear and concise description for each field, such as "Contact Details", "Dates", "Special Requests", and "Volunteer Details".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you to confirm your booking.
- **Dates** (`dates`, text, required/optional): Please provide the dates you are planning to stay with us, including the start and end dates.
- **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please choose the type of accommodation you prefer: Shared Accommodation or Private Accommodation.
  - Note: If you choose "Other", please provide more information about your accommodation request in the "Special Requests" field.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any specific requests or needs, please describe them here.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the event or purpose of your visit.
- **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required/optional): Please provide your name.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your booking.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you to confirm your booking.
- **Accommodation Arrangement** (`accommodation_arrangement`, text, required/optional): Please describe how you would like to arrange your accommodation.
- **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your volunteer experience, skills, and any other relevant information.
- **Event Details** (`event_details_2`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the event or purpose of your visit.
- **Special Request 1** (`special_request_1`, text, required/optional): Please provide any other special requests or needs you may have.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will get back to you as soon as possible to confirm your booking.
