# Hiking Trip Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Trip Check Out Form is used to collect essential information from hikers after completing a trip. This form helps to ensure that hiker safety and activity can be tracked and monitored within the park or wilderness area.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your trip using the date picker.
3. Type in the total distance you traveled during the trip.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Provide the location where you started the trailhead.
7. If you have any notes about the trailhead location, you can type them here (optional).
8. If you have any additional comments about your trip, you can type them here (optional).
9. You can optionally provide the name and contact information of the person or organization that submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hiker_name**: <u>Hiker's Name</u> (`hiker_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **trip_start_date**: <u>Trip Start Date</u> (`trip_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you started your trip.
* **trip_end_date**: <u>Trip End Date</u> (`trip_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you ended your trip.
* **total_distance**: <u>Total Distance</u> (`total_distance`, number, required): Type in the total distance you traveled during the trip.
* **hiker_email**: <u>Hiker's Email</u> (`hiker_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **hiker_phone**: <u>Hiker's Phone Number</u> (`hiker_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **trailhead_location**: <u>Trailhead Location</u> (`trailhead_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you started the trail.
* **trailhead_location_note**: <u>Trailhead Location Note</u> (`trailhead_location_note`, note, required): Provide any additional notes about the trailhead location.
* **hiker_comments**: <u>Hiker Comments</u> (`hiker_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or additional information about your trip.
* **form_submitted_by**: <u>Form Submitted By</u> (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization that submitted this form.
* **form_submitted_by_email**: <u>Form Submitted By Email</u> (`form_submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization that submitted this form.
* **form_submitted_by_phone**: <u>Form Submitted By Phone</u> (`form_submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization that submitted this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use this form for hiking trip purposes only.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask for assistance from park rangers or other authorized personnel.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for hiker activity within the park or wilderness area.
