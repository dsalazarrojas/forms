# Book Your Digital Marketing Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential clients to book a digital marketing consultation with our agency. It gathers information about their background, marketing goals, and preferences to ensure a successful consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and company information.
- Select your preferred consultation format (video call, phone call, or in-person).
- Choose a date and time that works best for you.
- Indicate your marketing goals and challenges.
- Select your preferred consultation type and industry.
- Choose your preferred timezone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, where we will send confirmation and other important information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for consultation coordination.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
- **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a website, enter its URL.
- **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary industry.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the number of employees in your company.
- **Type of Consultation** (`consultation_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of consultation you need.
- **Marketing Goals** (`marketing_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your primary marketing goals.
- **Current Marketing Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe your current marketing challenges.
- **Monthly Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `select_one`, optional): Enter your monthly marketing budget range.
- **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_consultation_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for your consultation.
- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_consultation_time`, `time`, required): Select a time for your consultation.
- **Consultation Format** (`consultation_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred consultation format.
- **Timezone** (`timezone`, `select_one`, required): Select your timezone.
- **Current Marketing Channels** (`current_marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your current marketing channels.
- **Previous Agency Experience** (`previous_agency_experience`, `select_one`, optional): If you've worked with marketing agencies before, select yes or no.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): How did you find our agency?
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any other information to share, enter it here.
- **I Agree to Consultation Terms** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select yes to agree to the consultation terms.
- **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, required): Date and time of the booking.
