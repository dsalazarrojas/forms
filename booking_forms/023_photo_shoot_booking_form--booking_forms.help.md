# photo_shoot_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo shoot booking form is used for photographers to book a photo shoot with a client. It collects essential information for the photographer and the client, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for a successful shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your photo shoot details.
2. Ensure that all required fields (e.g., date, time, location, photo shoot type, contact number, email) are filled in accurately.
3. Select the correct photo shoot type (portrait, wedding, family, event).
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Shoot Details** (`photo_shoot_booking_form_page`): Enter a brief description of your photo shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Details 2** (`photo_shoot_details_2`): Enter any additional information about the photo shoot (optional).
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the photo shoot in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the photo shoot in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Photo Shoot Location** (`photo_shoot_location`): Enter the location where the photo shoot will take place.
* **Photo Shoot Type** (`photo_shoot_type`): Select the type of photo shoot (portrait, wedding, family, event).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number, ensuring that the photographer can reach you for any updates or changes.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Message** (`message`): Enter any additional information or questions you have about the photo shoot (optional).
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`): Enter a brief confirmation of your booking, ensuring that your details are correct.

Note: The fields with required status `true` are crucial to the form and should be filled in accurately to avoid any issues with the booking.
