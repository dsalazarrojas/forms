# VIP Security Escort Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VIP Security Escort Booking Form is designed to collect information for clients or guests who require a security escort for their visit. Please ensure that you have been previously approved or cleared for escort services before submitting this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and any relevant phone number.
2. Enter the details of your event, including date, start and end times, and any specific requirements.
3. Specify any special requests or requirements for your escort.
4. Choose the type of escort service you require (Security Escort or Other).
5. Add any additional information or requests.
6. Verify your contact information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_9`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any phone number where you can be reached.
- **Email** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_10`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Booking Type** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_7`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of escort service you require, either Security Escort or Other.
- **Booking Date** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_6`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event.
- **Booking Time** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_5`, time, required/optional): Enter the start and end times of your event.
- **Escort Details** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_4`, text, required/optional): Provide any special information about the escort you require.
- **Additional Requests** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_8`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or requests.
- **Special Requests** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_3`, text, required/optional): Specify any special requirements or requests for your escort.
- **Event Details** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_2`, text, required/optional): Enter details about your event, including any specific requirements.
- **Contact Information** (`vip_security_escort_booking_form_page_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including name and any relevant phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure you have been previously approved or cleared for escort services before submitting this form.
- If you are unsure about any information or requirements, please contact your security escort service representative.
- Verify your contact information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
