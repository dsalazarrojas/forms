# Macs Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event organizers to provide information about their events and requirements for Macs catering services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event organiser's name and contact person's name in the respective text fields.
2. Select the support options that are required for your event from the "Support Requested" dropdown menu.
3. Specify the equipment required for your event from the "Equipment Required" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your email and phone number in the respective text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organiser Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person organizing the event.
* **Contact Person Name (text)**: Enter the name of the contact person who will be responsible for communication.
* **Support Requested (select_multiple)**: Select all the types of support you require for your event (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Food and Drinks Requested (text)**: This field is not used as it is not a part of the form (the field "food_and_drinks_requested" in the YAML is not actually used).
* **Equipment Required (select_one)**: Select the type of equipment required for your event from the options provided.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
