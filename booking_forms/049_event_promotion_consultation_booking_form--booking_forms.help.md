# Event Promotion Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect crucial details about an event from a customer, allowing us to effectively prepare and promote the event in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the event you wish to promote in the "Booking Details" field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "event_date" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Enter the start time of the event in the "start_time" field in the 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
4. Fill in the name of the agency or organization hosting the event in the "agency" field.
5. Add any additional event details you'd like to mention in the "event_details" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field, so we can get in touch with you for further questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Details** (`event_promotion_booking_form`, text, required): Please fill in the title of the event you're promoting. This will help us identify the event and ensure it's included in our promotional efforts.
* **event_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format. Ensure the date is accurate, as this is crucial for our planning and promotional activities.
* **start_time** (time, required): Fill in the start time of the event in 12-hour format, including AM/PM. This will help us determine the event's schedule.
* **agency** (text, optional): If you're representing an agency or organization hosting the event, please enter their name here. This information will be used for our promotional materials.
* **event_details** (text, optional): Use this field to add any extra information about the event that's not already included in the other fields.
* **contact_number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for any questions or updates about the event.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled to ensure a successful submission.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
