# Advertising Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from advertising clients to book a specific advertising campaign on various platforms, manage campaign details, and confirm the booking process. It will guide you through the required fields and sections to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and attachments ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields in each section accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Company Name**: Enter the legal name of your company placing the advertisement.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the campaign.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business email for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
### Advertising Campaign Details
* **Campaign Name**: Enter a name for the advertising campaign.
* **Advertising Platform**: Select where the advertisement will be placed from the available options.
* **Ad Format**: Choose the format the advertisement will take from the available options.
* **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the date when the campaign should start.
* **Campaign End Date**: Enter the date when the campaign should end.
* **Ad Placement Details**: Provide specific placement details for the ad.
### Budget and Pricing
* **Campaign Budget**: Enter the total budget allocated for the campaign.
### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms**: Choose how the payment will be handled from the available options.
### Creative Requirements
* **Creative Submission Deadline**: Enter the date when creative materials must be submitted.
### Booking Confirmation
* **Booking Confirmation**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the details of this advertising booking.

**Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the advertising representative for assistance.
