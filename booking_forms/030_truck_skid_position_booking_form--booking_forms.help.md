# Truck Skid Position Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Skid Position Booking Form is used to book a truck skid position for a specific date and time. This form is intended for logistics or operations teams to collect important information from drivers or contacts before assigning a truck to a skid position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form with the required information, including the arrival date and time, truck size, contact name, contact phone, contact email, and any additional notes.
2. Select the type of truck (Reefer, Dry, or Other) that will be arriving at the skid position.
3. Enter the booking date and time, if applicable.
4. Review and submit the form to complete the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Date** (Page 1, id: 1, date): Enter the date the truck will arrive at the skid position.
* **Truck Size** (Page 1, id: 2, select_one): Select the size of the truck that will be arriving (Large, Medium, or Small).
* **Arrival Time** (Page 1, id: 3, time): Enter the time the truck will arrive at the skid position (if different from the arrival date).
* **Skid Number** (Page 1, id: 4, number): Enter the number of the skid position to be booked.
* **Contact Name** (Page 1, id: 5, text): Enter the name of the contact person or representative.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 1, id: 6, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person or representative.
* **Contact Email** (Page 1, id: 7, email): Enter the email address of the contact person or representative.
* **Additional Notes** (Page 1, id: 8, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking.
* **Truck Type** (Page 1, id: 9, select_one): Select the type of truck (Reefer, Dry, or Other) that will be arriving at the skid position.
* **Booking Date** (Page 1, id: 10, date): Enter the date the booking was made (if different from the arrival date).
* **Booking Time** (Page 1, id: 11, time): Enter the time the booking was made (if different from the arrival date).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any confusion or errors during the booking process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the logistics or operations team for guidance.
