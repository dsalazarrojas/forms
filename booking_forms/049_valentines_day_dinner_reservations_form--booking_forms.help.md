# Valentines Day Dinner Reservations Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers book a dinner at one of our participating restaurants on Valentine's Day. Please fill out this form to make your reservation and provide any special requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reservation date for your dinner.
2. Choose the time for your reservation.
3. Enter the size of your party.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter the number of guests in your party.
7. If you have any special requests, please let us know.
8. Select the restaurant where you would like to make your reservation.
9. Finally, select "Submit" to send your reservation request to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date for your Valentine's Day dinner reservation.
* **Reservations Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for your reservation.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your reservation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number where we can reach you to confirm your reservation.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required/optional): If you have any special requests or requirements for your reservation, please let us know.
* **Restaurant** (`restaurant`, select_one, required/optional): Select the restaurant where you would like to make your reservation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Submit" to send your reservation request to us.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a valid reservation date and time that is available at your chosen restaurant.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information so we can reach you to confirm your reservation.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Special Requests" field.
* Double-check your selections before submitting your form to ensure accuracy.
