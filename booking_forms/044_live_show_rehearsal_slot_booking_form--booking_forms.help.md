# Live Show Rehearsal Slot Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a live show rehearsal slot from the production team. Please fill out the form with your team's production details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your production's name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the start and end dates of the slot you would like to book.
3. Choose the start time of the slot.
4. Specify the duration of the slot.
5. Add any necessary notes or details about your production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Name** (`production_name`, text, required): Enter your production's name, e.g., "The Great Production Show".
* **Slot Start Date** (`slot_start_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to start the rehearsal slot. If left blank, the default will be the current date.
* **Slot End Date** (`slot_end_date`, date, optional): Choose the end date of the slot. If left blank, the default will be the next day after the start date.
* **Slot Start Time** (`slot_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the slot, e.g., 10:00 AM.
* **Slot Duration** (`slot_duration`, text, optional): Specify the length of the slot, e.g., "2 hours".
* **Production Contact Email** (`production_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your production team's contact email address.
* **Production Contact Phone** (`production_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your production team's contact phone number.
* **Production Contact Name** (`production_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person in charge of your production team.
* **Production Contact Company** (`production_contact_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your production company.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about your production, e.g., special equipment required.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are booking a slot that fits your team's schedule and needs.
* If you have any questions or need to request changes to the slot, please contact the production team in advance.
