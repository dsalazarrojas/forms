# Team Building Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your team's accommodation preferences and needs for team-building events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. If you don't have an email address, leave this field blank.
5. Enter the size of your team in the "Team Size" field.
6. Select your preferred accommodation type (Hotel, Apartment, Hostel, or Other) in the "Accommodation Preferences" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of your event in the "Dates Start" and "Dates End" fields, respectively.
8. Choose your check-in and check-out times in the "Check In Time" and "Check Out Time" fields, respectively.
9. Provide any additional details about your accommodation requirements in the "Accommodation Details" field.
10. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Team Size**: Enter the number of team members attending the event.
* **Accommodation Preferences**: Select the type of accommodation you prefer (Hotel, Apartment, Hostel, or Other).
* **Dates Start**: Enter the start date of your event (yyyy-mm-dd format).
* **Dates End**: Enter the end date of your event (yyyy-mm-dd format).
* **Check In Time**: Choose your preferred check-in time (HH:MM format).
* **Check Out Time**: Choose your preferred check-out time (HH:MM format).
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide any additional details about your accommodation requirements (e.g., number of beds, specific amenities).
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requests that may not fit in the other fields.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted directly.

Please note that some fields are required to be filled out, and some are not. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your event coordinator or administrator for guidance.
