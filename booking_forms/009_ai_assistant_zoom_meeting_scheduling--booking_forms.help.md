# AI Assistant Zoom Meeting Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Assistant Zoom Meeting Scheduling form is designed to help you schedule a meeting with our AI strategy team for a consultation, technical support, or other purposes. Fill out the form with your details, and we will send you a Zoom meeting link and calendar invite once submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in all required fields**: The form requires your name, email address, meeting topic, meeting purpose, preferred date, time slot, time zone, desired meeting duration, and number of attendees. Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
2. **Select your preferences**: Choose your preferred time slot, time zone, and desired meeting duration from the available options.
3. **Add optional details**: If you have any specific agenda items or previous experience with AI implementations, feel free to add them in the respective fields.
4. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, click the submit button to send us your booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company email address where you would like to receive the meeting invitation and Zoom link.
* **Company/Organization**: Provide your company name or organization name (optional).
* **Meeting Topic**: Briefly describe the topic you would like to discuss during the meeting.
* **Primary Purpose of Meeting**: Choose from the available options: Initial Consultation, Technical Support / Troubleshooting, Product Demo, Partnership Inquiry, or Follow-up Session.
* **AI Assistant Platform Interest**: Select all the platforms or services you are using or interested in (multiple selection allowed).
* **Preferred Meeting Date**: Choose a date for the meeting. Note that the date should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select a time slot for the meeting: Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM), Afternoon (12:00 PM - 4:00 PM), or Evening (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM).
* **Your Time Zone**: Select your time zone: UTC-8 (PST), UTC-5 (EST), UTC+0 (GMT), UTC+1 (CET), or UTC+8 (SGT/HKT).
* **Desired Meeting Duration**: Choose the duration of the meeting: 15 Minutes (Quick Sync), 30 Minutes (Standard), or 60 Minutes (Deep Dive).
* **Estimated Number of Attendees**: Enter the number of attendees from your organization.
* **Specific Agenda Items or Questions**: Add any specific agenda items or questions you would like to cover during the meeting.
* **Previous Experience with AI Implementations**: Indicate if you have any existing AI implementations: Yes, currently in production, Yes, in development/pilot, or No, just starting out.
* **Mobile Number (Optional for SMS Reminders)**: Enter your mobile phone number to receive SMS reminders (optional).
* **Confirmation Note**: This field is for your reference and will not be submitted along with your booking details.
