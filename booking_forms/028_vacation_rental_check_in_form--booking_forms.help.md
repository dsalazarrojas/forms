# Vacation Rental Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from guests during their stay at a vacation rental property. This form helps the property management team to manage guest check-ins and ensure a smooth arrival experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the correct dates for check-in and check-out.
3. Choose your preferred arrival time.
4. If you have any specific preferences or requests, provide them in the "Guest Notes" field.
5. If you're traveling with a group, please select your group type from the options provided.
6. Once you've completed all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to finalize your check-in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Guest Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Guest Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact and emergencies.
* **Check In Date**: Select the date you plan to arrive at the property.
* **Check Out Date**: Select the date you plan to leave the property.
* **Guest Arrival Time**: Choose your preferred arrival time.
* **Property Address**: Enter the property address to ensure you arrive at the correct location.
* **Guest Notes**: Provide any specific requests, preferences, or instructions for your stay.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you're arriving at (e.g., House, Condo, Apt, Townhouse).
* **Guest Type**: Select the type of group you're traveling with (e.g., Individual, Group, Corporate, Family).
* **Check In Status**: This field will be populated automatically based on the property's check-in status. Please don't edit this field.
* **Guest Arrival Vehicle**: If you have a vehicle, enter its make and model.
* **Guest Arrival Mode**: Enter any specific arrival mode (e.g., walking, driving, flying).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or questions, contact the property management staff.
- This form is not designed to replace any official check-in documentation.
