# Angelstone Camper Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Angelstone Camper Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from campers and their guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience at the Angelstone camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Be as accurate and clear as possible when providing information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure that you've entered the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Full Name (\*):** Enter the camper's full name exactly as it appears on their birth certificate or other official documents.
* **Camper Date of Birth (\*):** Enter the camper's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender:** Select the camper's gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say).
* **Parent / Guardian Full Name (\*):** Enter the guardian's or parent's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Guardian Email Address (\*):** Enter the guardian's or parent's email address, which will be used for camp correspondence.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number (\*):** Enter the guardian's or parent's emergency contact phone number.
* **Select Camp Session:** Choose the desired camp session from the provided options (Summer Week 1, Summer Week 2, Fall Weekend, Winter Break Camp).
* **Primary Interests and Activities:** Select all the activities and interests that the camper would like to participate in during the camp (Horseback Riding, Swimming, Arts & Crafts, Sports, Nature Trail). This will help us tailor the experience to their preferences.
* **Known Food or Environmental Allergies:** If the camper has any known allergies or sensitivities, enter them here.
* **Special Medication Requirements or Schedules:** If the camper has any special medication needs or requirements, provide details here.
* **Swimming Proficiency Level:** Indicate the camper's swimming proficiency level (Non-swimmer, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Additional Comments for Camp Counselors:** If there's anything else that you'd like to share with the camp counselors or staff, enter it here (e.g., any medical conditions, special needs, or concerns).
