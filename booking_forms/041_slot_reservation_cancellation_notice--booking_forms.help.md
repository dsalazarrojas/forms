# Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice form is a formal way for customers to inform slot owners about their intent to cancel a previously scheduled slot. This form helps ensure that the slot owner is aware of the cancellation and can adjust their schedule accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Provide a clear reason for cancellation in the "reason_for_cancellation" field.
3. If applicable, include a message for the slot owner in the "message" field.
4. If you have further contact information, include the "contact_person", "contact_email", and "contact_phone" fields.
5. Submit the form to notify the slot owner of your cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Slot Reservation Cancellation Notice** (`slot_reservation_cancellation_notice`): This is a free-text field for you to enter a brief description of your cancellation notice.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`): Please provide a clear and concise reason for cancelling your reserved slot. This helps the slot owner understand your circumstances.
* **Date** (`date`): If you wish to include the date of the cancellation, please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Scheduled Time** (`scheduled_time`): If you wish to include the time of the cancellation, please use the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Reason for Cancellation (optional)** (`reason_for_cancellation`): If you've already provided a reason for cancellation above, you can skip this field.
* **Message (required)** (`message`): If you have further information or a message for the slot owner, enter it here.
* **Contact Person (optional)** (`contact_person`): Provide the name of the person to contact, if it's not you.
* **Contact Email (optional)** (`contact_email`): Enter the email of the person to contact, if it's not you.
* **Contact Phone (optional)** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person to contact, if it's not you.
