# Cargo Van Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cargo Van Reservation Form is designed to collect essential information for reserving a cargo van for your specific needs. This form is used to ensure that we have all the necessary details to prepare for your reservation and provide an efficient rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Enter your driver's license number in the "Driver's License Number" field.
5.  Choose your preferred van size from the options provided in the "Preferred Van Size" field.
6.  Select the total number of days for the rental duration in the "Rental Duration (days)" field.
7.  Choose your insurance package from the "Insurance Package" field options.
8.  Select whether you will need to add an additional driver in the "Add an additional driver?" field.
9.  Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" field options.
10. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "I agree to the rental terms and conditions" field.
11. Finally, enter your signature in the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, customer_name, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (3, customer_email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (4, customer_phone, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Driver's License Number** (5, driver_license, required): Enter your driver's license number for vehicle pickup.
* **Pickup Date** (7, pickup_date, required): Select the date you would like to pick up the van.
* **Pickup Time** (8, pickup_time, required): Select the time you would like to pick up the van.
* **Return Date** (9, return_date, required): Select the date you would like to return the van.
* **Return Time** (10, return_time, required): Select the time you would like to return the van.
* **Preferred Van Size** (11, van_size, required): Choose the capacity you need from the options provided.
* **Rental Duration (days)** (13, rental_duration_days, required): Enter the total number of days for the rental duration.
* **Insurance Package** (14, insurance_package, required): Choose your insurance package from the options provided.
* **Add an additional driver?** (15, additional_driver, required): Select whether you will need to add an additional driver.
* **Estimated Mileage for Rental** (18, estimated_mileage, optional): Enter the total distance you plan to travel.
* **Primary Purpose** (20, rental_purpose, required): Choose the primary reason for the rental from the options provided.
* **What will you be transporting?** (21, cargo_description, required): Describe the items you will be transporting.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (23, deposit_payment_method, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **I agree to the rental terms and conditions** (24, agreement_confirmation, required): Read and confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Customer Signature** (25, digital_signature, required): Enter your full name as a confirmation of your signature.
