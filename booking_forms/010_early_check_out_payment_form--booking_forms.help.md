# Early Check Out Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who wish to check out early from a hotel or other accommodation. It is used to submit a payment for an early check out and will be reviewed by hotel staff for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the date you wish to check out early.
*   Provide a reason for your early check out (e.g. "I need to catch a flight").
*   Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the early check out.
*   Select the payment type you would like to use (e.g. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to check out early. This date should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Reason for Early Check Out** (`reason_for_early_check_out`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you need to check out early. This helps us understand your situation.
*   **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount you are willing to pay for the early check out. This is the amount you are willing to pay for the extra days stayed.
*   **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you would like to pay for the early check out. You can choose from the following options:
    *   Visa
    *   Mastercard
    *   Amex
    *   PayPal
