# Innovation Leadership Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a retreat center for Innovation Leadership stays. It is intended for booking a specific type of stay, such as a retreat or other type of stay. The form is meant to gather necessary information from the guest to accommodate their needs during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of stay you are booking from the "Guest Details" page.
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your phone number, if required.
4. If you have any special requirements, please let us know in the "Special Requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retreat Center**: Enter the name of the retreat center you are interested in booking.
* **Guest Details**: Select the type of stay you are booking (e.g., Innovation Leadership Stay, Other).
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements for your stay, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you select the correct type of stay in the "Guest Details" field.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.

Please note that this form is meant to be simple and easy to use. If you have any confusion or need help, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
