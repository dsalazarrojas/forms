# Charter Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charter Service Request Form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their charter service needs, including personal details, trip details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the trip dates you require the charter service for.
3. Provide the pickup and drop-off locations for your trip.
4. Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Transportation, Executive Chauffeuring, etc.).
5. Enter the estimated budget for your charter service.
6. Add any additional requests or comments.
7. Finally, select the date you are submitting this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for charter service confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company or Organization** (5, text, optional): If you are a representative of a company or organization, enter their name.
* **-- Trip Details --** (6, note, optional): This is a section divider to separate the personal details from the trip details.
* **Trip Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date you require the charter service for.
* **Return Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date the charter service should end.
* **Pickup Location** (9, text, required): Enter the location where the charter service should start.
* **Service Destination** (10, text, required): Enter the final destination of the charter service.
* **-- Service Type --** (11, note, optional): This is a section divider to separate the trip details from the service type.
* **Type of Service Required** (12, select_one, required): Choose the type of service you require from the options provided.
* **Number of Passengers** (13, number, required): Enter the number of people that will be traveling.
* **Special Requirements or Amenities** (14, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or amenities you would like included in the charter service (e.g., WiFi, refreshments, etc.), enter them here.
* **-- Preferences --** (15, note, optional): This is a section divider to separate the service details from the preferences.
* **Vehicle Type Preference** (16, text, optional): If you have a preference for a specific type of vehicle (e.g., sedan, SUV, luxury, etc.), enter it here.
* **Driver Should Have Experience With** (17, text, optional): If you have any specific experience requirements for the driver (e.g., corporate events, long distance, etc.), enter them here.
* **-- Budget --** (18, note, optional): This is a section divider to separate the preferences from the budget.
* **Estimated Budget** (19, number, optional): If you have an estimated budget for the charter service, enter it here.
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (20, text, optional): If you have any additional requests or comments, enter them here.
* **Request Date** (21, date, required): This is the date you are submitting this request.
