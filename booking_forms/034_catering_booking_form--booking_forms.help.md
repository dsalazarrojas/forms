# Catering Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for clients to book catering services with your company. It collects essential information about the client, event details, and service requirements. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your billing address for payment and communication purposes.
3. Enter the date of the event and select a suitable time for your guests to arrive.
4. Fill in the details of the event, including the venue address and final guest count.
5. Select a menu package and service style that suits your event.
6. Choose a beverage package that matches your preferences.
7. If needed, choose additional services such as linens, glassware, or staff.
8. Provide a general overview of any dietary requirements.
9. Confirm your understanding of the deposit and terms.
10. Finally, sign the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your company's or individual's address for payment and communication purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact and communication.
* **Date of Event**: Select a date for the event in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Event Venue Full Address**: Enter the full address of the event venue.
* **Guest Arrival Time**: Choose a suitable time for your guests to arrive at the venue.
* **Final Guest Count**: Enter the minimum expected number of guests attending the event.
* **Menu Package**: Select a menu package that suits your event type (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Custom).
* **Service Style**: Choose a service style that matches your event requirements (Buffet, Sit-down Plated, Cocktail, or Family Style).
* **Beverage Package**: Select a beverage package that suits your preferences (Non-Alcoholic, Beer and Wine, Full Premium, or Cash Bar).
* **Additional Services Required**: Check any additional services needed for your event (Linens, Glassware, Wait Staff, etc.).
* **General Dietary Overview**: Enter a brief description of any dietary requirements or allergies.
* **Deposit Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand a 25% non-refundable deposit is required for the event.
* **Client Electronic Signature**: Add your electronic signature to confirm the booking details.
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date when the booking is being made.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name for reference and communication purposes.
