# finance_coaching_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The finance_coaching_booking_form is used to book and schedule coaching sessions with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Select a session type from the available options (coaching session or free coaching session).
2. Choose your preferred coaching method (video, phone, or in-person).
3. Select your available time for the coaching session.
4. Enter your client's name.
5. Provide any additional notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_types** (session_type, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of coaching session. Choose between "coaching session" or "free coaching session" to proceed.
* **preferred_coaching_method** (preferred_coaching_method, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select your preferred coaching method. You can choose one or more options (video, phone, or in-person).
* **available_time** (available_time, date, required): This field is used to select your available time for the coaching session. Please choose a date and time that works for you.
* **end_time** (end_time, time, optional): This field is used to select the end time of the coaching session. This is optional but helps in scheduling the session accurately.
* **client_name** (client_name, text, required): This field requires your client's name to be filled out accurately.
* **notes** (notes, text, optional): This field is used for any additional notes or comments about the coaching session. It is optional but can be helpful in tailoring the coaching experience to the client's needs.
