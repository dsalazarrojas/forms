# Golf Course Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Course Access Form is designed to collect essential details from golf course visitors, ensuring their needs and preferences are recorded accurately for a more enjoyable and efficient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your player information in the **Player Information** field.
2.  Select your event preferences from the **Event Preferences** field.
3.  Provide your payment details in the **Payment Details** field.
4.  You can add any **Additional Fields** if necessary.
5.  Confirm your details by selecting the correct answer in the **Confirm** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Information (Required)
*   **Player Information (Player Information, text, Required)**: Enter your name, contact number, and any other essential details for our records.
### Event Preferences (Required)
*   **Event Preferences (Event Preferences, text, Required)**: Choose from the list to select the events you are interested in participating in or attending.
### Payment Details (Required)
*   **Payment Details (Payment Details, text, Required)**: Enter the payment amount or method to ensure a smooth transaction.
### Additional Fields (Optional)
*   **Additional Fields (Additional Fields, text, Optional)**: Add any extra details that are relevant to your visit, such as special requests or comments.
### Confirm (Required)
*   **Confirm (Confirm, select multiple, Required)**: Select "True" to confirm you have accurately filled out the form and "False" otherwise.

## Tips
*   Double-check your details before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
