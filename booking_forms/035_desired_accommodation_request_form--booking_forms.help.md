# Desired Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Desired Accommodation Request Form" is used to submit booking requests for travel or stay reservations. It helps the system administrators gather necessary information to process and fulfill these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired accommodation type from the list of available options.
2. Enter the checkin date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Choose the checkin time from the list of options.
4. Enter the checkout date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Select the checkout time from the list of options.
6. Choose the room type(s) that suits your needs.
7. Select the accommodation name from the list of available options.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional message or comments for the booking request.
9. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
10. (Optional) Enter your phone number for additional contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Desired Accommodation Request
* **Desired Accommodation Request** (Required): Enter a brief description of your booking request.
### Accommodation Type (Required)
* **Accommodation Type** (Required): Choose the type of accommodation you require, such as hotel, hostel, or resort.
### Checkin Date (Required)
* **Checkin Date** (Required): Enter the date you want to check in, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
### Checkin Time (Required)
* **Checkin Time** (Required): Select the time you want to check in from the list of available options.
### Checkout Date (Required)
* **Checkout Date** (Required): Enter the date you want to check out, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
### Checkout Time (Required)
* **Checkout Time** (Required): Select the time you want to check out from the list of available options.
### Room Type (Required)
* **Room Type** (Required): Choose the type(s) of room that suits your needs, such as single, double, or family room.
### Accommodation Name (Required)
* **Accommodation Name** (Required): Select the name of the accommodation you're interested in, from the list of available options.
### Message (Optional)
* **Message** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your booking request.
### Email (Optional)
* **Email** (Required for communication): Enter your email address for the system administrators to contact you.
### Phone (Optional)
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
