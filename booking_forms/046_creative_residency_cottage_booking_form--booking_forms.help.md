# Creative Residency Cottage Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for artists and creatives to book a residency at a cottage. It gathers essential information about your stay dates, guests, project details, and other requirements for the residency.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your stay dates in the "Stay Dates" field. This is the period you would like to book the residency for.
2.  Provide your guest details in the "Guest Details" field. Please mention the number of guests and any specific requirements for your guests.
3.  Enter your artist/project name and description in the "Artist Info" and "Project Description" fields, respectively.
4.  Provide your project title in the "Project Title" field.
5.  Choose the type of residency you are interested in by selecting one or more options in the "Residency Type" field. You can select multiple options if you have a specific requirement or interest in more than one type.
6.  Specify your accommodation preferences by selecting one or more options in the "Accommodation Preferences" field. This is where you mention whether you require a single room or shared accommodation, and if you have any specific requirements.
7.  If you have any further requirements or details, please do so in the "Artist Info" field.
8.  Enter your start and end dates, if different from your overall stay dates in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
9.  Finally, provide your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Stay Dates** (stay_dates): The period you would like to book the residency for.
*   **Guest Details** (guest_details): Provide details about your guests, including the number and any specific requirements.
*   **Artist Info** (artist_info): Enter your name and any further information about yourself as an artist.
*   **Project Description** (project_description): Describe your project in a brief paragraph.
*   **Project Title** (project_title): Enter your project title.
*   **Residency Type** (residency_type): Select the type(s) of residency you are interested in, whether digital or traditional.
*   **Accommodation Preferences** (accommodation_preferences): Specify your accommodation preferences, including whether you need a single room or shared accommodation.
*   **Start Date** (start_date): Enter the start date of your residency, if different from your overall stay dates.
*   **End Date** (end_date): Enter the end date of your residency, if different from your overall stay dates.
*   **Email** (email): Provide your email address.
*   **Phone** (phone): Provide your phone number.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Artist Info" field.
*   Double-check your stay dates, accommodation preferences, and project details before submission.
