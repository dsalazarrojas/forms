# Private Swimming Pool Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Private Swimming Pool Booking Form" is designed for customers to book a private swimming pool for events, parties, or other functions. This guide is intended to walk you through each field to ensure accurate and complete submission of your booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you fill in the `Pool Name` field with the name of the pool you wish to book.
3. Choose a start and end date for your event by clicking on the date fields. For the start date, select the date you plan to start your event, and for the end date, select the date you plan to end it.
4. In the `Guest Count` field, type the expected number of guests attending your event.
5. Fill in the `Contact Name` field with the name of the person booking the pool and their contact phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.
6. Enter a valid email address in the `Contact Email` field to enable us to contact you regarding your booking.
7. If necessary, add a message in the `Message` field, e.g., any specific requests you might have or further details about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pool Name** (`pool_name`, text, required): The name of the pool you wish to book. Please ensure it's accurate to guarantee we process your request correctly.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to start your event. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty and we'll discuss further details with you.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the time you plan to start your event. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty and we'll discuss further details with you.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to end your event. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty and we'll discuss further details with you.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Select the time you plan to end your event. If you're unsure, you can leave this field empty and we'll discuss further details with you.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Enter the expected number of guests attending your event. Please be accurate to ensure we prepare adequately.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person booking the pool.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you wish to be contacted about your booking, fill in your contact phone number.
