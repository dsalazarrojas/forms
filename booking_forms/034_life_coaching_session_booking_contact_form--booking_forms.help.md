# life Coaching Session Booking Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users who are interested in booking a life coaching session. Please fill out the form to provide us with your details so we can assist you with your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the First Name field with your name.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. Select a date and time for the coaching session in the Date and Time field.
5. Choose one option from the Select One field.
6. (Optional) Provide any additional notes or requests in the Note field.
7. (Optional) Specify any additional coaching type preferences in the Coaching Type field.
8. Enter a valid email address in the Email Confirmation field to receive confirmation of your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to which we will send the booking confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, optional): Select a date and time for the coaching session.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option to specify the type of coaching you are interested in.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information or requests you have for your coaching session.
* **Coaching Type** (`coaching_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more coaching types that interest you (if you have any specific preferences).
* **Email Confirmation** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive confirmation of your booking.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth booking process.
- If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact us for assistance.
