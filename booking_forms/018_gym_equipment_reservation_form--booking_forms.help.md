# gym_equipment_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **gym_equipment_reservation_form** is used to reserve gym equipment for a specific date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reservation date in the format **MM/DD/YY** (e.g., 02/16/22).
2. Enter the equipment ID number of the machine you wish to reserve.
3. Select the start and end times for your reservation.
4. Enter the name of the equipment being reserved.
5. Enter your user ID, which is likely your account number or ID.
6. Choose the current reservation status of the equipment.
7. Add any notes about your reservation.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Confirm your reservation by selecting "Confirm" and add a note if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date you wish to reserve the equipment.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the ID number of the machine you wish to reserve.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of your reservation.
* **End Time**: Select the end time of your reservation.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment being reserved.
* **User ID**: Enter your account ID or number.
* **Reservation Status**: Choose the status of your reservation. Options are **Reserved** or **Available**.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your reservation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Reservation**: Select **Confirm** to confirm your reservation. If you selected "Not Confirm", add a note explaining why the reservation should not be confirmed.
* **Confirm Reservation Note**: Add any additional comments or reasons why your reservation should not be confirmed.
