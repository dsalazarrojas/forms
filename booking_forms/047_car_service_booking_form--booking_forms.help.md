# Car Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the booking of car services, allowing customers to provide their contact information, vehicle details, and service preferences in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the required fields, which include:
    *   First name
    *   Last name
    *   Email Address
    *   Phone Number
    *   Vehicle Year
    *   Vehicle Make
    *   Vehicle Model
    *   Current Mileage
    *   Preferred Date
    *   Preferred Time
    *   Time Flexibility
    *   Service Type
    *   Service Category
    *   Urgency
    *   Previous Service
    *   Loaner Vehicle Needed
    *   Payment Method
    *   Estimate Preference
    *   Authorization
    *   Diagnostic Authorization
    *   Contact Before Work
    *   Terms Agreement
    *   Booking Date
2.  Fill in any additional information that is relevant to your service booking.
3.  Double-check your form for any required fields that are missing before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name here.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address, which will be used for booking confirmation.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact number for any further assistance or updates.
*   **Vehicle Year** (Required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make** (Required): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Model** (Required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
*   **Current Mileage** (Required): Enter the current mileage reading of your vehicle.
*   **Preferred Date** (Required): Choose a date for your car service booking.
*   **Preferred Time** (Required): Choose a time for your car service booking.
*   **Time Flexibility** (Required): Indicate how flexible you are with your schedule.
*   **Service Type** (Required): Choose the type of service you need.
*   **Service Category** (Required): Choose the category of service you need.
*   **Urgency** (Required): Indicate how urgent your service need is.
*   **Previous Service** (Required): Indicate if you have been to this service provider before.
*   **Loaner Vehicle Needed** (Required): Indicate if you need a loaner vehicle.
*   **Payment Method** (Required): Choose how you would like to pay.
*   **Estimate Preference** (Required): Choose how you would like to receive the estimate.
*   **Authorization** (Required): Indicate if you would like to authorize diagnostic work.
*   **Contact Before Work** (Required): Indicate if you would like to be contacted before any additional work is done.
*   **Terms Agreement** (Required): Agree to the service terms.
*   **Booking Date** (Required): The form will display the current date.
*   **Address** (Optional): Enter your street address for service pickup or drop-off.
*   **City** (Optional): Enter your city for service pickup or drop-off.
*   **State** (Optional): Enter your state for service pickup or drop-off.
*   **ZIP Code** (Optional): Enter your ZIP code for service pickup or drop-off.
*   **Vehicle Information** (Optional): Enter details about your vehicle if necessary.
*   **Vehicle VIN** (Optional): Enter your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number if necessary.
*   **License Plate** (Optional): Enter your vehicle's license plate number if necessary.
*   **Problem Description** (Optional): Describe any issues with your vehicle.
*   **Symptoms** (Optional): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing with your vehicle.
*   **When Problem Occurs** (Optional): Describe when the issue occurs.
*   **Warning Lights** (Optional): Indicate if you have any warning lights on.
*   **Additional Services** (Optional): Choose any additional services you may need.
*   **Alternative Date** (Optional): Choose a second preferred date.
*   **Diagnostic Authorization** (Optional): Indicate if you authorize diagnostic work.
*   **Special Requests** (Optional): Enter any special requirements you have.
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional): Enter any other information.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've filled in all required fields.
*   Use clear and accurate information to avoid errors.
*   Contact the service provider if you have any questions or need further assistance.
