# Book A Room Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
Book a room at our hotel by providing your personal and accommodation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Provide your check-in and check-out dates.
3. Select your preferred room type and bed type.
4. Choose your smoking preference.
5. Choose your preferred floor and view.
6. List any special requests, accessibility needs, and desired room amenities.
7. Let us know if you need parking.
8. Choose your preferred payment method.
9. Provide any promotional code or special occasion.
10. If necessary, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
11. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive communication from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Check-In Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Check-Out Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to leave the hotel.
* **Number of Nights** (`number_of_nights`, number, optional): This field will be calculated based on your check-in and check-out dates.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, optional): Enter the number of children (12 and under) traveling with you.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of room that suits your needs.
* **Bed Type Preference** (`bed_type_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred bed type.
* **Smoking Preference** (`smoking_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your smoking preference.
* **Floor Preference** (`floor_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred floor.
* **Room View Preference** (`room_view_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred room view.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): List any special requests (e.g., late checkout, accessibility needs).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs (e.g., wheelchair accessibility).
* **Desired Room Amenities** (`amenities_desired`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the amenities you would like to have in your room (e.g., WiFi, air conditioning).
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need parking.
* **Breakfast Preference** (`breakfast_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose if you would like to include breakfast.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Promotional Code** (`promotional_code`, text, optional): If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
* **Special Occasion** (`special_occasions`, text, optional): If you are celebrating a special occasion, enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the room booking process, but please contact the hotel for any specific questions or concerns.
