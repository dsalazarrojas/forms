# Batting Lesson Scheduling Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule a batting lesson with a coach based on your player's details and preferences. It captures essential information such as player's name, age, skill level, and specific focus area, as well as scheduling preferences like date, time, and preferred instructor. Additionally, it captures contact details of the parent or guardian (if applicable) and preferred payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter Player's Details**: Fill in the player's full name, age, and select their current skill level from the provided options.
2. **Choose Scheduling Preferences**: Select the date and time slot you prefer for the lesson. You can choose from four available time slots.
3. **Select Preferred Instructor**: Choose a preferred instructor from the available options or select "Any Available Instructor" if you don't have a preference.
4. **Enter Contact Details**: If the player is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number.
5. **Select Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for the lesson (credit card online, cash/check at lesson, or prepaid package credit).
6. **Confirm Cancellation Policy**: Agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter the player's full name.
* **Player Age** (`3`, `number`, `required`): Enter the player's age.
* **Current Skill Level** (`5`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the player's current skill level (beginner, youth recreational, travel/select team, high school varsity, college/professional).
* **Specific Area of Focus** (`6`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the specific area of focus you want to work on (hand-eye coordination, power hitting, contact/bat control, bunting technique, plate discipline).
* **Requested Lesson Date** (`8`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you prefer for the lesson.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`9`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the time slot you prefer for the lesson (3:00-4:00 PM, 4:00-5:00 PM, 5:00-6:00 PM, 6:00-7:00 PM, 7:00-8:00 PM).
* **Preferred Instructor** (`10`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose a preferred instructor or select "Any Available Instructor" if you don't have a preference.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`12`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the parent or guardian (if the player is under 18).
* **Contact Email Address** (`13`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian (if the player is under 18).
* **Contact Phone Number** (`14`, `text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian (if the player is under 18).
* **Payment Method** (`15`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the payment method you prefer (credit card online, cash/check at lesson, prepaid package credit).
* **I Agree to the 24-hour Cancellation Policy** (`16`, `select_one`, `required`): Agree to the 24-hour cancellation policy.
* **Today's Date** (`17`, `date`, `required`): Enter today's date for confirmation.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to proceed with the scheduling process.
