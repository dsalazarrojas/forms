# Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Booking Form is designed to collect user information and tour details for booking purposes. Please fill out this form to provide your details and schedule a tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **User Info** field with your basic contact information.
2.  Enter the tour details in the **Tour Info** field.
3.  Choose a date for the tour using the **Tour Date** field.
4.  Select the tour time using the **Tour Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, or any other relevant contact information to allow us to reach you regarding your tour booking.
*   **Tour Info** (`tour_info`, text, required: false): Describe the tour you are interested in, including any specific activities or services you'd like to book.
*   **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for your tour. If you have a preferred date in mind, please select it. If not, we'll contact you to arrange a suitable date.
*   **Tour Time** (`tour_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your tour. Be as specific as possible to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.
