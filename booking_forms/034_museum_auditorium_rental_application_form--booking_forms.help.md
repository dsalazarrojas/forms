# Museum Auditorium Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for booking the museum's auditorium for events such as meetings, presentations, and exhibitions. It is designed to collect necessary information about the event, including schedule, dates, organizer's information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Event Details` section with a brief description of your event.
2. In the `Schedule` section, select the time slot that works best for your event.
3. Enter the dates for your event in the `Dates` section.
4. Provide your name and contact information in the `Organizer` and `Contact Name`, `Contact Email`, and `Contact Phone` sections.
5. Select the type of organization from the list in the `Organization` section (optional).
6. Add any additional notes about your event in the `Notes` section.
7. Finally, click the `Submit` button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (text, optional): A brief description of your event.
* **Schedule** (time, optional): Select a time slot for your event.
* **Dates** (date, optional): Enter the dates for your event.
* **Organizer** (text, optional): The name of the person or organization responsible for the event.
* **Organization** (select_one, optional): Select the type of organization (e.g., Community Group, School, Other).
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): The name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Any additional notes about your event.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your application.
* **Confirm** (text, optional): This field is not used in the current form configuration, but it is present in case additional functionality is added in the future.

Please note that all fields are optional, but the fields marked as required should be filled out to ensure that the museum can process your application correctly. If you have any issues or questions, please contact us.
