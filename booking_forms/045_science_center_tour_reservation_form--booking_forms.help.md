# science_center_tour_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of booking a science center tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your reservation details in the "Reservation Details" field.
2. Enter the required tour information in the "Tour Information" field.
3. Share your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Details** (text, required): This field asks for the details of your tour reservation, such as date, time, and any specific requirements.
* **Tour Information** (text, required): Provide any necessary information about the tour you'd like to take, including the type of tour or any specific interests.
* **Contact Information** (text, required): Enter your contact information so that the science center can reach you to confirm your reservation and provide any necessary communication.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the science center can tailor the tour to your needs.
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure that the science center can contact you successfully.
