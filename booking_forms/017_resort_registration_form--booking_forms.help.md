# Resort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Registration Form is used to collect information from guests about their stay at the resort. This form is designed to gather essential details about the guest and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's name in the **Guest Name** field.
2. Provide the guest's email address in the **Email Address** field.
3. Enter the guest's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter the date of check-in in the **Check In Date** field.
5. Enter the date of check-out in the **Check Out Date** field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the guest's stay in the **Guest Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): The guest's full name, including their first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): The guest's email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): The guest's phone number, which will be used for communication and emergency contact purposes.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required/optional): The date the guest plans to check-in at the resort.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): The date the guest plans to check-out at the resort.
* **Guest Comment** (`guest_comment`, note, required/optional): Any additional comments or notes about the guest's stay, such as special requests or preferences.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter the correct email address and phone number for the guest to ensure accurate communication.
* If the guest has any special requests or preferences, please include them in the **Guest Comment** field.
* If the guest is staying for multiple dates, please enter the check-in date as the start date and the check-out date as the end date.
* If the guest has any questions or concerns, please contact the resort staff for assistance.
