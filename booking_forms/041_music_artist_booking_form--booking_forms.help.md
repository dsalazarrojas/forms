# Music Artist Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about music artists for booking purposes. It includes fields for the artist's booking information, contact details, promotional details, other details, artist contact information, and booking date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Artist Booking Information**: Enter the artist's name, genre, or any other relevant information in the `artist_booking_info` field.
2. **Contact Details**: In the `contact_details` field, provide the contact person's name, email, or phone number associated with the artist or management team.
3. **Promotional Details**: Describe the artist's promotional strategy, such as social media presence, marketing materials, or any other relevant details in the `promotional_details` field.
4. **Other Details**: Add any additional information about the artist or booking that is not covered in the previous fields in the `other_details` field.
5. **Artist Contact**: Enter the artist's email address or phone number in the `artist_contact` field.
6. **Booking Date**: Select the date the artist is available for booking in the `booking_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Booking Information (artist_booking_info)**: Enter the artist's name, genre, or any other relevant information to help us understand their music and preferences.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Provide the contact person's name, email, or phone number associated with the artist or management team to help us get in touch with them efficiently.
* **Promotional Details (promotional_details)**: Describe the artist's promotional strategy, such as their social media presence, marketing materials, or any other relevant details to help us understand their promotional efforts.
* **Other Details (other_details)**: Add any additional information about the artist or booking that is not covered in the previous fields to provide a more complete picture.
* **Artist Contact (artist_contact)**: Enter the artist's email address or phone number to ensure we can contact them directly.
* **Booking Date (booking_date)**: Select the date the artist is available for booking, so we can schedule the booking accordingly.
