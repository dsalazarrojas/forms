# Beauty Hub Booking Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm client bookings and gather necessary information for service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the booking ID in the "Booking ID" field.
5. Select the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.
6. Select the appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.
7. Select the type of service you have booked in the "Service Type" field.
8. Select the duration of your appointment in the "Appointment Duration" field.
9. Enter the name of your therapist or stylist in the "Therapist or Stylist Name" field.
10. Enter the address of the salon or studio in the "Full Address" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the salon or studio in the "Location Phone Number" field.
12. Select whether parking is available at the location in the "Parking Available" field.
13. Enter the cost of the service in the "Service Cost" field.
14. If a deposit was paid, enter the deposit amount in the "Deposit Paid" field.
15. Select the method of payment used in the "Payment Method" field.
16. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy in the "Cancellation Policy" field.
17. Select "Yes" if you understand the cancellation policy, or "No" otherwise.
18. Select your preferred method of confirmation in the "How Would You Like Confirmation" field.
19. If you have any special requests or notes, enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.
20. Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client who made the booking.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with the booking.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the booking.
* **Booking ID** (`booking_id`, text, required): Enter the booking reference number.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service booked (e.g., Haircut, Color, Treatment, etc.).
* **Appointment Duration** (`duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the appointment (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, etc.).
* **Therapist or Stylist Name** (`therapist_stylist`, text, required): Enter the name of the therapist or stylist assigned to the booking.
* **Salon or Studio Location** (`salon_location`, text, required): Enter the address of the salon or studio where the appointment will be held.
* **Full Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the salon or studio.
* **Location Phone Number** (`phone_location`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the salon or studio.
* **Parking Available** (`parking_available`, select_one, required): Select whether parking is available at the location (Yes - Free, Yes - Paid, Limited, No).
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost of the service.
* **Deposit Paid** (`deposit_paid`, number, optional): If a deposit was paid, enter the amount paid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment used (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_understands`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you understand the cancellation policy, or "No" otherwise.
* **How Would You Like Confirmation** (`confirmation_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of confirmation (e.g., Email, SMS Text, etc.).
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or notes, enter them here.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure accurate and complete booking information.
* If you have any special requests or notes, please enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.
* Review the cancellation policy carefully to ensure you understand the terms.
