# Office Desk Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for users to book office desks in advance. You can use it to reserve desks for meetings, events, or personal use. To book a desk, you will need to provide necessary details such as your name, contact information, desk preferences, and date and time of booking.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name in the "name" field. This will be used to identify you and your booking details.
2.  Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is how we will contact you with booking confirmation and any further information.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This will be used for contact purposes.
4.  Enter the "desk name" or the name of the desk you wish to book. This helps the administrator to find the correct desk in the office.
5.  Enter the "desk id" number. This is the unique number assigned to each desk. If unsure, you can ask the administrator for the correct id.
6.  Enter the "capacity" of the desk. This is the number of people that can occupy the desk.
7.  Enter the "booking date" and "booking time". This is when you plan to book the desk. Please ensure the date and time are correct.
8.  Enter the "duration" of your booking. This is how long you plan to use the desk for.
9.  You can add any "Additional Notes" in the "notes" field. This is for any further information you want to provide to the administrator.
10. Confirm that you wish to book the desk by clicking "confirm". If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask the administrator.
11. Review and confirm your booking details before submitting the form. You can add more "Additional Notes" if needed.
12. Once you have reviewed and confirmed, click "Submit" to finalize your booking.
