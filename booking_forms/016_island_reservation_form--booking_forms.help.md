# Island Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Island Reservation Form is designed to gather information from users to make a reservation for an island. It's essential to provide correct information to ensure accurate processing of the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your booking details in the first section.
2. Choose your accommodation preference (yes or no) in the second section.
3. Enter the name of your chosen accommodation in the third section.
4. Select your dates of stay in the fourth section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details (booking_details)** (`text`, `optional`):
	+ This field is for entering any additional information about your booking.
* **Accommodation Choice (accommodation_choice)** (`select_one`, `optional`):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you want to book an accommodation on the island, or 'No' if you don't.
* **Accommodation Name (accommodation_name)** (`text`, `optional`):
	+ Enter the name of the accommodation you have chosen, if 'Yes' was selected in the previous field.
* **Dates of Stay (dates_of_stay)** (`date`, `optional`):
	+ Select the start and end dates of your stay on the island.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid errors in processing your reservation.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the 'Booking Details' field.
- Double-check your dates of stay to ensure they are correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
