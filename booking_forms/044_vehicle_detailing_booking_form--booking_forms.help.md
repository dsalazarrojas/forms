# Vehicle Detailing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking vehicle detailing services with your vehicle details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of service you would like to book (e.g., Wash, Polish, Detail, Paint, Repair).
2. Enter your vehicle's details: model, year, color, make, and VIN.
3. Provide your contact information: name, email, phone number, address, city, province, country, and postal code.
4. Choose your preferred appointment time and date.
5. Add any special instructions or notes for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Details** (vehicle_details): 
  Enter your vehicle's make and model.
  
- **Vehicle Model** (vehicle_model): Select the model of your vehicle (e.g., Ford, Toyota, Honda).
  
- **Vehicle Year** (vehicle_year): Enter the year of your vehicle.
  
- **Vehicle Colour** (vehicle_colour): Choose the color of your vehicle (e.g., Red, Blue, Green).
  
- **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter your name.
  
- **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter your email address.
  
- **Customer Phone** (customer_phone): Enter your phone number.
  
- **Appointment Time** (appointment_time): Select your preferred appointment time.
  
- **Appointment Date** (appointment_date): Select your preferred appointment date.
  
- **Service Type** (service_type): Select the type of service you would like to book (e.g., Wash, Polish, Detail, Paint, Repair).
  
- **Special Instructions** (special_instructions): Add any special instructions or notes for the service.
  
- **Vehicle Registration** (vehicle_registration): Enter your vehicle's registration number.
  
- **Vehicle Make** (vehicle_make): Enter your vehicle's make.
  
- **Customer Address** (customer_address): Enter your address.
  
- **Customer Postal Code** (customer_postal_code): Enter your postal code.
  
- **Customer City** (customer_city): Enter your city.
  
- **Customer Province** (customer_province): Enter your province.
  
- **Customer Country** (customer_country): Enter your country.
  
- **Customer Zip** (customer_zip): Enter your zip code.
  
- **Vehicle VIN** (vehicle_vin): Enter your vehicle's VIN.
  
- **Customer Note** (customer_note): Add any additional notes or comments.
  
- **Appointment Note** (appointment_note): Add any additional notes or comments for the appointment.
  
- **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type): Select the type of vehicle you have (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle).
  
- **Customer Vehicle Type** (customer_vehicle_type): Select the type of vehicle you have (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle).
  
- **Service Cost** (service_cost): Enter the estimated cost of the service.
