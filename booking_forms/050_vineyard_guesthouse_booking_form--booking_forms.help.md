# Vineyard Guesthouse Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vineyard Guesthouse Booking Form is used to book a stay at the Vineyard Guesthouse. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your booking is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Guest Name in the Guest Name field.
2.  Enter your Email in the Email field.
3.  Enter your Checkin Date in the Checkin Date field.
4.  Enter your Checkout Date in the Checkout Date field.
5.  Select your Guest Type from the options provided.
6.  If you require room service, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Room Service field.
7.  Enter your Arrival Time (if applicable).
8.  Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
9.  Enter any additional Message (if applicable).
10.  Select your preferred Payment Method from the options provided.
11.  Enter your Payment Details.
12.  Enter the Total Cost of your stay.
13.  Enter your Booking ID (will be provided by the system).
14.  Enter the Date Created (will be provided by the system).
15.  Enter the Date Modified (will be provided by the system).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name as it should appear on your booking confirmation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your booking details and any important updates.
* **Checkin Date** (`Checkin Date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check in to your room.
* **Checkout Date** (`Checkout Date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to check out of your room.
* **Guest Type** (`Guest Type`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options provided to indicate whether you are booking for yourself or for another person.
	+ Adult
	+ Child
	+ Senior
* **Room Service** (`Room Service`, select_multiple, required): If you require room service, please select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Arrival Time** (`Arrival Time`, time, optional): If you plan to arrive at the Vineyard Guesthouse at a specific time, enter it here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or information to share with us, enter it here.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Select how you plan to pay for your stay.
	+ Cash
	+ Credit Card
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required): Enter the payment details, such as card number and expiration date.
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of your stay.
* **Booking ID** (`Booking ID`, text, required): Enter the booking ID provided by the system.
* **Date Created** (`Date Created`, date, required): This field will be auto-populated by the system.
* **Date Modified** (`Date Modified`, date, required): This field will be auto-populated by the system.
