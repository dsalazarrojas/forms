# Banquet Hall Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for potential customers to book a banquet hall for their events. It will help our team understand their requirements and preferences to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your inquiry and event.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Choose the type of event you are planning.
4. Select a preferred event date and time.
5. Specify the expected number of guests and the type of layout you require.
6. Provide information on catering, budget, and audio/visual equipment needs.
7. Answer other relevant questions about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Date**: <i> (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required)
	+ This is the date you are inquiring about, and it is essential for planning purposes.
* **Your Name**: <i> (`inquirer_name`, `text`, required)
	+ Please provide your full name as the primary contact person.
* **Email Address**: <i> (`inquirer_email`, `email`, required)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: <i> (`inquirer_phone`, `text`, required)
	+ Provide your phone number to enable us to contact you for further inquiries.
* **Type of Event**: <i> (`event_type`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose the type of event you are planning (e.g., Wedding, Birthday Party, Corporate Event).
* **Preferred Event Date**: <i> (`event_date`, `date`, required)
	+ Select the date you would like to hold your event, considering our available dates.
* **Alternate Dates**: <i> (`alternate_dates`, `text`, optional)
	+ If your preferred date is not available, provide alternate dates you would consider.
* **Event Start Time**: <i> (`event_time`, `time`, required)
	+ Choose the start time of your event from our available options.
* **Expected Event Duration**: <i> (`event_duration`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the expected duration of your event (e.g., 2 Hours, 4 Hours).
* **Expected Number of Guests**: <i> (`guest_count`, `number`, required)
	+ Provide an estimate of the number of guests attending your event.
* **Is Guest Count Flexible?**: <i> (`guest_count_flexible`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ If your guest count may change, select 'Yes' or 'Possibly'.
* **Room Type Preference**: <i> (`room_preference`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Choose the desired layout type for your event (e.g., Theater Style, Banquet/Round Tables).
* **Room Size Preference**: <i> (`room_size_preference`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select the estimated number of guests to determine the required room size.
* **Is Catering Included?**: <i> (`catering_included`, `select_one`, required)
	+ If you plan to use our catering services, select 'Yes'.
* **Budget Per Person**: <i> (`budget_per_person`, `number`, optional)
	+ Provide an estimated budget per guest for your event.
* **Total Budget**: <i> (`total_budget`, `number`, optional)
	+ Enter your approximate total budget for the event.
* **Bar Service Needed?**: <i> (`bar_service`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ If you require full bar service, select 'Yes'.
* **Audio/Visual Equipment**: <i> (`audio_visual`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Choose the audio/visual equipment you require for your event (e.g., Projector and Screen, Sound System).
* **Parking Important?**: <i> (`parking_available`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ If parking is a concern for your guests, select 'Yes'.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: <i> (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, optional)
	+ Specify any accessibility requirements you may have for your event.
* **Additional Questions or Requests**: <i> (`additional_questions`, `text`, optional)
	+ If you have any further questions or requests, please provide them here.
