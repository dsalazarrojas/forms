# Animal Training Workshop Scheduling Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a workshop for animal training. This form is intended for organizations, animal shelters, and other groups that want to schedule a workshop for their staff or participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure that you have all the required information ready. This includes your name, email address, phone number, organization name, organization type, workshop type, desired duration, expected number of participants, participant skill level, and budget range.
2.  Fill in your name, email address, phone number, and organization name in the corresponding fields.
3.  Select the type of organization you belong to from the options provided.
4.  Choose the type of workshop you are looking for, the duration of the workshop, and the number of participants.
5.  Indicate the experience level of the participants and if you are planning to bring your dogs or not.
6.  Select the preferred start and end dates for the workshop, the time of day you prefer, and the day of the week.
7.  Choose the location type, provide the address of your facility, and indicate if there is parking available.
8.  Specify the topics you want to cover in the workshop, the learning objectives, and any specialized needs or requests.
9.  Indicate if you will need meals or refreshments, overnight accommodations, and any accessibility requirements.
10.  Specify your budget range for the workshop.
11.  If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name (1)**: Enter your full name.
*   **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Organization Name (4)**: Enter the name of the organization hosting the workshop.
*   **Organization Type (5)**: Select the type of organization you belong to from the options provided:
    *   Animal Shelter
    *   Dog Training Business
    *   Rescue Organization
    *   Educational Institution
    *   Corporate Team Building
    *   Community Center
    *   Other
*   **Workshop Details (6)**: This is a note field where you can provide more information about the workshop.
*   **Type of Training Workshop (7)**: Choose the type of workshop you are looking for:
    *   Basic Dog Obedience
    *   Advanced Behavior Training
    *   Puppy Training
    *   Reactive Dog Training
    *   Agility Training
    *   Service Dog Training
    *   Shelter Staff Training
    *   Other
*   **Desired Workshop Duration (8)**: Select the duration of the workshop:
    *   Half day (4 hours)
    *   Full day (8 hours)
    *   Two days
    *   Weekend workshop
    *   Custom duration
*   **Expected Number of Participants (9)**: Enter the number of participants you expect.
*   **Participant Skill Level (10)**: Select the experience level of the participants:
    *   Beginner
    *   Intermediate
    *   Advanced
    *   Mixed
*   **Dogs for Workshop (11)**: Indicate if you plan to bring your dogs or not:
    *   Participants bring their own dogs
    *   Facility provides dogs
    *   Mix of both
    *   Not applicable
*   **Preferred Scheduling (12)**: This is a note field where you can provide more information about your preferred scheduling.
*   **Preferred Start Date (13)**: Enter the earliest date you can start the workshop.
*   **Preferred End Date (14)**: Enter the latest date you can accept for the workshop.
*   **Preferred Time of Day (15)**: Select the time of day you prefer:
    *   Early Morning (6-9am)
    *   Morning (9am-12pm)
    *   Afternoon (12-3pm)
    *   Late Afternoon (3-6pm)
    *   Evening (6pm+)
    *   Flexible
*   **Preferred Day of Week (16)**: Select the days of the week you prefer:
    *   Monday
    *   Tuesday
    *   Wednesday
    *   Thursday
    *   Friday
    *   Saturday
    *   Sunday
*   **Workshop Location Type (17)**: Choose the location type:
    *   Your facility
    *   My facility
    *   Virtual
    *   Outdoor location
    *   Other location
*   **Your Facility Address (18)**: If you choose your facility, enter the address here.
*   **Facility Size and Amenities (19)**: Describe the size and amenities of your facility.
*   **Parking Available (20)**: Indicate if there is parking available:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   Limited
*   **Workshop Content Preferences (21)**: This is a note field where you can provide more information about your workshop content preferences.
*   **Specific Topics to Cover (22)**: Specify the topics you want to cover in the workshop.
*   **Learning Objectives (23)**: Describe the learning objectives you hope the participants will achieve.
*   **Specialized Needs or Requests (24)**: Indicate any specialized needs or requests for the workshop.
*   **Logistics and Accommodations (25)**: This is a note field where you can provide more information about the logistics and accommodations.
*   **Meals or Refreshments Needed (26)**: Indicate if you need meals or refreshments:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   Provide your own
*   **Overnight Accommodation Needed (27)**: Indicate if you need overnight accommodations:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   Some participants
*   **Accessibility Requirements (28)**: Describe any accessibility requirements for the participants.
*   **Budget Range for Workshop (29)**: Select the budget range you are willing to spend:
    *   Under $1000
    *   $1000-$2500
    *   $2500-$5000
    *   $5000-$10000
    *   $10000+
    *   Flexible
*   **Trainer Preferences (30)**: This is a note field where you can provide more information about your trainer preferences.
*   **Desired Trainer Experience Level (31)**: Select the experience level of the trainer:
    *   Entry Level
    *   Intermediate (5-10 years)
    *   Advanced (10+ years)
    *   Certified Professional
    *   No preference
*   **Trainer Specialization (32)**: Describe the specialization of the trainer.
*   **Additional Comments or Questions (33)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the workshop.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields to avoid any delays in processing your request.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing information about the workshop details to ensure that the trainer can tailor the workshop to your needs.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
