# Accommodation Management Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who are inquiring about accommodation management. It's meant for customers or clients who are looking to book or rent a property. Fill out this form to provide your details and preferences, and our team will be happy to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose a date for your inquiry.
3. Select the type of accommodation you are interested in (e.g., Hotel Reservation, Long-term Lease, etc.).
4. Provide the name of the property you are inquiring about.
5. Choose your preferred move-in date.
6. Indicate the length of your stay.
7. Select the number of occupants.
8. Choose your accommodation preferences (e.g., studio, 1-bedroom, furnished, etc.).
9. Enter your monthly budget range.
10. If applicable, provide any special requirements (e.g., pet-friendly, wheelchair accessible).
11. Choose your employment status.
12. Indicate whether you can provide income verification.
13. Confirm if you have references available.
14. Select your preferred method of communication.
15. Choose how urgent your accommodation needs are.
16. If applicable, provide any additional inquiries or comments.
17. Specify how you found out about this accommodation.
18. Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquirer Full Name** (`inquirer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, as per your identification documents.
* **Inquirer Email** (`inquirer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for responses.
* **Inquirer Phone** (`inquirer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of accommodation you are inquiring about (e.g., Hotel Reservation, Long-term Lease, etc.).
* **Property of Interest** (`property_of_interest`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Preferred Move In Date** (`preferred_move_in_date`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred move-in date.
* **Length of Stay** (`length_of_stay`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the length of your stay (e.g., Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, etc.).
* **Number of Occupants** (`number_of_occupants`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people who will be staying with you.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of accommodation you are looking for (e.g., studio, 1-bedroom, furnished, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Enter your monthly budget range for accommodation.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide any special requirements you have (e.g., pet-friendly, wheelchair accessible).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your employment status.
* **Income Verification** (`income_verification`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you can provide income verification.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have references available.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, text message).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose how urgent your accommodation needs are.
* **Additional Inquiries** (`additional_inquiries`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide any additional inquiries or comments.
* **Source of Information** (`source_of_information`, `select_one`, required): Specify how you found out about this accommodation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
