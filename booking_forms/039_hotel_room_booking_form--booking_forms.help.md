# Hotel Room Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to book a hotel room for a specific period. Please fill it out carefully to ensure that your booking request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- To book a hotel room, simply fill out this form with your required information.
- Make sure to include your contact email address so we can get back to you about your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, including any middle names or titles.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred room type from Hotel Room, Suite Room, or Other Room Types (please specify the type if you select this option).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests that will be occupying the room (including yourself).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for us to contact you about your booking.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
* **Booking Message** (`booking_message`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions for your booking.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your arrival and departure dates for accuracy.
- If you have any special requests, such as a room with a view or early check-in, please specify them in the Booking Message field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
