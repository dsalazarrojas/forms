# Custom Date Selection VillaVac - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for custom date selection for VillaVac services. It allows users to input specific dates for booking and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the villavac_form_data field with any relevant details about your booking.
2. Select a custom date for the booking using the villavac_custom_date field.
3. Choose a price for the booking using the villavac_price field.
4. Select the currency for the price using the villavac_price_currency field.
5. Select the payment method(s) you would like to use for the booking using the villavac_payment_method field. You can also provide additional information about your payment method in the villavac_payment_method_other field.
6. Optionally, add any notes about your booking in the villavac_note field.
7. Enter your email address in the villavac_email field.
8. Enter your phone number in the villavac_phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **villavac_form_data** (text): Enter any relevant details about your booking.
* **villavac_custom_date** (date): Select a custom date for the booking.
* **villavac_price** (number): Choose a price for the booking.
* **villavac_price_currency** (select one): Select the currency for the price.
* **villavac_payment_method** (select multiple): Select the payment method(s) you would like to use for the booking.
* **villavac_payment_method_other** (text): Optionally provide additional information about your payment method if not listed above.
* **villavac_note** (note): Add any notes about your booking.
* **villavac_email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **villavac_phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
