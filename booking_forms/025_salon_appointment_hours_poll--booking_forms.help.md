# salon_appointment_hours_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The salon_appointment_hours_poll form is designed to help salon clients confirm their availability and preferences for an appointment. It consists of a series of questions that gather crucial information to ensure a smooth booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Time Slot**: Check the box next to "Yes" to indicate if you are available to book a time slot.
2. **Select Available Hours**: Choose the hours of operation that you are available to visit the salon.
3. **Select Preferred Hours**: Select your preferred hours for the appointment.
4. **Provide Note**: If you have any specific requests or comments, enter them in the text area.
5. **Enter Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your appointment schedule.
6. **Enter Salon Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the salon you are interested in visiting.
7. **Select Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you would like to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Time Slot**: 
	+ This question asks if you are available to book a time slot for your appointment.
* **Select Available Hours**: 
	+ Choose the hours of operation that you are available to visit the salon.
* **Select Preferred Hours**: 
	+ Select your preferred hours for the appointment.
* **Note**: 
	+ Enter any specific requests or comments you have regarding your appointment.
* **Client Email**: 
	+ Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your appointment schedule.
* **Salon Phone Number**: 
	+ Enter the phone number of the salon you are interested in visiting.
* **Select Date and Time**: 
	+ Enter the date and time you would like to schedule your appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the salon directly.
* We will get back to you with your appointment schedule as soon as possible.
