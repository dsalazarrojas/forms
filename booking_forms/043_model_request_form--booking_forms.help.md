# model_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The model_request_form is designed to collect information from users for a specific purpose, such as requesting a model or a specific item. This form will help us understand your request and ensure we can provide the correct information to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "form information" page and fill out the form title.
2. On the subsequent pages, fill out the relevant fields as needed. For the "body size" field, you can enter a numerical value.
3. For the "body size unit" field, select the unit of measurement for your body size from the dropdown options (e.g., inch, cm, foot).
4. For the "applicant information" field, select "Yes" if you are the applicant or "No" otherwise.
5. You can add up to six photos to support your request.
6. Fill out the contact information, if applicable.
7. Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted.
8. Provide the address, if applicable.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the "note" field.
10. Enter the date for photoswipe and noteswipe.
11. Finally, fill out the "body size" field with the corresponding numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form information** (text, required): This is the title of the form you are filling out.
* **body size** (number, optional): Enter the numerical value of your body size.
* **body size unit** (select_one, required): Select the unit of measurement for your body size (e.g., inch, cm, foot).
* **photo 1** (text, optional): You can upload up to 6 photos to support your request.
* **photo 2** (text, optional): Enter the description for the second photo.
* **photo 3** (text, optional): Enter the description for the third photo.
* **photo 4** (text, optional): Enter the description for the fourth photo.
* **photo 5** (text, optional): Enter the description for the fifth photo.
* **photo 6** (text, optional): Enter the description for the sixth photo.
* **applicant information** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are the applicant or "No" otherwise.
* **contact** (text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted.
* **address** (text, optional): Enter your address, if applicable.
* **note** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **photoswipe** (date, optional): Enter the date when you took the photos.
* **noteswipe** (date, optional): Enter the date when the notes were taken.
* **weight** (number, optional): Enter the weight value for your body.
* **height** (number, optional): Enter the height value for your body.
* **photo 3** (text, optional): You can upload up to 6 photos to support your request.
* **photo 4** (text, optional): Enter the description for the fourth photo.
* **photo 5** (text, optional): Enter the description for the fifth photo.
