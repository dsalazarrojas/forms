# Portable AC Unit Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Portable AC Unit Booking Form is used to book a portable air conditioning unit for a specific time period. It is designed to capture customer information, unit details, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Customer Name and Email.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the Start Date and End Date for the unit rental period.
4. Choose the type of Unit you are interested in booking.
5. Specify the size of the unit you need.
6. If you have any special requests, please mention them in the Special Request field.
7. Select your preferred Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
8. Enter the Total Cost for your booking (this will be calculated based on your selections).
9. Choose the status of the unit you are booking (Available, Unavailable, or Reserved).
10. Specify your Customer Type (optional).
10. Enter the address of the unit you are booking (if different from your current location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your booking confirmation.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive booking confirmations and updates.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Start Date**: Select the date you would like to start using the portable AC unit.
- **End Date**: Select the date you would like to return the portable AC unit.
- **Unit Type**: Choose the type of portable AC unit you are interested in booking (e.g., window unit, portable air handler, etc.).
- **Unit Size**: Specify the size of the unit you need (e.g., small, medium, large).
- **Special Request**: If you have any specific requirements or requests, please mention them here.
- **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
- **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost for your booking (this will be calculated based on your selections).
- **Unit Status**: Choose the status of the unit you are booking (Available, Unavailable, or Reserved).
- **Customer Type**: If you are a business or residential customer, please specify your type here (optional).
- **Unit Address**: Enter the address of the unit you are booking (if different from your current location).
