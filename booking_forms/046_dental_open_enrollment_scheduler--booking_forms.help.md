# Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler" form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to enroll in a dental program. This form will help us to understand their needs and schedule appointments accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Business Name: This is the name of the business or organization you represent.
	* Business URL: This is the URL of your business or organization.
	* Contact Email: This is the email address of the contact person.
	* Contact Phone: This is the phone number of the contact person.
	* First Name: This is your first name.
	* Last Name: This is your last name.
	* Date Of Birth: This is your date of birth.
	* Social Security Number: This is your Social Security number.
	* Preferred Date: This is the date you prefer to enroll in the program.
2. Select your available start times:
	* You will be asked to select one or more of the available start times: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM, 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM, or 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM.
3. Add any additional notes:
	* This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This is the name of the business or organization you represent.

### Business URL
* **Business URL** (`business_url`, text, required): This is the URL of your business or organization.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of the contact person.

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your last name.

### Date Of Birth
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): This is your date of birth.

### Social Security Number
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): This is your Social Security number.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is your phone number.

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): This is the date you prefer to enroll in the program.

### Available Start Times
* **Available Start Times** (`available_start_times`, select_multiple, required): You will be asked to select one or more of the available start times: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM, 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM, or 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us process your enrollment request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can also reach out to us if you need to make any changes to your enrollment request.
