# Vacation Rental Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture payment information and details associated with a vacation rental booking. It is an important tool for accurately processing and managing rental payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not the guest will be providing payment information.
3. Enter the payment information in the text field.
4. Enter the rental details in the text field.
5. Select a date for the rental date.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the payment details such as credit card information or bank account number.
  - **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not the guest will be providing payment information. Select "Yes" if the guest will provide payment information, and "No" otherwise.
  - **Rental Details** (`rental_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the rental such as the property address or rental agreement number.
  - **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the rental in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
