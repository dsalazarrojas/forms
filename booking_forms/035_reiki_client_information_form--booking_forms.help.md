# reiki_client_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reiki_client_information_form is used to collect client information for Reiki sessions. Please fill out this form to provide us with your details so we can tailor the experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter your Phone Number so we can contact you.
3. Provide your Address to ensure we can reach you.
4. If you have a preferred Date of Birth, please enter it. If not, it's optional.
5. Select your preferred Reiki Practitioner from the list.
6. Choose your Client Type (New or Existing).
7. If you want, provide the length and number of Sessions Desired.
8. Select a Date and Time for your Reiki sessions.
9. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the Note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as per your official identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as per your official identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your current address for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): If you want to share your date of birth, this is the place to do it.
* **Reiki Practitioner** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred Reiki Practitioner from the list.
* **Client Type** (select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you are a New or Existing client.
* **Session Length** (number, optional): If you want to specify the length of your Reiki sessions, this is where you can do it.
* **Sessions Desired** (number, optional): If you want to let us know how many Reiki sessions you want, provide this information here.
* **Date and Time** (date, required): Select a date and time for your Reiki sessions.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences or needs.
