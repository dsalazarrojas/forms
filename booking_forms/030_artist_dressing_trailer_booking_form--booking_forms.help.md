# Artist Dressing Trailer Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your event and trailer needs to ensure we provide the correct trailers for your artist or performers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information section, including your name, email, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you to confirm your booking.
2. Provide the event name, artist or performer name, and type of event.
3. Specify the event date, trailer setup date, and pickup date. Make sure to include the total number of days you'll need the trailer.
4. Choose the type of trailer needed, number of trailers, and any special requirements or amenities.
5. Enter the venue location and parking requirements, if any.
6. If applicable, provide your estimated budget for the trailer rental and confirm if you will provide insurance.
7. Finally, add any additional notes or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person Name**: The name of the person making the booking.
* **Email Address**: Primary contact email for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for any questions or updates.
* **Organization or Company Name**: Production company, event venue, or tour company name.
* **Event Name**: Name of the event or show.
* **Artist or Performer Name**: Primary artist or band name.
* **Type of Event**: Select the type of event from the dropdown menu to help us understand your needs.
* **Event Date**: When the event is scheduled to take place.
* **Trailer Setup Date**: When the trailer needs to arrive at the venue.
* **Trailer Pickup Date**: When the trailer needs to be picked up after the event.
* **Total Number of Days Needed**: How many days you'll need the trailer.
* **Type of Trailer Needed**: Choose the type of trailer that suits your artist or performer's needs.
* **Number of Trailers Needed**: How many trailers you require.
* **Special Requirements or Amenities**: Any specific features or equipment your artist or performer needs.
* **Venue Location or Address**: Full address where the trailer will be delivered.
* **Parking or Setup Space Constraints**: Any space limitations or requirements at the venue.
* **Budget Range for Trailer Rental (GBP)**: Estimated budget for trailer rental.
* **Will You Provide Insurance?**: Confirm if you'll provide insurance or need rental insurance.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests**: Any other information you'd like to share with us.
