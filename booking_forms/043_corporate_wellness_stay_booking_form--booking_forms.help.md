# Corporate Wellness Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Wellness Stay Booking Form is used for employees to book a corporate wellness stay. Please fill out this form with your details and preferences so we can tailor the wellness program to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with your personal details.
2. Select your preferred booking date.
3. Choose a time that suits you.
4. Specify the number of people you are booking for.
5. Enter a contact number in case we need to reach you.
6. Add any special messages or requests you have.
7. Select the assigned tool for your wellness stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your wellness stay.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your wellness stay.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to book the wellness stay for.
* **Number of people** (`number_of_people`, number, optional): Specify the number of people you are booking for.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Add any special messages or requests you have.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the assigned tool for your wellness stay. (Options: chatjimmy, chatfrank)
