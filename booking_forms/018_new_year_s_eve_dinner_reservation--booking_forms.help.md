# new_year_s_eve_dinner_reservation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a dinner reservation for New Year's Eve. It helps our team to collect information about your guest's details, preferences, and contact information to prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm if you are bringing a party to the dinner.
2. Choose a date and time for your reservation.
3. Fill in any special requests or requirements for your party.
4. Provide a contact phone number for the person making the reservation.
5. Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
6. Enter the guest's name.
7. Enter the guest's phone number.
8. Enter the guest's email address.
9. Enter any additional information or notes about your guest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Years Eve Dinner Reservation** (`title`, text, required): Enter the name of the dinner reservation you would like to book.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, required): Please provide your guest's details for the dinner reservation.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are bringing a party to the dinner. If "Yes" is selected, we will need to know how many people are in your party.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, date, required): Choose a date and time for your dinner reservation.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements for your party, please let us know.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide a contact phone number for the person making the reservation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person making the reservation.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the guest's name.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, text, optional): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your guest.
