# Cruise Line Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Line Contract Form is a document that captures critical information for booking and confirming travel arrangements. It's designed to ensure that all necessary details are collected and verified in one place, making the booking process more efficient and reducing errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately**.
2. **Make sure each field is completed before proceeding**.
3. **Review and correct any mistakes before submitting**.
4. **Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Details**
	+ **Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	+ **Email** (Text): Enter your valid email address.
* **Payment Options**
	+ **Yes/No** (Select Multiple): Choose your preferred payment options.
* **Travel Dates**
	+ **Travel Dates** (Select One): Select a date range for your travel dates.
* **Contract Terms** (Note): Enter any contract-related terms or agreements.
* **Special Requests** (Note): Enter any special requests or requirements.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One): Choose your preferred payment term.
* **Passenger Confirmation**
	+ **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for passenger confirmation.
* **Agency Details**
	+ **Agency Details** (Text): Enter your agency's details or information.

## Tips
* Review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Double-check your contact information to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your travel agent or cruise line representative.
