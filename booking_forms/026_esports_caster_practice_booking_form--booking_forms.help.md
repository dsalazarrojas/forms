# esports_caster_practice_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to book a practice session with our caster.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your contact details.
2. Specify your game preferences.
3. Indicate your experience level and practice time.
4. Choose your preferred practice time.
5. Select the type of game you want to practice.
6. Specify the number of practice sessions you need.
7. Add any additional notes about your practice sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (id: 1)** (`contact_details`, text, required): Fill out your name, email, and phone number to get in touch with us for your practice session.
* **Game Preferences (id: 2)** (`game_preferences`, text, required): Specify the type of games you're interested in practicing (e.g., FPS, MOBA, etc.).
* **Experience Level (id: 3)** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level: High or Low.
* **Practice Time (id: 4)** (`practice_time`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have time to practice: Yes or No.
* **Preferred Time (id: 5)** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Choose when you prefer to practice: Yes or No.
* **Game Type (id: 6)** (`game_type`, select_multiple, required): Select which games you'd like to practice (e.g., FPS, MOBA, etc.).
* **Sessions (id: 7)** (`practice_sessions`, number, required): Specify the number of sessions you need (e.g., 1, 2, etc.).
* **Notes (id: 8)** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional information about your practice sessions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting your game preferences and experience level.
* If you need help with any of these fields, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review your form submission and get back to you with more information about your practice session.
