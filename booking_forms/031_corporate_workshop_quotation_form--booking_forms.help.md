# corporate_workshop_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate workshop quotation form is designed to gather information from customers or prospects to generate quotations for corporate workshops. Fill out this form to provide your company's details, workshop requirements, and pricing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your company's name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number.
3. Select the service option that best suits your requirements.
4. Enter the date and time of the event.
5. Specify the duration of the event.
6. Choose your pricing option(s).
7. Add any additional notes about your workshop requirements.
8. If applicable, assign a specific tool or software for the workshop.
9. Enter the output file name.
10. Provide a unique identifier for your form submission (optional, but recommended for record-keeping purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name to help us understand who the workshop is for.
* **Email Address**: Your company email address will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your company's phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Service Option**: Select the type of service you're interested in for your workshop (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Date of Event**: Enter the date of the workshop or event.
* **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the workshop or event.
* **Duration of Event**: Specify the length of the workshop or event.
* **Pricing Option**: Choose the pricing option that suits your needs (you can select multiple options if needed).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or requirements about your workshop.
* **Assigned Tool**: If specific tools or software are required for the workshop, please specify.
* **Output File**: Enter a name for the output file (if applicable).
* **Form IDs**: This field is for internal use; you can leave it blank unless instructed otherwise.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
- You can submit multiple forms if you need to book multiple workshops or events.
