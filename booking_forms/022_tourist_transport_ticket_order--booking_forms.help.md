# Tourist Transport Ticket Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourist Transport Ticket Order form is designed to collect essential information from tourists who wish to book a transport ticket for their trip. This form gathers details about the user's identity, travel plans, and ticket preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **User Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your booking.
2. **Route**: Select the route you would like to book a ticket for.
3. **Travel Dates**: Enter your preferred travel dates.
4. **Ticket Payments**: Select whether you would like to pay for your ticket now or at a later time.
5. **Confirm Booking**: Confirm your booking request.
6. **Confirm Dropoff Location**: Select the dropoff locations you would like to use.
7. **Confirm Dropoff Time**: Enter the time you would like to drop off.
8. **Confirm Dropoff Date**: Enter the date you would like to drop off.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your booking.
* **Route** (`route`, select_multiple, required): Select the route you would like to book a ticket for.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): Enter your preferred travel dates.
* **Ticket Payments** (`ticket_payments`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to pay for your ticket now or at a later time.
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, checkbox, required): Confirm your booking request.
* **Confirm Dropoff Location** (`confirm_dropoff_location`, select_multiple, required): Select the dropoff locations you would like to use.
* **Confirm Dropoff Time** (`confirm_dropoff_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to drop off.
* **Confirm Dropoff Date** (`confirm_dropoff_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to drop off.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.
