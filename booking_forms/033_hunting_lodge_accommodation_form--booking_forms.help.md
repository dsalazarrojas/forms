# Hunting Lodge Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage accommodations at a hunting lodge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your arrival at the lodge in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
2. Enter the date of your departure from the lodge in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Enter your guest's name as it appears on their identification.
4. Provide your contact details, including any relevant phone number and email.
5. Select the room types you are interested in booking from the list.
6. Select the amenities you require for your stay from the list.
7. Choose the type of accommodation you desire (if not already selected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Arrivel Date (1) (Required)
* The date you plan to arrive at the lodge.

### Departure Date (2) (Required)
* The date you plan to leave the lodge.

### Guest Name (3) (Optional)
* Your guest's name as it appears on their identification.

### Contact Details (4) (Optional)
* Your phone number and email address, in case we need to contact you.

### Phone (5) (Optional)
* A secondary phone number to contact you.

### Email (6) (Optional)
* A secondary email address to contact you.

### Room Types (7) (Optional)
* Select all room types you would like to book:
	+ Single room
	+ Double room
	+ Suite
	+ Twin room

### Room Amenities (8) (Optional)
* Select all amenities you require:
	+ Free Wi-Fi
	+ Free parking
	+ Free breakfast
	+ TV in room
	+ Balcony
	+ Gym
	+ Spa
	+ Air conditioning
	+ Mini bar
	+ View

### Accommodation Type (9) (Optional)
* Choose the type of accommodation:
	+ Shared Accommodation
	+ Private Accommodation
