# rv_parking_spot_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The RV Parking Spot Reservation Form is a tool to facilitate guests' parking spot reservations at a park or recreation center. It gathers essential information for both guests and parks to ensure smooth reservation, payment, and administration processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired date and time for your reservation.
2. Enter your guest details, including name, phone number, and email.
3. Provide your payment information, including payment method and status.
4. Fill in the parking spot details, including reservation ID and spot ID.
5. Confirm your reservation and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RV Parking Spot Form** (`rv_parking_spot_form`): This field is not a standard input field, but rather a label for the first page of the form. It indicates that the form is for reserving a parking spot.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to provide essential contact information.
* **Reservation Info** (`reservation_info`): Enter a brief description of your reservation, such as the purpose or type of event.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`): Select the date of your reservation using the calendar widget. The format should be in MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`): Select the time of your reservation using the time input field. The format should be in 12-hour clock (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Spot Details** (`spot_details`): Enter any additional details about the parking spot, such as special requests or requirements.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`): Enter your guest's name to help the park management identify and contact them.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`): Enter your guest's phone number to ensure they can be reached in case of questions or emergencies.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`): Enter your guest's email address to send them reservation confirmations or updates.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`): Enter any relevant payment information, such as payment method or payment status.
* **Fee** (`fee`): Enter the total fee for the parking spot reservation.
* **Total Fees** (`total_fees`): Enter the total fees paid for the parking spot reservation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method of payment, such as cash, credit card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the status of payment, such as paid, pending, or not paid.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the reservation.
* **Reservation ID** (`reservation_id`): Enter the reservation ID for internal park management purposes.
* **Spot ID** (`spot_id`): Enter the parking spot ID to identify the reserved parking spot.
* **User ID** (`user_id`): Enter your user ID to track your activity on the park's system.
* **Park Name** (`park_name`): Enter the name of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Address** (`park_address`): Enter the address of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Phone** (`park_phone`): Enter the phone number of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Email** (`park_email`): Enter the email address of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Hours** (`park_hours`): Enter the operating hours of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Description** (`park_description`): Enter a brief description of the park or recreation center.
* **Park Rate** (`park_rate`): Enter the rate or fee for parking at the park or recreation center.
