# Schedule A Demo Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Schedule A Demo Session" form is used to schedule demo sessions with the company. Please fill out the form below to request a meeting with a demo session.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Demo Session Request** in the text field.
2. Select one or more **Selected Demos** from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose a suitable **Meeting Time** in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`, e.g., `02:30 PM`.
4. Enter the **Meeting Duration** in minutes, e.g., `60` for 1 hour.
5. Select the desired **Meeting Date** in the `YYYY-MM-DD` format, e.g., `2022-01-01`.
6. Enter your **Contact Person**'s name.
7. Fill out your **Company** name.
8. Enter your **Job Title**.
9. Enter your **Email** address. This must be a valid email address.
10. Enter your **Phone** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Demo Session Request** (`demo_session_request`, text, optional): Enter a short description of the demo session you are requesting.
  - This field is for your convenience, please use it to briefly describe the meeting you want to schedule.
- **Selected Demos** (`selected_demos`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more demo sessions to meet with.
  - Select one or more demos to schedule meetings for. Please choose the sessions that interest you the most.
- **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Choose a suitable meeting time.
  - Choose a meeting time in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`.
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, optional): Enter the meeting duration in minutes.
  - Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
- **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Select the desired meeting date.
  - Select the date for the meeting from the calendar.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
  - Enter the name of the person who will be attending the meeting.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
  - Enter your company's name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
  - Enter your job title.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
  - Enter a valid email address associated with your company.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields as accurately as possible to ensure the meeting goes as smoothly as possible.
- If you've filled out this form before, it's best to contact the company to confirm that the previous meeting was successful.
- If you're having trouble filling out the form, please contact the company's support team for assistance.
