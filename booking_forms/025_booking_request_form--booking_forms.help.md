# Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Booking Request Form is a crucial part of the guest's interaction with the accommodation. It provides essential information needed to prepare for the guest's stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Plan your stay:
	* Enter the date you plan to arrive in the "Arrival Date" field.
	* Enter the date you plan to leave in the "Departure Date" field.
4. Specify your room preferences:
	* Select the preferred room type from the "Preferred Room Type" dropdown menu.
	* Enter the total number of people in your group in the "Number of Guests" field.
5. Arrange airport transfers (if necessary):
	* If you need an airport transfer, select the relevant option from the "Airport Transfer Needed" dropdown menu.
	* If you've selected "Yes, arrival only", enter the flight number in the "Arrival Flight Number" field.
	* If you've selected "Yes, departure only" or "Yes, both", you can optionally enter the flight number for both "Arrival Flight Number" and "Departure Flight Number" fields.
6. Make any special requests (if necessary):
	* Enter any special needs or preferences in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your given name. Please enter it as per your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Your surname. Please enter it as per your official identification documents.
* **Email Address**: The email address we will use to send confirmations and communications. Please make sure it's accurate.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number. We may contact you through this number for any urgent matters.
* **Arrival Date**: The date on which you plan to arrive at the accommodation. Use YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Departure Date**: The date on which you plan to leave the accommodation. Use YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Number of Guests**: The total number of people in your group.
* **Preferred Room Type**: Choose the type of room you would like to be accommodated in. Please refer to the "Additional Services" field for any room service preferences.
* **Number of Rooms**: The number of rooms you require. Please note that this should be equal to or more than the number of guests.
* **Airport Transfer Needed**: If you need a transfer from or to the airport, please select "Yes" and follow the instructions below.
* **Arrival Flight Number**: If you've selected "Yes, arrival only", enter the flight number that you will be on. Please note that this is optional.
* **Arrival Time**: If you've selected "Yes, arrival only", enter the time of your arrival. This is optional and used to schedule the transfer accordingly.
* **Departure Flight Number**: If you've selected "Yes, departure only" or "Yes, both", enter the flight number that you will be on. This is optional.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or preferences (e.g., room service, late check-out), please enter them here.
* **Additional Services Needed**: If you require any additional services (e.g., room service, late check-out), please select them from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- Ensure you enter accurate contact information for communication.
- If you have any special requests, please be as specific as possible.
- If you select any additional services, ensure you've considered the associated costs.
