# Car Rental Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental Customer Check In Form is a crucial document that allows customers to provide essential details about their rental experience. This form helps the rental staff to efficiently process the check-in and check-out of vehicles, ensuring that the customer's needs are met and that the vehicle is returned in good condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact details in the "Customer Information" section.
2.  Provide accurate details for your vehicle preferences, including vehicle category, make and model, color, and VIN.
3.  Confirm your reservation details, including reservation number and check-in date.
4.  Choose your payment method and provide any additional payment details.
5.  Select any additional services or add-ons required for your rental.
6.  Confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions, including fuel policy and mileage policy.
7.  Sign and confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**:
    *   `first_name`: Enter your first name.
    *   `last_name`: Enter your last name.
    *   `email`: Enter your contact email address.
    *   `phone`: Enter your contact phone number.
    *   `address`: Enter your street address.
    *   `city`: Enter your city.
    *   `state`: Enter your state.
    *   `zip_code`: Enter your ZIP code.
    *   `date_of_birth`: Enter your date of birth (must be 21 or older).
*   **License Information**:
    *   `license_number`: Enter your driver's license number.
    *   `license_state`: Enter the state that issued your license.
    *   `license_country`: Enter the country of issuance.
    *   `license_expiry`: Enter your license expiration date.
*   **Reservation Information**:
    *   `reservation_number`: Enter your reservation confirmation number.
    *   `check_in_date`: Enter your check-in date.
    *   `check_in_time`: Enter your check-in time.
*   **Vehicle Preferences**:
    *   `vehicle_category`: Select the category of vehicle you require (e.g. economy, SUV, etc.).
    *   `vehicle_make_model`: Enter the make and model of the vehicle.
    *   `vehicle_color`: Enter the exterior color of the vehicle.
    *   `vehicle_vin`: Enter the vehicle identification number.
*   **Payment Information**:
    *   `payment_method`: Select your payment method (e.g. credit card, debit card, etc.).
    *   `deposit_amount`: Enter the security deposit amount.
*   **Vehicle Condition**:
    *   `existing_damage`: Select the level of pre-existing damage on the vehicle.
    *   `vehicle_condition`: Select the overall condition of the vehicle.
*   **Tire Condition**:
    *   `tire_condition`: Select the condition of the tires.
*   **Additional Services**:
    *   Select any additional services required for your rental (e.g. GPS navigation, child safety seat, etc.).
*   **Insurance**:
    *   Select the insurance coverage options you require (e.g. collision damage waiver, liability insurance, etc.).
*   **Payment Confirmation**:
    *   Confirm your payment method and details.
*   **Terms Acknowledgment**:
    *   Confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions, including fuel policy and mileage policy.
*   **Staff Initials**:
    *   Enter the processing staff's initials.
*   **Check-In Completion Date**:
    *   Enter the completion date of the check-in process.
*   **Customer Signature**:
    *   Confirm that you understand the terms and conditions and that you are aware of the vehicle's condition.

Please note that this form is a mandatory document for the rental process. Please ensure that you fill out all fields accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues during your rental experience.
