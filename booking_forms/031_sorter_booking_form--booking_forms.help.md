# Sorter Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for booking a sorter, which is a machine or device used for precise sorting in various industries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **date and time** for the sorter booking in the `date_and_time` field. This is the date and time when the sorter is expected to be available.
2. Enter the **flow rate** of the sorter in the `flow_rate` field. This is the speed at which the sorter is expected to process or sort items.
3. Enter the **name of the sorter** in the `sorter_name` field. This is the identifier or name assigned to the sorter.
4. Enter the **name of the instrument** used with the sorter in the `instrument_name` field. This is the specific instrument or tool used with the sorter.
5. Enter any **notes** about the sorter booking in the `notes` field. This is a space for any additional comments or details about the booking.
6. Enter the **phone number** of the person booking the sorter in the `phone` field. This is the contact information for the person making the booking.
7. Enter the **email address** of the person booking the sorter in the `email` field. This is the contact information for the person making the booking.
8. Select the **flow rate** option from the dropdown menu in the `select_flow_rate` field. This is the desired flow rate for the sorter.
9. Select the **sorter** option from the dropdown menu in the `select_sorter` field. This is the specific sorter being booked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **date_and_time**: The date and time when the sorter is expected to be available.
* **flow_rate**: The speed at which the sorter is expected to process or sort items.
* **sorter_name**: The identifier or name assigned to the sorter.
* **instrument_name**: The specific instrument or tool used with the sorter.
* **notes**: A space for any additional comments or details about the booking.
* **phone**: The contact information for the person making the booking.
* **email**: The contact information for the person making the booking.
* **select_flow_rate**: A dropdown menu to select the desired flow rate for the sorter.
* **select_sorter**: A dropdown menu to select the specific sorter being booked.
