# Pet Boarding Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Boarding Reservation Form is designed for pet owners to reserve a spot for their pets at a boarding facility. This form will help the facility to ensure that the pet receives proper care during its stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reservation date for your pet's stay.
2. Fill in your pet's name.
3. Provide your name and contact information.
4. Choose the type(s) of boarding services your pet will need.
5. Add any special instructions for your pet's care.
6. Provide your contact number and email.
7. Add any additional notes about your pet's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (Reservation Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to board your pet. Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Pet Name** (pet name, text, required): Enter your pet's name.
* **Owner Name** (owner name, text, not required): Enter your name, the pet's owner.
* **Pet Breed** (pet breed, select one, required): Choose your pet's breed from the list:
	+ Labrador
	+ German Shepherd
	+ Golden Retriever
	+ Poodle
	+ Mixed
* **Reservation Length** (reservation length, number, not required): Enter the number of days you would like to board your pet.
* **Boarding Services** (boarding services, select multiple, required): Choose the type(s) of boarding services your pet will need:
	+ Standard Boarding
	+ Luxury Boarding
	+ Both
* **Special Instructions** (special instructions, note, not required): Add any special instructions for your pet's care.
* **Contact Number** (contact number, text, not required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (email, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Owner Notes** (owner notes, text, not required): Add any additional notes about your pet's needs.
