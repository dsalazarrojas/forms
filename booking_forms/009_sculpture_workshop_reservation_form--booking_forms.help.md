# Sculpture Workshop Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a sculpture workshop, including participant details and workshop schedule information. It is intended for users who want to book a spot in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the Participant Name field. This is a required field and should be filled in by the participant.
2. Enter your **Email** in the Email field. This is a required field and should be filled in by the participant.
3. Select the **Workshop Date** you would like to attend from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the **Start Time** of the workshop you would like to attend.
5. Select the **End Time** of the workshop you would like to attend.
6. Choose the **Duration** of the workshop you would like to attend.
7. Enter the **Participant Count** number of participants attending.
8. Select additional **Participant Info** if there are multiple participants in your group.
9. Enter any additional comments or information in the **Note** field.
10. Confirm your email address in the **Email Confirmation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive email confirmations and updates.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, select_one, not required): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, select_one, not required): Select the start time of the workshop.
* **End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, select_one, not required): Select the end time of the workshop.
* **Duration** (`workshop_duration`, select_one, not required): Select the duration of the workshop.
* **Participant Count** (`participant_count`, number, required): Enter the number of participants in your group.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, select_multiple, not required): Select additional participant information if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or information about your group.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Confirm your email address to receive updates and confirmations about the workshop.
