# disney_s_aulani_resort_vacation_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to request a quote for a vacation package at Disney's Aulani Resort. The goal of this form is to gather details about the user's travel plans and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including travel dates, adult count, children count, travel type, and contact person details (name and phone number).
2. If you have any special requirements or preferences, you can provide additional information in the "Arrival Information" field.
3. If you have a preferred contact method, you can enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, number, required): Enter the dates of your travel, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Adult Count** (`adult_count`, number, required): Enter the number of adults traveling with you.
* **Children Count** (`children_count`, number, required): Enter the number of children traveling with you.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you want a quote for a specific travel package, or "No" if you're open to options.
* **Arrival Information** (`arrival_info`, select_multiple, required): If you have any special arrival requirements, such as early or late arrival, select "Yes" or "No". If you need to provide more details, you can add notes in this field.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, note, required): Provide the name of the person responsible for contacting you, and include their phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure an accurate quote.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at your preferred contact method.
