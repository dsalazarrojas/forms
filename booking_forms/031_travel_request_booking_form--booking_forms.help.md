# Travel Request Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Request Booking Form is a form designed to gather information for travel requests, including trip details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the travel agency of your choice to book through.
- Choose your preferred trip dates. 
- Make sure to indicate whether you have client preferences.
- Enter the client's email and phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Request** (travel request, note, optional): Enter a brief note to provide more information about your travel request.
* **Trip Details** (trip details, select_multiple, optional): Select the trip type, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Client Preferences** (client preferences, select_one, required): Choose the client preference (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) that best fits your travel needs.
* **Trip Dates** (trip dates, select_one, required): Select the trip dates you would like to travel on (01/01/2024, 01/02/2024, or 01/03/2024).
* **Travel Agency** (travel agency, select_one, required): Select the travel agency you would like to book through (agency1, agency2, or agency3).
* **Client Email** (client email, email, optional): Enter the client's email for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone** (client phone, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Additional Details** (additional details, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your travel request. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in the booking process.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the "Additional Details" field.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance with the booking process, feel free to contact our support team.
