# Book Your UI/UX Mentoring Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help you book a UI/UX mentoring session with our team. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide the required information to schedule a session that suits your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which should be your first and last name.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, where we will send confirmation and any further instructions.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for session coordination. (Note: This field is not required, but it is recommended for a smooth communication experience.)
4. Specify your **Current Role or Title**, which should reflect your current job position.
5. Select your **Experience Level**, which will help us understand your background and tailor the session to your needs.
6. Choose your **Current Skills**, which will help us prepare for our conversation.
7. Select the **Mentoring Focus Area**, which will guide the session's content and goals.
8. Describe your **Specific Goals**, which will help us understand what you want to achieve during the session.
9. Discuss your **Current Challenges**, which will help us tailor the session's content and focus.
10. Provide the **Portfolio URL** if you have one.
11. Share your **LinkedIn Profile URL** if you have one.
12. Choose your **Preferred Session Date** and **Preferred Time** for the session.
13. Select the **Session Duration** and **Session Format** that suits your needs.
14. Select your **Timezone** so we can schedule accordingly.
15. If you have previous mentoring experience, let us know.
16. Choose how you **Heard About This Mentoring**.
17. Add any **Additional Comments** you'd like to share.
18. Finally, confirm that you agree to the **Mentoring Terms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: We will send confirmation and any further instructions here.
* **Phone Number**: For session coordination. (Not required, but recommended for a smooth experience.)
* **Current Role or Title**: Your current job title.
* **Experience Level**: Select your experience in UI/UX (Beginner, Intermediate, Mid-Level, Senior, or Expert).
* **Current Skills**: Select the skills you have currently.
* **Mentoring Focus Area**: Choose the focus area of the session (Career Guidance, Portfolio Review, Skill Development, etc.).
* **Specific Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve during the session.
* **Current Challenges**: Discuss the challenges you are facing.
* **Portfolio URL**: Link to your portfolio if you have one.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Link to your LinkedIn profile if you have one.
* **Preferred Session Date**: When you would like to have the session.
* **Preferred Time**: What time works best for you.
* **Session Duration**: Choose how long you want the session to last (30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Session Format**: Select how you want to have the session (Video Call, Phone Call, In Person, etc.).
* **Timezone**: Select your timezone so we can schedule accordingly.
* **Previous Mentoring**: If you have had mentoring experience, let us know.
* **How Did You Hear About This Mentoring**: How you found out about this mentoring opportunity.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other information you'd like to share.
* **I Agree to Mentoring Terms**: Confirm your agreement to the terms.

## Tips
* Be honest about your experience level and current challenges to help us tailor the session.
* Choose the session format that suits your needs (Video Call, Phone Call, In Person, etc.).
* Select your timezone to ensure a suitable session schedule.
* Confirm your agreement to the mentoring terms to proceed with the booking.
* Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or information to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
