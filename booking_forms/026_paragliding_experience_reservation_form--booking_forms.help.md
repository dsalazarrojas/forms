# Paragliding Experience Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a paragliding experience. Please fill in the required details to complete the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the reservation details form.
2. Provide your customer information.
3. Select the desired experience date and time.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number.
5. Choose the paragliding experience location.
6. Add any additional customer notes (if needed).
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **reservation_details** (text, required/optional): Enter the details of your reservation, such as any specific requests or requirements.
* **customer_info** (text, required/optional): Enter your name, surname, and any other relevant customer information.
* **choose_experience_date** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the date of your paragliding experience from the dropdown menu.
* **choose_experience_time** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the start and end time of your paragliding experience. 
  * Select one of the available time slots: 09:00-10:00, 10:00-11:00, 11:00-12:00, 13:00-14:00, 14:00-15:00, 15:00-16:00.
* **customer_email** (email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address for us to reach out to you.
* **customer_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your experience.
* **experience_location** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the location of your paragliding experience. Options are: Paragliding Experience 1, Paragliding Experience 2, Paragliding Experience 3.
* **customer_note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional information or notes about your experience (if needed). 

Note: The form is designed for customers to provide information to book their paragliding experience. Please fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.
