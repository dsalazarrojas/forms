# DJ Service Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "DJ Service Information Form" is designed to collect necessary information from clients to provide a personalized DJ service for their events. This form will help the DJ team to understand the client's needs and preferences, ensuring a successful and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are hosting (e.g., Wedding, Birthday, Party, Corporate, or Other).
2. Choose a date for your event.
3. Enter the start and end times of your event.
4. If required, enter the duration of your event.
5. If required, enter the number of guests attending your event.
6. Select your music preferences (e.g., Rock, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic).
7. Choose the equipment needed for your event (e.g., Sound System, Lighting, DJ Console, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of event you are hosting, such as a Wedding, Birthday, Party, Corporate, or Other. This information will help the DJ team to tailor the music selection and atmosphere accordingly.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your event. This will help the DJ team to schedule the event and ensure they are available.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of your event. This will help the DJ team to coordinate with other event staff and vendors.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of your event. This will help the DJ team to plan the event's duration and ensure they have enough time for setup and breakdown.
* **Event Duration** (`Event Duration`, number, not required): If required, enter the duration of your event in hours or minutes.
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, number, not required): If required, enter the estimated number of guests attending your event. This will help the DJ team to prepare for sufficient sound and lighting equipment.
* **Music Preferences** (`Music Preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the music genres you prefer, such as Rock, Pop, Hip-Hop, or Electronic. This will help the DJ team to curate a playlist that suits your taste.
* **Equipment** (`Equipment`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the equipment needed for your event, such as Sound System, Lighting, or DJ Console. This will help the DJ team to ensure they have the necessary equipment for a successful event.
