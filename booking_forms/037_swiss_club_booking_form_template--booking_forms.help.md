# Swiss Club Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swiss Club Booking Form is a user-friendly template designed to simplify the booking process for events at the Swiss Club. This guide will walk you through each field, ensuring you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your membership number in the Membership Number field.
2. Choose the correct Event Date and Event Time for your booking.
3. Input the Event Name, Member Name, and Member Phone accurately to enable smooth communication and event planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Number** (`membership_number`, Number, required: false): Please enter your unique membership number to validate your booking. Ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date of the event you wish to book, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, required: false): Enter the time of the event, in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, Text, required: false): Input the name of the event you are booking.
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, Text, required: false): Enter the name of the member booking the event.
* **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, Text, required: false): Input the phone number of the member booking the event, to enable efficient communication.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled, even if they are technically optional.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
