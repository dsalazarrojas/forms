# Booking Form With Calendar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book appointments with our team. It captures essential information about your visit, including your name, contact details, and preferred appointment date and time. The form also includes options for adding special requirements and meeting location preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If applicable, enter your organization name in the "Organization Name" field.
5. Select the purpose of your visit from the available options in the "Purpose of Visit" field.
6. Select a preferred appointment date from the calendar.
7. Choose a preferred appointment time from the available options in the "Preferred Appointment Time" field.
8. Select the expected duration of your meeting from the available options in the "Expected Duration" field.
9. Enter the number of attendees for your meeting in the "Number of Attendees" field.
10. If necessary, enter any special requirements in the "Special Requirements" field.
11. Choose a preferred meeting location from the available options in the "Meeting Location Preference" field.
12. If you are a returning visitor, select "Returning visitor" in the "Have you visited before" field.
13. If you would like to sync your appointment with your Google, Outlook, or Zoom calendar, select the corresponding option in the "Add to my calendar" field.
14. Enter any additional notes about your visit in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (visitor_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address (visitor_email)**: Enter your email address where we will send appointment details.
* **Phone Number (visitor_phone)**: Enter your best contact number for any last-minute questions or changes.
* **Organization Name (organization_name)**: If you represent an organization, enter your company or organization name. (Optional)
* **Purpose of Visit (visit_purpose)**: Select the reason for your visit from the available options.
* **Preferred Appointment Date (appointment_date)**: Select a date from the calendar for your preferred appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time (appointment_time)**: Choose a time slot for your appointment from the available options.
* **Expected Duration (duration_required)**: Select the expected length of your meeting.
* **Number of Attendees (number_of_attendees)**: Enter the number of people attending your meeting.
* **Special Requirements (special_requirements)**: Enter any special requirements or requests you may have.
* **Meeting Location Preference (meeting_location)**: Choose a preferred location for your meeting from the available options.
* **Have you visited before (prior_experience)**: If you have visited before, select "Returning visitor". Otherwise, select "First time" or "Not applicable".
* **Add to my calendar (calendar_sync)**: Select yes to sync your appointment with your Google, Outlook, or Zoom calendar.
* **Additional Notes (additional_notes)**: Enter any additional information about your visit.
