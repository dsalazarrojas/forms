# Tennis Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tennis enthusiasts to register for a tennis session. Please provide the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal information on the first page.
2. Select your availability on the second page.
3. Provide session details on the third page.
4. Enter your contact information on the fourth page.
5. Enter your payment details on the fifth page.
6. Choose your session preferences on the sixth page (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional information on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Personal Info
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and any other personal details you'd like to share with us.

### Page 2: Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): Select a time slot that suits you best to play the tennis session.

### Page 3: Session Info
* **Session Info** (`session_info`, `text`, required): Provide details about the session you'd like to register for, such as session type and date.

### Page 4: Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information to reach out to us if needed.

### Page 5: Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter your payment method and any other payment details.

### Page 6: Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred session format (if available). If not applicable, please leave blank.

### Page 7: Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information you think we should know about your session registration.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure a successful registration.
