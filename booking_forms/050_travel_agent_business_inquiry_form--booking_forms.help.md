# travel_agent_business_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate business inquiries between travel agents and their customers. It helps travel agents gather information about potential customers' needs and preferences, allowing them to provide personalized services and build strong relationships with their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required "Inquiry Details" field with accurate and specific information about your travel needs.
2. Enter the travel agent's name or username in the "Travel Agent" field (if provided by the travel agent).
3. Review and submit the form to send your inquiry to the travel agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, `text`, `required`): Describe the details of your travel inquiry, including the destinations, dates, and any specific requirements you have. This field is essential for the travel agent to understand your needs accurately.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `text`, `optional`): If your travel agent has provided you with their name or username, enter it here. If not, please leave this field blank.
