# Fine Dining Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fine Dining Reservation Form is designed to collect information from customers for fine dining reservations. This form helps gather essential details such as reservation dates, times, and guest counts, as well as customer contact information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the Reservation Date for your fine dining experience.
2. Enter the Guest Count for the number of people joining you.
3. Specify the Reservation Time for your desired dining time.
4. Select the Reservation Duration for the length of your reservation.
5. Choose the Reservation Status (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter the Customer Email address for communication purposes.
7. Provide the Customer Phone number for easy contact.
8. Add any Special Requests for unique needs or requirements.
9. Include any Customer Notes for additional information.
10. Enter the Reservation Notes for the reservation staff.
11. Add Restaurant Notes for the staff's reference.
12. Select the Assigned Tool (Chat Jimmy or Chatbot).
13. Choose the Output File (Fine Dining Reservation Form - Booking Forms or Fine Dining Reservation Form - Booking Forms1).
14. Select the Form Category (Booking Forms or Other Forms).
15. Select the Form Title (Fine Dining Reservation Form or Other Form).
16. Enter the Form Description for a brief explanation.
17. Select the Assigned Tool (Chat Jimmy or Chatbot).
18. Enter the Form Version number.
19. Select the Form Version Date.
20. Enter the Form Version Number.
21. Select the Last Updated by (User or System).
22. Enter the Last Update Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for your fine dining reservation.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people joining you.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, required/optional): Specify the desired dining time.
* **Reservation Duration** (`reservation_duration`, number, required/optional): Select the length of your reservation.
* **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between Active or Inactive status.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Dining Preferences** (`dining_preferences`, note, required/optional): Add any special requests or preferences for your reservation.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required/optional): Include any unique needs or requirements.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information for the staff.
* **Reservation Notes** (`reservation_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter notes for the reservation staff.
* **Restaurant Notes** (`restaurant_notes`, note, required/optional): Add notes for the staff's reference.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select between Chat Jimmy or Chatbot.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose between Fine Dining Reservation Form - Booking Forms or Fine Dining Reservation Form - Booking Forms1.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the Form ID.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select between Booking Forms or Other Forms.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, select_one, required/optional): Choose between Fine Dining Reservation Form or Other Form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief explanation of the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select between Chat Jimmy or Chatbot.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required/optional): Enter the Form Version number.
* **Form Version Date** (`form_version_date`, date, required/optional): Select the Form Version Date.
* **Form Version Number** (`form_version_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the Form Version Number.
* **Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, date, required/optional): Select the Last Updated by (User or System).
* **Last Updater** (`form_last_updater`, select_one, required/optional): Enter the Last Update Date.
