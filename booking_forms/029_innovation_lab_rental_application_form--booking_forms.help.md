# Innovation Lab Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Innovation Lab Rental Application Form is designed to collect necessary information for the innovation lab's rental process. Please provide accurate details about your project to ensure a smooth rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form title field with a descriptive name of your project.
2. Enter your project's details in the project-info field.
3. Provide your project's name in the project-name field.
4. Enter the project's start and end dates in the project-start-date and project-end-date fields, respectively.
5. Add additional project details in the project-details field (optional).
6. Specify your project's location in the project-location field (optional).
7. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and institution in the project-organiser field, project-organiser-email, project-organiser-phone, and project-organiser-institution fields, respectively.
8. Indicate whether you are affiliated with an institution in the project-organiser-institution field.
9. Choose the duration of your rental period in the rental-period field.
10. Select the start and end times for your project in the start-time and end-time fields, respectively.
11. Specify whether you need special equipment or not in the required-equipment field.
12. List any additional equipment needed in the special-equipment field (if required).
13. Provide your lab contact person's information, including name, phone number, and email in the lab-contact-person, lab-phone, and lab-email fields, respectively.
14. Specify the location of your lab or contact information in the lab-location-contact-info field (optional).
15. Enter your lab's website in the lab-website field (optional).
16. Select the equipment needed from the lab for your project in the lab-equipment field.
17. Finally, submit your form by clicking the form-submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title**: The form title should be a descriptive name of your project.
* **project-info**: Provide a brief description of your project.
* **project-name**: Enter your project's name.
* **project-start-date**: Enter the start date of your project.
* **project-end-date**: Enter the end date of your project.
* **project-details**: Provide any additional details about your project.
* **project-location**: Specify your project's location.
* **project-organiser**: Enter your name.
* **project-organiser-email**: Enter your email address.
* **project-organiser-phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **project-organiser-institution**: Indicate whether you are affiliated with an institution.
* **project_organiser_institution**: This field is to specify if you are affiliated with an institution. You should select 'Yes' if you are.
* **rental-period**: Choose the duration of your rental period.
* **start-time**: Select the start time of your project.
* **end-time**: Select the end time of your project.
* **required-equipment**: Specify whether you need any special equipment for your project.
* **special-equipment**: If you need special equipment, list them here.
* **lab-location**: Specify the location of your lab.
* **lab-contact-person**: Enter your lab contact person's name.
* **lab-location-contact-info**: Enter your lab contact person's contact information.
* **lab-phone**: Enter your lab contact person's phone number.
* **lab-email**: Enter your lab contact person's email address.
* **lab-website**: Enter your lab's website address.
* **lab-equipment**: Select the equipment needed from the lab.
* **form-submit**: Click this button to submit your form.

Note: The following fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable: project-details, project-location, project_organiser_department, lab-location-contact-info, and lab-website.
