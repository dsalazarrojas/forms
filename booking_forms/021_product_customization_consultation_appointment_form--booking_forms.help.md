# product customization consultation appointment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer and product-related information for a product customization consultation appointment. It helps ensure that both parties are on the same page before the consultation, promoting effective communication and accurate product customization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information in the "customer information" page.
2. Share your appointment details in the "appointment details" page.
3. Enter your product information in the "product details" page.
4. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): 
Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the product customization consultant. This will help the consultant get in touch with you and prepare for your consultation.
### 2. Appointment Details
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_info`, text, required/optional): 
Provide the date, time, and location of your appointment, as well as any special requests or requirements you may have. This will aid the consultant in preparing for your consultation and ensuring you arrive at the correct time and place.
### 3. Product Details
* **Product Details** (`product_info`, text, required/optional): 
Enter as much information as possible about the product you're seeking customization for. Describe the product's current state and any specifications you'd like the consultant to know in order to better serve you.

## Tips
Please review your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form. This will ensure that both you and the consultant are well-prepared for your consultation. In case of any discrepancies or changes, please let us know as soon as possible.
