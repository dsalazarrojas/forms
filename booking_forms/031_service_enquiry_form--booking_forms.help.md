# service_enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers or potential customers to submit an enquiry to book a service with your company. It is meant to collect relevant information from the user to facilitate a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Type in a brief **Enquiry Message** to describe your request in the field labeled "enquiry_message".
4. Provide your **Email Address** in the format of `username@domain.com` in the field labeled "email".
5. Optionally, select a **Date** for your booking in the field labeled "date" (if applicable).
6. Optionally, select a **Time** for your booking in the field labeled "time" (if applicable).
7. In the field labeled "enquiry", provide any additional information about your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is required and should contain your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is required and should contain your last name.
* **Enquiry Message**: This field is required and should be used to provide a brief description of your booking request.
* **Email**: This field is required and should be in the format of `username@domain.com`. This will be used as the contact email address for your booking.
* **Date**: This field is optional and allows you to select a date for your booking.
* **Time**: This field is optional and allows you to select a time for your booking.
* **Enquiry**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your booking request.
