# Book A Foundations Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for property owners or managers to gather information about a property's foundation and any concerns related to it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your information about the property, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of property you are dealing with (e.g., Single Family Home, Commercial Building, etc.).
3. Provide your contact information and preferred date and time for the assessment.
4. Answer questions about any reported foundation concerns and their severity.
5. Provide information about any previous inspections or assessments.
6. Answer questions about pest or water intrusion issues.
7. Specify your ownership status and the purpose of the assessment.
8. Finally, confirm your availability for the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (1)**: Your full name as the property owner or manager.
* **Email Address (2)**: Your email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Your phone number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Property Address (4)**: The street address of the property to be assessed.
* **City - State - Zip (5)**: The city, state, and ZIP code of the property.
* **Property Type (6)**: Select the type of property you are dealing with (e.g., Single Family Home, Commercial Building, etc.).
* **Year Built (7)**: The year the property was built.
* **Square Footage (8)**: The total square footage of the property (optional).
* **Foundation Type (9)**: Select the type of foundation the property has (e.g., Concrete Slab, Crawl Space, etc.).
* **Does Property Have Basement (10)**: Yes or no, does the property have a basement.
* **Reported Foundation Concerns (11)**: Select all concerns you have about the property's foundation.
* **Severity of Concerns (12)**: Rate the severity of the concerns you have.
* **How Long Have You Noticed Issues (13)**: How long you have noticed issues with the property's foundation.
* **Previous Foundation Inspections (14)**: Yes or no, have you had a previous foundation inspection.
* **Results of Previous Inspections (15)**: If you have had a previous inspection, provide the results here (optional).
* **Pest Damage Observed (16)**: Yes or no, have you observed pest damage on the property.
* **Water Intrusion Issues (17)**: Yes or no, are there water intrusion issues on the property.
* **Ownership Status (18)**: Select your ownership status (e.g., Homeowner, Renter, etc.).
* **Purpose of Assessment (19)**: Select the purpose of the assessment (e.g., Pre-Purchase Inspection, etc.).
* **Preferred Assessment Date (20)**: Select a preferred date for the assessment.
* **Available Morning Time (21)**: Are you available for the assessment during morning hours?
* **Available Afternoon Time (22)**: Are you available for the assessment during afternoon hours?
* **Additional Requests or Notes (23)**: Any additional requests or notes about the assessment.
* **Permission to Contact (24)**: Do you consent to be contacted for further information about the assessment?

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by property owners or managers, so the fields should be relevant to their role. Please take your time to answer each question accurately.
