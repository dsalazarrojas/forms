# Resident Visitation Booking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Resident Visitation Booking Request Form is a tool used by residents to request a visit from a visitor. It captures essential details about the visitor and the resident, ensuring a smooth and safe visitation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the first name and last name of the resident and visitor.
2. Provide the date of birth of the resident.
3. Specify the month and day of birth of the resident (if different from the date of birth).
4. Choose a date for the visit.
5. Select a visitation time.
6. Specify the duration of the visit.
7. Enter the resident's name.
8. Provide the room number of the resident.
9. Specify the facility where the visit will take place.
10. Enter the facility's phone number.
11. Enter the facility's email.
12. Enter the name of the visitor.
13. Enter the phone number of the visitor.
14. Enter the email of the visitor.
15. Provide the reason for the visit.
16. Enter any additional information about the visit.
17. Provide any additional notes or comments.
18. Specify who contacted the facility about the visit.
19. Enter comments or any further notes.
20. Obtain the resident's signature.
21. Obtain the visitor's signature.
22. Obtain the facility's signature.
23. Specify the review status of the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (form\_first\_name, Text): Enter the first name of the resident.
* **Last name** (form\_last\_name, Text): Enter the last name of the resident.
* **Date of birth** (form\_dob, Date): Enter the date of birth of the resident.
* **Month and Day of birth** (form\_dob\_mmdd, Date): Enter the month and day of birth of the resident (if different from the date of birth).
* **Visitation date** (form\_visitation\_date, Date): Choose a date for the visit.
* **Visitation time** (form\_visitation\_time, Time): Select a time for the visit.
* **Visitation duration** (form\_visitation\_duration, Text): Specify the duration of the visit.
* **Resident name** (form\_resident\_name, Text): Enter the resident's name.
* **Room number** (form\_room\_number, Text): Enter the resident's room number.
* **Facility** (form\_facility, Text): Specify the facility where the visit will take place.
* **Facility phone** (form\_facility\_phone, Text): Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Facility email** (form\_facility\_email, Email): Enter the facility's email.
* **Visitor name** (form\_visitor\_name, Text): Enter the name of the visitor.
* **Visitor phone** (form\_visitor\_phone, Text): Enter the phone number of the visitor.
* **Visitor email** (form\_visitor\_email, Email): Enter the email of the visitor.
* **Reason for visitation** (form\_reason\_visitation, Text): Provide the reason for the visit.
* **Additional information** (form\_additional\_info, Text): Enter any additional information about the visit.
* **Additional notes** (form\_additional\_notes, Text): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Who contacted** (form\_who\_contacted, Text): Specify who contacted the facility about the visit.
* **Comments** (form\_comments, Text): Enter comments or any further notes.
* **Resident signature** (form\_resident\_signature, Text): Obtain the resident's signature.
* **Visitor signature** (form\_visitor\_signature, Text): Obtain the visitor's signature.
* **Facility signature** (form\_facility\_signature, Text): Obtain the facility's signature.
* **Review status** (form\_review\_status, Select One):
  - **Approved**: The visit is approved.
  - **Denied**: The visit is denied.
  - **Pending**: The visit is pending review.
