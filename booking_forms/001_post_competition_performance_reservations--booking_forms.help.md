# Post-Competition Performance Reservations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect post-competition performance information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Reservation Date** in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/28/2023).
2. Enter the **Event Name** of the performance.
3. Enter the name of the **Performer** who participated in the performance.
4. Enter the **Performance Start Time** in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
5. Enter the **Performance Duration** in minutes (e.g., 30 for 30 minutes).
6. Enter the **Performance Date** in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/28/2023).
7. Select the **Event Type** from the options: Concert, Theatre, Semi-formal, or Formal.
8. Enter the **Performance Location** where the performance took place.
9. Enter any additional **Performance Notes** about the performance.
10. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
11. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
12. Click **Submit** to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to make the reservation for.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the performer you want to make the reservation for.
* **Performance Start Time** (`performance_start_time`, time, required): Enter the time the performance started.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the performance in minutes.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event (Concert, Theatre, Semi-formal, or Formal).
* **Performance Location** (`performance_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the performance took place.
* **Performance Notes** (`performance_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required): Click to submit your reservation.
