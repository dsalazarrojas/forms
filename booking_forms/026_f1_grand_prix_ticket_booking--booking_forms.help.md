# F1 Grand Prix Ticket Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The F1 Grand Prix Ticket Booking form is designed to allow users to book tickets for the F1 Grand Prix event. Users can provide their personal details, ticket preferences, and payment information to complete the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the required and optional fields according to your preferences and requirements.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.
3. Click submit to complete the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.

### Accessibility
* **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the accessibility options you require for your ticket (e.g. wheelchair access, sign language interpretation).

### Ticket Type
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of ticket you wish to book (e.g. Grandstand, Pitside, Pitlane).

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Specify the number of tickets you wish to book.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit Card, Bank Transfer).

### Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount due for the tickets. 

### Confirm Payment
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you have checked the payment method and amount correctly.

### Confirm Payment Method
* **Confirm Payment Method** (`confirm_payment_method`, `select_one`, `optional`: Confirm whether the selected payment method is correct or not.

Please take note that all fields with the label "Optional" are required only if you select them as a necessary option. If you have any doubts or concerns, please review the form carefully before submitting it.
