# City Hostel Dorm Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Hostel Dorm Booking Form is designed to collect personal information and booking preferences from guests who want to stay at the City Hostel. This form helps us to confirm their booking and ensure a smooth check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling in all required fields.
2. Ensure you enter your name, email address, and phone number correctly.
3. Select your preferred room type, bed type, and number of guests.
4. Choose your preferred payment method.
5. Read and agree to the cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. We will use this to confirm your booking.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including your country code if international.
* **Nationality**: Enter your nationality.
* **Check-in Date**: Select your arrival date.
* **Check-out Date**: Select your departure date.
* **Number of Nights**: This will be calculated automatically based on your check-out date.
* **Room Type**: Select your preferred accommodation type (4-bed dorm, 6-bed dorm, 8-bed dorm, or Private room).
* **Bed Preference**: Select your preferred bed location (Lower bed, Upper bed, or No preference).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people checking in.
* **Include Breakfast**: Choose whether you want breakfast included in your stay.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional needs or requests you may have.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer).
* **I agree to the cancellation policy**: Review and agree to our cancellation terms online.
* **Subscribe to our newsletter**: Choose whether you want to receive updates on special offers and events.
