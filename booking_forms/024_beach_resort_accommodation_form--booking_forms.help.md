# Beach Resort Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beach Resort Accommodation Form is designed to collect essential information from guests to book their accommodation at our resort. This information helps us to prepare for their stay and provide the best possible experience. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2.  Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Choose your check-in and check-out dates in the "Check-In Date" and "Check-Out Date" fields.
5.  Indicate the total number of guests and the number of adults and children.
6.  Specify the ages of the children.
7.  Select your preferred room type from the options provided.
8.  Choose your bed preference from the available options.
9.  If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
10.  Select your meal plan from the options.
11.  Indicate if you need airport transportation.
12.  Specify the purpose of your visit, if it's not a vacation.
13.  If you have any accessibility needs, type them in the field.
14.  If you are bringing a pet, indicate it in the field.
15.  Choose your payment method.
16.  Finally, let us know how you heard about us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: Please enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your contact email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your contact phone number for any last-minute inquiries.
* **Check-In Date (Check-In Date)**: Choose your arrival date for us to prepare for your stay.
* **Check-Out Date (Check-Out Date)**: Select your departure date to ensure we reserve your room until then.
* **Number of Guests (Number of Guests)**: Indicate the total number of guests in your party.
* **Number of Adults (Number of Adults)**: Specify the number of adults accompanying you.
* **Number of Children (Number of Children)**: Indicate the number of children under 18 years old.
* **Children Ages (Children Ages)**: List the ages of the children for us to provide suitable accommodations.
* **Room Type (Room Type)**: Choose your preferred room type from the options provided.
* **Bed Preference (Bed Preference)**: Select your preferred type of bed.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests)**: Type any special requests or preferences you may have.
* **Meal Plan (Meal Plan)**: Select your preferred meal plan from the options.
* **Airport Transportation Needed (Airport Transportation Needed)**: Indicate if you need airport transportation.
* **Special Occasion (Special Occasion)**: If your visit is for a special purpose, such as a honeymoon or birthday, select that option.
* **Accessibility Needs (Accessibility Needs)**: If you have any accessibility needs, type them in for us to be aware of.
* **Bringing a Pet (Bringing a Pet)**: If you are bringing a pet, indicate it in this field.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose how you plan to pay for your stay.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (How Did You Hear About Us)**: Let us know how you heard about us to help us improve our marketing efforts.

By following these steps and filling out the form accurately, you will ensure that we have all the necessary information to provide a comfortable and enjoyable stay for you and your party.
