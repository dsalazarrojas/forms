# Team Building Activity Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Activity Reservation Form is designed to collect information for team building activities, including activity type, date, time, participants, team members, reservation details, and leader contact information. This form helps organizers plan and coordinate team building events effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the activity type from the available options (Activity).
2. Select the date and time for the activity.
3. Enter the number of participants.
4. Choose team members involved in the activity.
5. Set the reservation start and end times.
6. Specify the duration of the activity.
7. Provide details about the activity, including location and description.
8. Add any additional activity images or files.
9. Enter the activity capacity (number of participants).
10. Select multiple team building activities offered.
11. Choose team leaders responsible for the activity.
12. Enter team leaders' email addresses.
13. Add team leaders' phone numbers.
14. Specify team leaders' addresses.
15. Include any message from team leaders.
16. Add images of team leaders.
17. Select the preferred comment option from team leaders.
18. Enter team leaders' first comment.
19. Enter team leaders' second comment.
20. Add any comments about the team building activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity** (Select One): Choose the type of team building activity to be performed (Activity 1, Activity 2, or Activity 3).
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the team building activity.
* **Time** (Time): Set the time for the team building activity.
* **Participants** (Number): Enter the number of participants in the activity.
* **Team Members** (Select Multiple): Choose the team members involved in the activity (Team Member 1, Team Member 2, or Team Member 3).
* **Reservation Start** (Time): Set the start time for the reservation.
* **Reservation End** (Time): Set the end time for the reservation.
* **Duration** (Number): Specify the duration of the activity.
* **Activity Details** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the activity, including location and any other relevant information.
* **Activity Image** (Text): Add any images or files related to the activity.
* **Capacity** (Number): Enter the maximum capacity for the activity.
* **Team Building Activities** (Select Multiple): Choose multiple team building activities to be performed (Activity 1, Activity 2, or Activity 3).
* **Team Leaders** (Select One): Choose the team leader responsible for the activity (Team Leader 1, Team Leader 2, or Team Leader 3).
* **Team Leaders Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Address** (Text): Enter the address of the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Message** (Text): Include any message from the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Image** (Text): Add any image of the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Comment** (Select One): Choose the preferred comment option from the team leader (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Team Leaders Comment 1** (Text): Enter the first comment from the team leader.
* **Team Leaders Comment 2** (Text): Enter the second comment from the team leader.
* **Team Building Activity Comments** (Text): Add any comments about the team building activity.

Note: The fields and their labels are based on the provided YAML structure.
