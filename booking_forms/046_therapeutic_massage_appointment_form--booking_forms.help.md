# Therapeutic Massages Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a therapeutic massage appointment. It will help our team to schedule and prepare for your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

- Read through each field carefully and enter the requested information accurately.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- Use the format provided for date fields (mm/dd/yyyy).
- If you have any medical condition or health information, please provide it in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, `date`, required:false): Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, `select_one`, required:false): Select 'Yes' if you are looking for a standard massage, 'No' if you are looking for a different type of treatment. 
* **Health Information** (`health_information`, `text`, required:false): Provide any relevant health information that may affect the massage, such as recent injuries or medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required:false): If you have a medical condition that may impact the massage, please describe it in detail.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required:false): Select any preferences you have for the massage, such as 'Yes' for aromatherapy or 'No' for relaxation. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information, as it will help us to provide the best service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
