# Book A Limo Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a limousine service for various events and occasions. It will guide you through the process of providing all necessary information to book a limousine that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a limousine, fill out this form with your details and preferences. Make sure to provide all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you during the service.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of your event.
* **Pickup Time**: Select the time you want to be picked up.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the street address where you will be picked up.
* **Dropoff Location**: Enter the street address where you want to be dropped off.
* **Type of Service**: Select the type of event or occasion you are booking the limousine for.
* **Vehicle Type Preferred**: Choose the type of limousine you prefer.
* **Number of Passengers**: Enter the number of people traveling with you.
* **Estimated Trip Duration**: Enter the estimated duration of the trip.
* **Additional Stops Required**: Select if you need any additional stops during the trip.
* **Details of Additional Stops**: If you selected "Yes - Multiple Stops", provide details of the stops, including locations and estimated duration for each stop.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests such as decorations, music, or amenities you would like to have during the trip.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, such as wheelchair or other accommodations, describe them here.
* **Desired Amenities**: Select any amenities you would like to have during the trip.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Deposit Amount**: If required, enter the deposit amount.
* **Promotional or Discount Code**: If applicable, enter any promotional or discount code you have.
* **Special Parking Instructions**: Enter any special parking instructions, such as parking lot or specific location.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the phone number of a contact person we can reach during the service.
* **Special Occasions or Celebrations**: Enter if this is a special occasion, such as a birthday or wedding.
* **I Confirm the Above Booking Details**: Select "I Confirm" to confirm your booking details. If you need to make changes, select "I Need to Make Changes". 

Please note that all required fields are marked as such. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
