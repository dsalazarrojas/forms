# modern_hotel_booking_form_mobile_responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather booking information from customers on their mobile devices, making it easy for customers to book a room at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your customer and travel information.
- Make sure to fill out each field with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information (Text)** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address. This information will be used to confirm your booking.
- **Travel Information (Text)** (`travel_info`, text, optional): Enter your travel dates, arrival time, and departure time for your stay. This will help the hotel staff to prepare for your arrival.
- **Pick Up Information (Text)** (`pickup_info`, text, optional): If you need transportation, please enter your preferred pick up time and location. This will help us to arrange transportation for you.
- **Special Requests (Text)** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, please let us know in this field.
- **Payment Information (Text)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Please enter your payment method (e.g., credit card number, cash, etc.) and any other payment details.
- **Confirm Information (Text)** (`confirm_info`, text, optional): Double-check your information to ensure everything is correct before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact information].
