# Custom Furniture Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a custom furniture appointment with a business. Customers can input their name, contact information, and a desired schedule time to discuss their furniture needs with a sales representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a desired schedule time using the "Schedule Time" field. This field is a date picker, and you can choose a date and time that works for you.
6. Select a desired schedule date using the "Schedule Date" field. This field is also a date picker, and you can choose a date and time that works for you.
7. Type a message in the "Message" field if you have any specific requests or questions for the sales representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, date, optional): Select a desired schedule time using the date picker. Choose a date and time that works for you.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Select a desired schedule date using the date picker. Choose a date and time that works for you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Type a message if you have any specific requests or questions for the sales representative.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number to ensure that we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
- Be as specific as possible with your schedule time and date to ensure that we can accommodate your request.
- If you have any specific requests or questions, type them in the "Message" field to help us better understand your needs.
