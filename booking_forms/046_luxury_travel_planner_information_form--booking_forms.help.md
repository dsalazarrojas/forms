# Luxury Travel Planner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Travel Planner Information Form is designed to collect essential information about travel agencies, allowing us to provide tailored travel recommendations. This form helps us understand the agency's contact details and travel preferences, enabling us to offer personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your travel agency's name in the "Travel Agency Name" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your travel dates from the list provided in the "Travel Dates" field.
7. Choose the type of travel you are interested in from the "Travel Type" field. You can select one of the following options:
	* Luxury
	* Business
	* Leisure
	* Adventure
8. If you have a budget in mind, you can enter it in the "Budget" field. This will help us provide more accurate recommendations.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency Name (Travel Agency Name)**: Enter your travel agency's name.
* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address for your travel agency.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Dates (select multiple)**: Select the dates you are planning to travel from the list provided.
* **Travel Type (select one)**: Select the type of travel you are interested in from the list:
	* Luxury
	* Business
	* Leisure
	* Adventure
* **Budget (Budget)**: Enter your budget for the trip. Use a numeric value.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your trip preferences or requirements.
