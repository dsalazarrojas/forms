# Recurring Appointment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm recurring appointments with clients, ensuring that all necessary details are accurately recorded and that clients have agreed to the appointment schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Client Details page with the client's information, including any relevant notes or contact details.
2. On the Appointment Details page, provide a clear and concise description of the appointment, including the date, time, and any other relevant details.
3. On the Recurring Details page, specify the frequency and duration of the appointment, and any other recurring details that need to be captured.
4. On the Communication Preferences page, indicate how you would like to communicate with the client, such as email or phone.
5. Add any additional Notes as necessary to capture any other important information.
6. Click the Submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required): Describe the appointment, including date, time, and any other important details.
* **Recurring Details** (`recurring_details`, text, required): Specify the frequency and duration of the appointment, and any other recurring details.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, required): Choose how you would like to communicate with the client, such as email or phone.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Capture any additional information or notes about the appointment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Complete and submit the form to confirm the appointment.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Do not submit the form, and start over.
