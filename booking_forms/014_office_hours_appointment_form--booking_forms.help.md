# office_hours_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an appointment with a staff member during their office hours. You can select a specific staff member, time slot, and other preferences to schedule your meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To book an appointment, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address.
2.  Select the time and date of your preferred meeting.
3.  Choose the length of your meeting, if applicable.
4.  Provide a subject to help staff understand the purpose of your meeting.
5.  Optionally, add any comments for the staff to review.
6.  Select your preferred time slot from the options.
7.  Confirm or decline the meeting request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for staff to contact you.
*   **Time** (text, required): Enter the time you prefer for your meeting.
*   **Date** (date, required): Select the date of your preferred meeting.
*   **Duration** (number, optional): Specify the length of your meeting (if different from default).
*   **Reason** (select one, optional): Indicate whether you have a meeting request.
*   **Subject** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your meeting purpose.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional information for staff review.
*   **Meeting Type** (select one, optional): Choose the type of meeting you wish to have (Meeting, No Meeting, Office Hours).
*   **Available** (select multiple, optional): Select the time slots you are available to meet.
*   **Available Time** (time, optional): Select the specific time you are available to meet.
*   **Available Date** (date, optional): Select the specific date you are available to meet.
*   **Meeting Duration** (number, optional): Specify the length of your meeting.
*   **Preferred Time** (time, optional): Select your preferred time for the meeting.
*   **Preferred Date** (date, optional): Select your preferred date for the meeting.
*   **Preferred Duration** (number, optional): Specify the preferred length of your meeting.
*   **Meeting Request** (select one, optional): Confirm or decline the meeting request.
*   **Meeting Request (2)** (select one, optional): This seems to be an error in the form design. Ignore this field, as it's a duplicate of "Meeting Request."
*   **Meeting Request (3)** (select one, optional): This seems to be another error in the form design. Ignore this field, as it's a duplicate of "Meeting Request."
