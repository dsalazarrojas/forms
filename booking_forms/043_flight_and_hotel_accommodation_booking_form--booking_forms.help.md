# Flight And Hotel Accommodation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book flights and hotel accommodations for travel-related activities, such as meetings, business trips, or leisure activities. It helps to gather necessary information for travel planning, including flights, hotel reservations, and meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respond to meeting invitation** (`respond_to_meeting_invitation`, text, optional): Respond to a meeting invitation if you have received one.
* **Book flight** (`book_flight_prophets`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more flights for your travel (e.g., United States, Canada, Mexico).
* **Get hotel accommodation prospects** (`get_hotel_accommodation_prophets`, number, optional): Please note the hotel number for your booking (e.g., 123).
* **Flight number** (`flight_number`, number, required): Enter the flight number for the booked flight.
* **Hotel number** (`hotel_number`, number, required): Enter the hotel number for the booked hotel.
* **Travel dates** (`travel_prophets`, date, optional): Select the date(s) of your travel.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of your travel.
* **End date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of your travel.
* **Travel purpose** (`travel_prophets`, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of your travel (e.g., Meeting, Business, Leisure).
* **Flight cost** (`flight_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of your flight.
* **Hotel cost** (`hotel_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of your hotel reservation.
* **Travel cost** (`travel_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total travel cost.
* **Meeting location** (`meeting_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your meeting or business trip.
* **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your meeting or business trip.
* **Flight details** (`flight_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your flight.
* **Hotel details** (`hotel_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your hotel reservation.
