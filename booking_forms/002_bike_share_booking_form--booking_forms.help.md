# Bike Share Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike Share Booking Form is designed for booking a bike share. Fill out this form with your personal and bike preference details to book a bike share for your intended journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Full name** (including given and family name) in the first field.
2. If you are a member of a program, provide your **Membership id** for any applicable discounts or benefits.
3. Enter a valid **Email address** for booking confirmations and receipts.
4. Enter a **Phone number** that we can reach you at for any updates or issues.
5. Choose a **Journey start date** for your bike share booking.
6. If your booking is for multiple days, select a **Journey end date**.
7. Choose the nearest **Pickup station** where you would like to collect your bike.
8. Select the **Dropoff station** where you will return the bike.
9. Choose any **Bike preferences** you may have, such as a helmet or child seat.
10. If you have any **Special requirements**, enter any accessibility or extra assistance details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name (including given and family name).
* **Membership id**: If you are a member of a program, enter your membership ID for any applicable discounts or benefits. (Optional)
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for booking confirmations and receipts.
* **Phone number**: Enter a reachable phone number for any updates or issues.
* **Journey start date**: Select the date you would like to start your bike share journey.
* **Journey end date**: If your booking is for multiple days, select the end date for your journey.
* **Pickup station**: Select the nearest station where you would like to collect your bike.
* **Dropoff station**: Select the station where you will return the bike.
* **Bike preferences**: Choose any preferences you have, such as a helmet or child seat.
* **Special requirements**: Enter any accessibility or extra assistance details you may need.
