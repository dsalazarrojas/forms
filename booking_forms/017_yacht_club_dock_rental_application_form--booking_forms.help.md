# Yacht Club Dock Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yacht Club Dock Rental Application Form is designed for users to submit their request for yacht club dock rental services. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a smooth and efficient processing of your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Request Form section with your contact information (Applicant Name, Email, Phone), information about your boat (Boat Type), and your request details (Rental Date, Time, Duration, and Special Requirements).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`1`, text, required/optional): Fill in your request message. This can be a brief description of your needs or any special requirements you have.
* **Applicant Name** (`2`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the applicant.
* **Email** (`3`, email, required/optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates on your application.
* **Phone** (`4`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Boat Type** (`5`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of boat you have (sail, power, or sail power).
* **Rental Date** (`6`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to rent the dock.
* **Rental Time** (`7`, time, required/optional): Select the time slot you prefer for your rental.
* **Duration** (`8`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your rental period in hours.
* **Request Details** (`9`, text, required/optional): Describe any special requirements or details about your request.
* **Special Requirements** (`10`, note, required/optional): If you have any special requirements for your request, please list them here.
* **Status** (`14`, select_one, required/optional): The status of your application (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly to ensure a successful processing of your application.
