# DJ Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DJ Service Inquiry Form is designed to collect information about potential events that may require DJ services. This form will be used to gather details about the event, including event information and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with information about the event you are inquiring about, such as the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** in the next section, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you to discuss your event further.
3. In the **Service Details** section, describe the type of services you are looking for, such as music genre, number of guests, or any specific requirements.
4. Finally, provide a brief description of your **Inquiry Details** to help us understand your event better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required: false): This is where you will provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, required: false): Describe the type of services you are looking for, such as music genre, number of guests, or any specific requirements.
* **Inquiry Details** (Inquiry Details, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your inquiry, such as any specific questions or requests you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure we can get in touch with you and provide the best service for your event.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please make sure to mention them in the **Inquiry Details** section.
* You can also attach any supporting documents or files to your inquiry if needed.
