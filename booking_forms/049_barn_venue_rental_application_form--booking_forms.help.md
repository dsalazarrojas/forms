# Barn Venue Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for booking and renting a barn venue for various events. The venue offers different services and amenities, including kitchen usage, rustic add-ons, and insurance requirements. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide essential details about your event, including the type of event, preferred date, and number of guests, among other things.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of event you are planning (e.g., wedding, corporate retreat, family reunion, etc.).
3. Choose a preferred date for your event.
4. Estimate the number of guests attending your event.
5. Choose the start and end times for your event.
6. Select whether you will need to use the kitchen facilities.
7. Choose any additional services you would like to book (e.g., hay bale seating, string lighting, etc.).
8. Acknowledge that you understand and agree to provide insurance for your event.
9. Confirm that you will serve alcohol according to the venue's policy.
10. Provide any additional details or requests for your event.
11. Sign and date the form to complete the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`): Enter your primary name as the contact person for your event.
* **Organization / Company Name** (`organization_name`): If you are booking the venue for a corporate or non-profit event, enter your organization's name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address where the venue can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number where the venue can contact you.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`): Select the type of event you are planning (e.g., wedding, corporate retreat, family reunion, etc.).
* **Preferred Event Date** (`preferred_event_date`): Choose a date for your event, considering that it will be checked for availability.
* **Estimated Number of Guests** (`estimated_guest_count`): Estimate the number of guests attending your event, with a maximum capacity of 200.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`): Enter the start time of your event, including guest arrival.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`): Enter the end time of your event.
* **Will you require use of the Prep Kitchen?** (`kitchen_usage`): Select whether you will need to use the kitchen facilities.
* **Rustic Add-on Services** (`rustic_add_ons`): Choose any additional services you would like to book (e.g., hay bale seating, string lighting, etc.).
* **Event Insurance Requirement** (`insurance_acknowledgement`): Acknowledge that you will provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) listing the venue as additionally insured.
* **Alcohol Policy** (`alcohol_policy_check`): Confirm that you understand and agree to serve alcohol according to the venue's policy.
* **Additional Details or Special Requests** (`detailed_request`): Describe any unique needs or requests for your event.
* **Submission Date** (`application_date`): This date will be automatically populated with the date the form is submitted.
* **Applicant Electronic Signature** (`digital_signature`): Sign and date the form to complete the application process.
