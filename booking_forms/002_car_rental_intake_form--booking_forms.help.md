# Car Rental Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book a car rental. Please fill out the form with accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth and convenient rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details: Provide your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
2. Enter your email address: This will be used for booking confirmation.
3. Enter your phone number: Include your area code.
4. Select the pickup and drop-off dates: Choose the dates for your rental period.
5. Choose your preferred vehicle type: Select from the options provided.
6. Enter your pickup and drop-off locations: Enter the branch or address where you'll pick up and drop off the vehicle.
7. Additional requests: Specify any additional requests, such as child seats or GPS accessibility needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license. 
* **Email address**: Provide your email address to receive booking confirmation. 
* **Phone number**: Include your area code in your phone number. 
* **Pick up date**: Select the date you want to pick up the vehicle. 
* **Drop off date**: Select the date you plan to return the vehicle. 
* **Vehicle type**: Choose the class of vehicle you prefer. 
* **Pick up location**: Enter the branch or address where you'll pick up the vehicle. 
* **Drop off location**: If different from pickup, enter the address where you'll return the vehicle. 
* **Additional requests**: Specify any additional requirements, such as child seats or GPS accessibility needs.

## Tips
- Please double-check your entered information for accuracy.
- Select the correct vehicle type to ensure availability.
- Consider specifying additional requests to make your rental experience more enjoyable.
- If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
