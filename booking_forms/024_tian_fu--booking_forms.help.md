# tian_fu_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for booking forms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) after the field label.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields:
	* "Shipping Info": Enter a brief description of the shipping details.
	* "Shipper": Enter the name of the shipper.
	* "Shipper Phone": Enter the phone number of the shipper.
	* "Shipper Email": Enter the email of the shipper.
	* "Shipping Method": Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether shipping is enabled.
	* "Shipping Date": Enter the date of the shipment.
	* "Airline Name": Enter the name of the airline.
	* "Flight Number": Enter the flight number.
	* "Flight Time": Enter the flight time.
	* "Customer Name": Enter the name of the customer.
	* "Customer Phone": Enter the phone number of the customer.
	* "Customer Email": Enter the email of the customer.
	* "Order Number": Enter the number of the order.
	* "Order Status": Select multiple options from "Active", "Inactive".
	* "Payment Method": Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether payment is method.
	* "Order Date": Enter the date of the order.
	* "Remarks": Enter any additional remarks.
	* "Order Type": Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of order.
	* "Shipping Status": Select multiple options from "Active", "Inactive".
	* "Remarks 2": Enter any additional remarks.
	* "Order Details": Enter any additional order details.
