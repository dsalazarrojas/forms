# Massage Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a massage appointment at our wellness center. Please fill out the form below to provide us with your massage preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose your answer for "Massage Reservation Form" (Yes/No)
2. Provide your name
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your phone number
5. Choose your massage duration and type
6. Select your preferred date for the massage
7. Choose the price range you are willing to pay
8. Provide a massage suggestion or note for our therapists
9. Leave any additional comments or requests

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Massage Reservation Form** (`select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" if you have a specific massage request or "No" if you are open to our recommendations.
* **Name** (`text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Massage Duration** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred massage duration. Options may vary depending on therapist availability and your health goals.
* **Massage Type** (`select_one`, optional): Select your preferred type of massage, such as Swedish or deep tissue.
* **Price** (`number`, optional): Choose the price range you are willing to pay for the massage.
* **Massage Suggestion** (`text`, optional): Provide any specific massage suggestion or request for your therapist.
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Leave any additional comments or requests for your massage appointment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you have a preferred therapist or "No" if you're open to being assigned one.
