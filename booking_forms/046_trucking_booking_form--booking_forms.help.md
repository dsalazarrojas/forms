# Trucking Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trucking Booking Form is used to book and manage trucking services for customers. It captures essential information for efficient and secure booking processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, select a **Date** for your booking in the "Date" field.
6. If applicable, select a **Time** for your booking in the "Time" field.
7. Enter your **Truck ID** in the "Truck ID" field.
8. Choose a **Booking Date** for your truck in the "Booking Date" field.
9. Choose a **Booking Time** for your truck in the "Booking Time" field.
10. Optionally, add any additional **Notes** for your booking.
11. If required, select a **Truck** from the dropdown list in the "Select Truck" field.
12. If necessary, **Select Multiple Trucks** from the available list in the "Select Multiple Trucks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is required to ensure accurate identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is required to identify you correctly.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address. This field is required for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is required for easy communication.
* **Date**: If you have a specific date in mind for your booking, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Time**: If you have a specific time for your booking, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Truck ID**: Enter the ID of the truck you want to book. This field is required for identification.
* **Booking Date**: Choose a date for your booking. This field is required for scheduling.
* **Booking Time**: Choose a time for your booking. This field is required for scheduling.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about your booking. (Optional)
* **Select Truck**: Select a single truck for your booking from the available options. (Optional)
* **Select Multiple Trucks**: Select multiple trucks for your booking from the available options. (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient booking processing.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
