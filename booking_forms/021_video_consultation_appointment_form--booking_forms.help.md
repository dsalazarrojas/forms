# video_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for video consultation appointments. It helps the service provider understand the client's preferences and needs for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the Client Information page with your basic details.
2. Select a preferred date for the appointment from the provided calendar.
3. Specify your preferred time for the appointment.
4. Choose the type of service you require from the options provided.
5. If necessary, select an exact appointment time.
6. Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
7. If applicable, select your chosen service provider.
8. Enter your email address for further communication.
9. Add any additional appointment notes if you have any specific requests or questions.
10. If necessary, specify the type of service you are seeking in more detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information**: Please enter your name, address, or contact information to help the service provider get in touch with you.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Gather basic client details.

- **Preferred Date**: Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Schedule your appointment on a date and time you prefer.

- **Preferred Time**: Specify a time for your appointment.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Choose a time that suits you for the appointment.

- **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Inform the service provider about the nature of your consultation.

- **Appointment Time**: If you've selected a preferred time, you can further specify it here.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Clarify the exact appointment time if you've already specified a preferred time.

- **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Ensure the service provider can contact you.

- **Service Provider**: Select the service provider you're interested in consulting with.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Allow the client to choose their preferred service provider.

- **Client Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Enable service providers to contact you via email.

- **Appointment Notes**: Add any additional comments or requests you have for the appointment.
  - Type: note
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Provide any specific needs or requests you have for the consultation.

- **Service Type Options**: Further specify the type of service you require.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false
  - Purpose: Provide more detailed information about the service you seek.
