# cricket_net_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a cricket net or a facility in the cricket domain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of booking: Cricket net or facility.
2. Choose the start time of the booking: 9:00 am, 9:30 am, 10:00 am, or 10:30 am.
3. Specify the duration of the booking.
4. Check the net availability: Booked, available, under maintenance, or private booking.
5. Provide player name.
6. Enter contact number.
7. Enter email.
8. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Net Type** (`net_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of booking: Cricket net or facility.
* **Booking Start Time** (`booking_start_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the start time of the booking: 9:00 am, 9:30 am, 10:00 am, or 10:30 am.
* **Duration** (`booking_duration`, number, required/optional): Specify the duration of the booking.
* **Net Availability** (`net_availability`, select_one, required/optional): Check the net availability: Booked, available, under maintenance, or private booking.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the player's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email.
* **Booking Comment** (`booking_comment`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments related to the booking.
* **Additional Comments** (`booking_comment_2`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the booking.
