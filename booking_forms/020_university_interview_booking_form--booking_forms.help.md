# university_interview_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for university students to book interviews with the institution. It captures necessary details to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
* Provide your contact phone number in the "Student Contact Phone" field.
* Select a date for the interview from the "Interview Date" field.
* Choose an interview time that suits you from the "Interview Time" field.
* Indicate the interview duration from the "Interview Duration" field.
* Select the interviewer(s) from the "Interviewers" field, if applicable.
* Choose the booking method for the interview from the "Booking Method" field, which can be "In-person", "Online", or "Phone".
* Select the location of the interview from the "Location" field, which can be "Campus", "Off-Campus", or "Remote".
* Update the booking status from the "Booking Status" field. This is a required field and should be set to "Confirmed" once the booking is confirmed.
* Add any comments or additional notes in the "Comments" field.
* Provide any additional information in the "Additional Notes" field.
* Finally, fill in the "Created By" field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (text)**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Interview Date (date)**: Select a date for the interview.
* **Interview Time (time)**: Choose an interview time.
* **Interview Duration (number)**: Indicate the duration of the interview in minutes or hours.
* **Interviewers (select_multiple)**: Select the interviewer(s) for the interview, if applicable.
* **Booking Method (select_one)**: Choose the method of booking: "In-person", "Online", or "Phone".
* **Location (select_one)**: Choose the location of the interview: "Campus", "Off-Campus", or "Remote".
* **Booking Status (select_one)**: Update the booking status to "Confirmed" once the booking is confirmed.
* **Comments (note)**: Add any comments or additional notes about the interview.
* **Additional Notes (text)**: Provide any additional information.
* **Created By (text)**: Enter your name.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant authorities.
