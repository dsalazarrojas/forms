# Vintage Car Exhibit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vintage Car Exhibit Reservation Form is a tool for attendees to reserve their spot for the event. It collects vital information such as attendee details, event schedule, and specific car model preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee information (page 1).
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address (optional).
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Choose the date you plan to visit.
6. Choose a time you plan to visit.
7. Select a car model you're interested in.
8. Specify the year of the car model.
9. Identify whether the car is participating in the event.
10. Enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information (1)** (`attendee_information`, text, required): Provide your full name and any other relevant details.
* **Name (2)** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or identification.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for event updates and information.
* **Phone (4)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date (5)** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date you plan to visit the exhibit.
* **Time (6)** (`time`, time, required): Select a time slot for your visit.
* **Car Model (7)** (`car_model`, select_one, required): Choose the vintage car model you're interested in.
* **Year (8)** (`year`, select_multiple, required): Select the year of the vintage car model you've chosen.
* **Event ID (9)** (`event_id`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the car model is participating in the event.
* **Comments (10)** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
