# Camping Ground Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users for booking a campsite at a camping ground. This form collects the reserver's personal information, reservation dates, campsite details, party details, equipment information, and preferred confirmation method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, street address, city, state, and zip code in the "Reserving Party Information" section.
3. Select your arrival and departure dates.
4. Choose the number of nights you plan to stay.
5. In the "Campsite Details" section, choose the type of campsite you prefer (e.g., Tent Site, RV Site with Full Hookup, etc.).
6. If you have a preferred campsite number, enter it in the "Preferred Campsite Number" field.
7. If you have a preference for a location, choose from the options provided.
8. In the "Party Details" section, enter the number of adults and children (ages 5-17) in your party.
9. If you have any toddlers under the age of 5, enter their number in the "Number of Toddlers" field.
10. If you're bringing a vehicle, enter its type and length.
11. If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them in the "Special Requirements or Requests" field.
12. Finally, choose your preferred confirmation method (e.g., email, phone, or SMS).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street Address** (Required): Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City** (Required): Enter the city where the camping ground is located.
* **State** (Required): Enter the state where the camping ground is located.
* **Zip Code** (Required): Enter the zip code where the camping ground is located.
* **Arrival Date** (Required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the camping ground.
* **Departure Date** (Required): Enter the date you plan to leave the camping ground.
* **Number of Nights** (Required): Enter the number of nights you plan to stay at the camping ground.
* **Campsite Type** (Required): Choose the type of campsite you prefer (e.g., Tent Site, RV Site with Full Hookup, etc.).
* **Preferred Campsite Number** (Optional): If you have a preferred campsite number, enter it here.
* **Location Preference** (Optional): Choose your preferred location (e.g., Waterfront, Forest, etc.).
* **Number of Adults** (Required): Enter the number of adults in your party.
* **Number of Children** (Optional): Enter the number of children (ages 5-17) in your party.
* **Number of Toddlers** (Optional): Enter the number of toddlers (under 5) in your party.
* **Vehicle Type** (Optional): If you're bringing a vehicle, enter its type (e.g., RV, trailer, etc.).
* **Vehicle Length** (Optional): If you're bringing a vehicle, enter its length in feet.
* **Special Requirements or Requests** (Optional): Enter any special requirements or requests you may have.
* **Preferred Confirmation Method** (Required): Choose how you'd like to receive confirmation of your reservation (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
