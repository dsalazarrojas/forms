# Flash Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for flash booking requests. It helps us understand your requirements and provide the best possible solution for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page (Form 1) and enter your service request in the **Service Request** field.
2. On the same page, select the appropriate **Service Type** from the dropdown list.
3. Proceed to the subsequent pages to fill in the remaining fields as needed, including **Prophets** fields.
4. Fill in the **Prophets2**, **Prophets3**, **Prophets4**, **Prophets5**, **Prophets6**, **Prophets7** fields with the required or desired information.
5. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, optional): Describe your issue or request for service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you require.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, optional): Enter prophets (this field may be used to collect specific information).
* **Prophets2** (`prophets2`, number, optional): Enter prophets 2.
* **Prophets3** (`prophets3`, number, optional): Enter prophets 3.
* **Prophets4** (`prophets4`, number, optional): Enter prophets 4.
* **Prophets5** (`prophets5`, number, optional): Enter prophets 5.
* **Prophets6** (`prophets6`, date, optional): Select a date for prophets.
* **Prophets7** (`prophets7`, time, optional): Select a time for prophets.
* **Prophets8** (`prophets7`, text, optional): Enter additional information about prophets.
