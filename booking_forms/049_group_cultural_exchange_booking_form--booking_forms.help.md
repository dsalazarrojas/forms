# Group Cultural Exchange Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Group Cultural Exchange Booking Form is a tool designed to gather information from participants about a group cultural exchange event. This form helps us understand your preferences, interests, and any necessary information required for a successful and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the guidelines below:

1. Please fill out each page carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the correct form for the correct event and that you are a registered participant.
3. Review each field to understand what it's for and how to fill it out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required=false): This field is for you to provide a brief description of the trip or event you are booking. Please write a short paragraph explaining the trip's purpose, duration, and any specific details that would help us understand your request.
* **Participants** (`participants`, select_one, required=false): Select the number of participants you are booking for this event from the provided options. Please choose the correct number that applies to your group.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, text, required=false): This field is for you to provide any specific preferences or requirements you have for the event. Please use this space to share any dietary restrictions, mobility concerns, or other relevant information.
* **Preference 1** (`participant_preferences_1`, select_multiple, required=false): Select the preferences you have from the options provided. Please choose one or more that apply to you.
* **Preference 2** (`participant_preferences_2`, select_one, required=false): Select the preference you have from the provided options. Please choose one that applies to you.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields and provide accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to the event description or consult with the event organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us and we will be happy to assist you.
