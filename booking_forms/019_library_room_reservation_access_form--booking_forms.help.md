# Library Room Reservation Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request access to library rooms for study, meetings, or other purposes. It is designed to collect necessary information to efficiently process your request and ensure that the room is available for you during the requested time period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Information**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
2. **Date and Time**: Select the start and end dates and times you would like to reserve the room.
3. **Room Preferences**: Choose the rooms you prefer from the list of available options.
4. **Availability**: Specify whether or not you prefer to be notified of room conflicts.
5. **Date and Time Format**: Choose the date and time format you prefer to use.
6. **Timezone**: Enter your preferred timezone.
7. **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your reservation request.
8. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for notifications.
9. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
10. **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your reservation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Date Start**: Select the start date for your reservation request.
* **Time Start**: Select the start time for your reservation request.
* **Date End**: Select the end date for your reservation request.
* **Time End**: Select the end time for your reservation request.
* **Room**: Choose the preferred room(s) for your reservation request.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your reservation request.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address for notifications.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Room**: Choose the preferred room(s) for your reservation request.
* **Room Availability**: Specify whether or not you want to be notified of room conflicts.
* **Date Format**: Choose the date format you prefer to use.
* **Time Format**: Choose whether or not to be notified for time conflicts.
* **Timezone**: Enter your preferred timezone.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your reservation request.
