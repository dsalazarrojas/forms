# Quick Meeting Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Meeting Scheduler form is used to request meetings with colleagues or team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter a title for your meeting request.
2. Choose a meeting time that suits you best.
3. Select a date for the meeting.
4. Choose a location for the meeting.
5. Indicate if you want to invite attendees or not.
6. Provide any additional meeting details.
7. Choose a follow-up option (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Request** (`meeting_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your meeting request.
* **Meeting Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a time slot that suits you best for the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to have the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who you want to invite to the meeting. You can select 'Yes' or 'No' for each attendee.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the meeting.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Choose a follow-up option to schedule another meeting or to send a reminder.
