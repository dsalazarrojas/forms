# Guest Wi Fi Access Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Wi Fi Access Booking Form is a document used for guests to request access to the office WiFi during their visit. It is meant to gather essential details about the guest's visit, such as their contact information and visit details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest details, including your name, email, phone number, and organization.
2. Provide your host's information, including their name, email, phone number, and organization.
3. Select the purpose of your visit and the type of visit.
4. Choose your visit start and end dates and times.
5. Read and acknowledge the policies and terms of use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as a guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Guest Address** (`guest_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Guest Organization** (`guest_organization`, `text`, optional): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Guest ID** (`guest_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your guest ID or any other relevant ID number.
* **Host** (`host`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or group hosting you.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the host.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the host.
* **Host Address** (`host_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the host.
* **Host Organization** (`host_organization`, `text`, optional): Enter the organization or company name of the host.
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of visit (Office visit, School visit, Hospitals visit, Venue visit).
* **Visit Purpose** (`visit_purpose`, `select_one`, optional): Select the purpose of your visit (Meeting, Event, Project, Other).
* **Visit Start Date** (`visit_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your visit start.
* **Visit Start Time** (`visit_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your visit start.
* **Visit End Date** (`visit_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your visit end.
* **Visit End Time** (`visit_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your visit end.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your visit.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, `text`, optional): Acknowledge the policies and terms of use.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 2** (`policy_acknowledgment_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you acknowledge or do not acknowledge the terms and conditions.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 3** (`policy_acknowledgment_3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you acknowledge or do not acknowledge the terms and conditions.
