# photo_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo session booking form is used to book a photo session with a photographer. To use this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and preferences, and submit it to the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the booking form with your information and preferences.
2. Select the session type you would like to book (Standard, Premium, or Extended).
3. Choose the date and time for your session.
4. Enter the location for your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking Form** (`booking_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main form where you can enter your information and preferences.
- **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of session you would like to book. Options include Standard, Premium, and Extended.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for your photo session.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for your photo session.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location for your photo session.
