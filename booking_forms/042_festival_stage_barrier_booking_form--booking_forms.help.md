# Festival Stage Barrier Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book festival stage barriers for your event. Please fill out the form with the required information and ensure that you are accurately representing your event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct options for each field and fill in the required information. If you have any issues or need assistance, please contact your event organizer or event coordinator for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Organizer
* **Event Organizer (Yes/No)**: Check if you are the event organizer or not. This field helps us determine your role in the event.
### Barrier Type
* **Barrier Type (Stage Barrier/Stage Wing)**: Select the type of barrier you need (Stage Barrier or Stage Wing).
### Quantity
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of barriers you need. This will help us determine the correct quantity of barriers to be delivered to you.
### Location
* **Location**: Enter the location where the barriers will be delivered. Make sure to enter the exact address or location to ensure accurate delivery.
### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like to receive the barriers. Please note that we will do our best to meet your requested date.
### Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time of day you would like to receive the barriers.
### Delivery Location
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the location where the barriers will be delivered. This should match the location you entered for the "Location" field.
### Rental Duration
* **Rental Duration (Weekdays/Weekends/Daily)**: Select how long you would like to rent the barriers for. Please note that we will charge according to your selection.
### Rental Date
* **Rental Date**: Select the date you would like to start renting the barriers. Please ensure this date matches the delivery date.
### Rental Time
* **Rental Time**: Select the time of day you would like to start renting the barriers.
### Comments
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your barrier request. This helps us understand your requirements better.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your barrier request.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
### Comments (2)
* **Comments**: This is a duplicate of the "Comments" field. Please ignore this field and only fill out the first "Comments" field.
### Event Name
* **Event Name (Yes/No)**: Check if your event has a name. If yes, please enter it. This helps us associate your event with the barrier request.
### Event Description
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of your event. This will help us understand the context of your barrier request.
