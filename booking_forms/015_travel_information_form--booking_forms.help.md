# travel-information-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting travel information for trips.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked as "required".
2. Select relevant information for non-required fields as per their labels.
3. Make sure to review your submission for consistency and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details**: A general description of the trip.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, `date`): The date of the trip.
* **Duration** (`trip_duration`, `select_one`): The duration of the trip.
* **Destination** (`destination`, `text`): The destination of the trip.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, `text`): The purpose of the trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`): The travel agency used for the trip.
* **Travel Method** (`travel_method`, `select_one`): The method of travel (e.g., car, plane, etc.).
* **Traveler Contact Info** (`traveler_contact_info`, `text`): Contact information of the traveler.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, `text`): Documents required for the trip.
* **Trip Leader** (`trip_leader`, `text`): The leader of the trip.
* **Trip Status** (`trip_status`, `select_one`): The status of the trip.
* **Trip Category** (`trip_category`, `text`): The category of the trip.
* **Travel Cost** (`travel_cost`, `number`): The cost of the trip.
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`, `select_one`): The currency of the trip cost.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`): The destination of the trip (same as Destination).
* **Duration 2** (`trip_duration_2`, `date`): The duration of the trip.
* **Duration 3** (`trip_duration_3`, `date`): The duration of the trip.
* **Duration 4** (`trip_duration_4`, `text`): The duration of the trip (not recommended to be filled in).
* **Duration 5** (`trip_duration_5`, `text`): The duration of the trip (not recommended to be filled in).
* **Trip Date 1** (`trip_date_1`, `date`): The date of the trip.
* **Trip Date 2** (`trip_date_2`, `date`): The date of the trip.
* **Trip Duration 2** (`trip_duration_2`, `date`): The duration of the trip.
* **Trip Duration 3** (`trip_duration_3`, `date`): The duration of the trip.
* **Trip Status 2** (`trip_status_2`, `select_one`): The status of the trip.
* **Trip Status 3** (`trip_status_3`, `select_one`): The status of the trip.
