# travel-information-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting travel information for trips.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked as "required".
2. Select relevant information for non-required fields as per their labels.
3. Make sure to review your submission for consistency and accuracy.
