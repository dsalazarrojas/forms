# resort_stay_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from guests to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay at the resort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact information and stay details.
2. Select your preferred room type and room count.
3. Choose any special room services you would like to be provided during your stay.
4. Provide any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (`guest_info`, text, required): This field is for your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to depart from the resort.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of room you prefer (Single, Double, Suite).
* **Room Count** (`room_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of rooms you will need.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Write any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the staff.
* **Assigned Staff** (`assigned_staff`, select_one, optional): Select the staff member who will be assigned to your room.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about your stay.
* **Room Services** (`room_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any room services you would like to have during your stay (Breakfast, Wi-Fi, Parking).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your stay is confirmed.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the resort staff for assistance.
