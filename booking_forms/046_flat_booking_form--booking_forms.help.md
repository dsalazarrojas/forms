# Flat Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic information for booking a flat or apartment.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your contact information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address (for communication purposes only).
2. Choose your booking preference:
	* **Building**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you would like to book a flat in this building.
	* **Floor**: Enter the desired floor number.
3. Provide more details:
	* **Unit**: Enter the desired unit number.
	* **Address 1**: Enter the first part of the address.
	* **Address 2**: Enter the second part of the address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
4. Add a message:
	* **Message**: Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Building** (`building_id`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your building preference.
- **Floor** (`floor`, number, required: false): Enter the desired floor number.
- **Unit** (`unit`, text, required: false): Enter the desired unit number.
- **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required: false): Enter the first part of the address.
- **Address 2** (`address2`, text, required: false): Enter the second part of the address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Add a Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information.
- **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number again to confirm.
- **Confirm Message** (`confirm_message`, text, required: false): Enter your message again to confirm.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for general information purposes only and is subject to change.
- Please review and agree to our terms and conditions before proceeding.
