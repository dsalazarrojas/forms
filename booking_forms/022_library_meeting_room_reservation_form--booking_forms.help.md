# Library Meeting Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Meeting Room Reservation Form is designed to help library staff reserve meeting rooms for events and activities. This form allows users to provide details about the meeting room, event, and attendees to ensure smooth and efficient reservation management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meeting room you are interested in (Meeting Room).
2. Choose the room number you wish to reserve from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the start time of your event.
4. Enter the end time of your event.
5. Provide the number of attendees (if applicable).
6. Select the type of meeting (if applicable).
7. Enter a brief description of your event.
8. Enter the event start date.
9. Enter the event end date.
10. Select the reservation status (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional notes or comments.
12. Enter the reservation user's name and contact information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Room** (`meeting_room`, select_one): Choose the meeting room you wish to reserve from the dropdown list.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text): Enter the specific room number you are interested in reserving.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of your event.
* **Room Capacity** (`room_capacity`, text): Enter the expected number of attendees for your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text): Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple): Check if there will be attendees at your event (Yes/No).
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, date): Enter the start date of your event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, date): Enter the end date of your event.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one): Select the type of meeting (if applicable).
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text): Enter a title for your event.
* **Room Configuration** (`room_configuration`, select_multiple): Check if any special room configuration is required (Yes/No).
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date): Enter the date of your meeting.
* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, time): Enter the start time of your meeting.
* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, time): Enter the end time of your meeting.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date): Enter the date you reserved the meeting room.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time): Enter the time you reserved the meeting room.
* **Meeting Room** (`meeting_room`, select_one): Select the meeting room you wish to reserve.
* **Reservation Status** (`reservation_status`, select_one): Select the current reservation status (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Reservation User** (`reservation_user`, text): Enter the name and contact information of the person reserving the meeting room.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the reservation user's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the reservation user's email address.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of your event.
