# credit_strategy_session_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit strategy session registration form is used to register and gather information about credit strategy sessions. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. Use the options provided for select fields to choose the most accurate response.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (session_details, text, required: false): This field captures a brief description of the session, including its purpose and objectives.
* **Session Participants** (participant_count, select_one, required: false): Choose the number of participants expected for this session.
	+ Select "Fewer than 5" for less than 5 participants.
	+ Select "5 or more" for 5 or more participants.
* **Participant Names** (participant_names, text, required: false): List the names of all participants in this session.
* **Participant Roles** (participant_roles, text, required: false): List the roles or positions of the participants in this session.
* **Schedule Date** (session_date, date, required: false): Enter the date for this session.
* **Session Time** (session_time, time, required: false): Enter the time for this session.
* **Session Notes** (session_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about this session.
* **Credit Strategy** (credit_strategy, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the credit strategy approach for this session.
	+ Select "Good" for a good credit strategy.
	+ Select "Fair" for a fair credit strategy.
* **Credit Strategy Description** (credit_strategy_description, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the credit strategy.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (contact_method, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred contact method.
	+ Select "Email" for email contact.
	+ Select "Phone" for phone contact.
* **Additional Notes** (additional_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about this session.

## Tips

* Please double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the project documentation or seek guidance from your team.
* Ensure that all relevant information is captured consistently across forms and data sources.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your team for clarification.
