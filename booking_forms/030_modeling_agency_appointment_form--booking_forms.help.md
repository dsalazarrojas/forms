# Modeling Agency Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a booking at a modeling agency, gathering details about the client, the agency, and the services required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the booking date on page 1.
2. On page 1, select the appointment time.
3. On page 2, enter the client's details, including their name, contact number, and email address.
4. On page 3, enter the agency's details, including their name, address, and contact information.
5. On page 4, enter the model's details, including their name and contact information.
6. On page 5, select the required services.
7. On page 6, enter any special requests.
8. Confirm the booking by selecting 'True' on page 7.
9. Finally, sign the form with your client signature on page 7 and the agency signature on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Date (page 1)**: Enter the date of the booking.
* **Appointment Time (page 1)**: Select the appointment time for the booking.
* **Client Details (page 2)**: Enter the client's name, contact number, and email address.
* **Agency Details (page 3)**: Enter the agency's name, address, and contact information.
* **Model Details (page 4)**: Enter the model's name and contact information.
* **Services (page 5)**: Select the required services for the booking.
* **Special Requests (page 6)**: Enter any special requests for the booking.
* **Confirmation (page 7)**: Confirm the booking by selecting 'True'.
* **Client Signature (page 7)**: Sign the form with your client signature.
* **Agency Signature (page 8)**: Sign the form with your agency signature.
* **Notes (page 8)**: Add any additional notes about the booking.
