# Kj Mobile Repair - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers to provide their information and select the type of service they require for their mobile device.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling out the "Customer Information" section, if desired.
2. Select the type of service you require for your mobile device from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
3. Finally, select the date you want to have your mobile device serviced using the "Service Date" calendar picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`Text`, Optional): Enter your details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Service Type (service_type)** (`Select One`, Optional): Choose the type of service you require for your mobile device. Options are:
	+ Mobile Repair
	+ Mobile Maintenance
	+ Mobile Diagnostic
* **Service Date (service_date)** (`Date`, Optional): Select the date you want to have your mobile device serviced.
