# Booking Form With Tip Option - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a booking service, allowing clients to provide details about their service needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of service you need (e.g., Cleaning, Delivery, etc.).
3. Choose your preferred Booking Date and Time.
4. Select the Estimated Service Duration.
5. Enter the Service Location Address.
6. Provide a brief description of the service you need.
7. Enter the total cost of the service.
8. Choose your preferred Payment Method.
9. Select your Tip Amount or enter a Custom Tip Amount if needed.
10. Provide any Special Instructions if necessary.
11. Review the Total Amount with Tip to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (text input): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (email input): Enter your email address to receive booking confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (text input): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Type** (select-one input): Choose the type of service you need (e.g., Cleaning, Delivery, etc.).
* **Preferred Booking Date** (date input): Enter the date when you need the service.
* **Preferred Time** (select-one input): Choose the time of the service.
* **Estimated Service Duration** (select-one input): Select the estimated duration of the service.
* **Service Location Address** (text input): Enter the address where the service will be provided.
* **Service Description** (text input): Provide a brief description of the service you need.
* **Total cost of the service** (number input): Enter the total cost of the service.
* **Payment Method** (select-one input): Choose the method of payment (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
* **Tip Amount** (select-one input): Select the tip amount or choose "Custom amount" to enter your preferred tip amount.
* **Custom Tip Amount** (number input): If you selected "Custom amount" in the previous field, enter the custom tip amount.
* **Special Instructions** (text input): Enter any special instructions or notes.
* **Total Amount with Tip** (number input): Review the total amount with tip to ensure accuracy.

Note: Some fields are optional, but all required fields are indicated by the "required" label in the form schema.
