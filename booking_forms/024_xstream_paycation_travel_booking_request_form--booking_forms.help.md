# xstream_paycation_travel_booking_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The xstream_paycation_travel_booking_request_form is a form used to request travel bookings, providing a platform for users to enter their travel preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Enter your travel date in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
3. Select the destination of your travel from the available options (if any).
4. Enter the duration of your stay in numerical format (e.g., 3 for 3 days).
5. Choose whether you have a 'Yes' or 'No' answer for the type of travel (e.g., business, leisure).
6. Enter your expected arrival time.
7. Indicate whether you will be traveling with 'Yes' or 'No' people.
8. Provide the name and contact information of the person you'd like to be the contact person.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter your email address.
11. If you have any special requests, enter them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **booking_date** (`booking_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your travel in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **travel_destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required: false): Type in the destination of your travel.
* **travel_duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your stay in numerical format (e.g., 3 for 3 days).
* **travel_type** (`travel_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the type of travel (e.g., business, leisure).
* **arrival_time** (`arrival_time`, time, required: false): Enter your expected arrival time.
* **travelers** (`travelers`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you are traveling with people, choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person you'd like to be the contact person.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests, enter them in this field.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your travel request is processed correctly.
- If you have any special requests, be as detailed as possible to help us better serve you.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the above explanations or contact us for assistance.
