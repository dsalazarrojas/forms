# Wedding Rehearsal Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a rehearsal slot for a wedding. Please fill it in with the necessary details so that our team can confirm your booking.


## How To Complete This Form
To fill in this form correctly, follow these steps:
- Fill in the couple's details, including any relevant personal information.
- Provide details about the rehearsal, including the date, time, and any other necessary information.
- Specify the venue where the rehearsal will take place.
- Select a date for the booking and choose whether you will be bringing attendees.
- Finally, add any additional message or comments if necessary.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Details** (`couple_details`, text, optional): Please fill in the name(s) of the couple getting married.
* **Rehearsal Details** (`rehearsal_details`, text, optional): Enter the specific details about the rehearsal, such as the type of rehearsal and any special requirements.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Specify the venue where the rehearsal will take place. This can be a church, hall, or any other location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the rehearsal. This can be a specific date or a date range.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the rehearsal. This can be a specific time or a time range.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the rehearsal. This can be a specific time or a time range.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to book the rehearsal. This will confirm your reservation.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Specify whether you will be bringing any attendees with you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add any additional message or comments about the booking if necessary.


## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate information to help us confirm your booking successfully.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please do not hesitate to include them in the message field.
- If you are unsure about anything, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification or further guidance.
