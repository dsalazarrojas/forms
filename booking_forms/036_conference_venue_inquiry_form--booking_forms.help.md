**Conference Venue Inquiry Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
The Conference Venue Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential details from potential clients who are interested in renting our venue for their events. This form will help us understand their needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our services to their specific requirements.

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Please fill out the form with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of event you are planning to host.
3. Provide the name of your event.
4. Specify your preferred dates for the event.
5. If you have alternative dates, please provide them.
6. Estimate the number of attendees you expect.
7. Indicate if you need breakout rooms or a main ballroom.
8. If you need an exhibition space, indicate the square footage required.
9. Specify your catering and AV requirements.
10. If you need hotel rooms, estimate the number of rooms and nights required.
11. Choose a budget range for your event.
12. If you have any special requirements, please specify them.
13. Select your preferred setup style for the rooms.
14. If you need parking spaces, indicate the number required.
15. If you need transportation services, specify your needs.
16. If you have used this venue before, indicate your experience.
17. Choose how you heard about our venue.
18. Specify when you plan to make your decision.
19. If you'd like to schedule a site visit, let us know.
20. Add any additional comments or information.
21. Choose your preferred follow-up method (email, phone call, or either).
22. Finally, confirm that the information you've provided is accurate.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Your Full Name**: Your name as the contact person for the event.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Event Type**: The type of event you are planning to host (e.g., Conference, Convention, Trade Show, etc.).
* **Event Name**: The name of your event.
* **Preferred Dates**: The dates you prefer for your event.
* **Alternate Dates**: Alternate dates in case the preferred dates are not available.
* **Expected Number of Attendees**: An estimate of the number of attendees you expect.
* **Number of Meeting Rooms**: The number of breakout rooms you need.
* **Main Ballroom Required**: Whether you need a large session space or not.
* **Exhibition Space**: Whether you need an exhibition space and if so, the required square footage.
* **Exhibition Square Footage**: If you need an exhibition space, the square footage required.
* **Catering Requirements**: Your catering needs (full service, light refreshments, external catering, or no catering needed).
* **AV Requirements**: Your audio-visual needs (full AV package, basic AV, minimal AV, external vendor, or no AV needed).
* **Hotel Rooms Needed**: The number of hotel rooms you need.
* **Number of Nights**: The number of nights you need the hotel rooms for.
* **Budget Range**: Your estimated budget for the event.
* **Special Requirements**: Any special requirements or needs you may have (e.g., accessibility needs).
* **Preferred Setup Styles**: The room arrangement you prefer (e.g., theater, classroom, boardroom, banquet, etc.).
* **Parking Requirements**: The number of parking spaces you need.
* **Transportation Needs**: Any transportation services you may need (e.g., shuttle, transfer, etc.).
* **Have You Used This Venue Before**: Whether you have used this venue before or not.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: How you heard about our venue (website, recommendation, previous event, advertisement, etc.).
* **Decision Timeline**: When you plan to make your decision.
* **Would You Like a Site Visit**: Whether you'd like to schedule a site visit.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
* **Preferred Follow-Up Method**: How you'd like to be contacted (email, phone call, or either).
* **Inquiry Confirmation**: Confirm that the information you've provided is accurate.
