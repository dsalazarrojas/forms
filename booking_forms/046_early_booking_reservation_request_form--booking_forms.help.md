# Early_Booking_Reservation_Request_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request early bookings. Please use this form to submit your requests for early bookings. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with all the required information, including your name, booking date and time, phone number, email, and note.
2. Select your availability for the requested booking.
3. Choose your status and source from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First_Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification. This field is not required.
* **Booking_Date**: Enter the date for which you are requesting early booking. This field is not required.
* **Booking_Time**: Enter the time for which you are requesting early booking. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. This field is not required.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or notes about your early booking request. This field is not required.
* **Availability**: Select whether you are available for early booking. This field is not required.
* **Status**: Select the status of your early booking request from the options provided (Pending, Approved, Declined). This field is not required.
* **Source**: Select the source of your early booking request from the options provided (Yes, No). This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required information is filled out accurately.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
