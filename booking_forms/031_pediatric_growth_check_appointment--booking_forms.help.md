# Pediatric Growth Check Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Growth Check Appointment form is used by healthcare providers to schedule appointments and track the growth and development of children. It is used to gather information about the child's first name, last name, age, date of visit, time of visit, appointment date, contact phone number, contact email, doctor's name, and doctor's phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps: 
  1. Enter the child's first name in the "First Name" field. 
  2. Enter the child's last name in the "Last Name" field. 
  3. Enter the child's age in the "Age of Child" field. 
  4. Enter the date of visit in the "Date of Visit" field. 
  5. Enter the time of visit in the "Time of Visit" field. 
  6. Enter the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field. 
  7. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field. 
  8. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field. 
  9. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field. 
  10. Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, text, required: false):** Enter the child's first name in this field. 
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required: false):** Enter the child's last name in this field. 
* **Date of Visit (Date of Visit, date, required: false):** Enter the date of the scheduled visit. 
* **Time of Visit (Time of Visit, time, required: false):** Enter the time of the scheduled visit. 
* **Age of Child (Age of Child, number, required: false):** Enter the child's age. 
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, date, required: false):** Enter the date of the appointment. 
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, required: false):** Enter the contact phone number. 
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required: false):** Enter the contact email. 
* **Doctor Name (Doctor Name, text, required: false):** Enter the name of the doctor. 
* **Doctor Phone (Doctor Phone, text, required: false):** Enter the doctor's phone number.
