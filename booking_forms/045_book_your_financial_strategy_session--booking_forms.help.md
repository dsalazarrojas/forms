# Book Your Financial Strategy Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help clients gather information about their financial situation and preferences, so we can provide personalized financial guidance and support. This information will be used to schedule a consultation with one of our financial advisors.

### <thinking>...</thinking>

Before proceeding, please be aware that:
- This form is not intended to be a comprehensive financial assessment.
- This form will be used for booking a consultation, which is a non-binding agreement.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Select your preferred options for each field.
3. Review and submit your application.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`): \* Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): \* Enter a valid email address where we will send confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): \* Enter your phone number for session coordination.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your birth date for demographic purposes.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`): \* Enter your current occupation.
* **Annual Income Range** (`annual_income`): Select one of the provided income ranges.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment status.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`): Select your marital status.
* **Number of Dependents** (`dependents`): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Type of Session** (`session_type`): Select the type of session you are interested in.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`): Select one or more of the provided financial goals.
* **Do You Have Current Investments** (`current_investments`): Select if you have investments.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`): Select how comfortable you are with risk.
* **When Do You Plan to Retire** (`retirement_timeline`): Select your retirement timeline.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_session_date`): Select a date for the session.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_session_time`): Select a time for the session.
* **Session Format** (`session_format`): Select how you would like to meet.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`): Select your time zone.
* **Do You Currently Have a Financial Advisor** (`current_financial_advisor`): Select if you have a current financial advisor.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`): Select how you heard about us.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any other information to share.
* **I Agree to Consultation Terms** (`terms_agreement`): Accept our terms of consultation.
* **I Agree to Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy_agreement`): Accept our privacy policy.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`): Select a date for the session.
