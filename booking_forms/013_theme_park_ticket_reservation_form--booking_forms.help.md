# Theme Park Ticket Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to input their reservation information for tickets to a theme park. It is intended for customers who have previously interacted with the theme park and have been given a reservation code. Please ensure you have a valid reservation code before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of ticket you wish to reserve (e.g., single ticket, group ticket, etc.).
2. Specify the number of tickets you wish to reserve (up to a maximum of 5).
3. Enter your desired date for the ticket reservation.
4. Select the time slot for your ticket reservation.
5. Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official document.
6. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for further communication.
8. Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
9. Click "Submit" to complete your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Type** (`Ticket Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of ticket you wish to reserve, such as a single ticket or a group ticket.
* **Number of Tickets** (`Number of Tickets`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 5 tickets.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date for your ticket reservation.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select the time slot for your ticket reservation.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official document.
* **E-mail** (`E-mail`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Payment Type** (`Payment Type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click "Submit" to complete your reservation.
