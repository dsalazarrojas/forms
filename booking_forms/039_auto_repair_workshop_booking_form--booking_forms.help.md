# Auto Repair Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to book an appointment at an auto repair workshop. To provide the best service, please complete the form with accurate and clear information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name and email address to ensure we can contact you for booking confirmation.
2. Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
3. Specify the make, model, and year of your vehicle to help our technicians prepare for your visit.
4. Select your preferred appointment date and time to ensure we have an available slot for you.
5. Choose the reason for your visit by selecting one of the service options (Minor Service, Major Service, Specific Repair, or State Inspection).
6. Let us know if you need transportation assistance or towing service during your visit.
7. If you have any special requests or concerns, please add them in the "Special Requirements or Notes" section.
8. Finally, select how you would like to be notified for booking confirmation (via email or SMS).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: This is the space where you can enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Please enter a valid email address where we can send you booking confirmation and any updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where we can contact you for any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Make and Model**: Specify the make and model of your vehicle to help our technicians understand your vehicle's needs.
* **Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle for accurate servicing.
* **Appointment Date**: Select your preferred date for the appointment from the calendar.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred time slot for the appointment.
* **Service Type**: Select the primary reason for your visit (Minor Service, Major Service, Specific Repair, or State Inspection).
* **Transportation Needs**: Choose how you would like us to assist you with transportation during your visit (False, Loaner Car, Shuttle Service, or Towing Required).
* **Special Requirements or Notes**: If you have any special requests or concerns, please add them here.
* **Confirmation Notice Preference**: Choose how you would like to be notified for booking confirmation (Email or SMS).

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient booking experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
