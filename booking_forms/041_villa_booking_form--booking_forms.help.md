# villa_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for booking a villa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the format of `your_name@domain.com` (no email addresses will be validated).
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX` (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Choose your **Start Date** and **End Date** using the calendar dropdowns.
5. Select your preferred **Booking Type** from the options provided (Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
6. Enter the **Number of Guests** (1-10).
7. Enter the **Number of Rooms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the guest(s) booking the villa.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication and booking confirmation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you wish to start your booking.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you wish to end your booking.
* **Booking Type** (`booking_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of booking you prefer (Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
* **Guests** (`guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests (1-10) you plan to book for.
* **Rooms** (`rooms`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of rooms you would like to book.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information in each field.
- Choose your booking type carefully as it may affect the booking rates and services offered.
- If you have any special requests or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
