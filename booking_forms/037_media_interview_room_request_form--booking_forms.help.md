# Media Interview Room Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Media Interview Room Request Form is designed to collect information for requesting access to a media interview room. This form helps in scheduling and coordinating media interviews efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the date you wish to schedule the interview for.
2.  Choose the time slot that suits you best from the available options.
3.  If you require more than one time slot, select multiple options.
4.  Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you for further arrangements.
5.  Add any additional notes or information about your request, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Date**: This field allows you to choose the date for your media interview. You can select from the given options for January 1, 2024. Please choose the date that suits you best.
*   **Select Time**: This is the first time slot you can choose for your interview. Select from 09:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 13:00 PM.
*   **Select Time 2**: If you need another time slot, this field allows you to select additional time options. You can choose multiple time slots by selecting from 14:00 PM, 15:00 PM, or 16:00 PM.
*   **Your Name**: Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you regarding your request.
*   **Your Email**: Provide your email address so we can communicate with you.
*   **Your Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
*   **Your Note**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information about your request.

## Tips

- Make sure to submit your form only once per request, as duplicate submissions will not be processed.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to aid in efficient scheduling and coordination.
- If you need to make any changes, contact the form administrators for assistance.
- Review this help guide for more information on form requirements and best practices.
