# Crowd Control Barrier Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowd Control Barrier Booking Form is a tool to collect essential information about events. It helps in planning and organizing events effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the text field provided on page 1 of the form. This field is not required to be filled, so you can skip it if not applicable.
2. Select the correct **Event Date** from the calendar provided. The field is not required to be filled.
3. Choose the event **Event Time** from the available options. Although the field is not required, it's essential for accurate planning and coordination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (text, optional)**: Enter a brief description or name of your event. This could be the title of the event or a short description that will help with identification and categorization.
* **Event Date (date, optional)**: Choose the date of your event from the calendar provided. This field is crucial for ensuring that the event occurs on the planned date without conflicts.
* **Event Time (time, optional)**: Select the time of your event from the available options. This helps in coordinating schedules and making sure the event starts and ends on time.

## Tips
* Be as precise as possible when selecting the event date and time. Inaccurate entries might lead to misunderstandings or conflicts with other events.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure that everything is correct.
* If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the support team for assistance.
