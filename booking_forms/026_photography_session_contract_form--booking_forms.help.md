# Photography Session Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage photography sessions with clients. It collects information such as date, time, and contact details to ensure a smooth and successful session. Please complete this form to book a photography session and understand the terms and conditions of your contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your photography session in the "Photography Session Date" field.
2. Provide the time of your session in the "Photography Session Time" field.
3. Enter your name as the photographer in the "Photographer's Name" field.
4. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your contact email in the "Contact E-mail" field.
6. If applicable, select "Yes" in the "Photography Session Type" field to indicate the type of session.
7. Enter any additional details about your session in the "Session Details" field.
8. Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" field, which outlines the contract details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Session Contract** (1, text, required: false): This field is for a brief description of the contract or agreement between you and the photographer.
* **Photography Session Date** (2, date, required: false): Enter the date of your photography session.
* **Photography Session Time** (3, time, required: false): Enter the time of your session.
* **Photographer's Name** (4, text, required: false): Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Contact Number** (5, text, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact E-mail** (6, email, required: false): Enter your contact email.
* **Contract Start Date** (7, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (8, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Duration** (9, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the contract.
* **Photography Session Type** (10, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate the type of session.
* **Session Details** (11, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about your session.
* **Terms and Conditions** (12, note, required: false): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of your contract.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to avoid any issues with your session.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your photographer for clarification.
