# Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form is a tool used to collect information from customers to book a specific type of venue. This form is designed to help us better understand the needs and requirements of our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on the first page.
2. Select your payment method on the second page.
3. Provide your booking details, including date, start and end times, and number of guests.
4. Add any additional notes if needed.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Step 1 - Booking Information**:
  * **Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form** (`title`, text, required): This is the title of the form. Please leave it as is.
  * **Payment Method** (`form_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you intend to pay for your booking.
  * **Booking Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to book the Mountain Yoga Chalet.
  * **Start Time** (`form_start_time`, time, required): Choose the start time for your booking.
  * **End Time** (`form_end_time`, time, required): Choose the end time for your booking.
  * **Number of Guests** (`form_number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending the booking.
* **Payment Method and Booking Details**:
  * **Payment Method** (`form_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you intend to pay for your booking.
* **Additional Information**:
  * **Notes** (`form_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or requests.
  * **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions**:
  * **Agreed Terms** (`form_agreed_terms`, select_multiple, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions. Choose one of the three options: "I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions", "I have not read the terms and conditions", or "I have not read the terms and prophets" (note: please be aware that "terms and prophets" is likely a typo and should not be considered a valid option).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please add them in the "Notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
