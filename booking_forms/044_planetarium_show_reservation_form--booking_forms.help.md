# Planetarium Show Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reservation information from customers or visitors to the planetarium for a specific show. Please fill out the form below to reserve a spot at the desired show date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired **Show Date** using the date picker provided.
2. Choose the desired **Show Time** using the time picker provided. 
3. Enter the **Show Title** with the title of the show you want to see.
4. Provide a brief description of the **Show Description**.
5. Choose an **Event ID** from the options provided (if applicable).
6. Enter the customer's **Customer Name** and **Customer Email** for contact purposes.
7. Enter the customer's **Customer Phone** for contact purposes.
8. Enter the **Number of Guests** you are reserving for.
9. Select the **Reservation Time** for the reserved show.
10. Add any **Notes** you have regarding the reservation.
11. Choose a **Submitted By** option from the options provided (if applicable).
12. Select the date you are **Submitted On**.
13. Enter any **Form Data** you have regarding the reservation.
14. Select any applicable **Planetarium Reservation** options.
15. Enter the customer's **Customer Address**, **Customer City**, **Customer State**, and **Customer Zip** for address purposes.
16. Enter the **Show Time 2** (if applicable).
17. Enter the **Planetarium Name**, **Planetarium Location**, and **Planetarium Phone** and **Planetarium Email** (if applicable).

* **Show Date**: Select the date of the show you want to see.
* **Show Time**: Select the time of the show you want to see.
* **Show Title**: Enter the title of the show you are reserving.
* **Show Description**: Describe the show you are reserving.
* **Event ID**: Choose an option for the event ID, if applicable.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person reserving the spot.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email of the person reserving the spot for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person reserving the spot.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you are reserving for.
* **Reservation Time**: Select the time of the reservation.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes regarding the reservation.
* **Submitted By**: Choose an option from the list, if applicable.
* **Submitted On**: Select the date you are submitting this form.
* **Form Data**: Enter any additional data regarding the reservation.
* **Planetarium Reservation**: Select any applicable options for the planetarium reservation.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the person reserving the spot.
* **Customer City**: Enter the city of the person reserving the spot.
* **Customer State**: Enter the state of the person reserving the spot.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter the zip code of the person reserving the spot.
* **Show Time 2**: Select the time of the show 2 (if applicable).
* **Planetarium Name**: Enter the name of the planetarium.
* **Planetarium Location**: Enter the location of the planetarium.
* **Planetarium Phone**: Enter the phone number of the planetarium.
