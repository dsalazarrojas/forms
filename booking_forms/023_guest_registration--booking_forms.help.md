This guide is intended to help users understand and complete the Guest Registration form. The form is designed for registering guests at a facility, collecting relevant information, and making reservations.

**Form Purpose:**
This form collects information from guests for registration and booking purposes.

**How To Complete This Form:**
1. Fill out each field with the required and optional information as requested.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

*   **guest_registration_form**: The first field is the form title, which is not a field to fill out but rather a heading that separates the form from other sections.
*   **guest_name**: Enter the guest's name.
*   **guest_email**: Enter the guest's email address.
*   **checkin_date**: Enter the date the guest plans to check in.
*   **checkin_time**: Enter the time the guest plans to check in.
*   **guest_phone**: Enter the guest's phone number.
*   **guest_note**: Enter any additional comments or requests from the guest.
*   **guest_checkin_date**: Enter the date the guest's stay actually begins.
*   **guest_checkin_time**: Enter the time the guest's stay actually begins.
*   **guest_checkout_date**: Enter the date the guest plans to check out.
*   **guest_checkout_time**: Enter the time the guest plans to check out.
*   **guest_room**: Select the guest room the guest has booked or is planning to book.
*   **guest_stay_length**: Enter the number of days the guest plans to stay.
*   **guest_stay_type**: Select the type of stay the guest is booking.
*   **guest_rate**: Select the rate the guest has chosen for their stay.

**Tips:**
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to complete the form.
- Double-check dates, times, and other sensitive information for accuracy.
- Review the guest's stay preferences and adjust the booking as needed.
