# Travel Agent Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agent Recommendation Form is designed to collect information from travelers about their preferences and requirements. This form helps our travel agency recommend the most suitable travel agent for each traveler's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the destination field with the specific location you are traveling to.
2. Select your preferred travel style (e.g., Family Friendly, Adventure, or Business Travel).
3. Choose any activities you are interested in (e.g., Hiking, Foodie, or Beach).
4. Enter your desired travel dates.
5. Enter your travel duration (if applicable).
6. Provide your email address for communication.
7. Enter your phone number for contact.
8. Add any additional notes about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Destination**: Enter the specific location you are traveling to (e.g., Paris, New York, or Tokyo).
* **Travel Style**: Select one of the following options: Family Friendly, Adventure, or Business Travel.
* **Activity**: Select all applicable activities that interest you (e.g., Hiking, Foodie, or Beach).
* **Travel Date**: Enter the start and end dates of your trip in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-06-15).
* **Travel Time**: Enter the duration of your trip in hours or days (e.g., 24 hours or 7 days).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication with our travel agency.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or notes about your trip.
* **Travel Agent**: Enter the name of the travel agent you are working with.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any specific tools or resources you require during your trip.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when completing this form to ensure accurate recommendations.
- Use a clear and concise format to make it easier for our travel agents to understand your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to our travel agency for assistance.
