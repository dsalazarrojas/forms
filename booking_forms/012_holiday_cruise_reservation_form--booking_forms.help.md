This form is designed to collect information for holiday cruise reservations. The purpose of the form is to gather essential details from customers or agents to facilitate a smooth and secure booking process. This includes destination, departure date and time, travel agent, contact information, number of guests, travel documents, payment method, payment amount, special requests, and confirmation of reservations and contact information.

Before proceeding, please ensure you have all the necessary information and that the form is not being used for any other purpose.

Please note that this form is used for internal purposes only. The information collected will be used to process the booking and communicate with the customer.

The fields in this form are as follows:

*   **Destination** (`Destination`, text, optional): Enter the destination for the holiday cruise.
*   **Departure date** (`Departure date`, date, optional): Select the date of departure for the holiday cruise.
*   **Departure time** (`Departure time`, time, optional): Select the time of departure for the holiday cruise.
*   **Time zone** (`Time zone`, select one, optional): Choose the time zone for the departure time. Options: EST, EDT, CDT, PDT.
*   **Travel Agent** (`Travel Agent`, text, optional): Enter the travel agent's name or ID.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests traveling.
*   **Travel Documents** (`Travel Documents`, select multiple, optional): Select any necessary travel documents (e.g., passport, ID, driver license, health insurance).
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select one, optional): Choose the payment method for the booking (credit card, bank transfer, wire transfer).
*   **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
*   **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements for the booking.
*   **Confirm Reservations** (`Confirm Reservations`, select one, optional): Confirm that the reservations are correct (True/False).
*   **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, select one, optional): Confirm that the email is correct (True/False).
*   **Confirm Phone** (`Confirm Phone`, select one, optional): Confirm that the phone number is correct (True/False).

Please ensure to fill out all relevant fields accurately to facilitate smooth processing of your booking.
