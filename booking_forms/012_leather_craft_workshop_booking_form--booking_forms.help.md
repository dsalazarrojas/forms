# Leather Craft Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help participants book and organize workshops in leather crafting, cutting, or finishing. It will help the workshop organizer with planning, communication, and logistics. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Participant Info section with your contact details.
2. Select the type of workshop you are interested in (Leather Crafting Workshop, Leather Cutting, or Leather Finishing).
3. Choose a start and end date for your workshop.
4. Add any participant notes if needed.
5. Provide the workshop organizer's name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info (participant_info, text, optional)**: Enter your name and contact information to let us know who you are.
* **Participant Name (participant_name, text, optional)**: Your name as you want to be addressed.
* **Participant Email (participant_email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Type (workshop_type, select_one, optional)**: Choose the type of workshop you are interested in.
	+ Leather Crafting Workshop
	+ Leather Cutting
	+ Leather Finishing
* **Start Date (start_date, date, optional)**: Choose the start date for your workshop.
* **End Date (end_date, date, optional)**: Choose the end date for your workshop.
* **Participant Notes (participant_notes, note, optional)**: Add any notes about your attendance.
* **Organizer Info (organizer_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the workshop organizer.
* **Organizer Email (organizer_email, email, optional)**: Enter the email address of the workshop organizer.
* **Organizer Phone (organizer_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the workshop organizer.
