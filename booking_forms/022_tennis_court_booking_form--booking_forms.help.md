# Tennis Court Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Court Booking Form is designed to allow users to book a tennis court for their activities. This form requires users to select the desired court, date, time, and provide any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired court from the "Court Selection" field.
2. Enter the date for the booking in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time for the booking in the "Time" field.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "User Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Court Selection** (`Court 1`, `Court 2`, `Court 3`, required): Select the desired tennis court for your booking.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date for the booking in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time for the booking in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **User Notes** (`note`, required): Provide any additional comments or notes for your booking.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to receive booking confirmations or updates).
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to receive booking confirmations or updates).
* **User Input 1-13** (`text`, optional): These fields are for additional user input, which is not explicitly defined in the provided YAML. Users can use these fields to enter any additional information as required.
