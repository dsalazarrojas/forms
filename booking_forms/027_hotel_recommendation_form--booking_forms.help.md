# Hotel Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users find their ideal hotel based on their specific needs and preferences for travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a travel date for your stay.
2. Choose the type of travel (Business, Leisure, or Adventure).
3. Enter your preferred destination or location.
4. Select your preferred accommodation type (Hotel, Hostel, or Guesthouse).
5. Choose your preferred room type (Single, Double, Triple, or Suite).
6. Select your preferred room view (City, Mountain, Sea, or Lake).
7. Enter your expected arrival time.
8. Enter your budget for the stay.
9. Select your preferred languages (English, Spanish, French, or Chinese), and you can choose multiple languages if needed.

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can provide accurate recommendations.
