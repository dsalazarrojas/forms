# Flight Search Filters - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight Search Filters form is a tool designed to help users narrow down flight search results based on various criteria. This form is used to gather specific user information that will allow for personalized flight searching.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your desired route from the options listed.
2. Choose your departure date.
3. If applicable, select your return date.
4. Specify your preferred cabin class.
5. Select your budget.
6. Enter your departure city.
7. Select the number of travelers.
8. Select a travel date range.
9. Choose your preferred travel time.
10. Enter your preferred flight number.
11. Select your travel agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route**: Choose the destination for your flight.
	+ Select 'Yes' to search flights to this destination.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you plan to depart.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Cabin Class**: Choose the cabin class for your flight.
	+ Select 'Yes' to search for flights in this cabin class.
* **Budget**: Choose the budget for your flight.
	+ Select 'Yes' to search for flights within this budget.
* **Departure City**: Enter the city of departure.
	+ Enter the airport code (e.g., LAX for Los Angeles International Airport)
* **Travelers**: Select the number of travelers.
	+ Select 'Yes' for one traveler, 'No' for two or more.
* **Travel Date Range**: Choose a travel date range.
	+ Format: From MM/DD/YYYY To MM/DD/YYYY
* **Travel Time**: Choose your preferred travel time.
	+ Select '08:00' for 8am, '10:00' for 10am, etc.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your preferred flight number.
	+ Enter the exact flight number (e.g., AA123)
* **Travel Agency**: Choose your travel agency.
	+ Select 'Yes' to search flights with this agency.

## Tips

* Make sure to select specific and relevant options to ensure accurate search results.
* If you are unsure of any field, you can always ask for assistance or clarification.
* This form is meant to be used for flight searching purposes only; any other use may be considered misuse.
