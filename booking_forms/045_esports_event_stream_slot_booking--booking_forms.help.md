# esports_event_stream_slot_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for booking a time slot for an esports event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the platform on which the team will be streaming. You can choose from the following options:
	* Twitch
	* Facebook
	* YouTube
	* Google
2. Fill out the team information section, including:
	* Team Name: Enter the name of your team.
	* Team Email: Enter the email address of your team.
	* Team Phone: Enter the phone number of your team.
	* Team Leader: Enter the name of the team leader.
3. Fill out the event information section, including:
	* Event Name: Enter the name of the event.
	* Stream Date: Choose a date for the stream.
	* Stream Time: Choose a time for the stream.
	* Event Description: Describe the event.
	* Event Location: Enter the location of the event.
	* Event Capacity: Enter the capacity of the event.
	* Event Sponsor: Enter the sponsor of the event.
