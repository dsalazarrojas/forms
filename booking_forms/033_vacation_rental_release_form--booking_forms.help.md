# Vacation Rental Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Release Form is used to document the details of a vacation rental property release, including guest information, agreement to terms and conditions, additional information, payment method, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Guest Information" page.
2. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
3. Check the "Agreement" field to confirm you have read and understood the rental terms and conditions.
4. If the agreement is not already checked, select "True".
5. In the "Rental Terms" field, enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your rental.
6. On the "Additional Information" page, enter any extra details you'd like to share about your stay.
7. On the "Payment Method" page, select the payment method you've chosen for your rental (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
8. On the "Special Requests" page, enter any special requests you have for your stay (e.g. early check-in, extra towels).
9. In the "Rental Duration" field, select the number of days you plan to stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information (text)**: Enter your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Agreement (select_one)**: Confirm you have read and understood the rental terms and conditions. 
* **Rental Terms (note)**: Provide any additional information about your rental.
* **Additional Information (text)**: Enter any extra details you'd like to share about your stay.
* **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: Select the payment method you've chosen for your rental (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Special Requests (text)**: Enter any special requests you have for your stay (e.g. early check-in, extra towels).
* **Rental Duration (number)**: Select the number of days you plan to stay.
