# Spoken Word Event Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spoken Word Event Reservation Form is designed for customers or attendees to reserve a spot at a spoken word event. To complete this form, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all the required fields (if applicable).
- Enter any additional information that's relevant to your reservation in the optional fields.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure all necessary details are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Attendee Registration
* **Attendee Registration (text)**: Please type your name or the name of the person reserving the spot on your behalf.
### 2. Event Details
* **Event Details (text)**: Briefly describe the type of spoken word event you're interested in or have already registered for.
### 3. Contact Details
* **Contact Details (text)**: Enter any additional contact information, such as a phone number or email, for the event organizers.
### 4. Event Date (date)
* **Event Date (date)**: Select the date you plan to attend or would like to attend.
### 5. Event Time (time)
* **Event Time (time)**: Choose the time slot you'd like to attend or are currently attending.
### 6. Event Venue (text)
* **Event Venue (text)**: Enter the venue where the spoken word event will take place.
### 7. Spoken Word (text)
* **Spoken Word (text)**: Specify the type of performance or event theme you're interested in, such as comedy, poetry, or storytelling.
### 8. Speaker (text)
* **Speaker (text)**: Enter the name of the speaker or artist performing at the event.
### 9. Email (email)
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for further communication or updates from the event organizers.
### 10. Phone Number (text)
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number in case the event organizers need to contact you directly.
