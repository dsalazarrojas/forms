# Photo Model Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a photo model release from a model, granting permission for a photographer to use their likeness in their work. It captures details about the model, the photo shoot, and the client or organization commissioning the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Make sure to sign and date the form, indicating that you understand and agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Model Name**: The name of the model granting permission for their likeness to be used.
- **Model Date of Birth**: The model's date of birth, if you are under the age of 18, please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Model Contact Details**: Enter the model's contact information, such as phone number or address.
- **Photo Shoot Details**: Describe the photo shoot, including the subject matter, number of photos taken, and any specific details about the shoot.
- **Photo Shoot Location**: The location where the photo shoot took place.
- **Photo Shoot Date**: The date of the photo shoot.
- **Photo Shoot Time**: The time of the photo shoot.
- **Photo Shoot Duration**: The length of the photo shoot.
- **Client Name**: The name of the client or organization commissioning the photo shoot.
- **Client Contact Details**: Enter the client's contact information, such as phone number or address.
- **Client Email**: The client's email address.
- **Client Name**: The name of the client or organization commissioning the photo shoot.
