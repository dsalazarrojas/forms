# resort_package_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to inquire about resort packages. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify your inquiry details, such as name, checkin and checkout dates, room type, number of guests, and any additional message or contact email. This form is used to gather information from users for resort package inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" at the bottom of the form to send your inquiry details to our team.
2. Fill out each field with the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Details** (Text, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or details about your inquiry. Please use this space to provide any relevant information to help us better understand your needs.
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Checkin Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to check in to the resort.
* **Checkout Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to check out of the resort.
* **Room Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of room you are interested in booking:
	+ 'Yes' for a specific type of room
* **Room Types** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple room types you are interested in booking:
	+ 'Yes' for a specific room type
* **Number of Guests** (Number, Optional): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Message** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information.
