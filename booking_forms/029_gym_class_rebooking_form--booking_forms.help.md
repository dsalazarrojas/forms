# Gym Class Rebooking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist students in rebooking their gym classes. It collects information about their rebooking preferences and other relevant details to facilitate a smooth rebooking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select your next term from the drop-down menu under "next_term".
2. Choose the class you want to rebook by selecting the relevant option under "rebooking_class".
3. If you'd like to provide a rebooking name, enter your rebooking name in the "rebooking_name" field.
4. Please enter your email address in the "rebooking_email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "rebooking_phone" field if you'd like to be contacted about your rebooking.
6. Enter your student ID in the "student_id" field.
7. If you have any additional class details, please enter them in the "class_details" field.
8. You can provide the name of your current instructor in the "instructor" field.
9. You can add a rebooking message in the "rebooking_message" field if you'd like to share any specific requirements or requests.
10. If you'd like to specify the terms of your rebooking, please enter them in the "rebooking_terms" field.
11. Finally, select your rebooking status from the available options under "rebooking_status".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **next_term**: Select the term you'd like to rebook into. Please choose one of the available options to proceed.
* **rebooking_class**: Choose the class you'd like to rebook into. Select one of the available options for a smooth rebooking process.
* **rebooking_name**: Enter the name you'd like to be rebooked under. This field is optional, but helps us understand your rebooking preferences.
* **rebooking_email**: Provide your email address to help us contact you about your rebooking.
* **rebooking_phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your rebooking. This field is optional.
* **student_id**: Enter your student ID to help us identify you in our records.
* **class_details**: If you have any specific class details, please enter them here. This field is optional.
* **instructor**: Provide the name of your current instructor if you'd like to request to rebook with them.
* **rebooking_message**: Add any additional rebooking message or requirements you'd like to share with us.
* **rebooking_terms**: Enter the terms of your rebooking if you'd like to specify any particular conditions.
* **rebooking_status**: Select your rebooking status from the available options to help us understand your rebooking preferences.
* **rebooking_terms**: Enter the terms of your rebooking to help us understand your specific needs.
* **rebooking_status**: This field is for you to indicate the status of your rebooking. Select one of the available options to communicate your preference.
 
Note: This guide provides general information and does not aim to cover all edge cases or special scenarios. If you have specific questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
