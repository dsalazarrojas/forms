# festival_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival_accommodation_request_form is used to request festival accommodations for attendees. This form collects personal details and accommodation preferences to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked with \*).
3. Double-check the festival name to ensure it matches the festival you are requesting accommodations for.
4. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (\*: First Name, text, ): This is the first name of the person requesting the accommodation.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, ): This is the last name of the person requesting the accommodation.
* **Address** (Address, text, ): This is the address of the person requesting the accommodation.
* **Email** (Email, email, ): This is the email address of the person requesting the accommodation.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, ): This is the contact phone number of the person requesting the accommodation.
* **Festival Name** (Festival Name, text, ): This is the name of the festival you are requesting accommodations for.
* **Accommodation Type* ** (Accommodation Type, select_one, ): This is the type of accommodation you are requesting (Hotel, Hostel, Home, or Other).
* **Accommodation Number* ** (Accommodation Number, number, ): This is the number of people you are requesting accommodations for.
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, ): This is the date and time of your arrival.
* **Duration of Stay** (Duration of Stay, number, ): This is the number of days you plan to stay.
