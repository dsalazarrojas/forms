# Talent Booking Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and event information from users to facilitate smooth talent booking communication between talent providers and event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your contact information, event details, and any other relevant information.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Mobile, Email, or Phone).
3. Enter your contact name and any event-specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to contact you for further communication.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details that will help the talent provider understand your needs.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Talent Information** (`talent_info`, text, optional): Enter any specific information about the talent you are booking, such as their name, title, or genre.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Provide any specific availability constraints or restrictions for the talent, such as time slots or specific dates.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Mobile, Email, or Phone).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or title to be used for communication with the talent provider.
* **Event Requirements** (`event_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific requirements for the event, such as equipment needs or special instructions.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when providing contact and event information to ensure smooth communication and successful talent booking.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
