# Dental Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Appointment Reservation Form is a tool for scheduling appointments with a dentist. Please use this form to provide essential information about your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Patient Info" section with your details.
2. Provide information about the appointment in the "Appointment Details" section.
3. Enter the necessary details about the dentist in the "Dentist Info" section.
4. Choose a suitable schedule in the "Schedule" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required: false): Describe the type of appointment you are booking (e.g., routine check-up, emergency appointment, etc.).
* **Dentist Info** (`dentist_info`, text, required: false): Enter the dentist's name, specialty, or other relevant information about the dentist.
* **Schedule** (`appointment_schedule`, text, required: false): Choose the date and time for your appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all input data for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, include them in the "Appointment Details" section.
* If you need to schedule multiple appointments, fill out this form multiple times with different information.
* Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure you've included all necessary information.
