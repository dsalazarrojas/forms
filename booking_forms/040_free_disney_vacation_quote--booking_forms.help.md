# free_disney_vacation_quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_disney_vacation_quote form is designed to collect information about a customer's preferences and needs for a Disney vacation package. This information will be used by travel agents or customer service representatives to assist with booking and planning the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. You can leave optional fields blank if you do not have the information or if they are not relevant to your trip.

* **Destination** (Destination, text, required: false): Enter the destination of your trip, such as "Orlando" or "Anaheim".
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, date, required: false): Select the dates of your trip.
* **Number Of Guests** (Number Of Guests, number, required: false): Enter the number of people in your party.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Travel Style** (Preferred Travel Style, select_one, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Disney Cruise
	+ Disney Land
	+ Disney World
* **Number Of Children** (Number Of Children, number, required: false): Enter the number of children in your party.
* **Number Of Adults** (Number Of Adults, number, required: false): Enter the number of adults in your party.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, required: false): If you have any special requests or needs, please enter them here.

## Tips
