# music_label_showcase_slot_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_label_showcase_slot_booking_form is a form designed to gather information from artists participating in a music label showcase event. The form is used by event organizers to understand the artist's preferences and technical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select whether or not you will be participating in the event.
2. Indicate your preferred time slots for the event.
3. List any technical requirements you may need to perform your set.
4. Provide any additional information you would like to share with the event organizers.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **artists (Yes/No)**: Indicate whether you are planning to participate in the event by selecting "Yes".
* **preferred_time_slots**: Select the time slots you prefer for the event from the available options.
* **technical_requirements**: List any technical requirements you have, such as specific equipment needed or setup requests.
* **additional_info**: If you have any other information you'd like to share with the event organizers, enter it here.
* **email**: Enter your email address so that event organizers can contact you with further instructions.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so that event organizers can reach you in case of any last-minute questions or changes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are eligible to participate in the event before submitting this form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with event organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact event organizers directly.
