# License Renewal Appointment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The License Renewal Appointment Booking Form is a tool used to collect contact and appointment details from individuals seeking to renew their licenses. This form helps facilitate the license renewal process by gathering essential information that will be used by the relevant authorities to process your license renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide your appointment details, including the date and time you wish to schedule your appointment.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you receive any necessary updates or notifications regarding your license renewal.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`): This field is used to specify the date and time you wish to schedule your appointment. Please enter the date and time in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM) to ensure a smooth scheduling process.
* **License Renewal Form** (`license_renewal_form`): This field is used to provide additional information related to your license renewal. Please enter any relevant details as requested, as this will help facilitate the license renewal process.
