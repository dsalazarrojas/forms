# spa_appointment_portal_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa_appointment_portal_access_form is designed to collect information from customers to gain access to the appointment portal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check your entries before submission.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (Customer Info)**:
	+ Label: Customer Info
	+ Type: Text
	+ Description: Enter your name and any other relevant customer information.
* **Appointment Details (Appointment Details)**:
	+ Label: Appointment Details
	+ Type: Text
	+ Description: Provide details about your appointment, including date, time, and any specific requests.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**:
	+ Label: Contact Info
	+ Type: Text
	+ Description: Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure smooth processing of your request.
