# Client Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Meeting Appointment Form is a crucial tool for scheduling and preparing meetings with clients. It ensures that all necessary information is collected and communicated effectively to both parties involved in the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name**, which is the title of the person you are meeting with.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** so we can contact you easily.
3. Select the **Meeting Purpose** from the options provided, which will help us understand the focus of the meeting.
4. Choose a **Preferred Meeting Date** from the available dates.
5. Select a **Preferred Time Slot** that suits you best.
6. Choose a **Meeting Format** that you prefer (In-Person, Video Call, or Phone Call).
7. Enter the **Meeting Location** if you would like to meet in-person, or leave it blank if you prefer a video call.
8. Specify the **Estimated Duration** of the meeting from the options provided.
9. Add any **Topics to Cover** in the meeting so we can prepare accordingly.
10. Finally, confirm that you understand our **Cancellation Policy**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Fill in the name of the person you are meeting with.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, optional): This is the name of the company you represent.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address so we can easily contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Add your phone number for easy contact.
* **-- Appointment Details --** (`section_appointment`, optional): This is a note section for you to add any additional details about the meeting.
* **What is the primary purpose of this meeting?** (`meeting_purpose`, required): Select the main reason for the meeting from the options provided.
* **Preferred Meeting Date** (`preferred_date`, required): Choose a date that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time`, required): Select a time that you are available.
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, required): Choose how you would like to meet (In-Person, Video Call, or Phone Call).
* **Meeting Location or Video Link** (`meeting_location`, optional): Enter the location if you would like to meet in-person, or leave it blank if you prefer a video call.
* **Estimated Duration** (`estimated_duration`, optional): Select the estimated length of the meeting.
* **Specific topics or questions to be covered** (`topics_to_cover`, required): Add any topics or questions you would like to cover in the meeting.
* **I understand that meeting cancellations require at least 24-hour notice** (`cancellation_policy_ack`, required): Confirm your understanding of our cancellation policy.
