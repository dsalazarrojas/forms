# Short Term Rental Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking short-term rentals, such as apartments or houses. It gathers information about the guest's contact details, accommodation preferences, and other relevant information to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest name.
2. Provide your contact details (email and phone number).
3. Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in (House or Apartment).
4. Select the check-in and check-out dates for your stay.
5. Specify your arrival time.
6. Enter the number of guests in your group.
7. Add any special requests you may have.
8. Enter the accommodation address in the following format: House/Street, Postcode, City, State, Country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Guest Contact Details** (`guest_contact_details`, note, optional): Enter your email and phone number.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "House" or "Apartment".
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to check-in.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to check-out.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter your arrival time in 12-hour format (e.g., 12:00 AM).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests in your group.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Add any special requests or requirements you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Accommodation Address 1** (`accommodation_address`, text, optional): Enter the accommodation address (House/Street, Postcode, City, State, Country).
* **Accommodation Address 2-5** (`accommodation_address_2`, `accommodation_address_3`, `accommodation_address_4`, `accommodation_address_5`, text, optional): Enter additional address lines for the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Postcode** (`accommodation_postcode`, text, optional): Enter the postcode of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Country** (`accommodation_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation City** (`accommodation_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation State** (`accommodation_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Postcode 2** (`accommodation_postcode_2`, text, optional): Enter the additional postcode of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Country 2** (`accommodation_country_2`, text, optional): Enter the additional country of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation City 2** (`accommodation_city_2`, text, optional): Enter the additional city of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation State 2** (`accommodation_state_2`, text, optional): Enter the additional state of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation ZIP** (`accommodation_zip`, text, optional): Enter the ZIP code of the accommodation.
* **Accommodation Street** (`accommodation_street`, text, optional): Enter the street name of the accommodation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure smooth communication and booking process.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
