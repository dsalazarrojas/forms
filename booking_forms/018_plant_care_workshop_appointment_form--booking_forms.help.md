# Plant Care Workshop Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Plant Care Workshop Appointment Form" is designed to collect information from users to plan and schedule workshops on plant care. This form is used to gather details about the workshop's specifics, such as date, time, location, and participants, as well as confirmations of participation and submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the workshop details, including date, time, location, and any other relevant information.
2. Indicate whether participants will be 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. Provide details about the instructor, such as their name and contact information.
4. Select the location where the workshop will take place from the options provided.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
6. Specify the plant species and quantity that will be covered in the workshop.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the workshop.
8. Identify the contact person responsible for the workshop.
9. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person.
10. Provide details about the workshop's address.
11. Add any workshop notes or comments.
12. Specify the workshop terms, such as whether it's indoors or outdoors.
13. Confirm the workshop status, whether it's scheduled, completed, or cancelled.
14. Specify the submission status, whether it's completed, pending, or in progress.
15. Enter the submission date and time.
16. Confirm the submission.
17. Set the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide a brief description of the workshop. Please enter the relevant details about the workshop's name, date, time, location, and any other specifics.
- **Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the workshop is scheduled to take place.
- **Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the workshop is scheduled to take place.
- **Participants** (`participants`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether participants will be attending the workshop by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Instructor Details** (`instructor_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide details about the instructor, such as their name and contact information.
- **Location** (`workshop_location`, select_one, required): Select the location where the workshop will take place from the options provided.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Confirm Participation** (`confirm_participation`, select_one, required): Confirm your participation in the workshop by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Confirm Terms and Conditions** (`confirm_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, required): This field is for you to provide any additional details about the submission.
- **Plant Species** (`plant_species`, select_multiple, optional): Select the plant species that will be covered in the workshop.
- **Plant Quantity** (`plant_quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of plants that will be covered in the workshop.
- **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the workshop.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required): Identify the contact person responsible for the workshop.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the workshop location.
- **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the workshop.
- **Workshop Terms** (`workshop_terms`, select_one, optional): Specify whether the workshop is indoors or outdoors.
- **Workshop Status** (`workshop_status`, select_one, optional): Confirm the workshop status, whether it's scheduled, completed, or cancelled.
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Specify the submission status, whether it's completed, pending, or in progress.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date and time.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the submission time.
- **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, select_one, required): Confirm that the submission is complete by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Confirm Submission Date** (`confirm_submission_date`, date, optional): Set the submission date.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is filled out for a valid purpose and the user is aware of the workshop specifics and terms and conditions.
