This form is not intended for internal administrative purposes but rather for end users to provide their personal details and preferences for tennis court booking.

## Tennis Court Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Court Reservation Form is designed to collect information from end users for the purpose of booking a tennis court. This form helps us understand the user's name, contact information, and booking preferences to manage the reservation process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use a valid email format.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose a date in the "date" field. The format should be in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Choose a time in the "time" field. The format should be in the 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00 or 14:00.
7. Select the preferred tennis court from the "reservation_time" dropdown list.
8. Select the desired duration for your booking from the "duration" dropdown list.
9. Optionally, you can add any additional comments or notes in the "tennis_court_reservation_form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: This is the field for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
* **last_name**: This is the field for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
* **email**: This is the field for your email address. Please use a valid email format, e.g., example@domain.com.
* **phone**: This is the field for your phone number. Please use the international format, e.g., +1-123-456-7890.
* **date**: This is the field for choosing a date for your reservation. Please use the standard YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 2022-01-01.
* **time**: This is the field for choosing a time for your reservation. Please use the 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00 or 14:00.
* **reservation_time**: This is the field to select the preferred tennis court for your reservation.
* **reservation_date**: This is the field for choosing a date for your reservation. Please use the standard YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 2022-01-01.
* **duration**: This is the field for selecting the duration of your booking. Please choose from the available options, e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, or 3 hours.
* **tennis_court_reservation_form**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about your reservation.
