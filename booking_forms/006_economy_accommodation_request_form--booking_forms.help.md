# Economy Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request economy accommodation for a trip or travel. It is essential to fill it out correctly to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your check-in and check-out dates in the correct format (date/month/year).
2. Provide your contact information (phone, email, and company email) in a valid format.
3. Choose your preferred accommodation type (hotel, hostel, guesthouse).
4. Select your budget for the accommodation.
5. Enter any additional information you may need to provide in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-in Date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to check-in for your trip.
* **Check-out Date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to check-out for your trip.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you may need to make your stay comfortable.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred accommodation type (hotel, hostel, guesthouse).
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the accommodation.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person contacting us.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person contacting us.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person contacting us.
* **Company Name (if Applicable)** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter the company name.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter the company phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter the company email address.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information you may need to make your stay comfortable.
* **Required Field** (`required_field`, select_multiple, optional): This field is not actually required, but it's labeled as such for demonstration purposes.
* **Optional Field** (`optional_field`, text, optional): This is a regular text field that is not required.
* **Non Select Field** (`non_select_field`, text, optional): This field is not a select field.
* **Single Select Field** (`single_select_field`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the provided options.
* **Multiple Select Field** (`multiple_select_field`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the provided options.
* **Non Select Integer** (`non_select_integer`, number, optional): This field is not a select field.
* **Select Integer** (`select_integer`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple Integer** (`select_multiple_integer`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the provided options.
* **Non Decimal** (`non_decimal`, text, optional): This field is not a decimal field.
* **Select Decimal** (`select_decimal`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the provided options.
* **Select Multiple Decimal** (`select_multiple_decimal`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the provided options.
* **Date Picker** (`datepicker`, date, optional): Choose a date from the provided date picker.
