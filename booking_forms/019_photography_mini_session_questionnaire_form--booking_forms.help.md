# photography_mini_session_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from users prior to a photo session with a photographer. It helps the photographer understand the session preferences, contact details, and any specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Share your phone number.
5. Enter your birthdate.
6. Select your preferred contact method (e.g., Home, Work, Mobile, Email).
7. Choose your preferred contact time.
8. Add any special instructions or requests.
9. Specify your photography preferences (e.g., Indoor, Outdoor, Both).
10. Select the type of photo session you would like to have (e.g., Family, Couple, Individual, Newborn, Senior, Senior - Couple, Senior - Group).
11. Enter the session length.
12. Choose a session date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Type in your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred way to be contacted (Home, Work, Mobile, Email).
* **Preferred contact time** (`preferred_contact_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time that works best for you.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required: false): Provide any specific instructions or requests you have.
* **Photography Preferences** (`photography_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your photography preferences (Indoor, Outdoor, Both).
* **Type of Photo Session** (`photo_session_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of photo session you would like to have (Family, Couple, Individual, Newborn, Senior, Senior - Couple, Senior - Group).
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the photo session.
* **Session Date** (`photo_session_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the photo session.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you select your preferred contact method and time.
* Provide clear instructions for any special requests.
* Choose the correct type of photo session and preferences for a successful experience.
