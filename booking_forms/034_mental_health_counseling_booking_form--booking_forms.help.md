# mental_health_counseling_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book an appointment with a mental health counselor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the availability of the therapist you would like to book a session with.
3. Choose the session length and start and end times for your appointment.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments for your booking.
5. Confirm your booking by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or identification.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Therapist** (`therapist`, Text, Optional): Select the therapist you would like to book a session with. Please note that not all therapists may be available.
* **Availability** (`available_dates`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the date(s) you are available to attend the session.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the start time for your session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the end time for your session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, Select One, Optional): Select the length of your session, 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
* **Booking Notes** (`booking_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for your booking.
* **Booking Summary** (`form_information`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief summary of your booking.
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, Select One, Optional): Select 'True' to confirm your booking.
* **Cancel Booking** (`cancel_booking`, Select One, Optional): Select 'True' to cancel your booking.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person booking the session.
* **Session Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the session.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of your appointment.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address.
* **Client City** (`client_city`, Text, Optional): Enter the city of the location of your appointment.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, Text, Optional): Enter the state of the location of your appointment.
* **Client Zip** (`client_zip`, Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the location of your appointment.
* **Client Country** (`client_country`, Text, Optional): Enter the country of the location of your appointment.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, Text, Optional): Select the type of session you would like to book.
* **Client Age** (`client_age`, Text, Optional): Enter your age.
* **Confirm** (`confirm_booking`, Select One, Optional): Select 'True' to confirm your booking.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_booking`, Select One, Optional): Select 'True' to cancel your booking.
