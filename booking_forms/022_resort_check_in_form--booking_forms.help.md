# Resort Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Resort Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from guests for a seamless check-in experience at the resort. This form will help the resort staff to prepare for the guest's arrival and ensure that all necessary arrangements are made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your date and time of arrival in the "Date of Arrival" and "Time of Arrival" fields.
4. Select the type of room you've booked from the "Room Type" options (e.g., Hotel Room, Suite, Villa).
5. Enter the room number where you will be staying.
6. If you have a reservation ID, enter it in the "Reservation ID" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any last-minute updates or requests.
*   **Date of Arrival**: Select the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
*   **Time of Arrival**: Select the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
*   **Room Type**: Select the type of room you've booked (e.g., Hotel Room, Suite, Villa).
*   **Room Number**: Enter the room number you've been assigned.
*   **Reservation ID**: If you have a reservation ID, enter it here (if applicable).
