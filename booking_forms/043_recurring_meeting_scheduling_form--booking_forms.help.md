# Recurring Meeting Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule recurring meetings, allowing you to specify the frequency, duration, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the meeting frequency from the options provided.
2. Select the start and end dates for the meeting.
3. Choose the recurring start and end times for the meeting.
4. Select the frequency of the meeting recurrence (daily, weekly, monthly, or annually).
5. Specify the number of occurrences after which the meeting will recur.
6. Choose how the meeting will recur (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly).
7. Choose whether the meeting will be repeated by day, week, month, or year.
8. Select the frequency of the meeting recurrence (number of days, weeks, months, or years).
9. Provide a name and description for the meeting.
10. Choose the location of the meeting.
11. Provide any additional meeting details.
12. Choose the time zone of the meeting.
13. Select who is organizing the meeting.
14. Select who will be attending the meeting.
15. Choose the calendar to use for the meeting.
16. Provide an iCal ID for the meeting.
17. Provide an iCS URL for the meeting.
18. Choose a unique ID for the meeting on the calendar.
19. Provide a link to the meeting.
20. Provide a URL for the meeting.
21. Add any notes for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the meeting (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Annually).
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of the meeting.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of the meeting.
* **Recurring Start Time**: Choose the start time of the meeting.
* **Recurring End Time**: Choose the end time of the meeting.
* **Recurring Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the recurring meeting (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Annually).
* **Recurring Until**: Choose when the recurring meeting will end (Never, After 1 occurrence, After 2 occurrences, etc.).
* **Repeat By**: Choose how the meeting will recur (Day, Week, Month, Year).
* **Repeat Every**: Choose the frequency of the meeting recurrence (Day, Week, Month, Year).
* **Repeat Every Frequency**: Enter the number of days, weeks, months, or years between each occurrence.
* **Recurring Meeting Name**: Enter a name for the recurring meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Description**: Enter a description for the recurring meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the recurring meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Details**: Enter any additional details for the recurring meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Time Zone**: Choose the time zone of the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Organizers**: Select who is organizing the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Attendees**: Select who will be attending the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting ID**: Enter a unique ID for the meeting on the calendar.
* **Recurring Meeting Calendar**: Choose the calendar to use for the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting iCal ID**: Enter an iCal ID for the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting iCS URL**: Enter an iCS URL for the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Calendar ID**: Enter a unique ID for the meeting on the calendar.
* **Recurring Meeting Link**: Enter a link to the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting URL**: Enter a URL for the meeting.
* **Recurring Meeting Notes**: Enter any notes for the meeting.
