# Halal Investment Consultation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a halal investment consultation with a designated halal investment consultant. The consultant will be available at specific times and dates.
## How To Complete This Form
To book a consultation, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and interests in the first two fields. 
2. Choose a preferred time for your consultation. 
3. Select a preferred date for your consultation. 
4. Enter your consultant's name and details, if applicable. 
5. Confirm the consultant's contact information. 
6. Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for reference and communication.
- **Client Interests** (`client_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of halal investments that interest you.
  - Halal Stocks
  - Islamic Bonds
  - Sukuk
  - Real Estate
  - Mutual Funds
- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of day you would like to schedule your consultation.
- **Consultant Details** (`consultant_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the consultant's name, if not the same as yours.
- **Prophet's Details** (`prophets_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any specific details of the prophet you would like the consultant to be familiar with.
- **Consultant Schedule** (`consultant_schedule`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the time you would like to book the consultant for.
  - 9:00 AM
  - 11:00 AM
  - 2:00 PM
- **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for your consultation.
- **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the consultant's name, if not the same as yours.
- **Consultant** (`consultant`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the consultant.
- **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the consultant's email address for communication.
- **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
- **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the consultant's phone number for communication.
- **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address for reference.
- **Consultant Address** (`consultant_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the consultant's address for reference.
