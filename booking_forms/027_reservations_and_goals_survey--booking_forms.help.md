# Reservations And Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their reservations and goals. It consists of a series of questions that help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. If you have multiple reservations, please select "Yes" for each applicable question.
3. For the "Customer Name", "Customer Email", and "Customer Phone" fields, please enter your real name, email address, and phone number, respectively.
4. For the "Reservation Details" field, please provide a brief description of your reservation request.
5. For the "Priority" field, select one of the three options (High, Medium, Low) to indicate the level of importance of your reservation.
6. For the "Goals Priority" field, select all applicable options (High, Medium, Low) to indicate the level of importance of your goals.
7. For the "Goals Description" field, please provide a brief description of your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservations request** (`reservation_request`, `note`, optional): This field is used to indicate whether you have a reservations request or not. Select "Yes" if you have a request, and "No" otherwise.
* **Customers request** (`customer_request`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to indicate whether you have any customers requests. Select "Yes" if you have a request, and "No" otherwise.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, optional): This field is used to enter the date of your visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, optional): This field is used to enter the time of your visit.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter your real name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your reservation request.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select the level of importance of your reservation. Select one of the three options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Goals Priority** (`goals_priority`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select the level of importance of your goals. Select all applicable options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Goals Description** (`goals_description`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your goals.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required information to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and honest information.
