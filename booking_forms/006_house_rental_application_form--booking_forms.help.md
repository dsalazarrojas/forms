# House Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Rental Application Form is used for potential renters to apply for a house rental. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your application is considered for a suitable rental opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **User Name** in the User Name field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the Email Address field.
3.  Provide your **Phone Number** in the Phone Number field.
4.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the Date of Birth field.
5.  Specify your **Desired Move In Date** in the Desired Move In Date field.
6.  Indicate the **Rental Duration** you're looking for in the Rental Duration field.
7.  Select your **Preferred Unit** from the options in the Preferred Unit field.
8.  Select your **Preferred Floor** from the options in the Preferred Floor field.
9.  Add any additional **Information** in the Additional Info field.
10.  Fill in the **Submitted By** field with your name.
11.  Enter the date you **Submitted On** in the Submitted On field.
12.  Enter your name again in the **Submitted By Name** field.
13.  Enter your phone number in the **Submitted By Phone** field.
14.  Enter your email address in the **Submitted By Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Desired Move In Date** (date, required/optional): Specify the date you would like to move in.
* **Rental Duration** (number, required/optional): Indicate how many months you are looking to rent for.
* **Preferred Unit** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of unit you prefer from the options.
* **Preferred Floor** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the floors you prefer from the options.
* **Additional Info** (note, required/optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful in your application.
* **Submitted By** (text, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Submitted On** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Submitted By Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name again for verification purposes.
* **Submitted By Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submitted By Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
