# Lash Extension Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and schedule lash extension services with a salon or technician. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow these steps:
- Select the required fields indicated with an asterisk \*.
- Enter your **First Name**, which is required to identify you properly.
- Enter your **Last Name**, which is required to identify you properly.
- Enter your **Phone** number, which is required for the salon to contact you.
- Enter your **Email**, which is required to communicate with you about your appointment.
- Choose your preferred **Service** from the available options.
- Select a date and time for your **Date** and **Time**, respectively.
- Choose your preferred **Technician** from the available options. 
- If desired, enter any **Notes** about your appointment.
- If desired, select who **Assigned To**, which is used to determine who your appointment is assigned to.
- Enter the duration of your **Service Time**, which is required to schedule your appointment properly.
- Select the desired **Service Duration** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your phone number so the salon can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email for communication about your appointment.
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, *): Select your preferred lash extension service.
* **Date** (`date`, date, *): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, *): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Technician** (`technician`, select_multiple, *): Choose your preferred technician.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, ): Enter any additional information about your appointment.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, ): If desired, select who you would like your appointment assigned to.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, *): Enter the duration of your appointment.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, select_multiple, ): Select the desired length of your lash extension service.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Choose your preferred date and time according to the salon's availability.
* Select your preferred technician for your appointment.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please enter them in the **Notes** field.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the salon for clarification.
