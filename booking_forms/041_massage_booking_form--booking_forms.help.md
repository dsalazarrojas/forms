# Massage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to book a massage appointment at your spa or wellness center. To ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Text): Enter your name and any additional information you'd like to share about your visit. 
* **Appointment Details** (Select One):
	+ Select one of the massage types: 30min, 60min, or 90min
	+ Choose from: 
		- Massage 30min
		- Massage 60min
		- Massage 90min
		- Deep Tissue
		- Swedish
		- Sports
* **Booking Date** (Date): Select a date for your appointment. Please note that this field is optional, but selecting a date will help you schedule your appointment more effectively.
* **Booking Time** (Time): Select a time for your appointment. This field is also optional, but choosing a time will help you and our staff better coordinate your visit.
* **Preferred Date** (Date): If you'd like to schedule your appointment on a specific date other than the one you've selected in the previous field, please enter it here. 
* **Appointment Time** (Time): Select a time for your preferred appointment. This field is for planning purposes only, and our staff will adjust according to your preferred time if possible.
* **Massage Type** (Select One/Select Multiple):
	+ Select one or multiple massage types: 30min, 60min, 90min, Deep Tissue, Swedish, Sports
* **Massage Style** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose from: 
		- 30min
		- 60min
		- 90min
		- Deep Tissue
		- Swedish
		- Sports
* **Preferred Time** (Time): Select a preferred time for your appointment. This field is optional and will help us better schedule your appointment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth and effective booking process.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the User Information field.
* Our staff will do their best to accommodate your preferred date and time; however, please be aware that availability might vary depending on the massage therapist and their schedule.
