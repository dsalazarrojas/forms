# Bike Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike Booking Form is used to collect information from customers to book a bike for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the booking details, including Pick-up Date, Return Date, and Preferred Pick-up Time.
2. Select the bike category that suits your needs.
3. Specify the number of bikes required.
4. Provide your rider height to ensure we can select the right frame size.
5. If needed, select any additional equipment you require.
6. Provide your contact information, including full name, email address, and phone number.
7. Confirm that you agree to the rental terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pick-up Date**: Enter the date you want to pick up the bike. This date will determine the start of your rental period.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return the bike. This date will determine the end of your rental period.
* **Preferred Pick-up Time**: Choose a preferred time for you to pick up the bike.
* **Select Bike Category**: Choose the type of bike that suits your needs, such as Standard Cruiser, Premium Road Bike, Mountain Bike, Tandem Bike, or Kids Bike.
* **Number of Bikes**: Specify the number of bikes you require.
* **Rider Height**: Enter your height to help us select the right frame size for you.
* **Additional Equipment Needed**: If you need any additional equipment, such as a helmet, bike lock, or repair kit, select all that apply.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for booking confirmation and other updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **I agree to the rental terms and conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the rental terms and conditions.

By following these steps and providing the necessary information, you will be able to book your bike rental with ease.
