# puppetry_workshop_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for booking a workshop. It collects participant information and preferences for the workshop. Fill out this form to register for the workshop and provide essential details for the organization of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your workshop preferences.
2. Fill out the participant details, which includes the participant's name (note: this is a numerical field, enter 0 or 1 to represent "yes" or "no" respectively).
3. Enter the participant's email address.
4. Select a start date for the workshop.
5. Choose the workshop duration in hours.
6. Confirm if you prefer to be contacted by the workshop organizer (note: this is a select one field).
7. Select your accommodation preferences.
8. Provide emergency contact information, including a person's name and their details (phone, email).
9. (Optional) Write a message for the participant and for emergency situations.
10. (Optional) Enter additional emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Preferences** (select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferences for the workshop.
- **Participant Details** (note, required): Enter participant's details, represented as 0 or 1 for "yes" or "no".
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Participant Name** (number, required): Enter your name.
- **Date Of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Workshop Start Date** (date, required): Choose the start date for the workshop.
- **Workshop Duration** (number, required): Select the duration of the workshop in hours.
- **Emergency Contact Person** (text, required): Enter a name for the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Message** (text, optional): Enter a message for emergency situations.
- **Participant Message** (text, optional): Enter a message for the participant.
- **Accommodation** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your accommodation preferences.
- **Workshop** (select_one, optional): Confirm if you prefer to be contacted by the workshop organizer.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (text, optional): Enter additional information for the emergency contact person.
