# Delivery Date Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for delivery schedules and details. It is designed for industries such as food, retail, and services delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your desired delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your availability.
2. Select your preferred delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
3. Choose your preferred delivery method from the available options in the "Delivery Method" field.
4. Enter your pickup location in the "Pickup Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Delivery Date (delivery_date, date, required: false)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your delivery. This field is optional, but it helps us plan ahead.
*   **Delivery Time (delivery_time, time, required: false)**: Select your preferred time for delivery. This field is essential for coordinating logistics.
*   **Delivery Method (delivery_method, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose between "Delivery Driver", "Delivery Person", and "Pickup" options to decide how you prefer your delivery to be handled. You can select more than one option if needed.
*   **Pickup Location (pickup_location, text, required: false)**: Enter the location where you would like to collect your delivery. Be as specific as possible to ensure smooth logistics.

## Tips
Remember that some fields are optional, but providing accurate information will help us serve you better. If you have any special requests or concerns, please let us know in the "pickup_location" field.
