# portrait_questionnaire_for_women - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a photography session for women. It requires you to provide essential details about the session, including the photographer, your name, contact information, and preferences for the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your name and contact information correctly.
3. Choose the location for the shoot (Studio, On-site, or At client).
4. Select the equipment required for the shoot (Micro, Macro, or Soft).
5. Choose the style for the shoot (Natural, Glamour, or Fashion).
6. Add any additional notes or comments for the photographer or model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer who will be working with you.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the photography session materials.
* **E mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication with the photographer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date for the photography session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time for the photography session.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Choose where the shoot will take place (Studio, On-site, or At client).
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select the equipment needed for the shoot (Micro, Macro, or Soft).
* **Style** (`style`, select_one, required): Choose the style for the shoot (Natural, Glamour, or Fashion).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests for the photographer.
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or requests for the photographer.
* **Model Notes** (`model_notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or requests for the model.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Choose the location and style according to your preferences.
* If you have any special requests, add them in the Notes field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
