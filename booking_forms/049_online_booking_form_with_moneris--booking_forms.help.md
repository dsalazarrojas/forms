# Online Booking Form With Moneris - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Booking Form With Moneris is a tool designed for users to book online with Moneris.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your Traveller Information (page 1).
2. Enter your Arrival and Departure details (page 2).
3. Enter your Pick up Address (page 3).
4. Select your Payment Method (page 4).
5. Enter your Payment Details (page 5).
6. Choose your Date and Time (page 6).
7. Enter your Email (page 7).
8. Enter your Phone number (page 8).
9. Select your Integration Method (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveller Information** (page 1, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant traveller information.
* **Arrival and Departure** (page 2, text, required: false): Provide your expected arrival and departure dates and times.
* **Pick up Address** (page 3, text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like to be picked up.
* **Payment Method** (page 4, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred payment method, Moneris or Another Method.
* **Payment Details** (page 5, number, required: false): Enter any additional payment details required for your booking.
* **Date and Time** (page 6, date, required: false): Choose your preferred date and time for the booking.
* **Email** (page 7, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (page 8, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Integration Method** (page 9, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the method you would like to use for integration with your system, Calendar, CRM or Spreadsheets.
