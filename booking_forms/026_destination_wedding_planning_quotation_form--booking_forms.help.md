# Destination Wedding Planning Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help couples plan their destination wedding by providing essential information about their requirements and preferences. It guides wedding planners, photographers, and other service providers in offering tailored quotations and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing basic information about the destination wedding.
2. Enter the details of the couple, including names and contact information.
3. Specify the venue and date of the wedding.
4. Choose the services you require, such as wedding planners, photographers, or videographers.
5. Select your preferred accommodation options.
6. Choose from the available food and drink options.
7. Decide on a wedding theme that suits your taste.
8. Enter your estimated budget for the wedding.
9. Finally, provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination Wedding Planning** (`destination_wedding_planning`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your destination wedding plans.
* **Couples Details** (`couples_details`, text, optional): Enter the names and contact information of the couple getting married.
* **Venue and Date** (`venue_and_date`, text, optional): Enter the location and date of the wedding.
* **Services** (`services`, select multiple, optional): Choose the services you require for the wedding, such as Wedding Planner, Photographer, or Videographer.
* **Travel** (`travel`, text, optional): Enter any travel plans or information related to the wedding.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, select multiple, optional): Choose your preferred accommodation options for the wedding guests, such as Hotel, Villa, or Resort.
* **Food and Drink** (`food_and_drink`, select multiple, optional): Select the catering options you prefer, such as Bar, Restaurant, or Catering.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, select one, optional): Choose a wedding theme that suits your style, such as Beach, Rustic, or Formal.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the wedding.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the service providers can offer you the best possible services.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
