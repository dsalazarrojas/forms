# Pet Grooming Appointment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Grooming Appointment Booking Form is used to schedule a pet grooming appointment at our facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field in the format of `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of service you would like to book from the "Service type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date you would like to schedule the service in the "Service date" field.
7. Enter the time you would like to schedule the service in the "Service time" field.
8. If you have any additional information or requests, enter it in the "Additional info" field.
9. Select the type of pet that requires service from the "Pet type" dropdown menu.
10. Sign and date the "Owner signature" field to confirm the booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (text, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Service type** (select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of service you would like to book, such as "Bath" or "Cut".
* **Service date** (date, required):
	+ Enter the date you would like to schedule the service in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Service time** (time, required):
	+ Enter the time you would like to schedule the service in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Additional info** (text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information or requests you have for your pet's service.
* **Pet type** (select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the type of pet that requires service, such as "Dog" or "Cat".
* **Owner signature** (text, optional):
	+ Sign and date the field to confirm the booking.

Note: If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
