# cultural_event_photography_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about your cultural event photography needs. Please complete this form to ensure our team has all the necessary information to provide you with the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client and event information sections, including the title of the event, event dates, and contact information.
2. Indicate the services you require from us, including whether you need any optional services.
3. Provide any additional comments or details about your event.
4. Upload any relevant images related to your event.
5. Confirm your payment information (if applicable).
6. Review and confirm your contact information and event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Page 1, `client_info`): Please provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Event Details** (Page 1, `event_info`): Enter the title of your event and a brief description.
* **Event Dates** (Page 1, `event_dates`): Provide the dates of your event.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1, `contact_info`): Enter your contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Services Required** (Page 2, `services_required`): Choose the services you need from us, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Photographer Information** (Page 2, `photographer_info`): Provide any relevant information about the photographer(s) you've hired for your event.
* **Payment Information** (Page 2, `payment_info`): Enter your payment details (if applicable).
* **Upload Image** (Page 2, `upload_image`): Upload any images related to your event.
* **Image Details** (Page 2, `image_details`): Describe the uploaded image(s) (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (Page 3, `additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or details about your event.
* **Optional Services** (Page 3, `services_optional`): Choose the optional services you'd like to add to your order.
* **Photographer Notes** (Page 3, `photographer_notes`): Add any notes or instructions for the photographer(s).
* **Client Confirmation** (Page 3, `client_confirmation`): Confirm that you've reviewed and understand the services and details provided.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is being completed by a client. If this is not the case, please adjust the guide accordingly.
