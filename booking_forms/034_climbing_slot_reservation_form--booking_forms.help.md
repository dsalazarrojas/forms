# Climbing Slot Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help climbers reserve a time slot for a climbing session at our climbing facility. The form collects essential information from customers to ensure a smooth and safe experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Provide your valid **Email Address** to allow us to contact you about your reservation.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication purposes.
4. Indicate your **Membership Status** to ensure we have your correct membership details.
5. If you are a member, enter your **Member ID** for reference.
6. Select your desired **Reserve Date** and **Preferred Time Slot** for your session.
7. Choose the type of **Session** you'd like to participate in (e.g., Open climbing, Beginner lesson, etc.).
8. Select the **Session Duration** for your session.
9. If you're booking for a group, provide the **Number of People in Group**, and you can optionally list the **Names of Group Members**.
10. Select the **Group Experience Level** to help us tailor the session to your needs.
11. If required, choose whether you need an **Instructor** for your session and specify the type of instruction you need.
12. Finally, provide any **Special Requests or Needs** you may have (e.g., accessibility requirements or specific routes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Customer Information, id: 2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address (Customer Information, id: 3)**: Enter your valid email address for us to contact you about your reservation.
* **Phone Number (Customer Information, id: 4)**: Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Membership Status (Membership Information, id: 6)**: Select your membership status to ensure we have the correct details.
* **Member ID (Membership Information, id: 7)**: If you are a member, enter your member ID for reference.
* **Desired Reservation Date (Reservation Details, id: 9)**: Select the date you'd like to reserve a time slot for your session.
* **Preferred Time Slot (Reservation Details, id: 10)**: Choose the time slot you prefer for your session.
* **Type of Session (Reservation Details, id: 11)**: Select the type of session you'd like to participate in (e.g., Open climbing, Beginner lesson, etc.).
* **Session Duration (Reservation Details, id: 12)**: Choose the session duration that suits your needs.
* **Number of People in Group (Group Information, id: 14)**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Names of Group Members (Group Information, id: 15)**: If you're booking for a group, list the names of all group members.
* **Group Experience Level (Group Information, id: 16)**: Select the group experience level to help us tailor the session to your needs.
* **Do You Need an Instructor? (Instruction Request, id: 18)**: Choose whether you need an instructor for your session.
* **Type of Instruction Needed (Instruction Request, id: 19)**: If you need an instructor, specify the type of instruction you require.
* **Special Requests or Needs (Special Requests, id: 21)**: Provide any special requests or needs you may have (e.g., accessibility requirements or specific routes).
* **Wall Preference (Special Requests, id: 22)**: Choose the wall preference for your session (e.g., Boulder walls, Top-rope walls, etc.).

By following these steps and explanations, you'll be able to complete the form accurately and ensure a smooth reservation process.
