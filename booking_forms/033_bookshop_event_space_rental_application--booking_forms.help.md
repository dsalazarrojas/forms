# Bookshop Event Space Rental Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help Bookshop staff manage event space rentals for events held at the Bookshop. To ensure accurate processing, please provide as much information as possible about your event. This will help us better understand your needs and provide the best possible experience for you and your guests. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our event coordinator.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting to fill out this form, please ensure you have the necessary information about your event. This includes the organizer's contact details, event details (type, date, start time, duration, and expected number of attendees), and any specific space and equipment requirements. 

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact information (organizer name, email, and phone number).
2.  Choose the event type (e.g., author reading, workshop, etc.).
3.  Select the preferred date and start time for your event.
4.  Indicate the expected duration of your event.
5.  Choose the type of space setup you need (e.g., theater style, classroom style, etc.).
6.  Select the equipment you need (e.g., microphone, projector, etc.).
7.  Choose your catering plans (e.g., Bookshop provides, you provide, etc.).
8.  If you're featuring books or authors, describe the focus of your event.
9.  If you have a budget, provide it (optional).
10.  Make any additional requests (e.g., specific room arrangement, extra amenities, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Name** (1) (`text`, required): Fill in your full name as the event organizer.
* **Email Address** (2) (`email`, required): Enter your contact email for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (3) (`text`, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (4) (`text`, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Event Type** (5) (`select_one`, required): Choose the type of event you're hosting (e.g., author reading, workshop, etc.).
* **Preferred Event Date** (6) (`date`, required): Select the date you'd like to host your event.
* **Event Start Time** (7) (`time`, required): Choose the start time of your event.
* **Event Duration** (8) (`select_one`, required): Select the length of your event (e.g., 1 hour, 1.5 hours, etc.).
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (9) (`number`, required): Enter the approximate number of people attending your event.
* **Space Setup Requirements** (10) (`select_one`, required): Choose the space arrangement you need (e.g., theater style, classroom style, etc.).
* **Equipment Needed** (11) (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional equipment you require for your event (e.g., microphone, projector, etc.).
* **Catering Plans** (12) (`select_one`, required): Choose who will provide refreshments (e.g., Bookshop provides, you provide, etc.).
* **Books or Authors Featured** (13) (`text`, optional): If applicable, describe the focus of your event (e.g., author reading, book signing, etc.).
* **Rental Budget** (14) (`select_one`, optional): Enter your expected budget for the event (optional).
* **Additional Requests** (15) (`text`, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, describe them here.
