# office_space_tour_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form, "office_space_tour_registration", is designed for prospective tenants to register for an office space tour. The form collects information about the prospective tenant's needs and preferences for the tour, including their contact information and preferred dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your prospective tenant information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select the description of the requested tour in the "Tour Request Details" field.
3.  Choose up to 3 preferred dates for the tour in the "Preferred Dates" field.
4.  Select your preferred tour times in the "Preferred Tour Times" field.
5.  Enter the requested tour duration in the "Requested Tour Duration" field.
6.  Provide additional comments about your tour request, if any.
7.  Select your preferred follow-up method in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Prospective Tenant Info** (text): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you.
*   **Tour Request Details** (text): Briefly describe the kind of tour you are looking for.
*   **Preferred Dates** (select multiple): Choose up to 3 dates that you would like to schedule the tour for. You can choose multiple dates to accommodate your schedule.
*   **Preferred Tour Times** (select multiple): Choose the tour time that best fits your schedule.
*   **Requested Tour Duration** (number): Enter the length of the tour you are expecting, in minutes.
*   **Additional Comments** (note): Add any additional comments or information that might help us accommodate your tour request.
*   **Email** (email): Provide your email address so we can get back to you about your tour schedule.
*   **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your tour.
*   **Follow-up** (select one): Choose how you would like us to follow up with you after submitting this form.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when describing your tour needs and preferences.
*   If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
*   Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number so we can get back to you efficiently.
