# Karate Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for booking a karate lesson with a chosen tutor. You will be asked to provide your contact information, choose a booking time, select a tutor, and add any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select a booking time from the options provided.
3. Choose a tutor from the list of available tutors.
4. If you have any additional requests or notes, enter them in the text field "Additional note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Details (Customer Details)
* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, text, required)
You must enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Booking Time (Booking Time)
* **Booking Time** (`Booking Time`, select_one, required)
Choose a time for your karate lesson. You can select from the available options, which are: Option A and Option B.

### Tutor (Tutor)
* **Tutor** (`Tutor`, select_one, required)
Select a tutor for your lesson. You can choose from: Tutor 1, Tutor 2, and Tutor 3.

### How can we contact you? (How can we contact you?)
* **How can we contact you?** (`How can we contact you?`, text, optional)
If you have any preferred contact information, such as a phone number or email address, enter it here. This is optional, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any contact preferences.

### Additional note (Additional note)
* **Additional note** (`Additional note`, note, optional)
If you have any additional notes or requests for your lesson, enter them in this text field. This is optional, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any additional notes.
