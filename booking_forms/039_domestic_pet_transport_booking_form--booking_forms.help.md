# domestic_pet_transport_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The domestic_pet_transport_booking_form is designed to collect information for domestic pet transport bookings. This form is used to gather all necessary details for a smooth and efficient transport process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Destination" field with the drop-off location of your pet.
2. Enter the "Transport Details" in the provided text field.
3. Select a "Pickup Date" for your pet's transport.
4. Specify the "Drop off Location" where you'd like your pet to be dropped off.
5. Enter "Pet Details" in the provided text field.
6. Provide your "Pet Owner Name" and "Pet Owner Contact" information.
7. Choose the transport method (Air or Land) from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter an "Estimated Cost" for the transport service.
9. Select a "Payment Method" (Cash or Card) from the dropdown menu.
10. Confirm your transport details and pet details by selecting "Yes" in the corresponding fields.
11. Confirm your "Signature" by selecting "Yes" in the field.
12. Enter your "Email" and "Phone" for contact information.
13. Enter any "Additional Info" regarding the transport.
14. Confirm your "Phone" contact by selecting "Yes" in the field.
15. Confirm your "Email" contact by selecting "Yes" in the field.
16. Confirm your "Payment Method" by selecting "Yes" in the field.
17. Confirm your "Signature" by selecting "Yes" in the field.
18. Finally, confirm your "Transport" and "Pet Details" by selecting "Yes" in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Destination** (`destination`, `text`, required): Enter the drop-off location of your pet.
* **Transport Details** (`transport_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional transport details.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for the pickup of your pet.
* **Drop off Location** (`drop_off_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where you'd like your pet to be dropped off.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant pet details.
* **Pet Owner Name** (`pet_owner_name`, `text`, required): Provide your name as the pet owner.
* **Pet Owner Contact** (`pet_owner_contact`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information as the pet owner.
* **Transport Method** (`transport_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between Air or Land transport options.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated cost for the transport service.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select between Cash or Card payment options.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions for the transport.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Confirm your signature by selecting "Yes" in the field.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone contact information.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information regarding the transport.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your phone contact by selecting "Yes" in the field.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your email contact by selecting "Yes" in the field.
