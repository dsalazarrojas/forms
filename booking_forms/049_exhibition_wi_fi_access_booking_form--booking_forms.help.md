# exhibition_wi_fi_access_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exhibition Wi-Fi access booking form is used to collect information from exhibitors to book Wi-Fi access for their exhibition during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibition Title** field with the name of the exhibition you are booking Wi-Fi access for.
2. Provide the **Event Name** where the exhibition will take place.
3. Enter the **Event Start Date** and **Event End Date** when the exhibition will be held.
4. Enter your **Exhibitor Name** and provide details about the devices you will use during the exhibition.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Usage Agreement**.
6. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
7. Add any additional **Notes** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Title** (title, text, required: false): Enter the name of the exhibition you are booking Wi-Fi access for.
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event where the exhibition will take place.
* **Event Start Date** (event_start_date, date, required: true): Enter the date when the event starts.
* **Event End Date** (event_end_date, date, required: true): Enter the date when the event ends.
* **Exhibitor Name** (exhibitor_name, text, required: true): Enter your name as the exhibitor.
* **Device Details** (device_details, text, required: true): Provide details about the devices you will use during the exhibition.
* **Usage Agreement** (usage_agreement, text, required: true): Confirm that you have read and agree to the usage agreement.
* **Email** (email, email, required: true): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required: false): Add any additional information or notes about your booking.
