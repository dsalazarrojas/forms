# Barre Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barre Class Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in registering for a Barre class. By completing this form, you'll help us understand your preferences and needs, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for you in our classes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your information, making sure to select the correct options for each field.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
3. Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be known in the class.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for class confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your best contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of Barre class you are interested in:
	+ Beginner Barre
	+ Intermediate Barre
	+ Advanced Barre
	+ Barre Fusion
	+ Prenatal Barre
	+ Senior Barre
* **Preferred Day of Week** (`preferred_day`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to attend:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time slot:
	+ Early morning - 6 AM to 8 AM
	+ Morning - 8 AM to 12 PM
	+ Afternoon - 12 PM to 4 PM
	+ Evening - 4 PM to 7 PM
	+ Night - after 7 PM
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current fitness level:
	+ Beginner - new to exercise
	+ Intermediate - some experience
	+ Advanced - very active
	+ Professional - instructor level
* **Previous Barre Experience** (`barre_experience`, select_one, required): Tell us about your Barre experience:
	+ Never tried Barre
	+ 1 to 5 classes
	+ 6 to 20 classes
	+ Regular practitioner
	+ Certified instructor
* **Medical Conditions or Injuries** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please let us know.
* **Are You Currently Pregnant** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are currently pregnant:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose your membership option:
	+ Drop in - single class
	+ 5 class package
	+ 10 class package
	+ Monthly unlimited
	+ Annual membership
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method:
	+ Credit card
	+ Debit card
	+ Cash
	+ Check
	+ Gift card
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Read and agree to our liability waiver:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate if you consent to photos and videos:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ No, I do not consent
* **Fitness Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose your fitness goals:
	+ Build strength
	+ Improve flexibility
	+ Lose weight
	+ Tone muscles
	+ Better posture
	+ Stress relief
	+ General fitness
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Let us know how you heard about us:
	+ Social media
	+ Friend referral
	+ Google search
	+ Walked by
	+ Previous client
	+ Other
* **Special Requests or Questions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or questions, please let us know.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us at any time.
