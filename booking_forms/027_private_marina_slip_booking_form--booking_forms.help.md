# Private Marina Slip Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Private Marina Slip Booking Form is designed to be a comprehensive tool for marina staff, slip owners, and visitors to book and manage marina slips. This form allows users to input their booking details, including boat information, dates, and payment preferences, to ensure a seamless and organized booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the booking process.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which include:
	* Boat name
	* Start date
	* Start time
	* End date
	* End time
	* Marina contact
	* Owner email
	* Owner phone
3. Optional fields can be filled out for additional information, such as:
	* Boat type
	* Slip size
	* Slip location
	* Payment method
	* Slip booking status
	* Notes
	* Booking duration
	* Boat length
4. Submit the form to complete the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Boat Name (1)** (`boat_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your boat, including the boat type and size.
* **Boat Type (2)** (`boat_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of your boat, which can be:
	+ Boat
	+ Kayak
	+ Yacht
* **Slip Size (3)** (`slip_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the slip size that best suits your boat, which can be:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Start Date (4)** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to start your booking.
* **Start Time (5)** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to start your booking.
* **End Date (6)** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to end your booking.
* **End Time (7)** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the time you would like to end your booking.
* **Slip Location (8)** (`slip_location`, text, required): Enter the slip location where you would like to be docked.
* **Booking Duration (9)** (`booking_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your booking in hours.
* **Boat Length (10)** (`boat_length`, number, required): Enter the length of your boat in feet.
* **Payment Method (11)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your booking, which can be:
	+ Online
	+ Offline
	+ Onsite
* **Slip Booking Status (12)** (`slip_booking_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your booking, which can be:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Cancelled
* **Notes (13)** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your booking.
* **Owner Email (14)** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the boat owner or contact person.
* **Owner Phone (15)** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the boat owner or contact person.
* **Marina Contact (16)** (`marina_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the marina.
* **Slip Number (17)** (`slip_number`, number, optional): Enter the slip number where your boat will be docked.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the required fields filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns during the booking process, please contact the marina contact for assistance.
* Review your booking details carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
