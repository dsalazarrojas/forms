# virtual_financial_consultation_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a virtual financial consultation with a client. Please fill out the form to schedule a meeting with a financial professional and provide all necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the client you would like to book a consultation with from the "Select Client" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date of the consultation in the "Consultation Date" field. The date must be in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Choose the time of the day for the consultation from 00:00 to 23:59.
4. Confirm if the financial professional will be assisting you during the consultation.
5. Specify the duration of the consultation in minutes.
6. Select the language you prefer the consultation to be conducted in from the options provided. Note that some consultations may not be available in all languages.
7. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the financial professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Client** (`Select Client`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the client you would like to book a consultation with from the dropdown menu.
* **Consultation Date** (`Consultation Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the consultation in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time Of Day** (`Time Of Day`, `time`, required): Choose the time of the day for the consultation from 00:00 to 23:59.
* **Financial Professional** (`Financial Professional`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the financial professional will be assisting you during the consultation.
* **Booking Duration** (`Booking Duration`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of the consultation in minutes.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the language you prefer the consultation to be conducted in from the options provided.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the financial professional.
