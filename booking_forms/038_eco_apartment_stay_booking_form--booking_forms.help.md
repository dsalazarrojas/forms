# eco_apartment_stay_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eco-apartment stay booking form is designed to gather essential information from guests to facilitate a smooth and eco-friendly booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the guest's details, including any necessary contact information.
2. Select the travel dates for the stay.
3. Agree to the policy agreement, indicating whether or not you accept the terms and conditions.
4. Provide accommodation details, including the type of accommodation and room you require.
5. Choose the accommodation type (Apartment, Studio, or House).
6. Select the room type (Shared or Private).
7. Enter the accommodation number (if applicable).
8. Choose the arrival date for the stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, required): Enter the guest's name, email, and phone number.
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, date, required): Select the dates for the stay, including the start and end dates.
* **Policy Agreement** (Policy Agreement, select_multiple, required): Accept or decline the policy agreement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Accommodation Details** (Accommodation Details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the accommodation, such as special requests.
* **Accommodation Type** (Accommodation Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of accommodation (Apartment, Studio, or House).
* **Room Type** (Room Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of room (Shared or Private).
* **Accommodation Number** (Accommodation Number, number, optional): Enter the accommodation number if applicable.
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, required): Select the date of arrival for the stay.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any issues with the booking.
- Use the provided hints for guidance on filling out the form.
- Review the policy agreement carefully before accepting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the accommodation provider directly.
