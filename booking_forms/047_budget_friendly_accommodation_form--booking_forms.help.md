# Budget Friendly Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Friendly Accommodation Form is designed for users to provide information necessary for booking budget-friendly accommodations. This form collects details such as guest names, contact information, and accommodation preferences to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your full name in the **Guest name** field.
*   Enter your email address in the **Contact email** field to receive booking confirmation.
*   Select your **Check in date** for your arrival.
*   Choose your **Check out date** for your departure.
*   Select your preferred **Room type** from the options provided.
*   If you have a **Max budget per night**, enter your maximum acceptable nightly rate.
*   If you have any **Accessibility needs**, describe them briefly in this field.
*   If you have any **Special requests**, provide details in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest name**: Enter your full name for booking purposes.
*   **Contact email**: Enter your email address to receive booking confirmation and any updates.
*   **Check in date**: Select your arrival date for your stay.
*   **Check out date**: Choose your departure date for your stay.
*   **Room preference**: Select your preferred type of room from the options provided.
*   **Max budget per night**: Enter your maximum acceptable nightly rate if you have one.
*   **Accessibility needs**: If you have any mobility or accessibility requirements, describe them briefly.
*   **Special requests**: If you have any additional requests, describe them in detail.
