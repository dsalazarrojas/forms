# Conference Room Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to subscribe to the conference room facilities. The form collects information about the subscriber's contact details, subscription preferences, and payment information to provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information: Your full name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your subscription type (e.g., Daily Access Pass, Weekly Subscription, etc.).
3. Select the preferred rooms for your meetings or events.
4. Choose the duration of your subscription (start and end dates).
5. Indicate your typical usage days and hours.
6. Select the average size of your meetings or events.
7. Specify the main purpose of your meetings or events (e.g., client meetings, internal meetings, etc.).
8. Choose the equipment package you require (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Custom).
9. Indicate if you require any special equipment or services.
10. Choose the catering option (e.g., no catering, coffee and tea service, etc.).
11. Select any support services you require.
12. Provide billing contact information (name, email, and address).
13. Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
14. If applicable, enter any promotional code.
15. Finally, select how you heard about us (e.g., website, social media, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Full Name (<code>subscriber_name</code>): Your complete name that will be used for contact purposes.**
* **Email Address (<code>email</code>): The email address we will use to communicate with you.**
* **Phone Number (<code>phone</code>): Your contact phone number.**
* **Company Name (<code>company</code>): The name of your organization.**
* **Department (<code>department</code>): Your department name (optional).**
* **Job Title (<code>job_title</code>): Your position in the company.**
* **Subscription Type (<code>subscription_type</code>): Choose the type of subscription that suits your needs.**
* **Preferred Rooms (<code>preferred_rooms</code>): Select the rooms you prefer for your meetings or events.**
* **Subscription Start Date (<code>subscription_start</code>): The date you would like to start your subscription.**
* **Subscription End Date (<code>subscription_end</code>): The date you would like to end your subscription.**
* **Typical Usage Days (<code>typical_usage_days</code>): Indicate which days you will typically use the facility.**
* **Typical Usage Hours (<code>typical_usage_hours</code>): Specify the time of day you will typically use the facility.**
* **Average Meeting Size (<code>average_meeting_size</code>): Indicate the number of attendees in your meetings or events.**
* **Primary Use (<code>primary_use</code>): Select the main purpose of your meetings or events.**
* **Equipment Package (<code>equipment_included</code>): Choose the equipment package that suits your needs.**
* **Equipment Details (<code>equipment_details</code>): Specify any special equipment or services you require.**
* **Catering Option (<code>catering_option</code>): Choose your catering option (e.g., no catering, coffee and tea service, etc.).**
* **Support Services (<code>support_services</code>): Select any support services you require (e.g., IT support, etc.).**
* **Billing Contact Name (<code>billing_contact</code>): Enter your name for billing purposes.**
* **Billing Email (<code>billing_email</code>): Enter your email address for billing purposes.**
* **Billing Address (<code>billing_address</code>): Enter your mailing address for billing purposes.**
* **Payment Method (<code>payment_method</code>): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, etc.).**
* **Promotional Code (<code>promo_code</code>): If applicable, enter any promotional code.**
* **How Did You Hear About Us (<code>referral_source</code>): Select how you found out about us (e.g., website, etc.).**
* **Special Requests (<code>special_requests</code>): Enter any special requests or needs.**
* **I Accept Subscription Terms (<code>terms_accepted</code>): Agree to the terms and conditions of the subscription.**

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive the correct subscription experience.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us.
- We will review and process your subscription request as soon as possible after submitting the form.
