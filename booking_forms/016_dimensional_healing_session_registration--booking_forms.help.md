# dimensional_healing_session_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before a dimensional healing session. The information collected will help the practitioner prepare for the session and ensure that it runs smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the required fields.
2. Enter your contact number and email in the Client Details section.
3. Select your preferred time and time end for the session in the Appointment Preferences section.
4. Agree to the Client Consent forms by selecting the relevant options.
5. Confirm your payment method in the Payment Method section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`title`): Enter a brief description of the session, such as the type of session or any specific details about the session.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`): Enter your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Appointment Preferences** (`appointment_preferences`): Select the day and time you would like to have the session.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`): Agree to the Client Consent Form by selecting "I have read and agree to the Client Consent Form".
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Select the time of the session (Monday to Sunday).
* **Preferred Time End** (`preferred_time_end`): Select the end time of the session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`): Select the duration of the session (15, 30, or 60 minutes).
* **Practitioner Details** (`practitioner_details`): Enter the name of the practitioner you will be working with.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you will be making payment (Online Banking or In Person).
* **Client Consent 2**, 3, 4, 5, 6** (`client_consent_2`, `client_consent_3`, `client_consent_4`, `client_consent_5`, `client_consent_6`): Agree to the Client Consent Form 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 by selecting the relevant options.
* **Practitioner Consent** (`practitioner_consent`): Agree to the Practitioner Consent Form by selecting the relevant options.
* **Practitioner Consent 2** (`practitioner_consent_2`): Agree to the Practitioner Consent Form 2 by selecting the relevant options.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.
