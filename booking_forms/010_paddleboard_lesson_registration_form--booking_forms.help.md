# paddleboard_lesson_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paddleboard lesson registration form is designed to collect information about participants and lesson details to schedule and register lessons correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the participant and lesson details before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the participant name, start date, start time, participant email, and phone fields accurately.
3. Enter the correct lesson length, lesson location, and lesson instructor details.
4. Specify the lesson date and time.
5. Enter the participant confirmation and form completion fields to confirm that the form has been filled out accurately.
6. Enter any additional notes about the participant or lesson.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter the participant's full name.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the date of the lesson.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required): Enter the start time of the lesson.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Lesson Length** (lesson_length, number, required): Enter the length of the lesson in minutes.
* **Lesson Location** (lesson_location, text, required): Enter the location of the lesson.
* **Lesson Instructor** (lesson_instructor, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor leading the lesson.
* **Lesson Date** (lesson_date, date, required): Enter the date of the lesson.
* **Lesson Time** (lesson_time, time, required): Enter the time of the lesson.
* **Lesson Start Date** (lesson_start_date, date, required): Enter the date the lesson will start.
* **Lesson End Date** (lesson_end_date, date, required): Enter the date the lesson will end.
* **Participant Notes** (participant_notes, text, required): Enter any notes about the participant.
* **Lesson Notes** (lesson_notes, text, required): Enter any notes about the lesson.
* **Form Confirmation** (form_confirmation, text, required): Confirm that you have filled out the form accurately.
* **Form Rejection** (form_rejection, text, required): Confirm that the form has been rejected.
* **Payment Type** (payment_type, text, required): Select the type of payment method used for the lesson.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status, text, required): Enter the status of the payment.
* **Participant Confirmation** (participant_confirmation, text, required): Confirm that the participant has been notified about the lesson details.
* **Form Completion** (form_completion, text, required): Confirm that the form has been completed accurately.
* **Form Revision** (form_revision, text, required): Enter any revision notes about the form.
* **Instructor Confirmation** (instructor_confirmation, text, required): Confirm that the instructor has been notified about the lesson details.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, text, required): Select the method of payment used for the lesson.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the lesson.
* **Instructor Name** (instructor_name, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor leading the lesson.

Note: Please make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure that the lesson is scheduled correctly and that the participant is properly registered.
