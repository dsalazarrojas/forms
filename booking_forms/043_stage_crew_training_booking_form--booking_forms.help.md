# Stage Crew Training Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a training session for the stage crew. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the training date, time, location, staff name, contact details, training session, duration, topic, and status of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the training date from the calendar picker.
2. Select the training time from the time picker.
3. Enter the training location in the text field.
4. Enter the name of the staff member attending the training.
5. Enter the email address of the staff member.
6. Enter the phone number of the staff member.
7. Choose the training session from the dropdown list.
8. Select the training duration from the dropdown list.
9. Enter a brief description of the training topic.
10. Indicate the status of the training (Booked or Not Booked).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Date (date)**: This is the date when the training is scheduled to take place. Please select a date from the calendar picker.
* **Training Time (time)**: This is the time of the training session. Select a time from the time picker.
* **Training Location (text)**: This is the location where the training will be held. Enter the address or name of the location.
* **Staff Name (text)**: This is the name of the staff member attending the training. Enter the name of the staff member.
* **Email (email)**: This is the email address of the staff member. Enter the email address of the staff member.
* **Phone (text)**: This is the phone number of the staff member. Enter the phone number of the staff member.
* **Training Session (select_one)**: This is the specific training session being booked. Select the session from the dropdown list.
* **Training Duration (select_multiple)**: This is the duration of the training session. Select one or more durations from the dropdown list.
* **Training Topic (text)**: This is a brief description of the training topic. Enter a short description of the topic.
* **Training Status (select_one)**: This is the status of the training session. Indicate whether the training is booked or not.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check the training date and time before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate contact information for the staff member attending the training.
* Select the correct training session and duration to ensure that the training is scheduled correctly.
