# College Equipment Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and book college equipment for academic or non-academic purposes. It helps the college equipment manager to track and manage equipment bookings, ensuring that the equipment is available when needed and returned on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name, email, and contact phone number.
2. Select your status as an Undergraduate Student, Graduate Student, Faculty, Staff, Researcher, or Other.
3. Provide your student or employee ID number.
4. Enter your department name.
5. If you're booking equipment for a class, enter the course name and number.
6. Select the equipment category you need.
7. Choose the specific equipment items required.
8. Specify the quantity needed.
9. Select the dates and times for pickup and return.
10. Choose the purpose of use (Class Project, Research, Thesis or Dissertation, etc.)
11. Describe the purpose of your project.
12. Indicate where the equipment will be used.
13. Confirm if you plan to take the equipment off-campus.
14. Confirm if you need off-campus approval.
15. Confirm if you need equipment training or have completed the required training.
16. Confirm if you have completed the required training (if applicable).
17. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per the college's records.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your school email address (if you have one).
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Your Status** (`requester_type`, select_one, required): Select your status as an undergraduate student, graduate student, faculty, staff, research, or other.
* **Student or Employee ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student or employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): If booking for a class, enter the course name.
* **Course Number** (`course_number`, text, optional): If booking for a class, enter the course number.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): If booking for a class, enter the instructor's name.
* **Booking Request Date** (`booking_date`, date, required): Select the date you need the equipment.
* **Equipment Category** (`equipment_category`, select_one, required): Select the type of equipment you need.
* **Specific Equipment Items** (`equipment_items`, select_multiple, required): Choose the specific equipment items you require.
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of equipment needed.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to collect the equipment.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select the preferred time for pickup.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to return the equipment.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, time, required): Select the preferred return time.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the rental period (Same Day, 1-3 Days, etc.).
* **Purpose of Use** (`purpose_of_use`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of use (Class Project, Research, Thesis or Dissertation, etc.).
* **Purpose Details** (`purpose_details`, text, required): Describe the project or event.
* **Where Will Equipment Be Used** (`location_of_use`, text, required): Indicate where the equipment will be used.
* **Will Equipment Leave Campus** (`off_campus_use`, select_one, required): Confirm if you plan to take the equipment off-campus.
* **Off-Campus Approval** (`off_campus_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm if you need off-campus approval.
* **Equipment Training Required** (`training_required`, select_one, required): Confirm if you need equipment training.
* **Have You Completed Required Training** (`training_completed`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have completed the required training (if applicable).
* **Accessories Needed** (`accessories_needed`, text, optional): Provide any additional accessories required.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Provide any special requirements or conditions.
* **Estimated Equipment Value Acknowledgment** (`estimated_value`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the equipment value.
* **Liability Agreement** (`liability_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the liability agreement.
* **Equipment Care Agreement** (`care_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the equipment care agreement.
* **Late Fee Acknowledgment** (`late_fee_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the late fee agreement.
* **Replacement Cost Acknowledgment** (`replacement_cost`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the replacement cost agreement.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, required): Track the status of your booking request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Be specific when selecting equipment category and items.
* Confirm your understanding of equipment value, liability agreement, and care agreement.
* Provide accurate pickup and return dates and times.
* Confirm if you need off-campus approval.
* Confirm if you need equipment training or have completed the required training.
* Read and understand all the terms and conditions before submitting your form.
