# Trainer Availability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trainer Availability Form is designed for trainers to input their availability and preferences for scheduling training sessions. This form helps us to match trainers with sessions that suit their needs and availability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Schedule" field and enter the dates you are available to work.
2. For the "Schedule Start Time" and "Schedule End Time" fields, enter the start and end times of your available sessions.
3. Select your specialties from the "Specialties" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your preferred location in the "Location" field.
5. Enter any location preferences you have in the "Location Preferences" field.
6. Enter your trainer ID in the "Trainer ID" field.
7. Describe your availability in the "Availability" field.
8. Select any conflicts you have in the "Conflict" field.
9. Select any conflicts you have in the "Conflicts" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Schedule** (`schedule`, text, optional): Enter the dates you are available to work.
- **Schedule Start Time** (`schedule_start_time`, text, optional): Enter the start time of your available session.
- **Schedule End Time** (`schedule_end_time`, text, optional): Enter the end time of your available session.
- **Specialties** (`specialties`, select_one, optional): Select any specialties you have, such as languages or expertise areas.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your preferred location for training sessions.
- **Location Preferences** (`location_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any location preferences you have, such as a preferred city or region.
- **Trainer ID** (`trainer_id`, number, optional): Enter your trainer ID.
- **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Describe your availability, such as your working hours or days off.
- **Conflict** (`conflict`, select_multiple, optional): Select any conflicts you have, such as scheduling conflicts with other trainers.
- **Conflicts** (`conflicts`, select_multiple, optional): Select any conflicts you have, such as scheduling conflicts with other sessions.
