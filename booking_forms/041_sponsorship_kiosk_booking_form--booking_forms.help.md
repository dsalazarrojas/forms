# Sponsorship Kiosk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand the purpose and requirements of this sponsorship kiosk booking form.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your organization's details in the "Organization Details" field.
2. Select one or more preferred kiosk locations from the list in the "Kiosk Location" field.
3. Choose a specific date for your booking in the "Booking Date" field.
4. Select a specific time for your booking in the "Booking Time" field.
5. Write a clear and concise terms of agreement in the "Terms Agreement" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details (required)**: This field is where you will provide information about your organization. Please enter your organization's name and contact details.
* **Kiosk Location (required, multiple selection)**: Here, you can select one or more preferred locations where you would like the kiosk to be set up. Please choose one or more locations from the given options.
* **Booking Date (required)**: Please enter the date when you would like the kiosk to be set up. Ensure that the date is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Booking Time (required)**: Select a specific time when you would like the kiosk to be set up. Be sure to select a time that is suitable for your event or purpose.
* **Terms Agreement (required)**: In this field, please write a clear and concise statement about the terms of agreement for the kiosk setup. This is a critical field, so please take your time to ensure you provide the correct information.
