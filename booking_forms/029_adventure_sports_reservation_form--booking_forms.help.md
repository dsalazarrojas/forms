# Adventure Sports Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to book adventure sports activities. It collects customer and activity information to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with \* (asterisk) as you go along the form.
2. Select the activity type from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date and preferred time for the activity.
4. Indicate how many people will participate and provide their ages.
5. Select a payment method and confirm the deposit amount.
6. Confirm the cancellation policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal full name as per your identification documents\*
* **Email Address**: A valid email address for booking confirmation\*
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number\*
* **Number of Participants**: How many people will be participating in the activity
* **Ages of Participants**: Provide ages of all participants separated by commas, for example, "12, 35, 45"
* **Activity Type**: Select the adventure sport activity you want to book from the dropdown menu
* **Activity Date**: Choose the date for the activity\*
* **Activity Time**: Choose the preferred time for the activity\*
* **Activity Duration**: Select the duration of the activity from the dropdown menu\*
* **Location Preference**: If you have a preferred location, provide it here (optional)
* **Experience Level**: Select your experience level with the activity from the dropdown menu\*
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements or accommodations, provide them here (optional)
* **Equipment Rental**: Select if you need to rent equipment\*
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the reservation\*
* **Deposit Amount**: Provide the amount of deposit required for the reservation\*
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Confirm you acknowledge and agree to the cancellation policy\*
