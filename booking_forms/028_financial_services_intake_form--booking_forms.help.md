# Financial Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Services Intake Form is used to collect information from individuals or families seeking financial assistance. This information helps our organization understand their needs and provide tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully and completely
- Provide clear and accurate information
- You can save and revisit your progress at any time if you need to

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact information`, text): Please provide your name, phone number, email address, or other contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact method`, select_one): Choose how you prefer to be contacted: Home, Email, Phone, In person, or Other (please specify)
* **Services Needed** (`Services needed`, select_multiple): Select the services you are seeking, such as Financial assistance, Credit counseling, Budgeting, Housing assistance, Employment assistance, or Other (please specify)
* **Financial History** (`Financial history`, note): Please provide a brief description of your current financial situation
* **Income** (`Income`, number): Please enter your monthly income
* **Expenses** (`Expenses`, number): Please enter your monthly expenses
* **Assets** (`Assets`, number): Please enter your total assets (e.g., savings, investments, properties)
* **Financial Goals** (`Financial goals`, text): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals
* **Financial Obligations** (`Financial obligations`, text): List any financial obligations you have, such as debts or financial responsibilities
* **Financial Awareness** (`Financial awareness`, select_multiple): Rate your understanding of personal finance: Low, Medium, High
* **Financial Services Sought** (`Financial services sought`, select_multiple): Select the financial services you are looking for, such as Financial assistance, Credit counseling, Budgeting, Housing assistance, Employment assistance, or Other
* **Financial Services Provided** (`Financial services provided`, select_multiple): Select the financial services you have received from our organization, such as Financial assistance, Credit counseling, Budgeting, Housing assistance, Employment assistance, or Other
* **Follow-up Contact** (`Follow-up contact`, date): Please schedule a follow-up contact date and time to review your progress
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up date`, date): Choose a date for the follow-up contact
