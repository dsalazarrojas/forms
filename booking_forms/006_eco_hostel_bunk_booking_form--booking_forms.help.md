# Eco Hostel Bunk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Hostel Bunk Booking Form is designed to collect information from customers to process their booking requests for a bunk in the eco-friendly hostel. This form is essential for ensuring that the hostel can accommodate your needs and provide an excellent experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing your booking information on the first page.
2. On the second page, please provide your payment details.
3. On the third page, enter your guest information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Information (booking_form_page_1_label)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the details of your booking, including dates and times for your stay.
* **Payment Information (payment_info_label)** (`text`, `optional`): Please provide your payment details to complete the booking process. This may include payment method and any other relevant payment information.
* **Guest Information (guest_info_label)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the details of your guests, including name, contact information, and any special requests you may have.
