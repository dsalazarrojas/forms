# Corporate Housing Lease Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather user information for corporate housing lease bookings. It collects details about the user, accommodation, payment, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user's information, including name, email address, and contact details.
2. Provide details about the accommodation, such as address and room rate.
3. Enter the payment method and payment details.
4. Choose the checkin and checkout dates and times.
5. Select the booking status and any relevant messages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Contact Details
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose a payment method (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal).
* **Accommodation ID** (`accommodation_id`, text, required): Enter accommodation ID.

### Other Details
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, text, required): Enter accommodation details.
* **Room Rate** (`room_rate`, text, required): Enter room rate.

### Date and Time
* **Checkin** (`checkin`, time, required): Select checkin date and time.
* **Checkout** (`checkout`, time, required): Select checkout date and time.
* **Booking Dates** (`booking_dates`, date, required): Select booking dates.

### Booking Status
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, text, required): Select booking status.
* **Booking Status Message** (`booking_status_message`, text, required): Enter booking status message.
* **Signature Message** (`signature_message`, text, required): Enter signature message.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name or message.

Note: All required fields are marked with `*`.
