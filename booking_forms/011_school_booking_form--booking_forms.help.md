# School Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect booking requests for school facilities such as the gym, pool, or school itself. Fill out this form to request a booking for a specific date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the date you would like to make your booking on.
2. Select the type of facility you would like to book.
3. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
4. Specify the start and end times of your booking.
5. Optionally, enter a message with any additional details about your booking request.
6. Confirm whether you are booking an appointment or not (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Date (1)**: Enter the date you would like to make your booking on. This should be in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yy.
* **Facility (2)**: Choose the type of facility you would like to book (e.g., School, Gym, Pool).
* **Start Time (3)**: Enter the start time of your booking. This should be in the format of 12:00 or 14:00.
* **End Time (4)**: Enter the end time of your booking. This should be in the format of 18:00 or 20:00.
* **Name (5)**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in your booking confirmation.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your booking.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Message (8)**: If you have any additional details or requests for your booking, enter them here.
* **Facility Booking (9)**: Select 'School' to book the school itself, 'Gym' to book the gym, or 'Pool' to book the pool.
* **Appointment Booking (10)**: Confirm whether you are booking an appointment or not. If you are booking an appointment, select 'True', otherwise select 'False'.
