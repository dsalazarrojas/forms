# Food Festival Vendor Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Festival Vendor Booking Form is used to book food vendors for a festival.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the festival for which you are booking the vendor.
2. **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the vendor.
3. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, false): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the vendor's contact.
4. **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, false): Select the type of vendor you are booking (e.g. food truck, food cart, etc.).
5. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter the email address of the person handling the vendor's contact.
6. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of the person handling the vendor's contact.
7. **Festival Dates** (`festival_dates`, select_multiple, false): Select the dates of the festival for which the vendor will be providing services.
8. **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, note, false): Enter any additional information about the vendor that you would like to include (e.g. special requests, etc.).
9. **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, false): Enter any additional information about the festival or vendor that you would like to include.
10. **Vendor Vehicles** (`vendor_vehicles`, select_multiple, false): Select the types of vehicles that the vendor will bring to the festival (e.g. food truck, etc.).
11. **Festival Setup** (`festival_setup`, date, false): Enter the date and time when the vendor will start setting up for the festival.
12. **Festival Teardown** (`festival_teardown`, time, false): Enter the date and time when the vendor will start tearing down after the festival.
13. **Vendor Setup** (`vendor_setup`, time, false): Enter the date and time when the vendor will start setting up for the festival.
14. **Vendor Teardown** (`vendor_teardown`, time, false): Enter the date and time when the vendor will start tearing down after the festival.
15. **Vendor Setup Details** (`vendor_setup_details`, note, false): Enter any additional details about the vendor's setup.
16. **Vendor Teardown Details** (`vendor_teardown_details`, note, false): Enter any additional details about the vendor’s teardown.
17. **Food Types** (`vendor_food_types`, select_multiple, false): Select the types of food and beverages that the vendor will be providing.
18. **Services** (`vendor_services`, select_multiple, false): Select the services that the vendor will be offering (e.g. delivery, etc.).
