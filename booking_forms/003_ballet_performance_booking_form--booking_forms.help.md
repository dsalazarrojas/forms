# Ballet Performance Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking ballet performance tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the performance you would like to attend and your preferred date and time.
3. Choose your seat selection, including the number of tickets and ticket type.
4. Specify any accessibility requirements and special requests.
5. Select your payment method and any special occasion or occasion for the booking.
6. Optional: Provide a mailing address for delivery or specify if you would like to purchase a souvenir program or gift wrap tickets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name**: Enter the name of the person making the booking.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for booking confirmation and ticket delivery.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for urgent updates.
* **Select Performance**: Choose the ballet performance you would like to attend.
* **Performance Date**: Select your preferred date for the performance.
* **Performance Time**: Choose the matinee or evening show time.
* **Seating Section**: Select your preferred seating area.
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you need.
* **Ticket Type**: Choose the type of ticket you want, including regular or discounted pricing.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Specify any special accessibility needs, such as wheelchair accessible seating.
* **Parking Required**: If you need parking at the venue.
* **Program Booklet**: Check if you would like to purchase a souvenir program.
* **Gift Wrap Tickets**: If this is a gift, do you want special packaging?
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for your tickets.
* **Special Occasion**: If you are celebrating something special.
* **How Did You Hear About This Performance**: Specify the marketing source that brought you here.
* **Mailing Address**: Provide your address for ticket delivery if not electronic.
* **Special Requests**: Any additional comments or requirements.
* **Parking Required**: If you need parking at the venue.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your mailing address for ticket delivery if not electronic.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional comments or requirements.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* Be specific when selecting your performance, seat, and date.
* If you have any special requests, please provide additional information.
* Double-check your contact and mailing addresses for accuracy.
* Review your order before submitting.
* If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact us.
