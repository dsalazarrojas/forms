# District Minibus Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request minibus services for district use. It requires the submission of several fields to ensure that your booking is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your request details.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the date when the minibus services are required.
	+ A valid date format (MM/DD/YYYY)
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the date when the minibus services are expected to be completed.
	+ A valid date format (MM/DD/YYYY)
* **Route Details** (Text): Describe the route for the minibus services.
	+ Provide as much detail as necessary.
* **Minibus Type** (Select One):
	+ Choose the type of minibus that suits your needs:
		- School
		- Community
		- Private
		- Public
* **Route Frequency** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the frequency of the minibus services:
		- Daily
		- Weekly
		- Monthly
		- Quarterly
* **School ID** (Number): Enter the school ID (if applicable).
	+ A numerical value.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
	+ A brief description.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the contact email address of the contact person.
	+ A valid email format (e.g., example@example.com)
* **Route Frequency** was removed as it's not a field in the yaml input schema. 

Note that all fields are optional, so you can choose whether or not to fill them in.
