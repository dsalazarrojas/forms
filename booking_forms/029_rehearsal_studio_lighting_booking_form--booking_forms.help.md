# Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form is designed to collect booking information for events that require custom lighting setup. This form will help you book a rehearsal studio and specify your lighting needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Booking Information** page with details about your event, such as the event name, date, and time.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** on the **Contact Information** page, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Specify your **Lighting Options** on the **Lighting Options** page, describing the type of lighting you need, such as color scheme or number of fixtures.
4. Add any **Additional Information** on the **Additional Information** page, such as special requests or technical requirements.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Booking Information (Page 1)
* **Booking Information** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your event, including the name, date, and time.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Contact Information** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your name, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you.

### Lighting Options (Page 3)
* **Lighting Options** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the lighting setup you require, including the type of lighting, color scheme, or number of fixtures.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **Additional Information** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Share any special requests, technical requirements, or additional information that might help us prepare for your event.

### Submission (Page 5)
