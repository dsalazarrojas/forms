# Library Study Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a study room at the library for a specific time period. Please fill out the form with your details to make a reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date you'd like to book the study room for in the "Booking Date" field.
2. Choose the start and end time of your reservation in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
3. Select the study room group where you'd like to book your room (Main Library 1, Main Library 2, or Quiet Room).
4. Select one or more room numbers within the chosen group.
5. Enter your name for contact purposes.
6. Provide your email address.
7. Optionally, you can provide a phone number.
8. If desired, you can include a message for your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`): Choose the date you'd like to book the study room for. This field is not mandatory.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Select the start time of your reservation.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Select the end time of your reservation.
* **Study Room** (`study_room`): Choose the study room group where you'd like to book your room (Main Library 1, Main Library 2, or Quiet Room).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`): Select one or more room numbers within the chosen group.
* **Your Name** (`name`): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Optionally, you can provide your phone number.
* **Reservation Message** (`reservation_message`): If desired, you can include a message for your reservation.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your reservation request.

## Tips
Please ensure that you choose a date and time that does not conflict with other bookings. Also, be considerate of other users and clean up after yourself when using the study room.
