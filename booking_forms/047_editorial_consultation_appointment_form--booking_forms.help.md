# Editorial Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for an editorial consultation. Please fill out the form to schedule a consultation and provide the necessary details for our team to prepare for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Choose the date and time for your consultation.
3. Select the location for the consultation.
4. Provide a brief message if you have any specific topics or questions you'd like to discuss.
5. Confirm whether you'd like to book a session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name
* **Name** (`name_field`, text, required): Please enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed during the consultation.

### Email
* **Email** (`email_field`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable our team to contact you and confirm your consultation details.

### Date
* **Date** (`date_field`, date, required): Select the date for your consultation. Please ensure it's a date in the future to allow for adequate preparation.

### Time
* **Time** (`time_field`, time, required): Choose the time slot for your consultation. Please note that our team will confirm the time slot with you before the meeting.

### Location
* **Location** (`location_field`, text, optional): If you have a preferred location, please specify it. Our team will ensure that it's available for the consultation.

### Message
* **Message** (`message_field`, text, optional): If you have any specific topics or questions you'd like to discuss during the consultation, please write them down here. This will help our team prepare accordingly.

### Book Session
* **Book Session** (`book_session`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you'd like to book a session. Please note that availability and details will be confirmed separately.

### Tips
- Please ensure that your email address is valid and accessible.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the message field.
- Our team will contact you to confirm your consultation details once you submit this form.
