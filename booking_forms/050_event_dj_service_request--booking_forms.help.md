# Event DJ Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an event, allowing the DJ service team to best plan and prepare for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
- Click on the form to access its fields.
- Fill out each field as prompted by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date (2)**: Enter the date of the event you are requesting DJ service for.
* **Event Time (3)**: Enter the time of the event you are requesting DJ service for.
* **Venue (4)**: Enter the location of the event, e.g. "The Grand Ballroom" or "The Community Center".
* **DJ Details (5)**: Enter any specific details about the DJ service you require, e.g. music genre or specific equipment needed.
* **Contact Number (6)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your request.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address so we can send you updates about your request.
* **Message (8)**: Enter any additional comments or requests you have for your event.
* **Event Type (9)**: Choose the type of event you are requesting DJ service for (e.g. Wedding, Party, Corporate).
* **Event Size (10)**: Choose the estimated size of your event (e.g. Small, Medium, Large). This will help us plan and prepare for your event.
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: Enter any specific equipment or tools assigned to you for your event.
* **Form ID (12)**: This is an internal field, do not fill this out.
* **Category (13)**: Enter any relevant category or tag for your event.
* **Description (14)**: Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Assigned Tool Name (15)**: Enter the name of the tool or equipment assigned to you for your event.
Please submit the form after filling out all required fields to request your DJ service.
