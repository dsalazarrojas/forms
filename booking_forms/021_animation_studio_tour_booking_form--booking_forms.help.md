# Animation Studio Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors to book a tour at an animation studio. Please fill out the form with accurate details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable tour experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your visitor information in the "Visitor Information" section.
2. Provide details about your group, including its size and type, in the "Group Information" section.
3. Select your preferred tour date and time in the "Tour Date and Time" section.
4. Choose a type of tour that suits your group's interests in the "Tour Type" section.
5. Specify the duration of the tour in the "Tour Duration" section.
6. If you have any special interests or specific departments you'd like to visit, please select them in the "Special Interests" section.
7. If you have any accessibility needs, please select and describe them in the "Accessibility and Special Needs" section.
8. Provide your tour leader or educator's name and contact information in the "Tour Leader Information" section.
9. If you have any additional requests or comments, please add them in the "Additional Requests or Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Please enter your complete name as you'd like it to be displayed for the tour.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for tour confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, text, required): Enter your direct contact number for reminders and updates.
* **School or Organization** (`visitor_organization`, text, optional): If you're visiting with a school or organization, please enter their name.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Type of Group** (`group_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of group you're with (e.g., School Field Trip, University Students, etc.).
* **Age Groups Represented** (`age_group_breakdown`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age ranges of participants in your group.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (`preferred_tour_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the tour from the calendar.
* **Preferred Tour Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select the time slot that works best for your group.
* **Type of Tour** (`tour_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of tour that suits your group's interests (e.g., General Studio Tour, Animation Department Focus, etc.).
* **Preferred Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the tour.
* **Areas of Special Interest** (`special_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select any specific areas of interest for the tour (e.g., Storyboarding Process, Character Animation, etc.).
* **Any Specific Departments to Visit** (`specific_departments`, text, optional): If there are specific departments you'd like to visit, please enter them.
* **Accessibility or Special Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any accessibility needs or special requirements.
* **Additional Details** (`accessibility_notes`, text, optional): Please describe any additional accessibility needs or special requirements.
* **Group Leader or Educator Name** (`tour_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the group.
* **Tour Leader Phone Number** (`tour_leader_phone`, text, optional): Enter the direct contact number for the tour leader.
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or comments for the tour.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and enjoyable tour experience.
