# Invoice Form For Dialycen In Spanish - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for generating an invoice for Dialycen services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide billing information in the "Billing Information" section.
2. Enter contact details, including phone number and email, in the "Billing Address" section.
3. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" section.
4. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" section.
5. Select the invoice date in the "Invoice Date" section.
6. Choose the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" section.
7. Select the payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
8. Enter the total price and payment amount in the "Total Price" and "Payment Amount" sections, respectively.
9. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Billing Information (1)**: Enter the billing name, billing company name, and billing address in this field.
* **Billing Address (2)**: Enter the billing address, including the address line 1, address line 2, city, state, zip, and country in this field.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter the contact phone number of the customer or billing contact.
* **Contact Email (4)**: Enter the contact email of the customer or billing contact.
* **Invoice Date (5)**: Select the date of the invoice, which is usually the date of service rendered or payment due date.
* **Payment Terms (6)**: Select the payment terms agreed upon with the customer, which may include options like "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Payment Method (10)**: Select the payment method, which may include options like "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Note (11)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the invoice or customer interaction.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check the information provided to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, it's best to ask the form creator or relevant stakeholders for clarification.
