# Telescope Reservation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for telescope reservations, allowing staff to efficiently manage and schedule telescope usage. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth reservation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to enter your name, email address, and phone number correctly.
2. **Select a Reservation Date**. Choose a date for your reservation request, ensuring it aligns with the available dates for the chosen telescope.
3. **Choose a Telescope**. Select the desired telescope type and size that suits your needs.
4. **Specify Equipment Preferences**. Select any equipment preferences you may have, such as telescope size (10-inch, 20-inch, 25-inch, 40-inch, 80-inch, or 120-inch).
5. **Select a Telescope Type**. Choose the type of telescope you would like to use (Refractor, Telescope, or Dobsonian).
6. **Specify Observation Time**. Enter the time you plan to use the telescope, ensuring it is within the available time slots.
7. **Specify Duration**. Enter the duration of your observation session, in hours.
8. **Specify Observers**. Enter the number of observers accompanying you.
9. **Enter Location**. Provide the location where you would like to use the telescope.
10. **Add any Additional Notes**. If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them in the notes field.
11. **Submit the form**. Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
