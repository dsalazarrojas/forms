# Spiritual Center Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book a tour at the Spiritual Center. Please fill out the form with your details and we will do our best to accommodate your tour request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose your preferred visit date and start time for the tour.
3. Select the duration of the tour (optional).
4. Choose a payment method (optional).
5. Add any special requests or notes about your tour (optional).
6. Confirm that you would like to visit the Spiritual Center (optional).
7. Select the type of tour you would like to take (optional).
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for tour details and updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for tour confirmations and updates.
* **Visit Date** (`Visit Date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to visit the Spiritual Center.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required): Select the time you would like to start your tour.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required): Select the time you would like to end your tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`Tour Duration`, `text`, required): Enter the estimated duration of your tour.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your tour (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes about your tour (e.g. accessibility requirements).
* **Confirm Visit** (`Confirm Visit`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm that you would like to visit the Spiritual Center.
* **Tour Type** (`Tour Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of tour you would like to take (Guided, Self-Guided, or Combination).
* **Special Request** (`Special Request`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes about your tour (e.g. dietary requirements).
* **Tour Capacity** (`Tour Capacity`, `text`, optional): Enter the estimated capacity of your tour group.
* **Special Request 2** (`Special Request 2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional special requests or notes about your tour (e.g. language preferences).
