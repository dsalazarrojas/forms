# monthly_appointment_scheduling_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help schedule a monthly appointment. It guides users to select the correct month and year, choose a date range, and pick time slots for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select a month and year from the "Select a Month and Year" dropdown menu.
2.  Choose a date range for the appointment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Choose a Date Range" field.
3.  Select your preferred time slots for the appointment in the "Time Slots" field.
4.  If required, enter the start time and end time for the appointment in the respective "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Select a Month and Year** (<name>: <type: select_one>, <required>): Select the month and year in which the appointment is to be held.
*   **Choose a Date Range** (<name>: <type: select_multiple>, <required>): Choose the start and end dates for the appointment.
*   **Time Slots** (<name>: <type: select_multiple>, <required>): Choose your preferred time slot for the appointment.
*   **Start Time** (<name>: <type: time, <required>): Enter the start time of the appointment.
*   **End Time** (<name>: <type: time, <required>): Enter the end time of the appointment.

## Tips

*   Be sure to double-check the form fields for errors before submission.
*   If a field is marked as required, ensure it is filled before submitting the form.
*   Review your form selections for accuracy and consistency before submission.
