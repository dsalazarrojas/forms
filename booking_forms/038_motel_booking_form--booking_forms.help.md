# Motel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a room or suite at a Motel. Please complete the form with your personal and accommodation preferences to ensure a smooth and comfortable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your basic information and accommodation preferences.
2. Choose the booking type that best suits your needs.
3. Select the type(s) of accommodation you would like to book.
4. Specify the number of rooms and adults you would like to book.
5. Add any children you would like to include in your booking.
6. Select your check-in and check-out dates.
7. Optionally, provide any comments or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Basic Information (1)**: Provide your basic contact information.
- **Booking Type (2)**: Select between "Hotel Stay", "Room Service", "Breakfast Incl", or "Bed & Breakfast" to specify your preferences.
- **Accommodation (3)**: Choose the type(s) of accommodation you would like to book, such as Single Room, Double Room, Family Room, or Suite.
- **Number of Rooms (4)**: Specify the number of rooms you would like to book.
- **Number of Adults (5)**: Enter the number of adults that will be staying in the booked rooms.
- **Number of Children (6)**: Enter the number of children that will be staying in the booked rooms.
- **Check In (7)**: Select the date you would like to check in.
- **Check Out (8)**: Select the date you would like to check out.
- **Comments (9)**: Optionally, provide any comments or special requests you may have for your stay.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the comments section.
- If you need to book multiple rooms, please specify the number of rooms in the "Number of Rooms" field.
