# Appointment Link Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about upcoming meetings, including meeting organizer details, meeting title, description, scheduled date and time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Meeting Organizer Information**: 
	* This section is not required, but if you're filling out the form as the meeting organizer, please provide your name and email address.
2. **Organizer Name**: 
	* Provide your name as the meeting organizer.
3. **Email Address**: 
	* Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and follow-up.
4. **Company or Organization**: 
	* If applicable, provide the name of your company or organization.
5. **Meeting Details**: 
	* This section is not required, but if you'd like to provide more context about the meeting, you can do so here.
6. **Meeting Title or Topic**: 
	* Enter the title or topic of the meeting.
7. **Meeting Description**: 
	* Provide a brief description of what will be discussed during the meeting.
8. **Scheduled Date and Time**: 
	* Select the date and time of the meeting using the calendar picker.
9. **Timezone**: 
	* If you're in a different timezone, please enter the relevant timezone.
10. **Expected Duration**: 
	* Choose the expected duration of the meeting from the provided options (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, or Other).
11. **Virtual Meeting Platform**: 
	* Select the platform where the meeting will take place (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, WebEx, or Other).
12. **Meeting Link or URL**: 
	* Enter the meeting link or URL.
13. **Meeting Password (if required)**: 
	* If the meeting requires a password, enter it here.
14. **Participant Information**: 
	* This section is not required, but if you'd like to provide details about the participants, you can do so here.
15. **Number of Expected Participants**: 
	* If you know the number of participants, enter it here.
16. **List of Expected Participants**: 
	* If you'd like to provide a list of participants, separate their names and email addresses with commas.
17. **Access Requirements**: 
	* If there are any special access requirements, such as registration or a waiting room, select the relevant option.
18. **Additional Information or Notes**: 
	* If you'd like to provide any additional context or notes about the meeting, enter it here.

## Tips
* Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure that the meeting details are up-to-date and consistent with your actual plans.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the meeting organizers.
