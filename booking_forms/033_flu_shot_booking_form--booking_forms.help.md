# Flu Shot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to book flu shots with our clinic. Fill out the form to schedule your appointment and provide essential contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To book your flu shot, please complete the form with the following steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Choose a **Date** for your appointment using the date picker.
4. Select a **Time** for your appointment using the time picker.
5. Select your preferred **Contact Method** to reach out to you (Phone, Email, or Mail).
6. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field, if you selected Phone as your contact method.
7. Enter your **Email** in the text field, if you selected Email as your contact method.
8. Select a **Appointment Date** for your flu shot using the date picker.
9. Choose a **Appointment Time** for your flu shot using the time picker.
10. Enter your **Address** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you selected Phone as your contact method, enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you selected Email as your contact method, enter your email address.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Select a date for your flu shot appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select a time for your flu shot appointment.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
