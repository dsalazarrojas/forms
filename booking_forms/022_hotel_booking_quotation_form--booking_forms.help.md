# Hotel Booking Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in booking a room at a hotel and would like to receive a quotation. It is a tool for the customer to provide their details and preferences, which will then be used by the hotel staff to create a personalized quotation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your name and contact information in the "User Input" field.
* Choose your arrival and departure dates from the "Dates" field.
* Provide any additional guest information in the "Guest Info" field.
* Check "Special Requests" box to indicate if you have any special requests.
* Choose your accommodation preferences (e.g. single, double, suite) from the "Accommodation Preferences" field.
* Select your payment terms (e.g. net or vat inclusive) from the "Payment Terms" field.
* If you have any other requirements, please select "Other" in the "Special Requests" field.
* Fill out your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
* Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your form to the hotel staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text, optional): Please fill out this field with your name and contact information.
* **Dates** (select multiple, optional): Choose your arrival and departure dates from the dropdown menu.
* **Guest Info** (text, optional): Provide any additional guest information that may be relevant to your booking.
* **Special Requests** (select one, required): Check this box if you have any special requests.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (select multiple, required): Choose the type of accommodation you are interested in booking.
* **Payment Terms** (select multiple, required): Select how you would like to pay for your booking (e.g. net or vat inclusive).
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Fill out this field with your contact information.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Click this button to send your form to the hotel staff.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the hotel staff for assistance.
* The hotel staff will use the information you provide to create a personalized quotation for your booking.
