# Itinerary Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Itinerary Assistance Form is designed to collect information about a trip, including trip details, participant information, accommodations, and travel-related data. This form is intended for users who plan or have already taken a trip and need to provide details for travel agencies or tour operators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide trip details, such as trip duration, date, and time.
2. List the trip participants and their corresponding names.
3. Select your preferred accommodations from the provided options.
4. Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the tour guide or travel agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Trip Details**
*   **trip_details**: A text field for providing a brief description of the trip. Please enter a short summary of your trip, including the destination, activities, or any notable events.

### 2. **Trip Participants**
*   **trip_participants**: A multiple-choice field for selecting the number of participants on your trip.
	+ Choose the number of participants on your trip (e.g., 1, 2, 3).

### 3. **Participant Names**
*   **participant_names**: A text field for listing the names of your trip participants.

### 4. **Accommodations**
*   **accommodations**: A single-choice field for selecting your preferred accommodation type.
	+ Choose your preferred accommodation type (e.g., Accommodation 1, Accommodation 2, Accommodation 3).

### 5. **Accommodation Name**
*   **accommodation_name**: A text field for entering the specific name of your accommodation, if applicable.

### 6. **Accommodation Details**
*   **accommodation_details**: A text field for providing any additional details about your accommodation.

### 7. **Travel Duration**
*   **travel_duration**: A numeric field for entering the length of your trip.
	+ Enter the duration of your trip in hours.

### 8. **Travel Date**
*   **travel_date**: A date field for selecting the date of your trip.
	+ Choose the date of your trip using the calendar picker.

### 9. **Travel Time**
*   **travel_time**: A time field for selecting the time of your trip.
	+ Choose the time of your trip using the clock picker.

### 10. **Tour Guide Name**
*   **tour_guide_name**: A text field for entering the name of your tour guide, if applicable.

### 11. **Tour Guide Email**
*   **tour_guide_email**: An email field for entering the contact information of your tour guide, if applicable.

### 12. **Tour Guide Phone**
*   **tour_guide_phone**: A text field for entering the phone number of your tour guide, if applicable.

### 13. **Additional Information**
*   **additional_info**: A note field for providing any additional information that might be helpful for the tour guide or travel agency.

### 14. **Submit**
*   **submit**: A single-choice field for saving or submitting your form.
	+ Choose to save the form for later or submit it for processing.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid errors.
*   Use the calendar and clock pickers for date and time fields to ensure accuracy.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing additional information, as it will help the tour guide or travel agency better understand your trip requirements.

This help guide aims to assist users in completing the Itinerary Assistance Form effectively and efficiently. Please review and adjust the content according to your organization's specific requirements and use case.
