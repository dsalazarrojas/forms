# Freight Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a freight shipment appointment with the required details of the customer and the shipment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
2. **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer.
3. **Shipment Date**: Enter the date on which the shipment will occur.
4. **Shipment Time**: Enter the time on which the shipment will occur.
5. **Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which the delivery is expected.
6. **Delivery Time**: Enter the time on which the delivery is expected.
7. **Shipping Service**: Select the type of shipping service required (Express, Standard).
8. **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where the shipment will be picked up.
9. **Delivery Location**: Enter the location where the shipment will be delivered.
10. **Freight Details**: Enter any additional information about the shipment.
11. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the customer.
12. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
13. **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This is a required field where you need to enter your name or the name of the person booking the shipment.
* **Customer Address**: This is a text field for you to enter your address.
* **Shipment Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to schedule the shipment.
* **Shipment Time**: Enter the time on which you would like to schedule the shipment.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which you expect to receive your shipment.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time on which you expect to receive your shipment.
* **Shipping Service**: Select the shipping service type that suits your needs. Options are Express or Standard.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where the shipment will be picked up from.
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the location where the shipment will be delivered to.
* **Freight Details**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about the shipment.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for us to reach you for any queries.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the shipment.
* **Special Instructions**: This is a text field where you can enter any special instructions or details that we need to know for a smooth shipment.

## Tips

- Be as accurate as possible when entering the details to avoid any delays or miscommunication.
- Make sure to enter your contact information so we can get back to you with any updates.
- If you need to make any changes to your shipment details, kindly contact us so we can update the records accordingly.
- This form is designed to be used for freight shipment booking purposes only. If you have any other requests, please contact us directly.
