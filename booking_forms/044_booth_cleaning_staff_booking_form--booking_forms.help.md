# Booth Cleaning Staff Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event organizers to book and manage cleaning staff for booth-related events. It collects necessary information for efficient scheduling and coordination of cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide event details: Enter the name of the event, date, and booth number or location.
3. Specify cleaning requirements: Select the cleaning services needed (e.g., daily, end-of-day, deep cleaning, etc.) and the approximate booth size.
4. Choose the preferred cleaning time: Select a time for the cleaning staff to start their work.
5. Estimate the duration: Choose how long the cleaning will take.
6. Determine the number of staff needed: Enter the number of cleaning staff required.
7. Specify any special equipment needed: Choose from available options if any specialized equipment is required.
8. Enter access requirements: If any special access is needed (e.g., keys, access codes).
9. Choose the billing type: Select how you would like to be charged (e.g., per hour, flat fee, etc.).
10. Add special instructions: Enter any additional details or instructions for the cleaning staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name**: Your full name.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Event Name**: Name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Date of the event.
* **Booth Number or Location**: Booth ID or location.
* **Booth Size**: Approximate booth dimensions. Select from 10x10, 10x20, 20x20, 20x30, or Other size.
* **Cleaning Service Type**: Select the cleaning services needed (e.g., daily, end-of-day, deep cleaning, etc.).
* **Preferred Cleaning Time**: When the cleaning staff should start their work.
* **Estimated Duration**: How long the cleaning will take (Select from 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, 3 hours, or More than 3 hours).
* **Number of Cleaning Staff Needed**: Enter the number of cleaning staff required.
* **Special Equipment Needed**: If any specialized equipment is required.
* **Access Requirements**: If any special access is needed (e.g., keys, access codes).
* **Billing Type**: Select how you would like to be charged (e.g., per hour, flat fee, etc.).
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional details or instructions for the cleaning staff.
