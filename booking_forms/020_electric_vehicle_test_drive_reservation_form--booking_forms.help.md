# electric_vehicle_test_drive_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who want to schedule a test drive for an electric vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "test_drive_details" section with any notes or comments about the test drive you would like to schedule.
2. In the "customer_info" section, enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
3. In the "vehicle_info" section, enter the make and model of the electric vehicle you would like to test drive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets test drive information** (`test_drive_details`, text, not required): This is where you can enter any additional information about the test drive you would like to schedule.
* **customer information** (`customer_info`, text, not required): Please enter your name and any other contact information you would like to be contacted with.
* **vehicle information** (`vehicle_info`, text, not required): Enter the make and model of the electric vehicle you would like to test drive.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the form administrator or contact us directly.
