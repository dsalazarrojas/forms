# Luxury Auto Test Drive Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather necessary information to arrange a test drive for you. Please fill in the required fields to schedule a test drive at our dealership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the test drive details, including your preferred test drive date and time.
2. Enter your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
3. Select your preferred contact method (Drive-in, Email, Phone, or Text).
4. Enter the vehicle details, including year, make, and model.
5. Choose the test drive type (New, Certified Pre-Owned, Used, or Lease).
6. Set the test drive status and priority.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the test drive.
8. Select the test drive type (Drive-in, Email, Phone, or Text) and test drive lead source.
9. Review and submit the form to schedule your test drive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Drive Details** (<name>: `test_drive_details`): 
    Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the test drive.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): 
    Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`): 
    Select one of the following contact methods:
    + Drive-in, Drive-in: Meet us at the dealership.
    + Email, Email: We'll contact you via email.
    + Phone, Phone: We'll contact you via phone call.
    + Text, Text: We'll contact you via text message.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`): 
    Enter the year, make, and model of the vehicle you would like to test drive.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): 
    Enter the year of the vehicle you would like to test drive.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): 
    Enter the make of the vehicle you would like to test drive.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): 
    Enter the model of the vehicle you would like to test drive.
* **Test Drive Duration** (`test_drive_duration`): 
    Set the desired length of the test drive in hours.
* **Test Drive Distance** (`test_drive_distance`): 
    Set the desired distance of the test drive in miles.
* **Test Drive Comments** (`test_drive_comments`): 
    Enter any additional comments or notes about the test drive.
* **Drive-in Details** (`drive_in_details`): 
    Enter any additional details about the drive-in test drive.
* **Email** (`email`): 
    Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): 
    Enter your phone number.
* **Test Drive Confirmation** (`test_drive_confirmation`): 
    Select a date and time for the test drive confirmation.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): 
    Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Dealer Notes** (`dealer_notes`): 
    Enter any additional notes or comments from the dealer.
* **Test Drive Status** (`test_drive_status`): 
    Select the current status of the test drive:
    + Pending, Pending: The test drive is pending confirmation.
    + Scheduled, Scheduled: The test drive is scheduled.
    + Completed, Completed: The test drive is complete.
    + Cancelled, Cancelled: The test drive is cancelled.
* **Test Drive Type** (`test_drive_type`): 
    Select the type of test drive:
    + New, New: A new vehicle test drive.
    + Certified Pre-Owned, Certified Pre-Owned: A certified pre-owned vehicle test drive.
    + Used, Used: A used vehicle test drive.
    + Lease, Lease: A lease test drive.
* **Test Drive Priority** (`test_drive_priority`): 
    Set the priority of the test drive:
    + High, High: High priority test drive.
    + Medium, Medium: Medium priority test drive.
    + Low, Low: Low priority test drive.
* **Test Drive Lead** (`test_drive_lead`): 
    Enter any additional details about the test drive lead.
* **Test Drive Source** (`test_drive_source`): 
    Enter any additional details about the test drive source.
* **Test Drive Status** (`test_drive_status`): 
    Select the current status of the test drive:
    + Pending, Pending: The test drive is pending confirmation.
    + Scheduled, Scheduled: The test drive is scheduled.
    + Completed, Completed: The test drive is complete.
    + Cancelled, Cancelled: The test drive is cancelled.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we can process your test drive reservation correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
