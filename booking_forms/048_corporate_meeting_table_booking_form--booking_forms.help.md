# corporate_meeting_table_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate meeting booking form is used to collect essential details about meetings, including attendee information and seating requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "Meeting Details Form" section, which asks for the meeting's title, and any other relevant details.
2. Proceed to the "Attendee Information Form" section, which requires you to provide attendee's names and any other required information.
3. Finally, in the "Seating Preferences" section, you can specify your seating preferences, such as the type of seat or any special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details Form (Meeting Details Form, text, required: false)**: This section is where you enter the meeting's title and any other relevant details.
* **Attendee Information Form (Attendee Information Form, text, required: true)**: This is a required field where you provide attendee's names and any other required information.
* **Seating Preferences (Seating Preferences, text, required: false)**: This optional section allows you to specify your seating preferences, including seat type or any special requirements.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requirements, specify them in the "Seating Preferences" section.
