# Game Prototype Testing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Prototype Testing Booking Form is used to book a testing session for a game prototype, allowing testers to provide information about their interest in testing, testing details, and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Form** (booking_form, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in booking a testing session.
* **Tester Details** (tester_details, text, optional): Provide details about yourself as a tester.
* **Testing Session** (testing_session, select_one, optional): Select your preferred testing session options.
* **Testing Duration** (testing_duration, text, optional): Specify the duration of the testing session.
* **Testing Date** (testing_date, date, optional): Choose a date for the testing session.
* **Testing Time** (testing_time, time, optional): Choose a time for the testing session.
* **Session Details** (testing_session_details, note, optional): Provide session details.
* **Tester Name** (tester_name, text, optional): Fill in your name.
* **Tester Email** (tester_email, email, optional): Fill in your email address.
* **Tester Phone** (tester_phone, text, optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Agreement** (tester_agreement, select_one, optional): Confirm your agreement to test.
* **Tester Occupation** (tester_occupation, text, optional): Fill in your occupation.
* **Tester Education** (tester_education, text, optional): Fill in your education.
* **Tester Experience** (tester_experience, text, optional): Fill in your experience.
* **Handedness** (tester_handedness, text, optional): Fill in your handedness.
* **Language** (tester_language, text, optional): Fill in your language.
* **Gender** (tester_gender, text, optional): Fill in your gender.
* **Age** (tester_age, number, optional): Fill in your age.
* **Availability** (tester_availability, text, optional): Fill in your availability.
* **Form Submit** (form_submit, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' to submit the form.
* **Submit Button** (submit_button, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to submit the button.
* **Game Session** (game_session, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' to book a game session.
* **Testing Schedule** (testing_schedule, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to schedule the testing session.
