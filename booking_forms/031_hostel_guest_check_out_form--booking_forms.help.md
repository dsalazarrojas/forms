# Prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prophets form is used to collect information about new payments for booking-related transactions. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure proper payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Check Out Date: Enter the date you plan to check out from the room.
	* Room Number: Enter the room number for the booking.
	* Payment Method: Select a payment method (Online Payment, Cash, or Other).
	* Total Amount: Enter the total amount for the payment.
	* Payment Status: Indicate if the payment is already made or still outstanding.
2. Fill out the non-required fields if necessary:
	* Email: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number for additional information.
	* Comments: Add any additional comments about the payment or booking.
3. Review and submit the form to complete the payment process.
