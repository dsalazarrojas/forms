# Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect event details and booking requests for hotels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** page with the requested information.
2. On the **General Information** page, provide your **Guest Name** and **Email**.
3. Choose the **Hotel Name** and **Room Type** for your event.
4. Select the **Arrival Date** and **Departure Date** for your event.
5. Fill in any **Special Requests** or **Other Event Details** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the person(s) attending the event.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person to contact you about the event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your event.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like to end your event.
* **Hotel Name**: Select the hotel where you would like to book your event.
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room you need.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number (if known).
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you will arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you will depart from the hotel.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests you may have for your event.
* **Other Event Details**: Provide any additional information about your event.
