# legalConsultationBookingForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legalConsultationBookingForm is a form used to book a legal consultation with a law firm. It gathers information necessary for scheduling a meeting or session with a lawyer or paralegal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including law firm name, client name, consultation date, client email, and consultation type.
2. Select the consultation duration from the provided options (15, 30, or 60 minutes).
3. If desired, add any additional information about the consultation.
4. Confirm that you are submitting the form by typing "Submitted by" with your name or initials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firm Name** (`law_firm_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the law firm.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the client or representative.
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to have the consultation.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the client or representative.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the client or representative.
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more types of consultations: Lawyer Consultation, Paralegal Consultation, or Other.
* **Consultation Duration** (`consultation_duration`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the duration of the consultation: 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If needed, add any other details about the consultation.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, note, optional): Confirm you are submitting the form by typing "Submitted by <your name/initials>".

Tips: 

* Make sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the law firm directly.
* The law firm will review and confirm your consultation booking after receiving this form submission.
