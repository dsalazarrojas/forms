# Outdoor Event Space Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide information about their outdoor events and submit a request for booking an event space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are planning (e.g., Outdoor event, Indoor event, Conference, etc.)
2. Enter the start and end times of your event.
3. Choose a date for your event.
4. Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
5. Specify the number of people attending your event.
6. Describe your event in brief.
7. Provide the contact person's name and email address.
8. Enter the contact person's phone number.
9. Confirm if the event space is available.
10. Set the event space booking fee (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: Please select the type of event you are planning (e.g., Outdoor event, Indoor event, Conference, etc.). This field helps us understand the nature of your event.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event. You can use the time selector to choose the hour and minute you plan to begin your event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your event. Like the start time, you can use the time selector to choose the hour and minute you plan to end your event.
* **Event Date**: Choose a date for your event. Please ensure that the date you select is available for the event space.
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Space Number**: Enter the number of people attending your event.
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe your event. This will help us understand your event's requirements and details.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Space Availability**: Confirm if the event space is available for your event.
* **Event Space Booking Fee**: If your event requires a booking fee, enter the amount. Please note that this field is not required if your event does not need a booking fee.
* **Event Terms**: Please provide any additional terms or requirements for your event.
