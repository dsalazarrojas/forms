# dj_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "dj_booking_form" is a comprehensive form designed to collect necessary details for DJ bookings. It is intended to facilitate accurate and thorough communication between clients and DJs, ensuring a smooth booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, as marked by *.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information, especially for client and event details.
3. Proofread your response before submitting to ensure accuracy and completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dj_details (Text)** (`dj_details`, text, not required): This field is for you to provide any relevant details about the DJ booking, such as the event type or specific requirements.
* **event_details (Text)** (`event_details`, text, not required): Enter details about the event, including date, time, and location.
* **contact_info (Text)** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Fill in your contact information, so we can reach you about the booking.
* **payment_terms (Text)** (`payment_terms`, text, not required): Mention any payment terms or requirements you have for the booking.
* **booking_agent (Text)** (`booking_agent`, text, not required): If there's someone else involved in the booking process, provide their details.
* **client_info (Text)** (`client_info`, text, not required): Include any information about the client, such as their contact or event requirements.
* **dj_equipment (Text)** (`dj_equipment`, text, not required): Specify if there are any special DJ equipment requirements for the event.
* **special_requests (Text)** (`special_requests`, text, not required): Mention any additional requests or specific requirements you have for the DJ.
* **terms (Text)** (`terms`, text, not required): Clarify any terms or agreements related to the booking.
* **confirm_booking (Text)** (`confirm_booking`, text, not required): Confirm your booking details before submission.
* **payment_info (Text)** (`payment_info`, text, not required): Provide payment information, such as methods or requirements.
* **booking_status (Text)** (`booking_status`, text, not required): Indicate the current booking status, if you'd like to track it.
* **form_submitted_by (Text)** (`form_submitted_by`, text, not required): If you're submitting on behalf of someone else, mention their name or details.
* **form_submitted (Text)** (`form_submitted`, text, not required): Confirm that you've submitted the form accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language to ensure easy understanding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the form creators or your contacts.
