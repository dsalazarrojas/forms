# seasonal_marina_reservation_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit a seasonal marina reservation application. The form gathers information about the user's vessel, their preferred dates for reservation, and other details related to their request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `seasonal_marina_reservation_form`
	* `vessel_details`
	* `preferred_dates`
	* `contact_person`
	* `contact_info`
	* `marina_details`
2. Answer questions about your vessel:
	* `vessel_type`
	* `vessel_size`
	* `vessel_length`
	* `vessel_width`
	* `vessel_draft`
	* `vessel_material`
	* `vessel_deck_material`
	* `vessel_superstructure_material`
3. Indicate your vessel usage and preferences:
	* `vessel_use`
	* `vessel_type_of_vessel`
	* `vessel_hull_material`
	* `season`
	* `berth_request_type`
	* `slip_length`
	* `slip_width`
	* `slip_type`
	* `slip_size`
