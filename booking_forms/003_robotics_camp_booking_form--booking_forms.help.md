# Robotics Camp Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a session at a robotics camp for children. It collects personal details about the child, their parents or guardians, and other relevant information to ensure the camp can provide a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is booking the camp session for the child.
2. Enter the child's email address so they can receive important updates and information about the camp.
3. Enter the child's phone number so the camp can contact them in case of an emergency.
4. Enter the child's name.
5. Enter the child's birthdate, which will be used to determine their age and eligibility for certain activities.
6. Select the arrival date for the child at the camp.
7. Enter the program duration (e.g., how many days the child will be at the camp).
8. Choose the type of program the child will be participating in (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
9. Select any additional session preferences for the child (e.g., specific activities or interests).
10. Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
11. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
12. Provide any medical conditions or allergies the child has.
13. List any special needs the child may have that the camp staff should be aware of.
14. Provide any relevant medical history.
15. Review all the information entered and submit the form to complete the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is booking the camp session for the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the child's email address so they can receive important updates and information about the camp.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the child's phone number so the camp can contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Childs Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the child's name.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): Enter the child's birthdate, which will be used to determine their age and eligibility for certain activities.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Select the arrival date for the child at the camp.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the program the child will be participating in (e.g., how many days).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of program the child will be participating in (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional session preferences for the child (e.g., specific activities or interests).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, required): Provide any medical conditions or allergies the child has.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required): List any special needs the child may have that the camp staff should be aware of.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Provide any relevant medical allergies the child has.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): Provide any relevant medical history for the child.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the camp session.
* Review all the entered information carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp staff for assistance.
