# Live Interview Stage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Interview Stage Booking Form is designed to collect information from candidates and hiring managers to schedule live interviews. It's an essential step in the hiring process, ensuring that all necessary details are exchanged and appointments are set correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to identify yourself and allow us to communicate with you.
2. Enter your phone number for any necessary follow-up communication.
3. Choose a date and time for the live interview session.
4. Select the desired interview stage, which will be used to determine the interview type and duration.
5. Confirm the booking by pressing the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself to the hiring manager.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your email address for us to communicate with you and send you updates about the booking.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any necessary follow-up communication.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the live interview session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time slot within the chosen date for the live interview.
* **Interview Stage** (`interview_stage`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the desired interview stage to determine the interview type and duration.
  * Option 1: Choose this stage for in-depth questions and detailed answers.
  * Option 2: Select this stage for a brief and time-sensitive discussion.
  * Option 3: Select this stage for an extensive review and preparation.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your booking by pressing this button.
* **Confirmation 1** (`confirmation_1`, `time`, required/optional): Please note that this field is not used in the live interview process, so you can skip it.
* **Confirmation 2** (`confirmation_2`, `time`, required/optional): This field is also not used in the live interview process, so it's not necessary to fill out.
* **Confirmation 3** (`confirmation_3`, `time`, required/optional): Similarly, this field is not used and can be skipped.
* **Confirmation 4** (`confirmation_4`, `time`, required/optional): Another field not used in the live interview process, so you can leave it blank.
* **Confirmation 5** (`confirmation_5`, `time`, required/optional): This field is also not utilized, so it's not required.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be submitted correctly.
