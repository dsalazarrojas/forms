# digital_detox_mountain_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Detox Mountain Booking Form" is used to collect information from guests for the Digital Detox Mountain Retreat program. This form is required for the booking process, ensuring we have accurate contact and accommodation details for each guest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Guest Name, which is your name as a guest.
2. Enter your Arrival Date in the format of DD/MM/YY (e.g., 12/03/2024).
3. Enter your Returning Date in the format of DD/MM/YY (e.g., 15/03/2024).
4. Enter your Email address, which is your primary contact information.
5. Enter your Phone number, which is required for communication during the program.
6. Select your accommodation preferences from the list of available options.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your booking.
8. Select any additional accommodation preferences (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as a guest.
- **Arrival Date** (`date_arrival`, `date`, required: true): Enter your planned arrival date in the format of DD/MM/YY.
- **Returning Date** (`date_returning`, `date`, required: true): Enter your planned returning date in the format of DD/MM/YY.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your primary email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more options for your accommodation preferences.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.
- **Accommodation Preferences 2** (`accommodation_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select additional accommodation preferences (if applicable).
- **Accommodation Preferences 3** (`accommodation_preferences_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select further accommodation preferences (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for Arrival Date and Returning Date.
- Double-check your accommodation preferences for accuracy.
- Use a valid email address that you check regularly for communication updates.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the Notes field.
- Please note that the Digital Detox Mountain Retreat team will reach out to you for further discussion on your accommodation preferences if necessary.
