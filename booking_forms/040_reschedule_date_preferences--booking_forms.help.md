# Reschedule Date Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reschedule Date Preferences" form is used to collect information about requests to change scheduled dates, appointments, or events. This form is used to gather details about the requested changes, including the new date, reason for the request, and preferred dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise reschedule request in the "Reschedule Request" field.
2. Enter the new date you would like to schedule the event on in the "New Date" field.
3. Explain the reason for your request in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter any preferred dates for the reschedule in the "Preferred Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reschedule Request** (text, optional): Enter a clear and concise description of why you are requesting to reschedule the event.
- **New Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule the event on.
- **Reason** (text, optional): Explain the reason for your request to reschedule the event.
- **Preferred Date** (date, optional): Enter any preferred dates for the reschedule.
