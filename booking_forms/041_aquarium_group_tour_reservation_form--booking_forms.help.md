# Aquarium Group Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Aquarium Group Tour Reservation Form" is designed to streamline the planning and reservation process for school groups, organizations, and other groups visiting the aquarium. Please fill out the form with accurate information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Fill out each section according to your group's needs.
- Review all fields carefully to ensure that your group's preferences and requirements are captured.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Group Contact Information
* **Group Contact Information** (<label>: Group Contact Information, <type>: note, required: false)
    This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes. Please fill out if your group has a contact person or organization.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (<label>: Contact Name, <type>: text, required: true)
    This field is required to identify the name of the contact person for your group.

### Organization or School
* **Organization or School** (<label>: Organization or School, <type>: text, required: true)
    This field is required to identify the organization or school your group represents.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (<label>: Email Address, <type>: email, required: true)
    This field is required to facilitate communication and confirm your group's information.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (<label>: Phone Number, <type>: text, required: true)
    This field is required for emergency contacts and last-minute communication.

### Tour Details
* **Tour Details** (<label>: Tour Details, <type>: note, required: false)
    This field is optional for any additional tour requests or instructions.

### Preferred Tour Date
* **Preferred Tour Date** (<label>: Preferred Tour Date, <type>: date, required: true)
    This field is required to schedule the tour on your preferred date.

### Preferred Tour Time
* **Preferred Tour Time** (<label>: Preferred Tour Time, <type>: select_one, required: true)
    Choose a time that suits your group's schedule from the available options: 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM.

### Number of Participants
* **Number of Participants** (<label>: Number of Participants, <type>: number, required: true)
    This field is required to schedule the tour according to your group size.

### Age Range of Participants
* **Age Range of Participants** (<label>: Age Range of Participants, <type>: text, required: false)
    This field is optional but helps us tailor the tour experience according to your group's age range.

### Type of Tour
* **Type of Tour** (<label>: Type of Tour, <type>: select_one, required: true)
    Choose the type of tour that suits your group's interests: Standard Tour, Interactive Tour, Educational Program, Behind-the-Scenes, Custom Tour.

### Special Interests or Focus Areas
* **Special Interests or Focus Areas** (<label>: Special Interests or Focus Areas, <type>: text, required: false)
    This field is optional but helps us tailor the tour according to your group's interests or focus areas.

### Dietary Restrictions or Allergies
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (<label>: Dietary Restrictions or Allergies, <type>: text, required: false)
    This field is optional but helps us ensure that we cater to your group's dietary needs.

### Accessibility or Accommodation Needs
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (<label>: Accessibility or Accommodation Needs, <type>: text, required: false)
    This field is optional but helps us ensure that we accommodate your group's accessibility needs.

### Tour Cost Estimate Acknowledged
* **Tour Cost Estimate Acknowledged** (<label>: Tour Cost Estimate Acknowledged, <type>: select_one, required: true)
    Please acknowledge that you have received the tour cost estimate to proceed with the booking.
