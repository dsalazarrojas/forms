# Chiropractic Treatment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Treatment Booking Form is a crucial step in the booking process for patients seeking chiropractic care. It gathers essential information about the patient's preferences and needs, ensuring that the healthcare provider can schedule an appointment that suits their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as per the patient's information.
2. Enter the contact email that you'd like to use for booking confirmations.
3. Choose a preferred date for your appointment from the available options.
4. Select a preferred time for your appointment from the available options.
5. Choose the type of session you would like to book (e.g., New Patient Exam, Follow-up, Treatment Session, Consultation).
6. (Optional) If you have an insurance provider, please enter the details.
7. Enter your contact phone number for booking confirmations.
8. If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, please specify in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as per the patient's information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email you'd like to use for booking confirmations.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, Date, Required): Choose a date for your appointment from the available options.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, Time, Required): Select a time for your appointment from the available options.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of session you would like to book.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, Text, Optional): If you have an insurance provider, please enter the details.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number for booking confirmations.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, please specify.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled in carefully and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
- Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
- This form is an essential part of the booking process, and its accuracy is crucial for providing the best possible care and service to the patient.
