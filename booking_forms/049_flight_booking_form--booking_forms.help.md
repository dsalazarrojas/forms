# Flight Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flight Booking Form is a tool used to record customer flight information for booking purposes. This form helps to gather essential details from the customer, such as their name, email, contact number, flight number, departure and return times, which will be used to process their flight booking request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the correct format (e.g., example@email.com). This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Customer Phone** number in the text field provided. This is an optional field.
4. Enter your **Flight Number** as provided by the airline or travel agency. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Departure Time** in the correct 24-hour time format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM). This is a required field.
6. Enter your **Return Time** in the correct 24-hour time format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM). This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A text field where you can enter your full name as it appears on your ID or travel documents.
* **Customer Email**: A field to enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates or further information.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for any queries or changes.
* **Flight Number**: A field to enter the unique flight number provided by the airline or travel agency for identification purposes.
* **Departure Time**: A field where you can enter the departure time of your flight in 24-hour format.
* **Return Time**: A field where you can enter the return time of your flight in 24-hour format, if applicable.
