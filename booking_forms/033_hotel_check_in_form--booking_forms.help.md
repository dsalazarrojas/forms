# Hotel Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect check-in and check-out dates, and contact information from hotel customers to facilitate a smooth check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your check-in date in the "Check In Date" field.
2. Enter your check-out date in the "Check Out Date" field.
3. Fill in your name in the "Client Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Client Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check into the hotel.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for hotel communication.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for hotel communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the dates you enter before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the "Client Name" field.
* Our staff will contact you using the email address you provide.
* Please note that this form is for hotel customers and is intended for check-in purposes only.
