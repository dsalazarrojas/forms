# Booking Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Booking Amendment Form is used to make changes to a booking. This can include changing dates, room types, number of guests, and adding or removing services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your customer information: full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your original booking reference number and dates (check-in and check-out).
3. Select the type of amendment you are requesting (change dates, room type, etc.).
4. If applicable, select the additional services you would like to add or remove.
5. Acknowledge any potential price differences and cancellation policies.
6. Select your preferred contact method and urgency of the amendment.
7. Provide any additional comments or special requests.
8. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your booking confirmation.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address for communication about your booking.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for communication about your booking.
* **Booking Reference Number**: The number assigned to your original booking.
* **Original Check-In Date**: The date you originally checked in.
* **Original Check-Out Date**: The date you originally checked out.
* **Type of Amendment**: Select the type of change you are making (change dates, room type, etc.).
* **New Check-In Date**: If you are changing your check-in date, enter the new date.
* **New Check-Out Date**: If you are changing your check-out date, enter the new date.
* **New Room Type**: If you are changing your room type, select the new type (standard, deluxe, suite, etc.).
* **New Number of Guests**: If you are changing the number of guests, enter the updated count.
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you would like to add (airport transfer, breakfast, etc.).
* **Services to Remove**: Select any services you would like to remove (airport transfer, breakfast, etc.).
* **Reason for Change**: Explain why you are making this change.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests or requirements.
* **Price Difference Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that there may be price differences due to changes.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that you understand the cancellation policies.
* **I Agree to Amendment Terms**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the amendment.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (email, phone call, text message).
* **Urgency**: Select the level of urgency for this amendment.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Amendment Request Date**: Date of this amendment request.
