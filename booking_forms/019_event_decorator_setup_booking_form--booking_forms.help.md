# Event Decorator Setup Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners set up their events with event decorators. It gathers information about the event, including the event's details and the planner's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your event.
2. Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any additional questions or confirmations.
3. Optionally, you can add any additional notes about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Decorator Setup**: Please provide the name of this form to be displayed to event decorators.
* **Event Planners Name**: Enter your name, this will be used to contact you regarding your event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, this will be used for us to contact you with any questions or confirmations.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, this will be used for us to contact you with any urgent matters.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of your event (HH:MM).
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event (HH:MM).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your event (HH:MM).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Event Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with the event decorators.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any) to ensure that we can process your event setup.
* Double-check the event details for accuracy to avoid any confusion or mistakes.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
