# Showreel Screening Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book a time slot for a showreel screening. Please fill out this form to schedule a time slot for your screening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the booking form with your details.
2. Select a date for your booking from the "Booking Date" field.
3. Select a time for your booking from the "Booking Time" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
7. Add any booking notes in the "Booking Notes" field, if necessary.
8. Select the status of your booking from the "Booking Status" field.
9. Review your entries to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Form**: Please fill out this text area with your basic details such as name, contact information, and other relevant booking information.
* **Booking Date**: Select a date for your booking from the available options.
* **Booking Time**: Select a time for your booking from the available options.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Booking Notes**: Add any additional information about your booking, if necessary.
* **Booking Status**: Select the status of your booking from the available options: Available, Not Available, or Pending.
