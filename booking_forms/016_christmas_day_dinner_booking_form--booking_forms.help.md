## Christmas Day Dinner Booking Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is for booking a Christmas Day dinner at our restaurant. It will help us gather the necessary information to accommodate your needs and preferences.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your diner information (first name, last name, and email address).
2. Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
3. Enter your reservation details (party size, seating preference, and preferred arrival time).
4. If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please let us know.
5. If there are children attending, enter the number and ages.
6. Let us know if you have any special occasions or celebrations.
7. Finally, confirm your payment method and confirm the booking.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, `first_name`, **Required**): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, `last_name`, **Required**): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email, `email`, **Required**): Your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Text, `phone`, **Required**): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Party Size (number of people)** (Number, `party_size`, **Required**): The number of people in your party.
* **Seating Preference** (Select One, `seating_preference`, **Optional**): Choose your preferred seating arrangement: Window seating, Regular seating, Private dining room, or No preference.
* **Preferred Arrival Time** (Time, `arrival_time`, **Required**): The time you would like to arrive for your dinner.
* **Estimated Departure Time** (Time, `estimated_departure`, **Optional**): The estimated time you will depart after your dinner.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Select Multiple, `dietary_needs`, **Optional**): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Nut allergies, Shellfish allergies, Other allergies).
* **Special Dietary Requirements** (Text, `special_requirements`, **Optional**): If you have any special dietary requirements, please provide details here.
* **Children Attending** (Note, `children_section`, **Optional**): If you are bringing children, let us know.
* **Number of Children** (Number, `number_of_children`, **Optional**): Enter the number of children.
* **Children Ages** (Text, `children_ages`, **Optional**): List the ages of the children in a comma-separated list.
* **Highchair Needed** (Select One, `highchair_needed`, **Optional**): If you need a high chair for a child, select 'True'.
* **Is this a special celebration?** (Select One, `celebration_type`, **Optional**): If this is a special occasion, choose one of the options: Birthday, Anniversary, Family reunion, Corporate gathering, Just a holiday dinner.
* **Details About Your Celebration** (Text, `celebrations_details`, **Optional**): Provide any details about your celebration.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Select One, `payment_method`, **Required**): Choose how you would like to pay (Credit card, Debit card, Cash, Bank transfer).
* **I understand this is a booking confirmation** (Select One, `confirmation`, **Required**): Confirm that this is a booking confirmation.
