# Seaside Creative Studio Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seaside Creative Studio Booking Form is designed for clients to book sessions with the studio for photography or graphic design services. It gathers essential information for the studio to manage bookings, payments, and client relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Session Details page with the necessary information about your session.
2. Provide your contact information on the Contact Info page.
3. Enter your project details on the Project Details page.
4. Select the reservation date and session time on the Reservation Date and Session Time pages.
5. Choose your preferred payment method on the Payment Method page.
6. Select the status of your booking on the Booking Status page.
7. Choose the type of reservation on the Reservation Type page.
8. Select the type of session on the Session Type page.
9. If necessary, add additional information about the client on the Client Info and Client Type pages.
10. Add any additional notes or messages on the Booking Note and Client Message pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Details
- **Session Details** (1, text, required): Enter the details of your session, such as the type of session or any specific requirements.
### Contact Info
- **Contact Info** (2, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
### Project Details
- **Project Details** (3, text, required): Enter the details of your project, including any relevant information about the project scope and requirements.
### Reservation Date
- **Reservation Date** (4, date, required): Select the date you would like to book your session.
### Session Time
- **Session Time** (5, time, required): Select the time of your session.
### Reservation Time
- **Reservation Time** (6, time, required): This field is likely a typo and not necessary. Please ignore it.
### Session Duration
- **Session Duration** (7, number, required): Enter the duration of your session.
### Client Info
- **Client Info** (8, text, required): Provide any additional information about the client.
### Project Info
- **Project Info** (9, text, required): Enter any additional information about your project.
### Payment Details
- **Payment Details** (10, text, required): Enter any payment details, such as the method of payment and amount.
### Message
- **Message** (11, text, required): Leave any additional message or instructions for the studio.
### Email
- **Email** (12, email, required): Enter your email address.
### Phone
- **Phone** (13, text, required): Enter your phone number.
### Client Name
- **Client Name** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the client.
### Reservation Notes
- **Reservation Notes** (15, text, required): Add any additional notes about your reservation.
### Session Notes
- **Session Notes** (16, text, required): Add any notes about your session.
### Session Status
- **Session Status** (17, select_one, required): Select the status of your session, such as In Progress, Pending, or Finished.
### Payment Method
- **Payment Method** (18, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
### Booking Status
- **Booking Status** (19, select_one, required): Select the status of your booking, such as Confirmed, Declined, or Pending.
### Reservation Type
- **Reservation Type** (20, select_one, required): Select the type of reservation, such as Private Session or Group Session.
### Session Type
- **Session Type** (21, select_multiple, required): Select the type of session, such as Photography or Graphic Design.
### Client Type
- **Client Type** (22, select_multiple, required): Select the type of client, such as Studio Owner or Client.
### Booking Note
- **Booking Note** (23, note, required): Add any additional notes about your booking.
### Client Message
- **Client Message** (24, note, required): Add any additional message to the client.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your booking is processed correctly.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the studio for support.
