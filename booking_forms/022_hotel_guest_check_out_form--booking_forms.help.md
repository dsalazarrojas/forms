# Hotel Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from hotel guests at the time of check-out. It is intended to be completed by the guests themselves, providing a convenient way to collect their feedback, comments, and any additional information about their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about yourself as a guest.
2. Provide details about your stay, including any notable events or incidents.
3. Share any feedback or comments you may have about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details you would like to share about yourself.
* **Stay Info** (`stay_info`, text, required: false): Describe your stay, including any notable events or incidents that occurred during your visit.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and feedback about your stay, including any positive or negative experiences you had.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* You can type in as much or as little information as you feel necessary.
* If you have any attachments or documents that you would like to submit, you can do so at the end of the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to hotel staff.
