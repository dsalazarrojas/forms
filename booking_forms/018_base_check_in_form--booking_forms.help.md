# Base Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Base Check In Form is a critical component of the event management process, designed to collect essential information from attendees. It helps event organizers provide a tailored experience and ensures effective communication with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This is the email address we will use for event communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please provide your best contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, optional): If you work or study at an institution, please provide its name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your position or role in your organization.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, select_one, required): Please select the name of the event you are attending (e.g., Annual Conference, Workshop Series, etc.).
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Choose your registration category (e.g., General Admission, VIP, etc.).
* **Registration Confirmation Number** (`confirmation_number`, text, optional): If you received a registration confirmation email, please provide the number.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Please select today's date.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, required): Select the current time.
* **Is This Your First Time?** (`first_time_attendee`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is your first time attending the event.
* **Sessions or Events Attending** (`events_attending`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the sessions or events you plan to attend.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your meal preference for catering purposes.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any food allergies or restrictions, please mention them.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): If you want a free event T-shirt, please select your size.
* **Name for Badge** (`badge_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as it should appear on your name badge.
* **Interested in Networking Opportunities** (`networking_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are interested in networking opportunities.
* **Plan to Visit Exhibitor Booths** (`exhibitor_booth_visit`, select_one, required): Tell us if you plan to visit sponsor booths.
* **Will to Provide Session Feedback** (`session_feedback_interest`, select_one, required): Let us know if you are willing to provide feedback after sessions.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Choose if you consent to being included in event photos and videos.
* **Share Contact Info with Exhibitors** (`contact_sharing`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are willing to share your contact information with exhibitors.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any special needs, please let us know.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, required): Tell us if you need parking information.
* **How Did You Get Here?** (`transportation_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you are traveling to the event.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Please select where you heard about the event.
* **What Are Your Expectations?** (`expectations`, text, optional): Share what you hope to gain from the event.
* **Special Requests or Questions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or questions, please mention them.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* Ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please mention them in the special requests field.
* If you are attending with a group, please ensure each group member fills out their own form accurately and completely.
