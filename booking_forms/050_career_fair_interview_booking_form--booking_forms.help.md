# Career Fair Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help candidates book interviews with potential employers during career fairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your resume or business card.
2.  Enter the company that you are applying to for an interview.
3.  Enter the job title or position you applied for.
4.  Select your preferred interview date.
5.  Select your preferred interview time slot (if available).
6.  Choose the format for your interview (in person, virtual, or phone).
7.  If you have a resume, enter the URL or attach it for the interviewer's reference.
8.  Enter your contact email address for easy communication and confirmation.
9.  Add any additional notes or information that may be relevant for the interviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate name**: Your full name as it appears on your resume or business card.
    \*   Enter your full name, including your first and last names.
*   **Company booking interview**: The employer conducting the interview.
    \*   Enter the company name that you are applying to.
*   **Position applied for**: The job title or position you applied for.
    \*   Enter the job title or position you are applying for.
*   **Preferred interview date**: Select your preferred date for the interview.
    \*   Choose a date that you are available for the interview.
*   **Preferred interview time**: Select your preferred time slot for the interview (if available).
    \*   Choose a time that suits you best.
*   **Interview format**: Choose the format for your interview (in person, virtual, or phone).
    \*   Select the format that you are most comfortable with.
*   **Resume link or attachment**: A URL or note about your resume.
    \*   If you have a resume, enter the URL or attach it for easy reference.
*   **Contact email**: Contact email for confirmation and communication.
    \*   Enter your email address for easy communication and confirmation.
*   **Additional notes**: Any other relevant information about yourself or the interview.
    \*   Enter any other relevant information that may be helpful for the interviewer.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the interviewer or contact us for help.
*   Please note that this form is intended for booking interviews during career fairs. If you have any other questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
