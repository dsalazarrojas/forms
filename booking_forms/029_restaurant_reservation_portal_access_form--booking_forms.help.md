# Restaurant Reservation Portal Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the reservation of a restaurant, allowing users to provide essential details for their visit. It gathers information about the date of the reservation, the size of the party, the desired time, contact details, phone number, and email. This form helps the restaurant staff to efficiently manage reservations and provide better service to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired **Reservation Date** by choosing a date from the calendar or typing it manually in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Choose the number of people in your **Party Size** by entering a whole number.
3. Select the desired **Time** for your reservation.
4. Enter your **Contact Details**, including any relevant information about yourself or your party.
5. Provide your **Phone Number** so we can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
6. Enter your **E-mail** address so we can communicate with you regarding your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: The date you would like to make your reservation. This field is used to schedule your visit.
* **Party Size**: The number of people in your party. Please enter the total number of guests, including children.
* **Time**: The time you would like to make your reservation. Please select from the available time slots provided.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any relevant information about yourself or your party. This could include any special requests or notes.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number, so we can reach you if there are any last-minute changes or updates.
* **E-mail**: Your email address, where we can contact you about your reservation and any special requests. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the date, time, and party size are accurate to avoid any conflicts with other reservations.
* Enter valid email and phone number formats for easy communication.
