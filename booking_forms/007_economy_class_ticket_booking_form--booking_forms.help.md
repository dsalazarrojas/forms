# Economy Class Ticket Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economy Class Ticket Booking Form is a tool designed for passengers to book economy class tickets for flights. By providing the necessary information in this form, passengers can successfully reserve a seat on their chosen flight.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Start by selecting your preferred **Flight Number** (page 1, field **Flight Number**).
- Choose your **Passenger Type** (page 1, field **Passenger Type**).
- Enter your **Travel Date** (page 1, field **Travel Date**).
- Select your **Travel Time** (page 1, field **Travel Time**).
- Provide your **Passenger Name** (page 1, field **Passenger Name**), **Passenger Email** (page 1, field **Passenger Email**), and **Passenger Phone** (page 1, field **Passenger Phone**).
- Choose your **Payment Method** (page 1, field **Payment Method**).
- Confirm your booking by selecting **Confirm Booking** (page 1, field **Confirm Booking**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Flight Number** (page 1, field **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, required/optional):
The flight number is the identifier for the flight you're booking. Enter the flight number as per the given options.
- **Passenger Type** (page 1, field **Passenger Type** (`passenger_type`, select_one, required/optional):
Select your passenger type from the options: **Adult**, **Child**, and **Infant**.
- **Travel Date** (page 1, field **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required/optional):
Enter your travel date in the specified format.
- **Travel Time** (page 1, field **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required/optional):
Select your travel time from the available options.
- **Passenger Name** (page 1, field **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, required/optional):
Enter your name as it should appear on your ticket.
- **Passenger Email** (page 1, field **Passenger Email** (`passenger_email`, email, required/optional):
Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Passenger Phone** (page 1, field **Passenger Phone** (`passenger_phone`, text, required/optional):
Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
- **Payment Method** (page 1, field **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional):
Choose between **Pay via Card** and **Pay via Bank**.
- **Confirm Booking** (page 1, field **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, select_one, required/optional):
Confirm your booking by selecting **True** or **False**.

## Tips
- Double-check your information to avoid errors.
- Ensure you have entered the correct flight number.
- Verify your email address and phone number are correct.
- Review your booking details before confirming.
