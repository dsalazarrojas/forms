# Vacation Rental Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your vacation rental preferences to help us provide you with the best possible accommodation suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide an accurate and helpful response, please fill out this form with the following information:

1. **Home Location**: Enter the location where you are interested in staying.
2. **Preferred Date**: Select your preferred date of stay.
3. **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people in your group.
4. **Budget**: Enter your budget for the stay.
5. **Preferred Type**: Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (Apartment, House, Cottage, or Other).
6. **Amenities**: Select the amenities you would like to have available (Wi-Fi, Pool, Gym, Parking, etc.).
7. **Preferred Area**: Indicate if you prefer a specific area (Yes or No).
8. **Home Type**: Choose if you prefer a home with a certain number of bedrooms (Yes or No).
9. **Home Bedrooms**: Select the number of bedrooms you require.
10. **Home Bathrooms**: Choose the number of bathrooms you need (Yes or No).
11. **Home Description**: Provide a short description of the type of accommodation you are looking for.
12. **Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can get back to you with the accommodation suggestions.
