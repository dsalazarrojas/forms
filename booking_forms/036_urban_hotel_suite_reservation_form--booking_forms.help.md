# urban_hotel_suite_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urban hotel suite reservation form is designed to collect guest information and booking details for the hotel's suite reservation system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as per your ID card in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the arrival and departure dates for your stay in the "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" fields.
5. Choose the type of room you prefer from the "Room Type" field.
6. Select the method of payment from the "Payment Method" field.
7. Provide any additional payment information in the "Payment Info" field if required.
8. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
9. Select the cancellation policy from the "Cancellation Policy" field.
10. Review the "Terms and Conditions" field for any additional terms.
11. Provide any additional guest information in the "Guest Info" field.
12. Provide any additional hotel policy information in the "Hotel Policy" field.
13. Enter the source of the booking in the "Booking Source" field.
14. Enter a message for the guest in the "Guest Message" field.
15. Provide any additional booking notes in the "Booking Notes" field.
16. Enter any additional payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
17. Enter any additional cancellation notes in the "Cancellation Notes" field.
18. If necessary, enter any further special requests in the "Special Requests (2)" field.
19. Provide any additional room details in the "Room Details" field.
20. Enter the status of the reservation in the "Reservation Status" field.
21. Enter the payment status in the "Payment Status" field.
22. Enter the total cost of the booking in the "Total Cost" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name (1)**: Enter your name as per your ID card.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Arrival Date (4)**: Select the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date (5)**: Select the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Room Type (6)**: Choose the type of room you prefer (Suite, Deluxe Room, Standard Room).
* **Arrival Time (8)**: Select the time you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Payment Method (9)**: Choose the method of payment (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash).
* **Payment Info (10)**: Provide any additional payment information.
* **Special Requests (11)**: If you have any special requests, enter them here.
* **Cancellation Policy (12)**: Select the cancellation policy you prefer.
* **Terms and Conditions (13)**: Review the terms and conditions for your booking.
* **Guest Info (14)**: Provide any additional guest information.
* **Hotel Policy (15)**: Provide any additional hotel policy information.
* **Booking Source (16)**: Enter the source of the booking.
* **Guest Message (17)**: Enter a message for the guest.
* **Booking Notes (18)**: Provide any additional booking notes.
* **Payment Terms (19)**: Enter any additional payment terms.
* **Cancellation Notes (20)**: Enter any additional cancellation notes.
* **Special Requests (21)**: If you have any further special requests, enter them here.
* **Room Details (22)**: Provide any additional room details.
* **Reservation Status (23)**: Enter the status of the reservation.
* **Payment Status (24)**: Enter the payment status.
* **Total Cost (25)**: Enter the total cost of the booking.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your booking.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the hotel staff for assistance.
