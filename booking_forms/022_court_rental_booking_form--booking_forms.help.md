# Court Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a booking system for users to reserve a court for their activities such as recreation, competition, or practice. Please fill in the form carefully to ensure accurate booking information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol. 
- Select the desired court name and type that suits your activity.
- Choose the correct booking duration based on your needs.
- Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
- Confirm the payment method you will use (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
- Update the booking status accordingly after payment is made or canceled.
- Confirm the court status after booking is made or canceled.
