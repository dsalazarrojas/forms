The In Home Cooking Appointment Form is used to book an appointment for in-home cooking services. The form is intended for customers to provide their session details, schedule preferences, and contact information.

Before starting the form, please ensure you have the following:

* You need to book an appointment for in-home cooking services.
* You have all the necessary information to complete the form.
* You have a valid email address.

The form consists of multiple pages, each with specific fields. Please complete each page thoroughly to ensure accurate processing.

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book an appointment for in-home cooking services. This form is designed to collect relevant information from customers to schedule a session at their preferred time and date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the session details you require (Chef Services, Menu Options, or Special Requests).
2.  Choose whether to schedule a session (Yes or No).
3.  Confirm that you have read and understand the details (Yes or No).
4.  Indicate whether you wish to manage the request process (Yes or No).
5.  Enter your contact email address and phone number (if required).
6.  Provide any additional notes or comments you have regarding your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Details** (`session_details`, `select_one`, required): Select the session details you require from the provided options (Chef Services, Menu Options, or Special Requests).
*   **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you wish to schedule a session (Yes or No).
*   **Confirm Details** (`confirm_details`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the details (Yes or No).
*   **Manage Request** (`manage_request`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you wish to manage the request process (Yes or No).
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address. This is a required field.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your phone number (if required).
*   **Notes** (`in_home_cooking_appointment_form_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments you have regarding your appointment.

## Tips

*   Ensure you complete all required fields to ensure accurate processing.
*   Be thorough when filling out the session details and schedule preferences.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   If you have any special requests, please provide them in the session details field.
*   If you wish to manage the request process, please be available to respond to communication from our team.
