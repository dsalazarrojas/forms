# hsvc_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hsvc_reservation_form is designed to collect information from customers about their booking needs for home services. This form aims to ensure that service requests are accurately captured and effectively relayed to the appropriate technicians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **customer name**, which is your name as it will appear in our records.
2. Provide your **contact phone number**, which we use to contact you about your booking.
3. Enter the **service address**, where the service will be performed.
4. Choose a **service date** that suits your schedule.
5. Select a **schedule time** that works best for you.
6. If necessary, provide **technician notes** for the assigned technician.
7. Choose the **service technician**, who will perform the service.
8. Select the **equipment type** (e.g., AC, Heat Pump, Water Heater, or Refrigerator) that requires service.
9. Indicate the **equipment location** within your home or office where the service will take place.
10. Choose the **service type** (e.g., Service 1, Service 2, Service 3, or Service 4) that you require.
11. Select the **schedule type** (e.g., Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3) that suits your needs.
12. Optionally, provide any additional **notes** about your service request.
13. Finally, confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name**: Enter your name as it will appear in our records.
* **Contact phone**: This is your contact number for any follow-up communication or updates.
* **Service address**: Specify the location where the service will be performed.
* **Service date**: Choose a date that works best for you and our technicians.
* **Schedule time**: Select a time that suits your schedule.
* **Technician notes**: If you have specific needs or requirements, provide them here for the assigned technician.
* **Service technician**: Choose the technician who will perform the service.
* **Equipment type**: Select the type of equipment that requires service (e.g., AC, Heat Pump, Water Heater, or Refrigerator).
* **Equipment location**: Indicate the location within your home or office where the service will take place.
* **Service type**: Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Service 1, Service 2, Service 3, or Service 4).
* **Schedule type**: Select the schedule type that best fits your needs (e.g., Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the booking details.
