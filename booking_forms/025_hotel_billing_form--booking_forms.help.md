# Hotel Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Billing Form is used to create a record of hotel billing information for financial tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the **Customer Email** field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
4. Enter the billing address in the **Billing Address** field.
5. Select the services billed from the **Services Billed** dropdown menu.
6. Enter the charges for the services billed in the **Charges** field.
7. Enter the total charges in the **Total Charges** field.
8. Select the date of billing from the calendar.
9. Choose the payment method from the **Payment Method** dropdown menu.
10. Provide any additional notes in the **Note** field.
11. Select whether the form is assigned to a specific user using the **Assigned To** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Hotel Billing Form** (object)
*   **Customer Name** (text)
    +   Enter the customer's name.

    +   Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Phone** (text)
    +   Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Billing Address** (text)
    +   Enter the billing address.
*   **Services Billed** (Select Multiple)

*   **Charges** (number)
    +   Enter the charges for the services billed.
*   **Total Charges** (number)
    +   Enter the total charges.
*   **Date Of Billing** (date)
    +   Select the date of billing from the calendar.
*   **Payment Method** (Select One)
    +   Choose the payment method from the dropdown menu.
*   **Note** (Note)

*   **Assigned To** (Select One)
