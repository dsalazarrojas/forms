# Meeting Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a meeting room for an event or meeting. Please fill out the form to request a meeting room and provide necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select "Yes" if you want to reserve a meeting room, or "No" if you don't.
* Enter your name in the text field.
* Provide your email address in the email field.
* Describe the purpose or topic of the meeting in the description field.
* Select the meeting room(s) you would like to reserve from the list of available options.
* If required, enter a unique reservation ID in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Yes/No (Meeting Room Reservation)** (`meeting_room_reservation_form`, select_one, required: false): 
Select "Yes" if you are reserving a meeting room, or "No" if you are not.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required: false): Describe the purpose or topic of the meeting.
* **Meeting Room** (`meeting_room`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the meeting room(s) you would like to reserve.
* **Reservation ID** (`reservation_id`, number, required: false): If required, enter a unique reservation ID.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Describe the meeting purpose, room requirements, or any other details that would be helpful for the meeting room staff.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the meeting room reservation is for a specific date and time.
* If you need to reserve a room for an extended period of time, please specify this in the description field.
* Make sure to select the correct meeting room(s) that align with your needs.
* If you have any additional requests or requirements, please include them in the description field.
