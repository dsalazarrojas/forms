# Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form is a digital form designed to collect information from sponsors for booking and gifting purposes. Its primary goal is to capture essential details about the sponsor, their preferences, and event-related information, making it easier to manage bookings and ensure a smooth gifting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the sponsor's gift area**: Choose the specific sponsor gift area you are interested in booking.
2. Fill in the required information**: Enter the relevant details in each field, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Review and submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, review your entries for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Sponsor Gift Area** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_1`)
* A text field to select the sponsor's gift area. This is the primary identifier for the booking process. Please choose the correct gift area for your booking.

### **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_2`)
* A text field to capture sponsor details. Enter the sponsor's name, company name, or any other relevant information as required.

### **Sponsor Contact** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_3`)
* A text field to collect sponsor contact information. This could include the sponsor's email, phone number, or any other contact method.

### **Gift Area Information** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_4`)
* A text field for capturing additional gift area information. Use this field to provide any necessary details about the gift area, such as its location or specific requirements.

### **Event Information** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_5`)
* A text field for entering event-related information. Provide details about the event, such as date, time, or location, if applicable.

### **Gift Area Booking** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_6`)
* A text field to book the sponsor gift area. Enter your booking details, including dates, times, or any other relevant information.

### **Confirmation** (`sponsor_gift_area_booking_form_page_7`)
* A text field to confirm your booking. Review your entries and submit the form once you are satisfied with your booking details.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields to complete the form accurately.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Review and confirm your booking details before finalizing the submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your form administrator for assistance.
