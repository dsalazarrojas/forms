# Laser Tag Online Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Tag Online Booking Form is a tool used to collect customer information and preferences for booking purposes. This form allows users to provide their personal details, such as name, email, phone number, and date and time preferences. It also includes options for selecting specific preferences, like date ranges and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the text fields provided.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive booking confirmations and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for any necessary contact or follow-up calls.
4. Select the desired **Date** and **Time** for the booking.
5. Choose the **Select One** option to confirm your preferences.
6. Select **Yes** or **No** for the **Select Multiple** option.
7. Enter the desired **Date Range** and **Time Range** for more specific booking requests.
8. Enter a list of **Emails** for email notifications.
9. Enter a list of **Phone Numbers** for follow-up calls.
10. Enter a list of **Dates** for the booking period.
11. Enter a list of **Times** for the specific time slots.
12. Select **Yes** or **No** for the **Select One List** option.
13. Select **Yes** or **No** for the **Select Multiple List** option.
14. Enter a list of **Dates** for the extended booking period.
15. Enter a list of **Times** for the specific time slots.
16. Enter a list of **Emails** for email notifications.
17. Enter a list of **Phone Numbers** for follow-up calls.
18. Enter a list of **Dates** for the extended booking period.
19. Enter a list of **Times** for the specific time slots.
20. Click the **Submit** button to complete and submit the form.

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive booking confirmations and updates.
* **Phone** (Text): Provide your phone number for any necessary contact or follow-up calls.
* **Date** (Date): Select the desired date for the booking.
* **Time** (Time): Select the desired time for the booking.
* **Booking ID** (Number): This field is not required.
* **Notes** (Note): Leave any additional comments or requests for your booking.
* **Select One** (Select One): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple): Select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Date Range** (Date): Select a date range for the booking.
* **Time Range** (Time): Select a time range for the booking.
* **Email List** (Email): Enter a list of emails for email notifications.
* **Phone List** (Text): Enter a list of phone numbers for follow-up calls.
* **Date List** (Date): Enter a list of dates for the booking period.
* **Time List** (Time): Enter a list of times for the specific time slots.
* **Select One List** (Select One): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Select Multiple List** (Select Multiple): Select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Date Range List** (Date): Enter a list of dates for the extended booking period.

* **Email List List** (Email): This field is not required.

* **Date List List** (Date): This field is not required.
