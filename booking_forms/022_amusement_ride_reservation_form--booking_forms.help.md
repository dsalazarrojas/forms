# Amusement Ride Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Amusement Ride Reservation Form is used to collect information from users for an amusement ride reservation. It is essential to provide accurate and complete details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the guest's full name in the "Guest name" field.
2. Specify the number of people in your group in the "Number of riders" field.
3. Choose the preferred date for the reservation.
4. Select the preferred time slot for the ride.
5. If applicable, provide any special requirements or accessibility needs in the "Special requirements" field.
6. Enter your email address for confirmation and ticketing purposes in the "Contact email" field.
7. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Contact phone" field (optional).
8. Confirm that the waiver has been signed by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Waiver signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter the full name of the guest.
* **Number of riders** (`number_of_riders`, number, required): Specify the number of people in your group.
* **Preferred date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to make the reservation for.
* **Preferred time slot** (`time_slot`, time, required): Choose the time slot you would like to visit.
* **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please let us know.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and ticketing purposes.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your number here.
* **Waiver signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that the waiver has been signed, or "No" if not.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Ensure that you select a valid date and time slot for the reservation.
* If you have any special requirements, please provide them so we can accommodate your needs.
* A valid email address is required for confirmation and ticketing purposes.
* A completed waiver is necessary for all riders.
