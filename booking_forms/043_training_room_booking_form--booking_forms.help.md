# Training Room Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a training room booking for a specific date and purpose. It provides a clear and organized way to submit your request to the facilities or administrative team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all the required information before starting, as some fields are mandatory.
2. Fill out your details and submit the form to initiate the booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Details** (`requester_details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department to ensure that your booking is routed to the correct team.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you wish to book the training room. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the booking. This is the date you expect the room to be returned to.
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees`, number, required): Specify the number of people attending the training session.
* **Equipment Required** (`equipment`, text, optional): If you need any specific equipment or facilities, please list them here.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_one, required): Select either "Approval" or "Decline" to indicate the status of your request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional information or comments you'd like to provide for your booking request.

## Tips

* Ensure you enter accurate dates to avoid conflicts or double bookings.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, contact the facilities or administrative team.
* Please respect other users and keep noise levels down during your session.
* If your equipment request is not listed, you can add it to the "Equipment Required" field.
