# multicultural_event_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about a multicultural event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: (`user_info`), text (not required): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant personal details. You can leave it blank if not required.
* **Event Info**: (`event_info`), text (not required): This field is for providing more details about the event you are booking or attending.
* **Event Details**: (`event_details`), text (not required): Use this field to add any additional information about the event.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your answers.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
