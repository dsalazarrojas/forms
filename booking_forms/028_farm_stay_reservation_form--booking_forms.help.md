# Farm Stay Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from guests to process their farm stay reservation requests. Please fill out the form below with accurate and complete information to ensure smooth processing of your reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your Guest Details.
2. Enter the date of your expected arrival.
3. Select the duration of your stay.
4. Enter the time of your expected arrival.
5. Choose your preferred room type(s).
6. Enter any accommodation details that you would like us to be aware of.
7. If applicable, mention any special requests you have.
8. Enter your contact information.
9. Select your preferred payment method.
10. Confirm the terms of your payment.
11. Review and agree to our Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details** (Text, Required): Please enter your name and any other details that may be useful for us to know about you.
* **Arrival Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the farm in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Duration** (Select One, Required): Choose the number of days you would like to stay.
* **Arrival Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the farm in the format 12:00 AM or 12:00 PM.
* **Room Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Private room
	+ Shared room
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Accommodation Details** (Note, Required): Enter any accommodation details that you would like us to be aware of, such as dietary restrictions or mobility requirements.
* **Special Requests** (Select One, Optional): If you have any special requests, such as requests for a specific room or equipment, please select Yes or No.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Required): Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other contact information that you are willing to share.
* **Farm Name** (Text, Required): Enter the farm name or the farm code if you have one.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional message or information to provide, please enter it here.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Optional): If you have any specific payment terms, such as a payment plan, please enter them here.
* **Terms And Conditions** (Note, Required): Review and agree to our Terms and Conditions before submitting the form.
