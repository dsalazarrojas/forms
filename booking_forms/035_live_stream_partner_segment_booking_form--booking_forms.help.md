# live_stream_partner_segment_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a live stream partner segment for booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Details.
2. Enter your Segment Details.
3. Fill in your Booking Details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This field is intended for users to input their personal information, such as their name, contact number, or email address.
* **Segment Details** (`segment_details`, text, optional): Here, users can provide details about the segment they are interested in or available for booking. This can include information about the segment's availability, duration, or any specific requirements.
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, text, optional): In this field, users can input information related to the booking, such as the booking date and time, or any specific preferences they may have.
