# Therapy Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Booking Form is a tool to help you book and schedule therapy sessions with a professional. Please use this form to provide your details and preferences for a convenient and efficient booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email in the **Email** field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the date for your scheduled session in the **Schedule Date** field.
6. Choose the time for your scheduled session in the **Schedule Time** field.
7. Select the duration of your session in the **Duration** field (in minutes).
8. Select your available time slot in the **Available Time** field.
9. Choose the type of therapy you are interested in from the **Therapy Type** dropdown menu (multiple selections are allowed).
10. Add any additional comments or messages in the **Message** field.
11. If applicable, enter the ID of the tool assigned to you in the **Assigned Tool** field.
12. If applicable, enter the Google Calendar ID for your session in the **Google Calendar ID** field.
13. If you are using Zoom for your session, enter the Zoom Meeting ID in the **Zoom Meeting ID** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your scheduled session.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Choose the time for your scheduled session.
* **Duration** (`schedule_duration`, number, optional): Select the duration of your session in minutes.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, optional): Select your available time slot.
* **Therapy Type** (`therapy_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of therapy you are interested in (multiple selections allowed).
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or messages here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the ID of the tool assigned to you.
* **Google Calendar ID** (`google_calendar_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the Google Calendar ID for your session.
* **Zoom Meeting ID** (`zoom_meeting_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the Zoom Meeting ID for your session.
