# Sustainable Travel Hub Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sustainable Travel Hub Booking Form is designed to collect information from guests who are interested in booking sustainable travel options. This form will help us understand your preferences and needs, ensuring that we can provide the most suitable and sustainable travel experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your stay dates, including check-in and check-out dates.
3. Select your preferred accommodation type from the list of options (hotel, resort, camping, etc.).
4. Indicate your sustainability preferences, including eco-friendly and sustainable options (such as eco-hotels, sustainable resorts, etc.).
5. Add any additional message or notes about your booking.
6. If applicable, provide any assigned tool or reference number.
7. Enter any additional form IDs if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
* **Stay Dates (2)**: Select your arrival and departure dates to ensure we can book your travel experience correctly.
* **Accommodation Type (3)**: Choose from hotel, resort, camping, or other options to specify your preferred accommodation type.
* **Sustainability Preferences (4)**: Indicate your sustainability preferences, such as eco-friendly and sustainable options, to help us tailor your experience.
* **Message (5)**: Add any additional message or notes about your booking, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Assigned Tool (6)**: If you have been assigned a specific tool or reference number, enter it here.
* **Form ID 1 (7)**: Enter any additional form ID 1 (if required).
* **Form ID 2 (8)**: Enter any additional form ID 2 (if required).
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
