# Children Playroom Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Playroom Rental Application is a form used to request rental of the children's playroom for a specific event. It collects information about the applicant, event details, and agreement to rental terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact email as the applicant or representative of the organization requesting the rental.
2. Choose the date and start and end times for the event.
3. Estimate the expected number of children attending the event.
4. Select the type of event (e.g., birthday, play date, educational event, or other).
5. Provide any special requests for equipment, catering, or setup.
6. Confirm your agreement to the rental terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name**: Your name or the name of the person or organization requesting the rental.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for booking confirmation and further communication.
* **Event Date**: Choose the desired date for the event.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Expected Number of Children**: Estimate the number of children attending the event.
* **Party Type**: Select the type of event (e.g., birthday, play date, educational event, or other).
* **Special Requests**: Provide any specific requirements for equipment, catering, or setup.
* **Agree to Rental Terms**: Confirm your understanding and agreement to the rental terms.
