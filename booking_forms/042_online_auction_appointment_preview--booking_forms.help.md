# Online Auction Appointment Preview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Auction Appointment Preview form is used to gather information about the user's preferences and requirements for an online auction appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for each field and select the one that best suits your needs.
2. Select all applicable options for the "Preview Time Slots" field.
3. Fill out the "Contact Details" field with the relevant information.
4. Choose an option for the "Auction Category" field.
5. Select an option for the "Platform Preferences" field.
6. Optionally, you can provide a message in the "Message" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Select an option for the "No Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preview Time Slots** (`preview_time_slots`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable time slots for the online auction appointment. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the contact details that best suit your needs.
* **Auction Category** (`auction_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of the auction.
* **Platform Preferences** (`platform_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your platform preferences.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional message or information about your appointment.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **No Preferences** (`no_preferences`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select this option if you do not have any preferences.
