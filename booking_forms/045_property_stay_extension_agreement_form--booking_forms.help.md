# Property Stay Extension Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Stay Extension Agreement Form is a document used to update and extend the stay of a guest or tenant in a property. This form is essential for accurately recording the new check-out date and any additional charges incurred during the extended stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the original check-out date in the `original_check_out_date` field.
2. Enter the new check-out date in the `new_check_out_date` field.
3. If there are any additional charges to be added or updated, enter them in the `added_charges` field.
4. If the existing charges need to be updated, enter the new charges in the `updated_charges` field.
5. Enter the total amount of the updated charges in the `updated_charges_amount` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Stay Extension Agreement Form 1** (`property_stay_extension_agreement_form_1`, text, optional): This field is a free-form text field where you can enter any additional comments or notes regarding the stay extension.
* **Original Check-Out Date** (`original_check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the original check-out date of the guest or tenant.
* **New Check-Out Date** (`new_check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the new check-out date after the stay has been extended.
* **Updated Check-Out Dates** (`updated_check_out_dates`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information regarding the updated check-out dates.
* **Added Charges** (`added_charges`, text, optional): Enter any additional charges that have been incurred during the extended stay.
* **Updated Charges** (`updated_charges`, text, optional): Enter any updated charges that need to be reflected in the new check-out date.
* **Updated Charges Amount** (`updated_charges_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the updated charges.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct original and new check-out dates to avoid any discrepancies.
* Clearly document any additional charges or updates to ensure accurate records.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
