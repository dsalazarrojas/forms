# Business Seminar Setup Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Seminar Setup Booking Form is used to collect information about a business seminar, including details about the organizer, seminar date and time, venue, and logistical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person or company organizing the seminar in the "Organizer Name" field.
2. Provide the email address of the contact person for the organizer in the "Organizer Email" field.
3. Enter the phone number of the contact person for the organizer in the "Organizer Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the name of the sponsoring organization or company in the "Company or Organization Name" field.
5. Provide the title or topic of the seminar in the "Seminar Title" field.
6. Select the preferred date for the seminar in the "Desired Seminar Date" field.
7. Enter the time the seminar should start in the "Seminar Start Time" field.
8. Enter the time the seminar should end in the "Seminar End Time" field.
9. Enter the estimated number of attendees in the "Expected Number of Attendees" field.
10. Select whether the seminar requires an in-person venue, virtual event, or hybrid format in the "Venue Needed" field.
11. If an in-person venue is chosen, select the preferred room type in the "Room Type Preference" field.
12. Select any necessary equipment or technical requirements in the "AV and Technical Requirements" field.
13. Select whether catering is required in the "Catering Required" field.
14. If catering is required, enter any special dietary or catering needs in the "Dietary or Special Requirements" field.
15. Select whether parking is required in the "Parking Needed" field.
16. Enter any special accessibility or equipment needs in the "Accessibility or Special Needs" field.
17. Enter any specific setup preferences or requirements in the "Setup Preferences or Notes" field.
18. Enter an approximate budget for the seminar in the "Budget Estimate" field.
19. Enter the date of the booking request in the "Booking Request Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, required): Enter the name of the person or company organizing the seminar.
* **Organizer Email** (`organizer_email`, required): Provide the email address of the contact person for the organizer.
* **Organizer Phone Number** (`organizer_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the organizer.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of the sponsoring organization or company.
* **Seminar Title** (`seminar_title`, required): Enter the title or topic of the seminar.
* **Desired Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, required): Select the preferred date for the seminar.
* **Seminar Start Time** (`seminar_start_time`, required): Enter the time the seminar should start.
* **Seminar End Time** (`seminar_end_time`, required): Enter the time the seminar should end.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, required): Enter the estimated number of attendees.
* **Venue Needed** (`venue_needed`, required): Select whether the seminar requires an in-person venue, virtual event, or hybrid format.
* **Room Type Preference** (`room_type`, optional): If an in-person venue is chosen, select the preferred room type.
* **AV and Technical Requirements** (`av_requirements`, optional): Select any necessary equipment or technical requirements.
* **Catering Required** (`catering_needed`, optional): Select whether catering is required.
* **Dietary or Special Requirements** (`catering_dietary_needs`, optional): If catering is required, enter any special dietary or catering needs.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, optional): Select whether parking is required.
* **Accessibility or Special Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): Enter any special accessibility or equipment needs.
* **Setup Preferences or Notes** (`setup_preferences`, optional): Enter any specific setup preferences or requirements.
* **Budget Estimate** (`budget_estimate`, optional): Enter an approximate budget for the seminar.
* **Booking Request Date** (`booking_date`, required): Enter the date of the booking request.
