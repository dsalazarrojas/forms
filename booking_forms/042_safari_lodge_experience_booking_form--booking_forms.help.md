# safari_lodge_experience_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safari_lodge_experience_booking_form is a booking form used to collect information for guest stays at a safari lodge. It gathers details about guests, their activity preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the guest details section, including any relevant information about the guest.
2. Choose the arrival and departure dates for the stay.
3. Select the activities you would like to participate in during your stay.
4. Choose your preferred payment method.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Provide any additional details about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details**: This is the first section of the form where you can enter the guest's name, etc.
  * **Arrivals Date**: Enter the date the guest will be arriving at the lodge.
  * **Departure Date**: Enter the date the guest will be departing from the lodge.
  * **Activity Choice**: Select the activities you would like to participate in during your stay. You can select multiple activities.
  * **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your stay.
  * **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any important information about your stay.
  * **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your stay.
  * **Activity Details**: Provide any additional details about the activities you are interested in.
  * **Room Details**: Provide any additional details about your accommodation needs.
  * **Other Details**: Add any other important information about your stay.
  * **Terms And Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of your stay.
