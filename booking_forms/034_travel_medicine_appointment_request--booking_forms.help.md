# Travel Medicine Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment at the travel medicine clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your travel date.
2. Enter your travel time.
3. Enter your travel destination.
4. Describe your medical condition, if applicable.
5. Provide details about your medical condition, if applicable.
6. Indicate if you have visited this travel medicine clinic before.
7. Enter your contact phone number.
8. Enter your contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your trip.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required): Enter the country or region you will be visiting.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): If you have a medical condition, please describe it briefly.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition_details`, text, required): If you have a medical condition, provide more details about it.
* **Travel Medicine Clinic** (`travel_medicine_clinic`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have visited this travel medicine clinic before.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Ensure that your travel date and travel time are accurate.
* If you have any medical conditions, please be as thorough as possible when describing and detailing them.
* Double-check that your contact phone number and contact email are up-to-date.
