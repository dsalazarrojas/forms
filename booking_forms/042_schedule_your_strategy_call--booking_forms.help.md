# Schedule Your Strategy Call - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule a strategy call with a client. It collects relevant information for the call, including the category, description, name, email address, date and time, and phone number (if provided). Fill out the form to schedule a call and receive follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category for the call.
2. Provide a brief description of the call.
3. Enter your name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Choose a date and time for the call.
6. If desired, provide your phone number.
7. If necessary, provide any follow-up details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Your Strategy Call**: This is the form title, which is not editable. 
* **category**: Select the category for the call. You can choose between "Booking Forms" and "Other". 
* **description**: Please provide a brief description of the call. You can write up to 1000 characters. 
* **name**: Enter your name for identification purposes. 
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the call. 
* **date and time**: Choose a date and time for the call. Make sure to select a time zone that is correct for your location. 
* **phone**: If you have a phone number, you can enter it for us to contact you about the call. 
* **follow up details**: If you have any additional details or instructions for follow-up, please provide them here. 

## Tips
- Make sure to select a date and time that works for you and is convenient for us to contact you. 
- If you have any special requests or instructions, please provide them in the "follow up details" field. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
