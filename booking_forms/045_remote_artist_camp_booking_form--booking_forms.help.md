# Remote Artist Camp Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for booking and managing remote artist camps. It collects information from participants and artists to facilitate a smooth and efficient camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth camp operations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Artist Information (artist-info)** (`artist-info`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the artist, such as their name, bio, or contact details.
*   **Discipline (Discipline)** (`discipline`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available disciplines: painting, drawing, sculpture, printmaking, mixed-media, photography, digital, or other.
*   **Start Date (Start Date)** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the camp or event.
*   **End Date (End Date)** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the camp or event.
*   **Preferred Date (Preferred Date)** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Enter the preferred date for the camp or event.
*   **Artist Notes (Artist Notes)** (`artist_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the artist.
*   **Artist Preferences (Artist Preferences)** (`artist_preferences`, note, required): Enter any specific preferences or requirements of the artist.
*   **Participant Information (Participant Info)** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the participant, such as their name, contact details, or special needs.
*   **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
*   **Participant Email (Participant Email)** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the participant.
*   **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
*   **Booking Form Status (Booking Form Status)** (`booking_form_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the booking form: pending, booked, or cancelled.
*   **Booking Form Owner (Booking Form Owner)** (`booking_form_owner`, select_multiple, required): Select who owns the booking form: admin, artist, or both.
*   **Additional Artist Info (Additional Artist Info)** (`additional_artist_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the artist.
*   **Artist ID (Artist ID)** (`artist_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the artist.
*   **Artist Name (Artist Name)** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist.
*   **Artist Email (Artist Email)** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the artist.
*   **Artist Phone (Artist Phone)** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the artist.
*   **Artist Image (Artist Image)** (`artist_image`, text, required): Enter the image or profile picture of the artist.

Note: This form requires the following fields to be filled out to be saved as a draft and completed.

Please contact us if you have any questions or require further assistance.
