# Hotel Reservation Form Brown Background - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Form Brown Background is designed to gather essential information from users about their hotel booking preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a hotel from the available options in the "Select Hotel" field. 
2. Choose a checkin date using the calendar picker in the "Checkin Date" field. 
3. Select a checkout date using the calendar picker in the "Checkout Date" field.
4. Choose the type of room you prefer from the options in the "Type of Room" field.
5. Provide any additional details or comments you'd like to be considered for your booking in the "Room Description" field (optional).
6. If you have a specific room number in mind, you can enter it in the "Room Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted about your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Hotel (Select Hotel)**: Choose a hotel from the provided options. This will help us understand which hotel you are interested in booking.
* **Checkin Date (Checkin Date)**: Select a date from the calendar picker to check-in. This is when you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Checkout Date (Checkout Date)**: Select a date from the calendar picker to check-out. This is when you plan to leave the hotel.
* **Type of Room (Type of Room)**: Select one or multiple types of rooms that interest you. This will help us suggest rooms that fit your preferences.
* **Room Number (Room Number)**: If you have a specific room number in mind, enter it here. This can be helpful if you have a preferred room or have already booked a room at this hotel.
* **Room Description (Room Description)**: Provide any additional information about your room preferences or requirements. This can be helpful in our internal processing.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your booking. We can use this to get in touch with you with any updates or questions.
