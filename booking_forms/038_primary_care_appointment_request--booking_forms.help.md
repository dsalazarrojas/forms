# primary_care_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from patients for scheduling appointments with primary care doctors. It is used to collect essential details, including patient name, contact information, medical history, and any additional information the patient wants to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. Select a date and time for your appointment that works best for you.
3. Enter the reason for your visit, which will help the doctor understand your medical needs.
4. If necessary, provide additional information that will help the doctor make informed decisions about your care.
5. List any allergies or medications you are currently taking, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and appointment reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for scheduling and communication.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Select a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Select a time for your appointment that works best for you.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, required): Describe the reason for your visit, which will help the doctor understand your medical needs.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that will help the doctor make informed decisions about your care.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, which will help the doctor take necessary precautions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking, which will help the doctor make informed decisions about your care.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or concerns, which will help the doctor understand your current health status.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you need to change your appointment date or time, please contact the medical staff directly.
- The information you provide will be reviewed by the medical staff before your appointment, and you will be contacted with any necessary arrangements or instructions.
