# Vacation Rental Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Exit Offboarding Form is a tool for tenants to provide information about their exit from a vacation rental property. This form helps property managers or administrators to keep a record of tenant interactions and improve the rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide feedback about your experience at the rental property.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the property.
4. Enter your contact phone number and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact name and details so that the property manager can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Feedback Form**: Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your stay at the rental property. This helps us to understand your experience and make improvements.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the property out of 5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor.
* **Contact Phone**: If you have a contact phone number, enter it so that the property manager can reach you directly.
* **Contact Email**: If you have a contact email, enter it so that the property manager can reach you via email.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your stay.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your completed form.
