# Home Cleaning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a form for customers to book a home cleaning service with a cleaner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of cleaning service you require from the "Type of Cleaning" dropdown.
2. Enter the date and time you would like to book the service in the "Service Date" and "Time" fields.
3. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field and the zip code in the "Zip Code" field. Ensure the address is valid and the zip code is correct.
4. Enter your city in the "City" field.
5. Select the type of services you require in the "Service" field.
6. Rate the service in the "Rate" field. This will help the cleaner understand how much to charge for the service.
7. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the "Name", "Phone", and "Email" fields, respectively.
8. If you would like to add any additional comments or details, you can enter them in the "Additional Note" field.
9. Finally, review the form and ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting it.
