# DJ Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from clients about their upcoming events, allowing our DJ services team to tailor their services to the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about your event.
3.  If you have any specific requirements or requests, make sure to mention them in the "Services Offered" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your company.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please fill in your name as the person responsible for this event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so our team can contact you about your event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so our team can reach you about your event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Describe the type of event you are planning, such as a wedding, party, or corporate event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are planning (Wedding, Party, Corporate Event).
* **Number of Guests** (`event_guests`, number, optional): Enter the estimated number of guests attending your event.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you would like us to provide for your event (Music, MC Services, Photography, Video Production, Lighting, DJ Equipment).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to help our team deliver the best experience for your event.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, make sure to mention them in the "Services Offered" field.
* If you need to make changes or updates, please fill out a new form with the updated information.
