# Jewelry Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients who are interested in booking a jewelry consultation. The collected data will be used to schedule and prepare for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the text fields for each section:

1. "Client Info" - Enter your name, email, and phone number.
2. "Appointment Details" - Provide details about your preferred date and time for the consultation.
3. "Project Details" - Briefly describe your project or the specific item you want to discuss during the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to contact you.
* **Appointment Details** (`title`, `text`, required/optional: no): Provide the date and time you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Project Details** (`description`, `text`, required/optional: no): Describe your project or the specific item you want to discuss during the consultation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information so we can reach out to you for further instructions.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing project information to ensure we can prepare adequately for your consultation.
