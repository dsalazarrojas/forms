# Vineyard Tour And Tasting Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential customers to book a vineyard tour and tasting experience. It gathers essential information to process and confirm their booking requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about your group or party in the "General Info" field.
2. Fill in the "Booking Details" field with specific information about your tour and tasting preferences.
3. Enter the tour and tasting details in the "Tour Tasting Info" field.
4. Sign the agreement in the "Signed Agreement" field, acknowledging the terms and conditions of your booking.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (General Info, text, required: false):** Enter a brief description of your group or party, including number of people attending, any special requirements, or any other relevant details.

* **Booking Details (Booking Details, text, required: false):** Provide specific information about your tour and tasting preferences, such as date and time, number of people, and any special requests.

* **Tour Tasting Info (Tour Tasting Info, text, required: false):** Enter the tour and tasting details, including the type of tour and tasting experience you are interested in (e.g., wine tasting, food pairing, etc.).

* **Signed Agreement (Agreement, text, required: false):** Sign and agree to the terms and conditions of your booking, acknowledging that you have read and understood the guidelines and policies.

* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, required: false):** Enter your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields (if any) are filled in before submitting the form.
- Make sure to clearly enter all necessary information to ensure efficient processing and confirmation of your booking.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us directly.
- Review and understand the terms and conditions of your booking before signing the agreement.
