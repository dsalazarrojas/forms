# tool_rental_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide information for tool rentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Customer Details" page with your name, email, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the tools you would like to rent from the "Tool Selection" page.
3. Enter your booking details, including the date and time you'd like to rent the tools, in the "Booking Details" page.
4. Finally, review and confirm your order on the "Review and Confirm" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and email address to help us contact you about your rental order.
- **Tool Selection** (`tool_selection`, text, required: false): Choose the tools you would like to rent by typing their names or numbers.
- **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, text, required: false): Enter the date and time you'd like to rent the tools.
- **Review and Confirm** (`review_and_confirm`, text, required: false): Double-check your order to ensure everything is correct before confirming your rental.
