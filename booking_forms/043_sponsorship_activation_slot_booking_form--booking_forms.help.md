# Sponsorship Activation Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book sponsorship activation slots for events or activities. It captures essential details to facilitate smooth sponsorship support and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and select the sponsor name you're representing.
2. Provide the event name and date.
3. Enter the start and end times for the slot you want to book.
4. Specify the length and count of the slots you need.
5. Choose the specific time slots you want to book.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Optionally, provide your address for future reference.
7. Add any relevant message for our team to get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Select the name of the sponsor you're representing. This could be a company, organization, or individual. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Select Sponsor
	+ Another Sponsor
	+ Third Sponsor
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where you'd like to book a sponsorship activation slot. This could be a conference, trade show, or festival.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event where you'll be activating your sponsorship.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the slot you want to book. This is usually in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00 or 09:00 AM).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the slot you want to book. This is also usually in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:30 or 10:30 AM). Please note that this is optional, and you can select a fixed length for your slot if you prefer.
* **Slot Length**: Specify the length of the slot you'd like to book in minutes.
* **Slot Count**: Enter the count of slots you need to book for your event or activity.
* **Time Slots**: Select the specific time slots you'd like to book. You can choose from multiple options.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you for further details.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Address**: Optionally, enter your address for our team to reference during the booking process.
* **Message**: Add any additional message or notes for our team to consider during the booking process.
