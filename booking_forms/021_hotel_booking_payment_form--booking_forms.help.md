# hotel_booking_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel_booking_payment_form is used for booking and payment details for hotel rooms. This form captures the guest's details, dates of stay, room selection, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Guest Details" section to provide your personal details.
2. Choose a date for your stay under "Date In" and "Date Out" fields.
3. Choose a room type from the options provided under "Room Type".
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
5. Provide any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: This is the personal information of the guest, such as their name.
* **Date In**: Choose the date you would like to check-in to the hotel.
* **Date Out**: Choose the date you would like to check-out from the hotel.
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room you would like to book. This could be a different room type such as single, double, or family.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number you would like to book.
* **Total Guests**: Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person booking the room.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person booking the room.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person booking the room.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or comments you would like to make for your stay.
