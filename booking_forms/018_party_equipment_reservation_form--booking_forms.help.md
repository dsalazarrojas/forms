# Party Equipment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Equipment Reservation Form is used to collect information from party hosts to reserve party equipment for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps above to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **party_equipment_reservation_form**: 
  * **event_date** (MM/DD/YYYY): Enter the date of the event.
  * **event_time** (24-hour clock format): Choose the event time.
  * **number_of_guests** (whole numbers only): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
  * **equipment_requested** ('Yes' or 'No'): Select 'Yes' if equipment is needed; 'No' otherwise.
  * **reservation_time** (24-hour clock format): Choose the reservation time for equipment pick-up and return.
  * **reservation_duration** (hours): Enter the reservation duration.
  * **contact_email** (email address): Enter the email address to contact for follow-up.
  * **contact_phone** (phone number): Enter the phone number to contact for follow-up.
  * **additional_comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or details about the event.
