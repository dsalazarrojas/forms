# Call For Presentations - Help Guide

## Purpose

Call For Presentations is a form used to submit proposals for presentations and talks at our events. Please ensure you have reviewed our Call For Presentations guidelines before completing this form to avoid duplication of effort and ensure your proposal is not rejected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Speaker Information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email Address: Enter your professional email address.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
	* Organization or Affiliation: Enter your current or past affiliation (optional).
2. Add your Presentation Details:
	* Presentation Title: Enter the title of your proposed presentation.
	* Presentation Summary: Provide a brief overview of what your presentation will cover.
	* Preferred Duration: Select the preferred duration for your presentation.
	* Presentation Track or Topic: Select the relevant topic or track for your presentation.
	* Target Audience Level: Select the level of your target audience.
3. Confirm your Availability and Preferences:
	* Audio/Visual Requirements: Select the equipment you require for your presentation.
	* Willing to Travel to Event Location: Select if you are willing to travel to the event location.
	* Available Presentation Dates: Select your available dates for the presentation.
4. Additional Information:
	* Previous Speaking Experience: Describe any prior conference presentations or speaking engagements.
	* Additional Comments or Special Requests: Add any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a professional email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization or Affiliation**: Enter your current or past affiliation (e.g., company, university, etc.).
* **Professional Biography**: Briefly describe your background and expertise in the field.
* **Presentation Title**: Enter a clear and concise title of your proposed presentation.
* **Presentation Summary**: Provide a brief overview of what your presentation will cover.
* **Preferred Duration**: Select the preferred duration for your presentation from the options provided.
* **Presentation Track or Topic**: Select the relevant topic or track for your presentation.
* **Target Audience Level**: Select the level of your target audience.
* **Previous Speaking Experience**: Describe any prior conference presentations or speaking engagements.
* **Audio/Visual Requirements**: Select the equipment you require for your presentation.
* **Will to Travel to Event Location**: Select if you are willing to travel to the event location.
* **Available Presentation Dates**: Select your available dates for the presentation.
* **Website or LinkedIn Profile**: Enter your professional website or LinkedIn profile (optional).
* **Marketing Permission**: Select if you grant permission for us to promote your presentation on our website and materials.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests**: Add any additional comments or requests.
