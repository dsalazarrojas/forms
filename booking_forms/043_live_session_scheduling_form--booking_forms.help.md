# Live Session Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Session Scheduling Form is used to schedule a live session for a client. The form helps gather information about the client's preferences for the live session, including their availability and any specific requirements they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session schedule you prefer from the options provided.
2. Enter your name to confirm your identity.
3. Enter the date and time for the scheduled session.
4. Choose the session length in the available units provided.
5. Select the start and end times for the session.
6. Confirm the date and time of the session.
7. Confirm the time zone for the session.

  * This field is used to determine the schedule of the live session.
  * Select the time that suits you best.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to confirm your identity.
  * This field is used to confirm your identity as a client.
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Scheduled Date** (`scheduled_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.
  * This field is used to schedule the session on the desired date.
  * Please ensure the date is in a format that can be easily understood (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the time of the session.
  * This field is used to select the time of the session.
  * Please select the time that best suits you.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, required/optional): Choose the length of the session.
  * This field is used to determine the duration of the session.
  * Please select the session length from the options provided.
