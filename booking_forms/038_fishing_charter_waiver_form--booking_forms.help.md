# fishing_charter_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and ensure their understanding of the terms and conditions of the fishing charter. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues or conflicts during the charter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your preference.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select whether you have any of the following details.
	+ Yes (e.g., medical conditions, allergies)
	+ No (no medical conditions or allergies)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, **required**): Enter your contact number so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you have an emergency contact.
	+ Yes (e.g., family member, friend)
	+ No (no emergency contact)
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, note, **required**): Please acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with fishing charters.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select whether you agree to the following terms:
	+ Yes (agree)
	+ No (disagree)
* **Customer Consent** (`customer_consent`, select_one, **required**): Select whether you give your consent to participate in the fishing charter.
	+ Yes (agree)
	+ No (disagree)
* **Signature** (`signature`, number, **required**): Sign this form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **not required**): If you have time, enter the time you sign this form. (This field is optional and not required for submission.)

This help guide is intended to ensure that customers understand what they need to do to complete the fishing charter waiver form accurately and successfully.
