# Shoe Service Appointment Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoe Service Appointment Booking form is designed to help customers book a shoe service appointment with a service provider. This form collects essential information to schedule the appointment efficiently and effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Start by filling out the form on the designated page labeled "Form Start".
2. Enter your Customer Name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Provide your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Select the service type you require from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the service location from the "Service Location" dropdown menu.
6. Select a date for your appointment from the "Appointment Date" field.
7. Choose an appointment time from the "Appointment Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name, as the service provider will use this information to communicate with you about your appointment.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where the service provider can reach you to confirm your appointment.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you require a service, 'No' if you do not.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the service location where you would like to receive the service.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for your appointment. Please select a date that is convenient for you.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select an appointment time that suits you best.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our support team for clarification.
* Ensure that the date and time selected are feasible for the service provider to accommodate your appointment.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the 'Service Type' field.
* After submitting the form, a service provider will contact you to confirm your appointment.
* Be on time for your appointment to ensure the service provider can assist you efficiently.
