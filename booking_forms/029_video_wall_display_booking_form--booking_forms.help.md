# Video Wall Display Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Wall Display Booking Form is a user-facing form used to book a video wall display. It's essential to fill out the form honestly and for the intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and other requested details.
2. Choose a specific date and start time for your booking request.
3. Select the duration of your booking.
4. Specify any display requirements you may have.
5. Agree to the terms and conditions of the video wall display.
6. Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, **required**): Choose a date for your booking request.
* **Booking Start Time** (`booking_start_time`, time, **required**): Choose a start time for your booking request.
* **Booking Duration** (`booking_duration`, number, **required**): Select the duration of your booking in minutes.
* **Display Requirements** (`display_requirements`, text, **optional**): Specify any special requirements you may have for your display request.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, note, **optional**): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the video wall display.
