# Jet Ski Rental Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Jet Ski Rental Reservation Form is designed to collect necessary information from users for a successful jet ski rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your correct and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check that all fields are correctly filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (text):
	+ This section collects your personal information, please fill in your name, email, and other details as prompted.
* **Reservation Info** (text):
	+ This section collects details about your reservation, including date, time, and preferred rental duration.
* **Rental Info** (text):
	+ In this section, you will find information about your rental options, including jet ski type, fuel level, and other rental specifics.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, please contact us.
