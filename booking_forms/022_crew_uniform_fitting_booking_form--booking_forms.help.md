# Crew Uniform Fitting Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule crew uniform fittings, which is a critical part of the uniform allocation process. It collects necessary information from crew members, including their availability and preferences for fitting sessions, to streamline the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your crew uniform fitting details, including date, time, location, and any specific sizes needed.
2. Indicate whether you have any crew uniform fitting preferences, such as sizes or locations.
3. Add any additional notes or details about your fitting session, if necessary.
4. Select the assigned user and their role.
5. Choose who submitted the form (e.g., yourself or another crew member).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Booking Form** (text): This is the title of the form.
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Date** (date): Select the date you would like to be fitted for your uniform.
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Time** (time): Choose the time slot you prefer for your fitting session.
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Location** (text): Provide the location where you would like to be fitted.
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Sizes** (select multiple): Indicate whether you have specific sizes needed for your uniform, such as extra-large or extra-small.
* **Crew Uniform Fitting Details** (text): Add any additional information about your fitting session, such as specific styles or requirements.
* **Crew Phone** (text): Enter your crew member's phone number.
* **Crew Email** (email): Enter your crew member's email address.
* **Crew Notes** (text): Add any notes or comments about your fitting session, if necessary.
* **Assigned User** (select one): Indicate whether you have been assigned a specific user for your fitting session.
* **Assigned User ID** (number): Enter the ID of the assigned user.
* **Assigned User Role** (select multiple): Select the role of the assigned user.
* **Submitter** (select one): Choose who submitted the form (e.g., yourself or another crew member).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any specific requirements or constraints for your fitting session, make sure to include them in the form.
* Be aware that the assigned user will be notified of your fitting session, and they will be responsible for ensuring that your uniform is ready for the session.
