# Aesthetics Clinic Online Daily Deal Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aesthetics Clinic Online Daily Deal Booking Form is designed for customers to book daily deals online. To book a deal, you will need to provide some personal details and select a preferred date and time slot for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred daily deal from the options provided in the "Select Today's Deal" field.
2. Enter any promo code if you have one (optional).
3. Provide your personal details: first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
4. Select a preferred date and time slot for the appointment.
5. Enter your credit card information, including the card number, expiry date, and CVV.
6. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Choose Your Deal --**: Select the daily deal you would like to book from the options provided.
* **Select Today's Deal**: Choose one of the available daily deals to book.
* **Promo Code (if applicable)**: If you have a promo code, enter it here (optional).
* **-- Client Details --**: Provide your personal details: first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you will receive the confirmation of your booking.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **-- Schedule Appointment --**: Select a preferred date and time slot for the appointment.
* **Preferred Date**: Select a date within 30 days of the purchase.
* **Preferred Time**: Select a time slot: Morning (9am - 12pm), Afternoon (12pm - 4pm), or Evening (4pm - 7pm).
* **-- Secure Your Deal --**: Confirm your credit card information and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Total Amount ($)**: This is the total amount of your deal.
* **Credit Card Number**: Enter your credit card number.
* **Expiry Date (MM/YY)**: Enter the expiry date of your credit card.
* **CVV**: Enter the 3-digit code on the back of your credit card.
* **Terms & Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree" or "Other" if you have a question.
