# Guest Information And Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information and preferences from guests to enhance their stay and tailor services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all required fields (none in this form) are filled in.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, not required): Enter the guest's full name, as you would normally introduce yourself.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, not required): Select the date of your arrival. Please ensure it's in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Check In** (`check_in`, time, not required): Choose the time of your planned check-in. If unsure, leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay. 
- If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, please refer to the form's title for clarification. 
- If you have any special requests or preferences, feel free to mention them in the notes or comments section (not shown in this form).
