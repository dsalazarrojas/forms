# Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent and medical information from clients before undergoing eyeliner retouching procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, answering all questions and signing both signature sections.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your medical history, allergies, and medications.
3. Select one of the options for the consent question, indicating your agreement to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form Questions** (`eyeliner_retouch_pigment_consent_form_questions`, text, required): This section asks a series of questions about your understanding of the eyeliner retouching procedure.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, required): Please provide information about any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may affect your eyes.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, date, required): Sign and date this section to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous eye injuries or eye-related medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Check if you have any allergies, and list any allergens that may cause a reaction.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including prescription medications and supplements.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Check any medical conditions that may affect your eyes or vision, such as cataracts, glaucoma, etc.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): This section asks you to confirm your agreement to the procedure. Please select 'Yes' to indicate your consent.
* **Client Signature 2** (`client_signature`, select_one, required): Sign this section to confirm your consent.
* **Client Signature 2** (`signature_2`, text, required): Sign and date this section to confirm your identity and agreement to the procedure.
