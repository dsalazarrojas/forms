# Makeup Technique Comfort Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your comfort level with various makeup techniques, your confidence in performing those techniques, and other related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and feelings.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all optional.

* **Introductory** (`id: 2`): Please select your level of experience with makeup techniques, from Beginner to Advanced.
* **Comfort Level** (`id: 3`): How comfortable do you feel with makeup techniques, from Very Comfortable to Very Uncomfortable?

* **Skill Level** (`id: 5`): Select your current level of skill with makeup techniques, from Basic to Advanced.

* **Practice** (`id: 7`): How many times per week do you practice your makeup skills?
* **Time Spent** (`id: 8`): How much time do you spend on average practicing your makeup skills per session?
* **Education Level** (`id: 9`): What level of education do you have related to makeup, from High to Low?
* **Training** (`id: 10`): Have you received any formal training in makeup, Yes or No?
* **Training Hours** (`id: 11`): How many hours have you spent on formal training?
* **Classes** (`id: 12`): Do you regularly attend makeup classes, Yes or No?
* **Content** (`id: 13`): What types of content are you interested in learning from, such as Blog, Video, Live, or Other?
* **Blog Hours** (`id: 14`): How many hours per week do you spend on blogging about makeup?
* **Content Type** (`id: 15`): What type of content do you create or enjoy consuming, from Blog to Live?
* **Product Knowledge** (`id: 16`): Do you have in-depth knowledge of makeup products, Yes or No?
* **Application** (`id: 17`): Do you apply makeup on clients or for special occasions, Yes or No?
* **Feedback** (`id: 18`): Do you receive feedback on your makeup skills, Yes or No?
* **Feedback Frequency** (`id: 19`): How often do you receive feedback, from Daily to Rarely?
* **Social Media** (`id: 20`): Are you active on social media platforms related to makeup, Yes or No?

* **Review** (`id: 22`): How often do you write reviews about makeup products or services?
* **Review Frequency** (`id: 23`): How often do you post reviews, from Daily to Rarely?
* **Review Location** (`id: 24`): Where do you post reviews, Online or Offline?
