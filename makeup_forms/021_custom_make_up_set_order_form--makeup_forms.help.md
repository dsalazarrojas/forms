# custom_make_up_set_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The custom_make_up_set_order_form is a form used to collect information about a makeup order. This form is used to gather essential details to process and fulfill the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the order details page with the required information about the order.
2. On the next page, enter the shipping address and contact information for the order.
3. On the following page, provide payment information and the preferred shipping method.
4. Add any additional order notes or comments on the next page.
5. Finally, enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, Text, Not Required): Enter the description of the order, such as the type of makeup set you are interested in purchasing.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, Text, Not Required): Enter your shipping address, including your name, street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Not Required): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to you about your order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, Text, Not Required): Enter any relevant payment information, such as the payment method or special instructions.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, Text, Not Required): Choose your preferred shipping method, such as UPS, USPS, or FedEx.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, Note, Not Required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order, such as special requests or preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Not Required): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and information about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

Note: All fields are optional and only required if you wish to provide additional information to us.
