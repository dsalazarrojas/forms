# Makeup School Instructor Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for makeup school instructors to renew their certification. Please ensure you have all required documents before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required documents (such as ID, proof of address, etc.) ready before starting the application.
2. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
4. Provide your address and zip code.
5. Select your school affiliation status.
6. Confirm that the documents listed are accurate.
7. Confirm your school affiliation details.
8. Confirm that all documents are accurate and complete.
9. Submit the application to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (5, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **City** (6, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (7, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your school is affiliated with a state-approved board.
* **Zip Code** (8, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **School Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Documents List** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the documents you have, and confirm you have them.
* **Document 1** (11, text, optional): Enter details about Document 1.
* **Document 2** (12, text, optional): Enter details about Document 2.
* **Document 3** (13, text, optional): Enter details about Document 3.
* **Confirm Documents** (14, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you confirm the accuracy of the documents.
* **Confirm Name** (15, date, required): Confirm your name by providing a date.
* **Confirm Address** (16, note, required): Confirm your address by providing a note.
* **Confirm School** (17, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your school affiliation is correct.
* **Confirm School Name** (18, text, optional): Enter your school's name.
* **Confirm School Address** (19, text, optional): Enter your school's address.
* **Confirm Document 1** (20, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if Document 1 is accurate.
* **Confirm Document 2** (21, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if Document 2 is accurate.
* **Confirm Document 3** (22, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if Document 3 is accurate.
* **Confirm Document 4** (23, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if Document 4 is accurate.
* **Confirm Document 5** (24, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if Document 5 is accurate.
* **Submit** (25, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to complete the application process.
