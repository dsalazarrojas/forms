# Permanent Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from you for a permanent makeup consultation. You can submit this form to schedule an appointment and discuss your personal preferences and service requirements with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you for follow-up questions or appointment confirmations.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
4. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This helps us understand your skin and hair type for the best advice.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This is not required, but it helps us to provide accurate estimates and confirm appointments.
6. Choose the service you're interested in from the "Service Requested" drop-down menu. Options include Ombre Brows, Ombre Brow, and Ombre Liner.
7. Select the area you'd like us to focus on from the "Area to Focus On" drop-down menu. Options include Brows, Brows and Lashes, Face, Eye, and Lips.
8. Choose a date and time for your appointment in the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields. These fields are not required, but they help us schedule your appointment efficiently.
9. Enter the name of the technician you prefer to work with in the "Technician" field. This is not required, but it helps us assign the best technician for your needs.
10. Provide any additional questions or comments in the "Consultation Notes" field. This is optional, but it helps us understand your requirements better.
11. Finally, add any additional questions or comments in the "Additional Questions" field. This is also optional, but it helps us to serve you better.
