**Lash Intake Form - Help Guide**

# Purpose
The Lash Intake Form is used to collect information from clients prior to their appointment with a lash technician, ensuring a safe and effective treatment experience.

**How To Complete This Form**

To ensure a thorough understanding of the client's needs and preferences, complete the form by providing the required information in the following sections:

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Client Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter the client's email for communication purposes.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service required (Classic Lashes, Volume Lashes, or Mega Lashes).
* **Lash Technician** (`lash_technician`, select_one, required): Choose the technician who will be performing the service.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the appointment date.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Enter the appointment time.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Confirm the consent date.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Provide a signature to indicate consent for treatment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Lash Type** (`lash_type`, select_one, required): Select Natural or Synthetic.
* **Lash Size** (`lashes_size`, select_one, required): Choose the lash size (10, 20, or 30).
* **Lashes Color** (`lashes_color`, select_multiple, required): Select the desired color (Red, Blue, or Green).
* **Lash Style** (`lash_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style (Full or Half).
* **Lash Thickness** (`lash_thickness`, select_one, required): Select the thickness (Thin, Medium, or Thick).
* **Lash Material** (`lash_material`, select_one, required): Choose the material (Silk, Silk-Blend, or Henna).
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments for the technician.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): Add internal comments.
* **Photos** (`photos`, text, optional): Add photos for reference.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Provide the medical history.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any applicable medical conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, or Epilepsy).
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, required): List any allergies.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, required): Provide information on medical treatment.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, note, required): Sign the consent statement to indicate consent for treatment.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
* Use clear and accurate information to ensure accurate treatment planning.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all information is provided before submission.
