# perfume_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to purchase perfume, allowing the business to gather details about the customer and their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Order Details" section with your order information.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" in the next section.
3. Finally, enter the "Order Info" in the last section.
You can submit the form multiple times with different information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter your order details here.

### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your customer information here.

### Order Info
* **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional): Enter your order information here.
