# Makeup Pre Event Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Pre Event Survey form is used to collect essential details about events where our makeup services will be provided. This information helps us prepare and deliver our services effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event details and time slots where our makeup services will be provided.
2. Provide your artist's details, including their name and availability.
3. Choose the artist's level of experience and the services they will be offering (Makeup, Hair, or Both).
4. Specify the client's preferences and contact information.
5. Enter the client's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Briefly describe the event where our makeup services will be provided.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Select the time slot for the event.
* **Artist Details** (`artist_details`): Enter your artist's name and any additional information you think is relevant.
* **Artist Availability** (`artist_availability`): Choose the time slot when your artist is available to work on the event.
* **Artist Experience** (`artist_experience`): Select the level of experience your artist has:
	+ Basic: For simple makeup applications.
	+ Intermediate: For medium-level makeup applications.
	+ Advanced: For detailed and intricate makeup designs.
* **Artist Services** (`artist_services`): Choose the services your artist will be offering:
	+ Makeup: Only providing makeup services.
	+ Hair: Only providing hair services.
	+ Both: Providing both makeup and hair services.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`): Briefly describe any specific requests or requirements from the client.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`): Enter any relevant contact information for the client.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`): Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
