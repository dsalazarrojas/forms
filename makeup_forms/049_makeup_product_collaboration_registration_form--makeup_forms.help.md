# Makeup Product Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from users who want to collaborate on makeup products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in the required fields with the requested information. The fields marked as "required" are essential to complete the form.
2.  For fields without a "hint", please provide the requested information as accurately as possible.
3.  Ensure that all fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): This field is for entering any additional information you'd like to share about your collaboration.
*   **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required): Please provide a detailed description of the makeup product you'd like to collaborate on.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address where you'd like the product to be shipped.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Please provide your payment details for the collaboration.
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Describe the product you'd like to collaborate on in detail.
*   **Product Photo** (`product_photo`, text, required): Upload a clear image of the product you'd like to collaborate on.
*   **Collaboration Terms** (`collaboration_terms`, text, required): Please provide any additional collaboration terms or conditions.
*   **Creator Interview** (`creator_interview`, text, required): Provide a brief interview with the product creator, if applicable.
*   **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, text, required): This field is for any comments or messages you may want to leave for the collaborator.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select one, required): Please select whether your submission is Approved, Declined, or Pending.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted this form.

## Tips

1.  Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
2.  Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the fields.
3.  If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
