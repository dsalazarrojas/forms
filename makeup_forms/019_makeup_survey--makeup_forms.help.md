# Makeup Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Makeup Survey is designed to help us understand your habits and preferences when it comes to makeup. Your feedback will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with your information. Make sure to enter your answers in the format specified in each field.
2. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
3. If you are unsure about a particular question, you can ask our support team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **How often do you wear makeup?** (Makeup Frequency, number, optional): Choose the number of days you wear makeup per week. For example, if you wear makeup 5 days a week, select 5.
* **What type of makeup do you usually wear?** (Makeup Type, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided to describe the type of makeup you usually wear.
* **How long do you spend on makeup each day?** (Makeup Routine, number, optional): Enter the number of minutes you spend on makeup each day.
* **Favorite product** (Favorite Product, text, optional): If you have a favorite product, enter the name and brand of the product.
* **Reason for using makeup** (Reason for using makeup, checkbox, optional): Choose one or both reasons for using makeup.
* **How long have you been using makeup?** (Time Using, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been using makeup.
* **Satisfaction with current routine** (Satisfaction, radio, optional): Choose your level of satisfaction with your current makeup routine.
* **Makeup Brand** (Makeup Brand, text, optional): If you have a preferred brand, enter the name of the brand.
