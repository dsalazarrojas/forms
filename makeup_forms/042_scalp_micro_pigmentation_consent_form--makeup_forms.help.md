# Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from clients for scalp micro pigmentation treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely, ensuring that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
2.  Review each section carefully and provide the required information.

* **Client Informed Consent** (`Client Informed Consent`, text, required: false): This section is intended to confirm that the client has been fully informed about the scalp micro pigmentation treatment process. Please provide a brief description of your understanding of the treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, required: false): Record the date of your consent.
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, note, required: false): Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the treatment process.

* **Consent to Treatment** (`Consent to Treatment`, select_one, required: false): Please indicate if you consent to the treatment.
