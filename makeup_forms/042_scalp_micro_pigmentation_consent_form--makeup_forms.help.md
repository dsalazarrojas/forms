# Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from clients for scalp micro pigmentation treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely, ensuring that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
2.  Review each section carefully and provide the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Informed Consent** (`Client Informed Consent`, text, required: false): This section is intended to confirm that the client has been fully informed about the scalp micro pigmentation treatment process. Please provide a brief description of your understanding of the treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, required: false): Record the date of your consent.
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, note, required: false): Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the treatment process.
* **Practitioner Name** (`Practitioner Name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the practitioner performing the treatment.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required: false): Record the contact number of the practitioner or contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required: false): Record the contact email of the practitioner or contact person.
* **Consent to Contact** (`Consent to Contact`, select_one, required: false): Please indicate if you consent to contact you for follow-up information.
* **Consent to Follow-up** (`Consent to Follow-up`, select_one, required: false): Please indicate if you consent to follow-up communication.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`Consent to Treatment`, select_one, required: false): Please indicate if you consent to the treatment.
* **Client Notes** (`Client Notes`, note, required: false): This section is for any additional notes from the practitioner or client.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to avoid any misunderstandings or disputes in the future.
This guide is intended to provide clarity on the purpose, completion, and fields of the Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form.
