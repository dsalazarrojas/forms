# Makeup Line Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Line Release Form is used to obtain consent from participants for the use of their image in photography-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. If you have a parent or guardian, please provide their name.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Choose whether or not you consent to the use of your image.
5. If you are a photographer, please provide your name, title, and email address.
6. Choose whether or not you consent to the use of your photo.
7. Select the release date.
8. Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
9. Sign the participant consent and any other required signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian if you are under the age of 18.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Participant Image Consent**: Check the box to indicate whether or not you consent to the use of your image.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer taking your photo.
* **Photographer Title**: Enter the title or profession of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the photographer.
* **Photographer Email**: Enter the email address of the photographer.
* **Participant Photo Release**: Check the box to indicate whether or not you consent to the use of your photo.
* **Photographer Photo Release**: Check the box to indicate whether or not the photographer consents to the use of their photo.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date when the release is valid from the current date.
* **Participant Agreement**: Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Photographer Agreement**: Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Consent Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If you are under the age of 18, sign the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Photographer Consent Signature**: Sign the name of the photographer.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date when you sign the form.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address if you want to provide it.
* **Parent or Guardian Address**: Enter the address of your parent or guardian if you are under the age of 18.
* **Participant City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Participant State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Participant Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Participant Photo Release**: Check the box to indicate whether or not you consent to the use of your photo.
* **Photographer Photo Release**: Check the box to indicate whether or not the photographer consents to the use of their photo.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in the required fields.
* If you are under the age of 18, please ensure that your parent or guardian signs the form.
* If you are a photographer, please ensure that you sign the form.
* Please review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
