# Permanent Makeup Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Release Form is a document that obtains client consent for permanent makeup treatments. It's crucial for ensuring the client's understanding and agreement with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter a brief title for the form, e.g., the client's name or the treatment type (e.g., "Permanent Eyeliner for Jane Doe").
2. Select the category of the treatment from the dropdown menu (e.g., "Makeup Forms").
3. Provide a detailed description of the treatment and its details.
4. If applicable, select the date you obtained the client's permission to use their photographs and videos.
5. Choose any applicable consents from the list (e.g., "I have read and understood the above." and "I give my consent to use my photographs and videos").
6. Include the client's signature, which confirms their understanding and agreement with the procedure.
7. The business signature is for our staff to sign, indicating their acknowledgment of the client's consent.
8. Finally, date the form to record the completion date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text, required): Enter the title of the treatment or the client's name to help us identify the document.
* **form_category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the treatment from the dropdown menu (e.g., "Makeup Forms").
* **form_description** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the treatment, including its details and any important information.
* **form_client_permission** (date, optional): If applicable, select the date you obtained the client's permission to use their photographs and videos.
* **form_consent** (select_multiple, optional): Choose any applicable consents from the list (e.g., "I have read and understood the above." and "I give my consent to use my photographs and videos").
* **form_client_signature** (note, optional): The client signs here to confirm their understanding and agreement with the procedure.
* **form_business_signature** (text, optional): Our staff signs here to acknowledge the client's consent.
* **form_date** (date, optional): Date the form to record the completion date.
