# Permanent Makeup Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Client Information Form is a tool designed to collect essential information from clients before providing permanent makeup services. This form helps to gather details about the client's identity, contact information, and medical history to ensure accurate and effective services are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Client Information** (Client Information, text, required/optional): This section is for you to provide your name, address, and other relevant details.
2.  **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): In this section, please provide your contact number and any other preferred contact method.
3.  **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): This section is for you to describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your experience with the permanent makeup services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information)**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant details to help us understand your needs.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your contact number and any other preferred contact method so we can get in touch with you about your appointments or services.
* **Medical History (Medical History)**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your experience with permanent makeup services. This will help us provide the best possible services for you.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide the best possible services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* We will review and process your information carefully and securely.
