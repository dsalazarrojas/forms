# Lip Product Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lip Product Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers to determine their lip product preferences and subscription frequency. This form is used to create a subscription plan tailored to the customer's needs, ensuring they receive the right products on a schedule that works for them. Please note that this form is only for customers interested in subscribing to lip products, and if you are not, please do not proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lip Product Subscription Form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Select how often you would like to receive your lip products by choosing from the options provided (Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
2.  Choose the type of lip product you are interested in (Lipstick, Lip gloss, Lip liner, or Lip balm).
3.  Enter your preferred start date for the subscription.
4.  Enter your preferred end date for the subscription (if you have one).
5.  Enter your email address for communication purposes.
6.  Enter your phone number for any additional requests or questions.
7.  Leave any additional notes about your subscription preferences or any other information you think would be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you want to receive your lip product?** (`subscription_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your lip product subscription. This will determine how often you will receive your lip products.
* **What lip product are you interested in?** (`lip_product_preference`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type(s) of lip product you would like to subscribe to. You can select more than one type if desired.
* **Start date**: (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to start your subscription.
* **End date**: (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to end your subscription, if you have a specific end date in mind.
* **Customer Email**: (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for any communication or updates about your subscription.
* **Customer Phone**: (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional requests or questions you may have.
* **Notes**: (`customer_notes`, note, required: false): Leave any additional notes or information about your subscription preferences.

Tips:
* Please make sure to enter the correct email and phone number to ensure timely communication and updates.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission and create a personalized subscription plan tailored to your preferences.
