# jolene_nails_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability release and waiver form used to gather information from patients seeking medical treatment during the COVID-19 pandemic. It is essential to understand the risks and limitations associated with seeking care during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, address, and date of birth to identify yourself as a patient.
2. Provide your medical practice name and address (if applicable).
3. Enter your medical contact information (if applicable).
4. Review and acknowledge the liability release and waiver statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (required, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Address** (required, text): Enter your current address or mailing address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (not required, date): If you are under 18 years old, please enter your date of birth. This field is not required, but it helps the medical practice to identify you correctly.
* **Medical Practice** (not required, text): If you are a patient of a specific medical practice, enter their name.
* **Medical Practice Address** (not required, text): If you are a patient of a specific medical practice, enter their address.
* **Medical Contact Info** (not required, text): If you have a preferred contact method for your medical practice, enter their contact information.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a clear understanding of the risks and limitations associated with seeking medical treatment during the COVID-19 pandemic.
* Review and acknowledge the liability release and waiver statement carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the medical practice for clarification.
