# Personalized Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers find personalized makeup recommendations based on their skin tone, hair color, eye color, and style preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Skin Tone**. This will help us understand your natural skin undertones.
   * Select one of the options: fair, medium, tan, dark, or very dark.
2. Please select your **Hair Color**. This will help us understand how your hair interacts with your skin tone.
   * Choose one of the options: blonde, brown, red, black, or grey.
3. Please select all the **Eye Colors** that you have. This will help us understand your eye color palette.
   * Choose one or more of the options: blue, green, hazel, brown, or grey.
4. Please select your **Skin Tone Preference**. This will help us understand how you like your skin to look.
   * Choose one of the options: fair, medium, tan, dark, or very dark.
5. Please select your **Style**. This will help us understand your makeup style and preferences.
   * Choose one of the options: natural, dramatic, bold, classic, or romantic.
6. Please select all the **Recommended Products** that you are interested in. This will help us provide you with personalized recommendations.
   * Choose one or more of the options: Foundation, Concealer, Blush, Eyeshadow, or Lipstick.
7. Add any **Notes** if you have any preferences or allergies we should be aware of.
   * This is an open text field where you can add any relevant information.
8. Provide your **Email**. This is where we will send you our personalized recommendations.
   * Please enter a valid email address.
9. Finally, click on **Submit**.
