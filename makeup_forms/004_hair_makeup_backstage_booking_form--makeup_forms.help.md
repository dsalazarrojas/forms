# Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form is designed to collect information about your upcoming event to ensure we provide you with the best hair and makeup services possible. This form is to be filled out by clients or event coordinators who wish to book a hair and makeup team for their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety to ensure we have all necessary information about your event.
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, especially when providing descriptions and contact information.
3. Double-check that you have entered all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your contract or agreement with us.
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you with booking updates and confirmations.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Date**: Select the date of your event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event.
* **Time**: Select the time of your event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your event, including any specific requirements or needs.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are booking us for (Wedding, Photoshoot, TV/Film, Commercial, or Event).
* **Crew Size**: Enter the expected number of crew members you will require for your event.
* **Equipment Needed**: Select the makeup kits and equipment you will need for your event.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted for any questions or concerns (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Event Date**: Select the date of your event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of your event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Crew Experience**: Provide any information about the experience level of your crew.
* **Event Length**: Enter the expected length of your event.
* **Event Notes**: Provide any additional notes or specific requirements for your event.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person we will be contacting.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person we will be contacting.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person we will be contacting.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have entered all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
* We will review your form submission and follow up with you to confirm your booking.
