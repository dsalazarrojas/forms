# Permanent Makeup Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and document information about permanent makeup treatments. It is meant to be used for keeping track of each treatment session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "treatment_area" field and enter the area of the body that was treated (e.g. eyebrows, eyeliner, etc.).
2. In the "procedure_date" field, enter the date of the procedure in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Enter the client's name in the "client_name" field.
4. Enter the technician's name in the "technician_name" field.
5. In the "treatment_notes" field, enter any additional notes about the treatment.
6. If a follow-up is required, enter the date in the "follow_up_date" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **treatment_area** (`treatment_area`, text, optional): Enter the area of the body that was treated. For example, if you treated the eyes, please enter "eyebrows" or "eyeliner".
* **procedure_date** (`procedure_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the procedure in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **client_name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **technician_name** (`technician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the treatment.
* **treatment_notes** (`treatment_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the treatment, such as the type of procedure, product used, or any issues that arose.
* **follow_up_date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If a follow-up is required, enter the date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
