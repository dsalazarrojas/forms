# Covergirl Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential models for the Covergirl brand. It will be reviewed by the brand team to determine suitability for brand ambassador roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact phone" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your work experience in the "experience" field.
6. List any relevant links to your portfolio in the "portfolio" field.
7. Select your primary social media platform from the "social links" field. You may choose one or multiple options.
8. Select relevant search terms that describe your expertise in the "search terms" field. You may choose one or multiple options.
9. Enter any additional search keywords related to your expertise in the "search keywords" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (text, required): Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **last name** (text, required): Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (email, required): A valid email address for communication.
* **contact phone** (text, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **experience** (note, optional): A brief description of your relevant work experience.
* **portfolio** (note, optional): A list of links to your relevant portfolio.
* **social links** (select_one, optional): Select your primary social media platform.
	+ Instagram
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ LinkedIn
	+ YouTube
	+ TikTok
	+ Twitch
	+ Pinterest
	+ Reddit
	+ Snapchat
* **search terms** (select_multiple, optional): Select relevant terms that describe your expertise.
	+ makeup
	+ beauty
	+ model
	+ brand ambassador
	+ search terms
* **search keywords** (text, optional): Any additional search keywords related to your expertise.
