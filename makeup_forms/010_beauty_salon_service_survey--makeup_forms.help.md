# Beauty Salon Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience at our beauty salon. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the salon.
- **Primary Service Received**: Choose the service you received during your visit, such as hair cut or style, hair color, makeup application, or facial or skin treatment.
- **Name of Service Professional**: If you received service from a specific professional, enter their name.
- **Overall Satisfaction**: Answer a question about your overall satisfaction with the service, using a scale of 1-5.
- **Professional Skill and Knowledge Rating**: Rate the service professional's skill and knowledge.
- **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the salon.
- **Booking Ease**: Answer how easy or difficult it was to book your appointment.
- **Promptness of Visit**: Choose whether you were seen immediately, within 10 minutes, 10-20 minutes, or more than 20 minutes after your appointment time.
- **Value for Money**: Choose whether you felt the service provided good value for the price.
- **Recommendation Likelihood**: Answer how likely you are to recommend the salon to a friend or family member, using a scale of 0 to 10.
- **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
- **Future Services Interest**: Check boxes for any other services you would be interested in receiving at the salon.
- **Follow-up Permission**: Check whether you would like the salon to contact you about your feedback.
- **Email Address (optional)**: If you would like the salon to follow up with you, enter your email address.
