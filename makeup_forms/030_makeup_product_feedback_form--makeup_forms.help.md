# Makeup Product Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to provide feedback on our makeup products. This feedback is used to help us improve and better serve our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the product ID (if provided) in the "Product Id" field. This is the unique identifier for the product you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Provide your feedback about the product in the "Feedback" field. Please use the note field for any comments or suggestions you may have.
3. Rate your experience with the product using the "Rating" field. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is optional, but it will allow us to contact you directly about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Id** (product_id, number, required: false): This field is where you enter the product's ID, if provided. If you don't know the product's ID, you can skip this field.
* **Feedback** (feedback, note, required: false): This is where you can provide any comments or suggestions you have about the product. Please use this field to tell us about your experience with the product.
* **Rating** (rating, number, required: false): This field is where you rate your experience with the product. Please use the numbers to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): This is an optional field where you can enter your email address. We will use this to contact you directly about your feedback, if needed.
