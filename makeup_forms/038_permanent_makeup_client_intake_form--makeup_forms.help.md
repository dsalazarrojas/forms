# Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients interested in permanent makeup services. It includes fields to gather client details, service requests, contact preferences, payment information, medical history, previous work, skin concerns, medical clearance, consent, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure that you complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
2.  Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information (Page 1)** (`client_info`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Please provide your name, email address, and phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Service Requests (Page 2)** (`service_requests`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Describe the services you are interested in (e.g., microblading, microshading, etc.).
*   **Contact Preferences (Page 3)** (`contact_preferences`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Indicate how you would prefer to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
*   **Payment Information (Page 4)** (`payment_info`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Provide any relevant payment information (e.g., credit card number or payment methods).
*   **Medical History (Page 5)** (`medical_history`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Share any medical history that may impact the permanent makeup services.
*   **Previous Work (Page 6)** (`previous_work`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Show your previous work experience with permanent makeup or a similar artistry field.
*   **Skin Concerns (Page 7)** (`skin_concerns`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Report any skin concerns or conditions you'd like the service providers to be aware of.
*   **Medical Clearance (Page 8)** (`medical_clearance`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Please share any medical clearance information (e.g., a doctor's note).
*   **Consent (Page 9)** (`consent`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Indicate your consent for the services to be performed.
*   **Contact Details (Page 10)** (`contact_details`, `text`, Optional)
    *   Provide additional contact information (e.g., alternate phone numbers).

Please submit the form accurately and completely to ensure that the permanent makeup service providers can best serve your needs.
