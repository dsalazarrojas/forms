# Permanent Makeup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Consent Form is a document that helps us understand your preferences and requirements for our permanent makeup services. It's an essential part of the booking process, and it helps us tailor your experience and deliver the best results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required information (marked as *Required*) to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to select the correct options for your chosen services.
3. Sign the Terms of Services section to confirm your agreement with our policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Please enter your name and other relevant contact details so we can get in touch with you regarding your appointment.
* **Contact Information**: Provide us with your contact details, such as email and phone number, so we can communicate with you effectively.
* **Services**: Select all the permanent makeup services you are interested in. This will help us prepare for your appointment and deliver the best results.
* **Location**: Choose the location where you'd like to receive your permanent makeup services.
* **Date**: Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Time**: Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Duration**: Please provide the duration of the appointment in hours.
* **Price**: This will be the total price for the selected services.
* **Terms of Services**: Read and agree to our terms of services. This includes our cancellation policies, service guarantees, and other essential details.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of services, and that you're ready to proceed with your appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any mistakes or misunderstandings.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
- Please arrive 10-15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to ensure a smooth and stress-free experience.
