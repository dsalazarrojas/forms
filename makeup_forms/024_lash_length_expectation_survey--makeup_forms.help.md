# Lash Length Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your expectations and experiences regarding lash length. This form is designed to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. For each field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for details on what information is required and expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lash Length Expectation Survey** (`lash_length_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. Please fill in a brief description of why you are filling out this form.
* **Lash Style** (`lash_style`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have expectations for your lash length, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Expected Lash Length** (`expected_lash_length`, number, optional): If you have expectations for your lash length, please fill in the length you expect.
* **Actual Lash Length** (`actual_lash_length`, number, optional): If you have experiences with your lash length, please fill in the length you have experienced.
* **Lash Technique** (`lash_technique`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have used any lash technique, and 'No' if you haven't. Multiple options can be selected.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please provide any additional information or comments you have regarding your experiences with your lash length.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please fill in a valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or further clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please fill in a valid phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will use the information you provide here to improve our services.
