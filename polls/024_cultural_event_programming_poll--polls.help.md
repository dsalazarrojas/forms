# Cultural Event Programming Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect details about upcoming cultural events. Event organizers can use this form to provide information about their events to the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your event on the "Main Page" section.
2. Provide the name of your event in the "Event Name" field.
3. Enter the date of your event in the "Event Date" field.
4. Enter the location of your event in the "Event Location" field.
5. Enter the time of your event in the "Event Time" field.
6. Estimate the number of attendees you expect for your event in the "Attendees" field.
7. Provide any additional comments about your event in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter the contact email and phone number of the person responsible for your event in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page**: Select the options that apply to your event:
	+ Yes: This event is happening and you can submit it for inclusion in our cultural event programming poll.
	+ No: This event is not happening or has already occurred.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event, e.g. "Summer Music Festival".
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event, e.g. "City Hall".
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of your event in the format "HH:MM".
* **Attendees**: Estimate the number of people you expect to attend your event.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your event, e.g. "Free admission for kids under 12".
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for your event.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your event.
