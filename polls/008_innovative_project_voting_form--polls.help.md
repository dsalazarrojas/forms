# Innovative Project Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and voting information for an "Innovative Project Voting Form". It aims to gauge user opinions on various projects and collect data for internal evaluation and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project name in the first field.
2. Indicate your project's category (select multiple options as needed).
3. Assign a vote score for the project (if applicable).
4. Provide a rating for the project (if applicable).
5. Leave comments if you'd like to add any additional feedback.
6. Enter the date and time you submitted your response.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Provide your phone number.
9. Add any optional comments if you'd like.
10. Enter the URL of the project.
11. Enter your name as the reviewer of the project.
12. Enter your title and organization.
13. Leave any final comments as the reviewer of the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you're voting on. This is a required field.
* **Project Category**: Select all applicable categories for your project. This field is not required but helps with data analysis.
* **Vote Score**: Assign a score to your project based on your assessment. This field is optional and only applicable if you're voting on a project.
* **Rating**: Provide a rating for your project based on your evaluation. This field is optional and only applicable if you're evaluating a project.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your project. This field is optional.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted your response. This field is required.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you submitted your response. This field is required.
* **E-Mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. This field is required.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is required.
* **Optional Comments**: Add any additional comments you'd like to share. This field is optional.
* **Project URL**: Enter the URL of the project you're evaluating. This field is required.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer of the project. This field is required.
* **Reviewers Title**: Enter your title or position. This field is required.
* **Reviewers Organization**: Enter your organization or company. This field is required.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Leave any final comments as the reviewer of the project. This field is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure reliable data collection.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting your response.
* Ensure you have not already submitted a response for the same project.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your project manager or administrator.
