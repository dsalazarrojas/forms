# University Outreach Program Success Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the University Outreach Program. The poll will help us understand how successful our outreach efforts have been and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official university ID or other university-related documentation.
2. Select the date of the event you are evaluating.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the program.
4. Provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (<name>, text, required: false)**: This field is where you should enter your name as it appears on your official university ID or other university-related documentation.
* **Event Date (<event_date>, date, required: false)**: Select the date of the event you are evaluating from the calendar.
* **Satisfaction Rating (<satisfaction_rating, select_one, required: false)**: Rate your satisfaction with the program by selecting one of the options: Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Comments (<comments, text, required: false)**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the program. This is an optional field, but we appreciate any feedback you can provide to help us improve the program.
