# Customer Digital Banking Trust Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Digital Banking Trust Poll Form is designed to gather information about your experience with our digital banking services. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your trust and satisfaction levels, which in turn will guide our development to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully, as they are designed to be completed in sequence.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the option that best matches your feelings towards the question.
4. You may leave the form at any time, but please save your progress and resume from where you left off.
5. When finished, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Home
* **Home** (`home_page`, text, optional): This page is an introduction to the form and provides a summary of the questions that follow. Please take a moment to read through the questions and think about your experience with our digital banking services.

### Page 2: Introduction
* **Intro** (`introduction`, note, optional): This page provides additional context about the form and its purpose. Take a moment to read through the text and let us know if you have any questions or concerns.

### Page 3: Trust
* **Trust** (`customer_trust`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your level of trust with our digital banking services. Please select the option that best matches your feelings:
	+ Very low
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

### Page 4: Security Confidence
* **Security Confidence** (`security_confidence`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your confidence in the security of your data with our services. Please select all options that apply:
	+ Not very secure
	+ Somewhat secure
	+ Secure

### Page 5: Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your satisfaction with your digital banking experience. Please select the option that best matches your feelings:
	+ Not satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied

### Page 6: Additional Comments (Optional)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This is the last page of the form, where you can leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with our digital banking services. This is your chance to share any further insights or suggestions.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to contact us.
* We value your feedback and look forward to improving our services based on your input.
