# Best Agent Voting Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users to recognize and reward outstanding performance of agents in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Choose the correct ratings for each category based on your experience with the agent.
3. Provide specific examples or reasons why the agent deserves recognition in the "Brief Success Story or Example" and "Additional Comments" fields.
4. Ensure that you are voting for an agent you have worked with and can provide an honest review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we will send confirmation to.
* **Organization or Department** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the department or organization you are associated with.
* **Voting Category** (`voting_category`, select_one, required): Select the category you are voting for (e.g. Sales Agent, Customer Service Agent, etc.).
* **Nominated Agent Full Name** (`nominated_agent_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the agent you are voting for.
* **Agent Company or Agency** (`agent_company`, text, required): Enter the company or agency the nominated agent represents.
* **Agent Location** (`agent_location`, text, optional): Enter the city or region where the nominated agent operates.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the nominated agent's overall performance (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Professionalism Rating** (`professionalism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the nominated agent's professionalism (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Skills Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the nominated agent's communication skills (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Responsiveness Rating** (`responsiveness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the nominated agent's responsiveness (Very Responsive, Mostly Responsive, Somewhat Responsive, Slow to Respond, Unresponsive).
* **Knowledge and Expertise Rating** (`knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the nominated agent's knowledge in their field (Expert Level, Very Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Somewhat Knowledgeable, Lacks Knowledge).
* **Duration of Relationship** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your professional relationship with the agent (Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 2 years, 2 to 5 years, More than 5 years).
* **Would You Recommend This Agent** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would recommend this agent to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Standout Qualities** (`standout_qualities`, select_multiple, required): Select the standout qualities of the nominated agent (e.g. Exceptional Communication, Deep Industry Knowledge, etc.).
* **Brief Success Story or Example** (`success_story`, text, optional): Share a brief example of how this agent helped you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other reasons why this agent deserves recognition.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Only vote for agents you have worked with and can provide an honest review.
- Do not vote for agents you have not worked with.
- If you are not sure or have no opinion, please select "Not Sure" or "Lacks Knowledge" for the relevant rating questions.
- Please provide specific examples or reasons why the agent deserves recognition.
