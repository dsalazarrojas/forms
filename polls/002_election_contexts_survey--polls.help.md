# Election Contexts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Election Contexts Survey is a form designed to gather information on end-users' perceptions of elections, voter participation, and the fairness of the process. The survey aims to collect data that can inform future improvement of the election process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant options for each question, and fill in any text fields as required.
* For the select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options from the given list.
* For the number field, enter a numerical value as prompted.
* For the text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
* Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to save your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Election Contexts Survey Form** (<type: text>): This is the title of the survey, which you do not need to answer. It's here to provide context.
* **Voter Participation** (<type: select_one>): Please select whether you have participated in the election process or not. You can choose from the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Information Sources** (<type: select_multiple>): Please select which sources you used to gather information during the election process. You can choose multiple options. You can choose from the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Election Fairness** (<type: number>): Please rate the fairness of the election process on a scale of 0-10.
* **Researcher Interest** (<type: text>): Provide a brief description of why you might be interested in researching the election context.
* **NGO Organization** (<type: text>): Provide a brief description of your non-governmental organization (NGO) or affiliation.
* **Civic Group** (<type: text>): Provide a brief description of your civic group affiliation.
* **Data Analysis** (<type: time>): If applicable, provide a time frame or period for data analysis.
* **Output File** (<type: text>): If applicable, provide a link or reference to an output file related to the survey.

Note: This guide is intended to provide a clear understanding of the form's purpose and functionality. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to ask.
