# Municipal Referendum Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and feedback from citizens on a municipal referendum. The purpose of this form is to collect data on how citizens view a proposed referendum, allowing the municipal authorities to gauge public opinion and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Provide your age in the "What is your age" field.
3. For the "Do you agree to the referendum" field, select one of the available options.
4. If you want to provide a specific date for when you think the referendum should be implemented, you can enter it in the "When do you think it should be implemented" field.
5. If you want to specify a time for when you think the referendum will end, you can enter it in the "When do you think it will end" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "What is your contact email" field.
7. Provide your phone number in the "What is your phone number" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the referendum in the "What is your note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (text): Enter your full name to help us know who you are.
* **What is your age** (number): Provide your age to help us know your demographic.
* **Do you agree to the referendum** (select_one): Select one of the available options to express your opinion on the referendum.
	+ Option 1: I agree to the referendum.
	+ Option 2: I do not agree to the referendum.
	+ Option 3: Undecided.
* **When do you think it should be implemented?** (date): If you think the referendum should be implemented on a specific date, enter it here.
* **When do you think it will end?** (time): If you think the referendum will end on a specific time, enter it here.
* **What is your contact email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your phone number** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your note** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the referendum.
