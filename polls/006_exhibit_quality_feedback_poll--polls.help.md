# Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll is a survey intended to gather end-users' impressions and opinions on various aspects of an exhibit, such as exhibit content, ambiance, staff interaction, and facilities. The collected feedback will help improve the overall quality of exhibits in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll" form.
2. Choose from the following steps:
	* Fill in all fields, as the form is not mandatory, but it will be helpful to provide as much detail as possible.
	* Select the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll** (`exhibit_quality_feedback`, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can express your overall impression of the exhibit.
* **Overall Quality Rating** (`overall_quality_rating`, number, Optional): Please rate the quality of the exhibit on a scale from 1 to 5. The higher the number, the better the quality of the exhibit.
* **Exhibit Content** (`exhibit_content`, text, Optional): Describe the content of the exhibit, including any notable features or shortcomings.
* **Exhibit Ambiance** (`exhibit_ambiance`, text, Optional): Rate the ambiance of the exhibit, including the comfort, decor, and overall atmosphere.
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, text, Optional): Comment on the quality of interaction with staff, including friendliness, helpfulness, and overall service.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, Optional): Describe the facilities provided, including amenities, availability, and overall condition.
* **Time** (`time`, time, Optional): Indicate the time spent at the exhibit.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Optional): Indicate the date you visited the exhibit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up or to receive updates about the exhibit.

## Tips

* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the "Exhibit Quality Feedback Poll" field.
* Please provide as much detail as possible, as this will help us better understand your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It's invaluable to us.
