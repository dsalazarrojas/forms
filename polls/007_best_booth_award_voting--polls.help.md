# Best Booth Award Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from event attendees on their experiences with different booths at events or trade shows.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and email address to receive a confirmation of your vote.
2. Enter the name of the event you are attending.
3. Specify the booth number or location you are voting for.
4. Rate the booth's design, visual appeal, staff engagement, interactivity, and overall experience.
5. Choose the most memorable features about the booth.
6. Select the reason(s) you chose this booth.
7. Provide additional comments or feedback about the booth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (email): We will send a confirmation to this email address.
* **Company Name** (text): If you work for a company, enter its name.
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event or trade show you are attending.
* **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event or trade show you are attending.
* **Booth Number or Location** (text): Enter the booth number or location you are voting for.
* **Company Represented at Booth** (text): Enter the name of the company represented at the booth you are voting for.
* **Booth Category** (select one): Select the category that best describes the booth's main focus or area of expertise. Options include:
	+ Best Overall Design
	+ Most Innovative Display
	+ Best Interactive Experience
	+ Best Product Demonstration
	+ Most Engaging Staff
	+ Best Use of Technology
	+ Best Sustainable Booth
	+ People Choice Award
* **Booth Design Rating** (select one): Rate the overall design of the booth, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Visual Appeal Rating** (select one): Rate the visual appeal of the booth, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Staff Engagement Rating** (select one): Rate the staff's engagement, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Interactivity and Engagement** (select one): Rate the level of interactivity, from Very Interactive to Not Interactive at All.
* **Most Memorable Features** (select multiple): Choose the features that stood out to you the most about this booth. Options include:
	+ Eye-catching Design
	+ Interactive Displays
	+ Product Demonstrations
	+ Giveaways and Promotions
	+ Knowledgeable Staff
	+ Technology Integration
	+ Comfortable Space
	+ Clear Branding
	+ Unique Concept
	+ Other
* **Overall Visit Experience** (select one): Rate your overall experience at the booth, from Exceptional to Below Average.
* **Would You Visit This Booth Again** (select one): If you were at a future event, would you visit this booth again? Options include:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Why Did You Choose This Booth** (text): Explain why you chose this booth.
* **Additional Comments** (text): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the booth.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Rate your answers honestly and accurately.
* If you have more than one booth to vote for, you may repeat the form for each booth.
