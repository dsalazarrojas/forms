# Historical Society Exhibit Theme Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Society is seeking your input to curate a captivating and informative exhibit. This form is designed to collect your suggestions on exhibit themes that align with the Historical Society's mission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide specific and detailed answers for each field.
4. Submit your completed form for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **voting_for_exhibits**: 
  * (Text, Optional) 
  * Please provide a brief description of the exhibit theme you're voting for. This could be a broad category, such as "Ancient Civilizations," or a specific historical figure, like "Abraham Lincoln."
* **historical_figures**: 
  * (Select One, Optional) 
  * Select one historical figure that interests you the most. Examples: 
    * historical figure 1
    * historical figure 2
    * historical figure 3
* **historical_figures**: 
  * (Select Multiple, Optional) 
  * Select all historical figures that interest you. Examples: 
    * historical figure 2
    * historical figure 3
    * historical figure 4
    * historical figure 5
* **historical_periods**: 
  * (Select One, Optional) 
  * Choose a historical period that aligns with your interests. Examples: 
    * historical period 1
    * historical period 2
    * historical period 3
* **historical_events**: 
  * (Text, Optional) 
  * Provide a brief description of historical events that interest you. This could be a series of events or a single event.
* **historical_events_1**: 
  * (Text, Optional) 
  * Similar to "historical_events," provide another brief description of historical events.
* **year**: 
  * (Text, Optional) 
  * Suggest a specific year or a range of years that interest you.
* **year_1**: 
  * (Select One, Optional) 
  * Choose a year that aligns with the historical event or period you selected.
* **notes**: 
  * (Note, Optional) 
  * Add any additional comments or notes about the historical event or period you're suggesting.

## Tips
* Be specific and detailed in your answers to help the Historical Society curate an engaging and informative exhibit.
* If you're unsure about a field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* Remember, the more thoughtful and creative your suggestions, the better the exhibit will be.
