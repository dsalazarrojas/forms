# public_park_renovation_poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and opinions from the general public on the renovation of public parks. It aims to inform decision-makers about the community's preferences for park upgrades and renovations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred park location from the list.
2. Choose your favorite park facilities by selecting all that apply.
3. Indicate the renovation budget you would be willing to support.
4. Prioritize the upgrades you would like to see in the park.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Community Input" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Park Location** (`park_location`, select_one, required): Select the public park you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Favorite Park Facilities** (`favorite_facilities`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the facilities you would like to see in the renovated park. (e.g., Park Bench, Public Restroom, Playground, Picnic Table)
* **Renovation Budget** (`renovation_budget`, number, required): Indicate the maximum budget you would be willing to support for the park renovation.
* **Park Upgrades** (`park_upgrades`, select_multiple, required): Prioritize the upgrades you would like to see in the park. (e.g., Park Benches, Playground, Public Restrooms, Park Lighting)
* **Community Input** (`community_input`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the park renovation.
