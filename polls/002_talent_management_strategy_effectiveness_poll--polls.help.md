# Talent Management Strategy Effectiveness Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees on the effectiveness of the company's talent management strategy. It will help us identify areas of improvement and gather suggestions for future talent management initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your experience with the company's talent management strategy.
2. For fields with dropdown or select options, choose the one that best aligns with your experience.
3. Enter your comments, suggestions, or feedback in the text fields.
4. Fill in your contact information (email and phone number).
5. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recruiting** (`talent_management_recruiting`, select_one): This question asks about your experience with the company's recruiting practices, such as how effectively the company is able to find and attract new talent.
* **Onboarding** (`onboarding`, select_one): This question is about your experience with the onboarding process, including how well the company integrates new employees into the team.
* **Development** (`development`, text): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you have about the company's development opportunities, such as training, mentorship, or career advancement.
* **Performance Practices** (`performance_practices`, select_one): This question is about your experience with performance evaluation and feedback practices, including how well they align with company goals and objectives.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, date): This field is for you to record the date you last checked in with your manager about your performance or development goals.
* **Time Spent Learning** (`time_spent_learning`, time): This field is for you to estimate the time you spend learning new skills or attending training sessions each week.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the company's talent management strategy.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text): This field is for you to enter your department name if you are not sure about your company size.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): This field is for you to enter your job title.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one): This question is about which team you are on at the company.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple): Select any departments you are on or have experience with.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text): This field is for any additional thoughts or comments you have about the company's talent management strategy.
* **Performance Metrics** (`performance_metrics`, text): This field is for you to enter any performance metrics or goals you have been set.
* **Employee Experience** (`employee_experience`, select_one): This question is about how long you've been with the company.
* **Feedback on Strategies** (`feedback_on_strategies`, select_multiple): Select any areas of the company's talent management strategy that you have feedback on.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): This field is for any suggestions you have for improving the company's talent management strategy.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one): This question is about the size of the company you are working for.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one): This question is about the industry your company is in.
