# App Feature Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is a step-by-step explanation of the App Feature Interest Poll form. The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their interests and preferences regarding various features of our app. This information will be used to inform our product development and feature prioritization process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best suits your preferences.
2. For the "Which single feature is your top priority?" question, please choose one of the options listed (Dark Mode, Offline Access, Social Integration, Advanced Analytics, or Other).
3. If you have any other feature suggestions, please enter them in the "Any other feature you think is missing?" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Are you a current user of our app?**: Please select one of the options to indicate your current user status (Yes, daily user, Yes, occasional user, No, but I'm interested, No, former user).
* **Which of these would you like to see most?**: This is a placeholder field for any new features. Please do not fill out this field as it is not relevant to your current experience.
* **Interest in Native Dark Mode**: Please select one of the options to indicate your level of interest in having a native dark mode feature (High Interest, Low Interest, No Interest).
* **Interest in Full Offline Access**: Please select one of the options to indicate your level of interest in having full offline access (High Interest, Low Interest, No Interest).
* **Interest in Social Media Sharing and Integration**: Please select one of the options to indicate your level of interest in integrating social media sharing and integration features (High Interest, Low Interest, No Interest).
* **Interest in Personal Advanced Analytics Dashboard**: Please select one of the options to indicate your level of interest in having an advanced analytics dashboard (High Interest, Low Interest, No Interest).
* **Which single feature is your top priority?**: Please select one of the options to indicate which single feature is most important to you (Dark Mode, Offline Access, Social Integration, Advanced Analytics, or Other).
* **Would you be willing to pay for a premium version with these features?**: Please select one of the options to indicate your willingness to pay for a premium version with the requested features (Yes, definitely, Possibly, depending on price, False).
* **How often do you use similar apps?**: Please select one of the options to indicate how often you use similar apps (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Any other feature you think is missing?**: Please enter any other feature suggestions you have in this text field.
