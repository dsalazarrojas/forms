# Marketing Campaign Reaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers or users about a marketing campaign, specifically to gauge their reaction to a particular offer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **What do you think of this offer**: This is a text field where you can write your thoughts and opinions about the marketing campaign.
2. **Which channel do you interact with most with our brand?**: This is a multiple-choice field where you can select the channel you use to interact with our brand the most. The options are Social Media, Email, and Phone.
3. **How would you rate the channel performance?**: This is a numeric field where you can rate the performance of the channel on a scale of your choice.
4. **How do you feel about this offer?**: This is a multiple-choice field where you can select how you feel about the offer. The options are Positive, Negative, and Neutral.
5. **Intent to act on this offer**: This is a numeric field where you can indicate your intention to act on the offer.
6. **Can we contact you by email?**: This is an email field where you can input your email address if you are willing to be contacted.
7. **Can we contact you by phone?**: This is a text field where you can input your phone number if you are willing to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think of this offer**: This field is for you to express your thoughts and opinions about the marketing campaign.
* **Which channel do you interact with most with our brand?**: This field helps us understand your preferred channel of interaction.
* **How would you rate the channel performance?**: This field helps us measure the performance of the channel.
* **How do you feel about this offer?**: This field helps us gauge your sentiment towards the offer.
* **Intent to act on this offer**: This field helps us determine your willingness to act on the offer.
* **Can we contact you by email?**: This field allows us to gather your email address if you are willing to be contacted.
* **Can we contact you by phone?**: This field allows us to gather your phone number if you are willing to be contacted.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the "What do you think of this offer" field.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "What do you think of this offer" field.
* Please select all that apply for the "How do you feel about this offer" field.
* If you are willing to be contacted by phone, please input your phone number in the "Can we contact you by phone" field.
* If you are willing to be contacted by email, please input your email address in the "Can we contact you by email" field.
