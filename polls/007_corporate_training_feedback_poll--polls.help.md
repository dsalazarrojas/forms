# Corporate Training Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who attended a workshop or corporate training session. The goal is to understand their experience, what they learned, and what they would like to see improved in future sessions. Your input will help us tailor our training programs to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop or training session you attended.
2. **Feedback**: Select one of the options regarding your experience with the workshop. 
3. **What did you learn?**: Share with us what new knowledge, skills, or insights you gained during the workshop. 
4. **Additional Feedback (if needed)**: If you have more to say, use this text area to provide your thoughts.
5. **Email (if provided)**: If you have an email address associated with your workshop registration, please enter it so we can follow up with you.
6. **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number, enter it so we can get in touch with you.
7. **Workshop Attended**: Select the workshop you attended from the options provided.
8. **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you attended. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
9. **Start/End Time**: Enter the start and end time of the workshop you attended (format: 12:HH AM/PM)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workshop_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the workshop you attended, e.g., "Team Leadership Training" or "Marketing Strategy".
* **how_was_the_workshop (Select One)**: This is your opportunity to tell us about your experience with the workshop. Choose one of the options provided.
* **what_did_you_learn (Text)**: Share with us what new knowledge, skills, or insights you gained during the workshop.
* **what_did_you_learn (Note)**: If you have more to add to your previous answer, use this space to provide additional details.
* **email (Email)**: If you have an email address associated with your workshop registration, please enter it so we can follow up with you.
* **contact_number (Text)**: Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.
* **workshop_attended (Select One)**: Choose the workshop you attended from the provided options.
* **workshop_date (Date)**: Enter the date of the workshop you attended (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **start_time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the workshop (format: 12:HH AM/PM).
* **end_time (Time)**: Enter the end time of the workshop (format: 12:HH AM/PM).
