# Dealership Maintenance Service Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers who have visited our dealership for maintenance services. We use this feedback to improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Please note that fields marked as "required" are mandatory and need to be completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was your overall experience at the Dealership?
* **What was your overall experience at the Dealership** (`Introduction`, `text`, `required`): Please provide a brief summary of your overall experience at the dealership. Share any thoughts, positive or negative, about your visit.

### Was your vehicle's maintenance service completed on time?
* **Was your vehicle's maintenance service completed on time?** (`maintenance`, `select_one`, `false`): Please select one of the options below to indicate whether your vehicle's maintenance service was completed on time.

* True
* False
* Not applicable

### Additional feedback
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, `false`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about your experience, please share them here.

### How would you rate the contact person's communication style?
* **How would you rate the contact person's communication style?** (`contact`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Please select all that apply to rate the contact person's communication style.

* Effective
* Not effective
* Neutral

### How would you rate your experience at the dealership?
* **How would you rate your experience at the dealership?** (`rating`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Please select all that apply to rate your experience at the dealership.

* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### What was the primary reason for your rating?
* **What was the primary reason for your rating?** (`rating_details`, `text`, `false`): If you selected a rating other than "Excellent", please provide a brief explanation of the primary reason for your rating.

### Visit details
* **Visit details** (`visit_details`, `text`, `false`): Please provide any additional details about your visit that might be helpful in understanding your experience.

### What type of vehicle was your visit for?
* **What type of vehicle was your visit for?** (`vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Please select all that apply to indicate the type of vehicle you visited the dealership for.

* Car
* Truck
* Motorcycle
* SUV
* Other

### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, `required`): Please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Please enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): Please enter your phone number (optional).

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `false`): Any additional comments or suggestions about your experience can be provided here.

Tips:

* Please answer the questions truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you have more than one visit to the dealership.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
