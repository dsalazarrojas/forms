# Business Support Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Business Support Satisfaction Poll" form, which is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the business support team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
4. Select the time frame you have been a customer of the business in the "How Long Have You Been Our Customer" field.
5. Rate the response time of the business support team in the "How Would You Rate Our Support Response Time" field.
6. Rate the quality of the support services provided in the "Quality of Support Services" field.
7. Rate the professionalism of the business support staff in the "Professionalism of Our Staff" field.
8. Select the most helpful aspect of the support experience in the "Most Helpful Aspect of Our Support" field (optional).
9. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments or Concerns" field (optional).
10. Rate the likelihood of continuing to use the business services in the "Likelihood to Continue Using Our Services" field.
11. Indicate if you would recommend the business to others in the "Would You Recommend Us to Other Businesses" field.
12. Finally, indicate your overall satisfaction with the business support team in the "Likelihood of Our Support" field.
