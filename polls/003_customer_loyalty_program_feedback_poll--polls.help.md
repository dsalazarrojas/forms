# Customer Loyalty Program Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the customer loyalty program to help us understand your experience and preferences. Your input will help us improve the program and provide a better service to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and program name in the respective fields.
2. Rate your feedback experience with the program by selecting one of the options.
3. Choose the reason for your feedback by selecting one or more options.
4. Provide any suggestions you have for improving the program.
5. Choose whether you want to be contacted for follow-up on this feedback.
6. Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted by us.
7. Finally, add any additional comments you have about your experience with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer loyalty program you are providing feedback on.
* **Your Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for follow-up on this feedback.
* **Select the Reason for Your Feedback** (`feedback_reason`, select_one, required: false): Select the reason why you are giving this feedback. Options are: 
  + Excellent
  + Good
  + Fair
  + Poor
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **Want to be Contacted for Follow-up?** (`contact_us`, select_one, required: true): Yes or No. If you select "Yes", we will contact you for follow-up on this feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted by us.
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your experience with the program.
