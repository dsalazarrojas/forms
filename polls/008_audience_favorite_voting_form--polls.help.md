# Audience Favorite Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from attendees of a performance or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Your Name" field. This is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer to vote anonymously.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer not to receive prize notifications.
3.  Enter the name of the performance or event in the "Event Name" field.
4.  Enter the date of the performance or event in the "Event Date" field.
5.  Enter the location of the performance or event in the "Event Location" field.
6.  Select your category from the "Voter Category" dropdown menu.
7.  Select your favorite performance from the "Favorite Performance" dropdown menu.
8.  Enter the name of your favorite performer in the "Favorite Actor or Performer" field.
9.  For the "Best Supporting Performance" field, enter the name of the performer who gave the standout supporting performance.
10. Rate the costume design, lighting, sound, and set design using the corresponding dropdown menus.
11. Rate your overall experience using the "Overall Rating" dropdown menu.
12. Indicate whether you would recommend this event to others using the "Would Recommend" dropdown menu.
13. If you'd like to enter the prize draw, select "Yes, enter me in the draw" from the "Prize Draw Entry" dropdown menu.
14. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (text): Enter your name. This is optional, and you can leave it blank for anonymous voting.
*   **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address. This is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer not to receive prize notifications.
*   **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the performance or event.
*   **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the performance or event.
*   **Event Location** (text): Enter the location of the performance or event.
*   **Voter Category** (select_one): Select what best describes you:
    *   General Audience
    *   Industry Professional
    *   Press or Media
    *   Student
    *   Performer or Cast Member
    *   Event Staff
*   **Favorite Performance** (select_one): Select your favorite performance:
    *   Opening Number
    *   Act One Finale
    *   Act Two Opening
    *   Closing Number
    *   Special Guest Performance
    *   Ensemble Piece
*   **Favorite Actor or Performer** (text): Enter the name of your favorite performer.
*   **Best Supporting Performance** (text): Enter the name of the standout supporting performer.
*   **Best Costume Design** (select_one): Rate the costume design:
    *   Excellent
    *   Very Good
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Best Lighting Design** (select_one): Rate the lighting design:
    *   Excellent
    *   Very Good
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Best Sound Design** (select_one): Rate the sound design:
    *   Excellent
    *   Very Good
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Best Set Design** (select_one): Rate the set design:
    *   Excellent
    *   Very Good
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Overall Rating** (select_one): Rate your overall experience:
    *   5 Stars - Excellent
    *   4 Stars - Very Good
    *   3 Stars - Good
    *   2 Stars - Fair
    *   1 Star - Poor
*   **Would Recommend** (select_one): Would you recommend this event to others?
    *   Definitely yes
    *   Probably yes
    *   Not sure
    *   Probably no
    *   Definitely no
*   **Prize Draw Entry** (select_one): Would you like to enter the prize draw?
    *   Yes, enter me in the draw
    *   No, thanks
*   **Additional Comments** (text): Provide any other feedback or suggestions.
*   **Prize Draw Entry** (select_one): You can choose to enter the prize draw or not.
