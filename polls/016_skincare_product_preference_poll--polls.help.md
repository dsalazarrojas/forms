# Skincare Product Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Product Preference Poll form is designed to collect information about your skincare habits, preferences, and experiences. This survey aims to gather insights that can help us understand your needs and preferences in the skincare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the answers that best fit your skincare habits and preferences.
3. Fill in any additional comments or questions you have.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what to expect.
* **What skincare products do you use?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the skincare products you currently use or have used in the past.
* **What is your daily skincare routine?** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how frequently you follow a daily skincare routine.
* **How often do you purchase skincare products?** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you purchase skincare products, if at all.
* **What factors do you consider when buying skincare products?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the factors that influence your purchasing decisions.
* **What are your preferences for specific skincare product types?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your preferences for various skincare product types.
* **How much do you typically spend on skincare products?** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your typical expenditure on skincare products, if any.
* **Have you visited a skincare clinic recently?** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you've recently visited a skincare clinic.
* **What factors influence your skincare product decisions?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the factors that influence your product decisions.
* **Which of the following skincare products do you own?** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all skincare products you own or have owned.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* You can select multiple options for most fields.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the space provided.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
