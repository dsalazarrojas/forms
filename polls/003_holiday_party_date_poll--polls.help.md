# Holiday Party Date Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a holiday party, specifically about the event date, time, location, number of attendees, and the list of attendees with their contact information. The data collected will help in planning and organizing the party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the holiday party on the "Event Date" field.
2. Choose the time of the event on the "Event Time" field.
3. Enter the location of the party on the "Event Location" field.
4. Select the number of attendees on the "Attendees Count" field.
5. List the attendees and their contact information on the "Attendees List" field.
6. Enter the email addresses of the attendees on the "Attendees Email" field.
7. Enter the phone numbers of the attendees on the "Attendees Phone" field.
8. Add any additional note on the "Note" field.
9. Choose whether each attendee will attend the party on the "Will Attend" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the holiday party.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the party.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`, number, required: false): Select the number of attendees.
* **Attendees List** (`attendees_list`, select_multiple, required: false): List the attendees and their contact information.
* **Attendees Email** (`attendees_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email addresses of the attendees.
* **Attendees Phone** (`attendees_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone numbers of the attendees.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional note about the party.
* **Will Attend** (`attendees_attended`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether each attendee will attend the party.
