# Social Club Presidential Election Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Club Presidential Election Voting Form is designed to collect user preferences and information in the internal presidential election of the social club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your top choice from the candidates listed.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your membership card.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
4. If you'd like to provide your phone number, you can do so here. (Optional)
5. Select whether you voted "Yes" or "No" for each candidate.
6. Provide any additional comments or other information in the "Other comments" field. (Optional)
7. Select the time you voted.
8. Select the date you voted.
9. Enter the name of the chapter you're affiliated with.
10. Enter the chapter number you're affiliated with.
11. Select your top choice from the list of candidates for each question.
12. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Other comments" field. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select your top choice**: 
  Select one of the candidates listed to indicate your top choice.
* **Name**: 
  Enter your name as it appears on your membership card.
* **Email**: 
  Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Phone**: 
  If you'd like to provide your phone number, please enter it here. This is optional.
* **Vote**: 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for each candidate.
* **Message**: 
  Provide any additional comments or information about your vote.
* **Time**: 
  Select the time you voted.
* **Date**: 
  Select the date you voted.
* **Chapter**: 
  Enter the name of the chapter you're affiliated with.
* **Chapter Number**: 
  Enter the chapter number you're affiliated with.
* **Candidate 1**: 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for the first candidate.
* **Candidate 2**: 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for the second candidate.
* **Candidate 3**: 
  Select "Yes" or "No" for the third candidate.
* **Other comments**: 
  Provide any additional comments or information about your vote. This is optional.
