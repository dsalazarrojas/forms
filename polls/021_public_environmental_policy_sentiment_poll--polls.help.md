# Public Environmental Policy Sentiment Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on public environmental policy sentiment from users. It is intended to measure public opinion on various environmental policies and understand how different groups of people perceive and respond to these policies.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your response to the question "how_do_you_feel" on page 1.
2. On page 2, choose one of the options for "environmental_policy".
3. On page 3, select all the options that apply for "policy_effectiveness".
4. On page 4, enter a numerical value for "environmental_concerns", which indicates the level of concern you have for environmental issues.
5. On page 5, you can add any additional comments you would like to provide.
6. On page 6, select your time zone.
7. On page 7, enter your email address.
8. On page 8, enter your phone number.
9. On page 9, enter the date and time you completed the form.
10. On page 10, enter the time of day you completed the form.
11. On page 11, select your time zone (again).
12. On page 12, enter a time value for "time_of_day".
13. On page 13, enter a time value for "time_zone".
14. On page 14, enter a time value for "time_of_day" (again).
15. On page 15, enter a time value for "time_zone" (again).
16. On page 16, enter your email address (again).
17. On page 17, enter your phone number (again).
18. On page 18, select all the options that apply for "address".
19. On page 19, enter your city.
20. On page 20, enter your state.
21. On page 21, enter your postal code.
22. On page 22, enter your zip code.
23. On page 23, select your country.
24. On page 24, add any additional comments you would like to provide.
25. On page 25, select your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_do_you_feel** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the first question of the form, asking how you feel about public environmental policy. Please select one of the options provided.
* **environmental_policy** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to choose one of the options for environmental policy.
* **policy_effectiveness** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply for policy effectiveness.
* **environmental_concerns** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate your level of concern for environmental issues.
* **additional_comments** (`5`, `note`, required: false): You can add any additional comments you would like to provide.
* **time_zone** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your time zone.
* **contact_email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **date_and_time** (`9`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time you completed the form.
* **time** (`10`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day you completed the form.
* **date** (`11`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **time_of_day** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the time of day you completed the form.
* **time_zone** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your time zone (again).
* **time_of_day** (`14`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day you completed the form (again).
* **time_zone** (`15`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time zone you are in (again).
* **email** (`16`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (again).
* **phone** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (again).
* **address** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply for your address.
* **city** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter your city.
* **state** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter your state.
* **postal_code** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter your postal code.
* **zip** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **country** (`23`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your country.
* **additional_comments** (`24`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments you would like to provide.
* **signature** (`25`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your response is submitted correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to hearing your thoughts on public environmental policy.
