# Vote For Your Favorite Bird - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users cast their vote for their favorite bird species. It is a simple and easy-to-use interface that collects users' preferences and provides a platform for them to share their opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite bird from the list provided in the "Favorite Bird" section.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Optionally, you can add a comment in the "Comments" field.
5. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Favorite Bird
* **Favorite Bird** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the listed bird species that you find the most appealing.
### Name
* **Name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you (if needed).
### Phone (optional)
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you (if needed).
### Comments (optional)
* **Comments** (`note`, optional): Share your thoughts or reasons why you chose your favorite bird.
 
## Tips
* Please select only one favorite bird from the list.
* Please enter a valid email address in the format of "example@example.com".
* Please enter your phone number in the international format, e.g., "+1-123-456-7890".
* You can add or remove lines in the comments field as needed.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.
