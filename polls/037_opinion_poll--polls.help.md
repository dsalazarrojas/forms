# opinion_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions from users on a new product. The gathered feedback will help us understand how users perceive our product and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions on each page, starting with "Introduction" and moving to the last "Comments".
2.  For questions where a rating is requested (e.g., "How would you rate our new product?" and "How would you rate this feature?"), use a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of agreement or satisfaction.
3.  For questions where a selection is requested (e.g., "What is your favorite feature?"), choose one of the listed options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **What is your opinion on our new product?** (`text`, required/optional): Answer this question with your thoughts on the new product. Be as specific as possible.
### Product Rating
* **How would you rate our new product?** (`number`, required/optional): Provide a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction or agreement with our new product.
### Favorite Feature
* **What is your favorite feature?** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the listed features that you like the most about our product.
### Rating Scale 2
* **How would you rate this feature?** (`number`, required/optional): Provide a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction or agreement with the selected feature.
### Comments
* **Do you have any comments?** (`note`, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about our product.
