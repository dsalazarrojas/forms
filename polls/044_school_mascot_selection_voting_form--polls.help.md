# School Mascot Selection Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to select a new school mascot, providing options for voting and submitting their choice.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Select an option on the "Select Mascot" page**: Choose one of the available options for the school's new mascot.
- **Enter the mascot's name**: Provide the full name of the selected mascot, if required.
- **Enter the mascot's image URL**: Provide the URL of the selected mascot's image, if required.
- **Enter the mascot's description**: Provide a brief description of the selected mascot, if required.
- **Cast your vote**: Ensure you've voted for the selected mascot.
- **Submit your selection**: Confirm your choice by submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Mascot**: Choose one of the available options for the school's new mascot.
* **Mascot Name**: Enter the full name of the selected mascot. (Optional)
* **Image URL**: Enter the URL of the selected mascot's image. (Optional)
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the selected mascot. (Optional)
* **Votes**: Ensure you've cast a valid vote for the selected mascot.
* **Submit**: Confirm your choice by selecting "Yes, I've reviewed and submit my vote."

## Tips
- Double-check that you have selected an option on the "Select Mascot" page before submitting the form.
- Ensure you've provided a valid URL for the mascot's image, if required.
- If a field is marked as optional, feel free to leave it blank or fill it in according to your needs.
