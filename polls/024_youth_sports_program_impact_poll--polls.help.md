# Youth Sports Program Impact Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in the Youth Sports Program. Please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible to help us understand the effectiveness of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select multiple and select one questions, choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
3. In the text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Make sure to confirm your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program participation** (`program_participation`, text, optional): Please describe your participation in the Youth Sports Program.
* **Satisfaction with program** (`satisfaction_with_program`, select one, optional): How satisfied are you with the program? Choose 'Satisfied' if you are generally happy with the program, or 'Dissatisfied' if you are not.
* **Frequency of participation** (`frequency_of_participation`, select multiple, optional): How frequently did you participate in the program? Choose 'Yes' if you participated, or 'No' if you did not.
* **Reasons for participating** (`reasons_for_participating`, select multiple, optional): What are your reasons for participating in the program? Choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **Program outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, select multiple, optional): What outcomes did you experience as a result of participating in the program? Choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **Satisfaction with outcomes** (`satisfaction_with_outcomes`, select one, optional): How satisfied are you with the outcomes of the program? Choose 'Satisfied' if you are happy with the outcomes, or 'Dissatisfied' if you are not.
* **Challenges facing** (`challenges_facing`, select multiple, optional): What challenges did you face while participating in the program? Choose one or more options that apply to you.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving the program?
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the program, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm email** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm phone** (`phone_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Please confirm your submission before sending.
