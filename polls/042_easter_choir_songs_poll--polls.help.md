# easter_choir_songs_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions on traditional Easter songs sung in choir. Please take a few minutes to answer the following questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your answers for each question. For select questions, choose one of the options. For text fields, type your answer. For date and time fields, enter the date and time you wish to submit.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields marked with a red asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **song** (select_one, optional): Select whether you like or dislike the song.
* **song_2** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate your preference for song 2.
* **song_3** (text, optional): Describe your thoughts about song 3.
* **singer** (text, optional): Share your favorite singer.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date you want to be associated with your response.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time you want to be associated with your response.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (will not be shared publicly).
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes (will not be shared publicly).
* **song_10** (select_one, optional): Select whether you like or dislike song 10.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're not sure or don't want to answer a question, please skip it.
