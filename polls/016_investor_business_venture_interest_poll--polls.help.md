# Investor Business Venture Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Investor Business Venture Interest Poll" form is designed to collect information from potential investors on their interests and priorities for a business venture. This form helps investors express their thoughts on various aspects of a business, enabling the creator to gauge the potential interest and feasibility of the venture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read through each field carefully and answer according to your interests and priorities. The form is divided into the following sections:

1. Home (Page 1)
2. Investment Areas (Page 2)
3. Priorities (Page 3)
4. Target Market (Page 4)
5. Growth Stage (Page 5)
6. Business Model (Page 6)
7. Industry (Page 7)
8. Location (Page 8)
9. Revenue Range (Page 9)
10. Employee Count (Page 10)
11. Company Type (Page 11)
12. Investor Interest (Page 12)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home** (`Home`, `note`, `required: false`)
	+ This page provides an introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Investment Areas** (`Investment Areas`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`)
	+ Select all the areas where you are interested in investing. This could be a list of industries or sectors.
* **Priorities** (`Priorities`, `select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Choose the priorities that are most important to you when evaluating a business venture.
* **Target Market** (`Target Market`, `text`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the target market you are interested in or have experience with.
* **Growth Stage** (`Growth Stage`, `select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Select the growth stage of a business that interests you, such as startup, growth, or maturity.
* **Business Model** (`Business Model`, `select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Choose the business model that interests you, such as product, service, or subscription.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, `select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Select the industry you are interested in or have experience with.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the location you are interested in or have experience with.
* **Revenue Range** (`Revenue Range`, `number`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the range of revenue you are interested in for a business venture.
* **Employee Count** (`Employee Count`, `number`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the number of employees you are interested in a business having.
* **Company Type** (`Company Type`, `select_one`, `required: false`)
	+ Select the type of company you are interested in, such as private, public, or non-profit.
* **Investor Interest** (`Investor Interest`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`)
	+ Select all the areas where you, as an investor, would be interested in investing.
