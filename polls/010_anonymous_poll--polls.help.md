# Anonymous Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on users' opinions and demographic data on a specific topic. It aims to gauge their opinion, familiarity, and impact of the topic on their daily life.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the poll question carefully and select the option that best represents your opinion.
2. Choose the correct answer for each question, and fill in any additional comments or feedback if necessary.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the data is complete and accurate.
4. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask or provide feedback on the "Any feedback on this poll?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which of the following best represents your opinion?** (`primary_vote`, `select_one`): This question is the main question for this poll. Select the option that best represents your opinion on the topic.
* **Why did you choose this option?** (`vote_reason`, `text`): This is an optional field to provide additional comments on why you selected your chosen option. If you don't have a reason, feel free to leave it blank.
* **Your Age Range** (`respondent_age`, `select_one`): This question asks for your age range. Select the correct option from the list.
* **How much does this topic impact your daily life?** (`impact_level`, `select_one`): This question gauges the impact of the topic on your daily life. Select the option that best represents your answer.
* **How familiar are you with this topic?** (`knowledge_level`, `select_one`): This question asks for your level of familiarity with the topic. Select the option that best represents your answer.
* **Would you participate in more polls like this?** (`future_participation`, `select_one`): This question is optional and asks if you would participate in more polls like this. Select the option that best represents your answer.
* **Suggested topics for future polls** (`suggested_topics`, `text`): This is an optional field to suggest topics for future polls.
* **Any feedback on this poll?** (`poll_feedback`, `text`): This is an optional field to provide any feedback on the poll itself.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the data is complete and accurate.
* If you have any suggestions for future poll topics, please provide them in the "Suggested topics for future polls" field.
* If you have any feedback on the poll, please provide it in the "Any feedback on this poll?" field.
