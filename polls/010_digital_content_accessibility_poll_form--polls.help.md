# Digital Content Accessibility Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Content Accessibility Poll Form" is designed to collect feedback on the accessibility of digital content. This form helps us understand your experiences and difficulties you may have encountered while interacting with digital content. The information you provide will help us improve the accessibility of our digital content for users like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experiences with digital content accessibility.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them in the "Other (please specify)" field.
4. Rate your experience using the scales provided in questions 6 and 7.
5. Finally, let us know if you would like to participate in this research again and how likely you are to recommend our service to a friend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Content Poll Form**: This is the title of the form. Please do not fill this field, as it is for identification purposes only.
* **Which of the following barriers have you encountered** (`question_1`): Choose one or more of the options that best describe the barriers you have encountered while interacting with digital content. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) while clicking on the options.
* **Which of the following barriers have you encountered** (`question_2`): This is similar to question 1, but you can select multiple options.
* **Which of the following barriers have you experienced** (`question_3`): This is the third question that asks about barriers, and you can select one option.
* **Other (please specify)** (`question_4`): If none of the options in questions 1-3 describe your experience, please use this field to provide more details.
* **Is there anything else you would like to mention** (`question_5`): Feel free to share any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **How would you rate this experience?** (`question_6`): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the worst and 5 is the best.
* **How would you rate this experience?** (`question_7`): This is a second question that asks about the same experience, but on a different scale.
* **Would you like to participate in this research again?** (`question_8`): Let us know if you would like to participate in this research again.
* **How likely are you to recommend this service to a friend?** (`question_9`): Rate how likely you are to recommend our service to a friend on a scale provided.
* **How long did you spend on the poll?** (`question_10`): Please provide the amount of time you spent on this poll.
* **Was this the first time you encountered an accessibility issue?** (`question_11`): Select one of the options to answer this question.
* **Was this issue reported previously?** (`question_12`): Select one of the options to answer this question.
* **How often do you encounter accessibility issues?** (`question_13`): Select one of the options to answer this question.

## Tips

* Take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly.
* If you have any difficulty selecting an option, feel free to ask for help.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your contribution to improving digital content accessibility.
