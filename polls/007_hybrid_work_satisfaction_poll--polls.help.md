# Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experience in a hybrid work setting. It aims to understand their preferences and satisfaction levels related to various aspects of their work, such as work location, work schedule, communication channels, and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your actual experience and preferences.
2. Select the options that best match your feelings and experiences.
3. Fill in any text fields with your thoughts and comments.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll**: This is the title of the form.
* **Work Location**: Select your usual work location (Home, Office, Hybrid).
* **How often do you work remotely?**: Answer how often you work remotely (Frequently, Rarely, Occasionally, Never).
* **How satisfied are you with your work?**: Select how satisfied you are with your work (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **What type of work environment do you prefer?**: Choose the type of work environment you prefer (Collaborative and open, Private and quiet, Flexible and adaptable, None of the above).
* **How many hours do you usually work per week?**: Enter the number of hours you usually work per week.
* **How many days do you usually work per week?**: Enter the number of days you usually work per week.
* **How many minutes per day do you usually spend on work?**: Enter the number of minutes you usually spend on work per day.
* **What time management tool do you use?**: Select the time management tool you use (Todoist, Trello, None of the above).
* **What is your preferred way of communication?**: Choose your preferred way of communication (Email, Chat, Phone, None of the above).
* **How often do you receive feedback from your manager?**: Answer how often you receive feedback from your manager (Frequently, Rarely, Occasionally, Never).
* **How often do you collaborate with your team?**: Select how often you collaborate with your team (Frequently, Rarely, Occasionally, Never).
* **How often do you participate in remote meetings?**: Answer how often you participate in remote meetings (Frequently, Rarely, Occasionally, Never).
* **What do you think about work-life balance?**: Select what you think about your work-life balance (Very good, Somewhat good, Neutral, Somewhat bad, Very bad).
* **How satisfied are you with your job?**: Choose how satisfied you are with your job (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **How secure do you feel about your job?**: Select how secure you feel about your job (Very secure, Somewhat secure, Neutral, Somewhat insecure, Very insecure).
* **How dynamic do you think your workplace is?**: Choose how dynamic you think your workplace is (Very dynamic, Somewhat dynamic, Neutral, Somewhat static, Very static).
* **What makes you satisfied or unsatisfied with your job?**: Fill in this field with your thoughts and comments.
* **LK (Note)**: This field is not relevant to this form.
* **MKT (Note)**: This field is not relevant to this form.
* **KPI (Note)**: This field is not relevant to this form.
* **Other**: Select what makes you happy or unhappy with your job (Very happy, Somewhat happy, Somewhat unhappy, Very unhappy).
* **How dynamic do you think your job is?**: Select how dynamic you think your job is (Very dynamic, Somewhat dynamic, Neutral, Somewhat static, Very static).

## Tips

* Please be honest and sincere in your answers.
* If unsure, choose the option that best matches your feelings and experiences.
* If you have any specific comments or thoughts that are not covered in the provided fields, please fill in the "What makes you satisfied or unsatisfied with your job?" field.
* Remember to submit the form when you are finished.
