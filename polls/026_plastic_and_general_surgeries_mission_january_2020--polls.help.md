# plastic_and_general_surgeries_mission_january_2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a post-mission survey for volunteers who participated in the "Plastic and General Surgeries Mission in January 2020" led by Blanca's House.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thank you for volunteering with the Blanca’s House Mission to Loja, Ecuador** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is a greeting message to express gratitude to the volunteers.
* **With your expertise** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please share your areas of expertise or skills that you used during the mission.
* **Commitment** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe your level of commitment to the mission and how you contributed to its success.
* **With your passion** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Share any personal stories or anecdotes that highlight your passion for the mission and its goals.
* **With your help we were able to cross borders** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe a moment or experience where you played a crucial role in achieving the mission's objectives.
* **We value your partnership** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on how you worked collaboratively with the team and other stakeholders during the mission.
* **We want to hear about your experience** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Share any interesting or notable experiences you had during the mission.
* **Please take a few minutes to fill out this Post-Mission Trip Survey** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This field is a reminder to take your time to fill out the survey thoroughly.
* **Your feedback will help improve our mission logistics and procedures and ensure our success** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This field acknowledges the importance of your feedback in improving the mission's processes.
* **Respond to our survey Galo** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This field is a call-to-action to encourage the volunteers to submit their responses. 

## Tips
* Please be sincere and honest when filling out this form.
* Make sure to proofread your responses before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
