# Music Festival Headliner Voting - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your preferences for potential headliners at the Music Festival. Your input will help us determine the most popular headliners among festival-goers. Please provide your honest feedback, and help us create an amazing lineup!

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, simply select the headliner you would like to see at the Music Festival from the list below.
2. You can choose one of the provided options for each headliner.
3. After making your selections, click "Submit" to complete the form.
4. If you need help, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which headliner would you like to see at Music Festival?** (`headliner1`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select one of the provided options for the first headliner you would like to see at the Music Festival. This field is required to ensure we get your honest feedback.
* **Which headliner would you like to see at Music Festival?** (`headliner2`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the options for the second headliner you would like to see at the Music Festival. This field is also required to ensure we get your honest feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address. This field is required so we can contact you with updates and follow-up questions. Please provide a valid email address.

## Tips

* Be sure to select only one option for each headliner.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your honest feedback and look forward to an amazing Music Festival!
