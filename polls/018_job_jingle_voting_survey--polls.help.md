# job_jingle_voting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on a jingle that has been shared in a work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the jingle and its characteristics.
2. Select your options for each field from the provided choices.
3. Enter your rating or feedback in the text fields.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **List Of Jingles** (`jingle_list`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all jingles you have heard, even if you don't have strong opinions about them.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter a score between 1 and 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose how you rate the jingle's quality.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your thoughts or reasons for your rating.
* **Email** (`email_jingle`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Vote** (`vote`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you like or dislike the jingle.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name for us to address you by name.
* **Company** (`company`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the company you work for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your job title for us to understand your perspective.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose your job role.
* **Jingle Length** (`jingle_length`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the length of the jingle.
* **Jingle Quality** (`jingle_quality`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the quality of the jingle.
* **Jingle Tune** (`jingle_tune`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the tune of the jingle.
* **Jingle Style** (`jingle_style`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the style of the jingle.
* **Jingle Tone** (`jingle_tone`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the tone of the jingle.
* **Jingle Mood** (`jingle_mood`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the mood of the jingle.
* **Rating Note** (`rating_note`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about your rating.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional feedback you have.
* **Would You Jingle** (`jingle_like`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you would sing along to the jingle.
* **Would Use** (`jingle_use`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you would use the jingle in your daily life.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose how you rate the jingle's quality again.
* **Job Jingle Voting Survey** (`job_jingle_voting_survey`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose whether you would jingle along.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose how you rate the jingle's quality once more.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Select all fields that apply to you.
* If you have any comments or ratings that don't fit in the provided fields, please use the "Comment" field.
* We value your opinions and look forward to hearing from you!
