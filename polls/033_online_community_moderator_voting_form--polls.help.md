# Online Community Moderator Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Community Moderator Voting Form" is designed to gather feedback and votes from the online community on various aspects of moderation. It helps to collect opinions and feedback on moderators' performance and ideas, making it easier for the community to have a say in the moderation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Moderator**: Choose one or more moderators from the list of available moderators.
2. **Choose Vote Type**: Select the type of vote you wish to cast (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
3. **Provide Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions on the moderators' performance.
4. **Enter Personal Details**: Optionally, enter your email address, phone number, or provide comments about the moderators.
5. **Submit Your Vote**: Click the "Submit" button to cast your vote and provide feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moderator Selection**: Choose the moderator(s) you wish to vote on.
	+ Type: Select multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Vote Type**: Choose the type of vote you wish to cast (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
	+ Type: Select one
	+ Required: No
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions on the moderators' performance.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up information or updates.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone**: **Not a relevant field for this form** (this field is unlikely to be used for this specific form).
* **Comments (email)**: Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up information or updates about the comments.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Submission Date**: **Not a field for the user to fill out**, this field is likely to be auto-filled by the system.
* **Submission Time**: **Not a field for the user to fill out**, this field is likely to be auto-filled by the system.
* **Comments**: **Not a field for the user to fill out**, this field is likely to be a placeholder for comments from other users.
* **Moderator Name**: Enter the name of the moderator.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Moderator Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience the moderator has.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Moderator Bio**: Enter a brief description of the moderator.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Community Name**: Enter the name of the community.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Moderator Ideas**: Enter a brief description of the moderator's ideas.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No

## Tips

* Please be respectful and constructive in your feedback and comments.
* Remember to choose the correct vote type and provide feedback that is relevant to the moderator's performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the community administrators.
