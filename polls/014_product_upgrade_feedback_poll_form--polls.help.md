# Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the product upgrade, including ratings, comments, and other suggestions. Please provide honest feedback to help us improve our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the required questions, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the labels below.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product upgrade.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form* (`product_upgrade_feedback_poll_form`, Number, **Required**): This field is the title of the form. Please do not fill in this field, as it is automatically generated.
* **Release Date* (`release_date`, Date, Not Required): Please enter the date you experienced the product upgrade, if any. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Feedback Rating* (`feedback_rating`, Select One, **Required**): Choose one of the options to rate your overall satisfaction with the product upgrade:
	+ 1 - Very satisfied
	+ 2 - Somewhat satisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 5 - Very dissatisfied
* **Feedback Text* (`feedback_text`, Text, **Required**): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product upgrade. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your feedback.
* **Email* (`email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your email address to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on our actions.
* **Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form Comments* (`product_upgrade_feedback_poll_form_comments`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the options to specify the type of issue you experienced:
	+ Issue with functionality
	+ Issues with performance
	+ Other
* **Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form Additional Comments* (`product_upgrade_feedback_poll_form_additional_comments`, Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Submit* (`submit`, Text, **Required**): Click this button to send your feedback to us.
