# Home Automation System Features Feedback Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will help you understand what the form is designed to collect, how to fill it out, and what each field represents.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer the questions honestly, providing genuine feedback on your experience with the Home Automation System.
* You can select one or multiple answers, depending on your preferences.
* Make sure to submit the form to share your thoughts with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **feedback_title**: Enter a brief title for your feedback (optional, but recommended for context).
* **How would you rate the overall quality of your current smart home system?**: This question assesses your satisfaction with the current system's performance.
* **How would you rate the responsiveness of your current smart home system?**: This question evaluates how well the system reacts to your commands or changes.
* **What feature would you like to see improved or added first?**: This is an opportunity to suggest a specific area where you'd like to see progress. Please choose one from the available options or provide a custom answer.
* **feedback_question_4**: This field is currently labeled incorrectly, and its purpose is unclear. It seems to be a redundant field and can be ignored. If you'd like to provide feedback on this field, please let us know in the notes section or through other means of communication.
