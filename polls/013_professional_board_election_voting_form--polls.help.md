# Professional Board Election Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect opinions and preferences from board members or employees on specific matters related to the board.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, taking into consideration your opinions and preferences.
2. Start by proceeding to the next page by clicking the "Next" button after completing each page.
3. Ensure that you fill out all the required fields to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read through it carefully before proceeding.
* **Eligibility Check 1** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, optional): Confirm your eligibility to vote. This is a check to ensure that you are eligible to participate in the election.
* **Election Preferences** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, required): Select your preferred candidate from the options provided. This is an essential part of the form that will influence the outcome of the election.
* **Eligibility Check 2** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required): Confirm your eligibility to vote again. This is another check to ensure that you are eligible to participate in the election.
* **Voting Records** (`id: 5`, `type: number`, required): Enter the number of votes you plan to cast. This is an essential part of the form that will help us understand your voting behavior.
* **Vote Options** (`id: 6`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that apply to your vote. This will help us understand your preferences and opinions.
* **Eligibility Confirmation** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, optional): Confirm your eligibility to vote once more. This is a final check to ensure that you are eligible to participate in the election.
* **Vote Summary** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, required): Confirm your vote by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand your voting decision.
* **Additional Details** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, optional): Provide any additional details that you think are relevant to your vote. This is an optional field that will help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
* **Review** (`id: 10`, `type: checkbox`, required): Review your form carefully before submitting. This will help you ensure that your form is complete and accurate.

Note: Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your vote is counted correctly. If you have any questions or doubts, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
