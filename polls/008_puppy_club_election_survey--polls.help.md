# Puppy Club Election Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects your feedback on the Puppy Club's leadership. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions and provide your honest opinion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Election Survey**:
	+ This is the main question for which you'll be providing feedback. Please answer it in as much detail as you like.
* **Category**:
	+ Choose the category that best describes your connection to the Puppy Club. Please select one from the list:
		- Puppy Club
		- Pet Store
		- Animal Shelter
* **Your Name**:
	+ Please enter your full name as you'd like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter a valid email address where we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact No**:
	+ Please enter your contact number (phone or phone) where we can reach you for more information.
* **Vote For**:
	+ Select the person you think would make the best president, vice president, or secretary for the Puppy Club. Choose from the options:
		- President
		- Vice President
		- Secretary
* **Leadership Feedback** (Optional):
	+ This is a free text area for you to provide more detailed feedback on our leadership. Please use this section to tell us how we can improve.
* **Vote For**: 
    (Optional, same explanation as above) 
	+ Select the person you think would make the best president, vice president, or secretary for the Puppy Club. Choose from the options:
        - President
        - Vice President
        - Secretary 

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide thoughtful answers.
- You can ask us if you have any questions or need clarification.
- We appreciate your participation and value your opinion!
