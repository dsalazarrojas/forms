# Hospitality Industry Trend Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Hospitality Industry Trend Poll form, which aims to gather insights on the trends and challenges faced by hospitality professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the hospitality industry from the options provided.
2. Choose your region from the list of available regions.
3. Select the type of organization you are associated with from the options provided.
4. Identify the challenges you are currently facing in your work.
5. Indicate the trends you are currently facing in your work.
6. Specify the time frames for your benchmarking, reporting, and planning activities.
7. Choose the frequency of your reporting and planning activities.
8. Repeat the process for each page of the form, as needed.
