# employee-nomination-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employee nomination form is used to collect information about a new or existing employee, including their first name, last name, job title, work status, date of birth, email, and phone number. This form helps to ensure that employee information is accurate and up-to-date, which is crucial for various internal processes, such as creating or updating employee records, assigning roles, and maintaining communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the first name field, which is required to identify the employee's first name.
2. Enter the employee's last name in the next field.
3. Select the employee's job title from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the employee's work status by selecting one or more options from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the employee's date of birth in the date field.
6. Enter the employee's email address in the email field.
7. Enter the employee's phone number in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`employee_nomination_form_page_1_first_name`, Text, Required)
Enter the employee's first name, which is a required field.

### **Last Name** (`employee_nomination_form_page_1_last_name`, Text, Required)
Enter the employee's last name, which is a required field.

### **Job Title** (`employee_title`, Select One, Optional)
Select the employee's job title from the dropdown list to identify their role within the organization.

### **Work Status** (`employee_status`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose the employee's work status to indicate whether they are active or inactive.

### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional)
Enter the employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
Enter the employee's email address for communication purposes.

### **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional)
Enter the employee's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant teams for clarification.
* This form is used for internal purposes only, so please do not share it with external parties.
