# Consulting Firm Performance Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients and stakeholders about their experience with a consulting firm. The data collected will help the firm identify areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions based on your experience with the consulting firm. Try to provide specific and concrete answers.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to use the "Additional Comments" field to explain your thoughts.
- For questions with options, select the one that best fits your experience.
- For questions that ask for text input, provide a short paragraph or a brief answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is your name, and it's optional. If you don't provide it, we'll still collect your feedback, but it will be anonymous.
- **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): This is your email address, and it's optional. We'll use it for follow-up purposes only, if needed.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your organization or company.
- **Type of Engagement** (`engagement_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project you worked on with the firm (e.g., Strategy Consulting, Management Consulting, etc.).
- **Engagement Duration** (`engagement_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of time you were engaged with the firm (e.g., Less than 1 Month, 1-3 Months, etc.).
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the firm's service (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
- **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value you received from the firm's service (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Consultant Expertise** (`consultant_expertise`, select_one, required): Rate the expertise level of the firm's consultants (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication with the firm (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how responsive the firm was to your needs (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Deliverable Quality** (`deliverable_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the firm's deliverables (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Timeline Adherence** (`timeline_adherence`, select_one, required): Rate whether the firm met deadlines (e.g., Always On Time, Mostly On Time, etc.).
- **Budget Adherence** (`budget_adherence`, select_one, required): Rate whether the firm stayed within budget (e.g., Under Budget, On Budget, etc.).
- **Problem Solving Ability** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Rate the firm's problem-solving skills (e.g., Excellent, Good, etc.).
- **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, select_one, required): Rate the positive or negative impact the firm had on your business (e.g., Significant Positive Impact, No Impact, etc.).
- **Have You Implemented Recommendations** (`recommendations_implemented`, select_one, optional): If you acted on the firm's recommendations, select one of the options (e.g., Yes, Fully, Yes, Partially, etc.).
- **Best Aspects of Working With This Firm** (`best_aspects`, text, optional): Share the best things the firm did for you.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Share areas where the firm could improve.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other thoughts or feedback about your experience with the firm.
