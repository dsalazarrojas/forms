# Audience Favorite Actor Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather audience voting preferences for a specific production, allowing users to select their favorite performer from a list of options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your favorite performer from the options in the "Voting Category" field.
2. Enter the name of the performer you are voting for in the "Name of Nominee" field.
3. Optionally, share why you chose this performer in the "Why are you choosing this performer?" field.
4. If you'd like to receive a confirmation of your vote and updates about future shows, enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voting Category** (`Best Leading Actor/Best Leading Actress/Best Supporting Performer/Best Ensemble Member/Best Breakthrough Performance`, required): Select the category you believe the performer belongs to.
* **Name of Nominee** (`nominee_name`, required): Enter the name of the performer you are voting for.
* **Why are you choosing this performer?** (`voting_reason`, optional): Share your reason for choosing this performer.
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, optional): Enter your name to receive a confirmation of your vote.
* **Your Email** (`voter_email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about future shows.
* **Would you like to receive updates about future shows?** (`contact_consent`, required): Select "Yes, please" to receive updates.
