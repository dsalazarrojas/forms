# Vote For Your Preferred Candidate - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is intended to collect data on users' opinions on their preferred candidate. It is used for gathering feedback and understanding users' perspectives on specific issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by selecting the options for each section. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Review your entries carefully to ensure you have provided accurate information.
3. When you have filled out all required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vote For Your Preferred Candidate** (`vote_for_your_preferred_candidate`, Number, Required): Select the number of your preferred candidate from the available options.
* **Enter Candidate** (`enter_candidate`, Text, Required): Type in the name or ID of the candidate you prefer.
* **Vote For Candidate** (`vote_for_candidate_label`, Select One, Required): Choose "Yes" to indicate that you prefer the candidate.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your preference.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participation Source** (`participation_source`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all sources where you participate (e.g., social media, in-person, online).
* **Key Issues** (`key_issues`, Select Multiple, Required): Identify the key issues that concern you the most.
* **Sentiment** (`sentiment_label`, Select One, Required): Choose "Yes" to express a positive sentiment about the candidate.
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirm_submit`, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are submitting your response.
