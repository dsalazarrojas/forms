# Best Day Poll Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Day Poll Survey is a form designed to collect information from users to understand their preferences and opinions. It's intended to be used for internal purposes and may be used to gauge user behavior, preferences, or opinions on a specific topic or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and its sections to understand the layout and content.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the form.
3. Answer each field accurately and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full name (`full_name`, text, required)
Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.

### Email address (`email`, email, required)
Enter a valid email address that you can be reached for contact purposes.

### Date (`reference_date`, date, required)
Select the relevant date that applies to your answer.

### Primary selection (`primary_selection`, select_one, required)
Choose the option that best applies to your situation.

### Please confirm agreement (`agreement`, select_multiple, required)
Select all that apply from the options provided.

### Final comments or notes (`final_comments`, text, required)
Provide any final comments or notes about your response.

### Phone number (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number, including the country code if you're outside the local region. This field is optional and only required if you have a specific reason to provide it.

### Additional details (`additional_details`, text, optional)
Provide any additional relevant information that might help clarify your response.

### Estimated value (`estimated_value`, number, optional)
Enter an estimated value, if applicable. Use the decimal format for numbers that have decimal places.

### Preferred time (`preferred_time`, time, optional)
Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required or relevant to your response.
