# Municipal Policy Public Opinion Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Municipal Policy Public Opinion Survey Form" is designed to gather feedback from citizens on various aspects of municipal services and policies. Your input will help the municipal government understand your views and make informed decisions that benefit the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you need.
5. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to leave it blank.
6. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
