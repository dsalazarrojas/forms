# Groundhog Day Opinion Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions about Groundhog Day, a holiday celebrated on February 2nd. It aims to gauge the public's understanding, experiences, and opinions on the holiday.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your familiarity and experiences.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you.
3. Provide additional notes if you'd like to share more information.
4. Submit your responses.
