# environmental_initiative_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and feedback from users who have submitted an environmental initiative.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: This is a text field where you can provide a title for your environmental initiative.
2. **What**: This is a text field where you can describe your environmental initiative.
3. **Sustainability Focus**: This is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or more sustainability focus areas related to your initiative.
4. **Additional Comments**: This is a free-text field where you can provide additional comments or notes about your initiative.
5. **Email**: This is a text field where you can enter your email address.
6. **Phone**: This is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
7. **Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your name.
8. **Info**: This is a free-text field where you can provide additional information.
9. **Organization**: This is a text field where you can enter your organization's name.
10. **Department**: This is a text field where you can enter your department name.
11. **Job Title**: This is a text field where you can enter your job title.
12. **Position**: This is a text field where you can enter your position.
13. **Level**: This is a text field where you can enter your level.
14. **Location**: This is a text field where you can enter your location.
15. **Comments**: This is a free-text field where you can provide comments.
16. **Date**: This is a date field where you can select a date.
17. **Time**: This is a time field where you can select a time.
18. **Checkbox 1**: This is a single-select field where you can choose one option from the options provided.
19. **Checkbox 2**: This is a single-select field where you can choose one option from the options provided.
20. **Checkbox 3**: This is a single-select field where you can choose one option from the options provided.
21. **Checkbox 4**: This is a single-select field where you can choose one option from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are submitting an initiative that has already been submitted, please do not resubmit it.
- You can upload files to support your initiative, if required.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
