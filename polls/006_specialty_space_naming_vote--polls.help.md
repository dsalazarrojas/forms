# Specialty Space Naming Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Space Naming Vote form is designed to gather your opinion on the best name for a specialty space. Your input will help us choose a name that represents the space's unique identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the specialty space in the `Specialty Space` field.
2. Select your top three rankings for the `Ranking 1`, `Ranking 2`, and `Ranking 3` fields from the provided options.
3. Optionally, provide any additional feedback in the `Feedback` field.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Specialty Space** (`specialty_space`): Enter the name of the specialty space you wish to vote for.
* **Ranking 1** (`ranking_1`): Select your top choice for the name of the specialty space from the options provided.
* **Ranking 2** (`ranking_2`): Select your second choice for the name of the specialty space from the options provided.
* **Ranking 3** (`ranking_3`): Select your third choice for the name of the specialty space from the options provided.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the naming process.
