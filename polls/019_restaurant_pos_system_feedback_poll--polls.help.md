# Restaurant POS System Feedback Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Restaurant POS System in different locations. The goal is to gather information about the user's main concerns, frequency of use, overall performance, and other aspects of the POS system, as well as their preferences and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location where you use the POS system.
2. Choose your role (Manager, Staff, or Customer) in the location.
3. Answer all the questions according to your experience with the POS system at the chosen location.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
