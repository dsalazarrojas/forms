# Boat Parade Voting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Parade Voting Survey is a tool to collect feedback and opinions from attendees on the different aspects of the parade.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your name and email address to ensure contact and verification purposes.
2. Select your favorite boat from the list provided for each category.
3. Rate the overall experience of the parade.
4. Share your favorite parade highlights.
5. Indicate if you plan to attend next year.
6. Provide suggestions for improving the parade.
7. If interested, indicate interest in volunteering for next year's event.
8. Finally, enter your zip code for local tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact and verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`voter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for future contact.
* **Which boat parade are you voting for** (`parade_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the parade you attended.
* **Most Decorated Boat** (`best_decorated_boat`, `select_one`, required): Vote for the boat that impressed you the most with its decorations.
* **Best Themed Boat** (`best_themed_boat`, `select_one`, required): Vote for the boat with the best theme.
* **Most Creative Design** (`most_creative_design`, `select_one`, required): Vote for the boat with the most creative design.
* **Best Lighting Display** (`best_lighting_display`, `select_one`, required): Vote for the boat with the best lighting display.
* **Audience Favorite** (`audience_favorite`, `select_one`, required): Vote for your favorite boat.
* **Overall Parade Experience Rating** (`parade_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall experience of the parade.
* **Parade Highlights** (`parade_highlights`, `text`, optional): Share your favorite moments from the parade.
* **Attend Next Year** (`participation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you plan to attend next year.
* **Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share ideas on how to improve the parade.
* **Volunteer** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for next year's parade.
* **Zip Code** (`voter_zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code for local community tracking purposes.
