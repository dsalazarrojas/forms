# Classroom Resources Requirement Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps teachers and staff members report their classroom resources requirements to inform purchasing decisions and budget allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name, role, and grade level.
2. Select the type of resources you need (e.g., classroom supplies, technology equipment).
3. Specify the specific items you require.
4. Indicate the urgency level and quantity needed.
5. Estimate the cost of the resources.
6. Provide an estimate of your current inventory status.
7. Justify why these resources are needed.
8. Explain how these resources will improve student learning.
9. If necessary, indicate alternative solutions and funding sources.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments or information to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (1): Your full name.
* **Role or Position** (2): Select your role in the school (e.g., teacher, librarian, administrator).
* **Grade Level** (3): Select the grade level you work with (e.g., Kindergarten, 1-2, 3-5, etc.).
* **Department** (4): If applicable, select your department or subject area.
* **Classroom Number** (5): Your primary classroom or room number.
* **Resource Category Needed** (6): Select the type of resources you need (e.g., classroom supplies, technology equipment, etc.).
* **Specific Items Needed** (7): List the specific items you require for the resources.
* **Quantity Required** (8): Approximate the quantity needed for each item.
* **Urgency Level** (9): Indicate how urgently you need these resources (e.g., critical, high, medium, low).
* **Estimated Cost** (10): If possible, estimate the approximate cost of the resources.
* **Current Inventory Status** (11): Indicate your current supply level (e.g., depleted, low, adequate, well-stocked).
* **Justification** (12): Explain why these resources are needed and the impact on student learning.
* **Impact on Learning** (13): Describe how these resources will improve student learning.
* **Alternative Solutions** (14): If applicable, indicate alternative solutions you have tried or considered.
* **Funding Source Known** (15): If applicable, indicate if you know which budget this could come from.
* **Willing to Share Resources** (16): Indicate if you are willing to share resources with other teachers.
* **Additional Comments** (17): Provide any other information to support your request.
