## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about your political views and opinions. It consists of 11 questions that cover various aspects of politics. Please complete the form to provide your honest and accurate responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For questions that require a choice between two options, select the most accurate answer that reflects your opinion.
3. For questions that require a numerical value, enter a whole number as your response.
4. For questions that require a date or time, enter it in the correct format.
5. For questions that require text, provide a clear and concise answer.
