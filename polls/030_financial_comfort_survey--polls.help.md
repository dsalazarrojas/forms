# Financial Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on users' financial comfort, satisfaction, and well-being. It's meant to be a quick and easy way to assess your financial situation and provide valuable insights for financial planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Use the options provided for the "Financial Satisfaction", "Felt Stressed", and "Felt Frustrated" questions.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as you need for the text fields.
4. Submit your answers when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Financial Comfort** (`overall_financial_comfort`, text, required: false): This is a text field where you can describe your overall financial comfort. Please provide a brief answer, e.g., "I feel comfortable" or "I'm not sure".
* **Current Financial Situation** (`current_financial_situation`, text, required: false): Describe your current financial situation, e.g., "I'm saving money" or "I'm struggling to make ends meet".
* **Financial Satisfaction** (`financial_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Select how satisfied you are with your current financial situation. Options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Financial Safety** (`financial_safety`, text, required: false): This is a text field where you can describe how safe you feel about your financial situation, e.g., "I feel secure" or "I'm not sure".
* **Financial Security** (`financial_security`, text, required: false): Describe how secure you feel about your financial situation, e.g., "I feel protected" or "I'm not sure".
* **Felt Stressed** (`have_you_felt_stressed`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you've felt stressed about your financial situation. Options are:
	+ Always
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
* **Felt Frustrated** (`feel_frustrated`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you've felt frustrated about your financial situation. Options are:
	+ Always
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
* **Financial Support Systems** (`financial_support_systems`, text, required: false): Describe any financial support systems you have in place, e.g., "I have a emergency fund" or "I'm not sure".
* **Financial Independence** (`financial_independence`, text, required: false): Describe how independent you feel about your financial situation, e.g., "I'm fully independent" or "I'm not sure".
* **Financial Well-being** (`financial_well_being`, text, required: false): Describe your overall financial well-being, e.g., "I'm content" or "I'm not sure".
