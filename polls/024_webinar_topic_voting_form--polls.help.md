# Webinar Topic Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect input on potential webinar topics and their details for the next webinar. The team will use this information to decide which topic to focus on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the proposed webinar topic in the **Webinar Topic** field.
2.  Select the format of the webinar, either **Webinar** or **Video**.
3.  Choose whether the webinar should be scheduled or not.
4.  Enter the start date of the webinar.
5.  Enter the end date of the webinar.
6.  Select the frequency of the webinar, either **Monthly** or **Quarterly**.
7.  Choose the priority of the webinar, either **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Webinar Topic** (`topic`, text, optional): Enter the proposed webinar topic.
* **Format** (`format`, select_one, optional): Select the format of the webinar, either **Webinar** or **Video**.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the webinar should be scheduled or not.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the webinar.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the webinar.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of the webinar, either **Monthly** or **Quarterly**.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority of the webinar, either **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
