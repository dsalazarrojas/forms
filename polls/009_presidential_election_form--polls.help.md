# Presidential Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and opinions from users on the topic of presidential elections. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability, and don't hesitate to leave comments if you have any feedback or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all the options that apply to your thoughts and opinions about the presidential election.
2. Fill out the `Presidential Candidate` field with your thoughts about the election.
3. If you're unsure about the election date or time, you can leave it blank or select "Unknown".
4. Enter your thoughts about the election location in the `Election Location` field.
5. If you have any additional comments or information, please use the `Voter Info` field.
6. Finally, rate the form itself in the `Rate Form` and `Comment About Form` fields, and optionally provide a comment to explain your rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vote For**
`Vote For` (`id: 1`, type: `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose all options that apply to your thoughts and opinions about the presidential election.

### 2. **Presidential Candidate**
`Presidential Candidate` (`id: 2`, type: `select_one`, required: false)
This field is for entering your thoughts about the presidential election. Please be specific and provide as much detail as you need.

### 3. **Election Date**
`Election Date` (`id: 3`, type: `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of the presidential election. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.

### 4. **Election Time**
`Election Time` (`id: 4`, type: `time`, required: false)
Enter the time of the presidential election. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.

### 5. **Election Location**
`Election Location` (`id: 5`, type: `text`, required: false)
Enter your thoughts about the election location.

### 6. **Voter Info**
`Voter Info` (`id: 6`, type: `note`, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or information about the voter experience, please use this field.

### 7. **Comments**
`Comments` (`id: 7`, type: `text`, required: false)
Leave any additional comments or thoughts about the form or the experience.

### 8. **Comments About Form**
`Comments About Form` (`id: 8`, type: `text`, required: false)
Rate the form and provide a comment to explain your rating.

### 9. **Rate Form**
`Rate Form` (`id: 9`, type: `number`, required: false)
Rate the form on a scale of 1 to 5.

### 10. **Comment About Form**
`Comment About Form` (`id: 10`, type: `text`, required: false)
Provide a comment to explain your rating.

### 11. **Rate Form Likert Scale**
`Rate Form Likert Scale` (`id: 11`, type: `number`, required: false)
Rate the form using the Likert scale, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
