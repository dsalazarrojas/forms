# Local Business Decoration Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by local business owners and managers to vote on decorations in their businesses. The form is designed to gather information on the current state of decorations, allowing for the collection of data and tracking of changes over time. This form is used to help businesses make informed decisions about their decoration strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the form and click on the "Decoration Choices" tab.
2. Select all the applicable decoration options for your business. Choose "Yes" if you have or intend to have the decoration item.
3. Click on the "Comments" tab and provide any additional comments about your business' decorations.
4. Click on the "Event" tab and describe any special events or occasions that may be related to your business' decorations.
5. Click on the "Results" tab to view the overall results of the poll.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Decoration Choices
* **Decoration Choices**: (Yes, No) 
  This field allows you to indicate whether your business currently has or intends to have the selected decoration items. Select "Yes" if you have the decoration item, "No" if you do not have it.

### Comments
* **Comments**: (Text) 
  This field is where you can provide any additional comments about your business' decorations. You can type in any information you think would be helpful for others to know.

### Event
* **Event**: (Text) 
  This field is used to describe any special events or occasions that may be related to your business' decorations.

### Results
* **Results**: (Note) 
  This field displays the overall results of the poll, allowing you to see how other businesses have responded to the decoration choices.
