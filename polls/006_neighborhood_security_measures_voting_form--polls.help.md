# neighborhood_security_measures_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from residents on their experiences with neighborhood security measures. It will help the local government or neighborhood association understand what works and what can be improved for better community safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin.
2. Select your preferred "Security Measures" from the list on the first page.
3. Add any comments or suggestions about security measures if you wish in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter a valid email address if you want to receive a notification about your submission.
5. Optionally, enter a phone number where you can be reached.
6. Choose your "Submission Type" if you want to specify how your submission should be treated.
7. Select the date and time when you want to submit your feedback.
8. Add any notes or comments in the "Submission Location" field.
9. Enter any notes or additional comments about your submission.
10. Select one or more "Resident Group" you are part of to help us understand your concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Security Measures** (`security_measures`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select one or more of the available security measures. You can choose only one or more options depending on your preference.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments or suggestions about the neighborhood security measures you have experienced or would like to see improved. 
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, `email`, required/true): Enter a valid email address if you want to receive a notification about your submission. 
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached. 
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you want your submission to be treated. 
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/false): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required/false): Choose the time you would like to submit your feedback. 
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, `text`, required/false): Enter any notes or comments about where you submitted your feedback. 
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, `note`, required/true): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission. 
* **Resident Group** (`resident_group`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Select one or more groups you are part of to help us understand your concerns. 

## Tips

* Please provide valid and relevant information for each field.
* Please make sure to submit only one form per person.
