# Financial Planning Service Opinion Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions from customers about their satisfaction with our financial planning service. We value your feedback and want to understand how we can improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Fill out the form entirely if you have an opinion on the financial planning service.
3. Do not fill out the form multiple times if you have already completed it.
4. Make sure to enter your email address correctly to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or to share your feedback with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Feedback
* **customer_feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text box where you can share any feedback you have about our financial planning service. Please be as specific as possible and let us know what we are doing well and what we can improve on.

### Satisfaction Level
* **satisfaction_level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, optional):
	+ This field asks for your level of satisfaction with our service, on a scale of 0 to 5. Please rate your satisfaction based on how well our service met your needs and expectations.

### Rating
* **rating** (`rating`, number, optional):
	+ This field also asks for your rating of our service, on a scale of 1 to 5. Please rate our service based on how likely you are to recommend us to others.

### Comments
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text box where you can share any additional comments or thoughts about our service. Please keep in mind that your comments will be reviewed by our team to better understand your needs and concerns.

### Email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to share your feedback with our team. We will not share your email with any other parties.

### Contact Method
* **contact_method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select how you would prefer to be contacted in case we need to follow-up with you. You can choose between "Yes" (you are comfortable with phone calls) or "No" (you would rather not be contacted by phone).

### Financial Advisor Name
* **financial_advisor_name** (`financial_advisor_name`, text, optional):
	+ If you had an advisor assist you with our service, please enter their name so we can ensure we are recognizing their contribution to your experience.

### Financial Firm
* **financial_firm** (`financial_firm`, text, optional):
	+ If you had an advisor from a specific firm assist you, please enter the name of the firm so we can recognize their involvement in your experience.

## Tips
* Please complete the form entirely and honestly to help us better serve you and others.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your feedback and use it to improve our service.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your opinion with us!
