# Wellness 101 Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your current health habits and behaviors. Please answer the questions accurately and honestly to get a comprehensive picture of your well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please answer each question to the best of your ability, considering your current situation.
*   Choose the options that best describe your habits and behaviors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
