# Municipal Election Voter Priority Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from registered voters on their priorities and sentiments regarding the municipal election. Please fill out this form to express your opinions and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer to each question by choosing one or multiple options from the provided choices.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they are not marked as required.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Poll** (`poll`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the topics that interest you most in the municipal election.
	+ 'Yes' for topics that interest you.
	+ 'No' for topics that do not interest you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Address** (`address`, note, required: false): Provide your current address for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Date of Birth** (`contact_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`contact_gender`, select one, required: false): Choose your gender from the provided choices.
* **Occupation** (`contact_occupation`, select one, required: false): Choose your occupation from the provided choices.
* **Vote Priority** (`voter_priority`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the topics that you value most in the municipal election.
* **Resident Satisfaction** (`resident_satisfaction`, select multiple, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with your current resident status.
* **Voting Sentiment** (`voting_sentiment`, select one, required: false): Choose how you feel about the current election.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **State** (`contact_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your zip code for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email for any additional information or follow-up questions.
* **Age** (`resident_age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Resident Gender** (`resident_gender`, select one, required: false): Choose your resident gender from the provided choices.
* **How Long Lived?** (`resident_lived`, select one, required: false): Choose how long you have lived in the area.
* **Employment Status** (`resident_employed`, select one, required: false): Choose your employment status.
* **Income** (`resident_income`, number, required: false): Enter your income.
* **Education** (`resident_education`, text, required: false): Enter your education level or background.
* **Resident Employment Status** (`resident_employment_status`, select one, required: false): Choose your current employment status as a resident.
* **Tips**
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Use the provided choices for guidance.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us at your provided email or phone number.
