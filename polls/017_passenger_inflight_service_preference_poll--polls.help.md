# Passenger Inflight Service Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect feedback from passengers about their preferences and experiences with in-flight meal service. Please take a few minutes to complete the form and let us know about your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information in each field. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose your preferences for the meal service and seat options using the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Meal service on flight**: Choose your preferred level of meal service:
	+ None: No meal service
	+ Snacks only: Limited meal service with only snacks available
	+ Full meal service: Full meal service with hot meals available
* **Seat number**: If you have a preferred seat number, please enter it here.
* **Travel class**: Choose your preferred travel class:
	+ Economy: Economy class
	+ Premium Economy: Premium economy class
	+ Business: Business class
	+ First class: First class
* **Cabin**: Choose your preferred cabin:
	+ Front: Front cabin
	+ Middle: Middle cabin
	+ Back: Back cabin
* **How satisfied were you with the in-flight meal service**: Rate your satisfaction with the meal service:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Do you recommend our in-flight meal service**: Yes, No, or Undecided
* **Have any suggestions for the in-flight meal service**: Enter any ideas or suggestions you may have.
* **Any additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and detailed as possible with your feedback.
* Your feedback is important to us and will help us improve passenger experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
