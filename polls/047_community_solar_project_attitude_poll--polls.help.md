# Community Solar Project Attitude Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from community members about a community solar project. Your input will help inform the development and implementation of the project, ensuring that it meets the needs and preferences of the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your contact information, including your name and email address, if you'd like to stay updated on the project's progress.
2. Answer the questions regarding your knowledge and familiarity with solar energy, your support for the project, and your preferred solar panel locations.
3. Consider the benefits and concerns you have about the project, and let us know what would prevent you from participating.
4. Provide information about your financial considerations, such as your monthly budget for a solar subscription, and your acceptable payback period.
5. Let us know how you'd like to be communicated about the project's updates.
6. If you're interested in attending an information session, please let us know.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_info`): This field is optional and allows you to provide your contact information, which will help us keep you updated on the project's progress.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): This field is optional and asks for your first name, which will help us identify you in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): This field is optional and asks for your last name, which will help us identify you in our records.
* **Email Address** (`email`): This field is optional and asks for your email address, which will help us communicate with you about the project's updates.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): This field is required and asks for your zip code, which will help us identify your location.
* **Are You a Resident** (`resident_type`): This field is required and asks you to select whether you are a homeowner, renter, business owner, property manager, or other.
* **Knowledge About Solar Energy** (`solar_knowledge`): This field is optional and provides a note section for you to describe your level of knowledge about solar energy.
* **Familiarity with Solar Energy** (`familiarity`): This field is required and asks you to select your level of familiarity with solar energy.
* **Solar Benefits** (`solar_benefits`): This field is optional and asks you to select all benefits you see from the community solar project.
* **Solar Concerns** (`solar_concerns`): This field is optional and asks you to select all concerns you have about the project.
* **Attitude Toward Community Solar** (`attitude_section`): This field is optional and provides a note section for you to express your attitude towards community solar.
* **Do You Support Community Solar Projects** (`overall_support`): This field is required and asks you to select your level of support for the project.
* **Would You Support Solar in Your Area** (`location_support`): This field is required and asks you to select whether you would support solar in your area.
* **Preferred Solar Panel Locations** (`preferred_location`): This field is optional and asks you to select all preferred locations for solar panels.
* **Participation Interest** (`participation`): This field is optional and asks you whether you'd like to participate in the project.
* **Would You Participate in Community Solar** (`would_participate`): This field is required and asks you to select whether you would participate in the project.
* **What Would Prevent Participation** (`participation_barriers`): This field is optional and asks you to select all barriers that would prevent you from participating.
* **Preferred Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`): This field is optional and asks you to select your preferred subscription type.
* **Monthly Savings** (`monthly_savings`): This field is optional and asks you to select your monthly budget for a solar subscription.
* **Acceptable Payback Period** (`payback_period`): This field is optional and asks you to select an acceptable payback period.
* **Incentives** (`incentives`): This field is optional and asks you to select all incentives that would help you participate in the project.
* **Information Needs** (`information_needs`): This field is optional and provides a note section for you to describe your information needs.
* **Topics You Want to Learn About** (`info_topics`): This field is optional and asks you to select all topics you'd like to learn about.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication`): This field is optional and asks you how you'd like to be communicated about the project's updates.
* **Would You Attend an Information Session** (`meeting_attendance`): This field is optional and asks you whether you'd attend an information session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This field is optional and asks you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

**Tips**

* Please answer all required questions to help us gather accurate and complete information.
* You can select multiple options for fields like benefits, concerns, and incentives.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
