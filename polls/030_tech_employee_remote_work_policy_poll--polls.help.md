# tech_employee_remote_work_policy_poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees on the technology employee remote work policy. The goal is to understand how well the policy works and what aspects can be improved to increase employee satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, as it will help us understand your preferences and needs.
2. Select one option for each field, even if you're unsure or neutral.
3. Enter your email address and phone number accurately.
4. Submit the form once you've filled out all the fields.
