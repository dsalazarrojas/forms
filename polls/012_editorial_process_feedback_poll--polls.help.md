# Editorial Process Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through how to fill out the Editorial Process Feedback Poll form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your form title in the "Form Title" field. This is the title you would like to give to your form.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions.
4. Rate the clarity of the instructions on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unclear and 5 is clear.
5. Rate the communication effectiveness of the instructions on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
6. Rate the timeliness of the feedback on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is late and 5 is on-time.
7. Check if there were any accessibility issues during the process.
8. Rate the quality of the feedback on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
9. Enter any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
10. Rate the overall quality of the process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): This is the title you would like to give to your form.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): This is your email address.
* **Clarity of Instructions** (`clarity_instructions`, number, not required): Rate the clarity of the instructions on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is unclear and 5 is clear.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, not required): Rate the communication effectiveness of the instructions on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Timeliness of Feedback** (`timeliness_feedback`, number, not required): Rate the timeliness of the feedback on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is late and 5 is on-time.
* **Accessibility Issues** (`accessibility_issues`, checkbox, not required): Check if there were any accessibility issues during the process.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, not required): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall Process Quality** (`overall_process_quality`, number, not required): Rate the overall quality of the process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

## Tips
Please rate each question on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. This will help us understand your overall experience with the editorial process.
