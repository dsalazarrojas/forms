# Business Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and feedback from employees on various business-related topics, including remote work, work-life balance, and industry trends. It aims to help us better understand your thoughts and opinions on these matters, which will be used to inform our business decisions and improve our work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information on each page. You can refer to the hints provided for each field for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Poll Date
**Poll Date** (`poll_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this poll.

### Your Name
**Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): If you'd like to identify yourself by name, enter it here. This information will not be shared publicly.

### Email Address
**Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, enter your email address. This information will not be shared publicly.

### Company Name
**Company Name** (`respondent_company`, text, optional): If you'd like to mention your company, enter it here. This information will not be shared publicly.

### Your Role
**Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, optional): Select your role or position within the company.

### What is your opinion on remote work?
**What is your opinion on remote work?** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose your position on remote work from the provided options.

### How important is work-life balance to you?
**How important is work-life balance to you?** (`question_2`, number, required): Rate the importance of work-life balance on a scale from 1 to 10.

### What is your biggest workplace challenge?
**What is your biggest workplace challenge?** (`question_3`, text, optional): Describe briefly your biggest challenge in the workplace.

### How satisfied are you with the current job market?
**How satisfied are you with the current job market?** (`question_4`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the current job market on a scale from 1 to 5.

### Which business trend excites you the most?
**Which business trend excites you the most?** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Choose the business trend that excites you the most from the provided options.

### What skills are most valuable in business?
**What skills are most valuable in business?** (`question_6`, text, optional): List your top 3 valuable skills.

### How often do you stay informed of industry news?
**How often do you stay informed of industry news?** (`question_7`, select_one, required): Choose how often you stay informed of industry news from the provided options.

### What would improve your workplace?
**What would improve your workplace?** (`question_8`, text, optional): Share your suggestions on how to improve the workplace.

### Likelihood to recommend your employer?
**Likelihood to recommend your employer?** (`question_9`, number, optional): Rate how likely you are to recommend your employer on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Additional Comments
**Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any other thoughts or feedback you may have.
