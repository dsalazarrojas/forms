# Academic Research - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect expert opinions on factors contributing to HIV/STD vulnerability from healthcare professionals, researchers, social workers, community advocates, academics, and students. The data collected will inform research gaps and policy recommendations to reduce HIV/STD vulnerability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your primary role in research or healthcare from the provided options.
2.  Enter your years of experience in your field of expertise.
3.  Identify the geographic region you primarily work in.
4.  Rate the impact of socioeconomic factors on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
5.  Rate the impact of healthcare access on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
6.  Rate the impact of educational disparities on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
7.  Rate the impact of structural racism on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
8.  Rate the impact of stigma and discrimination on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
9.  Rate the impact of cultural factors on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
10. Rate the impact of historical trauma on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
11. Rate the impact of healthcare system mistrust on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
12. Identify the most significant factor contributing to HIV/STD vulnerability from the provided list.
13. Choose the area that should be prioritized for interventions to reduce vulnerability.
14. If desired, identify any additional research gaps that need to be addressed.
15. Provide any additional comments on factors contributing to HIV/STD vulnerability.
16. Choose whether you consent to the collection of your demographic data for analysis.
17. Confirm your choice for demographic data collection consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Research Poll - Factors Contributing to HIV/STD Vulnerability**: This is the title of the poll. Please do not fill this field.
*  **Your Role in Research or Healthcare** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Please choose your primary role in research or healthcare from the options.
    * Healthcare professional
    * Public health researcher
    * Social worker
    * Community advocate
    * Academic researcher
    * Student
    * Other
*  **Years of Experience in Field** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience in your field of expertise.
*  **Geographic Region of Focus** (`geographic_region`, text, required): Enter the geographic region you primarily work in.
*  **Socioeconomic Factors Impact Rating** (`socioeconomic_factors_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of socioeconomic factors on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Healthcare Access Impact Rating** (`healthcare_access_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of healthcare access on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Education Disparities Impact Rating** (`education_disparities_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of educational disparities on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Structural Racism Impact Rating** (`structural_racism_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of structural racism on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Stigma and Discrimination Impact Rating** (`stigma_discrimination_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of stigma and discrimination on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Cultural Factors Impact Rating** (`cultural_factors_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of cultural factors on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Historical Trauma Impact Rating** (`historical_trauma_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of historical trauma on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Healthcare System Mistrust Impact Rating** (`healthcare_system_mistrust_rating`, number, required): Rate the impact of healthcare system mistrust on HIV/STD vulnerability on a scale of 1-5.
*  **Most Significant Contributing Factor** (`most_significant_factor`, select_one, required): Choose the most significant factor contributing to HIV/STD vulnerability from the list.
    * Socioeconomic factors
    * Healthcare access
    * Educational disparities
    * Structural racism
    * Stigma and discrimination
    * Cultural factors
    * Historical trauma
    * Healthcare system mistrust
*  **Priority Intervention Area** (`intervention_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the area that should be prioritized for interventions to reduce vulnerability.
    * Improving healthcare access
    * Addressing socioeconomic disparities
    * Education and prevention programs
    * Reducing stigma
    * Policy reform
    * Community engagement
    * Other
*  **Identified Research Gap** (`research_gap_identification`, text, optional): If desired, describe any other research gaps that need to be addressed in this area.
*  **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts on factors contributing to HIV/STD vulnerability.
*  **Demographic Data Collection Consent** (`demographic_data_collection_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to the collection of your demographic data for analysis.
    * Yes, I consent
    * Yes, with anonymity
    * No, I do not consent

## Tips

* Be honest and objective in your responses.
* Provide specific examples when possible.
* Consider the impact of various factors on HIV/STD vulnerability in your region or community.
* Your responses will contribute to identifying research gaps and informing policy recommendations to reduce HIV/STD vulnerability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
