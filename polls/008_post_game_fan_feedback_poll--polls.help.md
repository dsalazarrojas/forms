# Post Game Fan Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Game Fan Feedback Poll is a series of questions designed to gather opinions and experiences from game attendees. Your input will help us understand what made the event exciting, what you enjoyed the most, how we can improve, and what made the event a great experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant option for each question based on your experience with the event.
2. For questions with options like "Other (specify below)", please provide a brief description of your answer in the text field next to it.
3. Answer all required questions as they have a *next to them.
4. Take your time to give honest and thoughtful answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Was the event exciting?** (`event_attended`, `select_one`): This question asks whether you found the event exciting or not. Please select "Yes" if you found the event exciting, "No" if you didn't, or "Undecided" if you're unsure.
* **Overall event satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`): This question is about your overall experience at the event. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How often do you attend our games?** (`event_attended_frequency`, `select_multiple`): This question is about how frequently you attend our events. Please select all options that apply to you, such as "Regularly", "Occasionally", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **What was your favorite part of the event?** (`favorite_part`, `select_one`): This question is about what you enjoyed the most about the event. Please select from the given options, such as "Pre-game Festivities", "Food and Beverage", "Music and Entertainment", "Game Day Activities", or "Other (specify below)".
* **Other (if applicable)** (`other_favorite_part`, `text`): If you selected "Other (specify below)" in the previous question, please provide more details about what made your favorite part of the event.
* **What was your favorite food item?** (`favorite_food_item`, `select_one`): This question is about your favorite food item from the event. Please select from the given options, such as "Burger", "Hot Dog", "Popcorn", or "Other (specify below)".
* **Other (if applicable)** (`other_favorite_food_item`, `text`): If you selected "Other (specify below)" in the previous question, please provide more details about your favorite food item.
* **How would you rate the quality of our food?** (`overall_food_satisfaction`, `number`): This question is about your experience with the food at the event. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How friendly was the event staff?** (`staff_friendliness`, `number`): This question is about your experience with the event staff. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Unfriendly" and 5 is "Very Friendly".
* **Overall staff friendliness** (`overall_staff_friendliness`, `number`): This question is an average of your experience with the event staff. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Unfriendly" and 5 is "Very Friendly".
* **How would you rate the facilities at the venue?** (`venue_facilities`, `number`): This question is about your experience with the venue's facilities. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Overall event experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`): This question is about your overall experience at the event. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Would you recommend this event?** (`recommend`, `select_one`): This question is about whether you would recommend the event to others. Please select "Yes" if you would recommend it, "No" if not.
