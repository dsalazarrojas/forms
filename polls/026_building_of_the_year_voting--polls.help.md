# Building Of The Year Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect votes for the "Building Of The Year" award, which recognizes outstanding architecture projects. Voters are asked to provide their contact information, profession, and experience level, as well as their top three choices for "Building of the Year" and their preferences for different criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address on the first page.
2. Select your role and years of professional experience.
3. Choose your top three buildings for "Building of the Year" by selecting them in the respective fields.
4. Rate the importance of design excellence, sustainability, innovation, community impact, overall quality, and overall feedback on the competition.
5. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "General Feedback" field.
6. (Optional) Provide additional demographic information such as country and region.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voting Date**: This field is used to collect the current date when you submit the form.
* **Your Full Name**: This field requires your complete name.
* **Email Address**: This field is for verification and results purposes. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Organization or Company**: This is an optional field to specify where you work or represent.
* **Your Role**: This field helps us understand your profession. Choose from Architect, Designer, Developer, Client, Contractor, Educator, Student, Industry Professional, or Other.
* **Years of Professional Experience**: This field asks for your experience level, with options Less than 2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-15 years, Over 15 years.
* **Voting Category**: This field allows you to choose which category you are voting in. Select from Commercial Building, Residential Building, Public Building, Cultural Building, Healthcare Building, Educational Building, Sustainable Design, or Innovation.
* **First Choice - Building of the Year**: This field is for your top choice.
* **Why You Selected First Choice**: This field allows you to provide reasons for your selection.
* **Second Choice**: This is an optional field for your second favorite building.
* **Third Choice**: This is an optional field for your third selection.
* **Design Excellence Criteria**: This field asks you to rate the importance of design excellence, with options Critical, Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **Importance of Sustainability**: This field asks you to rate the importance of sustainability, with options Critical, Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **Importance of Innovation**: This field asks you to rate the importance of innovation, with options Critical, Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **Importance of Community Impact**: This field asks you to rate the importance of community impact, with options Critical, Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **Overall Quality**: This field asks you to rate the importance of overall quality, with options Critical, Very Important, Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important.
* **General Feedback on the Competition**: This field allows you to provide any comments or suggestions.
* **Optional - Voter Demographics**: This is an optional field to provide additional demographic information.
* **Country**: This field is optional to specify where you are based.
* **Region or State**: This field is also optional to specify your region.
