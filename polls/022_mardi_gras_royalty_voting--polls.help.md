Mardi Gras Royalty Voting - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose
The Mardi Gras Royalty Voting form is used to select the royalty of a Mardi Gras krew (krewe), which is typically a social group or organization participating in the Mardi Gras celebration. This form allows you to create and manage the voting process for selecting the krew's royalty.

### How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be presented with a series of multiple-choice fields for selecting the members of the krew's royalty. Each field represents a different member of the krew and allows you to choose one or multiple members as candidates. Please select the desired members and submit your response.
