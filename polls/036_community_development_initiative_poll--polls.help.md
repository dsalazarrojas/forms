# Development Initiative Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Initiative Poll is a form designed to gather feedback from residents on potential development initiatives in their neighborhood or community. It helps identify the top priority issues for development and gauges the level of support for proposed initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. If you are anonymous, you may be asked to provide your resident area or neighborhood to help us understand which area you are referring to.
2. Select the top priority issue for development in your area.
3. Indicate your level of support for the proposed initiative.
4. If you're willing, let us know if you'd like to participate in planning meetings or volunteer for related projects.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident area or neighborhood**: Enter the name of your neighborhood or area you are providing feedback for. (Optional text field, required only if you're anonymous)
* **Top priority issue for development**: Select one of the options listed to indicate the most important issue that needs to be addressed in your neighborhood. (Select one of the five options: Housing affordability, Local jobs and economy, Public safety, Parks and recreation, or Transportation)
* **Support level for proposed initiative**: Select one of the five options to indicate your level of support for the proposed initiative. (Select one of the five options: Strongly support, Support, Neutral, Oppose, or Strongly oppose)
* **Willing to participate in planning**: If you're willing, select True to indicate you'd like to participate in planning meetings or volunteer for related projects. (Optional select one option: True, False, or Maybe)
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share. (Optional text field)
