# Favorite Poster Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions on favorite posters. Users can vote for one or multiple posters on each page, and also provide additional notes if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select your favorite posters by choosing the posters you like from the options provided.
2. Make sure to select only the posters you are interested in, as you can choose multiple posters on each page.
3. If needed, provide a note to elaborate on your selections.
4. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite poster?** (`favorite_poster_voting_page1`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or multiple posters that you like from the options provided.
* **Select one or more of the following -** (`favorite_poster_voting_page2`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or multiple posters that you like from the options provided.
* **Which one do you like?** (`favorite_poster_voting_page3`, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose one poster that you like from the options provided.
* **Poster 1**, **Poster 2**, **Poster 3** (`favorite_poster_voting_page4`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or multiple posters that you like from the options provided.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): 
  This is a text field to provide a note if needed.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): 
  Provide a note to elaborate on your selections.
* **What is your favorite poster?** (`favorite_poster_voting_page6`, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose one poster that you like from the options provided.
* **Select one or more of the following -** (`favorite_poster_voting_page7`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Choose one or multiple posters that you like from the options provided.
