# Venue Options Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Venue Options Form is a tool used to collect information about the characteristics of a venue when creating new venue options. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the venue's details are properly recorded in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Venue Options Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the venue option you want to edit or create.
2. Choose the correct venue type from the options provided.
3. Select the costs associated with the venue from the options provided.
4. Enter a brief description of the costs, if necessary.
5. Choose the indoor amenities available at the venue.
6. Choose the outdoor amenities available at the venue.
7. Enter the contact information for the venue, including name, phone number, and email.
8. Enter the website of the venue, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Venue**: Select the venue option you want to create or edit.
- **Venue Type**: Choose the correct type of venue from the options provided. This field helps to categorize the venue and make it easier to search and filter.
- **Costs**: Select the costs associated with the venue, such as fees or charges. This field helps to understand the financial implications of using the venue.
- **Costs Description**: Enter a brief description of the costs, if necessary. This field helps to provide additional context about the costs.
- **Costs Amount**: Enter the amount of the costs, if applicable. This field is used to specify the exact financial amount associated with the venue.
- **Payment Information**: Enter any relevant payment information, such as payment methods or due dates.
- **Indoor Amenities**: Choose the indoor amenities available at the venue, such as Wi-Fi or air conditioning.
- **Outdoor Amenities**: Choose the outdoor amenities available at the venue, such as parking or a garden.
- **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the venue, including name, phone number, and email.
- **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the venue.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email of the contact person.
- **Contact Website**: Enter the website of the venue, if available.
