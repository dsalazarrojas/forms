# Drive Thru Service Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your drive-thru service experience, allowing us to understand and improve our service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your response for each of the questions below. You will need to answer the questions on the following pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drive Thru Speed** (`speed`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how you experienced the drive-thru service speed:
	+ Very Fast
	+ Fast
	+ Average
	+ Slow
	+ Very Slow
* **Order Accuracy** (`accuracy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate how many items were accurate or incorrect with your order:
	+ All Items Were Correct
	+ Some Items Were Incorrect
	+ Most Items Were Incorrect
* **Staff Experience** (`staff`, `select_one`, required: false): How would you rate the staff experience at the drive-thru?
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How would you rate the cleanliness of the drive-thru location?
	+ Very Clean
	+ Clean
	+ Somewhat Clean
	+ Not Very Clean
	+ Not Clean At All
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required: false): How far is the drive-thru location from you?
	+ Nearby
	+ Across the Street
	+ A Few Blocks Away
	+ More Than 5 Miles Away
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **Name (Optional)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name (optional, but if you would like to be contacted for further feedback).
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
