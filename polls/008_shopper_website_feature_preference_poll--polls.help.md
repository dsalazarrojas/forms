# Shopper Website Feature Preference Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Shopper Website Feature Preference Poll" form is designed to gather feedback and preferences from shoppers about various aspects of the website. This information will help the website improve its features and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and concisely.
2. For optional fields, choose all that apply.
3. For rating fields (1-5 scale), please select a number between 1 and 5 to rate your satisfaction or importance.
4. For the email address and phone number fields, please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
5. In the comments fields, please provide clear and concise feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your thoughts on our website feedback page?** (`shopper_website_feature_preference_page`): This is a text field where you can share your thoughts or opinions about the website's feedback page.
* **What features on our website do you find most important?** (`website_features_page`): This is a selection field where you can choose the features that you find most important on our website. Please select "Very Important", "Somewhat Important", or "Not Important" for each feature.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our website?** (`overall_satisfaction_page`): This is a number field where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-5.
* **What are the top improvements you'd like to see us make to our website?** (`improvement_priorities_page`): This is a selection field where you can choose the improvements that you'd like to see on our website. Please select "Yes", "No", or "Other" for each improvement.
* **What is your email address?** (`email_page`): This is a required field where you can enter your email address. We'll use this to contact you about any questions or concerns.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_page`): This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number. We'll use this to contact you about any questions or concerns.
* **Any comments about our website?** (`comments_page`): This is a text field where you can share any thoughts or suggestions about our website.
* **How would you rate your website satisfaction?** (`website_satisfaction_page`): This is a selection field where you can rate your satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-5.
* **What is your improvement plan for our website?** (`improvement_plan_page`): This is a selection field where you can choose the improvements that you'd like to see on our website. Please select "Yes", "No", or "Other" for each improvement.
* **What is the date of your last visit to our website?** (`date_of_visit_page`): This is a date field where you can enter the date of your last visit to our website.
* **What is the time of your last visit to our website?** (`time_of_visit_page`): This is a time field where you can enter the time of your last visit to our website.
* **Comments Page 2** (`comments_page_2`): This is a text field where you can share any additional thoughts or suggestions about our website.
