# Team Building Activity Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Activity Voting Form is designed to collect input from participants on various aspects of team building activities. This form is meant to be filled out by team members to provide their opinions and preferences on different event aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, ensuring you're on the correct page for your input.
2. For text inputs (e.g., **Team Form 1**, **Planning Details**), type your response in the provided text field.
3. For selection options (e.g., **Ranked Choices**, **Ranked Choices 2**), select one or more options that best represent your preferences.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as **required**).
5. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
