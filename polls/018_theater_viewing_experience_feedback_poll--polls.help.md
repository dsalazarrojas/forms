## Theater Viewing Experience Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Theater Viewing Experience Feedback Poll" form is designed to gather feedback from patrons after a theater event. It helps us understand their overall experience, demographics, and preferences, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Welcome** (Page 1): This page is a brief introduction to the form. Please take a moment to read it carefully.
2. Fill out each field in the order they appear.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audience Demographics** (Page 2): Please provide your age, location, and other demographic information.
* **What was your overall experience like?** (Page 3): Share your honest feedback about your experience at the theater.
* **Ratings** (Page 4): Please rate your experience on a scale (Good, Fair).
* **How likely are you to return to this theater in the next 6 months?** (Page 5): Rate your likelihood of returning to the theater.
* **Recommendations** (Page 6): If you have any suggestions or recommendations, type them here.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (Page 7): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Info** (Page 8): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your contact information.
* **Phone** (Page 9): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (Page 10): Please enter your email address.
* **Feedback** (Page 11): If you have any additional feedback, please type it here.
* **Ratings 2** (Page 12): Please rate your experience on a scale (Good, Fair).
* **Do you have any further comments?** (Page 13): Share any additional comments.
* **Contact Info 2** (Page 14): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your contact information.
* **Phone 2** (Page 15): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email 2** (Page 16): Please enter your email address.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
