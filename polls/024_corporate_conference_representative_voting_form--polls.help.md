This form is designed to collect votes and feedback from delegates at corporate conferences. The form will help to gather opinions on the conference sessions, providing a platform for representatives to express their level of agreement or disagreement with the presented topics. 

Before proceeding, check that you are the correct delegate and have not already submitted your vote for this session. Duplicate submissions will not be accepted. 

This form consists of the following fields:
* Delegate Name: a text field to input your name as a delegate. 
* Vote: a multiple-choice option to select your level of agreement or disagreement with the session. 
* Feedback: a text field for any comments or additional feedback you may have for the session. 
* Email: a field to input your work email address. 
* Phone: a text field to input your work phone number. 
* Company Name: a text field to input your company name. 
* Conference Name: a text field to input the name of the conference you are attending. 
* Session ID: a field to input the session ID of the presentation you are voting on.

Please make sure to fill in all required fields and submit the form accurately.
