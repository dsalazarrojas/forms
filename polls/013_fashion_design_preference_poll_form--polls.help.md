# Fashion Design Preference Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user preferences in fashion, including style, color, and design. The goal is to gather information to inform future design decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite fashion style from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred color palette from the available options.
3. Select your top design preference from the available options.
4. If you are 18 years old or older, enter your age.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Leave any additional feedback in the "Additional feedback" field if you have any further comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion Poll**: This is where you can select multiple fashion styles that you prefer.
  - Select the options that best fit your style preferences.
* **What type of fashion style?**: Choose the style that best describes your personal fashion taste.
  - Select one of the provided options.
* **What is your favorite color palette?**: Select the colors that you like the most.
  - You can select multiple options.
* **What is your design preference?**: Choose the style that appeals to you the most.
  - Select one option.
* **How old are you?**: This field is required if you are 18 years old or older.
  - Enter a whole number that represents your age.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
  - Make sure to enter a correct email address.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you.
  - Enter a valid phone number.
* **Additional feedback**: If you have any further comments or suggestions, use this field to share them.
  - This field is optional.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete the form.
  - Make sure to review your form before submitting.
