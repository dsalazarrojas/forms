# e_commerce_delivery_experience_poll_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their e-commerce delivery experience. It aims to gauge their satisfaction with various aspects of their delivery experience, such as timeliness, packaging condition, and communication. This feedback is valuable for us to improve our delivery services and provide better customer experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the relevant options for each field. You can find the form divided into several pages, with each page addressing a different aspect of your delivery experience. Please select your answers carefully and provide any additional comments or feedback you may have in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **e commerce delivery experience** (`1`, `note`, required: false)
Please provide a brief description of your overall e-commerce delivery experience. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand your overall impression of our delivery services.

### **Timeliness** (`2`, `number`, required: false)
Rate the timeliness of your delivery experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very bad and 5 being very good. Please select the corresponding number that best reflects your experience.

### **Packaging condition** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the option that best describes the condition of your package when it arrived. The options are:
	* Very good
	* Good
	* Neutral
	* Bad
	* Very bad

### **Communication** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false)
Rate the communication from our team regarding your delivery experience. Select the option that best describes your experience:
	* Very good
	* Good
	* Neutral
	* Bad
	* Very bad

### **Additional Comments** (`5`, `text`, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or feedback about your delivery experience, please provide them here. This will help us understand your thoughts and concerns and make improvements accordingly.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your feedback in the best possible way you can.
* Select the options that best describe your experience, even if it's not what you expected.
* Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or feedback that may help us improve our services.
