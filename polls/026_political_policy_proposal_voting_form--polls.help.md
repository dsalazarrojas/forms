# Political Policy Proposal Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Policy Proposal Voting Form is used to collect feedback and opinions from users regarding a particular policy proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you are eligible to participate in the voting process.
2. Select the "Proposal Details" field to indicate your opinion on the proposal.
3. In the "Vote" field, choose whether you "Yes" or "No" to the proposal.
4. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. In the "Eligible Voters" field, enter the number of eligible voters.
6. In the "Voting Threshold" field, enter the voting threshold.
7. In the "Vote Tracking" field, select the date and time when the vote was tracked.
8. In the "Vote Tracking Details" field, enter any additional comments on the vote tracking process.
9. In the "Eligibility Criteria" field, enter any eligibility criteria for the proposal.
10. In the "Vote Outcome" field, enter the outcome of the vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Political Policy Proposal Voting Form** (Page 1, Required: false): This page serves as a note and is not a required field.
* **Eligibility Checks** (Page 2, Required: false): This field allows you to specify the eligibility criteria for the proposal.
* **Proposal Details** (Page 3, Required: true): Select the proposal title, description, category, status, priority, and deadline to provide feedback on the proposal.
* **Vote** (Page 4, Required: true): Choose whether you support or oppose the proposal by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5, Required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts on the proposal.
* **Eligible Voters** (Page 6, Required: false): Enter the number of users who are eligible to vote on the proposal.
* **Voting Threshold** (Page 7, Required: false): Enter the minimum number of votes required for the proposal to pass.
* **Vote Tracking** (Page 8, Required: false): Select the date and time when the vote was tracked.
* **Vote Tracking Details** (Page 9, Required: false): Enter any additional comments on the vote tracking process.
* **Eligibility Criteria** (Page 10, Required: false): Enter any additional eligibility criteria for the proposal.
* **Vote Outcome** (Page 11, Required: false): Enter the outcome of the vote.
