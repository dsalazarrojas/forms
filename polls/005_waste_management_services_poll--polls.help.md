# Waste Management Services Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions on waste management services provided by your local waste management service provider. Your input will help identify areas of improvement and enhance the quality of services in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to ensure that your feedback is accurate and informative.
2. For each field, choose the option that best answers the question or provides the feedback.
3. Don't worry if you're unsure of the correct terminology or jargon; just provide your best answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you rate the service quality (service_quality)**:
	+ This question helps us understand your perception of the current service quality.
	+ Please rate the service quality on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **What issues do you face with waste management services (issues_faced)**:
	+ This field is for you to share your experiences and concerns about the waste management services.
	+ Please be as specific as possible when describing the issues you face.
* **Improvement priorities for waste management services (improvement_priorities)**:
	+ This question helps us understand what changes you would like to see in the waste management services.
	+ Please select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Improved collection and collection frequency
		- Better communication from service providers
		- Improved recycling and waste reduction programs
		- Other (please specify)
		- None of the above
* **Resident feedback (resident_feedback)**:
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the waste management services.
	+ Please be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.
* **Manager feedback (manager_feedback)**:
	+ This field is for managers or representatives of the waste management service to provide feedback and suggestions.
	+ Please be specific and provide any relevant details.
* **Manager improvement priorities (manager_improvement_priorities)**:
	+ This question helps us understand what managers think are the top priorities for improvement in waste management services.
	+ Please select all that apply from the options provided:
		- Improved collection and collection frequency
		- Better communication from service providers
		- Improved recycling and waste reduction programs
		- Other (please specify)
		- None of the above

Tips:

* Please note that some fields are optional, but your input is valuable and will help us improve the waste management services.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is anonymous, and your input will be kept confidential.
