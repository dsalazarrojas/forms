# Vote For Game Revival - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vote For Game Revival" form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions on which games should be revived or continued. This form is intended for players to express their opinions and vote for games that they would like to see revived.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the game you want to revive or continue by choosing from the list of games provided under the "game_to_revive" field.
2. Optionally, provide a brief reason why you want the selected game to be revived in the "reason_for_vote" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so that you can be contacted about your feedback.
4. Share your personal feedback and thoughts about the selected game in the "player_feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **game_to_revive** (`game_to_revive`, Select One, Required): Choose the game you would like to see revived or continued. Select one game from the list of options provided.
* **reason_for_vote** (`reason_for_vote`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief reason or explanation for your vote. This can be a simple sentence or a short paragraph.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address so that you can be contacted about your feedback.
* **player_feedback** (`player_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your personal feedback and thoughts about the selected game.

## Tips
* Make sure to select only one game from the list, as only one vote per submission is allowed.
* Keep your reason for voting brief and to the point, as this will help us better understand your opinion.
* Your email address will only be used for internal purposes, such as contacting you about your feedback.
* Your personal feedback is highly valued, so feel free to share your thoughts and ideas.
