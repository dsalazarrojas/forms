# HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect opinions and preferences from HOA residents on the landscaping plans proposed for the community. The purpose of this form is to gather information and feedback on the different options presented, ensuring that the community's needs and expectations are considered in the final decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the options presented.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form**: This is a title section that simply states the purpose of the form.
* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form (Page 1)**: This is the beginning of the form where you can start providing your feedback and preferences.
	+ The following three sections are not visible in this version of the form, but they will appear if you are accessing the form as a resident who has not previously accessed it. They are intended to gather more feedback on your thoughts and opinions.
* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form (Page 2)**: On this page, you will be asked to select one or more options for the landscaping plans. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preferences.
	+ Select all that apply for the best representation of your thoughts.
* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form (Page 3)**: This field is a text box where you can provide additional comments or suggestions about the landscaping plans.
* **HOA Landscaping Plan Voting Form (Page 4)**: This field is similar to Page 3, where you can enter any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the HOA management team for assistance.
* Your feedback and preferences are valuable to us and will be taken into consideration in the final decision-making process.
