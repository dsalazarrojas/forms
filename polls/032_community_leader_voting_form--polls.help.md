# Community Leader Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect votes from eligible community members for a community leader election. The purpose of this form is to facilitate a fair and secure voting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the voting instructions carefully and confirm your eligibility to vote.
2. Enter your name and email address (if you want to provide this information for verification purposes).
3. Select your chosen candidate from the list of options.
4. If desired, rank your preferred candidates in order of priority.
5. Optionally, provide reasons for your selection in the endorsement comments field.
6. Confirm your eligibility to vote by selecting "Confirm" from the options.
7. If you are submitting on behalf of another voter, indicate this and provide their name and your relation to them.
8. Sign your submission to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter name** (Voter name, text, required): Enter your name to verify your identity.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, optional): Provide your email address for verification purposes (not visible to other voters).
* **Select candidate** (Select candidate, select_one, required): Choose one of the candidates listed (A, B, C, or D).
* **Ranked preferences** (Ranked preferences, text, optional): Rank your preferred candidates in order of priority (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Endorsement comments** (Endorsement comments, text, optional): Provide reasons for your selection (e.g., why you chose this candidate).
* **Confirm eligibility** (Confirm eligibility, select_one, required): Select "Confirm" to verify your eligibility to vote.
* **Use proxy vote** (Use proxy vote, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are submitting on behalf of another voter.
* **Proxy details if applicable** (Proxy details if applicable, text, optional): Provide the name and relation of the proxy voter.
* **Submitter signature name** (Submitter signature name, text, required): Sign your submission to confirm your identity.
