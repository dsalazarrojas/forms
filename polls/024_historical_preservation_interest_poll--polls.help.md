# Historical Preservation Interest Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your interest in historical preservation. It helps us understand your preferences and interests in preserving historical sites, artifacts, and cultural heritage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your options from the available choices for each question.
2. Make sure to complete all the pages of the form.
3. If you are unsure of any question, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Preservation Priority (Page 1)
* **What is your interest in historical preservation?** (`preservation_priority`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your level of interest in preserving historical sites, artifacts, and cultural heritage. You can select all that apply.

### Participate Interest (Page 2)
* **What is your interest in participating in historical preservation?** (`participate_interest`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your willingness to participate in activities related to historical preservation. You can select one option that best describes your interest.

### Preservation Method (Page 3)
* **Which preservation method do you prefer?** (`preservation_method`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about your preferred method of preserving historical sites, artifacts, and cultural heritage. You can select all that apply.

### Preservation Location (Page 4)
* **Which historical preservation location do you prefer?** (`preservation_location`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your preferred location for historical preservation activities. You can select one option that best describes your preference.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.
- Thank you for taking the time to participate in this poll. Your input is valuable to us.
