# Community Noise Ordinance Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from residents on the Community Noise Ordinance. Your input will help shape the future of noise regulation in our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields, indicated by a * in the label.
2. Select the correct answer for each field.
3. Enter your thoughts and feedback in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Noise Ordinance Vote --**: * (Note: This field is just a header and not a field to fill out.)
* **Voter name**: *Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents. This is to confirm your residency and ensure you are eligible to vote.*
* **Address**: *Enter your home address in the format "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345". This is to confirm your residency in our community.*
* **Email address**: *Optional: enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates on the results and any further discussions.*
* **Position on ordinance**: *Choose how you stand on the ordinance: Support, Oppose, or Neutral.*
* **Specific concerns or support reasons**: *Optional: briefly explain your position, e.g., why you support or oppose the ordinance.*
* **Preferred noise levels guidelines**: *Optional: If you're supporting the ordinance, provide suggestions on what noise levels you'd like to see implemented.*
* **Will you discuss further with committee**: *Optional: indicate if you'd like to discuss your position further with the committee. If yes, please provide your email address.*
* **Voting by proxy**: *Optional: indicate if you're voting on behalf of someone else. If yes, please provide your proxy's name and email address.*
* **Additional comments**: *Optional: add any other thoughts or feedback on the ordinance.*
