# Group Participation Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on group participation from team members. It includes questions on sports participation, availability, and preferences to ensure the team's collective participation and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
4. Provide answers for each question in the corresponding field.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
