# Upcoming Production Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Upcoming Production Voting Form is designed to collect information and opinions from users about upcoming productions. This form helps to gather data on various aspects of the production, such as production type, production date, performance start time, and contact information. The form is used to facilitate decision-making and ensure that all necessary details are captured for the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start the voting process.
2. Select the desired options for each field as prompted.
3. Enter any required information, such as contact email and phone number.
4. Review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Type** (`production_type`, select_one): Choose whether the production is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Show Title** (`show_title`, text): Enter the title of the show.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date): Enter the date of the production.
* **Performance Start Time** (`performance_start_time`, time): Select the start time of the performance.
* **Show Length** (`show_length`, number): Enter the length of the show in minutes.
* **Voting Option** (`voting_option`, select_multiple): Select the desired voting options, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Show Score** (`show_score`, number): Enter the score of the show.
* **Theater Name** (`theater_name`, text): Enter the name of the theater.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the contact email of the production team.
* **Theater Number** (`theater_number`, text): Enter the theater number.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Show Location** (`show_location`, text): Enter the show location.
* **Description** (`description`, text): Enter a brief description of the production.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text): Enter the contact address.
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, select_one): Choose the production status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Production ID** (`production_id`, text): Enter the production ID.
* **Production Link** (`production_link`, text): Enter the production link.
* **Voting Link** (`voting_link`, text): Enter the voting link.
* **Show Image** (`show_image`, select_multiple): Select whether to show the image of the production.
* **Production Score** (`production_score`, number): Enter the production score.
* **Show Score 2** (`show_score_2`, number): Enter the secondary score of the production.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text): Enter a message for the contact person.
* **Vote Type** (`vote_type`, select_multiple): Select the vote type, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Show Status** (`show_status`, select_one): Choose the show status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
