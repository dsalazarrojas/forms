# thanksgiving_drinking_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The thanksgiving_drinking_poll form is used to collect information about user's favorite Thanksgiving drinks and their drinking habits. This form is used to gather data for a poll, hence the name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, one by one.
2. Select your favorite drink from the options on page 1.
3. If you have a favorite soda, select it on page 2.
4. Choose how often you drink on page 3.
5. If you drink, indicate how much you drink on page 4.
6. Select the most popular drink trend on page 5.
7. If you have a favorite drink not listed, describe it on page 6.
8. Provide your contact information on page 7 (email).
9. Enter your phone number on page 8.
10. Select a preferred time to drink on page 9.
11. Choose a date to drink on page 10.
11. Select a day of the week you usually drink on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which of the following drinks is your favorite on Thanksgiving?** (select multiple, required)
	+ This question is asking which drinks you enjoy the most on Thanksgiving.
* **Favorite Soda** (select one, optional)
	+ Please select your favorite type of soda.
* **How often do you drink?** (number, optional)
	+ Choose how often you drink, on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is never and 5 is every day.
* **How much do you drink?** (number, optional)
	+ If you drink, estimate how much you drink on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is none and 5 is a lot.
* **Which drink trend is most popular?** (select one, optional)
	+ Select the drink trend that is currently most popular, such as juice, wine, beer, or other.
* **Any other drink not listed?** (note, optional)
	+ If you have a favorite drink not listed, describe it here.
* **Contact Information** (email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **What time of day do you usually drink?** (time, optional)
	+ Choose the time of day when you usually drink.
* **What date do you usually drink?** (date, optional)
	+ Choose the date when you usually drink.
* **Day of the week** (select one, optional)
	+ Select the day of the week when you usually drink.
