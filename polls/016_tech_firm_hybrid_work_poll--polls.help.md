## Purpose

This form is designed to gather insights and opinions from employees about their experiences with hybrid work arrangements, particularly from home. It helps employers understand the advantages and disadvantages of working from home and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the provided sections and answer each question based on your actual experience working from home.
2.  Select the most relevant options for each question.
3.  Provide specific examples or details to support your answers whenever possible.
