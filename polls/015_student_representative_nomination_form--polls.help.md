# Student Representative Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate a representative for a group or organization, allowing users to provide suggestions for potential candidates. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the nominees' names, characteristics, and relevant details for the selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your nomination**: Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
2. Fill in the form with the necessary details for each field.
3. Submit your nomination to ensure it is considered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This is the first page of the form.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
* **Name2**: Enter any additional name-related information or clarifications about the nominee.
* **Name3**: Enter any further details or explanations about the name.
* **Field 5**: Enter a description or details about the nominee's qualifications or experiences.
* **Field 6**: Enter a reason or justification for the nomination.
* **Field 7**: Enter any additional information or context about the nominee.
* **Field 8**: Enter the nominee's contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Field 9**: Enter a message or supporting statement for the nomination.
* **Field 10**: Enter any further comments or explanations.
* **Field 11**: Enter any additional information or context about the nominee.
* **Field 12**: Enter the name of the organization or group the nominee is associated with.
* **Field 13**: Enter a description of the nominee's role or position within the organization.
* **Field 14**: Enter the nominee's skills or qualifications.
* **Field 15**: Enter the nominee's achievements or accomplishments.
* **Field 16**: Enter any further details or justifications about the nomination.
* **Field 17**: Enter any additional information or context about the nominee.
* **Field 18**: Enter any further comments or explanations.
* **Field 19**: Enter the nominee's availability or willingness to serve.
* **Field 20**: Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Field 21**: Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **Field 22**: Enter the nominee's address.
* **Field 23**: Enter any further details or justifications about the nominee.
* **Field 24**: Enter the name of a reference or person familiar with the nominee.
* **Field 25**: Enter any additional comments or explanations from the reference.
