# Hospital Patient Care Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Care Improvement Poll is a form used by patients to provide feedback on their experiences with hospital staff and services. The poll helps the hospital identify areas for improvement and ensures that patient needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the date of your visit by clicking on the "Visit Date" field.
2. Type in the name of the staff member (e.g., doctor or nurse) who provided care during your visit.
3. Rate the staff member's performance using the provided rating system (e.g., 1-10 scale).
4. Enter any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Visit Date
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of your visit to the hospital.

### Staff Name
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the staff member who provided care during your visit.

### Staff Rating
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the staff member's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department or area of the hospital where you received care (e.g., emergency room, surgery, etc.).

### Doctor
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the doctor who provided care during your visit.

### Nurse
* **Nurse** (`nurse`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the nurse who provided care during your visit.

### Hospital Quality
* **Hospital Quality** (`hospital_quality`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional feedback about the quality of care you received at the hospital.

### Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required/optional): Rate the communication skills of the staff member, such as their ability to explain medical procedures or answer questions.

### Cleanliness
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, text, required/optional): Rate the cleanliness of the hospital facilities or equipment.

### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care you received at the hospital, on a scale of 1-10.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience at the hospital.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when rating the staff member's performance.
* If you have a positive experience, be sure to provide specific details about what made it great.
* If you have a negative experience, provide constructive feedback to help the hospital identify areas for improvement.
