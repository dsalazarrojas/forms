# Music Lesson Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students regarding their music lessons. It's a crucial tool for music schools and instructors to refine their teaching methods and tailor their lessons to better meet the needs of their students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to your recent music lesson:
	* Music Lesson Feedback
	* Lesson Date
	* Teacher Name
	* Student Name
	* Instrument
	* Lesson Length
	* Feedback
	* Improvement Areas
	* Lesson Topics
2. Fill in the relevant fields with your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Lesson Feedback** (`music_lesson_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your recent music lesson. You can choose 'Yes' for feedback that was positive, 'No' for feedback that was negative.
* **Lesson Date** (`lesson_date`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date of your recent music lesson. This will help the teacher keep track of your progress and adjust the lesson plan accordingly.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your music teacher. This will help the teacher understand your feedback better and address any concerns.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, the student who attended the music lesson. This is required to associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, `text`, required: false): Enter the instrument you are learning or the type of music you are studying. This will help the teacher tailor the lesson to your specific needs.
* **Lesson Length** (`lesson_length`, `text`, required: false): Enter the length of your recent music lesson. This will help the teacher adjust the lesson plan accordingly.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional feedback you have for your music teacher. You can describe anything you found helpful or unhelpful in the lesson.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `select_one`, required: false): Select all the areas where you feel you need improvement in your music lessons. You can choose 'Yes' if you feel you need improvement in that area.
* **Lesson Topics** (`lesson_topics`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the lesson topics that you found helpful or unhelpful during your music lesson. You can choose 'Topic 1' or 'Topic 2' depending on what you learned.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, it will help the teacher improve their skills.
* Make sure to fill in all the fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, feel free to include them in the 'Feedback' field.
