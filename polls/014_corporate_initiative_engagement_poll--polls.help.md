# Corporate Initiative Engagement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions and insights on the Corporate Initiative Engagement Poll. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand how well we're doing and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read each question and select the most accurate response. You may select all that apply for questions that allow multiple selections. Please answer all questions truthfully, and don't hesitate to skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate employee participation in this initiative?** (`employee_participation`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks for your honest assessment of how engaged employees are with this initiative. Please rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very engaged".
* **How many of the following initiatives have you heard about?** (`initiative_awareness`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your level of awareness of company initiatives. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Which of the following communication channels do you use to stay updated on company news?** (`communication_channel`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the channel(s) you prefer to stay updated on company news.
* **How often do you receive email updates?** (`email_updates`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question is asking about your frequency of receiving email updates. Please select the most accurate answer.
* **Which channels do you use to provide feedback to company initiatives?** (`feedback_channel`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your preferred method of providing feedback. Select all that apply.
* **How would you rate the level of engagement on this initiative?** (`engagement_level`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking for your assessment of engagement. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very engaged".
* **How would you rate the effort put into communicating the initiative?** (`communication_effort`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about the communication effort. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very engaged".
* **How would you rate the impact of this initiative on you?** (`initiative_impact`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about your personal experience. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very positively".
* **How would you rate the impact of this initiative on departments?** (`departmental_impact`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about the departmental impact. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very positively".
* **How would you rate the image of the company?** (`company_image`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about your opinion on the company's image. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very negatively" and 5 is "very positively".
* **How would you rate your job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about your job satisfaction. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **How would you rate your team satisfaction?** (`team_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about your team satisfaction. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **How would you rate the support from our leadership team?** (`leadership_support`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about leadership support. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very supportive".
* **How aligned are company values with your own personal values?** (`company_values`, `number`, required/optional): This question is asking about the alignment of company values with personal values. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all" and 5 is "very aligned".
* **Why do you think you're aware of this initiative?** (`initiative_awareness_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for being aware of the initiative. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you think you participate in this initiative?** (`initiative_participation_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for participating. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you prefer to receive communication about company news?** (`communication_channels_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your preferred method of communication. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you provide feedback to company initiatives?** (`feedback_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for feedback. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you rate the level of engagement on this initiative?** (`engagement_level_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for assessing engagement. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you rate the image of the company?** (`company_image_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for rating the company image. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you rate your job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for assessing job satisfaction. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Why do you rate your team satisfaction?** (`team_satisfaction_why`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question is asking about your reasons for assessing team satisfaction. Select all that apply from the options provided.
