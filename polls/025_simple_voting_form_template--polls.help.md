# Simple Voting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user votes on various categories, questions, and choices. It is intended to be a simple and straightforward way to collect opinions and feedback from users. Please use this form to submit your vote, and make sure to fill out all required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category for your vote.
2. Choose a question to answer.
3. Select your choice(s) for the question.
4. Confirm your choice.
5. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
6. Enter any relevant information (e.g., email, time, text, number, or date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a category for your vote** (`form_category`, select_one): Choose a category for your vote. This field determines the type of vote you are submitting. You can select one of the three available categories: Polls, Events, or Contests.
* **Select a question to answer** (`form_question`, select_multiple): Choose a question to answer from the provided options. This field is not required but can help provide more context for your vote.
* **Select choice 1** (`form_choice_1`, select_multiple): Select one or more choices for your answer to the question. You can select multiple options.
* **Select choice 2** (`form_choice_2`, select_multiple): Similar to choice 1, select one or more choices for your answer to the question.
* **Select choice 3** (`form_choice_3`, select_multiple): Select one or more choices for your answer to the question.
* **Confirm your choice** (`form_confirm`, select_one): Confirm your choice by selecting one of the two options: Confirm or Decline.
* **Add a note** (`form_note`, text): Enter any additional notes or comments you want to include with your vote.
* **Select time** (`form_time`, time): Select a time for your vote (e.g., 8:00 AM or 10:00 PM).
* **Enter email** (`form_email`, email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your vote.
* **Enter text** (`form_text`, text): Enter any additional text you want to include with your vote.
* **Enter number** (`form_number`, number): Enter a numerical value for your vote.
* **Select date** (`form_date`, date): Select a date for your vote (e.g., January 1, 2022).
