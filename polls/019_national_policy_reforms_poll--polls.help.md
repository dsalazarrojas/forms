# National Policy Reforms Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Policy Reforms Poll is a survey designed to gather responses on national policy reforms. It aims to understand public opinions and preferences on various policy-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your honest opinion.
2. Select the relevant options for each question that best represent your view.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4. If you have any further comments or suggestions, you can use the text fields provided for this purpose.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.
