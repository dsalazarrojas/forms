# Library Genre Highlight Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and ratings from library users about their favorite genres in the library. This information will help us understand which genres are most popular among our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the "favorite_genre" question by selecting your favorite genre.
2. If you are interested in other genres, select "Yes" or "No" next to "other_genre".
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select the date and time of your last visit to the library in the "when_were_you_last_here" field.
5. Enter any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **library_genre_highlight_poll_form**:
	+ This is the main content area of the form.
* **favorite_genre**:
	+ Select your favorite genre from the available options.
* **other_genre**:
	+ Indicate if you are interested in other genres by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **when_were_you_last_here**:
	+ Enter the date and time of your last visit to the library.
* **email**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **additional_comments**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the library.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
* Your email address will be used to contact you with updates and news from the library.
