# Community Election Ballot - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Election Ballot is a digital form designed to facilitate a secure and transparent voting process for communities. It is intended for voters to submit their selections and comments in a private and confidential manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Voter Name** in the "Voter name" field.
2. Provide your **Voter Address** to ensure you are eligible to vote.
3. Select your chosen candidate for **Position 1**.
4. Select your chosen candidate for **Position 2**.
5. If you wish to, leave any **Ballot comments** about the election.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter name**: A text field for you to enter your name as a voter.
	+ Required: No, but recommended for identification purposes.
	+ Hint: Optional, but may be required depending on the ballot type.
* **Voter address**: A text field to provide your address for eligibility verification.
	+ Required: Yes, to validate your voting eligibility.
	+ Hint: Please provide the address associated with your voter registration.
* **Choice for position 1**: A single-select dropdown menu to select one candidate.
	+ Required: Yes, to ensure a valid vote.
	+ Hint: Choose one candidate from the list provided.
* **Choice for position 2**: A single-select dropdown menu to select one candidate.
	+ Required: Yes, to ensure a valid vote.
	+ Hint: Choose one candidate from the list provided.
* **Ballot comments**: A text field for optional remarks about the election.
	+ Required: No, this is an optional field.
	+ Hint: Leave any comments you may have about the election process.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your selections before submitting the form.
* Ensure your address is accurate for verification purposes.
* If you experience any issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
