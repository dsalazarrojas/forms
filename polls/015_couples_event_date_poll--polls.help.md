# Couples Event Date Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the preferred dates and times for a couples event, with options for both the individual and their partner to input their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **event_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you would prefer for the event.
2. **partner_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your partner.
3. **partner_email** (Email, Optional): Enter your partner's email address.
4. **partner_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your partner's phone number.
5. **preferred_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would prefer for the event.
6. **partner_preferred_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your partner would prefer for the event.
7. **available_dates** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple dates that you are available for the event.
8. **preferred_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you would prefer for the event.
9. **partner_preferred_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time your partner would prefer for the event.
10. **preferred_date_format** (Select One, Optional): Select the preferred date format for the event (e.g. dd-mm-yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy).
11. **partner_preferred_date_format** (Select One, Optional): Select the preferred date format for the event (e.g. dd-mm-yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy).
12. **partner_event_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would prefer for the event (this field is likely a duplicate of the event_date field, but it's included for consistency).
13. **event_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you would prefer for the event (this field is likely the main date field for the event).
