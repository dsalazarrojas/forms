# Patient Telehealth Service Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients on the quality of the telehealth service they received. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and ensure that the telehealth service meets the needs and expectations of our patients. This form is an essential part of our quality assurance process and helps us to continually improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you want to receive a follow-up email about your feedback.
3. Rate your experience in the "Rating" field, using the stars as a guide:
	* 1 star: Very dissatisfied
	* 2 stars: Somewhat dissatisfied
	* 3 stars: Neutral
	* 4 stars: Somewhat satisfied
	* 5 stars: Very satisfied
4. If you have any issues or concerns, select all that apply in the "Issue Encountered" field.
5. Share your suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions" field.
6. Enter the date you received the telehealth service in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time you received the telehealth service in the "Time" field.
8. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
9. If you have already received an email from us, enter "Yes" in the "Email Sent" field.
10. Select whether you want to be followed up with by our team in the "Follow-up Action" field.
11. Enter any additional next steps or follow-up actions you think are necessary in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive a follow-up email about your feedback.
* **Rating** (Required): Rate your experience using the stars as a guide.
* **Issue Encountered** (Required): Select all issues you experienced with the telehealth service.
*   + Technical issues: Check if you experienced any technical issues during the telehealth service.
	+ Other: Check any other issues you experienced that are not listed.
* **Suggestions** (Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the telehealth service.
* **Date** (Optional): Enter the date you received the telehealth service.
* **Time** (Optional): Enter the time you received the telehealth service.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by our team.
* **Email Sent** (Optional): Check if you have already received an email from us.
* **Follow-up Action** (Optional): Select whether you want to be followed up with by our team.
* **Next Steps** (Optional): Enter any additional next steps or follow-up actions you think are necessary.
