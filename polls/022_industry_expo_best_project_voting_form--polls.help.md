# Industry Expo Best Project Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help attendees vote on and rate projects in the Industry Expo. Your input will help project managers gauge interest and opinions on the projects presented during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form for each project you are interested in.
2. Make sure you have your project's ID and your attendee ID ready.
3. Select the category that best describes your project.
4. Vote "Yes" or "No" on the projects you want to support.
5. Rate your projects with a number from 0 to 5.
6. Provide a score between 0 and 100 for your project.
7. Add any comments about your project if you'd like.
8. Enter your contact email and phone number so we can get back in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project ID** (`project_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the project you are interested in.
* **Attendee ID** (`attendee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your attendee ID provided at the event registration.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes your project. Options are: Industry Expo Best Project, Other.
* **Vote** (`vote`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Vote "Yes" or "No" on the projects you want to support.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your project with a number from 0 to 5.
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, required: false): Provide a score between 0 and 100 for your project.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments about your project if you'd like.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email so we can get back in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your project's name.
