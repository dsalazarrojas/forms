# Travel Dress Theme Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather user opinions on a set of travel dress themes. You can provide your opinions on which themes you are in favor of, select your preferred theme, and provide additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the travel dress themes you are in favor of by checking all the boxes that apply to you.
2. Provide a number value between 1 and 10 to express how much you like each theme.
3. Choose your preferred theme by selecting one option from the available choices.
4. If you want to be contacted for further information, provide your email address.
5. You can also leave a note or comment about the themes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel dress theme options** (travel_dress_theme_voting_page_1, select_multiple, not required): Check all the boxes for the themes you are in favor of.
* **Theme 1** (travel_dress_theme_choice_1, select_one, not required): Choose one theme that you prefer from the available options.
* **Theme 2** (travel_dress_theme_choice_2, select_one, not required): Choose one theme that you prefer from the available options.
* **How much do you like this theme?** (travel_dress_theme_liking, number, not required): Provide a number value between 1 and 10 to express how much you like this theme.
* **Preferred theme 1** (travel_dress_theme_preferred_1, select_one, not required): Choose one theme that you prefer from the available options.
* **Preferred theme 2** (travel_dress_theme_preferred_2, select_one, not required): Choose one theme that you prefer from the available options.
* **Comments** (travel_dress_theme_comment, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the themes.
* **Email** (travel_dress_theme_email, email, not required): If you want to be contacted for further information, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (travel_dress_theme_phone, text, not required): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Note** (travel_dress_theme_note, note, not required): Leave a note or comment about the themes.
