# eco_friendly_business_investor_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "eco_friendly_business_investor_poll" form is designed to collect information on the preferences and priorities of eco-friendly business investors. This form is a starting point for gathering insights into sustainable investing strategies and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the prompts and select the relevant answers for each section. Each section is designed to elicit specific information, and the options provided are meant to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `label: Introduction`, `type: note`)
* This section is an introduction to the form and its purpose.

### 2. **Sustainable Investor Preferences** (`id: 2`, `label: Sustainable Investor Preferences`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select all the sustainable investor preferences that apply to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You consider sustainable investing when making investment decisions.
	+ **No**: You do not consider sustainable investing when making investment decisions.

### 3. **Decision Drivers** (`id: 3`, `label: Decision Drivers`, `type: select_one`)
* Select the primary driver for your investment decisions:
	+ **Yes**: Financial returns are the primary driver for your investment decisions.
	+ **No**: Impact and sustainability are the primary driver for your investment decisions.

### 4. **Impact Investors** (`id: 4`, `label: Impact Investors`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select all the impact investors that are relevant to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You invest in organizations that have a positive social or environmental impact.
	+ **No**: You do not invest in organizations with a positive social or environmental impact.

### 5. **Other Impact Investors** (`id: 5`, `label: Other Impact Investors`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select any other impact investors that are relevant to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You invest in organizations that have a positive social or environmental impact.
	+ **No**: You do not invest in organizations with a positive social or environmental impact.

### 6. **Eco-Friendly Principles** (`id: 6`, `label: Eco-Friendly Principles`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select all the eco-friendly principles that are relevant to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You invest in organizations that adhere to eco-friendly principles.
	+ **No**: You do not invest in organizations that adhere to eco-friendly principles.

### 7. **Decision Factors** (`id: 7`, `label: Decision Factors`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select all the decision factors that are relevant to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You consider financial considerations when making investment decisions.
	+ **No**: You do not consider financial considerations when making investment decisions.

### 8. **Other Decision Factors** (`id: 8`, `label: Other Decision Factors`, `type: select_multiple`)
* Select any other decision factors that are relevant to your investing strategy:
	+ **Yes**: You consider social or environmental considerations when making investment decisions.
	+ **No**: You do not consider social or environmental considerations when making investment decisions.

### 9. **Investor Perspectives** (`id: 9`, `label: Investor Perspectives`, `type: note`)
* Please provide any additional perspectives or comments that inform your investment strategy.

### 10. **Additional Comments** (`id: 10`, `label: Additional Comments`, `type: note`)
* Provide any additional comments or insights that may be helpful in understanding your investing strategy.

## Tips
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be specific and provide clear explanations where possible.
* Feel free to add additional comments or insights as needed.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
