# # Tech Gadget Consumer Preference Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences and experiences with gadgets to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question and provide any additional information that you think is relevant. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Gadget Ownership
* **Gadget Ownership** (`gadget_ownership`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you about the type of gadgets you own, such as Mobile Phone, Tablet, Smartwatch, Laptop, Desktop, or Smart TV.

### Feature Priorities
* **Feature Priorities** (`feature_priorities`, text, required): List the top 3 features you value the most in a new gadget. For example, "Camera quality, Battery life, Display size".

### Buying Intent
* **Buying Intent** (`buying_intent`, select_one, required): This question asks about your intention to buy a new gadget. Please select "I am looking to buy" if you plan to buy, "Not looking to buy" if you are not looking to buy, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.

### Age
* **What is your age?** (`demographic_age`, select_one, required): Please select your age group from the options provided.

### Gender
* **What is your gender?** (`demographic_gender`, select_one, required): Please select your gender from the options provided.

### Income
* **What is your income?** (`demographic_income`, select_one, required): This question asks about your income level. Please select your income range from the options provided.

### Top 3 Features
* **What are the top 3 features you value in a new gadget?** (`gadget_features`, text, required): List the top 3 features you value the most in a new gadget.

### Education
* **What is your level of education?** (`demographic_education`, select_one, required): Please select your level of education from the options provided.

### Job Role
* **What is your current job role?** (`demographic_job`, select_one, required): This question asks about your current job role. Please select one of the options provided.

### City
* **What city do you live in?** (`demographic_city`, select_one, required): Please select the city where you live.

### Gadget Purchase Frequency
* **How often do you purchase gadgets?** (`gadget_purchase`, select_one, required): This question asks about how often you purchase gadgets. Please select one of the options provided.

### Gadget Repair
* **Have you ever had a gadget repaired?** (`gadget_repair`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if you have ever had a gadget repaired, "No" if you have not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.

### Reason for Returning a Gadget
* **Why did you return a gadget in the past?** (`gadget_return`, select_multiple, required): If you have ever returned a gadget, please select the reason for returning it.

### Gadget Warranty
* **What is your understanding of a warranty for a gadget?** (`gadget_warranty`, text, required): Please provide your understanding of a warranty for a gadget.

### Recommend Gadget
* **Would you recommend our gadget to a friend or family member?** (`gadget_recommend`, select_multiple, required): Please select "Yes" if you would recommend, "No" if you would not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure.

### Gadget Satisfaction
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with our gadget?** (`gadget_satisfaction`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.

### Reason for Returning a Gadget
* **If yes, what reasons for returning a gadget?** (`gadget_return_reasons`, text, required): If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, please provide the reasons for returning the gadget.

### Interests
* **Which of the following interests you?** (`demographic_interest`, text, required): Please provide your interests from the options provided.

### Gadget Frequency of Use
* **How often do you use our gadgets?** (`gadget_frequent_use`, select_multiple, required): Please select how often you use our gadgets.

### Language
* **Which language do you speak?** (`demographic_language`, select_one, required): This question asks about the language you speak. Please select one of the options provided.

### Budget
* **What is your budget for a new gadget?** (`gadget_price`, number, required): Please provide your budget for a new gadget.

### Price Range
* **What price range for a new gadget?** (`gadget_price_range`, text, required): This question asks about the price range you are looking for in a new gadget. Please provide your answer.

### Gadget Satisfaction Level
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with our gadget?** (`gadget_satisfaction_level`, number, required): Please rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.

### Reason for Satisfaction or Dissatisfaction
* **Why are you satisfied or not satisfied with our gadget?** (`gadget_satisfaction_why`, text, required): Please provide your reasons for satisfaction or dissatisfaction.

### Improvement
* **What can we do to improve our gadget?** (`gadget_satisfaction_improvement`, text, required): Finally, please provide your suggestions for improving our gadget.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully to help us improve our services.
- Use the available options provided for each question.
