# SPA Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **SPA Evaluation Form** is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from customers about the aesthetic services and products provided by the spa. This form helps spa management to assess the quality of services, identify areas for improvement, and provide better customer experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read the form carefully and fill in the required information.
*   Answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate feedback.
*   Select relevant options from the drop-down menus when applicable.
*   Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Evaluation**: Describe your overall experience with our product or service.
*   **Product Name**: Please provide the name of the product or service you received.
*   **Medical Information**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your experience with our services, please list them here.
*   **Product Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with our product or service on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Product Rating Comment**: Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with our product or service.
*   **Skin Care Services**: Check all the services you received during your visit, such as Facial, Massage, Waxing, Hair Removal, Microderm, or Other.
*   **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your experience, please select one that applies.
*   **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience with our spa.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our management team for assistance.
*   We value your feedback and appreciate your time in helping us improve our services.
