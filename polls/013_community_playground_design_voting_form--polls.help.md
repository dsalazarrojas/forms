# Community Playground Design Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather input from community members on the preferred design for the new public space. By participating in this form, you will have the opportunity to express your opinions on what features are most important to you and how you envision the playground being used. Your input will be instrumental in shaping the final design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page, and for each field, select or enter the information as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Community Playground Design Vote --** (`voting_intro_note`, note, required: false)
	+ This is an introduction to the form and the design process.
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in the results.
* **Your Neighborhood or General Address** (`neighborhood_location`, text, required: true)
	+ This will help us ensure a diverse range of responses.
* **Which design option is your favorite?** (`design_choice`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Select one of the three provided design options: "Design A - The Natural Woods", "Design B - Modern Splash Park", or "Design C - Sky-High Adventure".
* **Rate the accessibility features of these designs** (`accessibility_rating`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Rate the designs based on their accessibility features on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Which features are most important to you?** (`priority_features`, select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Select up to three features that are most important to you:
		- Swings
		- Slides
		- Climbing Structures
		- Picnic Areas
		- Shaded Seating
		- Public Art
* **Is there any one feature that is a must-have for you?** (`top_priority_feature_text`, text, required: true)
	+ Enter one feature that is absolutely essential to you.
* **Any suggestions for design improvements?** (`design_improvements_feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ Share any ideas you may have to enhance the design.
* **Do you have children who will use this playground?** (`have_children_check`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Indicate whether you have children who will be using the playground.
* **How many children are in your household?** (`household_children_count`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of children in your household.
* **Ages of children who will use the playground** (`household_children_ages`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the ages of the children in your household.
* **Would you be interested in a community build day?** (`volunteer_intent`, select_one, required: true)
	+ Indicate your interest in participating in a community build day.
* **Contact Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address if you are interested in volunteering.
* **-- Next Steps --** (`next_steps_note`, note, required: false)
	+ This is a note about the next steps in the process.
* **Today's Date** (`vote_submission_date`, date, required: true)
	+ This is the date the form is submitted.
