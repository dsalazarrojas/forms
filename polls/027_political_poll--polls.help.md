# Political Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Poll form is designed to gather opinions on various political topics, allowing you to understand the preferences and opinions of the respondents. This form is part of our polls category and helps us to collect relevant and non-duplicating data for further analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the political party of your choice in the "Political Party" field.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Political Candidate" field, indicating your support or opposition to a specific candidate.
3. For the "Political Issue" field, select all the issues you support or oppose.
4. In the "Political Party Affiliation" field, choose whether you have an affiliation or not with a political party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Political Party**: This field allows you to select the political party you support or have the most affinity for. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Political Candidate**: Indicate whether you support or oppose a specific candidate by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Political Issue**: Select all the issues you support or oppose. You can choose multiple options.
* **Political Party Affiliation**: Check the box if you have a political party affiliation or not. You can select "Yes" or "No" for this field.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to provide valuable insights to our team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Make sure to fill out the form only once to avoid duplication of data.
