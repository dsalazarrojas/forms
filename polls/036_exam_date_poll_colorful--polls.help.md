# Exam Date Poll Colorful - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple poll designed to collect information about exam dates and preferences. It is intended to be completed by users who want to express their opinions on exam dates and color preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "exam_date" field with your preferred exam date. This field is optional, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a preferred exam date.
2. Select one or more colors from the "color" field that you like. This field is required, so make sure to select at least one color.
3. Choose a time period for the exam from the "exam_period" field. This field is also required, so choose one option from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exam_date** (`exam_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred exam date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **color** (`color`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more colors that you like. You can choose multiple colors by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) while selecting colors.
* **exam_period** (`exam_period`, select_one, required): Choose a time period for the exam that you prefer.
