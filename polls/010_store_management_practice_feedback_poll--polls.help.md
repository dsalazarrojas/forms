# Store Management Practice Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and practice responses from store managers on their current store management practices. The form is a poll that allows managers to express their opinions on different aspects of store management, including their satisfaction with store location, manager's name and email, and feedback on store practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of store management practice you are providing feedback on. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
2. Choose the subcategory that best describes the specific aspect of store management you are providing feedback on. This will help us understand the specific topic of your feedback.
3. Provide a brief description of the store management practice you are providing feedback on.
4. Select the store location where you are providing feedback on.
5. Enter the name and email of the store manager (if applicable).
6. Answer the feedback question with a brief response.
7. Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with the feedback question.
8. Choose a date and time for when you submitted the feedback.
9. Add any additional comments about your feedback.
