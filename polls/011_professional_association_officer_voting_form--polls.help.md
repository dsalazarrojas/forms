## Professional Association Officer Voting Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to collect votes for officer positions within a professional association. The form allows users to select their preferred officer positions, submit their vote, and provide necessary contact information for the chapter they represent.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the officer positions for which you would like to vote.
3. Select your chapter's name, email, phone number, and location.
4. Choose the date of the election.
5. Enter the term start and end dates for the term.
6. Select the chapter's organizers.
7. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting your vote.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Officer Positions**: Select the positions for which you would like to vote.
	+ This field is required. You must select at least one option.
* **Candidate List**: This field is not used in the current form.
* **Vote**: This is the text field for your vote.
* **Candidate Positions**: Select from the list of available positions.
* **Election Results**: This is a note field for election results.
* **Chapter Name**: Enter the name of your chapter.
* This field is required.
* **Chapter Location**: Enter the location of your chapter.
* **Election Date**: Select the date of the election.
* This field is required.
* **Chapter Email**: Enter your chapter's email address.
* This field is required.
* **Chapter Phone**: Enter your chapter's phone number.
* This field is required.
* **Officer Name**: Enter the name of the officer.
* **Officer Title**: Enter the title of the officer.
* This field is required.
* **Officer Position**: Select the officer's position.
* This field is required.
* **Term Length**: Enter the length of the officer's term.
* **Term Start**: Select the start date of the officer's term.
* This field is required.
* **Term End**: Select the end date of the officer's term.
* This field is required.
* **Chapter Organizers**: Select the chapter's organizers.
* This field is not used in the current form.

Tips: Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting your vote. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the chapter's organizers.
