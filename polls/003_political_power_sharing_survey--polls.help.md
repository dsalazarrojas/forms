# Political Power Sharing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on how they think power should be shared or distributed within a political institution or community. The questions are intended to be open-ended and will help in understanding the general opinions and sentiments of the respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Ensure that you have read and understood each question before submitting your answers.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
5. Submit your answers when you are finished.
