# Poll Answer Slip - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect answers from participants in a poll. It is intended to be used by poll administrators to gather data on participant responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are logged in and have access to this form.
2. Select the question you would like to answer from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the title of the question you are answering.
4. Choose your answer(s) from the options provided.
5. Enter any additional text if necessary.
6. Click submit to submit your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **question id** (id: 1, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the question being asked.
* **title** (id: 2, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the question.
* **question** (id: 3, text, required/optional): Enter the question being asked.
* **answers** (id: 4, select multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more answers from the provided options.
* **answer id** (id: 5, number, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the answer you have selected.
* **participant id** (id: 6, number, required/optional): Enter the participant's unique identifier.
* **participant title** (id: 7, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's title.
* **participant question title** (id: 8, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the question being answered by the participant.
* **participant question id** (id: 9, number, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for the question being answered by the participant.
* **participant question** (id: 10, text, required/optional): Enter the question being answered by the participant.
* **answer text** (id: 11, text, required/optional): Enter the text of the answer being provided.
* **answer** (id: 12, text, required/optional): Enter the answer being provided.
* **participant answer** (id: 13, select one, required/optional): Choose the answer that best matches your response.
* **participant answer text** (id: 14, text, required/optional): Enter any additional text for your response.
* **participant question answer** (id: 15, select multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple answers that best match your question.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the form administrator.
