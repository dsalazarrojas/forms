# Botox Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather patient information and ensure informed consent for Botox treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. If applicable, provide your medical record number.
4. Select the date you are scheduled for the Botox treatment.
5. Choose the type of Botox treatment you are seeking.
6. Select all areas of the face that you would like to be treated.
7. List any medical conditions that apply to you.
8. List all your current medications.
9. Indicate if you are taking any blood thinning medications.
10. Answer if you have received Botox before.
11. Confirm that you understand the potential risks and side effects.
12. Indicate if you have discussed alternative treatments with your provider.
13. Confirm that you have had all your questions answered.
14. Give your voluntary consent for the treatment.
15. Agree to attend follow-up appointments as recommended.
16. Choose how you consent to clinical photography for medical records.
17. Sign your name as electronic signature.
18. Provide the date you signed the form.
19. Enter the name of your treating physician or practitioner.
20. Sign the form with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Name (Full Legal Name) (**Patient Name**):
* This is the name you use on your identification and should be accurate for insurance purposes.

### 2. Date of Birth (MM/DD/YYYY) (**Patient DOB**):
* Enter your date of birth in the specified format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### 3. Patient ID Number (Optional) (**Patient ID**):
* If you have a medical record number, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

### 4. Procedure Date (Scheduled Date) (**Procedure Date**):
* Enter the date you are scheduled for the Botox treatment.

### 5. Procedure Type (Select One) (**Procedure Type**):
* Choose the type of Botox treatment you are seeking.

### 6. Treatment Areas (Select Multiple) (**Treatment Areas**):
* Select all areas of the face you would like to be treated.

### 7. Relevant Medical History (Select Multiple) (**Medical History**):
* List any medical conditions that apply to you.

### 8. Current Medications (List All) (**Current Medications**):
* Include all your current medications, including prescriptions, OTC, and supplements.

### 9. Blood Thinner Use (Select One) (**Blood Thinner Use**):
* Indicate if you are taking any blood thinning medications.

### 10. Previous Botox (Select One) (**Previous Botox**):
* Answer if you have received Botox before.

### 11. Understanding of Risks (Select One) (**Understand Risks**):
* Confirm that you have read and understand the potential risks and side effects associated with Botox treatments.

### 12. Alternatives Discussed (Select One) (**Alternatives Discussed**):
* Indicate if you have discussed alternative treatments with your provider.

### 13. Questions Answered (Select One) (**Questions Answered**):
* Confirm that all your questions have been answered by your provider.

### 14. Voluntary Consent (Select One) (**Consent**):
* Give your voluntary consent for the treatment.

### 15. Follow-Up Agreement (Select One) (**Follow-Up**):
* Agree to attend follow-up appointments as recommended by your provider.

### 16. Photo Documentation (Select One) (**Photo Documentation**):
* Choose how you consent to clinical photography for medical records.

### 17. Patient Signature (Electronic Signature) (**Patient Signature**):
* Type your full name as electronic signature.

### 18. Date of Signature (Date Signed) (**Signature Date**):
* Enter the date you signed the form.

### 19. Provider Name (Name of Treating Physician or Practitioner) (**Provider Name**):
* Enter the name of your treating physician or practitioner.

### 20. Provider Signature (Provider Electronic Signature) (**Provider Signature**):
* Sign the form with your electronic signature.
