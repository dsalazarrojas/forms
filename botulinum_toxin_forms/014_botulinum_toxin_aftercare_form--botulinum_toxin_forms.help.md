# Botulinum Toxin Aftercare Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botulinum Toxin Aftercare Form is designed to help patients understand and follow the necessary guidelines after receiving botulinum toxin treatments. This form will guide patients in understanding the aftercare instructions, expected side effects, and necessary precautions to take during the recovery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Treatment Date**: Provide the date when you received the botulinum toxin treatment.
	* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address for further communication.
2. Confirm your treatment details:
	* **Treatment Areas**: Select the areas on your body that received the treatment.
3. Verify your understanding of aftercare instructions:
	* **Aftercare Instructions Reviewed**: Confirm that you have received and understood the aftercare instructions.
	* **Activity Restrictions Understood**: Confirm that you understand the activity restrictions, such as avoiding strenuous exercise for 24 hours.
	* **Positioning Restrictions Understood**: Confirm that you understand the positioning restrictions, such as remaining upright for 4 hours after treatment.
	* **Touching Restrictions Understood**: Confirm that you understand not to touch or massage treated areas.
	* **Heat Exposure Restrictions**: Confirm that you understand to avoid saunas, hot tubs, and excessive heat.
	* **Alcohol Restrictions**: Confirm that you understand to avoid alcohol for 24 hours.
	* **Makeup Application**: Confirm that you understand when you can apply makeup after treatment.
4. Report any symptoms or concerns:
	* **Expected Side Effects Understood**: Confirm that you understand normal side effects like redness, swelling, or bruising.
	* **Current Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are experiencing, such as mild redness, swelling, or bruising.
	* **Symptom Severity**: Rate your discomfort level, from no discomfort to severe.
	* **Questions or Concerns**: List any questions or concerns you have.
5. Acknowledge and sign the form:
	* **Patient Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you have received and understood the aftercare instructions.
	* **Patient Signature**: Sign your name as a confirmation of your understanding.
