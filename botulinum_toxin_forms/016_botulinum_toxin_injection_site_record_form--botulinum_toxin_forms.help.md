# Botulinum Toxin Injection Site Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botulinum Toxin Injection Site Record Form is used to document the details of a patient's botulinum toxin injection procedure. This form helps providers track and record treatment information, including patient demographics, injection sites, dosages, and post-treatment instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name and ID number to ensure accurate identification.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter the date of the procedure, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Select the brand of botulinum toxin used for the procedure from the provided list.
5. Enter the lot number of the botulinum toxin product used.
6. Enter the product's expiration date.
7. Enter the amount of saline used for reconstitution in milliliters (ml).
8. Enter the total units of botulinum toxin administered.
9. Select the number of injection sites for the forehead, glabellar, crow's feet, bunny line, masseter, and neck areas. Enter the total units injected in each area.
10. Choose the size of the needle used from the provided options.
11. Select the type of anesthesia used during the procedure, if any.
12. Evaluate the patient's tolerance to the procedure, selecting from the provided options.
13. Record any immediate side effects observed after the procedure.
14. Determine if post-treatment instructions were provided to the patient, selecting the relevant option.
15. Check if a follow-up appointment has been scheduled.
16. Enter the date of the follow-up appointment, if scheduled.
17. Sign and date the form electronically.
17. Date the record entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Treatment Date**: Enter the date of the procedure in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Treating Provider**: Enter the name of the physician or injector who performed the procedure.
* **Product Name**: Select the brand of botulinum toxin used from the provided list.
* **Lot Number**: Enter the product's lot number.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the product's expiration date.
* **Dilution Amount**: Enter the amount of saline used for reconstitution in milliliters (ml).
* **Total Units Used**: Enter the total units of botulinum toxin administered.
* **Forehead Sites**: Select the number of injection sites in the forehead area.
* **Forehead Units**: Enter the total units injected in the forehead area.
* **Glabellar Sites**: Select the number of injection sites between the eyebrows.
* **Glabellar Units**: Enter the total units injected in the glabellar area.
* **Crow's Feet Sites**: Select the number of injection sites around the eyes.
* **Crow's Feet Units**: Enter the total units injected in the crow's feet area.
* **Bunny Line Sites**: Select the number of injection sites on the nose.
* **Bunny Line Units**: Enter the total units injected in the nose area.
* **Masseter Sites**: Select the number of injection sites in the masseter muscles.
* **Masseter Units**: Enter the total units injected in the masseter muscles.
* **Neck Sites**: Select the number of injection sites in the neck.
* **Neck Units**: Enter the total units injected in the neck.
* **Other Sites**: Enter any other areas treated, if applicable.
* **Other Units**: Enter the total units injected in other areas.
* **Needle Gauge**: Select the size of the needle used from the provided options.
* **Anesthesia Used**: Select the type of anesthesia used, if any.
* **Patient Tolerance**: Evaluate the patient's tolerance to the procedure.
* **Immediate Side Effects**: Record any immediate side effects observed.
* **Post-Treatment Instructions**: Determine if post-treatment instructions were provided.
* **Follow-up Scheduled**: Check if a follow-up appointment has been scheduled.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment, if scheduled.
* **Provider Signature**: Enter the electronic signature of the treating provider.
* **Documentation Date**: Date the record entry.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete documentation.
- Check for any duplicate entries before submitting the form.
- Use the provided options to select the correct information and ensure accuracy.
- If a follow-up appointment is scheduled, make sure to enter the correct date.
- Sign and date the form electronically to verify completion.
