# Botulinum Toxin Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botulinum Toxin Treatment Record Form is used to record new botulinum toxin treatments. It helps keep accurate and consistent records of client information, treatment procedures, outcomes, and follow-up schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Client Name" field with the client's full legal name.
2. Enter the client's ID number, if applicable.
3. Provide the client's phone number for follow-up communication.
4. Enter the client's email address for follow-up communication.
5. Fill out the "Treatment Date" field with the date the procedure was performed.
6. Enter the "Time of Treatment" field with the start time of the procedure, if applicable.
7. Select the product used from the "Product Used" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the total units of the product administered.
9. In the "Areas Treated" section, select all areas injected from the list provided.
10. Provide a detailed breakdown of the injection locations and units administered in the "Specific injection locations and units per area" field, if desired.
11. Fill out the "Immediate appearance post-treatment" field with notes on any redness, swelling, or symmetry issues.
12. Select the client's satisfaction level with the treatment in the "Client Satisfaction Level" field.
13. In the "Follow-up Schedule" section, schedule a follow-up appointment for the client, typically 2 weeks after the treatment.
14. Select the "Provider Acknowledgment" field to confirm that the treatment record is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (2, text, required): Enter the client's full legal name.
* **Client ID Number** (3, text, optional): Enter the client's medical record or ID number, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter the client's primary phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Email Address** (5, email, optional): Enter the client's email address for follow-up communication.
* **Treatment Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date the procedure was performed.
* **Time of Treatment** (8, time, optional): Enter the start time of the procedure, if applicable.
* **Provider Name** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the treating professional.
* **Product Used** (10, select_one, required): Select the product used for the treatment from the dropdown menu.
* **Total Units Administered** (11, number, required): Enter the total units of the product administered.
* **Areas Treated** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all areas injected from the list provided.
* **Specific Injection Locations and Units per Area** (14, text, optional): Provide a detailed breakdown of the injection locations and units administered, if desired.
* **Immediate Appearance Post-Treatment** (16, text, optional): Enter notes on any redness, swelling, or symmetry issues.
* **Client Satisfaction Level** (17, select_one, optional): Select the client's satisfaction level with the treatment.
* **Recommended Follow-up Date** (19, date, required): Schedule a follow-up appointment for the client, typically 2 weeks after the treatment.
* **Next Treatment Scheduled** (20, date, optional): Schedule the next treatment, if applicable.
* **Provider Acknowledgment** (21, select_one, required): Select "I confirm this record is accurate" to complete the form.
