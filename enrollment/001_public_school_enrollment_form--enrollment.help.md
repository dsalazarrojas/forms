# Public School Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public School Enrollment Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to enroll their child in a public school. This form is designed to gather essential details to ensure a smooth and successful enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may delay the enrollment process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the school administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Information (Page 1)** (`public_school_enrollment_form`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is the introduction to the form and provides a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **General Information (Page 2)** (`general_information`, text, required: false):
	+ In this section, please provide any general information about yourself and your child, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Agreement Details (Page 3)** (`agreement_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Please provide any specific details about your agreement or understanding regarding the enrollment.
* **Terms And Agreements (Page 4)** (`terms_and_agreements`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to indicate your level of agreement with the terms and conditions of the school. Please select "I agree", "Not at all", or "Somewhat" to indicate your understanding.
* **Submit (Page 5)** (`submit_button`, text, required: false):
	+ Once you have filled out the form, click "Submit" to complete the enrollment process.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the school administration for assistance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you encounter any issues, please reach out to us for support.
