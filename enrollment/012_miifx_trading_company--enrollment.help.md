# miifx_trading_company_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential investors or traders who want to join a trading company. It's intended to gather the necessary details to process your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. If you're interested in joining over one million, select "Join" on the second page.
2. Choose whether you're an "Investor" or "Trader" on the third page.
3. Complete the relevant section with your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Join over one million** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate your interest in joining a large group of people. If you want to join over one million, please provide a brief description in this field.
*   **Register to learn more** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select "Join" to proceed with the registration process.
*   **Investor or trader** (`3`, `text`, required): Please indicate whether you're interested in being an "Investor" or "Trader". This will help us understand your role within the trading company.
