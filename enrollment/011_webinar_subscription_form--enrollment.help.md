# Webinar Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Subscription Form is designed for users to subscribe to a webinar event. This form allows you to provide your contact information and select your relevant role and events of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. If you have a company affiliation, enter it in the Company field.
5. Select your relevant role from the options provided.
6. Select the events you are interested in (Upcoming Webinar, Past Webinar, or All Events).
7. Check the Accept Terms of Service checkbox to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Webinar Subscription** (`webinar_subscription_form`): Enter a brief description of your interest in the webinar event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`): If you have a company affiliation, enter it here.
* **Role** (`role`): Select your relevant role (Customer, Sales Representative, Marketing Specialist, CEO, or Other).
* **Event** (`event`): Select the events you are interested in (Upcoming Webinar, Past Webinar, or All Events).
* **Accept Terms of Service** (`terms_accepted`): Check this box to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms.
