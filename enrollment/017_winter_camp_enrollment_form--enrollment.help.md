# winter_camp_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The winter_camp_enrollment_form is designed to collect information from parents/guardians for the Winter Camp Enrollment process. This form is meant for parents to provide details about themselves and their children, along with selecting a suitable camp for their child's enrollment and specifying dates available for attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the on-screen instructions and fill out the form accordingly.
- Fill in the required fields as prompted.
- Ensure that all fields are accurately and thoroughly completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Info**: A text field where parents can provide any relevant personal information about themselves.
  - This field is an open text field for parents to provide any necessary personal details about themselves.
  - This field is not required for submission, but is recommended for camp administrators to have a better understanding of the parent's identity and context.
  
- **Child Info**: Another text field where parents can provide details about their child.
  - Similar to the Parent Info field, this is an open text field for parents to provide any necessary details about their child.
  - Like the Parent Info field, this is also not required for submission, but recommended for camp administrators to have a better understanding of the child's identity and context.

- **Select A Camp**: A multiple-select field where parents can choose one or multiple camps for their child's enrollment.
  - This field is required for submission, as it directly affects the child's placement in the chosen camps.
  - Parents can choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
  - The options provided are 'Camp 1' and 'Camp 2', representing different available camp choices.

- **Dates Available**: A date field where parents can specify the dates their child is available for attendance.
  - This field is not required for submission, but is useful for camp administrators to plan the child's schedule.

- **Start Time**: A time field where parents can specify the start time for their child's attendance.
  - This field is not required for submission, but is helpful for camp administrators to plan the child's schedule.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurately completed before submission.
- Double-check the chosen camps and dates available for accuracy.
- If unsure about any field, contact the designated camp administrators for assistance.
