# Coaching Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a coaching application form designed for individuals interested in becoming coaches for various sports and activities. It is meant to gather information about your background, experience, and availability to help us determine your fit for a coaching position. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide your date of birth and highest level of education completed.
3. Answer questions about your coaching experience, certifications, and background.
4. Select your preferred coaching position, sport or activity, and availability.
5. Provide information about your references, including their name, phone number, and relationship with you.
6. Enter your preferred start date for coaching and any additional comments.
7. Finally, confirm your consent to a background check and provide any additional details about your background if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, select_one, required): Select which coaching position you are applying for (Head Coach, Assistant Coach, Volunteer Coach, Youth Coach, Skills Coach, Fitness Coach).
* **Sport or Activity** (`sport_or_activity`, text, required): Enter the sport or activity you will be coaching.
* **Age Group Preference** (`age_group_preference`, select_one, required): Select which age group you prefer to coach (Ages 4-7, Ages 8-12, Ages 13-15, Ages 16-18, Adult, No Preference).
* **Coaching Experience Level** (`coaching_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of coaching experience (No Experience, 1-2 Years, 3-5 Years, 5-10 Years, Over 10 Years).
* **Previous Teams or Organizations** (`previous_teams`, text, optional): Enter any previous teams or organizations you have coached for.
* **Playing Experience** (`playing_experience`, text, optional): Describe your playing background.
* **Coaching Certifications Held** (`certifications_held`, text, optional): List any coaching certifications you hold.
* **CPR and First Aid Certified** (`cpr_first_aid_certified`, select_one, required): Select if you have current CPR and first aid certification (Yes Current, Yes But Expired, False).
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed (High School, Some College, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctorate).
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select when you are available to coach (Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Saturday Mornings, Saturday Afternoons, Sunday Mornings, Sunday Afternoons).
* **Season Preference** (`season_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select which seasons you are available to coach (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Year Round).
* **Consent to Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to a background check (Yes I Consent, False).
* **Any Criminal History** (`background_check_history`, select_one, required): Select if you have any criminal history (False, Yes Please Explain).
* **Background Check Details** (`background_check_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your background if applicable.
* **Your Coaching Philosophy** (`coaching_philosophy`, text, required): Describe your approach to coaching.
* **Why Do You Want to Coach** (`why_do_you_want_to_coach`, text, required): Enter your motivation for wanting to coach.
* **Your Strengths as a Coach** (`strengths_as_coach`, text, required): Enter what makes you a strong coach.
* **Number of References Provided** (`references_count`, select_one, required): Select how many references you can provide (1-2, 3-4, 5 or More).
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, text, required): Enter how you know this reference.
* **When Can You Start** (`start_date_availability`, date, required): Enter your available start date.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
