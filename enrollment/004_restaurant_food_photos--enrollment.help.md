# Restaurant Food Photos - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restaurant Food Photos form is designed for capturing and sharing information about food photos from restaurants. This form helps you to provide a visual representation of your menu items, facilitating better food ordering and management.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Tips
- Please fill out the form completely, especially if you're providing a photo URL.
- Make sure to upload a high-quality photo for better representation.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Food Photos** (text, not required): This is a placeholder for the form title. You can ignore it, as it's not a field you'll be filling out.
* **Take a photo of the menu item being plated** (text, not required): This field is where you describe the food item being photographed. Describe the dish as if you're explaining it to someone who has never seen it before.
* **Client Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the client (restaurant) who provided this photo.
* **Date delivered** (date, not required): Indicate the date on which the food was delivered for this photo.
* **Time delivered** (time, not required): Specify the time the food was delivered for this photo.
* **Menu item name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the menu item being photographed.
* **Restaurant name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the restaurant where the photo was taken.
* **Photo description** (text, not required): Describe the photo, including any notable features or details.
* **Photo upload** (text, not required): Upload a high-quality photo of the food item.
* **Photo URL** (text, not required): If the photo is publicly available online, you can provide its URL instead of uploading it.
* **Client phone** (text, not required): Enter the phone number of the client who provided the photo.
* **Assigned tool** (text, not required): If applicable, enter the tool or equipment used to take the photo.
* **Category** (text, not required): Enter a category for the photo, such as "breakfast," "lunch," etc.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help ensure that the photo is properly indexed and accessible for future reference. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
