# School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Sports Clinic Enrollment Form is used by parents or guardians to enroll their children into the school sports clinic program. This form collects necessary information for registration, payment, and medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
3. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
4. Select the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
5. Choose the program the student wants to enroll in from the "Program Selection" field.
6. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
7. Enter any additional payment method details in the "Payment Method Details" field.
8. Provide the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
10. Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the student in the "Relationship" field.
11. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
12. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
13. Describe any medical conditions the student has in the "Medical Conditions" field.
14. Enter the student's street address in the "Street Address" field.
15. Enter the student's city in the "City" field.
16. Enter the student's state in the "State" field.
17. Enter the student's zip code in the "Zip" field.
18. Enter the program cost in the "Program Cost" field.
19. Select the payment status from the "Payment Status" field.
20. Select the program status from the "Program Status" field.
21. Select the student's attendance status from the "Student Attendance" field.
22. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
23. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
24. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, select_one, optional): Choose the program the student wants to enroll in.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method.
* **Other (Please specify)** (`payment_method_other`, text, optional): If the payment method is "Other", provide the payment method details.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions the student has.
* **Street Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's street address.
* **City** (`student_city`, text, optional): Enter the student's city.
* **State** (`student_state`, text, optional): Enter the student's state.
* **Zip** (`student_zip`, text, optional): Enter the student's zip code.
* **Program Cost** (`program_cost`, number, optional): Enter the program cost.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment status.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the program status.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student's attendance status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, text, optional): Provide any payment method details.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Check for errors before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the school sports clinic staff for assistance.
