# Class Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Enrollment Form is a tool to help you enroll in a class. Fill out the form accurately to ensure your enrollment is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**: Each field marked as "required" must be completed in order to submit the form.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Ensure your name, contact information, and class details are accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all required fields, click the "Submit" button to finalize your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender from the options provided. If you prefer not to specify your gender, select "Non-binary".
- **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, optional): If you are transferring or continuing, enter your current student ID number.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you live.
- **State or Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where you live.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code (zip code).
- **Class Name** (`class_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the class you want to enroll in.
- **Class Level or Grade** (`class_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level or grade you are enrolling in (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Other).
- **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of the course (Full semester, Half semester, 4 weeks, 8 weeks, 12 weeks, or Other).
- **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you prefer to start the class.
- **Schedule Preference** (`schedule_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred schedule (Morning sessions, Afternoon sessions, Evening sessions, Flexible, Online, or Hybrid).
- **Enrollment Type** (`enrollment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your enrollment type (New student, Returning student, Transfer student, Audit).
- **Learning Goals for this Class** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required): Enter your learning goals for this class.
- **Previous Experience with this Subject** (`previous_experience`, `text`, optional): Enter any previous experience you have with the subject.
- **Any Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, optional): Enter any special accommodations you may need.
- **I agree to the enrollment terms and conditions** (`consent_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes, I agree, to confirm you have read and agree to the enrollment terms and conditions.
