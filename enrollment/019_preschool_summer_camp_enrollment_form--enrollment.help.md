# Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Summer Camp Enrollment Form is used to enroll a child in the preschool summer camp. This form collects information such as name, contact details, and enrollment dates to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your child's First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter a valid email address.
3. Select the enrollment dates for your child.
4. Choose a program for your child (Morning, Afternoon, or Full-Day).
5. Add any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.
6. Select the Category (Enrollment or Other).
7. Review the form and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your child's phone number (optional).
* **Select Enrolment Dates**: Choose the dates your child will be enrolled in the summer camp. 
* **Register**: Review and submit the form. (Note: This field is not editable.)
* **Select Program**: Choose a program for your child (Morning, Afternoon, or Full-Day).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your child.
* **Category**: Select the category for your child's enrollment (Enrollment or Other).
