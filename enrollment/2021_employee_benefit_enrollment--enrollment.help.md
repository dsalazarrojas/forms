# 2D Animation Course Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For the optional fields, you can choose to provide feedback or leave them blank.
3. Use the provided help text for each field for guidance on what to expect.
4. Fill out the form carefully, and double-check your answers before submission.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: Course & Instructor Feedback

* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name, or leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Student email (optional)** (`student_email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to receive follow-up feedback or communication from us.
* **Course completion date** (`course_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you completed the course.
* **Instructor effectiveness** (`instructor_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the instructor's effectiveness on a scale of 1-10, with 10 being exceptional teaching.
* **Relevance of course content** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the relevance of the course content using the options: Highly relevant, Mostly relevant, Somewhat relevant, or Not relevant.
* **Technical skills gained** (`technical_skill_gain`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the technical skills you gained during the course, using the options: Traditional frame-by-frame, Tweening & interpolation, Rigging characters, Timing and pacing, Compositing & effects, or None of the above.
* **Software/tools used in course** (`software_used`, `text`, optional): List the software and tools used during the course, e.g., "Toon Boom, TVPaint, After Effects".
* **Quality of project assignments** (`project_quality_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the quality of project assignments on a scale of 1-10, with 10 being industry-ready quality.
* **Pace and workload suitability** (`pace_and_workload`, `select_one`, required): Rate the pace and workload of the course, using the options: Too fast, Well-paced, Too slow, or Inconsistent.
* **Would you recommend this course?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Select your willingness to recommend the course using the options: Definitely, Probably, Not sure, or False.
* **Most useful topic or lesson** (`most_useful_topic`, `text`, optional): Describe the most useful topic or lesson you learned during the course.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`improvements_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide specific suggestions for improving the course.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other comments or feedback you have about the course or instructor.
