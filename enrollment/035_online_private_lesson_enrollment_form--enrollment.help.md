# online_private_lesson_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for online private lesson enrollment. The form is used by both instructors and students to input relevant details about the lesson, instructor, and student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely**. Ensure that you've entered all required and optional fields.
2. **Verify the accuracy of your inputs**. Double-check your information for errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Input**: Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself as the instructor.
* **Student Info**: Enter the student's name, ensuring it matches their identity verification.
* **Lesson Date Time**: Choose a date and time for the lesson, considering your availability and the student's schedule.
* **Instructor Message**: Leave a note to the student with any important details about the lesson, such as materials needed or expectations.
* **Student Message**: Leave a note to the student with any important details about themselves or the lesson.
* **Submit and Save**: Click to finalize the lesson enrollment. A confirmation message will be displayed once submitted.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's contact phone number for future communication.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address for correspondence.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address for in-person lessons or deliveries.
* **Lesson Topic**: Enter the topic of the lesson to be conducted.
* **Lesson Length**: Enter the expected length of the lesson in minutes.

## Tips
- Always keep the lesson topic and length clear for both parties.
- Use the student's contact information for future correspondence and lesson scheduling.
- Double-check the accuracy of the student's information for verification purposes.
