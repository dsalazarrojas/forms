# zoo_crew_4_h_enrollment_form_2021_2022 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and usage of the zoo_crew_4_h_enrollment_form_2021_2022 form, which is used for enrolling participants in various zoo crews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the crew you wish to enroll in from the "Which Zoo Crew" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out the remaining fields with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Form** (`enrollment_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, but is optional and can be left blank.
* **Participant Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant enrolling in the zoo crew.
* **Participant Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant or a contact person in case of emergencies.
* **Which Zoo Crew** (`zoo_crew`, select_one, optional): Select the zoo crew you wish to enroll in from the dropdown menu. Options include: Crew 1, Crew 2, Crew 3, or Crew 4.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to start participating in the zoo crew.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to end participating in the zoo crew.
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you plan to work per week.
* **Hours Per Week Holiday** (`hours_per_week_holiday`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you plan to work per week on holidays.
* **Weeks Per Year** (`weeks_per_year`, number, optional): Enter the number of weeks you plan to work per year.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your workday.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your workday.
* **Days Off Per Week** (`days_off_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of days off you plan to take per week.
* **Hours Per Week Day** (`hours_per_week_day`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you plan to work per day.
* **Weeks Per Year** (`weeks_per_year`, number, optional): Enter the number of weeks you plan to work per year.
* **Zoo License** (`zoo_license`, text, optional): Enter any relevant zoo licenses or certifications.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship to the participant (e.g. "spouse", "parent", etc.).
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm participation.
* **Zoo Signature** (`zoo_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm the participant's participation on behalf of the zoo.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm the participant's participation on behalf of the supervisor.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm the participant's participation on behalf of the reviewer.
* **Zoo Crew 4 H Enrollment Form Comments** (`zoo_crew_4_h_enrollment_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the participant's enrollment.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the reviewer.
