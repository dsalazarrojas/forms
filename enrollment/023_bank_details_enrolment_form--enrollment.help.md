# Bank Details Enrolment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Details Enrolment Form is a required step for customers who wish to open new accounts with our bank. This form helps us verify your identity and collect essential information for account setup, ensuring a smooth and secure onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out all the fields to provide your accurate and complete information.
2.  Ensure your answers match your actual identification documents.
3.  If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Legal Name (`applicant_name`)
*   **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`)
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Social Security Number or Tax ID (`ssn_ein`)
*   **Social Security Number or Tax ID**: Enter your Social Security Number or Tax ID as it appears on your identification documents.

### Residential Street Address (`residential_address`)
*   **Residential Street Address**: Enter your current residential address where you receive mail.

### Mobile Phone Number (`mobile_phone`)
*   **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number for multi-factor authentication.

### Email Address (`email_address`)
*   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for account updates and notifications.

### Desired Account Types (`accounts_to_open`)
*   **Desired Account Types**: Select the account types you wish to open from the list.

### Estimated Initial Deposit (`initial_deposit_amount`)
*   **Estimated Initial Deposit**: Enter the total amount to fund your new accounts.

### Source of Initial Funding (`funding_source`)
*   **Source of Initial Funding**: Select the method you will use to fund your accounts.

### Preferred Online Banking Username (`username_request`)
*   **Preferred Online Banking Username**: Choose a unique username for online banking access.

### Security Question 1 (`security_question_1`)
*   **Security Question - Mother's Maiden Name**: Answer this question with your mother's maiden name.

### Security Question 2 (`security_question_2`)
*   **Security Question - First School Attended**: Answer this question with your first school attended.

### Backup Withholding Certification (`backup_withholding_cert`)
*   **Backup Withholding Certification**: Check the box to confirm you are not subject to backup withholding.

### E-Sign Consent and Disclosure (`e_sign_consent`)
*   **E-Sign Consent and Disclosure**: Confirm you agree to receive disclosures electronically.

### Enrollment Date (`submission_date`)
*   **Enrollment Date**: Date the enrollment form is submitted.

## Tips
*   Please ensure you have accurate identification documents ready before filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
