# New Stylist Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Stylist Check In form is designed to help stylists update their status and communicate with clients and other team members in the salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the New Stylist Check In form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **User Name** to identify yourself as the stylist.
2. Enter your **Stylist Name** to confirm your name as a member of the styling team.
3. Select **New Client?** to indicate whether you are checking in for a new client or an existing one.
4. If you are checking in for a new client, provide a brief description of the new client in the **New Client Comments** field.
5. Choose the contact method you used to communicate with the client, such as **Phone**, **Email**, or **In Person**.
6. If desired, rate your **Stylist Rating** of the client's experience.
7. Share any additional comments or feedback about the client's visit in the **Stylist Comments** field.
8. Provide a summary of the **Next Steps** to ensure seamless communication with the client and your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: Enter your username to access your profile and view your schedule.
- **Stylist Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your stylist profile.
- **New Client?**: Select **True** if the client is new, or **False** if it's an existing client.
  *This field is used to differentiate between new and existing clients.*
- **New Client Comments**: Briefly describe the new client, including any relevant details or notes.
- **Contact Method**: Choose how you communicated with the client, such as phone, email, or in-person.
  *For example, if you met the client at an event, select "In Person".*
- **Stylist Rating**: Rate your experience with the client on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
- **Stylist Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about the client's visit.
- **Next Steps**: Summarize the actions you plan to take next, such as following up with the client or scheduling a future appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately identify the client as new or existing.
* Be concise in your communication with clients to ensure seamless and efficient communication.
* Use the **Stylist Rating** and **Stylist Comments** fields to provide detailed feedback for future reference.
* Keep the **New Client Comments** field brief and focused on the essential details.
