# Win Free Days Of Enrollment Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for the "Win Free Days Of Enrollment Contest Entry". The contest aims to provide free days of enrollment to participating students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section with your personal details.
2. Fill out the school information section with your school details.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your school name?
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of your school.
* This field is required to determine your school affiliation.
### What type of school do you attend?
* **School Type** (`school_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of school you attend.
* This field is required to determine your eligibility.
* Options: High School, Middle School, Other
### What is your age range?
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your age range.
* This field is required to determine your eligibility.
* Options: 10-13, 14-17, 18-24, 25 or older
### What is your gender?
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Please select your gender.
* This field is required to determine your eligibility.
* Options: Male, Female, Other
### What is your phone number?
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* This field is required for contact purposes.
### What is your email address?
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* This field is required for contact purposes.
### What is your address?
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* This field is required to verify your location.
### Do you want to provide additional information?
