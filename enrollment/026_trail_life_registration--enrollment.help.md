# trail_life_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trail_life_registration form is designed to collect information about a family's enrollment status and demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering your title or name in the "Interested Family" field.
2. Provide details about the youth in your family, including any relevant information in the "Youth Details" field.
3. Enter the size of your family in the "Family Size" field.
4. If applicable, enter your family's income in the "Family Income" field.
5. Indicate the employment status of your family members in the "Family Employment Status" field.
6. Select the employment type of your family members from the options provided in the "Family Employment Type" field.
7. Specify the education level of your family members in the "Family Employment Education" field.
8. If necessary, register your family for the Trail Life program by selecting the registration date and time in the "Trail Life Registration Date" and "Trail Life Registration Time" fields.
9. Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for your family's enrollment in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Interested Family** (`interested_family`, text, required): Enter the title or name of your family.
* **Youth Details** (`youth_details`, text, required): Provide any relevant information about the youth in your family.
* **Family Size** (`family_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your family.
* **Family Income** (`family_income`, number, optional): Enter your family's annual income.
* **Family Employment Status** (`family_employment_status`, text, optional): Indicate the employment status of your family members (e.g., working, not working).
* **Family Employment Type** (`family_employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the employment type of your family members (e.g., Part-time, Full-time, Self-employment).
* **Family Employment Education** (`family_employment_education`, select_one, optional): Specify the education level of your family members (e.g., Less than High School, High School Diploma, Some College, College Degree, Postgraduate Degree).
* **Trail Life Registration Date** (`trail_life_registration_date`, date, optional): If necessary, select the date for your family's registration.
* **Trail Life Registration Time** (`trail_life_registration_time`, time, optional): If necessary, select the time for your family's registration.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for your family's enrollment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your family's information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid errors or duplication of effort.
* If you need assistance or have questions about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant contact person.
