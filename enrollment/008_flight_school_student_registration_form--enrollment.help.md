# Flight School Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who are enrolling in the Flight School program. It collects personal details, course information, and other relevant data for administrative and logistical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the email field.
3. Provide your Phone Number in the text field.
4. Select the Course ID, Course Name, and Assigned Tool (if applicable) for the course you are enrolling in. Course ID is optional.
5. Choose a start date and end date for your course enrollment.
6. Select your city, state, zip, and country of residence.
7. Fill in your Nationality (if applicable).
8. Select your Birth Date (if applicable).
9. Provide your Passport Number (if applicable).
10. Select your Passport Date (if applicable).
11. Fill in your License Number (if applicable).
12. Select your License Type (if applicable).
13. Enter your License Expiration (if applicable).
14. Provide your License Authority (if applicable).
15. Add any additional Notes you'd like to include (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this text field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this text field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Course ID**: Select the course ID for the course you are enrolling in. This is optional.
* **Course Name**: Select the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the course.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you plan to start your course.
* **End Date**: Select the date you plan to end your course.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality if applicable.
* **Birth Date**: Select your birth date if applicable.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number if applicable.
* **Passport Date**: Select the date of your passport if applicable.
* **License Number**: Enter your license number if applicable.
* **License Type**: Select your license type if applicable.
* **License Expiration**: Enter your license expiration date if applicable.
* **License Authority**: Enter your license authority if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes you'd like to include.
