# Account Enrollment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Account Enrollment Request Form is used to collect necessary information from applicants for account creation. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked with \*).
2. Ensure that all required fields have accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Choose your preferred account type from the options provided.
4. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the account.
5. Review and acknowledge the privacy policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, \*): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Applicant Date of Birth** (`applicant_date_of_birth`, date, \*): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, email, \*): Enter your valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, \*): Enter your active phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Applicant Address** (`applicant_address`, text, \*): Enter your current address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Applicant City** (`applicant_city`, text, \*): Enter the city of your residence.
* **Applicant State** (`applicant_state`, text, \*): Enter the state of your residence.
* **Applicant Zip Code** (`applicant_zip_code`, text, \*): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, \*): Enter your Social Security Number (SSN) for identity verification.
* **Account Type Requested** (`account_type_requested`, select_one, \*): Choose from:
	+ Individual Account
	+ Joint Account
	+ Business Account
	+ Trust Account
	+ Other
* **Enrollment Purpose** (`enrollment_purpose`, text, \*): Briefly explain why you are enrolling for this account.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If you have an employer, enter their name.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, optional): Enter your approximate annual income.
* **Source of Income** (`source_of_income`, select_one, optional): Choose from:
	+ Employment
	+ Self-Employment
	+ Investment
	+ Retirement
	+ Other
* **Identification Type** (`identification_type`, select_one, \*): Choose from:
	+ Driver's License
	+ State ID
	+ Passport
	+ Military ID
	+ Other
* **Identification Number** (`identification_number`, text, \*): Enter the number on your identification document.
* **Identification Expiry Date** (`identification_expiry_date`, date, \*): Enter the expiration date of your identification document.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (`terms_and_conditions_agreement`, select_one, \*): Choose from:
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ No, I do not agree
* **Privacy Policy Acknowledgment** (`privacy_policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, \*): Choose from:
	+ Yes, I acknowledge
	+ No, I have not read
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, \*): Type your full name to sign this form electronically.
