# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Enrollment Form is a mandatory form used to gather essential information for your child's enrollment in our summer camp program. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure a smooth and safe experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your child's name and your relationship with them.
2. Share your child's medical history and any relevant medical information.
3. Enter your emergency contact information, including any relevant phone numbers and addresses.
4. Choose a payment method and provide the payment details.
5. Enter the payment date and amount.
6. Confirm the payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter your child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship you have with the child, such as "Mother", "Father", or "Guardian".
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe your child's medical history, including any allergies, conditions, or previous medical procedures.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you plan to make payment for the summer camp, such as "Cash", "Check", or "Bank Transfer".
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant payment information, such as a check number or bank account details.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to make the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you plan to pay for the summer camp.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the payment status as "Paid" or "Unpaid".
* **Payment Note** (`payment_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are essential to complete the form accurately. Please ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
