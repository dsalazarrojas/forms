# ChallengeGroupAssesment 1/16/2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about group assessments, providing a framework to evaluate their dynamics, participation, engagement, and potential areas for improvement. The goal is to gather data that can be used for further development or follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the assessment date from the calendar.
2. Enter the name of the group being assessed.
3. Enter the name of the facilitator.
4. Choose the type of program the group is participating in.
5. Enter the total number of participants in the group.
6. Enter the number of participants present at the assessment.
7. Select the length of the assessment session.
8. Rate the group's dynamics, participation level, and engagement quality using the provided options.
9. List the strengths observed during the assessment (optional).
10. Identify areas for improvement (optional).
11. Provide recommendations for future development (optional).
12. Indicate if a follow-up assessment is needed and, if so, schedule the follow-up date.
13. Sign off with the facilitator's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the group assessment.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the group being assessed.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assessment facilitator.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of program the group is participating in (e.g., Youth development, Community outreach, etc.).
* **Total Participants** (`total_participants`, number, required): Enter the total number of participants in the group.
* **Participants Present** (`participants_present`, number, required): Enter the number of participants present at the assessment.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of the assessment session (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Group Dynamics Rating** (`group_dynamics`, select_one, required): Rate the group's dynamics (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`, select_one, required): Rate the overall participation level (Very high, High, Moderate, Low, Very low).
* **Engagement Quality** (`engagement_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of engagement (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Group Dynamics Rating Explanation** (`assessment_area_1`, text, required): Note the strengths observed during the assessment.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas that need improvement.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for future development.
* **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if a follow-up assessment is needed and, if so, schedule the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the follow-up assessment (if needed).
* **Facilitator Signature** (`facilitator_signature`, text, required): Sign off with the facilitator's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Take the time to provide thoughtful and constructive feedback in the optional fields.
