# Educational Benefit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Benefit Form is a tool for gathering information about educational benefits received by students. This form will help track and document these benefits, ensuring accurate and consistent recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the institution that is providing the educational benefit.
2. Enter the name of the student who is receiving the benefit.
3. Specify the academic program or course that the benefit is related to.
4. Choose one or more benefit types from the list of options provided.
5. Enter the amount of the benefit received.
6. Select the period during which the benefit was received.
7. Enter the start date of the benefit period.
8. Enter the end date of the benefit period.
9. Provide any additional notes about the benefit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Institution**: Enter the name of the institution that is providing the educational benefit.
* **Student**: Enter the name of the student who is receiving the benefit.
* **Academic Program**: Enter the academic program or course that the benefit is related to.
* **Benefit Type**: Select one or more of the following benefit types:
	+ Financial Aid
	+ Grant
	+ Scholarship
	+ Other
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the benefit received.
* **Period**: Select the period during which the benefit was received:
	+ Academic Year
	+ Semester
	+ Other
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the benefit period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the benefit period.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the benefit.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field completely and accurately to ensure consistent and accurate tracking of educational benefits.
* If a benefit type is not listed in the options, select "Other" and provide a description in the "Notes" field.
* If the benefit period is not academic year or semester, select "Other" and enter the specific period in the "Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant support staff for assistance.
