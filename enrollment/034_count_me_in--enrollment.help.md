# count_me_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "count_me_in" form is used to collect user information for enrollment purposes. This form is typically used for registration, membership, or other programs where user data is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by * (required).
2. Enter your User Name, email, and if applicable, phone number in the respective fields.
3. If you are enrolling for a specific program, select the correct enrollment status (New comer or Old comer).
4. Enter your date of birth and time of birth, if applicable.
5. Select the assigned tool(s) for your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Name (`user_name`, text, false)
Enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like it to be displayed for the purpose of this program.
### 2. email (email, required, true)
Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
### 3. phone (phone, text, false)
If you have a phone number associated with your enrollment, enter it here. This will help with communication, but is not required.
### 4. date_of_birth (date, false)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 5. time_of_birth (time, false)
Enter your time of birth in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00. If you are unsure, leave this field blank.
### 6. enrollment_status (select_one, false)
Select the correct enrollment status: New comer or Old comer.
### 7. church_name (text, false)
Enter the name of your church or organization associated with this enrollment.
### 8. assigned_tool (select_multiple, false)
Select the tool(s) assigned to you for this enrollment. Choose from chatjimmy or jimmy.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you enter accurate information to avoid delays or errors in your enrollment process.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator or the relevant department for clarification.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct tool(s) for your enrollment.
