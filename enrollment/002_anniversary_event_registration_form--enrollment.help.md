# Anniversary Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anniversary Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees to facilitate event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your email address for event communication.
3. Enter your phone number as an optional emergency contact number.
4. Indicate the number of guests attending, including yourself.
5. Choose your attendance type: in-person or virtual.
6. List any dietary restrictions or preferences.
7. Indicate if accommodation is needed and provide brief details if so.
8. Enter any special requests or comments for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name of attendee**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for event communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number as an optional emergency contact number.
* **Number of guests attending including yourself**: Enter the total number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Attendance type**: Choose between in-person or virtual attendance.
* **Dietary restrictions or preferences**: List any allergies or dietary needs you may have.
* **Accommodation needed**: Indicate if you require accommodation assistance.
* **Accommodation details**: Provide brief details if you need accommodation assistance.
* **Special requests or comments**: Enter any additional requests or comments for the event organizers.

## Tips
* Please ensure you submit only one registration form per attendee.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers.
