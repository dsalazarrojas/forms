# CHW RHSP Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CHW RHSP Referral Form is used by community health workers (CHWs) to refer clients to relevant services. This form is essential for ensuring that clients receive the necessary assistance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the relevant information for the client.
2. Enter the full name of the community health worker referring the client.
3. Enter the client's contact phone number.
4. Enter the client's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the reason for referral, providing a brief summary of the client's needs or services requested.
6. Select the urgency level of the referral (Low, Medium, or High).
7. Choose the preferred contact method for the client (Phone, Email, or Text message).
8. Add any additional notes about the client's situation or background.
9. Set the referral date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer name**: The full name of the community health worker referring the client. This information is required and helps the receiving organization track who referred the client.
* **Referrer phone number**: The contact phone number of the community health worker referring the client. This helps the receiving organization contact the CHW for follow-up or clarification if needed.
* **Client name**: The full legal name of the client as it appears on their ID.
* **Client date of birth**: The client's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD. This helps the receiving organization assess the client's needs and provide relevant services.
* **Client contact**: The best phone number to reach the client. This helps the receiving organization contact the client for follow-up or services.
* **Reason for referral**: A brief summary of the client's needs or services requested. This provides essential information for the receiving organization to understand the client's situation.
* **Urgency level**: The level of urgency required for the service, indicated by Low, Medium, or High.
* **Preferred contact method**: The method by which the receiving organization should contact the client, such as phone, email, or text message.
* **Additional notes**: Any other relevant background or barriers that the client may have. This allows the receiving organization to provide more personalized support.
* **Referral date**: The date the referral was submitted. This helps track the referral's status and timeline.
