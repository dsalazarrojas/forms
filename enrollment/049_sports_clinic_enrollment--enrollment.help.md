# sports_clinic_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports clinic enrollment form is used to collect information from students or potential students for sports clinic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the desired course from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the student's name in the "student name" field.
3. Enter the student's contact email in the "contact email" field.
4. Enter the student's contact phone number in the "contact phone" field.
5. Select the student's birthdate.
6. Enter the student's address.
7. Enter the student's city.
8. Enter the student's state.
9. Enter the student's zip code.
10. Leave any additional comments about the student in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course (`Course`, select_one, not required):** Choose the sport or activity you'd like to participate in at the sports clinic.
* **Student Name (`student name`, text, not required):** Enter the student's name.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, not required):** Enter the student's contact email.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, not required):** Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Birthdate (`birthdate`, date, not required):** Enter the student's birthdate.
* **Address (`address`, text, not required):** Enter the student's address.
* **City (`city`, text, not required):** Enter the student's city.
* **State (`state`, text, not required):** Enter the student's state or province.
* **Zip Code (`zip`, text, not required):** Enter the student's zip code.
* **Notes (`student_notes`, note, not required):** Leave any additional comments about the student, such as medical conditions or special requirements.
