# Chamber Of Commerce Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chamber of Commerce Enrollment Form is designed to collect necessary information from businesses to enroll them in the Chamber's membership program. This form helps the Chamber understand the business's type, address, contact information, and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select the enrollment date**: Choose the date when the business was enrolled in the Chamber.
2. **Enter business information**: Provide the legal name and type of business, as well as its primary industry category.
3. **Enter address and contact details**: Enter the business's full address, phone number, email, and website (if applicable).
4. **Enter employee and revenue details**: Provide the number of employees and approximate annual revenue.
5. **Enter contact and membership details**: Choose the primary contact person, their title, and desired membership level.
6. **Specify membership source**: Indicate how you heard about the Chamber.
7. **Choose membership preferences**: Select the desired membership level and indicate interest in networking events and committee participation.
8. **Specify payment method**: Choose the method for paying membership dues.
9. **Agree to terms**: Confirm agreement with the Chamber's terms and conditions.
10. **Sign and date**: Sign and date the form to confirm enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of business enrollment in the Chamber.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Provide the legal name of the business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of business entity (e.g., Sole proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary industry category for the business (e.g., Retail, Restaurant, Professional services, etc.).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full business address.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, optional): If different from business address, enter the mailing address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the primary business phone number.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, optional): Provide the URL of the business website (if applicable).
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, required): Enter the primary business email address.
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, `number`, required): Enter the year the business was founded.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, required): Choose the approximate number of employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate annual revenue range.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the desired membership level (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold, etc.).
* **Source of Referral** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how you heard about the Chamber (e.g., Current member referral, Chamber website, Social media, etc.).
* **Referred By** (`referred_by`, `text`, optional): If referred by a current member, enter their name.
* **Networking Interest** (`networking_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of interest in networking events (e.g., Very interested, Somewhat interested, Not interested).
* **Committee Interest** (`committee_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate interest in joining a committee (e.g., Yes, No, Maybe).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method for paying membership dues (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, Check, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm agreement with the Chamber's terms and conditions.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the authorized signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signing.
