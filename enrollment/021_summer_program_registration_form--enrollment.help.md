# summer_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer program registration form is designed to collect information from students who want to participate in our summer programs. This form is intended for students who are interested in enrolling in a program that meets their needs and interests. Please ensure that you are filling out this form only if you have a genuine interest in participating in a summer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student information section, which includes your student ID number and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the program that suits your interests by selecting multiple programs from the dropdown menu. If you're unsure which program is right for you, don't worry! You can select multiple options.
3. Enter your start and end dates for the program. This will help us plan our program dates accordingly.
4. Fill out the student details section, which is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is important for us to know.
5. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you with any important updates or notifications.
6. Lastly, provide any necessary parent information if you are a minor and have a parent or guardian who needs to be notified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (Student ID Number, Name, etc.)** (`student_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you can input your student ID number, name, and any other relevant details about yourself. Please ensure that your student information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Program Choice** (`program_choice`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section is where you can select the program that interests you. You can choose multiple programs if you're unsure which one is right for you. Our programs are designed to cater to different interests and needs.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter the date you would like your program to start. This will help us plan our program dates accordingly.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like your program to end. This will help us plan our program dates as well.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about yourself that you think is important for us to know. This might include any special needs or requirements you have.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact information so we can reach out to you with any important updates or notifications.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a minor and have a parent or guardian who needs to be notified, please provide their contact information here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected multiple programs if you are unsure which one is right for you.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be sure to provide any necessary parent information if you are a minor.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
