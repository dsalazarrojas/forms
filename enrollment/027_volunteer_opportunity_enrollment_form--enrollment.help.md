# Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help us manage volunteer opportunities and ensure accurate and efficient enrollment. It requires basic information from you, such as your name, email, and phone number, as well as specific details about the volunteer opportunity you are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Enter your email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you about the volunteer opportunity.
3. **Enter your phone number**: Provide your phone number in case we need to reach you.
4. **Enter your date of birth**: This is required to ensure you are eligible for the volunteer opportunity.
5. **Select the enrollment start date**: Choose the date you want to start volunteering.
6. **Select the enrollment end date**: Choose the date you want to end volunteering.
7. **Select the enrollment frequency**: Choose how often you want to volunteer (e.g., every day, every other day, etc.).
8. **Select the volunteer opportunity enrollment status**: Choose whether the opportunity is active or inactive (e.g., if it's paused or closed).
9. **Select the volunteer opportunity enrollment type**: Choose whether the opportunity is virtual or on-site (in-person).
10. **Enter any comments or feedback**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the volunteer opportunity.
11. **Agree to the terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer opportunity.
12. **Enter your name as a volunteer**: Provide your name in case you are a volunteer.
13. **Enter your email as a volunteer**: If you are a volunteer, provide your email address.
14. **Enter your phone number as a volunteer**: If you are a volunteer, provide your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment** (`1`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the volunteer opportunity you want to enroll in.
* **Name** (`2`, text, required/optional): Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`3`, email, required/optional): Your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`4`, text, required/optional): Your phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, date, required/optional): This is required to ensure you are eligible for the volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Start Date** (`6`, date, required/optional): The date you want to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment End Date** (`7`, date, required/optional): The date you want to end volunteering.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Time** (`8`, time, required/optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Frequency** (`9`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you want to volunteer (e.g., every day, every other day, etc.).
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Status** (`10`, select_one, required/optional): This is likely for internal use and may be used to indicate if the opportunity is active or inactive.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Type** (`11`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you select whether the opportunity is virtual or on-site.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Comments** (`12`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Agreed To Terms** (`13`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Volunteer Name** (`14`, text, required/optional): If you are a volunteer, this is where you provide your name.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Volunteer Email** (`15`, text, required/optional): If you are a volunteer, provide your email address.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrollment Volunteer Phone** (`16`, text, required/optional): If you are a volunteer, provide your phone number.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or duplication.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact our support team.
* Please review the terms and conditions before agreeing to the volunteer opportunity.
* If you are a volunteer, make sure to provide your contact information so we can reach you.
* If you are unsure about any information or have any concerns, please reach out to us.
