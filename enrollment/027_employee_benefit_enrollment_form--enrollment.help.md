# Employee Benefit Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefit Enrollment Form is used to collect employee information and benefit enrollment preferences for HR and Payroll purposes. This form is designed to gather essential data about employees' demographics, benefits, and job information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Provide your contact information (Phone and Email).
3. Choose your Health Insurance Plan, Life Insurance Plan, Medical Group, and Dependents enrollment status.
4. Select your Job Title, Department, Job Classification, Job Family Status, Job Type, Job Level, and Supervisor Department.
5. Enter your Supervisor's Name, Phone, and Email (if applicable).
6. Add any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your last name. This is a required field.
* **Date of Birth** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, date, required): Please provide your date of birth. This is a required field.
* **Phone** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your phone number. This is a required field.
* **Email** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, email, required): Please provide your email address. This is a required field.
* **Health Insurance Plan** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_multiple, required): Please select your Health Insurance Plan. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Life Insurance Plan** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your Life Insurance Plan. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Medical Group** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_multiple, required): Please select your Medical Group. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Dependents** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_multiple, required): Please select your Dependents enrollment status. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Job Title** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your job title. This is a required field.
* **Department** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your department. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Job Classification** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your job classification. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Job Family Status** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your job family status. You can choose "Active" or "Inactive" from the options.
* **Job Start Date** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, date, required): Please provide your job start date. This is a required field.
* **Job End Date** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, date, required): Please provide your job end date. This is a required field.
* **Job Type** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your job type. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Job Level** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your job level. You can choose "High" or "Low" from the options.
* **Annual Salary** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, number, required): Please provide your annual salary. This is a required field.
* **Job Title Description** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide a description of your job title. This is a required field.
* **Job Duties** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please list your job duties. This is a required field.
* **Supervisor Name** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your supervisor's name. This is a required field.
* **Supervisor Phone** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, text, required): Please provide your supervisor's phone number. This is a required field.
* **Supervisor Email** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, email, required): Please provide your supervisor's email address. This is a required field.
* **Supervisor Department** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, select_one, required): Please select your supervisor's department. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Note** (Employee Benefit Enrollment Form, note, required): Please add any additional comments or notes. This is a required field.
