# the_tropical_art_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The the_tropical_art_registration_form is designed to collect user information for the purpose of enrollment in a tropical art program or class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the "User Information" section.
2. Fill out the required fields: Name and Email.
3. Fill out the optional Phone field if you feel it is necessary.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, `false`): This is the introduction section of the form. Please fill out this section to provide a brief description of your interest in the tropical art program or class.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, `false`): Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or your name as you would like to be known in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `false`): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding the program or class.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): If you feel it is necessary, please enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you regarding the program or class.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid any issues with your enrollment.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is not intended for commercial use. Please do not use it for any purpose other than enrolling for a tropical art program or class.
