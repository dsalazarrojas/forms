# Drug Test Employer Account Set Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employers set up their account for drug testing purposes. It collects essential information about the employer, their employee, and account details. This form is a crucial step in the drug testing process, allowing employers to provide accurate and reliable drug test results.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide the requested information in each field.
2.  Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employer Information** (`employer_info`, `text`, required: false):
    This field is for the employer to provide their company name, address, and contact details. Please enter your company's name and address to help us verify your identity and provide you with accurate account information.
*   **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, `text`, required: false):
    In this field, please enter your employee's name, date of birth, and ID number. This information is necessary for us to accurately process the drug test.
*   **Account Details** (`account_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Here, you can provide your account type (e.g., account type) and other account-specific details. Please ensure that your account type is accurate to avoid any issues with your account.
*   **Test Scheduling** (`test_scheduling`, `text`, required: false):
    In this field, you can enter any specific test scheduling requirements or instructions. Please provide any relevant information about the test scheduling.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Here, you can enter your payment method and other payment-related information. Please ensure that your payment method is accurate and secure.
*   **Test Results** (`test_results`, `text`, required: false):
    This field is for any additional test results or feedback you may want to provide. Please use this space to share any important information about the test results.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
*   If you are unsure about any field or information, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
