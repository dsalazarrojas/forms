# valentines_day_cookie_class_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to enroll participants in an internal Valentine's Day cookie class. It is used to gather essential information about the participant for identification and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, enter the participant's **First Name**, which should be a brief identifier for the participant.
2. Enter the participant's **Email**, which will be used for internal communication and updates.
3. Enter the participant's **Participant Phone**, which will be used for any necessary phone communication.
4. Enter the participant's **Participant Email**, which should match the email provided earlier.
5. Enter the participant's **Participant Address**, which will help with any potential deliveries or mailings.
6. Enter the participant's **Participant City**, which will aid in identifying the participant's location.
7. Enter the participant's **Participant State**, which will help with any necessary regional communication.
8. Enter the participant's **Participant Zip**, which will aid in identifying the participant's postal location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A brief identifier for the participant, typically their personal name.
* **Email**: The participant's contact email address for internal updates and communication.
* **Participant Phone**: The participant's phone number for potential phone contact or communication.
* **Participant Email**: Ensure this matches the participant's email address provided earlier to avoid confusion.
* **Participant Address**: The participant's full address for any necessary deliveries or mailings.
* **Participant City**: The city where the participant is located, helping with regional communication.
* **Participant State**: The state where the participant is located, aiding in understanding their regional context.
* **Participant Zip**: The participant's zip code, helping to identify their postal location.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the participant's information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the authorized personnel for clarification.
* Only authorized personnel should be filling out this form.
