# Gymnastics Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and parents/guardians for enrolling students in a gymnastics program.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please fill in the required fields with the corresponding information. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank.
- Make sure to enter the correct class time for the student's scheduled gymnastics classes.
- If you are a parent/guardian, please provide your contact information as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day for the student's scheduled gymnastics classes.
*   - 8am
*   - 9am
*   - 10am
*   - 11am
*   - 12pm
*   - 1pm
*   - 2pm
*   - 3pm
*   - 4pm
*   - 5pm
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Enter any relevant gymnastics experience the student may have.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, note, optional): Enter a brief note about any emergency contact information.
*   - Please enter any relevant emergency contact information here.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not the parent/guardian has given permission for the student to enroll.
*   - 'True'
*   - 'False'
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
* **Class Level** (`class_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the class level for the student.
*   - Beginner
*   - Intermediate
*   - Advanced

# Tips
- Make sure to double-check all the information entered before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the gymnastics organization directly.
- All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form.
