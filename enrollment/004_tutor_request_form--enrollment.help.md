# Tutor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Request Form is designed to help students seek assistance from tutors for specific subjects. By filling out this form, you can request help with a particular subject and receive guidance from experienced tutors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your subject of need in the "What subject do you need help with" field.
	* This is where you specify the subject you require help with.
2. Provide a detailed description of the problem or topic you are struggling with in the "Description of help needed" field.
	* This will help tutors understand the nature of the problem and provide more focused assistance.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
	* Your name will be used to contact you, if the need arises.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* This will ensure that you receive updates about your tutor request.
5. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
	* This will help tutors contact you to discuss further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What subject do you need help with** (`subject`, required): Enter the subject you need help with, such as math or science.
* **Description of help needed** (`description`, optional): Describe the problem or topic you are struggling with. This will help tutors understand your needs and provide more focused assistance.
* **Name** (`name`, required): Enter your name to allow us to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on your tutor request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number to help tutors contact you for further discussion.
