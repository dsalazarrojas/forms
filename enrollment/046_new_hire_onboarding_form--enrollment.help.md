# New Hire Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Form is used to capture personal and professional information from new employees. It helps the company to get to know the new hire better and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the company.
4. **Email**: Enter your work email address.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. **Department**: Select the department you will be working in.
7. **Job Type**: Select the type of employment agreement you have with the company (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
8. **Hire Date**: Enter the date you were hired.
9. **Start Date**: Enter the date you will begin working.
10. **End Date**: Enter the date you will end your employment (if applicable).
11. **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
12. **Manager Phone Number**: Enter the direct manager's phone number.
13. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
14. **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
15. **Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Spouse, Partner, Friend, or Family).
16. **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
17. **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
18. **Emergency Contact State**: Select the state of your emergency contact's address (Yes or No).
19. **Emergency Contact Zip**: Enter the zip code of your emergency contact's address.
20. **Emergency Contact Country**: Select the country of your emergency contact's address (Yes or No).
21. **Emergency Contact Region**: Select the region of your emergency contact's address (Yes or No).
22. **Emergency Contact Note**: Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
23. **Manager Address**: Enter the address of your direct manager.
24. **Manager Phone Number**: Enter the direct manager's phone number.
25. **Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your manager (Spouse, Partner, Friend, or Family).
