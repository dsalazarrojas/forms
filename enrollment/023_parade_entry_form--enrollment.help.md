# Parade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parade Entry Form is designed to gather essential information from parade participants. This form will be used to organize and manage parade entries, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for both participants and event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Entry Type, Theme, and Entry Fee).
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Select the type of entry (Parade Entry or Entry).
4. Choose a theme (Classic, Modern, or Retro).
5. If you have a custom template, select it and provide the corresponding URL and image.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it should appear on the participant list.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it should appear on the participant list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of entry you are participating with (Parade Entry or Entry).
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, number, optional): Enter the fee associated with your entry (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding your entry.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the person in charge.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for the person in charge.
* **Theme** (`theme`, select_one, required): Choose a theme that best describes your entry (Classic, Modern, or Retro).
* **Custom Template** (`custom_template`, select_one, optional): Select if you have a custom template.
* **Custom Template URL** (`custom_template_url`, text, optional): If you have a custom template, enter the URL.
* **Custom Template Image** (`custom_template_image`, text, optional): If you have a custom template image, enter the URL or image name.
* **Entry Fees** (`entry_fees`, number, optional): Enter the fee associated with your custom template (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Note on Entry Fees** (`entry_fees_note`, text, optional): Add a brief note regarding your entry fees.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Entry Type, Theme, and Entry Fee).
* If you have a custom template, ensure you have permission from the template owner to use it.
* If you have a custom template image, ensure it is the correct image for your entry.
