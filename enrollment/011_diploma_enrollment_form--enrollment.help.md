# Diploma Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diploma Enrollment Form is used to enroll students in a diploma program. This form is required for the enrollment process, and it helps us to collect information from students that is necessary for processing their applications and providing them with the services they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student_name field with your name as it should be displayed in our records.
2. Select whether or not you are interested in enrolling in the program (program_selection).
3. If applicable, enter your desired start date in the date field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. If you have any specific time slots in mind, enter it in the time field.
5. Add any relevant notes in the note field for our reference.
6. Enter your email address in the email field to ensure we can contact you.
7. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
8. Select your preferred payment method (payment_method).
9. Enter the amount of your payment (payment_amount).
10. Indicate the status of your payment (payment_status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: Please enter your name as it should be displayed in our records. This field is required.
* **program_selection**: Select whether or not you are interested in enrolling in the program. This field is required.
* **date**: If you have a preferred start date, enter it in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This field is optional.
* **time**: If you have a specific time slot in mind, enter it here. This field is optional.
* **note**: Add any relevant notes for our reference. This field is optional.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you. This field is required.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred payment method. This field is required.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the amount of your payment. This field is optional.
* **payment_status**: Indicate the status of your payment. This field is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any delays in the enrollment process.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
