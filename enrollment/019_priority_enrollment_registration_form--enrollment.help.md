# Priority Enrollment Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Priority Enrollment Registration Form is a crucial tool for students to register their priority status and other necessary details to be considered for enrollment. Please complete this form to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address correctly, as it will be used for future communication.
3. If applicable, provide your phone number for ease of contact.
4. Enter your Date of Birth to ensure accurate records.
5. Select your current Priority Status from the provided options.
6. If needed, attach any supporting documents to your application.
7. Review all the details carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for future communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, you can enter it for ease of contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Priority Status** (`priority_status`, select_one, required): Select your current Priority Status from the options provided: Active or Inactive.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional): If required, attach any relevant documents that support your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your application for review.

Remember to double-check all the fields before submitting to ensure accuracy and avoid any issues with your enrollment.
