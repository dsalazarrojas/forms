# Charity Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in enrolling as charities or partners in a specific cause or program. The form collects personal and contact information, details about how they want to contribute (volunteer, donate, etc.), their availability and donation frequency, and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting the type of enrollment**. Choose how you want to enroll (Volunteer, Donor, Member, Partner organization, or Beneficiary).
2. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the text fields. These are required fields.
3. Enter your **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Mailing Address** to help the organization contact you.
4. If you're enrolling as a volunteer, select your **Date of Birth** for age verification purposes.
5. **Enrollment Type**: Select how you want to contribute (Volunteer, Donor, etc.).
6. If you're a volunteer, **Select areas of interest** you'd like to help with (e.g., Event planning, Fundraising, Administrative support).
7. **Choose your availability** when you can help (e.g., Weekday mornings, Weekday afternoons, Flexible schedule).
8. If you're donating, **select your donation frequency** (e.g., One-time, Monthly, Quarterly).
9. **Select your donation amount** (e.g., $25, $50, $100, etc.).
10. **Enter emergency contact name and phone number** in case the organization needs to reach you.
11. **Describe any relevant skills or experience** you have to offer.
12. **Let us know how you heard about us** (e.g., Website, Social media, Friend).
13. **Agree to terms and conditions** by selecting "Yes I agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required** for volunteers): For age verification purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, **required**): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, **required**): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, **required**): Enter your postal code.
* **Enrollment Type** (`enrollment_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you want to contribute (e.g., Volunteer, Donor, etc.).
* **Volunteer Areas of Interest** (`volunteer_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas of interest if you're enrolling as a volunteer.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose when you can help.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, **optional**): Select if you're donating.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, select_one, **optional**): Suggest amount if donating.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, **optional**): In case of emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, **optional**): Emergency number.
* **Relevant Skills or Experience** (`skills_experience`, text, **optional**): Describe any relevant skills or experience.
* **How did you hear about us** (`referral_source`, select_one, **optional**): Let us know how you heard about us.
* **Do you agree to terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes I agree".
