# Enrollment Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to provide necessary information and agreements for enrollment in educational programs. It collects details about school policies, student and parent information, and agreements related to student responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
*   Provide the requested information in the corresponding fields.
*   Check the required fields to ensure they are completed.
*   Review and sign the agreements as a student and parent.
*   Date and sign each agreement accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School policy rules and goals (id: 1)**: This field is for providing information about the school's policies and goals. Please enter the relevant details as per your program's rules and objectives.
*   **Parent Information (id: 2)**: This field is for gathering information about the parent(s) of the student. Please enter the names, contact numbers, and any other relevant details about the parent(s).
*   **Parent Signature Date (id: 3)**: This field is for the date the parent signs the agreement. Please enter the date in DD/MM/YY format (e.g., 12/02/2024).
*   **Student Information (id: 4)**: This field is for gathering information about the student. Please enter the student's name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
*   **Student Signature Date (id: 5)**: This field is for the date the student signs the agreement. Please enter the date in DD/MM/YY format (e.g., 12/02/2024).
*   **Parent Signature (id: 6)**: This field is for the parent to sign the agreement, acknowledging their understanding and commitment to the program.
*   **Student Signature (id: 7)**: This field is for the student to sign the agreement, acknowledging their understanding and commitment to the program.
*   **Student Agreement (id: 8)**: This field is for the student to agree to the terms and conditions of the program. Please review the agreement carefully before signing.
*   **Parent Agreement (id: 9)**: This field is for the parent to agree to the terms and conditions of the program. Please review the agreement carefully before signing.
*   **Student Acknowledgement (id: 10)**: This field is for the student to acknowledge their understanding of the program's rules and expectations.
*   **Parent Acknowledgement (id: 11)**: This field is for the parent to acknowledge their understanding of the program's rules and expectations.
