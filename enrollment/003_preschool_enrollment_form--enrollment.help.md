# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Enrollment Form is a critical document that helps us understand your child's background and your family's needs. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please complete the form by providing the required information in each field.**
2. Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (Parent Information, text, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you regarding your child's enrollment.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, so we can stay in touch with you regarding your child's preschool experience.
* **Child Information** (Child Information, text, required: false): Please provide details about your child, including their name, age, and any relevant medical history.
* **Parent Relationships** (Parent Relationships, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with your child's other parent/guardian, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required: false): List the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Information** (Medical Information, text, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history or allergies your child may have.
* **Payment Information** (Payment Information, text, required: false): If you're enrolling your child in the preschool program, please provide payment details here.
* **Signature** (Signature, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have accurately completed this form.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select_one, required: false): Indicate who submitted this form, typically a parent or guardian.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time** (Submitted Time, time, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes or errors.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Only submit one enrollment form per child to avoid any issues or miscommunications.
