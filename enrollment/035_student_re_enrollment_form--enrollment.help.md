# Student Re Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Re Enrollment Form is used to re-enroll a student in the system, allowing them to update their information and confirm their re-enrollment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to re-enroll for the correct academic year.
3. Check that your student information is up to date.
4. If you have any issues with the form, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Student Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your student number if you have one.

Note: Please ensure that you have completed all fields accurately and truthfully.
