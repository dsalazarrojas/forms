# navy_flexible_spending_account_fsa_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and employment information for enrollment in a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter your personal details in the Personal Info page.
3. Enter your employee ID and name.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Select your department and job title.
6. Choose your account type (e.g., Flexible, Health, Dependent Care, Medical, or Transit).
7. Enter your start and end dates for the FSA enrollment.
8. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal details, including any relevant information about yourself.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number to link your personal info to your employment records.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address to receive FSA-related updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your department from the provided options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as it appears in your personnel records.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of FSA account you are interested in (e.g., Flexible, Health, Dependent Care, Medical, or Transit).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start your FSA enrollment.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to end your FSA enrollment.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your department for FSA purposes.

Tips:

* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting your form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR or payroll representative for assistance.
