# Incentive Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incentive Program Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from employees or participants for the incentive program. This form helps to gather relevant details about the employee, including name, job title, department, date of birth, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your company from the "Company" field.
3. Choose your job title from the "Job Title" field.
4. Select your department from the "Department" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. If applicable, enter your phone number and department ID in the respective fields.
7. Review your entries to ensure they are accurate and complete.
