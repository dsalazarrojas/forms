# hunter_education_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hunter education registration form is used to collect information from individuals who have completed a hunter education course. This form is required for hunter education program management and is used to verify completion status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: first name, last name, date of birth, phone number, email, certificate number, and date.
2. Select your Field Day status (Yes or No).
3. Choose your hunter education completion status (Active or Inactive).
4. Add any additional information that may be relevant to your registration.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it is spelled.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name as it is spelled.
- **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-01-01).
- **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
- **Hunter Number** (Text, Optional): If you have a hunter number, please enter it here. This is an optional field.
- **Certificate Number** (Text, Required): Enter the number on your hunter education certificate.
- **Utah Hunter Education Registration** (Text, Optional): If you have a Utah hunter education registration, please enter it here. This is an optional field.
- **Field Day** (Select One, Required): Select Yes if you have completed Field Day, No otherwise.
- **Hunter Education Completion Status** (Select One, Required): Select Active if you have completed the hunter education course, Inactive otherwise.
- **Additional Information** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional information relevant to your registration, please enter it here. This is an optional field.
- **Signature** (Note, Optional): Please sign this form to confirm your registration. This is not a required field.
- **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
