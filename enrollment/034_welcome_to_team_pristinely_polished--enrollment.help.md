# welcome_to_team_pristinely_polished - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general enrollment form used to collect information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name, including your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your date of birth (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the text box provided.
6. Select the tool you would like to be assigned from the dropdown menu.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your enrollment application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Form** (`enrollment_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is currently empty.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field requires a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is where you enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): This field is not currently utilized and does not seem to be a required field for the form. Please disregard this field for now.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): This field is for additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select from a list of available tools (chatjimmy, chatjimmy2, or chatjimmy3).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a submit button to complete and submit your enrollment application.
