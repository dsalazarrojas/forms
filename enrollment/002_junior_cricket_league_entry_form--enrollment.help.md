# junior_cricket_league_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The junior cricket league entry form is used to collect contact and team information from individuals or teams wishing to join the league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Information page with your details.
2. Select your preferred league type.
3. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
4. Enter your manager's information (name and email).
5. Select your team(s) and preferred match dates.
6. Provide any additional comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: Enter your name, address, and other relevant contact details. This will help the league coordinators reach you with important updates and information.
* **League Type (league_type)**: Choose the type of league you wish to join (e.g., League 1 or League 2). 
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Manager Name (manager_name)**: Enter the name of the team's manager.
* **Manager Email (manager_email)**: Enter the email address of the team's manager.
* **Team Names (team_names)**: Select the team(s) you wish to join. You may choose multiple teams.
* **Match Date Preferences (match_dates)**: Select the date(s) you prefer for matches.
* **Comments (comments)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your team (optional).

## Tips
Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure smooth league coordination. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to the league coordinators for clarification.
