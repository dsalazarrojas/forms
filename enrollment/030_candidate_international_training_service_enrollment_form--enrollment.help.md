# Candidate International Training Service Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for the International Training Service. It includes various sections to gather details about the applicant's profile, academic background, motivation, and logistical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information in the **Applicant Information** section.
2. Choose your preferred training program from the list of options.
3. Select your preferred enrollment term.
4. Indicate your level of study (e.g., undergraduate, graduate, certification).
5. Describe your highest level of education.
6. Briefly explain your work experience.
7. State your reason for choosing the training program.
8. Rate your English proficiency.
9. Indicate if you need help with your student visa application.
10. Choose whether you need accommodation assistance.
11. Mention any dietary requirements or medical needs.
12. Confirm that all the details provided are accurate.
13. Submit the form by indicating the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`): Enter your full name as per your passport.
* **Nationality** (`applicant_nationality`): Enter your country of primary citizenship.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_dob`): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Passport Number** (`applicant_passport_number`): Enter your passport number for international enrollment and visa processing purposes.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter your primary email address for all enrollment communication.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Preferred Training Program** (`preferred_training_program`): Choose your preferred training program from the list of options.
* **Enrollment Term** (`enrollment_term_choice`): Select your preferred enrollment term from the list of options.
* **Level of Study** (`level_of_study_pref`): Choose the academic level of the course you are applying for.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education_summary`): Enter your most recent degree or qualification.
* **Relevant Work Experience** (`work_experience_brief`): Briefly describe your professional background.
* **Reason for Choosing this Program** (`motivation_reason_text`): Explain why you are interested in this training program.
* **English Proficiency** (`english_proficiency_level`): Rate your command of the English language from the options provided.
* **Visa Assistance Required** (`visa_assistance_req`): Indicate if you need help with your student visa application.
* **Accommodation Assistance** (`accommodation_assistance_req`): Choose whether you need accommodation assistance.
* **Dietary Requirements/Medical Needs** (`dietary_medical_needs`): Mention any dietary requirements or medical needs we should be aware of.
* **Confirm Accurate Information** (`data_accuracy_certify`): Confirm that all the details provided are accurate.
* **Date of Application** (`application_submission_date`): Enter the date you submit this form.

## Tips
- Review each section carefully to ensure all required fields are filled.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
