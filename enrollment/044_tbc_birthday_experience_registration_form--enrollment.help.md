# TBC Birthday Experience Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect basic contact information and birth date from customers or users who are interested in experiencing the benefits of TBC (The Birthday Club). The form helps us understand your preferences and demographics, ensuring we can tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field next to it.
3. Select your **Birth Date** from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Provide your **Email** address to stay updated on our services and promotions.
5. Enter your **Phone** number to enable us to contact you for further information or to confirm any appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birth Date**: Enter the date of your birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. If you are not familiar with this format, you can select the date from the calendar picker.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about our services, promotions, and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you for further information or to confirm any appointments.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive updates and notifications from us.
* If you have any issues with the form, feel free to contact us, and we'll be happy to assist you.
* We value your privacy and security, and we will never share your information with third parties unless it's necessary for our services or as required by law.
