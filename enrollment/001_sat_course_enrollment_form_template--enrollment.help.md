# SAT Course Enrollment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll students into a course, collecting essential information such as contact details, payment methods, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your user information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields. These fields are not required for form submission.
   - **User**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
   - **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
   - **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.

2. **Select your preferred course**: Choose the course you wish to enroll in from the available options. This field is not required for form submission.
   - **Course**: Select your preferred course from the available choices.

3. **Choose your payment method**: Select your preferred payment method, which is required for form submission. If you have other payment methods, select "Other".
   - **Payment Method**: Select either "PayPal" or "Other" to proceed with your enrollment.

4. **Enter your payment token**: If you have selected "Other" as your payment method, select "None" or enter the other payment token provided to you. This field is not required for form submission.
   - **Payment Token**: Enter the payment token if your method is "Other".

5. **Submit your enrollment**: Click the "Submit" button to complete your enrollment process. This field is not required for form submission.
   - **Submit**: Click this button to finalize your enrollment.

6. **Additional Information**: Enter the date and time of your preferred attendance, and any relevant notes about your enrollment. These fields are not required for form submission.
   - **Date**: Enter the date of your preferred attendance.
   - **Time**: Enter the time of your preferred attendance.
   - **Notes**: Provide any relevant notes or information about your enrollment.

7. **Enter your SAT score (if applicable)**: If you are required to enter an SAT score, enter it in the provided field. This field is not required for form submission.
   - **SAT Score**: If you are required to take the SAT, enter your score here.

8. **Enter your student name (if applicable)**: If you are required to enter your student name, enter it in the provided field. This field is not required for form submission.
   - **Student Name**: If you have a student name, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: This field is for you to enter your name, which helps us identify you.
* **Course**: This field is for you to select the course you wish to enroll in.
* **Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is for you to enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Payment Method**: This field is for you to select your preferred payment method, which is required for form submission.
* **Payment Token**: If you have selected "Other" as your payment method, this field is for you to enter the other payment token provided to you.
* **Submit**: This field is for you to finalize your enrollment.
* **Date**: This field is for you to enter the date of your preferred attendance.
* **Time**: This field is for you to enter the time of your preferred attendance.
* **Notes**: This field is for you to provide any relevant notes or information about your enrollment.
* **SAT Score**: If you are required to take the SAT, enter your score here.
* **Student Name**: If you are required to enter your student name, enter it here.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields, especially your name, email, and payment method to complete the enrollment process successfully.
- Ensure you have all necessary information for the SAT score and student name fields if they are required for your enrollment.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
