# Summer Youth Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Youth Program Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Summer Youth Program. To be eligible, applicants must fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. This form is for new applications only; if you have already submitted an application, please do not duplicate this process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is marked as "required" in each field.
2. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the program.
* **Email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX XXX XXXX.
* **Address** (`applicant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address if it is different from your phone number.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant work or life experience that makes you a good fit for the program.
* **Thoughts** (`thoughts`, text, required): Share your thoughts on why you are interested in the program and what you hope to achieve from it.
* **Essay** (`essay`, text, required): Write a short essay (max 500 words) explaining why you are a good fit for the program and what you hope to gain from it.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is on your government-issued ID.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX XXX XXXX.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship you have to the applicant:
	+ Parent: You are a parent of the applicant.
	+ Guardian: You are a guardian of the applicant.
	+ Spouse: You are the spouse of the applicant.
	+ Other: Select this option if you do not identify as a parent, guardian, or spouse.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators.
* Only submit one application per person.
