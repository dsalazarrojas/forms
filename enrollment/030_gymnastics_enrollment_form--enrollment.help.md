# Gymnastics Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gymnastics Enrollment Form is a simple registration form designed to collect necessary information from students who wish to enroll in a gymnastics class. This form is intended for students to provide their personal details, class preferences, and any relevant experience they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields. These fields are required to identify you as a student.
2. Provide a valid Email address that we can use to contact you about your class schedule and any important updates.
3. Enter your Phone number to stay in touch with us and receive important notifications.
4. Select a date for the class you wish to attend. Choose a date that suits your schedule best.
5. Choose the time of the class that you're interested in.
6. Indicate the days you plan to attend the class.
7. If you have any relevant experience in gymnastics, describe it in this field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your enrollment form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address so we can contact you about your class schedule and any important updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to stay in touch with us and receive important notifications.
* **Class Date**: Select a date for the class you wish to attend. Please choose a date that suits your schedule best.
* **Class Time**: Choose the time of the class that you're interested in.
* **Class Days**: Indicate which days you plan to attend the class. Check the days that apply to you.
* **Experience**: If you have any relevant experience in gymnastics, describe it here. This will help us tailor the class to your needs.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your enrollment form once you've completed all the fields.
