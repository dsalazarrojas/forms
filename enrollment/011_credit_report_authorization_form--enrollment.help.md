# Credit Report Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for credit reporting purposes, allowing users to authorize access to their credit report data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic contact information: Enter your phone number and email address in the "phone" and "email" fields.
2. Select your credit bureau: Choose from "Equifax", "TransUnion", or "Experian" in the "credit_bureaus" field.
3. Specify your authorization preferences: Select "Landlord", "Lender", or "Employer" from the "authorization" field.
4. Enter your date of birth: Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter your address: Enter your address in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Credit Report (credit_report)**: Enter your credit report details, if any.
* **Authorization (authorization)**: Specify who you are authorizing to access your credit report data. Choose from "Landlord", "Lender", or "Employer".
* **Credit Bureaus (credit_bureaus)**: Select which credit bureau you want to access your report from. Choose from "Equifax", "TransUnion", or "Experian".
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your address.
