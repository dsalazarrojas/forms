# Education Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Class Registration Form is designed to collect information from students to facilitate their registration for an education class. This form helps gather personal details, contact information, and preferences for the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Please enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2.  If you have a parent/guardian's contact number, enter it in the "Do you have a parent/guardian's contact number?" field. This is optional.
3.  Check the "Are you a returning student?" checkbox if you have previously attended this class.
4.  Select the class you would like to enroll in from the "Which class do you want to enroll in?" dropdown menu.
5.  Choose how you would like to be contacted from the "How would you like to be contacted?" dropdown menu.
6.  Enter your date of birth in the "What is your date of birth?" field in MM/DD/YY format.
7.  If you have any allergies or health issues, list them in the "Do you have any allergies or health issues?" field.
8.  Check the "Do you have any previous education experience?" checkbox if you have relevant experience.
9.  If you have any special needs requirements, list them in the "Do you have any special needs requirements?" field.
10. Enter the time you would like to be contacted during the day in the "What time would you like to be contacted during the day?" field in 24-hour format.
11.  Add any additional comments or concerns in the "Do you have any other comments or concerns?" field.
12.  Finally, confirm your enrollment by selecting "Yes" from the "Do you confirm your enrollment?" radio button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is your name? (1)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Do you have a parent/guardian's contact number? (2)**: If you have a contact number, enter it here. This field is not required.
*   **Are you a returning student? (3)**: Check this box if you have attended this class before.
*   **Which class do you want to enroll in? (4)**: Select the class you would like to join from the dropdown menu.
*   **How would you like to be contacted? (5)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting one of the options.
*   **What is your date of birth? (6)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
*   **Do you have any allergies or health issues? (7)**: If you have any allergies or health issues, list them here.
*   **Do you have any previous education experience? (8)**: Check this box if you have relevant experience.
*   **Do you have any special needs requirements? (9)**: List any special needs you may have here.
*   **What time would you like to be contacted during the day? (10)**: Enter the time you would like to be contacted in 24-hour format.
*   **Do you have any other comments or concerns? (11)**: Add any additional comments or concerns here.
*   **Do you confirm your enrollment? (12)**: Select "Yes" to confirm your registration.

The form is designed to collect necessary information for a smooth registration process. Please ensure that you answer each question accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your registration.
