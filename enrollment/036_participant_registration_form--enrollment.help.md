# Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for registration and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section with your first and last name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Fill out the emergency contact section with the contact's name, phone number, email, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. List any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
4. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
5. List any medical allergies you have in the "Medical Allergies" field.
6. Select a date and time for your participant consent.
7. Select a date and time for your emergency contact's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Select your state of residence from the dropdown list.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact State**: Select the state of the emergency contact person from the dropdown list.
* **Emergency Contact Zip**: Enter the zip code of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have (e.g. Diabetes, High Blood Pressure, etc.).
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Allergies**: List any medical allergies you have (e.g. Peanut, Shellfish, etc.).
* **Participant Consent Date**: Select a date for your participant consent.
* **Participant Consent Time**: Select a time for your participant consent.
* **Emergency Contact Consent Date**: Select a date for your emergency contact's consent.
* **Emergency Contact Consent Time**: Select a time for your emergency contact's consent.
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Provide a brief statement indicating your consent to be listed as an emergency contact.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* Make sure to include all required information.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your emergency contact for help.
* Make sure to review your information before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes to your information, contact the form administrators.
