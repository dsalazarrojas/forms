# Temporary Employee Payroll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Temporary Employee Payroll Form is used to collect and record information about temporary employees, including tax details, employment specifics, and emergency contact information. This form ensures accurate and up-to-date data for administrative and tax purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct tax ID type (EIN, SSN, or ITIN) and enter the corresponding ID number.
2. Enter the new hire's first name, last name, and address.
3. Choose the state of residence from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the zip code.
5. Select the start and end dates of employment.
6. Enter the hourly wage (if applicable).
7. Enter the tax rate (if applicable).
8. Enter the new hire's phone number and email address.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
10. Enter the emergency contact's address (if applicable).
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the new hire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire**: Enter the name of the temporary employee being added to the payroll system.
* **Tax Id**: Enter the tax ID type (EIN, SSN, or ITIN) and the corresponding ID number.
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the new hire.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the new hire.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the new hire.
* **City**: Select the city of the new hire's residence.
* **State**: Select the state of the new hire's residence.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the new hire's residence.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of employment.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of employment.
* **Hourly Wage**: Enter the hourly wage of the new hire (if applicable).
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate of the new hire (if applicable).
* **Tax Id Type**: Select the type of tax ID.
* **SSN**: Enter the social security number.
* **ITIN**: Enter the individual tax ID number.
* **EIN**: Enter the employer identification number.
* **Bank Account**: Enter the new hire's bank account information.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number for the bank account.
* **Direct Deposit**: Enter the details for direct deposit (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the new hire.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered all required fields (start date and end date).
* Double-check the tax ID type and ensure it matches the corresponding ID number.
* Use the correct format for dates (yyyy-mm-dd).
* Be accurate and thorough when entering the new hire's address and contact information.
* Use the correct format for phone numbers and email addresses.
