# COVID Student Intake Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health and safety information from students and their parents or guardians to ensure the student's health and safety while at school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each page carefully and provide the requested information. You can use the hints provided for each field to guide you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, required): This field is used to collect the student's complete legal name. Please enter the student's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Grade Level or Class Year** (`student_grade_level`, required): This field is used to collect the student's current grade or year. Please select the correct level or year.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_full_name`, required): This field is used to collect the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, required): Enter the parent or guardian's contact number.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms** (`student_current_symptoms`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the student has any symptoms of COVID-19.
* **Exposure to Someone Positive** (`student_exposed_positive`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the student has been exposed to someone positive in the last 14 days.
* **Recently Tested Positive** (`student_tested_positive`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the student has recently tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Date of Positive Test if Applicable** (`student_positive_test_date`, optional): If the student has tested positive, enter the date of the positive test in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`student_vaccination_status`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate the student's vaccination status.
* **Medical Conditions** (`student_medical_conditions_header`, optional): This field is for any ongoing health conditions the student may have.
* **Has Chronic Conditions** (`student_chronic_conditions`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the student has any chronic medical conditions.
* **If Yes, Please Describe Conditions** (`student_condition_details`, optional): If the student has any chronic conditions, provide a brief description of the conditions.
* **Medication or Vaccine Allergies** (`student_medication_allergies`, optional): If the student has any known allergies, describe them briefly.
* **Parent Understands COVID-19 Risks** (`parent_covid_understanding`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the parent or guardian understands the risks of COVID-19.
* **Student Will Follow Health and Safety Guidelines** (`parent_health_compliance`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the parent or guardian agrees that the student will follow health and safety guidelines.
* **I Certify Information Provided is Accurate** (`parent_intake_completion`, required): Select the correct answer from the options provided to indicate whether the parent or guardian certifies that the information provided is accurate.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, required): Enter the date and time the parent or guardian signed the agreement in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
