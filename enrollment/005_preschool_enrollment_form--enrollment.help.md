# preschool_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from families and parents for preschool enrollment purposes, including family and student details, payment methods, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Family Information section with your family's details.
2. Provide the student's information, including name, date of birth, grade level, and address.
3. Select the payment type and method.
4. Add any additional payment details.
5. Enter the contact person's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Information** (`family_info`, text, required: false)
Family Information is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your family.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, required: false)
Provide the student's name in this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
Enter the contact person's name and relevant contact details.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional payment details.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required: false)
Provide any relevant information about the parent.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of payment method you would like to use. Options include: Cash, Check, Credit Card, and Bank Transfer.
* **Credit Card Information** (`credit_card_info`, text, required: false)
Enter the credit card details if you selected Credit Card as the payment method.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional payment details.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the student's name.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required: false)
Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required: false)
Enter the student's grade level.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false)
Select the method of payment. Options include: Credit, Debit, Online Banking, or Other.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false)
Enter the student's address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, required: false)
Enter the student's city.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, required: false)
Enter the student's state.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`, number, required: false)
Enter the student's zip code.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, required: false)
Enter the parent's address.
* **Parent City** (`parent_city`, text, required: false)
Enter the parent's city.
* **Parent State** (`parent_state`, text, required: false)
Enter the parent's state.
* **Parent Zip** (`parent_zip`, number, required: false)
Enter the parent's zip code.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the status of payment. Options include: Paid, Partially Paid, and Not Paid.
