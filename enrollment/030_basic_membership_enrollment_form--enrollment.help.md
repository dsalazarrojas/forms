# Basic Membership Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basic Membership Enrollment Form is used to collect information for enrollment into a membership program. This form gathers your personal details, membership preferences, and payment information to create a membership profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin the enrollment process.
2.  Fill out the form in sections: Personal Information, Address, Membership Type, Membership Details, Interests and Preferences, Communication Preferences, Payment Information, Emergency Contact, and Agreement and Confirmation.
3.  Review each field carefully, ensuring that you provide accurate and complete information.
4.  Click on the "Submit" button to submit your enrollment application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact and verification purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address or physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country where you reside.
* **Membership level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the membership level that best suits your needs (Basic, Premium, Gold, Platinum, Student, Senior, Family).
* **Membership duration** (`membership_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your membership (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, Lifetime).
* **Enrollment date** (`enrollment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are enrolling into the membership program.
* **Select your areas of interest** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more areas of interest that you'd like to participate in (Sports, Recreation, Wellness, Social Events, Professional Development, Community Service, Other).
* **Preferred activities** (`preferred_activities`, `text`, optional): Enter any preferred activities you'd like to participate in.
* **Preferred communication method** (`communication_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, Text Message, Mail, No Contact).
* **Subscribe to newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you'd like to subscribe to the newsletter (True, False).
* **Receive event notifications** (`event_notifications`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you'd like to receive event notifications (True, False).
* **Preferred payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, PayPal, Cash).
* **Billing frequency** (`billing_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the billing frequency that suits your needs (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to the membership terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the membership terms and conditions (I Agree, I Do Not Agree).
* **I consent to privacy policy and data usage** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you consent to the privacy policy and data usage (I Agree, I Do Not Agree).
* **I confirm all information is accurate** (`enrollment_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that all the information you've provided is accurate (I Confirm, I Need to Review).

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only. Please refer to the original form for the most accurate and up-to-date information.
