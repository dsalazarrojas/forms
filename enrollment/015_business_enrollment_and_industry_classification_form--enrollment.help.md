# Business Enrollment And Industry Classification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Enrollment And Industry Classification Form is used to collect detailed information about businesses, including their name, structure, industry classification, location, and other relevant details. This information helps us to better understand and analyze business data for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill out the form in one attempt, without errors or omissions.
3. Ensure that the form is properly completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Business Name** (`Business Name`, Text, Required): Provide the registered name of the business.
* **DBA Name (if different)** (`DBA Name`, Text, Optional): If the business operates under a different name, provide it here.
* **Date of Business Registration** (`Date of Business Registration`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the business was registered.
* **Business License Number** (`Business License Number`, Text, Required): Enter the business license or registration ID.
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`Tax ID or EIN`, Text, Optional): Enter the Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Tax ID.
* **Business Structure** (`Business Structure`, Select One, Required): Select how the business is organized (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, S-Corporation, C-Corporation, Non-Profit, Cooperative, Other).
* **Structure Details** (`Structure Details`, Text, Optional): Provide additional information about the business structure.
* **Primary Industry** (`Primary Industry`, Select One, Required): Select the main industry category the business operates in.
* **Secondary Industries (if applicable)** (`Secondary Industries`, Text, Optional): If the business operates in other industries, list them here.
* **NAICS Code** (`NAICS Code`, Text, Optional): Enter the industry classification code.
* **Primary Business Activities** (`Primary Business Activities`, Text, Required): Describe what the business does.
* **Main Products or Services** (`Main Products or Services`, Text, Required): List the key offerings of the business.
* **Total Number of Employees** (`Total Number of Employees`, Number, Required): Enter the current employee count.
* **Employee Count Range** (`Employee Count Range`, Select One, Required): Select the employee count range (e.g., 1-9, 10-49, 50-249, 250-499, 500-999, 1000+).
* **Principal Place of Business** (`Principal Place of Business`, Text, Required): Enter the main office address.
* **City** (`City`, Text, Required): Enter the city of the main office.
* **State/Province** (`State/Province`, Text, Required): Enter the state or province of the main office.
* **Country** (`Country`, Text, Required): Enter the country of the main office.
* **Number of Operating Locations** (`Number of Operating Locations`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of offices or facilities the business operates.

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "Required" are completed accurately and completely.
