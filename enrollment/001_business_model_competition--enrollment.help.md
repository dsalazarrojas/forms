# Business Model Competition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business model competition entries from teams. It captures essential information about each team, including team members, project title, project summary, and other relevant details. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully and provide clear and concise answers.
3. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the official name of your team. This will be displayed on our competition platform.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the full name of the team lead or contact person. We will use this for correspondence and communication.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. Please include your email address with the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number including the country code if it's outside your local area. For example, if you're from the US, you would enter `+1-123-456-7890`.
* **Institution or Company** (`institution_or_company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the university or company you are affiliated with, if applicable.
* **Team Member Names** (`team_member_list`, `text`, required/optional): List the names of all team members, separated by commas.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a short descriptive title for your project, summarizing its main idea.
* **Project Summary** (`project_summary`, `text`, required/optional): Write a 150-300 word summary of your business model and its value proposition.
* **Competition Category** (`competition_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best fits your project from the options below:
	+ Technology
	+ Social Impact
	+ Consumer Product
	+ Services
	+ Other
* **Development Stage** (`development_stage`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the current stage of your venture from the options below:
	+ Idea
	+ Prototype
	+ Revenue Generating
	+ Scaling
* **Website or Pitch Deck Link** (`website_or_pitch_deck_link`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a link to more information about your project, such as a website or pitch deck.
* **Agreement to Rules and Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that your team agrees to the competition rules and judging decisions.

Note: The following fields are optional but can provide additional context and information about your project:
* Primary Contact Phone (if not required)
* Institution or Company (if not required)
* Website or Pitch Deck Link (if not required)
