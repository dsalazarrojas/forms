# Online Enrolment Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The online enrolment form template is a crucial tool for collecting student information for online enrolment. Its primary function is to gather essential details about the student, including their name, contact information, programme specifics, and enrolment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your student address in the "Student Address" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. If applicable, enter your contact phone extension in the "Contact Phone Ext" field.
7. If applicable, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
8. Enter the date and time of your enrolment in the "Date of Enrolment" and "Time of Enrolment" fields, respectively.
9. Choose the duration of your enrolment period in the "Enrolment Duration" field.
10. Select the level of your programme from the "Enrolment Level" dropdown menu.
11. Select the type of programme you are enrolled in from the "Programme" dropdown menu.
12. Choose the duration of your programme from the "Programme Duration" dropdown menu.
13. Select the level of your programme from the "Programme Level" dropdown menu.
14. Choose the type of programme you are enrolled in from the "Programme Type" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the start date of your programme in the "Programme Start Date" field.
16. (Optional) Enter the end date of your programme in the "Programme End Date" field.
17. Select the status of your programme from the "Programme Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your student name.
* **Student Email (2)**: Enter your student email address.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Student Address (4)**: Enter your student address.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Phone Ext (6)**: If applicable, enter your contact phone extension.
* **Emergency Contact Name (7)**: If applicable, provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (8)**: If applicable, enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Date of Enrolment (9)**: Enter the date of your enrolment.
* **Time of Enrolment (10)**: Enter the time of your enrolment.
* **Enrolment Duration (11)**: Choose the duration of your enrolment period.
* **Enrolment Level (12)**: Select the level of your enrolment from the dropdown menu.
* **Programme (13)**: Select the type of programme you are enrolled in from the dropdown menu.
* **Programme Duration (14)**: Choose the duration of your programme.
* **Programme Level (15)**: Select the level of your programme from the dropdown menu.
* **Programme Type (16)**: Choose the type of programme you are enrolled in from the dropdown menu.
* **Programme Start Date (17)**: Enter the start date of your programme.
* **Programme End Date (18)**: (Optional) Enter the end date of your programme.
* **Programme Status (19)**: Select the status of your programme from the dropdown menu.
