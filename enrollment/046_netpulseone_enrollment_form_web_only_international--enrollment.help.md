# NetpulseOne Enrollment Form (web Only)_International - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NetpulseOne Enrollment Form (web Only)_International is used to capture enrollment information for international web-based processes. This form helps in collecting essential details to enroll or register a user or entity for a specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. On the first page, you will be asked for Enrollment Information.
3. On the subsequent pages, you will be asked for specific details such as Name, Email, Phone, Date, Time, and Additional Information.
4. Select your Enrollment Status from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Information** (`enrollment_page_1`, text, required): This field is a placeholder for any additional information you may want to provide for enrollment purposes.
* **Name** (`enrollment_form_1`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`enrollment_form_2`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`enrollment_form_3`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`enrollment_form_4`, date, required): Enter the date for which you are enrolling.
* **Time** (`enrollment_form_5`, time, optional): Enter the time for which you are enrolling (if applicable).
* **Additional Information** (`enrollment_form_6`, note, optional): Provide any additional details or comments for the enrollment process.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_form_7`, select_one, required): Select the status of your enrollment from the provided options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submission.
- Double-check your email address for any spelling or formatting errors.
- The phone number and time fields are optional but recommended for better contactability.
- Use the Additional Information field to provide any necessary context or details for your enrollment.
