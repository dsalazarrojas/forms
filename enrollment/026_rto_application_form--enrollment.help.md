# RTO Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RTO Application Form is a mandatory form to be completed by individuals who wish to apply for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) to enhance their skills and career development. This form will help us understand your background and preferences to assess your eligibility and guide you through the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow the below steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that the email and phone number fields are in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com for email, and 123-123-1234 for phone number).
3. Be accurate when entering your date of birth, as it will be used to verify your age eligibility.
4. Review your application carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RTO Application Form** (`rto_application_form`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your purpose for applying for RTO. This can be a few sentences explaining why you are interested in pursuing RPL.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your valid email address to receive further communication about your application.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, Optional): Enter your 10-digit phone number in the format (123) 123-1234.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., 12/31/1990). This will be used to verify your age eligibility for RPL.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have read and understood the RTO eligibility criteria before applying.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for further assistance.
