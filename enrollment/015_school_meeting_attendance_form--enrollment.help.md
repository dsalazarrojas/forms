# school_meeting_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record a user's attendance status during a school meeting. This form can be used to report attendance to school administrators or management.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details on **PersonalDetails** page.
2. Provide your contact information on **ContactDetails** page.
3. Select your attendance status on **AttendanceStatus** page. Choose from the options: **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**.
4. (Optional) Enter any notes or reasons for your absence on **Notes** page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **PersonalDetails**:
	+ Label: **PersonalDetails**
	+ Type: **text**
	+ Required: **false** (not mandatory)
	+ Explanation: Fill in your name and any other personal information required for your attendance to be recorded correctly.
- **ContactDetails**:
	+ Label: **ContactDetails**
	+ Type: **text**
	+ Required: **false** (not mandatory)
	+ Explanation: Provide your contact information so that we can reach you for any follow-up questions or notifications.
- **AttendanceStatus**:
	+ Label: **AttendanceStatus**
	+ Type: **select_one**
	+ Required: **false** (not mandatory)
	+ Explanation: Select the status that best describes your attendance during the meeting.
- **Notes**:
	+ Label: **Notes**
	+ Type: **note**
	+ Required: **false** (not mandatory)
	+ Explanation: Please provide any additional comments or reasons for your absence.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unable to attend a meeting, please provide a valid reason for your absence.
- If you need help or have questions, contact our school administrators.
