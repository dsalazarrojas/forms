# Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Registration Form is designed to collect information from athletes and their guardians for registration purposes. This form is intended to ensure that all necessary contact and consent information is gathered for proper athlete management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your athlete's name and any other relevant details.
3. Provide your guardian's contact information (first name, last name, email, and phone number).
4. Confirm that you and your guardian have read and consent to the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **disclaimer_text** (`disclaimer_text`, text, required: false): This is a general text field where you can enter any important information or instructions related to the form.
* **athlete_name** (`athlete_name`, text, required: false): Enter your athlete's name as they would like to be addressed.
* **position_please** (`position_please`, text, required: false): If you have a specific position in mind for the athlete, enter it here. This is optional and will be used to help match the athlete with available positions or teams.
* **preferred_position** (`preferred_position`, text, required: false): If you have a preferred position for the athlete, enter it here.
* **guardian_first_name** (`guardian_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the athlete's guardian or parent.
* **guardian_last_name** (`guardian_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the athlete's guardian or parent.
* **guardian_email** (`guardian_email`, text, required: false): Enter the email address of the athlete's guardian or parent.
* **guardian_phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the athlete's guardian or parent.
* **guardian_consent_terms** (`guardian_consent_terms`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you, as the guardian, have read and consent to the terms of this form by selecting "Yes".
* **athlete_consent_terms** (`athlete_consent_terms`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you, as the athlete, have read and consent to the terms of this form by selecting "Yes".
* **athlete_consent_terms** (`athlete_consent_terms`, select_one, required: false): This is a duplicate field of the same field "athlete_consent_terms". Please disregard this field as it is redundant.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
