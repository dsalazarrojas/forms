# Enrolment Form PTQ - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrolment Form PTQ is used to collect information about trainings and courses for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the trainings you are interested in from the "Select a training" section.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter the start date of your training in the "Start Date" field.
4. Enter the start time of your training in the "Start Time" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Describe any additional training details in the "Training Details" field.
8. Select the courses you are interested in from the "Select a course" section.
9. Select the courses you are currently enrolled in from the "Enrolled Course" section.
10. Choose the status of the training you are enrolling in from the "Training Status" section.
11. Choose the status of the course from the "Course Status" section.
12. Enter any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
13. Select the type of training you are enrolling in from the "Training Type" section.
14. Select the type of course from the "Course Type" section.
15. Enter the user who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
16. Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the "Submitted On" and "Submitted On Time" fields respectively.
17. Enter the user who last updated the form in the "Updated By" field.
18. Enter the date and time the form was last updated in the "Updated On" and "Updated On Time" fields respectively.
19. Enter the user's phone number in the "Submitted By" field.
20. Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the "Submitted On Date" and "Submitted On Time" fields respectively.
21. Enter the user to whom the form is assigned in the "Assigned To" field.
22. Enter the user who created the form in the "Created By" field.
