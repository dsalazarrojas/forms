# Enrollment Charge Clearance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Charge Clearance Request form is used to request clearance for enrollment charges. It's essential for students to complete this form accurately to ensure timely and efficient processing of their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Double-check for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide general information about yourself, including your name and contact details.
- **Fee Details** (`Fee Details`, `text`, required): In this field, provide detailed information about the fees you're requesting clearance for, including the fee type and amount.
- **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide any additional payment-related information, such as payment method or date.
- **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, `text`, required): As a student, sign this field to confirm your request.
- **Faculty Signature** (`Faculty Signature`, `text`, required): If you're not a student, a faculty member or staff should sign this field to confirm the request.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or context about your request.
- **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `select_one`, required): Choose who submitted the request: Student, Faculty, or Other.
- **Date Submitted** (`Date Submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted the request.
- **Time Submitted** (`Time Submitted`, `time`, required): Enter the time you submitted the request.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any doubts or issues with the form, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
