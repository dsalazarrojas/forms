# Dance Competition Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from dancers participating in the competition. This form helps us to gather essential details such as name, contact information, event details, and dancer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **First Name** field with your first name.
2. Fill in the **Last Name** field with your last name.
3. Enter a valid **Email** address that we can use to contact you.
4. Fill in the **Phone** field with your contact phone number.
5. Fill in the **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip Code** fields with your current address.
6. Enter the **Event Title** of the competition you are participating in.
7. Select the **Event Date** and **Event Time** of the competition from the calendar.
8. Enter the **Event Location** where the competition will take place.
9. Select your preferred **Competition Type** from the available options.
10. Select your corresponding **Age Group** from the list.
11. Choose your **Gender** from the options provided.
12. If you have an emergency contact, fill in the **Emergency Contact Name**, **Relationship**, **Emergency Contact Phone**, and enter a brief **Emergency Contact Message**.
13. If you have a guardian, fill in their **Guardian Name**, **Guardian Phone**, and enter a brief **Guardian Message**.
14. Answer **Competition Type 2**, **Age Group 2** questions by selecting your answer from the options provided.
14. Click on the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): This is the first name of the dancer.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): This is the last name of the dancer.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): A valid email address of the dancer or their guardian.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): A contact phone number for the dancer or their guardian.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): The home address of the dancer or their guardian.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): The city where the dancer lives.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): The state where the dancer lives.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, `text`, required): The zip code of the dancer's residence.
* **Event Title** (`Event Title`, `text`, required): Name of the event the dancer is participating in.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): The date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, `time`, required): The time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, `text`, required): The location of the event.
* **Competition Type** (`Competition Type`, `select_one`, required): Select from a list of available competition types (Ballet, Hip-Hop, Contemporary, Jazz, Tap, Ballroom).
* **Age Group** (`Age Group`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from a list of age groups (5-8, 9-12, 13-16, 17-20).
* **Gender** (`Gender`, `select_one`, required): Select from a list of available gender options (Male, Female, Non-binary).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required): Name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, `text`, required): Relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (`Emergency Contact Message`, `text`, required): Additional message for the emergency contact person.
* **Guardian Name** (`Guardian Name`, `text`, required): Name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (`Guardian Phone`, `text`, required): Phone number of the guardian.
* **Guardian Message** (`Guardian Message`, `text`, required): Additional message for the guardian.
* **Competition Type 2** (`Competition Type 2`, `select_one`, required): Select from a list of available competition types (Yes, No).
* **Age Group 2** (`Age Group 2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select from a list of age groups (Yes, No).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Select your preferred competition type and age group correctly.
* Enter a valid email address for emergency contact.
* Provide a valid phone number for emergency contact.
* Provide a brief message for the emergency contact person and guardian.
* Answer the competition type 2 and age group 2 questions truthfully.
* Click on the **Submit** button to complete the form.
