# 2021 Employee Benefit Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide employees through the 2021 employee benefit enrollment process. It collects personal and benefit-related information to ensure accurate enrollment and processing of benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and follow the instructions provided.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Select your preferred options for benefits.
4. Provide accurate information for your primary beneficiary.
5. Complete the form by signing with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID Number**: Enter your 6-digit employee ID number from your badge.
* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your Social Security card.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Work Email Address**: Enter your work email address to receive benefit-related communications.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter your primary phone number for urgent benefits notifications.
* **Current Mailing Address**: Enter your current mailing address (street, city, state, ZIP).
* **Health Insurance Selection**: Select your preferred health insurance plan:
	+ PPO Premium (Higher cost, lower deductible)
	+ PPO Standard (Mid-range coverage)
	+ HDHP with HSA (Lower premiums, high deductible)
	+ HMO Network (Primary care coordinator required)
	+ Decline Medical Coverage
* **Coverage Level**: Select who will be covered under your medical plan:
	+ Employee Only
	+ Employee + Spouse/Partner
	+ Employee + Child(ren)
	+ Employee + Family
* **Dental Coverage**: Select your preferred level of dental coverage:
	+ Basic Preventive (Cleanings only)
	+ Standard Coverage (Fillings, basic procedures)
	+ Premium Plus (Orthodontia included)
	+ Decline Dental Coverage
* **Vision Coverage**: Select your preferred level of vision coverage:
	+ Basic Vision ($150 frame allowance)
	+ Enhanced Vision ($250 allowance, contact lens coverage)
	+ Decline Vision Coverage
* **Life & Disability Insurance**: This section is for selecting life and disability insurance options.
* **Supplemental Life Insurance**: Select the level of supplemental life insurance you want:
	+ 1x Salary (Company paid basic)
	+ 2x Annual Salary (Employee paid supplemental)
	+ 3x Annual Salary (Maximum coverage)
	+ Decline Supplemental Life
* **Short-Term Disability**: Select your preferred level of short-term disability coverage:
	+ 60% of salary (Standard)
	+ 70% of salary (Enhanced)
	+ Decline Coverage
* **Voluntary Benefits**: Select any voluntary benefits you want to enroll in (select all that apply):
	+ Accident Insurance
	+ Critical Illness Coverage
	+ Hospital Indemnity
	+ Legal Services Plan
	+ Identity Theft Protection
* **Retirement Savings**: This section is for selecting your retirement savings options.
* **Contribution Percentage**: Enter the percentage of your salary to contribute to your retirement savings.
* **Contribution Tax Treatment**: Select how you want your contributions to be taxed:
	+ Traditional Pre-Tax
	+ Roth After-Tax
	+ Split 50/50 Between Both
* **Beneficiary & Final Details**: This section is for selecting your primary beneficiary.
* **Primary Beneficiary Full Name**: Enter the full name of your primary beneficiary.
* **Relationship to Beneficiary**: Enter your relationship to your primary beneficiary.
* **Tobacco Use Status**: Select your tobacco use status:
	+ Non-Tobacco User (No use in past 6 months)
	+ Tobacco User
* **Commuter Benefits Election**: Select your commuter benefits preferences:
	+ Monthly Parking Pass
	+ Public Transit Pass
	+ Both Parking and Transit
	+ No Commuter Benefits
* **Special Enrollment Qualifying Events**: Enter any special qualifying events that may affect your benefits.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign with your full legal name to confirm that the selections are accurate.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
* The information collected will be used to process your benefits and ensure accurate enrollment.
