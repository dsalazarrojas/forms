# Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form is a tool used to request membership in the Academic Peer Network. The network is a platform for academics to collaborate, share knowledge, and discuss relevant topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Network Participation Information (optional)**: This is a space for you to briefly introduce yourself and your interest in joining the network.
2. Fill in your **Full Name** in the format you use professionally.
3. Enter your **Contact Email**, which can be an institutional or preferred email address.
4. Select your **Role in Academia** from the options provided: Student, Faculty, Staff, Researcher, or Other.
5. Choose your **Areas of Interest** by selecting one or more options from the list: Teaching practice, Research collaboration, Curriculum design, Mentorship, or Policy and administration.
6. Write a brief description of your **Goals for Joining the Network**, explaining what you hope to achieve.
7. Indicate your **Availability for Meetings** by describing typical days or times you are available.
8. Choose whether you **Consent to Share Your Profile** with other network members.
9. Finally, include any **Additional Information** you feel would be helpful for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Network Participation --**: This field is optional and serves as a space for you to introduce yourself and your interests in joining the network. You can write a brief paragraph describing your academic goals and interests.
* **Full Name**: Enter the name you use professionally for work and academic purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your institutional or preferred email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Role in Academia**: Choose the role that best describes your current position in academia. This will help the network understand your perspective and expertise.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select all the areas relevant to your academic interests and goals from the options provided. These options represent the main categories of activities within the network.
* **Goals for Joining the Network**: Write a brief description of what you hope to achieve by joining the network. This could be professional development, finding collaborators, or staying up-to-date with industry developments.
* **Availability for Meetings**: Describe the days and times when you are usually available for meetings or collaborations.
* **Consent to Share Your Profile**: Choose whether you agree to share your contact information with other network members for potential collaborations and communication.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional relevant details you want to share with the network, please include them here.
