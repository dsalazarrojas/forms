# Dog Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about dog training sessions. It's meant for dog owners or guardians to provide details about their dog's behavior, training progress, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide information about behavior modifications, if any.
2. Enter the date and time of the last training session.
3. Choose the assigned trainer for the session.
4. Select the duration of the session.
5. Note any additional session notes.
6. Provide the contact information of the dog's owner or guardian.
7. Describe the owner's relationship with the dog.
8. Specify the dog's type (size: small/big).
9. Enter details about any exercises performed during the session.
10. Indicate the duration of the exercise.
11. Choose the dog's food type (kibble/raw).
12. Select the training area where the session took place (house/yard).
13. List the dog's owner(s).
14. Choose the training protocol (standard/modified).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dog-training-form-1** (`dog-training-form-1`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the dog's behavior modifications.
* **behavior-modifications** (`behavior-modifications`, note, optional): Add any notes about the dog's behavior.
* **training-sessions** (`training-sessions`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the last training session.
* **assigned-trainer** (`assigned-trainer`, select_one, optional): Choose the assigned trainer for the session.
* **session-duration** (`session-duration`, time, optional): Select the duration of the session.
* **session-notes** (`session-notes`, note, optional): Add any additional session notes.
* **owner-contact** (`owner-contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of the dog's owner or guardian.
* **owner-relationship** (`owner-relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the owner's relationship with the dog (good, bad).
* **dog-type** (`dog-type`, select_one, optional): Specify the dog's type (small/big).
* **exercise** (`exercise`, text, optional): Enter details about any exercises performed during the session.
* **exercise-duration** (`exercise-duration`, number, optional): Indicate the duration of the exercise.
* **food** (`food`, select_one, optional): Choose the dog's food type (kibble/raw).
* **training-area** (`training-area`, select_multiple, optional): Select the training area where the session took place (house/yard).
* **dog-owner** (`dog-owner`, select_multiple, optional): List the dog's owner(s).
* **training-protocol** (`training-protocol`, select_one, optional): Choose the training protocol (standard/modified).
