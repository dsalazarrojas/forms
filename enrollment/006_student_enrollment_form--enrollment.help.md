# Student Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new students who are enrolling in the educational institution. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the student's information is correctly recorded in the institution's database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the fee you wish to pay in the "Fee" field.
5. Select your preferred fee payment method from the options provided in the "Fee Payment Method" field.
6. If you have a non-standard fee payment method, you can enter it in the "Other Fee Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (`Name`, text, required)**: This field is used to enter your name.
* **Email (`Email`, email, required)**: This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth (`Date of Birth`, date, required)**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Fee (`Fee`, number, required)**: Enter the fee you wish to pay.
* **Fee Payment Method (`Fee Payment Method`, select_one, required)**: Select one of the provided options for your preferred payment method.
* **Other Fee Payment Method (`Other Fee Payment Method`, text, optional)**: If you have a non-standard fee payment method, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with your submission, contact the institution's administration for assistance.
