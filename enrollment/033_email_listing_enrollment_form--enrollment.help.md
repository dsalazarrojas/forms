# Email Listing Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Listing Enrollment Form is designed to collect information for creating or updating email listings. This form helps ensure that the email listings are accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you want to create or update an email listing.
2. Select the relevant options for "Email Listings", "Email Listings 2", and other fields as needed.
3. Enter your first and last name, if applicable.
4. Enter your company name, if applicable.
5. Select your title and industry, if applicable.
6. Enter your department and job title information, if applicable.
7. Enter a valid email address for the email listing.
8. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email Listings Form** (`email_listings_form`, number, optional): This field indicates that you want to create or update email listings for this form. If you select "Yes", the form will proceed to the next page.
* **Email Listings** (`email_listings`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have email listings to submit. If you don't have email listings, select "No".
* **Email Listings 2** (`email_listings_2`, select_one, optional): This is similar to the "Email Listings" field, with a single selection option.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if applicable.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if applicable.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Title** (`title`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a title. If you don't have a title, select "No".
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have an industry. If you don't have an industry, select "No".
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your secondary job title, if applicable.
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, text, optional): Enter your tertiary job title, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the email listing.

Note: The form is designed to collect information for creating or updating email listings. It is essential to ensure that the email listings are accurate and complete. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department or person for clarification.
