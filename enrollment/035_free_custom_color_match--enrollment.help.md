# free_custom_color_match - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "free_custom_color_match" form is designed to collect user information to match them with free tools or resources based on their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input** field with your name or any other relevant identifier.
2. Select your preferred **color match** from the options provided.
3. Enter your **date of birth** in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Enter your **time of birth** in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Provide your **email address** in the format [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com).
6. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field.
8. Indicate your agreement to enrollment terms in the **Enrollment Agreement** field.
9. Choose your current **enrollment status** (Active, Pending, or Cancelled).
10. Select the **Assigned Tool** that you're interested in.
11. Answer **Yes** or **No** to the free_form_field_1 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any name or identifier that you'd like to be associated with your input.
* **color match** (`color_match`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more color options that you prefer or match with.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **time_of_birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address in the format [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your input.
* **Enrollment Agreement** (`enrollment_agreement`, text, optional): Indicate your agreement to enrollment terms.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current enrollment status (Active, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Select the tool you've been assigned or are interested in.
* **free_form_field_1** (`free_form_field_1`, select_one, optional): Answer **Yes** or **No** to a field with no specific label or purpose.
