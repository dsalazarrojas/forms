# Family Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a family camper for a camp session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
3. Submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical information about the camper, such as allergies, medical conditions, or previous injuries.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Camper Activity** (`camper_activity`, text, required/optional): Describe the camper's interests or activities that they would like to participate in during the camp session.
* **Questions/Comments** (`questions_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the camper's participation in the camp session.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure the camper's safety and well-being.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the camp administration for clarification.
* Only submit the form once you have finished filling it out to avoid duplication.
