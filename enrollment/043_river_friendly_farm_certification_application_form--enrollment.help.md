# River Friendly Farm Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The River Friendly Farm Certification Application Form is used to gather information from farmers about their farms and practices, with the goal of promoting sustainable and responsible practices in riverine areas.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the relevant fields.
2. Provide information about your farm's general information.
3. Select your farm's type (Organic, Conventional, Specialty, or Other).
4. Choose the crops you grow on your farm (select multiple if applicable).
5. Indicate if your farm uses an irrigation system (True, False, or Hybrid).
6. Provide information about your water source.
7. Select your certification status (Certified, Not Certified).
8. Enter your certification date, if applicable.
9. Enter your certification expiration date, if applicable.
10. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
11. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (General Info, text, required): Enter a brief description of your farm.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as it would appear on official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Land Area** (Land Area, number, required): Enter the size of your farm in acres.
* **Farm Type** (Farm Type, select_one, required): Select the type of farm you have (Organic, Conventional, Specialty, or Other).
* **Crop List** (Crop List, select_multiple, required): Select the crops you grow on your farm (Wheat, Barley, Oats, Rye, Corn, Soybean, Sorghum, Cotton).
* **Irrigation System** (Irrigation System, select_one, required): Select if you use an irrigation system (True, False, or Hybrid).
* **Well Depth** (Well Depth, number, required): Enter the depth of your well in meters.
* **Water Source** (Water Source, text, required): Enter the source of your water.
* **Certification Status** (Certification Status, select_one, required): Select your current certification status (Certified, Not Certified).
* **Certification Date** (Certification Date, date, required): Enter the date of your certification, if applicable.
* **Expiration Date** (Expiration Date, date, required): Enter the date of your certification expiration, if applicable.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes.
* **Submit Note** (Submit Note, note, optional): Add any additional information before submitting your application.
* **Submit Button** (Submit Button, text, optional): This is a dummy field, please do not fill it.
* **Submit Email** (Submit Email, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about your application.
* **Submit Phone** (Submit Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive updates about your application.
* **Review Note** (Review Note, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback for the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the review, if applicable.
* **Review Comment** (Review Comment, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about your farm's certification status.

Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
