# Enrolment Form PTQ - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an enrolment or training session as part of the PTQ process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose a training option from the "Choose a training" dropdown menu.
2. Select courses for the participant from the "Select courses" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date of enrollment in the "Date of enrollment" field.
4. Enter the start date of the training in the "Start date" field.
5. Enter the duration of the training in the "Duration of the training" field.
6. Enter the number of hours of training in the "Hours of training" field.
7. Enter the name of the training facility in the "Training facility" field.
8. Enter the location of the training in the "Location of training" field.
9. Enter the time of the training in the "Time of training" field.
10. Add any additional notes for the enrolment in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter the email of the participant in the "Email of the participant" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the participant in the "Phone of the participant" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a training**: Select the type of training the participant will be enrolled in from the dropdown menu. This will help us determine the type of training required.
* **Select courses**: Choose the courses the participant will be enrolling in from the dropdown menu. This will ensure that the correct courses are selected.
* **Date of enrollment**: Enter the date the participant is enrolling in the training. This is an important date for our records.
* **Start date**: Enter the start date of the training. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.
* **Duration of the training**: Enter the number of days or weeks the training will last. This will help us plan the training schedule.
* **Hours of training**: Enter the number of hours of training the participant will have. This will help us track the training hours.
* **Training facility**: Enter the name of the training facility where the participant will be trained. This will help us locate the training facility.
* **Location of training**: Enter the location of the training, e.g., city, state, or country. This will help us locate the training location.
* **Time of training**: Enter the time of the training, e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening. This will help us schedule the training time.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the enrolment. This will help us understand the participant's requirements.
* **Email of the participant**: Enter the email of the participant. This will help us contact the participant with updates and information.
* **Phone of the participant**: Enter the phone number of the participant. This will help us contact the participant with updates and information.
