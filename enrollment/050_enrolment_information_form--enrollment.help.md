# Enrolment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Enrolment Information Form is to provide the necessary information for educational institutions to process your admission or registration. This form is designed to collect essential details about you or the individual you are enrolling.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure all the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
3. Submit the form once all fields are complete.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (school_name, text, Not required): Enter the name of your current or desired school.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, Not required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the individual's emergency contact.
* **Email** (email, email, Not required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, Not required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, Not required): Enter the individual's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
