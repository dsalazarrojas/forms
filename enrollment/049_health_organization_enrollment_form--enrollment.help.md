# Health Organization Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for enrolling health organizations into the system. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to create a new enrollment before proceeding. If you are unsure, please refer to your internal documentation or contact your supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the health organization.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Submit the form once you have verified the accuracy of the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Organization Name** (organization_name)
Organization Name: <input> Enter the full name of the health organization.
### 2. **Organization Address** (organization_address)
Organization Address: <input> Enter the address of the health organization.
### 3. **Contact Person** (contact_person)
Contact Person: <input> Enter the name of the contact person for the health organization.
### 4. **Email** (email)
Email: <input> Enter the email address of the contact person. This field must be in the format `example@domain.com`.
### 5. **Phone Number** (phone_number)
Phone Number: <input> Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### 6. **Organization Type** (organization_type)
Organization Type: <input> Choose "Health Organization" if the organization is a health-related organization, otherwise select "Other".
### 7. **Organization License** (organization_license)
Organization License: <input> Enter the license number for the health organization (if applicable).
### 8. **Tax ID Number** (tax_id_number)
Tax ID Number: <input> Enter the tax ID number for the health organization (if applicable).
### 9. **Enrollment Date** (enrollment_date)
Enrollment Date: <input> Enter the date the health organization was enrolled into the system.
### 10. **Enrollment Status** (enrollment_status)
Enrollment Status: <input> Select "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending" to indicate the status of the health organization's enrollment.
### 11. **Notes** (notes)
Notes: <input> Enter any additional comments or notes for the health organization's enrollment.
### 12. **Submit** (submit)
Submit: <button> Review and submit the form once you have verified the accuracy of the information.
### 13. **Back** (back)
Back: <button> Return to previous page.

Please note that the form can be submitted multiple times, but each submission will overwrite the previous one. If you need to make changes, please use the back button to return to the previous page.
