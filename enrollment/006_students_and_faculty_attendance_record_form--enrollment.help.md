# Students And Faculty Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the attendance of students and faculty members, including their attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, or Excused) and any notes or reasons for their absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Name field with the name of the student or faculty member being recorded.
2. Enter the Email field with the contact information of the student or faculty member.
3. Select the Role field with the correct category ("Student" or "Faculty") for the individual.
4. Enter the Date field with the date of the attendance being recorded.
5. Enter the Time field with the time of attendance or absence.
6. Select the Attendance field to indicate the individual's attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, or Excused).
7. If necessary, add a Note field to provide any additional information about the individual's absence or attendance.
8. Select the Assigned Tool field to indicate any tool or resource assigned to the individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the name of the student or faculty member being recorded.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the contact information of the student or faculty member.
* **Role (role)**: Select "Student" or "Faculty" to indicate the category of the individual.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the attendance being recorded.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of attendance or absence.
* **Attendance (attendance)**: Select the attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, or Excused) of the individual.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional information about the individual's absence or attendance.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Select the tool or resource assigned to the individual.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and date.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
