# social_media_online_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll a user in an online course on social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Select your date of birth.
4. Choose the social media platforms on which you would like to be enrolled in the course.
5. Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
6. Select the start and end dates of the course.
7. Choose the status of your enrollment (Active or Inactive).
8. Enter a note (optional).
9. Assign a tool for the course (e.g., "Social Media Poster").
10. Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
11. Enter the form ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Select your date of birth.
* **Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more social media platforms on which you would like to be enrolled in the course.
	+ Select one or more from: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of the course.
* **Status** (`enrollment_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your enrollment (Active or Inactive).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional note or comment about your enrollment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Assign a tool for the course (e.g., "Social Media Poster").
* **Assigned Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required): Enter the form ID for the course enrollment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your course administrator.
