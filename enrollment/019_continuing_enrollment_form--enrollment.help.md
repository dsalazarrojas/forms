# Continuing Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Continuing Enrollment Form is designed to help students enroll in courses for the current semester. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (Student Full Name, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your university records.
* **Student ID Number** (Student ID Number, text, **Required**): Enter your university-issued student ID number.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, **Required**): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **Required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Major** (Major, text, **Required**): Enter your primary field of study.
* **Minor** (Minor, text, **Optional**): Enter your secondary field of study, if applicable.
* **Academic Year** (Academic Year, select_one, **Required**): Select your current year level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Graduate).
* **Semester Enrolling** (Semester Enrolling, select_one, **Required**): Select the semester you are enrolling in (Spring 2026, Summer 2026, Fall 2026).
* **Enrollment Status** (Enrollment Status, select_one, **Required**): Select your enrollment status (Full-time, Part-time).
* **Credit Hours** (Credit Hours, select_one, **Required**): Select the number of credits you are enrolling for (1-5 credits, 6-9 credits, 10-12 credits, 13-15 credits, 16+ credits).
* **Course Selection 1** (Course Selection 1, text, **Required**): Enter your first choice course.
* **Course Selection 2** (Course Selection 2, text, **Required**): Enter your second choice course.
* **Course Selection 3** (Course Selection 3, text, **Optional**): Enter your third choice course, if applicable.
* **Course Selection 4** (Course Selection 4, text, **Optional**): Enter your fourth choice course, if applicable.
* **Course Selection 5** (Course Selection 5, text, **Optional**): Enter your fifth choice course, if applicable.
* **Current GPA** (Current GPA, number, **Optional**): Enter your current GPA, if applicable.
* **Academic Standing** (Academic Standing, select_one, **Required**): Select your current academic standing (Good standing, Dean's list, Honor roll, Probation, Other).
* **Financial Aid Status** (Financial Aid Status, select_one, **Required**): Select your financial aid status (Receiving aid, Applying for aid, Not receiving aid).
* **Tuition Payment Plan** (Tuition Payment Plan, select_one, **Required**): Select your tuition payment plan (Pay in full, Monthly installments, Financial aid, Scholarship, Loan).
* **Advisor Name** (Advisor Name, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of your academic advisor, if applicable.
* **Special Circumstances** (Special Circumstances, text, **Optional**): Enter any special circumstances affecting your enrollment, if applicable.
* **Terms Agreement** (Terms Agreement, select_one, **Required**): Select whether you agree to the enrollment terms (I agree, I do not agree).
