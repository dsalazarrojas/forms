# Windchill And SAP Integration LIVE DEMO With WEBridge - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to enroll in a Windchill and SAP integration live demo with WEBridge. It is intended for users who want to participate in the demo and have their integration set up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Yes" option for each field that applies to you.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to review your submission carefully before confirming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **enrollment_form** (`enrollment_form`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you want to enroll in the demo and have your integration set up.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **sap_username** (`sap_username`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP username.
* **sap_password** (`sap_password`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP password.
* **webbridge_url** (`webbridge_url`, `text`, not required): Enter the URL of your WEBridge installation.
* **sap_server** (`sap_server`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP server name or IP address.
* **sap_client** (`sap_client`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP client name.
* **sap_system** (`sap_system`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP system name.
* **sap_group** (`sap_group`, `text`, required): Enter your SAP system group name.
* **windchill_version** (`windchill_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of Windchill you are using.
* **sap_version** (`sap_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of SAP you are using.
* **integration_type** (`integration_type`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have completed the integration setup.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have assigned the tool for the integration.
* **submitted** (`submitted`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have submitted your data for integration.
* **submitter** (`submitter`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have submitted your data on behalf of someone else.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have submitted your data on behalf of someone else.
* **submitted_to** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have submitted your data to someone else.
* **submission_date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **submission_time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_zone** (`submission_time_zone`, `time`, required): Enter the time zone of your submission.
* **submission_time_offset** (`submission_time_offset`, `number`, not required): Enter the offset of your submission time in minutes.
* **submission_date_offset** (`submission_date_offset`, `number`, not required): Enter the offset of your submission date in days.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required", which means that you must fill out this field. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant team for guidance.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the "Windchill And SAP Integration LIVE DEMO With WEBridge" form accurately and submit your data for integration purposes.
