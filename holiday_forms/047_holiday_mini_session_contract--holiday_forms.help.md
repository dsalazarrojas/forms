**Holiday Mini Session Contract - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The "Holiday Mini Session Contract" form is designed to capture essential information from clients regarding their session preferences and agreements. This form is intended for photographers and similar professionals who offer mini sessions during holidays or special occasions. By completing this form, clients will be able to specify their session details, payment terms, and provide consent for the photographer's work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your client's information, including their full name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and address.
3. Specify the session details, such as the date and time of the session.
4. Select the session type (Mini Session, Full Session, or Extended Session).
5. Choose the payment terms (Payment 1, Payment 2, or Payment 3).
6. Confirm your agreement with the contract terms and conditions.
7. Select your consent for the session (Consent 1, Consent 2, or Consent 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (1): Enter your client's full name and contact information.
* **Full Name** (2): Enter your client's full name.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your client's phone number.
* **Address** (5): Enter your client's address.
* **Session Details** (6): Describe the session details, such as the occasion and the number of participants.
* **Date** (7): Select the date for the session (if already known).
* **Time** (8): Select the time for the session (if already known).
* **Session Length** (9): Choose the session duration (length).
* **Location** (10): Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Type** (11): Choose the type of session (Mini Session, Full Session, or Extended Session).
* **Payment Terms** (12): Choose the payment terms (Payment 1, Payment 2, or Payment 3).
* **Terms and Agreement** (13): Confirm your understanding of the contract terms and conditions.
* **Consent Form** (14): Select the consent options for the session (Consent 1, Consent 2, or Consent 3).
* **Contract Terms** (15): Read and confirm your understanding of the contract terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review and verify your information before proceeding with the session.
