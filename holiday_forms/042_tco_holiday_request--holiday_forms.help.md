# Tco Holiday Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tco Holiday Request form is used to submit a holiday request, which can be for work or a project deadline. This form helps you to provide necessary details about your holiday, including the dates and a description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the "Holiday Request" field, briefly state the holiday or the reason for your request.
2. In the "start_date" field, enter the date when your holiday is scheduled to start.
3. In the "end_date" field, enter the date when your holiday is scheduled to end.
4. In the "Description" field, provide any additional information about your holiday request, such as where you will be or the activities you will be doing during your holiday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Request (required: false)** (`holiday_request`, text): A field to state your holiday or the reason for your request.
* **start_date (required: false)** (`start_date`, date): Enter the date when your holiday is scheduled to start.
* **end_date (required: false)** (`end_date`, date): Enter the date when your holiday is scheduled to end.
* **Description (required: false)** (`description`, text): Provide any additional information about your holiday request. 

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when providing dates and descriptions.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
