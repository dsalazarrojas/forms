# Holiday Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holiday Request Form is designed to allow employees to submit requests for holiday time off. This form is meant to be a straightforward and easy-to-use tool for employees to communicate their holiday plans with their managers or HR department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your holiday period in the "Start date" and "End date" fields, respectively.
3. Once you've filled in all the required and optional fields, review your submission before submitting it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee name (`employee_name`): 
Enter your name as it appears on your employee records.

### Start date (`start_date`): 
Select the start date of your holiday period using the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).

### End date (`end_date`): 
Select the end date of your holiday period using the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-08).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the start and end dates before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
* This form is designed to be easy to use, so take your time and fill it in carefully!
