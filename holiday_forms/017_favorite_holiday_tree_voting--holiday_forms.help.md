# favorite_holiday_tree_voting - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "favorite_holiday_tree_voting" form is used to collect users' favorite holiday traditions or activities in a fun and engaging way. It allows users to submit their preferences through a simple and easy-to-use interface.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Confirm your eligibility to vote by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "eligibility_confirmation" field.
4. Add any comments or notes about your favorite holiday tradition in the "comments" field.
5. Complete the captcha field to verify your identity (this is for security purposes).
6. Optionally, you can enter a GA ID and/or UTM ID in the "ga_id" and "utm_id" fields for additional tracking purposes.
7. Click the "submit" button to submit your preferences.
8. If you change your mind, you can click the "cancel" button to start over.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (text): Enter your name to identify yourself in the form.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address to contact you if needed.
* **eligibility_confirmation** (select_one): Confirm your eligibility to vote by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **comments** (text): Add any comments or notes about your favorite holiday tradition.
* **captcha** (text): Complete the captcha field to verify your identity (security purposes).
* **captcha** (text): This is a duplicate of the captcha field, likely a mistake in the form design. Please ignore this field.
* **_ga_id** (text): Enter your Google Analytics ID for tracking purposes (optional).
* **_utm_id** (text): Enter your UTM ID for tracking purposes (optional).
* **submit** (text): Click this button to submit your preferences.
* **cancel** (text): Click this button to start over if you need to make changes.
