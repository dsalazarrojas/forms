# Mother's Day Tea Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mother's Day Tea Invitation form is designed to collect information from guests about their attendance and any special requests or messages for the hostess.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please start by completing the first page, which contains a brief description of the event.
- On the second page, enter the guest's name, email, and phone number.
- On the third page, select the RSVP option to indicate whether the guest can attend.
- On subsequent pages, enter any additional details or message for the hostess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (first_page)**: Enter a brief description of the event, such as "Join us for Mother's Day Tea".
* **Guest Details (guest_details)**: Enter the guest's name, email, and phone number.
* **RSVP (rsvp)**: Select "RSVP 1" to indicate that the guest will attend, "RSVP 2" for unable to attend, or leave a message if there's a reason for not attending.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the event in the desired format (e.g., "2024-05-12").
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., "14:00").
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the guest's email address.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional information or message for the hostess.
* **Additional Details (first_page_section_2)**: Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Last Page (last_page)**: Enter any final comments or messages for the hostess.
* **Guest Name (guest_name)**: Enter the guest's name.
* **Guest Email (guest_email)**: Enter the guest's email address.
* **Guest Phone (guest_phone)**: Enter the guest's phone number.
* **RSVP Message (rsvp_message)**: Enter any message or reason for the guest's RSVP response.
