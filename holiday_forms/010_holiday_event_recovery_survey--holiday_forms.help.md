# Holiday Event Recovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and information from attendees about their experience with a holiday event. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have attended the holiday event and have not filled out this survey before.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Enter your email address and name as it appears on your contact information.
4. Enter your contact number, if available.
5. Answer the attendee feedback and future interest questions.
6. Select your overall well-being status.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
8. Review and submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Feedback** (1, text, required): This field is a text box where you can provide feedback about your experience with the holiday event. Please be as detailed and honest as possible to help us understand what we can improve.
* **Future Interest** (2, select_one, required): This field asks whether you would be interested in attending a similar event in the future. Please select 'Yes' if you would like to attend another event and 'No' otherwise.
* **Overall Well-being** (3, select_multiple, required): This field asks about your overall well-being status during the event. Please select all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Event Details** (4, note, optional): This field is a free text box where you can provide any additional information about the event that you think is relevant to your feedback.
* **Program Interest** (5, select_one, required): This field asks whether you were interested in the programs or activities offered at the event. Please select 'Yes' if you were interested and 'No' otherwise.
* **Comments** (6, text, optional): This field is a free text box where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Email** (7, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears in your contact information.
* **Name** (8, text, required): Enter your name as it appears in your contact information.
* **Contact Number** (9, text, required): If you have a contact number, please enter it as it appears in your contact information.
