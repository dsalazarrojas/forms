# Holiday Open House RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you fill out the Holiday Open House RSVP form. Please make sure to read this guide carefully before filling out the form to ensure you're providing all necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Holiday Open House RSVP form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Specify the number of guests you will be attending with in the Guest Count field.
6. Choose whether you will attend the holiday event by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will Attend field.
7. Select a date for the RSVP By field, which is the deadline for your RSVP.
8. Choose whether you will attend the holiday dinner by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Attend Holiday Dinner field.
9. Select your holiday dinner preferences from the list of options.
10. Choose whether you will attend the holiday party by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Attend Holiday Party field.
11. Select your holiday party preferences from the list of options.
12. Choose whether you will attend the New Year's dinner by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Attend New Years Dinner field.
13. Choose whether you will attend the New Year's brunch by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Attend New Years Brunch field.
14. Choose whether you will host the holiday event by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Host field.
15. Choose whether you will help host the holiday event by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Help Host field.
16. Choose whether you will volunteer for the holiday event by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Volunteer field.
17. Choose whether you will work at the host by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the Will You Work at the Host field.
18. If you have any special requests or comments, enter them in the Special Request field.
