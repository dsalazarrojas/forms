# Claim Your Holiday Coupon - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to claim their holiday coupons, including their name, email address, and preferred shipping method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Claimant name" field. This is a required field to ensure we have accurate contact information for you.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used for coupon delivery and any marketing materials.
3. Choose your preferred shipping method in the "Shipping option" field. Select from Email, Postal mail, or In store pickup.
4. Confirm that you are eligible to claim the coupon by selecting "I confirm" in the "Eligibility confirmation" field.
5. If you have a promo code, enter it in the "Promo code" field. This is an optional field.
6. If you have any additional notes or instructions, enter them in the "Additional notes" field.
7. Select the type of coupon you would like to claim from the "Coupon selection" field.
8. Finally, submit the form using the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant name**: This is the name of the person claiming the holiday coupon. Please enter your full name.
* **Email address**: This is the email address we will use to deliver the coupon to you. Please enter your valid email address.
* **Coupon selection**: Choose the type of coupon you would like to claim. Options include 10 percent, 20 percent, or Free shipping.
* **Promo code**: If you have a promo code, enter it here. This is an optional field.
* **Shipping option**: Choose how you would like to receive your coupon. Options include Email, Postal mail, or In store pickup.
* **Eligibility confirmation**: Confirm that you are eligible to claim the coupon by selecting "I confirm".
* **Consent to receive marketing**: Select "True" to consent to receive marketing materials from us. This is an optional field.
* **Redemption instructions note**: This is a note for you to provide any instructions on how to redeem the coupon.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions you have for your coupon.
* **Submission date**: This field will automatically display the current date. However, please note that you cannot change this field.

Note: This help guide is for informational purposes and is subject to change as the form evolves.
