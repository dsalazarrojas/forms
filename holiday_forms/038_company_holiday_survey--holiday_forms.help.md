# Company Holiday Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Holiday Survey is designed to gather information from employees about their holiday preferences and availability. This will help us plan events that cater to everyone's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Employee name**: This is your name that will be associated with your responses.
2. Choose your **Preferred holiday event type**: Select one of the three options: In person gathering, Virtual celebration, or Hybrid event.
3. Specify your **Preferred date range**: Enter the dates that work best for you.
4. **Dietary restrictions**: If you have any food allergies or preferences, please specify them here.
5. **Interest in gift exchange**: Choose whether you'd like to participate in a gift exchange (True, False, or Maybe).
6. **Volunteer to help organize**: Indicate whether you'd like to help with planning or logistics (True, False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official employee records.
* **Preferred holiday event type**: This determines the type of event you'd like to participate in. Choose one of the three options:
	+ **In person gathering**: A physical event where everyone attends in person.
	+ **Virtual celebration**: A virtual event where everyone attends online.
	+ **Hybrid event**: A mix of both in-person and virtual events.
* **Preferred date range**: Enter the dates that work best for you to attend the event. Please format dates in the usual MM/DD-YYYY format (e.g., 01/15-01/17).
* **Dietary restrictions**: If you have any food allergies or preferences, specify them here. This will help us plan the event menu.
* **Interest in gift exchange**: Check "True" if you'd like to participate in a gift exchange, "False" if you don't, or "Maybe" if you're undecided.
* **Volunteer to help organize**: Check "True" if you'd like to help with event planning or logistics, "False" if you don't, or "Maybe" if you're willing to consider it.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your responses are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the event organizers.
