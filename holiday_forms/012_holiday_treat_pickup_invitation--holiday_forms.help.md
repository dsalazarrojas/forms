# Holiday Treat Pickup Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you express your interest in bringing a holiday treat to a celebration and specify the type of treat you'll bring.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your interest in bringing a holiday treat by checking the box for "Holiday Treat Type" that corresponds to the type of treat you'll bring (e.g., Cupcakes, Cookies, etc.). 
2. If you're interested in bringing a holiday treat, fill out the "Name" field to provide your name.
3. Choose a date to RSVP (respond to the invitation) in the "RSVP" field.
4. Specify the "Pickup Time" for your holiday treat.
5. Enter your contact information (e.g., email and phone number) in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
6. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Holiday Treat Form Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Treat Type** (`holiday_treat_type`, select multiple): Select all the types of holiday treats you're interested in bringing, such as Cupcakes, Cookies, Cake, or Sweets.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name so we can know who's bringing what type of holiday treat.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, date): Choose a date to RSVP (respond to the invitation) for the holiday celebration.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time): Specify the time when you'd like to make your holiday treat available for pickup.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Holiday Treat Form Comment** (`holiday_treat_form_comment`, text): Add any additional comments or messages to the holiday treat form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all types of holiday treats you're interested in bringing.
* Be specific with your name and contact information.
* Choose a date to RSVP to let us know you're coming.
* Specify a pickup time to avoid conflicts.
