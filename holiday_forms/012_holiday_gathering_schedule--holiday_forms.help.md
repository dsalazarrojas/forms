# Holiday Gathering Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect and record information about a holiday gathering event. It is intended for the person or team organizing the event to gather all necessary details before sharing them with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the host's name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide the start and end dates of the holiday gathering.
3. Add any details about the potluck.
4. Enter the number of guests invited and their RSVP status.
5. Select the RSVP deadline.
6. Add any additional notes about the event.
7. Fill in the holiday name and date if applicable.
8. Specify the location of the event.
9. Provide the location of the potluck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hosts Name** (`host_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team organizing the event.
* **Hosts Email** (`host_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or team organizing the event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the holiday gathering.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the holiday gathering.
* **Potluck Details** (`potluck_details`, text, optional): Add any details about the potluck, such as food to be brought or potluck themes.
* **Guests Invited** (`guests_invited`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests invited to the event.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate if guests have RSVP'd or not.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Holiday Name** (`holiday_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the holiday or occasion being celebrated.
* **Holiday Date** (`holiday_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the holiday or occasion.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Potluck Location** (`potluck_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the potluck.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_by`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for RSVPs to be made.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the event has an RSVP status.
* **Maybe** (`maybe`, select_one, optional): A possible option for RSVP status, indicating that a guest is not sure if they will attend.
* **True** (`true`, select_one, optional): Indicate that a guest has RSVP'd yes.
* **False** (`false`, select_one, optional): Indicate that a guest has RSVP'd no.
