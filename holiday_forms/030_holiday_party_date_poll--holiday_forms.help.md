# Holiday Party Date Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about team members' availability on holiday party dates. It helps in planning and deciding on a date that works best for the majority of team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on "Choose Date" and select one or multiple dates from the given options.
2. If you have no preference, select "No" to indicate that you don't want to participate in the holiday party.
3. If you're unsure about your availability, select "Maybe" under "Attendance".
4. Add any comments or suggestions you might have about the holiday party under "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Choose Date** (`holiday_party_date_poll_form`): Select one or multiple dates that you are available to attend the holiday party. This will help determine the date that works best for the majority of team members.
* **Date** (`holiday_party_date_poll_form_date`): Choose a date that you are available to attend the holiday party. This field is only required if you select "Yes" under "Choose Date".
* **Attendance** (`holiday_party_date_poll_form_attendance`): Select "Available" if you can attend the holiday party, "Not Available" if you cannot attend, or "Maybe" if you're unsure about your availability.
* **Comments** (`holiday_party_date_poll_form_comments`): Add any comments or suggestions you might have about the holiday party. This is an optional field where you can provide more information about your preferences.

## Tips

* Be honest about your availability to help in planning the holiday party.
* If you have no preference, select "No" under "Choose Date" and "Not Available" under "Attendance".
* Use the "Comments" field to provide any additional information that might be helpful in planning the event.
