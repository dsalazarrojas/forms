# Holiday Party Food Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Party Food Preferences" form is designed to gather information from guests about their food preferences and restrictions for a holiday party. This information will help the party planners or hosts to plan food arrangements that cater to everyone's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Attending** status by choosing either "Attending" or "Not Attending".
2. Choose your dietary **Dietary Restrictions** by selecting any options that apply to you (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free).
3. Select any **Allergies** you may have (Peanuts, Shellfish, Soy).
4. Choose your **Food Preferences** by selecting any cuisines you enjoy (Mexican, Italian, Chinese).
5. Use the **Additional Notes** field to provide any other information you think is relevant.
6. Enter your **RSVP Email** and **RSVP Phone** for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Preferences**: This field allows you to provide your food preferences, such as any specific cuisine or dish you enjoy.
* **RSVP**: Select this option to indicate whether you will be attending or not. This helps us know how many guests are coming.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Check any of the dietary restrictions that apply to you. This will help us plan the food arrangements.
* **Allergies**: Check any allergies you may have. This will help us avoid any food that may cause an allergic reaction.
* **Cuisine Preferences**: Select any cuisines you enjoy. This will help us get a sense of your food preferences.
* **Additional Notes**: Use this field to provide any other information you think is relevant.
* **RSVP Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **RSVP Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
