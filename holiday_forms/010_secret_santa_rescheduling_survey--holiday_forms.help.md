# Secret Santa Rescheduling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Rescheduling Survey is a form designed to gather information from participants about their schedule and preferences for the Secret Santa event. This information helps the organizers plan the event efficiently and ensure that everyone's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Participant Name.
2.  Enter your Participant Email.
3.  Select your availability for each day of the week.
4.  Choose your preferred date and time for the event.
5.  Specify the Event Format you prefer (In-person or Virtual).
6.  Enter any additional details or preferences in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is the field where you should enter your name as a participant. It's essential to enter the name exactly as you want it to be displayed in the Secret Santa list.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): This is where you select your availability for each day of the week. Please choose the days you're available for the Secret Santa event.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to help the organizers communicate with you about the event.
* **Will you be available on Monday?** (`availability_0`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Monday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Tuesday?** (`availability_1`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Tuesday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Wednesday?** (`availability_2`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Wednesday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Thursday?** (`availability_3`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Thursday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Friday?** (`availability_4`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Friday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Saturday?** (`availability_5`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Saturday (yes or no).
*   **Will you be available on Sunday?** (`availability_6`, select_one, required): Select the day you are available on Sunday (yes or no).
*   **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a specific date for the Secret Santa event.
*   **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Select a specific time for the event.
*   **Event Format** (`event_format`, select_one, required): Choose whether you prefer the event to be In-person or Virtual.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional details or preferences you have.
*   **Participant Organisation** (`participant_organisation`, text, required): Enter your work organisation.
*   **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, text, required): Enter your department.
*   **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, required): Enter your location.
*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes you have for the organizers.

Note: The required fields are marked as such in the form and are essential to complete the form accurately. Please ensure you fill out all the required fields.
