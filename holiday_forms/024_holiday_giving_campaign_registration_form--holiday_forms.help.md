# Holiday Giving Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register donors, capture gift details, and collect contact information for a holiday giving campaign. This campaign aims to support local charities and organizations during the holiday season. To avoid duplication, please ensure that you only submit one registration per donor or gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Donor Info** section with the necessary information about the donor, such as their name, email, and address.
2. In the **Gift Details** section, provide the gift description and details about the charity or organization the gift is being made to.
3. In the **Contact Info** section, enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. The **Organization Info** section is optional, but if you're registering on behalf of a charity or organization, please provide their name, email, and contact person's name.
5. Finally, add any additional **Notes** about the gift in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Info**
	+ Label: Donor Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Description: This section is for entering information about the donor, such as their name, address, and any other relevant details. Please be sure to include the donor's name as it should appear on any related materials or communications.
* **Gift Details**
	+ Label: Gift Details
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Description: Describe the gift you're offering, including any specific details about it, such as monetary value or a description of any items donated.
* **Contact Info**
	+ Label: Contact Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Description: Enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding this gift or any campaign updates.
* **Organization Info**
	+ Label: Organization Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Description: If you're representing an organization, enter their name and contact information so we can connect with them directly.
* **Notes**
	+ Label: Notes
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
	+ Description: Add any additional information or context about the gift, such as the occasion for the gift or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate processing of your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us and we'll be happy to help.
* You can submit this form multiple times, but please avoid duplication of registrations for the same donor or gift.
