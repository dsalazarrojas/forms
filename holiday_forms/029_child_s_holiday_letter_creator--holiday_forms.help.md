# Child's Holiday Letter Creator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Child's Holiday Letter Creator" form is a tool for kids to create a personalized holiday letter, sharing their thoughts, experiences, and wishes with a recipient. It's a fun and interactive way for them to reflect on their year and show appreciation for those around them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form instructions and ensure you understand the purpose and guidelines.
2. Complete the form with the help of a parent or guardian, if necessary.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form):
	* Letter Date
	* Child First Name
	* Child Last Name
	* Child Age
	* Child Grade Level
	* Parent or Guardian First Name
	* Parent or Guardian Last Name
	* Parent or Guardian Email
	* Holiday Occasion
	* Letter Recipient
	* Letter Greeting
	* Favorite Holiday Memory
	* Holiday Activities Enjoyed
	* Favorite Holiday Food
	* Holiday Wishes for Recipient
	* Letter Closing
	* Child Signature
	* Include a Drawing (if desired)
	* Letter Style Preference
4. Answer any additional questions, if relevant and suitable for your response.
5. Review your answers for completeness and suitability before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Letter Date** (`letter_date`, `date`, required): The date you are writing the letter, to be included in the letter's heading.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, `text`, required): The legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, `text`, required): The legal last name of the child.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, `number`, required): The current age of the child in years.
* **Child Grade Level** (`child_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): The current grade level of the child.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`, `text`, required): The legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`, `text`, required): The legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): The primary email address for contact.
* **Holiday Occasion** (`holiday_occasion`, `select_one`, required): The holiday or occasion for which the letter is being written.
* **Letter Recipient** (`letter_recipient`, `select_one`, required): The intended recipient of the letter.
* **Letter Greeting** (`letter_greeting`, `text`, required): The opening sentence or phrase to start the letter.
* **Favorite Holiday Memory** (`favorite_holiday_memory`, `text`, required): A memory or experience from the holiday.
* **Holiday Activities Enjoyed** (`holiday_activities`, `text`, required): Activities or events participated in during the holiday season.
* **Favorite Holiday Food** (`favorite_holiday_food`, `text`, required): The favorite food or treat from the holiday.
* **Holiday Wishes for Recipient** (`holiday_wishes`, `text`, required): Wishes or messages for the recipient.
* **Letter Closing** (`letter_closing`, `text`, required): The closing sentence or phrase to end the letter.
* **Child Signature** (`child_signature`, `text`, required): The child's name as their signature.
* **Include a Drawing** (`include_drawing`, `select_one`, required): Whether to include a drawing with the letter.
* **Letter Style Preference** (`letter_style`, `select_one`, required): The preferred style or theme for the letter.
* **Additional Message** (`additional_message`, `text`, optional): Any extra message or thought to include.
* **Favorite Subject** (`favorite_subject`, `text`, optional): The favorite subject in school (if applicable).
* **Hobbies and Interests** (`hobbies_and_interests`, `text`, required): Current hobbies or interests.
* **Goals for Next Year** (`goals_for_next_year`, `text`, optional): Goals or aspirations for the coming year.
* **Something New Learned** (`something_learned`, `text`, optional): Something new or interesting learned.
* **Favorite Gift Received** (`gifts_received`, `text`, optional): The favorite gift received during the holiday.
* **Gifts Given** (`gifts_given`, `text`, optional): Gifts given to others during the holiday.
* **Family Holiday Traditions** (`holiday_traditions`, `text`, required): Holiday traditions practiced by the family.
* **Include a Drawing** (`include_drawing`, `select_one`, required): Whether to include a drawing with the letter.
* **Additional Message** (`additional_message`, `text`, optional): Any extra message or thought to include.
