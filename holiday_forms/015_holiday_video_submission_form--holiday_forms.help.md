# holiday_video_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about holiday videos or content from users. It's meant to be used for submitting holiday-related media, such as videos, images, and audio files.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of your holiday video in the "holiday_video_entry" field.
2. If you have a video, click "upload_video" and select the video file from your device or computer.
3. Alternatively, if you have the URL of your holiday video, enter it in the "video_url" field.
4. Add a brief description of your holiday video or content in the "description" field.
5. Select the categories for your holiday video by checking or unchecking the "Yes" and "No" options under the "categories" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. If you have any additional information or context, you can provide it in the "phone" field.
8. If you have an image related to your holiday video, click "image" and select the image file from your device or computer.
9. Alternatively, if you have the URL of your holiday image, enter it in the "image_url" field.
10. If you have an audio file related to your holiday video, click "audio_file" and select the audio file from your device or computer.
11. Alternatively, if you have the URL of your audio file, enter it in the "audio_url" field.
12. For videos with a specific length, enter the length in the "video_length" field.
13. For videos with a maximum length, enter the maximum length in the "video_length_max" field.
14. Select the tags for your holiday video by checking or unchecking the "Yes" and "No" options under the "tags_2", "tags_3", "tags_4", "tags_5", and "tags_6" fields.
15. Finally, enter your name in the "name" field.
