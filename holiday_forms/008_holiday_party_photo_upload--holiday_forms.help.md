# Holiday Party Photo Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Party Photo Upload form is designed to allow users to upload photos related to holiday parties. This form gathers information and media about holiday parties to capture memories and share with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the first field. This is the name of the holiday party, such as "Christmas Party 2022".
2. If you have additional information about the event, you can enter it in the **Event Details** field. This can include any relevant details about the event, such as the date, time, and location.
3. In the **Photo URL** field, enter the URL of the photo you would like to upload.
4. Enter the **Attendee Name** of the person in the photo.
5. Enter the **Attendee Email** of the person in the photo, if applicable.
6. Enter the **Attendee Phone** of the person in the photo, if applicable.
7. Write a **Photo Caption** to describe the photo and the event.
8. Click on the **Upload Photo** button to upload the photo.
9. If you have an **Event Photo**, you can also upload it using the **Upload Event Photo** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the holiday party, such as "Christmas Party 2022".

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): If you have additional information about the event, you can enter it here, such as "December 24th, 2022, at 6pm, at the beach".

### Photo URL
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the photo you would like to upload.

### Attendee Name
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person in the photo.

### Attendee Email
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person in the photo, if applicable.

### Attendee Phone
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person in the photo, if applicable.

### Photo Caption
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption`, text, optional): Write a description of the photo and the event.

### Upload Photo
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`, text, optional): Click on this button to upload the photo.

### Upload Event Photo
* **Upload Event Photo** (`upload_event_photo`, text, optional): If you have an event photo, you can also upload it using this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the photo you upload is suitable for public display.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties, contact your IT department for assistance.
