# Holiday Luncheon Entrée Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information for a holiday luncheon event. It helps event planners get a headcount and track RSVPs, as well as gather details such as entrée choice and dietary restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're filling out the correct information for the intended event.
2. Select the correct page (Main Page, RSVPs, Guest Details, etc.) based on your needs.
3. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Main Page
* **Main Page**: This is the initial page of the form where you'll find general information about the event. No action required.

### RSVPs
* **RSVPs**: This field is to enter your RSVP information, which is a yes or no response. Select **Option 1** or **Option 2** accordingly.

### Guest Details
* **Guest Details**: This field allows you to select multiple options related to your preferences. Select **Yes** or **No** based on your needs.

### Entrée Choice
* **Entrée Choice**: Select the preferred entrée for the event from the provided options (**Entrée Choice 1**, **Entrée Choice 2**, or **Entrée Choice 3**).

### Dietary Restrictions
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your needs.

### Special Request
* **Special Request**: This is a text field where you can enter any special requests you may have. No specific format or structure is required, just a brief description of your needs.

### Guest Name
* **Guest Name**: Enter your full name in this text field.

### Guest Email
* **Guest Email**: Enter your email address in this format: `name@example.com`. Please ensure it's a valid email address.

### Guest Phone
* **Guest Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format: `(XXX) XXX-XXXX`. Please use a format like this: 123-456-7890.
