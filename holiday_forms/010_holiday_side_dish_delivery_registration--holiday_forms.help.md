# holiday_side_dish_delivery_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register holiday side dishes for a specific holiday or event. It gathers information about customer preferences, details, and delivery options for multiple side dishes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your side dish preferences from the options provided.
2. Enter your customer details in the text field.
3. Choose your delivery preference (Delivery Time, Pick Up, or Delivery).
4. Select the side dishes you would like to register.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the side dishes.
6. Provide your email address if you wish to receive updates or notifications.
7. If relevant, select a specific time for the delivery.
8. If relevant, select a specific date for the delivery.
9. Enter a list of side dishes in the text field.
10. Confirm your holiday side dish list ID if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Side Dish Preferences** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the side dishes you are interested in registering from the provided options.
* **Customer Details** (Text, Required): Enter your customer details to be associated with the registered side dishes.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to receive your registered side dishes (Delivery Time, Pick Up, or Delivery).
* **Selected Sides** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the specific side dishes you would like to register.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the registered side dishes.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Provide your email address if you wish to receive updates or notifications.
* **Time** (Time, Optional): If you chose "Delivery Time", select a specific time for the delivery.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): If you chose "Delivery", select a specific date for the delivery.
* **Side Dish List** (Text, Required): Enter a list of side dishes you would like to register.
* **Holiday Side Dish List** (Number, Optional): Confirm your holiday side dish list ID if you have one.
* **Side Dish 1-10** (Text, Optional): Enter specific details about side dishes 1-10.
* **Holiday Side Dish 2-3** (Text, Optional): Enter specific details about holiday side dishes 2-3.
