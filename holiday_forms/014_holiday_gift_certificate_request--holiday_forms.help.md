# Holiday Gift Certificate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a holiday gift certificate for a colleague or friend. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure the gift is delivered correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Full Name**, which is the name you want to be associated with the gift certificate request.
2. Provide a valid **Email** address, which will be used to confirm your request and deliver the gift certificate.
3. Enter the **Certificate Number** of the gift card you want to request.
4. Enter the **Recipient Name** who will receive the gift certificate.
5. Enter the **Receiver Email** of the recipient who will receive the gift certificate.
6. Specify the **Gift Card Value** you want to request.
7. Choose the **Payment Method** you prefer (Stripe, PayPal, or Cash).
8. Select a **Delivery Date** for the gift certificate to be delivered.
9. Choose a specific **Delivery Time** for the gift certificate delivery.
10. Enter the **Delivery Address** where the gift certificate should be delivered.
11. Optionally, enter any **Delivery Notes** if you have any special requests.
12. Review the **Agreement** about the payment terms and conditions before submitting your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name, which will be associated with the gift certificate request.
* **Email** (2): Enter a valid email address to confirm your request and receive the gift certificate.
* **Certificate Number** (3): Enter the certificate number of the gift card you want to request.
* **Recipient Name** (4): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift certificate.
* **Receiver Email** (5): Enter the recipient's email address, where the gift certificate will be delivered.
* **Gift Card Value** (6): Specify the value of the gift card you want to request.
* **Payment Method** (7): Choose how you want to pay for the gift certificate (Stripe, PayPal, or Cash).
* **Delivery Date** (8): Select the date when you want the gift certificate to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (9): Choose the time for the gift certificate delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (10): Enter the address where the gift certificate should be delivered.
* **Delivery Notes** (11): Optionally, enter any special notes for the gift certificate delivery.
* **Agreement** (12): Review and agree to the payment terms and conditions before submitting your request.
* **Submit** (13): Click to submit your gift certificate request.
