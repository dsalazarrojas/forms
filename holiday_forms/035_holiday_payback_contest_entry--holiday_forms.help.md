# Holiday Payback Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit their entries for a holiday payback contest. The purpose of the form is to collect information about the contest entry, including the story, contact details, and entry status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Entry section, providing a brief story about your entry.
2. Enter your contact information in the Contact section, including your phone number and email address.
3. If you have won a prize, enter the value of the prize in the Prize Value field.
4. If the entry is still active or inactive, select the corresponding option in the Entry Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry** (`entry`, `note`, required: false): A brief story about your entry.
* **Story** (`story`, `text`, required: false): Enter the story about your holiday payback.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `number`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the holiday payback.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the holiday payback.
* **Contest** (`contest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you participated in a contest.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if your entry is yes or no.
* **Prize** (`prize`, `number`, required: false): If you have won a prize, enter the value of the prize.
* **Prize Value** (`prize_value`, `number`, required: false): If you have won a prize, enter the value of the prize.
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if your entry is active or inactive.

## Tips

* Make sure to include a brief and detailed story about your holiday payback.
* Enter valid contact information and prize values.
* Select the correct entry status (active or inactive).
* Do not submit duplicate entries.
