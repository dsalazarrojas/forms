# Holiday Recognition Awards Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Recognition Awards Nomination form is used to recognize and reward employees for their contributions and efforts during the holiday season. This form allows you to submit a nomination for an employee who deserves recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the nomination details in the text area labeled "nomination".
2. Provide the reason for the recognition in the text field labeled "reason_for_recognition".
3. Enter the employee's name and title in the text fields labeled "employee_name" and "manager_title", respectively.
4. Enter the nominee's name and email address in the text fields labeled "nominee_name" and "nominee_email", respectively.
5. Enter the nominee's phone number in the text field labeled "nominee_phone".
6. Enter your name and email address in the text fields labeled "nominator_name" and "nominator_email", respectively.
7. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "nominator_phone".
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **nomination (Note)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the nomination.
* **reason_for_recognition**: Explain the specific reasons why the employee deserves recognition.
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **manager_title**: Enter the employee's manager's title.
* **nominee_name**: Enter the name of the employee being recognized.
* **nominee_email**: Enter the nominee's email address.
* **nominee_phone**: Enter the nominee's phone number.
* **nominator_name**: Enter your name.
* **nominator_email**: Enter your email address.
* **nominator_phone**: Enter your phone number.

Note: All fields are optional, but it is highly recommended to fill them out thoroughly to ensure that your nomination is complete and accurate.
