# holiday_treehouse_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for booking a treehouse during holidays. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
3. Double-check your entries for correctness and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, optional):
  This field is for providing any relevant details about the guest(s) staying at the treehouse.
* **Stay Dates** (`stay_dates`, text, optional):
  Enter the dates of your stay at the treehouse, including start and end dates.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional):
  If you have any special requests or requirements for your stay, such as dietary needs or extra amenities, enter them here.
* **Treehouse Details** (`treehouse_details`, text, optional):
  Provide information about the treehouse, including its name, location, and any unique features.
* **Host Info** (`host_info`, text, optional):
  Enter any relevant information about your host, such as their name, phone number, or email address.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, optional):
  Specify your preferred method of contact, such as phone or email, and any specific contact information.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional):
  Enter your payment information, including the method and any relevant details.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional):
  If there's anything else you'd like to mention or discuss during your stay, add it here.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the form owner or admin.
