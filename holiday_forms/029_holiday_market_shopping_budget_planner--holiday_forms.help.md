# holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help shoppers and organizers plan and track holiday market shopping budgets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Holiday Market Spending Planning (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_planning)**: Enter a brief description or note about the holiday market shopping planning process.
2. **Products (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_products)**: Enter the products you plan to buy or have already bought during the holiday market shopping period.
3. **Currency (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_currency)**: Select the currency you plan to use for shopping (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP).
4. **Shopping Date (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_date)**: Enter the date you plan to go shopping.
5. **Shopping Time (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_time)**: Enter the time you plan to start shopping.
6. **Shopping Location (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_location)**: Enter the location where you plan to shop.
7. **Shopping Method (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_method)**: Select whether you plan to shop in person or online.
8. **Shopping Duration (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_duration)**: Enter the duration of your shopping trip.
9. **Shopping Frequency (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopping_frequency)**: Select how often you plan to shop (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
10. **Budget Planned (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_budget_planned)**: Enter your planned budget for the shopping trip.
11. **Budget Actual (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_budget_actual)**: Enter your actual spending during the shopping trip.
12. **Budget Difference (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_budget_difference)**: Calculate the difference between your planned and actual spending.
13. **Event Feedback (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_event_feedback)**: Enter any feedback or comments about the shopping event.
14. **Additional Comments (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_additional_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the shopping experience.
15. **Post Event Feedback (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_post_event_feedback)**: Enter any feedback or comments after the shopping event.
16. **Shopper Name (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopper_name)**: Enter your name as the shopper.
17. **Shopper Phone (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopper_phone)**: Enter your phone number as the shopper.
18. **Shopper Email (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopper_email)**: Enter your email address as the shopper.
19. **Organizer Name (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_organizer_name)**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
20. **Organizer Email (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_organizer_email)**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
21. **Organizer Phone (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_organizer_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
22. **Additional Organizer Comments (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_additional_organizer_comments)**: Enter any additional comments from the event organizer.
23. **Shopper Comments (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_shopper_comments)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the shopper.
24. **Post Event Feedback Frequency (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_post_event_feedback_frequency)**: Select how often you want to receive post-event feedback (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
25. **Additional Shopper Comments (holiday_market_shopping_budget_planner_additional_shopper_comments)**: Enter any additional comments from the shopper.
