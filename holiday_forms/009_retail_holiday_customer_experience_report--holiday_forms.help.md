# Retail Holiday Customer Experience Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Holiday Customer Experience Report is a survey designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences during holidays. The report helps us understand how we can improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Choose the holiday season you visited.
2. Answer the questions about your experiences, such as what you saw, what you liked, and what we could improve.
3. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
4. Finally, provide your email and phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Shopping Location** (holiday_shopping_location, Text, Optional): Where did you visit for holiday shopping?
* **Visited Holiday Store** (visited_holiday_store, Select One, Optional): Did you visit any holiday stores?
	+ If yes, please select the correct store.
* **Visited Other Store** (visited_others, Select Multiple, Optional): Did you visit any other stores?
	+ If yes, please select all the stores you visited.
* **Date Visited** (holiday_date, Date, Optional): When did you visit the holiday store?
* **Start Time** (start_time, Time, Optional): What time did you start your visit?
* **End Time** (end_time, Time, Optional): What time did you end your visit?
* **Holiday Experience** (holiday_experience, Text, Optional): Describe your experience at the holiday store.
* **Feedback** (feedback, Note, Optional): Do you have any feedback or comments about your experience?
* **Email** (email, Email, Optional): Please provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone, Text, Optional): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Comments** (comments, Text, Optional): Do you have any additional comments or feedback to share with us.
* **What Was Done While Visiting** (holiday_experience_what_was_done, Text, Optional): What was done while you were visiting the store that you were happy or unhappy with?
* **Why Holiday Season** (holiday_experience_why, Text, Optional): Why do you think the holiday season was a success or a failure?
* **Holiday Shopping Experience** (holiday_shopping_experience, Text, Optional): Describe your overall experience with holiday shopping.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (holiday_experience_suggestions, Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions for how we can improve our services?
* **Holiday Experiences** (holiday_experiences, Select Multiple, Optional): How would you rate your holiday experiences?
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **Holiday Employee Experience** (holiday_experience_employees, Select One, Optional): How would you rate your experience with holiday employees?
	+ Please select one that best describes your experience.
* **Holiday Products** (holiday_experience_products, Select Multiple, Optional): How would you rate the holiday products?
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **Suggestions for Holiday Products** (holiday_experience_products_suggestions, Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions for holiday products?
* **Holiday Price** (holiday_experience_price, Select One, Optional): How would you rate the price of the holiday products?
	+ Please select one that best describes your experience.
* **Holiday Employee Friendliness** (holiday_experience_employees, Select One, Optional): How would you rate the friendliness of the holiday employees?
	+ Please select one that best describes your experience.
* **Holiday Parking** (holiday_experience_parking, Select Multiple, Optional): Was the parking situation good, bad, or difficult to find?
* **Holiday Facilities** (holiday_experience_facilities, Select Multiple, Optional): Were the facilities good, bad, or not available?
* **Suggestion** (holiday_experience_suggestion, Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions or feedback for us?
* **Submission** (submission, Note, Optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Name** (submission_name, Text, Optional): Please provide your name so we can address you correctly.
