# submit_your_location_for_holiday_carols - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "submit_your_location_for_holiday_carols" form is used to gather information about your location and preferences for the holiday carols event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Enter your location:
	* Address
	* Street
	* City
	* State
	* Zip
3. Choose your preferred time for the holiday carols:
	* Select one or more of the following options:
		+ Carol 1
		+ Carol 2
		+ Carol 3
4. (Optional) Add any accessibility information or notes:
	* Accessibility Info
	* Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Street** (`street`, text, required): Enter your street or building name.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city or town.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, date, required): Choose a date and time for the holiday carols event.
* **Accessibility Info** (`accessibility_info`, text, optional): Provide any accessibility information that we should know about you.
* **Holiday Carols** (`holiday_carols`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more holiday carols that you would like to participate in.
* **Holiday Carols Time** (`carols_time`, time, required): Choose the time for the holiday carols event.
* **Holiday Carols Details** (`holiday_carols_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about your holiday carols participation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* If you have any specific requests or accommodations for the event, please add them in the "Accessibility Info" field.
* You can select multiple carols for the holiday carols event.
* For the "Holiday Carols Time", choose a date and time that suits your schedule.
