# Staff Holiday Gift Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees select and manage their holiday gifts. It allows employees to express their gift preferences, confirm delivery details, and provide any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer the holiday form questions on page 1.
2. Select your delivery preferences on page 2.
3. Choose your gift preferences on page 3.
4. Enter your delivery address and confirmation details on page 4.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Holiday Form Questions
* **Holiday Form Questions** (`holiday_form_questions`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information or comments you would like to provide about your holiday gift preferences.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, text, optional): This field is for employees to confirm their holiday gift preferences and any additional comments.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): This field is for the person submitting the form to provide their name and contact information.

### Page 2: Delivery Preferences
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you prefer delivery or not.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your preferred delivery method (e.g., email, in-person, etc.).

### Page 3: Gift Picked
* **Gift Picked** (`gift_picked`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you want to select a gift.
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional details about the gift you have chosen.

### Page 4: Delivery Address
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address for the gift.
* **Confirm Delivery Date** (`confirm_delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to confirm delivery.
* **Confirm Delivery Time** (`confirm_delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to confirm delivery.
* **Confirm Delivery Date/Time** (`confirm_delivery_date_time`, text, optional): This field is for confirming the delivery date and time.
* **Delivery Currency** (`delivery_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency of the gift.

### Page 5: Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the gift.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the employee.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the manager.
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the gift.
* **Gift Currency Value** (`gift_currency_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the gift in the selected currency.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional): This field is for recording the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Gift Currency** (`gift_currency`, text, optional): Select the currency of the gift.
