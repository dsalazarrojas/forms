# Pre Consultation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pre Consultation Checklist" form is designed to collect essential information from clients before their consultation. This form helps you gather crucial details about the client's condition, appointment schedule, and other relevant factors to ensure a productive and successful consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's details, including their name, contact information, and any relevant contact preferences (optional).
2. Describe the symptoms you are experiencing, including the nature and duration of the symptoms.
3. Provide details about the appointment, such as the date, time, and location.
4. Confirm your consent for the consultation.
5. Share any concerns or questions you have about the consultation.
6. Enter the consultant's contact information, including their name and availability.
7. Schedule the appointment, selecting a suitable date and time.
8. Add any follow-up instructions or next steps after the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (Client Details)** (`client_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Symptoms (Symptoms)** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false):
    Describe the symptoms you are experiencing, including their nature and duration.
* **Appointment Details (Appointment Details)** (`appointment_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide details about the appointment, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Consent (Consent)** (`consent`, `text`, required: false):
    Confirm your consent for the consultation. Please check this box to indicate your agreement.
* **Client Concerns (Client Concerns)** (`client_concerns`, `text`, required: false):
    Share any concerns or questions you have about the consultation.
* **Consultant Details (Consultant Details)** (`consultant_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the consultant's contact information, including their name and availability.
* **Appointment Schedule (Appointment Schedule)** (`appointment_schedule`, `time`, required: false):
    Schedule the appointment, selecting a suitable date and time. Please choose a time that works best for you.
* **Follow Up (Follow Up)** (`follow_up`, `text`, required: false):
    Add any follow-up instructions or next steps after the consultation.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in each field to ensure the consultant has a clear understanding of your needs.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
