# Eye Exam Appointment Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help eye exam teams gather and document crucial information about patients during an eye exam.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the patient information page.
	* Enter the patient's name and other relevant identifying details.
	* Ensure that you have obtained their consent to proceed with the exam.
2. Proceed to the medical history page and enter any relevant medical history information.
3. On the allergies page, list any allergies the patient may have.
4. On the medications page, list any medications the patient is currently taking.
5. On the test results page, enter any relevant test results.
6. On the notes page, include any additional comments or observations from the exam.
7. On the medical team page, select the medical team members involved in the exam (Dr Smith, Dr Johnson, Dr Thompson, or Dr Brown).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies the patient may have, including any medications or substances that cause reactions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required: false): Additional medical history information.
* **Allergies 2** (`allergies_2`, `text`, required: false): Additional allergies information.
* **Medications 2** (`medications_2`, `text`, required: false): Additional medications information.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant test results from previous exams or tests.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Include any additional comments or observations from the exam.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the medical team members involved in the exam (Dr Smith, Dr Johnson, Dr Thompson, or Dr Brown).
* **Medical Team 2** (`medical_team_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select additional medical team members involved in the exam (Dr Brown, Dr Lee, Dr Patel).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have obtained patient consent before completing this form.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that it remains relevant to your practice.
* Use this form as a template and customize it according to your specific needs.
