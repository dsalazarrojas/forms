# legal_consultation_appointment_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist template used to collect essential information for legal consultations, including client contact details, appointment specifics, and follow-up requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including client contact details and appointment details.
2. Choose the preferred method of communication for the appointment (in-person, phone, or video call).
3. Specify the meeting location, date, and time.
4. Enter the expected duration of the meeting.
5. Enter the client's name, phone number, and email address.
6. Enter the attorney's name and contact information.
7. Add any relevant notes about the meeting.
8. Schedule a follow-up date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Contact Information** (`client_contact_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the client's contact information, such as name, phone number, and email address.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select the method of communication for the appointment, including in-person, phone, or video call.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false): Enter the specific location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting in the format of HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM 24-hour.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the expected duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the attorney.
* **Attorney Contact Information** (`attorney_contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter the attorney's contact information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any relevant notes about the meeting.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the follow-up, if required.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the follow-up, if required.
* **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the follow-up, if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the client's contact information for correctness.
* Ensure the appointment details are precise and clear.
* Enter any relevant notes about the meeting.
* Schedule follow-up dates and times according to your needs.
