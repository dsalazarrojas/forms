# Tape In Hair Extension Placement Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tape In Hair Extension Placement Checklist" is a form designed to collect information related to tape-in hair extension placement appointments. This form helps gather essential details about the client, the stylist, and the placement process, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the stylist's name in the "Stylist" field.
3. Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field (optional).
4. Select the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.
5. Choose the services preferred by the client in the "Services Preferred" field (multiple options can be selected).
6. Choose the status of the tape-in application in the "Tape-In Application Status" field.
7. Enter any additional client conversation in the "Client Conversation" field (optional).
8. Enter any notes from the stylist in the "Stylist Notes" field (optional).
9. Enter any client comments in the "Client Comments" field (optional).
10. Enter a follow-up appointment date in the "Follow Up Appointment" field (optional).
11. Enter the client's contact information in the "Client Contact" field (optional).
12. Enter the stylist's signature in the "Stylist Signature" field (required).
13. Choose the current tape-in status in the "Tape-In Status" field.
14. Select the type of tape-in extension in the "Tape-In Extension Type" field.
15. Enter the tape-in location in the "Tape-in Location" field (optional).
16. Enter the tape-in application time in the "Tape-In Application Time" field (optional).
17. Enter the tape-in color in the "Tape-in Color" field (optional).
18. Enter the tape-in length in the "Tape-in Length" field (optional).
19. Enter the tape-in width in the "Tape-in Width" field (optional).
20. Enter the tape-in quantity in the "Tape-in Quantity" field (optional).
