# dental_appointment_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental appointment checklist form is used to gather information for a dental appointment, including new patient details and appointment scheduling information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "New Patient" section to enter new patient information if applicable.
2.  Enter the patient's name in the "prophets_name" field.
3.  Provide the patient's contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4.  Enter the patient's email address in the "patient_email" field.
5.  Select the appointment date in the "appointment_date" field.
6.  Choose the appointment time in the "appointment_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient**: A section to enter new patient information if applicable. 
    *   **prophets_name (Patient Name)**: Enter the patient's full name.
    *   **contact_number (Contact Number)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
    *   **patient_email (Patient Email)**: Enter the patient's email address.
*  **Appointment**: A section to schedule the appointment.
    *   **appointment_date (Appointment Date)**: Select the date for the appointment.
    *   **appointment_time (Appointment Time)**: Choose the time for the appointment.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
*   If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
*   Submit the form once all information is complete and accurate.
