# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Complaint Form is designed to collect information about incidents of harassment that occur in the workplace. This form is a confidential and secure channel for employees to report their experiences and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required and optional fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
3. If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to this guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workplace Harassment Complaint Form** (`complaint_form`, text, required): This is the main field for describing the incident of harassment you are reporting.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Please enter the date when the incident occurred.
- **Description of Harassment** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the harassment incident, including any relevant details such as location, time, and witnesses.
- **Harasser** (`harasser`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person involved in the harassment incident.
- **Relationship with Harasser** (`relationship_with_harasser`, select_one, required): Select the type of relationship you have with the harasser (e.g., Manager, Colleague, Supervisor, Subordinate).
- **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): If you have taken any action or reported the incident to anyone, please describe it in this field.
- **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text, optional): If you have reported the incident to someone, please enter their name or title.
- **Witness** (`witness`, text, optional): If there were witnesses to the incident, please list their names or contact information.
- **Resolution** (`resolution`, text, optional): If the incident has been resolved, please describe the outcome.
- **Company Response** (`company_response`, text, optional): If the company has responded to your report, please describe their response.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide or ask for help.
* Remember that this form is a confidential channel for reporting incidents of harassment.
