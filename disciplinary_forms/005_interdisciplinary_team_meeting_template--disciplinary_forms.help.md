# interdisciplinary_team_meeting_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track meetings within the team, facilitating effective communication and collaboration among team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting type (Team or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of the meeting.
3. List the attendees.
4. Choose the meeting location.
5. Enter the start and end times.
6. Set the meeting duration.
7. Add any meeting notes.
8. Select the next meeting date (if applicable).
9. Indicate the meeting status (Scheduled, Canceled, or In Progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the meeting's purpose and goals.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one, not required): Choose whether the meeting is a Team meeting or Other.
* **Description** (`meeting_description`, text, not required): Provide a more detailed description of the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, select_multiple, not required): List the team members attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, time, not required): Choose the start time of the meeting.
* **End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, time, not required): Choose the end time of the meeting.
* **Duration** (`meeting_du`, number, not required): Set the meeting duration in minutes.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the meeting.
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting`, date, not required): If applicable, select the next meeting date.
* **Status** (`meeting_status`, select_one, not required): Indicate the meeting status (Scheduled, Canceled, or In Progress).
