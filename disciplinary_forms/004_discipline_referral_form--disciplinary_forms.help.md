# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discipline Referral Form is used to bring a student's behavior to the attention of a higher authority, typically for incidents that are not minor and are not already being addressed through other means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the field labeled "student_name".
2. Enter the teacher's name in the field labeled "teacher_name".
3. Enter the student's ID number in the field labeled "student_id".
4. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the referral in the field labeled "reason_for_referral".
5. Enter the date and time of the incident in the fields labeled "date_of_incident" and "time_of_incident".
6. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the field labeled "location".
7. Describe the action taken or planned to address the incident in the field labeled "action_taken".
8. Record the student's response to the incident in the field labeled "student_response".
9. Enter your name and title in the field labeled "administrator_name".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: Enter the name of the student being referred.
* **teacher_name**: Enter the name of the teacher or staff member referring the student.
* **student_id**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **reason_for_referral**: Briefly explain the reason for the referral.
* **date_of_incident**: Enter the date of the incident using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **time_of_incident**: Enter the time of the incident using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **action_taken**: Describe the action taken or planned to address the incident.
* **student_response**: Record the student's response to the incident.
* **administrator_name**: Enter your name and title.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the student's behavior is properly addressed.
* Use this form only when necessary and when other processes or forms are not sufficient.
* Review the fields and ensure all required information is provided before submitting the form.
