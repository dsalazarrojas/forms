# Verbal Warning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verbal Warning Form is used to document incidents where an employee's behavior or performance is not meeting the expected standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and manager's name in the respective fields.
2. Select the date of the incident.
3. Provide a clear and detailed explanation of the reason for the verbal warning in the "Reason for Verbal Warning" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears in your personnel records.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Manager's Name**: Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who is issuing the verbal warning.
* **Reason for Verbal Warning**: Describe the specific behavior or performance issue that led to the verbal warning. Be clear, concise, and objective, focusing on the facts and avoiding subjective opinions or personal opinions.
  * Tip: Consider the company's policies, procedures, and industry standards when writing the reason for the verbal warning.
* **Additional Notes**: [No additional notes are explicitly available on this form; consider adding a note for further context or details. If you need additional space, consider using a separate document or attachment.]

## Tips
* Be mindful of the employee's rights and ensure that the form is used fairly and objectively.
* Consider the impact of the verbal warning on the employee's well-being and work environment.
* Follow your organization's procedures for issuing verbal warnings and for handling employee disciplinary actions.
* Store the form in a secure location to ensure confidentiality and maintain employee trust.
