# Sexual Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about incidents of sexual harassment within the workplace, providing a clear and detailed account of what happened. This form helps ensure that the necessary steps are taken to address and resolve the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure you have reported the incident to the relevant authority before filling out this form.
3. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
Employee Name: This is your name as an employee.
### 2. Incident Location (`incident_location`, text, required: false)
Incident Location: Describe the location where the incident occurred, including the room number or floor if applicable.
### 3. Description (`description`, text, required: false)
Description: Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any relevant details about the perpetrators and witnesses.
### 4. Witness Names (`witness_names`, select_multiple, required: false)
Witness Names: Check all boxes that apply for witnesses who saw or were aware of the incident. This could include colleagues, supervisors, or other employees.
### 5. Date of Incident (`date_of_incident`, date, required: false)
Date of Incident: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
### 6. Time of Incident (`time_of_incident`, time, required: false)
Time of Incident: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
### 7. Witness Statement (`witness_statement`, text, required: false)
Witness Statement: If you have any witness statements or information about what the witnesses said or did, please include it here.
### 8. Reporting Officer (`reporting_officer`, select_one, required: false)
Reporting Officer: Select the officer to whom you reported the incident. Options include:
	* Badge Number: Enter the badge number of the reporting officer.
	* Name: Enter the name of the reporting officer.
	* Email: Enter the email address of the reporting officer.
	* Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the reporting officer.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority for guidance.
* If you have any supporting evidence or documentation, please attach it to the form.
