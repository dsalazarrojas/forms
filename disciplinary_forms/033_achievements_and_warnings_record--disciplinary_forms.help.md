# Achievements And Warnings Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Achievements And Warnings Record" form is used to document notable events or actions taken by individuals in the organization, such as achievements or warnings. This form helps create a centralized record of these events, providing a clear and concise account of what happened, who was involved, and what actions were taken or recommended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   * `Person name`: Enter the name of the individual involved in the event.
   * `Record type`: Select "Achievement" or "Warning" to indicate the type of event.
   * `Date of record`: Enter the date when the event occurred.
   * `Description`: Provide details about the event, including any relevant context.
   * `Issued by`: Enter the name of the person or entity that issued the record (e.g., manager, teacher, or issuer).
   * `Confirmed signature`: Add your name and title to confirm the record's accuracy.

2. Optionally, provide additional information:
   * `Role or position`: If relevant, enter the individual's job title or class and year.
   * `Recommended actions`: If applicable, suggest any actions that should be taken in response to the event.
   * `Outcome`: Briefly describe the result of any disciplinary or reward process.
   * `Related documents`: Attach any relevant documents or references.
   * `Reviewer comments`: Add any comments or notes from reviewers for HR or administrative use.

* **Person name** (required): Enter the name of the individual involved in the event.

* **Date of record** (required): Enter the date when the event occurred.
* **Description** (required): Provide details about the event, including any relevant context.
* **Issued by** (required): Enter the name of the person or entity that issued the record.
* **Recommended actions** (optional): If applicable, suggest any actions that should be taken in response to the event.
* **Outcome** (optional): Briefly describe the result of any disciplinary or reward process.
* **Related documents** (optional): Attach any relevant documents or references.
* **Reviewer comments** (optional): Add any comments or notes from reviewers for HR or administrative use.
* **Confirmed signature** (required): Add your name and title to confirm the record's accuracy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions and recommendations.
* Attach relevant documents or references when applicable.
* Review and verify the accuracy of the record before confirming it.
