# Disciplinary Process Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Process Time Off Form is used to manage employee time off and leave requests, tracking absences, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "I am an employee" or "I am a manager/supervisor" to indicate your role.
2. Fill out the form accordingly, providing the requested information.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the employee.
	+ This field is required for internal tracking and identification purposes.
* **Time Off Start Date (2)**: The date the employee is expected to begin their time off.
	+ This field is optional, but helps in scheduling and tracking absences.
* **Time Off End Date (3)**: The date the employee is expected to end their time off.
	+ This field is optional, but helps in scheduling and tracking absences.

	+ This field is optional, but helps in tracking the progress of the approval process.
